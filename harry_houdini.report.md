# Harry Houdini (Erik Weisz), 1874-1926, research report

*Compiled 2026-08-31 from the verified pool for slug `harry_houdini`. **[A]** = attested, source named. **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition, or publicity copy. Contradictions are flagged, not adjudicated.*

The governing difficulty is that he was a professional liar about his own life and a meticulous archivist of everybody else's. So the evidence divides cleanly: where a police court, a patent office, a passenger manifest, or his own printed book speaks, the record is firm; where his press agentry speaks, it is copy.

## 1. Budapest, Appleton, Milwaukee, 1874-1887

- Born **Erik Weisz**, 24 March 1874, Erzsébetváros, Budapest, to Rabbi Mayer Sámuel Weisz and Cecília Steiner **[A: Wikipedia; PBS timeline]**. He claimed **Appleton, Wisconsin** throughout his career: false publicity, not error.
- Emigration on the **SS Frisia**, landing New York 3 July 1878, Cecelia pregnant **[A]**. Castle Garden as the depot is an inference from port and year, not a manifest citation **[R]**.
- Appleton: Mayer Weiss holds the **Zion Reform** pulpit until his 1882 dismissal; naturalized 6 June 1882; then Milwaukee poverty 1882-1887, children selling papers and shining shoes. **[A: PBS; Silverman; History Museum at the Castle]**
- **Contradiction, unresolved.** PBS dates the five-cent backyard circus ("Ehrich, the Prince of the Air") to **1883 at Appleton**, while the same sources put the family in **Milwaukee from 1882**. Both carried; the circus is **[R]** anyway.
- 1887: father takes Ehrich ahead to New York; they board on **East Seventy-ninth Street**; necktie cutting; Robert-Houdin's memoirs. **[A: PBS names the street]** **Gap:** no house number anywhere reachable.

## 2. Dime museums, 1891-1899

- **The Brothers Houdini** with Jacob Hyman, 1891; name from **Jean-Eugène Robert-Houdin**; Theodore replaces Hyman within a year **[A]**. Chicago 1893: the fringe around the **World's Columbian Exposition**, an obscure act, *not* a fair attraction **[A: Wikipedia; Silverman]**.
- Married **Bess Rahner** 22 June 1894 after roughly three weeks. **[A as to date]** **[R as to venue and rite]**: sources differ on site, officiant, and whether there were civil, Jewish, and Catholic elements or a subset. Not adjudicated.
- **Metamorphosis** and the first handcuff exhibitions, 1895, with the Welsh Brothers Circus **[A: PBS]**. No pin exists, so this is folded into the marriage and dime-museum stops.
- 1898: catalogue for **Harry Houdini's School of Magic**, ready to quit. **[A: PBS]**
- **11 January 1899:** handcuffs defeat him at **Middleton's Clark Street Dime Museum, Chicago** **[A: Wikipedia names the venue]**. The pool flagged it as unverified against a primary clipping; the venue naming settles part of that doubt, not the clipping.
- **Martin Beck** sees the act in a St. Paul beer hall, brings his own irons, then books him on the Orpheum circuit with the instruction to drop the magic. **[A: PBS]** **Gap:** month and day unrecorded, so the stop sorts before the January defeat although it came after. Stated in the stop.

## 3. Europe, 1900-1904

- **New Scotland Yard**, 1900, before police of Superintendent Melville's world, room unconfirmed **[A: CIA, "Was Houdini a Spy?"; PBS]**; then the **Alhambra**, at roughly $300/week and international headline status **[A]**; and **Berlin**, escapes before police **[A: PBS]**, folded into the Cologne stop since no venue is named.
- **Cologne, 1902:** Werner Graff and the *Rheinische Zeitung* accuse him of bribery and duplicate keys under the headline "The Unmasking of Houdini"; he sues, demonstrates in court, wins, and wins the appeal with costs against Graff **[A: PBS, "The Slander Trial"]**. The judge's-safe legend is **[R]** and not carried.
- **Moscow, 1903:** escape from a Siberian prison transport van **[R]**.
  - **Tag contradiction inside the pool:** its chronology lens tags this **[A]**, its geography lens **[R]**. **[R]** is defensible, since Wikipedia says only "in Moscow he escaped from a Siberian prison transport van" and never names **Butyrka**, whose own article never mentions Houdini. The "only key in Siberia" line is copy; a private performance for **Tsar Nicholas II** is weaker still and not carried.
- **London Hippodrome, 17 March 1904:** the *Daily Mirror* nested cuffs, ~4,000 spectators, over an hour, coat cut off with a knife in his teeth. **[A: Wikipedia; PBS; Silverman on the diary]**

## 4. The challenge circuit, 1904-1909

- **278 West 113th Street** bought 1904, reportedly $25,000. **[A]** **Contradiction:** Wikipedia gives 1904, PBS gives 1905. Both carried, 1904 used.
- **Washington, D.C. jail, 1906.** **[A: PBS]** The pool called the Guiteau-cell detail "popular legend, not verified", but its own source states it outright: "Houdini escaped from the Washington, D.C. jail that once held President Garfield's assassin." Carried, no primary jail record reached.
- **Rochester, 1907.** The pool's claim was a generic "challenge escape"; its own PBS source is specific: "His first filmed manacled bridge jump was captured in Rochester, New York." Rewritten **[A]**. **Gap:** the bridge is unnamed, so the pin is city-level.
- ***Conjurers' Monthly Magazine*** 1906-1908 **[A: Steinmeyer]**; *The Right Way to Do Wrong*, 1906 **[A]**; ***The Unmasking of Robert-Houdin***, 1908 **[A: read directly, Gutenberg #42723]**.
- **Milk Can Escape**, debut dated **25 January 1908** by Wikipedia, which names no city; **St. Louis** rests on PBS and Silverman, theater unidentified, pin city-level.
- **Boston, c. 1908-1911:** the sea-creature challenge escape **[R]**. Neither the pool nor this pass could verify date or venue, so it is **dropped from the journey file**, recorded here.

## 5. Aviation, 1909-1910

- **Voisin biplane**, powered flight **near Hamburg, 26 November 1909**, with mechanic Antonio Brassac, after one training wreck. **[A: Smithsonian Air and Space]**
- **Diggers Rest, 18 March 1910:** three flights, longest ~7 min 37 sec over ~6 miles, witnessed, certified, recognised by the **Aerial League of Australia** **[A]**.
  - **Date contradiction:** Jewish Virtual Library gives **16 March** against the usual **18 March**. **Priority contradiction:** **Colin Defries** (9 Dec 1909) and **Ralph Banks** (1 Mar 1910) made earlier brief hops, so "first in Australia" turns on what counts as a flight; both counter-claims are carried in the campa. **Access gap:** the Museums Victoria article could not be re-fetched (server block), the Aerial League record being corroborated elsewhere.
- **Rosehill near Sydney, 1910:** a further flight before he abandons aviation **[R]**, airfield unconfirmed. **Dropped from the journey file.**

## 6. Upside down, 1912-1918

- **East River packing box, 7 July 1912.** **[A]**
- **Chinese Water Torture Cell**, **Circus Busch, Berlin, 21 September 1912**, debuted as the one-act play "Houdini Upside Down!" to secure copyright **[A]**. He called it USD. The "Chinese" is exoticist marketing with no Chinese content.
- **Cecelia Weiss dies 17 July 1913** in New York while he is touring Europe **[A]**. **Gap:** no source names the European city where the news reached him, so the stop sits at the Harlem house where she died and to which he returned, saying in `date_confidence` that he was not at that pin on that date.
- **Wax cylinders, Flatbush, 29 October 1914**, the only known recordings of his voice **[A: Public Domain Review]**. **Near Santa Ana, 1915:** buried in six feet of earth without a coffin, nearly suffocated **[A: his diary via Silverman and Begley]**.
- **S.A.M. national president from 1917 until death**; **New York Hippodrome 1917-1918**, vanishing elephant, war bonds credited above $1 million; **draft registration 12 September 1918** as "Harry Handcuff Houdini" (card images confirmed to exist, transcription not independently verified); ***The Master Mystery***, 1918. **[A: PBS; National Archives Atlanta]**

## 7. Films, patent, lodge, 1918-1924

- **Laurel Canyon, 1918-1920**, during the Famous Players-Lasky films, training at **Ralph M. Walker's** neighbouring property. The estate marketed for a century as the "Houdini Estate" **was Walker's** **[A]**. **Gap:** Houdini's rented address unconfirmed, pin general.
- **US Patent 1,370,316**, diver's suit with a quick-release waist: filed **30 June 1917**, granted **1 March 1921** **[A: the patent]**.
- **Freemasonry:** initiated at **St. Cecile Lodge No. 568**, **1923**; raised **31 July and 21 August 1924**; Scottish Rite benefit before ~4,000, folded into the same stop for want of a separate address. **[A: freemason.com]** **Correction:** the pool's precise **17 July 1923** is not supported by its own cited source, which gives only the year.

## 8. The war on the mediums, 1922-1926

- **Atlantic City, Ambassador Hotel:** Lady Jean Conan Doyle's automatic writing, about fifteen pages, in English, headed with a cross, purporting to come from Cecelia Weiss **[A]**.
  - **Date contradiction, unresolved and internal to the pool.** Houdini's own witnessed statement and printed account give **17 June 1922**, his mother's birthday; wildabouthoudini.com ("The Mama Message", 2019) argues from his private notes that it was **Sunday 18 June** and that he misdated it publicly. The pool's chronology lens carries 17 June, its geography lens 18 June, and its two correction notes instruct opposite things. **The journey file uses 1922-06-17 and states the conflict.**
- **Letter to Doyle, 15 December 1922**, to Windlesham, Crowborough. **[A: printed by Houdini himself, *A Magician Among the Spirits*, ch. IX]**
  - **Source and text corrections.** The pool cited a blog for two quotations Houdini published in his own book; both are re-sourced to the book (Gutenberg #66451), which shows both were altered: a comma inserted after "sincere", and a sentence restitched with its opening clause dropped ("I was heartily in accord and sympathy at that seance but...") and "Mother" lowercased. The pool also regularised the em-dashes in Lady Doyle's message to commas "per house style". A purported spirit message is exactly the text where silent repunctuation must not happen, so the verbatim reading is restored, double hyphens included. Quote fields are verbatim and exempt from house style.
- ***A Magician Among the Spirits***, 1924 **[A: read directly]**. The dedication in capitals ("IF GOD / IN HIS INFINITE WISDOM / EVER SENT AN ANGEL UPON EARTH IN HUMAN FORM / IT WAS MY MOTHER") is on the **dedication page**, not the introduction as the pool had it.
- **Margery (Mina Crandon)**, Boston 1924: control cabinet built, phenomena stop, conclusion that she rang the bell box with a foot; pamphlet privately printed that November. **Symphony Hall, January 1925:** public exposure with the cabinet on stage, Crandon reply at Jordan Hall days later, prize denied in February. **[A: PBS]**
- **Washington, 1926:** testimony on **H.R. 8989**, criminalising paid fortune-telling in the District; clashes with Jane B. Coates and Madam Grace Marcia; the bill fails. **[A: Young, *Journal of Social History* 48.2 (2014); HRC finding aid]** **Correction:** the pool's geography lens named only a **House** subcommittee, its own PBS source says "Senate and House subcommittees". Widened. Room unconfirmed.
- **Hotel Shelton, 5 August 1926:** sealed casket in the pool, **91 minutes** (Wikipedia's figure, not the pool's "about ninety"), answering Rahman Bey **[A]**. Repeated at the **Worcester YMCA, 28 September 1926**, about 71 minutes **[R: Silverman; Begley]**.

## 9. Montreal, Detroit, Machpelah

- **Princess Theatre, Montreal, 22 October 1926:** on a couch with a fractured ankle, struck repeatedly in the abdomen by McGill student **J. Gordon Whitehead** before bracing; **Sam Smilovitz** and **Jacques Price** describe "four or five terribly forcible, deliberate, well-directed blows." **[A: history.com]**
- **Garrick Theatre, Detroit, 24 October 1926:** last performance, fever near 104°F, against medical advice **[A: history.com]**.
- **Grace Hospital, 25-31 October 1926:** ruptured gangrenous appendix, diffuse peritonitis, death on Halloween. **[A: Wikipedia; Jewish Virtual Library]** **[R as to the hour, about 1:26 p.m., and the bedside detail: history.com; the Archives of Michigan certificate cited in the pool but not read]** **The causal question is left open:** whether the blows ruptured an already-infected appendix, or the appendicitis was independent and the assault only fixed the date in the public mind, is medically disputed.
- **Funeral 4 November 1926**, more than 2,000 mourners, burial at **Machpelah Cemetery**, Queens, under the S.A.M. crest **[A]**.

**Coordinates.** Eleven pool pins were wrong: Chicago 1899, Cologne, Moscow, 278 W 113th, the D.C. jail, the East River box (wrong water), Circus Busch, the Ambassador, the Shelton, the Princess Theatre, and Machpelah, where the pool's two lenses disagreed with each other. Each is corrected in the journey file with its reason in `date_confidence`; two stay unverified, listed under gaps.

## Afterlife (recorded, not staged in the journey file)

He was dead for all of these. A stop belongs to whoever was there; those touching his grave are `suggested_refs` on the Machpelah stop.

- **The grave** **[A]**: bust smashed or stolen four times between 1975 and 1993, the S.A.M. giving up; replacement installed 27 September 2011 by Dorothy Dietrich, Dick Brookz, and Stephen Moore of the Houdini Museum, Scranton. **Correction:** the pool said this had "permission from Houdini's family and the cemetery"; the source describes an **unannounced** installation, the cemetery's approval coming after the fact.
- **Bess** **[A]**: died 11 February 1943, heart attack, aged 67, buried at **Gate of Heaven**, Westchester, despite wishing to lie beside Harry. **Correction:** the pool's reason, "her family refused to allow her burial in a Jewish cemetery", is **not** in the fetched text, which gives wish and outcome without a cause **[R]**. Also hers: the Arthur Ford "Rosabelle, believe" message of 1929, endorsed then repudiated **[R]**, and the ten Halloween séances ending on the Knickerbocker roof, 31 October 1936.
- **Relics and legends** **[A unless marked]**: the 1995 fire at the Houdini Magical Hall of Fame, **Niagara Falls, Ontario**, not NY as the pool's label had it (Water Torture Cell frame survived, restored by John Gaughan; props auctioned 1999 and 2008); the Radner auction, 2004; the restored *Grim Game* on TCM, 2015; the Kalush and Sloman spy-and-murder thesis, 2006 **[R]**; the 2007 exhumation announcement, publicist-run and never filed; the HRC exhibition, 2026-2027; Houdini Plaza, Appleton, a monument built on the birthplace myth.

## Apparatus relations for the operator

Houdini is **not** listed in `EXCEPTIONS.md`. These relations are not rooted in his own travelled life. They are recorded rather than silently deleted, with the direction caught named, so the operator can decide whether he belongs there.

**Caught FORWARD (not licensable without the author's separate say-so; carried nowhere in the journey file):**

1. **Julius Evola**, *Maschera e volto dello spiritualismo contemporaneo* (1932), dismissing theosophy, spiritualism, and "mediums confusing passivity with attainment" six years after Houdini's death. A posthumous echo. New hash, old block.
2. The afterlife list above, strictly speaking. Other people's journeys, cited only as `suggested_refs` where they touch his grave.

**Caught BACKWARD (licensable if the author adds him).** Houdini's apparatus is the magician's exposure method: any séance-room phenomenon can be reproduced by a professional using ordinary means, and is, on a stage, to prove it. Applied backward it fits four corpus episodes exactly. All four would sit at his own 278 West 113th Street pin, where his library was. None is in the journey file.

3. **Blavatsky's Société Spirite, Cairo**: testing mediums, finding frauds with gauze and wire, writing home that séance-room spooks were cast-off skins. The same discovery from the inside, decades early. (`blavatsky.journey.json`)
4. **Carl Menger, Vienna 1881**: pulling the curtain aside with Hans Canon to expose a medium mid-séance, then publishing at Crown Prince Rudolf's request. Houdini's gesture, forty years early. (`carl_menger.journey.json`, where the stop is itself flagged as reconstruction)
5. **Woodhull and Claflin**, mediums and brokers at 44 Broad Street. (`cornelius_vanderbilt.journey.json`)
6. **Roebling's séance and Swedenborgian library**, Brooklyn Heights; he died in 1869. (`john_a_roebling.journey.json`)

**Excluded as shared geography only:** Pilates at Scotland Yard in 1913 (thirteen years later, no meeting); Waite's London séance years; Le Plongeon's 1871 London meeting; Reuss's 1908 Paris congress; Guénon's *L'Erreur spirite*; Jung's and Preiswerk's Basel and Zurich mediumship material. None of them crossed his path, and the map already shows co-location.

**One genuine relation has no file yet.** **Arthur Conan Doyle** is direct and two-directional: friendship, the séance, the December 1922 letter, the rupture over *A Magician Among the Spirits*. He is named in three campas, being inseparable from the biography. He sits in `QUEUE.md` (lines 211-212) paired with Houdini but has no journey file, so he is **not** returned as an interlock slug; nor is **Jean Conan Doyle**. Doyle's Allahakbarries membership, in `james_matthew_barrie.journey.json`, is Doyle's and Barrie's fact and stays there.

**Negative finding, carried from the pool:** no Kaaba, Temple Mount, or Buenos Aires connection exists in any source reached. No canonical pin was inherited because none applies, and none was fabricated to reach one.
