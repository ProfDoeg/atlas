## 4. Der Judenstaat and the first campaign (1895-1896)

**Hirsch meeting** [CONTR, unresolved]. The dossier says 3 June 1895; de.wikipedia says May
1895; standard scholarship says 2 June 1895, and the verifier states flatly that no fetched
source supports 3 June. The file uses 1895-06-02 **only to order the segment** and lists all
three. The Paris location is the geography lens's inference [R].

**Manuscript completed 17 June 1895** [A: de.wikipedia]. **Der Judenstaat published 14 Feb
1896, Vienna and Leipzig, M. Breitenstein** [A: Project Gutenberg; Wikipedia]. **English
translation, London, May 1896** [A: Wikipedia].

**Argentina weighed against Palestine in the text** [R]: dossier-only per the interlock lens,
**not confirmed here**. No Buenos Aires stop was written, Herzl never having gone there.
**Hechler, 10 March 1896** [A for meeting and date: Wikipedia]; [R] for the embassy's 1896
address and its identity as the site. **Grand Duke Frederick of Baden, Karlsruhe, 1896** [R]:
dossier, palace and date unverified, dated 1896-04-01 purely to order the segment.

**Constantinople, 15-29 June 1896** [A: Wikipedia]. **Framing corrected:** the chronology
claimed Herzl "met Grand Vizier Halil Rifat Pasha instead" of the Sultan. Halil Rifat was Grand
Vizier then, but **no fetched source confirms Herzl met him.** What is attested [A:
en.wikipedia, 'Abdul Hamid II'] is that Herzl's agent Philipp de Newlinski met the Sultan, who
declined: "as long as I am alive, I will not have our body divided; only our corpse they can
divide." The file follows the geography lens, which had this right, and drops Halil Rifat.
**Coordinate corrected:** 41.0136, 28.955 falls near the Grand Bazaar, ~2 km west of the
government quarter; the Sublime Porte is ~41.0119, 28.9790. 1896-06-15 is an arrival date.
**Whitechapel mass meeting, 12 July 1896** [A: Wikipedia]; [R] for the hall (the dossier names
the Jewish Working Men's Club) and its address.

## 5. Basel (1897-1898)

**Munich dropped after the German rabbis' protest, 1897** [A: Wikipedia, 'First Zionist
Congress']; "Protestrabbiner" is dossier-sourced, consistent with that article. **Die Welt

deficits. **First Zionist Congress, Stadtcasino Basel, 29-31 Aug 1897** [A:
Wikipedia]: Basel Program adopted, Zionist Organization constituted, Herzl elected president.
[A, from verification] It met in the **Musiksaal**, and the Steinenberg hall hosted ten Zionist
congresses before 1948, more than any venue anywhere [de.wikipedia, 'Stadtcasino Basel'].

**[CONTR, left standing, operator should rule.]** The verifier corrected the Stadtcasino to
**47.554444, 7.589444**, flagging the pool's 47.557, 7.5906 as ~300 m off. But
`martin_buber.journey.json` and `samuel_preiswerk.journey.json` both pin it at **47.5559,
7.5883**. Per byte-identical inheritance all four Basel stops here use 47.5559, 7.5883, each
confidence field naming the corrected figure. Fixing it is a three-file edit.

**Diary entry of 3 September 1897.** Tagged [R] in the pool; the verifier **lifted it to [A]**
from the primary text: *The Complete Diaries of Theodor Herzl*, ed. Patai, tr. Zohn (Herzl
Press, 1960), vol. II p. 581, heading "September 3. Vienna", read at
archive.org/details/TheCompleteDiariesOfTheodorHerzl_201606. Three corrections. **Spelling:**
Zohn has "Basel", not "Basle"; "Basle" is Lowenthal's access-restricted 1956 abridgment, so
that spelling is cited to nothing. **Framing, the substantive one:** the bare sentence "At
Basel I founded the Jewish State" strips Herzl's own hedge, "which I shall guard against
pronouncing publicly", a load-bearing clause since it marks a private note he explicitly
declined to say aloud. The file quotes the full passage. **Place:** the entry is written **in
Vienna**, three days after the congress closed, so the stop sits at the Vienna pin. Anyone who
has seen the line on a wall in Basel has seen a relocation. **Second Congress, Basel, Aug 1898**
[A: en.wikipedia, 'World Zionist Congress']; day defaulted.
