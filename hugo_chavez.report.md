# Hugo Rafael Chávez Frías (1954-2013): research report

*2026-08-18, for `hugo_chavez.journey.json`. 44 stops, 8 segments, gregorian, register "national mythology: the canon is true".*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named · **[R]** reconstruction or tradition.

Almost the whole life is covered by contemporaneous reporting and by observation missions with published methods, so the [A] to [R] ratio here is far higher than for a pre-modern traveler. The disputes are rarely about what happened; they are about what it *was*. The 2007 broadcasting decision is both a lawful non-renewal of a spectrum licence and the heaviest blow to media pluralism in the country's democratic era. Both readings are recorded, and the campa states the act without the adjective.

---

## 1. Sabaneta and Barinas, 1954-1971

- Born 28 July 1954 in Sabaneta, Barinas, to schoolteachers Hugo de los Reyes Chávez and Elena Frías. **[A: Britannica]**
- The house was his paternal grandmother Rosa Inés Chávez's, three rooms on Calle 10, no. 85 per the 2014 heritage decree. He was born in it and raised in it by her. **[A: en.wikipedia; maduradas.com]** The pool carried this as two geography items implying two buildings; it is one, and the file merges them. Its "one of two sons" is also wrong: second of **seven**, elder brother Adán.
- Secondary school in Barinas city, serious baseball, an intention to pitch professionally. **[R: Marcano and Barrera Tyszka, via the NYRB summary]** The campus identity (Liceo O'Leary) and the years are unconfirmed; the stop says so and 1966-01-01 is a placeholder. The Barinas family house became a second museum by order of July 2022 **[A: El Nacional]**, the birth house a Cultural Asset of the Nation on 16 March 2014 **[A: maduradas.com]**; both are carried inside campas, not as afterlife stops.

**Gap.** Nothing reachable dates the move from the grandmother's house to boarding in Barinas, or says how seven children were divided between two households. The biographies assert the arrangement undated.

## 2. Academy and barracks, 1971-1982

- Entered the Academia Militar 1971, commissioned second lieutenant 1975, later airborne service. **[A: Britannica; Marcano and Barrera Tyszka]** "Eighth in his class" is repeated everywhere but traces to the biographers, not to an academy record seen here: strictly **[R]**.
- Formation in Bolívar, Simón Rodríguez and Ezequiel Zamora as one doctrine is standard in the literature on his ideology. **[R]** The campa states the reading without importing the later slogan.
- Paratroop postings at Maracay from the late 1970s. **[R: FAV-Club; 42nd Paratroop Brigade]** Individual assignment dates are established nowhere reachable; 1978-01-01 orders the segment and the confidence field says only that.

## 3. The oath, 17 December 1982

The most heavily mythologised event in the file, handled as tradition.

- A clandestine cell was founded in December 1982, commemorated on the 17th, the anniversary of Bolívar's death. **[A]** It was named **EBR-200** and redesignated MBR-200 only later; the pool's "MBR-200" at founding is an anachronism. **[A: en.wikipedia]**
- The ceremony under the tree, the adapted Monte Sacro oath, and the named participants (Urdaneta Hernández, Acosta Carles, Baduel) rest on later participant recollection and official commemoration, not on any contemporaneous record. **[R: Fundación Comandante Eterno; PSUV; Venelogía]**
- **Contradiction, flagged, not resolved.** A Venezuelan commentator argues the oath was sworn earlier and less ceremonially in the officers' casino of the Cuartel Páez at Maracay, the tree attached afterwards. **[R: Reeditor.com]** Both versions sit in the stop's `date_confidence`. The file keeps the tree, where the state commemorates, and says the location is disputed.
- **Contradiction inside the pool itself.** The chronology lens tagged this **[R]**, the afterlife lens the identical claim **[A]**. Since the next afterlife item disputes the location, **[R]** is defensible and is what the file uses. Three coordinates for the one tree also appeared; reconciled. The tree was a national monument from 1933, under Juan Vicente Gómez, half a century before any Chavista association **[A]**, which is what the campa carries in place of the pool's "now a declared national monument".

## 4. Caracazo, coup, prison, 1989-1994

- Caracazo, 27 February 1989: riots and a lethal military and police response after an IMF-agreed austerity package. **[A: Corrales and Penfold; Britannica]** The toll is disputed between official hundreds and unofficial thousands; the campa gives both rather than picking one. 4 February 1992: Chávez leads the rising, the capital objectives fail, he is allowed to broadcast and orders the rest to surrender. **[A: Britannica; Not Even Past]**
- **Correction, and it moves a pin.** The pool staged the "por ahora" broadcast at a VTV studio. Contemporary accounts place the *recording* at the Ministry of Defence in Fuerte Tiuna, the tape then broadcast nationally; the VTV building was one of the rebels' assault objectives that morning, the likely source of the confusion. Restaged, competing account in `date_confidence`.
- **Quote correction.** The circulated line drops a word: "no fueron logrados en la **ciudad** capital". **[A: Rebelión, PSUV.org.ve, Qué Pasa]** The pool cited PBS Frontline, which publishes no transcript; citation replaced. The broadcast's second line, a loose English paraphrase in the pool, sits in `suggested_refs` in Spanish rather than as a quotation.
- 27 November 1992: a second uprising by sympathetic officers, Chávez imprisoned and commanding nobody. **[A]** Folded into the Yare stop, since he was not there.
- Release **26 March 1994**: a sobreseimiento granted by Caldera to Chávez and fourteen others, conditioned on Chávez requesting retirement. **[A: Presidencia de Venezuela archive; PSUV; Analítica]** The pool gave only "1994" and made the retirement a bar rather than the condition.

## 5. Havana, ballot, constitution, 1994-2000

- Attested spine: 14 December 1994, first Cuba visit after release, received by Fidel Castro with head-of-state honours at the Aula Magna **[A: CIPI; Presidencia de Cuba]**; 1997, the Movimiento Quinta República formed; 6 December 1998, 56.20 percent and 3,673,685 votes against Salas Römer's 39.97 **[A: Carter Center; official results]**; April 1999, referendum authorising a Constituent Assembly, and 15 December 1999, the constitution approved by 71.8 percent on turnout near 44 **[A: ConstitutionNet]**; 30 July 2000, reelected against Francisco Arias Cárdenas, a 1992 co-conspirator, by about twenty points, observers noting machine problems but concluding the result reflected the vote **[A: Carter Center]**, folded into the OPEC stop.
- 2 February 1999, the "moribunda" oath. **[R as to exact wording]** The text circulates in Venezuelan secondary reproductions (PSUV, TalCual) and was not verified against a primary gazette or audiovisual transcript. Carried as a `quote` with the hedge in `quote_source` and `date_confidence`, where house style puts it and never in the prose.
- That December vote coincided with the Vargas landslides, stated flatly in the campa and not made to mean anything.

## 6. Oil, polarisation, coup, 2000-2004

- **Second OPEC Summit, Caracas, 26-28 September 2000**, first since Algiers 1975, Chávez presiding, closing with the Declaration of Caracas. **[A: OPEC record; Efemérides Venezolanas; Revista Mercado]** The pool opened it on the 27th and staged it at the Poliedro; both sessions were at the **Teatro Teresa Carreño**, and its coordinate was wrong even for the building it named. Restaged, and the [R] venue hedge dropped for the sourced venue.
- Attested spine: 30 October 2000, the Comprehensive Cooperation Convention, oil for medical and technical personnel **[A: agreement text; Cubadebate]**; November 2001, 49 decree-laws under enabling legislation **[A: HRW]**; 11-14 April 2002, brief detention, Carmona proclaimed, gone in about 48 hours, La Orchila the holding place **[A: Carter Center, which calls it a temporary coup; OAS]**; 2003, Barrio Adentro begins **[A: The Lancet]**, its inaugural clinic **[R]**; 15 August 2004, recall survived about 59 to 41, audits endorsing the count against fraud allegations **[A]**.
- July 2002: the State Department Inspector General's review found **no evidence** of Department participation in the overthrow, while documenting American funding of opposition-linked civil society groups. **[A: OIG report via CRS]** A `suggested_ref` on the return stop, not a scene: a Washington document, not a place Chávez went.
- 2 December 2002: the strike begins, PDVSA crippled for weeks. The government calls it sabotage, the opposition a strike to force resignation or early elections. **[A: Reuters; Carter Center]** Both in the campa. By 31 January 2003 roughly 5,300 employees are dismissed **[A: Reuters]**; the larger figure usually cited, about 18,000, is **[R]** here, so the campa says "thousands more follow" without a number.

## 7. High tide, 2004-2010

- 14 December 2004: ALBA founded in Havana with Castro **[A: ALBA-TCP; Granma; Azicri]**. 19 March 2006, Aló Presidente 249: "Eres un donkey, Mr. Danger", in English, inside a volley including *cobarde*, *genocida*, *borracho*. **[A: PSUV.org.ve; Christian Science Monitor]** Upgraded from the pool's [R] Wikiquote sourcing, with the broadcast as citation. The programme travelled, so the stop uses the canonical Caracas pin and says so.
- 20 September 2006, UN General Assembly, the morning after Bush. **[A: Wikisource; Democracy Now; Fox News]** **Quote correction:** the pool's rendering matches no single transcript, blending the Wikisource and Fox translations. The file uses Wikisource verbatim; other verified lines go in `suggested_refs`.
- Attested spine: 3 December 2006, 62.84 percent against Rosales's 36.9, folded into the Orinoco stop; May 2007, the RCTV concession not renewed **[A: HRW]**, both framings in the campa because the pool attests both; 1 May 2007, Orinoco projects to joint ventures at 60 percent state ownership or more, ExxonMobil and ConocoPhillips leaving for arbitration **[A: regulatory record]**, CANTV and electricity in the same stop; 2 December 2007, the reform defeated and the loss publicly accepted **[A: Carter Center]**; 15 February 2009, term limits eliminated **[A: official results]**; 16 July 2010, Bolívar's sarcophagus opened before dawn, no evidence of poisoning found **[A: Time; Skeptical Inquirer]**.
- November 2007: "Fascists are not human. A snake is more human", said of José María Aznar at the Ibero-American Summit, where King Juan Carlos told him to shut up. **[A: Al Jazeera, 12 November 2007]** **Place correction, a real one:** the pool put this in Caracas, tagged [R]. It was said in **Santiago de Chile**. Moved, upgraded, target named. **Gap:** the day within the summit is unconfirmed; `date_confidence` says so.

## 8. Illness and death, 2011-2013

- Attested spine: 30 June 2011, a cancerous pelvic tumour removed in Havana, chemotherapy from July, cancer-free declared in October **[A]**, the surgery date approximated from the announcement; 7 October 2012, about 55 percent against Capriles **[A: Carter Center]**; 8 December 2012, the televised designation of Maduro **[A: Bloomberg]**; 11 December 2012, fourth operation, respiratory complications, absent from the 10 January inauguration, the Supreme Tribunal ruling of 9 January folded into the same stop **[A, known only through press reporting]**; 18 February 2013, returned to the military hospital, never seen in public again **[A]**.
- **26 February 2012**, not the 28th, is the second operation: lesion announced 21 February, a roughly 2 cm recurrence removed on the 26th, radiation begun after his return to Cuba on 24 March. **[A: es.wikipedia cronología; La Nación; El Colombiano]**
- 5 March 2013, about 4:25 p.m.: death announced by Maduro. **[A: Britannica; Reuters; AP]** No histopathological diagnosis was ever disclosed **[A]**, and the campa states that absence, the load-bearing fact behind every later claim about the illness. The 14 April 2013 succession election, Maduro over Capriles by roughly 50.6 to 49.1 and disputed **[A: Carter Center]**, gets no stop: Chávez was dead, and it is Maduro's event.
- **Attribution correction on the last words.** "I don't want to die. Please don't let me die." The pool credited the report to Maduro and Information Minister Ernesto Villegas. It was **General José Ornella**, head of the presidential honour guard, to the Associated Press outside the military academy; Ornella said Chávez could not speak and mouthed the words. **[A as to who reported it: AP via Fox News; MercoPress and Al Jazeera, 7 March 2013; PRI]** They remain a single secondhand account of a private, unrecorded moment, framed that way in both campa and `quote_source`.

## 9. Afterlife

- The embalming plan, announced days after the death and abandoned around 15 March 2013: a pathologist advised preservation had to begin within about eight hours of death, a Russian commission estimated seven or eight months of work in Russia, and three formaldehyde treatments were applied for the wake instead. **[A: Infobae; Cuatro.com]**
- **Interment corrected.** The remains went to the Cuartel de la Montaña on **15 March 2013**, when the Mausoleo "Flor de los Cuatro Elementos", sarcophagus by Fruto Vivas, was inaugurated; the pool said "2013-07 onward". **[A: es.wikipedia]** Lying in state extends accordingly: funeral 8 March, body at the academy until the 15th. Daily cannon at 4:25 p.m., honour guard, pilgrimage within a month **[A: Reuters]**.
- July 2024: statues toppled in at least five states after the disputed 28 July election, the La Guaira figure dedicated by Maduro in 2017 dragged behind motorcycles and burned, across roughly 187 demonstrations in 20 of 23 states; smaller attacks in 2017 and 2019. **[A: PBS NewsHour; Infobae]**
- 3 January 2026: during the U.S. military operation that ended with Maduro's capture, viral posts and some Venezuelan outlets claimed the mausoleum had been struck **[A that the claim circulated: Univision]**; Chequeado, EFE Verifica and Newtral found no evidence, satellite imagery showing the structure intact **[A: Chequeado; CiberCuba]**. Both halves are in the campa. Newest material here, and the reporting is still moving.
- The "pajarito" legend, Maduro's account of a small bird in a Barinas chapel in 2013 and 2014. **[R: Infobae; Libertad Digital]** **Not staged:** Maduro's experience in Maduro's chapel, which under the standing rule belongs on his file if he ever gets one. Separately, Chávez's own body of writing is **mediated**, the long books under his name being interview-books by Harnecker (2002) and Ramonet (2013). **[A: WorldCat]**

## 10. Interlocks

Named in campa, Chávez having a real relation to each:

- **`bolivar`**, four stops. The project is named after him, the 1982 oath is modelled on the Monte Sacro oath, the republic was renamed Bolivarian, and the sarcophagus was opened in 2010 on Chávez's own theory that the Liberator was murdered. One-directional across two centuries, which the standing rule accepts.
- **`fidel_castro`**, four stops. Met December 1994, allied from 1999, co-signatories of the 2000 convention and the 2004 ALBA declaration; Castro's Granma Reflexión supplies the lying-in-state quote.
- **`george_w_bush`**, two stops. Addressed by nickname on Aló Presidente 249, and answered from the same UN rostrum the morning after Bush spoke from it. His file carries both scenes from the other side and the pins match.

**Deliberately not named**, shared geography and nothing more, which the map already shows: `che_guevara`, `juan_peron`, `isabel_peron`, `humboldt`, `aime_bonpland`, `manuela_saenz`, `sucre`, `christian_dior`, `jair_bolsonaro` (the pool's "contemporary foil", a writer's convenience rather than a relation).

**`cristina_fernandez_de_kirchner`** is the hard case, left out on purpose. A relation exists, they were allies and met; but the only thing the pool attests is her vigil in Caracas on 5-7 March 2013, and **that is her stop, on her file, already there**. From this side the form would be a `suggested_ref`, not a scene.

**Canonical pins inherited byte-identical**: Caracas 10.4806, -66.9036 (the two stops with no verified building) and the UN 40.7489, -73.968. Kaaba and Temple Mount are inheritance-only anchors, unused here.

## 11. Apparatus relations for the operator

Chávez is **not** on the list in `EXCEPTIONS.md`, and nothing in the pool needed it. Recorded anyway, so nothing is deleted silently:

1. **Bolívar read through Chávez** looks like an apparatus relation and is not one. It runs **backward** (a 2013 file embedding an 1830 hash, the legal direction) and is rooted in Chávez's own life: he swore the oath, renamed the state, opened the tomb. No exception needed, none claimed, nothing written into `bolivar.journey.json`.
2. **The 2026 mausoleum claim** postdates the subject by thirteen years but is not a framework applied forward: it is an event at the monument holding his body, and posthumous events at a subject's own tomb are already stops at that subject's pin. Flagged because an operator scanning dates will notice it.
3. **Nothing forward-facing was found or written.** He would be a plausible candidate for the backward list, Bolivarianism being precisely a framework its holder applied retrospectively to events he did not witness; not done here, and it would need the author's say-so.

## 12. Sources

**Reachable and used.** Named per claim in sections 1 to 11. Four families carry most of the weight: election observation (Carter Center, OAS); wire and international press (Reuters, AP, Bloomberg, The Guardian, Al Jazeera, PBS, Infobae, Univision); Venezuelan and Cuban official archives (Presidencia de Venezuela, PSUV, ALBA-TCP, Granma, Cubadebate, CIPI); and reference or verification work (Britannica, ConstitutionNet, The Lancet, HRW, Wikisource, es. and en.wikipedia, Chequeado, EFE Verifica, Newtral). OpenStreetMap and gazetteers supplied every corrected coordinate. The `george_w_bush`, `fidel_castro` and `bolivar` journey files were read for pin consistency.

**Named and not reached.** **PBS Frontline, "The Hugo Chávez Show"**, cited by the pool as the source of the 1992 transcript: the site exists, discusses the broadcast, and publishes no transcript, so it is a bad citation rather than an inaccessible source. **Marcano and Barrera Tyszka's biography**, not read directly, reaching this file through the NYRB summary, and carrying most of the childhood material. **The Supreme Tribunal ruling of 9 January 2013**, known only through press reporting. **The 1999 inaugural oath in a primary transcript**, sought and not found, carried as [R]. **A protocol list for the 8 March 2013 funeral**, the "thirty-two heads of state" figure coming from press aggregation. And **any histopathological record of the illness**, never published: not an access failure at this end but a gap in the world, and the reason the death stop states an absence rather than a diagnosis.

**Coordinates.** Ten pins were wrong in the pool, several of them repeated across lenses and two of them internally contradictory (the Samán, the Cuartel de la Montaña). All are corrected in the journey file; the largest errors were Sabaneta at 99 km, Yare at 23 km, CIMEQ at 4.3 km and the Academia Militar at 2.6 km.

**Quote density.** Seven of forty-four stops carry a `quote`, below the third house style suggests. The pool holds quotable material for eight occasions in a fourteen-year presidency conducted on live television; its remaining quotations were misattributed, blended from two translations, or unverified. Raising the density means another pass on the Aló Presidente archive.
