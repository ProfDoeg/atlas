# Yoshua Bengio (1964- ): research report
*Compiled 2026-08-27 for `yoshua_bengio.journey.json`. Subject living; the file ends open.*

Legend: **[A]** = attested, source named · **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition, or a claim
carried from the working dossier that no reachable source confirms. Corrections to tags and
coordinates are recorded rather than quietly applied; contradictions are left standing.

The file holds **44 stops in 9 segments**, Paris 1964 to Mile-Ex 2026. Most sit inside about
eight kilometres of Montreal, which is not a defect of the research but the shape of the
life.

## 1. Paris and Casablanca (1964-1977)

- Born **5 March 1964 in Paris** to Carlo Bengio and Celia Moreno, students, both from
  Moroccan Jewish families **[A, Wikipedia; ACM Turing Award biography]**. The father
  finishing a pharmacy internship and CNRS work, the mother reading law and economics
  **[A, ACM biography]**. **The hospital is unrecorded**; the pin is the canonical Paris
  coordinate 48.8566 / 2.3522.
- **Celia Moreno's own street**, rue Abderrahmane Sahraoui (formerly rue Chevandier de
  Valdrome), Casablanca **[R, mazagan24.com (2020), not refetched]**. The pool tagged this
  [A]; generous for a single unrefetched profile, so it is downgraded. **The pool's
  coordinate (33.5975 / -7.6187) is ~500 m north of the street**; Nominatim gives 33.5931 /
  -7.6203. *No stop was written there*: Bengio was not born yet. It sits in the Casablanca
  stop's `suggested_refs`, where a parent's address belongs.
- **The return to Casablanca during the father's military service.** The pool contradicts
  itself: Wikipedia says "a year," timing unspecified; the chronology says "roughly a year,"
  1971; the geography says "one to two years," 1971-1973, tagged [A]. **Flagged, not
  resolved.** The file takes the minimum: **[R]**, one year, 1971-01-01 ordering the segment.
- **Settlement in Montreal at twelve, 1977 [R]**: Wikipedia confirms a childhood
  relocation without a year; 1977 reaches us through the ACM account via the dossier.
- **L'Ecran humain**, Celia Moreno's multimedia troupe with Paul St-Jean, whose eight and
  later twenty-four projectors ran on software the two Bengio brothers wrote **[A, ACM
  biography; mazagan24.com]**. **Gap:** no venue, address or date. Pinned to a central
  Montreal placeholder, dated 1980 only to order the segment, both stated in the file.
  **Samy Bengio**, the younger brother, is later a machine-learning researcher **[A]**.
