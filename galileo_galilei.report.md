# Galileo Galilei (1564-1642), research report

*2026-08-29, for `galileo_galilei.journey.json`. Register: national mythology: the canon is true. Calendar **gregorian**: his life straddles the 1582 reform, adopted at once in Tuscany, and the pre-1582 dates are birth and childhood only.*

**Legend. [A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction or tradition. Contradictions are flagged, not resolved. Where this pass corrected the dossier, the old value is recorded.

---

## 1. Pisa and Vallombrosa (1564-1578)

- Born **15 Feb 1564 at Pisa**, eldest of six children of the lutenist **Vincenzo Galilei** and **Giulia Ammannati**; baptized **19 Feb**. **[A: Museo Galileo chronology]** Baptistery pin corrected: dossier 10.3925, about 110 m too far west, against 43.72333/10.39389.
- **The birth house is not settled and the dossier got it wrong.** It gave the **Chiasso dei Mercanti**, 43.7168/10.4023; Italian Wikipedia gives the *casa Ammannati* near **Sant'Andrea Forisportam**, identified only in the late nineteenth century, with the baptismal register putting the family "in Chapella di S.to Andrea", about 300 m east. **Unresolved.** The file pins Sant'Andrea (43.7167/10.4058), keeping the Chiasso as named tradition. **[R for the address]**
- **Move to Florence**, Galileo left in Pisa with a relative and joining later. **Year contradictory: MacTutor 1572, Museo Galileo 1574**; 1574 used. **[A for the move]**
- **Vallombrosa**, schooled by the monks **[A: English Wikipedia, "education: 1575-1578"]**. **The dossier dated this 1580, wrong by several years**, colliding with the 5 Sept 1580 matriculation. The **novitiate** and the **eye inflammation** given as his father's pretext for removing him rest on **Viviani** (1654). **[R]** Pin corrected: dossier 43.728/11.5442, about 1.1 km off, against 43.73194/11.55778. **MacTutor calls the house Camaldolese; it is Vallumbrosan.**

## 2. Student, then unemployed (1580-1588)

- **Matriculates in Arts at Pisa 5 Sept 1580** for medicine; **leaves with no degree, 1585. [A]**
- **Ricci's Euclid.** MacTutor: Ricci taught Euclid at **the University of Pisa in 1582-83** with Galileo attending, meeting him again at Florence in summer 1583. **[A]** **The dossier's geography lens put this at the Florentine court (43.7663/11.2483), the wrong city.** Corrected to Pisa (43.7172/10.3999).
- **The cathedral lamp.** Stated under 1583 by Museo Galileo and MacTutor, but nothing in Galileo's hand from that year records it: his earliest statement of isochronism is the **1602 letter to Guidobaldo del Monte**, and isochronism holds only for small arcs. **[R]**
- **Siena 1585-86**, private pupils and a public post, ***La bilancetta* 1586**, first printed **1644**; **Rome 1587**, visiting **Clavius**. **[A]** Pin corrected: the dossier used 41.8983/12.4823 at **all three** Collegio Romano stops, about 170 m east of the building (41.8980861/12.4802611).
- **Dante lectures at the Accademia Fiorentina, 1588 [A: MacTutor]**, where **the dossier's two lenses disagreed** (chronology 1588, geography 1587-1588).

## 3. The chair at Pisa, and the tower (1589-1592)

- **Inaugural address 12 Nov 1589**, teaching from the fourteenth, at a salary far below philosophy **[A]**. **Vincenzo Galilei dies 1591 [A: MacTutor, year only; no day in any reachable source]**, kept in prose rather than given a stop.
- **The Leaning Tower.** The public drop rests **solely on Viviani**, decades later, from what an old blind man told an assistant. Galileo's own writings never mention the tower; where he describes cannonball beside musket ball he reports the heavier arriving slightly ahead. **[R: physicsworld.com]**

## 4. Padua, the eighteen years (1592-1610)

- **Senate appoints him 26 Sept 1592 [A]**, with the **first lecture given as 7 Dec by MacTutor and teaching from 13 Dec by Museo Galileo**; both recorded, 7 Dec used.
- **The chair Bruno wanted.** `giordano_bruno.journey.json`: the chair Bruno sought and was refused went to a thirty-year-old from Pisa. Succession over one office, not shared geography, so it is named in the campa. **Campanella at Padua 1593**, per `tommaso_campanella.journey.json`: named, but the scene stays on his file. **[A]**
- **1594 patent; compass 1597** by **Mazzoleni**, sold with paid instruction; **first letter to Kepler 1597**; ***Le operazioni del compasso* 1606**, first signed book. **[A]** **The Arsenal** is a standing relationship with no dated visit, so it sits inside the patent stop rather than getting a pin.
- **Children by Marina Gamba** at San Lorenzo, Padua: **Virginia 13 Aug 1600, Livia 18 Aug 1601, Vincenzo 21 Aug 1606. [A]** The pin is the historic parish site, the period footprint approximate. **[gap]**
- **The Capra affair. The dossier said the *Difesa* appeared "the following year". Wrong.** English Wikipedia: Capra's tract, the rectors' judgment, and Galileo's ***Difesa... contro Baldessar Capra* (Venice, 1607)** all fall in **1607**. **[A]**

## 5. The telescope and the move to Florence (1609-1610)

- **Aug 1609**, demonstration from the Campanile, tenure and raised salary following; power given as **eight or nine**, MacTutor dating the spyglass letter to May 1609 **[discrepancy standing]**. **7-13 Jan 1610**, four moving points near Jupiter **[A]**; the Padua house address is in neither fetched source and the pin approximates the university quarter. **[gap]**
- ***Sidereus nuncius***, Venice, about 550 copies. **Three sources, three dates: Museo Galileo 12 March, Wikipedia 13 March, MacTutor May.** 13 March used, all recorded.
- **The move to Florence was mis-dated in the dossier**, which collapsed three events into "June 1610, Florence". MacTutor: **resigned Padua June, contract July, arrived September**. Corrected and split. **[A]** **MacTutor's "Chief Mathematician at the University of Pisa" is misleading**: no teaching duty attached, the court title being Philosopher and Chief Mathematician to the Grand Duke.
- **Phases of Venus**, autumn 1610: fatal to Ptolemy, **still compatible with the Tychonic arrangement**, a qualification the file carries unsoftened. **[A]**

## 6. Rome confirms, Rome warns (1611-1616)

- **Spring 1611**, Collegio Romano mathematicians confirm the observations; **Bellarmine's written query to them and their affirmative reply** are attested and become material in 1616. **[A]**
- **Lincei, 25 April 1611 [A]**, with the **coordinate wrong by about 1.2 km** (dossier 41.9036/12.465, apparently a transposition of 41.8936). Palazzo Corsini is the Academy's modern seat; in 1611 it worked from **Cesi's palace in Borgo, c. 41.9018/12.4620**, which the file uses. **"Telescopio", coined by Demisiani** at a banquet that spring, is well attested but **the venue is not**, so that stop is dropped and the coining folded into the admission stop. **[R for any venue]**
- ***Istoria... macchie solari*, March 1613**, Lincean imprint, **Scheiner** having seen sunspots independently; no printing-house address is reachable, so the stop sits at the Lincean establishment that financed it. **Letter to Castelli, 21 Dec 1613**: Galileo was not at the dinner that prompted it, Castelli was. **Letter to Christina, 1615**, manuscript only, **first printed 1636**. **[A]**
- **Caccini at Santa Maria Novella. Museo Galileo gives 21 Dec 1614** against the geography lens's "20-21"; 21 Dec used. **Pin corrected**: dossier 43.7766/11.2496, about 220 m north, against **43.7746/11.2493**. Galileo was absent, and per the standing rule the stop records the pulpit the accusation came from, not a scene he witnessed.
- **The 1616 sequence was dated one day late in the dossier**, which put the qualifiers' report on 25 Feb and had the Pope act "the next day". Correct order: **19 Feb** referral, **24 Feb** report, **25 Feb** Paul V instructs Bellarmine, **26 Feb** admonition. **[A]**
- **The admonition venue is not established**: it took place at **Bellarmine's own residence**, not the Collegio Romano, and the file keeps the Jesuit-quarter pin with the corrected coordinate and says so. **[R]** **The two 1616 documents do not say the same thing**: the unsigned minute ("not to hold, teach, or defend in any way whatsoever") against Bellarmine's certificate of 26 May 1616 (holding and defending only). That divergence is the hinge of the 1633 prosecution and is left open. **[A for both]**
- **Index decree, 5 March 1616**: *De revolutionibus* suspended pending correction, Foscarini prohibited, Galileo unnamed. **[A]**

## 7. The Assayer, the Dialogue, the trial (1618-1633)

- **The comets of 1618.** Grassi placed them beyond the moon by parallax, **correctly**; Galileo, through Guiducci's *Discorso delle comete* which he largely wrote, argued they were optical effects of terrestrial vapours and **was wrong**. Stated flat. ***Il Saggiatore* 1623**, dedicated to the newly elected Urban VIII. **[A]**
- **Six papal audiences, 1624**, the dossier's basis being a Fantoli/Finocchiaro synthesis; Museo Galileo independently dates an audience with a microscope demonstration to **10 May 1624**, used to order the stop. The chamber is **[gap]**.
- ***Dialogo*, Florence, 22 Feb 1632 [A]**; the licensing history is attested in outline, the exact terms at each stage contested and **not adjudicated here**. **Summons 1 Oct 1632 [A]**; arrival at Palazzo Firenze Feb 1633, the reported threat to fetch him in irons standard in the biographies but **not re-verified against the correspondence [A/R]**.
- **12 April 1633**, first interrogation, lodged inside the Holy Office in an official's rooms, not a cell. **21 June 1633**, formal threat of rigorous examination; **no record shows torture was administered**, and this is folded into the abjuration stop. **[A]**
- **22 June 1633**, Santa Maria sopra Minerva: vehemently suspected of heresy, abjuration, *Dialogue* prohibited, imprisonment commuted, weekly penitential psalms for three years. **[A: Museo Galileo]** **The Minerva as venue is standard identification and is not named in the fetched sentence document**; caveat kept. Pin **inherited byte-identical from `tommaso_campanella.journey.json`: 41.8986 / 12.4783.**
- **Villa Medici, then Siena under Piccolomini, July to December 1633**, the Villa Medici stay described inside the Siena stop rather than pinned; **return to Arcetri 1 Dec 1633. [A]**

## 8. Arcetri (1633-1642) and afterlife

- **Villa Il Gioiello's coordinate was wrong in all three lenses**: 43.7519/11.2461 in the chronology and geography, about 1.1 km off and repeated at four stops, and 43.75917/11.2425 in the afterlife, about 1.9 km off and the dossier's worst positional error. Italian Wikipedia: **43.7476/11.2576**. Corrected everywhere.
- **Suor Maria Celeste dies 2 April 1634 [A]**; the 124 surviving letters, and the loss of his own side, come through the **Rice Galileo Project**, named in the dossier as its basis but **not re-fetched [A/R]**.
- **Blindness** complete by 1638, **cause undetermined**; the **Florentine inquisitor's Feb 1638 report** calls him "more a cadaver than a living person". **Feb 1638**, a house on the Costa San Giorgio permitted after an earlier request had drawn a threat of imprisonment. ***Discorsi***, Elzevir, Leiden, July 1638 **[A]**; route and carriers **not established [gap]**. **Viviani joins 1639, Torricelli 1641 [A]**, the pendulum escapement drawn and **not built as a working clock in his lifetime**. **Milton's visit** is reported **only by Milton**, in *Areopagitica* (1644), uncorroborated in Galileo's correspondence. **[R]**
- **Dies 8 Jan 1642**, aged 77 **[A for the date]**. **The hour, the fever and palpitations, and the presence of Viviani and Torricelli are all Viviani's testimony**, twelve years later, attributed as such rather than reported as record. **[R]** **Buried 9 Jan 1642** at Santa Croce, Rome having blocked the Medici monument; English Wikipedia describes the grave as "an unconsecrated box underneath the Santa Croce bell tower". **Reburial 12 March 1737** from funds **Viviani** left for it, his own remains moved into the new grave. **[A]**
- **The relics: the dossier's verify pass over-corrected, and is itself corrected here.** Museo Galileo names **three fingers and a tooth** (middle finger, Inv. 2432) because those are what it holds; English Wikipedia names **five items**, adding the **fifth lumbar vertebra**, at Padua since 1823. The Museo's silence is a **scope limit, not a contradiction**, and the vertebra is restored as fact. **Maria Celeste's supposed reinterment beside him is NOT supported by either source reached**, though both lenses asserted it. **Dropped from the campa, flagged in `date_confidence`. [R]**
- **"Eppur si muove."** First known print appearance **Baretti, *The Italian Library* (London, 1757)**, 124 years after the abjuration, absent from the trial record, the correspondence, and Viviani; the 1643/45 Van Belle painting produced in 1911 as earlier evidence is now doubted. **[R]** **It is never rendered as spoken words anywhere in the file**: once in the abjuration stop's `date_confidence` with the Baretti fact attached, once in `suggested_refs`.

## Quote verification

Seven of forty-five stops carry a quote, all verbatim from the pool. Four are clean: the sentence of 22 June 1633; the 1610 letter to Kepler; the *Sidereus Nuncius* passage on Jupiter (Carlos 1880 p. 47, and it does say *three*, recording the nights before the fourth appeared); and the inquisitor's "more a cadaver than a living person", a quotation inside a secondary source and attributed as such **[A/R]**. Three needed intervention:

- **"...absurd, philosophically false, and formally heretical." Attribution corrected.** Verbatim at sourcebooks.fordham.edu, but standing there inside *The Crime of Galileo: Indictment and Abjuration of 1633*, i.e. **the 1633 indictment reciting the Qualifiers' censure**. Fordham carries no separate 24 Feb 1616 report, and that report is conventionally translated "foolish and absurd in philosophy, and formally heretical", **not this wording**, so citing 1616 as its source would be false.
- **"...how one goes to heaven, not how heaven goes." Note corrected.** Galileo never names Baronio in the *Letter to Christina*, introducing the line as heard "from an ecclesiastic of the most eminent degree"; the Baronio identification is later editorial attribution, and `quote_source` says so.
- **"Philosophy is written in this grand book...", *Il Saggiatore*, Drake pp. 237-8.** House style forbids the em dash and Drake has one where a comma stands in the `quote` field: a **deliberate, disclosed** deviation, recorded in `quote_source`, and the only such change in the file.

Verified but unused: the two abjuration lines and the "divine Word" line from the *Letter to Christina*, one quote per stop being the limit.

3. **The 1758 lifting of the Index's blanket ban, the 1820 licensing dispute, the 1835 Index edition, and John Paul II's 1979-1992 reconsideration: FORWARD.** All attested (English Wikipedia, "Galileo affair"), all concerning his condemnation rather than his life.
