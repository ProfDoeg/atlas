# Carl Menger (1840-1921), research report

*Compiled 2026-08-25 for `carl_menger.journey.json`. Galician lawyer's son; Vienna journalist and civil servant; author of the* Grundsätze *(1871); tutor to Crown Prince Rudolf; founder of the Austrian School.*

**Legend.** **[A]** attested, source named inline. **[R]** reconstruction or tradition. **[C]** sources contradict; preserved, not adjudicated.

The pool had already been through an adversarial pass and several claims came back CORRECTED. Those corrections are carried in full, with the direction of the error stated, because several invert the original meaning rather than sharpening it.

---

## 1. Origin, family, name (1840-1859)

- **[A]** Born 23 February 1840 at Neu-Sandez (Nowy Sącz), Galicia, to the lawyer Anton Menger and Caroline Geržabek. *University of Vienna faculty history; Deutsche Biographie, gnd118580841.*
- **[C] The birth date is contested.** Two independent institutional records give 23 February; the Mises biography prints **28 February**, widely reprinted. No parish register was consulted; **not resolved**. The journey carries 23 February, with the dispute in `date_confidence`. Not cosmetic: on the 28 February date his death on 26 February 1921 falls *before* his eighty-first birthday, not after it.
- **[R]** "Around noon" as the hour of birth is in neither institutional source; dropped. **Coordinate corrected** to **49.6217, 20.6975** (the Rynek); the pool's figure fell about 700 m south.
- **[A]** The family bore "von Wolfensgrün", and Carl and his brothers laid it down as a profession of bourgeois conviction (German Wikipedia), closing part of a gap the pool flagged. **[R]** Its dating to 1865 and the origin of the ennoblement remain unsourced.
- **[R]** Childhood at Biała, school from 1843, the father's law office; the Geržabek land at Maniowy; the 1846 story of tenants standing round the house; Gymnasium at Teschen 1851-57, Troppau 1857-58, final year at Kraków. All family-archival reconstruction, chiefly Karl Menger's draft biography via the Duke papers scholarship. **Maniowy coordinate corrected** by 5.5 km to 49.4597, 20.2692; the village chronology is dam resolution 1964, resettlement through the 1970s-80s, inundation 1995.
- **Gap.** No document from his first nineteen years was reached. Everything before 1859 is family memory.

## 2. Universities and newspapers (1859-1867)

- **[A]** Law and state sciences at Vienna and Prague, 1859-1863 (*Mises*). The Prague detail (Brinz on Roman law, Schulte on canon law, the first state examination cum laude) rests on Britannica, which **returned HTTP 403**; all of it is weakened, and the named Vienna lecturers Arndts and Siegel are **dropped from the prose**.
- **[A]** The Vienna building attribution is source-backed, not inferred: the university did not move to the Ring until 1884. **Coordinate corrected** by 170 m to **48.2088, 16.3774**.
- **[R]** The Mensur, the fraternity, the scar on the nose, no venue named anywhere reached; kept inside the Karolinum stop, and the pool's separate "dueling ground" pin, a stand-in for an undocumented site, is dropped. **[R]** The Tagesbote aus Böhmen and the Botschafter / Die Presse work. **[A]** Journalism in Lviv; **[R]** the title *Lemberger Zeitung* and the editorial rank.
- **[A, correcting the source]** Met Minister-President Richard Belcredi in 1865 (*Mises*), but **Belcredi was a Conservative, not a Liberal.** Mises says "the Liberal Austrian prime minister"; English Wikipedia gives "a declared Conservative" who replaced Schmerling's Liberal government. The pool copied the error faithfully; the journey states the correction inside the campa rather than quietly fixing it.
- **[A]** Co-editor of a newly founded Vienna daily, 1865-1866 (German Wikipedia), which **upgrades** what the pool held at [R]. **[C] The paper's name varies** across *Wiener Tagblatt*, *Neues Wiener Tagesblatt* and *Neues Wiener Tagblatt*; not resolved, the journey uses the last. **[R]** All the ornaments: Lackenbacher as partner, the one-kreuzer price, circulation about 35,000, the market-halls and prison-reform programme, the February 1866 sale, the 26 November 1865 founding date.
- **[A, chronology corrected]** The Wiener Zeitung economic section **ends** in autumn 1866; it does not begin then. Mises says of 1866 that he "departed from this official newspaper to prepare for doctoral examination." The pool's stop dated "1866 and 1871-1875" conflated two jobs, the 1871-75 market work belonging to the press office. Corrected by splitting them.

## 3. Doctorate, civil service, book (1867-1873)

- **[A]** Dr. iur., Jagiellonian University, Kraków, 1867; month unknown, defaulted to January. **[R]** The quarrel with the Prague dean Schneider said to have kept him from Prague's oral doctorate rests on an undated polemical manuscript; not asserted.
- **[A]** Apprentice legal practice from May 1867; intensive study of economics from September 1867; cabinet press department 1870 (*Mises*). The systematic market work belongs to that post, from about 1870/71: "systematischen Beobachtung und Analyse der Marktlage" at the Ministerratspräsidium (German Wikipedia).
- **[R]** That the price columns stimulated the value theory: Menger's own later report and his students', a famous causal story rather than an independent record. The journey states the discrepancy without asserting the causation.
- **[A]** *Grundsätze der Volkswirthschaftslehre*, Vienna: Wilhelm Braumüller, 1871. He refused reprints and translations in his lifetime, intending a revised system that never appeared; first English translation **1950**.
- **[R]** Jevons (1871) and Walras (1874) as simultaneous discoverers: universally reported, but **no source in the verified pool carries it**, so it is omitted from the journey rather than smuggled in.
- **[A]** Habilitation as Privatdozent 1872; associate professor 1873, Mises giving September. **[R]** The failed trial lecture, Stein's resistance, the credit-and-banking course with about forty students, the declined posts at Wiener Neustadt, Karlsruhe and Basel.

## 4. The Crown Prince (1876-1889)

- **[A]** Appointed tutor to Crown Prince Rudolf 1876; both the University of Vienna and the Deutsche Biographie give **only the year**. **[R]** The day-level date 3 January 1876 is in neither, and the Hofburg as lesson venue is the pool's acknowledged inference.
- **[A, factual correction]** Rudolf's age: the pool called him eighteen in January 1876, but born 21 August 1858 he was **seventeen**. Fixed.
- **[R]** The June 1876 examination before Franz Joseph; the roughly three hundred dinners; the Order of the Iron Crown Third Class and Menger's private mockery of it.
- **[A]** The court's annual months at Gödöllő 1867-1918. **Coordinate corrected** by 650 m to **47.59611, 19.34778**. Menger's own presence there stays **[R]**.
- **[A]** European and British study travel with Rudolf, 1877/1878; the library reached about 25,000 volumes by his own 1911 estimate. **[R]** The Paris-Calais-London itinerary in detail, and the Quaritch bookshop visit, kept as reconstruction nested inside an attested trip and said to be so.
- **[R]** The 1878 pamphlet *Der österreichische Adel und sein constitutioneller Beruf*, pseudonym "Friedrich Saalfelden", both authors abroad at publication. **[C] The authorship share is contested** between Brigitte Hamann and Karl Menger; the campa says the argument was still running a century later and picks no winner.

## 5. The chair and the Methodenstreit (1879-1888)

- **[A]** Full professor February 1879. **[R]** The declined calls to Karlsruhe, Basel and Zürich.
- **Coordinate note.** Faculty stops from 1879 use the Ringstraße building, 48.2136, 16.3597, which did not open until 1884; the pin anticipates the longer tenure, as `date_confidence` says.
- **[A, corrected]** The deanships are **academic years, not calendar years**: "1881/82: Served as Dean, Faculty of Law and State" and "1888/89: Served as Dean". The pool recorded 1881 and 1888; the journey dates them October 1881 and October 1888 and says why.
- **[R]** The 1881 séance, Menger and the painter Hans Canon pulling the curtain aside, and the published notice at Rudolf's request.
- **[A]** *Untersuchungen über die Methode der Socialwissenschaften*, 1883, and *Die Irrthümer des Historismus in der deutschen Nationalökonomie*, addressed to Schmoller, 1884. **[R]** That Schmoller returned the copy unread, and that the quarrel cost Menger the German universities for a generation: standard account, not in the pool.
- **[A]** *Zur Theorie des Kapitals*, 1888, disputing Böhm-Bawerk's definition of capital. **[R]** Böhm-Bawerk's three finance ministries and his theory of interest: general record, not in the pool.

## 6. Money, the state, and the honors (1889-1911)

- **[A]** Rudolf died at Mayerling 30 January 1889, public historical record. **[R]** Menger's reaction, the phrase about an ingenious man and a heavy loss, and the private lament about years that had not advanced his own work. **The stop is placed in Vienna, not at Mayerling**: Menger was not there.
- **[A]** "On the Origin of Money", *Economic Journal*, 1892, trans. Caroline A. Foley (*Econlib*). The article was **not fetched**; the quotation at that stop comes from the corresponding chapter of the 1871 book, and `quote_source` says so.
- **[A]** Testified before the Austro-Hungarian currency commission in 1892. **[R, demoted]** The span 8-17 March 1892 appears in no readable source; Britannica, cited for it, returned 403. The hearing room is an inferred Vienna pin.
- **[A]** Hofrat 1896; honorary doctorates Budapest 1896 and Prague 1899. Whether he travelled to either city is **not recorded anywhere reached**, so both sit at his Vienna pin rather than being staged abroad.
- **[R]** Meran from 1880 and the Austrian Fishing Society; the journey's June 1893 is a placeholder for a habit, not a visit.
- **[A]** Life member of the Herrenhaus 1900. **[R]** The Constitutional Party group, the independent listing by 1907, the nominal participation.
- **[A]** Karl Menger born 13 January 1902, Vienna; died 5 October 1985, Highland Park, Illinois (English Wikipedia, which names him "the son of the economist Carl Menger"). This closes a gap the pool flagged as needing the Duke finding aid.
- **[R] The mother's name is not given in any source reached.** Hermina Andermann appears only as the person the **library** was bequeathed to, and the journey names no mother. **[R]** The mathematical achievements (dimension theory, the Menger sponge); the April 1911 rescript of legitimation; the joining of the households by about 1912/13.

## 7. The end (1912-1921)

- **[R]** Menger burned two cartons of correspondence c. 1912, telling his son he was burning his own biography. A striking claim resting on a **single family report**, Karl Menger's, who was ten. Kept as the most-quoted anecdote about him, flagged for what it rests on.
- **[A, corrected] The Duke papers.** The pool said 28 boxes described in a finding aid by Mason Barnett; **both halves are wrong.** hope.econ.duke.edu/node/1657 lists Barnett as author of an *article* at pp. 15-28, so the "28" is a page range, and the Duke finding aid returned a **bot-verification block**. No box count is stated in the journey.
- **[A, corrected]** Honorary doctorate Dr. rer. pol. h.c., academic year 1919/20. **The superlative is dropped**: the pool claimed the NDB calls him "First Doctor of Political Science" at Vienna in 1920, and the NDB page fetched contains no such phrase.
- **[R]** The uremic illness, the withdrawal among the books, the revision of *Geld*, the interleaved annotated copies of the *Grundsätze*; the institutional sources confirm only the death date and city. **[A]** The refusal of reprints is attested. **[A]** Died 26 February 1921 in Vienna; **[R]** the early hours and death at home.
- **[A, upgraded]** The grave: Zentralfriedhof, Group 0, Row 1, Number 88, Ehrengrab (German Wikipedia); the pool withheld the plot as unverified, and it is verifiable. **Coordinate corrected** to **48.1494, 16.4404**, immediately inside Tor 2 on Simmeringer Hauptstraße; the pool's figure sat 500 m west.
- **[A, corrected]** The grave "inscription" is not an inscription: "Carl Menger Ehrengrab, Zentralfriedhof Wien" is a **Wikipedia photograph caption**, and a caption cannot be cited as epigraphy. Quoted nowhere. **[A]** Eugen von Böhm-Bawerk (d. 1914) lies in the same cemetery.

## 8. Afterlife: editions, library, monuments

- **[A]** Second German edition of the *Grundsätze*, ed. Karl Menger, 1923, reconstructed from his father's revision manuscripts and not a text he approved; English translation 1950. **[R]** Hayek's four-volume *Gesammelte Werke*, 1933-36: from the pool's bibliography, since the Mises page on it **returned HTTP 404**.
- **[A, upgraded] The library.** German Wikipedia corroborates the chain: roughly 25,000 volumes, bequeathed to **Hermina Andermann**, sold by her to Japan, "ein großer Teil heute an der Hitotsubashi-Universität in Tokio". Andermann turns a vague "sold after his death" into a documented transfer. **The Hitotsubashi page returned HTTP 403**, so price and shipment year are unknown, and the journey's 1926 date is an **ordering placeholder resting on nothing**.
- **[A]** Arkadenhof relief by Alfred Hoffmann, unveiled 1929; plaque on the law faculty honor roll 1950; gate named jointly for Carl and Karl Menger 1998/99. All three are posthumous; they are carried because they stand at his own university, at his own pin, with the posthumity stated.
- **[A]** No Menger citation in the Bitcoin white paper: Nakamoto cites Wei Dai, Haber and Stornetta, Back and Merkle, and names **no economist at all**; the Bitcoin Magazine piece runs the Austrian lineage through Mises's regression theorem without naming Menger. **[R]** "Menger predicted Bitcoin" is reception-history myth: he died in 1921 and never wrote on cryptography or digital scarcity. None of it is in the journey; see section 10.
- **[R]** No feature film or documentary biography of Menger is known, a negative that cannot be confirmed by fetching. **[A, reattributed]** The "research program begun by Carl Menger in 1870" quotation is **Karen I. Vaughn's**, per the Duke HOPE page, not Bruce Caldwell's; Caldwell edited the volume.
