## 2. Four universities, one public office, 1932-1941

**[A]**, all on Wikipedia and the RAC record: Harvard 1932-1936, cum laude, B.S. economics; LSE, Hayek, work criticizing underconsumption theory; Ph.D., Chicago, 1940, *Unused Resources and Economic Waste*; secretary to Mayor La Guardia, 1940-1941; married Margaret McGrath 7 September 1940. **[R]** Exposure to Schumpeter at Harvard: RAC phrases it as exposure, not tutelage. **[A], no stop written**: assistant regional director, Office of Defense Health and Welfare Services, 1941, no place given beyond "United States."

**Date contradiction, settled by a third source.** Chronology dated the LSE year 1937, geography 1936-1937. LSE's history blog says he arrived "in the fall of 1937," so the year is 1937-1938. File uses 1937-10-01.

**Name error corrected.** The chronology lens wrote the bride as "Margaret McGrath 'Peggy' Dulany," fusing the wife with their daughter Peggy Dulany. The pool collapses these two women twice (see Stone Barns).

**Gap.** The wedding venue, St. Matthew's Episcopal, Bedford, appears **only** in the dossier, in neither RAC nor Wikipedia. The coordinate rests on that single unverified claim, and the stop says so.
