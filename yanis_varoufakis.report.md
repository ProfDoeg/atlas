# Yanis Varoufakis: research report

*Compiled 2026-08-21 for `yanis_varoufakis.journey.json`. Subject living; coverage to February 2026.*

**Ioannis Georgiou Varoufakis**, b. 24 March 1961, Palaio Faliro. Greek and Australian dual national. Economist; Greek Minister of Finance 27 January to 6 July 2015; founder of DiEM25 and MeRA25.

**[A]** attested, source named · **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, self-report, or a claim carried only by the research dossier · **[B]** source named but refused fetching this pass.

**Method note.** The pool arrived as four lenses (chronology, geography, quotes, afterlife) which **disagree with each other**, on tags and on facts, in at least nine places. Those disagreements are listed rather than smoothed over, and each correction records the superseded value so the error does not return next pass.

**Attested to the day:** the election of 25 January, the appointment of 27 January, the ECB collateral decision of 4 February (primary fetched), the 27 June referendum announcement, the result of 5 July (61.31 percent No against 38.69 percent Yes), and the resignation of 6 July with Tsakalotos sworn in the same day. **Everything else in the five months is [R]**: the February capitals tour, the 11-20 February Eurogroup sequence and the extension, the *Paris Match* shoot, Brookings, Riga, the 27 April reshuffle, the May taping admission, the 29-30 June bank holiday, capital controls, 60 euro limit and missed IMF payment, and the inner-cabinet vote of referendum night, which is memoir self-report and disputed. **Tag contradictions inside the pool, all resolved downward to [R]:** the **30 January Dijsselbloem press conference** ([R] chronology, [A] geography; nothing re-fetchable, Guardian, Reuters, BBC, DW and Al Jazeera all refusing, Dijsselbloem's own article silent); **Riga, 24 April** ([R] / [A]; anonymous-source journalism must never carry an attested tag); the **May taping admission** ([R] / [A], reuters.com blocked); **EuroLeaks** ([R] chronology, [A] afterlife, see §8).

## 9. Afterlife and iconography

He is alive, so the usual afterlife frame (tomb, remains, relics, posthumous cult) **does not apply**, recorded as a structural gap rather than silently skipped. In its place: no statue, plaque, wax figure, stamp, mural or museum object depicting him, an absence-of-evidence finding, checked and negative. The iconography is the film, the documentary, the *Paris Match* photographs, the Zagreb clip and the motorcycle-and-leather image he denies was a calculated brand. Translations **[R]**: *The Global Minotaur* into nine languages, *Game Theory* into Japanese, *Foundations of Economics* into Mandarin.

**A legend kept as a legend.** May 2015 press speculation that **Danae Stratou** was the wealthy Greek sculpture student at Central Saint Martins in Pulp's "Common People". Jarvis Cocker has never confirmed it and another candidate (Katerina Kana) has been proposed. Unresolved folklore, **[R]**, **not** in the journey file: a claim about a song, staged nowhere he stood.

## 10. Apparatus relations for the operator

Varoufakis is **not** listed in `EXCEPTIONS.md`. Two relations surfaced that are not rooted in his own travel. Neither was silently deleted.

**(a) Backward: the Global Minotaur reading of 1944 and 1971.** The book argues substantively about Bretton Woods and the 1971 Nixon shock, the deliberate American deficit as a global surplus-recycling mechanism. Under the standing rule those events belong to whoever was there, and his engagement is a one-directional citation belonging as a *source* on their stops, not as scenes in his file. Were he listed, such stops could sit **at his own Athens pin**, dated only to order the segment. **Direction caught: backward**, licensable if the author says so. Not taken.

**(b) Backward: the sovereign-debt rhyme with Argentina.** The interlock lens built this case and then demolished it: neither `paul_singer.journey.json` nor `mark_brodsky.journey.json` mentions Varoufakis, Greece, Athens, Syriza, Tsipras or the troika, checked by full-text search. `QUEUE.md` line 86 asserts a "direct interlock with the corpus's sovereign-debt material", but it is **thematic**: holdout litigation against official-sector negotiation, "default" disputed as a label in both, IMF debt-sustainability findings in both. The claim that he has referenced Argentina's 2001 default in his own writing is itself uncorroborated. **Direction caught: backward.** Not taken.

**One atlas traveler is named in a campa**, a real relation rather than a shared pin: **`karl_marx`**, in the Technofeudalism campa. He has called himself an "erratic Marxist" and then a "libertarian Marxist", and *Technofeudalism: What Killed Capitalism* argues that the object Marx anatomised no longer exists. That is the substantive one-directional engagement the naming rule licenses. Explicitly **not** named, shared geography being no reason: `paul_singer` and `mark_brodsky`. Draghi, Schäuble, Lagarde, Dijsselbloem, Tsipras, Tsakalotos, Newell and Galbraith have **no journey file at all**, confirmed by filename search; `bill_clinton.journey.json` surfaced on a keyword match with no content check and is unused.
