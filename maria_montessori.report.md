# Maria Montessori (1870-1952), research report
*Compiled 2026-08-29 from the verified research pool (chronology, geography, quotes, interlock and afterlife lenses, each already adversarially checked). This records what the pool supports, what it contradicts, and what it leaves open. It resolves nothing the sources leave unresolved.*

**Legend.** **[A]** attested, source named · **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or movement lore · *gap* the sources consulted are silent and no claim is made.

Journey file: `maria_montessori.journey.json`, 45 stops in 8 segments, calendar `gregorian`, register `national mythology: the canon is true`.

## 1. Chiaravalle and the schooling, 1870-1890

- Born **31 August 1870** at Chiaravalle, Ancona, only child of **Alessandro Montessori** (state financial administration) and **Renilde Stoppani**; full name Maria Tecla Artemisia. **[A, en.wikipedia; it.wikipedia]**
- Birth house **Piazza Mazzini 10**, now a museum of the **Fondazione Chiaravalle-Montessori**, with a bronze plaque. **[A, it.wikipedia]**
- **Coordinate correction, load-bearing.** The pool's lenses gave wrong coordinates for Chiaravalle and disagreed with each other: chronology 43.6167/13.3333 (about 1.9 km north of town), geography and afterlife 43.5269/13.3167 and 43.5254/13.3172 (about 8 km south-southwest, open country toward Monsano). The town is at **43.600, 13.333**. The birth stop is pinned at **43.6003, 13.3253**, the Piazza Mazzini house.
- **Florence 1873**, following the father's postings; **Rome 1875**; **elementary school 1876**, of which the surviving record is certificates for **conduct** and **needlework** (the prodigy anecdotes are later movement supply). **1883** Regia Scuola Tecnica Michelangelo Buonarroti, a boys' school; **1886** Regio Istituto Tecnico Leonardo da Vinci; physics-mathematics course completed **1890**. **[A, en.wikipedia]** No residence identified in Florence or Rome; city pins stand in. *gap*
  - **Contradiction, not resolved.** en.wikipedia has two successive institutions; **it.wikipedia treats them as one school renamed** ("attuale Istituto Tecnico Leonardo da Vinci").
  - The pool's geography lens tagged the Leonardo da Vinci institute **[R]** because it was "not found in independently fetched Wikipedia." That is wrong; en.wikipedia names it. **Retagged [A].**
- Aiming at **engineering**, she turned to **medicine** in 1890. **[A, en.wikipedia]**

## 2. The medical degree, 1890-1898

- **1890** enrols in natural sciences, University of Rome; **1892** diploma di licenza; **1893** begins medical study; **July 1896** graduates. **[A, en.wikipedia; Stanford Encyclopedia]**
- **Not the first woman physician in Italy.** **Ernestina Paper** qualified at Florence in **1877**. The "first Italian woman doctor" claim is movement lore. **[A, en.wikipedia]**
- **[R] Pope Leo XIII.** Tradition holds the pope intervened personally to open medical study to her. **No primary confirmation in anything reachable**; the documented route is the gradual one through natural sciences.
- **[R] The solitary dissections.** Tradition holds propriety barred her from mixed dissection, so she worked alone at night and smoked against the formaldehyde. Plausible for the period, resting on later recollection.
- **September 1896, Berlin:** International Congress for Women's Rights, arguing equal pay. Verbatim: **"the same wage for the same work."** **[A, AMI Montessori Archives, '1896 International Congress for Women's Rights']**
- **Turin 1897** National Medical Congress (juvenile crime as social neglect); **Turin 1898** First Pedagogical Conference (institutions and trained teachers for children classed as deficient). **31 March 1898:** birth of **Mario**, by her colleague **Giuseppe Montesano**, registered **Mario Pipilli, parents unknown**, raised apart in his early years. **[A, en.wikipedia]** **Ordering error in the pool:** the chronology array placed the 1898 birth *after* the 1900 Orthophrenic School appointment. Corrected.

## 3. Paris, London, the Orthophrenic School, 1899-1904

- **1899 Paris:** studies **Jean Itard** and **Édouard Séguin**. **[A, Stanford Encyclopedia; AMI biography]** Pinned at the atlas's canonical Paris **48.8566, 2.3522**, byte-identical.
- **1899 London:** International Council of Women; women workers and Sicilian child labour; received with the delegates by **Queen Victoria**. **[A, en.wikipedia]** Venue beyond "London" unidentified. *gap*
- **1900:** co-director with Montesano of the **Scuola Magistrale Ortofrenica**; her pupils sit and pass state examinations set for ordinary children. **1904:** **libera docente** in anthropology, lecturing in pedagogical anthropology through 1908, anthropometry on Roman schoolchildren. **[A, en.wikipedia]**
- **Contradiction, three dates for one break, not resolved.** Popular accounts merge into one event what the sources date three ways: **1901**, leaves the Orthophrenic School and private practice (en.wikipedia); **1911**, gives up practice and the university post (**AMI's timeline**); **1919**, the university resignation (en.wikipedia, separately). The file takes 1901 for the school and states the other two in `date_confidence`.

## 4. San Lorenzo and the method, 1906-1911

- **1906:** **Edoardo Talamo**, manager for the company that renovated the San Lorenzo tenements, asks her to organise the tenants' small children. **[A in the standard literature and the Stanford Encyclopedia]**
  - **Source-attribution error flagged.** The pool tagged Talamo **[A]** to en.wikipedia, where the string "Talamo" **does not appear**. The fact stands on other sources; that attribution does not.
- **6 January 1907:** first **Casa dei Bambini**, **Via dei Marsi**, San Lorenzo. **[A for date and district, en.wikipedia; AMI]**
  - **Address disputed, not resolved.** **AMI's archive gives Via dei Marsi 53**; **58** is common in secondary citation; **en.wikipedia gives no number at all**.
  - **Ages.** en.wikipedia: "50 or 60 children between the ages of two or three and six or seven," wider than the pool's "two and a half to six." The wider range is used.
- **7 April 1907:** second Casa dei Bambini, same district. **1909, Città di Castello:** first training course; **Il Metodo della Pedagogia Scientifica** published by **S. Lapi**. **[A, en.wikipedia]**
- **1911:** third training course, Milan. **[A, en.wikipedia]**
- **1910, Antropologia pedagogica**, from the university lectures, carrying the anthropometric and racial-hierarchy content of contemporary positivist anthropology. **[R]**: publication confirmed via the Wikipedia bibliography, contents characterised from dossier synthesis, not read page by page.

## 5. America, 1911-1915

- **October 1911:** first North American Montessori school, **Tarrytown, New York**. **[A, en.wikipedia, 'Maria Montessori']**
  - **Contested primacy, not resolved.** en.wikipedia's **'Montessori education'** says instead that "in 1913 Narcissa Cox Vanderlip and Frank A. Vanderlip founded the **Scarborough School**, the first Montessori school in the U.S." Two Wikipedia articles disagree; the campa names Tarrytown without asserting primacy.
  - **Ordering error in the pool:** the 1911 Tarrytown item sat *after* the 1912 translation item.
- **The Bell household class. Place disputed, and the pool had it wrong.** The pool put a second American class in the Bells' **Washington, D.C.** household (38.9219/-77.0559). The cited source says the school "was opened in their **Canadian home**," i.e. **Beinn Bhreagh / Baddeck, Nova Scotia** (46.0946, -60.7160). **Not staged as a stop**; see §9.
- **1912:** **Anne E. George's** translation, *The Montessori Method*, a US bestseller. **December 1913:** lecture tour, New York, Washington, Philadelphia, Boston, Pittsburgh, Chicago. **1915:** **Panama-Pacific International Exposition**, San Francisco, glass-walled model classroom run by **Helen Parkhurst**, plus a California training course. **[A, en.wikipedia]**

## 6. Barcelona, the Fascist bargain, the flight, 1916-1936

- **1916:** settles in **Barcelona**. **[A for year and city, en.wikipedia; AMI]**
  - **Attribution corrected.** The pool credited the **Mancomunitat de Catalunya**, tagged **[A]** to en.wikipedia, where "Mancomunitat" **does not appear**; **AMI says she moved at the invitation of the city government**. The Mancomunitat was also **suppressed in 1925** and cannot span 1916-1936.
  - Not a smooth twenty years: the **military directory closed her model school in 1924**, the **Second Spanish Republic funded a new course in 1933**. **[A, en.wikipedia]** Both are in the campa.
- **1917:** lectures in Amsterdam, **Netherlands Montessori Society** founded. **1924:** meets **Mussolini**; state sponsorship; the friends' society reorganised as the **Opera Nazionale Montessori** under royal charter, Mussolini honorary president **from 1926**. **[A, en.wikipedia]** The **1923 Vienna** lectures published as *Das Kind in der Familie* are **[R]** and get no stop; see §9.
- **1929, Elsinore:** first International Montessori Congress; **Association Montessori Internationale** founded with Mario. **[A, en.wikipedia]**
  - **Omission corrected.** The pool dated the AMI headquarters at **Koninginneweg 161, Amsterdam** to "1935 onward" and skipped what came first. **AMI's timeline: BERLIN 1929-1935, Amsterdam thereafter.**
  - The lenses also disagreed on Koninginneweg's coordinates (52.3572/4.8657 vs 52.3567/4.8635); resolved by not pinning the address as a stop.
- **1932:** peace lecture at **Nice**; address to the **International Peace Club, Geneva**. **[A, en.wikipedia]**
  - **Date corrected.** The pool carried a **1937 Copenhagen** congress as **[A]**; the cited source has no such event, saying only that she "held peace conferences **from 1932 to 1939** in Geneva, Brussels, Copenhagen, and Utrecht." **Utrecht was missing from the pool entirely** and is restored.
- **1932** surveillance of her and Mario by the Fascist authorities; **1933** resignation from the Opera Nazionale Montessori; **1934** departure for Barcelona as the Italian institutions are absorbed or closed. **[A, en.wikipedia]**
- **July 1936:** the coup; she leaves Barcelona.
  - **[R], not [A].** The pool tagged the **British warship** rescue **[A]**. "Warship" appears in neither en.wikipedia nor AMI, which says only that she "flees Barcelona for England and then Amsterdam." **Retagged [R]** here and in `date_confidence`.
  - **The England leg was missing from the pool's chronology entirely.** en.wikipedia: they "left Barcelona for **England**, and soon moved to Laren, near Amsterdam." A London stop is added whose `date_confidence` states that no source gives a date, an address or a length of stay.
  - No source dates the departure to a day; the file's 1936-07-19 is an ordering device and says so.
- **1936:** settles at **Laren**, North Holland (52.2559/5.2262 confirmed). **[A, en.wikipedia]**

## 7. India and Ceylon, 1939-1949

- **1939:** to **Adyar, Madras**, at the **Theosophical Society's** invitation; her claimed 1899 membership of the Society's European Section is **dossier-only [R]**. **June 1940:** Italy enters the war, **Mario interned as an enemy alien**, Maria confined to the compound, Mario released after about two months. **[A, en.wikipedia]** The "imprisoned for seven years" version is exaggeration.
- **1942:** cosmic education, the four planes of development, the six-to-twelve course, worked out with Mario at **Kodaikanal**. **[A, en.wikipedia]**
  - **Date range corrected.** The pool gave Kodaikanal as **1940-1946**. She was confined at Adyar from 1940; it.wikipedia dates the **Kodaikanal internment 1942-1944**, en.wikipedia says only "Madras and Kodaikanal until 1946." The places overlap; Kodaikanal does not cover the span.
- **1944:** travels to **Sri Lanka**; some thirty lectures and a government-recognised course. **[A for the country, en.wikipedia]**
  - **Tag corrected.** The pool tagged this **[A]** with **Colombo** coordinates; "Colombo" does not appear in the source, which names only the country. **Retagged [R]** for the city; the pin is Colombo and `date_confidence` says why.
- **1945:** first **All India Montessori Conference**, Jaipur (folded into the Ceylon campa). **1946:** returns to Europe, London training course, a Montessori Centre opening there in 1947. **1947:** courses at Adyar and **Ahmedabad**, whose transcripts are edited into **The Absorbent Mind**, a book built from student notes rather than a manuscript she wrote. **1949:** Karachi course, **Pakistan Montessori Association** founded. **[A, en.wikipedia]**

## 8. The last years, the death, the afterlife

- **1949, Sanremo:** eighth International Montessori Congress. **[A, en.wikipedia]**
  - **Nobel count corrected.** The pool said "first of **three** nominations." en.wikipedia: nominated "in 1949, and again in 1950 and in 1951... a total of **six nominations**." AMI agrees on the years. **Six nominations across three years, none successful.**
- **1949:** **Legion of Honour**, **Officer of the Order of Orange-Nassau**, honorary doctorate from the **University of Amsterdam**; no month is recorded for any of the three (*gap*). **1950:** UNESCO-associated conference, **Florence**, and the twenty-ninth international training course at **Perugia** (folded into the Florence campa). **19 June 1951:** preliminary meeting of the **UNESCO Governing Board**, Wiesbaden, the only precisely dated item of her last two years. **1951:** ninth International Montessori Congress, **London**, and a training course at **Innsbruck** (folded into the London campa). **[A, en.wikipedia]**
- **6 May 1952:** dies of a **cerebral hemorrhage** at **Noordwijk aan Zee**, staying with Mario, while considering an invitation to travel to Africa. **[A, en.wikipedia; Stanford Encyclopedia]**
- Buried in the **Roman Catholic cemetery at Noordwijk**, having asked to be buried wherever death found her; the monument is a **semicircular construction designed by her eldest grandson, Mario Montessori Jr.** **[A, AMI]** No source gives the funeral date; the file's 1952-05-10 is an ordering device and says so.
- **The inscription: two texts in print, not reconciled.** **AMI's page**, describing the stone, reads "...per la **construzione** della pace..." (non-standard, presumably as engraved); **it.wikipedia** gives the standard "...per la **costruzione**..." with commas added. The pool's lenses each picked a different one and contradicted each other. The `quote` field uses **AMI's engraved form**, since AMI describes the physical stone; the variance is in `date_confidence`.
- **Afterlife [A] unless noted.** The birth house is a museum with a bronze plaque; the foundation's office is at Via XXV Aprile 46/48, Chiaravalle, and **no museum founding year is established in anything reachable** (*gap*). She appeared on the **1,000-lire note** (first printed 3 October 1990, circulating to 28 February 2002, reverse "Bambino allo studio" by Armando Spadini) and on stamps of the **Netherlands, India, Italy, the Maldives, Pakistan and Sri Lanka**; Sri Lanka is explicitly in en.wikipedia's list and the pool's hedge against it was backwards. A **KLM MD-11 (PH-KCB)** carried her name until November 2014. The **AMI archive** is at **Koninginneweg 161, Amsterdam**, her former home; **Montessori-Pierson** still issues previously unpublished material. **Léa Todorov's** film *Maria Montessori* / *La Nouvelle Femme* premiered at Zurich, 5 October 2023, opening in France 13 March 2024.
  - **TIME date corrected, against the pool's own correction.** The pool moved this to **2019** on en.wikipedia's Montessori article. That sentence is itself wrong: en.wikipedia's **'Time Person of the Year'** dates the 89 covers to **2020**, for International Women's Day. **2020 is correct**; Montessori representing **1931** is confirmed.
  - **[R] The giving-away myth.** Tradition holds she gave her method freely away and never sought to control it. The record shows AMI controlling certification, authorised apparatus, translations and the name, with the post-1967 AMI/AMS and generic-name disputes following from that control. Stated plainly at the Elsinore stop.

## 9. Stops the pool proposed and this file does not stage

**A stop belongs to whoever was actually there.** Three pool items are places the *method* travelled and she did not.

1. **Tarrytown, October 1911.** Founded by others in her absence. Named inside the New York 1913 campa, not staged as a scene.
2. **The Bell household class, 1912.** Opened by the Bells in their own home, in her absence, and per the cited source in **Nova Scotia** (see §5). A Bell stop belongs on the Bells' own file.
3. **Buenos Aires (canonical pin -34.6037, -58.3816).** Offered by the interlock lens on a dossier-only claim of Montessori classes in Argentina by 1911. **She never went to Argentina.** Not used, and flagged: for an Argentine-first corpus that 1911 thread is worth a targeted follow-up, and it is currently unconfirmed.

Dropped for thinness rather than location: the **Vienna 1923** lectures (**[R]**, one line in the source), and separate stops for **Jaipur 1945**, **Perugia 1950** and **Innsbruck 1951**, each folded into an adjacent campa.

## 10. Apparatus relations for the operator

**Maria Montessori is not listed in `EXCEPTIONS.md`.** Relations the interlock lens found that are **not rooted in her own life** are kept and flagged here rather than silently deleted, with the direction named.

**Named in a campa** (direct or real one-directional relation, no exception needed):

- **`froebel`**: **backward**, named at Citta di Castello. She inherited the kindergarten and its Gifts as precedent and argued explicitly against Froebel's adult-imposed fantasy and symbolic gifts when she built her own apparatus; writing against a predecessor is a real citable relation. The pool tags the explicit criticism **[R]** (the Froebel file never mentions her; the 1909 Italian text was not verified here) and `date_confidence` says so.
- **`alexander_graham_bell`**: **direct**, named in the New York campa. The Bells opened a Montessori class and took the presidency of an American Montessori association: patronage, not shared geography. The association detail is **[R]**.
- **`mussolini`**: **direct**, named at Rome 1924. Met 1924; honorary president of her Opera Nazionale Montessori from 1926; his regime surveilled her from 1932 and drove her out by 1934.

**Kept in the record, NOT named in any campa, flagged for the operator:**

- **`pestalozzi`**: **backward, indirect.** Shared ancestor of the sense-experience tradition, reaching her through Itard and Séguin rather than directly. His file does not mention her; nothing in the pool shows her writing about him. Itard and Séguin carry the lineage in the Paris campa instead. If she can be attested citing Pestalozzi by name (her 1909 book is where to look), this becomes nameable.
- **`steiner`**: **lateral.** Closest rival reformer, same decade, opposed premises, **no direct meeting documented anywhere**. Shared period is not a relation.
- **`annie_besant`**, **`blavatsky`**: **backward, institutional only.** Blavatsky died 1891, Besant 1933, six years before Montessori reached Adyar. Her link is to the institution (the claimed 1899 European Section membership, **[R]**, dossier-only) and to the Arundales who hosted her.
- **`jiddu_krishnamurti`**: **lateral.** Same Theosophical network, no documented meeting; he was largely in Ojai during her Indian years.
- **`rabindranath_tagore`**: **lateral, one live thread.** The dossier says Tagore's Bengal schools associated themselves with Montessori methods, producing variably defined "Tagore-Montessori schools." If true that is a real one-directional institutional relation and would license naming him. **Dossier-only, unconfirmed.** Worth a targeted follow-up.
- **`victoria_ocampo`**, **`sarmiento`**: **lateral.** Shared axis and shared reform current, no documented contact either way. Offered for the Argentine-reader bridge, which the map already supplies by co-location.

**Direction summary.** All backward or lateral. **No forward reach was found or written**: nothing applies her framework to anything postdating 1952, and nothing was written into any earlier subject's file.
