# Alberto Fujimori, research report
*2026-08-21. Alberto Kenya Fujimori Inomoto, 1938-2024. President of Peru 1990-2000, convicted 2009, pardoned 2017, pardon annulled 2018 and reinstated 2022, released 2023, died 2024. Slug `alberto_fujimori`. Companion: `alberto_fujimori.journey.json`, 9 segments, 45 stops.*

**Legend.** **[A]** = attested, source named inline · **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition, or dossier-only synthesis not independently reachable in this pass. Source shorthand: **enwp** = en.wikipedia, **eswp** = es.wikipedia, **wq** = es.wikiquote, **ws** = es.wikisource, **D** = the incoming research dossier, by line number. Contradictions are recorded where they arise and **not adjudicated**. Twelve pool coordinates were wrong; each correction is stated in its own stop's `date_confidence`, with the value replaced and the value used.

## 1. Origin and schooling, 1934-1956

**[A enwp]** Born in Lima. **Contradiction, unresolved:** the electoral register and his promoted biography give **28 July 1938**; enwp and other civil-record reporting give **26 July 1938**, in Miraflores. The ISO date defaults to 28 July, the day on his own papers and Peru's national day.

**[A enwp, reporting Caretas 1997]** A rival account held he was born at **Kawachi, Kumamoto**, on a falsified Peruvian record: a real dispute, not folk rumour. **[A D, Japan MOFA finding, Dec 2000]** It lost force when Japan found the birth **registered at its Lima consulate**, which a birth in Japan would not have needed.

**[R D, CIDOB-derived]** Parents Naoichi Fujimori and Mutsue Inomoto, both of Kumamoto, emigrated jointly in 1934 aboard the *Boyoku/Boyuko Maru*, whose name is spelled inconsistently across sources.

**[A enwp]** Schools: Hoshi Shokai Gakuen (1946-1948), La Rectora (1949), Colegio Nuestra Señora de la Merced, Gran Unidad Escolar Alfonso Ugarte, leaving 1956. The pool dated La Merced "c. 1949-1952" and tagged it [A]; enwp puts it in the **secondary** years, and La Rectora was missing entirely. Downgraded to **[R]**, four schools folded into one stop, the pin a declared approximation since Nominatim finds no historic *La Merced* in central Lima.

**Kumamoto dropped as a stop.** The geography lens itself notes "no record found of Alberto himself ever traveling to Kumamoto." The prefecture is his **parents'** departure point, so it survives only in the birth stop's campa and `suggested_refs`. Where it bites on his own life is the December 2000 nationality finding, which has its own stop.

## 2. University, France, the United States, 1957-1969

**[A enwp]** UNALM, agricultural engineering, **first in his class, 1961**. Returned to teach mathematics and physics, dean of sciences, **rector 1984-1989**, **president of the National Assembly of Rectors from 1987**.

**[A enwp; eswp for the subject]** University of Strasbourg, **1964**, reading **Física Pura**, not the pool's "mathematics/physics." **No completed Strasbourg degree is established anywhere**; campaign biographies inflating it into a doctorate are treated as inflation.

**[A enwp]** University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee, **MS mathematics, 1969**, Ford Foundation scholarship; later profiles wrongly relocate it to Madison.

## 3. Concertando and Cambio 90, 1987-1990

**[A enwp; D 79, 219, 462]** Hosted **Concertando** on state channel 7, 1987-1989. The pool put the studios in **Jesús María**; TNP/Canal 7 broadcasts from the **IRTP building, Santa Beatriz**. Place string and pin rewritten.

**[A eswp "Cambio 90"]** Registered **5 October 1989**. **Misquote killed:** the pool gave the motto as "Honestidad, Tecnología, Trabajo." It is **"Honradez, Tecnología, Trabajo,"** and the wrong word appears nowhere in the journey file.

**[A, upgraded from [R] on a primary artifact]** *"Un presidente como tú."* Two primary artifacts carry it: a 1990 newsprint poster in the **Sam L. Slick Collection**, UNM, and a 1990 campaign spot of the same title in the **PUCP audiovisual archive**. **Framing held honest:** a slogan addressed to the voter, **not** staged as something Fujimori said; its `quote_source` names it as a poster title.

**[A enwp "1990 Peruvian general election"]** Runoff **10 June 1990**: 4,489,897 votes, **62.38%**, against 37.62% for Mario Vargas Llosa. First round ~29%, second place, is **[R]**. The pool filed the runoff win and the swearing-in under one date; **28 July is the inauguration**, so they are separate stops.

## 4. The Fujishock and Grupo Colina, 1990-1992

**[R D 87, not re-fetched]** 29 June 1990, UN Headquarters: a session convened by **Javier Pérez de Cuéllar** with **Camdessus** (IMF), **Conable** (World Bank) and **Iglesias** (IDB). Return leg via **Miami**, where Rodríguez Pastor and Boloña argued for shock over the gradualist "seven samurai" programme; folded into this stop.

**[A enwp]** Inaugurated **28 July 1990**. **[R D, not re-verified against a primary news account]** **8 August 1990**: prime minister **Juan Carlos Hurtado Miller** announces the *Fujishock*. **[A enwp "Barrios Altos massacre"]** **3 November 1991**, 840 Jirón Huanta: fifteen killed including an eight-year-old, four wounded, by the detachment later named **Grupo Colina**.

**[R D 111, 304-306]** **29 January 1992, Pativilca**: six residents abducted, tortured, killed by Colina members. **Gap that matters:** the case was **still in hearings on the day he died** and produced **no verdict** against him. The pin approximates the town, not the site.

## 5. The autogolpe and the 1993 charter, 1992-1993

**[A enwp; D 104-105]** **5 April 1992**: televised dissolution of Congress, suspension of the constitution, intervention of judiciary, public ministry, constitutional tribunal and comptroller, with troops in Congress and the newsrooms.

**[A wq, seen verbatim]** *"Disolver, disolver temporalmente el Congreso de la República."* **Chain-of-custody gap:** wq cites Vargas Llosa (2001) p. 46 and Raúl Wiener (2001) p. 98, **neither page reachable** (404s), so this rests on a compilation's authority alone.

**[A susana_higuchi.journey.json, which itself flags it contested]** The family's removal into the **SIE "Pentagonito"** is **Susana Higuchi's own account**, denied by the government and established by no court. Carried with that caveat, at her file's exact pin.

**[A enwp "La Cantuta massacre"]** **18 July 1992**: nine students and professor **Hugo Muñoz** abducted, killed, secretly buried. The burial sites are **not pinned**: no recoverable place name was reached, and inventing one would be worse than the gap.

**[A eswp "Abimael Guzmán"]** **12 September 1992**, Surquillo: Guzmán taken without a firefight, with **Elena Iparraguirre and two other women**. **Anachronism corrected:** the pool credited **DINCOTE**, which is that directorate's later name; in 1992 it was the **GEIN of DIRCOTE**, under General Ketín Vidal.

**[A enwp; D 118-119]** CCD elections 22 November 1992 (Cambio 90-Nueva Mayoría, 44 of 80 seats); charter approved by referendum ~52% on 31 October 1993, promulgated 29 December. **[A enwp "Constitution of Peru"]** The catalogue of signatories records **"President Alberto Fujimori (signature erased)."** **[R, unconfirmed]** A reported congressional vote of 6 March 2025 to restore it could not be confirmed; not carried.

## 6. Second term, 1994-1998

**[R D, no contemporaneous account]** **August 1994**: Susana Higuchi removed from first-lady duties after accusing relatives of diverting Japanese aid; **Keiko**, nineteen, installed. **[A eswp "Susana Higuchi"]** Marriage pinned at **25 July 1974**; the **divorce was formalised in 1995**, not 1994, correcting the pool's conflation.

**[R D 131]** Cenepa War, January-February 1995, with front-line presidential visits; **Tiwinza control claims were disputed at the time** and are not adopted. **[A enwp]** Reelected April 1995 with ~64.4% against **Javier Pérez de Cuéllar**. Folded into the Cenepa stop.

**[A enwp "Japanese embassy hostage crisis"]** **17 December 1996**: fourteen MRTA under **Néstor Cerpa Cartolini** seize the residence during a reception for **Emperor Akihito's birthday**. **Headcount corrected:** the pool said "roughly 800"; enwp gives **over 600 guests**, down to ~381 men, then the final 72.

**[A D 141-145 + fetched crisis account]** **22 April 1997**, Chavín de Huántar: 140 commandos, all 14 captors killed, one hostage dead (**Carlos Giusti**), two commandos dead, 71 of 72 rescued. **Contested:** a DIA cable and forensic testimony allege some captors were shot after surrendering, substantiated by the Inter-American Court for one victim (**Cruz Sánchez**) without any conviction of Fujimori.

**[A, verbatim in two independent 2017 sources]** *"Cuando analizaba opciones distintas a la salida pacífica, los admirables túneles de Chavín felizmente vinieron en un sueño que inspiró el rescate."* **Date corrected** from the pool's "1997 retrospective remark": La Vanguardia (EFE) and RPP, both 20 April 2017, establish it as a **handwritten letter written in prison, released through Kenji Fujimori's Twitter account**. Attached to the 1997 stop it describes.

**[A D 155; IACtHR]** 1997: **Baruch Ivcher Bronstein** stripped of Peruvian nationality and of Frecuencia Latina after broadcasting the Leonor La Rosa torture and the SIN interceptions. **Gap:** no day or month reachable, so the stop's month is a declared placeholder.

**[A enwp "Cenepa War"]** **26 October 1998**, Brasília Presidential Act with **Jamil Mahuad**, four guarantor states present. The Vancouver and Kuala Lumpur APEC stops were cut for the ceiling and folded into this campa.

## 7. The third term, the tape, the fax, 2000

**[R D, not re-fetched]** 28 May 2000: declared winner of a disputed third term after monitors documented fraud and **Alejandro Toledo** withdrew. **[A enwp]** Sworn in 28 July 2000 amid the **Marcha de los Cuatro Suyos**. **[A D 161, 340-342]** Six security guards died in a **Banco de la Nación** fire during the march; **responsibility was never judicially established against Fujimori**, is not asserted, and is folded into the oath stop.

**[R D, not re-fetched]** **14 September 2000**: the first *vladivideo*, **Montesinos** paying congressman **Alberto Kouri** US$15,000, broadcast on Canal N after a screening at the Gran Hotel Bolívar.

**[A ws, primary]** **16 September 2000**, *Mensaje a la Nación*. Both of the pool's "c. 2000" quotations belong to **this single speech**: *"He gobernado el Perú diez años..."* and *"Mis dos periodos de Gobierno, lo reconozco, no están exentos de errores u omisiones."* The pool's dating rested on a Johnson (2001) secondary reprint; see Sources for why that volume could not be checked.

**[A enwp]** Departed for **APEC at Brunei on 13 November**; travelled **Brunei to Tokyo on 17 November**. **Misreading corrected, contradiction left open:** the pool asserted en.wikipedia dates the resignation fax to 17 November. It does not; that is the **travel date** in the sentence cited, and enwp never dates the fax. Congress **refused** it on 21 November and **voted him out on 22 November** (62-9, nine abstentions), so the fax falls **between 17 and 21 November**. The dossier gives the 20th, the press usually the 19th. **Not resolved.**

## 8. Tokyo, Santiago, extradition, 2000-2007

**[R D; substance corroborated by enwp]** **12 December 2000**: Japan finds he holds Japanese nationality by the 1938 consular registration, and does not extradite its nationals. **[R D 165-169]** Five Tokyo years of lecturing, writing in Japanese, and the **Sí Cumple** movement; no residence address located.

**[A eswp "Juicio a Alberto Fujimori"]** Arrived Santiago **by private jet, as a tourist**, stayed at the **Hotel Marriott**, arrested **about ten hours later**. **A false attribution removed:** the pool claimed enwp gives 5 November 2005 for the arrest; **it gives no such date**, only "hours after his arrival." eswp gives **6 November**, much of the press **7 November**. Dated 2005-11-06, both stated. **Not resolved.**

**[R D 173-175]** Bail in **Las Condes** from 18 May 2006; marriage to **Satomi Kataoka** registered 6 April 2006; an unsuccessful run for the Japanese upper house from Chilean house arrest. **[A enwp; D 177]** **21 September 2007**: Chile's Supreme Court authorises extradition on **seven of thirteen** counts (Barrios Altos, La Cantuta, the Gorriti and Dyer kidnappings, several corruption files).

## 9. Barbadillo, 2007-2023

**[A RPP retrospective + El Correo dispatch]** *"Si se cometieron hechos execrables los condeno, pero no fueron ordenes de quien habla. Por eso señor presidente rechazo los cargos totalmente. ¡Soy inocente!"* **Date corrected, elision restored.** The pool's **4 July 2017** is only the RPP retrospective's publication date; the words were spoken **10 December 2007** at the opening hearing before the **Sala Penal Especial at the DIROES base**. The pool also **spliced two non-adjacent sentences**; the middle is restored.

**[A enwp]** **7 April 2009**, indirect perpetration of Barrios Altos and La Cantuta plus the Gorriti and Dyer kidnappings, **25 years**; **20 July 2009**, embezzlement, 7½ years; **30 September 2009**, bribery and buying a channel's editorial line, 6 years. One stop, all handed down at the same site.

**[A enwp]** **24 December 2017**, humanitarian pardon by **Pedro Pablo Kuczynski**, days after a bloc led by **Kenji Fujimori** withheld the impeachment votes that saved him. The causal link is asserted in every account and **was never established by any court**, which is what the campa says.

**[A enwp]** **3 October 2018** pardon annulled; **17 March 2022** reinstated 4-3 by the Constitutional Court; **5 December 2023** release ordered, **6 December 2023** walked out. The pool flagged 5 vs 6 December as a source conflict; it is not one, since **5 December is the court's order and 6 December the walk-out.**

**[A eswp "La palabra del chino"]** *el intruso*, written at Barbadillo with **Víctor Paredes** and **Ricardo Rivera**, 2021; *el inconforme*, written during his post-pardon liberty, presented posthumously in **Iquitos** by Keiko. **Two details dropped:** the pool's 2022 ISBN and the month "January" appear nowhere in the cited article, which gives no ISBN and only the year. The further-manuscripts claim stays **[R]**.

## 10. Death and afterlife, 2024-

**[A enwp]** Died **11 September 2024** at Keiko's home in San Borja. Lay in state at the **Museo de la Nación** 12-14 September, state funeral at the **Gran Teatro Nacional** on the 14th, buried at **Campo Fe, Huachipa**, the same day. Museum and theatre stand one block apart, which only the corrected pins reflect, so they are one stop.

**[A enwp "El Ojo que Llora"]** **23 September 2007**, the day after his extradition arrival, the Campo de Marte memorial was vandalised with smashed stones and **orange paint, his campaign colour**. **Placement decision:** the vandals were anonymous sympathisers, not a traveler with a file, so this is a clause in his burial campa and a `suggested_ref`, never a scene of his own. The **Lugar de la Memoria**, Miraflores, 2015, is cited the same way.

**[A/R, honours]** Brazil's Southern Cross, 1999: the pool called it the **Grand Collar**, but the order's own list files him under **"Unknown Class,"** so the grade is dropped. The Gembloux (1986) and San Martín de Porres (1987) honorary doctorates **[R D 69, 75]** were cut as stops, the USMP pin being ~3.5 km off with the venue unknown.

**[A enwp "Keiko Fujimori"]** Keiko won the runoff of **7 June 2026** with **50.13%**, defeating **Roberto Sánchez** by 49,641 votes, inaugurated **28 July 2026**. The pool left the margin general; it is 0.13 points. **Not a stop in Alberto's file:** it postdates his death by twenty-two months and belongs to `keiko_fujimori.journey.json`.

## Apparatus relations for the operator

Alberto Fujimori is **not** in `EXCEPTIONS.md`, so no apparatus stops are licensed. Per the adversary's duty nothing was silently deleted: four relations in the pool are not rooted in his own life, each recorded below with the direction caught.

1. **William Colby / the Phoenix Program read onto the SIN.** Direction **backward**. Colby died in 1996 with no attested contact with Fujimori or Montesinos, and the pool itself calls it "weak, purely thematic." Not written in; if wanted it belongs on `william_colby.journey.json`, who is not on the list either.
2. **George H. W. Bush / CIA counter-narcotics funding of the SIN.** Direction **backward**, and **unverified**: the pool could not confirm the funding overlapped Bush's term (1989-93) rather than Clinton's (1993-2000, the bulk of the decade). Not written in; check before either file uses it.
3. **Augusto Pinochet as structural template.** Direction: neither. This is a **parallel**, not a relation: strongman arrested abroad on a foreign warrant, house arrest, extradition litigation, trial over a secret-police apparatus. The two are **not attested to have met**; Fujimori's Chilean ordeal merely ran in the same city and years as Pinochet's own prosecutions. Shared geography and shared shape are not a reason to name-drop, so **`augusto_pinochet` is deliberately not named in any campa.** Flagged because the brief named the pairing.
4. **Che Guevara's 1952 Lima stop.** Direction: none. Same city, forty years apart, no interaction. Not used.

Declined on the reverse of the same rule: **Keiko's Chorrillos detention (2018-2020)** is her stop, on her file, and Alberto's Barbadillo stops do not claim it.
