# José Rafael Hernández (1834-1886), research report

## 1. Birth and childhood, 1834-1845

- Born **10 November 1834** at the **Chacra de Perdriel / Chacra Pueyrredón**, partido de General San Martín, on his mother's family's property, to **Rafael Hernández**, mayordomo on rural estates, and **Isabel de Pueyrredón**, dead when he is eight or nine. **[A: esw-JH; pueyrredon.com]** Raised partly by aunts and grandparents. **[A: biografiasyvidas.com]**
- **Coordinate contradiction, decided and flagged.** Four values: geography lens **-34.538, -58.562**; afterlife lens **-34.5473, -58.5511**; OSM/Nominatim on the present museum **-34.56217, -58.57250**; and the atlas pin already carried for the same chacra in `juan_martin_de_pueyrredon.journey.json`, **-34.5633, -58.5692**, which the journey uses, ~300 m from the museum geocode and 2.7 km from the geography lens.
- Baptized **27 July 1835**, **Basílica de Nuestra Señora de la Merced**, eight months and seventeen days after birth. **[A: esw-JH]** The parish register was **not consulted** and the pool calls the church identification traditional. The interval is noted by biographers and explained by none. **Gap.**
- **Barracas**, the boy left with his paternal grandfather **José Gregorio Hernández Plata** when the Pueyrredón side leaves for Montevideo: **[R]**, family tradition, no primary document, Cervantes Virtual returning 403.
- **Liceo Argentino de Pedro Sánchez, San Telmo, 1841-1845**, the whole of his formal schooling. **[A: esw-JH]** The tradition that Sánchez waived his fees is family testimony only, not carried.

## 2. The frontier, 1846-1852

Withdrawn from school for a chest ailment and sent to his father in the **Camarones / Laguna de los Padres** zone, where Rafael Hernández worked estates linked to **Juan Manuel de Rosas**. **[R: esw-JH carries the father's employment "en las estancias del gobernador Rosas"; the ranches are unlocated, so the pin is Laguna de los Padres.]** This is the most consequential undocumented stretch of the life: every biography treats it as formative and none can date a day of it. **Honest gap.**

## 3. Militia service, 1852-1856

[A: esw-JH]** No month; the entry defaults to 1 January with the anomaly stated in `date_confidence`, the enlistment necessarily following the 3 February battle. [A, upgraded from the pool's R.]** es.wikipedia: "Sólo muy pocos pudieron escapar, entre ellos el coronel Campos y el joven José Hernández." **Two corrections.** (a) The pool framed 1853 as a *successful repression* of Lagos; he was on the **defeated** side, Gregorio Paz having beaten Pedro Rosas y Belgrano. (b) The geography lens put the fight near **Bolívar (-36.2333, -61.1)**, ~300 km from the real site at the **mouth of the Río Salado, partido de Chascomús**. - **Batalla de El Tala, 8 November 1854.** Battle **[A: es.wikipedia]**; **his presence and lieutenancy [R]**, the battle article not mentioning him. The pool had tagged this A; **downgraded**. Pin moved from San Pedro town to the Río Tala vicinity, ~**-33.79, -59.79**.
- **La Reforma Pacífica, from 1 December 1856. [A: esw-JH]** **Standing gap:** no street address was located for any newspaper, press, lodge, the Liceo, the hotel, or the legislature; those stops carry city-centre pins and say so in `date_confidence`.

## 4. Paraná and the Confederation, 1857-1863

- **Settles in Paraná. Contradiction decided and flagged.** esw-JH: "Radicado en Paraná desde **1857**." The pool's chronology lens carried **1858**, the year his signed contributions to *El Nacional Argentino* begin, while its geography lens said "1857-1858 onward". The journey uses 1857. Commerce, then administration, then **stenographer to the Confederation Congress**. **[A: esw-JH]**
- **Cepeda, 23 October 1859** and **Pavón, 17 September 1861**: battles **[A]**, his presence **[R]**, neither article naming him and no muster roll reached. Pins inherited byte-identical from `julio_argentino_roca.journey.json`.
- **Cañada de Gómez, 22 November 1861. [A]** es.wikipedia names him among those spared, notes the survivors were pressed into Mitre's army, and that they deserted. The strongest attestation in the file, and the one that most directly prefigures the poem. **"Vincha" in the Paraná press, 1860** and **secretary to Vice-President Pedernera, 1861**, carried in one stop. **[A: esw-JH]**
- **Freemasonry: contradiction flagged, the pool's A-tag corrected.** The geography lens carried **3 March 1865** from masonic sources it could not fetch. esw-JH: "El 28 de agosto de **1861**, ingresó en la Logia Masónica Asilo del Litoral (junto con Leonidas Echagüe y Teófilo García)," secretary the year after, corroborated by es.wikipedia, "Leonidas Echagüe"; and its note that he was proclaimed a free member for **25 years of militancy** shortly before his death points to 1861. The journey uses 1861; 1865 stands unrefuted. **Not adjudicated.**
- **El Litoral** (Evaristo Carriego's paper), 1862, with notices advertising him as a **bankruptcy syndic**: tagged A in the pool, carried at reduced confidence, resting on **Ortale's** unfetched study. **Named gap.**
- **Marriage to Carolina González del Solar, Paraná, 8 June 1863.** The pool tagged this R; two sources give the same date, so **[A]**. Church named nowhere. **Gap.** Eight children, 1865-1879, and **El Argentino founded mid-1863**. **[A: esw-JH]**
- **Rasgos biográficos del general D. Ángel V. Peñaloza** (later *Vida del Chacho*). **[A: esw-JH; biografiasyvidas]** Peñaloza was killed at **Olta, 12 November 1863**, so the pamphlet follows and the entry defaults to December. **Stop-ownership decision:** the lens proposed Olta (-30.6167, -66.2667) as a pin. **Declined**, he was never there and the killing is already staged in `sarmiento.journey.json`. The stop sits at Paraná.

## 5. Corrientes, the Paraguayan war, Rosario, 1864-1868

- **Nogoyá**, secretary to the Entre Ríos constitutional convention, and the April 1865 letter opening **"Chinita querida": [R]**, reported held by the Biblioteca Pública de las Misiones, unreachable. **Named gap.**
- **Paysandú, December 1864 to January 1865.** Siege **[A: es.wikipedia]**, which does **not** mention him. His proximity is **[R]** and disputed; nothing reached puts him inside the town. The stop is written as the open question the record leaves.

## 6. El Río de la Plata, 1869-1870

- Run from **Victoria 202**, today **Hipólito Yrigoyen 202**. **[A: esw-JH]** **Founding date not established**: the pool's chronology gives 1868 (R), its geography lens 1869-1870, and the journey defaults to 1 January 1869. **Coordinate corrected** from the lens's -34.6091, -58.3773, ~500 m west, to **-34.61, -58.372**.
- Programme: municipal autonomy, popular election of local officials, abolition of the frontier levy, sovereignty over the **Malvinas**. **[A for the first three: esw-JH. The Malvinas editorials come from the pool alone, never verified: named gap.]** The one verifiable editorial quotation, 19 August 1869, *"Nosotros no tenemos el derecho de expulsar a los indios del territorio y menos de exterminarlos..."* **[A: elhistoriador.com.ar]**, carries the flag that the article's title promises a Malvinas quotation and its body reproduces this instead.
- **Closed 22 April 1870, by Hernández himself, after Urquiza's assassination. [A: esw-JH]** This corrects the pool's flat "the paper closes," which omitted the agency. Urquiza was killed at the **Palacio San José, 11 April 1870**; Hernández was not there and no San José stop was created.

## 7. The rising, exile, and the poem, 1870-1875

- **Ñaembé, 26 January 1871. [A: es.wikipedia, "entre sus compañeros se encontraba el escritor José Hernández"]** Pin inherited byte-identical from `julio_argentino_roca.journey.json` (**-29.2, -59.3**) rather than the geography lens's -29.1382, -59.2662, so both files put the battle in one place.
- **Exile at Santana do Livramento, April 1871 to early 1872. [A: esw-JH]** **Gran Hotel Argentino, 1872.** Named by esw-JH in connection with the composition **[A]**; the **butcher's paper or pulpería ledger** detail is **[R]** folklore, no manuscript or hotel register located either way. ## Apparatus relations for the operator

José Hernández is **not** listed in `EXCEPTIONS.md`. Two relations in the pool are **not rooted in his own life** and were therefore not staged as scenes. Neither was silently deleted. Both are **forward-direction** catches: a later person engaging with his work, which a file whose clock stopped in 1886 cannot embed as a pointer.
