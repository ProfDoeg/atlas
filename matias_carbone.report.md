# Matías Carbone: research report
*Buenos Aires textile designer; brand "Carbone." Compiled 2026-08-13 for the Atlas of Journeys.*

**What this file is.** A phase-by-phase account of everything reachable about Matías Carbone, with each claim marked
**[A]** (attested, source named) or **[R]** (reconstruction, tradition, inference, or single-source self-description).
Contradictions between sources are flagged where they sit and are not adjudicated. Gaps are printed as gaps.

**The shape of the evidence, stated up front.** Carbone is documented in two kinds of source and no third kind.
The first is the essay corpus itself: two Instagram posts of 2023, both written by the author, in which Carbone is a
person in a room. The second is his own commercial web presence: ccarbone.com.ar and the verified Instagram account
@matiascarbone, both written by the brand about the brand. Independent press, exhibition catalogues, academic
treatment, and encyclopedic coverage were searched for and not found. A LinkedIn snippet and a Facebook page
corroborate exactly one biographical fact (the 1999 school). Everything else in his professional chronology rests on
a single self-authored page.

## 2. Training and the modelling years (1999 - c. 2003)

- **Begins fashion studies in 1999.** **[A: ccarbone.com.ar "About Carbone"; independently repeated in a LinkedIn
  snippet at ar.linkedin.com/in/matias-carbone-417a0877, "Matías Carbone inició sus estudios de moda en 1999 en la
  Universidad ABM", and on facebook.com/matiascarbonedes]** This is the only fact in his biography confirmed by a
  source that does not derive from the brand site.
- **The institution.** **[A/R]** The brand's wording is "Universidad ABM." The school at **Marcelo T. de Alvear 1155**
  is *ABM Instituto de Educación Superior en Diseño*, granting a three-year *Diseño de Indumentaria* degree. It is not
  a university. **Flagged, not resolved:** the brand's own word for its founder's school is not the school's own word
  for itself.
  - *Coordinate correction, recorded:* an earlier pass placed ABM at -34.595 / -58.3931 and glossed the barrio as
    "Barrio Norte/Recoleta." Nominatim resolves Marcelo T. de Alvear 1155 to approximately **-34.5967 / -58.3818,
    Retiro, Comuna 1**, about a kilometre away. The corrected pin is used.
  - "Asociación Biblioteca de Mujeres" is a real body founded 1903; whether the design institute is that body is
    **[R]**, not established here.
- **First industry post: the house of Paula Cahen D'Anvers.** **[R: ccarbone.com.ar only]** No dates, no address, no
  second source. Palermo is a *placement*, not a finding.
- **Modelling in France and Japan.** **[R: ccarbone.com.ar only]** The page names two countries and nothing else: no
  city, no agency, no client, no year. An earlier pass tagged Paris and Tokyo **[A]**; that is an overstatement and is
  demoted here. The city pins are researcher placeholders standing for nations, and the journey file says so in each
  `date_confidence`.
  - Also struck: the earlier gloss that Japan gave him "an early exposure to Japanese textile and tailoring culture
    that recurs later in the brand's seasonal collections." No source says this. It was invention.

## 5. The studio in Belgrano (30 January 2023)

Source: `/home/drdoeg/essays/instagram/2023-01-31-ayer-fui-al-estudio-de-matias.md`. The corpus is locked; nothing here
edits it.

- **Date.** **[A: the essay's own wording]** The post is dated 2023-01-31 and opens "Ayer fui al estudio de Matias
  Carbone en Belgrano" (line 5). The visit therefore falls on **2023-01-30**. Earlier passes carried 2023-01-31 as the
  visit date in the chronology lens and 2023-01-30 in the interlock lens; the essay decides it.
- **The invitation was anonymous.** **[A: essay]**
- **Techniques: knit, crochet, weaving, embroidery.** **[A: essay]** Motifs: the geometry of the circle and the
  emblem of the dog. **[A: essay]**
- **Conversation: textile construction, knots, nodes, networks, "la Pasha," and the dog symbolism.** **[A: essay]**
- **The etymology.** **[A as reported speech; the etymology itself partly wrong]** Latin *nodus* < PIE **ned*, "to
  bind," is correct (Etymonline). Of the Spanish words the essay lists, *nudillo*, *nudo* and *nodo* descend from
  *nodus*; **desnudo does not**; it is from Latin *nudus*, which Etymonline assigns to PIE **nogʷ-* "naked" (Greek
  *gymnos*, Sanskrit *nagna*). The false cognate is **flagged and left standing**, as the corpus is verbatim.
  - A separate error entered only in the *English* rendering of a later summary, which tracked "knuckle, naked, knot,
    node" to the same root. English *knot* is OE *cnotta* < Proto-Germanic **knuttan-*, and *knuckle* is likewise
    Germanic; neither belongs to **ned-*. Recorded so it does not propagate.
- **Indra's net** invoked as a historical frame for knotted mesh construction, applied to one of Carbone's embroidered
  knit shirts. **[A: essay]**
- **Anubis and the funerary knot.** **[A as essay content; R as claim]** Anubis is externally attested as god of
  embalming and mummification. **No Egyptological source found makes him a specialist in knotting the funerary body.**
  The knot is the essayist's reading. Earlier passes tagged the whole item **[A]**; the interpretive half is demoted
  here.
- **The visit ends before an embroidered Anubis in a work titled "Christophia."** **[A: essay]** Verbatim:
  *"Terminamos nuestra conversación mirando la representación bordada de Anubis en la obra titulada Christophia
  presentada por Laura Renée Maier."* **Correction:** *presentada por* means presented by, not made by. An earlier pass
  attributed the work's authorship to Maier. That goes beyond the source and is withdrawn.
- **"No puedo entender desenredar los hilos de este nudo Gordiano."** **[A: essay]** The author links the piece to his
  own "skyrmion" textile imagery.

## 6. The sun dog (22 May 2023)

Sources: `2023-05-22-katrin-vates-matiascarbone-dogecoin-bourlemont-sotah.md` and its English variant
`2023-05-22-sin-titulo.md`.

- **A finished winter collection whose signature material is a Shiba Inu**, shown running, chasing birds, gazing
  skyward, and "guarding a secret"; the essay calls it a sun dog. **[A: essay]** Whether the garments were exhibited
  or sold is **not established** anywhere.
- **Sotah 49b.** **[A: Sefaria: פְּנֵי הַדּוֹר כִּפְנֵי הַכֶּלֶב, "The face of the generation will be like the face of
  a dog"]** **Dating flagged:** the essay dates the line to the 5th century. The dictum is **mishnaic** (Sotah 9:15),
  tannaitic material of roughly the 2nd-3rd century CE, quoted inside a Babylonian Talmud redacted c. 500-600. The
  5th-century date is the essayist's and is attributed to him.
- **The linkage running Sotah to Dogecoin to Carbone is the essayist's own frame.** **[R]** Not corroborated outside the corpus.
- **La Verna and the Bourlémont thread.** **[A as essay content; R as genealogy]** The post sets Carbone's dog
  collection beside Ekaterina Sinchinova's embroidery revealing La Verna, refuge of Francis of Assisi, stitched over a
  Dogecoin blockchain image bearing the sun dog's face. The Francis-to-Bourlémont link runs through his mother, named
  in late tradition **Pica di Bourlemont**, "about whom little is known except that she was a noblewoman originally
  from Provence": poorly attested, and a *Provençal* family distinct from the Lorraine Bourlémont of Domrémy that
  anchors the corpus's Joan of Arc thread. See `bourlemont_roster.md`, secondary flag.
- **Name discrepancy, flagged:** the English variant of the same post calls the artist **"Ekaterina Vates"**, not
  Sinchinova. Both forms are in the corpus.
- **Handles named in the two posts:** @matiascarbone, @laurareneemaier, @katrin.vates. **[A]**

## 9. Contradictions carried forward, not resolved

1. **Two addresses.** Belgrano studio (essay, Jan 2023) vs Av. Alvear 1807, Recoleta (brand site + Instagram bio, the
   standing address). Roughly 8 km apart. Nothing dates a move; a working studio and a showroom need not be the same
   room. **Both pins are kept.**
2. **Belgrano coordinate, internally.** The afterlife lens gave -34.5631 / -58.4547; the geography lens gave
   -34.5627 / -58.4583 for the same studio, ~330 m apart. Both fall inside Belgrano. **The journey file uses
   -34.5631 / -58.4547 throughout**, because that is the pin `laura_renee_maier.journey.json` already carries, and the
   atlas prefers an existing pin to a new one.
3. **Visit date.** 2023-01-31 (post date, used by the chronology and afterlife lenses) vs 2023-01-30 (the essay's own
   "ayer," used by the interlock lens and by Maier's file). **Resolved in favour of the essay's wording**, and the
   resolution is recorded as a resolution.
4. **"Universidad ABM"** (brand's wording) vs *ABM Instituto de Educación Superior en Diseño* (an institute, not a
   university). Not adjudicated.
5. **Paris Fashion Week: corroborated or not.** One pass said no; a later pass said yes by citing the brand's own page
   as a second source for the brand's own claim. Held at **[R]**, and the reasoning is printed.
6. **"Ekaterina Sinchinova" vs "Ekaterina Vates"** across the Spanish and English variants of the 2023-05-22 post.
7. **Nodus concordance arithmetic.** An earlier pass called the studio post unmatched "among the 52 Instagram posts."
   Conclusion right, number wrong: `_nodus_match.md` lists it at line 329 under "Instagram essays NOT in the nodus
   corpus," and its header reads "All 52 nodus essays matched. They draw on 142 of the 331 Instagram essay-posts."
   **52 counts essays.** The post is not among the **142** Instagram posts those essays draw on.
