# Katja Wulff (Käthe Wulff), 1890-1992: research report
*Compiled 2026-08-31 for the Atlas of Journeys. Slug `katja_wulff`. Companion: `katja_wulff.journey.json`, 9 segments, 43 stops.*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named · **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or single-source claim I could not reach. Contradictions are flagged and left open; unreachable sources are named with the reason.

**The central honest fact.** The only accessible source of any substance is the German Wikipedia article **'Katja Wulff'**, which is short. Everything more detailed (the Cologne finding aid, the HLS entry, Meyer's 1990 interview volume, Arp's 1955 memoir) was **unreachable in this pass**, so claims resting on those are [R] however confidently the secondary literature repeats them. Wulff's biography as commonly retold rests on an archive nobody here could open.

## 1. Hamburg, 1890-1915

- Born **31 August 1890, Hamburg**, to Hugo Friedrich Wulff and Marie Elisabetha Amalia Wulff née Döring. **[A: de.wikipedia; Wikidata Q43267718]**
- **Zeichen- und Turnlehrerin** (drawing and gymnastics teacher) in Hamburg, **1912-1915**. **[A: de.wikipedia]**
- **Claim stripped.** The pool asserted a *Gewerbeschule*, diplomas 1912 and 1914, certificates 1914 and 1916. **None is in the cited source**, which says only "liess sich in Hamburg zur Zeichen- und Turnlehrerin ausbilden und unterrichtete von 1912 bis 1915." It may come from the Cologne finding aid; that is **HTTP 404**. Not carried as fact.
- Dance study under **Gertrud Falke**, **1913/14**. **[A: de.wikipedia]** The lifelong correspondence and shared photo album are **[R]**, finding-aid description only.

**Gap:** no schooling record, no address, no sibling names beyond the unnamed sister of the 1917 letter.

## 2. Monte Verità, summer 1914

- Attended **Laban's summer course on Monte Verità above Ascona, 1914**. **[A: de.wikipedia]**
- **Correction.** The pool called it **'Tanz, Ton, Wort'**. de.wikipedia calls it only "einen Ferienkurs beim Tanztheoretiker und Ausdruckstänzer Rudolf von Laban auf dem Monte Verità," and de.wikipedia 'Monte Verità' names his institution there the *Schule für Kunst*. 'Tanz, Ton, Wort' is the triad of his later **Zürich** school, retro-applied. Not used.
- **Meisenbach** photographed the group that season; Wulff is the figure in the **orange dress**; later captions misplace it at **Lake Lugano**. **[A: stadtgeschichtebasel.ch]**
- **The rucksack and the storage box. [R]** From **Preston-Dunlop, *Rudolf Laban: an Extraordinary Life* (2008), p. 35**, seen only in Montabord's **French rendering**. No contemporaneous document. Carried as tradition, saying so in its own confidence field.
- **War scatters the colony, August 1914. [A]** for the event; that Wulff was still on the hill is **[R]**: neither `ida_hoffmann` nor `laban` names her.

## 3. Zürich, 1916-1919

- Entered **Laban's Schule für Bewegungskunst, 1916**, under **Laban** and **Mary Wigman**, alongside **Suzanne Perrottet**. **[A: de.wikipedia 'Katja Wulff', corroborated by de.wikipedia 'Suzanne Perrottet', which names Wulff with Perrottet and Wigman as Laban's key collaborators]**
- **Diploma, 1918**, *tänzerisches Turnen und Kunsttanz*. **[A: de.wikipedia]**
- **Schule für Eurythmie with Perrottet. [A] that it happened. Contradiction flagged:** de.wikipedia dates it **1918**, `suzanne_perrottet.journey.json` **September 1919**, calling its own date inferred. Neither is hard-attested to the month. The file uses 1918 and states the conflict.
- **Dada participation.** Wulff at the **Cabaret Voltaire** and the **Galerie Dada** is **[A]** through three atlas files that name her (`laban`, `suzanne_perrottet`, `mary_wigman`), resting on Hugo Ball's *Flight Out of Time* and the Doerr and Manning monographs. **de.wikipedia 'Dada' does not mention her**, a real limit, stated in the stops.
- **The 'Heldin Dadas' episode. [R], downgraded from the pool's [A].** At the **Kaufleuten** she read Arp's poems from under a large **fiery-red paper bag** and kept reading as hostile spectators moved on her; **Arp** later called her the *Heldin Dadas*. Source: **Arp, 'Dadaland', in *Unsern täglichen Traum* (Zürich: Arche, 1955)**, written thirty-six years after, **not retrieved**. The pool tagged it [A] because **HLS 044712** repeats the phrase; **HLS 044712 returns HTTP 403** on every path and form tried, so that corroboration is **not established**. **Staging corrected:** the pool put the venue on Talstrasse at 47.3705/8.5389, "circa 1919"; the Kaufleuten is **Pelikanstrasse 18** (47.3718/8.5364) and the eighth Dada soirée is **9 April 1919**.
- **The hair letter, 1917. [A]** Fragment quoted by stadtgeschichtebasel.ch, cut off mid-word: *"runter ist; es ist sogar so, dass ich mich besser bewege. Ausserdem geht das kurze Haar, so wie ich es nun trage, gut zu einem grossen Teil meines Wesens."*
- **Sang an die Sonne, 18 August 1917. [R] for Wulff.** The festival is attested (`laban.journey.json`; de.wikipedia 'Sang an die Sonne'); later scholarship places her among Laban's Monte Verità dancers but **no cast list giving her a role was located**.
- **Teaching Vera Skoronel, Easter 1919. [A]** Two atlas files independently state that Skoronel, twelve, was taught at Zürich by **Perrottet and Wulff**: `vera_skoronel.journey.json` (1919-04-20) and `engelmann.journey.json` (1919-04-01). Best evidence that Wulff was a **teacher** there, not only a student.

**Gap:** no O.T.O. or Masonic affiliation is evidenced. `theodor_reuss.journey.json` puts the 1917 Anational Congress on the same hill in the same week; proximity only, not in her file.

## 5. Basel: the school, 1923-1939

- **Ausdruckstanz school founded in Basel, 1923**, the **Basler Labanschule** / **Wulffschule**. **[A: de. and en.wikipedia]** The Basel-because-the-other-cities-were-taken reasoning: **[R]**.
- Two posters, **"Körperbildung, Bewegungskunst, Labanschule Käthe Wulff"**, Plakatsammlung Tanzarchiv Leipzig, UB Leipzig, **NL 408/1608-1609**. **[R: Kalliope listing per the pool, endpoint HTTP 400]**
- **From 1926, Tanzstudio Wulff**, with **Mariette von Meyenburg** choreographing most of the repertory, and collaboration from 1926 with **Paul Sacher, Max Bill, Meret Oppenheim**. **[A: de. and en.wikipedia]** **Friedrich Glauser** inscribed a poem *"für Kathia"*, 1926. **[R: Cologne finding aid, 404; the poem's text is reproduced nowhere reached]**
- **Stadttheater Basel, 27 February 1927. [R]** Casella with **Sacher's** chamber orchestra plus **Barabau** to **Rieti**, barefoot. Date and programme from background material said to derive from Basel city-history and Tanzarchiv records, **not retrieved**. **Coordinate corrected:** the pool's 47.5556/7.5886 lands near Barfüsserplatz; the 1875-1975 Stadttheater stood on the **Steinenberg**, ~**47.5535/7.5903**.

- **Magdeburg 1927 [R], contradiction flagged, not resolved.** A congress card and a photograph of the Wulffschule's stand are claimed from the Cologne finding aid (404); de.wikipedia's window excludes 1927; the Kalliope record cited in support (**DE-611-BF-120807**) says nothing about Magdeburg. **Kept, and kept as [R].**
- **SAFFA, Bern, 1928. [A: de.wikipedia]** 26 Aug to 30 Sep 1928. **Coordinate corrected** from Bern city centre to the **Viererfeld**, ~**46.9584/7.4373** (de.wikipedia, 'Schweizerische Ausstellung für Frauenarbeit').
- **Hyspa, Bern, 1931. [R]** Finding aid via the pool; unverified, grounds not located, Bern coordinate flagged.

### A quote removed for misattribution

The pool carried under Wulff's name *« c'était comme si j'arrivais à la maison ! [...] et je sus que j'appartenais à ce lieu »*. **These are Mary Wigman's words.** They stand in Montabord immediately after the Wulff line as the second of two dancer testimonies, and footnote [14] reads: *"Wigman citée dans Laure Guilbert, La naissance de la danse moderne en Allemagne, mémoire de DEA, IEP Paris, 1990, p. 37."* The pool collapsed footnotes 13 and 14. **Removed. It belongs to `mary_wigman.journey.json`** with the Guilbert citation. Also for the record: *"Quand vous pensez, ressentez et écrivez sur quelque chose, vous voulez le montrer"* is Wulff's, but from **p. 99**, and the pool's note that "the essay gave no page number" is wrong. (Not used in the journey file.)
