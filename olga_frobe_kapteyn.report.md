# Olga Fröbe-Kapteyn (1881-1962): research report
*Prepared 2026-08-28 for `olga_frobe_kapteyn.journey.json`. Founder, convener and editor of the Eranos conferences at Ascona-Moscia; founder of the Eranos Archive for Research in Symbolism; painter of the Meditation Plates and the Visions.*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or single-source claim no second source confirms. Contradictions are flagged, not adjudicated.

The indispensable modern source is **Chloë Sugden, "Embodying the Great Mother: Olga Fröbe's Spiritual Authority at Eranos," *Approaching Religion* 15, no. 2 (Dec 2025): 89-110**, open access, from an ETH Zurich dissertation, citing unpublished correspondence in the C. G. Jung Papers, the Cary Baynes Archive and the Eranos Foundation Archives. Bare page numbers below are hers, and several correct the numbers the intake dossier carried.

## 1. London and Zürich, 1881-1909

**Birth [A: en.wikipedia; aras.org, ARAS Connections 2020 issue 4].** Born Olga Kapteyn in London, eldest of three, to two Dutch parents.

- **Day contested, flagged.** English sources give **19 October 1881**, some German ones **18 October**. Unresolved; carried in `date_confidence`, not picked.
- **Father, corrected.** Albertus Philippus Kapteyn moved to London in 1881 for the **Westinghouse Air Brake Company** and was Director-General of the London site by 1887 [A: en.wikipedia, verbatim]. The dossier said "Westinghouse Brake & Signal Company," a firm formed by merger only in **1920**.
- **Mother, corrected.** Geertruida "Truus" Muysken Kapteyn (1855-1920) is "a feminist and social activist" (en.wikipedia), a *Frauen- und Lebensreformerin* (de.wikipedia). **No source calls her a writer**, as the dossier did.
- **Stop dropped, with reason.** The geography lens pinned a Westinghouse office at 51.5155, -0.0922, which is Aldgate and matches no attested Westinghouse address. The employment is folded into the birth campa rather than pinned to an invented address.

**School [A: en.wikipedia].** North London Collegiate School; reported schoolfellow of Marie Stopes.

- **Coordinate corrected.** The lens gave 51.539, -0.1425, Camden Town station. NLCS occupied **Sandall Road** (approx. **51.5465, -0.1346**) from 1879 until the 1929 Canons Park move.
- **Claim dropped, recorded so it is not lost.** The lens attached "family darkroom work with her photographer father," cited to en.wikipedia. It is **not in that article** and no source for it was found. It would matter if sourced: it would make her photostat habit a childhood one.

**Zürich [A: aware; en.wikipedia].** Family relocated by the end of the century; Truus kept a reform circle; Olga skied and climbed. **[R]:** the dossier's link from Truus's salon to **Shaw** and **Kropotkin** is confirmed by nothing reachable and is not asserted.

**Training.** Kunstgewerbeschule from **1900** [A: aware; aras]; art history at the **University of Zürich, 1906-1909** [A]; **no degree established, and none denied**. **Coordinate flagged as placeholder:** the Ausstellungsstrasse building (now Museum für Gestaltung) **opened in 1933** and cannot be the 1900 site, which is not established anywhere; the pool's 47.3823/8.5309 is neither building.

## 2. Marriage and widowhood, 1909-1915

**Marriage 1909 [A: en.wikipedia; aware]** to **Iwan Hermann Fröbe**, flautist and conductor. **Nationality contested, flagged:** en.wikipedia says "Croatian-Austrian flutist and conductor," de.wikipedia says Austrian, of **Slovenian** descent, 1880-1915. Unreconciled; the file states the disagreement.

**German years [R].** Braunschweig, Munich, Berlin by c. 1910; the sequence varies between short biographies and no document fixes it. **Gersau [R]:** one mention, in aras.org, with no dates, house or duration.

**Berlin, the Nadel circle, 1911 [A, upgraded from the dossier].** AWARE states it outright: collaboration with the philologist **André Jolles** on the *Nadel* sewing circle, founded 1911, garments after classical Greek models, which Olga embroidered. The chronology lens tagged it [R] while the geography lens tagged the identical fact [A] from that same source.

**Table Ronde, Zürich [A for the salon, R for 1914].** en.wikipedia names the salon and de.wikipedia independently records a literary salon in Zurich during the war; the lenses again disagreed, and only the starting year is weak.

**Twins, May 1915 [A: en.wikipedia].** Place probable, not documented; no names anywhere.

**Fischamend, September 1915 [A: en.wikipedia, verbatim "a plane crash in September 1915 in Fischamend near Vienna"]. Correction:** the dossier hedged Fischamend itself as unconfirmed. It is confirmed. What is unconfirmed is the widely repeated detail that he was **testing an aerial camera for the Austro-Hungarian military**: no source consulted, English or German, mentions a camera. The hedge now covers the camera alone.

## 3. Ascona, 1919-1932

**Monte Verità, 1919 [A: aware; aras; en.wikipedia].** A cure with her father, at a hill past its colony phase and passing into sanatorium operation.

**Casa Gabriella, Moscia [A for the fact; year and transaction contested].** **Year:** de.wikipedia says **1919**, immediately after the cure, AWARE and en.wikipedia say **1920**; the chronology lens asserted a flat 1920 [A] where the geography lens correctly wrote "1919/1920." Both are carried. **Transaction:** accounts differ over whether her father bought it for her or she bought it from an ophthalmologist named Hoffmann with paternal inheritance [A that they differ].

**THE COORDINATE PROBLEM, and how it was resolved.** The largest defect in the intake pool; it propagated through six stops.

- Geography lens: Casa Gabriella at **46.1503, 8.7746** = Via Albarelle, Ascona, 1.6-1.8 km **east** of Moscia, which lies **west** toward Ronco (reused for the study, Spiritual Centre, Visions and death stops); Casa Eranos at **46.1508, 8.7750** = Via della Torre, the town centre (reused for the conference and editorial stops). Afterlife lens: **the same building on the same date** at **46.1547, 8.7639**, on the Monte Verità slope. Two lenses, two wrong pins for one house, 900 m apart.
- Independent check: the Eranos Foundation's address is **Via Moscia 125, 6612 Ascona**, and swisstopo and OSM address points for that street run between 8.740 and 8.767 of longitude. A defensible value is about **46.1518, 8.7528**.
- **The file uses 46.146, 8.755**, the canonical corpus pin inherited byte-identical from `jung.journey.json` and shared with `engelmann`, `antoinette_de_saint_leger`, `ida_hoffmann`, `arnold_rikli`: about 700 m from the derived value and on the correct side of Ascona. **Flag for the operator: if the Moscia pin is ever corrected, correct it corpus-wide in one pass, not here.**

**Seven years of study, c. 1920-27 [R].** Single source (aras.org, ARAS Connections 2020 issue 4, not re-fetchable this pass), as are the **I Ching** encounter and the **Richard Wilhelm** meeting, which appear in nothing readable.

**Keyserling's School of Wisdom [A for contact, R for the Jung meeting].** Contact in the 1920s [A: en.wikipedia]. That she **met Jung at Darmstadt around 1930** is institutional memory, confirmed by nothing. Corpus corroboration for the school and its overlapping personnel: `martin_buber.journey.json` (Buber there in 1922, with Jung, Tagore and Richard Wilhelm named) and `keyserling.journey.json`. **Marburg [R]:** one brief mention, nothing establishing a visit rather than correspondence.

**Casa Eranos built, 1928 [A: Tevebring; en.wikipedia].** Purpose-built hall, first conceived as an "ashram." **Rudolf Otto** supplied the name [A: en.wikipedia]. **Meditation Plates, c. 1926-34 [A: Sugden 89-94].**

**Alice Bailey, 1930-32 [A: Sugden 91].** She visited Bailey in Connecticut in 1930 and proposed a non-sectarian centre; they co-directed a school on the property for three summers, closing in 1932 over personal conflict [A: Tevebring; en.wikipedia].

- **Naming contradiction, flagged.** Sugden's archival source is headed **"Report of First Session, INTERNATIONAL CENTER FOR SPIRITUAL RESEARCH"** (HS1016:299 49, ETH Zürich); en.wikipedia calls it the **"School of Spiritual Research"**; "Spiritual Centre" is **Sugden's descriptive phrase**, mistaken by the dossier for the body's name. All three appear in the campa.
- **Coordinate corrected.** The lens gave **41.3, -73.4**, open country near Redding, associated with nobody. Bailey's base was **Stamford** (approx. **41.05, -73.54**). Sugden gives only the state, so the town is genuinely unestablished and the pin says so.

**Die Verkündigung, 1932 [A: Sugden, fig. 3 caption].** **Medium corrected:** not a screenprint. The caption reads *"Tempera, India ink and gold leaf on cardboard. Item no. 0.35BIBO, Folder 35002, C. G. Jung-Institut Bildarchiv, Küsnacht."* The dossier appears to have merged it with the Met's separate **Theosophical Prints** (ca. 1930), which are colour silkscreen.

## 4. Eranos, 1933-1961

**First conference, August 1933 [A: Sugden 90; aware; en.wikipedia]**, theme *Yoga und Meditation im Osten und im Westen*. `jung.journey.json` fixes **1933-08-07** and the pin **46.146, 8.755** and already names her as convener; this file's stop is written from her side, so as not to restage Jung's scene.

**The yearbooks [A: en.wikipedia, "the first 30 Eranos Yearbooks (1933-1961)"; Rhein-Verlag confirmed II/1934 to XX/1951 in Sugden's bibliography]. Citation corrected:** the dossier cited "Fröbe 1935" for the first volume, but that is the Vorwort to *Eranos-Jahrbuch II/1934*, published 1935, the **second** yearbook. The **first** (I/1933) appeared in **1934**. HathiTrust, cited in the dossier for the editorship, returned **HTTP 403**.

**Eranos Archive founded 1934 [A: Sugden 92].** Over 6,000 core images by the late 1940s. **Jung's commissions 1934-38 [A: aware; Sugden 92]:** Küsnacht's Bildarchiv holds roughly 6,500 copies.

**Crete, 1934 [R].** A trance she called a *psychosomatic collapse*, reported in her own 1938 letters to Cary Baynes and Dion Fortune. **Sugden treats it as self-fashioning, not verified event** (92, 96), and no source names the site; Knossos is a declared marker.

**The Visions [A: Sugden 89, 92, 100]. Count sharpened:** Sugden 100, *"The 315 Visions drawings span twelve albums bound in blue."* **315**, not "over 300." **Range contested:** the abstract says 1934-37, the body text 1934-38, dated sheets run March 1934 to June 1937. **Claim corrected:** the dossier said Jung warned her about identification with archetypal forces; Sugden documents no such warning, but does document **Fröbe herself** writing of the threshold of "archetypal possession."

**Warburg approached 1936 [A: Tevebring]. Coordinate corrected, anachronism removed:** the lens gave **51.522, -0.1291**, Woburn Square, where the Institute moved only in **1958**. Through both the 1936 approach and the mid-1950s transfer it was in the **Imperial Institute Buildings, South Kensington** (1937-58; Thames House 1934-37), approx. **51.4995, -0.1745**. Used for both stops.

**1938, the Great Mother year [A: Sugden 91, 93]. The most consequential correction:** the dossier said she "delivered or organized" *Kult der Magna Mater* and *Die gnostische Vorstellung der Sophia*. She **organized**. Sugden's fn. 22 lists these as **titles on the 1938 programme**, papers by invited speakers; her own contribution is the **Vorwort** to *Eranos-Jahrbuch V/1938*. **She is nowhere recorded as an Eranos lecturer in twenty-eight years.** The file states this outright.

**The Mellons [A for the introduction: Tevebring; R for the rest].** Introduced during a Mellon visit to Switzerland in the late 1930s. Her hope that Bollingen or Paul Mellon would buy the property and keep her as custodian, and its collapse with Mary Mellon's death in 1946, rests on **Hakl (2013) and McGuire (1989), both HTTP 403** and unread.

**The winters in the libraries [A: en.wikipedia for the institutions; Tevebring; Sugden 92, 97, 99].** Acropolis Museum (a gorgoneion photostat filed under *Die Grosse Mutter: Die Gorgo*), Bibliothèque nationale, Vatican Library, British Museum, Morgan Library.

- **Coordinate corrected.** The lens gave **48.8656, 2.3212**, the Tuileries area, 1.25 km off. In the 1930s-40s the BnF meant **Richelieu**, 58 rue de Richelieu, **48.8673, 2.3382**. Tolbiac opened in 1996.
- **Units corrected.** Sugden 99: *"dedicated twelves [sic] BOXES of the Archive's eighty-four FOLDERS."* Different units; the dossier read twelve **of** eighty-four. And the 1948 statement says **"for the past five years"**, c. 1943-48, not "decades."

**Wartime [A: Sugden 91, 98].** The letter to Ximena de Angulo-Roelli of **21 September 1941** (PP/CBA/C.1, Wellcome) carries both the *"typical synthetic creation of the new age"* line and the filing instructions. **Date corrected:** the dossier put the "new age" line in **1939** and called it pre-war; it is **1941** and wartime, and Sugden glosses it as "a beacon amid the surrounding darkness of war." **Place corrected:** the dossier staged *"cross-indexing is quite hopeless"* and the folder instructions **at the Warburg Institute**, but in 1941 the Archive was still in her house; both are staged at Moscia. **Truncation flagged:** the folder quotation stopped mid-sentence with a period, where Sugden's fn. 15 continues *"...labelled perhaps Mythology, or Christianity, or pre-Christian, or Mother Cults, etc."* The ellipsis is restored.

## 5. Transfer, last years, death

**The Archive to the Warburg: date contradiction, re-weighted.** For **1946**, aras.org's history page alone, which the dossier and chronology lens followed and tagged [A]. For the **mid-1950s**, AWARE ("Mid-1950s"), Sugden 97 ("In the 1950s"), and Tevebring dating **Erich Neumann's** letter about the photographs leaving her house to **15 April 1955**. Three independent sources against one: the file centres the transfer on **c. 1955**, names 1946 as the competing claim, and proves neither. The afterlife lens compounded the error by misdating the Neumann letter itself to 1946.

**Thirty yearbooks through the 1961 cycle [A: en.wikipedia]. Death, 25 April 1962, Casa Gabriella, aged 80 [A: en.wikipedia; aware; aras; Sugden 89].**

- **Two honest negatives.** No source gives a **cause of death**. No source locates a **grave, tomb or marker of any kind**. The second is the louder: the death is fixed to the day and the body then leaves the record.
- **Succession, source corrected.** The afterlife lens cited Tevebring for **Adolf Portmann** succeeding her; **Tevebring does not say that**, citing Portmann only as an author on Eranos in 1955. His leadership is standard in the literature (Hakl 2013, unread) and is carried on that basis. **Rudolf Ritsema** is named her successor by `census_real_persons_2026-08-02.md`, line 125.

## 7. Apparatus relations for the operator

**None found, in either direction.** She is not listed in `EXCEPTIONS.md`, and nothing in the pool required a framework applied outside her own life. She had no framework: Sugden's article is built on the observation that she exercised authority by hosting, curating, archiving and drawing rather than by doctrine.

**Names in campas.** `jung`, direct and continuous: lectured from 1933, commissioned iconographic research, corresponded for three decades. `keyserling`: she made contact with his School of Wisdom in the 1920s, and his gathering is the structural precedent for hers. `martin_buber` and `wolfgang_pauli`: both lectured at Eranos at her invitation and were edited by her into the yearbooks. **Not** named, the relation being shared geography only: `ida_hoffmann` (her colony predates the 1919 cure, no meeting attested), `antoinette_de_saint_leger` (a few hundred metres along the same shore, no source puts her at Olga's table), `engelmann`, `arnold_rikli`, `hermann_hesse`.

**One thinner relation, flagged for judgement.** **Aby Warburg (`aby_warburg.journey.json`) is named once**, in the 1936 stop. She had no contact with Warburg, dead in 1929; the relation is that she deliberately placed her life's archive inside the institution he founded, whose Photographic Collection is the only European precedent for what she did alone at Moscia. Real and one-directional, but thinner than the other four names, and the operator may want it struck.

## 9. Standing gaps

1. **Where she was buried.** Nothing at all. The loudest silence in the file. **Cause of death:** nothing.
2. **Her daughters.** Twins born May 1915, and then nothing: no names, no trace, nothing about who raised them or whether they were in the house during the conference decades.
3. **The birth day**, 18 or 19 October 1881. **Whether she took a degree** at Zürich. **The sequence of German cities** in the marriage years. **The 1900 premises of the Kunstgewerbeschule**, and so that stop's real coordinate.
