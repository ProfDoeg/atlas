# Meyer Lansky, research report

*2026-08-10. Slug `meyer_lansky`. Maier Suchowljansky, b. Grodno 1902, d. Miami Beach 1983. Financier of the national crime syndicate; builder and loser of the Havana casino economy.*

**Legend.** **[A]** = attested, source named in the same sentence. **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition, or a claim the source itself hedges. Contradictions are printed and left open; unreachable sources are named with the reason.

**Method note.** The documentary base is thin for so famous a subject: the two encyclopedic sources disagree on the birthplace's modern country and on the month of death, **Lacey's *Little Man* was not read directly**, and two documentary cross-checks returned 404 (see Sources).

## 1. Grodno and the crossing, 1902-1911

- Born **Maier Suchowljansky, 4 July 1902, in Grodno**, a garrison town in the Pale of Settlement. **[A, Wikipedia]**
- **Contradiction, unresolved.** Wikipedia places Grodno in the Russian Empire, now **Belarus**. The **Jewish Virtual Library** gives "Grodno, Russia (now **Poland**)." The city is not in dispute; its modern sovereign is. Some genealogies also give **1901** for the birth year. **[R, flagged]**
- **Emigrated in 1911 via the port of Odessa** with his mother and brother Jacob; the father had gone ahead. **[A, Wikipedia]** The route is contested: other biographies route the family through Hamburg or England, and **no ship manifest is produced by any consulted source**. **[R]**
- **Ellis Island, downgraded to [R].** The geography lens tagged the landing as attested while its own text conceded the date "is not fixed in any source consulted." **No consulted source documents that Lansky was processed at Ellis Island.** The coordinate 40.6995, -74.0396 is right for the station; the inference that he passed through it is not. The journey carries the stop as reconstruction.

## 2. Hester Street to the Bugs and Meyer Mob, 1911-1929

- The family settled on the **Lower East Side**. **[A, Wikipedia]** After leaving school he was **apprenticed as a toolmaker**. **[A, Jewish Virtual Library]** No tenement address survives in any consulted source. **[R on the address]**
- **Met Siegel and Luciano around 1920. [A, Wikipedia; FBI "Famous Cases" summary via secondary extraction]** Accounts differ: some place the Siegel friendship in childhood and the Luciano alliance in his teens, after an attempted street extortion. Left open. **[R on the dating]**
- Through the 1920s he ran the **Bugs and Meyer Mob** with Siegel (bootlegging, hijacking, protection) in the orbit of **Joe Masseria** and **Arnold Rothstein**. **[A, FBI summary; Wikipedia]** The garage front is attested but **no address is recorded**. **[R on the address]**
- **Rothstein murdered in 1928. [A, Wikipedia]** The pool attests the year only. **Month, day, and site are outside the verified material**; the journey's coordinate is a midtown Manhattan marker, not a documented address, and `date_confidence` says so.
- **Married Anna Citron in 1929. [A, Wikipedia]** Three children, at least one with a disability whose medical costs strained him. Divorced **1946**.
- **Atlantic City, corrected.** The pool credited Lansky with *organizing* the 1929 conference. **[A, Wikipedia "Atlantic City Conference"]:** it ran **13 to 16 May 1929** at the **Ritz-Carlton and Ambassador hotels**; Lansky, Torrio, Luciano and Costello *planned* it, but the host was **Enoch "Nucky" Johnson**, who supplied lodging and police protection.

## 3. Laundering and the first Cuban foothold, 1932-1939

- **1932: proceeds begin moving into Swiss offshore accounts. [A, Wikipedia]** *Which* Zurich institutions is **not established anywhere consulted**. **[R on the institutions]**
- **1932, New Orleans:** Costello's slot-machine network under a tolerant state government, for which the Bugs and Meyer men had worked the Gulf as enforcers. **1936: operations in Florida, New Orleans, and Cuba. [A, Wikipedia; FBI summary]**
- **Saratoga Springs (Piping Rock Club)**, **Hot Springs (Southern Club)**, and **Hallandale, Florida (Colonial Inn**, with his brother Jake), through the 1930s and 1940s, protected by local machines. **[A, Lacey and Eisenberg et al. via Wikipedia]** No precise dates; the journey uses placeholder years.

**The Nacional / Oriental Park fusion, corrected.** The pool claimed Lansky took the **Hotel Nacional casino in 1937** *and* renovated **Oriental Park** for Batista, fusing two properties eighteen years apart.

- **Oriental Park is right. [A, Wikipedia "Oriental Park Racetrack"]:** "In 1937, gangster Meyer Lansky gained control of the racetrack and casino." A celebration marked the renovation in **1939**.
- **The Nacional half is wrong by about eighteen years.** Wikipedia's `Hotel Nacional de Cuba` places Lansky's casino there in the **mid-1950s**: Intercontinental took partial ownership September 1955, Lansky ran the **Casino Internacional** with his brother Jake and Wilbur Clark fronting, and the gaming wing "opened in 1956 with a performance by Eartha Kitt." The *Lansky* article's "as early as 1937" is **contradicted by the hotel's own article**, which the journey follows.
- **The gold keys are reversed.** The pool had Batista *presenting* keys to Lansky in 1939. **[A, Wikipedia]** states the opposite: **Batista received** them. The passage had built a political reading on the direction of the gift.
- **Coordinate corrected.** Oriental Park: 23.0754, -82.4487 was **3.0 km west** of the Marianao site. Wikipedia gives **23.073386, -82.419766**; the journey uses 23.0734, -82.4198.

## 4. Yorkville: a real quote, a fabricated date removed

- **The quote is real and traceable. [A]** Michael Feldberg, "How Jewish Gangsters Fought the Nazis," AJHS. The **ajhs.org URL is dead (404)**; full text is live at myjewishlearning.com. Feldberg credits the historian **Robert Rockaway**.
- **The pipeline's wording was Wikipedia's smoothed rendering, not Feldberg's.** Three fixes: "a picture of **Hitler**," not "Adolf Hitler"; "only **15** of us," not "fifteen"; and Feldberg's **three ellipses, which the pipeline silently closed up**, notably `We ... threw some of them out the windows`.
- **Verbatim, as the journey now carries it:** "The stage was decorated with a swastika and a picture of Hitler. The speakers started ranting. There were only 15 of us, but we went into action. We ... threw some of them out the windows...Most of the Nazis panicked and ran out. We chased them and beat them up...We wanted to show them that Jews would not always sit back and accept insults."
- **The date 1938-04-20 was fabricated and has been dropped.** Feldberg gives **no date at all**: no year, no April 20. That April 20 is Hitler's birthday makes the invented precision especially dangerous, since it reads as a deliberate choice by the participants that no source supports. The journey carries a placeholder year and says in `date_confidence` that no date is attested.
- **Place is confirmed.** Feldberg writes that Lansky recalled breaking up a Brown Shirt rally in the **Yorkville** section of Manhattan. No 86th Street address. A false sourcing claim is also removed: the pipeline said Wikipedia "reproduces the Feldberg piece in full." It cites it as footnote 23.
- **Second quote, one step short of primary. [A]** "I am a Jew, and I feel for the Jews in Europe who are suffering. They are my brothers." Lansky declining payment from **Judge Nathan D. Perlman**. Quoted in Michael Benson, *Gangsters Vs. Nazis*, confirmed via Howard Lovy in the *Jewish Telegraphic Agency*, 6 June 2022. **JTA does not name Benson's own source.**

## 5. The war, the Flamingo, the Conference, 1942-1948

- **1942, Operation Underworld.** Lansky is **reported** to have helped the Office of Naval Intelligence secure the New York waterfront, part of the arrangement leading to Luciano's release. **[A as a report, Wikipedia; the operational claim remains contested]** The journey pins the Third Naval District offices and marks the coordinate a reconstruction: **no source documents a meeting place.**
- **Havana Conference, 20 December 1946, Hotel Nacional. [A, Wikipedia "Havana Conference" and "Hotel Nacional de Cuba", which agree on the day]** Luciano organized it; **Lansky made the practical arrangements**. Agenda: Siegel's Flamingo overruns, narcotics routes, the Luciano and Genovese rivalry. The pool's undated 1946 is sharpened to the day.
- **The Flamingo: the popular sequence is wrong. [A, Wikipedia "Flamingo Las Vegas"]** Lansky financed it off the books, approved the **Nevada Projects Corporation**, and covered overruns past **six million dollars** (both check out). But "bad first weekend, shut within days" is legend. Sourced: **opened 26 December 1946; closed 6 February 1947**, about six weeks later; **reopened 1 March 1947**.
- **Siegel killed 20 June 1947, 810 North Linden Drive, Beverly Hills. [A, Wikipedia "Bugsy Siegel"]** Whether Lansky **ordered, tolerated, or failed to prevent** it is **disputed among biographers and not resolved here**.
- **Married Thelma Schwarz, 16 December 1948. [A, Wikipedia]** Two fixes: the surname is **Schwarz**, not Schwartz; and the picturesque **Havana lawyer's-office signing is not in the retrieved Wikipedia text**.
- **The Waldorf Astoria negotiation with Batista** (late 1940s) is **[R]**: widely cited, **date and room not documented**, only the outcome attested. Cut from the journey.

## 6. The Havana years, 1952-1958

- **After Batista's 10 March 1952 coup, Lansky was brought in as gambling advisor. [A, Wikipedia]** The densest interlock: coordinate **23.1136, -82.3666 inherited byte-identical** from `fidel_castro.journey.json`.
- **1955: Cuban gambling law altered** to permit casino licences in hotels worth **$1 million or more**; **1956: Casino Internacional at the Hotel Nacional** (see section 3 for the correction removing the 1937 claim). **[A, Wikipedia; "Hotel Nacional de Cuba"]**
- **Montmartre Club, Sevilla Biltmore, Hotel Internacional (Varadero):** casino interests through the 1950s, spread deliberately across properties. **[A on the operations, Wikipedia; Lacey]** **Three coordinates were wrong and are corrected:**
  - **Sevilla Biltmore**: 23.1378, -82.3556 was **450 m too far east**, out toward the bay rather than into the colonial core the passage describes. Now about **23.1406, -82.3596** (Trocadero 55, Habana Vieja).
  - **Hotel Internacional, Varadero**: 23.1382, -81.2775 was **3.5 km southwest of the beachfront and off the peninsula**, destroying the beach-plus-tables geography the passage was built on. Now about **23.154, -81.248**.
  - **Havana Riviera**: the two lenses disagreed with each other (23.1367, -82.3961 vs 23.1367, -82.3894) **and both sat some 800 m inland**, contradicting their own prose that the hotel opens on the Malecon. English and Spanish Wikipedia both give **23.1397, -82.404**, used now for both Riviera stops.
- **Havana Riviera opened 10 December 1957. [A, Wikipedia "Hotel Habana Riviera"]** Cost **US$8 million**; the casino "would make over $3 million in its first four months of operation." Opening performer **Ginger Rogers**, revue by **Jack Cole**. **31 December 1958, the last New Year's party** there is **[R, Lacey; the hour at which news of Batista's flight arrived is not independently sourced]**.

## 7. Exile, Israel, and the trial, 1959-1973

- **Fled Cuba for the Bahamas on 7 January 1959**, leaving an estimated **$17 million** in cash. **[A, Wikipedia]** **Contradiction noted:** other accounts describe a later, more gradual withdrawal over subsequent weeks. **Not resolved.** Through the 1960s **US law enforcement linked him to smuggling, pornography, prostitution, and extortion**, with FBI holdings estimates near **$300 million**. **[A, FBI summary via secondary extraction]**
- **Bahamas coordinates corrected.** **Nassau** (25.0782, -77.3389) sat 4 km northeast of Nassau proper, **nearly on top of the pool's own Paradise Island point** (25.0836, -77.3186), collapsing two entries the narrative treats as distinct. Now **25.048, -77.3554**. **Lucayan Beach** (26.5333, -78.6833) was a Freeport centroid 6 km inland of the beachfront the entry is about; now about **26.506, -78.630**. The Pindling backing is **[R]**: Wikipedia calls it **suspected, never proven in court**.
- **1970: fled to Herzliya Pituah, Israel**, seeking residency under the **Law of Return**. **[A, Wikipedia; Jewish Virtual Library]** **Coordinate corrected:** the afterlife lens gave 32.1624, **34.8442**, the town centre 4.2 km inland; the geography lens gave the correct coastal **32.1624, 34.7997**, now used in both.
- **1972: deported after roughly two years. [A, Wikipedia]** **Geography corrected by about 55 km:** the pool put the Supreme Court ruling in **Tel Aviv** (32.0853, 34.7818). **The court sits in Jerusalem**, at Givat Ram, **31.7808, 35.2036**, and never in Tel Aviv.
- **1973: acquitted of federal tax evasion**; other charges dropped citing poor health. **[A, Wikipedia; FBI summary]** **The absolute framing is corrected:** the gloss that "no American court ever secures a conviction against him that survives appeal" **overstates the record**. He **pleaded guilty to gambling charges in Saratoga County in 1953 and served roughly two months**, a conviction that stood, and was **convicted of criminal contempt in 1973**, overturned in 1974. Neither appears in the retrieved article, so the acquittal claim is source-accurate while the absolute claim built on it is not.
- **Vincent Teresa mischaracterized.** Wikipedia's `Meyer Lansky` calls him a loan shark, so the pipeline was faithful to its source; `Vincent Teresa` identifies him as a **Boston-based Patriarca family member** turned protected witness.

## 8. Death, estate, afterlife

- **Died of lung cancer in Miami Beach, aged 80.** **Direct contradiction, unresolved:** **Wikipedia gives 15 January 1983; the Jewish Virtual Library gives 15 May 1983.** The journey dates the stop to 15 January and prints the contradiction in `date_confidence`. Sources also differ on **hospital versus home [R]**. **Buried at Mount Nebo Memorial Gardens, Miami [A, Wikipedia]**, plot unconfirmed.
- **The estate.** Against decades of press estimates of a hidden fortune, and the FBI's own figure in the hundreds of millions, **the estate held few assets in his own name and left no substantial fortune**. **[A, Wikipedia]** Sources note the discrepancy without resolving it, and so does the journey. His brother's remark, "Don't expect a lot of money. If your father died today, he's broke," is **[R]**: *about*, not *by*, Lansky, via Wikipedia footnote 45 to Lacey pp. 258-259.
- **The Medal of Freedom: wrong medal, certainty overstated. Re-tagged [R].** Wikipedia says a **Medal of Freedom (1945)**, which "**possibly** had been secretly awarded to him for his deal with the ONI," was found among his possessions. This is the **Truman award of 1945** for wartime civilian service, **not** the Presidential Medal of Freedom, which Kennedy created in 1963. The pipeline dropped the source's "possibly." The **2022 Julien's Auctions sale is unconfirmed**: the site returned **403**.
- **Riviera afterlife, dates tightened. [A, Wikipedia "Hotel Habana Riviera"]** Nationalized **October 1960**; Iberostar took over management **2017**; the **35 million euro renovation was announced in 2018**, not at takeover; Iberostar **left Cuba in 2026**.
- **Books, with a co-author corrected.** *Daughter of the King* (Weinstein Books, 2014) is by **Sandra Lansky and William Stadiem**. **Michael Franzese, whom the pool named, is an unrelated Colombo family figure and had no part in it.**
- **Screen. [A, Wikipedia]** Strasberg as the Lansky-derived Hyman Roth (1974), Kingsley (1991), Dreyfuss (1999), Yusef in *Boardwalk Empire*, Keitel in *Lansky* (2021), whose plot is an FBI hunt for the hidden millions, dramatizing what the record does not support. **No museum or monument is documented anywhere consulted. [R]**

## Honest gaps

1. **No ship, no manifest, no landing date** for the 1911 crossing, and no source documenting Ellis Island at all.
2. **No street addresses** for the Lower East Side tenement, the Bugs and Meyer garage, or the Havana lawyer's office.
3. **Which Swiss banks** held the accounts, arguably the most consequential fact about his career, is documented nowhere reachable.
4. **The Siegel killing** and **the estate** both stay open: whether he ordered the one, and whether the concealment worked or the fortune never existed, are unanswerable on the record.
5. **Lacey's *Little Man* was never read directly.** Every Lacey citation here and in the journey passes through Wikipedia's footnotes: the dossier's largest weakness.
