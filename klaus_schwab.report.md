# Klaus Schwab: research report
*2026-08-27. Klaus Martin Schwab, b. Ravensburg 30 March 1938, living at the time of writing. Founder of the World Economic Forum (as the European Management Forum, 1971), chairman for fifty-four years, resigned 21 April 2025.*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named · **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or single-source dossier assertion.

A living subject with an active legal dispute and an active conspiracy legend attached to his name. Both raise the evidentiary bar. This report keeps apart three things the literature fuses: what Schwab did, what the Forum did, and what the internet says he did.

## 1. Ravensburg and the two passports, 1938-1957

**Birth [A].** 30 March 1938, Ravensburg, Württemberg, under National Socialist rule (en.wikipedia, "Klaus Schwab"). Father **Eugen Wilhelm Schwab**, commercial manager of the Ravensburg works of the Swiss firm **Escher Wyss**; mother **Erika Epprecht**. Birth address not established; the pin marks the town.

**The wartime works [A, PolitiFact].** Refetched (politifact.com, 8 February 2024, rating False). The plant Eugen Schwab managed **supplied equipment to the Wehrmacht**, **employed about 200 forced labourers from nearby Nazi camps**, and held a **"National Socialist Model Company"** designation. His **denazification files show no Nazi Party membership**, **the committee acquitted him**, and they **contain no information that he worked for Hitler**. An expert quoted there says the Model Company designation "says little" about ties to Nazi leadership.
*Scope warning, not resolved:* PolitiFact rates **only** the "worked for Hitler" claim, **not** the Nazi-general, concentration-camp or atomic-bomb strands. Citing it as debunking those over-reads it. The journey file states only what the check states.

**Wädenswil [R].** The family lives for a period at **Au**, a hamlet of Wädenswil on Lake Zurich; Klaus attends two years of primary school before the household returns to Ravensburg (en.wikipedia; de.wikipedia: "im Wädenswiler Ortsteil Au ZH"). **No school register located.** Coordinate corrected to Au, 47.2451, 8.6434; the figure first carried lands ~2.6 km southeast in Wädenswil centre.

**Swiss nationality claim refused [R for the primary document].** Federal Supreme Court, **BGE 76 I 374, 21 December 1950**, holding naturalization rather than recognition was required. servat.unibe.ch/dfr/pdf/c1076374.pdf, **not fetched this pass**; the judgment's content is dossier-sourced. Schwab was twelve and not present; the stop sits at the court because the ruling decides his own status.

**Abitur [A].** Spohn-Gymnasium, Ravensburg, 1957. Coordinate corrected to 47.7854, 9.6203 (Nordstadt), not the old town.

## 2. Two Swiss doctorates and one American master's, 1957-1967

**ETH Zurich, Diplom-Ingenieur [A, year contested].** Mechanical engineering with shop-floor work at industrial firms. **The contradiction, stated:** en.wikipedia has "In 1961, he graduated as a mechanical engineer from the Swiss Federal Institute of Technology in Zurich, with a doctorate in engineering," collapsing two degrees into one year; de.wikipedia separates them and dates the Dr. sc. techn. to **1965**; the dossier gives a **1962** Diplom. File uses **c. 1962 Diplom, 1965 doctorate**, hedged in `date_confidence`. **Do not print "1961 Diplom-Ingenieur, per Wikipedia": English Wikipedia does not say that.**

**ETH thesis [R].** On long-term export credit in the German machine-building industry (ETH research collection, via dossier). **Distinct from the Fribourg thesis** on public investment and growth; the two are routinely merged and must not be.

**Fribourg [A, structure corrected].** en.wikipedia gives "He also earned a doctorate in economics from the University of Fribourg" with **no year**. de.wikipedia: "studierte er bis **1963** Betriebswirtschaftslehre und wurde dort **1967** zum Doktor der Wirtschaftswissenschaften (Dr. rer. pol.) promoviert." So **licence to 1963, Dr. rer. pol. 1967**, resolving in the dossier's favour a discrepancy the first pass left open. Dissertation printed at Ravensburg 1966 (WorldCat, not refetched). Coordinate corrected to Miséricorde, 46.8061, 7.1523.

**VDMA, Frankfurt, 1963/1966 [R]: STOP CUT.** Assistant to the director-general of the German Machine-Building Association. Dossier-asserted, **not in any encyclopedia**, VDMA's historical pages unreached; and its seat at Lyoner Strasse 18, Niederrad is **a modern building**, so the stop would be anachronistic on top of unattested. Cut on the geography pass's recommendation. **This leaves a real gap: three postgraduate years missing from the itinerary.**

**Harvard Kennedy School [A].** MPA, **1966-1967** (range verbatim in de.wikipedia). Meets **Henry Kissinger** there in 1966; the relationship runs decades and brings Kissinger repeatedly to Davos. Coordinate corrected to 42.3716, -71.1217. **Claims of an intelligence-recruitment relationship are noted in the dossier as unsupported and appear nowhere in this file.**

**Escher Wyss / Sulzer, Zurich, 1967-1970 [A firm, R tenure].** Managing board as Sulzer absorbs Escher Wyss; sulzer.com confirms the 1966-1969 takeover and Zurich seat, the board dates are dossier-sourced. Same group whose Ravensburg works his father managed. See §7.

## 3. The symposium and the doctrine, 1971-1973

- **First European Management Symposium [A].** Davos, **24 January to 7 February 1971**, **444 participants from 31 countries**. **Foundation [A]:** European Management Forum registered in Geneva 1971, renamed **World Economic Forum 1987**, scope widened to conflict resolution. **Book [A]:** *Moderne Unternehmensführung im Maschinenbau*, 1971, arguing management serves stakeholders beyond shareholders.
- **Marriage [A].** **Hilde Stoll**, who answered a 1970 newspaper advertisement for an assistant and helped organize the symposium. Reformed church in the **Sertig valley**. Coordinate corrected by ~3.9 km: the first figure sat near Clavadel, not in the Sertig at all; **Sertig Dörfli is 46.7258, 9.8479**. The specific chapel is unresolved and the file says so.
- **University of Geneva [A].** Professor of Business Policy 1972-2003, honorary after. Coordinate corrected to Uni Dufour, 46.1997, 6.1422. **Davos Manifesto [A]:** 1973; a second follows under Schwab's byline in December 2019.

## 4. The Forum as neutral ground, 1979-1999

- **China [A].** Deng Xiaoping invited 1978; a Chinese delegation attends 1979. **India [R]:** first India Economic Summit, New Delhi, with PM **Rajiv Gandhi**, 1985, Wikipedia-only, absent from the dossier, not cross-checked; venue unspecified, so the pin is the city centre.
- **Özal and Papandreou: the collapse corrected [A].** The first pass flagged a contradiction (Wikipedia 1986 vs dossier 1988). **There is none. These are two events two years apart.** en.wikipedia: "In 1986 Schwab arranged a first public meeting between Turkish Prime Minister Turgut Özal and Greek Prime Minister Andreas Papandreou, personally inviting both leaders to appear on the same panel in Davos," and then, "two years later," shuttle diplomacy bringing both back to Switzerland to negotiate and sign the Davos Declaration with Cyprus near war. Written as **two stops, 1986 and 1988**. Both sources caution against overstating his role; the file credits venue, invitations and shuttling only.
- **Mandela and de Klerk [A, one name trimmed].** 1992. en.wikipedia frames this as their "first joint public appearance outside South Africa," **not** Mandela's first appearance abroad. **Mangosuthu Buthelezi does not appear in the fetched text and is dropped.** The dossier's claim that Schwab travelled to South Africa to arrange it is [R] and is **absent from `nelson_mandela.journey.json`, which does not mention Davos at all**: a gap in the existing Mandela file the operator may want closed.
- **Davos Pact 1996 [A].** Business leaders at Davos coordinate informal backing for Yeltsin's re-election. Kept: the critique it carries is about the room Schwab built. **Schwab Foundation for Social Entrepreneurship [A]:** 1998, with Hilde.
- **STOPS CUT for budget:** the 1994 Israeli-Palestinian Gaza/Jericho draft [A] and Kofi Annan's 1999 Global Compact proposal from the Davos platform [A]. Both are other people's scenes; Schwab supplied the room. No Annan journey file exists.

## 5. The pipeline, the phrase, the sentence, 2002-2017

**New York, 2002 [A move, R venue].** Annual meeting moved from Davos to New York after September 11. **Waldorf Astoria** is the standard identification but was not refetched; the file states the uncertainty.

**Young Global Leaders: founding date corrected [A].** Dossier and first geography pass both said Schwab "established" the body in 2004 with the **US$1 million Dan David Prize**. en.wikipedia, "Forum of Young Global Leaders": founded **1992 as Global Leaders for Tomorrow**, **renamed Young Global Leaders 2003**, **inaugural class of 237 in 2005**. The million funded the **relaunch**, not the founding. Two profile fixes: **under 38 at selection** (not "in their thirties"), and **237** (not ~100). dandavidprize.org returned **403**.

**Global Shapers [A].** 2011. **Host-state agreement, 23 January 2015 [A]:** Switzerland recognizes the WEF as an "international body," integrating it into International Geneva while it **remains a private Swiss not-for-profit, not a treaty-based intergovernmental body**. admin.ch **403**; **no signing venue is named in any source**, so the pin is the Forum's own address.

**The Fourth Industrial Revolution [A].** Essay 14 January 2016, then the book. Opening line and three further passages retrieved from weforum.org **via the r.jina.ai reader proxy**, direct fetch returning 403 throughout. **San Francisco centre, tag upgraded [R to A]:** en.wikipedia, verbatim, "On **10 October 2016**, the WEF announced the opening of its new Center for the Fourth Industrial Revolution in San Francisco," replacing the first pass's "2016-2017" resting on an unfetchable press page. **Street address still unestablished**; the coordinate is flagged approximate.

**"penetrate the cabinets": sourcing rebuilt [A for the words].** The first pass carried two wrong anchors, an Institute of Politics URL and the date **20 September 2017**. Both fail. iop.harvard.edu/events/improving-state-world-conversation-klaus-schwab, fetched, documents **one event, 27 March 2014**, with Schwab, David Gergen and Iris Bohnet. The remark names **Justin Trudeau's cabinet**, and Trudeau became prime minister in **November 2015**, so the 2014 event cannot be its source either. **Correct anchor:** a Harvard Kennedy School JFK Jr. Forum conversation of **2017**, archived as *Strengthening Collaboration in a Fractured World* at archive.org/details/strengthening-collaboration-in-a-fractured-world-featuring-special-guest-yo-yo-ma, phrase at approx. 18:25. **The IOP URL, the unverified YouTube id 7TdiND82vFg and the 20 September date are all dropped**; `date_confidence` carries a year-only hedge.

## 6. The Great Reset, the legend, the fall, 2020-2026

- **Launch [A].** 3 June 2020, virtual forum, video message from **Charles, then Prince of Wales**. Not an in-person Davos event; the pin is the institutional base. **Book [A, sharpened]:** *COVID-19: The Great Reset*, with **Thierry Malleret**, **9 July 2020**, Forum Publishing / Agentur Schweiz.
- **The legend [R].** isdglobal.org explainer, single-sourced: a Bitchute video calling it a New World Order power grab drew 100,000+ views **by 7 June 2020**; Fox News called it socialist that summer; by November 2020 Carlson, Shapiro and Beck were amplifying it; by January 2023, 260,000+ Facebook posts and 245 million TikTok views.
- **"You'll own nothing. And you'll be happy." [A].** From a **2016 WEF-published essay by Danish MP Ida Auken** and a **2018 WEF video**. **Schwab did not write or say it**; his photographs are nonetheless "often used to illustrate the phrase online especially in memes." Dated 2021 in the file, origins in `date_confidence`.
- **Stakeholder Capitalism quote: attribution corrected [A].** The "We cannot continue with an economic system driven by selfish values" passage is **from the book with Peter Vanham (Wiley, 29 January 2021)**, not a WEF press release as the pool had it. The book wording is used.
- **Milei [A].** 17 January 2024, introduced as bringing "a new spirit" to Argentina. The WEF story page **403**. `javier_milei.journey.json` dates Milei's own address **18 January** and records the speech but **not the introduction**, so the introduction is [R] against the corpus. Both dates appear in `date_confidence`.
- **Transition [R].** WEF statement of **21 May 2024**: executive chairman to non-executive chair by January 2025, president **Børge Brende** taking executive responsibility. Dossier-sourced, not refetched.
- **Wall Street Journal, June 2024 [A, narrowed].** en.wikipedia, verbatim: "In June 2024, The Wall Street Journal reported allegations of harassment and workplace misconduct against Schwab by former WEF employees." **The racial-discrimination and pregnancy-discrimination particulars circulating in coverage are not in the fetched article**, so the file writes the narrower sentence and says so.
- **Two legends left as gaps.** The **April 2024 death rumour [R]**: the AP fact-check (apnews.com/article/fact-check-klaus-schwab-hospital-dead-alive-607968130071) **could not be reached** and Wikipedia has no mention, so it is not carried as a stop. The **Rothschild-descent legend [R]**: circulating trees contain mismatched names and conflict with **Erika Epprecht**; the dossier calls it debunked but **no independent fetch backs the debunking**, so the file states only that the trees do not match the documented mother.
- **Resignation, 21 April 2025 [A].** Chair and trustee, immediate effect, ~54 years after founding. Statement quoted verbatim. Coincides with an anonymous whistleblower letter alleging misuse of Forum resources and manipulation of the competitiveness rankings. **Criminal complaint, 29 May 2025 [R]:** Geneva, unknown persons, defamation and coercion. **reuters.com is blocked to this session's fetch tool**; FT unreachable. No court address located; the pin is central Geneva.
- **Board clears him, 15 August 2025 [R for the language].** "No evidence of material wrongdoing," no misconduct by Hilde, "minor irregularities." **Larry Fink** (BlackRock) and **André Hoffmann** (Roche) become **interim co-chairs**. WEF release **403**; confirmed via en.wikipedia and `larry_fink.journey.json`, which restores the intervening step, **Peter Brabeck-Letmathe** as acting chairman through the investigation.
- **Schwab Academy, August 2025 [A].** Grand-Rue 25, 1204 Geneva, verbatim from the Academy's own site. **Date corrected** from "late 2025-2026". **Attribution corrected:** the Academy's page does **not** use "Intelligent Age" for the book series; that is Wikipedia's framing, cited as such.
- **University of Johannesburg, 29 May 2026 [A, upgraded from R].** Bunting Road Campus; date, venue and quote confirmed by direct fetch of news.uj.ac.za. Coordinate corrected from -26.19, 28.03 (~3 km east) to **-26.1878, 27.9979**.
- **Reported attempt to return, 6 July 2026 [R].** Reuters relaying the WSJ: an advisory position, demands and legal warnings to trustees. **Reuters unreachable.** No completed return documented.
- **Listening device, 7 July 2026 [A].** Covert device in his Geneva home office, apparently installed within the preceding three years; complaint against persons unknown. Direct fetch of swissinfo.ch. No street address given; the pin marks greater Geneva.
- **No tomb [A].** Alive at 88. The template category does not apply, and the absence is the finding.

## 7. Apparatus relations for the operator
