### Quote provenance correction (important)

The quotes lens sourced **"The real problem is that programmers have spent far too much time worrying about efficiency in the wrong places and at the wrong times; premature optimization is the root of all evil (or at least most of it) in programming"** to *"Structured Programming with go to Statements,"* Computing Surveys 6:4 (Dec 1974), p. 268.

**That citation is wrong, and the error is the commonest misattribution in the Knuth literature.** Two 1974 Knuth papers carry two different sentences:

1. **"Computer Programming as an Art," CACM 17:12 (December 1974), p. 671**, the Turing lecture, carries the "**real problem**" wording. Confirmed against Wikiquote's raw wikitext, which pins it to that page.
2. **"Structured Programming with go to Statements," Computing Surveys 6:4 (December 1974), p. 268** carries the *other* famous sentence: "We should forget about small efficiencies, say about 97% of the time: premature optimization is the root of all evil. Yet we should not pass up our opportunities in that critical 3%." Corroborated via Wikipedia's "Program optimization," which attributes the 97%/3% wording to that page and does **not** contain the "real problem" wording at all.

The journey uses wording (1) with citation (1). **[A]**

## 7. Literate programming and the LaTeX lineage, 1984-1986

- **"Literate Programming," The Computer Journal 27:2 (1984), p. 97ff** **[A]**. **The TeXbook, 1984**; **TeX: The Program** and **The METAFONTbook, 1986** **[A, MacTutor]**.
- **LaTeX, DATING CONTRADICTION, CORRECTED.** Chronology dated Lamport's work "1983" and called it the point LaTeX "becomes" a system; the afterlife lens claimed a **1983 user manual**. Wikipedia supports neither: Lamport wrote TeX macros for his own use at SRI **in the early 1980s**, public releases followed in **1984** (version 2.06a, September) and **1985**, and the **user's manual, commissioned by Addison-Wesley editor Peter Gordon, appeared in 1986**. There was no 1983 manual. The journey dates the SRI stop `1984-09-01` and puts the manual in `suggested_refs`.
- **Engine dependency confirmed [A]**: LaTeX provides macros for TeX to interpret and has no engine of its own. This is the lineage QUEUE.md named as the reason the two were queued together. **Lamport is named in a campa** on that strength but is **not** counted as an interlock slug, no `leslie_lamport.journey.json` existing yet.

## Honest gaps

1. **The marriage and the household.** Nancy Jill Carter appears once, in a CV line dated 24 June 1961, and never again in anything reachable. No children are named in the verified pool.
2. **The IDA year is classified** and likely to stay so. What Knuth did in cryptography in 1968-69 is unknown.
3. **The Stanford start year** (1968 CV against 1969 Wikipedia) is unresolved across three lenses.
4. **Two venues are reconstructions on which the journey pins stops**: San Diego for the 1974 Turing ceremony, the White House for the 1979 medal.
5. **The family printing press** rests on one lens's reading of the Stanford biography.
6. **The Oxford visiting professorship** has an end date and nothing else.
7. **Knuth's own book-length autobiographical material was not fetched**: *Things a Computer Scientist Rarely Talks About* (1999), the *Companion to the Papers of Donald Knuth*, and the Charles Babbage Institute oral history. Any of the three would settle several items above.
