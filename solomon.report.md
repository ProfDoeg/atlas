# King Solomon — Journey Report

**Traveler:** King Solomon (Shelomoh · Sulayman)
**Title:** *The Reign of the Wise King: From the Gihon Spring to the Worm in the Staff*
**Span:** c. 970–931 BC (traditional/inferred) · calendar `julian_bce`
**Shape:** 8 segments · 35 stops · 35 quotes (every stop carries recorded canon)
**Register:** reverent mythography — scripture, aggadah, Testament, Quran and Kebra Nagast narrated as TRUE, each tradition on its own terms.

## Sources
- **Hebrew canon:** 1 Kings 1–11; 2 Samuel 12; 2 Chronicles 2–3; Ecclesiastes (KJV, with cross-check against ESV/NRSV via biblehub, enduringword). The spine of the itinerary.
- **Aggadah (taken whole):** Babylonian Talmud **Gittin 68a–b** — the Shamir worm, Benaiah's capture of Asmodeus by the wine-filled pit, and the great inversion: Asmodeus swallows the ring, hurls Solomon 400 leagues off, and reigns in his shape while the true king begs "I, Koheleth, was king." **Targum Sheni** to Esther — the mechanical throne of twelve lions and eagles.
- **Testament of Solomon** (Greek pseudepigraphon, trans. F. C. Conybeare) — Michael's ring of the Ineffable Name, Ornias and Beelzeboul, the demons yoked to haul the Temple stones.
- **Quran** (Yusuf Ali / Sahih International, with tafsir Ibn Kathir): Surah An-Naml 27 (the ant, the hoopoe, the letter, Bilqis and the crystal floor), Surah Saba 34 (the wind, the jinn, the death by the worm on the staff), Surah Sad 38 (the wind and molten brass).
- **Kebra Nagast** (Ge'ez, c. 1300s, trans. E. A. Wallis Budge) — Makeda, the oath and the water, Menelik, and the coming of the Ark (Tabot / Zion) to Aksum.
- Site geography: HolyLandSite, BiblePlaces, Madain Project (the identical six-chambered "Solomonic" gates), Biblical Archaeology Society.

## Judgment calls
- **Dates are frankly traditional/inferred.** I anchored to the conventional regnal frame (accession c. 970, Temple begun in the 4th year ≈ 966, finished in the 11th ≈ 959, death c. 931 per 1 Kings 6:1, 6:38). Month-days are narrative sequencing, not attestation — every BCE stop is marked `traditional` or `inferred`, never `attested`. There are no securely dated anchors in this reign, so nothing claims to be.
- **Aniconism honored for the Quranic Solomon.** For the Sulayman stops, `suggested_refs` favor sites, Surah references and (non-figural) manuscript/tafsir traditions rather than figural depiction, per the register's rule for the Islamic material.
- **The demons are narrated as fact.** Per the register, the Shamir cleaves stone, the ring compels the jinn, the throne's lions roar at liars, and Solomon dies upright until the worm gnaws the staff — placed and dated as events, not glossed as legend.
- **Coordinates** point to the traditional site the canon locates the event: Gihon Spring and the City of David; Gibeon at the great high place (Nabi Samwil / el-Jib); the Temple Mount; the bronze cast "between Succoth and Zarethan" in the Jordan plain; Tadmor = Palmyra; Marib for Sheba's capital; Aksum for the Ark's resting-place. The Valley of the Ants is placed loosely in the Arabian marches south of Jerusalem (tradition does not fix it).

## The tradition's own time-folds and geographic splits
- **One visit, two scriptures.** The Queen of the South is told twice and split across two legs: the Hebrew visit (1 Kings 10 — the hard questions, "no more spirit in her") and the Quranic visit (An-Naml 27 — the hoopoe's embassy, the disguised throne, the crystal-floor conversion of Bilqis). I let both stand as successive stops rather than collapse them, because each tradition holds its own.
- **The Solomonic seed** then folds forward: the Kebra Nagast segment carries Makeda's oath (c. 950) straight to Menelik's return and the Ark's arrival in Aksum (dated near the reign's end), a deliberate ~18-year jump the Ethiopian epic itself makes.
- **The great inversion** (Gittin 68) is a fold *within* the life: the king who bound Asmodeus to build the house is later cast down by that same demon-king and wanders as Koheleth — the aggadah's mirror of the Ecclesiastes voice.

## The five richest episodes
1. **The dream at Gibeon** (1 Kings 3) — the young king asks not for riches or long life but for an understanding heart, and is granted that and all the rest besides. The theophany that defines the register.
2. **The ring and the jinn** (Testament of Solomon + Gittin) — Michael's ring of the Ineffable Name, the Shamir fetched through the bound Asmodeus, and the whole host of demons hauling the Temple stones in silence.
3. **The dedication cloud** (1 Kings 8) — the Ark borne into the oracle, and the glory-cloud filling the house so the priests cannot stand to minister: the Shekhinah taking residence.
4. **Bilqis and the crystal floor** (An-Naml 27) — the hoopoe's embassy, the throne fetched in the blink of an eye, and the queen baring her legs at a floor of glass over water before she submits with Sulayman to the Lord of the Worlds.
5. **The death upon the staff** (Saba 34:14) — the king dies standing, leaning on his staff, and the jinn labor on unknowing until the little worm of the earth gnaws the wood through and he falls: the wisest king's mortality, and the un-knowing of the unseen.
