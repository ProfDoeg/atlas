## 1. Denver and Madison, 1932-1952

**[A Wikipedia, 'John Searle (philosopher)'; Britannica; APA In Memoriam]** John Rogers Searle is born in Denver, Colorado, on 31 July 1932, to G. W. Searle, an electrical engineer at AT&T, and Hester Beck Searle, a physician.

**[R]** No street, hospital, or length of residence is fixed anywhere in the pool. The Denver coordinate (39.7392 / -104.9903) is a city-centre pin, not a birthplace pin.

**[R Grokipedia; Reason interview, 2000]** He enrolls at the University of Wisconsin-Madison, reading philosophy and economics, and leaves before taking a degree. **Downgraded from [A] on review:** Grokipedia is machine-generated and carries no attestation weight, and Wikipedia does not corroborate "philosophy and economics". Wikipedia attests only the Wisconsin enrolment and the departure on the Rhodes.

**Coordinate corrected.** The pool's Bascom Hall pin, **43.0755 / -89.4013**, lands about 270 m east of the building, down Bascom Hill near Library Mall. The file uses **43.0757 / -89.4046**, Bascom Hall itself.

**Contradiction, not resolved.** Wikipedia gives Searle as **secretary** of Students Against Joseph McCarthy; Grokipedia and the 2000 Reason interview give him as **president of the Wisconsin Student Association**. Not incompatible, but no source carries both and no primary record was reachable. Both go into `date_confidence`. **[A/R]**

**Contradiction, not resolved.** His age at the Rhodes award is given as **nineteen** in some pool entries and **twenty** in others; Wikipedia dates it to his **junior year, 1952 [A]**, and his birth date makes him nineteen until August. The file uses nineteen and records the split.

**Gap.** Nothing describes the Rhodes selection, the interview, or the crossing. **[R, absent]**

**Corpus gap.** A grep of all `*.journey.json` files for Wisconsin/Madison returns no hits: no existing traveler is at Madison in these years, so the segment carries no interlock at all.

## 2. Oxford, 1952-1959

**[A Wikipedia; Justapedia; Catedra Ferrater Mora]** Searle goes up to **Christ Church, Oxford** on the Rhodes Scholarship in 1952, takes his BA in 1955 and completes the doctorate in 1959 under **J. L. Austin** and **P. F. Strawson**. The thesis title is attested in full: *Problems arising in the theory of meaning out of the notions of sense and reference*. Never published as a book.

**[A Justapedia; Barry Smith, 'John Searle: From Speech Acts to Social Reality']** He holds a Christ Church lectureship from 1956 to 1959, overlapping the last three years of the doctorate.

**Contradiction, flagged, resolved in favour of the corpus pin.** This pool's geography lens puts Austin's **Saturday Mornings** group at **Magdalen College**, tagging the venue unconfirmed ("exact meeting room not independently confirmed this session"). `j_l_austin.journey.json` fixes it at **All Souls College** from 1947, at **51.7526 / -1.2529**. The All Souls pin is inherited byte-identical, the Magdalen alternative recorded in `date_confidence`. Austin's file carries a further unresolved split on the same group (its pool dated it 1953). Not adjudicated.

**Magdalen additionally ruled out on review.** Austin held the Magdalen fellowship from 1935, but from **1952** he was White's Professor of Moral Philosophy and a **Professorial Fellow of Corpus Christi College** (approx. 51.7503 / -1.2535), which covers the whole of Searle's Oxford period. So the pool's Magdalen attribution is wrong for these years whatever the meeting room was. Corpus Christi is recorded as the live alternative to All Souls in `date_confidence`; note that the interlock lens gives Corpus Christi as 51.7513 / -1.2536 and the geography lens's correction gives 51.7503 / -1.2535, a further unreconciled split of about 110 m. Neither is used, since the All Souls canonical pin governs.

**[R]** Searle's personal attendance is standard in accounts of Austin's circle (Warnock's memoir is the usual citation) but is documented against no dated meeting. The stop is dated 1953 as an ordering device only.

**Coordinate note.** The pool's University College pin, **51.7513 / -1.2494**, sits on the High Street frontage and shares a longitude with Austin's file's Examination Schools pin (51.7519 / -1.2494). No better coordinate was available; carried unchanged and flagged. **[R]**

**Gap.** Strawson has no file of his own; the interlock lens found him only inside Austin's circle. No Strawson interlock exists to inherit.

## 4. Speech acts and the quarrel with Derrida, 1969-1988

**[A Wikipedia, 'Speech Acts (book)'; CUP record]** *Speech Acts: An Essay in the Philosophy of Language*, Cambridge University Press, 1969: Austin's illocutionary acts systematized into rule-governed conditions.

**Verbatim quote used** on this stop, [A SEP, 'Speech Acts', fetched directly]: the passage from "Human communication has some extraordinary properties..." to "I have succeeded in telling it to him." In full in the journey file.

**Coordinate contradiction.** Cambridge, England is **52.2053 / 0.1218** in the chronology lens and **52.2043 / 0.1149** in the geography lens; neither is the press's Shaftesbury Road address. The second is used, matching the Cambridge pin in `alan_turing.journey.json`.

**[A Wikipedia, 'Searle-Derrida debate']** Derrida's 'Signature Event Context' (1972) argues that iterability undermines a theory grounded in a singular present speaker. Searle replies in *Glyph* (Johns Hopkins) in 1977 as 'Reiterating the Differences'. In 1988 he refuses permission to reprint that reply in Northwestern's *Limited Inc*, which carries Derrida's rejoinder anyway.

**Stop-ownership decision.** Derrida's 1972 essay, written in France, is **not** staged as a stop here. Searle's stop is the 1977 reply, with the 1972 essay in its campa and `suggested_refs`. Derrida has no file in the corpus (checked), so nothing is poached; the rule applies regardless.

**[A Wikipedia bibliography]** *Expression and Meaning* (1979) extends the framework to indirect speech acts, metaphor and fictional discourse; the fiction essay first appeared as 'The Logical Status of Fictional Discourse', *New Literary History*, 1974-75.

**Contradiction, flagged.** The pool pins *Expression and Meaning* both to **Cambridge University Press** (chronology lens) and to **UC Berkeley** (geography lens). The Berkeley pin is used, on the ground that the stop is Searle's and not the press's; the alternative is recorded. The same split occurs for *Intentionality* (1983) and for *The Construction of Social Reality* (1995), where the New York pin was preferred instead because the Free Press imprint is all the chronology lens attests. The inconsistency is deliberate and recorded so it can be overridden.

**[A AbeBooks/WorldCat; Internet Archive scan of a 1990 printing]** *Speech Acts* appears in Spanish as *Actos de habla*, Ediciones Catedra, Madrid, in printings from at least 1986 to 2017; a separate translation by Luis M. Valdes Villanueva circulates through Planeta-Agostini, Barcelona, 1992. **[R]** No first-printing date is fixed anywhere reachable; 1986 is the earliest seen.

## 5. The Chinese Room, 1980-1992

**[A Searle, 'Minds, Brains, and Programs', *BBS* 3 (1980): 417-457; Britannica; SEP]** The thought experiment: a person with no Chinese, manipulating Chinese symbols by an English rulebook, produces fluent-seeming answers while understanding nothing. Syntax is not sufficient for semantics.

**Verbatim quotes used, all [A SEP, 'The Chinese Room Argument', fetched directly]:** "Syntax is not by itself sufficient for, nor constitutive of, semantics" (1980); "The computer understanding... is zero" (1980); and the 1989 restatement on formal symbols meaning nothing "except insofar as someone outside the system gives it to them." All three are in full in the journey file. A fourth ("If the man in the room does not understand Chinese...") was left unused.

**[A SEP; BBS]** The paper ran with peer commentaries and Searle's replies attached, and is reported as the journal's most-commented target article. **[R]** That superlative is the journal's own, not independently checked.

**Interlock, one-directional and real.** The argument is written against **Alan Turing's** 1950 imitation game. `alan_turing.journey.json` carries 'Manchester: Computing Machinery and Intelligence' (53.4668 / -2.2339). **No Manchester stop is created here**: Searle never went, and the paper is Turing's own stop. The relation is a campa mention plus a `suggested_ref`, authored new, since no existing stop states it.

**[A Wikipedia bibliography]** *Intentionality: An Essay in the Philosophy of Mind* (1983); *The Rediscovery of the Mind* (MIT Press, 1992).

**[R, flagged in the pool]** The 1984 BBC **Reith Lectures**, published as *Minds, Brains and Science*, carry the pool's own note: "not independently re-verified by web search this session due to search budget exhaustion." The Broadcasting House pin (51.5181 / -0.1441) is representative, not a documented studio, and the number of lectures is not asserted.

**[R, flagged in the pool]** Placing Searle's teaching in **Moses Hall** (37.8707 / -122.2597) is called "reasonable inference, not independently sourced this session" by the pool itself. Carried as reconstruction.

**Coordinate note.** The pool's Cambridge, Massachusetts pin (42.3736 / -71.1097) serves both MIT Press volumes; Austin's file uses 42.3736 / -71.1190 for Harvard University Press in the same city, about 700 m off. Not reconciled: different presses. **[R]**

## 7. The Title IX finding and the revocation, 2016-2019

**[A Daily Nous, 'Searle Found to Have Violated Sexual Harassment Policies' (June 2019); Daily Cal]** The university investigation dates the conduct to **July-September 2016**, against a former student who had stayed on as his research employee, and finds sexual harassment and retaliation. It was reported to Berkeley's Office for the Prevention of Harassment and Discrimination in **November 2016**.

**[A Kristensen LLP announcement (2017); Inside Higher Ed, 24 March 2017; Daily Cal]** On **21 March 2017** Joanna Ong, a 2014 graduate and former research assistant, filed suit against the UC Regents and Searle, alleging harassment and assault including an incident in which he locked his office door. Inside Higher Ed reported prior complaints to the university.

**Verbatim quote used, [A complaint text quoted in The Daily Californian, fetched directly]:** "While Ong was employed at U.C. Berkeley, Searle sexually assaulted Ong and then continued to harass her as her employment continued." Flagged in the pool and again here as **a civil-complaint allegation, not an adjudicated finding and not a statement by Searle**.

**[A Daily Nous, 21 June 2019; Daily Cal; Leiter Reports; Wikipedia]** On **19 June 2019**, after a confidential hearing before Berkeley's Privilege and Tenure Committee and a formal finding of violation, UC President **Janet Napolitano** approved revoking Searle's emeritus status, and the Slusser title with it.

**Verbatim quote used, [A Janet Gilmore, UC Berkeley spokesperson, quoted in The Daily Californian, fetched directly]:** "Sexual harassment and retaliation have no place in the UC Berkeley Community..."

**[A Wikipedia; Daily Nous]** Searle disputed the finding. Recorded, not adjudicated.

**Gaps.** The Privilege and Tenure record is confidential; what OPHD did between November 2016 and June 2019 is not public; and the disposition of the Ong civil suit appears **nowhere in this pool**. The last is the most consequential gap in the file.

**[A Philosophy Now, 'John Searle (1932-2025)']** Searle's wife **Dagmar** died in 2017, the year the allegations became public. **Gap:** no month or day, so whether her death fell before or after the March filing is unestablished; the stop is placed after it for reading order only, with that stated in `date_confidence`. Her place of death is undocumented and the Berkeley pin is an assumption. **[R]**

## 8. Death and afterlife, 2025

**[A Daily Nous, 28 September 2025; Leiter Reports; Daily Cal obituary; Semafor; APA In Memoriam]** Searle died in **Safety Harbor, Florida, on 17 September 2025**, aged 93.

**Coordinate contradiction.** Three Safety Harbor pins are in the pool: **27.9906 / -82.6987**, **28.0186 / -82.6954**, **28.0086 / -82.6923**, spread over about 2.5 km. The third is used as nearest the town centre; the others are recorded in `date_confidence`.

**[A Leiter Reports, 'In Memoriam: John Searle (1932-2025)', fetched directly]** Ned Block in the memorial thread: "Searle was a pugilistic philosopher, much more interested in winning than in truth." Used verbatim.

**Contradiction inside a single source, left open.** The same thread carries the claim "There is no serious doubt that he had numerous sexual liasons with his students over many years," and, further down, Jennifer Hudin disputing it on the ground that the investigation found no such evidence. The journey file describes the exchange without quoting either side. The disputed sentence is **not** used as a quote field: a contested comment-thread assertion about a named person's conduct does not meet the bar for verbatim quotation in a canon file.

**[A APA In Memoriam; Daily Cal, 'Former UC Berkeley professor John Searle dies, leaves complicated legacy']** Every obituary carries both the work and the 2019 finding, and none reconciles them.

**[A Internet Archive; UC Berkeley Library subject guides; Calisphere]** There is no dedicated Searle archive: Bancroft holds general faculty material, Calisphere scattered items, and the bulk of the surviving teaching is a set of course webcasts on the Internet Archive.

## 9. Interlock decisions

**Named in a campa (direct relation only):**

- **`j_l_austin`**: Searle's own doctoral supervisor. Named at the Saturday Mornings stop, the Berkeley arrival stop (Austin lectured there in 1958, a year before his student arrived: supervisor and student, not co-location), and the *Speech Acts*, *Expression and Meaning* and *Construction of Social Reality* stops. Austin's file already carries the forward-reference to Searle's 1977 Glyph reply, so the interlock is bidirectional and pre-existing.
- **`alan_turing`**: the 1980 Chinese Room argument is written against Turing's 1950 imitation game. One-directional and real: Searle engaged Turing's paper, Turing could not have engaged Searle's. Named once, with a `suggested_ref` to Turing's Manchester stop and no stop created there.

**Deliberately NOT staged as stops, though heavily present in the research pool:**

- **Joan of Arc, Merlin, the Bois Chenu prophecy, Domremy, Vaucouleurs, Rouen, Geoffrey of Monmouth.** A large share of the pool goes to the Merlin prophecy read onto Joan of Arc, on the argument that a written prophecy fixed by a later act of reading is a performative and therefore Searle-adjacent. **Searle is nowhere on record addressing Joan of Arc, Merlin, or medieval prophecy.** A stop belongs to whoever was there, and a shared theme is not a relation, so none of it is staged here. Both files already carry the prophecy, cross-referenced to each other; one `suggested_ref` on the *Speech Acts* stop points a viewer there and says Searle never addressed the case.
- **The Bodleian manuscript-tradition stop** in the pool's geography lens, linking Oxford's *Prophetiae Merlini* manuscripts to Searle's Oxford training. The pool itself labels it "this corpus's own framing, not a documented historical link." Dropped.
- **Noam Chomsky.** The interlock lens offers Austin's 1955 Harvard meeting with Chomsky as an upstream node. Searle did engage Chomskyan linguistics, but that is Austin's stop, Chomsky has no file, and this pool documents no Searle-Chomsky encounter with a place or date. Not used.

**Known relation not pursued.** **Michel Foucault** has a file and taught at Berkeley in Searle's years, and Searle's anecdote about asking him why he wrote obscurely is widely repeated. **None of it is in this pool**: no dates, no venue, no citable source. Left out rather than authored from memory, and flagged as the best unexplored interlock for a later pass.

## 11. Addendum: two segments added by hand after the run, 2026-08-17

Sections 1 to 10 above are the pipeline's own record and are left as it wrote
them. This section records what was added afterwards, on the author's direction,
and it is the honest place to look for what has *not* been through the
adversarial verify stage.

The run stripped two things the subject was commissioned for: the Merlin
prophecy and the LLM material. Both were removed by the same mechanism. Verify
tests every relation for biographical attestation, finds that Searle is nowhere
on record on prophecy or on language models, and deletes. The reading was never
a claim about what Searle discussed; it is his apparatus applied to material he
did not treat, which is a licensed move under `EXCEPTIONS.md`, written this day
and naming him.

**"The Sentence That Waits: Prophecy as Declaration"**, placed after the
iterability quarrel, which supplies the written-mark half of the argument.
Three stops, all at Searle's Berkeley pin 37.8719/-122.2585, dated 1988-06 as an
ordering device only, on the template set by `j_l_austin.journey.json`'s Briar
Rose segment. The declaration and the constitutive rule; the prophecy from the
hoary wood; the maiden from Lorraine. The stand-in `suggested_ref` that had
stood on the *Speech Acts* stop is removed, superseded by these.

**"The Room Answers: Written Acts Without a Speaker"**, placed before Safety
Harbor, so the file reads 2016-2019, then the machines, then the death. Three
stops at the same Berkeley pin, dated 2022-11-30, 2023-06 and 2024-06. Berkeley
follows this file's own convention of pinning the work rather than the man: it
already pins the 2025 obituary notices and the missing archive there, after he
is dead. **Searle is not claimed to be at Berkeley, or anywhere.** He lost the
emeritus title in 2019 and died in Florida, and his whereabouts from 2019 are
not documented in any source consulted.

**"The Rites: Baptism, Anointment, Coronation, Inauguration"**, placed after
The Construction of Social Reality, which supplies the apparatus, and dated
2010-06 so it sits cleanly between that segment's last stop (2010-01-01) and
The Office Door (2016-07-01). Four stops, running from the rite that confers a
name to the secular residue that confers an office: baptism, the anointing from
the Sainte Ampoule at Reims, the coronation of 1429, and the presidential oath.

Three of the four sit at the Berkeley pin. The third does not, and it is **the
only formal device in this file.** 'Reims: the man in the room' places Searle in
the cathedral at the coronation, along the wall with a rulebook he does not
understand, matching symbol to symbol exactly as instructed while a Dauphin goes
in and a king comes out. He is in the room and he is not in the room, which is
the structure of his own argument turned on its author. Added on the author's
direction, 2026-08-17. It inherits `joan_of_arc.journey.json`'s Reims pin
byte-identical (49.2539/4.0349) so the two files share the coordinate, but keeps
a 2010 date rather than 1429: **the conceit puts him in the room, not in the
century**, and the file's chronology and the globe's time scrubber are left
intact. Its `date_confidence` opens by declaring itself a device, so no reader
can mistake it for a claim that Searle went to Reims.

Checked after the additions: `temporal.regressions()` reports exactly one
regression in this file, the pre-existing 2017 pairing of the lawsuit and Dagmar
Searle's year-only death date. The three added segments introduce none. House
style clean on all counts: no em dashes, no curly quotes, every new campa inside
the 450-650 band.

**Not verified, and wanting a pass before promotion to canon:**
- The Merlin prophecy's transmission. The virgin-from-the-wood prophecy is NOT
  in Geoffrey of Monmouth's *Prophetiae Merlini* (c. 1130-1135); it belongs to
  the later French Merlin tradition, and its attribution to Merlin is
  traditional rather than textual. That asymmetry is the point the stop turns
  on, so it needs to be right. The Latin tag *ex nemore canuto* and its line of
  transmission want checking against a primary edition.
- The nullification-trial testimony (1455-1456) about a prophecy of a maid from
  the Bois Chenu near Domremy, which reaches us a generation after the burning
  and is a memory of a reading rather than a record of the prophecy's text.
- Christine de Pizan's *Ditie de Jehanne d'Arc*, completed 31 July 1429, and its
  naming of Merlin, the Sibyl and Bede.
- The claim, made only negatively and in the confidence fields, that no source
  records Searle commenting on large language models. It rests on the research
  pool of this run, in which the strings LLM, large language model and GPT do
  not appear at all. That is an absence of evidence in one pool, not a
  demonstrated silence.
- The Sainte Ampoule and the anointing of Clovis at Reims, conventionally dated
  496. Reported in the stop as a tradition rather than asserted as fact, which
  is deliberate: the apparatus does not ask whether there was a dove, it asks
  who accepted. The date and the transmission still want a source.
- The coronation of Charles VII at Reims on 17 July 1429 with Joan present is
  taken from `joan_of_arc.journey.json`, whose own stop carries it as attested.
  Not independently re-verified here.
