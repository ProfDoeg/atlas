# Atlas network survey — texture & integrity suggestions
*2026-07-12. Measured against the 82 `.journey.json` files (3,392 located stops) with grep/haversine/date-parse scripts; all counts verified against the files, not estimated.*

## A. New-subject candidates (orphan hubs — mentioned in N existing journeys, no file of their own)

1. **NAPOLEON** — the atlas's largest orphan hub by far; his shadow already organizes the whole 1789–1815 band (San Martín fought for Spain against him, Fichte's Addresses answer him, Jahn/Froebel took arms, Goethe met him, Barbier was his soldier, the Oppenheim bank rose on his Rhine) — **16 journeys** mention him: alvear, barbier, belgrano, bolivar, clovis, delsarte, fichte, froebel, goethe_full, johann_jahn, juan_peron, miranda, paul_barbier, pestalozzi, salomon_oppenheim, san_martin.
2. **NIETZSCHE** — hinge between the existing `zoroaster` journey and the whole modern esoteric wing (Steiner worked in his archive; Jung's Zarathustra seminar; d'Annunzio "carried Nietzsche north in his baggage"; Fichte's own Schulpforta "will one day form Nietzsche") — **7 journeys**: dannunzio, fichte, jung, laban, savitri_devi, serrano, steiner.
3. **ÉMILE JAQUES-DALCROZE** — the missing teacher of the entire dance cluster; Hellerau would also knot in Gurdjieff (who saw the Dalcroze demonstrations) and Alexandre von Salzmann (already in gurdjieff + mary_wigman) — **4 journeys**: gurdjieff, laban, mary_wigman, vera_skoronel.
4. **PEDRO SUBERCASEAUX** — the painter through whose canvases the atlas already *sees* its libertadores (his paintings are cited as suggested_refs/imagery); Benedictine monk, Rome–Chile arc; a meta-journey of the image-maker of the national mythology — **4 journeys**: belgrano, lautaro, ohiggins, san_martin.
5. **ANTONIO JOSÉ DE SUCRE** — completes the libertador constellation with the one arc it lacks (Ayacucho → assassination at Berruecos); person-mentions verified in **3 journeys**: alvear (Cerro Rico summit with Bolívar), bolivar, ohiggins (Ayacucho news).
6. **ISADORA DUNCAN** — the bridge the dance wing lacks between American Delsartism (delsarte, stebbins) and European Ausdruckstanz (charlotte_bara) — **3 journeys**: charlotte_bara, delsarte, stebbins.
7. **SIGMUND FREUD** — both psych journeys orbit him (jung: the thirteen hours at Berggasse, fainting in Jung's arms at the Park Hotel; reich: the Wednesday circle); his 1938 Vienna→London flight is itself a mythic journey — **2 journeys, heavily**: jung, reich.
8. **SUZANNE PERROTTET** — the Dalcroze→Laban pivot in person, Monte Verità resident — **3 journeys**: laban, mary_wigman, vera_skoronel (Käthe Wulff and Berthe Trümpy are equal-count alternates, 3 each).
9. **ALEXANDER VON HUMBOLDT** — the traveler-scientist; already a teacher-figure in bolivar (the Monte Sacro thread), protector in symmes, audience in schelling's Berlin — **3 journeys**: bolivar, schelling, symmes.
10. **ANNIE BESANT** — succession hub of the theosophy branch (blavatsky→steiner schism runs through her) — **3 journeys**: blavatsky, keyserling, steiner. (Valentin Haüy, 2 mentions in barbier+braille, is the equivalent hub for the blind-writing cluster.)

## B. Interlock / cross-reference repairs (asymmetries measured at co-located stops)

11. **reich never names Elsa Lindenberg** — 21 co-located moments including THREE byte-identical pin+date siblings (Lucerne 1934-08-26, Malmö 1934-04-01, Oslo harbor 1939-08-19 — her stop is "the ship she does not board", his is "the last ship out"); her file is built around him, his is silent — add her to reich.journey.json's Berlin/Oslo stops.
12. **dannunzio never names Guido Keller** — 39 co-located moments; their Santa Entrata pins are byte-identical (45.3271, 14.4422), Keller is *buried beside him* at the Vittoriale mausoleum (keller stop 1929-11-15) — the Comandante should return the gaze. Same repair for **Charlotte Bara** (danced at the Vittoriale 1924-09-20, 27m from his pin; she names him, he doesn't).
13. **goethe_full returns none of the Jena constellation's gazes** — fichte, novalis, schelling all mention Goethe (he *hired* Fichte and Schelling); goethe_full mentions only Schiller. For the atlas's namesake file, one campa line each at the Weimar/Jena stops would close three loops (measured: fichte→goethe True/goethe→fichte False; same for novalis, schelling).
14. **alvear <> belgrano — zero cross-mentions in either direction** despite 9 co-located moments (Buenos Aires 1814–15, Río de Janeiro 1815) and shared institutions (Asamblea del Año XIII appears in both files) — the two founder-files pass through each other silently.
15. **che_guevara <> the Peróns — total mutual silence**: the string "Perón"/"Evita" appears nowhere in che_guevara.journey.json although his Faculty of Medicine years (1947–53) and both departures unfold inside Peronist Buenos Aires (9 co-locations with eva_peron incl. one identical pin, 4 with juan_peron); Che left Retiro 1953-07-07, one year after Evita's death stop.
16. **Falconetti dies in Perón's Buenos Aires and nobody notices** — falconetti "the death of Falconetti" [1946-12-12] is ~1.1km and six months from juan_peron's inauguration stop [1946-06-04]; the screen Joan of Arc starving to death amid the descamisados, with a joan_of_arc file already in the atlas — a one-line interlock in falconetti + eva_peron would braid three journeys.
17. **laban <> steiner — the two rival movement cosmologies never acknowledge each other**: Munich 1910–13 (Laban's "school for dance, sound and word" 1913-03-01 sits 746m from Steiner's "eurythmy shown to the members" 1913-08-28), then Stuttgart 1919–20 again ~1km apart; 10 co-located moments, no cross-mention.
18. **Artaud and Evita in Paris, July 1947** — Galerie Pierre vernissage [1947-07-04] is 1.4km and 18 days from eva_peron's Ritz/Dior stop [1947-07-22]: the flayed body and the gilded body in one city — pure texture, one campa clause each.
19. **charly_garcia born six days after Evita's last balcony** — birth stop [1951-10-23] vs eva_peron "17 de octubre de 1951, sostenida en pie" [1951-10-17], ~1km apart; neither file folds the eras together.
20. **serrano never mentions Maria Orsic or Vril** (verified: zero hits) though maria_orsic's file mentions Serrano, and both hold Antarctic stops in adjacent lone cells — asymmetric; also consider a shared Antarctica pin (see 32).
21. **wigman/laban never mention Vera Skoronel** though she mentions both, entered the Dresden school at Wigman's own byte-identical pin (51.0658, 13.7669, stop 1922-04-01), and died [1932-03-24] while elsa_lindenberg was postering Berlin 600m away (elsa <> skoronel: 22 co-locations, mutual silence).
