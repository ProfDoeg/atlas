# David Belle (b. 1973), research report

*Compiled 2026-08-31 for `david_belle.journey.json`. Subject is living; nothing here is a death, a tomb, or an afterlife.*

**Legend.** **[A]** attested, source named in place · **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, community lore, or self-report no reachable document confirms. Contradictions are flagged, **not adjudicated**. Unreachable sources are named with the reason.

**Caveat on the source base.** The biography is carried almost entirely by two Wikipedias that disagree, a self-published official site, a partisan retrospective at `parkour.com`, and Wikiquote, whose citations here point overwhelmingly at dead hosts. The high [R] ratio is structural, not thin searching.

---

## 1. The inheritance: Raymond Belle, 1939-1999

**Born 30 October 1939 at Huế; died December 1999 at Villeneuve-Saint-Georges. [A, Wikidata Q7298587].** This corrects the pool twice. The bare "1939 / Vietnam" can be replaced with a day, month and city. And **there is no English Wikipedia article on Raymond Belle**: it is a `#REDIRECT` to `Parkour#History` (verified via `Special:Export/Raymond_Belle`), so "Wikipedia's article on him gives no death date" misdescribes a redirect as an article. The death date is verifiable; the **burial place is not**, and none is invented.

**Father killed, mother lost in the First Indochina War; military orphanage at Đà Lạt at around seven; shipped to France after Điện Biên Phủ, 1954. [A, English Wikipedia, *Parkour*].** No year is given for the orphanage; c. 1946-47 is derived from the birth plus "around age seven" and dated as such.

**Joins the Brigade de sapeurs-pompiers de Paris at nineteen; develops his own regimen, *les parcours*. [A as fact; date [R]].** The source says only "remained in military education until the age of 19, when he joined the Paris Fire Brigade", with no year. Nineteen spans October 1958 to October 1959, so the circulating "1958" is an inference; the file pins it to his nineteenth birthday as an ordering device and says so.

**The Notre-Dame flag, 1969. [R].** Tradition holds Raymond removed a Viet Cong flag from a tower of Notre-Dame on a helicopter cable and was decorated for it. No reachable source confirms any element; a French Wikipedia fetch for *Raymond Belle* returned 404. Retained as the family legend told *to David*, flagged in `date_confidence`, at the atlas's existing Notre-Dame pin (48.853 / 2.3499).

**The Hébertian line. [A institutionally, never personally].** English Wikipedia's *Georges Hébert* calls his teachings "an important influence on the emergence of parkour (by David Belle)", and the *méthode naturelle* is the doctrine of the military and fire-brigade culture Raymond was formed in. No source asserts personal contact between Hébert and Raymond; `georges_hebert.journey.json` records that guardrail itself, and the file says so.

## 2. Birth and childhood, 1973-1988

**Born David Nicolas Belle, 29 April 1973, Fécamp, Seine-Maritime. [A, both Wikipedias].** Sourcing error in the pool: `davidbelleparkour.com/pages/about`, refetched, gives the date only and no birthplace, so it cannot corroborate Fécamp. Two coordinates circulate ~500 m apart (49.7593 / 0.3735 and 49.7599 / 0.3757); the first is used, the discrepancy recorded.

**Mother Monique Kitten; maternal grandfather Gilbert Kitten, sergeant-major in the Paris Fire Brigade. [A, English Wikipedia].** Two pool claims are unsourced and dropped: that Gilbert was a "senior firefighter" in the sense of command (sergeant-major is a warrant-officer rank), and that he was a principal early caregiver telling the boy stories of Raymond.

**Half-brother Jean-François ("Jeff") Belle, Paris firefighter. [A, French Wikipedia, which confirms Belle followed grandfather, father and brother into the Brigade].** Two further pool claims are unsupported and dropped: that Jeff taught rope climbing and gymnastic movement, and that he organised the 1997 demonstration and filming, which French Wikipedia credits to the *Stade 2* team.

**Contradiction 1, unresolved: where the childhood happened.** English Wikipedia puts the Lisses move at about **eleven (1984)**; other secondary biographies at **fourteen or fifteen**; a promotional biography places the **first fourteen years at Les Sables-d'Olonne**, not Normandy at all ([R], uncorroborated by English Wikipedia or the official site). Nothing reached settles this. The file dates the Lisses stop 1984 with the dispute in `date_confidence`, and carries a separate Les Sables-d'Olonne stop solely to keep the conflict visible rather than silently pick a town.

**Contradiction 2, resolved on evidence: Lisses is not Courcouronnes.** The pool treats one place as two and two places as one. **Lisses** is **48.5981 / 2.4244** (English Wikipedia; Nominatim 48.5960 / 2.4238); the pool's 48.5844 / 2.3985 is ~2.3 km off and its alternative 48.6167 / 2.4167 ~2.1 km off the other way. **La Dame du Lac** (Pierre Székely) is **not in Lisses**: French Wikipedia's *Parkour* puts it "dans le parc du lac d'Évry-Courcouronnes" and OSM has the landmark at **48.6132 / 2.4219**, so the pool's 48.585 / 2.397 is ~3.6 km off. English Wikipedia's *Lisses* confirms the pair independently **[A]**. The pool also cites `parkour.com`'s "Definitive Origin" for the Dame du Lac, and that article does not mention it at all; the anecdote about Raymond doing it at nine traces to Wilkinson, unfetchable, and is carried as tradition.

## 3. The uniforms that did not hold, c. 1988-1992

**Leaves school at about fifteen; first-aid certificate, UFOLEP gymnastics diploma, national service. [R, self-reported, French Wikipedia].** Certificates not located.

**Paris Fire Brigade attempt, injury, no resumption. [A, French Wikipedia].** The pool tags this "unverified", which understates it: French Wikipedia states it directly. The source does not say it was a wrist, or that he found "too many rules", and it places the episode **before** Vannes. The pool's ordering and its "mid-1990s" dating are wrong; the file follows French Wikipedia.

**Marine infantry (Troupes de marine) at Vannes. [A as service, both Wikipedias, with honours for rope climbing and the obstacle course].** The circulating range "c. 1989-1991" is unsourced. The rope record said to have been Raymond's, the gymnastics certificate and an Essonne obstacle-course championship are self-reported and appear in no military record.

**Contradiction 3, unresolved: when India happened.** The pool contradicts itself on the same trip: chronology lens c. 1991-1992, geography lens c. 1996-1997, neither sourced. English Wikipedia says only "3 months in India studying kung fu"; French Wikipedia says after national service, with a black belt. **No source names a year, a city or a region**; duration is three months in one account and six in another; the black belt is undocumented. The file uses an India country centroid (20.5937 / 78.9629) as an explicit placeholder, with `date_confidence` saying that no place is known and the coordinate is not a claim about where he was.

## 4. The circle, the name, the division, c. 1995-2001

**A training circle forms in Lisses and Évry, including cousins Châu Belle-Dinh and Williams Belle, and Sébastien Foucan. [A as fact, [R] as to date].** No founding date in any source; accounts differ on who was present when.

**The Lisses-to-Évry traverse in eight hours. [R].** From `parkour.com`'s partisan "Definitive Origin", which also claims Belle "created an entirely new movement philosophy". Untimed outside the circle, undated, consistent with the lone-inventor myth the pool itself flags.

**1997: the fire-brigade demonstration, ninja costume, the name Yamakasi, the *Stade 2* broadcast; the word *traceur* enters French. [A, French Wikipedia].** No caserne or ceremony site is identified anywhere, so the file uses the canonical Paris pin (48.8566 / 2.3522).

**The word.** *Parkour* from *parcours du combattant*; tradition credits the actor **Hubert Koundé** with the harder **k** [A etymology, [R] attribution]. **1997-98: Belle and Foucan separate [A].** Seven remain as Yamakasi with Foucan's *art du déplacement*; Belle keeps *parkour*.

**Correction: freerunning is 2003, not 1997.** The pool dates Foucan's term "1997 onward". English Wikipedia is explicit that it first appears in Mike Christie's 2003 documentary *Jump London*, starring Foucan, who then kept it. Dated 2003 in the file, and Jump London is **not staged as a scene**: it is Foucan's episode, cited as a source on Belle's own split stop.

**Correction: *Yamakasi* (2001) is not "Luc Besson's film".** Besson wrote and produced; it was **directed by Ariel Zeitoun and Julien Seri**, released 4 April 2001, cast including Williams Belle, Châu Belle Dinh and Charles Perrière. Belle's absence is right; the attribution is not. Music-video and advertising work of c. 1999-2002 is **[R]**, with inconsistent titles and no shoot city, and gets a clause rather than a stop of its own.

## 6. Governance, games, and the present

**FIG and Parkour Earth.** English Wikipedia gives parkour as a demonstration sport at the **2016 Winter Youth Olympics** under FIG, then "In 2017, several national governing bodies created Parkour Earth" in opposition **[A]**. Three pool assertions are unsupported by anything reachable and stripped: the "February 2017 FIG approval"; a dated chairmanship (English Wikipedia carries only a flat undated "He is the chair of the Parkour Committee of the International Federation of Gymnastics", and FIG's own parkour page 404s); and a resignation dated 21 October 2018 (no reachable source records a resignation at all, `parkourearth.com` redirects to a domain-sale page with no Wayback snapshot, and English Wikipedia still says he *is* chair). The Lausanne stop says outright that no date is establishable and that it is unknown whether Belle ever went there.

**Dying Light (Techland).** English Wikipedia's filmography row reads "2015 — Dying Light — Antoine Merpe — Also Mo-Cap, Stunt Coordinator & Counseling"; French Wikipedia files it under 2014; the game shipped January 2015. Dated in the file as development c. 2014, release Jan 2015. **The character name Antoine Merpe was not verified against the end titles.** *Dying Light 2 Stay Human* (4 Feb 2022), motion capture, likeness and voice for **Hakon**: **[A]**, well corroborated. Wrocław is Techland's headquarters as a general company fact and is an inference, not an attested visit, on both stops.

**Books.** *Parkour*, Septième Choc, 12 Feb 2009, 166 pp., ISBN 9782357560253, **[A]** via the Internet Archive record (catalogued English though the OCR reads French; print-disability collection). *Parkour: des origines à la pratique*, Perrière and Belle with Éric Coutard, Amphora, 2014, 187 pp., ISBN 978-2-85180-884-4, **[A] bibliographically**, some listings adding a Besson preface. Whether it is a new work or a revision of the 2009 book is undetermined.

**Not written, for want of a place. [R].** The post-2016 Chinese credits (*Super Express*, *Defying Chase*, *The Ultimate Code*, *Invisible Tattoo*) do not appear on the AlloCiné filmography fetched directly. The Take Flight association with Adam Dunlap is real but does not establish Belle travelling to the US. Neither gets a stop. The claim that Belle married Josie Maran in 2018 refers to a **different, unrelated David Belle**, a photographer.

**No monument.** None found in any source consulted: an absence of evidence, sourced to the search. The pool attributes to English Wikipedia's *Parkour* two quoted sentences about "dedicated parkour museums or memorial sites" and "plaques or monuments honoring David Belle, Raymond Belle, or other founders"; the raw wikitext was pulled and **contains neither string**, so they are not Wikipedia's words and are not reproduced. What the article does carry is a caption of "traceurs in Lisses re-painting a wall and repairing shoe scuff marks from parkour", and that closes the file.

## 7. Quotations

**Verbatim on the official site [A],** on the About page rather than the Interviews page as the pool states: "Faire, faire bien, faire vite et bien", under the heading "Do it, Do it well, Do it Fast and Well" (corroborated in different English wording on Wikiquote, cited to americanparkour.com, now 404); and Besson's "Watching him flirt with gravity…", all three sentences confirmed, but with no interview, publication or date given, so cited as an unsourced site attribution. A second, differently worded Besson remark sits on the homepage; the site may be conflating two. Also on the site: Belle's *Prince of Persia* caption (the misspelling "Gyllenhall" is original), Bruckheimer, Paul Walker's GQ remarks (original not located), Thierry Lafon of Lisses.

**Attributed but unverifiable at the primary [R].** All reach us only via Wikiquote, every cited host dead: "At the physical end, Parkour is getting over all the obstacles…" and "If you go from A to B…" (parkour.net); "My thing from the beginning is to have it be useful…" (americanparkour.com 404, Wikiquote renders it with a trailing ellipsis, so the pool's full-stop version is a truncated fragment, and the ellipsis is restored); "Parkour belongs to the ones who live it…" (a 25 July 2006 "communique", never located); "Our aim is to take our art to the world…" (BBC News, unreachable).

**One quotation rescued from a misframing.** "I'm not a monkey, I can't be treated like one" is not a standalone quotation: on Wikiquote it sits inside a longer ezboard-cited passage about people pressuring him to perform big drops for photographs. Quoting the sentence alone strips the context, so the file quotes the surrounding passage.

## 8. Apparatus relations for the operator

**David Belle is not listed in `EXCEPTIONS.md`.** Relations not rooted in his own life are not silently deleted but recorded here, with the direction named.

**Written in, a real relation.** **Georges Hébert**, named in the campa of "Paris, the fire brigade and les parcours". A genuine influence relation, not shared geography, running Hébert → military and fire-brigade physical culture → Raymond → David. `georges_hebert.journey.json` already closes with a stop naming David Belle ("Tourgéville, the method comes back in English", 2014), so the reciprocal anchor exists in canon and is not restaged.

**Kept, flagged (direction: BACKWARD).** The four Raymond Belle stops (Huế 1939, Đà Lạt 1946, Paris 1958, Notre-Dame 1969) are stops where **Raymond**, not David, was actually there. They open the file on the principle by which `jean_paul_sartre.journey.json` opens on his father's Indochina service, and each `date_confidence` says so explicitly. If the operator reads "whoever was actually there" strictly enough to exclude a parent's pre-birth formation, these four are the cut, and the file survives at 32 stops.

**Found and deliberately NOT written: shared geography or era only, no relation of Belle's. All BACKWARD in direction, none an apparatus relation, so none licensable under `EXCEPTIONS.md` as it stands.**

- **Victor Hugo**, "Notre-Dame de Paris, the word in the tower" (1831): the same cathedral as the Raymond flag episode. Coordinate 48.853 / 2.3499 inherited byte-identical; Hugo is not named.
- **John Foster Dulles**, two 1954 stops on Điện Biên Phủ and the Geneva partition: these are the events that put Raymond on a ship to France, so the link is to the event, not to Dulles.
- **Jean-Paul Sartre**, his father's fever brought home from Indochina, 1906: a generational rhyme between unconnected men.
- **Jacques Derrida**, Ris-Orangis, ~5 km from Lisses: pure proximity. His forward grant in `EXCEPTIONS.md` is for LLMs, on his own file, and licenses nothing here.
- **Leon Trotsky**, Natalia Sedova's death at Corbeil-Essonnes, 1962: that stop's actual pin is in Mexico City and the Essonne is a place-name inside a sourcing note, flagged so no later writer reads it as a plotted crossing.
- **Eugène Grasset** (Indochina stamps, 1904) and **Alexandra David-Néel** (opera in Indochina, 1895-97): the same colonial system, decades before Raymond's birth.
- **Erwan Le Corre / MovNat**: a sibling descendant of the same Hébertian trunk, but Belle has no documented relation to Le Corre. Hébert's material, already in Hébert's file.

**No forward reference of any kind was written**, and nothing is proposed for insertion into any existing file.

## 9. Honest gaps

Raymond Belle's grave is unknown, and not invented. The India trip has no year, city or region. The Lisses move is dated to two ages four to five years apart, and the childhood to two different coasts. The FIG chairmanship has no start date and the resignation no evidence at all. Whether the 2014 Amphora book is new or a revision is undetermined. The post-2016 Chinese filmography does not survive contact with AlloCiné. The Rush Hour rooftops, the 1997 caserne, the Évry buildings and the individual walls of Lisses are unidentified in every source consulted; canonical or general pins are used and declared as such.
