# Ludwig Derleth (1870-1948): research report

*Compiled 2026-09-01 for `ludwig_derleth.journey.json`.*

Legend: [A] attested, source named · [R] reconstruction or tradition carried only by
literature not examined here. Contradictions are **flagged, not adjudicated**. Unreachable
sources are named with the reason.

There is no shortage of *claims* about Derleth, only of *documents*. Nearly the whole intimate
record runs through Jost's 1965 biography and Christine Derleth's 1973 memoir, neither examined
here; Doerr's article, which quotes the Marbach letters verbatim, does most of the work below.

---

## 1. Franconia, 1870-1889

- [A] Born **3 November 1870, Gerolzhofen**, to Johann Derleth, Bavarian district judge, and
  Maria née Strobel. Named **Ludwig Benjamin**. *(Deutsche Biographie gnd11852478X;
  Literaturportal Bayern; de./en.wikipedia.)*
- [A] Childhood at **Stadtprozelten, Bischofsheim a. d. Rhön, Münnerstadt, Nuremberg**, following
  the father's postings; [A] Gymnasium at Münnerstadt. **No source dates any of the moves**; the
  file's 1875/1880/1884/1888 are ordering devices and say so.
- [R] FLAGGED. The return to **Münnerstadt as a teacher** is in **English Wikipedia only**,
  absent from Literaturportal Bayern, usually cited beside it. So is the repeated **Abitur at
  Nuremberg, 1889**: Literaturportal Bayern, the source normally credited, names Nuremberg only
  among the childhood towns, with no examination and no year.
- **Coordinates corrected (OSM/Nominatim); every childhood pin was off.** Gerolzhofen
  **49.9013, 10.3505** (~770 m out); Stadtprozelten **49.7809, 9.4053** (~2.3 km, had landed in
  woods); Bischofsheim **50.4012, 9.9934**; Münnerstadt **50.2526, 10.1875**.

## 3. Paris and the Kosmiker, 1898-1904

- [A] **1898 Paris**, meeting **Georg Kolbe**. *Verbatim, Georg Kolbe Museum record:* "Georg
  Kolbe lernte den charismatischen Dichter und schwärmerischen Sektierer 1898 in Paris kennen."
  CORRECTION: the story of an **engagement** to Derleth's sister **ending in 1898** is not what
  that record says. Kolbe "brach auch die Verbindung mit Derleths Schwester ab, mit der er eine
  Liebesbeziehung hatte" after leaving Paris: a **love relationship**, sister **unnamed**, **no
  end date**.
- CONTRADICTION, how he and Stefan George met. Either the 1898 Paris stay, or de.wikisource's
  editorial note to George's "An Derleth", which has George knowing him **through the Munich
  Kosmiker**. The file flags both and asserts neither.
- [R] UNSOURCED, kept out of the prose. That Derleth spent the Paris year **recruiting young men
  for a religious-military order** appears in **no source reached**, the Kolbe record cited for it
  included. Carried in `date_confidence` only.
- [A] Association with the **Kosmiker** around **Alfred Schuler** and **Ludwig Klages**; circle
  dissolves **1904**. The **break with Schuler** is dated to 1902 by Literaturportal Bayern, and
  de.wikipedia "Kosmiker" supports the substance, naming the rival renewals as **Roman antiquity**
  (Schuler) against **early Christianity** (Derleth). CORRECTION: Jost's "**emperor and
  Galilean**" formulation is **not** in Deutsche Biographie or Literaturportal Bayern as fetched,
  and is attributed to Jost directly in the file.
- [A] **Kolbe's portrait bust**, 1904; contributions to George's ***Blätter für die Kunst***,
  c. 1896-1910.

## 4. The Proclamations, 1904-1910

- [A] **Three evening readings** of the unpublished *Proklamationen* in his Munich rooms, a
  disciple reading aloud; **Thomas Mann attended one of the three**; the invitation fell **"in der
  Karfreitagswoche 1904"**, Good Friday *week* *(de.wikipedia "Kosmiker" and "Beim Propheten";
  das-blaettchen says Easter week)*. [R] THE DAY IS A RECONSTRUCTION: Good Friday 1904 did fall on
  **1 April**, so the usual arithmetic is sound, but no source puts Mann there on that day.
- [R] THE ADDRESS IS UNCONFIRMED. **Destouchesstraße 1** is cited to
  stadtgeschichte-muenchen.de's address book; **that page returns 404**. The pin is still
  corrected to that street's geocode (**48.1647, 11.5825**), the circulating 48.1595 sitting
  ~580 m south. [R] **Franziska zu Reventlow's presence** and a **next-day letter to Kurt
  Martens** are attested in nothing reachable.
- [A] **"Beim Propheten"** (1904) is credited as modeled on Derleth; **Doktor Faustus** (1947)
  revives the type as **Daniel zur Höhe**.
- [A] ***Die Proklamationen*, Leipzig, Insel-Verlag, 1904, 83 pp.** *(K10plus.)* **[R]:**
  "1 December 1904" and "500 numbered copies on handmade paper" are in neither the catalogue
  record nor Literaturportal Bayern as fetched.
- [A] **1906:** leaves Gymnasium service. **No dismissal, disciplinary record, or pension dispute
  is documented**, only that he left. [A] **1907:** George's **"An Derleth"** in the *Tafeln* of
  *Der siebente Ring*. [R] NOT LOCATED: ***"Per un cenobio laico"***, *Coenobium* (Lugano, 1907),
  via Doerr, the essay not found; carried as a publication-site stop.

**The quotations.** All *Proklamationen* lines come through **Balthasar, "Integralismus"
(1963)**, trans. Huff and Carpenter, at theologyandsociety.com. **Verified verbatim there:**
*"War and Cross. Shoot the ecumenical synagogue on fire."* **The antisemitism is stated as
recorded, not endorsed** and nowhere softened: the 1904 book prints it as a command. CORRECTION:
those lines are **not** the close of Balthasar's block and do not precede his turn to Maurras.

## 5. The Mediterranean and Near Eastern travels

- [A] Sources list **Algeria, Carthage, Budapest, Rhodes, Syria, Lebanon, Iraq**
  *(Literaturportal Bayern; en.wikipedia)*. [R] THE ITINERARY DOES NOT EXIST: no dates, ports,
  sites, letters, or notebook, only country names.
- Method. Five pins (Algiers, Carthage, Rhodes, Baalbek, Babylon), each flagged as a **mapping
  reconstruction, not a confirmed stop**, Baalbek standing in for "Libanon" and Babylon for
  "Irak". **Budapest and Syria were given no pin**, being named in a stop's `date_confidence`
  instead: a Damascus stand-in would be a third arbitrary placeholder.
- **Nothing ties Derleth to Mecca, the Kaaba, or any Islamic holy site.** *Der fränkische
  Koran* is a **genre claim**, a recited world-scripture, not a pilgrimage record and not
  evidence of conversion. Said outright in the Perchtoldsdorf campa.

## 6. Olga Fröbe-Kapteyn, 1910/1916-1948

- CONTRADICTION. When they met: **Doerr** says "mindestens seit 1910"; **Jost 1965 p. 74**
  says **1916**.
- [A] The correspondence opens with a sheet headed ***"Haag, 27. Aug. 20"***, i.e. **from The
  Hague, not Ascona**, and runs to his death *(Doerr, from the Derleth-Nachlass, DLA Marbach)*.
  Quoted verbatim there: the "obedient she-wolf" letter of May 1921, *maitre fabuleux*, and the
  last letter on **ERANOS TAGUNGEN letterhead**.
- Transcription corrections. *maitre fabuleux* has **no circumflex** in Doerr. The torch letter
  reads "…daß es über mich **ausbräche**…", subjunctive, where a circulating "ausbreche" is a
  different word; Doerr calls that sheet "Einzelnes Blatt ohne Anrede", dated 3 Aug. 21. The last
  letter's **'Rosengarten des Blutes'** carries **quotation marks in the original**, marking a
  phrase she cites **back to Derleth**; dropped, they read as her coinage. Restored in the file.
- [A] **October 1922, Ascona to Rome with Fröbe-Kapteyn.** CITATION CORRECTED: the source is
  **Jost 1965, p. 103**, quoted in Doerr. **Deutsche Biographie contains no such chronology** and
  should stop being cited for it.
- [R] Visits to **Villa Monte Tabor**, location unconfirmed, Ascona centroid used. [R] That he
  **never attended the mature Eranos meetings** at Casa Gabriella is from Christine Derleth's
  memoir, unconfirmed. [A] **Eranos (1933)** is credited to Fröbe-Kapteyn with **Jung and Rudolf
  Otto** in the founding roles; Derleth's part is **formative, not founding**.
- GAP IN ANOTHER FILE. `olga_frobe_kapteyn.journey.json` contains **no occurrence of the string
  "Derleth"**, a real gap on her side. **Nothing was written into her file**: operator's call.

- [A] **1911:** meets **Christine Ulrich**; **Regina Ullmann converted in 1911 "apparently under
  Ludwig Derleth's influence"** *(en.wikipedia)*. [R]: the **Altötting** exercises and the month

  the stop sits at **Munich**, not the shrine, which is Ullmann's stop.
- [R] **Marriage in Rome, 15 March 1924**, from Wikipedia; no register consulted, no church or
  civil office identified.
- [A/R] **The Katia Pringsheim claim, restated correctly.** Christine Derleth (1973), p. 183, in
  Doerr n. 21: Katia "hegte zarte Gefühle für Ludwig Derleth, der aber keine Ehe eingehen wollte",

## 9. San Pietro di Stabio, 1935-1948

- [A] Settles at **San Pietro di Stabio**, Ticino, **1935**, after the Nazi seizure of power.
  **No documentary evidence establishes flight from a specific personal threat.**
- COORDINATE CORRECTED AND STANDARDIZED. The circulating **45.8494, 8.9349 is the village of
  Stabio**, not the **San Pietro** quarter where he lived and died; Chiesa di San Pietro geocodes
  to **45.8592, 8.9357**, and **all San Pietro stops use 45.859, 8.936**. A rival **45.8656,
  8.9425** for the *same* place ~1.9 km away is **discarded**, not averaged.
- [A] ***Die Lebensalter*, Kassel, Bärenreiter, 1937, 112 S.** and ***Seraphinische Hochzeit*,
  Salzburg/Leipzig, Müller, 1939, 172 S.**, the latter reviewed in *Stimmen der Zeit* 136 (1939)
  no. 9 p. 200. **CORRECTION: neither was published in Switzerland**; the *writing*, not the
  printing, belongs at San Pietro.
- [R] The **"Sibyllinisches Buch"** portrait of **Hitler** as a blind destroyer of the order of
  history filling an abyss with corpses is **in none of the sources reached**; reported tradition,
  composition dated variously c. 1935-1940. Carried as **[R], not attestation**, precisely because
  it is the fact that most changes how a reader weighs the rest of him.
- [A/FLAGGED] ***Der Tod des Thanatos***, Josef Stocker, Lucerne: **bibliographies vary between
  1945 and 1946**; both Wikipedias say 1945, used, conflict recorded. [R] **Haro op van Veld's
  pencil portrait**, October 1947, not examined, month unconfirmed.
- [A] **Died 13 January 1948 at San Pietro di Stabio, aged 77.** **No reliable cause of death**
  in any accessible source.
- GAP, STATED AS A GAP. **No cemetery is named, no headstone described, no account of
  whether a grave survives or was cleared**, not in Deutsche Biographie, either Wikipedia, the
  Kalliope record, or the Ticino *Oltreconfiniti* page. An **open question**, not a finding that
  "the grave is lost."

## Interlocks with existing atlas travelers

Two, both direct relations, both named in a campa.

1. **`olga_frobe_kapteyn`**: twenty-eight years of correspondence, an intellectual formation he
   supplied, and a journey together from Ascona to Rome in October 1922. Named in the
   Marienplatz, Hague-letter, Ascona, Rome and last-letter campas.
2. **`nietzsche`**: a **one-directional textual influence**, the prophetic voice and commanding
   aphorism the *Proklamationen* take over whole. Named in the Munich university and Basel
   campas, the latter stating plainly that Nietzsche died in 1900, that they never overlapped in
   Basel, and that Derleth never acknowledged the debt. Basel pin inherited byte-identical from
   `nietzsche.journey.json` (47.559, 7.589).

**Deliberately not named**, because shared geography is not a relation and **no documented
meeting, correspondence or citation exists in either direction** for any of them: `jung`,
`mircea_eliade`, `keyserling` (Eranos institutionally), `gurdjieff`, `rene_guenon` (Paris),
`julius_evola` (Munich and Rome, plus cognate "proto-fascist" labels applied by scholars),
`ida_hoffmann` and `henri_oedenkoven` (Monte Verità is the adjacent hillside, and Derleth is not
documented as ever going up it).

**No journey file exists** for **Thomas Mann**, **Stefan George**, **Georg Kolbe**, **Alfred
Schuler**, **Ludwig Klages**, **Regina Ullmann**, or **Christine Derleth**, each a real relation
named in the campas as an ordinary historical person.

**Canonical pins: none apply.** The lens looked for Kaaba, Temple Mount and Buenos Aires and
found **no documented Derleth connection to any of them**. Per the house note on
aesthetic-ancestry entries, the absent Argentina link is expected and was not invented.
