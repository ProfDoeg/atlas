# Sam Bankman-Fried (b. 5 March 1992): research report
*Compiled 2026-08-22 for `sam_bankman_fried.journey.json`. Slug: `sam_bankman_fried`.*

Legend: **[A]** = attested, source named · **[R]** = reconstruction or journalistic retelling with no primary anchor reachable from here.

A living subject, still in federal custody. Every date after 2024 is provisional in a way the rest is not: the record is still being made, and the last stop in the journey file is two months old.

---

## Method

The pool came in five lenses (chronology, geography, quotes, interlock, afterlife) already through an adversarial verification pass. That pass caught a great deal, and its corrections are what the journey file carries. They are itemised below, because a reader of the journey file will otherwise not know that, for instance, the Davos stop was cut because the meeting did not physically take place. Nothing was fetched fresh; four claims rest on material outside the pool and are listed at the end.

**Register note.** The canon is true is easier to hold for an emir than for a man convicted of taking eight billion dollars of his customers' money. The file writes him neither as a monster nor as a boy who made an error. The fraud is stated flat: the money was customers' money, it was moved, a jury convicted on all seven counts.

## Phase 1: Stanford, 1992 to 2010

Born 5 March 1992 at Stanford, to **Joseph Bankman**, professor of tax law at Stanford Law School, and **Barbara Fried**, professor at the same school and a writer on distributive justice **[A: Wikipedia]**. Brother **Gabriel** born 1995 **[A]**. Childhood on the Stanford campus in faculty housing **[A: Michael Lewis, *Going Infinite* (2023)]**. Lewis is an embedded-access source with a documented sympathy problem, used here only for physical and domestic facts, never for motive.

Schooling at **Crystal Springs Uplands School**, Hillsborough **[A]**. **Canada/USA Mathcamp** attendance attested **[A]**, but no reachable source gives the year or campus, and Mathcamp rotates hosts annually. The gathering lens proposed a Mathcamp stop pinned to Colby College because Colby is a frequent host: a pin on a campus he may never have entered, cut. It survives inside the Crystal Springs campa, where it costs nothing to state without coordinates.

## Phase 2: MIT and Brookline, 2010 to 2014

MIT, physics degree with a mathematics minor, 2014 **[A]**. Resident of **Epsilon Theta** **[A]**, which the gathering lens placed "near MIT, Cambridge" at 42.36, -71.085, i.e. Kendall Square. It is an MIT Independent Living Group at 259 St. Paul Street, **Brookline**, 42.3466, -71.1163 **[A: Wikipedia; OpenStreetMap]**, 2.7 km away and in another municipality. Not pedantry: living off campus in a self-governing group house is the first instance of the pattern running through Berkeley, Hong Kong and Nassau, in which the household and the firm are the same people in the same rooms.

The **lunch with William MacAskill** at which "earning to give" was pitched to him is the most repeated scene in the biography and the least anchored: in neither Wikipedia's "Sam Bankman-Fried" nor its "William MacAskill", descending instead through the Sequoia, New Yorker and Lewis retellings, with 2012 nowhere independently fixed. Carried as **[R]**, and the false citation to "Wikipedia, Effective Altruism" struck.

## Phase 3: Jane Street, 2013 to 2017

Summer intern 2013, full-time from 2014, departure September 2017 **[A]**. International ETF desk **[R]**: widely reported, not primary in the pool, kept because it is the ordinary description of what he traded. **Address unresolved:** the gathering lens pinned both stops at 250 Vesey Street, Jane Street's *present* headquarters; the firm occupied One New York Plaza earlier in the decade and no reachable source gives a move date. The file names "Lower Manhattan" with no street number.

## Phase 4: Berkeley and Alameda, 2017 to 2018

Director of development at the **Centre for Effective Altruism**, Berkeley, October to November 2017 **[A]** (pool typo "1917-10"). **Alameda Research** co-founded with **Tara Mac Aulay** **[A: Wikipedia, "Alameda Research"]**. The gathering lens made **Caroline Ellison** a co-founder drawn from Jane Street. She was not: she joined in 2018, became co-CEO, and became sole CEO in August 2022 on Sam Trabucco's resignation **[A]**.

The **January 2018 Japan trade** is attested **[A]**: up to twenty-five million dollars a day on the spread between Japanese and American bitcoin prices, with a trip in person because Japanese banks required a body in the branch. The pool called it a "kimchi premium" trade, which names the *Korean* spread.

## Phase 5: Hong Kong and FTX, 2018 to 2021

Macau conference late 2018, leading to the Hong Kong move **[R for the conference, A for the move]**. No venue is named anywhere, and the pool's "peninsula convention district" coordinates were in Cotai, 5 km south. Peninsula pin, ignorance stated.

Alameda headquarters to Hong Kong early 2019 **[A]**. FTX **founded April 2019, opened May 2019** with **Gary Wang** **[A]** (the pool collapsed the two events into one date). Blockfolio acquired August 2020 for roughly $150 million; Series B July 2021 at $18 billion with the Binance stake bought out; Forbes ranked him 41st-richest American in 2021 on roughly 90 percent ownership of Alameda **[all A]**. No Hong Kong office building is attested; district pin, stated.

## Phase 6: Nassau, 2021 to 2022

Headquarters Hong Kong to Nassau, September 2021 **[A]**. Residence in the gated **Albany** resort: a five-bedroom penthouse shared with roughly ten Alameda and FTX colleagues, several in relationships with each other and with him **[A: Forbes (2022)]**. Stated flat in the campa, once, without elaboration.

Three lens entries gave two different and both wrong sets of Albany coordinates; the file standardises on **25.0034, -77.5088** (OpenStreetMap).

House Financial Services testimony, Rayburn building, 8 December 2021; Series C January 2022 at $32 billion plus a $2 billion venture fund the same month; Crypto Bahamas at Baha Mar, April 2022, with Clinton, Blair and Katy Perry; Giving Pledge June 2022; roughly $160 million committed by the Future Fund to about 110 organisations by September 2022 **[all A]**.

**Arena naming rights**: $135 million over nineteen years, effective **June 2021**, terminated **January 2023** **[A: Wikipedia, "Kaseya Center"]**; the pool's range was wrong at both ends. Folded into a campa, since his presence at the arena is nowhere attested.

## Phase 7: The collapse, November 2022

2 Nov, CoinDesk publishes the leaked Alameda balance sheet, $3.66 billion of largely unlocked FTT among roughly $14.6 billion in assets. **6 Nov**, Zhao announces Binance will liquidate its FTT; the chronology lens dated this 8 November, wrong by two days, and it matters, because 6 November triggers the run and 8 November is the letter of intent, a separate event. 8 Nov, "FTX is fine. Assets are fine."; FTT loses roughly 80 percent; Binance signs a non-binding LOI. 9 Nov, Binance withdraws. 10 Nov, the entire Future Fund team resigns. 11 Nov, Chapter 11 in Delaware by FTX, Alameda and 130-plus affiliates, and resignation as CEO. **[all A]** 12 Nov, interviewed by the Royal Bahamas Police Force **[R]**. 16 Nov, the Kelsey Piper direct messages **[A: Wikipedia reproducing the Vox text; Vox blocked the fetch]**.

## Phase 8: Arrest, extradition, bail, 2022 to 2023

Arrested at Albany 12 December 2022 **[A]**, the night before he was to testify to the House committee. Held at **Fox Hill** roughly ten days **[A]**; pool coordinates 6.5 km out, corrected to 25.0278, -77.2919. Extradition consented to; flight to Westchester County Airport 21 December **[A: AP, Reuters]**; arraigned at 500 Pearl Street 22 December on a **$250 million bond**, the largest in American criminal history **[A]**. Not guilty plea 3 January 2023; bail tightened 1 February over contact with former FTX employees; four further counts 23 February including 300-plus illegal campaign contributions; a $40 million Chinese bribery count in March **[all A]**; roughly $100 million of misappropriated customer money routed into politics alleged August 2023 **[R: prosecutors' allegation]**. Bail revoked for witness tampering, remand to **MDC Brooklyn**, 11 August 2023 **[A]**; coordinates corrected from Red Hook to Sunset Park, 40.6603, -74.0044.

## Phase 9: Trial and sentence, 2023 to 2024

Trial opened 3 October 2023 before Judge **Lewis A. Kaplan**; conviction on all seven counts (two wire fraud, five conspiracy) 2 November 2023. Sentence 28 March 2024: twenty-five years, forfeiture of **$11.02 billion**, effective term estimated near eighteen years with First Step Act credits. **Barbara Fried** wrote to the judge in February 2024 seeking leniency. *Going Infinite* was published 3 October 2023, the week the trial opened. **[all A]**

Co-defendants: Ellison two years (24 Sept 2024); Singh and Wang time served (30 Oct, 20 Nov 2024) **[A]**. None is a stop: each was in that courtroom on their own account, not his. Ellison's cooperation appears only where he was present for it, at his own trial.

## Phase 10: Custody and appeal, 2024 to 2026

Transferred to **FCI Terminal Island** March 2025 after an unauthorised Tucker Carlson interview, reportedly followed by solitary confinement **[A for transfer and interview; R for the solitary, secondhand, no BOP statement]**. Register number **37244-510** throughout. Appeal argued 4 November 2025, denied **12 June 2026** **[A: secondary summary]**. **Terminal Island closed 13 June 2026**, one day after the denial, and he went to **FCI Lompoc I**, which lists him among current inmates with release scheduled **14 December 2044** **[A: Wikipedia]**. The pool's terminal stop was Terminal Island, now two months stale; the file ends at Lompoc.

Estate recovery: reorganisation plan approved **7 October 2024**, with recoveries estimated at **118 to 142 percent** of petition-date claim values, driven by post-collapse appreciation of recovered holdings **[A]**. Claims are valued at November 2022 prices, so creditors repaid "in full" still lost the intervening appreciation.

## Contradictions flagged and not resolved

1. **Palo Alto or Stanford.** Chronology lens: "his parents' home in Palo Alto". Geography lens: Stanford faculty housing. Wikipedia's "Joseph Bankman" says Stanford; its "Sam Bankman-Fried" says only "in California". Some campus mailing addresses fall inside Palo Alto city limits, the likely source of the slip. The file pins Stanford and says so.
2. **Alameda's founding month.** Wikipedia "Alameda Research": September 2017. Wikipedia "Sam Bankman-Fried": November 2017. The same encyclopedia disagreeing with itself. Dated 2017-11-01 to order the segment after the CEA post; conflict stated, not resolved.
3. **The Lompoc sequence.** The geography lens has him assigned to Lompoc in 2024 after sentencing; the afterlife lens has Lompoc *before* Terminal Island, which the verification pass judged inverted; Wikipedia has him transferred to Lompoc *from* Terminal Island, in 2026. One Lompoc stop, at the end, with the conflict recorded in `date_confidence`.
4. **The prepared congressional testimony.** The only reachable rendering is bracket-edited, `"[FTX] is solvent"`, in a *Guardian* live blog of 13 December 2022; the original subject word is unrecoverable. The same live blog is the only source for "pressured", one common word carrying no distinctive voice. Both are stated as prose, not quoted, with the substantive detail kept (the general counsel, Ryne Miller).
