# Mark Stewart Cohen, research report
*Atlas of Journeys. Slug `mark_cohen`. Written 2026-08-22 against the verified research pool and `dossiers/mark_cohen.dossier.md` (research cutoff 19 August 2026).*

**Subject.** Mark Stewart Cohen, professionally Mark S. Cohen. New York trial lawyer. Assistant U.S. Attorney for the EDNY in the first half of the 1990s; co-founder (2002) and New York managing partner of Cohen & Gresser LLP; court-appointed monitor of the New York City Fire Department from 2011; U.S. counsel to Ghislaine Maxwell from 2009; lead trial counsel to Sam Bankman-Fried in 2023. Living at the cutoff.

**Legend.** **[A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction, inference, or unconfirmed firm self-description. Contradictions flagged, not resolved. Gaps stated as gaps.

## 0. Identity control

"Mark Cohen" is a common name. In April 2025 a *different* Mark Cohen, formerly general counsel of HIAS, published an opinion piece about being confused online with men of the same name **[A, Washington Post, 8 Apr 2025]**. He is not this man. Nothing was admitted on a name match alone. The discriminator is the middle name, in Companies House filings for Cohen & Gresser (UK) LLP and in federal dockets **[A, PSC register, OC421038]**.

## 1. Origins and education, 1963-1987

**Birth.** Companies House gives **January 1963**, month and year only, no day **[A]**. The pool offered 1962, reconstructed backward from the 1984 graduation on an assumed age **[R]**; the filing supersedes it and the journey uses 1963. No source gives a **birthplace**: not a town, not a state, not a country.

**Family.** Parents, siblings, ancestry, spouse, children: nothing established **[gap]**, and the dossier refuses to import any of it because of the name problem. No religious identity is established; the listed UJA-Federation Lawyers Division affiliation is professional and is not evidence of belief **[A as affiliation, firm biography]**. Nothing at all is documented before Cornell **[gap]**.

**Cornell.** B.A. 1984, Phi Beta Kappa **[A, firm biography]**. Major, thesis, teachers, activities: not identified anywhere reachable **[gap]**.

**Michigan Law.** J.D. *magna cum laude* 1987, Note Editor of the *Michigan Law Review* **[A]**. Two student publications, verified against the law school's repository, are the earliest direct evidence of his mind:

- **"William Hastie: Grace Under Pressure," 84 Mich. L. Rev. 861 (1986)**, on the civil rights lawyer and federal appellate judge **[A]**.
- **"American Indian Sacred Religious Sites and Government Development," 85 Mich. L. Rev. 771 (1987)**, arguing that courts mishandle ordinary Free Exercise analysis where government development affects Native sacred sites, and proposing a restructured test **[A, University of Michigan Law School repository]**.

The 1987 note is his only sustained constitutional scholarship, and it does **not** establish a personal religious or political position **[dossier's own caution, adopted]**.

**Michigan again, 2018.** Gifts totalling **$100,000** from Cohen ('87) and Gresser ('86) to the Cohen & Gresser Scholarship Fund **[A, Michigan Law *Quadrangle*]**. No source places him in Ann Arbor for it, so no stop was built; the fact sits in the campa for his 1987 degree.

## 2. Fried Frank and the Eastern District, 1987-1996

**Fried, Frank, Harris, Shriver & Jacobson.** He practised there as an associate before founding Cohen & Gresser, **with no years attached** **[A as fact, undated]**. The firm's New York office of the era was One New York Plaza **[A as address, OpenStreetMap]**.

> **CONTRADICTION, FLAGGED AND NOT RESOLVED.** The pool's chronology lens put Fried Frank **before** government service; its afterlife lens had him beginning his career as an AUSA, i.e. after; its geography lens gave "c. 1993-2002," after as well but a decade on. The dossier states flatly that the sources "do not establish with certainty whether his Fried Frank service fell entirely before his federal prosecutorial period, entirely after it, or on both sides of it." Three lenses, three orderings, no source. The journey places the stop at 1987 and says in `date_confidence` that the sequencing is unresolved.

**AUSA, EDNY.** Attested by court records, not by the firm's own page. **27 March 1995**: *United States v. Gangi* names **Mark Cohen** and Elisa Liang as Assistant U.S. Attorneys for the EDNY **[A, CaseMine]**. **1996**: a Second Circuit opinion calls him **"Former AUSA Cohen"** **[A, Justia, 101 F.3d 683]**. **The start date rests on no primary personnel record.** The pool assigned 1990 **[R]** in one lens and "c. 1988-1993" **[R]** in another, which the 1995 opinion contradicts. The journey uses 1990 as a reconstruction anchored on 1995.

**United States v. Pitera.** The firm biography places Cohen as a junior prosecutor on the trial team in a seven-week jury trial of Bonanno soldier and contract killer Thomas Pitera, among the first federal death-penalty-eligible cases tried in New York **[A as firm-reported; trial and 25 June 1992 conviction corroborated by English Wikipedia]**. Pitera got life; no death sentence was returned.

**1996-2002.** Not documented year by year **[gap]**.

## 3. Cohen & Gresser, 2002 onward

**Founding.** Cohen & Gresser LLP, New York, **2002**, by Cohen and Lawrence T. Gresser **[A, Wikipedia; firm's New York office page]**. He is New York managing partner and heads the litigation and white-collar groups **[A]**. Headquarters **800 Third Avenue**; offices Paris 2014, Washington 2016, London 2018, Dubai 2025 **[A]**. A practitioner bibliography runs from 2005 through antitrust, bank enforcement, insider trading, the FCPA, and federal sentencing **[A, firm list]**; sole authorship is not assumed where title-page metadata is not exposed.

**Adverse to JPMorgan Chase, 2008-2009.** In *JP Morgan Chase Bank v. Coleman-Toll Ltd. P'ship*, out of the failed **Inspirada** development at Henderson, Nevada, Cohen represented the Meritage Homes entities against JPMorgan and its co-lenders **[A, CaseMine]**. This is the clearest direct Cohen/JPMorgan contact and it is **adverse**. The journey pins it at **his own office**: no source places him in Nevada, and a stop belongs to whoever was actually there.

**FDNY monitorship, 2011.** Judge Nicholas Garaufis, having found the city's firefighter hiring discriminatory, ordered a monitor over FDNY hiring and retention, and Cohen was appointed **[A, firm biography; Wikipedia "Nicholas Garaufis" and "Vulcan Society"]**. Firm materials date it to **November 2011** and describe roughly a decade in the role; the litigation settled for **$98 million**. **End date is a gap.**

**Securities defence wins.** **30 May 2014**: Cohen and Jonathan Abernethy won a jury verdict for **Peter Black**, a former Wynnefield Capital analyst, in an SEC insider-trading case **[A as firm-reported, date from the dossier]**. **July 2018**: the same pair won dismissal of an SEC complaint against **Vanja Baros** of Och-Ziff **[A as firm-reported]**. The firm never names the **venue** of the 2014 trial; *SEC v. Obus* was SDNY, so the journey's 500 Pearl Street placement is inference and says so.

**Paris.** The firm opened in Paris in 2014, and Cohen is a longtime visiting lecturer in U.S. white-collar criminal law at **Universite Paris I Pantheon-Sorbonne**; in 2017 he taught a course so titled with Muriel Goldberg-Darmon **[A]**. No start date is given; the journey places it in September 2014 and says so. He never became resident in France **[A, dossier's explicit negative]**.

**Offices and honours.** Fellow, American College of Trial Lawyers; chair of its **Federal Rules of Evidence Committee, 2020-2022**, and **New York Downstate Committee, 2022-2024**. Board, New York Council of Defense Lawyers, 2008-2011. Trustee, Supreme Court Historical Society. Chambers USA white-collar from **2013**, securities from **2016** **[all A, firm biography]**.

## 4. Ghislaine Maxwell, 2009-2022

**2009.** The **2016 privilege opinion in *Giuffre v. Maxwell*** recites Maxwell's declaration that she retained **Brett Jaffe**, then of Cohen & Gresser, in 2009 for U.S. legal matters and a deposition, and that when Jaffe left the firm **Mark Cohen continued as her United States counsel**; the court found an email consistent with her account **[A, via Justia]**. This is the load-bearing document of his biography outside the trials, and it makes the common claim that he became her lawyer only in 2020 **materially incomplete**.

**July 2020.** Arrested 2 July, charged in the SDNY; the docket records **Mark Stewart Cohen** entering an appearance shortly after **[A, CourtListener; DOJ case page]**. Bail refused 14 July; held at MDC Brooklyn.

**2 February 2021.** A DOJ discovery letter addressed in part to **Christian Everdell and Mark Cohen at Cohen & Gresser** carried an index line reading **"REPRODUCTION, JPMorgan Chase materials,"** roughly 794 pages **[A, EFTA00011267, rollcall.com]**. JPMorgan-sourced records entered his case file. That is all it establishes; the dossier's list of what it does not establish is adopted in full.

**Trial and sentence.** 29 November to 29 December 2021 before Judge Alison Nathan; conviction on five of six counts. **28 June 2022**: 240 months, five years supervised release, a $750,000 fine **[A, DOJ]**.

> **ROLE, FLAGGED.** The pool twice called Cohen a "strategic advisor." The firm's own words: he **handled the initial proceedings and served as strategic adviser to the trial team**, and was "part of the team representing the defendant." The courtroom team was Sternheim, Everdell, Menninger, Pagliuca. Calling him her lead trial lawyer is an oversimplification; calling him an outside adviser understates a representation running from 2009. The journey stop says both halves.

## 5. Sam Bankman-Fried, 2022-2024

**6 December 2022**: Reuters reports that Bankman-Fried, after Paul Weiss withdrew citing conflicts, has hired **Mark S. Cohen** **[A]**; Everdell comes on as principal co-counsel. **12 December**: arrest in Nassau **[A]**. **22 December**: extradition and release on a $250 million bond secured against his parents' Palo Alto home **[A]**. **3 January 2023**: plea of not guilty **[A]**.

**15 June 2023**: prosecutors agree that counts added after extradition be severed and held for a March 2024 trial while the Bahamas-consent question under the doctrine of specialty remains open **[A, Reuters]**. The firm calls this the removal of six charges; the record shows counts **set aside**, and only after conviction did the government decline to bring them **[A, Reuters, 29 Dec 2023]**. Both readings recorded, neither smoothed. **11 August 2023**: bond revoked over witness tampering; remanded to MDC Brooklyn **[A]**.

**The trial**, before Judge Lewis A. Kaplan, prosecutors Nicolas Roos and Danielle Sassoon **[A, Wikipedia "Trial of Sam Bankman-Fried," corroborated where noted]**: jury selection **3 Oct 2023**; **openings 4 Oct** (Time, 5 Oct); Ellison **10-11 Oct**; Singh **16 Oct**; the defendant testifying **outside the jury's presence 26 Oct**; direct before the jury **27 Oct**; **cross beginning 30 Oct**; redirect and the defence resting **31 Oct**, three witnesses in all; closings **1 Nov**; the jury out about 3:15 p.m. on **2 Nov** and back guilty on all seven counts in under five hours.

**29 December 2023**: no second trial **[A, Reuters]**. **28 March 2024**: 25 years, roughly $11 billion forfeited **[A, DOJ]**. **11 April 2024**: notice of appeal **[A]**. **13 September 2024**: new-trial motion alleging judicial bias **[A]**. The defence was criticised in real time for repetitive objections and weak cross-examinations; WIRED carried both the criticism and practitioners arguing the record left few options **[A]**. **No court finding located holds that Cohen rendered ineffective assistance.**
