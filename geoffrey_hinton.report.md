# Geoffrey Everest Hinton (b. 1947): research report
*Compiled 2026-08-28 for `geoffrey_hinton.journey.json`. Living subject; the file is open-ended and its last stop is May 2026.*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named · **[R]** reconstruction, or a claim resting on a source that could not be fetched. Contradictions are flagged, not adjudicated.

The spine is Hinton's own **short CV** (`cs.toronto.edu/~hinton/shortcv.pdf`, 6 January 2025 revision, read with pypdf) and his **awards page**. The CV is primary in the way that matters: he wrote it, it is dated, it lists posts month by month. It is also thin, naming no colleges, no venues, no reasons and no people. Almost every narrative claim the CV does not carry comes from retrospective interviews given since about 2016, and is marked [R] here however often it is repeated.
