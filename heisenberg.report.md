# Heisenberg journey — report

**Sources.** David Cassidy's *Uncertainty: The Life and Science of Werner Heisenberg* (1992) anchors the biographical spine throughout. Primary voice comes from Heisenberg's own memoir *Der Teil und das Ganze* / *Physics and Beyond* (1969/1971), read against the AIP History Heisenberg web exhibit and the 2025 centenary retrospectives (Physics World, APS News, AIP.org) on Helgoland, which explicitly flag how much of the "rock at dawn" story may be Heisenberg's own later embellishment — I kept it but marked its stop `traditional` rather than `attested`. The Nazi-era material (Das Schwarze Korps, the Himmler letter, the Göttingen Manifesto) is drawn from the documents themselves and Cassidy. The 1941 Copenhagen meeting uses both Heisenberg's postwar account and the 2002 Bohr Archive release of Bohr's unsent draft letters, which flatly contradict each other — the campa states this contradiction rather than adjudicating it. Farm Hall draws on Jeremy Bernstein's edited transcripts, *Operation Epsilon* (1993).

**Judgment calls.** (1) The moral-ambiguity directive is handled by never resolving the 1941 Copenhagen meeting or the wartime Uranverein calculations — both stops state the historical dispute openly (Heisenberg's self-serving postwar version vs. Bohr's colder memory) rather than picking a side. (2) The Farm Hall "lecture he could not quite finish" stop is the closest the dataset comes to a verdict: it reports, factually, that his first critical-mass estimate was wrong and he corrected it only in captivity — letting the reader draw the "did he sandbag it or did he just not know" conclusion Frayn's play also refuses to resolve. (3) I include the Neupfadfinder/Freikorps material because it is the least mythologized part of his youth and it seeds the mountain motif (Walchensee) that returns literally at his 1945 capture.

**Gaps / time-folds.** Coordinates for the 1919 scouting camps and the 1941 Bohr meeting are approximate (Walchensee generally, Bohr's Carlsberg residence rather than a documented exact room). The "world formula" episode (1958) is compressed — the Pauli break played out over many months, not one. No stop covers Heisenberg's 1929 world lecture tour (Chicago, Tokyo) or his extensive postwar Pugwash-adjacent diplomacy in detail; both were judged less load-bearing than the ten segments chosen.

**Five richest episodes.** (1) Helgoland, June 1925 — the hay-fever cure that becomes the birth of matrix mechanics, with Heisenberg's own "strangely beautiful interior" quote. (2) The 1926 Einstein encounter in Berlin, whose "it is the theory which decides what can be observed" reshapes Heisenberg's thinking for a full year before resurfacing as the uncertainty principle. (3) The 1937 "white Jew" affair and Himmler's letter — physics as a target of Nazi racial ideology, resolved only by his mother's intervention. (4) The disputed 1941 Copenhagen meeting with Bohr, told from both sides without resolution. (5) Farm Hall, August 1945 — the recorded, unguarded disbelief at Hiroshima and the corrected bomb calculation, the rawest primary-source moment in the whole dataset.
