# Rudolf Ritsema (1918-2006): research report
*2026-09-01, for `rudolf_ritsema.journey.json`. Dutch bookseller; editor of the Eranos-Jahrbuch; last sole executive of the Eranosstiftung at Moscia-Ascona; translator of the I Ching by the core-word method.*

**Legend.** **[A]** attested, source named in the line. **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or an intake-dossier lead that nothing reached this pass confirms. Contradictions are flagged in place and left standing. Unreachable sources are named with the reason.

**One structural fact governs the file.** From 1947 Ritsema is paralyzed from the waist down, and from the winter of 1947-48 he lives on one estate on Lake Maggiore for fifty-eight years. Twenty-eight of the forty-four stops sit on a single pin: the shape of the life, not a shortfall in research.

## 1. Netherlands and the Geheeb schools, 1918-1940

**Born 3 October 1918 at Velp, Gelderland**, son of a chemist. **[A** en.wikipedia, "Rudolf Ritsema".**]** No civil register or obituary was reachable; the coordinate 51.9959/5.9751 matches OSM's Velp (Rheden) to 0.00 km. **Correction:** the dossier called this "four weeks before the Armistice"; it is five and a half.

The **Odenwaldschule at Ober-Hambach above Heppenheim** is **[A** de.wikipedia: founded April 1910 by Paul and Edith Geheeb**]**, but the **year 1929 is [R]**, since en.wikipedia says only "At age 11, Ritsema entered the Odenwaldschule" and 1929 is arithmetic off the birth year. **The Geheebs emigrate in 1934, not 1933**, founding the Ecole d'Humanite at **Versoix**, Canton Geneva, as guests at the Institut Monnier **[A** de.wikipedia "Paul Geheeb", "Schulen im Exil"**]**. **Correction:** the dossier's "1934 onward" misleads; they were at Versoix **1934-1936** only, then Schwarzsee from October 1939 and Hasliberg-Goldern from May 1946. That **Ritsema** was there, and met **Catherine Gris** there, is **[R]**: the dossier concedes his school dates are unclear.

**Biology at Geneva; wartime loss of funds from the occupied Netherlands; no degree. [R]** No matriculation record located; the coordinate is the Bastions campus. Catherine Gris as a Geneva music teacher is **[A** Manas Penalver and Loredo Narciandi (hereafter MP-LN) 2020, p.19**]**.

## 5. The rupture, 24-25 August 1988

The best-documented episode of the life, resting on one eyewitness.

- **[A]**, all of it, from Schabert, eranos.org PDF pp.3-4, as a participant: Ritsema asked the speakers to gather "after dinner in a library room of the Casa Gabriella"; "Curtly, Ritsema stated that this had been the last Eranos Tagung"; he would devote himself exclusively to the I Ching; **Gilbert Durand** "refused to accept it, and spoke of betrayal"; a **piano concert by Catherine Ritsema** was already arranged that evening "in the Ritsema's spacious living room at the Casa Eranos"; **Erik Hornung** lectured next morning **on Echnaton (Akhenaten)**, closing "The fundamentalism, which heaves up its head everywhere today, has no future"; Ritsema ended the uproar by calling out "The buses have arrived, they depart in five minutes!"
- The pool tagged most of the scene **[R]**; it is **upgraded to [A]** on Schabert, with one correction: the dossier omitted that Hornung's lecture was on Akhenaten.
- **Bias declared, not discounted.** Schabert is a participant who went on to lead the breakaway association, so the fullest account of the rupture is written by the losing party of that evening. Hakl reportedly differs on sequence while confirming the essentials, and was not read directly. **Ritsema's own account was not found in anything reached.**
- **Autumn 1988 circular converting the Foundation to the I Ching project, against the 1989 report to the supervisory authority describing a pause to find a successor and clarify finances. [A** Schabert p.5.**]** The file preserves the tension and does not decide which states his intention.
- **Associazione Amici di Eranos, 1989. [A** Schabert pp.6-7.**] Correction:** the dossier said it was "formally created under Erik Hornung and Tilo Schabert". Schabert says otherwise: twelve met **11 February 1989 at the Hotel Al Porto, Ascona** (Hornung and Schabert among them), the association was founded **31 October 1989 at the Hotel Tamaro**, the driving figures in Ascona being **Bieri, Vacchini and Schnetzler**, and their first conference, "Resurrection and Immortality", opened at **Casa Serodine, 19 August 1990**, two hundred attending (pp.7-8). **None of this is staged as a scene here**, because he was at none of it.

## Interlocks named in campa, and those refused

- **`olga_frobe_kapteyn`** (six stops). Direct and central: met winter 1947-48, worked with her from 1956, took the organizing at her death in 1962. Her own file already carries the bridge: "Eranos goes on without her, under Adolf Portmann and afterward Rudolf Ritsema."
- **`jung`** (once, stop 5). Not a meeting: nothing attests they met, and Jung's last Eranos lecture (1951) precedes Ritsema's institutional role by five years. The relation used runs *into* Ritsema's formation and is attested: his analyst von Keller was seeing patients Jung referred from Zurich, and Wilhelm's German I Ging reached that consulting room through the Zurich circle that had championed it. **Flagged as the weakest of the three:** if the standard is a documented personal relation, this naming should come out.
- **`mircea_eliade`** (once, stop 13). Organizer to speaker: Eliade was at Eranos from 1950 and on the 1961 roster in Frobe-Kapteyn's file, so he was a speaker in the years Ritsema was arranging the conference. A working relation, not co-location, but **no direct contact is attested** and the stop says so.
- **`wolfgang_pauli`: offered by the interlock lens, refused.** Pauli lectured at Eranos in the late 1940s on the same hill where Ritsema was an annual guest: shared geography and nothing else, since Ritsema had no institutional role until 1956 and no contact is attested. **`martin_buber` and the rest of the 1961 roster** (Kerenyi, Scholem, Corbin, Suzuki, Neumann, Zimmer) are refused for the same reason. The file prints no roster.
- **No Argentina link**, per the aesthetic-ancestry convention, and none is invented. **No `adolf_portmann.journey.json` exists** here, so the succession chain cannot be cross-checked; Portmann is named as a historical person, not as an interlock.

## Gaps

- No place, cause, funeral, or grave for the 2006 death; no primary record for the birth, the schooling dates, the Geneva enrolment, or the marriage location; no medical record of any kind for the 1947 polio, across a fifty-nine-year paraplegia.
- **No stand-alone scholarly biography exists.** The fullest continuous account is by his own late collaborator Sabbadini, sympathetic and inside the story, and what Wikipedia says tracks it. The historiography of this subject descends from one insider's memoir.
- Ritsema's own voice on both decisive controversies (August 1988, the break with Karcher) is absent from everything reached; both are known from the other side.
- *The Periplus of the Eranos Archetype* rests on Hakl's report alone.
