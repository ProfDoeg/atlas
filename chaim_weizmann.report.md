# Chaim Weizmann (1874-1952): research report

*2026-08-29, from the verified research pool. **[A]** = attested, source named. **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition, or memoir. Contradictions are flagged, not adjudicated. Where the verification pass issued a correction it is followed and the superseded reading recorded.*

Weizmann has a clean documentary spine and a dirty margin. The spine is public: congresses, cabinet minutes, a signed agreement, a declaration, a presidency. The margin is memoir. *Trial and Error* (1949) supplies several of the most quoted scenes in his life, written by a man of seventy-five who had spent forty years being the person those stories were about. What reaches this file through it alone is **[R]**.

## 1. Motol and Pinsk, 1874-1892

**[A]** Born 27 November 1874 in Motol on the Pina near Pinsk, third of fifteen children of the timber merchant Oizer Weizmann and Rachel Leah Czemerinsky. **[A]** Heder, then the Russian Real-Gymnasium at Pinsk from about eleven, graduating with honours in 1892. *(Wikipedia, 'Chaim Weizmann')*

- **Correction, governorate.** The pool put Motol in Minsk Governorate. Wikipedia, 'Motal': it sat in the Kobrinsky Uyezd of **Grodno** Governorate until 1917. Minsk held Pinsk.
- **Correction, pin.** The pool's 52.1667, 25.95 falls ~26 km east in open country. Wikipedia, 'Motal': 52°08'N 25°36'E, that is **52.133, 25.600**. Nothing in the village fixes a tighter point.
- **Gaps.** No consulted source gives coordinates for the Pinsk school building; the stop sits at the city pin and says so. Nothing ties his Hovevei Zion exposure to a named circle, so the campa names nobody.

## 2. The German and Swiss laboratories, 1892-1904

**[A]** 1892 Darmstadt. **[A]** 1894 Berlin-Charlottenburg, dyes and aromatic chemistry, Russian-Jewish student circles with Motzkin and Sokolow. **[A]** 1897 Fribourg, doctorate there 1899. **[A]** 1899 Geneva organic chemistry, assistant lecturer 1901. **[A]** 1900 involvement with Vera Khatzman, marriage 1906. *(Wikipedia, 'Chaim Weizmann')*

- **Contradiction, engagement date.** The pool carries the engagement to Sophia Getzowa at both 1898 and 1897. The pass argued 1897: Wikipedia places the Fribourg move in 1897, then says "That year he became engaged," and the engagement ran four years before he told her about Vera Khatzman in 1900. Left standing; the campa asserts no year.
- **Two corrections.** The pool's Geneva pin 46.1955, 6.1447 falls ~410 m south of the university; Wikipedia, 'University of Geneva' gives **46.19917, 6.14500**. And the pool put the marriage in Switzerland; Wikipedia, 'Vera Weizmann' has it "in Zoppot, West Prussia (present-day Sopot, Poland)", pin 54.4418, 18.5601.
- **[R]** The mock trial his fellow students are said to have staged over the broken engagement rests on biographical tradition (Norman Rose and related accounts), unverified. Carried with the word "tradition" doing the work in the prose.

## 3. The congresses, 1898-1902

**[A]** Misses the First Congress (1897), attends the Second (1898). **[A]** At the Fifth (1901) presses with Buber and Berthold Feiwel for a Jewish university, and the Democratic Fraction is organized there by Weizmann, Buber, Motzkin and Feiwel against Herzl's diplomatic-only method, claiming Ahad Ha'am as authority without his participation *(`ahad_ha_am.journey.json`)*. **[A]** 1902 co-founding of the Jüdischer Verlag with Buber, Feiwel and E. M. Lilien *(`martin_buber.journey.json`)*. Weizmann is a founder, so that is his own stop, not a borrowed one.

- **Coordinate divergence in the corpus, recorded not resolved.** Four pins for the Basel congress hall circulate: **47.5559, 7.5883** (`theodor_herzl`, `martin_buber`, `samuel_preiswerk`), **47.5596, 7.5886** (`ahad_ha_am`), **47.5556, 7.5878** (this pool), and de.wikipedia's 47.554444, 7.589444. This file takes the three-file majority and names the divergence in each `date_confidence`; the lens's `ahad_ha_am` value is recorded, not followed. Dates 1898-08-28 and 1901-12-26 inherited byte-identical.

## 4. Manchester, 1904-1914

**[A]** 1904 senior lecturer at Manchester. **[A]** 1905 consulting for Clayton Aniline; elected to the General Zionist Council *(Jewish Virtual Library)*. **[A]** 1907 first visit to Ottoman Palestine, confirming the commitment and the dissatisfaction with existing colonization methods. **[A]** 1910 naturalized, papers reportedly signed by Churchill as Home Secretary; no stop, because the pool attaches no place beyond "United Kingdom." It sits in the Manchester campa.

- **Contradiction, the Balfour meeting.** The pool dates it 1905 in one entry, 1906 in another, calling the year disputed. `arthur_balfour.journey.json` carries the stop at **1906**, pin **53.4808, -2.2426**, naming Charles Dreyfus as intermediary. Both inherited byte-identical, dispute stated in `date_confidence`. The site within Manchester is unconfirmed.
- **Correction, the dialogue.** The pool's draft had Weizmann saying that abandoning Jerusalem would be like an Englishman abandoning London, a symmetry the memoir does not contain. Wikipedia's version, followed: "Would you give up London to live in Saskatchewan?", Balfour answering that the British have always had London, Weizmann answering "Yes, and we lived in Jerusalem when London was still a marsh." Meeting **[A]**, words **[R]**, said so in the campa's prose, not only in metadata.

## 5. The war, the acetone, and the Declaration, 1914-1917

**[A]** 12 December 1914, Balfour's house at Carlton Gardens, "It is a great cause and I understand it," with no policy behind it. **[A]** 1915, the anaerobic bacterium converting starch to acetone, butanol and ethanol, wanted for cordite. **[A]** June 1917, Ahad Ha'am consulted on the drafts in London, arguing for "national home" against language implying a single Jewish nationality; his name is on no draft. **[A]** Summer 1917, drafts between the Zionist office and the Foreign Office, Balfour revising in August. **[A]** 31 October War Cabinet approval, 2 November the signed letter to Lord Rothschild. *(Balfour and Ahad Ha'am stops inherited byte-identical from their own files.)*

- **Correction, the pilot plant.** The pool pinned a "J. & W. Nicholson distillery, Bow" at 51.5292, -0.0246, ~1.5 km west of the works. Wikipedia, 'J&W Nicholson & Co': the firm bought the Three Mills Distillery at Bromley-by-Bow in 1872, and "Three Mills was used by the government to produce acetone during the first world war." Corrected to ~**51.5283, -0.0035**. Attributing the pilot work to Nicholson's is still the pool's synthesis.
- **Contradiction, the London house.** Wikipedia gives 16 Addison Road; English Heritage's blue plaque of 1980 is at **67**, ~51.4999, -0.2072, and English Heritage researches plaque addresses from residence records. The plaque address is used, the conflict stated not resolved.
- **[R]** New Court, St Swithin's Lane as the letter's receiving point is an inference from the addressee, named in the campa but given no pin. **[R]** 31 October 1917, president of the British Zionist Federation: unused pool material.
- **[R] The acetone legend.** That the fermentation was bartered for British support of the Declaration is a popularization. Wikipedia's 'Balfour Declaration', consulted directly, does not mention an acetone bargain at all, and the pass found no source naming and debunking the myth, so the flag rests on absence. The journey makes no quid-pro-quo claim.

## 6. The Commission, Faisal, and the Peace Conference, 1918-1919

**[A]** 1918 Zionist Commission to Palestine. **[A]** June 1918, the meeting with Emir Faisal. **[A]** February 1919, the statement of Zionist aims at the Paris Peace Conference.

- **[R]** The Dead Sea mineral survey (31.5590, 35.4732, April 1918). **Stop dropped for space and recorded here:** cut to hold the file at 45 stops and make room for the Peel testimony, which carries a verified quote. Restore it to segment five if wanted.
- **[R]** For the Faisal meeting: the 4 June date, the camp coordinates (29.5321, 35.0063) and the T. E. Lawrence brokerage are the pool's synthesis, not fetched sources.
- **Correction, signing venue.** The pool had the agreement signed in **Paris**. Wikipedia, 'Faisal-Weizmann Agreement': 3 January 1919 at the **Carlton Hotel, London** (~51.5083, -0.1315). The Charing Cross fallback pin is dropped. Faisal's Arabic reservation is the stop's quote; the memorandum it names is dated the day *after* the signing.
- **Correction, quote citation.** "as Jewish as England is English" was cited only to "public statements on Zionist aims." Wikipedia dates it to the Peace Conference, February 1919, citing Cleveland (2004) p. 228 and *Trial and Error*.
- **One-directional, cited not staged.** Balfour's memorandum to Curzon of 11 August 1919, that Britain does not propose to consult the present inhabitants and that Zionism outweighs the desires of the 700,000 Arabs there, is **Balfour's** stop and stays on his file. Here it is inside the Paris campa as the weather Weizmann worked in, and in `sources`.

## 7. The presidency of a movement, 1920-1931

**[A]** 1920 first WZO presidency, held to 1931. **[A]** July 1920 the London congress founds Keren Hayesod with Weizmann, Einstein and Jabotinsky among its early leaders; Weizmann brings Jabotinsky onto the Executive the next year, and **[A]** in January 1923 Jabotinsky resigns, contrasting his urgency with "Weizmann's method." **[A]** 1 April 1925 the Hebrew University opens on Mount Scopus. **[A]** Shortly before 19 March 1930 Weizmann visits Balfour at Hook Heath, Woking. **[A]** 1931 loses the presidency after the Passfield White Paper. *(Jabotinsky stops from `ze_ev_jabotinsky.journey.json`, the others from `arthur_balfour.journey.json`, inherited byte-identical.)*

- **Correction, the "dazed" quote.** "tired, hungry, thirsty and completely dazed" is verbatim in Smithsonian Magazine's article on Einstein's 1921 welcome, but the pool's *context* was wrong: it does not describe arriving in Manhattan. The motorcade went slowly through the Lower East Side instead of to the hotel, and the party reached the Hotel Commodore at 11:30 p.m. on 2 April 1921 in that state.
- **Coordinate flagged as this file's own.** No Commodore pin exists in the pool, whose alternatives were a generic New York point and the Biltmore. The stop uses ~**40.7519, -73.9755** and says so. **Not staged here:** `einstein.journey.json` has Einstein lecturing four days at Princeton on that tour, and nothing places Weizmann there.
- **[R] and imprecise, Cleveland 1921.** The confrontation with Brandeis's American faction is the pool's synthesis, no primary account refetched, and **the pool gives the year only**. The stop defaults to 1921-01-01, earlier in the calendar than the April tour it followed; the segment keeps historical order and says so.
- **Divergence recorded, Mount Scopus.** `einstein.journey.json` uses 31.7788, 35.2396 for a 1923 lecture on the same hill; `martin_buber.journey.json` flags 31.7936, 35.2447.
- **Honest gap, the 1925 opening.** The pool's source lists Balfour, Allenby, Churchill and Herbert Samuel as present but **does not explicitly place Weizmann on the platform**. His centrality to the founding from 1918 is separately attested; the campa claims the founding, not the platform.
- **Gap, the 1931 congress.** The pool names neither month nor city. Month and day default to 01; the stop sits at the standing Basel pin as this file's **[R]** inference from the congress series.

## 8. Rehovot and the closing of the doors, 1934-1946

**[A]** 1934 the Daniel Sieff Research Institute at Rehovot, Weizmann directing after Fritz Haber's death forecloses that appointment. **[A]** 25 November 1936 Peel Commission testimony *(Letters and Papers: Series B, 1983, p. 102)*. **[A]** 1939 the St James's Conference and the White Paper capping immigration at 75,000 over five years; the pool gives the year only. **[A]** May 1942 the Biltmore Program. **[A]** December 1946, not reelected at the 22nd Congress, Basel; the Massada statement quoted there is verbatim, semicolons and capitalised "It" included, citation *Letters and Papers*, Series B, Papers vol. II (1984), Paper 87, pp. 636-37. **[A]** 1935 return to the Zionist Organization presidency to 1946: no place in the pool, so no stop.

- **Gap, the Peel testimony.** The pool gives date and text but **no place**. The stop sits at the Jerusalem pin because the Commission's late-1936 public hearings sat in Palestine, and `date_confidence` marks that inference **[R]**.
- **Correction, citation.** The Chamberlain letter of 29 August 1939 is verbatim; Wikipedia's reference is the JTA report of 6 September 1939. The pass **did not open** the JTA archive page, so the date is cited *via* that report, and `quote_source` says so.
- **Tag corrected upward, Michael Weizmann.** The pool carried the son's death at **[R]** because the CWGC listing was unfetched. Raised to **[A]**: Wikipedia states that Flight Lieutenant Michael Oser Weizmann (1916-1942), No. 502 Squadron RAF, was shot down over the Bay of Biscay in February 1942, and Times of Israel gives his age as 25 and the body as never recovered. The CWGC clause is dropped rather than hedging the event.
- **Correction, house coordinates.** The Rehovot house was pinned at 31.9061, 34.8074, a kilometre west on the campus. Wikipedia, 'Weizmann House': **31.9065, 34.8186**. Every house stop uses it; the two campus stops keep 31.9070, 34.8093.

## 9. Statehood, the presidency, and the garden, 1947-1952

**[A]** 18 March 1948, the secret Truman meeting after Eddie Jacobson's appeal: broadly documented, no primary transcript refetched, so the meeting is **[A]** and its content **[R]**. **[R]** 25 May 1948, the public Washington meeting and the Torah scroll, carried with the pool's qualification (Clifford and the American Zionist organizations, not Weizmann alone) inside the campa. **[R]** November 1951 Knesset reelection: pool synthesis, seat coordinate not reverified. **[A]** 9 November 1952, death at Rehovot, aged 77; Vera died 24 September 1966 and lies beside him.

- **Correction, and the largest in the pool.** The pool placed the 1947 UN appearance at **Flushing Meadows** before the General Assembly. Wrong twice: his documented testimony was before **UNSCOP in Palestine**, in its twelve public hearings of 4-17 July, "as a private citizen" holding no office *(Wikipedia, 'UNSCOP')*, and his Negev address was to the **Ad Hoc Committee at Lake Success** in October. The file has **two** stops where the pool had one wrong one, both coordinates the pass's approximations.
- **Correction, city of the Provisional State Council.** The pool had "Beit Ha'am, **Jerusalem**," 31.7807, 35.2192. Wikipedia, 'Provisional State Council': it met in **Tel Aviv** at the Jewish National Fund building, and Jerusalem was under siege. Pin moved to ~**32.0637, 34.7708**. The rest holds: elected in absentia from the United States, serving 16 May 1948 to 17 February 1949.
- **Correction, two dates collapsed.** The pool gave 1949-02-17 for the election. Wikipedia, 'President of Israel': "The first presidential election took place on 16 February 1949." Elected **16 February**, sworn in **17 February**.
- **Placement flagged.** The swearing-in was in Jerusalem, but the pool gives no coordinate for that chamber and its one Jerusalem chamber pin belongs to a body that met in Tel Aviv. Rather than invent one, the stop sits at the Weizmann House, presidential residence from 1949, and says where the ceremony was. The 2 November 1949 renaming of the institute is in the same campa.
- **Flagged, the death.** The pool's "5:55 a.m." appears in **no source the pass fetched** and is dropped; the two heart attacks are kept. Burial in the garden is **[A]** *(Wikipedia, 'Weizmann House')*; the burial **date** of 11 November is unsourced and **[R]**.
- **One-directional, licensed.** Ben-Gurion had Abba Eban offer the presidency to Einstein, who declined from Princeton on 18 November 1952 *(`einstein.journey.json`)*. That is Einstein's stop and stays there; here it is one closing sentence in the burial campa, the licensed direction, because the offer exists only because his office was vacant.

## Afterlife, and two quote decisions

**Dropped as unsupported:** the pool's claim of a December 1952 'President Dr. Weizmann' commemorative stamp, in no fetched source. **Citation corrected:** "A state cannot be created by decree" is verbatim but was cited to an unspecified "Series B"; it is *Letters and Papers*, Papers vol. I (1983), p. 301. **Weakest-sourced quote in the pool, not used:** "Should Palestine fall within the British sphere of influence... a very effective guard for the Suez Canal", wording confirmed and dated 1914, but Wikipedia's only citation is a news article (Al Jazeera, 2017) and no primary document is identified. Left out rather than attributed to a document nobody has seen.

## Apparatus relations for the operator

Weizmann is **not** in `EXCEPTIONS.md`, so no framework-applied stops are licensed here. Four interlock relations are **not rooted in his own life**, and all four run **forward**, the direction the exceptions file never licenses. None is in the journey. Named rather than silently deleted:

1. **Forward, `adi_shamir`.** Born Tel Aviv 6 July 1952, four months before Weizmann's death; MSc and PhD at the Weizmann Institute; nearly his whole career on the campus bearing the name. Belongs on Shamir's file.
2. **Forward, `benjamin_netanyahu`.** Benzion Netanyahu was Jabotinsky's secretary, and Revisionism broke from Weizmann's Synthetic Zionism in 1923, making Netanyahu the ideological heir of Weizmann's chief internal rival. The 1923 break **is** in this file, at Weizmann's own London pin, because he lived it; the extension to a man born in 1949 is not, and the pool itself calls that framing inference.

The actors' scene, not his.
