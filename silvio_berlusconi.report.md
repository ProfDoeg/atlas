# Silvio Berlusconi, 1936-2023: research report

*Atlas of Journeys working file. Compiled 2026-08-06 from the verified research pool for slug `silvio_berlusconi`. This is the evidence layer under the journey file and does not share its register.*

**Legend.** **[A]** = attested, source named in the same sentence. **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition, or press commonplace not run down to a primary record in this pass. Contradictions are flagged and **not adjudicated**; where the pool's own lenses disagreed with each other, that is recorded rather than smoothed over. Coordinates carry their provenance, because several in the raw pool were wrong by kilometres.

## 1. Milan, family, formation (1936-1961)

Born **29 September 1936 in Milan** to **Luigi Berlusconi**, an employee of the Banca Rasini, and **Rosa Bossi**; eldest of three **[A, en.wikipedia "Silvio Berlusconi"]**. Salesian college, then law at the University of Milan **[A, same]**. **Law degree 1961, with honours, thesis on the legal aspects of advertising** **[A, same]**, located by the pool at **Universita degli Studi di Milano, via Festa del Perdono 7, 45.4595 / 9.1963**; that coordinate checks out and is used unchanged.

**Double bass, cruise-ship crooning and bar work during the student years, with Fedele Confalonieri**, later Mediaset's chairman **[R]**. The pool tagged this **[A]** in the chronology lens and **[R]** in the geography lens, the latter noting that no ship name, line, or itinerary was located. **[R] is the honest tag** and is what the file carries. **No coordinate exists for the episode**, so there is no stop for it; it is folded into the university stop rather than given an invented Mediterranean point. **Carla Dall'Oglio married 1965, Marina born 1966, Pier Silvio 1969, divorce 1985** **[A, same]**: none carries a place more specific than "Italy", so none becomes a stop.

## 2. Concrete: via Alciati, Brugherio, Milano 2 (1961-1979)

The phase the raw pool got most wrong. The corrections change the map.

- **First land: via Alciati, Milan, 1961, 190 million lire, guaranteed by the banker Carlo Rasini**, his father's employer **[A, it.wikipedia "Edilnord"]**. **First large development: a model town for about 4,000 residents at BRUGHERIO, begun 1964, first blocks ready 1965, built by Edilnord** **[A, same]**. The pool's geography lens had placed the whole 1961-1969 period at **Segrate**, which is wrong; Segrate enters only with Milano 2 at the end of the decade. The Brugherio stop uses **45.55 / 9.30**, a comune-level coordinate, **not** a verified site.
- **Milano 2, Segrate, about 4,000 apartments, Edilnord, 1969-1979** **[A, it.wikipedia "Milano 2"]**, correcting the pool's 1970 start. **Coordinate correction, load-bearing.** The pool gave Milano 2 as **45.4783 / 9.318**, which lands near Linate and Novegro, outside the development, about 4.5 km off. it.wikipedia gives **45.4978 / 9.2641** (OpenStreetMap 45.4944 / 9.2647). **The file uses 45.4978 / 9.2641** for both Milano 2 stops.
- **Villa San Martino, Arcore, bought December 1974** from **Anna Maria Casati Stampa**, **500 million lire in stock certificates against a valuation near 1.7 billion**, the family broken by the **1970 murder-suicide** **[A, it.wikipedia "Villa San Martino (Arcore)"]**. **Internal contradiction in the pool:** its afterlife lens dated the purchase to 1973 in one item and 1974 in that item's own note, while the geography lens said 1974. it.wikipedia says December 1974. **1974 used.**
- **Coordinate correction.** Three points for one house appeared across the pool: 45.6303 / 9.321 (geography, twice) and 45.6206 / 9.3239 (afterlife, four times). OpenStreetMap gives **45.6264 / 9.3169**. **All Arcore stops use it.**
- **Fininvest founded 1975; Cavaliere del Lavoro 1977** **[A, en.wikipedia]**.

## 3. The cable, the networks, the law (1974-1993)

**TeleMilano begins cable transmission inside Milano 2 on 25 September 1974** **[A, it.wikipedia "TeleMilano"]**. The pool's geography lens dated it September **1975** and said Berlusconi launched it. **Both wrong.** It was founded by **Giacomo Properzj and Alceo Moretti** with Pierfranco Faletti; Berlusconi **bought** it, which en.wikipedia states outright ("buying from Giacomo Properzj and Alceo Moretti a small cable television company, TeleMilano").

Then, all **[A, en.wikipedia]**: **1977**, transmission moved into central Milan, cable to airwaves; **1980**, Canale 5, assembled by synchronising local stations because national private broadcasting was reserved to RAI; **1982**, Italia 1 from Rusconi; **1984**, Rete 4 from Mondadori, magistrates ordering the channels dark and an emergency government decree restoring nationwide transmission; **1990**, the Mammi law granting the three channels full national status; **Mediaset**, Cologno Monzese operational from 1978 and the brand formalised 15 December 1993, 45.5312 / 9.2762. Two additions from general record rather than the pool, both **[R]**: that the 1984 decree was **Craxi's**, and that Christian Democrat ministers resigned over the Mammi law. The Craxi attribution is **the one claim in the journey file that goes past the pool**, flagged here for that reason.

## 4. Propaganda Due

**Joined P2 in 1978** **[A, en.wikipedia "Silvio Berlusconi": "In 1978 he joined the Propaganda Due masonic lodge."]**. The pool's chronology lens hedged to "1970s" and tagged **[R]** on the grounds that the source did not fix a date. It does. **Tag lifted to [A].**

**March 1981: a list of 962 alleged members found at Licio Gelli's premises** **[A, en.wikipedia "Propaganda Due"]**. **Place correction:** the raid was on Gelli's **Giole textile works at Castiglion Fibocchi, province of Arezzo**, not the city of Arezzo, about 20 km apart. The file uses **43.5083 / 11.7683**, comune-level; the factory site is not fixed in accessible sources. The day is commonly given as 17 March, but the pool gives only the month, so the file uses **1981-03-01** with the hedge in `date_confidence`.

## 5. AC Milan (1986-2017)

Bought **February 1986**, sold **13 April 2017** to **Rossoneri Sport Investment Lux for 830 million euros after 31 years** **[A, en.wikipedia]**. **Internal contradiction:** the pool's afterlife lens says the ownership ran "1986 to 2012", while its chronology and geography lenses both say 2017. **2017 is correct.** **European Cup final, Athens, 18 May 1994, Milan 4 Barcelona 0** **[A, UEFA record]**, 38.0356 / 23.7822. The 2003 Old Trafford final is **[A]** in the pool but **not** a stop: the file was at its ceiling. **Gap:** the pool found **no documented Berlusconi monument at San Siro or Milanello**, but this rests on the absence of a mention in one article, which is not evidence of absence. Unproven negative, **[R]**.

## 6. The three governments

The pool's chronology lens **conflated election dates with swearing-in and term-start dates four times over**. All corrected:

| Event | Pool said | Correct |
|---|---|---|
| First election win | 1994-04-15 | election **27-28 March 1994**; 15 April is the seating of parliament |
| Berlusconi I sworn | 1994-05-11 | **10 May 1994** |
| First resignation | 1995-01-17 | resigned **22 December 1994**; 17 Jan 1995 is Dini's accession (253 days) |
| Elected MEP | 1999-07-20 | election **13 June 1999**; 20 July is the term opening |
| Second election / PM | both 2001-06-11 | election **13 May 2001**; sworn **11 June 2001** |
| Third election / PM | both 2008-05-08 | election **13-14 April 2008**; sworn **8 May 2008** |
| Re-elected MEP | 2019-07-02 | election **26 May 2019**; 2 July is the term opening |
| Final resignation | 2011-11-16 | **resigned 12 November 2011**; the cabinet ended 16 November with Monti |

Sources are the en.wikipedia cabinet and election articles named. All Palazzo Chigi stops are pinned to the **swearing-in**, with the election date in the narrative, so no stop asserts a date the record contradicts.

Other items, all **[A]** unless marked: **26 January 1994**, the videotaped "discesa in campo" and the founding of Forza Italia, no venue given anywhere; **Lega Nord and Alleanza Nazionale in the first cabinet**, Bossi withdrawing that December, the first cabinet presence of the MSI's successors since 1945 being general record and **[R]** as to framing; **Palazzo Grazioli, via del Plebiscito 102, rented about 1995 to 2021**, 41.8967 / 12.4805; **G8 Genoa, 19-22 July 2001, Palazzo Ducale**, with Carlo Giuliani's killing and the Diaz raid; **April 2005**, eleven of thirteen regions lost; **17 May 2006**, second term ends, and **25 June 2006**, the constitutional reform rejected 61.3 per cent; **1 March 2006**, address to a joint meeting of Congress, Capitol 38.8899 / -77.0091; **18 November 2007**, The People of Freedom announced in Piazza San Babila, 45.4666 / 9.1974, Forza Italia dissolved 21 November 2008; **30 August 2008**, the Italy-Libya Treaty at Benghazi, 32.1167 / 20.0667, the five-billion figure and the migration quid pro quo being general record and **[R]**; **June 2009**, Gaddafi's tent at Villa Doria Pamphilj, 41.8925 / 12.4553; **G8 L'Aquila, 8-10 July 2009, Coppito**, 42.3742 / 13.3287.

**Two venue corrections.** The **G7 Naples** venue, which the pool called unconfirmed, was the **Royal Palace of Naples**, roughly **40.8365 / 14.2497** **[A, "20th G7 summit"]**; the city-centre placeholder is dropped. The **2011 resignation** was not announced from Palazzo Grazioli, as the pool had it, but **tendered to President Napolitano at the Quirinale** on the evening of 12 November, with crowds at both addresses; **the stop is at the Quirinale, 41.8996 / 12.4869**.

**Two deliberate omissions.** **Crawford, Texas, 2003** is **[R]**, press-derived with no itinerary verified, so it is not a stop and survives only as a hedged clause in the Capitol narrative. **The 2009 White House meeting with Obama** is **[A]** but undated past the year and thin; not a stop. Separately, the **13 December 2009 statuette attack** is **[A]** on BBC and Reuters reporting, but no dedicated article for it could be retrieved, and that limit is carried into the stop.

## 7. Arcore, the courts, the Senate

**February to May 2010: the alleged Arcore dinners involving Karima El Mahroug, then 17** **[A, en.wikipedia "Karima El Mahroug"]**, correcting the pool's looser "2010-2011". **24 June 2013:** Milan convicts, seven years plus lifetime ban; **18 July 2014:** appeal overturns both counts; **March 2015:** Cassation makes the acquittal final **[A, same]**. **1 August 2013:** Cassation confirms the Mediaset tax-fraud conviction, four years with three remitted and an office ban, served as community service **[A]**, Piazza Cavour 41.9042 / 12.4667. **27 November 2013:** expelled from the Senate under the Severino law **[A]**, Palazzo Madama 41.8994 / 12.4739; **Forza Italia relaunched the same year [A]**. **28 December 2012:** divorce settlement near 48 million dollars a year to Veronica Lario, who announced the filing in May 2009 **[A]**, carried in the pool's own units. **13 October 2022:** returns to the Senate for Monza **[A]**.

**11-12 September 2015, Crimea with Putin: place correction, large.** The pool put it at "Foros / Yalta area, 44.4064 / 33.7864". Foros is not documented as a stop and that point is about 30 km from each documented one. The trip took them to **Chersonesus Taurica in Sevastopol** (about 44.6117 / 33.4930) and to the **Massandra winery above Yalta**, **44.5169 / 34.1867** **[A, en.wikipedia "Massandra Winery"]**, which also records the 1775 bottle behind the embezzlement case. **The file uses Massandra.**

## 8. Death and afterlife

- **12 June 2023, 9:30 a.m., San Raffaele hospital, via Olgettina 60, Milan; 86; complications of chronic myelomonocytic leukaemia; Forbes at death 6.8 billion dollars; a national day of mourning, flags at half mast three days, and the rector Tomaso Montanari publicly refusing to lower his university's flag** **[A, "Death and state funeral of Silvio Berlusconi"]**, 45.5049 / 9.2632.
- **14 June 2023: state funeral at Milan Cathedral, Ambrosian rite, Archbishop Mario Delpini, about 2,300 inside and roughly 15,000 in the piazza; Mattarella, Meloni, four former premiers, Orban and Infantino present, Conte declining. Cremation at the Tempio Crematorio Valenziano "Panta Rei", Valenza; ashes interred in the chapel of Saint Martin at Villa San Martino beside Luigi, Rosa, and Maria Antonietta** **[A, same]**. The Valenza coordinate 45.018 / 8.639 is taken from the pool and **not** verified against a second source; flagged.
- **"Volta celeste", the Pietro Cascella monument at Arcore**, 100 tonnes of marble and travertine with an underground chamber **[A, it.wikipedia]**. **The 1990 commission date is [R]**: it.wikipedia gives no year, so the file states the monument without dating the commission. **Unresolved:** it is described both as a completed "mausoleo funebre" and as never having received the authorisations a burial site requires, and Wikipedia does not say whether it was ever legally viable as a tomb. **Not adjudicated.**
- **"Una storia italiana": [R], redated from 1994 to 2001**, the spring campaign booklet for the 13 May election, the run commonly put near twelve million copies. **Caveat kept:** no dedicated Wikipedia article exists (en 404s; the it page of that title is a 1993 RAI miniseries), and "2001 Italian general election" does not mention it. This rests on general press record alone and appears only inside the 2001 stop's narrative, with the print run hedged.

## Gaps, stated as gaps

**No birth address**: city only, so the birth stop is a city-centre placeholder, not a landmark. **No cruise-ship record**: no ship, line, itinerary, or dates beyond "student years", so no stop. **No venue for the 26 January 1994 videotape**: city placeholder. **The Giole works at Castiglion Fibocchi** and **the Brugherio development site** are not fixed at street level; comune coordinates are used for both. **The Mammi law's parliamentary geography** (chamber, vote) is not in the pool, so the file sites the law's consequence at Cologno Monzese, the place it benefited, rather than inventing a Rome pin. **Quote dating**: several Wikiquote items carry no date. They sit at stops whose subject they fit, with the source and its own date in `quote_source`, and are never presented as spoken on the stop's date.

## Quotations corrected before use

Five of the fourteen were truncated or misattributed, and all are restored in the journey file's `quote` fields. The **"wrong side"** line was cut before its real clause, *"They were against Hitler, but not against Stalin"* (la Repubblica, 3 February 2005). The **Bucha** line lost *"and other localities"* (BBC News, 23 September 2022). The **Erin Burnett** line lost *"and there are a lot of them"*; the pool correctly flagged it as a remark about Berlusconi rather than by him, and `quote_source` attributes it to Burnett, never to him (26 February 2013). The **Mussolini "confino"** line was both misworded and misdated: the real source is the interview with **The Spectator, 4 September 2003**, reported by Corriere.it on 11 September 2003, **not** Holocaust Memorial Day 2013, which was a separate remark about the racial laws. Redated, and the **[R]** lifted. The **2001 boast** was truncated after "confrontarsi con me"; its source is ANSA, 7 March 2001, cited in Gomez and Travaglio, *Le mille balle blu* (Bur, 2006), p. 2137.

## Interlock with the existing atlas

Named in the journey narrative where the paths genuinely cross, and nowhere else.

- **napoleon** at the Duomo: crowned himself King of Italy under that vault on 26 May 1805; the state funeral is in the same building on 14 June 2023. **The canonical Duomo pin, 45.464 / 9.1895, is inherited byte-identical** for both cathedral stops.
- **mark_twain** at the same pin: he climbed that roof in July 1867, and the 2009 attack is with a souvenir model of it.
- **mussolini** at Palazzo Chigi, 1994. **Piazzale Loreto (45.4865 / 9.2162) is not reused**, Berlusconi being nowhere attested there; the crossing is political, not geographic, and the file says so in words rather than by planting a pin. Predappio is likewise not reused, per the lens's warning.
- **dannunzio** at the University of Milan: *Il Fuoco* published there in 1900, *La figlia di Iorio* at the Teatro Lirico (45.4718 / 9.1832), the same cluster as the law faculty. **That pin is not inherited**; there is no attested Berlusconi event at it.
- **constantino_el_grande** at the Canale 5 launch: the February 313 settlement at Milan. The lens gives no coordinate and none is invented.
- **mikhail_bakunin** at Genoa: he fled there under a false name in October 1870. The lens tagged this **[R]**, and the crossing is stated as coincidence of place, not of cause.

**Not inherited**, per the lens's warning: the Kaaba, Temple Mount, Paris, and Buenos Aires pins, none of which has an attested Berlusconi connection. **La Cinq, his 1986 French channel, is not made a Paris stop**: the pool gives only "France" as its place, and using it to justify the Paris pin would be the force-inheritance the lens warned against. **fermi** is not named, the Rome overlap being **[R]** and the only shared fact the city. **giulio_camillo** is not named: the lens flagged its own Milan claim as unverified at stop level and asked that the file be opened first, and it was not opened in this pass. **An open gap.**
