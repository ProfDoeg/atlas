## The canon and its register
The canon here is Blahyi's own testimony — his sworn account before Liberia's Truth and Reconciliation Commission (Jan 2008, broadcast live nationwide), his 2013 autobiography, his many interviews, and the documentary *The Redemption of General Butt Naked* (Strauss & Anastasion, 2011). Per the brief, the sacred-and-monstrous is told straight: the atrocities as fact, the vision as the event he testifies to, the conversion as real. This is the modern Saul-on-the-Damascus-road carried to the extreme, and the register neither excuses the horror nor denies the turning. The miraculous — the devil's bargain in the grove, the belief that nakedness and rites make him bulletproof, the Man of light on the battlefield — is narrated as event, because in his canon it is.

## Judgment calls
- **Coordinates for the grove** (initiation, first sacrifice, devil's covenant) are *traditional* — placed in the interior Sarpo/Sapo forest of Sinoe County (~5.6, -9.0), the tribe's homeland, since no site is named. Nudged apart by hundredths of a degree so the three grove-stops don't stack on one pin.
- **The 20,000 figure** is stated as his own confession, not as verified body-count. The canon *is* his testimony; the report flags that historians (Lidow, Stanford; fellow warlords) dispute the number as inflated — but the register places what he testified, and he testified 20,000.
- **The disputed childhood.** His relatives say he was a third-grade dropout and market boy turned criminal, not a boy-priest. The brief's register renders the life *as he narrates it*, so the grove is told as event; this report is the honest place to note the fold.
- **Dates.** Attested where anchored (birth 30 Sep 1971; Nimba invasion 24 Dec 1989; Doe's death 9 Sep 1990; April 6 1996 battle; TRC Jan 2008). Initiation ages (7, 11) fix traditional years; brigade/battle stops are inferred/traditional within the mid-1990s. The present-day pulpit stop is dated 2020 as a stand-in for the ongoing present.

## The five richest episodes
1. **The altar of the grove** (1982) — the eleven-year-old's first sacrifice, the beating heart eaten; the origin of the fourteen-year monthly offering.
2. **The battle of April 6** (1996) — the general atop a truck, rifle in one hand and a severed trophy in the other; the day's dead he later counts.
3. **The vision of the Man of light** (July 1996) — the Damascus event: "Repent and live, or refuse and die"; his last day on the battlefield.
4. **The TRC confession** (Jan 2008) — before the nation at the Centennial Pavilion: "I'm 100 percent guilty… They are right not to forgive me."
5. **The villages of the dead** — the five-year road begging pardon of the families, the bulletproof general now defenceless.

## The tradition's folds and gaps
Two canons war inside this life: the testimony (grove, sacrifice, vision) and the skeptics (inflated toll, invented priesthood, conversion-as-legal-shield). The register follows the testimony but the report marks both. Geographic gaps: the grove has no attested site; the Ghana camp is Buduburam (attested); the exact TRC venue is the Centennial Memorial Pavilion, Monrovia.
