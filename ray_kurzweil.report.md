# Ray Kurzweil (b. 1948): research report
*2026-08-27, for `ray_kurzweil.journey.json`. Subject living. 43 stops, 9 segments.*

**[A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or dossier-only. Contradictions are
recorded, **not adjudicated**. Unreachable sources are named with the reason. **FIX** marks a correction
applied to the research pool.

**Standing caution.** Kurzweil is his own principal biographer. Most of what circulates traces to his
books, thekurzweillibrary.com, his interviews, and profiles reprinting those. The Academy of Achievement
transcript and the American Foundation for the Blind (AFB) oral history are detailed but **self-report**;
Wikipedia's article on him is the densest reachable secondary source and is itself largely built from
that circuit. Figures living only inside it are **[R]** here even when repeated everywhere.

## Interlocks: who is named in a campa, and who is not

A name enters a campa only where **Kurzweil himself** has a direct or indirect relation to that person.
Shared geography is not a relation.

**Named.** **`john_von_neumann`**, in the *Age of Spiritual Machines* stop: Kurzweil credits him with the
first use of *singularity* for a point past which prediction fails, a real one-directional citation
relation, since von Neumann could never have cited Kurzweil **[R as to the attribution's accuracy]**,
hedged in the campa's own wording. **`alan_turing`**, in the Long Bet stop: the wager is formally on
**Turing's** test, which Kurzweil has argued about in print for thirty years, and the campa names

Intelligence". **`john_searle`**, in *The Singularity Is Near* stop: Kurzweil answers the Chinese Room
inside the book **[R: dossier-stated; the book's text was not fetched]**, but Searle's 1980 Berkeley
paper is atlas-attested and the relation has the right shape, Kurzweil engaging Searle whether or not
Searle engaged back.

**Deliberately not named.**
