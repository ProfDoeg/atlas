# Journey queue

Subjects approved for the atlas, in no order, NOT yet begun. Each becomes a
journey by the standing pipeline: research to a <slug>.report.md with [A]/[R]
evidence tags, then the <slug>.journey.json, then interlock against the
existing corpus (inherit canonical pins for shared sites; name those already
in the atlas who cross the road). Nothing here starts without the author
saying so.

court scribe.

## tschiffely

Aime Felix Tschiffely. Buenos Aires to Washington, 1925 to 1928, ten
thousand miles on the two criollos Mancha and Gato, the horses of the pampa
proving themselves across the Andes, the Darien, and the whole spine of the
Americas. Swiss schoolmaster from the same Buenos Aires English-school world
the corpus already walks; the ride enters the national mythology within his
lifetime and the horses are buried at Recoleta. Interlocks waiting: the
libertador geography ridden in reverse, and the Buenos Aires canonical pin
(-34.6037, -58.3816) shared by eight journeys.
