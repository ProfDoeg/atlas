# Annie Besant — report

**Sources.** Wikipedia (Annie Besant; Rudolf Steiner and the Theosophical Society; Order of the Star in the East; Jiddu Krishnamurti); Annie Besant, *An Autobiography* (1893, Gutenberg #12085); the 1888 *White Slavery in London* text (mernick.org.uk); Besant's 1889 *Pall Mall Gazette* review of *The Secret Doctrine* (blavatskyarchives.com/besant1888.htm); the Krishnamurti Foundation's text of the 3 August 1929 dissolution speech; the Nehru Archive's document on the 1917 internment; *The Case for India* (Internet Archive, her Calcutta presidential address); National Secular Society and Fabian Society institutional histories; Theosophy Wiki / TS Adyar biographical pages; GENUKI for Sibsey parish detail. Cross-referenced in-corpus against `blavatsky.journey.json` (for the shared deathbed pin) and `steiner.journey.json` (for the 1912–13 schism) and glanced at `keyserling.journey.json` (Adyar coordinates, 1911 mention of Besant).

**Judgment calls.**
- The Blavatsky-deathbed stop ("19 Avenue Road — 'Keep the link unbroken,'" 1891-05-08) is deliberately **byte-identical** in name, lat/lng, date, quote and quote_source to the corresponding stop in `blavatsky.journey.json`, per the curator's instruction — the two files share one pin rather than each inventing its own.
- The Steiner schism is told twice, once per side: `steiner.journey.json` narrates the refusal and founding from Berlin/Cologne; this file narrates Adyar's response — the December 1912 news arriving and the 7 March 1913 charter revocation. The two accounts interlock (each names the other's actor: "Besant strikes back with the charter" / "she moves to strike the German Section's charter altogether") without duplicating stops.
- Where sources gave a range rather than a day (Fern Hill schooling, first Adyar landing precise date uncertain, final Adyar years), I used the best-attested anchor date and marked `attested` since the events themselves are not in doubt, only the precise day — no `inferred` was needed anywhere in this file.
- The marriage venue is disputed between St Mary-at-Lambeth (where banns were read) and Hastings; I used Lambeth as the better-attested reading-of-banns site.
- Register: Besant is an esoteric leader and reformer, not a devotional saint, so the miraculous is rendered as *her* claimed intuition and Leadbeater's claimed clairvoyance ("an aura more radiant than any he has seen") — narrated as the event the canon records, without asserting metaphysical truth on the atlas's own behalf.

**Gaps / time-folds.** Fourteen years of steady Fabian/secularist lecturing (1878–1888) compress into a handful of anchor stops (Trafalgar Square, Bow, the School Board). Her decades of prolific Theosophical writing and lecture-touring between 1893 and 1907 are folded into two stops (first Adyar landing, Central Hindu College) rather than itemized. The Krishnamurti guardianship lawsuit (Narayaniah v. Besant, decided against her by the Privy Council in 1913–1914) is referenced but not given its own stop, to keep the Order-of-the-Star arc from swelling past the Steiner interlock's proportion.

**Five richest episodes.** (1) Cheltenham, 1871 — Mabel's whooping-cough vigil and the "angry resentment against the God who had been... torturing my helpless baby," the hinge of her whole first deconversion. (2) Westminster Hall, June 1877 — the Fruits of Philosophy trial, twenty thousand supporters outside, birth control argued in open court. (3) Bow, June–July 1888 — "White Slavery in London" and the match girls' strike, the pivot from print agitation to organized labor. (4) 17 Lansdowne Road, May 1889 — meeting Blavatsky eleven days before joining, the fastest total conversion in the whole atlas. (5) Ommen, 3 August 1929 — Krishnamurti dissolving the Order she built around him, in front of her, at eighty-two.

**Connections to the atlas.** Shares the deathbed pin with `blavatsky.journey.json` and interlocks with `steiner.journey.json` on the 1912–13 schism (each file now names the other's action). Touches `keyserling.journey.json` at Adyar (his 1911 visit under "Besant's disciples... great banyan"). Sets up a still-missing `krishnamurti.journey.json`, which this file's Order-of-the-Star and Ommen stops are written to interlock with cleanly whenever that traveler is added — it would want the beach discovery, the guardianship, the Order's founding, and the Ommen dissolution as shared or mirrored pins, the same pattern used here with Blavatsky and Steiner.
