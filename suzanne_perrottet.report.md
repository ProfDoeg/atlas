# Suzanne Perrottet — build report

**Sources.** The spine is the Sächsische Biografie entry (ISGV e.V., 'Suzanne Perrottet 1889-1983'), which gives the fullest dated chronology (birth in Rolle, Marteau's violin class, the Dalcroze demonstration circuit through Solothurn/Paris/Basel/Amsterdam, Dresden Ständehaus, Hellerau, the Russian tour, the Weißer Hirsch sanatorium meeting with Laban, and the postwar teaching decades). English/German Wikipedia and the Historisches Lexikon der Schweiz confirm and date-check the Laban/Monte Verità/Zurich arc and family details (father Émile Jules, mother Adèle Simon, son Allar/André, 1916-1956). The kreis4unterwegs.ch profile supplied the Zett-Haus/Badenerstrasse 18 studio (1931) and the Landesausstellung 1939 detail. The *Republik* piece "Wie klang Dada?" (17 Feb 2022) supplied the one verbatim first-person quote from her memoir *Ein bewegtes Leben* on playing atonal music at the Dada soirées. The atlas's own laban.journey.json and mary_wigman.journey.json supplied five byte-identical shared pins (Hellerau, Munich, Monte Verità ×4, Zurich wartime school, Cabaret Voltaire) and the single reused quote — Wigman's recollection of Perrottet's own words about Laban ("...tanzen läßt").

**Judgment calls.** (1) The curator's brief hedged her birthplace as "Vevey/Yverdon-region"; every source gives Rolle, on Lake Geneva — accurate but not the Froebel/Pestalozzi Yverdon-castle cluster the brief anticipated, so that coincidence was dropped rather than forced onto the wrong town. (2) laban.journey.json places "the break from music" in Munich, Oct 1912, while independent sources place the actual first meeting with Laban at the Lahmann-Sanatorium, Weißer Hirsch, near Dresden, months earlier in 1912. Both are kept as separate, chronologically ordered stops (sanatorium meeting → Munich formalization) rather than treated as one event or a contradiction. (3) The curator's "Mühlebachstrasse studio" could not be independently corroborated by search (only the Zett-Haus/Badenerstrasse address, 1931 onward, is documented); it is kept as a later (post-1931, "inferred") studio stop on the curator's authority, flagged here as the one unverified detail in the file. (4) The Cabaret Voltaire/Dada material folds two attested facts — she performed there 1916-19 and gave the concert programs at the larger Kaufleuten hall as the soirées grew — into two stops rather than one, to give the "only Dada door" its due weight.

**Gaps.** Exact days are unattested for most of the 1906-1936 demonstration/teaching stops (Solothurn, Paris, Basel, Amsterdam, Vienna, the school renamings, the 1921 lecture, the 1923 Gluck staging); these carry `"inferred"` day-precision on a solid year. No first-person quote survives for the Laban meeting itself or for the 1979 school closing — left `null` rather than invented.

**Five richest episodes.** (1) The Weißer Hirsch sanatorium — two overworked reformers of the body convalescing side by side, one about to un-teach the other everything Dalcroze built. (2) Hellerau, 15 Jan 1913 — her own declared apostasy, overheard by Nolde, later recorded by Wigman, and reused here as the atlas's cleanest cross-file quote. (3) Hombrechtikon, 30 Jan 1916 — Allar's birth into the "largely harmonious triangle" with Laban and his wife, the deepest root of the whole defection. (4) Cabaret Voltaire, 1916 — her own memoir's account of playing Schoenberg to ears that found it "grässlich," the atlas's only Dada door. (5) 1979 — the school she inherited from Laban in the winter of 1918 closing after sixty-one continuous years under one teacher, the longest-run institution in the whole Ausdruckstanz cluster.

**Connections in the atlas.** Five pins and one quote are byte-identical with laban.journey.json and mary_wigman.journey.json (Hellerau ×2, Munich, Monte Verità ×4, Zurich wartime school, Cabaret Voltaire — see `suggested_refs` cross-notes in each stop). Segment 7 names Vera Skoronel and Max Terpis as her own pupils, tying forward into vera_skoronel.journey.json. The file is written to pin-share the Geneva Conservatoire/Dalcroze stop for whenever jaques_dalcroze.journey.json is completed in this fleet.
