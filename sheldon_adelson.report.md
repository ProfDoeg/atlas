# Sheldon Gary Adelson (1933-2021): research report

*Written 2026-08-31 from the verified research pool for slug `sheldon_adelson`. Companion: `sheldon_adelson.journey.json`, 8 segments, 44 stops.*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction, legend, or a single uncorroborated account. Where the pool's lenses disagreed the disagreement is printed, not silently adjudicated; where a lens was corrected, the correcting source is named.

Evidentiary floor: much of the primary record was unreachable (sec.gov and justice.gov 403, the Globe and WSJ obituaries paywalled, the Jewish Press page carrying his most-quoted sentence 404). Much of what is marked [A] rests on Wikipedia's *cited wikitext*, its quotation of a source read with the `<ref>` visible, not on the source.

## 1. Dorchester, 1933 to c.1953

**Birth [A].** Boston, 4 August 1933; one-room Dorchester tenement, two parents, three siblings; father Arthur a taxi driver, mother Sarah (née Tonkin) a knitting-shop keeper. Wikipedia, 'Sheldon Adelson'; Connie Bruck, 'The Brass Ring,' *The New Yorker*, 30 June 2008. No street address reached, so the pin is the neighborhood.

**The newspaper corners [R], contradiction.** A $200 loan from an uncle buys corner pitches. The pool disagrees with itself on age: chronology lens "ten to twelve," Wikipedia **ten**, geography lens "about twelve, c. 1945." Ten from an August 1933 birth gives 1943-44, which the chronology lens's own date field carries; **the geography lens is the outlier and is not followed.** Nothing documents the loan, the uncle or the corners: it is his own founding story, repeated by the company.

**The vending machines [R], second contradiction.** Chronology lens: fifteen, $10,000 loan, 1948. Geography lens: sixteen. The pool's correction note sides with fifteen/1948, as does the journey.

**City College of New York [A as attendance].** Attended, reportedly reading real estate and finance, left without a degree; convergent across the Post, WSJ and Las Vegas Sun obituaries, none fetchable. Kahn is *not* named though his pin is used: shared enrolment a decade apart is co-location, not a relation.

**Army [R].** About three and a half years after high school, reportedly a court reporter, no combat. **No post, base, or discharge date reached**, so no pin; folded into the CCNY stop, which declares the fold.

**Serial ventures, 1950s-1970s [R].** Toiletry kits, De-Ice-It, mortgage brokering, charter tours, real estate, trade magazines; millionaire and not-millionaire more than once. Individually undated everywhere reached, so one Boston stop dated 1960-01-01 holds them all.

## 2. COMDEX and the Sands, 1979-1995

**First COMDEX [A].** MGM Grand, 3 December 1979: **167 exhibitors, 3,904 attendees** (Wikipedia, 'Comdex'); the Review-Journal rounds to 4,000. Partners Katzeff, Chafetz, Cutler, Shapiro.

**Sands Hotel bought [A], price corrected.** 1989, **$110 million**, from MGM Grand, Inc., which had taken it bundled with the Desert Inn for $167m in 1988. **The pool's "$110-128 million" range is unsupported; the $128m bound is dropped.** No month or day established. The formation of Las Vegas Sands Corp., 17 November 1988 [A], folds in here.

**Sands Expo [A], superlative corrected.** Opened **9 November 1990**. Wikipedia, 'Sands Expo': "the only privately owned and operated convention center in the United States," second-largest in the world. The pool's "largest privately owned convention facility in the United States" garbles two superlatives, and 'Las Vegas Sands' gives a third version, "in the world". Flagged, not adjudicated.

**COMDEX sold to SoftBank [A], amount unsettled.** February 1995; reported **$800m to $862m**, his share estimated above $500m. No transaction document reached, so the campa leaves the range open.

## 3. The Venetian, 1991-2001

**Marriage and honeymoon [A dates, R origin story], contradiction.** The chronology lens has him meeting Miriam Farbstein Ochshorn on a blind date in 1989 and honeymooning in Venice "that year," contradicting itself: Wikipedia, 'Miriam Adelson', has the date in 1989 and the marriage in **1991**, so the honeymoon falls in 1991 and the journey follows that. Conception of the Venetian in the Piazza San Marco is **[R]**: corporate origin narrative, no independent witness.

**The building [A].** Sands closed June 1996, imploded 26 November 1996 (wire coverage not fetched); groundbreaking 14 April 1997, roughly $1.5bn; soft opening the evening of 3 May 1999, public just after midnight on 4 May.

**The Culinary picket [A], date corrected.** More than 1,000 Culinary members picketed **1 March 1999**, joined by Representative John Lewis, whom Adelson declined to meet because the offer included union representatives. **The geography lens dated it February 1999, contradicting its own chronology lens; March is used.**

**The sons' suit [A], attribution corrected.** "Paternal kindliness" is the **trial court's** phrase: CourtListener has *Adelson v. Adelson*, Mass. Superior Court, Zobel J., 13 Mass. L. Rptr. 171, filed **18 April 2001**, as the only opinion containing it, the Appeals Court affirmance being the separate 2004 event, 60 Mass. App. Ct. 753. One stop dated 2001-04-18 carries both, rather than putting the judge's words in the appellate room.

**Mitchell Adelson's death [R].** Adopted son, dead 2005 after years of heroin and cocaine dependence; reported as a driver of the family's addiction-treatment funding and its spending against marijuana legalization. **No place of death reached**, so no stop; stated flat inside the Boston court campa.

**Peripheral neuropathy [A on convergence only].** Diagnosed 2001; eventually a motorized scooter and seated testimony. **No primary or contemporaneous medical source located**, none names where the diagnosis was made, so the pin is his workplace. Any relation to the lymphoma is unestablished.

## 4. Macau and Singapore, 2001-2010

**Beijing [R].** Meetings with Vice Premier Qian Qichen and others in 2001, brokered by Richard Suen, known chiefly through testimony in Suen's Nevada litigation (Nevada Supreme Court 58740, not fetched). **The Great Hall of the People venue is a reconstruction.**

**Sands Macao [A], cost corrected.** Opened 18 May 2004 **at $240 million**; bonds retired May 2005. **The "$240-265 million" range is dropped**, its upper bound unsourced.

**IPO [A], float corrected.** December 2004, NYSE, LVS, $29 a share, **6.8 percent** offered. **The "6.8-12 percent" range is dropped**; 12 percent appears nowhere fetched. **Forbes 2007 [R]:** about $26.5 billion, third-richest American.

**Marina Bay [A].** Bid won 27 May 2006 after submitting alone when the local partner withdrew; phased opening from 27 April 2010, cost escalating from an initial S$3.85bn commitment toward roughly S$8bn; Safdie named as architect.

**Venetian Macao [A date, R cost].** Opened **28 August 2007**. The $2.4bn cost is dossier-carried, **absent from the Wikipedia article**, unverified. The Cotai reclamation folds into this stop.

## 5. Israel Hayom and the near collapse, 2006-2011

**Yad Vashem [A].** $25 million pledged, 26 October 2006; the press release is primary but 403, corroborated by JTA (fetched).

**Israel Hayom [A].** Launched 30 July 2007; at least $50m to start, roughly **$190m accumulated losses 2007-2014**; in 2016 his legal representative said the paper was technically owned by a relative. Miriam Adelson is now publisher.

**The 2008 crisis [A on convergence only].** Roughly $8.8bn long-term debt by mid-2008; November covenant-default and bankruptcy warning; shares above $140 in 2007 under $2 in 2009; about $1bn injected by the family. **No primary filing re-fetched.**

**Jacobs dismissed [A].** July 2010; Nevada suit that October alleging he was fired for resisting instructions involving surveillance of Macau officials and the retention of lawyer-legislator Leonel Alves. No source says where, so the pin is Sands Macao.

**The Birthright remark [A as quotation, R as to venue].** December 2011, read verbatim in Wikipedia's cited wikitext with a full `<ref>` to Revital Blumenfeld, *Haaretz*, 26 December 2011; **the live page gave only headline and paraphrase (paywall)**, and **no source names the venue**, so the pin is central Jerusalem as an explicit stand-in.

## 6. The donor, 2012-2015

**TribeFest [A].** March 2012, at his own Venetian; six minutes spent on a Democratic representative, called by an organizer a luxury nobody else would have been afforded (JTA). The $93.3m 2012 super-PAC total is dossier-carried, **not re-fetched from an FEC record**.

**EuroVegas collapses [A in outline].** 2013, Alcorcón near Madrid; Sands sought tax, smoking and labor assurances Spain would not give. Business press **not fetched**. No month reached, so the date defaults to 2013-01-01.

**Yeshiva University [A], transcription conflict.** 22 October 2013. The journey uses the Wikipedia-cited Jewish Press wording ("cell phone", "OK, let it go"); **the original 404s**. Times of Israel, 24 October (fetched), gives "cellphone" and double quotes. **The two transcriptions differ and are not reconciled.**

**Online-gambling lobbying [A].** January 2015 at the Palazzo, with House Judiciary members including Jason Chaffetz; "fool's gold" is his own phrase from a signed Forbes essay, cited through Time (fetched).

**Jacobs testimony [A], undated within the year.** Several days of combative pretrial testimony in 2015, Eighth Judicial District Court; **no source gives the days**, so month and day default. Settled 31 May 2016, sum undisclosed, reported above $75m.

**Review-Journal bought [A].** News + Media Capital Group LLC, $140m, completed **11 December 2015**; Dumont's role confirmed. Courthouse News (403) and CJR not fetched.

## 7. Regulators, police, and the last years, 2016-2022

**SEC order [R as to text].** Sands had notified the Commission of likely accounting-provision violations in Macau in March 2013 [A], which the 2016 stop carries. 7 April 2016: settled cease-and-desist, no admission, $9m penalty, more than $62m to a consultant without adequate documentation, conduct 2006 to at least 2011. **sec.gov 403, order 34-77555 not read.** **DOJ [R as to text]:** 19 January 2017, $6.96m, books-and-records and internal-controls, no personal charge; **justice.gov 403**, the release not read.

**Case 2000 questioning [A], upgraded from the pool's [R].** The afterlife lens marked this uncorroborated because it checked the *Case 2000* Wikipedia article, which never mentions Adelson. Wrong article. Times of Israel (fetched): testimony **25 May 2017 at the Lahav 433 unit in Lod**, both Adelsons agreeing to speak after being told they were not suspects. JTA (fetched): **second questioning 26 June 2017 in Jerusalem**, again as non-suspects. Both now [A], each with its own stop.

**Lymphoma [A].** Disclosed 28 February 2019, after his lawyers told a Nevada court that treatment made him unfit for further deposition in the Suen case.

**The witness list [A].** December 2019, 333 names filed by the attorney general; the Adelsons and Larry Ellison on it for Case 2000, per the Times of Israel headline cited in `larry_ellison.journey.json`.

**Pollard flight [A in outline].** 30 December 2020, the Pollards flown to Israel on Adelson's aircraft; Israeli press not fetched. **He was not aboard and no source places him at the airport**, so the campa says so.

**Death and burial [A].** Malibu, 11 January 2021, complications of lymphoma treatment (White House statement DCPD-202100015). Coffin at Ben Gurion under both flags with Netanyahu at the tarmac, 14 January; private burial on the Mount of Olives, 15 January. **The pool's geography and afterlife lenses gave different pins for both places**; the journey uses the corrected values and flags the conflict in each stop.

**Posthumous [A].** March 2021 announcement, February 2022 close, of the $6.25bn sale of the Venetian, the Palazzo and the Sands Expo to VICI Properties and Apollo affiliates; buildings keep their names; the company shifts to Macau and Singapore. Its stop declares that it postdates the traveler.
