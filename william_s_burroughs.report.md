# William S. Burroughs (1914-1997)
*2026-08-17. Slug `william_s_burroughs`. From the verified pool after adversarial correction. Output: 45 stops in 9 segments.*

**[A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or inferred coordinate. Contradictions between the pool's lenses are flagged, not silently resolved; where the file follows one lens over another the choice is repeated in that stop's `date_confidence`. Fourteen pool coordinates were replaced with OpenStreetMap geocodes, errors running from 80 m (222 Bowery) to 120 km (the Putumayo, wrong side of the basin); each replacement is written out in its own stop's `date_confidence`.

A note on register. This life contains a killing, thirty years of opiate addiction, and a son dead at thirty-three of alcoholic liver failure. Per the corpus rule those are stated flat: no aestheticising, no redemption arc, no resonance drawn out.

## 4. Mexico City, and the killing of Joan Vollmer (1949-1952)

- **Cerrada de Medellín 37, Colonia Roma, 1949-1950 [A]**, today Calle José Alvarado 37; Kerouac and Cassady rented next door in 1950 **[A]**.

- **6 September 1951, Monterrey 122**: the party in **John Healy's third-floor apartment above the Bounty Bar**; Burroughs shot Vollmer in the forehead attempting a "William Tell" trick. Thirteen days in custody, bail through a bribed lawyer, later convicted in absentia of *homicidio culposo*, two-year suspended sentence. **[A: RealityStudio, *Documents on the Death of Joan Vollmer Burroughs*; García-Robles, *The Stray Bullet*, 2013]**
  - *Two factual corrections from the verify pass.* "Killed her instantly, dead at the scene" is **wrong**: she was carried out and died at the nearby **Red Cross post** while Burroughs waited outside. "He is twenty-eight" is **wrong**: Burroughs was **37**, Joan was 28.
  - *Unresolved.* The afterlife lens says he "received a suspended sentence and returned to St. Louis". The verify pass contradicts both halves: he **jumped bail** for **Palm Beach, Florida**, and the sentence was passed after he had gone. The file follows the verify pass; the St. Louis claim is rejected here, not adjudicated.
  - *Left standing.* The "corner of Chihuahua" descriptor on Monterrey 122 is **unconfirmed**; other sources place the address near Álvaro Obregón. Not used.
- **Queer** written in Mexico City, 1952, unpublished until 1985 **[A]**. **Palm Beach, late 1952 [A]**; the month orders the segment, no source dates the crossing.

## 8. Nova, and the obscenity trials (1961-1966)

- **The Soft Machine (1961), The Ticket That Exploded (1962), Nova Express (1964) [A]**. *Correction to the afterlife lens:* it gave the trilogy as "The Soft Machine, The Nova Express, The Ticket That Exploded". The order is Soft Machine, Ticket, Nova Express, and **Nova Express takes no definite article**.
- **Grove Press US edition, 1962**, and the prosecutions **[A]**. **Massachusetts Supreme Judicial Court, 7 July 1966**: not legally obscene, four of seven justices calling it grossly offensive; Mailer, Ginsberg and Ciardi testified **[A: Harvard Crimson; casemine.com]**.
- *Quotation corrections, all carried into the file.* "Cut word lines / Smash the control images" was placed in *The Soft Machine*, "Chapter One: 'Dead on Arrival'": book right, but it closes **"The Mayan Caper"**, and a full *Nova Express* text fetched and grepped does **not** contain the sequence, excluding that common misattribution too. The "word is a virus" text spliced two parallel sentences, making the **flu virus** damage the central nervous system; the file carries the correct passage (*Ticket*, Grove 1962, pp. 49-50), including "may **once have** been". "Junk is the ideal product" is **not** the 1959 introduction but **"Deposition: Testimony Concerning a Sickness" (1960) [A: full text fetched]**; the pool's indices 9 and 12 are the **same paragraph, seventeen words apart**, and the file uses one. "You can cut the mutter line of the mass media" is **[A]** verbatim in **The Electronic Revolution, p. 14**; the attribution to **"The Invisible Generation" (1966)** is **not verified** and has been dropped. His orthography is **"cut/up"** with a slash. The **Queer** introduction was off in three places; the file carries the fetched text, with the restored "threat of possession" sentence without which the Ugly Spirit clause is illegible.

## 10. The Bunker, Lawrence, death (1974-1997)

- **Return to New York; City College post arranged by Ginsberg; supervised withdrawal; Grauerholz arrives, 1974 [A]**.
