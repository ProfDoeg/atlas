# Griselda Blanco Restrepo (1943-2012): research report
*Compiled 2026-08-12. Slug `griselda_blanco`. Companion file: `griselda_blanco.journey.json`, 8 segments, 44 stops.*

Legend: **[A]** = attested, source named · **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition, single-source claim, or press repetition with no primary document · *gap* = the record does not contain it.

A warning governing the whole file. Blanco's biography is carried almost entirely by two encyclopedia articles (English and Spanish Wikipedia), one commercial profile (Biography.com), and journalism written around the 2024 Netflix series. No monograph, no court record read directly, no birth or death certificate, no DEA file. The tagging below is heavy on [R] by necessity, not by caution.

---

## 1. The coast and the comuna (1943 - c. 1963)

**Birth, 15 February 1943 [A date / R place].** The date is consistent across en.wikipedia and Biography.com. The place is not: en.wikipedia gives **Cartagena de Indias**, es.wikipedia carries her son **Michael Corleone Blanco's** claim of **Santa Marta**, 200 km east. **Contradiction flagged, not resolved.** No civil register entry is produced by any source. Mother: **Ana Restrepo [A]**. Father unnamed (*gap*).

*Coordinate note.* The pool's 10.4236, -75.5518 for Cartagena sits roughly 2 km west of the historic centre, on or past the bay edge. Corrected to Wikipedia's 10.4167, -75.5333.

**Relocation to Medellín at about age three, c. 1946 [A].** en.wikipedia. No barrio (*gap*).

**Pickpocketing in central Medellín, from c. 1953 [A].** The pool framed this as teenage work; en.wikipedia puts it **before her teens**, so the window opens around age ten.

**The kidnapped boy, c. 1954 [R, single source, flagged by its own carrier].** That at about eleven she helped abduct a boy from a wealthy family and shot him when the ransom went unpaid reaches the record through **Charles Cosby**, a partner of her later prison years; en.wikipedia carries it as alleged. One man's account, decades after, about a woman he met in custody. It opens nearly every popular treatment.

**Sex work in her teens [R].** Biography.com and en.wikipedia; one account calls it speculative.

**Marriage to Carlos Trujillo, document forger, at about thirteen, c. 1956 [A].** en.wikipedia states she *married* him at 13. The pool had her merely *meeting* him at 13 and marrying in the 1960s; **both halves corrected**.

**Three sons: Dixon, Uber, Osvaldo [A names / gap dates].** en.wikipedia, es.wikipedia, Biography.com. **No birth date for any of them anywhere consulted.** The pool's "all born by the time she is 26" is unsourced in every page retrieved and is dropped.

**Flight from the family home at about nineteen, c. 1962, reportedly to escape sexual abuse [A as reported].** en.wikipedia.

**Sequence contradiction flagged.** The pool's geography lens had her running away at nineteen and *then* marrying Trujillo. With the marriage at 13, the flight at 19 comes **after** it; the journey file reorders and drops the causal "then marries".

**The Bogotá false-papers operation, early to mid 1960s [R].** A recurring detail in journalistic profiles: Blanco and Trujillo forging passports out of Bogotá, the trade that becomes her route into smuggling people and then cocaine. **No primary document located.** Retained as [R] because it is the only bridge the record offers between a pickpocket and an importer.

## 2. Queens and the first indictment (1964 - 1975)

**Illegal entry on fraudulent documentation; settlement in Queens with three sons and second husband Alberto Bravo, 1964 [A].** en.wikipedia. No address (*gap*).

**Jackson Heights as the operational centre, late 1960s to 1974 [R].** Journalistic accounts only, cocaine carried in clothing and furniture; no address documented.

**Building the cocaine business with Alberto Bravo, 1970s [A in outline].** Biography.com, en.wikipedia. The specifics popularly attached to this period (the underwear with sewn compartments, the invention of courier methods) are **not in the verified pool** and are excluded, despite being the most repeated anecdote about her.

**Suspicion that she ordered the killing of Carlos Trujillo, early 1970s [R].** Biography.com, which states it was never proven. Place given only as Colombia (*gap*); Medellín pins as a stand-in.

**Federal indictment of Blanco and roughly thirty subordinates, April 1975, "Operation Banshee" [A].** en.wikipedia, Biography.com. She does not answer it; the family leaves for Colombia.

**Jurisdiction flagged.** The pool assigned this to the **Southern District of New York**. No source names a district, and her operation was based in **Queens**, in the **Eastern District**. The journey file pins lower Manhattan (40.7143, -74.0021) as a generic marker and calls the attribution unsourced. It should not harden into fact in a later file.

**Killing of Alberto Bravo outside Bogotá, 1975 [A].** Biography.com: a falling-out over money taken from the organisation; several associates die with him per some accounts. No exact site (*gap*), no prosecution recorded. Year only, and the event follows the April indictment, so the 1975-01-01 default is hedged.

## 3. Miami (c. 1978 - 1985)

**Third marriage, to Darío Sepúlveda; birth of Michael Corleone Blanco, 5 August 1978, in Medellín [A].** The pool placed the marriage in the early 1980s and the birth in Miami. **Both wrong.** The birth is Colombian and of 1978, which pushes the marriage into the middle-to-late 1970s.

**Return to the United States and the Miami operation, latter half of the 1970s [A].** en.wikipedia, Biography.com. No year fixed by either.

**The guarded mansion and the hundreds-of-millions fortune [R].** General press characterisation. **No address anywhere consulted** (*gap*). The journey file inherits 25.7907, -80.13 from `meyer_lansky.journey.json` byte-identically and says in the campa that it is Lansky's pin, not a located house.

**Responsibility for a large share of South Florida drug killings, 1979-1981 [R].** Biography.com. The pool compressed this to 1979 alone; **widened per the source**. The motorcycle method is attributed to her by en.wikipedia.

**Approximately 3,400 pounds of cocaine per month at the ring's height [R].** Biography.com, **unattributed estimate**, repeated everywhere since without a second source.

**The killing of Johnny Castro, February 1982, Miami [A/R, upgraded].** The pool held this as an anonymous [R] anecdote about "a two-year-old". It is better documented and is named: **Johnny Castro, aged two**, killed when a crew under **Jorge "Rivi" Ayala** shot up the car of the target, Blanco's former enforcer **Jesús "Chucho" Castro**, who survived. **Ayala's confession is the source**, which is why this death alone among the dozens carries a name and an age. No day, no street (*gap*).

**Total murders attributable to her organisation: roughly 40 to 250 [R].** Biography.com, unattributed range. No figure is authoritative; all are in print.

**Medellín Cartel membership [R].** en.wikipedia treats formal affiliation as claimed but not established. The journey file asserts it nowhere.

**Killing of Darío Sepúlveda in Medellín, 1983, by men posing as police, order attributed to Blanco [A as reported].** en.wikipedia, Biography.com. Michael is four. No charge recorded.

## 4. Arrest, trial, custody (1985 - 2004)

**Arrest by DEA agents, 17 February 1985 [A].** The pool flagged a "direct conflict" between en.wikipedia ("in her home") and Biography.com ("Irvine, California"). **There is no conflict**: the home was in Irvine and Wikipedia omits the city. The disputed framing is deleted; place given as Irvine.

**Pretrial detention, 1985 [gap].** Neither encyclopedia nor Biography names the facility or the transfer date east. Recorded as a gap in the campa and pinned to the court district.

**Trial in New York; conviction on conspiracy to manufacture, import and distribute cocaine; fifteen years, 1985 [A].** en.wikipedia, Biography.com. No month or day (*gap*). Nothing from Miami is in the charges.

**Federal incarceration, 1985-2004 [R, partly closable].** The pool recorded the span as blank. Accounts of the **Cosby correspondence** place her in the early 1990s in a federal facility in **northern California near Oakland**, commonly identified as **FCI Dublin**, with a later transfer to Florida custody. **No Bureau of Prisons record reached**, so the institution stays [R].

**Contemplated kidnapping of John F. Kennedy Jr. during incarceration [R, single source, undated].** Biography.com, no confirmation. Framed as a claim about her legend, not her conduct.

**Florida charges: three counts of first-degree murder, 1990s [A]; the 1994 filing date [R].** en.wikipedia dates them loosely to the 1990s; the pool's 1994 is unconfirmed and kept as an approximation.

**Collapse of the Florida case: the phone-sex scandal, 1998 [A].** en.wikipedia. The case rested on **Jorge "Rivi" Ayala**, confessed hitman turned witness, found to have conducted phone sex with secretaries at the **Miami-Dade State Attorney's Office**, some of whom took money. The pool dated it to "the 1990s"; **narrowed to 1998**, the year of the plea.

**Guilty plea to three counts of second-degree murder; twenty years concurrent, 1998 [A].** en.wikipedia, Biography.com. No day (*gap*).

**Release and deportation, June 2004 [A]; "after serving her full sentence" [rejected].** es.wikipedia gives release on **6 June 2004**, then deportation. The 1998 Florida term ran **concurrently** and credited time from 1985, so she left **short of both nominal terms**.

**Arrival in Colombia [R, inferred].** Deportation flights in this period land at **El Dorado**, Bogotá, but **no record of her arrival** was found. Marked as inference in campa and `date_confidence`.

## 5. The Medellín years and the killing (2004 - 2012)

**Quiet life in Medellín, 2004-2012 [R].** es.wikipedia and family accounts in press profiles. No address, no detail.

**Contact with an Ecuadorian associate called "Yedai", c. 2008 [R].** es.wikipedia, one line, **no city given**. Quito is a placeholder and the campa says so.

**Deaths of the three Trujillo sons [R, corrected].** The pool carried "one of her sons, Osvaldo or Uber, shot in Colombia c. 2009". The dominant account differs: **all three were killed while their mother was in US prison**, before the 2004 release, with **Uber reported shot during a drug deal in Miami**, documented in a **Miami New Times** report of **July 2008**. Places and years for the other two conflict and are **not resolved here**.

**Assassination, 3 September 2012, Belén, Medellín [A].** A gunman riding pillion on a motorcycle shoots her twice in the head as she leaves a butcher shop. Date, method, two head shots and age 69 confirmed across en.wikipedia, es.wikipedia and Colombian press. **Legally unsolved**; outlets still run pieces headlined "¿Quién mató a Griselda Blanco?", used as the single quote in the journey file since the quote pool is empty.

**Two corrections to the death, both material:**

1. **She did not die at the scene.** es.wikipedia records that she was taken to the **Unidad Intermedia de Belén** and pronounced dead there. The Spanish account is followed, the English one flagged.
2. **Coordinate conflict inside the pool.** The geography lens placed the shooting at **6.2270, -75.5930**, the afterlife lens placed the same event at **6.2308, -75.6047**, **1.3 km apart**. The file keeps 6.2270, -75.5930, beside the clinic pin (6.2268, -75.5951); the western coordinate is dropped.

**The street, unresolved.** en.wikipedia says 29th Street, es.wikipedia says 30th. Spanish press gives the **Carnicería Cardiso** at **carrera 29 with calle 30**, reconciling the two as *carrera* against *calle*. **But** a carrera-29 address sits oddly with Belén's carrera-70s/80s grid, so the numbering stays soft. **Flagged, not resolved.**

**Burial at Jardines Montesacro [A], in Itagüí and not Medellín [corrected].** Gold-coloured coffin with metal plaques; the grave lies **just over a hundred paces** from **Pablo Escobar's** tomb, per Semana and other outlets. Montesacro is in **Itagüí**, a separate municipality; several sources say "Medellín" loosely. The pool's geography lens recorded the cemetery as an unnamed gap while its afterlife lens named it: **contradiction resolved in favour of the named cemetery**, 6.1719, -75.5991.

## 6. Afterlife (2012 - 2026)

- **Griselda Records**, Buffalo, founded **2012** by **Westside Gunn, Conway the Machine and Mach-Hommy**, named after her [A]. The pool omitted **Conway**. Named in lyrics by Rick Ross, Nicki Minaj, Drake, Pusha T [A].
- **Cocaine Cowboys** (2006) and its 2008 sequel fix the "Godmother" framing in nonfiction film [A].
- **La viuda negra** (2014, Ana Serradilla) [A]; **Cocaine Godmother** (2018, Catherine Zeta-Jones) [A].
- **Netflix, "Griselda"**, January 2024, Sofía Vergara [A]. Drives the subsequent wave.
- **José Guarnizo, "Griselda"** (Planeta, 2024) [A, Infobae]: twelve years of investigation, built around the victims. The only account in the pool that centres the dead.
- **Cemetery tourism**: a February 2024 TikTok tour of Montesacro by **Antonio Arbesu** goes viral [R, press aggregation; primary video not reached].
- **The Miami Beach "phantom mansion"** in short-form video [R, online lore; no museum, no tour, no confirmed address].
- **"La Viuda Negra"** as a posthumous epithet [R, popular tradition].
- **The corrido [R, re-dated].** "El Corrido de Griselda Blanco", also circulated as "La Madrina", by **Perla Quintanilla**, was composed in **2022, before the Netflix series**, and cannot evidence a post-series surge. The pool used it as exactly that; the argument is dropped.
- **State seizure [A, re-dated by two years].** Colombia's **SAE** recovered a Medellín house in the **El Nogal** area operating as an elder-care home, removing residents on **14 May 2026**, executing an **extinción de dominio** ruling standing since **2017**; a mansion of over **2,253 m²** in southern Medellín was recovered in the same action, one property later assigned to reincorporation and peace-building programmes. The pool dated this to **2024**.

## 8. Gaps stated as gaps

1. **No birth record.** Two candidate cities, no document, no father named.
2. **No birth dates for the three Trujillo sons**, and conflicting places and years for two of their deaths.
3. **No address anywhere**: not the childhood barrio, not the Queens premises, not the Miami mansion, not the El Nogal house seized in 2026.
4. **No jail named** for the 1985 pretrial detention; **no BOP institution confirmed** for 1985-2004, only the Cosby-derived FCI Dublin.
5. **No court record read**: not the 1975 indictment, not the 1985 conviction, not the 1998 plea. Sections 2 and 4 come through encyclopedia summaries.
6. **No day** for the 1985 trial, the 1998 plea, the 1982 Castro shooting, or the 2012 burial.
7. **No verified quotations at all.** The pool's quote lens returned empty; the single quote in the journey file is a press headline, not words of hers. For a woman who spent nineteen years corresponding from prison, the absence of one sentence in her own voice is itself the finding.
8. **Nothing on the victims.** The state's case named three murders and pleaded them down; those names appear in no source consulted. Only Guarnizo's 2024 book is reported to address them, and it was not read.
