# Nikolai Aleksandrovich Bernstein (1896-1966): research report

*Compiled 2026-08-31 for `nikolai_bernstein.journey.json`. Moscow physiologist, founder of Soviet biomechanics, author of the degrees-of-freedom problem; expelled from every laboratory he held in 1949-50, rehabilitated only in print and only after his death.*

**Legend.** **[A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction or tradition, not independently confirmed here. Contradictions are flagged and **not adjudicated** unless a fetched source settles them outright.

Two warnings. **The two Wikipedias disagree on institutional names far more than on dates**: the year is usually stable, the letterhead is not. And **the richest material is dossier tradition attributed to Sirotkina (2018) and Vera Talis's oral history, neither opened here**, so it stays [R]: the 1949-55 persecution, the morphine dependence, the 1929 itinerary, the Tashkent logarithms.

## 1. Family and birth, to 1913

- **Born 5 November 1896 NS / 24 October OS, Moscow. [A x4]** en.wikipedia, ru.wikipedia, Nauka i Zhizn and apni.ru all give the same day in the same OS/NS pairing. **The dossier's "disputed birth date" is spurious and dropped:** the supposed rival "5 October NS" is in none of them, and apni.ru, cited in the pool as evidence of the dispute, gives the two dates as one day.
- **Father Alexander Nikolaevich Bernstein, 1870-1922, psychiatrist [A ru.wikipedia];** his death on 26 May 1922, training under Korsakov, directorships, birth name Solomon Natanovich and conversion to Orthodoxy are all **[R]**, attested nowhere fetched. **Mother Aleksandra Karlovna, b. 1867 [R]**, died 1941 in the evacuation; **place of death not located [gap]**. **Paternal uncle Sergei Natanovich Bernstein, 1880-1968, the mathematician [A]**.
- **Grandfather Natan Osipovich Bernstein, 1836-1891. [A Russian Jewish Encyclopedia]** That source gives only "Odessa physician and public figure, co-editor of the journal Sion". **Correction: it does not call him a physiologist**, and the Novorossiysk chair and the ceding of physiology to Sechenov in 1871 are absent from it, so both drop to **[R]**, though ru.wikipedia confirms Sechenov held that chair 1871-1876. **The pool carried this at [A] on a source that does not support it.** The address, Bolshoy Levshinsky pereulok 10, apt. 4, in a 1901 building, is **[A Talis, verbatim]**; Nauka i Zhizn has him living very poorly there, in one room after densification.
- **The Shchukina Street "contradiction" is deleted, not flagged.** The pool carried a conflict between the Levshinsky flat and Korolyov's placing his 1960s life on ulitsa Shchukina. Talis states the lane was called ул. Щукина from 1939 to 1994: one street, two names, covering exactly the years at issue.
- **Medvednikov gymnasium; silver medal [R]. [A Nauka i Zhizn for the school]** **Contradiction:** completion 1913 (en.wikipedia) against 1914 (dossier). Unresolved; 1913 used. The corrected pin puts the school ~600 m from the family flat, in the same quarter.

## 2. University and war, 1914-1921

- **Entered Moscow University, historical-philological faculty, 1914. [A Nauka i Zhizn, verbatim]**
- **Graduated in medicine 1919. [A both Wikipedias on the year]** **Contradiction:** en.wikipedia says "graduated from medical college", ru.wikipedia dates a Moscow State University graduation to the same year. **Gap:** Nauka i Zhizn gives **no graduation year at all**. The hospital-orderly detail is **[R]**.
- **Explicitly not supported:** a second degree from a mathematical faculty; only private special-mathematics coursework in autumn-winter 1921 **[R]**.
- **Red Army physician 1919-1921 [A en.wikipedia]**; December 1919 to March 1921 **[R]**. **Correction: Kazan is the wrong anchor.** Nauka i Zhizn says he was sent to units fighting Kolchak, the Urals-Siberian axis; Kazan had been retaken by the Reds in September 1918, over a year before his mobilisation. The file anchors at Yekaterinburg and states that this is an anchor for a moving front, not a posting. **Gap: no regimental record, no memoir naming a unit.**
- **Explicit negative finding:** no source records combat, political policing, or violence by Bernstein in these fifteen months. Medicine only. A gap in the record, not proof of a negative.

## 4. Psychology, music, walking, 1925-1933

- **State Institute of Experimental Psychology (Kornilov), 1925-1927. [A ru.wikipedia]** The Anna Rudnik marriage and the Vygotsky-circle proximity are **[R]**; the file keeps the dossier's distinction that he worked near Vygotsky, Luria and Leontiev without being their disciple.
- **Walking experiments from 1926; "General Biomechanics" and "Biomechanics for Instructors", 1926. [A en.wikipedia]**
- **State Institute of Musical Science. [A Frontiers in Neuroscience 14:553 (2020), fetched]** Calls the institute short-lived, names the subjects as "the Russian, Konstantin Igumnov, and the Dutchman, Egon Petri", notes Bernstein was a fine pianist, dates the work only to "the late 1920s". **Correction: Heinrich Neuhaus is not in that source**, though the pool listed him as a third pianist on its authority; removed. The Popova collaboration on piano touch is **[R]**.
- **Contradiction, three-way, unresolved:** the GIMN tenure is April 1926 to January 1940 (ru.wikipedia), September 1927 to January 1941 (dossier), or "the late 1920s" (Frontiers). The file uses ru.wikipedia and states the rest.

## 5. The 1929 Western tour

The tour is well established; **not one stop on it was independently confirmed, and all are [R]**. The dossier reports 365 numbered letters sent home; **none was located, the largest documentary gap in the file.** The stops kept are the Institut Marey; Sainte-Anne, for Lahy's labour-physiology laboratory; the Institut Pasteur, where he lodged with his aunt Margarita Nikolaevna Bernstein (Aitova); the showing of his cycle records to Paul Langevin, with no transcript and no venue, the file using the general Paris pin already in use for Langevin and calling it a placeholder; a Dortmund lecture; and a visit to Albrecht Bethe, Frankfurt being an approximation. **The Conservatoire des Arts et Métiers is dropped from the journey file** for having no content beyond "he went there", and is recorded here so it is not silently lost.

**Lineage note.** Bernstein's acknowledged forerunners, per the dossier, are Marey, Demenÿ, Braune and Fischer. **Muybridge is not in that list;** the interlock lens added him by extension. The file names Marey and Demenÿ in prose and cites `muybridge.journey.json` only in `sources`.

## 8. The destruction, 1949-1955

All **[R]** except where a fetched source is named. **Neither Wikipedia mentions the persecution at all:** the standard reference account omits the years in which his life was taken apart.

- **Letter to Stalin, 7 March 1949.** An archival "Conclusion on Professor N. A. Bernstein's letter ... to Comrade I. V. Stalin" is reported to survive; **the letter itself was not located and its contents are unknown.**
- **The 1949 press attack. [A Korolyov]** An editorial on the patriotic education of physical-culture cadres in *Teoriya i praktika fizicheskoy kultury* 1949 No. 4, then in No. 5 **A. N. Krestovnikov's "Na porochnykh pozitsiyakh"** on *On the Construction of Movements*. Title and target attested, upgrading the pool.
- **The nameplates. [A Korolyov, verbatim]** The incoming director smashed the door plates "Laboratory of Biomechanics" and "Professor N. A. Bernstein" **with a hammer**, which the pool's paraphrase dropped; kept.
- **Quote attribution corrected.** The pool attached "Bernstein removed from all positions" to the March-April 1949 departures; in Korolyov it belongs to the **August 1950 Pravda episode**. Moved, and the 1949 stop re-sourced to Krestovnikov.
- **Pravda, 21 August 1950. [A Korolyov]** **Correction:** the article had a co-author, **A. Kozhin**, omitted by the dossier. Zhukov is the same P. I. Zhukov who denounced him at the Burdenko in May 1951.
- **Burdenko Institute, senior researcher. [A ru.wikipedia: Jun 1950-Jan 1952; dossier to Dec 1951, unresolved]**
- **Morphine dependence. [R]** On witness recollection including Vyacheslav Ivanov's, and a personal file said to survive at the Burdenko. **The file was not accessed; no clinical document was seen.** Stated flat in the journey file, onset and cause unknown. No source shows trafficking, prosecution, or intoxicated misconduct.
- **January 1952: transfer to the Institute of Neurology with a reduced post and one laboratory-assistant line. April 1955: retirement on a category-II disability pension. Never arrested, tried, or imprisoned.** All **[R]**.

## 10. Quotes: verified, and how far upstream

All three were confirmed **verbatim inside a citing article**; **none was read in the volume it comes from**, and the journey file says so at each stop.

1. **The degrees-of-freedom sentence**, from *The Co-ordination and Regulation of Movements* (Pergamon, 1967), verified in Morasso (2022), PMC8801616.
2. **"It is true that the human brain is a multistoried building..."** Verified in Petrynski et al. (2022), PMC9097918, which renders it lowercase mid-sentence; capitalising the first word is the only change. **Citation corrected:** *O lovkosti i yeyo **razvitii*** (the pool had "rozvitii"), English in the Latash/Turvey *Dexterity and Its Development* (1996), p. 99.
3. **"Tonus ... is not a condition of elasticity but a condition of readiness."** Verified in Cacciatore, Anderson and Cohen, *Frontiers in Neuroscience* (2024), PMC11665217. **Citation corrected:** the intermediary is **Gurfinkel et al. (1999)**, not 2006, and the journal, given in the pool only as "Front (2024)", is *Frontiers in Neuroscience*. **A two-remove quote-of-a-quote**, framed in the file as reported via Gurfinkel et al., never as a direct reading of Bernstein 1940.

## 11. Interlocks used, and leads with no anchor

**Named in campa, all rooted in Bernstein's own life:** **`etienne_jules_marey`** (his cyclography descends from Marey's chronophotography, and Marey and Demenÿ are forerunners he credits); **`muybridge`** (in `sources` only, inference flagged in §5); **`john_dewey`** (his lineage names Dewey directly for circular sensorimotor coordination against the linear reflex arc [R], at the 1935 VIEM stop and at the Wiener stop, where `norbert_wiener.journey.json` independently records Wiener studying under Dewey at Columbia in 1914-15); **`norbert_wiener`** (met, interpreted for, handed a 1935 paper).

**Leads with no anchor, kept so they are not lost:** **Ivan Pavlov**, a documented meeting and a suppressed book, with no file in existence (`anna_pavlova` is an unrelated ballerina) and **suggested here as a queue entry**; Luria, Vygotsky and Leontiev, real proximity from 1925 and no files, the "luria" matches in `moses_de_leon` and `sabbatai_zevi` being Isaac Luria the kabbalist; Gastev; Gelfand, Tsetlin, Lyapunov, Gurfinkel, Chkhaidze, Feigenberg. **Paul Langevin** has no file and appears only inside `henri_bergson`, `henri_poincare` and `satyendra_nath_bose`; he is named in Bernstein's campa because Bernstein showed him the plates, while those three travelers are **not**, shared geography being no reason to name-drop.

## Apparatus relations for the operator

**Bernstein is not in `EXCEPTIONS.md`, and nothing here requires him to be added.** No stop applies his framework to material he never treated, in either direction. Two items for visibility:

1. **Backward, mild, handled without the exception:** the Muybridge relation. 2. **Forward, caught and refused:** the pool's afterlife lens carries posthumous material (the 1991 and 2003 publications, the 1996 and 2006 translations, the 2021 commemorations, a 2018 podcast, Talis's 2022 oral history). **None is staged as a stop.** Publication of a manuscript he finished is recorded in the campa of the stop where he wrote it: the 1991 book is his 1949 manuscript, and the file says so at 1949. The rest are other people's engagements with him and belong on their own files. **Listed here rather than deleted.**

## Explicit gaps

The 365 letters of the 1929 tour, none located, so the whole European itinerary rests on secondary chronology; the letter to Stalin, of which only a bureaucratic "Conclusion" is reported; the Burdenko personal file, unaccessed, so the morphine account is second-hand; the Tashkent logarithm manuscript; Verkhoshansky's 1991 memoir; a death certificate; and necropol.ru's grave record, which failed on a certificate error and does not matter.

**No monument, plaque, street, or named institution anywhere. [A as a negative finding across four sources]** ru.wikipedia, apni.ru, Korolyov and internationalsportkinetics.org all discuss his legacy at length and none names a memorial. **A gap in the record, not proof that none exists.**
