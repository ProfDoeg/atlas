# Blavatsky journey — research report

**Dataset:** `blavatsky.journey.json` — 9 segments, 40 stops, 1831–1945 (the traveler's life 1831–1891; the last segment carries the descent to 1945), gregorian.
**Register:** national mythology — the canon is true. Blavatsky's own account of her wanderings and her encounters with the Masters Morya and Koot Hoomi is narrated as event, marked by confidence where the record is only her word (nearly the whole "veiled period"). Her books (*Isis Unveiled*, *The Secret Doctrine*, *The Voice of the Silence*, *The Key to Theosophy*), her *Letters to A. P. Sinnett*, Olcott's *Old Diary Leaves*, Sinnett's *Incidents in the Life of Madame Blavatsky* and *The Occult World*, and Wachtmeister's *Reminiscences* are treated as true canon. The Coulomb/Hodgson episode is narrated *from inside the register* — the Masters are real, and the SPR, deceived by the movement's enemies, condemns the truest witness of the age as its cleverest impostor (the register's own tragic fold, vindicated when the SPR retracted in 1986).

## Judgment calls
- **Calendar:** gregorian throughout. Her birth is 12 Aug 1831 N.S. (31 July O.S.); I used the gregorian date the tradition celebrates, and made the same date her 1851 birthday for the Hyde Park meeting.
- **Confidence convention:** `attested` (19) for day/event-exact anchors in the public record (founding, publications, arrivals, the Hodgson report, the death). `traditional` (17) for events resting on the canon's / her own testimony (the Master in Hyde Park, the Tibet years, Shigatse, the 1870 letter, Sikkim, the Mentana wounds, the descent). `inferred` (4) where I picked the month to sequence her own vague accounts (Saratov childhood, Constantinople, New Orleans, Peru).
- **The "veiled period" (1848–1858) and the Tibet years** are fog by the canon's own design — she "spoke vaguely of a seven years' sojourn." I chose representative datable stations (Darjeeling border 1854, Kashmir entry 1856) and placed the great Shigatse retreat at 1868–1870, the period she most consistently claimed, rather than multiplying uncertain earlier crossings.
- **Coordinates** are the actual/traditional sites: the Fadeyev house in Dnipro, Hyde Park, Tashilhunpo at Shigatse, the Lamasery at 302 W 47th, Adyar, Lansdowne Road and Avenue Road, Woking. Djelal-Oghlu (the Erevan-province posting she fled) stands for the marriage flight.
- **The last segment is legacy, not biography** — Müller (1893), Steiner (1902), the Adyar ashes/White Lotus Day (1925), and the esoteric descent to Savitri Devi and Serrano (1945). Per the prompt, the descent figures are rendered descriptively as the mythos they authored, explicitly "a darkness she never meant," without endorsing the ideology.

## The tradition's folds and gaps
The canon's central gap is deliberate: HPB gave almost no verifiable dates for the decades of wandering, and the Masters' existence rests on her testimony and the phenomena. The register's sharpest fold is the Coulomb/Hodgson trial — the movement itself holds the phenomena true and the verdict a miscarriage, and history's own reversal (SPR 1986; Vernon Harrison 1997) sits inside the story rather than against it. A second fold is the descent: the same *Secret Doctrine* cosmology of root-races feeds both the luminous heir (Besant, Krishnamurti, modern spirituality) and the shadow-heirs (esoteric Hitlerism) — the atlas records both from one seed.

## Five richest episodes
1. **Hyde Park, 1851** — the childhood Protector walks out of the crowd of the Great Exhibition in the flesh; the mission of a life sealed on a park bench, in her own words to Sinnett.
2. **Shigatse, 1868–1870** — the hidden heart: living at the feet of Morya and Koot Hoomi under the golden roofs of Tashilhunpo, studying the Book of Dzyan; the 1870 letter that materializes in Odessa to comfort her family.
3. **The Lamasery, 1877** — *Isis Unveiled* written under dictation amid Eastern silks and the stuffed baboon in academic collar mocking Darwin; a thousand copies gone in ten days.
4. **Simla and the Coulomb trap, 1880–1884** — the cup dug from the earth and the brooch in the cushion, and then the same Shrine torn open to reveal trapdoors; the arc from wonder to indictment in one Occult Room.
5. **Würzburg / Ostend, 1885–1887** — broken by the verdict, refusing death in a coma to finish *The Secret Doctrine*; the masterwork rising straight out of the ruin, and "Keep the link unbroken" four years later.

## Connection to the atlas
This journey is the keystone of the atlas's esoteric cluster. It feeds **Steiner** (who rose in her Society before breaking to Anthroposophy) and **Gurdjieff** and **Keyserling** and **Jung**'s milieu directly; her root-race cosmology descends darkly to **Savitri Devi** and **Serrano** (esoteric Hitlerism), and it is answered from the academy by **Max Müller**'s *Sacred Books of the East*. Structurally it rhymes with **Gurdjieff**'s "way of the seeker" — both are Caucasus-born wanderers who claim a hidden Eastern brotherhood, both leave a canon whose dates are fog by design, and both are placed here with the wonders narrated as fact. Geographically it stitches Yekaterinoslav/Tiflis (shared with the Caucasus travelers) to New York, to Adyar and the Himalaya (shared with the India-facing journeys), making Blavatsky the hinge between the Western occult revival and the modern Western turn to the East.
