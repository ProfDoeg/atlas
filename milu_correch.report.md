# Milu Correch, research report

*Slug `milu_correch`. Compiled 2026-08-14 from the verified research pool (chronology, geography, quotes, interlock and afterlife lenses, adversarially corrected). The subject is **living and currently working**; not a posthumous file.*

**Legend.** **[A]** = attested, source named inline · **[R]** = reconstruction, inference, or a claim resting on absence of evidence. Where sources disagree both are printed and neither is adjudicated.

**The structural fact about this subject.** Almost the whole itinerary rests on **one document, her own CV at milucorrech.com/cv**, which gives year, festival name and town and nothing else. Roughly forty of the fifty-odd stops in her working life are attested *only* there; independent press exists for about a dozen. Wherever this report or a campa says the image is a gap, the visit is attested and the picture is not.

## 1. Origins: Villa Urquiza, before 2013

**[A, Infobae, 13 March 2018, Alejo Santander]** Villa Urquiza, in the north of Buenos Aires, is *her* barrio. Verbatim: *"cuando mi barrio de Villa Urquiza se empezó a llenar de murales, me enamoré del formato y empecé a pintarlos luego de un taller de gran formato."*

Corrections carried forward from the verification pass:

- The pool asserted she was **born and raised** in Villa Urquiza. **No consulted source states this.** Infobae supports residence only. Downgraded to "lives in / is from". **[A residence; birth claim unsupported]**
- **No birth year exists in any accessible source**: not the CV, Infobae, La Nación, Instagram or Street Art Cities, and both ES and EN Wikipedia 404 on the name. The journey's `years` reads "active 2013-2026 (birth year unrecorded)" and **no age appears anywhere**; an earlier draft's "twenty-something" is cut as fabrication. **[R, gap]**
- **Causal order corrected.** The pool had the workshop turning her toward the street, after which the barrio filled. Infobae has it the other way: the barrio filled **first**, that made her fall for the format, the workshop came **after**. **[A]**

**[A, La Nación, nid1643210]** The workshop was **Leandro Frizzera's** and what it taught was scaling: *"Me ensañaron a pasar eso que tenés en una hoja A4 a un mural de varios metros."* Neither paper dates it or names a venue **[R]**; the journey places it 2013-01-01 with the inference in `date_confidence`.

## 2. First walls, 2013

**[A, buenosairesstreetart.com, 14 March 2013]** The earliest documented Correch wall anywhere: "New designs by El Marian, Milu Correch and ByGermy", Villa Urquiza. Her piece is titled **"Queen of the Jungle"**, part of **"Un pueblo, un mural. Tour de muralistas 2013"**. Corrections: the pool dropped a **fourth artist, María Ajras** (restored); "walls a few blocks apart" was **invented**, the post names no streets; "only the finished piece survives" is **contradicted by the source**, itself a photo set; "the figures are modest still" was unsourced editorialising. All cut.

**[R]** Infobae's "hace unos cinco años" framing reconstructs a start around **2013**, consistent with the blog post and the first CV entries.

**[A, CV]** 2013: Meeting of Styles (Perpignan, Wiesbaden), ibug (Zwickau), KAT (Paris), Galería Urbana (Salamanca), Murs Lliures (Barcelona). **[A, German Wikipedia, ibug edition list]** The 2013 ibug site is named: **Zwickau, Eisenwerk**, the derelict ironworks. The pool's "no wall address has surfaced" was too pessimistic; the venue is documented, the image is not.

## 3. 2014-2015: the European circuit and the Italian coast

**[A, CV, Projects and Festivals]** 2014: **Street Art Museum, Amsterdam** and **Mechelen Muurt**, both repeated 2015. **Correction:** the pool called Amsterdam a group exhibition. It is not; the CV lists it under Projects and Festivals for both years, i.e. mural work, and its Shows section carries **no** Amsterdam entry in any year. **[A]**

**[A, CV]** 2015: Arce, Torre Pellice, Sapri, San Potito, Girona, Rome, Louvain-la-Neuve, Fundão. Shows: Mechelen Muurt and Mosaico (Sapri), both collective.

### Contradictions in this phase, not resolved

1. **Wool: Fundão or Covilhã.** The CV reads "Wool (Fundao, Portugal)". **[A, woolfest.org]** WOOL is the **Covilhã** Urban Art festival, thirteen editions, all in Covilhã's historic centre, and its own 2015 page lists only **Pantónio** and **Samina**, both in Covilhã, with no mention of Fundão or of Correch. The towns lie about sixteen kilometres apart. The journey keeps **Fundão** (40.1382, -7.5077, the CV's own claim) and states the contradiction in the campa.
2. **"Incipit" as a 2015 solo show.** The pool asserted one. **False on the CV's own text:** Shows 2015 are Mechelen Muurt and Mosaico, both collective; "Incipit (Sapri, solo show)" appears under Shows **2016** and **2018**. **[A]**
3. **Sapri undercounted.** An earlier reading gave three entries; the CV carries **six** (Incipit as a project in 2015, 2016, 2017; as a solo show in 2016 and 2018; Mosaico collective 2015). A six-entry attachment **[A]** that *no source explains* **[R, gap]**.
4. **San Potito** is omitted from the journey: ambiguous between San Potito Ultra (Avellino) and San Potito Sannitico (Caserta), with nothing to disambiguate. Listed here rather than pinned to a guess.

**[A, milucorrech.com press index]** Girona did receive press: **El Punt Avui, "L'art embelleix Girona"**, so the pool's claim that these years survive "unphotographed in any archive currently reachable" is **false**; the page also indexes Nässjö, Aberdeen, Ostend, Fanzara and Quilmes items. **[A, mechelenmuurt.be, negative]** That festival's domain is squatted, so its founding year is **unverifiable** and is cut from the campa, which states the domain loss instead.

## 4. Buenos Aires walls, 2015-2016

The best-documented murals of her early career, and almost the only ones described rather than listed.
**[A, buenosairesstreetart.com, December 2015]** **Caldas Street, Villa Ortúzar**, November 2015: a man holding an **umbrella stripped of its fabric**, only the ribs left over his head. Same post: **Niceto Vega, Palermo Hollywood**, a woman **lying down** reading with a chain of **mice around her neck**, titled **"Time to Read"**. **Correction:** the pool had the mice "running from the pages" and treated "time to read" as the title of a separate second Palermo wall. It is the title of **this** one; the post mentions a second Palermo piece finished earlier and leaves it undescribed.

**[A, buenosairesstreetart.com, 13 April 2016]** **Coghlan**, on the front of a **parking garage**: two musicians drumming either side of the door, one with a **crow**, the other with a **monkey**. The pool's "no further description survives" was wrong; it is in the cited source, and is restored.

**[A, CV, Shows 2016]** Two solo shows: **"Incipit" (Sapri)** and **"Coche bomba" (Buenos Aires)**, both repeating under Shows 2018.

**Contradiction, not resolved: which show was "the first".** The CV shows two solos already in 2016 **[A]**; Infobae calls **"Entre Cenizas"** *"tu primera exposición"* **[A]**. The journey calls **no** show her first and states the conflict in that campa. No venue is named for Coche bomba **[R, gap]**, so the centroid stands in.

## 5. 2016-2017: Sweden, the scattered year, Belo Horizonte

**[A, CV]** 2016: Bromölla, Halmstad, Nässjö, Bonito, Lioni. 2017: Racale, Vienna, Airola, Fanzara, Ahtopol, Helsinki, Quilmes, Belo Horizonte.

**[R]** The Finnish entry is internally inconsistent: "Street Art Vantaa" is a Vantaa programme, the CV gives the city as **Helsinki**, and these are distinct municipalities. The CV does not clarify; the journey pins Helsinki and says so in `date_confidence`.

**Belo Horizonte, CURA 2017** is the best-corroborated foreign mural in the record; the pool's "the visit is attested, its content is a gap" was overstated. **[A, Infobae]** her own words: *"Mi último muro en Belo Horizonte en un edificio de 28 pisos de altura"*; also on the festival's walls, **Dic, Doxs, Pavor**. **[A, O Tempo, Falabella]** other artists collaborated on the building gables of Correch and the Belo Horizonte writer **DMS**. Scale, collaborator and format are documented; only the image is missing.

**[A, Infobae]** The celebrated Quilmes mural stands **in the barrio of Los Álamos**, and it is the wall **Street Art Today** selected in **November 2017** among the world's best of that month, correcting the pool's "no wall address survives".

## 6. 2018: recognition, and her objection to it

The only year with a substantial interview record, and the year she states her position. All **[A, Infobae, 13 March 2018, updated 15 March, fetched directly]**:

- *"si el dominio de esa página lo hubiese pagado un tucumano, nadie me estaría preguntando por la mención"*
- *"Esto pertenece a ese lado sensacionalista de esta disciplina que me incomoda."*
- *"su consumo por medio de las redes sociales ha devorado un poco la instantaneidad y la experiencia sin mediadores"*
- *"el capitalismo captura absolutamente todo y eso es inevitable. Creo que es necesario que los actores de esta actividad la repensemos y tomemos, al menos, mínimas posiciones ya que a veces somos el circo de procesos nefastos y arcaicos como el 'embellecimiento urbano'"*
- *"Yo por suerte cambio siempre."*
- Asked **"¿De qué hablan las obras de Milu Correch?"**: *"De la ambigüedad, de lo que quieras."* **Correction:** the pool attributed this to a question about "Entre Cenizas"; it answers the general question, and the journey's `quote_source` says so.

**[A, La Nación, nid1643210]** *"Tu mural es tu publicidad, estás vendiendo y exponiendo tu trabajo"*; *"una de las principales razones por las que pinto es porque la paso bien"*; on pricing: *"Cuanto menos libertad tengo, más caro lo cobro."*

**Dating contradiction on "Entre Cenizas", not resolved.** Infobae uses the past tense, *"En abril de este año presentaste Entre Cenizas, tu primera exposición"*, but is bylined **13 March 2018**, inconsistent with its own dateline; and the title appears **nowhere on her CV**. The journey dates it 2018-04-01 with the contradiction in `date_confidence`. Title attested, date and "first" status contested.

**[A, CV]** 2018 walls: Campobasso, Stavanger, Sagunto, Carballo, Valencia, Rabat, Aberdeen, Ostend, Berazategui, and a Milestone (Girona) return. Print: "Mural Masters" (Greece). Show: Scope Art Base (Miami, collective).

Corrections in this block:

- **Stavanger and Aberdeen are the same year**, both on the 2018 CV line: a double invitation from one programme in one season. An earlier draft had Aberdeen "the following year". **[A]**
- **[A, This Is Colossal, 14 May 2018]** confirms her in the Aberdeen lineup, confirms 2018 as the **second** edition, and records a **theme**: the artists addressed regional history and legends. So "no mural description survives" was wrong; the brief survives, the image does not.
- **[A, StreetArtNews, 18 May 2018]** confirms Ostend, 50+ interventions. **[A, De Morgen headline on her press index]** supports 2016 as the festival's first year and refers to her mooring at Ostend **for the third time**, a count her CV does not show. Both printed, neither adjudicated.
- **Coordinates corrected:** Ostend to 51.2247, 2.9153; SCOPE to 25.781, -80.13 (the pool gave downtown Miami, ~6 km off the fairground). **[R]** The Miami Beach siting is an inference; the CV says only "Miami".
- **Berazategui** is dropped for stop count; its pool description carried a **fabricated** characterisation ("known more for its street named for the Berazategui family"). Corrected coordinate: -34.7628, -58.2033.

**[A, La Voz de Galicia, 25 May 2018]** At Carballo the paper describes her process as **dressing** the wall, one of very few pieces of method reporting anywhere in the record.

## 7. 2019: Hypatia, the cemetery, Exilio

**[A, Valencia Plaza, May 2019, plus CV]** **Murales Interactivos Mujeres de Ciencia** is run jointly by the **Universitat Politècnica de València, Las Naves and FECYT**. Correch painted **Hipatia de Alejandría**, with an astrolabe and lettering referencing the correspondence about her, on the **Orriols health centre at Duque de Mandas**, approx. 39.487, -0.363. **Correction:** the pool had her painting "women scientists" on the **UPV campus**; UPV is a co-organiser, not the site, and she painted **one** scientist.

**[A, Página Siete, 6 November 2019, plus CV]** **Ñatinta, La Paz**, painting in the **Cementerio General**. **Correction:** the pool glossed "ñatinta" as Aymara or Quechua for ink or dye. **No consulted source supports this; it is probably invented.** The name more plausibly plays on the **ñatitas**, the skulls the city crowns with flowers each November in that same cemetery. The gloss is cut; the campa leaves the etymology unresolved **[R]**. Página Siete (403) and La Razón (402) both refused their articles; only the headline is confirmed.

**[A, buenosairesstreetart.com, 6 May 2019]** **Barracas**: mural **"Exilio"** on a workshop, finished "a couple of weeks ago" (mid-to-late April), a **doll's face with flames, smoke and a matchbox**, **with Pol Corona**, **supported by Sullair Argentina**, tying it to the CV's 2019 "Sullair cultura". Coordinate corrected to -34.6437, -58.3781.

**[A, CV]** Other 2019: Río Grande Pinta, North West Walls (Belgium), Civitacampomarano, Monte Grande, San Rafael. Shows: "Tapia" (Barcelona) and "Viral" (Buenos Aires), **both collective** per the CV's own labels, correcting the pool's "solo". Print: "Street Art Today II".

Two further corrections. The pool claimed **All City Canvas** named her among the year's best murals; that link on her own press page returns **404**, so the inclusion is **unverifiable from the live web** **[R]** and is unused. And its "map of her own country in a single set of municipal invitations" for 2019 is a construction: the CV's 2019 Argentine set is Río Grande, San Rafael and Monte Grande, Quilmes and Berazategui being earlier years. **San Rafael is in Mendoza.**

## 8. 2020-2026: the silence and the return

**[A, CV]** **2020 contains exactly one entry of any kind**: the duo show **"JacobaxMilu" (Copenhagen)**, plus the French print "Guide 2020, Street Art Magazine". No mural anywhere that year. The sharpest structural fact in the later record, and the journey treats it as one.

**[A, CV]** 2021: Ordes, Bueu. 2022: Stigliano, Bodø, Stornara, Dundalk; show "Anfibios" (Buenos Aires, collective). 2023: Montesilvano, Caá Catí, Tbilisi Fest, Peramo (Athens), Vigo, Cheste.

**[A, milucorrech.com Walls page]** **2026**: **"Contra la gentrificación del imaginario"**, OhMyGad festival, **Gallipoli, Italy**. Undated: "Huir de las estatuas y las mareas sin rabia", @gulia_urbana, Castagna, Italy.

Corrections, all **[A]**: the town is **Stornara** (province of Foggia, 41.2833, 15.7667), not the CV's "Stronara" or the pool's "Sronara"; Italian Wikipedia dates Stramurales there to 2018. Likewise **Dundalk** for "Dundlack", **Bodø** for "Bodo". **"Anfibios" is collective**, not solo. The pool called Montesilvano "a fourth Appartengo return"; the CV shows **two** entries ever. The Castagna title was misquoted as *"las manadas"* (herds); the Walls page reads **"las mareas"** (tides). **Gallipoli** corrected to approx. 40.0556, 17.9917.

**[A, Substack and Instagram, fetched Aug 2026]** An active bilingual Substack, **"Brocha y Muro"**, subtitled Politics, Philosophy and Art; Instagram at roughly **22,600 followers**.

**[A, streetartcities.com profile]** Documented mural titles draw on myth and literature: "Medea", "Hypàtia d'Alexandria", "Juno Sospita", a portrait referencing Carlos Ruiz Zafón, "Alicia Duerme", "The Lady (Dulcinea)": the clearest evidence of a consistent subject, large narrative figures out of classical, folkloric and literary sources.

## 9. The brief's framing does not fit the subject

The brief asked for **afterlife, tomb, and legends that grew later**. There is none. **[A, Walls page dated 2026; Instagram live; Substack © 2026]** She is alive, painting and posting; her most recent recorded wall is this year. **[R, argument from absence]** No obituary, memorial, tomb, gravesite or rediscovery narrative was found in any consulted source: a reconstruction from absence rather than a confirmed negative, stated as a gap rather than resolved silently, though the 2026 mural effectively settles it. The journey has **no death segment and no tomb stop**; its last stop leaves the artist at work.
