# Ștefan Odobleja (1902-1978) research report

*`stefan_odobleja.journey.json`: 8 segments, 43 stops, gregorian, register "national
mythology: the canon is true".*

Romanian military physician, garrison doctor of the Banat and the lower Danube, author of
*Psychologie consonantiste* (Maloine imprint, Paris; printed at Lugoj; vol. I 1938, vol. II
1939), which states a general law of closed-loop causality ten years before Wiener published
*Cybernetics*. His posthumous reputation rests entirely on that decade. His life is documented
overwhelmingly in Romanian, commemorative and family-held sources; the priority claim is
documented thinly and one-sidedly.

**Legend.** **[A]** = attested, source named inline. **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition or
single-source family testimony. Conflicts are printed and left standing. Corrections against
the research pool are marked *Correction* and repeated in `date_confidence`.

## 1. Origins: Mehedinți, 1902-1922

**Birth, 13 October 1902 [A: en.wikipedia, ro.wikipedia, ICR].** The date is stable in every
source; the place-name is not. en.wikipedia gives Valea Izvorului, the ICR Izvorul Aneștilor,
local tradition Valea Hoțului ("the thief's valley"), all three for one settlement in
Livezile commune, renamed Ștefan Odobleja by law in 2005 (§8). Parents Ștefan and Maria
Odobleja, peasants; about three hectares with vineyard, pasture and orchard **[R]**.
*Correction:* the village is at 44.57556, 22.83139; the pool's afterlife lens carried 44.73,
22.78 in two items, wrong by about 18 km.

**Primary school from 1909, first in his class five years running [A: ro.wikipedia].** The
teacher N. T. Ismană and the reading in the fields with the cattle are **[R]**.

**Liceul Traian, Turnu Severin [A as institution; entry year R]**, confirmed as his secondary
school by ro.wikipedia (founded 1883). **No source gives 1914 as his year of entry**; it is
inferred from his age and used only to order the segment. The French teacher Chiricu, the
*Wilhelm Tell* translations and the year lost in 1916 are **[R]**. His brother Dumitru's
death of typhus in 1917 is **[A]** through the dedication of *La Phonoscopie*.

**Substitute teacher at Malovăț, 1 Sept 1921 to 1 Sept 1922 [R]**, and teaching on the Danube
island of **Ada Kaleh** in the same stretch **[R; island itself A]**. *Correction:* Malovăț
is at 44.70556, 22.72944, not 44.7167, 22.6167, 9 km west of it.

**Brăila, pontoon regiment, from 1928 [A: ro.wikipedia, ICR]**; the evaluators' remarks and

*Metoda de transonanță toracică* [A: ICR]**, described there as the study announcing his law
of reversibility; *Correction:* the pool's title drops the word thoracic.

**Turnu Severin, 95th Infantry, from 10 October 1930 [R: High Decree no. 3304]**, with the
recurring evaluation language (orderly, cultivated, absent from officers' society).

## 3. Lugoj and the two books, 1933-1939

The decade everything else hangs on.

**Detachment to Lugoj, 15 April 1933 [Lugoj A: ro.wikipedia, ICR; date R]**. The 1937
evaluation praising a low sick rate across a regiment of some 1,900 men is **[R]**.

**\*La Phonoscopie\*, 1935 [A: BnF catalogue]**, which holds *La Phonoscopie, nouvelle
méthode d'exploration clinique, par le Dr St. Odobleja*, dated **1935**. Both en.wikipedia
and ro.wikipedia give **1932**, and the pool's two lenses disagreed with each other.
**Flagged, not argued away:** the file uses 1935 on a national-library catalogue record and
says so. Printed at Lugoj, distributed from Paris by Gaston Doin **[R]**. Prize "Medic
General dr. Papiu Alexandru", 1937 **[A: en.wikipedia, ICR]**.

**IX International Congress of Military Medicine and Pharmacy, Bucharest, 1937 [A:
en.wikipedia]**. The dates 8-12 June, the title "Démonstration de phonoscopie", the French
prospectus and the interest of **William Seaman Bainbridge** of the American naval medical
delegation are **[R]**. No correspondence, citation or library accession has ever been
produced linking any prospectus to any recipient. This is the most consequential gap here:
it is where a transmission chain to the American milieu would have to be.

**The Auspitz printing quarrel, 1937-1938 [R: via the 1983 *Revista Arhivelor* publication of
the personal archive].** Contract 21 September 1937 with Iosif Schlinger's Auspitz concern,
2,000 copies, 35,000 lei advance, roughly 65 lost working days for want of paper, lawsuit
withdrawn 24 October 1938.

**\*Psychologie consonantiste\*, vol. I 1938, vol. II 1939 [A: en.wikipedia]**, Maloine
imprint, printed at Lugoj, about 300 figures. Extent given as nearly 900 pages by
en.wikipedia and 884 by the ICR; both carried, neither adjudicated.

**Four verbatim quotations from the book are verified**, traced to Iancu's full text and
checked word for word, including the unclosed quotation mark in the long ethics enumeration,
which is the source's own typographic defect. *Correction affecting all of them:* the pool
cited the article as "Information & Security: An International Journal". **The journal is
misnamed:** the masthead reads *Annals of the Academy of Romanian Scientists, Series on
Science and Technology of Information*, ISSN 2066-8562, vol. 2, no. 1 (2009), pp. 41-58.

## 4. The frontier and the war, 1938-1944

Almost the whole of this phase is **[R]**, resting on the pool's reading of the military
personnel file, which was not fetched: detailed in the way a real file is, but unseen.

- **Lipcani, Hotin (Bessarabia), from 1 April 1938.** He said in a 1978 interview that the
  transfer was disciplinary, made while he was suspected of communist or materialist
  sympathies. **No Siguranța file confirming it has surfaced [gap].**
- **Turda, chief physician of a motorised rifle group, 1 May 1940; Cernavodă, 36th Infantry,
  25 May 1941.**
- **The Don bend, 1942:** a dugout aid station holding about twenty wounded, evacuation work
  praised in the file. Coordinate approximate to the sector (49.3, 41.9).
- **Operation Uranus, 19 November 1942 [date A; his route R]:** separated from his regiment,
  through Varlamov (Varlamar in one reading), Derșam and fighting around Vislogubova toward
  Kamenka. **None of these localities could be located [gap].**
- **Odessa, late 1942 to early 1943:** frostbite, field hospital, evacuation to the
  interior-zone hospital; his own unit had entered him as dead or missing. Later retellings
  compress this to "declared dead at Stalingrad", which the file does not say.
- **Bucharest equipment depot 1944, then Ambulanța 37 Munte westward after the coup of
  23 August 1944.** His role is medical throughout, and no source ties him to a combat
  command or to police work.

## 5. The silent decades, 1946-1971

- **Retirement from active service, 1946 [A: en.wikipedia]**; specifics **[R]**: Someș
  territorial circle around Dej and Cluj, lieutenant-colonel 8 May 1946, available cadre by
  High Decree no. 2269 of 22 July 1946, after about eighteen years.
- **Marriage, spring 1946 [R: family archive compilation]**, to a woman of the Cluj district
  **whose name the compilation does not record [gap]**. *Contradiction, unresolved:* the
  compilation gives three children; a 2018 newspaper account two, one of whom died.
- **Withdrawal of his medical stamp on returning to Mehedinți [R]**, by an authority the pool
  states it could not recover **[gap]**. More than **50,000 manuscript pages [A:
  en.wikipedia]** follow over nearly thirty years; 450 volumes of his library go to the
  commune **[R]**.
- **\*Pentru o logică vie\* declined by the Romanian Academy, c. 1959 [R: his correspondence
  archive]**, the year approximate. *Correction:* the Academy's seat is Calea Victoriei 125,
  geocoding to 44.44517, 26.08945; the pool used 44.4396, 26.0963, 800 m southeast. The
  corrected point serves all three Academy stops.
- **Corroborating first-person testimony [A: Ion Oancea-Stroe]**, verified verbatim: that
  Odobleja "never told me that he wrote such important works", that the volumes moved with
  him to Turnu Severin around 1964, and that he read from them at the Al. Vlahuță circle
  after 1968. *Correction:* not 2009; Iancu cites Oancea-Stroe and V. Pârvănescu,
  "Microenciclopedie severineană", *Viitorul* (Mehedinți), **12 May 1972**.
- **The house fire [R: son and foundation testimony only].** Dated 1973 in one account and
  just after the 1975 congress in another, with an implied political motive nowhere
  documented and nowhere disproved. Carried as legend.

## 6. The rediscovery, 1971-1975

**Literary circle at the Casa de Cultură, Drobeta-Turnu Severin, from 1971 [R]**, with
Ileana Roman among the first local intermediaries. Oancea-Stroe's 1972 recollection puts his
participation "after 1968", consistent but not identical.

**Reading Wiener, 1972 [R: family and foundation testimony].** The hinge of the later story,
and not independently attested. What *is* attested is the negative:
`norbert_wiener.journey.json` mentions Odobleja, *Psychologie consonantiste* and Lugoj
nowhere. **Discussion of his work at the Romanian Academy, April 1975 [R]**, with no minutes,
agenda or Academy publication confirming the session located **[gap]**.

**Third International Congress of Cybernetics and Systems, Bucharest, 25-29 August 1975
[congress A; his presentation R].** *Upgrade from the pool:* OpenLibrary holds *Modern Trends
in Cybernetics and Systems*, proceedings of the third congress, Springer 1977. **His own
presentation there is still dossier-sourced** and keeps its [R] qualifier. The removal of
pages from copies of volume II rests on an editor's recollection; **which copies were cut has
never been collated [gap]**.

## 7. The last year, 1978

- **Television interview, March 1978 [R]**, by Aristide Buhoiu, at the Hotel Triumf per the
  family; broadcast withheld until 1982. **Radio recording through professor Constantin
  Negreanu, 1978 [R]**, first broadcast in the Fonoteca de Aur series on 20 December 1980.
  *Correction:* Hotel Triumf is at 44.46257, 26.08196 (OSM), not 44.455, 26.0847.
- **Spitalul Militar Central, mid-1978 [R]**, pancreatic cancer given verbally to his son by
  the commander, General Marinescu. **No chart survives**; en.wikipedia gives only "cancer".
  *Correction:* Calea Plevnei 134, 44.4436, 26.0688, not 44.438, 26.068.
- **Fourth International Congress of Cybernetics and Systems, Amsterdam, August 1978
  [congress A: OpenLibrary, *Current topics in cybernetics and systems*, Springer 1978].**
  He was too ill to travel. **The reader's name is not supported by the source carrying the
  story:** Iancu says only "a Romanian representative", so the pool's "Stelian Bajureanu" is
  kept out of the prose. The chair, **B. H. Rudall**, is quoted verbatim. **The chant of
  "40 years of cybernetics" has no transcript behind it: legend.**
- **Memorandum of 2 September 1978 [R: family archive]**, inventorying surviving copies and
  four decades of his own mailing of the book; its arithmetic is not fully transparent.
- **Death: three dates, unresolved [A on all sides].** en.wikipedia and Radio România Oltenia
  give **4 September 1978**; the ICR profile **2 October**; press accounts **4 October**.
  4 September is used as best supported, the contradiction printed in the campa itself.
- **Burial, Orthodox cemetery, Drobeta-Turnu Severin [A: en.wikipedia; name "Sfântu
  Gheorghe" A: ro.wikipedia].** Military honours and five priests are **[R]**. *Coordinate
  honesty:* OSM lists **no** cemetery under that name; the two it holds lie at 44.63053,
  22.61457 and 44.63633, 22.59977, four to five km west of the point used. **The grave is
  not located.**
- **\*Psihologia consonantistă și cibernetica\*, Craiova, 1978 [A: OpenLibrary].** *Not
  confirmed:* the pool's Mihai Golu preface is in no catalogue record or on the ICR page.

## 8. After, 1982-2024

- **Lugoj symposium, 22-24 October 1982 [A: ro.wikipedia, "Iosif Constantin Drăgan"]:**
  "Ștefan Odobleja, 80 de ani de la nașterea întemeietorului ciberneticii generale", organised
  by Drăgan, proceedings through Editura Nagard. **This resolves a conflation:** en.wikipedia's
  "Cybernetics Academy established in Lugoj (1982), registered in Lugano" runs the symposium
  together with the **Academia Internațională de Cibernetică Generalizată Ștefan Odobleja**,
  registered at **Lugano** (46.0037, 8.9511).
- **First Romanian edition of *Psihologia consonantistă*, 1982 [A: en.wikipedia]**, with
  Mihai Drăgănescu's introductory study, which dates his verbatim assessment. **French
  reissue *Cybernétique générale*, Éditions Nagard, Montreal/Rome, 1983 [A: en.wikipedia].**
- **Noica's preface to *Introducere în logica rezonanței*, Craiova, 1984, ed. Alexandru Surdu
  [A via Iancu].** *Corrections:* the quotation is dated 1984, not undated, and Noica writes
  about Odobleja's works generally, not about "unpublished manuscripts".
- **Romanian Academy, posthumous election, 1990 [A].** *Category settled:* the membership list
  (ro.wikipedia, "Lista membrilor Academiei Române") carries "Ștefan Odobleja | 1902-1978 |
  medic | **membru post-mortem** | 1990", so en.wikipedia's "honorary member" is the outlier.
  **The day, 13 November 1990, could not be confirmed**; acad.ro paths 404 **[gap]**.
- **Ștefan Odobleja Foundation, Drobeta-Turnu Severin, 1999 [A: en.wikipedia]**, founded by
  his son, engineer Ștefan Odobleja Jr. **Reinterment 2010; stamp 2011 [A: ro.wikipedia].**
- **Village renamed, 2005 [A, contradiction resolved].** ro.wikipedia, "Ștefan Odobleja,
  Mehedinți" gives **Legea 197/2005**, enacted 23 June 2005; the *biographical* article says
  2004, so **the two pages contradict each other** and 2004 is treated as the error.
  Monitorul Oficial returned 403 **[gap]**.
- **CRIFST session "Ștefan Odobleja și Cibernetica", Aula of the Romanian Academy,
  24 October 2024 [A: crifst.ro]**, marking 85 years since volume II, with a government
  fellowship programme in his name announced alongside.

## Apparatus relations for the operator

**Ștefan Odobleja is NOT listed in `EXCEPTIONS.md`.** Nothing has been silently deleted. Two
relations in the pool are not rooted in his own life; both are recorded here for your
decision rather than written into the file:

1. **Stafford Beer, BACKWARD direction.** The pool proposes that Beer's adoption of Wiener's
   coinage at MIT in 1950, and *Cybernetics and Management* (1959), run parallel to
   Odobleja's reframing of his 1938-39 work from 1972: two non-Wiener figures fitting a
   borrowed word onto pre-existing work. But there is **no attested contact in either
   direction**, and Beer's stops predate the reframing, so writing it would be a backward
   reach from Odobleja's file onto Beer's material. **Not written.** Licensable only by adding
   Odobleja to `EXCEPTIONS.md`, which I do not think this parallel earns.
2. **Claude Shannon, BACKWARD direction.** The pool proposes a 1948 three-way cluster of
   Wiener, Shannon and Odobleja. Shannon has no attested relation to him in any direction.
   **Not written.**

**No forward reach was found or attempted.** Nothing in the file points at an artifact later
than Odobleja's own posthumous reception.

**One rule application worth your eye.** The Fourth Congress at Amsterdam (August 1978) is
staged **at Odobleja's own city pin, 44.6233, 22.6678**, not at the canonical Amsterdam pin
(52.3676, 4.9041) used by `norbert_wiener.journey.json`. He was dying in Mehedinți; the
traveller was the unnamed Romanian delegate who read his paper, and that delegate's journey
is not his. The congress appears as the event he sent a paper to, with Rudall's response
quoted on Odobleja's own stop. The same logic keeps him out of Paris: Maloine and Gaston Doin
were distributors, no source places him in France, so the canonical Paris pin is **not**
inherited and the imprints are named from Lugoj.

## Interlocks written into campa

- **`norbert_wiener`**, named at two stops. Direct and one-directional: Odobleja read Wiener

## Honest gaps

1. **No transmission chain to Wiener exists in any direction.** The 1937 prospectus at the
   Bucharest congress is the only route proposed, and no recipient, citation or accession
   has been produced.
2. **The military personnel file was not seen.** The war narrative, the garrison chronology
   and all the evaluation quotations reach this file through the pool.
3. **The wife's name is not recorded**; the number of children is contested.
4. **The grave is not located.** The cemetery has a name but no coordinate.
5. **Three death dates, three institutions.**
6. **The corpus of over 50,000 manuscript pages is unpublished** and uncatalogued.
7. **The April 1975 Academy discussion has no located institutional record**, and the removed
   pages of volume II have never been collated against surviving copies.
