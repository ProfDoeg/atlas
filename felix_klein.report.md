# Felix Klein (1849-1925) - research report

*2026-08-23. Christian Felix Klein, mathematician. Written against a verified research pool in which an adversarial pass had already corrected the gathering pass; those corrections are carried into the journey file and itemised here.*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named · **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or single-source archival biography, chiefly Renate Tobies, *Felix Klein* (Springer, 2021), reached only through the dossier's synthesis and never opened in this pass.

The controlling problem is not obscurity but **false precision**: the dossier carried exact days (12 December 1868, 7 December 1872, 1 April 1875, 17 August 1875, 28 February 1898, 15 March 1915) that no reachable source states, nearly all traceable to Tobies alone or to nothing. Where a day could not be confirmed the file keeps the year and says so in `date_confidence`. That field, not the prose, carries every hedge. Bracketed numbers point into the **Contradictions** list.

**Coordinates.** Ten pins were wrong and are corrected in the journey file, by Nominatim except Jackson Park and the Stadtfriedhof, which take Wikipedia's readings. Eight were out by 210 to 800 m; **Wilhelm-Weber-Straße 3** was out by 1.1 km, at a city-centre point on no part of that street, corrected to **51.5388, 9.9422**. Wrong pairs had been reused across two or three stops each, so the corrections propagate.

## 6. Göttingen rebuilt, 1886-1898

- **Foreign Member of the Royal Society 1885 [A: MacTutor; RS NA5396].** **Chicago, World's Columbian Exposition and the 1893 congress [A: en.wikipedia; the fair detail Tobies]**; the fair ran 1 May to 30 October, so August is consistent.

## 8. War and last years, 1914-1925

- **Signs "An die Kulturwelt!", the Manifesto of the Ninety-Three, October 1914 [A: the manifesto's date, content and signatory list are well documented; Klein's signature via Tobies and Tollmien].** **The claimed Académie des Sciences annulment of 15 March 1915 is [R] and probably wrong [6]; the file asserts it nowhere.** The exculpatory reconstruction (he did not grasp the text, regretted it, opposed reprisals) is **[R]**.
- **Wartime seminars at his own house, becoming the *Vorlesungen über die Entwicklung der Mathematik im 19. Jahrhundert* [R: Tobies].** **That the Vorlesungen open on Gauss is standard description, not verified here [R]**; it is the ground on which Gauss is named in that campa.
- **1918 papers on Hilbert's foundations of physics and on conservation of momentum and energy in Einstein's gravitation theory [R: Tobies, cross-referenced against Klein's published papers].** **The Klein-Einstein correspondence, well documented generally, was not fetched here [R].** **Klein publicising Noether's theorems [R: dossier only]**; `QUEUE.md`'s Noether entry does not name Klein.
- **Notgemeinschaft der Deutschen Wissenschaft founded 30 October 1920 [A: de.wikipedia, naming Haber and Schmidt-Ott]**, which does not mention Klein, so his committee chairmanship is **[R]**, Tobies only. ***Gesammelte mathematische Abhandlungen*, Springer, 1921-1923 [A; his participation and the arrangement by domain [R]].** **"Göttinger Professoren (Lebensbilder von eigener Hand)", 1923 [R: Tobies].**
- **Dies at Göttingen 22 June 1925, aged 76 [A: MacTutor; en/de.wikipedia; RS NA5396].** Long ill health is documented; **no terminal cause is established anywhere consulted**, and none is supplied. Whether he died in the Wilhelm-Weber-Straße house or a clinic is unconfirmed; it is used as his last documented residence.

## Contradictions flagged, not resolved

1. **Doctoral supervisor 1868**: Plücker (MacTutor, en.wikipedia) / both, promoted under Plücker (de.wikipedia) / Lipschitz formally, Plücker being dead (Tobies). MacTutor never mentions Lipschitz.
2. **Berlin 1869**: Stolz and Kiepert (dossier, falsely cited to MacTutor, which names neither) against Kronecker, Kummer, Weierstrass (de.wikipedia).
3. **Mathematische Annalen**: 1873 with Gordan (dossier) against 1876-1924 with no Gordan (de.wikipedia, MacTutor).
4. **The wedding city 1875**: Munich (encyclopedia.com, dead URL) against Erlangen (Karl Hegel's chair).
5. **Two rival "canonical" Göttingen pins in the corpus.** `heisenberg` and `gauss` both carry **51.534, 9.935** and call it canonical; `john_von_neumann` carries **51.5413, 9.9158** and *also* calls it "the corpus's canonical Gottingen pin, inherited byte-identical". **This file inherits 51.534, 9.935**, two files against one. **The contradiction is not mine to resolve**: one of those files will have to be amended by hand.
6. **The Académie annulment**: fr.wikipedia's *Manifeste des 93* records that "L'Académie des sciences fut la plus hésitante", hesitation rather than expulsion, and no consulted source records an annulment of Klein's membership or that date. Removed.
7. **The bottle's name**: Göttingen's model collection asserts the Fläche/Flasche mistranslation, Linda Hall Library calls it conjecture, no first mistranslator is named anywhere reachable.
8. **The grave's coordinates**: two different wrong pairs in the dossier for one plot, 51.5276/9.9508 and 51.526/9.917, both corrected to 51.5325, 9.9097.
9. **Grace Chisholm's priority**: turns on how Kovalevskaya's 1874 degree in absentia is counted. The careful formulation is "first woman awarded a German doctorate through the regular examination process".

## Corpus interlocks used

Three existing travellers are named in campas, each on a relation rooted in Klein's own life.
- **`hegel`**: Klein marries **Anna Hegel**, granddaughter of Georg Wilhelm Friedrich Hegel and daughter of the Erlangen historian Karl Hegel. `hegel.journey.json` ends with the philosopher's death in 1831 and never reaches his descendants, so **the atlas cannot corroborate the marriage from its own side**; the link rests on de.wikipedia and MacTutor.

- **`gauss`**: named once, in the wartime home-seminars stop, because Klein wrote a history of nineteenth-century mathematics that opens on Gauss. **That it opens on Gauss is [R] in this pass.** He is *not* named for shared Göttingen geography, and since Klein's 1886 chair was Stern's, no chair-succession claim is made.
