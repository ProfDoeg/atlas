# Stafford Beer: research report

*Compiled 2026-08-28 for `stafford_beer.journey.json`. Anthony Stafford Beer, 25 September 1926 (London) - 23 August 2002 (Toronto). Operational researcher, founder of management cybernetics, author of the Viable System Model, director of Project Cybersyn / Proyecto Synco under Salvador Allende.*

Legend: **[A]** = attested, source named · **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition, or dossier-only claim not independently confirmed. Contradictions are flagged and left open; inaccessible sources are named with the reason. Journey file: 8 segments, 44 stops, 8 quotes.

## 1. Birth and schooling, 1926-1944

**Birth date 25 September 1926 [A: en.wikipedia, 'Stafford Beer'].**

**Birthplace: contested, not resolved.** Wikipedia gives **Fulham**, where his parents were living [A]. Rosenhead's obituary profile, working from a late autobiographical fragment of Beer's own, gives a downstairs room in his **maternal grandparents' house in Putney** [R]. The ResearchGate copy of Rosenhead returned **HTTP 403**, so Putney reaches this file only through the dossier. No certificate consulted. The file pins **Fulham** and states the conflict in `date_confidence`.

**Whitgift School, Croydon [A].** Refused the forced choice between mathematics, literature and classics streams; left or was expelled at about seventeen, c. 1943. Expelled or self-expelled is told both ways, **not adjudicated**. Coordinate corrected from 51.3627, -0.1029 (≈300 m north of the grounds) to **51.3600, -0.1031** (Haling Park, South Croydon CR2 6YT). **Conversion to Roman Catholicism at seventeen [R]**: dossier tradition, unconfirmed.

**University College London, philosophy and psychology [A].** Corroborated by the Concordia citation: *"Left university after one year to join the British Army."* No degree by examination was ever conferred on him. **Date correction:** the pool dated UCL entry to **1944** while also dating enlistment to 1944 and allowing "about a year" there. Corrected to **autumn 1943**, consistent with leaving school at seventeen and Wikipedia's army span 1944-1949.

**Aberystwyth [R].** UCL's wartime evacuation there is well documented; **Beer's personal attendance is not**, resting on the autobiographical fragment via the dossier. Coordinate corrected from -4.0819 to **52.4147, -4.0885** (Old College, King Street SY23 2BH).

## 6. After the coup, 1973-1984

**Massey Lectures: year corrected.** The pool said **1974**. Wikipedia's Massey list reads *"1973 - Stafford Beer, Designing Freedom"*; the **book** is 1974 (Open Library). The file splits them: **delivered 1973, published 1974.**

**Quote correction, verified against the primary text.** The pool submitted *"What is to be done with cybernetics…"*. The book (ch. 4; full OCR at archive.org, item `beer-stafford-designing-freedom`) reads **"What WILL be done"**; a grep for "is to be done" returns nothing in the whole text, and the pool's version imports a Leninist echo Beer did not write. Corrected; the rest is exact.
