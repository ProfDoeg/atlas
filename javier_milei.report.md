# Javier Milei, research report
*Compiled 2026-08-11 for `javier_milei.journey.json`. Subject: Javier Gerardo Milei, b. 22 October 1970, president of Argentina since 10 December 2023. **Living subject, sitting head of state.** Everything below is dated to the compilation date and will go stale.*

**Legend.** **[A]** = attested, source named inline. **[R]** = reconstruction, inference, tradition, or a claim carried only by a secondary compilation. Contradictions are printed as contradictions and left standing. Unreachable sources are named with the reason. Gaps are stated as gaps.

**Method.** Desk report on a verified research pool of five lenses (chronology, geography, quotes, corpus interlock, afterlife) whose adversarial pass had already flagged errors inside the pool itself. Those flags are honoured, and every one is listed in section 7 with the wrong value beside the right one so a later compiler working from the same pool does not reintroduce it. Nothing was re-fetched from the open web during the writing pass, so the accessibility notes are the pool's, not fresh.

## 0. A refusal carried forward

The pool's **afterlife lens refused the brief** and produced no items, on the stated ground that fabricating tomb fate, burial and posthumous legend for a living sitting head of state, and tagging it as canon, would be misinformation about a real person. **[A: refusal recorded verbatim in the pool]**

The refusal is upheld. `javier_milei.journey.json` has **no death stop, no burial stop, no posthumous segment**, unlike most files in the corpus. The last stop is the October 2025 midterm and the file simply stops; `years` reads `1970-` for the same reason. A later compiler should extend the final segment, not close it.

## 1. Palermo, Devoto, Sáenz Peña: 1970-1989

- Born **22 October 1970, Palermo, Buenos Aires**, named Javier Gerardo; father **Norberto Horacio Milei**, bus driver who came to own a bus company, described by him as **authoritarian**; mother **Alicia Luján Lucich**; sister **Karina**. **[A: Wikipedia EN and ES, 'Javier Milei']**
- Family in **Villa Devoto**, later **Sáenz Peña, Tres de Febrero** **[A: en.wikipedia]**; the geography lens tagged the move [R] and the correction upgrades it to [A]. **Colegio Cardenal Copello**, Villa Devoto, where classmates nickname him **"El Loco"** for combative outbursts **[A: en.wikipedia]**.
- **Goalkeeper, Chacarita Juniors youth divisions, c. 1983-1989.** **[A: es./en.wikipedia]** Two pool claims rejected as **[R] editorial inference**: that "El Loco" came from the pitch (it is sourced to the school), and that he left football because of the 1989 hyperinflation (no source links them). The journey states the hyperinflation as an adjacent fact of the same year and makes no causal claim.
- Two postgraduate programmes begun, neither finished: **economic theory at IDES**, **master's at Universidad Torcuato Di Tella**. **[A: es./en.wikipedia]**

**Contradiction, not resolved.** es.wikipedia's biography has him **enrolling at the Universidad de Belgrano in 1988** (average **9.43**); the same article's geographic sourcing gives the study span as **1989-1993**. Both cannot be exactly right. The journey uses 1988-03-01 and prints the disagreement in `date_confidence`. **[R for the year, A for the 9.43]**

**Gap.** The birth hospital and street are named nowhere reachable. The pin is the Palermo centroid, not a building.

## 2. Inside the machinery: 1992-2008

- **BCRA internship, December 1992 to June 1993.** **[A: es.wikipedia]**
- **Economic adviser, Tucumán province, 1994-1995.** **[A for the post]** **Contradiction, flagged and not resolved:** the chronology lens and the es.wikipedia text name **Antonio Domingo Bussi**, the geography lens of the same pool names **Ramón "Palito" Ortega**, and the pool's own correction calls the Ortega reading wrong. The journey names Bussi as better attested and records the disagreement. A primary Tucumán source is wanted.
- **Senior economist, HSBC Argentina, from 1996**, through the 2001-2002 collapse; **chief economist, Máxima AFJP, from 2002**; **teaching macroeconomics at UBA and elsewhere from 2004**; **columns in La Nación, El Cronista and Infobae from 2010**. **[A: es.wikipedia]**
- **Corporación América** (Eduardo Eurnekian's airport group), chief economist and investment-risk adviser. **Contradiction:** es.wikipedia dates the start to **2008**, en.wikipedia describes a tenure of **about fifteen years** with no start year. The pool tagged the item **[R]**; the journey uses 2008-01-01 and prints the disagreement.

**[R] background used in campa, flagged here.** The HSBC and Máxima stops carry two pieces of general Argentine history absent from the pool: the December 2001 *corralito* with its rapid presidential succession, and the 2008 nationalisation of the AFJP system under Cristina Fernández de Kirchner. Standard and uncontroversial, but unsourced here. The pool supports the frame, calling Máxima the system "he will later campaign to defend against state absorption."

## 10. Honest gaps

1. **No primary Argentine source was reached at all.** Everything rests on two Wikipedia editions, one *Le Grand Continent* essay, one *Spectator Australia* column, Wikiquote and general wire coverage. No *Boletín Oficial*, no electoral-authority returns, no INDEC series, no Argentine newspaper archive.
2. **Four unresolved contradictions:** the Tucumán governor (Bussi vs Ortega), the Belgrano enrolment year (1988 vs 1989-1993), the Corporación América start (2008 vs a fifteen-year tenure), the Jerusalem visit year (2023 vs February 2024).
3. **The Montevideo venue is unknown**; the pin is deliberately imprecise.
4. **No day-precision** for Decree 70/2023, the January 2024 general strike, the UNGA address, the February 2025 CPAC appearance, or the October 2025 midterm. Each carries a placeholder day that says so in `date_confidence`.
5. **The birth hospital is unknown.**
6. **Karina Milei** appears twice in the sources, as the sister he grew up with and as secretary general of the presidency implicated in a 2025 corruption scandal, with no independent treatment here. She is a living person under investigation and nothing beyond the two sourced sentences was written.
7. **The file has no ending and cannot have one.**
