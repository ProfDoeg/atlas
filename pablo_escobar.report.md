# Pablo Emilio Escobar Gaviria (1949-1993): research report

*Compiled 2026-08-18. Slug `pablo_escobar`. Companion file: `pablo_escobar.journey.json`, 8 segments, 40 stops, 13 quotes.*

Legend: **[A]** = attested, source named · **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition, single-source claim, or press repetition with no primary document · *gap* = the record consulted does not contain it.

**Warning governing the whole file.** This life is carried here by encyclopedia articles (English and Spanish Wikipedia, some fifteen pages), one commercial profile (biography.com, A&E), and a Britannica biography that returned **HTTP 403 on refetch** and reaches this file only at second hand through the pool. No monograph was read; no Colombian court file, DEA file, Kerry Committee volume or HRW report was opened. Every impressive number here (30 billion dollars, 70-80 tons a month, 25,000 mourners) is an unattributed estimate with no primary behind it. Much of what is printed about Escobar as fact originates in listicles, television, and the memoirs of his employees: two of the five quotations most associated with him have **no traceable original utterance at all**.

## 1. Rionegro and the comunas (1949 - c. 1975)

**Birth, 1 December 1949, Rionegro, Antioquia [A].** en.wikipedia; the Rionegro article lists him among notable natives. Third of seven children of **Abel de Jesús Escobar**, small farmer, and **Hermilda Gaviria**, schoolteacher. Coordinates are the town centre. *Gap:* no hospital, no civil register entry.

**The poverty question: contradiction flagged, not resolved.** en.wikipedia says he grew up "in poverty"; the dossier described a comfortable rural family with land and cattle; family accounts dispute the romanticized destitution. Escobar's own recorded version sides with poverty, but that quotation is itself [R]. Both sit in the Envigado campa; neither is adjudicated.

**Leaves school shortly before turning 17, 1966 [A]** (month and day *gap*). **Forged diploma, Universidad Autónoma Latinoamericana, drops out for lack of funds, 1968 [A]**, en.wikipedia also carrying the ambition to be a lawyer and then president; no enrollment record located. **Petty crime, c. 1970 [R]**: contraband cigarettes, counterfeit lottery tickets, motor vehicle theft.

**The tombstones: the pool's sourcing note was wrong and is corrected.** The pool claimed en.wikipedia carries a denial by Escobar and his mother. It does not: **en.wikipedia states the theft as plain fact**, and the family denial comes from other reporting the pool did not identify. Tag stays **[R]**, but the "legend, denied" framing must not be attributed to Wikipedia.

**Diego Echavarría Misas: contradiction flagged, not resolved.** The pool tagged this [A], dated "summer 1971". Both wrong. en.wikipedia's *Diego Echavarría Misas* gives the death as **19 September 1971** and attributes it to the gang of **"El Mono" Trejos**, never mentioning Escobar; en.wikipedia's *Pablo Escobar* makes the Escobar attribution. **Retagged [R]**, date corrected, retained because it is load-bearing for his comuna reputation, with the contradiction in `date_confidence`.

## 2. The cartel founded (1976 - 1980)

**Medellín Cartel founded with Gustavo Gaviria and the Ochoa brothers, 1976 [A].** en.wikipedia; Britannica (via pool) gives "founded 1976". *Gap:* no month, no document, no membership list. **Marriage to María Victoria Henao, March 1976 [A]**: she is 15, he is 26, Church dispensation granted; **her own Wikipedia page returned 404**, so this rests on the Escobar article alone.

**Arrest with 39 kg in a spare tire returning from Ecuador, May 1976; judges bribed, case dropped [A].** *Gap: the place of arrest is not published anywhere consulted.* The file pins the canonical Medellín coordinate and says so; it must not harden into a located fact later. **Assassination of the arresting officer, 1977 [R]**: presented as established with no court citation, and there was no conviction.

**19,000 kg into the US by end of 1978; Norman's Cay through 1982 [A for traffic, R for his presence].** The island operation is documented as **Carlos Lehder's personally**; nothing places Escobar on it, and the campa says so.

**Hacienda Nápoles, 1979 [A].** 20 km², zoo, sculpture park, airstrip, Formula 1 track, Piper Super Cub over the gate. **Coordinate corrected** to en.wikipedia's **5.91444, -74.72167**; the pool's afterlife lens was **11 km off**.

**5860 North Bay Road, Miami Beach [A as address, R as detail].** **Coordinate corrected** to OSM's **25.8404, -80.1323**, 0.9 km from the pool's value. The "6,500 square feet" figure and the Britannica attribution could not be reverified (403).

**Isla Grande, Islas del Rosario [R, single source].** Britannica only, 403. No deed, no date; labelled undocumented in its own campa.

## 3. The Robin Hood years (1981 - 1983)

**Muerte a Secuestradores founded at Puerto Boyacá after the Martha Nieves Ochoa kidnapping [A].** en.wikipedia: organized with military officers, politicians and landowners, spreading across eight departments. The pool gave both 1981 and 1982; **not resolved**, and the file uses 1981 with the ambiguity stated.

**Sworn in as alternate representative for Medellín, 20 July 1982, resigning 26 October 1983 [A].** Britannica via pool for the dates; en.wikipedia for the immunity and diplomatic passport.

**Medellín sin Tugurios, 1984, and Barrio Pablo Escobar [A].** es.wikipedia: 250 initial houses for families from the **Moravia garbage dump**; official name **Loreto**; roughly 4,000 homes and 16,000 people by 2011; legalized **15 August 2012**.
- **Location corrected.** The barrio is in **Comuna 9 (Buenos Aires)**, not Comuna 8; the afterlife lens's coordinate falls near the city centre on the wrong side of the river, 3.6 km away. The file uses **6.2313, -75.5582** (OSM).
- **The "some sixty soccer fields" figure is dropped.** It is not in the cited Spanish article and could not be sourced.

**Wider infrastructure patronage [R]**, biography.com only, unattributed. **Atlético Nacional [A in outline]**: en.wikipedia, with Maturana's own statement, the 1989 Copa Libertadores, and the CONMEBOL ban over referee intimidation. That article declines to separate **Escobar's personal role from the cartel's**, and neither does the file. *No year is attested for the money entering the club.* The stop carries **1984-06-01 as a placeholder**, set after the Medellín sin Tugurios stop so that the segment reads in order; the earlier 1983 placeholder ran backwards against it and was corrected during the style pass. Nothing in the campa depends on the year.

## 4. Plata o plomo (1984 - 1989)

**Announced retirement from politics, January 1984 [A].** **Correction to the Galán relation:** the pool said Galán "expelled Escobar from the Liberal Party", but in 1982 Galán rejected him from **Nuevo Liberalismo**, his own faction. Corrected in both files.

**Rodrigo Lara Bonilla assassinated, 30 April 1984 [A].** en.wikipedia: Autopista Norte at Calle 127, Mac-10 from a motorcycle, order traced to Escobar. **No Colombian court ever tried him for it.**

**Clínica Shaio coordinate corrected.** The pool's 4.6684, -74.0578 lands in Chapinero, **3.8 km** from the Fundación Clínica Shaio (Carrera 70D, Suba). Corrected to **4.6980, -74.0732** per OSM.

**Panama and Noriega, c. 1984: contested, flagged not resolved.** The Kerry Committee has Escobar's associates meeting intermediaries close to **Manuel Noriega** at Coclesito over transshipment and laboratory protection; Noriega's memoir denies it flatly. **Escobar's own presence at any such meeting is nowhere established**, a dedicated source returned 404, and the Noriega article never names him. Retained because the institutional relation is real, sourced and contested; Noriega's 1988 Miami indictment is [A] and cited as corroboration.

**Los Extraditables, autumn 1985 [A]. Date corrected.** The pool gave "1984-1985". en.wikipedia is specific: the conditional-surrender offer came in the **fall of 1985**, was rejected, and the founding followed.

**Palace of Justice siege, 6-7 November 1985 [A for siege, R for financing]. Figures corrected.** **Two days, not one**; **101 dead, not 98**; **11 of 25 justices killed**. On financing, the allegation rests largely on **Virginia Vallejo's** testimony, while the same article records a **1986 Special Commission concluding that destroying files was not an M-19 goal**. The campa states both and asserts neither.

**Edificio Mónaco car bomb, 13 January 1988 [A, upgraded from R].** es.wikipedia: 80 kg of dynamite in a grey Toyota, ordered by **Pacho Herrera** of the Cali cartel, **Manuela Escobar's hearing permanently damaged**. The pool treated it as unconfirmable; the Spanish article confirms it. **Coordinate corrected** to **6.19131, -75.57808**, the pool's value falling **2.3 km** away. **Storey count: contradiction flagged**, es.wikipedia saying eight floors and en.wikipedia six; the campa reports the disagreement.

**Carlos Mauro Hoyos killed, 25 January 1988 [A for killing, R for attribution]. Two corrections.** His title was **Procurador General de la Nación**, not Attorney General; and the municipality was **Rionegro, not Guarne**, es.wikipedia having him kidnapped near the José María Córdova airport and the body found at the **finca San Gerardo beside the vía Las Palmas**. The pool's 6.2789, -75.4453 is an accurate coordinate for Guarne, the wrong place; corrected to **6.155, -75.389**. The attribution rests on the sicario **John Jairo Velásquez's** testimony.

**Luis Carlos Galán assassinated, 18 August 1989, Soacha [A].** Rally of roughly 10,000, 18 bodyguards, prior warnings. Plot attributed to Escobar with **Gonzalo Rodríguez Gacha** and **Alberto Santofimio**, the latter convicted in 2007. **Avianca Flight 203, 27 November 1989 [A]**: bomb five minutes after takeoff, 107 aboard and 3 on the ground dead, César Gaviria the intended target and not aboard, two American dead bringing the US into the hunt. **DAS headquarters bombing, 6 December 1989 [A]**: an estimated 9,000 kg of dynamite, 63 killed, over 2,200 injured, Miguel Maza Márquez the target and a survivor; the pool flagged the date as unreconfirmed and **the dedicated article confirms it**.

## 5. La Catedral (1991 - 1992)

**The 1991 constitution bans extradition of nationals [A]; the causal claim is [R].** en.wikipedia states the ban and that it is widely believed to reflect his influence. **No source establishes pressure on any named delegate.** The stop is dated to the promulgation purely for ordering.

**Surrender and confinement, June 1991 [A].** Negotiated shielding from extradition, prison built by him on his own land, football pitch, bar, jacuzzi, telescope on the family residence. *Gap:* exact day. **Galeano and Moncada tortured and murdered inside, July 1992 [A]** (*gap:* no day). **Escape through a pre-weakened wall, 22 July 1992, after one year and one month [A].** Benedictines have run the site since 2007.

## 6. The hunt and the roof (1992 - 1993)

**Los Pepes [A for composition and conduct; date corrected].** Castaño brothers leading, Cali Cartel financing, killings of guards, accountants and lawyers, two of Hermilda Gaviria's haciendas burned, intelligence traded with the Search Bloc. **The pool dated the group flat to 1992; the article gives 1992-1993 active and no founding date.** The stop is dated August 1992 as an ordering device and says so.

**Roberto Escobar's surrender: resolved against the dossier, residue flagged.** en.wikipedia has him escaping with Pablo in July 1992 and surrendering "a year later", c. 1993; the dossier's 1996 / US-authorities version has **no retrievable support**. The file follows 1993 and states the disagreement. *Gap:* no place. **Memoir corrected**: *The Accountant's Story*, **2009**, not "The Accountant" (2010).

**Death at Los Olivos, 2 December 1993 [A].** The day after his 44th birthday, bodyguard **Álvaro de Jesús Agudelo ("Limón")** dead with him, the fatal shot **disputed** between police fire and suicide. **Coordinate corrected** to **6.25378, -75.59789**; the pool's value sits 1.3 km east.

## 7. Afterlife (1993 - 2026)

**Burial at Jardines Montesacro, Itagüí, 3 December 1993, roughly 25,000 mourners [A].**

**Cemetery coordinate: four values, flagged not silently resolved.** `griselda_blanco.journey.json` (interlock lens, canonical) 6.1719, -75.5991; the pool's afterlife lens 6.1719, -75.6116; its geography lens 6.1728, -75.6142; OpenStreetMap 6.1604, -75.6205. The file inherits the **griselda_blanco value byte-identically**, per the standing instruction on canonical pins, and because the two graves lie a hundred paces apart on one lawn and must share a pin or the map lies about them. **The OSM discrepancy of roughly 1.5 km is real**: if the operator prefers geographic accuracy over map consistency, both files must move together.

**Exhumation, 28 October 2006 [A].** DNA sample for a paternity claim, RCN broadcast, Marroquín's objection; no longer dependent on the unfetchable Britannica citation. *Gap: the test result is reported nowhere.* **Barrio legalized, 15 August 2012 [A].**

**Demolition of the Edificio Mónaco: date conflict flagged, not resolved.** en.wikipedia gives **22 February 2019 at 11:53 AM** and both other pool lenses agree; **es.wikipedia gives 24 February**. The timestamped record is preferred and the conflict is stated inside the campa.

**The hippopotamuses [A].** Four imported 1981, 40 by 2014, around two hundred by 2026; GonaCon sterilization by October 2021; euthanasia of more than eighty approved **13 April 2026** at 7.2 billion pesos. Head-counts are moving estimates. Theme park since 2014; main house collapsed, reported February 2015.

**Folk-saint veneration [R].** en.wikipedia states it as fact, names **no ethnographic source**, quantifies nothing; the strongest evidence is the funeral quotation from an **unnamed** mourner via biography.com. A thin base for what is arguably the most important claim in the file, flagged rather than dressed up. The stop's date is **not an event**: the devotion is continuous from December 1993, and the stop carries a terminal ordering date at the end of the file's clock so that the standing condition closes the sequence. The earlier 1994 value placed it before the 2026 hippopotamus stop and broke the segment's order; corrected during the style pass. The **Museo Casa Pablo Escobar** article returned **404**, so the museum is uncorroborated. *Narcos* (2015) [A] has **no real-world site**, so no stop, only one clause in the closing campa.

## Quotations: an audit

**[A].** (1) *"Lo que más vale en la vida son los amigos"*: verbatim in es.wikiquote's wikitext, source upgraded to **Luis Cañón, *El patrón*, Planeta, 1994, p. 83**. *Correction:* biography.com does **not** corroborate the wording. (2) *"Yo soy un exportador de flores..."*: a **1984 interview by Elizabeth Mora of El Tiempo**, shelved when Lara Bonilla was killed days later, published February 2025 by **El Reverbero**. Infobae renders it in **reported speech**, so the sentence is Wikiquote's compression. (3-8) Four biography.com lines, the unnamed mourner, and Marroquín on his father: wording seen at a named outlet, but **no interview date or primary transcript for any**.

**[R], downgraded here from the pool's [A].** (9) *"Yo tengo 38 años de edad..."*: Wikiquote's whole citation is a **bare YouTube URL**, no broadcast or date, and the video ID is **malformed**, 12 characters where YouTube uses 11, so the link is dead as written. (10) *"Pues mi familia no tuvo unos recursos económicos..."*: es.wikipedia's sole citation is the **guioteca listicle**, which carries the line only **truncated**, so **the sentence Wikipedia prints exceeds its own cited source**; the "Colombian national television" framing is Wikipedia editorial voice.

**[R] from the outset.** (11) *"Plata o plomo"*, no primary transcript. (12) *"A veces yo soy Dios..."* and (13) *"Del hambre nació la picardía"*, both from the same unsourced listicle.
