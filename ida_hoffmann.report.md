# Ida Hofmann (1864-1926): research report

*Compiled 2026-08-02 for the Atlas of Journeys. Slug `ida_hoffmann`. The corpus census spells her with a double f; every source consulted spells her **Hofmann**, single f. The slug is kept for continuity with QUEUE.md and the census; the traveler field uses the correct spelling.*

**Legend.** **[A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or inference from a source that does not state the thing directly. Contradictions between sources are flagged and left open. Sources that could not be reached are named with the reason.

Her own memoir, *Monte Verità. Wahrheit ohne Dichtung* (1906), is the only primary text used. It was read in the Internet Archive OCR layer (identifier `MonteVeritaWahrheitOD`). Every German quotation below was traced to a line in that text layer and every page number checked against the running page headers in the same file. Several page citations in circulation proved wrong by one page; see the Sources section.

## 1. Freiberg and Vienna, 1864-c.1894

**[A, de.wikipedia "Ida Hofmann"]** Born 5 October 1864 in Freiberg, Kingdom of Saxony, daughter of Raphael Hofmann, mining engineer, and Luise Orges. The Bergakademie of Freiberg is the natural context for the father's profession; the maternal line is said to connect the family to Justus Möser and Bernhard von Cotta.

**Contradiction, resolved but recorded.** A WebFetch of it.wikipedia "Ida Hofmann" gave the birthplace as **Freiburg im Breisgau**. That is the outlier: de.wikipedia and Wikidata Q16744619 both give Freiberg, and the Bergakademie detail only works in Saxony. Recorded because the Italian article is one of the three encyclopedia entries a later reader meets first.

**[A, de.wikipedia]** The family moves to Vienna in 1879. She studies piano under Wilhelm Dörr and music pedagogy under Julius Epstein and takes diplomas in both. **[R]** No source names the institution; Epstein taught at the conservatory of the Gesellschaft der Musikfreunde, which is why the atlas pins the study years to the Musikverein. That is a landmark, not an attestation.

**[A, de.wikipedia]** She then works as a piano teacher in the households of the Viennese nobility.

**Gap.** Nothing reachable documents the years between the diplomas and the Montenegrin post: no address, no pupil, no date. Roughly a decade of her twenties is blank.

## 2. Cetinje, c.1895-1899

**[A, de.wikipedia; it.wikipedia]** She takes a music post in Cetinje, Montenegro, at an institution founded under the Russian Tsarina for daughters of high families.

**Contradiction, flagged.** One digest dates the post to **1890**. The German article does not support that: it says she was "Anfang dreissig," in her early thirties, when the position was offered. Born October 1864, she was 25 in 1890; "early thirties" puts the offer c.1894-1897. Both readings circulate. The atlas uses c.1895 and marks the date contested rather than choosing silently.

**[R]** The institution is identifiable, though no source consulted names it in connection with her: the **Institut carice Marije**, the Empress Maria Girls' Institute of Cetinje, founded 1869 under Tsarina Maria Alexandrovna (en.wikipedia, "Cetinje"). Inference from founder and town, not a documented link.

**Contradiction, unresolved: Transylvania.** English accounts of Monte Verità, following Landmann and Green, describe her at this period as a music teacher **from Transylvania**. This is not merely an English Wikipedia artifact. The it.wikipedia bibliography cites Julia Schiff, "Extremes Denken und Fanatismus. Ida Hofmann, eine Siebenbürger Pionierin für ein alternatives Lebensmodell," *Südostdeutsche Vierteljahresblätter* 47 (1998), Folge 4, pp. 339-343: a scholarly article whose subtitle calls her a Transylvanian pioneer outright. The claim therefore carries academic weight and should be tagged **[A]** as an attribution in the literature, not dismissed.

Counter-evidence in the same breath: the Gräser brothers were demonstrably Transylvanian. Gusto Gräser was born 16 February 1879 at Kronstadt (Brașov), and monteverita.org calls Karl Gräser "from Transylvania." Conflation with the Gräsers is the likeliest origin. The German biography, which is the fullest, runs Freiberg to Vienna to Cetinje with no Transylvanian stage at all. **Not adjudicated.** Schiff's article was not read; it is a print journal not digitized anywhere reachable.

## 3. Veldes, 1899

**[A, memoir p. 4]** In the summer of 1899, during a shared stay at Arnold Rikli's Naturheilanstalt at Veldes (Bled, then Austria-Hungary), Oedenkoven won her over to the project:

> Im Sommer 1899, gelegentlich eines gemeinsamen Aufenthaltes in der Naturheilanstalt Rikli in Veldes gewann mich Henri Oedenkoven für das in grossen Zügen geschilderte Zukunftsunternehmen.

**[A, de.wikipedia]** She had come to Bled to visit her ailing father. **[A, de.wikipedia; en.wikipedia "Monte Verità"]** At the institute she meets Henri Oedenkoven, son of a Belgian industrialist, and Karl Gräser, a former Habsburg officer.

**Error corrected.** One digest wrote "Karl (Gusto) Gräser" as though these were one man with a nickname. They are two brothers: **Karl Gräser** (1875-1916), the officer, whom she met at Bled, and **Gustav "Gusto" Gräser** (1879-1958), the artist-vagabond, who appears only from the Munich meeting and is expelled in 1901.

**[A, memoir p. 4]** On Oedenkoven's road to vegetarianism: "Krankheit bis zum Grabesrande führte ihn auf den Läuterungsweg des Vegetarismus."

**[A, de.wikipedia]** A year later, in 1900, the relationship becomes what they called a **Reformehe**, also a "vegetarian marriage."

**Flag.** Not a marriage in law. de.wikipedia on Oedenkoven says he "lebte danach mit ihr in freier Ehe," a free union, and that he legally married Isabelle Adderley later. Where the atlas says "marry," it means a self-declared union. Her own position, memoir p. 18:

> Liebe, als eine in der Natur »frei« sich vollziehende Vereinigung zweier Wesen betrachtend, verwerfen wir für uns sowohl den priesterlichen Segensspruch als den Staatsakt.

**Flag on place.** A geography digest pinned the Reformehe to Monte Verità. Impossible: the union dates to 1900, and the group only reached the Ticino that autumn, after the October meeting in Munich. The atlas attaches it to Munich and says so in `date_confidence`.

## 4. Munich to Ascona, autumn 1900

**[A, en.wikipedia]** A founding meeting at Munich in October 1900 gathers Hofmann, Oedenkoven, Karl and Gusto Gräser, her sister Jenny, Lotte Hattemer, and Ferdinand Brune, to plan a vegetable cooperative and pool personal property.

**[A, de.wikipedia; it.wikipedia]** The group travels south through Oberammergau and Cadenabbia toward the Ticino.

**[A, en.wikipedia]** They buy Monte Monescia, a hill of some 320 metres above Ascona, from **Alfredo Pioda**, and enlarge the holding to roughly four hectares across several purchases. The hill is renamed Monte Verità.

**Corpus interlock [A, census_real_persons_2026-08-02.md:286]** Pioda is already in the census, entered precisely as the seller of that ground. Oedenkoven sits on the row directly above hers (lines 156-157), both tagged "co-founder of the Monte Verità colony"; Arnold Rikli is at line 155, same batch.

## 5. The mountain, 1901-1906

**[A, de.wikipedia]** 1901: building begins on the **Licht-Luft-Hütten**, the light-air huts for the sun and air cure.

**Correction.** Casa Selma has been described as a permanent building standing "alongside" the huts. de.wikipedia "Monte Verità" calls it "eine typische Licht-Luft-Hütte": it **is** one of the huts. It survives as a museum piece.

**[A, de.wikipedia]** 1901: **Gusto Gräser is expelled** after disputes with the other founders. Karl Gräser and Jenny Hofmann afterward set up their own household on adjoining ground. **[R]** No source consulted locates the parcel; the atlas pin is approximate.

**[A, de.wikipedia]** 1902: **"Wie gelangen wir Frauen zu harmonischen und gesunden Daseinsbedingungen?"** Note the order: *harmonischen* before *gesunden*. Several digests invert it.

**[A, en.wikipedia, quoting Hofmann]** On the name, she wrote that the founders "in no way claim to have found the 'truth'... but that we... in word and deed... help the lie to be destroyed, the truth to be successful." Her German, memoir p. 38:

> »Monte Veritä« nennen wir den Boden unseres von Wahrheitssuchern gegründetes und Wahrheit Suchenden geweihtes Unternehmen.

(Spelling and grammar as printed.)

**[A, memoir p. 10]** On how the colony looked from outside:

> Unsere einfache und luftige Kleidung, unsere Hutlosigkeit, unsere entweder blossen oder nur mit Sandalen bekleideten Füsse erregten allenthalben grosses Aufsehen.

**[A, memoir p. 49]** "Gustav Nagel tritt am 17. November vor unsere erstaunte Gruppe. Heftiges Schneegestöber hindert ihn nicht, blossfüssig und nur mit einem kurzen Hemde bekleidet einherzugehen." A de.wikipedia footnote attributes the episode to her via TicinArte.ch, but that domain now redirects to an unrelated casino site and could not be checked. The book itself is used.

**[A, de.wikipedia]** 1903: the Dutch reformer **Joseph Salomonson** persuades her to go fully vegan. Her fruit-diet formulation, memoir p. 50: "die ausschliessliche Fruchtnahrung als die ihnen von der Natur bestimmte und bekömmlichste Ernährungsweise betrachten."

**[A, de.wikipedia]** 1905: **"Vegetabilismus! Vegetarismus!"**

**[A, de.wikipedia "Lotte Hattemer"]** 19 April 1906: **Lotte Hattemer dies at Ascona**, aged 29.

**Contradiction, unresolved, and two errors corrected.** A digest called her "a mayor's daughter from Stettin" who died "of poison administered by Otto Gross." (1) de.wikipedia "Lotte Hattemer" gives her born in **Berlin**, 24 November 1876, daughter of Heinrich Hermann Hattemer, a Berlin official; Stettin appears nowhere. (2) The manner of death is disputed: de.wikipedia "Ida Hofmann" says a drug-dependent physician supplied the poison, while de.wikipedia "Lotte Hattemer" records that the official 1909 police report ruled **suicide**, that the cocaine-and-opium account is rumour, and that Johannes Nohl and Erich Mühsam were also named in it. Left open.

**[A]** 1906: **"Monte Verità. Wahrheit ohne Dichtung. Aus dem Leben erzählt,"** printed by **Karl Rohm, Lorch (Württemberg)**. Its opening line, p. 3:

> Wahrheit und Freiheit in Denken und Handeln sollten künftig als teuerster Leitstern ihr Streben begleiten.

**Fabricated imprint corrected.** A citation circulating in the research pool gave the publisher as "Verlag Genossenschaftsbuchdruckerei, Ascona/Lugano." No such imprint appears in the book. The title page reads: "Monte Veritä / Wahrheit ohne Dichtung. / Aus dem Leben erzählt von Ida Hofmann-Oedenkoven. / Lorch (Württemberg) / Druck und Verlag von Karl Rohm / 1906." Archive.org metadata and the de.wikipedia bibliography agree. Strike the Ascona/Lugano imprint wherever it appears.

## 6. The hill fills with strangers, 1907-1918

Hofmann and Oedenkoven ran the sanatorium continuously from 1900 to about 1920. Everything the corpus records as happening on Monte Verità in those years happened on their ground, under their management, with her resident. None of the files below names her.

- **[R, hermann_hesse.journey.json:212-221]** April 1907: Hesse climbs from a Locarno cure and finds "vegetarians, nudists, theosophists, anarchists, and sun-worshippers" resigning from the nineteenth century.
- **[A, laban.journey.json:358-373]** Summer 1913: Laban opens his School for Art on the hill. **[R, mary_wigman.journey.json:124-134]** Wigman climbs up that summer. **[R, suzanne_perrottet.journey.json:294-304]** Perrottet arrives on the same shared pin. **[R, jaques_dalcroze.journey.json:335,369]** Jaques-Dalcroze loses both women "up the mountain above Ascona."
- **[R, suzanne_perrottet.journey.json:370-379]** August 1914: the international colony scatters as enemy nationals.
- **[R, laban.journey.json:531-541]** August 1917: *Sang an die Sonne* on the Monte Verità meadow, with a midnight rite at Gusto Gräser's grotto below the colony. Gräser, expelled in 1901, is by then living on the same hill outside it.

**Contradiction, flagged.** en.wikipedia dates Laban's school on the hill 1913-1918; de.wikipedia says summer courses 1913-1917, April to October each year. The atlas gives the range as contested. **No source consulted ties Hofmann personally to the Laban school**; the connection is co-tenancy of one hill, not collaboration.

**[A, de.wikipedia; de.wikipedia "Henri Oedenkoven"]** During the war years Oedenkoven meets the dancer **Isabelle (Bien) Adderley**, born 1890 in India, marries her in law, and has three children. She reportedly remains on friendly terms with both. **[R]** No source names the place or date of the meeting.

**[A, en.wikipedia]** 1917: the occultist **Theodor Reuss** stages a congress at Monte Verità on nationalism, women's rights, and mystical themes; his erotic ritual practice leads Oedenkoven to expel him.

**[R]** A geography note has her playing Wagner at evening gatherings in Casa Anatta. The Wagner detail is unattested in anything reached; tradition only, and it should not be hardened.

## 7. Leaving, c.1920-1926

**[A, de.wikipedia works list]** **"Beiträge zur Frauenfrage,"** ca. 1920, **Winnenden** (Württemberg). The date is circa; the place *is* given in the source, contrary to a note claiming the source is silent on it. A further claim that this continued writing "she began under the pseudonym Vera" rests on a misreading and is dropped: nothing consulted supports a Vera pseudonym.

**[A, de.wikipedia "Henri Oedenkoven"]** In 1920 Oedenkoven, Isabella, and Hofmann leave Monte Verità and try a further vegetarian colony **in Spain**, later in Brazil. The Spanish stage is routinely omitted by summaries that jump straight to Brazil. **[R]** No Spanish location is named anywhere reached, so it cannot be mapped; the atlas folds it into the departure stop. de.wikipedia hedges the year itself as "Um 1920."

**[A, de.wikipedia]** In Brazil they attempt **Monte Sol**, modeled on Monte Verità; it fails quickly, reportedly for climatic reasons. **[R]** No municipality named. The atlas pin is a São Paulo state placeholder, not a located site.

**[A, de.wikipedia]** "Eine kurze Zeit arbeitete sie beim Rundfunk": she works briefly in radio. **[R]** No station, city, or year. Pinning this to São Paulo asserts more than the source does; the atlas marks the pin a placeholder.

**[A, de.wikipedia]** Gravely ill, she takes refuge with her youngest sister **Lilly**, married to the Protestant pastor **Friedrich Wilhelm Brepohl**, at **Lapa, Paraná**. **[R, unverifiable]** The onward clause, that she was then moved to São Paulo for specialist treatment, rests solely on gusto-graeser.info, which returned 404 throughout this session. Downgraded.

**[A, de.wikipedia; it.wikipedia; Wikidata Q16744619]** **She dies at São Paulo on 12 July 1926, aged 61**, not 62: her sixty-second birthday was three months away. The building is not named.

**Gap, stated as a gap.** **No accessible source names a grave, cemetery, or tomb.** The burial site is undocumented in everything reached, including the family album listed on gusto-graeser.info as `Album-Ida-Hofmann.pdf`, which was not opened in this pass and may hold what the encyclopedias lack.

## 8. Afterlife

**[A, de.wikipedia "Monte Verità"]** **1926**: Baron **Eduard von der Heydt** buys the property for 160,000 francs, converts Casa Anatta into a private residence for his art collection, has Emil Fahrenkamp build a modernist hotel in **1929**, and bequeaths the estate to the canton in **1964**.

**Conflation corrected.** A pool claim had von der Heydt's Monte Verità become "a gathering point for the Eranos circle." **Eranos** was founded in **1933** by Olga Fröbe-Kapteyn on her own estate at **Moscia**, on the lake shore near Ascona (Casa Gabriella), not on Monte Verità and not on von der Heydt's land (en.wikipedia "Eranos"; jung.journey.json:962-967 pins Eranos at 46.146 / 8.755, below the hill). Monte Verità became an occasional Eranos venue only much later. Neighbours, not the same thing.

**[A]** **1978**: Harald Szeemann curates *Monte Verità: Berg der Wahrheit* (Italian *Le mammelle della verità*) and edits the catalogue credited with reviving interest in the colony. **[R, charlotte_bara.journey.json:725-729]** The corpus records Szeemann setting Charlotte Bara among the exemplary artists beside "Laban and the barefoot prophets she outlived." The barefoot prophets are Hofmann's founding cluster; the corpus file does not name her.

**[A, monteverita.org]** **Casa Anatta** is reactivated for exhibition in **1979** and becomes the official History Museum in **1981**, open April to October. A digest folded both into 1981; they are distinct.

**[A]** **1986**: Martin Green, *Mountain of Truth: The Counterculture Begins, Ascona 1900-1920*, treats her as a central figure and is the main English route to her.

**[A, it.wikipedia bibliography]** **1998**: Julia Schiff's article (full citation in section 2) is a study of Hofmann specifically, not general Lebensreform scholarship, and its subtitle is the probable origin of the Transylvania attribution. Unread.

**[R, isbn.de]** Modern reissues circulate: a German *Monte Verità* under ISBN 979-1255590217, advertised as told "ohne Dichtung, aber mit spitzer Zunge," and an Italian *Monte Verità: Verità senza poesia* under ISBN 979-1255590019. Publisher and date unconfirmed; the 979-12 prefix is an Italian ISBN block, suggesting an Italo-Swiss small press behind both.

**[R, unverifiable]** The piano she played at Monte Verità is said to be kept in the Kursaal of Ascona. The page carrying the claim 404s; nothing else consulted mentions the instrument.

## 9. Corpus position

**[A]** No existing journey file names Ida Hofmann. The census carries her at line 157 as "Ida Hoffmann," co-founder and pianist, directly below Oedenkoven and two rows below Rikli. Nine files touch her ground without touching her: laban, mary_wigman, suzanne_perrottet, hermann_hesse, charlotte_bara, jung, jaques_dalcroze, elsa_lindenberg, vera_skoronel. Two reach her by descent rather than geography: elsa_lindenberg.journey.json:52 calls a Berlin garden "the mountain of Monte Verità come down," and vera_skoronel.journey.json:90 says a 1919 Zurich school "still breathes Monte Verità air."

**Gap.** None of the four canonical example pins (Kaaba, Temple Mount, Paris, Buenos Aires) connects to any file touching Ascona, Oedenkoven, or Hofmann. Her Brazilian years produce no pin the corpus already holds; São Paulo is not Buenos Aires, and forcing the two together would be false.
