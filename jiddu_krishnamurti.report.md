# Jiddu Krishnamurti (1895-1986): research report
*Written 2026-08-28 for `jiddu_krishnamurti.journey.json`. 44 stops, 7 segments.*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, single secondary source, or an interested party's own claim. Contradictions are flagged and left standing. Unreachable sources are named with the reason.

A structural warning. Nearly everything documented before 1930 was written by an organization with a doctrinal stake in what he was, and nearly everything after 1930 by organizations founded to publish him. The Theosophical Society chronology (ts-adyar.org) and the Krishnamurti Foundation of America timeline (thelifeofkrishnamurti.kfa.org) are the two spines of the biography, and they disagree with each other and with Wikipedia at several points.

## 1. Madanapalle and Kadapa, 1895-1908

**Birth, Madanapalle, 11 May 1895 [A].** ts-adyar.org; the same date is recorded in the 1914 Privy Council judgment. Father Jiddu Narayaniah, an official of the colonial administration and a Society member since 1882; mother Sanjeevamma. *Contradiction, unresolved:* the underlying registration is not secure, some sources give 12 May, and earlier biographers argued the year into 1896. The file carries 11 May with the dispute in `date_confidence`, per the rule that the hedge lives there and never in prose.

**Move to Kadapa, 1903 [A].** en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiddu_Krishnamurti states it directly. *The pool tagged this [R] as "family tradition"; that downgrade was wrong and is corrected upward here.*

**Malaria, frailty, poor school performance [R].** Biographical tradition transmitted mainly through Mary Lutyens. No school records located. That teachers took him for a slow child is what the biographers report, not something documented from the period.

**Death of Sanjeevamma, 1905 [A for the death, R for the reported visions].** KFA timeline; ts-adyar.org. *Correction of a four-year anachronism:* the pool placed this stop at the Adyar estate, with Adyar coordinates, but she died while the family lived in the Kadapa region and they did not reach Adyar until January 1909. The file pins the death at Kadapa, 14.4674, 78.8241.

## 2. Adyar, 1909-1911

**Family moves onto the Society's ground, January 1909 [A].** KFA timeline; ts-adyar.org.

**Leadbeater on the beach, April 1909 [A for the encounter, R for the aura].** The KFA timeline states the encounter as fact. The aura wholly without selfishness is Leadbeater's own occult claim, uncorroborated outside Theosophical testimony, and the campa reports it as his claim rather than as an event.

**Guardianship letter, 6 March 1910 [R].** Wikipedia, corroborated by the Privy Council record. The letter itself was not seen.

**At the Feet of the Master, 1910, under the name Alcyone [A for publication, R for authorship].** ts-adyar.org; the 1914 judgment discusses the book's origin. The manuscript notes disappeared, Krishnamurti later said he did not remember writing it, and authorship is left open. theosophy.wiki, which carries the edition history, returned 403, so the frequently quoted translation counts are omitted.

**Head of the Order of the Star in the East, 11 January 1911 [A].** ts-adyar.org, corroborated by `annie_besant.journey.json`, which dates the founding to the same day at Adyar.

*Contradiction, unresolved:* en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Star_in_the_East places the founding at Benares in April 1911, not Adyar in January. Both datings sit in that stop's `date_confidence`; neither is preferred.

## 3. England, Sicily, the courts, 1911-1914

**Departure for England, 22 April 1911 [A].** ts-adyar.org lists it as its own entry. *Correction of a conflation:* the pool read 22 April as the date of his first public speech. ts-adyar.org separately dates that to the 1920 Theosophical World Congress in Paris; the KFA timeline and Wikipedia support a first address to Order **members** in London during 1911, plus the meeting with Emily Lutyens. The file dates the stop 1911-04-22 and says in `date_confidence` that this is the departure date.

*Gap:* no documented Theosophical London address matches the pool's coordinate (51.4934, -0.1611, in Belgravia). Candidates are Tavistock Square, Gloucester Place and New Bond Street. Rather than pick one on no evidence, the file pins central London and says the venue is undocumented. The pool's further claim that Besant announced the Order at the London headquarters is dropped as unsupported: both rival datings place the founding in India.

**Benares convention, December 1911 [A for the event, R for the "outpouring"].** The certificate distribution is the documented Order-organising occasion; Wikipedia's Order article places the founding at Benares in 1911. *Contradiction, unresolved:* ts-adyar.org's chronology lists a Benares convention for December **1921**, not 1911, so the pool's cited source does not say what was claimed of it.

**Taormina, May to July 1912 [A].** ts-adyar.org. What the "occult and social training" consisted of is described only by Theosophical sources.

**Narayaniah revokes the guardianship and sues, 11 July 1912 [R].** Wikipedia. The Madras High Court opinion at indiankanoon.org/doc/1214788/ was cited in the pool but not independently fetched, so the Chingleput and High Court stages rest on secondary summary.

**Privy Council allows the appeal, 25 May 1914 [R, resting on a reported primary].** casemine.com's summary of the judgment, which returned 403 on refetch. The decision turned on jurisdiction: the Indian court had no authority over boys then living in England, and the Committee did not rule on the truth of the World Teacher claims. *Correction of a building:* the pool pinned the Judicial Committee at 51.4995, -0.1248, which is Parliament Square and Middlesex Guildhall, where the JCPC has sat only since 2009. In 1914 it sat in the Privy Council Chamber off Downing Street, about 51.5034, -0.1272.

## 4. Ojai, 1922-1925

**Arrival in the Ojai valley, 1922 [A].** Wikipedia; ts-adyar.org; ojaihistory.com. Travelled via Australia seeking a climate for Nitya's tuberculosis; supporters acquired Pine Cottage. *Coordinate correction, applied at ten stops:* the pool's Ojai coordinate (34.4664, -119.2415) sits in the foothills north of downtown, in the wrong drainage. Pine Cottage is 1098 McAndrew Road and the Krishnamurti Retreat 1130 McAndrew Road (kfa.org/contact/); McAndrew Road geocodes to about 34.4553, -119.1841, and the file uses **34.455, -119.184** throughout for the East End property. ojaihistory.com confirms Arya Vihara as a ranch house on the same road, so the pool's separate Arya Vihara coordinate (34.4581, -119.2232), 3.6 km west, is not used.

**The process, 17-19 August 1922 [A for the episode, R for the interpretation].** Wikipedia dates it precisely. The neck pain, extreme sensitivity and childlike speech are witnessed; the unitive experience of road worker, pickaxe, stone, grass and tree is his own account. Its framing as a spiritual "process" is a household label, and the pepper tree as its site is devotional tradition.

**Helen Knothe, from 1921 [A].** *Tag corrected upward from the pool's [R]:* en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Nearing states the relationship independently, citing Mary Lutyens, *Krishnamurti: The Years of Awakening* (1975). The place is unspecified in both articles, so it is carried as a clause inside the Ojai arrival campa rather than staged at an invented location.

**Nitya's death, Ojai, 13 November 1925 [A].** Wikipedia; ts-adyar.org corroborates the year. *Cause corrected:* Wikipedia gives influenza and complications of tuberculosis, age 27, not tuberculosis alone. Krishnamurti was at sea; the shipboard collapse is reported by companions rather than documented.

**Adyar convention address, 28 December 1925 [A].** ts-adyar.org. *Gap:* what he privately believed in the four years between Nitya's death and the Ommen dissolution is undocumented in anything reachable, and the campa says so rather than filling it in.

## 5. The schools and the dissolution, 1926-1934

**Rishi Valley: three sources, three answers, unresolved.** KFA timeline: land acquired 1925, school founded 1928. en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rishi_Valley_School: founded 1926, land mostly acquired by 1929, teaching beginning on the site in **September 1931** with about ninety students, after a 1930 cyclone forced a move from the Guindy School at Madras. The pool gave 1926 citing the KFA timeline, which does not say 1926. All three sit in the stop's `date_confidence`; coordinate tightened to 13.6339, 78.4539.

**Rajghat [A, pool's date wrong by eight years].** KFA timeline: "1926: Rajghat and Benares lands acquired for schools." Wikipedia: the school was **founded 1934**, at 25.3277, 83.0356. The file dates the stop to the 1926 acquisition, names 1934 for the foundation, and uses the corrected coordinate.

**Hollywood Bowl, 1928, audience reported at 16,000 [A].** KFA timeline.

**Dissolution of the Order, Ommen, 3 August 1929 [A].** KFA timeline; Wikipedia's Order article for the roughly 3,000 present and the Dutch radio broadcast; ts-adyar.org for the 75,000-plus membership. The full text at organism.earth returned 404 on refetch, so the quotations come from Wikiquote and the Order's Wikipedia article, which carry them verbatim with citation. *Quotation correction:* the pool's headline quote silently dropped its opening clause, turning a framed assertion into a bare declarative. The sentence is "**I maintain that** Truth is a pathless land, and you cannot approach it by any path whatsoever, by any religion, by any sect." The full form is used, and it matches byte for byte the quote already in `annie_besant.journey.json`.

**Eerde: dates and coordinate both corrected.** nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasteel_Eerde has van Pallandt granting the castle to the Order in **1923** (not 1912) and the Pallandts recovering it in **1931** (not 1929); the KFA timeline independently gives "1931: Eerde castle returned to van Pallandt." The estate was about 1,400 ha, roughly 3,500 acres, not 5,000. Kasteel Eerde is at 52.4844, 6.4528; the pool's 52.5187, 6.4319 is Ommen town, 4 km north. *A deliberate exception:* the file nevertheless pins Ommen at **52.5233, 6.4225**, inherited byte-identical from `annie_besant.journey.json`, because the two files narrate the same afternoon and must land on the same point. The true Eerde coordinate is named in each affected `date_confidence`, so an operator can retire the inherited pin across both files at once if he chooses.

**Arya Vihara household [A for the household, R for the relationship].** The Rajagopal marriage (1927) and Radha's birth (1931) are attested via Wikipedia. The sexual relationship with Rosalind, dated from about 1932 and running roughly 25 years, rests on Radha Rajagopal Sloss, *Lives in the Shadow with J. Krishnamurti* (1991), published five years after his death, and its particulars are disputed by Mary Lutyens, whose rebuttal was not fetched. Recorded as contested.

**Oak Grove talks from 1932 [A for the date, site disambiguated].** KFA timeline. The pool's coordinate (34.4544, -119.2472) matches neither candidate site: not the East End grove beside Arya Vihara, not the Meiners Oaks grove of the later school. The 1932 Sunday talks were given at the grove adjoining Arya Vihara, and the file pins them there and says so.

**Sydney and Melbourne tours, 1932-1934 [A].** KFA timeline. The 1922 Australian transit is pool chronology [R], carried only as a clause.

## 6. 1935-1946

**South American tour, 1935 [A].** KFA timeline: eight months through Brazil, Uruguay, Argentina, Chile and Mexico; the Catholic pamphlet *Contra Krishnamurti*; tear gas brought by disruptors.

**Last Ommen camp, 1937, and the ban in Rome [A].** KFA timeline, which adds that the Ommen site became a German concentration camp during the war and was never used by him again.

**Eddington, Pennsylvania, 1936 [A, cut for space].** Two pool errors recorded so they are not lost: the coordinate should be 40.0844, -74.9450 (Bucks County), not -74.8807; and "Happy Valley Foundation area" is a conflation, that foundation being in Ojai.

**Meeting Aldous Huxley, Ojai region, 1938 [A].** Wikipedia. Huxley's 1954 foreword to *The First and Last Freedom* is attested. There is no `aldous_huxley.journey.json`, so this relation rests on the outside record alone.

**Wartime Ojai and FBI surveillance, 1940-1944 [A, pool's characterization corrected].** The KFA timeline confirms that agents under Hoover infiltrated his talks taking notes and that surveillance ended when he was judged no threat. The same entry records twice-weekly Oak Grove discussions and twice-weekly Hollywood meetings through 1940-1944, and a stay in Ojai until 1946. So "largely suspending public talks" overstates it: he suspended large public talks and international touring while keeping the small meetings.

**Krishnamurti Writings, Inc. founded, 1944 [R].** Wikipedia, single source.

## 7. 1947-1985

**Return to India, 1947 [A].** Wikipedia: Nehru, the Mehta sisters Pupul and Nandini, talks at Bombay, Madras, Delhi and Varanasi.

**David Bohm [A, pool corrected twice].** en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Bohm: they had met in 1959, the sustained interaction began in 1961, and the collaboration lasted a quarter of a century, about twenty-five years, not "nearly two decades". *Gap:* the location of the 1959 meeting is not confirmed anywhere fetched. The file pins the stop in central London, where Bohm was then working, dates it 1961, and states in `date_confidence` that the coordinate is an ordering device rather than an attested site.

**Saanen [A, start year corrected].** KFA timeline: talks began at Saanen in **1962**, not 1961, and ran yearly until 1985. Coordinates 46.4881, 7.2549 are correct.

**Krishnamurti Foundation Trust (UK) 1968, Brockwood Park School 1969 [A].** kfoundation.org/brockwood/ for the Trust, Wikipedia for the school. *Coordinate corrected by about 10 km:* Brockwood Park is at 51.0339, -1.1086, in Bramdean and Hinton Ampner; the pool's 51.0736, -1.2394 lands near Cheriton, and a variant of the same wrong pin (51.07, -1.22) appeared in the afterlife material.

**The Rajagopal litigation: contradiction, unresolved.** Wikipedia dates litigation from 1971 with a final settlement in 1986, shortly after his death. The KFA timeline has a complaint filed in the Superior Court in **1972** to recover assets from Krishnamurti Writings, Inc., and "1974: Lawsuit settled in favor of KFA". The file dates the stop 1971 and describes filing, settlement, reopening and a 1986 close, without asserting which chronology is right.

**Oak Grove School [A].** Founded 1975 at Arya Vihara with three students and two teachers (ojaihistory.com; kfa.org); moved 1977 to 175 acres on Lomita Avenue, Meiners Oaks. *Coordinate note:* the school is at 220 West Lomita Avenue, 34.4418, -119.2811, not the 34.4506, -119.2661 or 34.4552, -119.2764 carried in the pool. The stop is pinned at the founding site, with the Meiners Oaks coordinate in the confidence field.

**Kennedy Center, "1980s", and Carnegie Hall, "1960s-1970s" [A but undated, both cut].** ts-adyar.org lists them without years, too thin to stage as stops.

**Los Alamos, 1984 [A].** ts-adyar.org. The content of the discussions is undocumented in anything fetched; the campa's account of the topic is inference from his standing subject matter and is flagged as such.

**United Nations address and Peace Medal, 1985 [A for the event, R for the substance].** KFA archival pages and kfoundation.org; Wikipedia gives 1984-1985 for UN-related talks. The medal is described as a UN Peace Medal by the foundation itself, an interested party.

## 8. 1986

**Final public talk, Vasanta Vihar, Adyar, 4 January 1986 [A].** KFA timeline; Wikipedia. *Coordinate corrected:* Vasanta Vihar is on Greenways Road, Kesavaperumalpuram, geocoding to 13.0198, 80.2571. It is the Krishnamurti Foundation India headquarters and a separate property from the Theosophical estate about 2 km south; the pool reused the TS coordinate for it.

**Return and diagnosis, 10 January [A]; final recording, 7 February [A]; death, 17 February 1986 at ten minutes past midnight Pacific Time, pancreatic cancer, aged 90, no memorial service [A].** Wikipedia and KFA timeline, corroborated for date and age by ts-adyar.org. The Los Angeles Times obituary cited in the pool blocks WebFetch and is not cited in the file.

**Ashes divided in three, dispersed at Ojai, in England and in India, with immersion in the Ganga at Varanasi and Gangotri and off Adyar beach [A].** Wikipedia. Exact dispersal dates are undocumented; the file's 1986-03-01 is an ordering device and says so.

## Apparatus relations for the operator

Krishnamurti is **not** listed in `EXCEPTIONS.md`, so relations not rooted in his own life were not written into his file. They are kept here rather than deleted, with the direction named.

**Forward direction, not licensable, not written in:**

1. **Radha Rajagopal Sloss's 1991 memoir.** Sole basis for the Rosalind material, postdating his death by five years. Carried only as a `suggested_ref` and as a dated clause inside his own household stop, never as a scene. Mary Lutyens's rebuttal is likewise a `suggested_ref` only.
2. **Bruce Lee.** The pool listed Lee among those he influenced; the researcher then over-refuted it. Checking Lee's own article and finding no mention establishes only that the two articles are inconsistent, not that the claim is false: Lee **is** named in the list of individuals influenced by Krishnamurti on his Wikipedia article, with Alan Watts, Henry Miller, Jackson Pollock, Terence Stamp and Eckhart Tolle. Reported but thin, and unreciprocated. If Bruce Lee ever gets a file, this is his stop. Nothing about it is in this one.
3. **The digital afterlife**: online archives, the official podcast, the 17-volume *Collected Works* (Kendall/Hunt, 1991). All postdate him, none is staged. The archive scale figures are used only where he himself is on the platform, in the Vasanta Vihar stop, attributed to the foundation's own count.

**Backward direction: none found.**

## Interlocks

**Named in campas, a real relation in each case:**

- **`annie_besant`.** Direct and reciprocal; the spine of the file's first half. Both files narrate the same beach in April 1909, the same guardianship in March 1910, the same Order founding, the same afternoon at Ommen. Four coordinates inherited byte-identical from her file: Adyar compound 13.008, 80.259; Adyar beach 13.0046, 80.2586; Ommen 52.5233, 6.4225; Benares 25.3176, 82.9739.
- **`steiner`.** One-directional but specific: Steiner refused the World Teacher proclamation about Krishnamurti and lost the German Section charter over it in March 1913. Named once, in the 1911 Order stop. **No Berlin or Cologne stop was written**, since Krishnamurti was never there for it; that is Steiner's own scene, on his own file, where it already sits.
- **`blavatsky`.** One-directional: she died in 1891, but he was raised inside the Society she founded and inside her doctrine of the Masters, and the 1929 dissolution repudiates that apparatus. Named once, at the Adyar arrival.

**Found by the interlock lens, deliberately not named:**

- **`dion_fortune`.** Her 1927 Christian Mystic Lodge presidency was framed as a rival answer to Besant's presentation of Krishnamurti as World Teacher: real, one-directional, and **hers**. Her file already carries it, and the pool records no reliable evidence of a meeting.
- **`victoria_ocampo`.** Connected only through shared third parties (Huxley, Nehru, later Indira Gandhi), and her file never names Krishnamurti. Shared acquaintances are not a relation between the two of them, and co-location in Buenos Aires in 1935 is already visible on the map.
- **`giuseppe_tucci`.** Inferred parallel only, through Nehru and through separate encounters with the same Dalai Lama decades apart. Not a crossing.

**New anchors this file adds:** Ojai, Saanen, Brockwood Park, Rishi Valley, Rajghat, Vasanta Vihar, Madanapalle, Kadapa. A grep across all `*.journey.json` returns nothing for any of them, nor for the Rajagopals.

## Honest gaps

1. Three places are undocumented and pinned by default, each saying so in `date_confidence`: the venue of the 1911 London speech, the site of the 1959 first meeting with Bohm, and the place of the 1921 Helen Knothe attachment.
2. What he privately believed between November 1925 and August 1929 is undocumented. No letter or diary fixing when he stopped believing the Theosophical account of himself was located.
3. The substance of the 1984 Los Alamos discussions and the 1985 UN address is unrecorded in anything fetched; both campas describe the topic from his standing subject matter and say so.
4. No primary edition of *The First and Last Freedom* or *Freedom from the Known* was reachable, so every book quotation is marked in `quote_source` as coming from Wikiquote.
5. The final 1986 private remark that no comparable body would appear for hundreds of years is transmitted only through associates' recollection (the Zimbalist and Jayakar circle), could not be verified, and is **not** in the journey file.
