# Bernard Arnault: research report

*Compiled 2026-08-23 for `bernard_arnault.journey.json`. Subject living (b. 5 March 1949): no death, no tomb, no posthumous reception, so the closing segments carry the living public record instead.*

**Legend.** **[A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or single-source lead not independently confirmed. Where sources disagree both figures are printed and neither is preferred. Inaccessible sources are named with the reason.

**The source base is lopsided.** The two Wikipedias are readable and disagree with each other at several points. The corporate sources (lvmh.com, fondationlouisvuitton.fr, Dior corporate history) timed out or returned 403 every time. The press that has actually investigated him, Le Monde, Reuters, The Guardian, Libération, is uniformly blocked to WebFetch here. What is left at the critical points is encyclopaedic summary plus hostile advocacy journalism, chiefly `fakirpresse.info`, founded by François Ruffin, a named party to two of the disputes in this file.

**Eleven pool coordinates were wrong** and are corrected against the Wikipedia article for the place named, each correction stated in its own stop's `date_confidence`: Faidherbe, Flixecourt, Cheval Blanc, Via dei Condotti, Quarona, Libération, Tribunal de Paris, Le Monde, Oxford Union, Delaware Chancery, the Fondation. Errors ran from 400 m to about 9 km. Two need naming. **Delaware Chancery:** no page giving the Justice Center's exact coordinates could be loaded, so 39.7459/-75.5479 is a street-level geocode and says so. **Fondation Louis Vuitton:** the pool gave 2.2649 in one lens and 48.8666/2.2506 in another, so the file inherits **48.8766/2.2634 byte-identical from `louis_vuitton.journey.json`**. Inherited unchanged: Paris **48.8566/2.3522**, Dior **48.8657/2.3033**, Le Bon Marché **48.8512/2.3244**, Roubaix **50.6942/3.1746**.

## 1. Roubaix and the family firm, 1949-1981

Born **Bernard Jean Étienne Arnault**, Roubaix, Nord, **5 March 1949** **[A: en. and fr.wikipedia, "Bernard Arnault"]**; Britannica 403, the LVMH governance page timed out twice. Father **Jean Arnault** ran **Ferret-Savinel**, construction and public works **[A]**. Schooled at **Lycée Maxence Van Der Meersch** then **Lycée Faidherbe**, Lille **[A: en.wikipedia; fr.wikipedia lists him among Faidherbe's alumni]**; the dossier named only Faidherbe, and **no source gives the years**, so 1966 is an ordering device. **École Polytechnique**, admitted 1969, graduated **1971**, civil engineering and mathematics **[A: both Wikipedias]**. *Anachronism corrected:* the dossier's place read "Palaiseau/Paris", but en.wikipedia records the school's move to Palaiseau in **1976**; in 1969-1971 it stood at 5 rue Descartes, where the stop is pinned.

**Joins Ferret-Savinel as an engineer, 1971** **[A]**; construction director 1974, chief executive 1977 **[R: dossier only]**. **Marries Anne Dewavrin, 1973** **[A]**. **President from 1978** **[A, both Wikipedias]**, but **contradiction carried, not resolved:** en.wikipedia ends the presidency in **1984**, fr.wikipedia runs it to **1995**. The start is common ground, the terminus is not, and the dossier's blended "1978/1984" is not used. The pivot to holiday property as **Férinel** is **[R: dossier only]**.

**Gap.** No address for Ferret-Savinel or Férinel could be verified, so the Roubaix stops sit on the city pin, which is also the birthplace pin.

## 2. America and the return, 1981-1985

**Relocation to the United States after the May 1981 Socialist victory; condominiums at Palm Beach** **[A: en.wikipedia]**. No Florida property record or contemporary press could be fetched; fr.wikipedia cites **Airy Routier** on the venture's failure, the only critical view reachable.

**The New York taxi anecdote** is **[R]**, and self-created: it survives only in Arnault's own retellings across forty years, with no witness, date, or corroboration. It is kept as a stop because it is the origin he chose, and its `date_confidence` says so.

**Boussac Saint-Frères acquired in a state-managed rescue, 1984**, bringing **Christian Dior** and **Le Bon Marché** **[A]**; Le Monde's 1987 account could not be read. The **44-page plan** promising to preserve **12,200 of 15,000 jobs** is **[R, attributed]**, from `fakirpresse.info`. **Chief executive of Christian Dior, 1985** **[A]**.

**The Flixecourt problem.** The dossier tagged the **February 1985 job-shedding at the Bâcherie and ACM plants** and **3,000 posts in a year** as **[A]** on `fakirpresse.info` citing *Courrier Picard*. **Downgraded to [R] with in-text attribution**: a contested labour claim resting on one hostile source is alleged, not attested. What is **[A]** is the larger figure, en.wikipedia: "After laying off 9,000 workers within two years, he earned the nickname 'The Terminator' while restoring profitability."

**Nickname disentangled.** **"L'Ange exterminateur"** is not, as the dossier had it, a rendering of "the Terminator": it is the **title of Routier's biography**, unlisted by fr.wikipedia, so that attribution stays **[R]**. Note also that **fr.wikipedia does not carry "the Terminator" at all**; its epithet is **"le loup en cachemire"**. Both stand; neither is adjudicated.

## 4. The contests, 1999-2013

**Gucci.** LVMH took roughly a third of the shares; **PPR bought 40 per cent in March 1999 at $75**, diluting LVMH to **20.7 per cent**; settled **September 2001**, LVMH exiting at a reported profit **near $700 million** **[A: en.wikipedia, "Gucci" and "Bernard Arnault"]**. **A staging was removed:** the dossier placed this at the **Enterprise Chamber of the Amsterdam Court of Appeal** with an employee stock-ownership defence, and en.wikipedia's account mentions **neither**, so the stop was rewritten as the dilution and pinned to the canonical Paris pin. With the Ondernemingskamer ruling in hand an Amsterdam stop becomes writable. **Dead end:** no Gucci, Kering or **François Pinault** file exists in the atlas.

**Phillips.** Bought 1999 **[R]**; **70 per cent sold to Simon de Pury and Daniella Luxembourg, March 2002** **[A: theartnewspaper.com, fetched directly]**. **Carrefour.** 9.8 per cent with Colony Capital in 2007 near 47 euros, last 5.7 per cent sold 2021 at 16 euros **[R: dossier only; reuters.com blocked]**.

**Hermès.** ~17.1 per cent disclosed **October 2010** via equity swaps, later ~23.1 per cent; the family reorganises; settled 2014 **[R throughout: dossier only; lvmh.com timed out]**. **AMF fine, 8 million euros, 25 June 2013** **[R]**: decision **SAN-2013-15** was reached but **only header metadata was retrievable**, so amount and date come from the dossier, not the ruling.

**Bulgari, 2011.** en.wikipedia, "Bulgari": the family sold **50.4 per cent for about 3 per cent of LVMH**, an **all-share** deal at **4.3 billion euros ($6.0 billion)**, completed **4 October 2011**. en.wikipedia, "Bernard Arnault": **$5.2 billion**, the March announcement figure. thefashionlaw.com: **$6.01 billion**. The file prints 4.3bn euros with the $5.2-6.0bn range, keeps the all-share structure, and **drops the dossier's "share-and-cash"**. **Not adjudicated.** Also **Via dei Condotti 10 is the flagship store, not a headquarters**.

**Princess Yachts and Royal Van Lent, 2008.** Dates hold, but the **agent is wrong for Princess**: en.wikipedia records that in June 2008 it was LVMH's vehicle **L Capital 2 FCPR** that took 75 per cent from Graham J. Beck, Royal Van Lent alone being acquired by LVMH directly. **No stop was written**, a stated gap: **no verified coordinate for either yard was in the pool**.

## 6. The public man, 2014-2019

**Fondation Louis Vuitton opened 20 October 2014**, Gehry, 8 avenue du Mahatma-Gandhi, initial budget ~100m euros, **final cost reported May 2017 at 780 million** **[A: en.wikipedia, "Fondation Louis Vuitton"]**, upgraded from [R]. The dossier's "close to 790 million" is corrected to **780 million**; the **Cour des comptes figure of ~518m euros in tax reductions stays [R]**. **Oxford Union address, 2016** **[R]**: dossier only, the cited recording never fetched.

**Macron endorsement, 2017** **[A, from the primary].** The dossier's English wording is a **paraphrase merging two sentences from different parts of the piece, reversing their order, and mistranslating one noun**. Primary: **Bernard Arnault, "Pourquoi je vote Emmanuel Macron", *Les Échos*, 5 May 2017**, via the Wayback capture of 31 May 2022:

> "Tout, dans le programme de Marine Le Pen, en dépit de son habillage de souveraineté, respire la crainte et la faiblesse."

and, closing the piece:

> "C'est sans hésiter que je vote pour Emmanuel Macron."

It is **le programme**, the platform, not "the campaign", and **la crainte**, fear. The file uses the French with the Les Échos citation; the BrainyQuote rendering is discarded and WWD stayed paywalled at 402.

**Notre-Dame: 200 million euros pledged after the 15 April 2019 fire, fulfilled by the December 2024 reopening** **[A: Forbes]**, part of **more than 880 million pledged within a day**, with the withdrawn **90 per cent deduction** proposal **[A: en.wikipedia, "2019 Notre-Dame de Paris fire"]**, which **does not single out criticism of the Arnault pledge**. ## Apparatus relations for the operator

Arnault is **not** listed in `EXCEPTIONS.md`, so no apparatus stop is licensed. Per the adversary's duty, relations found that are **not rooted in his own life** are recorded rather than silently deleted, with the direction of each catch.

1. **Mahatma Gandhi and the Fondation: FORWARD, and already correctly placed elsewhere.** `mahatma_gandhi.journey.json` carries a stop at the 20 October 2014 opening on the avenue bearing Gandhi's name. That is Gandhi's stop about Gandhi's name; Arnault has no relation to Gandhi whatever, so **Gandhi is named in no campa here**, and that file's coordinate was not inherited, being ~1.7 km off.
2. **École Polytechnique alumni: no relation, dropped.** A grep found Eiffel, Cauchy, Galois, Haüy, Jacquard, Dreyfus, Sorel and Saint-Simon linked to the school, all a century or more earlier. Shared institution across generations is not a crossing, so none appears; the Palaiseau pin that grep produced went unused too, the dossier-only **2017 Palaiseau stop having been cut** for want of a verifiable record.
3. **Frank Gehry: a real direct relation to a person with no atlas file.** Arnault commissioned the Fondation from him personally, so Gehry is named as its architect, but there is **no `frank_gehry.journey.json`** to interlock with; the Gehry buildings in `ron_rivest.journey.json` and `josefina_ayerza.journey.json` are other commissions and are not referenced.
4. **David Rockefeller Award for Corporate Citizenship, 2011: left out.** The dossier asserts he received it **[R, unverified]**, but an award bearing a man's name is not a relation to that man on the standing test, so **`david_rockefeller` is named in no campa**. If the operator disagrees, its place is a `suggested_ref`, never a stop, and it needs verifying first.
5. **Phillips against Christie's and Sotheby's: shared industry only.** Those houses recur in `david_rockefeller`, `michelangelo` and `auguste_rodin` as venues for dispersals; Arnault competed with the firms, not with those travellers. Not named.
6. **Trump and Epstein: explicitly ruled out.** The dossier notes the two Trump inaugurations and that `jeffrey_epstein.journey.json` documents Trump's circle. **No connection between Arnault and Epstein exists in any source.** Trump is named in the Texas campa only because Arnault stood beside him there, a real crossing; there is no Trump file.
7. **Roubaix and Le Bon Marché: confirmed dead ends.** No atlas file mentions Roubaix, and the Bon Marché grep hit inside `gustave_eiffel.journey.json` was on the term "Polytechnique", not the store.

**Interlocks named in campas: `christian_dior`, `louis_vuitton`.** Both are relations to houses Arnault owns and runs, and the Vuitton crossing is already attested inside the atlas by that file's own "Paris, Arnault takes the chair" stop. **The afterlife apparatus does not apply and was not forced into shape.** Arnault is alive **[A: en.wikipedia, fetched]**: no tomb, no monument, no posthumous reception. He is a businessman, not an author, so **there is no work, and therefore no editions and no translations** to trace.
