# Isabel Perón (María Estela Martínez Cartas de Perón): research report

*2026-08-06. Subject living. `isabel_peron.journey.json`, 8 segments, 45 stops.*

**Legend.** **[A]** attested, source named inline. **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or single thin source. Contradictions are flagged and **not adjudicated**. Holes are named.

**Structural note.** The subject is **living**, so the standard closing movement, the tomb and its fate, has nothing to stand on. The journey ends open, at a house behind a gate in Villafranca del Castillo, and the last stop states the gap instead of inventing a resting place. **[A: es.wikipedia, residence field and 2024 sightings]**

---

## 1. La Rioja and the boards, 1931 to 1955

- Born **La Rioja, 4 February 1931**. **[A: en.wikipedia and es.wikipedia]**
- Father dies, family moves to Buenos Aires **1938**; raised largely by **José Cresto and Isabel Zoila Gómez de Cresto**, informal adoptive parents. **[A: es.wikipedia]** That her stage name derives from Isabel Zoila's is widely repeated and is carried as a hedged clause, not as fact. **[R]**
- **Correction, the Cervantes.** The pool claimed "1950: begins performing as a dancer, adopting the stage name Isabel." Wrong twice. es.wikipedia says only "a sus jóvenes veinte años ingresó al cuerpo de baile del Teatro Nacional Cervantes": at twenty, i.e. **c. 1951** given the February 1931 birth; and it does **not** tie the name "Isabel" to that year. **[A for the corps de ballet; 1950 vs c. 1951 disputed in `date_confidence`, not resolved]**
- **Teatro Avenida, 1953**: "ingresó a la compañía de baile del Teatro Avenida, compartiendo escenarios con Haydée Padilla." **[A: es.wikipedia]**
- Provincial circuits, **Córdoba, Entre Ríos, Santa Fe, 1952 to 1954**, plus Montevideo **1953 to 1955**. **[A: es.wikipedia]** Given as spans with no months; the journey pins the circuit to 1952 and folds Montevideo into it rather than inventing a date.

**Gap.** No independent documentation of the dance career was reachable. This entire phase rests on one encyclopaedia article.

## 2. The tour that ends in Panama, 1955 to 1960

- Tour with **Faustino García's** company through Chile, Peru, Colombia and Panama, **1955**. **[A: es.wikipedia]**

**The meeting with Perón: a live contradiction, not resolved.**
- es.wikipedia: **Panama City, December 1955 to January 1956**, cohabiting from January 1956. **[A]**
- Britannica-derived summary: **1960**, in Panama. **[A as a source claim, incompatible with the above]**
- Corpus `juan_peron.journey.json`: **Colón, January 1956**, with the detail that Perón bought out her contract. **[A: corpus]**

Two conflicts at once, **1956 vs 1960** and **Panama City vs Colón**. The journey splits the material: Panama City carries the arrival and the cabaret circuit and says in prose that sources differ on the venue; Colón carries the meeting at the inherited corpus pin with the Britannica dating flagged in `date_confidence`. Neither is settled.

- **Caracas from August 1956**; refuge in the **Dominican Republic's embassy** during the fall of Pérez Jiménez, January 1958. **[A: es.wikipedia]** **Gap:** the embassy building is not located anywhere reachable, so residence and embassy share one placeholder pin and one stop.
- **Ciudad Trujillo, January 1958 to 26 January 1960**, under Trujillo. **[A: es.wikipedia]**

## 3. Puerta de Hierro, 1960 to 1965

- **Madrid, 26 January 1960**, at the Puerta de Hierro estate, the villa Perón calls 17 de Octubre. **[A: es.wikipedia and en.wikipedia]**
- **Marriage: date contradiction, not resolved.** Announced as **15 November 1961**, a Catholic ceremony at the Madrid home of Perón's physician **Dr. Francisco Flores Tascón**. An alternative account places it on **2 or 5 January 1961** with the announcement withheld for months. **[A for the marriage: en.wikipedia and es.wikipedia; date disputed, es.wikipedia carries both]** The journey uses the announced date and puts the conflict in `date_confidence`. **Gap:** the street address of the Flores Tascón house is nowhere reachable, so the stop uses a generic Madrid placeholder and says so.

## 4. The delegate, 1965 to 1972

The phase the popular account omits, and the one in which she becomes a political actor.
- **Ezeiza, 10 October 1965**, arrival as Perón's personal political delegate. **[A: es.wikipedia]**
- Brief lodging in **Recoleta** (hotel unnamed in the source), then residence at the **Luz y Fuerza union building on Callao**, merged into one stop at the Callao pin. **[A: es.wikipedia]**
- **Over six hundred meetings** nationwide, including **Mendoza, 17 April 1966**; **return to Madrid with López Rega, 9 July 1966**. **[A: es.wikipedia]**
- **Second mission, Ezeiza, 7 December 1971.** **[A: es.wiki]**

**Correction, the López Rega meeting.** The pool asserted "1966-07-09, Madrid: she meets José López Rega." Both place and date are wrong for the meeting. es.wikipedia: "durante esa estadía en Argentina ... supuestamente conoció a José López Rega, en casa del teniente coronel Bernardo Alberte." The meeting is in **Buenos Aires**, during the mission, around **May 1966**; 9 July 1966 belongs only to the return to Spain in his company. The journey splits these into two stops. Note the hedge **supuestamente**: the circumstance is **[R]**, only the association and its consequences are **[A]**.

### The letters

The richest primary material located is Isabel's private correspondence to Perón during the mission, published by **Claudia Peiró, Infobae, 25 June 2017** (fetched directly). Three passages are used as quotes with their letter dates: **20 April 1966** (Mendoza), **6 December 1965** (Catamarca), **9 to 10 June 1966**. **[A: Infobae]** Two corrections:

1. **"En Rosario en un solo día recibí más de 8.000 personas..."** and **"El día que llegues sólo podrás andar en helicóptero..."** Wording exact, **date wrong**: Infobae prints both in the letter of **21 November 1965**, not 21 October. The journey uses 21 November, quotes the first with its printed clause about the callus, and folds the second into the campa. **[A]**
2. **"Vandor etc. han mostrado la hilacha..."** Verbatim in Infobae **but truncated in the pool**, which drops the opening name: "...lo fundamental (es que) **Delia,** Vandor etc. han mostrado la hilacha y no hay que darles tregua porque son una basura." That changes who is called basura, Delia Parodi as well as Vandor. Infobae attaches **no date**, so the pool's "April 1966" is unsupported. The journey does not quote it and names both figures in the Mendoza campa. **[A for the wording, date unsupported]**

**The coffin, 3 September 1971.** Eva Perón's exhumed body is delivered to the Puerta de Hierro villa and Isabel is set to comb and tend it. **[A: corpus `eva_peron.journey.json` and `juan_peron.journey.json`]** That López Rega worked rites over the two women to pass Eva's power into Isabel is **[R]**: es.wikipedia, "José López Rega", frames the soul-transfer story as reported, not documented.

## 5. Perón-Perón, 1973 to 1974

- **China and North Korea, 8 to 18 May 1973**, with López Rega, including a meeting with **Kim Il-sung**. **[A: es.wikipedia]** **Correction:** the pool called her "vice-presidential candidate" on this trip. She was not. The source frames it as "pocos días antes de la jura del gobierno electo," days before Cámpora's 25 May inauguration; the ticket was not proclaimed until 5 August. The journey says outright that she holds no office and no candidacy.
- **Ezeiza massacre and the diversion to Morón, 20 June 1973.** **[A: es.wikipedia, "Masacre de Ezeiza"]** **Caveat:** that article does not itemize Isabel's presence aboard the flight; it follows standard biographical sequencing and is **[R]** on that narrow point.
- **Ticket proclaimed, Teatro Colón, 5 August 1973**; **elected with 61.85 percent, 23 September 1973**, folded into the same stop. **[A: en.wikipedia and es.wikipedia]**
- **Sworn in as vice president, 12 October 1973.** **[A: corpus and en.wikipedia]**

## 6. The presidency, 1974 to 1976

**Succession.** Heart attacks **28 June 1974**, sworn in as acting president next day, Perón dies **1 July 1974** and she is formally sworn in. **[A: Britannica-derived summary for June; en.wikipedia and es.wikipedia for 1 July]**

**Superlative corrected.** The pool claimed "the first woman in the world to hold the title of head of state." Overstated, not carried. en.wikipedia says "one of the first female republican heads of state in the world, and the first woman to serve as President of a country." Earlier women held head-of-state office (Khertek Anchimaa-Toka of Tuva, 1940 to 1944) and Sirimavo Bandaranaike had been head of government since 1960. The journey uses **first woman to hold the title of President**.

**Speech.** The televised address of 1 July 1974 is quoted from the recording "Isabel anuncia la muerte de Perón," cited as a primary source in es.wikipedia. **[A at one remove: cited through the Wikipedia article, not independently played.]** A second verified sentence from the same broadcast, "Que Dios me ilumine y me fortifique...", is not used.

**Acts of the government**, all **[A]** via Britannica-derived summary and en.wikipedia except where noted: anti-terrorism law **30 Sept 1974**; state of siege, habeas corpus suspended, **6 Nov 1974** (merged with the first into one stop); Eva Perón's body repatriated to Olivos **17 Nov 1974** **[A: corpus]**; **Operativo Independencia** in Tucumán from **5 Feb 1975**; the **Rodrigazo** of June 1975, prices doubling May to August, and the first general strike ever called against a Peronist government, July 1975 **[A: en.wikipedia]** (the 12 percent May 1974 inflation baseline is **[R]** in the pool); López Rega's flight to Spain **19 July 1975**; the decrees ordering the armed forces to annihilate subversion **6 Oct 1975**.

**Triple A, count corrected.** The pool assigned "nearly 300 murders" to calendar 1974. en.wikipedia reads "between late 1973 and late 1974 the Triple A had already carried out nearly 300 murders," and gives "some 600 people" overall. The journey states it as a running total over roughly fifteen months. **[A: en.wikipedia]**

**Interlock.** The DINA killing of General Carlos Prats and his wife, Buenos Aires, 30 September 1974, is named in the Triple A stop. **[A: corpus `augusto_pinochet.journey.json`]**

## 7. The helicopter, 1976 to 1981

**24 March 1976.** She works late against a business lockout, boards the presidential helicopter after midnight, is landed at **Aeroparque Jorge Newbery** on a stated mechanical pretext, and is deposed and arrested by **General José Rogelio Villarreal** at about 3:10 a.m. **[A: en.wikipedia; es.wikipedia, "Golpe de Estado ... de 1976"]**

**Quote sourcing corrected, and a method failure recorded.** The pool gave Villarreal's words as "Señora, las Fuerzas Armadas han decidido tomar el control político del país y usted queda arrestada," sourced to Infobae, 24 March 2019, **fetched via the citing Wikipedia article rather than re-fetched**. Infobae in fact prints "...se han hecho cargo del poder político y usted ha sido **destituida**." The pool's wording is nonetheless attested in **TN, "24 de marzo: del horror a la esperanza"**, Diario La Vanguardia and EcuRed. The journey keeps that wording, cites TN, and records the Infobae variant inside `quote_source`. Citing through Wikipedia without re-fetching is the failure that caused this, and it is recorded so it is not repeated. Villarreal's reply to her attempt to negotiate, "Le han dibujado un país ideal, un país que no existe," shares the defect and is paraphrased rather than quoted.

**Detention.** **El Messidor**, the Neuquén governor's residence at Villa La Angostura, about seven months from March 1976, with her housekeeper Rosarito **[A: es.wikipedia, "El Messidor"]**; later the **Naval Arsenal at Azul**, roughly five years of house arrest in all **[R: sources differ on the sequence and number of sites, and some mention undisclosed locations itemized nowhere reachable]**. Azul is folded into the El Messidor campa because its dating cannot be fixed. **Released and exiled to Spain, July 1981** **[A: en.wikipedia and es.wikipedia]**.

**Interlock.** Eva Perón's burial in the Duarte vault at **Recoleta, 22 October 1976**, while Isabel is in custody, sharing ground with `julio_argentino_roca` (there since 1914) and `tschiffely` (1954). **[A: corpus]**

## 8. Madrid, and no tomb, 1981 to the present

- Resettles at **Puerta de Hierro**; resigns as titular head of the Justicialist Party, **February 1985**. **[A: en.wikipedia]**
- Guest of honour at **Alfonsín's inauguration, December 1983**; policy talks **May 1984**; a probate visit in **1988** **[R in the pool, carried as a clause, not a stop]**.
- **1984**, a bomb aboard an Aerolíneas Argentinas flight carrying her, found before detonation, and the remark "nadie se muere cinco minutos antes del plan de Dios" on landing at Barajas. **[R: one Peronist outlet, agendaperonista.com.ar, reached only through the Wayback Machine via es.wikipedia.]** This is the one stop where the sourcing problem is narrated inside the campa rather than left in `date_confidence`, because the quote would otherwise read as established.
- **Spanish citizenship 2000**; Puerta de Hierro sold and the move to **Villafranca del Castillo, 2001**. **[A: es.wikipedia]**
- **Arrested 12 January 2007** on a Mendoza extradition request over 1976 forced disappearances; **extradition refused by the Audiencia Nacional, 28 March 2008**, the limitation period held to have run and the charges declined as crimes against humanity; **Supreme Court dismisses further petitions, 21 June 2017**. **[A: Britannica-derived summary; es.wikipedia]**

**Correction of a false negative.** The pool flagged an October 2024 meeting with Vice President **Victoria Villarruel** as uncorroborated and placed it in Argentina. The opposite is true: it is attested and took place **in Madrid**. es.wikipedia, "Victoria Villarruel", has her visiting Perón at Villafranca del Castillo on **17 October 2024**, and en.wikipedia carries the same meeting on the same date.

**The contradiction this creates, not resolved.** Infobae of the same date reports Villarruel **inaugurating a bust of Isabel Perón in the Argentine Senate**. She cannot have been in the Senate and at a house outside Madrid on one day. Both are carried, in the Senate stop's `date_confidence` and campa, neither preferred.

**Gathered, mostly not used:** the CGT plaque and recorded message, Nov 2020 **[R: Infobae]**; Mazzieri's authorized biography *por siempre de Perón*, 2020 **[A: es.wikipedia]**; Tomás Eloy Martínez, *La Novela de Perón*, 1985 **[A: en.wikipedia]**; *Happyland*, *Arde Madrid*, *Una casa sin cortinas* **[R: es.wikipedia, undated]**; Piumato's 2022 call to dismiss the Triple A case and the 2017 Venegas funeral notice **[R: La Nación]**; her declining Milei's Pacto de Mayo invitation, July 2024 **[A: Infobae]**; Francis's video greeting, Oct 2024 **[R: Infobae]**. Only the March 2024 photograph and the greeting reach the journey.

**Her own two books**, *Las veinte verdades de la mujer justicialista* (1972) and *Las verdades del justicialismo* (1983) **[R: es.wikipedia, "Libros"]**. **GAP:** no foreign-language edition of either found.
