# Franz Anton Mesmer (1734-1815) - research report

*2026-08-02, for the Atlas of Journeys. Physician, of Iznang on the Bodensee; author of animal magnetism; the man the Academy of Sciences of France was convened to examine and did not examine.*

**Legend.** **[A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or an unsupported assertion in a source. **de.wiki / en.wiki / fr.wiki** = the German, English and French Wikipedia articles on Mesmer unless another is named; **RPT** = the 1785 London Franklin commission report (cited in full in section 11), the only primary text read entire. Contradictions are printed side by side; where the journey prefers a reading, the loser is kept on the page.

**Method.** Four lenses (chronology, geography, quotation, atlas interlock), adversarially checked against the cited pages. About a third of first-pass coordinates were wrong by 400 m to 4 km, some in open water or outside the cemetery they named; two factual inversions were caught, the 1798 pension and the Constance move.

---

## 1. Bodensee and the Jesuits, 1734-1759

- Born **23 May 1734** at **Iznang** on the Untersee, now part of Moos am Bodensee; son of **Anton Mesmer**, forester to the prince-bishop of Constance. **[A de.wiki]** *Coord. corrected* to the birth house, Hoeristrasse 10, **47.71495, 8.95704** (OSM), the first-pass pin being 2.5 km offshore.
- **1742-1746**, school at **Kloster Gruenenberg**, music and Latin. Tagged **[R]** in the first pass, over-cautiously: de.wiki states it flatly citing Warndorf, so **[A]**. *Coord. corrected* to the memorial marker, 47.69582, 8.95261, 1 km off.
- **1746-1750**, **Jesuit college of Constance**. **[A de.wiki]** *Coord. corrected:* the college stood at the present Stadttheater, Konzilstrasse 11, **47.66384, 9.17742**, 390 m north of the first-pass pin. The Muenster is 150 m away, so the "within sight of the cathedral" image survives.
- **1750-1754**, **Jesuit university of Dillingen an der Donau**: logic, metaphysics, theology. **1753**, brief theology at **Ingolstadt**, then mathematics, philosophy, physics, languages. **[A de.wiki]** *Strain:* the two spans overlap in de.wiki's own account. Not adjudicated.

## 2. Vienna: thesis, palais, fluid, 1759-1778

- **1759**, Vienna; law, then medicine under **Gerard van Swieten** and **Anton de Haen**. **1766**, doctorate after public disputation, ***De planetarum influxu in corpus humanum***. **[A en.wiki; de.wiki]**
- **10 January 1768**, marries **Maria Anna von Posch** (nee Eulenschenk), widow, owner of Schloss Rothmuehle; they settle at **Landstrasse 261**, with laboratory and private theater. **[A de.wiki]** *Coord. corrected twice:* Landstrasse 261 is the Palais Mesmer site, **Rasumofskygasse 29, 48.203163, 16.391392** (de.wiki 'Palais Mesmer'; OSM). Two first-pass figures were 700 to 900 m off; all Vienna household stops now use 48.2032, 16.3915.
- **Mozart's *Bastien und Bastienne* in the garden, 1768: [R].** en.wiki calls it alleged and notes Nissen found no proof; de.wiki gives the commission to his cousin **Joseph Conrad Mesmer**. One lens asserted it flat under **[A]**; that tag is withdrawn and the campa carries both attributions and the absence of proof.
- **1774**, learns of **Maximilian Hell**'s magnetic cures and experiments with magnets **[A de.wiki]**; treats **Franziska Oesterlin** with magnets and an iron preparation **[A en.wiki]**.
- **1775**, publishes the ***Sendschreiben an einen auswaertigen Arzt ueber die Magnetkur*** and is called to **Munich** over **Johann Joseph Gassner**'s exorcisms. **[A en.wiki; de.wiki]** That he judged the cures real but reattributed them to animal magnetism is consensus in both, **[A]**; that he reproduced the convulsions himself is **[R]** as to staging.
- **Late 1776 to mid 1777**, treats the blind pianist **Maria Theresia Paradis**. **[A en.wiki 'Maria Theresia von Paradis']** Two caveats, both in the hedge field: the Empress was **not** her godmother, so "favorite of Empress Maria Theresa" overstates it; and the parental pension motive, though in Wikipedia, is contested (Pattie 1979), so **[R]**.
- **1777**, a **Viennese imperial commission** declares the methods fraudulent; **20 January 1778**, he leaves for Paris. **[A de.wiki; en.wiki]**

## 3. Paris: the tub and the subscription, 1778-1784

- **Contradiction, unresolved and load-bearing.** fr.wiki puts his first French ground at **Creteil in May 1778**, the hotel Bourret on the Place Vendome afterwards; de.wiki dates the **Place Vendome practice to February 1778**, three months before that arrival. Both stops keep their source dates and the conflict goes in `date_confidence`. The Vendome pin, within 100 m of the square centroid, is kept.
- **1779**, ***Memoire sur la decouverte du magnetisme animal***, Geneva, P. Fr. Didot le jeune, 85 pp., 27 propositions. **[A fr.wiki; en.wiki]**
- **1780**, group treatment by **baquet** **[A en.wiki]**. fr.wiki places the largest tub at the **Hotel Bullion**, corner rue Coq-Heron and rue du Louvre, but **without a year**; the 1780-1782 window is **[R]**.
- **1781**, retreat to **Spa**, where he writes the ***Precis historique des faits relatifs au magnetisme animal jusques en avril 1781*** (London, 1781) **[A fr.wiki]**, *corrected* to **229 pages, not 1,229**. **1781-1782**, the **Bergasse and Kornmann subscription** raises **340,000 livres**. **[A de.wiki]**
- *Correction: two events had been conflated under 1781.* The subscription is 1781-82; the **Societe de l'Harmonie universelle** is not. fr.wiki dates it **1784**, the usual scholarly date is **1783**. The journey separates them, uses 1784, records 1783 as competitor, and takes **twenty** affiliated societies from de.wiki.
- *Dropped for want of a source:* a working entry gave a further salon at the **Hotel de Coigny**, 1782-1784, with "two hundred Parisians a day". fr.wiki gives no years and no coordinate, and the figure misreads its source, where a visiting English physician notes in May 1784 that Mesmer could treat up to two hundred "en meme temps", at one time. No stop written.

## 4. The commission of the King, 1784

- **12 March 1784**, Louis XVI names the commission: four physicians of the Faculty of Paris plus Academy members including **Franklin, Lavoisier, Bailly, d'Arcet, de Bory, Guillotin**. **[A RPT; en.wiki 'Franklin Commission']** Trials partly at **Franklin's residence at Passy**, he being too gouty to travel in.
- **The commission never tested Mesmer.** "The commission did not examine Mesmer specifically, but instead observed the practice of d'Eslon" **[A en.wiki]**. **Charles Deslon** was the sole subject; a working entry saying the commission "also examines" his clinic inverted whose practice the verdict describes. Deslon accepted the finding. *His clinic pin (48.86, 2.34) is a round number with nothing behind it*, carried because a stop needs one.
- **11 August 1784**, the report is **presented to the King**, not to the Academy **[A en.wiki]**, and is dated "the 11th day of August, 1784", not the twelfth **[A RPT]**. The Louvre pin, right for the Academie's seat, is kept as the institutional pin, the delivery corrected in the hedge field. Franklin heads the signature block, at the **foot** of the report. **[A RPT p. 106]**
- **16 August 1784**, the **Societe Royale de Medecine** commission reports separately to the same effect **[A en.wiki]**; its coordinate is approximate, a soft pin. *Not unanimous across both commissions:* **Antoine Laurent de Jussieu** dissented and issued his own report, so "unanimous scientific weight" holds for the Franklin report only.
- **1784, Buzancy**: the **Marquis de Puysegur** reports the magnetic sleep of **Victor Race**, founding the somnambulist line distinct from Mesmer's. **[A en.wiki 'Animal magnetism']** *Gap:* no coordinate in the pool; the journey uses the commune of Buzancy in the Aisne, flagged.
- **1785**, leaves Paris for good **[A fr.wiki]**, against de.wiki's looser 1784/1788 for the departure from France and the travels through England and Germany. Both printed.

## 5. The wandering years, 1785-1801

- **c. 1785-1789, England.** **[R fr.wiki]** London under an assumed identity: no address, no patient, no year inside the span. The largest hole in the biography, stated as one.
- **June 1788, Karlsruhe**, after Margrave **Karl Friedrich** extends recognition. **1790**, death of **Maria Anna von Posch**, the couple then living apart. **[A de.wiki]** *Contradiction:* fr.wiki says only "En 1790, il est de retour a Vienne"; a working entry's motive, "to settle claims on his late wife's inheritance", is **unsourced and dropped**. de.wiki records **no 1790 Vienna visit at all** and has him arrive **14 September 1793**. The journey carries 1793 as **[A]** and asserts no 1790 visit.
- **1793**: arrival 14 September, **detained 17 November** for Jacobin sympathies, **released 9 December**, exiled under surveillance to the region of his birth. **[A de.wiki]**
- **1794**, residency rights in **Thurgau**; **1794-1798** at **Wagenhausen**. **[A de.wiki]** *Coord. corrected* to 47.65963, 8.84849, 3 km off; a vague canton pin was dropped and the two events merged. *"Keeping bees" here: cut*, being in no source consulted, and recorded so it is not silently reintroduced.
- **1798**, French state pension of **3,000 francs** a year for investments devalued by the Revolution, obtained as creancier d'etat. **[A de.wiki, citing Kerner]** *Inversion corrected:* one lens wrote that he sought it "unsuccessfully", the opposite of the source.
- **1798-1801**, Paris and Versailles; publishes ***Memoire sur ses decouvertes*** (1799) and ***Briefe ueber den Ursprung der Blattern*** (1800); **February 1801**, moves to **Versailles**. **[A de.wiki]**
- **[R]** "a millionaire" by 1798: de.wiki's estate summary, a characterization, not a document.

## 6. Return to the lake, 1802-1815

- **Winter 1802/1803**, to **Riedetsweiler**, north of Meersburg. **[A de.wiki; R for the closing year, inferred from the 1808 Frauenfeld move]** *Coord. corrected* to the hamlet node 47.70350, 9.28956, 4.3 km east of the first-pass pin.
- **1808-1812**, **Frauenfeld**, Zuercherstrasse 153, practising largely without charge; **September 1808**, receives **Karl Christian Wolfart**, who in **1814** publishes the late writings as ***Mesmerismus oder System der Wechselwirkungen***. Most of those manuscripts are later lost, and the edition prints the given name as **Friedrich**. **[A de.wiki; fr.wiki]**
- **Date error corrected by four years.** de.wiki's body text moves him to the **Hardthaus, Hussenstrasse 17, Constance, in December 1808**, contradicted by its own preceding section (Frauenfeld until 1812), its own photo caption ("von 1812 bis 1814") and de.wiki 'Hussenstrasse' (likewise). The journey dates it **December 1812** and prints the contradiction. *Coord. corrected* to "Zum Hardthaus", 47.65999, 9.17274, 400 m off.
- **11 April 1814**, holograph testament at Constance: simple burial, autopsy requested. **Summer 1814**, **Riedetsweiler** again, a converted barn on **Sebastian Futterer**'s farmstead, the former inn "zu den Drei Luemmeln"; the 1912 successor at Baitenhauser Strasse 5 carries a concealed Mesmer arms dated 1814. **[A de.wiki]**
- **Autumn 1814**, into the **Heilig-Geist-Spital, Meersburg**, arranged by his nephew **Cajetan Strommayer**, the Spitalpfleger. *Corrected: the room is the Pfruendnerstube "im 1. Stock", one storey up, not a ground-floor room.* **[A de.wiki]**
- **26 February 1815**, falls ill; **5 March 1815**, dies of a stroke at 80, by a contemporary account "composedly, smiling", awaiting glass-harmonica music that never came. **[A de.wiki; a contemporary account, no more]** *Corrected:* fr.wiki's "un ou deux ans avant sa mort" for the settling at Meersburg is about **five months** on the German account. The death house is pinned at **47.6938, 9.2731**; a rival figure at Vorburggasse 11 sits 170 m off, unresolved.

## 7. Afterlife

- **Grave**, Friedhof Meersburg, beside the **Hochkreuz**, about **10 m from the western wall**, **47.696006, 9.278171** (de.wiki; the Friedhof Meersburg article gives 47.695595, 9.27783). **[A]** The first-pass figure fell 1 km southwest, outside the cemetery. The **[R]** hedge about an unverified plot is withdrawn: the plot is precisely described.
- **Monument, 1830**, renovated 1902: a triangular white marble block on three sandstone steps, paid for by the Berlin **Gesellschaft der Naturforscher**, cut by **Sporer** of Konstanz, carrying the eye of providence, the solar system in circles, a torch crossed with a palm, and a compass and sundial on top. **[A de.wiki]** The **Heilig-Geist-Spital** now houses the **Vineum Bodensee**.
- **1843**, **James Braid** coins "hypnotism"; **1851**, repudiates "Transcendental Mesmerism" for a physiological account. British practitioners had adopted "mesmerism" precisely to distance themselves from the fluid theory. **[A en.wiki 'Animal magnetism']** *Gap:* no location in the pool; the journey uses the canonical London pin and says so. Braid worked in Manchester.
- **1846**, the practitioner **Theodore Leger** writes that Mesmer's own part of the doctrine "has been completely abandoned"; **1766-1925**, several hundred books on animal magnetism and its offshoots, and recurring stage ridicule; **1905**, a satirical film by **Georges Melies**. **[A en.wiki]**
- **2013**, bronze bust by **Friedhelm Zilly** at **Iznang** harbour **[A de.wiki]**; *coord. corrected* to about 47.7135, 8.9585, the first-pass pin being 2.4 km east. **Peter Lenk**'s *Magische Saeule* at Meersburg is attested **without a year**: **[A]** for the work, **[R]** for any date. **2019**, plaque at **Rasumofskygasse 29**, Vienna, on the building erected in **1920** over the palais site **[A de.wiki]**; *coord. corrected* to 48.203163, 16.391392, the first-pass pin being 1.4 km off.
- **[R]** Contemporary reassessment of Mesmer as a precursor of dynamic psychotherapy rather than a plain charlatan: fr.wiki's synthesis of modern historiography, no single work named.

## 8. Quotations: what was actually reached

Eleven quotations are used in the journey, all checked against RPT.

- **[A]**, verified at the stated page: Mesmer on the fluid, p. 20; his rebuttal on proof, p. 38; Deslon on the rod and on sound, p. 24 (the rod is used); the commissioners on patients at the baquet and on the crisis, p. 26; the blindfold conclusion, pp. 64-65; the tree experiment, p. 69.
- **"The imagination singly produces all the effects attributed to the magnetism..."** Verbatim, two apparatus errors corrected. *Page:* "p. 70 (approx.)" is too early; offset calibration against the span p. 20 to p. 106 puts it near pp. 82-83, so the page number is dropped. *Context:* it concludes the mademoiselle B---- experiment, not the whole series, and is at once qualified by "Only one objection can be suggested to this experiment". The original opens "The inevitable conclusion is,".
- **The final verdict**, p. 106. Verbatim; the leading ellipsis correctly marks the omitted "they have". Date corrected from 12 to 11 August 1784. The sentence runs on past the excerpt, ascribing the symptoms to compression, imagination and mechanical imitation; `quote_source` notes the truncation.
- **The baquet-room description** and **"the most sensible effects... on the approach of Mesmer"**: an English doctor's eyewitness letter, quoted in **Lisa Morton, *Calling the Spirits*** (Reaktion, 2022), via en.wiki. *Gap:* author and first publication unestablished.

## 9. Atlas interlock

No `mesmer.journey.json` existed before this one, so every crossing is to another traveler. Canonical pins inherited byte for byte.

- **Paris, 48.8566, 2.3522**, already the pin for Gurdjieff, Delsarte and Freud's return from Charcot, is inherited for the subscription, the Societe de l'Harmonie, the 1785 departure, the 1798 pension and the Melies stop. **Vienna, 48.2082, 16.3738**, from `freud.journey.json`, serves the 1778 departure and the 1793 arrest; household stops use the finer Rasumofskygasse pin. **[A]**
- **Salpetriere, 48.8388, 2.3612**, from `delsarte.journey.json` (Delsarte at the asylum c. 1835, studying the gestures of the mad); also Charcot's later hypnosis clinic, under whom Freud studied before advertising "electrotherapy and hypnotic suggestion" in Vienna (`freud.journey.json`). **[A]** for both as written; **[R]** for the genealogy from animal magnetism through Charcot to Freud, which neither states.
- **London, 51.5074, -0.1278**, shared with `bulwer_lytton.journey.json`: *A Strange Story* (1862) has a mesmerist villain, and at Knebworth in 1871 he tells John Forster his vril means electricity, "not mesmerism". **[A that journey]**; **[R]** for descent from Mesmer.
- Named in campa without shared pins: **Kircher** (Rome, 1641, magnetism as the principle binding the cosmos), **Schelling** (Leipzig, 1798, one force graded as magnetism, electricity, chemistry, life), **Gurdjieff** (Tashkent, 1911, "professor of supernatural sciences"). **[A]** for each journey as written, **[R]** for descent from or to Mesmer, which none states.
- **False friends, rejected.** Einstein's 1939 letter to Franklin D. Roosevelt shares only a surname with Benjamin Franklin. Sarmiento read Franklin's autobiography and "wished to be Franklin", but that journey means the American persona, not the Paris commission. Miranda's 1784 diary datelines Boston. The offered pins **Kaaba, Temple Mount, Buenos Aires** intersect nothing in Mesmer's life, by grep across `working/journeys/*.journey.json`. `census_real_persons_2026-08-02.md` line 135 counts three corpus documents naming Mesmer, outside `working/journeys`, not consulted here.

## 10. Standing gaps

**The English years, c. 1785-1789**: no address, no patient, no date. **The abandonment of the priesthood, c. 1753-1759**: no reason in any reachable source. **Deslon's clinic address**: placeholder pin. **Buzancy coordinate**: from general geography, not the pool. **The eyewitness letter** behind two quotations: author and first publication unknown. **Hotel de Coigny**: years and coordinate unsourced, no stop written. **Peter Lenk's *Magische Saeule***: no date of erection found. **Meersburg death house**: two coordinates 170 m apart, unresolved. **No archival material consulted** in Konstanz, Meersburg, Vienna or Paris holdings.
