# Fidel Castro (1926-2016): research report

*Compiled 2026-08-06 for the Atlas of Journeys. Slug `fidel_castro`. Register of the journey file: "national mythology: the canon is true". The campa speaks in the voice of the canon; this report is where the canon is audited.*

**Legend.** **[A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or single-witness claim carried by the literature without a document behind it. Where two reachable sources disagree, the disagreement is printed and left standing. "Pool" means the verified research pool handed to this stage; its corrected coordinates are given with the reason.

---

## 1. Birán and the Jesuits (1926-1945)

- **Birth [A].** 13 August 1926, Finca Manacas, Birán, Oriente (today Holguín), on the sugar estate of Ángel Castro y Argiz, a Galician who came as a Spanish conscript and stayed to buy land; his mother Lina Ruz González was the household cook, and the child is born out of wedlock. Wikipedia, "Fidel Castro"; "Birán". Pin **20.53 / -75.8956**, the estate not the village centroid.
- **Baptism [A, corrected].** Pool: 1934. Wikipedia has him baptized "aged eight", which puts it in late 1934 at the earliest and conventionally in January 1935. **Moved to 1935**, folded into the Dolores stop.
- **Schooling [A, pool range wrong].** Pool: "Colegio de Dolores, 1935-1941", wrong at both ends and skipping a school. Wikipedia has him boarded c.1932 with his primary teacher in Santiago, baptized at eight, sent to **La Salle**, and moved to the Jesuit **Colegio de Dolores** only afterward, leaving for Belén in 1942. Defensible: La Salle c.1935-1938, Dolores c.1938-1942; the stop is dated 1938 and says so in `date_confidence`. The teacher's house is **[R]** (Volkogonov, Quirk, Wikipedia) and **no primary document names it**, so no pin was created.
- **Colegio de Belén, Marianao [A].** 1942-1945, the Jesuit school of the Havana rich, campus later the Instituto Técnico Militar. Pin **23.0722 / -82.4383**, approximate. The 1945 yearbook line about "brilliant pages" is **[R]**: quoted everywhere, reproduced nowhere reachable.
- **University of Havana [A, pin corrected].** Law from 1945, Doctor of Law September 1950. Pool: 23.1367 / -82.3666, about 1.6 km too far east and in Centro Habana. Wikipedia: 23°8'3.49"N 82°22'55.32"W = **23.1343 / -82.3820**, the hill in Vedado.

## 2. The student with a pistol (1945-1952)

- **Cayo Confites [A as event; pin and ending corrected].** Pool pin 22.567 / -77.883 sits roughly 45 km out in open water; the cay lies in the Sabana-Camagüey archipelago off northern Camagüey, approximately **22.17 / -77.65**. *Unresolved:* Wikipedia, "Fidel Castro" says the force "intended to sail from Cuba in July 1947" and was stopped by Grau under United States pressure, while Wikipedia's Caribbean Legion coverage dates the arrest of the roughly 1,200-man force to **September 1947**. The pool's "the Cuban navy intercepts and aborts the venture before it sails" overstates that and has been softened; date 1947-07-01, spread in `date_confidence`. The swim across the bay of Nipe is **[R]**, his own later account.
- **Bogotá [A].** April 1948 with a Cuban student group; Gaitán shot 9 April, the Bogotazo follows. Pin **4.5981 / -74.0758**. **The pool's chronology lens dated this 1947 and welded Cayo Confites onto it: a two-year conflation, now split.**
- **Marriage [A / R].** Mirta Díaz-Balart, 1948 [A]; son Fidel Ángel September 1949 [A]. The three-month New York honeymoon of October 1948 rests on Quirk and is **[R]** as to address, which nothing reachable names, so it is folded into the Havana stop rather than pinned falsely.
- **Gang broadcast 13 November 1949 [A]; doctorate September 1950 [A].** Folded into the 1952 stop; printed here so nothing is lost.
- **Chibás [A, month not in the pool]** shot himself at the microphone in 1951, year only. Conventionally 5 August for the shot and 16 August for the death, **not carried, because the pool does not attest it**.
- **The coup [A].** 10 March 1952, cancelling the elections in which Castro was to stand for congress. His constitutional brief before the Court of Appeals is **[R]**; the pool attests only that he "begins organizing armed resistance."

## 3. Moncada and the sentence (1953-1955)

- **The assault, 26 July 1953 [A, headcount contradiction].** The pool's own lenses disagree: chronology "roughly 165 revolutionaries", geography (citing Wikipedia, "Moncada Barracks") "roughly 135". **Not adjudicated; the campa prints both and says the sources disagree.** Six rebels die fighting, 22 are executed afterward without trial [A]. Pin **20.0264 / -75.8192**. Pre-attack speech quote [A], Marxists Internet Archive. Trial opens at the Palace of Justice 21 September, fifteen-year sentence 16 October [A], merged into one stop.
- **"History will absolve me" [A, upgraded from [R]].** The pool tagged the closing line [R] because the Marxists Internet Archive page was served truncated: a retrieval artefact, not evidence against the line. Two independent carriers traced: Speakola's transcript of the speech from the dock, with this exact punctuation, and Wikiquote citing the *Fidel Castro Reader* (Ocean Press) p. 105, "Condemn me, it does not matter: history will absolve me." Spanish: "Condenadme, no importa, la historia me absolverá." **Tag raised to [A];** the quote source records that punctuation varies by translator.
- **A real gap.** No stenographic transcript of the address survives. The pamphlet *La historia me absolverá*, secretly printed by El Curita at Plaza del Vapor, Havana, and circulated in 1954 [A], is a reconstruction from memory, **and historians dispute its fidelity to the two-hour speech**.
- **A misattribution caught [A, corrected].** "He is not dead, because to die for one's country is to live forever" appears verbatim in the speech but **is not Castro's own sentence**: he is reporting Haydée Santamaría's answer to her jailers on being told her fiancé Boris Luis Santa Coloma was killed, in a line that echoes the national anthem. **Excluded from the quote fields.**
- **Presidio Modelo [A].** Isla de Pinos, October 1953 to May 1955: the Abel Santamaría Academy classes, the renaming of the conspiracy as the 26th of July Movement. Pin **21.8778 / -82.7664**. Amnesty 15 May 1955 [A]. The dockside line about not staying in a country where he cannot speak or write is **[R]**.

## 4. Mexico and the Granma (1955-1956)

- **Emparán 49 [A, interlock].** July 1955: Raúl brings Fidel to María Antonia González's apartment, and Guevara signs on as the expedition's physician by dawn, third confirmed member after the brothers (`che_guevara.journey.json`). **Canonical pin inherited byte-identical: 19.4326 / -99.1332.** Bayo's drills on a ranch outside Chalco are [A] as event but carry **no coordinate** in the interlock and none verified here, so no pin was invented.
- **Tuxpan, 25 November 1956 [A, one claim corrected].** Departure at 2 a.m. from the river mouth, pin **20.955 / -97.4033** (Wikipedia, "Granma (yacht)"). The pool said the yacht was "meant to hold seven"; the same article says the 60-foot vessel was "modified postwar to accommodate 12 people" and carried 82. **Corrected to twelve.**
- **Playa Las Coloradas, 2 December 1956 [A, pin corrected].** Pool gave two wrong pins, 19.8833 / -77.4667 (about 20 km inland) and 19.8394 / -77.585. The beach is on the west-facing Niquero coast inside Desembarco del Granma National Park, park coordinates 19°53'N 77°38'W. **Carried as 19.92 / -77.67, approximate.** *Unresolved:* the pool says "near Los Cayuelos", Wikipedia names Playa Las Coloradas; Los Cayuelos has no source and is not carried.
- **Survivor figures [A / contradiction].** The pool attached "only 19 reach the Sierra Maestra" to the *grounding*; the attrition happened three days later at Alegría de Pío. **Moved.** The count is contested: "Granma (yacht)" says roughly 12, "Fidel Castro" says 19, "Battle of Alegría de Pío" says Castro could initially rally only eight, most sources 20-22. **The campa prints twelve and twenty-two as outer bounds and refuses to pick.**
- **Alegría de Pío, 5 December 1956 [A, pin corrected].** Pool: 19.9931 / -77.4864, about 14 km too far north. Wikipedia: 19°52'31"N 77°31'26"W = **19.8753 / -77.5239**. Ambush at roughly 4 p.m. confirmed. **Cinco Palmas** (regrouping at Ramón "Mongo" Pérez's farm, "now we have won the war" on being told seven rifles were saved) is [A] via `che_guevara.journey.json`, folded into the same campa; no coordinate anywhere reachable.

## 5. The Sierra Maestra (1957-1958)

- **La Plata, 17 January 1957 [A, pin approximate].** First rebel victory, a coastal-guard post at the river mouth. Pin **19.9958 / -76.9219**; the garrison site is not independently pinpointed.
- **Herbert Matthews [A, month not in the pool].** The New York Times interview destroyed Batista's claim that Castro was dead. **The pool gives 1957 only.** Conventionally 17 February; the file uses 1957-02-01 and calls the month conventional in `date_confidence`. Marching the same men past the reporter twice is **[R]**.
- **El Uvero, 28 May 1957 [A, pin corrected].** Pool: 19.9264 / -77.0189, roughly 45 km too far west. Wikipedia, "Uvero, Cuba": 19°56'57.3"N 76°35'24.2"W = **19.9493 / -76.5901**.
- **Pico Turquino [A, upgraded from [R]].** The pool tagged this [R] and claimed "no specific dated event on the summit itself is documented for Fidel personally". **Its own source refutes that:** Wikipedia states that "Fidel Castro and his soldiers summited the peak in 1957 during their insurgency", quotes Guevara on its "almost mystical significance", and records the José Martí bust placed there in 1953. Coordinates 19.9894 / -76.8358 are exact. **Tag raised to [A].** The month is not attested, so the summit rides in the Comandancia campa rather than taking an invented date.
- **La Comandancia de la Plata [R].** Headquarters, hospital, press and the Radio Rebelde transmitter, carried by Cuban National Monument records and general histories rather than a contemporaneous document; **tag left at [R]**. Pin **19.9667 / -76.9**, approximate. Guevara's El Hombrito, the first liberated territory (`che_guevara.journey.json`), is named in the same campa.
- **General strike, 9 April 1958 [A]**, which failed: no separate stop. **Column 8, 21 August 1958 [A, interlock]:** Castro's written order creates the column and names Guevara its chief.

## 6. The caravan (December 1958 to January 1959)

- **Batista's flight and Santa Clara [A, interlock].** 31 December 1958, reportedly with more than $300 million: reported, not audited, and the campa says "by one account". Guevara's armored-train battle takes the city, and that night Castro's order sends Cienfuegos and Guevara down the highway (`che_guevara.journey.json`). Pin 22.4069 / -79.9644, the standard city coordinate; the interlock supplies none.
- **Santiago de Cuba: a three-way date contradiction, printed not resolved.** The chronology lens dates the entry and the independence speech to **2 January 1959**, then corrects itself to **1 January**, noting that Wikipedia puts the Santiago entry with the 1 January collapse and the Havana columns on 2 January. The quotes lens dates the Céspedes Park speech to **3 January 1959**, citing the Marxists Internet Archive. **These cannot all be true and nothing reachable adjudicates.** The stop is dated 1959-01-01, the quote keeps its own 3 January source line, and the spread is stated in `date_confidence`.
- **La Cabaña, 3 January 1959 [A, interlock].** Guevara takes the fortress and presides over the revolutionary tribunals. **Canonical pin inherited byte-identical: 23.1497 / -82.3494.** The executions at the moat wall are attested in the general literature; **the number is disputed across the whole political spectrum and none is given here.**
- **Havana, 8 or 9 January [A, corrected].** The chronology lens said 9 January and contradicted itself: its own geography lens uses "8-9 January", Wikipedia, "Habana Libre" says "following his entry into Havana on January 8, 1959", and the Camp Columbia address is universally dated the night of **8 January 1959**. **Carried as 1959-01-08.** The white dove on his shoulder and the exchange "¿Voy bien, Camilo?" / "Vas bien, Fidel" are **[R]**: canon, partly on film, absent from the pool. This journey's register is that the canon is true; this report notes that the canon is what they are.
- **Habana Libre [A, pin corrected].** Pool: 23.1425 / -82.383, about 400 m north of the building. Wikipedia: 23°08'21"N 82°22'58"W = **23.1392 / -82.3828**, Calle L between 23 and 25, Vedado, with what the pool lacked: the **Continental Suite, room 2324**, occupied three months, the first press conference in the ballroom on **19 January 1959**, and the later Castellana Suite, room 2224, now a museum.

## 7. Revolution first, elections later (1959-1963)

- **Prime Minister 16 February 1959 [A]**; "revolution first, elections later" 9 April 1959 [A]. **July resignation, 18 July 1959 [A]**, blaming Urrutia for "fevered anti-Communism", resumed days later with Dorticós installed: no stop of its own, background to the Camagüey campa.
- **Washington, April 1959 [A].** Invited by American newspaper editors, not the government; Eisenhower declines, Nixon receives him. Capitol pin **38.8899 / -77.0091**; Nixon's verdict is **[R]** as to wording and is paraphrased. **Princeton, 20 April [A]** (archival accounts, not fetched) and **Central Park Zoo, April [R]** (press photographs only) fold into the same campa, provenance stated there.
- **First Agrarian Reform Law, May 1959 [A].** Cap of 993 acres, roughly 15 percent of national wealth redistributed within the year. That the Birán finca was among the estates taken is **[R]**. **Buenos Aires, May 1959 [A]**, inter-American economic conference, the plea for United States aid refused: **canonical corpus pin inherited byte-identical, -34.6037 / -58.3816**; the thirty-billion-over-ten-years figure is **[R]**.
- **Huber Matos, 19 October 1959 [A].** Camagüey, pin 21.3808 / -77.9169; the full twenty years served and Cienfuegos's aircraft vanishing nine days later are **[R]**, standard in the literature and absent from the pool.
- **National Bank, 26 November 1959 [A, interlock]**, the "good economist" / "good communist" exchange: **canonical pin inherited byte-identical, 23.139 / -82.352**; banknotes signed "Che" are **[R]**. **La Coubre, 4-5 March 1960 [A, interlock]**, about a hundred dead, "Patria o Muerte" coined over the coffins, Korda's frame of Guevara: **canonical pin inherited byte-identical, 23.144 / -82.342**.
- **Hotel Theresa and the UN, September 1960 [A].** The move to Harlem after a dispute over a cash deposit; Khrushchev, Nasser, Nehru and Malcolm X at the Theresa (pin 40.8086 / -73.9494, folded into the UN stop). The 26 September speech ran over four hours, the longest ever given there. The chicken-plucking story is **[R]**, tabloid in origin.
- **Playa Girón, 17-20 April 1961 [A, pin and quote corrected].** Pool: 22.0919 / -81.1258, roughly 10 km off and in open water; Wikipedia: 22°04'N 81°02'W = **22.067 / -81.033**. The pool also silently altered the 23 April speech, changing "in less than 72 hours we have destroyed the invasion..." to "We destroyed the invasion..." and dropping the lead-in carrying the 72-hour claim. **The verbatim fragment is restored.**
- **Missile Crisis, October 1962 [A].** Castro's letter urging a first strike in the event of invasion is real and published but absent from this pool, so it is carried with the hedge that he spent decades explaining it. **Moscow, 1963 [A, range corrected]**: pool "1963-04 to 1963-05", actual **27 April to 3 June 1963**, thirty-eight days; Kremlin pin 55.752 / 37.6175, with May Day on the mausoleum and Hero of the Soviet Union **[R]**.

## 8. The long reign and the ashes (1965-2016)

- **Havana airport, 14 March 1965 [A, interlock].** Castro meets Guevara returning from Algiers; forty hours of conversation; Guevara never again seen in public in Cuba. The farewell letter, written 1 April and read aloud 3 October 1965, folds into the same campa.
- **Chile, November-December 1971 [A, duration corrected].** Pool: "23-day". Spanish Wikipedia, "Visita de Fidel Castro a Chile": **10 November to 4 December 1971**, twenty-four days (some accounts twenty-five), arrival at Pudahuel, received by Allende, itinerary through Chuquicamata, Antofagasta, Iquique, Valparaíso, Puerto Montt, Punta Arenas, Tierra del Fuego. **The Concepción address is confirmed but its date, 18 November, is not attested and is not carried; the destroyer *Riveros* detail is unsourced and dropped.** La Moneda pin -33.4426 / -70.6547.
- **Angola, 1975 [A].** Luanda pin -8.8383 / 13.2344. The intervention is [A]; the name Operation Carlota, the fifty-thousand figure, and the Ogaden and Cuito Cuanavale extensions are **[R]** at that precision.
- **Non-Aligned Movement, 1979 [R for the venue].** Sixth summit in Havana, Castro chairing 1979-1983; Palacio de las Convenciones pin 23.1075 / -82.4306, tagged [R] in the pool and left there. The October 1979 UN address as chairman is [A], folded in. **Moscow, November 1982 [A]**, Brezhnev's funeral, and **the Special Period, 1991 [A]**, are recorded here and folded into later campa rather than given stops.
- **CIMEQ, 31 July 2006 [A, location approximate].** Emergency intestinal surgery, provisional transfer to Raúl. **Official announcements did not name the facility**; the Siboney pin 23.1 / -82.47 is a stated placeholder. Presidency resigned 24 February 2008 [A]; First Secretary 19 April 2011 [A]. **Seventh Party Congress, 19 April 2016 [A]**, quote carried verbatim; the Obama visit of March 2016 and Castro's newspaper reply are **[R]** in this pool.
- **Death, 25 November 2016, 22:29 [A].** Cremation 26 November, private [A]. Lying in state at the José Martí Memorial 28-29 November and the rally of the 29th [A]; the pool's plaza coordinate 23.1177 / -82.3833 is about 700 m out and is **corrected to 23.1228 / -82.3867**. The cortege of 29 November to 3 December, roughly 900 km retracing the 1959 caravan in reverse, and the vehicle that broke down and was pushed by soldiers, are both [A]. All folded into the death campa.
- **Santa Ifigenia, 4 December 2016, 07:00 [A, pin corrected].** The afterlife lens gave 20.0245 / -75.8408, the geography lens the correct **20.0356 / -75.8403**; aligned. Press barred. The tomb is a rough boulder bearing the single word FIDEL, no dates, no surname [A]; Raúl's declaration that no statues, busts, streets or institutions will bear his brother's name is binding by National Assembly resolution [A].

## 10. Honest gaps

No pin was created for the Santiago boarding house of c.1932, for Bayo's ranch at Chalco, or for Cinco Palmas: no primary document, no coordinate in the interlock, none verified. Unresolved and left unresolved: the Granma survivor count (twelve, nineteen, twenty-two, or eight rallied initially, depending on the article); the Moncada headcount (135 or 165); the date of the Santiago speech (1, 2, or 3 January 1959); the fidelity of the 1954 pamphlet to a courtroom address of which no stenographic transcript exists; and the execution figures at La Cabaña and after, contested across the political spectrum and given no number here.

**The 638 assassination attempts** come from former Cuban intelligence chief Fabián Escalante, popularized by Dollan Cannell's Channel 4 documentary *638 Ways to Kill Castro* (28 November 2006) with its exploding cigars, poisoned fountain pen and poisoned wetsuit. **[R]**: a tally by an interested party, unverified against declassified United States records. **Not carried into the journey file at all**, because no stop could honestly anchor it.
