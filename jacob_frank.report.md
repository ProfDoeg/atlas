# Jacob Frank (1726-1791): research report
*Slug `jacob_frank`. Yaakov Leibowicz of Podolia; baptized Józef Frank at Lwów, 17 September 1759; styled Baron of Offenbach. Journey file: `jacob_frank.journey.json`, 8 segments, 44 stops, 11 quotes.*

**[A]** attested, source named in line · **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or a claim reaching this file only through secondary synthesis. Contradictions are flagged, not resolved; unreachable sources are named with the reason. Coordinate corrections are collected in section 9; every other correction to the pool is stated in place.

Frank left no archive. What survives is the sayings his scribes took down, translated by Lenowitz; the Frankist Chronicle, an internal sectarian document; Catholic and rabbinic polemic written to destroy him; and thin administrative paper reaching this file mostly through Maciejko and Doktór. There is no neutral witness anywhere in the record.

---

## 1. Birth and flight, 1726 to c. 1740

**The birthplace is contested and is not resolved.** Three traditions, three places, all three carried as separate stops, because the disagreement is itself a fact about the subject.

- **Korolówka (Korolivka), Podolia** **[A** as JHI's position, "Born in 1726 in Korolivka, Podolia"**]**. Primary birth pin.
- **Buchach**, per Gaudenty Pikulski, *Złość żydowska* (Lwów, 1760), hostile testimony from Frank's lifetime **[R**, Pikulski not accessed**]**.
- **A village near Husiatyn**, first two years at Korolówka, per the Frankist Chronicle in Lenowitz's edition **[R**, not accessed; the village goes unnamed even in the summary**]**.
- **Flight to Bukovina** **[A]** en.wikipedia, "ca. 1730: Family moved to Czernowitz, Bukovina", after heresy accusations against Frank's father. **[R]** Doktór dates the flight itself c. 1727.
- **Schooling: no enrolment, roll, or attendance record exists [A as an absence].** The *prostak* self-presentation is a posture, not a school record **[R]**. **Gap:** nothing between c. 1730 and 1750 is documented to a place and a date; one inferred stop covers the period, pinned at Czernowitz.

## 2. The Ottoman years, c. 1750 to 1755

- **Smyrna** **[A** as a source statement**]** en.wikipedia, "1750-1752: Studied in Izmir with Rabbi Issohar", a name later accounts attach to Hayim Malakh's lineage. **Flagged as thin:** the chain behind it is tradition, not a contemporary outside record.
- **The name.** *Frenk*, Ottoman and Sephardi usage for a Western European outsider **[A]**. The cloth-merchant profile attached to it is **[R]** and is not carried.
- **Marriage at Nikopol, 1752** **[A**, en. and pl.wikipedia agree on year and place**]**. **Contradiction flagged, not resolved:** pl.wikipedia makes the bride a Sabbatean rabbi's daughter, the circulating version a merchant's, a different alliance. Witnesses **[A]**: "followers of Osman Baba".
- **Salonica, November 1753.** Travel with Mordecai ben Elias Margalit **[A**, en.wikipedia**]**. Entry into the Karakaş / Barukhian Dönmeh circle descended from Barukhiah Russo (d. 1720) **[R**, Frankist Chronicle via Maciejko and Doktór, uncorroborated**]**.
- **Rachel, later Eve, born at Nikopol 1754** **[R]**: standard reconstruction, no register located.

## 3. Podolia, the scandal, the ban, 1755 to 1756

- **Return, 1755** **[A]** en.wikipedia, "reappeared in Podolia, gathered a group of local adherents". A formal Dönmeh commission is **[R]**, Doktór's contested reconstruction; the file splits the tags inside one `date_confidence` field.
- **Lanckoruń, 1756.** Gathering, exposure, arrests **[A**, en.wikipedia, "Lanckoron/Zarichanka scandal"**]**. The recorded accusation is dancing around a half-naked woman crowned like a Torah scroll, for the Shekhinah. The expanded orgy narrative is **[R]**, on no neutral eyewitness, and the file says so without pretending the arrests did not happen.
- **Satanów tribunal, 1756** **[A]** en.wikipedia, "rabbinical court held in Satanow". The confessions survive; the conditions under which they were taken do not. Whether the court heard a description of systematic practice or produced one under interrogation is **not answerable**. Stated as a gap.
- **Brody herem, June 1756** **[R]**: rabbinic responsa tradition per Scholem, not independently verified. Kept and tagged.

## 4. The Contra-Talmudists, 1757 to 1760

- **Kamieniec Podolski, 1757** **[A]** JHI, "Participated in public disputes with rabbis in Kamieniec Podolski (1757)". Bishop Mikołaj Dembowski ruled for the Contra-Talmudists and ordered the Talmud burned on 17 October.
- **Dembowski's death, 17 November 1757** **[A]** pl.wikipedia: at Czarnokozińce near Kamieniec, holding an appointment as Archbishop of Lwów he never took up. **[R]:** the reading of the death as divine retribution. **Gap:** no coordinate for Czarnokozińce was found, so the stop sits on the Kamieniec pin and says the death was not in the city.
- **Iwanie, 1757-1759** **[A]** en.wikipedia, "Frankism": "There were four company locations, in Ivanie, Częstochowa, Brno, and Offenbach am Main." Whether Frank declared himself successor to Zevi and Barukhiah or followers assigned him the role is disputed and left open.
- **Lwów disputation, 17 July to 19 September 1759** **[A]** de.wikipedia. The **blood accusation** advanced there is **[A]** as advanced and **[A]** as untrue; that Church officials required it as the price of protection is **[R]**.
- **Baptism at Lwów, 17 September 1759** **[A]** en.wikipedia. Godparents Anna Brühl and Franciszek Rzewuski. Scale of the wave **disputed and left open**: JHI and Doktór about 2,000, Maciejko 3,000 to 4,000.
- **Second baptism, Warsaw, 18 November 1759.** A contradiction the pool asked to publish as unresolved, which **resolves**: pl.wikipedia independently confirms 18 November at the Saxon Palace with Augustus III as godparent, matching Maciejko; en.wikipedia's "the next day" is the outlier.
- **Pin note.** The canonical atlas Warsaw coordinate **52.2297, 21.0122** is inherited here exactly as the interlock lens supplied it, though the Saxon Palace's own coordinate is 52.2411, 21.0114, about 200 m off.

## 5. Częstochowa, 1760 to 1773

- **Arrest, 6 or 7 February 1760. Contested by one day, given both ways in the file.** en.wikipedia: 6 February. de.wikipedia: 7 February, in the church of the Warsaw Bernardine monastery, then held with the Camaldolese and handed to the Inquisition on 13 February.
- **Jasna Góra** **[A]** Wikipedia: "Between 1760 and 1772 in the monastery was imprisoned Jacob Frank... who considered himself a messiah." The confinement was porous and the movement ran from inside it **[A in general terms; no visitor record reached]**.
- **Chana's death, c. 1771** **[R]**: no civil death record located. de.wikipedia dates it two years before the release, with burial in the caves near Olsztyn.
- **Release, 21 January 1773** **[A]** de.wikipedia, Russian forces holding Częstochowa freeing Frank and Eva; pl.wikipedia agrees on the year. **Bibikov is weakly attached:** his own article mentions neither Częstochowa nor Frank, and the file names him only as an en.wikipedia attribution.
- **The espionage rumour** **[R]**, unresolved. No archive, ledger, or commission has ever been identified.

## 6. Brno, 1773 to 1786

- **Warsaw stopover and the journey out, early 1773** **[R]**: Frankist Chronicle via Maciejko and Doktór; the party of roughly eighteen disguised as a merchant's servants is not independently confirmed.
- **The Dobruška household** **[A]** en.wikipedia, "Moses Dobruschka": born Brno 12 July 1753, converted 1775, ennobled at Vienna as Franz Thomas Edler von Schönfeld 25 July 1778, later Junius Frey in Paris, guillotined 5 April 1794. Schöndl Dobruška née Hirschel was Frank's first cousin and Moses's mother, making Moses a first cousin once removed, not the mother's cousin the pool made him; the Tuileries detail the pool attached to him is unsourced and dropped.
- **The court.** Uhlan guards, disciplined attendants, pilgrims, remittances from Poland and Moravia **[A in structure, across secondary sources and Habsburg police material; no accounts extant]**.
- **Goliński's denunciation, 1776** **[A** per pl.wikipedia's entry on *Daas*, describing a 1776 Austrian inquiry opened after the former follower's denunciation**]**.
- **Vienna** **[A]** en.wikipedia, "Visited Vienna multiple times; gained favor of Maria Theresa and Joseph II". **[R]** and not carried: stories of unusual imperial intimacy, including a claimed joint visit by Joseph II and the future Paul I.
- **The Asiatic Brethren**, the only documented Frankism-to-Freemasonry channel. **[A]** en.wikipedia: "developed in 1782 by Hans Heinrich von Ecker und Eckhoffen", active 1783-1790, blending Rosicrucianism with Freemasonry, Frankism, Martinism, alchemy and Kabbalah, and "one of the first such order, where Jews could become members". **Ephraim Joseph Hirschfeld is chronologically impossible as a contact of Frank's**, being in Offenbach "from 1792 until his death" against Frank's death in December 1791. **He gets no stop.**
- **Departure, 1786** **[A]** en.wikipedia, "Left Brno". Causes (the Goliński complaint, the armed guard, foreign money, an unclassifiable sect) are **[R]**, dossier synthesis of Habsburg police records.

## 7. Offenbach and death, 1786 to 1791

- **Oberrad** **[A]** offenbach.de: "Frank and his entourage initially stayed in Oberrad while renovations were completed."
- **Isenburg Castle: a three-way date conflict, published as such.** de.wikipedia, citing the Frankist Chronicle, gives 3 March 1787 with fifty to eighty followers, not several hundred; pl.wikipedia March 1786; offenbach.de 1788. The file uses the most specific to order the segment and names all three. **[A] from offenbach.de:** town population about 5,000, and the three-million-gulden figure. **Lease versus purchase is open.** **[A]** Jewish Encyclopedia (1906): "Frank, assuming the title of Baron of Offenbach, kept his court".
- **Pollakengarten** **[A]** offenbach.de: "Older city maps officially show an area on Rosenhohe with the name Pollakengarten." The pin is approximate; no plot boundary was recoverable, and the file says so.
- **Death, 10 December 1791** **[A]** across en.wikipedia, de.wikipedia, JHI, offenbach.de, with a stroke as the cause given independently by de.wikipedia and offenbach.de. The only real discrepancy is the hour, JHI's 4 a.m. against de.wikipedia's 4:30. Both are given.
- **Funeral, 12 December 1791** **[A]** de.wikipedia: bells, roughly 800 Frankists in procession, bodyguards with rifles, officers with bare sabres, no priest and no Christian rite, reported cost about 10,000 guilders. offenbach.de describes the same funeral and adds the ladies in white.

## 8. Afterlife

- **Pilgrimage and green markers** **[A]** de.wikipedia: followers marked the graves with concealed green paint, the Sabbatean colour of reverence, from the burial to the 1866 clearance.
- **Eve Frank, 7 September 1816** **[A]** de.wikipedia, preceded by a March 1816 Hofgericht attachment of her estate at a Mainz creditor's instance, documenting the debts. **[R] and unverified:** Joseph's death in 1807 and Roch's in 1813 without heirs. **[R]** the rumour that Eve was got out of the city rather than dying, recorded independently by de.wikipedia and the 1906 Jewish Encyclopedia ("either was spirited away or died suddenly"): two sources record its existence, neither treats it as history.
- **1866, the cemetery cleared and the skull taken** **[A]** de.wikipedia, via Kraushar's bibliography citing Emil Pirazzi, *Bilder aus Offenbach's Vergangenheit* (1879): graves opened, Pirazzi kept Frank's skull on a shelf, Kraushar photographed it in 1894. **Gap:** the chain of custody after Pirazzi is established nowhere checked, and no modern identification exists.
- **2009-2011, the vaults under Wilhelmsplatz** **[A]** de.wikipedia: three masonry-vaulted burial chambers about 1.2 m down, remains relocated to the current municipal cemetery, no monument today. **[A as an absence]** offenbach.de narrates the funeral in detail and says nothing about the grave's present condition.
- **The manuscripts** **[A]** en.wikipedia, "Frankism": the last known **complete** manuscript, sayings 1 to 2192, was destroyed in the destruction of Warsaw, which is compatible with the Jagiellonian (6968, 6969/1-3) and Lublin (2118) partials the Doktór and Lenowitz editions rest on.
- **Editions and reception.** Doktór edited the critical Polish *Księga słów Pańskich* and the standard biography, 1991/2023 **[A**, JHI**]**; Lenowitz the first complete English translation, 2004, 482 pp. **[A**, Stanford SearchWorks**]**; **Tokarczuk, *Księgi Jakubowe*** **[A]**, Polish 2014, 912 pp., English trans. Croft, Fitzcarraldo 15 November 2021 and Riverhead 1 February 2022, Nobel 2018. **Daas (2011)**, Adrian Panek: **[R], weak sourcing**, culture.pl returned HTTP 403.

**The quotations.** Eleven of 44 stops carry a quote, all from Lenowitz on Jacket2, read directly: sayings 1, 44, 451, 504, 793, 804, 868. Three pool errors were fixed. **Saying 44** is genuine, but the printed text carries a dash rather than the pool's comma and glosses the bitch as canine in brackets; the `quote_source` notes the omission. **Saying 804** was misreported as ending in refusal, where the refusal is the opening move and the visitors go on to receive his blessing; only the confirmed portion is quoted, since the lead-in "I answered them," could not be confirmed. **Saying 2**, the dream at Dziurdziów: the continuation the pool called unlocatable is printed in full on both cited pages, and the pool's quote items 4 and 13 both draw on saying 2, so they are not independent. **Dziurdziów could not be located to a coordinate**, so no stop is staged there.

**The famous line is deliberately not used.** "I have come to abolish all laws and religions in order to bring life to the world" is **[R]**: **confirmed absent** from the Lenowitz translation on Jacket2. The Frumious Consortium blog carries it verbatim and attributes it not to the sayings but to **Czesław Miłosz, *The History of Polish Literature*, pp. 164-66**, quoted second-hand, and **the Miłosz locus is unverified**. It appears in no `quote` field.

## 10. Apparatus relations for the operator

Frank is **not** listed in `EXCEPTIONS.md`. Relations found in the pool that are not rooted in his own life are recorded here rather than silently deleted. **All run FORWARD, which the exception never licenses**, so none was written into the journey file, and **the interlock list returned by this pass is empty**. There is no *backward* apparatus here that a listing would unlock, so on this evidence Frank does not need adding. The operator decides.

1. **Cagliostro and the Comte de Saint-Germain** (`cagliostro`, `comte_de_saint_germain`). Full-text searches of both English Wikipedia biographies returned **zero** hits for Frank, Frankism, Sabbateanism, or Dobruška, and Cagliostro's dated 1776-1791 itinerary never intersects Frank. The pool offers shared Warsaw a generation apart, deaths nine months apart in 1791, and a shared milieu: geography and period, ruled out as grounds for naming. The claimed axis lives in later conspiracy literature, which did not exist when Frank's clock stopped. **Neither is named in any campa.**
2. **Mozart, Mesmer, Goethe** (`wolfgang_amadeus_mozart`, `mesmer`, `goethe_full`). Mozart dying five days before Frank in Vienna; Mesmer in Vienna before 1778; Goethe's Frankfurt 6 km from Offenbach. Pure co-location. **Not named.**
3. **Buber and Scholem.** `martin_buber.journey.json` has Buber teaching at the Freies Jüdisches Lehrhaus alongside Scholem, 1923-1933. Scholem's redemption-through-sin work is a genuine one-directional engagement with Frank, cited as a **source** on the Brody stop. Buber has none that the pool found and **is not named in any campa**.
4. **Later engagers with no atlas file:** Kraushar, Doktór, Lenowitz, Tokarczuk, Panek, Pirazzi, all carried as `sources` and `suggested_refs` on Frank's own stops, none staged as a stop. Kraushar's 1894 Offenbach trip is **Kraushar's**, named inside Frank's grave campa only because the object photographed is Frank's skull.

**Queue note.** No atlas file exists for Sabbatai Zevi, Nathan of Gaza, Barukhiah Russo, Jacob Emden, Scholem as a subject, or Moses Dobruška; Zevi is in QUEUE.md, so Frank's file inherits no pins from the Sabbatean chain. **Dobruška is the strongest new candidate**, the only documented bridge from this household into the Asiatic Brethren, the Illuminati, and Jacobin Paris.

**Stops deliberately excluded.** **Kalisz** and **Rzeszów**, unverified pre-birth family geography **[R]**. **Kamieniec Podolski as a mass-baptism site**, **[R]**, unconfirmable as distinct from Lwów and Warsaw. **Vienna Stephansplatz**, an arbitrary pin. **Dziurdziów**, where Frank was but for which no coordinate could be obtained: named in prose, no pin. **Hirschfeld at Offenbach (1792, 1820)**, postdating Frank.
