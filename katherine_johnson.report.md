# Katherine Johnson: research report

*Creola Katherine Coleman Goble Johnson, 26 August 1918 (White Sulphur Springs, West Virginia) to 24 February 2020 (Newport News, Virginia). NACA/NASA Langley, 1953-1986.*

Legend: **[A]** = attested, source named. **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition, single-source genealogy, or a claim this pass could not independently confirm.

Journey file: `katherine_johnson.journey.json`. 8 segments, 44 stops, register `national mythology: the canon is true`, calendar `gregorian`.

## 1. Family background (pre-1918)

Everything before Katherine's own birth rests on **one source**, MacTutor's genealogical reconstruction, which presents itself as reconstruction.

- Mother **Joylette Roberta Lowe**, born 1887, **Caswell County, North Carolina**. **[R]**. No birth record located; pin is a county centroid.
- **Joshua McKinley Coleman marries Joylette Roberta Lowe**, **Danville, Virginia, 29 September 1909**. **[R]**. No marriage record located.
- Katherine is the **youngest of four**; Joshua Coleman worked seasonally at **the Greenbrier hotel**. **[A]**.

**Contradiction flagged, not resolved.** The pool's lenses disagreed on status: chronology tagged the Danville marriage and the Caswell birth **[R]**, geography tagged both **[A]**. No independent document stands behind either, so both are carried as **[R]**. Coordinates checked and acceptable (Danville 36.586/-79.395, within ~1 km of city center; Caswell 36.3854/-79.3453, within ~2 km of centroid).

## 2. Childhood and schooling (1918-1937)

- Born **26 August 1918, White Sulphur Springs, Greenbrier County**, as **Creola Katherine Coleman**. **[A]**.
- **Greenbrier County provided no public secondary schooling for Black children**, so the family wintered at **Institute**, on the West Virginia State College campus, about 120 miles away. **[A]**.
- Entered the **WVSC laboratory high school around age 10 (c. 1928)**; **graduated at 14 (1932)**. **[A]**.
- Entered **West Virginia State College at 14 (1932)**, mathematics and French. Mentored by **Angie Turner King** and **W. W. Schieffelin Claytor**, who added courses for her, including analytic geometry of space. **[A]**.
- **Graduated summa cum laude, B.S. mathematics and French, 1937, age 18.** **[A]**.

**Gap.** No reachable source gives the enrollment month for either school, or the 1937 commencement date. All three stops carry ordering-only day values with the hedge in `date_confidence`.

## 3. Teaching, marriage, the WVU test case (1937-1940)

- **First teaching post, segregated Black public school, Marion, Virginia**: mathematics, French, music. **[A]** as to the post.
- **Married James Francis Goble, 1939.** **[A]** as to year. **Place given only as "West Virginia"**, no town, so the marriage gets **no stop of its own** and is carried inside the 1939 Morgantown stop.
- **Selected by WVSC president John W. Davis** as one of three Black students, the only woman, to integrate graduate study at **WVU** after *Missouri ex rel. Gaines v. Canada*; **first Black woman graduate student there**, 1939. **Withdrew 1940 on becoming pregnant, no degree.** **[A]**.

**Contradiction flagged, not resolved.** MacTutor's narrative places the Marion post from **1937**; MacTutor's own career timeline gives **1939-1943**, which is incompatible with the 1939 admission and 1940 withdrawal. Neither the pool nor this pass can adjudicate. The stop is dated 1937-09-01 and states the conflict rather than picking a winner.

## 4. Langley: the West Area computers (1953-1959)

- **Family relocated to Newport News, 1953**; **hired by NACA at Langley, June 1953**, into the segregated **West Area Computing** unit under **Dorothy Vaughan**. **[A]**.
- **Within about two weeks, loaned to the Maneuver Loads Branch, Flight Research Division**, where the engineers kept her. About four years on gust loads, wake turbulence, and a crash investigation attributed to wake effects. **[A]**.
- **James Francis Goble died of an inoperable brain tumor, December 1956.** **[A]** as to fact and month. **Place given only as "Virginia"**; the stop is pinned at the Newport News family home and says so.
- **Sputnik 1, 4 October 1957.** Langley pivots to spaceflight; Johnson contributes mathematics to the roughly 600-page **Notes on Space Technology**. **[A]**. **No date attaches to her contribution**; the stop is dated to the launch that occasioned it.
- **NACA dissolved into NASA, 1 October 1958.** **[A]**.
- **Married James A. "Jim" Johnson, 1959**, met through church and family networks. She sang in the choir at **Carver Memorial Presbyterian**, a member about fifty years. **[A]**.

**Coordinate correction.** The geography lens pinned Carver Memorial at **37.0299, -76.3452**, which falls in **Hampton**, about 9 km away. Carver Memorial is a **Newport News** congregation (Marshall Avenue corridor, ZIP 23607, near 36.99, -76.42). The file uses **36.99, -76.42** and states that Nominatim returns no record for the church itself and the street address is unconfirmed. **[R]** as to exact address.

## 5. The flight years (1960-1972)

Report dates are NTRS catalogue dates. All **[A]**.

TN D-233 (Sept 1960, with Ted H. Skopinski); D-1366, Echo I (June 1962, Gertrude C. Westrick); D-2379, reentry flight-path angle (July 1964, Jack A. White); D-2890, solar-system geometry (1965, W. Thomas Blackshear); **D-4246**, single-fix midcourse guidance (**Dec 1967**, Blackshear and Harold A. Hamer); then six with Hamer: D-5963 (Oct 1970), D-6343 (July 1971), D-6461 (Sept 1971), D-6752 (May 1972), TP-1184 (July 1978), TP-1740 (Dec 1980). Full titles are in the journey file's `suggested_refs`.

**Contradiction resolved on catalogue evidence.** The geography lens dated **TN D-4246 to October 1967**; the chronology lens and the NTRS record both give **December 1967** (Blackshear, Hamer, Johnson). December is used and the disagreement is recorded.

Mission work:

- **TN D-233 is the first Flight Research Division report NASA credits to a woman.** **[A]**.
- **Freedom 7, 5 May 1961**: trajectory analysis, launch timing, reentry and splashdown prediction. **[A]**. That she alone "sent Shepard into space" **overstates a collaborative mission design**; the campa says so.
- **Friendship 7, 20 February 1962**: at Glenn's request she hand-verified the electronic computer's figures. **[A]**. NASA's 2015 wording: Glenn "requested that she personally recheck the calculations made by the new electronic computers before his flight."
- **Apollo 11, 1969**: navigation and rendezvous work, synchronizing the Lunar Module ascent with the Command and Service Module. **[A]**. She named this, not the Glenn verification, as the work she valued most. **[A]**.
- **Apollo 13, April 1970**: earlier backup navigation parameters and charts she contributed to were part of the resources used in the return. **[A]**. Her personally saving the mission is **[R]**, contradicted in her own words.
- **Lunar Orbiter and Apollo group achievement awards**: **[R]**, asserted without individual citation. Later work widened into **Landsat, Space Shuttle, and large flexible spacecraft control**: **[A]**.
- **Retired 1986 after 33 years.** **[A]** as to year; the **month (August)** is one lens only, **[R]**.

**Contradictions and gaps in the Glenn story, flagged not resolved.**

1. NASA's November 2015 wording is sober and single-sentence. The vivid "get the girl" phrasing circulates only in **post-2016** retellings. No source documenting how the film altered the wording was reachable (space.com and Smithsonian both 403), so the *exaggeration itself* is **[R]**. What is [A] is only that NASA's account is more restrained than the legend.
2. The line **"If she says they're good, then I'm ready to go"** is real text on NASA's biography page, but **it is not Glenn on the record**. NASA's framing: *"'If she says they're good,' Katherine Johnson remembers the astronaut saying, 'then I'm ready to go.'"* It is **Johnson's memory of Glenn**, and the file's `quote_source` says exactly that.
3. Robert Pearlman's Smithsonian-linked investigation is reported to have found **no contemporaneous 1962 document** fixing the ultimatum wording. Not re-verifiable this pass; noted as reported.

**Where the stops are pinned.** Johnson was at **Langley**, not the Cape, for both the Freedom 7 and Friendship 7 work, so the corpus's Cape Canaveral / LC-39B coordinate (28.6272, -80.6208, from `richard_feynman.journey.json`) is **deliberately unused**. Shepard and Glenn traveled to Florida; a stop belongs to whoever was there.

## 6. Apollo 11, 20 July 1969: where she actually was

- She was **at a meeting in the Pocono Mountains**, watching on **a small television** with a few others. **[A]** (Wikipedia: "During the Moon landing, Johnson was at a meeting in the Pocono Mountains. She and a few others crowded around a small television screen watching the first steps on the Moon.").
- The widely repeated addition that this was an **Alpha Kappa Alpha sorority gathering** is **not supported** by the sources checked; NASA's Langley article does not mention the Poconos at all. **The sorority detail is dropped**, and `date_confidence` says so.
- Coordinate **regional only** (41.109, -75.3624); no hall or resort identified, stated as a gap. The 2016 film places her in Mission Control that night. Not historical; the campa says so.

**Correction: Hidden Figures dates.** The pool paired "December 2016" with "United States (wide release)", conflating three events: the film **premiered 10 December 2016 at the SVA Theatre, New York City**, went to **limited release 25 December 2016**, and reached **wide release 6 January 2017**. **No premiere stop is given**: nothing attests Johnson's presence at the SVA Theatre. Both dates appear inside the Dolby campa as context.

**Coordinate corrections applied.** Five pins in the pool were wrong. **NASA IV&V, Fairmont**: 39.4718/-80.1434 (geography lens) and 39.4851/-80.1426 (afterlife lens), three different points for one building, both about 6 km off, corrected to **39.43, -80.19778**. **George Mason SciTech**: 38.7509/-77.4491, 6 km east of campus, corrected to **38.75819, -77.51995**. **SUNY Farmingdale**: 40.7248/-73.4454, 3.5 km southwest, corrected to **40.7529, -73.4266**. **WVSU**: 38.3921/-81.8412 (afterlife lens), 6.5 km off, corrected to **38.3825, -81.7656**, which the geography lens already had right. **Carver Memorial**: 37.0299/-76.3452, wrong city, corrected to **36.99, -76.42**.

**Death: contradiction flagged, not resolved.** NASA states she died **at home**; several obituaries place the death at a **retirement facility**. The sources do not reconcile. **No cause of death was made public.**

**Burial: an honest gap.** No cemetery, grave marker, interment record, or memorial service is named on **any** institutional page covering the death in detail: NASA's biography and news pages, the Foundation's biography page, Wikipedia. All three were checked. She **has no publicly known grave**, and the family appears to have kept it out of the record deliberately. Stated as a gap in the death stop.

**WVU papers: [R].** The donation of the Johnson Papers (A&M 4536) by daughters Joylette Hylick and Katherine Moore **could not be verified against the WVU finding aid** (HTTP 403). Date given only as "2021-2022". Carried as a stop because the objects are her own and the pin is the campus that admitted her under court order in 1939.

**Gold Medal ceremony: [R].** The **authorization is solid**: PL 116-68, 8 November 2019, covering Johnson, Dorothy Vaughan, Mary Jackson, Christine Darden, and collectively the NACA and NASA women computers. ## 8. Quote provenance audit

Sixteen of 44 stops carry a quote. Three carry a corrected or downgraded attribution.

1. **"I counted everything..."** Words **confirmed verbatim** as the opening line of NASA's "The Girl Who Loved to Count". The pool's provenance ("her remarks on receiving the Presidential Medal of Freedom, 2015") **is wrong**: NASA gives **no occasion**. Its only attribution is the following sentence, "So said Katherine Johnson, recipient of the 2015 National Medal of Freedom", which identifies who she is, not when she spoke. The file cites **the NASA page alone, undated**.
2. **"If she says they're good, then I'm ready to go."** See section 5. Reattributed from "Glenn's own words" to **"John Glenn, as Katherine Johnson remembered him saying"**, tag **[R]**.
3. **"I know how good you are in French, but you will also major in mathematics."** **[R]**. Johnson quoting **James Carmichael Evans**, embedded in her recollection; MacTutor names no underlying interview.

The remaining thirteen are **[A]** and verbatim: two from NASA's biography page, five from MacTutor, four from the AAPB oral history, two from the Foundation biography (thin provenance: it cites no transcript, though the words are as printed), one from NASA's 2016 naming article.

## 9. Interlocks with the existing corpus

**Named in a campa (1): `john_f_kennedy`**, once, in the Apollo lunar-rendezvous stop. The relation is real and one-directional: his public commitment set the deadline and the mission architecture Johnson's rendezvous timing served, and she executed it inside a laboratory reorganized around it. Influence, not co-location.

**Deliberately not named**, though the interlock lens surfaced them, because the only connection is shared geography, era, or thematic resemblance, and the map already shows co-location:

- `john_von_neumann`. The IBM 7090 she checked is a von Neumann-architecture machine, and the lens itself marked the connection "inferred, not stated". She never cited him; he died in 1957. A machine's design lineage is not a relation between two people.
- `richard_feynman`. Challenger, LC-39B, January 1986; she retired seven months later. Same calendar year, no relation. Her retirement campa names Challenger as an event, not Feynman.
- `emmy_noether`, a pattern rather than a link, flagged unverified by the lens itself; `robert_oppenheimer` and Los Alamos, an era rather than a relation; `harriet_tubman`, `john_brown`, `edward_witten`, West Virginia geography only.
- `bill_gates`, `bob_kahn`, `vint_cerf`, `friedrich_hayek`. Fellow Medal of Freedom recipients at the same White House pin. **The pin is inherited byte-identically (38.8977, -77.0365) and none of them is named.** Coordinate reuse is the right inheritance; naming a fellow medallist would be the co-location error exactly.

**Gap in corpus coverage.** No atlas file exists for **John Glenn, Alan Shepard, Neil Armstrong, Wernher von Braun, Dorothy Vaughan, or Mary Jackson**. This file introduces the corpus's first Mercury/Apollo and first "human computer" geography rather than inheriting one. Vaughan is named inside campas as her actual supervisor, which is biography, not an interlock.

## 10. Apparatus relations for the operator

**Katherine Johnson is not listed in `EXCEPTIONS.md`.** No apparatus stops were written and **none were silently deleted**. Below is what the interlock lens produced that is **not rooted in Johnson's own life**, kept for the operator.

**Direction caught: all backward.** Each would put a later framework, or a later person's engagement, onto Johnson's file, or put Johnson into someone else's geography. None is a forward reference out of her file into an earlier subject's, so nothing here breaks the block rule; it is simply not licensed, and Johnson has no theoretical apparatus of her own in any case.

1. **von Neumann and the 1962 IBM verification.** The lens proposed reading the hand-check as the meeting point of von Neumann's computing lineage with human calculation. That belongs on **von Neumann's own file** if anywhere, and he died in 1957, so from his side it is a **forward** reach and is not granted. Dropped both ways.
2. **Feynman's LC-39B stop reused as a "space program continuity" pin.** This would stage Johnson at Cape Canaveral, where she never was, on the strength of Feynman's 1986 presence. Refused; it belongs as a `suggested_ref` on **Feynman's own** Challenger stop.
3. **Shetterly's book and the 2016 film as scenes.** Other people's engagement with Johnson. Cited as context inside her own stops with **no stops of their own**; the SVA Theatre premiere has no stop because she is not attested as present. If Shetterly ever gets a file, the premiere is hers.
4. **Noether, Oppenheimer, Tubman, Brown, Witten pairings.** Kept in section 9 rather than deleted, per the anti-silent-deletion rule, but they are resemblance and geography, not apparatus.

**Cut for stop budget, not for evidence.** Both **[A]** and restorable: **Capitol College, South Laurel MD, honorary D.Sc., 2006** (corrected coordinate 39.0475, -76.8515; renamed Capitol Technology University in 2014; the pool's 39.0993/-76.8483 is about 6 km north), and **William & Mary, 2018** (37.2707, -76.7075). The file sits at 44 stops against a 45 ceiling.
