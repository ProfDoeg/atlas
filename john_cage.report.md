# John Cage (1912-1992): research report

*Compiled 2026-08-23 for `john_cage.journey.json`: 45 stops, 9 segments, gregorian, register "national mythology: the canon is true".*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, self-report, or a claim the pool could not corroborate against a fetched source. Contradictions are flagged and left standing, not adjudicated.

## 1. Los Angeles, 1912-1930

- **Born 5 September 1912, Good Samaritan Hospital**, only child of the inventor John Milton Cage Sr. and Lucretia "Crete" Harvey Cage. **[A: Wikipedia, "John Cage"]** Coordinate 34.0562, -118.2653 is the present site at 616 South Witmer Street; the 1912 footprint may differ, as the pool flags.
- **The strychnine episode, c. 1914. [R]** Biographical tradition, uncorroborated against the original newspaper report. Kept, with the age discrepancy (fifteen months or eighteen) left visible in the campa.
- **Hollywood Bowl prize speech proposing a national day of quiet, 1928.** Speech and prize **[A]**. Reading it as a prophecy of 4'33" is **[R]**, retrospective interpretation, which the campa states rather than endorses.
- **Valedictorian, Los Angeles High School, 1928. [A]** *Coordinate corrected, see the table below.*
- **Pomona College, 1928-1930**, enrolling for theology and writing, leaving over the uniformity of assigned study. **[A]** *Coordinate corrected, see the table.* Duchamp and Joyce reach him here per the geography lens's claim text. The library-shelf anecdote is carried as Cage's framing of why he left, not as a documented scene.

## Apparatus relations for the operator

Cage is **not** in `EXCEPTIONS.md`, so no apparatus stops are licensed. Nothing was silently deleted. What I caught, and its direction:

1. **Halberstadt, 2001-2640. Direction: forward.** The performance began nine years after Cage's death and he never went there. Kept as the file's final stop, on the precedent that `merce_cunningham.journey.json` carries four posthumous stops on Cunningham's own file. The distinction applied: a posthumous stop is permissible where it is the subject's own work still being performed, impermissible where it is another person's travel. The operator may disagree, in which case the stop is cut, not moved.
2. **The Batt litigation (2002, 2010) and the John Cage Trust at Bard. Direction: forward.** No stops: other parties, in places Cage never travelled to. Both are `suggested_refs` instead, on the 4'33" and Ramapo stops.
3. **No backward apparatus relations were found.** Cage has no framework in the `EXCEPTIONS.md` sense applied to material he never treated. The one temptation is 4'33" and the history of silence, and I did not take it.

## Interlocks used, and interlocks refused

Named in a campa, all with a direct Cage relation:

- **`merce_cunningham`**: Cornish 1938 (they meet), the 1944 solo concert (Cage plays), Black Mountain 1948 and 1953, the 1952 untitled event, the 1964 tour, Bank Street, the death. Fifty-four years, both directions.
- **`erik_satie`**: Black Mountain 1948, Cage staging *Le Piege de Meduse*; Pocket Theatre 1963, Cage organizing the first complete *Vexations*. One-directional, across sixty years.
- **`buckminster_fuller`**: Black Mountain 1948, Fuller cast as the Baron in a production Cage directed.
- **`james_joyce`**: *Roaratorio*, 1979, a mesostic text drawn through *Finnegans Wake* on Joyce's own name, recorded in the places the book names. One-directional, a substantive engagement with the other man's work.

Refused: no traveler was named for merely sharing a place or a period. Nobody from the Black Mountain rosters (Albers, de Kooning, Olson) is an interlock, only a fact of who was in the building.
