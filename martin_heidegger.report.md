# Martin Heidegger (1889-1976): research report

*Compiled 2026-08-24 for `martin_heidegger.journey.json`. 42 stops, 8 segments.*

**[A]** = attested, source named · **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition, or interpretive claim
not independently corroborated. Contradictions are flagged, **not** adjudicated.

---

## 1. Meßkirch and the church's scholarships (1889-1911)

- **Born 26 September 1889, Meßkirch, Baden** [A: SEP; Wikipedia]. First child of Friedrich
  Heidegger, master cooper and sexton of St Martin's, and Johanna; the family lived in the
  sexton's house beside the church. *Naming*: de.wikipedia uses **Mesmerhaus**, one dossier lens
  **Mesnerhaus**; not adjudicated, the file takes the local form.
- **1903-1906, Konstanz** [A: Wikipedia; de.wikipedia]. The Heinrich-Suso-Gymnasium on a church
  scholarship pointed at the priesthood, boarding at the **Konradihaus**.
- **1906-1909, Freiburg, Berthold-Gymnasium and archiepiscopal seminary** [A].
  **CONTRADICTION, unresolved**: the dossier's chronology dates the transfer to **1909**, its
  geography lens and de.wikipedia to **1906**, 1909 being the year he finished school and
  matriculated. The file splits the two and follows de.wikipedia; the chronology entry stands
  unreconciled. The Gymnasium's pre-1965 site was not verified, so **the pin is unresolved**.
- **September 1909, Jesuit novitiate at Tisis near Feldkirch** [A: Wikipedia]. Discharged within
  ~two weeks, reportedly for a heart condition; neither day is recorded.
- **1909-1911, Catholic theology at Freiburg; 1911, course abandoned after further illness** [A:
  Wikipedia]. Shift to philosophy, mathematics, natural science. *[R]*: the dossier's framing of
  this as gradual separation from confessional orthodoxy rather than a clean break is
  interpretive; the enrollment and the change of course are [A].

## 2. The Freiburg apprenticeship (1913-1919)

- **1913, doctorate under Arthur Schneider**, *Die Lehre vom Urteil im Psychologismus* [A: SEP;
  Wikipedia]. **1915, habilitation under Heinrich Rickert**, *Die Kategorien- und
  Bedeutungslehre des Duns Scotus* [A: SEP; Wikipedia]: the treatise is now assigned to **Thomas
  of Erfurt**, a reassignment of later scholarship and not his error.
- **23 June 1916, fails to obtain the Freiburg chair for Catholic philosophy** [A: Wikipedia].
- **21 March 1917, marries Elfride Petri** in the **Freiburg Münster**, ceremony by **Engelbert
  Krebs**; **Protestant ceremony 25 March 1917 at Wiesbaden** [A: de.wikipedia]. *Correction*:
  the chronology gave only "Germany (Freiburg area)" and misplaced the second ceremony there.
  Wiesbaden is **folded into the same campa** rather than pinned, the pool supplying no
  coordinate for it.
- **1918, meteorological unit near the western front [R].** Postal and weather duties from 1915;
  cardiac classification limited him to non-combat roles. **No named unit, no station, no
  corroborating primary source.** Coordinate 49.6, 5.4 is an Ardennes/Meuse placeholder,
  declared as such.
- **1919, Husserl's assistant at Freiburg** [A: SEP; Wikipedia].

## 3. The hut and Marburg (1922-1927)

- **1922, the "Natorp Report"**, *Phänomenologische Interpretationen zu Aristoteles*, written to
  support his candidacy and instrumental in the Marburg appointment [A: SEP].
- **9 August 1922, the Todtnauberg hut completed** by the carpenter **Pius Schweitzer**, arranged
  by Elfride [A: de.wikipedia]. **COORDINATE CONFLICT, unresolved**: two dossier values for one
  building and **no verifiable pin in any source checked**, neither de.wikipedia article carrying
  coordinates and OSM having no entry. See the coordinate section below.
- **1923, associate professorship at Marburg** [A: SEP; Wikipedia]; Rudolf Bultmann the colleague
  named in the dossier.
- **The Arendt affair. CONTRADICTION, unresolved.** Wikipedia gives **1925**, the dossier
  **1924**, and 1924 could not be confirmed from a primary source; the file dates the stop 1925
  and states the disagreement. The dossier is explicit that characterisations of coercion are
  interpretive and were never the subject of any proceeding; the campa states the dispute rather
  than settling it.
- **Sein und Zeit, 1927** [A: SEP; Wikipedia; en.wikipedia "Being and Time"]. **CONTRADICTION,
  unresolved**: SEP dates it **January 1927**, the dossier **February 1927**, *Jahrbuch* VIII
  carries only the year; the file defaults to 1927-01-01 and says so. The **Husserl dedication**
  is confirmed, as is its **removal in the 1941 printing and restoration in 1953**; Heidegger's
  1966 defence, that it stood until the fourth edition of 1935, is carried as a quote with
  provenance stated, not as the last word.

## 4. The chair, the nothing, Davos (1928-1929)

- **1928, succeeds Husserl in the Freiburg chair** [A: SEP; Wikipedia].
- **1929, inaugural lecture *Was ist Metaphysik?*** [A: Wikipedia]. **Gap**: neither month nor
  day is in the pool. The file defaults to 1929-01-01 and says so, which is the only reason it
  precedes Davos in segment order. Carnap's attack is carried in `suggested_refs`.
- **Davos, the disputation with Cassirer** [A: SEP; de.wikipedia "Davoser Disputation"]. The
  Hochschulkurse ran **17 March to 6 April 1929**, the disputation itself **26 March 1929, in the
  Konversationssaal of the Grand Hotel Belvédère**; the dossier's venue hedge is dropped, the
  venue being confirmed. **Coordinate: canonical pin inherited over the geocode.** The corpus
  holds a town-level Davos pin at **46.801, 9.836** (`reich.journey.json`); byte-identical
  inheritance was applied and the ~800 m gap to the Belvédère is recorded in `date_confidence`.
  **Operator call**: the venue geocode would give the corpus two Davos pins.

## 5. The rectorate (1931-1936)

Verified sequence, all [A: Wikipedia, SEP, de.wikipedia as noted]: the Schwarze Hefte begin
**1931**; elected rector **21 April 1933** after von Möllendorff resigns under pressure; joins
the NSDAP **1 May 1933**, member **3,125,894**, dues paid to the Party's 1945 dissolution with
**no documented resignation**; rectoral address *Die Selbstbehauptung der deutschen Universität*
**27 May 1933**; the Leipzig professors' declaration for Hitler and the League plebiscite
**11 November 1933**; resigns the rectorate **April 1934** while remaining a member;
*Einführung in die Metaphysik* **1935**; Rome and Karl Löwith **1936**.

- **Leipzig venue is wrong in the dossier.** The geography lens placed the rally at the
  University of Leipzig; verification rejects that and the hall is not identified anywhere
  reachable. The file keeps the city-level pin 51.34, 12.3765 and says so.
- **The "inner truth and greatness" parenthesis. CONTESTED, unresolved.** The quotation is
  verbatim (Richardson's 1966 interview translation, the interviewers reading it out). Not
  settled is Heidegger's claim that the parenthetical gloss stood in the 1935 manuscript: the
  page is missing from Marbach, and the standard view (en.wikipedia, "Introduction to
  Metaphysics (Heidegger book)") is that it was not in the original lecture. **The dossier's
  note calling the wording "confirmed by Heidegger as his own 1935 manuscript wording" is
  corrected here**: he claimed it, scholars dispute it, the file states both.
- **Resignation date**: 23 and 27 April 1934 both appear, for different steps; file uses
  1934-04-23. **Löwith [A, memoir-grade]**: his 1940 memoir, a participant account, not a court
  finding, and the campa says so.
- **Schwarze Hefte tag corrected.** The chronology tagged the 2014 publication **[R]** because
  the article 404'd in that pass. It is **[A]**: publication began February 2014, ed. Trawny, as
  GA 94-102, notebooks from 1931 onward [de.wikipedia]; the afterlife lens gives nine volumes,
  ~3,384 pages, run extended 2014-2022. *Placement*: the stop sits at Heidegger's own Freiburg
  pin, dated 1931 for the writing, with the 2014 publication as its closing fact, so the stop is
  his act and not a publisher's event.

## 6. The ban (1944-1951)

- **Autumn 1944, Volkssturm fortification labour on the Rhine plain** [A: Wikipedia]. Coordinate
  47.95, 7.6 is a placeholder for the digging effort, not a site.
- **The teaching bar. CORRECTION, and it matters.** The chronology dated it **1945**. There are
  two acts: the **Freiburg committee dismissed him from the university in September 1945**; the
  **formal French-zone teaching ban is dated 5 October 1946** [de.wikipedia; en.wikipedia]. The
  file gives 1945 the dismissal stop and states the 1946 ban date inside it.
- **Karl Jaspers's 1945 assessment** [A]: separates the stature of the thinking from the
  political reliability of the man, recommends temporary exclusion from teaching.
- **Winter 1945-46, collapse and treatment** [A, with caveat]. Viktor von Gebsattel was chief
  physician of the private psychiatric clinic **Schloss Hausbaden** near Badenweiler after the
  war [de.wikipedia]. Clinical details were not verified against a primary medical source;
  biographers say depression or nervous breakdown without a retrospective diagnosis, and so does
  the file. **The dossier reports no evidence of addiction; that absence is a gap, not filled.**
- **10 November 1946 Beaufret's questions; December 1946 the letter; 1947 published** [A:
  en.wikipedia "Letter on Humanism"].
- **March 1949, classified Mitläufer** [A: Wikipedia].
- **1949, the Bremen lectures at the Club zu Bremen** [A for content; venue not re-verified].
  The passage placing mechanised agriculture, atomic weapon manufacture and the production of
  corpses in gas chambers and extermination camps inside one technological essence is carried
  **flat, unsoftened and unresolved**: read as recognition, as levelling and as evasion, and the
  file says so and stops.
- **1950/1951, ban lifted, readmitted, emeritus** [A: Wikipedia]. Both years appear in the pool;
  the file uses 1951 and flags the discrepancy.

## 7. The late work (1959-1975)

- **1959-1969, the Zollikon seminars** with Medard Boss, and the criticism of Ludwig
  Binswanger's use of *Sein und Zeit* [A].
- **1962 and 1967, Greece [R].** The travel years are attested; **the Acropolis is emblematic,
  not confirmed as an itinerary point**, and the dossier itemises no route.
- **1966, 1968, 1969, Le Thor seminars hosted by René Char** [A]. Coordinate marks the village.
- **23 September 1966, the Der Spiegel interview** with Augstein and Wolff, on condition of
  posthumous publication [A]. *Correction*: the chronology gave "Freiburg im Breisgau /
  Todtnauberg"; it was given at his **Freiburg home** and the alternative is dropped.
- **25 July 1967, Paul Celan at the Todtnauberg hut** [A: Wikipedia "Todtnauberg"]. **Gap
  preserved**: no transcript survives, and claims that Heidegger either apologised or refused are
  both undocumented. The campa states the absence.
- **1975, the Gesamtausgabe begins with Klostermann** [A for the start and the arrangement].
  **CORRECTION: "106 volumes complete as of October 2025" is rejected.** The Gesamtausgabe
  article lists GA 103 and GA 104 as forthcoming in 2025, capping completed volumes near 102,
  and gives 37,736 pages across four series. The file says only that the edition has passed a
  hundred volumes and is unfinished.

## 8. Death and burial (1976)

- **26 May 1976, dies at home in Freiburg, aged 86** [A: SEP; Wikipedia]. No detailed terminal
  diagnosis is documented; general accounts say natural causes. Stated as a gap.
- **31 May 1976, the postponed Spiegel interview published**, five days after his death [A].
  Folded into the death campa: a magazine issue is not a place he went.
- **28 May 1976, buried in the Meßkirch cemetery beside his parents** [A].
  **CORRECTION: Welte's role narrowed.** de.wikipedia records that **Bernhard Welte gave the
  funeral oration (Grabrede)**. That Welte *conducted the Catholic burial rite*, and that he did
  so **at Heidegger's prior request**, is **not supported by anything reachable**. The file
  writes "graveside address by Bernhard Welte," reports the Catholic rite as reported, and states
  the request as unconfirmed. **The gravestone bears a single star**, for his 1947 line that to
  think is to confine yourself to a single thought that one day stands still like a star in the
  world's sky [A]. **COORDINATE CONFLICT: the sources carry three values for this one grave.**

## Posthumous material, and two legends left open

Carried in `suggested_refs` and here, not staged as scenes: the **Martin-Heidegger-Museum**, east
wing of Schloss Meßkirch, with an archive of ~1,200 photographs, ~40 broadcasts and interviews,
~15 recordings, largely unpublished [A: de.wikipedia "Meßkirch"]; **Günter Figal**'s resignation
of the Martin-Heidegger-Gesellschaft chairmanship in **January 2015**, telling Südwestrundfunk
the Schwarze Hefte made it impossible to go on representing "the society and the person" [A:
de.wikipedia]; and the English *Sein und Zeit*, Macquarrie and Robinson, SCM, **1962**,
Stambaugh, SUNY, **1996**, revised by Schmidt **2010**, with Cyril Welch's annotated translation
forthcoming from Yale, dated 2026 in the source and therefore provisional [A].

- **The "peasant sage of the Black Forest" [R].** The dossier holds the persona was cultivated by
  Heidegger himself through essays, photographs, dress and speeches from the 1920s, obscuring a
  strategic, networked academic. Only the hut's reality and its use for writing could be
  corroborated; the file carries the persona as a made thing ("the photographs are also work")
  without the stronger psychological claim.
- **The Husserl library ban [R], a live dispute.** The story that Heidegger as rector personally
  barred Husserl from the Freiburg library **could not be confirmed by any extant order**; the
  dossier calls it legend built on a real state-level discriminatory measure, and this pass found
  no primary source either way. **The file does not carry it**, saying instead that the state
  stripped Husserl of his university privileges, the attested part. Deliberate omission.

## Apparatus relations for the operator

Heidegger is **not** in `EXCEPTIONS.md`. Nothing has been silently deleted. Three classes caught:

1. **Forward-direction reception relations, four atlas travellers.** Every artifact postdates
   his 1976 clock stop; the relation runs one way, from them to him. `jacques_derrida`:
   *De l'esprit* (1987), tracking "Geist" to the 1933 rectoral address, and the 1997 Cerisy
   address on the animal. `gilles_deleuze`: *Empiricism and Subjectivity* (1953), framed against
   a Paris scene "governed by Hegel and Heidegger"; he also audited Jean Beaufret in 1945.
   `slavoj_zizek`: Ljubljana 1967-1971, "Heideggerian, then structuralist, then Lacanian."
   `nick_land`: an Essex PhD reported as on Heidegger's 1953 Trakl essay against a rival
   tradition naming a Kant thesis, which that file already flags as unresolved. **Handling**:
   per the block rule none is named in a campa or staged as a scene; Derrida's *De l'esprit* is
   carried as a `suggested_ref` and `source` on Heidegger's own rectoral-address stop, the
   sanctioned one-directional citation. **Direction caught: forward.** Not licensable by an
   agent; naming any of the four in prose is the author's call.
2. **A canonical pin the standing rule forbids inheriting.** The interlock lens recommended a
   *Kantbuch* stop inherit the corpus Königsberg pin **54.7085, 20.5095** from `kant`.
   **Declined**: Heidegger never went to Königsberg. Kant is named in the Davos campa instead,
   at Heidegger's own pin, legitimate because he wrote a book on Kant in his own lifetime.
   Recorded so the refusal reads as rule, not oversight.
3. **The Paris pin, likewise declined.** The lens offered 48.8566, 2.3522 for "any Heidegger
   stop concerning his French reception." No such stop exists: the Letter on Humanism was
   written at Zähringen and the reception happened to other people, on their own files.

Place-only echoes are **not** interlocks and appear in no campa: `max_weber` (Freiburg chair
1894-96, no overlap, no contact), `w_g_sebald` (Freiburg student 1963-65, no source naming
Heidegger; his writing on German memory-avoidance is thematic resonance, not a link), `reich`
(the Davos pin only). The parallel between Sartre's 1939-40 meteorological service in Alsace and
Heidegger's own 1918 weather posting is atmospheric, not contact.

**Atlas travellers named in campas.** Three, each rooted in Heidegger's own acts:
**`aristotle`** (the 1922 Natorp Report, the Freiburg Aristotle courses from 1919); **`kant`**
(the Davos disputation and *Kant und das Problem der Metaphysik*, both 1929);
**`jean_paul_sartre`** (the *Brief über den Humanismus* of December 1946 declines Sartre's
existentialism by name).

**Figures central to the life with no atlas file**, a gap and not a claim, not queued by this
pass: Arendt, Husserl, Jaspers, Cassirer, Marcuse, Celan, and likewise Bultmann, Löwith, Medard
Boss, René Char, Jean Beaufret. None has a `<slug>.journey.json` or a row in `QUEUE.md`. That
absence is why the interlock list is short.
