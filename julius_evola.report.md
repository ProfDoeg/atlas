# Julius Evola (1898-1974): research report

*For `julius_evola.journey.json`. 45 stops, 8 segments, gregorian, register "national mythology: the
canon is true".*

**[A]** attested, source on the line · **[R]** reconstruction, self-report, or a claim whose source could
not be reached. Contradictions are flagged and left open; in the journey file the hedge lives in
`date_confidence`, never in campa prose. The pool arrived with an adversarial pass applied and about a
third of its [A] items corrected, marked **CORR** here; three matter enough that nobody should quote
Evola from the older material, see section 9.

## 1. Birth and family, Rome 1898

- Born **19 May 1898, Rome**, registered **Giulio Cesare Andrea Evola**. [A: en/it/de/es Wikipedia;
  Treccani, *DBI*]
- Parents **Vincenzo Evola** and **Concetta Mangiapane**, both born at **Cinisi**, Palermo. [A] for the
  Sicilian Catholic origin and the father's name.
- **CONTRADICTION, open.** it.wikipedia ("entrambi originari di Cinisi") and es.wikipedia give
  **Mangiapane**; Treccani gives **Frangipane**, a Roman patrician surname where the claim itself
  asserts Sicily, so Treccani looks to be in error. The file follows Mangiapane and records the variant.
  **CORR**: the dossier cited English Wikipedia, which names no parents at all.
- **GAP.** No birth address reached. Pin: Rome centroid 41.9028, 12.4964.

## 2. Schooling and the refused degree, 1910s

- **Istituto Tecnico Leonardo da Vinci**, then **engineering at the University of Rome**: coursework
  completed, degree **deliberately not taken**, on stated contempt for bourgeois titles.
  [A: it.wikipedia; Treccani]
- **CORR, two pins.** The dossier's school pin, 41.896, 12.488, falls near the Imperial Fora; the
  institute is at Via Cavour 258, about 41.8930, 12.4930. It also pinned the engineering study at
  Palazzo della Sapienza, whereas by the 1910s the course was taught at the **Regia Scuola di
  Applicazione per gli Ingegneri, via Eudossiana** (about 41.8940, 12.4939). The two are merged into one
  stop, venue problem stated in the file.
- **GAP.** No year for either; 1915 is a declared placement.

## 3. Futurism, the war, Dada, 1915-1923

- **First paintings 1915, first poems 1916**, in the Roman Futurist milieu around **Giacomo Balla**.
  [A: Treccani]
- **Artillery training, Turin, 1917** [A: it.wikipedia]; **Monte Cimone di Tonezza**, with a surviving
  photograph of him there in uniform [A: en.wikipedia]; **Asiago, 1917-1918, no major action securely
  recorded** [A: en.wikipedia; Treccani]. **CORR, pin**: Nominatim gives Monte Cimone at 45.8217,
  11.3476, a kilometre off the dossier's guess.
- **April 1919, Palazzo Cova, Milan**, Marinetti's Grande Esposizione Nazionale Futurista, showing
  *Fucina, studio di rumori* and *Five o'clock tea*. [A: Treccani; en.wikipedia] **CORR, title**:
  Treccani gives that work in English; the dossier's "Cinque ore del te" is its own back-translation,
  meaning five hours of tea. [R] on the Via Silvio Pellico address.
- **January 1920, solo show at the Casa d'Arte Bragaglia**, with the manifesto *Arte astratta: posizione
  teorica* [A: en.wikipedia; Treccani], and **5 January 1920, the letter to Tristan Tzara** declaring
  himself Dada [A: Treccani, citing the correspondence published 1991 as *Lettere di Julius Evola a
  Tristan Tzara*].
- **1920, co-founds *Bleu* at Mantua** with Fiozzi and Cantarelli, three issues. [A] for the review
  (Treccani); [R] that Evola went to Mantua at all, so it is carried inside the Rome stop.
- **January 1921, sixty paintings at Der Sturm, Berlin**; **April 1921, Roman Dada evenings at the
  Grotte dell'Augusteo**, Evola painting the backdrop; **June 1921, Salon Dada, Galerie Montaigne,
  Paris**. All [A: Treccani; en.wikipedia]. **CORR, tag**: the dossier had the Salon at [R] on a stale
  "search budget exhausted" note.
- **1923, ceases painting and poetry.** [A: Treccani, "nel 1923 [l'E.] cessò l'attività pittorica e
  poetica"] **CORR**: the dossier's "1922/1923" collapses to the attested year. The **c. 1922
  near-suicidal crisis** ended by a sentence from an early Buddhist text is [R] entirely, from *Il
  cammino del cinabro* (1963), with no diary, letter or witness. **Giulio to Julius** is likewise [R],
  **CORR** from the dossier's [A]: Treccani gives no year, and Dada works were signed Julius in 1920.

## 4. Magical idealism, Ur, the first collision, 1925-1930

- **1925, *Saggi sull'idealismo magico* and *L'uomo come potenza*, Atanor**, and **1 May 1925, "Stato,

- **1925, lectures at the Independent Theosophical League of Rome**, whose president **Decio Calvari**
  edited *Ultra*, for which Evola had written since 1923. [A: Treccani] **CORR, tag**: [R] on a stale note.
- **1925/1926, the affair with Sibilla Aleramo** (b. 1876), treated in her 1927 *Amo dunque sono*.
  [A: it.wikipedia] **CORR**: the dossier's character name "**Tellegra**" appears in **no source
  reached** and is dropped, and it cited Treccani, which does not mention Aleramo at all.
- **Late 1926, the Gruppo di Ur forms around Arturo Reghini**, with Giovanni Colazza; *Ur* monthly

- **1928, *Imperialismo pagano*,** attacking the Church just before the Lateran settlement. [A: Treccani]
- **February to June 1930, *La Torre*, ten issues, closed after Achille Starace's intervention.**
  [A: Treccani; it.wikipedia] The printers'-boycott mechanism is slightly more specific than the sources

## 7. The German years, Vienna, the injury, 1943-1948

- **After 25 July 1943, Preziosi names Evola to the Germans** [A: Treccani]; **after 8 September, a
  period in Munich** [A: it.wikipedia].
- **Presence at the Wolfsschanze, Rastenburg, in the party receiving the rescued Mussolini.**
  **[R], and the most over-tagged item in the dossier, which carried it [A].** It appears in **none** of
  Treccani, en, de or it Wikipedia, and traces to **Evola's own *Il cammino del cinabro*** (1963). Its
  sole external source, centrostudilaruna, dates his presence to **8 September 1943**, four days
  **before** the 12 September rescue, so it cannot describe a party receiving the rescued Duce. A meeting
  with Hitler is not established at all.
- **Movimento per la Rinascita dell'Italia in occupied Rome, 1943-1944.** [R], wholly undocumented: no
  membership, statute or paper found. **Not carried as its own stop**, but named in the Vienna stop's
  `date_confidence` so it is not lost.
- **1944, Vienna, work on Masonic and Jewish material seized by the SS, in the orbit of the
  Sicherheitsdienst and RSHA Amt VII.** [A: Treccani, citing Cospito and Neulen, eds., *Julius Evola nei
  documenti segreti del Terzo Reich*, 1986] **His status is genuinely disputed among historians**, and
  the dispute is left open in both files.
- **Spring 1945, spinal injury during the bombardment of Vienna after deliberately refusing the
  shelters; permanent paraplegia.** [A] for injury, refusal (Treccani: "rifiutava deliberatamente di
  scendere nei rifugi") and paralysis. **CONTRADICTION, month, open**: Treccani April, it.wikipedia
  March. **CONTRADICTION, projectile, open**: a Soviet air raid (de), a Soviet shell fragment (en), "un
  fragmento de proyectil" (es), only "un bombardamento" (Treccani). **GAP**: no street or building
  named, so the Vienna centroid is used.
- **Roughly two years in Austrian clinics, ending with the 1948 return.** [A: Treccani, "Dopo due anni
  trascorsi nelle cliniche austriache, nel 1948 rientrò in Italia"] **CORR**: the dossier's geography

## 9. Quotations

Nine are carried. Three widely circulated Evola quotations proved misattributed.

1. **"E FANTASTICA L'ACCUSA DI ANTIFASCISMO, SE MAI SIAMO SUPERFASCISTI"**, *La Torre*, 1930, from the
   facsimile of Evola's notice in the Italian *Il cammino del cinabro*. **This is the true home of
   "superfascista", and the most important correction in the pool.** English Wikipedia, compressing
   Cassina Wolff (*Patterns of Prejudice* 50:4-5, 478-494), attaches it to the **1951 trial**; Evola's
   actual *Autodifesa*, read in full in Godwin's translation, contains **no occurrence of it**. The word
   is his, first person **plural**, for a journal twenty-one years earlier.
2. **"I do not intend to present myself either as an antifascist or as a victim of Fascism."**
   *Autodifesa*, 1951, trans. Godwin, in *Men Among the Ruins* (Inner Traditions, 2002).
3. **"At this time the social bond was no longer a fides of a warrior type..."** *Revolt*, trans. Stucco
   (1995), p. approx. 327. **CORR**: the dossier's wording, "The social bond assumes a utilitarian and
   economic character", **appears nowhere in Evola**, and its citation to *Men Among the Ruins* p. 34 is
   **false**, "social bond" occurring **zero times** there.
4. **"The blood of the heroes is closer to God than the ink of the philosophers..."** **Words real,
   attribution wrong**, as `quote_source` says: verbatim in *Revolt*, p. approx. 125, but Evola is there
   listing traditional sayings in quotation marks, beside "Paradise lies under the shade of the swords",
   a hadith.
5. Four taken from en.wikiquote with the citation given there, not cross-checked against scans:
   **"Danger reawakens the spirit."** (*Metaphysics of War*, p. 66); **"In a large city, in mass
   society..."** (*Ride the Tiger*, p. 121); **"Prussia had been the creation of a dynasty..."** (*Notes
   on the Third Reich*, p. 37); and, [R], **"God does not exist..."** (en.wikipedia fn. 71, *Saggi
   sull'idealismo magico*). The first and third sit at stops predating them, and the files say so.
   Ninth: **Serrano's chronology line**, verbatim.

**Not carried.** "Americans do not think, yet they are.", cited to *Civiltà Americana* (1945): **idea
real, wording and citation are not**, with zero hits across the 38-volume corpus. Genuine versions exist
in *Recognitions* and *Revolt*, and in both **Evola himself puts the phrase in quotation marks**. Also
dropped: "Only of an elite may one say that 'it is of a race'...", [R], en.wikipedia fn. 100, dated 1969
with **the work unnamed**.

## 10. Apparatus relations for the operator

Per `EXCEPTIONS.md`, relations not rooted in the subject's own life are not silently deleted, and Evola
is **not** on the list, so this was kept out of the journey and is reported for the author.

**Direction caught: BACKWARD.** The interlock lens proposed extending the corpus's canonical Kaaba pin,
**21.4225, 39.8262**, to Evola, because the Hyperborean-pole and sacred-centre doctrine of *Rivolta*
treats a transhistorical polar centre as Guénon's file treats Mecca, the qibla he never travelled to.
The lens itself called this "a doctrinal or thematic echo, not a biographical event". Evola never went
to Mecca. Applying his apparatus to the Kaaba would be backward, the licensable direction, but it needs
him added to the list first. **No Mecca stop was written**, and the pin is recorded here byte-identical
so it can be inherited exactly if granted.

Two further lens findings, wartime Vienna as a node shared with Guénon's network and Eliade's postings,
and a publisher overlap with Guénon whose houses were in fact all French, were dropped as shared
geography, not apparatus.

**No forward reference was written.** Dugin's translation and Bannon's remark are other people's
engagements with Evola after his death. The 2022 Mart retrospective and Pautasso's 2025 catalogue
raisonné concern objects **Evola himself made**, so they sit in `suggested_refs` on his own painting
stops, never staged as scenes.
