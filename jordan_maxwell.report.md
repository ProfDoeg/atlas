# Jordan Maxwell (Russell Joseph Pine), 1940-2022

*Research report, 2026-09-01, for `jordan_maxwell.journey.json`. The corpus already carries James Clerk Maxwell, Robert Maxwell and Ghislaine Maxwell: this subject is always **Jordan Maxwell** in full, never a bare "Maxwell".*

**[A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or a claim carried only by the subject. Contradictions are flagged, not adjudicated. Eight coordinates inherited from the earlier crawl were wrong; each correction and its reason is stated in that stop's `date_confidence` and is not repeated here.

**The central evidentiary problem.** Two-thirds of what is known of his movements comes from one document: the career chronology at `jordanmaxwell.com/bio.html`, self-published, controlled since 2010 by a company he was litigating against, self-contradictory in at least four places, and for most of his life the only source there is. Below, "bio" means that page, and bio-only items are [A] strictly as *what the page says*.

## Phase 1: Russell Pine, 1940-1959

- **Birth [R].** Pensacola, Florida, 28 December 1940, from Wikidata Q2395889, `jordanmaxwellshow.com` and FamilySearch GLRL-XSG, which returned only a browser-compatibility notice to me. No birth certificate, census entry or newspaper notice exists in anyone's account. *Contradiction:* memorial pages give age 81 at death, the Euro Weekly News notice of 25 March 2022 says **82**. Do not attribute "age 81" to that article.
- **Los Angeles, c. 1959 [R].** Arrival at about nineteen; the gift of *The Complete Books of Charles Fort* from an acquaintance named **Bob Ferney**. In every interview-derived biography, in no document, and "Bob Ferney" appears in no source I could reach.
- **1959 as self-dated origin [A, self-report].** Bio: "His interest in these subjects began as far back as 1959." *Correction:* the widely quoted "Jordan Maxwell began researching occult and religious philosophy in 1959" is **not on the page**. Year right, quotation fabricated.
- **The Vatican great-uncle [A as an utterance, R as a fact].** Told on air 17 June 1993: a maternal great-uncle, a civilian in the Secretariat of State, brought home accounts of how religion and politics were arranged. Never named; no record or relative produced, and nobody has looked in Vatican files.
- **Honest gap, 1959-1989.** Thirty years. A chronology listing call signs and lecture titles by the dozen carries **nothing**: no employment, education, address, marriage or military record.

## Phase 2: the seminar circuit, 1989-1992 (all bio)

- **1989.** Los Angeles seminars begin; board of **United Sensitives of America**, an entity with no corporate record located.
- **1991.** *The Naked Truth* / *The Hidden Truth*, written by him, hosted by **Derek Partridge** for the International Research and Educational Society, "broadcast on KCLA-TV (Channel 9) in Los Angeles, and New York City." *Contradiction with the external record:* no Los Angeles KCLA-TV on channel 9 is documented; channel 9 was KHJ-TV, renamed **KCAL-TV** in 1989. Wrong, or leased airtime; unresolved. The bio adds, unexplained, that he "is no longer affiliated in any way with IRES Co."
- **1991.** *The Tom Leykis Show*, KFI, then at 610 South Ardmore Avenue.

- **February 1991.** Lecture at the **Masonic Lodge of La Crescenta**: a venue, not a membership. The circulating "32nd-degree Freemason" claim has no document behind it in any jurisdiction.
- **December 1991.** The Bob Grant show, WABC, New York.

## Phase 4: the long circuit, 1994-1999 (bio unless noted)

- **KABC, 1994-1996.** Ray Briem's show with newsman **Bill Jenkins**, 1994; *Main Street America* with Jenkins, 1995; Doug Stephen, 1996. *Correction:* the range is 1994-1996, not 1992-1996, Jenkins's 1991-92 appearances having been at Maxwell's own seminars.
- **May and August 1994.** An antiquarian book fair at Burbank, consistent with his trade in Victorian comparative mythology (**Gerald Massey**, **Godfrey Higgins**); then "Century City Rotary Club / Century Plaza Hotel, **Beverly Hills**, Ca / 'The Law of God'." *Contradiction:* the hotel is in Century City, Los Angeles.
- **December 1994, internally contradictory.** "International UFO Congress, Mosquite [*sic*], Nevada, Dec. 1994" and, under Lectures, the same congress at **Laughlin**, "December 1994 and August 1998." Two towns, one month, one page. Unresolved.
- **19 March 1995 [A], corrected programme.** **Dreamland**, Art Bell's Sunday-night show, **not Coast to Coast AM**, per the archive.org item title, `jordanmaxwellshow.com`, and the bio's own "Dreamland, Pahrump, Nevada, 3 hours, 1995." That date was a Sunday, and the episode was shared with **Linda Moulton Howe**. The "Coast to Coast AM, The Dark Side of Religion" citation in wide circulation is wrong.
- **March and April 1995.** UCLA, USC and **Ventura College** in one month, consolidated into one campus stop at the UCLA pin. Then "University of Georgia / **Atlanta**, Georgia," impossible as written, UGA being in **Athens**. Athens is a **reconstruction**: the bio may instead have garbled the institution, Georgia State and Georgia Tech being in Atlanta.
- **May 1995.** NPR *Morning Edition*, his most mainstream credit. No transcript located, and in-studio or down a line is not stated.
- **Remainder.** Global Sciences Congress, Denver, August 1995 (no stop); University of Hawaii at Manoa, May 1996; Area 51 perimeter events near **Rachel, Nevada**, December 1994 and August 1996, with no evidence of entry and none claimed; Kronia conference, Portland, January 1997, the claimed 1995 board role unverified; Lapis conference, Manchester, October 1998; "Egypt in the New Millennium," Giza, May 1999, no itinerary located.

## Phase 5: the books, 2000

Two Book Tree titles, both 2000 **[A]**: ***Matrix of Power*** (one Open Library edition, OCR at archive.org) and ***That Old-Time Religion***, with **Paul Tice** and **Alan Snow**. An earlier *Jordan Maxwell on Religion and Politics* (1994) **[R]** is unconfirmed, WorldCat returning HTTP 429.

- **(a) "Control both parties" is misquoted, and half of it is not his.** *Matrix of Power* p. 5. His sentence: "What I have said, however, is that they do control both parties, among many other things in the world." The freestanding "They do control both parties…" invents a sentence boundary not in the source. The lead-in about "the little coterie of powerful international bankers" is **Maxwell quoting Mayor John F. Hylan of New York, 1922**.
- **(b) The parachute line, [R] to [A], wording corrected.** The Goodreads form ("it doesn't work unless it's open") is the floating aphorism usually given to Thomas Dewar. The real line, *Matrix of Power* p. 87, is: "Remember, your mind is like a parachute, it will not work if it's not open."
- **(c) "Ignorance and its denial…" dropped.** Goodreads files it under his name, but the book cited, *Symbols, Sex, and the Stars in Popular Beliefs*, is **Ernest Busenbark's** (1945/1949); Maxwell wrote only a **preface** to the Book Tree reprint. I grepped the complete 1949 text for four phrases from it: zero hits. Never cite it as his book.
- **(d) "Israel = Isis-Ra-El": real claim, no verbatim wording, never in quote marks.** Michael S. Heiser's critique, via Wayback, is "**Syllabic Silliness with Jordan Maxwell**," PaleoBabble, 30 July 2008; the title "Jordan Maxwell: 'Israel' Is Isis, Ra, and El" appears invented. Heiser paraphrases from Maxwell's website and nowhere quotes him, so the etymology is **attributed paraphrase, never in quotation marks**.

## Phase 6: the federal case, 2003-2004

- **Filing [A].** *FTC v. Jordan Maxwell*, **CV 03-0128 NM (CWx)**, C.D. Cal. Memorandum Decision p. 1: "The Federal Trade Commission initiated this action on January 7, 2003." *Contradiction:* the FTC's own case page dates it **16 January 2003**, the day of the "Operation License for Trouble" press release. The court's date is used.
- **Caption correction.** **BBCOA was not a third defendant.** Two individuals are named: Jordan Maxwell, "also known as RUSSELL PINE," doing business as BBCOA, and Vic Varjabedian. The court terminated "Russell Pine" as a party on 23 January 2003 "because it was an alias of Jordan Maxwell."
- **Claims [A], default [R].** Fake International Driver's Permits, bogus credit repair and sham debt-termination programs, under the FTC Act and the Credit Repair Organizations Act. Clerk's entry of default, January 2004, after service by publication in the *Los Angeles Times*, he having reportedly evaded personal service; the notice was not located.
- **Judgment [A], date corrected.** "$444,554.66 for equitable monetary relief which is equal to the amount paid by victims of Defendants' illegal scheme" (p. 9), jointly and severally, signed 27 April 2004 and filed 28 April, the injunction posted under **17 June 2004**. "January 2004" belongs only to the entry of default.
- **A civil action and a default judgment, not a criminal conviction.** No criminal charge appears in any record located. *Citation removed:* the earlier crawl rested this on *Maxwell v. Dolezal*, whose full text mentions neither the FTC nor this case.

Contemporaneous (bio): *Signs of Destiny II*, Tempe, November 2003; Coast to Coast AM with **George Noory**, March 2004 and April 2005.

## Phase 7: Egypt, Malta, Zeitgeist, 2004-2007

- **Tours (bio).** A further Egypt tour in 2004, no months given; Malta, 25 February to 5 March 2006, at the Radisson SAS Golden Sands, the bio naming the **hotel** and no temple, so **Hagar Qim stands as a representative landmark of the island's megalithic sites, not a confirmed stop**; Egypt again 25 March to 6 April 2006, a solar-eclipse tour, the eclipse of 29 March having crossed Egypt.
- **18 June 2007 [A].** **Peter Joseph's *Zeitgeist: The Movie*** released online, using audio from Chogyam Trungpa Rinpoche, **Jordan Maxwell** and George Carlin in the opening montage (Wikipedia). The widest circulation his ideas ever reached, and he neither made, controlled nor was paid for it. *Placement:* made without him present, so the stop sits at **his own Los Angeles pin**, not New York.

## Phase 8: the name assigned away, 2010-2018

- **30 March 2010 [R].** Russell Joseph Pine, signing as Jordan Maxwell, assigns the Jordan Maxwell copyrights, trademarks and domains to **Josef Dolezal**. The instrument was not obtained, only its recitation in later records.
- **January 2013 [R] and 4 November 2014 [A].** **Momentum Development LLC** applies to register JORDAN MAXWELL; the same month he sues Dolezal in state court in propria persona. In ***Maxwell v. Dolezal***, B254893, 231 Cal.App.4th 93 (full text obtained), the publicity claim fails because he consented in the 2010 assignment, while the breach-of-contract claim is revived and remanded. A pleading-stage ruling only; the disposition on remand is in no record located.

No. 5,377,518 issued 16 January 2018**.
- **19 June 2018 [A].** **Momentum Development LLC**, chapter 7, **1:18-bk-11538**, Bankr. C.D. Cal. ## Phase 9: Secret Life of Symbols, death, contested name

- ***Secret Life of Symbols*, Gaia [A, corrected].** **Nine** episodes, not ten: Gaia's own person page, archived 1 May 2019, lists Season 1 Ep1-Ep9 and no tenth, the titles being in the stop's `suggested_refs`. The **2018 date is unevidenced**, earliest evidence being May 2019, and the live URLs now 404. **Louisville, Colorado is Gaia's headquarters and an inference, not a documented shooting location.**
- **Death, 23 March 2022 [A as an announcement date only].** Euro Weekly News, 25 March 2022: "has died at the age of 82… his death was announced on Wednesday, March 23." It names **no city, no institution, no cause**; Wikidata Q2395889 carries the date and is derivative. **"Died in California" is not supported by any source located**, and the coordinate is an explicit placeholder. **No grave:** FindAGrave, Legacy.com and the aggregators turn up no cemetery, funeral home or plot.
- **The rival sites [A].** `jordanmaxwell.com`, run by Momentum, footer: "© 2022 Momentum Developments LLC | JORDAN MAXWELL is a registered trademark." Note **LLC**, not "LCC" as previously transcribed; the registrant of record is **Momentum Development LLC**, singular. Against it, `jordanmaxwellshow.com` brands itself "The LEGITIMATE Jordan Maxwell," runs the podcast archive and an estate Patreon, and is stamped "Copyright © 2013 - 2024"; **do not assert it operates past 2024**. Its masthead maxim about taking authority as the truth is attributed by the site itself to **Jordan Maxwell quoting Gerald Massey**, so the framing "an afterlife built on his own catchphrase" inverts the record.
- **Wikipedia [A, scope corrected].** `en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_Maxwell` returns **HTTP 404**, but Wikidata carries sitelinks to a **Dutch Wikipedia article** and a **Spanish Wikiquote page**, so the "outside mainstream reference culture" point must be English-Wikipedia-specific. Nor is the canon untranslated: Open Library returns Spanish compilations crediting him with **Colin Rivas** and **Anthony Hilder**, which matters for this readership.
