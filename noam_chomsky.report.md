# Noam Chomsky: research report

*Compiled 2026-08-21 for `noam_chomsky.journey.json`. Avram Noam Chomsky, b. East Oak Lane, Philadelphia, 7 December 1928; linguist, MIT Institute Professor emeritus, political writer; resident since 2023 in Sao Paulo.*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source on the line. **[R]** reconstruction or inference, including anything the pool could not anchor to a primary or near-primary source. Contradictions are flagged, not adjudicated in silence; where a pin forced a choice, that stop's `date_confidence` names the loser. The pool arrived as five lenses (chronology, geography, quotes, interlock, afterlife), several carrying correction notes that contradict the claim they annotate. Those are its most valuable lines and are reproduced rather than absorbed.

---

## 1. Philadelphia, 1928 to 1945

**Birth, 7 December 1928**, to William Chomsky, Hebrew grammarian, and Elsie Simonofsky, children of Ashkenazi immigrants **[A: Britannica]**.

**Birthplace pin, two errors.** Geography gave 40.0453, -75.1305, about 800 m south in Logan/Olney. The afterlife lens gave 40.0428, -75.1197, in Lawncrest/Olney, and called the birthplace "the Elkins Park / East Oak Lane section of Philadelphia." Elkins Park is not in Philadelphia; it is in Cheltenham Township. The file uses the East Oak Lane centroid, 40.052, -75.128 **[A: Wikipedia]**.

**William Chomsky at Gratz College and the Mikveh Israel school. [R: Barsky; family accounts]** Geography pinned 39.9531, -75.1497, the congregation's 44 North Fourth Street synagogue, occupied only from July 1976: four decades wrong. Through the 1930s and 1940s Mikveh Israel stood at Broad and York, on the Gratz and Dropsie campus, 39.9905, -75.1545 **[A: Wikipedia; mikvehisrael.org]**. The corrected pin is used, with a note that no dated occasion places the boy there, only the household's known shape.

**Oak Lane Country Day School**, Deweyite, entered at two **[A: Chomsky's accounts; Barsky]**. Not in Philadelphia: it stood on White Road in Cheltenham Township until the 1965 move to Blue Bell. Pin moved north of the city line, about 40.067, -75.130 **[A: Wikipedia; Glenside Local]**.

**The article on the fall of Barcelona: contradiction inside the pool.** Chronology dates it 1938, its own correction note February 1939. Barcelona fell 26 January 1939, and a February 1939 article by a boy born in December 1928 makes him ten, which is what he has always said **[A: his repeated accounts; Wikipedia; New Statesman, 2022]**. The file takes 1939-02-01. The text does not survive **[gap]**.

**Central High School, 1940 to 1945. [A]** Geography labelled the stop "Broad and Green," 39.9743, -75.1594; Central moved to Ogontz and Olney Avenues in 1939, before he arrived. Corrected to about 40.0399, -75.1508 **[A: Wikipedia]**.

**The New York trips, the most tangled item in the pool.** Chronology said "the anarchist bookshops of Union Square"; geography said "Fourth Avenue bookstalls, Manhattan (his uncle's newsstand)," 40.7328, -73.9905. Both correction notes agree the uncle's stand was a newsstand at the back of a 72nd Street subway exit on the Upper West Side, about 40.7784, -73.9819, obtained under a Depression-era disability program, and that the Fourth Avenue book district was a separate haunt. **[A: Biography.com; Amy Goodman interview, chomsky.info/20041126]** One stop, at the corrected pin, saying in the campa that the two are routinely merged. Trips begin around age twelve or thirteen; no individual visit is dated **[R]**. Rocker read here **[A]**. The pool's second Fourth Avenue stop ("adult years," 1960s to 1970s, **[R]**) was dropped: no event, a coordinate now known wrong, a general pattern in place of a scene.

## 2. Penn and the kibbutz, 1945 to 1955

Enrolled at sixteen in philosophy, logic, Arabic, funded partly by teaching Hebrew **[A: Wikipedia]**. Met **Zellig Harris** through **Avukah**, the radical Zionist student circle, 1947, and was redirected into linguistics **[A: Jewish Book Council; New Voices]**. Geography adds that he enrolled "intending to drop out for Palestine," widely repeated and resting on his own retrospective telling **[A/R]**. Relationship with **Carol Doris Schatz** from 1947 **[R]**, marriage 1949 **[A]**; the chronology lens lists that marriage after its 1951 MA entry, which shows it was assembled unsorted. **MA 1951 [A]**, folded into the marriage stop.

**Kibbutz HaZore'a, 1953: contradiction.** Chronology said "a kibbutz-adjacent milieu," "observing collectivist experiments"; geography said "several months." The correction note says six weeks, on a Harvard travel grant that also took the couple through England, France, Switzerland and Italy; that he later said he came close to returning there to live; and that he was appalled by the anti-Arab racism and pro-Stalinist current he found **[A: Wikipedia]**. Six weeks taken; coordinates verified within about 300 m. **PhD 1955**, Penn, cut from the larger *Logical Structure of Linguistic Theory* drafted at Harvard **[A]**.

## 3. Cambridge, 1951 to 1966

**Harvard Society of Fellows, 1951 to 1955**, under Goodman and Quine **[A]**. Pin: the canonical Harvard Yard coordinate, 42.3744, -71.1169, inherited byte-identical from `j_l_austin.journey.json`. Geography offered 42.3736, -71.119, the canonical Harvard University Press pin; either is canonical, and the Yard was chosen so the fellowship and the Austin meeting sit together. **J. L. Austin, April 1955 [A: `j_l_austin.journey.json`]**, framed there as "two roads out of one problem." A genuine two-way interlock: they met. Named in the campa; day not fixed there, defaulted.

**MIT 1955**, appointment secured by Roman Jakobson, office in **Building 20** adjoining Morris Halle **[A: MIT News; Wikipedia]**. **Syntactic Structures 1957 [A]**; daughter **Aviva** born that year, place given only as "Massachusetts" **[gap]**, so she is named inside the Cambridge stop. **Full professor 1961 [A]**, folded into the **Cartesian Linguistics 1966 [A]** stop.

**The MIT pin**, canonical at 42.3592, -71.0921 (shared with `claude_shannon.journey.json` and, via `robert_oppenheimer.journey.json`, Feynman), is used byte-identical by every MIT stop here except Building 20, which keeps 42.3609, -71.0913. Its 1998 demolition is not a stop: that is a fact about the building after he left it.

**Institute Professor 1976.** Geography staged this at "MIT commencement, Killian Court"; its own correction note calls the venue invented, Institute Professor being an internal faculty appointment. Killian Court dropped, fabrication stated in `date_confidence`.

## 4. The war years, 1962 to 1971

Public protest from **1962**, Boston churches and homes **[A: Wikipedia]**; individual meetings itemized nowhere reachable **[gap]**. **"The Responsibility of Intellectuals," NYRB, February 1967 [A]**, placed at the MIT pin rather than New York: he wrote it in Cambridge, and the printing is the magazine's act. **RESIST, 1967**, with Goodman, Levertov, Coffin and Macdonald **[A]**, folded into the Boston Common stop.

**16 October 1967: contradiction, resolved in the pool's favour and flagged.** Geography staged Chomsky's speech at Arlington Street Church. The correction note produces a catalogued photograph, UMass Amherst muph009-v04a-sl073-i022, "Noam Chomsky speaking at rally on Boston Common, October 16, 1967" **[A]**; the draft-card turn-in and burning was the church service later that day **[A]**. Two stops, Boston Common (42.3550, -71.0656) and Arlington Street Church (42.3517, -71.0713), each naming the conflation.

**Pentagon march and arrest, 21 October 1967**, with Norman Mailer **[A]**; overnight custody at the Occoquan workhouse in Lorton, confirmed by Mailer's *The Armies of the Night* **[A]**. The pool called it a "federal detention facility"; it was a District of Columbia institution. Occoquan is folded into the Pentagon stop, correction kept.

**Laos, March 1970: contradiction.** Geography says "spends weeks"; its correction note says he expected one day, missed the ICC flight, and stayed about a week **[A: "A Visit to Laos," NYRB, 23 July 1970]**. One week taken. **The Plain of Jars camp** was pinned at 17.9333, 102.6, about 7 km out, where Chomsky's own text says about thirty-five miles; rather than invent a coordinate the refugee interviews are folded into the Vientiane stop with the distance stated in the campa, which is the pool's own second option **[gap: the camp is not locatable]**.

**Hanoi, 10 April 1970**, a week, lectures at the university, published as "In North Vietnam" **[A]**. **Nixon enemies list 1971**, **Guggenheim 1971**, **National Academy of Sciences 1972 [all A]**: one stop pinned at the White House, labelled as the seat of the administration that compiled the list and not a place he visited.

**Chomsky and Foucault, Eindhoven, 1971.** Carried by the interlock lens as **[R]**: famous, filmed and broadcast, but absent from this file's chronology and geography lenses, and `michel_foucault.journey.json` is silent on it, covering Foucault's Paris GIP work instead. The stop is kept, 51.4416, 5.4697, with that evidential position stated and the month defaulted. It is his own travel.

## 5. Anarcho-syndicalism and the propaganda model, 1976 to 1988

**Peter Jay interview, 25 July 1976. [A: chomsky.info/19760725; libcom.org]** The "libertarian left / voluntary socialism" passage is the stop's quote, verbatim.

**Quote-lens corrections, all accepted.** Three pool quotes were rejected rather than used with silent repairs: "I'm a derivative fellow traveler, let's say," where every transcript reads "a derivative fellow traveler [of anarchism]"; "Libertarianism, in my view, in the current world, is just a call for some of the worst kinds of tyranny...," whose lead-in is not verbatim; and "It's quite possible to build the institutions of a future society within the present one," where the source has Chomsky reporting Bakunin. A fourth, "anarcho-syndicalism is a conception of a very organized society...," was tagged **[R]** until the correction note found the primary source (chomsky.info/20130528), where the subject is a pronoun with a bracketed gloss. Promoted to **[A]**, unused for want of a stop.

**Faurisson affair, 1980. [A]** Stated flat in the campa: the man denies the Nazi extermination of the Jews; Chomsky defended the right to publish, wrote a free-speech essay, it appeared as the preface without his knowledge, he called the claims false and repellent, his standing in France did not recover. Pin is Paris, 48.8566, 2.3522, labelled as the site of the controversy; no travel over the affair is attested **[gap]**.

**Managua: direct contradiction between lenses.** Chronology said 1985, geography 1986; the correction note settles on March 1986 per the publishers of both lecture volumes **[A: South End Press; MIT Press]**. UCA Managua is 12.1253, -86.2719, not 12.135, -86.2789 **[A: Wikipedia]**.

**Manufacturing Consent, 1988**, with Edward S. Herman, collaboration dating from 1973 **[A]**. Pinned at MIT, noting that Herman worked at Wharton and the book has no single site. The 1992 Achbar and Wintonick documentary is real **[A: Wikipedia]** but is the film-makers' travel: `suggested_refs` only. **Kyoto Prize, 11 November 1988 [A: kyotoprize.org]**, coordinates corrected from 35.04, 135.7847 to 35.0611, 135.7831. The pool's **"Occupied West Bank border crossing, near Ramallah," 1988, [R]** was dropped and is recorded here rather than deleted in silence: no event, a round coordinate (31.9, 35.2), a source line naming no travel.

## 6. 1992 to 2011

**Sydney Town Hall, January 1995**, East Timor **[A: contemporary accounts]**.

**Honorary degrees.** A dozen in the pool, including Chicago 1967, Swarthmore (undated, 1970 per the correction), Cambridge 1995, McGill (undated, 1998 per the correction), Columbia 1999, Calcutta 2001, Buenos Aires (undated) **[A: Wikipedia, List of honorary degrees]**. Fifty-plus institutions is a category, not a set of scenes. One is pinned, Buenos Aires, and the campa says why: the degree is undated, and the undatedness is the shape of the relation.

**Delhi lectures: contradiction.** The pool dates the series 2001; the correction note establishes that *Democracy and Power: The Delhi Lectures* (Open Book Publishers, 2014, ed. Dreze) collects two visits, 1996 and 2001 **[A]**. The stop says so.

**September 2001 and the 2002 retirement [A]**, one Cambridge stop; the 1992 MIT citation study (3,874 citations in the AHCI 1980 to 1992, eighth most cited source overall, most cited living person) rides in `sources` **[A: news.mit.edu/1992/citation-0415]**. **Istanbul, 14 February 2002 [A: HRW]**: Fatih Tas acquitted on the first day at the prosecutor's own request, Chomsky in court asking to be tried as co-defendant.

**World Social Forum, Porto Alegre, 2003.** Geography tagged this **[R]** off a general "documented alignment"; its correction note upgrades it to **[A]**: closing session, football stadium, about 15,000 people, platform shared with Arundhati Roy, "A World Without Wars" **[Democracy Now!, 18 February 2003]**. Carried as attested, noting that the lens erred in the cautious direction.

**Beirut, May 2006. [A: Kfoury, ZNetwork]** Two lectures at AUB; meeting with Hassan Nasrallah in the southern suburbs **[A: regional press]**, coordinate approximate.

**Allenby Bridge, 16 May 2010. [A: Democracy Now! transcript, 17 May 2010]** Coordinates corrected 31.8611 to 31.8742 and 35.5397 to 35.5408; "questioned for three hours" corrected to about five hours at the crossing with roughly two of intermittent questioning; passport stamped with two red lines and "Entry denied" in English and Hebrew, *entry* misspelled. **The video link: two more errors.** The pool dated it 16 May and placed it in "a hotel." The 17 May transcript has Chomsky describing it as still to come; it was given 18 May 2010 from the University of Jordan **[A]**, the date the Amman stop takes. **Birzeit**, offered as a stop for the students who gathered to watch, is dropped: Chomsky was never there, and that is their assembly. It survives in the Amman stop's `suggested_refs`, which makes the coordinate error the pool caught there moot here. **Occupy Boston, 2011 [A]**.

## 7. Late life, 2014 to 2023

**Marriage to Valeria Wasserman 2014 [A]**, Sao Paulo residence from 2015 **[A]**. Carol Chomsky died 19 December 2008 **[A: Philadelphia Inquirer]**, folded into the Sao Paulo marriage stop; the Lexington pin, 42.443, -71.229, is there if the operator wants it.

**DiEM25 founding collective, 9 February 2016. [A: `yanis_varoufakis.journey.json`]** Pinned at Chomsky's own MIT base, not at the Volksbuhne: the launch is Varoufakis's scene and lives in his file. **University of Arizona, announced 17 August 2017**, Laureate Professor, Agnese Nelms Haury chair **[A: Arizona Daily Wildcat; sbs.arizona.edu/chomsky]**.

**Stroke, June 2023: contradiction.** Chronology placed it in Sao Paulo and said he "relocates permanently to Brazil." The correction note establishes that the stroke happened in the United States, where he was based in Tucson; that Valeria flew him by air ambulance only once he was stable enough to travel; and that "relocates permanently" overstates a move to a city where the couple had held a residence since 2015 **[A: AP/Seattle Times; Time, 2024, citing Valeria Chomsky]**. Two stops, Tucson and the Sao Paulo hospital, the first naming the error. Transfer month **[R]**.

**MIT Chomsky Papers**, 1945 to 2013, 260-plus boxes, deeded 2012, digitized from 2017 **[A: MIT Libraries; ArchivesSpace 1305]**: in the final stop's sources. An archive accession is not a journey.

## Honest gaps

- **The Barcelona article** does not survive; everything about it comes from his own retellings, consistent and unverifiable. **The 1941 to 1942 New York trips** have no dated visit; that stop's year is a reconstruction.
- **The Plain of Jars camp** cannot be located beyond "about thirty-five miles" from Vientiane. No pin was invented.
- **Aviva Chomsky's birthplace** is "Massachusetts" and nothing finer; his other children are not in the pool and are not named in the file.
- **The Buenos Aires doctorate has no year** in any reachable source; the 2000-01-01 date carries no evidential weight and says so. **The Sao Paulo transfer date** is not public. **Occupy Boston** and **Sydney 1995** have no day.
- **Chomsky on large language models**, including the March 2023 New York Times piece, is absent from the pool entirely and therefore from the file: the most conspicuous hole in a 2026 treatment of this subject, and the first thing to commission on a second pass. Nothing was invented to fill it. The 2009 Venezuela visit and the Kurdish provinces after the Istanbul trial are likewise passed near and not asserted.
