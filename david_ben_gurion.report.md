# David Ben-Gurion (1886-1973): research report

*For `david_ben_gurion.journey.json`, 2026-08-29. [A] = attested, source named. [R] = reconstruction, tradition, or a claim traced only through a secondary aggregator. Contradictions are flagged, not adjudicated. Unreachable sources are named with the reason. Every hedge that reaches the journey file lives in a stop's `date_confidence`.*

The register is national mythology, which is not agreement with him: the canon is written as true, and it includes Lydda, Qibya, and the Altalena.

## 1. Płońsk and Warsaw, 1886-1906

- Born David Grün, 16 October 1886, Płońsk, Congress Poland, to Avigdor and Scheindel Grün, an early-Zionist household; his mother dies in 1897 after a stillbirth. [A: Wikipedia, "David Ben-Gurion"]
- At fourteen he and two friends form the youth club **Ezra**, for spoken Hebrew and emigration. [A: same] *Contradiction internal to the pool:* chronology lens 1903, geography lens 1900. Wikipedia's "At the age of 14 … a youth club, Ezra", with an October 1886 birth, yields 1900/1901. The file carries 1900; 1903 is recorded here rather than dropped.
- Warsaw, 1904: fails the Wawelberg school entrance, teaches Hebrew; joins **Poale Zion** October 1905; briefly arrested in the 1905 unrest for political activity and weapons possession. [A: Jewish Virtual Library; Wikipedia. Arrest count and durations unfixed.]
- *Coordinate flag:* the pool's note read "present-day Płock/Płońsk town center." Płock is a different city, 70 km southwest. The pin (52.6299/20.3789) is sound, about 800 m north of Płońsk center; the file keeps it and strikes the note. [A: Wikipedia, "Płońsk"]

## 2. The Second Aliyah, 1906-1910

- Jaffa, 7 September 1906: "Today at the ninth hour I alighted on the shore of Jaffa." [A: Jewish Virtual Library]
- Day labor in the citrus groves of Petah Tikva, irregular, badly paid, punishing [A: Wikipedia]; further field labor at Rishon LeZion [A], Kfar Saba [R: dossier synthesis, not re-verified], Zichron Yaakov [A]. Compressed into one itinerant-labor stop rather than three pins; Kfar Saba's weaker tag is declared there.
- Płońsk, 1908: returns for conscription, deserts, re-enters Palestine on false papers. [A: Wikipedia]
- Sejera (today the moshav Ilaniya): takes part in Jewish self-defense; two Jewish deaths there are documented in 1909 [A: Wikipedia]. His own later account of the guard episode is flagged in the literature as heroized. *Span contradiction:* geography lens 1907-1909 against Wikipedia, "Ilaniya", "13 months from 1908"; the file dates 1908 and names it. *Coordinate correction:* the pool's 32.7167/35.4167 lies 4.7 km south-southeast of the moshav; Ilaniya is 32.7583/35.4019. [A: Wikipedia, "Ilaniya"]
- Jerusalem, 1910: joins *Ha-Aḥdut* and first signs his writing **Ben-Gurion**. [A: Jewish Virtual Library; Wikipedia] The editorial office is not identified anywhere reachable; the pin is the Old City area. **Gap.**

## 3. Ottoman law

- Salonika, 1911: about eight months of Turkish with Yitzhak Ben-Zvi, preparing to press Zionist claims through Ottoman law. Istanbul, Darülfünun, 1912-1914: enrols in law; the Balkan Wars, money, and the war prevent a degree. March 1915: deported by the Ottomans with Ben-Zvi, travelling via Egypt to the United States. [A: Wikipedia]
- The Darülfünun coordinate (41.0106/28.9640) was **not** used: the atlas already carries a shared Constantinople pin at **41.0138/28.9497** (Herzl's 1896 Yildiz audience, Jabotinsky's 1909 years), inherited byte-identically and declared in the stop.

## 4. America and the Legion, 1915-1919

- New York, 1915-1918: organizes Poale Zion, tours American Jewish communities, researches at the New York Public Library [A: Wikipedia]; co-authors with Ben-Zvi the Yiddish *Eretz Yisroel in fergangenheit un gegenvart*, 1918 [R: bibliographic record standard in the biographies; not held in hand]. Marries **Paula Munweis**, 1917 [A: Jewish Virtual Library]. *Gap:* the pool fixes only the year; the file dates 1917-01-01 and says so.
- Fort Edward, Windsor, Nova Scotia, May 1918: enlists in the Jewish Legion and trains there. [A: Wikipedia, "David Ben-Gurion"; "Fort Edward (Nova Scotia)"] *Three-way contradiction, unresolved:* the battalion is the 38th ("David Ben-Gurion"), the 39th ("Fort Edward", which puts 1100 Jewish troops under Lt Col Eliezer Margolin there and quotes Ben-Gurion on becoming a corporal at Windsor), or the 40th ("Jewish Legion"). The 38th was British-raised and trained in England, so the Ben-Gurion article contradicts its own sentence. **The file names no battalion number.**
- Cairo, 1918: dysentery keeps him out of the Legion's advance; he guards Ottoman prisoners. Sarafand, 1918: goes absent without leave to see friends in Jaffa, demoted from corporal to private and fined. [A: Wikipedia]
- Jaffa, November 1919: demobilized, reunited with Paula and Geula. *Corrected:* the chronology lens read "Daughter Geula born … 1919-11." Geula was born 11 September 1918 in New York while he was in the Legion; November 1919 is the reunion, the child already about fourteen months old. [A: Wikipedia, "Paula Ben-Gurion"; "David Ben-Gurion"]

## 5. The Histadrut and the Agency, 1919-1942

- **Ahdut HaAvoda** founded March 1919 [A: Wikipedia]. *Gap:* the pool gives the place only as "Palestine"; the file pins Tel Aviv-Jaffa as the movement's center, an approximation, and says so.
- **Histadrut** founded December 1920, conceived not as a trade union but as a federation carrying health, construction, settlement, and defense; Secretary-General 1921-1935; **Mapai** formed 1930. [A: Wikipedia; Jewish Virtual Library] Pinned at central Haifa; the congress hall is unconfirmed. **Gap.**
- Ben-Gurion House, 17 Ben-Gurion Boulevard, Tel Aviv. *Corrected twice.* The geography lens gave 32.0809/34.7806 and "1931-1973." The house is at **32.0853/34.7717**, 1.1 km northwest and in another block, and the residence runs June 1931 to 1953. [A: Wikipedia, "Ben-Gurion House"] The pool's afterlife lens already held the correct pin, so its two lenses disagreed.
- London, 1934: meetings with **Ze'ev Jabotinsky**, brokered with Pinhas Rutenberg, producing short-lived labor-violence accords the Histadrut membership then voted down. He had called Jabotinsky "Vladimir Hitler" in print beforehand. [A: via `ze_ev_jabotinsky.journey.json`]
- Chairman of the Jewish Agency Executive, 1935; supports negotiating from the Peel partition proposal, 1937. [A: JVL]
- The letter to his son Amos, 5 October 1937, with crossed-out Hebrew later central to the "expel the Arabs" controversy. [R: contested manuscript; the Morris / Karsh dispute is carried as a dispute, not settled.] *Gap:* where he wrote it is unfixed; the file pins his Tel Aviv house.
- "Fight Hitler as if there were no White Paper, and the White Paper as if there were no war," 1939. [R: repeated throughout the literature; no primary transcript located] Biltmore Program, New York, May 1942. [A: standard record]
- *Opposition, recorded as opposition.* Martin Buber's Brit Shalom and later Ihud circles argued binationalism against exactly the statist course Ben-Gurion drove through the Agency. [R: inferred from `martin_buber.journey.json`; Buber's file never names Ben-Gurion in the passages checked.] A standing antagonism, not a shared scene, and written as one.

## 6. The state, 1947-1953

- 29 November 1947: the UN General Assembly votes for partition, 33-13-10. *Conflation corrected:* the chronology lens read "The Jewish Agency … accepts the UN Partition Plan" at that date. That is the vote, not an acceptance; Wikipedia's partition article gives no acceptance date, only that the Agency accepted "reluctantly" and "with misgivings." [A: Wikipedia, "United Nations Partition Plan for Palestine"] The file stages no acceptance ceremony.
- 14 May 1948, 16 Rothschild Boulevard: proclaims the state, signs first among thirty-seven. [A: Wikipedia; Declaration text, Avalon Project, Yale Law School] The Declaration names Herzl "the spiritual father of the Jewish State," the textual tie to `theodor_herzl.journey.json` [A]. Herzl died in 1904, two years before Ben-Gurion reached Palestine; they never met, and the file writes the descent as institutional. [R]
- His diary for that day, released as images by the Ben-Gurion Heritage Institute, 28 April 2025: "At 4 p.m., Jewish independence was declared and the state was founded," and "I mourn among the joyful, as on November 29." [A: Times of Israel] The original had not been located when the images were released.
- 17 May 1948: Prime Minister and Defense Minister [A: Wikipedia]. June 1948, the **Altalena**: he orders state forces to compel the Irgun ship's surrender; shelling sets it ablaze off Tel Aviv, killing sixteen Irgun members and three IDF soldiers. [A: standard 1948-war scholarship; Gruweis Kovalsky 2023 per the dossier]
- July 1948: Israeli forces carry out the removal of the Arab population of **Lydda and Ramle** in Operation Dani. [A: Morris, *The Birth of the Palestinian Refugee Problem 1947-1949*]
- 1950: the Knesset and ministries transfer to Jerusalem. The canonical Jerusalem pin **31.778/35.2354**, used by `golda_meir.journey.json` and `martin_buber.journey.json`, is inherited byte-identically.
- November 1952, Rehovot: Weizmann's funeral. [R: attendance likely given his office, unconfirmed against a primary source] After the death he instructed Ambassador Abba Eban to offer **Einstein** the presidency at Princeton; Einstein declined by return letter, 18 November 1952. [A: via `einstein.journey.json`] The Princeton visit is Eban's and Einstein's and is **not** staged as a scene here, only named as Ben-Gurion's own instruction, from his own pin.

## 7. Sde Boker and the second premiership, 1953-1963

- October 1953, **Qibya**. After the murder of a mother and two children at Yehud, Unit 101 under Ariel Sharon attacks Qibya and kills 69 Palestinians. Ben-Gurion publicly and falsely denies IDF involvement. [A: Sharett's diary and standard Qibya scholarship, per the dossier]
- 7 December 1953: first premiership ends; he retires to Kibbutz **Sde Boker**, presenting his own pioneering as a national example while still shaping defense affairs. [A: Wikipedia; Jewish Virtual Library] *Coordinate correction:* the pool's 30.8811/34.7783 is 1.7 km off [A: Wikipedia, "Sde Boker"]. The pool blurred three Negev points; the file keeps them apart: kibbutz 30.8736/34.7931, hut 30.8742/34.7892, grave 30.8522/34.7833.
- 21 February 1955: Defense Minister again; 3 November 1955: Prime Minister again, to 1963 [A: Wikipedia]. 18 June 1956: moves **Golda Meir** to the Foreign Ministry and instructs her, as he did the rest of the diplomatic service, to Hebraize her surname. [A: via `golda_meir.journey.json`]
- October 1956, the **Sèvres protocol**: secret coordination with France and Britain; on 29 October Israel invades the Sinai. [A: Wikipedia] The villa is not pinpointed anywhere reachable; the pin is Sèvres town center. **Gap.**
- Lessons with **Moshe Feldenkrais** from 1956, after conventional treatment for chronic back pain failed [R: dossier-cited National Library of Israel materials, not independently reachable]; the photograph is by Paul Goldman, 1957 [A: Times of Israel]. *Two contradictions, neither resolved.* Location: geography lens Herzliya beach (32.1624/34.7967), `moshe_feldenkrais.journey.json` a Tel Aviv beach (32.0853/34.7818), the statue at Frishman Beach (32.0809/34.7679); the file takes the Herzliya pin and names the other two. Pose: headstand, handstand, and "inverted" all appear; the file says inverted.
- 29 October 1957: a grenade is thrown into the Knesset chamber at Froumine House; both Ben-Gurion and Golda Meir are wounded, he in the leg. [A: via `golda_meir.journey.json`] Pin inherited byte-identically: **31.778/35.2199**.
- 11 May 1960: Mossad agents abduct **Adolf Eichmann** in Buenos Aires on his authorization [A: Wikipedia, "Capture of Adolf Eichmann"]; on 23 May he announces it to the Knesset. *Corrected:* the chronology lens gave 22 May, the date Eichmann arrived, the announcement coming "the following afternoon" [A: Wikipedia, "Adolf Eichmann"]. *Minor, unresolved:* the geography lens pins Beit Frumin at 31.7784/35.2213, `golda_meir.journey.json` at 31.778/35.2199; the file inherits the interlock pin for both Froumine House stops.
- 1961, the Lavon Affair: resigns, returns after an election. [A: Jewish Virtual Library; the dates cluster in 1961 with no agreed day in the pool] 1963, the last resignation. *Both readings kept:* the pool gave 16 June, Wikipedia's infobox 26 June. The 16th is the announcement, the 26th is when the office passed to Levi Eshkol. [A: Wikipedia] The file dates the announcement and states both.

## 8. Last years

- The Sde Boker hut, home permanently after 1963, preserved by the terms of his will and opened as a museum. [A: Wikipedia, "Ben-Gurion's Hut"]
- 1964: the Mount Herzl reburial of Jabotinsky, refused for fifteen years on the stated ground that the country needed "living Jews, not bones," proceeds under Eshkol. [A: via `ze_ev_jabotinsky.journey.json`] The ceremony is Jabotinsky's stop; the refusal is Ben-Gurion's, and sits in his own London 1934 campa.
- 1965: splits from Mapai, forms **Rafi** with Moshe Dayan and Shimon Peres. [A: standard political history]
- January 1968: Paula Ben-Gurion dies. [A: Segev per the dossier] *Gap:* the pool gives the place only as "Israel"; the file pins Tel Aviv. 27 May 1970: leaves the Knesset; *corrected*, the pool said June 1970. [A: Wikipedia]
- 18 November 1973: cerebral hemorrhage, hospitalized at Sheba Medical Center; dies there 1 December 1973, aged 87 [A: Wikipedia]. Buried beside Paula at what becomes Ben-Gurion Tomb National Park, Midreshet Ben-Gurion, on a cliff over the Zin Valley, by his own request. [A: Wikipedia, "Midreshet Ben-Gurion"; 30.8522/34.7833]

## Placement decisions

Three stops carry events at which he was not personally present. Each is his own act or his own government's operation, not another traveler's engagement with a place, so the rule against staging other people's scenes does not exclude them; the absence is stated in each stop's `date_confidence`. **Lydda, July 1948:** he was at headquarters; the stop is pinned at Lod because the geography is the substance of the event, and *the pool supplied no Lydda coordinate*, so the file uses 31.9514/34.8953, Lod town center, labeled as not from the pool. **Qibya, October 1953:** pinned in Jerusalem, at the government, because the stop is the false denial; the broadcast site is unfixed in the pool. **The Altalena, June 1948:** pinned on the Tel Aviv shoreline at 32.0809/34.7679, the August 2026 wreck discovery going to `suggested_refs`.
