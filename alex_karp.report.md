Legend: **[A]** attested, source named inline · **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, self-report or dossier-only · *gap* checked and not found. Where a correction in the pool overturned a dossier claim, this report follows the correction.

## 3. Frankfurt, c.1995-2001, and the doctorate of 2002

The phase where the dossier chronology was worst.

**Arrival.** The dossier said **1994**. en.wikipedia: "He lived in Frankfurt from the mid-1990s to 2001 and considered staying permanently, but ultimately decided to go back to the U.S. for work"; de.wikipedia agrees **[A]**. So arrival c.1995, departure 2001, not 2002. Arrived without German **[A as recollection, Brede interview]**.

**The Habermas question: contradiction flagged, not resolved.** English Wikipedia has stated plainly that Karp studied philosophy *under* **Jürgen Habermas** and took the doctorate under him. German Wikipedia names **Karola Brede** as *Doktorvater* while Habermas *taught* him, and en.wikipedia's current text lists Brede as referee; the dossier's correction, from a 2026 Brede interview, agrees with the German edition. So: Habermas taught him **[A]**, did not supervise **[A]**, and the popular line is erroneous. A **second strain is chronological and left open**: Habermas held the Frankfurt chair until 1994 and Karp arrived c.1995, so contact falls at the end of that tenure or in the emeritus years, and no source fixes a date, course or seminar.

**Sigmund Freud Institute.** The dossier put a research post there in **2002, "following his doctorate."** Both halves fail on the sources' own account: de.wikipedia places the assistantship *during* doctoral study, and en.wikipedia has him gone from Frankfurt in 2001. Re-dated to the later 1990s **[A]**; content (standing in for Brede's post while she was a visiting professor abroad; groups on authoritarianism, sociology, psychoanalysis) **[A, Brede interview]**. *Coordinate correction:* the dossier pin sits ~800 m east of Myliusstraße 20, Westend, the institute's home since 14 October 1964. Journey uses **50.1197, 8.6608**.

**The dissertation, 2002 [A, en. and de.wikipedia]:** *Aggression in der Lebenswelt: Die Erweiterung des Parsonschen Konzepts der Aggression durch die Beschreibung des Zusammenhangs von Jargon, Aggression und Kultur*. First examiner **Karola Brede**, second **Hans-Joachim Busch**, *magna cum laude*. *Gap:* it was **not read**; d-nb.info/966060652/34 returned **404**. No English edition exists, and the circulating English title is a journalists' rendering **[R, a documented negative]**.

*Campus pin.* The dossier used the IG Farben building (50.1273, 8.6656), a campus that did not open to the humanities until 2001, the year Karp left. Journey uses **50.1191, 8.652**, Bockenheim.

**Caedmon Group, London, c.2002 [R].** en.wikipedia places it in London and gives the seed as a **$12,000 inheritance from his paternal grandfather [A]**. Domicile, assets and dissolution could not be established and no filing was reached, so the pin is an explicit placeholder.

## 5. The decade of contradictions, 2010-2019

**HBGary Federal, 2010-2011 [A, en.wikipedia 'Palantir Technologies']:** a Palantir employee implicated with HBGary Federal and Berico Technologies in a proposal to discredit WikiLeaks supporters and the journalist Glenn Greenwald; Karp apologizes publicly and severs ties. No evidence he authorized it. The dossier pinned Sacramento while conceding Karp is not documented there; the stop sits at Palo Alto instead.

**Foundry and the commercial turn, c.2016 [R]**; the launch year is not pinned to a primary source.

**Department of Labor, 2016-2017 [R as to date and pin]:** the allegation of discrimination against Asian applicants in engineering hiring, and the ~$1.7m settlement without admission, from dossier-cited DoL materials plus en.wikipedia. An administrative proceeding, not a personal visit.

**Axel Springer, dossier corrected twice [A, de.wikipedia]:** joined the **supervisory board (Aufsichtsrat)** in **2018**, left it **January 2020**, moved to the **shareholders' committee**, served until it was **dissolved in 2025**. Fixed: "supervisory/executive board" conflates two organs distinct in a German SE, and Karp was never on the management board; and the role did not end in 2020, it changed form. **Contradiction flagged:** en.wikipedia gives "director 2018-2020, shareholders committee **2022**-2025", a two-year gap the German edition lacks. The dossier's "rejoins in 2022" item is that English version, not a third event.

**BASF [A, de.wikipedia]:** appointed **3 May 2019**, departed **July 2020**; the dossier's "2010s" is wrong at the end. The pin 49.4771, 8.4353 is the city of Ludwigshafen, not the works complex north of it.

**Politics, 2018 [A]:** identifies publicly as socialist and progressive, says he voted for the Democratic candidate in 2016, does not follow Thiel into support for Donald Trump. No single date or venue attaches, so the stop is an editorial placement.
