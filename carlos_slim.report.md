# Carlos Slim Helú: research report
*Prepared 2026-08-23 for the Atlas of Journeys. Subject living (b. 28 January 1940, Mexico City). Slug `carlos_slim`.*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named · **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or dossier-only · *gap* looked for, not found, left open.

A living subject changes the report's shape once: no tomb, no death, no posthumous cult. The afterlife lens returns instead monuments raised in his own lifetime, books written while he can answer them, and legends around a man still adding to the record.

## 1. The father's crossing (1888-1914)

- **Jezzine, Mount Lebanon, 17 July 1888 [A: en-wiki].** Julián Slim Haddad born Khalil Salim Haddad Aglamaz; stated motive for emigration is escape from Ottoman conscription.
- **Veracruz and Tampico, 1902 [R].** The family narrative has him arrive by ship at fourteen, alone, without Spanish, joining four brothers who reached Mexico in 1898. *The port of entry is not independently confirmed.*
- **La Estrella de Oriente, Centro Histórico [R].** **The family's own English and Spanish biographies give 1904 and 1911 for the founding.** Not adjudicated; the file uses 1904 and states the conflict. The May 1914 buyout of José's half and the Revolution-era property buying are family-archive figures [R].
- **Hidalgo del Parral [A birthplace, R press].** Linda Helú Atta was **"born in Parral, Chihuahua"** (en-wiki), upgraded from the dossier's [R]; coordinates 26.93, -105.6667 correct. **The printing claim is looser than the dossier's:** en-wiki says her parents "founded one of the first Arabic-language magazines," not that José Helú imported an early press. *Her birth year is not established anywhere reviewed;* the file's 1908 is a declared ordering device.

## 2. Formation (1940-1961)

- **Birth 28 January 1940, fifth of six [A: en-wiki; Britannica]. Julián dies 1953, Carlos thirteen [A: Britannica],** exact date not established.
- **The allowance and the notebook [A, Britannica error corrected].** Britannica compresses two purchases and misnames the bank. Per en-wiki: **savings bond at eleven** (1951); **first stock purchase at twelve**, in an **unnamed** Mexican bank; only **by fifteen** a shareholder in Mexico's largest bank, i.e. Banamex, c. 1955. **Banamex must not be attached to the age-eleven or age-twelve purchase.** The allowance-plus-written-account practice is tradition [R], corroborated in profiles, not from a primary document.
- **UNAM, Facultad de Ingeniería [A, two corrections].** Civil engineering; he teaches algebra and linear programming in the same faculty as a student. **Date conflict:** carlosslim.com dates the degree 1961, es-wiki gives 1958-1962; both stated. **Coordinates corrected** to about 19.3327, -99.1836, the dossier's pin lying about 350 m southwest in CU green space.

## 3. The broker (1965-1980)

- **Inmobiliaria Carso, January 1966 [A, dossier name wrong].** Not "Inmuebles Carso"; the month is documented. Car (Carlos) plus so (Soumaya).
- **Marriage to Soumaya: contradiction, flagged.** carlosslim.com and es-wiki give **1966**, en-wiki and Britannica **1967**; the dossier tagged 1967 [A], overstating it. If 1966 is right the company was named for her *in the same year as* the wedding, and the afterlife lens's "the year before he married" construction collapses.

The dossier's "1970s" **is unsupported by any source located**, and its "first president" overstates a source saying only "chaired." The file's date is a declared ordering device.

## 4. Buying in the crisis (1982-1990)

- **1982 [A: Britannica].** Peso collapse, bank nationalization, capital flight; he buys distressed but operating companies with cash. **Seguros de México, 1984, about US$13 million [A].**
- **Sanborns and the Casa de los Azulejos: the dossier's date is wrong by twelve years.** es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanborns: **"En el año de 1985, la empresa fue adquirida por el Grupo Carso."** The dossier's 1997 is the **Sears Roebuck de México** year. The Sanborn brothers bought the House of Tiles in **1917**, so Slim acquired the chain that already held the building. Coordinates 19.4342, -99.1402 exact.
- **Cigatam [R].** Dated by the dossier only to "the 1980s," no site finer than the capital; the file uses 1986 to order the segment and says so. The 2007 sale of the 50.1% stake to Philip Morris International for about US$1.1 billion is [R].
- **Nacobre and Química Fluor, 1988 [R: Britannica].** **Grupo Carso lists 1990, Porcelanite the same year [A: Britannica];** the June date and the renaming at listing are [R].

A one-directional relation of the licensed kind, sitting on Slim's own stop, in his own office, at no Rockefeller pin. **Refused.** `emin_arslan`, twice (shared Ottoman push-factor; diaspora Arabic press): same current, no crossing, no contact. `diego_rivera` (Trotsky killed in Coyoacán seven months after Slim's birth): coincidence of city and year. `frida_kahlo` (same city, Slim later a patron of its art): she died in 1954, within his adult life, and **no record of contact was found.** `cortes` (Tenochtitlan pins inside blocks the Historic Center trust later restored): geographic overlap the map already shows. `mayer_amschel_rothschild`: the "richest man" motif, thematic only. `adnan_khashoggi`: **no evidence they met or transacted.** Allen Dulles and William Casey, offered as Cold War counterparts to the brother's DFS career: that relation is the brother's.

**`genghis_khan`, the near-miss.** The dossier says Slim's library holds material on him, formally the *same* reading relation that licensed Rockefeller. Refused twice over: the fetched Osorno text does not confirm the Genghis Khan detail, and Rockefeller is a model Slim is plainly working from where Genghis Khan is a book on a shelf. **A judgement call, recorded as one**, overturnable either way.

**Bill Gates** is a genuine direct relation (co-funder with the Slim and Telmex Foundations of Salud Mesoamérica in 2010 and the 2013 polio pledge; the man he passed on the list), but **no `bill_gates.journey.json` exists**, so he cannot be an interlock; he is named in campa prose as an ordinary historical person. **Trump, Ochs-Sulzberger, López Obrador, Sheinbaum:** searched, **no journey files exist.**
