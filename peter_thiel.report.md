# Peter Thiel: research report

*2026-08-24. Peter Andreas Thiel, b. Frankfurt am Main 11 October 1967, living. Slug `peter_thiel`. Accompanies `peter_thiel.journey.json`: 8 segments, 45 stops, 12 quotes.*

**[A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or single-source dossier claim not independently confirmed. Contradictions are recorded, not adjudicated. Unreachable sources are named with the reason. Caveats not repeated here stand in the relevant stop's `date_confidence`.

The subject is **alive**, aged fifty-eight. No tomb, no relic, no posthumous reception: the afterlife lens is necessarily anticipatory, which is a gap in the lens and not in the record, and nothing was manufactured to fill it. The legends that do exist are legends about a living man, section 7.

## 6. The Antichrist material and the South, 2022-2026

The least verifiable stretch of the file: every stop from Wanaka onward is **[R]** except the Meta board departure of 7 February 2022, which is **[A]** but placeless and sits at the Los Angeles base by declaration.

[A] for the land, [R] for the litigation; the bunker is a media label.** Wikipedia, 'Wanaka' has Thiel owning "a 193-hectare estate to the north of the Wanaka township" and gives the township at **-44.700 / 169.150**, which is what the file uses: the township, not the estate, which was not located. **Four Antichrist lectures, San Francisco, September-October 2025. [R]** Nominally off the record, with recordings and notes leaking to The Washington Post and The Guardian. **The venue was never located and none of the reporting was independently retrieved**: every fetch failed or 404'd and WebSearch was exhausted. **Rome, near the Vatican, March 2026. [R]** No venue located; PBS News, America Magazine and the National Catholic Reporter are cited in the pool, not fetched.

### The quotation corrections, all going the same way

The dossier attributed four passages to "Against Edenism, First Things, November 2025". No such essay exists under that date. "Christians debated these prophecies for millennia..." is from Thiel and Sam Wolfe, **"Voyages to the End of the World", First Things, November 2025**. The other three ("The future will look very different from the past..."; "We cannot change this reality..."; "The reign of science foretold in New Atlantis...") are all from **"Against Edenism", First Things, June/July 2015**. "Antichrist" does not appear in that essay, nor do "prophec-", "millennia", "preach" or "arrive", and the dossier's supporting note was impossible on its face: a 2017 TechCrunch piece cannot quote a 2025 essay. Three are carried with corrected dates; "We cannot change this reality" is unused.

**One dropped entirely.** "I badly miss the misguided optimism of a Faust, at least he was motivated to try" is **truncated**, the original ending "to try to do something". Rather than silently repair a quotation, it is left out.

**One reattributed.** "We wanted flying cars, instead we got 140 characters" is **not** from a 2014 Yale commencement address, and **Thiel never gave a Yale commencement address**. It opens Founders Fund's manifesto "What Happened to the Future?" (2011), quoted in The New Yorker, 21 November 2011; the Yale Daily News used it as a headline over a 2014 campus talk, the likely origin of the error. The file cites the manifesto and names the misattribution in the quote source.

## 7. Legends about a living man

All **[R]**, all named as legends, none staged as fact.

- **The doomsday bunker.** Substrate: citizenship, 193 hectares, a lodge consent application under ordinary planning law. No reachable source confirms an underground structure.
- **The vampire billionaire.** Transfusions from young donors, out of 2016-17 reporting on his stated interest in parabiosis. **Thiel has denied personally undergoing transfusions**, and the originating reporting could not be re-verified. **Not used in the journey file at all**, recorded here so its absence is a decision and not an oversight.
- **The Antichrist meme cycle, 2025-26.** Subcultures recast Thiel *as* the Antichrist; the substrate is the inverse, he having lectured on the Antichrist as a figure to fear. The file carries the lectures, not the meme.
- **The flight to Buenos Aires.** Documented: a purchase, a multi-month stay, a child in school, a meeting with Milei. Not documented: motive or permanence.

## 9. Apparatus relations for the operator

**Peter Thiel is not listed in `EXCEPTIONS.md`.** Per the standing instruction, relations not rooted in his own life are flagged here rather than deleted.

**Nothing in this pool required an apparatus reading, backward or forward, and none was written.** Thiel is alive and the whole file is his own biography. Three relations were nonetheless one-directional or place-only, and the decisions are set out so the operator can see them.

1. **Nick Land to Thiel. Direction: backward, legal with no exception needed.** Land's Dark Enlightenment cites Thiel's 2009 essay: a later writer citing an earlier one is a real one-directional relation, and Thiel's file is the *later* block, so embedding the 2012 serialisation is the ordinary case. Land is **named** in the Cato-essay campa. `nick_land.journey.json` was **not touched** and must not be. Softness noted: Wikipedia dates only the 2022 book edition and gives no year for the coinage, so the campa says "some years later" rather than an interval. Also corrected: **the Shanghai placement for 2012 is unsupported**, Land's article attesting his residence there only "as of 2017". Nothing in the Thiel file places Land anywhere.
2. **Curtis Yarvin.** Thiel's patronage is documented in outside reporting (Joshua Tait's "most important connection"; "house political philosopher"; Thiel and Andreessen investing in Tlon). Yarvin is **named** in the Cato-essay campa as the author of the blog Land worked up. He is **queued, not researched** (QUEUE.md line 148 already logs the Thiel link), so he is not counted as an atlas interlock. **Date correction:** the pool anchored the relation to 2010, but **Tlon was not founded until 2013**. The file makes no dated claim about the funding.
3. **Erich Fromm and Murray Rothbard: rejected deliberately.** Fromm shares Frankfurt as a birth city 67 years earlier, on the same pin. Rothbard shares Ayn Rand and the American libertarian current. **Neither is named anywhere in the journey file.** Shared geography is not a relation and the map already shows co-location; the Rand overlap is a tradition, not a link between two people. Recorded so the operator can see they were considered and dropped on the rule, not missed.

**Two gaps for the operator.** `javier_milei.journey.json` has Casa Rosada stops ending 2025-02-26 and **does not carry the 23 April 2026 meeting with Thiel**. That is a gap in the Milei file, not filled from here: the meeting is written into Thiel's file because Thiel was there. Whether Milei's file gains a matching stop is the operator's call, and it would be a legal one, both men living. Separately, **René Girard has no journey file in this checkout**, so the whole Stanford formation cluster (Girard, Hamerton-Kelly, the Review, David Sacks, Palaver) inherits **zero** atlas interlocks.

**Interlocks used**, each on a documented person-to-person relation and named in a campa: `elon_musk` (the September 2000 board removal that made Thiel CEO; pin **37.4443 / -122.1598** inherited byte-identical); `nick_land` (cites Thiel's 2009 essay; named, not placed); `javier_milei` (received Thiel at the Casa Rosada; pin **-34.6083 / -58.3712** inherited byte-identical).
