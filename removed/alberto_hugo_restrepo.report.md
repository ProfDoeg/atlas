# Alberto Hugo Restrepo Saldarriaga (birth unrecorded - 2025): research report

*Compiled 2026-08-12 for the Atlas of Journeys. Slug: `alberto_hugo_restrepo`.*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named · **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or inference · contradictions are flagged and **not adjudicated**. Hedges live in the journey file's `date_confidence` fields, never in campa prose.

No scholarly literature, no encyclopedia entry, no archive. The record is: one gallery's house copy replicated across three commercial directories; one podcast show-notes page; two newspaper pieces; one blog tribute; a handful of social posts. The verification pass overturned roughly a third of the first round, mostly by fetching pages known only from search snippets. Every correction is adopted with the superseded claim recorded.

The largest structural problem: **the gallery's promotional text contradicts the gallery's own biographical text** about when the gallery was founded, and the contradictory text is replicated across Artsy, LinkedIn and AiYellow, where it reads as three confirmations. It is one text. Flagged in section 4, left open.

---

## 1. Origin, education, and the vocation that predates the record

**Birthplace and birth year: total gap.** No source located gives a birth date, a birth year, or a birthplace **[R]**. The first round asserted "native of Antioquia" as **[A]**; verification demoted it. What exists: the tributes call him *antioqueño*, and his second surname **Saldarriaga** is characteristically Antioquian **[A for the usage, R for the inference]**. Lifelong Medellín residence is plausible and unattested. **Left as a gap.**

**Universidad EAFIT, Medellín: Business Administration [A, Galería El Coleccionista].** Verbatim: "Es Administrador de Negocios de la Universidad EAFIT y tiene un postgrado en Mercadeo."

> **Sourcing corrected.** The first round attributed both education claims to `ahfineart.co/nosotros`. That page was fetched in full and **mentions neither EAFIT, nor Business Administration, nor a marketing postgraduate**. Both claims come from the El Coleccionista podcast page and nowhere else.

> **Overstatement corrected.** The postgraduate in Mercadeo is attested; **the institution is not**. The first round's "also at Universidad EAFIT" is removed **[R, unsupported]**.

**Galería Pluma, and a chronology that will not sit still.** The tribute reads: "Vocación cultural que nace hace más de 5 décadas, cuando Alberto se puso al frente de la galería Pluma." The first round rendered this as a gallery he founded and directed "for 5+ decades," then flagged that span as implausible. **The implausibility was an artifact of the paraphrase [R].** The five decades attach to the birth of a cultural vocation; *se puso al frente* means he took charge of Pluma, not that he founded it; Pluma's own operating span is not given. What survives: Pluma is real, he ran it, and the tribute dates the start of his cultural life to **before about 1975** **[A for the sentence, R for the arithmetic]**. This sits badly against "nearly 40 years in the art world" and against a late-1980s entry. **Flagged, not resolved.**

**Entry into the art trade: late 1980s to early 1990s [A, Galería El Coleccionista].** Verbatim: "Entró al mundo del arte a finales de los años 80 y a principios de los años 90, [cuando] se empieza a conocer como un importante consultor." The bracketed word is inserted for grammatical linkage only. No source gives a start year; the journey file uses 1988 for ordering and says so.

## 2. The Vorpal commission and the New York houses, early 1990s

**Vorpal Gallery representation for the Colombian market [A, ahfineart.co/nosotros].** The page names Vorpal's three locations, New York, San Francisco, and Laguna Beach, and says he represented them into Colombia.

> **Date corrected.** The page says only "a principios de los años 90." The first round's **1991** was an interpolation and is softened to "early 1990s" **[R for any specific year]**.

**Travel unconfirmed [R].** No source says he visited any Vorpal location in person; the representation is a commercial relationship on paper, and all three stops carry that caveat.

> **Coordinate corrected.** The first round put Vorpal New York at 40.7614, -73.9776, Midtown around 57th and Fifth. Contemporary references place Vorpal's New York space **on West Broadway in SoHo** ("Vorpal Gallery. West Broadway. SoHo, New York 1987."). SoHo's West Broadway runs near **40.725, -74.002**, four kilometres southwest. The journey file uses the SoHo coordinate and states that it is the street, not a verified number.

**Marlborough, Acquavella, James Goodman [A, ahfineart.co/nosotros].** He collaborated with these houses, advising Colombian collectors on Old Masters and modern masters. The Midtown coordinate, wrong for Vorpal, is correct for this group and is used there and only there.

**San Francisco and Laguna Beach: no addresses [R].** None was found for either outpost; the journey file uses city-level coordinates and says so.

## 3. Bogotá, the Paris Mint, and the European trade, from 1993

**Bogotá, first art office, 1993, oriented to the European market [A, ahfineart.co/nosotros].** **Representation of the Monnaie de Paris (Casa de la Moneda) in Colombia [A, same page]**, undated there; the journey file places it with the Bogotá office and hedges. **Picasso, Dalí, Miró sold to Latin American collectors [A, same page].**

> **Provenance and date corrected.** The first round had these works "acquired from Brussels and Paris" in 1990. The page **names no acquisition point and gives no date**. Brussels, Paris-as-source, and 1990 are struck **[R, unsupported]**, and no Brussels stop appears in the journey file.

**France, Spain, the United States: three countries, no cities [R].** An aggregated biography lists these as markets where he "traveled and searched the international market," distinct from the Vorpal relationship. No city, venue, fair, or date is named anywhere, and whether they overlap with the Vorpal cities is not established. One Madrid stop carries the whole indictment in its `date_confidence`. **Gap.**

## 4. AH Fine Art: the founding date that does not close

**The contradiction, stated plainly and not adjudicated.**

- **1991**, "fundada en 1991, bajo la dirección de Alberto Hugo Restrepo, quien dio inicio a un espacio que ofrece una exposición permanente de Maestros Colombianos y Extranjeros" **[A, AiYellow; same date on Artsy]**.
- **Early twenty-first century**, "A principios del siglo XXI funda la Galería AH Fine Art" **[A, ahfineart.co/nosotros; corroborated by El Coleccionista]**.

> **Both sides are the gallery.** This is not an outside claim against an inside claim. Artsy is a partner-supplied profile, AiYellow a paid listing, LinkedIn a company page: the **identical 1991-plus-500-works wording recurs across all three**, so they are one gallery-supplied text replicated, not three confirmations. `ahfineart.co/nosotros` is also the gallery. It contradicts itself across two of its own registers by roughly a decade. **Not resolved.** The journey file carries both foundings as separate stops, each dated to its own source, each naming the other.

**Likely origin of the confusion, offered as [R] and nothing more.** The `nosotros` page describes **his career** as "más de 30 años de experiencia en el sector," not the gallery's age. A reader collapsing career length into gallery age lands near 1991. A hypothesis, not a finding.

**Permanent collection of approximately 500 works, classical, modern and contemporary [A, Artsy and AiYellow]**, subject to the replication caveat above.

**Addresses, two of them, partially reconciled.**

- **Calle 10 #43C-71, El Poblado**: the contact address published on `ahfineart.co` today **[A]**. OSM puts Calle 10 at 6.2093, -75.5670; the journey file uses 6.2085, -75.567, within about 100 metres.
- **San Fernando Plaza, El Poblado**: where *El Tiempo* placed the gallery for the Ospina installation of 21 November 2017 **[A]**.

> **Corrected in two directions.** The first round stated San Fernando Plaza as the gallery's location without qualification; it should be dated to about 2017, not given as the current address. And its coordinate, 6.2095, -75.5701, is off by roughly 600 metres: OSM places San Fernando Plaza (Calle 1 Sur, Patio Bonito) at **6.2041, -75.5709**. **Which address superseded which, and when, no source establishes. Gap.**

**Residence: Calle 10, El Poblado, from 1993, with his son Santiago, in one of the old *casonas* [A, Vivir en El Poblado, "En la calle 10, de puertas para dentro"].** The URL returned 404; the claim rests on a search snippet. **Family [A, blog tribute]:** three children, **Paulina, Camila, Santiago**; a close friendship kept with his former partner **Irene Acevedo** after their separation.

## 5. The trade in person, 2011 to 2022

**El Tiempo, "Los tesoros de AH Fine Art," 11 October 2011 [A].**

> **Date fixed and 404 removed.** The first round could not reach the article and inferred "circa 2011-2013" from the "23 years" figure. It was located at `eltiempo.com/archivo/documento/MAM-4888154`, dated **11 October 2011**. Verbatim: "Alberto Hugo Restrepo Saldarriaga lleva 23 años metido en el mundo del arte. Él, un especialista en mercadeo, vive de las dos grandes pasiones de su vida." It adds that the gallery operates **privately, by appointment**, and holds **Rayo, Obregón, Ospina and Botero**.

The arithmetic: 2011 minus 23 lands on **1988**, agreeing with "finales de los años 80" and with the "casi 40 años" of the 2025 memorial post. It does not agree with the tribute's fifty-year vocation, nor with a 1991 gallery founded by a man three years into the trade. **Flagged.**

**Nadín Ospina represented; significant works by Andrés de Santamaría dealt [A, blog tribute].** *El Tiempo*, 21 Nov 2017, places Ospina's "Arte de Magia" at San Fernando Plaza **[A]**.

**XXXIV Subasta de Arte Cariño, curator [R, uncorroborated].** The venue is settled: the auditorium of the **Laboratorio Clínico Hematológico, Patio Bonito, Carrera 43C No. 5-33, El Poblado**, per the auction's own event copy **[A]**.

> **Coordinate corrected and tag demoted.** The first round gave 6.2015, -75.567; OSM places that building at **6.2065, -75.5731**, 850 metres away. More seriously, the **Corporación de Amor al Niño Cariño names no curator at all**. His curatorship of the XXXIV edition rests solely on gallery-side copy, so the tag is **[R]**. His quoted line about such events being "plastic, aesthetic, playful and cultural" comes from the same text.

**Television: "Más Arte Más Ciudad," on Botero and a French tribute exhibition [A, YouTube, "ALBERTO HUGO RESTREPO EN MAS ARTE MAS CIUDAD"].** **Undated;** no broadcast date was recoverable, and the journey file uses a flagged placeholder.

**Capitolio Nacional, Bogotá: Hugo Zapata in the Patio Mosquera, 19 July to 20 September 2022 [A].** "Testigos y Vigías frente a una ciudadanía en flor," the first time sculpture was installed in that space.

> **Role corrected.** The first round said Restrepo **co-curated** it. He did not. The curator was **María del Pilar Rodríguez**; the blog casts Restrepo and Paola Gutiérrez de Piñeres as *apoyo*, backers of the show **[A]**. "Co-curated" is struck.

**Museo de Arte Moderno de Cartagena de Indias, reopening [A for the involvement, corrected twice].**

> **Overstatement corrected.** The blog says the museum was marking "50 años de su fundación" and needed support for a **January** reopening: he **helped secure support**, he did not fund it, and the reopening falls in **January 2023**. The anniversary arithmetic is itself contested: the museum's own history page uses **1979** as its founding, while other accounts date the collection to **1972**, the reading that makes a 2022 cinquantenary work. **Not adjudicated.**

**Guarantor for the young [A, blog tribute].** He signed as *fiador* for young artists opening a cooperative gallery in **Barrio Colombia**, Medellín. Neither the gallery nor any artist is named; the journey file uses an approximate district point.

**The beans, and the upgrades [A, blog tribute].** He cooked *fríjoles* for friends and colleagues, and the tribute's title is built on it. He upgraded airline tickets to first class for artists in need. **No route, airline, beneficiary or year is named. Gap.**

**Punto Cadeneta [A, El Reverbero de Juan Paz].** Member of the Medellín *tertulia* presided over by **Augusto López Valencia**. No meeting venue is named. **Close friendship with the sculptor Hugo Zapata [A, blog tribute].**

**Character, as the tribute writer draws him [R].** "Seductor y hedonista," with a personal devotion to faith. One writer's characterization of a friend, days after his death.

## 6. Death, and the arithmetic left open

**Died on the morning of 22 November 2025, in Medellín [A, El Reverbero de Juan Paz].** "Esta mañana falleció en Medellín el querido amigo Alberto Hugo Restrepo, integrante de nuestra tertulia Punto Cadeneta." The companion Instagram post (@elreverberodejuanpaz) is dated **22 November 2025**.

> **Corrected.** The first round had "shortly before December 4, 2025," taking the blog tribute's publication date for the death date. The death is now pinned to the day. **Half of the first round's biggest gap is closed; the birth date and birth year remain genuinely unlocated.**

> **Access caveat retained.** The obituary body on `juanpaz.net` returned **HTTP 403 Forbidden**. Only the title and a search-engine snippet were recoverable; the snippet is the sentence quoted above.

**AH Fine Art memorial post, 24 November 2025 [A].** "Nuestro querido fundador y director, el señor Alberto Hugo Restrepo Saldarriaga, dedicó casi 40 años de su vida al mundo del arte," retrieved via the gallery's Facebook mirror.

> **Date corrected and ambiguity resolved.** The first round dated it vaguely to 2025-12 and flagged as unresolved whether it marked a death, a retirement, or an anniversary. It falls **two days after his death**, so the past-tense homage is memorial. **Flag removed.**

**"Réquiem a unos fríjoles impresionistas," 4 December 2025 [A]**, on `personajespormapyrosa.blogspot.com`. The single richest source on the man as opposed to the dealer, and a friend's eulogy, not a biography.

> **A first-round judgement softened.** The first round concluded that "nearly all indexed material is commercial gallery promotion" and that no mythologizing narrative exists. The *Réquiem* is precisely that: it builds him into a figure through the beans, the first-class upgrades, the guarantor's signature in Barrio Colombia. The absence claim is **overstated**.

**The years that do not add up, all four figures kept.** "Casi 40 años" (memorial post, 2025); "23 años" (El Tiempo, 2011, implying 1988); "más de 30 años de experiencia en el sector" (ahfineart.co, undated); "más de 5 décadas" of cultural vocation (tribute, implying before 1975). Against foundings of 1991 (34 years to his death) and "principios del siglo XXI" (about 25). **No source reconciles them. Nothing here reconciles them either.**

**No published book, catalogue, or edition by him was located [R].** Sources describe him consistently as dealer, appraiser, auctioneer (*martillo de subastas*), curator, gallery director, and *editor de arte*. Gallery catalogues under the AH Fine Art imprint may exist and were not found.

**No monument, plaque, street naming, or civic memorial [R].** His largest physical trace is the gallery itself on Calle 10. **No Wikipedia article, Spanish or English [A, in-site search of es.wikipedia, 423 results, none matching].** A Pinterest account "Alberto Hugo Restrepo Saldarriaga" (handle `albertohugo`) exists, authenticity unverified **[R]**, recorded as a lead.

## 7. Interlocks with the existing corpus

No file in `working/journeys` names him, and `census_real_persons_2026-08-02.md` returns zero matches for "Restrepo" **[A, grep]**. Every interlock is geographic or thematic, never documented contact.

**Inherited byte-identical:**

- **Bogotá, 4.5981, -74.076.** Earned twice: the 1993 art office and the 2022 Capitolio Nacional exhibition are both genuinely in Bogotá. **Manuela Sáenz** sits on the same node.
- **Paris, 48.8566, 2.3522.** Earned by the Monnaie de Paris representation. The pin already carries **Diego Rivera**, **Christian Dior**, and the **Bethsabée de Rothschild** confiscation on the rue Saint-Florentin.

**Declined, and why.** The lens surfaced the **Kaaba (21.4225, 39.8262)**, the **Temple Mount (31.778, 35.2354)**, and **Buenos Aires (-34.6037, -58.3816)** as available canonical pins, and flagged all three itself as having no attested connection here. Nothing in the pool puts Restrepo in Mecca, Jerusalem, or Argentina. **Not used.** A pin is inherited when a life touches it, not when a coordinate is convenient. The Bogotá pin's **Bolívar** entry of 10 August 1819, **Policarpa Salavarrieta's** execution of 1817, **Camilo Torres Restrepo's** birth of 3 February 1929, and **Che Guevara's** transit of July 1952 were originally named in the two Bogotá campas as well; removed under the house-style tightening of 2026-08-14, since none of the four has any documented bearing on Restrepo's own life beyond sharing the same coordinate decades or centuries apart.

**Thematic, not documented.** The lens raised **Griselda Blanco** (queued, no file) and **Agha Hasan Abedi**, whose BCCI file records Colombian cartel money moving through the bank's Tampa division. Nothing connects Restrepo to any such money; the parallel is not carried into the journey file. **Surname note:** Restrepo is a marquee Antioquian name and **no source states kinship** with Camilo Torres Restrepo.
