# Engelbert Humperdinck (1854-1921): research report

*Atlas of Journeys. Compiled 2026-08-11 for `engelbert_humperdinck.journey.json`. Queued in `QUEUE.md` line 257 as an author pick.*

**Legend.** **[A]** = attested, source named. **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition, or inference, including anything the sources give only in the general. Contradictions are flagged and left standing; unreachable sources are named with the reason. "The pool" means the verified research pool this file was written from.

**Scope warning.** This life rests almost entirely on English Wikipedia `Engelbert Humperdinck (composer)` and German Wikipedia `Engelbert Humperdinck`, plus the two opera articles. The reference works that would normally arbitrate between them, **Deutsche Biographie** and **Grove Music Online**, were unreachable this session (404 and 403), so where the two Wikipedias disagree nothing here breaks the tie.

## 1. Siegburg and the schoolroom, 1854-1871

**Birth. [A]** 1 September 1854, Siegburg, Rhine Province, Prussia.

**Birth house and father. [A, both corrected.]** The pool called the birth house unidentified and used a town-centre pin; it is identified and standing, **Markt 46**, built from 1826 as the town Gymnasium, now the **Stadtmuseum Siegburg**, approx. 50.7963 N, 7.2043 E. The pool also made Gustav Humperdinck a school principal; German Wikipedia makes him a **Gymnasiallehrer**, a teacher, who taught in the building the family lived in. Mother Gertrud was a cantor's daughter.

**Baptism at St. Servatius. [R]** Siegburg civic tradition; no parish register consulted. The pool called St. Servatius a Benedictine abbey church; German Wikipedia makes it the **parish and provost church** (50.79621 N, 7.20761 E), the Benedictine house being the **Abtei Michaelsberg** above the town.

**Childhood composition. [R]** A first piece at about seven, two singspiele at about thirteen, both undated anecdotes in English Wikipedia. The journey file's 1861 and 1867 are arithmetic performed on two vague phrases, and `date_confidence` says so. Nothing of the pieces is traceable.

**Parental opposition. [A, contradicted by the pool.]** English Wikipedia says his **parents discouraged music** and preferred architecture. The pool asserted he enrolled at Cologne "with his mother's support," unattested in either article and opposed to the English one. Both named in the journey file, neither settled.

**Abitur. [A]** Gymnasium Theodorianum, Paderborn, 1871. Year only.

## 2. Cologne and Munich, 1871-1879

**Cologne Conservatory. [A, date corrected.]** A **Baulehre** is begun and broken off in **1871**. The pool dated the conservatory enrolment 1871 too; German Wikipedia gives **1872**, and only the apprenticeship belongs to 1871. Teachers **Ferdinand Hiller** (composition) and **Isidor Seiss** (piano).

**Mozart Prize. [A]** From Frankfurt, **1876**, easing real hardship. Year only.

**Munich, Royal Music School. [A, year contested, coordinate corrected.]** German Wikipedia gives **1877**, English **1876**; the journey file uses 1877 and records the disagreement. The pool placed the Königliche Musikschule at 48.1394, 11.5773, the Max-Joseph-Platz block, and **reused that identical pair** for the Munich Hofoper stop: two venues, one coordinate, one certainly wrong. The school was in the **Odeon on Odeonsplatz**, approx. 48.1428 N, 11.5786 E. Teachers **Franz Lachner** and **Josef Rheinberger**.

**Mendelssohn Prize. [A]** Awarded 1879 by the **Mendelssohn-Stiftung**, Berlin; the Munich studies close the same year. The award site is **not recorded**, and the journey file's pin is a declared city-centre placeholder.

## 3. Italy and Wagner, 1879-1883

**Itinerary. [R, explicit gap.]** English Wikipedia says he "travelled through Italy, France, and Spain." That is all of it. The pool listed **Rome** as **[A]** while conceding the city "is not separately documented in the source consulted"; that tag is **downgraded to [R]**. Beyond Naples and a passing mention of Palermo, several years hold no cities and no dates, and the journey file carries a gap stop at 42.5, 12.5 marked as an absence.

**Naples, Villa Angri. [A]** He seeks out Wagner in **1880**, the family wintering on Posillipo. The pool's 40.8272, 14.1966 falls in the Fuorigrotta basin west of the ridge; the canonical pin from `richard_wagner.journey.json`, **40.8272, 14.21**, is inherited byte-identical instead, and needs cadastral re-verification before anyone calls it precise.

**Bayreuth assistantship. [A]** January 1881 to July 1882 (German Wikipedia): copying and correcting the **Parsifal** score, tutoring **Siegfried Wagner**. The pool put Wahnfried at 49.9469, 11.575; English Wikipedia gives **49.9413 N, 11.5831 E**, some 800 m off. The pool also said he "lives in Bayreuth as Wagner's assistant"; the sources say he **worked** there, a distinction the journey file keeps.

**Festspielhaus and the trombone bars. [R]** That he wrote extra bars to cover a scene change in the 1882 Parsifal run, and that Wagner tolerated it, is Wagner-Bayreuth historiography (Newman), not a document reproduced here. The pool's 49.9457, 11.5894 sits 1.8 km from the theatre; the canonical pin **49.9599, 11.5797** is inherited byte-identical.

**Wagner's death. [A]** 13 February 1883, Venice; German Wikipedia says it affected him deeply. The Palazzo Vendramin-Calergi coordinate was not separately verified.

**Effect on his composing. [A, quoted.]** From a letter to an unnamed friend, quoted in German Wikipedia out of Wolfram Humperdinck's 1965 biography: "Seitdem ich zu Wagner nach Bayreuth gekommen bin, hat es mit der eigenen Produktion ein plötzliches Ende genommen." Recipient and date **not given**.

## 4. The journeyman years, 1883-1889

**Cologne Stadttheater. [R]** Kapellmeister post ending in dismissal; German Wikipedia gives neither cause nor span, and the pool offers only 1883-1885. The journey file dates it 1883-04-01 as a declared placeholder.

**Krupp. [A]** Musical companion connected to **Alfred Krupp**, 1885. No place beyond "Germany," so the journey file folds it into the Cologne stop rather than invent an Essen pin.

**Barcelona. [A, duration contested.]** Professorship at the conservatory attached to the **Gran Teatre del Liceu**. German Wikipedia gives **autumn 1885 to summer 1886**; English says **roughly two years**, irreconcilable with a summer-1886 return. The pool's chronology entry was internally contradictory on exactly this. The journey file uses the German span and records the English figure as unresolved.

**Return to Cologne. [A]** 1887 (English Wikipedia); conservatory post left in **1888** (German Wikipedia). The pool dated the departure "1888-10"; **neither article gives October**, so the month is dropped.

**Music criticism. [Contradiction, split rather than merged.]** English Wikipedia attaches a critic's post to the 1887 Cologne return; German Wikipedia names the **Bonner Zeitung**, a different paper in a different city. No source reconciles them, so Bonn gets its own stop.

**B. Schott's Söhne, Mainz. [A]** Music editor, preparing scores including Wagner piano reductions. The pool cited only New Grove, "not directly re-consulted"; German Wikipedia independently confirms the employment, so nothing here rests on an unfetched reference. The 1887-1889 span stays approximate. The pool gave latitude **50 exactly**, a placeholder rather than a location; the firm is at Weihergarten 5, approx. 49.9987 N, 8.2707 E.

## 5. Frankfurt and Hänsel und Gretel, 1890-1895

**Appointments. [A]** 1890: teacher at **Dr. Hoch's Konservatorium**, opera critic for the **Frankfurter Zeitung**, and per English Wikipedia harmony at the Stockhausen vocal school. The pool's 50.1155, 8.6842 is the Zeil area, 600 m from the institution at **Eschersheimer Landstraße 4**, approx. 50.1187 N, 8.6779 E.

**Origin of the opera. [A]** His sister **Adelheid Wette** asked him to set songs for a children's fairy play from the Grimm tale; it grew into the opera. The date of the request is **not specified** anywhere.

**Where it was composed. [Contradiction inside the pool.]** The pool's geography lens calls the Frankfurt residence unidentified and uses a city-centre approximation; its afterlife lens names **Scheffelstraße 1** with a plaque. The address appears in **neither** article as fetched. The journey file uses the Nordend street coordinate (approx. 50.1235 N, 8.6935 E) so the pin matches the address being claimed, and flags that address as unsourced.

**Overture letter. [A]** A long German passage from a letter to his brother-in-law **Hermann Wette**, 16 December 1891, calling the overture "eine Art symphonischer Prolog, der ein 'Kinderleben' genannt werden könnte." Verbatim from German Wikipedia, `Hänsel und Gretel (Oper)`; **not cross-checked against an archival scan**. The genre label "Märchenoper" is likewise given as his own coinage in English Wikipedia **without citing a letter or interview**.

**Marriage. [A as to fact, contested as to day.]** To **Hedwig (Louise Hedwig Laura) Taxer**, daughter of bookseller Robert Taxer, at Poppelsdorf near Bonn, May 1892. English Wikipedia gives **19 May**, German **18 May**; the pool's chronology lens left this open and its geography lens then hard-coded 1892-05-19, silently resolving it. The journey file dates the stop **1892-05-01** and refuses the day. The pool's 50.7128, 7.0805 lies south of the district, which centres near 50.720 N, 7.085 E.

**Premiere. [A]** 23 December 1893, **Grossherzogliches Hoftheater, Weimar**, under **Richard Strauss**, an immediate and widespread success. Canonical Weimar pin **50.9795, 11.3235** inherited from `saint_barbara.journey.json`.

**England and America. [A]** London, **Daly's Theatre**, 26 December 1894, in **Constance Bache's** translation, which becomes the standard English text. American premiere **8 October 1895**, New York; **the source names no theatre**, so no pin is set and the journey file says why.

**Contemporary verdict. [A]** "the first German composer of distinct individuality since Wagner": critic **R. A. Streatfeild**, quoted in Krehbiel (see Sources). About him, not by him.

## 6. Königskinder, Boppard, Berlin, 1894-1911

**The commission. [A, attribution corrected.]** The pool said **Elsa Bernstein** (Ernst Rosmer) commissioned the incidental music. English Wikipedia, `Königskinder`, says it was **Heinrich Porges**, the playwright's father, who asked him in 1894. Bernstein did not commission it; she **refused** to let her play become an opera, which is why Humperdinck wrote a melodrama, and relented only in **1907**.

**Melodrama premiere. [A]** 23 January 1897, Munich court opera, in notated **Sprechgesang**.

**Boppard and the professorship. [A, years contested.]** He resigns the Frankfurt teaching and takes the villa still called the **Humperdinck-Schlösschen**. English Wikipedia dates Wilhelm II's professorship to **1896**, German ties the Boppard move to **1897**; both kept, no month for either.

**Berlin master class. [A]** November 1900, head of the **Meisterschule für musikalische Komposition** at the Royal Academy of Arts, held into 1920. Both Wikipedias agree on the month, which is unusual in this file.

**Die Heirat wider Willen. [A]** Premiere 14 April 1905, Berlin Court Opera.

**Max Reinhardt. [A, venue and date corrected.]** The pool placed a 1905 *Ein Sommernachtstraum* collaboration at the **Deutsches Theater**. Per German Wikipedia, `Max Reinhardt`, that staging premiered **January 1905 at the Neues Theater am Schiffbauerdamm**; Reinhardt leased the Deutsches Theater only in autumn 1905 and bought it in 1906, and its coordinate was also off by about 470 m (approx. 52.5244, 13.3822). The journey file pins the Deutsches Theater, dates the stop to the **later** collaboration, and names the Schiffbauerdamm rather than absorbing it silently.

**Königskinder as opera. [A]** World premiere **28 December 1910**, Metropolitan Opera, New York. The old Met at 1411 Broadway is **40.75417 N, 73.98750 W**; the pool's two lenses gave two different pins for the same building, both wrong.

*Quotation handled with care:* "die wichtigste Oper seit Wagners Parsifal" **is** verbatim in German Wikipedia, but the source sentence is passive and names **no speaker**; it describes the reception of the 1910 premiere and is usable only in that form, never as an individual's dictum. The pool's guess that it conflates a Siegfried Wagner line about *Hänsel und Gretel* cannot be demonstrated from any traced source and is **not** repeated in the journey file.

## 7. The stroke and the last decade, 1911-1921

**London, Das Mirakel. [A]** 23 December 1911; he falls severely ill during the visit. The hall is not named, so the journey file uses a declared central London pin.

**The stroke. [A as to date and effect, [R] as to place.]** 5 January 1912, severe stroke, left hand permanently paralysed (English Wikipedia). The pool tagged a Berlin location **[A]**; English Wikipedia **names no city**, and German Wikipedia ties the decline to the London journey without this date. Berlin is an inference from residence, marked so in `date_confidence`.

**Manifesto of the Ninety-Three. [A]** Among the signatories, 1914. Year only in the reachable sources; the journey file defaults to 1914-01-01, asserting no day.

**Hedwig's death. [A]** 1916 (German Wikipedia). **No day, no place**; Berlin inferred and flagged.

**Gaudeamus. [A, genre contested.]** English Wikipedia calls it a choral work completed 1918 with Wolfram's help; German Wikipedia lists a **Spieloper**, "Szenen aus dem deutschen Studentenleben," composed 1915-1919, premiered **18 March 1919 in Darmstadt**. The pool's geography lens had the genre right while its chronology lens had it wrong. The journey file reports both and reconciles neither, keeping only what both allow: Wolfram wrote the notes down at his father's dictation. Wolfram also wrote *Das Leben meines Vaters* (1965), ultimate source of at least one quotation reaching this file through German Wikipedia, and **not consulted directly**.

**Neustrelitz. [A, opera corrected.]** 26 September 1921: he attends a performance and suffers a heart attack. The pool named the opera *Hänsel und Gretel* conducted by Wolfram; both Wikipedias name **Weber's Der Freischütz**, and German Wikipedia describes Wolfram as **staging** it, not necessarily conducting.

**Death. [A as to date, contested as to cause.]** 27 September 1921, Neustrelitz, aged 67. English Wikipedia calls the fatal event a **second heart attack**, German Wikipedia a **Schlaganfall**, a stroke. A real and unresolved disagreement about how the man died, printed here rather than settled.

**Burial. [A]** **Südwestkirchhof Stahnsdorf**, **Block Erlöser**, beside his wife; the plot later carries **Ehrengrab der Stadt Berlin** status, with no grave number in either source. A separate memorial stands at **Friedhof Zehlendorf**, Field 008, and neither article explains why. Recorded as a gap.

## 9. Atlas interlocks

**Genuine crossings.** **`richard_wagner`** is the strongest: Naples 1880 and Bayreuth 1881-1882 are the same rooms in both lives, though Wagner's file names neither the Villa Angri visitor nor the Parsifal copyist, so the connection comes from outside biography. Canonical pins inherited byte-identical, Naples **40.8272, 14.21** and Festspielhaus **49.9599, 11.5797**. **`brothers_grimm`** holds the Kassel pharmacy where **Dortchen Wild** gave the Grimms the tale Adelheid Wette adapted; that file does not mention Humperdinck, so the descent is inferred **[R]**.

**Overlap only, no contact attested.** **`nietzsche`** and **`tchaikovsky`** are both at Bayreuth (foundation stone 1872, first Ring 1876) before Humperdinck arrives, with no meeting recorded between either of them and Humperdinck; house-style cleanup (2026-08-14) removed the pairing from the Festspielhaus campa as a coincidental crossing rather than a documented one. **`saint_barbara`** quotes the *Abendsegen* of the fourteen angels in its own Weimar stop; the resemblance to Humperdinck's "vierzehn Engel" is a numeric echo built across two files, not a citation running in either direction, and the same cleanup removed it from the Weimar campa here. **`goethe_full`** is Weimar coordinate reuse only, Goethe dying twenty-two years before Humperdinck is born; *the pool's interlock lens reported the Goethe Weimar coordinate transposed as lat 11.329, lng 50.9795, while the file itself is correct at `lat 50.98, lng 11.329`, checked directly.* **`catorce_santos_ayudantes`** supplies only a Rhineland anchor at Cologne, Saint Pantaleon; the fourteen-helpers resonance with the fourteen angels of the *Abendsegen* is juxtaposition, not a claim, and was likewise dropped from the Cologne campa in the same cleanup. For **Paris**, **no Humperdinck-specific source exists**, so no stop was written and the French travel folds into the gap stop.

**Queue and census.** `QUEUE.md` line 257 lists him as an author pick **[A]**; `census_real_persons_2026-08-02.md` line 114 carries a misspelled duplicate, "Engebert Humperdinck," which is the same person and one traveler per the dedupe rule **[A]**. No journey file intersects him at the Kaaba, Temple Mount, or Buenos Aires; an absence, not a forced link.
