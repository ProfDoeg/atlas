## 4. The National Security Council, 1981-1986

- **Arrival and rise [A: Wikipedia; Tower Commission Report 1987; congressional Iran-Contra report 1987].** NSC staff from 1981, first in liaison and lobbying; lieutenant colonel 1983; deputy director for political-military affairs from 1983, in the Old Executive Office Building under Robert McFarlane and then John Poindexter, with CIA Director William Casey's encouragement.
- **Casey's knowledge [C].** North's testimony asserts Casey and his superiors knew and approved; some White House figures denied full knowledge. The congressional record does not settle the chain of authorization above North, and neither does the journey: the campa states the encouragement as North described it, and the July 1987 stops carry his claim in his own words.
- **Grenada, 1983 [R, dropped].** The pool records NSC planning connected to Urgent Fury as "not independently verified in this pass." Not carried; recorded here rather than silently omitted.
- **Boland Amendment, October 1984 [A: congressional record; National Security Archive chronology].** Second iteration, barring US intelligence and defense agencies from aiding the Contras. Pinned at the Capitol.
- **PROF notes [A: Tower Commission Report; notes in evidence in *US v. North*].** Believed deleted, recovered from backup, used as trial evidence. Which meetings occurred physically in the Situation Room rather than in North's office is not consistently distinguished **[gap, in `date_confidence`]**.

## 6. The Iran channel

- **Tel Aviv, 1985-1986 [A: Tower Commission Report; contemporaneous Israeli and American press].** Meetings with Amiram Nir and others; Israel as supplier and go-between shipping American-origin missiles to Iran before US stocks were drawn on. Trip dates vary between North's testimony and the Israeli sources **[C]**.
- **Achille Lauro and NAS Sigonella, 10 October 1985.** **Tag correction:** the pool tagged this **[R]**, "widely reported but not independently source-checked." Wikipedia's hijacking article documents the role directly: North and James Stark originated the interception idea, North contacted Israeli Major General Uri Simhoni, and the two passed it to Poindexter. Upgraded to **[A]**.
- **London, 1985-1986 [A: congressional report].** Meetings with Manucher Ghorbanifar over prices, quantities, schedules. His later account to investigators conflicts with North's over what was promised and to whom **[C]**; no single hotel is consistently named **[gap]**.
- **Tehran, 25 May 1986 [A: Tower Commission Report; McFarlane's congressional testimony].** McFarlane, North, and a small delegation; inscribed Bible, missile spare parts, key-shaped cake; received by mid-level officials, not the promised leadership; no hostages released. **Pin decision flagged:** the geography lens sites this at Mehrabad, 35.6892, 51.3134; the atlas already carries Tehran at **35.6892, 51.389** (`ayatollah_khomeini.journey.json`), and the journey inherits the canonical pin, noting in `date_confidence` that the landing was five kilometres west.
- **Frankfurt.** **Tag correction:** the pool tagged Frankfurt **[R]**, hedging that "some chronologies place equivalent meetings elsewhere in Europe." The congressional report documents meetings in February and October 1986, the latter opening the Iranian Second Channel. Upgraded to **[A]**, hedge dropped.

## 8. Hearings, trial, appeal, 1987-1991

**Venue correction.** The pool sited the July 1987 hearings at the Capitol (38.8899, -77.0091). The joint committees sat in the Senate Caucus Room of the Russell Senate Office Building and in the Rayburn House Office Building; both testimony stops use Russell, 38.8927, -77.0067.

**Quote discipline.** Three corrections:

1. **"I was given a mission" is deleted.** The pool's geography lens attached it to the hearings and called it "a defining phrase of the affair." It is not a documented North line.
2. **Dates fixed to 7 July 1987.** Wikiquote gives 8 July for both "I was authorized to do everything that I did" and the "23 years... never violated an order" line. The verbatim transcript (*Taking the Stand*, 1987) puts both in the Tuesday 7 July session, in reply to House counsel John Nields, the second following immediately on the first. The journey uses 7 July for the utterance and hangs the recitation on the 14 July stop, in `quote_source`.
3. **The Cigar Aficionado "order to kill North" phrase is not quoted.** It is Wikipedia's editorial paraphrase, not North's words; the underlying interview (Gordon Mott, May/June 2002) contains no such language.

**Two pool quotes tagged [R], not used:** "I am here to accept responsibility for that which I did..." and "I came here to tell you the truth, the good, the bad and the ugly." Both reach the pool from BrainyQuote without primary citation. The second is widely repeated and used by the pool's own afterlife lens, unverified against the transcript. **[C:** the afterlife lens treats it as canonical, the quotes lens does not.**]**

**The trial [A: Walsh, *Final Report of the Independent Counsel*, 1993, ch. 2; *US v. North* docket].** Indictment 16 March 1988, sixteen counts, with Poindexter, Secord, Hakim. One wire-fraud count dismissed late November 1988 as duplicative. Counts One and Two (conspiracy, theft) dismissed 13 January 1989 after the administration refused to declassify necessary material. Jury sworn 21 February 1989 before Judge Gerhard Gesell on the remaining twelve. Verdict 4 May 1989: guilty on three (Counts Six, Nine, Ten), not guilty on nine. Sentence 5 July 1989: suspended term, fine, probation, community service.

**Major factual correction, stated inside the campa.** The pool's chronology lens claimed "the jury deadlocks on remaining charges, which are later dropped." **No deadlock occurred.** Three guilty plus nine not guilty accounts for all twelve counts that reached the jury; the journey states the arithmetic, "three of twelve" being the load-bearing number.

**Appeal [A: *United States v. North*, 910 F.2d 843 (D.C. Cir. 1990)].** 20 July 1990: convictions vacated and remanded on Kastigar grounds, the immunized 1987 testimony having potentially tainted trial witnesses. 16 September 1991: Gesell dismisses the rest. **Legend correction, stated in the campa:** the court found a procedural taint, not factual innocence. Popular retelling flattens this into "Oliver North was exonerated," which the opinion does not support **[C, not resolved]**.

**Coordinate correction.** The pool gave three points for one building: 38.8977, -77.0365 (the White House), 38.8951, -77.0364 (17th Street NW, 1.9 km from any courthouse), and 38.8951, -77.0158. The E. Barrett Prettyman Courthouse, 333 Constitution Avenue NW, is at **38.8934, -77.0147**; all four courthouse stops use it.

## 11. Gaps, stated as gaps

1. **The subject is alive, so the usual afterlife frame does not apply.** No tomb, grave, or funerary monument. The pool could not re-confirm live status for 2026, its search budget having run out first; this report inherits the gap. Nothing in the journey asserts a death.
2. **No monument [R].** No statue, plaque, museum wing, or dedicated monument is documented at any public site; the closest analogues are the C-SPAN hearing footage and the National Archives Iran-Contra collection. An absence inferred from silence in standard databases, not a verified negative, and recorded as such in the final stop's `suggested_refs`.
3. **"Ollie North" as political slang [R].** Reconstructed from press and satire references, 1990s to 2010s; no single source catalogs the usage. Not carried.
4. **The chain of authorization above North is not established.** The central unresolved question of the affair; neither the congressional report, the Tower Commission, nor Walsh closes it.
5. **The Basic School instructor tour has no fixed span**, and individual travel dates to Miami, Tegucigalpa, Tel Aviv, London, and Geneva are inconsistently recorded or absent throughout.
