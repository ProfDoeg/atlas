# Arkadi Kremer (Aron Iosifovich Kremer), 1865-1935: research report

*Slug `arkadi_kremer`. Compiled 2026-08-29 from the verified research pool for `arkadi_kremer.journey.json`. Founder-organizer of the Bund; author of `Ob agitatsii`; member of the first RSDLP Central Committee; electrical engineer; author of the first Yiddish textbook of electrical engineering.*

**Legend.** **[A]** = attested, source named on the line. **[R]** = reconstruction, lexicon tradition, or a claim whose cited source does not carry it. Contradictions between the pool's two lenses are printed, not resolved.

**Structural fact.** Almost the whole political spine rests on one unpublished source, H. Shukman's 1961 Oxford D.Phil. thesis, which could not be read this pass. Everything reachable online is thinner and in places silently derives from it. Where a particular appears in only one place, that place is Shukman.

---

## 1. Sventsian and the technical track, 1865-1889

- Born 25 August 1865 in Sventsian (Švenčionys), Vilna Governorate. **[A: Congress for Jewish Culture lexicon.]** encyclopedia.com carries only the year and province; the day-and-month date and the enlightened-Hebrew-teacher father come from CJF, not encyclopedia.com as the pool claimed. *Coordinate corrected:* the pool's 55.1394, 26.1719 sits about 1.4 km ENE of the town centre; journey uses 55.1333, 26.1500.
- To Vilna at about twelve, into a Realschule. **[R: CJF only; no school record located.]**
- Saint Petersburg Technological Institute; student radicalism; contact with the Polish Marxist Proletariat. **[A: Wikipedia, and Shukman.]** The pool cited encyclopedia.com, which does not mention the Institute. *Coordinate corrected:* the pool's 59.9187, 30.3428 is about 1.3 km east of the campus; journey uses Wikipedia's 59.9183, 30.3200.
- Riga Polytechnic. **[R: CJF tradition only; no register, enrolment date or faculty confirmed.]**
- First arrest 1889, imprisonment, ban on residing in the capital. **[A: encyclopedia.com; CJF.]** Charge, prison, sentence length: **gap in every source consulted.**

## 2. Vilna and the circles, 1890-1896

- **Contradiction, not resolved.** The chronology lens dates the arrival with Matilda "Pati" Srednitskaia to 1889 (Shukman p.3); the geography lens gives 1890, and Wikipedia has him released only in 1890. The journey uses 1890 and prints the disagreement. The 1889 line cannot stand if the arrest and exile do.
- Founding cadre: Kopelzon, Gozhanskii, John Mill, Aiznshtat, Pati Srednitskaia. **[A: Shukman p.3.]** 60-70 in the intelligentsia circles, 150+ in the workers' circles by the early 1890s. **[R: Shukman p.3, citing memoirs. Memoir figures, not a count.]**
- Central organizer from 1891, teaching economics, history and mathematics. **[A: encyclopedia.com, verbatim, "central organizer in the Jewish Social Democratic Group in Vilna from 1891-1897".]**
- **Downgrade applied.** The geography lens named Anna Rozental as his mathematics pupil, citing encyclopedia.com; a fetch confirms she appears nowhere in that entry, and the only reachable link, the JewishGen Pinkas Hakehillot page, merely lists her among Vilna Bund leaders. **[R]**, dropped from the journey rather than asserted.
- Martov arrives in Vilna, 1893. **[A: Shukman p.3.]**
- `Ob agitatsii` drafted, Martov editing. **[A]**, date contested: Wikipedia 1893, Russian Jewish Encyclopedia and Shukman 1894. Journey uses 1894 and prints the range. **Not resolved.**

## 3. Geneva and the Kyiv attempt, 1896-1897

- Meeting with Plekhanov, Axelrod and Zasulich. The chronology lens tagged it **[A]** in Zurich; the geography lens **[R]** in Geneva, unconfirmed. The Emancipation of Labour group was based in Geneva, so the journey uses Geneva and **[R]**; no fetched source confirms the meeting at all. Shukman's claim that Plekhanov's refusal pushed Kremer toward unification is the hinge of the 1896-97 story and rests on that one inaccessible thesis.
- `Ob agitatsii` printed in Geneva by the Union of Russian Social Democrats Abroad, Axelrod's postscript, 1896. **[A: GARF fond 1741 op.2 delo 624, via docs.historyrussia.org.]** Quoted in Minister of Justice Muravev's 1907 report. **[A: Shukman p.20.]**
- Kyiv unification conference, March 1897; Vilna absent. **[R, downgraded from the pool's A.]** The Jewish Virtual Library, the only reachable account of the pre-founding talks, does not mention Kyiv.

## 4. The founding and the liquidation, 1897-1900

- Founding congress, Vilna, 7-9 October 1897, thirteen delegates, eight of them working men. **[A: Jewish Virtual Library, verbatim.]** Wikipedia's "September 1897" is the same event in the Julian calendar, not a rival date.
- **Two names corrected.** At its founding the body was the General Jewish Labour Bund in Russia and Poland; "Lithuania" entered the title only in 1901 (English Wikipedia), so the pool's 1901 name for the 1897 congress is anachronistic. And the third CC member was Avrom Mutnik, underground name Gleb: the pool's "Gleb Mutnik" conflates surname with pseudonym. Kremer opens the congress and is elected to that CC with Kossovsky and Mutnik **[A: Shukman pp.24, 27, citing John Mill, "Arkadi un der ershter tsuzamenfor"]**; the CC moves to Minsk late in 1897 **[R: Shukman p.27]**, its press address a **gap**.
- First Congress of the RSDLP, Minsk, March 1898; Kremer to its three-man CC with Eidelman and Radchenko; the Bund admitted as autonomous in Jewish proletarian affairs. **[A: Shukman pp.1, 45; JVL for "three of the nine delegates were Bundists".]** Meeting house: **gap.**
- Zubatov takes the whole Bund CC, 26 July 1898; presses seized at Bobruisk and Minsk; 55-70 arrested. (The geography lens wanted a separate Bobruisk stop, **[R]** and unconfirmed; none was written, and Bobruisk appears only here.) **[R: Shukman p.38, citing Menshchikov, *Okhrana i revoliutsiia*.]** Old Style or New Style is not stated by any source consulted, and the journey says so.
- Imprisonment in Moscow, "nearly two years". **[R, downgraded from A.]** encyclopedia.com says only "Following imprisonment for revolutionary activities, he escaped abroad in 1900", naming no city and no term; CJF says only "spent time in prison"; the eleven.co.il URL returns 404. Moscow and the two years rest on Shukman alone. Note also *escaped*, not "released".
- The cipher system and coding machine. **[R, flagged as a probable modern accretion.]** Wikipedia states he developed in prison "a system of cryptography and a coding machine that came to be widely used in the Russian revolutionary movement", citing Shukman 1961. No patent, no device description, no party record, no independent confirmation. Kept as the most-repeated story about him, the stop declaring it stands on one 1961 thesis.

## 5. The Foreign Committee, 1900-1902

- Foreign Committee work in Geneva from 1900. **[A for presence; R for particulars.]** The Swiss listing of Kremer as a proofreader on the Chemin des Pervenches could not be fetched. *Coordinate decision:* the geography lens gave 46.2088, 6.1225; the interlock lens recommends 46.2044, 6.1432 from `vladimir_medem.journey.json`, and that pin is used for all three Geneva stops.
- Detained with Chaim Bernstein after an anti-Russian demonstration, then expelled by the Federal Council, 17 April 1901, despite a lawyer and 100+ petitioners. **[R.]** Known only through dokumen.pub, *East European Jews in Switzerland*; the Swiss primary record was not reachable.
- Paris to London with the committee and its press, 1901. **[R, downgraded from the pool's chronology tag of A.]** The geography lens already called this "the dossier's chronological reconstruction".
- Bialystok conference, March 1902; the organizing body liquidated within a week; Kremer one of five to escape. The chronology lens tags **[A]** on Shukman p.100, citing *Iskra* no. 26 and a Bund Archives manuscript by Kremer; the geography lens could not confirm it. **Lens contradiction, printed in `date_confidence`, not resolved.**
- Lenin's letter to Kremer, 4 May 1902. **[A: read in full at marxists.org, from *Leninskii Sbornik* XIII.]** The only verbatim primary text in the pool, and a letter *to* him, not by him. That Kremer was in Paris and was the "Alexander" addressed comes from the editorial apparatus, not the letter's body.

## 6. The Second Congress and the return, 1903-1908

- Second RSDLP Congress, Brussels then London, 1903; Kremer under the pseudonym "Wolf" with a consultative vote **[A: marxists.org 1903 delegate biographies]**; federal demand rejected, delegation withdraws **[A: JVL]**. The pool names Lenin and Martov as leading the rejection.
- United States, 1904, and the fundraising network later recalled by Abraham Cahan in "Kremer in amerike". **[R: CJF tradition; no itinerary or dates located.]** New York is inferred from Cahan's base at the *Forward*.
- Returns to Russia, 1905. **[A: encyclopedia.com, "he returned to Russia in 1905 to continue Bund work".]** **Split applied:** the pool bundled the Petersburg Soviet into the same [A]. That connection is in no fetchable source; the journey carries it as tradition, marked in `date_confidence`.
- Second arrest, 1907. **[R: CJF tradition alone. Charge, prison, sentence and release date: gap; the place is unspecified**, and the journey uses the Saint Petersburg pin for want of any location.]
- 1908, withdrawal from underground work while remaining a Bund member. **[R: encyclopedia.com gives "1908: Abandoned political life"; CJF supplies the "remained a member" qualifier.]** 1908-1912 is an **empty record**.

## 7. Toulouse and the war, 1912-1921

- Enrols at the Toulouse Electrotechnical Institute, 1912 **[A: encyclopedia.com; CJF]**; graduates as an engineer, 1914 **[A: CJF, verbatim]**. No month recorded; the journey's defaulted 1914-01-01 does not place the diploma before August. Electrical engineer in France, 1912-1921 **[A: encyclopedia.com timeline, verbatim]**.
- Alignment with Entente-supporting socialists. **[R: YIVO tradition. No text by Kremer on the war has been located: no article, no letter, no speech.]**
- CJF describes his engineering education as spread across "Switzerland, Germany, England, and France", which does not match the single Toulouse institution named elsewhere in the same entry. **Noted, not resolved.**

## 8. Wilno again, 1921-1935

- Return with Pati, 1921 **[A: encyclopedia.com; CJF on Pati]**. Teaches first-year electrical engineering at the Vilna Technicum (ORT), 1922, and at the Teacher Seminar **[A: CJH blog, "Electrical Engineering in Yiddish", 18 November 2025]**.
- **Booklet corrected.** The pool described a booklet listing algebra, geometry, physics, drafting and mechanics. The CJH blog identifies the 1922 record as Leon Daniel's, first-year electrical engineering under Kremer, signed by the director Israel Okun; Szrejber signs the separate 1930 textbook inscription. The subject list is not in the blog and was not written into the journey.
- `Elektrotekhnik`, Part I, handwritten and lithographed, first five terms, 1927. **[A: CJH blog.]** CJF dates his Vilna technical publishing more loosely to 1925-1936; noted, not resolved. Part II, second edition, cover by Yitskhok Shkop, issued by the Technicum mutual-aid administration **[A]**, but dated 1928-1929 in the blog, not 1928.
- 1928, Bund activity resumed. **[A for the generic resumption: encyclopedia.com, "resumed his activities in the local branch of the Bund", "a moral authority for Polish Bundists".]** **[R] for the two named offices:** neither the chairmanship of the Vilna organization nor the Jewish Community Council seat appears in any reachable source, and the eleven.co.il URL cited for them returns 404. The journey states both readings side by side.
- Photograph with his grandson, 1930. **[A for photograph and caption: Wikimedia Commons, from Center for Jewish History archives.]** **[R] for the name:** the Commons page names no child, and "Joseph Dobrinsky" is the pool's inference. English Wikipedia confirms Isaac Dobrinsky married Vera Kremer in 1926 (resolving the pool's "1926 or 1928") but records no children. The place is not in the caption either.
- Death in Vilna, 20 September 1935, aged seventy. **[A: CJF; marxists.org delegate biography confirms the year.]** **Cause of death: gap.**

## 9. After, 1935-2026

- "Buried with great honours by the Bundists." **[A: English Wikipedia, verbatim; no cemetery, plot or gravestone named.]** Funeral procession and mourning notice, 22 September 1935 **[R]**: the *Naye Folkstsaytung* of that date is named in compilations as carrying the notice, but the NLI page could not be fetched.
- Burial in the Zaretcha / Užupis Jewish cemetery. **[R: inference from dates and place only. No register entry, no gravestone.]** *Coordinate corrected, significantly:* the pool's 54.6935, 25.265 lies about 2.5 km NW of Užupis, near the older Šnipiškės cemetery, which closed in 1831 and cannot be his burial place; journey uses 54.6800, 25.3030. lt.wikipedia dates the ground 1831-1940, en.wikipedia 1828 to 1943/1948. **Both printed, neither adjudicated.**
- Pati (Matla Srednicki) Kremer, born 2 January 1867, killed in the liquidation of the Vilna ghetto, September 1943. **[A: CJF. Killing site disputed: Sobibor or Ponary. Not resolved.]**
- `Arkadi: Zamlbukh tsum ondenk fun Arkadi Kremer`, New York, 1942. **[A: CJF, which names only that volume.]** **Corrected:** the pool attributed a Vilna 1939 first edition and about 463 pages to the same entry, which carries neither, and both stand unverified.
- Koppel S. Pinson, *Jewish Social Studies* 7:3 (1945), pairing Kremer as founder-organizer with Medem as later theorist. **[R: known from Bund historiography; not fetched.]** No stop (see §10).
- Center for Jewish History, 2025: blog post 18 November **[A]**; Commons upload of the 1930 photograph 18 February **[A]**. **Corrected:** the digital object number is 47, not 511486.
- Molly Crabapple, *Here Where We Live Is Our Country* (Milkweed Editions, 2026), centring Pati and Arkadi Kremer; NYT list at number four, fourth printing in three days. **[A for the publication; the sales claims are the publisher's own.]**

## 10. Apparatus relations for the operator

**Kremer is not on the `EXCEPTIONS.md` list, and no apparatus relation of the licensable kind was found.** He has no theoretical framework in the Austin/Searle/Derrida sense, only an organization and a pedagogy, so nothing needs a backward grant. Per the standing instruction, the relations not rooted in his own travel are kept and named rather than deleted:

1. **Basel, the First Zionist Congress, 29-31 August 1897.** The interlock lens proposes inheriting Buber's Basel Stadtcasino pin (47.5559, 7.5883) for a "Herzl/Kremer 1897 pairing". Kremer was never in Basel. Direction caught: **not apparatus, but the plain co-location rule.** *Resolution:* no Basel stop; the congress is named in one sentence inside Kremer's own Vilna founding stop. Note that `theodor_herzl.journey.json` now exists, contrary to the interlock lens's note; nothing was written into it and nothing should be.
2. **Rudolf Rocker, London East End Yiddish press, 1898-1903.** Same milieu, overlapping years, no documented meeting; the interlock lens itself tagged it [R]. Direction: **co-location only.** Not named; the milieu is described without him.
3. **Golda Meir and Pinsk.** Flagged by the interlock lens as weak and geographic-only. Direction: **co-location only. Not used.**
4. **Leon Trotsky at the Second Congress.** Co-delegacy over one motion, more than shared geography, but the pool establishes no documented exchange with Kremer. Direction: **borderline.** Not named; if the operator wants him in, the honest form is one clause in the London walkout stop, sourced to `leon_trotsky.journey.json`.
5. **Koppel Pinson, 1945.** A scholar's article about Kremer ten years after his death. Direction: **the other person's act.** Cited, not staged. The 2025 CJH digitization and the 2026 Crabapple book do get stops, being posthumous events about Kremer's own objects, sited where they happened.
6. **Canonical pins checked, none applicable:** Kaaba (21.4225, 39.8262), Temple Mount (31.7780, 35.2354), Buenos Aires (-34.6037, -58.3816). Kremer left Europe only in 1904, and the Bund's doikayt was constitutively anti-Zionist, making a Jerusalem pin thematically opposite rather than a crossing. Do not add one later.

**Pins inherited byte-identical:** Geneva 46.2044, 6.1432 and Białystok 53.1325, 23.1688 (`vladimir_medem.journey.json`); Paris 48.8566, 2.3522 and London 51.5074, -0.1278 (`vladimir_lenin.journey.json`, `leon_trotsky.journey.json`).

**Travelers named in a campa, and why.** **Lenin**: documented correspondence (4 May 1902) and the antagonist of the 1903 vote. **Medem**: joined the organization Kremer founded, sat on the Foreign Committee he had run, walked out beside him, succeeded him as theorist. **Herzl**: the rivalry the Bund was constituted against, named once, inside Kremer's own founding stop.

## 11. Standing gaps

- **No text by Kremer in his own voice was located.** `Ob agitatsii` unread, the Bialystok manuscript unreached, no letter or speech found. The only verbatim primary document in the pool is a letter written *to* him by Lenin.
- **1908-1912: empty. The war years: empty of his own voice. Cause of death: unrecorded. Grave unlocated**, no register, no stone, the cemetery destroyed. **No monument, plaque, street name or statue** was found; absence of evidence, not a confirmed negative.
- **The Shukman dependency.** Roughly half the [A] tags on the political spine trace to a 1961 thesis nobody in this pass could open.
