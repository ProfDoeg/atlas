# Max Levchin, research report
*2026-08-28. Kyiv 1975 to San Francisco, present. Compiled for `max_levchin.journey.json`.*

Legend: **[A]** = attested, source named · **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition, or the subject's own retrospective telling with no independent record. Contradictions are recorded, not adjudicated. Unreachable sources are named with the reason.

**The subject is alive and working.** No death, no tomb, no posthumous reception, no transmission history. The afterlife lens returns an inversion instead of a gap: rather than a monument raised to him, Levchin has endowed the honorific apparatus of his own field and lives to watch it award his predecessors.

**Most of the early life is [R] on his own authority.** The Soviet childhood, the Chernobyl evacuation, the Pan Am flight, the arrival cash, the birthday lunch that founded Yelp: all of it is Levchin telling the story decades later. Where a primary filing exists it is preferred over profile journalism and over Levchin himself, and in five places it corrects one or the other.

## 1. Kyiv, 1975-1991

**Birth.** Kyiv, Ukrainian SSR, into a family of scientists, writers and artists **[A: Wikipedia, "Max Levchin"]**. **Contradiction, unresolved:** English sources give **11 July 1975**, some Russian-language material **15 July 1975** **[R: dossier note]**. No civil birth record located; the file uses 1975-07-11 and carries the conflict in `date_confidence`.

**The grandmother.** Frima Lukatskaya, his mother's mother, an astrophysicist working on **variable and unstable stars**, source of the maxim **"Go right through I can't."** Carried into this pass as **[R]**, on an Illinois page that 404'd on every URL tried. **Upgraded to [A]:** the page is live in archive at `archive.storied.illinois.edu/maximum-impact` and states all three. **Open question:** it says only "the Kiev Observatory," and Kyiv has two candidates, the **Taras Shevchenko University observatory** on Observatorna Street (50.4524, 30.4984) and the **Main Astronomical Observatory** at Holosiiv (50.364, 30.497). The pool's 50.458, 30.496 matches neither. The file uses the university coordinate and calls the choice unsettled. **Do not treat Observatorna Street as established.**

**The science-fiction club.** His father was "a highly active member of Zoryaniy Shlyah - Kyiv's possibly first (and possibly only, at the time) sci-fi fan club" **[A: Levchin, max.levch.in, fetched directly]**, a network he calls the family's "one secret advantage" on arrival in Chicago.

**Chernobyl, 26 April 1986.** He recalls his parents learning of the explosion ahead of any announcement and sending him and his brother Sergey to Crimea, where they were screened with a Geiger counter **[R: his own account, no documentary record]**. The **Crimean destination is unknown** in every source checked. The file pins Simferopol as the rail gateway and says so: an inference about a route, not a claim about a town.

**Cryptography, c. 1990.** "I had fallen desperately in love with secret codes and cryptography about a year before we left Ukraine" **[A: max.levch.in]**.

## 4. Confinity and PayPal, 1998-2002

**Incorporation, date corrected.** The pool gave **3 December 1998**. **The day is invented.** PayPal's final prospectus (424B4, 15 February 2002) says only "Confinity, Inc., a development stage company, incorporated in **California** in **December 1998**," and the bios repeat it. Note **California, not Delaware**. Levchin as **CTO and director**, Thiel's roughly **$100,000**, the Fieldlink name: **[A: Wikipedia, "PayPal" and "Peter Thiel"]**. No first office address is established in any filing.

**Stanford advisers.** Dan Boneh advising the handheld design, Martin Hellman advising and investing **[R: dossier only]**. It matters, since it would make Levchin's line to public-key cryptography a working one rather than the honorific one his prize implies, and **it needs an independent fetch before being treated as fact**. The stop says so.

**The merger, made exact.** 424B4: "**On March 30, 2000**, X.com merged with Confinity." **X.com Corporation was the legal survivor**; the rename came later. The pool's "1-30 March 2000" range collapses to the one date. Confinity holders taking about 46.5% is **[R]**, and the shared-building claim is **dossier-only**; the two atlas pins are a block apart.

**The Gausebeck-Levchin test, tag dropped.** The distorted-text verification system built with **David Gausebeck** is well attested in later retrospectives, but **neither "Gausebeck" nor any description of it appears anywhere in the final prospectus**, searched in full, and the **2000** date has **no primary support**. Tag drops to **[R]**: the claim stays, the certainty does not.

**Musk's removal, September 2000.** The board action replacing Musk with Thiel during his honeymoon is **[A]** across `elon_musk.journey.json`, `peter_thiel.journey.json` and the corporate histories. **Levchin's presence at it is nowhere stated**, so proximity is inferred and the stop says so. That he opposed Musk's technical direction is **[R]**. **Renaming, unresolved:** Wikipedia dates the adoption of the PayPal name to **June 2001**, other sources to **February 2001**.

**The IPO, two dates split.** Priced at **$13.00 the evening of 14 February 2002**; the 424B4 was filed **15 February** and **trading opened that day** under **PYPL**; **5,400,000 shares** plus an **810,000-share over-allotment**. **eBay, 3 October 2002, $1.5 billion in stock [A]**. `peter_thiel.journey.json` deliberately leaves a further contradiction open ($1.5B announced against $1.4B at closing), and this file does not close it either. **Departure, December 2002 [A]**, the "psychologically difficult" aftermath being **[R]**, his own interview language. **MIT Technology Review TR100, 2002 [A]**.
