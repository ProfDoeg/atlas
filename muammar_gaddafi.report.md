# Muammar Gaddafi: research report

*2026-08-11, Atlas of Journeys. Slug `muammar_gaddafi`. Companion: `muammar_gaddafi.journey.json`, 8 segments, 45 stops.*

Legend: **[A]** = attested, source named on the line. **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition, or tertiary-only claim. Contradictions are printed, not adjudicated. Gaps are stated as gaps.

The verified pool arrived with corrections attached to about a third of its entries: bad coordinates, truncated quotations, two events fused into one, a date contradiction between its own lenses. Those are carried through and named where they bite.

---

## 1. Origins, 1942 to 1965

Born to a Bedouin herding family of the Qadhadhfa tribe **in** Qasr Abu Hadi, outside Sirte, Italian Libya **[A Wikipedia, 'Muammar Gaddafi']**; the pool said "near", Wikipedia says in. Primary school at Sirte, the only boy without a fixed house **[A Blundy and Lycett, *Qaddafi and the Libyan Revolution*, 1987]**. Sabha 1956-1961: preparatory school, pan-Arabist cell, expulsion for agitation **[A Blundy and Lycett; Vandewalle, *A History of Modern Libya*]**; October 1961, a demonstration against Syria's secession from the UAR, arrest, and the family expelled from Sabha **[A Wikipedia]**, month attested and day unrecorded. Misrata 1961-1963, where the Free Officers network is assembled **[A Vandewalle]**; Royal Military Academy, Benghazi, 1963-1965 **[A BBC obituary 21 Oct 2011; Wikipedia]**.

- **The birth date is disputed and cannot carry [A].** Wikipedia: "c. 1942". Simple Wikipedia and many 2011 obituaries: 7 June 1943. No registration exists for a herding family in that district. The journey carries `1942-06-07` with the dispute in `date_confidence`. **[R]**
- Paternal grandfather Abdessalam Bouminyar said to have been killed fighting the Italian invasion of 1911: family tradition, undocumented **[R Wikipedia]**. The journey frames the 2008 reparations treaty against it, so that frame rests on an [R].
- **Britain, 1966. Contradiction, left standing.** The pool gave "British Army Signal School, Beaconsfield" at 51.61, -0.6459 as [A]. Beaconsfield hosted the **Army School of Education** (an English course); signals instruction was at **Bovington Camp, Dorset**. Wikipedia names no British location, only "further military training". Year **[A Wikipedia]**, institution and town **[R]**.

## 2. The coup and the first republic, 1969 to 1972

1 September 1969, Operation Jerusalem: airports, police depots and radio stations seized in Tripoli and Benghazi, King Idris abroad, the Libyan Arab Republic proclaimed, bloodless; he chairs the Revolutionary Command Council, takes the rank of colonel, holds no constitutional office **[A Wikipedia]**. 1970: British and American bases withdrawn, Jihad Fund founded, Italian assets expropriated in October and the roughly 12,000-strong Italian community expelled with the remaining Libyan Jews. September 1971: he resigns his posts and returns within a month. December 1971: BP's Sarir share expropriated. **[A Wikipedia]** The 7 October day in the journey is the later national holiday, **[R]**. Damascus talks toward the Federation of Arab Republics, proclaimed 1972, never functional **[A Vandewalle]**; the 17 April 1971 signature date is standard, **[R]** at day level. Repeated Cairo travel to Nasser and Sadat **[A Blundy and Lycett]**, no meeting individually dated, so that stop is pinned to Nasser's death. 1972: Libya funds Black September, which carries out the Munich massacre **[A Wikipedia]**; the source carries funding, not an operational role, and the journey claims no more.

- **Correction, city-level.** The pool placed the Berka barracks he personally seized in **Tripoli** at 32.8872, 13.1913. Berka (Barqa) barracks is in **Benghazi**; he led from there while Umar Muhayshi took the Tripoli barracks and Abdessalam Jalloud the anti-aircraft batteries **[A Wikipedia, '1969 Libyan revolution']**. The journey splits the stop: Benghazi at 32.12, 20.07, and the pool's coordinate (a correct Tripoli pin) for the proclamation.

## 3. The Popular Revolution and the Jamahiriya, 1973 to 1977

21 February 1973: Flight 114 shot down by Israeli fighters over Israeli-held Sinai, 108 dead including former foreign minister Salah Busir **[A Wikipedia, 'Libyan Arab Airlines Flight 114']**. 16 April 1973: the Zuwarah speech and its five points, including the General People's Committees **[A Wikipedia; Vandewalle]**. September 1973: 51 percent of foreign oil producers nationalized; March 1976: RCC members executed over a coup plot; 2 March 1977: the Jamahiriya proclaimed **[A Wikipedia]**.

- **Flight 114 framing dropped.** The pool carried the year only and asserted "Libya alleges the strike targeted" Busir. Unsupported: the aircraft strayed off course in bad weather with instrument failure, and the recrimination fell on Egypt.
- **Internal contradiction, flagged.** The pool's chronology dates the Zuwarah speech **16 April**, its geography **15 April**. 16 April is used because the Wikipedia article carries it; the contradiction is printed in `date_confidence` and not resolved.
- **The Green Book, corrected against the pool's own chronology.** The pool said "three volumes", dated 1975. Per the dedicated article: **Part I late 1975, Part II early 1977, Part III September 1981** **[A Wikipedia, 'The Green Book (Muammar Gaddafi)']**; the main Gaddafi article's "1975 and 1979" conflicts with it. Reception **[A same]**: English and bilingual London editions 1976, 42 languages, two hours a week in Libyan schools.
- The pool gives only "Libya" for the Jamahiriya declaration. The congress that issued it sat at Sabha, but no pool source says so, so the journey attaches it to the Bab al-Azizia stop. A deliberate under-claim.
- **Bab al-Azizia: three coordinates in the pool, all wrong.** **32.8567, 13.1633** (geography, twice) and **32.862, 13.1913** (afterlife). The compound is at **32.87222, 13.17361** **[A Wikipedia, 'Bab al-Azizia']**, used at all three journey stops.

## 4. The long reach, 1972 to 1994

Aouzou Strip annexed and occupied 1973-1994, the ICJ ruling for Chad in February 1994 by 16 votes to 1 with withdrawal complete by June **[A ICJ, *Territorial Dispute (Libya/Chad)*; Wikipedia, 'Aouzou Strip']**. Chad 1978-1987 including the Toyota War **[A Wikipedia; Vandewalle]**, the March 1987 pin being the decisive phase and day-level dating **[R]**. Uganda 1972-1979: alliance with Idi Amin, Libyan troops to Kampala taking losses when Tanzania invades **[A BBC]**. Burkina Faso, 1980s: ties with Sankara within the wider funding of African movements **[A press and academic accounts]**, undated. Moscow, 1970s-1980s: repeated visits and Soviet arms purchases **[A CIA declassified assessments]**, no visit individually dated.

- **Aouzou coordinate correction.** The pool gave 21.2833, 17.4167; the oasis lies at roughly **21.83, 17.42**, some 60 km north. Longitude right.
- **Tripoli, 1974.** José López Rega, Isabel Perón's minister of social welfare and founder of the Triple A, travels to Tripoli for the Argentine government. **No agenda, interlocutor or result is recorded in any reachable source** **[A `jose_lopez_rega.journey.json`]**. The attached Licio Gelli material (P2 grandmaster brokering oil and arms between Libya, Italy and Argentina) is flagged by `licio_gelli.journey.json` itself as reconstruction: **[R]**, and the campa says "later and unverified accounts".
- **Mecca is a tradition, not a fact.** The pool has him perform the hajj "at least once during his rule", tagged [R], **no year confirmed**; `date_confidence` states that 1980 is a placeholder inside the reign. The interlock lens separately found that **no journey file documents any Gaddafi connection to the Kaaba or the Temple Mount**. Belgrade attendance at Non-Aligned summitry is likewise **[R]** and dropped rather than pinned.

## 5. Isolation, 1986 to 1999

15 April 1986, Operation El Dorado Canyon: US strikes on Tripoli and Benghazi including Bab al-Azizia, retaliation for the West Berlin discotheque bombing **[A US DoD records; BBC obituary 2011]**. 21 December 1988: Pan Am 103, 270 dead; indictments of Megrahi and Fhimah 13 November 1991; UN sanctions 1992 **[A Wikipedia, 'Pan Am Flight 103']**. September 1989: UTA 772 destroyed over Niger, attributed to Libyan agents **[A Wikipedia]**. 29 June 1996: the Abu Salim massacre after a prison riot, admitted 18 April 2004 **[A Human Rights Watch (2006); Wikipedia, 'Abu Salim Prison']**. Mandela's defence of Libya against Western pressure through the 1990s **[A press coverage]**. 5 April 1999: the two accused handed to Scottish police in the Netherlands; trial 3 May 2000 to 31 January 2001, Megrahi convicted, Fhimah acquitted **[A Wikipedia, 'Lockerbie trial'; BBC]**.

- The claim that an adopted infant daughter, Hana, died in the 1986 raid is his own: **[R]**, Wikipedia recording "much skepticism". UTA 772's 19 September day and crash coordinates are the standard published ones, not re-verified in this pool: **[R]**.
- **Reagan's sentence, corrected.** The pool ended it at "a goal of world revolution." The transcript (*The President's News Conference*, 9 April 1986, American Presidency Project) opens "Well,", keeps the article in "a world revolution", and runs on through "Moslem fundamentalist revolution, which is targeted on many of his own Arab compatriots." All three restored. **Reagan does not name Gaddafi there.**
- **Abu Salim, two corrections.** The prison is at **32.8333, 13.1739**, not 32.85, 13.2167 (about 4.5 km east); HRW's estimate is **roughly 1,270** dead, not "estimated 1,200".
- **The pool's worst coordinate defect.** Pretoria as **25.7479, 28.2293**, a positive latitude putting the pin in northern Sudan, 5,700 km off; it is **-25.7461, 28.1881**. The October 1997 visit date is the usual one and **[R]**, the pool giving only "1990s".
- **Camp Zeist.** The pool called it "The Hague / Camp Zeist" at 52.0907, 5.25, matching neither city; Camp Zeist is near Utrecht at approximately **52.128, 5.287**. "The Hague" removed.

## 6. Rehabilitation, 2003 to 2010

2003: WMD programme renounced under international inspection, over 2 billion US dollars paid to Lockerbie families; from 1999 the turn to pan-Africanism **[A Wikipedia; 'Pan Am Flight 103']**, the 19 December day being standard and **[R]**. December 2007, Paris: five-day state visit at Sarkozy's invitation, tent in the gardens of the Hôtel de Marigny, domestic criticism **[A BBC and Reuters]**; Madrid 2007, same post-sanctions push **[A press reporting]**, folded into the Paris stop. 30 August 2008, Benghazi: Berlusconi signs the Treaty of Friendship, Partnership and Cooperation, Italian investment over twenty years as colonial reparation plus a formal apology, against Libyan interdiction of migrant boats to Lampedusa **[A `silvio_berlusconi.journey.json`]**. June 2009, Rome: state visit, tent in the park of Villa Doria Pamphilj, a photograph of a Libyan rebel of the colonial period pinned to his uniform **[A Reuters and BBC; same file]**, the rebel unnamed here, see Gaps item 5. 23 September 2009: 96-minute UN General Assembly address, pages torn from the Charter **[A UN transcript; BBC and Reuters]**, encampments blocked at Bedford, New York and Englewood, New Jersey **[A NYT and Reuters]**.

- **A conflation in the pool, split into two stops.** Its geography lens carried one entry: proclaimed "King of Kings of Africa" at the AU summit in Addis Ababa, February 2009. **Two events were fused.** The proclamation was **in Benghazi on 28 August 2008**, by more than 200 African traditional kings and chiefs **[A Wikipedia, 'King of Kings of Africa']**; the **AU chairmanship** is distinct, elected 2 February 2009 at Addis Ababa **[A Wikipedia; BBC News]**. The journey gives them two cities and seven months.

## 7. The uprising and the death, 2011

February 2011: protests in Benghazi over the arrest of a human rights lawyer spread across the east into armed uprising **[A BBC timeline]**. March: NATO air operations begin **[A Wikipedia]**. February to August: staged foreign press briefings at the Rixos Hotel **[A BBC and Reuters correspondents]**. 29 August: Safia, Aisha, Hannibal and Mohammed cross into Algeria; September: Saadi given refuge at Niamey despite an arrest warrant **[A BBC]**; 2012 onward part of the family relocates to Oman on terms and dates not documented **[R]**. August to September: refuge at Bani Walid after the fall of Tripoli **[A Reuters and Al Jazeera]**. 20 October, Sirte: convoy struck while fleeing, captured in a drainage culvert, beaten and killed by NTC fighters **[A BBC, 'Gaddafi killed as Sirte falls'; NATO statement]**.

- The arrested lawyer represented the Abu Salim families; the journey draws that line because both facts are separately attested, not because a source draws it.
- **21 February 2011, Malta: two corrections.** The defecting aircraft are usually reported specifically as **Mirage F1s**, and the landing was at **Malta International Airport, Luqa** (approx. 35.857, 14.478), not Valletta city (35.8989, 14.5146) as the pool pinned it. The pool also flags that **the date could not be re-verified against a live source**: reported but unconfirmed.

## 8. Afterlife, 2011 to 2026

21-24 October 2011: the body kept in a commercial freezer at Misrata with public viewing, NTC oil minister Ali Tarhouni saying it was kept "to make sure that everybody knows he is dead"; 25 October, burial announced before dawn at an undisclosed desert location with Mutassim and Abu Bakr Younis Jabr, the information minister citing a ruling that the place must not be known, lest it become a shrine **[A Wikipedia, 'Death of Muammar Gaddafi']**. Bab al-Azizia overrun 23 August 2011 and its statues broken, including the fist crushing a US fighter jet; bulldozers on the outer walls from 17 October, stopping short of levelling; by February 2012 the Friday market had moved into the ruins **[A Wikipedia, 'Bab al-Azizia']**. 2011: Green Book copies burned, its monuments demolished **[A Wikipedia, 'The Green Book']**. Saif al-Islam captured near Sabha 19 November 2011, sentenced to death in absentia 28 July 2015, released by his Zintan captors 10 June 2017; on 14 November 2021, in his first public appearance since 2014, he registers a presidential candidacy at Sabha, rejected 16 November and reinstated 2 December; assassinated at Zintan 3 February 2026, buried at Bani Walid 6 February **[A Wikipedia, 'Saif al-Islam Gaddafi']**.

- **The grave remains unlocated. [R]**: the pool records that **no primary source was fetched confirming its status as of 2026** (search budget exhausted mid-task). The journey's coordinate there, 30.0, 17.0, is **an approximation of the desert interior south of Sirte, explicitly not a claim about the site**; `date_confidence` says so. Survival and body-double rumours since 2011 are **[R]**, likewise unpinned.
- The father was expelled from Sabha in 1961 for a speech; the son registered for the presidency there in 2021. The journey states both and does not gloss the pairing.

2. **The hajj.** No year confirmed; the pilgrimage itself is tradition.
3. **Kaaba and Temple Mount interlocks.** The lens grepped every journey file and found **no textual intersection** with Gaddafi material. Closing that needs fresh research, not inheritance.
4. **The grave.** No primary source in this pool confirms its status as of 2026.

## Quotation integrity

Five quotations arrived truncated or spliced. Recorded so the corrections are auditable.

1. **Green Book, plebiscites.** Cut at "the modern conception of democracy.", dropping the clause supplying the reason, "as they are not allowed to say more than 'yes' or 'no'". Restored.
2. **Green Book, the press.** Cut at "is groundless.", dropping "because it actually expresses the viewpoint of that private individual." Restored.
3. **Letter to Obama.** Cut at "by means of missiles.", dropping "and aircraft, or by backing armed member of AlQuaeda in Benghazi", and silently capitalizing "democracy". Restored verbatim. **The letter is signed "Tripoli 5.4.2011"**; 6 April is the reporting date.
4. **Letter to Obama, the Baraka line.** Pool: "your intervention is a must." Actual: "your intervention **is the name of the U.S.A.** is a must, so that Nato would withdraw finally from the Libyan affair." An internal splice without ellipsis, smoothing broken English into a sentence he did not write; the pool also miscalls it the salutation. **Dropped.**
5. **Reagan, "mad dog".** See section 5.

The Fallaci "opposition" line was missing an article ("what need is there for **an** opposition?"); restored, its attribution resting on Wikiquote and mirrors since the *Corriere della Sera* original was not inspected. The Fallaci-on-Gaddafi line ("He's clinically stupid...") is [R] in the pool because the ellipsis suggests trimming; not used. The Internet Archive identifier the pool cited, `gaddafi-green-book_202407`, **404s**; the working one is `gaddafi-green-book`.
