# Diego Rivera (1886-1957): research report
*Compiled 2026-08-12. Slug `diego_rivera`. Companion: `diego_rivera.journey.json`, 8 segments, 44 stops.*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named · **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or contested · *Corrected* marks where the verification pass overturned the gathering pass, the superseded value kept so the disagreement stays visible. Contradictions are flagged, not adjudicated.

---

## 1. Guanajuato and the Academy, 1886-1907

- Born **8 December 1886**, Guanajuato, to Diego Rivera Acosta and María del Pilar Barrientos; twin brother **Carlos** dies at about two. **[A: en.wikipedia]**
- The chalkboards installed on the walls so the child could draw: **[R]**, anecdote with no document behind it.
- Birth house, now **Museo Casa Diego Rivera**, Positos 47. *Corrected:* **21.0183, -101.25527** (pool gave 21.019, -101.2574, ~230 m off), and the house was **not** converted by the family; it became a museum in **September 1975**. **[A: es.wikipedia]**
- Family moves to the capital near the **Alameda Central** c. 1893 when the mining ventures fail. **[A: Marnham; Wolfe]** Month and day unrecorded.
- **Academia de San Carlos.** *Corrected coordinate:* **19.43309, -99.12884** (Academia 22), not 19.4327, -99.1301. **[A: en.wikipedia]**
  - **Contradiction, unresolved.** Chronology lens: entry **1896**, "at around age ten." Geography lens: attendance **1898-1905**, while itself saying "at age ten." Born Dec 1886, age ten falls 1896-97. Journey file uses 1896-01-01 and carries the conflict in `date_confidence`.
- **Veracruz**, January 1907, on the pension of governor **Teodoro Dehesa**. *Corrected coordinate:* **19.19028, -96.15333** (pool gave 19.1738, -96.1342, ~2.7 km south). Also flagged: es.wikipedia records an **earlier 1905 pension from Justo Sierra**, so a single Dehesa stipend is a simplification; the campa names both. **[A]**
- **The cannibalism claim.** *Corrected:* the gathering pass placed it in **Paris, c. 1910s**; en.wikipedia places it in **Mexico, 1904**, so the file attaches the quotation to the San Carlos years. Wikipedia calls the anecdote "factually suspect or an elaborate lie." **[R]**

## 2. Spain and the Cubist decade, 1907-1920

- Madrid under **Eduardo Chicharro**, 1907; copying Goya and Velázquez at the **Prado**. **[A]**
- **Toledo** for El Greco: **[R]**, undated in the major biographies. Folded into the Prado stop rather than pinned as a separate undated coordinate.
- **Meeting Angelina Beloff. Contradiction, unresolved:** Marnham gives **Brussels**, other accounts **Bruges**. The file pins Bruges and says so.
- **The Beloff marriage. Contradiction, unresolved, three ways:** (1) en.wikipedia 'Angelina Beloff', married in Paris **by the end of 1909**; (2) the Rivera infobox, **m. 1911, div. 1921**; (3) several biographers, **never a legal marriage**. The pool contradicts itself: the geography lens calls her his "common-law wife" while the chronology lens tags a 1911 marriage **[A]**. *Corrected:* tag downgraded to **[R]**, all three readings in `date_confidence`.
- **La Ruche.** *Corrected, two errors:* coordinates **48.83243, 2.29681**, 2 Passage de Dantzig (pool gave 48.8398, 2.3007, ~880 m off); and the quarter is the **15th arrondissement, Vaugirard**, not Montparnasse. Chagall, Soutine, Modigliani, Léger, Zadkine, Archipenko confirmed residents. **[A: fr.wikipedia]**
- Studio at **Rue du Départ 26**, 1913-1917; **Modigliani portrait 1914**; public break with Cubism c. 1917 after the quarrel with **Pierre Reverdy**. **[A: Marnham; Wolfe]**
- **The son Diego.** *Corrected:* born **1916**, died **1918** at fourteen months of lung complications, not born and died in 1916 (en.wikipedia: "Diego (1916-1918)"; the Beloff article gives age and cause). Sources giving one date have conflated the two. **[A]**
- Daughter **Marika Rivera** born to **Marevna** (Maria Vorobieff-Stebelska), 1919. **[A]** Break from Cubism complete by **1918**. **[A: PBS American Experience]**

## 3. Italy and the return to Mexico, 1920-1927

- **Honest gap.** The 1920 Italian itinerary is reconstructed from later interviews and sketchbooks, not from dated correspondence. **Florence** is the strongest stop; **Assisi** and **Ravenna** are tagged **[R]** in the journey file for that reason. What is [A] is the outcome: he returns in 1921 committed to fresco.
- *Corrected coordinates:* Santa Maria Novella **43.7746, 11.2493** (~420 m off); Assisi **43.07472, 12.60556** (~450 m south); San Vitale **44.4206, 12.1964** (~350 m off).
- *Content caveat:* Masaccio's principal cycle is at Santa Maria del Carmine and Santa Maria Novella holds his *Trinity*; Ghirlandaio's Tornabuoni Chapel is at Santa Maria Novella. The campa names both churches rather than repeating the pool's compression.
- **'Creation'**, Bolívar Amphitheatre, **San Ildefonso**, begun January 1922, unveiled 1923, painted in **encaustic**, not true fresco, under **José Vasconcelos**. **[A]** *Coordinate:* the file inherits the canonical pin **19.4361, -99.1305** from `artaud` and `frida_kahlo`, which matches the corrected en.wikipedia value (19.43605, -99.13066) to four decimals.
- **SEP murals**, 124 panels. *Corrected:* campaign runs **September 1922 to 1928**, not 1923-1928 (en.wikipedia: "begun in September 1922 … finished in 1928"). Panel count confirmed. *Coordinate:* inherits canonical **19.4372, -99.1315** from `frida_kahlo`; the geography lens proposed 19.4365, -99.1315, ~80 m apart, canonical pin wins by house rule.
- **Chapingo.** *Corrected:* **1924-1927**, not 1925; coordinates **19.489644, -98.893551** (~570 m off); 41 panels over 700 m²; principal panel *Tierra Fecunda*. **[A: en.wikipedia]**
- Co-founds the Revolutionary Union of Technical Workers, Painters and Sculptors; joins the **Mexican Communist Party**; divorces Beloff and marries **Guadalupe Marín**, June 1922. **[A]**

## 4. Moscow and the marriage to Kahlo, 1927-1930

- **Moscow, November 1927**, delegation for the October Revolution decennial; meets **Alfred H. Barr Jr.**, which leads to the MoMA show. **[A: Marnham; MoMA institutional history]**
- **Red Army club commission, 1928. [R], honest gap:** the building is not identified in any reachable source and accounts differ on how much was painted before he was ordered out of the USSR. The file pins Krasnaya Presnya at district resolution (55.76, 37.56) and says so.
- **First meeting with Kahlo. Contradiction, unresolved:** sources range from **1922** (she watched him paint *Creation* as a schoolgirl) to **1928** (the SEP scaffold, three canvases). The file uses 1928 and states the range. Herrera dates the quoted recollection c. 1928.
- **Marriage 21 August 1929, Coyoacán**; he is 42, she 22. **[A]** **Expelled from the Mexican Communist Party, 1929**; seeks readmission 1954. **[A]**
- **Palacio de Cortés, Cuernavaca**, 1929-1930, commissioned by ambassador **Dwight Morrow**, culminating in the Zapata panel. **[A: INAH]** File inherits canonical **18.9219, -99.2318** from `frida_kahlo` / `cortes` over the geography lens's 18.9186, -99.2342.

## 5. The American walls, 1930-1934

- **Pacific Stock Exchange / City Club, San Francisco**, arrival November 1930, fee **$2,500**, arranged by **Timothy Pflueger**. **[A]**
  - **Contradiction inside the pool, flagged.** The geography lens calls Kahlo his "fourth wife" at this stop and Emma Hurtado a "third marriage" at the death stop. Both cannot hold. Order: **Beloff, Marín, Kahlo, Hurtado**, so Kahlo is **third** and Hurtado **fourth** (fifth ceremony, counting the 1940 remarriage). The file uses third and fourth.
- **California School of Fine Arts**, *The Making of a Fresco*, 1931. *Corrected coordinate:* **37.803456, -122.417144** (800 Chestnut St); the pool's -122.4194 is near the generic San Francisco centroid. **[A]**
- **MoMA retrospective.** (1) **Date contested:** the pool gives 1931-11, which is when Rivera **arrived** in New York to paint portable frescoes; MoMA's record gives **23 December 1931 to 27 January 1932**. File uses 1931-12-23 and carries the conflict. (2) **Place corrected:** the pool's 40.7614, -73.9776 is MoMA's **current** 53rd Street site, on an entry labelled "original building." MoMA opened 1929 in the **Heckscher Building, 730 Fifth Avenue** (~40.7633, -73.9744), reaching 53rd Street only in **1939**; the file uses the Heckscher coordinates. (3) **Inaccessible source:** see Sources. **Re-verify before promotion to canon.**
- **Detroit Industry Murals**, 27 panels, 1932-33, part-funded by **Edsel Ford**. **[A]** Canonical pin **42.3594, -83.0644** from `frida_kahlo.journey.json`; the DIA's own coordinate is -83.0654, ~80 m apart.
- **Man at the Crossroads.** *Corrected, several:* proposed by **Abby Aldrich Rockefeller** (Jan 1932), contracted Nov 1932, begun March 1933, so the pool's attribution of the commission to **John D. Rockefeller Jr.** personally is dropped. Work **halted 10 May 1933** by **Hugh Robertson** of Todd, Robertson & Todd (Nelson Rockefeller had ordered it plastered over); Rivera offered **Lincoln** and refused to remove **Lenin**; paid **$21,000** in full, netting about $7,000; **destroyed February 1934**. The pool collapsed halt and destruction into "1933," and the afterlife lens credited the destruction to Nelson Rockefeller's order, which overstates the record. **[A]**
- Returns to Mexico **December 1933**; repaints as **Man, Controller of the Universe**, Palacio de Bellas Artes, **1934**, with Rockefeller added to the cast. **[A]**

## 6. Coyoacán, Trotsky, the National Palace, 1934-1947

- **Casa Estudio San Ángel.** *Corrected:* coordinates **19.34956, -99.19733** (~760 m off); built **1931-32**, occupied **1934**, museum from **16 December 1986**. **[A: es.wikipedia]**
- **Palacio Nacional.** *Corrected:* coordinates **19.4325, -99.13139** (~190 m off); staircase **1929-1935** confirmed; corridor / pre-Hispanic campaign is **1944-1952**, not the widely repeated 1942-1951. **[A: es.wikipedia]**
- **Trotsky's asylum, 1937**: Rivera petitions **Lázaro Cárdenas**; Trotsky and Natalia Sedova live in the Casa Azul to 1939; Trotsky assassinated four streets away, 1940. **[A]**
- **Manifesto for an Independent Revolutionary Art, 1938**, signed Breton and Rivera, ghostwritten in part by Trotsky, drafted at the Blue House. **[A: `frida_kahlo.journey.json`]**
- **Divorce, 1939. [A]** Month and day absent from the pool, defaulted to January 1 with the hedge in `date_confidence`.
- **Pan American Unity**, begun **5 June 1940** at $1,000 a month, complete by **29 November 1940**. **[A]** *Coordinate gap:* the pool gives none; the file uses **Treasure Island, 37.8235, -122.3706**, and says this is the fair site, not a surveyed mural position. **Flag for review.** *Present location corrected:* the pool's "on long-term display at SFMOMA" is **out of date**. Shown in SFMOMA's Roberts Family Gallery from **June 2021**, then **2024** disassembled into storage at an undisclosed CCSF location. **[A]**
- **Remarriage, San Francisco, 8 December 1940**, on his birthday. **[A]**
- **Pyramid in the Casa Azul garden, 1941. [A: `frida_kahlo.journey.json`]** Direct Rivera authorship of a stop already in the atlas.
- **Anahuacalli.** *Corrected:* construction begins **1942**, not 1945; coordinates **19.3227, -99.14451** (~730 m off); finished **1963**, opened **18 September 1964** by **Ruth Rivera** with **O'Gorman** and **Heriberto Pagelson**; collection over **40,000** pieces. **[A]**
- **Dream of a Sunday Afternoon in the Alameda Central.** *Corrected:* **1946-1947**, not 1947; painted for the **Versalles dining room** of the Hotel del Prado, 4.70 x 15.6 m; hotel damaged in the **1985 earthquake**, mural moved to the **Museo Mural Diego Rivera**. **[A: es.wikipedia]**

## 7. Last years and afterlife, 1954-1957 and after

- **Kahlo dies 13 July 1954. [R]** for Rivera's reported reaction: "the most tragic day of my life" comes from his own later published statements, repeated without a document under it.
- **Marriage to Emma Hurtado, 29 July 1955**, his dealer since 1946. **[A]** Fourth wife; see the numbering contradiction in section 5.
- **Moscow cancer treatment, 1955-56. [R], honest gap:** the institution is unconfirmed. **Botkin Hospital** is the most-cited candidate and the file says exactly that rather than asserting it.
- **East Berlin, 21 March 1956.** *Corrected, place not date.* de.wikipedia confirms the GDR visit and the corresponding membership of the **Akademie der Künste**, but the pool's 52.5163, 13.3777 is **Pariser Platz**, the present-day AdK site. The GDR academy was at **Robert-Koch-Platz 10** (~52.529, 13.379); the documented appearance, photographed with **Rudolf Engel** and **Otto Nagel**, is at **Ostbahnhof** (~52.5103, 13.4344). File pins the station, where the evidence is. **[A]**
- **Exekatlkalli, Acapulco, 1956-57. [R], dropped from the journey file.** The address is unconfirmed and the offered coordinates (16.846, -99.889) come from general hillside description. Recorded as a real late work whose geography the atlas cannot yet carry honestly.
- **Death 24 November 1957**, San Ángel studio, **23:20** per es.wikipedia, heart failure after cancer treatment, age 70. **[A]**
- **Interment 26 November 1957**, Rotonda de las Personas Ilustres (then Rotonda de los **Hombres** Ilustres), Panteón Civil de Dolores. *Corrected:* the date is the 26th, not the 25th; coordinates **19.407, -99.204**. The pool's two lenses gave 19.4103, -99.1919 and 19.4139, -99.1932, both ~1.3-1.4 km off, and they disagreed with each other. **[A: en.wikipedia]**
- **The ashes. [R]:** the wish to have them mixed with Kahlo's at Anahuacalli rests on secondary biography (Herrera and others), not on the primary record checked this session. Carried in the campa as tradition.
- **Posthumous [A]:** *My Art, My Life* published **1960** (Gladys March, from c. 2,000 pages of recorded conversation); Casa Azul opens as **Museo Frida Kahlo, 1958**; 'Viva la Vida', Moscow, **2018**; 'Diego Rivera's America', SFMOMA, **2022-2023**.
- **Monument to Rivera and Kahlo, Coyoacán. [R], not pinned.** The monument exists (es.wikipedia carries a captioned image) but the pool's coordinates for it are the **Casa Azul's**, duplicated verbatim from the ashes entry. Coordinates would be false precision.

## Quotes: what survived verification

Seven quotations reached the journey file. Two were found corrupted in their circulating form.

1. **"... rather than mutilate the conception, I should prefer the physical destruction of the conception in its entirety, but preserving, at least, its integrity."** *Corrected:* across ~44 Open Library hits the wording is **"I should prefer,"** not "I shall prefer" (Wolfe; Hurlburt; Roussel; Krinsky; *My Art, My Life*). Two cautions in `quote_source`: some editions read **"composition"** for "conception" (Rochfort; Helms); and Okrent says the letter was **drafted by Ben Shahn**, so it is cited as sent over Rivera's signature, not written by him.
2. **The cannibalism passage**, *My Art, My Life* p. 20. **[A]** as quotation, **[R]** as event.
3. **"To affirm 'God does not exist,'..."** Stein, *Siqueiros*, p. 176. **No precise date in the source**, only "nine years later" than the 1947-48 mural, i.e. roughly the mid-1950s.
4. **"My Jewishness is the dominant element in my life."** Weakest chain in the set: journalism (2010) reporting a 1935 statement, not the document. Labelled in `quote_source`.
5. **Rivera on Kahlo's canvases**, c. 1928, Herrera pp. 86-87, via Wikipedia, not Herrera directly.
6. **"Here it is—the might, the power, the energy, the sadness, the glory, the youthfulness of our lands."** *Corrected twice:* the punctuation is an **em dash**, not a comma (Wolfe p. 278), kept verbatim in the `quote` field, which house style exempts. And the **context warning stands**: Wikiquote's gloss "on the layout of New York City" is unsupported, and every corroborating text (Herrera; Lacey, *Ford*; Wollen & Kerr, *Autopia*) is a machine or Detroit title, so the file says only "on the American scene." Martin Walker's variant drops "the energy."
7. **"Your engineers are your great artists..."** *Source upgraded:* the primary is **Wolfe**, who reports it as said **to him personally**. The Yale-New Haven curriculum unit (99.02.06) Wikiquote cites does **not** contain the passage on reachable pages; that citation is dead and dropped.

## Interlocks with the existing atlas

Named directly in campa (mutual gaze):

- **`frida_kahlo`**: San Ildefonso 1922 (she watches him paint *Creation*); the SEP scaffold 1928; the Casa Azul marriage 1929; San Francisco 1930 and Eloesser; Detroit 1932 and the Henry Ford Hospital miscarriage; Trotsky's asylum 1937; the 1938 manifesto; the 1939 divorce and her Paris Mexique show; the 1940 remarriage; the garden pyramid 1941; her death 1954.
- **`cortes`**: Coyoacán as Cortés's 1521 headquarters under the Casa Azul; the Palacio de Cortés at Cuernavaca, where Rivera paints the conquest onto the conqueror's own loggia; the garden pyramid, layering 1521, 1938 and 1941 on one coordinate.
- **`artaud`**: San Ildefonso, where Artaud lectures in 1936 "under the murals of Orozco," fourteen years after Rivera painted *Creation* in the same complex. The pool is explicit that those murals were **Orozco's, not Rivera's**; the campa says so.
- **`che_guevara`**: Mexico City 1954-55, "the city of Trotsky's tomb." One inferential step, stated as tomb-to-asylum, not as a meeting.

**Canonical pins inherited byte-identical:** San Ildefonso 19.4361/-99.1305 · SEP 19.4372/-99.1315 · Casa Azul 19.3551/-99.1624 · Mexico City 19.4326/-99.1332 · Palacio de Cortés 18.9219/-99.2318 · SF Stock Exchange 37.7913/-122.4013 · Detroit Institute of Arts 42.3594/-83.0644 · Rockefeller Center 40.7587/-73.9787 · Paris 48.8566/2.3522.

**Checked and negative.** The pool lists the **Kaaba** (21.4225, 39.8262), **Temple Mount** (31.778, 35.2354) and **Buenos Aires** (-34.6037, -58.3816) as canonical pins to test. No journey file and no source ties Rivera to any of them; they are **not** in the file. **Gap:** QUEUE.md groups Rivera and Kahlo with **Orozco** and **Siqueiros**, neither of whom has a journey file yet.
