- Maye married Errol in Pretoria 1970; her father Joshua Haldeman emigrated from Canada in 1950 and opened a chiropractic practice in the city; he was killed in a light-aircraft crash near Pretoria in January 1974, when Musk was two and a half, and his own file says the grandson "will remember nothing of him directly" **[A: `joshua_haldeman.journey.json`, stops 'Pretoria, the clinic near the square', 'Pretoria, the daughter marries', 'Wonderboom, the powerline']**.
- Parents divorce 1979 **[A]**. Part of the childhood in Durban **[R: Isaacson; city-level placement only]**.
- Schools: Waterkloof House Preparatory c. 1977-1983 **[R, years approximate]**; Bryanston High, Johannesburg, c. 1984-1986 **[R]**; Pretoria Boys High 1986-1988 **[A]**. Severe stairwell assault in adolescence: **[A]** as to the fact, **[R]** as to the campus.
- Leaves South Africa June 1989 on his mother's Canadian citizenship, partly to avoid SADF conscription **[A]**.

**Pin correction.** Pretoria Boys High (251 Roper Street, Brooklyn) is at `-25.7606, 28.2239` **[A]**; the geography pass carried `-25.755, 28.2166`, about a kilometre off.

**Pin decision, declared.** The geography pass gives the birth coordinate as `-25.7479, 28.2293`; the interlock pass gives the canonical corpus pin `-25.7461, 28.1881` from the grandfather's file, whose own `suggested_refs` name it as canonical.
