# Simón Rodríguez (Samuel Robinson), 1769-1854: research report

*Atlas of Journeys, 2026-08-23. Built from a verified pool assembled across four lenses (chronology, geography, quotations, interlock/afterlife), each re-checked against primary facsimiles. Where two lenses contradicted each other, the contradiction is reported, not harmonized.*


**Legend.** **[A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or single-witness retrospective. Unreachable sources are named with the reason. **The spine** is the Fundación Empresas Polar *Diccionario de Historia de Venezuela* (DHV), entries `Rodríguez, Simón` and `Juramento del Monte Sacro`; everything else is checked against it or flagged as going beyond it.

---

## 1. Caracas: the foundling, 1769-1797

- Born Caracas **28 October 1769** **[A: DHV]**. **Unresolved:** older literature, including Picón Salas's book title, gives **1771** **[R]**. Neither is anchored to a baptismal entry reproduced anywhere reachable.
- Cabildo appointment **May 1791** to the municipal *escuela de primeras letras* alongside Guillermo Pelgrón; **114 pupils** by 1793; the nine-year-old **Bolívar** enrolls **1792** **[A: DHV]**. **Corrected:** the geography lens staged the enrollment at the **Casa Natal del Libertador**, pinned 10.5063 / -66.9143, and both are wrong. The venue was the municipal school, and that coordinate is Plaza Bolívar (es.wikipedia puts the Casa Natal at 10.49528 / -66.91694). Stop retitled, pin declared a placeholder.
- Marries **María de los Santos Ronco** June 1793, describing himself *expósito de esta feligresía* **[A: DHV, citing the record]**. The strongest documentary fact about his parentage, and a negative one.
- *Reflexiones sobre los defectos que vician la escuela de primeras letras de Caracas*, **1794** **[A: es.wikipedia, cross-checked against DHV; text in the *Obras completas*]**. That it was not adopted is the uniform report of the secondary literature; no council minute was read here.
- **1795**, the orphaned Bolívar lodged some months in Rodríguez's household **[R]**: chronology lens **[R]**, geography lens **[A]** plus a coordinate, 10.507 / -66.915, which is **fabricated**, no source giving the location of his house. Discarded. The stop inherits byte-identical the pin `bolivar.journey.json` carries for the same episode, **10.5064 / -66.915, 1795-07-23**.
- **1797**, linked to the **Gual, España and Picornell** conspiracy; leaves Venezuela for good **[A: DHV]**. **Gap:** the sources say only *vinculado*, and no trial record was located.

## 2. Samuel Robinson, 1797-1804

- **Kingston, 1797**, takes the name **Samuel Robinson**, DHV says probably for concealment **[A]**. The *Robinson Crusoe / Émile* reading of the alias is a commonplace of later literature, not his own statement, and is in the campa with that status declared.
- **United States until late 1800** **[A: DHV]**; **Baltimore, printing trade** **[R]**: chronology lens tagged it **[A]**, geography **[R]**, and the DHV names neither city nor trade. No employer record located.
- **Bayonne, April 1801**, then Paris **[A: DHV]**. **Gap:** how he crossed the Atlantic, and at whose expense, is nowhere stated.
- **1801**, a Spanish *Atala* at Paris associated with Robinson and **Fray Servando Teresa de Mier** **[A: DHV]**; the division of translating labour is **disputed by scholars** and left disputed **[R]**.
- **1804**, reunion with Bolívar **[A: DHV]**. **Contradiction with the atlas:** `bolivar.journey.json` stages "the return of the teacher" at **1805-04-01**, the DHV at **1804**. The file takes the DHV date and inherits the Bolívar pin (48.8687 / 2.3401), so the two sit on one point while disagreeing openly about the year.

## 3. The Alps and the oath, 1805

- Route **[A: DHV]**: "por la vía de Lyon, Chambéry, Turín, Milán... Montichiari, Venecia y Florencia, llegaron hacia julio a Roma." **Milan, 26 May 1805**, Napoleon crowns himself King of Italy **[A: en.wikipedia, it.wikipedia]**; Spanish Wikipedia's "23 de mayo" is an outlier error. **Substantive doubt:** the pool asserted the two men **witnessed the coronation**, but the DHV itinerary includes **Montichiari**, where Bolívar saw the June troop review, and several scholars hold the party reached Milan **after** the crowning and saw the review instead. Presence at the crowning is **[R]**, and the campa states both readings.
- **Monte Sacro, 15 August 1805**, Bolívar swears before Rodríguez and **Fernando Rodríguez del Toro** **[A: DHV]**, corroborated by Bolívar's letter of 19 January 1824. **The wording is [R]:** the polished oath text is a later reconstruction transmitted through Rodríguez.
  - **Coordinate contradiction, unresolved.** The file inherits `bolivar.journey.json`'s **41.9404 / 12.5266** per the byte-identical rule; **it.wikipedia `Monte Sacro (Roma)` gives 41.9333 / 12.5333**, about 1.5 km southeast. Both are in `date_confidence`. **To move this pin, `bolivar.journey.json` must move with it.**
- **Struck as unsourced:** the pool's "lodge near the Spanish Steps." Nothing fetched names their Roman lodging, so the Piazza di Spagna stop became a Rome stop on the Bolívar pin.

## 4. The lost decades, 1806-1823

- Separates from Bolívar in Paris 1806; over twenty years across **Italy, the German lands, Prussia, Poland and Russia** **[R: DHV, resting on his own later retrospective]**.
- The famous self-account ("trabajé en un laboratorio de química industrial... juntas secretas de carácter socialista... regenté una escuela de primeras letras en un pueblecito de Rusia") is now **traced**: not a Rodríguez text but reported speech, **Manuel Uribe Ángel, *El Libertador, su ayo y su capellán* (Bogotá, 1884), p. 73**, via the Ayacucho 1990 chronology. **[R]**, but with a citable origin at last. **Corrected:** "Permanecí en Europa por más de veinte años" is **not part of that quotation** and is dropped; **Enfantin, Olinde Rodrigues and Pierre Leroux**, which the pool cut, are restored as the most dateable thing in it. **Chronological strain, flagged:** their Saint-Simonian church is a phenomenon of **1825 onward**, after Rodríguez left Europe in 1823, so either the recollection compresses a later contact or Uribe Ángel merged two things he said. Nothing reached settles it.
- **The coordinate is an avowed placeholder**, 55 / 37: no town, laboratory, meeting or school has ever been identified. The date, 1810, is an ordering device with no documentary basis, and says so.
- **1823, London**, meets or renews contact with **Andrés Bello**, decides to return **[A: DHV]**. No `andres_bello.journey.json` exists, so Bello is named in prose without an interlock.

## 5. The return: Cartagena to Chuquisaca, 1823-1826

- **Cartagena, 1823**, lands and resumes his own name **[A: DHV]**. The chronology lens dated the landing 1824, geography 1823; the DHV puts the return in 1823 and the Bogotá school-workshop in 1824, and the file splits them so.
- **Bogotá, 1824**, a school-workshop joining elementary teaching to productive crafts **[A: DHV]**, and **Bolívar's Pativilca letter of 19 January 1824**, "Ud. formó mi corazón para la libertad, para la justicia, para lo grande, para lo hermoso" **[A: DHV; fuller text in Lecuna, *Cartas del Libertador*, vol. IV, letter 1.047]**.
  - **A stop belongs to whoever was there.** Rodríguez was **not** at Pativilca; Bolívar was. The interlock lens proposed a Pativilca stop and it is refused: the letter is the **quote on Rodríguez's own Bogotá stop**, with `date_confidence` saying so.
- **Ecuadorian sierra, 1824-1825**: agriculture and botany at **Latacunga**, an eastern colonization plan at **Quito**, a mutual-aid society at **Ibarra** **[A: DHV]**. **Two hemisphere errors corrected:** the pool gave Latacunga **+0.9346** and Quito **+0.22**; both lie south of the equator, so the pins fell 207 km and 49 km north of their towns.
- **Lima, 1825**, received by Bolívar and added to his collaborators; from April the party travels Arequipa, Cuzco, Puno, then La Paz, Oruro, Potosí, Chuquisaca **[A: es.wikipedia, cross-checked against DHV]**.
- **Chuquisaca, November 1825**, appointed Director of Public Instruction, Physical and Mathematical Sciences and Arts, and Director General of Mines, Agriculture and Public Roads of Bolivia **[A: DHV]**. The only state office of his life.
- **Chuquisaca, 1826**, the workshop-school for poor, orphaned and Indigenous children; collapse in conflict with President **Sucre**, clergy and local officials over cost, discipline and feasibility; resignation **[A: es.wikipedia, cross-checked against DHV]**. **Not re-verified against a Bolivian archival document: the single largest documentary gap in the file.** Two unsourced clauses were removed: the pool's **7 January 1826** for Bolívar's departure, which nothing reached gives, and "No court finding of fraud or corruption was ever established against him," which defends against an accusation none of the sources makes.

## 6. Arequipa and the sentence, 1826-1833

- Settles at Arequipa, teaches, prints **[A: DHV]**; the *Pródromo* of *Sociedades americanas en 1828* appears **1828** **[A]**.
- **The largest correction here: "o Inventamos o Erramos" is not in the 1828 Arequipa Pródromo.** It is only in the **second edition, Lima 1842**. Zero hits for *inventamos* or *erramos* in either 1828 facsimile at archive.org; present in the 1842 file at line 867, under an Ayacucho editor's footnote reading "* Texto de la segunda edición, publicada en Lima el año 1842." Verbatim: "La América Española es orijinal — orijinales han de ser sus Instituciones i su Gobierno = i orijinales los medios de fundar uno i otro. / o Inventamos o Erramos." (The dash is the compositor's, preserved as quoted matter.) The quotation therefore sits on the **Lima 1842** stop; Arequipa 1828 carries a genuinely first-edition line, "El autor es Republicano, y tanto! ...que no piensa en ninguna especie de Rey."
- The same applies to **two further quotations** the pool filed under Arequipa 1828, both absent from it: "DENSEME LOS MUCHACHOS POBRES..." and "¿Cuál es la causa de las revoluciones, sino la Ignorancia?" DENSEME is used on the **Chuquisaca workshop-school** stop with its 1842 provenance in `quote_source`. A third quotation is genuinely first-edition but had one word wrong: **"escríbase"**, not "escribir."
- **Observaciones sobre el terreno de Vincocaya** **[A: DHV]**, dated **1830** by the geography lens and **1832** by the textual verification; the stop uses 1830 and records both. **A published error corrected:** "Si los americanos quieren... hagan una revolución económica y empiécenla por los campos" is attributed by **the DHV itself** to *Consejos de amigo* (1851), where the word *campos* never occurs. It belongs to the *Observaciones económicas* of the Vincocaya study, and is moved there.
- **Second marriage or union with Manuela Gómez, probably 1831, in Peru [R]**: the DHV gives no place, so the pool's **Arequipa** pin is discarded as invented and no stop is made; the fact appears in the 1854 burial entry, where the record puts it. **Bolívar dies at Santa Marta, 17 December 1830**, with Rodríguez elsewhere, so no stop; he is named in the Vincocaya campa as the patron lost.

## 7. Chile, 1834-1840

- **Concepción, 1834**, directs a school, publishes *Luces y virtudes sociales*, distinguishing instruction from education **[A]**. **February 1835**, a report on the post-earthquake rebuilding **[R: authorship not re-verified against a primary copy]**.
- **Trilaleubu and Monteblanco 1836, Tucapel 1837** **[A: DHV]**. **Coordinate corrected by about 73 km:** Tucapel, Biobío, is **-37.2833 / -71.95**, not -37.3167 / -72.7833.
- **Santiago, 1836-1838**, meets **Andrés Bello** again **[A]**. The DHV cautions against inflating this into ongoing collaboration; the campa does not.
- **Lastarria's testimony corrected and upgraded [R] to [A].** The pool had it via Wikipedia with **"estaría"**; the Ayacucho 1990 edition reads **"estaba"** and gives the setting, Lastarria having met Rodríguez in Bello's house. His own texts are traceable through Grases, ed. (Caracas, 1954), pp. 128-131, and *Recuerdos literarios* (2nd ed. 1885), p. 48.
- **Paired with it**, the independent **Vendel-Heyl** testimony of 1840: told that his ideas resembled Fourier's and Saint-Simon's, Rodríguez said he "no había oído sus nombres, sino poco tiempo antes; y no había leído sus obras." The strongest evidence that his convergence with the utopian socialists was independent, and it sits directly against the Uribe Ángel recollection of section 4. **Both are in the file. Neither cancels the other.**
- **Valparaíso, 1838**, another *Luces y virtudes sociales* from the Imprenta del Mercurio; he writes for *El Mercurio* **[A]**. Which articles are his is not established.

## 8. The northern road and the end, 1842-1854

- **Lima, 1842**, the second edition of *Sociedades americanas en 1828*, further material 1843 **[A: DHV]**. See section 6.
- **Paita, 1843**, meets **Manuela Sáenz** **[R]**: geography lens **[A]**, chronology **[R]**. es.wikipedia confirms she was "visitada por... el venezolano Simón Rodríguez" at Paita but gives **no year**; only the DHV dates the visit 1843, and no contemporary correspondence was located.
- **Guayaquil late 1843**, then **Latacunga, Colegio de San Vicente, 1843-1851** **[A]**; *Consejos de amigo dados al Colejio de Latacunga*, **1851**, replacing Latin-centred scholasticism with Spanish, Quechua, physics, chemistry and trades **[A: DHV]**.
- **Túquerres, New Granada, 1847** **[A]**; **Bogotá, *El Neo-Granadino*, 1849**, printing the *Extracto sucinto de mi obra sobre la educación republicana* **[A]**.
- **Guayaquil, last years: a fire destroys much of his work [A as to the fire, R as to when].** The DHV gives **no year**; "1852-1853" is the pool's own, and **no 1852-53 city fire was confirmed in any source fetched**, Guayaquil's documented conflagrations being 1764 and 1896. The stop is dated 1852 only to order the segment and says so.
- **Final journey, 1853**, accompanied at stages by his son **José** and by **Camilo Gómez**, whose recollection is a principal source for the end **[A: DHV]**.
- **Dies at Amotape 28 February 1854; buried 1 March in San Nicolás [A: DHV, citing the burial entry]**: about ninety, lucid at confession, from Caracas, twice married, widower of Manuela Gómez of Bolivia, father of one son. **Coordinate corrected by about 42 km, the pool's lenses disagreeing:** geography gave -4.95 / -80.63 and -4.9167 / -80.6167, both in the dry Piura interior; the afterlife lens gave the correct **-4.88155 / -81.01507**, which is used. **Not asserted:** the priest's name, given in the pool as Santiago Sánchez, did not surface in the DHV text fetched.
- **1924**, remains transferred to the **Panteón de los Próceres**, Lima **[A]**. **Genuine contradiction between two independently fetched pages:** es.wikipedia `Simón Rodríguez` calls him the **first person interred** there, while `Panteón de los Próceres`, listing the roughly two dozen sets of remains moved in 1924, **does not name him**. The campa states both. Of the pool's three coordinates, the Panteón's own, **-12.0541 / -77.0325**, is used.
- **28 February 1954**, the centenary: repatriated to the **Panteón Nacional**, Caracas **[A: es.wikipedia, listing him by that date as "El maestro de Bolívar"]**. **Coordinate choice declared:** es.wikipedia gives **10.5129 / -66.9126**, the file uses **10.5123 / -66.9146**, the pin the atlas already carries in `manuela_saenz.journey.json`, so two travellers are not 550 m apart at one building. To prefer the Wikipedia figure, both files must move together.

## 9. Afterlife, verified separately

Commemorations carried in the last stop's `suggested_refs` rather than staged as stops: Misión Robinson (2003) **[A]**, asteroid (149528) Simónrodríguez (2008) **[A]**, the Premio Simón Rodríguez (1949) **[R]**, the Barcelona sculpture **[R, undated]**. Four corrections. **UNESR was founded 24 January 1974, not 1971.** **Banknotes:** the researcher's own mid-course correction, that the 50 Bs.F note shows Luisa Cáceres de Arismendi rather than Rodríguez, is itself wrong; `Bolívar fuerte` shows **both** the 50 Bs.F and the 10,000 Bs.F bearing Rodríguez, and the 20 Bs.S too. **Uslar Pietri's *La isla de Robinson* (1981) is upgraded [R] to [A]** after the pool's 404. **Salas's oath canvas [A, location disputed]:** the DHV puts it in the Panteón Nacional, es.wikipedia puts *Juramento en el Monte Aventino* in the Palacio Federal Legislativo, and Salas painted murals in both.

## Apparatus relations for the operator

Per `EXCEPTIONS.md`: Rodríguez is **not** on the apparatus list and nothing here needed him to be. No stop applies a framework backward to material he never discussed, and nothing reaches past 1954 except the ordinary afterlife stops for his remains. Three items are reported rather than deleted:

1. **Backward, rooted in his own life.** The *Robinson Crusoe / Émile* reading of the 1797 alias is a later readers' interpretation, not his statement. **Kept** in the Kingston campa with that status in `date_confidence`, since the underlying relation (he read Rousseau) is his own. To keep only what he said himself, delete the second and third sentences of that campa.
2. **Dropped as shared geography only, named so the deletion is not silent.** The interlock lens offered **Alexander von Humboldt**, whose Bogotá 1801 and Quito 1802 stops overlap cities Rodríguez reached two decades later with no textual link in either file, and **Rousseau's Les Charmettes** as a place-echo on the 1805 Alpine route. Humboldt is **not named** here; Rousseau **is** named at Chambéry, for the reading relation.
3. **Forward, refused.** Nothing was written into any other traveller's file. `bolivar.journey.json` holds a Monte Sacro coordinate this report disputes; that fix, if wanted, is a separate operator edit.

## Travellers named in campas, and why

Naming is by relation, never co-location. **`bolivar`**: pupil, host in 1795, companion 1804-1806, witness of his oath, his appointing minister, addressee of the Pativilca letter. **`jean_jacques_rousseau`**: read him; the contested *Émile* thesis; the alias. **`napoleon`**: the Milan coronation. **`henri_de_saint_simon`**: his own recollection names Enfantin, Olinde Rodrigues and Leroux. **`sucre`**: refused to fund the Chuquisaca school. **`charles_fourier`**: Lastarria places him beside Fourier; Vendel-Heyl put the comparison to him. **`manuela_saenz`**: the Paita visit. **Andrés Bello**, a genuine relation named twice in prose, has no journey file, so he is not an interlock.
