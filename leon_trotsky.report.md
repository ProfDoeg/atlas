# Leon Trotsky: research report
*Compiled 2026-08-24 for `leon_trotsky.journey.json`. Lev Davidovich Bronstein, 1879-1940. 45 stops in 9 segments.*

Legend: **[A]** = attested, source named inline · **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition, self-report, or a claim carried without independent confirmation. Contradictions are flagged and **not** adjudicated. Gaps are stated as gaps.

One caution governs everything below. Trotsky is among the most self-documented lives of the twentieth century and the documentation is overwhelmingly **his own**: *My Life* (1930), *Diary in Exile* (1935), *Hue and Cry Over Kronstadt* (1938), the *History of the Russian Revolution*. All first person, all written in exile, all written against a state that was simultaneously erasing him from its record. Where a detail is tagged [R] and sourced to "his own account," the only witness is the interested party.

---

## 1. Yanovka and the schools of the south (1879-1898)

Born 7 November 1879 (26 October O.S.) at Yanovka, Kherson governorate, now Bereslavka, Ukraine; fifth of eight children of David and Anna Bronstein, prosperous Jewish farmers. Sent at about eight to relatives in Odessa and enrolled at St Paul's Realschule, a Lutheran German-language school. At Nikolayev from c.1896 he converted to Marxism against Aleksandra Sokolovskaya's arguments in a garden circle and helped organise the **South Russian Workers' Union**. **[A: Wikipedia, 'Leon Trotsky']** The Odessa relative is standardly named **Moisey Shpentzer**, printer and publisher **[R: geography lens only]**; the school suspension and the sibling mortality are **[R]**, standard biography not in the pool, carried lightly.

**Contradiction, not resolved: the graduation year.** The pool dates first-class honours plus a brief Odessa University enrolment to **1897**. Its own correction note reports English Wikipedia giving honours in **1896**, with the university dropped in early 1897. Both readings sit in the Nikolayev stop's `date_confidence`.

## 2. The prisons and the first Siberia (1898-1902)

Arrested January 1898 with 200+ union members; roughly two years across **Nikolayev, Kherson, Odessa and Moscow** prisons. Married **Aleksandra Sokolovskaya** in a Moscow prison, July 1900; four years' Siberian exile handed down the same year. Daughters **Zinaida** (1901) and **Nina** (1902) born in exile. Wrote for *Vostochnoye obozreniye* as **Antid Oto**. Escaped summer 1902 in a hay cart, west on a false passport in the name **Trotsky**. **[A: Wikipedia; Antid Oto from the geography lens]**

**Flagged [R]: the origin of the name.** "Trotsky" as a head jailer at the Odessa prison is **his own account in *My Life*** and nothing else; the geography lens marks it "unverified, not independently confirmed." The campa states the doubt in its own prose as well as in `date_confidence`. The most-repeated anecdote about the man rests on one witness.

**Coordinates corrected:** Butyrka 55.7864/37.5866 → **55.7844 / 37.5939** (~500 m); Ust-Kut 56.7833/105.7833 → **56.783 / 105.633** (~9 km E); Verkholensk 53.9667/105.05 → **54.10 / 105.57** (~40 km SW).

**Also flagged: the marriage prison may not be Butyrka.** The Butyrka article does not list Trotsky among its inmates; the identification rests on his own phrase "the Moscow transfer prison." Corrected Butyrka coordinates used, doubt in `date_confidence`. **[R on the identification, A on the marriage.]**

**Gaps.** The **Kherson transit prison** pin was dropped for stop-count reasons and folded into the Nikolayev-prison campa; named so it is not lost. What Trotsky and Sokolovskaya said to each other when he left her in a Lena village with two infants is **in no reachable source**; the campa says so in those words rather than inventing a scene.

## 3. Iskra, the split, and 1905 (1902-1907)

London 1902: the *Iskra* circle, the byline **Pero**, Lenin seeking to add him to the board over Plekhanov's objection. Met **Natalia Sedova** in 1902, partners from 1903 until his death. Returned to Russia February 1905; addressed a crowd estimated at 200,000 on 15 October; chairman of the St Petersburg Soviet from 26 November after **Khrustalev-Nosar**'s arrest. **Financial Manifesto** 2 December, leadership arrested next day, convicted 4 October 1906 to perpetual Siberian exile with loss of civil rights. Escaped **Berezov** January 1907 by reindeer sledge and rail. **[A: Wikipedia, 'Leon Trotsky' and 'Natalia Sedova']** The feigned illness and hired guide at Berezov are **[R: *My Life*]**.

**Contradiction corrected: the Second Congress was not in London.** The pool says he "attends the RSDLP's Second Congress in London" in August 1903. Its own correction note establishes from Wikipedia, '2nd Congress of the RSDLP': opened **Brussels, 30 July 1903** in a flour warehouse, delegates expelled from Belgium **6 August**, reconvened London, closed **23 August**. The journey file splits this into **two stops**. A second error in the same material: the geography lens named the venue as the **Maison du Peuple**, whereas Wikipedia describes a "flea-ridden flour warehouse"; the Maison du Peuple stood at ~50.8407/4.3521, not the 50.8503/4.3517 given, and was demolished in 1965. The venue name is **dropped**, Brussels city-centre coordinates used, the warehouse address recorded as unestablished.

**Contradiction corrected: Lev Sedov was not born in Vienna.** The pool places his birth at Vienna, 24 February 1906. Impossible: Trotsky did not reach Vienna until **October 1907**. Russian Wikipedia ('Седов, Лев Львович') gives **Saint Petersburg**; English Wikipedia adds he was "born when his father was in prison," Trotsky then held there awaiting the 1906 trial. The birth is moved into the Peter and Paul Fortress stop. **Sergei Sedov**, born Vienna 21 March 1908, is unaffected.

**Gap: Old Style / New Style.** The supplied chronology does not distinguish Julian from Gregorian for Russian events and this pass does not repair it. Russian dates before February 1918 should be treated as possibly O.S., except the birth and the October Revolution, where both styles are given.

## 4. Vienna, the war, and the planet without a visa (1907-1917)

Vienna most of seven years; edited a Russian-language ***Pravda*** with Joffe and Skobelev from October 1908, folding April 1912, distinct from the later Bolshevik paper; covered the Balkan Wars for *Kievskaya mysl*. Left Vienna 3 August 1914, France 19 November 1914, **Nashe Slovo** from January 1915, **Zimmerwald** September 1915 drafting its manifesto. Deported to Spain September 1916, to the United States 25 December 1916. New York from 13 January 1917: 1522 Vyse Avenue, the Bronx, *Novy Mir*, Bukharin and Kollontai. Sailed 27 March aboard the **SS Kristianiafjord**. **[A: Wikipedia; Kievskaya mysl from the geography lens]**

**Contradiction corrected: Halifax and Amherst are two dates.** The pool compresses removal and release into 1917-04-29. Its correction note, from Wikipedia's 'Amherst Internment Camp': taken off the ship and held at the **Halifax Citadel on 3 April**, moved to Amherst, **released 29 April**. Split into two stops. The same note records that **the family was not interned**: Natalia and the boys stayed at a Halifax hotel reporting daily to police.

**Coordinates corrected:** Zimmerwald 46.8608/7.5136 → **46.883 / 7.467** (~4 km SE); Amherst 45.8331/-64.2036 → **45.8191 / -64.2108** (~1.6 km).

**[R] carried:** the **four coaches** remark about Zimmerwald (his own, widely quoted, not verbatim from a fetched source); the Bronx flat's fittings (*My Life*). **Omitted rather than carried:** the Madrid prison name and the routing to the port, attested nowhere reachable. The **Wall Street financing** allegation is a later conspiracy claim which the pool states finds no support in the record; the campa says so rather than ignoring it.

## 5. Petrograd and the Commissariats (1917-1925)

Petrograd 17 May 1917, joining the **Mezhraiontsy**, which merged with the Bolsheviks; chairman of the Petrograd Soviet 8 October; directed the **Military Revolutionary Committee**, 24-25 October O.S. / 6-7 November N.S. Commissar for Foreign Affairs from 8 November: published the secret tsarist treaties, led **Brest-Litovsk**, the "neither war nor peace" formula, resigned when Lenin's terms prevailed. Commissar for Military and Naval Affairs **14 March 1918 to January 1925**; Red Army of roughly **5.5 million** by late 1920. **[A: chronology and dossier institutional section]**

**Coordinate corrected:** Smolny 59.9506/30.3959 → **59.9464 / 30.3964** (~470 m). **[R] carried:** the **July Days arrest** and the Sixth Congress Central Committee election, standard but not in the pool, flagged in `date_confidence`. **Cut as unverified and grave:** the frequently repeated claim that military specialists' families were held as surety.

**Sviyazhsk, August 1918.** The dossier attests a tribunal executing a regimental commander and commissar for unauthorised retreat under Trotsky's threatened-death order, and that **the scale of a literal decimation is contested**. The two executions are stated as fact; the decimation question is left open in `date_confidence`. Coordinates 55.7833/48.9833 were **~21 km east of the island, in open country**; corrected to **55.7703 / 48.6528**, the largest error in the geography lens after the Coyoacán house.

**Kronstadt, March 1921.** The pool contains Trotsky's 1938 statement of responsibility but **no geography and no chronology entry** for the rising. No Kronstadt stop is staged: he did not command the assault in person, and placing him on the ice would break the stop-ownership rule. The material sits at the **Kremlin**, seat of the government that ordered the suppression, carrying his own 1938 words, with the reasoning in `date_confidence`.

**Quote correction applied.** The pool's *"Civil war is no school of humanism... the main point is that 'excesses' flow from the very nature of revolution"* was flagged in its own note as an ellipsis welding **two separate sentences** and truncating the second before "which in itself is but an 'excess' of history," which carries the argument. The journey file uses only the first sentence, whole. The composite appears nowhere.

## 6. Expulsion and exile (1927-1937)

Expelled from the party **1927** after United Opposition demonstrations; the pool gives only the year, so the standard datings (7 and 14 November) are **[R]** and the stop is dated 1927-01-01 for ordering. **Alma-Ata** January 1928 to January 1929: Opposition correspondence, Comintern analysis, refusal to recant, the letter and telegram counts being his own record **[R]**. Deported February 1929 from **Odessa**; Istanbul, then **Büyükada** 1929-1933. France 1933-1935 under surveillance at **Royan** and near **Barbizon**. Norway 1935 at **Wexhall**, the Konrad Knudsen house near Hønefoss, dictating *The Revolution Betrayed*. Mexico 1937, asylum from **Lázaro Cárdenas**. **[A: Wikipedia and dossier exile section]**

**Contradiction corrected: the Norwegian internment was at Hurum, not Oslo.** The geography lens placed it at Oslo. Its correction note, from Wikipedia's 'Konrad Knudsen': Nasjonal Samling members broke into the Norderhov house **5 August 1936**; Justice Minister **Trygve Lie** ordered Trotsky and Sedova sent on **2 September 1936** to a farm at **Hurum** (Storsand/Sundby), ~60 km from Oslo, held under house arrest until deportation **19 December 1936**. Oslo was the seat of the government that ordered it, not the site. Re-placed at Hurum (~59.60 / 10.40, approximate); the Oslo pin dropped.

**Flagged [R]: the 1931 Prinkipo fire.** The dossier asserts fire destroyed part of the Büyükada residence, accident and arson both suspected, no conclusive finding. **Not independently confirmed in this pass.** Carried with the ambiguity in the campa's own prose.

**Quote correction applied.** *"Old age is the most unexpected of all the things that happen to a man"* was tagged [R] in the pool, which could not reach the marxists.org diary pages. Its correction note establishes the failure was a **bad URL, not a bad quote**: MIA does not host *Diary in Exile* at all. Authentic, entry of **8 May 1935**, verified in the Harvard 1958 edition. Used as **[A]** on the Wexhall stop, where he was living that May.

**Barbizon** had its own pin (48.4489 / 2.6172) and was folded into the Royan campa for stop-count reasons. Named so the pin is not lost.

## 7. Coyoacán and the killing (1937-1940)

**Tampico** aboard the tanker *Ruth*, 9 January 1937; **Casa Azul** January 1937 to April 1939 with the **Dewey Commission** there in April 1937 and the affair with Frida Kahlo; **Avenida Viena 19** (now Av. Río Churubusco 410) from 1939, after the Rivera rupture. **[A: dossier Mexico section]** On **24 May 1940** a raid led by **David Alfaro Siqueiros** with NKVD operative **Iosif Grigulevich** sprayed the house; Trotsky and Sedova survived by taking cover, grandson **Sieva** was shot in the foot, guard **Robert Sheldon Harte** was murdered. On **20 August 1940** **Ramón Mercader**, alias Frank Jacson, struck his skull with a shortened Austrian piolet (maker **Werkgen Fulpmes**) concealed under a raincoat, on the pretext of an article: ~7 cm into the brain, struck with the **adze**. He died **21 August**; an estimated **200,000-300,000** filed past the coffin, 21-27 August; Mercader was convicted in 1943, served twenty years at **Lecumberri**, was released in 1960 and later honoured in the USSR. **[A: Wikipedia, 'Assassination of Leon Trotsky' and 'Ramón Mercader']**

**The largest coordinate problem in the supplied pool.** The Trotsky house was mis-pinned in three places by two lenses which also disagreed with each other:

| lens | gave | status |
|---|---|---|
| geography (indices 39, 40, 42) | 19.3538 / -99.1495 | ~1.1 km too far east, outside the historic centre |
| afterlife (four entries) | 19.3467 / -99.1619 | ~1.3 km too far south |
| both correction notes | 19.3578 / -99.1596 | English and Spanish Wikipedia agree |
| **interlock lens, canonical atlas pin** | **19.3577 / -99.1595** | inherited byte-identical from `andre_breton.journey.json` |

The journey file uses **19.3577 / -99.1595** on all four Avenida Viena stops, per the instruction to inherit canonical pins exactly. It differs from the Wikipedia-derived correction by about **fifteen metres**, stated in `date_confidence` on two stops so no later reader mistakes it for an uncorrected error. The **Casa Azul** pin is separately inherited byte-identical at **19.3551 / -99.1624**; the two buildings are ~1.9 km apart and the atlas keeps them distinct.

**Flagged [R] inside the assassination:** the instruction to the guards **not to kill Mercader "because he must talk"** is reported, not documented; the **Cruz Verde hospital** pin (19.418 / -99.145) is the pool's own [R], exact address not found; Mercader's two-year cultivation of a woman in the circle and his number of prior visits are standard literature, not in the pool.

**The ice axe.** Chain of custody per Wikipedia: Mexico City police evidence room, officer **Alfredo Salas** removed it "for posterity," daughter **Ana Alicia** sold it in **2005**, **H. Keith Melton** bought it and **donated** it (the pool's correction: the museum did not buy it) to the **International Spy Museum**. The coordinates given (38.8951 / -77.0209) are the museum's **former F Street site**; it moved in 2019 to 700 L'Enfant Plaza SW, **38.8839 / -77.0261**. **No stop is placed at the Spy Museum**: Trotsky never went to Washington, and the object's later travels are the object's. The custody chain, the museum, and **Esteban Volkov's public doubt that it is the real axe** are carried as a `suggested_ref` on the study stop, where the axe was in his life. Stop-ownership applied to an object.

**The "ice pick" misnomer.** Legend and later dramatisation collapsed the weapon into an "ice pick," which historians and the museum reject: a mountaineering **ice axe** with pick and flat adze, the fatal blow struck with the adze. **[A]** In `date_confidence`; the campa says "axe" and "adze," never "pick."

**Afterlife.** The **O'Gorman stele** holds the ashes of Trotsky, **Natalia Sedova** (d. 23 January 1962, Corbeil-Essonnes) and **Esteban Volkov** (b. 7 March 1926 at **Yalta**, d. 16 June 2023, aged 97): the closing stop. The pool hedged Volkov's death to "the year only" and implied Coyoacán as his birthplace; its own correction fixes both and the fixes are applied. Monument declaration **24 September 1982**; museum opened **20 or 21 August 1990**, English Wikipedia saying the 20th and Spanish the 21st: **contradiction flagged, not resolved**. Three further items get **no stop**, not being places he stood: **rehabilitation in Russia, 16 June 2001**, never rehabilitated in the Soviet period **[A]**; **Isaac Deutscher's** trilogy (1954, 1959, 1963), researched partly through access **Natalia Sedova** granted to closed Harvard archive sections **[A]**, which is Deutscher's stop on Deutscher's file; and the **September 2017** press resurfacing of the axe **[R]**.

## Apparatus relations for the operator

Trotsky is **not** in `EXCEPTIONS.md`, so no framework-applied-backward stops are licensed and none were written. Per the standing instruction, relations found in the pool that are **not rooted in his own life** are flagged rather than silently deleted.

**Forward, therefore never licensable, and not written in.** **Che Guevara**, arriving in Mexico City in September 1954, fourteen years after the killing, the text calling it "the city of Trotsky's tomb": already Guevara's stop on his own file, shared geography only. **George Orwell**, the Blairs denounced at Valencia on 13 July 1937 as "rabid Trotskyists": about the *word* as a Stalinist weapon, not the man, and Orwell's later fictionalising of him as Snowball is likewise Orwell's. **Erich Mühsam**, Zenzl Mühsam arrested in Moscow in 1936 "as a Trotskyist" and sentenced to eight years at Potma: the pool itself frames it as the charge sweeping up people with no operative connection to him. **Mikhail Bakunin** and **Gurdjieff** are false-positive grep hits on "Bolshevik," flagged as such by the pool and recorded here so a later pass does not chase them.

**Two that are not forward, and still are not written in.**

1. **Nestor Makhno / Rudolf Rocker.** Makhno's 1925 Berlin visit is framed around fighting "the Bolsheviks across Ukraine." Trotsky as War Commissar directed Red Army operations against Makhno, which **would** be a genuine relation inside his own life. But `rudolf_rocker.journey.json` **never names Trotsky** and the pool tags the inference **[R]**. **Not written in for lack of attestation, not for lack of direction.** It needs a real source and a Ukrainian civil-war stop this file does not have.
2. **Benito Mussolini.** The one relation here genuinely **two-directional and inside Trotsky's life**: the young Mussolini in Swiss exile crossing paths with Lenin and Trotsky in the Lausanne émigré cafés. Not written into a campa, because the pool gives no lat/lng, no date and no source beyond the atlas file, and the verified chronology has **no Lausanne stop** to attach it to; staging it would mean inventing a place and a date. **For the operator:** if `mussolini.journey.json`'s own pin and date hold on inspection, this is a legitimate campa mention on a Swiss stop and should be added by hand. The `frida_kahlo.journey.json` retelling is a secondary dramatisation, not a source.

**Buenos Aires** (-34.6037 / -58.3816). The canonical Argentine pin was supplied per convention, with the lens stating plainly that **no atlas file attests any Trotsky-Buenos Aires connection**. None was invented; this subject is aesthetic-ancestry material and the absence is not a defect.

**Interlocks actually written into campas**, all passing the direct-relation test: **Diego Rivera** (petitioned Cárdenas, housed him, collaborated, ruptured, co-signed the manifesto), **Frida Kahlo** (met the ship, housed him, affair), **André Breton** (co-wrote the 1938 manifesto at Trotsky's table). **Lenin** is named throughout as a historical actor; he is a queued subject with no journey file yet, so he is not a pointer.

**A place deliberately not staged: Périgny.** The pool lists "He founds the Fourth International near Paris, 1938, Périgny." **Trotsky was in Mexico and did not attend.** A Périgny pin would put him where he never went, so none exists; the founding is carried in the Avenida Viena campa as something that happened elsewhere on a programme he wrote at his own desk. Flagged so a future editor seeing Périgny in the pool knows where it went.
