# William Joseph Casey (1913-1987): research report

*Compiled 2026-08-11 for the Atlas of Journeys. Slug `william_casey`. OSS Secret Intelligence
officer, tax lawyer and legal publisher, SEC chairman, Under Secretary of State, Export-Import
Bank chairman, Reagan's 1980 campaign manager, DCI 1981-1987.*

**Legend.** **[A]** = attested, source named. **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition,
single-journalist claim, or inference. Contradictions are flagged in place and **not
adjudicated**; the unresolved ones are the tumor-against-stroke framing at death, "Sophia"
against "Sofia" Casey, the NY-3 1966 disagreement between the pool's own lenses, the Under
Secretary title, the Point Four dating, and Casey's knowledge of the diversion. Gaps are stated
as gaps.

The dominant reachable source is the English Wikipedia article "William J. Casey", a compression
of Persico's 1991 biography, which this pass could not open. Several details tagged [A] against
Wikipedia by the gathering pass are not in Wikipedia at all; several that are in it are wrong.

---

## 1. Origins and education, 1913-1937

**Birth, 13 March 1913 [A: Wikipedia].** Born William Joseph Casey in New York City.

**Elmhurst, Queens as the birthplace [R].** The cited article says only "New York City"; the
Elmhurst detail traces to Persico. Corrected from the pool's [A]. The coordinate sits about 370 m
from OSM's Elmhurst centroid, so the pin is defensible where the attribution is not.

**Raised at Bellmore, Long Island [A: Wikipedia],** in an Irish Catholic household loyal to
Democratic machine politics. The "after early childhood in Elmhurst" clause carries the [R]
problem above and is kept separate.

**Fordham University, B.A., 1934 [A: Wikipedia].** Jesuit, Rose Hill, the Bronx.
**St. John's University School of Law, LL.B., 1937 [A: Wikipedia],** then housed in Brooklyn.

**Catholic University of America, graduate study, mid-1930s [R].** No primary enrolment record
located; no dates, no coursework. Recorded as a gap, not an event.

## 2. Law, publishing, and the secret war, 1938-1946

**Partner, Buckner, Casey, Doran and Siegel, 1938-1942 [A: Wikipedia].**

**Chairman of the board of editors, Research Institute of America, 1938-1949 [A: Wikipedia].**

**CORRECTION, founding claim withdrawn.** The pool had Casey co-founding RIA with Leo Cherne. He
did not: RIA grew from Carl Hovgard and Cherne's "Social Security Coordinator" venture of the
mid-1930s (Wikipedia, "Leo Cherne"), and the Casey article claims only the board chairmanship.
Removed from both artifacts.

**United States Naval Reserve, 1943-1946 [A: Wikipedia].** **Office of Strategic Services from
1943 [A: Wikipedia, "Office of Strategic Services"],** run from the converted National Institutes
of Health campus at 2430 E Street NW.

**Head of the OSS Secret Intelligence Branch for Europe, London, 1944 [A: Wikipedia].**
Lieutenant; Bronze Star. OSS Europe ran from 70-72 Grosvenor Street, Mayfair.

**CORRECTION, chronology reversed.** The pool placed agent networks "under Casey's Secret
Intelligence Branch" feeding order of battle toward Normandy in June 1944. This is backwards, and
the [R] hedge does not repair it: the same pool dates the SI appointment to late 1944. In June
1944 he was a junior staff officer in London under David Bruce. The journey reframes Normandy as
OSS/London activity Casey served within, not directed.

**Allen Dulles's Bern station, 1944-1945 [R].** The station is attested; no record was found of a
personal Casey visit, and the link is institutional only. Dropped from the journey rather than
carried as an unsupported pin.

**Agent penetration of the Reich, late 1944-1945 [R].** The source is Casey's own posthumous
memoir *The Secret War Against Hitler* (1988), self-interested and not read directly. No drop
sites located; Berlin stands in the journey as a symbolic center, and the campa says so.

## 3. Postwar Washington and the Long Island estate, 1947-1962

**Special counsel to a Senate committee, 1947-1948 [A: Wikipedia].** Committee unnamed in any
reachable source. Gap.

**Associate general counsel, Point Four Program, "1948" [R, anachronistic as dated].** The
sharpest dating problem in the file. Point Four is named for the fourth point of Truman's
inaugural address of **20 January 1949** and was not funded until the Act for International
Development of 1950 (Britannica), so no such program existed in 1948: Wikipedia's "1948" is
itself the propagated error. His 1948 federal legal work is more plausibly the Marshall Plan's
Economic Cooperation Administration, but no source places him there. The journey dates the stop
1949-01-01, tags it [R], and states the anachronism in the campa.

**Purchase of Locust Knoll, Roslyn Harbor, 1948 [A: Wikipedia].** 8.2 acres, an 1854 Jacobethan
house, fifty thousand dollars, renamed Mayknoll. Residence for life, the fact Wikipedia's infobox
later confuses with his place of death.

**Lecturer in tax law, NYU School of Law, 1948-1962 [A: Wikipedia].** **Institute for Business
Planning founded 1950 [A: Wikipedia];** no address located, so it is folded into the
law-practice stop rather than given a fabricated pin.

**Partner, Hall, Casey, Dickler & Howley, 1957-1971 [A: Wikipedia].** The pool's Midtown
coordinate is a generic point, not a located address, and is flagged as such in the journey.

## 4. Elective defeat and appointive office, 1966-1976

**CORRECTION, NY-3, 1966.** The pool's chronology lens called Casey "the Republican candidate"
for New York's 3rd district. He was not. Wikipedia's wikitext: "He ran as a Republican for New
York's 3rd congressional district in 1966, but was defeated in the primary by former Congressman
Steven Derounian." He never reached a general election. The pool's geography lens had this right
while its chronology lens had it wrong; resolved toward the wikitext, the only version with a
source attached. **[A: Wikipedia]** The coordinate is a generic central-Nassau point, defensible
as a district centroid but not a located site.

**Chairman, SEC, 14 April 1971 to 2 February 1973 [A: Wikipedia].** In these years the Commission
sat at 500 North Capitol Street NW, moving to 450 Fifth Street NW only in 1988. See section 11.

**Under Secretary of State for Economic Affairs, 2 February 1973 to 14 March 1974 [A: Office of
the Historian].** The department's modern listing renders the title as Under Secretary for
Economic Growth, Energy, and the Environment. Recorded, not smoothed.

**Chairman, Export-Import Bank, 14 March 1974 to 2 January 1976 [A: Wikipedia],** with the
**President's Foreign Intelligence Advisory Board, 1975-1976 [A: Wikipedia]** running concurrent;
the board has no separate address located and is folded into the Ex-Im stop.

**Of counsel, Rogers and Wells, 1976-1981 [A: Wikipedia];** clients included the governments of
Indonesia and South Korea.

**CORRECTION, the Manhattan Institute, 1978.** Casey and Antony Fisher founded the
**International Center for Economic Policy Studies (ICEPS)** in 1978; it became the Manhattan
Institute for Policy Research only in **1981** (Wikipedia). Year and co-founder are right, the
name is anachronistic, and the coordinate offered sits about 60 m from the Rogers and Wells pin:
an unsourced placeholder, not a founding address. Both problems are stated in the campa.

## 5. The 1980 campaign

**Campaign manager, Reagan for President, 1980 [A: Wikipedia].** Casey took over in early 1980
after John Sears was dismissed. Headquarters at 901 South Highland Street, Arlington; coordinate
corrected, see section 11. **CORRECTION:** "helps broker the Reagan-George H.W. Bush ticket
alliance" is not in the cited article, and the Detroit decision is usually laid to the collapse
of the Ford "co-presidency" talks. Dropped.

**Debategate, October 1980 [R].** Classified Carter debate briefing papers reach the Reagan
campaign. Timing and Casey's role are disputed; the pool's 28 October date is carried as a pin
only, with the dispute in `date_confidence`.

**The October Surprise allegation [R], and a contradiction inside the pool.** The geography lens
asserted at [A] that Casey steered the campaign "through the October surprise controversy", while
the pool's own afterlife lens tags the same allegation [R] and records the task force finding of
**fabrication**. An [A] entry must not narrate a conspiracy allegation as an event. Retagged [R]
throughout. The claim, per the Iranian intermediary Jamshid Hashemi, is meetings with Mehdi and
Hassan Karroubi at the Hotel Ritz, Madrid, in July and August 1980. **Newsweek, November 1991
[A],** could account for Casey's whereabouts and placed him at a conference in London. **Gary
Sick's April 1991 New York Times op-ed [R]** alleged a Paris deal in October 1980. **CORRECTION:**
the pool dated the task force finding 1992; the task force was created in February 1992 but
reported on **13 January 1993**.

**Reagan-Bush transition team, November-December 1980 [A: Wikipedia].**

## 6. Director of Central Intelligence, 1981-1987

**Sworn in 28 January 1981 with Cabinet rank [A: Wikipedia],** breaking with precedent. He
rebuilt covert action capacity stripped out after the Church Committee reforms.

**The Vatican channel [R, downgraded].** Wikipedia has Casey flying "secretly to Rome in a
windowless C-141 black jet" to brief John Paul II, sourced to Woodward's *Veil*: one journalist,
no itemized dates, as the pool conceded in its own note while still tagging the entry [A]. That
is the standing the project assigns at [R] to Woodward's deathbed claim. **Retagged [R].**
Coordinate corrected, see section 11.

**Honduras and the Contra camps, 1981-1986 [R]:** general Contra-war geography, not itemized to
Casey by name. **CORRECTION, the chain of command.** The pool asserted at [A] that North operated
"under
his direction" in facilitating Contra funding. The Walsh Final Report, chapter 15, states:
**"Independent Counsel obtained no documentary evidence showing Casey knew about or approved the
diversion."** North's testimony that Casey endorsed it was uncorroborated; Casey denied it. The
entry's own note conceded the chain of command is disputed, which is the definition of [R].
**Retagged [R]**, as is the November 1986 entry calling Casey "an architect of the covert
financing scheme". Walsh also judged Casey's October-November 1986 conduct "more consistent with
the behavior of a person aware of the diversion." Both halves are carried.

**Arms to Iran, June 1985 [A: Wikipedia, "Iran-Contra affair"].** Casey supports McFarlane's
draft directive. The reachable text runs: "Only the director of the CIA William J. Casey
supported McFarlane's plan to start selling arms to Iran." A third-party paraphrase, not a Casey
quotation, and used as such. **McFarlane's Tehran trip, May 1986 [R]:** Casey's own presence in
Tehran is not documented.

**Ilopango, El Salvador, 1985-1986 [R].** Casey's operational knowledge of that field is disputed
and unconfirmed. **Panama, 1980s [R]:** no primary Casey-Noriega record located, and
`manuel_noriega.journey.json` dates Noriega's payroll status to 1971 with the 1976 continuation
approved by George H. W. Bush, an asset Casey inherited.

**BCCI, Leadenhall Street [A within the atlas corpus only].** See section 10: the Lombardi
sentence placing Casey inside the BCCI network is the project's own editorial assertion, not an
external documentary one. The Kerry Committee report on BCCI implicates ties to intelligence
services, arms dealing and Noriega.

## 7. Collapse, testimony, and death, 1986-1987

**Seizures 15 December 1986; surgery 18 December 1986 [A: Wikipedia].** A previously undiagnosed
brain lymphoma. Hospital: **Georgetown University Hospital**, 3800 Reservoir Road NW, confirmed
by UPI and the Los Angeles Times, September 1987. Coordinate corrected, see section 11.

**CORRECTION, "his illness prevented testimony".** Wrong as stated. Casey had already testified
to Congress on Iran-Contra before he fell ill: Walsh records that "Casey testified in December
1986", and he appeared before the Senate and House intelligence committees in November and again
in December. What the 15 December seizures prevented was his **scheduled 16 December appearance**
and anything after it. The journey carries both facts as separate stops so the correction is
visible rather than merely asserted.

**Leave from 18 December 1986; tenure ends 29 January 1987 [A: Wikipedia].**

**Woodward's hospital visit, January 1987 [R].** *Veil* (1987) narrates a four-minute bedside
exchange: "His head jerked up hard. He stared, and finally nodded yes."

**CORRECTION, the hospital in the Woodward account.** The pool placed this at a Manhattan
hospital in one lens and at Bethesda Naval Hospital in another. Both are wrong. UPI, 25 September
1987: Woodward "claims he slipped into Casey's Georgetown University Hospital room in January",
and the Los Angeles Times of the next day agrees. Quotation, four-minute framing, 1987 date and
[R] tag are correct; the place is not. Adamantly denied by his widow, Sofia Casey
**[A: Wikipedia]**. Not adjudicated.

**CORRECTION, place and cause of death.** Casey did not die at the Roslyn Harbor estate. He died
of pneumonia in a hospital at Glen Cove, Long Island, on **6 May 1987**, aged 74. New York Times:
"died of pneumonia yesterday in Glen Cove Community [Hospital]"; UPI and the Sun-Sentinel concur.
Wikipedia's infobox "Roslyn Harbor" records his residence. Date and age are right.

**A residual contradiction, not resolved.** One account in the Iran-Contra article has him
hospitalized for a **stroke** rather than a tumor at the time of death. Neither is preferred.

**Requiem Mass, St. Mary's, Roslyn, May 1987 [A: Wikipedia],** celebrated by Fr. Daniel Fagan.
**Bishop John R. McGann, presiding, used the pulpit to criticize Casey's ethics and his actions
in Nicaragua, with President and Mrs. Reagan in attendance [A: Wikipedia].**

**Burial, Cemetery of the Holy Rood, Westbury [A: Wikipedia].** The pool carried three
coordinates for the church and two for the grave; both standardized on OSM, see section 11.
**Sophia Kurz Casey dies, 2000 [A: Wikipedia];** the same pool renders her name both "Sophia" and
"Sofia", not resolved.

## 9. Quotations: what is real and what is not

1. **"His head jerked up hard. He stared, and finally nodded yes."** Woodward, *Veil* (1987).
   Woodward's narration, not Casey's speech; contested by the family, contradicted by Walsh, and
   used once, at the Georgetown Hospital stop, with the dispute stated in the campa. The same
   applies to the reported February 1987 admission, which is likewise Woodward paraphrased.
2. **"The Resurrection of 'Wild Bill'"**: **CORRECTION.** Not a spoken characterization by the
   outgoing DCI Stansfield Turner, as the pool had it, but the **chapter subtitle** of Turner's
   *Burn Before Reading* (2005), chapter nine, verified by extracting the book's own contents page
   and chapter head. The pool's note that Wikipedia cites no underlying publication is also
   wrong: ref 36 is that Turner book. Used as a chapter title, not as a 1981 utterance.
3. **"Only the director of the CIA William J. Casey supported McFarlane's plan to start selling
   arms to Iran."** Wikipedia, "Iran-Contra affair". A third-party paraphrase, labelled as such
   in `quote_source`.

**No verbatim Casey speech was recovered in this pass.** For a man who wrote a war memoir, edited
a tax service and testified repeatedly before Congress, that is a gap in the reachable sources
rather than in the record.
