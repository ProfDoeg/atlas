# Allen Welsh Dulles (1893-1969): research report

*Compiled 2026-08-15. Slug `allen_dulles`; companion `allen_dulles.journey.json`, 8 segments, 45 stops.*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named · **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or secondhand report · **[gap]** checked and not found.

The verified pool arrived with adversarial corrections attached to individual items. They are carried here as flagged contradictions, not silently absorbed: where the pool's lenses disagreed, both readings are printed and the adopted one is named with its reason.

---

## 1. Watertown and the grandfather's table (1893-1914)

**Birth, 7 April 1893**, in the manse of the First Presbyterian Church, Watertown, New York, where his father Allen Macy Dulles is pastor; mother Edith Foster Dulles **[A: Wikipedia, "Allen Dulles"]**. Pin corrected to 43.9756, -75.9064. *Contradiction flagged:* the geography lens claimed John Foster Dulles was born "five years earlier in the same house." False: Wikipedia gives 25 February 1888, Washington, D.C. **[A]**. He grew up in Watertown, he was not born in the manse, and the file states the five-year gap and the shared boyhood house, never a shared birthplace.

**Henderson Harbor.** Boyhood summers at the Lake Ontario house of their maternal grandfather John Watson Foster, Benjamin Harrison's Secretary of State **[R: Kinzer, *The Brothers* (2013)]**: the standard biographical explanation of both careers, and a causal claim rather than a document.

**Princeton, class of 1914**, MA 1916, active in the American Whig-Cliosophic Society **[A: Wikipedia; Princeton records]**. *Two corrections.* The chronology lens gave 1911, which would make him eighteen at graduation with five idle years before the Foreign Service, while the pool's own geography and afterlife lenses both give 1914, which is adopted; and Foster's Princeton years were six before Allen's, not "a decade." *Coordinates:* the pool gave 40.3431/-74.6551 while naming Nassau Hall, which is at 40.3487, -74.6594 (Wikipedia, "Nassau Hall"). The corrected pin serves both the 1910-1914 stop and the 1966 award stop.

**Allahabad, 1914-1915** **[R: Grose, *Gentleman Spy* (1994)]**. Wikipedia says only that he "taught school in India before entering the diplomatic service in 1916" **[A]**; city and school **[gap]**, named as such in the stop.

## 2. The legations (1916-1926)

**Entry to the service, 1916** **[A]**. *Coordinates flagged:* the pool pinned the Department of State at 38.8977/-77.0365, which is the White House, and reused that pair for two later White House stops. In 1916 the Department sat in the State, War, and Navy Building, approximately 38.8977, -77.0387, the pin used here. **Vienna, 1916** **[A]** is the first posting.

**Bern, 1917, the Lenin telephone call.** Transferred shortly before American entry **[A]**. Wikipedia: "Later in life, Dulles said he had been telephoned by Vladimir Lenin, seeking a meeting with the American embassy on April 8, 1917, the day before Lenin left Switzerland" **[A as to Dulles having told it]**. Whether the call happened is **[R]** and disputed. *Two errors corrected:* the chronology lens dated it to "the day before the Bolshevik uprising," but the October Revolution was seven months later; and the geography lens inverted the attribution, saying "Dulles himself calls the story apocryphal in later years," where Wikipedia has him as its source, not its debunker.

**Paris, 1919.** American delegation to the peace conference, alongside Foster as legal counsel **[A: both brothers' Wikipedia articles]**. Hôtel de Crillon, 48.8656, 2.3212.

**Berlin, 1920-1922** **[R: Grose]**, in the biographies and absent from the standard chronologies; dropped as a stop. The 1920 marriage to Martha "Clover" Todd **[A; place **[gap]**]** is carried at the 1926 stop instead.

**Constantinople, 1921.** Embassy posting; takes part in exposing *The Protocols of the Elders of Zion* as a forgery **[A]**. Pera, 41.037, 28.9773. *Contradiction flagged:* the geography lens placed the 1922-1926 Near East division chieftaincy here too, but the pool's own chronology lens is right that it was a Washington desk **[A]**. Two separate postings, separated in the file.

**Law degree and Wall Street.** LLB from George Washington University and the move to Sullivan & Cromwell **[A as facts]**; the year **[R]**. Chronology lens 1927, geography lens 1926, Wikipedia none, Grose and Kinzer 1926. 1926 adopted, the ambiguity carried in date_confidence rather than prose.

## 3. Sullivan & Cromwell and the German business (1926-1941)

**48 Wall Street**, joining the firm Foster runs **[A: Wikipedia; Kinzer]**.

**The I.G. Farben cartel.** Under Foster the firm helps incorporate I.G. Farben into an international nickel cartel, aiding the German arms buildup **[A: Wikipedia, "Sullivan & Cromwell"]**, dated only to "1930s". **[gap]** on Allen's personal role.

**Council on Foreign Relations**, director from 1927, later secretary, later president **[A]**. *Anachronism corrected:* the pool pinned this at the Harold Pratt House, which Wikipedia dates to a 1944 donation and an April 1945 move-in. The directorship and secretaryship were served at 45 East 65th Street, approximately 40.7671, -73.9679.

**Berlin, 1933.** On firm business, received by Adolf Hitler, calls on Joseph Goebbels **[A: Wikipedia; Talbot, *The Devil's Chessboard* (2015)]**. The pool's April dating is **[R]**.

**Berlin, 1935.** Returns "somewhat disturbed," leads the junior partners in forcing the closure of the Berlin office and Frankfurt subsidiary, Foster alone opposing; the firm then backdates the closure announcement to 1934 to obscure how long it had continued **[A: Wikipedia, both articles]**. The sharpest documented fact of the interwar section, and it gets its own stop. *Correction carried:* the pool's 1945 Berlin item called him "Hitler's guest" in 1933 and put 1945 "twelve years after his last visit." Both wrong; the 1935 trip makes the interval ten years.

**Congressional run, 1938.** Loses the Republican nomination in New York's 16th district **[A]**; no vote, opponent, or address reachable **[gap]**, so the stop is pinned generically in Manhattan and says so.

## 4. Herrengasse 23 (1941-1945)

**October 1941.** Recruited by Donovan into the forming OSS as liaison with British Security Co-ordination; New York station at Room 3603, 630 Fifth Avenue **[A: Wikipedia; OSS institutional history]**. **12 November 1942.** Arrives as OSS station chief for Switzerland, renting Herrengasse 23 in the Bern old town **[A]**, crossing on the last day the border is passable before the occupation of Vichy seals it.

**Fritz Kolbe, 1943-1944**, German Foreign Office diplomat: material on the Me 262 and on active German agents **[A]**. **The Maier network, 1943-1944**, around the Austrian priest Heinrich Maier: V-weapons, tanks, aircraft production, feeding Operation Crossbow **[A]**. **Schröder, 1944-1945**: involvement with German industrialists and the SS financier Kurt Freiherr von Schröder in moving Nazi-linked funds out of Germany **[A as reported]**; the intent is contested, tagged as reported.

**Operation Sunrise.** 8 March 1945, Lucerne: Max Waibel arranges the meeting at which SS General Karl Wolff first presents surrender terms for Italy. 15 and 19 March: Wolff works out the mechanics with the American general Lyman Lemnitzer and the British general Terence Airey. 29 April: the surrender is signed at Caserta **[A: Wikipedia, "Operation Sunrise (World War II)"]**. Protection for Wolff from later Nuremberg prosecution, offered for cooperation **[A: Wikipedia, "Allen Dulles"]**. *Location error corrected:* the pool placed the follow-on meetings near Zurich, citing Max Husmann's school, but Husmann's school was the Institut Montana on the Zugerberg above Zug, and the Lemnitzer/Airey meetings Wikipedia documents took place at Ascona on Lake Maggiore, roughly 150 km south. Ascona, 46.1553, 8.7736, is used.

*The historians' verdict*, both **[A: cited in the same article]**. Weinberg, that Sunrise reflects very badly on Dulles, who let himself be carried away and acted over Joint Chiefs orders; his own sentence carries an em dash, so the judgement is paraphrased in the Caserta campa and named to him, never quoted. Galbraith: "Never, not even in the Bay of Pigs, was his capacity for detached misjudgment more disastrous than in his management of Operation Sunrise, as the Wolff negotiations were called." Used verbatim at Caserta.

*Dansey, splice flagged.* Wikipedia: Claude Dansey "described Dulles as a 'fool' who 'swallows [false information] easily'" **[A as to the words; citing Jeffery, *MI6* (2010), pp. 380-382, 507, 509-512]**. The pool rendered this as one continuous quotation; it is two fragments joined by Wikipedia's connective "who". The file quotes only the second verbatim and keeps the editorial brackets.

**Berlin, 1945.** Roughly six months as OSS station chief in the occupied city before returning to Bern; OSS dissolved that October **[A]**.

## 5. The Georgetown Set (1947-1953)

1947, senior staff on the Herter Committee, and the "Georgetown Set" **[A]**. 1948, advises Dewey's campaign alongside Foster **[A]**, folded into the Herter stop **[gap]** on location. 1949, co-authors the Dulles-Jackson-Correa Report, sharply critical of the CIA's organization and performance **[A]**; that the author of the indictment then inherited the agency is left to the sequence of stops. 4 January 1951, Deputy Director for Plans; 23 August 1951, Deputy Director of Central Intelligence; 26 February 1953, first civilian DCI **[A]**, a month after Foster takes the State oath **[A]**. Headquarters throughout: 2430 E Street NW, 38.8938, -77.0524.

## 6. The brothers' decade (1953-1959)

**Iran, August 1953.** Approves TPAJAX, the joint CIA-SIS operation removing Mohammad Mosaddegh and restoring the Shah **[A: Wikipedia; Kinzer, *All the Shah's Men* (2003)]**. He does not travel to Tehran; Kermit Roosevelt Jr. and station officers run it **[A]**.

*Interlock, direct and one-directional.* `mohammad_mosaddegh.journey.json` carries the shelling of the Kakh Street house and the surrender the next morning, naming the CIA but not Dulles, who approved the operation that ended that government. He is named in Dulles's Washington campa; the Tehran stops stay Mosaddegh's.

**Guatemala, 1953-1954.** August 1953, Eisenhower authorizes PBSuccess **[A: Wikipedia, "1954 Guatemalan coup d'état"]**; both brothers had arranged deals for the United Fruit Company as Sullivan & Cromwell partners, a relationship critics say let the administration conflate commercial and national interest **[A: same]**. The "Lincoln" base at Opa-locka is the operational centre **[A: Wikipedia, "Operation PBSuccess"]**, but Dulles's presence there is **[gap]**, so it gets only a suggested_ref; the Matamoros Fortress bombing of 18 June **[A]** is likewise not his. On 22-23 June, with the Castillo Armas column stalling, he arranges three more P-47 fighters, judged critical **[A]**: a Dulles act, and a Washington stop. On 27 June Árbenz leaves the palace at 8 p.m. **[A]**.

*Correction carried, twice.* The pool said the coup "installs Castillo Armas" on 27 June and that Árbenz resigned "within days" of the bombing. Power passed first to a junta under Col. Carlos Enrique Díaz, with Cols. Elfego Monzón and José Ángel Sánchez; Castillo Armas was elected president of the junta only on 7 July, and the interval from bombing to resignation is nine days. The file states the junta.

**McCarthy, 1953-1954.** At Dulles's request Eisenhower shuts down McCarthy's subpoenas of CIA personnel; documents declassified in 2004 show the agency, on Dulles's orders, burgled McCarthy's Senate office and fed him disinformation to discredit him **[A as to the declassified record]**. **U-2, 1954-1957**, developed under Dulles, operational 1957 **[A]**; no site given and **[gap]** on whether he visited one, so it is folded into the 1960 shootdown stop.

**Langley cornerstone, 3 November 1959.** *Attribution corrected:* the pool credited Dulles, but Wikipedia, "George Bush Center for Intelligence" has Eisenhower laying the cornerstone. Dulles was present.

**Foster's death, 24 May 1959**, colon cancer at Walter Reed, buried at Arlington **[A]**. *Correction carried:* the pool said Allen died "ten years almost to the day" after him. The gap is nine years and eight months, in a different month of the year. Struck.

## 7. The fall (1960-1961)

**Lumumba, 27 July 1960.** `patrice_lumumba.journey.json` carries "Washington, the State Department" at 38.8951, -77.0479, naming Dulles directly, calling Lumumba's removal "an urgent and prime objective" while Lumumba is in Washington asking State for backing **[A: corpus]**. Separately the agency allocates roughly $100,000 toward killing him, never carried out directly **[A: Church Committee Report (1975)]**. The strongest interlock in the corpus, and direct; the pin is inherited byte-identical. *Coordinate error flagged:* the pool gave Léopoldville as +4.4419, 15.2663, a sign error putting the point roughly 975 km north of the city (Kinshasa is -4.3219, 15.3119). Moot here, since Dulles never went to the Congo.

**U-2, 1 May 1960.** An SA-2 brings down Francis Gary Powers over Sverdlovsk **[A]**; Dulles is in Washington, so Sverdlovsk is not his stop.

**Bay of Pigs, April 1961.** Planned under Dulles, launched ten weeks into the Kennedy administration, and it fails **[A]**. He is away from Washington at the landing, giving a speech in Puerto Rico **[A]**. The brigade lands 17 April and surrenders 20 April **[A]**; the pool's "crushed within three days" ending 19 April is a day short. Playa Girón gets no stop: he was not there, and the absence is itself the documented point.

*Two quotations, both* **[R]** *as to primary evidence.* "The worst day of my life," cited at Wikipedia to Rorabaugh (2002), p. 29, replacing the pool's unverifiable Grose attribution; used as a quote field at San Juan. And Kennedy "reportedly said he wanted to 'splinter the CIA into a thousand pieces and scatter it into the winds,'" traced to *The New York Times*, 25 April 1966, p. 1, not to Schlesinger's *A Thousand Days* as the pool had it: a newspaper account attributing the line secondhand through an unnamed official, three years after Kennedy's death. The word "reportedly" must survive any use of it, and it appears in the file as reported speech, never as a quote field.

**Resignation, 28-29 November 1961.** Kennedy presents the National Security Medal at Langley on 28 November **[A]**; the letter is released the next day. Bissell and Cabell go too, McCone succeeds, and the failed Algiers putsch against de Gaulle is cited alongside the Bay of Pigs **[R]**. *Venue error flagged:* the pool's geography lens put the ceremony at the White House while its own afterlife lens put it correctly at Langley, 38.9517/-77.1467, which is used.

## 8. The commission and the last years (1963-1969)

**The Craft of Intelligence, 1963**, largely ghostwritten **[A]**. **Warren Commission, 29 November 1963**, appointed by Johnson under Executive Order 11130 **[A]**, working from the VFW Building, 200 Maryland Avenue NE **[A: Warren Commission Report (1964)]**. His appointment is criticized given Kennedy's dismissal of him **[A as criticism]**, and CIA officials under McCone reportedly withheld from the commission what they knew of agency plots against Castro **[A as reported]**. Final report, 888 pages, 24 September 1964 **[A]**.

*Interlock: John F. Kennedy.* Direct and reciprocal: Kennedy fired him, decorated him, and was the subject of the commission he then sat on. `john_f_kennedy.journey.json` carries the Bay of Pigs and the Dallas and Bethesda stops without naming Dulles; those stops are Kennedy's and do not appear here, while Kennedy is named in Dulles's own campas.

**Princeton, 1966**, James Madison Award **[A]**. **Great True Spy Stories, 1968**, edited **[A]**. **Death, 29 January 1969**, Georgetown, influenza complicated by pneumonia, age 75 **[A]**; reportedly Alzheimer's disease in his final years **[R: family report]**. His sister Eleanor Lansing Dulles's claim of "at least a hundred" extramarital affairs **[R: family recollection, no verifiable count]** is carried in one campa as a family claim, not a count.

**Burial, Green Mount Cemetery, Baltimore** **[A]**. *Coordinates corrected* to 39.3075, -76.6072 (Wikipedia, "Green Mount Cemetery"); the pool carried three inconsistent variants. *Framing corrected:* the pool called it "the historic Dulles-Foster family burial ground," which is unsupported. Wikipedia lists Allen individually with no family plot, and Foster and their grandfather John Watson Foster are both at Arlington. Clover Todd's Baltimore connections are the likelier explanation, given hedged.

**Afterlife, not staged as stops.** A stop belongs to whoever was actually there, so none of the following gets a scene; each is carried as a source or suggested_ref on a stop Dulles occupied. Helms's plaque at Langley **[R; no date]**; Oliver Stone's *JFK* (1991) **[R as portrayal]** and Peter McRobbie in *Bridge of Spies* (2015) **[A]**; Talbot, *The Devil's Chessboard* (2015), arguing he orchestrated Kennedy's assassination and steered the commission to protect himself, a case reviewers called far ahead of the evidence **[A as to the book and its reception]**; a CIA in-house "Official History of the Bay of Pigs" **[R: author and release unconfirmed]**; the pipe, tweed, and rimless glasses **[R: no citable source for the pattern]**.

## 9. Honest gaps

1. The India year: city and school unconfirmed, Allahabad the circulating figure only.
2. The 1938 congressional run: no vote, opponent, or campaign location.
3. Allen's personal role in the I.G. Farben nickel cartel, as distinct from the firm's.
4. The 1920 marriage to Clover Todd: no place, no church. The Helms plaque: no date.
5. His presence at Opa-locka, at the U-2 test site, and in Dallas with the commission: unattested in every case, therefore unmapped.
6. The corpus. A grep of every `*.journey.json` for "Sullivan Cromwell", "OSS", "Operation Sunrise", and "Bern" returned no genuine hits, only false positives. The law practice, the Bern station, and Sunrise were sourced fresh rather than inherited.
7. No Dulles paper collection was opened. Princeton's Allen W. Dulles Papers and the CIA's declassified holdings are the next layer and were not reached.
