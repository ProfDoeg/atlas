# Hillary Rodham Clinton: research report

*Compiled 2026-08-10 for the Atlas of Journeys. Slug `hillary_clinton`. Born 26 October 1947; alive at time of writing.*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named · **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, inference, or contested.

**Method note.** She is alive, and that governs the document. The template asks for tomb, remains, and cult of the grave; none apply. The afterlife lens flagged the mismatch [A] and collected what exists instead: portraits, a library, a Broadway play, two conspiracy traditions. That is the journey's closing segment, not an afterlife.

## 1. Park Ridge, 1947-1965

Birth 26 October 1947, Edgewater Hospital, Chicago; first child of Hugh and Dorothy Rodham [A, Wikipedia "Hillary Clinton"; Bernstein, *A Woman in Charge* (2007); White House biography]. Park Ridge from 1950, Republican and Methodist household [A, Wikipedia; Bernstein; Clinton, *Living History* (2003)]. Goldwater volunteer 1964; South Side canvass at thirteen after the 1960 election [A, Wikipedia; no coordinate]. Top five percent at graduation, 1965 [A].

**Contradiction internal to the pool, birth coordinate.** Geography lens 41.9847, **-87.6693**; afterlife lens, same event, 41.9847, **-87.6598**, ~790 m apart. Verification put the hospital site near 41.9856, -87.6690. **The journey uses 41.9847, -87.6690**: a decision, not a finding, since no pool source fixes it.

**Gap, the house number.** The lens gave "236 Wisner Street"; verification could not re-source it and found the home published elsewhere as **235** [R, Park Ridge article]. **The atlas states no house number**, and 42.0111, -87.8406 locates the town, not the house.

**Displacement, the 1962 King speech.** Don Jones takes her to hear Martin Luther King Jr. [A, *Living History*], dated 1962 at Orchestra Hall [A, Wikipedia]. **The pool has no Orchestra Hall coordinate**, so it is narrated at the church pin, where the van left from. Flagged so nobody reads that pin as the site of the speech.

**Contradiction, one high school or two.** The lens gave one entry, "Maine South (Maine East) High School," 42.0028, -87.8442, spanning 1961-1965. Verified: **Maine East** 1961-1964, then redistricting, then graduation from **Maine South** in its first graduating class, 1965 [A, Wikipedia]. Maine East **42.0381, -87.8581**; Maine South **42.0014, -87.8531**. The lens pin sits ~750 m from one and 4.6 km from the other, at neither. **The journey splits it into two stops** and discards the original pin.

## 2. Wellesley and Yale, 1965-1973

Wellesley 1965-1969, political science with department honours; Young Republicans chair, then a move left; College Government Association president 1968; first student commencement speaker in the college's history 1969, profiled in *Life* [A, Wikipedia; Wellesley archives; *Life*, 20 June 1969]. 1968 Republican National Convention at Miami Beach in a Rockefeller effort [A, Wikipedia]; **no coordinate, narrated at the Wellesley pin.** Yale Law 1969-1973; Child Study Center; *Yale Review of Law and Social Action*; meets Bill Clinton in the library; "Children Under the Law," 1973 [A, Yale records; *Living History*; Wikipedia]. Summer 1970 with Marian Wright Edelman [A, Wikipedia]; **no coordinate, unpinned.**

**Two Wellesley coordinates kept deliberately.** Afterlife lens: commencement 1969-05-31 at 42.2951, **-71.3049**; geography lens: the four years at 42.2951, **-71.3057**. Eight metres apart. Both kept on their own stops so each lens's value stays traceable.

**The Rayburn stop is [R].** The geography lens tagged the 1974 House Judiciary impeachment-inquiry post **[R]** on Bernstein alone; the chronology lens omits it. It is carried because it is load-bearing for 1998, with `date_confidence` saying so. Not to be read as attested.

## 3. Arkansas, 1974-1992

Fayetteville law faculty from August 1974; marriage 11 October 1975 at their Fayetteville home; Rose Law Firm February 1977, first woman partner 1979; Arkansas Advocates for Children and Families 1977; Chelsea Clinton born 27 February 1980; Education Standards chair 1983; HIPPY 1985; Walmart board 1986 as first woman member; among the hundred most influential lawyers in America 1988 [A, Wikipedia; *Living History*; *The National Law Journal*]. **Bentonville has no coordinate, not pinned.** Whitewater and the Rose billing records [A, Independent Counsel reports]: the atlas states the subpoena and makes no finding, because the sources made none. 1992: the 60 Minutes appearance and the Tammy Wynette remark [A]; the Old State House victory speech 3 November 1992 [A, AP wire photos].

**Coordinate correction, Governor's Mansion.** Lens 34.7379, -92.2896. Verified 1800 Center Street, **34.7319, -92.2760** (Wikipedia infobox), ~1.4 km away; the lens value lands near the Capitol grounds. **Journey uses the corrected value.** Terms 1979-1981 and 1983-1992 confirmed.

## 4. First Lady, 1993-2001

Inaugurated 20 January 1993; health care task force chair January 1993; plan abandoned September 1994; CHIP, Whitewater, Lewinsky [A, Wikipedia; White House Historical Association; *Living History*].

**Venue correction, Beijing 1995.** The lens placed the "women's rights are human rights" speech at **Huairou** (40.3167, 116.6333). Verified: the 5 September plenary keynote was at the **Beijing International Convention Center**, approx. **39.9925, 116.3900**; Huairou hosted the parallel NGO Forum ~50 km out, addressed **separately on 6 September** [A, Wikipedia]. **Journey uses the convention centre** and keeps Huairou as the separate event it was.

**Quote status, Beijing.** The string is **Wikiquote only**, abbreviated; the State Department transcript could not be fetched (state.gov and americanrhetoric.com blocked). **Exact wording is not established**, and `quote_source` says so.

**Kolkata dropped.** The lens carried a March 1995 Mother Teresa meeting with a coordinate ~2.5-3 km off the Motherhouse (approx. 22.5645, 88.3590), and verification could not confirm it happened in Kolkata at all. **Not pinned.** An open lead, not a fact.

**Tuzla, 25 March 1996** [A, CBS News footage]; coordinate and date confirmed. **Source-date correction:** the sniper-fire claim was made **17 March 2008** and the rebuttal followed ~**21 March 2008**, not February 2008 as the lens said. Sinbad and Sheryl Crow corroboration confirmed. The atlas sets the 1996 footage beside the 2008 claim and adjudicates no further.

**1998** is carried at the White House pin; the approval figure and midterm gain narrated there are **[R]** general public record, and `date_confidence` says so.

## 5. New York and the Senate, 1999-2009

Elected November 2000, first woman elected statewide in New York to that office; sworn in 3 January 2001; Armed Services Committee; Ground Zero 12 September 2001; Iraq vote October 2002; Baghdad and Bagram November 2003; defeated for the 2008 nomination [A, Wikipedia; US Senate Historical Office; CNN; AP].

**County correction, Pindars Corners.** Lens: **Otsego County**, 42.7217, -75.226. Verified: a hamlet in the town of **Davenport, Delaware County**, approx. **42.45, -74.93**, some 40 km away [A, Wikipedia]. The 7 July 1999 launch at Moynihan's farm is confirmed. **Journey uses 42.45, -74.93**, hamlet-level, not farm-level.

**Label and coordinate correction, Chappaqua.** Lens: "Rodham family home," 41.1595, -73.7987. Both wrong: it is the **Clinton** family home, bought for 1.7 million dollars in 1999, and 15 Old House Lane is approx. **41.155, -73.772**, the lens value ~2.2 km west of the hamlet. **Journey uses the corrected value.**

**Quote status, Iraq vote.** "This is probably the hardest decision I have ever had to make" is **Wikiquote only**; Congressional Record S10288 returned HTTP 403, wording unverified.

**Pin discrepancy disclosed, 7 June 2008.** The "18 million cracks" remarks were given at the National Building Museum, for which **the pool has no coordinate**. The journey carries the stop at the Capitol pin (38.8899, -77.0091) and names the real venue in prose: a placeholder, flagged rather than fudged. Wikiquote has only the short clause; the fuller sentence used is the widely reported form.

## 6. Secretary of State, 2009-2013

Sworn in 21 January 2009 as the 67th Secretary; out 1 February 2013; nearly a million miles to 112 countries [A, history.state.gov]. Islamabad October 2009 [A, NYT]; Tripoli 18 October 2011 [A, State Dept press pool].

**Date and place contradiction at the nomination.** The chronology lens carried "nominated, 2008-11, Chicago" from history.state.gov. Verified: the Historian narrates the *decision* as November 2008, but the **Chicago announcement was 1 December 2008** and the formal nomination reached the Senate **20 January 2009**. **No Chicago stop is carried** (no venue coordinate); it is narrated inside the State Department stop.

**Yangon, two corrections.** Lens 16.7967, 96.1381 and 1 December 2011. Verified: 54 University Avenue is **16.8258, 96.1505**, ~3.5 km away, and the meeting there was **2 December 2011**; 1 December was a private dinner elsewhere. "First Secretary of State since 1955" confirmed. **Journey uses the corrections.**

**Quote correction, Geneva.** The lens carried the truncated "Gay rights are human rights." The full sentence is **"Gay rights are human rights, and human rights are gay rights."** [A, verified via Open Library full-text search returning several published books quoting it and attributing it to the 6 December 2011 Human Rights Day address]. Wikiquote has only the first clause, citing *The Week*, 10 December 2011. **Journey uses the full sentence.**

**Attribution correction, Cartagena.** The lens said the April 2012 Secret Service prostitution scandal involved "her advance team." It did not: the agents belonged to **President Obama's** protective detail, in country ahead of the president [A, Wikipedia]. Nothing ties it to Clinton's staff. **Journey states the correct attribution.**

**Two corrections, Great Hall of the People.** Lens 39.9046, 116.3915 falls in Tiananmen Square, ~370 m off; the Great Hall is **39.9033, 116.3875**. And February 2009 was **not** an S&ED round: the first was July 2009 in Washington, the Beijing rounds **May 2010 and May 2012**. **Journey carries one May 2012 stop at the corrected pin.** The Chen Guangcheng material is **[R]** general public record.

**Benghazi, [R] by the lens and kept [R].** Three corrections: 32.1167, 20.0667 is a city centroid, not the compound, which stood in **Western Fwayhat** at approx. **32.086, 20.052**; the facility was a **Temporary Mission Facility**, not a consulate; and **only Stevens and Sean Smith died there**, Woods and Doherty being killed early on 12 September at the **CIA annex a mile away**. The dispute stands: the **Accountability Review Board (2012)** and the **House Select Committee report (2016)** are named against each other and the atlas adjudicates neither.

**Quote status, "What difference, at this point, does it make?"** Tagged **[R]**, widely reported from the 23 January 2013 testimony and also reported as House Oversight testimony that week; **no transcript reachable**.

**Vladivostok dropped for length.** APEC, Russky Island, 8 September 2012, 43.0189, 131.8992 [A, State Dept travel schedule] is in the pool and *not* in the journey: a choice, not a hole.

## 7. 2016

Nominee and first woman to head a major US party ticket; wins the popular vote, loses the Electoral College; nomination accepted 28 July 2016; Javits Center 8 November; concession the next morning [A, Wikipedia; DNC transcript; NYT 8-9 November 2016].

**Quote correction, Obama on Clinton.** The pool's "There is no one, ever, not me, not Bill, nobody more qualified..." is a **paraphrase**. Verbatim, per the UCSB American Presidency Project transcript of the 27 July 2016 DNC address: *"And that's why I can say with confidence there has never been a man or a woman, not me, not Bill, nobody, more qualified than Hillary Clinton to serve as President of the United States of America."* The transcript's dashes are rendered as commas, with `quote_source` declaring it. Said *about* her.

**"Basket of deplorables": in the pool, not in the journey.** Full wording with "what I call" restored: *"You could put half of Trump's supporters into what I call the basket of deplorables."* 9 September 2016, LGBT fundraising event, New York [A, Wikipedia; Wikiquote's 10 September date is the article's publication date]. **No venue coordinate**, so no stop and no quote. A real loss, named as one.

**"Such a nasty woman":** Trump, third debate, UNLV, 19 October 2016 [R, widely reported, no transcript checked]. **No coordinate, not carried.**

**Email quote, venue corrected, not carried.** The lens attributed "I did not send classified material..." to a 10 March 2015 UN press conference in New York; Wikiquote's raw citation gives **Las Vegas, 18 August 2015**, and Wikipedia carries it as a repeated campaign formulation with dated variants. **No coordinate, not carried.**

**Venue correction, the concession.** Lens: "New York Presbyterian venue," 40.7527, -73.9772: garbled name, longitude ~1.4 km off. It was the ballroom of the **New Yorker Hotel**, 481 Eighth Avenue, **40.7528, -73.9936**. **No verbatim concession quote is carried**: the pool has only a paraphrase, so the journey narrates the moment without quotation marks.

**Canonical pins inherited exactly.** **Temple Mount, 31.778, 35.2354**, from `abraham.journey.json` line 541 and `adriano.journey.json` line 367, both travellers named in the campa; her Jerusalem visits 2009-2013 have **no date fixed in the pool**, so the journey's 2010-09-01 is an inference stated as one. **Paris, 48.8566, 2.3522**, atlas-wide canonical; her Paris travel is real in general and unfixed in particular, and the stop says it stands for a pattern, not a day.

**Inheritance explicitly rejected.** The lens proposed the canonical **Kaaba** pin (21.4225, 39.8262) for her Saudi travel, then flagged its own proposal: a non-Muslim official would not have entered the Sacred Mosque, and it would need re-siting to Riyadh or Jeddah. **Declined; no Saudi stop.** **Buenos Aires, -34.6037, -58.3816** (`augusto_boal.journey.json` line 249) for a 1995 Argentina trip [R] is likewise not carried, the trip being attested nowhere else in the pool.

**Six marital-proximity interlocks, all [R], none carried as stops:** Bill Clinton on Epstein's aircraft, September 2002 (`jeffrey_epstein.journey.json` line 318, which attests **Bill's** presence, hers being spousal inference); the 1994 Naples G7 with Berlusconi (same file line 330; `silvio_berlusconi.journey.json` line 212); the Sendak medal ceremony (`maurice_sendak.journey.json` line 455); the 1992 defeat of Bush and the December 2018 Bush 41 funeral (`george_hw_bush.journey.json` lines 448 and 549, the latter **not stating her attendance**); the library dedications circuit (`george_w_bush.journey.json` line 568). The Bushes are named in campa only where the atlas independently has her in the room: 1992, and the two 2004 ceremonies. **Larry Summers** (`QUEUE.md` line 724) and **the Epstein flight-log contradiction** (`jeffrey_epstein.journey.json` line 321) are [R] by proximity and stay in their own files.
