## 2. Warwick and the CCRU, c. 1990-1999

**PhD completed at Warwick 1999, thesis *Flatline Constructs: Gothic Materialism and Cybernetic
Theory-Fiction*, and founding CCRU membership [A]** (Wikipedia).

**Supervision by Sadie Plant: NOT ESTABLISHED, [R].** *Corrected:* the pool carried it [A] on the

**CCRU formed by Plant c. 1993-94, formalized at Warwick late 1995, Land the driving force after
her departure [A]** (Wikipedia, "Cybernetic Culture Research Unit"); *corrected*, the pool gave a

**Plant resigns 1997; Kodwo Eshun, Fisher, Ray Brassier, Reza Negarestani and Steve Goodman named

## Quotations: kept and thrown out

Twelve are carried, all verbatim from directly fetched pages. Four pool candidates were rejected.

1. **"It is easier to imagine the end of the world than to imagine the end of capitalism." REJECTED
   as a quotation.** The exact string is attested nowhere; the book's text is 403 or loan-gated
   everywhere reachable, and the doubled "to imagine" appears in no source. It is also **not
   Fisher's coinage**: on k-punk (9 April 2005) he credits Zizek, and Wikipedia renders the book's
   version as "it is easier to imagine an end to the world than an end to capitalism," attributed
   by Fisher to Jameson and Zizek. The journey **paraphrases it without quotation marks**.
2. **"It would be best, perhaps, to think of an alternate world..." REJECTED, MISATTRIBUTED:**
   **Fredric Jameson's** words, from *Valences of the Dialectic*, quoted by Fisher on k-punk, 25
   December 2009. Now a `suggested_ref` attributed to Jameson.
3. **"The market is essentially a ploy by which one group of peoples interests are imposed on the
   rest of us." REJECTED, MISATTRIBUTED:** the pseudonymous postal worker **Roy Mayall's** words,
   from "Not Nostalgia", LRB blog, 3 December 2009. Now a `suggested_ref` attributed to Mayall.
4. **"Everything is impossible now, including the so-called status quo." KEPT, ATTRIBUTION
   CHANGED.** Fisher's words, but not k-punk prose: in the 25 December 2009 post the sentence is the
   **title of Matthew Fuller's Mute interview with him**, and **the Mute page could not be
   reached**, so it is cited as a title and nothing more.

The *On Vanishing Land* quotation carried dashes around its parenthetical in the original; house
style forbids the character, so it is rendered without them, meaning unchanged.
