# R. Buckminster Fuller — Milton to Los Angeles (1895–1983)

**31 stops across 7 segments**, gregorian calendar. The canon: Fuller's own books (*Nine Chains to the Moon*, *Operating Manual for Spaceship Earth*, *I Seem to Be a Verb*, *Synergetics*, *Critical Path*), the *Dymaxion Chronofile*, the *Everything I Know* transcripts, and the biographies. Register: national-mythology — the 1927 vision and the deathbed cry are narrated as events, because in the canon they are.

## Judgment calls
- **The Lake Michigan vision** is marked `traditional`, not `attested`: scholars combing the 45-ton Chronofile found no contemporaneous record of a suicide attempt, and Fuller told it as a shaped, recurring lecture-legend. In this register I narrate the vision as event while flagging the date's softness — the honest fold.
- **Quotes**: I used verbatim canon where possible (the voice's words, *Spaceship Earth*, *Synergetics*, "I seem to be a verb", "Call me Trimtab"). Several stops carry Fuller aphorisms marked "(attributed)" — genuinely his but of uncertain first-utterance; a handful are placed thematically rather than at a documented occasion.
- **Chronofile stop** dated 1927 (the turning year) though the daily 15-minute logging is usually dated from 1920 — I placed it where it belongs narratively, in the vow's aftermath, and say so in the campa.
- **Kabul dome (1956)**: the USIA airlift domes to the trade fairs are attested; Kabul is the emblematic instance chosen to carry the "domes travel the earth" beat.

## The tradition's own gaps
Fuller's is the *most-documented* life ever lived, yet its founding miracle is the least documented moment in it — the Chronofile is silent exactly where the myth is loudest. The canon also smooths the failures (Stockade, the fatal car, the never-produced Wichita house) into a redemption arc; I kept the failures sharp, since the register's truth is his, and he narrated ruin as the engine of the turn.

## The five richest episodes
1. **The shore of Lake Michigan (1927)** — the suicide-resolve and the sphere of white light; the axis of the whole journey.
2. **The two deaths in Los Angeles (1983)** — "She is squeezing my hand!", the heart attack at the bedside, Anne following 36 hours later after 66 years.
3. **Black Mountain's supine dome (1948)** — the blessed failure that folds to the grass, then the first true geodesic sphere in 1949.
4. **The Dymaxion car crash at the Century of Progress (1933)** — the land-going aircraft buried under a headline.
5. **The Montreal Biosphère (1967)** — the skybreak bubble whose memory names the buckyball, writing his geometry into carbon itself.

## Connection to the atlas
His **suicide-turn** ("you belong to the universe") faces every seeker in the atlas pulled back from the edge — Gurdjieff's grandmother's injunction, the water's-edge conversions. His **domes and whole-earth** face the architects and cosmologists: he is a modern **Steiner** (the Goetheanum's sculpted domes) and a modern **Nizami** (the seven-domes cosmology), a builder of sacred spheres in a secular key. As a **Milton transcendentalist** and grandnephew of **Margaret Fuller**, he roots into the New England esoteric lineage; as world-circling lecturer and system-builder he rhymes with **Fichte** and **Froebel** (whose kindergarten gifts literally shaped his geometry). The Dymaxion map — no top, no bottom — is the atlas's own governing image: one island Earth, no privileged north.

## Addendum, 2026-08-11: the Froebel line checked, no stop added

The Froebel connection was re-examined in an enrichment pass covering Feynman and Fuller together, on the standard that only a real, specific, documented personal fact earns a stop, not a broad art-historical grouping. **Fuller passes the standard, and the journey already carries it.** No stop was added and no campa was touched; this is a sourcing note plus one dating flag.

**[A] The peas-and-toothpicks episode is Fuller's own, first person.** Fuller: "One of my first days at kindergarten the teacher brought us some toothpicks and semi-dried peas, and told us to make structures... I found that the triangle held its shape when nothing else did." Quoted in Norman Brosterman, *Inventing Kindergarten* (1997), p. 84; reproduced with that citation in Michael Rumbelow, "Give peas a chance," University of Bristol CTLC blog, 29 March 2021. Fuller tells it again in his own idiom in the *Synergetics* dictionary entry "Model of Toothpicks and Semi-dried Peas," where he dates it to "kindergarten at the age of four." This is not Brosterman grouping Fuller thematically with Wright and the Bauhaus; it is Fuller's own recollection, and Brosterman is the transcriber.

**[A] The material is Froebel's, not generic.** Pea-and-stick work is a named Froebel occupation, documented in English as early as Bertha Ronge's *A Practical Guide to the English Kindergarten* (1854), Ronge having studied under Froebel directly. Fuller's kindergarten in New England around 1900 is described as Froebelian in the secondary literature (Rumbelow; Froebel Web, "Peas and Sticks"; 99% Invisible, "Inheriting Froebel's Gifts"). The existing stop "Milton Academy, the Froebel table" therefore stands on real ground, and the line in "Connection to the atlas" above, that Froebel's "kindergarten gifts literally shaped his geometry," is upheld rather than softened.

**[R] Dating flag, logged not fixed.** The existing stop is dated **1904-09-01** and folds kindergarten together with Milton Academy. Fuller's own account puts the peas and toothpicks at **age four**, and a published Fuller chronology gives "1899 - Enters kindergarten where he builds the first octet truss using dried peas and toothpicks" (buckyfullernow.com, Sec. 2). 1899 is also the year the spectacles corrected his farsightedness, which the campa of the birth stop already carries and which the kindergarten campa leans on. The 1904 date and the Milton Academy label are consequently five years late for the episode the campa actually narrates. **Errata logged, not rewritten**, per corpus practice: correcting it would mean splitting one good campa into two, and that is a decision for the atlas's author, not for a verification pass. Its `date_confidence` is already `inferred`.

**Note on the attached quote.** The stop's `quote` field carries "Nature is trying very hard to make us succeed..." marked "(attributed, widely quoted)" and sourced as "recalling his kindergarten geometry," which it is not: the line has nothing to do with the peas. A verbatim, correctly occasioned replacement is available in the Brosterman p. 84 quotation above. Flagged for the author; not swapped here.
