**31 stops across 7 segments**, gregorian calendar. ## Judgment calls
- **The Lake Michigan vision** is marked `traditional`, not `attested`: scholars combing the 45-ton Chronofile found no contemporaneous record of a suicide attempt, and Fuller told it as a shaped, recurring lecture-legend. In this register I narrate the vision as event while flagging the date's softness — the honest fold.
- **Quotes**: I used verbatim canon where possible (the voice's words, *Spaceship Earth*, *Synergetics*, "I seem to be a verb", "Call me Trimtab"). Several stops carry Fuller aphorisms marked "(attributed)" — genuinely his but of uncertain first-utterance; a handful are placed thematically rather than at a documented occasion.
- **Chronofile stop** dated 1927 (the turning year) though the daily 15-minute logging is usually dated from 1920 — I placed it where it belongs narratively, in the vow's aftermath, and say so in the campa.
- **Kabul dome (1956)**: the USIA airlift domes to the trade fairs are attested; Kabul is the emblematic instance chosen to carry the "domes travel the earth" beat.

## The tradition's own gaps
Fuller's is the *most-documented* life ever lived, yet its founding miracle is the least documented moment in it — the Chronofile is silent exactly where the myth is loudest. The canon also smooths the failures (Stockade, the fatal car, the never-produced Wichita house) into a redemption arc; I kept the failures sharp, since the register's truth is his, and he narrated ruin as the engine of the turn.

## The five richest episodes
1. **The shore of Lake Michigan (1927)** — the suicide-resolve and the sphere of white light; the axis of the whole journey.
2. **The two deaths in Los Angeles (1983)** — "She is squeezing my hand!", the heart attack at the bedside, Anne following 36 hours later after 66 years.
3. **Black Mountain's supine dome (1948)** — the blessed failure that folds to the grass, then the first true geodesic sphere in 1949.
4. **The Dymaxion car crash at the Century of Progress (1933)** — the land-going aircraft buried under a headline.
5. **The Montreal Biosphère (1967)** — the skybreak bubble whose memory names the buckyball, writing his geometry into carbon itself.

## Connection to the atlas
His **suicide-turn** ("you belong to the universe") faces every seeker in the atlas pulled back from the edge — Gurdjieff's grandmother's injunction, the water's-edge conversions. His **domes and whole-earth** face the architects and cosmologists: he is a modern **Steiner** (the Goetheanum's sculpted domes) and a modern **Nizami** (the seven-domes cosmology), a builder of sacred spheres in a secular key. As a **Milton transcendentalist** and grandnephew of **Margaret Fuller**, he roots into the New England esoteric lineage; as world-circling lecturer and system-builder he rhymes with **Fichte** and **Froebel** (whose kindergarten gifts literally shaped his geometry). The Dymaxion map — no top, no bottom — is the atlas's own governing image: one island Earth, no privileged north.
