# Diego Gutiérrez Zaldívar: research report

*2026-09-09. Co-founder of ONG Bitcoin Argentina (2013), organizer of the first LaBitConf (Hotel Meliá, Buenos Aires, 7-8 December 2013), co-founder and CEO of RSK Labs / IOV Labs / RootstockLabs (2015-2023, 2025- ).*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named and reached. **[R]** reconstruction, or a claim resting on a source not reached or verified in this pass. Contradictions are flagged, not adjudicated. *(fetched)* means fetched directly here; everything else is carried from the dossier's citation trail. The dominant source problem is not scarcity but **circularity**: much of the record is corporate founding-team biographies plus one user-editable wiki, none resting on a document.

## 3. The scene forms, 2011-2013

**Who introduced him, and when: a live contradiction.** The dossier and later profiles say **Wences Casares, 2011**. **His own retrospective does not.** iproup, 8 June 2021 *(fetched)*: "En el 2012, vino un amigo y me dijo: esto es espectacular", and that friend "me depositó el equivalente a u$s50.000". The same piece dates the first organized meetup to **2012, not 2011**, and names only **Rodolfo Andragnes** as met there, **not Franco Amati**. Casares as introducer stays **[R]**. Firm: he was a listed speaker at LaBitConf 2013. The dossier's "fifteen days of reading" and the mining purchase are in **no source reached**.

**The economic weather, 2012 [A].** Nathaniel Popper, "Can Bitcoin Conquer Argentina?", *NYT Magazine*, 29 April 2015. **Correction to the dossier's own downgrade note: the PDF at profmex.org is readable.** It was fetched and all 15 pages parsed with pypdf here. **First organized bitcoin fair, La Maquinita, Palermo Soho, 29 October 2013 [R].** El Cronista, **not re-fetched**.

**Founding of ONG Bitcoin Argentina, 2013 [A].** `bitcoin.ar` "Nosotros" *(fetched)*: three founding partners, Andragnes, Amati, Gutiérrez Zaldívar; he was president in the early years. **No month or day given**; the journey defaults both to 01.

**First LaBitConf, Hotel Meliá, 7-8 December 2013 [A on date and venue; figures unverified].** Cointelegraph's Rio 2014 piece *(fetched)* quotes attendees calling it the best conference of the year. **Two dossier errors corrected:** its "18 December 2013 or thereabouts" is wrong, and its **~400 attendees / 30+ exhibitors** rest on nothing found, the conference's archived site giving "over 25 International Speakers" and **no counts at all**.

## 4. Espacio Bitcoin, Rootstock, ministries, 2014-2026

**The Bitcoin Embassy, 16 June 2014 [A for the building; R for the personnel].** El Cronista, Verónica Dalto *(fetched)*: 500 m² at Reconquista and Marcelo T. de Alvear, seven offices, training room for fifty, coworking for twenty-four, one meeting room, one triple suite; Andragnes quoted as **vice president** on the embassy "passport". Two corrections: attribute to **El Cronista alone**, not "La Nación/El Cronista"; and **the article names neither Gutiérrez Zaldívar nor Franco Amati**, so it cannot carry the dossier's claims that Amati cofounded and hosted the space or that Diego and the foundation impelled it. Those are **[R] with no source**.

**RSK Labs, 2015, and its seed round [A for the two-person core and the US$1M].** FinSMEs, **28 March 2016** *(fetched; no 403 this pass, correcting the dossier's note)*: co-founded in 2015 by Sergio Lerner as chief scientist and Gutiérrez Zaldívar as chief executive, with a "$1m initial seed funding round" led by Bitmain, Coinsilium and Digital Currency Group. **Kurman, Eidelman and Altman appear in no source reached** and stay [R], as do the **~US$100,000 January 2016 tranche** and the **~US$350,000 opening round**.

**Rootstock mainnet: the dossier's date is probably wrong.** Retrospectives say **January 2018**. Wayback captures of `rsk.co` walked directly here show **no mainnet language on 4 December 2017**, and from the **6 December 2017** capture onward "we are opening our MainNet Network in a staged approach ... whitelist your project", still there 9 January and 3 May 2018. **The launch was staged and whitelisted, which is why retrospectives disagree.**

**Taringa! acquisition, 27 September 2019 [A].** Criptonoticias *(fetched)*: Spanish quote verbatim, Matías Botbol staying on, **more than thirty million users** and about **1,000 active communities** (the dossier said "nearly thirty million"). The English sentence "This acquisition puts us at the forefront of distributed ledger adoption", given by the dossier as verbatim, **is not in that article**. **Inactive by 2024 [R]**, no shutdown notice re-verified.

## 5. Criticism, and what is not alleged

**Powpeg / federation trust [R].** research.rsk.dev thread, **not re-fetched**. A project representative acknowledged that trustless verification that federation operators retain no key copies cannot be absolutely guaranteed, even with secure hardware. A critique of a trust model, **not fraud**.

**RIF concentration and governance [A on the whitepaper's own concession].** The RootstockCollective whitepaper (June 2025) concedes that tying governance to a pre-existing token requires justification. **No SEC, GFSC or CNV sanction, and no market-manipulation finding, surfaced here.**

**"NGO or business network?" [R].** An informal critique pointing at overlapping personnel across the foundation, LaBitConf, Espacio Bitcoin and Magic Internet Money SRL. **Its original primary statements could not be located**, only the documented overlap. No ruling declaring the educational mission false; no audited accounts located. On lavaca 2021, see section 4.
