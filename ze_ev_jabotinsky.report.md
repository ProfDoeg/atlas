# Ze'ev (Vladimir Yevgenyevich) Jabotinsky, 1880-1940: research report

*2026-08-29. 42 stops in 9 segments.*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named inline · **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or a dossier lead no reachable source confirmed. Contradictions are flagged and **not adjudicated** unless a source in the pool resolves them; where verification overturned a dossier claim, the correction is printed with its evidence, since several change the *shape* of the life and not only its dates.

## 1. Odessa, 1880-1898

- Born Vladimir Yevgenyevich Zhabotinsky, Odessa, Kherson Governorate, 17 October 1880 **[A: English Wikipedia]**. Birth pin 46.4825 / 30.7233 is the city centre; no fetched source gives a house or street **[R as a birth pin]**.
- Father Yevno / Yevgeny (Yona) Grigoryevich died 1886, leaving the household reduced **[R]**. **Gap:** the competing tradition of an affluent childhood could not be checked against it. The campa says the household "drops out of comfort" and dates nothing prior.
- Three quotations are verbatim in **Shmuel Katz, *Rebel and Statesman*** **[A]**, and all three had their provenance corrected. "He who steals from me is poorer than I..." was framed by the dossier as quoted *by* Jabotinsky in his memoir; Katz narrates it directly, of Yona the father. "I am myself." sits in **Katz's third-person narration**, not memoir. "Exactness of dates..." is attributed by Katz to **a letter to his sister**, not to unspecified autobiographical material.
- Left Odessa at seventeen as a newspaper correspondent, via Bern, 1898 **[A]**. The Bern speech, his first public address: Katz gives its content as "In the Diaspora, the Jews were facing a St. Bartholomew's Night" **[A that Katz says this; R as to his actual words]**. It is **reported speech in past tense**, not a transcript, so it appears in no `quote` field; the campa paraphrases and names it as the biographer's report.
- Law student at the University of Rome from autumn 1898, attending irregularly about three years, never graduating, writing as Altalena **[A]**. Sapienza pin is the historic seat; the specific building **[R]**.

## 2. 1902-1906: prison, self-defense, the Congress

- Arrested for anti-establishment feuilletons, held roughly two months, largely in isolation **[A]**. April 1902 and the radical Italian publication are **[R]**.
- **The most consequential correction in the pool.** The dossier had him joining Zionism and founding the Odessa Jewish Self-Defense Organization *in response to* the Kishinev pogrom of April 1903. English Wikipedia states the reverse: "**Prior to** the Kishinev pogrom of 1903, Jabotinsky joined the Zionist movement... he established the Jewish Self-Defense Organization" **[A]**. The body **predates** the pogrom. Dated 1903-01-01 to hold the order; the campa says the sequence is usually told backward. The familiar reading of a man radicalised by atrocity is the wrong way round.
- Russian delegate to the Sixth Zionist Congress, Basel, August 1903, Herzl's last; opposed the Uganda scheme **[A]**. **Venue [R]:** the Stadtcasino is general knowledge, not confirmed by a fetch for 1903. Pin inherited byte-identical from `martin_buber.journey.json`.
- Bialik translation: **dossier dated it 1903, too early [corrected]**. Encyclopaedia Judaica has the Russian *Songs and Poems* published **1910**, a bestseller; the poem is of 1903-04. Composition dated c.1904 as an ordering device, 1910 named in the campa **[A for 1910, R for c.1904]**.
- Co-edited *Yevreiskaya Zhizn*, St. Petersburg, 1904, month unknown; chief speaker at Helsingfors, November 1906, and the Gegenwartsarbeit programme **[both A]**.

## 3. 1907-1914: marriage, Constantinople, the family

- Married Johanna (Joanna / Ania) Galperina, October 1907 **[A]**. **Gap, stated as a gap in the campa itself:** nothing in the pool documents Johanna's own views, work, or movements independently of his.
- Constantinople 1908-1914, editing *Le Jeune Turc*, Zionist press work aimed at Ottoman opinion **[A]**. Pera/Beyoglu is **[R]**, general knowledge, and the file says so.
- Son Eri (Ari) Theodore born Odessa. **Dossier gave 16 December 1910; corrected to 26 December 1910** (Wikipedia, 'Eri Jabotinsky') **[A]**. Knesset service confirmed: Herut 1949-51, leaving with Hillel Kook 20 February 1951.
- War correspondent for *Russkie Vedomosti* from 1914 **[R]**, unconfirmed. No stop of its own; folded into the Alexandria campa.

## 4. 1915-1919: Mule Corps, Legion, Jordan Valley

- Met Trumpeldor among Jews deported from Ottoman Palestine to Alexandria; co-initiated the proposal that became the Zion Mule Corps, 1915 **[A: Wikipedia, 'Zion Mule Corps']**.
- **Coordinate correction and a rename.** The dossier labelled the stop "Alexandria, Camp Jabari" at 31.1975 / 29.8925, which is exactly Wikipedia's coordinate **for the city**, not the Gabbari camp; the camp site could not be separately sourced. Renamed **"Alexandria, the refugees and Trumpeldor"**, with `date_confidence` stating the pin is the city.
- Private, 20th Battalion London Regiment, January 1917 **[A]**; East End recruiting among roughly 30,000 refugees **[A]**. Officer, 38th Battalion Royal Fusiliers; led the second company that took the Umm al-Shert bridge, 22 September 1918, after the first was repulsed **[A: Wikipedia, 'Jewish Legion']**. Coordinates 32.38 / 35.57 are **approximate** and match no fixed modern landmark.
- Advance into Gilead and garrison at As-Salt, September 1918 **[A]**; the exact day is not fixed, and the file's 25 September is an ordering date.
- Demobilization September 1919 after criticising the reduction of the Legion **[R]**; MBE December 1919 **[R]**. Both dossier leads, combined in one stop at the canonical Jerusalem pin, noting that **his location at demobilization is not established**.

## 5. 1920: Nabi Musa, the sentence, the amnesty

- Self-defense network organised openly with Rutenberg, about 600 volunteers drilling daily by late March; Storrs confiscated his pistol; he went voluntarily to jail insisting on arrest as commander; arrested 6 April 1920; fifteen years' hard labour; released under Herbert Samuel's July 1920 amnesty **[all A: Wikipedia, '1920 Nebi Musa riots' and 'Ze'ev Jabotinsky']**.
- **An apparent source conflict that verification resolved, recorded rather than left open:** the Nebi Musa article has Congreve reducing the sentence to one year after public outcry, the dossier has release under the amnesty. These are **sequential, not competing**, and the file states them in that order.
- **Coordinate correction.** The dossier's 32.9282 / 35.0822 is essentially Wikipedia's point for the modern town, about 1.2 km east of the Ottoman citadel and Mandate prison; Wikipedia carries no coordinate for "Acre Prison." The file uses **32.927 / 35.069**, an approximation of the citadel, and says so, rather than ship a town centroid as a prison.
- The Ben-Dov film of the nineteen Acre prisoners, three minutes, silent **[R]**: the dossier's account of Institute / Cinematheque holdings, with `en.jabotinsky.org` returning HTTP 403. Closes the Acre campa, with the 403 named.

## 6. 1920-1925: inside the Executive, then outside it

- **Keren Hayesod misdated by a year in the dossier (1921). Corrected:** established at the World Zionist Congress in London, **7-24 July 1920**, Weizmann, Einstein and Jabotinsky among its early leaders **[A: Wikipedia, 'Keren Hayesod']**. 1921 belongs only to his joining the Zionist Executive **[R]**.
- Slavynsky negotiation for a Jewish gendarmerie with Petliura's government-in-exile; **no unit ever formed** **[R]**. Placed at the canonical Paris pin, noting that the sources fix it to exile diplomacy and not to one city.
- Resigned the Executive 18 January 1923 **[R for the date; A for the 1923 break]**. Betar founded at Riga 1923 after his visit, Propes arranging the meeting **[A]**; the file's 1923-01-01 is an ordering date and the founding falls later in the year.
- **Two events the dossier collapsed, separated here.** 1923 is the break; the **World Union of Zionists-Revisionists was founded in 1925**, per Encyclopaedia Judaica and Wikipedia, 'Revisionist Zionism' **[A]**. Two stops.
- Iron Wall, Rassvet, 4 November 1923 **[A]**: **all five Iron Wall quotations in the pool were confirmed** against the fetched marxists.org transcription. That he was **based in Berlin** 1923-25 while editing Rassvet is unconfirmed; the paper's base moved between Berlin and Paris. The ethical sequel and its 11 November date are **[R]**; the sentence quoted from it is verified.
- "Every man is a king." **Raised from [R] to [A], and its meaning corrected.** The dossier called it a Betar/Hadar discipline slogan. It is not: Kremnitzer and Fuchs (IDI, 2013, PDF extracted with pypdf) call it "his well-known expression," a **liberal-individualist maxim** on the primacy of the individual over the state, quoting his own use in "On State and Social Problems," p. 52. Placed on the 1925 Hatzohar stop.

## 7. 1928-1935: locked out

- *Do'ar HaYom* editorship taken from Itamar Ben-Avi, 1928 **[A]**; barred by the Colonial Office after leaving on a lecture tour in 1930, never re-entered **[A]**.
- **A coordinate rejected rather than shipped.** The dossier's HaSolel Street pin (31.7807 / 35.2258) lands about 600 m east of that street, inside the Old City; HaSolel/Havatzelet runs off Jaffa Road at roughly 31.7815 / 35.2190, and no authoritative coordinate could be fetched. The stop uses the **canonical Jerusalem pin** and explains why.
- **Rosh Betar: dossier said 1929, place "unspecified." Corrected:** elected **1931 at Betar's first world conference in Danzig** **[A]**. Both year and place were wrong.
- Arlosoroff shot 16 June 1933; Ahimeir, Stavsky, Rosenblatt arrested; the defense organised from Europe; unsolved **[R throughout]**. Ben-Gurion meetings in London 1934 via Rutenberg, accords rejected by Histadrut referendum **[R]**: widely reported, not verified against a fetched source.
- Civitavecchia naval academy under Fascist sponsorship, run by Jeremiah Halpern **[R]**. Wikipedia, 'Betar', confirms the academy existed but gives no location or date. **No evidence, fetched or otherwise, places Jabotinsky at Civitavecchia.** Kept as his movement's institution, with that stated in campa and `date_confidence`.
- New Zionist Organization founded at Vienna, September 1935, 310 delegates from 34 countries, elected president **[A]**.

## 8. 1936-1939: Poland, the novel, the two forecasts

- Evacuation Plan, roughly 1.5 million over ten years **[A that he solicited Polish support; R for the figure and for Beck by name]**.
- **Begin's headship of Betar Poland: contested, left contested.** The dossier said Jabotinsky appointed him in 1939. Wikipedia, 'Menachem Begin': "In 1937 he was the active head of Betar in Czechoslovakia and became head of the largest branch, that of Poland." **No fetched source names Jabotinsky as the appointer or gives 1939.** The campa states only that Begin takes over the Polish branch, without date or appointer.
- *Piatero* (The Five), Paris 1936; first substantial critical essay Margolin 1960; Nakhimovsky 1992; Michael R. Katz's English translation, Cornell, 2005 **[A: Barry P. Scherr, *Slavic Review*, fetched]**.
- **The Warsaw volcano speech, Tisha B'Av 1938: disputed on the face of the stop.** Wikipedia reports a **2023 study by Goldstein and Huri** concluding he never made the speech, that the published text differed from what he said, and that the prophetic wording may have been fabricated **in 1958** to support the reburial campaign **[A that this is Wikipedia's report; R as to the archival argument, not read directly]**. **Two legends were conflated in the dossier and are separated here:** the Haaretz article it cited is **not retrievable (404/400)**, the separate quote it concerned ("Eliminate the Diaspora, or the Diaspora will surely eliminate you") appears nowhere in the Wikipedia article, and the claim that figures close to the Netanyahu family popularised it is **unsourced in anything fetched**. That material is out of the journey entirely.
- The March 1939 forecast, "[T]here will be no war..." **[A as to wording: Jewish Review of Books, "Confusion and Illusions, 1939", fetched]**, with two caveats carried into the file: JRB prints it as a **bracketed mid-sentence excerpt** and **names no original outlet**, so it cannot be cited as a located primary document. His whereabouts in March 1939 are not established; the stop sits at the canonical Paris pin as his European base and says so.

## 9. 1940-1964: America, the camp, the bones

- To the United States in 1940 for the NZO and the Jewish army; offered Churchill 130,000 volunteers on 12 May 1940 **[A]**. **Gap:** whether in person, by letter, or by cable, and from which city, is **not established**. Benzion Netanyahu as his secretary **[A as a statement in `benjamin_netanyahu.journey.json`]**, **not dated or placed** by this pool, and `date_confidence` says so.
- **The death date is contested and the dossier had the evidence backwards.** It claimed the JTA wire established 3 August. It does not: the obituary carries **publication date 5 August 1940** and reads "died of coronary thrombosis shortly before midnight last night at Camp Betar near Hunter, N.Y.", pointing to the night of **4 August**. Wikipedia gives **3 August**, Encyclopaedia Judaica **4 August**, and Hebcal confirms **29 Tammuz 5700 = 4 August 1940**, a Hebrew day beginning at sunset on 3 August. **The reconciliation is the Hebrew-day boundary, not the wire.** The stop is dated 1940-08-03, prints the disagreement in `date_confidence`, and lays the three datings side by side in the campa without adjudicating them. Hunter coordinates are the town, not the camp.
- **Three different pairs in the pool for one cemetery, reconciled.** Geography lens 40.7326 / -73.4321 (about 3.9 km off); afterlife lens 40.7359 / -73.4079 (about 2 km off); Wikipedia gives **New Montefiore at 40.7264 / -73.3872**, which the file uses. The cemetery is physically in Pinelawn with a Farmingdale postal address, so the stop is named for Pinelawn.
- The 1935 will (buried where he died; moved only by order of a future Jewish government), its 3 November date, and Johanna's 1949 death **[R]**: the ANU Museum page was not retrievable. Ben-Gurion's repeated refusal **[R]**, cross-referenced only against that page's title.
- Reinterment on Mount Herzl 9 July 1964 by order of Eshkol's government, alongside Johanna **[A for fact and site: Wikipedia, which supplies 31.7739 / 35.1806; R for the precise date and the 15 March cabinet approval]**. The dossier's ceremonies in New York, Paris, and Tel Aviv could not be re-confirmed and are not in the campa.
- **57 streets, parks and squares** named for him in Israel, more than for any other person **[A]**; Kyiv renamed Murom Street as Volodymyr Zhabotynsky Street in 2022 **[A]**; the April 2021 Odesa mural **[R]**, unconfirmed after two failed fetches. All three sit inside the Mount Herzl campa as commemoration, not as stops.
