# Pedro Benoit (1836-1897): research report

*2026-08-21. Engineer, surveyor, architect. Head of the Architecture Section and then of the Departamento de Ingenieros of the province of Buenos Aires; intendente of La Plata 1893-94; designer of the cathedral of Mar del Plata. Known to every Argentine schoolchild as the man who designed La Plata, an attribution the state has itself withdrawn in print.*

**Legend.** **[A]** attested, source named inline. **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or dossier-only assertion this pass could not confirm against a source it reached. Where sources conflict, both are recorded and neither preferred.

**The problem in one line.** Almost everything popularly known about Benoit rests on one signature, on one sheet, drawn six years after the event it is taken to prove, and the Ministry of Culture says so in its own file on the city (quoted at §5).

---

## 1. Origins and apprenticeship, 1836-1861

**Birth, 1836, Buenos Aires [A, museo.filo.uba.ar]**: the year only, a French father, an Argentine mother, the name Pedro Simón del Corazón de Jesús Benoit. **The day 18 February, universally repeated online, is not in that source [R]** and was corroborated nowhere. Stop dated 1836-01-01.

**Birth house, Bolívar 793 at Independencia, San Telmo [R]**: dossier genealogy only, geocoding to -34.6170, -58.3731. The address being unverified, the file uses the corpus Buenos Aires pin per the interlock lens and records the alternative in the stop.

**The father, Pierre Benoit [A for the emigration, R for the rest]**: museo.filo.uba.ar attests a French-born architect, engineer and surveyor who emigrated to the Río de la Plata in 1818 for political reasons and worked in the technical circle around the state's public works. The dossier adds Freemasonry and collaboration with Prospero Catelin **[R]**. The Louis XVII legend is at §7.

**Departamento Topográfico, c. 1850, at about fourteen [A, derecho.uba.ar]**. No personnel record located, premises unidentified, so the pin is the canonical Buenos Aires point.

**Masonic initiation, 26 October 1858, lodge Consuelo del Infortunio No. 3 [R]**: masoneria-argentina.org.ar and symbolos.com via the dossier, not reached; his Freemasonry as such is attested by derecho.uba.ar only for the 1884 La Plata lodge. **First catalogued signed plan, 20 August 1859, Domingo Iglesias's ground at the Riachuelo mouth [R]**: dossier works table, Dirección de Geodesía archive not accessed.

**Marriage to Dolores Vázquez Silva [A for 1860, derecho.uba.ar; R for day and parish]**. **CONTRADICTION, not resolved:** a cemetery-derived source in the dossier gives **1880**, and the dossier prefers 1860 on grounds this pass could not check. Twenty years is not a slip of the pen; both sit in the stop's `date_confidence`.

**Registration as public surveyor, 1861, examination waived [A, museo.filo.uba.ar]**, eleven years of service counted in its place. He holds no diploma of any kind at any point in his life.

**Children [A, 0223.com.ar]**: ten; one, Pedro Juan, followed him into practice and completed the Mar del Plata cathedral. **The Villa Santa Paula attribution is [R], corroborated nowhere, and is dropped rather than carried unmarked.**

## 2. The country towns, 1859-1874

Weakest-sourced phase of the life, almost entirely **[R]**.

- **Merlo laid out 1859 on Juan Dillon's commission, c. twenty years' residence [R]**, local histories via the dossier; 0223.com.ar confirms Merlo as one of his towns, undated. **Its first school 1860 [R]** and **church of la Merced 1863 with the master builder Antonio Ayerbe [R]**, no signed plan seen.
- **Riachuelo rectification and canalization, 1863 [A, museo.filo.uba.ar]**, the only firm item of the period.
- **Santa Rosa de Ituzaingó, 1872 [A, es.wikipedia]**, upgrading the pool's [R]. The landowner Manuel Rodríguez Fragio stays [R].
- **First visit to Mar del Plata, 1874 [A, 0223.com.ar]**: local journalism on family memory, uncorroborated by any heritage source, carried as locally reported tradition and flagged.

**Volume claims, both overstated in the pool and corrected.** museo.filo.uba.ar says *más de 1.800 planos* **[A]**; the pool's upper figure of 1,825 has no source, dropped. 0223.com.ar says *more than ten* cities, naming Merlo, Morón, Azul, Bahía Blanca, Ituzaingó and Magdalena **[A]**; "more than a dozen" is inflation, dropped. **Eight churches** is confirmed **[A]**.

## 3. Public buildings of the old capital, 1877-1880

**UBA Faculty of Law, Moreno 350 [A for the building, contested for the year].** museo.filo.uba.ar names it among the public buildings he built and **gives no year at all**; derecho.uba.ar, the only source reached that dates it, says 1878; the pool said 1877. Stop dated 1878-01-01 with both readings printed.

**Cabildo adapted as Casa de Justicia, 1879 [A].** argentina.gob.ar's Cabildo record: *la remodelación proyectada por Pedro Benoit para adaptar al Cabildo como sede de los Tribunales de Justicia*. This **upgrades the pool's [R] to [A]** and **removes the pool's claim that the page names Jorge Coquet**: it does not. That attribution has no reachable source and is not carried.

**1880 defensive works and the rank of lieutenant colonel of engineers [R]**: dossier synthesis, no order located, no battery site identified, coordinate an admitted approximation of the southern perimeter. Kept despite its thinness because the 1880 defeat is the hinge of everything after: the province loses the city and needs a new capital.

## 4. La Plata, 1881-1891: the contested authorship

**Site selection [A, argentina.gob.ar]**: the Lomas de la Ensenada de Barragán, chosen for room to grow, a future overseas port, rail and coast; Rocha and D'Amico commissioned the studies **[A]**. Benoit's individual role in locating, levelling and subdividing is corroborated there **only in general terms, through the Department of Engineers**. **Plan approved and named, 1882 [A, same]**: credited to that **Department** as a body, the diagonal cross-and-rhombus geometry to the architect **Juan Martín Burgos**, the name to a suggestion of **José Hernández**.

> **A dating inference this file makes and no source does.** visitalaplata.com.ar records a plaque installed on the Casilla Benoit on **6 June 1948** marking the **sixty-sixth anniversary of the approval of the urban plans**; 1948 minus 66 is 1882. The stop is dated 1882-06-06, `date_confidence` names the inference as this file's, and the operator may reset it to 1882-01-01.

**Foundation, 19 November 1882, Plaza Moreno [A]**; layout complete in about 25.5 months **[A, derecho.uba.ar]**.

**Casa de Gobierno, cornerstone 27 November 1882 [A]**, es.wikipedia confirming the date exactly, construction 1883-1892, Jules Dormal directing. The monument decree (norma-304920) has the Architecture Section under Benoit drawing the plans and the work directed by Benoit *secundado por el arquitecto italiano Luis BALDI*. **CONTRADICTION, not resolved:** Baldi is in the decree and **not** in the encyclopedia article, which names Dormal alone.

**Casilla Benoit, February 1884 [A, visitalaplata.com.ar]**: fifty prefabricated wood-and-zinc cabins arrived by ship from the United States, the survivor serving as his office as chief of the Engineers Corps, metres from San Ponciano. **CONTRADICTION, not resolved:** visitalaplata gives the corner as **5 and 47**, es.wikipedia's Parque Saavedra article as **5 and 46**. The same source that repeats the legend that Benoit drew the 1882 plan inside this hut also records its 1884 arrival, so the legend is impossible and is nonetheless why the object survives.

**Credited to his section, not verified against signed plans, all [R]:** San Ponciano (1882-83); the Departamento de Ingenieros building (1883, dropped from the file as the thinnest of the group); the Bosque arch (1884, demolished 1915); Dardo Rocha's *casa de los sesenta días* (1885-86); the municipal cemetery (1886-87); the coat of arms (17 April 1891, drawn by Ernesto Meyer). A separate report of **Chacarita cemetery as early as 1870 [R]** is uncorroborated, flagged, not adopted.

**Cathedral of La Plata [A in part]**: es.wikipedia confirms Benoit, the architect Ernesto Meyer, foundation stone 1884, **Emilio Coutaret** as collaborator, towers finished 1999. **The dossier's co-attribution to Pedro Pico is uncorroborated there, flagged, not adopted.**

**Municipal councillor, 1890 [A, 0223.com.ar]**, upgrading the pool's [R]; the rochista alignment stays [R]. **Intendente [A for the start, contested for the end]:** 18 September 1893 agreed; es.wikipedia ends it **15 September 1894**, the municipal archive's authority record in three broken stretches ending **25 September 1894**. Both carried.

## 5. The 1888 signature and the exhibition of 1889

**1888, Plano Actualizado signed by Benoit for exhibition abroad [A]**: *El Plano Actualizado de 1888 fue firmado por el ingeniero Pedro Benoit, a quien se ha atribuido erróneamente la autoría del mismo* (argentina.gob.ar).

**1889, Gold Medal at the Paris Exposition Universelle [A, same source].** **No source reached places Benoit himself in Paris at any time in his life.** The traveller is the drawing, not the man. The file keeps the stop at the corpus Champ de Mars pin and says so in `date_confidence`; if the rule is that only bodies travel, this is the one stop to cut.

**The revisionist thread [A, eldia.com 2017, both parts].** In the 1950s Professor **José María Rey** publishes the first serious challenge to the attribution (*the pool gave a bare 1950; the article says "in the decade of the 1950s", and the decade is what the file uses*). In 2000 the engineer **Antonio Enrique Corrado** petitions the La Plata council, and an ordinance recognises **Juan Martín Burgos** as *inspirador* of the plan. The article names three figures and no more: **Burgos** for the design, **Carlos Glade** as the surveyor who executed the official plan, **Benoit** as head of the Departamento. **The pool's fourth name, Jorge Coquet, is NOT in the fetched article and is dropped.** The historian **Alberto S. J. de Paula** (26 March 2017): *De manera que de ahí en más, sigue la atribución a Benoit, sobre la autoría del plano de La Plata, pero no es el autor.*

**Against all of that stands the family [A, eldia.com 2021]**, verbatim: *No hay duda alguna que fue quien hizo los planos, el trazado y la construcción de La Ciudad y muchas más obras que se desconocen*, Alejandro Zuccarelli Benoit. **The contradiction between descendant and state is direct, live, and deliberately not resolved.** Both are in the file, the state's sentence on the 1888 stop and de Paula's on the year-2000 stop.

## 6. Mar del Plata and death, 1893-1897

**Cathedral cornerstone, January 1893 [A, es.wikipedia]**, construction 1893-1905, Benoit initially unpaid; the day **18 January** is dossier-only **[R]**, and the Mitre 1780 address corroborates the reported San Martín and Mitre corner. **The unpaid work [A as reported testimony]**: 0223.com.ar quoting the descendant Alejandro Zuccarelli Benoit that he charged nothing and added his own money. **No payment record located**, so this is attested reporting of family testimony, not proof, and the file says so.

**Permanent relocation, late 1896 [A, 0223.com.ar]**, with a welcome organised by Peralta Ramos. **The Avenida Colón house is [R]**, descendant testimony only, its coordinate a stated approximation. **Vice-dean-elect, Facultad de Ciencias Físico-Matemáticas, Universidad Provincial de La Plata, 14 February 1897 [R]**, dossier chronology not reached. **Flagged inflation [R]:** retellings promote this into a chair and vice-deanship at the Faculty of Engineering of the UBA, which the fuller chronology contradicts and no reached source supports.

**Death, 4 April 1897 [A, doubly]**: 0223.com.ar and argentina.gob.ar agree. Cardiac insufficiency, age 61, the morning inspection of the worksite and the refusal of treatment from Dr Ángel Garay are **locally reported and ultimately descendant-sourced [A as reporting, R as fact]**; no clinical or eyewitness record survives. **Burial, Recoleta [A for the place, R for date and oration]**: the heritage page confirms the tomb, while the c. 6 April date and Dardo Rocha's funeral oration come from commemorative literature not reached.

## 7. Afterlife, 1940-2024

**Casilla Benoit**: property bought by Carlos Servente and donated to the municipality in **1940**, cabin moved to **Parque Saavedra**, bronze plaque installed **6 June 1948 [A, visitalaplata.com.ar]**.

**The three busts [A, eldia.com 2021].** The **Boveri bust**, by **Atilio Boveri**, in the plazoleta of San Ponciano, reduced from a full statue for budget reasons, was removed at night by municipal order in **1951**, taken to a depot, and has not been seen since. *The pool dated its installation "1940s-1951"; the article does not date it at all, and neither does the file.* The **Maldonado bust**, by **Máximo Maldonado**, calles 12 y 64, **1970s**, was vandalised, removed for restoration and never reinstalled. **The Raimondi bust, 2021, Palacio Municipal garden [R], is DROPPED from the journey file**: the dossier asserts it, and the fetched 2021 El Día article, which covers the lost busts and the surviving indoor ones, **never mentions Raimondi or a 2021 installation**.

**Plaza San Martín obelisk [A, eldia.com 2021]**, its plaque dedicated to *quienes participaron* in the founding rather than to Benoit alone; surviving busts are indoors, at the cathedral, the Museo Rocha and the intendente's office. *Undated and not Benoit's act, so folded into the Maldonado campa rather than given a stop; as a stop it would be -34.9160, -57.9481.*

**Decree 2317/1983, National Historic Sepulchre [A, argentina.gob.ar]. Cathedral towers completed end of 1999 [A, es.wikipedia]**, contract 16 February 1998 to Carner and Palma-Rol Ingeniería under Duhalde, 25 bells and pinnacles.

**The Louis XVII legend [A that the claim is current, R as to the claim]**: eldia.com, 18 November 2024, headlining the maker of the La Plata plan as a grandson of Louis XVI and Marie Antoinette, quoting the descendant that he sees indications whatever the French government considers closed, and the historian **Nicolás Colombo** citing DNA work at Louvain and Münster c. 2000 comparing hair attributed to Marie Antoinette with heart tissue from the Temple child, taken as confirming that child was the authentic Louis XVII. **The finding runs against the legend, the family holds it anyway, and both are in the file.**

## Apparatus relations for the operator

Pedro Benoit is **not** listed in `EXCEPTIONS.md`, so no framework may be applied backward on his file, and nothing here needed that licence. Two relations were found that are **not rooted in Benoit's own life**; neither has been silently deleted.

1. **Carlos María della Paolera, 1944, Gran Centro Cívico for La Plata. Direction caught: FORWARD** (Benoit d. 1897, the project 47 years later). His file stages it explicitly as an insertion into "Pedro Benoit's 1882 founding plan of La Plata", pinned at Plaza Moreno. Under the standing rule that is **della Paolera's stop, on his own file**, where it already sits. Here it is carried **only as a `suggested_ref` and `source`** on the Plaza Moreno and 1888-plan stops, never as a scene, and he is **not named in any campa**. No forward grant is claimed.

2. **Juan Antonio Buschiazzo, 1888-89, removal of Benoit's Cabildo tower. Direction: FORWARD, of a different kind.** He did not visit a Benoit place; he **destroyed a Benoit object**, and his own file names Benoit and dates the tower to "a decade ago". The fate of a structure Benoit built is a fact about that structure, so it sits on Benoit's own Cabildo stop, where Buschiazzo is named in one sentence. On a stricter reading that sentence is the thing to cut; the `suggested_ref` carries the same information.

**Deliberately NOT used**, because shared geography and shared professional generation are not relations: Carlos Thays (Buenos Aires June 1889, the year of the Paris medal, no contact attested), Joseph Bouvard (1907-1910), Jean-Claude Nicolas Forestier (1923-1925). Inherited plan-lineage at best, none named in any campa. **Julio Argentino Roca is named**, on the 1880 stop only, for a real adversarial relation: Benoit fortified the city for the provincial side in the revolt over Roca's election, and Roca's own file states as fact that Benoit drew La Plata's plan.

**Canonical pins declined.** The Kaaba (21.4225, 39.8262) and Temple Mount pins have no basis in the Benoit record, per the interlock lens itself. They are not in the file, and no connection was manufactured.

## Gaps, stated as gaps

- **No primary document was read in this pass.** Everything [A] rests on official heritage pages, one municipal archive authority record, encyclopedia articles and newspaper features. The Dirección de Geodesía plan archive, the municipal archive beyond its authority record, the 1897 university minutes, the 1891 arms ordinance and the parish registers were all named in the dossier and none was accessed.
- **1850-1861 is essentially empty**: eleven years, no teacher, no colleague, no project. **The Merlo decades are local tradition throughout**: twenty years of a life, not one dated document reached.
- **No source reached explains why the 1888 sheet needed a single signature at all**, which is the question the whole dispute turns on. **The 1880 military role has no located order** and no identified site. **The eight churches have no list**, parishes claim him variously, and Uribelarrea may be his son's.
- **Nothing survives of his own voice.** Not one letter, memorandum, report or recorded remark by Benoit was found. Every quotation in the journey file is either an official record about him or a descendant speaking a century later. For a man who signed more than eighteen hundred plans, that is the conspicuous absence.
