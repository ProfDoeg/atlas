# Marc Andreessen: research report

*2026-08-23. For `marc_andreessen.journey.json` (44 stops, 9 segments). Marc Lowell Andreessen, b. 1971, living. Co-author of NCSA Mosaic; co-founder of Netscape, Loudcloud/Opsware, Ning, Andreessen Horowitz; director of Meta and Coinbase.*

**Legend.** **[A]** attested, source named inline. **[R]** reconstruction or single-source report not verified this pass. Contradictions are flagged where they sit, **not adjudicated**. The subject is living, and much of what the popular record "knows" about him traces to profiles whose paywalls were all unreachable in research; anything resting only on the dossier's reading of one of those is [R].

## 1. Wisconsin, 1971-1989

- **Born Marc Lowell Andreessen, Cedar Falls, Iowa, 9 July 1971** [A, University of Illinois Distributed Museum exhibit page, fetched; Wikipedia]. No hospital named anywhere reachable.
- **Raised in New Lisbon, Juneau County, Wisconsin** [A, Wikipedia]; the athletics-centred school and the boy reading outside it come only from Tad Friend's 2015 New Yorker profile, **paywalled**, via the dossier [R]. **Began programming around age twelve, c. 1983** [A on the fact, Wikipedia; R on the year, derived from "around twelve"].
- **Barbara Blackstone**, his New Lisbon High School teacher, disappeared and was found murdered; unsolved. Andreessen later offered a **$25,000 reward** [A on both, Wikipedia; the pool had it at [R] and the verifier upgraded it].

**Flagged, not resolved.** The pool dates the disappearance to **9 July 1987**, Andreessen's own sixteenth birthday, and the reward to **2009**, both from **dossier-cited local Juneau County reporting not fetched this session**. The birthday coincidence is what a chain of secondary retellings invents and is attested nowhere reachable; the file carries the date and warns in `date_confidence` against repeating the coincidence as fact.

- **Two internships at IBM in Austin** as an undergraduate [A; Wikipedia says "interned twice", not one co-op term as the pool had it]. **The 1990 year is unsourced** and orders the segment only: the 1995 Smithsonian oral history it came from **returned HTTP 403**, with web.archive.org also unreachable. - **Andreessen and Bina, 'NCSA Mosaic: A Global Hypermedia System', *Internet Research* 4:1 (1994), pp. 7-17** [A, publisher record verified at emerald.com]. Cited on the Mosaic stop rather than given its own, since by publication he had left Illinois.

## 7. 2014-2019: Icahn, the tweet, the special committee

- **Resigned from eBay's board October 2014**, before the PayPal separation, amid **Carl Icahn's** campaign accusing him of a conflict of interest over the 2009 Skype sale [A, Wikipedia; the Reuters coverage not fetched].
- **February 2016, the Free Basics tweet**: "Anti-colonialism has been economically catastrophic for the Indian people for decades," deleted, then an apology "in full and without reservation" [A on the words and retraction, widely reported then, not re-fetched; the day is unsourced].
- **Facebook Class C reclassification, 2016-2017.** He served with Susan Desmond-Hellmann and Erskine Bowles on the special committee meant to negotiate at arm's length; discovery produced text messages showing him privately coaching Zuckerberg during that negotiation; Facebook abandoned the plan in **September 2017**, days before trial [A via the Chancery record, *In re Facebook Class C Reclassification Litigation*, per the dossier; law.justia.com returned 403]. The committee sat in California, so this is one stop at Menlo Park.
