# Antonio Gramsci (1891-1937): research report

*Compiled 2026-08-24 for `antonio_gramsci.journey.json`. Sardinia, Turin, Moscow, Vienna, Rome, Ustica, Milan, Turi, Formia. Forty-four stops in nine segments.*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named · **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or a claim not grounded in a fetched source. Contradictions are printed, not adjudicated.

Spine of the chronology: the **International Gramsci Society chronology** (`internationalgramscisociety.org/about_gramsci/chronology.html`), the most granular reachable timeline, taken as default where Wikipedia or Britannica differ by a day or two. Larger disagreements are printed below.

---

## 1. Sardinia, 1891-1911

**Birth [A].** 22 January 1891, Ales, province of Cagliari, fourth of seven children of Francesco Gramsci and Giuseppina Marcias (IGS; SEP). Two dossier errors fixed: seven *children*, not sons (it.wikipedia names Gennaro, Grazia, Emma, Mario, Teresina, Carlo besides Antonio, so four sons and three daughters); and the coordinates, wrong and mutually inconsistent across two lenses (39.9167/8.7833 and 39.7833/8.7833). Ales is **39.767, 8.817**. **[contradiction]** Marcias (IGS, English sources) vs Marcia (some Italian). File uses Marcias.

**Sorgono and the deformity [A/R, split].** The dossier's single [A] claim, a fall in 1895 near Nuoro, is two claims and one is wrong. **[A]** it.wikipedia: the deformity came from **morbo di Pott**, spinal tuberculosis, contracted at about eighteen months, i.e. mid-1892, at **Sorgono**, treated with superstition rather than medicine. Sorgono is in Nuoro province, so "near Nuoro" is defensible; 1895 is not. **[R]** The fall, from a servant's arms or down stairs, is biographical tradition from Fiori (1966). The file carries both and marks which is which in `date_confidence`.

**Sorgono duration [A, corrected].** Not "briefly": autumn 1891 to 1898, about seven years, the whole of his infancy (it.wikipedia). Pin 40.017, 9.100.

**Father's prosecution [A, date corrected].** Dossier said 1897; IGS places it under **1898**. it.wikipedia: the inspection was of the **Sorgono** office, charges *peculato, concussione e falsità in atti*, trial closed **27 October 1900**, five years eight months twenty-two days. Move to Ghilarza likewise 1898.

**Ghilarza coordinates [A, corrected].** OpenStreetMap geocodes *Casa Gramsci, 57, Corso Umberto Primo* to **40.1231, 8.8376**. The geography lens gave 40.0838/8.8339, about 4.4 km out and *below* the town centre (Ghilarza itself is 40.117, 8.833); the afterlife lens gave 40.1183/8.8339, about 600 m out. All Ghilarza stops use the corrected pin.

**The tax agency [A, upgraded from R].** casamuseogramsci.it: two years at the *Agenzia delle Imposte dirette e del Catasto*, on account of the family's poverty. **[R]** on the Fiori colour (registers heavier than the boy) and on the nine-lire wage, standard but unfetched; the file marks the wage.

**Santu Lussurgiu [A, coordinate corrected].** 1905-1908 (it.wikipedia). The dossier's longitude 8.75 is about 8.5 km east of the town; correct is **40.133, 8.650**. **[R]** on "three teachers covering five years".

**Oristano, Cagliari [A].** Diploma at Oristano c. 1908 (casamuseogramsci.it). Liceo Dettori 1908-1911, lodging with his brother Gennaro, whose *Avanti!* copies orient him (SEP; it.wikipedia). First article in *L'Unione Sarda* 1910 and graduation summer 1911 are tagged **[R]** in the dossier and the file keeps the hedge. Cagliari pin 39.2238, 9.1217 is city-centre, not the school. Gap, stated.

## 2. Turin, 1911-1917

**Scholarship, enrolment [A].** Collegio Carlo Alberto scholarship October 1911, enrolled in Letters November 1911 (IGS). **[R]** on ninth place, thirty-nine places and the seventy-lire stipend: standard, unfetched, marked in the file. **Togliatti [A]:** fellow scholarship student, friendship from 1912 (IGS).

**Bartoli [A].** Linguistics under Matteo Bartoli, who thought him exceptionally gifted (SEP). The line that Bartoli expected him to overturn the neogrammarians is **[R]**, tradition, flagged.

**The party card [contradiction, unresolved].** Dossier tagged [A] at a flat 1913. IGS hedges ("Probably... during this period", after October 1913); it.wikipedia gives 1914 on Togliatti's recollection; Gramsci never recorded it. Stop dated 1913-10-01 only to order the segment, with the disagreement printed in `date_confidence`.

**Last examination, *Avanti!* [A].** Final examination 12 April 1915, no degree, joins the Piedmont staff of *Avanti!* (IGS; Britannica). Earlier *Il Grido del Popolo* contributions from 1914 (SEP). **La Città Futura [A]:** one issue, 11 February 1917. **Secretary of the Turin PSI section, September 1917 [A]** (IGS).

**"La rivoluzione contro il Capitale" [contradiction, unresolved].** IGS and the marxists.org header: **24 December 1917**, national edition of *Avanti!*. it.wikipedia: **24 November 1917**. Both printed in `date_confidence`; the stop is dated 1917-12-24 as a labelled convenience.

## 3. L'Ordine Nuovo and the councils, 1919-1921

All from IGS unless noted, all **[A]**: founding with Angelo Tasca, Umberto Terracini and Palmiro Togliatti, first issue 1 May 1919; arrest during a political strike, July 1919; lockout 27-28 March 1920, general strike of over 200,000 on 13 April, movement beaten by 24 April.

**Fiat pin [A as declared approximation].** 45.0522, 7.6752 approximates the early-twentieth-century industrial district. Lingotto (1916-23) only partly overlaps the events and Mirafiori (1939) postdates them, so no honest landmark coordinate exists. Declared in the stop.

**Livorno.** Teatro Goldoni, XVII PSI Congress 15-21 January 1921, Via Goldoni 83, published position **43.546, 10.3134** (it.wikipedia). **Teatro San Marco [coordinate corrected]:** the PCd'I was founded there 21 January 1921; the dossier admitted its coordinate was a guess and it is about 750 m out. it.wikipedia's *Teatro San Marco* gives 43°33'18.65" N, 10°18'34.82" E = **43.5552, 10.3097**, and supplies the detail the file uses: the hall leaked so badly the delegates sat under open umbrellas indoors. **[R]** on the vote figures, widely reported, unfetched; the file rounds them and marks the rounding.

## 4. Moscow and Vienna, 1922-1923

- **Departure and arrival [A]:** leaves 26 May 1922 with Antonio Graziadei and Amadeo Bordiga, arrives via Latvia 23 June 1922 (IGS; Britannica "June 1922"). Route via Berlin is **[R]**.
- **Serebryany Bor [A, upgraded from R, coordinate corrected]:** IGS names the venue outright and says he met Julia Schucht there. The district is **55.7806, 37.4161**; the dossier's 55.8047/37.4283 sits about 2.8 km north-east.
- **Mokhovaya 16 [A for the plaque, R for the working address]:** en.wikipedia records a commemorative plaque naming Gramsci a founder of the PCd'I and marking a building where he worked during the Comintern posting. **Installation date not given: a gap.** Moscow stops use 55.7522, 37.6096; the plaque sits in `suggested_refs`.
- **Vienna [A]:** February 1923 round-up and warrant; assigned November 1923; arrives 3 December 1923 to run the foreign bureau (IGS). "una stanza fredda e inospitale della capitale austriaca" is given as his own words by it.wikipedia and is carried as a quote.
- ***l'Unità* [A, city corrected]:** founded **12 February 1924**, initially printed in **Milan**, not Rome (en.wikipedia, *l'Unità*). The dossier put it in Rome with a Rome pin. Gramsci was in Vienna and did not travel to Milan for it, so no Milan stop was staged: the founding sits inside the Vienna campa, where he actually was. That is also the correct answer under the stop-ownership rule.

## 5. The deputy, 1924-1926

**Election and return [contradiction, minor].** IGS: elected in absentia for the Veneto **6 April 1924**, returns **12 May 1924**; Wikipedia gives 24 May for the election. File uses IGS, prints the variance. **General secretary [R]:** Britannica "Summer 1924", IGS pins no day; stop dated 1924-08-15 as a declared ordering device.

**The two births [A].** Delio, Moscow, 10 August 1924; Giuliano, Moscow, 30 August 1926, never met by his father (IGS; Wikipedia). Gramsci was in Italy for both, so neither is a Moscow stop. They are carried inside the Rome stops for those months, as news reaching him.

**16 May 1925 speech [A, translation caveat].** Against the bill on secret associations; Mussolini interrupts. The marxists.org English is paraphrase-adjacent rather than certified verbatim, so the file quotes nothing from it and reports the argument in prose.

**Lyon [A].** Third Congress, clandestine, 20-26 January 1926, theses at 90.8% (IGS; Britannica). Pin 45.764, 4.8357 is city-centre; **no source reached identified the venue building.** Gap.

**October 1926 letter [A].** Political Bureau letter to the Soviet party, backing the majority against Trotsky, Zinoviev and Kamenev while warning that a coercive factional victory would cost the International its unifying authority; Togliatti in Moscow declines to transmit it as written (marxists.org).

**Arrest [contradiction, minor].** IGS **8 November 1926**; en.wikipedia's summary 9 November. File uses 8 November, prints both.

## 6. Confino, prison, trial, 1926-1928

**[A], IGS unless noted:** Regina Coeli, Via della Lungara, 41.8953, 12.4644 (en.wikipedia); five years' *confino* by administrative sentence on 18 November 1926, no trial; Ustica from 7 December 1926, where the internees' school is attested; San Vittore, Milan, from 7 February 1927, with interrogations by magistrate **Enrico Macis** from the 9th (en.wikipedia lists him among San Vittore inmates **without dates**; the dates are IGS's); trial in Rome 28 May to 4 June 1928, twenty-two defendants, sentence of 20 years, 4 months, 5 days on 4 June (Britannica gives "May 1928" for the sentence, a printed variance).

**[R]:** the Palermo Ucciardone transit, 38.1328, 13.3578 (en.wikipedia), whose date and duration are attested nowhere reached, though the transit itself is standard; carried inside the Ustica campa rather than given a stop. Also the six hundred Ustica internees and the forty-four days there, computed or standard rather than fetched.

**The prosecutor's line [R]: three corrections.** (1) The dossier's *almeno* ("at least") appears in no source reached; standard Italian is *"Per vent'anni dobbiamo impedire a questo cervello di funzionare."* (2) **No reachable source attributes it to Michele Isgrò**; Wikiquote says only "a prosecutor at Gramsci's criminal trial", en.wikipedia names nobody. Name dropped. (3) IGS renders it "We must prevent this brain from functioning for 20 years"; en.wikipedia and Joseph A. Buttigieg's translation render it "For twenty years we must stop this brain from functioning." The file uses Buttigieg verbatim and states in `date_confidence` that it is trial tradition, not a documented transcript line.

## 7. Turi, 1928-1933

**Coordinates [A, corrected, all stops affected].** The dossier repeated one bad latitude across **all five** of its Turi entries. Turi, Metropolitan City of Bari, is **40.9167, 17.0167** (it.wikipedia); 40.7647 lands about 17 km south in open country. Longitude was right, so this reads as a transposition. All seven Turi stops use the corrected pin.

**The building [A].** it.wikipedia, *Turi*: begun 1850 as a convent for the Poor Clares, never occupied by nuns, seized after 1866, used for the disabled, a house of detention by about 1895. Gramsci's cell is preserved with its original furnishings.

**[A], all IGS unless noted:** arrival 19 July 1928 as prisoner **7047**, single cell under surveillance; the "Main Topics" list opening the First Notebook on 8 February 1929 (also Britannica), though the count of sixteen topics is **[R]**; the November 1930 discussions among political prisoners on intellectuals and a constituent assembly, discontinued after negative reactions; the August 1931 health crisis, with the spitting of blood **[R]**; commutation to twelve years four months on 10 November 1932; Arcangeli's examination and report of 20 March 1933, the international campaign with Romain Rolland and Henri Barbusse among its organizers, and departure from Turi on 19 November 1933.

**1930-1931 isolation** over the Third Period line and "social fascism": **[A] as consensus, [R] as episode.** The dossier's caveat is kept: the evidence is later testimony and censored correspondence, not a contemporaneous neutral record.

**30 December 1932**, Giuseppina Marcias dies at Ghilarza and the news is withheld. **[A]** death; **[R]** concealment, standard in secondary accounts without a fetched primary. **When and from whom he learned is a genuine gap**; the campa leaves it open as a question rather than filling it.

**Notebook content [A as scope]** from SEP. The "morbid symptoms" passage is quoted from **Notebook 3 §34, c. 1930**, Hoare and Nowell-Smith trans., *Selections from the Prison Notebooks* (1971), 275-76.

## 8. Formia and Rome, 1933-1937

**[A]:** the Cusumano clinic at Formia from 7 December 1933, still a prisoner under guard (IGS; Fondazione Gramsci *tappa-06*), pin 41.2564, 13.6053 being town-centre because **the clinic's street address was not located**, a gap; conditional freedom 25 October 1934, with outings in the company of his brother Carlo, Tatiana Schucht and Piero Sraffa; the Quisisana, Via Gian Giacomo Porro 5, from 24 August 1935, run by the Sisters of the Holy Cross; the letter to Giuliano of 24 November 1936 (marxists.org); expiry of the sentence 21 April 1937; and the death sequence, notification at 12:30 p.m. on 25 April that conditional liberty had ended and surveillance was suspended, cerebral haemorrhage that evening with the left side paralysed, worse on the 26th, dead early on the 27th.

**Quisisana coordinate corrected:** OpenStreetMap gives **41.9286, 12.4889**; the dossier's 41.9214/12.4886 is about 800 m out. Both Quisisana stops use the corrected pin.

**Relationship error corrected.** The dossier called Tatiana Schucht "his sister". She was his **sister-in-law**, Giulia's sister. The file says sister-in-law throughout.

## 9. The ashes

**Funeral 28 April 1937 under police surveillance, ashes in a common grave at the Campo Verano, transferred to the Cimitero Acattolico in late 1938 [A, upgraded from R].** IGS states the sequence outright, so the intermediate Verano burial is attested, not dossier-carried. **[contradiction, printed]** en.wikipedia describes burial at the Cimitero Acattolico without mentioning Verano at all.

**Coordinates.** Verano corrected to **41.9015, 12.5203** (it.wikipedia, 41°54'05.46" N, 12°31'13.08" E); the dossier's 41.8964/12.5286 is about a kilometre out. Cimitero Acattolico **41.8764, 12.4800**, en.wikipedia's DMS exactly; the dossier's two lenses gave 41.8764/12.48 and 41.876/12.4808. **The plot within the grounds is unspecified.** Gap.

**Casa Museo, Ghilarza [A]** for address, 1898-1911 residence and museum function (casamuseogramsci.it). **The date the house became a museum is not stated on the site.** Gap. Carried as a `suggested_ref` on the childhood stops, not as a stop.

**Editions.** Togliatti and Platone, *Lettere dal carcere*, Einaudi 1947 **[A]** as an edition; its **Viareggio Prize is [R]**, IGS does not name the prize. Gerratana's critical edition, Einaudi 1975 **[A, upgraded from R]** (it.wikipedia, *Quaderni del carcere*), with the refinement that he gave full philological treatment to **29** notebooks, 17 from Turi and 12 from Formia, setting four translation notebooks aside, superseding the six-volume thematic Togliatti/Platone edition of 1948-1951. Hoare and Nowell-Smith 1971 **[R]** as the specific vector of Anglophone diffusion; IGS corroborates diffusion generally without naming the edition. All three sit in `suggested_refs` on the burial stop; none is staged as a scene.

## Quotations used and refused

Nine quotes are carried, all verbatim from fetched text, all sourced in the file's `quote_source` fields.

**Refused.** *"The old world is dying, and the new world struggles to be born: now is the time of monsters."* is a **mistranslation**, listed under *Misattributed* on English Wikiquote. Gramsci wrote *"i fenomeni morbosi più svariati"*, morbid symptoms; the monsters come from a French rendering popularized by Žižek. It is **not carried at all, not even flagged**, since quoting it inside a journey file would lend it the authority the correction withdraws. The Hoare and Nowell-Smith passage is used instead. *"Pessimism of the intellect, optimism of the will"* is genuinely his usage but the letter or notebook page was not fetched, and he credited the formula to Romain Rolland: left out rather than asserted.

## Interlocks written into campas

Five, each grounded in something Gramsci himself did. **`karl_marx` [A]:** the 1917 essay argues about Marx's *Capital* by title, and the notebooks rework Marxism as the philosophy of praxis. **`vladimir_lenin` [A]:** the same essay defends Lenin's revolution against the reading of Marx that would have forbidden it, and Gramsci then served the International Lenin founded, as PCd'I delegate 1922-23; the notebooks' censor-code "Ilyich" is **[R]**, standard but unfetched. **`georges_sorel` [R]:** Sorel's general strike as myth as a formative influence on the 1919-20 council writing is widely reported but was not confirmed against a primary source this pass, and Sorel's own file never names Gramsci; the hedge is in `date_confidence`. **`mussolini`:** **[A]** for the 16 May 1925 speech and the imprisonment until death, **[R]** for the interruptions from the government bench, which come from the dossier and marxists.org's framing rather than a stenographic record. **`leon_trotsky` [A]:** the October 1926 letter argues explicitly about him.

`karl_marx.journey.json` contains no mention of Gramsci; the link is one-directional and backward-pointing, the licensed direction.

**Canonical pins inherited byte-identical:** Rome 41.9028, 12.4964 (as in `mussolini.journey.json` and `rabindranath_tagore.journey.json`); Chamber of Deputies 41.901, 12.4788, verified by grep against `mussolini.journey.json`, which uses that exact pair for the Acerbo Law, the Aventine Secession and the 3 January 1925 speech.
