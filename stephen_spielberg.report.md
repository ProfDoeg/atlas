# Steven Spielberg: research report

*2026-08-23. QUEUE.md and the census both write "Stephen Spielberg". The attested spelling is
**Steven Allan Spielberg** and the journey's `traveler` field uses it; the slug stays
`stephen_spielberg` so the file matches the queue row. Correcting the queue line is a repository
edit and is not made here.*

Born Cincinnati, 18 December 1946. **Living at the research cutoff**, so there is no tomb and
no death date: the afterlife register here can only mean living legacy, which is institutional.
Honours, a museum gallery, a theme-park ride, a testimony archive, a restoration policy, and a
legend he tells about himself.

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or
production-history consensus not confirmed to primary-source level here. Journey file: **43
stops, 8 segments**, register `national mythology: the canon is true`, calendar `gregorian`.

## 1. Cincinnati to Saratoga, 1946 to 1965

  `george_w_bush.journey.json` pins Cincinnati at 39.1098 / -84.5379, which the interlock lens

- **The tattooed forearm**, a survivor his grandmother taught teaching him his numbers off the

  **Contradiction flagged, not resolved:** one pool item gives "c. 1949-1953", against the same

- Phoenix, Arcadia district, from 1957 **[A]**. The **toy-train wreck** staged and filmed on his
  father's 8mm camera **[A as a quotation: note 35]**; backyard war films, admission charged
  **[R: DGA Quarterly (2006)]**.
- **Escape to Nowhere**, 40 minutes, statewide prize; Eagle Scout; bar mitzvah year **[A]**.

## 4. The features, 1974 to 1984

- **The Sugarland Express**, 1974: debut feature, Best Screenplay at Cannes, first John
  Williams collaboration **[A]**. **Gap:** nothing places Spielberg himself at Cannes, so no
  Cannes stop is made and the prize sits on a stop at the studio.
- **Jaws**, 1975 **[A]**. Photography **2 May to 6 October 1974, 159 days against a planned 55**
  **[A: Wikipedia]**; the pool's "1974-1975" conflated shoot and release. Menemsha confirmed;
  coordinate adjusted to 41.3548 / -70.7650.
- **Close Encounters**, 1977 **[A]**. **Correction that matters:** one competitive Oscar, Best
  Cinematography (Zsigmond); the sound-effects-editing honour to Frank Warner was a **Special
  Achievement Award**, not a competitive category. The pool's wording is wrong.
- **1941**, 1979, over $92 M worldwide and panned, and **Raiders**, 1981, "multiple Academy
  Awards" **[A]**: the pool enumerates neither location nor awards, so the campa says the film
  swept the craft categories rather than invent a count. **Amblin Entertainment: contradiction
  flagged, not resolved.** Wikipedia dates the founding to **1975**, Kennedy and Marshall
  joining **1980**; the cited **1981** belongs to the Kennedy/Marshall company. Stop dated 1981,
  `date_confidence` calling that the arrival year. Base: Bungalow 477, Universal backlot.
- **E.T.**, 1982: four competitive Oscars **[A]**. **Gross corrected:** $797.3 M cumulative
  across all releases, roughly $619 M on the 1982-83 original run. The pool's "roughly $700
  million" splits two real figures; the journey gives both, labelled.
- **Poltergeist**, 1982 **[A: Wikipedia; LA Times via reverseshot.org]**. Hooper credited
  director; Spielberg barred by an E.T.-related contract clause, originated the story, co-wrote,
  and by crew accounts dominated the set; the DGA investigated; an arbitrator ordered MGM/UA to
  pay Hooper $15,000; Spielberg published a letter affirming Hooper's direction. **The extent of
  his direction is disputed and is not resolved here.** The **"curse"** (Dunne murdered 1982,
  O'Rourke dead 1988) is **[R]**: deaths and folklore attested, causal framing folklore.
  **Twilight Zone: The Movie**, 1983 **[A]**, gets **no stop**: no location in the pool and
  nothing on the helicopter deaths during the Landis segment.
- **Temple of Doom**, 1984: met Kate Capshaw on set; content contributed to the creation of
  PG-13 **[A]**. Relocation of the India sequences to Sri Lanka is **[R]**; India's objections
  are not documented to primary-source level anywhere reached.

## 6. Krakow and the archive, 1993 to 1998

- **Photography on Schindler's List begins 1 March 1993 in Krakow [A]**, Podgorze, 50.0475 /

  gates [R: widely reported, not primary-sourced]**.
- **"Blood money": retagged R to A.** The researcher's note called it Wikipedia's own
  paraphrase; the correction found it inside quotation marks, attributed to Spielberg, footnoted
  to **McBride (1997), p. 416**. It is his **forgone directing salary** he called blood money,
  not the profits, and it is a two-word fragment with no traced original sentence, so it appears

- **First Best Director Oscar, 66th ceremony, 21 March 1994 [A: Jewish Virtual Library]**, the

## 7. Adaptations, honours, withdrawals, 2001 to 2017

- **Honorary KBE, 2001 [A]**, folded into the **Walk of Fame star, 10 January 2002**, 6801

  enhancements"; 2012 and 2017 discs restore only the 1982 cut **[R]**.
- **Minority Report**, 2002; **War of the Worlds** and **Munich**, both 2005 **[A]**. See

- **DreamWorks sold. Conflation corrected:** **announced 11 December 2005**, **completed
  1 February 2006**, about $1.6 bn including roughly $400 M assumed debt. The pool's "2005-12,
  completed around this time" merges the two.
- **Kennedy Center Honor, 2006**, with Lloyd Webber, Mehta, Parton, Robinson **[A]**; the
  Honors fall in early December, so the stop's month is conventional and says so.
- **Beijing, February 2008:** withdrew as artistic adviser to the Olympic opening ceremonies
  over China's support for the Sudanese government and Darfur **[A]**. **Coordinate corrected**
  from 39.9928 / 116.3975 to 39.9931 / 116.3906.
- **Lincoln. Three corrections.** (a) Filming ran **17 October to 19 December 2011**, not 2012,
  the release year. (b) Locations were **Richmond, Fredericksburg and Petersburg**, the Virginia
  State Capitol doubling for the US Capitol; the pool's place string also carried Hasbrouck
  Heights and Loyola University campus areas "standing in for Illinois", for which **no support
  exists**, deleted. (c) The Connecticut roll-call objection came from **Rep. Joe Courtney**;
  the pool's claim of a corrected "educational cut" is **unsupported and dropped**.
- **Presidential Medal of Freedom. Contradiction flagged, not resolved:** the White House press
  release is dated **16 November 2015**, a pool item gives the award as **24 November 2015**,
  and the archived page could not be re-fetched. The stop uses 24 November and names both.
- **Harvard, 365th Commencement, 26 May 2016**, honorary Doctor of Arts; spoke on history,

  **California Hall of Fame:** the pool called it "undated induction", the correction dates it

  **Caveat retained in the stop, not resolved:** he holds a Tony statuette for *A Strange Loop*
  but is **not credited in Tony records as a winning producer**, so "competitive EGOT" is
