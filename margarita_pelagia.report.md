**Judgment calls.** (1) Placed Thecla's persecution in *Syrian* Antioch rather than Pisidian Antioch — the Acts of Paul and Thecla text is itself ambiguous/contested between the two, and Syrian Antioch lets the proto-timeline (T0) land in the same city as the dragon-life, the leap, the pearl, and the runaway bride, so the whole book pivots on one geography with five faces. Noted as a deliberate fold, not an error. (2) The Golden Legend's twin-named "Margaret-Pelagia" runaway-bride episode (T5) gives no city; I set it in Antioch too, for the same reason. (3) Assigned approximate but traditional dates throughout (the era is legendary, not chronicled) — Thecla c. 47-90 AD, Margaret/Marina 291-306, Pelagia the Virgin c. 311, Pelagia the Penitent c. 412-422, the runaway bride/Marina the Monk c. 350-445 — spaced to keep each timeline internally coherent without claiming false precision; all marked "traditional." (4) The Anna-Kapelle stop is dated ~1780 (the Nothelferaltar's documented construction era) since no earlier date for the verse itself survives — a deliberate anachronism bridging the ancient timelines to a still-standing plaque.

**Time-folds / gaps.** T1 (Margaret) and T2 (Marina) share three pins and dates exactly by design — the "same life, eastern timeline" the brief calls for. The CODA folds five more centuries into six stops spanning 1424-1780, jumping from Domrémy (1424) to Lyon (1429) to Beaurevoir (1430) to Rouen (1431) to a German chapel three and a half centuries later — the leap in time is the point: Margarita's voice outlives every one of her own deaths. Saint Barbara and Catherine Alexandria do not yet exist as sibling files in this run, so the Anna-Kapelle pin (49.8358, 9.1310, Obernburg am Main) is offered as the byte-identical target for those two files' writers to match, not yet cross-verified against them.

**Five richest episodes.** (1) The dragon swallowing Margaret whole and bursting from the cross inside her — the coronal event that follows, sky and thunder and a crown of plasma over the whole square. (2) Marina's hammer-and-small-demon mirror of the same cell, same night, Byzantine glass over Latin glass. (3) Pelagia the Penitent's baptism and flight in Nonnus's own garment to the Mount of Olives, ending in the discovery of the body of "brother Pelagius" a decade later. (4) Marina the Monk's false-paternity confession and ten years raising another woman's child in silence outside the monastery gate. (5) The CODA's Beaurevoir leap, read explicitly as Pelagia the Virgin's rooftop leap relived eleven centuries later — Article 65 naming the very rhyme this whole file is built on.

**Connections to the atlas.** Byte-identical pins with joan_of_arc.journey.json at Domrémy (the father's garden, first voice) and Beaurevoir (the leap). The Mount of Olives cell is placed adjacent to (not overlapping) the Gethsemane/Ascension pins in jesus.journey.json.
