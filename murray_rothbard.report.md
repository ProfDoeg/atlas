# Murray Newton Rothbard (1926-1995): research report

*Compiled 2026-08-22 for `murray_rothbard.journey.json`.*

Legend: **[A]** = attested, source named · **[R]** = reconstruction or uncorroborated single-source claim. Verify-stage corrections are marked **CORRECTED** at the point of the error, with the outlier named. Contradictions are flagged, not adjudicated.

Two cautions. **(1) The New York coordinate problem:** most of his adult life happened in Manhattan and almost none of the addresses survive, so the file repeats a Manhattan-generic pin (40.7580, -73.9855) across a run of stops and says so in each `date_confidence`. A real gap, not a shorthand. **(2) The Mises Institute profile is the most-used source and the least reliable on dates:** four datings in it are wrong (II, IV, VI, VII). Where it disagrees with an imprint, the imprint wins.

## I. The Bronx and Columbia, 1926-1945

- **Birth, 2 March 1926, New York City**, to David Rothbard, a chemist out of Poland, and Rae Rothbard, out of Russia. **[A: Wikipedia; Mises Institute profile]**
- **Birthplace, unresolved.** Gather put the apartment on the Upper West Side at 40.787, -73.9754. **CORRECTED:** no source reached places the Rothbards there; that coordinate is an invention, and it had also been copied onto the Circle Bastiat stop, where it is likewise removed. Wikipedia's only locational evidence is "the public school system he had attended in the Bronx," and standard accounts have him raised there. The file uses a Bronx-generic point (40.8448, -73.8648), **[R]**, address unrecovered.
- **Arthur Burns.** Wikipedia supports that Burns was a family friend and neighbour who blocked, then approved, the dissertation. It does **not** support gather's claim that he was a tenant of the family's building; dropped.
- **Public school in the Bronx. [R]** Only support is the phrase above. No school named, no enrolment dates. Gather's "c. 1932-1938" is reconstruction.
- **Birch Wathen School, Manhattan. [A]** for attendance and preference. **CORRECTED twice:** it was not "Birch Wathen Lenox" in his lifetime (that name dates from the 1991 merger); and gather's 40.7726, -73.9629 reconstructs an East 71st Street site the school occupied only 1962-1989, sitting 400 m off it anyway. The pre-1962 address is in no reachable source; the stop is **[R]** on location.
- **BA in mathematics, Columbia, 1945. [A]** Coordinate 40.8075, -73.9626.

## II. FEE, the Mises seminar, the winter of 1949

- **Foundation for Economic Education, Irvington-on-Hudson.** Gather dated this 1946. **CORRECTED:** FEE was founded 7 March 1946 and moved to Irvington later that year, and the FEE article does not state Rothbard worked there at all. It states that he credited **FEE the institution**, not Baldy Harper personally, with a "crucial open center," naming Harper separately as an influence. Redated to the late 1940s, **[R]**. Coordinate corrected to 41.0376, -73.8686 (30 South Broadway).
- **Mises's private seminar, NYU School of Commerce, 90 Trinity Place**, from 1949. **[A on attendance; R on the building]**, identified from general knowledge and not re-verified. Coordinate 40.7079, -74.0138.
- **Turn to individualist anarchism, winter 1949. [R: Mises Institute profile only]** No contemporaneous document cited.
- **Error of decade, corrected.** Gather wrote that "as a teenager and young man in the 1940s" he read *Human Action* and joined Rand's circle. **CORRECTED:** *Human Action* appeared September 1949, when he was 23; he joined Rand's circle in **1954**. Only the Old Right formation and the Young Republican Club belong to the 1940s.
- **William Volker Fund, Kansas City, 1951**, senior analyst under F. A. "Baldy" Harper's talent-spotting program. **[A]** Coordinate 39.0997, -94.5786.
- **Marriage, 1953**, to JoAnn Beatrice Schumacher, New York City; she becomes his editor for life. **[A]** Venue and month not recovered: **gap**.

## III. Rand, the Circle Bastiat, the doctorate, 1954-1962

- **Ayn Rand's circle, 1954, first break**, departing on the argument that her system is derivative of Aristotle, Aquinas, and Spencer. **[A]** Coordinate 40.7484, -73.9807 approximates her Murray Hill apartment from general knowledge; **not confirmed**.
- **Second stay, 1958, final break** after *Atlas Shrugged*. **[R]**
- **Circle Bastiat**, "by the mid-1950s." **[A]** **CORRECTED:** gather gave a hard 1954, more precise than the source. Meeting address not recovered: **gap**.
- **PhD, Columbia, 1956**, under Joseph Dorfman, on the Panic of 1819, which becomes the standard work. **[A]** Burns blocking then approving is **[A]**. The eleven-year BA-to-PhD gap is arithmetic from two attested dates.
- **Mont Pelerin Society** membership, 1950s. **[A]** No stop: no location, no date.
- **Volker Fund moves to Burlingame, then collapses, 1962. [A]** Coordinate 37.5779, -122.3484.

## VI. Las Vegas and the paleo turn, 1986-1994

- **Leaves Brooklyn Polytechnic, 1986 [A]**

- ***Review of Austrian Economics*, founded 1987. [A]** **CORRECTED:** "later renamed the *Quarterly Journal of Austrian Economics*" is false and appears in two separate gather items. No founding venue is recorded; the stop sits at the Auburn pin as an ordering device and says so.
- **"Just War," Costs of War conference, Atlanta, May 1994 [A]**. See Quotes.

## VII. Death, burial, posthumous

**Death, 7 January 1995**, heart attack, St. Luke's-Roosevelt Hospital Center, Manhattan, age 68. **[A: two independent fetches]** **Contradiction 1, not resolved:** geography gave 40.7714, -73.9878, the **Roosevelt** campus (1000 Tenth Ave); afterlife gave 40.8047, -73.9636, the **St. Luke's** campus (1111 Amsterdam Ave), verified at 40.8056, -73.9617. The two merged in 1978 into one center with two campuses and **no source reached names the building**. The file uses 40.8056, -73.9617 and states the campus undetermined.

**Burial, Oakwood Cemetery, Unionville, Orange County, Virginia, 1995. [A: Wikipedia infobox citing Find a Grave]** Find a Grave returned HTTP 403. **Contradiction 2:** geography gave 38.1897, -77.9250 and afterlife 38.2136, -78.0316 for the same grave, about 10 km apart, and **both are wrong**. Unionville is at **38.2597, -77.9683**, which the file uses; the plot is not located.

**Posthumous works.** ***An Austrian Perspective on the History of Economic Thought***, vols. I *Economic Thought Before Adam Smith* and II *Classical Economics*, Edward Elgar, 1995 **[A]**; **CORRECTED:** gather called only the second posthumous, but he died 7 January 1995 and **both are**. ***The Logic of Action***, 2 vols., 1997 **[R: profile only]**. ***A History of Money and Banking in the United States***, **2002**, Mises Institute **[A]**; **CORRECTED:** the profile's 1999 is wrong by three years. ***Conceived in Liberty*, vol. V**, 2019 **[A]**.

**Tributes, corrected.** Gather had Rockwell calling him "the founder of right-wing anarchism" in a Mises Institute memorial statement, and Hoppe calling him "one of the most important philosophers in history." **CORRECTED, half right:** Rockwell's line is confirmed but was given **to the New York Times**. Hoppe's attested words differ: he lamented that, like Mises, Rothbard died without the Nobel, and called him an **"intellectual giant."** The other phrasing is unsupported and not used. Buckley's critical *National Review* obituary, 1995, is **[A]**.

**Spanish reception.** *Historia del Pensamiento Económico* (2 vols., 2000), *La Ética de la Libertad* (2nd ed., 2009), *Hacia una Nueva Libertad*. **[A: Spanish Wikipedia]** A gather claim that in December 2024 Spanish networks linked Rothbard to Milei through Jesús Huerta de Soto, citing *Panorama Directo*, 27 December 2024, is **CORRECTED: no such passage exists**, and is dropped.

**Flagged, not used.** Spanish Wikipedia's **category metadata** lists Rothbard under "Judíos antisionistas" and "Activistas por Palestina de Estados Unidos." **[R]** Not corroborated by any biographical prose fetched in either session. A contradiction in the sourcing, not adjudicated, not written into the journey.

## Quotes: verified and unverified

Verified verbatim against extracted PDF text of *Anatomy of the State* (Mises Institute, 2009 [1974]): **p. 11**, the definition of the State (**CORRECTED:** gather cited "pp. 11-12"; the sentence sits entirely on p. 11); **p. 16**, "Since production must always precede predation..."; **p. 17**, the Ruritania parable; **p. 25**, "The greatest danger to the State is independent intellectual criticism..." (also the edition's epigraph); **p. 28**, "Thus, ideological support being vital to the State..." (**CORRECTED:** gather cited "p. 28-29"; it ends on p. 28). The Albert Jay Nock passage at **p. 16 n. 5** is **not Rothbard's own words** but a quotation he endorses, so it is carried as a `suggested_ref` on the Ruritania stop rather than as a `quote`.

Endorsements from the Mises Institute page for *Man, Economy, and State with Power and Market*: Mises's "epochal contribution" **[A]**, Hoppe's "modern classic... one of the two towering achievements" **[A]**. The Hoppe line is about the book, not the Institute; the stop carrying it says so.

**"Rights may be universal, but their enforcement must be local." [A on the words, CORRECTED on the source.]** Wikiquote attributes it to "Two Just Wars: 1776 and 1861," which is the title mises.org gives the **audio**. The printed essay is **"Just War,"** Costs of War conference, Atlanta, May 1994, in John V. Denson, ed., *The Costs of War* (1997); also circulated as "America's Two Just Wars: 1775 and 1861" (1775, not 1776). Verified from the LewRockwell.com text via a 2013 Wayback snapshot. Rothbard gives it inside quotation marks as a slogan, paired with the Irish rebels' motto. The file cites the printed essay.

**Two quotes carried [R], primary texts not opened:** "I define anarchist society as one where there is no legal possibility for coercive aggression..." attributed to "Society Without a State" (1975) and placed on the 1973 *For a New Liberty* stop, which that stop's `date_confidence` declares; and "It is no crime to be ignorant of economics..." from "The Death Wish of the Anarcho-Communists" (1970).

## Honest gaps

- No street address survives for the birth apartment, the Bronx school, Birch Wathen before 1962, the Circle Bastiat's rooms, the Libertarian Forum office, the Center for Libertarian Studies, Cato's first San Francisco office, IHS within Menlo Park, the John Randolph Club's founding venue, or the wedding.
- Which hospital campus he died in is unknown; which cemetery plot holds him is unknown, with three coordinates in circulation.
- The Volker Fund's collapse is attested; the quarrel behind it is not. No memorial is attested anywhere. Whether he had children is not addressed by any source reached, and the file says nothing about it.
- The June 1974 South Royalton conference could not be verified in any reachable source, despite being universally reported in secondary Austrian-school histories.
