# René Noël Théophile Girard (1923-2015): research report
*2026-08-31. Written for `rene_girard.journey.json` (9 segments, 44 stops). Avignon, Paris, Bloomington, Durham, Bryn Mawr, Baltimore, Buffalo, Baltimore again, Stanford; posthumously to Paris and Avignon.*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction or dossier claim not independently confirmed in this pass. Contradictions are recorded and left standing; nothing is silently adjudicated.

- **Born 25 December 1923, Avignon.** [A: en.wikipedia and fr.wikipedia, "René Girard", agreeing on date and city.]
- **Full name René Noël Théophile Girard.** [A: both editions.] The pool's chronology lens dropped "Théophile"; the file restores it.
- **Birthplace a house on the Arrousaire road, since demolished.** [R: local Avignon family-history material via the dossier; street and house not independently confirmed.] **Coordinate corrected:** the pool's 43.9335, 4.8225 falls ~600 m outside the bounding box of avenue de l'Arrousaire (OSM: roughly 43.9427/4.8100 to 43.9343/4.8165). File uses **43.9395, 4.8126**.
- **Father Joseph Girard, curator of the Musée Calvet and administrator of the Palais des Papes.** [A: academie-francaise.fr, "Les Immortels: René Girard".] The c. 1906-1949 span is [R: dossier only]. Pins: Calvet 43.9481, 4.8047; Palais 43.9503, 4.8075 (the afterlife lens gave 43.9506, 4.8071, a 40 m difference; one value used).

**Gap.** Nothing reachable documents his schooling, his mother, or the family under the occupation before 1943. The two childhood stops are dated 1928 and 1932 only to order the segment.

## 3. Indiana, Duke, Bryn Mawr, 1947-1957

- **Left France 1947 on a French government teaching fellowship to Indiana University, Bloomington, meant to last a year.** [A: fr.wikipedia, corroborated en.]
- **PhD in history, Indiana, 1950, "American Opinion of France, 1940-1943".** [A: both editions.]
  - **CONTRADICTION, unresolved.** The Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy dates the doctorate to **1947**. 1950 is used because two language editions corroborate it; the IEP figure is named inside the campa rather than smoothed away.
- **Married Martha McCullough, American librarian, 18 June 1952.** [A: fr.wikipedia, "bibliothécaire américaine".] The dossier's **"Methodist ceremony"** could not be found anywhere in this pass and is set aside. **The place of the ceremony is a genuine gap:** the pool says only "United States", so the stop is pinned at Bloomington as an ordering device, not a location.
- **Children Martin 1955, Daniel 1957, Mary 1960.** [A: en.wikipedia.]
- **Indiana departure. CONTRADICTION, unresolved.** en: 1947-1953. fr: 1947-1952, with Duke from 1952. The file presents **1947 to 1952/53**; the pool's flat 1953 is undone. Whether it was a non-renewal or a firing is [R: biographical retellings via the dossier; no personnel record located] and is left open.
- **Duke. CONTRADICTION, unresolved.** fr: Duke 1952-1953. en: Duke and Bryn Mawr together 1953-1957, unsplit. **The pool's "1953-1955" is supported by neither source and is rejected as an invented split.** Pin 36.0016, -78.9382 verified sound. Cynthia Haven's linkage of the segregated South to his later interest in crowds is [R: dossier citing *Evolution of Desire*, 2018]; Girard is not on record making it.

## 4. Baltimore, 1957-1968

- **Johns Hopkins from 1957, full professor 1961, until 1968.** [A: fr.wikipedia, corroborated en.] Pin **39.3299, -76.6205**, inherited byte-identically from the Barthes, Derrida and Lacan files.
- **Conversion, intellectual then religious, while completing the first book; a return to Catholic practice; en.wikipedia frames it through his reading of Dostoevsky.** [A: en.wikipedia.] No source dates it; the stop is dated 1959 as an ordering device.
- **Guggenheim Fellowships. CONTRADICTION, unresolved.** en.wikipedia: 1959 and 1966. Dossier: 1960 and 1967. One year out on both; the campa says only "the same year, or the next; the sources differ."
- **Mensonge romantique et vérité romanesque, Grasset, 1961.** [A: both editions.] The five-novelist roster (Cervantes, Stendhal, Flaubert, Proust, Dostoevsky) is the standard account of the book and was **not** separately verified [R]. Only the Don Quixote line is quote-attested.
- **MLA William Riley Parker Prize for "Camus's Stranger Retried", 1965.** [R: dossier's citation of MLA records; not confirmed on a fetched page.] The campa's precis of the essay is the writer's compression.
- **The colloquium, Johns Hopkins, 18-21 October 1966.** [A: en.wikipedia, "Structure, Sign, and Play": organized by Girard and Richard A. Macksey for the new Humanities Center on Ford Foundation money, Derrida reading on 21 October.] The pool's "404, rests on fr.wikipedia only" hedge is obsolete: the dedicated page exists under the lecture's title, not the conference's.
  - **Two contradictions, both recorded.** (i) en.wikipedia names Eugenio Donato a **proceedings co-editor**; fr.wikipedia a **third organizer**. (ii) The title appears as "The Language of Criticism" (en, singular) and "The Languages of Criticism" (fr and the 1970 Macksey-Donato volume, plural). Participants beyond Derrida (Barthes, Lacan, Hyppolite, Goldmann, Todorov) come from the dossier and `roland_barthes.journey.json`.
- **The Lacan-Derrida introduction. CONTRADICTION, kept open as a stop of its own.** One account, in both the Lacan and Derrida files and traced in both to booksandtales.com, has Girard making the introduction, the two men talking about death and posthumous reception, and Lacan afterwards telling Girard that the difference between himself and Derrida is that Derrida "has nothing to do with people who suffer." Roudinesco's account gives Girard no role. Neither is preferred; the dossier does not mention it and this pass could not confirm it. *Signal for the operator: two journey files rest a scene on a single crawl of booksandtales.com.*

## 7. Peter Thiel, the interlock the queue itself flagged

`QUEUE.md` row 0 names it outright: Girard "taught at Stanford, where Peter Thiel already done studied under him, a direct interlock the author flagged." It is the anchor connection, and where the pool held the most error.

- **Attested:** Thiel took a Stanford philosophy BA in 1989 and a JD in 1992 [A: en.wikipedia, "Peter Thiel"]. Its whole statement on the meeting is **"While at Stanford, Thiel met René Girard, whose mimetic theory influenced him."** No timing beyond "at Stanford."
- **CORRECTION: the pool asserted a contradiction that does not exist in the source.** Both the chronology and geography lenses claimed en.wikipedia places the meeting at Stanford *Law School*, conflicting with profiles calling Thiel an undergraduate student of Girard's. The article says nothing of the kind, and the file flags no such tension.
- **The account the file uses:** Salmagundi's "From Philosophy to Power" (fetched) says the contact was a **biweekly, roughly ten-person reading group Girard ran out of a trailer on campus** in the early-to-mid 1990s, not a registered course, and that Girard blurbed Thiel and David Sacks's *The Diversity Myth* (1995). [A as to the source's account; R as to date.] That timing sits awkwardly after a 1989 BA, and `peter_thiel.journey.json` stages its own Stanford stop at 1985-09-01; Girard's stop is dated 1993 as an ordering device on his side rather than competing. **No registrar record of Thiel in any Girard course has ever been produced**, stated as a gap in the campa.
- **Imitatio, 2008, from discussions among Girard, Thiel and Robert Hamerton-Kelly, run as a program of the Thiel Foundation.** [A: imitatio.org.] The dossier's negative finding is retained: nothing amounts to a documented equity interest for Girard in any Thiel company.
- **Not staged in Girard's file.** "Politics and Apocalypse" (2004) and "The Straussian Moment" [R: dossier], the Facebook investment, *Zero to One*, and the Meta board are real one-directional relations (Thiel reading Girard) already staged on Thiel's own file. None is a place Girard went.

## 8. Death and afterlife

- **Died 4 November 2015 at home in Stanford, California, in his ninety-second year, after a long illness whose diagnosis is not publicly specified.** [A: academie-francaise.fr, "Décès de M. René Girard"; both editions.] Exact address not located; 37.4241, -122.1661 approximates faculty housing.
- **Jean-Christophe Rufin's tribute in Académie session, 12 November 2015.** [A: academie-francaise.fr; the description of the session is the writer's.]
- **Paris homage, Saint-Germain-des-Prés, 15 February 2016:** Michel Serres's eulogy around Haydn's *Seven Last Words of Christ*, played movement by movement by a quartet made up entirely of Girard's nieces and nephews, Serres asking for no applause between movements "given the ecstatic character of the work." [A: academie-francaise.fr.] The best-attested item in the pool.
- **Archive to the BnF: corrected and narrowed.** bnf.fr's "La bibliothèque de René Girard" says only that the archives "rejoint les collections de la BnF **en 2018**". No day, and nothing about a 2011 request from Girard for classification, restoration and digitization; the pool's "17 May 2018" and its 2011 origin story are unsupported by that page and are not stated in the file. Benoît Chantre's *René Girard: Biographie* (Grasset, 2023, ~1,200 pages, from this archive) [A: rene-girard.fr] is folded into the same stop.
- **Personal library to the Bibliothèque municipale d'Avignon (Ceccano).** [A: bnf.fr, same page], upgraded from the pool's [R]. **Coordinate corrected:** OSM places the Médiathèque Ceccano (2bis rue du Laboureur) at **43.9462, 4.8064**; the pool's 43.9478, 4.8085 is ~250 m northeast, off the building. **The transfer date is a real gap:** no source gives one. The 18 October 2025 Maison Jean Vilar presentation by Karine Klein [A: bnf.fr] is folded in as a sentence rather than staged: Klein travelled, Girard did not, and she has no journey file.
- **Not staged, and why.** The Stanford memorial service of 19 January 2016 [R: dossier, not reconfirmed] adds nothing the death stop and the Paris homage do not carry. The documentary *René Girard, la vérité mimétique* (dir. Yves Bernanos, 52 min., 2021, KTO / Crescendo Media Films) [A: crescendomediafilms.fr] is a posthumous film, the filmmaker's work and not Girard's travel.
- **Ashes repatriated to the family vault in Avignon, 16 December 2023.** [A: fr.wikipedia, footnote 17.]
  - **Centenary framing corrected.** fr.wikipedia reads "Le 16 décembre 2023, les cendres de René Girard sont rapatriées dans le caveau familial d'Avignon. René Girard aurait eu 100 ans." He would have turned 100 *that year*, not that day; his birthday is 25 December, so the repatriation fell **nine days before** the centenary.
  - **The vault's location is a real gap.** The afterlife lens's coordinate (43.9493, 4.8055) sits inside the walled city near the Palais des Papes, where there is no cemetery, and contradicts the geography lens's Saint-Véran location, which itself rests on a single social-media photograph with no cemetery register behind it [R]. The stop uses 43.9367, 4.8264 and states that the pin is approximate to Avignon, not the grave.
  - **The eight-year gap between death and burial of the ashes is unexplained by any source**, and is stated as unexplained.

## Apparatus relations for the operator

**René Girard is not listed in `EXCEPTIONS.md`.** Nothing has been silently deleted; two candidates are named here for a ruling.

1. **FORWARD (not licensable without the author's say-so).** The afterlife lens carries a claimed post-2015 revival of Girard among Thiel- and Vance-adjacent "Catholic entrepreneurs" and technologists, fascinated by the Antichrist and apocalypse material in *Battling to the End*, reported by Financial Times, Wired and Le Monde. None of those three could be reached and the search quota was exhausted; it stands on the dossier's and fr.wikipedia's characterization alone [R]. Beyond the sourcing problem it is a **forward** reach, applying Girard's apparatus after his death, and he holds no forward grant, so it is **not written into the journey as an interpretive move.** In the file there is only the biographically-rooted residue: the 2008 Imitatio founding, where Girard is named a founder, and one flat clause in the emeritus campa noting that Silicon Valley readers took the theory as a description of markets, which is reception within his lifetime. **To stage the 2020s revival, Girard needs a forward grant in `EXCEPTIONS.md` and the journalism needs to be reachable.**
2. **NO SECOND TRAVELER YET (a pointer, not a deletion).** The dossier's "Thiel-Vance axis" has no counterpart file: a grep across `working/journeys/*.journey.json` finds no J. D. Vance journey (only `volodymyr_zelenskyy.journey.json` matches the name, unrelated). The dossier's material (Thiel introduced Vance to Girard's ideas; Vance was never taught by Girard) supplies the claim if a Vance file is ever researched, but it cannot be sourced from the corpus today.

**No backward apparatus relation was found.** Girard's writing on Machaut, Greek tragedy, the Gospels, Shakespeare and Clausewitz is not an exception: he wrote about all of it himself, in print, so those stops are ordinary one-directional engagements rooted in his own life and pinned where he was when he wrote.
