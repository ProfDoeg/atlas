# Mark Lombardi (1951-2000): research report

*Compiled 2026-08-18 for `mark_lombardi.journey.json`. Slug: `mark_lombardi`.*

American artist. Trained as an art historian, worked fifteen years as a curator, gallerist and
public-library reference librarian in Houston, then from 1994 made the graphite "narrative
structures": large diagrams of banking, political-finance and intelligence-linked networks,
drawn entirely from published sources. Died in Brooklyn, March 2000, at forty-eight.

**Legend.** **[A]** = attested, source named inline · **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition, or a
claim whose source could not be produced. Contradictions are flagged, not adjudicated. A widely
circulated claim that failed verification is recorded as failed, not quietly dropped.

## 1. Origins and Syracuse, 1951-1974

- **Born 1951 near Syracuse, New York. [A / R split]** The *year* and the city of Syracuse are
  institutional (MoMA, Pierogi). The *day*, 23 March 1951, and the village of **Manlius** are
  obituary-derived (Wikipedia, after Smith, *NYT*, 25 March 2000). The two levels of specificity
  are not merged in the file; both are named in `date_confidence`.
- **Chief researcher, "Teapot Dome to Watergate," 1973. [A, Wikipedia]** Year and role attested.
  **Gap:** no source gives the exhibition a venue or a city; the common "at Syracuse" is
  unsourced, and the stop pins Syracuse University only because he was enrolled there.
- **B.A. in art history, Syracuse University, 1974. [A, Pierogi CV; Wikipedia]** **Gap:** no
  matriculation year is attested; the "1970-1974" range in circulation invents its start year.

## 2. Houston: the day jobs, 1975-1993

- **Assistant curator, Contemporary Arts Museum Houston, under James Harithas, 1975. [A,
  Wikipedia]** **Geographic correction:** 5216 Montrose Boulevard geocodes to **29.7264,
  -95.3915**; the circulating 29.7392, -95.391 lands 1.4 km north in residential Montrose.
- **General reference librarian, Fine Arts department, Houston Public Library. [A, Wikipedia]**
  **Wording correction:** a general reference librarian *assigned to* Fine Arts, not a "Fine
  Arts reference librarian." He built a regional-artist archive there. **Date gap:** no source
  gives a start year; the usual 1976 is inference from the end of the museum tenure.
  **Geographic correction:** the Julia Ideson Building is **550** McKinney Street, not 500, at
  **29.7589, -95.3690**; a circulating 29.7594, -95.3632 is 560 m too far east. That the day job
  rehearsed the card-index method is an art historian's reading, not a statement of his own [R].
- **Square One gallery [R, Wikipedia only] and a "Lombardi Gallery" [R, biographical tradition
  only].** Pierogi's CV mentions neither, and the claim that they rest on "MoMA/Pierogi archival
  biographies" is false. **Contradiction:** one account implies a 1976 opening for Square One,
  another "late 1970s-1980s"; neither is sourced. **Gap:** no address anywhere reachable.
- **Years as an abstract, neo-geometric painter. [R]** MoMA/Pierogi consensus, undated.
- **A 1984 work said to prefigure the diagrams. [R, weak]** Cited in retrospective
  literature; **no such work was located in any reachable collection database or CV**. It is
  in any case not part of the mature series, which his own statement and MoMA date to 1994.

## 3. The break, 1990-1996

- **Research into BCCI, the savings and loan collapse and Harken begins in the early 1990s.
  [A, Wikipedia]** **Date correction:** no source gives a year. The circulating "1990" is the
  start of the MoMA papers' claimed inclusive range (1990-2000), a different thing. The file
  says "early 1990s" and uses 1992 explicitly as an ordering device.
- **1994: abandons painting for the narrative structures. [A]** Dated by his own statement
  (*Cabinet* 2, Spring 2001) and by MoMA's dating of the series.
- **The Khashoggi tree-chart origin anecdote. [R]** The quotation circulates in secondary
  accounts and was **not** verified against a primary text; carried with that status in
  `quote_source`.
- **First Houston showing, 1995. [R, contested]** One account asserts a 1995 debut without naming
  exhibition or venue. **Contradiction:** Pierogi's CV lists no 1995 Houston show and gives the
  1996 Lawndale exhibition as the earliest solo, though its group list reaches back to 1995.
  Downgraded from [A].
- **"Over The Line: Drawings 1994-6," Lawndale, 23 Nov 1996 to 18 Jan 1997. [A, Lawndale's own
  archive]** Dates exact. **Three corrections:** it was a **solo** show, not one he curated
  (Pierogi files it under solo exhibitions); the venue was then the **Lawndale Art and
  Performance Center**; coordinates are **29.7299, -95.3867**, 1.1 km from the circulating pin.

## 5. The work itself

- **Declared method. [A, his own statement]** "Every statement of fact and connection depicted
  in the work is true and based on information culled entirely from the public record"
  (*Cabinet* 2, Spring 2001). **Correction:** a circulating version dates this rule to 1999,
  which is arbitrary and unsourced; the codifying text is Spring 2001, and the file treats the
  rule as a standing principle dated only for ordering. **Caveat kept:** museums repeating the
  standard have not audited every connection in every drawing.
- **Formal vocabulary. [A, same statement]** Parallel lines for a time frame; radiating arrows
  for hierarchy and money flow; black for the story, red for legal action; drafting curves.
- **Subjects. [A for clusters, [R] for individual nodes]** BCCI/ICIC/FAB (ten drawings, Whitney,
  culminating in the 4th Version, 1996-2000); Oliver North, Lake Resources of Panama and
  Iran-Contra; George W. Bush, Harken Energy and Jackson Stephens; World Finance Corporation,
  Miami; Nugan Hand Ltd, Sydney; UPI Saga; Keating and Lincoln Savings. That Clifford, Casey and
  Noriega appear as named nodes rests on art-historical accounts rather than on reproductions
  examined here, and is tagged [R] in the relevant `date_confidence` fields.
- **The 4th Version's dimensions. [failed]** "Roughly 11.5 feet wide" could not be verified;
  whitney.org returns 404 to every path tried. The ten-work holding is kept, the measurement
  dropped.
- **The card archive. [contradiction, unresolved]** MoMA's finding aid is *reported* to
  describe over **10,000** 3x5 cards in 16 boxes, 16 linear feet, dated 1990-2000, colour-coded
  pink for companies and green for people. Contemporary exhibition materials give **c. 14,500**.
  Both figures are stated in the file; neither corrects the other. **Asymmetry that must not be
  smoothed:** only the ~14,500 figure is independently attested (Wikipedia); the finding-aid
  description, the 2006 donation year and Donald Lombardi as donor are all uncorroborated, since
  moma.org returns 403 and archive.org holds no snapshot.

## 6. Death, 22 March 2000

- **Died in his Williamsburg apartment, one day before his 49th birthday. [A]** MoMA's
  archival finding aid, the record of the museum that holds his papers, states the death was
  **suicide, by hanging**; corroborated by Smith's obituary (*NYT*, 25 March 2000) and Adrian
  Dannatt's (*The Independent*, 16 May 2000).
- **Murder rumours. [R, recorded as rumour]** Circulating since 2000, amplified by Goldstone's
  *Interlock* (2015). No located evidence overturns the institutional finding; the file states
  that finding without hedging and names the rumour and its carrier.
- **The FBI request, 17 October 2001. [A, Wikipedia]** An agent contacted the Whitney seeking
  access to or a reproduction of the BCCI-ICIC & FAB drawing, five weeks after the September 11
  attacks and eighteen months after Lombardi's death. It therefore cannot demonstrate any
  pre-death surveillance, and the "Lombardi predicted 9/11" claim is a myth the timeline
  refutes; both points are stated in the file. Robert Hobbs's phrase, that the agent
  "unwittingly made history," is verbatim in Wikipedia and sits in `suggested_refs`, not staged
  as a scene. **Two pool corrections:** one entry dated the contact "2001-09," contradicting its
  own note, and in October 2001 the drawing hung at the Whitney's **Breuer** building on Madison
  Avenue, the Gansevoort building not opening until 2015.

## 7. Afterlife

- **SFMOMA acquires "UPI Saga," 2004. [R]** From a collection record cited in the brief, not

- **Museo Reina Sofia. [A holding / R work and year]** Pierogi's CV lists it among his
  collections, so the holding is real; the Nugan Hand identification and the 2008 year are
  unverified (every collection URL 404, site search empty).
- **dOCUMENTA (13), 2012. [A for inclusion / failed for the work]** Confirmed on documenta.de's

- **"Lombardi drawings" in graph theory, 2012. [A]** Duncan, Eppstein, Goodrich, Kobourov and

- **"Everything Is Connected: Art and Conspiracy," 18 Sep 2018 to 6 Jan 2019. [A, Pierogi]**
  **Venue correction: The Met Breuer, 945 Madison Avenue (40.7734, -73.9638), not the Fifth
  Avenue building**, some 700 m apart. With Haacke, Holzer, Kelley, Paglen.
- **"Fragments of Memory," Menil Drawing Institute, 20 Sep 2024 to 26 Jan 2025. [A, the Menil's

  holds his "Untitled," 1994, ink on paper, acc. 2020-13.13.

## Apparatus relations for the operator

Mark Lombardi is **not** listed in `EXCEPTIONS.md`. Two relations were caught that are not
rooted in his own life. Neither is written into the journey file; both are recorded here rather
than deleted, per the adversary's duty.

1. **Direction caught: backward.** His apparatus (published-record nodes and lines, red for
   adjudicated fact) applies cleanly to networks he never drew and that predate his research:
   **Licio Gelli's Propaganda Due and the Banco Ambrosiano collapse**, **Prescott Bush's Union
   Banking Corporation, seized in 1942 under the Trading with the Enemy Act**, and **Lucky
   Luciano's Havana Conference of 1946**. Each is the same genre, publicly documented and
   structurally illegible in prose, which is the condition the method was built for. Licensing
   this would mean adding Lombardi to `EXCEPTIONS.md`, the stops sitting at his own Williamsburg
   pin with the interpretive status in `date_confidence`. Not written.
2. **Direction caught: forward.** Two things reach past his death and touch his work: the
   **FBI's consultation of the BCCI drawing on 17 October 2001** and the **2012 naming of
   "Lombardi drawings"** as a class of graph layout. Neither is staged as another person's
   scene: the FBI request is one clause and a `suggested_ref` on his own stop for the object he
   made, and the paper is a `suggested_ref` on the Kassel stop. Flagged so the operator can rule
   differently if he wants them expanded.

The posthumous stops in segments 7 and 8 are stops for **his objects at their locations**, not
scenes built around curators or filmmakers. Hobbs, Wegener and Donald Lombardi are named because
they acted on his work or his papers, not because they travelled somewhere he did.
