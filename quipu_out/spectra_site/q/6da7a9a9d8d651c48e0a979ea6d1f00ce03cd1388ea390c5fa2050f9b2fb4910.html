Domrémy

by Ekaterina Sirichinova

Joffrey Bourlémont, French nobleman turned Crusader, set for Jerusalem to find peace, war and the Well of Souls. He returned to Vosges weary and heartbroken. Solace came through a clearing in Domrémy. Here he built a well and tower for children to gather and hear to tales of dragons, ciphers and Celtic magic. Two centuries later Joan of Arc would draw power from this enchanted place, and thread a national identity.
