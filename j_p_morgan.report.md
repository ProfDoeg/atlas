# J. P. Morgan (1837-1913): research report
*2026-08-31. From the verified research pool for slug `j_p_morgan`. Companion: `j_p_morgan.journey.json`, 45 stops in 9 segments.*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named · **[R]** reconstruction or tradition no reachable primary document supports. Contradictions are flagged, **not adjudicated**. Where the pool's lenses disagreed, both readings are printed and the journey states the dispute in `date_confidence`, never in prose.

Standing caution: much of the chronology reaches the pool through a fetched **dossier** whose citations were not re-fetched (the carbines, the 1864 gold export, Dabney Morgan & Co., the Corsair conferences, Cragston, the Metropolitan Club, the 1895 syndicate, the McKinley contribution, the Northern Pacific corner, the library, the Exchange rescue, Aix-les-Bains). **[A dossier]** means attested *in a summary*, not against a document.

## I. Hartford, Boston, schooling abroad, 1837-1857

Born 17 April 1837 at his maternal grandfather's house on Asylum Street, Hartford, to Junius Spencer Morgan and Juliet Pierpont **[A Wikipedia; Strouse]**. Episcopal Academy at Cheshire c. 1847-1851 **[A]**: no stop, the pool gives a range and nothing else. Boston English High School, 1851 **[A]**; *coordinate corrected* from 42.3555, -71.0655 (Boston Common) to **42.3521, -71.0603**, the Bedford Street building the school shared with Boston Latin 1844-1881, moving to Warren Street only in 1881 **[A Wikipedia, 'English High School of Boston']**. April 1852, rheumatic fever, sent alone to the Azores for months **[A]**. Bellerive school, La Tour-de-Peilz, 1856 **[A]**.

**Göttingen, 1856-1857: contradiction, unresolved.** The chronology lens tags the art-history degree **[A]** on English Wikipedia's "earned a degree in art history within six months." The geography lens tags it **[R]**, noting that Göttingen's record supports roughly a year in residence with lectures in mathematics and chemistry and daily German, and that Strouse does not support the degree. **The lenses contradict each other.** Harmonised to **[R]**, dispute in `date_confidence`. No one has produced the diploma.

## II. The clerk, 1857-1861

George Peabody & Co., 22 Old Broad Street, London, August 1857 **[A]**. Duncan, Sherman & Co., New York, 1858-1861, foreign exchange and trade finance **[A Wikipedia; dossier]**. New Orleans cotton correspondents, early 1859 **[A]**; the anecdote of the clerk buying a shipload of coffee on his own authority is **not in the pool** and is unused. J. Pierpont Morgan & Co. opens at 53 Exchange Place, 1861 **[A]**.

## III. Memie, and the business of the war, 1861-1865

$20,000 lent to Simon Stevens against surplus Hall carbines resold at a large markup to Frémont's command; the 1863 congressional committee denounced arms profiteering generally and did not charge Morgan **[A dossier, citing the investigation and biographers Wasson, Myers, Josephson, Morris]**. The biographers split on the moral reading from identical documents; the journey states the split and does not settle it. Month unknown, so the stop is dated 1861-06-01 to order the segment, and says so.

Marries Amelia "Memie" Sturges at 5 East Fourteenth Street, October 1861, she already tubercular **[A]**. Algiers, winter 1861-62 **[A dossier, corroborated in outline by Wikipedia on the journey to Nice]**, month inferred. Amelia dies at Nice, February 1862 **[A]**. Buys gold in New York and exports it through the London house, 1864 **[A dossier]**: legal, reputationally damaging, never prosecuted. Dabney, Morgan & Co., 1864-1871 **[A dossier]**. Marries Frances Louisa "Fanny" Tracy at St. George's, Stuyvesant Square, 31 May 1865 **[A]**. *Coordinate corrected* to Wikipedia's **40.7344, -73.9850** for 209 East 16th Street; the geography lens gave 40.7334, -73.9835 (170 m southeast), the afterlife lens 40.7335, -73.9847. Used at the marriage and the 1913 funeral alike. Children Louisa 1866, John Pierpont Jr. 1867, Juliet 1870, Anne Tracy 1873 **[A]**, carried in the marriage campa rather than given stops. Senior warden for decades; funded the industrial school, sewing classes, soup kitchens and health programmes under Rector Rainsford (1883-1906) and the St. George Memorial House, 1886; the parish was called Morgan's Church **[A]**.

## IV. The Corner, 1871-1883

Drexel, Morgan & Co. formed with Anthony J. Drexel at 23 Wall Street, 1 July 1871 **[A]**.

Drexel, Morgan finances the Edison Electric Light Company in 1878, before Edison has a working lamp **[A `thomas_edison`; Wikipedia]**. 219 Madison Avenue lit throughout by an Edison system, 6 June 1882, with fires and mishaps **[A]**. The 1883 placement of William H. Vanderbilt's New York Central block is **[R Wikipedia]**: no stop, carried as one clause in the Corsair campa with the tag in that stop's `date_confidence`.

## V. Order imposed, 1889-1896

Railroad presidents aboard the *Corsair*, the agreement on "public, reasonable, uniform and stable rates," 1889-1890 **[A dossier]**, month set to July 1889. Edison's companies consolidated into Edison General Electric, 24 April 1889 **[A]**, folded into the 1892 stop.

**Junius Spencer Morgan's death: date corrected.** The pool gave "1890-04." The carriage accident above Monte Carlo was **3 April 1890** (horses bolted at an oncoming train, the carriage struck a pile of stone, broken wrist and concussion); he died still unconscious **8 April 1890**, buried at Cedar Hill **[A Wikipedia, 'Junius Spencer Morgan']**.

13-14 Prince's Gate, Kensington **[A]**, undated in the pool ("various"), dated 1890-06 to order the segment. Metropolitan Club founded 1891 after John King is blackballed at the Union Club: site donated, Stanford White commissioned, Morgan president **[A dossier]**. Edison General Electric merged with Thomson-Houston into General Electric, 1892; Edison loses control **[A]**. February 1895, the Cleveland Treasury contract with the Morgan and Belmont/Rothschild syndicate, about $62 million in 30-year bonds against gold **[A dossier; no primary Treasury source]**. Firm renamed J. P. Morgan & Co. on Drexel's death, 1895 **[A dossier]**: no stop, carried in the 1913 procession campa. A reported $250,000 to McKinley in 1896 **[A dossier]**, closing clause of the Treasury stop, marked as reported.

## VI. The trust maker, 1898-1904

New York Yacht Club: as commodore (1897-1899) he buys the 75-by-100-foot plot on West 44th Street for $148,000 in 1898 **[A]**. *Coordinate corrected* from 40.7551, -73.9789 (about 200 m east) to **40.7553, -73.9813**, 37 West 44th Street.

Tesla contract, March 1901: $150,000 for 51% of the company and of present and future wireless patents; Stanford White designs the Shoreham tower **[A Wikipedia; `nikola_tesla`]**. That Tesla pitched telegraphy while privately intending broadcast power is **[A `nikola_tesla`]**. **U.S. Steel: date corrected.** The pool said February 1901; February is when the terms were settled, and the New Jersey incorporation is **2 March 1901**, capitalisation $1.4 billion, constituents Carnegie Steel, Federal Steel, National Steel **[A Wikipedia]**. The December 1900 Schwab dinner and the Carnegie negotiations are **[A]**. Carnegie paid roughly $225.64 million in fifty-year gold bonds **[A `andrew_carnegie`]**; Rockefeller's Mesabi ore ranges and lake fleet bought that spring out of 26 Broadway for roughly $90 million **[A `john_d_rockefeller_sr`]**; Frick takes a board seat and runs nothing operationally after **[A `henry_clay_frick`]**.

Northern Pacific corner and panic, May 1901, settled by organising Northern Securities **[A dossier]**; the 9th orders the stop, the pool giving only the month. International Mercantile Marine, 1902, White Star inside it **[A]**. British opinion treated the purchase as a national loss; the legislative response is not in the pool and is stated in the campa only in general terms.

**The Tesla refusal: date corrected twice.** The chronology lens dated it 1904, the afterlife lens July 1901. The closing response ("impossible for me to do anything in the matter") is **14 July 1903**; Tesla's expanded request came first, in July 1901 **[A Wikipedia]**. **The free-energy legend is [R] and stays [R]**: no located Morgan document says he cut Tesla off because wireless power could not be metered. The documented causes are cost overruns, change of scope, and Marconi's December 1901 signal; there is no meter remark anywhere in the record.

*Northern Securities Co. v. United States*, 14 March 1904, 5-4, dissolution ordered, Morgan a named civil defendant **[A]**. *Coordinate corrected*: the pool gave 38.8905, -77.0045, the Supreme Court building at 1 First Street NE, **not built until 1935**. In 1904 the Court sat in the Old Senate Chamber inside the Capitol: **38.8899, -77.0091**.

## VII. The collector, 1904-1910

Trustee from 1888, vice president, then president of the Metropolitan Museum from 1904 until his death; finances acquisitions and Egyptian excavation **[A]**. The McKim library completed 1906 **[A]**. *Address corrected*: the library is **225** Madison at East 36th Street, **219** Madison the residence next door; the pool labelled the 1907 command-post stop "219 Madison" and the 1906 stop "225 Madison" for one building. The journey uses 225 for the library, 219 only for the 1882 lighting. Belle da Costa Greene is his librarian from 1905 **[A]**, a historical person here, not an interlock: she has no file.

Morganite proposed by George Frederick Kunz at the New York Academy of Sciences, 5 December 1910, for a pink beryl found that year off Madagascar, honouring Morgan's gem gifts to New York and Paris **[A Wikipedia]**. The pool fixes no coordinate for the Academy meeting, so the stop is pinned at the American Museum of Natural History, which he endowed and which holds the gems, and says so.

## VIII. The Panic of 1907

Returns from the Episcopal convention at Richmond on 19 October; the library becomes the command post **[A Wikipedia]**. On 22 October the Knickerbocker Trust is judged unsound and left to fail, about $8 million withdrawn in three hours, while the Trust Company of America is judged salvageable and roughly $8.25 million organised to hold it **[A]**. Neither trust company's address is pinned in the pool, so that stop sits at the Corner, where Morgan worked that day: **a stop belongs to whoever was actually there.**

The Stock Exchange kept open, 24 October **[A dossier, outline only]**. The pool gives no date beyond October and no figures; the reported $25 million and the cheer from the floor are **not in the pool** and are not stated. **The lock-in: date corrected.** The pool dated the library conference 1907-11-04. The conference was the evening of Saturday **2 November** (key in his pocket), agreement about 4:45 a.m. Sunday **3 November**, Roosevelt's clearance of the U.S. Steel purchase of Tennessee Coal, Iron and Railroad Monday **4 November** before the markets opened **[A]**.

## IX. The reckoning, 1912-1913

The *Titanic* sinks 15 April 1912; Morgan had booked a suite with a private promenade deck and did not sail **[A]**. **That he was at Aix-les-Bains is [R].** The geography lens tagged it [A dossier] and its own correction note downgrades it: Wikipedia confirms only the booking and that "those plans were later changed," and no source reached this pass places him at Aix. The coordinate 45.6883, 5.9155 is correct *for Aix-les-Bains*; whether he was in it is open.

Pujo testimony under Samuel Untermyer, 18-19 December 1912: **[R]** for the exact dates and the famous exchange. The *New York Times* covered the appearance on 19 December under "Morgan Reveals Business of Firm" **[A Wikipedia]**; a fetch could not confirm the dates or reproduce the exchange, and the transcript could not be retrieved. His partners later blamed his death on the stress of it: **[A that they said it]** / **[R as a causal claim]**, printed in the journey as their claim, in their voice.

Dies in his sleep in Rome, 31 March 1913, aged 75 **[A]**. The hotel is **upgraded from [R] to [A]**: Italian Wikipedia names the **Albergo Roma, the future Grand Hotel Plaza**, independently of English Wikipedia. *Coordinate corrected* to **41.9016, 12.4790** (Via del Corso 126); the afterlife lens used the Rome centroid, 1.4 km off, the geography lens a point near Piazza Barberini, 500 m off.

NYSE closed two hours for the procession; burial at Cedar Hill after services at St. George's **[A]**. *Coordinate corrected* to **41.72222, -72.70333** (453 Fairfield Avenue), which the afterlife lens already carried; the geography lens put the cemetery about 4 km east. The monument is red granite by George W. Keller, reportedly conceived to evoke the Ark of the Covenant **[A]**, undated in the source. The three April stops carry day 01 because the pool gives only the month.

## X. Quotations: what could be verified

Five of forty-five stops carry a `quote`. A low proportion, because most famous Morgan lines do not survive contact with a source.

**Used, [A].** "Because a man I do not trust could not get money from me on all the bonds in Christendom." Pujo testimony, December 1912, quoted verbatim and footnoted in Moen and Rodgers (2022), p. 172, PDF fetched. The transcript itself was not pulled.

**Verified, not used.** The firm's open letter to the Pujo committee ("Such ills... are the outcome of a clumsy and outworn banking system rather than of the schemes of men") is **[A** O'Sullivan 2015, p. 24**]**, but it is the *firm's* signed letter, not a spoken remark; it is a `suggested_ref` on the Pujo stop. **Excluded as too weak, all [R]:** "A man always has two reasons for what he does" (anonymous precursors from the 1800s, first attached to Morgan in Wister's 1930 memoir); "The first thing is character" and "Money is gold, and nothing else" (unretrievable from the transcript, so the journey paraphrases the character exchange and prints only its verified companion).

## XII. Apparatus relations for the operator

**J. P. Morgan is NOT listed in `EXCEPTIONS.md`.** These relations are therefore **kept and flagged rather than silently deleted**, and none was written into the journey. All are **FORWARD**, which the exception file never licenses by an agent's own judgement: Morgan's file stops in 1913 and cannot embed a later hash.

1. **`jamie_dimon`.** JPMorgan Chase descends from J. P. Morgan & Co. via the 2000 Chase Manhattan merger; in 2008 the bank bearing Morgan's name bought Bear Stearns' headquarters at 383 Madison Avenue, which the pool reads as a structural echo of 1907.
2. **`larry_fink`.** BlackRock retained in 2009 by Treasury and the Fed to unwind toxic assets including Morgan Stanley's; Morgan Stanley was founded in 1935 by Morgan's grandson Henry S. Morgan after Glass-Steagall split the House of Morgan.
3. **The 2012-2023 rescue of Wardenclyffe** (Inman and Alcorn's crowdfunding, the 2 May 2013 purchase for $1.6 million, Musk's $1 million in 2014, the fire of 21 November 2023) **[A Wikipedia]**. The site's modern fame rests on Morgan's refusal, which is why the pool surfaced it; `elon_musk.journey.json` exists and would be the pointer, which is exactly what must not be written.
4. **JPMorgan Chase's 2000s acknowledgment** that 19th-century predecessor institutions accepted enslaved people as loan collateral **[A]**: predecessor firms, not Pierpont Morgan's conduct.

**What was written in, and why it differs.** Two posthumous facts close the Cedar Hill campa: the 1924 opening of the library to the public by his son as a memorial, with Belle da Costa Greene as first director. These are the fate of Morgan's own estate and institution, handled as `abdelkader.journey.json` handles the 1966 repatriation of the emir's remains. They name no later traveler and point at no later file. If the operator judges even this too far forward, it is the last sentence of the last stop and lifts out cleanly.

Not used: the Morgan Library's 2024-2025 Greene exhibition and the scholarship (Ardizzone 2007, Parker 2024) establishing that Greene was born to Black parents and passed as white **[A]**. Greene's stops, not Morgan's, and she has no file.

## XIII. Gaps

**The Pujo transcript**, the most important primary document for his own voice. FRASER holds it digitised and searchable **[A]**; the fetch was refused. Everything about December 1912 beyond one secondary-quoted sentence rests on that failure.

**The 1903 Steichen portrait**, whose chair-arm highlight reads as a dagger, and the tradition that Morgan disliked or destroyed a print: unconfirmed by any reachable page, Wikipedia's Steichen article not mentioning Morgan at all. One of the most famous images of him, and it gets **no stop**: no verified place, date, or event to hang one on.

**Morgan, Harjes & Co., Paris**: no address, no date, no stop. **No scholarly edition of his words exists**: only testimony, correspondence and circulars, never gathered. **The 1902 anthracite strike mediation** appears only inside the Roosevelt coverage-gap note, with no date, place, or citation: no stop. **Cheshire Academy and Cragston**: attested places, no usable dates, dropped, recoverable.
