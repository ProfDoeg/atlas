# Volodymyr Zelenskyy: research report

*2026-08-22. Volodymyr Oleksandrovych Zelenskyy, b. Kryvyi Rih 25 January 1978; sixth President of Ukraine from 20 May 2019. Journey file: `volodymyr_zelenskyy.journey.json`, 9 segments, 45 stops, 6 verbatim quotes.*

Legend: **[A: source]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, paraphrase, or press consensus with no primary document. Contradictions are flagged and left standing; where a supplied claim was found wrong, the correction is recorded with its reason rather than silently dropped. This is a living subject in an ongoing war, and the evidence splits sharply between official transcripts and video on one side and unnamed officials relayed by wire services on the other.

## 1. Kryvyi Rih and Erdenet, 1978 to 1994

**Birth [A: Wikipedia, "Volodymyr Zelenskyy"; Shuster, *The Showman* (2024); Time and BBC profiles].** 25 January 1978, Kryvyi Rih, into a Jewish family; father **Oleksandr** a professor of computing and cybernetics, mother **Rymma** an engineer. **Gap:** the hospital is unnamed in any reachable source, so the pin is city-centre Kryvyi Rih. **Olena Kiyashko born in the same city 6 February 1978 [A: Wikipedia, "Olena Zelenska"].**

**Erdenet, Mongolia [A as to fact, R as to dates].** Four years there before elementary school, while his father worked as a mining engineer. **Contradiction adjudicated:** the pool anchored this to **1978**, his birth year, and elsewhere to **c. 1979-1983**; Soviet schooling began at seven, so the residence falls at roughly **c. 1981-1985**, and the journey uses 1981-01-01 with the range in `date_confidence`. **Coordinates corrected [A: Wikipedia, "Erdenet"]:** the pool's 49.0333 / 104.0833 sits about three kilometres east of the town; the correct pair is **49.0278 / 104.0444**.

**The 95th quarter [R: Ukrainian press profiles of Kvartal 95's origin]**, source of the troupe's and the studio's name. **Gap:** no street address anywhere reachable, so the pin (47.97 / 33.36) is approximate to the Ternivskyi district and says so. **TOEFL and the refused grant to Israel, age 16 [A]**, the father refusing permission; no month recorded, so the journey defaults to June, and the granting institution is unnamed.

## 2. KVN and the Moscow circuit, 1995 to 2003

**Joins KVN at 17 [A]**, becoming dance director, then writer, then front man of **Zaporizhzhia-Kryvyi Rih-Transit**, which wins the **1997 championship [A]**, tying with an Armenian team.

**Three corrections to the supplied geography entry [A].** The pool said **Premier League**, but Transit played the **Higher League**, the Premier being a separate junior league. The pool gave the MSU Palace of Culture, Sparrow Hills (55.7033 / 37.5322), but Higher League games ran at the **Moscow Palace of Youth (55.7253 / 37.5822)**, about 3.5 km off. The pool gave the Moscow run as 1999-2003, but Transit was active **1995-2000**, and **2003** is the break with KVN, not the team's run; split into two stops.

**Kvartal 95 founded as a troupe, 1997 [A]. Law degree, 2000 [A]**, Kryvyi Rih Institute of Economics, never practised; folded into the 1994 stop, and the folding is declared in `date_confidence`.

**Break with KVN, 2003 [A as to the fact, R as to the reasons].** The itemised grievances, withheld fees and censored material and an antisemitic remark by a Russian producer, rest on his own later accounts and on Shuster: testimony, not documentation, and written as his account.

## 6. February 2022: the contested week

**Address to the Russian people [A: Wikisource, "Zelensky's emotional appeal to Russians", from the NBC News full transcript].** Three quotes checked verbatim, all faithful, though the pool truncates the "two completely different countries" line just before its payoff, "The most important difference is that ours is real". **Date correction [A]:** the pool dated all three **23 February**; Wikisource and the NBC transcript behind it date the address **24 February**, recorded late on the 23rd and aired an hour or so before the invasion. **Martial law and general mobilisation, same day [A: Wikipedia, "Timeline of the 2022 Russian invasion of Ukraine"]**, folded into the same stop.

**"We are all here", 25 February 2022 [A as to the video].** **Contradiction flagged and left visible in `date_confidence`:** the pool placed the filming at the **Presidential Office on Bankova** with coordinates **50.4478 / 30.5326**, which miss 11 Bankova (50.4445 / 30.5290) by about 450 m in any case, while Wikipedia places the video **outside the Mariinskyi Palace, c. 50.4497 / 30.5372**. The journey uses Mariinskyi and states the disagreement rather than resolving it. No verbatim English transcript is in the pool, so `quote` is left null.

**"I need ammunition, not a ride", 26 February 2022: tag flipped from [R] to [A].** The largest correction in the file, running the opposite way from the rest: the pool tagged it [R] as unverifiable, and that note is out of date. Per Wikipedia, "I need ammunition, not a ride", it was first reported by **James LaPorta for AP at 04:04 UTC on 26 February 2022**, sourced to "a senior American intelligence official with direct knowledge of the conversation". **Glenn Kessler, Washington Post Fact Checker, 6 March 2022**, could not confirm it; NSC officials denied it and, per LaPorta in CBS News on 21 May 2026, demanded a retraction AP refused. **Zelenskyy then confirmed it himself twice on the record:** February 2023 to Dmytro Komarov in the documentary *Year*, naming Biden as the caller, and on 24 February 2026 in his own voice, filmed in the government bunker, on president.gov.ua. Applied: tag **A**, place sharpened to the **bunker**, first reporter corrected to **AP** rather than Reuters or NPR. The dispute is preserved inside the campa. **Residual uncertainty:** the call is dated on or about 25 to 26 February, and the 26th is the report date.

**Snake Island [A as to the announcement; its premise was false].** Zelenskyy named thirteen border guards "reported killed" Heroes of Ukraine. **The men were not killed:** the Ukrainian Navy confirmed on **28 February 2022** that all were alive and captured, and the radio operator was released that March and decorated. The journey keeps the episode but pins it **in Kyiv**, not on Snake Island, where he never set foot [A: Wikipedia, "Attack on Snake Island"].

## 7. The video addresses: a structural decision, stated openly

The pool listed **six foreign legislatures and one Davos appearance as geography entries with venue coordinates**: the European Parliament, UK Parliament, Canadian Parliament, Bundestag and Knesset between 1 and 20 March 2022, and the World Economic Forum in January 2023. **Zelenskyy was in Ukraine for all of them**, and the pool's own verifier had already called a pinned Davos stop a false stop, to be dropped or marked explicitly as a remote address. His only in-person WEF appearance was 22 January 2020.

**Decision, applied to all seven and not only to Davos.** A journey file is a map of where a body went, and a speech delivered by video from a bunker is not travel. Three stops are written **at the Kyiv pin, 50.4445 / 30.5290**, named for the chambers addressed, each `date_confidence` stating that the chamber is elsewhere and Zelenskyy is in Kyiv; the **January 2023 Davos entry is dropped entirely**. The in-person Westminster visit of 8 February 2023 and the in-person UNGA appearance of 19 September 2023 keep their real pins, and the difference between the two kinds of appearance is the point of the segment. **[R] inside the three video stops:** the rhetorical specifics of the Brussels, Ottawa and Bundestag speeches come from parliamentary records and coverage rather than verbatim transcripts, and the **Shevchenko Day observance of 9 March 2022** is reconstructed from standing presidential practice, not verified against president.gov.ua this pass.

**Knesset quotes [A: Wikisource].** "We intend to remain alive. Our neighbors want to see us dead." is **Golda Meir's**, quoted by Zelenskyy inside his own address, and is presented as hers; "People of Israel, now you have such a choice." is his and carries the `quote` field.

## 8. Liberations and the wartime circuit, 2022 to 2026

Bucha, Irpin with the three visiting leaders, Kherson, Bakhmut, Westminster in person, Hiroshima, the UN General Assembly and the 2025 Oval Office confrontation are attested without complication and carried into the journey with their sources. The rest needed work:

- **Izium, 14 September 2022: causality corrected [A: Wikipedia, "Izium mass graves"].** The pool had him arriving "days after investigators begin exhuming a mass burial site". **The sequence is the reverse:** he visited on the 14th and raised the flag, the graves were **found on the 15th**, exhumations began on the **16th**.
- **US Congress, 21 December 2022: tag flipped from [R] to [A].** The pool called this "reconstructed from general reporting". It is fully documented: a **joint meeting**, pedantically not a joint session, **in person**, his first foreign trip since the invasion; a separate address **by video** took place 16 March 2022. The **Patriot pledge at the White House the same day [A]** is folded into this stop.
- **Vilnius, 11 July 2023 [A: NATO records]: coordinate correction.** The pool's 54.6863 / 25.2564 sits about 2.5 km north-east of the venue; Litexpo is at **54.67861 / 25.22500**.
- **Kupiansk, 2023 [R]**, carried with **no confirmed date** and therefore **dropped** rather than dated by guess. **Kharkiv, 29 May 2022 [A]** is attested and **cut for length** against the 45-stop ceiling, not for doubt.
- **Casa Rosada, Buenos Aires, 10 December 2023 [A: `javier_milei.journey.json`]**, Milei's inauguration, coordinates **inherited byte-identical**, -34.6083 / -58.3712.
- **Term expiry, May 2024 [A].** The five-year term lapsed and martial law bars elections; folded into the closing stop as the legal position, stated without judgement. **Fourth-anniversary bunker address, 24 February 2026 [A: president.gov.ua]** supplies the closing quote and the confirmation of the 2022 Biden call in his own voice.

## 10. Apparatus relations for the operator

Zelenskyy is **not** listed in `EXCEPTIONS.md`, so no framework of his may be applied backward, and nothing was written into any earlier subject's file. Relations found in the pool that are **not rooted in Zelenskyy's own life** are flagged here rather than deleted, with their direction. **Direction caught: backward in every case**, each proposing an *earlier* traveller's stop as meaningful because of what *later* happened in the same place under Zelenskyy. Under the block rule these would be legal as embeddings, old hash in a new block; they are excluded not for direction but because **shared geography is not a relation at all**.

Co-location only: Odessa 1911 (`meyer_lansky`), Odessa 1918 (`moshe_feldenkrais`), St Vladimir's University Kiev 1898-99 (`vladimir_medem`), Solotvyno 1923 (`robert_maxwell`), Berlin 1918 (`rudolf_rocker`), Saint Petersburg and the imperial sphere (`george_balanchine`, the pool's own weakest item). Institution rather than person: the Kyiv sovereign-debt position of 2015 (`mark_brodsky`). Shared world-event with Zelenskyy unnamed: Athens, 24 February 2022 (`yanis_varoufakis`). Venue type rather than relation: Davos and Brussels (`yuval_noah_harari`). Speculative pin-reuse for stops that do not exist: the Western Wall and Temple Mount pins (`benjamin_netanyahu`), the Jerusalem Supreme Court of 1972 (`meyer_lansky`), and the canonical Paris pin, where the Elysee's own coordinates are used instead.

One is excluded for a sharper reason. **Kaniv, Chernecha Hora, 1861 (`taras_shevchenko`):** the grave is Shevchenko's stop, not Zelenskyy's, and staging it here would be exactly the error the standing rule forbids. Zelenskyy's real relation to Shevchenko is a citation, and it sits at Zelenskyy's own pin.

**No forward-direction relation was found and none was written.** No git command was run.

## 11. Honest gaps

- **Ukrainian-language sources were not reached this pass.** Everything here is English or English-relayed, which for a Ukrainian head of state is a real limitation and bears hardest on the Kryvyi Rih and KVN material.
- **No street address is confirmed** for the birth hospital, the 95th quarter block, the Kvartal 95 studio at founding, the Inter studio, or the 1+1 studio; five pins are approximate and each says so. **The 2003 marriage record** was not seen. **The Rada immunity vote tally** and **the Olimpiyskiy attendance figure** are not in the pool, so no numbers are stated.
- **Recorded but given no stop:** Time Person of the Year, December 2022 [A], because the naming produces no travel and a New York pin for an editorial decision he was not present for would be exactly the false stop avoided in section 7; the 2022 Peace Formula and 2024 Victory Plan [A], which are documents rather than journeys; and the Ghost of Kyiv [R], which is not his myth at all, though the pool's date for the Ukrainian Air Force's clarification is wrong and belongs on **30 April 2022**, not 2023.
