# Jan Amos Komenský (Comenius), 1592-1670: research report

*2026-08-30, for `jan_amos_comenius.journey.json`. Slug: `jan_amos_comenius`.*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named · **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or single-source claim not
corroborated · **[C]** contradiction between sources, recorded and not adjudicated.

**The evidentiary spine is one institution.** The biography maintained by the Muzeum Jana Amose
Komenského at Uherský Brod (`mjakub.cz/zivot?idm=37`) carries roughly two-thirds of this itinerary
alone: a good, apparatus-bearing life, but a single voice. Where a national Wikipedia article could
corroborate a place, date, or coordinate, that is said; where it could not, the tag drops to [R].

## 1. Origins and formation, 1592-1617

- **28 March 1592 [A]** (museum; encyclopedia.com), to Martin and Anna Komenský.
- **Birthplace unresolved [C]**; the custodian museum says the evidence permits no unique answer.
  **Nivnice**, the name he put on title pages as *Nivnicensis*, is the strongest thread, and a mill
  there, Bartkův mlýn no. 99, has been the marked monument since 1993 [A monument, R birth].
  **Uherský Brod** has family residence and a later epitaph tradition. **Komňa** is ancestral:
  cs.wikipedia confirms the grandfather was local magistrate and the surname derives from it [A],
  though nobody claims it as a residence. No birth record survives from any of the three.
- **1604 [A]** both parents die inside one year; placed with his aunt Zuzana Nohálová. **1605 [A]**
  Brethren school at Strážnice, the town later burned in the Bocskai uprising [A, museum, undated].
  **1608-1611 [A]** higher Brethren school at Přerov; first Latin from Tomáš Dubina; patronage from
  bishop Jan Lánecký and Karel the Elder of Žerotín.
- **1611-1613 [A]** Herborn, under Piscator, Gutberleth, and above all **Johann Heinrich Alsted**,
  whose encyclopedism and chiliastic chronology are the acknowledged scaffolding of the pansophy.
  **1612 [R]**: *Problemata miscellanea* and *Sylloge quaestionum controversarum*, museum index only.
- **1613-1614 [A]** Heidelberg under **David Pareus**, a theological disputation completed. **1614
  [R]**: the Copernicus *De revolutionibus* manuscript and the walk home via Nuremberg and Prague, both
  reported by the museum as tradition and with the manuscript's fate expressly unestablishable.
- **1614-1617 [A]** heads the Přerov school; begins *Theatrum universitatis rerum*, never finished.

## 2. Ordination, Fulnek, and the loss of everything, 1616-1623

- **25 April 1616 [A]** ordained at the Žeravice synod. The pool had year and place only; cs.wikipedia
  supplies the exact day and the figure of thirty-two men ordained, Comenius among them.
- **1618-1621 [A]** pastor and school rector at Fulnek on Jan Skrbenský of Hříště's estate; marries
  Magdalena Vizovská; two sons. en.wikipedia (Fulnek) independently confirms the residence dates.
- **8 November 1620 [A]** the Estates' army broken at White Mountain. **1620-1621 [R]** imperial (the
  museum says Spanish) troops devastate Fulnek, school and church: **en.wikipedia's Fulnek article
  contains nothing about a Spanish occupation or a burning**, so this rests on the museum alone.
- **1621 [R]** refuge near Vikštejn castle, local tradition only; cs.wikipedia's Vikštejn article
  carries no Comenius tradition. **[C] of geography**: the pool filed the castle under the Oderské
  vrchy, cs.wikipedia under the **Nízký Jeseník**, which the file follows.
- **1622 [A deaths, R place]** Magdalena and both sons die, probably of plague, while he is in hiding.
  The museum says only "the Moravian mountains, then the Velké Losiny area"; cs.wikipedia's Velké
  Losiny article mentions neither Comenius nor Žerotín for 1622. Pinned there, hedged in the file.
- **1623 [R]** library and manuscript drafts publicly burned in the Fulnek square. Destruction
  attested, responsibility not documented. **"The Jesuits burned his books" appears in the campa as
  the popular version, not as an assertion.**

## 3. Brandýs, the errand, the frontier, 1623-1628

- **1623 [A]** first version of *Labyrint světa a ráj srdce* at Brandýs nad Orlicí; printed 1631.
  **1624 [A]** marries Marie Dorota Cyrillová there: the pool gave "Bohemia/Moravia", cs.wikipedia
  supplies the place and its documentary basis, the entry in the **Brandýs town books**.
- **1625-1626 [A, with an internal contradiction]** the errand for the Unity: Görlitz, Sprottau
  (Szprotawa), Leszno, Berlin, The Hague, plus the rescue of Žerotín's library and the transfer of the
  Kralice press to Wrocław. **[C]**: the pool's chronology lens dated the whole route 1625, its
  geography lens dated the Hague audience 1626, the museum's framing is "diplomatic journeys
  1625-1626". Spread across 1625-1626 in sequence, contradiction recorded rather than argued away.
- **1626 [A]** delivers Christoph Kotter's anti-Habsburg predictions in person to Frederick V of the
  Palatinate at The Hague.
- **1626-1628 [R for the details]** resident mainly at Bílá Třemešná under Jiří Sadovský of Sloupno;
  cs.wikipedia has a Comenius memorial there but **names neither Sadovský nor the residence**. **1627
  [R]** reads Elias Bodinus's *Didactica oder Lehrkunst* in Adam Zilvar of Silberstein's library at
  Vlčice; cs.wikipedia confirms only the family's tenure of the village. **1627 [A, museum]** meets
  the visionary Kristina Poniatowská at Horní Branná.
- **The Renewed Land Ordinance, two instruments now split.** The pool carried one claim dated 31 July
  1627 for "Ferdinand II's Renewed Land Ordinance and patent". Per cs.wikipedia (*Obnovené zřízení
  zemské*) these are separate: the Ordinance for **Bohemia** 10 May 1627, for **Moravia** 10 May 1628,
  and the imperial **emigration patent** 31 July 1627.
- **The frontier crossing, the worst error in the pool. 4 February 1628 [A]** for the date. The pool
  put the crossing near Náchod; cs.wikipedia (Žacléř) documents it about **70 km away**, at the Růžový
  palouček memorial, with his last Bohemian night in Protestant Žacléř. That route fits the Vlčice
  shelter 10 km south; the pool's point does not. **[C] left open**: Vlčice *also* keeps a last-night
  tradition, which cs.wikipedia flags "citation needed". Both are in the Vlčice campa, unresolved.

## 4. Leszno, London, Endegeest, Elbing, 1628-1648

- **1628 [A]** teaches, then rector, at the Leszno gymnasium; writes the Czech *Didaktika*, later
  Latinized as *Didactica magna* (composed 1627-1638, first printed 1657; Keatinge's English, 1896).
- **1631 [C]** *Janua linguarum reserata* at Leszno: museum bibliography 1631 for the original,
  encyclopedia.com 1633 for a variant. Not adjudicated; 1631 used. Same year, **[R]**, a short tract
  during a plague outbreak against abandoning the sick and trading in infected animals.
- **1632 [C]** recorded as bishop and secretary of the exiled Unity, but other summaries reserve
  "senior bishop" for the 1648 election. Not reconciled; the campa states the office as work done.
- **21 September 1641 [A/R]** London: nine months with **Samuel Hartlib**, drafting *Via lucis* and
  joining the abortive pansophic "college of light" with Hartlib, Dury, Hübner, and Haak, which
  collapses as England moves toward civil war. The pool tags this [R] on the Oxford DNB Hartlib entry
  and the EMLO catalogue. No Hartlib journey file exists; he is a direct associate, not an interlock.
- **July 1642 [A]** meets **René Descartes** at Endegeest en route to Sweden under Louis de Geer's
  patronage (see interlocks); **1642 [A]** Stockholm, Oxenstierna, the school-reform commission;
  **1642-1648 [A]** settled at Elbing (Elbląg), Royal Prussia under Swedish influence, financed by de
  Geer, deadlines slipping as he gives time to pansophy and church peacemaking.
- **1645 [A]** the Colloquium Charitativum at Thorn (Toruń); his irenicism displeases Swedish sponsors.

## 5. Westphalia, the Kšaft, Sárospatak, 1648-1654

- **August 1648 [A]** Marie Dorota dies, leaving four children: Dorota Kristina, Alžběta, Zuzana,
  Daniel (museum and encyclopedia.com agree on August). **1649 [A]** marries Jana Gajusová, who
  survives him; folded into the 1648 stop to keep the file inside its ceiling.
- **1648 [A]** Westphalia neither restores the Bohemian Protestant estates nor permits the Unity's
  return; he answers with *Kšaft umírající matky, Jednoty bratrské*, and becomes senior bishop in the
  common formulation (but see the 1632 contradiction).
- **1650-1654 [A]** Sárospatak, invited by Zsuzsanna Lorántffy and Sigismund Rákóczi; a seven-grade
  pansophic school designed, only three grades implemented. **[R]** for the books written or revised
  there (*Schola ludus*, *Orbis sensualium pictus* in draft, *Praecepta morum*, *Leges scholae bene
  ordinatae*): the museum gives no years.
- **1650-1654** Brethren communities in the Slovak country: Skalica [R], Trnava [A], Púchov [A],
  Levoča [A], Prešov [A]. **No route is reconstructable**, only a list, so the file stages one stop
  (Trnava) and names the rest inside its campa. At **Lednica [R]** he renews contact with **Mikuláš
  Drabík**: the pool tagged this [A], but sk.wikipedia's article mentions neither man.
- **1654 [A]** leaves after conflict with rector János Tolnai Dali over authority and curriculum.
- **Recorded, not used**: the pool asserts he helped arrange the 1651 marriage of Sigismund Rákóczi to
  Henriette Marie of the Palatinate, and concedes it "was not independently refetched from a primary
  source". Load-bearing for a Palatinate thread, so logged and kept out of the campas.

## 6. Leszno burned, Amsterdam, death, 1654-1670

- **1654 [R]** petitions Oliver Cromwell for money and troops toward an England-Sweden-Transylvania
  alignment against the Habsburgs; **1655 [R]** greets Charles X Gustav's invasion of Poland in print
  with *Panegyricus Carolo Gustavo*. Combined into one stop, dated 1655.
- **28-29 April 1656 [A]** Polish forces and local insurgents burn Leszno; he escapes and loses house,
  library, the Brethren archive, printing resources, and much of the Czech-Latin *Thesaurus*. **That he
  personally caused the burning is not established.** The campa's "the fire is answering something he
  had done" means the town's collaboration and his endorsement of it in print, not arson.
- **1656 [A]** Laurens de Geer brings him to Amsterdam; the city council grants a regular income and
  printing subsidies. **1657 [A]** *Opera didactica omnia*, four parts, work of 1627-1657, dedicated to
  that council; digitized at `mateo.uni-mannheim.de`.
- **1658 [A]** *Orbis sensualium pictus*, Nuremberg, Latin-German. **[C] on the illustration medium**:
  the pool says woodcut, en.wikipedia says copperplate across 150 chapters, and **the file states
  neither**. The "first children's picture book" claim is given in `date_confidence` as the pool's own
  hedge: among the earliest systematic illustrated textbooks for children, not an uncontested first.
  Editions [A, en.wikipedia]: Hoole's English 1659, quadrilingual 1666, Breuer's Levoča 1685,
  Latin-German-Hungarian 1708, twelfth London edition 1810, also the first American.
- **1667 [R]** distributes *Angelus pacis* at the Breda talks; no formal diplomatic authority.
  **1669 [R]** *Admonitio fraterna* and its 1670 *Continuatio*, preserving one of the fullest surviving
  autobiographical accounts of his career; **not staged as a stop**, for want of budget.
- **15 November 1670 [A]** dies at Amsterdam, aged 78. **No reliable cause of death in the principal
  sources.** **22 November 1670 [A]** buried in the Walloon Reformed chapel at Naarden; grave location
  later uncertain; remains officially reinterred 25 July 1929; chapel transferred to Czechoslovakia
  1933 and rebuilt 1933-1937 as the Comenius Mausoleum by Ladislav Machoň, with glass, metalwork, and
  carving by Jaroslav Benda, Jaroslav Horejc, and Karel Štipl (nl.wikipedia).

## Interlocks

**Named in a campa**, both rooted in Comenius's own relations:

1. **`rene_descartes`**, Endegeest, July 1642. A real meeting, attested by the museum, corroborated by
   encyclopedia.com. Coordinates **52.1697, 4.4644** inherited byte-identical from
   `rene_descartes.journey.json`'s own Endegeest stop (1641-1643), over the pool's 52.1697, 4.4611.
   The later dueling-dialogue versions are invention and are disclaimed in the campa.
2. **`ramon_llull`**, Herborn, 1611. Indirect: Alsted transmits Llull's combinatorial art into the
   method Comenius takes from him. **[R]**, labeled so in `date_confidence`, because the pool's own
   lens "could not independently confirm Alsted's Llull/Bruno interest beyond the dossier's assertion".
   Alsted's *Clavis artis Lullianae* (1609) is the suggested ref and the anchor for confirming it.

**Cited as a ref, not staged as a scene:** **Frances Yates**, whose *Rosicrucian Enlightenment* (1972)
treats the Palatine court and the aftermath of White Mountain, the catastrophe Comenius served as
prophecy-courier for at The Hague in 1626. Three centuries apart, so the relation sits as a
**suggested_ref** on his own Hague stop, named in no campa.

**Dropped:** **`giordano_bruno`** (via Alsted, the same [R] chain as Llull but weaker; via his 1588
Prague dedication to Rudolf II, shared geography only), **`john_dee`** (shared city, two generations
early), **`johannes_kepler`** (shared Prague and Rudolfine court, died 1630, never met, never cited),
and **`kircher`** ("weak/speculative" by the pool's own lens, with a negative grep). **White Mountain
gets no stop of its own**: Comenius was at Fulnek, not at the battle, so it is named as cause inside
the Fulnek sack campa with the battlefield as a suggested_ref. **Rembrandt's *Portrait of an Old Man***
(1665, Uffizi) is only probably Comenius; wga.hu says "probably", and no `rembrandt.journey.json`
exists to hold it.

**Canonical pins**: Kaaba, Temple Mount, Buenos Aires, and Paris do not apply, and the pool's own lens
says so. **Amsterdam is the one inherited: 52.3676, 4.9041**, byte-identical, on all three Amsterdam
stops.

## Apparatus relations for the operator

**Comenius is NOT listed in `EXCEPTIONS.md`.** One relation is real and not rooted in his own life. It
was kept out of the journey file rather than silently deleted, and is flagged here.

- **Direction caught: FORWARD.** Not licensable by an agent, and not by the backward clause even if
  Comenius were added to the list.
- **What it is**: `QUEUE.md` (line 158) enters Comenius as the **earliest root of the pedagogy wing**,
  which holds `pestalozzi` (b. 1746), `froebel` (b. 1782), `maria_montessori` (b. 1870), and
  `john_dewey` (b. 1859). All four are downstream of him and none crosses his lifetime. A grep confirms
  **no pedagogy-wing file mentions Comenius by name**, so the interlock exists in neither direction.
- **Why it is not in the campas**: writing any of the four into a 1670 file is a 2026 hash in an old
  block, which `EXCEPTIONS.md` calls "not a thing that can be written". Forward grants there are
  subject-by-subject from the author directly, and there is no Comenius grant.
- **The legal move is the reverse one.** Each later file may embed Comenius, an old hash. If the wing
  is to be wired together, the edit belongs in `pestalozzi`, `froebel`, `maria_montessori`, and
  `john_dewey`, naming him as their root. This pass touched only the two Comenius artifacts.

No **backward** apparatus relation was found: Comenius has no reading instrument of the kind
`EXCEPTIONS.md` contemplates, his pansophy being a program rather than a lens.

## The Durant quotation: dropped

The pool's "We are all citizens of the world, we are all of one blood... let us put aside all
selfishness in the consideration of language, nationality, or religion" is **not fabricated**: English
Wikipedia footnotes it to Will and Ariel Durant, *The Age of Reason Begins* (1961), p. 582. But the
wording is **misleadingly elided**, the ellipsis dropping the clause carrying the argument ("To hate a
man because he was born in another country... is a great folly"), and **no primary Comenius work could
be named**: tracing it to *Panorthosia*, *Angelus pacis*, or *Via lucis* failed when the WebSearch
budget hit 200/200; Wikiquote does not carry it. **Dropped entirely.** Any future use requires "as
quoted by Will and Ariel Durant" on its face and the full wording restored.

All ten `quote` fields are verbatim from Keatinge's 1896 *Great Didactic*, Lützow's 1901 *Labyrinth*,
and the English *Orbis Sensualium Pictus* preface. Four are **chapter headings**, and say so.

## Honest gaps

Gaps, not resolved: **one institution carries the life** (remove `mjakub.cz` and most of the itinerary
loses its only source); **the birthplace**, which the custodian says cannot be resolved; **where his
first family died**, deaths attested and location not; **who burned the Fulnek books**; **the last
night in Bohemia**, two towns and two traditions, one explicitly uncited; **the Slovak itinerary**, a
list and not a route; the **Orbis Pictus illustration medium**, left unstated; the origin of the name
**"Amos"**, unestablished in anything reached and so not asserted in the file; and the **1669-1670
*Admonitio fraterna***, which gets no stop for want of budget. Two items need spelling out.

1. **The 1934 Halle rediscovery of the *Consultatio catholica*** and a **UNESCO Memory of the World
   inscription**, both in the original dossier, are neither confirmable: the UNESCO page did not
   resolve to confirming content, cs.wikipedia's *Obecná porada* carries no rediscovery history, and
   en.wikipedia on Dmytro Chyzhevsky, usually credited with the find, does not mention it. **The 1966
   Prague publication IS confirmed** (Academia; Czech translation, Svoboda, 1992), and only that is
   asserted. No Halle stop.
2. **The 1892 tercentenary**, split rather than conflated. Comenius Hall at Moravian College was built
   in 1892 and named for him (en.wikipedia), and the Naarden museum was founded in 1892 on the
   tercentenary (nl.wikipedia). But the Moravian page connects its building to no tercentenary, and the
   1892 founding of the Comenian Society is uncorroborated. A coincidence of dates until documented
   otherwise, and none of it is in the journey file.
