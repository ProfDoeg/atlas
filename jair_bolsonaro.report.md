# Jair Bolsonaro: research report
*Compiled 2026-08-11 for the Atlas of Journeys. Slug `jair_bolsonaro`. Subject alive at compilation (b. 21 March 1955), under house arrest on health grounds since 24 March 2026 after conviction and imprisonment for a coup plot.*

**Legend.** **[A]** = attested, source named · **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition, or a claim not tied to a primary or corroborated source. Contradictions are flagged and left standing, and the flags are carried into the journey file's `date_confidence` fields.

**Method and its limits.** The evidence pool is overwhelmingly encyclopedic: English and Portuguese Wikipedia, Portuguese Wikiquote, the English articles on the attempted assassination and the 2023 Congress attack, and Portuguese Wikipedia on Ustra's *A Verdade Sufocada*. **No court filing, no Chamber session record, no Federal Police document and no newspaper archive was fetched directly.** Where the two Wikipedias disagree, both readings are printed. Coordinates were checked against Wikipedia infobox figures or OpenStreetMap/Nominatim. For a life this consequential the base is thin, and the thinness is the most important thing to say about this file.

---

## 1. Origins and childhood, 1955-1972

**[A, EN/PT]** Born 21 March 1955 in Glicério, interior São Paulo state, to Percy Geraldo Bolsonaro and Olinda Bonturi; family of Italian (Veneto, Calabria) and German descent. Six children; five siblings named.

**Contradiction flagged and then withdrawn.** The pool's geography lens tagged the birthplace **[R]** because Glicério is far from the Vale do Ribeira towns "documented in the same years" and from Campinas. This does not survive checking: those residences are 1964-66, nine to eleven years **after** the birth, and Campinas is a late civil registration, not a rival birthplace. **No source disputes Glicério.** Corrected to **[A]**, and the false contradiction recorded so it is not reinvented.

**[A, PT]** Birth registered in **Campinas on 1 February 1956**, nearly eleven months late. The Portuguese article notes the gap and nothing explains it. Recorded as an oddity, not resolved.

**[A, PT]** 1964: family moves to **Jundiaí**, where Percy practises dentistry without a formal licence. 1965: **Sete Barras**. **[A, EN/PT]** 1966: settles at **Eldorado**. Friends there nickname him **"Palmito"**, which shortens over time to **"Mito"** (myth), later adopted by his movement as a title.

**Gap.** Nothing reachable documents the household's income, the schools attended, or the reason for the moves beyond "dental and prospecting work".

## 2. The army, 1973-1988

**[A, EN/PT]** 1973: enters the **Escola Preparatória de Cadetes do Exército**, Campinas. 1974: **AMAN**, Resende; commissioned in artillery **1977**. 1978: marries Rogéria Nantes Braga, first of three wives.

**Posting order: contradiction flagged, not adjudicated.** English Wikipedia sends him from AMAN to **Nioaque (MS)**, then the physical training school in Rio, then the 21st and 8th parachutist groups. Portuguese Wikipedia inverts the first two and dates each: **21º GAC, São Cristóvão, Rio (1977-78)**, **9º GAC, Nioaque (1979-81)**, **EsEFEx, Urca (1982)**, **8º GAC Paraquedista, Deodoro (1982-88)**. The journey file follows the Portuguese order, as the more detailed and internally dated, and says so in `date_confidence`.

### The Veja affair, 1986-1988

**[A, EN/PT]** 1986: publishes an article in *Veja* on low officers' pay; arrested and held **fifteen days**. **[A, PT]** October 1987: *Veja* publishes **"Operação Beco Sem Saída"**, a plan to place low-power bombs in barracks lavatories at Vila Militar and AMAN and at the **Guandu adutora** (water main). Bolsonaro tipped off the reporter **Cássia Maria** and was himself accused of drawing the sketch of the placements. Three corrections to the pool: the target was the **adutora**, not the treatment station; he was **source and accused draughtsman**, the Federal Police later matching the sketch's handwriting to his; and the day **27 October** is uncorroborated.

**[A, EN/PT] Date correction.** The pool anchored the acquittal to October 1987; the **Superior Tribunal Militar** decided in **June 1988**. **Vote: contradiction flagged.** EN: acquitted **9-4**. PT: the court absolved Bolsonaro and Captain **Fábio Passos da Silva unanimously**. Left standing. December 1988: leaves the army, retiring as **captain**.

**Gap.** No reachable source establishes that the acquittal ended his prospects of promotion, though every account treats the two as connected. That causal link is **[R]** and is not asserted as fact.

## 3. Councilman and deputy, 1988-2017

**[A, EN/PT]** 1988: elected city councillor of Rio for the **Christian Democratic Party**; serves 1989-1991, described in contemporary accounts as quiet, discreet, conservative. 1990: elected federal deputy for Rio de Janeiro state; **seven consecutive terms, 1991-2018**. October 2014: roughly **465,000 votes**, the most of any federal-deputy candidate in the state. **Family:** father Percy dies 1995; marries Ana Cristina Valle 1997 (divorced 2007) and Michelle de Paula 2007; mother Olinda dies 21 January 2022, aged 94.

### The record of what he said

All quotations are as reproduced on **pt.Wikiquote**, which cites the underlying record in each case. The *Diário da Câmara*, the tapes and the newspaper originals were not fetched; the chain runs through Wikiquote and is **[A]** only that far.

- **1991, *Tribuna da Imprensa*:** "O Brasil tem que ter a bomba atômica; é preciso acabar com esse tabu." **[A]**
- **24 June 1993, tribune of the Chamber:** "Sou a favor de uma ditadura, de um regime de exceção, desde que esse Congresso dê mais um passo rumo ao abismo, que está muito próximo." **[A]**. *Correction:* the pool truncated this without an ellipsis; restored.
- **25 July 1993, *The New York Times*:** "Eu simpatizo com o Fujimori. A Fujimorização é a saída para o Brasil." **[A]**. *Correction:* the pool gave the verb as "Eu defendo" and the source as *Folha*, 25 June 1993; both wrong, that Folha page carrying a different quotation. **Not used in the journey file**, the stop having been cut for length.
- **2 December 1998, *Veja*:** "Pinochet devia ter matado mais gente." **[A]**
- **21 May 1999, *Câmera Aberta*, TV Bandeirantes:** "Não há a menor dúvida. Eu daria golpe no mesmo dia." **[A]**
- **16 June 1999:** "É o que dá torturar e não matar. Se a ditadura tivesse matado muita gente no passado, teria melhorado." **[A]**
- **28 December 1999, Rio:** "Para o crime que ele (FHC) está cometendo contra o país, sua pena devia ser o fuzilamento." **[A]**
- **19 May 2002, *Folha de S.Paulo*:** "Não vou combater nem discriminar, mas, se eu vir dois homens se beijando na rua, vou bater." **[A]**
- **31 March 2004, Chamber, fortieth anniversary of the 1964 coup, delivered kneeling:** "Os brasileiros devem homenagear os militares que, nesta data que deve ser comemorada, evitaram - repito - fosse instalada em nosso País ditadura totalitária de esquerda, cujo resultado seria a implantação de regime semelhante ao de Cuba e, para todos nós, o paredão ou a plantação de cana." **[A]**. *Correction:* the pool deleted "- repito -" from inside the span and cut after "de esquerda"; restored.
- **9 August 2018, TV Bandeirantes debate:** "Brasil acima de tudo, Deus acima de todos." **[A]**
- **"Yes, I'm homophobic, and very proud of it", attributed to a 2013 interview: [R].** No primary text, Portuguese wording, or outlet traceable. **Not used.**
- **28 April 2020, on the COVID death toll: "E daí? Lamento. Quer que eu faça o quê?" [R].** Reported contemporaneously by Folha and G1; no primary transcript fetched. **Used in the journey file with [R] stated in `date_confidence`.**

**[A, PT, *A Verdade Sufocada*]** Colonel **Carlos Alberto Brilhante Ustra's** memoir defending the DOI-CODI apparatus reaches fourteen editions and becomes a bestseller after Bolsonaro praises it; in **2016** he dedicates his vote to impeach Dilma Rousseff to Ustra's memory. **Gap:** no Bolsonaro-authored book, memoir or translated title could be confirmed to exist, so the atlas's usual "the book and its editions" material attaches to **Ustra's** book, not his own.

## 4. The 2018 campaign

**[A, EN]** January 2018: leaves the Social Christian Party for the **PSL**; nominated 22 July; certified 6 August.

**[A, EN/PT and "Attempted assassination"]** **6 September 2018:** stabbed in the abdomen by **Adélio Bispo de Oliveira** while carried on supporters' shoulders in Juiz de Fora; the attacker later says he was on "a mission from God". Liver, lung and intestine reached; 40-50% of blood volume lost; four surgeries; arrival in severe hypotension.

**Location: contradiction flagged, resolved to the more specific source.** The pool's geography lens named **Praça Antônio Carlos** (-21.7642, -43.3503), its afterlife lens **Rua Halfeld** (-21.7614, -43.3496), PT Wikipedia the corner of **Batista de Oliveira and Halfeld** at about 15:25; and neither pool figure matches Praça Presidente Antônio Carlos (-21.7621, -43.3437). The journey file uses the Halfeld corner, **-21.7600, -43.3460**, recording the ~400 m disagreement in `date_confidence`.

**[A, EN/PT]** Airlifted to the **Albert Einstein**, Morumbi; discharged **29 September**; campaigns by video from the hospital. **7 October:** first round, about 46% (49.2 million votes), followed by a speech threatening to jail "reds" and "petralhas" and invoking a Navy facility used for torture under the dictatorship. **28 October:** wins the run-off with **55.13%**.

**[A/R, general press record]** Iconography: the **"arminha"** pistol gesture, the yellow-green football shirt, the nickname **"Bozo"**. The pool marks these [A] on prior general knowledge but flags them **not freshly sourced**; the journey file uses them only as description of crowds, never as a dated claim. **[R]** Duelling conspiracy theories about the stabbing circulated widely; Federal Police investigations consistently concluded Adélio acted alone. The official conclusion is **[A]**, the folk narratives **[R]**, and the latter are not repeated.

## 5. The presidency, 2019-2022

**[A, EN/PT]** **1 January 2019:** sworn in as the 38th President, succeeding Michel Temer. Cabinet: Guedes, Moro, Pontes, Tereza Cristina. *Venue correction:* the pool placed the swearing-in at the **Planalto**; the oath is taken before a **joint session of Congress**, after which the president crosses to the Planalto for the sash. The colostomy-bag detail is correct; reversal surgery January 2019.

**[A, EN]** 2019: **FUNAI** stripped of authority to demarcate indigenous land. 21 May: Datafolha shows 36.2% disapproval, the first time disapproval exceeds approval. November: leaves the PSL; the new party, **Alliance for Brazil**, fails to gather the required signatures. **Foreign travel [A, EN, list of presidential trips]:** Washington 18-20 March 2019, Trump on the 19th, Brazil named a **major non-NATO ally** (*correction:* the pool called this a state visit; Wikipedia records a **working visit**); **La Moneda, 22 March**, launching **Prosur**; **Jerusalem, 1 April**, Western Wall **[A]**, Yad Vashem **[R]**, the "controversial remarks from his circle" unsourced to a speaker or date and not repeated; **G20 Osaka, 28-29 June**; **UN General Assembly, 24 September**; **Budapest, February 2022**, Orbán; **the Kremlin, 16 February 2022**, Putin, days before the invasion of Ukraine, declaring "solidarity".

**[A, EN]** COVID-19: minimises the pandemic, calling the virus no deadlier than "the flu" and at one point a media "fantasy". **March 2021:** health minister **Eduardo Pazuello** replaced by **Marcelo Queiroga** (23 March); **defence** minister **Fernando Azevedo e Silva** dismissed 29 March. *Correction:* the pool called Azevedo the health minister, misreading the English article. **July 2021:** the **Covaxin** scandal, roughly ten times the originally negotiated price. **2020:** the **OCCRP** names him its Person of the Year. **2022:** loses the presidential election to Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva. *Date note:* the pool gives only the year; the run-off was 30 October 2022, and the journey file uses that date while recording in `date_confidence` that the day was not verified from the pool.

## 6. Exile, insurrection, prosecution, 2022-2026

**[A, EN; 2023 Congress attack article]** Flies to Florida **30 December 2022**, two days before his term ends, and stays near Orlando until **30 March 2023**, skipping the handover of the sash. *Correction:* the pool dated the departure 30 January 2023; the Congress-attack article says he had been in Orlando "since the last days of 2022, even before the end of his term". **Gap:** the rented address was never made public, so the coordinate approximates the reported Kissimmee neighbourhood. Hospitalised at AdventHealth Celebration in a January-February 2023 window, no day given.

**[A, EN/PT]** **8 January 2023:** supporters storm the Congress, Supreme Court and Planalto on the **Praça dos Três Poderes**, calling for a military coup, days after Lula's inauguration; Bolsonaro is in the United States. **30 March:** returns, landing at Brasília International Airport. **30 June:** the **Superior Electoral Court** bars him from office until **2030** for abusing government communication channels and seeking to undermine confidence in the electronic voting system. **Gap:** no verified coordinate for the TSE building is anywhere in the pool, so that stop is pinned at the canonical Brasília coordinate from `augusto_boal.journey.json` rather than an invented address.

**[A, EN]** **November 2024:** indicted by the Federal Police over the alleged plot to overturn the 2022 result. *Two-step correction:* the prosecutor-general **charged** him **18 February 2025**, the STF First Panel **accepted the charge** on **26 March 2025**, making him a defendant obliged to stand trial, and the pool collapsed both into "February 2025". **4 August 2025:** house arrest, for using a mobile phone and reaching social media through his son Eduardo in breach of pre-trial restrictions. **2 September:** trial opens before the **Supremo Tribunal Federal**. **11 September:** convicted of attempted violent abolition of the democratic rule of law, attempted coup, and participation in an armed criminal organisation; sentenced to **27 years and 3 months**.

**[A/R] Tag conflict inside the pool.** **22 November 2025:** arrested after allegedly attempting to remove his ankle monitor; the chronology lens tags this **[A]**, the geography lens **[R]**, and the split is recorded, not resolved. **[A, EN] 25 November:** final appeals rejected, case declared *res judicata*, sentence ordered enforced. **[A, PT] January 2026:** transferred to the **"Papudinha"**, the special-treatment battalion (officially the 19º Batalhão de Polícia Militar do DF) inside the **Complexo Penitenciário da Papuda**. *Source correction:* the pool cited English Wikipedia, which **omits the transfer entirely**; the claim is carried only by the Portuguese article and is cited to it.

**[A, EN] Tag correction.** **24 March 2026:** granted 90 days of house arrest on health grounds, later extended. The chronology lens tagged this **[R]** as post-cutoff while the geography lens tagged the identical event **[A]**; the English article states it verbatim, so it is harmonised to **[A]**.

**[A, EN, "Attempted assassination"]** The knife of 2018 is held by the Federal Police and displayed in the corporation's museum in Brasília. Mentioned in a campa, not given its own stop. **[R] Gap, stated as a gap.** No statue, monument or memorial could be confirmed to exist, and he is alive, so there is no tomb and none of the atlas's usual "tomb and its fate" material applies. The pool's searches found nothing, but the budget was exhausted before an exhaustive check: **absence of evidence is not certified here as evidence of absence.**
