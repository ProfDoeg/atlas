# Erwin Schrödinger (1887-1961): research report

*Compiled 2026-08-23 for `erwin_schrodinger.journey.json`. 45 stops, 8 segments, calendar
gregorian, register "national mythology: the canon is true".*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named inline. **[R]** reconstruction or uncorroborated
lead. Contradictions are flagged and **not** adjudicated. Where the research pool carried an
error of coordinate, date, venue or attribution, the correction is stated here and the
journey file carries the corrected value with a note in `date_confidence`.

Load-bearing sources: the **MacTutor** biography
(mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Schrodinger/) and the English, German and
topic-specific **Wikipedia** articles, all fetched. **Walter Moore, *Schrödinger: Life and
Thought* (1989)**, the standing biography and ultimate source of the diary material, was
**not opened** here, so everything resting on Moore arrives at one remove.
**nobelprize.org returned HTTP 403** and could not reconfirm the Nobel lecture.

---

## 1. Vienna, 1887-1913

- Born **12 August 1887, Vienna-Erdberg**, only child of **Rudolf Schrödinger**, linoleum
  manufacturer and amateur botanist, and **Georgine Emilia ("Emily") Bauer**. **[A:
  de.wikipedia, corroborated MacTutor]**
- Tutored at home to about age ten, then the **Akademisches Gymnasium, 1898-1906**. **[A:
  MacTutor]**
- **University of Vienna from 1906** under **Franz Exner** and **Friedrich Hasenöhrl**, both
  carriers of Boltzmann's statistical tradition. **Doctorate 20 May 1910**, on conduction of
  electricity over insulator surfaces in moist air. **Exner's assistant from 1911**; **first
  significant paper 1914**, extending Boltzmann. **[A: MacTutor]**
- **1913, Seeham (Salzburg):** atmospheric-radioactivity measurements with Fritz Kohlrausch.
  **[A: en.wikipedia, which misplaces Seeham in Switzerland]**

**Correction (coordinate).** Pool gave Seeham as 47.9167, 13.0667. En.wikipedia (*Seeham*)
gives **47.9667, 13.0667**; the pool value sits 5.6 km south of the village, in open country
below the Trumer lakes. File uses the corrected pair.

**Gaps.** No address for the Erdberg birth house or the family works; the birth coordinate is
the district. The 1911 assistantship is carried in the doctorate stop's campa.

## 2. The war and the lost fortune, 1914-1919

- **Habilitation dated 1 September 1914** **[A: MacTutor]**, five weeks after the
  declaration of war.
- Officer of **Austro-Hungarian fortress artillery**. MacTutor attests only "Italian border
  (1914-1915), transferred to Hungary (1915), returned to Italian front", plus command of a
  battery. **[A in general terms only]**
- The four toponyms in the dossier lead, **Gorizia, Duino, Sistiana, Prosecco**, are
  corroborated by nothing reached. **[R]** The file keeps one Isonzo-sector stop at Gorizia
  whose `date_confidence` says the place is an uncorroborated lead. The pool's geography lens
  tagged this **[A]** while its own source note said **[R]**; that internal contradiction is
  resolved here in favour of **[R]**. Italy also entered the war only in May 1915, so 1914
  service was on the border, not an active front, and the stop is dated 1915-06.
- **Spring 1917:** returned to Vienna, taught meteorology to officers. **[A: MacTutor]**
- **1919:** **Rudolf Schrödinger dies** as postwar inflation erodes the linoleum fortune.
  **[A: en.wikipedia]** No date or address anywhere reached; no stop of its own, carried in
  the Jena stop.

## 3. Marriage and the five chairs, 1920-1921

- Marries **Annemarie ("Anny") Bertel**. **Sources conflict, unreconciled:** MacTutor gives
  **24 March 1920** (engagement 1919), en.wikipedia **6 April 1920**; both agree on the year
  and her Salzburg background. No venue established, so the coordinate marks the region.
- **1920:** **Haitinger Prize**; assistantship under **Max Wien at Jena**; **extraordinary
  professor at Stuttgart from September**. **1921:** full chair at **Breslau**, then the
  Zürich chair accepted late in the year; **mother Emily dies**. **[A: MacTutor]**

**Correction (coordinate).** Pool gave Stuttgart as 48.7758, 9.1097, which lands in the
Botnang woods 5 km west of any university site. En.wikipedia (*University of Stuttgart*)
gives **48.7817, 9.1753**, the Keplerstraße campus of the former Technische Hochschule. File
uses the corrected pair.

## 4. Zürich, 1921-1927

- **November 1925:** reads **de Broglie's** thesis, writes to **Einstein** within days. **[A:
  MacTutor, which quotes the letter]**
- **Christmas and New Year 1925-26, Arosa**, Anny left in Zürich, an **unidentified
  companion** present; the wave equation is developed in those weeks. **[A as to holiday and
  timing: en.wikipedia; MacTutor gives "developed wave equation within weeks" of the de
  Broglie reading]**. The Arosa detail and the companion descend from **Moore, not opened
  here**. The pool's "Villa Herwig" address is uncorroborated and is not used.
- **1926:** four installments of **"Quantisierung als Eigenwertproblem"** in *Annalen der
  Physik*, plus the *Physical Review* paper and the equivalence papers against
  Heisenberg-Born-Jordan matrix mechanics. **[A: MacTutor, en.wikipedia]**
- **Autumn 1926, Copenhagen:** the Bohr argument, Bohr pressing him to his sickbed with
  Heisenberg continuing at the bedside. **[A: heisenberg.journey.json]** That file's text says
  autumn 1926 while its own date field reads 1926-05-01, Heisenberg's return to Copenhagen.
  **Discrepancy flagged, not resolved**; this file dates the stop 1926-10 and says so.
- **January 1927:** visiting lecturer at the **University of Wisconsin, Madison**. **[A:
  MacTutor]** **Deliberately given no stop:** no source reached supplies a coordinate, and a
  fabricated campus pin for a one-month visit was judged worse than an omission.

### Conduct: Itha Junger

Around **1926-27**, aged 39, Schrödinger began **tutoring Itha ("Ithi") Junger, then
fourteen**. The tutoring became a sexual relationship. **[A: en.wikipedia, summarizing Moore
on Schrödinger's own diaries]**

**Her age when it became sexual is disputed and is not resolved here.** Moore, per
en.wikipedia, gives **seventeen**; **de.wikipedia reports Junger's own account placing it at
nineteen** and notes sources conflicting between 17 and 20. The stop carries both figures.
The **[R]** tag attaches to the age, not to the fact.

**Not included, for want of a source in the verified pool:** a reported pregnancy and
abortion. Left out rather than asserted, and recorded as a gap for whoever next works this
file with Moore in hand.

## 5. Berlin and Oxford, 1927-1936

- **1 October 1927:** takes **Planck's chair** in Berlin; **Matteucci Medal** the same year.
  **[A: MacTutor]**
- **24 October 1927, Brussels, Fifth Solvay Conference.** Attested by **three journey files
  independently** (dirac, einstein, wolfgang_pauli). **[A]** **Coordinate conflict across the
  atlas, flagged not resolved:** dirac and einstein give **50.8466, 4.3528**; wolfgang_pauli
  50.8175, 4.3528; niels_bohr 50.841, 4.379. This file inherits the first byte-identical.
- **1932, Berlin:** **von Neumann's** *Mathematische Grundlagen der Quantenmechanik*
  formalizes the equivalence, "one theory written twice". **[A: john_von_neumann]**
  Coordinate 52.5169, 13.3934 inherited byte-identical.
- **1933:** leaves Nazi Germany although not a target of the racial laws; **arrives Oxford 4
  November 1933**, Fellow of **Magdalen College**, placed by **Lindemann**. **[A: MacTutor]**
- **10 December 1933, Stockholm:** Nobel Prize for 1933 shared with **Dirac**, at the ceremony
  that also conferred **Heisenberg's** delayed 1932 prize. **[A: dirac and heisenberg files,
  mutually corroborating; nobelprize.org unreachable]** Coordinate 59.333, 18.064 inherited
  byte-identical from both.
- **30 May 1934:** **Ruth Georgie Erica March** born in Oxford to **Hildegunde March**, wife
  of Arthur March, the three adults keeping one household **[A: MacTutor for date and place;
  en.wikipedia for the household]**; the same year he lectures at **Princeton** and declines
  the anticipated permanent offer, for reasons that are the secondary literature's inference
  and are presented as such. **[A: MacTutor, en.wikipedia]**
- **1935:** three-part **"Die gegenwärtige Situation in der Quantenmechanik"**, answering
  **Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen**, coining **Verschränkung**, staging the cat as a reductio.
  **[A]** Text not re-fetched; the cat passage is quoted from **Trimmer's 1980 translation via
  Wikipedia**.

## 6. Graz and the flight, 1936-1939

- **1936:** chair at **Graz**, after an Edinburgh offer stalled on a work permit. **1938:**
  following the March Anschluss he publishes a **conciliatory statement** toward the regime,
  hoping to keep the post, and is **dismissed on 26 August for "political unreliability"**
  regardless. **[A: MacTutor; en.wikipedia for the statement, whose text MacTutor quotes]**
  **Gap:** nothing reached gives the statement's date or names the newspaper, so its stop is
  dated 1938-04 only to order it before the dismissal.
- **September 1938:** flees via Italy, **Rome**. **1938-1939:** visiting professorship at
  **Ghent** on the **Fondation Francqui**. **[A: MacTutor]** **1939:** visas for Anny and for
  Hilde March after a personal invitation from **Taoiseach Éamon de Valera**. **[A:
  en.wikipedia]**

## 7. Dublin, 1939-1955/56

- **October 1939:** arrives in Dublin **[A: MacTutor]**.
- **DIAS, two dates distinguished.** The Act was signed **19 June 1940**, but Schrödinger
  **"moved into 65 Merrion Square in February 1941"**. **[A: en.wikipedia, *Dublin Institute
  for Advanced Studies*]** The pool conflated the legislative founding with his working
  presence; the file dates the stop **1941-02**.
- **Residence: Kincora Road, Clontarf**, plaque-marked, **no house number** anywhere reached.
  **[A as to street: en.wikipedia]** **Pool contradiction resolved:** 53.3611, -6.1972 against
  53.3644, -6.2039 for one street, 550 m apart; this file uses the second throughout.
- **Summer 1942:** **Dirac visits him in Dublin**, nine years after the shared prize. **[A:
  dirac.journey.json]** That file places DIAS on **Burlington Road** (53.3305, -6.2436) while
  other sources place it at Merrion Square in the same years. **Discrepancy flagged, not
  resolved**; the visit stop inherits the Burlington Road pin byte-identical.
- **February 1943:** the Trinity lectures that become **What Is Life?** (Cambridge, 1944),
  the gene as an **"aperiodic crystal"**. **[A: en.wikipedia, *What Is Life?*; MacTutor]**
  Influence on Watson, Crick and Wilkins attested by MacTutor, the **1953 Crick letter held
  at DIAS** by en.wikipedia; both carried as sources on Schrödinger's own stop, no later
  reader staged as a scene (see §10). **Coordinate conflict in the pool:** 53.3441, -6.2577
  against 53.3438, -6.2546; this file uses the second for every Trinity stop. **Gap:** the
  hall is nowhere named and the audience figures in circulation were not corroborated.
- **1947: "Affine Field Theory". Correction (venue and framing).** The pool called this a
  "Dublin press conference" pinned at Merrion Square. It was an announcement to the **Royal
  Irish Academy, 19 Dawson Street** (about 53.3413, -6.2588), de Valera present, carried by
  the Irish press as news; **Einstein** objected publicly that the claim was premature. **[A:
  en.wikipedia; MacTutor for the quotation]** The 1947 newspaper text was not fetched.
- **1948:** naturalized **Irish citizen**, keeping Austrian nationality **[A: MacTutor and
  en.wikipedia]**, carried in the **1954** stop for *Nature and the Greeks* **[A: MacTutor]**,
  which the pool placed ambiguously "Vienna/Dublin" and which is pinned in Dublin here, where
  he was working. An editorial decision, not an attestation.
- **Retirement, 1955 or 1956: genuine conflict, not resolved.** En.wikipedia has the post
  "held until his retirement in 1955"; MacTutor "he retired in 1956 when he returned to
  Vienna". The stop is dated 1955 and names both.

## 8. Vienna again, and Alpbach, 1956-1961

- **1956:** returns to a **specially created chair** at Vienna and receives the **first Erwin
  Schrödinger Prize**. **[A: MacTutor]**
- **1958: *Mind and Matter*. Correction (who delivered the lectures).** The book derives from
  the **1956 Tarner Lectures at Cambridge**, which Schrödinger **did not deliver**: the list
  of Tarner Lecturers gives 1956 as "Professor Erwin Schrödinger (read by Professor J.
  Wisdom)", title "The physical basis of consciousness". He was too ill to travel. **[A:
  en.wikipedia, *Tarner Lectures*]** The reading happened where he was not, so the stop stands
  at **Vienna**, with Cambridge in `suggested_refs`.
- **4 January 1961:** dies of **tuberculosis** in **Vienna-Alsergrund**, aged 73 **[A:
  de.wikipedia; MacTutor corroborates]**; *Meine Weltansicht* appears the same year, the
  English *My View of the World* (trans. Hastings) posthumously in 1964 **[A: MacTutor;
  Wikiquote]**, carried in the death stop rather than given one of its own.
- **Buried at Alpbach, Tyrol**, in Catholic ground, permitted on account of his membership
  of the **Pontifical Academy of Sciences** though he was not a practising Catholic; the
  headstone carries the **equation**. **[A: en.wikipedia, de.wikipedia; MacTutor's grave
  photograph caption reads "Note the wave equation!"]**
  **Correction (coordinate).** The pool held **three** values for one grave: 47.3833/11.9333
  (geography lens), 47.3897/11.9358 (afterlife lens), and en.wikipedia's village coordinate
  **47.3985, 11.9439**. The first two are 1.9 km and 1.2 km off. File uses the third.

## 9. Posthumous record

All **[A: en.wikipedia]**: the **bust in the Arkadenhof**; the **1000-schilling note,
1983-1997**; the **lunar far-side crater**; the **Erwin Schrödinger International
Institute**; the two **Dublin plaques**; Trinity's **"What Is Life?" lecture series since
1995**; and the **January 2022** Trinity recommendation to rename the lecture theatre.

**Correction (ESI year).** En.wikipedia says founded 1992; the institute's own site says
"since its opening in 1993". Both recorded, the stop dated 1992, the disagreement named in
`date_confidence`. Its current name ends "for **Mathematics and Physics**".

**Correction (Le Guin and Crease).** The pool dated Le Guin's encounter with the cat to 1974.
En.wikipedia (*Schrödinger's cat*) has her learning of it in **1972** while researching *The
Dispossessed*, with **Crease crediting her 1974 story** for carrying the idea into popular
culture. The stronger pool claim, that the cat is "more prominent in popular culture than in
physics itself", is unsupported by the cited page and has been dropped.

**The conduct material of 2021-22.** The renaming is well attested; the **diary evidence**
behind it (Itha Junger from age fourteen, an infatuation with the then-twelve-year-old
Barbara MacEntee) descends from **Moore** and the **2021 Irish Times investigation**, neither
opened here, so **[A] at one remove**. No charge was brought and no trial took place. All of
it is in the file, stated flat.

## 10. Apparatus relations for the operator

**Schrödinger is NOT listed in `EXCEPTIONS.md`.** No apparatus stop has been written and
nothing silently deleted. Three relations not rooted in his own life were caught, all
**forward**, which the exception never licenses, so each is kept as a source or
suggested_ref on his own stop for the object and never staged as a scene.

1. **Ursula K. Le Guin and the cat**, 1972 encounter, 1974 story: a real one-directional
   engagement with the 1935 thought experiment. A `suggested_ref` on the Oxford cat stop and
   **not named in any campa**, since naming a later traveler would write a hash that did not
   exist when this file's clock ran.
2. **Crick's 1953 letter to Schrödinger**, held at DIAS: inside his lifetime and addressed
   to him, so nearer correspondence than reception. A `suggested_ref` on the 1943 Trinity
   stop; Crick is not staged in Dublin.
3. **The 2022 Trinity renaming and the 1992 Vienna institute.** Posthumous events concerning
   Schrödinger himself, no other traveler in them, given stops at Trinity's and Vienna's own
   pins with `date_confidence` marking them posthumous. If the rule is that a file ends at the
   grave, these two are the ones to cut; flagged rather than assumed.

Nothing was written into another traveler's file.

## 12. Quotations: what was fixed

Eleven of the 45 stops carry a quotation, all verbatim from the pool, each `quote_source`
naming its intermediary rather than implying a primary reading. Three were defective:

1. **"God knows I am no friend of probability theory..."**, cut mid-clause without an
   ellipsis, losing the words naming Born. **Not used** in the file.
2. **Nobel lecture**, "We are never in a position to say what really is...", restored to the
   full sentence ending "in any concrete individual case." Used on the Stockholm stop.
3. **"Consciousness is never experienced in the plural, only in the singular."** attributed
   in the pool to *What Is Life?* (1944). Wikiquote files it there but cites Wilber's
   *Quantum Questions* (1984) as a *translation*, incoherent for a book written in English;
   Wilber's selection is "The Oneness of Mind", chapter 4 of *Mind and Matter* (1958).
   **Quoted nowhere here.**
