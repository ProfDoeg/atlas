# Sabino Arana Goiri (1865-1903), research report
*2026-08-31. Abando, Orduña, Barcelona, Bilbao, Sukarrieta. Founder of Basque nationalism and of the party now called EAJ-PNV; designer, with his brother Luis, of the ikurriña. Written for `sabino_arana.journey.json` (9 segments, 44 stops).*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named in the same sentence · **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or a claim reaching this file through one unverified relay. Contradictions are flagged and **not adjudicated**. The research arrived in four verified lenses (chronology, geography, quotes, afterlife) and **the lenses disagree with each other**, most visibly on the birth date and on where Sukarrieta is. Twelve coordinates in the pool were wrong; each correction, with both values, is stated in the relevant stop's `date_confidence`. The three largest: **Universitat de Barcelona**, 43.3729/-2.1637 → **41.3866, 2.1637**, open water in the Bay of Biscay 500 km off, latitude replaced and longitude sign flipped; **Cárcel de Larrinaga**, on all three of its entries, 43.268/-2.931 → **43.2565, -2.9210**, the pool putting the prison across the ría in Deusto; **Sukarrieta**, on six entries → **43.3961, -2.6950**, three lenses having given three locations for one village.

## 1. Abando, birth, the Carlist household (1865-1872)

**Born 26 January 1865 in Abando** [A: es.wikipedia lead and infobox both read "26 de enero de 1865"; en.wikipedia and eu.wikipedia agree], eighth and youngest child of **Santiago Arana Ansostegi**, shipbuilder and Carlist, and **Josefa Pascuala Goiri** [A: eu.wikipedia]. Baptized **Sabino Policarpo** at the Abando parish of **San Vicente Mártir** [A as to the parish; the register was **not viewed** and only the year is attested].

**Contradiction, flagged.** The chronology lens carried **25 January**, sourced to "institutional biography"; the geography lens carried 26 January. No source fetched supports the 25th, and the file records the disagreement. Corrected coordinates put the **parish 40 m from the birth house**, which is why the two are one stop.

## 2. Exile in the French Basque Country (1872/1873-1876)

Exile follows Santiago Arana's involvement in the Carlist rising [A: es.wikipedia]. **Contradiction, unresolved:** the chronology lens dates the exile to **1872**, the geography lens dates the Hendaye crossing to **1873**; the file pins 1873 and says so.

**Bayonne, Collège Saint-Louis-de-Gonzague, 1873/1874** [A: es.wikipedia]; sources disagree on how much schooling was in classrooms and how much with private tutors, one account naming an Álava Carlist officer as tutor at Saint-Jean-de-Luz [R]. **Saint-Jean-de-Luz** (1874) and **Guéthary** (1873-1876) are both [R], with **no primary document located**; both sit inside the Bayonne campa without pins. **Hondarribia, entrance examination on the return, 1876** [R: dossier chronology only]. The dossier also hedged "Guéthary/Getaria" without resolving it, though these are different towns in different states; recorded as an **unresolved crawl ambiguity**, with only Guéthary named, in prose, and no coordinate.

## 3. Orduña, the sickbed, the conversation with Luis (1876-1882)

**Colegio de Nuestra Señora de la Antigua, Orduña, 1876-1881**, boarding with Luis [A: es.wikipedia, eu.wikipedia, en.wikipedia]. **Bachillerato, June 1881**, examinations reportedly sat while bedridden with tuberculosis [A: es.wikipedia]; a **separate prolonged convalescence at sixteen** is [R], since es.wikipedia documents the examinations but not a distinct 1881-1882 convalescence.

**The conversation with Luis, Easter Sunday, 9 April 1882** [R as to date and setting]. Easter Sunday 1882 did fall on 9 April and eu.wikipedia places the conversation then, but **no source fetched attests the garden**. The pool reports that **José Luis de la Granja** argued the Easter dating and resurrection symbolism were systematized only in 1932, for the first Aberri Eguna; **that argument could not be confirmed from any fetched source** and is carried as an unverified lead.

## 4. Barcelona (1883-1888)

**Death of Santiago Arana, 1883**; **move to Barcelona, irregular enrolment in Law and in Philosophy and Letters, 1883-1888, no degree** [A: es.wikipedia, eu.wikipedia]. **"¿Basco o Vasco?", Revista de Vizcaya, 15 July 1886** [A: es.wikipedia], his first known printed article, opening the controversy with **Miguel de Unamuno** [A as stated in the dossier's own text; **not independently re-verified from a primary source**]. **Pliegos histórico-políticos, Barcelona, 7 April 1888** [A].

**The chair of Vascuence at the Instituto de Bilbao, 1888** [A: es.wikipedia, which gives the vote as **Azkue 11, Unamuno 3, Arana none**]. The pool carried this as an unelaborated en.wikipedia line dated to "the 1890s" and tagged R; verification **upgraded it to A, dated it 1888, and supplied the vote**. Material: he did not merely lose, he received no votes.

**Death of Pascuala Goiri in Barcelona, 1888**; Sabino abandons his studies and returns to Biscay with his sister Paulina [A: es.wikipedia]. **Gap:** the pool does not order the three 1888 events, so two are dated 1888-01-01.

## 5. Bizkaitarra and the founding years (1892-1895)

**Bizkaya por su independencia. Cuatro glorias patrias, 1892** [A: es.wikipedia]. **The Larrazabal address, Begoña, 1893** [A as to the speech: es.wikipedia; **3 June** as the day is eu.wikipedia, R]; the farmhouse plot is **not confirmed**. **First number of Bizkaitarra, June 1893**, thirty-two numbers to its suppression in 1895 [A: es.wikipedia]; the day **8 June** and the printer **Sebastián de Amorrortu** come from sabinoarana.eus [R], and Amorrortu's address is **not given by the source**. **Castejón, the Gamazada, 18 February 1894**, banner reading in substance "God and Old Law, Biscay greets Navarre" [A: eu.wikipedia].

**Euskeldun Batzokija opens at Calle Correo 22, 14 July 1894**; the flag later called the **ikurriña** raised at six in the evening by **Ciriaco de Iturri**, oldest member and former Carlist officer [A: eu.wikipedia, es.wikipedia, en.wikipedia]. Arana elected president of the board on **15 July** after a mass at Begoña [A: es.wikipedia]; the basilica has **no coordinate in the pool**, so the presidency is narrated inside the Batzokija stop. **First Bizkai Buru Batzar, July 1895** [A]; the day **31 July** and the roster of the first executive are pool chronology, **not independently confirmed** [R].

## 6. Larrinaga, the first imprisonment (1895-1896)

**A correction that changes the event, not just the date.** The chronology lens read "Convicted on 10 August 1895." es.wikipedia reads *"El 10 de agosto Sabino comparece en el juicio"*: that is the day he **appeared at trial**. The sentence followed; imprisonment began 28 August.

The charge was **insulting a neighbour, the physician Filomeno Soltura**, in a Bizkaitarra article, and he refused a settlement [A: es.wikipedia]. **Entered Larrinaga 28 August 1895**, held until January 1896 [A]. **Batzokija and Bizkaitarra ordered closed 12 September 1895** for inciting rebellion; the figure of 110 members processed is pool synthesis [R]. **Released January 1896** on reduced bail (day 13 January from pool synthesis, R) and **absolved in November 1896** [A].

## 7. The deputy, the marriage, the congress (1898-1901)

**Elected provincial deputy for Bilbao, 11 September 1898**, seat held to 1902, house stoned in the same period [A: es.wikipedia, verbatim]. **Municipal elections 14 May 1899**, five nationalist councillors; **royal decree of 15 May 1899** suspending constitutional guarantees in Biscay and closing the nationalist centres; **guarantees restored April 1900** [all A]. **El Correo Vasco, June 1899**, 103 numbers [A as to the paper; the 4 June launch and 15 September closure are pool chronology, R].

**Nicolasa "Nikole" Achica-Allende**, born 1873 at the Abiña baserri, Busturia; religious instruction with Carmelite nuns arranged before the marriage; a controlling courtship correspondence, though the **deia.eus reporting on the letters returned HTTP 406**, so their characterization is R. **Married 2 February 1900 in the hermitage of San Antonio de Abiña, after a courtship of one year** [A: es.wikipedia]. **Place-name correction: Busturia, not Pedernales.** **Miscarriage at about four months, August 1900** [A]; no other child documented.

**The islet of San Andrés.** The afterlife lens said Arana **purchased** it; es.wikipedia's Sukarrieta article says the opposite, that it **belonged to the Abiña baserri and came to him through the marriage**, his **widow selling it in 1906 to Claudio Urrutia for 15,000 pesetas** [A]. Recorded because "he bought it" is what the founder-cult literature wants to be true.

**Lourdes honeymoon, 1900** [**R**]: in the dossier's synthesis and in **none** of the Spanish, English or Basque articles; the thinnest-sourced stop in the file, and it says so. **Hendaye orthographic congress, 1901**, Arana as vice-president [A as to year and office: es.wikipedia]; **the exact date 16 September 1901 carried in the pool is unsupported**, and eu.wikipedia's Euskaltzaleen Biltzarra article, which confirms the 1901 founding and lists Arana, Azkue, Kanpion and Adema among some 35 founders, **assigns no offices**, so the *joint* vice-presidency with Campión stays attributed [R].

## 8. The Roosevelt telegram and the second imprisonment (1902)

**Centro Vasco sacked, May 1902** [A: es.wikipedia]; address **not located**. **The telegram to Theodore Roosevelt, 24 or 25 May 1902**, congratulating the United States on Cuban independence and adding that the Basque nation would be free too if Europe imitated the example, **intercepted before delivery** [A as to the episode: es.wikipedia, en.wikipedia; the 24 May date is sabinoarana.eus, R]. Quoted in full in the journey file from **es.wikisource**. **A variant wording** circulates via the Sabino Arana Fundazioa, differing in word order and punctuation, which points to transcription variance rather than two telegrams. Flagged, not resolved.

**Arrested 30 May 1902**, before judge Mauro Santiago Portero, on a rebellion charge under amended article 248, the prosecutor seeking eight years and one day: **all R**, sabinoarana.eus only. **"Grave y transcendental", 22 June 1902, from prison** [A: es.wikipedia, which spells it *transcendental*]; **the venue "La Patria" and the label "Liga de Vascos Españolistas" appear in none of the fetched sources** [R]. **Acquitted by jury, 8 November 1902**; the prosecutor appeals; Arana crosses into France as **"Sylvain de Arbeste"** [A]. **The crossing point is not specified by any source**; Hendaye is offered as probable [R].

## 9. Sukarrieta and death (1903)

**Libe**, four-act drama on the battle of Mungia under the pseudonym **Jelalde**, March 1903, and the **diagnosis of Addison's disease in August 1903** by Carlos de Iruarrizaga, are both [R]: dossier crawl only. He **leaves the direction of the PNV in September 1903**, successor **Ángel de Zabala Ozámiz, "Kondaño"** [A: es.wikipedia]; **the exact day, 30 September, is unsupported**. He **died 25 November 1903 at Pedernales (Sukarrieta), aged 38** [A: es.wikipedia, en.wikipedia], in **rented lodgings** the family never owned [R: naiz.eus].

**Contested causation, flagged.** The claim that imprisonment caused the fatal Addison's disease is repeated by both Wikipedia articles (en.wikipedia says prison "ruined his health") and is **not medically demonstrated**; adrenal insufficiency does not have confinement as a documented cause. Stated as contested inside the campa. **Buried in the local cemetery, November 1903** [A]; the pin is approximate and the plot unverified.

## 10. Afterlife

**Sabin Etxea.** Built 1857 by Santiago Arana, a *palacete* **de estilo neovasco**, **not neoclassical** as the pool had it; seized by the Falange in 1937; used by Auxilio Social; demolished **1960-1961**; lot bought back by the PNV in 1979; new building occupied October 1992, completed 1995 [all A: es.wikipedia, "Sabin Etxea"]. The catch: the original was **razed**, not merely occupied, which looser summaries get wrong. **The first Aberri Eguna, 27 March 1932**, ended at the restored birth house with a plaque marking the 1882 conversation [R: sabinoarana.eus]; per the standing rule this is narrated **only as the origin-point of his own cult at his own birthsite**, never as a scene of his own action.

**The ikurriña.** Designed 1894 for Biscay alone; **adopted 19 October 1936**; prohibited from the aftermath of the 1936 coup (the **1938** start date from nabasque.eus is **not corroborated**); **legalised 19 January 1977**; established by **article 5 of the Statute of Autonomy, 25 October 1979** [A: es.wikipedia, "Ikurriña"]. **The anthem**, *Ley 8/1983*, was adopted with **no official lyrics**, and its melody is **traditional and anonymous, not Arana's** [A: es.wikipedia]; "Gora ta gora" is the PNV party anthem. Cut from the journey file rather than asserted loosely.

**Obras Completas.** *Sabindiar Batza*, founded at Bayonne **14 September 1950**; printed by **Artes Gráficas Amorrortu** in **Buenos Aires**, published **17 April 1965** on Aberri Eguna in the centenary year, **2,450 copies of 2,478 pages** [R: sabinoarana.eus]. Open Library record **OL83579A** lists twelve editions, all Spanish or Basque, **no translation into any other language** [A].

**The Albia statue** [A as to existence: es.wikipedia]; **sculptor and installation date are a flat gap**. The Euskotren stop and metro entrance of that name are **1.5 km west** of Sabin Etxea, so the pool's claim that the stop runs "near the Sabin Etxea headquarters area" is **wrong**. **February 2017:** the PP of the Basque Country calls for renaming his streets, arguing he "made possible the existence of phenomena such as the ETA terrorist group" [A: en.wikipedia]. **The Sukarrieta site erased:** the property passed to **Ramón de la Sota** and became **colonias marítimas** for working-class children [R: naiz.eus], framed there as physical erasure of Arana from the landscape.

## Quotations: ten used, one real misquote caught

**"Fuerismo es separatismo", Bizkaitarra, 22 April 1894.** The pool carried *"...independiente **en todas sus** relaciones con España que las internacionales,"* which is ungrammatical and **inverts the sense**. Granja's footnote reads *"**sin otras** relaciones con España que las internacionales."* Arana's point is that Biscay would have **no** relations with Spain **other than** international ones. Corrected text used. The pool also modernised the **Pliegos** quotation (*"mas también"* to *"sino también"*); the file uses the shorter Pliegos quote instead.

**Not used, and why.** One Granja quotation is cited to ***El Partido Carlista y los Fueros Vasco-Nabarros*, Bilbao, 1897**, but **Open Library gives that work's title as *El Partido Carlista y los fueros baskos*, catalogued at 1912** and neither Wikipedia mentions it at all. With title contested and the 1897 date unverified, no stop was built; **this stays open**, since Granja cites an Obras Completas page and may be right. The four **Politika.io** quotations are used **in that source's English translations**; the Spanish was not independently located, and the Madagascar passage is not attributed there to a specific article.
