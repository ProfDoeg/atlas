# Maria Orsic — Zagreb to the Vanishing (report)

**Register.** This journey is rendered as *myth reported as myth she lived*. The Vril/Aldebaran cosmology is narrated descriptively, in the present tense of the tradition — her channelings as events of her life — without endorsing the ideology or asserting the claims as fact-about-the-world. Where the record is empty and the tradition is loud, confidences are flagged honestly: almost every stop is `traditional`, and only the genuinely documented antecedents/reception (Bulwer-Lytton 1871, Symmes 1818, Pauwels-Bergier 1960, Savitri Devi 1958, Serrano) are `attested`.

**The tradition's own gap — and the biggest judgment call.** There is *no* verified Maria Orsic: no confirmed birth record, no census entry, no contemporary press. Her name first surfaces in print in Pauwels & Bergier's *Le Matin des magiciens* (1960), and the detailed biography (Zagreb 1895, the séances, the discs, "niemand bleibt hier") is largely the work of 1990s authors — Jan van Helsing (Jan Udo Holey) and Norbert-Jürgen Ratthofer — whose source documents have never been independently verified. I treated this exactly as the brief asks: the canon is the *legend itself*, so the stops ARE the legend's stations, coordinated to the real/traditional sites (Zagreb, the Café Schopenhauer in Vienna, Berchtesgaden, Munich, Kolberg, Neuschwabenland), and dated to the tradition's own chronology. The report layer (this file) carries the honesty the campa register cannot.

**The five richest episodes.**
1. **Berchtesgaden, Dec 1919** — the forester's-lodge séance: automatic writing in two scripts (Templar-German cipher + "ancient Sumerian"), decoded not as prophecy but as flying-machine blueprints. The founding miracle.
2. **Aldebaran / the empire "Sumeran"** — the cosmology proper: two inhabited worlds, the "light-god people" (Aryans) and their decline-born lesser kinds, the seeding of Mars, Mallona, and Earth. Quoted verbatim from the Living Moon / Ratthofer lineage.
3. **Rudolf Hess's Munich rooms, Nov 1924** — the trance turns from paper to voice; the "Sumi" announce themselves. Ties the séance circle to the Nazi leadership directly.
4. **The Jenseitsflugmaschine → Neuschwabenland Base 211** — the material arc: the "Beyond-Machine" in Munich, the RFZ discs at Arado, the Vril/Haunebu craft, the Antarctic redoubt and the disc that departs 21 April 1945.
5. **The vanishing, 11 March 1945** — "Niemand bleibt hier" — the whole journey's key: not a death but (the circle says) a departure homeward to the star.

These are placed as antecedent and reception stops, not as endorsements.

**Connection to the atlas.** This is a companion to the esoteric-modern arc already in the directory — **Gurdjieff, Jung, Keyserling, Laban** — the early-20th-century occult/psychic milieu, and specifically its *darkest fold*: where mediumship and mythic race-cosmology run into the Nazi penumbra. It rhymes structurally with the *legendary-life* journeys (Ulysses/Aeneas-type, where "the stops ARE the epic's stations"): Orsic's only verifiable life is her legend, so her itinerary is her mythos, exactly as the brief specifies. It sits opposite the atlas's saints and liberators (Joan, Clotilde, the Bolívar/Belgrano/Miranda liberation cluster) as their shadow — a national/racial mythology authored to survive a defeat, and the one journey in the set whose canon the report must openly mark as unverified.
