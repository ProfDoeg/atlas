# Ghislaine Maxwell: research report

*Compiled 2026-08-11. Subject: Ghislaine Noelle Marion Maxwell, b. 25 December 1961, Maisons-Laffitte. Living and incarcerated at the date of compilation.*

Legend: **[A]** = attested, source named · **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition, allegation, or press inference not resolvable to a primary document. Contradictions are flagged in place and listed at the end; none are silently resolved.

A note on register. The subject is alive and the events are crimes with living victims. The atlas register ("national mythology: the canon is true") is a register of statement, not endorsement: where a court found a fact the report says so, and where an accuser alleged what no court adjudicated it says so and marks it **[R]**.

## 1. Oxford and the house on the hill, 1961-1985

**[A: Wikipedia, "Ghislaine Maxwell"]** Born 25 December 1961 at Maisons-Laffitte, ninth and youngest child of Robert Maxwell (born Jan Ludvik Hoch) and Elisabeth Meynard. Pin 48.9469, 2.1456, corrected 1.1 km.

**[A: Wikipedia]** Two days after her birth her brother Michael is injured in a car accident and remains in a coma until his death in 1968.

**[A: Wikipedia, "Robert Maxwell"]** The family home is Headington Hill Hall, Oxford, a 53-room mansion rented from Oxford City Council and doubling as Pergamon Press headquarters; Maxwell held the lease for his last 32 years. Pin 51.7552, -1.2311 (OpenStreetMap), corrected 500 m.

## 2. London society and the crossing, 1986-1992

**[A: Wikipedia]** In 1986 Robert Maxwell names his yacht *Lady Ghislaine* for her. **[A: Tatler and Vanity Fair profiles via Wikipedia]** Through the late 1980s she is a fixture of the Belgravia social scene and founds a women's club modelled on the Kit-Cat Club. Belgravia pin throughout: 51.4972, -0.1527.

**[R: Vanity Fair 2021 profile]** A November 1990 flight to New York carrying an envelope for her father is reported as early emissary work. No primary document is reachable; carried at JFK (40.6413, -73.7781) as reconstruction.

**Contradiction, flagged and not resolved.** The gathering pass had Robert Maxwell buying the *Daily News*, sending Ghislaine to Manhattan as emissary, and the two crossing together by Concorde **in May 1991**. Verification supports neither the month nor the joint crossing: he agreed to buy the paper in January 1991 and took control in March. The posting is anchored to **January-March 1991 [A]**; the Concorde crossing is **[R], unsupported**.

**Contradiction, flagged and not resolved.** When she first meets Jeffrey Epstein is genuinely unsettled: Wikipedia records that accounts differ between the late 1980s and the early 1990s. **[R]** throughout; no date is asserted.

**[A: Wikipedia, "Robert Maxwell"; 1991 news archives]** 5 November 1991: Robert Maxwell disappears from the *Lady Ghislaine* off the Canary Islands; the body is landed at Puerto de La Luz, Las Palmas (28.1235, -15.4363). Autopsy and inquest disagree on cause, and his daughter rejects both accident and suicide. Reported, not adjudicated.

**[A: Wikipedia; 1991 news archives]** On 10 November 1991 he is buried on the Mount of Olives, Jerusalem, at a state funeral attended by Prime Minister Yitzhak Shamir and President Chaim Herzog. Ghislaine attends.

**Coordinate contradiction, flagged.** The pool gives the Mount of Olives as both 31.7784, 35.241 and 31.7784, 35.2453, about 400 m apart. The journey uses 35.241; neither figure traces to a survey point, so the discrepancy is recorded, not settled.

**[A: Wikipedia]** In 1992 she moves to a Manhattan apartment overlooking Central Park (press accounts: lent by an Iranian friend), works at a Madison Avenue real estate office, and receives roughly £80,000 a year from a trust. Residence and job are **[A]**; the pins (40.7812, -73.9665; 40.7736, -73.9666) are **[R]**, neither address being publicly confirmed.

## 3. The Epstein years, 1993-2004

Straus House at 9 East 71st Street (40.7727, -73.9647) becomes the centre of their shared social world and, as prosecutors established at trial, of the conduct for which she was convicted.

**[A: prosecutors' allegations, via Wikipedia]** Prosecutors allege an intimate relationship through the mid-1990s. **[R: Maxwell's own account]** She has said it ended in 2001. **[R: Epstein's pilot]** The pilot dates her separation to about 2004. ## 4. The quiet decade, 2007-2019

**[A: Wikipedia]** From 2007 she lives with Scott Borgerson in Manchester-by-the-Sea, Massachusetts (42.5751, -70.7728). **Internal contradiction in the pool, corrected:** one lens gave 2016-2020, the chronology lens 2007. 2016 is the marriage year, not the move. The window used is c.2007-2019.

**[A: Wikipedia]** 2008: Epstein is convicted in Florida of soliciting a minor and serves 13 months. May 2009: Giuffre, as Jane Doe 102, sues Epstein and Maxwell; settled that year for a reported $500,000.

**[A: Wikipedia]** 2012: she founds the TerraMar Project and attends UN-affiliated events at United Nations Headquarters (40.7489, -73.968) through 2019. In 2014 she speaks at TEDx Charlottesville. **Gap:** no verified coordinate for Charlottesville; carried in the TerraMar campa, not pinned.

**[A: Wikipedia; SDNY docket]** January 2015: filings in a related case detail a deposition, later attributed to Giuffre as Jane Doe 3, describing grooming. September 2015: Giuffre sues Maxwell for defamation in the SDNY.

**[A: verified transcript]** 22 April 2016: the deposition in *Giuffre v. Maxwell*, No. 1:15-cv-07433-LAP (S.D.N.Y.), taken at Boies Schiller & Flexner, 465 pages. "I don't recall" appears 76 times (plus one "I do not recall"), 7 of them as the complete answer. Filed on the public docket as Dkt. 1137-13 (Exhibit 6) on **22 October 2020**. **Correction to a repeated claim:** it was *not* unsealed in August 2020; that release was a different batch.

Verified exchange, transcript pp. 101-102, used as the journey quote:

> Q. In 1992 I know you gave me the description of the work that you were performing for him, how much was he paying you, do you remember?
> A. I don't recall.
> Q. Do you know for example in 2001 how much he was paying you?
> A. I don't recall.

**Editorial note from verification:** "I don't recall" alone is a stock deposition answer with no distinctive meaning, so it is quoted only inside the verified pair, never floated on its own.

**[A: Wikipedia]** 2016: she marries Scott Borgerson, secretly.

**Date contradiction, flagged and corrected.** The gathering pass dated the defamation settlement to May 2017; the Giuffre article gives **June 2017**, under seal, Maxwell reportedly paying "millions". June is used.

## 5. Arrest and trial, 2019-2022

**[A: Wikipedia]** August 2019: Epstein dies in the Metropolitan Correctional Center, Manhattan; ruled a suicide. **Gap:** no verified coordinate for the MCC, and the courthouse pin was deliberately not substituted. The death is carried in the In-N-Out campa.

**[A: New York Post, August 2019]** Weeks later she is photographed at an In-N-Out Burger in the Los Angeles area (34.09, -118.3617), the image used to show she had not left the country. **The branch has never been independently identified**; the pin is approximate and flagged in date_confidence.

**[A: Wikipedia]** Late 2019: she moves to a 156-acre property in Bradford, New Hampshire (43.2703, -71.9509). **Gap:** verification offered no correction here and the pin was not re-verified; carried as received.

**[A: DOJ press release, 2 July 2020]** 2 July 2020: FBI agents arrest her at Bradford, having located her by IMSI-catcher. Six federal counts, including sex-trafficking conspiracy.

**[A: BOP records; SDNY filings]** 14 July 2020: not guilty plea, bail denied as a flight risk. Held at MDC Brooklyn (40.6603, -74.0044, corrected 3.9 km from a figure landing in Gowanus) to July 2022.

**[A: Wikipedia]** 29 March 2021: a superseding indictment adds sex trafficking of a minor aged 14, alleged between 2001 and 2004.

**[A: SDNY docket; DOJ press releases]** 29 November 2021: opening statements at 500 Pearl Street (40.7143, -74.0018). 29 December 2021: conviction on five of six counts, including sex trafficking of a minor, acquittal on enticing a minor to travel. 28 June 2022: Judge Alison Nathan imposes 20 years (240 months), plus fine and supervised release.

**Coordinate contradiction, flagged.** The pool gives the SDNY courthouse as both -74.0018 and -74.0025 at 40.7143, and the Second Circuit as 40.7136, -74.0021. The journey uses -74.0018 and -74.0021; the roughly 60 m discrepancy is recorded, not resolved.

## 7. The afterlife lens, which does not apply

**[A: public record]** Ghislaine Maxwell is alive. She has no tomb, grave, monument, memorial, or published book. Any afterlife framing is a category error while she lives, and the journey ends at a scheduled release date rather than a burial. This is a load-bearing correction to the atlas method, not a discovered fact. **[R]** Tabloid claims since 2021 that she was drafting a manuscript in custody are unsubstantiated. The nearest analogues to sites are institutional: 500 Pearl Street and the three facilities that have held her.
