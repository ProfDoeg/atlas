# Eduardo Sergio Elsztain: research report

*2026-08-23. Subject living (b. 26 January 1960, Buenos Aires). Chairman of IRSA and Cresud; largest private shareholder of Banco Hipotecario; honorary president of Chabad-Lubavitch Argentina; WJC vice-president. Slug `eduardo_elsztain`. Journey file: 9 segments, 45 stops, 12 quoted stops.*

## 1. Origin: Formosa, Belgrano, 1917-1960

- Paternal grandfather, named in later family profiles as **Isaac Elsztain**, emigrates **1917** from territory then Russian Empire, later Poland; keeps a general store at **Las Lomitas, Formosa**. **[R: es.wikipedia, echoing family-history profiles.]** Country of departure attributed inconsistently.
- Family settles permanently in **Buenos Aires c. 1950**, in **Belgrano**. **[R: es.wikipedia for the year; Belgrano is profile material only, no address in any record reached.]**
- **Born 26 January 1960** to **Mario Julio Elsztain**, physician, and **Alba Rubín**, teacher. **[A: en.wikipedia, citing Bloomberg Businessweek.]** No hospital named; the pin is the corpus city pin, declared as such.
- The grandfather's inflation rule ("one square metre, one more hectare of farm or one more parking space") is **[A]** as a quotation (Bloomberg / Buenos Aires Times 2023), but it is Elsztain telling a family story, so as biography of the grandfather it is **[R]**.

**Gap.** Nothing reached on his mother's family, on siblings other than Alejandro, or on the "earlier real-estate fortunes thinned in the provincial economy" the dossier asserts.

## 4. Soros, the Rebbe, IRSA, 1989-1994

- Lives in **New York** from c. 1989. **[R]**

The surrounding relationship is confirmed (Lubavitch News, 27 January 2009). Every account paraphrases the Rebbe rather than transcribing him, so no quote field carries the line.
- **June 1991: control of IRSA for ≈US$120,000**, with **Marcelo Mindlin**. **[A: en.wikipedia.]**

- **1991: marries Mariana Carmona**, psychologist. **[A: en.wikipedia citing La Nación.]** No month.

- **December 1994: IRSA lists on the NYSE, raising US$110 million.** **[A: en.wikipedia.]** Cresud's own US listing is separate: NASDAQ 1997-98, US$92m capital increase.

**Error corrected, not merely flagged.** The pool carried a 1992 entry (Soros capital, Cresud control, international markets). Cresud's own history dates the takeover to 1994 and the NYSE entry was December 1994. **There is no 1992 event**; no stop was written for one.

## 9. Contradictions found and left standing

1. **Hectares of the former Ciudad Deportiva de Boca.** en.wikipedia (Elsztain): **22 ha**. IRSA's own Ramblas material and the 2025 filing: **~70 ha**. Same parcel, same purchase, two figures, no reconciliation anywhere. Both stated in the 1997 stop; neither picked.
2. **Lipstick Building.** en.wikipedia (Elsztain): 30% in 2007, 49% in November 2010. en.wikipedia (Lipstick Building): owner 2007-2010 was Metropolitan 885 Third Avenue LLC, IRSA majority only in **2011** after the bankruptcy. The November 2010 49% is the only shared point; the pool's [A] is unearned and the file says so. One pool entry also called the building Art Deco; it is Johnson and Burgee, 1986.
3. **Banco Hipotecario: 1997 or 1999.** Pool geography says the stake was January 1999; en.wikipedia frames the contested privatization as 1997. Likeliest reconciliation is a 1997 law and a 1999 offering; the file states both dates as the two dates they are.
4. **Llao Llao: "late 1990s" (pool) vs "since 2000" (en.wikipedia).** 2000 used as the more specific; the earlier left standing.
5. **The Infobae headline quote.** "...vamos a ver a muchos más **empresarios** haciendo cosas en el país" is Infobae's **headline**. The body carries what he said: "...que es el mayor elemento que marca la inflación, vamos a ver mucha más **gente**...". The file uses the body form and the `quote_source` names the substitution.
6. **Elsztain urging Milei to visit the Ohel.** Reported (per the dossier) by El País and **dated 2024**; Milei's attested visit was **27 November 2023**, so the urging postdates the visit as the sources stand. The file states both dates and resolves nothing. elpais.com was blocked; no corroborating piece found.
7. **Casposo 2026 restart.** The pool tags **[A]** a 2026 restart, >US$15m refurbishment, ~US$60m export forecast. Austral Gold's own page says **Care & Maintenance**, no restart date, neither figure. **No stop written.**
8. **Challenger Gold / Hualilán.** Pool dates the A$6.6m / 12.7% IFISA investment to January 2025; en.wikipedia puts the toll-processing partnership in **late 2024**. The 12.7% is confirmed, A$6.6m is not, and the coordinate could not be validated (Hualilán is in Ullum department). **No stop written.**
9. **Abasto, 1893 vs 1934.** The market dates from 1893; the Art Deco building is **1934**, by Delpini, Sulčič and Bes. One pool entry conflated the two.
