# Adnan Khashoggi (1935-2017): research report
*2026-08-11. Slug `adnan_khashoggi`. Saudi arms broker; Lockheed and Northrop commissions; Triad Holding Corporation; Iran-Contra middleman; co-defendant of Imelda Marcos, acquitted 1990.*

**Method.** The reachable spine is encyclopedic. The English Wikipedia biography is unusually error-prone, and several of its statements are contradicted by the very articles it links to; those collisions are the most valuable material here and are printed rather than smoothed. The French record (a 1977 *Le Point* interview, a 2005 *Le Monde* piece) is the only place Khashoggi speaks at length about his own commissions. News archives were almost uniformly closed to this host; that limit is itemized in Sources.

**Legend.** [A] = attested, source named. [R] = reconstruction, tradition, inference, or a claim whose only carrier is contradicted elsewhere. A date absent from the source is [R] even where the event is [A]; that is the most frequent correction below.
