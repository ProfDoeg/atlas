# Cristina Fernández de Kirchner: research report

*Slug: `cristina_fernandez_de_kirchner`. Compiled 2026-08-11 from the verified research pool. Subject living at compilation; the file is open-ended by necessity.*

**Legend.** **[A]** = attested, source named. **[R]** = reconstruction or press attribution not confirmed against a first-generation record. Contradictions are flagged and left standing. Wrong coordinates are corrected here with the replacement value and the reason.

## Method, and what this is not

The pool is overwhelmingly encyclopaedic (English and Spanish Wikipedia, cross-read), plus primary texts fetched from `cfkargentina.com` and one first-generation news account from Ámbito. No archival work: no court files, no Senate records in the original, no INDEC series. Where the two Wikipedias disagree, both readings are recorded. The subject is living and litigating; the report records the conviction, the confirmation, the sentence, the ban, and her own published characterization of all four as attested facts, and adjudicates none of them.

## I. Tolosa and the Faculty of Law, 1953 to 1975

**Birth [A].** Cristina Elisabet Fernández, **19 February 1953**, in **Tolosa**, a barrio of the La Plata partido (en and es Wikipedia agree on date and place).

- *Year corrected.* One pool entry gave 1954-02-19 and contradicted the pool's own chronology. **1953** stands.
- *Coordinate corrected.* The pool gave `-34.9214, -57.9544`, which is La Plata's centre near Plaza Moreno. es.wikipedia *Tolosa (La Plata)* gives 34°53'39"S 57°57'53"O = **`-34.8942, -57.9647`**, about 3 km northwest. The journey uses the corrected point for the birth stop only; later La Plata stops keep the city pin.
- *Parents [A].* Father **Eduardo Fernández** (1921-1982), colectivo driver. Mother **Ofelia Esther Wilhelm** (1929-2019), employee of the **Dirección General de Rentas** and general secretary of her union AERI. The pool's "municipal clerk" for the mother is wrong and is replaced.
- *Gap.* No reachable source names the clinic of birth.

**University [A].** Law at the **Facultad de Ciencias Jurídicas y Sociales, UNLP**, where she meets fellow law student **Néstor Carlos Kirchner** of Río Gallegos. Consistent across Argentine press biographies and both Kirchners' own accounts. Coordinate `-34.9198, -57.9522` checks out.

**Ezeiza, 20 June 1973 [R].** Some journalistic retrospectives place the couple, or her alone, in the crowd on the day of the shooting. Others do not corroborate it. Left [R], unresolved.

- *Coordinate corrected.* The pool used Ezeiza International Airport (`-34.8222, -58.5358`). Per en.wikipedia *Ezeiza massacre* the shooting was at **Puente 12**, about 10 km up the access road at the junction of the General Ricchieri freeway and the Camino de Cintura. Corrected to **`-34.746, -58.582`**.

**Marriage [R, date disputed].**

- *Contradiction, not resolved.* en.wikipedia: **9 May 1975**. es.wikipedia: *"Se casaron el jueves 8 de mayo de 1975"*, and 8 May 1975 was in fact a Thursday, which makes the Spanish date internally consistent. The pool tagged this [A], which overstates it. Recorded as **8 or 9 May 1975, disputed, [R]**.
- *Place corrected.* The pool named a "Registro Civil, La Plata" with a street-level coordinate. Neither Wikipedia names a venue; es.wikipedia gives no location at all. That precision was invented. Recorded as **La Plata, venue unspecified**, pinned at the atlas-canonical La Plata point `-34.9215, -57.9545` (inherited from `julio_argentino_roca.journey.json`).

## II. The far south, 1976 to 1991

**The degree, in two stages [A, es.wikipedia].** The pool's "completes her law degree, 1976-07-03, after relocating" is wrong in date and framing. The record: **21 April 1976**, qualifies as **procuradora**, three subjects short; **1 October 1979**, completes the **abogacía** after sitting Derecho Internacional Privado. The procuradora title *precedes* the move south. 1976-07-03 appears in no reachable source and is dropped.

**Río Gallegos [A].** Relocation to Néstor's home town; joint practice **Kirchner y Kirchner**, real estate and civil matters, through the dictatorship. Registration as a practicing attorney in Santa Cruz, **1980** [A].

**Máximo, contradiction flagged.** The pool gave 23 February 1977, Río Gallegos. es.wikipedia *Máximo Kirchner* gives **16 February 1977, born in La Plata**; the es.wikipedia CFK article says only *"Máximo, nacido en febrero de 1977."* Both cannot stand, and the La Plata birthplace undercuts the neighbouring claim that the couple had already settled south. Recorded with the es.wikipedia date and place, discrepancy left visible in `date_confidence`.

**Florencia [R].** 2 September 1990, Río Gallegos. Standard biographical record, not independently confirmed; kept [R].

**Elected office [A].** Provincial deputy for Santa Cruz, **1989**. Brief **interim governor** in **1990** after the impeachment of Governor Ricardo del Val. Organizer of Néstor's winning campaign for the governorship, **1991**. Legislatura (`-51.6242, -69.2186`) and Casa de Gobierno (`-51.6236, -69.2181`) are distinct from the town pin (`-51.623, -69.2168`); all three are used.

## III. The Congress of the Nation, 1994 to 2005

- **1994 [A]** delegate to the constitutional assembly, seated at Santa Fe. No precise convention coordinate in the pool; the journey uses the city.
- **1995 [A]** elected national senator for Santa Cruz.
- **1997 [A]** removed from the Justicialist senate bloc over an internal misconduct dispute; resigns the seat, runs for deputy in the same year's midterms.
- **2001 [A, correction].** The pool said she "runs for senator." She won: es.wikipedia, *"En 2001 fue elegida nuevamente senadora por la provincia de Santa Cruz."*
- **2003 [A, venue corrected].** Néstor Kirchner inaugurated **25 May 2003**. The pool placed the ceremony at the Casa Rosada. Per en.wikipedia *Néstor Kirchner* the oath is taken before the **Legislative Assembly at the Palace of the Argentine National Congress**; sash and staff are handed over afterwards at the Casa Rosada. The journey pins the oath at Congress (`-34.6095, -58.3924`) and says so.
- **2005 [A]** wins a Buenos Aires Province senate seat, defeating Hilda "Chiche" González de Duhalde.

## IV. The sash: first presidency, 2007 to 2011

- **28 Oct 2007 [A]** wins the presidency with about 45 percent.
- **10 Dec 2007 [A, same venue correction]** inaugurated 55th President, first woman *elected* to the office rather than succeeding a spouse in it. Oath again before the Legislative Assembly at Congress, not the Casa Rosada. The Casa Rosada coordinate `-34.6083, -58.3712` is itself sound (es.wikipedia gives -34.60806, -58.37028 for Balcarce 50, within ~85 m) and is used for the Casa Rosada stops.
- **Mar 2008 [A]** Resolution 125, sliding-scale agricultural export tax; the farm conflict follows. **Apr 2008 [A]** Economy Minister **Martín Lousteau** resigns.
- **15 Nov 2008 [A, coordinate corrected].** Attends the first G20 leaders' summit in Washington (14-15 Nov 2008). The pool's `38.8951, -77.0492` falls near the Lincoln Memorial. The venue was the **National Building Museum**, approx. **`38.8977, -77.0170`**. *This stop was cut from the journey for length; the correction is recorded so it is not lost.*
- **2009 [A]** the **Asignación Universal por Hijo** is created. The pool attests the year only; the decree date is not asserted.
- **Dec 2009 / Jan 2010 [A, two events disentangled].** The pool conflated them. The **Fondo del Bicentenario** was created by DNU in **December 2009** (DNU 2010/09). The separate **DNU 18/2010 of 7 January 2010** removed Central Bank president **Martín Redrado**. Per es.wikipedia *Martín Redrado*, Redrado **resigned 29 January 2010** and a bicameral commission confirmed the removal on 3 February 2010. He was not simply "dismissed in January."
- **27 Oct 2010 [A, discrepancy flagged].** **Néstor Kirchner dies** at El Calafate. One fetched Wikipedia extraction in the pool listed 23 October 2010; the widely reported date is **27 October**. Flagged, not silently resolved; the journey uses 27 October and says so.
- **28 Oct 2010 [A]** the body lies in state at the Casa Rosada.
- **29 Oct 2010 [A, apparent contradiction resolved as a sequence].** Burial at Río Gallegos. Cemetery versus mausoleum is not a conflict: interment in the **municipal cemetery** in October 2010, transfer to the purpose-built **family mausoleum** in 2011 (en.wikipedia *Néstor Kirchner* names the Mausoleum of Néstor Kirchner, Río Gallegos). Recorded as a sequence.

## V. Fifty-four percent: second presidency, 2011 to 2015

- **23 Oct 2011 [A]** re-elected with **54.11 percent**. **10 Dec 2011 [A]** second term begins.
- **2012 [A]** the **cepo cambiario**, strict foreign exchange controls.
- **Sep 2012 [A]** cacerolazo against a proposed amendment permitting her re-election. **8 Nov 2012 [A]** the **8N** demonstration, estimated crowd approaching 500,000 in Buenos Aires alone.
- **Mar 2013 [A, date and event corrected].** The pool put her in the 8 March Chávez funeral procession to the Academia Militar. Per en.wikipedia *Death and state funeral of Hugo Chávez*, she was among the **first heads of state to arrive, on 5 March 2013**, paid respects at the military hospital chapel, and **returned home before the 8 March funeral**. The procession to the Military Academy was 6 March. Corrected to a **5 to 7 March 2013 visit**.
- **18 Mar 2013 [A]** received by **Pope Francis** days after his election.
- **Apr 2013 [A]** severe flooding in Buenos Aires and **La Plata**; judicial reform proposals in the same period; the **18A** demonstration on 18 April.
- **18 Jan 2015 [A]** prosecutor **Alberto Nisman** found dead the night before he was to testify. The pool folded the **18F** march into the same entry; that march was **18 February 2015**. Kept distinct.
- **Feb 2015 [A, year corrected].** The Beijing state visit and the trade, infrastructure and defence agreements signed at the **Great Hall of the People**. The pool dated this February **2014**; per en.wikipedia *Argentina-China relations* the visit was **February 2015** (Xi's own visit to Argentina was July 2014). Coordinate `39.9046, 116.3915` kept.
- **23 Apr 2015 [A]** meets Putin at the **Kremlin**. *Cut from the journey for length; recorded here.*
- **10 Dec 2015 [A]** term ends. She declines the formal handover with Mauricio Macri and addresses supporters from the Casa Rosada balcony instead.

## VI. Out of office, and the courts, 2016 to 2023

- **2016 onward [A]** base established at the **Instituto Patria**, Buenos Aires.
- **2015 [A]** indicted over alleged obstruction of the AMIA investigation (the Iran memorandum case).
- **12 Mar 2017 [A, sourcing corrected, tag upgraded].** The leaked audio in which she tells Oscar Parrilli *"Hay que salir a apretar a los jueces."* The pool attributed it to "Perfil and El Liberal, January 2017," which is wrong. The audio was broadcast on *La Cornisa* and reported **12 March 2017**. A first-generation account was fetched directly: **Ámbito**, *"En nueva escucha, Cristina dice a Parrilli: 'Hay que salir a apretar a los jueces'"*, 12 March 2017. Corroborated same-story by La Nación and Télam (12 March), La Gaceta and El Observador (13 March). Tag upgraded **[R] to [A]**, with the standing caveat that this is press reporting of a leaked recording, not a transcript she has acknowledged.
- **2017 [A]** arrest warrant on a treason charge tied to the Iran memorandum; senator-elect status grants immunity and blocks detention. **10 Dec 2017 [A]** takes her Senate seat for Buenos Aires Province.
- **2018 [A]** indicted for corruption over bribery in public works contracts (the cuadernos / Vialidad-linked case).
- **23 to 25 Apr 2019 [A]** *Sinceramente* announced on Twitter 23 April, published 25 April by Editorial Sudamericana, 594 pages in ten chapters plus epilogue, drafts moved by USB drive rather than email against espionage. The 60,000-copy first run sells out in a week; by 1 May a third printing puts circulation past 210,000.
- **9 May 2019 [A, coordinate corrected].** Presented at the **Buenos Aires Book Fair** with giant screens rigged outside. The pool's `-34.5455, -58.4497` lies about 4.5 km northwest in Núñez/Saavedra. The fair is at **La Rural, Predio Ferial de Buenos Aires**, 34°34'43"S 58°25'15"O = **`-34.5786, -58.4209`**.
- **10 Dec 2019 [A]** inaugurated 37th Vice President under Alberto Fernández. **Feb 2020 [A]** *Sinceramente* presented abroad for the first time at the **Havana International Book Fair**.
- **1 Sep 2022 [A, two corrections].** Outside her Recoleta residence **Fernando André Sabag Montiel** points a Bersa Lusber 84 at her face and pulls the trigger; it does not fire. (1) *Coordinate*: en.wikipedia's article on the attempt gives 34°35'34"S 58°23'15"W = **`-34.5928, -58.3875`**; the pool's geography entry was about 570 m off and inconsistent with its own afterlife entry. (2) *Fact*: the pool said the gun was "later found chambered." That is the opposite of the record. The pistol held five cartridges but had **no round in the chamber**, which is the accepted explanation for the misfire. Removed.
  - *Aftermath [A].* Mass demonstrations at Plaza de Mayo; a Senate investigative commission; staged-attack conspiracy theories in some opposition media. The pool's "day of national mourning" is wrong: nobody died. The president declared the **following day a national holiday**, in repudiation. Sabag Montiel, Brazilian-born, resident since 1993, bore Nazi tattoos and had prior arrests for carrying a knife; tried from 26 June 2024 and **sentenced 8 October 2025 to 10 years** for attempted aggravated homicide.
- **6 Dec 2022 [A]** the Tribunal Oral en lo Criminal Federal N.2 at Comodoro Py convicts her in the **Vialidad** case: six years and a lifetime ban from public office. Upheld on appeal November 2024.
- **10 Dec 2023 [A]** leaves the vice presidency.

## VII. House arrest, and a living afterlife, 2024 to 2026

- **17 Nov 2024 [A]** assumes the presidency of the **Partido Justicialista**, with José Mayans as vice.
- **Dec 2024 [R]** the Supreme Court orders her to face trial in the Iran memorandum case, which a federal court had voided in October 2021. Sources conflict on whether that track is live. Left [R].
- **Jun 2025 [A]** the **Corte Suprema** upholds the Vialidad conviction and the lifetime ban.
- **17 Jun 2025 [A, address R]** placed under **house arrest** with an electronic ankle monitor. The apartment is commonly reported as **San José 1111**, Constitución. The address could not be independently confirmed: an Infobae article on it returned HTTP 404. Coordinate `-34.6217, -58.3812` used and tagged accordingly.
- **17 Oct 2025 [A]** the Day of Peronist Loyalty message on cfkargentina.com: *"Exactamente 80 años después, otra vez nos ponen en la misma disyuntiva. En aquel entonces: era Braden o Perón. Hoy pareciera ser Bessent o Perón."* And: *"el 26 es Milei o Argentina."*
- **13 Nov 2025 [A]** message to the *Jornadas internacionales Lawfare*, Instituto Fray Bartolomé de las Casas.
- **29 Jul 2026 [A, fetched directly]** the open letter *"Carta abierta: El costo democrático de la politización de la justicia"*, accompanying her petition to the **UN Human Rights Committee**.

**Afterlife, literally [A].** She is alive. No tomb, no repatriation, no reburial. Unlike every other figure in the Argentine presidential line already in the atlas, her afterlife stop is a balcony and a website. `cfkargentina.com` is a self-curated archive of speeches and writing; the journey treats it as a shrine its subject maintains in person.

**Gaps [R].** No monuments, statues, or murals specifically depicting her were found in the sources consulted. This is a gap, not a confirmed absence: Argentine street muralism, much of it centred on Néstor Kirchner and Eva Perón, was not surveyed. Portuguese and English editions of *Sinceramente* are referenced across Wikipedia language editions, but no publisher record was verified.

## IX. Contradictions recorded and left standing

1. **Marriage date**: 8 May 1975 (es.wikipedia, consistent as a Thursday) versus 9 May 1975 (en.wikipedia).
2. **Máximo Kirchner's birth**: 16 February 1977 in La Plata (es.wikipedia) versus the pool's 23 February 1977 in Río Gallegos. Consequential: it bears on when the couple settled south.
3. **Néstor Kirchner's death**: 23 October 2010 in one fetched extraction versus the reported 27 October 2010.
4. **Néstor's burial**: cemetery versus mausoleum, resolvable as a sequence (2010, then 2011).
5. **The AMIA / Iran memorandum track**: voided October 2021, trial ordered December 2024; sources conflict on whether it is live.
6. **The house-arrest address**: San José 1111 is commonly reported; the confirming article 404'd.
