# Keiko Fujimori Higuchi (b. 1975): research report
*Compiled 2026-08-16 for the Atlas of Journeys. The subject is alive and in office; this is a life still running.*

Legend: **[A]** = attested, source named · **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition, or inference. Contradictions are flagged, not adjudicated. Unreachable sources are named with the reason.

Standing caution: the two Wikipedias carry this whole dossier and disagree with each other on at least six points of date and figure. Nothing below rests on a primary document held in hand.

---

## 1. Birth and household, 1974-1990

- Born **25 May 1975** in **Jesús María**, Lima, eldest child of Alberto Fujimori and Susana Higuchi **[A: en.wikipedia]**. The **clinic is not named** in any reachable source, so the pin (-12.0733, -77.0489) is district-level.
- Parents married **25 July 1974** **[A: en.wikipedia, "Susana Higuchi"]**, at the Capilla de la Virgen de la O **[A: susana_higuchi.journey.json]**. Keiko is born ten months later.
- Siblings **Hiro Alberto** (Dec 1976), **Sachi Marcela** (Mar 1979), **Kenji Gerardo** (May 1980) **[A: es.wikipedia]**.
- Schooling at the **Colegio Sagrados Corazones Recoleta**, secondary cycle finished 1993 **[A for the claim: en.wikipedia]**. See C1 on its location; **the colegio's own article does not list her among its alumni**, so the attendance claim rests on her biography alone.
- **28 July 1990**: her father inaugurated; the household moves into the Palacio de Gobierno on the Plaza Mayor. Keiko is fifteen **[A: susana_higuchi.journey.json]**.

## 2. The palace and the autogolpe, 1992-1993

- **28 March 1992** and after: Susana Higuchi accuses her husband's sisters of selling clothing donated as Japanese aid, and describes a chain drawn across the palace gate **[A: susana_higuchi.journey.json]**. Keiko is sixteen and in the same household; **her own presence at these scenes is nowhere attested [R for any Keiko-specific claim]**.
- **5 April 1992** autogolpe: Higuchi's contested account holds the family was moved into the army intelligence installations at San Borja, the Pentagonito; the government denied it **[A that the account exists and was denied: susana_higuchi.journey.json; R as to Keiko]**.
- **1993**: enrolls at **Stony Brook University** in business administration **[A: en.wikipedia]**.

## 3. First Lady at nineteen, 1994-2000

- **August 1994**: Higuchi accuses her husband of corruption and of enrichment by relatives; he strips her of the First Lady title **[A: en.wikipedia, "Susana Higuchi"]**.
- **23 August 1994**: Keiko, nineteen, installed as First Lady in her mother's place **[A: both Wikipedias; corroborated by susana_higuchi.journey.json]**. Holds the office to **21 November 2000** (see C2).
- She **directs** the **Fundación por los Niños del Perú** **[A: es.wikipedia, "dirigió"]**. The pool said she *founded* it; **no source supports "founded"** for that entity and the word is dropped. She is credited with creating the **Fundación Cardioinfantil del Perú**, **March 1996** **[A: es.wikipedia]**.
- Linkage of that work to the **Instituto Nacional de Salud del Niño** (Av. Brasil 600, Breña) is **[R]**: a reconstruction from programme descriptions, not an attested tie. Coordinates corrected to **-12.064718, -77.046181** (pool was ~1 km off).
- **1995**: parents divorced **[A: en.wikipedia]**; the existing atlas file places the proceeding at the Palacio de Justicia **[A]**.
- **May 1997**: bachelor's degree in business administration at **Boston University**, transferred from Stony Brook **[A: both Wikipedias]**.
- es.wikipedia lists **rooms in the Palacio de Gobierno repainted pink** among the three main controversies of the tenure **[A]**.
- **21 November 2000**: Congress removes Alberto Fujimori after his fax resignation from Tokyo; Keiko leaves the palace **[A: en.wikipedia]**.

## 4. Exile, marriage, New York, 2001-2005

- **2001**: travels to Tokyo to see her father, by then a Japanese citizen beyond extradition **[R: es.wikipedia; the trip is attested, but no site, duration or frequency is given anywhere reachable]**.
- **13 July 2004**: marries **Mark Vito Villanella**, a U.S. citizen **[A: es.wikipedia]**. District (Miraflores) attested; **church name (Virgen Milagrosa) and officiant (Archbishop Cipriani) are [R]**, one source, unconfirmed.
- **2004-2008**: MBA at **Columbia Business School**, begun after the marriage ("Tras casarse, Keiko comenzó una maestría...") **[A: es.wikipedia]**, correcting the pool's "2002/2008". Shareholder in the aromatherapy exporter **Summit Products** until 2008 **[A]**.
- Daughters **Kyara Sofía** (2007), **Kaori Marcela** (2009) **[A for the years: es.wikipedia]**. **Birthplaces are not stated in the cited source**; the pool's "United States" is unsupported and dropped.
- **6/7 November 2005**: Alberto Fujimori arrested in Santiago, ten hours after landing from Tokyo, having checked into the Hotel Marriott in Las Condes **[A: es.wikipedia]**; he landed on the 6th and was arrested early on the 7th, correcting the pool's flat "6 November". **Keiko was not there**, and the pool's "returned from her studies as the case unfolds" is a false causal link: she returned to head the Alianza por el Futuro list, registered 6 January 2006 **[A]**. *No Santiago stop is written into her file: the arrest belongs to whoever was actually there.*

## 5. Congresswoman, 2006-2010

- **6 January 2006**: presides over **Alianza por el Futuro** **[A: es.wikipedia]**.
- **9 April 2006**: elected for Lima with **602,869 preferential votes**, "la congresista más votada" **[A: both Wikipedias]**. The pool's 26 July 2006 is **not the election date** but the start of term; both are kept as separate stops.
- **25 September 2007**: the quotation on evidence against her father (Q1) **[A: es.wikipedia, cite note 99]**.
- **7 April 2009**: Alberto Fujimori sentenced to 25 years for Barrios Altos and La Cantuta **[A: general record, in both Wikipedias]**. Keiko, a sitting congresswoman, opposes the verdict publicly from then on **[A]**.
- **22 July 2009**: assumes formal leadership of what becomes Fuerza Popular **[R: en.wikipedia, uncorroborated in this pass]**. **9 March 2010**: **Fuerza 2011** registered **[A: es.wikipedia]**.

## 6. First candidacy and runoff, 2010-2011

- **7 December 2010**: first presidential candidacy formalized at **Huaycán**, Ate **[A: es.wikipedia]**. Coordinates corrected to **-12.01389, -76.825** (pool was ~2.7 km off).
- **10 April 2011**: second place, 23.5%, into a runoff **[A]**.
- 2011 campaign: the "reconocer y pedir perdón" statement (Q2) **[A: es.wikipedia, cite note 152]**.
- **5 June 2011**: loses to Ollanta Humala. **The two Wikipedias give inconsistent decimals** (48.51-48.55% against 51.34-51.49%); see C4.
- The **Plaza San Martín closing rally of 2011** offered by the geography lens is unsourced, resting on the general pattern of Lima campaigns. **Not written as a stop**; the 2011 material is pinned to Lima.

## 7. Fuerza Popular and the second runoff, 2013-2016

- **4 January 2013**: Fuerza 2011 renamed **Fuerza Popular** **[A: es.wikipedia]**. Jesús María headquarters coordinates are **[R], approximate**.
- **3 April 2016**: signs the written pledge **"Compromiso de Honor con el Perú"** (Q4) **[A: es.wikipedia, cite note 195]**. A document, not a speech.
- **5 April 2016**: more than **50,000** fill the Plaza San Martín on the anniversary of the 1992 autogolpe, under the cry "Fujimori nunca más" **[A: es.wikipedia]**. **The pool dated this to 2021, five years wrong.** A smaller march followed in early June.
- **10 April 2016**: first round won with **39.86%**; Fuerza Popular takes **73 of 130** seats **[A: en.wikipedia on the election; seats per es.wikipedia]**. The es.wikipedia biography's "9 de abril" is an error; see C3.
- **May 2016**: campaigns through **Barranca, Pativilca and Paramonga** with her mother after twenty years of estrangement **[A: susana_higuchi.journey.json]**, whose verbatim remarks on that road are preserved in that file **[A: RPP Noticias 2016, via the file]**.
- **5 June 2016**: loses to Pedro Pablo Kuczynski, 49.88% to 50.12% **[A]**.

## 8. The cócteles case and the detentions, 2017-2020

- The investigation into undeclared Odebrecht campaign money opens in **2017**, not 2016 **[A: case file Exp. 00299-2017-335-5001-JR-PE-10, cited by es.wikipedia]**. Odebrecht's U.S. plea deal was December 2016; the pool conflated the two. The probe centred on **2011** first.
- **June 2018**: Kenji Fujimori's public remark to his sister (Q8) **[A: cite note 247]**. **Tone, sarcasm or surrender, is disputed in the Peruvian press and the source does not settle it.**
- **10 October 2018**: first arrest **[A]**; holding facility (DIRANDRO / Prefectura) **[R], approximate**. **18 October**: released on appeal **[A]**.
- **31 October 2018**: **36 months** of preventive prison ordered by judge **Richard Concepción Carhuancho** for money laundering, criminal organization, obstruction of justice and false declaration **[A: es.wikipedia]**. **Venue caveat (C7)**: that judge sat on the Juzgado de Investigación Preparatoria Nacional, not necessarily in the Palacio de Justicia. The stop uses the Palacio de Justicia pin inherited from susana_higuchi.journey.json (**-12.0577, -77.035**, correcting a pool value ~1.1 km off); the building is the weakest element of the stop.
- Prison: the **Establecimiento Penitenciario Anexo Mujeres de Chorrillos**, the Santa Mónica complex off Av. Defensores del Morro **[A as the named facility: es.wikipedia; Fuerza Popular article, "fue recluida en el Penal Anexo de Mujeres"]**. Coordinates: see C5.
- **29/30 November 2019**: released on Constitutional Court habeas corpus after ~13 months **[A: es.wikipedia, citing BBC]**. **The pool inverted this**, describing her as re-detained in November 2019.
- **28 January 2020**: third detention, 15 months ordered by judge **Víctor Zúñiga Urday** **[A]**. **5 May 2020**: released **on bail** **[A]**; the pool's "29 May" is wrong.
- **Total preventive custody ~16 months** **[A: es.wikipedia, "Caso Odebrecht en Perú"]**. The pool's "roughly two years" is used nowhere. See C6.

## 9. Third defeat, deaths in the family, 2021-2024

- **June 2021**: loses to Pedro Castillo, 49.87% to 50.13%, roughly 44,000 votes, with a prolonged refusal to concede **[A: en.wikipedia; margin from susana_higuchi.journey.json]**. Platform built on **"mano dura"**, government plan "Rescate 2021" **[A: es.wikipedia]**.
- **Campaign geography for every run is essentially undocumented.** The pool offered Arequipa and Cusco (2016), Trujillo and Iquitos (2021) as reconstructions from general tour patterns, with **no source for any specific visit**. Only **Trujillo** is written, tagged **[R]**; the rest are dropped rather than invented. (Iquitos, if ever needed: -3.749275, -73.244364.)
- **8 December 2021**: Susana Higuchi dies of cancer at the **Oncosalud clinic, San Borja**; Keiko announces it the same day (Q7) **[A: susana_higuchi.journey.json; quote verified against es.wikipedia]**.
- **2022**: divorce from Mark Vito Villanella **[A: en.wikipedia]**.
- **7 December 2022**: Pedro Castillo's self-coup attempt fails within hours; Congress removes and arrests him, Dina Boluarte is sworn in the same afternoon **[A: en.wikipedia, "2022-2023 Peruvian protests"; en.wikipedia, "Pedro Castillo"; es.wikipedia, "Crisis política en Perú de 2022"]**. Fuerza Popular's specific floor votes that day are not itemized by name in the sources consulted; only the bloc's documented pattern of obstructing Castillo (2021-2022) and its post-crisis support for Boluarte is attested. Added to the file without a corresponding pool item; no orchestrator note found explaining the addition.
- **December 2022-January 2023**: the crackdown following the self-coup kills 69 civilians nationwide **[A: en.wikipedia, "2022-2023 Peruvian protests"]**. Named specifically: Ayacucho airport, 15-16 December, 8 dead, security forces firing on a crowd nearing the airport; Juliaca/Puno airport, 9 January 2023, 17 dead, the single worst day, same pattern. Cusco's own federations march within days of Castillo's arrest, joined by Arequipa, Puno and other regional capitals demanding Boluarte's resignation and a new constitution. **No documented statement from Fujimori specifically addressing the deaths was located**; Fuerza Popular's support for Boluarte is documented at the party level, not as an individual floor vote by Fujimori on this question. Stop pinned at Cusco (-13.5319, -71.9675), the named anchor of the regional wave, not at her own location.
- **5/6 December 2023**: the Court orders Alberto Fujimori's release; he leaves **Barbadillo** on the 6th **[A: en.wikipedia]**. **The pool's 4 December is a day early.** Coordinates corrected to **-12.04286, -76.91356** (pool was ~7.4 km off). **Keiko's presence at the gate that day is not attested [R]**; her visiting across the years is general knowledge, not a cited fact.
- **11 September 2024**: Alberto Fujimori dies at eighty-six, Keiko present through the final illness **[A]**. **Exact site of death not reverified**; the Surco family residence is used (-12.135, -76.993).

## 10. The Court, the fourth candidacy, the presidency, 2025-2026

- **October 2025**: the Constitutional Court annuls the cócteles trial **[A: en.wikipedia]**. **Venue correction**: the Court's de jure seat is **Calle Misti 102, Yanahuara, Arequipa (-16.3914, -71.5411)**, not the Plaza de Armas the pool gave, and in practice it sits in Lima at the Casa de Pilatos. The campa does not claim she was in the room.
- **14 January 2026**: case dismissed and archived **[A: both Wikipedias]**. **The pool's reason was wrong**: the court did not find that prosecutors failed to try her in time. It held that "la figura penal de "receptación patrimonial" no podía aplicarse porque fue recién incorporada al código penal en el año 2016", a **non-retroactivity** holding, plus insufficient evidence of illicit origin. Not an acquittal on the facts, and the journey file says so.
- **7 June 2026**: wins the runoff against **Roberto Sánchez** (Juntos por el Perú), 50.13% to 49.87%, **49,641 votes** out of more than 18 million, after a **22-day count** turning on overseas ballots; Sánchez refuses to recognize the result, authorities and observers find no significant irregularity **[A: en.wikipedia]**.
- **28 July 2026**: sworn in, first woman elected to the Peruvian presidency by direct popular vote **[A: en.wikipedia]**.
- **Slogan gap**: the pool asserted a 2026 slogan "Perú con orden". **No source found supports it** in either Wikipedia. Dropped entirely: a slogan is either documented or it is not.

## 11. Afterlife and iconography: a category that does not apply

Alive and in office; no tomb, no cult. **No statue, monument or mural to her is documented**, the one mural reference being generic 2011 campaign material at Huaycán **[R: documentary gap, not a confirmed negative]**. **No book or memoir by her** was located, unlike her father's prison writings, so "editions and translations" has nothing to report **[R]**. The durable iconography of the career is her opponents': "Fujimori nunca más", the Plaza San Martín marches, the runoff maps.

## Contradictions flagged, not resolved

- **C1. The colegio.** The pool put it in San Isidro at -12.0928,-77.0428. es.wikipedia gives Av. Circunvalación del Golf Los Incas 368, **La Molina**, **-12.075, -76.961**, about 9 km away; the historic seat from 1893 was the Plazuela de la Recoleta in central Lima. Never San Isidro. La Molina used.
- **C2. End of the First Lady tenure.** The pool says 22 November 2000 in one line; both Wikipedias end it **21 November 2000**, the day of the vacancia. 21 November used.
- **C3. The 2016 first round.** es.wikipedia's biography dates both the vote and her reaction to 9 April; en.wikipedia's election article gives **10 April**, and a reaction cannot precede the result. The file dates the election to the 10th, carries Q3 there, and records the 9 April in date_confidence.
- **C4. 2011 runoff decimals.** The two Wikipedias disagree; both ranges given, neither adjudicated.
- **C5. Chorrillos coordinates.** The two research lenses gave -12.1875,-77.0075 and -12.175,-77.0122 for the same prison; neither is sourced and Wikipedia has none. **-12.172, -77.013** used in both stops for internal consistency, tagged approximate.
- **C6. Total detention.** ~13 months for the first stretch against "16 meses" total including 28 Jan to 5 May 2020. Both stated in their proper places.
- **C7. Venue of the 31 October 2018 order.** Judge, date and term are firm; the room is not.

## Interlock discipline

The only existing atlas traveler named inside a campa is **susana_higuchi**: mother, predecessor in the office of First Lady, campaign companion in 2016, guardian of the grandchildren during the 2018-2019 detention, and subject of Keiko's own quoted announcement of her death. Each is a direct familial relation, not shared geography.

Deliberately **not** named: **augusto_pinochet** and **manuel_noriega**.
