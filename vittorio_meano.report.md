# Vittorio Meano (1860-1904) research report
*2026-08-21. For `vittorio_meano.journey.json`. Born Gravere, Val di Susa; shot dead in his own house in Buenos Aires, 1 June 1904. Architect of the Palacio del Congreso de la Nación Argentina, second architect of the Teatro Colón, author of the winning scheme for Uruguay's Palacio Legislativo.*

**Legend. [A]** attested, source named · **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or a single-source report the gathering pass could not open directly. Contradictions are flagged and left standing.

**The central document.** Almost everything rests on **Baeza, Molinos and Sabugo, *Vittorio Meano*, Cuaderno de Crítica no. 77 (IAA-FADU-UBA, 1997)**, the only archival study of the man. Access split: the quotation pass **read** the PDF with printed-page precision, the geography pass got corrupted binary and could **not**. Hence several geographical claims carry [R] where the same study supports an [A] quotation elsewhere.

## 1. Piedmont, 1860-1884

**Birth [A].** 2 April 1860 at **Gravere**, Val di Susa, son of the notary Giovanni Battista Meano and Susanna Favretto (Museo Regionale dell'Emigrazione Piemontese, fetched). The study (p. 3) gives only "nace en 1860 en Gravere de Susa"; later summaries collapse Gravere into **Susa**, shorthand rather than a rival tradition. *Coordinate corrected* to **45.1061 / 7.0274**. **Gap:** the story that Susanna Favretto died bearing him is **not** in the study as read and was found nowhere independently.

**Father, Venaus, orphaning [R].** The study says only "huérfano desde muy pequeño." The father as notary of **Venaus** reaches this file through the unread PDF, and **no date for his death exists in anything reachable**. *Coordinate corrected* to **45.1549 / 7.0126**. The brother **Cesare**, an engineer, took charge of his education and later his employment [R].

### Contradiction 1: where he went to school

| source | claim |
|---|---|
| 1997 study, p. 3 | instituto, escuela técnica, gimnasio of **Pinerolo** |
| es.wikipedia | "se gradúa de geómetra en el Instituto de **Pinerolo**", 1878 |
| Museo dell'Emigrazione Piemontese (fetched) | "Frequentò le scuole tecniche di **Torino**" |

The museum is the same institution trusted for the birth date, so it cannot be demoted for one claim and kept for the other in silence. The dossier's [A] overstated Pinerolo; the stop keeps it with the Turin variant named.

**Geometra, 1878 [R].** Reportedly first in his class, with a 300-lire distinction from the Turin Chamber of Commerce and Arts. Note what the title is: a surveyor's qualification, the fact the Buenos Aires press reaches for when the Congress overruns.

**Accademia Albertina [R].** Petriella and Cutolo have him studying architecture there; the study repeats it only as "según algunos autores" and **flags it as unconfirmed by primary documentation**. No matriculation or diploma record located, so whether he held an architect's qualification is open in the literature itself.

**Recruitment and departure, 1884 [A for the year, R for the rest].** Tamburini took him on having "visto, apreciado y alabado sus dibujos," and on nothing else; single source, Eugenio Ollivero, speaking at a memorial six weeks after the murder. The departure and autumn arrival are in the study (p. 3) and independently in es.wikipedia and it.wikipedia, the often-repeated 1886 arrival being weaker. **Genoa is inferred**, not documented, and the story that Meano and **Luigia Fraschini** boarded as "Mehan" and as husband and wife is unverified.

## 2. Tamburini's studio, 1884-1890

*The dossier's geography lens carried **twelve latitude sign errors**, putting Buenos Aires stops in the North Atlantic. All corrected.*

**Cerrito 680 [R].** First address, from the unread PDF. Nominatim **-34.6006 / -58.3826**, one block from the future Colón site.

**Contradiction 2: the Casa Rosada.** Infobae (2020) puts the young Meano on Tamburini's state works "including the Casa Rosada." **Two direct fetches cut against it**: casarosada.gob.ar and es.wikipedia credit Kihlberg, Åberg and Tamburini and **never mention Meano**, and the dossier itself concedes no detail can be assigned to him. **Moreno 1550 [A for the building, R for Meano].** `francesco_tamburini.journey.json` attests the June 1886 project and the handoff from **Juan Antonio Buschiazzo**; Meano's hand is reported only by the study. Study and theatre agree, against the atlas. The stop is dated 1890-12-04 and says so.

## 4. The Congress, 1894-1904

**Law 3187, 20 November 1894 [A]** (argentina.gob.ar), with a February decree organizing the competition. **Entrants [A]:** 28 or 29 submissions, 17 identified since, Meano first and M. Turner second.

**Contradiction 4: when he won.** The dossier carried the win at **1895** (study pp. 8-9; argentina.gob.ar agrees) and a formal award at **8 January 1896** (argentina.gob.ar), reconciling neither. es.wikipedia separates the steps: competition 1895, deadline **12 October 1895**, winner named in the commission's seventh acta; **8 Jan 1896** approval of the *work-direction contract*; **31 Jul 1896** the contract itself. The file adopts that sequence and records the disagreement.

**"Génesis del Congreso" (1895) [A as text].** Two quotations used, study pp. 24-26 (primary pp. 29, 32): architecture as the eternal book, granite against mortar; and the eclectic method.

### Contradiction 5: the Besana contract

| element | Molinos 2011 | es.wikipedia | argentina.gob.ar |
|---|---|---|---|
| works begin | March 1897 | August 1897 | 1898 |
| contract sum | 5,776,545 m/n (via study p. 13) | **5,776,745** m/n | not given |

Two hundred pesos apart: a transcription slip in one chain or the other. **The file states no figure to the peso** and names all three datings.

**Foundations [R, downgraded].** Huergo's soil report, 23 February 1897 (Molinos 2011, note 9); roughly 1,500 piles and an inverted substructure under the dome; Massini and Duclout alongside. The dossier tagged this [A] on argentina.gob.ar, but **that page has no engineers' names, no pile count, no foundation dates**, having been fetched and checked for exactly those. The material traces only to Molinos 2011, whose text is 403-locked.

**Stone facing from 1898 [A]** (study p. 13; Molinos 2011 pp. 159-160). The hinge of the cost history: the legislature's own decision to face its house in cut stone, which Meano had argued for in 1895, sets off the supplementary contracts that produce the scandal.

**Correction: "Fray Gerundio" is not José Sixto Álvarez.** The dossier gave the January 1899 Caras y Caretas description of the scaffolded works to "Fray Gerundio (José Sixto Álvarez)." Álvarez signed himself **Fray Mocho**; *Fray Gerundio* was a short-lived **periodical** he wrote for (es.wikipedia). The file leaves the author unidentified.

**1900 [A for the cover, R for the rest].** Mayol's "El Hormigón" ran in Caras y Caretas no. 106, 13 October 1900. **Roca's verdict**, "antiartística, inadecuada y monstruosa," reported in Molinos 2011 with **no primary transcript located**, is placed at the building rather than the Casa Rosada, see §7. A commission including Ramos Mexía and Bermejo found Meano's and Besana's conduct in order, laying the overrun on the stone decision; **Law 3974** followed in November. The 1901-1902 suspension and capped certificates from 1903 are [R, downgraded]: the dossier tagged them [A] on a page that is merely "consistent with" a suspension, by its own admission, and consistency is not attestation.

**The 1900 album [R on the modern edition].** The originals of *El nuevo Teatro Colón y los principales teatros del mundo* were not located; the 2019 facsimile was confirmed **by page count only**, on a listing naming no compiler, publisher, ISBN or year, so the attribution to Molinos and Puppo is unsupported.

## 5. Montevideo, 1902/1903-1904

**Contradiction 6: who called the competition, and when.** The study (p. 11) dates the call to **1903** under President **José Batlle y Ordóñez**; es.wikipedia gives an enabling **law of 1902**, when **Juan Lindolfo Cuestas** was in office, Batlle taking office 1 March 1903. Crediting Batlle is an anachronism unless the study means the 1903 opening of submissions.

**Deadline and entries [A].** 15 April 1904, twenty-seven proposals, confirmed by es.wikipedia and the study (p. 12). The **"Agraciada"** motto, the Vienna Parliament model and the domeless Greek scheme are [R] (Tosoni 2019; PDF returned corrupted binary).

**Award [A in substance].** The advisory jury under **Juan Alberto Capurro** left first prize **vacant**, ranked Mendoza y Sáez's "Hispania" second and "Agraciada" third; the governing commission then judged Agraciada the more executable and voted to build it. es.wikipedia confirms the vacant prize, the award, and that Meano was "asesinado antes de poder ser contactado."

**Correction: the "great ship" quotation boundary.** The study's sentence, p. 14, is *"Habría sido (al decir de Meano) como 'un gran buque navegando angustiosamente entre las estrechas orillas de un pequeño río'."* **"Habría sido" is the study authors' framing, not Meano's**, so the file quotes only what sits inside the study's own quotation marks. Study page **14**, not 15; primary **Meano 1904, p. 27**. **Also unresolved:** one dossier entry places a near-identical metaphor in a **private letter of May 1900 about the Congress**. Both recorded, neither preferred.

## 6. 1 June 1904, and after

**The killing [A in outline].** Returned from the works about 9:55 a.m., found his former servant in the house, went up the stairs after him, was shot twice and died within minutes. Study p. 15 for the witness testimony, La Nación (2019) and Infobae (2020) for address and sequence; **date, former servant and two shots independently confirmed** by Juan Forn, *Página/12*, 3 April 2009. **Unverified:** the limp, the cane, the door frame, the left lung. **Contradiction:** Forn places the encounter "en un dormitorio," awkwardly beside the dossier's insistence that the "caught in bed" tableau is not established.

### Contradiction 7: the killer's name and age

| source | name | age |
|---|---|---|
| 1997 study, p. 15 | **Carlo** Passera | 28 |
| La Nación, 21 Nov 2019 | **Carlos M.** Passera | 25 |
| es.wikipedia; Sabugo's own essay | **Juan** Passera | not given |

The commonest online rendering (Juan) is the one the dossier omitted; the file names no forename in prose. **Last words [R]:** "¡Me han muerto, que embalsamen mi cadáver!" is press reconstruction (study p. 15), used with its status stated.

**Investigation [A].** Nogueira first on the scene; flight onto Rivadavia; capture order from Comisaría 7ª under Enrique Quintana; surrender to judge Constanzó within hours, pleading self-defence, accounts differing on whether that fell the same evening or the next morning. Conviction, **seventeen years** [R]. The precinct address was never established and the pin says so.

**Burial [A, upgraded].** Cementerio del Norte (Recoleta), cortege at 3 p.m., the SCA calling out its members (study p. 15). Forn (2009) **independently confirms** the vault of the **Botto and Solari** families, Genoese friends from the crossing; the dossier had this [R]. *Coordinate corrected* to **-34.5877 / -58.3936**, the two lenses having put the grave in different hemispheres. **Open gap:** whether the remains are still there, or the vault marked, nothing says.

**Correction: the "Ch" byline is not Christophersen.** **Alejandro Christophersen**, president of the SCA, gave a signed graveside address [A]. Separately an unsigned notice in *Revista Técnica-Arquitectura* I:4 (1904), bylined **"Ch"**, says Meano lacked "ese don de asimilación" a transplanted man needs; the dossier rendered that byline "Ch. [Alejandro Christophersen?]". **The study's apparatus contradicts the guess:** it lists *"Ch", "Víctor Meano"* and, separately, *Christophersen, "Discurso en el sepelio de Victor Meano"* in the same issue, and gives another unsigned 1904 piece there to "probablemente Chanourdie," its editor. The identification is dropped; study page **4**, not 5.

**The widow.** Letters to her found in the killer's lodging with a wardrobe and dental prescriptions (Caras y Caretas 1904, via study p. 15) [A]; questioned at the house 8 June, briefly detained [A]; instruction closed 10 June, homicide and concealment [R]; exonerated, returned to Italy [R]. **Later that year** a second dismissed servant, **Gaspar Pecchio** in the study and unnamed in La Nación, shot her through the arm and killed himself on the bed [R]; the study says "weeks after," La Nación about three months. **The motive is a gap and the scholarship treats it as one:** the biography's third annex is "El móvil de Passera."

## 7. Stops that belong to somebody else

Four relations in the pool are **another person's engagement** with Meano or his buildings, not scenes he attended. Per the standing rule they are cited as sources on his own stops and **not staged**:

- **Ollivero's memorial address**, Turin, 30 June 1904, a month after the death: cited on the Turin drawing-office stop.
- **Anatole France (1909)** and **Jules Huret (1910)**: cited at Meano's own Congress stop, which is about the building, not their travels.
- **Roca's verdict**: the dossier proposed staging it at the Casa Rosada; it sits at the Congress, the object being judged, Roca named as the client and not as a co-resident of the city.
- **Sabugo's and Forn's writing**: the "La Fama" segment describes the literature about Meano at Meano's own pin and follows no scholar anywhere.

**Two stops are memorials to Meano himself** and are treated as his: the Montevideo plazoleta (2019) and the Frossasco museum. Flagged so the operator can overrule.

### Apparatus relations for the operator

**None found requiring the exception.** `EXCEPTIONS.md` was read before anything was dropped. Meano is not listed and nothing in the pool needed him to be: no framework of his is applied backward, and nothing forward-references an artifact postdating 1904. The posthumous stops (1906 to 2020) are the reception of his own buildings and his own body, ordinary afterlife material rather than apparatus.

### Correction: the "arquitectos malditos" anachronism

The dossier claimed a "1900s-era Buenos Aires yellow press" grouped Meano with **Julián García Núñez** and **Arturo Prins** as cursed architects, dated 1907. **García Núñez died 12 November 1944, of colon cancer** (es.wikipedia), not violently and forty years after Meano; **Prins** likewise belongs to the 1940s, so no 1900s campaign can have grouped them, and Forn's sentence is retrospective, written in **2009**. The **1907 and 1914 commissions were not about the murder**: Forn has them investigating the **construction's finances**, with Palacios, Newbery and de la Torre among the members. De la Torre's separate pursuit of the elimination theory produced nothing. The file carries all three that way.

## 9. Gaps, stated as gaps

Year of the father's death: unknown. Whether Meano held a formal architecture qualification: unresolved in the literature itself. The 1884 ship and port: no manifest. The killer's forename and age: three renderings, two ages; the motive: no court established one. Disposition of the remains: unrecorded. Authorship of the standard biography: three attributions (Sabugo, per Digitalia; Molinos, per es.wikipedia; both, per the dossier), and the claimed 2004 first edition appears nowhere on the Digitalia page. Anatole France's original French: not located. No Buenos Aires memorial: a negative finding, not proof of absence.
