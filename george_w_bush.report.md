# George W. Bush: research report

*Compiled 2026-08-10 for the Atlas of Journeys. The subject is living (b. 6 July 1946): there is no tomb, and the afterlife lens records monuments and reputation instead.*

**Legend.** [A] attested, source named in line. [R] reconstruction, tradition, or contested interpretation. Where sources disagree, both readings are printed and neither is adjudicated. Unreachable sources are named with the reason. "The pool" is the verified research pool this was written from; its lenses are chronology, geography, quotes, interlock, afterlife.

## 4. The Guard (1968-1974)

The pool's largest single defect sits here, and it is a defect in Wikipedia's own prose.

- Chronology carried "Bush is commissioned into the Texas Air National Guard after joining the U.S. Air Force". That is a compression of ANG accession procedure, not two services: he enlisted directly in the Texas ANG on 27 May 1968, was commissioned second lieutenant, and never served in the regular Air Force. [A: Wikipedia, "George W. Bush military service controversy"] The Wikipedia sentence stands and is contradicted here.
- *Contradiction between the pool's own lenses.* Geography placed F-102 training at Moody AFB (1969-1970). Moody was 54 weeks of undergraduate pilot training on the T-37 and T-38; F-102 training followed at the 147th's Combat Crew Training School, Ellington Field, soloing March 1970 and graduating that June. [A: same source] Both are true of different places; the lens conflated them. The file carries the corrected sequence in one Ellington stop (29.6072, -95.1588).
- 1972: suspended from flying status for missing the annual physical; drills with the 187th at Montgomery; honorably discharged 21 Nov 1974. [A: Wikipedia] Two unit-name inconsistencies, not resolved: the 147th is "Reconnaissance Wing" in the modern article and "Fighter-Interceptor Group" in period usage; the Montgomery unit is "187th Fighter Wing" in chronology and 187th Tactical Reconnaissance Group in period records. Dannelly Field coordinates (32.3006, -86.3936) are this compilation's.
- *Gap.* The 2004 controversy over Alabama attendance and the Killian documents has no document-level source here. The file calls the paper trail thin on both sides and adjudicates nothing.

## 5. Harvard, Midland, the fortieth birthday (1973-1988)

- MBA, Harvard Business School, 1975 (42.3656, -71.1206 accepted). [A: Wikipedia; HBS records] *The pool contradicts itself:* chronology says "the only US president to hold that degree", geography "the first". "Only" is right; "first" implies a successor who does not exist.
- Marries Laura Welch 5 Nov 1977, Midland, after a few months' courtship [A: Miller Center]; Arbusto Energy founded 1977, merged into Spectrum 7 in 1984 with Bush as chairman [A: Wikipedia].
- *Date contradiction.* Chronology dates the Harken absorption of Spectrum 7 to 1987; the correction to 1986, for 200,000 shares, Bush then joining Harken's board. [A: Wikipedia] The file uses 1986 and records the disagreement in `date_confidence`.
- 1978: loses the Texas 19th district House race to Kent Hance. [A: Wikipedia] Lubbock coordinates (33.5779, -101.8552) are this compilation's; the pool gave no pin.
- Twins Barbara and Jenna born Nov 1981, Dallas, named for their grandmothers; July 1986, Colorado Springs, quits alcohol on his fortieth birthday, framed as the end of a pattern of selfishness rather than a diagnosis. [A: Miller Center] Broadmoor coordinates (38.7906, -104.8460) are this compilation's; the pool named the city only.

## 6. Rangers and governorship (1989-2000)

- April 1989: investor group buys the Texas Rangers for $89 million; Bush becomes managing general partner, secures public financing for a new stadium, and sells out in 1998 at a profit of about $15 million. [A: Wikipedia; Rangers organizational history]
- *Figure contradiction, left open.* Chronology says Bush personally invested $500,000; the verification note says the cited source gives $800,000 and that $500,000 is unsupported by it. The file states no figure.
- *Venue anachronism.* Geography dated "The Ballpark in Arlington" 1989-1994; it opened April 1994, and the Rangers played at Arlington Stadium 1989-1993. Coordinates 32.7513, -97.0829 are the Ballpark's. The file names both and says which pin it takes.
- Nov 1994: elected governor with 53.5% against Ann Richards; sworn in 17 Jan 1995 as the 46th governor; Nov 1998 re-elected with 68%. [A: Wikipedia; Miller Center; Texas State Archives]
- Prairie Chapel Ranch, Crawford, bought 1999, 1,583 acres (the pool's "1,600" rounds acceptably). *Largest positional error in the geography lens:* given 31.6621, -97.3811, roughly 19 km off; correct 31.5825, -97.5439. [A: Wikipedia]

## 7. The 2000 election

- *State count.* Chronology says 29 states; he carried 30 (Gore 20 plus D.C.). The "29" is in the Wikipedia "George W. Bush" article and is contradicted by the election article and the state tally. [A: Wikipedia] The file states no state count.
- *Bush v. Gore date.* Chronology gives 9 Dec 2000 for the ruling. That was the stay; the case was argued 11 Dec, decided 12 Dec. [A: Wikipedia] The file dates the stop 2000-12-12 and names both orders.
- 271 electoral votes to 266; popular vote lost by 543,895; Cheney picked 25 July 2000. [A: Wikipedia]

## 9. Second term (2004-2009)

- Re-elected Nov 2004, 286 electoral votes and 50.7%; 2005, partial privatization of Social Security the top domestic priority, not carried as a stop for want of geography or an event date; Katrina landfall 29 Aug 2005 and the Jackson Square address 15 Sept 2005 (29.9575, -90.0630); Nov 2006 Democrats take both houses; Jan 2007 the surge; Dec 2007 the longest post-WWII recession begins; 2008 TARP. [A: Wikipedia; White House archives]
- *Wrong place entirely in the pool.* Geography named Anbar province in its text and pinned Al Faw Palace, Baghdad (33.2778, 44.2000): a contradiction inside one entry. The 3 Sept 2007 visit was to Al Asad Airbase in Anbar (33.80, 42.43), about 100 miles west of Baghdad, with Rice, Gates and Pace. [A: Wikipedia] Al Faw Palace is at 33.2840, 44.2561, so even the Baghdad pin sat 5 km off its own named building.
- *Tag correction.* Chronology tagged the 15 Jan 2009 farewell address [R], "date not confirmed". It is [A] and primary: whitehouse archives release 20090115-17.
- 8 Aug 2008, Beijing National Stadium (39.9928, 116.3975), first sitting US president at an Olympics abroad, recorded here and not carried as a stop; 20 Jan 2009, leaves office from Andrews (38.8108, -76.8669). [A: contemporary coverage]

## 11. Quotes: verified, corrected, rejected

The most heavily corrected part of the pool. **Carried into the journey file:**

1. Saddam Hussein, Iraqi Special Tribunal, 1 July 2004: "This is all theater. The real criminal is Bush" [A: Wikipedia, "Trial of Saddam Hussein", first hearing, verbatim]. *The lens's note is struck:* the article does not support the claim that he accused the tribunal of being staged to help Bush's re-election; that belongs to the 5 Nov 2006 verdict-date episode. The source carries no terminal period and the file reproduces it so.
2. Chavez, televised speech, March 2006, "Mr. Danger" [A: en.wikiquote.org]. Every phrase is genuine and in this order, but the lens rendered a compression as contiguous speech. The file carries the ellipsis-marked version and the source's "You are immoral, Mr. Danger".
3. Chavez, UN General Assembly, 20 Sept 2006 [A: Wikisource, A/61/PV.12]. The lens spliced Wikiquote's informal rendering onto a press rendering; the file uses the official UN translation verbatim. The lens's note calling the sulphur line a day-after remark is wrong: it is one sentence later in the speech.
4. Verbatim and uncorrected: Putin to Bush, Bucharest, 4 Apr 2008 [A: Time, via Wikiquote]; Bush at the WTC site (2001), aboard USS Abraham Lincoln (2003), and "a grave and gathering danger" (Cincinnati, 2002) [A: White House archives].

**Verified but not carried as stops, recorded so they are not lost.** Chavez, Caracas, 1 Mar 2004, "He was an asshole to believe them." [A: Wikiquote], with a gap: no context for what "them" refers to, so rather than invent one it is left out · Chavez, *Alo Presidente*, 20 Feb 2005, "If I am assassinated, there is only one person responsible: the president of the United States." [A: Wikiquote] · bin Laden, 2004, "President Bush and his ilk, the media giants, and the U.N. . . all are a fatal danger to the world" [A: Smith, Long and Johnson, *Strategic Culture and Violent Non-state Actors*, USAF INSS, 2008, p. 33, via Wikiquote] · Putin to Sarkozy, 12 Aug 2008, "I am going to hang Saakashvili by the balls." [A: Levitte, via *Le Nouvel Observateur* and *The Telegraph*, 13 Nov 2008], whose follow-on appears as reported speech in the Bucharest stop · Kofi Annan, CNBC obituary, 18 Aug 2018, on Bush telling the UN it was headed toward irrelevance [A: Wikiquote], referenced in the 2002 UN stop without being quoted.

**Rejected as unverifiable.** Ahmadinejad, open letter to Bush, 8 May 2006, "Can one be a follower of Jesus Christ ... and have countries attacked?" [R]. The Wikisource text was unfetchable (repeated HTTP 404 on the title held by the pool), so the wording could not be checked against the primary. Not used in any form.

**Duplicate flagged.** The lens carried the Chavez UN passage twice: English dated 20 Sept, Spanish dated 21 Sept and tagged [R] as having no locatable primary. The Spanish primary was located (es.wikisource.org, "Discurso del Presidente Hugo Chávez en la Asamblea General de la ONU de 2006"): "Ayer vino el Diablo aquí. Ayer estuvo el Diablo aquí, en este mismo lugar. [...] Huele a azufre todavía esta mesa donde me ha tocado hablar." Three defects: word order; the sulphur line truncated; the date, which is 20 September. Only the official English translation is carried.

## 13. Honest gaps

1. The 2004 Guard controversy has no document-level source in this pool; nothing is adjudicated.
2. The Rangers personal stake: $500,000 in one lens, $800,000 per the correction's reading of the same source. No figure is stated.
3. Who hung the "Mission Accomplished" banner is not settled by any source here.
4. The start of the painting practice ("around 2012") is soft in every available source.
5. *Decision Points* translated editions: the pool declines an authoritative list, and so does this file.
6. The 2004 Chavez "asshole" quote is verified as text and unusable without its missing context.
7. The Ahmadinejad letter could not be fetched; named with the reason.
8. The 187th and 147th unit designations are inconsistent between sources; not resolved.
9. Dannelly Field, Lubbock, the Broadmoor, Memorial Sloan-Kettering and Al Asad coordinates were supplied by this compilation, not by the pool, which named towns only. Flagged in `date_confidence`, to be checked against a gazetteer before any globe rebuild. Al Asad is given by the correction to two decimals only (33.80, 42.43) and is carried at that precision, not falsely extended.
