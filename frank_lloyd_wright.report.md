# Frank Lloyd Wright (1867-1959): research report

*Compiled 2026-08-11. Slug `frank_lloyd_wright`. Queued at QUEUE.md line 280 after Brosterman's *Inventing Kindergarten*, which puts Wright in the same Froebel-descended lineage as the already-researched Buckminster Fuller.*

**Legend.** **[A]** = attested, source named. **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition, self-report, or conjecture. Contradictions are flagged and **not** adjudicated. Coordinates and dates that could not be pinned are stated as gaps rather than filled.

The pool arrived with its own audit: roughly a third of its entries carry a `note` beginning CORRECTED, where a verification pass caught the gathering pass in a bad date, a coordinate off by one to three kilometres, or an inflated tag. Those are carried forward; where one contradicts another entry in the same pool, **both readings are recorded and neither is chosen.**

## 1. Wisconsin and the Gifts (1867-1886)

- Born **8 June 1867**, Richland Center, to William Cary Wright and Anna Lloyd Jones **[A: Wikipedia; Secrest (1992)]**. Pin 43.3378, -90.3854; the birth house is demolished, so the pin is the town. Wright claimed **1869** through much of his adult life **[A]**: the gathering pass filed it [R], verification corrected to [A] as a documented self-backdating, not a legend. Accepted.
- **Weymouth, Massachusetts**, William ministering at the First Congregational church. **Dates contradict inside the pool:** geography gives 1876-1878, verification corrects to **1874 to c. 1877-78**. File uses 1874. **[A for the residence, contested for the span.]**
- **1876: the Froebel Gifts** enter the household **[A: Wikipedia]**. But **the Centennial Exhibition claim is [R], not [A].** That Anna met the system *at* the 1876 Philadelphia Centennial rests **solely on Wright's 1932/1943 autobiography**; Wikipedia carries the Gifts *without* it. The gathering pass tagged this [A] twice (geography[34], afterlife[4]) and verification demoted both as retrospective self-mythology. Carried [R], pinned at Fairmount Park (39.9812, -75.2078) as a tradition, not a documented movement.
- Wright credits the Gifts as the root of his sense of form **[A as a statement he made] / [R as causal history]**. One pool entry calls the blocks "the cube, sphere, and triangle"; Froebel's Second Gift is **sphere, cylinder, cube**. The file does not repeat the error.
- **1878-1885, Madison**, East Gorham Street, local schools, summers on the Lloyd Jones farms **[A]**. **Coordinate corrected:** the pool's 43.0731, -89.3812 is on the Monona side, south-east of the Capitol; East Gorham runs along Mendota, north of it. Re-pinned 43.0806, -89.3781. **1881** separation; **1884** William files; **1885** divorce final, William leaves, father and son never meet again **[A]**.
- **1886** admitted to UW-Madison as a **special student** under Allan D. Conover; leaves without a degree **[A]**. **Contradiction:** geography[4] gives 1885-1887, chronology[5] gives 1886. Both recorded, 1886 used.

**Gap.** Nothing reachable names *which* Lloyd Jones farm, or the years worked. The Valley stop is pinned at the later Taliesin coordinate as a stand-in.

## 2. Chicago and Lieber Meister (1887-1893)

- **1887:** draftsman for **Joseph Lyman Silsbee**, on projects including All Souls Church **[A]**. That Silsbee designed the Lloyd Jones chapel, and that this is why Wright went to him, is **[R]** and stays [R].
- **November 1887:** joins **Adler and Sullivan** **[A]**. **Contradiction flagged:** geography gives 1888-1893 at the Auditorium Building; verification corrects the start to 1887 and notes **the firm did not occupy its Auditorium tower offices until 1890**. The file pins the Auditorium and states the first years were worked elsewhere. Sullivan is **"Lieber Meister"** **[A: *An Autobiography*]**; his loan for the Oak Park house is biography-standard, **not in the pool**, flagged in the file.
- **1889:** marries **Catherine "Kitty" Tobin**; Oak Park house **[A]**. **Coordinate corrected** to 41.8942, -87.7997; the pool's pin is ~370 m south-east. **1890:** head draftsman with **George Elmslie**. **1893:** the **"bootlegged houses"** discovered, departure after a dispute, own practice in the **Schiller Building** **[A]**.
- **1894:** the **Winslow House**, River Forest **[A]**. **Gap: no coordinate in the pool;** pinned at the River Forest centroid. **1896:** Steinway Hall, with **Marion Mahony** among the Prairie School collaborators **[A]**; folded into the Winslow stop.

**Contested, left contested.** A claim surfaced that Wright, under Sullivan, helped design pavilions for the 1893 Columbian Exposition. **Nothing in the verified pool attests this**, and he left the firm that year. The file's 1893 stop puts Wright and the White City in one city and one year without asserting a working relationship.

## 3. Oak Park and the Prairie (1898-1909)

- **Feb 1898:** studio added to the Oak Park house **[A]**. **1900:** ~fifty projects, four houses inaugurating the **Prairie Style**; **1901:** the two *Ladies' Home Journal* house articles **[A]**.
- **1902-1907: Hillside Home School** for Nell and Jane Lloyd Jones **[A]**. Pin 43.1417, -90.0781. **1903-1905: Darwin D. Martin House**, Buffalo **[A]**, pin uncorrected; chronology gives completion 1904, geography 1903-1905, both recorded.
- **1904-1906: Larkin Administration Building** **[A]**. Designed Jan 1903 to Apr 1904, opened late 1906, demolished Feb-July 1950. **Coordinate corrected ~1.7 km** to 42.8772, -78.8519 (680 Seneca St).
- **Chronology entry 19 is internally broken and is flagged, not used.** It reads: "1905: Wright completes Unity Temple and designs the Larkin Administration Building." **Unity Temple was designed 1905, built to 1908, dedicated Sept 1909**; **Larkin was designed 1903-04 and opened 1906.** Both halves contradict the pool's own geography array. The file splits them onto the geography dates.
- The remark that Unity Temple is where he ceased to be an "architect of structure" and became an "architect of space" **[A as attributed]**; **gap:** no primary text located. **1903:** Wright becomes enamoured of **Mamah Borthwick Cheney** **[A]**.
- **1907-1908: Boynton House**, Rochester **[A]**. The pool's coordinate self-declares approximate and points downtown; the real address is 16 East Boulevard, ~43.157, -77.591. **Dropped from the file for length**, the coordinate recorded so a later pass need not re-derive it. **1908-1910: Robie House**, Hyde Park **[A]**, pin 41.7906, -87.5964.

**Rejected as a routing fiction.** The pool's geography entry for "Aurora, New York / Sunnyside area" (42.7548, -76.7028) calls itself a waypoint with "precise stop uncertain," tagged **[R]**, inferred from period rail routes, "not independently attested." **Not used.** A journey file should not carry a pin whose only warrant is that a man must have passed through somewhere.

## 4. Europe, Taliesin, and the hatchet (1909-1914)

- **1909:** leaves Catherine and six children for Europe with Mamah Cheney, nearly a year abroad **[A]**. **1910: Berlin**, the **Wasmuth Portfolio** **[A: Secrest; Alofsin]**. **Date contradiction:** geography dates the Berlin work 1910, chronology dates publication **1911**. Probably the overseeing then the publication, but no source reached says so; both carried, stop dated 1910.
- **1910: Fiesole [A for the residence]**. **Two flags.** The pool names the house **"Villa Fortuna"**, judged invented or garbled by verification, which gives **Villino Belvedere, Via Verdi**; the file names no villa. And Borthwick spent part of 1910 in Germany and Sweden pursuing her divorce, so continuous cohabitation there is over-tight.
- **May 1911: Taliesin** begun on land his mother buys **[A]**. **Coordinate corrected** to 43.1411, -90.0706; the pool's 43.1478, -90.0725 is ~750 m north, an error repeated at **four separate entries**, all corrected. **1913-1914: Midway Gardens**, demolished during Prohibition **[A]**, pin 41.7853, -87.6033; no demolition year is in the pool, so none is stated in the file.
- **15 August 1914: Julian Carlton** fires Taliesin's living quarters and kills seven, including Mamah Borthwick and her two children **[A: *Weekly Home News*, Aug 1914, via Secrest]**. **Correction:** both pool entries say axe; verification gives a **roofing hatchet or shingling tool**, used here with the divergence flagged. The other four dead are named only in standard accounts, not in the pool, so the file does not itemise them.

## 5. Tokyo, Los Angeles, the second fire (1916-1927)

- **The Japan dating contradicts three ways, left contradicted.** Chronology[23]: work begins **1917**. Geography[16]: **1913-1923**. Verification: first visit **1905**, commission **1916**, resident supervising **1917-1922**. The file opens at **1916** and states all three.
- **1919-1921: Hollyhock House** for Aline Barnsdall **[A]**. Delegation to Schindler and Lloyd Wright, and Barnsdall's gift to the city, are institutional-literature standard, **not in the pool**, flagged in the file.
- **1923: Millard House, "La Miniatura,"** first of four textile-block houses **[A]**. **Coordinate corrected ~850 m** to 34.1554, -118.1618; and the pool filed it under Los Angeles when **Pasadena is a separate city**. **1923: Imperial Hotel** completed, survives the **Great Kanto Earthquake** that year, demolished 1968 **[A]**.
- **1924-1925: Ennis House**, largest of the textile-block houses **[A]**. **Coordinate corrected ~800 m** to 34.1161, -118.2928. **"Last" is disputed:** the construction order is contested, some accounts placing Freeman after Ennis. The file says largest, not last.
- **1922** Catherine grants the divorce; **Nov 1923** marriage to **Miriam Noel**, collapsing within a year, reportedly over morphine (reported, not documented); **1923** Anna dies **[all A]**.
- **20 April 1925: the second Taliesin fire. Cause contradicted, not resolved.** Chronology[32]: **crossed telephone wires**. Geography[20]: a **lightning-caused electrical fire**, stated as fact. Verification: neither is established, Wright's own account putting the origin near a bedroom telephone and scholars only **theorising** a storm surge. The dispute goes in `date_confidence`. Second correction: it destroyed the **living quarters of Taliesin II**, not a "bungalow." The **Japanese print collection** burns; he rebuilds as **Taliesin III**.
- **1924** meets Olgivanna; **1925** they move to Taliesin; **3 Dec 1925** Iovanna born; **Oct 1926** both arrested at **Tonka Bay, Minnesota** on Mann Act charges, later dropped; **1927** Noel divorce final; **1928** marries Olgivanna **[all A]**. **Omission on purpose:** the pool carries **no coordinate for Tonka Bay**, so the arrest gets no stop rather than a guessed pin.

## 6. Fellowship, Broadacre, Usonia (1929-1941)

- **1929: Ocatillo**, canvas-and-wood desert camp near Chandler, drawn to win the never-built San Marcos-in-the-Desert **[R, stays R]**. Pin 33.2861, -111.8256. **1932:** *The Disappearing City* and **Broadacre City**, an acre a family on 2,560 acres **[A]**.
- **1932: Taliesin Fellowship**, 23 initial students **[A]**. **Location contradiction:** the pool gives **three** pins (43.1417/-90.0781 Hillside; 43.1441/-90.073; 43.1411/-90.0706 Taliesin) and its own note says it operated out of **Hillside**. The file uses Hillside. **1934: Willey House**, Minneapolis, early Usonian prototype **[A]**: **no coordinate in the pool**, so no stop. Gap.
- **1935-1939: Fallingwater**, ~**$155,000 including an $8,000 fee** **[A]**. Geography gives 1935-1939, chronology 1935-1937; both defensible, neither chosen. The cantilever dispute with Kaufmann's engineers is not in the pool.
- **1936-1939: Johnson Wax**, hired late July 1936, built from Sept 1936, completed April 1939 **[A]**. **Coordinate corrected ~1.6 km** to 42.7136, -87.7908. The column load test is traditional, not in the pool. **1937: Jacobs First House**, Madison, first fully realised Usonian **[A]**; **no coordinate in the pool**, pinned on Madison.
- **1937-1938: Taliesin West** **[A]**. **Three corrections:** land bought **1937**, construction begins **1938**; the pool's **"Cave Creek Rd" is wrong** (Taliesin West is at 12621 N. Frank Lloyd Wright Blvd); **coordinate corrected** to 33.6064, -111.8453.
- **Florida Southern College:** commission **1938** (Spivey's approach), first building **1941**, span **1941-1958**, **12 of 18** built, largest single-site collection **[A]**. **The pool dates the commission 1941**, conflating it with the first completion; corrected. **No coordinate in the pool;** pinned on the campus.

## 7. The spiral and the end (1943-1959)

- **1943: Guggenheim** design begun, occupying him until death **[A]**. Pin 40.783, -73.959, byte-identical from `hilma_af_klint` and `cecilia_vicuna`. **1947-1951: First Unitarian Society Meeting House**, Madison **[A]**, pin 43.0761, -89.4442. **1952-1956: Price Tower**, his only realised skyscraper **[A]**, pin 36.7473, -95.9758.
- **9 April 1959:** dies at Phoenix, aged 91, after surgery **[A]**. **Two flags:** **St Joseph's Hospital** is standard but **could not be confirmed from a fetchable source**; and the pool's pin is ~800 m off, so the file uses 33.4795, -112.0872.
- **Pool self-contradiction on the interval:** geography[31] says "five months" to the Guggenheim opening; afterlife[8] and geography[27] say six. **9 April to 21 October is just over six.** Arithmetic, not a source disagreement; six is used.

## 8. Afterlife

- **April 1959:** buried in the Lloyd Jones graveyard beside **Unity Chapel** **[A]**. **Coordinate corrected** to 43.1325, -90.0614; the pool's two entries (43.1394/-90.0669 and 43.144/-90.076) are 0.9 and 1.7 km off. Day of burial not reached. **21 October 1959:** the **Guggenheim** opens, six months after his death **[A]**. **1963:** Fallingwater to the Western Pennsylvania Conservancy **[A]**; no stop, recorded here.
- **1985:** at Olgivanna's instruction, Wright's remains are **exhumed, cremated, and mixed with hers** at **Taliesin West**, against the Wisconsin family's wishes and over Wisconsin Legislature objections **[A: Wikipedia; Secrest; Huxtable]**. **Coordinate corrected** to 33.6064, -111.8453; the pool's pin was ~2.9 km off. The Wisconsin headstone reportedly remains over an empty grave **[R: Building Conservancy notes and local press, unconfirmed]**, and the "body-snatching" characterisation is **[R]**, interpretive tradition on an attested event.
- **2012:** the Foundation archive goes jointly to **Avery Library** (Columbia) and **MoMA** **[A]**. Pin 40.8075, -73.9626. Contents not itemised in the pool, so no counts are given. **July 2019:** UNESCO inscribes **eight** buildings as one distributed World Heritage Site, the first modern US architecture listed **[A]**; the pool names only four of the eight, so the file names those four and pins at Taliesin, the site having no single coordinate.
- ***An Autobiography*** published 1932, substantially rewritten 1943, the two treated as distinct texts for dating his self-mythology **[A: Levine]**. **Gap:** the extent and motive of the 1943 revisions, and whether the Froebel origin story was reshaped in them, was not resolved. That story is the hinge on which Wright's presence in this atlas turns.

## 10. Interlocks with the existing atlas

One hard negative first: **no journey file in `working/journeys` contains the string "Taliesin" or "Fallingwater."** Wright's whole atlas presence was two references to a spiral ramp and one bridge from Froebel to Fuller.
