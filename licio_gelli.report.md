# Licio Gelli (1919-2015): research report

*Compiled 2026-08-11. Companion file: `licio_gelli.journey.json` (44 stops, 9 segments).*

**Legend.** **[A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, single-source report,
or a claim the sources themselves flag as unconfirmed. Contradictions are printed, not adjudicated.
Where nothing was reachable, that is said.

One warning first. Gelli's biography was, in his lifetime, an instrument: he claimed credit for
things he was acquitted of and denied things he was later convicted of. The two Wikipedias disagree
on his marriage venue, his employer, the town of his last arrest, the building his list was found
in, and the city of his funeral. Almost every correction below runs one way: the Italian record is
more precise and more often right.

---

## I. Pistoia and Spain, 1919-1939

- Born **Pistoia, 21 April 1919**, modest family. **[A** Wikipedia EN/IT**]** Pin is Piazza del
  Duomo, a town point; no birth address is given anywhere.
- **Seventeen years old**, at the **liceo classico**, he strikes a teacher and is **expelled from
  every school in Italy**. **[A** IT Wikipedia: "espulso da tutte le scuole d'Italia (aveva preso a
  schiaffi un professore)"**]** **CORRECTED:** the "1930" of English summaries is wrong by about six
  years; aged seventeen means **c. 1936-37** (in 1930 he was ten). Journey dates it `1936-01-01`.
  School coordinate approximate (Liceo Forteguerri, Piazza San Francesco, c. 43.9330 / 10.9142).
- **1937**: volunteers for Franco, fights at **Málaga** (February), his brother **Raffaello** is
  killed, **Franco decorates him in person** as the youngest recruit of the contingent. **[A]**
- **1939**: member of the **National Fascist Party**. **[A]** No federation, no enrolment date;
  folded into the Málaga stop.

## II. War, Salò, the postwar hole, 1940-1968

- **1940**: **liaison officer between the Italian government and Nazi Germany**. **[A]** No posting,
  unit or city named anywhere. Gap.
- After the **armistice of September 1943**: Republican Fascist Party, and the **Italian Social
  Republic** at Salò with **Giorgio Almirante**. **[A]** Journey pins Salò (45.6053 / 10.5205) and
  dates `1943-09-23`, the RSI's own foundation, flagged as not a date attached to Gelli personally.
- **16 December 1944**: marries **Wanda Vannacci**. **[A** IT Wikipedia, to the day**]**
  **CORRECTED:** the source pool placed the wedding in the Cattedrale di San Zeno. **No source names
  a church.** The venue is dropped; the pin is the town, and `date_confidence` says so.
- **c. 1945, the ratlines.** The tradition that Gelli moved Nazi and fascist fugitives to South
  America. **[R]** Repeated throughout the P2 literature, **confirmed by nothing reachable**: no
  route, port, date or name. **Left out of the journey entirely** rather than given a fabricated pin.
- **1956**: **commercial director of Permaflex at Frosinone**, in Cassa del Mezzogiorno territory.
  **[A** IT Wikipedia**]** **DOUBLE CORRECTION:** English summaries had him a *salesman* in *1950*
  in *Tuscany*, pinned at Florence "as a regional placeholder". Wrong role, year and region.
  Frosinone is in Lazio, 41.6396 / 13.3419, c. 220 km south-east of that pin. The pool's [R] can be
  **upgraded to [A]**: the Italian source does name the city.
- **1968**: buys the villa at Arezzo, then **Villa Carla**, from the **Lebole** family on taking a
  partnership in their mattress firm Dormire, and renames it **Villa Wanda**. **[A** IT Wikipedia**]**
  **CORRECTED:** the pool's "anchored his life since the marriage that named it" implies 1944 and is
  wrong by twenty-four years.

## III. The lodge, 1963-1975

- **6 November 1963**: initiated in Rome, lodge **Gian Domenico Romagnosi**, **Grande Oriente
  d'Italia**. **[A** EN Wikipedia, "Propaganda Due"**]** **VENUE FLAGGED:** the pool pinned Palazzo
  Giustiniani, the Grand Orient's seat; **no source documents it as the initiation venue**. Pin kept
  and labelled approximate; longitude corrected from 12.4728 to **12.4750** (c. 180 m).
- **1970**: appointed to **reorganize Propaganda Due**. **[A]**
- **7-8 December 1970, Golpe Borghese**: Gelli **reportedly** tasked with arresting President
  **Saragat** at the Quirinal. **TAG CORRECTED [A] to [R]:** IT Wikipedia records the role as
  **"non confermato"**; he was never tried for it. Quirinal coordinate corrected to 41.8997 /
  12.4874 (pool's point was c. 340 m west).
- **1975**: **Venerable Master**; P2 becomes clandestine and illegal. **[A]**
- The **Piano di rinascita democratica** (magistracy under the executive, two parties, a smaller
  parliament, provinces abolished, the press acquired, universities as private foundations).
  **[A** IT Wikipedia, after the **Anselmi commission**.**]** Three verbatim passages carried as
  quote fields. **GAP:** the pool's "fifty-three points" is supported by nothing reachable.

- **August 1974**: **Gran Cruz de la Orden del Libertador** from chancellor **Alberto Vignes**.

- **Oil and arms brokerage Libya-Italy-Argentina; consultancy to Isabel Perón and López Rega.**
  **[R] CORRECTED:** the pool tagged this [A] citing EN Wikipedia, "Licio Gelli"; **none of it is in

## V. The bank, 1971-1982

- **Roberto Calvi**: general manager of **Banco Ambrosiano** 1971, chairman 1975, P2 member. **[A]**
  - **ADDRESS CORRECTED:** headquarters is **Largo Bortolo Belotti** (c. 45.4676 / 9.1869), not Via
    Clerici; the two are adjacent and the square is the address EN Wikipedia gives.
  - **FIGURE CORRECTED:** the hole is **$1.287 billion**, not $1.4 billion.
- **Banco del Gottardo**, Lugano, 45 percent through the Luxembourg holding. **[A]**
- **Banco Ambrosiano Overseas**, Nassau, with **Marcinkus** on the board, and **Banco Ambrosiano
  Andino**, Lima. **[A]** Both verified; both **cut from the journey for length**, and left to the
  queued Calvi, Sindona and Marcinkus files (QUEUE.md 747-749).
- **IOR**, Vatican City; **$224 million** paid to creditors in 1984 in recognition of "moral
  involvement". **[A]** **1981**: Calvi convicted of illegal currency export. **[A]**

## VI. The list, 1980-1981

- **2 August 1980**: **Bologna station bombing**, 85 dead. **[A]** See section IX.
- **January 1981**: **invited to Reagan's inauguration**. **[A]** 20 January is the ceremony date,
  not one any source attaches to his attendance.
- **17 March 1981**: the **Guardia di Finanza** searches **Villa Wanda** *and* the **Giole works at
  Castiglion Fibocchi**, recovering the roll of **962 names**, **Silvio Berlusconi** among them,
  enrolled 1978. **[A** Wikipedia, "Licio Gelli" and "Propaganda Due";
  `silvio_berlusconi.journey.json`**]**
  - **CONTRADICTION, NOT RESOLVED.** IT Wikipedia's *P2* article puts the roll in the **factory
    archive**; its *Villa Wanda* article describes the villa search and the **gold ingots buried in
    the garden**; English summaries put the roll in the villa. The journey splits the day into two
    stops and prints the disagreement.
  - **COORDINATE CONFLICT, DELIBERATELY UNRESOLVED.** `silvio_berlusconi.journey.json` pins
    Castiglion Fibocchi at **43.5083 / 11.7683**; the geography lens computed **43.5533 / 11.7694**,
    c. 5 km north. Canonical pins are inherited byte-identical, so the journey uses the former and
    records the latter.
  - **VILLA WANDA COORDINATE CORRECTED, eight times.** IT Wikipedia gives **43.4467 / 11.8819**;
    the pool carried 43.47 / 11.885 and 43.46 / 11.88, c. 1.5 to 2.6 km north of the house.
- **1980**: a **Madonna della Consolazione of 1512**, stolen from a sanctuary at **Rotonda**,
  recovered from the villa's garden. **[A** IT Wikipedia**]**
- **31 October 1981**: **expelled from the Grande Oriente d'Italia**. **[A]**
- **Parliament declares P2 a criminal conspiracy against the constitutional order and dissolves it
  by law.** **[A** `silvio_berlusconi.journey.json`**] GAP:** dated 1981 without a day, and it must
  postdate the March seizure. Folded into the 31 October stop with the missing date stated.

## VIII. Fugitive and defendant, 1984-1998

- **1984-1987**: fugitive in South America, **reportedly Chile**. **[R] TAG CORRECTED:** the pool's
  chronology lens said [A] while its geography lens said [R] for the identical claim. Both made [R]:
  no address, arrival date or host is documented.
- **September 1987**: **surrenders in Geneva** to judge **Jean-Pierre Trembley**. **[A]**
  **DETAIL DROPPED:** "sentenced to two months in Swiss prison" is in neither Wikipedia article and
  could not be confirmed. Palais de Justice sharpened from 46.2 / 6.15 to 46.2003 / 6.1478.
- **15 December 1987**: **eight years in absentia at Florence** for financing right-wing terrorism
  in Tuscany. **[A]**
- **February 1988**: **extradited** under an operation reported to involve c. 100 marksmen, decoy
  cars, a train, roadblocks, two armoured cars. **[R]** The pool's lenses tagged this [A] and [R];
  the lower tag is kept, the detail reading as press description.
- **July 1988, Bologna**: **acquitted of subversive association, convicted of slander**, five years.
  **[A]**
- **1992**: **18 years 6 months** for Ambrosiano fraud, reduced to **12** on appeal; **April 1998**
  the **Cassation** confirms the 12. **[A]**
- **April 1994**: **17 years** for divulging state secrets. **[A] GAP:** the appellate outcome is
  recorded nowhere reachable.
- **23 November 1995**: the **Cassation sentences him definitively to 10 years as promoter of the
  depistaggio** of the Bologna investigation. **[A** IT Wikipedia, "Strage di Bologna"**]**
  **Absent from the pool's chronology lens altogether**, and restored: it is what makes the pool's
  "no definitive terrorism conviction" claim misleading. Pinned institutionally at Piazza Cavour;
  **no source gives a venue**.
- **1996**: **house arrest** near Arezzo **[A]**; **Nobel Prize in Literature nomination** **[R**,
  uncheckable: the committee seals nominations fifty years**]**.
- **May 1998** he vanishes from house arrest; **September 1998** arrested on the **Côte d'Azur** with
  over **$2 million in gold bullion**. **[A]** **CONTRADICTION, NOT RESOLVED:** EN Wikipedia says
  **Cannes**, IT Wikipedia **Villefranche-sur-Mer** (43.7038 / 7.3111), 30 km apart. Both agree on
  the gold. Journey pins Cannes and prints the alternative.

## IX. Last years, death, and the posthumous verdicts

- **28 September 2003**: interview with **Concita De Gregorio**, la Repubblica. **[A]**
  - **QUOTE CORRECTED.** The English line on EN Wikipedia ("All is becoming a reality little by
    little... I should have had the copyright... Berlusconi is an extraordinary man, a man of
    action") is a loose translation splicing two passages. The Italian, from the full text at
    disinformazione.it, is used instead. Note **diritti d'autore**, not "copyright"; **fuori dal
    comune**, not "extraordinary man".
  - **SECOND QUOTE CORRECTED.** The pool carried "Ho una vecchiaia serena. **Ogni mattina** parlo
    con le voci della mia coscienza, ed è un dialogo che mi tranquillizza..." tagged [A] and noted
    "Verbatim quote." IT Wikipedia renders it "**Tutte le mattine**", and the continuation is not
    confirmable word for word. Only the confirmed clause is carried.
- **2005-2009, the Calvi file. CORRECTED:** the pool said Gelli was **indicted, one of five
  charged**. EN Wikipedia says he received **notification of formal investigation on 19 July 2005**,
  the other four having been **indicted in April 2005**; he was **never one of the five who stood
  trial**. **June 2007**: all five acquitted for insufficient evidence, the court confirming
  **murder, not suicide**. **May 2009**: charges dropped. **[A]**
- **2006**: archive to the **Archivio di Stato di Pistoia**, the **rubrica dei 500** withheld;
  marries **Gabriela Vasile**. **[A]**
- **Villa Wanda under the state**: failed auctions to recover c. **€1.5 million** in trial costs,
  with Gelli buying it back **[A**, year not specified: **gap]**; **seizure from 10 October 2013**
  over an alleged **€17 million** tax evasion **[A]**.
- **15 December 2015**: **dies at Villa Wanda, aged 96**, brought home from the **San Donato**
  hospital days earlier. **[A]**
- **17 December 2015**: lies in state at the villa; **funeral Mass at the Chiesa di Santa Maria
  delle Grazie, Arezzo**. **[A** IT Wikipedia**] CONTRADICTION INSIDE THE POOL, resolved in favour of
  the Italian source:** two lenses put the Mass at the Chiesa della Misericordia in **Pistoia**; the
  afterlife lens quoted IT Wikipedia for **Arezzo**. Journey follows Arezzo and prints the
  disagreement. Church coordinate approximate (c. 43.449 / 11.882, Viale Mecenate).
- **Burial: family chapel, Cimitero della Misericordia, Pistoia**, beside Wanda Vannacci (d. 1993).
  **[R] SINGLE-SOURCE FLAG:** only the English record names Pistoia; the Italian article's death
  section names **no cemetery at all**. Day-level date defaulted to the funeral date, and said to be.
- A **handwritten will** names **Bartolomeu Constantin Săvoiu**, Grand Master of the National Grand
  Lodge of Romania, sole spiritual heir. **[A]**
- **The Bologna verdicts. LARGEST CORRECTION IN THIS DOSSIER.** The pool said "renewed **2023
  investigative reporting**" reopened Gelli's role, "though earlier proceedings had closed decades
  before **without a definitive terrorism conviction**". Both halves are wrong. **[A** IT Wikipedia,
  "Strage di Bologna"**]** It was not reporting but **verdicts**: the **2020 Cavallini sentence**
  found Gelli a co-organiser and funder with **Umberto Ortolani**; the **assize verdict of 6 April
  2022** convicting **Paolo Bellini** named the two as **mandanti** who **financed** the bombing;
  the **appeal of 8 July 2024** upheld it, citing "ingenti somme" channelled to the executors, on
  the evidence of the **documento Bologna**, a payments note in Gelli's hand. And the 1995 Cassation
  depistaggio conviction already stood. The 2023-reporting claim is **dropped** as unsourced.

## Contradictions printed, not adjudicated

1. **Where the roll was found**: Giole factory, Castiglion Fibocchi (IT Wikipedia, "P2") vs Villa
   Wanda (EN-derived summaries). Two stops, both printed.
2. **Castiglion Fibocchi coordinates**: 43.5083 / 11.7683 (inherited) vs 43.5533 / 11.7694. c. 5 km.
3. **Town of the September 1998 arrest**: Cannes (EN) vs Villefranche-sur-Mer (IT). c. 30 km.
4. **City of the funeral Mass**: Arezzo (IT) vs Pistoia (two pool lenses). Arezzo carried.
5. **Burial**: Pistoia family chapel (EN only) vs no cemetery named (IT). Carried [R].
6. **"Noi con la P2 avevamo l'Italia in mano"**: the pool cited Costanzo, Corriere della Sera, 5
   October 1980; it.wikiquote attributes it to the **Klaus Davi interview of 4 December 2008**. The
   journey re-cites Davi and does not cite the 1980 Corriere.
7. **Date of the declaration dissolving P2**: year only, necessarily after 17 March 1981.
