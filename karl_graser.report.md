# Karl Gräser (1875-1916), research report

*Slug `karl_graser`. Written 2026-08-18 from the verified research pool, each lens already adversarially corrected.*

**Subject.** Carl Samuel Josef Gräser, called Karl. Born Kronstadt (Brașov), 13 August 1875. Oberleutnant, k.u.k. Infanterieregiment Nr. 58, 18 August 1894 to 16 September 1901. Co-founder with Leopold Ferdinand of Austria-Tuscany of the association *Ohne Zwang* in the Przemyśl garrison. One of the buyers of Monte Monescia above Ascona, late autumn 1900, the hill that became Monte Verità. Left the community December 1901 and lived on adjoining ground with Jenny (Eugénie) Hofmann. Died Kassel 28 October 1916, one month after marrying her, aged 41; or 1920, or 1915, depending which page of which source is read (§6).

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction or tradition.

The governing problem is not scarcity but **absorption**: almost everything about Karl reaches us through documents about somebody else. He is the founder nobody wrote a book about.

---

## 1. Kronstadt and the regiment, 1875-1901

- Born 13 August 1875 at Kronstadt, son of **Carl Samuel Gräser** and **Charlotte Caroline née Pelzer** **[A, de.wikipedia "Karl Gräser (Offizier)"]**; its Personendaten give the full name **Gräser, Carl Samuel Josef**. No birth address documented. Brothers **Gustav "Gusto"**, b. 16 February 1879, d. 1958, and **Ernst H. Graeser**, 1884-1944, painter and graphic artist **[A]**.
- Father 13 June 1839 to **16 May 1894**, mother 1853-1920 **[A, same sentence]**; the pool carried the father's year only. **Adjacency, stated not interpreted:** the father dies 16 May 1894, Karl enters regimental service 18 August 1894, aged nineteen, and **no source connects the two.** Flagged in `date_confidence`, not asserted causally.
- **Birth coordinate corrected.** The pool's 45.6579/25.6012 sits about 1.9 km NE of the historic centre. The journey uses **45.6427, 25.5887** (Piața Sfatului), byte-identical from `gusto_graeser.journey.json`.

**The service sentence**, on which everything about seven years rests: *"vom 18. August 1894 bis zum 16. September 1901 Oberleutnant im k.u.k. Infanterieregiment 'Erzherzog Ludwig Salvator' Nr. 58"* **[A]**. Two pool errors follow from misreading it. **He did not enter as Oberleutnant**: the sentence gives the span and the rank recorded for it, and he was nineteen, so the journey states entry into regimental service and the rank held, never an entry rank. And **Przemyśl was not a seven-year posting**: de.wikipedia says he served there *"unter anderem"*, among other places, where the pool read a continuous garrison. No other station is named. **No personal papers** survive from the seven years **[R, gap]**.

**The quotation that is not there.** The phrase widely attached to him, *"die geistlose Zurichtung des Körpers wie des Verstandes zu militärischen Zwecken"*, **does not appear** in de.wikipedia "Karl Gräser (Offizier)" (full wikitext export, last edited 20 May 2026), which says only *"Beide empfanden tiefe Verachtung für den soldatischen Drill."* Not reproduced in the journey as his words.

## 2. Leopold Ferdinand and *Ohne Zwang*

Leopold Ferdinand commanded in the Przemyśl garrison; his article calls Gräser *"einer seiner Offiziere"* there and credits **Gräser** with introducing **him** to Lebensreform ideas **[A, de.wikipedia "Leopold Wölfling"]**. The association had **Gräser as Geschäftsführer, Leopold as Präsident**, its name alluding to **Charles Fourier**, whose philosophy Gräser esteemed **[A, citing Bollmann 2017 p. 25]**.

**Anachronism corrected.** The pool called him "Leopold Wölfling, the former Archduke, who had renounced his titles." At Przemyśl he had not: the renunciation is a letter from Zürich to the Emperor, **14 December 1902**. He was a serving Hauptmann and **Gräser's superior**.

**Misattribution corrected.** The pool carried *"Alles, was sich auf Zwang gründet, ist hinfällig und Mangel an Geist"* as Gräser's founding rationale. The Wikipedia sentence introduces it as *"so hatte Fourier unter anderem formuliert"*: **these are Fourier's words.** The journey's `quote_source` names Fourier and Bollmann, never Gräser.

**Undated, unplaced, single-sourced.** No source dates the founding (the pool's 1899 is invented; the journey uses c. 1898 as an ordering placeholder) or gives its place (Przemyśl is used because that is where the two served) **[R]**. Wölfling's own article does not mention *Ohne Zwang* at all. It also **predates Ascona**: the pool wrongly placed the Geschäftsführer/Präsident structure there in 1900 with a coordinate, and that placement is deleted. Wölfling never took part in the Ascona settlement.

## 3. Veldes (Bled), late summer 1899

Gräser takes a cure at **Arnold Rikli's** naturopathic institute, apparently for serious illness, and there meets **Henri Oedenkoven** and **Ida Hofmann**, who was at Bled visiting her gravely ill father **[A, de.wikipedia "Karl Gräser (Offizier)" and "Ida Hofmann"]**.

- **Duplicate merged.** The pool carried this meeting twice, at 1899 and again at 1900. One event, 1899. The 1900 meeting is Munich-Schwabing (§4).
- **Fabricated quote removed.** The pool had the three discovering a shared wish for "a life where origin was erased," in quotation marks. That phrase is in neither cited article; de.wikipedia says only that a common concern bound them for all their difference. The journey paraphrases that and uses instead Bollmann's real verbatim sentence, marked as **his authorial prose about the three, not testimony by any of them**. The pool's gloss ("the break Gräser sought from his military past") is also wrong and is not used.
- **Institute name unsettled:** *Mallerbrunn* in Karl's article, *Mallnerbrunn* in Ida's. Neither preferred; flagged. Rikli's self-description was **Heliopath**.
- **Corpus coordinate divergence, for the operator.** `ida_hoffmann.journey.json` pins Veldes at **46.3599, 14.1076**, `engelmann.journey.json` at **46.3684, 14.1147**, and the pool's interlock lens reported a third value while citing the Ida file, which does not carry it. The journey inherits ida_hoffmann's byte-identical. Two Veldes pins exist in canon and should be unified.

## 4. The founding journey, 1900-1901

Zürich lectures with Gusto (1900) · Munich-Schwabing reunion constituting the cooperative (autumn 1900) · the brothers' *Erkundungszug* on Lago Maggiore and the Engelmann pension (Nov 1900) · purchase of c. 4 ha on Monte Monescia from Alfredo Pioda (late autumn 1900) · formal discharge (16 Sept 1901). All **[A]**; three carry corrections or contradictions, below.

**Zürich re-cited.** The pool sourced the lectures to de.wikipedia "Gustav Gräser," whose full wikitext contains **no mention of Zurich lectures**; the attestation is on **Karl's own** article, which strengthens rather than weakens it. No hall, month, subject or audience given.

**Munich-Schwabing corrected in six particulars:** autumn 1900, not early 1900; the flat was the Hofmann family's; Lilly's real name was **Julia**, Jenny's **Eugénie**; **Lotte Hattemer was from Berlin**, Ida's article's *"aus Stettin"* being erroneous; **Ferdinand Brune was the son of a Graz landowner**, not a landowner; Gusto turned up unannounced.

**Karl coordinated nothing in the purchase.** The pool had him "coordinating the acquisition"; no source credits him. Money came *"vor allem aus Oedenkovens Besitz"*; the seller was **Alfredo Pioda**; the four hectares came through further purchases, not one. **And Karl was not on the Oberammergau/Cadenabbia walk**: he and Gusto had gone ahead as the *Erkundungszug*. Those two stops stay on `ida_hoffmann.journey.json` and are deliberately absent here.

### The pool's "no surviving words" claim is wrong

Both the chronology lens and the interlock lens state flatly that no first-person text by Karl Gräser survives. **It does.** `engelmann.journey.json` carries, from Ida Hofmann's 1906 memoir p. 15 via Müller's gusto-graeser.de PDF, a letter of Karl's from Ticino to the Munich group: *„Hier findet man Menschen", „auch langhaarige - vegetarische Pensionen u.s.w." schrieb Karl, „kommet zu uns"*. The one located scrap of his own voice, quoted on the scouting-march stop. **The Engelmann contradiction is carried, not resolved:** Müller has the brothers stumbling on the pension during their own search, a rival account has Engelmann writing to Munich to summon them.

**The discharge contradiction.** De.wikipedia gives **16 September 1901** as the end of service, and the **same article** says that at the autumn 1900 Munich meeting Gräser was *"inzwischen aus dem Militärdienst ausgeschieden"*, already out. Both stand, neither is withdrawn. Flagged, not resolved. No place is recorded for the discharge.

## 5. The ground adjoining, 1901-1907

- They **bought** their own plot **[A, de.wikipedia "Ida Hofmann"]**; the pool said they were *granted* land. **The union predates the December split**: the corpus called it a free marriage "since December 1901", but that is when the pair separated **from Oedenkoven and Ida Hofmann**, while Karl's mother's diary records a **letter of 10 July [1901]** announcing he had married Jenny **[A, gusto-graeser.info]**, a source that also warns the diary carries false datings.
- **Jenny's profession is contradictory and the pool picked a side silently.** Karl's article calls her *"einer Pianistin und Erzieherin"* in one sentence and *"die ausgebildete Opernsängerin"* in another; Ida's article independently calls her a trained opera singer, the reading used, with the contradiction stated. Two birth dates also exist, 2 August against **12 August 1863**.
- **Departure December 1901 [A]**, while `gusto_graeser.journey.json` and `ida_hoffmann.journey.json` date **Gusto's** expulsion to **June 1901**. Two brothers, two departures, two dates, two reasons; flagged as possibly conflated, not resolved.
- *"prähistorische Menschen"* in a derelict vineyard hut, rejecting animal and machine labour: **verbatim [A]**, kept as a quotation. The moneyless life is qualified in the source, *"das die beiden jedoch praktisch nie vollständig erreichten"*: the pool dropped that clause, the journey restores it.

**Two contemporary verdicts.** Erich Mühsam, *Ascona* (Locarno, 1905), p. 40, gives the only favourable one, and it is about method, not doctrine **[A, quoted verbatim in the journey]**.

**Ida Hofmann, *Monte Verita: Wahrheit ohne Dichtung*** (Lorch, 1906), p. 96, verified **against the primary source** (archive.org `MonteVeritaWahrheitOD`). Three corrections to the pool: it is about **one woman, singular**, her own sister Jenny, not "women drawn into his household"; the setting is **Gräser's own adjoining ground**, not Monte Verità; and the usual fragment **is not a sentence**, its main verb arriving far later, so the journey marks the ellipsis. **Hofmann is a hostile witness inside a factional split, writing about her sister**, stated in the stop. Two phrasings circulating in her name, "suggestive power" and "helpless instrument of his deranged views", are in neither Wikipedia article and are not used.

**Casa Francesco, and a contradiction nobody reconciles.** Karl's house on the hill is **Casa Francesco**, Heimatschutzstil, by the architect **Paul Evertz**, with two frescoes by **Alexander Wilhelm de Beauclair**, dated 1903-1906 **[A]**. A named architect and a commissioned fresco painter sit badly against the moneyless "prehistoric people" regime on the same ground in the same years. Flagged, not resolved.

The **Locarno exhibition** is winter **1906/07**, not 1906 **[A]**, and the gallery it produced is in **Casa Francesco on the hill**, not Ascona town as the pool had it. It also contradicts the December 1901 departure, unexplained by any source. The **Gräser-Höhle near Losone** is **[R]**: undated, no period of residence, not distinguished from Gusto's Arcegno cave.

**The eight-year hole.** Between the 1906/07 gallery and the 1915 illness, no accessible source places Karl anywhere, records anything he did, or names anyone who saw him. The journey gives this a stop of its own **[R, gap]**.

## 6. Nassau, Kassel, and the death that will not settle

**Sequence corrected.** The pool had "Karl and Jenny move from Nassau to Kassel, likely so Karl can be near medical care." Wrong three ways. He was **nursed at Nassau by Lilly Brepohl née Hofmann** (real name Julia, the youngest sister) *"zunächst bis Anfang August 1916"*; **Jenny was already registered in Kassel** since 14 June; so **Karl moves alone**, away from his carer, toward his partner. The medical-care motive is in no source and is dropped. **Coordinate corrected, the largest error in the pool:** Nassau an der Lahn is at **50.31, 7.8039**, while the pool's 50.1969/7.8163 lies about 13 km SSE in open country.

**The Kassel register**, transcribed by gusto-graeser.info, which is the footnote de.wikipedia actually cites here, **not** Bollmann as the pool stated: Jenny registered **Kohlenstr. 350, Kassel-Wilhelmshöhe, 14 June 1916**; Karl, entered as **"Gutsbesitzer ungarischer Nationalität"**, into the same house **12 August 1916**; married **28 September 1916**; dead **28 October 1916**, aged 41.

**Four death dates are in print:** 28 October 1916 Kassel, from that register, which de.wikipedia follows; **1920, "wahrscheinlich" Kassel**, in the biographical header of the **same** page that transcribes the 1916 register beneath it; **1920** again in the *Wiener Zeitung* of 28 June 2020, flagged in de.wikipedia's Weblinks; and **1915, gestorben in Ascona**, on the archived ticinARTE page. The same site warns that the Hofmann family diary carries false datings, chiefly of his death year. The journey presents 28 October 1916 as **the register's date, the strongest evidence, against a divided literature**, and gives the dispute a stop of its own pinned to 1920.

**No cause of death** anywhere, and **no burial place, grave or marker** described: a gap in the sources consulted, not a finding that no grave exists. gusto-graeser.info reports he became *"gemütskrank"* and ended in an asylum, and *"soll… seine früheren Überzeugungen widerrufen haben"*: marked **"soll"** there, hearsay, carried as such. **Not a war death**: the illness is dated 1915 and began in Switzerland, and the brief's "died in 1916, during World War I" must not be read as implying one.

## 7. Afterlife, and one more corrected citation

- **The chair** survives and is displayed at **Museo Casa Anatta [A]**, with no acquisition date, provenance, catalogue number or photograph in the source. Pin byte-identical from `ida_hoffmann.journey.json` (46.1544, 8.7681).
- **The estate after him.** De.wikipedia "Gustav Gräser" dates Gusto's residence with his family on **Karl's property** to **1916-1918**, a span opening in the year of Karl's October death, unreconciled by either article. Flagged. It does establish that Karl held real property on the hill in his own name.
- **The literature, re-cited.** Bollmann's *Monte Verità 1900* (2017) is heavily cited on de.wikipedia's "Ida Hofmann" but **does not appear in the Literatur of Karl's own article**, which lists Hofmann 1906, Grohmann 1904, and **Schwab, *Monte Verità: Sanatorium der Sehnsucht* (2003)**. The pool's claim that Bollmann "marks the modern rediscovery of his role" is unsupported and dropped.
- **The one-line reputation.** The disambiguation page carries him as *"deutsch-österreichischer Offizier und Aussteiger"*, an encyclopedic descriptor and not period testimony, used with that status stated.
