# Jeffrey Epstein (1953-2019): research report

*Compiled 2026-08-11 for the Atlas of Journeys. Slug: `jeffrey_epstein`. Companion: `jeffrey_epstein.journey.json`, 45 stops in 9 segments.*

**Legend.** **[A]** attested, source named in line. **[R]** reconstruction or contested attribution. Contradictions are flagged where they occur and are **not** adjudicated. Coordinate fixes are gathered in section 10; the journey carries corrected values only.

On register: the canon here is a criminal file, not a legend. The discipline throughout is to state what a police report, a docket, a deed, or a contemporaneous magazine says and let the sequence interpret. The journey asserts no conspiracy and denies none.

## 1. Brooklyn, 1953-1971

Born 20 January 1953 in Brooklyn to Pauline Stolofsky and Seymour George Epstein, a parks department employee; second son **[A: Wikipedia, "Jeffrey Epstein"]**. Raised in **Sea Gate**, the gated working-class enclave at the western tip of Coney Island; PS 188 and Mark Twain Junior High **[A: Wikipedia; Vanity Fair, Vicky Ward, 2003]**; **Interlochen** National Music Camp as a teenager, violin **[A: Miami Herald, "Perversion of Justice," Julie K. Brown, 2018]**. **Lafayette High School**, graduated 1969 at sixteen, two grades skipped **[A]**.

*Gap.* The pool placed the birth at **Coney Island Hospital**. No consulted source confirms a hospital; Wikipedia gives only "Brooklyn." **The maternity-ward detail is dropped as unattested colour.** Mark Twain JHS and Interlochen are dropped from the journey for stop count, not for doubt.

**Cooper Union, 1969-1971:** advanced mathematics without matriculating; the institution's 2019 statements say he was never a fully enrolled degree student **[R]**. Kept [R]: the statement is real, but no registrar record has been produced. **NYU Courant, September 1971 to June 1974:** **mathematical physiology**, no degree **[A: Wikipedia]**. *Corrected:* the pool said "physics." It was not.

## 2. Dalton and Bear Stearns, 1974-1981

**Dalton, September 1974.** Hired at 21 with no bachelor's degree to teach calculus and mathematics **[A: Wikipedia]**. *Corrected:* the hiring headmaster was **Donald Barr**, and the pool's "recommendation of a student's parent" belongs to the 1976 Bear Stearns hire. Dismissed early 1976 **for poor performance**; the pool's "accounts differ" overstates the disagreement in reachable sources.

**Bear Stearns, 1976.** A Dalton father recommended him to **Alan Greenberg**; he started as a low-level junior assistant **[A: Wikipedia]**. *Corrected:* the pool named Greenberg as the Dalton parent. He was the executive who took the call. *Anachronism removed:* the pool placed him at **245 Park Avenue**, which Bear Stearns did not lease until 1987 **[A]**, six years after he resigned.

**1980:** limited partner at 27 on $200,000 a year; *Cosmopolitan* "bachelor of the month," July 1980 **[A]**. **1981:** internal investigation into a **$15,000 personal loan**, then fine, two-month suspension, resignation **[A: Wikipedia]**. *Contradiction, unresolved:* later reporting calls the conduct variously a securities violation or an unauthorized private favour **[R: Vanity Fair 2003; NYT 2019]**. Bear Stearns never put a full account on the record. Both readings sit in the campa; neither is chosen.

## 3. Independence and the Ponzi adjacency, 1981-1993

**Intercontinental Assets Group**, founded August 1981, offices on Central Park South **[A: Wikipedia for the firm; Vanity Fair 2003 for the street]**. The suite is in no reachable record; the street is. **Cayman Islands, 1984.** Recovered mismanaged client funds, returned them, became a millionaire on the recovery **[A: Wikipedia]**. *No stop:* no source supplies a coordinate and inventing one would be worse than omission. A deliberate geographic gap.

**Towers Financial.** Consultant to **Steven Hoffenberg** at $25,000 a month from 1987 **[A: Wikipedia]**, working from the **Villard Houses**, 457 Madison Avenue **[A: New York Times, 2019]**. Left 1989. Towers collapsed in 1993 as the largest Ponzi scheme uncovered to that date, losses above $450 million **[A]**. **Epstein was never charged in it.**

*Contradiction, left open.* Hoffenberg later claimed Epstein helped run Towers; the claim is **uncorroborated** and Epstein's representatives denied it **[R]**. Claim and denial share a campa; neither is adjudicated. **J. Epstein & Company** founded 1988 **[A]**.

## 4. Wexner, 1986-1996: the source of the money

Introduction through mutual acquaintances in Palm Beach, 1986; financial adviser from 1987; **full power of attorney over Wexner's financial and personal affairs, July 1991 [A: Wikipedia]**. This is the load-bearing fact of the life: townhouse, aircraft, working capital, and probably the ranch all run back through one relationship with one client.

*Contradiction, unresolved.* Wexner's 2019 letter to the Epstein Victims' Compensation Program says he was **defrauded of tens of millions** and cut ties around 2007 **[A]**; the estate disputed it **[R]**. His account emerged only after the exposure was public and is partly self-serving. The journey reports both, dated to 2019.

**358 El Brillo Way, Palm Beach**, bought **1990 for $2.5 million [A: Wikipedia]**. **Zorro Ranch**, near Stanley, New Mexico, **7,622 acres**, bought **1993** from former governor Bruce King for about $12 million **[A: Wikipedia, "Zorro Ranch"]**. That he meant to seed the human race with his DNA there is reported through **named associates** in the New York Times (2019), never from his own words: **[A** as reporting, **R** as to intent**]**.

**Financial Trust Company** relocated to **St. Thomas, USVI**, 1996, for the territory's tax treatment **[A: Wikipedia]**. No documented street address exists in any reachable source: a soft pin, **[R]** as to exact site.

## 5. The townhouse and the first reports, 1995-2001

**9 East 71st Street**, the Herbert N. Straus House, seven limestone storeys, among the largest private residences in Manhattan. *Contradiction, left open.* Wikipedia's "Herbert N. Straus House" says **Wexner transferred it in 1995**; "Properties of Jeffrey Epstein" says **1998, $20 million, paid through March 2000**. The pool's "token sum around 1996" fits neither. The stop is dated 1996-01-01 with the dispute in `date_confidence` and both accounts in the campa. Interior detail (massage tables, photographs of women some of whom appear to be children, displayed openly) comes from **later testimony and the July 2019 search return [A]**, not from any 1990s source.

**Maria Farmer, 1996:** reported to the FBI that Epstein took nude photographs of her 12- and 16-year-old sisters **[A: Wikipedia]**. **Little Saint James, 1998.** Bought by L.S.J. LLC for **$7.95 million**, 70-78 acres **[A: Wikipedia]**. Villa, cabanas, helipad, striped temple whose gold dome came off in Hurricane Maria, 2017. Trafficking and abuse there is testified to by multiple accusers including Virginia Giuffre **[A: Giuffre v. Maxwell, SDNY]**.

## 6. The moneyman in print, 2002-2003

**Landon Thomas Jr., "Jeffrey Epstein: International Moneyman of Mystery," New York Magazine, 28 October 2002**, verified against an article reproduction (shakedowntitle.com PDF) and cross-checked at Wikiquote and quotecatalog.com. **Vicky Ward, "The Talented Mr. Epstein," Vanity Fair, March 2003**, verified at vickyward.com.

*Punctuation corrections.* The pool silently replaced em dashes with commas in three quotes: "I invest in people," "No doubt about it" (Trump on a speakerphone, the original split by an attribution clause; the pool's note claiming that dash was "left untouched" is false), and "if my client needs protecting." The journey restores all three, quote fields being verbatim and exempt from the no-em-dash rule. The first was said **to friends**; Epstein granted no interview.

**The Michael Wolff tape, re-tagged R to A.** The pool carried a truncated line attributed to "Vicky Ward/Wolff-linked reporting." Both truncation and attribution are wrong. It is Epstein on audio tape to **Michael Wolff, 2017**, excerpts released November 2024 (HuffPost, Salon, 3 Nov) and lodged in the U.S. House document repository, 27 February 2025. The pool's ellipsis deleted "To some extent it's a typical tragedy where," turning a qualified remark flat; the journey carries the full sentence. Used at the 2018 stop, nearest in time.

**Bill Clinton and the 2002 Africa trip.** *Contradiction, unresolved.* Logs published by Fox News and Gawker (2016) indicate Epstein-owned aircraft carried part of the itinerary; which legs is reported inconsistently, some naming Senegal, others different stops; Clinton's staff say he flew a handful of times with Secret Service throughout **[R: logs versus the Clinton Foundation itinerary, not reconciled]**. Tagged **[R]**; the campa says the sources conflict.

**Institutional money.** **MIT**: *corrected*, the pool's "roughly $800,000 to the Media Lab" conflates two figures. The 2020 outside-counsel review found **about $850,000 to MIT overall, about $525,000 of it to the Media Lab**, the rest largely to Seth Lloyd's group and earlier gifts to Marvin Minsky, logged as anonymous to conceal that the donor was a registered sex offender; Joi Ito resigned September 2019 after Ronan Farrow's New Yorker piece **[A]**. **Harvard**: $6.5 million to the Program for Evolutionary Dynamics under Martin Nowak, campus office used after the 2008 conviction, gift not returned in 2020 **[A]**. **Santa Fe Institute**: **[R]**, dates unconfirmed, dropped rather than asserted.

## 8. The agreement, 2007-2009

**The indictment that was not filed.** A **53-page, 60-count federal indictment** drafted **June 2007** by AUSA **Marie Villafana [A: Wikipedia, "Alexander Acosta"]**. *Corrected:* the pool credited it to "Alexander Acosta's office." Acosta was the U.S. Attorney who negotiated it away.

**Non-prosecution agreement** signed **24 September 2007**, not October **[A: Wikipedia; plea discussions entered August 2007]**: immunity from federal sex-trafficking charges. **Plea, 30 June 2008:** one count solicitation of prostitution, one count solicitation of a minor under 18; 18 months; sex offender registration **[A]**.

**Work release.** *Corrected:* not August 2008. Wikipedia gives the privileges, up to 12 hours a day six days a week, as granted **after approximately 3.5 months**, so roughly **mid-October 2008**. Served at the **Palm Beach County Stockade**, released after about **13 months** of the 18 **[A]**. The Stockade pin is at corridor, not building, resolution, flagged **[R]**. The rented office, for a foundation with a scientific-sounding name, received a stream of visitors during release hours **[A: Miami Herald, 2018]**; what else it was for, the sheriff's logs do not say. A gap.

**D.B. Zwirn**, *corrected and dropped*: not "$80 million, 2002-2009" but **$80 million across 2002-2005**, grown to about $140 million by a **refused November 2006 redemption** **[A: Wikipedia]**.

## 9. 2016-2019, and after

- **Great Saint James**, bought **2016** for **$22.55 million**, **165 acres** *(corrected from the pool's "roughly $18 million")* **[A: Wikipedia]**. USVI civil suit over unpermitted construction damaging shoreline and reef **[A: 2020 complaint]**.
- **Miami Herald, "Perversion of Justice," 2018 [A]**. Credited with reopening scrutiny and producing the 2019 arrest.
- **6 July 2019:** arrested at **Teterboro** as his plane lands from Paris **[A: SDNY complaint]**. Townhouse searched the same night: a safe with cash, loose diamonds, an **expired Austrian passport** bearing his photograph under another name and a Saudi address, and CDs labelled with women's names **[A: warrant return, bail filings]**.
- **8 July 2019:** arraigned before Judge **Richard Berman**, not guilty **[A]**. **Bail denied 18 July** *(corrected: the pool said 18 August, impossible, he died on the 10th)*. Case dismissed 29 August.
- **10 August 2019:** found dead at the **Metropolitan Correctional Center**, 150 Park Row. Cellmate removed days earlier; the two assigned guards later admitted falsifying observation logs **[A: DOJ Inspector General, 2023]**. Chief Medical Examiner **Barbara Sampson** ruled **suicide by hanging** on 16 August **[A]**. *Contradiction, not resolved:* the family and pathologist **Michael Baden** dispute it, **[A** that the dispute exists, **R** as to merits**]**. MCC closed in 2021.
- **5 September 2019:** buried in an **unmarked tomb beside his parents at the IJ Morris Star of David mausoleum**, Palm Beach County **[A]**. *Corrected:* not August, and the grave is about 25 km from El Brillo Way, not "a short drive." Estate about **$600 million**.

**Property afterlives**, corrected, all kept out of the journey for stop count **[A: NYT; Wikipedia]**: 9 East 71st Street sold 2021 to Michael Daffey; 358 El Brillo Way *not* demolished in 2019 but sold to Todd Michael Glaser **January 2021**, demolition **April 2021**; Little and Great St. James sold **together** **May 2023** to Stephen Deckoff for **$60 million** *(the pool gave the whole sum to Little St. James alone)*, and **the demolition claim there is unsupported**, Wikipedia recording none and noting the structures still standing in 2026, so it is downgraded to an announced intention **[R]**; Zorro Ranch sold **2023**, not 2021.

**"He didn't kill himself"** became a durable meme within days **[A: Washington Post, 2019]**, legend-formation distinct from the forensic finding. *Corrected:* **Surviving Jeffrey Epstein** is a **Lifetime** documentary premiered **9 August 2020**, not Peacock and not 2023. On the **2025-2026 DOJ file releases [A: AP, Reuters]**, the scope of what remains unreleased is contested, and **no claim about the content of unreleased files is made anywhere in the journey**. One further overreach: "no monument, plaque, or memorial marker exists anywhere" is too strong, because he has a grave. The journey says instead that the grave is **deliberately unmarked** and no commemorative site exists.
