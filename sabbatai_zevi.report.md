# Sabbatai Zevi (1626-1676): research report
*2026-08-30, for `sabbatai_zevi.journey.json`. Smyrna to Ulcinj; 44 stops in 8 segments; gregorian.*

**[A]** = attested, source named · **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition, or single partisan witness. Contradictions are flagged and left standing; unreachable sources are named with the reason. EJ = Encyclopaedia Judaica, "Shabbetai Zevi," via encyclopedia.com. JE = Kohler & Malter, "Shabbethai Zebi b. Mordecai," *Jewish Encyclopedia* (1901-1906).

**Warning first.** The pool arrived carrying corrections that reverse several of its own headline claims. The worst: the "Ottoman provincial order of 27 October 1676" stopping the convert "Mehmet Aziz"'s stipend, called in the pool its **strongest external anchor**, is **not in either source cited for it**. EJ has no such document, date, or name; Wikipedia says only that "the Turks grew tired of Sabbatai's antics and ended his doorkeeper's salary." **It is kept out of the file.**

---

## 1. Smyrna: birth, ordination, first banishment (1626-c. 1651)

**Birth** in Smyrna to Mordecai Zevi, conventionally 9 Av **[A: EJ; Wikipedia, "allegedly on Tisha B'Av"]**. 9 Av AM 5386 = **1 August 1626** Gregorian, 21 July Julian. EJ itself flags that the date may have been fitted to the tradition retrospectively; no birth register survives.

**Contradiction, unresolved.** JE gives **23 July 1626**, matching neither the Gregorian nor the Julian equivalent of 9 Av (two days off Julian). The pool called this a calendar-conversion dispute; **that does not survive arithmetic** and has been struck. Either a JE error or an independent tradition.

**Family** **[A: JE verbatim]**: his father "had been a poor poultry-dealer in the Morea. Later... Mordecai became the agent in that town of an English house." **Correction:** the pool's "English *and Dutch* merchants" is unsupported and "Dutch" is dropped; the Romaniote/Patras origin, missing from the pool's claim text, is restored.

**Ordination** under **Joseph Escapa**; *hakham* by about eighteen **[A: JE; EJ gives 1644]**. No building is identified in any source. **Seclusion**: asceticism, Zohar and Lurianic study, alternating depressive and exalted states **[A: EJ, 1642/1648]**, the manic-depressive reading being **modern and retrospective [R]**.

**The Name, and the banishment.** Revealed himself as redeemer at about twenty-two **[A: JE, 1648]**; pronounced the Tetragrammaton **[A: EJ]**; banished with his followers **[A]**. **Year contested: c. 1651 (JE) against 1651/1654 (EJ)**, no document, and no source names the rabbis who signed. **Gap:** the two reported early marriages dissolved unconsummated are not in this pool.

## 2. The wandering years (c. 1654-1665)

**Salonika, the Torah as bride** **[R: Baruch of Arezzo and derivative accounts; no contemporary document]**. EJ places it 1654/1658. **Name deliberately dropped:** one summary names the expelling rabbi **Abraham de Boton**, but JE and encyclopedia.com name nobody, and the surname collides with Abraham di Boton of the *Lehem Mishneh*, dead c. 1605.

**Constantinople and Abraham ha-Yakini**, and the forged *Hokhmat Shelomo ha-Gedolah* **[A: JE prints the text and lays the forgery at ha-Yakini's own door]**. JE's exact wording is restored in the quote field: the pool had inserted "Hebrew" before "year" and dropped the "[God's]" gloss. **Contradiction, unresolved:** JE gives **1653-1658**, EJ **about nine months in 1658**; the file prefers neither.

**Rhodes** **[A: upgraded from the pool's [R]]**: EJ states explicitly, "Late 1662: traveled to Jerusalem via Rhodes and Cairo." The pool tagged it [R] because Wikipedia and JE omit Rhodes, but EJ carries it.

**Cairo and Raphael Joseph Halabi**, mint master and tax farmer **[A: Wikipedia]**. **Contradiction, unresolved:** Wikipedia and JE give **c. 1660-1662**, EJ an **emissary return in 1663 running to spring 1665**; the pool's geography entry carried a date field of "1662-1665" attached to a quote reading "approximately 1660-1662."

**Jerusalem.** Night psalm-singing and asceticism **[A: JE, c. 1663]**; then chosen **emissary to Egypt** against the community's tax burden **[A: Wikipedia; EJ, 1663]**. **The Western Wall is not named in any biographical source**; the site is inferred. **Marriage to Sarah** in Cairo **[A: EJ, 31 March 1664]**: Polish-born, orphaned in the 1648 Cossack violence, self-declared as destined to marry the Messiah. **Contradiction:** JE puts it inside its 1660-1662 window.

## 3. Gaza and the proclamation (1665)

**Nathan of Gaza** receives him as a physician receives a patient and declares him Messiah instead **[A: EJ, April 1665; Wikipedia]**. Nathan's claimed **February throne-vision [R]**: the earliest surviving written account is from **1667**. **The proclamation: 31 May 1665, 17 Sivan AM 5425 [A: EJ verbatim, "May 31, 1665 (17th of Sivan): proclaimed himself Messiah in Gaza"]**.

**Major correction, applied twice.** Both the chronology and geography lenses framed this as a **Shavuot** proclamation. Shavuot 5425 (6 Sivan) fell on **20 May 1665**, eleven days earlier; what happened on Shavuot night was **Nathan's maggid-possession vision** (Wikipedia, "Nathan of Gaza": "on the evening of Shavuot in the spring of 1665"). The file separates the two, and the campa says most accounts tell them as one.

**Aleppo**, enthusiastic reception en route north **[A: JE]**; the dates 20 July-12 August come from the pool's chronology, unconfirmed.

## 4. Smyrna, autumn and winter 1665

**Return and public declaration [A: EJ, September 1665; JE, autumn 1665]**; fasts abolished and kept as feasts, prophetic outbreak, commerce halted **[A: EJ]**.

**The Portuguese synagogue** **[A: upgraded from the pool's [R], and the day corrected]**. The geography lens tagged it [R] saying neither Wikipedia nor JE mentions the episode; encyclopedia.com carries it verbatim: "On Friday, December 11" a crowd stormed an opponent's house, and "on the following day" he "proceeded to the locked doors of the Portuguese congregation." **12 December 1665 was a Saturday** (computed), which is the date the file uses for the doors, keeping the 11 December riot as the previous day's separate event.

**Aaron Lapapa deposed [A: EJ, 23 December 1665; JE, autumn, no day]**; **sailing for Constantinople, 30 December 1665 [A: EJ]**.

**Deliberately absent**, standard in the literature but not in this pool: the Meliselda ballad, the distribution of the kingdoms of the earth, Hayyim Benveniste as Lapapa's replacement.

## 5. Arrest and the fortress (February-September 1666)

Intercepted at sea in the Marmara, **6 February 1666 [A: EJ]** (the 40.75, 28.5 pin is approximate; no source gives the position); ashore in chains **8 February [A: EJ]**, imprisoned by order of grand vizier Köprülüzade Fazıl Ahmed Pasha **[A: Wikipedia]**; transferred **19 April 1666**, whereupon the believers rename the fortress **Migdal Oz** and turn it into a paying pilgrimage court **[A: EJ; Wikipedia]**.

**Site unresolved; the pool's pin rejected.** EJ says **Gallipoli**; JE calls the prison **Abydos** and does not locate it. The pool pinned **Kilitbahir Castle (40.1478, 26.3794)**, wrong for both readings: Kilitbahir is on the European bank opposite Çanakkale, ~35 km south of Gelibolu, while Abydos was **Asian**-side near Nara Burnu, and Wikipedia's Kilitbahir article mentions neither man nor place. **The file pins Gelibolu (40.41, 26.67) per EJ and calls the site unsettled.**

**The paschal lamb with its forbidden fat, and the blessing "who permits the forbidden"** **[A as act: Wikipedia]**; the blessing's wording is in the pool's claim text, untraced to a manuscript. Dated 1666-04-20 from the 19 April transfer. **Nehemiah ha-Kohen** disputes with him 3-4 September **[A: EJ, "September 3 or 4"; Wikipedia confirms the Polish communities sent him and that he "declared Sabbatai an impostor"]**. **Tag split:** the pool claimed "neither source names Nehemiah ha-Kohen," and **both do**; the dispute is [A], while the denunciation to Ottoman authorities and the causal chain to the summons stay **[R]**.

## 6. The turban, and Adrianople (1666-1672)

**Conversion: 16 September 1666.** EJ alone gives the 15th; JE ("converted to Islam on the following day, Sept. 16, 1666") and Wikipedia give the 16th, and JE's phrasing reconciles them: before the sultan on the 15th, converted on the 16th. Independent check: **16 September 1666 = 16 Elul AM 5426**, Scholem's date. The file uses two stops.

**The archery ordeal [R: Paul Rycaut, *The History of the Turkish Empire* (1680)]**: a European diplomatic account, no Ottoman transcript, and the campa says Rycaut published fourteen years later and was not present. The physician Hayatizade, standard in the literature, is not in this pool and not named.

**Titles and stipend.** *Effendi*, and the palace rank **Kapiçi Bashi** **[A: encyclopedia.com]**; Wikipedia gives only "the title effendi... on a generous salary." **Currency corrected, unit still disputed:** the pool said "about 150 aspers/akçe per day," where encyclopedia.com reads **"a royal pension of 150 piasters per day"**, a gap of roughly two orders of magnitude. The file reports **"150 piasters" and attributes it to EJ by name** rather than converting silently. "Aziz Mehmed Effendi" appears only in encyclopedia.com; the campas use "Mehmed Effendi." **Letter to his brother Elijah** ("God has made me an Ishmaelite...") **[A as text, one remove]**, read in Wikipedia, sourced onward to JE, whose page was not fetched for this line.

**Sarah's conversion. [R: downgraded from the pool's [A]; timing dropped.]** Neither source gives timing; Wikipedia says only "Sarah and approximately 300 families among his followers also converted to Islam." **"About a week after" is unsupported and removed**, and the name Fatima is in the pool alone.

**The double life, 1667-1672 [A: EJ]**, textured by **Baruch of Arezzo**, a believer writing after the death, whose translated text was read directly and supplies three of the file's nine quotations. **Circumcision of Ishmael Mordecai [R]**, Baruch and the Najara Chronicle, both believer sources. **Divorce and remarriage of Sarah, April 1671 [A: EJ, corroborated by Nathan]**.

**Petition to the sultan for power to punish the Jews of Constantinople, December 1671 [R: single partisan witness]**: Nathan of Gaza's own disapproving report and nothing else, kept because a witness hostile to the episode is strong evidence it happened. **Rearrest, August 1672, at Constantinople and not Adrianople:** the geography lens placed it at Adrianople while quoting a JE passage headed **Constantinople**, and encyclopedia.com says "August 1672: arrested in Constantinople."

## 7. Alkum, and the death (1673-1676)

**Exile to Dulcigno, January 1673 [A: EJ; Wikipedia]**; the followers call it **Alkum**. **The 1673 concealment letter** is **[A as text seen, [R] as to transmission]**: the article reproducing it was fetched, the manuscript was not traced.

**Sarah dies, 1674, at Ulcinj.** The pool's "probably in Adrianople" is an invented inference, contradicted by Wikipedia ("his wife died here in 1674") and by the pool's own geography lens. **Second marriage to Esther / Jochebed / Ayşe**, daughter of **Joseph Filosof** **[A: EJ, 1674/1675; [R] as to the Jewish rite, in the pool alone]**.

**The last autograph letter, August 1676**, to the Jews of **Berat**, asking urgently for a *mahzor*, signed at Alkum **[A in substance: Wikipedia; [R] as to the shelfmark]**; **Ben-Zvi MS 2262, fol. 79** comes from catalogue description. **Cardozo and Carrillo visit, 5 September 1676 [R]**, resting on Cardozo's own later writings.

**Death at Ulcinj on Yom Kippur, 17 September 1676, aged fifty [A: Wikipedia]**. 10 Tishri AM 5437 = 17 September Gregorian, 6 September Julian. **Contradiction, unresolved, pool's explanation struck:** JE says he "died, according to some, on the Day of Atonement (Sept. 30), 1676, at Duleigno," and **30 September matches neither calendar's rendering of Yom Kippur 5437**, a plain source disagreement JE itself hedges, not a conversion artifact. **Nathan's doctrine of the occultation [A: encyclopedia.com]** is the movement's own theology and presented as such.

## 8. The grave, and the believers

**Two burial traditions, contradicting each other, both kept as stops.** (1) **Ulcinj**, local tradition identifying the **Murat Dadaj türbe** **[R: the Wikipedia articles on Ulcinj and Ulcinj Old Town do not mention his burial at all; no inscription, no excavation]**; research published **1985** argued for Dulcigno over Berat **[R]**. (2) **Berat**, a Sufi lodge in the yard of the **King's Mosque**, standing until **1967** **[A: Wikipedia]**; that 1967 is the year of Albania's demolition of religious buildings is **inference, not a statement of the source**. The file keeps both and lets the Berat mahzor letter argue against the Berat tomb.

**Dönme pilgrimage** to a tomb of his into the early twentieth century **[A: Wikipedia verbatim]**. **Attribution stripped:** the pool credited it to **Gershom Scholem**, and the sentence carries no such attribution. **A statue in Ulcinj today [R: a photo caption, uncorroborated]**, kept only in a `suggested_ref`.

**The Dönme, 1683**: about three thousand Sabbateans converted collectively in Salonika, shortly after Nathan's death **[A: Wikipedia, "Dönmeh"]**. **Leadership corrected:** the pool credited **Jacob Querido**; Wikipedia credits **Joseph Filosof**, Esther's father, with "a key role in founding the new religious sect," and describes Querido as "the brother of Tzevi's last wife" who "claimed to be Tzevi's reincarnation," i.e. founder of the later Jacobite sub-sect.

**Not staged as stops** (§9): the Yeni Mosque of Thessaloniki (c. 1902), the Bülbülderesi gravestones in Üsküdar (unconfirmed even in the pool), the restored house near the Izmir Agora, the 2026 Jerusalem exhibition. Two appear as `suggested_refs` on stops properly his.

## 9. Apparatus relations for the operator

Sabbatai Zevi is **not** listed in `EXCEPTIONS.md`. The relations below sit in the verified pool and are **not rooted in his own life**. None has been silently deleted. **All are FORWARD**: his file is a block stamped in 1676, every item below an artifact that did not exist then, so none is licensable as it stands.

- **Gershom Scholem**, *Sabbatai Sevi: The Mystical Messiah* (1957; trans. 1973), and the Scholem Collection of Sabbatean manuscripts. Not a stop; Scholem is cited **only** as the source of one quotation (Nathan's 1673 letter, p. 204), on a stop that is Nathan's and Sabbatai's own.
- **Walter Benjamin** and Scholem, Berlin 1915; the *Angelus Novus* in Scholem's Jerusalem flat 1940s-1987. Not in the file: Benjamin has no relation of any kind to Sabbatai Zevi, the connection running wholly through a twentieth-century scholar. **Martin Buber** and Scholem's case about Lurianic Kabbalah: same, and likewise absent.
- **Theodor Herzl**, *Altneuland* (1902), asserted to contain a fictional opera titled "Sabbatai Zevi", **unverified**; the pool says its search budget ran out before the text could be checked. Not in the file: even verified it is Herzl's stop on Herzl's file. Live lead.
- **Museum for Islamic Art, Jerusalem**, "Sabbatai Zevi: Truth or Falsehood," to 7 November 2026. Not a stop: a museum's act in 2026. ynetnews also corrects the pool, the lion image having been **added by a believer** to the 1665 Venice *Mishneh Torah*, not printed on it.

**Relations that ARE in the file, because they are his own**, each a documented one-directional relation of Sabbatai's:

- **Maimonides** (`maimonides`), ordination campa: the law he was trained and ordained in is Maimonides' codification. Study, not co-location. *An earlier draft named him again at Cairo via the shared Fustat institutional world; struck as pure co-location.*
- **Moses de Leon** (`moses_de_leon`) and **Rabbi Shimon bar Yochai** (`rabbi_shimon_bar_yochai_rashbi`), seclusion campa: the Zohar he read nightly is the book de Leon put into circulation under Rashbi's name, and his own late *Raza di-Meheimanuta* works the same Zoharic Aramaic vocabulary.
- **Abraham Abulafia** (`abraham_abulafia`), **deliberately not named**, recorded rather than deleted: proposed as the medieval precedent for the 1648 Tetragrammaton, tagged [R] and stated in neither file. A historian's lineage, not his relation.
- **Evliya Çelebi** (`evliya_celebi`), **deliberately not named**: Jerusalem, Aleppo and Edirne are co-location under one empire, which the rule excludes. His pins are inherited, his name is not used.

## 10. Honest gaps

**No Ottoman archival document is in this pool.** Everything on the Ottoman side reaches us through European or Jewish sources, and the one Ottoman document the pool claimed is not in its cited sources: the largest hole in the file. **Sarah has almost no record**: no birth year, death month, burial place, reported words, or account of what she made of the conversion or the divorce, and two campas say so rather than filling the silence. **The Gallipoli/Abydos fortress is not identified.** **No building is located anywhere**, not Escapa's yeshiva, the Portuguese synagogue, Raphael Joseph's house, Nathan's house in Gaza, the Ulcinj residence, or the grave; every urban pin is a quarter or a city, and each stop says so. **The February 1665 throne-vision** is known only from a document of 1667, and **several of the file's quotations were not read at their primary source** (§11).
