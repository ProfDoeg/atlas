# John Uri Lloyd (1849-1936): research report

*Compiled 2026-09-01. Pharmacist, chemist, professor without a degree, founder of the Lloyd
Library, author of Etidorhpa. Queued under the hollow-earth cluster (QUEUE.md row 96).*

**Legend.** **[A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or dossier-only
report that no reachable source confirms. Contradictions are printed, not adjudicated.

**Method.** The spine is the **John Uri Lloyd Papers finding aid** (PDF, lloydlibrary.org),
which lists manuscript titles folder by folder, so most of Lloyd's travel is attested not by a
diary entry but by an essay about a place. That proves he wrote about Aden, not the day he
stood there; every stop built that way is dated as an ordering device and says so in
`date_confidence`. The second spine is the long biography at **nkyviews.com**, the only
reachable connected life narrative, and where it contradicts Wikipedia it has proven right.

---

## 1. Kentucky boyhood, 1849-1863

- Born **19 April 1849, West Bloomfield, Ontario County, New York**, to Nelson Marvin and
  Sophia Webster Lloyd. **[A** Wikipedia; nkyviews**]** Coordinate corrected to 42.9059,
  -77.5392 (OSM); dossier value ~4 km too far east.
- **c. 1853** to **Burlington, Boone County, Kentucky**, the father taking work surveying a
  proposed Covington-to-Louisville railroad **[A** finding aid; nkyviews**]**, then on to
  **Petersburg** for teaching work **[A** nkyviews**]**. Coordinates corrected to 39.0218,
  -84.7203 and 39.0692, -84.8683 (the second was ~10 km south). Lloyd later wrote up **John
  Norris**, "The Last Survivor of Perry's Victory," and his War of 1812 recollections (Box 40,
  Folders 578-579), plus **Norman's Mill in Normansville, renamed Petersburg** (Box 45, Folder
  700). **[A]**
- **Florence** is the settled home c. 1854-1863, the ground fictionalized as **Stringtown**;
  he saw wartime troop movement here. **[A** boonecountykyhistory.org**]**
- **[A** as writings, undated, and so **[R]** as to any dated boyhood visit**]**: **Big Bone
  Lick**, two essays (Box 43, Folder 760; Box 39, Folder 551), coordinate corrected to 38.8823,
  -84.7476; and **Fort Mitchell**, "Old Fort Mitchell On The Height" (Box 40, Folder 587),
  39.0595, -84.5474, folded into the Florence campa rather than given a stop.

## 2. Apprenticeship, 1863/64-1871

- At about fourteen he crosses to Cincinnati, apprenticed to **William J. M. Gordon** **[A**
  finding aid; Wikipedia**]**. **Sources give 1863 or 1864; unresolved**, the dossier grounding
  1864 in Flannery's chronology and Lloyd's first diary, neither reachable. The **East Third
  Street** boarding-house attic is **[R]**, nkyviews alone.
- The **George Eger** apprenticeship is **[A]**, not tradition: Box 14, Folder 295, "John Uri
  Lloyd's apprenticeship notebook with George Eger, **1866**," in a notebook series running
  1866-1917; nkyviews makes Eger a second two-year term after Gordon, both done by about 1867.
  *Upgrade on the dossier, which had Eger as [R].* Eger's address is in no reachable source
  and the coordinate is a declared approximation.
- **25 March 1870**: **Article of Agreement** buying Gordon's chemical apparatus. **[A**
  Series XI, Box 18, Folder 351**]**
- **1871**: chemist at **H. M. Merrell & Company**. **[A** nkyviews; finding aid**]**
  **Downgraded:** that **John King and John Milton Scudder** brokered the move, and that Lloyd
  was "laboratory manager," is attested nowhere reachable; King appears only as a
  correspondent (Box 9, Folder 168). **[R]**, dropped from the campa.

## 3. The firm and the two marriages, 1876-1886

- **December 1876**: marries **Adaline "Addie" Meader** **[A** year and month, nkyviews**]**;
  the dossier's **27 December** is unsupported, and nkyviews's "ten days after marriage"
  before her 7 January death implies the **28th**, so the day is dropped.
- **7 January 1877**: Addie dies of **acute peritonitis** **[A** nkyviews; archive Box 2,
  Folders 33-34: obituary, funeral bulletin, portraits, lock of hair**]**. The **New York
  honeymoon** is **[R]**, attested nowhere; the stop keeps the NYC coordinate but states that
  the record does not name the city.
- **1877** buys **T. C. Thorp's** interest (Merrell, Thorp & Lloyd) and **1881** Nelson Ashley
  replaces H. M. Merrell (Thorp & Lloyd Brothers): both **[R]**, company tradition.
- **c. 1880**: marries **Emma V. Rouse** of Boone County, daughter of Thomas Rouse, called a
  childhood friend **[A** year, nkyviews**]**; **10 June 1880** is in no reachable source. The
  finding aid's "Emma Rouse Lloyd, 1868-1900" correspondence (Box 6, Folder 58) is consistent
  with long acquaintance, not proof of it.
- **c. 1882**: **2604 Harris Avenue, Norwood**, the Norwood advocacy, and the cross street
  renamed Lloyd Avenue **[A** Norwood Ohio Historical Society**]**, corroborated by four essay
  titles in the finding aid. Coordinate corrected to 39.1631, -84.4437.
- **1886**: **Lloyd Brothers, Pharmacists, Inc.** **[A** year and name: Wikipedia; finding
  aid**]**, but the mechanism differs by source: Wikipedia has the brothers purchasing Merrell
  and Thorpe, the finding aid credits John and Nelson Ashley, and the dossier's version
  (Curtis replacing Abner Thorp) is attested nowhere. **[R]** on the mechanism.
- **Missouri**: "Travel expenses to Missouri, addresses, misc., n.d." (Box 1, Folder 7) is
  **[A]** as an expense record, with no itinerary. The paired dossier claim that **Nelson
  Lloyd died in 1882 after falling ill surveying in the Ozarks** is supported by nothing
  reachable, nkyviews giving no death year for the father: **[R]**, and dropped.

## 4. Teaching and the profession, 1878-1900

- **Eclectic Medical Institute**. **The dossier's 1878 start is attested nowhere reachable and
  the tag is downgraded [A] to [R].** nkyviews says only "many years as professor of chemistry"
  and **president 1896-1904**; the finding aid says only that he taught there. The "1895 vs.
  1907 end-year dispute" is unsupported too. The stop uses 1878 to order the segment and
  prints the contradiction.
- **Cincinnati College of Pharmacy**, **1882-1887** **[A** nkyviews**]**; the dossier's 1883
  start has no reachable support and is named, not followed. The **1882** Ebert Prize and
  **1887** APhA presidency are **[R]**: no award record or minute reached.
- **1888-1889**: the **Famous Hydrastis Controversy**, **[A]** and upgraded from the dossier's
  [R] (Box 2, Folder 15, an autobiographical composition by Lloyd himself; two years, not one).
  **1898-1900**: Treasury circulars against the war revenue tax on proprietary medicines (Box
  18) **[A]**, folded into the Hydrastis campa.

## 5. Etidorhpa, 1892-1896

- **1892**: *Elixirs and Flavoring Extracts*. **[A** Wikipedia**]**
- **1895**: *Etidorhpa; or, The End of Earth*, illustrated by **J. Augustus Knapp**, signed
  **Author's Edition**. **[A]** *Two corrections:* the publisher is **J. U. Lloyd himself**
  (Internet Archive, `etidorhpaorend00lloy`, binding by **Cranston & Curts**), **not** the
  Robert Clarke Company; and **"more than 1,200 subscribers"** appears in no reachable source.
  The archive holds the prospectus and order forms (Box 28, Folder 401), evidence of a
  subscription scheme, not of its size. **[R]** on the number.
- **1896**: **sixth edition**, Cincinnati, **The Robert Clarke Company**. **[A** Gutenberg
  #37775**]**
- **The cave.** Public Domain Review says only that the entrance "is to be found in Kentucky,"
  but the novel's own front matter names **"a cavern discovered, Biswell's Hill"** and "The
  Punch Bowls and Caverns of Kentucky" **[A** Gutenberg #37775**]**. The dossier's coordinate
  (37.187, -86.100) is **Mammoth Cave**, a substitution no source makes; kept only as a
  declared stand-in.
- **Quotations** were checked verbatim against Gutenberg #37775. "Each volume adds to the
  oppression..." is **not** Chapter I: it stands in the **Preface**, in Lloyd's own voice, not
  the narrator Drury's. Re-attributed. The **Inter Ocean review**'s dossier wording ("this
  desk," no comma) follows the Public Domain Review and is inexact; the book's own **"Some
  Press Opinions"** appendix (pp. 385-386) reads **"his desk, and all kinds of books."** The
  1895 newspaper page was **not** located: Chronicling America returns no match. **Gap.**

## 6. The Stringtown novels, 1897-1930

- **1897** *The Right Side of the Car: A Story of the Northern Pacific Railway* **[A**
  Wikipedia**]**. The archive keeps **Mount Rainier** as its own subject file (Box 45, Folder
  695) but does not date the trip; pinning the 1897 story to the mountain is an inference,
  flagged in the stop.
- **1900** *Stringtown on the Pike*, **[A]** and **corrected against Wikipedia's 1901, which
  is wrong**: Open Library; Internet Archive (Dodd, Mead); marker #2540; Boone County bio.
  The finding aid also holds Scrapbook Volume 10 of reviews, 1900-1901.
- **1901** *Warwick of the Knobs* **[A** Wikipedia**]**. That **Grover Cleveland** read it,
  corresponded 1901-1907 and fished with Lloyd is **dossier-only [R]**, unconfirmed anywhere
  reachable; the campa names it as family tradition.
- **1903** *Red Head* (Reginald B. Birch); **1904** *Scroggins*, Dodd, Mead, **corrected
  against Wikipedia's 1900** (Open Library first_publish_year 1904; Internet Archive, 1904);
  **1909** *Life and Medical Discoveries of Samuel Thomson*; **1910** *The Eclectic
  Alkaloids*; **1911** *History of the Vegetable Drugs of the Pharmacopoeia of the United
  States*; **1930** *Felix Moses, the Beloved Jew of Stringtown on the Pike*, 500 signed
  copies, Knapp illustrating, from a real Boone County peddler. **[A** Wikipedia for all**]**

## 7. Travel: the 1906 Ottoman voyage and the American journeys

Every folder citation below was verified against the finding aid PDF.
- **Constantinople, 1906** [A, Box 19, Folder 375]: American College for Girls material,
  consular introductions · **Smyrna** [A, Box 19, Folders 362, 364]: Ahmed Ramsay
  correspondence and addresses, 1906-1911 · **Ephesus** [A, Box 39, Folder 561 and Box 40,
  Folder 592] · **Aden** [A, Box 41, Folder 609], "The Water Tanks of Aden Were A Surprise."
- **The whirling dervishes**: the essay is attested verbatim (Box 41, Folder 610); **the
  venue is not.** Nothing names the Galata Mevlevihanesi or any tekke, so that coordinate is
  inference: **[A]** essay, **[R]** place.
- **Rhodes, not Chios.** The dossier put the mastic stop on **Chios** because mastic is a
  Chios crop. The finding aid folder reads **"The Island of Rhodes / Home of Mastic"** (Box
  40, Folder 573), with "Mastic and Its Oriental Uses" in Boxes 53-54 and "Mastiche notes and
  recipe" at Box 42, Folder 643, and nothing attests Lloyd went to Chios at all. The stop
  moves to **Rhodes (36.4349, 28.2176)**, discrepancy stated in the campa, not resolved.
- Two stops dropped: **"Turkish Drugs"** (Box 43, Folder 668) is **[A]** but has no place more
  precise than the empire, and the dossier pinned it to a country centroid, so it is folded
  into the Constantinople campa; and **Washington, D.C.**, c. 1912-1929 (Box 44, Folder 672),
  is **[R]**, the archive not separating visits from correspondence.
- **Loreto, Baja California Sur**: "Journey to the Sea of Cortez" (Box 2, Folder 18) and "My
  Visit To Loreto," **[A]**, undated. *Citation corrected:* the latter is Box 41, Folder 605,
  not Box 54, Folder 750, which is the alphabetical index entry.
- **Canyon de Chelly** [A as a writing, undated, Box 39, Folder 563]: coordinate corrected to
  36.1434, -109.4416, the dossier value falling ~9 km outside the canyon. The **Arizona cliff
  dwellings** stop is folded in here: Box 39, Folder 555 and Box 40, Folder 574 are verified
  and **Judge Albert C. Thompson** is confirmed as a correspondent (Box 13, Folder 265), but
  **the finding aid names no cliff-dwelling site** and the dossier's Montezuma Castle stand-in
  was itself mis-coordinated by ~8 km.

## 8. The library, the reagent, the trusts, 1890-1919

- **3901 Clifton Avenue**: Romanesque, architect **James W. McLaughlin**, NRHP **7 March
  1973**, reference **73001461** **[A** NPS**]**, which gives the structure **completed 1890**,
  not the dossier's "built 1888-1890"; coordinate corrected to **39.1573, -84.5176**.
- **1902** acquisition of **224 West Court Street**; **1907** a separate library building at
  **309 West Court Street**; **1912** expanded fifty feet. **[A** Lloyd Library history**]**
  *Contradiction kept open:* the dossier has 309 arising earlier and merely expanded in 1912,
  and dates the firm there to "the 1890s onward," impossible if the building went up in 1907.
  Coordinate corrected to 39.1054, -84.5192.
- **Patent US 1,048,712**, aluminium silicate for alkaloid extraction ("Lloyd's reagent"),
  filed 23 February 1912, granted 31 December 1912. **[R]:** USPTO record not pulled; number
  and dates from the dossier's patent table.
- **1917** Curtis's trust **[A** NLM**]**; **1919** the brothers' broader trust for public
  access **[A** Wikipedia**]**; **c. 1925** roughly **50,000 volumes** **[A** Lloyd Library,
  "mid-1920s"; the single year is an approximation**]**.

## 9. Last years and death

- **1925** Nelson Ashley dies **[R]**. **1926** **Curtis Gates Lloyd** dies **[A** twice: the
  Lloyd Library history and the accession "Curtis Gates Lloyd Papers 1859-1926"**]**, though
  the dossier's **11 November** is in neither. **1932** **Emma Rouse Lloyd** dies **[A** the
  accession "Emma Rouse Lloyd Papers 1858-1932"**]**, the day **27 November** unattested.
- **15 March 1933**: **a dinner in honor of Albert Einstein**, its own dated file (Box 45,
  Folder 686). **[A]** as an event with that date; **the venue is not named**, and no reachable
  source says whether Einstein was present or whether the two men met. The stop says so.
- **9 April 1936**: dies of **pneumonia** at the Van Nuys home of his daughter **Anna Lloyd
  Welbourn**, ten days short of eighty-seven **[A** Wikipedia; ExploreKYHistory**]**. Cremated
  remains interred at **Hopeful Lutheran Church Cemetery, Florence**, near both wives **[A**
  Boone County history; ExploreKYHistory; BCPL item 13254**]**. *Coordinate corrected* to
  **38.9997, -84.6593**; the dossier gave 38.999, -84.6266, central Florence, not the church.
  **The interment date is recorded nowhere reachable**; the file uses a declared placeholder.

## 11. Apparatus relations for the operator

Lloyd is **not** listed in `EXCEPTIONS.md`. These relations are not rooted in his own
documented life, and per the standing rule are recorded rather than deleted, direction named.

1. **John Cleves Symmes Jr. (d. 1829), backward.** Symmes lectured the hollow earth from
   Cincinnati platforms from January 1820, turning a wooden globe with cut-away poles; Lloyd
   wrote the most famous American hollow-earth novel from the same city seventy-five years
   later. **No reachable source shows Lloyd citing, reading or naming Symmes.** This is the one
   such relation kept inside a campa (the Biswell's Hill stop), on the ground that what
   Etidorhpa inherits is the doctrine and not the city, and it is hedged in `date_confidence`.
   If that is judged too thin, delete the clause; nothing else depends on it.
2. **Kircher** and **Jules Verne** (backward), genre ancestors of Etidorhpa's hollow earth,
   and **Bradshaw** (lateral), whose *Goddess of Atvatabar* (1892) is Symmesian throughout and
   three years earlier: none carried into a campa, nothing attesting that Lloyd read any of
   them. **Lovecraft** (forward) is not licensable in any case, and unattested: section 10.
3. **Bartholdi** (forward and lateral): reached only through Grover Cleveland at the 1886
   Statue of Liberty dedication. Lloyd's connection is to Cleveland, not Bartholdi, and even
   that is dossier-only. Not carried.
4. **Pamela Colman Smith** (lateral): reached only through **Sir Henry Irving**, of whom the
   dossier says Lloyd wrote "Reminiscences of Sir Henry Irving." Irving would be a real
   one-directional relation if the title is real, but it could not be verified in the
   finding-aid text searched, and Smith has no relation to Lloyd at all. Neither is named in
   the file. Flagged in case the operator wants the Irving memoir chased.

The only atlas travelers named inside a campa are therefore **Symmes** (hedged, item 1) and
**Einstein** (attested event, section 9). Samuel Thomson, Grover Cleveland and William Morgan
are named as historical persons with real one-directional Lloyd relations; none has a file.
