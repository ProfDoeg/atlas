# Leo Chiachio: research report

*Compiled 2026-08-11 for the Atlas of Journeys. Argentine embroidery artist, born 1969, one half of the duo Chiachio & Giannone with Daniel Giannone (born Cordoba, 1964). Both are alive and working as of the most recent sources checked. A career in progress, not a life closed.*

**Legend.** **[A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or a claim circulating without a locatable document. Contradictions are printed and left open; unreachable sources are named with the reason; gaps are stated as gaps.

---

## 0. Two problems that govern the whole file

**The brief presupposed a death that has not happened.** The request was framed around a tomb, its fate, and later legends. There is no tomb. English Wikipedia (July 2026 state) and Spanish Wikipedia ("Ambos artistas permanecen activos") both have the artists living and working; the CV runs to a 2024 solo show **[A]**. No death date, grave, monument, or posthumous legend exists, and none has been invented. The nearest thing to a monument is four Argentine public collections (section 8). The journey file ends on the living studio.

**The subject is one man; nearly every attested fact is about two.** Chiachio has a separately documented birth, birthplace, and training. From 2003 the record is almost entirely that of Chiachio & Giannone, a single signature, and the interviews are mostly transcribed without speaker labels. Where a source splits the speakers this file follows the split (section 10); where it does not, the claim is recorded as the duo's. Any sentence implying Chiachio acted alone after 2003 would be a fabrication.

## 1. Origins, 1964-1969

**Chiachio, born 1969 [A on the year; contradictory on the place].** The official CV gives only "Buenos Aires, Argentina." Both Wikipedia editions give **Banfield, Lomas de Zamora, Buenos Aires Province**. A difference of precision rather than an outright conflict; not adjudicated. No month, day, hospital, street, or parent's name survives anywhere reachable.

**Coordinate contradiction inside this dossier.** Geography gives Banfield as **-34.7481, -58.3958**; the afterlife pass gives **-34.75, -58.3944**, about 200 m away. Neither is sourced to a gazetteer. The journey file uses the geography value.

**Giannone, born 1964, city of Cordoba [A, CV and both Wikipedia editions].** Nothing of his childhood is documented. The two biographies run unconnected for thirty-nine years.

## 2. Training, dates unknown

**Escuela Nacional de Bellas Artes "Prilidiano Pueyrredon" and Escuela Superior de Bellas Artes "Ernesto de la Carcova" [A, CV and both Wikipedia editions].** Chiachio trained as a painter under **Pablo Suarez, Tulio de Sagastizabal, Ahuva Szlimowicz, Graciela Canero**; Giannone under **Sergio Bazan, Teresa Lascano, Graciela Canero**. Canero taught both, before they met.

**No enrollment or graduation years appear in any source reached.** Roughly 1969 to 2003 contains no dated event at all. The journey file carries two training stops with placeholder dates and the hedge stated in `date_confidence`.

**Three coordinate corrections, propagated.**

- **Prilidiano Pueyrredon**, given as -34.5786, -58.4736 with a "Belgrano campus" gloss, both wrong. The school is at **Av. Gral. Las Heras 1749, Recoleta, -34.5917, -58.3913** (es.wikipedia); the discarded pin lies about 8 km northwest in Villa Urquiza. Folded into the UNA in 2014.
- **Ernesto de la Carcova**, given as -34.6011, -58.3888, a central-city placeholder. The school is at **Av. Espana 1701, Costanera Sur, -34.6175, -58.3564** (es.wikipedia), about 3.6 km southeast. Decreed 28 February 1921, opened 1923, renamed 1928, absorbed into the IUNA/UNA in the 1990s.
- A third pass attached **-34.6037, -58.3816** to the education claim. That is Plaza de Mayo. Dropped.

**The turn to thread is undated.** One account has embroidery entering from a skill learned in primary school **[R]**. No source dates it or names the first embroidered work.

## 3. The meeting and the founding of the signature, 2003

**They meet at a friend's house in Buenos Aires, 2003 [A, English Wikipedia].** The house is not identified. The city coordinate **-34.6037, -58.3816** is used as an explicit placeholder; it is the atlas's canonical Buenos Aires pin, inherited byte-identical from the interlock lens.

## 7. The flags, 2017-2020

**2017 [A, CV]:** finalist, **Loewe Craft Prize, Madrid**; "Arqueologia suave," Ruth Benzacar; **"Queer Threads: Crafting Community and Identity" (AMMO Books)** published.

**2018 [A, CV and en.wikipedia]:** residency, **Swatch Art Peace Hotel, the Bund, Shanghai**, with "Comrades in Shanghai"; **"Familia a seis colores," Centro Cultural Kirchner**. The CCK residency is the best-documented episode in the file, because a journalist was let into room 204. From "Hacia la marcha del orgullo gay," Revista N, all **[A]**: the greeting "Bienvenido al taller-sala"; a month installed with two more to come, Monday to Friday, eight hours daily; ninety invited collaborators sewing one flag, intended for visibility on the day of the pride march. The method used twice more in California is stated here in the artists' own words.

**"Splash criollo": the year is contradicted, and the earlier pass took the wrong side.** Spanish Wikipedia dates the Premio Adquisicion Presidencia de la Nacion to **2018**. The CV and ruthbenzacar.com both date it to **2019**, at the **108th Salon Nacional de las Artes Visuales**. Two primary-adjacent sources against one encyclopedia. The journey file uses 2019; the contradiction is the source of a chronology-versus-geography split inside this dossier.

**The Palais de Glace is withdrawn as the venue.** An earlier pass inferred it from the salon's customary host site. The Palais de Glace (Posadas 1725, -34.5858, -58.3894) was closed for restoration in that period, its exhibitions relocated and its offices moved to the Centro Cultural Borges. **The actual 2019 venue was not established.** The journey file pins the award to the city coordinate and states the gap.

**2019, MOLAA, Long Beach [A, en.wikipedia].** Residency March to May; **"Celebrando la Diversidad," a 120-foot embroidered flag** carrying contributions from **more than 3,500** LGBT community members; carried in the **Long Beach Pride Parade** and shown at Pride at the Port, San Pedro. **Coordinate correction:** MOLAA is at 628 Alamitos Avenue, East Village Arts District, **33.7746, -118.1796** (en.wikipedia); an earlier pass gave 33.7683, -118.1892, about 1.1 km off. Parade pin 33.7701, -118.1937 and San Pedro pin 33.7395, -118.2687 unchallenged; San Pedro is folded into the parade stop.

**2020, the LATINX Flag Project, San Diego County [A, CV and en.wikipedia].** Residency at the **Lux Art Institute** (25 January to 21 March per en.wikipedia), workshops at three venues, and **"La segunda piel de l@s amig@s" at Museo Sivori**, Buenos Aires. Three coordinate corrections:

- **Lux Art Institute**, given as 33.0687, -117.2445, about 4.5 km too far north. Lux is at 1550 S El Camino Real, southern Encinitas beside Cardiff-by-the-Sea: approximately **33.030, -117.262**. The glass-walled public studio is the institute's signature format, not re-verified.
- **The San Diego museum was misnamed.** An earlier pass wrote "Contemporary Art Museum, San Diego / Barrio Logan" at 32.6989, -117.1428. English Wikipedia says **Museum of Contemporary Art San Diego**: La Jolla (32.8466, -117.2782) and downtown (32.7157, -117.1700). Neither is in Barrio Logan. Which site hosted the workshops is **not established**; the downtown pin is used and the uncertainty stated.
- **The Studio Door**, placed in Normal Heights at 32.7594, -117.1225, is at 3867 4th Ave in Hillcrest: approximately **32.7476, -117.1601**. Recorded, not carried; **The Front Arte & Cultura, San Ysidro (32.5546, -117.0342)** is carried, because the border location is the point of it.

## 10. Quotation problems

Three defects in the quote pool, all corrected in the journey file.

**1. Misattribution.** "Nos consideramos pintores y lo que nos seduce es el manejo del color... pintamos con otros materiales." The words are real and they are **Giannone's, not Chiachio's**: the Mazzei transcript labels the speaker "-Gi:". The elided sentence runs "Es indistinto estar pintando con pinceles, oleos y acrilicos, que con agujas e hilos." In an entry on Leo Chiachio this must be credited to Giannone; the journey file names the speaker in `quote_source`.

**2. Silent splice.** The Glusman answer was quoted as "We do all our work together and we are present at all stages of the process. No area of specialization. We create together." It actually reads: "...at all stages of the process, from the sketching to the embroidery itself. Together. No area of specialization. We create together." Two clauses dropped, a comma turned into a period, no ellipsis. Full wording restored.

**3. An idea upgraded into a maxim.** "Todo acto artistico es un acto politico" appears at the Baulo URL as a lowercase subordinate clause: "Si bien comulgamos con la idea que todo acto artistico es un acto politico...". Quoting it as a flat declarative turns an idea they align with into their own aphorism. **Dropped** rather than trimmed.

**Attribution note.** The Baulo and Mazzei interviews are transcribed as duo answers except where a speaker label appears, so every quote in the journey file is credited to the duo rather than to Chiachio alone. The single exception is "Con el transformamos el mundo," attributed to Chiachio personally about Giannone (La Nacion, citing Canal Encuentro).
