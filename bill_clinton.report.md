# Bill Clinton: research report

*Compiled 2026-08-11 for `bill_clinton.journey.json`. William Jefferson Clinton, born William Jefferson Blythe III, Hope, Arkansas, 19 August 1946; 42nd President of the United States, 1993 to 2001.*

**Living subject.** No death, no tomb, no posthumous reception; library, portrait, and litigation analogues stand in their place, and the absence is stated, not filled.

**Legend.** **[A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, disputed characterization, or claim circulating without a document behind it. **[G]** gap, stated and not filled.

**Method.** The verified pool arrived in five lenses (chronology, geography, quotes, interlock, afterlife) with an adversarial pass applied. That pass returned eleven corrections, all carried into the journey and listed below rather than quietly absorbed. Coordinates come from the pool where the verifier did not object and byte-for-byte from the verifier where it supplied a replacement; four pins are my own geocodes, flagged in the stop and here.

## 1. Hope and Hot Springs, 1946 to 1964

- Born **19 August 1946**, Hope, three months after his father died in a car accident in Missouri; the South Hervey Street house is his maternal grandparents', he is in their care until about four, and it is later a national historic site. **[A: NPS; Britannica; History.com]**
- Virginia marries **Roger Clinton Sr.**, a car dealer with a drinking problem; the family moves to **Hot Springs**, 1950. Brother **Roger Jr.** born 1956. **[A: History.com; Wikipedia]**
- Surname used informally at school, formalized about age fifteen, c. 1962; the mechanism and exact date of the legal change are unestablished. **[A: Wikipedia; G]** St. John's Catholic Elementary, Ramble Elementary, Hot Springs High School; saxophone in a jazz trio. **[A: Wikipedia; Clinton, *My Life*, 2004]**
- **24 July 1963**: Boys Nation delegate, shakes Kennedy's hand in the **Rose Garden**; photograph in the JFK Library archive. **[A: Miller Center; JFK Library]** That the encounter *caused* the vocation is the subject's own framing, repeated by biographers who cite him. **[R]** The campa narrates the retelling, not the causation.
- Graduates high school **1964**; enrolls at **Georgetown**, School of Foreign Service. **[A: History.com; Miller Center]**

## 2. Georgetown, Oxford, the draft, Yale, 1964 to 1973

- Georgetown 1964 to 1968, B.S. foreign service; part-time work for **Senator J. William Fulbright**, later credited as the formative mentor. **[A: Britannica; Miller Center]** **Rhodes Scholarship 1968**, University College, Oxford; no degree; travel in Europe and the Soviet Union. **[A: Wikipedia; Rhodes Trust]**
- The draft sequence is the pool's one outright chronological error: the chronology lens bundled scholarship and draft notice at 1968, and the verifier split them. Rhodes **1968**; induction notice dated **April 1969**, reaching him at Oxford; **July to August 1969** ROTC arrangement with **Col. Eugene Holmes**, then abandoned; **December 1969** lottery, number **311**; the Holmes letter published during the **1992** campaign. **[A: Wikipedia, early life and draft section; Miller Center]** One stop carries the sequence, the split spelled out in `date_confidence`.
- **Yale Law School** 1970; meets **Hillary Rodham** in the law library **1971**; J.D. **1973**. Enrollment and degree **[A: Britannica]**; the library meeting rests only on the couple's own account, which Wikipedia attributes to "standard biographical accounts" with no independent document **[R]**.

## 3. Arkansas, 1974 to 1992

- **1974**: loses the 3rd district House race to **John Paul Hammerschmidt** while teaching law at Fayetteville. **[A: Miller Center]** **11 October 1975**: marries Hillary Rodham, Fayetteville (Britannica gives the year only, History.com the full date and place). **[A]**
- **1976**: elected **Attorney General**, serving January 1977 to January 1979. **1978**: elected **Governor** at 32, youngest in the nation. **[A: Britannica; Miller Center]** **February 1980**: **Chelsea Victoria Clinton** born, Little Rock, month only. **[A month: History.com; G day]**
- **1980**: loses reelection to **Frank White**. **[A: Miller Center]** The 1982 televised apology advertisement is general record, not in the pool. **[R]**
- **1982**: reelected, then again in **1984, 1986, 1990** (correction 3). **[A: Wikipedia]** **NGA chairman 1986**; **DLC chairman 1990**. **[A: Britannica]**
- **1988**: the over-long Dukakis nominating speech, Atlanta. **[A: Miller Center]** No stop: no venue coordinate in the pool, and I decline to geocode the hall from memory. **[G]**
- **Renaissance Weekend**, Hilton Head, recurring New Year's through the 1980s and 1990s, later moved to Charleston. **[A: Wikipedia]** The journey bounds the series rather than leaving it open.

## 4. First term, 1993 to 1996

- **November 1992**: elected over incumbent **George H. W. Bush** and **Ross Perot**, 370 electoral votes. **[A: Britannica]** **20 January 1993**: inaugurated at 46. **[A: History.com]**
- **December 1993**: signs the **NAFTA** implementation legislation; month only, day defaulted. **[A: Britannica]** **November 1994**: Republicans take both houses. **[A: Britannica; Miller Center]**
- **6 June 1994** Omaha Beach, 50th D-Day anniversary; **12 July 1994** Brandenburg Gate, on the Allied withdrawal; **Kremlin summits with Yeltsin**, given as two bare years, 1994 and 1998. **[A: Clinton Presidential Library speech archive; Wikipedia]**
- **8 July 1994**: **20th G7**, **Royal Palace of Naples**, hosted by **Silvio Berlusconi** two months into office, with Kohl, Mitterrand, Major, and guest Yeltsin. **[A: silvio_berlusconi.journey.json, stop 1994-07-08]**
- **23 April 1995**: memorial service after the **Oklahoma City bombing**, 168 dead. **[A: Wikipedia; OKC National Memorial]** That the speech lifted his ratings and reshaped the presidency's tone is a historians' judgment. **[R]**
- **November 1995**: hosts the talks producing the **Dayton Agreement** at **Wright-Patterson AFB**; **1995 Belfast** and **1997 Sarajevo** are equally attested. **[A: Wikipedia]**
- **Western Wall, Jerusalem, 1994 and 1998**: visits and folded-note custom are **[R]** in the pool, sourced to a general article and photo captions, years without months. Kept **[R]**, defaulted to 1994-01-01, pinned to the canonical **Temple Mount 31.778, 35.2354** from `joffrey_rudel.journey.json` per the interlock lens.
