# Joseph Alois Schumpeter (1883-1950): research report

*Compiled 2026-08-25 for `joseph_schumpeter.journey.json`. Sections by life phase.
**[A]** = attested, source inline. **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition, or a research-pool
claim not independently confirmed. Contradictions are flagged, not adjudicated. Pool
corrections are consolidated in their own section, each with its evidence.*

## 1. Moravia and Graz, 1883-1893

- **Born 8 February 1883 at Triesch (Třešť), Moravia**, only child of the textile manufacturer
  Josef Schumpeter (1855-1887) and Johanna Grüner. **[A: Deutsche Biographie sfz106815;
  Harvard Crimson obituary, 9 Jan 1950]**
- **Father dies 14 January 1887. [A: Deutsche Biographie]** The **hunting accident** of the
  retellings is **[R]**: no death record located, and the stop says so.
- **Move to Graz, 1888**, entering Volksschule. **[A: de.wikipedia; HET profile]**

## 2. Vienna: the Theresianum, 1893-1901

Johanna marries Feldmarschallleutnant **Sigismund von Kéler**; the marriage opens the
**Theresianum**, attended 1893-1901. **[A: HET; Deutsche Biographie lists Kéler as stepfather
from 1893]** His age gap to Johanna is standard in the literature but not re-derived from a
register: **[R]**.

## 3. University of Vienna, 1901-1906

- **Enrolled in law**, economics being taught inside that faculty. **[A: HET]**
- **Böhm-Bawerk's private seminar from October 1904**, with Ludwig von Mises, Otto Bauer and
  Rudolf Hilferding. **[A, and attested inside the corpus: `eugen_von_bohm_bawerk` stages it
  as "Vienna, four men at one table" (1905-10-01), `ludwig_von_mises` as "University of
  Vienna, Bohm-Bawerk's seminar" (1904-10-01); both name Schumpeter.]** Pin 48.2131 / 16.3608
  inherited byte-identical. Per stop-ownership the *scene* is the teacher's; Schumpeter's file
  carries only his own presence at the table.
- **Doctorate in law, February 1906, aged 23. [A: Deutsche Biographie; Crimson; HET]**

## 5. Czernowitz and Graz, 1909-1918

- **Extraordinary professor, Franz Joseph University, Czernowitz, 1909-1911. [A: Deutsche
  Biographie; HET]** The 1909 premises are **a gap**: unnamed in every source consulted, so
  the file labels the stops by city.
- **The duel with the librarian. [R].** No contemporaneous police, university or newspaper
  record located, and the Graz Schumpeter Centre page is silent on it, independently checked.
  **[HBS Working Knowledge (2007), reporting Thomas McCraw]**
- **Theorie der wirtschaftlichen Entwicklung, first edition 1911**, written at Czernowitz.
  **[A: Graz Schumpeter Centre; HET]**
- **Full professor at Graz by Supreme Resolution, 30 October 1911**, over Richard Hildebrand's
  opposition, sole economist in the faculty. **[A: Graz Schumpeter Centre]**
- **Columbia exchange professorship, 1913-1914**; Fisher, Mitchell, Taussig; honorary doctorate
  at 30. **[A: Deutsche Biographie; Graz Centre; HET]** Separation from Gladys here **[A]**;
  divorce date contradicted, section 8.
- **Dean of the law faculty, 1916/17. [A: Graz Schumpeter Centre]** The memoranda to Emperor
  Karl I are **[R]** and uncited.
- **Epochen der Dogmen- und Methodengeschichte, 1914**, in Max Weber's *Grundriss der
  Sozialökonomik*. **[R]** at corpus level, **[A]** bibliographically: Weber's file never
  names Schumpeter, so the link rests on the bibliographic record.

## 6. Revolution and office, 1918-1919

- **German Socialization Commission, from late 1918. [A: Deutsche Biographie; HET]** Members
  alongside him: Hilferding, Lederer, Ballod, Wilbrandt, Hue, Vogelstein, with Heimann as
  secretary-general. **[A: de.wikipedia]**
- **State Secretary for Finance under Renner from 15 March 1919, aged 36. [A: Deutsche
  Biographie; Crimson; en.wikipedia]** Programme (capital levy, currency stabilization,
  opposition to immediate Anschluss, Vienna as a regional financial hub) **[A:
  schumpeter.info index of ministerial speeches and programmes]**.
- **Out of office 17 October 1919. [A: Deutsche Biographie; de.wikipedia]**
- **The Kola / Alpine Montangesellschaft affair, 1919. [R].** The covert currency operation
  through Richard Kola, the alleged Italian purchase of Alpine Montan shares to block Bauer's
  socialization plan, and the later finding of no personal wrongdoing all come from pool
  synthesis; **no primary investigative record was consulted**. Kept as a hedged stop.

## 7. The Biedermann Bank, 1921-1926

- **President of M. L. Biedermann & Co. Bankaktiengesellschaft from 1921. [A: en.wikipedia]**
  The date 26 July 1921, the roughly 240 employees by 1923 and the deceiving Czechoslovak
  investor come from **Peneder and Resch, 2025**, **unfetchable** behind a publisher
  authentication redirect; carried as reported and flagged in the stop.
- **Wels (electrical engineering) and Hainfeld (porcelain insulators). [R]**, from the same
  unfetchable study. Kept as flagged stops: the venture failure is independently corroborated
  and the siting is the only concrete evidence of what the credit financed.
- **Forced to resign September 1924** amid the Vienna franc-speculation crash **[A:
  en.wikipedia]**; **the bank itself failed in 1926 [A: Deutsche Biographie]**. That he
  assumed the debts personally and repaid them from later salary is standard in the
  literature: **[R]** at that precision.
- **The Braun-Stammfest investigation, 1925. [R]**, retagged from the pool's [A].

## 8. Bonn and the deaths of 1926

- **Chair of public finance, Bonn, from October 1925**, via Arthur Spiethoff; German
  citizenship 1925; students Stolper, Lösch, Singer, Kläre Tisch; second edition of the
  *Theorie*, 1926. **[A: HET; Deutsche Biographie for citizenship]**
- **Conversion to Lutheranism and marriage to Anna Josefina "Annie" Reisinger, Vienna,
  5 November 1925. [A: Deutsche Biographie]** **The church is a gap**; coordinate is the city.
- **Johanna dies 22 June 1926; Annie and the infant son Joseph die in childbirth 3 August
  1926. [A]** on year and closeness. **The day-order between the two fetched sources is not
  fully consistent** and the stop says so. **Annie's place of death is inferred** from the
  couple's Bonn residence; no hospital or address is documented.
- **Harvard visiting appointments, 1927-1928 and 1930. [A: HET]**
- **Unresolved contradiction, carried into the file.** Deutsche Biographie dates the divorce
  from Gladys to **1920**, Wikipedia to **1925**, the year of the second marriage; neither
  reproduces a document or explains the other. The Columbia stop and the 1925 marriage stop
  both name the disagreement in their campa text.

## 10. Harvard, 1932-1949

- **Permanent Harvard professorship, 1932**, before Hitler took power; later the George F.
  Baker chair. **[A: en.wikipedia; Crimson; Econlib, all agreeing on the year]**
- **Marriage to Elizabeth Boody Firuski, New York, 1937**; last visit to Europe the same year.
  **[A: Deutsche Biographie]** **Venue a gap**; generic midtown coordinate.
- **Naturalised US citizen, 1939. [A: Deutsche Biographie; en.wikipedia]**
- ***Business Cycles*, 2 vols., 1939. [A: Econlib]** Kuznets criticism and poor reception
  **[A: HET]** as fact, **[R]** as to the campa's characterisation.
- ***Capitalism, Socialism and Democracy*, 1942. [A: en.wikipedia; Econlib]**
- **Wartime federal investigation of both Schumpeters, nothing found. [R]**: pool synthesis of
  the FBI record, not independently re-checked; the stop says so.
- **President of the American Economic Association for 1948. [A: AEA past-presidents list]**
- **First president of the International Economic Association in designation only, dying before
  presiding over a meeting. [A: Crimson obituary]**

## 11. Death and after, 1950-1954

- **Died in his sleep of a cerebral hemorrhage at his country home, Taconic (Salisbury),
  Connecticut, early Sunday 8 January 1950, aged 66. [A: Crimson obituary; corroborated by
  Deutsche Biographie and en.wikipedia. 8 January 1950 was a Sunday.]**
- **Funeral at St. John's Episcopal Church, Salisbury, 10 January 1950**, a separate memorial
  service being planned at Harvard's Memorial Church. **[A: Crimson obituary]**
- **Burial: an honest gap.** No source consulted names a cemetery or grave. The Crimson gives
  the funeral church, not interment; en. and de.wikipedia are silent; FindAGrave was blocked.
  Stated as a gap in the campa, not guessed at.
- ***History of Economic Analysis*, 1954**, completed from the unfinished manuscript by
  Elizabeth Boody Schumpeter. **[A: Econlib; de.wikipedia]**

## Corrections applied to the research pool

- **Birth name.** Pool: "Joseph Alois *Julius*", father "Joseph Alois Karl". Deutsche
  Biographie: no third forename (Julius was the maternal grandfather), father "Josef".
- **Birth pin.** The pool gave two different wrong values for one town, 49.3167 / 15.4833
  (geography lens) and 49.3211 / 15.4442 (afterlife lens), 2.9 and 4.6 km outside it in
  different directions. Třešť is 49.29083 / 15.48222; file uses **49.2908 / 15.4822**.
- **1887-1893.** Pool: "moved to Vienna following his father's death", 1893. Record: Graz in
  1888 after the death, Vienna in 1893 after the remarriage (de.wikipedia; HET).
- **Berlin and England.** Pool: Berlin "during his Vienna studies", England 1907. HET: Berlin
  summer 1906, after the February doctorate; LSE 1906-1907.
- **The duel.** Pool: "c. 1910", Schumpeter challenging. McCraw via HBS: 1909 ("he was only
  26, and had just started teaching"), the librarian issuing the challenge.
- **Czernowitz pin.** Pool: 48.2884 / 25.926 as "Chernivtsi National University". That
  building was not the university until 1955; file uses **48.2921 / 25.9358**, by city.
- **Graz deanship.** Pool: memoranda to Emperor Karl I, cited to the Graz Centre, whose page
  carries the deanship and nothing else. They are **[R]** and uncited.
- **Socialization Commission.** Pool: "chaired by Kautsky". de.wikipedia: co-chaired with
  Ernst Francke, work ceasing early April 1919.
- **17 October 1919.** Pool: "the government was replaced". Record: Renner II resigned after
  Saint-Germain, Renner III was elected the same day without Schumpeter, and Renner was
  chancellor throughout 1918-1920. de. and en.wikipedia.
- **Biedermann.** Pool, following HET: "collapsed 1924". Record: resignation September 1924
  (en.wikipedia); bank failed 1926 (Deutsche Biographie).
- **Braun-Stammfest, the pool's most consequential tagging error.** Tagged **[A]** on a source
  line reading "dossier synthesis", while no source consulted (Deutsche Biographie, en. and
  de.wikipedia, HET, schumpeter.info) names him, the case, or any questioning. Retagged
  **[R]**: an attested tag on an uncorroborated criminal claim about a named private
  individual is not acceptable.
- **Tokyo 1931.** Pool: cited to HET, "Tokyo College of Commerce", January-February. Record:
  HET has no mention of Japan at all; the school was Tokyo University of Commerce 1920-1944
  (en.wikipedia); the month range is unsupported and defaults to 01.
- **Taconic pin.** The pool contradicted itself, 41.9807 / -73.3287 (geography) against
  41.9843 / -73.4218 (afterlife). The afterlife value is right; file uses **41.985 / -73.422**.
- **The funeral.** Pool: "the next day". Record: two days after, the death being 8 January and
  the 9 January obituary announcing services "tomorrow at 2 p.m."
- **The self-joke.** Pool: paraphrase, economist first, "achieved two of the three". Crimson,
  10 January 1950: a direct first-person quotation, order lover / horseman / economist,
  asserting only that he failed at one.
- **Four quotation fixes**, against the 1942 Harper first edition, the Routledge text and the
  HEA OCR. "Can capitalism survive? No." upgraded **[R] to [A]**, period not comma, Part II
  Prologue p. 61, and the file carries the following sentences, since the first line alone
  inverts his meaning into the doom-prophecy he was disclaiming. **"doors"** plural not
  "door", ch. VII p. 82. "illustrate **the same** process of industrial mutation", p. 83,
  **not used as a quotation** because it immediately precedes "This process of Creative
  Destruction is the essential fact about capitalism" and consecutive sentences must not
  become separate scenes; it sits in that stop's `suggested_refs`. "the **professional**
  psychologists of their **times**", HEA pp. 27-28, with the clause carrying the argument.
- **One quotation with the right words and the wrong book, and it is not used.** "the doing of
  new things..." is not in CSD: zero occurrences, full text searched twice. Wikipedia's own
  cite is *JEH* 7:2 (1947): 149-159, confirmed via Crossref, but the article was unopenable,
  so the file substitutes the verbatim CSD ch. XII sentence on the function of entrepreneurs.
- **Five further quotations used were verified only through reproductions**, not print
  originals; they are listed with their sources in the journey file's `quote_source` fields.

## Apparatus relations for the operator

Schumpeter is **not** listed in `EXCEPTIONS.md`. Two pool relations are not rooted in his own
life. Per the adversary's duty they are **kept and flagged here, not silently deleted**, and
both run **forward**, the direction the exception never licenses.

1. **Creative destruction applied to the digital economy.** The afterlife lens carries
   McCraw's 2007 report that Lawrence Summers and Brad DeLong each called the twenty-first
   century "the century of Schumpeter", crediting the revival to internet-driven
   entrepreneurship, and Detlef Siebert's 2016 documentary *The Man Who Discovered
   Capitalism*, arguing the digital economy vindicates his theory. He died in 1950 and is not
   on record on any of it. **No stop was written**, and nothing about the internet or
   twenty-first-century entrepreneurship appears in the file. If the author wants him added to
   the forward-grants table, the natural site is the creative-destruction stop, one or two
   sentences, at his own Harvard pin, on the Burroughs and Derrida pattern.
2. **The reception apparatus generally, and much weaker.** The Graz Schumpeter Centre, the
   Schumpeter Society, the Wuppertal school, *The Economist*'s column, the Chernivtsi plaque,
   the street names and the birth house are other people's later acts, not his travels. **No
   stop was written for any of them**; six are carried as `suggested_refs` on his own stops
   for the corresponding places.

No **backward** apparatus relation was found, so nothing is proposed for the main list.
