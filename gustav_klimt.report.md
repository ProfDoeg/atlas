# Gustav Klimt (1862-1918): research report

*Compiled 2026-08-16 from the verified research pool for the journey `gustav_klimt`. Four lenses (chronology, geography, quotations, afterlife) were checked against the German and English Wikipedia articles and the records they cite. What survived, what was corrected, what the lenses contradict each other about, and what nothing could settle.*

**Legend.** **[A]** = attested, source named. **[R]** = reconstruction or tradition, repeated without primary documentation. **[corr.]** records what the pool said and what the check found; corrections are logged rather than silently applied.

**Coordinate corrections.** Every pin below was wrong in the pool and is corrected in the journey. Two lenses gave different wrong figures for the same cemetery and the same Hietzing studio, the clearest sign that coordinates here were generated rather than looked up.

Magnitude of the pool's error, worst first: Schloss Immendorf 71 km, Schloss Kammer 12 km, Villa Paulick 10 km, Litzlberg 8 km, Palais Stoclet up to 1.9 km, Hietzinger Friedhof up to 1.5 km, the Baumgarten birth house and the Stadttheater Karlsbad 1.4 km each, the family apartment (a bare Vienna placeholder) 1 km, Hermesvilla 700 m, Klimt Villa up to 600 m, and five more between 200 and 400 m (Kunstgewerbeschule, San Vitale, Kunstschau pavilion, Allgemeines Krankenhaus, Bloch-Bauer residence). The corrected values are in the journey file.

## 1. Baumgarten and the school, 1862-1883

- Born 14 July 1862 at Baumgarten, then a village outside Vienna and now inside the fourteenth district, second of seven children of the gold engraver Ernst Klimt the Elder and Anna Klimt, nee Finster. **[A, parish record via Klimt Foundation and both Wikipedias]**
- **[corr.]** The pool called the birth site unmarked ("only the street's name in the parish register"). It is not: the house stood at **Linzer Strasse 247**, demolished 1965, and carries a commemorative plaque.
- Poverty and repeated moves. **[A]** The sister Anna dies at five in 1874; the sister Klara develops a lasting mental disturbance. **[A]**
- Enters the Kunstgewerbeschule 1876 under Ferdinand Laufberger, after 1881 under Julius Victor Berger; brother Ernst follows in 1877. **[A]** Ferstel's building was occupied from 1875, so address and year agree.
- The Kunstler-Compagnie (Gustav, Ernst, Franz Matsch) takes joint commissions from about 1880; the three leave the school and share a studio in 1883. **[A]**
- **Gap.** Nothing documents who suggested the boy apply, or how the scholarship was obtained.

## 2. The Kunstler-Compagnie on the road, 1883-1891

- **Reichenberg (Liberec)** theatre ceilings, the first paid commission. **[R]** The year drifts between accounts and no contract text was reachable.
- **Hermesvilla**, Lainzer Tiergarten, 1884-1885, the villa built 1882-1886. **[A]** **[corr.]** The salon ceiling *Der Fruehling* is by Matsch with **Gustav and Georg** Klimt. Georg was the metalworker brother, not Ernst, so "the Kunstler-Compagnie" is the wrong label here. The campa names Georg.
- **Fiume (Rijeka)** theatre ceiling, 1885. **[A]** **[corr.]** The pool called Ivan pl. Zajc a "Croatian playwright"; he was a **composer**.
- **Karlsbad** theatre, 1886. **[A]** **[corr.]** The pool implied the frescoes are anonymous: two ceiling scenes are individually Klimt's, and the curtain's right lunette carries self-portraits of all three painters, the three Klimt sisters opposite.
- **Burgtheater** staircase ceilings 1886-1888; the gouache of the old auditorium recorded before the 1888 demolition; Golden Cross of Merit 1888; the first **Kaiserpreis**, 1890, for that gouache. **[A]** **[corr.]** Two errors here. The pool attached to 1888 an honorary membership of "the University of Munich and the University of Vienna"; the German article records honorary membership of the **Munich Academy in 1906**, and the claim is dropped rather than redated since the Vienna half is unverifiable. And the geography lens attached the Kaiserpreis to the Kunsthistorisches Museum commission; it belongs to the gouache.
- **Kunsthistorisches Museum** staircase spandrels, including *Ancient Greece*. **[A]** The German chronology dates the work from **1891**, English accounts 1890-1891; recorded as 1891, disagreement in `date_confidence`.
- **Munich Glaspalast**. **[R]** The pool gave "c. 1888-1892"; the German record narrows the travel to **1888/89**. **[corr.]** The pool said the 1931 fire destroyed works by Delacroix; it destroyed the German Romantics show, roughly 3,000 works.
- **Trieste. Contradiction, flagged not resolved.** The geography lens places Trieste on the **1903 Italian journey**, citing "itinerary letters"; the German article places the **Krakow-Trieste-Venice-Munich travel in 1888/89** and gives the 1903 itinerary as Venice and Ravenna only. The journey follows the German dating. No letter text was reachable.

## 3. The year two men die, and the University commission, 1891-1897

- Studio at Josefstaedter Strasse 21. **[corr.]** The pool gave 1892-1911; the German record gives **from 1891**, with the move to Feldmuehlgasse in 1911/12.
- Father dies of a stroke, July 1892; brother Ernst dies of pericarditis, December 1892, at twenty-eight. Gustav supports both households and paints little for several years. **[A]** **[corr.]** The pool put the family apartment at a generic Vienna placeholder; the address of record is **Westbahnstrasse 36, Vienna 7**, and the same placeholder error recurs at the 1918 stroke entry.
- **Emilie Louise Floge. [A]** for the relationship, **[corr.]** for the kinship: the pool called her "sister-in-law of his widowed sister-in-law Helene Floge". Ernst Klimt married **Helene Floge**, making Helene Gustav's sister-in-law; **Emilie was Helene's sister**.
- University of Vienna Great Hall commission, 1894, for Philosophy, Medicine and Jurisprudence; the offer went first to Matsch. **[A]**
- **Gap.** Klimt's account of what he intended survives only in the three-sentence gloss on *Philosophy* quoted below. No correspondence, journal or studio notebook of the commission is reachable.

## 4. Ver Sacrum: the Secession, 1897-1905

- Founding member 1897, first president, the periodical *Ver Sacrum*; Olbrich's building opens November 1898 with a Klimt-curated exhibition. **[A]**
- **Contradiction, flagged not resolved.** How long was he president? The afterlife lens says **1897-1905**; the German article **1897-1899**, 1905 being instead the year he **resigned**. The journey claims only "elected its first president".
- *Pallas Athena* (1898) adopted as a Secession emblem; *Nuda Veritas* (1899). **[A]** **[corr.]** The Schiller epigram is verbatim and correctly assigned, but the pool placed it "on the reverse/surface". It stands **above the figure, on the face of the canvas**.
- Faculty paintings shown and denounced from 1900. **[corr.]** The petition against *Philosophy* carried **87** signatures, not eighty; and "Klimt keeps the fee" is false, since in 1905 he **repaid the state advance**, financed by August Lederer, to buy the paintings back.
- Eighth Secession exhibition, November 1900: a Mackintosh interior crated from Glasgow and installed; Hoffmann and the future Wiener Werkstaette circle study it in person. **[A, via charles_rennie_mackintosh.journey.json]** **Gap:** nothing documents a personal meeting between the two men; the campa claims only the institutional link.
- Beethoven Frieze, 1902: 34 m, casein and gold leaf on plaster over wire mesh, about 60,000 visitors in two months, bought by Carl Reininghaus to prevent demolition. **[A]** **[corr.]** The afterlife lens says the Secession "still displays it in situ". The frieze was cut off the wall and has been shown since 1985 in a basement room, owned by the Belvedere.
- **Resignation. Contradiction, flagged not resolved.** The chronology lens dates the departure to **1906**; the Secession's record and the geography lens to **14 June 1905**, with the Kunstschau group coalescing 1906-1908. The journey follows 1905 and names the conflict.

## 5. Italy and the gold ground, 1903-1911

- **Ravenna**, December 1903, San Vitale, the fixed point of the Italian year. **[A]**
- **Venice**, spring 1903, San Marco. **[R]** Attested as part of the year's travel; nothing fixes what he looked at or wrote there.
- **Florence. Contradiction, flagged not resolved.** The pool offered Florence as a contested leg of 1903; the German record dates the visits to **1908 and 1911**. An [R] hedge does not rescue a wrong year, so the journey places Florence at 1908 and states the disagreement.
- **Palais Stoclet, Brussels. [A] with a date dispute.** The English Klimt article says "in 1904 he collaborated"; the Stoclet articles date the building and the cartoons **1905-1911**. **[corr.]** The mosaics were executed on site by **Leopold Forstner**, and Klimt's visit to the finished frieze is dated **1914** against the pool's 1910-1911.
- **Portrait of Adele Bloch-Bauer I**, painted 1903-1907, over 100 preparatory drawings; Adele dies February 1925. **[A]** **[corr.]** Both lenses say the picture was seized "after the 1938 Anschluss". The article on the painting dates the **theft to 1941**; Ferdinand Bloch-Bauer fled in 1938. Two events compressed into one.
- **1908 Kunstschau. [A]** **[corr.]** "Sixteen rooms of his own work" is wrong by an order of magnitude: Klimt had **one room**, the Klimt-Saal, hung with **sixteen paintings**. The show ran 1 June to 16 November, and *The Kiss*, catalogued as *Liebespaar*, was bought unfinished by the state there.
- **1909 journey** to Spain with Carl Moll (Toledo, El Greco) and to Paris. **[R]** Both legs attested; the stylistic conclusions drawn from them are contested between Whitford and Natter.
- ***Death and Life*** takes first prize at Rome, Valle Giulia, 1911. **[A]** Whether Klimt attended the opening is **not settled** by surviving correspondence.
- ***The Kiss*** as a portrait of Klimt and Emilie Floge: **[R]**, a widely held reading, not a documented one. Not asserted as fact in the journey.

## 6. The Attersee summers, 1900-1916

- **[corr.]** The pool said Klimt summered at the Attersee "from the late 1890s onward". The German record gives regular stays **1900 to 1916**, largely at the Villa Paulick, Seewalchen.
- **[corr.]** The Attersee pins were the worst-placed in the pool, all three between 8 and 12 km out, and two errors of fact came with them: the Villa Paulick was placed "at Litzlberg" when it stands in **Seewalchen**, and Litzlberg was put on the **eastern** shore when it lies on the **western**.
- **[corr.]** Schloss Kammer does **not** house a Klimt museum; the Attersee centre is the separate Gustav Klimt Zentrum at Kammerl, Schoerfling.
- **[corr.]** The Weissenbach forester's house is dated 1912-1914 in the pool. The local record is explicit: **1914-1916**, "In den Jahren 1914-1916 mietete sich Gustav Klimt in diesem Haus ein und malte das Forsthaus zweimal". Weissenbach is a locality of Steinbach am Attersee; the 1908-1912 summers belong at Kammer.
- The nickname *Waldschrat*, the square format, the cardboard viewfinder and the telescope from the boathouse: **[A]**. The daily swimming is **[R]**, local tradition unconfirmed by any photograph; the campa says so itself.

## 7. Feldmuehlgasse and the end, 1911-1918

- Studio moved to Feldmuehlgasse 11, Hietzing, 1911/1912; memorial rooms opened 30 September 2012. **[A]** **[corr.]** Two lenses gave two different wrong pins for the building.
- Mother Anna dies, 1915. **[A]**
- ***Death and Life*** reworked, 1915. **Three-way contradiction, flagged not resolved.** The chronology lens says the ground went from gold to **blue**, the geography lens **black**, the article on the painting **grey** with mosaic elements added. Two lenses of one pool disagree with each other and both disagree with the article; the campa states the disagreement instead of picking a winner.
- Final portraits including *Lady with a Fan*; *The Bride* and *Adam and Eve* left unfinished in the studio. **[A]**
- Stroke, 11 January 1918, right side paralysed. **[A]** **[corr.]** The geography lens claims sources differ on the date and also has him living "with his mother", who died in 1915. Both Wikipedias give 11 January without qualification; the supposed dispute is recorded as unverified.
- Dies 6 February 1918 at the Allgemeines Krankenhaus, pneumonia following the stroke during the influenza pandemic, aged 55. **[A]** **[corr.]** The pool said Schiele died "within eight months" of Klimt: Schiele died **31 October 1918**, just under nine months later.
- Buried at the Hietzinger Friedhof, Maxingstrasse 15, honorary grave group 5 no. 194. **[A]** **[corr.]** Three different coordinates appear in the pool for this one cemetery.

## 8. Posthumous, carried as sources rather than as stops

Nothing after 9 February 1918 is staged as a stop, on the rule that a stop belongs to whoever was actually there. These are recorded here and, where they attach to a place Klimt stood, cited in that stop's `sources` or `suggested_refs`.

- **Schloss Immendorf, 8 May 1945.** Ten major Klimt works burn, including all three Faculty paintings. **[A]** for fire and date, **[R]** for the agency: attribution to a retreating SS unit rests on a 1946 police report since questioned by Tina Maria Storkovich. **[corr.]** The afterlife lens put Immendorf "near Schweiggers", **71 km too far west**. Klimt was never there, so this is folded into the burial campa and given no pin.
- **Adele Bloch-Bauer I** restituted to Maria Altmann after *Republic of Austria v. Altmann* (2004) and arbitration (January 2006), sold June 2006 to Ronald Lauder for USD 135 million, now at the Neue Galerie. **[A]** That is Altmann's and Lauder's travel, cited in `suggested_refs` on the Elisabethstrasse stop rather than pinned in New York.
- **[corr.]** The afterlife lens calls the Leopold Museum "the largest single public repository of Klimt works". The Leopold holds the largest **Schiele** collection; the **Belvedere** the largest collection of Klimt paintings.
- **"Traum und Wirklichkeit. Wien 1870-1930". Internal contradiction:** the date field says 1985, the prose "the 1986 exhibition". It ran **28 March to 6 October 1985**, mounted by the Historisches Museum der Stadt Wien at the **Kuenstlerhaus**. Not re-verifiable from a live page. **Klimt 2018**, the centenary exhibition year, is **[R]** tourist-board material.
- **The fourteen children. Direct contradiction between two lenses of one pool.** The chronology lens tags "at least fourteen children, four officially recognised" **[A]**; the afterlife lens tags the identical claim **[R]**. The German record documents **seven** confirmed descendants. The journey says only that he fathered children by other women and never married.

## 9. Quotations: what survived the check

Seven quotations are carried in the journey. Wording held; attribution is where the pool failed.

Clean: **"probably the ultimate stage of my development of ornament"** (Stoclet Frieze, **[A]**); **"Our art is not a combat of modern artists against those of the past..."** (Bahr, first *Ver Sacrum*, 1898, **[A]**, attributed to Bahr and not to Klimt); **"If you cannot please everyone..."** (Schiller on *Nuda Veritas*); **"Emilie must come."** (**[R]**, secondhand, no document in Klimt's hand).

Problems carried into `quote_source`: **"I have never painted a self-portrait..."**, *Commentary on a non-existent self-portrait*, **[A as text]** but with the German manuscript and its date unconfirmed. **"On the left a group of figures..."**, verbatim, **[corr.]** cited by the pool to "Philosophy (Klimt)", a redirect with no article text; the passage is in *Klimt University of Vienna Ceiling Paintings*. **"I've had enough of censorship..."**, verbatim, but the pool's date of **1905 is unsupported by the cited source**, which attaches no year and whose context is 1901; carried with the year dropped.

**Rejected.** The passage beginning **"the yearning for happiness is assuaged in poetry"** appears in the *Beethoven Frieze* article only as an **uncited gallery caption**. The pool presented it as text from a 1902 Secession brochure; the article never links that brochure to this wording, and the 1902 catalogue was not reachable. **Also set aside:** Bahr's 1901 speech, "Just as only a lover can reveal to a man what life means to him...", is genuine and about Klimt but attaches to no place, so it has no stop to sit on.

## 10. Interlocks

Only relations in which Klimt himself is one of the two parties are named in the journey prose. Shared geography is not a relation.

- **le_corbusier**: **[A]**, named in the Kunstschau campa. The Le Corbusier file has Charles-Edouard Jeanneret, aged twenty, wintering in Vienna in 1907-08 and **meeting Klimt directly**, the only unambiguous personal encounter in the pool. That winter precedes the Kunstschau opening, and the campa keeps the order straight.
- **charles_rennie_mackintosh**: **[A]** for the exhibition, **[R]** for any personal contact; named in the eighth-exhibition campa. The Glasgow room was installed inside the Secession hall in November 1900, in a show mounted by the association Klimt founded and led. The Mackintosh file concedes Klimt is not named there, so the campa stages no meeting.
- **Declined.** itten (Vienna "in the shadow of Klimt and Kokoschka", but he arrives in the year of Klimt's death, and one-directional influence belongs on his own file), loie_fuller, laban, otto_gross, freud, wolfgang_pauli, steiner: shared-city overlaps, several tagged **[R]** with "no direct mention of Klimt in the file". *Buenos Aires* (-34.6037, -58.3816) is a queue negative finding and gets no stop; *Paris* (48.8566, 2.3522) does, at the canonical coordinates, because the 1909 leg is attested.

## 11. Honest gaps

- **Klimt wrote almost nothing.** No diary, no memoir, no sustained correspondence about his own work is reachable. Where the journey states a motive, it is a contemporary's report or a modern reading, and the campa says which.
- **The 1903 Italian journey has no travel record.** Ravenna in December is fixed; Venice in spring is attested but unelaborated; Trieste and Florence were both wrongly attached to it. **Attendance at Rome, 1911** is unresolved: the prize is documented, the man's whereabouts are not. **The Attersee routine** rests on photographs and on local memory collected decades later.
- **No primary Klimt Foundation, Belvedere or Attersee-Klimt-Zentrum page was fetched in this pass.** Claims credited to those institutions reach this report through the pool; treat institutional attributions in the journey's `sources` arrays as pool-relayed rather than independently confirmed.
