# Robert Moses (1888-1981), research report

*Written 2026-08-23 for `robert_moses.journey.json`. Legend: **[A]** attested, source named · **[R]**
reconstruction, tradition, or single-source claim not confirmed independently here. Contradictions are
flagged, never adjudicated; gaps are stated as gaps. Nearly every popularly known fact about Moses
arrives through one 1974 book, so a claim reaching us only through Caro's* The Power Broker *is tagged
[R] unless something primary or institutional was reached independently.*

## 1. Formation (1888-1918)

- **18 Dec 1888**, born at **83 Dwight Street, New Haven**, to Emanuel and Isabella ("Bella") Cohen
  Moses; nine years there, two blocks from Yale. **[A: NPS Gateway NRA; NYPL finding aid,
  archives.nypl.org/mss/2071; address per Wikipedia]**
- **1897**, the family sells the New Haven business and property for Manhattan, East 46th Street;
  Emanuel never works again **[A: Encyclopedia.com; NPS]**. The address recurs in 1982 as the start of
  the Robert Moses Playground parade, as 20 East 46th Street **[A: Wikipedia]**. Bella's Ethical
  Culture ties are in Moses's own words **[A: Reswick interview, *The Day*, 26 Oct 1934, via JTA]**.
- **GAP, schooling before Yale.** The pool gave one school ("Mohegan Lake School, near Peekskill...
  this military academy"); Wikipedia lists **three** (Dwight School, Mohegan Lake School, a military
  academy near Peekskill). Unresolved, and the pin is ~1.6 km off (Mohegan Lake approx. 41.3195,
  -73.8479). **No schooling stop written.**
- **1905-1909, Yale**: entered at seventeen, B.A. cum laude and Phi Beta Kappa; prizes in Latin and
  public speaking, the *Yale Courant*, varsity water polo **[A: Columbia C250; The Atlantic, Feb
  1939]**. The swimming-team donor episode is in the Atlantic as well as Caro, so [A] on the Atlantic.
- **1909-1911, Wadham College, Oxford**, jurisprudence, B.A. 1911, M.A. 1913 **[A: Columbia C250;
  Wikipedia]**. An Oxford M.A. comes by seniority without residence, so 1913 does not place him there.
- **CORRECTION**: **elected president of the Oxford Union** is **not supported** by the Columbia C250
  page cited for it, nor by Wikipedia. **Dropped from the file. [R, probably false]**
- **1914**, Ph.D., Columbia; dissertation published as *The Civil Service of Great Britain*
  **[A: Columbia C250]**.
- **1913-1917**, **Bureau of Municipal Research**, technical adviser on civil-service reform under
  Mayor **John Purroy Mitchel** **[A: Columbia C250]**. Mitchel loses in 1917; the merit plan, resisted
  by clerks, unions and Tammany, is never implemented and Moses loses the post **[A: Encyclopedia.com;
  Caro, 'The Best Bill Drafter in Albany', *The New Yorker*, 22 July 1974]**. **CORRECTION, pin**: the
  pool's 40.742, -73.99 matches no known Bureau address; it was at **261 Broadway** (40.7141, -74.0063).
- **Aug 1915**, marries **Mary Louise Sims** of Dodgeville, Wisconsin; daughters Barbara and Jane
  **[A: record synthesized from the NYPL finding aid]**.

## 2. Albany and the parks machine (1919-1929)

- **1919**, chief of staff, **State Reconstruction Commission**, under **Belle Moskowitz**; plan
  folding roughly **187** agencies into a smaller cabinet **[A: Columbia C250]**.
- **1924**, president of the **Long Island State Park Commission** and chairman of the **State Council
  of Parks**, roughly forty years in both **[A: Columbia C250]**. The appropriation power in the
  enabling statute (enter, post notice, let the owner sue afterwards) is a Caro reading of a public
  statute: statute [A], emphasis [R].
- **17 Jan 1927**, Secretary of State of New York; **1 Jan 1929**, out when **Franklin D. Roosevelt**
  declines to reappoint him, beginning a lasting rivalry **[A: NYPL finding aid]**.
- **1929**, **Moreland Act commissioner** under Acting Governor Herbert Lehman, investigating the
  failed City Trust Company **[R: Commercial & Financial Chronicle, 29 June 1929, via FRASER]**. Not in
  the file: single-source, no location beyond "New York City".
- **4 Aug 1929**, **Jones Beach State Park** opens **[A: NPS; NY State Parks]**.
- **THE LOW BRIDGES.** Caro's account that the parkway overpasses were built low to exclude buses, and
  thus poor and Black New Yorkers, rests on testimony from aide **Sidney Shapiro**; later scholars treat
  it as unproven and **no written order has been located** **[R: Caro 1974; contested per Wikipedia]**.
  The clearances are not disputed; the motive is.
- **THE ESTATES.** The Northern State Parkway realignment off Otto Kahn's golf course and the
  north-shore holdings onto smaller farms is Caro's; the **personal-bribe** reading is disputed by later
  scholars, who contest the payment and not the route. **[R]**

## 3. Commissioner of everything (1934-1950)

- **1934**, first unified **NYC Parks Commissioner** after La Guardia consolidates the five borough
  departments **[A: Columbia C250]**. Exact day **not established**.
- **6 Nov 1934**, Republican nominee for governor against **Herbert H. Lehman**; loses by roughly
  800,000 votes, about **36.7% to 58%**, his only run for office **[A: contemporary election
  reporting]**. The Reswick interview in *The Day*, fetched directly, yields four verbatim quotations
  used in the file **[A]**.
- **1934**, chairman of the reshaped **Triborough Bridge Authority**; **11 July 1936** the bridge opens,
  PWA participation reported at **$44.2 million** **[A: NYPL; 1936 press]**.
- **Summer 1936**, **eleven** monumental public pools open in one season with WPA and PWA labour
  **[A: NYC Parks, nycgovparks.org/about/history/pools]**. The file pins the group at the Astoria Play
  Center; that pool's exact opening day is **not established**.
- **17 July 1939**, the **War Department rejects the Brooklyn-Battery Bridge** on navigation and defence
  grounds after opposition from **George McAneny** and **Albert Bard**; a tunnel is built and the
  campaign against Castle Clinton runs to about 1950 **[A: nypap.org]**.
- **1939-40 World's Fair**: Moses's department prepares site and roads, but **the fair corporation was
  led by Grover Whalen, not Moses**, a distinction routinely blurred in secondary accounts and kept here
  **[A: NYPL fair finding aid; NYC Municipal Archives]**.
- **Exported method**: Pittsburgh arterial plan 1939 **[R: Pittsburgh City Archives]**; *Portland
  Improvement* 1943 **[A: pdxscholar.library.pdx.edu/oscdl_bonner/207]**; **São Paulo** for Nelson
  Rockefeller's IBEC 1950 **[R: Rockefeller Archive Center]**. Portland and São Paulo are in the file,
  Pittsburgh cut for length; the extent of his presence in Brazil is a **gap**.
- **Cut for length, not doubt** **[A: NYPL; NYC Parks]**: Henry Hudson Bridge (Dec 1936), Marine
  Parkway-Gil Hodges Bridge (July 1937), Bronx-Whitestone Bridge (Apr 1939, Ammann's organization).

**CONTRADICTION, the 1942/1946 transposition.** The NYPL finding aid prints "NYC Construction
Coordinator (1942-1960)" and "NYC Planning Commissioner (1946-1960)"; Wikipedia's infobox and standard
accounts reverse them, the coordinator post having been created for Moses under Mayor **William
O'Dwyer** in 1946. The pool inherited the finding aid's pair and carried the transposition into two
items. The file follows **1942 Planning / 1946 Coordinator**; not adjudicated further, since the finding
aid is the better archive and the worse date here and no third source settling it was reached.

## 4. Title I (1946-1959)

- **1949**, chairs the **Mayor's Committee on Slum Clearance**; ultimately **35 planned and 17 completed
  projects, $65.8 million** in Title I funds **[R: Chronopoulos, *Journal of Urban History*,
  journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1538513213487149]**.
- **1946-1952, United Nations**: Moses coordinates traffic, site preparation and city-state relations
  after the Rockefellers assemble the East River site **[R: dossier lead, NOT confirmed against a UN or
  Rockefeller primary source]**. Le Corbusier on the 1947 board of design is **[A]** from his own file.
- **1948 onward, Cross Bronx Expressway**: staged construction, corridor displacement estimated at
  **40,000-60,000** **[A: NYC Mayor's Office reimagining transcript, Dec 2022]**, against Caro's focus on
  about **1,500** in East Tremont. Different measures; both reported.
- **1951-1954, Manhattantown**: conveyed to a sponsor group under **Samuel Caspert** at a written-down
  price; investigators documented delays, continued rent collection in condemned buildings, weak
  supervision. **No indictment of Moses followed** **[A: Senate Banking Committee hearings, via
  Chronopoulos]**.
- **1955-1962, Lincoln Square**: clearance of the racially mixed **San Juan Hill** district, **1,647
  families** displaced per the redevelopment record **[A: WQXR]**.
- **1956, Tavern on the Green**: clearing for a parking lot halted by organized mothers and litigation
  **[A: tclf.org]**, though the interlock pass could source the episode itself only to the dossier.
  Exact day **not established**.
- **1957, the Dodgers**: Moses refuses Title I condemnation for **Walter O'Malley's** privately financed
  domed stadium at Atlantic and Flatbush and offers Flushing Meadows; the Dodgers leave after the 1957
  season **[A: SABR, 'Walter O'Malley Was Right']**.
- **1959, Joseph Papp**: a court finds the department's attempt to charge admission for free Shakespeare
  **arbitrary and capricious**; the Delacorte follows in 1962 **[A: WNYC]**.

**CONTRADICTION, Washington Square.** The pool says the Board of Estimate closed the park to traffic in
1958; that was a **trial**, with **permanent closure in August 1959** (Wikipedia). Both are in the stop.
The plan to run Fifth Avenue through the square dates from **1952**, **Shirley Hayes** led "Save the
Square!" from that year and **Jane Jacobs** joined later, so the pool's "led at different stages by
Hayes and Jacobs" is fair and kept.

## 5. The rivers (1954-1962)

- **1954**, chairman of the **Power Authority of the State of New York**, appointed by Governor **Thomas
  E. Dewey** **[A: NYPL; Discover Niagara]**.
- **5 May 1958**, the FPC's order delineates the **1,383-acre** reservoir area on the **Tuscarora
  Reservation** **[A: Wikipedia, 'FPC v. Tuscarora Indian Nation']**. **CORRECTION**: the pool attached
  the acreage to a **28 March 1958 condemnation petition**, unsupported by the court records; Tuscarora
  filed in the D.C. Circuit **16 May 1958**, the Supreme Court argued **7 Dec 1959** and decided **7
  March 1960**. **UNVERIFIED, OMITTED**: the circulated final-impact figures of about **550 acres and 37
  homes**, and the claim that the Authority initially sought ~1,000 acres of a 4,000-acre parcel, are in
  neither the court records nor Wikipedia, and neither is in the file.
- **July 1958**, first **R. H. Saunders** unit at the **Moses-Saunders Power Dam**; roughly **6,500
  people** in ten eastern Ontario communities relocated ahead of the flooding that made Lake St.
  Lawrence, the "Lost Villages" **[A: PASNY history; 1958-59 press; Wikipedia]**.
- **1961**, first power from the **Robert Moses Niagara Power Plant**, built after the **Schoellkopf**
  collapse of 7 June 1956 **[A: Discover Niagara]**. Precise day **not established**. **1962**, resigns
  the PASNY chairmanship under **Nelson Rockefeller's** consolidation of state boards **[A:
  institutional record, consistent with the NYPL finding aid]**.

## 6. The fair, the merger, the book (1959-1975)

- **1959-1964, Verrazzano-Narrows**: Brooklyn approaches displace working-class Bay Ridge blocks;
  **Othmar Ammann's** organization designs the span **[A: NYPL; standard bridge histories]**.
  Displacement counts circulate in secondary accounts, were not in the verified pool, and are **not** in
  the file.
- **1960**, Wagner's ethics reorganization requires Moses to give up the Parks Department, his Planning
  Commission seat and the slum-clearance chairmanship on taking the World's Fair presidency
  **[A: NYPL]**. Exact day **not established**.
- **1962, Fire Island**: Moses walks out of a hearing after being compared to Hitler; the road is
  foreclosed by the **Fire Island National Seashore** (1964), after a campaign by the Fire Island Voters
  Association with **Irving Like** and Interior Secretary **Stewart Udall** **[A: nps.gov/fiis]**.
- **22 April 1964**, the fair opens under Moses's presidency for two seasons, the **BIE** having refused
  recognition over scheduling rules **[A: NYC Municipal Archives]**. **CORRECTION**: the pool's "144
  attractions from 80 countries" is garbled. Standard figures: **139 pavilions and 34 concessions**,
  exhibits from **66 nations** (80 counting regional representations) plus **24 US states**, official
  exhibits from only **six** major nations (Egypt, India, Indonesia, Japan, Mexico, Pakistan);
  attendance **51.6 million** against a projected **70 million**, deficit reported up to **$40 million**.
- **1 March 1968**, the **TBTA** is merged into the new **MTA**, ending Moses's control of the toll
  surplus after he resisted its diversion to transit **[A: NYPL]**, which also **corrects secondary
  sources misdating the end of his TBTA chairmanship to 1981**.
- **16 Sept 1974**, Caro's *The Power Broker* published by Knopf, cut by **Robert Gottlieb** from a
  manuscript of roughly **1,050,000 words** **[A: Wikipedia]**. **CONTRADICTION, page count**: the pool
  gives **1,246 pages**, the Wikipedia article it cites gives **1,336**; neither is in the file, which
  says "twelve hundred pages" as an order of magnitude. **CONTRADICTION, the pamphlet**: the pool gives
  **26 Aug 1974** and **23 pages** for *Comment on a New Yorker Profile and Biography*, while the
  Internet Archive record it cites gives **48 p., photocopy of typescript, no publisher stated, 1974**,
  supporting **the year only**. The file uses 48 pages and keeps 26 August as an ordering date, the
  pamphlet answering the **July** New Yorker profile and so plausibly preceding the September book.
- **1975**, Pulitzer Prize in Biography **[A: pulitzer.org]** and the Francis Parkman Prize **[A]**.
  **CORRECTION**: the pool's "Modern Library... in 2001" is wrong; the list was created 1998 and released
  1999, and Wikipedia is internally inconsistent (2001 in one article, 2002 in another). **Dropped.**

## 7. Death and afterlife

- **29 July 1981**, dies of heart failure at **Good Samaritan Hospital, West Islip**, aged 92; Episcopal
  funeral at **St. Peter's-by-the-Sea**, Bay Shore; interred in a crypt in an outdoor community mausoleum
  at **Woodlawn Cemetery**, the Bronx **[A: Washington Post obituary, 30 July 1981; UPI, 31 July 1981;
  Wikipedia]**.
- **28 March 1982**, **Robert Moses Playground** dedicated on First Avenue between 41st and 42nd Streets,
  the resolution introduced August 1981, Koch signing 27 Jan 1982, roller-skate parade from 20 East 46th
  Street **[A: Wikipedia]**. **2008**, Triborough renamed the **Robert F. Kennedy Bridge** **[A]**.
  **2016**, Robert Moses State Parkway renamed the **Niagara Scenic Parkway** **[A: Wikipedia]**.
- **Cut for length, recorded here**: the **statue** beside Village Hall in Babylon Village
  **[A: Wikipedia]**, with **no erection date found** (gap); Harrison's 1938 *Fortune* map "The Land of
  Moses" and the 2014 Christin and Balez graphic biography **[A: Internet Archive records]**.
- **Historiography**: 1990s-2007 revisionists (Wallock, Doig, Jackson, Ballon, Lopate) recovered the
  design achievements and national policy context; a later wave of environmental and civil-rights history
  re-emphasized displacement and residents' voices **[R: dossier synthesis. A fetch of a Columbia GSAPP
  page for the 2007 "Robert Moses and the Modern City" exhibition returned an unrelated 2015 exhibition,
  so the 2007 hinge is unconfirmed.]**

## 8. Family, quotes, and what could not be settled

- **Paul Moses.** Bella changed her will against Robert's older brother, an engineer, who alleged
  Robert turned their mother against him and blocked his access to public engineering work. **No court
  finding of undue influence. [R: Caro 1974, interviews with Paul]** Not in the file.
- **"If the end doesn't justify the means, what does?"** Attributed via profiles, Caro's excerpt title
  (29 July 1974) and Moses's memoir. **No authenticated first utterance located. [R]** Used with the
  attribution in `quote_source`.
- **"I raise my stein to the builder who can remove ghettos without moving people..."** Genuinely in
  the 1974 rebuttal, but **two fetches of the archive.org transcription read "salute" and "saluto"**
  against "stein" in the dossier and Wikiquote; nytimes.com unfetchable. **[R on wording]. Not used.**
- **"Those who can, build. Those who can't, criticize."** Pool attributed it to the 1981 NYT obituary;
  **verified verbatim in Caro**, World's Fair section, which the file cites. **[R] upgraded to [A].**
- **"...hack your way with a meat ax."** Misquoted twice: Caro ch. 36 reads "any kind of picture you
  **like**" and "Canberra **or** Brasilia", and his source note reads '"Meat ax": Dangerous Trade',
  i.e. Moses's *Public Works: A Dangerous Trade* (1970).
- **"Critics are ex post facto prophets..."** Truncated mid-sentence in the pool. Full: "...at a time
  when they were in diapers, in rompers or Invisible." The capital-I "Invisible" is reproduced verbatim
  from the Internet Archive text; typescript or OCR artefact **undetermined**.
- **He never learned to drive.** Sourced only to the dossier crawl. **[R, worth a re-fetch against the
  1939 Atlantic profile]. Not used.** Same for the **Colonie Hill Health Club, Hauppauge**, where he
  swam in retirement: **[R, address unconfirmed]**, so the swimming is in the death stop and the club
  is not a stop.

## Interlocks

**Named in campas**, each a relation of Moses's own rather than shared geography.
`frederick_law_olmsted` and `calvert_vaux`: he administered Central Park from 1934 and rebuilt inside
their 1858 Greensward design (zoo, Tavern on the Green, playgrounds at entrances Olmsted designed to
have none), and in 1956 tried to clear their landscape for a parking lot. `le_corbusier`: the same
project rather than the same place, Le Corbusier on the 1947 UN board of design while Moses handled the
city side, Moses's half **[R]**. `frank_lloyd_wright`: direct and mutual, Wright calling him a night
crawler and Moses answering by name in the 1974 pamphlet, which the quote carries.

**Pins inherited byte-identical from the interlock pass**: Oxford (51.7548, -1.2544); Albany Executive
Chamber (42.6526, -73.7562), for the 1929 Roosevelt stop, the 1934 election and the 1954 PASNY
appointment; Tavern on the Green (40.7739, -73.9784); the UN site (40.7489, -73.968).

**Declined, kept here rather than deleted.** `nikola_tesla`, offered on the Niagara gorge pin (Adams
station, 1895-96): Moses never engaged with Tesla or claimed his lineage, so this is shared geography
and a shared river, which the map already shows. `charles_lucky_luciano` and `john_foster_dulles`,
reaching Moses only through **Herbert Lehman** (Moreland 1929, the 1934 election; Dewey as special
prosecutor 1935; the 1949 Senate special) and **Thomas E. Dewey** (PASNY 1954; Dulles to the Senate
1949): real hinges, but a third-party hop. `john_d_rockefeller_sr`: the Rockefeller here is **Nelson**
(IBEC 1950, the 1962 PASNY squeeze, the 1968 MTA merger) and **no Nelson file exists**. **Franklin D.
Roosevelt**: the rivalry is direct and load-bearing, and **no FDR file exists**, the corpus's largest
missing counterpart for Moses. **Buenos Aires / Argentina**: checked per standing instruction, **no
link found**, recorded so nobody re-searches.

**Other people's stops, not staged here.** The New-York Historical Society exhibition **"Turn Every
Page"** is Caro's archive and Caro's institution, and the pool dated it wrongly besides: it opened
**22 October 2021** as the first permanent public exhibition of a living author's archive, **not** a
2024 fiftieth-anniversary show, and nyhistory.org returned no content, so the 2024 framing has no
source at all. Cited instead on Moses's own pamphlet stop, as are the 99% Invisible read-along of
18 January 2024 **[R]**, the graphic biography, the *Fortune* map and the 2024 e-book edition.

## Apparatus relations for the operator

**None found, in either direction.** Moses is not a theorist and has no framework that could be applied
backward to material he never treated, so the `EXCEPTIONS.md` question does not arise and nothing was
dropped on that ground. The relations declined above are third-party hops and shared geography, not
apparatus, and are listed rather than deleted so the operator can overrule. The one needing a ruling is
the **Tesla/Niagara** pin: if industrial-successor relations count, that is a **backward** reach from
