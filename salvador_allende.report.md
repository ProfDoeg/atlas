# Salvador Allende Gossens (1908-1973): research report
*2026-08-23. Prepared for `salvador_allende.journey.json`: 9 segments, 44 stops. Chile;
Tacna under Chilean administration; New York; Moscow.*

Legend: **[A]** = attested, source named. **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition, or a dossier
claim no source reached in this pass confirms. Contradictions are flagged where they stand,
not adjudicated. Where the journey file hedges, the hedge lives in that stop's
`date_confidence`; this report is where the reasoning is written out.

---

## 1. Birth and the peripatetic childhood, 1908-1925

Born **26 June 1908 [A: en.wikipedia; bcn.cl reseña biográfica; es.wikipedia]**, son of the
lawyer **Salvador Allende Castro** and **Laura Gossens Uribe [A]**. Grandfather **Ramón
Allende Padín** is the origin of the family's Masonic tradition **[A: en.wiki]**.

**Birthplace: not a genuine 50/50 contradiction.** Three sources of primary grade give
Santiago: en.wikipedia, the Congress library's entry, and es.wikipedia, which names the
house ("en una casa ubicada en avenida España 615") **[A]**. The Valparaíso version, which
put the dossier's birth pin roughly 100 km wrong, is a secondary tradition attached to the
family's home city, with no source of comparable standing **[R]**; a minority give 26 July
**[R]**. Stop at -33.4525 / -70.6694 (Nominatim); the campa keeps Valparaíso as a claim.

**Tacna, c. 1909-1916 [A: en.wiki]**, following the father's postings. **Iquique from
c. 1916 [A: en.wiki]**; that the nitrate zone founded his politics is his own retrospective
account, flagged by the dossier as partly overstated **[R]**, and the company-token economy
is period context **[R]**. **Instituto Nacional, Santiago, 1918 [A]**; **Liceo de Valdivia,
1919 [A]**; **Liceo Eduardo de la Barra, Valparaíso, from 1922 [A: en.wiki; bcn.cl]**, where
the anarchist shoemaker **Juan De Marchi** was his first political teacher **[A]**, the
single-conversation origin story about that friendship being shorthand **[R]**. Coordinates
corrected (Nominatim): Instituto Nacional to -33.4454 / -70.6507 (1 km west in the dossier),
Liceo Eduardo de la Barra to -33.0501 / -71.6144 (840 m off).

### Contradiction 1: the cavalry year

| source | regiment | city | year |
|---|---|---|---|
| en.wikipedia | Cuirassier Regiment | Tacna | 1925 |
| es.wikipedia | Regimiento de Lanceros | Tacna | done by 1924 |
| bcn.cl (official) | Regimiento Coraceros | Viña del Mar | 1926 |

The dossier contradicted itself here too (chronology Tacna, geography Viña). About a year of
voluntary cavalry service before university is **[A]**; every particular is **[R]**. The stop
is kept, placed at Viña del Mar on the Congress biography's authority, dated 1925-01-01 as
an ordering device, the disagreement in `date_confidence`. Its coordinate is the city centre
and explicitly not a located barracks: the dossier's -33.0153 / -71.55 sat 1.2 km north of
the centre and named no building.

## 2. Medicine, expulsion, the morgue, the party, 1926-1936

**Medical school, Universidad de Chile, from 1926 [A: en.wiki]**, with student leadership, a
Marxist study group, agitation against Ibáñez, and arrest **[A]**. **President of the
medical students' centre, 1927 [A]**. **Vice-president of the FECh, 1929 [R: en.wiki, no
document]**.

**Contradiction 2: the 1931 relegation has no named destination.** The dossier attached
"Valparaíso / Caldera" and the 1932 Socialist Republic to it. en.wikipedia says only: "In
1931, he was expelled from the university and relegated to the north," and attaches Caldera
separately to 1935; the dossier's version is reconstruction **[R]**. The file gives 1931 no
stop of its own, and invents no pin for a place the sources decline to name. An honest gap.

**Contradiction 3: the graduation year.** The dossier's own sentence ("graduating ... at age
23 ... 1933") cannot hold for a man born June 1908. en.wikipedia has him retake his sixth
year and graduate at 23; bcn.cl gives the title in 1932; all three sources date the thesis
*Higiene mental y delincuencia* to 1933 **[A]**. The file asserts no graduation year.

**Van Buren morgue, Valparaíso, from 1932 [A: en.wiki, "he began practicing as a physician
and anatomo-pathologist in the morgue of the Van Buren Hospital"; bcn.cl for Asistencia
Pública 1932-1936]**. Coordinate corrected to -33.0514 / -71.6108 (Nominatim, San Ignacio
725), 540 m off in the dossier. **Valparaíso branch of the Partido Socialista de Chile,
1933 [A: en.wiki; bcn.cl]**, with Grove, Matte and Schnake nationally **[A]**.

**Contradiction 4: Freemasonry, 1928 or 1935.** The lodge is agreed (Progreso No. 4,
Valparaíso) **[A]**; the year is not, en.wikipedia giving 1928 and es.wikipedia 1935. A
seven-year gap between two language editions of the same encyclopedia is not an [A]-grade
date, and no Gran Logia de Chile record was reachable. Stop dated 1928-01-01, both years
named. **Unresolved.**

**Contradiction 5: Caldera or Calama, 1935.** en.wikipedia gives Caldera on the Atacama
coast, "for the second time"; es.wikipedia gives Calama in the Loa desert, six months, under
the second government of **Arturo Alessandri** (the dossier wrongly implied an Ibáñez-era
successor). The towns are about 500 km apart. Placed at Caldera. **Unresolved; the pin is at
risk.**

Dropped for thinness: the Puerto Montt hospital practice (bcn.cl only, undated, no second
source).

## 3. Deputy, minister, author, senator, 1937-1948

**Deputy, Sixth District, Valparaíso and Quillota, 1937-1941 [A: bcn.cl, explicit]**. The
dossier's "Valparaíso-Aconcagua" variant is a conflation with a seat 24 years later (bcn.cl:
"Senator 1961-1969, Third Region: Aconcagua and Valparaíso") and is dropped. Polling day
unrecorded, defaulted to 1937-03-01 **[gap]**.

**Minister of Health, Welfare and Social Assistance from 28 September 1939 [A: bcn.cl;
en.wiki]** under **Pedro Aguirre Cerda**. es.wikipedia gives a divergent 28 August **[R]**,
and the end date is contested (bcn.cl 7 April 1942, both Wikipedias 2 April) **[flagged]**,
so the file gives the start only. The ministry coordinate (-33.446 / -70.658) is an
unverified downtown placement. **La realidad médico-social chilena, 1939 [A: en.wiki;
bcn.cl]**: no verbatim passage was reachable, so that stop carries no quote **[gap]**.

**Contradiction 6: the marriage day, and how they met.** **Hortensia Bussi Soto, 1940 [A:
bcn.cl, year only]**. es.wikipedia's article on her gives **17 March 1940**, its article on
him **16 September 1940**. Unresolved; the file keeps 17 March and names the conflict. The
dossier's "met after the 1939 Chillán earthquake" is wrong: es.wikipedia (Hortensia Bussi)
has them meeting in Santiago **on the night of** 24 January 1939, during it **[A]**.

**Senator from 15 May 1945 [A: bcn.cl]**, for Valdivia, Llanquihue, Chiloé, Aisén and
Magallanes. **Opposition to the 1948 Ley de Defensa Permanente de la Democracia [R: en.wiki,
no roll call reached]**; Pisagua is period context **[R]**.

## 5. The fifty days, September to November 1970

**René Schneider shot 22 October 1970, died 25 October [A: en.wiki, 'Salvador Allende' and
'René Schneider']**, resisting a kidnapping linked to Track II / Project FUBELT **[A]**. The
intersection is unnamed in every source reached; the pin (-33.42 / -70.575) is approximate
**[gap]**. The cable of 16 October 1970 ("It is firm and continuing policy that Allende be
overthrown by a coup"), conveyed by deputy director Thomas Karamessines, is a **declassified
United States document [A: National Security Archive EBB No. 8]**, quoted at that stop as
Washington on Allende's government, not Allende's words.

**Congress confirms, 24 October 1970, 153 to 35 with seven blanks [A: es.wikipedia,
'Elección presidencial de Chile de 1970'; bcn.cl]**, after the **Statute of Constitutional
Guarantees [A]**. **Inaugurated 3 November 1970 [A: bcn.cl; en.wiki]**. The formula calling
him the first self-identified Marxist elected head of state in a competitive liberal
democracy is imprecise, no fixed global definition governing it: kept in `date_confidence`,
out of the campa. **Tomás Moro residence, Las Condes [A: en.wiki; es.wikipedia for the air
attack on it]**, corrected to -33.4107 / -70.5516 (Nominatim, Avenida Tomás Moro 200), 2 km
off in the dossier.

## 6. The thousand days, 1971-1972

**Correction 7: three famous quotations were filed under the wrong speech.** The Marxists
Internet Archive page at `.../allende/1970/september/20.htm` is titled as a speech of 20
September 1970. That is a cataloguing artifact: the text self-dates to the **First Annual
Message to the National Congress, 21 May 1971** ("In 6 months of Government"; copper to come
under Chilean ownership later that year; a forward reference to the April 1972 UNCTAD
conference), and Allende was not president in September 1970. So "Today Chile is the first
nation on earth to put into practice the second model of transition to a socialist society"
and "The Chilean people are achieving political power without having used arms..." belong to
May 1971 **[A, corrected]**. A third, "We are moving towards socialism, not from an academic
love for a doctrinaire system...", tagged **[R]** by the dossier as unverifiable beyond a
Wikipedia footnote, is in that same text and is **retagged [A]**.

- **Copper: Law 17,450 promulgated 11 July 1971 as the Day of National Dignity [A]**,
  unanimous in Congress, covering Anaconda and Kennecott at Chuquicamata, El Salvador and El
  Teniente. The venue within Rancagua (plaza or stadium) is unconfirmed **[gap]**.
- **Castro's visit, 10 November to 4 December 1971 [A: es.wikipedia, 'Visita de Fidel Castro
  a Chile']**. Correction: the farewell speech on the severity of the class conflict was
  given at the **Estadio Nacional on 2 December**, not at La Moneda; the stop is the hosting
  locus only, with the stadium in `suggested_refs`.
- **October 1972 truck owners' strike [A: es.wikipedia, 'Gobierno de Salvador Allende',
  start in the early hours of 9 October]**. The "24-day" figure is dropped: 24 to 26
  depending on the endpoint **[flagged]**. Cybersyn's telex use during it is **[A:
  en.wikipedia, quoting Gustavo Silva on moving supplies with about 200 trucks]**. **UN
  General Assembly, 4 December 1972 [A: MIA text]**.
- **Moscow, December 1972 [R]**: visit and disappointing outcome rest on the dossier and
  general consensus, no primary record reached, Kremlin venue unconfirmed. Kept as a stop,
  tagged as reconstruction.

**Correction 8: the dossier put the Opsroom on the wrong side of the river.** es.wikipedia's
'Proyecto Synco' places it on **Avenida Santa María, near the Philips offices**, that is
Providencia, north bank of the Mapocho, about -33.4308 / -70.6260 by Nominatim; the
dossier's -33.445 / -70.655 is 2 km southwest in the civic centre. The **30 December 1972
visit date is unsupported by anything reached** and is flagged; Beer's account of Allende at
the viable-system diagram is Cybersyn scholarship (Eden Medina), not re-fetched **[R]**.

## 7. The last year and the eleventh of September, 1973

**Parliamentary elections, 4 March 1973 [A: es.wikipedia: UP 44.03 per cent and 63 deputies,
CODE 55.70 and 87]**. The dossier's 43.4 sits at the low end of a 43.4-44.0 range varying
with which parties are counted **[flagged]**; the campa says "about forty-four per cent."
**Tanquetazo, 29 June 1973, Roberto Souper, suppressed with Carlos Prats [A]**; the tank
route was not confirmed street by street, so the pin is approximate **[gap]**. **Prats
resigns, Pinochet appointed, 23 August 1973 [A]**.

**Correction 9: the Chamber of Deputies tally is unsourced.** The **date (22 August 1973)**
and the phrase **"grave quebrantamiento del orden constitucional y legal"** are confirmed
**[A: es.wikipedia, 'Golpe de Estado en Chile de 1973'; text on Wikisource, officially
noticed 23 August]**. The widely repeated **81-47** count could not be confirmed by any page
reached: a targeted search returned four articles citing the Acuerdo and none giving a
tally. **The numbers are omitted from the file**, and the omission is stated.

**11 September 1973.** Sequence corrected against **en.wikipedia, 'Death of Salvador
Allende'**: at about **1:50 p.m. he ordered the defenders to surrender**, shook hands along a
line of supporters, then walked to the Salón Independencia; **the shot follows and is not at
1:50**, where the dossier put the death. **Guijón's account** and the body's arrival at the
**Hospital Militar about 17:30** are verbatim **[A]**. The rest of the clock, from
es.wikipedia's coup article **[A]**: fleet off Valparaíso around dawn; Radio Magallanes 10:15
a.m.; tanks and artillery 10:30; Hawker Hunters about 11:30 to 12:08; infantry breach about
2:30 p.m. The Bolivian blanket is public record **[R]**. **Clandestine burial, 12 September
1973, in the Grove family tomb, Cementerio de Santa Inés, Viña del Mar [A: es.wikipedia,
'Fundación Salvador Allende']**, upgrading a claim the researcher could not confirm;
coordinate corrected to -33.0062 / -71.5329 (Nominatim), 600 m off in the dossier.

**Correction 10: the Nixon line, traced to the primary document.** Carried as **[R]** from a
Wikipedia summary, it is now read directly: **National Security Archive EBB No. 437,
Document 7, "Telcon, Kissinger - Nixon, September 16, 1973, 11:50 a.m."**, a scan with no OCR
layer, read as page images **[A]**. The wording differs from the dossier's: "Well we didn't -
as you know - our hand doesn't show on this one though." It is a telephone call, not a
meeting. The file quotes the scan, trailing "though" included.

## 8. Afterlife

**State funeral and reburial, 4 September 1990, Mausoleo Salvador Allende, Cementerio
General de Santiago [A: es.wikipedia, 'Fundación Salvador Allende']**, the date chosen
because it was the traditional presidential polling day **[A]**. Another dossier claim
upgraded from unconfirmed. **Hortensia Bussi's return from exile in 1988 precedes it [A:
es.wikipedia, 'Hortensia Bussi']**. The mausoleum sits nearer the southern entrance, around
-33.419 / -70.649, flagged as approximate, not a verified plot **[gap]**.

**2011: court-ordered exhumation and international forensic panel [A: en.wikipedia, 'Death
of Salvador Allende']**, concluding self-inflicted gunshot wounds from a rifle set to
automatic fire, the rifle carrying **a dedication from Fidel Castro [A]**. **Sequencing
correction**: the court order came in **May 2011** and so **preceded** the 31 May television
report of a 300-page secret military account and the two experts who initially suggested
assassination; the dossier had it the other way round. The exhumation day is unconfirmed
**[gap]**, so the stop is dated to the July finding. The family never disputed it **[A]**.

**Attested, not made stops**, belonging to other people or to institutions: the **Plaza de
la Constitución statue** (Hevia, unveiled 26 June 2000 by Ricardo Lagos, criticised by
Hermes H. Benítez as a Concertación-era image of a "passive, defenseless being") **[A]**,
cited in the 1990 stop; **Castro's Havana speech of 28 September 1973** that Allende died
"wrapped in a Chilean flag, firing at Pinochet troops" **[R]**, seed of the heroic-combat
legend and of Robinson Rojas's 1975 book, and Castro's stop on Castro's file; **Espacio
Fundación Salvador Allende**, Av. República 475 (-33.4483 / -70.6684), which **visited**
rather than hosted the April 2026 "Con el alma llena de banderas" showing, as the dossier
had it **[A]**; **"50 discursos"**, where **2023 is the funding call and the site launched
in 2024 [A, corrected]**; and the **"Salvador Allende Reader", first published 2000 [A: Open
Library]**, upgraded from **[R]**, its Ocean Press imprint unconfirmed **[gap]**.

## 9. Interlocks used, and interlocks refused

Named in a campa, because Allende himself stood in a direct relation: **augusto_pinochet**
(he appointed him commander-in-chief on 23 August 1973, nineteen days before Pinochet's coup
killed him); **fidel_castro** (hosted for twenty-four days in 1971; died with the rifle
Castro gave him); **serrano**, Miguel Serrano (stripped of his ambassadorship and dismissed
from state service on Allende's election, per `serrano.journey.json`: one-directional, but
Allende's government is the actor).

Refused as shared geography, shared date, or a later person's engagement, all of which the
map already shows: **che_guevara** (Santiago and Valparaíso, March 1952, noting the election
in his diary; the famous Che inscription to Allende is not in the verified pool, so this
becomes a real interlock only if sourced later); **cecilia_vicuna** (her London exile is her
stop); **william_colby** (his Senate accounting is his stop; the operations reach this file
through the 16 October 1970 cable); and the six listed in section 10. **Milton Friedman**
and **Stafford Beer** are queued and carry no slug; Beer is a genuine direct relation, named
in the Opsroom campa as a person.
