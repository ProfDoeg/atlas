# Augusto Pinochet Ugarte (1915-2006), research report

*2026-08-03. Atlas of Journeys, slug `augusto_pinochet`. Companion file: `augusto_pinochet.journey.json`, 45 stops in 9 segments, register "national mythology: the canon is true", calendar gregorian (the whole life falls after 1582).*

**Legend.** **[A]** = attested, source named. **[R]** = reconstruction, or a claim carried only by a compilation with no primary behind it. Where two reachable sources disagree, the disagreement is printed and left standing.

**A note on shape.** The verified pool arrived carrying an unusual number of corrections logged against itself: coordinate errors through most of the geography lens, month-level date errors, a wrong actor, a wrong attribution of a massacre, and a quote lens in which nearly every entry had a back-translation standing in for the attested Chilean wording. Each is applied in the journey file and itemized below.

## 1. Valparaíso and the third application (1915-1937)

**[A: English Wikipedia, "Augusto Pinochet"]** Born 25 November 1915 in Valparaíso, eldest of six children of a customs employee. Exact house unidentified; the pin is the city centroid. Schooling at the **Rafael Ariztía Institute, Quillota** (-32.8823 / -71.2495) and, **[R, coordinate explicitly approximate in the pool]**, the San Rafael Seminary in Valparaíso, dropped from the journey for that reason. Two failed applications to the Escuela Militar, admitted on the third **[A: Spanish Wikipedia]**. September 1937: Chacabuco Regiment, Concepción **[A]**, city centroid, barracks address unverified; the Lota and Coronel coalfields are context supplied here, **[R]**.

**Contradiction, flagged, not resolved.** Entry to the Escuela Militar: **11 March 1933** (Spanish Wikipedia) against **1931** (English Wikipedia). Graduation: **29 December 1936**, alférez 1 January 1937, against **1935**. The journey uses the Chilean dates because 1933 / December 1936 / September 1937 form a coherent chain and the English figures fit neither end of it. That is an argument from internal consistency, not a document; it settles nothing, and the conflict is printed in the stop's `date_confidence`.

**Coordinate corrected.** The pre-1955 school was the Balmaceda-era barracks on Blanco Encalada near Parque O'Higgins, about **-33.455 / -70.667** **[A: Spanish Wikipedia]**. The pool's -33.4515 / -70.6845 is 1.5 km west; the Las Condes campus is later still.

## 4. The Caravan of Death, September-October 1973

**[A: Wikipedia, "Caravan of Death"]** Sergio Arellano Stark, army brigadier general, appointed by Pinochet as **Official Delegate of the Commander-in-Chief**, travels south to north by Puma helicopter from Cauquenes to Calama, taking prisoners out of military jails and executing them.

**Date corrected at the month level.** The Caravan runs **30 September to 22 October 1973**; the pool's "1973-10" loses the September start. **Toll contested and left contested**: at least 75 (BBC, army custody figures) against 97 (Memoria y Justicia: 26 south, 71 north). The pool declines to resolve it, and so does the journey.

**Description corrected.** 19 October: **General Joaquín Lagos** counts 56 executed in his zone and protests to Pinochet, checking the Caravan's expansion **[A]**. He was not an "intendant" as the pool said; he commanded the army's **1st Division** and was zone chief at Antofagasta.

**Attribution withdrawn.** The pool credited the October 1973 executions at **Cerro Chena** to Arellano's column, which travelled by helicopter northward from Cauquenes and did not operate at San Bernardo; those killings were the work of the **San Bernardo Infantry School garrison**. Coordinate corrected to **-33.5944 / -70.7431** from -33.63 / -70.7. The journey keeps the stop and reframes it, which makes the sharper point: the Caravan demonstrated a method already in general use.

## 6. Constitution to handover (1980-1990)

## 9. Quotations: what was found and what was thrown out

**English Wikiquote is not used as a source for any quotation in the journey file.** Every pool entry citing it carried a Spanish wording that turned out to be a back-translation from the English, differing from the attested Chilean text in verb, word order, or inserted words. The corrections applied:

Corrections applied, the source of record in each case being La Nación 11 dic 2006, El Mercurio same date, the TVN / 24 Horas archive, Revista Qué Pasa, Las Últimas Noticias or Agencia DPA:

- *11 sep 1973*: the pool dropped the whole Allende clause and wrote *salvar* for *sacar*. Full wording restored. *sep 1974*: *tenemos* corrected to *disponemos de*, bare "1974" refined to September.
- *17 jun 1975*: the pool's three-sentence version, including *El que me suceda también moriría*, is unattested; short form used. *2 sep 1983*: *Esto no es una dictadura sino una dictablanda* is a paraphrase found in no source, and the full attested sentence, ending *vamos a tener que apretar la mano*, is used instead.
- *24 abr 1986*: *cara de amargado* corrected to *la cara agria*, sentence unsplit, the pool's "outlet not located" resolved to Revista Qué Pasa. *nov 1998*: *sólo* returned to its place, one sentence not two.
- *11 sep 1994*: third clause *¿A los que trataron de liquidar a la Patria?* restored; the cited author is **Sandrine** Lefranc, not "Marie-Ève"; date recovered from "unknown". *25 nov 2006*: the pool inserted *plena* and replaced *de todo lo obrado* with the softer *de todo lo que pasó*, and the attested phrase is restored.
- *oct 1981*, the hoja line: verb *caer* corrected to *mover*, and the surviving video variant (*Aquí no se mueve una hoja*) recorded in `quote_source` beside the newspaper wording. *21 sep 1995*: *al problema* corrected to *para el problema*.

Two further findings. The claim that Pinochet **coined** *dictablanda* is **false**: the word is Spanish, popularized in 1930 for Dámaso Berenguer's government. And *hacer de Chile una nación de propietarios y no de proletarios* is cited in Chilean scholarship to the Junta's **Declaración de Principios of March 1974**, while English Wikiquote dates it to 24 April 1987 and the pool said "mid-1975", matching neither. The journey attributes it to the 1974 Declaración, prints the conflict in `quote_source`, and notes it may not be a personal utterance at all.

Eleven of the 45 stops carry a quotation. All are at one remove.

- **No coordinate exists in the pool for the Letelier bombing or for Riggs Bank.** Both supplied from outside it and flagged. **The Los Boldos estate is not pinned**; commune centroid used, declared. **Neltume (1981) has no coordinate** and was dropped rather than guessed. **The San Rafael Seminary building is unverified**; dropped. **Barracks addresses** at Concepción, Iquique, Arica and the Santiago garrison command are unverified; centroids throughout.
- **"Geopolítica", "El día decisivo" and "Camino Recorrido" were not opened**, no critical edition circulating, so all characterizations are secondhand. **Chilean court files** (the Guzmán indictments, the 2004 Court of Appeals ruling) were not opened either; every court fact here is at one remove through encyclopaedia summaries.
