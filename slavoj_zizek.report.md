# Slavoj Žižek: research report
*Compiled 2026-08-15 for the journey file `slavoj_zizek.journey.json`. Subject living as of compilation; born Ljubljana, 21 March 1949.*

Legend: **[A]** = attested, source named · **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition, or inference from a pattern rather than a dated record. Contradictions between lenses are flagged where they stand and are **not** adjudicated. Coordinates given in this report are the ones carried into the journey file.

A note on the evidence base. Žižek is a living, prolific subject whose biography circulates through his own retellings, through profile journalism, and through the English Wikipedia article that most profiles quietly follow. The record is firm at its published end (books, films, debates, appointments) and soft at its lived end (which barracks, which apartment, which hall). Several pool entries arrived with venue-level precision the underlying sources do not carry; those are demoted below.

---

## 1. Ljubljana and the coast, 1949-1967

**Birth. [A]** Born 21 March 1949 in Ljubljana, then capital of the People's Republic of Slovenia within Yugoslavia, to a middle-class secular family: father **Jože**, an economist and civil servant; mother **Vesna**, an accountant. Source: English Wikipedia, 'Slavoj Žižek'; the same facts appear in Rex Butler, *Slavoj Žižek: Live Theory* (2005).

**Birth venue. [R, demoted]** A pool entry placed the birth at the Ljubljana University Medical Centre (46.0562 / 14.5197) while conceding in its own note that the hospital was a proxy; the coordinate does not even sit on it (UKC, Zaloška cesta 2, is nearer 46.0505 / 14.5245). The file uses the plain city coordinate 46.0569 / 14.5058 and claims nothing about a hospital. The confirmed facts are the date and the city.

**Portorož. [A for the fact, R for the dating]** Part of his childhood is spent in the Adriatic resort town of Portorož (45.5150 / 13.5896), within reach of Western film and consumer goods unusually early for socialist Yugoslavia. No year within the decade is fixed anywhere in reach; the file dates it 1955 and records the decade-only basis in `date_confidence`.

**Bežigrad. [A]** Attends Bežigrad High School after the family's return to the capital. The school's coordinate was not verified against any source in the pool, so the file re-uses the city pin rather than inventing a campus one.

## 2. The Faculty of Arts and the dismissal, 1967-1977

**Enrolment and the Derrida translation. [A]** Enrols at the University of Ljubljana in 1967 to read philosophy and sociology, and in the same year publishes the first Slovenian translation of a text by **Jacques Derrida**. Source: English Wikipedia, 'Slavoj Žižek'.

**Faculty of Arts coordinate. [A, corrected]** The pool gave Aškerčeva 2 as 46.0479 / 14.4983. The address sits nearer **46.0457 / 14.5010**, about 300 m southeast. The corrected coordinate is used.

**The 1971 dismissal. [A]** He loses an assistant researcher post at the university after his **Master's** thesis is judged 'non-Marxist' by the authorities. Source: English Wikipedia, 'Slavoj Žižek'.

**Contradiction, flagged.** A pool entry called 1971 an **undergraduate** degree with a thesis on French structuralism. That conflates two degrees: *The Theoretical and Practical Relevance of French Structuralism* is the **1981 Doctor of Arts** dissertation. The file keeps 1971 as the dismissal.

**The years outside. [A for the fact, R for the detail]** Wikipedia states a four-year span of military service and unemployment, with a garrison at **Karlovac** in Croatia (45.4871 / 15.5478). No source in reach dates the induction, the discharge, or the unit. The file dates the Karlovac stop 1974 and records the hedge.

**The translations. [A]** Through the 1970s and 1980s he edits and translates **Lacan**, **Freud**, and **Louis Althusser** into Slovene, and publishes in the alternative journals *Praxis*, *Tribuna*, and *Problemi*. Source: English Wikipedia, 'Slavoj Žižek'.

**The institutional base. [A]** Senior researcher at the **Institute for Sociology and Philosophy, University of Ljubljana**, the post he holds across his whole career.

**Contradiction, flagged.** One pool entry named the post as senior researcher at the **Institute of Philosophy of ZRC SAZU, Novi trg 2**, a different body, on a coordinate (46.0489 / 14.5065) that does not match Novi trg 2 either. Two other lenses name the University of Ljubljana institute. The file follows them and drops the Novi trg pin.

## 3. Paris and the Lacanian line, 1981-1986

**Paris VIII. [A]** He studies psychoanalysis at the **Université Paris VIII Vincennes-Saint-Denis** under **Jacques-Alain Miller**, Lacan's son-in-law, executor, and editor of the published *Seminar*. The Saint-Denis coordinate 48.9357 / 2.3610 is correct for the post-1980 campus.

**Contradiction, flagged.** The geography lens dated his Paris VIII period **1978-1985** and asserted a pre-1980 contact at the old Vincennes site. Nothing in reach supports either. His Paris study under Miller is conventionally placed in the early 1980s. The file opens the Paris period at 1981 and records the dispute in `date_confidence`.

**Second contradiction, flagged.** The **defence year of the second doctorate** is given as **1985** by the geography lens and as **1986** by both the chronology and afterlife lenses. Wikipedia gives 1986. The dissertation is titled *La philosophie entre le symptôme et le fantasme*. The file uses 1986 and names the disagreement.

**The École de la Cause freudienne. [A for the institution, R for the address]** Miller founded the ECF in 1980-81 after Lacan dissolved the École freudienne de Paris. The pool's rue Huysmans address (48.8486 / 2.3277) is the modern premises used as a proxy, honest but unverified. A canonical ECF pin already exists in `jacques_lacan.journey.json` ('Paris: la Cause freudienne', 48.8566 / 2.3522, 1980-02-21); the file inherits it byte-identically for the institutional stop and keeps rue Huysmans only for the flagged [R] seminar-circuit stop.

**Lacan himself. [A, as an absence]** Lacan dies in September 1981, at the very start of Žižek's Paris period. **No source claims the two met.** The overlap window is short and the absence is treated here as a finding, not a gap.

## 4. 1988-1991: the party card, the book, the ballot

**The resignation. [A]** A member of the League of Communists of Slovenia until **October 1988**, when he resigns over the JBTZ trial (the 'Trial of the Four'). Activist with the **Committee for the Defence of Human Rights**, 1988-1990.

**First film book. [A]** *Pogled s strani*, his first book on film theory, published in Slovene in 1988. **Contradiction, minor, flagged:** the pool glosses the title both as 'A Glance Askew' (chronology lens) and 'A Glance Sideways' (afterlife lens). Both are loose renderings of the same Slovene phrase; neither is adjudicated here.

**The Sublime Object of Ideology. [A]** Published by **Verso**, London, 1989. The Meard Street, Soho coordinate (51.5136 / -0.1345) is a historically associated Verso address, not a verified 1989 office; recorded as such.

**Contradiction, flagged.** The afterlife lens called the book 'the founding text of what is later called the Ljubljana School of psychoanalysis'. The source describes Žižek as *an associate of* the Ljubljana School, a Slovene-language circle already forming around Žižek, **Mladen Dolar**, **Renata Salecl**, and **Alenka Zupančič** through the 1980s, before and independently of this English-language book. The file recasts it as the text that carried the school's method into Anglophone theory.

**Laclau at Essex. [R]** The routing of his early English-language work toward Verso through **Ernesto Laclau** at the University of Essex (51.8813 / 0.9530) is drawn from Laclau's own interviews and from Žižek's acknowledgments, not from a dated record. Kept as [R].

**The presidential run. [A, corrected]** In Slovenia's first multi-party elections he stands for the **Liberal Democratic Party** for a seat on the four-member collective Presidency of the Republic of Slovenia, first round **8 April 1990**. He finishes **fifth of twelve candidates with 9.68%** and is not elected.

**Contradiction, flagged and corrected.** A pool entry said 'one of four candidates', placed him 'in the middle of the field', and staged the campaign at **Cankarjev dom** while conceding the venue was unconfirmed, all tagged [A]. Twelve candidates ran for four seats; he came fifth; the venue is a reconstruction. The file drops the Cankarjev dom staging and uses the city coordinate.

**Looking Awry** (MIT Press, 1991), the Lacan-through-Hitchcock book that fixes his method. **[A]**

**Visiting posts. [A in substance, R in dating]** Michigan (42.2780 / -83.7382), Minnesota, the New School (40.7358 / -73.9958), NYU (40.7295 / -73.9965), the European Graduate School at Saas-Fee (46.1086 / 7.9297), the Institute for Human Sciences in Vienna (48.2308 / 16.3617). **Abercrombie & Fitch. [A]** In 2003 he writes an essay accompanying **Bruce Weber**'s photographs for an Abercrombie & Fitch quarterly catalog.

The file uses a Berlin coordinate for a German production and states the disagreement in `date_confidence`; a viewer should treat the pin as country-level at best.

## 7. The squares, 2011-2016

**Zuccotti Park, 9 October 2011. [R, well documented]** Addresses the Occupy Wall Street encampment; the speech is transcribed and widely reprinted (Verso, n+1). Tagged [R] only because the pool did so.

**Ljubljana, 2012-2013. [R]** Named among prominent public supporters of the Slovenian anti-austerity and anti-corruption protests centred on **Trg republike** (46.0505 / 14.5028). **Gap:** no dated appearance and no venue confirmed. Left [R].

**Athens, 2015. [R]** Public support for the Greek left during the referendum crisis, alongside **Yanis Varoufakis**. **Gap:** venue unconfirmed; central Athens coordinate is a placeholder.

**DiEM25, February 2016. [A, upgraded]** Joins the Democracy in Europe Movement 2025, co-founded by Varoufakis. A pool entry carried a Volksbühne stop tagged [R] for want of any dated appearance; DiEM25 was **launched at the Volksbühne am Rosa-Luxemburg-Platz in February 2016**, which supplies the missing anchor. The file merges the two and raises the stop.

**The Ecuadorian embassy, 2016. [R, contradiction unresolved]** A publicized exchange involving **Julian Assange**, then confined at the embassy in Knightsbridge, with Varoufakis. **Sources disagree on whether Žižek was physically present or joined by video link.** Not resolved. The stop is kept because the exchange happened; the presence question is stated on the stop's own sources.

## 8. Controversies, marriages, spectacle

**The 2014 plagiarism affair. [A, heavily corrected]** The pool's chronology lens described a *Critical Inquiry* piece 'on Hegel and the film Antigone's Claim' with a defence of 'careless notes'. **Every element of that is wrong.** Per the English Wikipedia sections 'Allegations of plagiarism' and 'Careless scholarship': two separate accusations ran in 2014, the *New York Times* charging **self-plagiarism** in an op-ed that reused his own earlier pages, and *Newsweek* reporting in **July 2014** that bloggers had found passages in a *Critical Inquiry* article reproduced from **Stanley Hornbeck**'s review of **Kevin MacDonald**'s *The Culture of Critique* (published in *American Renaissance*, 2006). His stated defence was not careless notes but that a friend had supplied the material and assured him it could be used freely; he apologised for 'not knowing that my friend's resume was largely borrowed.' *Antigone's Claim* is **Judith Butler**'s 2000 book, not a film; Antigone enters his record separately via a 'careless scholarship' critique by **Yannis Stavrakakis**. The file follows the corrected account.

**Marriages. [A with a correction]** Third marriage, 2005, to **Analía Hounie**, whom the cited source calls an **Argentinian model**; two pool lenses promote her to 'philosopher' and 'philosophy graduate', and the file says model. Fourth marriage to the Slovene journalist, author and philosopher **Jela Krečič**, widely reported as 2013 though the Wikipedia Personal life section does not date it. **Dropped as unsupported:** the claim that 'the couple later have a son.' Wikipedia gives only two adult sons, **Tim** and **Kostja**, who predate this marriage. One pool entry also dated the stop 2005 while its text was about 2013; the file dates it 2013.

**Wedding venues. [Gap]** No source in reach names a wedding location for either marriage. Ljubljana is used as his lifelong home base, not as an attested venue.

**Bell's palsy, 2018. [A]** An episode affecting the right side of his face. Place unspecified in every source in reach.

**RT, 2019. [A]** Begins hosting 'How to Watch the News with Slavoj Žižek' on the Russian state broadcaster, drawing sharp criticism given RT's role.

**The Peterson debate, 19 April 2019. [A]** 'Happiness: Capitalism vs. Marxism' against **Jordan Peterson** at the Sony Centre for the Performing Arts, since renamed **Meridian Hall**, 1 Front Street East, Toronto.

**Birkbeck. [A, corrected]** International director of the **Birkbeck Institute for the Humanities** from **2004 until September 2025**. Two pool entries got this wrong, one giving 'c. 2001-present', one '1990s-2025'; the Institute did not exist in the 1990s. Malet Street coordinates 51.5220 / -0.1308 are accurate.

## 9. Interlocks with existing journey files

Only relations where Žižek himself is one of the two parties are carried into campa text. Shared geography alone is not enough.

- **jacques_lacan**: the central one. His second doctorate is supervised by Lacan's son-in-law and editor; he translates Lacan into Slovene; the graph of desire from Seminar V and the Royaumont paper are the theoretical spine of *The Sublime Object of Ideology*. **The canonical ECF pin (48.8566 / 2.3522) is inherited byte-identically.** The Sainte-Anne and Royaumont pins are **not** taken as stops: Žižek was not there. They are cited on his own Paris stops as sources and suggested refs instead.
- **freud**: he translates Freud into Slovene and cites him throughout. Berggasse 19 is **not** taken as a stop; he did not go there in any record in reach.
- **gilles_deleuze**: *Organs without Bodies* (Routledge, **2003**, not 2004) is a full-length critical study. **Correction to the pool:** it is not a flat charge that Deleuze is 'insufficiently dialectical'. Žižek argues there are two Deleuzes and that the better one is nearer Hegel and Lacan than Deleuze's followers admit; the position he writes against is the Deleuze-and-Guattari of *Anti-Oedipus*, with Guattari blamed for the turn. The pool's lenses contradict each other here.
- **felix_guattari**: the *Anti-Oedipus* target. No meeting; Guattari died in 1992. Textual and one-directional, which is a real relation.
- **michel_foucault**: repeatedly criticized from a Lacanian position on power and subjectivity. No documented meeting; Foucault died in 1984 while Žižek's profile was still forming.

**Not carried:** `arnold_rikli` (Ljubljana coincidence only), `nikola_tesla` and `san_vito` (Yugoslav backdrop, no relation), `nick_land` and `roland_barthes` (grep hits, no verified link either way), `tommaso_campanella` (grep false positive). **Josefina Ayerza**, founder-editor of *Lacanian Ink*, sits in the census awaiting research and would be a genuine two-way interlock once her file exists.
