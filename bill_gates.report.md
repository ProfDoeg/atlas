# Bill Gates: research report

*2026-08-28. William Henry Gates III, b. Seattle 28 October 1955, living. Journey file: `bill_gates.journey.json`, 7 segments, 44 stops, gregorian, register "national mythology: the canon is true".*

Legend: **[A]** = attested, source named. **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition, or a lead not brought back to a primary source this pass. Where verification overturned or sharpened the first pass, that is stated as a correction. Contradictions are flagged and left standing.

Nine first-pass coordinates were wrong, by between 230 m and 12 km. Each correction is the relevant Wikipedia article's own figure, restated in the affected stop's `date_confidence`; the worst kept are Lakeside School (~4.5 km out), Aiwan-e-Sadr (~3 km) and the Medina estate (~830 m, out into Lake Washington).

Gates is alive, so the "tomb and its fate" register most files here close on does not apply, and nothing was invented to fill it. The last segment ends at a law firm's report instead of a grave.

## 1. Seattle, 1955-1973

- Born 28 October 1955, Seattle **[A: Wikipedia, 'Bill Gates']**. Father a lawyer; mother Mary Maxwell Gates on corporate and charitable boards; sisters Kristi and Libby **[A: Wallace and Erickson, *Hard Drive* (1992); Smithsonian comphist profile]**. The Laurelhurst placement is **neighborhood-level only**; no childhood address was pinned in anything reachable.
- Lakeside from about thirteen, 1968; Mothers' Club rummage sale funds a Teletype; programming with Paul Allen, Kent Evans, Ric Weiland **[A: Smithsonian; Wikipedia]**.
- **Kent Evans's death, 1972, climbing accident before graduation [R].** Carried by the Smithsonian material, Wallace and Erickson, and Gates's own repeated accounts; no primary account refetched. Site unknown; the stop uses a regional Cascade placeholder and says the coordinate is not a landmark. Weakest-sited stop in the file, and kept anyway.
- Congressional page, 1972 **[A: Smithsonian]**; month unsourced, the stop's June is an ordering placeholder. Graduated 1973, reportedly 1590/1600 **[A: Wikipedia]**; the story that he arranged his own class placement is **his own later telling [R]** and the campa attributes it to him.

## 2. Harvard and Albuquerque, 1973-1978

- Harvard autumn 1973; Currier House; met Ballmer on the same floor; an early software deal made from that room **[A: Wikipedia, 'Currier House (Harvard College)']**. Pancake-sorting bound with Papadimitriou, *Discrete Mathematics* 27 (1979) **[A: the paper]**.
- **Honeywell summer job, 1974 [A: Smithsonian]. Cut from the file** on siting, not truth: the source confirms the job, not a site address.
- Altair BASIC written against a PDP-10 simulator with Allen and Monte Davidoff; Allen's MITS demonstration, 1975 **[A: Smithsonian]**. Micro-Soft founded 1975 **[A: Wikipedia]**; no day in any source reached, which is why the office stop hangs on the registration date instead.
- **'An Open Letter to Hobbyists', 3 February 1976 [A, primary text fetched in full]** at digibarn.com, read end to end. All quoted lines in the file's Albuquerque material are verbatim from it.
- Trade name Microsoft registered in New Mexico, 26 November 1976 **[A: Wikipedia]**. Two Park Central Tower as the early office **[R]**: Albuquerque certain, building unreverified.
- 1977 Albuquerque traffic matter and booking photograph **[A: Smithsonian]**. The charge, court and disposition were **not** established from any primary record.

- IBM Boca Raton; failed Digital Research CP/M talks; the return to Microsoft **[A: news.microsoft.com; Smithsonian]**. - **Xerox PARC: stop cut.** The pool tagged it [R] and noted "Direct Gates personal visit to PARC not confirmed... the personal stop is reconstructed." ## Contradictions flagged, not resolved

1. **Three coordinates for one townhouse.** 9 East 71st Street sits at **40.7726, -73.9655** in the corpus (`jeffrey_epstein` and `bill_clinton`, twice each), **40.77155, -73.96654** in Wikipedia's 'Herbert N. Straus House', and **40.7724, -73.9631** in the afterlife lens. The file inherits the **corpus pin byte-identical** per the canonical-pin rule and records the disagreement. Reconcile all four existing stops at once or not at all.
2. **Two coordinates for the Strasbourg meeting**, 1.6 km apart (48.5846, 7.7686 geography lens; 48.5734, 7.7521 afterlife lens), neither the Palais de l'Europe, and the residence is not publicly addressed. The file uses the afterlife figure, attached to the Wikipedia-sourced claim. The pool also tagged that entry [A] while citing only "reporting cited in the 2026 WilmerHale-adjacent review process" and a 403 page: the fact survives via Wikipedia, the justification did not. Little Saint James likewise carries two pool coordinates against the corpus's own.
3. **QUEUE.md overstates the divorce filing.** The queue row says the Epstein meetings were "cited directly in the Gates divorce filing." The petition is reported to have said only that the marriage was "irretrievably broken", naming no cause and no person; Melinda named Epstein separately, afterward, as one of several factors. **The operator should correct QUEUE.md.**
4. **Buffett's motive.** The pool claimed he halted donations "pending review of the Gates-Epstein connections." Wikipedia records only that he ended the partnership in 2026 after an estimated $48bn. **The causal attribution is unsourced**; the file states the ending flat and drops the motive.
5. **"Irretrievably broken" as a quotation.** Uniformly reported as the petition's language, but the filing could not be read (WSJ paywalled, court-document sites 403/404). Reported as reported, not as verbatim court language; no `quote` field set for it.
