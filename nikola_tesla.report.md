# Nikola Tesla (1856-1943): research report

*2026-08-10. Atlas of Journeys, slug `nikola_tesla`. Born Smiljan, Military Frontier of the Austrian Empire; died New York City.*

**Legend.** **[A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or the subject's own retrospective testimony with no independent record. Where reachable sources disagree, both readings are printed and the contradiction is left standing.

**Caution about the source base.** Most early-life detail comes from *My Inventions* (1919), written thirty to sixty years after the events; Carlson (*Tesla*, 2013) reads several of its set pieces as retrospective construction. Anything resting on it alone is **[R]**, however often repeated. The next largest body is O'Neill's *Prodigal Genius* (1944), a friend's devotional biography and the origin of scenes later printed as fact. Those are **[R]** on sight.

## 1. Lika: birth and schooling, 1856-1875

- Born **10 July 1856** at **Smiljan**, to **Milutin Tesla**, Serbian Orthodox priest, and **Đuka Mandić**. **[A: Wikipedia, "Nikola Tesla"; Tesla Universe timeline]**
- Midnight birth in a lightning storm; "child of the storm," answered "no, of light." **[R: oral tradition, carried in O'Neill and popular accounts. No contemporary record.]**
- **Coordinate contradiction inside the pool.** Geography lens: **44.4958, 15.3241**, empty terrain south-east of the village. Afterlife lens, for the same building as the Memorial Center: **44.5595, 15.3087**, confirmed by the Center's published position (44°33′34″N 15°18′31″E) and used in the journey file. Seven km apart.
- **1862**: family moves to **Gospić**; primary school, then the **lower** Real Gymnasium there. **[A]** The pool put the *Higher* Real Gymnasium here, which its own next entry contradicts: he left for Karlovac in 1870 because the higher school was there.
- **1870-1873**: boards with an aunt at **Karlovac**, completing the four-year course in three. **[A: Wikipedia]**
- **1873**: returns to **Smiljan** and contracts **cholera**, nine months bedridden. **[A: Wikipedia, explicit that the illness followed the return to Smiljan.]** Versions placing it at Karlovac or "the Gospić area" are unsupported. The bedside bargain, engineering school instead of the seminary: **[R: *My Inventions*, uncorroborated.]**
- **1874**: months in the **Lika highlands** avoiding conscription. **[A for the evasion. R for the claim that it rebuilt his constitution: O'Neill.]**

## 2. The unfinished degrees, 1875-1880

- **1875**: enters the **Imperial-Royal Technical College (Joanneum), Graz**, on a Military Frontier scholarship; the scholarship lapses, gambling and overwork follow, and in **December 1878** he leaves **without a degree**, concealing it from his family. **[A: Wikipedia; Carlson]**
- **January 1879**: draftsman at **Maribor**, 60 florins a month; arrested for lacking a residence permit and deported to Gospić. **[A: Wikipedia; Carlson]**
- **17 April 1879**: **Milutin Tesla dies**, aged 60. **[A]** Tesla teaches a term at the Gospić gymnasium. **[A: Carlson]**
- **January 1880**: uncles fund **Prague**; lectures at **Charles-Ferdinand University**, most probably as an auditor, no degree. **[A for attendance: Wikipedia. A for the auditor reading: Carlson.]** **Coordinate correction:** pool pin 50.0865, 14.4114 sits near the Klementinum, 0.9 km off; the **Karolinum** is at **50.0863, 14.4235**.

## 3. The rotating field: Budapest, Paris, Strasbourg, 1881-1884

- **1881**: telegraph office, then **Budapest Telephone Exchange** under **Tivadar Puskás**, rising to chief electrician. **[A]** The amplifying repeater is his own claim. **[R]**
- **The park epiphany**, Városliget, sunset, Goethe, the diagram drawn in sand. **[R, and the pool misstated its own source:]** it claimed Wikipedia dates the insight to February 1882, where Wikipedia says only that *Tesla claimed* 1882. February 1882 is his own date, given in 1919; Carlson reads the scene as retrospective construction. Rendered as "Tesla later dated it to February 1882" in the journey file's `date_confidence`.
- **1882**: **Continental Edison Company**, Paris. **[A]** Offices at **57 rue de Clichy**, roughly **48.8797, 2.3297**; the pool's pin lay 2 km away near Châtelet. **[Corrected.]**
- Pitching the induction motor to Continental Edison management: **[R: *My Inventions*. Carlson finds no company record.]**
- **1883**: **Ivry-sur-Seine** works, then **Strasbourg**, where he builds and runs the first working **induction motor** prototype in off-hours. **[A for a Strasbourg prototype: teslasociety.com. R for the manner of it: *My Inventions*.]**

## 4. The Atlantic and the Edison works, 1884-1886

- **June 1884**: arrives New York with a letter from **Charles Batchelor** to **Edison**. **[A]** The four cents, the book of poems and the stolen ticket: **[R: *My Inventions*]** The letter as usually quoted ("two great men") is not confirmed against an original: **[R: O'Neill]**
- **Port of departure contested.** The pool asserted **Le Havre** aboard the **SS City of Richmond**, an Inman Line vessel working the Liverpool-Queenstown-New York run. **[R, unresolved. The journey file omits the departure stop rather than pin a contested port.]**
- **Edison Machine Works, Goerck Street**; quits after about six months over an unpaid bonus. Diary: **"Good By to the Edison Machine Works."** **[A: Wikipedia]** The $50,000 promise and the "American humor" reply are Tesla's account, disputed by Edison biographers. **[R]**
- **1885**: **Lemuel W. Serrell** introduces **Robert Lane** and **Benjamin Vail**; **Tesla Electric Light & Manufacturing** at Rahway; first US patents, arc lighting. **[A]** *Internal pool contradiction:* chronology lens dates the company 1885 with the walkout in 1886; geography lens dates the venture 1886-1887. The 1885 formation is better attested and is used.
- **1886**: investors take the patents; he digs ditches for about $2 a day. **[A for the collapse. R for the ditch-digging figure, which survives only in his own telling.]**

## 5. The polyphase system, 1887-1891

- **April 1887**: **Alfred S. Brown** and **Charles F. Peck** back him; laboratory at **89 Liberty Street**. **[A]** **May 1888**: **US patent 381,968**, the polyphase induction motor system. **[A]**
- **16 May 1888**: **"A New System of Alternate Current Motors and Transformers"** read to the **AIEE**. **[A: text in T. C. Martin, ed. (1894), Gutenberg 39272.]** **Coordinate correction, 5.5 km:** the pool pinned 40.7075, -73.9987, the City Hall district; Columbia College stood at **Madison Avenue and 49th Street** until 1897, approximately **40.7568, -73.9770**.
- **July 1888**: Westinghouse licenses the patents. **$5,000 cash plus 150 shares**, commonly summarized as $60,000; **$2.50 per AC horsepower** royalty; **$2,000 a month** consultancy. **[A]** **Place corrected:** the deal was made at Tesla's **New York** laboratory; he moved to Pittsburgh afterward. The pool located the deal at Pittsburgh.
- **1888-1889**: about a year at the **Pittsburgh works**, clashing over frequency standards. **[A: Carlson]**
- **1891**: **releases the per-horsepower royalty** during Westinghouse's refinancing crisis. **[A for the release.]** The torn contract and the "richest man in America" counterfactual are **[R: O'Neill 1944]**; the instrument was a renegotiated agreement. **Place strain flagged:** the pool put this at Pittsburgh in 1891, but Tesla had returned to New York in 1889; the journey file pins the works and says so.
- **30 July 1891**: naturalized. **[A]**
- **Tesla coil.** Pool: "summer 1889, patented the following year." Both halves wrong: the coil is dated **1891** and **US patent 454,622** issued in **1891**. **[Corrected.]**

## 6. High frequency, Europe, the fair, 1891-1893

- **Laboratory addresses, corrected.** Wikipedia's chronology: **175 Grand Street, 1889-1892**; **33-35 South Fifth Avenue, 1892-1895**; **46 and 48 East Houston Street, 1895-1902**. The pool's geography lens reversed the first two and so attached the **13 March 1895 fire** to Grand Street. The fire took **South Fifth Avenue**. **[A: NYT, 14 March 1895.]**
- **3 February 1892**: **Royal Institution** lecture, London, repeated for the Institution of Electrical Engineers. **[A: Martin, ed., 1894]**
- **February 1892**: repeated in **Paris** for the Société Internationale des Électriciens. **[A]** The telegram announcing his mother's illness: **[R: *My Inventions*]**
- **April 1892**: reaches **Gospić** before **Đuka** dies. **[A for the death: Tesla Universe, which alone dates it. R for the vision of her spirit: *My Inventions*, where he also prints his own naturalistic explanation of it.]**
- **31 May 1892**: received at **Belgrade**, decorated by **King Alexander I**, addressed by the Serbian Royal Academy. **[R: Belgrade press accounts cited in Carlson; no press text read here.]**
- **24 February 1893**: **Franklin Institute**, Philadelphia. **[A]** **Coordinate correction:** the pool pinned the Parkway building, opened **1934**; in 1893 the Institute was at **15 South 7th Street**, approximately **39.9495, -75.1533**. **March 1893**: the same demonstrations at the **NELA** convention, St Louis. **[A: Carlson]**
- **May-October 1893**: the **World's Columbian Exposition** is lit by Westinghouse on Tesla's polyphase system after underbidding General Electric; Tesla demonstrates high-frequency effects on the grounds. **[A: Carlson; Wikipedia]**

### Interlock: the fairgrounds, and a shared pin

The corpus holds seven travelers on this ground; the Exposition pin **41.788, -87.597** comes from `muybridge.journey.json` and is inherited **byte-identical**. In each case the fair is attested in the other file and the Tesla layer is the addition: **[R]** from their side, **[A]** from his.

## 7. Niagara, the fire, the demonstrations, 1895-1898

- **13 March 1895**: the **South Fifth Avenue laboratory burns**, uninsured; notes, models and apparatus lost. **[A]**
- **1895**: founds the **Nikola Tesla Company** with **Edward Dean Adams** among the backers. **[A]**
- **August 1895 to November 1896**: the **Adams Power Station** at Niagara transmits AC to Buffalo. **[A: Carlson; teslasociety.com]**
- **Pin inheritance.** The pool gives the station as 43.0896, -79.0378; the corpus Niagara pin, used for **Jules Verne's** 1867 visit, is **43.0828, -79.0742**, inherited byte-identical, with the station's own coordinate recorded here. **Lord Kelvin** chaired the 1893 International Niagara Commission that chose polyphase AC. **[R: `lord_kelvin.journey.json` attests only a cable ship named Niagara, unrelated to the falls. The coincidence of name is noted, not built on.]**
- **March 1896**: X-ray imaging with a coil-driven vacuum tube. **[A]**
- **1898**: the radio-controlled boat, his **"teleautomaton,"** at **Madison Square Garden**; refused by the US military as a guided torpedo. **[A]** **Gap:** no sourced coordinate for the 1898 hall was reached; the journey file pins the second Madison Square Garden at 40.7423, -73.9877, which is **[R]** as a pin.

## 8. Colorado Springs and Wardenclyffe, 1899-1917

- **May 1899 to January 1900**: the **Colorado Springs** station, part-funded by **$100,000 from John Jacob Astor IV**; discharges of about 135 feet; final journal entry 7 January 1900. **[A: *Colorado Springs Notes*; Wikipedia]**
- **Coordinate correction, 3.4 km, plus a wrong descriptor.** The pool put the station in the "Foothills" at 38.8608, -104.8214. It stood on **Knob Hill**, East Kiowa Street at North Foote Avenue, **east** of town: **38.8347, -104.8006**. The foothills lie west. Razed 1904.
- **December 1899**: repeating signals reported, Mars and Venus raised in print later. **[A that he made the claim: *Collier's*, 9 February 1901. Nothing about the signals themselves is attested.]**
- **March 1901**: **$150,000 from J. P. Morgan** against 51% of the wireless patents; **Wardenclyffe** begun September 1901. **[A]** **Coordinate contradiction inside the pool:** geography lens 40.9506, -72.8817 against afterlife lens **40.9476, -72.8982**, 1.4 km apart; the published position confirms the second, used throughout so 1901, 1917 and 2013 share one pin.
- **December 1901**: **Marconi** transmits the letter S from Poldhu to St John's. **[A]** The pool made this the immediate cause of the collapse. **Contradiction printed, not resolved:** the Wardenclyffe article says work ceased around **1906** on Morgan's refusal; the Tesla article gives **1905**. Best rendering: staff let go and work slowed 1905, abandonment 1906.
- **10 July 1906**: bladeless turbine, 200 horsepower at 16,000 rpm. **[A: Tesla Universe; teslasociety.com, not confirmed against a contemporary technical account.]**
- **Edison Medal year corrected.** Pool: 1917, from teslasociety.com. The IEEE list reads **1916 Nikola Tesla**, 1917 John J. Carty; Wikipedia's infobox reads 1916. The **banquet** was **18 May 1917**. The journey file dates the stop to the banquet and carries the award year in the hedge.
- **July-September 1917**: tower **dynamited for scrap** by the **Smiley Steel Company of New York**; salvage **$1,750**. **[A]** The pool's 4 July date is unsupported; the day is dropped.

## 9. The hotel years, 1918-1943

- **The pool compressed four decades into one 1918 stamp.** The sequence is right, the chronology is not. **1918**: leaves the **Waldorf-Astoria** owing roughly **$20,000**, the hotel holding his baggage and models against the debt. Then the **St Regis**, **Marguery**, **Governor Clinton**, **Pennsylvania**, reaching the **Hotel New Yorker** in **1934**. **[A: Wikipedia, which dates the last move separately.]**
- **Hotel coordinates corrected.** The pool gave **40.7505, -73.9934** for both the Pennsylvania and the New Yorker, one pin for two buildings; both are wrong. Pennsylvania, 401 Seventh Avenue: **40.7500, -73.9906**. New Yorker, 481 Eighth Avenue: **40.7527, -73.9938**.
- **1919**: **"My Inventions,"** six parts in *Electrical Experimenter*, February to June and October, introduced by **Hugo Gernsback**; no standalone book until **1982**. **[A]**
- **20 July 1931**: cover of *Time*, "All the world's his power house," congratulations from **Einstein** among others. **Age corrected:** he turned **75**, not 81; the pool transposed his age at the 1937 taxicab accident. **[A]**
- **11 July 1934**: announces **"teleforce"** on his 78th birthday. **[A: *Herald Tribune*, via Wikisource]** **1937**: struck by a taxicab, aged 81; never fully recovers. **[A]**
- **7 January 1943**: dies in **Room 3327** of the Hotel New Yorker, aged 86, about 10:30 p.m., coronary thrombosis. **[A: Wikipedia; NYT obituary, 8 January 1943]**
- **Discovery interval corrected twice over.** Both pool lenses said two days undiscovered. Wikipedia: the maid found him **the next day**; it was the **Do Not Disturb sign** that had hung **three days**.

## 10. Afterlife, 1943-2021

- **The seizure.** Two days after the death the **FBI** asked the **Office of Alien Property Custodian** to seize his belongings. **[A: Wikipedia.]** The pool tagged this **[R]** and gave the wrong basis: the hook was the foreign citizenship of the claimant, his nephew **Sava Kosanović**, not Tesla's own status. **Promoted to [A], basis corrected.**
- **1943**: **John G. Trump** of MIT examines the papers for the government: nothing of military value, no death ray. **[A]**
- **10 January 1943**: La Guardia's eulogy broadcast. **12 January**: **Serbian Orthodox service** in the **Cathedral of St John the Divine**, which is Episcopal; over two thousand mourners; cremation at **Ferncliff**, Hartsdale, the same day. **[A: NYT, 13 January 1943; Ferncliff records via Seifer.]** **Three Nobel laureates as pallbearers: [R], repeated everywhere, confirmed nowhere reachable.**
- **Belgrade, three dates the pool collapsed into one.** Effects **shipped from New York 7 September 1951**; museum **established 5 December 1952** by government decision, in a 1927 villa built for **Đorđe Genčić** at **Krunska 51**; exhibition **1955**; urn on **public display 1957**. The pool gave a single 1957 transfer to a "newly founded" museum. **[A: museum institutional history; Wikipedia.]** The urn as "spherical bronze" symbolizing rotating fields is devotional description, usually reported as gold-plated. **[R]** Coordinate: geography lens 44.8021, 20.4651 against afterlife lens **44.805, 20.4708**, which matches Krunska 51 and is used.
- **2003**: the archive (over 160,000 documents, 2,000 books and journals, 1,500 photographs) enters UNESCO's **Memory of the World**. **[A]**
- **1960**: the **CGPM** names the SI unit of magnetic flux density the **tesla**, proposed by **France Avčin**. **[A]** **Gap:** no session coordinate sought; the journey file pins Paris generically, **[R]** as a pin.
- **10 July 2006**: the reconstructed birth house opens as the **Nikola Tesla Memorial Center** at Smiljan, with a statue by **Mile Blažević**, on the 150th anniversary. **[A]**
- **2012-2014**: **Matthew Inman's** campaign raises **$1.37 million** in a week; the **Tesla Science Center** completes the Wardenclyffe purchase **2 May 2013**; **Elon Musk** pledges **$1 million** and a Supercharger, July 2014. **[A]**
- **Tesla Motors attribution corrected.** Founded **July 2003** by **Martin Eberhard** and **Marc Tarpenning**; Musk led the Series A and became chairman in **2004**, recognized as co-founder later by settlement. The pool's "Elon Musk's company, incorporated in 2003" compresses this wrongly. Musk is in `QUEUE.md`, which makes this the corpus's one already-planned direct-naming interlock.
- **7 January 2021**: the **Tesla Science Foundation Serbia** petitions the Holy Synod to consider **canonization**, proposing 10 July as his feast day. **[A: Wikipedia, "Nikola Tesla in popular culture."]** **Month corrected:** the pool gave 7 July. It is **7 January**, the anniversary of his death and Orthodox Christmas together, which is the point of the choice.

## Contradictions recorded, not adjudicated

1. **Wardenclyffe halt, 1905 or 1906**: two Wikipedia articles disagree; printed as a range.
2. **The pool's two lenses disagreed with each other on three buildings**: birth house by 7 km, Wardenclyffe by 1.4 km, Belgrade museum by 500 m. Each resolved above against an external position; the pattern is a warning about the pool.
3. **Laboratory chronology**: Grand Street and South Fifth Avenue inverted, and the 1895 fire attached to the wrong one. Corrected against Wikipedia's lab chronology, itself a secondary compilation.
4. **Port of embarkation, June 1884**: unresolved; the stop is omitted rather than pinned.
5. **The 1891 royalty release**: attested; the torn contract is O'Neill's scene; the pool's Pittsburgh does not match his 1891 residence.
6. **Tesla Electric Light & Manufacturing**: 1885 against 1886-1887, inside the same pool.

## Honest gaps

- **No primary FBI document was read.** The seizure comes from Wikipedia's summary; the inventory of what was taken and returned is not established here.
- **Carlson, Seifer and O'Neill were not read directly.** They reach this report through citation in the reachable secondary material; claims resting on them alone are flagged.
- **Three pins are reconstructions**: the 1898 Madison Square Garden hall, the 1917 Edison Medal banquet venue, and the 1960 CGPM session, marked in the journey file's `date_confidence`.
- **Three attested events are recorded here but not pinned in the journey file, for length**: the March 1893 NELA lecture at St Louis, the March 1896 X-ray experiments, the 10 July 1906 bladeless turbine.
- **Đuka Mandić's death date** rests on the Tesla Universe timeline alone.
- **The Belgrade reception of 31 May 1892** rests on press accounts cited in Carlson, not on a text read here.
- **Nothing in the Colorado Springs signal claim is independently attested.** What is attested is that Tesla made the claim, in *Collier's*, in 1901.
