# José Luis Álvarez Enparantza, "Txillardegi" (1929-2012)

*Research report, 2026-08-31, for `jose_luis_alvarez_emparantza_txillardegi.journey.json`: 44 stops, 7 segments, register "national mythology: the canon is true", calendar gregorian.*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction, family tradition, participant testimony, or a claim carried by one unverifiable page. Contradictions below are flagged and left standing; none is adjudicated. Every hedge also lives in the relevant stop's `date_confidence`, never in campa prose.

On the name: born Álvarez Enparantza (Basque spelling) / Emparantza (Spanish). The slug uses the Spanish form; the file's `traveler` field uses Enparantza, matching Euskaltzaindia's own register. Txillardegi, adopted 1954, is the name he published under for fifty-eight years.

## 1. Antigua and the notebook, 1929-1950

**Birth**, 27 September 1929, Gutenfelder house, no. 579 Amilibia street (now Zumalakarregi), Antigua quarter of Donostia. **[A** Euskaltzaindia official biography page for date and quarter; eu.wikipedia "Txillardegi" for street and house.**]**

*Contradiction flagged.* Several agency obituaries printed 1927 and called him 84 at death. Euskaltzaindia gives 1929, followed here as the stronger attestation; the variant is not reconciled.

*Coordinate corrected.* The dossier's 43.3176/-1.9945 falls 600-700 m east of Antigua toward Miraconcha. Antigua's core, cross-checked against Gaskuña plaza (43.31408/-2.00232, eu.wikipedia "Txillardegi plaza (Donostia)"), sits near **43.3148, -2.0018**, used for all Antigua stops. The house is not independently pinned.

**Family.** Paternal line from Madrid to Tolosa before the Second Carlist War; maternal line, the Enparantza, from around Oñati. Spanish at home, the mother's Basque kept for her sisters and the market. A childhood spell at Calle San Martín with a grandmother while the father worked in Cuba. **[R]** Background inside the birth campa; Tolosa, Oñati and San Martín get no stops, since the ancestral-town material records where his forebears lived, not where he travelled.

**1947, the notebook.** A month's illness; he interrogates his mother about Basque and writes a small grammar into a notebook made from the family lithography workshop's paper. **[A** for the event, txillardegi.wordpress.com/biografia; **[R]** for "Casa Victoria" and the workshop's failure.**]** Same year, **the leaflets** under doors in the Parte Vieja, which he later called his first political action: **[R]**, lead material only, month invented to order the segment.

**1948**, sustained study, meets Jon Etxaide and Elbira Zipitria **[A]**. **1949**, moves to Bilbao for engineering **[A** corroborated by eu.wikipedia; the lead material's "Academia Necoechea" specifically is **[R]]**.

**19 January 1950.** Meets Jone Forcada Adarraga at a romería; the two groups converged because both were speaking Basque. **[R** for square and exact date; **[A]** for a 1950 San Sebastián festival meeting, per Jone Forcada's obituary, Gaur8/Naiz, 15 November 2025.**]** Later in **1950**, first articles in Basque and an arrest on a charge of belonging to Jagi-Jagi **[A]**, month not established.

## 2. Ekin, the naming, the prisons, 1952-1960

**1952, Ekin** co-founded with fellow students. **[A]**

*Simplification flagged.* eu.wikipedia frames Ekin as founded "while at university in Bilbao"; es.wikipedia "Euskadi Ta Askatasuna" describes a merger of a Bilbao group with a Donostia group, later merged with the PNV youth wing and split from it in May 1958. The file uses the Bilbao pin and states the simplification.

**1952 and 1954, Zamora.** Two military service periods, not one. **[A** txillardegi.wordpress.com/biografia has both entries explicitly; the dossier chronology carried only one. Zamora 41.5033/-5.7446 verified.**]** Compressed into one stop dated 1954, stated in the field. **1954, the pseudonym**, from a house near the family home in Antigua **[A** eu.wikipedia**]**. **1955**, Oiargi prize for "Kierkegaard-en hazia Unamuno-gan lore" **[A]**, folded into the Ferrol stop.

**1956, Ferrol.** Final six months of service; writes *Leturiaren egunkari ezkutua*. **[A** txillardegi.wordpress.com/biografia, which the dossier wrongly tagged [R] although it is the dossier's own primary source and states this explicitly. Ferrol 43.4832/-8.233 verified.**]**

**1957.** *Leturiaren egunkari ezkutua* published, later called the first modern Basque novel **[A** en.wikipedia**]**; named euskaltzain urgazlea on 26 July **[A** Euskaltzaindia**]**. One stop, on the Academy's date.

**Late 1958, Deba: the naming.**

*Correction applied.* The dossier dated the naming 1959 and cited en.wikipedia "Txillardegi", which does **not** say he proposed the name. The actual source is es.wikipedia "Euskadi Ta Askatasuna": proposed by Txillardegi at Deba in **late 1958**, with del Valle, Madariaga and Larramendi present, ATA rejected for meaning "duck" in Gipuzkoan; first public use 31 July 1959. The file dates Deba 1958-12-01, the four initials 1959-07-31. **[R]** on the historiography: no primary record establishes one founding day, de Pablo treats it as a process, and the Deba scene rests on retrospective participant testimony, which the stop says.

*Pin inheritance.* `julen_madariaga.journey.json`, written since the interlock lens ran (which reported no Madariaga file), carries "Deba, the duck rejected" at **43.2958, -2.3554**, dated 1958-12-01. Inherited byte-identical, over the dossier's 43.2892/-2.3556 and the geographic 43.2969/-2.3517. Likewise **Bilbao 43.263, -2.935** and **Donostia 43.3183, -1.9812**; **Bayonne 43.4933, -1.4748** and **Brussels 50.8503, 4.3517** agree between that file and the dossier.

**31 July 1959.** The divided Eusko Gaztedi adopts the name; Agirre is notified the same day. **[A** Txillardegi's own 1971 internal document, *Documentos Y* vol. I p. 22, quoted in de Pablo p. 52; PDF fetched and read.**]** His presence at that meeting is **[R]** and is not asserted in the campa.

**August to November 1960.** Arrested, held incommunicado at the Civil Guard command in Antigua, then imprisoned at Martutene until November. **[A** eu.wikipedia.**]**

## 3. Exile: Hasparren, Bayonne, Brussels, 1961-1975

**1 January 1961**, crosses into the French Basque Country. **[A** year and destination. **[R]** New Year's Day and the specific border point.**]** *Contradiction flagged and left standing:* txillardegi.wordpress.com/biografia names France, Switzerland and Belgium; every other account names Hasparren, Paris, Belgium, Bordeaux and Hendaia, with no Switzerland. No Swiss stop is staged; the disagreement is recorded in the crossing stop.

**1962-1965, Hasparren**, mathematics at the Collège Saint-Joseph. **[A** for the post. **[R]** for the director Piarres Xarriton and the 1965 dismissal; neither encyclopedia names the school.**]**

**1963, Bayonne.** Euskal Idazkaritza founded, he leads its language work **[A** eu.wikipedia**]**. The resignation letter to Euskaltzaindia through *Enbata* is **[R]**.

**29-30 August 1964, the Bayonne Congress.** *Correction applied twice.* The dossier gave one day and tagged the congress **[R]** as absent from the encyclopedias. It is **[A]** and two days long: eu.wikipedia, *"1964ko abuztuaren 29 eta 30ean, Euskal Idazkaritzak Baionako Biltzarra antolatu zuen."* Stop dated to the first day, span stated. Bayonne coordinates verified.

**1965, Brussels.** Settled as apatride with a UN passport **[A** eu.wikipedia**]**. The "sixteen residences in twenty years" figure is **[A]** from the same source; Waterloo as one address is **[R]** and gets no separate stop.

**1966-1971, Branka [A]**; **1967, resignation from ETA** after the Fifth Assembly's Marxist-Leninist turn **[A]**.

**7 June 1968, Pardines.** ETA's first generally accepted killing, of Civil Guard traffic officer José Antonio Pardines on the Aduna-Villabona road, shot by Txabi Etxebarrieta. **[A** web.guardiacivil.es efemérides.**]** Txillardegi had left the year before and was in Belgium. **Not staged at the Aduna road**: that stop belongs to the people who were there. It is the content of a Brussels stop at his own pin, on the date the news reached him, paired with the 1968 Txomin Agirre prize for *Elsa Scheelen* **[A]**. Likewise the **1969 Andima award at Caracas [R]** gets no Caracas stop, since no source places him in Venezuela; it is one clause inside the Bordeaux stop.

**1970, Bordeaux**, assistant teacher. **[A** txillardegi.wordpress.com/biografia; es.wikipedia confirms "Universidad de Burdeos III Michel de Montaigne".**]** *Coordinate corrected:* the Pessac campus is near **44.7975, -0.5967**, not the dossier's 44.8073/-0.6083 (1.4 km off).

**c. 1970-1977, Hendaye.** *Correction applied.* The dossier gave 1972-1977. Jone Forcada's obituary says the family settled at Hendaia "around 1970"; eu.wikipedia dates her teaching at the Hendaia ikastola to 1970-1976. Stop dated 1970-06-01, both datings named.

**1971, the internal document**, his earliest recorded account of the name change **[A** de Pablo, from *Documentos Y***]**. Place of writing is **[R]**, inferred from the Bordeaux and Hendaia years and stated as inferred. **1975, Paris**, licence in linguistics **[A]**.

## 4. Return and politics, 1976-1992

**1976 or 1977, the return.** *Contradiction flagged and left standing.* en.wikipedia and es.wikipedia say 1976; txillardegi.wordpress.com/biografia dates the definitive return to 1977. The file stages one protected 1976 crossing at Irun and names both. The brother's approach to the civil governor, and the policeman who said "welcome to Spain" then corrected himself to "welcome to the Basque Country", are **[R]**. **1976-1983, EUTG**, teaching phonology in Donostia **[A]**.

**1976 or 1977, ESB.** *Contradiction flagged.* en.wikipedia: "In 1976 he and Iñaki Aldekoa formed a political party, the Euskal Sozialista Biltzarrea (ESB)". txillardegi.wordpress.com/biografia and orain.eus put it in 1977. The dossier silently took 1977; the hedge is now in the stop, matching the return.

**1977 or 1978, Herri Batasuna.** *Citation error flagged.* The dossier dated it 1978 and cited en.wikipedia, which actually says he participated "in the foundation in 1977 of Herri Batasuna". HB was constituted after the April 1978 Mesa de Alsasua, so 1978 is the standard date. Stop dated 1978-04-01, with the cited source's disagreement stated.

**1980.** Expelled from ESB; Zilarrezko Lauburu for *Euskal Gramatika*. **[A]** The stop borrows the day of Sartre's death (15 April 1980) purely to order the segment, stated in the field. **1981-1982, UCLA**: **[A]** for the California year (eu.wikipedia, whose fetched summary garbled the institution's name), **[R]** for UCLA specifically.

**1982 or 1983, UPV/EHU Zorroaga.** *Contradiction flagged.* txillardegi.wordpress.com/biografia gives 1982 for the return to EHU; eu.wikipedia gives 1983-1999 for the Zorroaga post. Both named. Coordinates 43.2965/-1.9645 verified.

**1983, Bellaterra**, doctoral thesis on Basque accentuation, published 1984 as *Euskal azentuaz*. **[A** Euskaltzaindia, txillardegi.wordpress.com/biografia.**]** Both encyclopedias say only "University of Barcelona"; the Autonomous University is **[R]**. The thesis is dated 1983 in some accounts, 1984 in others.

**1985**, *Gertakarien lekuko*, autobiographical **[A]**, not staged. **1986, senator** for Gipuzkoa on the HB list **[A** en.wikipedia, txillardegi.wordpress.com/biografia; a 20 November 1989 ministry archive photograph identifies him among HB deputies and senators**]**; the month is taken from that year's general election, not from a source naming his own dates.

**1989-1992**, second Senate term, 29 October 1989 to 12 May 1992 **[A** es.wikipedia**]**; founds *Bat Soziolinguistika Aldizkaria*, directs it to 2002 **[A]**. **1993**, Euskaltzaindia's pronunciation commission **[A]**; **1995**, minority-languages conference at Santiago de Compostela **[R]**. Neither staged: the first has no place, the second is too thin for a pin.

## 5. The refusals and the end, 2001-2012

**2001, Aralar. [R** on the year: en.wikipedia and orain.eus place him in Aralar without dating his membership; Aralar was constituted in 2001.**]**

**26 November 2004, honorary membership declined.** *Contradiction flagged and left standing.* Euskaltzaindia's own page says he **declined the appointment**; most secondary tributes call him simply an honorary academic from 2004 and mention no refusal. Both readings are in the stop. The **2004 Argizaiola at Durango** is **[R]**, lead material only, with the month taken from the fair's usual early-December dates.

**The Bidarte farmhouse, Amasa-Villabona**, sold on the condition that the buyer speak Basque, piano and much of the library left behind. **[R** lead material only, no document produced. The 2005 date is invented purely to order the stop and is stated as such.**]**

**May 2007, leaving Aralar.** *Correction applied.* The dossier gave 22 May 2007, citing en.wikipedia for date and reasons (Ezker Batua alliance; a government act of solidarity with ETA's victims). en.wikipedia gives **no date and no reason**, only that "for a time he was active in Aralar". eu.wikipedia gives the month: *"2007ko maiatzean, ordea, alderdia uztea erabaki zuen."* Stop dated 2007-05-01, day marked unsourced, reasons marked as carried by no source that also carries a date.

**2008**, EAE-ANV Senate list, candidacy blocked by the party's judicial suspension **[A]**, not staged but noted in the 2011 stop's confidence field. **February 2011**, honoured by Euskal Herrian Euskaraz after a national demonstration, one of his last public speeches recorded **[R** lead material only**]**.

**14 January 2012**, death at home in Donostia, aged 82, around midday, after an illness of almost a year. **[A** Euskaltzaindia, both encyclopedias, orain.eus.**]** The Parte Vieja detail is **[R]**; the campa says the accounts disagree rather than choosing.

**15 January 2012, cremation.** *Correction applied.* The dossier's chronology said **buried**; its own afterlife lens said **cremated**, and the afterlife lens is right (txillardegi.wordpress.com/biografia: cremated 15 January, further commemoration 29 January). **No source found names a grave, niche, columbarium, or the fate of the ashes.** Stated in the file as a gap.

**The ikurriña.** *Correction applied.* The coffin was covered with the ikurriña that had covered Txomin Iturbe Abasolo's, and the source records the reuse: *"Txomin Iturbe Abasolo Txomin ETAko kidearen ohial berarekin estali zuten Txillardegiren gorpua."* But the dossier's account of Iturbe's death was **fabricated**: not a 1987 Guatemalan shootout. He died on 27 February 1987 near Berrouaghia, Algeria, where he had been deported, officially in a road accident (es.wikipedia "Txomin Iturbe"). Corrected in the campa. The source also does **not** say the arrangement was Txillardegi's own prior request; the campa says the assertion circulates and is absent from the source recording the cloth.

## 8. Apparatus relations for the operator

**Txillardegi is not listed in `EXCEPTIONS.md`.** No apparatus stop is staged and none was needed: nothing in the material required applying his framework backward to events he never discussed.

One item is flagged rather than silently deleted, per the standing duty. **Direction caught: neither backward nor forward, but pure co-location.** The interlock lens offered Sartre and Camus on the basis that his Paris years (1961-1977) share the canonical Paris pin with their files across the same decades, with no attested contact, and offered Chomsky on the same basis (Chomsky's file has no Basque Country or Spain stop in the period). Neither is an apparatus relation and neither would be licensable as one. Both are retained here as **shared-geography inferences, not staged**; all three men are named in campas only on the independent citation and influence relations in section 7, rooted in Txillardegi's own reading and teaching. If the operator wants either treated differently, this is the paragraph to overrule.

No forward reference was written. No stop was staged where the record does not put him: not the Aduna road (June 1968, he was in Belgium), not Caracas (1969 award, no travel found), not Arantzazu (the 2018 plaque is posthumous), not the 2023 human chain, not the 2018 plaza dedication.
