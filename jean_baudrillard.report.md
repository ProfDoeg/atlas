# Jean Baudrillard (1929-2007): research report

*For `jean_baudrillard.journey.json`. 9 segments, 44 stops, 10 quotes.*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named on the line · **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or dossier-only claim not independently confirmed · **[CORR]** a correction to the research pool itself, with the reason.

His *bibliography* is dated to the year with near-perfect agreement. His *life* is not: almost every anchor outside it has a competing date somewhere, several in otherwise reliable reference works. Hence the density of flagged conflicts below, none resolved by fiat.

## 1. Reims, 1929-1948

**Birth. [A** en.wikipedia; criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php?art=433 **]** Reims (Marne), 27 July 1929, into a family of Ardennais peasant origin one generation into the civil service: gendarme father, postal-worker mother by one account.

**Contradiction, unresolved: the day.** Wikipedia and criticalsecret give **27 July**; the dossier reports **Le Monde** giving **20 July** and the **Guardian** **29 July**. Neither obituary could be fetched (both blocked WebFetch), so that spread is relayed, not confirmed. **Gap:** no birth address, hospital, or parish; the coordinate is Reims city centre.

**First of the family to university. [A** Wikipedia **]** He described the rupture as a distance opening from his parents' milieu; no primary interview text was located. **Peillet and 'pataphysics [R** Wikipedia and SEP both report it without a primary document **]**: his lycée philosophy teacher handed him Jarry. Neither names the building, so the coordinate is again city centre and the 1945 date is a placeholder.

**Hypokhâgne at Lycée Henri-IV, 1948. [A** criticalsecret **]** Entered on scholarship, on the École normale supérieure track, then broke it off. **Gap: no source gives a reason.** The consequence is structural: no agrégation, no normalien title, no Collège de France chair, unlike Lévi-Strauss (1959), Foucault (1970), Barthes (1977).

**[CORR] Wikipedia's "1948" for the Nietzsche/Luther DES is untenable and is not used as an anchor.** A *diplôme d'études supérieures* is post-licence and cannot precede the licence; 1948 is the year he *entered* Henri-IV, aged 19 (criticalsecret). The degree belongs to the early 1950s, before the 1952-53 Tübingen lectorate. The file dates it c. 1949-1952.

**Arles, mason. [A** criticalsecret, sole source **]** Mason's work, and by some accounts farm labour, after leaving the hypokhâgne track. **Gap: no town, worksite, employer, or duration named anywhere**, so the coordinate stands for the region. The thinnest-documented stop in the file.

## 3. Nanterre, 1966-1970

**Appointment and thesis, 1966. [A** Wikipedia, SEP, criticalsecret, concurring **]** Assistant to **Henri Lefebvre** in the new Paris X-Nanterre sociology department; defended *Le Système des objets* before a jury of **Lefebvre, Roland Barthes and Pierre Bourdieu**, dated September 1966 by one archival source. Sources disagree on his rank; the file asserts nothing beyond "assistant".

**Barthes. [A** for the jury; **R** for the seminar attendance, which is dossier-only **]** Barthes is named in the campa on the strength of the jury, a direct relation; Mythologies as precursor of sign-value is a standard reading.

**May 1968. [A** SEP timeline, which lists his participation **]** **Gap, stated as such: no source gives him a leadership role, an arrest, an injury, or a movement membership.** None of the sources I fetched mentions May 1968 in connection with him beyond that one SEP line.

**Guattari, 1962. [R** dossier only **]** That Guattari and Baudrillard together helped found the **Association populaire franco-chinoise** could not be confirmed. It is nevertheless the only outlined *direct personal* relation between Baudrillard and any post-1968 Vincennes-Nanterre figure, so it is carried in the May 1968 campa, hedged, and flagged as a lead to verify. **Publications, 1968 through 1992. [A** SEP and Wikipedia concur on every year **]**

**Foucault. [A** SEP, Wikipedia for the 1977 polemic **]** *Oublier Foucault* is Baudrillard writing directly about Foucault, so Foucault is named. The riposte "the correct title would be Forget Baudrillard" is **[R]**: folklore, no primary Foucault text located.

**Martine "Marine" Dupuis. [A** Wikipedia **]** Met at Nanterre 1970; she 25, he 41. Shared domestic life only after roughly twenty years; married 1994, a minority of notices giving 1995 (unresolved). Her 2016 intertheory.org interview is the richest first-hand source on his private life and supplies the rue Sainte-Beuve address, Urbino, the illness chronology and the camera redating.

## 5. The American reception, 1983 onward

**Simulations, Semiotext(e), 1983. [A** archive.org/details/simulations0000baud **]** An editorial compilation assembled by **Sylvère Lotringer** from excerpts of several French books; no French volume has that title or those contents. The complete translation of *Simulacra and Simulation* follows only in **1994 [A** University of Michigan Press catalogue data, trans. Glaser; the UM Press page 404'd, so imprint and year are corroborated secondarily **]**. Eleven years separate the two, the most consequential single fact about his Anglophone reputation and why the Semiotext(e) volume gets a stop of its own.

**The Matrix, 1999. [A** Wikipedia **]** The Wachowskis credited *Simulacra and Simulation*; Neo hides money and disks in a hollowed copy on screen. **Baudrillard publicly disclaimed the connection.** The online rumour that he sued or demanded credit has no evidence behind it **[R]**.

## 6. Nanterre to Dauphine, 1986-1990

**[CORR] The worst error in the pool.** SEP's "retired from Nanterre in 1987" misstates the event: he left in **1986**, by **transfer**. fr.wikipedia gives "directeur scientifique, Paris-IX Dauphine, 1986-1990"; en.wikipedia gives the move to IRIS at Dauphine in 1986; criticalsecret gives habilitation 1986, four years there, and **1990** as the permanent exit from the university. All three **[A]**. The file anchors on 1986 and 1990, recording SEP's 1987 as a variant. **Gap:** no source gives a Dauphine coordinate; the file supplies the campus and says so.

## 7. The events, 1991-2001

**Gulf War essays, 1991. [A** SEP and Wikipedia for the year; the dossier gives the Libération dates as 4 January, 6 February, 29 March **]** The argument concerns the one-sided, pre-scripted, televised character of the campaign as a non-event for Western media audiences. **He explicitly discussed real destruction and real deaths**, so the popular paraphrase that he claimed nobody died is a misreading **[R** for the legend; the essay dates are well attested **]**. Norris's *Uncritical Theory* (1992) is the best-known hostile reply.

**Latin America, 1989 and 1991. [R** dossier only **]** Roughly ten Brazilian universities in 1989; a 1991 tour of Brazil, Argentina, Venezuela, Colombia, Chile. **Buenos Aires is the weakest-evidenced item in the file:** no source names an *Argentine city*, only the country, so the stop is a placement on the corpus canonical pin (-34.6037, -58.3816, inherited byte-identical from `borges.journey.json`), not a documented venue. The 1989 São Paulo stop was **dropped entirely** for the same reason, the pool having conceded its placement was "illustrative, not documented."

**Sarajevo, 8 January 1994. [A** for the essay in Libération **]** **[CORR] Three pool quotes withdrawn, one replaced.** All three were sourced to *Aperture* (1996 reprint), which could not be substantiated (paywalled; its search returns nothing for Baudrillard), and their wordings match **no published translation**. Two verified translations exist and differ materially throughout: Turner's in *The Perfect Crime* (Verso, 1996), used here, and Petterson's in *This Time We Knew* (NYU Press, 1996), named in `suggested_refs`.

**Contradiction, unresolved: the Libération column run.** SEP dates twice-monthly columns **June 1987 to May 1997**; other chronologies give **June 1995 to June 1996**, disagreeing on both endpoints and on the duration by an order of magnitude. Flagged at the *Écran total* stop, where the 1997 book and its quoted line are **[A** via radicalphilosophy.com **]** and its writing on AIDS and homosexuality is stated flat, not softened.

**9/11 essay. [A** fr.wikipedia gives the Le Monde publication as **3 November 2001 [CORR]** **]** SEP dates it 2 November; Le Monde's printed issues carry a cover date one day ahead of newsstand appearance, which is why both circulate, and the printed date is the citable one. Substance verified: the "absolute event" framing, the West both fearing and wishing the strike, no claim of foreknowledge or moral endorsement.

**Death. [A** en.wikipedia; fr.wikipedia ("Paris, 6e arrondissement, 6 March 2007"); SEP **]** At home on the **rue Sainte-Beuve**, aged 77. **[CORR] Street name:** the geography lens spelled it "Rue Saint-Beuve" throughout; the afterlife lens had it right. **[CORR] Two coordinates for one address:** geography `48.8443, 2.3288` against afterlife `48.8434, 2.3305`, some 200 m apart; the file uses the afterlife value at both the marriage and the death stops. **Contradiction, unresolved and material: when he fell ill.** Wikipedia dates the **cancer diagnosis to 2005**. **Marine Baudrillard**, the closest witness, dates onset to **around 2001**, then treatment, then spread into the nervous and circulatory systems, calling it a **four-year illness** and pointing to c. 2001-2003. ## Apparatus relations for the operator

**Jean Baudrillard is NOT listed in `EXCEPTIONS.md`.** No apparatus stops are licensed and none were written. Per the adversary's duty, the relations I caught are recorded rather than deleted, with the direction named.

**1. His framework applied FORWARD to social media, deepfakes, VR/AR and generative AI. Direction: FORWARD. Not licensed, not written.** The afterlife lens carries this as a reception fact **[R**, dossier synthesis, unverified against a named article **]**: since roughly the 2010s he has been credited with anticipating platform conditions through model-precession and the collapse of stable reference, and this "prophetic" reading is what revived his reputation after an early-2000s decline. He predicted no specific platform or architecture, and he died in 2007. This is the most obvious candidate for a forward grant in the queue, adjacent to the Derrida grant already recorded: his would cover the claim that the model precedes and produces the real it appears to describe. **Nothing of the kind is in the file.** If granted, the host is the 1981 *Simulacres et simulation* stop, one or two sentences, declared in `date_confidence` per the Burroughs scope ruling.

**2. Nick Land downstream of Baudrillard. Direction: FORWARD from this file. Not licensed, not written.** Land's theory-fiction and accelerationism run on the Baudrillard-adjacent vocabulary of simulation and hyperreality that reached English departments through Semiotext(e), with **no documented contact in either direction**. This is Land engaging Baudrillard, so it is Land's relation and belongs on Land's file. Recorded here, absent from the journey.
