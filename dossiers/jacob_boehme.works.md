## Works by Jacob Boehme

### Theosophical treatises and scriptural interpretations

- **1612–1613:** *Aurora, oder Morgenröte im Aufgang*. An unfinished theosophical-cosmological treatise and Böhme’s first work; it circulated in manuscript without his authorization. The title *Aurora* was supplied in the manuscript tradition, so even its familiar name is partly a later manifestation of the text. [International Jacob Böhme Society](https://www.jacob-boehme.org/de/Werke.html)

- **1618–1619:** *De tribus principiis, oder Beschreibung der drei Prinzipien göttlichen Wesens*. A large systematic treatise developing Böhme’s doctrine of three structuring principles; the standard collected edition dates it 1619, while chronologies commonly describe its composition as extending from 1618 into 1619. [Frommann-Holzboog](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/autoren/jacob_bohme), [Bruce Janz’s bibliography](https://jacobboehmeonline.com/assets/docs/JACOB_BOEHME-BIOGRAPHY_AND_LIST_OF_DOCUMENTS_BIOGRAPHY.35193551.pdf)

- **1619–1620:** *De triplici vita hominis, oder Vom dreifachen Leben des Menschen*. A substantial theosophical-anthropological treatise begun by Advent 1619 and reported complete in 1620; its subject is the interrelation of divine, psychic, and external human life. [International Jacob Böhme Society](https://www.jacob-boehme.org/de/Werke.html)

- **1620:** *Psychologia vera, oder Vierzig Fragen von der Seele*. A commissioned set of answers to questions posed by Balthasar Walther—less a continuous “book” than an extensive written interview about the soul. Its appended diagram and explanation, *Das umgewandte Auge*, make the object partly textual and partly visual. [International Jacob Böhme Society](https://www.jacob-boehme.org/de/Werke.html), [Frommann-Holzboog](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/autoren/jacob_bohme)

- **1620:** *De incarnatione verbi, oder Von der Menschwerdung Jesu Christi*. A three-part Christological treatise: on the incarnation, on Christ’s suffering, death, and resurrection, and on the “tree of faith.” The parts can look like separate works in catalogues, but the standard bibliography treats them as one tripartite obra. [Bruce Janz’s bibliography](https://jacobboehmeonline.com/assets/docs/JACOB_BOEHME-BIOGRAPHY_AND_LIST_OF_DOCUMENTS_BIOGRAPHY.35193551.pdf)

- **1620:** *Von sechs theosophischen Punkten*. A compact systematic treatise, also called the “Great Six Points,” condensing six central problems of Böhme’s theosophy; some chronologies place or complete it in 1621. [Bruce Janz’s bibliography](https://jacobboehmeonline.com/assets/docs/JACOB_BOEHME-BIOGRAPHY_AND_LIST_OF_DOCUMENTS_BIOGRAPHY.35193551.pdf), [International Jacob Böhme Society](https://www.jacob-boehme.org/de/Werke.html)

- **1620:** *Kurze Erklärung sechs mystischer Punkte*. A separate miniature glossary-like exposition, sometimes called the “Small Six Points”; it should not be collapsed into *Von sechs theosophischen Punkten*. [International Jacob Böhme Society](https://www.jacob-boehme.org/de/Werke.html)

- **1620:** *Gründlicher Bericht vom irdischen und himmlischen Mysterio* (*Mysterium Pansophicum*). A short pansophical treatise attempting to articulate the relation between the visible earthly world and its otherwise inexpressible heavenly ground. [International Jacob Böhme Society](https://www.jacob-boehme.org/de/Werke.html), [Bruce Janz’s bibliography](https://jacobboehmeonline.com/assets/docs/JACOB_BOEHME-BIOGRAPHY_AND_LIST_OF_DOCUMENTS_BIOGRAPHY.35193551.pdf)

- **1621–1622:** *De signatura rerum, oder Von der Geburt und Bezeichnung aller Wesen*. A natural-philosophical, medical, and theosophical treatise on “signatures,” correspondences, generation, corruption, and healing. Older chronologies date it 1621, whereas the standard German collected edition and specialist chronology give 1622; the dating should therefore remain qualified. [International Jacob Böhme Society](https://www.jacob-boehme.org/de/Werke.html), [Frommann-Holzboog](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/autoren/jacob_bohme)

- **1623:** *Von der Gnadenwahl*. A concentrated doctrinal treatise on election, grace, freedom, and predestination, written in response to a major confessional controversy. [International Jacob Böhme Society](https://www.jacob-boehme.org/de/Werke.html), [Frommann-Holzboog](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/autoren/jacob_bohme)

- **1623:** *Mysterium Magnum, oder Erklärung über das erste Buch Mosis*. Böhme’s longest work: a seventy-eight-chapter theosophical commentary on Genesis extending through the death of Joseph. It is simultaneously biblical exegesis, cosmology, anthropology, and a summation of his mature project. [International Jacob Böhme Society](https://www.jacob-boehme.org/de/Werke.html), [Frommann-Holzboog](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/autoren/jacob_bohme)

- **1624:** *Von Christi Testamenten*. A treatise in two parts, on baptism and the Lord’s Supper, treating the sacraments as Christ’s “testaments.” The surviving work exists in two versions and remains fragmentary in at least one respect, so it resists presentation as a single settled text. [International Jacob Böhme Society](https://www.jacob-boehme.org/de/Werke.html)

### Devotional protocols and transformative dialogues

- **1621:** *Von vier Complexionen*. A consolatory tract on fear, melancholy, anxiety, and the four temperaments; it functions less as speculative description than as spiritual therapy. [International Jacob Böhme Society](https://www.jacob-boehme.org/de/Werke.html)

- **1622:** *Von wahrer Buße*. A devotional protocol for repentance and the surrender of self-will; it later became one constituent of *Der Weg zu Christo*. [International Jacob Böhme Society](https://www.jacob-boehme.org/de/Werke.html)

- **1622:** *Von wahrer Gelassenheit*. A devotional protocol teaching resignation of the individual will to God; it is a practical spiritual exercise rather than merely a discussion of “serenity.” [International Jacob Böhme Society](https://www.jacob-boehme.org/de/Werke.html)

- **1622:** *Von der neuen Wiedergeburt*. A tract on spiritual regeneration or the “new birth,” intended to effect and guide transformation in the reader. [International Jacob Böhme Society](https://www.jacob-boehme.org/de/Werke.html)

- **1622–1624:** *Vom übersinnlichen Leben*. A short master-and-disciple dialogue about relinquishing selfhood and entering the life beyond sense. The specialist chronology assigns it to 1622, while older English bibliographies place it among the writings of 1624. [International Jacob Böhme Society](https://www.jacob-boehme.org/de/Werke.html), [Bailey, *Milton and Jakob Boehme*, bibliography](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a2/Milton_and_Jakob_Boehme%3B_a_study_of_German_mysticism_in_seventeenth-century_England_%28IA_miltonjakobboehm00bailiala%29.pdf)

- **1622–1624:** *Von göttlicher Beschaulichkeit*. A contemplative tract concerned with spiritual vision; it is unfinished. The standard German chronology dates it 1622, whereas older bibliographies group it with the unfinished writings of 1624. [International Jacob Böhme Society](https://www.jacob-boehme.org/de/Werke.html), [Bailey, *Milton and Jakob Boehme*, bibliography](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a2/Milton_and_Jakob_Boehme%3B_a_study_of_German_mysticism_in_seventeenth-century_England_%28IA_miltonjakobboehm00bailiala%29.pdf)

- **1623:** *Kurzer Begriff der Buße*. A brief compendium or epitome of Böhme’s teaching on repentance, designed as a practical condensation rather than a new systematic treatise. [Bailey, *Milton and Jakob Boehme*, bibliography](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a2/Milton_and_Jakob_Boehme%3B_a_study_of_German_mysticism_in_seventeenth-century_England_%28IA_miltonjakobboehm00bailiala%29.pdf)

- **1624:** *Epistola oder Sendebrief an eine hungrige und durstige Seele*. Despite its epistolary title and actual dispatch to Johann Sigismund von Schweinichen, this is a staged trialogue among a hungry soul, the devil, and an enlightened soul; it acts as spiritual drama and instruction, not merely correspondence. [International Jacob Böhme Society](https://www.jacob-boehme.org/de/Werke.html)

- **1624:** *Gebetbüchlein auf alle Tage in der Woche* (also *Die heilige Woche*). An unfinished prayer book organizing devotional practice across the week; its operative, repeatable instructions make it a liturgical protocol more than a discursive treatise. [International Jacob Böhme Society](https://www.jacob-boehme.org/de/Werke.html), [Bailey, *Milton and Jakob Boehme*, bibliography](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a2/Milton_and_Jakob_Boehme%3B_a_study_of_German_mysticism_in_seventeenth-century_England_%28IA_miltonjakobboehm00bailiala%29.pdf)

### Polemical and occasional writings

- **1620:** *Unterricht von den letzten Zeiten*. An eschatological instruction assembled from two letters to Paul Kaym. It resists treatment as an independent “book”: the standard bibliography preserves it as a work, but the same material also belongs to Böhme’s correspondence. [Bruce Janz’s bibliography](https://jacobboehmeonline.com/assets/docs/JACOB_BOEHME-BIOGRAPHY_AND_LIST_OF_DOCUMENTS_BIOGRAPHY.35193551.pdf), [Bailey, *Milton and Jakob Boehme*, bibliography](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a2/Milton_and_Jakob_Boehme%3B_a_study_of_German_mysticism_in_seventeenth-century_England_%28IA_miltonjakobboehm00bailiala%29.pdf)

- **1621:** *Erste Schutzschrift gegen Balthasar Tilke*. An apologia defending *Aurora* against Tilke’s now-lost hostile pamphlet; it is an intervention in a manuscript controversy rather than an autonomous exposition. [International Jacob Böhme Society](https://www.jacob-boehme.org/de/Werke.html)

- **1621:** *Zweite Schutzschrift gegen Balthasar Tilke*. An apologia responding to Tilke’s marginal notes or attached slips criticizing Böhme on incarnation and election. Tilke’s annotations have not survived, so the object is one surviving side of a lost textual exchange. [International Jacob Böhme Society](https://www.jacob-boehme.org/de/Werke.html)

- **1621:** *Bedenken über Esaias Stiefels Büchlein*. A critical response to Esaias Stiefel’s teaching on the threefold state and new birth; although often grouped among “apologies,” it is better understood as a dialogical theological memorandum. [International Jacob Böhme Society](https://www.jacob-boehme.org/de/Werke.html)

- **1622:** *Vom Irrtum der Sekten Esaias Stiefels und Ezechiel Meths*. A second, sharper intervention concerning Stiefel and Ezechiel Meth, completed in April 1622 and directed especially against claims of human perfection. [International Jacob Böhme Society](https://www.jacob-boehme.org/de/Werke.html)

- **1624:** *Apologia oder Schutzrede gegen Gregor Richter*. A polemical defense against Görlitz’s chief pastor Gregor Richter, especially concerning Böhme’s writings on repentance and resignation. Its purpose is performative and juridical—self-defense before a hostile religious public—as much as doctrinal. [International Jacob Böhme Society](https://www.jacob-boehme.org/de/Werke.html), [Bailey, *Milton and Jakob Boehme*, bibliography](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a2/Milton_and_Jakob_Boehme%3B_a_study_of_German_mysticism_in_seventeenth-century_England_%28IA_miltonjakobboehm00bailiala%29.pdf)

### Tables, keys, questionnaires, and epitomes

- **1623–1624:** *Tafeln der göttlichen Offenbarung* (also *Tafel der drei Welten*). A synoptic table or diagrammatic exposition of divine manifestation and the threefold world. It is not simply prose: its tabular form attempts to make Böhme’s cosmology spatially visible. [Bailey, *Milton and Jakob Boehme*, bibliography](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a2/Milton_and_Jakob_Boehme%3B_a_study_of_German_mysticism_in_seventeenth-century_England_%28IA_miltonjakobboehm00bailiala%29.pdf)

- **1623–1624:** *Tafeln der drei Prinzipien*. A diagrammatic synopsis of the three principles, accompanied in transmission by explanatory matter. The specialist chronology gives 1623/24, while older bibliographies assign it to 1624; the table is a visual-textual teaching instrument rather than an ordinary treatise. [International Jacob Böhme Society](https://www.jacob-boehme.org/de/Werke.html)

- **1624:** *Quaestiones theosophicae, oder Betrachtung göttlicher Offenbarung* (the “177 Theosophical Questions”). A questionnaire of 177 problems for clarifying Böhme’s teaching, with answers completed only through the thirteenth question. It is therefore an unfinished project whose planned architecture—not just its surviving answers—is the obra. [International Jacob Böhme Society](https://www.jacob-boehme.org/de/Werke.html), [Bailey, *Milton and Jakob Boehme*, bibliography](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a2/Milton_and_Jakob_Boehme%3B_a_study_of_German_mysticism_in_seventeenth-century_England_%28IA_miltonjakobboehm00bailiala%29.pdf)

- **1624:** *Kurzer Auszug oder Epitome des Mysterium Magnum*. A compressed recapitulation of the Genesis commentary, made as an orienting synopsis rather than as an independent cosmology. [Bailey, *Milton and Jakob Boehme*, bibliography](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a2/Milton_and_Jakob_Boehme%3B_a_study_of_German_mysticism_in_seventeenth-century_England_%28IA_miltonjakobboehm00bailiala%29.pdf)

- **1624:** *Clavis oder Schlüssel etlicher vornehmsten Punkte und Wörter*. A late explanatory key to Böhme’s characteristic terms and structures. It is a reader’s instrument—a protocol for navigating the other writings—rather than a freestanding statement of the system. [International Jacob Böhme Society](https://www.jacob-boehme.org/de/Werke.html)

### Composite publication

- **1624:** *Der Weg zu Christo*. Böhme’s first printed book, formed from shorter devotional writings including *Von wahrer Buße*, *Von wahrer Gelassenheit*, and related texts. It is a genuine made object as a collection, but not an additional independent tractate: counting both the volume and all its constituents without qualification would double-count the textual works. [Bruce Janz’s bibliography](https://jacobboehmeonline.com/assets/docs/JACOB_BOEHME-BIOGRAPHY_AND_LIST_OF_DOCUMENTS_BIOGRAPHY.35193551.pdf)

### Correspondence

- **1618–1624:** *Theosophische Sendbriefe*. The surviving corpus conventionally comprises sixty-two letters, including pieces also transmitted as prefaces or separate instructions. It emphatically resists being called one work: the corpus is incomplete, the incoming half of the correspondence is largely absent, and modern scholarship has identified errors introduced by manuscript and early printed transmission. [International Jacob Böhme Society](https://www.jacob-boehme.org/de/Werke.html), [Monash University research record](https://research.monash.edu/en/publications/the-broken-tradition-uncovering-errors-in-the-correspondence-of-j/)

### Lost project

- **1624:** *Vom jüngsten Gericht*. A work on the Last Judgment recorded in the early bibliographical tradition but now lost. Only its reported project and title survive, so it cannot securely be described as a completed text or assessed as an extant obra. [Bailey, *Milton and Jakob Boehme*, bibliography](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a2/Milton_and_Jakob_Boehme%3B_a_study_of_German_mysticism_in_seventeenth-century_England_%28IA_miltonjakobboehm00bailiala%29.pdf)

### Later objects not made by Boehme

- **1690:** *Idea chemiae Böhmianae adeptae*. An anonymous posthumous compilation of passages from Böhme, not a work authored by him; its association with his name records the manufacture of an alchemical “Boehme” by later readers. [Urszula Szulakowska-Zuber, *Spiritual Alchemy*](https://pure.uva.nl/ws/files/13086009/ZUBER_Spiritual_Alchemy_definitief.pdf)

- **1695:** *Metallurgia Böhmiana*. Another anonymous posthumous compilation, likewise not part of Böhme’s authored oeuvre despite its bibliographical attachment to him. [Urszula Szulakowska-Zuber, *Spiritual Alchemy*](https://pure.uva.nl/ws/files/13086009/ZUBER_Spiritual_Alchemy_definitief.pdf)

### Sources

- [Böhme, Jacob. *Sämtliche Schriften*. Facsimile reprint of the 1730 *Theosophia Revelata*, edited by Will-Erich Peuckert, 11 vols., Frommann-Holzboog, 1955–1989](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/autoren/jacob_bohme). The standard collected-works edition and principal basis of the list.

- [International Jacob Böhme Society, “Die Werke: Versuch einer Einführung”](https://www.jacob-boehme.org/de/Werke.html). Specialist chronological register keyed to the Peuckert facsimile of the 1730 collected edition, with bibliographical notes by Günther Bonheim.

- [Janz, Bruce B. *Jacob Boehme: Bibliography and List of Documents*, 3 March 2002](https://jacobboehmeonline.com/assets/docs/JACOB_BOEHME-BIOGRAPHY_AND_LIST_OF_DOCUMENTS_BIOGRAPHY.35193551.pdf). Identifies Peuckert’s edition as the nearest available equivalent to a critical collected edition and records Werner Buddecke’s editions of the autograph texts and manuscript catalogue.

- [Buddecke, Werner. *Die Urschriften*, 2 vols., Frommann, 1963–1966](https://jacobboehmeonline.com/assets/docs/JACOB_BOEHME-BIOGRAPHY_AND_LIST_OF_DOCUMENTS_BIOGRAPHY.35193551.pdf). Edition of surviving autograph texts; bibliographical details are reported in Janz’s scholarly bibliography.

- [Buddecke, Werner. *Verzeichnis von Jakob-Böhme-Handschriften*. Göttingen, 1934; and *Die Jakob-Böhme-Ausgaben*, 2 parts, 1937 and 1957](https://jacobboehmeonline.com/assets/docs/JACOB_BOEHME-BIOGRAPHY_AND_LIST_OF_DOCUMENTS_BIOGRAPHY.35193551.pdf). Standard manuscript and edition bibliographies used to distinguish works from later editions and translations.

- [Bailey, Margaret Lewis. *Milton and Jakob Boehme: A Study of German Mysticism in Seventeenth-Century England*. Oxford University Press, 1914, pp. 183–184](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a2/Milton_and_Jakob_Boehme%3B_a_study_of_German_mysticism_in_seventeenth-century_England_%28IA_miltonjakobboehm00bailiala%29.pdf). Early scholarly chronological bibliography preserving the unfinished and lost items and the traditional thirty-two-item enumeration.

- [Zuber, Mike A. *Spiritual Alchemy: From Jacob Boehme to Mary Anne Atwood*. Oxford University Press, 2021](https://pure.uva.nl/ws/files/13086009/ZUBER_Spiritual_Alchemy_definitief.pdf). Used to identify the anonymous posthumous alchemical compilations and prevent their attribution to Böhme.

- [Penman, Leigh T. I. “The Broken Tradition: Uncovering Errors in the Correspondence of Jacob Boehme.” *Reformation & Renaissance Review* 19, no. 3 (2017)](https://research.monash.edu/en/publications/the-broken-tradition-uncovering-errors-in-the-correspondence-of-j/). Used for the unsettled transmission and identification of Böhme’s letters.
