## Works by José Antonio de Aguirre y Lecube

### Books and sustained political narratives

- **1935–1936:** *Entre la libertad y la revolución, 1930-1935: la verdad de un lustro en el País Vasco*. Political memoir and documentary argument about the campaign for Basque autonomy; the title page bears 1935, while bibliographies also date the first edition to 1936, so the dating is not uniform. ([Obras completas, cited edition](https://dialnet.unirioja.es/servlet/autor?codigo=2509942); [scholarly bibliography](https://addi.ehu.es/bitstream/handle/10810/74219/67cadc0571b28.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1))

- **1937:** *El informe del presidente Aguirre al Gobierno de la República sobre los hechos que determinaron el derrumbamiento del frente del Norte*. An official explanatory report—part testimony, part institutional defence—written for the Republican government after the loss of northern Spain. It remained unpublished as a complete work until 1977; “Sancho de Beurko” (Luis Ruiz de Aguirre) was its later editor and annotator, not securely a co-author despite catalogues that sometimes list him as one. ([scholarly bibliography](https://addi.ehu.es/bitstream/handle/10810/74219/67cadc0571b28.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1); [study of the Sancho de Beurko archive](https://static.errenteria.eus/web/eu/herria/artxiboa/Bilduma/Bilduma%2012_1998/Bilduma12_199_1.pdf))

- **1941–1942; published 1943:** *De Guernica a Nueva York pasando por Berlín*. Autobiographical escape narrative, political testimony and anti-totalitarian argument, composed after Aguirre crossed occupied Europe under an assumed identity; completion is documented in May 1942, while the first Ekin edition appeared in Buenos Aires in 1943. Its documentary material overlaps with, but substantially reshapes, the private diary. ([Agirre in Berlin project](https://agirreinberlin.eus/de/von-gernika-nach-new-york-uber-berlin/); [1943 edition record](https://books.google.com/books/about/De_Guernica_a_Nueva_York_pasando_por_Ber.html?id=wC_PmlJoi6wC))

- **1942; published 1944:** *Cinco conferencias pronunciadas en un viaje por América*. A made sequence of five public lectures from Aguirre’s Latin American journey, subsequently organized as a book; it is therefore both an oratorical cycle and a printed political work, not simply a monograph. ([Gobierno Vasco historical study](https://www.euskadi.eus/contenidos/informacion/06_urazandi_coleccion/es_712/adjuntos/urazandi17.pdf); [bibliographical study](https://www.eusko-ikaskuntza.eus/PDFAnlt/riev/43/43117147.pdf))

- **1944:** *Guernica: recuerdo de un crimen franquista*. Political memorial pamphlet issued by the Basque delegation in Colombia. Its separate publication is a manifestation of Aguirre’s continuing account of Guernica rather than an entirely independent research work. ([Archivo Histórico de Euskadi bibliographical guide](https://www.artxibo.euskadi.eus/contenidos/informacion/eah_publicaciones/es_def/adjuntos/guia_documentales_biblio.pdf))

- **1945:** *Basque Thought at the Present*. English-language political essay published in *The Journal of Educational Sociology*; it condenses Aguirre’s democratic-Christian, anti-fascist and internationalist argument for Basque political personality. ([Gobierno Vasco, *Urazandi* 28](https://www.euskadi.eus/contenidos/informacion/06_urazandi_coleccion/es_712/adjuntos/urazandi28.pdf))

- **1956:** *Veinte años de gestión del Gobierno Vasco (1936-1956)*. A long retrospective address delivered at the World Basque Congress in Paris: simultaneously an institutional report, an apologia for the government in exile and a political speech-act asserting that government’s continuity. It was later circulated as a book, but its original kind is an official address. ([Basque Government exhibition bibliography](https://www.euskadi.eus/eusko-jaurlaritza/-/bibliografia-erakusketa/); [historical assessment](https://paralalibertad.org/aguirre-carisma-y-pragmatismo/))

### Unfinished historical project

- **By 1960; partially published 1965–1966:** *Historia del Pueblo Vasco*. An unfinished project for a multi-volume national history, not a completed book: at Aguirre’s death it comprised approximately 640 manuscript pages, mostly eight historical chapters reaching the Middle Ages plus one chapter of a projected juridical section. Its repeatedly revised medieval portion appeared posthumously as *Fin de la dinastía pirenaica: reinado de Sancho el Fuerte*; publication of that fragment must not be mistaken for completion of the projected history. ([2024 scholarly study and bibliography](https://addi.ehu.es/bitstream/handle/10810/74219/67cadc0571b28.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1); [documented account of the projected three volumes](https://es.scribd.com/document/441693146/urazandi28-pdf))

### Diary

- **7 January 1941–28 May 1942:** *Diario de Aguirre* / *Diario de José Antonio Agirre*. A private daily record kept during Aguirre’s flight through Germany and his first months in the Americas; it was not conceived as a public work and therefore resists being called one without qualification. The 1998 publication was made from a copy and publicly disauthorized by Aguirre’s heirs, while a later critical edition returned to the surviving diary evidence; sources sometimes give 23 rather than 28 May as the endpoint, but the facsimile and critical accounts support 28 May. ([critical edition](https://www.agirreinberlin.eus/wp-content/uploads/Edicion-Critica-Diario-Castellano.pdf); [report on the heirs’ objection](https://elpais.com/diario/1998/11/20/paisvasco/911594423_850215.html); [facsimile-edition report](https://www.deia.eus/politica/2010/05/26/aguirre-puno-letra-5648022.amp.html))

### Manifestos, messages and protocol-bound speech acts

- **7 October 1936:** *Juramento de Gernika* (oath on assuming office as lehendakari). A protocol-bound performative utterance: the words enact an office and obligation rather than merely describing them. The surviving sound recording is a manifestation of the oath, not a separate authored composition. ([Basque Government historical account](https://www.euskadi.eus/eusko-jaurlaritza/-/bibliografia-erakusketa/); [Archivo Histórico de Euskadi bibliographical guide](https://www.artxibo.euskadi.eus/contenidos/informacion/eah_publicaciones/es_def/adjuntos/guia_documentales_biblio.pdf))

- **1936–1940:** *Mensajes del Lendakari, 1936-1940*. A body of presidential radio addresses and public messages, including wartime and Christmas broadcasts; these are political interventions that act—reassuring, mobilizing and claiming governmental legitimacy—rather than a single book Aguirre planned under this title. The 1975 volume is a posthumous compilation and thus a later manifestation of the individual broadcasts. ([Archivo Histórico de Euskadi bibliographical guide](https://www.artxibo.euskadi.eus/contenidos/informacion/eah_publicaciones/es_def/adjuntos/guia_documentales_biblio.pdf); [contemporary notice of the compilation](https://www.eusko-ikaskuntza.eus/PDFFondo/irujo/26236.pdf))

- **1937:** *Tres discursos del Presidente Aguirre*. A contemporary pamphlet gathering three presidential speeches; the pamphlet is a bibliographical object, but the collected speeches—not their later binding together—are Aguirre’s made things. ([Archivo Histórico de Euskadi bibliographical guide](https://www.artxibo.euskadi.eus/contenidos/informacion/eah_publicaciones/es_def/adjuntos/guia_documentales_biblio.pdf))

- **21 December 1938:** *Discurso pronunciado por S. E. el Presidente del Gobierno Vasco José Antonio de Aguirre a los puestos de radio de Cataluña*. A radio address and wartime political act, issued separately in Spanish, French and English; those language issues are manifestations, not three different works. ([Archivo Histórico de Euskadi bibliographical guide](https://www.artxibo.euskadi.eus/contenidos/informacion/eah_publicaciones/es_def/adjuntos/guia_documentales_biblio.pdf))

- **1940–1945:** *Mensajes del Lendakari, 1940-1945*. A second posthumously defined corpus of broadcasts and presidential messages, published in 1976. As with the earlier series, the collective title belongs to the editor’s compilation; Aguirre made the constituent addresses in particular political circumstances. ([bibliographical study](https://www.eusko-ikaskuntza.eus/PDFAnlt/riev/43/43117147.pdf); [publication-history account](https://negritasycursivas.wordpress.com/2018/07/13/alberto-elosegui-y-ediciones-gudari-otro-ejemplo-de-edicion-vasca-en-caracas/))

### Dispersed writings and correspondence

- **1930–1960:** *Discursos, conferencias, alocuciones, declarations, articles, interviews, letters and governmental writings*. This is a body of work rather than one fixed-title obra: Aguirre continually made short political texts whose identity often lies in an occasion, addressee or broadcast date rather than a stable title. Martín Ugalde’s two-volume *Obras completas* (1981) is the standard collected-works edition used to establish this corpus, but “complete” means the texts then recovered and edited, not proof that every letter, speech or institutional document survives; individual government documents may also be collaborative and should not automatically be assigned to Aguirre alone. ([record for the collected works](https://dialnet.unirioja.es/servlet/autor?codigo=2509942); [Basque Government record](https://www.euskadi.eus/eusko-jaurlaritza/-/bibliografia-erakusketa/); [sample archival description of correspondence and propaganda material](https://pares.mcu.es/ParesBusquedas20/catalogo/description/6132501))
