## Works by Johann Heinrich Pestalozzi

### Fiction, dialogues, fables, and literary works

- **1766:** *Agis*. A political-historical drama and Pestalozzi’s first separately identifiable literary work; the critical edition treats it as a youthful writing rather than a mature pedagogical text.
- **1766:** *Wünsche*. A short youthful political-literary work preserved in the critical edition.
- **c. 1770:** *Criton und Thyrsis. Ein Gespräch über den Eid bei der Wahl des Zunftmeisters*. A dialogue about civic office and oath-taking; its date is uncertain in the critical edition.
- **1781–1787:** *Lienhard und Gertrud* (first version, four parts). A serialized social and educational novel—also conceived as a “book for the people”—in which narrative operates as a model of communal reform.
- **1782:** *Christoph und Else*. A didactic dialogue or “living-room lesson” in which two readers discuss *Lienhard und Gertrud*; it lies between fiction, commentary, and a protocol for domestic reading.
- **1790–1792:** *Lienhard und Gertrud* (second version, three completed parts). A substantial rewriting of the novel rather than a mere reprint; a projected fourth part and a draft chronicle of Bonnal remained unfinished.
- **1795–1797:** *Figuren zu meinem ABC-Buch*. A collection of prose fables, commonly shortened in later editions to *Fabeln*; several manuscript and printed states survive.
- **1799–1800:** *Siben Tag by Pfarrer Samuel*. An unfinished dialect narrative or dialogue whose unstable form resists classification as either fiction or pedagogical treatise.
- **1819–1820:** *Lienhard und Gertrud* (third version, four parts). Pestalozzi’s late rewriting for his collected writings; drafts survive for a fifth part whose completed text is lost.

### Diaries, autobiography, and self-interpretation

- **1769–1770:** *Tagebuch von Heinrich und Anna Pestalozzi*. A shared domestic diary and therefore not straightforwardly Pestalozzi’s sole-authored work.
- **1774:** *Tagebuch Pestalozzis über die Erziehung seines Sohnes*. An observational diary and educational experiment conducted through the upbringing of his son; it is both record and working protocol.
- **1799; published 1807:** *Über den Aufenthalt in Stanz* (“Stanser Brief”). An autobiographical account of the Stans orphanage framed as a letter. The original is lost, the addressee is disputed, and the surviving text reflects later publication and revision.
- **1801–1802:** *Wie Gertrud ihre Kinder lehrt*. Fourteen letters presenting and defending Pestalozzi’s educational method; despite its epistolary fiction, it is a systematic pedagogical manifesto.
- **1821–1826:** *Meine Lebensschicksale als Vorsteher meiner Erziehungsinstitute in Burgdorf und Iferten*. An institutional autobiography and polemical account of the conflicts surrounding his schools.
- **1826:** *Pestalozzi’s Schwanengesang*. A late composite publication joining autobiography, retrospective argument, and a final exposition of elementary education; it is not a single-genre “book.”

### Political, social, and philosophical writings

- **1778–1779?:** *Der Wert der Landessitten*. A social and political essay; the critical edition leaves its dating uncertain.
- **1778–1779?:** *Wenn ist der Zustand in der Societet besser als der im Wald?* A fragmentary inquiry into society and the state of nature; both the wording and date remain editorially uncertain.
- **1779:** *Von der Freyheit meiner Vaterstatt!* A political address on Zürich’s constitution and civic liberty.
- **1780:** *Abhandlung über die Frage: In wie fern ist es schicklich dem Aufwande der Bürger … Schranken zu setzen?* A prize essay on regulating luxury in a commercial republic.
- **c. 1780:** *Fragment über den Aufwand der Reichen*. An unfinished political-economic essay; it resists treatment as a completed work.
- **written 1780; published 1783:** *Über Gesezgebung und Kindermord. Wahrheiten und Träume, Nachforschungen und Bilder*. A social-legal inquiry into infanticide that combines evidence, argument, imagined scenes, and proposals for reform.
- **1782:** *Memorial ad „Über Verbrechen und Straffen“*. A memorandum on crime and punishment.
- **1782:** *Memoire über Eigentum und Verbrechen*. A political-philosophical memorandum connecting property relations with criminality.
- **1783:** *Fragment über den Stand der Natur und der Gesellschaft*. An unfinished philosophical investigation rather than a settled treatise.
- **1784:** *Particularschreiben … die Saz und Ordnungen E. L. Ehegerichts der Stadt Zürich betreffend*. A public-intervention letter concerning Zürich’s marriage-court regulations; its function is closer to a petition or manifesto than to descriptive prose.
- **1785:** *Memorial über das französische Prohibitions-Arret vom 10. Juli 1785*. A policy memorandum on French trade restrictions.
- **1785–1786:** *Bemerkungen zu gelesenen Büchern*. Reading notes and extracts; they are working documents and resist being called a finished work.
- **1786–1787?:** *Über die Entstehung der sittlichen Begriffe in der Entwicklung der Menschheit*. A philosophical draft on the formation of moral concepts; its dating is uncertain.
- **1790:** *Mémoire über die Verbindung der Berufsbildung mit der Volksschule*. A reform proposal connecting vocational work with popular schooling.
- **1792–1793; first published 1897:** *Ja oder Nein? Äußerungen über die bürgerliche Stimmung der europäischen Menschheit … von einem freien Mann*. A political manifesto responding to the French Revolution; it remained unpublished during Pestalozzi’s lifetime.
- **1797:** *Meine Nachforschungen über den Gang der Natur in der Entwicklung des Menschengeschlechts*. A philosophical anthropology tracing “natural,” social, and moral existence; extensive preparatory drafts survive and should not be confused with the published work.
- **1798–1799:** *Political writings of the Helvetic Republic*. A large, internally diverse corpus of proclamations, memoranda, newspaper interventions, constitutional reflections, and appeals—including *An Helvetiens Volk* and the *Zehntenblätter*. Treating it as one work is imperfect, but many constituent pieces are anonymous, occasional, fragmentary, or editorially titled.
- **1815:** *An die Unschuld, den Ernst und den Edelmuth meines Zeitalters und meines Vaterlandes*. A public polemic and manifesto defending Pestalozzi’s educational undertaking during the crisis at Yverdon.
- **1823:** *Langenthaler Rede*. A public address to the Swiss Society of Education, combining autobiography, educational argument, and exhortation.

### Educational theory, protocols, and teaching materials

- **1775?:** *Ideen zu einem christlichen Lied für eine Arbeitsstube meistens armer Kinder*. A draft for a communal working-room hymn; it is an unrealized project rather than a securely finished song.
- **1777–1778:** *Aufsätze über die Armenanstalt auf dem Neuhofe*. A cluster of appeals, reports, and letters describing and soliciting support for the Neuhof institution, including *Eine Bitte an Menschenfreunde und Gönner*, *Briefe … über die Erziehung der armen Landjugend*, *Bruchstück aus der Geschichte der niedrigsten Menschheit*, and *Zuverlässige Nachricht von der Erziehungs-Anstalt*. The cluster acts upon the institution by recruiting money and legitimacy.
- **1779–1780:** *Entwurf zu der Abendstunde eines Einsiedlers*. A preparatory version whose survival makes the “work” visibly a process.
- **1779–1780:** *Die Abendstunde eines Einsiedlers*. A sequence of aphoristic reflections on human formation, society, religion, and education; it is neither a conventional narrative nor a systematic treatise.
- **1799:** *Die Sprache als Fundament der Kultur*. Drafts and a fragment on language and human formation; no completed work survives.
- **1800:** *Die Methode*. A memorandum presenting Pestalozzi’s emerging instructional system; a fragmentary parallel version also survives.
- **1801–1803:** *Elementarbücher and exercises of the Burgdorf circle*. A collaborative body of primers and teaching protocols generated by Pestalozzi and his assistants. Individual authorship is often mixed, so “Pestalozzi’s method” is more secure than sole attribution of every exercise.
- **1803:** *Buch der Mütter, oder Anleitung für Mütter, ihre Kinder bemerken und reden zu lehren*. A domestic teaching manual and protocol of observation and language instruction, produced with substantial involvement from Pestalozzi’s collaborators; sole authorship should not be assumed.
- **1803:** *ABC der Anschauung, oder Anschauungs-Lehre der Maßverhältnisse* (two fascicles). A geometry and visual-perception teaching instrument, preceded by surviving drafts and created within the collaborative Burgdorf institute.
- **c. 1803:** *Über fremdsprachlichen Unterricht*. A pedagogical study of language teaching.
- **1803:** *Gedanken über Volkserziehung auf dem Lande*. A memorandum addressed to Countess Charlotte Schimmelmann on rural popular education.
- **1803:** *Auseinandersetzung mit Johann Rudolf Steinmüller*. Notes, fragments, and a fuller polemical essay; the critical edition preserves multiple compositional stages rather than one stable text.
- **1803–1804:** *Der natürliche Schulmeister*. A practical instructional manual for foundational teaching before age six; its dating is approximate.
- **1803–1804:** *Fragment über die Grundlagen der Bildung*. Two surviving versions of an unfinished theoretical work.
- **1805:** *Geist und Herz in der Methode*. A pedagogical argument that refuses to reduce education to instructional technique.
- **October 1805:** *Zweck und Plan einer Armen-Erziehungs-Anstalt*. A plan and operating proposal: a project in the strict sense, intended to bring an institution into existence.
- **late 1805:** *Aufruf für die Armenanstalt*. A fundraising appeal whose performative purpose makes it a manifesto as much as an explanatory text.
- **early 1806:** *Gutachten über ein Seminar im Kanton Waadt*. An expert report and institutional design for teacher education.
- **spring 1806:** *Grundzüge der Töchteranstalt*. A program for the Yverdon girls’ institute; it is partly a protocol for an institution implemented with other educators.
- **1806:** *Über Volksbildung und Industrie*. A social-pedagogical essay connecting elementary education, poverty, and productive work.
- **c. 1806:** *Ein Gespräch über Volksaufklärung und Volksbildung*. A pedagogical dialogue.
- **c. 1806:** *Tischgebet*. A short devotional text embedded in the daily life of an educational community.
- **1805–1807:** *Ansichten und Erfahrungen, die Idee der Elementarbildung betreffend*. A body of interconnected reflections on elementary education, assembled from texts written across several years rather than conceived as one uniform treatise.
- **1807:** *Journal für die Erziehung*. A pedagogical periodical and collective institutional manifestation. It included material by Pestalozzi and collaborators and was also intended to support a projected new edition of *Wie Gertrud ihre Kinder lehrt* that remained unfinished.
- **1809:** *Bericht an die Eltern und an das Publikum über den gegenwärtigen Zustand und die Einrichtungen der Pestalozzischen Anstalt in Iferten*. An institutional report, prospectus, and act of public accountability; collaborators participated in the institute and its published self-description.
- **1809–1810:** *Über die Idee der Elementarbildung* (“Lenzburger Rede”). A public lecture and programmatic statement, not simply a book.
- **c. 1810:** *Schriften über Körperbildung und Turnen*. Plans and reflections on physical education associated with the Yverdon curriculum; their manuscript states and collaborative setting resist reduction to a single finished work.
- **1816–1818:** *Writings on industry, poverty, and education*. Memoranda and proposals seeking to integrate productive labor with elementary schooling; these are institutional projects as well as texts.
- **1826:** *Versuch einer Skizze über das Wesen der Elementarbildung*. A late theoretical sketch; the title itself marks it as provisional rather than complete.

### Newspapers, serials, and editorial undertakings

- **1782:** *Ein Schweizer-Blatt*. A weekly newspaper conceived, edited, and largely written by Pestalozzi. Its individual numbers contain many separately titled and untitled pieces, so the serial is simultaneously one obra and a composite body of works.
- **1807:** *Wochenschrift für Menschenbildung*. A collaborative periodical associated with the Yverdon institute; Pestalozzi contributed to it, but attribution of the whole journal to him alone would be false.
- **1819–1826:** *Pestalozzi’s sämmtliche Schriften* (15 volumes). A self-curated collected edition containing revised versions of earlier works and new late writings. Because Pestalozzi extensively rewrote many texts, it is also a late authorial project rather than merely a publisher’s reprint.

### Educational, agricultural, and social obras

- **1769–c. 1773:** *Neuhof agricultural enterprise*. A model-farm project at Birr intended to unite agricultural improvement, household economy, and social reform; it failed commercially but remained the physical base of Pestalozzi’s later undertakings.
- **1773/1774–1780:** *Neuhof poor children’s institute*. A residential educational and industrial community in which poor children learned while farming, spinning, and weaving. It was created with Anna Pestalozzi and others, so it resists sole authorship and the separation of pedagogy, architecture, labor, household, and social intervention.
- **December 1798–June 1799:** *Stans orphanage*. An emergency residential community for children affected by war. It was state-authorized, short-lived, and terminated when the premises were requisitioned; its “work” survives primarily as practice and retrospective testimony.
- **1799–1800:** *Burgdorf experimental elementary classes*. Classroom experiments through which Pestalozzi and his associates developed “elementary education”; the method was made collectively through teaching rather than authored as a single text.
- **1800–1804:** *Burgdorf institute*. A combined boarding school, experimental school, teacher-training establishment, and orphan provision in Burgdorf Castle. It approaches a Gesamtkunstwerk of curriculum, communal life, printed manuals, observation, and teacher formation.
- **1804:** *Münchenbuchsee institute*. A short-lived transfer and attempted partnership with Philipp Emanuel von Fellenberg. Because control and authorship were divided and the arrangement rapidly broke down, calling it simply “Pestalozzi’s work” obscures its contested institutional character.
- **1804/1805–1825:** *Yverdon institute for boys*. Pestalozzi’s largest educational manifestation: boarding school, experimental laboratory, teacher seminary, international demonstration site, household, and publishing center. It was collectively made by pupils, family members, teachers, and administrators and cannot responsibly be treated as his sole creation.
- **1806 onward:** *Yverdon institute for girls*. A related but separately organized institution directed initially by Rosette Kasthofer; Pestalozzi promoted and helped frame it, but operational authorship belonged substantially to its women educators.
- **1813 onward:** *Yverdon work with deaf pupils*. An experiment conducted principally by Johann Konrad Naef within Pestalozzi’s institutional orbit. It is a manifestation of the broader project, not securely an obra made by Pestalozzi himself.
- **1818–c. 1819:** *Clendy poor school*. A short-lived poor-school foundation near Yverdon, later absorbed into the castle institute. Like the earlier institutions, it joined residence, labor, instruction, and teacher training.
- **1825–1827:** *Projected renewed poor institute at Neuhof*. A final attempt to return to the Neuhof project after Yverdon’s collapse; it remained incompletely realized at Pestalozzi’s death and is therefore a project, not a completed institution.

### Correspondence as a body of work

- **1760s–1827:** *Letters by Johann Heinrich Pestalozzi*. More than a sequence of private documents, the correspondence includes love letters, institutional directions, petitions, pedagogical arguments, fundraising appeals, and public letters. The critical edition occupies fourteen volumes; thousands of individual letters survive, while losses and uncertain datings prevent the corpus from being treated as perfectly complete.
- **1767–1769:** *Courtship correspondence with Anna Schulthess*. A jointly produced epistolary relationship and documentary obra; only Pestalozzi’s side can be attributed to him, while the exchange itself belongs to both correspondents.
- **Throughout his institutional years:** *Circulars, petitions, reports, and appeals*. Hundreds of occasional documents preserved among the works and letters acted directly upon schools, governments, patrons, parents, and colleagues. Many have only editorial titles, uncertain dates, or shared authorship and therefore resist conversion into a tidy list of autonomous “works.”
