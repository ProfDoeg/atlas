## Works by José López Rega

### Esoteric manuals and cosmological texts

- **1957; printed 1961:** *Conocimientos espirituales*. Esoteric initiation manual with an autobiographical conclusion, completed at Easter 1957 and printed by Claufer in Porto Alegre four years later. Contemporary testimony says Victoria Montero tore up the manuscript presented to her, so the relationship between that destroyed copy and the published text is not completely documented. ([Larraquy, pp. 49–53, 268, 276](https://condor-atlanta.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/39-lopez-rega-el-peronismo-y-la-triple-A.pdf))

- **After 1957; publication status unknown:** *El hombre, un mundo desconocido*. Esoteric-autobiographical manuscript begun after Montero rejected *Conocimientos espirituales*; Larraquy describes at least three chapters, but no evidence of a distributed edition. It is therefore a work or substantial project, not merely an advertised title. ([Larraquy, pp. 51–52](https://condor-atlanta.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/39-lopez-rega-el-peronismo-y-la-triple-A.pdf))

- **1960:** *Zodíaco multicolor*. The separately published Brazilian first part of what became *Astrología esotérica*, illustrated by Norma López Rega and Héctor Prieto Roca. Its status resists a simple “book” classification: it is both an autonomous publication and an earlier state of a larger obra. ([Larraquy, p. 79](https://condor-atlanta.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/39-lopez-rega-el-peronismo-y-la-triple-A.pdf))

- **1962:** *Astrología esotérica: secretos develados*. Large illustrated manual joining astrology to colours, perfumes, plants, music, dance and bodily correspondences; López Rega intended it as a practical daily guide. Larraquy dates its appearance to November 1962, although one later scholarly bibliography gives 1961. ([Larraquy, pp. 77–79](https://condor-atlanta.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/39-lopez-rega-el-peronismo-y-la-triple-A.pdf); [Academia Nacional de la Historia catalogue record](https://biblioteca.anh.org.ar/cgi-bin/koha/opac-detail.pl?biblionumber=53663&shelfbrowse_itemnumber=52864); [UNLP bibliography giving 1961](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/90689/Documento_completo.pdf-PDFA.pdf%3Fsequence%3D1%26isAllowed%3Dy))

- **1963:** *Alpha y Omega: un mensaje para la humanidad*. Esoteric revelation or manifestation presented as teachings communicated by the archangel Gabriel, rather than as ordinary discursive authorship; it combines Christian, astrological and occult cosmology. ([Asciutto et al., *Negocios y dictadura*](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Julieta_Mira/publication/342572115_Negocios_y_Dictadura_La_conexion_argentino-italiana/links/5efba551299bf18816f5e7a5/Negocios-y-Dictadura-La-conexion-argentino-italiana.pdf); [Page discussion reproduced by Encuentro Tres de Julio](https://encuentrotresdejulio.blogspot.com/2020/05/diario-de-la-cuarentena-dia-42.html))

- **1965:** *Astro Agenda: primera agenda astrológica*. Calendar-protocol calculated for the days of 1965, combining the calendar of saints and planetary positions with purportedly favourable hours and short practical instructions. It is an agenda and divinatory instrument, not a conventional book. ([Pignatelli, “La astroagenda de López Rega”](https://www.infobae.com/sociedad/2021/02/21/la-astroagenda-de-lopez-rega-horoscopos-consejos-esotericos-y-un-encuentro-que-cambio-la-historia/))

- **1968:** *Astromondas* [title uncertain]. An illustrated text registered in Spain as López Rega’s own text and illustrations and reproduced by Reprográficas Royal; the surviving official OCR reads “Astromodss/Astromondas,” so the exact title and the object’s contents remain uncertain. The registration proves a made object but not general publication or circulation. ([Spanish *Boletín Oficial del Estado*, registry item 144.046](https://www.boe.es/boe/dias/1969/10/11/pdfs/C00001-00045.pdf))

- **1977; sometimes dated 1972:** *El sabio hindú*. Cycle of eight esoteric tales containing an interpolated autobiography and a master-disciple narrative. Hernán Facundo López inspected a 1977 edition and notes that Larraquy misnames it *El sabio y el hindú*; claims of a 1972 publication remain insufficiently substantiated. ([Hernán Facundo López, “José López Rega, ‘El Brujo’: producción esotérica y violencia política”](https://pdfcoffee.com/jose-lopez-rega-el-brujo-produccion-esoterica-y-violencia-politica-en-la-decada-del-setenta-pdf-free.html))

### Dramatic work

- **1965:** *Preguntas en la noche: comedia psicológica musical*. Printed dramatic text combining dialogue, music and an esoteric initiation plot; its subtitle makes it a musical-psychological comedy rather than an occult treatise. No evidence located establishes that it was staged. ([Larraquy, pp. 80–81, 268, 311–12](https://condor-atlanta.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/39-lopez-rega-el-peronismo-y-la-Triple-A.pdf))

### Political and autobiographical texts

- **1973:** *La Doctrina Justicialista*. Programmatic article published in *Las Bases*; as a statement defining political doctrine, it approaches a manifesto rather than merely describing one. ([Ramos, “A la derecha de Dios”](https://portal.amelica.org/ameli/journal/293/2934220010/movil/))

- **1974:** *La Hora de la Verdad*. Political intervention published in *Las Bases* on 1 October 1974. Its setting and declarative purpose make it a manifesto-like public act, although it is also an article. ([Ramos, “A la derecha de Dios”](https://portal.amelica.org/ameli/journal/293/2934220010/movil/))

- **1975:** *El idioma de la verdad: el pensamiento y la palabra del Señor Ministro de Bienestar Social de la Nación, Don José López Rega, 1973–1974*. Ministry-issued compilation of López Rega’s speeches, political interpretations and autobiographical self-defence. Because it collects ministerial utterances under an institutional title, it is better classified as an authorized political corpus than as a wholly new, continuously authored book. ([Krmpotic, bibliography to “Las formas estatales de las políticas sociales en la Argentina”](https://cdsa.aacademica.org/000-071/421.pdf); [Ramos, “A la derecha de Dios”](https://portal.amelica.org/ameli/journal/293/2934220010/movil/))

- **1986; unpublished:** *Second part of El idioma de la verdad* [no secure separate title]. Manuscript written while López Rega was detained in the United States, defending his political record and discussing Libya, Perón, Isabel Perón and the recovery of Eva Perón’s body. Its reported survival makes it an unfinished or unpublished project; it should not be silently promoted into a published book. ([Pignatelli, “Exclusivo, el libro inédito de López Rega”](https://www.infobae.com/historia/2019/03/02/exclusivo-el-libro-inedito-de-lopez-rega-profecias-diatribas-contra-la-izquierda-y-su-relacion-con-peron-e-isabelita/?outputType=amp-type))

### Announced projects with no verified completed object

- **Announced by 1962:** *Génesis de la nueva era*. Project advertised as “in preparation”; no evidence located verifies completion or distribution, so the project—not a presumed missing book—is the obra that can safely be named. ([Recorded list of López Rega’s advertised titles](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_L%C3%B3pez_Rega))

- **Announced by 1962:** *Tratado de canto, impostación y arte escénico*. Proposed protocol or instructional treatise drawing on López Rega’s vocal training; no completed or distributed text has been verified. ([Recorded list of López Rega’s advertised titles](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_L%C3%B3pez_Rega))

- **Announced by 1962:** *El libro de los desheredados*. Advertised project for which neither a surviving manuscript nor publication has been established. It resists being called a work except insofar as an announced intention is itself part of López Rega’s self-fashioned oeuvre. ([Recorded list of López Rega’s advertised titles](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_L%C3%B3pez_Rega))

- **Announced by 1962:** *Libro madre del éxito: predicciones y guía diaria válida hasta el año 2000*. Proposed predictive guide or protocol; no evidence located shows that the advertised project became a completed object. ([Recorded list of López Rega’s advertised titles](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_L%C3%B3pez_Rega))

- **Announced in 1965:** *Astrofinanzas*. Title promised as a forthcoming practical astrological study, but no completed text or edition has been verified. ([Contemporary material reproduced in “López Rega y la Triple A”](https://contexthistorizar.blogspot.com/2009/07/lopez-rega-y-la-triple.html))

### Political and architectural constructions

- **1973:** *Alianza Anticomunista Argentina (Triple A)*. Clandestine parapolice organization and apparatus of political violence gestated within a broader repressive network; Larraquy identifies López Rega as its commander, but later scholarship disputes the reductive account in which it was solely his creation or merely obeyed his personal designs. It is a made organization, but calling a death squad an “obra” is ethically resistant and must not aestheticize its crimes. ([Larraquy, *López Rega: el peronismo y la Triple A*](https://books.google.com/books?id=PtZyDwAAQBAJ); [López de la Torre, “La Alianza Anticomunista Argentina”](https://dialnet.unirioja.es/servlet/articulo?codigo=7680453); [Rostica, “Apuntes ‘Triple A’”](https://doi.org/10.12804/revistas.urosario.edu.co/desafios/a.1801))

- **1973:** *Árbol de Navidad del Obelisco*. Temporary public Gesamtkunstwerk commissioned through the Ministry of Bienestar Social: the Obelisk became the structure for an illuminated monumental tree accompanied by religious ritual, provincial displays, food distribution and a public entertainment program. It was institutionally and collectively constructed, so López Rega was its political author and promoter, not its sole fabricator. ([Archivo Prisma, surviving newsreel description](https://www.archivorta.com.ar/asset/peron-isabel-y-lopez-rega-inauguran-un-arbol-navideno-en-el-obelisco-1973/); [Larraquy, pp. 154–55](https://condor-atlanta.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/39-lopez-rega-el-peronismo-y-la-Triple-A.pdf))

- **1974–1975; unfinished and later demolished:** *Altar de la Patria*. State-sponsored monumental mausoleum project intended to gather the remains of national figures including San Martín, Eva Perón and Juan Perón. López Rega conceived and promoted it, while engineer Juan Carlos Basile received the construction commission; Parliament authorized it by law, but the larger project was never completed. ([Larraquy, pp. 184–86](https://condor-atlanta.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/39-lopez-rega-el-peronismo-y-la-Triple-A.pdf); [“El sueño incumplido del brujo”](https://www.lacapital.com.ar/cultura-y-libros/el-sueno-incumplido-del-brujo-n2569318.html/amp))

### Performances without an attributable composed work

- **Circa 1951:** *Radio song performances* [individual titles unrecorded]. López Rega sang tangos, boleros, Spanish and Italian songs on radio, accompanied by the station guitarist Jiménez; these were performances of repertoire, not demonstrated compositions, and the consulted biography supplies no reliable titles. They are manifestations of a performing practice rather than authored musical works. ([Larraquy, biographical account](https://www.penguinlibros.com/es/tematicas/146404-ebook-lopez-rega-9789500762182/fragmento))
