## Works by Elsa Gindler

### Pedagogical notebooks and course records

- **1912:** *Übungsheft*. A training notebook made while Gindler studied with Hede Kallmeyer; it is the earliest item in Edith von Arps-Aubert’s scholarly edition of Gindler’s surviving papers. It records received and tested exercises rather than a finished public work. [Edition contents](https://d-nb.info/1004026242/04)

- **17 October 1927–16 February 1928:** *Notizen zum Kurs vom 17.10.1927–16.2.1928*. Notes for a course or working community: documentary traces of a changing pedagogical event, not a self-contained protocol or book. [Edition contents](https://d-nb.info/1004026242/04)

- **19 October 1927–4 February 1928:** *Notizen zum Kurs vom 19.10.1927–4.2.1928*. A second set of course notes from the same season; its status is that of working documentation rather than a polished text. [Edition contents](https://d-nb.info/1004026242/04)

- **24 October 1934–27 May 1935:** *Notizen zum Einführungskurs vom 24.10.1934–27.5.1935*. Notes documenting an introductory course and the experiments performed within it. The course itself was collectively enacted, while the surviving written object is Gindler’s working record. [Edition contents](https://d-nb.info/1004026242/04)

- **28 November 1934–27 March 1935:** *Notizen zum Atemkurs vom 28.11.1934–27.3.1935*. Notes from a course concerned with breathing; neither a fixed exercise system nor a publication, but a record of situated investigation. [Edition contents](https://d-nb.info/1004026242/04)

- **16 October 1936–10 February 1937:** *Notizen zum Kurs vom 16.10.1936–10.2.1937*. Course notes surviving as evidence of an improvised pedagogical process. Von Arps-Aubert reconstructs this course under the editorial rubric “Liegen oder Ruhen,” which should not be mistaken for a title supplied by Gindler. [Edition and reconstruction contents](https://d-nb.info/1004026242/04)

- **17 December 1940–25 March 1941:** *Notizen zum Kurs vom 17.12.1940–25.3.1941*. Working notes for a wartime course, preserved and edited posthumously rather than issued by Gindler as a work. [Edition contents](https://d-nb.info/1004026242/04)

- **17 October 1946–24 March 1947:** *Notizen zum Kurs vom 17.10.1946–24.3.1947*. Notes documenting a postwar working community; the surviving text records a process whose full obra also comprised bodies, speech, objects, and shared experiments. [Edition contents](https://d-nb.info/1004026242/04)

- **19 February–11 March 1952:** *Notizen zum Kurs vom 19.2.–11.3.1952*. A short sequence of course notes, unpublished in Gindler’s lifetime and inseparable from the teaching event for which it was made. [Edition contents](https://d-nb.info/1004026242/04)

- **14 January–25 March 1955:** *Notizen zum Kurs vom 14.1.–25.3.1955*. Working documentation of a course rather than a literary composition or standardized teaching protocol. [Edition contents](https://d-nb.info/1004026242/04)

- **13–21 July 1959:** *Notizen zum Sommerferienkurs vom 13.7.–21.7.1959*. Notes from a summer working community. Von Arps-Aubert reconstructs this course as an inquiry into the “basic conditions of functioning”; that phrase is an analytic description, not securely a title given by Gindler. [Edition contents](https://d-nb.info/1004026242/04)

- **25 July–5 August 1960:** *Notizen zum Ferienkurs vom 25.7.–5.8.1960*. Notes from Gindler’s last course. They document an event-based practice and therefore resist identification with the course itself. [Edition contents](https://d-nb.info/1004026242/04)

### Lectures, articles, and lecture manuscripts

- **circa 1925:** *Ja, nein und trotzdem*. A lecture manuscript, its date explicitly approximate in the scholarly edition; the quotation marks used there may indicate an archival or editorial title rather than a formally published one. [Edition contents](https://d-nb.info/1004026242/04)

- **1926:** *Die Gymnastik des Berufsmenschen*. A public lecture delivered at the Düsseldorf GeSoLei exhibition and then printed in *Gymnastik*, nos. 5/6. It was Gindler’s only publication during her lifetime; later English and German appearances are reprints or translations, not additional works. [Contemporary-publication context](https://pbc.gda.pl/Content/93556/1928_1929.pdf) [Scholarly account and English reprint](https://www.ibpj.org/issues/archive/Vol3No1%20USABP%20Journal%202004.pdf)

- **1931:** *Gymnastik als Erwachsenenbildung*. A lecture for the general assembly of the Deutscher Gymnastikbund, surviving in draft or manuscript form and published only later. The title appears in the scholarly edition, while other sources describe it simply as Gindler’s 1931 general-assembly lecture. [Edition contents](https://d-nb.info/1004026242/04) [Specialist study](https://www.fachportal-paedagogik.de/literatur/vollanzeige.html?FId=3211595)

- **Date unknown:** *Die Frau zwischen 30 und 50*. A lecture text included in the scholarly edition; the accessible edition contents supply no date, so none can safely be assigned. [Edition contents](https://d-nb.info/1004026242/04)

- **circa 1946 or 1949:** *Neue Aufgaben der Körpererziehung*. A lecture manuscript concerning movement and education. Von Arps-Aubert dates it approximately 1946, whereas the archive-holding Heinrich Jacoby–Elsa Gindler Foundation describes the manuscript as dating from 1949; the discrepancy remains unresolved here. [Scholarly edition](https://d-nb.info/1004026242/04) [Foundation publication record](https://www.jgstiftung.de/publikationen)

- **1950s:** *Natürliches Heilwesen*. An incomplete lecture manuscript or fragment; both the decade and the title are presented cautiously in the scholarly edition. Its unfinished state is part of the object, not a defect to be silently repaired. [Edition contents](https://d-nb.info/1004026242/04)

### Recorded pedagogical manifestations

- **1958–1960:** *Sind wir erreichbar? Aus ihren Berliner Ferien-Arbeitsgemeinschaften 1958–1960*. A posthumously assigned title for transcribed tape records, accompanied in the 2019 edition by original recordings. The obra is not a book authored in 2019: it is a set of spoken, collective course manifestations made in 1958–60 and subsequently transcribed and edited. [Foundation publication record](https://www.jgstiftung.de/publikationen) [Archive description](https://stiftungsarchive.de/archive/322)

### Institutions and collective projects

- **1917 onward:** *Seminar für Harmonische Körperausbildung*. Gindler’s own Berlin school and teacher-training enterprise, opened in 1917. It was an institutional and pedagogical project rather than a text, and its “work” consisted in sustained teaching and the formation of approximately sixty gymnastics teachers by the mid-1920s. [Geuter, Heller, and Weaver’s historical account](https://judythweaver.com/writings/elsa-gindler-and-her-influence-on-wilhelm-reich-and-body-psychotherapy/) [Biographical account](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsa_Gindler)

- **1925–1933:** *Deutscher Gymnastikbund e.V.* A professional organization collectively founded by Gindler, Franz Hilker, and representatives of other gymnastics schools; Gindler served as its deputy chair until 1933. This is a jointly made institutional project, not a work attributable to her alone. [Von Arps-Aubert’s scholarly study, contents](https://d-nb.info/1004026242/04) [Historical overview](https://www.bewegungsforschung.ch/contribute/ElsaGindlerUSAabstract.pdf)

### Unfixed lifetime obra

- **circa 1914–1960:** *Gindler’s work with the human being* (no fixed title). A continually revised practice of movement investigation, adult education, bodily attention, conversation, and experiment, developed independently and—after 1924—in sustained exchange with Heinrich Jacoby. Gindler did not name it as a method: “Arbeit am Menschen” is reported as the closest she came, while *Gindler work*, *Jacoby–Gindler work*, and *Sensory Awareness* are later designations; this obra therefore resists reduction to a protocol, proprietary system, or bounded text. [Weaver, “The Influence of Elsa Gindler”](https://www.ibpj.org/issues/archive/Vol3No1%20USABP%20Journal%202004.pdf) [Von Arps-Aubert abstract](https://www.bewegungsforschung.ch/contribute/ElsaGindlerUSAabstract.pdf)

- **1924–1939, with later continuing exchange:** *Collaborative investigations with Heinrich Jacoby* (no authorial title). Joint courses and reciprocal research into perception, behaviour, expression, and human development formed a collaborative project without erasing the two participants’ independent practices. Because the teaching was improvised and relational, the project is better understood as a recurring Gesamtkunstwerk-like educational event than as a coauthored text. [Jacoby/Gindler biographical account](https://www.jacobygindler.ch/gruender/biografien/elsa-gindler-1885-1961/) [Von Arps-Aubert abstract](https://www.bewegungsforschung.ch/contribute/ElsaGindlerUSAabstract.pdf)

- **Before 1945:** *Destroyed working records* (titles and dates largely unknown). Bombing destroyed Gindler’s Berlin studio near the end of the Second World War and with it much of the documentation of her work. These lost records cannot be itemized without invention; consequently, even the scholarly edition of the surviving estate is not a catalogue of everything she originally made. [Historical account](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsa_Gindler) [Weaver’s account of the surviving publication](https://www.ibpj.org/issues/archive/Vol3No1%20USABP%20Journal%202004.pdf)
