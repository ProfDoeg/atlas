## Works by Johannes Cusanus

### Arithmetic protocols

- **1502–1514:** *Algorismus proiectilium de integris novus*; revised as *Algorithmus linealis proiectilium*. A Latin instructional protocol for calculating with counters on a reckoning board, including basic operations, progressions, and commercial arithmetic; the first edition was printed at Zwolle on 20 October 1502, and a revised manifestation appeared at Vienna on 11 July 1514. A. J. E. M. Smeur’s specialist bibliography treats the Zwolle arithmetic as Cusanus’s work, while the Vienna record also associates Hermann von dem Busche with it, so responsibility beyond Cusanus’s preparation and publication is not entirely transparent. ([Smeur bibliography record](https://www.gewina.nl/wp-content/uploads/2010/07/index_sh_pre2005.pdf); [1502 bibliographical description](https://www.sothebys.com/buy/e1f1e6ca-ae21-4a47-b628-71aea6ab12e6/lots/2b5222a0-674f-4dbf-967f-6a90382d0377); [1514 digitized-book record](https://www.e-rara.ch/search?operation=searchRetrieve&query=dc.date%3D%221512+-+1515%22+and+vl.domain%3Derara+sortBy%3Ddc.title%2Fasc))

### Mnemonic teaching apparatus

- **c. 1500–1519; Cusanus editions 1510, 1514, and 1519:** *Tractatulus artificiose memorie*. A protocol for artificial memory combining verbal instruction, mnemonic images, and knowledge supplied orally in Cusanus’s classes; because its printed diagrams are not fully explained without a teacher, it resists classification as a self-sufficient text and is better understood as one component of a teaching performance. All three editions name Cusanus, and he certainly prepared them for print, but the critical edition assigns the textual history jointly to Henricus Vibicetus and Cusanus: scholarship argues that the substantially identical, now-lost *Aureum reminiscendi memorandique perbreve opusculum* of 1501/1502 points to Vibicetus as the probable underlying author, with Hermann von dem Busche responsible only for an accompanying epigram; Cusanus’s authorship may therefore be an appropriation rather than original composition. ([critical-edition contents and dating](https://real.mtak.hu/38204/1/art_of_memoryintro.pdf); [publication history and surviving copies](https://real.mtak.hu/38233/1/Culture_of_memory_Kiss_Valentinus_u.pdf); [attribution analysis](https://repozytorium.amu.edu.pl/bitstreams/a00bac5e-d32c-461f-8a4a-7dfa064b2d6d/content); [evidence of classroom use](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/master/gdc/gdceserialspublic/20/22/21/58/39/-0/02/-0/1/2022215839-002-01/2022215839-002-01.pdf))
