## Works by Aaron David Gordon

### Philosophical obra and unfinished book-project

- **c. 1910–1922:** *האדם והטבע: הגיונות וחלומות של קיצוני* (*Man and Nature: Meditations and Dreams of a Radical*). Gordon’s principal philosophical obra, joining ethics, aesthetics, religion, ecology, and a call to creative life; its first two chapters appeared in 1910 and 1913, while the remaining manuscript was published posthumously. Earlier collected editions omitted passages restored from the Degania archive in the 2020 critical edition, so the familiar book is partly an editorial reconstruction rather than a text Gordon definitively completed. [Magnes Press](https://www.magnespress.co.il/en/book/Man_and_Nature-5981), [critical-edition preview](https://www.magnespress.co.il/api/magnes/book/5981/preview), [Jewish Quarterly Review](https://www.jstor.org/stable/27102865)
- *הנצח והרגע* (*Eternity and the Moment*). A philosophical essay or manuscript unit incorporated into the posthumous *Man and Nature* complex; its status as an independent work is less secure than its separate title in the collected edition suggests. [Collected writings](https://benyehuda.org/collections/17018)
- *לבירור רעיוננו מיסודו* (*Toward a Fundamental Clarification of Our Idea*). A systematic philosophical essay extending Gordon’s account of human life within nature and modern civilization; scholarship sometimes treats it and Gordon’s later writings as continuations of the larger *Man and Nature* project. [Collected writings](https://benyehuda.org/collections/17018), [Jewish Quarterly Review](https://www.jstor.org/stable/27102865)
- *הערכת עצמנו* (*Our Self-Evaluation*). A philosophical-national essay, preserved as an autonomous work in the standard collected edition. [Bialik Institute contents](https://www.bialik-publishing.co.il/index.php?cs=5412&dir=site&op=item&page=catalog)
- *בירור ההבדל בין היהדות והנצרות* (*Clarifying the Difference between Judaism and Christianity*). A comparative religious-philosophical essay. [Collected writings](https://benyehuda.org/collections/17018)
- *הלכות דעות ומלחמת דעות* (*Laws of Opinion and the Struggle of Opinions*). A philosophical essay about conviction and ideological conflict. [Collected writings](https://benyehuda.org/collections/17018)

### Essays on labor and practical pioneering

- *החלום ופתרונו* (*The Dream and Its Realization*). A programmatic essay connecting Zionist aspiration with labor; it approaches a manifesto because it seeks to produce a form of life, not merely describe one. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- **1909:** *פתרון לא־רציונלי* (*A Non-Rational Solution*). An early published essay explicitly presenting Gordon’s position as resistant to ordinary standards of practicality. [Center for Educational Technology](https://tarbutil.cet.ac.il/article/%D7%90%D7%94%D7%A8%D7%9F-%D7%93%D7%95%D7%93-%D7%92%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%93%D7%95%D7%9F/), [collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *על “המעשי” ו“הדמיוני”* (*On the “Practical” and the “Imaginary”*). A polemical essay questioning the opposition between practical work and visionary thought. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *תשובת פועל* (*A Worker’s Reply*). A public intervention cast as the response of a laborer rather than as detached theory. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *על התמיכה* (*On Subsidy/Support*). A practical-political essay on material assistance and independent labor. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *על “הדברים הנוראים”* (*On the “Terrible Things”*). A polemical occasional essay; the title depends upon the controversy to which Gordon was responding. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *מעט התבוננות* (*A Little Reflection*). A short reflective essay in the labor sequence of the collected writings. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *העבודה* (*Labor*). Gordon’s central manifesto-like essay on labor as personal, national, ethical, and creative transformation; the posthumous 1923 book bearing this title is an edition, not a separate obra. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18), [collected-writings bibliography](https://he.hamichlol.org.il/%D7%90._%D7%93._%D7%92%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%93%D7%95%D7%9F)
- *מקוצר רוח* (*Out of Impatience*). A short polemical essay on the labor question. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *על עניני העבודה* (*On Matters of Labor*). An occasional essay treating practical and ideological questions of work. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)

### Cultural and institutional projects

- *אוניברסיטה עברית* (*A Hebrew University*). A programmatic intervention concerning the projected Hebrew university and the relation between knowledge, culture, and national renewal. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *הקונגרס* (*The Congress*). A political essay on the Zionist Congress as institution and representative act. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *כנסת ישראל* (*Knesset Israel / The Community of Israel*). An institutional-national essay; its title names the organized collective rather than the later Israeli parliament. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *הדיבור העברי* (*Hebrew Speech*). A cultural essay on spoken Hebrew as lived national practice. [Collected writings](https://benyehuda.org/collections/17018)

### Nation, society, and diaspora

- *לבירור עמדתנו* (*Toward a Clarification of Our Position*). A programmatic essay defining Gordon’s national and ethical position. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *עבודתנו מעתה* (*Our Work from Now On*). A prospective political essay whose exhortative function makes it close to a manifesto. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *בניין אומה* (*Building a Nation*). A programmatic essay presenting nationhood as something made through labor and culture. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *עם־אדם* (*A Human People*). A philosophical-political essay joining Jewish national existence to universal humanity. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *עבודת־התחייה בארצות־הגולה* (*The Work of Revival in the Diaspora Lands*). A practical-cultural program for Jewish renewal outside Palestine. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *לא זו הדרך* (*This Is Not the Way*). A corrective polemic; its force is performative, rejecting a course of collective action rather than merely analyzing it. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *מה מקור המבוכה בחשבוננו* (*What Is the Source of the Confusion in Our Reckoning?*). A diagnostic essay on collective self-understanding. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *על החינוך העצמי ועבודת התחייה* (*On Self-Education and the Work of Revival*). A programmatic essay treating personal formation as part of national reconstruction. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *בשעה זו…* (*At This Hour…*). An intervention addressed to an immediate political moment; the ellipsis belongs to the collected title. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *קצת עיון בהלכה פסוקה* (*Some Reflection on a Settled Ruling*). A polemical reconsideration of an accepted political judgment. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *משעבוד פראי לשעבוד תרבותי* (*From Savage Subjection to Cultural Subjection*). A social-critical essay about changing forms of domination. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *לזכרון* (*In Memoriam*). A memorial text whose precise occasion must be recovered from its original context; the collected title alone does not securely identify its subject or date. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *לחברי ברוח המנוצחים* (*To My Comrades in the Spirit of the Defeated*). An address to a political community, functioning partly as a manifesto intended to alter its readers’ disposition and conduct. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *איחוד של פירוד* (*A Union of Division*). A political polemic on organizational unity and its contradictions. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *מתוך מועקה נפשית* (*Out of Spiritual Distress*). A political-ethical meditation whose personal form resists a clean division between essay and confession. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *על האיחוד* (*On Unification*). A direct intervention in the question of political or labor-movement union. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)

### Writers, readers, and public language

- *מתוך קריאה* (*From Reading*). A reading-note or literary essay rather than a self-contained theoretical treatise. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *מעניין לעניין באותו עניין* (*From One Matter to Another on the Same Matter*). A discursive public essay whose title advertises its deliberately associative construction. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *הסופרים והעובדים* (*The Writers and the Workers*). An essay on the relationship between intellectual or literary production and manual labor. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *הערות* (*Notes*). A set of notes rather than an unequivocally unified work; the generic editorial title makes its resistance to independent-work status important. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *במקום הקדמה* (*In Place of a Preface*). A prefatory text with an instrumental relation to another publication, although the collected edition presents it separately. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *מבחוץ* (*From Outside*). An occasional essay written from the declared position of an outsider. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)

### Rural settlement and protocols

- *שותפות טבעית* (*Natural Partnership*). An essay on rural cooperation and communal organization. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *יסודות לתקנות למושב עובדים* (*Principles for Regulations of a Workers’ Settlement*). A protocol-like constitutional draft: it instructs and structures a form of cooperative settlement rather than merely describing one. It also circulated under the variant title *יסודות לתקנות מושב־עובדים*. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18), [Project Ben-Yehuda record](https://benyehuda.org/collections/17018)
- *על המועצה החקלאית* (*On the Agricultural Council*). An institutional intervention concerning organized agricultural labor. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)

### Zionist meditations and polemics

- *פתרון רציונלי (מהגיונות של קיצוני)* (*A Rational Solution [From the Meditations of a Radical]*). An ironic or polemical meditation, explicitly presented as part of the larger “Meditations of a Radical” mode rather than necessarily as a freestanding obra. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *הדגל הציוני מה הוא?* (*What Is the Zionist Flag?*). A short inquiry into the meaning and function of a political symbol. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *חוסר חיי משפחה* (*The Absence of Family Life*). A social essay on domestic and communal life among the pioneers. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)

### Public letters and epistolary essays

- *מכתב מארץ ישראל* (*Letter from the Land of Israel*). A published letter whose epistolary form also makes it a public report and ideological intervention. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *מכתב גלוי לי״ח ברנר* (*Open Letter to Y. H. Brenner*). A public letter and polemical act addressed to the writer Yosef Haim Brenner. [Collected-edition contents](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)
- *מכתב שלא נשלח בזמנו לידידי אשר בגולה* (*A Letter Not Sent at the Time to My Friends in the Diaspora*). An unsent letter recast as public essay; its title records the failure of the original communicative act. [Collected writings](https://benyehuda.org/collections/17018)
- *מכתבים לגולה: מכתב ראשון* (*Letters to the Diaspora: First Letter*). The first installment of a five-part public epistolary sequence. [Collected writings](https://benyehuda.org/collections/17018)
- *מכתבים לגולה: מכתב שני* (*Letters to the Diaspora: Second Letter*). The second installment of the public sequence. [Collected writings](https://benyehuda.org/collections/17018)
- *מכתבים לגולה: מכתב שלישי* (*Letters to the Diaspora: Third Letter*). The third installment of the public sequence. [Collected writings](https://benyehuda.org/collections/17018)
- *מכתבים לגולה: מכתב רביעי* (*Letters to the Diaspora: Fourth Letter*). The fourth installment of the public sequence. [Collected writings](https://benyehuda.org/collections/17018)
- *מכתבים לגולה: מכתב חמישי* (*Letters to the Diaspora: Fifth Letter*). The fifth and concluding installment preserved by the collected edition. [Collected writings](https://benyehuda.org/collections/17018)
- *ממכתביו הפרטיים של מתיישב ועובד בא״י* (*From the Private Letters of a Settler and Worker in the Land of Israel*). An editorially selected epistolary assemblage: the individual letters are Gordon’s acts of correspondence, but this title and bounded “work” are products of publication. [Collected writings](https://benyehuda.org/collections/17018)
- *מכתבים* (*Letters*, nos. 1–93 in the standard three-volume edition). A corpus of private correspondence, not a single work Gordon made; the numbered sequence, selection, and collective title are editorial. Later editions include letters to Gordon alongside letters by him, so those reciprocal collections must not be mistaken for additional authored works. [Bialik Institute contents](https://www.bialik-publishing.co.il/index.php?cs=5412&dir=site&op=item&page=catalog), [Project Ben-Yehuda](https://benyehuda.org/author/18)

### Journals, fragments, and posthumous editorial constellations

- *מטיולי בכבישים* (*From My Walks on the Roads*). A travel-observation or reflective sequence; its placement as a collected unit does not establish that Gordon conceived it as a finished book. [Collected writings](https://benyehuda.org/collections/17018)
- *קטעי יומן* (*Diary Fragments*). Surviving pieces of a diary rather than a completed public work; both “fragments” and their grouping are editorial facts essential to their classification. [Bialik Institute contents](https://www.bialik-publishing.co.il/index.php?cs=5412&dir=site&op=item&page=catalog)
- *הרהורים והגיונות* (*Reflections and Meditations*). A posthumously grouped body of short reflections, not securely a single obra with a final authorial boundary. [Collected writings](https://benyehuda.org/collections/17018)
- *רשימות אחרונות* (*Last Notes*). Late manuscript notes grouped by editors after Gordon’s death; chronology supplies the title, but the grouping resists treatment as a work Gordon deliberately completed. [Collected writings](https://benyehuda.org/collections/17018)
