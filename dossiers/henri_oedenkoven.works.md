## Works by Henri Oedenkoven

### Settlement projects and Gesamtkunstwerke

- **1900–1920:** *Monte Verità*. A jointly founded Lebensreform settlement, vegetarian cooperative, nature-cure sanatorium, landscape, and way-of-life project above Ascona; Oedenkoven supplied much of its capital and managed the sanatorium, but authorship belongs collectively to him, Ida Hofmann, Karl and Gusto Gräser, Lotte Hattemer, and other participants. It resists classification as a single work: Andreas Schwab treats its institutions, buildings, social practices, and unrealized constitutions together as an experiment in life. ([Historical Dictionary of Switzerland](https://hls-dhs-dss.ch/en/articles/027825/2009-11-17/); [Schwab, *Monte Verità: Sanatorium der Sehnsucht*](https://andreasschwab.ch/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/Monte-Verit%C3%A0_Scan_2.pdf))

- **1900–1902:** *Vegetabile Cooperative / Naturheilstätte Sonnen-Kuranstalt*. The founders’ original settlement became a nature-cure establishment intended to finance the communal project; its transformation into the *Sanatorium Monte Verità* makes it an institution and social protocol rather than merely a business. Sources differ in distributing initiative among Oedenkoven, Hofmann, and the other founders. ([Historical Dictionary of Switzerland](https://hls-dhs-dss.ch/en/articles/027825/2009-11-17/); [Bundeskunsthalle exhibition guide](https://www.bundeskunsthalle.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Pressemeldungen/2025/Para-Moderne/Press_kit_PARA-MODERNISM.pdf))

- **1902–1920:** *Sanatorium Monte Verità*. A jointly directed therapeutic institution combining vegetarian diet, sun and air bathing, dress reform, exercise, accommodation, and a regulated daily life: effectively a lived protocol manifested as buildings, bodies, landscape, and commerce. It cannot be assigned to Oedenkoven alone, although contemporary prospectuses name Henri and Ida Oedenkoven-Hofmann as its directors. ([Ganahl, *Karl Kraus und Peter Altenberg*](https://www.wallstein-open-library.de/openaccess/9783835390591.pdf); [Historical Dictionary of Switzerland](https://hls-dhs-dss.ch/en/articles/027825/2009-11-17/))

- **1920s:** *Monte Sol*. An attempted naturist and vegetarian colony in Brazil, undertaken with Ida Hofmann and Isabelle Oedenkoven after an initial attempt in Spain. Accounts disagree over its precise location and institutional maturity; it appears to have remained a short-lived or incompletely realized project, so calling it a completed “work” would be misleading. ([Historical Dictionary of Switzerland: Oedenkoven](https://hls-dhs-dss.ch/en/articles/049204/2009-08-20/); [International Vegetarian Union](https://ivu.org/marly-winckler/2323-monte-verita-and-beyond-a-history-of-utopias-vegetarianism-and-alternative-lifestyles.html))

### Architecture and designed environment

- **1901 onward:** *Licht-Luft-Hütten* (“light-and-air huts”). The small wooden dwellings formed part of Monte Verità’s therapeutic architecture and its protocol of exposure to light and fresh air. They were collective constructions rather than securely attributable architectural designs by Oedenkoven, although they belonged to the settlement he financed and administered. ([Schwab, *Monte Verità: Sanatorium der Sehnsucht*](https://andreasschwab.ch/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/Monte-Verit%C3%A0_Scan_2.pdf); [Elisarion, “Gründer, Vegetarismus”](https://www.elisarion.ch/de/monte_verita/der_monte_verita/du_oktober_1978/gruender_vegetarismus.html))

- **1902:** *Casa Anatta*. The founders’ wooden residence, incorporating curved or double walls and a roof used for sun bathing; a chronology describes it as constructed from Oedenkoven’s ideas, while other scholarship associates the architectural execution with Walter Hoffmann. It is therefore best treated as a commissioned or collaboratively conceived building, not an undisputed solo design. ([Monte Verità chronology](https://www.werkstatt-theater.ch/site/assets/files/1204/mte_verita_chronologie.pdf); [Elisarion, “Gründer, Vegetarismus”](https://www.elisarion.ch/de/monte_verita/der_monte_verita/du_oktober_1978/gruender_vegetarismus.html))

- **1902–1904:** *Zentralhaus / Gesellschaftshaus*. The sanatorium’s central communal building, commissioned by Oedenkoven and associated with Walter Hoffmann; its architecture and repeated yin-yang ornament made the building a manifestation of the colony’s spiritual and therapeutic program. Much of it was subsequently absorbed into or displaced by later construction, so the surviving object is fragmentary. ([Dierkens, “Mystique, avant-garde et marginalité”](https://dipot.ulb.ac.be/dspace/bitstream/2013/61869/3/i97828004136_000_f.pdf); [Monte Verità chronology](https://www.werkstatt-theater.ch/site/assets/files/1204/mte_verita_chronologie.pdf))

### Prospectuses, protocols, and constitutions

- **1902:** First prospectus for the projected nature-cure establishment at Monte Verità; no secure fixed title is supplied by the scholarly accounts consulted. Jointly prepared by Oedenkoven and Hofmann, it announced a cooperative sanatorium and introduced *Monte Verità* as the enterprise’s programmatic name, making the leaflet simultaneously advertisement, manifesto, and act of naming. ([Ganahl, *Karl Kraus und Peter Altenberg*](https://www.wallstein-open-library.de/openaccess/9783835390591.pdf); [Schwab, *Monte Verità: Sanatorium der Sehnsucht*](https://andreasschwab.ch/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/Monte-Verit%C3%A0_Scan_2.pdf))

- **1904:** *Sanatorium Monte Verità*. An illustrated, Art Nouveau-styled prospectus naming Henri and Ida Oedenkoven-Hofmann as directors and setting out the establishment’s therapeutic principles, exclusions, accommodation, climate, and cures. It is both an advertisement and a protocol for remaking everyday life; the surviving copy is held by the Swiss National Library. ([Ganahl, *Karl Kraus und Peter Altenberg*](https://www.wallstein-open-library.de/openaccess/9783835390591.pdf))

- **1905:** *Provisorische Statuten der vegetabilischen Gesellschaft des Monte Verità*. A 45-article constitution and promotional manifesto jointly composed by Hofmann and Oedenkoven for a proposed cooperative society. It was never put into operation, so the project—the intended social organization—is the work more precisely than any realized institution. ([Schwab, “Die Statuten”](https://andreasschwab.ch/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/Monte-Verit%C3%A0_Scan_2.pdf))

- **1913 or 1915:** *Statuten der individualistischen Cooperative von Monte Verità*. A 58-article protocol for reorganizing the colony, again unrealized. Schwab dates the document 15 March 1913 from a copy in the Szeemann Archive, whereas a later exhibition catalogue captions a surviving copy 15 March 1915; the discrepancy should remain unresolved pending examination of the witnesses. ([Schwab, “Die Statuten”](https://andreasschwab.ch/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/Monte-Verit%C3%A0_Scan_2.pdf); [Bollettino della Società Storica Locarnese, *Dalla visione al chiodo*](https://andreasschwab.ch/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/Quaderno-BSSI_12_total.pdf))

- **By 1916:** Membership card and organizational apparatus of the *Sozial-Vegetarisch-Anationale Gesellschaft*. These are administrative manifestations of Oedenkoven’s effort to formalize an international cooperative rather than securely attributable literary works; surviving evidence establishes the society’s identification card but not a complete Oedenkoven-authored textual corpus. ([Bennett, “Sites of Re-Enchantment”](https://www.mdpi.com/2077-1444/13/2/110))

### Writing system

- **Early twentieth century; exact date unfixed:** Oedenkoven’s reform orthography. A phoneticizing German spelling practice developed principally by Oedenkoven and used by Ida Hofmann in correspondence and Monte Verità texts; no separately published, securely titled rulebook was identified. It is a protocol embodied in other writings rather than a conventional autonomous text. ([FORVM, “Monte Verità”](https://forvm.contextxxi.org/%2B-monte-verita-%2B.html); [Ganahl, *Karl Kraus und Peter Altenberg*](https://www.wallstein-open-library.de/openaccess/9783835390591.pdf))

### Newspaper interventions and public statements

- **1906:** *Monte Verità*. A statement to the *Thurgauer Zeitung* responding to its depiction of the colony and discussing the sanatorium’s conflicts with other settlers around Ascona; the documentary archive attributes the response to Oedenkoven but does not present it as a separately published authored work. ([Monteverita.net, “Stellungnahmen”](https://monteverita.net/stellungnahmen/))

- **1906:** *Der Leiter und Besitzer der Naturheilanstalt Monte Verita*. A notice or supplied statement announcing the winter season and correcting reports that conflated Oedenkoven’s sanatorium with the wider colony of “Naturmenschen.” Its printed heading may be editorial rather than an authorial title. ([Monteverita.net, “Stellungnahmen”](https://monteverita.net/stellungnahmen/))

- **1907:** *Die Naturmenschen in Ascona*. A communication from the sanatorium’s management printed in the *Neue Freie Presse* on 10 January 1907; its collective institutional voice makes Oedenkoven’s individual authorship uncertain. ([Monteverita.net, “Stellungnahmen”](https://monteverita.net/stellungnahmen/))

- **1907:** *Le végétalisme*. A letter by Oedenkoven, printed in *La Meuse* on 9 July 1907, correcting a newspaper account of vegetarianism and the sanatorium. It is a polemical public intervention rather than a freestanding treatise. ([Monteverita.net, “Stellungnahmen”](https://monteverita.net/stellungnahmen/); [Bennett, “Sites of Re-Enchantment”](https://www.mdpi.com/2077-1444/13/2/110))

- **1909:** *Boîte aux Lettres*. Oedenkoven’s letter to *Le Figaro*, dated 3 September and published 6 September 1909, correcting claims about Monte Verità. The heading is the newspaper column’s designation, not necessarily Oedenkoven’s title. ([Monteverita.net, “Stellungnahmen”](https://monteverita.net/stellungnahmen/))

- **1909:** *Vor einiger Zeit …*. A response in *Der Bund* on 13 September 1909 denying a report that Monte Verità was to be sold. The archive describes it as Oedenkoven’s rejoinder, but the displayed phrase is an editorial incipit rather than a secure title. ([Monteverita.net, “Stellungnahmen”](https://monteverita.net/stellungnahmen/))

- **1909:** *Entgegnung*. A further denial by “Henri Odenkoven” in the *Neue Zürcher Zeitung* on 14 September 1909. The spelling of his surname and the generic title follow the newspaper record. ([Monteverita.net, “Stellungnahmen”](https://monteverita.net/stellungnahmen/))

- **1909:** *Les primitifs Ascona*. Oedenkoven’s letter in the *Tribune de Genève* on 17 September 1909, part of the same corrective press campaign concerning Monte Verità and its supposed “primitives.” The catalogue heading may reproduce the newspaper’s wording rather than an authorial title. ([Monteverita.net, “Stellungnahmen”](https://monteverita.net/stellungnahmen/))

- **1909:** *Herr H. Oedenkoven-Hofmann in Ascona schreibt uns*. A statement published by the *Zürcherische Freitagszeitung* on 1 October 1909. Its heading explicitly presents it as correspondence and should not be mistaken for the title of an essay. ([Monteverita.net, “Stellungnahmen”](https://monteverita.net/stellungnahmen/))

- **1917:** *Une mise au point*. Oedenkoven’s corrective statement in *La Sentinelle* on 20 July 1917. It belongs to his recurrent practice of using brief newspaper letters to act upon Monte Verità’s public identity. ([Monteverita.net, “Stellungnahmen”](https://monteverita.net/stellungnahmen/))
