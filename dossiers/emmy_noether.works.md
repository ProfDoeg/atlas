## Works by Amalie Emmy Noether

### Dissertation, research articles, and mathematical surveys

- **1907:** *Über die Bildung des Formensystems der ternären biquadratischen Form*. A four-page preliminary report of Noether’s dissertation results, distinct from the full dissertation published the following year. [Bibliography no. 1](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
- **1907–1908:** *Über die Bildung des Formensystems der ternären biquadratischen Form*. Noether’s doctoral dissertation, printed in 1908 with two tables; it is the full work of which the 1907 publication was a preliminary manifestation. [Bibliography no. 2](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
- **1911:** *Zur Invariantentheorie der Formen von n Variabeln*. A full research article in invariant theory, expanding the 1909 lecture and its 1910 report. [Bibliography no. 4](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
- **1915:** *Körper und Systeme rationaler Funktionen*. A research article on fields and systems of rational functions, completed in 1914 and submitted in 1915 as an attempted habilitation thesis. [Bibliography no. 6](https://emmy-noether.net/Emmy-Noethers-Veroeffentlichungen/)
- **1916:** *Der Endlichkeitssatz der Invarianten endlicher Gruppen*. A short research article applying and supplementing the preceding work on rational-function systems. [Bibliography no. 7](https://emmy-noether.net/Emmy-Noethers-Veroeffentlichungen/)
- **1916:** *Über ganze rationale Darstellung der Invarianten eines Systems von beliebig vielen Grundformen*. A research article in invariant theory. [Bibliography no. 8](https://emmy-noether.net/Emmy-Noethers-Veroeffentlichungen/)
- **1916:** *Die allgemeinsten Bereiche aus ganzen transzendenten Zahlen*. A research article, completed in 1915; Noether appended a correction to her 1920 paper *Zur Reihenentwicklung in der Formentheorie*. [Bibliography no. 9](https://emmy-noether.net/Emmy-Noethers-Veroeffentlichungen/)
- **1916:** *Die Funktionalgleichungen der isomorphen Abbildung*. A research article on the functional equations of isomorphic mappings. [Bibliography no. 10](https://emmy-noether.net/Emmy-Noethers-Veroeffentlichungen/)
- **1917:** *Gleichungen mit vorgeschriebener Gruppe*. A research article on equations with a prescribed group, completed in 1916 and later corrected in 1920. The 1983 collected works and some bibliographies date it 1918, but Tollmien identifies the journal publication as 1917. [Bibliography no. 11 and dating note](https://emmy-noether.net/Emmy-Noethers-Veroeffentlichungen/)
- **1918:** *Invarianten beliebiger Differentialausdrücke*. A research note on invariants of general differential expressions, presented to the Göttingen society by Felix Klein. [Bibliography no. 12](https://emmy-noether.net/Emmy-Noethers-Veroeffentlichungen/)
- **1918:** *Invariante Variationsprobleme*. A research article containing the two results now called Noether’s theorems; the manuscript was presented by Klein and subsequently accepted in 1919 as Noether’s habilitation thesis. [Bibliography no. 13](https://emmy-noether.net/Emmy-Noethers-Veroeffentlichungen/)
- **1919:** *Die arithmetische Theorie der algebraischen Funktionen einer Veränderlichen in ihrer Beziehung zu den übrigen Theorien und zu der Zahlkörpertheorie*. A substantial survey and continuation of Max Noether and Alexander Brill’s 1894 report; it is therefore both an authored synthesis and an intervention in an inherited project. [Bibliography no. 14](https://emmy-noether.net/Emmy-Noethers-Veroeffentlichungen/)
- **1919:** *Die Endlichkeit des Systems der ganzzahligen Invarianten binärer Formen*. A research article on the finiteness of integral invariants of binary forms. [Bibliography no. 15](https://emmy-noether.net/Emmy-Noethers-Veroeffentlichungen/)
- **1920:** *Zur Reihenentwicklung in der Formentheorie*. A research article accepted in 1919; its final page also carries corrections to two earlier papers, so the printed object has a secondary corrective function. [Bibliography no. 16](https://emmy-noether.net/Emmy-Noethers-Veroeffentlichungen/)
- **1920:** *Moduln in nichtkommutativen Bereichen, insbesondere aus Differential- und Differenzenausdrücken*. A jointly authored research article with Werner Schmeidler on modules in noncommutative domains. [Bibliography no. 17](https://emmy-noether.net/Emmy-Noethers-Veroeffentlichungen/)
- **1921:** *Idealtheorie in Ringbereichen*. A foundational research article in abstract ideal theory. [Bibliography no. 19](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
- **1922:** *Ein algebraisches Kriterium für absolute Irreduzibilität*. A research article giving an algebraic criterion for absolute irreducibility. [Bibliography no. 20](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
- **1923:** *Algebraische und Differentialinvarianten*. A research article relating algebraic and differential invariants. [Bibliography no. 23](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
- **1923:** *Eliminationstheorie und allgemeine Idealtheorie*. A full research article connecting elimination theory with general ideal theory. [Bibliography no. 24](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
- **1926:** *Der Endlichkeitssatz der Invarianten endlicher linearer Gruppen der Charakteristik p*. A research article proving a finiteness result in positive characteristic. [Bibliography no. 30](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
- **1926:** *Abstrakter Aufbau der Idealtheorie in algebraischen Zahl- und Funktionenkörpern*. A major research article giving an abstract axiomatic construction of ideal theory. [Bibliography no. 31](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
- **1927:** *Der Diskriminantensatz für die Ordnungen eines algebraischen Zahl- oder Funktionenkörpers*. A research article on discriminants and orders in number and function fields. [Bibliography no. 32](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
- **1929:** *Hyperkomplexe Größen und Darstellungstheorie*. A major research article connecting noncommutative algebra, representation theory, modules, and ideals. Its printed exposition was prepared with Bartel van der Waerden from Noether’s 1927–28 lectures, so sole authorship does not describe the entire process of textual manufacture. [Tollmien chronology](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherlebensdaten.html)
- **1929:** *Über Maximalbereiche von ganzzahligen Funktionen*. A research article published in the journal of the Moscow Mathematical Society. [Bibliography no. 36](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
- **1932:** *Normalbasis bei Körpern ohne höhere Verzweigung*. A research article on normal bases in fields without higher ramification. [Bibliography no. 38](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
- **1932:** *Beweis eines Hauptsatzes in der Theorie der Algebren*. A jointly authored research article by Richard Brauer, Helmut Hasse, and Noether—the work now associated with the Brauer–Hasse–Noether theorem. [Bibliography no. 39](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
- **1933:** *Nichtkommutative Algebren*. A research article on noncommutative algebras, submitted in June 1932 and published the following year. [Bibliography no. 41](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
- **1933:** *Der Hauptgeschlechtssatz für relativ-galoissche Zahlkörper*. A research article on the principal genus theorem for relatively Galois number fields. [Bibliography no. 42](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
- **1934:** *Zerfallende verschränkte Produkte und ihre Maximalordnungen*. A research monograph or extended paper in the memorial series for Jacques Herbrand; unlike most of Noether’s works, it appeared as a separately issued fascicle. [Bibliography no. 43](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
- **1950:** *Idealdifferentiation und Differente*. A full research article published fifteen years after Noether’s death. Its posthumous appearance makes it a manifestation of surviving mathematical work rather than a publication she supervised to completion. [Bibliography no. 44](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)

### Lecture reports, abstracts, and concise communications

- **1910:** *Zur Invariantentheorie der Formen von n Variabeln*. A short published report of Noether’s 1909 Salzburg lecture, later developed into the 1911 article of the same title. [Bibliography no. 3](https://emmy-noether.net/Emmy-Noethers-Veroeffentlichungen/)
- **1913:** *Rationale Funktionenkörper*. A published report of Noether’s Vienna lecture, continued and expanded in *Körper und Systeme rationaler Funktionen*. [Bibliography no. 5](https://emmy-noether.net/Emmy-Noethers-Veroeffentlichungen/)
- **1919:** *Über ganzzahlige Polynome und Potenzreihen*. A printed summary of a 26 November 1918 lecture to the Göttingen Mathematical Society. It is absent from the 1983 collected works, illustrating that the collected edition is extensive but not literally complete. [Supplementary bibliography no. 17a](https://emmy-noether.net/Emmy-Noethers-Veroeffentlichungen/)
- **1921:** *Über eine Arbeit des im Kriege gefallenen K. Hentzelt zur Eliminationstheorie*. A one-page preliminary report on Kurt Hentzelt’s dissertation; it announces the much more extensively mediated text published in 1923. [Bibliography no. 18](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
- **1922:** *Formale Variationsrechnung und Differentialinvarianten*. A short, separately attributed section embedded inside Roland Weitzenböck’s encyclopedia article rather than a freestanding publication; its location makes it resist classification as an ordinary paper. [Bibliography no. 21](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
- **1924:** *Eliminationstheorie und Idealtheorie*. A concise published communication, distinct from the longer 1923 article with the similar title *Eliminationstheorie und allgemeine Idealtheorie*. [Bibliography no. 25](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
- **1924:** *Abstrakter Aufbau der Idealtheorie im algebraischen Zahlkörper*. A one-page communication announcing the project later realized in the 1926 article on number and function fields. [Bibliography no. 26](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
- **1925:** *Hilbertsche Anzahlen in der Idealtheorie*. A one-page research communication reporting a lecture or result rather than a full article. [Bibliography no. 27](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
- **1925:** *Gruppencharaktere und Idealtheorie*. A one-page research communication linking group characters and ideal theory. [Bibliography no. 28](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
- **1926:** *Ableitung der Elementarteilertheorie aus der Gruppentheorie*. A short published communication deriving elementary-divisor theory from group theory; it was omitted from the 1983 collected works. [Bibliography no. 29](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
- **1928:** *Hyperkomplexe Größen und Darstellungstheorie in arithmetischer Auffassung*. A three-page congress synopsis, not the full 1929 article bearing the shorter version of the title. [Bibliography no. 34](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
- **1929:** *Idealdifferentiation und Differente*. A one-page communication announcing material that appeared as a full, posthumous article in 1950. [Bibliography no. 37](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
- **1932:** *Hyperkomplexe Systeme in ihren Beziehungen zur kommutativen Algebra und zur Zahlentheorie*. The published text of Noether’s invited plenary address at the International Congress of Mathematicians in Zürich. [Bibliography no. 40](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)

### Collaborative, adapted, and embedded works

- **1914:** *Paul Gordan*. An obituary formally headed by Max Noether, with the stated assistance of Felix Klein and Emmy Noether. It is a collaborative memorial text whose precise division of authorship is not specified, so it resists treatment as Emmy Noether’s sole work. [Supplementary attribution](https://emmy-noether.net/Emmy-Noethers-Veroeffentlichungen/)
- **1923:** *Zur Theorie der Polynomideale und Resultanten*. Noether’s “completely free adaptation” of the essential parts of Kurt Hentzelt’s 1914 dissertation. She supplied a conceptual rewriting and simplification, while explicitly assigning the underlying ideas and proofs to Hentzelt; it therefore resists both sole authorship and mere editorship. [Bibliography no. 22](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
- **1927:** *Über minimale Zerfällungskörper irreduzibler Darstellungen*. A jointly authored research article by Richard Brauer and Noether. [Bibliography no. 33](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
- **1927:** *Notwendige und hinreichende Multiplizitätsbedingungen zum Noetherschen Fundamentalsatz der algebraischen Funktionen*. Heinrich Kapferer’s article contains an expressly identified addendum made jointly with Noether. Only the addendum is her work, not the whole Kapferer paper. [Contemporary attribution](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Mathematische_Annalen/Inhalt_1869%E2%80%931943)
- **Winter 1929–30; published 1983:** *Algebra der hyperkomplexen Größen*. A lecture-course text made from Noether’s teaching but written up by Max Deuring and published only in her collected works. It is neither straightforwardly her authored paper nor merely Deuring’s independent work; it is a mediated textual manifestation of an oral course. [Scholarly account](https://www.mathi.uni-heidelberg.de/~roquette/hasse-noether/hano_038_22-11-193_.html)

### Editorial obra

- **1930–1932:** *Gesammelte mathematische Werke* by Richard Dedekind. A three-volume collected-works edition jointly edited by Robert Fricke, Emmy Noether, and Öystein Ore; Noether made the editorial object, not the underlying Dedekind works. [Editorial attribution](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
- **Prepared before 1935; published 1937:** *Briefwechsel Cantor–Dedekind*. An edition of the Cantor–Dedekind correspondence jointly prepared by Noether and Jean Cavaillès and published after Noether’s death. It is a posthumous editorial manifestation, not a correspondence authored by her. [Bibliographic record](https://celebratio.org/Noether_E/article/109/)

### Reviews and reviewing work

- **Various dates:** *Kurzmitteilungen und Buchbesprechungen von Emmy Noether*. This is a dispersed body of short communications, abstracts, and mathematical book reviews rather than one fixed-title work. The standard collected edition treats it as a separate list on pp. 776–777, while the fuller Merzbach bibliography occupies pp. 173–182; because the publicly accessible scholarly descriptions do not expose every review title, inventing individual titles or dates would falsely imply completeness. [Scope note and bibliographic location](https://www.cordula-tollmien.de/noetherpublikationen.html)
