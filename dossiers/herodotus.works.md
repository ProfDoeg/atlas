## Works by Herodotus of Halicarnassus

### Surviving obra

- **Published in the 420s BCE; composition date uncertain:** *Histories* (*Historiai*, “Inquiries”). A single, genre-defying prose obra combining historical inquiry, ethnography, geography, political analysis, oral tradition, anecdote, and travel narrative; its familiar division into nine separately named books is probably Hellenistic rather than Herodotus’s own. Modern scholarship recognizes this as Herodotus’s only securely attested work, although the intended finality of the transmitted ending remains debated.

### Project of uncertain realization

- *Assyrian logoi* (“Assyrian accounts”; no secure title or date). Herodotus twice announces further treatment of Assyrian history in the *Histories* (1.106 and 1.184), but no independently transmitted text survives: scholars disagree whether this was a projected separate obra, an intended digression never completed, material incorporated elsewhere in the *Histories*, or a work subsequently lost. It therefore resists classification as a made work at all and is safest described as an attested project of uncertain realization.
