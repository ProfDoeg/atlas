## Works by Wifredo Viladrich

### Sculptural corpus and its limits

- **1939–1976:** *Sculptural corpus*. The estate records 383 sculptures—reliefs, statuettes, portraits, busts, statues, and monuments—but does not publish a complete item-level catalogue; consequently, the individually named works below are the recoverable portion of that corpus, not a silently reconstructed catalogue raisonné. The same source also reports unquantified ceramics, ivory carvings, drawings, paintings, and murals, so “everything” cannot presently be reduced to a verified title list. [Estate biography](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/sintesis-biografica/)

### Busts, heads, and sculptural portraits

- **1939–1940:** *Muchacho de Catamarca*. Patinated-plaster bust, 39 × 40 × 20 cm; the date is explicitly given as a range. [UADE exhibition catalogue](https://www.uade.edu.ar/media/bpiai135/viladrich_ok.pdf)

- **1940:** *El Guaica, muchacho catamarqueño*. Bust, documented in patinated cement; it received the 1942 National Salon encouragement prize and a 1949 acquisition prize, indicating later exhibition of the same named obra or another cast rather than necessarily a newly made work. [UADE catalogue](https://www.uade.edu.ar/media/bpiai135/viladrich_ok.pdf), [estate prize record](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/premios-obtenidos/)

- **1943:** *Hijo de la Tierra – Jujuy* (also catalogued as *Hijo de la tierra*). Bust made at Tilcara and documented in plaster and bronze manifestations; the estate says it won a 1944 acquisition prize, while the Museo Sívori catalogue dates its bronze to 1943. [UADE catalogue](https://www.uade.edu.ar/media/bpiai135/viladrich_ok.pdf), [Museo Nacional de Bellas Artes exhibition catalogue](https://media.bellasartes.gob.ar/h/Publicaciones/la_hora_americana_catalogo.pdf), [estate prize record](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/premios-obtenidos/)

- **By 1948:** *Retrato de A.M.*. Sculptural portrait awarded a special mention by Sociedad Hebraica in 1948; its medium and exact making date are not supplied by the consulted record. [Estate prize record](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/premios-obtenidos/)

- **By 1948:** *Sarita*. Sculptural portrait jointly cited with *Retrato de A.M.* for a 1948 special mention; no secure date or medium is published in the consulted record. [Estate prize record](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/premios-obtenidos/)

- **By 1949:** *Hombre de Belén*. Sculpture awarded second prize at the 1949 Bahía Blanca Salon; the award year is only a terminus ante quem, not a securely documented making date. [Estate prize record](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/premios-obtenidos/)

- **By 1950:** *Chacho, el Boyero*. Sculpture awarded an encouragement prize at the 1950 Mar del Plata Salon; its exact date and material remain unspecified in the consulted catalogue material. [Estate prize record](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/premios-obtenidos/)

- **By 1960:** *Viejo criollo (Martín Fierro)*. Historical sculpture awarded an honorable mention in 1960; the documentary source preserves both titles, so neither should be silently discarded as merely a variant. [Estate prize record](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/premios-obtenidos/)

- **1966 or earlier:** *Pacha Mama*. Sculpture awarded both the Quinquela prize and a national acquisition prize in 1966; the cited record does not establish whether 1966 is its making date. [Estate prize record](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/premios-obtenidos/)

- **1974:** *Raíces*. Travertine-marble head, 28 × 28 × 14 cm; a sculptural evocation of Indigenous American identity. [UADE catalogue](https://www.uade.edu.ar/media/bpiai135/viladrich_ok.pdf)

### Statues, statuettes, groups, and reliefs

- **1945:** *Pueblo español*. Viladrich’s first full statue: a nude, chained man. It also functioned as political intervention—he refused a jury’s demand to rename it, and it was consequently excluded from the 1946 Municipal Salon—so the obra approaches a manifesto enacted through sculpture. [Estate statue catalogue](https://wifredoviladrich.com/sus-obras/estatuas/), [estate biography](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/sintesis-biografica/), [contemporary report](https://transhemisferica.com.ar/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/810-01-06-1946.pdf)

- **1946:** *Adelante – Juventud en marcha* (also *Juventud en Marcha – Adelante*). Statue whose reversed compound titles are both used by the estate; later public manifestations include an example at Pinamar. [Estate statue catalogue](https://wifredoviladrich.com/sus-obras/estatuas/), [Pinamar sculpture-park account](https://www.guiadeplaya.com.ar/la-isla-del-golf-pinamar.html)

- **1947:** *Alborada*. Statue; the estate gallery’s multiple images are views and details of one obra, not separate works. [Estate statue catalogue](https://wifredoviladrich.com/sus-obras/estatuas/)

- **1947:** *Hijo de la tierra*. Statue bearing the same or a closely related title as the 1943 bust; the estate classifies this as a separate statue, so the shared title should not collapse the two objects. [Estate statue catalogue](https://wifredoviladrich.com/sus-obras/estatuas/)

- **1955:** *El olivo y la paloma – Los niños por la paz*. Patinated-plaster sculptural group, 120 × 115 × 65 cm; its allegorical and activist purpose gives it something of a sculptural manifesto for peace. [UADE catalogue](https://www.uade.edu.ar/media/bpiai135/viladrich_ok.pdf)

- **1955:** *El indio de la quena*. Circular terracotta relief, 20.5 cm in diameter; it is distinct from the larger 1963 statue *Indio de la quena – Humahuaqueño*. [UADE catalogue](https://www.uade.edu.ar/media/bpiai135/viladrich_ok.pdf)

- **1955:** *Clamor de Libertad*. Statue characterized by the estate as an affirmation of freedom and human rights. [Estate statue catalogue](https://wifredoviladrich.com/sus-obras/estatuas/), [estate biography](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/sintesis-biografica/)

- **1956:** *Gaucho de Güemes*. Statue; gallery entries labelled “profile” and “detail” are photographs of this same obra. [Estate statue catalogue](https://wifredoviladrich.com/sus-obras/estatuas/)

- **1956:** *La Minera*. Statue concerned with a working figure; the estate publishes frontal and detail views. [Estate statue catalogue](https://wifredoviladrich.com/sus-obras/estatuas/)

- **1956:** *La maestrita rural*. Statue of a rural teacher; repeated gallery records are different views rather than multiple obras. [Estate statue catalogue](https://wifredoviladrich.com/sus-obras/estatuas/)

- **1963:** *Indio de la quena – Humahuaqueño* (also *Humahuaqueño – Indio de la quena*). Patinated-cement statue, 100 × 60 × 62 cm; the sources reverse its compound title, but clearly identify the same work. [UADE catalogue](https://www.uade.edu.ar/media/bpiai135/viladrich_ok.pdf), [estate biography](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/sintesis-biografica/)

- **1963:** *América joven*. White-cement statue, 100 × 75 × 60 cm, belonging to Viladrich’s sustained representation of American and Indigenous subjects. [UADE catalogue](https://www.uade.edu.ar/media/bpiai135/viladrich_ok.pdf)

- **1966:** *Tamborilera*. Patinated reconstituted-stone statuette, 45 × 40 × 38 cm; it won the Sesostris Vitulo prize in the 1966 Manuel Belgrano Salon. [UADE catalogue](https://www.uade.edu.ar/media/bpiai135/viladrich_ok.pdf), [estate prize record](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/premios-obtenidos/)

- **1973:** *Madre coya I*. Small patinated-plaster statuette, 15 × 10 × 10 cm; the Roman numeral indicates a sequence, but no securely documented *Madre coya II* was found in the consulted catalogue material. [UADE catalogue](https://www.uade.edu.ar/media/bpiai135/viladrich_ok.pdf)

- **1973:** *Paz*. Patinated-plaster high relief, 50 × 70 cm; like *El olivo y la paloma*, it is both an art object and an activist manifestation of the peace movement. [UADE catalogue](https://www.uade.edu.ar/media/bpiai135/viladrich_ok.pdf)

- **Date not established:** *Sin Palabras*. Statue named in the estate’s overview, but not dated or materially described there. [Estate biography](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/sintesis-biografica/)

- **Date not established:** *Maternidad*. Statue named by the estate; it should not automatically be identified with any of the separately documented municipal monuments to motherhood. [Estate biography](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/sintesis-biografica/)

- **Date not established:** *Juventud Hoy*. Statue known by title from the estate overview; no secure date or material is supplied there. [Estate biography](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/sintesis-biografica/)

- **Date not established:** *Liberación*. Statue and political allegory defending freedom and human rights; the consulted estate account gives no exact date. [Estate biography](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/sintesis-biografica/)

- **Date not established:** *España encadenada*. Politically charged sculpture opposing dictatorship and oppression; whether it is materially related to *Pueblo español* is not resolved by the estate account, so it is retained as a separately named work. [Estate biography](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/sintesis-biografica/)

- **Date not established:** *Después del bombardeo*. Sculpture denouncing the violence of war; no secure date or material appears in the consulted catalogue record. [Estate biography](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/sintesis-biografica/)

- **Date not established:** *Elegía a unos soldados*. Sculptural elegy and anti-war work; its exact form and date are not specified in the consulted source. [Estate biography](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/sintesis-biografica/)

- **Date not established:** *Fruto de racismo*. Sculptural denunciation of racial discrimination; its date and material remain undocumented in the consulted account. [Estate biography](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/sintesis-biografica/)

- **Date not established:** *Estado de sitio*. Sculpture documented photographically while being modelled in Viladrich’s workshop; its surviving status, completion, date, and material are not established, so it resists confident classification as a completed obra. [Estate workshop archive](https://wifredoviladrich.com/)

### Monumental obras, commissions, and projects

- **Date not established:** *Monumento a la Madre – Christophersen*. Public monumental sculpture identified by locality in the estate gallery; the consulted record supplies neither a secure date nor enough information to determine whether the displayed figure repeats another motherhood composition. [Estate public-works catalogue](https://wifredoviladrich.com/sus-obras/obras-en-lugares-publicos/)

- **Date not established:** *Monumento a la Madre – Lima*. Public monument distinguished by its location; no secure making or installation date is given in the consulted estate listing. [Estate public-works catalogue](https://wifredoviladrich.com/sus-obras/obras-en-lugares-publicos/)

- **Date not established:** *Monumento a la Madre – María Teresa*. Public monument comprising at least a statue and its monumental setting; photographs of the whole, statue, and details describe one complex obra rather than separate works. [Estate public-works catalogue](https://wifredoviladrich.com/sus-obras/obras-en-lugares-publicos/)

- **Date not established:** *Monumento a la Madre – Sancti Spiritu*. Public monument documented from multiple viewpoints; its date is not supplied by the consulted listing. [Estate public-works catalogue](https://wifredoviladrich.com/sus-obras/obras-en-lugares-publicos/)

- **Date not established:** *Sarmiento*. Public sculptural portrait or monument; the estate gallery names it but does not provide a secure date or medium. [Estate public-works catalogue](https://wifredoviladrich.com/sus-obras/obras-en-lugares-publicos/)

- **1970:** *Monumento al General Juan Martín de Pueyrredón*. Competition-winning monumental project subsequently executed and installed in Plaza Grand Bourg, Buenos Aires; the 1970 date belongs securely to the award and commission, not necessarily every stage of fabrication. [Estate prize record](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/premios-obtenidos/), [estate biography](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/sintesis-biografica/)

- **1970:** *Monumento al General Güemes*. Second-prize competition project. It may have remained a model or unexecuted intention, but that project-status is itself the obra and should not be erased because no installed monument is documented. [Estate prize record](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/premios-obtenidos/)

- **1970:** *Liliana Crociati de Szaszak*. Life-size bronze funerary portrait installed at the Crociati de Szaszak tomb in Recoleta Cemetery; secondary sources disagree over the sitter’s birth year, and the sculpture apparently lacks a fixed official title, so the descriptive name is preferable to inventing one. [Buenos Aires Legislature cemetery guide](https://pergamo.legislatura.gob.ar/pgmedia/biblioteca/LIBROS%20DIGITALIZADOS/018085_D.pdf), [photographic monument record](https://universalcompendium.com/gen_images/ucg/1sculpture/recoleta-cementerio-liliana-crociati-szaszak-argentina-cemetery-buenos-aires.htm)

- **After 1970; exact date not established:** *Sabú*. Bronze figure of Crociati’s dog added to the tomb after the animal’s death. Its physical and narrative integration with *Liliana Crociati de Szaszak* makes the ensemble resist a simple count as either one work or two. [Photographic monument record](https://universalcompendium.com/gen_images/ucg/1sculpture/recoleta-cementerio-liliana-crociati-szaszak-argentina-cemetery-buenos-aires.htm)

- **1971:** *Monumento al Almirante Guillermo Brown*, winning project. First-prize competition project executed as the public monument between Mar del Plata’s Casino and Hotel Provincial. [Estate prize record](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/premios-obtenidos/), [estate biography](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/sintesis-biografica/)

- **1971:** *Monumento al Almirante Guillermo Brown*, second-prize project. A different competition model by Viladrich received second prize in the same contest; despite sharing its subject and conventional title with the winning design, it is a separate project, while its completion and present whereabouts are undocumented. [Estate prize record](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/premios-obtenidos/), [estate biography](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/sintesis-biografica/)

- **1972–1973:** *Alas de la Patria*. Competition-winning monument for Mar del Plata, inaugurated in 1973; later replicas were installed in Buenos Aires and Río Cuarto, making this an obra with multiple public manifestations rather than three independently conceived works. [Estate prize record](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/premios-obtenidos/), [estate biography](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/sintesis-biografica/), [Buenos Aires Legislature guide](https://pergamo.legislatura.gob.ar/pgmedia/biblioteca/LIBROS%20DIGITALIZADOS/018085_D.pdf)

- **Date not established:** *Juventud, Armonía y Belleza*. Public sculptural obra recorded by the estate; no secure date or location is exposed in the consulted gallery text. [Estate public-works catalogue](https://wifredoviladrich.com/sus-obras/obras-en-lugares-publicos/)

### Writings, lectures, and manifesto-like interventions

- **1946:** Untitled public statement concerning *Pueblo español* and the Municipal Autumn Salon. Published by *España Republicana* as a “manifiesto,” it acts publicly against opaque jury conduct rather than merely describing the statue; its original heading is not securely established, so none is invented here. [Contemporary publication](https://transhemisferica.com.ar/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/810-01-06-1946.pdf)

- **1964:** *El artista y su tiempo*. Lecture or written address on artists’ relationship to lived historical time; only an excerpt and title are publicly reproduced by the estate, so its complete textual status and publication history remain uncertain. [Estate text archive](https://wifredoviladrich.com/)

- **1971:** *Cómo ver un cuadro*. Expository or pedagogical text about viewing art; the estate preserves only an excerpt, and the consulted source does not establish whether it was a lecture, essay, or formally published book. [Estate text archive](https://wifredoviladrich.com/)

### Other media whose individual obras are not recoverable

- **Dates and titles not published:** *Decorative ceramics*. The estate reports several works but supplies no complete itemized catalogue; this is therefore a documented body of work rather than a safely enumerable list of individual titles. [Estate biography](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/sintesis-biografica/), [estate ceramics gallery](https://wifredoviladrich.com/)

- **Dates and titles not published:** *Ivory carvings*. The estate records the existence of this body of work without giving a count or title list; individual entries would require access to the underlying archive. [Estate biography](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/sintesis-biografica/)

- **Dates and titles not published:** *Drawings*. A documented but unquantified body of work; the estate gallery is selective and cannot responsibly be converted into a complete catalogue. [Estate biography](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/sintesis-biografica/), [estate works index](https://wifredoviladrich.com/)

- **Dates and titles not published:** *Paintings*. A documented but unquantified body of work; no complete title-level inventory was found in the consulted scholarly or estate sources. [Estate biography](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/sintesis-biografica/)

- **Dates and titles not published:** *Oil murals*. The estate records mural production and maintains a combined drawings-paintings-murals gallery, but does not expose a complete catalogue with titles, dates, and locations. [Estate biography](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/sintesis-biografica/), [estate works index](https://wifredoviladrich.com/)

### Sources

- Wifredo Viladrich estate, [“Síntesis biográfica”](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/sintesis-biografica/). Principal corpus statement and thematic overview; reports 383 sculptures but does not publish their complete item-level inventory.

- Wifredo Viladrich estate, [“Estatuas”](https://wifredoviladrich.com/sus-obras/estatuas/). Selective object-type catalogue with titles and dates.

- Wifredo Viladrich estate, [“Obras en lugares públicos”](https://wifredoviladrich.com/sus-obras/obras-en-lugares-publicos/). Selective catalogue of public manifestations; repeated photographs were not counted as separate works.

- Wifredo Viladrich estate, [“Premios obtenidos”](https://wifredoviladrich.com/trayectoria/premios-obtenidos/). Chronology of prize-winning works and monument projects.

- Wifredo Viladrich estate, [works and text archive](https://wifredoviladrich.com/). Selective galleries, workshop documentation, and excerpts from Viladrich’s writings and lectures.

- Universidad Argentina de la Empresa, [*Wifredo Viladrich: Esculturas*](https://www.uade.edu.ar/media/bpiai135/viladrich_ok.pdf), exhibition catalogue, curatorial text by Lucila Lara. Provides eleven object-level records with dates, media, and dimensions.

- Museo Nacional de Bellas Artes, [*La hora americana 1910–1950*](https://media.bellasartes.gob.ar/h/Publicaciones/la_hora_americana_catalogo.pdf), exhibition catalogue. Consulted for the 1943 bronze manifestation of *Hijo de la tierra*.

- *España Republicana*, [1 June 1946 issue](https://transhemisferica.com.ar/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/810-01-06-1946.pdf). Contemporary evidence for *Pueblo español* and Viladrich’s manifesto-like public statement.

- Legislatura de la Ciudad Autónoma de Buenos Aires, [Recoleta Cemetery guide](https://pergamo.legislatura.gob.ar/pgmedia/biblioteca/LIBROS%20DIGITALIZADOS/018085_D.pdf). Consulted for the Crociati funerary sculpture and the later Buenos Aires manifestation of *Alas de la Patria*.

- The Universal Compendium, [“Tomb of Liliana Crociati de Szaszak and her dog, Sabú”](https://universalcompendium.com/gen_images/ucg/1sculpture/recoleta-cementerio-liliana-crociati-szaszak-argentina-cemetery-buenos-aires.htm). Photographic documentation of the bronze funerary ensemble and its two-stage formation.

- Guía de Playa, [“La Isla del Golf Pinamar”](https://www.guiadeplaya.com.ar/la-isla-del-golf-pinamar.html). Consulted for the public manifestation of *Adelante* at Pinamar.
