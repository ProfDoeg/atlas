## Works by Rudolf Laban

### Choreographic works, dance-theatre pieces, movement choirs, and unrealized stage projects

- **1897–1914:** *Szenische Entwürfe zur „Erde“* (*Scenic Designs for “The Earth”*). An evolving Gesamtkunstwerk-project combining drawings, creation myths, movement, music, and festival theatre; the catalogue separates *Hymne vom Traum von der Erde*, *Fiur-Schöpfungsmythos*, and *Das Fest* as constituent designs, so it resists identification as a single completed performance. [Dörr’s catalogue](https://d-nb.info/957453590/04)
- **1912–1914?:** *Die Geburt des Menschen*. A manuscript dance or cultic stage project, probably tried in Munich or Ascona; evidence of public performance is uncertain. [Dörr’s catalogue](https://d-nb.info/957453590/04)
- **1912–1914?:** *Anarchos*. A manuscript dance project probably associated with Munich or Ascona; its performance status is uncertain. [Dörr’s catalogue](https://d-nb.info/957453590/04)
- **1912–1914?:** *Zwischen den Schatten*. A manuscript choreography or dance-poem; it may have been performed, but the surviving record does not establish a premiere. [Dörr’s catalogue](https://d-nb.info/957453590/04)
- **1899:** *Fest der Fähnriche*. A cadets’ festival staged at the military academy in Vienna—an occasional pageant rather than an autonomous repertory dance. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1907:** *Fest des Tigers*. A festival-performance created in Nice; documentation is sparse. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1913:** *Der Trommelstock tanzt*. A dance made at Ascona. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1913:** *Istars Höllenfahrt*. A dance-theatre work made at Ascona around the myth of Ishtar’s descent. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1912–1914?:** *Der mystische Schrei*. A manuscript dance project, probably performed in Munich or Ascona but not securely documented as a production. [Dörr’s catalogue](https://d-nb.info/957453590/04)
- **1914:** *Hexensabbat – Die Geburt des Tanzes aus der Hölle*. A carnival dance-theatre manifestation in Munich, joining grotesque movement and festival ritual. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1914:** *Ein Tag im Hain des Äskulap*. A Munich carnival performance; it is an occasional theatrical event rather than a preserved dance text. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1914:** *Tanz-Demonstrationen in München* / *Der freie Tanz – Der Tanz ohne Musik*. A lecture-demonstration protocol made manifest through numerous movement studies, rhythmic sequences, duets, trios, lentos, grotesques, and dances; the catalogue treats the event as a work although its components were exercises as well as performances. [Dörr’s catalogue](https://d-nb.info/957453590/04), [chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1914:** *Sieg des Opfers*. A dance drama based on Hans Brandenburg’s play; it reached rehearsals at Ascona but apparently no public performance, making it an unrealized project rather than a completed manifestation. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1918?:** *Der Tod*. A manuscript dance or dramatic sketch, possibly performed at the Zürich school; the date and performance status remain uncertain. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1918?:** *Die drei Tänze*. A manuscript dance project possibly performed in Zürich; documentation does not securely distinguish script, choreography, and event. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1918?:** *Das Geheimnis*. A manuscript dance work possibly performed at the Zürich School for the Art of Movement. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1916:** *Der Spielmann*. A danced fairy tale presented in Zürich and described as Laban’s first extended dance work. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1916–1917:** *Tanzabende in Zürich*. School dance-evenings comprising solos, ensemble studies, pantomimic scenes, and dances to Schubert, Rameau, Brahms, Strauss, Beethoven, and others; they resist being treated as one fixed work because their programmes changed. [Dörr’s catalogue](https://d-nb.info/957453590/04), [chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1918?:** *Macbeth*. A projected stage work surviving only in drawings; it is a visual-dramaturgical project, not a documented choreography in performance. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1917:** *Sonnenfest*. A festival Gesamtkunstwerk made for an esoteric congress at Ascona, incorporating *Sang an die Sonne*, *Die Wunderblume*, and *Alte Riten und Tänze*. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1917:** *Die Wunderblume*. A constituent dance of *Sonnenfest*, also catalogued separately; its double status as part and work should be retained. [Dörr’s catalogue](https://d-nb.info/957453590/04)
- **1917:** *Alte Riten und Tänze*. A constituent sequence within *Sonnenfest*, combining invented ritual and dance. [Dörr’s catalogue](https://d-nb.info/957453590/04)
- **1918?:** *Die Tänzer vom Berg*. A manuscript dance work, perhaps performed in Zürich; realization is not firmly established. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1918?:** *Der Pfad der Tugend*. A manuscript dance or pantomime, possibly staged at the Zürich school. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1918?:** *Die Persische Braut*. A manuscript dance-theatre work whose performance is uncertain. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1918?:** *Die Grimasse des Sultans*. A manuscript dance or pantomime, possibly performed in Zürich. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1918?:** *Gualdi-Fragment*. A fragmentary manuscript, probably conceived as film pantomime; incompletion is part of its status as an obra. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1921:** *Tannhäuser-Bacchanal* (Mannheim version). Laban’s choreographic contribution to Wagner’s opera at Mannheim, not authorship of the opera itself. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1921:** *Epische Tanzfolge in vier Reigen (Die Geblendeten)*. A four-part “epic dance sequence” and political dance drama premiered at Mannheim; sources also shorten the title to *Die Geblendeten*. [Dörr’s catalogue](https://d-nb.info/957453590/04), [Maletic](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9783110861839_A19971535/preview-9783110861839_A19971535.pdf)
- **1922:** *Himmel und Erde*. A dance-theatre piece with music by Friedrich Wilckens and spoken German text, staged at the Württembergisches Landestheater. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1922:** *Mosaik*. A stage choreography presented at Lübeck’s Stadttheater. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1922:** *Fausts Erlösung*. A dance–sound–word Gesamtkunstwerk using a speech choir and including the dances *Das Feuer* and *Grabgesang*. [Laban Guild](https://labanguildinternational.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/MovementDanceDramaSpring2016.pdf), [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1922:** *Der schwingende Tempel*. A large movement-and-colour “symphony” in five rounds, lasting approximately three hours; later English usage rendered the title *The Swinging Cathedral*. [Laban Guild](https://labanguildinternational.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/MovementDanceDramaSpring2016.pdf), [Maletic](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9783110861839_A19971535/preview-9783110861839_A19971535.pdf)
- **1923:** *Schwingende Gewalten*. A choreography performed in Stuttgart. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1923:** *Drachentöterei*. A chamber-dance satire presented within a programme of serious and comic dances; its autonomy from that programme is not entirely stable. [Dörr’s catalogue](https://d-nb.info/957453590/04), [chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1923:** *Tschaikowski-Serenade*. A choreography presented at Rostock’s Stadttheater. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1923:** *Lichtwende* (also *Sehnsucht Lichtwende*). A chamber dance presented at Hamburg’s Conventgarten; sources preserve variant titles. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1923:** *Josephslegende*. Laban’s choreographic contribution to the Hamburg staging of Richard Strauss’s work. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1923:** *Gaukelei*. A socially inflected dance drama using music by Mozart and Haydn; surviving writings and stage plans mediate a work whose original performance is lost. [The Laban Sourcebook](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781136979484_A24268502/preview-9781136979484_A24268502.pdf), [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1923:** *Komödie*. A dance-theatre comedy staged in Hamburg. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1923:** *Romanze*. A chamber dance catalogued independently although it appeared inside a mixed dance evening. [Dörr’s catalogue](https://d-nb.info/957453590/04)
- **1923:** *Wintermärchen*. Laban’s choreographic contribution to a production at Hamburg’s Deutsches Schauspielhaus, not authorship of the underlying drama. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1923:** *Prometheus*. A dance drama staged in Hamburg, incorporating spoken or choral language as well as movement. [Maletic](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9783110861839_A19971535/preview-9783110861839_A19971535.pdf), [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1924:** *Agamemnons Tod*. A movement-choir work based on Aeschylus’s *Oresteia*, staged in Hamburg. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1924:** *Faust I*. Choreographic material made for a Hamburg production of Goethe’s drama. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1924:** *Sommernachtstraum*. Choreographic material for a Hamburg production of *A Midsummer Night’s Dream*. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1924:** *Phantastische Revue – Wachsfigurenkabinett in zwölf Bildern*. A fantastic dance revue or “waxworks” in twelve scenes; it is a composite theatrical event rather than a unitary dance. [Dörr’s catalogue](https://d-nb.info/957453590/04), [chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)

- **1925:** *Kampf der Farben*. A dance work presented with *Dämmernde Rhythmen* at Hamburg’s Stadttheater. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)

- **1925:** *Terpsichore*. A staging derived from Handel, choreographed jointly by Laban and Dussia Bereska; sole authorship should therefore not be assigned to Laban. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)

A set of satirical solo and group dances—including *Psychomechanik*, *Verkettungen*, *Klub der Sonderlinge*, *Gedankenflucht*, *Firlefanz*, *Zeitlupe*, *Militarismus*, and *Atonale*—whose identity lies between cycle and programme. A manuscript work perhaps conceived earlier in Zürich; sources disagree implicitly by placing it among both the early scripts and the later catalogue sequence, and no performance is secure. [Dörr’s catalogue](https://d-nb.info/957453590/04), [chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)

### Chamber-dance cycles and programme-works

- **1919–1929:** *Kammertanz-Inszenierungen*. Dörr catalogues Laban’s chamber-dance productions year by year as a distinct body of work rather than as one stable repertory; programmes repeatedly regrouped solos, duets, études, grotesques, “ornamental,” “ecstatic,” and “eukinetic” dances, so treating every printed programme-title as an autonomous work would manufacture boundaries the practice did not possess. [Dörr’s catalogue](https://d-nb.info/957453590/04)
- **1923:** *Zaubergarten*. An ornamental danced fairy tale shown on the Hamburg Zoo rehearsal stage. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1924:** *Ornament*. A chamber-dance work or programme item presented in Hamburg. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1924:** *Burleske Tanzrevue*. A Hamburg dance revue; its precise programme is not securely recovered. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1925:** *Orchestik*. A chamber-dance series presented at Hamburg’s Volksoper, better understood as a cycle than a single fixed choreography. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1925:** *Choreographische Tänze*. A programme divided into dances, choreographic studies, grotesques, and a burlesque dance suite; its constituent studies moved between pedagogical exercise and public artwork. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1925:** *Tanzszenen aus Richard Wagner*. A programme of independent dance responses to *Der Ring des Nibelungen*, *Tristan und Isolde*, and *Parsifal*, including *Alberich und Minne*, *Heldisches Thema*, *Feuerzauber*, *Trauertanz*, and *Klingsor und Kundry*. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1926:** *Ekstatische Tänze*. A Berlin programme or cycle comprising such pieces as *Gebet*, *Mosaik*, *Schatten*, *Träume*, and *Kristall*; the boundary between cycle and evening is unstable. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1927:** *Die lustigen Sterne* / *Überdrehtes Planetarium*. A dance work performed under two titles, probably at the Hamburg Zoo rehearsal stage. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1927:** *Eine Biedermeiergeschichte*. A programme-work presented in Leipzig with *Der Besuch*, *Elegie*, *Mammon—Liebe—Technik*, and *Die Maske*. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1928:** *Ka-Ta-Bü-Revue*. A chamber-dance-company revue presented with *Der rote Punkt*; surviving descriptions do not fix a single authoritative form. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1929:** *Gesellschaftssatiren*. A cycle of social satires assembled from a Persian march, Strauss quartet, tango, grotesque march, masquerade, and material from *Narrenspiegel*; it is a mutable programme rather than a closed dance text. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)

### Film works, filmed manifestations, and film projects

- **1924–1925:** *Orchidee*. An ornamental dance incorporated into Wilhelm Prager’s film *Wege zu Kraft und Schönheit*; Laban made the choreography, not the encompassing film. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **1928:** *Bewegungschorische Sequenz (Viering)*. A filmed movement-choir sequence catalogued among recordings of Laban’s choreographic work. [Dörr’s catalogue](https://d-nb.info/957453590/04)
- **1928:** *Ikosaeder (Dimensionen)*. A filmed demonstration or study using Laban’s icosahedral spatial model; it lies between pedagogical document and experimental dance film. [Dörr’s catalogue](https://d-nb.info/957453590/04)
- **1928:** *Ikosaeder (Tänzertypen)*. A filmed study of dancer-types within the icosahedral model. [Dörr’s catalogue](https://d-nb.info/957453590/04)
- **1928:** *Bewegungschor (Runde)*. A filmed round-form movement choir, catalogued as documentation of Laban’s choreography. [Dörr’s catalogue](https://d-nb.info/957453590/04)
- **c. 1928–1930:** *Das lebende Bild*. A film manuscript rather than a securely completed film. [Dörr’s catalogue](https://d-nb.info/957453590/04)
- **c. 1928–1930:** *Plan zum Gruppenform-Lehrfilm*. A protocol and plan for an instructional film about group form; its intention, rather than a finished motion picture, is the surviving work. [Dörr’s catalogue](https://d-nb.info/957453590/04)
- **c. 1928–1930:** *Tanz ist Leben*. A film manuscript or proposed sound-film work; completion is not established. [Dörr’s catalogue](https://d-nb.info/957453590/04)
- **c. 1928–1930:** *Die Befreiung des Körpers*. A film script/project about bodily liberation; it should not be silently promoted into a completed film. [Dörr’s catalogue](https://d-nb.info/957453590/04)
- **c. 1928–1930:** *Tanz der Menschheit*. A film manuscript conceived on a civilizational scale; the surviving obra is the project-text. [Dörr’s catalogue](https://d-nb.info/957453590/04)
- **1934:** *Spuk im Spielklub* / *Ein Spiel Karten*. A dance film directed and choreographed by Laban and premiered under the former title; sources differ in which title they privilege. [Dörr’s catalogue](https://d-nb.info/957453590/04), [chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)

### Books and autonomous treatises

- **1920; book edition 1922:** *Die Welt des Tänzers: Fünf Gedankenreigen*. A philosophical dance book organized as five “thought-rounds”; the sourcebook dates the text to 1920, while the standard bibliography records the Stuttgart edition of 1922. [The Laban Sourcebook](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781136979484_A24268502/preview-9781136979484_A24268502.pdf), [ICKL bibliography](https://ickl.org/resources/bibliography-of-labans-books/)
- **1926:** *Choreographie: Erstes Heft*. A theoretical and graphic treatise on choreography and spatial organization; “first booklet” announces a larger project that was not completed in this published form. [ICKL bibliography](https://ickl.org/resources/bibliography-of-labans-books/)
- **1926:** *Gymnastik und Tanz*. A treatise connecting movement education, gymnastics, and dance. [ICKL bibliography](https://ickl.org/resources/bibliography-of-labans-books/)
- **1926:** *Gymnastics and Dance for Children*. A pedagogical text or protocol treated separately from *Gymnastik und Tanz* in the collected-writings scholarship; its precise original publication status is less clear than that of the German volume. [The Laban Sourcebook](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781136979484_A24268502/preview-9781136979484_A24268502.pdf)
- **1928:** *Schrifttanz: Methodik, Orthographie, Erläuterungen*. A protocol for writing movement and an exposition of Kinetographie Laban: simultaneously a book, a notational grammar, and an instrument for making future dances. [ICKL bibliography](https://ickl.org/resources/bibliography-of-labans-books/), [Library of Congress](https://findingaids.loc.gov/repositories/15/archival_objects/861459)
- **1930:** *Script Dancing / La Danse écrite*. A multilingual presentation of movement notation, not a new notation system independent of *Schrifttanz*. [ICKL bibliography](https://ickl.org/resources/bibliography-of-labans-books/)
- **1930:** *Kleine Tänze mit Vorübungen*. A collection of small written dances with preparatory exercises: part score, part teaching protocol. [ICKL bibliography](https://ickl.org/resources/bibliography-of-labans-books/)
- **1935:** *Ein Leben für den Tanz: Erinnerungen*. An autobiography or constructed memoir; its later English translation *A Life for Dance* is an edition, not a separate work. [ICKL bibliography](https://ickl.org/resources/bibliography-of-labans-books/)
- **1938; published 1966:** *Choreutics*. A treatise on spatial harmony written in 1938 but published posthumously under Lisa Ullmann’s editorship; the published object is therefore jointly shaped by authorial manuscript and editorial manifestation. [The Laban Sourcebook](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781136979484_A24268502/preview-9781136979484_A24268502.pdf), [ICKL bibliography](https://ickl.org/resources/bibliography-of-labans-books/)
- **mid-1940s:** *The Art of Movement in Education, Work and Recreation*. An unpublished or incompletely published synthesis identified in the collected-writings edition; it is a manuscript project rather than a conventional book issued in Laban’s lifetime. [The Laban Sourcebook](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781136979484_A24268502/preview-9781136979484_A24268502.pdf)
- **1947:** *Effort*. A co-authored study with F. C. Lawrence applying movement analysis to industrial labour; the 1974 title *Effort: Economy of Human Movement* is a revised edition, not another original work. [ICKL bibliography](https://ickl.org/resources/bibliography-of-labans-books/)
- **1948:** *Modern Educational Dance*. A pedagogical protocol for creative movement education; later editions and translations are manifestations of the same work. [ICKL bibliography](https://ickl.org/resources/bibliography-of-labans-books/)
- **1950:** *The Mastery of Movement on the Stage*. A movement manual for performers; later editions shortened the title to *The Mastery of Movement*. [ICKL bibliography](https://ickl.org/resources/bibliography-of-labans-books/)
- **early 1950s:** *Answers to Ten Questions on Industrial Rhythm*. A concise explanatory text on applying movement analysis to work, preserved and published through later collected writings. [The Laban Sourcebook](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781136979484_A24268502/preview-9781136979484_A24268502.pdf)
- **early 1950s:** *Effort and Recovery*. An unfinished manuscript investigation linking movement, exertion, recuperation, and psychological life; excerpts and associated Withymead notes survive, but no final authorial book-form was fixed. [The Laban Sourcebook](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781136979484_A24268502/preview-9781136979484_A24268502.pdf)
- **early 1950s:** *The Harmony of Movement*. An unpublished manuscript body on harmonic movement structures; it resists the singular “book” category because later scholarship reconstructs it from writings and drawings. [The Laban Sourcebook](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781136979484_A24268502/preview-9781136979484_A24268502.pdf)
- **1954:** *Principles of Dance and Movement Notation*. A late protocol explaining dance and movement notation; later editions were revised and annotated by Diana Baddeley and Roderyk Lange and should not be confused with Laban’s unmediated original text. [ICKL bibliography](https://ickl.org/resources/bibliography-of-labans-books/)

### Systems, methods, and institutional obra

- **c. 1913–1928, subsequently revised by others:** *Kinetographie Laban* / *Labanotation*. A symbolic system for recording bodily movement, publicly codified in 1928; it is both Laban’s made protocol and a collaborative, continuing language subsequently developed by pupils and institutions. [Library of Congress](https://findingaids.loc.gov/repositories/15/archival_objects/861459), [Trinity Laban](https://www.trinitylaban.ac.uk/student-life/libraries/collections-and-archives/)
- **1910s–1950s:** *Choreutics*. A theory and practice of spatial harmony embodied in scales, spatial forms, drawings, teaching, and the icosahedron as working model; it is a body of work rather than only the posthumous book bearing its name. [The Laban Sourcebook](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781136979484_A24268502/preview-9781136979484_A24268502.pdf)
- **1920s–1950s:** *Eukinetics*. A practical-analytic system for the dynamic qualities of movement; it persisted through teaching and notation rather than as one fixed object. [The Laban Sourcebook](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781136979484_A24268502/preview-9781136979484_A24268502.pdf)
- **1910s–1930s:** *Bewegungschor* (movement choir). A participatory form for composing mass movement, realized in many different communities and spectacles; it is a reproducible social protocol and body of work, not a single choreography. [The Laban Sourcebook](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781136979484_A24268502/preview-9781136979484_A24268502.pdf)
- **1910s–1920s:** *Tanz–Ton–Wort*. A multimedia compositional and teaching practice integrating dance, sound, and spoken word; it functions as a Gesamtkunstwerk-principle rather than a separately premiered work. [Bloomsbury description of Doerr](https://www.bloomsbury.com/us/rudolf-laban-9798765185605/)
- **1940s–1950s:** *Effort theory*. A movement-analysis system developed with important contributions from F. C. Lawrence, Lisa Ullmann, and later Warren Lamb; attribution to Laban alone would conceal its collaborative formation. [The Laban Sourcebook](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781136979484_A24268502/preview-9781136979484_A24268502.pdf), [University of Surrey archive](https://www.surrey.ac.uk/library/archives-and-special-collections/our-collections)
- **1940s–1950s:** *Laban–Lawrence Industrial Rhythm*. A collaborative method for observing and reorganizing movement in industrial work; it is an applied protocol, consultancy practice, and archive rather than a bounded artwork. [University of Surrey archive](https://www.surrey.ac.uk/library/archives-and-special-collections/our-collections)
- **1913 onward:** *Laban schools and Choreographic Institutes*. A transnational educational and organizational project begun in Ascona and developed through Zürich, Hamburg, Würzburg, Berlin, and affiliated schools; because pupils and local directors made much of the network, it is properly a collaborative institutional obra rather than Laban’s sole creation. [The Laban Sourcebook](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781136979484_A24268502/preview-9781136979484_A24268502.pdf)
- **1934 onward:** *Deutsche Tanzfestspiele*. A national dance-festival project initiated by Laban under the Nazi cultural administration; it is institution-making, politically compromised work, not simply a choreography. [German biographical account](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rudolf_von_Laban)

### Drawings, paintings, sculpture, architecture, and models

- **c. 1897:** *Drawings for Die Erde*. The earliest surviving conception of *Die Erde* took the form of scenic drawings, making the images both visual works and plans for a larger theatrical project. [Dörr-derived chronology](https://milleballetti.weebly.com/laban.html)
- **Before 1910–1958:** *Visual-art oeuvre*. Laban made paintings, drawings, sculpture, architectural designs, movement diagrams, and geometric studies throughout his life; no cited catalogue raisonné itemizes every sheet, and the archival collections therefore preserve this as an open, incompletely enumerated body of work rather than a safely countable list of titled objects. [Bloomsbury description of Doerr](https://www.bloomsbury.com/us/rudolf-laban-9798765185605/), [University of Leeds collection](https://library.leeds.ac.uk/special-collections/collection/931)
- **1920s:** *Icosahedron movement model*. A three-dimensional spatial instrument used to investigate and teach pathways of movement; it is simultaneously sculpture, research apparatus, pedagogical protocol, and emblem of choreutics. [The Laban Sourcebook](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781136979484_A24268502/preview-9781136979484_A24268502.pdf)
- **Undated:** *Movement and space drawings*. Numerous diagrams and drawings formulate scales, spatial pathways, dynamic forms, and harmonic structures; their resistance to separation into “art,” “notation,” and “research” is intrinsic to what they are. [University of Leeds collection](https://library.leeds.ac.uk/special-collections/collection/931), [University of Surrey archive](https://www.surrey.ac.uk/library/archives-and-special-collections/our-collections)
