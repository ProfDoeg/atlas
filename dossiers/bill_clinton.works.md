## Works by William Jefferson Clinton

### Political manifestos and programmatic books

- **1991:** *The New Covenant speeches*. A three-part series of campaign addresses delivered at Georgetown University, setting out Clinton’s governing philosophy; they function collectively as a manifesto rather than as a conventional book. The speeches survive within a much larger, partly collaborative rhetorical record shaped by advisers and speechwriters. [Georgetown University](https://www.georgetown.edu/news/the-clinton-lectures/); [James D. Boys, *Politics*](https://doi.org/10.1177/0263395720935782)

- **1992:** *Putting People First: How We Can All Change America*. A presidential-campaign program coauthored with Al Gore: simultaneously a book, manifesto, and prospective governing plan. Its collective authorship should not be collapsed into sole authorship by Clinton. [Google Books bibliographic record](https://books.google.com/books?id=5spySXfHRtIC); [Christopher Newport University primary-source guide](https://cnu.libguides.com/primarypresidents/billclinton)

- **1996:** *Between Hope and History: Meeting America’s Challenges for the 21st Century*. A short statement of Clinton’s “New Democrat” principles and first-term policy record, published during his reelection campaign; its political timing and purpose make it as much a manifesto as a reflective book. [*Washington Post* review and publication record](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/entertainment/books/1996/08/25/ruffles-and-flourishes/11fcb79f-122f-406c-a696-73879e872258/)

- **2007:** *Giving: How Each of Us Can Change the World*. A work of civic exhortation combining examples of philanthropy with a protocol for individual action. [Penguin Random House author bibliography](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/authors/5160/bill-clinton/)

- **2011:** *Back to Work: Why We Need Smart Government for a Strong Economy*. A policy argument and proposed program for economic recovery; it is a manifesto in the active sense of urging governmental and citizen action. [Penguin Random House author bibliography](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/authors/5160/bill-clinton/)

### Memoirs

- **2004:** *My Life*. Clinton’s presidential autobiography and life narrative through the end of his presidency. The author-narrated audiobook is a manifestation of the same obra, not a separate work; the publication’s acknowledged editorial production does not displace Clinton’s stated authorship. [Penguin Random House author bibliography](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/authors/5160/bill-clinton/)

- **2024:** *Citizen: My Life After the White House*. A second memoir covering Clinton’s post-presidential life and public activity from 2001 onward. It continues rather than republishes *My Life*. [Penguin Random House author bibliography](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/authors/5160/bill-clinton/); [Associated Press publication announcement](https://apnews.com/article/9daddfec321b49c84052962b84bd16b2)

### Novels made with James Patterson

- **2018:** *The President Is Missing*. A political thriller coauthored with James Patterson; the divided contribution of the two writers is not publicly reducible to separately attributable passages, so the novel is properly treated as a joint obra. [James Patterson’s official bibliography](https://www.jamespatterson.com/titles/james-patterson/the-first-gentleman/9780316565103/)

- **2021:** *The President’s Daughter*. A political thriller coauthored with James Patterson and connected to *The President Is Missing* by authorship and presidential subject matter, though not simply a new edition of it. [James Patterson’s official bibliography](https://www.jamespatterson.com/titles/james-patterson/the-first-gentleman/9780316565103/)

- **2025:** *The First Gentleman*. A political thriller coauthored with James Patterson. It is a separate fictional work, not a continuation of the earlier novels, despite being marketed through the same collaboration and presidential expertise. [James Patterson’s official book record](https://www.jamespatterson.com/titles/james-patterson/the-first-gentleman/9780316565103/)

### Presidential corpus

- **1993–2001:** *Public Papers of the Presidents of the United States: William J. Clinton*. The official collected corpus of Clinton’s public presidential writings, addresses, remarks, proclamations, executive orders, interviews, and related statements. It resists treatment as either one personally written work or thousands of straightforwardly authored works: presidential texts act with Clinton’s authority but were frequently drafted, revised, or assembled collaboratively, while the printed annual books are editorial manifestations created by the Office of the Federal Register. [GovInfo collection description](https://www.govinfo.gov/help/ppp); [1993, Book I record](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/PPP-1993-book1)

- **1993–2001:** *Clinton Administration White House websites and Virtual Library*. A born-digital administrative corpus containing executive orders, radio addresses, press briefings, speeches, and other White House publications. It is a collective Gesamtkunstwerk of text, government action, institutional authorship, and early-web presentation, not a work made by Clinton alone; five successive website versions are preserved. [William J. Clinton Presidential Library research guide](https://www.clintonlibrary.gov/research)

### Recorded and broadcast musical performances

- **1992:** *“Heartbreak Hotel” on The Arsenio Hall Show*. A televised tenor-saxophone performance by Clinton with the program’s band. It is a performance rather than a composition or book, and Clinton did not create the underlying song. [History](https://www.history.com/this-day-in-history/june-3/bill-clinton-saxophone-arsenio-hall); [*Los Angeles Times* contemporary review](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1992-06-05-ca-472-story.html)

- **1994:** *“Summertime” and “My Funny Valentine” at Reduta Jazz Club, Prague*. Improvised tenor-saxophone performances with a Czech ensemble, subsequently issued on CD. Clinton made the performances but neither composed the standards nor made the recording as a sole creator; the CD is their recorded manifestation. [*Los Angeles Times*](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1994-09-05-ca-35120-story.html)

### Institutional project

- **2001–present:** *Clinton Foundation*. A continuing operating-foundation project established by Clinton, later shaped and governed with Hillary Rodham Clinton, Chelsea Clinton, employees, partners, and donors. It therefore resists classification as his individual obra: what Clinton made was an institutional framework whose programs and outcomes are collective and unfinished. [Clinton Foundation](https://www.clintonfoundation.org/get-involved); [Clinton Foundation 2012 annual report](https://www.clintonfoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/clintonfoundation2012annualreport.pdf)
