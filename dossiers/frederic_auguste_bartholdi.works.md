## Works by Frédéric Auguste Bartholdi

### Monumental sculpture, fountains, and public statuary

- **1852–1856:** *Monument au général Rapp*. Monumental bronze statue commissioned for Colmar, exhibited at the 1855 Exposition universelle and inaugurated in 1856; dismantled during the German occupation and subsequently restored. ([Musée Bartholdi](https://www.musee-bartholdi.fr/decouvrir-bartholdi/biographie/); [Musée Bartholdi chronology](https://www.musee-bartholdi.fr/decouvrir-bartholdi/reperes-chronologiques/))
- **1857–1859:** *Projet de fontaine monumentale pour Bordeaux* (later *Char de la Liberté* or *Fontaine Bartholdi*). A prizewinning architectural-sculptural project for the Place des Quinconces that Bordeaux did not execute; substantially reworked decades later for Lyon, so project and realized fountain are related but not identical things. ([Musée Bartholdi](https://www.musee-bartholdi.fr/sites/musee-bartholdi/files/kiosque/colmar-mag-03-bd.pdf); [Lyon tourism office](https://www.visiterlyon.com/decouvrir/sites-et-monuments/sites-et-monuments-remarquables/la-fontaine-bartholdi))
- **1859:** *Palais Longchamp*. Unexecuted Gesamtkunstwerk-like project combining waterworks, architecture, landscape, and monumental sculpture for Marseille; the commission went to Henri-Jacques Espérandieu, and Bartholdi pursued an unsuccessful authorship claim over elements of the resulting design. ([Musée Bartholdi chronology](https://www.musee-bartholdi.fr/decouvrir-bartholdi/reperes-chronologiques/); [Musée Bartholdi biography](https://www.musee-bartholdi.fr/decouvrir-bartholdi/biographie/))
- **1861–1863:** *Monument à Martin Schongauer* or *Fontaine Schongauer*. Fountain-monument made for the Unterlinden cloister in Colmar, incorporating the standing figure of Schongauer and four allegories of his arts; it was later dismantled, leaving its constituent sculptures to separate institutional histories. ([CRDP, 1860–1869](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr2.html); [Musée Bartholdi chronology](https://www.musee-bartholdi.fr/decouvrir-bartholdi/reperes-chronologiques/))
- **c. 1861–1863:** *L’Orfèvrerie*. Allegorical figure made as part of the Schongauer fountain ensemble; its present separation from that monument makes it both an autonomous sculpture and a surviving fragment of a larger obra. ([Paris Musées](https://www.parismuseescollections.paris.fr/fr/recherche/auteur/Bartholdi%2C%20Fr%C3%A9d%C3%A9ric%20Auguste))
- **c. 1861–1863:** *L’Étude*. Allegorical figure belonging to the original Schongauer monument and now treated as an individual museum object. ([CRDP, 1860–1869](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr2.html))
- **c. 1861–1863:** *La Gravure*. Allegorical figure from the Schongauer monument, subsequently detached from its architectural-fountain context. ([Paris Musées](https://www.parismuseescollections.paris.fr/fr/recherche/auteur/Bartholdi%2C%20Fr%C3%A9d%C3%A9ric%20Auguste))
- **c. 1861–1863:** *La Peinture*. Allegorical figure made for the Schongauer monument; its identity resists a clean division between independent statue and component. ([CRDP, 1860–1869](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr2.html))
- **1861–1864:** *Monument à l’amiral Armand-Joseph Bruat* or *Fontaine Bruat*. Monumental fountain in Colmar combining Bruat’s portrait statue with allegorical figures; the original bronzes were destroyed under German occupation, and the present monument is a postwar restitution rather than the untouched original obra. ([CRDP, 1860–1869](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr2.html); [Musée Bartholdi chronology](https://www.musee-bartholdi.fr/decouvrir-bartholdi/reperes-chronologiques/))
- **1866:** *La France victorieuse*. Bas-relief project for the Palais de l’Exposition universelle de 1867; preserved as a model rather than realized as the proposed architectural decoration. ([CRDP, 1860–1869](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr2.html))
- **1867–1868:** *Monument au général Jean-Thomas Arrighi de Casanova, duc de Padoue*. Monumental bronze portrait statue erected at Corte. ([CRDP, 1860–1869](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr2.html); [Musée Bartholdi chronology](https://www.musee-bartholdi.fr/decouvrir-bartholdi/reperes-chronologiques/))
- **1869:** *Le Petit Vigneron* or *Jeune Vigneron alsacien*. Bronze fountain figure made for Colmar’s covered market; casts and versions also circulate under such titles as *Le Vigneron buveur d’eau* and *L’Assoiffé*, so the title does not identify one uniquely situated object. ([CRDP, 1860–1869](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr2.html); [Wikisculpture](https://www.wikisculpture.fr/index.php/bios-sculpteur/554-bartholdi-frederic-auguste))
- **1869–1903:** *Vercingétorix*. Equestrian monument conceived and exhibited in plaster at the 1870 Salon, substantially completed around 1901 and erected at Clermont-Ferrand in 1903; its thirty-year development makes it a long project as well as a monument. ([Base Salons](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/36371); [Musée Bartholdi](https://www.musee-bartholdi.fr/sites/museebartholdi/files/musee-bartholdi-visite-scolaire-biographie-auguste-bartholdi.pdf))
- **1872–1876:** *Lafayette arrivant en Amérique*. Monumental statue developed from a plaster exhibited in 1873 and installed in Union Square, New York, in 1876. ([CRDP, 1870–1879](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr3.html); [Base Salons](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/36371))
- **1872–1880:** *Le Lion de Belfort* or *Monument aux défenseurs de Belfort*. Colossal sandstone relief carved into the citadel hillside in commemoration of the 1870–71 siege; the completed site-bound monument and Bartholdi’s numerous models and reductions are distinct manifestations. ([CRDP, 1870–1879](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr3.html); [CRDP, 1880–1889](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr4.html))
- **1874:** *Les Quatre Étapes de la vie chrétienne*. Four architectural bas-reliefs and trumpet-bearing angels designed for Brattle Square Church, Boston; because they form part of the building, the cycle resists treatment as a single freestanding sculpture. ([Musée Bartholdi chronology](https://www.musee-bartholdi.fr/decouvrir-bartholdi/reperes-chronologiques/); [Base Salons](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/36371))
- **1875–1878:** *Jean-François Champollion*. Monumental portrait statue first shown at the 1875 Salon and subsequently installed in marble at the Collège de France; terracotta and plaster versions survive as separate manifestations. ([CRDP, 1870–1879](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr3.html); [Wikisculpture](https://www.wikisculpture.fr/index.php/bios-sculpteur/554-bartholdi-frederic-auguste))
- **1875–1878:** *Fontaine du Capitole*. Cast-iron monumental fountain shown at the 1876 Philadelphia exhibition and installed in Washington in 1878; its architecture, lighting, figures, water, and industrial casting make it closer to a Gesamtkunstwerk than to a single statue. ([Musée Bartholdi chronology](https://www.musee-bartholdi.fr/decouvrir-bartholdi/reperes-chronologiques/))
- **1876–1879:** *Monument à Jean-Baptiste Vaquette de Gribeauval*. Standing portrait statue developed through museum models and installed at the Hôtel des Invalides in Paris; the Salon chronology dates the exhibited statue to 1879, while institutional summaries sometimes say 1878. ([CRDP, 1870–1879](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr3.html); [Musée Bartholdi chronology](https://www.musee-bartholdi.fr/decouvrir-bartholdi/reperes-chronologiques/))
- **1878–1880:** *Lion de Belfort, place Denfert-Rochereau*. Reduced monumental version in hammered copper, with a pedestal attributed in the Salon catalogue to M. Hugé and execution by Mesureur and Monduit; it is a new public object derived from, not merely identical with, the Belfort relief. ([Base Salons](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/36371); [Musée Bartholdi chronology](https://www.musee-bartholdi.fr/decouvrir-bartholdi/reperes-chronologiques/))
- **1881–1882:** *Monument à Rouget de Lisle*. Bronze portrait statue of the author of the “Marseillaise,” exhibited in plaster in 1881 and inaugurated at Lons-le-Saunier in 1882. ([CRDP, 1880–1889](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr4.html))
- **1884:** *Monument à Denis Diderot*. Monumental bronze portrait statue erected at Langres. ([CRDP, 1880–1889](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr4.html); [Musée Bartholdi chronology](https://www.musee-bartholdi.fr/decouvrir-bartholdi/reperes-chronologiques/))
- **1888:** *Fontaine Roesselmann* or *Monument à Jean Roesselmann*. Public fountain and bronze portrait statue in Colmar, preceded by a surviving plaster model. ([CRDP, 1880–1889](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr4.html))
- **1891–1892:** *Char de la Liberté* or *Fontaine Bartholdi*. Monumental fountain realized for Lyon from Bartholdi’s much earlier Bordeaux project; sources differ between installation or inauguration in 1891 and the commonly assigned completion date 1892. ([CRDP, 1890–1904](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr5.html); [Lyon tourism office](https://www.visiterlyon.com/decouvrir/sites-et-monuments/sites-et-monuments-remarquables/la-fontaine-bartholdi))
- **1891:** *Monument à Léon Gambetta*. Public portrait monument inaugurated at Ville-d’Avray; it is not the same object as Bartholdi’s earlier small silver homage to Gambetta. ([CRDP, 1890–1904](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr5.html))
- **1891–1895:** *Lafayette et Washington*. Double-figure monument exhibited in 1892 and inaugurated in the Place des États-Unis, Paris, in 1895; a replica was installed in New York in 1900. ([CRDP, 1890–1904](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr5.html); [Base Salons](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/36371))
- **1893–1894:** *Monument à Gustave-Adolphe Hirn*. Monument in Colmar combining Hirn’s portrait statue with an architectural setting and allegorical or scientific elements; the plaster model and realized bronze are distinct manifestations. ([CRDP, 1890–1904](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr5.html))
- **1891–1895:** *La Suisse secourant les douleurs de Strasbourg pendant le siège de 1870*. Monumental group commissioned for Basel, modeled by 1891, exhibited in 1895, and inaugurated that year; later reductions do not constitute the same physical object. ([CRDP, 1890–1904](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr5.html); [Paris Musées](https://www.parismuseescollections.paris.fr/fr/recherche/auteur/Bartholdi%2C%20Fr%C3%A9d%C3%A9ric%20Auguste))
- **1895–1896:** *Le Nirsbrey*. Bas-relief belonging to the Basel monument and exhibited separately at the 1896 Salon; its status oscillates between component, study, and autonomous exhibited work. ([Base Salons](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/36371))
- **1897–1898:** *Fontaine Schwendi* or *Monument à Lazare de Schwendi*. Bronze figure and fountain installed in Colmar’s Place de l’Ancienne-Douane. ([CRDP, 1890–1904](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr5.html))
- **1897–1899:** *Monument aux Français morts en 1870–1871* or *Victoria*. Funerary-public monument at Birr/Schinznach in Switzerland, surmounted by a winged Victory and commemorating soldiers of the Bourbaki army; place-names and titles vary among catalogues. ([CRDP, 1890–1904](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr5.html))
- **1901–1902:** *Les Grands Soutiens du monde*. Monumental bronze group in which Justice, Labor, and Patriotism sustain the globe, shown in plaster and then bronze at successive Salons; installed in the courtyard of Bartholdi’s former home only after his death. ([CRDP, 1890–1904](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr5.html))
- **1901–1902:** *Le Tonnelier alsacien*. Architectural statue modeled in plaster and installed atop the Maison des Têtes in Colmar; it is sculpture made as part of a building rather than an independent monument. ([CRDP, 1890–1904](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr5.html))
- **1902–1912:** *Monument des Trois Sièges*. Belfort memorial to the sieges of 1813–14, 1815, and 1870–71, preserved first as Bartholdi’s model and completed posthumously; the 1912 monument therefore includes execution beyond his lifetime and resists attribution to him alone. ([CRDP, 1890–1904](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr5.html))
- **1903–1906:** *Monument aux aéronautes du siège de Paris* or *Ballon des Ternes*. Monument designed by Bartholdi, exhibited posthumously in 1904 and inaugurated in 1906; it was melted down in 1941, so the obra survives chiefly through models and documentation. ([CRDP, 1890–1904](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr5.html))

### The Statue of Liberty as project, monument, and manifestations

- **1869:** *L’Égypte éclairant l’Orient* (also reported as *L’Égypte éclairant l’Asie*). Unexecuted project for a colossal lighthouse-woman at the entrance to the Suez Canal; only designs and models appeared, and the uncertain conventional title should not disguise its status as a proposal. ([CRDP, 1860–1869](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr2.html); [Musée Bartholdi chronology](https://www.musee-bartholdi.fr/decouvrir-bartholdi/reperes-chronologiques/))
- **c. 1870:** *La Liberté éclairant le monde*, first terracotta study. An early small model preserved at the Musée Bartholdi; it is a material stage in a project, not a miniature made after the New York monument. ([CRDP, 1870–1879](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr3.html))
- **1875–1886:** *La Liberté éclairant le monde*. Colossal copper monument for New York Harbor, conceived and modeled by Bartholdi, with structural engineering successively involving Eugène Viollet-le-Duc and Gustave Eiffel’s office; its collective fabrication means “made by Bartholdi” names artistic authorship, not sole manufacture. ([Musée Bartholdi chronology](https://www.musee-bartholdi.fr/decouvrir-bartholdi/reperes-chronologiques/); [Musée d’Orsay](https://www.musee-orsay.fr/fr/oeuvres/liberte-4813))
- **1876:** *La Liberté éclairant le monde: bras et flambeau*. Full-scale fragment displayed at the Philadelphia Centennial Exhibition as an autonomous fundraising attraction while also belonging conceptually to the unfinished monument. ([CRDP, 1870–1879](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr3.html))
- **1878:** *La Liberté éclairant le monde: tête*. Full-scale head exhibited in Paris in 1878; simultaneously a manufactured fragment, public manifestation, and demonstration of the incomplete monument. ([CRDP, 1870–1879](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr3.html))
- **1878:** *Tête de la Liberté*. Reduced bust or head preserved at the Musée Bartholdi, distinct from the full-scale exhibition fragment. ([CRDP, 1870–1879](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr3.html))
- **1878:** *La Liberté éclairant le monde, modèle dit “du Comité”*. Reduction made for the monument’s promotional and fundraising campaign; it belongs to the project’s material apparatus but is also a finished statuette. ([CRDP, 1870–1879](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr3.html))
- **1878:** *La Liberté éclairant le monde, modèle d’exécution au 1/16*. Plaster working model used in scaling the monument; its utilitarian role makes it closer to a protocol embodied in sculpture than to an independent display piece. ([Musée Bartholdi](https://www.musee-bartholdi.fr/decouvrir-bartholdi/biographie/); [Musée d’Orsay](https://www.musee-orsay.fr/fr/oeuvres/liberte-4813))
- **1885 or 1889:** *La Liberté éclairant le monde, île aux Cygnes*. Reduced bronze erected in Paris; the documentary chronology contains both 1885 and 1889 entries, while 1889 is the date generally attached to the present cast, so the dating should not be silently harmonized. ([CRDP, 1880–1889](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr4.html); [Musée d’Orsay](https://www.musee-orsay.fr/fr/oeuvres/liberte-4813))
- **1889:** *Liberté*. Bronze reduction later acquired for the Musée du Luxembourg and now held by the Musée d’Orsay; this is a separately cast obra, not the New York monument itself. ([Musée d’Orsay](https://www.musee-orsay.fr/fr/oeuvres/liberte-4813))
- **Undated editions during and after Bartholdi’s lifetime:** *La Liberté éclairant le monde*, authorized reductions. Numerous casts were produced at different scales and destinations; without a complete foundry census they resist enumeration as a single work, and later posthumous replicas should not automatically be counted as things Bartholdi personally made. ([Musée d’Orsay](https://www.musee-orsay.fr/fr/oeuvres/liberte-4813))

### Independent groups, statues, reliefs, and decorative objects

- **1852:** *Françoise de Rimini*. Early bas-relief made while Bartholdi was associated with Ary Scheffer’s studio; its date and status are reported by an early survey rather than by the modern selective chronology. ([L’Art en Alsace-Lorraine](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/cc/L%27art_en_Alsace-Lorraine_%28IA_lartenalsacelorr00mena%29.pdf))
- **1853:** *Le Bon Samaritain*. Small bronze group and Bartholdi’s first exhibited Salon work; it is an unusually intimate sculpture in an oeuvre dominated by monuments. ([Musée d’Orsay](https://www.musee-orsay.fr/en/artworks/le-bon-samaritain-25547))
- **1855:** *Les Sept Souabes*. Small bronze narrative group, signed and dated, made after a German folktale; the Musée Bartholdi identifies its surviving bronze as a *chef-modèle*. ([Musée Bartholdi](https://www.musee-bartholdi.fr/sites/museebartholdi/files/musee-bartholdi-visite-scolaire-1er-etage.pdf))
- **1857:** *La Lyre chez les Berbères, souvenir du Nil* or *La Lyre berbère*. Bronze group inspired by Bartholdi’s first Egyptian journey and shown at the 1857 Salon; the museum retains a model while the exhibited bronze entered a Lyon collection. ([Musée Bartholdi biography](https://www.musee-bartholdi.fr/decouvrir-bartholdi/biographie/); [Wikisculpture](https://www.wikisculpture.fr/index.php/bios-sculpteur/554-bartholdi-frederic-auguste))
- **1859:** *Le Génie dans les griffes de la misère*. Sculptural group exhibited at the Salon; its known title describes an allegory rather than a monument. ([Wikisculpture](https://www.wikisculpture.fr/index.php/bios-sculpteur/554-bartholdi-frederic-auguste); [L’Art en Alsace-Lorraine](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/cc/L%27art_en_Alsace-Lorraine_%28IA_lartenalsacelorr00mena%29.pdf))
- **1861–1864:** *Le Martyre moderne* or *Le Martyr moderne*. Allegorical group identifying partitioned Poland with a modern Prometheus, modeled by 1861 and shown at the 1864 Salon; singular and masculine forms of the French title both occur in cataloguing. ([CRDP, 1860–1869](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr2.html))
- **1867–1868:** *Les Loisirs de la Paix*. Bronze group, also exhibited in plaster, shown at the Salons of 1868 and 1873; those presentations are manifestations of one sculptural conception rather than necessarily one unchanged object. ([CRDP, 1860–1869](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr2.html); [Base Salons](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/36371))
- **1871:** *La Malédiction de l’Alsace*. Small silver group shown at the 1872 Salon, made as a politically charged commemorative object after annexation. ([CRDP, 1870–1879](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr3.html))
- **1872:** *À Gambetta, les Alsaciens reconnaissants*. Commemorative presentation object or group offered in gratitude to Gambetta; its status lies between sculpture, political gift, and manifesto in metal. ([Musée Bartholdi, world-work list](https://sevres-92310.fr/doc/Auguste_Bartholdi.pdf))
- **1875:** *Le Trio des enfants de chœur*. Terracotta bas-relief documented in a regional exhibition catalogue; it falls outside the usual monument-based summaries of Bartholdi’s oeuvre. ([Base Salons](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/36371))
- **1875:** *La Fête à papa*. Terracotta child figure documented by the Salon and exhibition database; its present status is not established by the consulted catalogue. ([Base Salons](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/36371))
- **1881:** *Fillette debout tenant un cerceau*. Plaster statuette preserved at the Musée Bartholdi. ([CRDP, 1880–1889](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr4.html))
- **1885:** *La Borne-frontière*. Plaster allegory preserved at the Musée Bartholdi, responding to the Franco-German border; it acts politically but is not literally a manifesto-text. ([CRDP, 1880–1889](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr4.html))
- **1889–1891:** *L’Alsace et la Lorraine se réfugiant au pied de l’Autel de la Patrie*. Marble allegorical group exhibited in 1891. ([CRDP, 1880–1889](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr4.html); [Base Salons](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/36371))
- **1893:** *Les Maraudeurs* or *Les Faunes maraudeurs attrapés*. Marble decorative bowl exhibited at the 1893 Salon; the expanded and shortened titles in catalogue records appear to designate the same decorative sculpture. ([Base Salons](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/36371))
- **1893:** *Monument à Christophe Colomb*. Colossal figure or monument made for the World’s Columbian Exposition in Chicago; its subsequent fate is uncertain, while a signed 1893 bronze reduction survives in Providence. ([Musée Bartholdi biography](https://www.musee-bartholdi.fr/decouvrir-bartholdi/biographie/))
- **1899:** *La Suisse secourant les douleurs de Strasbourg*, reduced version. Small-scale manifestation of the Basel monument preserved at the Musée Bartholdi. ([CRDP, 1890–1904](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr5.html))

### Portrait busts, statues, statuettes, and medallions

- **1857:** *Jean-Daniel Hanhart*. Funerary portrait medallion at Colmar; it is recorded in repertories of Bartholdi’s works but omitted from the later selective CRDP chronology. ([Musée Bartholdi, world-work list](https://sevres-92310.fr/doc/Auguste_Bartholdi.pdf))
- **1860:** *Jean-Paul-Adam Schramm*. Portrait bust exhibited at the 1861 Salon. ([CRDP, 1860–1869](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr2.html))
- **1861:** *Louis Hugot*. Portrait bust of the Colmar librarian, preserved at the Musée Bartholdi. ([CRDP, 1860–1869](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr2.html))
- **1862:** *Armand-Joseph Bruat*. Terracotta portrait or model connected with the Bruat monument but catalogued as a separate object. ([CRDP, 1860–1869](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr2.html))
- **1865:** *Général non identifié*. Portrait bust in the Musée Carnavalet; because the sitter is uncertain, assigning a supplied personal name would falsify the catalogue record. ([CRDP, 1860–1869](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr2.html))
- **1865:** *Édouard-René Lefebvre de Laboulaye*. Portrait bust of the jurist and political sponsor of the Statue of Liberty project, shown at the 1866 Salon. ([CRDP, 1860–1869](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr2.html))
- **1865:** *G. B. Lorentz*. Portrait bust exhibited in 1867; the catalogues also associate Bartholdi with Lorentz’s funerary monument, which is a separate work. ([CRDP, 1860–1869](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr2.html))
- **1868:** *Général Émile Mellinet*. Statuette made from life and later exhibited at the 1894 Salon; some chronologies date the surviving object 1893, but the Salon title explicitly says the likeness was made in 1868. ([Paris Musées](https://www.parismuseescollections.paris.fr/fr/recherche/auteur/Bartholdi%2C%20Fr%C3%A9d%C3%A9ric%20Auguste); [Base Salons](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/36371))
- **1869:** *Rencker*. Portrait bust recorded without a secure expanded identity in the consulted chronology. ([CRDP, 1860–1869](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr2.html))
- **1869:** *Ismaïl Pacha*. Drawn portrait of the Egyptian ruler; this is a work on paper rather than a bust or public monument. ([CRDP, 1860–1869](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr2.html))
- **1871:** *Hercule de Peyerimhoff*. Portrait bust of the former mayor of Colmar, preserved at the Musée Bartholdi. ([CRDP, 1870–1879](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr3.html))
- **1872:** *Émile Erckmann et Alexandre Chatrian*. Pair of portrait busts of the writers, exhibited together at the 1872 Salon. ([CRDP, 1870–1879](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr3.html))
- **1872:** *Louis Bouilhet*. Portrait bust of the poet, preserved at the Musée Bartholdi. ([CRDP, 1870–1879](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr3.html))
- **1873:** *Marquis de Lafayette*. Plaster portrait preserved at the Musée Bartholdi; later marble busts and monumental figures should not be conflated with it. ([CRDP, 1870–1879](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr3.html))
- **1877:** *Léon Belly*. Portrait bust of the painter and Bartholdi’s fellow traveler, preserved at the Musée Bartholdi. ([CRDP, 1870–1879](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr3.html))
- **1878:** *Pierre-Eugène Secrétan*. Portrait bust of the industrialist responsible for supplying copper to the Statue of Liberty project. ([CRDP, 1870–1879](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr3.html))
- **1878:** *Lafayette et George Washington*. Pair of portrait busts made for the United States diplomatic mission in Paris and exhibited at the 1878 Salon; these are not the later double-figure monument. ([CRDP, 1870–1879](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr3.html))
- **1879:** *Honoré Monduit*. Portrait medallion of the art metalworker involved in Bartholdi’s monumental production. ([CRDP, 1870–1879](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr3.html))
- **1879:** *Lucien Arbel*. Portrait bust exhibited at the 1879 Salon. ([CRDP, 1870–1879](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr3.html))
- **1881:** *Maurice A.*. Plaster portrait statue exhibited at the 1881 Salon; the sitter’s abbreviated catalogue name should not be expanded conjecturally. ([CRDP, 1880–1889](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr4.html); [Base Salons](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/36371))
- **1881:** *William Maxwell Evarts*. Terracotta portrait bust of the American statesman, exhibited in 1883 and preserved at the Musée Bartholdi. ([CRDP, 1880–1889](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr4.html); [Base Salons](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/36371))
- **1882:** *Pierre C.*. Plaster portrait statue exhibited at the 1882 Salon; the sitter remains only partly named in the catalogue. ([CRDP, 1880–1889](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr4.html))
- **1882–1883:** *Ignace Chauffour*. Portrait bust made in plaster and marble, with distinct examples recorded at the Musée Bartholdi and the Maison de l’Avocat in Colmar. ([CRDP, 1880–1889](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr4.html); [Base Salons](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/36371))
- **1885:** *Gustave-Adolphe Jundt*. Portrait bust of the painter, exhibited at the 1885 Salon; Bartholdi also made Jundt’s funerary monument. ([CRDP, 1880–1889](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr4.html))
- **1886:** *Lafayette dans sa jeunesse*. Marble portrait bust exhibited at the Salon, distinct from Bartholdi’s standing Lafayette monuments. ([Base Salons](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/objects/372125))
- **1896:** *Charles-Frédéric Faudel*. Portrait medallion of the Colmar scientist. ([CRDP, 1890–1904](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr5.html))
- **1897:** *Charles-Frédéric Faudel*. Plaster portrait bust or model preserved at the Musée Bartholdi, separate from the medallion and finished bust. ([CRDP, 1890–1904](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr5.html))
- **1897:** *Charles-Frédéric Faudel*. Finished portrait bust preserved at Colmar’s natural-history and ethnography museum. ([CRDP, 1890–1904](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr5.html))
- **1899:** *Georges Kern*. Funerary portrait medallion at Colmar’s municipal cemetery. ([CRDP, 1890–1904](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr5.html))
- **1900:** *Claudius Guichard*. Portrait bust of the deputy mayor of Lyon, exhibited at the 1900 Salon and preserved at the Musée Bartholdi. ([CRDP, 1890–1904](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr5.html))

### Funerary monuments

- **1857:** *Médaillon funéraire de Jean-Daniel Hanhart*. Grave medallion at Colmar; its portrait function also places it within Bartholdi’s medallions. ([Musée Bartholdi, world-work list](https://sevres-92310.fr/doc/Auguste_Bartholdi.pdf))
- **1860:** *Tombeau d’André Robberechts*. Funerary monument to the violinist in Montmartre Cemetery; some sources abbreviate the surname or title. ([CRDP, 1860–1869](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr2.html); [Wikisculpture](https://www.wikisculpture.fr/index.php/bios-sculpteur/554-bartholdi-frederic-auguste))
- **1865–1866:** *Le Génie funèbre*. Veiled mourning genius made for a tomb in Montmartre Cemetery; sources differ over whether the associated tomb is that of Nefftzer, while later Colmar display complicates identification of the original site-object and surviving sculpture. ([CRDP, 1860–1869](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr2.html); [Wikisculpture](https://www.wikisculpture.fr/index.php/bios-sculpteur/554-bartholdi-frederic-auguste))
- **1865:** *Monument funéraire de G. B. Lorentz*. Tomb monument recorded separately from Bartholdi’s portrait bust of Lorentz; its location is not supplied in the consulted chronology. ([CRDP, 1860–1869](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr2.html))
- **1870:** *Monument funéraire de Marguerite Scheurer*. Funerary monument included in repertories of Bartholdi’s work but not in the CRDP’s selective chronology. ([Musée Bartholdi, world-work list](https://sevres-92310.fr/doc/Auguste_Bartholdi.pdf))
- **1872:** *Monument à Voulminot, Linck et Wagner* or *Tombeau des gardes nationaux*. Colmar cemetery monument commemorating three guards killed in 1870, built around the image of hands raising a stone slab; Salon records span 1872 and a later re-exhibition. ([CRDP, 1870–1879](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr3.html))
- **1872:** *Monument funéraire d’Henri-Gustave Saltzmann*. Tomb of the landscape painter at Lancy near Geneva. ([CRDP, 1870–1879](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr3.html))
- **1884:** *Monument funéraire de Salam Penquer*. Tomb of the former mayor of Brest; the consulted chronology records the work but not its present location or survival. ([CRDP, 1880–1889](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr4.html))
- **1886:** *Monument funéraire de Gustave-Adolphe Jundt*. Tomb inaugurated in Montparnasse Cemetery, distinct from Bartholdi’s bust of Jundt. ([CRDP, 1880–1889](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr4.html))
- **1886–1888:** *Monument funéraire de Paul Bert*. Tomb monument at Auxerre, exhibited in both sculpture and architecture sections of the 1888 Salon; the double classification accurately reflects its refusal of a single medium. ([CRDP, 1880–1889](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr4.html); [Base Salons](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/36371))
- **c. 1890:** *Monument funèbre de M. H.*. Funerary monument exhibited at the 1890 Salon; the catalogue suppresses the deceased’s full name, which cannot responsibly be supplied. ([Base Salons](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/36371))
- **1890–1891:** *Monument funéraire de Théodore Deck*. Tomb of the ceramicist in Montparnasse Cemetery. ([CRDP, 1890–1904](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr5.html))
- **1891:** *Monument funéraire de Jean-François Soitoux*. Tomb of the sculptor in Montparnasse Cemetery. ([CRDP, 1890–1904](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr5.html))
- **1904:** *Monument funéraire du sergent Hoff*. Funerary monument exhibited in 1904 and inaugurated after Bartholdi’s death; its final realization therefore crosses the boundary between lifetime project and posthumous completion. ([CRDP, 1890–1904](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr5.html))

### Unrealized monuments and competition projects

- **1855–1856:** *Projets de mausolée à Mohammed Ali*. Two drawn proposals made in Cairo, showing the former viceroy beside a lion; neither was executed, but each is an authored project rather than a missing monument. ([Musée Bartholdi biography](https://www.musee-bartholdi.fr/decouvrir-bartholdi/biographie/))
- **1855–1856:** *Projet de fontaine monumentale pour Le Caire*. Design produced during Bartholdi’s first Egyptian journey and not realized. ([Musée Bartholdi biography](https://www.musee-bartholdi.fr/decouvrir-bartholdi/biographie/))
- **1872:** *Monument commémoratif de Washington et de l’Indépendance américaine*. Sculptural project preserved at the Musée Bartholdi; it precedes and should not be collapsed into the later *Lafayette et Washington*. ([CRDP, 1870–1879](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr3.html))
- **1873:** *Monument aux victimes du siège de Brisach*. Unexecuted commemorative project whose surviving state is not specified in the consulted chronology. ([CRDP, 1870–1879](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr3.html))
- **1874:** *Fontaine Sainte-Marie*. Model for an unrealized water-tower or public-fountain project for Rouen, preserved at the Musée Bartholdi. ([CRDP, 1870–1879](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr3.html))
- **Before 1886:** *À la mémoire de Lafayette et de l’Indépendance américaine*. Monument project for Washington, represented by a plaster shown at the 1886 Salon; it is an intention materialized as a model, not an erected monument. ([CRDP, 1880–1889](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr4.html))
- **Before 1899:** *Lafayette et l’Indépendance américaine*. Monument model exhibited at the 1899 Salon; whether it is a substantially revised state of the Washington project or an autonomous proposal is not resolved by the consulted chronology. ([CRDP, 1890–1904](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr5.html))
- **Undated:** *Projet de monument pour Callao*. Unexecuted proposal intended for Peru; the consulted institutional summary supplies neither a secure date nor an established formal title. ([Musée Bartholdi overview](https://www.musee-bartholdi.fr/decouvrir-bartholdi/biographie/))
- **Undated:** *Projet de monument de l’Union postale universelle*. Unexecuted competition or commission project; no secure date is supplied in the consulted repertories. ([Auguste Bartholdi, works and projects](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auguste_Bartholdi))
- **Undated:** *Projet de monument à Thiers et Denfert-Rochereau*. Unrealized Belfort memorial project; it survives as an intention or model rather than as a public monument. ([Auguste Bartholdi, works and projects](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auguste_Bartholdi))
- **1904:** *Méta*. Project honoring athletics, left unrealized at Bartholdi’s death; the surviving record calls it simply a project, so neither medium nor intended site should be invented. ([CRDP, 1890–1904](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr5.html))

### Paintings, drawings, and photographic bodies of work

- **1855–1856:** *Photographs from Egypt, Nubia, and Yemen*. A body of travel photographs made during Bartholdi’s first journey, including architectural remains, landscapes, and posed figures; it resists reduction to one titled work, and no complete plate-by-plate catalogue was available in the consulted online corpus. ([Musée Bartholdi chronology](https://www.musee-bartholdi.fr/decouvrir-bartholdi/reperes-chronologiques/); [Musée Bartholdi collection description](https://pop.culture.gouv.fr/notice/museo/M0027))
- **1855–1856:** *Drawings and watercolors from the first journey in the Orient*. A travel corpus rather than one fixed obra; a Musée Bartholdi publication catalogues fifty-two drawings, but the surviving body is described more broadly by the museum. ([DRAC Grand Est catalogue](https://pmb.culture.fr/opac/index.php?id=3148&l_typdoc=a%2Co%2Cq%2Cv%2Cz&lvl=categ_see&nbr_lignes=63&page=4); [Musée Bartholdi biography](https://www.musee-bartholdi.fr/decouvrir-bartholdi/biographie/))
- **1860:** *Café sur les bords du Nil*. Oil painting shown at the 1861 Salon and preserved at the Musée Bartholdi. ([CRDP, 1860–1869](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr2.html))
- **1869:** *L’Égypte éclairant l’Orient*. Design drawing for the Suez lighthouse project; although inseparable intellectually from the monument project, the sheet is also an individually made work on paper. ([CRDP, 1860–1869](https://archives.plarela.eu/www.crdp-strasbourg.fr/bartho/tab_chr2.html))
- **1886:** *La Californie de jadis*. Painting exhibited at the 1886 Salon; it forms a deliberate pair with *La Californie nouvelle*. ([Base Salons](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/36371))
- **1886:** *La Californie nouvelle*. Painting exhibited at the 1886 Salon as the modern counterpart to *La Californie de jadis*. ([Base Salons](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/36371))
- **Date uncertain:** *Ancient California*. Painting preserved at the Musée Bartholdi and probably identifiable with *La Californie de jadis*; the English museum-facing title is treated here as a manifestation of that work, not confidently as an additional painting. ([Kiwix biographical record](https://kiwix.thunderforge.net/content/wikipedia_en_all_maxi_2025-08/Fr%C3%A9d%C3%A9ric_Auguste_Bartholdi))
