## Works by Jean-Baptiste Alliette

### Cartomantic protocols and oracle books

- **1770; revised 1773 and 1783:** *Etteilla, ou manière de se récréer avec un jeu de cartes*. A protocol for divination with a thirty-two-card piquet pack plus a thirty-third significator card; the differently titled *La seule manière de tirer les cartes* and *Instructions sur l’art de tirer les cartes* are revisions of this obra, not three independent works. It is the earliest surviving substantial French exposition of systematic cartomancy. ([Decker, Depaulis and Dummett, pp. 74–99](https://books.google.com/books?id=WR4YAQAAIAAJ); [Stone, pp. 14–16](https://journals.library.mun.ca/index.php/bookish/article/download/2829/2300))

- **1772:** *Le Zodiaque mystérieux, ou les oracles d’Etteilla*. An astrological-oracular compilation arranged for consultation rather than continuous argument: part almanac, part divinatory mechanism. ([Wellcome Collection](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/p66ehvp5); [Decker, Depaulis and Dummett, pp. 82–83](https://books.google.com/books?id=WR4YAQAAIAAJ))

- **1782:** *Instruction sur le loto des Indiens que nous a donné en 1772 Mr Etteilla, professeur d’algèbre*. A short protocol for a numerical lottery-oracle devised or promoted by Etteilla; its title retrospectively dates the “Indian lottery” system to 1772, not necessarily this printed instruction. ([Stone, p. 15](https://journals.library.mun.ca/index.php/bookish/article/download/2829/2300))

- **1782:** *Instruction sur la combinaison hislérique, extraite du Loto des Indiens*. A second leaflet extracting one combinatory procedure from the preceding lottery system; “hislérique” names Etteilla’s operation rather than a separate literary genre. ([Stone, p. 15](https://journals.library.mun.ca/index.php/bookish/article/download/2829/2300))

- **1783–1785:** *Manière de se récréer avec le jeu de cartes nommées tarots*. Etteilla’s principal tarot obra and a deliberately composite project: separately issued cahiers, supplements, diagrams and instructions intended to turn an ordinary tarot into a divinatory “Book of Thoth.” The publication order did not follow its numerical order, and descriptions variously count four main cahiers, six principal publications, or nine component booklets. ([Morgan Library](https://www.themorgan.org/printed-books/167790); [Decker, Depaulis and Dummett, pp. 84–94](https://books.google.com/books?id=WR4YAQAAIAAJ); [Stone, pp. 16–17](https://journals.library.mun.ca/index.php/bookish/article/download/2829/2300))

- **1783:** *Manière de se récréer avec le jeu de cartes nommées tarots, pour servir de troisième cahier à cet ouvrage*. The first-published component of the larger tarot project, despite being designated its third cahier; it is a protocol for renumbering and annotating an existing tarot pack. ([BnF](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb403538270); [Decker, Depaulis and Dummett, pp. 84–86](https://books.google.com/books?id=WR4YAQAAIAAJ))

- **1783:** *Manière de se récréer avec le jeu de cartes nommées tarots, pour servir de premier cahier à cet ouvrage*. A theoretical and instructional component centred on Etteilla’s reconstructed tarot and its correspondences. It was issued with, or followed by, a separately titled supplement. ([Morgan Library](https://www.themorgan.org/printed-books/167790); [Stone, p. 16](https://journals.library.mun.ca/index.php/bookish/article/download/2829/2300))

- **July 1783:** *Supplément au premier cahier de la manière de se récréer avec le jeu de cartes nommées tarots*. An operational extension of the first cahier presenting a new method of tarot cartomancy; although often bound continuously with that cahier, it was announced and titled as a distinct component. ([Stone, p. 16](https://journals.library.mun.ca/index.php/bookish/article/download/2829/2300))

- **1784:** *Supplément au troisième cahier*. A further component of the tarot project, dated 19 May 1784 in the specialist reconstruction of the series. Its dependence on the third cahier makes it resist treatment as a wholly autonomous work. ([Stone, p. 16](https://journals.library.mun.ca/index.php/bookish/article/download/2829/2300); [UQAM study, p. 145](https://archipel.uqam.ca/1028/1/D1656.pdf))

- **1785:** *Manière de se récréer avec le jeu de cartes nommées tarots, pour servir de second cahier à cet ouvrage*. The belated second part of the composite tarot course, combining explanation and practical instruction. ([Morgan Library](https://www.themorgan.org/printed-books/167790); [UQAM study, p. 145](https://archipel.uqam.ca/1028/1/D1656.pdf))

- **1785:** *Manière de se récréer avec le jeu de cartes nommées tarots, pour servir de quatrième cahier à cet ouvrage*. The astrological component of the series, later reprinted under the editorial title *L’Astrologie du Livre de Thot*. That later title is a manifestation of the cahier, not another work by Alliette. ([Morgan Library](https://www.themorgan.org/printed-books/167790); [Decker, Depaulis and Dummett, pp. 88–90](https://books.google.com/books?id=WR4YAQAAIAAJ))

- **1785, possibly first issued 1783:** *Fragment sur les hautes sciences, suivi d’une note sur les trois sortes de médecines données aux hommes, dont une mal-à-propos délaissée*. An esoteric essay printed inside the third volume of the tarot compilation but furnished with its own title page. A surviving copy is dated 1785, while Etteilla himself referred to an edition of 1783; the earlier manifestation has not been securely established. ([Morgan Library](https://www.themorgan.org/printed-books/167790); [Millet-Saint-Pierre bibliography](https://www.massimomarra.net/millet-saint-pierre-jean-baptiste-ricerche-sullultimo-mago-e-sullultima-scuola-di-magia-traduzione-e-note-di-massimo-marra-seconda-parte))

- **1785:** *Philosophie des hautes sciences, ou la clef donnée aux enfans de l’art, de la science & de la sagesse*. A synthetic occult treatise and proposed “key” to Etteilla’s system; contemporary advertisements presented it as completing a ten-cahier body of teaching, so it functions both as an independent obra and as the capstone of the tarot project. ([Warburg Institute, Innes Collection catalogue, p. 18](https://resources.warburg.sas.ac.uk/pdf/Innes_Catalogue.pdf); [UQAM study, p. 145](https://archipel.uqam.ca/1028/1/D1656.pdf))

- **1787:** *Science. Leçons théoriques et pratiques du livre de Thot. Moyennes classes*. A graded teaching manual for Etteilla’s students: less a general treatise than a course protocol within his projected school of occult instruction. ([Decker, Depaulis and Dummett, pp. 90–94](https://books.google.com/books?id=WR4YAQAAIAAJ); [bibliographic list](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etteilla#Publications))

- **1788:** *Jeu des tarots, ou le livre de Thot ouvert à la manière des Égyptiens, pour servir ici à l’interprétation de tous les rêves, songes et visions diurnes et nocturnes*. A dream-divination manual joining tarot, dream interpretation and Etteilla’s imagined Egyptian genealogy. Despite *Jeu* in the title, the recorded object is a printed instructional work, not itself the pack of cards. ([Decker, Depaulis and Dummett, pp. 90–94](https://books.google.com/books?id=WR4YAQAAIAAJ); [bibliographic list](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etteilla#Publications))

- **1790:** *Cours théorique et pratique du livre de Thot, pour entendre avec justesse l’art, la science et la sagesse de rendre les oracles*. A mature course manual consolidating Etteilla’s tarot vocabulary, layouts and methods of interpretation. It is a protocol and curriculum as much as a book. ([Decker, Depaulis and Dummett, pp. 94–98](https://books.google.com/books?id=WR4YAQAAIAAJ); [Stone, p. 17](https://journals.library.mun.ca/index.php/bookish/article/download/2829/2300))

- **1791:** *Tablette catégorique des significations du Livre de Thot, avec des annotations par M. Etteilla*. A tabular interpretive aid: the title credits Etteilla specifically with annotations, so the extent to which the whole table is his creation is uncertain. It belongs to his teaching apparatus rather than securely constituting a wholly authored treatise. ([Bibliographic notice and title transcription](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etteilla#Opere))

### Other divinatory and hermetic treatises

- **1786:** *Les Sept Nuances de l’œuvre philosophique-hermétique, suivies d’un traité sur la perfection des métaux*. An alchemical treatise, sometimes bound with *Philosophie des hautes sciences* but bearing its own title and structure. The work is undated in the surviving printing: 1786 is the conventional bibliographical date, while some catalogues assign 1785. ([Warburg Institute, Innes Collection catalogue, pp. 18–19](https://resources.warburg.sas.ac.uk/pdf/Innes_Catalogue.pdf))

- **1787:** *L’Art de connoître les hommes par l’inspection du front, ou élémens de métoposcopie suivant les anciens*. A physiognomic protocol for interpreting the forehead; it extends Etteilla’s divinatory project beyond cards. ([Decker, Depaulis and Dummett, p. 94](https://books.google.com/books?id=WR4YAQAAIAAJ); [bibliographic list](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etteilla#Publications))

- **1787:** *L’Art de lire dans les lignes et caractères qui sont dans les mains, ou élémens de chiromance*. A practical palmistry manual, paired bibliographically and conceptually with the work on metoposcopy. ([Decker, Depaulis and Dummett, p. 94](https://books.google.com/books?id=WR4YAQAAIAAJ); [bibliographic list](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etteilla#Publications))

### Cards and mixed-media divinatory instruments

- **From 1770; early physical form uncertain:** *The Etteilla, later called the Petit Etteilla*. A cartomantic system-object consisting of thirty-two ordinary cards plus a thirty-third consultant card, meanings and a method of laying them out. The 1770 book securely documents the system, but surviving packs are later manifestations, so no extant early deck can simply be identified as an original physical work manufactured by Alliette. ([Stone, pp. 14–15](https://journals.library.mun.ca/index.php/bookish/article/download/2829/2300); [BnF digitized pack](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b10529586n))

- **Designed by 1788; printed about 1788–1789:** *Livre de Thot*, commonly called the *Grand Etteilla*. A seventy-eight-card divinatory deck whose sequence, captions and imagery embody Etteilla’s system; it is a Gesamtkunstwerk joining pictures, numbered text, cosmology and performative protocol. The exact first printing is disputed, and scholars report that no securely identifiable copy of the earliest state survives; later packs and recut plates should not be mistaken for separate works by him. ([Depaulis, “Le vrai tarot d’Etteilla,” pp. 448–454, cited here](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etteilla#Notes_et_références); [Stone, p. 17](https://journals.library.mun.ca/index.php/bookish/article/download/2829/2300); [British Museum later complete pack](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/P_1982-U-4601-1-78))

### Projects, manifestos and institutional works

- **1783; reissued 1791:** *L’Homme à projets*. A collection or sequence of schemes concerning civic, economic and social reform, later republished to claim that Etteilla had anticipated the Revolution. No copy of the asserted 1783 issue was available to the nineteenth-century bibliographer Millet-Saint-Pierre, so its original form and completeness remain uncertain. ([Decker, Depaulis and Dummett, pp. 96–97](https://books.google.com/books?id=WR4YAQAAIAAJ); [Millet-Saint-Pierre bibliography](https://www.massimomarra.net/millet-saint-pierre-jean-baptiste-ricerche-sullultimo-mago-e-sullultima-scuola-di-magia-traduzione-e-note-di-massimo-marra-seconda-parte))

- **1784:** *Épître à M. Court de Gébelin, salut, Etteilla, premier de l’an vulgaire 1784*. A public polemical letter asserting Etteilla’s position in relation to Court de Gébelin’s Egyptian interpretation of tarot. It behaves as a manifesto—an intervention claiming authority—more than as a neutral exposition. ([Millet-Saint-Pierre bibliography](https://www.massimomarra.net/millet-saint-pierre-jean-baptiste-ricerche-sullultimo-mago-e-sullultima-scuola-di-magia-traduzione-e-note-di-massimo-marra-seconda-parte); [Decker, Depaulis and Dummett, pp. 86–88](https://books.google.com/books?id=WR4YAQAAIAAJ))

- **1788–1791:** *Société littéraire des Associés libres des interprètes du Livre de Thot* / *Nouvelle École de magie*. An institutional project founded around Etteilla’s divinatory teaching, correspondence and public lessons. It resists being called a “work” in the bibliographical sense, but it was something he made: an organization intended to enact and perpetuate the system described in his books and cards. ([Decker, Depaulis and Dummett, pp. 94–99](https://books.google.com/books?id=WR4YAQAAIAAJ); [Millet-Saint-Pierre account](https://www.massimomarra.net/millet-saint-pierre-jean-baptiste-ricerche-sullultimo-mago-e-sullultima-scuola-di-magia-traduzione-e-note-di-massimo-marra-seconda-parte))

- **1790–1791:** *Aperçu sur la nouvelle école de magie établie à Paris*. Two public-school discourses presenting the purpose and practices of Etteilla’s free “school of magic.” It is simultaneously a report, manifesto and institutional protocol; surviving descriptions disagree between a 1790 first discourse and a 1791 collected form containing both discourses. ([Theosophical Library catalogue](https://www.theosofie.nl/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Hoofdrubriek-Esoterie-PDF-10-11-2024.pdf); [Decker, Depaulis and Dummett, pp. 97–98](https://books.google.com/books?id=WR4YAQAAIAAJ))

- **November 1790:** *L’Oracle pour et contre mil sept cent quatre-vingt-onze*. A sixteen-page political-prophetic pamphlet applying Etteilla’s oracle-making to the coming year. Its “for and against” form makes it an intervention in revolutionary uncertainty rather than a timeless divination manual. ([BnF](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb300121110))

- **February–July 1791:** *Journal projétique et patriotique*. A serial project of seventeen known numbers, generally four-page pamphlets, mixing political schemes, welfare proposals, attacks on capital punishment, professional advocacy and occult teaching. The series is incomplete in surviving collections and its issue-count chronology has required bibliographical reconstruction. ([Decker, Depaulis and Dummett, pp. 96–99](https://books.google.com/books?id=WR4YAQAAIAAJ); [Millet-Saint-Pierre account](https://www.massimomarra.net/millet-saint-pierre-jean-baptiste-ricerche-sullultimo-mago-e-sullultima-scuola-di-magia-traduzione-e-note-di-massimo-marra-seconda-parte))

### Uncertain, collaborative or non-surviving texts

- **1772 or undated:** *Lettre sur l’oracle du jour*. A laudatory pamphlet signed by a “Duchesse de ***” and associated with *Le Zodiaque mystérieux*. It belongs to the contemporary Etteilla corpus but cannot securely be called something Alliette made; its authorship is unidentified, and inclusion in older lists of his productions reflects association rather than proof. ([Decker, Depaulis and Dummett, pp. 82–83](https://books.google.com/books?id=WR4YAQAAIAAJ); [nineteenth-century catalogue entry](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4c/Catalogue_des_livres%2C_imprim%C3%A9s_et_manuscrits%2C_composant_la_biblioth%C3%A8que_de_feu_M._L.-_M.-_J._Duriez_%28IA_cataloguedeslivr00gall_0%29.pdf))

- **Before 1791:** *Aphorismes d’Etteilla*. Aphorisms attributed to him are cited in the contemporary or near-contemporary *Aperçu d’un rigoriste sur la cartonomancie et sur son auteur*, but no securely identifiable independent edition has been established. They resist classification as a work because they may have been sayings, manuscript notes or excerpts rather than a completed authored text. ([Millet-Saint-Pierre’s bibliographical notes](https://www.massimomarra.net/millet-saint-pierre-jean-baptiste-ricerche-sullultimo-mago-e-sullultima-scuola-di-magia-traduzione-e-note-di-massimo-marra-prima-parte))

- **Before December 1791:** *Unprinted occult manuscripts*. Éliphas Lévi reported examining unpublished Etteilla manuscripts still circulating in nineteenth-century Paris, but supplied neither a dependable inventory nor sufficient descriptions to separate individual works. Their existence belongs to the history of Etteilla’s oeuvre, while their titles, dates, completeness and authorship cannot now be responsibly reconstructed. ([Dictionary of Gnosis & Western Esotericism, “Etteilla”](https://uodiyala.edu.iq/uploads/PDF%20ELIBRARY%20UODIYALA/EL45/Dictionary%20of%20Gnosis%20%26%20Western%20Esotericism.pdf))
