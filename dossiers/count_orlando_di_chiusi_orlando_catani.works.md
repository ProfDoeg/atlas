## Works by Count Orlando di Chiusi

### Juridical act

- **c. 1213:** Oral grant of Mount La Verna to Francis of Assisi and his community. This is an enacted gift rather than a titled text or material artwork, and thus resists classification as a conventional “work”; the earliest surviving narrative is the *Actus beati Francisci et sociorum eius* (1327–1340), more than a century later. The familiar date 8 May 1213 derives from a purported 1274 confirmation now generally regarded as a later forgery; Orlando made no written instrument, and even his title “count” is anachronistic—contemporary usage supports *dominus* or lord of Chiusi. ([Licciardello, pp. 30–34](https://www.esolibri.it/LIBRI%20ACCADEMIA%20TEMPLARE/TOSCANA/Storia%2C%20architettura%20ed%20arte%20alla%20Verna%20%20dalle%20origini%20al%20primo%20Quattrocento.pdf))
