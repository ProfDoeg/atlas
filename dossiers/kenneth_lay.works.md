## Works by Kenneth Lee Lay

### Scholarly obra

- **August 1970:** *The Measurement of the Timing of the Economic Impact of Defense Procurement Activity: An Analysis of the Vietnam Buildup*. Doctoral dissertation in economics submitted to the University of Houston; the standard scholarly bibliography separately identifies its abstract, so the abstract and dissertation should not be mistaken for two independent works. [Bibliographic record](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

### Institutional project

- **1985–2001:** *Enron*. A corporate project produced through the merger of Houston Natural Gas and InterNorth under Lay’s leadership, then developed collectively by executives, employees, advisers, and counterparties. It resists attribution as Lay’s individual obra: scholarship calls him its founder or directing figure, but Enron was a legal institution and collective system rather than a personally authored object. [Business History Review](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/business-history-review/article/enron-and-the-california-energy-crisis-the-role-of-networks-in-enabling-organizational-corruption/457B1E245C6E6DE8903F531DD768D3F4); [SEC corporate history](https://www.sec.gov/files/divisions/investment/opur/filing/35-27809.htm)

### Pamphlet and contributed chapters

- **1990:** *The Enron Story*. A corporate-history pamphlet published by the Newcomen Society for the United States: part narrative and part institutional self-presentation. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **1995:** *A New Vision*. A contributed essay in *The Oil Makers: Insiders Look at the Petroleum Industry*, pp. 351–365. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **1996:** *The Energy Company of the 21st Century*. A contributed chapter in *The Global Energy Company of the 21st Century*, pp. 33–41. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **June 1997:** *The Energy Industry in the Next Century: Opportunities and Constraints*. A seminar paper published in *Energy After 2000*, pp. 13–26. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **1998:** *Coming Soon to Your Home and Business: The New Energy Majors*. A contributed executive essay in *Straight from the CEO*, pp. 250–256. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **1999:** *Give All Customers the Right to Choose, Immediately*. A contributed policy essay in *Customer Choice: Finding Value in Retail Electricity Markets*, pp. 291–296; its imperative title gives it some of the force of a manifesto for retail-electricity deregulation. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **1999:** *The $30 Billion Corner Store*. A contributed executive narrative in *Lessons from the Top*, pp. 215–219. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

### Articles, columns, and public letters

- **1984:** *From the Chairman*. A chairman’s message in the Houston Natural Gas annual report for employees—corporate address rather than independent book or essay collection. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **Winter 1991–92:** *A North American Perspective*. An energy-policy article published in *Gascope*, pp. 11–15. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **1992:** *Take It to the Top*. A recurring contribution in various issues of *Enron People*. The bibliography does not enumerate the installments, so this survives bibliographically as a series rather than a securely countable set of individual works. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **21 September 1992:** *Lay Rips Hefner’s “Dream Team,” Favors GOP*. A letter to the editor published in *Natural Gas Week*, p. 3. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **13 October 1992:** *Give Bush Credit, He Is the “Energy President”*. An opinion article published in the *Houston Chronicle*. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **March 1993:** *Rebuilding the US Oil and Gas Industry*. A policy article published in *Petroleum Economist*, pp. 8–9. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **28 February 1994:** *Greenpeace Wrong in Attacking Natural Gas*. A letter to the editor published in *Natural Gas Week*, p. 3. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **23 December 1995:** *White Paper: Electric Deregulation and Enron*. A corporate policy paper published in the *American Oil and Gas Reporter*; it functions partly as a manifesto for Enron’s preferred market structure. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **1996:** *In the Middle of One of the Greatest “Energy Plays” in History*. An industry article published in *World Gas Yearbook 1996*. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **July–August 1996:** *Corporate Citizenship: A Priority in the Enron Workplace*. A chairman’s article in *Enron Business*, pp. 2 and 8. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **1 September 1996:** *Downtown’s a Natural for Sports Arenas*. A jointly authored *Houston Chronicle* opinion article by Lay and Jim McIngvale; authorship is explicitly shared. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **10 September 1996:** *Investment Agency Helps US Companies*. A policy article published in the *Journal of Commerce*. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **Spring 1997:** *Toward the “Heroic Enterprise”*. An article published in *Dilemma*, pp. 1–3. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **July 1999:** *Change and Innovation: The Evolving Energy Industry*. A signed journal article in *Energy & Environment*, presenting corporate and policy argument as executive analysis. [SAGE record](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1260/0958305991499630?download=true)

### Speeches and presentations

- **January 1986:** *Houston Outlook ’86: Energy & Manufacturing Panel*. A conference presentation; the bibliography lists it twice in slightly different forms, but these appear to be one event rather than two works. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **13 July 1991:** *Talking Points*. Speaking notes for the Aspen Institute Energy Policy Forum. This is a preparatory performance-text, not necessarily a verbatim record of what Lay said. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **9 March 1992:** *Natural Gas: The Power Fuel for the 1990s*. A presentation to the Institute of Gas Technology in Chicago. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **7 and 11 May 1992:** *If Natural Gas Is the Fuel of the Future, When Does the Future Start?* A presentation delivered on two documented occasions in Santa Fe; these are two manifestations of one titled speech. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **20 May 1992:** *Natural Gas and America’s Energy Future: Beyond Compliance*. A presentation to the National Clean Air Conference in Houston. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **23 June 1992:** *Natural Gas: The Cost-Effective Link Between Robust Economic Growth and Aggressive Environmental Protection*. A presentation to the Alliance to Save Energy’s “Global Warming and the Earth Summit” conference in Washington. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **1995:** *The Importance of Vision*. A speech text addressed to Enron employees. Its status is corporate rhetoric, and individual composition is not certain: the scholarly source identifies Robert L. Bradley Jr. as Lay’s speechwriter, so “by Lay” here means delivered or institutionally attributed to him, not necessarily written without assistance. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio); [book description](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/book/10.1002/9781119493709)

- **13 September 1995:** *The New Worlds of Natural Gas*. A presentation to the Pacific Coast Gas Association’s annual business meeting in Houston. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **9 February 1996:** *A Vision for the 21st Century Asia*. A speech to the Asia Society/Baker Institute conference “China, the United States, and Asia: Challenges for United States Policy and Business.” [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **14 February 1996:** *Enron in the 21st Century*. A presentation to the fifteenth annual Cambridge Energy Research Associates executive conference in Houston. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **11 March 1997:** *New Energy Visions: Enron Corp.* A presentation in the University of Colorado Boulder’s Profiles in American Enterprise series. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **19 June 1998:** *Natural Gas and Global Energy Challenges*. A speech to the Florida Natural Gas Association’s annual convention in Naples. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **1 October 1998:** *Deregulation of Gas and Electricity*. A presentation to the Japan–US Southern Conference in New Orleans. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **6 April 1999:** *Corporate Governance: Ethics Across the Board*. A conference speech whose title and delivery are preserved in a later Senate hearing record. Because the surviving official source quotes and characterizes it rather than furnishing a separately catalogued original, its textual completeness is uncertain. [Senate hearing record](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-107shrg82951/html/CHRG-107shrg82951.htm)

- **14 September 1999:** *Enron New Hire Orientation*. An internal orientation speech in Houston—an enacted corporate text intended to induct employees into an institutional culture. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **25 January 2000:** *Enron in the New Economy*. A presentation to a Harvard Business School conference in Boston. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

### Testimony and regulatory interventions

- **1987:** *Statement on the Natural Gas Utilization Act of 1987*. Congressional testimony before the Senate Subcommittee on Energy Regulation and Conservation, printed at pp. 147–163. It is both evidence and intervention: a public text designed to influence legislation. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **1989:** *Statement and Testimony on Clean Air Act Reauthorization*. Congressional evidence before the House Subcommittee on Energy and Power, delivered on behalf of the Interstate Natural Gas Association of America and American Gas Association and printed at pp. 471–490 and 516–517; its institutional representation complicates purely individual authorship. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **1989:** *Natural Gas Wellhead Decontrol*. Testimony before the Senate Committee on Energy and Natural Resources, printed at pp. 99–104 of the hearing record. The bibliography supplies the hearing title rather than a distinct title for Lay’s statement. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **1993:** *Testimony on NAFTA: Energy Provisions and Environmental Implications*. A statement submitted for the record to the House Subcommittee on Energy and Power, printed at pp. 187–195; submission rather than oral delivery is the documented manifestation. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **15 May 1996:** *Electricity Regulation: A Vision for the Future*. Prepared testimony before the House Subcommittee on Energy and Power. It is a policy manifesto in testimonial form, advocating universal customer choice; the Department of Energy copy preserves both an executive summary and the statement. [Department of Energy record](https://www.energy.gov/sites/prod/files/maprod/documents/enron1996.pdf)

- **29 April 1997:** *Enron Corp Comments to FERC on Issues and Priorities for the Natural Gas Industry*. Regulatory comments filed in FERC Docket PL97-1. The title names Enron as the speaking body while the bibliography attributes the filing to Lay, so it should be treated as a corporate intervention bearing his authorship rather than an exclusively personal essay. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **12 February 2002:** *Statement of Kenneth L. Lay, Former Chairman and CEO, Enron*. A brief oral statement before the Senate Commerce Committee announcing that he would decline questions under the Fifth Amendment. It resists the ordinary category of testimony: formally placed in a hearing record, it chiefly performs a refusal to testify. [Official hearing transcript](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-107shrg82951/html/CHRG-107shrg82951.htm)

### Memoranda, correspondence, and administrative texts

- **24 October 1986:** *Letter to T. Boone Pickens Jr.* A piece of business correspondence; the bibliography also records a transmittal note to Jack Bowen attached to it, which is a separate paratext rather than another version of the letter. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **24 October 1986:** *Transmittal Note to Jack Bowen*. A short administrative paratext accompanying Lay’s letter to T. Boone Pickens Jr.; it barely resists “work” status because its purpose was transmission, not independent circulation. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **12 March 1992:** *Letter to Martin Allday*. Correspondence addressed to the chairman of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission and functioning as a regulatory intervention. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **30 November 1995:** *Letter to Enron’s Business Partners*. A corporate circular letter whose addressees are collective rather than personal. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **6 June 1996:** *Letter to Christopher C. DeMuth*. Correspondence addressed to the president of the American Enterprise Institute. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **7 November 1996:** *Tuesday’s Vote for Proposition 1*. A memorandum to Houston-based Enron employees; it acts upon its readers by urging electoral conduct and therefore has something of a manifesto’s performative character. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **1997:** *Kenneth L. Lay: Required Biographical Information, Part B—Career/Current Position*. A self-presentational administrative text prepared for nomination to the Horatio Alger Association. It resists treatment as an autonomous work because it was produced to satisfy a form or nomination protocol. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **20 May 1997:** *Don’t Drop the Ball*. A memorandum to Houston-based Enron employees. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

### Interviews and collective speech-events

- **17 January 1996:** *Interview: 1996 Goals and Enron 2000*. A typescript interview jointly attributed to Lay and Rich Kinder. It is a recorded conversational creation, not a singly authored essay. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)

- **17 November 2000:** *Transcribed Interviews*. Transcriptions from the 2000 Enron Management Conference, attributed to Lay and other participants. The bibliography does not identify the individual interviews or speakers exhaustively, so this is a collective documentary assemblage rather than a securely bounded Lay work. [Scholarly bibliography](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119493709.biblio)
