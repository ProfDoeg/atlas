## Works by Claude Lévi-Strauss

### Ethnographic monographs and theoretical books

- **1948:** *La Vie familiale et sociale des Indiens Nambikwara*. Ethnographic monograph and doctoral thesis derived from the 1938–1939 Brazilian expedition; its later book forms are manifestations of the same study, not separate works.

- **1949:** *Les Structures élémentaires de la parenté*. Doctoral thesis recast as a major theoretical monograph on kinship and exchange; the substantially revised 1967 edition changes the work but does not constitute an independent title.

- **1952:** *Race et histoire*. Commissioned UNESCO essay against racial hierarchy and evolutionary rankings of cultures; it is an intervention in public debate as well as an anthropological text.

- **1955:** *Tristes Tropiques*. A deliberately hybrid obra—travel narrative, ethnography, memoir, philosophy, literary construction and critique of travel writing—that resists classification as either scientific monograph or autobiography alone.

- **1958:** *Anthropologie structurale*. Constructed collection of previously published and revised essays; the arrangement makes it a theoretical book rather than merely a reprint container.

- **1962:** *Le Totémisme aujourd’hui*. Critical theoretical monograph dismantling “totemism” as a supposedly unitary institution.

- **1962:** *La Pensée sauvage*. Theoretical and philosophical anthropology of classification; conceived in relation to *Le Totémisme aujourd’hui* but an independent work.

- **1964:** *Le Cru et le Cuit*. First volume of *Mythologiques* and the opening movement of a four-volume analysis of Indigenous American myths.

- **1967:** *Du miel aux cendres*. Second volume of *Mythologiques*; its individual book status coexists with its role inside the larger cycle.

- **1968:** *L’Origine des manières de table*. Third volume of *Mythologiques*.

- **1971:** *L’Homme nu*. Fourth and concluding volume of *Mythologiques*.

- **1964–1971:** *Mythologiques*. Four-volume project comprising *Le Cru et le Cuit*, *Du miel aux cendres*, *L’Origine des manières de table* and *L’Homme nu*. It is also an obra in its own right: a continuous intellectual and literary construction whose musical architecture exceeds the usual essay collection.

- **1973:** *Anthropologie structurale deux*. Second constructed collection of theoretical papers, distinct from the 1958 volume rather than a revised edition of it.

- **1975; enlarged 1979:** *La Voie des masques*. Study of Northwest Coast masks first issued in two illustrated volumes and later substantially augmented by “Trois excursions”; image, material object and structural analysis are inseparable, giving the work something of a minor Gesamtkunstwerk.

- **1978:** *Myth and Meaning: Five Talks for Radio*. Five talks composed for and broadcast as the 1977 CBC Massey Lectures, then made into an English-language book. Its first medium was radio, so the printed volume is a manifestation of an oral-broadcast work.

- **1983:** *Le Regard éloigné*. Deliberately assembled collection of essays on anthropology, race, culture, kinship, myth and modernity.

- **1984:** *Paroles données*. Collection made from Lévi-Strauss’s annual teaching summaries and lectures of 1951–1982; it is simultaneously a book and the documentary trace of a long pedagogical project.

- **1985:** *La Potière jalouse*. Comparative mythological monograph extending the structural analysis of American myths beyond the completed *Mythologiques* cycle.

- **1991:** *Histoire de Lynx*. Mythological monograph that returns to American transformations and links them to the problem of cultural dualism.

- **1993:** *Regarder, écouter, lire*. Aesthetic essay moving among painting, music and literature; its method refuses a strict separation of the arts, although the made object itself remains a book.

### Essays, lectures and public interventions issued as distinct works

- **1933:** *Le socialisme et la colonisation*. Political article in *L’Étudiant socialiste*; an early public intervention rather than an ethnographic work.

- **1935:** *O cubismo e a vida cotidiana*. Essay on Cubism and everyday life, published in Portuguese during Lévi-Strauss’s Brazilian period.

- **1935:** *Em prol de um instituto de antropologia física e cultural*. Institutional proposal for an institute of physical and cultural anthropology; as a project-text, it proposes an institution rather than merely describing one.

- **1936:** *Contribution à l’étude de l’organisation sociale des Indiens Bororo*. Early ethnographic study, also published in Portuguese as *Contribuição para o estudo da organização social bororo*; the two publications are linguistic manifestations of substantially the same work.

- **1936:** *Os mais vastos horizontes do mundo*. Short Brazilian-period essay or témoignage; the title was later borrowed for a posthumous editorial collection.

- **1936:** *Entre os selvagens civilizados*. Newspaper article from the Brazilian period.

- **1936/1943:** *Guerre et commerce chez les Indiens de l’Amérique du Sud*. Anthropological essay whose publication history is bibliographically unstable: standard lists record a 1936 attribution as well as the securely documented 1943 appearance in *Renaissance*.

- **1937:** *A propósito da civilização chaco-santiaguense*. Archaeological-ethnological article published in Brazil.

- **1937:** *La sociologie culturelle et son enseignement*. Programmatic essay about cultural sociology and its teaching.

- **1937:** *Poupées Karajá*. Short ethnographic study of Karajá dolls.

- **1937–1938:** *Os contos de Perrault e sua significação sociológica*. Sociological reading of Perrault’s tales.

- **1940:** *À travers les déserts et les forêts du Brésil central*. Brief account of travel and fieldwork in central Brazil.

- **1942:** *Indian Cosmetics*. Ethnographic-surrealist essay published in *VVV*.

- **1942:** *Souvenir of Malinowski*. Memorial text published in *VVV*.

- **1944:** *The Social and Psychological Aspects of Chieftainship in a Primitive Tribe: The Nambikwara of Northwestern Mato Grosso*. Ethnographic article on Nambikwara leadership.

- **1944:** *On Dual Organization in South America*. Comparative anthropological article.

- **1945:** *Le dédoublement de la représentation dans les arts de l’Asie et de l’Amérique*. Comparative study of split representation, later incorporated into *Anthropologie structurale*.

- **1945:** *French Sociology*. Survey chapter later issued in French as *La Sociologie française*; these are versions of one commissioned overview rather than two wholly independent works.

- **1945:** *L’Analyse structurale en linguistique et en anthropologie*. Foundational methodological article linking structural linguistics and anthropology.

- **1946:** *La Technique du bonheur*. Philosophical-cultural essay.

- **1947:** *La Théorie du pouvoir dans une société primitive*. Study of political authority in a non-state society.

- **1947:** *Le Serpent au corps rempli de poissons*. Short mythological analysis later incorporated into *Anthropologie structurale*.

- **1948:** *The Tupi-Kawahib*. Ethnographic handbook chapter.

- **1948:** *The Tribes of the Upper Xingu River*. Regional ethnographic synthesis.

- **1948:** *The Nambicuara*. Ethnographic handbook chapter; its spelling reflects the publication in which it appeared.

- **1948:** *The Tribes of the Right Bank of the Guaporé River*. Regional ethnographic synthesis.

- **1949:** *L’Efficacité symbolique*. Essay on healing, myth and symbolic action, later incorporated into *Anthropologie structurale*.

- **1949:** *Le Sorcier et sa magie*. Essay on belief, shamanism and social efficacy, later incorporated into *Anthropologie structurale*.

- **1950:** *Introduction à l’œuvre de Marcel Mauss*. Substantial interpretive introduction to Mauss’s *Sociologie et anthropologie*; although attached to another author’s collection, it became an autonomous work in later editions.

- **1950:** *The Use of Wild Plants in Tropical South America*. Ethnobotanical handbook chapter.

- **1950:** *Documents Rama-Rama*. Edition and analysis of linguistic-ethnographic materials.

- **1951:** *Language and the Analysis of Social Laws*. Methodological article later revised for *Anthropologie structurale*.

- **1951:** *Les Sciences sociales au Pakistan*. Report arising from Lévi-Strauss’s UNESCO mission.

- **1951:** *Miscellaneous Notes on the Kuki of the Chittagong Hill Tribes, Pakistan*. Ethnographic field note.

- **1951:** *Le Pakistan : foyer spirituel, réalité nationale*. Public-facing travel and cultural essay.

- **1952:** *Kinship Systems of Three Chittagong Hill Tribes (Pakistan)*. Comparative kinship article.

- **1952:** *Le Syncrétisme religieux d’un village mogh du territoire de Chittagong*. Ethnographic study of religious mixture.

- **1952:** *La Notion d’archaïsme en ethnologie*. Critical essay later incorporated into *Anthropologie structurale*.

- **1952:** *Le Père Noël supplicié*. Essay on the ritual destruction of a Father Christmas effigy; later booklet editions are manifestations of this previously published article.

- **1952:** *Toward a General Theory of Communication*. Mimeographed conference paper. Its limited circulation and unpublished status make it resist the category of a finished public work.

- **1953:** *Panorama de l’ethnologie (1950–1952)*. Survey of contemporary ethnological research.

- **1953:** *Social Structure*. Major theoretical chapter for *Anthropology Today*.

- **1954:** *Place de l’anthropologie dans les sciences sociales et problèmes posés par son enseignement*. UNESCO study that operates partly as a protocol for locating and teaching anthropology institutionally.

- **1954:** *L’Art de déchiffrer les symboles*. Essay on symbolic interpretation.

- **1954:** *Qu’est-ce qu’un primitif ?*. Public essay contesting the supposedly self-evident category of the “primitive.”

- **1955:** *Diogène couché*. Polemical essay responding to criticism of *Tristes Tropiques*.

- **1955:** *Les Mathématiques de l’homme*. Programmatic essay on mathematical methods in the human sciences.

- **1955:** *The Structural Study of Myth*. Foundational article on structural myth analysis; the later French version contains alterations and additions.

- **1955:** *Des Indiens et leur ethnographe*. Reflective essay on relations between ethnographer and Indigenous subjects.

- **1956:** *The Family*. Synthetic essay commissioned for *Man, Culture, and Society*.

- **1956:** *Les Organisations dualistes existent-elles ?*. Comparative study of dual social organization.

- **1956:** *Structure et dialectique*. Methodological essay written for a volume honoring Roman Jakobson.

- **1956:** *Sur les rapports entre la mythologie et le rituel*. Lecture and published discussion on myth and ritual.

- **1957:** *Le Symbolisme cosmique dans la structure sociale et l’organisation cérémonielle de plusieurs populations nord- et sud-américaines*. Comparative conference paper.

- **1959:** *La Geste d’Asdiwal*. Extended structural analysis of a Tsimshian myth; subsequently revised and republished, notably in *Anthropologie structurale deux*.

- **1960:** *Four Winnebago Myths: A Structural Sketch*. Comparative myth analysis later incorporated into *Anthropologie structurale deux*.

- **1960:** *Ce que l’ethnologie doit à Durkheim*. Intellectual-historical essay.

- **1960:** *Les Trois Sources de la réflexion ethnologique*. Account of the traditions represented by Rousseau, Chateaubriand and Montaigne.

- **1960:** *La Structure et la forme: réflexions sur un ouvrage de Vladimir Propp*. Critical encounter with formalism, also published under the title *L’Analyse morphologique des contes russes*; the titles designate closely related manifestations of the same argument.

- **1960:** *Leçon inaugurale*. Inaugural lecture for the chair of Social Anthropology at the Collège de France, later known in some editions as *Éloge de l’anthropologie*.

- **1961:** *La Crise moderne de l’anthropologie*. Public essay on the discipline’s changing historical conditions.

- **1962:** *Les “Chats” de Charles Baudelaire*. Structural analysis of Baudelaire’s poem, co-authored with Roman Jakobson; neither collaborator’s authorship should be collapsed into the other’s.

- **1962:** *Jean-Jacques Rousseau, fondateur des sciences de l’homme*. Lecture-essay presenting Rousseau as a precursor of anthropology.

- **1971:** *Race et culture*. UNESCO lecture and essay. Its arguments differ significantly from *Race et histoire* and provoked controversy; it should not be treated as a revised edition of the 1952 work.

### Conversations and dialogic works

- **1961:** *Entretiens avec Claude Lévi-Strauss*. Broadcast conversations conducted by Georges Charbonnier and issued as a book. Authorship is dialogic: Charbonnier shaped the questions and encounter, while Lévi-Strauss supplied the spoken argument.

- **1988:** *De près et de loin*. Book-length conversations with Didier Eribon. It is neither a sole-authored treatise nor merely raw testimony but a collaboratively structured autobiographical-intellectual dialogue.

- **2002:** *Claude Lévi-Strauss, un itinéraire*. Published conversation with Marcello Massenzio, recorded in 2000.

- **2005:** *Loin du Brésil*. Book-length interview with Véronique Mortaigne; a collaborative oral work transformed into print.

### Photographic works and visual assemblages

- **1935–1939:** *Brazilian field-photograph corpus*. Photographs made during the Caduveo, Bororo, Nambikwara and Tupi-Kawahib journeys. They were initially field records and aides-mémoires rather than a fixed, titled artwork; their later selection, enlargement and sequencing created several different photographic manifestations.

- **ca. 1935–1937:** *São Paulo negatives*. A surviving group of forty-four urban negatives now held by the Instituto Moreira Salles. The group is an archival corpus, not a title Lévi-Strauss necessarily gave to a finished work.

- **1994:** *Saudades do Brasil*. Photographic memoir assembled from images made during the 1935–1939 expeditions and accompanied by retrospective text. Matthieu Lévi-Strauss produced the enlargements and is credited by Claude Lévi-Strauss with a co-authorial role, so the object is not unproblematically sole-authored.

- **1995:** *Saudades de São Paulo*. Photographic book made from Lévi-Strauss’s 1930s São Paulo negatives. The book is a retrospective visual assemblage, distinct from the original photographic corpus.

### Films

- **1935:** *Trabalhos do Gado no Curral de uma Fazenda do Sul do Mato Grosso*. Short ethnographic film associated with Claude and Dina Lévi-Strauss. Surviving catalogues credit the couple jointly, while later archival research emphasizes Dina’s leading role in the filmmaking; sole attribution to Claude would therefore be misleading.

- **1935:** *A Vida de uma Aldeia Bororo*. Short ethnographic film jointly associated with Claude and Dina Lévi-Strauss; the division of direction and camera work is not securely separable.

- **1935:** *Rio Vermelho / Cerimônias Funerais entre os Índios Bororo II*. Ethnographic film of Bororo funerary ceremonies. Titles vary between catalogues, and current BnF interpretation attributes the expedition films primarily to Dina.

- **1935–1936:** *Aldeia de Nalike*. Short ethnographic film made during the first Mato Grosso expedition; jointly catalogued under Dina and Claude Lévi-Strauss, with disputed or incompletely documented roles.

- **1936:** *Festa do Divino Espírito Santo*. Documentary film of a Brazilian religious celebration. As with the other expedition films, couple-level attribution survives, but recent archival work identifies Dina as the filmmaker most directly responsible.

### Field documents, collections and unfinished projects

- **1935–1939:** *Brazilian field notebooks and working papers*. A large collaborative corpus of notebooks, vocabulary lists, kinship diagrams, musical notations, drawings and ethnographic observations produced by Claude and Dina Lévi-Strauss. It resists being called a single work: it is simultaneously research apparatus, travel record, collaborative archive and the material prehistory of later books.

- **1938–1939:** *Nambikwara notebooks*. Approximately two thousand leaves of descriptions, drawings, linguistic records, musical notation and kinship diagrams from the second Brazilian expedition. The road journals that might have supplied the narrative spine of *Tristes Tropiques* appear to be missing, so the surviving corpus is explicitly incomplete.

- **1930s–2008:** *Fichiers de travail*. Extensive research files accumulated across Lévi-Strauss’s career and deposited with his archive at the Bibliothèque nationale de France. They constitute a working intellectual machine rather than a finished text and therefore resist ordinary work-status.

- **1940s; unfinished:** *L’Apothéose d’Auguste*. Project for a new dramatic treatment of the material of Corneille’s *Cinna*, discussed and partly preserved around *Tristes Tropiques*. It remained an unrealized theatrical project; the intention and surviving fragments, rather than a completed play, are the work.

- **1959–1982:** *Collège de France courses and annual summaries*. A sustained pedagogical and research project rather than a single book. Parts entered *Paroles données*, but the lectures, notes and summaries also survive as an archival body with their own incomplete and uneven status.

- **2007:** *Fonds Claude Lévi-Strauss, NAF 28150*. The archive transferred to the Bibliothèque nationale de France comprises manuscripts, proofs, correspondence, field notebooks, working files, photographs and personal documents. The fonds is a consciously transmitted body of work, but its archival arrangement is partly the creation of custodians and cannot be attributed to Lévi-Strauss alone.

### Academic speech-acts

- **1974:** *Discours de réception de Claude Lévi-Strauss à l’Académie française*. Ceremonial address delivered on taking seat 29; it acts institutionally by receiving its speaker into the Académie and commemorating Henry de Montherlant.

- **1977:** *Réponse au discours de réception d’Alain Peyrefitte*. Institutional response delivered for the Académie française.

- **1979:** *Réponse au discours de réception de Georges Dumézil*. Institutional response and tribute delivered for the Académie française.

- **1982:** *Discours pour l’inauguration d’une place Henry-de-Montherlant*. Commemorative public speech tied to a civic naming ceremony.

- **1987:** *Hommage prononcé à l’occasion du décès de M. André Roussin*. Academic memorial address.

### Posthumous manifestations and editorial constructions

- **2008:** *Œuvres*. Author-approved Pléiade selection containing seven previously existing books, with scholarly apparatus. It is an important collected-works manifestation, not the complete works and not seven new works.

- **2011:** *L’Anthropologie face aux problèmes du monde moderne*. Posthumous French publication of three lectures delivered in Japan in 1986. The lectures are Lévi-Strauss’s works; their arrangement as this book is editorial.

- **2011:** *L’Autre face de la lune: Écrits sur le Japon*. Posthumous collection of previously dispersed writings on Japan. It is an editorially constituted manifestation rather than a book designed in this form by Lévi-Strauss.

- **2013:** *Nous sommes tous des cannibales*. Posthumous collection centered on columns written for *La Repubblica* between 1989 and 2000, with additional texts. The individual essays are Lévi-Strauss’s; the collection as such is an editorial construction.

- **2024:** *Les Plus Vastes Horizons du monde*. Posthumous assembly of early Brazilian writings, images and archival material. Its contents include works by Lévi-Strauss, but the modern book and its title-as-collection are editorial manifestations and should not be silently backdated to the 1930s.

- **2025:** *Aux sources de Tristes tropiques: les carnets de terrain de Claude et Dina Lévi-Strauss (1935–1939)*. Scholarly edition of the couple’s field notebooks, directed by Emmanuel Désveaux. The underlying documents are Claude and Dina’s collaborative field corpus; the published edition is the later editors’ work and is included here only as its documentary manifestation.
