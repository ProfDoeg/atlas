## Works by John Chapman

### Nurseries — living and commercial obras

- **ca. 1797–1804:** Nursery on Big Brokenstraw Creek near White’s, Warren County, Pennsylvania. A seedling-apple nursery known through early local testimony; its precise site and dates remain approximate. [Price catalogue, p. 91](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=103)
- **ca. 1798–1804:** Nursery near Franklin on French Creek, Venango County, Pennsylvania. A traditionally attributed nursery rather than a surviving, individually titled object. [Price catalogue, p. 91](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=103)
- **ca. 1801:** Nursery on the old Ward farm, southwest of Carrollton, Ohio. The attribution rests on later county history and newspaper testimony. [Price catalogue, pp. 91–92](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=103)
- **ca. 1801:** Nursery at the headwaters of Big Stillwater Creek, between Morristown and Freeport, Belmont County, Ohio. A cultivated living site, not a text or conventionally titled work. [Price catalogue, p. 92](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=104)
- **ca. 1801:** Nursery near the mouth of George’s Run, below Steubenville and opposite Wellsburg, Jefferson County, Ohio. Its identification comes from a county history published decades afterward. [Price catalogue, p. 92](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=104)
- **ca. 1807–1812:** Nursery in New Castle Township near the Giffin-section blockhouse, Coshocton County, Ohio. A seedling nursery whose historical location was described by later ownership rather than by a fixed title. [Price catalogue, p. 92](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=104)
- **ca. 1807–1812:** Nursery on “Nursery Island” in the Mohican River, near the Coshocton–Knox county line. Its very medium was unstable—a cultivated river island—and the attribution is historical rather than archaeological. [Price catalogue, p. 92](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=104)
- **ca. 1807–1812:** Nursery on the north bank of Owl Creek in the “Indian Fields,” west of Center Run, Knox County, Ohio. A living agricultural installation recorded in nineteenth-century county history. [Price catalogue, p. 92](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=104)
- **ca. 1807–1812:** Nursery at Mount Vernon on the later site of James W. Forest’s pottery, Knox County, Ohio. The work survives as a reported location, not as an intact nursery. [Price catalogue, pp. 92–93](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=104)
- **ca. 1807–1817:** Nursery near the Mohican River in Tiverton Township, Coshocton County, Ohio. A bounded planting identified approximately as lot 36, section 3. [Price catalogue, p. 92](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=104)
- **ca. 1812:** Nursery at Scotland Farm, northeast of Newark, Licking County, Ohio. Its date and attribution derive from an 1872 pioneer account. [Price catalogue, p. 92](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=104)
- **ca. 1812:** Nursery on the Ruffner quarter-section beside the Black Fork, Ashland County, Ohio. A cultivated landscape known from local history rather than a surviving authorial record. [Price catalogue, p. 93](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=105)
- **ca. 1812–1820:** Nursery on the west bank of Rocky Fork at Mansfield, near the later railway depot. Multiple early local histories associate Chapman with this nursery, although its boundaries are no longer fixed. [Price catalogue, p. 93](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=105)
- **ca. 1815–1818:** Nursery on the Michael Hogan farm beside the Black Fork, Green Township, Ashland County, Ohio. Its catalogue entry combines reminiscence, newspaper evidence, and land records. [Price catalogue, p. 93](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=105)
- **ca. 1818:** Nursery in Sandusky Township, Richland County, Ohio. Unlike many traditional sites, this attribution is tied to a legally described quarter-section in Chapman’s land records. [Price catalogue, p. 93](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=105)
- **ca. 1818:** Nursery at the later site of Leidigh’s Mill, Orange Township, Ashland County, Ohio. A local-history attribution with no surviving title or physical completeness. [Price catalogue, p. 93](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=105)
- **ca. 1823:** Nursery on Jerome Fork, Mohican Township, Ashland County, Ohio. Its surveyed land description connects this living work to documentary holdings. [Price catalogue, pp. 93–94](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=105)
- **ca. 1825:** Nursery on the George Ganung lands east of the marsh, New Haven Township, Huron County, Ohio. The attribution comes from the *Fire Lands Pioneer*. [Price catalogue, p. 95](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=107)
- **1828:** Nursery along the Auglaize River on land leased from Jacob Harter, Amanda Township, Allen County, Ohio. The lease provides documentary footing stronger than that of many traditionally located nurseries. [Price catalogue, p. 94](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=106)
- **ca. 1828:** Nursery on the Dye Sutherland farm, section 15, Amanda Township, Allen County, Ohio. Its date and identity are supplied by a later county history. [Price catalogue, p. 94](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=106)
- **1828:** Nursery south of St. Marys on land leased from Picket Doute, Auglaize County, Ohio. This was a commercial seedling plantation attached to a documented lease. [Price catalogue, p. 94](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=106)
- **ca. 1828:** Nursery on the William Berryman farm, Logan Township, Auglaize County, Ohio. A later county history locates it in the northeast quarter of section 27. [Price catalogue, p. 94](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=106)
- **1828:** Nursery on the St. Marys River below Shanesville, now Rockford, Mercer County, Ohio. Its attribution is supported by both county history and land-holding documentation. [Price catalogue, pp. 94–95](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=106)
- **ca. 1828:** Nursery near the headwaters of the Blanchard River, Hancock County, Ohio. Pioneer reminiscences place the nursery but do not give it a formal title. [Price catalogue, p. 94](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=106)
- **ca. 1828:** Nursery on Mill Branch at the later Alonzo and Allen West farm, Logan County, Ohio. Its attribution depends on Joshua Antrim’s 1872 county history. [Price catalogue, p. 95](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=107)
- **ca. 1828:** Nursery somewhere on Stony Creek, Logan County, Ohio. The source itself leaves the site indefinite, so this resists treatment as a securely bounded individual work. [Price catalogue, p. 95](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=107)
- **ca. 1828:** Nursery at the mouth of the Tiffin River above Defiance, Ohio. A county history places it on land later owned by Charles Krotz. [Price catalogue, p. 94](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=106)
- **ca. 1828 or 1845:** Nursery west of the St. Joseph River, north of Fort Wayne, Indiana. The catalogue preserves radically different proposed dates rather than choosing between the newspaper and letter evidence. [Price catalogue, p. 95](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=107)
- **ca. 1829:** Nursery near the southeast corner of section 27, Green Township, Ashland County, Ohio. A lease from John Oliver documents the associated land holding. [Price catalogue, p. 93](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=105)
- **ca. 1829–1830:** Nursery on the Maumee River opposite Snaketown, now Florida, Ohio. The attribution is reported in an 1883 county history. [Price catalogue, p. 94](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=106)
- **1834 or later:** Nursery on the north bank of the Maumee River, Milan Township, Allen County, Indiana. Chapman purchased the land in 1834; the catalogue does not supply a more exact planting date. [Price catalogue, p. 95](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=107)
- **1836 or later:** Nursery north of the Wabash River, Wabash Township, Jay County, Indiana. It is connected to land Chapman purchased in 1836, but its planting date and extent are not fixed. [Price catalogue, p. 95](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=107)
- **Undated:** Nursery between Charles Mills and Mansfield on the later Pittinger farm, Richland County, Ohio. The source proposes no date, and the attribution survives only through local historical testimony. [Price catalogue, p. 93](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=105)
- **Undated:** Nursery on the Black Fork near Petersburg, now Mifflinville, Ashland County, Ohio. An 1873 newspaper supplies the attribution but no planting date. [Price catalogue, p. 93](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=105)
- **Undated:** Nursery on the John Murphy place in Green Township, Ashland County, Ohio. The site is known only relationally—northeast of the former James Rowland farm—and no date is suggested. [Price catalogue, p. 94](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=106)

### Commercial documents and collaborative acts

- **Undated:** Apple-tree order bearing Chapman’s signature. This is a transactional manuscript object, not a literary work; the consulted source reproduces it but does not establish that Chapman composed every word rather than merely signing it. [Silverman, pp. 39–40](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=51)
- **Undated:** IOU issued by Chapman. A small commercial instrument connected with his nursery business; it is evidence of an act and obligation more readily than an autonomous “work.” [Silverman, pp. 42–43](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=54)
- **Early nineteenth century:** Two petitions supporting the ordination of Silas Ensign. Chapman signed these collective documents, but the surviving evidence does not make him their sole author; they therefore resist inclusion as works “by” him, though his signatures are documented participatory acts. [Silverman, pp. 67–68 and 127–28](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=79)

### Unrecoverable oral and written projects

- **Before 1845:** Missionary talks, Bible stories, and “tall tales.” Contemporary and later testimony credits Chapman with repeated oral performances, but no stable titles or authoritative texts survive; they are manifestations rather than recoverable works. [Silverman, pp. 64–69, 76](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=76)
- **Before 1845:** Correspondence concerning Swedenborgian books and missionary organization. Chapman’s letters have not survived, so their number, dates, wording, and status as discrete works cannot responsibly be reconstructed. [Silverman, pp. 64–65](https://swedenborg.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/SF_TheCoreofJohnnyAppleseed.pdf#page=76)
