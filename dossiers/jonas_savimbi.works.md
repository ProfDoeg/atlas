## Works by Jonas Malheiro Savimbi

### Manifestos, programmes, and political projects

- **16 July 1964:** *Declaration of Resignation from the GRAE/FNLA*. A manifesto delivered in Cairo that publicly broke with Holden Roberto and helped prepare Savimbi’s independent political project; it circulated under variant descriptive titles, including *Why We Quitted the U.P.A. and G.R.A.E. of Mr. Holden Roberto* and *Roberto Betrays Both the Revolution and the Masses*. [[ISCTE documentary appendix](https://repositorio-iul.iscte.pt/bitstream/10071/3265/4/ANEXOS%20FINAL.pdf); [UNITA document index](https://en.marxists.info/subject/africa/unita/index.htm)]
- **1964–1966:** *The UNITA project*. Savimbi and Tony da Costa Fernandes reportedly drafted constitutional statutes in Switzerland in 1964; UNITA was formally founded in Angola in March 1966. This is an institutional and military-political obra rather than a text, and its collective authorship prevents its attribution to Savimbi alone. [[Universidade de Évora study](https://rdpc.uevora.pt/handle/10174/12105); [Ribeiro, *História da UNITA*](http://hdl.handle.net/10174/35959)]
- **1966:** *The Programme of UNITA*. A collective political programme—a manifesto in the operative sense—subsequently printed in the 1972 special issue of *Kwacha–Angola*; the surviving bibliographic record does not securely distinguish Savimbi’s individual wording from collective party authorship. [[UNITA document index](https://en.marxists.info/subject/africa/unita/index.htm)]
- **1967:** *Joint Statement on the AAPSO Khartoum Conference*. A collective declaration issued with the Pan-Africanist Congress of South Africa, ZANU, and COREMO. It belongs to Savimbi’s political activity but resists classification as his individual work; catalogues also record a related or reissued statement dated 1969. [[UNITA document index](https://en.marxists.info/subject/africa/unita/index.htm)]
- **1968:** *Angola—Seventh Year*. A UNITA political publication associated with Savimbi’s leadership; surviving listings do not establish sole personal authorship, so it is best treated as a collective manifestation of the movement. [[UNITA document index](https://en.marxists.info/subject/africa/unita/index.htm)]
- **1969:** *The Second Congress of UNITA*. A congress publication rather than a single-author book; it records a collective political event and includes material associated with Savimbi. [[UNITA document index](https://en.marxists.info/subject/africa/unita/index.htm)]
- **1970:** *An International Conspiracy Against the African Peoples*. A polemical UNITA publication preserved in the movement’s documentary corpus. Its proximity to Savimbi’s discourse is clear, but the available record does not prove that every word is individually his. [[UNITA document index](https://en.marxists.info/subject/africa/unita/index.htm)]
- **1971:** *Exposing the Lies of Basil Davidson on Angola*. A polemical intervention from the UNITA press environment; individual authorship is not firmly specified in the surviving index. [[UNITA document index](https://en.marxists.info/subject/africa/unita/index.htm)]
- **1971:** *Portugal and Allies (NATO) Facing Defeat*. A political-propaganda text associated with UNITA’s anticolonial campaign; it resists secure attribution to Savimbi alone. [[UNITA document index](https://en.marxists.info/subject/africa/unita/index.htm)]
- **1971:** *Basil Davidson in the Service of Modern Revisionism*. A second polemic against Davidson, preserved as a UNITA document but not securely established as Savimbi’s sole composition. [[UNITA document index](https://en.marxists.info/subject/africa/unita/index.htm)]
- **1972:** *UNITA: The MPLA–GRAE Opportunistic Reconciliation*. A party polemic published in *Kwacha–Angola*; it manifests Savimbi’s political line but remains institutionally rather than securely individually authored. [[UNITA document index](https://en.marxists.info/subject/africa/unita/index.htm)]
- **1984:** *The Angolan Road to National Recovery* / *A Via de Recuperação Nacional de Angola: Definição dos Princípios e Objectivos*. A privately printed national-reconstruction programme: simultaneously policy project, manifesto, and proposed protocol for a future state. English and Portuguese titles circulate, and the exact publication history is poorly documented. [[Encyclopedia.com selected writings](https://www.encyclopedia.com/people/history/african-history-biographies/jonas-savimbi); [UNITA commemoration citing the Portuguese title and date](https://unitaespanha.blogspot.com/2015/08/declaracao-alusiva-ao-3-de-agosto.html)]

### Protocols and military instruction

- **1968:** *1.ª Edição da Cartilha do Guerrilheiro da UNITA*. A twenty-five-page protocol for commanders, combining political organization, military doctrine, discipline, and relations with civilians. A contemporary Portuguese-security-service note calls it Savimbi’s work and dates it to 1968; later bibliographies sometimes date editions to 1977 or 1979, so composition and publication dates must not be conflated. [[Scholarly analysis in *Cadernos de Estudos Africanos*](https://journals.openedition.org/cea/pdf/7110); [Portuguese Defence Archive guide](https://www.portugal.gov.pt/upload/ficheiros/i007076.pdf)]
- **Date not securely established:** *Guia Prático do Quadro*. A training manual or course digest reportedly used at UNITA’s Kapessi Kafundanga study centre. Its existence is attested by former movement sources, but no dependable first-edition date or complete bibliographic description has been located; it therefore resists treatment as a conventional published work. [[Report on Savimbi’s writings and papers](https://club-k.net/index.php?Itemid=641&catid=23&id=4487%3Adiario-de-savimbi-em-posse-das-faa&lang=pt&option=com_content&view=article)]

### Books and documentary assemblages

- **1978:** *O Que É o “Espírito de Bissau”? Todos os Portugueses Poderão Regressar a Angola?* A political essay or extended intervention addressing regional diplomacy and the possible place of Portuguese settlers in Angola. Scholarly references disagree over the imprint—Jamba/UNITA or Aveiro/Tipave—apparently reflecting production or distribution variants rather than separate works. [[Ribeiro, *História da UNITA* bibliography](http://hdl.handle.net/10174/35959); [scholarly citation with Aveiro imprint](https://www.ispsn.org/sites/default/files/documentos-virtuais/pdf/politica_externa_angolana.pdf)]
- **1978:** *Quo Vadis, Angola Nossa?* A political diagnosis and national programme published by UNITA in Paris. It is consistently named in the scholarly bibliography, although a detailed contents description and definitive publication history remain unavailable. [[Ribeiro, *História da UNITA* bibliography](http://hdl.handle.net/10174/35959)]
- **1979:** *Angola: A Resistência em Busca de uma Nova Nação*. A documentary-political assemblage containing historical narrative, congress material, programmes, and Savimbi’s arguments for UNITA’s post-independence national project; it is not a unitary monograph in the ordinary sense. [[Melícias bibliography](https://repositorio.pucrs.br/dspace/bitstream/10923/10912/1/000485860-Texto%2BCompleto-0.pdf); [Costa’s scholarly analysis](https://tede2.pucrs.br/tede2/bitstream/tede/9394/6/DIS_RENATA_DARIVA_COSTA_COMPLETO.pdf)]
- **1986:** *Por um Futuro Melhor*. An illustrated collection of speeches, interventions, and political texts rather than a newly composed continuous book. Sources variously give 192 or 194 pages, an edition-level discrepancy rather than evidence of two separate obras. [[AfricaBib record](https://www.africabib.org/rec.php?RID=038047926); [Melícias bibliography](https://repositorio.pucrs.br/dspace/bitstream/10923/10912/1/000485860-Texto%2BCompleto-0.pdf)]

### Poetry

- **1985:** *Quando a Terra Voltar a Sorrir um Dia*. A poetry collection published in Lisbon by Perspectivas & Realidades and associated with Savimbi’s poetic persona “Jaguar Negro dos Jagas.” Some later references use the title for its best-known individual poem, so the poem and the containing book should not be silently treated as identical objects. [[Costa’s scholarly discussion and bibliography](https://tede2.pucrs.br/tede2/bitstream/tede/9394/6/DIS_RENATA_DARIVA_COSTA_COMPLETO.pdf); [contemporary bibliographic notice](https://euandoaler.blogspot.com/2016/12/livro-quando-terra-voltar-sorrir-um-dia.html)]

### Speeches, interviews, letters, and essays

- **1966:** *UNITA Was Born*. A retrospective interview with Savimbi, later printed in the 1972 special issue of *Kwacha–Angola*. It is an oral-textual manifestation jointly made by speaker, interviewer, translator, and editor, not straightforwardly an authored essay. [[UNITA document index](https://en.marxists.info/subject/africa/unita/index.htm)]
- **1967:** *Revolution and Reconstruction*. A press conference with Savimbi, subsequently textualized in *Kwacha–Angola*. Its work-status lies between political performance and edited transcript. [[UNITA document index](https://en.marxists.info/subject/africa/unita/index.htm)]
- **1967:** *Jonas Savimbi Interview with a McGill University Student*. An interview later printed in the 1972 *Kwacha–Angola* special issue; the bibliographic record does not name the student or establish how heavily the transcript was edited. [[UNITA document index](https://en.marxists.info/subject/africa/unita/index.htm)]
- **1969:** *Savimbi: We Have Our Own Philosophy*. A discourse delivered to UNITA’s Second Congress and later printed in *Kwacha–Angola*. It is both a speech-act defining party doctrine and an edited textual record. [[UNITA document index](https://en.marxists.info/subject/africa/unita/index.htm)]
- **October 1969:** *Letter to the Liberation Committee of the Organization of African Unity*. A diplomatic letter published in *Kwacha–Angola*. Although occasion-bound correspondence, its public circulation made it function as a political manifesto. [[UNITA document index](https://en.marxists.info/subject/africa/unita/index.htm)]
- **circa 1970:** *The Role of the Army in Angola*. An interview setting out Savimbi’s view of armed organization, later reprinted in *Kwacha–Angola*; the archive index supplies no more precise original date. [[UNITA document index](https://en.marxists.info/subject/africa/unita/index.htm)]
- **1970:** *Interview with Dr. Jonas Savimbi*. An interview published later in the *Kwacha–Angola* special issue. Its generic title and unnamed interviewer make it difficult to establish as a fully independent work rather than editorial documentation. [[UNITA document index](https://en.marxists.info/subject/africa/unita/index.htm)]
- **1971–1973:** *Letters connected with “Operation Timber”*. A corpus of clandestine correspondence between Savimbi/UNITA and Portuguese military authorities. These are discrete, instrumental texts rather than a work conceived as a whole; their authenticity and meaning became fiercely disputed because they were used to allege collaboration with the colonial regime. [[Melícias’s archival discussion](https://repositorio.pucrs.br/dspace/bitstream/10923/10912/1/000485860-Texto%2BCompleto-0.pdf); [Portuguese historical overview](https://guerracolonial.pt/1966-construir-um-bastiao-branco-na-africa-austral/jonas-savimbi-e-a-unita/)]
- **1 October 1976:** *1976 Begins the Defeat of Soviet-Cuban Imperialism in Angola*. A taped address to UNITA’s external cadres, delivered at the Central Base and subsequently translated and printed. The surviving English text is therefore a mediated transcript rather than Savimbi’s fixed authorial manuscript. [[Publication transcript](https://www.marxists.org/history/erol/ncm-1/workers-advocate/6-9.html)]
- **1978–1979:** *Jonas Savimbi: O Homem do Projecto Angolano*. The full text of an interview given to journalist João Rosa and published as a book in 1979. It is co-produced oral testimony rather than a sole-authored book; later notices also associate related material with the title *O Desafio de Savimbi*. [[Ribeiro, *História da UNITA* bibliography](http://hdl.handle.net/10174/35959)]
- **June 1980:** *We Are Working for One Angola*. An article or interview published in *Worldview*, volume 23. The bibliographic record assigns it to Savimbi but does not resolve how much of its final wording was editorially shaped. [[Bibliography in *A Political History of the Civil War in Angola*](https://s3-euw1-ap-pe-df-pch-content-store-p.s3.eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/9781315083292/4caba7ab-60fe-439a-b1c6-d79c48d6b0a1/preview.pdf)]
- **Winter 1986:** *The War Against Soviet Colonialism: The Strategy and Tactics of Anti-Communist Resistance*. A programmatic essay in *Policy Review* 35, presenting UNITA’s war as anti-colonial resistance. It acts as a manifesto addressed to a United States policy audience, not merely as historical description. [[Contemporary journal copy](https://iiif.library.cmu.edu/file/Heinz_box00275_fld00002_bdl0023_doc0002/Heinz_box00275_fld00002_bdl0023_doc0002.pdf); [Melícias bibliography](https://repositorio.pucrs.br/dspace/bitstream/10923/10912/1/000485860-Texto%2BCompleto-0.pdf)]
- **5 October 1989:** *The Coming Winds of Democracy in Angola*. A Heritage Foundation lecture, subsequently issued as Lecture/Report no. 217. It is simultaneously an oral political performance and a printed advocacy text. [[Heritage Foundation record](https://www.heritage.org/africa/report/the-coming-winds-democracy-angola); [PolicyArchive description](https://www.policyarchive.org/handle/10207/12623)]

### Unpublished or disputed remains

- **Before 22 February 2002:** *Memoirs or diary reportedly recovered after Savimbi’s death*. A press report claims that Savimbi had completed memoirs and that a manuscript or diary was seized by the Angolan armed forces after his final battle. No publicly examinable manuscript, settled title, catalogue description, or independent authentication is available, so this resists being called an extant work rather than a reported one. [[Contemporary report](https://club-k.net/index.php?Itemid=641&catid=23&id=4487%3Adiario-de-savimbi-em-posse-das-faa&lang=pt&option=com_content&view=article)]
