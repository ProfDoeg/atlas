## Works by Camille Rosalie Claudel

### Sculptures: portraits, heads, and figure studies

- **c. 1879–1881:** *Buste d’homme ou Bismarck*. Portrait bust; the sitter and therefore the alternative title remain uncertain. ([catalogue no. 1](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1881:** *Diane*. Sculptural figure representing the goddess Diana. ([catalogue no. 2](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1881:** *Paul Claudel à treize ans ou Jeune Achille*. Portrait of Claudel’s brother presented alternatively as the mythological Achilles. ([catalogue no. 3](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1882:** *La Vieille Hélène ou Buste de vieille femme*. Portrait or character-study bust of an elderly woman. ([catalogue no. 4](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1882–1885:** *Torse de femme debout*. Sculptural study of a standing female torso. ([catalogue no. 5](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **c. 1884–1885:** *Femme accroupie*. Full sculptural study of a crouching woman. ([catalogue no. 6](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **c. 1884–1885:** *Torse de femme accroupie*. Fragmentary or deliberately partial torso study, surviving in mutilated condition. ([catalogue no. 7](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **c. 1883–1885:** *Buste de Jessie Lipscomb*. Portrait bust of Claudel’s fellow sculptor and studio companion. ([catalogue no. 8](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1884:** *Jeune Romain ou Mon frère à seize ans*. Portrait bust of Paul Claudel, also given an all’antica identity; the catalogue distinguishes two manifestations of the model. ([catalogue nos. 9.1–9.2](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **c. 1885:** *Jeune femme aux yeux clos*. Sculpted female head or bust with closed eyes. ([catalogue no. 10](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **c. 1885:** *Buste de Louise Claudel ou Buste de Louise de Massary ou Buste de jeune fille*. Portrait bust whose three titles reflect uncertainty or variation in identifying the sitter. ([catalogue no. 11](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1885:** *Étude pour un bourgeois de Calais*. Sculptural study associated with Rodin’s *Bourgeois de Calais* project; the catalogue records two manifestations, making Claudel’s contribution a workshop collaboration as well as an autonomous object. ([catalogue nos. 12.1–12.2](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1885:** *L’Homme aux bras croisés*. Male figure study with crossed arms. ([catalogue no. 13](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **c. 1885:** *Tête d’esclave*. Sculpted character head; the historical title is retained as a catalogue title rather than endorsed as a description of the model’s status. ([catalogue no. 14](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **c. 1885:** *La Main*. Anatomical sculptural study of a hand. ([catalogue no. 15](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1885:** *Giganti ou Tête de brigand ou Homme de peine*. Male character head known under three titles, none securely privileged by the catalogue. ([catalogue no. 16](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **c. 1885:** *Tête de rieuse ou de rieur*. Expressive head whose sitter’s sex, and consequently the title, is unresolved. ([catalogue no. 17](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1886:** *L’Homme penché*. Sculptural study of a bending male figure. ([catalogue no. 18](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1887:** *La Jeune Fille à la gerbe*. Female figure carrying or associated with a sheaf; a freestanding sculptural composition. ([catalogue no. 19](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1888:** *Ferdinand de Massary*. Sculptural portrait. ([catalogue no. 20](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **c. 1888:** *Jeune fille au chignon*. Sculpted head of a young woman, catalogued as one of three distinct works under no. 24. ([catalogue no. 24.1](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **c. 1888:** *Tête de négresse*. Sculpted female head; the catalogue’s historical title is retained for identification but uses terminology now recognized as offensive. ([catalogue no. 24.2](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1888–1889:** *Buste d’Auguste Rodin*. Portrait bust of Rodin and an autonomous work despite its origin within their personal and workshop relationship. ([catalogue no. 25](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1889:** *Buste de Charles Lhermitte*. Portrait bust. ([catalogue no. 26](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1889:** *La Prière ou Psaume ou L’Inspirée*. Devotional or visionary figure known by three alternative titles. ([catalogue no. 27](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1893:** *Torse de Clotho*. Torso study for Claudel’s figure of the Fate Clotho. ([catalogue no. 31](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1893:** *Clotho*. Full sculptural figure of the mythological Fate; distinct from the later work catalogued under the same title. ([catalogue no. 32](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1893–1896:** *La Petite Châtelaine*. Portrait bust developed through at least ten materially and formally distinct manifestations, especially variations in the child’s braid and openwork hair; it is one obra with unusually numerous sculptural states rather than ten unrelated subjects. ([catalogue nos. 33.1–33.10](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1893:** *Chienne affamée*. Animal sculpture of a starving dog. ([catalogue no. 35](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1894:** *Tête de vieille femme*. Sculpted character head of an elderly woman. ([catalogue no. 39](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1894:** *Tête de vieil homme*. Sculpted character head of an elderly man, known in two catalogued manifestations. ([catalogue nos. 40.1–40.2](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1894:** *Léon Lhermitte*. Sculptural portrait of the painter Léon Lhermitte. ([catalogue no. 44](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1894:** *Vieil Aveugle chantant*. Sculpted character figure of an elderly blind singer. ([catalogue no. 45](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1894–1895:** *Étude pour L’Hamadryade*. Preparatory sculptural study surviving or recorded in three states, two with bases and one without. ([catalogue nos. 46.1–46.3](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1895:** *Buste de fillette*. Portrait or character bust of a young girl. ([catalogue no. 67](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1897:** *L’Hamadryade*. Finished mythological female figure developed from the preceding studies. ([catalogue no. 47](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1897:** *Femme lisant une lettre*. Small narrative or genre sculpture of a woman reading. ([catalogue no. 49](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1897:** *Comtesse Arthur de Maigret*. Sculptural portrait. ([catalogue no. 50](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1897:** *Clotho*. A later sculptural treatment of Clotho, catalogued separately from the 1893 torso and figure. ([catalogue no. 54](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1899:** *Comtesse Christian de Maigret*. Sculptural portrait. ([catalogue no. 51](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1899:** *Comte Christian de Maigret*. Sculptural portrait. ([catalogue no. 52](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1901–1908:** *L’Aurore*. Allegorical sculptural head or bust known in separately dated 1901 and 1908 manifestations. ([catalogue nos. 34.1–34.2](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **c. 1904:** *Tête d’enfant*. Sculpted child’s head. ([catalogue no. 66](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1905–1910:** *Paul Claudel à trente-sept ans*. Portrait bust of Paul Claudel, recorded in manifestations dated 1905 and 1910; the latter date may describe a later realization or edition rather than fresh modeling by the artist. ([catalogue nos. 63.1–63.2](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1906:** *Niobide blessée*. Mythological female figure representing a wounded daughter of Niobe. ([catalogue no. 64](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))

### Sculptural groups, narratives, and decorative objects

- **1885:** *Étude pour L’Avarice et la Luxure*. Sculptural study for a larger allegorical project; its status as a study preserves an intention rather than necessarily a completed composition. ([catalogue no. 29](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1886–1888:** *Première étude pour Çacountala*. First sculptural study for Claudel’s embracing-couple project. ([catalogue no. 21](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1886–1888:** *Deuxième étude pour Çacountala*. Second sculptural study for the same project. ([catalogue no. 22](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1886–1888:** *Çacountala*. Completed sculptural group inspired by the story of Śakuntalā; later manifestations bear the titles *Vertumne et Pomone* and *L’Abandon*, complicating any claim that title and obra are identical. ([catalogue no. 23](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **c. 1890:** *Croquis d’amateur*. Small sculptural “sketch,” a modeled scene whose title deliberately gives it the provisional status of a croquis. ([catalogue no. 30](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1892–1905:** *La Valse*. Sculptural group of a dancing couple, developed through eight catalogued manifestations—including veiled and differently finished plasters, stoneware, and bronze editions—so that the obra is inseparable from its sequence of material states. ([catalogue nos. 28.1–28.8](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1893–1897:** *Les Causeuses*. Intimate group of four conversing women, realized in at least five states with or without a screen and in different material combinations; Claudel called such small narrative ensembles “croquis d’après nature.” ([catalogue nos. 43.1–43.5](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1894:** *Le Dieu envolé, deuxième version*. Sculptural figure or fragment belonging to the project that developed into *L’Implorante* and *L’Âge mûr*; “second version” makes it a state within an evolving project. ([catalogue no. 37](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1894:** *Étude pour L’Implorante*. Preparatory figure for the kneeling woman later separated from, and also incorporated within, *L’Âge mûr*. ([catalogue no. 38](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1894–1905:** *L’Implorante ou Imploration*. Kneeling female figure that functions both as an autonomous obra and as a constituent of *L’Âge mûr*; the catalogue records the “boulangère” example and large, small, and intermediate Blot editions. ([catalogue nos. 41.1–41.4](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1897:** *La Vague ou Les Baigneuses*. Small sculptural group of bathers confronted by a wave, combining figure sculpture with a decorative, materially hybrid setting. ([catalogue no. 48](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1897–1903:** *Persée et la Gorgone*. Monumental mythological group, first dated 1897–1902 and then represented by a 1903 manifestation; its different realizations belong to one extended sculptural project. ([catalogue nos. 57.1–57.2](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1898–1907:** *L’Âge mûr*. Three-figure sculptural drama developed in two substantially different versions and a later Blot reduction; its changing composition makes it a project with successive manifestations rather than a single fixed object. ([catalogue nos. 42.1–42.3](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1898–1905:** *La Profonde Pensée*. Contemplative female figure realized in five catalogued manifestations, including marble, bronze, marble-and-bronze, and onyx-and-bronze versions. ([catalogue nos. 53.1–53.5](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1899–1905:** *Rêve au coin du feu*. Small domestic or introspective figure realized with a chair, with a stool, in marble and bronze, and in bronze alone; its furniture is part of the composition rather than merely a display support. ([catalogue nos. 55.1–55.4](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1900:** *La Fortune*. Allegorical figure, subsequently issued in bronze; the date belongs to the model rather than every later cast. ([catalogue no. 56](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1903–1905:** *La Joueuse de flûte ou La Sirène*. Decorative mythological female figure playing a flute, known under two identities. ([catalogue no. 60](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1905:** *Vertumne et Pomone*. Marble manifestation of the embracing-couple composition begun as *Çacountala*; materially a new realization, but not an entirely independent invention. ([catalogue no. 61](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1905:** *L’Abandon*. Large and small bronze manifestations of the *Çacountala/Vertumne et Pomone* composition, issued under a third title. ([catalogue nos. 62.1–62.2](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))

### Sculptures with disputed attribution

- **c. 1888:** *Tête d’enfant avec socle de granit*. A small relief-like child’s head. Reine-Marie Paris accepts it on the reported provenance from Eugène Blot’s collection, while Anne Rivière, Bruno Gaudichon, and Danielle Ghanassia reject the attribution, in part because relief sculpture is otherwise uncharacteristic of Claudel. ([Paris catalogue no. 24.3 and attribution note—misnumbered 24.8 in that note](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **c. 1893:** *Le Chat*. Animal sculpture accepted by Reine-Marie Paris but rejected by Rivière, Gaudichon, and Ghanassia; Paris reports that they regarded it as a minor work and its signature as insufficiently legible. ([Paris catalogue no. 36 and attribution note](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **Date uncertain:** *L’Écume*. Signed sculptural object accepted by Reine-Marie Paris but rejected by Rivière, Gaudichon, and Ghanassia because its provenance and documentation are lacking; neither date nor attribution should be treated as settled. ([Paris catalogue no. 58 and attribution note](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1900?:** *La Vérité sortant du puits*. Composite sculptural conception related to *La Fortune* and the small *Persée*. Reine-Marie Paris accepts it and proposes a date around 1900 from its reported Eugène Blot provenance, while Rivière, Gaudichon, and Ghanassia reject it as an assemblage; both authorship and date remain disputed. ([Paris catalogue no. 65 and attribution note](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))

### Drawings, paintings, and collages

- **1885–1886:** *Femmes de Gérardmer*. A sequence of three works on paper or painted studies: *Mennie Jean*, an untitled sheet, and *A Quiet Nap*. The online catalogue groups paintings and drawings together, so their precise medium cannot safely be inferred from the list alone. ([catalogue nos. 68.1–68.3](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1886:** *Vieillards de l’île de Wight*. Two works on paper or painted studies titled *A Fisher Woman* and *Old Granny*; they form a related observational sequence rather than a single fixed image. ([catalogue nos. 69.1–69.2](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1886:** *Jessie Lipscomb*. Drawn or painted portrait; the online catalogue does not specify the exact medium. ([catalogue no. 70](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1886:** *Étude de femme nue*. Figure study on paper or in paint; the online catalogue leaves the medium unspecified. ([catalogue no. 71](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1886:** *Doctor Jeans*. Drawn or painted portrait study. ([catalogue no. 72.1](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1886:** *Florence Jeans*. Drawn or painted portrait study. ([catalogue no. 72.2](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))

- **1881:** *Eugénie Plé*. Drawn or painted portrait. ([catalogue no. 74](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1887:** *Louise Claudel*. Drawn or painted portrait of Claudel’s sister. ([catalogue no. 75](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))

- **1888:** *Auguste Rodin*. Drawn or painted portrait. ([catalogue no. 77](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))

- **1888:** *Cacountala*. Two-dimensional study or presentation of the sculptural *Çacountala* project; the variant spelling follows the catalogue. ([catalogue no. 79](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1892:** *Le Réveil*. Work on paper associated in the catalogue with the following experimental sequence. ([catalogue no. 80.1](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1892:** *Le Système cellulaire, premier dessin*. First drawing in a named two-part graphic project. ([catalogue no. 80.2](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1892:** *Le Système cellulaire, deuxième dessin*. Second drawing in the project. ([catalogue no. 80.3](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1892:** *Le Collage*. Collage rather than a conventional drawing or sculpture; it resists the catalogue section’s broad binary of “paintings and drawings.” ([catalogue no. 80.4](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1893:** *La Valse*. Graphic work related to the sculptural project of the same title, not merely another cast or state of the sculpture. ([catalogue no. 81](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **c. 1893–1894:** *Croquis*. Sketch without a more specific fixed title. ([catalogue no. 82](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1894:** *L’Homme de l’île de Wight*. Drawn or painted figure study. ([catalogue no. 83](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))

- **1896:** *Rodin regardant le sujet*. Drawing or painted study of Rodin examining a model or subject. ([catalogue no. 85](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **Undated:** *Jeune femme au divan*. Work on paper or painting of a young woman on a divan; no defensible date is supplied by the catalogue. ([catalogue no. 86](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1903:** *Comtesse Arthur de Maigret*. Drawn or painted portrait distinct in medium from the 1897 sculpture of the same sitter. ([catalogue no. 87](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
- **1905:** *Louis-Prosper Claudel*. Drawn or painted portrait of the artist’s father. ([catalogue no. 88](https://camille-claudel.fr/Liste-des-Oeuvres.html))
