## Works by János Czetz

### Military-language works

- **1844:** *Magyar hadnyelvtan a császári királyi osztriai hadsereg tisztjei számára* (*Hungarian Military Grammar for Officers of the Imperial-Royal Austrian Army*). A hybrid obra combining grammar, German–Hungarian military lexicon, regulations, tactical principles, model exhortations, and practical instruction; first printed in Pest and reissued in 1848. Soma Sztankó later claimed a part in its composition, but modern scholarship says that participation cannot be verified. ([Kréz, pp. 439–40](https://acta.bibl.u-szeged.hu/74513/1/forum_discipulorum_2021_437-468.pdf); [Magyar Könyvszemle bibliography, item 76](https://epa.oszk.hu/00000/00021/00173/pdf/00173.pdf))

- **1846–1848, unpublished or publication unverified:** *German translation of Elek Fényes’s Magyarország statisztikája*. Kréz records that Czetz translated Fényes’s statistical description of Hungary while serving at the Military Geographical Institute, but does not identify a printed edition; it therefore survives in the record as a translation project rather than a securely identifiable publication. ([Kréz, p. 440](https://acta.bibl.u-szeged.hu/74513/1/forum_discipulorum_2021_437-468.pdf))

- **Date unverified:** *Spanish translation or adaptation of a German manual on the tactics of the three arms*. Reported as something Czetz “prepared,” without a securely established title, date, manuscript location, or evidence of publication; it resists treatment as a completed work. ([“Coronel Juan Fernando Czetz”](https://historiaeltriunfo.blogspot.com/2008/08/coronel-juan-f-czetz.html))

### Military histories and memoirs

- **1850:** *Bem’s Feldzug in Siebenbürgen in den Jahren 1848 und 1849*. A German-language military history and participant’s account of Bem’s Transylvanian campaign, published in Hamburg—the first book-length account of those operations. Its Hungarian translation appeared in 1868 as *Bem erdélyi hadjárata 1848–49-ben*; that later title is an edition, not a separate obra. ([Kréz, p. 465](https://acta.bibl.u-szeged.hu/74513/1/forum_discipulorum_2021_437-468.pdf); [Hungarian electronic edition](https://mek.oszk.hu/09600/09664/09664.pdf))

- **1851:** *Der Nationalkrieg in Ungarn und Siebenbürgen in den Jahren 1848 und 1849*. A two-volume history of the Hungarian war through May 1849, made jointly with György Klapka and issued under Klapka’s name. Scholarship and bibliographical records explicitly associate Czetz with its composition, so it is best classified as a collaborative obra whose title-page attribution does not fully disclose its authorship. ([Kréz, p. 465](https://acta.bibl.u-szeged.hu/74513/1/forum_discipulorum_2021_437-468.pdf); [bibliographical record naming Klapka and Czetz](https://kozpontiantikvarium.hu/hu/node/70808))

- **1853:** *Mémoire* concerning the defence of the Ottoman Empire. A jointly prepared political-and-military memorandum by Czetz and Klapka proposing how the Ottoman government might resist a Russian invasion during the crisis preceding the Crimean War. It is a strategic intervention addressed to decision-makers—closer to an operational manifesto or policy project than to a descriptive book—and the consulted scholarship cites it as a manuscript in separate political and military parts. ([“An 1853 Formula for Ottoman Victory”](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/austrian-history-yearbook/article/abs/an-1853-formula-for-ottoman-victory/3B82E6F969B3453F01494CB8552DA82E))

- **Late nineteenth century; exact composition dates unsettled:** *Mis memorias / Emlékezéseim*. Czetz’s Spanish-language autobiographical manuscript, covering his childhood, military career, revolution, exile, and Argentine life; portions appeared in Hungarian periodicals, while a Hungarian book edition titled *Emlékezéseim* was issued only in 2001. The manuscript, serial manifestations, excerpts, translations, and posthumous edition should not be mistaken for several independently made memoirs. ([Kréz, citations to the 2001 edition](https://acta.bibl.u-szeged.hu/74513/1/forum_discipulorum_2021_437-468.pdf); [Amerikai Magyar Lexikon entry](https://olvasodelalfold.sk-szeged.hu/index.php?title=Czetz_J%C3%A1nos); [2001 edition record](https://www.antikvarium.hu/konyv/czetz-janos-emlekezeseim-357493-0))

- **1898:** *Czetz János tábornok életrajza*. An autobiographical series published in the periodical *Arménia*, rather than an independently conceived third life narrative; at least its second instalment, on his officer’s career in 1842–48, is documented. Its exact textual relationship to *Mis memorias* requires manuscript comparison and should not be silently assumed. ([Kréz, p. 440 and note 7](https://acta.bibl.u-szeged.hu/74513/1/forum_discipulorum_2021_437-468.pdf))

### Military-geographical manuals

- **1885:** *Ensayos de geografía militar de la República Argentina*. A military-geographical textbook intended for the Colegio Militar and the school for corporals and sergeants: an instructional obra joining territorial description to operational military knowledge. ([“Coronel Juan Fernando Czetz”](https://historiaeltriunfo.blogspot.com/2008/08/coronel-juan-f-czetz.html))

- **Date unverified:** *Tratado de fortificación permanente y pasajera*. A technical treatise on permanent and field fortification. The title and Czetz’s authorship are repeatedly reported, but the consulted sources do not establish a reliable publication date or distinguish decisively between printed book, teaching text, and manuscript; those uncertainties belong to the work’s status. ([Juan Czetz biography](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Czetz); [“Coronel Juan Fernando Czetz”](https://historiaeltriunfo.blogspot.com/2008/08/coronel-juan-f-czetz.html))

### Maps, surveys, and territorial designs

- **Before 1865; precise date and title unverified:** *Map of the boundaries of the Argentine Republic with Brazil and Paraguay*. An official boundary-cartography project undertaken while Czetz headed the engineers’ section of the Inspectorate of Public Works. The surviving descriptions name the object generically rather than supplying a stable map title, so a title should not be invented. ([I Simposio Iberoamericano de Historia de la Cartografía](https://razoncartografica.com/wp-content/uploads/2007/08/i-simposio-iberoamericano-de-historia-de-la-cartografc3ada-carla-lois-y-otros.pdf))

- **1866:** *Plan and delineation of Rojas*. An urban-survey and town-planning obra, not a literary text; the available account credits Czetz with delineating the settlement but does not provide a fixed documentary title. ([Juan Czetz biography](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Czetz))

- **1866:** *Railway project from Santa Fe to Colonia Esperanza*. An infrastructure project or proposed route rather than a securely identified published plan. Its documentary components and degree of execution are not specified in the consulted sources. ([Juan Czetz biography](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Czetz))

- **1866:** *Road project along the Río Dulce between Santa Fe and Santiago del Estero*. A route-design and surveying project whose intention is documented, although its surviving plans, completion, and official title are not established by the consulted sources. ([Juan Czetz biography](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Czetz))

- **1868:** *Duplicado de mensura del ejido y traza del pueblo Olavarría*. A cadastral survey and urban-layout plan made in Czetz’s professional capacity as surveyor; its archival identification is Olavarría, duplicate survey no. 1. ([Academia Nacional de la Historia bulletin](https://repositorio.anh.org.ar/bitstream/anh/92/1/BaANH48658_Bolet%C3%ADn_de_la_Academia_Nacional_de_la_Historia_XXXIV_%281%29_1963.pdf))

- **1869:** *Plano general de la frontera de la República al norte y este del territorio de la Pampa*. A military-frontier map made jointly with G. Hoffmeister. Because it embodies survey, strategic intelligence, and the state’s projected frontier, it is simultaneously a cartographic obra and an operational instrument. ([Digital Image Archive, University of Pennsylvania](https://dia.upenn.edu/es/maps/ARG0046/))

- **Late 1860s:** *Plans for a new frontier line and associated forts*. A distributed territorial work made with Colonel Antonio Benavidez: surveys, a projected line, command posts, and forts rather than one self-contained object. Individual fort attributions are not sufficiently catalogued in the consulted scholarship to justify converting the ensemble into a fabricated list of separately titled works. ([Piedritas historical account](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piedritas); [Kréz on Czetz’s surveying and fortification work](https://acta.bibl.u-szeged.hu/74513/1/forum_discipulorum_2021_437-468.pdf))

- **1875–1883:** *Cadastral and topographical survey of Entre Ríos*. A province-wide body of official plans produced under Czetz’s direction as head of the provincial topographical department. This is best treated as a collective cartographic obra: the evidence supports the programme and corpus, but not personal authorship of every sheet made by the office. ([Juan Czetz biography](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Czetz); [Kréz, p. 466](https://acta.bibl.u-szeged.hu/74513/1/forum_discipulorum_2021_437-468.pdf))

- **1893:** *Project for the reconnaissance and topographical survey of the Cuyo and Neuquén Andes*. A designed programme for surveying routes and unresolved internal and international boundaries. Czetz made the project, but the later fieldwork was carried out by others; consequently it is an authored project rather than a completed personal survey of the Andes. ([“Juan Czetz: Historias de dos patrias”](https://www.colegiomilitar.mil.ar/rediu/pdf/ReDiU_2052_art03-Juan%20Czetz.pdf); [Hungarian military lexicon](https://real.mtak.hu/153916/1/790_hadtudomanyi_lexikon_2019.pdf))

### Institutions, curricula, and organizational obras

- **1865:** *Argentina’s first military engineering unit*. Czetz is credited with organizing this formation. It is an institutional obra—personnel, technical practice, and command structure—rather than a text, and its collective life cannot be reduced to the founder’s authorship alone. ([Kréz, pp. 465–66](https://acta.bibl.u-szeged.hu/74513/1/forum_discipulorum_2021_437-468.pdf))

- **1870–1874:** *Organization and curriculum of the Colegio Militar de la Nación*. Czetz did not single-handedly create the state institution, which had been established by presidential authority, but as its first director he organized its teaching, discipline, and technical formation. It is therefore a collaborative institutional obra and educational protocol, not simply a building or a book. ([Kréz, pp. 465–66](https://acta.bibl.u-szeged.hu/74513/1/forum_discipulorum_2021_437-468.pdf); [Juan Czetz biography](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Czetz))

- **1885:** *Project for an Escuela de Ingenieros Militares*. A legislative and curricular project submitted by Czetz to the chief of the General Staff and implemented by decree. The resulting school exceeds his individual authorship, but the proposal itself operated as a protocol: it brought a new system of military-engineer education into being. ([I Simposio Iberoamericano de Historia de la Cartografía](https://razoncartografica.com/wp-content/uploads/2007/08/i-simposio-iberoamericano-de-historia-de-la-cartografc3ada-carla-lois-y-otros.pdf))

- **1884–1896:** *IV Section of the General Staff: Military Engineers, Topography and Cartography*. Under Czetz’s leadership, this was an organizational and technical body producing surveys, maps, communications plans, and military works. It resists being called a personal “work”: it was a collective state apparatus that he organized and directed. ([I Simposio Iberoamericano de Historia de la Cartografía](https://razoncartografica.com/wp-content/uploads/2007/08/i-simposio-iberoamericano-de-historia-de-la-cartografc3ada-carla-lois-y-otros.pdf); [Kréz, p. 466](https://acta.bibl.u-szeged.hu/74513/1/forum_discipulorum_2021_437-468.pdf))
