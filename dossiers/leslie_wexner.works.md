## Works by Leslie Herbert Wexner

### Retail obras and brand projects

- **1963–2021:** *The Limited / The Limited, Inc. / Limited Brands / L Brands*. Wexner founded the enterprise as a single women’s-apparel store and directed its transformation into a publicly traded, multibrand retail system; the changing names designate successive corporate manifestations rather than separate creations. The company became Bath & Body Works, Inc. after spinning off Victoria’s Secret in 2021. ([Bath & Body Works annual report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/701985/000119312523105113/d408589dars.pdf))

- **1980–:** *Express*. A fashion-retail concept launched inside The Limited and later made independent; it is a corporate creation of Wexner’s organization, not securely attributable to him as a solitary designer. ([The Limited 1999 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/701985/000102140800001562/0001021408-00-001562-d1.html))

- **1987–1989:** *The Limited men’s line / Structure*. A men’s-apparel project launched in 1987 and renamed *Structure* in 1989; its two names mark stages of one developing retail concept rather than two unequivocally separate obras. ([Express Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1483510/000119312511073120/d10k.htm))

- **1987–:** *Limited Too*. A girls’ apparel concept first established as departments or adjoining stores within The Limited. It later became a separate company and eventually traded under other names, so the durable made thing is the retail format and brand, not any one corporate shell. ([Too, Inc. Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1085482/000095015205003103/l13173ae10vk.htm))

- **1990–:** *Bath & Body Works*. A personal-care and home-fragrance retail concept launched by The Limited under Wexner’s leadership; surviving filings attribute it to the company collectively, so individual authorship should not be overstated. ([The Limited 1999 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/701985/000102140800001562/0001021408-00-001562-d1.html))

- **By 1999–:** *White Barn Candle Company*. A home-fragrance brand and store-within-a-store environment developed as part of Bath & Body Works. Contemporary filings group White Barn with Bath & Body Works, making its precise moment and individual authorship less definite than later corporate histories sometimes suggest. ([The Limited 1999 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/701985/000102140800001562/0001021408-00-001562-d1.html); [Bath & Body Works annual report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/701985/000119312523105113/d408589dars.pdf))

- **2002–:** *PINK*. A Victoria’s Secret sub-brand conceived for younger customers and incubated within Limited Brands. It belongs to the corporate body of work made under Wexner’s chairmanship, but available filings do not justify naming him as its sole creator. ([Victoria’s Secret corporate history](https://www.victoriassecretandco.com/our-company/company-history/); [Victoria’s Secret & Co. Form 10-K](https://victoriassecret.gcs-web.com/sec-filings/sec-filing/10-k/0001856437-26-000004))

### Acquisitions remade as projects

- **1982 onward:** *Victoria’s Secret under The Limited*. Wexner did not create Victoria’s Secret: Roy and Gaye Raymond founded it in 1977, and The Limited acquired it in 1982. What Wexner made was a prolonged project of repositioning and expansion—retail stores, catalogue, beauty business, imagery and eventually PINK—whose authorship was necessarily collective. ([Harvard Business School profile](https://www.hbs.edu/leadership/20th-century-leaders/details?profile=leslie-wexner); [Victoria’s Secret company history](https://www.victoriassecretandco.com/our-company/company-history/))

- **1982–1988:** *Lane Bryant under The Limited*. An acquired chain rather than a Wexner creation: Lane Bryant originated in 1900 and entered The Limited portfolio in 1982. Its inclusion in his retail system is therefore an act of acquisition and management, not original authorship. ([The Limited 1999 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/701985/000102140800001562/0001021408-00-001562-d1.html))

- **1985 onward:** *Henri Bendel under The Limited*. Another acquired historical retailer, subsequently reformatted and expanded by Wexner’s company. The remaking may be treated as a corporate project, but *Henri Bendel* itself was not something Wexner originally made. ([Harvard Business School profile](https://www.hbs.edu/leadership/20th-century-leaders/details?profile=leslie-wexner))

- **1985 onward:** *Lerner Shops / Lerner New York under The Limited*. An existing chain acquired and incorporated into Wexner’s portfolio, later becoming New York & Company. This resists classification as his work except in the limited sense of a corporate restructuring and repositioning project. ([Harvard Business School profile](https://www.hbs.edu/leadership/20th-century-leaders/details?profile=leslie-wexner))

- **1988–1998:** *Abercrombie & Fitch under The Limited*. The Limited acquired an already old sporting-goods name and remade it as a youth-fashion business before spinning it off. Wexner is thus a principal patron and corporate author of the reinvention, not the founder of the original Abercrombie & Fitch. ([Abercrombie & Fitch corporate history](https://corporate.abercrombie.com/about-us/); [Harvard Business School profile](https://www.hbs.edu/leadership/20th-century-leaders/details?profile=leslie-wexner))

### Institutional projects and protocols

- **1983–:** *The Wexner Foundation*. A leadership-education institution created from Wexner’s ideas and capital and co-founded, according to the foundation’s own history, with Rabbi Maurice Corson. It is better understood as an enduring project and protocol for producing communal leaders than as a conventional charitable grant-making foundation. ([The Wexner Foundation, “Our Story”](https://www.wexnerfoundation.org/our-story/); [“Our Structure and Funding”](https://www.wexnerfoundation.org/our-structure/))

- **1985–:** *The Wexner Heritage Foundation and Wexner Heritage Program*. An institution and adult-learning protocol for North American Jewish volunteer leaders, developed with Rabbi Herbert A. Friedman. Foundation materials variously fold its history into the 1983 Wexner Foundation and distinguish a separate 1985 organization; that institutional ambiguity should be retained rather than silently harmonized. ([Independent-review report](https://www.wexnerfoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/Wexner-Foundation-Report-Following-Independent-Review4.pdf); [Wexner Heritage Program](https://www.wexnerfoundation.org/programs/wexner-heritage-program/))

- **1980s–:** *Wexner Graduate Fellowship*. A funded educational and professional-development protocol for emerging North American Jewish communal professionals. It is an institutional creation sustained by faculty, staff, fellows and later partners, not a text authored by Wexner alone. ([Wexner Foundation program overview](https://www.wexnerfoundation.org/adding-to-your-wexner-toolkit/))

- **1980s–:** *Wexner Israel Fellowship*. A leadership-development protocol for Israeli public officials, subsequently associated with Harvard’s Kennedy School. The foundation identifies it as one of its three long-running core programs but does not supply a sufficiently unambiguous public founding date to justify a more exact date here. ([Wexner Foundation program overview](https://www.wexnerfoundation.org/adding-to-your-wexner-toolkit/))

### Urban project and Gesamtkunstwerk

- **1987–:** *The New Albany Company*. A real-estate development company co-created by Wexner and Jack Kessler to direct the transformation of New Albany, Ohio. It is both an organization and the mechanism through which the larger environmental project was made. ([New Albany Company history](https://newalbanycompany.com/about-us/); [founders](https://newalbanycompany.com/about-us/founders/))

- **Late 1980s–:** *The remaking of New Albany, Ohio*. An unfinished, continuously developing urban Gesamtkunstwerk comprising land planning, Georgian-Palladian architectural controls, landscape, fences, signs, roads, civic spaces, housing and commercial development. Wexner and Kessler articulated the intention, but architects, planners, governments, businesses and residents made its manifestations; calling the city Wexner’s individual “work” would erase that distributed authorship. ([New Albany Company, “The Vision”](https://newalbanycompany.com/about-us/the-vision/); [New Albany Realty history](https://newalbanyrealty.com/the-new-albany-experience/history/))

### Collections and commissioned manifestations

- **Dates not fixed:** *The Wexner Family Collection*. A private collection assembled by Leslie and Abigail Wexner, especially strong in modern European art. A collection is a made constellation of other people’s obras rather than an original artwork by its owners; its most public manifestation was the 2014 exhibition *Transfigurations: Modern Masters from the Wexner Family Collection*. ([Wexner Center exhibition and catalogue record](https://wexarts.org/transfigurations))

- **1983–1989:** *Wexner Center for the Arts*. Wexner’s gift materially enabled the center, but the building was an Ohio State University project designed by Peter Eisenman with Richard Trott and landscape architect Laurie Olin. It therefore resists inclusion as a Wexner work: he was patron and namesake—the name honors his father—not architect or sole institutional author. ([Wexner Center architectural history](https://wexarts.org/architecture); [Cambridge architectural history excerpt](https://assets.cambridge.org/97805214/44491/excerpt/9780521444491_excerpt.pdf))
