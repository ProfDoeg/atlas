## Works by Christiane Wilhelmine Sophie von Kühn

### Calendar diary

- **January 1795:** *Calendar diary*. A sequence of brief daily entries written in a printed calendar rather than a retrospectively composed literary diary. Only extracts are securely documented in the consulted sources, so its original extent and completeness cannot be established here; the text resists treatment as a formally titled “work.” ([Blei, *Novalis*, chap. 4](https://projekt-gutenberg.org/authors/franz-blei/books/novalis/chapter/4/))

### Letters and contributions to letters

- **December 1794?:** *Letter to Friedrich von Hardenberg (Novalis)*. An untitled manuscript letter whose date is supplied conjecturally by the archival catalogue; it should be understood as an act of correspondence, not a literary work. ([Kalliope, Novalis virtual collection](https://kalliope-verbund.info/de/findingaid?fa.id=ead_DE-F25_37_VirtuellerBestand&htmlFull=false&lang=de))

- **26 March 1795:** *Letter to Friedrich von Hardenberg (Novalis)*. An untitled manuscript letter with a postscript, catalogued under the Hardenberg papers; the surviving object is private correspondence rather than a publication. ([Kalliope, manuscript record HS-535804](https://kalliope-verbund.info/de/findingaid?fa.id=ead_DE-F25_37_VirtuellerBestand&htmlFull=false&lang=de))

- **20 October 1795:** *Letter to Friedrich von Hardenberg (Novalis)*. An untitled manuscript letter catalogued with this date in Kalliope. The catalogue date should not be confused with Kühn’s distinct letter from Jena dated 20 October 1796. ([Kalliope, Novalis virtual collection](https://kalliope-verbund.info/de/findingaid?fa.id=ead_DE-F25_37_VirtuellerBestand&htmlFull=false&lang=de))

- **17–19 November 1795:** *Letter to Friedrich von Hardenberg (Novalis)*. A short untitled letter dated to a range by the editors rather than by a complete authorial date; the manuscript is held by the Freies Deutsches Hochstift as FDH 11939 and is edited in the historical-critical Novalis edition, IV, p. 408. ([Korrespondenzen der Frühromantik, no. 18768](https://briefe-der-romantik.de/letters/view/18768?left=druck))

- **12 February 1796:** *Letter to Friedrich von Hardenberg (Novalis)*. An untitled manuscript letter with a postscript, preserved in the Hardenberg papers. Its work-like identity is archival and editorial: Kühn herself did not give it a title or prepare it for publication. ([Kalliope, manuscript record HS-535802](https://kalliope-verbund.info/de/findingaid?fa.id=ead_DE-F25_37_VirtuellerBestand&htmlFull=false&lang=de))

- **21 March 1796:** *Letter to Friedrich von Hardenberg (Novalis)*. An untitled manuscript letter with a postscript, catalogued as a discrete correspondence object. ([Kalliope, manuscript record HS-535805](https://kalliope-verbund.info/de/findingaid?fa.id=ead_DE-F25_37_VirtuellerBestand&htmlFull=false&lang=de))

- **30 July 1796:** *Postscript to Friederike von Mandelsloh’s letter to Friedrich von Hardenberg*. Kühn contributed only a very brief signed intervention to Mandelsloh’s longer letter; it is therefore a jointly made correspondence object, and treating Kühn’s contribution as an independent “work” would distort its material form. The manuscript is FDH 11924; the historical-critical edition prints it in IV, pp. 446–447. ([Korrespondenzen der Frühromantik, no. 18802](https://briefe-der-romantik.de/letters/view/18802?left=druck))

- **16 September 1796:** *Letter to Friedrich von Hardenberg (Novalis)*. An untitled manuscript letter with a postscript, sent from Jena and preserved in the Hardenberg papers. ([Kalliope, manuscript record HS-535806](https://kalliope-verbund.info/de/findingaid?fa.id=ead_DE-F25_37_VirtuellerBestand&htmlFull=false&lang=de))

- **18 October 1796:** *Letter to Friedrich von Hardenberg, with a continuation by Friederike von Mandelsloh*. Kühn wrote the principal letter and Mandelsloh added her own message, making the surviving manuscript a collaborative correspondence object rather than two wholly separable works. It is FDH 11941 and is edited in the historical-critical edition, IV, pp. 457–458. ([Korrespondenzen der Frühromantik, no. 18817](https://briefe-der-romantik.de/letters/view/18817?left=druck))

- **20 October 1796:** *Letter to Friedrich von Hardenberg (Novalis)*. An untitled letter sent from Jena, preserved as FDH 11942 and edited in the historical-critical edition, IV, p. 458. Its date is authorial and explicit, unlike several editorially dated letters in the corpus. ([Korrespondenzen der Frühromantik, no. 18818](https://briefe-der-romantik.de/letters/view/18818?left=druck))

- **11 or 12 December 1796:** *Postscript to Karoline von Kühn’s letter to Friedrich von Hardenberg*. Kühn added a short signed message to her sister’s letter while ill; the editors cannot decide between the two possible dates. The composite manuscript is FDH 11948 and is printed in the historical-critical edition, IV, pp. 463–464. ([Korrespondenzen der Frühromantik, no. 18825](https://briefe-der-romantik.de/letters/view/18825?left=druck))

- **16 December 1796:** *Letter to Friedrich von Hardenberg (Novalis)*. An untitled letter discussing, among other matters, commissioned miniature portraits of Kühn; the portraits were made by an unnamed painter and therefore are not works by Kühn. The letter’s existence and date are documented through the commentary to the historical-critical Novalis edition. ([Stockinger, “Sophia sey mein Schuz Geist”](https://www.db-thueringen.de/servlets/MCRFileNodeServlet/dbt_derivate_00061855/Stockinger_Sophia%20sey%20mein%20Schuz%20Geist.pdf))

### Domestic making documented as process

- **1796, date not fixed:** *Waistcoat for Friedrich von Hardenberg*. Kühn is documented knitting a waistcoat while other members of the household composed a letter. This is a textile-making project rather than a text; the evidence establishes the activity but not that the garment was completed or survives, so it resists entry as a securely extant obra. ([*Literatur für Leser:innen* 2/2022, discussion and note 32](https://blog.studiumdigitale.uni-frankfurt.de/literatur-fuer-leser-innen/files/2023/12/LFL020221_Gesamt.pdf))
