## Works by Max Perelman

### Compositions: songs securely credited to him

- *Parnuse un gezint* (*A Living and Health*). A Yiddish comic-moral song credited solely to Perlman in Silvia Glocer’s scholarly biographical dictionary; its date is not established. A Makolit recording preserves Perlman’s own performance. [Glocer, pp. 156–57](https://publicaciones.filo.uba.ar/sites/publicaciones.filo.uba.ar/files/Diccionario%20tomo%20II_interactivo.pdf)

- *Mir gefelt es zeyer* (*I Like It Very Much*). A song with words by Abraham Levin and music credited to Perlman; the surviving RCA Victor recording features Perlman with Simón Tenovsky’s orchestra. No composition or recording date is supplied. [Glocer, p. 156](https://publicaciones.filo.uba.ar/sites/publicaciones.filo.uba.ar/files/Diccionario%20tomo%20II_interactivo.pdf)

### Songs treated as Perlman’s obra, but without complete surviving credits

- *Madagaskar*. An undated Yiddish satirical song about the proposal to establish a Jewish settlement in Madagascar. Fiszman and Skura place it within Perlman’s repertory, but the consulted source does not reproduce a label credit establishing whether he wrote both words and music. [Fiszman and Skura, pp. 2–3](https://periodicos.ufmg.br/index.php/maaravi/article/download/21724/20437)

- *Benzin* (*Gasoline*). A comic song composed in Argentina about automobiles, gambling, money, and sexual bargaining; Belling calls it Perlman’s song, while the surviving sources consulted here do not separate lyric and music credits. [Belling](https://web.uwm.edu/yiddish-stage/siz-night-dos-vos-amok-is-geven-max-perlman-1909-1985); [Fiszman and Skura, pp. 2, 6](https://periodicos.ufmg.br/index.php/maaravi/article/download/21724/20437)

- *Poker*. An undated Yiddish–Spanish comic song about poker displacing religious and social obligations. Fiszman and Skura treat it as part of Perlman’s production, but note that his œuvre has never received a complete scholarly survey. [Fiszman and Skura, pp. 6–8](https://periodicos.ufmg.br/index.php/maaravi/article/download/21724/20437)

- *A pokerl* (*A Little Poker Game*). An undated comic song about a private four-person card game. It resists secure attribution: Fiszman and Skura classify it among “songs of Max Perlman,” but also document its appearance on a collection devoted to Jevel Katz, sung by Perlman; performer, repertory owner, and author may therefore have been conflated. [Fiszman and Skura, pp. 3, 6, 14–18](https://periodicos.ufmg.br/index.php/maaravi/article/download/21724/20437)

- *Rulete* (*Roulette*). An undated Yiddish–Spanish satire of casino gambling and social ascent. The scholarly study treats it as Perlman’s production but supplies no separate composer-and-lyricist credit. [Fiszman and Skura, pp. 6–7, 25–27](https://periodicos.ufmg.br/index.php/maaravi/article/download/21724/20437)

- *Kh’vil nor a bisele* (*I Only Want a Little Bit*). A moralizing song about health, love, modest prosperity, and Israel. Belling discusses it as Perlman’s song, but Glocer’s discography credits the recorded work to Abraham Levin and Henry Gerro; Perlman’s authorship is therefore doubtful, and this may be an interpretive creation rather than a composition. [Belling](https://web.uwm.edu/yiddish-stage/siz-night-dos-vos-amok-is-geven-max-perlman-1909-1985); [Glocer, p. 156](https://publicaciones.filo.uba.ar/sites/publicaciones.filo.uba.ar/files/Diccionario%20tomo%20II_interactivo.pdf)

- *Vos mer men hot* (*The More One Has*). An undated comic song about appetite, children, and repeated marriage, discussed by Belling as part of Perlman’s oeuvre; the consulted source gives no formal authorship credits. [Belling](https://web.uwm.edu/yiddish-stage/siz-night-dos-vos-amok-is-geven-max-perlman-1909-1985)

- *Ahavah* (*Love*). An undated multilingual love song playing with Hebrew vocabulary inside a Yiddish performance. Its lyric and music authorship remain unstated in the consulted scholarly account. [Belling](https://web.uwm.edu/yiddish-stage/siz-night-dos-vos-amok-is-geven-max-perlman-1909-1985)

- *Kh’hob lib a meydele fun Tel-Aviv* / *A meydele fun Tel-Aviv* (*I Love a Girl from Tel Aviv*). A comic love song about an Israeli woman. Glocer’s discography credits Max Perlman and Jacobo Sclar jointly, without specifying their respective lyric and music roles. [Glocer, p. 156](https://publicaciones.filo.uba.ar/sites/publicaciones.filo.uba.ar/files/Diccionario%20tomo%20II_interactivo.pdf); [Belling](https://web.uwm.edu/yiddish-stage/siz-night-dos-vos-amok-is-geven-max-perlman-1909-1985)

- *Mayn meydele, mayn* (*My Girl, My Girl*). An undated Yiddish love song incorporating a Hebrew verse. Belling includes it in Perlman’s repertory but provides no discrete authorship credit. [Belling](https://web.uwm.edu/yiddish-stage/siz-night-dos-vos-amok-is-geven-max-perlman-1909-1985)

- **After 1948:** *S’iz nisht dos vos amol iz geven* (*It Is Not What It Used to Be*). A long comic-social song about transformations in courtship, dress, family life, and Jewish political agency after Israel’s establishment. Belling calls it Perlman’s composition, but no more exact date is established. [Belling](https://web.uwm.edu/yiddish-stage/siz-night-dos-vos-amok-is-geven-max-perlman-1909-1985)

- *Es toyg shoyn nisht mer* (*I Can’t Take It Any Longer*). An undated comic song about traffic frustration, identified by Belling as part of Perlman’s mundane urban repertoire; separate authorship credits are unavailable. [Belling](https://web.uwm.edu/yiddish-stage/siz-night-dos-vos-amok-is-geven-max-perlman-1909-1985)

- *Misht zikh nisht arayn* (*Don’t Get Involved*). An undated song of comic advice. It is named in the scholarly career study but lacks a surviving date and explicit lyric/music division. [Belling](https://web.uwm.edu/yiddish-stage/siz-night-dos-vos-amok-is-geven-max-perlman-1909-1985)

- *S’iz gut far mir* (*It’s Good for Me*). An undated comic food song about herring, onion, borscht, potato, and sour cream. Its status is repertorial rather than securely bibliographical because the source does not state the component authorship. [Belling](https://web.uwm.edu/yiddish-stage/siz-night-dos-vos-amok-is-geven-max-perlman-1909-1985)

- *Ven du lakhst* (*When You Smile*). An undated song whose relationship to the 1929 American standard “When You’re Smiling” is described as an echo rather than documented as a licensed adaptation. One archive credits Perlman as composer, but the degree of musical derivation remains unresolved. [Save the Music Archives](https://savethemusic.com/artist/max-perlman-2/); [Belling](https://web.uwm.edu/yiddish-stage/siz-night-dos-vos-amok-is-geven-max-perlman-1909-1985)

- *Zol es geyn vi es geyt*. An undated song reminiscent of “Que Sera, Sera”; Belling does not establish whether it is an adaptation, parody, or independently composed analogue, so its status as an autonomous obra remains uncertain. [Belling](https://web.uwm.edu/yiddish-stage/siz-night-dos-vos-amok-is-geven-max-perlman-1909-1985)

### Recorded manifestations

- *A meydele fun Tel-Aviv*. A Makolit 78-rpm recording, catalogue number 12028-2, preserving the jointly credited Perlman–Jacobo Sclar song; no recording date is supplied. [Glocer, p. 156](https://publicaciones.filo.uba.ar/sites/publicaciones.filo.uba.ar/files/Diccionario%20tomo%20II_interactivo.pdf)

- *Ikh vil nor a bisale*. A Makolit LP 198 performance by Perlman of a song credited to Abraham Levin and Henry Gerro; it is his recorded manifestation, not securely his composition. [Glocer, p. 156](https://publicaciones.filo.uba.ar/sites/publicaciones.filo.uba.ar/files/Diccionario%20tomo%20II_interactivo.pdf)

- *Parnuse un gezint*. Perlman’s Makolit LP 198 recording of his own credited composition; its recording date is not given. [Glocer, p. 156](https://publicaciones.filo.uba.ar/sites/publicaciones.filo.uba.ar/files/Diccionario%20tomo%20II_interactivo.pdf)

- *Mir gefelt es zeyer*. An RCA Victor P-1347-A recording by Perlman and Simón Tenovsky’s orchestra of the Levin–Perlman song. [Glocer, p. 156](https://publicaciones.filo.uba.ar/sites/publicaciones.filo.uba.ar/files/Diccionario%20tomo%20II_interactivo.pdf)

- *Chiu-chiu: Pajarito*. An undated RCA Victor Argentina performance by Perlman with Simón Tenovsky’s orchestra; Glocer records no claim that Perlman composed it. [Glocer, pp. 156–57](https://publicaciones.filo.uba.ar/sites/publicaciones.filo.uba.ar/files/Diccionario%20tomo%20II_interactivo.pdf)

- *A yidishe meluye* / *A meydele fun Tel-Aviv*. A two-sided Longdisc record, R.L. 1228, performed by Perlman with Simón Tenovsky’s orchestra; the first title is documented here only as his performance. [Glocer, p. 157](https://publicaciones.filo.uba.ar/sites/publicaciones.filo.uba.ar/files/Diccionario%20tomo%20II_interactivo.pdf)

- **1971:** *Max Perlman at the Yiddish Theatre, Volume One*. A twelve-inch commercial LP containing ten sung performances. The record is a manifestation of Perlman’s stage repertory, not proof that he composed every included song. [National Library of Israel](https://www.nli.org.il/en/systemlibrary/umbracoitempage?docId=NNL_MUSIC_AL997012399428605171&lang=en&type=items&vid=NLI)

- **1971:** *The Art of Max Perlman*. A commercial recording documented under this title and year in the Barry Reisman collection; it appears to be an album manifestation rather than a separately authored textual work. [University of Pennsylvania finding aid](https://findingaids.library.upenn.edu/records/UPENN_RBML_PUSP.PRINTCOLL.90)

- *Max Perlman: Greatest Hits, Volumes 1 and 2*. Posthumously circulating compilations used by Belling as a repertory source. They resist treatment as works created by Perlman: their sequencing and compilation are later editorial creations, although they preserve his performances. [Belling](https://web.uwm.edu/yiddish-stage/siz-night-dos-vos-amok-is-geven-max-perlman-1909-1985)

### Stage creations and roles

- **9 May 1942:** *Max Perlman and Gita Galina*. A named theatrical-musical appearance at Buenos Aires’s Teatro Excelsior with Leo Schwarz conducting; the programme title does not identify a fixed dramatic text, so it is best understood as a performance event rather than a conventional authored work. [Glocer, p. 157](https://publicaciones.filo.uba.ar/sites/publicaciones.filo.uba.ar/files/Diccionario%20tomo%20II_interactivo.pdf)

- **1948:** *First South African musical revue*. An otherwise untitled revue starring Perlman that ran eight weeks at His Majesty’s Theatre, Johannesburg. Its missing fixed title and collaborative revue form make it resistant to treatment as a single stable work. [Belling](https://web.uwm.edu/yiddish-stage/siz-night-dos-vos-amok-is-geven-max-perlman-1909-1985)

- **1949–1955:** *Here Comes Grandpa*. A Yiddish stage vehicle performed by Perlman and Gita Galina at London’s Grand Palais during their postwar touring seasons; the source does not establish which of their 1949, 1951, or 1955 engagements contained it. [Belling](https://web.uwm.edu/yiddish-stage/siz-night-dos-vos-amok-is-geven-max-perlman-1909-1985)

- **1949–1955:** *The Shlemmazl*. A London comic stage vehicle built around Perlman’s acting, singing, and dancing. It is a collaborative performance creation rather than a text attributed to him as author. [Belling](https://web.uwm.edu/yiddish-stage/siz-night-dos-vos-amok-is-geven-max-perlman-1909-1985)

- **26 August 1955:** *Der Amerikanische Littwak* (*The American Litvak*). A musical staged at Buenos Aires’s Teatro Soleil, directed by Hermann Ludwig and performed by Perlman and Gitta Galina; Perlman’s contribution was performance, not documented textual authorship. [Glocer, p. 157](https://publicaciones.filo.uba.ar/sites/publicaciones.filo.uba.ar/files/Diccionario%20tomo%20II_interactivo.pdf)

- **1962:** *Honeymoon in Israel*. Perlman’s New York debut production at the Roosevelt Theater, a Yiddish comedy by Meyer Ben Yehuda. His obra here is the live comic and musical performance, not the playtext. [Belling](https://web.uwm.edu/yiddish-stage/siz-night-dos-vos-amok-is-geven-max-perlman-1909-1985)

- **1963:** *Don’t Worry, Brother*. A Meyer Ben Yehuda musical starring Perlman at the Roosevelt Theater. Contemporary accounts emphasize his hat-based physical comedy and rapport with the audience; authorship of the musical itself belongs to others. [Belling](https://web.uwm.edu/yiddish-stage/siz-night-dos-vos-amok-is-geven-max-perlman-1909-1985)

- **1964:** *Good Luck!*. An American Yiddish musical by Sholem Secunda at the Anderson Yiddish Theater, with Perlman playing Zisel. Its mixture of Yiddish and English makes the production—not merely its written script—the relevant manifestation. [Glocer, p. 157](https://publicaciones.filo.uba.ar/sites/publicaciones.filo.uba.ar/files/Diccionario%20tomo%20II_interactivo.pdf); [Belling](https://web.uwm.edu/yiddish-stage/siz-night-dos-vos-amok-is-geven-max-perlman-1909-1985)

- **1968:** *Never Too Late for Happiness*. A bilingual Yiddish-English musical at the Actors’ Playhouse in New York, led by Perlman in the role of an immigrant son learning to live in America. This was an acting creation within a collaborative production. [Belling](https://web.uwm.edu/yiddish-stage/siz-night-dos-vos-amok-is-geven-max-perlman-1909-1985)

- **1969:** *Oh What a Wedding!*. An Israeli musical comedy by G. Yisraelev, with music by Murray Rumshinsky and lyrics by Jacob Jacobs, staged at the Anderson Theatre. Perlman created the performed role of the schlemiel opposite Menora Zahav but did not author the underlying work. [Belling](https://web.uwm.edu/yiddish-stage/siz-night-dos-vos-amok-is-geven-max-perlman-1909-1985)

- **October 1981:** *Dem shmids tekhter* (*The Blacksmith’s Daughters*). Ben Bonus and Mina Bern’s centenary production of Peretz Hirshbein’s play at the Norman Thomas Theater; Perlman played the grandfather. The performance is his creation, while the play remains Hirshbein’s. [Belling](https://web.uwm.edu/yiddish-stage/siz-night-dos-vos-amok-is-geven-max-perlman-1909-1985)

- **1984:** *Oy Mama! Am I in Love*. Perlman’s last documented New York production, presented by the Shalom Yiddish Musical Comedy Theatre with Mary Soreano. Projected English translations made the staging a mixed-media manifestation rather than simply a performance of a fixed Yiddish text. [Belling](https://web.uwm.edu/yiddish-stage/siz-night-dos-vos-amok-is-geven-max-perlman-1909-1985)

- **1985:** *Final United States–Australia–Israel tour with Menora Zahav*. A late-career touring performance project rather than a single stable work; neither a complete itinerary nor a fixed programme title is preserved in the consulted sources. [Glocer, p. 156](https://publicaciones.filo.uba.ar/sites/publicaciones.filo.uba.ar/files/Diccionario%20tomo%20II_interactivo.pdf); [Belling](https://web.uwm.edu/yiddish-stage/siz-night-dos-vos-amok-is-geven-max-perlman-1909-1985)

### Film performance

- **1938:** *Mamele*. A Polish Yiddish musical film directed by Joseph Green and Konrad Tom, in which Perlman—credited as Maks Perelman—created the screen role of Dawid Samet. This is a performed contribution to a collaborative film, not a film authored by him. [Museum of the Yiddish Theatre](https://www.museumoffamilyhistory.com/moyt/film/mamele.htm); [IMDb cast record](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0030407/fullcredits/)

### Unresolved body of work

- **Principally 1939–1985:** *Unsurveyed song repertory*. Fiszman and Skura located approximately thirty songs associated with Perlman in the Fundación IWO and Jewish Museum of Buenos Aires, but technical limitations allowed them to examine only ten; they explicitly state that his work still lacks a comprehensive survey. This resists conversion into thirty invented entries: titles, dates, and component authorship cannot responsibly be supplied until the archival corpus is catalogued. [Fiszman and Skura, pp. 3, 6](https://periodicos.ufmg.br/index.php/maaravi/article/download/21724/20437)

- **1928–1985:** *Itinerant performance oeuvre*. Perlman performed professionally across Eastern and Western Europe, South America, South Africa, Israel, the United States, and Australia, often in revues and short-lived touring companies whose programmes are incompletely preserved. This body of acting, dancing, comedy, and audience-participation performance is an obra without a complete work-list; the surviving named productions above should not be mistaken for the entirety of it. [Zylbercweig, *Leksikon fun yidishn teater*, vol. 3, p. 1837, in translation](https://www.museumoffamilyhistory.com/yt/lex/P/pearlman-max.htm); [Belling](https://web.uwm.edu/yiddish-stage/siz-night-dos-vos-amok-is-geven-max-perlman-1909-1985)
