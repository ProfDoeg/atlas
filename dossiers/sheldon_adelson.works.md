## Works by Sheldon Gary Adelson

### Commercial ventures and institutions

- **c. 1945:** An untitled Boston newspaper-selling concession. Adelson obtained the concession while still a child; it was an entrepreneurial venture rather than a authored work, and the surviving accounts do not supply a business name. ([Sands China biography](https://investor.sandschina.com/board-member/sheldon-gary-adelson))

- **c. 1948–1949:** An untitled candy-vending-machine business. Biographical accounts disagree over whether Adelson began it at fifteen or sixteen, so neither a single year nor a fixed title can safely be assigned. ([CBS News obituary](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/sheldon-adelson-dies-87-casino-mogul-gop-power-broker/))

- **1950s:** An untitled toiletry-kit business. This was one of several early, short-lived enterprises described in biographies; its precise dates, partners, and trading name are not securely documented. ([CBS News obituary](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/sheldon-adelson-dies-87-casino-mogul-gop-power-broker/))

- **1950s:** *De-Ice-It*. A business marketing a chemical windshield-deicing spray, not merely a product text; standard biographical summaries do not establish its exact dates or corporate form. ([Sheldon Adelson biographical summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheldon_Adelson))

- **1960s:** An untitled charter-tour business. It belongs to Adelson’s long sequence of ventures, but the accessible biographical record does not preserve a stable title or exact date. ([Sheldon Adelson biographical summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheldon_Adelson))

- **mid-1970s:** *Interface Group*. A trade-show and investment enterprise established with partners and later used to develop COMDEX; it is a corporate platform and collaborative obra rather than Adelson’s solitary creation. ([Sands China biography](https://investor.sandschina.com/board-member/sheldon-gary-adelson))

- **1979–1995:** *COMDEX (Computer Dealers Exposition)*. A recurring computer-industry trade show created and developed by Adelson and his partners, hence an event-series and commercial institution rather than a single text or object; its show division was sold to SoftBank in 1995. ([Sands China biography](https://investor.sandschina.com/board-member/sheldon-gary-adelson), [Washington Post obituary](https://www.washingtonpost.com/local/obituaries/sheldon-adelson-casino-magnate-who-influenced-policy-from-dc-to-jerusalem-dies-at-87/2021/01/12/639974ec-54d8-11eb-a931-5b162d0d033d_story.html))

- **1989–2021:** *Las Vegas Sands*. The resort-development corporation Adelson founded and led as chairman and chief executive; it is both an enterprise and the production apparatus through which the later buildings were collectively made. The historical Sands Hotel was purchased, not created by Adelson, and is therefore not separately counted as his obra. ([Las Vegas Sands history](https://www.sands.com/company/our-story/), [Sands China biography](https://investor.sandschina.com/board-member/sheldon-gary-adelson))

### Convention center and integrated resorts

- **1990:** *Sands Expo and Convention Center*. A privately developed Las Vegas convention complex conceived as a permanent counterpart to Adelson’s trade-show business; later integrations and expansions make its boundaries with the Venetian–Palazzo complex somewhat fluid. ([Las Vegas Sands history](https://www.sands.com/company/our-story/), [Las Vegas Sands 2011 Environmental Report](https://www.sands.com/content/uploads/2021/07/2011-environmental-report_compressed.pdf))

- **1999:** *The Venetian Resort Las Vegas*. A Venice-themed hotel, casino, retail, entertainment, and convention environment developed by Adelson’s company, with the originating concept attributed jointly to Sheldon and Miriam Adelson. Its deliberate fusion of architecture, spectacle, commerce, transport simulation, and gaming makes “integrated-resort Gesamtkunstwerk” more exact than “building,” although its architects and numerous corporate collaborators prevent exclusive personal authorship. ([Las Vegas Sands history](https://www.sands.com/company/our-story/), [“The Vision of the Integrated Resort”](https://www.sands.com/news/the-vision-of-the-integrated-resort/))

- **2004:** *Sands Macao*. A casino-hotel and entertainment complex developed by Las Vegas Sands and the company’s first Macao property; corporate filings describe it as the first Las Vegas-style casino in Macao. It is a collaborative commercial development, not a personally designed building. ([Sands China 2015 Annual Report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1300514/000119312516514047/d167795dex991.htm), [Sands China 2016 Annual Report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1300514/000119312517102627/d364612dex991.htm))

- **2007:** *The Venetian Macao*. The anchor integrated resort of Adelson’s larger Cotai project, combining hotel, casino, retail, performance, arena, and convention spaces. Like its Las Vegas precursor, it resists classification as a single building and is better understood as a corporately produced themed environment. ([Sands China 2016 Annual Report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1300514/000119312517102627/d364612dex991.htm), [Sands China 2022 Annual Report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1755281/000119312523084406/d425334dex991.pdf))

- **2007:** *The Palazzo*. A hotel-casino and convention development conceived as part of the Venetian complex in Las Vegas; operational reports often treat the Palazzo, Venetian, and Sands Expo as one integrated property, so its status as an independent obra is institutionally unstable. ([Las Vegas Sands 2011 Environmental Report](https://www.sands.com/content/uploads/2021/07/2011-environmental-report_compressed.pdf))

- **2008:** *The Plaza Macao and Four Seasons Hotel Macao*. A jointly operated integrated development comprising hotel, casino, retail, dining, and meeting facilities. The corporate record groups the components together, and the Four Seasons branding and operation make sole Adelson authorship especially inappropriate. ([Las Vegas Sands 2008 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1300514/000095015309000169/p14105e10vk.htm), [Sands China 2016 Annual Report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1300514/000119312517102627/d364612dex991.htm))

- **2009:** *Sands Bethlehem*. A Pennsylvania casino, hotel, retail, and entertainment complex developed by Las Vegas Sands. It was a corporate integrated-resort manifestation subsequently sold and renamed, so neither its later identity nor all of its later fabric belongs to Adelson’s project. ([Las Vegas Sands 2011 Environmental Report](https://www.sands.com/content/uploads/2021/07/2011-environmental-report_compressed.pdf))

- **2010:** *Marina Bay Sands*. A Singapore integrated resort uniting three hotel towers, casino, convention center, retail complex, museum, theaters, restaurants, and the elevated SkyPark. It is paradigmatically a corporate Gesamtkunstwerk: Adelson championed the resort model, while the realized architecture and engineering were the work of extensive professional teams. ([Las Vegas Sands history](https://www.sands.com/company/our-story/), [Marina Bay Sands company information](https://www.marinabaysands.com/company-information.html))

- **2012:** *Sands Cotai Central*. A multi-hotel, casino, retail, entertainment, and convention development opened in phases from April 2012. Its later conversion into *The Londoner Macao* continued after Adelson’s death, so the Londoner should not simply be back-attributed to him as a completed work. ([Sands China 2015 Annual Report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1300514/000119312515296734/d81573dex991.htm), [Las Vegas Sands 2024 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1300514/000130051425000040/lvs-20241231.htm))

- **2016:** *The Parisian Macao*. A Paris-themed integrated resort containing hotel, gaming, retail, dining, entertainment, and meeting spaces; Adelson explicitly presented its opening as a further realization of his Cotai vision. Its status is again collaborative and corporate rather than that of a personally authored architectural work. ([Las Vegas Sands 2016 shareholder report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1300514/000119312517047390/d359204dex991.htm), [Sands China 2016 Annual Report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1300514/000119312517102627/d364612dex991.htm))

### Urban-scale projects, including unrealized work

- **early 2000s–2021:** *Cotai Strip*. Adelson’s master project for transforming reclaimed land in Macao into a concentration of convention-based integrated resorts. It is an evolving urban and commercial intention rather than a single finished object; only part of the larger vision was realized during his lifetime. ([Sands China 2015 Annual Report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1300514/000119312516514047/d167795dex991.htm), [Sands China 2016 Annual Report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1300514/000119312517102627/d364612dex991.htm))

- **2010–2013:** *EuroVegas*. An unrealized proposal for a vast resort, casino, hotel, convention, retail, golf, and entertainment district near Madrid. This is properly a project—the intention itself is the surviving work—not a building; Las Vegas Sands abandoned it in December 2013 after negotiations over legal, fiscal, and smoking-policy conditions failed. ([El País on the proposal](https://elpais.com/sociedad/2013/05/29/actualidad/1369860186_172833.html), [EuroVegas project history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurovegas))

- **2001–2007:** An untitled online-gambling project. Las Vegas Sands explored creating an internet-gaming operation but abandoned the effort; it survives principally as evidence of a corporate intention and is not a completed product. ([Time](https://time.com/3703989/sheldon-adelson-george-pataki-lindsey-graham-gambling/))

### Media

- **2007:** *Israel Hayom*. A free Hebrew-language daily newspaper founded and financed by Adelson. It is a continuing media institution rather than a text authored by him, and its journalism belongs to its editors and writers; calling the entire newspaper his “work” therefore requires the qualification that his role was founder, proprietor, and political patron. ([Israel Hayom obituary of its founder](https://www.israelhayom.com/2021/01/12/sheldon-adelson-1933-2021/), [Washington Post obituary](https://www.washingtonpost.com/local/obituaries/sheldon-adelson-casino-magnate-who-influenced-policy-from-dc-to-jerusalem-dies-at-87/2021/01/12/639974ec-54d8-11eb-a931-5b162d0d033d_story.html))

### Medical and philanthropic institutions

- **1993:** *Dr. Miriam and Sheldon G. Adelson Clinic for Drug Abuse Treatment and Research, Tel Aviv*. A nonprofit clinical and research institution founded by the Adelsons and affiliated with Tel Aviv Sourasky Medical Center. Miriam Adelson was the physician who established and directed the clinical model, so attributing the clinic to Sheldon alone would misstate its authorship. ([peer-reviewed comparative study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3810137/), [Reichman University account](https://www.runi.ac.il/media/fapdg4jf/%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%90%D7%93%D7%9C%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%9F-%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%A4%D7%99-12-07.pdf))

- **2000:** *Dr. Miriam and Sheldon G. Adelson Clinic for Drug Abuse Treatment and Research, Las Vegas*. A nonprofit outpatient opioid-treatment and research clinic modeled on the Tel Aviv institution. It is a joint philanthropic and medical creation, with Miriam Adelson supplying the principal clinical authorship. ([Adelson Clinic](https://adelsoncliniclasvegas.com/payment-options/), [peer-reviewed comparative study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3810137/))

- **date not securely established:** *Adelson Medical Research Foundation*. A philanthropic research network supporting collaborative work in neurology, immunology, and oncology. Accessible institutional sources identify the foundation and its model but do not provide a dependable founding date, so none is imposed here; its scientific findings are the work of funded researchers, not Adelson’s personal works. ([Adelson Foundation leadership](https://www.adelsonfoundation.org/AFF/leadership.html), [RAND assessment](https://adelsonfoundation.org/pdf/rand_report.pdf))

- **2007:** *Adelson Family Foundation*. A private philanthropic institution established jointly by Sheldon and Miriam Adelson, principally supporting Israel- and Jewish-related programs. Grants made through it are acts of patronage and should not be confused with authorship of the recipient organizations’ work. ([Adelson Family Foundation](https://www.adelsonfoundation.org/AFF/index.html))

### Political organizations and advocacy machinery

- **2007–2008:** *Freedom’s Watch*. A conservative advocacy organization co-created by Adelson and other donors, initially to conduct an advertising campaign supporting the George W. Bush administration’s Iraq policy. Adelson was a founder and dominant funder, but not the author of every advertisement or position issued in its name; the organization became inactive after the 2008 elections. ([The New Yorker](https://www.newyorker.com/reporting/2008/06/30/080630fa_fact_bruck), [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/politics/2007/10/22/a-battle-over-freedom/036bad2a-e76c-4675-9f38-4430b69bad83/))

- **2013:** *Coalition to Stop Internet Gambling*. An advocacy organization formed and funded by Adelson to seek a federal prohibition on online gambling. It functioned as political machinery and a policy campaign rather than as a personal manifesto, although it acted upon his stated position. ([Time](https://time.com/3703989/sheldon-adelson-george-pataki-lindsey-graham-gambling/))

- **2015:** *Maccabee Task Force*. A campus advocacy organization launched with Adelson funding to oppose the Boycott, Divestment and Sanctions movement and promote pro-Israel activity. Sources variously describe Sheldon alone, Sheldon and Miriam Adelson, or a broader group including Haim Saban as founders; the collective and disputed attribution must be retained. ([Maccabee Task Force account](https://www.maccabeetaskforce.org/campus-arena-maccabee-task-force-wages-battle-without-credit/), [Jewish Currents](https://jewishcurrents.org/maccabee-games), [ProPublica nonprofit record](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/474908810))
