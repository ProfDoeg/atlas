## Works by Prescott Sheldon Bush

### Articles and public arguments

- **June–July 1954:** *Timely Monetary Policy*. A two-part article in *Banking*, explicitly presented as extracts from Bush’s Senate speech on monetary policy; the surviving issues establish 1954, although later bibliographies sometimes misdate it to 1955. ([June issue](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/22/Banking_1954-06-_Vol_46_Iss_12_%28IA_sim_american-bankers-association-aba-banking-journal_1954-06_46_12%29.pdf); [July issue](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/51/Banking_1954-07-_Vol_47_Iss_1_%28IA_sim_american-bankers-association-aba-banking-journal_1954-07_47_1_%29.pdf))

- **1955; revised manifestation 1960:** *The Special Responsibility of Men in Finance*. An essay first associated with the *Yale Daily News* publication *Wall Street 1955* and subsequently revised for *Wall Street Twentieth Century—A People’s Capitalism*; Senator Jacob Javits entered the latter manifestation in the *Congressional Record* on August 26, 1960. ([Congressional Record](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GPO-CRECB-1960-pt13/pdf/GPO-CRECB-1960-pt13-7-1.pdf))

- **July 1959:** *To Preserve Peace—Let’s Show the Russians How Strong We Are!*. A *Reader’s Digest* article credited to Bush, but its authorship is materially disputed: former Defense Department comptroller Wilfred J. McNeil later said that Bush signed and published excerpts from a text McNeil himself had prepared. It is therefore best classified as an attributed manifestation rather than an uncontested sole-authored obra. ([McNeil oral-history transcript and bibliographic note](https://history.defense.gov/Portals/70/Documents/oral_history/OH_Trans_McNeilWilfred6-7-1976.pdf?ver=2014-05-28-133511-900))

- **May 1960:** *Politics Is Your Business*. An article or civic manifesto published in the New York State Chamber of Commerce *Bulletin*, urging political participation; the standard short checklist of Bush’s writings records it, but no critical bibliography or collected edition establishes whether the surviving title represents the complete text or an edited address. ([bibliographic checklist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prescott_Bush#Writings))

### Legislative texts and projects

- **August 31, 1962:** *Individual Views of Senator Prescott Bush*. A separately signed dissenting or supplementary statement appended to the Senate Special Committee on Aging’s report *Housing for the Elderly*. It is a public argument within a collective government document, not a freestanding book. ([U.S. Senate Special Committee on Aging](https://www.aging.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/reports/rpt562.pdf))

- **March 1962:** *Bipartisan Civil-Rights Legislative Package*. A collaborative legislative project addressing voting rights, school integration, housing, violent resistance to desegregation, and equal employment; it remained a package of proposals rather than a single enacted obra and was later described as a precursor of the Kennedy administration’s comprehensive civil-rights bill. Bush’s individual share cannot be separated cleanly from that of the other sponsoring senators. ([Jefferson Morley, “Bush and the Blacks: An Unknown Story”](https://www.nybooks.com/articles/1992/01/16/bush-and-the-blacks-an-unknown-story/))

### Elicited testimony

- **1966:** An oral-history interview conducted by the Columbia University Oral History Research Office. This is a jointly produced manifestation—Bush’s spoken recollection shaped by an interviewer and subsequently transcribed—rather than a conventionally authored work; the publicly available archival description supplies no fixed title or interviewer’s name. ([George H. W. Bush Presidential Library, Prescott Bush Materials](https://www.bush41library.gov/digital-research-room/search?f%5B0%5D=level_of_description%3ASeries&f%5B1%5D=record_type%3ATextual%20Records&page=19))

### Institutional creation

- **1937:** *Yale Glee Club Associates*. An alumni organization founded by Bush to preserve Yale’s singing tradition and support the Glee Club. This is an institutional obra rather than a text, and its continuing collective life resists attribution to Bush as a wholly individual work. ([Yale Glee Club](https://gleeclub.yale.edu/chorus-database/alumni))
