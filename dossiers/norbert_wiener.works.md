## Works by Norbert Wiener

### Philosophical and foundational writings

- **1913:** *On the Rearrangement of the Positive Integers in a Series of Ordinal Numbers Greater than That of Any Given Fundamental Sequence of Omegas*. Mathematical paper.
- **1914:** *The Highest Good*. Philosophical article on ethics.
- **1914:** *Relativism*. Philosophical article.
- **1914:** *A Simplification of the Logic of Relations*. Paper in symbolic logic.
- **1914:** *A Contribution to the Theory of Relative Position*. Mathematical-logical paper.
- **1915:** *Studies in Synthetic Logic*. Paper in formal logic.
- **1915:** *The Shortest Line Dividing an Area into a Given Ratio*. Mathematical-philosophical article.
- **1917:** *Certain Formal Invariances in Boolean Algebras*. Mathematical paper.
- **1920:** *Bilinear Operations Generating All Operations Rational in a Domain*. Mathematical paper.
- **1920:** *A Set of Postulates for Fields*. Axiomatic mathematical paper.
- **1920:** *Certain Iterative Characteristics of Bilinear Operations*. Mathematical note.
- **1920:** *On the Theory of Sets of Points in Terms of Continuous Transformations*. Congress communication.
- **1920:** *Certain Iterative Properties of Bilinear Operations*. Congress communication closely related to Wiener’s other 1920 paper; its status as a separate obra is consequently slight.
- **1921:** *A New Theory of Measurement: A Study in the Logic of Mathematics*. Extended foundational paper derived from Wiener’s doctoral work.
- **1921:** *The Isomorphisms of Complex Algebra*. Mathematical note.
- **1922:** *The Relation of Space and Geometry to Experience*. Three-part philosophical study.
- **1922:** *The Group of the Linear Continuum*. Mathematical paper.
- **1923:** *On the Nature of Mathematical Thinking*. Philosophical and psychological essay.
- **1934:** *Leibniz and Haldane*. Philosophical essay.
- **1936:** *The Role of the Observer*. Philosophy-of-science article.
- **1958:** *Logique, probabilité et méthode des sciences physiques*. Brief contribution to a collective work on scientific method.

### Analysis, probability, and pure mathematics

- **1920:** *The Mean of a Functional of Arbitrary Elements*. Mathematical paper initiating Wiener’s integration over function space.
- **1921:** *A New Vector in Integral Equations*. Joint mathematical paper with Frank L. Hitchcock.
- **1921:** *The Average of an Analytical Functional*. Mathematical paper.
- **1921:** *The Average of an Analytical Functional and the Brownian Movement*. Mathematical paper connecting function-space integration with Brownian motion.
- **1922:** *A New Type of Integral Expansion*. Mathematical paper.
- **1922:** *Limit in Terms of Continuous Transformation*. Mathematical paper.
- **1922:** *The Equivalence of Expansions in Terms of Orthogonal Functions*. Joint paper with J. L. Walsh.
- **1923:** *Note on a Paper of M. Banach*. Mathematical note.
- **1923:** *Differential Space*. Extended mathematical paper establishing a program later applied to quantum theory.
- **1923:** *Note on a New Type of Summability*. Mathematical note.
- **1923:** *Note on the Series S(+1/n)*. Mathematical note; the standard bibliography’s transcription of the formula is imperfect.
- **1924:** *The Quadratic Variation of a Function and Its Fourier Coefficients*. Mathematical paper.
- **1924:** *The Average Value of a Functional*. Mathematical paper.
- **1924:** *Un problème de probabilités dénombrables*. Short probability note.
- **1925:** *Note on a Paper of O. Perron*. Mathematical note.
- **1925:** *The Solution of a Difference Equation by Trigonometrical Integrals*. Mathematical paper.
- **1925:** *A Contribution to the Theory of Interpolation*. Mathematical paper.
- **1925:** *Note on Quasi-Analytic Functions*. Mathematical note.
- **1925:** *On the Representation of Functions by Trigonometrical Integrals*. Mathematical paper.
- **1925:** *Verallgemeinerte trigonometrische Entwicklungen*. Mathematical note on generalized trigonometric expansions.
- **1926:** *The Harmonic Analysis of Irregular Motion*. Mathematical paper.
- **1926:** *The Harmonic Analysis of Irregular Motion: Second Paper*. Continuation of the preceding study.
- **1926:** *The Operational Calculus*. Mathematical paper.
- **1926:** *Analytical Approximations to Topological Transformations*. Joint paper with Philip Franklin.
- **1927:** *On the Closure of Certain Assemblages of Trigonometrical Functions*. Mathematical note.
- **1927:** *Laplacians and Continuous Linear Functionals*. Mathematical paper.
- **1927:** *The Spectrum of an Array and Its Application to the Study of the Translation Properties of a Simple Class of Arithmetical Functions*. Mathematical paper.
- **1927:** *On a New Definition of Almost Periodic Functions*. Mathematical note.
- **1927:** *On a Theorem of Bochner and Hardy*. Mathematical paper.
- **1927:** *Une généralisation des fonctionnelles à variation bornée*. Mathematical note.
- **1927:** *Une méthode nouvelle pour la démonstration des théorèmes de Tauber*. Mathematical note.
- **1928:** *The Spectrum of an Arbitrary Function*. Extended mathematical paper.
- **1928:** *A New Method of Tauberian Theorems*. Mathematical paper.
- **1929:** *Hermitian Polynomials and Fourier Analysis*. Mathematical paper.
- **1929:** *Harmonic Analysis and Group Theory*. Mathematical paper.
- **1929:** *A Type of Tauberian Theorem Applying to Fourier Series*. Mathematical paper.
- **1929:** *Fourier Analysis and Asymptotic Series*. Mathematical appendix to Vannevar Bush’s *Operational Circuit Analysis*.
- **1930:** *Generalized Harmonic Analysis*. Major mathematical memoir rather than a book.
- **1931:** *A New Deduction of the Gaussian Distribution*. Mathematical paper.
- **1932:** *Tauberian Theorems*. Major mathematical memoir.
- **1932:** *A Note on Tauberian Theorems*. Mathematical note.
- **1932:** *Characters of Infinite Abelian Groups*. Congress paper with R. E. A. C. Paley.
- **1932:** *Über eine Klasse singulärer Integralgleichungen*. Joint paper with Eberhard Hopf; it produced what became known as the Wiener–Hopf equation.
- **1933:** *The Fourier Integral and Certain of Its Applications*. Mathematical monograph.
- **1933:** *Notes on the Theory and Application of Fourier Transforms, I–VII*. Seven-part collaborative paper sequence with R. E. A. C. Paley, treated here as one sustained obra.
- **1933:** *A One-Sided Tauberian Theorem*. Mathematical note.
- **1933:** *Characters of Abelian Groups*. Joint paper with Paley.
- **1933:** *Notes on Random Functions*. Joint paper with Paley and Antoni Zygmund.
- **1933:** *The Total Variation of g(x+h)−g(x)*. Joint paper with R. C. Young; bibliographies transmit the displayed formula inconsistently.
- **1934:** *A Class of Gap Theorems*. Mathematical paper, published across the bibliographic range 1934–36.
- **1934:** *Fourier Transforms in the Complex Domain*. Collaborative mathematical monograph with Paley, published after Paley’s death.
- **1934:** *Random Functions*. Mathematical paper.
- **1935:** *The Closure of Bessel Functions*. Published abstract, not a full paper.
- **1935:** *Fabry’s Gap Theorem*. Mathematical paper.
- **1935:** *A Theorem of Carleman*. Mathematical paper.
- **1936:** *Sur les séries de Fourier lacunaires: Théorème direct*. Joint note with Szolem Mandelbrojt.
- **1936:** *Séries de Fourier lacunaires: Théorèmes inverses*. Joint note with Mandelbrojt.
- **1936:** *Gap Theorems*. Congress communication.
- **1936:** *A Tauberian Gap Theorem of Hardy and Littlewood*. Mathematical paper.
- **1937:** *Taylor’s Series of Entire Functions of Smooth Growth*. Joint paper with W. T. Martin.
- **1937:** *Random Waring’s Theorems*. Abstract with Norman Levinson; it resists classification as a completed paper.
- **1938:** *On Absolutely Convergent Fourier–Stieltjes Transforms*. Joint paper with H. R. Pitt; some bibliographies incorrectly date it 1935.
- **1938:** *Fourier–Stieltjes Transforms and Singular Infinite Convolutions*. Joint paper with Aurel Wintner.
- **1938:** *Remarks on the Classical Inversion Formula for the Laplace Integral*. Joint note with D. V. Widder.
- **1938:** *The Homogeneous Chaos*. Mathematical paper.
- **1938:** *The Historical Background of Harmonic Analysis*. Historical-mathematical address published as an essay.
- **1939:** *Convergence Properties of Analytic Functions of Fourier–Stieltjes Transforms*. Joint paper with R. H. Cameron.
- **1939:** *Generalization of Ikehara’s Theorem*. Joint paper with H. R. Pitt.
- **1939:** *On Singular Distributions*. Joint paper with Aurel Wintner.
- **1939:** *The Ergodic Theorem*. Mathematical paper.
- **1940:** *A Canonical Series for Symmetric Functions in Statistical Mechanics*. Published abstract, not a full surviving exposition.
- **1941:** *Harmonic Analysis and Ergodic Theory*. Joint paper with Aurel Wintner.
- **1941:** *On the Ergodic Dynamics of Almost Periodic Systems*. Joint paper with Wintner.
- **1942:** *On the Oscillation of the Derivatives of a Periodic Function*. Joint paper with George Pólya.
- **1943:** *The Discrete Chaos*. Joint mathematical paper with Aurel Wintner.
- **1946:** *A Generalization of the Wiener–Hopf Integral Equation*. Joint paper with Albert E. Heins.
- **1947:** *Sur les fonctions indéfiniment dérivables sur une demi-droite*. Joint mathematical note with Szolem Mandelbrojt.
- **1950:** *Some Prime-Number Consequences of the Ikehara Theorem*. Joint paper with Leonard Geller.
- **1955:** *On the Factorization of Matrices*. Mathematical paper.
- **1957:** *The Definition and Ergodic Properties of the Stochastic Adjoint of a Unitary Transformation*. Joint paper with E. J. Akutowicz, including a published addendum.
- **1957:** *Notes on Pólya’s and Turán’s Hypotheses Concerning Liouville’s Factor*. Mathematical paper.
- **1957:** *On the Non-Vanishing of Euler Products*. Joint paper with Aurel Wintner.
- **1959:** *A Factorization of Positive Hermitian Matrices*. Joint paper with E. J. Akutowicz.

### Potential theory

- **1923:** *Nets and the Dirichlet Problem*. Joint mathematical paper with H. B. Phillips.
- **1923:** *Discontinuous Boundary Conditions and the Dirichlet Problem*. Mathematical paper.
- **1924:** *Certain Notions in Potential Theory*. Mathematical paper.
- **1924:** *The Dirichlet Problem*. Mathematical paper.
- **1924:** *Une condition nécessaire et suffisante de possibilité pour le problème de Dirichlet*. Mathematical note stating a criterion now associated with Wiener.

### Physics, quantum theory, and statistical mechanics

- **1926:** *A New Formulation of the Laws of Quantisation for Periodic and Aperiodic Phenomena*. Joint paper with Max Born.
- **1926:** *Eine neue Formulierung der Quantengesetze für periodische und nichtperiodische Vorgänge*. German manifestation of the Born–Wiener quantization work; its status as a distinct obra rather than another-language publication is debatable.
- **1927:** *Quantum Theory and Gravitational Relativity*. Joint note with Dirk J. Struik.
- **1927:** *A Relativistic Theory of Quanta*. Joint paper with Struik.
- **1927:** *Sur la théorie relativiste des quanta*. Joint note with Struik.
- **1927:** *Sur la théorie relativiste des quanta*. A second short communication under the same title; the bibliography distinguishes it by pagination.
- **1928:** *The Fifth Dimension in Relativistic Quantum Theory*. Joint paper with Struik.
- **1928:** *Coherency Matrices and Quantum Theory*. Mathematical-physics paper.
- **1929:** *Harmonic Analysis and the Quantum Theory*. Mathematical-physics article.
- **1929:** *On the Spherically Symmetrical Statistical Field in Einstein’s Unified Theory of Electricity and Gravitation*. Mathematical-physics paper.
- **1929:** *On the Spherically Symmetrical Statistical Field in Einstein’s Unified Theory: A Correction*. Joint correction with Manuel Sandoval Vallarta; standard lists sometimes misprint its year as 1920.
- **1939:** *A New Method in Statistical Mechanics*. Abstract with Brockway McMillan, not a full paper.
- **1939:** *The Use of Statistical Theory in the Study of Turbulence*. Scientific note.
- **1953:** *A New Form for the Statistical Postulate of Quantum Mechanics*. Joint paper with Armand Siegel.
- **1953:** *Distributions quantiques dans l’espace différentiel pour les fonctions d’ondes dépendant du spin*. Mathematical-physics note.
- **1953:** *The Differential-Space Theory of Quantum Mechanics*. Joint communication with Armand Siegel; its overlap with their longer paper makes its independent status uncertain.
- **1953:** *Optics and the Theory of Stochastic Processes*. Scientific paper.
- **1955:** *The Differential-Space Theory of Quantum Systems*. Joint paper with Armand Siegel.
- **1956:** *The “Theory of Measurement” in Differential-Space Quantum Theory*. Joint paper with Armand Siegel.
- **1963–64:** *Random Theory in Classical Phase Space and Quantum Mechanics*. Conference paper with Giacomo Della Riccia, delivered in 1963 and published in 1964.
- **1966:** *Differential Space, Quantum Systems, and Prediction*. Posthumous collaborative monograph with Armand Siegel, Bayard Rankin, and W. T. Martin, assembled from Wiener’s research program; shared authorship should not be collapsed into sole authorship.

### Prediction, communication, control, and cybernetics

- **1934:** *Notes on the Kron Theory of Tensors in Electrical Machinery*. Published abstract rather than a developed paper.
- **1942:** *Extrapolation, Interpolation, and Smoothing of Stationary Time Series*. Classified wartime report, commonly called the “Yellow Peril”; its public 1949 book is a later manifestation of the same technical obra.
- **1943:** *Behavior, Purpose and Teleology*. Joint theoretical paper with Arturo Rosenblueth and Julian Bigelow; a foundational cybernetic manifesto in the functional sense that it proposed a new vocabulary of purposive behavior.
- **1945:** *The Role of Models in Science*. Joint philosophy-of-science paper with Arturo Rosenblueth.
- **1945:** *La teoría de la extrapolación estadística*. Technical paper in prediction theory.
- **1946:** *The Mathematical Formulation of the Problem of Conduction of Impulses in a Network of Connected Excitable Elements, Specifically in Cardiac Muscle*. Joint mathematical-biological study with Arturo Rosenblueth.
- **1948:** *Teleological Mechanisms*. Multi-author symposium contribution with Lawrence K. Frank, G. E. Hutchinson, W. K. Livingston, and Warren S. McCulloch; authorship of the whole symposium record is distributed.
- **1948:** *Cybernetics; or, Control and Communication in the Animal and the Machine*. Interdisciplinary monograph and disciplinary manifesto that named cybernetics; the expanded 1961 edition is a revised manifestation, not a wholly new obra.
- **1948:** *Time, Communication and the Nervous System*. Scientific essay.
- **1948:** *Cybernetics*. Expository article in *Scientific American*, distinct from the book despite the shared title.
- **1948:** *An Account of the Spike Potential of Axons*. Joint experimental-theoretical paper with Arturo Rosenblueth, Walter Pitts, and J. García Ramos.
- **1949:** *Sur la théorie de la prévision statistique et du filtrage des ondes*. Conference paper on prediction and filtering.
- **1949:** *New Concept of Communication Engineering*. Technical article.
- **1949:** *Sound Communication with the Deaf*. Article on sensory prosthesis.
- **1949:** *Some Problems in Sensory Prosthesis*. Joint scientific note with L. Levine.
- **1950:** *The Thinking Machine*. Popular expository article.
- **1950:** *Cybernetics*. Brief academy communication, not the monograph.
- **1950:** *Some Maxims for Biologists and Psychologists*. Interdisciplinary position paper.
- **1950:** *Purposeful and Non-Purposeful Behavior*. Joint paper with Arturo Rosenblueth.
- **1950:** *Speech, Language, and Learning*. Brief acoustical-science paper.
- **1950:** *Comprehensive View of Prediction Theory*. Expository congress lecture, published in 1952.
- **1950:** *Entropy and Information*. Symposium paper.
- **1951:** *Problems of Sensory Prosthesis*. Expository scientific paper.
- **1951:** *Homeostasis in the Individual and Society*. Cybernetic social-science essay.
- **1953:** *Les machines à calculer et la forme (Gestalt)*. Short conference intervention on computing and form.
- **1953:** *The Concept of Homeostasis in Medicine*. Medical-cybernetic article.
- **1953:** *The Future of Automatic Machinery*. Engineering essay.
- **1951–55:** *Nonlinear Prediction and Dynamics*. Conference paper delivered in the early 1950s and published in the Third Berkeley Symposium proceedings; bibliographies disagree in how they present its date.
- **1955:** *Thermodynamics of the Message*. Book chapter connecting information and neurochemistry.
- **1955:** *Time and Organisation*. Second Fawley Foundation Lecture: a performed lecture subsequently treated as a text.
- **1956:** *The Theory of Prediction*. Expository book chapter for engineers.
- **1956:** *Brain Waves and the Interferometer*. Scientific paper.
- **1957:** *Rhythms in Physiology with Particular Reference to Encephalography*. Medical-society lecture published in proceedings.
- **1957:** *The Prediction Theory of Multivariate Stochastic Processes, I: The Regularity Condition*. Joint paper with P. R. Masani.
- **1958:** *The Prediction Theory of Multivariate Stochastic Processes, II: The Linear Predictor*. Joint paper with P. R. Masani.
- **1958:** *My Connection with Cybernetics: Its Origin and Its Future*. Intellectual-historical essay.
- **1958:** *Random Time*. Scientific note.
- **1958:** *Time and the Science of Organization*. Cybernetic essay.
- **1958:** *Sur la prévision linéaire des processus stochastiques vectoriels à densité spectrale bornée, I*. Joint note with P. R. Masani.
- **1958:** *Sur la prévision linéaire des processus stochastiques vectoriels à densité spectrale bornée, II*. Joint note with Masani; one widely reproduced bibliography misprints its year as 1953.
- **1958–59:** *Nonlinear Problems in Random Theory*. Mathematical monograph, issued in 1958 but dated 1959 in some bibliographies.
- **1959:** *Non-Linear Prediction*. Joint chapter with P. R. Masani.
- **1959:** *On Bivariate Stationary Processes and the Factorisation of Matrix-Valued Functions*. Joint paper with Masani, also circulated in English translation.
- **1960:** *Preface to Cybernetics of Natural Systems*. Prefatory text for D. Stanley-Jones’s book.
- **1960:** *Some Moral and Technical Consequences of Automation*. Public scientific and ethical intervention.
- **1960:** *Kybernetik*. Dictionary contribution defining cybernetics.
- **1961:** *Über Informationstheorie*. Expository article on information theory.

- **1962–65:** *L’homme et la machine*. Conference contribution delivered at Royaumont in 1962 and published in 1965.

- **1962:** *The Mathematics of Self-Organising Systems*. Book chapter.

- **1964:** *Dynamical Systems in Physics and Biology*. Futurist scientific essay for the series “Fundamental Science in 1984.”

### Social criticism and public interventions

- **1935:** *Mathematics in American Secondary Schools*. Educational essay; its original title and journal details are imperfectly transmitted in the standard bibliography.
- **1938:** *The Decline of Cookbook Engineering*. Engineering-education polemic.
- **1947:** *A Scientist Rebels*. Public letter or manifesto refusing military work; it acts by announcing a professional and ethical refusal.
- **1948:** *A Rebellious Scientist after Two Years*. Follow-up public intervention on scientific responsibility.
- **1950:** *The Human Use of Human Beings: Cybernetics and Society*. Public-facing cybernetic and social-ethical book; the substantially revised 1954 edition is a new manifestation of the same work.
- **1956:** *Moral Reflections of a Mathematician*. Essay reprinted from *I Am a Mathematician*, so it is not wholly independent as a composition.
- **1957:** *The Role of the Mathematician in a Materialistic Culture*. Conference address, also reported under the title *A Scientist’s Dilemma in a Materialistic World*; the title is therefore unstable.
- **1960:** *The Grand Privilege*. Public essay.
- **1964:** *God & Golem, Inc.: A Comment on Certain Points Where Cybernetics Impinges on Religion*. Short speculative book on learning machines, creation, and responsibility.

### Autobiography, fiction, and abandoned projects

- **1952:** *The Brain*. Science-fiction story published under “W. Norbert”; the pseudonymous byline makes attribution less immediately visible but is accepted in Wiener bibliographies.
- **1952:** *Miracle of the Broom Closet*. Science-fiction story, later reprinted in 1954.
- **1953:** *Ex-Prodigy: My Childhood and Youth*. First volume of Wiener’s autobiography.
- **1954:** *Invention: The Care and Feeding of Ideas*. Abandoned book project: Wiener wrote the manuscript but withdrew it during editing and returned his advance. Its 1993 publication is posthumous, and its resistance to “finished work” is essential to its classification.
- **1956:** *I Am a Mathematician: The Later Life of a Prodigy*. Second autobiographical volume.
- **1959:** *The Tempter*. Historical novel about invention, communication engineering, and Oliver Heaviside.
- **1964:** *Selected Papers of Norbert Wiener*. Authorial selection and retrospective arrangement of papers, accompanied by essays from other scholars; it is a collection-manifestation rather than a newly composed scientific obra.

### Works whose separate status is doubtful

- **1943:** *Ergodic Dynamics of Almost Periodic Systems*. A review entry referring back to the 1941 Wiener–Wintner paper, not a new obra despite its appearance as a separate item in some publication lists.
- **1946:** *Theory of Statistical Extrapolation*. A review or cross-reference to *La teoría de la extrapolación estadística*, not securely a separate work.
- **1961:** *Cybernetics*, second edition. A substantially revised manifestation of the 1948 book, important bibliographically but not counted here as an independent obra.
- **1964:** *Time Series*. Paperback reprinting of *Extrapolation, Interpolation, and Smoothing of Stationary Time Series*, not a new work.
- **1966:** *Generalized Harmonic Analysis and Tauberian Theorems*. Posthumous book-form manifestation of earlier mathematical memoirs rather than a separately completed Wiener composition.
- **1976–85:** *Norbert Wiener: Collected Works with Commentaries*, four volumes. Posthumous scholarly Gesamtausgabe edited by P. R. Masani: indispensable evidence for Wiener’s body of work, but the editorial object itself was not made by Wiener alone.
