## Works by María Estela Martínez Cartas de Perón

### Doctrinal books and pamphlets

- **1972; official edition 1974:** *Las veinte verdades del justicialismo*. A doctrinal commentary presenting and interpreting Perón’s twenty principles. María Celeste Napal calls it Martínez de Perón’s own work and dates its composition to 1972, while the government imprint used by scholars is dated 1974; the alternative title *Las veinte verdades de la mujer justicialista* is insufficiently supported and may be a later bibliographic corruption. ([Napal, pp. 152–166](https://historiapolitica.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/PolHis13.pdf); [Napal, primary-source bibliography](https://repositoriodigital.uns.edu.ar/bitstream/123456789/2988/1/Napal%2C%20Ma.%20Celeste.%20Tesina.pdf))

- **1983:** *Las verdades del justicialismo*. A 132-page political book in which the former president comments on Justicialist doctrine, published by Ediciones Castañeda with a prologue by Antonio Cafiero. It substantially revisits the terrain of the earlier pamphlet, but the available evidence does not establish whether it is a revision, expansion, or wholly distinct text. ([Bibliographic record](https://www.terralibro.es/libro/isbn/9509014079.html))

### Speeches issued as official pamphlets

- **10 June 1974:** *La señora vicepresidente habla sobre el desabastecimiento*. An official speech-pamphlet concerning shortages, issued by the presidential press office while Martínez de Perón was vice-president. Its title is descriptive and institutional rather than demonstrably authorial. ([Napal, primary-source bibliography](https://repositoriodigital.uns.edu.ar/bitstream/123456789/2988/1/Napal%2C%20Ma.%20Celeste.%20Tesina.pdf))

- **June 1974:** *Habla en el Instituto de Cultura Hispánica*. A speech delivered in Madrid; the scholarly source supplies only this descriptive title, so it should not be treated as a fixed authorial title. ([Napal, primary-source bibliography](https://repositoriodigital.uns.edu.ar/bitstream/123456789/2988/1/Napal%2C%20Ma.%20Celeste.%20Tesina.pdf))

- **June 1974; published July 1974:** *Mensaje a los trabajadores del mundo: la señora vicepresidente María Estela Martínez de Perón habla en la 59.ª reunión de la O.I.T.* A transnational workers’ address delivered at the International Labour Conference and issued as a government pamphlet. It is simultaneously an oration, a political intervention, and a printed manifestation of that performance. ([Napal, primary-source bibliography](https://repositoriodigital.uns.edu.ar/bitstream/123456789/2988/1/Napal%2C%20Ma.%20Celeste.%20Tesina.pdf); [CELS archival inventory](https://www.cels.org.ar/common/documentos/herramientas_descriptivas_AR_AP_EFM.pdf))

- **24 July 1974:** *La señora presidente habla a los signatarios del Pacto Social*. An official address to the parties to Argentina’s Social Pact, separately manifested as a presidential press-office pamphlet. ([Napal, primary-source bibliography](https://repositoriodigital.uns.edu.ar/bitstream/123456789/2988/1/Napal%2C%20Ma.%20Celeste.%20Tesina.pdf))

- **29 July 1974:** *La Presidente de la Nación inaugura la Conferencia Interamericana sobre Planificación de Seguridad Social*. An inaugural conference address issued by the presidential press office; it is a ceremonial and policy-making speech rather than a book. ([Napal, primary-source bibliography](https://repositoriodigital.uns.edu.ar/bitstream/123456789/2988/1/Napal%2C%20Ma.%20Celeste.%20Tesina.pdf))

- **27 August 1974:** *La presidente habla a los trabajadores de la energía: acto de adhesión con motivo de la nacionalización del comercio de derivados del petróleo*. A public address and commemorative political performance, also circulated as an official pamphlet. ([Napal, primary-source bibliography](https://repositoriodigital.uns.edu.ar/bitstream/123456789/2988/1/Napal%2C%20Ma.%20Celeste.%20Tesina.pdf))

- **20 September 1974:** *Mensaje de la presidente de la República señora María Estela Martínez de Perón a los trabajadores de todo el país*. A nationally directed workers’ message issued as an official pamphlet. Its status lies between political address and manifesto: it sought allegiance and action rather than merely describing policy. ([Napal, primary-source bibliography](https://repositoriodigital.uns.edu.ar/bitstream/123456789/2988/1/Napal%2C%20Ma.%20Celeste.%20Tesina.pdf))

- **26 September 1974:** *Mensaje oficial de la presidenta María Estela Martínez de Perón*. A radio-and-television address responding to the previous day’s violence and announcing proposed antisubversion legislation. The surviving object is audiovisual as well as verbal, and its repository title is descriptive rather than necessarily supplied by the speaker. ([UNLP Archivo de la Palabra](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/handle/10915/29740))

- **8 October 1974:** *El país contra la violencia: discurso pronunciado por la presidente de la Nación María Estela Martínez de Perón en la Asamblea Multisectorial*. A separately issued speech whose rhetoric solicited collective political action; in that performative sense it approaches a manifesto. ([Napal, primary-source bibliography](https://repositoriodigital.uns.edu.ar/bitstream/123456789/2988/1/Napal%2C%20Ma.%20Celeste.%20Tesina.pdf))

- **21 March 1975:** *Adhesión al Año Internacional de la Mujer: mensaje dirigido al mundo por la presidente de la Nación, señora María Estela Martínez de Perón*. A globally addressed presidential message issued by the government press office, combining ceremonial declaration with an intervention about women’s public role. ([Napal, primary-source bibliography](https://repositoriodigital.uns.edu.ar/bitstream/123456789/2988/1/Napal%2C%20Ma.%20Celeste.%20Tesina.pdf))

- **1 May 1975:** *Discurso de la excelentísima Sra. Presidente de la Nación Doña María Estela Martínez de Perón desde el Congreso Nacional y mensaje al pueblo desde el balcón de la Casa de Gobierno*. A compound political event joining two speeches, two sites, and two audiences in one official publication; it resists classification as a single text because the pamphlet binds a legislative address to a mass-public balcony performance. ([Napal, primary-source bibliography](https://repositoriodigital.uns.edu.ar/bitstream/123456789/2988/1/Napal%2C%20Ma.%20Celeste.%20Tesina.pdf))

- **17 October 1975:** *Discurso pronunciado por la Excma. Sra. Presidente de la Nación, doña María Estela Martínez de Perón, desde el balcón histórico de la Casa de Gobierno, con motivo de la celebración del Día de la Lealtad*. A ritual balcony oration and official pamphlet whose meaning depends partly upon its performance at a foundational Peronist commemoration. ([Napal, primary-source bibliography](https://repositoriodigital.uns.edu.ar/bitstream/123456789/2988/1/Napal%2C%20Ma.%20Celeste.%20Tesina.pdf))

### Collected presidential discourse

- **8 July–12 September 1974:** *Discursos y mensajes de la presidente de la Nación, señora María Estela Martínez de Perón*. A 111-page official collection of presidential speeches and messages, not a new unitary composition. It overlaps with separately issued addresses above, so counting both the collection and every contained speech as independent works would confuse obra with edition or manifestation. ([CEDINPE scholarly bibliography](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/autores/2256/martinez_de_peron_maria_e); [publication record](https://books.google.com/books/about/Discursos_y_mensajes_de_la_presidente_de.html?id=AgUKAQAAIAAJ&output=html_text))

### Speeches preserved principally through newspapers

- **2 July 1974:** *Comunicado del Poder Ejecutivo*. A presidential communiqué preserved in *La Nueva Provincia*. Because the cited source is a newspaper extract and uses an institutional heading, individual authorship and the text’s exact completeness cannot be assumed. ([Napal, primary-source bibliography](https://repositoriodigital.uns.edu.ar/bitstream/123456789/2988/1/Napal%2C%20Ma.%20Celeste.%20Tesina.pdf))

- **10 July 1974:** *Señora Presidente*. A brief item preserved under a newspaper-supplied heading in *La Nueva Provincia*; the scholarly bibliography does not establish a fixed authorial title or whether the newspaper printed the complete utterance. ([Napal, primary-source bibliography](https://repositoriodigital.uns.edu.ar/bitstream/123456789/2988/1/Napal%2C%20Ma.%20Celeste.%20Tesina.pdf))

- **30 September 1974:** *Habló la presidente a los trabajadores del Frigorífico Nacional*. A workers’ address reported in *La Nueva Provincia* on 1 October. The heading describes an event rather than naming a securely bounded work, and completeness is uncertain. ([Napal, primary-source bibliography](https://repositoriodigital.uns.edu.ar/bitstream/123456789/2988/1/Napal%2C%20Ma.%20Celeste.%20Tesina.pdf))

- **12 October 1974:** *Visita el interior la jefa de Estado*. A speech or cluster of remarks from her provincial tour, preserved in the following day’s *La Nueva Provincia*. It resists treatment as a discrete work because the newspaper heading names the visit, not necessarily a single authored text. ([Napal, primary-source bibliography](https://repositoriodigital.uns.edu.ar/bitstream/123456789/2988/1/Napal%2C%20Ma.%20Celeste.%20Tesina.pdf))

- **13 October 1974:** *Nuevo mensaje presidencial*. A presidential message delivered during the northern provincial tour and preserved by *La Nueva Provincia*. The title is editorial, and the scholarly citation does not demonstrate that the printed version is complete. ([Napal, primary-source bibliography](https://repositoriodigital.uns.edu.ar/bitstream/123456789/2988/1/Napal%2C%20Ma.%20Celeste.%20Tesina.pdf))

- **13 October 1974:** *La oración presidencial*. A prayer or prayer-like presidential utterance reported by *La Nueva Provincia*. It is a performed devotional-political act rather than straightforwardly a literary work, and the newspaper supplied the surviving title. ([Napal, primary-source bibliography](https://repositoriodigital.uns.edu.ar/bitstream/123456789/2988/1/Napal%2C%20Ma.%20Celeste.%20Tesina.pdf))

- **17 October 1974:** *Habló la presidente ante la concentración popular*. A Día de la Lealtad balcony address surviving in a newspaper extract; neither that extract’s completeness nor an authorial title is established. ([Napal, primary-source bibliography](https://repositoriodigital.uns.edu.ar/bitstream/123456789/2988/1/Napal%2C%20Ma.%20Celeste.%20Tesina.pdf))

- **4 July 1975:** *Habló ayer la presidente*. A presidential speech printed by *La Nación* on 5 July. This is an editorial headline, not a secure title, and the bibliography does not establish whether the newspaper transcription is complete. ([Napal, primary-source bibliography](https://repositoriodigital.uns.edu.ar/bitstream/123456789/2988/1/Napal%2C%20Ma.%20Celeste.%20Tesina.pdf))

- **22 December 1975:** *Cumpliré con mi deber irrevocable*. A presidential declaration preserved under this headline by *La Nación* on 23 December. The phrase functions as a newspaper title, while the underlying speech or statement has no independently verified fixed title. ([Napal, primary-source bibliography](https://repositoriodigital.uns.edu.ar/bitstream/123456789/2988/1/Napal%2C%20Ma.%20Celeste.%20Tesina.pdf))

- **25 December 1975:** *Saludo de Navidad de la Presidente*. A Christmas message published by *La Nación* the following day. It is a ceremonial broadcast or statement preserved through a press manifestation, not securely a separately published authored work. ([Napal, primary-source bibliography](https://repositoriodigital.uns.edu.ar/bitstream/123456789/2988/1/Napal%2C%20Ma.%20Celeste.%20Tesina.pdf))

- **1 January 1976:** *Discurso de Isabel Perón en el Teatro Colón con motivo de la entrega de sables y espadas a los nuevos oficiales de las Fuerzas Armadas*. A ceremonial military address known through a later newspaper citation and Victoria Itzcovitz’s documentary study. Its long “title” is a scholarly description of the occasion, and the cited corpus does not establish textual completeness. ([Napal, primary-source bibliography](https://repositoriodigital.uns.edu.ar/bitstream/123456789/2988/1/Napal%2C%20Ma.%20Celeste.%20Tesina.pdf))

### Transnational and broadcast messages

- **26–31 January 1976:** *Mensaje de Isabel Perón a las mujeres salvadoreñas*. A message reproduced in an educational publication from El Salvador; the scholarly bibliography supplies a date range rather than a precise delivery date. It is a transnational political communication whose original medium is not conclusively identified. ([Napal, primary-source bibliography](https://repositoriodigital.uns.edu.ar/bitstream/123456789/2988/1/Napal%2C%20Ma.%20Celeste.%20Tesina.pdf))

- **10 March 1976:** *Presentación del Decreto n.º 906*. A radio event comprising Casildo Herrera’s speech, a reading of the decree establishing the Comisión Especial de las Remuneraciones, la Productividad y la Participación, and Martínez de Perón’s address. This is not wholly her work: it is a composite broadcast and institutional protocol in which her speech forms only one component. ([Napal, primary-source bibliography](https://repositoriodigital.uns.edu.ar/bitstream/123456789/2988/1/Napal%2C%20Ma.%20Celeste.%20Tesina.pdf))
