## Works by Giuseppe Balsamo

### Masonic system and institutional project

- **c. 1784–1789:** *Haute Maçonnerie égyptienne* (Egyptian Freemasonry). An initiatory system created and directed by Balsamo under the name Alessandro Cagliostro: part institution, part ritual theatre, part spiritual-regeneration project, and therefore best understood as a Gesamtkunstwerk rather than as a book. Its three degrees, ceremonies, catechisms, lodge organization, and admission of both men and women survive principally through ritual and inquisitorial manuscripts; the precise stages of its composition remain uncertain. ([Dictionary of Gnosis & Western Esotericism](https://ndl.ethernet.edu.et/handle/123456789/38998); [Trowbridge, *Cagliostro*](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/74618/74618-h/74618-h.htm))

- **1784:** *La Sagesse Triomphante*. The mother lodge founded at Lyon as the institutional manifestation of Egyptian Freemasonry. It is a collective body rather than a conventional “work,” but its organization, offices, ceremonies, and governing texts materialized Balsamo’s project. ([German National Library authority account and historical summary](https://d-nb.info/gnd/118518909); [Robert Amadou bibliography cited by Court de Gébelin](https://courtdegebelin.fmtl.fr/travaux/2017-cagliostro-et-la-haute-ma%C3%A7onnerie-%C3%A9gyptienne))

### Protocols, constitutions, and ritual texts

- **1784:** *Rituel de la Maçonnerie égyptienne*. A protocol-corpus for the three degrees of Egyptian apprentice, companion, and master, combining stage directions, prayers, initiatory actions, catechisms, theurgy, and schemes of moral and physical regeneration. The surviving text derives from manuscripts and later copies rather than an edition published by Balsamo; its internal parts should not be mistaken for separately authored books. ([BnF](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/affiner.do?afficheRegroup=false&critereRecherche=&index=TOUS3&listeAffinages=FacPays_fr%3BFacLocal_Lcl2DRdjRich&motRecherche=&nbResultParPage=10&numNotice=12546515&triResultParPage=5&trouveDansFiltre=NoticePUB&typeNotice=p); [Robert Amadou bibliography](https://courtdegebelin.fmtl.fr/travaux/2017-cagliostro-et-la-haute-ma%C3%A7onnerie-%C3%A9gyptienne))

- **24 December 1784:** *Statuts et règlements de la Respectable Loge de la Sagesse Triomphante*. The constitution and operating protocol of the Lyon mother lodge. Although issued under Balsamo’s authority and integral to his Masonic project, its formulation was institutional and may have involved lodge officers; exclusive personal authorship cannot safely be asserted. ([BnF authority bibliography](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb12546515g); [*Dictionary of Gnosis & Western Esotericism*](https://ndl.ethernet.edu.et/handle/123456789/38998))

### Judicial memoirs and petitions

- **1786:** *Mémoire pour le comte de Cagliostro, accusé, contre M. le procureur-général, accusateur, en présence de M. le cardinal de Rohan, de la comtesse de La Motte, et autres co-accusés*. A forensic manifesto in the Diamond Necklace Affair: it defends Balsamo, constructs his public identity, and acts upon the court and public rather than merely recounting events. It was prepared with and formally presented by his advocate Jean-Charles Thilorier, so it is a collaborative legal production, not an uncontested sole-authored autobiography. ([Google Books bibliographic record](https://books.google.com/books?id=6Wtlhca-M9MC); [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL2355191W/M%C3%A9moire_pour_le_comte_de_Cagliostro_accus%C3%A9))

- **24 February 1786:** *Requête au Parlement, les chambres assemblées, par le comte de Cagliostro*. A short procedural petition supplementing the preceding defence and seeking relief for his imprisoned wife. Like the other pleadings, it speaks in Balsamo’s name but belongs to the collaborative practice of his advocate Thilorier. ([ILAB catalogue description](https://ilab.org/assets/catalogues/catalogs_files_c184.pdf); [*Dictionary of Gnosis & Western Esotericism*](https://ndl.ethernet.edu.et/handle/123456789/38998))

- **1786:** *Requête à joindre au Mémoire du comte de Cagliostro*. A supplementary legal petition associated with Thilorier and the Necklace Affair defence. It is an operative court document, not an independent literary book, and its exact division of authorship between client and advocate is undocumented. ([Wellcome Collection](https://identity.wellcomecollection.org/works/brb338uk); [*Dictionary of Gnosis & Western Esotericism*](https://ndl.ethernet.edu.et/handle/123456789/38998))

- **1786:** *Mémoire pour le comte de Cagliostro, demandeur, contre Me Chesnon, le fils, commissaire au Châtelet de Paris, et le sieur de Launay, gouverneur de la Bastille*. A civil pleading concerning Balsamo’s arrest, confinement, and seized property. Catalogues associate both Balsamo and Thilorier with it; it should therefore be treated as a commissioned or collaborative legal obra rather than silently assigned to Balsamo alone. ([Wellcome Collection](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/qefzdxgz); [WorldCat record](https://search.worldcat.org/title/21303218))

- **1787:** *Réponse à la pièce importante dans l’affaire de Launay*. A polemical reply produced in the litigation against Bastille governor Bernard-René de Launay. It belongs to the Balsamo–Thilorier legal dossier; personal authorship by Balsamo is possible but not securely separable from his counsel’s work. ([Contemporary-pamphlet catalogue](https://www.bibliorare.com/cat-vent_varenne17-05-09-cat.pdf); [*Dictionary of Gnosis & Western Esotericism*](https://ndl.ethernet.edu.et/handle/123456789/38998))

- **1787:** *Requête au Roi pour le comte de Cagliostro contre le sieur Chesnon, fils, commissaire au Châtelet, et le sieur de Launay, gouverneur du château de la Bastille*. A seventy-two-page petition seeking royal intervention in the same dispute. It is a protocol of redress and public accusation, composed in Balsamo’s cause with legal assistance rather than an uncomplicated sole-authored work. ([Interencheres catalogue](https://media.interencheres.com/66/2018/02/19/180927_78a50c0261fcc8e05a6e04c05a2f0598.pdf); [*Dictionary of Gnosis & Western Esotericism*](https://ndl.ethernet.edu.et/handle/123456789/38998))

- **1787:** *Deuxième requête au Roi pour le comte de Cagliostro contre M. Chesnon*. A further royal petition in the Chesnon–de Launay litigation. It survives as part of the legal pamphlet sequence and has the same unresolved collaborative authorship as the other pleadings. ([*Dictionary of Gnosis & Western Esotericism*](https://ndl.ethernet.edu.et/handle/123456789/38998))

### Public letters and manifestos

- **1786:** *Lettre du comte de Cagliostro au peuple anglais, pour servir de suite à ses mémoires*. A public letter defending Balsamo against attacks published in Britain after the Necklace Affair. Its addressee and polemical circulation make it a manifesto of self-defence rather than private correspondence; contemporary attribution is to Cagliostro, although editorial mediation cannot be excluded. ([*Dictionary of Gnosis & Western Esotericism*](https://ndl.ethernet.edu.et/handle/123456789/38998))

- **20 June 1786; first known printing 1789:** *Lettre au peuple français, traduction d’une lettre écrite à M. … par M. le comte de Cagliostro*. A political-prophetic manifesto attacking ministerial despotism and anticipating the fall of the Bastille and abolition of *lettres de cachet*. The text claims a 1786 London date but appeared after the predicted events in 1789, making its date, textual history, and possibly its authenticity contested rather than securely prophetic. ([*Dictionary of Gnosis & Western Esotericism*](https://ndl.ethernet.edu.et/handle/123456789/38998); [Trowbridge, *Cagliostro*](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/74618/74618-h/74618-h.htm))

### Attributed works that resist inclusion

- **Manuscript, date uncertain:** *La Très Sainte Trinosophie*. An illustrated alchemical-initiation manuscript sometimes attributed to Cagliostro and sometimes to the comte de Saint-Germain. No firm documentary evidence establishes Balsamo as its maker, and it is absent from the standard scholarly bibliography of his writings; it is therefore an attribution, not a securely catalogued work by him. ([Getty Research Institute record](https://primo.getty.edu/permalink/01GRI_INST/19t5q1q/alma99100325144971551); [Manly P. Hall attribution summarized here](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Most_Holy_Trinosophia))

- **1788–1789 manuscript; published in 1910:** *Liber memorialis de Caleostro cum esset Roboreti*, later titled *L’Évangile de Cagliostro*. This is not a work made by Balsamo: it is an anonymous, partly ironic eyewitness account of his stay at Rovereto, later translated and published by Marc Haven. Its familiar modern title creates a misleading appearance of authorship and should be retained only as a rejected attribution. ([BnF catalogue](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/affiner.do?afficheRegroup=false&critereRecherche=&index=TOUS3&listeAffinages=FacPays_fr%3BFacLocal_Lcl2DRdjRich&motRecherche=&nbResultParPage=10&numNotice=12546515&triResultParPage=5&trouveDansFiltre=NoticePUB&typeNotice=p); [Marc Haven’s introduction](https://livres-mystiques.com/partieTEXTES/MarcHaven/Cagliostro1/Introduct.html))

- **1786:** *Mémoires authentiques pour servir à l’histoire du comte de Cagliostro*. Despite its title and first-person pose, this pamphlet is generally identified as a hostile fabrication by Jean-Pierre-Louis de Luchet, not a memoir made by Balsamo. Its resistance to inclusion is bibliographically important because editions and catalogues have repeatedly allowed the impersonated voice to become an author attribution. ([Trowbridge’s critical bibliography](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/74618/74618-h/74618-h.htm))
