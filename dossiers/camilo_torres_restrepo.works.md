## Works by Camilo Torres Restrepo

### Devotional writings and reflections on priesthood

- **1947–1954:** *Meditación sobre el sacerdocio*. A private seminary meditation: devotional writing rather than a publication intended as a public work. [The collected edition preserves it among Torres’s earliest writings.](https://ulibros.com/index.php/camilo-torres-restrepo-textos-ineditos-y-poco-conocidos-vol1-qtd8c.html)
- **Before 29 August 1954:** *Oración antes de la ordenación sacerdotal*. A personal prayer composed before his ordination; its status lies between text, spiritual exercise, and performed devotion. [It appears in both the expanded collected works and the 1985 compilation.](https://www.ensayistas.org/critica/liberacion/TL/documentos/camilotorres.pdf)
- **1954:** *Palabras a su madre con motivo de la ordenación sacerdotal*. A familial address made for the occasion of his ordination, preserved as a text but originally belonging to an oral and ceremonial setting. [Collected-works contents.](https://ulibros.com/index.php/camilo-torres-restrepo-textos-ineditos-y-poco-conocidos-vol1-qtd8c.html)

### Sociological studies, research projects, and teaching protocols

- **1956:** *Proyecto del Equipo Colombiano de Investigación socio-económica*. A research project in the strict sense: it establishes an intention and an institutional method rather than reporting completed findings. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **1956:** *Soluciones latinoamericanas*. A sociological-political essay proposing that Latin American problems require locally grounded analysis. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://es.scribd.com/document/863219808/Cristianismo-y-revolucio-n)
- **1956:** *Los problemas sociales en la universidad actual*. An essay or conference-text about the university’s social responsibilities. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **1956:** *El cristianismo es un humanismo integral*. A theological-sociological essay joining Christian anthropology to social action. [Expanded collected-works contents.](https://ulibros.com/index.php/camilo-torres-restrepo-textos-ineditos-y-poco-conocidos-vol1-qtd8c.html)
- **1956–1958:** *Círculos viciosos del subdesarrollo*. An analytical note or essay from the Louvain period. The accessible contents do not establish a more exact date. [Expanded collected-works contents.](https://ulibros.com/index.php/camilo-torres-restrepo-textos-ineditos-y-poco-conocidos-vol1-qtd8c.html)
- **1956–1958:** *Medios para la formación de equipos*. A protocol-like text concerned with forming research or action groups. [Expanded collected-works contents.](https://ulibros.com/index.php/camilo-torres-restrepo-textos-ineditos-y-poco-conocidos-vol1-qtd8c.html)
- **1956–1958:** *Equipo Colombiano de Investigaciones*. An institutional/research prospectus; the thing made is partly a proposed collective, not merely its written description. [Expanded collected-works contents.](https://ulibros.com/index.php/camilo-torres-restrepo-textos-ineditos-y-poco-conocidos-vol1-qtd8c.html)
- **1958:** *Plan de la memoria para la licenciatura*. A research plan for Torres’s licentiate thesis, valuable precisely as a project preceding the finished study. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://es.scribd.com/document/863219808/Cristianismo-y-revolucio-n)
- **1958:** *Bogotá, ciudad pre-industrial*. A sociological study of Bogotá’s urban and economic structure. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **1958:** *El nivel de vida de Bogotá: ensayo de metodología estadística*. A quantitative urban-sociology thesis, subsequently republished under other titles; those later books are editions of this made study, not additional works. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://es.scribd.com/document/863219808/Cristianismo-y-revolucio-n)
- **1958:** *Monografía: El nivel de vida de Bogotá*. A related monographic manifestation of the thesis research; the compilation lists it separately, although its boundary from the preceding study is editorially uncertain. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://es.scribd.com/document/863219808/Cristianismo-y-revolucio-n)
- **1958:** *Objetivos inmediatos del Equipo Colombiano Pro Estudio y Progreso (ECEP)*. A programmatic protocol for a proposed research-and-action collective. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **Date not established:** *Métodos de investigación científica*. A methodological teaching text rather than an independent monograph. [Expanded collected-works contents.](https://ulibros.com/index.php/camilo-torres-restrepo-textos-ineditos-y-poco-conocidos-vol1-qtd8c.html)
- **Date not established:** *Programa de Sociología*. A curriculum or course protocol: it instructs and organizes study rather than presenting a completed argument. [Expanded collected-works contents.](https://ulibros.com/index.php/camilo-torres-restrepo-textos-ineditos-y-poco-conocidos-vol1-qtd8c.html)
- **Date not established:** *Metodología de la investigación científica: guía de cátedra n.º 5*. A classroom guide and explicit protocol for research practice. [Expanded collected-works contents.](https://ulibros.com/index.php/camilo-torres-restrepo-textos-ineditos-y-poco-conocidos-vol1-qtd8c.html)
- **Date not established:** *Aclaraciones de terminología sociológica*. A technical glossary or teaching clarification, difficult to treat as a freestanding “work” outside its pedagogical use. [Expanded collected-works contents.](https://ulibros.com/index.php/camilo-torres-restrepo-textos-ineditos-y-poco-conocidos-vol1-qtd8c.html)
- **1961:** *La sociología en Colombia*. A disciplinary survey and intervention concerning Colombian sociology. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **1961:** *El problema de la estructuración de una auténtica sociología latinoamericana*. A programmatic disciplinary essay. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **1961:** *Un nuevo paso en la sociología latinoamericana*. A short disciplinary intervention. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **1961:** *Las escuelas radiofónicas de Sutatenza-Colombia*. A sociological study of rural radio education. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **1962:** *Investigación sobre la asimilación de la familia inmigrante a la ciudad*. A research design or report on rural-to-urban migration; its title makes the project character explicit. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **1962:** *Urbanización y reforma urbana*. A study prepared for the Seminario Nacional de los Equipos Universitarios de Colombia, therefore both scholarly paper and conference intervention. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, text and contents.](https://dokumen.pub/cristianismo-y-revolucion.html)
- **1963:** *La violencia y los cambios socio-culturales en las áreas rurales colombianas*. A substantial sociological study of political violence and rural social change. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **1963:** *Democracia en los países subdesarrollados*. A sociological-political essay on formal democracy and power. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **1964:** *Los grupos de presión en Colombia*. A transcribed round-table intervention; it is a collaborative oral manifestation, not a solely composed essay. [The compilation identifies its origin in a recorded AEXANDES discussion.](https://dokumen.pub/cristianismo-y-revolucion.html)
- **1964:** *Cómo ejercen el gobierno los grupos de opresión*. A political-sociological article or intervention on elite power. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **1964:** *La desintegración social en Colombia: “Se están gestando dos subculturas”*. A sociological diagnosis whose transmitted title combines a subject heading and journalistic formulation. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **Date not established:** *Factores externos que han influido sobre el sistema administrativo colombiano*. An administrative-sociology study. [Expanded collected-works contents.](https://ulibros.com/index.php/camilo-torres-restrepo-textos-ineditos-y-poco-conocidos-vol1-qtd8c.html)
- **Date not established:** *Reforma agraria como canal de ascenso social: el canal administrativo*. A study joining agrarian reform to administrative and mobility analysis. [Expanded collected-works contents.](https://ulibros.com/index.php/camilo-torres-restrepo-textos-ineditos-y-poco-conocidos-vol1-qtd8c.html)
- **Date not established:** *Las estructuras del subdesarrollo*. A sociological essay on structural underdevelopment. [Expanded collected-works contents.](https://ulibros.com/index.php/camilo-torres-restrepo-textos-ineditos-y-poco-conocidos-vol1-qtd8c.html)

A community-development project made collectively by Torres and his team; it resists reduction to a text because its principal medium was organized social action. - **Before 1956:** *Comentarios a publicaciones periodísticas universitarias*. Early commentary preserved without a secure public date; the plural heading may represent a dossier rather than one unified work. [Expanded collected-works contents.](https://ulibros.com/index.php/camilo-torres-restrepo-textos-ineditos-y-poco-conocidos-vol1-qtd8c.html)

- **Before 1956:** *Responsabilidad y posición política*. An early reflective or political note. [Expanded collected-works contents.](https://ulibros.com/index.php/camilo-torres-restrepo-textos-ineditos-y-poco-conocidos-vol1-qtd8c.html)
- **Before 1956:** *Temas universitarios*. A set of university reflections whose generic heading may be editorial rather than Torres’s fixed title. [Expanded collected-works contents.](https://ulibros.com/index.php/camilo-torres-restrepo-textos-ineditos-y-poco-conocidos-vol1-qtd8c.html)
- **1956–1958:** *¿Por qué Esprit?*. A note about the French personalist review and its intellectual orientation. [Expanded collected-works contents.](https://ulibros.com/index.php/camilo-torres-restrepo-textos-ineditos-y-poco-conocidos-vol1-qtd8c.html)
- **1961:** *Anatomía de la violencia*. A short sociological-political analysis. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **1961:** *¿El sacerdote es un brujo?*. A polemical reflection on priestly authority and popular perceptions of religion. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **1963:** *Además de política, ¿qué?*. The article to which Torres replied; it is by Alberto Baldoví Herrera and therefore is not a work by Torres, although the 1970 compilation reproduces it to supply the dialogic setting. Torres’s published reply to *Además de política, ¿qué?*; it is an intervention in a printed exchange rather than an isolated essay. [The edition dates and contextualizes the reply.](https://dokumen.pub/cristianismo-y-revolucion.html)
- **1963:** *El hombre bidimensional*. An essay joining spiritual and material dimensions of human life. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **1964:** *La ciencia y el diálogo*. An essay on scientific inquiry and intellectual or ideological dialogue. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **1964:** *La revolución, imperativo cristiano*. A conference-paper or essay whose title functions manifestically, asserting revolution as an obligation rather than merely describing it. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **1964:** *La universidad y el cambio social*. A public intervention on the university as an agent of structural change. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **1964:** *Llamamiento*. A call to action: a manifesto-form whose generic transmitted title gives no more specific addressee. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **1964:** *Crítica y autocrítica*. A political reflection or address. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **September 1964:** *Programación económica y exigencias apostólicas*. A paper presented at the second Pro Mundi Vita meeting in Louvain, joining development planning to Christian action. - **Date not established:** *La Iglesia, los cristianos y el cambio social en Latinoamérica*. A theological-political essay. [Expanded collected-works contents.](https://ulibros.com/index.php/camilo-torres-restrepo-textos-ineditos-y-poco-conocidos-vol1-qtd8c.html)

### Frente Unido: political project, newspaper, platforms, manifestos, and protocols

- **1964–1965:** *Movimiento de unidad popular / Frente Unido del Pueblo*. A political project intended to unite otherwise divided popular and opposition forces; the organization, its committees, platforms, meetings, and newspaper form a multimedia obra rather than a single text. [The biographical chronology describes the platform’s development and the creation of local committees.](https://www.ensayistas.org/critica/liberacion/TL/documentos/camilotorres.pdf)
- **1965:** *Carta* [opening the Frente Unido dossier]. A political letter whose addressee and full title are not supplied by the standard compilation’s contents; it should not be silently retitled. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **1965:** *Plataforma para un movimiento de unidad popular*. A manifesto and practical political protocol intended to establish minimum points of united action. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **1965:** *Plataforma del Frente Unido del pueblo colombiano*. A revised or organizational platform; the standard compilation treats it separately from the preceding platform, though their textual relationship should not be mistaken for two unrelated projects. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **26 August–21 October 1965:** *Frente Unido*. A weekly newspaper founded and politically led by Torres; it is a collective periodical creation, not a book by him, and only particular editorials and messages can securely be assigned to his hand. [The chronology dates its first issue and describes its role in creating the movement.](https://www.ensayistas.org/critica/liberacion/TL/documentos/camilotorres.pdf)
- **26 August 1965:** *Por qué no voy a las elecciones*. An editorial manifesto explaining electoral abstention. [The first issue and date are identified in the standard chronology and bibliography.](https://filosofia.org/ave/001/a230.htm)
- **26 August 1965:** *Mensaje a los cristianos*. A manifesto addressed to Christians, printed in the first issue of *Frente Unido*; it calls for action rather than merely describing belief. [Publication details.](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/camilo/cristianos.htm)
- **2 September 1965:** *Mensaje a los comunistas*. A manifesto defining both cooperation and disagreement between Torres and the Communist Party. [The original publication date and venue are documented here.](https://www.archivochile.cl/Homenajes/camilo/d/H_doc_de_CT-0025.pdf)
- **1965:** *Consignas*. A set of slogans or directives: compressed political protocol rather than discursive essay. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **9 September 1965:** *Mensaje a los militares*. A manifesto addressed to members of the armed forces. [The issue sequence is documented in the scholarly chronology.](https://filosofia.org/ave/001/a230.htm)
- **16 September 1965:** *Mensaje a los no alineados*. A manifesto aimed at people outside established parties. [The issue sequence is documented in the scholarly chronology.](https://filosofia.org/ave/001/a230.htm)
- **23 September 1965:** *Mensaje a los sindicalistas*. A manifesto and organizing appeal to trade unionists. [The issue sequence is documented in the scholarly chronology.](https://filosofia.org/ave/001/a230.htm)
- **1965:** *Saludo a los estudiantes de la Universidad Central de Venezuela*. A transnational political greeting; an occasional address rather than an essay. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **7 October 1965:** *Mensaje a los campesinos*. A manifesto directed to rural workers and communities. [The issue sequence is documented in the scholarly chronology.](https://filosofia.org/ave/001/a230.htm)
- **1965:** *Unión y organización de la base popular*. An unsigned or editorially headed *Frente Unido* editorial attributed to Torres in the standard compilation; periodical production makes individual authorship less transparent than a signed message. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **1965:** *¿María Helena de Crovo causa problemas al parlamento colombiano?*. A topical newspaper article included among Torres’s *Frente Unido* writings. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **14 October 1965:** *Mensaje a las mujeres*. A manifesto addressed to women. [The issue sequence is documented in the scholarly chronology.](https://filosofia.org/ave/001/a230.htm)
- **1965:** *El Frente Unido del pueblo*. A newspaper editorial; as with other unsigned editorials, its attribution is mediated by the later compilers. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **21 October 1965:** *Mensaje a los estudiantes*. A manifesto addressed to students. [The issue sequence is documented in the scholarly chronology.](https://filosofia.org/ave/001/a230.htm)
- **1965:** *Los comandos del Frente Unido*. A newspaper editorial and organizing protocol for local committees. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)

### Attributed works, collective documents, and exclusions

- **1961–1962:** *Acción Cultural Popular: “El Campesino”*. This is a correspondence dossier, not one work by Torres: only his letter to Monseñor Jorge Monastoque is securely his, while the collected sequence also includes letters written by Monastoque and José Joaquín Salcedo. [*Cristianismo y revolución*, contents.](https://es.scribd.com/document/863219808/Cristianismo-y-revolucio-n)
- **1964:** *Principios programáticos del Ejército de Liberación Nacional*. A collective ELN protocol included for context in Torres compilations, not securely an individually authored work by Torres. [The standard compilation places it in the ELN section rather than establishing personal authorship.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **7 January 1965:** *Manifiesto de Simacota*. A collective ELN manifesto issued before Torres publicly joined the organization; it should not be reassigned to him merely because a collected-works edition reproduces it. [The standard compilation lists it as an ELN document.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **28 October 1965:** *Mensaje a los desempleados*. Published under Torres’s name, but the 1970 compilers state that it was written in Bogotá by Frente Unido colleagues; it therefore resists classification as his work. [The attribution problem is summarized from the compilers’ appendix.](https://www.opuslibros.org/Index_libros/Recensiones_1/torres_cri.htm)
- **18 November 1965:** *Mensaje a los presos políticos*. Published under Torres’s name, but the 1970 compilers attribute its composition to Frente Unido colleagues in Bogotá. [Attribution discussion.](https://www.opuslibros.org/Index_libros/Recensiones_1/torres_cri.htm)
- **1965:** *Mensaje al Frente Unido del pueblo*. Published or circulated under Torres’s name, but identified by the 1970 compilers as one of three messages written by colleagues in Bogotá. [Attribution discussion.](https://www.opuslibros.org/Index_libros/Recensiones_1/torres_cri.htm)
- **9 December 1965:** *Mensaje a la oligarquía*. Traditionally attributed to Torres and printed under his name, but the 1970 compilers expressly doubt that he wrote it; it must remain an attributed work, not a settled one. [The compilers’ doubt is reported here; the publication date is independently documented.](https://www.opuslibros.org/Index_libros/Recensiones_1/torres_cri.htm)
- **1959:** *Camilo Torres y su equipo de trabajo obtienen el Premio Nacional de Beneficencia*. This collected-works heading records a reception event, not a text made by Torres; the made thing behind it is the collective Tunjuelito project already listed above. [Expanded collected-works contents.](https://ulibros.com/index.php/camilo-torres-restrepo-textos-ineditos-y-poco-conocidos-vol1-qtd8c.html)
- **1965:** *Declaraciones and letters by Cardinal Luis Concha Córdoba and the bishops of Medellín*. These documents are reproduced in Torres’s church-conflict dossier but were made by his ecclesiastical opponents, not by Torres. [The standard contents distinguish their authorship.](https://archivo-obrero.com/camilo-torres-cristianismo-y-revolucion/)
- **After 15 February 1966:** *Carta de Isabel Restrepo; La madre de Camilo se dirige al presidente Lleras Restrepo; Que entreguen el cuerpo de mi hijo*. These appear in the expanded edition’s documentary apparatus but were made by Torres’s mother after his death, not by Torres. [Expanded collected-works contents.](https://ulibros.com/index.php/camilo-torres-restrepo-textos-ineditos-y-poco-conocidos-vol1-qtd8c.html)
