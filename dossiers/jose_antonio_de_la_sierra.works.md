## Works by José Antonio de la Sierra

### Assayed coinage

The objects below are collaborative mint products rather than individually authored artworks. Sierra, formerly the Potosí mint’s *fundidor mayor*, became *ensayador mayor* on 15 May 1813; his “J” records responsibility for the metal’s legally prescribed fineness, not authorship of the design or engraving. Pedro Benavides cut the dies, while the imagery was prescribed by the Assembly of the Year XIII. Older scholarship misidentified “J” as Juan Palomo y Sierra; Arnaldo Cunietti-Ferrando established the attribution to José Antonio de Sierra. ([Pezzano, pp. 16–18](https://monedasuruguay.com/bib/p/pezzano.pdf); [Cunietti-Ferrando](https://www.monedasuruguay.com/bib/c/cunietti.pdf))

- **1813:** *Eight-escudo coin of the Provincias del Río de la Plata, Potosí J*. A gold coin type bearing Sierra’s assay mark; only a few specimens are known. It is a collective, mechanically multiplied obra—not a design by Sierra. ([Banco de la Nación Argentina catalogue](https://www.bna.com.ar/Downloads/20220208MonedasdelaRepublicaArgentinaVolumenI.pdf); [Eliasberg collection catalogue, lot 1001](https://media.stacksbowers.com/VirtualCatalogs/CatalogLibrary/louiseeliasbergs2005amer.pdf))

- **1813:** *Four-escudo coin of the Provincias del Río de la Plata, Potosí J*. Contemporary and later summaries include this denomination in the authorized gold series, but specialist accounts report no probative surviving specimen. It therefore resists classification as an extant work: it may represent an intended or undocumented issue rather than a demonstrably struck coin. ([“Unidad monetaria, vertebración,” pp. 239–240](https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/1018781.pdf); [Bolivian Numismatic Circle account](https://www.bolivian.com/cnm/mrplata.html))

- **1813:** *Two-escudo coin of the Provincias del Río de la Plata, Potosí J*. A seven-gram gold coin type catalogued as unique; Sierra assayed the metal, while its physical production and imagery were collective institutional work. ([Banco de la Nación Argentina catalogue](https://www.bna.com.ar/Downloads/20220208MonedasdelaRepublicaArgentinaVolumenI.pdf))

- **1813:** *One-escudo coin of the Provincias del Río de la Plata, Potosí J*. A 3.3-gram gold coin type marked “J”; the cited catalogue records only two known specimens. ([Banco de la Nación Argentina catalogue](https://www.bna.com.ar/Downloads/20220208MonedasdelaRepublicaArgentinaVolumenI.pdf))

- **1813:** *Eight-real coin of the Provincias del Río de la Plata, Potosí J*. A silver coin type issued with laureled edge; the catalogue distinguishes the ordinary issue, an inverted-reverse variety, and a chain-edge variety. These are production variants of one assayed type, not separate designs by Sierra. ([Banco de la Nación Argentina catalogue](https://www.bna.com.ar/Downloads/20220208MonedasdelaRepublicaArgentinaVolumenI.pdf); [Pezzano](https://monedasuruguay.com/bib/p/pezzano.pdf))

- **1813:** *Four-real coin of the Provincias del Río de la Plata, Potosí J*. A silver coin type bearing Sierra’s assay initial; surviving pieces include minting irregularities such as double striking, but those accidents are manifestations of production rather than separately authored works. ([Banco de la Nación Argentina catalogue](https://www.bna.com.ar/Downloads/20220208MonedasdelaRepublicaArgentinaVolumenI.pdf); [1813 coin survey](https://monedaspatrias.blogspot.com/p/acunacion-de-1813.html))

- **1813:** *Two-real coin of the Provincias del Río de la Plata, Potosí J*. A silver coin type catalogued under the “J” assay, with the common patriotic sun-and-arms scheme prescribed for the series. ([Banco de la Nación Argentina catalogue](https://www.bna.com.ar/Downloads/20220208MonedasdelaRepublicaArgentinaVolumenI.pdf); [CoinArchives specimen record](https://www.coinarchives.com/w/results.php?results=100&search=sierra))

- **1813:** *One-real coin of the Provincias del Río de la Plata, Potosí J*. A silver denomination belonging to Sierra’s assayed series. Its existence is established by the specialist national catalogue; individual striking errors do not constitute securely intentional additional works. ([Banco de la Nación Argentina catalogue](https://www.bna.com.ar/Downloads/20220208MonedasdelaRepublicaArgentinaVolumenI.pdf))

- **1813:** *Half-real coin of the Provincias del Río de la Plata, Potosí J*. The smallest silver denomination in the 1813 series, bearing Sierra’s assay mark. As with the other coins, “made by Sierra” means certified under his office, not personally engraved or individually fashioned by him. ([Banco de la Nación Argentina catalogue](https://www.bna.com.ar/Downloads/20220208MonedasdelaRepublicaArgentinaVolumenI.pdf); [“Unidad monetaria, vertebración,” pp. 239–240](https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/1018781.pdf))

### Assay documentation

- **31 May 1813:** *Certificate concerning silver bars introduced into the Potosí mint*. An administrative assay certificate signed by Sierra as *Ensayador Mayor*, preserved as AHCNMP, CRM-2153. This is a protocol-like documentary act—certifying material and official responsibility—rather than a literary text; the published study reproduces it but does not supply a formal contemporary title. ([Pezzano, fig. 6 and notes 43–44](https://monedasuruguay.com/bib/p/pezzano.pdf); [*El Reverso*, no. 75, p. 6](https://www.monedasuruguay.com/bib/rev/rev075.pdf))
