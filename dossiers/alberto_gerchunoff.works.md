## Works by Alberto Gerchunoff

### Narrative works and literary miscellanies

- **1910:** *Los gauchos judíos*. A cycle of twenty-four prose narratives and costumbrista tableaux about Jewish agricultural colonists in Entre Ríos, rather than a single novel; some pieces had appeared separately in periodicals in 1909–1910, and later editions enlarged the original collection. ([Senkman](https://www.autoresdeconcordia.com.ar/articulos/416/print); [UNPSJB contents record](https://sistema-biblio.unp.edu.ar/cgi-bin/koha/opac-detail.pl?biblionumber=763&query_desc=))
- **1919:** *Cuentos de ayer*. A booklet collecting three previously written stories: “El día de las grandes ganancias,” “El breviario del padre Albornoz,” and “El ciclo heroico.” ([1919 edition and contents](https://es.wikisource.org/wiki/%C3%8Dndice%3ACuentos_de_ayer_-_Alberto_Gerchunoff.pdf); [2023 critical collected-works contents](https://eduner.uner.edu.ar/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Alberto-Gerchunoff_Obra-literaria_Indice-y-extracto.pdf))
- **1925:** *La asamblea de la bohardilla*. A heterogeneous sequence of imaginary debates, dialogues, satirical encounters, and short prose pieces; its instability between story, essay, and dramatic conversation is intrinsic to the work. The title is frequently corrupted in catalogues as *boardilla*, but the critical edition prints *bohardilla*. ([critical collected edition](https://eduner.uner.edu.ar/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Alberto-Gerchunoff_Obra-literaria_Indice-y-extracto.pdf))
- **1926:** *Historias y proezas de amor*. A collection of six extended narratives and dialogues about love, including historical and mock-exemplary tales. ([critical collected edition](https://eduner.uner.edu.ar/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Alberto-Gerchunoff_Obra-literaria_Indice-y-extracto.pdf); [Feierstein volume](https://la-periferica.com.ar/public/978-987-24885-7-4.pdf))
- **1926:** *El hombre que habló en la Sorbona*. A literary miscellany of travel impressions, cultural criticism, urban sketches, and imaginative prose—not one continuous narrative. ([critical collected edition](https://eduner.uner.edu.ar/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Alberto-Gerchunoff_Obra-literaria_Indice-y-extracto.pdf))
- **1926 or 1927:** *Pequeñas prosas*. Ten brief lyrical and reflective prose pieces. Bibliographies disagree between 1926 and 1927, so the date cannot safely be reduced to one year. ([critical collected edition](https://eduner.uner.edu.ar/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Alberto-Gerchunoff_Obra-literaria_Indice-y-extracto.pdf); [Szurmuk](https://ri.conicet.gov.ar/bitstream/handle/11336/198705/CONICET_Digital_Nro.32b6c8d3-970b-4b8d-8e21-6cee16a5c433_B.pdf?sequence=2))
- **1934:** *El hombre importante*. A political satire presented as a novel, directed at the empty prestige and studied evasiveness of a Latin American public man; an “Advertencia pertinente” forms part of the constructed object. ([critical collected edition](https://eduner.uner.edu.ar/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Alberto-Gerchunoff_Obra-literaria_Indice-y-extracto.pdf); [Historia Hoy](https://historiahoy.com.ar/gerchunoff-alberto-1884-1950-n1478))
- **1937:** *La clínica del doctor Mefistófeles: moderna milagrería en diez jornadas*. A Faustian satirical fiction organized into ten “jornadas”; its dialogue and episodic architecture make it resemble a dramatic work without resolving cleanly into theatre. ([critical collected edition](https://eduner.uner.edu.ar/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Alberto-Gerchunoff_Obra-literaria_Indice-y-extracto.pdf))
- **Before 1950; collected 2023:** *Misceláneas cotidianas y sensibles*. A modern editorial grouping—not a book constituted by Gerchunoff—of ten dispersed short pieces, including “La fiesta del río,” “Problemas simples,” “Canciones infantiles,” and “Pipa y pipas.” It resists treatment as a single obra because the unity and title belong to the collected-works editors. ([critical collected edition](https://eduner.uner.edu.ar/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Alberto-Gerchunoff_Obra-literaria_Indice-y-extracto.pdf))

### Cervantine works

- **1913:** *Nuestro Señor Don Quijote*. A public conference delivered on 23 April 1913 and published the following month: simultaneously an oratorical event and a printed essay. ([Stella Longo](https://cvc.cervantes.es/literatura/cervantistas/congresos/cg_2006/cg_2006_84.pdf))
- **1916:** *El convivio: Nuestro Señor Don Quijote*. A separate Costa Rican manifestation of the 1913 conference, issued as the inaugural number of Joaquín García Monge’s El Convivio series; it is the same core text in a newly constituted editorial object, not an independent composition. ([Repertorio Americano notice](https://repositorio.una.ac.cr/bitstreams/30e40cc2-8758-4236-84a1-c60318a61403/download); [Feierstein volume](https://la-periferica.com.ar/public/978-987-24885-7-4.pdf))
- **1921:** *El viejo amigo Don Miguel de Cervantes Saavedra*. A Cervantine essay or lecture, subsequently situated by scholars within Gerchunoff’s continuing sequence of Quixotic meditations. ([Feierstein volume](https://la-periferica.com.ar/public/978-987-24885-7-4.pdf); [successive-works bibliography](https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/8259850.pdf))
- **1922:** *La jofaina maravillosa: agenda cervantina*. A composite book incorporating the earlier *Nuestro Señor Don Quijote* alongside “Perfiles cervantinos,” an epilogue, and “Acotaciones”; it is therefore both a new obra and a recomposition of prior manifestations. ([contemporary notice and contents](https://repositorio.una.ac.cr/bitstreams/30e40cc2-8758-4236-84a1-c60318a61403/download); [Bujaldón](https://bdigital.uncuyo.edu.ar/objetos_digitales/15008/00-revista-completa-22.pdf))
- **1932:** *Los amores de Baruj Spinoza*. A literary-philosophical essay or imaginative biographical meditation, usually grouped with Gerchunoff’s Cervantine and humanist prose rather than classified as documentary biography. ([Feierstein volume](https://la-periferica.com.ar/public/978-987-24885-7-4.pdf); [Szurmuk](https://ri.conicet.gov.ar/bitstream/handle/11336/198705/CONICET_Digital_Nro.32b6c8d3-970b-4b8d-8e21-6cee16a5c433_B.pdf?sequence=2))
- **1951, posthumous:** *Retorno a Don Quijote*. An editorially assembled posthumous volume of Cervantine and literary essays, with a prologue by Jorge Luis Borges; Gerchunoff made the constituent texts, but not necessarily the final book-object in the form published. ([Szurmuk](https://ri.conicet.gov.ar/bitstream/handle/11336/198705/CONICET_Digital_Nro.32b6c8d3-970b-4b8d-8e21-6cee16a5c433_B.pdf?sequence=2); [Encyclopedia.com](https://www.encyclopedia.com/humanities/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/gerchunoff-alberto-1884-1950))

### Political, religious, biographical, and national essays

- **1914:** *Autobiografía*. A short autobiographical text rather than a full memoir; later anthologists republished it, and its independent bibliographical status is less secure than that of Gerchunoff’s books. ([Enciclo biobibliography](https://itesm.enciclo.es/articulo/ibero/alberto-gerchunoff); [Szurmuk’s discussion](https://ri.conicet.gov.ar/bitstream/handle/11336/198705/CONICET_Digital_Nro.32b6c8d3-970b-4b8d-8e21-6cee16a5c433_B.pdf?sequence=2))
- **1918:** *El nuevo régimen*. A collection or extended work of political and social criticism, not fiction. ([Szurmuk](https://ri.conicet.gov.ar/bitstream/handle/11336/198705/CONICET_Digital_Nro.32b6c8d3-970b-4b8d-8e21-6cee16a5c433_B.pdf?sequence=2); [Feierstein volume](https://la-periferica.com.ar/public/978-987-24885-7-4.pdf))
- **1924:** *El cristianismo precristiano*. A religious-historical essay connecting Jewish scripture and ethics with Christianity. Some derivative lists conjecture 1920, but the scholarly oeuvre chronology gives 1924. ([Szurmuk](https://ri.conicet.gov.ar/bitstream/handle/11336/198705/CONICET_Digital_Nro.32b6c8d3-970b-4b8d-8e21-6cee16a5c433_B.pdf?sequence=2); [Feierstein volume](https://la-periferica.com.ar/public/978-987-24885-7-4.pdf))
- **1925:** *Nuestros escritores: Roberto J. Payró*. A compact critical-biographical study of the Argentine writer Roberto J. Payró. ([Szurmuk](https://ri.conicet.gov.ar/bitstream/handle/11336/198705/CONICET_Digital_Nro.32b6c8d3-970b-4b8d-8e21-6cee16a5c433_B.pdf?sequence=2); [Feierstein volume](https://la-periferica.com.ar/public/978-987-24885-7-4.pdf))
- **1927 or 1929:** *Enrique Heine: el poeta de nuestra intimidad*. A critical-biographical essay on Heinrich Heine. Bibliographical accounts give both 1927 and 1929, apparently reflecting different manifestations or an inherited dating error; the discrepancy remains unresolved. ([Encyclopedia.com](https://www.encyclopedia.com/humanities/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/gerchunoff-alberto-1884-1950); [Szurmuk](https://ri.conicet.gov.ar/bitstream/handle/11336/198705/CONICET_Digital_Nro.32b6c8d3-970b-4b8d-8e21-6cee16a5c433_B.pdf?sequence=2))
- **1931:** *Las imágenes del país*. A sequence of Argentine landscapes, portraits, and cultural impressions—part travel writing, part national interpretation, part literary sketch. Earlier bibliographical notices sometimes give 1921, but the standard oeuvre chronology places the book in 1931. ([Szurmuk](https://ri.conicet.gov.ar/bitstream/handle/11336/198705/CONICET_Digital_Nro.32b6c8d3-970b-4b8d-8e21-6cee16a5c433_B.pdf?sequence=2); [Encyclopedia.com](https://www.encyclopedia.com/humanities/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/gerchunoff-alberto-1884-1950))
- **1945:** *El problema judío*. A political-historical intervention written in the aftermath of Nazism and the Shoah and advocating a Jewish national home; as an intervention intended to alter public judgment, it approaches a manifesto rather than merely describing a “problem.” ([Encyclopedia.com](https://www.encyclopedia.com/humanities/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/gerchunoff-alberto-1884-1950); [Historia Hoy](https://historiahoy.com.ar/gerchunoff-alberto-1884-1950-n1478))
- **1950:** *Buenos Aires, la metrópoli de mañana*. An illustrated civic projection of the future city, first associated with an illustrated periodical presentation and issued as a municipal booklet. Records also give 1960, evidently for a later edition; the work itself is documented in 1950. ([Buenos Aires municipal catalogue](https://catalogobibliotecas.buenosaires.gob.ar/pergamo/opac.php?a=bsqMateria&n=CUADERNOS+DE+BUENOS+AIRES); [historical study of the illustrated text](https://www.scielo.sa.cr/scielo.php?pid=S2215-26362025000200030&script=sci_arttext))
- **1950:** *Entre Ríos, mi país*. An autobiographical and regional book in which personal memory, family history, landscape, and national reflection are inseparable; it is memoir but not a continuous life narrative. ([Encyclopedia.com](https://www.encyclopedia.com/religion/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/gerchunoff-alberto); [Historia Hoy](https://historiahoy.com.ar/gerchunoff-alberto-1884-1950-n1478))
- **1952, posthumous:** *Argentina, país de advenimiento*. A posthumously constituted collection of essays on Argentina as a country of immigration and futurity; Gerchunoff made its prose, while the final book-object was assembled after his death. ([Szurmuk](https://ri.conicet.gov.ar/bitstream/handle/11336/198705/CONICET_Digital_Nro.32b6c8d3-970b-4b8d-8e21-6cee16a5c433_B.pdf?sequence=2); [BnF-derived record](https://www.viaouest.com/auteurs-bnf.php?id=9198))
- **1952, posthumous:** *El pino y la palmera*. A posthumous collection of stories and reflective prose. Its title names a book assembled after Gerchunoff’s death, so the authorship of the individual texts should not be confused with responsibility for their final selection and arrangement. ([Encyclopedia.com](https://www.encyclopedia.com/religion/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/gerchunoff-alberto); [Szurmuk](https://ri.conicet.gov.ar/bitstream/handle/11336/198705/CONICET_Digital_Nro.32b6c8d3-970b-4b8d-8e21-6cee16a5c433_B.pdf?sequence=2))

### Projects and works that resist book-form classification

- **Announced by 1910; unfinished or unpublished:** *Tierra de Sión*. A projected novel advertised with *Los conversos* in the first edition of *Los gauchos judíos*. A 1919 editorial notice says Gerchunoff did not bring either announced novel to publication; the project, rather than a recoverable finished text, is therefore the obra that can responsibly be named. ([1919 notice](https://es.wikisource.org/wiki/Cuentos_de_ayer))
- **Announced by 1910; reportedly still being completed in 1919:** *Los conversos*. A projected novel advertised in *Los gauchos judíos*. The 1919 notice simultaneously says it had not been written/published and announces its “completion,” leaving its ultimate manuscript status uncertain; it should not be silently promoted into a finished book. ([1919 notice](https://es.wikisource.org/wiki/Cuentos_de_ayer))
- **1900s–1950:** *Periodical journalism, lectures, speeches, and uncollected essays*. This is a vast body of made texts rather than one obra: the standard bibliography records roughly 3,000 articles and essays, including unsigned pieces identified with help from Gerchunoff’s son-in-law Manuel Kantor. Without reproducing Gover de Nasatsky’s item-level bibliography, it would be misleading either to call this corpus a single book or to pretend that the familiar book list exhausts it. ([Jewish Virtual Library](https://jewishvirtuallibrary.org/jsource/judaica/ejud_0002_0007_0_07194.html); [Kahan on Gover de Nasatsky’s exhaustive survey](https://ri.conicet.gov.ar/bitstream/handle/11336/3767/CONICET_Digital_Nro.4959_A.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=2))
- **Dates dispersed:** *Public conferences subsequently absorbed into books*. Several Gerchunoff books are assemblages of essays first printed in newspapers or magazines and texts first delivered orally; this is not an additional title but a recurrent mode of manifestation in which lecture, article, and book chapter are distinct appearances of the same made text. ([Bujaldón](https://bdigital.uncuyo.edu.ar/objetos_digitales/15008/00-revista-completa-22.pdf); [1913 conference study](https://cvc.cervantes.es/literatura/cervantistas/congresos/cg_2006/cg_2006_84.pdf))
