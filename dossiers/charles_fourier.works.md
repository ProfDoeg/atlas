## Works by Francois Marie Charles Fourier

### Journal projects, poems, letters, and early interventions

- **1800:** *Journal de Lyon et du département du Rhône: Prospectus*. Prospectus for a projected newspaper planned with Martainville; the periodical itself never appeared, so the project rather than a completed journal is the obra. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1801–1802:** *Articles in the Journal de Lyon et du Midi*. A body of newspaper contributions whose individual titles are not supplied by the standard bibliography; their uncertain extent resists treatment as a securely enumerated work. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1803:** *Satire envoyée à M. A. J.*** sur son énigme…*. A satirical poem published under the name “Fourrier” in the *Bulletin de Lyon*. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1803:** *Réplique à Clotilde D****. A verse reply in the *Bulletin de Lyon*. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1803:** *Harmonie universelle*. A short newspaper announcement of Fourier’s purported discovery of universal unity—part declaration, part manifesto for the lifelong project that followed. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1803:** *Ode à Madame A. F., reconnue*. A poem in the *Bulletin de Lyon*. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1803:** *Clôture des débats sur la satire F. Stances aux Lyonnaises…*. A verse intervention in a local newspaper controversy. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1803:** *Triumvirat continental et paix perpétuelle sous trente ans*. A geopolitical newspaper article and political project, later absorbed into the collected works. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1803:** *Le Ballon de Zambeccari, qui fait trois cents lieues d’un seul trait*. A topical newspaper article. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1803:** *Lettre au grand juge*. An open political letter, subsequently republished separately; its addressee and performative form make it an intervention rather than a treatise. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1804:** *À l’auteur de l’Inventaire des plaisirs de Lyon*. A newspaper response. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1804:** *Acceptation des lettres de change*. A commercial-legal newspaper note. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1803:** *Sur les empires qui ont des vapeurs comme les jolies femmes*. A political squib in the *Journal de Lyon, Nouvelles de la France et de l’étranger*. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1803:** *Lettre à Monsieur Devror*. A newspaper letter. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1803:** *Invitation aux échos*. A brief newspaper intervention. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1807 or 1808:** *Sur les charlataneries commerciales*. A short commercial polemic or pamphlet; the original printing date is uncertain, and the later Fourierist editors reported possessing only one copy. [Bibliography and publication note](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/french-texts/charles-fourier-sur-les-charlataneries-commerciales-1807-1808/)
- **1811:** *Réponse à l’article signé “Philoharmonicos”*. A newspaper reply later recovered in the posthumous *Publication des manuscrits*. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1831:** *Lettre aux rédacteurs du journal Le Globe*. An open letter dated 2 September and published on 19 October; it intervenes in the dispute between Fourierism and Saint-Simonism. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)

### Books, pamphlets, protocols, and announcements published during Fourier’s life

- **1808:** *Théorie des quatre mouvements et des destinées générales: prospectus et annonce de la découverte*. An anonymously issued book, but deliberately styled a “prospectus and announcement”: simultaneously speculative cosmology, social theory, manifesto, and advertisement for a much larger unfinished science. [Collected-works description](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL33021310M/OEuvres_compl%C3%A8tes_de_Charles_Fourier)
- **1822:** *Traité de l’association domestique-agricole*. A two-volume but unfinished social and industrial treatise; later editors republished and reorganized it as *Théorie de l’unité universelle*, so the two titles should not be counted as wholly separate creations. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1823:** *Sommaire de la théorie d’association domestique-agricole, ou Attraction industrielle*. A concise guide to the larger treatise. A nearly identical title reported as *Sommaire du Traité…* may denote the same object with additions rather than another independent work. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1823:** *Sommaires et annonce du Traité de l’association domestique-agricole*. An extended synopsis and promotional announcement continuing the pagination of the *Traité*; it operates as both epitome and manifesto. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1823:** *Supplément à l’avant-propos*. A separately paginated addition to the *Traité*, not securely an autonomous obra. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1823:** *Antienne du chapitre III*. An inserted supplement to the *Sommaires et annonce*, resisting classification as a freestanding work. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1823:** *Appendice aux conclusions*. An unpaginated appended sheet attached to the *Sommaires et annonce*. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1823:** *Sur les banques rurales*. A separately paginated addition proposing rural financial institutions. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1823:** *Ordre de lecture préparatoire* / *Direction pour les trois classes de lecteurs*. A tiny reading protocol telling different audiences how to enter the larger theory; the variant title is editorial. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1823:** *Instructions pour le vendeur et l’acheteur*. A publishing and reading protocol originally pasted inside copies of the treatise, not a conventional essay. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1824:** *Mnémonique géographique, ou Méthode pour apprendre en peu de leçons la géographie, la statistique et la politique*. An anonymously printed pedagogical protocol employing analogy and comparison; later reprinted among the manuscripts. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1829:** *Le Nouveau Monde industriel et sociétaire, ou Invention du procédé d’industrie attrayante et naturelle distribuée en séries passionnées*. A systematic exposition and practical project for associative society; Fourier nevertheless presented only a fraction of his manuscript system. [Fourier archive](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/fourier/index.htm)
- **1830:** *Le Nouveau Monde industriel… Livret d’annonce*. An 88-page advertisement and digest attached by continuous pagination to the 1829 book; it is a promotional manifestation of that project rather than a wholly separate theory. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1831:** *Pièges et charlatanisme des deux sectes Saint-Simon et Owen, qui promettent l’association et le progrès*. A polemical pamphlet and manifesto urging an immediate experimental association. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1835–1836:** *La Fausse Industrie morcelée, répugnante, mensongère, et l’antidote, l’industrie naturelle, combinée, attrayante, véridique*. A two-volume polemical treatise, conceived as a “mosaic” rather than a linear system; publication stopped with Fourier’s death approaching, and it does not complete the promised science. [Collected-works description](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL33021310M/OEuvres_compl%C3%A8tes_de_Charles_Fourier)

### Designs and projects embedded in the manuscripts

- **1803–1806:** *Formation d’une phalange d’attraction*. An extended manuscript design for a settlement, including its palace, rue-galerie, exchanges, gardens, education, ceremonies, kitchens, and daily routines. It is a Gesamtkunstwerk-like social project joining architecture, agriculture, theatre, food, pedagogy, and government, but Fourier never built it. [Manuscript synopsis](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **Date unknown:** *The phalanstère*. Not one executed building or a single fixed drawing, but a repeatedly revised architectural-social protocol distributed across Fourier’s books and manuscripts; later images and realized communities made by disciples must not be attributed to Fourier as objects he built.
- **Date unknown:** *The phalange or attractive association*. Fourier’s central collective project: a designed body of roughly 1,600–1,800 people organized into passionate series. It remained an intention and protocol in his lifetime rather than a creation that actually appeared.
- **Date unknown:** *The system of universal analogy and attraction*. A lifelong, unfinished total-science project spanning cosmology, psychology, economics, sexuality, education, architecture, gastronomy, music, and planetary history. Because it refuses the separation of these media and disciplines, it is closer to a conceptual Gesamtkunstwerk than to a single book.

### Posthumous editorial creations that should not be mistaken for new Fourier works

- **1841–1845:** *Œuvres complètes de Charles Fourier*, first series. A six-volume edition produced by Fourier’s disciples from publications and manuscripts; it preserves Fourier’s work but its organization, editorial titles, and claim to completeness are not his. [Edition contents](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1843:** *Théorie de l’unité universelle*. The editorially retitled and expanded republication of the 1822 *Traité de l’association domestique-agricole*, not an independently composed later work. [Edition history](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL3748928W/%C5%92uvres_compl%C3%A8tes_de_Ch._Fourier_..)
- **1847:** *Égarement de la raison… et fragments*. A separate posthumous edition combining manuscript materials; the conjunction is editorial.
- **1848:** *Analyse du mécanisme de l’agiotage et de la méthode mixte en étude de l’attraction*. A posthumous pairing of two manuscripts.
- **1849:** *Cités ouvrières: des modifications à introduire dans l’architecture des villes*. An editorial extract from *Du garantisme*, not a freestanding book planned under this title by Fourier.
- **1849:** *L’Harmonie universelle et le phalanstère*. A two-volume selection arranged and titled by disciples; the selection is a reception-object, not Fourier’s authored whole.
- **1850:** *Sur l’esprit irréligieux des modernes et dernières analogies*. A posthumous separate edition combining two previously serialized manuscripts.
- **1851–1858:** *Manuscrits publiés par La Phalange*. Four editorial volumes whose contents derive from Fourier but whose material form and grouping were made by the Fourierist school. [Collected-edition record](https://pmb.culture.fr/opac_anmt/index.php?id=1960&lvl=publisher_see)
- **1857–1860:** *Pieces reportedly published in the Bulletin sociétaire / Bulletin du mouvement sociétaire*. Fourier’s manuscript inventory records twenty-seven draft pieces or excerpts—including drafts of *Le Nouveau Monde*, *La Fausse Industrie*, writings on commerce, Bonaparte, passions, luxury, Tahiti, and government—but Bourgin could not locate the periodical publication. Their existence as manuscripts is documented, while their alleged printed manifestation remains unverified. [Bourgin bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/utopian-and-scientific/bibliography-of-the-works-of-charles-fourier/)
- **1873:** *L’Association et le travail attrayant*. Extracts from *Le Nouveau Monde industriel et sociétaire* coordinated and rearranged by Charles Pellarin; it is an editorial anthology, not a new Fourier obra.
- **1966–1968:** *Œuvres complètes*, twelve-volume Anthropos edition. The principal modern collected corpus: facsimiles of the major books, *Le Nouveau Monde amoureux*, and the manuscript publications. It is the basis for delimiting the corpus here, but “complètes” does not mean that every surviving note or variant was transformed into a discrete authored work. [Edition record and contents](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL33021310M/OEuvres_compl%C3%A8tes_de_Charles_Fourier)
