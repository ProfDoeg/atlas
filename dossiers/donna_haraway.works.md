## Works by Donna Jeanne Haraway

### Biological-historical projects and monographs

- **1972:** *The Search for Organizing Relations: An Organismic Paradigm in Twentieth-Century Developmental Biology*. Doctoral dissertation in biology and the history of science; it is the research project from which Haraway’s first book emerged, not merely an unpublished preliminary to it. ([UCSC](https://thi.ucsc.edu/week-4-on-fungi-and-transformations-an-interview-with-donna-haraway/))
- **1975:** *The Transformation of the Left in Science: Radical Associations in Britain in the 1930s and the U.S.A. in the 1960s*. Historical essay on radical scientific organizations. ([UCI bibliography](https://www.lib.uci.edu/sites/all/docs/wellek/docs/Wellek2011Haraway.pdf))
- **1976:** *Crystals, Fabrics, and Fields: Metaphors of Organicism in Twentieth-Century Developmental Biology*. Monograph recasting the dissertation as a history of how metaphors organize experimental biology; the 2004 volume is a revised manifestation, not a separate work. ([UCI bibliography](https://www.humanities.uci.edu/critical/may-2011-donna-haraway-bibliography))
- **1978:** *Animal Sociology and a Natural Economy of the Body Politic, Part I: A Political Physiology of Dominance*. Scholarly article and the first half of a paired study of primate science as political knowledge. ([UCI bibliography](https://www.humanities.uci.edu/critical/may-2011-donna-haraway-bibliography))
- **1978:** *Animal Sociology and a Natural Economy of the Body Politic, Part II: The Past Is the Contested Zone*. Second half of the paired study, treating primate accounts of production and reproduction as historically contested constructions. ([UCI bibliography](https://www.humanities.uci.edu/critical/may-2011-donna-haraway-bibliography))
- **1979:** *The Biological Enterprise: Sex, Mind, and Profit from Human Engineering to Sociobiology*. Historical-critical essay connecting biological research, political economy, sex, and social control. ([UCSC selected publications](https://directory.ucsc.edu/cd_detail?guid=G000107826))
- **1981:** *In the Beginning Was the Word: The Genesis of Biological Theory*. Essay on language, narrative, and origin stories in biology. ([UCI bibliography](https://www.humanities.uci.edu/critical/may-2011-donna-haraway-bibliography))
- **1983:** *The High Cost of Information in Post-World War II Evolutionary Biology: Ergonomics, Semiotics, and the Sociobiology of Communications Systems*. Essay treating biological information as a historically situated technoscientific construction. ([UCI Wellek bibliography](https://www.lib.uci.edu/sites/all/docs/wellek/docs/Wellek2011Haraway.pdf))
- **1983:** *Signs of Dominance: From a Physiology to a Cybernetics of Primate Society, C. R. Carpenter, 1930–70*. Extended history-of-biology study of changing models of primate social order. ([UCSC selected publications](https://directory.ucsc.edu/cd_detail?guid=G000107826))
- **1984:** *Primatology Is Politics by Other Means*. Polemical scholarly essay: neither neutral survey nor simple history, but an intervention in how primate knowledge is produced. ([UCI Wellek bibliography](https://www.lib.uci.edu/sites/all/docs/wellek/docs/Wellek2011Haraway.pdf))
- **1984–1985:** *Teddy Bear Patriarchy: Taxidermy in the Garden of Eden, New York City, 1908–1936*. Essay on Carl Akeley’s museum dioramas as imperial, racial, gendered, and taxidermic world-making; bibliographies variously date its first journal appearance to 1984 or 1985, while the frequently cited anthology manifestation is from 1993. ([UCI bibliography](https://www.humanities.uci.edu/critical/may-2011-donna-haraway-bibliography))
- **1989:** *Primate Visions: Gender, Race, and Nature in the World of Modern Science*. Monograph and historiographic project about primatology’s narratives, specimens, images, institutions, and colonial inheritances. ([UCI bibliography](https://www.humanities.uci.edu/critical/may-2011-donna-haraway-bibliography))
- **1989:** *Monkeys, Aliens, and Women: Love, Science, and Politics at the Intersection of Feminist Theory and Colonial Discourse*. Essay joining feminist science studies to analysis of colonial discourse. ([UCI bibliography](https://www.humanities.uci.edu/critical/may-2011-donna-haraway-bibliography))

### Manifestos and texts that act

- **1985:** *A Manifesto for Cyborgs: Science, Technology, and Socialist Feminism in the 1980s*. Manifesto: an intentionally political and ironic text that proposes the cyborg as a figure for coalition rather than merely describing technological bodies; later titled *A Cyborg Manifesto*. ([UCI Wellek bibliography](https://www.lib.uci.edu/sites/all/docs/wellek/docs/Wellek2011Haraway.pdf))
- **2003:** *The Companion Species Manifesto: Dogs, People, and Significant Otherness*. Manifesto and compact multispecies obra, generated through dog–human histories, training, labor, affection, and coevolution. ([UCI bibliography](https://www.humanities.uci.edu/critical/may-2011-donna-haraway-bibliography))
- **2016:** *Manifestly Haraway*. Composite manifestation bringing *A Cyborg Manifesto* and *The Companion Species Manifesto* together with a new conversation; it is partly a new relational object and partly a republication, not two newly written manifestos. ([Duke University Press](https://www.dukeupress.edu/manifestly-haraway))

### Feminist technoscience essays and interventions

- **1988:** *Situated Knowledges: The Science Question in Feminism and the Privilege of Partial Perspective*. Epistemological essay that makes embodied, accountable positioning a condition of knowledge rather than a defect to be eliminated. ([Feminist Studies](https://www.jstor.org/stable/3178066))
- **1989:** *The Biopolitics of Postmodern Bodies: Constitutions of Self in Immune System Discourse*. Essay reading immunology simultaneously as laboratory practice, political language, and apparatus for making bodies. ([UCI Wellek bibliography](https://www.lib.uci.edu/sites/all/docs/wellek/docs/Wellek2011Haraway.pdf))
- **1990:** *A Manifesto for Cyborgs: Science, Technology, and Socialist Feminism in the 1980s—Postscript*. Retrospective supplement rather than an autonomous manifesto; its status depends upon the earlier text it revisits. ([UCI Wellek bibliography](https://www.lib.uci.edu/sites/all/docs/wellek/docs/Wellek2011Haraway.pdf))
- **1991:** *Simians, Cyborgs, and Women: The Reinvention of Nature*. Authored essay collection whose arrangement forms a larger obra about nature, bodies, primates, feminism, and technoscience; several constituent texts predate the book. ([UCI bibliography](https://www.humanities.uci.edu/critical/may-2011-donna-haraway-bibliography))
- **1991:** *Ecce Homo, Ain’t (Ar’n’t) I a Woman, and Inappropriate/d Others: The Human in a Post-Humanist Landscape*. Essay and critical figuration concerning racialized humanity, feminism, and the limits of universal “Man.” ([UCI Wellek bibliography](https://www.lib.uci.edu/sites/all/docs/wellek/docs/Wellek2011Haraway.pdf))
- **1992:** *The Promises of Monsters: A Regenerative Politics for Inappropriate/d Others*. Long essay, political fabulation, and cartographic intervention; it resists the boundary between criticism, theory, science studies, and speculative world-making. ([UCI Wellek bibliography](https://www.lib.uci.edu/sites/all/docs/wellek/docs/Wellek2011Haraway.pdf))
- **1992:** *Otherworldly Conversations, Terran Topics, Local Terms*. Essay on location, difference, and worldly conversation; its 2008 appearance in *Material Feminisms* is a later manifestation. ([UCSC selected publications](https://directory.ucsc.edu/cd_detail?guid=G000107826))
- **1994:** *A Game of Cat’s Cradle: Science Studies, Feminist Theory, Cultural Studies*. Programmatic essay that uses string figures as both conceptual object and method. ([UCSC selected publications](https://directory.ucsc.edu/cd_detail?guid=G000107826))
- **1995:** *Universal Donors in a Vampire Culture: It’s All in the Family—Biological Kinship Categories in the Twentieth-Century United States*. Essay on kinship, blood, race, genetics, and exchange in technoscientific culture. ([UCI Wellek bibliography](https://www.lib.uci.edu/sites/all/docs/wellek/docs/Wellek2011Haraway.pdf))
- **1997:** *The Virtual Speculum in the New World Order*. Essay on visualization, reproductive technoscience, and the remaking of bodies under global capitalism. ([UCI bibliography](https://www.humanities.uci.edu/critical/may-2011-donna-haraway-bibliography))
- **1997:** *Mice into Wormholes: A Comment on the Nature of No Nature*. Critical essay in which laboratory organisms and technoscientific figures undermine any simple opposition between nature and artifice. ([UCI Wellek bibliography](https://www.lib.uci.edu/sites/all/docs/wellek/docs/Wellek2011Haraway.pdf))
- **2000:** *Deanimations: Maps and Portraits of Life Itself*. Essay on visualization and the material-semiotic manufacture of “life itself.” ([UCI Wellek bibliography](https://www.lib.uci.edu/sites/all/docs/wellek/docs/Wellek2011Haraway.pdf))
- **2001:** *Race: Universal Donors in a Vampire Culture*. Revised manifestation of Haraway’s kinship-and-blood argument rather than an entirely independent project. ([UCI Wellek bibliography](https://www.lib.uci.edu/sites/all/docs/wellek/docs/Wellek2011Haraway.pdf))
- **2007:** *Speculative Fabulations for Technoculture’s Generations: Taking Care of Unexpected Country*. Essay that makes speculative fabulation a practice of responsibility and care rather than prediction. ([UCSC selected publications](https://directory.ucsc.edu/cd_detail?guid=G000107826))
- **2012:** *Awash in Urine: DES and Premarin® in Multispecies Response-ability*. Essay tracing pharmaceuticals, horses, women, laboratories, and industrial agriculture as one multispecies apparatus. ([Women’s Studies Quarterly](https://www.jstor.org/stable/23333466))
- **2013:** *SF: Science Fiction, Speculative Fabulation, String Figures, So Far*. Essay-performance in which “SF” is simultaneously genre, method, game, and unfinished practice. ([Ada: A Journal of Gender, New Media, and Technology](https://adanewmedia.org/2013/11/issue3-haraway/))
- **2015:** *Anthropocene, Capitalocene, Plantationocene, Chthulucene: Making Kin*. Conceptual intervention proposing multiple names for planetary crisis and insisting that naming is itself consequential world-making. ([Environmental Humanities](https://doi.org/10.1215/22011919-3615934))
- **2015:** *Anthropologists Are Talking—About the Anthropocene*. Multi-author roundtable rather than a singly authored work; Haraway is one participant among Noboru Ishikawa, Scott Gilbert, Kenneth Olwig, Anna Tsing, and Nils Bubandt. ([Ethnos](https://doi.org/10.1080/00141844.2015.1105838))
- **2018:** *Staying with the Trouble for Multispecies Environmental Justice*. Short intervention adapting Haraway’s multispecies argument to environmental-justice debate. ([Dialogues in Human Geography](https://doi.org/10.1177/2043820617739208))

### Hybrid books, visual-textual constructions, and Gesamtkunstwerk-like objects

- **1997:** *Modest_Witness@Second_Millennium.FemaleMan©_Meets_OncoMouse™: Feminism and Technoscience*. Hybrid technoscience obra made with Lynn M. Randolph’s paintings: criticism, scientific iconography, typographic performance, political theory, and visual art remain deliberately inseparable. Authorship is therefore collaborative even though bibliographies conventionally file the book under Haraway. ([UCI bibliography](https://www.humanities.uci.edu/critical/may-2011-donna-haraway-bibliography))
- **2004:** *The Haraway Reader*. Author-selected retrospective arrangement of essays; a curated manifestation of an existing body of work, with new framing, rather than a wholly new monograph. ([UCI bibliography](https://www.humanities.uci.edu/critical/may-2011-donna-haraway-bibliography))
- **2008:** *When Species Meet*. Multispecies monograph combining philosophy, history, memoir, dog training, science studies, and situated storytelling; it resists separation into impersonal theory and autobiography. ([UCI bibliography](https://www.humanities.uci.edu/critical/may-2011-donna-haraway-bibliography))
- **2016:** *Staying with the Trouble: Making Kin in the Chthulucene*. Composite book of essays, images, string figures, multispecies histories, and speculative fiction; several chapters revise earlier lectures or articles, so the book is both new obra and assembled manifestation. ([Duke University Press](https://www.dukeupress.edu/staying-with-the-trouble))
- **2016:** *The Camille Stories: Children of Compost*. Speculative-fiction cycle embedded in *Staying with the Trouble*, developed through a collective storytelling workshop and subsequently written by Haraway; its collaborative genesis complicates solitary authorship. ([Duke University Press front matter](https://doi.org/10.1515/9780822373780-fm))
- **2018:** *Making Kin Not Population: Reconceiving Generations*. Edited intellectual project made with Adele E. Clarke; Haraway is co-editor and co-author of the framing intervention, not sole author of the contributed chapters. ([Prickly Paradigm Press](https://press.uchicago.edu/ucp/books/book/distributed/M/bo28562707.html))

### Companion-species essays and multispecies protocols

- **2003–2004:** *From Cyborgs to Companion Species: Dogs, People, and Technoculture*. Lecture and essay manifestation marking Haraway’s shift from the cyborg toward companion-species relations; the lecture circulated in more than one venue and format. ([Townsend Center](https://townsendcenter.berkeley.edu/events/donna-haraway-cyborgs-companion-species-dogs-people-and-technoculture))
- **2006:** *Encounters with Companion Species: Entangling Dogs, Baboons, Philosophers, and Biologists*. Essay constructing an encounter among field science, philosophy, and lived human–animal relations. ([UCI bibliography](https://www.humanities.uci.edu/critical/may-2011-donna-haraway-bibliography))
- **2008:** *Otherworldly Conversations, Terran Topics, Local Terms*. Reworked anthology manifestation of the earlier essay; listed here only to mark its changed material context, not as a second work. ([UCI bibliography](https://www.humanities.uci.edu/critical/may-2011-donna-haraway-bibliography))
- **2009:** *Becoming-with-Companions: Sharing and Response in Experimental Laboratories*. Essay treating laboratory relations as practices of response and mutual formation rather than one-way experimentation. ([UCI Wellek bibliography](https://www.lib.uci.edu/sites/all/docs/wellek/docs/Wellek2011Haraway.pdf))
- **2010:** *When Species Meet: Staying with the Trouble*. Short essay and response derived from the larger companion-species project, not the 2008 book under another title. ([UCI bibliography](https://www.humanities.uci.edu/critical/may-2011-donna-haraway-bibliography))
- **2011:** *Species Matters, Humane Advocacy: In the Promising Grip of Earthly Oxymorons*. Essay on advocacy, killing, care, and the contradictory conditions of multispecies responsibility. ([UCI Wellek bibliography](https://www.lib.uci.edu/sites/all/docs/wellek/docs/Wellek2011Haraway.pdf))
- **2017:** *Symbiogenesis, Sympoiesis, and Art Science Activisms for Staying with the Trouble*. Essay connecting biology, collaborative art, environmental activism, and collective world-making. ([University of Minnesota Press](https://www.upress.umn.edu/9781517902377/arts-of-living-on-a-damaged-planet/))
- **2019:** *Tools for Multispecies Futures*. Collaborative, illustrated conversation with Drew Endy and other participants; it functions partly as a conceptual toolkit or protocol, although it does not issue fixed instructions. ([Journal of Design and Science](https://doi.org/10.21428/7808da6b.05eca6f1))
- **2019:** *Revisiting Catland in 2019: Situating Denizens of the Chthulucene*. Autobiographical-critical essay returning to Haraway’s childhood “Catland” stories as situated multispecies inheritance. Publication dates may appear as 2019 or 2021 because the essay’s titular date and the volume’s formal publication date differ. ([publisher preview](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781000637786_A43165717/preview-9781000637786_A43165717.pdf))
- **2019:** *Reflections on the Plantationocene: A Conversation with Donna Haraway and Anna Tsing*. Edited transcript of a recorded conversation moderated by Gregg Mitman; it is a co-produced oral event turned into a booklet, not a conventionally authored essay. ([Edge Effects](https://edgeeffects.net/haraway-tsing-plantationocene/))

### Conversations and oral-history works

- **1990:** *Cyborgs at Large: Interview with Donna Haraway*. Collaborative interview with Constance Penley and Andrew Ross; its dialogic authorship resists classification as a Haraway essay. ([UCI Wellek bibliography](https://www.lib.uci.edu/sites/all/docs/wellek/docs/Wellek2011Haraway.pdf))
- **1992:** *No Eden under Glass*. Conversation with P. K. Jamison, made as an exchange rather than a retrospectively authored treatise. ([UCI Wellek bibliography](https://www.lib.uci.edu/sites/all/docs/wellek/docs/Wellek2011Haraway.pdf))
- **1994:** *Shifting the Subject*. Conversation with Kum-Kum Bhavnani, transcribed by Justine Meyers; speaker, interlocutor, and transcriber share in the resulting textual object. ([UCI bibliography](https://www.humanities.uci.edu/critical/may-2011-donna-haraway-bibliography))
- **1996:** *Writing, Literacy, and Technology: Toward a Cyborg Writing*. Interview with Gary A. Olson whose form performs the relational account of writing it discusses. ([UCI Wellek bibliography](https://www.lib.uci.edu/sites/all/docs/wellek/docs/Wellek2011Haraway.pdf))
- **1997:** *The Unlikely Cyborg*. Interview with Hari Kunzru; a journalistic conversation and public manifestation of Haraway’s cyborg project, not a separately composed manifesto. ([Wired](https://www.wired.com/1997/02/ffharaway/))
- **1999–2000:** *How Like a Leaf: An Interview with Donna J. Haraway*. Book-length conversation with Thyrza Nichols Goodeve; sources date the first edition to either 1999 or 2000, and the dialogic form makes sole authorship inappropriate. ([UCSC](https://directory.ucsc.edu/cd_detail?guid=G000107826), [UCI bibliography](https://www.humanities.uci.edu/critical/may-2011-donna-haraway-bibliography))
- **2006:** *When We Have Never Been Human, What Is to Be Done?* Interview with Nicholas Gane; a jointly made theoretical conversation. ([UCI bibliography](https://www.humanities.uci.edu/critical/may-2011-donna-haraway-bibliography))
- **2007:** *Edges and Ecotones: The Worlds of Donna Haraway at UCSC*. Oral history made with interviewer Irene Reti, existing as audio and an edited transcript with minor differences; it is simultaneously testimony, archival object, and finding aid. ([UCSC University Library](https://library.ucsc.edu/reg-hist/haraway))
- **2008:** *The Age of Entanglement*. Interview with Liz Else, made for a general scientific readership. ([UCI bibliography](https://www.humanities.uci.edu/critical/may-2011-donna-haraway-bibliography))
- **2009–2010:** *Science Stories*. Interview with Jeffrey J. Williams; issue dating spans fall 2009–spring 2010. ([UCI bibliography](https://www.humanities.uci.edu/critical/may-2011-donna-haraway-bibliography))
- **2010:** *Kiwi Chicken Advocate Talks with Californian Dog Companion*. Conversation with Annie Potts whose title foregrounds the speakers’ situated multispecies allegiances. ([UCI bibliography](https://www.humanities.uci.edu/critical/may-2011-donna-haraway-bibliography))
- **2017:** *Staying with the Manifesto*. Interview with Sarah Franklin, recorded in 2016 and published in 2017; a retrospective conversation generated alongside *Manifestly Haraway*. ([Theory, Culture & Society](https://doi.org/10.1177/0263276417693290))
- **2018:** *Making Kin*. Interview with Christopher Lydon; an oral-publication object rather than a new monograph. ([Los Angeles Review of Books](https://lareviewofbooks.org/article/making-kin-an-interview-with-donna-haraway))
- **2020–2021:** *In the Heart of the Storm*. Interview with Katherine Bryant and Erik Wallenberg, conducted in summer 2020 and published subsequently in parts; the editors state that it was shortened and edited. ([Science for the People](https://magazine.scienceforthepeople.org/vol23-3-bio-politics/in-the-heart-of-the-storm-an-interview-with-donna-haraway/))
- **2021:** *Donna Haraway and Banu Subramaniam in Conversation*. Edited excerpt from an April 2021 public conversation; its incompleteness is part of the object’s status. ([Catalyst](https://catalystjournal.org/index.php/catalyst/article/view/37646))
- **2023:** *Figures of Inheritance*. Conversation with Martha Kenney about inheritance, sympoiesis, art, and multispecies storytelling. ([M+ Museum](https://www.mplus.org.hk/en/magazine/donna-haraway-critique-anthropocene-capitalocene/))

### Lectures and unfinished or event-bound projects

- **2011:** *Playing Cat’s Cradle with Companion Species*. Three-part Wellek Library Lecture project. Its primary existence was performative and event-bound; later textual manifestations do not erase that it was first a lecture sequence. ([UCI Wellek series](https://www.lib.uci.edu/library/publications/wellek/wellek-series.html))
- **2014:** *Anthropocene, Capitalocene, Chthulucene: Staying with the Trouble*. Public lecture and evolving conceptual project whose ideas subsequently entered articles and the 2016 book; it should not be mistaken for a separate book. ([Aarhus University](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1x0oxUHOlA8))
- **2019:** *Making Oddkin: Story Telling for Earthly Survival*. Public lecture that performs storytelling through biology, art, and activism; it remains primarily an event and recording rather than a fixed print work. ([Yale University](https://news.yale.edu/2019/09/25/donna-haraway-deliver-wilbur-cross-medal-lecture))
- **2024:** *Chicken Little in Perplexity*. Talk documented in Haraway’s archive and quoted as the title-source for a 2025 archival exhibition; without a stable published text, it resists being called a completed textual work. ([UCSC CART report](https://guides.library.ucsc.edu/cart/impact-report-2025))

### Filmic and archival manifestations with contested authorship

- **2016–2017:** *Donna Haraway: Story Telling for Earthly Survival*. Feature documentary directed by Fabrizio Terranova and built around Haraway’s staged conversations, home, gestures, stories, and collaborative fabulation. It is not simply “a film by Haraway,” yet treating her only as its passive subject would also misdescribe how the object was made. ([official film site](https://earthlysurvival.org/), [Society for Cultural Anthropology](https://culanth.org/fieldsights/storying-otherwise-a-review-of-donna-haraway-story-telling-for-earthly-survival))
- **1966–2026:** *Donna Jeanne Haraway Papers*. Accumulating archival body of manuscripts, revisions, correspondence, teaching files, recordings, translations, and more than 17,500 digital files. This is a material remainder of work and relationships, not a single authored obra; its completeness is necessarily provisional because the collection includes accruals and records extending through 2026. ([Online Archive of California](https://oac.cdlib.org/findaid/ark%3A%2F13030%2Fc8b283b2), [UCSC CART report](https://guides.library.ucsc.edu/cart/impact-report-2025))
