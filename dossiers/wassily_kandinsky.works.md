## Works by Wassily Kandinsky

### Catalogued visual obra

- **1900–1915:** *Oil paintings, catalogue raisonné, volume I*. The first half of Kandinsky’s catalogued painted obra; individual titles, dates, lost works, doubtful attributions, and untitled pictures are preserved as separate entries rather than reconstructed here from later museum or auction records. [Roethel and Benjamin](https://books.google.com/books?id=F9xOAAAAYAAJ)
- **1916–1944:** *Oil paintings, catalogue raisonné, volume II*. More than 575 entries, including 536 recorded in Kandinsky’s own handlists; the catalogue also registers lost works and objects of unknown location, so physical survival is not a condition of inclusion. [Roethel and Benjamin](https://books.google.com/books/about/Kandinsky_1916_44_V_2.html?id=i_kyAQAAIAAJ)
- **1900–1921:** *Watercolours, gouaches, and temperas, catalogue raisonné, volume I*. An obra of approximately 550 catalogued works on paper; the category includes early temperas and Russian-period works whose media resist a simple “watercolour” label. [Barnett](https://bha-test.inist.fr/vibad/index.php?action=getRecordDetail&idt=oba_0074643&lang=fr)
- **1922–1944:** *Watercolours and gouaches, catalogue raisonné, volume II*. More than 800 catalogued works from the Bauhaus and Paris periods, including untitled and incompletely documented objects. [Barnett](https://www.art-books.com/pages/books/00-0665/vivian-endicott-barnett/kandinsky-watercolours-catalogue-raisonne-1922-1944)
- **1889–1944:** *Individual drawings*. A catalogue-raised obra of 1,236 drawings, ink works, sketches, and studies; its volume also adds fifteen watercolours and two oil paintings absent from the earlier catalogues, demonstrating that the catalogue is authoritative but not absolutely closed. [Barnett](https://search.worldcat.org/title/84657161)
- **1889–1943:** *Thirty-eight surviving sketchbooks*. These are working objects rather than merely collections of autonomous drawings: sequences of pages preserve travel observations, exercises, compositional trials, annotations, and abandoned possibilities. An earlier announcement said thirty-five, while the published volume catalogues thirty-eight; the discrepancy should not be silently harmonized. [Barnett/Bloomsbury](https://www.bloomsbury.com/uk/kandinsky-drawings-vol-2-9780856676369/)
- **ca. 1902–1942:** *Original graphic work*. Roethel’s catalogue raisonné identifies 203 original prints, one doubtful print, twenty-four reprints, and two screenprints; “graphic work” here is therefore a critically delimited obra, not every printed image carrying Kandinsky’s name. [Roethel catalogue description](https://galeriagrabado.com/produkt/wassily-kandinsky-werkverzeichnis-graphik/?lang=en)
- **Dates various:** *Wood sculptures and decorative objects*. Rare carved and applied-art objects catalogued in the appendix to the drawings catalogue; they resist the hierarchy that would treat them as marginal “craft” rather than made things. [Barnett/Bloomsbury](https://www.bloomsbury.com/uk/kandinsky-drawings-vol-2-9780856676369/)

### Artist’s books, portfolios, and editorial Gesamtkunstwerke

- **1911; published 1912:** *Über das Geistige in der Kunst, insbesondere in der Malerei* (*On the Spiritual in Art, Especially in Painting*). A book-length theoretical obra, not a manifesto in the strict programmatic sense, although it argues actively for an art governed by “inner necessity”; its Russian and German textual states differ, and later editions introduced revisions. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1911–1912:** *Der Blaue Reiter Almanach*. A collaborative editorial Gesamtkunstwerk made with Franz Marc, combining essays, reproductions, ethnographic and popular materials, music, and Kandinsky’s stage composition *The Yellow Sound*; Kandinsky made the editorial conception and several contributions, not the entire contents. [Scholarly account](https://erenow.org/modern/german-expressionism-der-blaue-reiter-legacies/1.php)
- **1909–1912; published 1912/1913:** *Klänge* (*Sounds*). An artist’s book or “musical album” comprising thirty-eight prose poems and fifty-six woodcuts—twelve color and forty-four black-and-white—with typography, binding, words, and images conceived as a synthetic unity rather than as illustrated literature. Sources disagree whether to date publication 1912 or 1913; the book itself is dated 1913. [Guggenheim](https://www.guggenheim.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/guggenheim-press-kit-vasily-kandinsky-around-the-circle-20211005.pdf)
- **1913:** *Kandinsky 1901–1913*. An autobiographical exhibition album containing *Reminiscences* and commentaries on *Composition IV*, *Composition VI*, and *Picture with the White Edge*; it is simultaneously self-history, catalogue, and interpretation of paintings. [MoMA chronology](https://assets.moma.org/documents/moma_catalogue_448_300063127.pdf)
- **1922:** *Kleine Welten* (*Small Worlds*). A portfolio obra rather than a conventional book: twelve prints in woodcut, lithography, and drypoint demonstrate several print processes as related but nonidentical “worlds.” It is separately registered within the graphic catalogue. [Complete writings contents](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1926:** *Punkt und Linie zu Fläche* (*Point and Line to Plane*). A book-length theoretical and pedagogical protocol analyzing point, line, plane, tension, and movement; its diagrams function as operative demonstrations, not simply illustrations. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)

### Stage compositions and theatrical projects

- **1908–1909:** *Daphnis und Chloe*. An early, incompletely realized stage project known through sketches; because its surviving state is preparatory, the project—not a performance—is the obra. [Stinton](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/10157836/1/Stinton__thesis.pdf)
- **1908–1909:** *Schwarz und Weiß* (*Black and White*). A synesthetic stage composition coordinating sparse speech, music, movement, light, and colored costume; it was not staged during Kandinsky’s lifetime. [Philharmonie de Paris](https://collectionsdumusee.philharmoniedeparis.fr/collectionsdumusee/exposition-kandinsky.aspx?_lg=fr-FR)
- **1909:** *Stimmen, oder Grüner Klang* (*Voices, or Green Sound*). An unfinished or unperformed stage composition in which textual instructions evoke colored scenic events, music, silence, and movement. Its status is a project rather than a historical production. [Stinton](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/10157836/1/Stinton__thesis.pdf)
- **1909–1912:** *Der gelbe Klang* (*The Yellow Sound*). A six-part stage composition and proposed Gesamtkunstwerk made with music by Thomas de Hartmann; Kandinsky’s scenario was published in *Der Blaue Reiter*, but the planned prewar staging never occurred and later productions are posthumous manifestations, not Kandinsky’s own performances. [Guggenheim](https://www.guggenheim.org/articles/findings/photograph-of-kandinskys-the-yellow-sound-der-gelbe-klang)
- **1914; revised 1926:** *Violett* (*Violet*). Kandinsky’s most fully developed surviving stage project, with seven tableaux, interludes, diagrams, scenic watercolours, sound instructions, and fragments of music; revision in 1926 makes a single fixed date misleading. [Stinton](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/10157836/1/Stinton__thesis.pdf)
- **1928:** *Bilder einer Ausstellung* (*Pictures at an Exhibition*). A realized stage manifestation for Mussorgsky’s music at the Friedrich Theater in Dessau, translating musical sequence into moving colored and geometric scenery; it was the exceptional Kandinsky stage work actually produced during his lifetime. [Philharmonie de Paris](https://collectionsdumusee.philharmoniedeparis.fr/collectionsdumusee/exposition-kandinsky.aspx?_lg=fr-FR)

### Architectural, domestic, and monumental environments

- **1911:** *Interior decoration for Gabriele Münter’s house, Murnau*. Stenciled furniture and a painted stair rail form a domestic environment rather than an easel painting or single portable object. [Centre Pompidou](https://mediation.centrepompidou.fr/education/ressources/ENS-kandinsky-mono-EN/ENS-kandinsky-monographie-EN.html)
- **1914:** *Four panels for Edwin R. Campbell’s apartment*. A commissioned decorative ensemble for a New York interior; removal into separate museum objects obscures its original architectural relation. [Centre Pompidou](https://mediation.centrepompidou.fr/education/ressources/ENS-kandinsky-mono-EN/ENS-kandinsky-monographie-EN.html)
- **1922:** *Juryfreie Kunstschau entrance-hall murals*. A monumental environment for an imagined museum foyer, designed by Kandinsky as five gouache models and executed on black fabric by Bauhaus students. The original installation disappeared; surviving maquettes and the 1977 reconstruction are respectively project documents and a later manifestation, not interchangeable originals. [Centre Pompidou](https://www.centrepompidou.fr/fr/ressources/oeuvre/cejrLj4)
- **1931:** *Musiksalon* (*Music Salon*). A polychrome ceramic architectural ensemble created for the Berlin Bauausstellung; surviving models informed a 1975 reconstruction, so the historical installation, models, and reconstruction must be distinguished. [Centre Pompidou](https://mediation.centrepompidou.fr/education/ressources/ENS-kandinsky-mono/ENS-kandinsky-monographie.html)

### Scientific, legal, and ethnographic texts

- **1889:** *On Punishments Imposed by the Volost Courts of Moscow Province*. An early scholarly report in law and social administration, not an art text. [Avtonomova](https://kandinsky-art.ru/library/mnogogranniy-mir-kandinskogo10.html)
- **1889:** *From Materials on the Ethnography of the Sysola and Vychegda Zyryans: National Deities (According to Contemporary Beliefs)*. An ethnographic article arising from Kandinsky’s Vologda-region research; its objectifying scholarly conventions distinguish it sharply from his later artistic writing. [Avtonomova](https://kandinsky-art.ru/library/mnogogranniy-mir-kandinskogo10.html)
- **1889–1890:** *Ethnographic reviews*. A series of reviews of newly published ethnographic studies; the scholarly source records the series but does not supply a stable collective title or complete title-by-title enumeration, so none should be invented. [Avtonomova](https://kandinsky-art.ru/library/mnogogranniy-mir-kandinskogo10.html)
- **1893:** *On the Legality of Workers’ Wages*. A legal-economic study from Kandinsky’s pre-artistic academic career; bibliographies vary in translating its Russian title, so the English wording is descriptive rather than a claim to an authorized title. [Bibliographic record noted in the German bibliography](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wassily_Kandinsky)

### Writings on art, institutions, and pedagogy

- **1901:** *Critique of Critics*. Kandinsky’s first published text devoted to art, an intervention in Moscow critical debate. [Medvedkova](https://www.sciencespo.fr/artsetsocietes/en/archives/2081)
- **1901:** *Correspondence from Munich*. Art criticism in letter form; the collected edition treats the correspondence as a textual unit rather than as a book. [Lindsay and Vergo contents](https://www.si.edu/object/siris_sil_738615)
- **1909:** *Foreword to the Catalogue of the First Exhibition of the Neue Künstlervereinigung München*. An institutional declaration introducing the association’s exhibition. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1909–1910:** *Letters from Munich*. Five art reports published in *Apollon*; they constitute a serial critical text, not a retrospectively authored monograph. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1910:** *From the Catalogue of the Second Exhibition of the Neue Künstlervereinigung München*. A catalogue statement tied to a collective exhibition. [Lindsay and Vergo contents](https://www.si.edu/object/siris_sil_738615)
- **1910–1911:** *Content and Form*. A theoretical essay defining the work of art through the relation of inner content and material form. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1911:** *Footnotes to Schoenberg’s “On Parallel Octaves and Fifths”*. A responsive marginal text attached to another author’s music-theoretical argument. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1911:** *Whither the “New” Art?*. A polemical essay on the direction of contemporary art. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1911:** *The Battle for Art*. A polemical public intervention whose rhetoric approaches manifesto, though the collected edition classifies it as an essay. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1911–1912:** *The First Exhibition of the Editors of the Blaue Reiter*. An exhibition statement acting on behalf of a new artistic formation. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1912:** *Schoenberg’s Pictures*. A critical essay on Arnold Schoenberg as painter. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1912:** *Second Exhibition of the Editors of the Blaue Reiter*. A second exhibition statement, inseparable from the group’s public activity. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1912:** *Eugen Kahler*. A memorial-critical text included in the *Blaue Reiter* context. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1912:** *On the Question of Form*. A major theoretical essay arguing that no form is intrinsically prescribed or prohibited. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1912:** *On Stage Composition*. A theory and protocol for synthetic theater coordinating color, sound, word, and movement. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1912–1913:** *On Understanding Art*. An essay on reception and the conditions under which new art becomes intelligible. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1912:** *Autobiographical Note/Postscript*. A short self-account appended to a publication; its paired title reflects the editors’ treatment of related textual functions. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1913:** *Letter to the Editor*. A public protest after four poems were reprinted without Kandinsky’s consent in *A Slap in the Face of Public Taste*; it acts to disavow an imposed affiliation. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1913:** *Painting as Pure Art*. A theoretical essay advancing painting’s independence from representation. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1913:** *Reminiscences*. An autobiographical text embedded in *Kandinsky 1901–1913*. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1913:** *Composition 4*. A verbal account of a particular painting, functioning neither as the painting itself nor as ordinary criticism from outside the work. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1913:** *Composition 6*. A companion account of the painting’s genesis and compositional problem. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1913:** *Picture with the White Edge*. A companion account of the work also known as *Painting with White Border*. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1914:** *Cologne Lecture*. A lecture text rather than a publication conceived as a book. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1914:** *Letters to Arthur Jerome Eddy*. Explanatory correspondence about Kandinsky’s art; its status as a “work” is resistant because the letters were occasioned communications later gathered editorially. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1916:** *On the Artist*. An essay first issued in Swedish as *Om konstnären*. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1916:** *Art without Subject*. A theoretical defense of nonobjective art. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1919:** *Little Articles on Big Questions*. A paired miniature series comprising *On Point* and *On Line*, anticipating the later systematic book. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1919:** *Russia’s Artistic Spring*. A report on contemporary Russian artistic life. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1919:** *Self-Characterization*. A concise autobiographical and theoretical declaration. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1920:** *Artistic Life in Russia*. A survey of postrevolutionary artistic institutions and activity. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1920:** *The Museum of the Culture of Painting*. An institutional proposal: simultaneously analysis, project, and protocol for a new kind of museum. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1920:** *The “Great Utopia”*. A manifesto-like proposal for artistic synthesis and collective transformation. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1920:** *Steps Taken by the Department of Fine Arts in the Realm of International Art Politics*. An administrative report whose institutional action is part of what the text is. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1920:** *Program for the Institute of Artistic Culture*. A protocol and institutional project, not merely a description of INKhUK. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1921:** *Report to the Pan-Russian Conference, 1920*. A conference report dated in the collected edition by its publication context; the title itself preserves the earlier meeting date. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1921:** *Interview with Charles-André Julien*. A co-produced conversational text; its words cannot be treated as a wholly autonomous authored essay. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1922:** *Foreword to the Catalogue of the First International Art Exhibition, Düsseldorf*. A framing text for an exhibition. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1922:** *A New Naturalism?*. A critical essay interrogating contemporary returns to representation. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1922:** *Foreword to the Catalogue of the Kandinsky Exhibition at Gummesons*. A self-framing exhibition text. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1923:** *On Reform of Art Schools*. A pedagogical protocol proposing structural change in artistic education. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1923:** *The Basic Elements of Form*. Kandinsky’s contribution to *Bauhaus 1919–1923*, condensing his foundation-course method. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1923:** *Color Course and Seminar*. A pedagogical account and protocol for color study at the Bauhaus. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1923:** *Abstract Synthesis on the Stage*. A theoretical continuation of the stage-composition project. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1925:** *Yesterday—Today—Tomorrow*. A historical and prospective statement about modern art. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1925:** *Twilight*. A short critical text on a transitional cultural condition. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1925:** *Abstract Art*. A concise theoretical statement preceding Kandinsky’s fuller 1926 system. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1926:** *Dance Curves: The Dances of Palucca*. An analytic text with graphic tracings of Gret Palucca’s movement; neither dance notation alone nor conventional criticism. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1926:** *The Value of Theoretical Instruction in Painting*. A defense of theory as artistic pedagogy rather than prescription of style. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1927:** *i10*. A contribution identified in the collected edition by the journal’s title, functioning as a frame for two related texts rather than an independently descriptive title. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1927:** *And, Some Remarks on Synthetic Art*. An essay on the coordination of arts and sensory media. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1927:** *Painting and Photography*. A comparison of two image-making media without collapsing one into the other. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1928:** *Art Pedagogy/Analytical Drawing*. A teaching protocol based on analyzing structural forces rather than copying appearances. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1928:** *Statement in the Catalogue of the October Exhibition, Galerie F. Möller*. A short exhibition declaration. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1929:** *Bare Wall*. An essay on the wall as an active pictorial and architectural field. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1929:** *Two Suggestions*. A short programmatic intervention. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1929:** *The Psychology of the Productive Personality*. An inquiry into artistic production and temperament. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1929:** *Reply to a Questionnaire*. A solicited response whose form and questions are partly made by another party; it resists treatment as a wholly self-determined work. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1930:** *The Blaue Reiter (Recollection)*. A retrospective account of the almanac and association. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1930:** *Pictures at an Exhibition*. Kandinsky’s account of his 1928 stage production. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1931:** *Tribute to Klee*. A text honoring Paul Klee and their artistic relationship. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1931:** *Reflections on Abstract Art*. A mature reassessment of abstraction. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1932:** *Extract from a Letter*. An editorially isolated fragment; its status as an autonomous work is doubtful because the collected edition extracts it from correspondence. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1933:** *Reply to a Questionnaire*. A second solicited response, dependent on an external interrogatory framework. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1935:** *Art Today*. An assessment of the contemporary artistic situation after Kandinsky’s move to France. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1935:** *Thesis—Antithesis—Synthesis*. A dialectically structured theoretical essay. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1935:** *Line and Fish*. A short reflection connecting abstract form and organic imagery. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1935:** *Baumeister*. A critical text on Willi Baumeister. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1935:** *Two Directions*. An essay distinguishing tendencies within contemporary art. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1935:** *Empty Canvas, etc.* A group of brief reflections whose editorial “etc.” signals that it has no fully stable autonomous title. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1936:** *Abstract Painting*. A mature definition and defense of Kandinsky’s practice. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1936:** *Reply to Gaceta de Arte*. A response produced for the Tenerife journal’s inquiry. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1936:** *Franz Marc*. A memorial recollection of Kandinsky’s collaborator. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1937–1938:** *Assimilation of Art*. An essay on art’s social and cultural reception. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1937–1938:** *Interview with Karl Nierendorf*. A dialogic document whose authorship is shared with its interviewer and editorial transmission. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1937–1938:** *Four Poems*. A poetic group preserved in the collected writings; the group title is editorial and does not erase the poems’ individuality. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1937–1938:** *Concrete Art*. A statement distinguishing Kandinsky’s terminology from both representation and a merely geometric style. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1937–1938:** *My Woodcuts*. A retrospective account of Kandinsky’s graphic practice. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)

- **1937–1938:** *Letter to Irmgard Burchard*. A letter later admitted to the collected writings because of its theoretical content; its primary status remains correspondence. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1937–1938:** *Abstract or Concrete?*. A terminological and theoretical intervention. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1937–1938:** *Statement in Journal des Poètes*. A brief publication-context statement rather than a freestanding treatise. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1937–1938:** *Stabilité animée*. A French-language reflection whose title proposes stability as active rather than inert. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1939:** *Three Poems*. A late poetic group; the collected edition supplies the grouping, while the individual poems remain separate texts. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1939:** *Abstract and Concrete Art*. A late synthesis of Kandinsky’s preferred categories. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1942:** *Every Spiritual Epoch*. A short late statement on the relation between historical periods and artistic form. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1943:** *Domela*. A text associated with César Domela and his work. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)
- **1943:** *The Colored Reliefs of Sophie Taeuber-Arp*. Kandinsky’s late critical text on Taeuber-Arp’s reliefs and their treatment of color and space. [Lindsay and Vergo](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf)

[Volume I record](https://books.google.com/books?id=F9xOAAAAYAAJ); [volume II description](https://books.google.com/books/about/Kandinsky_1916_44_V_2.html?id=i_kyAQAAIAAJ).
- Vivian Endicott Barnett, *Kandinsky: Watercolours, Catalogue Raisonné*, vol. I, 1900–1921, and vol. II, 1922–1944. [Volume I](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark%3A/12148/cb37452150c); [volume II](https://www.art-books.com/pages/books/00-0665/vivian-endicott-barnett/kandinsky-watercolours-catalogue-raisonne-1922-1944).
- Vivian Endicott Barnett, *Kandinsky Drawings: Catalogue Raisonné*, vol. I, *Individual Drawings*, and vol. II, *Sketchbooks*. [Volume I record](https://search.worldcat.org/title/84657161); [volume II and contents](https://www.bloomsbury.com/uk/kandinsky-drawings-vol-2-9780856676369/).
- Hans K. Roethel, *Kandinsky: Das graphische Werk*, the catalogue raisonné of the prints. [Catalogue description](https://galeriagrabado.com/produkt/wassily-kandinsky-werkverzeichnis-graphik/?lang=en).
- Kenneth C. Lindsay and Peter Vergo, eds., *Kandinsky: Complete Writings on Art*, covering 1901–1943 and including published writings, selected interviews, lecture notes, and editorially identified fragments. [Digitized contents](https://web.flu.cas.cz/scan/323587661.pdf); [Smithsonian record and contents](https://www.si.edu/object/siris_sil_738615).
- Helmut Friedel, ed., *Wassily Kandinsky—Gesammelte Schriften 1889–1916*, used as the bibliographic control for the early scholarly and pre-1917 writings. [Standard-bibliography overview](https://thekandinskyproject.wordpress.com/wassily-kandinsky-bibliography/).
- Solomon R. Guggenheim Museum, *Vasily Kandinsky: Around the Circle* press materials, especially the catalogue description of *Klänge*. [Guggenheim PDF](https://www.guggenheim.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/guggenheim-press-kit-vasily-kandinsky-around-the-circle-20211005.pdf).
- Centre Pompidou, Kandinsky monograph and object records for the Murnau interior, Campbell panels, Juryfreie environment, and Music Salon. [Monograph](https://mediation.centrepompidou.fr/education/ressources/ENS-kandinsky-mono-EN/ENS-kandinsky-monographie-EN.html); [Juryfreie project record](https://www.centrepompidou.fr/fr/ressources/oeuvre/cejrLj4).
- Philharmonie de Paris, *Kandinsky: Music and the Stage*, for the stage compositions and their performance status. [Exhibition resource](https://collectionsdumusee.philharmoniedeparis.fr/collectionsdumusee/exposition-kandinsky.aspx?_lg=fr-FR).
