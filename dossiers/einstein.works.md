## Works by Albert Einstein

### Early scientific writings and dissertation

- **1895:** *On the Investigation of the State of the Ether in a Magnetic Field*. An unpublished youthful scientific essay sent to Einstein’s uncle Caesar Koch; because no publication was intended, it is better classified as a surviving manuscript than as a paper. [Einstein website](https://einstein-website.de/en/scientific-publications/)
- **1901:** *Folgerungen aus den Capillaritätserscheinungen* (*Conclusions Drawn from the Phenomena of Capillarity*). Einstein’s first published scientific paper, on intermolecular forces. [CPAE bibliography, pp. 46–47](https://assets.press.princeton.edu/books/einstein11/e_biblio.pdf)
- **1902:** *Ueber die thermodynamische Theorie der Potentialdifferenz zwischen Metallen und vollständig dissociirten Lösungen ihrer Salze und über eine elektrische Methode zur Erforschung der Molecularkräfte*. A journal article applying thermodynamics to molecular forces and electrical potential differences. [CPAE bibliography, p. 47](https://assets.press.princeton.edu/books/einstein11/e_biblio.pdf)
- **1902:** *Kinetische Theorie des Wärmegleichgewichtes und des zweiten Hauptsatzes der Thermodynamik*. A scientific paper on kinetic theory, thermal equilibrium, and the second law. [CPAE bibliography, p. 47](https://assets.press.princeton.edu/books/einstein11/e_biblio.pdf)
- **1903:** *Eine Theorie der Grundlagen der Thermodynamik*. A scientific paper continuing Einstein’s foundational work in statistical thermodynamics. [CPAE bibliography](https://assets.press.princeton.edu/books/einstein11/e_biblio.pdf)
- **1904:** *Zur allgemeinen molekularen Theorie der Wärme*. A scientific paper on fluctuations and the molecular theory of heat. [CPAE bibliography](https://assets.press.princeton.edu/books/einstein11/e_biblio.pdf)
- **1905:** *Eine neue Bestimmung der Moleküldimensionen* (*A New Determination of Molecular Dimensions*). Einstein’s doctoral dissertation at the University of Zurich, later published in revised form; it is both a qualifying academic object and an original scientific work. [CPAE scientific publications catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1905:** *Über einen die Erzeugung und Verwandlung des Lichtes betreffenden heuristischen Gesichtspunkt* (*On a Heuristic Point of View Concerning the Production and Transformation of Light*). A journal article proposing light quanta and applying them to the photoelectric effect. [CPAE/Schilpp-indexed catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1905:** *Über die von der molekularkinetischen Theorie der Wärme geforderte Bewegung von in ruhenden Flüssigkeiten suspendierten Teilchen*. A journal article giving a quantitative theory of Brownian motion. [CPAE/Schilpp-indexed catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1905:** *Zur Elektrodynamik bewegter Körper* (*On the Electrodynamics of Moving Bodies*). The journal article formulating special relativity; it is a paper rather than a book-length treatise. [CPAE/Schilpp-indexed catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1905:** *Ist die Trägheit eines Körpers von seinem Energieinhalt abhängig?* (*Does the Inertia of a Body Depend upon Its Energy Content?*). A short scientific paper deriving the mass–energy relation as a consequence of special relativity. [CPAE/Schilpp-indexed catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)

### Quantum, radiation, and statistical works

- **1906:** *Zur Theorie der Lichterzeugung und Lichtabsorption* (*On the Theory of Light Production and Light Absorption*). A scientific paper extending the light-quantum hypothesis. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1907:** *Die Plancksche Theorie der Strahlung und die Theorie der spezifischen Wärme*. A journal article applying quantum theory to the specific heat of solids. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1909:** *Über die Entwicklung unserer Anschauungen über das Wesen und die Konstitution der Strahlung*. A lecture-paper on the changing conception of radiation, including wave–particle duality. [CPAE bibliography](https://assets.press.princeton.edu/books/einstein11/e_biblio.pdf)
- **1910:** *Theorie der Opaleszenz von homogenen Flüssigkeiten und Flüssigkeitsgemischen in der Nähe des kritischen Zustandes*. A scientific paper explaining critical opalescence through fluctuations. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1916:** *Strahlungs-Emission und -Absorption nach der Quantentheorie*. A paper on quantum emission and absorption. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1917:** *Zur Quantentheorie der Strahlung* (*On the Quantum Theory of Radiation*). A scientific paper introducing the transition coefficients later called the Einstein A and B coefficients and deriving stimulated emission. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1924:** *Quantentheorie des einatomigen idealen Gases* (*Quantum Theory of the Monatomic Ideal Gas*). The first of Einstein’s papers extending Satyendra Nath Bose’s counting method to material particles; its authorship is Einstein’s, but the project explicitly depends on Bose’s preceding work. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1925:** *Quantentheorie des einatomigen idealen Gases, Zweite Abhandlung*. A second paper on Bose–Einstein statistics, containing the prediction now called Bose–Einstein condensation. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1925:** *Zur Quantentheorie des idealen Gases*. The third paper in Einstein’s ideal-gas quantum project, addressing fluctuations and wave properties of matter. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1926:** *Die Ursache der Mäanderbildung der Flußläufe und des sogenannten Baerschen Gesetzes*. A short scientific explanation of river meanders and related flow phenomena; it resists the usual image of Einstein’s œuvre as consisting only of fundamental physics. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1935:** *Can Quantum-Mechanical Description of Physical Reality Be Considered Complete?* A jointly authored scientific paper with Boris Podolsky and Nathan Rosen, now called the EPR paper; Podolsky prepared the published text, and Einstein later objected that its wording did not express his preferred argument. [Physical Review](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.47.777)
- **1936:** *Physics and Reality*. A synthetic essay on physical concepts and the relation between theory and experience. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1948:** *Quanten-Mechanik und Wirklichkeit* (*Quantum Mechanics and Reality*). A short philosophical-scientific essay criticizing the claim that quantum mechanics supplies a complete description of an individual physical system. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1951:** *The Advent of the Quantum Theory*. A historical and conceptual essay on the emergence of quantum physics. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)

### Relativity and gravitation

- **1907:** *Über das Relativitätsprinzip und die aus demselben gezogenen Folgerungen*. A major review article extending the relativity principle and introducing the equivalence principle as a project for a theory of gravitation. [CPAE bibliography](https://assets.press.princeton.edu/books/einstein11/e_biblio.pdf)
- **1911:** *Über den Einfluss der Schwerkraft auf die Ausbreitung des Lichtes*. A scientific paper predicting gravitational redshift and light deflection; its numerical deflection was superseded by the completed general theory. [CPAE bibliography](https://assets.press.princeton.edu/books/einstein11/e_biblio.pdf)
- **1912–1913:** *Zurich Notebook*. A working notebook recording Einstein’s and Marcel Grossmann’s search for generally covariant gravitational field equations; it is an exploratory project full of abandoned paths, not a polished work. [Tilman Sauer, “Einstein’s Working Sheets”](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.06106)
- **1913:** *Entwurf einer verallgemeinerten Relativitätstheorie und einer Theorie der Gravitation*. A jointly authored treatise with Marcel Grossmann presenting the provisional “Entwurf” theory; Einstein supplied the physical portion and Grossmann the mathematical portion. [CPAE bibliography](https://assets.press.princeton.edu/books/einstein11/e_biblio.pdf)
- **1913:** *Zum gegenwärtigen Stande des Gravitationsproblems*. A review of the then-current gravitation project, published before Einstein reached the final field equations. [CPAE bibliography](https://assets.press.princeton.edu/books/einstein11/e_biblio.pdf)
- **1914:** *Formale Grundlage der allgemeinen Relativitätstheorie*. A long academy paper developing the still-incomplete “Entwurf” theory. [CPAE bibliography](https://assets.press.princeton.edu/books/einstein11/e_biblio.pdf)
- **1915:** *Zur allgemeinen Relativitätstheorie*. One of the November 1915 academy communications through which Einstein successively revised the field equations. [CPAE bibliography](https://assets.press.princeton.edu/books/einstein11/e_biblio.pdf)
- **1915:** *Erklärung der Perihelbewegung des Merkur aus der allgemeinen Relativitätstheorie*. A scientific paper deriving Mercury’s anomalous perihelion advance from general relativity. [CPAE bibliography](https://assets.press.princeton.edu/books/einstein11/e_biblio.pdf)
- **1915:** *Die Feldgleichungen der Gravitation*. The brief academy paper presenting Einstein’s final generally covariant gravitational field equations. [CPAE bibliography](https://assets.press.princeton.edu/books/einstein11/e_biblio.pdf)
- **1916:** *Die Grundlage der allgemeinen Relativitätstheorie* (*The Foundation of the General Theory of Relativity*). A comprehensive review article giving the first systematic exposition of the completed theory. [Scholarly study and translation history](https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0405066)
- **1916:** *Näherungsweise Integration der Feldgleichungen der Gravitation*. A paper deriving gravitational waves in a weak-field approximation. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1917:** *Kosmologische Betrachtungen zur allgemeinen Relativitätstheorie*. A scientific paper constructing a static relativistic model of the universe and introducing the cosmological term. [Centennial scholarly review](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.07261)
- **1918:** *Über Gravitationswellen*. A corrected treatment of gravitational waves, distinguishing physically significant transverse waves from coordinate effects. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1918:** *Prinzipielles zur allgemeinen Relativitätstheorie*. A short conceptual defense of general relativity and Mach’s principle. [CPAE bibliography](https://assets.press.princeton.edu/books/einstein11/e_biblio.pdf)
- **1923:** *Bietet die Feldtheorie Möglichkeiten für die Lösung des Quantenproblems?* A speculative paper asking whether field theory might account for quantum phenomena; it is best understood as a research proposal rather than a completed solution. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1931:** *Zum kosmologischen Problem der allgemeinen Relativitätstheorie*. A paper abandoning the static model and examining an expanding universe. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1932:** *On the Relation between the Expansion and the Mean Density of the Universe*. The Einstein–de Sitter paper, jointly made with Willem de Sitter, proposing a spatially flat expanding cosmological model. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1936:** *Lens-Like Action of a Star by the Deviation of Light in the Gravitational Field*. A short paper calculating what is now called gravitational lensing; Einstein published it after prompting from Rudi W. Mandl and did not regard the observable effect as likely. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1936:** *Do Gravitational Waves Exist?* A paper with Nathan Rosen whose submission history is contested and instructive: an erroneous version denied the waves’ existence, while the corrected publication described cylindrical gravitational waves. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1938:** *On the Gravitational Equations and the Problem of Motion*. A jointly authored paper with Leopold Infeld and Banesh Hoffmann deriving motion from the field equations; the method is known as the Einstein–Infeld–Hoffmann approximation. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1941:** *Demonstration of the Non-Existence of Gravitational Fields with a Non-Vanishing Total Mass Free of Singularities*. A general-relativity paper on singularities and mass. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1943:** *Non-Existence of Regular Stationary Solutions of Relativistic Field Equations*. A jointly authored paper with Wolfgang Pauli. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1945:** *On the Cosmological Problem*. A scientific essay, also used as an appendix to *The Meaning of Relativity*, reconsidering relativistic cosmology. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1945:** *Influence of the Expansion of Space on the Gravitation Fields Surrounding the Individual Stars*. A paper with Ernst G. Straus; corrections and additions followed in 1946. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1949:** *Motion of Particles in General Relativity Theory*. A jointly authored paper with Leopold Infeld on equations of motion in general relativity. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)

### Unified-field project

- **1918–1955:** *Unified-field theory*. A decades-long project seeking a common geometrical basis for gravitation and electromagnetism, later broadened toward matter and quantum phenomena. It resists treatment as one finished obra: its surviving manifestations comprise changing theories, articles, correspondence, notebooks, and a large, incompletely identified corpus of calculations. [Tilman Sauer, “Einstein’s Working Sheets”](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.06106)
- **1923:** *Zur allgemeinen Relativitätstheorie*. A paper exploring an affine formulation influenced by Arthur Eddington’s work; it was one manifestation of the changing unified-field project. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1925:** *Einheitliche Feldtheorie von Gravitation und Elektrizität*. A scientific paper proposing a unified theory using an asymmetric connection. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1928:** *Riemann-Geometrie mit Aufrechterhaltung des Begriffes des Fernparallelismus*. A paper introducing Einstein’s teleparallel approach to unification. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1928:** *Neue Möglichkeit für eine einheitliche Feldtheorie von Gravitation und Elektrizität*. A two-part academy communication developing the distant-parallelism theory. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1930:** *Auf die Riemann-Metrik und den Fern-Parallelismus gegründete einheitliche Feldtheorie*. A systematic presentation of the teleparallel unified-field theory. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1938:** *On a Generalization of Kaluza’s Theory of Electricity*. A paper with Peter Bergmann revisiting five-dimensional unification. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1944:** *Bivector Fields I*. A mathematical paper jointly made with Valentine Bargmann. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1944:** *Bivector Fields II*. The second paper in the bivector project; the catalogue identifies Einstein as sole author of this installment. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1945:** *Generalization of the Relativistic Theory of Gravitation*. A paper proposing a nonsymmetric unified-field theory. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1946:** *Generalization of the Relativistic Theory of Gravitation, II*. A continuation jointly authored with Ernst G. Straus. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1948:** *Generalized Theory of Gravitation*. A further published manifestation of the nonsymmetric-field project. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1950:** *The Bianchi Identities in the Generalized Theory of Gravitation*. A technical paper within the unified-field program. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1950:** *On the Generalized Theory of Gravitation*. A popular exposition in *Scientific American*, distinct from the technical papers even though it describes the same project. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1953:** *A Comment on a Criticism of Unified Field Theory*. A short reply defending the unified-field project. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1954:** *Algebraic Properties of the Field in the Relativistic Theory of the Asymmetric Field*. A paper with Bruria Kaufman. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1955:** *A New Form of the General Relativistic Field Equations*. A paper with Bruria Kaufman, published after Einstein’s death; it is among the final manifestations of his unfinished unified-field project. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **c. 1920s–1955:** *Working sheets on unified field theory*. Thousands of pages of calculations, drafts, dead ends, and unidentified fragments survive as a corpus rather than a titled work; scholarly reconstruction remains incomplete, and additional sheets have entered the archive after Einstein’s death. [Tilman Sauer, “Einstein’s Working Sheets”](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.06106)

### Books, lecture cycles, and synthetic obras

- **1917:** *Über die spezielle und die allgemeine Relativitätstheorie (gemeinverständlich)* (*Relativity: The Special and the General Theory*). A book-length popular exposition written by Einstein; later editions added material, so “the book” exists as a developing sequence of authorially revised manifestations. [Schilpp/CPAE catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1920:** *Äther und Relativitätstheorie* (*Ether and the Theory of Relativity*). The published form of Einstein’s Leiden inaugural lecture, reconsidering the term “ether” within general relativity without restoring the mechanical ether rejected by special relativity. [CPAE bibliography](https://assets.press.princeton.edu/books/einstein11/e_biblio.pdf)
- **1922:** *Vier Vorlesungen über Relativitätstheorie* (*Four Lectures on the Theory of Relativity*). A book made from the Stafford Little Lectures delivered at Princeton in 1921; its English manifestation became *The Meaning of Relativity*. [Einstein website](https://einstein-website.de/en/scientific-publications/)
- **1922–1955:** *The Meaning of Relativity*. An evolving English-language book based on the Princeton lectures; successive editions acquired new appendices on cosmology and generalized gravitation, so no single edition exhausts the obra. [Scientific-publications catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1933:** *Les fondements de la théorie de la relativité générale*. A French volume combining translations of earlier relativity works with the newly written essay *Sur la structure cosmologique de l’espace*; it is partly a translated compilation and partly a new Einstein work. [Scientific-publications catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1934:** *Mein Weltbild* (*The World as I See It*). A collection assembled from Einstein’s essays, addresses, letters, and statements rather than a continuously composed book; editions in different languages do not contain identical selections. [AIP Einstein bibliography](https://history.aip.org/exhibits/einstein/bibliog.htm)
- **1938:** *The Evolution of Physics: The Growth of Ideas from Early Concepts to Relativity and Quanta*. A jointly authored popular history of physical concepts made with Leopold Infeld. [AIP Einstein bibliography](https://history.aip.org/exhibits/einstein/bibliog.htm)
- **1949:** *Autobiographical Notes*. An intellectual autobiography written for Paul Arthur Schilpp’s *Albert Einstein: Philosopher–Scientist*; it recounts the formation of ideas rather than supplying a conventional life narrative. [Scientific-publications catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1949:** *Remarks Concerning the Essays Brought Together in This Co-operative Volume*. Einstein’s extended replies to critics in *Albert Einstein: Philosopher–Scientist*; this is a dialogic philosophical work embedded in another editor’s book. [Scientific-publications catalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scientific_publications_by_Albert_Einstein)
- **1950:** *Out of My Later Years*. An English-language collection of previously written scientific, philosophical, political, and Jewish-affairs essays; it is an editorial assemblage, not wholly new composition. [AIP Einstein bibliography](https://history.aip.org/exhibits/einstein/bibliog.htm)
- **1954:** *Ideas and Opinions*. A composite collection based partly on *The World as I See It* and other writings; it should not be counted as though every constituent essay were first made in 1954. [AIP Einstein bibliography](https://history.aip.org/exhibits/einstein/bibliog.htm)

### Inventions and technical devices

- **1926–1930:** *Einstein–Szilard refrigerator*. A project with Leo Szilard for a refrigerator using no moving mechanical parts in its refrigerating circuit; it produced several designs, prototypes, and patents rather than one uniquely fixed object. [Patent study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.wpi.2005.10.012)
- **1926–1930:** *Electromagnetic pump for liquid metal*. An Einstein–Szilard refrigeration component in which electromagnetic action circulated a conductive liquid; it was patented as part of their technical collaboration. [Patent study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.wpi.2005.10.012)
- **1920s:** *Automatic camera*. A patented light-sensitive camera design developed with Gustav Bucky; the patent is the durable manifestation, while its status as a commercially realized object is limited. [Survey of Einstein’s patents](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.00666)
- **1920s:** *Sound-reproduction or hearing device*. A patented technical project associated with Einstein and Rudolf Goldschmidt, intended to convert acoustic vibrations; descriptions vary between hearing-aid and sound-reproduction terminology. [Survey of Einstein’s patents](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.00666)
- **1930s:** *Gyrocompass improvements*. Patent work associated with Einstein and Hermann Anschütz-Kaempfe; because it concerns improvements to an existing instrument, it resists attribution as an invention by Einstein alone. [Survey of Einstein’s patents](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.00666)

### Manifestos, declarations, and public interventions

- **1914:** *Aufruf an die Europäer* (*Manifesto to the Europeans*). A pacifist counter-manifesto drafted chiefly by Georg Friedrich Nicolai and signed by Einstein and two others; Einstein participated in making it, but sole authorship would be false. Its purpose was performative—an attempt to organize Europeans against nationalist war—so “manifesto” is literal. [CPAE bibliography and writings register](https://assets.press.princeton.edu/books/einstein11/e_biblio.pdf)
- **1918:** *Manifest des Bundes Neues Vaterland*. A political manifesto associated with the pacifist New Fatherland League and signed by Einstein; signature and organizational participation do not necessarily establish authorship of every word. [CPAE bibliography](https://assets.press.princeton.edu/books/einstein11/e_biblio.pdf)
- **1932:** *Dringender Appell* (*Urgent Appeal*). An anti-fascist electoral manifesto associated with Einstein, Käthe Kollwitz, Heinrich Mann, and others, calling for cooperation between Germany’s anti-Nazi parties; it acts politically rather than merely describing conditions. [Einstein Papers Project](https://www.einstein.caltech.edu/)
- **1939:** *Letter to Franklin D. Roosevelt on uranium*. A letter signed by Einstein and drafted through the efforts of Leo Szilard, Eugene Wigner, and Edward Teller, warning that uranium chain reactions might make powerful bombs possible. It is a consequential intervention, but calling it Einstein’s sole composition would erase its collaborative genesis. [Atomic Heritage Foundation](https://ahf.nuclearmuseum.org/ahf/key-documents/einstein-szilard-letter/)
- **1946:** *The Negro Question*. A short public essay condemning racism in the United States; it belongs to Einstein’s political writing rather than his scientific bibliography. [Einstein Papers Project](https://www.einstein.caltech.edu/)
- **1946:** *Atomic War or Peace*. A public argument for international control of atomic energy and supranational government. [Einstein Papers Project](https://www.einstein.caltech.edu/)
- **1947:** *Open Letter to the General Assembly of the United Nations*. A public political letter issued through the Emergency Committee of Atomic Scientists, urging enforceable international authority. [Einstein Papers Project](https://www.einstein.caltech.edu/)
- **1955:** *Russell–Einstein Manifesto*. A manifesto against nuclear war initiated and drafted by Bertrand Russell, then discussed and signed by Einstein shortly before his death; Einstein is a co-maker and namesake, not its sole author. It was publicly issued after his death on 9 July 1955. [University of Bologna facsimile](https://site.unibo.it/addu/it/scienza-e-opinione-pubblica/the-russell-einstein-manifesto-1955.pdf/%40%40download/file/The%20Russell-Einstein%20Manifesto%201955.pdf)

### Correspondence, notebooks, and unfinished manifestations

- **1895–1955:** *Correspondence*. Thousands of letters to family, colleagues, institutions, political leaders, and members of the public form a made textual corpus, but not one work with a stable title or authorial boundary: incoming letters belong to other authors, while jointly drafted and secretarially prepared letters complicate attribution. The Einstein Papers Project separately inventories correspondence rather than disguising it as a series of books. [Einstein Papers Project](https://www.einstein.caltech.edu/)
- **1912–1913:** *Einstein–Besso manuscript*. A collaborative calculation manuscript by Einstein and Michele Besso testing the “Entwurf” gravitation theory against Mercury’s perihelion motion; it was abandoned unpublished, then became crucial evidence for the route to general relativity. [Einstein Papers Project](https://www.einstein.caltech.edu/)
- **1925–1927:** *Research notes and manuscripts from the emergence of quantum mechanics*. A dispersed set of calculations, drafts, and exchanges rather than a single titled work; the documentary edition preserves their incomplete and conversational character. [Introduction to CPAE Volume 15](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.10662)
- **c. 1920s–1955:** *Scientific working sheets*. A large, partly uncatalogued body of loose calculations, many discovered behind filing cabinets after Einstein’s death. They resist individuation into “works”: much remains unidentified, undated, repetitive, or abandoned, and scholars estimate that the sheets would occupy several documentary volumes. [Tilman Sauer, “Einstein’s Working Sheets”](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.06106)
- **April 1955:** *Draft radio address for Israel’s Independence Day*. An unfinished political address on Israel and international conflict left on Einstein’s desk when he died; its incompletion is part of the object’s status and should not be repaired into a finished speech. [Einstein Papers Project](https://www.einstein.caltech.edu/)

### The catalogue as an unfinished project

- **1879–1955; edition ongoing:** *Albert Einstein’s written legacy*. This is not itself an obra made by Einstein but a posthumously constituted archival body: the Einstein Papers Project reports 700 Einstein writings in its published volumes, more than 4,500 letters to and from him in full text, another 5,900 documents in abstract, and an archive database exceeding 90,000 records. Because the critical edition currently reaches only to 1931 and large bodies of working papers remain unidentified, no finite list can truthfully claim to enumerate “everything Einstein made” without confusing documents, works, editions, translations, signatures, and archival fragments. [Einstein Papers Project](https://www.einstein.caltech.edu/)
