## Works by Juan Domingo Perón

### Dramatic juvenilia of uncertain attribution

- **c. 1915:** *Silvino Abrojo*. A one-act theatrical comedy published under the name “José M. Casais” and traditionally attributed to the young Perón. It does not appear in the main sequence of his *Obras completas*, so both date and attribution should be treated as provisional rather than canonical. ([Bibliographic notice](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Domingo_Per%C3%B3n))

- **c. 1915:** *El detective de la máscara negra*. A theatrical comedy or detective farce attributed to Perón’s youth; surviving bibliographic accounts provide neither a secure date nor a comparably firm publication history. It therefore resists treatment as an authenticated work. ([Bibliographic notice](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Domingo_Per%C3%B3n))

### Military protocols, manuals, studies, and geographical works

- **1923:** *Reglamento de gimnasia alemán para el Ejército y la Armada*. An illustrated adaptation of German military physical-training regulations, made for instructional use rather than as an autonomous literary book. Its exact degree of translation, adaptation, and authorship by Perón is not clearly settled in the collected-works record. ([Production bibliography](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Domingo_Per%C3%B3n))

- **1925:** *Higiene militar: capítulo “F” del Manual del aspirante*. A protocol-like training text for the Argentine Army’s Escuela de Suboficiales; the surviving printing calls the larger manual “in preparation.” ([Biblioteca Juan Domingo Perón catalogue](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/biblioteca_de_juan_domingo_peron.pdf))

- **1925:** *Moral militar: capítulo “E” del Manual del aspirante*. A forty-page instructional text on military ethics, conceived as part of the same unfinished or projected aspirant’s manual. ([Biblioteca Juan Domingo Perón catalogue](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/biblioteca_de_juan_domingo_peron.pdf))

- **1925–1928:** *Campañas del Alto Perú (1810–1814)*. A military-historical study first issued through the *Revista Militar*; sources disagree between 1925 and 1928 because composition, periodical appearance, and later republication are not consistently distinguished. ([Biographical chronology](https://lalupamemoriacolectiva.org.ar/juan-domingo-peron-cronologia-de-vida-y-obra/); [collected-works summary](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Domingo_Per%C3%B3n))

- **1930:** *Lo que yo vi de la preparación y realización de la revolución del 6 de septiembre de 1930*. A first-person political-military memoir, written after the coup but published as a component of *Tres revoluciones militares* in 1963; it is simultaneously testimony and retrospective self-positioning. ([CEDINPE contents record](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-juan-d-tres-revoluciones-militares-0))

- **1931:** *Guerra mundial 1914: operaciones en la Prusia Oriental y la Galitzia, Tannenberg, Lagos Masurianos, Lemberg. Estudios estratégicos*—cover title *El frente oriental de la guerra mundial en 1914*. A two-volume strategic history; the shorter cover title became the customary title and that used by the *Obras completas*. ([Biblioteca Juan Domingo Perón catalogue](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/biblioteca_de_juan_domingo_peron.pdf); [*Obras completas* contents](https://opac.unla.edu.ar/cgi-bin/koha/opac-ISBDdetail.pl?biblionumber=152141))

- **1932:** *Apuntes de historia militar*. A manual produced from Perón’s teaching at the Escuela Superior de Guerra and revised in subsequent editions. Its successive versions make it a developing pedagogical protocol rather than a text with one wholly stable form. ([*Obras completas* contents](https://opac.unla.edu.ar/cgi-bin/koha/opac-ISBDdetail.pl?biblionumber=152141); [editorial history](https://editoriales.conabip.gob.ar/sites/default/files/CATALOGO%20FABRO%202020%20-%20web.pdf))

- **1933–1934:** *Apuntes de historia militar: guerra ruso-japonesa de 1904–1905*. A multi-part campaign analysis used at the Escuela Superior de Guerra and later treated as *La guerra ruso-japonesa*. The catalogue records a 1933 first part and a 1934 second volume, so a single publication date would conceal its serial construction. ([Biblioteca Juan Domingo Perón catalogue](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/biblioteca_de_juan_domingo_peron.pdf))

- **1934:** *Memoria geográfica sintética del Territorio Nacional del Neuquén*. A military-geographical reconnaissance report encompassing terrain, climate, communications, population, and practicability; its annex also examined earlier military campaigns in Patagonia. ([CEDINPE collected-works record](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-juan-d-obras-completas-4-memoria-geogr%C3%A1fica-sint%C3%A9tica-del-territorio-nacional-del); [Biblioteca Nacional study](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/pdfs/2131c1bc7053848b69979e11176487cd.pdf))

- **1935–1936:** *Toponimia patagónica de etimología araucana*. A toponymic and etymological lexicon, published in two annual instalments and extending well beyond a simple place-name list. Its serial appearance makes 1935–1936 preferable to the dates of its later book editions. ([Biblioteca Nacional study](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/pdfs/2131c1bc7053848b69979e11176487cd.pdf); [CEDINPE collected-works record](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-juan-d-obras-completas-4-memoria-geogr%C3%A1fica-sint%C3%A9tica-del-territorio-nacional-del))

- **1937:** *Memorándum sobre la campaña del diario La Aurora, referente a intenciones imperialistas argentinas*. A diplomatic-military memorandum written during Perón’s posting in Chile; it is an administrative intervention rather than a book and survives through the documentary corpus. ([CEDINPE collected-works record](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-juan-d-obras-completas-4-memoria-geogr%C3%A1fica-sint%C3%A9tica-del-territorio-nacional-del); [*Obras completas* contents](https://opac.unla.edu.ar/cgi-bin/koha/opac-ISBDdetail.pl?biblionumber=152141))

- **1938:** *La idea estratégica y la idea operativa de San Martín en la Campaña de los Andes*. A historical-strategic conference paper associated with the Second International Congress of American History; accounts differ over whether its operative date should be the congress, preparation, or publication. ([CEDINPE collected-works record](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-juan-d-obras-completas-4-memoria-geogr%C3%A1fica-sint%C3%A9tica-del-territorio-nacional-del))

- **1939:** *Las operaciones en 1870*. A substantial study of the Franco-Prussian War co-authored with Enrique I. Rottjer; sole attribution to Perón would therefore be incorrect. ([Biblioteca Juan Domingo Perón catalogue](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/biblioteca_de_juan_domingo_peron.pdf); [*Obras completas* contents](https://opac.unla.edu.ar/cgi-bin/koha/opac-ISBDdetail.pl?biblionumber=152141))

- **Date not securely fixed:** *Observaciones recogidas en la organización, instrucción y conducción de tropas alpinas*. A report drawn from Perón’s observation of Italian alpine forces. Its nature is that of a professional field memorandum, and the consulted collected-works index does not establish a reliable original date. ([CEDINPE collected-works record](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-juan-d-obras-completas-4-memoria-geogr%C3%A1fica-sint%C3%A9tica-del-territorio-nacional-del))

- **1941–1943:** *Infantería de montaña*. A serial body of technical articles in the Argentine Army’s *Revista Militar*, including “Tropas de montaña”; the final instalment appeared in April 1943. It is a dispersed work rather than a single original book. ([Biblioteca del Congreso critical edition](https://bcn.gob.ar/uploads/Peron1943-1944discursos.pdf))

- **c. 1941–1943; published posthumously:** *Antecedentes de la Campaña Libertadora del General San Martín desde 1814 a 1817*. A surviving manuscript study later reproduced in facsimile; its exact composition date is uncertain, and the title belongs to a work not issued by Perón himself. ([CEDINPE author bibliography](https://www.cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/autores/1201/peron_juan_domingo?page=2))

### Collective political documents and governmental projects

- **1943:** *Documents of the GOU*. Circulars, memoranda, organizational rules, and revolutionary proclamations associated with the secret officers’ group appear in volume 6 of the *Obras completas*. They resist classification as Perón’s individual works: authorship is collective or disputed, some documents attributed to the GOU have been challenged as apocryphal, and inclusion in his collected papers does not prove that he wrote each one. ([*Obras completas* contents](https://opac.unla.edu.ar/cgi-bin/koha/opac-ISBDdetail.pl?biblionumber=152141); [Biblioteca del Congreso critical edition](https://bcn.gob.ar/uploads/Peron1943-1944discursos.pdf))

- **1946–1951:** *Primer Plan Quinquenal*. A governmental project and operative protocol for economic and social transformation, promoted and politically authorized by Perón but produced through the machinery of the state. It should not be reduced either to a personally authored book or to the later pamphlets explaining it. ([*Obras completas* contents](https://opac.unla.edu.ar/cgi-bin/koha/opac-ISBDdetail.pl?biblionumber=152141))

- **1952–1955:** *Segundo Plan Quinquenal*. A second collective state project, presented under Perón’s authority and accompanied by his explanatory addresses. Its institutional authorship makes “work by Perón” an attribution of political direction, not proof that he drafted every provision. ([*Obras completas* contents](https://opac.unla.edu.ar/cgi-bin/koha/opac-ISBDdetail.pl?biblionumber=152141); [Biblioteca Juan Domingo Perón catalogue](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/biblioteca_de_juan_domingo_peron.pdf))

- **1973–1976:** *Plan Trienal para la Reconstrucción y la Liberación Nacional*. A projected three-year economic program associated with Perón’s third government and Economy Minister José Ber Gelbard. It was interrupted by political and economic change, and it resists individual attribution: the *Obras completas* includes it, but scholarship also treats it as Gelbard’s ministerial project. ([*Obras completas* contents](https://opac.unla.edu.ar/cgi-bin/koha/opac-ISBDdetail.pl?biblionumber=152141); [Gelbard project study](https://repositoriosdigitales.mincyt.gob.ar/vufind/Record/RDUNLAM_922056e90da4fc5ce1c31ae12205278984C86E0208B926A))

### Speeches, lectures, manifestos, and instructional discourse

- **1943–1974:** *Speeches, messages, declarations, interviews, instructions, and presidential documents*. Perón made a vast, open-ended body of oral and administrative texts rather than a finite shelf of books. The national documentary edition organizes this corpus by year—currently including 1943–1945, 1949, 1967, and 1974—while explicitly describing its own holdings as only part of the material collected, so no honest census can claim that every individual speech has yet been critically fixed. ([Biblioteca del Congreso, “JDP, los trabajos y los días”](https://bcn.gob.ar/juan-domingo-peron))

- **1944:** *El pueblo quiere saber de qué se trata*. An official compilation of speeches and addresses, not a continuously composed book; its objecthood was produced editorially from Perón’s public oratory. Later publications with similar titles—including *El pueblo ya sabe de qué se trata*—rearrange or reproduce this material and are editions or anthologies rather than new works. ([Biblioteca Juan Domingo Perón catalogue](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/biblioteca_de_juan_domingo_peron.pdf); [Biblioteca del Congreso critical edition](https://www.cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/sites/default/files/pdfs/peron_1945_jdp_tomo_i.pdf))

- **10 June 1944:** *Significado de la defensa nacional desde el punto de vista militar*. A conference that acts both as strategic analysis and as a public policy manifesto; later pamphlet printings should not be mistaken for separate works. ([CEDINPE record](https://www.cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/autores/1741/peron_juan_d))

- **9 April 1949:** *La comunidad organizada*. A philosophical conference delivered at the closing of the First National Congress of Philosophy and only later stabilized under its familiar title. Its textual history is disputed: the spoken introduction, the published version, and later editions differ, while proposed ghostwriters such as Carlos Astrada, Hernán Benítez, or Juan Sepich remain speculation rather than demonstrated attribution. ([Biblioteca del Congreso textual study](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/sites/default/files/pdfs/castellucci_o-_lacomunidad_organizada_1era.pdf); [Biblioteca Juan Domingo Perón catalogue](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/biblioteca_de_juan_domingo_peron.pdf))

- **1951:** *Conducción política*. A sequence of classes delivered by Perón as an extraordinary professor at the Escuela Superior Peronista; subsequent books are transcripts and editorial arrangements of an oral pedagogical protocol. ([Biblioteca del Congreso](https://bcn.gob.ar/juan-domingo-peron); [Biblioteca Juan Domingo Perón catalogue](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/biblioteca_de_juan_domingo_peron.pdf))

- **1951–1952:** *Política y estrategia (No ataco, critico)*. A serial body of newspaper interventions published in *Democracia* under the pseudonym “Descartes,” later gathered as a book. Because the collection was constructed from separate occasional articles, its serial form is primary and its book form secondary. ([*Obras completas* contents](https://opac.unla.edu.ar/cgi-bin/koha/opac-ISBDdetail.pl?biblionumber=152141); [Biblioteca del Congreso textual study](https://digitales.bcn.gob.ar/files/textos/Comunid-org-2da-edicion.pdf))

- **1951–1952:** *Los mensajes de Perón*. A collected body of presidential messages rather than a newly authored unitary work; its book title names an editorial container for performative state texts. ([Biblioteca Juan Domingo Perón catalogue](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/biblioteca_de_juan_domingo_peron.pdf))

- **1972:** *Mensaje ambiental a los pueblos y gobiernos del mundo*. An international ecological manifesto: a text designed to warn, persuade, and call governments to action rather than merely describe environmental problems. ([Biographical and production summary](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Domingo_Per%C3%B3n))

- **1 May 1974:** *Modelo argentino para el proyecto nacional*. An unfinished national project announced to Congress, not simply a book. Perón died before completing or implementing it; no unquestionably final authorial copy survives, and more than twenty later editions contain textual differences, making the establishment of its text an editorial problem. ([Biblioteca del Congreso critical edition](https://bcn.gob.ar/juan-domingo-peron/peron.-modelo-argentino-para-el-proyecto-nacional); [edition history](https://digitales.bcn.gob.ar/files/textos/Peron.-Modelo-argentino-para-el-proyecto-nacional-2a.-ed-.pdf))

### Exile books, memoirs, and political interventions

- **1956:** *Del poder al exilio: quiénes me derrocaron*. A body of explanatory and polemical articles written after the 1955 overthrow and circulated in newspapers abroad before being collected. It is serial political testimony rather than a text born as a single book. ([CEDINPE bibliography](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/bibliografias/autores_argentinos?page=209); [*Obras completas* contents](https://opac.unla.edu.ar/cgi-bin/koha/opac-ISBDdetail.pl?biblionumber=152141))

- **1956; first editions dated 1958 in some catalogues:** *La fuerza es el derecho de las bestias*. Perón’s first major post-coup polemic, written in exile in Panama. Bibliographies alternate between its composition date and the date of the Montevideo Cicerón edition, so both must be retained. ([Biblioteca Juan Domingo Perón catalogue](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/biblioteca_de_juan_domingo_peron.pdf); [*Obras completas* contents](https://opac.unla.edu.ar/cgi-bin/koha/opac-ISBDdetail.pl?biblionumber=152141))

- **1957–1958:** *Los vendepatria: las pruebas de una traición*. An exile polemic presenting the overthrow and subsequent government as national betrayal. Sources differ by a year because clandestine or early circulation and the identified *Línea Dura* edition are not consistently separated. ([*Obras completas* contents](https://opac.unla.edu.ar/cgi-bin/koha/opac-ISBDdetail.pl?biblionumber=152141); [biographical bibliography](https://studylib.es/doc/6583772/biograf%C3%ADa-del-teniente-general-don-juan-domingo-per%C3%B3n))

- **1963:** *Tres revoluciones militares*. An editor-assembled historical testimony covering the military-political crises of 1930, 1943–1945, and 1955; Rogelio García Lupo prepared the original edition. It includes the much earlier *Lo que yo vi…*, so not every component should be redated 1963. ([CEDINPE contents record](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-juan-d-tres-revoluciones-militares-0))

- **1967:** *Latinoamérica: ahora o nunca*. A geopolitical essay and continental-unity manifesto. Parts circulated through contemporary articles and addresses, meaning that the named book consolidates a broader intervention rather than exhausting it. ([CEDINPE bibliography](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/bibliografias/autores_argentinos?page=209); [*Obras completas* contents](https://opac.unla.edu.ar/cgi-bin/koha/opac-ISBDdetail.pl?biblionumber=152141))

- **1968:** *La hora de los pueblos*. A geopolitical essay-manifesto directed toward decolonization and Third World solidarity. It is treated as an autonomous work in both the national documentary edition and the *Obras completas*. ([Biblioteca del Congreso](https://bcn.gob.ar/juan-domingo-peron); [CEDINPE record](https://www.cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/autores/1201/peron_juan_domingo?page=1))

- **1970–1971:** *Memorias*. An oral autobiographical narrative produced through interviews with Tomás Eloy Martínez and later edited and published in differing forms. It resists sole authorship as a conventional memoir because Perón supplied the testimony while interview, transcription, selection, and arrangement shaped the textual object. ([*Obras completas* contents](https://opac.unla.edu.ar/cgi-bin/koha/opac-ISBDdetail.pl?biblionumber=152141); [CEDINPE interview record](https://www.cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/bibliografias/orden_tematico/juan_domingo_peron?page=6))

### Correspondence and political instructions

- **1946–1974:** *Political correspondence, messages, and instructions*. Perón’s letters to movement leaders, unions, governments, and individual correspondents form a large dispersed corpus now partly incorporated into annual documentary editions. It is a body of operative texts—often instructions that produced political action—rather than one closed work, and the national edition does not claim that collection is complete. ([Biblioteca del Congreso, “JDP, los trabajos y los días”](https://bcn.gob.ar/juan-domingo-peron); [*Obras completas* contents](https://opac.unla.edu.ar/cgi-bin/koha/opac-ISBDdetail.pl?biblionumber=152141))

- **1956–1972:** *Perón–Cooke: correspondencia*. A sustained political dialogue between Perón and John William Cooke, subsequently published in two volumes. Each man authored his own letters, while the named work itself is a post hoc editorial construction and therefore cannot be attributed wholly to Perón. ([CEDINPE author bibliography](https://www.cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/autores/1201/peron_juan_domingo?page=2))

- **1961:** *Carta al presidente John F. Kennedy*. A diplomatic-political letter made as a direct intervention in inter-American affairs; later publication does not convert it into a separately composed book. ([CEDINPE bibliography](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/bibliografias/autores_argentinos?page=209))

### Audiovisual manifestations

- **1971:** *Perón: La revolución justicialista*. A filmed oral testimony created through interviews with Perón in Madrid. Perón made its extended verbal and performative argument, but Fernando Solanas and Octavio Getino directed, scripted, framed, and edited the film; it therefore resists attribution as “a film by Perón.” ([Universidad Carlos III film record](https://humanidadesdigitales.uc3m.es/s/cine-de-movilidad/item/57604))

- **1971:** *Perón: Actualización política y doctrinaria para la toma del poder*. A second filmed political testimony, recorded by Solanas and Getino between June and October 1971 and subsequently transcribed into book form. It acts as audiovisual instruction for a movement seeking power, but cinematic authorship belongs to the filmmakers and discursive authorship to Perón. ([CEDINPE record](https://www.cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/autores/1201/peron_juan_domingo?page=1); [production account](https://www.clarin.com/cine/pino_solanas-el_legado_estrategico_de_peron_0_S1UgpN6uPmg.html))
