## Works by Alfonso X el Sabio

### Legal protocols and political projects

- **c. 1252–1255:** *Fuero real*. A royal law code intended to standardize municipal government and procedure; it is a protocol of rule produced by the Alfonsine chancery, not a text composed solely in the king’s hand. Its precise relationship to the *Espéculo* and *Partidas* remains disputed. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_8/)

- **c. 1255–1260:** *Espéculo*. An unfinished legal project, preserved in five books although its internal plan envisages more; whether it preceded the *Partidas*, developed alongside them, or represents a recension of the same legislative enterprise is contested. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_8/)

- **1256–1265, with later revisions:** *Libro del fuero de las leyes / Siete Partidas*. A seven-part legal, political, theological, and social encyclopedia meant to act upon the kingdom as law, although its promulgation and its supposedly definitive Alfonsine form are disputed; the manuscript tradition preserves several redactions rather than one stable original. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_8/)

- **Uncertain; possibly begun under Fernando III and reworked under Alfonso X:** *Setenario*. A sevenfold doctrinal and legislative compilation whose date, original patron, completeness, and relationship to the *Primera Partida* remain contested. It resists classification as a simple law book because it combines royal ideology, theology, natural knowledge, and legal instruction. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_8/)

- **c. 1265–1278:** *Leyes nuevas*. A small, textually unstable collection of royal responses and supplementary rulings associated with the *Fuero real*; it is better understood as a developing body of juridical practice than as a securely delimited authored book. [Jerry R. Craddock, *The Legislative Works of Alfonso X el Sabio: A Critical Bibliography*](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/38r0s439)

- **After 1255; compilation probably late thirteenth century:** *Leyes del estilo*. A collection of judicial usages connected with the royal court and the *Fuero real*. Its contents derive from Alfonsine legal practice, but the surviving compilation is probably post-Alfonsine and therefore resists attribution as something Alfonso personally made. [Jerry R. Craddock, *The Legislative Works of Alfonso X el Sabio: A Critical Bibliography*](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/38r0s439)

- **1274:** *Ordenamiento de las Cortes de Zamora*. A legislative record defining, among other matters, the royal “casos de corte”; it belongs to Alfonso’s governmental production, although—as an act of king, court, and chancery—it is not a singly authored literary work. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_8/)

- **1276:** *Ordenamiento de las tafurerías*. A protocol regulating gaming houses, traditionally included among Alfonso’s legal works but drafted by Master Roldán at royal command; Alfonso is its commissioner and legislator rather than its sole textual author. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_8/)

### Historical projects

- **c. 1270–1274; resumed 1282–1284 and continued after Alfonso’s death:** *Estoria de España*. A collaborative vernacular history of the Iberian Peninsula, produced in successive primitive and critical redactions; Alfonso initiated and supervised it, but neither redaction was completed in his lifetime, and the familiar *Primera crónica general* includes continuation under Sancho IV. [Boletín Oficial del Estado](https://www.boe.es/biblioteca_juridica/publicacion.php?id=PUB-LH-2022-258)

- **c. 1270–1284:** *General estoria* (or *Grande e general estoria*). An immense project for a universal history integrating biblical, classical, and other traditions. Six parts were planned, but the sixth survives only fragmentarily and the project remained unfinished; its translations, compilations, and prose were collective products of the royal workshop. [Universidad de Salamanca](https://revistas.usal.es/uno/index.php/Studia_H_Historia_Medieval/es/article/view/8724)

### Translations, narrative compilations, and game protocols

- **1251:** *Calila e Dimna*. A Castilian translation and adaptation of the Arabic framed collection of animal tales, commissioned while Alfonso was still infante. The attribution is therefore to his initiative and workshop rather than securely to his personal composition. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_6/)

- **1264:** *Libro de la escala de Mahoma*. A Castilian translation of an Arabic account of Muhammad’s ascent, commissioned by Alfonso; the Castilian text is lost, surviving indirectly through Latin and French translations made at his court by Bonaventura da Siena. It is consequently a lost work-project with surviving derivative manifestations. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_6/)

- **1283:** *Libro del axedrez, dados e tablas* (or *Libro de los juegos*). An illustrated protocol and anthology of chess, dice, tables, and astronomical games, begun and completed at Seville in 1283 under royal direction. Its integration of rules, mathematical structures, social scenes, and illumination makes it something close to a courtly Gesamtkunstwerk. [Real Biblioteca del Monasterio de El Escorial](https://rbme.patrimonionacional.es/s/rbme/item/13125)

### Astronomy, instruments, and computational works

- **c. 1252–1272:** *Tablas alfonsíes*. Astronomical tables produced by Alfonso’s scientific collaborators, especially Yehudá ben Mošé and Isaac ben Sid, for calculating celestial positions. The relation between the lost or fragmentary Castilian tables made at Alfonso’s court and the later Latin “Alfonsine Tables” disseminated across Europe is disputed, so the latter cannot simply be treated as Alfonso’s unchanged work. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_4/)

- **1276–1279, incorporating earlier translations:** *Libros del saber de astronomía*. A royal manuscript-compilation of fifteen treatises on the heavens, astronomical instruments, and clocks, made by Jewish, Christian, and Muslim collaborators under Alfonso’s editorial direction. It is both an obra and a workshop Gesamtkunstwerk of text, diagrams, tables, and illumination; the principal manuscript is mutilated. [Universidad Complutense de Madrid](https://www.ucm.es/historica/libros-del-saber-de-astronomia), [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_4/)

- **1250s; revised within the 1276–1279 compilation:** *Libro de la ochava esfera*. A catalogue and visual description of the fixed stars and constellations, preserved as the opening treatise of the *Libros del saber de astronomía*; it is a collaborative translation and revision rather than an independent authorial book in the modern sense. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_4/)

- **1250s; revised 1276–1279:** *Libro de la alcora*. A protocol for understanding and constructing a celestial globe, incorporated into the astronomical compilation and produced through translation and courtly revision. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_4/)

- **1250s–1279:** *Libro del astrolabio redondo*. An instructional treatise on the spherical astrolabe and its use, surviving as part of the *Libros del saber de astronomía*. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_4/)

- **1250s–1279:** *Libro del astrolabio llano*. A construction-and-use protocol for the plane astrolabe, translated and revised by the Alfonsine scientific workshop. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_4/)

- **1250s–1279:** *Libro de la lámina universal*. A treatise and instrument protocol for a universal astronomical plate; its identity is inseparable from the diagrams and apparatus it describes. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_4/)

- **1250s–1279:** *Libro de la açafeha*. A Castilian treatment of the universal astrolabe or ṣafīḥa, produced from Arabic astronomical learning and incorporated into Alfonso’s instrument-making corpus. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_4/)

- **1250s–1279:** *Libro de las armellas*. A protocol for constructing and using an armillary instrument, preserved within the royal astronomical compilation. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_4/)

- **1250s–1279:** *Libro de las láminas de los siete planetas*. An instructional text for planetary plates, combining astronomical calculation with the making of an instrument. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_4/)

- **1250s–1279:** *Libro del cuadrante*. A protocol for a quadrant and its astronomical operations, transmitted as one component of the *Libros del saber de astronomía*. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_4/)

- **1250s–1279:** *Libro de la piedra de la sombra*. A construction-and-use text for a sundial or shadow-measuring device; the made thing is simultaneously a textual protocol and a projected instrument. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_4/)

- **1250s–1279:** *Libro del reloj del agua*. A protocol for making a water clock, preserved within the astronomical compilation. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_4/)

- **1250s–1279:** *Libro del reloj del argent vivo*. A protocol for a mercury clock; it describes a machine and therefore resists reduction to “a book.” [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_4/)

- **1250s–1279:** *Libro del reloj de la candela*. Instructions for timekeeping by a calibrated candle, included among the Alfonsine horological treatises. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_4/)

- **1250s–1279:** *Libro del palacio de las horas*. A protocol for an elaborate time-indicating structure or mechanism; the contemplated object is architectural and mechanical as well as textual. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_4/)

- **1250s–1279:** *Libro del instrumento llano para hacer atazir*. An instructional work for a plane instrument used in astronomical calculation, preserved as the final treatise in the compilation. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_4/)

### Astrology, natural knowledge, and astral magic

- **1250; revised and materially assembled c. 1276–1279:** *Lapidario*. A four-treatise compilation relating stones and their powers to celestial bodies, translated by Yehudá ben Mošé with Garci Pérez and later furnished as an illuminated royal codex. It crosses natural philosophy, medicine, astrology, magic, image, and luxury bookmaking, and is thus closer to a Gesamtkunstwerk than to a single-author treatise. [Université Bordeaux Montaigne](https://revues.u-bordeaux-montaigne.fr/conceptos/article/download/59/581?inline=1), [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_5/)

- **1254:** *Libro conplido en los iudizios de las estrellas*. A Castilian translation of ʿAlī ibn Abī al-Rijāl’s eight-book astrological manual, made by Yehudá ben Mošé at Alfonso’s command; only five books survive continuously in the principal Castilian manuscript, with portions of the remainder recovered from other witnesses. [Université Bordeaux Montaigne](https://revues.u-bordeaux-montaigne.fr/conceptos/article/download/59/581?inline=1), [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_5/)

- **c. 1254–1256:** *Quadripartitum* with the commentary attributed to ʿAlī ibn Riḍwān. An astrological translation project associated with Alfonso’s court; the Castilian intermediary is lost and only a court-produced Latin translation survives, making its Alfonsine form indirect. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_5/)

- **1256:** *Picatrix*. A Castilian translation of the Arabic *Ghāyat al-ḥakīm*, ordered by Alfonso as a manual of astral magic; the Castilian version is lost and the work survives chiefly through a Latin translation made from it. Its textual boundaries and transmission remain problematic. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_5/)

- **1259:** *Libro de las cruzes*. A Castilian astrological compilation, translated by Yehudá ben Mošé and organized into chapters with the assistance of a collaborator named Juan; it applies celestial interpretation to political and worldly events. [Université Bordeaux Montaigne](https://revues.u-bordeaux-montaigne.fr/conceptos/article/download/59/581?inline=1), [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_5/)

- **Mid-thirteenth century:** *Livre des secrez de nature*. A magical-natural translation associated with the Alfonsine workshop; its Castilian form is lost and it survives in an Old French translation, so its exact title, limits, and degree of Alfonso’s involvement cannot be securely recovered. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_5/)

- **Mid- to late thirteenth century:** *Liber Razielis*. A compilation of astral and angelic magic translated or assembled under Alfonso’s patronage. The Castilian text is lost, while the Latin version survives in several mostly incomplete manuscripts; it is a court project rather than demonstrably Alfonso’s personal composition. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_5/)

- **1276–1279:** *Libro de las formas e imágenes que son en los cielos*. A planned compilation of astral-image magic; only its prologue and general table survive. Its unfinished or lost state is essential to what it is: a project whose surviving framework records an intended work rather than a complete text. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_5/)

- **Late thirteenth century:** *Libro de astromagia*. A modern title for a fragmentary, untitled or textually composite work on talismans, zodiacal images, and planetary magic in Vatican manuscript Reg. lat. 1283. It resists being called a single finished work because only thirty-six fragmentary folios survive and its relationship to the other Alfonsine magical compilations is unsettled. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_5/)

- **Date uncertain:** *Clavis sapientiae*. An alchemical or natural-magical text transmitted in Latin under Alfonso’s name; no Castilian manuscript survives, and the attribution to Alfonso or his workshop is doubtful. It should be recorded as an attributed manifestation, not silently accepted as an authentic Alfonsine obra. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_5/)

### Song, poetry, image, and manuscript Gesamtkunstwerk

- **c. 1252–1284, with some manuscript work possibly continuing afterward:** *Cantigas de Santa Maria*. A corpus of 427 Galician-Portuguese Marian songs preserved in four overlapping manuscript realizations, three with musical notation and three richly illuminated. Alfonso conceived, sponsored, edited, and probably composed part of it, but most songs belong to his circle; the changing manuscripts make it simultaneously a continuing project, musical repertory, image cycle, and Gesamtkunstwerk rather than a single stable book. [Oxford Bibliographies](https://academic.oup.com/reference/62384/reference-article-abstract/555079113)

- **Mid- to late thirteenth century:** *Cancionero profano* (forty-four attributed cantigas). A modern collective designation for Alfonso’s Galician-Portuguese secular songs—principally satire, with love poetry and other forms—not an original book title. Individual authorship is generally accepted, though two poems are jointly authored; most pieces have no fixed medieval “title” beyond their incipits, so inventing titles would misdescribe them. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonso_x_el_sabio/su_obra_bibliografia_12/), [Edinburgh University Press study](https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.1515/9781474425247-006/pdf?licenseType=open-access)
