## Works by Vladimir Petrovich Demikhov

### Apparatus and experimental preparations

- **1937–1938; revisited 1958:** *Implantable mechanical heart*. A metal, motor-driven double-pump apparatus made to replace a dog’s heart and sustain circulation; accounts disagree about whether the 1937 animal survived approximately two hours or whether circulation was maintained for as long as 5.5 hours, apparently conflating different trials. It is an apparatus and experimental project, not a text. ([Langer](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21620094/); [Pulatov and Marzo](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41508321/); [DeVries et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10387043/))

- **1950:** *A new and simpler variant of the heart–lung preparation of a warm-blooded animal*. A surgically assembled, surviving-organ preparation used for physiological experiments; its published description also constitutes a protocol. ([bibliographic record](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%94%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%B2,_%D0%92%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%80_%D0%9F%D0%B5%D1%82%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87#%D0%9D%D0%B0%D1%83%D1%87%D0%BD%D1%8B%D0%B5_%D1%82%D1%80%D1%83%D0%B4%D1%8B))

- **1950:** *Method for preparing surviving animal organs for experimental purposes*. A protocol embodied in USSR inventor’s certificate SU 85878; the made thing is principally a reproducible method rather than a single physical object. ([certificate listing](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%94%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%B2,_%D0%92%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%80_%D0%9F%D0%B5%D1%82%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87#%D0%90%D0%B2%D1%82%D0%BE%D1%80%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%B8%D0%B5_%D1%81%D0%B2%D0%B8%D0%B4%D0%B5%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BB%D1%8C%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B2%D0%B0_%D0%A1%D0%A1%D0%A1%D0%A0_%D0%BD%D0%B0_%D0%B8%D0%B7%D0%BE%D0%B1%D1%80%D0%B5%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%B8%D1%8F))

- **1959:** *Method for cultivating foot-and-mouth-disease virus in transplanted organs*. A co-invented experimental protocol, with F. S. Shulyak, recorded in USSR inventor’s certificate SU 130629; some bibliographies incorrectly render “foot-and-mouth disease” as “lizard.” ([certificate listing](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%94%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%B2,_%D0%92%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%80_%D0%9F%D0%B5%D1%82%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87#%D0%90%D0%B2%D1%82%D0%BE%D1%80%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%B8%D0%B5_%D1%81%D0%B2%D0%B8%D0%B4%D0%B5%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BB%D1%8C%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B2%D0%B0_%D0%A1%D0%A1%D0%A1%D0%A0_%D0%BD%D0%B0_%D0%B8%D0%B7%D0%BE%D0%B1%D1%80%D0%B5%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%B8%D1%8F))

- **1970:** *Device for biological preservation of a heart–lung preparation*. A co-invented preservation apparatus, with N. I. Gerasimenko, A. A. Priymak, and B. S. Demidov, recorded in USSR inventor’s certificate SU 313542. ([certificate listing](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%94%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%B2,_%D0%92%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%80_%D0%9F%D0%B5%D1%82%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87#%D0%90%D0%B2%D1%82%D0%BE%D1%80%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%B8%D0%B5_%D1%81%D0%B2%D0%B8%D0%B4%D0%B5%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BB%D1%8C%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B2%D0%B0_%D0%A1%D0%A1%D0%A1%D0%A0_%D0%BD%D0%B0_%D0%B8%D0%B7%D0%BE%D0%B1%D1%80%D0%B5%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%B8%D1%8F))

- **1970:** *Method for treating oxygen deficiency*. A co-invented therapeutic protocol, with N. I. Gerasimenko, A. A. Priymak, and B. S. Demidov, embodied in USSR inventor’s certificate SU 342631. ([certificate listing](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%94%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%B2,_%D0%92%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%80_%D0%9F%D0%B5%D1%82%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87#%D0%90%D0%B2%D1%82%D0%BE%D1%80%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%B8%D0%B5_%D1%81%D0%B2%D0%B8%D0%B4%D0%B5%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BB%D1%8C%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B2%D0%B0_%D0%A1%D0%A1%D0%A1%D0%A0_%D0%BD%D0%B0_%D0%B8%D0%B7%D0%BE%D0%B1%D1%80%D0%B5%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%B8%D1%8F))

### Surgical projects and living experimental constructions

- **1946 onward:** *Heterotopic intrathoracic transplantation of an additional heart*. A continuing experimental project in dogs, placing a donor heart beside the native heart; the “work” is both a surgical configuration and a series of short-lived animals, not one stable object. A successful heart–lung version dated 30 June 1946 survived 9.5 hours. ([Konstantinov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2763473/); [Oxford review](https://academic.oup.com/ejcts/article/44/2/201/441445))

- **1946 onward:** *Orthotopic replacement of the heart and lungs*. A family of canine procedures replacing the recipient’s cardiopulmonary complex without cardiopulmonary bypass; whether the first 1946 operation should be called fully orthotopic is stated differently in later histories. ([Cooper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/4978618/); [Konstantinov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2763473/))

- **1947 onward:** *Isolated lung transplantation*. An experimental surgical project in dogs, generally credited as the first orthotopic experimental lung transplantation; it produced techniques and temporary living preparations rather than a durable artifact. ([Margreiter](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27788817/); [Langer](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21620094/))

- **1948 onward:** *Experimental liver transplantation*. A continuing canine project rather than a single operation; later work appeared jointly with I. M. Grigorovskii and L. L. Gugushvili. ([Langer](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21620094/); [1968 article record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/4905319/))

- **1951 onward:** *Orthotopic replacement of the heart*. A canine surgical method for replacing the native heart without a heart–lung machine; several historical accounts call Demikhov’s 1951 procedure the first successful orthotopic heart transplant, although “successful” here denotes technical survival in an animal experiment, not durable cure. ([Matskeplishvili](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29232448/); [Langer](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21620094/))

- **1952–1953 onward:** *Internal-mammary-to-coronary arterial bypass*. A canine myocardial-revascularization procedure joining the internal mammary artery to a coronary artery; sources variously date the first operation to 1952 or 1953. It is a surgical protocol and experimental anastomosis, not a freestanding object. ([Matskeplishvili](https://doi.org/10.1093/eurheartj/ehx697); [Konstantinov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2763473/))

- **1954–1959:** *Two-headed dogs*. A series of approximately twenty operations grafting the head and upper body of a smaller dog onto a larger dog’s vascular system. These living preparations strongly resist the neutral designation “work”: they were sentient experimental animals, short-lived and subject to rejection, not sculptures or successful whole-head replacements. ([Langer](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21620094/); [Glyantsev](https://medj.rucml.ru/journal/4e432d5452414e53504c414e542d41525449434c452d323031352d302d332d302d38392d313030); [head-transplantation review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5116034/))

- **1950s–1986:** *Kidney, adrenal–kidney, esophageal, limb, and reproductive-tract transplantation studies*. A heterogeneous laboratory project comprising methods and individual animal preparations; surviving summaries do not support a complete operation-by-operation catalogue, so this should not be mistaken for one unified obra. ([Langer](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21620094/); [Demikhov and Rembez bibliographic record](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00787187))

### Monographs and public expositions

- **1959:** *Пересадка органов: это возможно?* (*Organ Transplantation: Is It Possible?*). A public scientific pamphlet issued by the All-Union Society for the Dissemination of Political and Scientific Knowledge; it explains and advocates the transplant project rather than serving as a surgical protocol. ([Glyantsev bibliography](https://medj.rucml.ru/journal/4e432d5452414e53504c414e542d41525449434c452d323032302d31322d312d302d36312d3735))

- **1960:** *Пересадка жизненно важных органов в эксперименте* (*Experimental Transplantation of Vital Organs*). Demikhov’s synthetic monograph and dissertation project, combining literature review, experimental report, illustrations, and operative method; it appeared in authorized English (1962), German (1963), and Spanish (1967) translations, which are manifestations of the same obra rather than new works. Sources alternate between 259 and 260 pages. ([National Electronic Library record](https://rusneb.ru/catalog/000199_000009_004969785/); [Glyantsev’s study](https://www.jtransplantologiya.ru/jour/article/download/154/191); [foreign-edition study](https://www.jtransplantologiya.ru/jour/article/download/421/488))

### Articles, protocols, reports, and recorded interventions

- **1949:** *Гомопластическая пересадка сердца и легких у теплокровных (собак)* (*Homoplastic Transplantation of the Heart and Lungs in Warm-Blooded Animals [Dogs]*). A conference paper reporting experimental procedures first presented at the 1947 All-Union Conference on Thoracic Surgery. ([bibliographic listing](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%94%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%B2,_%D0%92%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%80_%D0%9F%D0%B5%D1%82%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87#%D0%A1%D1%82%D0%B0%D1%82%D1%8C%D0%B8))

- **1950:** *Гомопластическая пересадка второго дополнительного сердца в эксперименте на собаках* (*Homoplastic Transplantation of a Second, Additional Heart in Experiments on Dogs*). A coauthored experimental article with V. M. Goryainov; some secondary transcriptions mistakenly print “centre” instead of “heart.” ([bibliographic listing](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%94%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%B2,_%D0%92%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%80_%D0%9F%D0%B5%D1%82%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87#%D0%A1%D1%82%D0%B0%D1%82%D1%8C%D0%B8))

- **1950:** *Гомопластическая замена сердца и легких в эксперименте на собаках* (*Homoplastic Replacement of the Heart and Lungs in Experiments on Dogs*). An experimental report on cardiopulmonary replacement. ([bibliographic listing](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%94%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%B2,_%D0%92%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%80_%D0%9F%D0%B5%D1%82%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87#%D0%A1%D1%82%D0%B0%D1%82%D1%8C%D0%B8))

- **1950:** *Методика пересадки легкого в эксперименте* (*Technique of Experimental Lung Transplantation*). A concise protocol article instructing how to perform the operation. ([bibliographic listing](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%94%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%B2,_%D0%92%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%80_%D0%9F%D0%B5%D1%82%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87#%D0%A1%D1%82%D0%B0%D1%82%D1%8C%D0%B8))

- **1950:** *Об ошибочности «закона сердца» Старлинга* (*On the Erroneousness of Starling’s “Law of the Heart”*). A polemical physiological article: closer to a scientific counter-thesis than a protocol. ([Glyantsev bibliography](https://medj.rucml.ru/journal/4e432d5452414e53504c414e542d41525449434c452d323032302d31322d312d302d36312d3735))

- **1951:** *Пересадка сердца и легких в эксперименте* (*Experimental Transplantation of the Heart and Lungs*). A chapter or proceedings paper reporting the transplantation project; bibliographies also record a similarly titled four-page contribution in volume 5 of the same proceedings, and it is unclear from accessible records whether these are distinct texts or variant descriptions. ([bibliographic listing](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%94%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%B2,_%D0%92%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%80_%D0%9F%D0%B5%D1%82%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87#%D0%A1%D1%82%D0%B0%D1%82%D1%8C%D0%B8))

- **1951:** *Выступление в прениях* (*Contribution to the Discussion*). A one-page printed record of an oral intervention; it resists classification as an independently composed work because the publication preserves an event of speech. ([bibliographic listing](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%94%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%B2,_%D0%92%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%80_%D0%9F%D0%B5%D1%82%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87#%D0%A1%D1%82%D0%B0%D1%82%D1%8C%D0%B8))

- **1951:** *Экспериментальное обоснование замены сердца механическим прибором в остром опыте* (*Experimental Basis for Replacing the Heart with a Mechanical Device in an Acute Experiment*). An experimental article connecting the artificial-heart apparatus to its physiological proof. ([bibliographic listing](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%94%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%B2,_%D0%92%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%80_%D0%9F%D0%B5%D1%82%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87#%D0%A1%D1%82%D0%B0%D1%82%D1%8C%D0%B8))

- **1952:** *Demonstration of the Operation and of a Dog with Replaced Heart and Lungs at the Extramural Session of the USSR Academy of Medical Sciences in Ryazan in 1950: Discussion*. A published record of a live demonstration and ensuing discussion; the manifestation is textual, but the primary made event was performative and biological. ([bibliographic listing](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%94%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%B2,_%D0%92%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%80_%D0%9F%D0%B5%D1%82%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87#%D0%A1%D1%82%D0%B0%D1%82%D1%8C%D0%B8))

- **1953:** *Проблема трансплантации органов в соответствии с мичуринской теорией* (*The Problem of Organ Transplantation in Accordance with Michurinist Theory*). A theoretical article framed by the officially sanctioned Soviet biology of its moment; that ideological framing is part of what the text is. ([PubMed-linked bibliographic record](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%94%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%B2,_%D0%92%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%80_%D0%9F%D0%B5%D1%82%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87#%D0%A1%D1%82%D0%B0%D1%82%D1%8C%D0%B8))

- **1953:** *Хирургическое лечение недостаточности коронарного кровообращения* (*Surgical Treatment of Coronary Circulatory Insufficiency*). A short proceedings report on experimental myocardial revascularization. ([bibliographic listing](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%94%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%B2,_%D0%92%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%80_%D0%9F%D0%B5%D1%82%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87#%D0%A1%D1%82%D0%B0%D1%82%D1%8C%D0%B8))

([bibliographic listing](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%94%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%B2,_%D0%92%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%80_%D0%9F%D0%B5%D1%82%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87#%D0%A1%D1%82%D0%B0%D1%82%D1%8C%D0%B8))

- **1961:** *On the Humoral Transmission of Substances with Neural Action in Crossed Carotid Circulation in Dogs*. A coauthored physiological article with R. Baumann, L. Sieke, and K. Hecht, using surgically joined circulation as an experimental system. ([bibliographic listing](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%94%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%B2,_%D0%92%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%80_%D0%9F%D0%B5%D1%82%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87#%D0%A1%D1%82%D0%B0%D1%82%D1%8C%D0%B8))

- **1964:** *Итоги и перспективы трансплантации сердца* (*Results and Prospects of Heart Transplantation*). A conference paper assessing the completed experiments and proposing a future project. ([bibliographic listing](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%94%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%B2,_%D0%92%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%80_%D0%9F%D0%B5%D1%82%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87#%D0%A1%D1%82%D0%B0%D1%82%D1%8C%D0%B8))

- **1966:** *Культивирование вируса ящура на органах-трансплантатах* (*Cultivation of Foot-and-Mouth-Disease Virus in Transplanted Organs*). A coauthored report with F. S. Shulyak, publishing the experimental application of their patented method. ([bibliographic listing](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%94%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%B2,_%D0%92%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%80_%D0%9F%D0%B5%D1%82%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87#%D0%A1%D1%82%D0%B0%D1%82%D1%8C%D0%B8))

- **1967:** *О причинах гибели гомотрансплантатов органов и тканей* (*On the Causes of the Death of Homografts of Organs and Tissues*). A contribution to the collection *Problems of Homoplasty and Alloplasty*, not—as some secondary lists label it—a separate monograph. ([bibliographic listing](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%94%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%B2,_%D0%92%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%80_%D0%9F%D0%B5%D1%82%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87#%D0%9C%D0%BE%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B3%D1%80%D0%B0%D1%84%D0%B8%D0%B8))

- **1968:** *Оценка состояния системы кровообращения у собак с пересаженным сердцем* (*Assessment of the Circulatory System in Dogs with a Transplanted Heart*). A coauthored experimental article with R. M. Baevsky and N. M. Sharovskaya. ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/4873184/))

- **1968:** *Перспективы пересадки органов и тканей* (*Prospects for Organ and Tissue Transplantation*). A coauthored programmatic article with F. S. Shulyak; it describes a future field and thus functions partly as a project statement. ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/5736792/))

- **1968:** *Пересадка полового тракта у собак* (*Transplantation of the Female Genital Tract in Dogs*). A coauthored experimental article with I. N. Rembez, documenting another transplantation method. ([DOI record](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00787187))

- **1968:** *Пересадка печени в эксперименте* (*Experimental Liver Transplantation*). A coauthored article with I. M. Grigorovskii and L. L. Gugushvili reporting the liver-transplant project. ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/4905319/))

- **1969:** *Пересадка сердца, легких и других органов* (*Transplantation of the Heart, Lungs, and Other Organs*). A retrospective synthesis of Demikhov’s experimental program; a later English translation is a manifestation of this article, not a separate work. ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/4904612/); [translation notice](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0167527310001592))

- **1970:** *30-летний опыт экспериментальных исследований по пересадке органов* (*Thirty Years’ Experience of Experimental Research on Organ Transplantation*). A brief conference retrospect presenting the many operations as one long research project. ([bibliographic listing](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%94%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%B2,_%D0%92%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%80_%D0%9F%D0%B5%D1%82%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87#%D0%A1%D1%82%D0%B0%D1%82%D1%8C%D0%B8))
