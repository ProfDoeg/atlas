## Works by Simón Rodríguez

### Educational protocols and institutional proposals

- **1793:** *Representación al Ayuntamiento de Caracas*. An administrative petition connected with Rodríguez’s work as a primary-school teacher. It appears in bibliographic reconstructions but not in the 2016 *Obras completas*, so its textual independence and place in the canon remain uncertain. ([Campos Kennett](https://prueba.repositorioinstitucionaluacm.mx/jspui/bitstream/123456789/2835/1/Gloria%20Soledad%20Campos%20Kennett.pdf); [2016 *Obras completas*](https://biblioteca.clacso.edu.ar/clacso/se/20190926042843/Simon_Rodriguez_Obras_Completas.pdf))

- **1794:** *Estado actual de la escuela y nuevo establecimiento de ella* (also known as *Reflexiones sobre los defectos que vician la escuela de primeras letras de Caracas y medio de lograr su reforma por un nuevo establecimiento*). A diagnostic report joined to a protocol for reorganizing Caracas’s primary school: criticism and executable institutional design in one document. The varying titles describe the same work rather than separate books. ([2016 *Obras completas*](https://biblioteca.clacso.edu.ar/clacso/se/20190926042843/Simon_Rodriguez_Obras_Completas.pdf); [Revista Colombiana de Educación](https://revistas.upn.edu.co/index.php/RCE/article/view/19314))

- **1825–1826:** *Project of popular education for Bolivia*. A state-sponsored network of schools and workshops intended to educate and employ poor and socially excluded children; at least an experimental establishment operated at Chuquisaca, but the national system was abandoned after Rodríguez’s conflict with the government. The obra here is chiefly an institutional project—partly realized practice rather than a single surviving text. ([Fundación Empresas Polar](https://bibliofep.fundacionempresaspolar.org/dhv/entradas/r/rodriguez-simon/); [Sociedades americanas, documented facsimile edition](https://biblioteca.clacso.edu.ar/clacso/se/20191002033211/Sociedades_americanas_en_1828.pdf))

- **1830 or 1832:** *Observaciones sobre el terreno de Vincocaya con respecto a la empresa de desviar el curso natural de sus aguas y conducirlas por el río Zumbai al de Arequipa*. A technical-environmental report evaluating a proposed hydraulic diversion and its economic and social consequences. The original imprint is commonly dated 1830, while the 2016 collected edition assigns 1832; the discrepancy should not be silently regularized. ([Fundación Empresas Polar](https://bibliofep.fundacionempresaspolar.org/dhv/entradas/r/rodriguez-simon/); [2016 *Obras completas*](https://biblioteca.clacso.edu.ar/clacso/se/20190926042843/Simon_Rodriguez_Obras_Completas.pdf))

- **1850–1851:** *Consejos de amigo dados al Colegio de Latacunga*. A requested program of studies and institutional advice for the Colegio de San Vicente at Latacunga—part educational protocol, part philosophical memorandum. Sources variously date its composition to 1850–1851 or simply 1851; it was published posthumously in 1854. ([2016 *Obras completas*](https://biblioteca.clacso.edu.ar/clacso/se/20190926042843/Simon_Rodriguez_Obras_Completas.pdf); [*Inventamos o erramos* contents](https://elsudamericano.wordpress.com/2013/11/16/edicion-de-textos-de-simon-rodriguez-inventamos-o-erramos-1794-1850/))

### Editorial Gesamtkunstwerk: *Sociedades americanas en 1828*

- **1828–1842, unfinished:** *Sociedades americanas en 1828: cómo serán y cómo podrían ser en los siglos venideros*. Rodríguez’s overarching philosophical, political, pedagogical, typographical, and editorial project. It is best treated as an unfinished Gesamtkunstwerk rather than as one stable book: five authorial print manifestations survive, while the projected complete work does not. ([UAM, documented facsimile edition](https://casadelibrosabiertos.uam.mx/gpd-sociedades-americanas-en-1828.html); [Revista de Crítica Literaria Latinoamericana](https://rcllletras.unmsm.edu.pe/index.php/content/pt_BR/article/view/756))

- **1828:** *“Pródromo” a Sociedades americanas en 1828*. A twenty-four-page prospectus printed at Arequipa to announce, exemplify, and seek support for the larger projected work. It acts as manifesto and specimen rather than merely introducing a finished book. ([2016 *Obras completas*](https://biblioteca.clacso.edu.ar/clacso/se/20190926042843/Simon_Rodriguez_Obras_Completas.pdf); [Museo del Libro Venezolano](https://museodellibrovenezolano.libroria.com/sociedades-americanas-en-1828/))

- **1834:** *“Galeato” a Luces y virtudes sociales*. A defensive prologue printed at Concepción with material belonging to the developing *Sociedades americanas* project; it answers attacks on the *Pródromo* and therefore functions as a polemical shield or manifesto. Older bibliographies often call the whole 1834 printing *Luces y virtudes sociales*, while the collected edition isolates the “Galeato.” ([2016 *Obras completas*](https://biblioteca.clacso.edu.ar/clacso/se/20190926042843/Simon_Rodriguez_Obras_Completas.pdf); [documented facsimile edition](https://biblioteca.clacso.edu.ar/clacso/se/20191002033211/Sociedades_americanas_en_1828.pdf))

- **1840:** *Extracto de la Introducción a una obra intitulada Sociedades americanas en 1828*. A newspaper excerpt published in *El Mercurio* of Valparaíso: simultaneously an autonomous periodical intervention and an advertisement or fragment of the still-unfinished project. It is collected under the editorial heading *Extractos*, not securely as a freestanding book. ([Carlos H. Jorge](https://es.scribd.com/document/389772219/Articulo-Carlos-Jorge); [2016 *Obras completas*](https://biblioteca.clacso.edu.ar/clacso/se/20190926042843/Simon_Rodriguez_Obras_Completas.pdf))

- **1840:** *Sociedades americanas en 1828: cómo serán y cómo podrían ser en los siglos venideros. Primera parte: Luces y virtudes sociales*. A typographically composed philosophical-pedagogical treatise printed at Valparaíso. Its spatial arrangement, changes of type, diagrams, and “logography” make the printed page part of the argument, resisting reduction to verbal content alone. ([Museo del Libro Venezolano](https://museodellibrovenezolano.libroria.com/sociedades-americanas-en-1828/); [2016 *Obras completas*](https://biblioteca.clacso.edu.ar/clacso/se/20190926042843/Simon_Rodriguez_Obras_Completas.pdf))

- **1842:** *Sociedades americanas en 1828: cómo serán y cómo podrían ser en los siglos venideros*. The Lima manifestation of the project, substantially reconfigured and expanded but still not the promised complete work. Calling it merely a new “edition” obscures Rodríguez’s continuing remaking of the text and its visual form. ([UAM, documented facsimile edition](https://casadelibrosabiertos.uam.mx/gpd-sociedades-americanas-en-1828.html); [2016 *Obras completas*](https://biblioteca.clacso.edu.ar/clacso/se/20190926042843/Simon_Rodriguez_Obras_Completas.pdf))

- **1847, lost or unlocated:** *Extracto de mis ideas*. A notebook reportedly sent to Anselmo Pineda and known only from Rodríguez’s letter of 2 February 1847. Its existence is referential rather than material: no original is known, so it resists treatment as an extant work and may have contained material from the broader *Sociedades americanas* project. ([UNAM thesis on the editorial project](https://tesiunamdocumentos.dgb.unam.mx/ptd2015/abril/0727588/0727588.pdf))

### Political manifestos and public interventions

- **1830:** *El Libertador del Mediodía de América y sus compañeros de armas, defendidos por un amigo de la causa social*. A book-length public defense of Bolívar and the emancipatory cause. It is less detached biography than an intervention intended to alter political judgment—a manifesto in the form of historical vindication. ([2016 *Obras completas*](https://biblioteca.clacso.edu.ar/clacso/se/20190926042843/Simon_Rodriguez_Obras_Completas.pdf); [Fundación Empresas Polar](https://bibliofep.fundacionempresaspolar.org/dhv/entradas/r/rodriguez-simon/))

- **1840:** *Extractos de la Defensa de Bolívar*. Three selections from the 1830 defense, republished in *El Mercurio* of Valparaíso. They are manifestations and strategic recirculations of the earlier work rather than a wholly new treatise. ([Campos Kennett](https://prueba.repositorioinstitucionaluacm.mx/jspui/bitstream/123456789/2835/1/Gloria%20Soledad%20Campos%20Kennett.pdf); [Carlos H. Jorge](https://es.scribd.com/document/389772219/Articulo-Carlos-Jorge))

- **1840:** *Partidos*. A sequence of eleven newspaper articles published in *El Mercurio* of Valparaíso, analyzing political faction and republican social order. The sequence is a serial public intervention, not a book, although collected editions bind it as one textual unit. ([2016 *Obras completas*](https://biblioteca.clacso.edu.ar/clacso/se/20190926042843/Simon_Rodriguez_Obras_Completas.pdf); [Campos Kennett](https://prueba.repositorioinstitucionaluacm.mx/jspui/bitstream/123456789/2835/1/Gloria%20Soledad%20Campos%20Kennett.pdf))

- **1839; published 1844:** *Carta del señor Rodríguez a cinco bolivianos a la caída de la Confederación Perú-Boliviana*. A political open letter composed on 8 June 1839 and subsequently printed in the Valparaíso press. It occupies the unstable boundary between private correspondence, public manifesto, and newspaper article; the text itself has also been reported as unlocated in later archival surveys. ([Campos Kennett](https://prueba.repositorioinstitucionaluacm.mx/jspui/bitstream/123456789/2835/1/Gloria%20Soledad%20Campos%20Kennett.pdf); [Memorias de Venezuela](https://memoriasdevenezuela.wordpress.com/2016/11/21/documentos-ineditos-dan-luces-para-reconstruir-con-mayor-precision-la-vida-y-obra-de-simon-rodriguez/))

- **1843:** *Crítica de las providencias del Gobierno*. A political-economic critique printed at Lima, directed at governmental measures and their social consequences. Its immediate argumentative purpose makes it a public intervention rather than a systematic treatise. ([2016 *Obras completas*](https://biblioteca.clacso.edu.ar/clacso/se/20190926042843/Simon_Rodriguez_Obras_Completas.pdf))

### Pedagogical condensations

- **1848–1849:** *Extracto sucinto de mi obra sobre la educación republicana*. A condensed statement of Rodríguez’s educational project, published as three newspaper installments in Bogotá’s *El Neo-Granadino*. Some bibliographies date it 1848, but the newspaper appearance and the collected edition date it 1849; it is an extract from a larger conceived obra, not evidence that the complete parent work survives. ([2016 *Obras completas*](https://biblioteca.clacso.edu.ar/clacso/se/20190926042843/Simon_Rodriguez_Obras_Completas.pdf); [*Inventamos o erramos* contents](https://elsudamericano.wordpress.com/2013/11/16/edicion-de-textos-de-simon-rodriguez-inventamos-o-erramos-1794-1850/))

### Correspondence

- **1824–1853:** *Cartas a distintos corresponsales*. A surviving body of twenty-six letters in the 2016 collected edition: four to Simón Bolívar; five to Bernardino Pradel; three to Roberto Ascázubi; two each to Pedro Antonio Torres, José Ignacio París, and Anselmo Pineda; and individual letters to Diego Ibarra, Bolívar’s secretary, General Salom, Francisco de Paula Otero, Manuel Carvajal, Santiago Duquet, Pedro Fernández, and José Trinidad Morán. This is an incomplete archival remainder rather than a work designed as a collection; additional letters are reported lost, missing, or subsequently discovered. ([2016 *Obras completas*](https://biblioteca.clacso.edu.ar/clacso/se/20190926042843/Simon_Rodriguez_Obras_Completas.pdf); [UNESR, *Simón Rodríguez: Cartas*](https://biblioteca.clacso.edu.ar/clacso/se/20191003045015/cartas.pdf))

### Disputed and excluded attributions

- **1801, attribution rejected:** *Atala* by François-René de Chateaubriand, Spanish translation. Pedro Grases’s earlier *Escritos* and the 1975 *Obras completas* attributed this translation to Rodríguez, but the editors of the 2016 *Obras completas* exclude it because they do not regard his authorship as established. It therefore cannot safely be listed as something Rodríguez made. ([1975 edition catalogue record](https://catalagobiblioteca.uma.edu.ve/cgi-bin/koha/opac-detail.pl?biblionumber=11953); [2016 *Obras completas*, editorial criteria](https://biblioteca.clacso.edu.ar/clacso/se/20190926042843/Simon_Rodriguez_Obras_Completas.pdf))

- **1835, joint attribution rejected:** *Informe presentado a la Intendencia de la provincia de Concepción por Ambrosio Lozier, Simón Rodríguez y Juan José Arteaga, nombrados para reconocer la ciudad de Concepción y sus cercanías después del terremoto del 20 de febrero de 1835*. Earlier bibliographies treated this survey as a jointly authored scientific report; the 2016 editors exclude it because Rodríguez’s authorship is not securely established. It is consequently a disputed collaborative document, not an uncontested Rodríguez obra. ([2016 *Obras completas*, editorial criteria](https://biblioteca.clacso.edu.ar/clacso/se/20190926042843/Simon_Rodriguez_Obras_Completas.pdf); [UNAM thesis on the editorial project](https://tesiunamdocumentos.dgb.unam.mx/ptd2015/abril/0727588/0727588.pdf))
