## Works by Marie Sophie Taglioni

### Choreographic obras

- **1860:** *Le Papillon*. A collaborative ballet-pantomime in two acts and four tableaux: Taglioni made the choreography, Jules-Henri Vernoy de Saint-Georges the libretto, and Jacques Offenbach the music. It premiered at the Paris Opéra on 26 November; specialist accounts identify it as Taglioni’s only completed ballet, so the theatrical Gesamtkunstwerk should not be mistaken for her sole-authored object. [BnF](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb396844084.public), [INHA](https://www.inha.fr/actualites/marie-taglioni-a-la-pointe-du-romantisme/)

### Unfinished choreographic projects

- **1862–1863:** *Zara*. An unrealized second ballet for the Paris Opéra, planned for Emma Livry with a libretto by Charles Nuitter. Work stopped after Livry’s fatal accident and after difficulties replacing her; it survives as a project rather than a performed creation. [Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/taglioni_%28Dizionario-Biografico%29/)

- **Undated:** *Pandore*. A preliminary ballet draft preserved in the Fonds Taglioni. The public finding aid supplies neither a performance nor evidence of completion, so this is an archival project, not a securely constituted ballet. [Fonds Taglioni finding aid](https://archives.nypl.org/dan/19720)

- **Undated:** Preliminary drafts for other, untitled ballets. The Fonds Taglioni groups these drafts with *Pandore* but does not enumerate or individually describe them; they therefore resist division into securely titled works, and their completeness and precise attribution cannot be settled from the finding aid. [Fonds Taglioni finding aid](https://archives.nypl.org/dan/19720)

### Autobiographical obra

- **Undated:** *Souvenirs*. An autobiographical work surviving in seven authorial notebooks together with additional manuscript leaves and several later copies or typescripts. Bruno Ligore’s critical edition reconstructs the dispersed text while removing interpolations introduced by earlier editors; its archival multiplicity makes it a textual obra without one simple original book-form. [INHA](https://www.inha.fr/actualites/marie-taglioni-a-la-pointe-du-romantisme/), [account of Ligore’s critical edition](https://www.ilcorrieremusicale.it/recensionelibri/ricordi-di-maria-taglioni/), [Fonds Taglioni finding aid](https://archives.nypl.org/dan/19720)

### Fragmentary and scribal objects

- **Undated:** Miscellaneous writings and maxims. A typescript dossier, catalogued as pages 147–159 in the Fonds Taglioni, rather than a work with a documented authorial title or settled boundaries. It resists treatment as a distinct finished text because the finding aid does not identify the underlying manuscripts or their dates. [Fonds Taglioni finding aid](https://archives.nypl.org/dan/19720)

- **Undated; source diary covers 1831–1870:** Transcript of Philippe Taglioni’s daily diary. An eighty-page manuscript copied by Marie Taglioni: materially her scribal production, but not her authored work, since the diary itself is her father’s. [Fonds Taglioni finding aid](https://archives.nypl.org/dan/19720)
