## Works by Saint Erasmus of Formia

### No securely attributable obra

- No text, artwork, building, protocol, manifesto, or other made thing can be securely attributed to Erasmus. The standard hagiographic repertory records instead an anonymous *Martyrdom of Saint Erasmus* (BHL 2578–2585; BHG 602), a work **about** him: its earliest Latin witnesses are ninth-century manuscripts, its original language and date remain disputed, and no recension claims Erasmus as author.
