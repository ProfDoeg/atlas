## Works by Paul Adrien Maurice Dirac

### Scientific papers, lectures, and reports, 1924–1948

- **1924:** *Dissociation under a Temperature Gradient*. Scientific paper on thermodiffusion; Dirac’s first published research.
- **1924:** *Note on the Relativity Dynamics of a Particle*. Scientific note in relativistic mechanics.
- **1924:** *Note on the Doppler Principle and Bohr’s Frequency Condition*. Scientific paper connecting relativity with early quantum theory.
- **1924:** *The Conditions for Statistical Equilibrium between Atoms, Electrons and Radiation*. Scientific paper, co-authored with Ralph H. Fowler.
- **1925:** *The Adiabatic Invariance of the Quantum Integrals*. Scientific paper in the old quantum theory.
- **1925:** *The Effect of Compton Scattering by Free Electrons in a Stellar Atmosphere*. Astrophysical paper.
- **1925:** *The Fundamental Equations of Quantum Mechanics*. Scientific paper establishing Dirac’s algebraic formulation of quantum mechanics.
- **1926:** *The Adiabatic Hypothesis for Magnetic Fields*. Scientific paper.
- **1926:** *Quantum Mechanics and a Preliminary Investigation of the Hydrogen Atom*. Scientific paper applying quantum mechanics to hydrogen; it was also Dirac’s doctoral thesis, so it exists both as a dissertation and as a published paper.
- **1926:** *The Elimination of the Nodes in Quantum Mechanics*. Scientific paper.
- **1926:** *Relativity Quantum Mechanics with an Application to Compton Scattering*. Scientific paper.
- **1926:** *Quantum Mechanics*. Expository scientific article, not the later book of that general subject.
- **1926:** *On Quantum Algebra*. Scientific paper.
- **1926:** *On the Theory of Quantum Mechanics*. Scientific paper introducing the quantum treatment of identical particles associated with Fermi–Dirac statistics.
- **1927:** *The Compton Effect in Wave Mechanics*. Scientific paper.
- **1927:** *The Physical Interpretation of the Quantum Dynamics*. Scientific paper developing transformation theory and the probabilistic interpretation of quantum mechanics.
- **1927:** *The Quantum Theory of the Emission and Absorption of Radiation*. Scientific paper foundational to quantum field theory.
- **1927:** *The Quantum Theory of Dispersion*. Scientific paper.
- **1927:** *Über die Quantenmechanik der Stoßvorgänge*. German-language scientific paper on collision processes.
- **1928:** *The Quantum Theory of the Electron, I*. Scientific paper presenting the relativistic electron equation now called the Dirac equation.
- **1928:** *The Quantum Theory of the Electron, II*. Continuation of the preceding scientific paper.
- **1928:** *Quantum Mechanics: The Quantum Postulate*. Conference contribution responding to the conceptual foundations of quantum mechanics.
- **1928:** *Über die Quantentheorie des Elektrons*. German-language exposition of Dirac’s electron theory.
- **1928:** *Zur Quantentheorie des Elektrons*. German-language scientific contribution concerning the electron theory.
- **1929:** *The Basis of Statistical Quantum Mechanics*. Scientific paper introducing the density-operator treatment of statistical ensembles.
- **1929:** *Quantum Mechanics of Many-Electron Systems*. Scientific paper on relativistic many-electron theory.
- **1930:** *A Theory of Electrons and Protons*. Scientific paper developing the hole theory that preceded Dirac’s explicit positron interpretation.
- **1930:** *On the Annihilation of Electrons and Protons*. Scientific paper; its “protons” belong to Dirac’s then-current hole interpretation and should not be silently modernized into positrons.
- **1930:** *Note on Exchange Phenomena in the Thomas Atom*. Scientific paper.
- **1930:** *The Proton*. Short scientific contribution reflecting Dirac’s evolving electron–hole theory.
- **1930:** *Approximate Methods*. Scientific lecture or proceedings contribution on approximation in quantum mechanics.
- **1931:** *Note on the Interpretation of the Density Matrix in the Many-Electron Problem*. Scientific note.
- **1931:** *Quantised Singularities in the Electromagnetic Field*. Scientific paper proposing magnetic monopoles and the electric-charge quantization condition.
- **1931:** *Quelques problèmes de mécanique quantique*. French-language lecture text on problems in quantum mechanics.
- **1932:** *Photo-Electric Absorption in Hydrogen-Like Atoms*. Scientific paper.
- **1932:** *Relativistic Quantum Mechanics*. Scientific lecture or proceedings paper.
- **1932:** *On Quantum Electrodynamics*. Scientific paper co-authored with Vladimir Fock and Boris Podolsky.
- **1932:** *Paramagnetic Susceptibility; Electron Energy in a Magnetic Field*. Two-part scientific contribution.
- **1932:** *Some Problems in Quantum Theory*. Lecture text or conference contribution.
- **1933:** *The Lagrangian in Quantum Mechanics*. Scientific paper whose amplitude-over-histories idea became an important precursor of the path integral.
- **1933:** *The Reflection of Electrons from Standing Light Waves*. Scientific paper co-authored with Peter Kapitza.
- **1933:** *Homogeneous Variables in Classical Dynamics*. Mathematical-physics paper.
- **1933:** *Statement of a Problem in Quantum Mechanics*. Short problem statement rather than a conventional research exposition; its resistance to being called a full “work” is part of its form.
- **1934:** *Théorie du positron*. French-language lecture text on positron theory.
- **1934:** *Teoriya pozitrona*. Russian-language manifestation of Dirac’s positron lecture; the collected edition treats it separately, although its status as a distinct work rather than a translated manifestation is debatable.
- **1934:** *Theory of Electrons and Positrons*. Scientific lecture or proceedings contribution.
- **1934:** *Discussion of the Infinite Distribution of Electrons in the Theory of the Positron*. Discussion contribution rather than a self-contained paper.
- **1935:** *The Electron Wave Equation in de Sitter Space*. Scientific paper.
- **1936:** *Does Conservation of Energy Hold in Atomic Processes?* Short scientific and conceptual article.
- **1936:** *Relativistic Wave Equations*. Scientific paper developing higher-spin relativistic equations.
- **1936:** *Wave Equations in Conformal Space*. Mathematical-physics paper.
- **1937:** *The Cosmological Constants*. Short scientific paper introducing Dirac’s large-numbers reasoning.
- **1937:** *Physical Science and Philosophy*. Philosophical lecture or essay rather than a technical research paper.
- **1937:** *Complex Variables in Quantum Mechanics*. Scientific paper.
- **1937:** *The Reversal Operator in Quantum Mechanics*. Scientific paper.
- **1938:** *A New Basis for Cosmology*. Scientific paper developing the large-numbers hypothesis.
- **1938:** *Classical Theory of Radiating Electrons*. Scientific paper on radiation reaction.
- **1939:** *The Relation between Mathematics and Physics*. Public lecture and philosophical statement about theoretical physics.
- **1939:** *A New Notation for Quantum Mechanics*. Scientific paper introducing the bra–ket notation.
- **1939:** *La théorie de l’électron et du champ électromagnétique*. Extended French-language lecture course or monograph-length exposition, not merely a journal article.
- **1940:** *Dr. M. Mathisson*. Obituary of the physicist Myron Mathisson.
- **1941:** *The Theory of the Separation of Isotopes by Statistical Methods*. Wartime research report. It was not conventionally published at the time and therefore resists identification with a normal publication.
- **1942:** *The Physical Interpretation of Quantum Mechanics*. Bakerian Lecture.
- **1942:** *On Lorentz Invariance in the Quantum Theory*. Wartime scientific paper co-authored with Rudolf Peierls and M. H. L. Pryce.
- **1942:** *The Motion in a Self-Fractionating Centrifuge*. Classified wartime isotope-separation report, later published in the collected edition.
- **1942:** *Expansion of U-Sphere Enclosed in a Container: Solution of Differential Equation for the Expansion of the Boundary Layer*. Wartime manuscript concerning uranium-chain calculations; it survives as a research report rather than an ordinary publication.
- **1942:** *Neutron Multiplication in a Sphere of Uniform Density Surrounded by a Shell of Non-Uniform Density*. Wartime nuclear-research manuscript.
- **1942:** *Estimate of the Efficiency of Energy Release with a Non-Scattering Container*. Wartime nuclear-research manuscript.
- **1943:** *Quantum Electrodynamics*. Extended wartime lecture or research text.
- **1943:** *Approximate Rate of Neutron Multiplication for a Solid of Arbitrary Shape and Uniform Density, I: General Theory*. Classified wartime report.
- **1943:** *Approximate Rate of Neutron Multiplication for a Solid of Arbitrary Shape and Uniform Density, II: Application to the Oblate Spheroid, Hemisphere and Oblate Hemispheroid*. Classified wartime report and application of the preceding theory.
- **1944:** *Note on the Approximation in MS.D.3*. Brief wartime technical note whose archival identifier forms part of its title.
- **1944:** *Perturbation Method for U-Sphere in a Scattering Container*. Wartime technical report.
- **1945:** *Unitary Representations of the Lorentz Group*. Mathematical-physics paper.
- **1945:** *On the Analogy between Classical and Quantum Mechanics*. Scientific paper.
- **1945:** *Application of Quaternions to Lorentz Transformations*. Mathematical-physics paper.
- **1946:** *Developments in Quantum Electrodynamics*. Lecture or proceedings contribution.
- **1946:** *Elementary Particles and Their Interactions*. Lecture or proceedings contribution.
- **1947:** *The Difficulties in Quantum Electrodynamics*. Scientific lecture addressing unresolved divergences.
- **1948:** *On the Theory of Point Electrons*. Scientific paper.
- **1948:** *Quelques développements sur la théorie atomique*. Published French lecture delivered in 1945.
- **1948:** *Quantum Theory of Localizable Dynamical Systems*. Scientific paper.
- **1948:** *The Theory of Magnetic Poles*. Scientific paper extending Dirac’s monopole theory.
- **1948:** *Magnetic Poles*. Short expository article; related to, but not identical in form with, the full paper *The Theory of Magnetic Poles*.

### Books and sustained lecture-texts

- **1930:** *The Principles of Quantum Mechanics*. Foundational treatise and obra in its own right. Dirac substantially revised it in later editions—especially the second edition of 1935, third of 1947, and fourth of 1958—but translations and reprints are manifestations rather than separate works.
- **1964:** *Lectures on Quantum Mechanics*. Graduate lecture monograph on constrained Hamiltonian systems; a second printing appeared in 1967.
- **1966:** *Lectures on Quantum Field Theory*. Graduate lecture monograph based on Dirac’s 1963–64 Yeshiva University lectures.
- **1971:** *The Development of Quantum Theory*. Book-form manifestation of the Oppenheimer Memorial Prize address rather than an unrelated second work.
- **1974:** *Spinors in Hilbert Space*. Lecture-derived mathematical-physics monograph based on lectures delivered at the University of Miami in 1969.
- **1975:** *General Theory of Relativity*. Concise textbook or lecture monograph.
- **1978:** *Directions in Physics*. A book comprising five lectures delivered in Australia and New Zealand in 1975; it is a constructed lecture-cycle rather than a conventional systematic textbook.

### Conceptual constructions

- **1926:** *Fermi–Dirac statistics*. A theoretical construction made independently in closely related form by Dirac and Enrico Fermi; it is not a text, and sole authorship should not be assigned to Dirac.
- **1927:** *Transformation theory*. A mathematical framework for quantum mechanics developed by Dirac and, independently, Pascual Jordan; it is a body of theory rather than one bounded text.
- **1927:** *Quantum theory of radiation*. A theoretical framework in which radiation modes are quantized and particles may be created or destroyed; it is manifested principally in Dirac’s 1927 radiation paper.
- **1928:** *The relativistic electron equation*. A mathematical object now called the Dirac equation. It is not itself a publication, although its primary manifestation is *The Quantum Theory of the Electron, I*.
- **1928:** *Dirac matrices and spinor electron theory*. Mathematical structures created within the relativistic electron project rather than separately titled works.
- **1930–1931:** *Hole theory of the positron*. A developing theoretical project spread across several papers. Its early identification of the hole with the proton was abandoned, and the later positron interpretation should not be projected backward without qualification.
- **1931–1948:** *Magnetic-monopole theory*. A continuing theoretical project manifested in *Quantised Singularities in the Electromagnetic Field* and *The Theory of Magnetic Poles*.
- **1933:** *Quantum action-phase correspondence*. The conceptual construction in *The Lagrangian in Quantum Mechanics* that anticipated the path-integral formulation; the later complete path integral was Feynman’s, not Dirac’s.
- **1937–1979:** *Large-numbers cosmology*. A long-running theoretical project rather than a single work, developed through *The Cosmological Constants*, *A New Basis for Cosmology*, and later papers on varying \(G\).
- **1939:** *Bra–ket notation*. A made symbolic system introduced in *A New Notation for Quantum Mechanics*. It is a notation or protocol for writing quantum mechanics, not a book.
- **1949:** *The three forms of relativistic dynamics*. A classificatory theoretical construction—instant, point, and front forms—made in *Forms of Relativistic Dynamics*.
- **1950s–1960s:** *Constrained Hamiltonian dynamics*. A body of methods, now associated with Dirac constraints and brackets, developed across papers and *Lectures on Quantum Mechanics* rather than existing as one bounded text.
- **1962:** *Extensible electron model*. A theoretical model whose primary textual manifestation is *An Extensible Model of the Electron*.

### Unfinished, archival, and problematic objects

- **1939–1945:** *Classified wartime research for the British atomic-energy project*. A body of calculations, memoranda, and reports concerning isotope separation and neutron multiplication. Dalitz’s collected edition prints the surviving identified papers, but the later comprehensive bibliography warns that additional classified reports may remain secret; consequently no honest list can claim documentary completeness.
- **Throughout Dirac’s career:** *Lectures surviving through notes, transcriptions, translations, or later publication*. These resist simple authorship classification because the intellectual content is Dirac’s while wording, editing, and sometimes titles may be supplied by recorders or editors.
- **Undated or unpublished:** *Research notebooks, calculations, correspondence, and lecture manuscripts in the Paul A. M. Dirac papers*. These are archival traces and working materials rather than a finite catalogue of titled works; treating every sheet as a separately authored obra would manufacture boundaries the archive itself does not supply.
