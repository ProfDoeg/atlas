## Works by Ștefan Odobleja

### Medical thesis and monographs

- **1928:** *Accidentele de automobile: studiu medico-legal*. Doctoral thesis in medicine and surgery, prepared at Bucharest’s Institute of Legal Medicine under Mina Minovici. Some later references shorten the title to *Accidentele de automobil*; a scholarly biographical study reproduces the cover with the plural form used here. [Marin Vlada, “Viața și opera lui Ștefan Odobleja”](https://studii.crifst.ro/doc/2020/2020_03_2.pdf), pp. 123–124.

- **1929:** *Metodă de transonanță toracică*. Clinical study, first published in *Buletinul medico-terapeutic* on 1 May; bibliographies sometimes treat it as a separate booklet, but the evidence consulted securely establishes the journal study, not a distinct obra in book form. [Vlada](https://studii.crifst.ro/doc/2020/2020_03_2.pdf), p. 124; [bibliographical listing](https://ro.sberopedia.org/wiki/%C8%98tefan_Odobleja).

- **1935:** *La phonoscopie: nouvelle méthode d’exploration clinique*. Medical monograph proposing “phonoscopie” as an acoustic method of clinical exploration; printed at Lugoj and distributed through Gaston Doin in Paris. Sources repeatedly give 1935, but some later bibliographies give **1932**, so the publication date is not bibliographically settled. [Vlada](https://studii.crifst.ro/doc/2020/2020_03_2.pdf), pp. 124–126; [Viața Medicală](https://www.viata-medicala.ro/-tefan-odobleja-un-precursor-6621); [contrary 1932 listing](https://www.curteadelaarges.ro/arhiva/XIII_10_143/XIII_10_143.pdf).

### Consonantist psychology and general cybernetics

- **1937:** *Prospect announcing Psychologie consonantiste*. French prospect distributed at the Ninth International Congress of Military Medicine and Pharmacy in Bucharest. It is a paratext and publishing manifesto—an announcement intended to bring the projected treatise into existence—rather than the treatise itself; the consulted sources do not supply a securely fixed independent title. [Vlada](https://studii.crifst.ro/doc/2020/2020_03_2.pdf), pp. 124–125; [Viața Medicală](https://www.viata-medicala.ro/-tefan-odobleja-un-precursor-6621).

- **1938–1939:** *Psychologie consonantiste*. Two-volume, approximately 880-page French treatise issued through Librairie Maloine in Paris: a deliberately interdisciplinary obra joining psychology, logic, physiology, epistemology, social analysis, and a proposed general science of reversible causal relations. Volume I appeared in 1938 and volume II in 1939; later Romanian, English, and French publications are translations or re-editions, not additional works. [Vlada](https://studii.crifst.ro/doc/2020/2020_03_2.pdf), pp. 125–126; [bibliographical record and publication history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C8%98tefan_Odobleja).

- **1975; proceedings 1976/1977:** *Cibernetica și psihologia consonantistă* / *Cybernetics and Consonantist Psychology*. Congress paper presented at the Third International Congress of Cybernetics and Systems in Bucharest and subsequently printed in the proceedings. Sources disagree whether the proceedings appearance should be dated 1976 or 1977; the presentation itself is securely dated 1975. [Vlada](https://studii.crifst.ro/doc/2020/2020_03_2.pdf), p. 129; [Academy of Romanian Scientists study](https://www.aos.ro/wp-content/anale/IFVol2Nr1Art.5.pdf), p. 51.

- **1978:** *Psihologia consonantistă și cibernetica*. Synthetic and polemical book identifying consonantist psychology with an earlier general cybernetics and asserting Odobleja’s priority. He completed it and saw proofs, but the printed volume appeared only after his death; it is therefore his obra while its final material realization was posthumous and editorially mediated. [Vlada](https://studii.crifst.ro/doc/2020/2020_03_2.pdf), p. 129; [Viața Medicală](https://www.viata-medicala.ro/-tefan-odobleja-un-precursor-6621); [Ștefan Odobleja Jr. interview](https://www.gds.ro/Actualitate/2012-07-14/Stefan-Odobleja-Jr-Tatal-meu-a-fost-un-vizionar/).

- **1978:** *Diversity and Unity in Cybernetics*. Congress paper sent to the Fourth International Congress of Cybernetics and Systems in Amsterdam. Because illness prevented Odobleja from attending, Stelian Bajureanu presented it for him on 21–25 August; the work is Odobleja’s, while its spoken manifestation belongs to an emissary. [Congress proceedings](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-642-93104-8); [Nicolae Jurcău, “Two Specialists in Cybernetics”](https://www.bu.edu/wcp/Papers/Comp/CompJurc.htm); [Academy of Romanian Scientists study](https://www.aos.ro/wp-content/anale/IFVol2Nr1Art.5.pdf), p. 51.

### Medical studies and clinical protocols

- **1928:** *Procedeu practic pentru a împiedica aburirea oglinzilor laringoscopice*. Short practical protocol published in *Revista sanitară militară*, nos. 11–12, instructing clinicians how to prevent laryngoscopic mirrors from fogging. [Vlada](https://studii.crifst.ro/doc/2020/2020_03_2.pdf), p. 124.

- **1929:** *Aplicațiile gravitațiunii în terapeutică*. Therapeutic study published in *Revista sanitară militară*, no. 4. Its title is also modernized in scholarship as *Aplicațiile gravitației în terapeutică*. [Vlada](https://studii.crifst.ro/doc/2020/2020_03_2.pdf), p. 124; [bibliographical listing](https://ro.sberopedia.org/wiki/%C8%98tefan_Odobleja).

- **1929:** *Un nou semn în pleurezie*. Clinical diagnostic note published in the March issue of *Buletinul medico-terapeutic*. [Bibliographical listing](https://ro.sberopedia.org/wiki/%C8%98tefan_Odobleja).

- **1929:** *Celulita*. Medical article published in the December issue of *Buletinul medico-terapeutic*. [Bibliographical listing](https://ro.sberopedia.org/wiki/%C8%98tefan_Odobleja).

- **1930:** *Atitudinile corpului și secreția sudorală*. Experimental medical study published in the January issue of *Buletinul medico-terapeutic*. One later scholarly narrative confusingly calls this the subject of his 1928 thesis, although the reproduced thesis cover reads *Accidentele de automobile*; the article and thesis should therefore not be conflated. [Vlada](https://studii.crifst.ro/doc/2020/2020_03_2.pdf), pp. 123–124; [bibliographical listing](https://ro.sberopedia.org/wiki/%C8%98tefan_Odobleja).

- **1930:** *Procedeu pentru înregistrarea grafică a secreției sudorale*. Experimental protocol published in the March–April issue of *Buletinul medico-terapeutic*. [Bibliographical listing](https://ro.sberopedia.org/wiki/%C8%98tefan_Odobleja).

- **1930:** *Tabel sinoptic de durata boalelor infecțioase pe faze*. Synoptic diagnostic or teaching table published in the August issue of *Buletinul medico-terapeutic*. Its tabular form makes it an information instrument as much as a conventional article. [Bibliographical listing](https://ro.sberopedia.org/wiki/%C8%98tefan_Odobleja).

- **1930:** *Valoarea comportării sunetelor de plescăit și de frecare*. Clinical-acoustics study published in the October–November issue of *Buletinul medico-terapeutic*. [Bibliographical listing](https://ro.sberopedia.org/wiki/%C8%98tefan_Odobleja).

- **1930:** *Transsonanța sunetelor de frecare*. Clinical-acoustics article published in *Spitalul* in December. The spelling “transsonanță” is retained because it appears in the bibliographical source and may represent Odobleja’s technical terminology rather than a typographical variant. [Bibliographical listing](https://ro.sberopedia.org/wiki/%C8%98tefan_Odobleja).

- **1933:** *Percuția cordului*. Clinical study published in the December issue of *Buletinul medico-terapeutic*. [Bibliographical listing](https://ro.sberopedia.org/wiki/%C8%98tefan_Odobleja).

- **1934:** *Semnul banului*. Diagnostic study published in *Mișcarea medicală română*, nos. 4–5. [Bibliographical listing](https://ro.sberopedia.org/wiki/%C8%98tefan_Odobleja).

- **1934:** *Fonoscopia*. Expository medical study published in *Mișcarea medicală română*, nos. 7–8, presenting the method that Odobleja developed more fully in his monograph. [Bibliographical listing](https://ro.sberopedia.org/wiki/%C8%98tefan_Odobleja); [Vlada](https://studii.crifst.ro/doc/2020/2020_03_2.pdf), pp. 124–126.

- **1934:** *Transsonanța sunetelor de frecare și plescăit de cord*. Clinical-acoustics study published in the May issue of *Buletinul medico-terapeutic*. [Bibliographical listing](https://ro.sberopedia.org/wiki/%C8%98tefan_Odobleja).

- **1934:** *Fonoscopia în colecțiile pericardice*. Applied clinical study published in *Mișcarea medicală română*, nos. 7–8. [Bibliographical listing](https://ro.sberopedia.org/wiki/%C8%98tefan_Odobleja).

- **1935:** *Percuția și fonoscopia ficatului*. Applied diagnostic study published in *Mișcarea medicală română*, nos. 1–2. [Bibliographical listing](https://ro.sberopedia.org/wiki/%C8%98tefan_Odobleja).

- **1936:** *La phonoscopie et son importance sémiologique* / *Fonoscopia și semiologia acustică*. Study in *Revista sanitară militară*, no. 2, extending the monograph’s method into a broader acoustic semiology. Bibliographies give both French and Romanian forms, and it is unclear from the accessible descriptions whether these designate two language versions or alternative renderings of one article. [Vlada](https://studii.crifst.ro/doc/2020/2020_03_2.pdf), p. 124; [bibliographical listing](https://ro.sberopedia.org/wiki/%C8%98tefan_Odobleja).

- **1937:** *Démonstration de phonoscopie*. Scientific communication delivered at the Ninth International Congress of Military Medicine and Pharmacy in Bucharest, 8–12 June, and printed in volume II of its proceedings. It was both a demonstration—an enacted manifestation of the diagnostic method—and a congress text. [Vlada](https://studii.crifst.ro/doc/2020/2020_03_2.pdf), p. 124; [bibliographical listing](https://ro.sberopedia.org/wiki/%C8%98tefan_Odobleja).

### Military psychology and theory

- **1938:** *Teoria și practica*. Conceptual study published in *Spirit militar modern*, no. 1, examining the definitions and reciprocal dependence of theory and practice in military organization. [Vlada](https://studii.crifst.ro/doc/2020/2020_03_2.pdf), p. 126.

- **1939:** *Cantitate și calitate*. Military-psychology study published in *Spirit militar modern*, nos. 5–6, applying the relation between quantity and quality to the selection of military personnel. [Vlada](https://studii.crifst.ro/doc/2020/2020_03_2.pdf), p. 126.

- **circa 1939–1940:** *Simetria și dualismul războiului* / *Simetria și dualismul în știința războiului*. Military-theory study published in *Spirit militar modern*. Sources give variant titles and do not consistently supply the issue or year, so neither a single title form nor a more exact date should be forced. [Vlada](https://studii.crifst.ro/doc/2020/2020_03_2.pdf), p. 126; [later survey of *Spirit militar modern*](https://www.cultuleroilor.ro/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Romania-eroica-2-74-2025.pdf).

### Unfinished projects and manuscript bodies

- **from 1946 to 1978:** *Logica rezonanței*. Vast philosophical and logical project begun after Odobleja left the army and never completed as a finished book. The surviving manuscript body reportedly includes about 15,000 pages on logic; *Introducere în logica rezonanței: scrieri inedite* (1984, second edition 2003) is an editor-selected posthumous manifestation, prepared by Alexandru Surdu with a preface by Constantin Noica, not a book finalized in that form by Odobleja. [Vlada](https://studii.crifst.ro/doc/2020/2020_03_2.pdf), p. 129; [contemporary report on the manuscripts and 1984 edition](https://www.bibliotecadeva.ro/periodice/flacara/1984/08/flacara_1984_08_32.pdf); [Romanian Academy digital bibliography](https://www.crifst.ro/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Stefan_Odobleja_biblioteca_digitala.pdf).

- **principally 1946–1978:** *The surviving manuscript corpus*. Approximately 50,000 manuscript pages are reported in the Ștefan Odobleja archival fonds at the Mehedinți branch of Romania’s National Archives. This is a body of work rather than one titled obra: only part has been inventoried or edited publicly, so its individual components and completeness cannot responsibly be converted into a fabricated list of “works.” [Contemporary archival report](https://www.bibliotecadeva.ro/periodice/flacara/1984/08/flacara_1984_08_32.pdf); [Vlada](https://studii.crifst.ro/doc/2020/2020_03_2.pdf), pp. 128–129.

- **date not fixed:** *Autobiographical writings*. Odobleja left autobiographical manuscript material used by later biographers, including recollections of his education, medical career, intellectual development, and the genesis of consonantist psychology. The accessible scholarly account does not establish a finished, author-approved autobiography with a fixed title; it therefore resists classification as a discrete completed work. [Vlada](https://studii.crifst.ro/doc/2020/2020_03_2.pdf), especially pp. 119–125.

- **after 1942:** *War memoir*. Manuscript recollections of Odobleja’s Eastern Front service, including a section identified by the archival scholar as a “memoriu de război.” No evidence consulted establishes it as an independently completed or published work, and its title and date remain unfixed. [Vlada](https://studii.crifst.ro/doc/2020/2020_03_2.pdf), pp. 126–128.

- **dates not fixed:** *Poems*. Odobleja composed poems expressing, among other things, his frustration over the relationship between his consonantist psychology and later cybernetics. The scholarly source confirms their existence but supplies neither a reliable title-list nor evidence that they form a completed collection; they should consequently be recorded as an archival manifestation, not silently promoted into a named poetry book. [Vlada](https://studii.crifst.ro/doc/2020/2020_03_2.pdf), p. 128.

- **1975–1978:** *Scientific and institutional correspondence concerning the “Odobleja case”*. A body of letters sent to individuals and institutions while Odobleja sought recognition for his contribution to cybernetics. This correspondence acts—petitioning, documenting, and recruiting support—rather than merely describing; later *Corespondența* volumes are archival editions assembled after his death and include documents by other hands, so they are not single authored works in their published form. [Tudor Rățoi, “Un nou volum de corespondență dr. Ștefan Odobleja”](https://limbaromana.org/revista/un-nou-volum-de-corespondenta-dr-stefan-odobleja/).
