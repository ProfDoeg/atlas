## Works by Antoine Court de Gébelin

### Editorial work

- **1760:** *Histoire des troubles des Cévennes, ou de la guerre des Camisards sous le règne de Louis le Grand*. A three-volume history written by his father, Antoine Court, but prepared and brought into print by Court de Gébelin; it is therefore his editorial obra, not his authored text. ([Musée du Désert/Camisards](https://camisards.info/antoine-court/))

### Religious, historical, and political interventions

- **During his Lausanne studies, undated:** *Discours et dissertations pour la Société des étudiants*. A surviving manuscript body of student addresses and dissertations, occupying 633 folios in the Court papers; it resists division into securely titled individual works. ([Société de l’histoire du protestantisme français, Ms 620](https://www.shpf.fr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Ms-1-1792.pdf))

- **c. 1754–1763:** *Cours de religion*. A manuscript teaching course from his years at the Lausanne seminary, eventually printed in 1970; its modern publication is a manifestation of an earlier pedagogical text rather than a work composed for print. ([Lumières.Lausanne](https://www.lumieres.unil.ch/fiches/bio/43/))

- **1763:** *Les Toulousaines, ou Lettres historiques et apologétiques en faveur de la religion réformée, et de divers protestants condamnés dans ces derniers temps par le Parlement de Toulouse, ou dans le Haut-Languedoc*. An anonymously issued sequence of thirty apologetic letters concerning the Rochette, Calas, and Sirven affairs: at once historical argument, religious defence, and manifesto for toleration. Two substantially different impressions appeared in 1763, but these are manifestations of one obra, not two separate works. ([Hubert Bost’s critical-edition documentation](https://www.honorechampion.com/fr/intro?ean=9782745359742); [Tolosana](https://tolosana.univ-toulouse.fr/fr/notice/075240130))

- **1767, unrealized:** *Projet d’un journal protestant*. A protocol and institutional project for a Protestant periodical, preserved in his papers but never converted into a continuing publication; its non-realization is part of what the thing is. ([Daniel Robert, “Court de Gébelin et les Églises”](https://www.persee.fr/doc/dhs_0070-6760_1985_num_17_1_1542); [contemporary manuscript study cited by the SHPF](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/9c/Bulletin_de_la_Soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9_de_l%27histoire_de_France_%28IA_BulletinDeLaSocieteDeLHistoireDeFrance1853%29.pdf))

- **Written before 1784; published 1789:** *Devoirs du prince et du citoyen*. A short political treatise published posthumously as a continuation of the *Déclaration des droits de l’homme*; the 1789 imprint is securely dated, but the composition date is not. ([Lumières.Lausanne](https://www.lumieres.unil.ch/fiches/bio/43/))

### The unfinished encyclopedic Gesamtkunstwerk

- **1772–1784:** *Monde primitif, analysé et comparé avec le monde moderne*. An encyclopedic project intended to reconstruct humanity’s primordial language, symbols, myths, calendar, sciences, and institutions; prose, dictionaries, comparative tables, maps, and engraved images make it a genuine Gesamtkunstwerk of antiquarian knowledge. Court de Gébelin published nine volumes in 1773–1782, approximately the first third of a scheme that would have required about thirty; manuscripts for continuations were dispersed after his death, so the project—not merely the printed volumes—is the incomplete obra. ([Anne-Marie Mercier-Faivre](https://www.persee.fr/doc/dhs_0070-6760_1992_num_24_1_1880); [Lanjuinais’s bibliographical account](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/1d/Histoire_naturelle_de_la_parole%2C_ou%2C_Grammaire_universelle_a_l%27usage_des_jeunes_gens_%28IA_histoirenaturel00cour%29.pdf); [Wellcome Collection](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/fk43vbjc))

- **Printed 1772; published 1773:** *Plan général et raisonné des divers objets et des découvertes qui composent l’ouvrage intitulé Monde primitif, analysé et comparé avec le monde moderne, ou Recherches sur les antiquités du monde*. The project’s prospectus and architectural protocol, issued separately before being incorporated into the first volume; sources consequently give either 1772, the printing date, or 1773, the dated publication. ([Lumières.Lausanne](https://www.lumieres.unil.ch/fiches/biblio/5771/); [Biblioteca Braidense record distinguishing imprint and printing dates](https://opac.bibliotecabraidense.org/opac_braidense/opaclib?db=solr_braidense&do_cmd=search_show_cmd&from=1&item%3A1003%3ANomi%3A%3A%40frase%40=+Didot+%3Ceditore%3E&nentries=10&resultForward=opac%2Fbraidense%2Fbrief.jsp&searchForm=opac%2Fbraidense%2Ferror.jsp))

- **1773:** *Monde primitif… considéré dans son génie allégorique et dans les allégories auxquelles conduisit ce génie*. The first published livraison, a comparative mythology and theory of allegory supplied with engraved figures; bibliographically it is both an independent titled text and part of volume I. ([Bibliographie de Belgique](https://www.belgianbibliography.be/Library/doc/SYRACUSE/16706915/monde-primitif-analyse-et-compare-avec-le-monde-moderne-considere-dans-son-genie-allegorique-et-dans))

- **1773:** *Allégories orientales, ou Le Fragment de Sanchoniaton*. An antiquarian reconstruction presented as another separately titled component of the first livraison; surviving copies were also bound and circulated independently, so its status oscillates between constituent text and separate book. ([Lumières.Lausanne](https://www.lumieres.unil.ch/fiches/bio/43/); [Wellcome Collection](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/fk43vbjc))

- **1774:** *Lettre à l’auteur anonyme de deux prétendus extraits insérés dans le Journal des savans des mois de novembre et décembre 1773*. A polemical letter defending the *Plan général* and *Allégories orientales*; it appeared separately but was also incorporated into sets of the *Monde primitif*. ([CiNii bibliographical record](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BA20053659); [National Library of Ireland contents record](https://catalogue.nli.ie/Record/vtls000472385/TOC))

- **1774:** *Monde primitif… considéré dans l’histoire naturelle de la parole, ou Grammaire universelle et comparative*. Volume II of the project: a philosophical and comparative grammar rather than merely a dictionary or linguistic handbook. ([CLADE digital bibliography](https://bibliotheques-numeriques.defense.gouv.fr/ega/document/dd2840d0-ee30-433c-b0aa-c73f4b02eac3); [Frantext bibliography](https://blog.frantext.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Corpus_Frantext_Octobre_2020.pdf))

- **1775:** *Monde primitif… considéré dans l’histoire naturelle de la parole, ou Origine du langage et de l’écriture*. Volume III, combining a theory of linguistic origins with a theory and illustrated history of writing; some bound sets interchange the order assigned to the grammar and language-origin volumes, but the original sequence dates this one to 1775. ([CLADE digital bibliography](https://bibliotheques-numeriques.defense.gouv.fr/ega/document/dd2840d0-ee30-433c-b0aa-c73f4b02eac3); [National Library of Ireland contents record](https://catalogue.nli.ie/Record/vtls000472385/TOC))

- **1776:** *Monde primitif… considéré dans l’histoire civile, religieuse et allégorique du calendrier, ou Almanach*. Volume IV, an illustrated comparative history of calendars that treats the calendar simultaneously as scientific instrument, religious system, and symbolic image-world. ([CLADE digital bibliography](https://bibliotheques-numeriques.defense.gouv.fr/ega/document/dd2840d0-ee30-433c-b0aa-c73f4b02eac3); [Archives municipales de Dijon](https://archives.dijon.fr/ARCHIVES/doc/SYRACUSE/776966/monde-primitif-analyse-et-compare-avec-le-monde-moderne-par-m-court-de-gebelin?_lg=fr-FR))

- **1778:** *Monde primitif… considéré dans les origines françoises, ou Dictionnaire étymologique de la langue françoise*. Volume V, an etymological dictionary embedded within the larger reconstruction of a primitive language. ([Catalogue collectif de France](https://ccfr.bnf.fr/portailccfr/ark%3A/16871/0018703504))

- **1779:** *Monde primitif… considéré dans les origines latines, ou Dictionnaire étymologique de la langue latine, première partie*. Volume VI and the first half of the Latin dictionary. It was conceived as a constituent of the encyclopedia, not as an independently initiated lexicographical project. ([CLADE digital bibliography](https://bibliotheques-numeriques.defense.gouv.fr/emd/document/1bb30ab8-39ca-4ca5-ba7c-d2e99162f40e))

- **1780:** *Monde primitif… considéré dans les origines latines, ou Dictionnaire étymologique de la langue latine, seconde partie*. Volume VII, completing the Latin dictionary within the printed project. ([CLADE digital bibliography](https://bibliotheques-numeriques.defense.gouv.fr/emd/document/1bb30ab8-39ca-4ca5-ba7c-d2e99162f40e); [bibliographical record for the two-part dictionary](https://www.zvab.com/Monde-primitif-analys%C3%A9-compar%C3%A9-moderne-consider%C3%A9/1259798137/bd))

- **1781:** *Monde primitif… considéré dans divers objets concernant l’histoire, le blason, les monnaies, les jeux, les voyages des Phéniciens autour du monde, les langues américaines, etc., ou Dissertations mêlées*. Volume VIII, explicitly designated the first tome of a miscellany whose second tome never appeared. Its essay on tarot is not a separately published book by Court de Gébelin but one constituent dissertation; the volume also incorporates responses and texts by other writers, so authorship of the physical volume is composite. ([digitized original](https://books.google.com/books/about/Monde_primitif_analyse_et_compare_avec_l.html?id=pa68D6bPgaUC); [Lanjuinais’s contents analysis](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/1d/Histoire_naturelle_de_la_parole%2C_ou%2C_Grammaire_universelle_a_l%27usage_des_jeunes_gens_%28IA_histoirenaturel00cour%29.pdf))

- **1782:** *Monde primitif… considéré dans les origines grecques, ou Dictionnaire étymologique de la langue grecque, précédé de recherches et de nouvelles vues sur l’origine des Grecs et de leur langue*. Volume IX and the last volume Court de Gébelin completed. It combines an ethnolinguistic preliminary study with a French-language Greek etymological dictionary. ([digitized original](https://play.google.com/store/books/details/Antoine_Court_de_G%C3%A9belin_Monde_primitif_analys%C3%A9_et?id=jr8GAAAAQAAJ); [CLADE digital bibliography](https://bibliotheques-numeriques.defense.gouv.fr/emd/document/1bb30ab8-39ca-4ca5-ba7c-d2e99162f40e))

- **Unfinished at his death in 1784:** *Unpublished continuations of the Monde primitif*. Notes existed for further volumes, and Court de Gébelin expected the minister Moulinié to arrange them, but the manuscripts were dispersed and largely disappeared. These remains constitute an interrupted project rather than recoverable, individually titled works. ([Anne-Marie Mercier-Faivre](https://www.persee.fr/doc/dhs_0070-6760_1992_num_24_1_1880); [Bibliothèque nationale de France authority notice](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark%3A/12148/cb118979670.public))

### Recastings of the encyclopedic project

- **1776:** *Histoire naturelle de la parole, ou Précis de l’origine du langage et de la grammaire universelle*. A compact pedagogical recasting, made by Court de Gébelin himself from volumes II and III of the *Monde primitif*. It is a new textual arrangement and intended use, not simply another edition of either source volume. ([Library of Congress digitization](https://www.loc.gov/resource/gdcmassbookdig.histoirenaturel00cour/?st=grid); [CiNii bibliography](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/author/DA07327144))

- **1780:** *Dictionnaire étymologique et raisonné des racines latines, à l’usage des jeunes gens*. A teaching abridgment derived from volumes VI and VII of the *Monde primitif*; its educational protocol and reduced form make it a distinct obra despite its reused substance. ([Lanjuinais’s bibliographical notice](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/1d/Histoire_naturelle_de_la_parole%2C_ou%2C_Grammaire_universelle_a_l%27usage_des_jeunes_gens_%28IA_histoirenaturel00cour%29.pdf); [Lumières.Lausanne](https://www.lumieres.unil.ch/fiches/bio/43/))

### Mesmeric manifesto

- **Dated 31 July 1783; issued 1783/1784:** *Lettre de l’auteur du Monde primitif à Messieurs ses souscripteurs, sur le magnétisme animal*. A public testimonial addressed to his subscribers and intended to act on the controversy surrounding Mesmer: less a neutral account than a manifesto of personal experience and belief. Bibliographies variously date the undated first printing to 1783 or 1784; the 1784 manifestation added a supplement and should not be mistaken for a separate original work. ([Hanegraaff, *Dictionary of Gnosis and Western Esotericism* bibliography](https://ndl.ethernet.edu.et/bitstream/123456789/38998/1/33.pdf.pdf); [contemporary-response bibliography](https://www.abebooks.com/first-edition/LETTRE-D%C3%89COUVERTE-MAGNETISME-ANIMAL-Court-Gebelin/32024001545/bd))

### Institutional creations and collective projects

- **Before 1781:** *Société de l’Étoile*. An associational project that Court de Gébelin founded or helped to found; because the surviving account does not isolate his individual share, it resists attribution as a wholly personal work. ([Lumières.Lausanne](https://lumieres.unil.ch/fiches/bio/43/))

- **Before 1781:** *Société libre d’émulation de France*. Another learned-society project associated with his organizing activity rather than an authored text; the evidence describes participation in its foundation, not sole creation. ([Lumières.Lausanne](https://lumieres.unil.ch/fiches/bio/43/))

- **c. 1780–1781:** *Société Apollinienne / Musée de Paris*. A multidisciplinary society over which Court de Gébelin presided and which became the Musée de Paris in 1781. It is an institutional obra produced collectively—an organized setting for lectures, experiments, and correspondence—rather than a book or a securely attributable personal creation. ([Lumières.Lausanne](https://lumieres.unil.ch/fiches/bio/43/); [Franklin Papers](https://www.franklinpapers.org/yale%3Bjsessionid%3D4D631DF8033CD06D057936DC54BFC4E8?page=028b&vol=35))

### Archival bodies that resist treatment as single works

- **Throughout his working life:** *Correspondence*. Court de Gébelin left a large, dispersed epistolary corpus concerning Protestant affairs, scholarship, the *Monde primitif*, and learned societies. Individual letters are acts and documents rather than parts of a fixed authored obra; only portions have received scholarly editions, including the correspondence with the Protestants of Orthez. ([Lumières.Lausanne archival and bibliographical notice](https://www.lumieres.unil.ch/fiches/bio/43/); [Franklin Papers](https://www.franklinpapers.org/yale?page=513b&vol=37))

- **Undated and variously dated:** *Extracts, reading notes, autobiographical notes, theological materials, and collected Protestant documents*. These papers survive intermingled with those of his father in the Bibliothèque de Genève and the Société de l’histoire du protestantisme français. They are material traces of intellectual labor, but not a catalogue of separately titled works; attribution is sometimes shared or uncertain, and treating every archival unit as an autonomous obra would falsely manufacture titles and boundaries. ([SHPF manuscript catalogue](https://www.shpf.fr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Ms-1-1792.pdf); [BnF authority notice](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark%3A/12148/cb118979670.public))
