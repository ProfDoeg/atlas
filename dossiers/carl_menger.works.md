## Works by Carl Menger

### Books and book-scale projects

- **1871:** *Grundsätze der Volkswirthschaftslehre. Erster, allgemeiner Theil*. A theoretical-economics treatise and the only completed part of a projected four-part system; the promised parts on income, credit and money, applied economics, and social reform never materialized as finished works.
- **1883:** *Untersuchungen über die Methode der Socialwissenschaften und der politischen Oekonomie insbesondere*. A book-length methodological treatise whose intervention initiated the *Methodenstreit*.
- **1884:** *Die Irrthümer des Historismus in der deutschen Nationalökonomie*. A polemical book composed as sixteen letters: less a neutral treatise than a manifesto-like intervention against the German Historical School.
- **1892:** *Der Übergang zur Goldwährung: Untersuchungen über die Werthprobleme der österreichisch-ungarischen Valutareform*. A policy monograph on currency reform, made in direct conjunction with Menger’s public and official interventions of that year.
- **c. 1871–1921:** *Projected enlarged system of economics and revision of the Grundsätze*. An unfinished lifelong project surviving in annotated books, loose drafts, fragments, and displaced chapters rather than a recoverable final text; it therefore resists treatment as a completed *obra*.
- **1923:** *Grundsätze der Volkswirtschaftslehre*, second edition. A posthumous manifestation assembled by Karl Menger from his father’s disorderly revision materials. Its status is disputed because Karl had to select, arrange, and sometimes reformulate fragments, making the respective contributions of father and son difficult to disentangle.

### Articles and reference works on economic theory and method

- **2–3 January 1873:** *Der Zwiespalt unter den deutschen Volkswirthen*. A two-part newspaper essay on division within German economics.
- **1884:** *Gegendarstellung C. Mengers*. A published rejoinder to Emanuel Leser’s review of the *Untersuchungen*; it acts as a corrective intervention rather than a freestanding treatise.
- **1888:** *Zur Theorie des Kapitals*. A journal article presenting Menger’s theory of capital.
- **1888:** *Contribution à la théorie du capital*. A French manifestation of the capital essay in the *Revue d’économie politique*, not a wholly independent theoretical work.
- **1889:** *Grundzüge einer Klassifikation der Wirtschaftswissenschaften*. A methodological journal essay classifying the economic sciences.
- **6 and 8 January 1891:** *Die Social-Theorie der classischen National-Oekonomie und die moderne Wirtschaftspolitik*. A two-part newspaper essay connecting classical social theory with contemporary economic policy.
- **1892:** *Geld*. An encyclopaedia article in the first edition of the *Handwörterbuch der Staatswissenschaften*.
- **1892:** *On the Origin of Money*. An English-language manifestation of part of Menger’s monetary argument, published in the *Economic Journal*.
- **1892:** *La Monnaie mesure de valeur*. A French-language manifestation of Menger’s work on money as a measure of value.
- **1897:** *Geld- und Münzwesen seit 1857*. A historical-reference article for the *Österreichisches Staatswörterbuch*.
- **1897:** *Ein Gesetz gegen Kartelle*. A brief journal intervention on anti-cartel legislation.
- **1900:** *Geld*. A substantially enlarged second-edition manifestation of the encyclopaedia article, published in the *Handwörterbuch der Staatswissenschaften*.
- **1906:** *Geld- und Münzwesen seit 1857*. A revised second-edition manifestation of the reference article; this item is omitted from Hayek’s original list but identified in later scholarship.
- **1909:** *Geld*. The third-edition version of the encyclopaedia article and the fullest lifetime manifestation of Menger’s systematic theory of money.
- **1907/1921:** *Zur Zinstheorie Marshalls*. A short piece composed in 1907 but published only in 1921; its delayed appearance makes its publication date different from its date of making.

### Currency-reform interventions and official testimony

- **12 December 1889:** *Die Kaufkraft des Guldens österreichischer Währung*. A newspaper article on the purchasing power of the Austrian florin.
- **31 January/February 1892:** *Von unserer Valuta*. A lecture to the Vienna Juridical Society subsequently manifested as a printed article.
- **1892:** *Die Valutaregulierung in Oesterreich-Ungarn*. A long journal study issued across three instalments; a shortened separate printing followed in June.
- **8–17 March 1892:** *Statements before the Währungs-Enquête-Commission*. Oral expert testimony preserved within the commission’s stenographic protocol. The protocol as a whole is collective and institutional, so only the identified interventions are Menger’s work.
- **15–16 June 1893:** *Das Goldagio und der heutige Stand der Valutareform*. A two-part newspaper policy article also issued as a separate pamphlet.
- **November–December 1903:** *Expert contribution to the Enquête zur Reform der Gebäudesteuer*. Oral evidence preserved in an official stenographic protocol published in 1904; it resists classification as a self-contained authored text.

### Teaching and institutional memoranda

- **30 September 1875:** *Exposé über die Lehrvorträge über politische Oekonomie und Statistik*. An archival teaching plan prepared for Crown Prince Rudolf, not a public publication.
- **25 July 1876:** *Bericht über den Unterricht in politischer Oekonomie*. An archival report on Rudolf’s instruction in political economy.
- **20 December 1876:** *Bericht über den Unterricht in der allgemeinen europäischen und österreichisch-ungarischen Statistik*. An archival teaching report on statistics.
- **1887:** *Gutachten … über die Studienreform im allgemeinen*. A jointly authored institutional opinion by Adolf Exner and Menger on reform of legal studies.
- **1887:** *Separatvotum … betreffend die Stellung der Volkswirtschaftspolitik, der Statistik und der Staatsrechnungswissenschaft im Studienplan*. Menger’s separate institutional opinion within the same reform proceeding.
- **25 December 1901:** *Beitrag zur Enquête “Die katholische Universität”*. A newspaper contribution to a public inquiry rather than a conventional essay.
- **23 February 1907:** *Die Reform der juristischen Studienordnung*. A newspaper intervention on reforming the legal curriculum.

- **30 November 1892:** *Nationalökonomische Literatur*. An omnibus review of the *Handwörterbuch* and writings by Wagner, Philippovich, Böhm-Bawerk, Schullern-Schrattenhofen, Mensi, and others.
- **21 December 1892:** *Review of Adolph Wagner, Lehr- und Handbuch der politischen Oekonomie*. A newspaper review.
- **1893:** *Review of studies by Ernest Mahaim and Henri St.-Marc on teaching political economy in Berlin, Vienna, Germany, and Austria*. A comparative review article without a securely recorded independent title.

### Biographical, commemorative, and cultural essays

- **15 May 1873:** *John Stuart Mill*. A newspaper obituary.
- **24 May 1873:** *Weltausstellung 1873: Der Pavillon des k.k. Ackerbauministeriums*. A journalistic report on the Agriculture Ministry’s exhibition pavilion.
- **31 January 1889:** *Nachruf auf Kronprinz Rudolf*. An obituary written immediately after Rudolf’s death.
- **6 September 1889:** *Friedrich List*. A biographical and intellectual newspaper essay.
- **10 and 20 August 1889:** *Zum hundertjährigen Geburtstag von Friedrich List*. A two-part centenary commemoration distinct from the longer newspaper essay.
- **30 January 1890:** *Ein Porträt des Kronprinzen*. A commemorative portrait of Crown Prince Rudolf.
- **1891:** *Lorenz von Stein † 23. September 1890*. A scholarly obituary and intellectual assessment.
- **16 June 1894:** *Wilhelm Roscher*. A biographical newspaper essay.
- **23 April 1905:** *Friedrich Schiller*. A cultural commemorative essay published in the supplement to *Die Zeit*.
- **22 May 1906:** *John Stuart Mill*. A later biographical-intellectual essay, distinct from the 1873 obituary and review.
- **28 May 1907:** *Emil Steinbach*. A newspaper memorial essay.
- **1915:** *Eugen von Böhm-Bawerk*. An academy memorial and intellectual biography.

### Prefaces and paratexts

- **1893:** *Preface to Eteocle Lorini, La questione della valuta in Austria-Ungheria*. A substantial prefatory essay attached to another author’s book.
- **3 July 1895/1896:** *Preface to Giovanni Montemartini, Il risparmio nella economia pura*. A short paratext dated in 1895 and published with the 1896 book.
- **1905:** *Preface to Markus Ettinger, Die Regelung des Wettbewerbes im modernen Wirtschaftssystem, part I*. A short prefatory intervention on cartels and competition.

### Corrections, letters, and other resistant texts

- **28 June 1883:** *Letter to Léon Walras*. Private correspondence later printed in Walras’s collected correspondence; publication does not turn it into a public essay.
- **February 1884:** *Letter to Léon Walras*. A private letter subsequently published.
- **13 November 1884/1921:** *Letter to Eugen von Böhm-Bawerk on the theory of capital interest*. A theoretical letter published only decades after composition.
- **27 January 1887:** *Letter to Léon Walras*. A private letter subsequently published.
- **10 December 1889:** *Berichtigung*. A newspaper correction concerning remarks on industrial legislation; its action is rectification rather than exposition.
- **26 June 1911:** *Letter to Ismar Feilbogen*. A private letter excerpted in Feilbogen’s account of the Austrian school and therefore only partially manifested in print.
