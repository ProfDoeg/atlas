## Works by Nick Land

### Dissertation and monographs

- **1987:** *Heidegger’s “Die Sprache im Gedicht” and the Cultivation of the Grapheme*. Doctoral dissertation submitted at the University of Essex: an early philosophical study of Heidegger’s reading of Georg Trakl, not originally a commercial book. [The British Library’s EThOS record identifies the thesis and date.](https://ethos.bl.uk/OrderDetails.do?uin=uk.bl.ethos.381663)

- **1992:** *The Thirst for Annihilation: Georges Bataille and Virulent Nihilism (An Essay in Atheistic Religion)*. A monograph that is at once philosophical interpretation, atheological experiment, and self-undermining theoretical performance. Some bibliographies give 1991, reflecting its copyright or production history; Routledge and the collected-edition bibliography give 1992. [Routledge/Google Books record.](https://books.google.com/books?id=ZQGIAgAAQBAJ)

- **1996:** *Machinic Postmodernism: Complexity, Technics and Regulation*. A collaboratively authored theoretical book credited to Land, Keith Ansell-Pearson, and Joseph A. McCahery; Land’s individual share should not be silently separated from the collective object. [Google Books identifies all three as authors.](https://books.google.com/books/about/Machinic_Postmodernism.html?id=GBTvkQEACAAJ)

### Essays, papers, talks, and theory-fictions collected in *Fanged Noumena*

- **1988–89:** *Kant, Capital, and the Prohibition of Incest: A Polemical Introduction to the Configuration of Philosophy and Modernity*. A polemical philosophical essay first published in *Third Text* 2.5; its argument operates across philosophy, political economy, kinship, race, and colonialism. [The collected edition’s “List of Sources” records the original publication.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **1990:** *Narcissism and Dispersion in Heidegger’s 1953 Trakl Interpretation*. A scholarly essay derived from Land’s dissertation and published in *Philosophers’ Poets*. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **1991:** *Delighted to Death*. A philosophical essay first published in *Pli*; its argumentative prose already pushes toward the performative intensity of Land’s later work. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **1991:** *Art as Insurrection: The Question of Aesthetics in Kant, Schopenhauer, and Nietzsche*. A contribution to an edited scholarly volume, but also an insurrectionary theory of art rather than conventional aesthetic commentary. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **1992:** *Circuitries*. A philosophical essay published in *Pli* that recasts capital and subjectivity as machinic processes. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **1993:** *Spirit and Teeth*. A scholarly chapter on Heidegger and Derrida whose title and rhetoric pull it toward theory-fiction. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **1993:** *After the Law*. A jurisprudential and philosophical essay first published in *Closure or Critique: New Directions in Legal Theory*. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **1993:** *Making It with Death: Remarks on Thanatos and Desiring-Production*. A journal article that intervenes in psychoanalysis and Deleuze–Guattari through a theory of the death drive. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **1993:** *Machinic Desire*. A journal article in *Textual Practice* and one of Land’s central formulations of impersonal, machinic desire. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **1994/1997:** *Meltdown*. First delivered at Virtual Futures in May 1994 and first printed in CCRU’s *Abstract Culture 1* in 1997; it is simultaneously conference performance, theory-fiction, and accelerationist manifesto. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **1995:** *Shamanic Nietzsche*. A scholarly essay published in *Nietzsche: A Critical Reader*, although its “shamanic” construction deliberately strains academic commentary. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **1995:** *Cyberrevolution*. A short manifesto-like text first issued in the privately published *Collapse 1*—the earlier publication bearing that name, not Urbanomic’s later journal. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **1995:** *Hypervirus*. A compressed theory-fiction first issued in the privately published *Collapse 2*. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **1995:** *No Future*. A conference paper left unpublished until *Fanged Noumena*; it survives as the textual remainder of an event rather than a securely dated original publication. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **1995:** *Cyberspace Anarchitecture as Jungle-War*. A brief architectural and cybercultural essay first published in *Architectural Design* 118. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **1995:** *Meat (or How to Kill Oedipus in Cyberspace)*. A journal article in *Body & Society* that combines cyberculture, psychoanalysis, and anti-humanist theory. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **1996:** *A zIIgothIc–==X=coDA==–(CookIng–lobsteRs–wIth–jAke–AnD–DInos)*. An experimental text made for Jake and Dinos Chapman’s *Chapmanworld*; it belongs partly to an art-world collaboration and resists isolation as an ordinary essay. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **1996:** *KataςoniX*. The textual component of a multimedia presentation by ***Collapse, O(rphan) d(rift>), and Doghead Surgeri at Virtual Futures ’96. Its authorship and ontology are collective: Land’s text is only one manifestation of the Gesamtkunstwerk-like event. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **1998:** *CyberGothic*. An essay first published in the *Virtual Futures* proceedings; its mixture of cultural theory, fiction, and machinic Gothic resists a single disciplinary label. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **1998:** *Mechanomics*. A philosophical essay first published in *Pli* 7. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **probably 1998–99:** *Origins of the Cthulhu Club*. An online CCRU theory-fiction whose date is explicitly uncertain in the collected edition; it constructs an invented institutional genealogy and therefore acts as hyperstitional fabrication as well as narrative. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **1999:** *Barker Speaks: The CCRU Interview with Professor D. C. Barker*. A counterfeit interview or pseudodocument first published in *Abstract Culture: Hyperstition*; “Professor Barker” belongs to CCRU’s fictional system, so the work resists classification as factual interview. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **1999:** *Cryptolith*. A text from *Syzygy*, the collaborative CCRU–O(rphan) d(rift> installation at Beaconsfield, London. The printed text is one residue of a multimedia manifestation, not the whole work. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **1999:** *Occultures*. Another textual component of *Syzygy*; its proper object is partly installation, event, and collective fiction rather than autonomous essay. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **date not established:** *Non-Standard Numeracies: Nomad Cultures*. An unpublished essay first made publicly stable by *Fanged Noumena*; the editors provide no original date, and none should be invented. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **2005–07:** *Introduction to Qwernomics*. An online theory-fiction about the QWERTY keyboard, numeracy, and contingency; the collected edition dates the relevant group of posts only to this range. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **2005–07:** *Tic-Talk*. An online experimental text later collected in *Fanged Noumena*; no more exact date is established in the edition’s source history. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **2005–07:** *Critique of Transcendental Miserablism*. An online polemic against philosophical and political investment in negation; it functions partly as a manifesto. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **2005–07:** *A Dirty Joke*. An online theory-fiction later collected in *Fanged Noumena*; its precise original posting date is not fixed by the collected edition. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **2006:** *Qabbala 101*. An essay first published in *Collapse* I that turns numerology and qabbalistic procedures into philosophical machinery. [Urbanomic’s contributor catalogue and the collected-edition source list both identify it.](https://www.urbanomic.com/contributor/nick-land/)

### Collective projects, performances, and multimedia manifestations

- **mid-1990s–early 2000s:** *Cybernetic Culture Research Unit (CCRU)*. A collective research-fiction project associated with Land, Sadie Plant, and a changing group of participants; its seminars, pamphlets, performances, websites, Numogram, and unattributed writings resist conversion into an individual Land bibliography. Attribution of any unsigned CCRU text to Land alone would therefore be misleading. [Urbanomic describes the CCRU corpus as collective conceptual production.](https://www.urbanomic.com/book/ccru-writings-1997-2003/)

- **1995–96:** ****Collapse*. A small collective and privately published experimental platform involved in *Cyberrevolution*, *Hypervirus*, and *KataςoniX*. It should not be confused with the later Urbanomic journal *Collapse*, and its productions cannot all be assigned solely to Land. [The distinction and publication history appear in the *Fanged Noumena* source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **1996:** *KataςoniX at Virtual Futures ’96*. A collaborative audiovisual performance involving Land’s text, O(rphan) d(rift>), ***Collapse, Doghead Surgeri, and jungle music; the event, rather than the later transcript alone, is the made thing. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **1999:** *Syzygy*. A CCRU–O(rphan) d(rift> collaborative installation at Beaconsfield, London, incorporating *Cryptolith* and *Occultures*. It approaches a Gesamtkunstwerk of text, sound, visual environment, occult system, and fictional research. [Collected-edition source list.](https://kyl.neocities.org/books/%5BPHI%20LAN%5D%20fanged%20noumena.pdf)

- **1990s:** *Numogram*. A diagrammatic-numerical occult system developed within CCRU rather than a conventionally authored text. Land was a major participant, but the system’s collective and iterative construction makes exclusive authorship inappropriate. [Urbanomic’s CCRU edition presents the Numogram as part of the collective’s reconstructed time-sorcery project.](https://www.urbanomic.com/book/ccru-writings-1997-2003/)

### Journalism, guidebooks, and editorial objects

- **2010:** *The Shanghai World Expo Guide 2010*. A practical illustrated guidebook and map-bearing urban object, written for visitors to the Shanghai Expo; it is also an essayistic history of world fairs and a compact statement of Land’s Sino-futurism. [Contemporary discussion identifies Land as its author and Urbanatomy Books editor.](https://urbanresearchlab.net/?p=97)

- **2010:** *Shanghai Basics*. An Urbanatomy city guide edited, rather than wholly authored, by Land. The editorial object belongs in his made corpus, but its individual constituent texts should not automatically be attributed to him. [The bibliographic record identifies Land explicitly as editor.](https://www.marymartin.com/web/viewbooks?bookId=125787)

### Collected and remediated works

- **2011:** *Fanged Noumena: Collected Writings 1987–2007*. A retrospectively constructed collection edited by Robin Mackay and Ray Brassier; it is a new bibliographical and editorial manifestation of thirty earlier texts, not thirty newly written works. Its editors describe the contents as papers, talks, articles, theory-fictions, poetics, cryptography, and other hybrids. [Urbanomic](https://www.urbanomic.com/fanged_noumena-2/) and [MIT Press](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780955308789/fanged-noumena/) document the collection and its editorial status.

- **2013:** *Calendric Dominion*. An Urban Future electronic pamphlet assembled from Land’s writing on calendrical power and cultural time. It is both an essay and a remediation of serialized blog material. [The standard published-work list records the pamphlet.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Land#Books)

- **2013:** *Suspended Animation*. An Urban Future pamphlet whose five parts began as blog posts written in November–December 2011; the pamphlet is therefore a later manifestation of a serialized essay. [The pamphlet’s own introduction records this composition history.](https://s3.amazonaws.com/arena-attachments/282161/9886a6a2101c647792b45628640ae9a2.pdf)

- **2014:** *Fission*. A short standalone Urbanomic publication and theory-fiction, also catalogued by Urbanomic as a chapter-length work; its unstable position between essay, narrative, and pamphlet is part of the object. [Urbanomic’s author catalogue lists it as both publication and authored chapter.](https://www.urbanomic.com/contributor/nick-land/)

- **2014:** *Templexity: Disordered Loops through Shanghai Time*. An electronic pamphlet produced from Land’s Urban Future writing, combining urban theory, calendrics, and temporal fiction. [Urbanomic identifies it among Land’s authored works.](https://www.urbanomic.com/contributor/nick-land/)

- **2014:** *Shanghai Times*. An Urbanatomy electronic pamphlet on Shanghai and temporal modernity, originating in the broader Urban Future project. [The published-work bibliography records it separately.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Land#Books)

- **2014:** *Dragon Tales: Glimpses of Chinese Culture*. An Urbanatomy electronic collection of short cultural writings; it belongs to Land’s Shanghai journalism rather than his horror or academic philosophy. [The published-work bibliography records the title and year.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Land#Books)

- **2014:** *Xinjiang Horizons*. An Urbanatomy electronic publication associated with Land’s editorial and journalistic work in China. Available bibliographies list Land under the title, but the extent of his authorship as distinct from editorial responsibility is not consistently specified. [Published-work bibliography.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Land#Books)

- **2022:** *The Dark Enlightenment*. A book manifestation of Land’s previously serialized political writing, not an entirely new text composed in 2022. It systematizes the neoreactionary project developed on his blogs. [Published-work bibliography.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Land#Books)

- **2024:** *Xenosystems*. A selected book arrangement of posts from the earlier blog, organized by theme; it is a curated manifestation of that body of writing rather than a newly composed continuous monograph. [The edition description identifies it as selected excerpts from the blog.](https://neodb.social/book/65clZOnPHRXF8S7o9mAaDe)

- **2025:** *Urban Future*. A book-form retrospective manifestation of the Urban Future blog and pamphlet project. Because it reorganizes earlier serialized material, it should not be counted as wholly new prose without comparison at the text level. [Published-work bibliography.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Land#Books)

- **2025:** *Outsideness: 2013–2023*. A retrospective collection drawn from a decade of online writing. Its status is archival and curatorial as well as authorial. [Published-work bibliography.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Land#Books)

- **2025:** *Occult Xenosystems*. A collected manifestation of Land’s occult, numerological, and xenosystemic writing. Its components overlap the longer online project, so the book should not be mistaken for an entirely discrete body of newly made texts. [Published-work bibliography.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Land#Books)

### Abstract-horror fiction

- **2014:** *Phyl-Undhu: Abstract Horror, Exterminator*. A philosophical horror novella—or paired abstract-horror narratives—whose speculative argument is inseparable from its fiction. Scholarship treats it as one of Land’s two principal abstract-horror novellas. [Publisher-era record](https://timespiralpress.wordpress.com/tag/tsp/) and [Vincent Le’s scholarly study](https://intellectdiscover.com/content/journals/10.1386/host_00009_1).

- **2015:** *Chasm*. A philosophical horror novella published by Time Spiral Press; its movement between manifesto, metaphysics, and narrative makes “fiction” necessary but insufficient as a category. [Time Spiral Press’s publication notice](https://timespiralpress.wordpress.com/2015/12/19/chasm/) and [Vincent Le’s scholarly study](https://intellectdiscover.com/content/journals/10.1386/host_00009_1).

### Later standalone essays and interventions

- **2009:** *Hyperstition: An Introduction*. An interview conducted by Delphi Carstens with Land; it is a dialogic work and should not be treated as Land-authored prose alone. [The Orphan Drift archive identifies both participants.](http://www.orphandriftarchive.com/neo-future/delphi-carstens-hyperstition-tedx/)

- **2014:** *Teleoplexy: Notes on Acceleration*. A commissioned essay for *#Accelerate: The Accelerationist Reader*, extending Land’s accelerationism into a theory of self-reinforcing machinic intelligence. [Urbanomic lists Land as its author and identifies the containing volume.](https://www.urbanomic.com/contributor/nick-land/)

- **2014:** *Transcendental Risk*. A philosophical essay published in *Collapse* VIII, joining capitalism, artificial intelligence, and risk. [The scholarly bibliography in Simon O’Sullivan’s study records the essay and publication.](https://simonosullivan.net/articles/Accelerationism_Hyperstition_and_Myth-Science.pdf)

- **2014:** *Odds and Ends*. A contribution to *Collapse* VIII, catalogued separately by Urbanomic; it should not be merged silently with *Transcendental Risk*. [Urbanomic author catalogue.](https://www.urbanomic.com/contributor/nick-land/)

- **2016:** *Sore Losers*. An online polemical essay responding to Alain Badiou and the 2015 Paris attacks. [Urbanomic dates and hosts the document.](https://www.urbanomic.com/document/sore-losers/)

- **2017:** *A Quick-and-Dirty Introduction to Accelerationism*. A concise explanatory intervention first published by *Jacobite*; unlike Land’s earlier theory-fictions, it presents itself as an introduction, though still polemical rather than neutral. [The bibliographic record identifies the original date as 25 May 2017.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Land)

### Serial platforms and unfinished bodies of work

- **2011–14:** *Urban Future*. A blog and pamphlet-generating research project on cities, modernity, economics, China, and nonlinear time. Its posts were subsequently recombined as *Calendric Dominion*, *Suspended Animation*, *Shanghai Times*, and *Templexity*, so “the work” is both serial project and several later book manifestations. [The *Suspended Animation* pamphlet identifies itself as part of “Urban Future Pamphlets, Series 1: Time Sequence (2011–13).”](https://s3.amazonaws.com/arena-attachments/282161/9886a6a2101c647792b45628640ae9a2.pdf)

- **2012–22:** *Outside In / Xenosystems*. A large, serial blog project encompassing neoreaction, accelerationism, capitalism, cryptocurrency, horror, numerology, and short-form commentary. It resists treatment as a finite “work”: no scholarly collected edition supplies a complete post-level catalogue, the site disappeared from the live web in 2022, and later books select and rearrange only parts of it. [The archived blog and its relation to Land are documented through the “Outside In” link here.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Land#External_links)

- **2013–present:** *Outsideness*. A newsletter and continuing online writing project rather than a closed text; *Outsideness: 2013–2023* is a bounded selection from it, not necessarily the project’s endpoint. [Current newsletter archive.](https://zerophilosophy.substack.com/)

- **2023–present:** *Xenocosmography*. A social-media project oriented toward religion, number, calendrics, and occult cosmology. It remains open-ended and highly fragmentary, and therefore resists being called a completed work or exhaustively itemized text. [The project’s launch and thematic shift are documented in the biographical record.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Land)
