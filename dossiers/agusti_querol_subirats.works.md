## Works by Agustí Querol i Subirats

### Autonomous sculptures and reliefs

- **1881:** *Cabeza de Dolorosa*. Early devotional sculpture, known from the scholarly chronology; later inventories also record a wooden *Dolorosa*, but it is uncertain whether these are the same object or separate works. [Guinda Pérez](https://aacadigital.com/articulos/el-legado-postumo-de-agustin-querol-su-estudio-madrileno-su-museo-no-realizado-ni-en-madrid-ni-en-tortosa/); [Museo del Prado](https://www.museodelprado.es/coleccion/artista/agustin-querol-y-subirats/4a182299-ca0a-44ff-84a9-79f60360ff01)
- **1885:** *Guerrero vencido* / *El vencido de hoy*. Statue of a defeated warrior, subsequently documented in Querol’s estate and later reported damaged; the two titles may designate successive descriptions of the same obra rather than two independent sculptures. [Guinda Pérez](https://aacadigital.com/articulos/el-legado-postumo-de-agustin-querol-su-estudio-madrileno-su-museo-no-realizado-ni-en-madrid-ni-en-tortosa/); [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **1886–1888:** *Sagunto* / *Los suicidas de Sagunto*. Marble narrative group of a mother killing herself over her dead child during the Roman assault; conceived as a pension submission in Rome, completed first in plaster, and subsequently manifested in marble and other casts. Sources disagree between 1886, 1888 and the exhibition date 1906, so these should not be collapsed into a single unqualified date. [Azcue Brea](https://recercat.cat/bitstream/handle/2072/522519/04Escultors_Azcue.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1); [Museo del Prado](https://content3.cdnprado.net/imagenes/proyectos/personalizacion/7317a29a-d846-4c54-9034-6a114c3658fe/cms/pdf/memoria_2010.pdf)
- **1886–1888:** *Cabeza de estudio para Sagunto*. Independent head derived from the larger group and catalogued separately in Querol’s posthumous inventory; it resists a simple distinction between preparatory study, fragment and autonomous sculpture. [Guinda Pérez](https://aacadigital.com/articulos/el-legado-postumo-de-agustin-querol-su-estudio-madrileno-su-museo-no-realizado-ni-en-madrid-ni-en-tortosa/)
- **1887:** *La Tradición*. Multi-figure allegorical group of an elderly woman transmitting inherited stories to children; the obra survives through several materially distinct manifestations in bronze, marble and plaster rather than as one unique object. [Museo del Prado](https://www.museodelprado.es/coleccion/artista/agustin-querol-y-subirats/4a182299-ca0a-44ff-84a9-79f60360ff01); [Azcue Brea](https://recercat.cat/bitstream/handle/2072/522519/04Escultors_Azcue.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)
- **1887:** *Niño desnudo*. Figure extracted from, or conceived in relation to, *La Tradición*, surviving in plaster and marble versions; its status oscillates between fragment, study and autonomous obra. [MNAC](https://www.museunacional.cat/es/colleccio/nino-desnudo/agusti-querol/200318-000); [Ribera Gassol](https://museogoya.fundacionibercaja.es/boletines/J0ob8W5D_s4H.pdf)
- **1887:** *Tulia pasando sobre el cadáver de su padre*. Large narrative relief made in Rome as a pension submission, representing the legendary daughter of Servius Tullius; it is chiefly known through documentation and related heads. [Azcue Brea](https://recercat.cat/bitstream/handle/2072/522519/04Escultors_Azcue.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)
- **1887:** *Tulia*. Sculptural head derived from the relief, with multiple marble, plaster and possibly later commercial manifestations; museum catalogues distinguish at least two examples rather than one singular object. [Azcue Brea](https://recercat.cat/bitstream/handle/2072/522519/04Escultors_Azcue.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1); [Guinda Pérez](https://aacadigital.com/articulos/el-legado-postumo-de-agustin-querol-su-estudio-madrileno-su-museo-no-realizado-ni-en-madrid-ni-en-tortosa/)
- **1889:** *Mater Dolorosa* / *Virgen Dolorosa*. Devotional sculpture published under the Latin title and represented in museum and estate records by marble and wooden manifestations; whether every recorded *Dolorosa* belongs to one model remains unresolved. [Universitat de Barcelona conference proceedings](https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/libro/739618/2.pdf); [Guinda Pérez](https://aacadigital.com/articulos/el-legado-postumo-de-agustin-querol-su-estudio-madrileno-su-museo-no-realizado-ni-en-madrid-ni-en-tortosa/)
- **1890–1891:** *San Francisco curando a los leprosos*. Monumental marble relief, begun as Querol’s first submission during his second Roman pension and delayed after accidental breakage; plaster fragments and the finished marble are separate manifestations of the obra. [Azcue Brea](https://recercat.cat/bitstream/handle/2072/522519/04Escultors_Azcue.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)
- **Undated:** *Cabeza de San Francisco de Asís*. Bust or head related to Querol’s Franciscan imagery, known in bronze and plaster versions; inventories treat it independently from the relief of the saint healing lepers. [Guinda Pérez](https://aacadigital.com/articulos/el-legado-postumo-de-agustin-querol-su-estudio-madrileno-su-museo-no-realizado-ni-en-madrid-ni-en-tortosa/); [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **Undated:** *San Francisco*. Patinated-plaster bust preserved through the national museum collections; it may be another manifestation of the preceding model rather than an altogether separate conception. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **Undated:** *La cigarra*. Small terracotta sculpture preserved in Tortosa; the consulted catalogues do not establish a secure date. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **Undated:** *L’hi caurà*. Terracotta known from a private collection and exhibited in Tortosa in 1999; neither its original date nor the precise nature of its subject is securely documented in the consulted sources. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **Undated:** *Mestizia*. Symbolist or funerary female figure documented as a signed work; the accessible scholarly record does not supply a dependable date. [Diccionari d’artistes catalans, valencians i balears](https://artistes.iec.cat/artista.asp?id=525)
- **Undated:** *Emperatriz Elena*. Female bust known from a plaster in Querol’s posthumous holdings and a bronze in the Reales Alcázares; the relation between cast, model and finished manifestation is not fully resolved. [Guinda Pérez](https://aacadigital.com/articulos/el-legado-postumo-de-agustin-querol-su-estudio-madrileno-su-museo-no-realizado-ni-en-madrid-ni-en-tortosa/); [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **Undated, attribution uncertain:** *Dolorosa de la capilla de la Misericordia de Tui*. Devotional carving attributed to Querol in biographical notices, but not securely accepted by the catalogue record. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **Undated, attribution uncertain; destroyed 1936:** *Cristo de la Agonía*. Devotional image formerly in the church of the Asunción at Cieza; known only through an attribution in secondary notices and therefore resistant to inclusion as a secure Querol obra. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)

### Portraits

- **Undated:** *Retrato del tenor Angelo Masini*. Terracotta portrait bust preserved by the Museu Nacional d’Art de Catalunya; no secure creation date is supplied in the consulted catalogue summaries. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **Undated:** *Retrato de Eduard Toda*. Terracotta portrait bust preserved by the Biblioteca Museu Víctor Balaguer. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **1891:** *María Cristina, reina regente*. Marble portrait bust represented by versions in the Palacio Real and the MNAC; these are multiple manifestations of one portrait model. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **Undated, identification unconfirmed:** *Alfonso XIII niño*. Portrait bust reportedly in the Palacio Real; the institutional identification or attribution was not confirmed by the consulted scholarly compilation. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **By 1909:** *María de las Mercedes, princesa de Asturias*. Portrait bust documented in frontal and profile photographs in Querol’s posthumous inventory; those two records are views, not necessarily two separately made busts. [Guinda Pérez](https://aacadigital.com/articulos/el-legado-postumo-de-agustin-querol-su-estudio-madrileno-su-museo-no-realizado-ni-en-madrid-ni-en-tortosa/)
- **Undated:** *Pierre Curie*. Marble portrait bust preserved in Tortosa. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **Undated:** *Marie Curie*. Marble portrait bust preserved in Tortosa; older records call the sitter “Madame Curie.” [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **Undated:** *Santiago Ramón y Cajal*. Portrait bust documented photographically in Querol’s posthumous inventory; its material, location and date remain unstated there. [Guinda Pérez](https://aacadigital.com/articulos/el-legado-postumo-de-agustin-querol-su-estudio-madrileno-su-museo-no-realizado-ni-en-madrid-ni-en-tortosa/)
- **Undated:** *Manuel Pedro de Peña*. Patinated-plaster portrait bust preserved at the Museo de Bellas Artes Juan Silvano Godoi in Asunción. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **Undated:** *Busto de un mártir*. Terracotta portrait or ideal head preserved by the Biblioteca Museu Víctor Balaguer; the sitter is not securely named. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **Undated:** *Bustos femeninos*. Four female portrait or ideal heads in the Museu d’Història de Tarragona; the accessible catalogue summary does not provide individual titles or dates, so forcing names upon them would create fictitious works. [Ribera Gassol](https://museogoya.fundacionibercaja.es/boletines/J0ob8W5D_s4H.pdf)

### Architectural sculpture and Gesamtkunstwerk-like programs

- **1889:** *San Félix y Santa Eulalia*. Pair of stone saints made for the neo-Gothic façade of Barcelona Cathedral; architectural sculpture rather than a freestanding pair conceived for gallery viewing. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **1892–1903:** *Frontón de la Biblioteca y Museos Nacionales*. Vast architectural-sculptural program organized around an allegory of Spain and figures of intellectual and artistic disciplines; the ensemble approaches a Gesamtkunstwerk because architecture, national iconography and sculpture are inseparable. [Biblioteca Nacional de España](https://www.bne.es/); [Diccionari d’artistes catalans, valencians i balears](https://artistes.iec.cat/artista.asp?id=525)
- **1892–1903:** *España con el león*. Crowning figure within the Biblioteca Nacional program; it can be named as a component but resists treatment as a wholly independent obra. [Diccionari d’artistes catalans, valencians i balears](https://artistes.iec.cat/artista.asp?id=525)
- **1892–1903:** *El Genio*. Allegorical component of the Biblioteca Nacional fronton, meaningful primarily within the larger architectural program. [Diccionari d’artistes catalans, valencians i balears](https://artistes.iec.cat/artista.asp?id=525)
- **1892–1903:** *La Paz*. Allegorical component of the Biblioteca Nacional fronton, represented with the olive branch. [Diccionari d’artistes catalans, valencians i balears](https://artistes.iec.cat/artista.asp?id=525)
- **1892–1903:** *La Guerra*. Allegorical component of the Biblioteca Nacional fronton. [Diccionari d’artistes catalans, valencians i balears](https://artistes.iec.cat/artista.asp?id=525)
- **1892–1903:** *La Poesía*. Allegorical component of the Biblioteca Nacional fronton. [Diccionari d’artistes catalans, valencians i balears](https://artistes.iec.cat/artista.asp?id=525)
- **1892–1903:** *La Escultura*. Allegorical component of the Biblioteca Nacional fronton. [Diccionari d’artistes catalans, valencians i balears](https://artistes.iec.cat/artista.asp?id=525)
- **1892–1903:** *La Historia*. Allegorical component of the Biblioteca Nacional fronton. [Diccionari d’artistes catalans, valencians i balears](https://artistes.iec.cat/artista.asp?id=525)
- **1892–1903:** *La Filosofía*. Component documented separately in posthumous photographs, but originally part of the Biblioteca Nacional program. [Guinda Pérez](https://aacadigital.com/articulos/el-legado-postumo-de-agustin-querol-su-estudio-madrileno-su-museo-no-realizado-ni-en-madrid-ni-en-tortosa/)
- **1892–1903:** *Las Artes*. Component or collective designation within the Biblioteca Nacional program, documented photographically after Querol’s death. [Guinda Pérez](https://aacadigital.com/articulos/el-legado-postumo-de-agustin-querol-su-estudio-madrileno-su-museo-no-realizado-ni-en-madrid-ni-en-tortosa/)
- **1897–1905:** *La Gloria y los Pegasos*. Three colossal rooftop groups for the Palacio de Fomento: a synthesis of architecture, allegory and monumental sculpture. The original marble groups were removed and dispersed to Madrid public spaces, while bronze replicas now occupy the building, making “the work” a divided material history rather than a single object. [MAPA catalogue reference](https://www.mapa.gob.es/); [Galán Caballero](https://www.mapa.gob.es/es/ministerio/servicios/publicaciones/)
- **1897–1905:** *La Gloria*. Central component of the Palacio de Fomento crown, also documented independently in Querol’s posthumous records. [Guinda Pérez](https://aacadigital.com/articulos/el-legado-postumo-de-agustin-querol-su-estudio-madrileno-su-museo-no-realizado-ni-en-madrid-ni-en-tortosa/)
- **1897–1905:** *Las Artes*. Component of the Palacio de Fomento program; the title in the estate record may describe a fragment rather than an autonomous sculpture. [Guinda Pérez](https://aacadigital.com/articulos/el-legado-postumo-de-agustin-querol-su-estudio-madrileno-su-museo-no-realizado-ni-en-madrid-ni-en-tortosa/)
- **Circa 1897:** *Moisés y las Leyes* / *Grupo de las Leyes*. Colossal crowning group for Barcelona’s Palace of Justice; inseparable from the building and variously titled in the sources. [Enciclopèdia Catalana](https://www.enciclopedia.cat/el-modernisme/lescultura-aplicada-a-larquitectura); [Guinda Pérez](https://aacadigital.com/articulos/el-legado-postumo-de-agustin-querol-su-estudio-madrileno-su-museo-no-realizado-ni-en-madrid-ni-en-tortosa/)
- **1909–1911:** *Cuatro Pegasos*. Four bronze groups allegorizing lyric and dramatic genres for the Palacio de Bellas Artes in Mexico City; fabricated and installed after Querol’s death, so they are posthumous manifestations of his design rather than wholly autographic finished objects. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)

### Public monuments

- **1890:** *Monumento a Casto Méndez Núñez*. Public commemorative monument in Vigo, combining the admiral’s statue with allegorical and architectural elements. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **1891–1897:** *Monumento a los Bomberos de La Habana*. Funerary-public monument in the Cementerio de Colón, won in competition and made with architect Julio Martínez Zapata; its angel carrying a dead fireman makes it both memorial architecture and narrative sculpture. [Gutiérrez Viñuales](https://ifc.dpz.es/recursos/publicaciones/35/10/_ebook.pdf)
- **1893–1901:** *Monumento a Miguel López de Legazpi y Andrés de Urdaneta*. Colonial commemorative monument designed with architect Luis María Cabello Lapiedra, transferred to Manila and completed under the subsequent American administration; its extended, politically discontinuous realization complicates the idea of a single completion date. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **1896:** *Monumento a José Elduayen*. Public monument in Vigo comprising the statesman’s portrait and four allegories of the ministries he held. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/); [Vigo en Fotos](https://www.vigoenfotos.com/es/vigo/escultura/agustin-querol)
- **1896, attribution disputed:** *Monumento a Teodoro González de la Vega, llamado Ros de Medrano*. Monument in Tortosa for which Querol’s participation has been claimed but remains doubtful; it should not be treated as securely autographic. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **1900:** *Monumento a Aureliano Linares Rivas*. Public commemorative monument in A Coruña. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **1900:** *Monumento a Claudio Moyano*. Public portrait monument in Madrid, joining a standing figure of the educational reformer to an architectural pedestal and subordinate imagery. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **1901–1920:** *Monumento al general Justo José de Urquiza*. Querol designed and began the pedestal but died before completing the monument; Mariano Benlliure supplied the equestrian statue. It is therefore a composite, posthumously completed project rather than an exclusively Querol-made monument. [Gutiérrez Viñuales](https://ifc.dpz.es/recursos/publicaciones/35/10/_ebook.pdf)
- **1902:** *Monumento a Francisco de Quevedo*. Public monument first installed in Madrid’s plaza de Alonso Martínez and later moved to the glorieta de Quevedo; the statue also exists as a separate marble manifestation at the Real Academia Española. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **1902:** *Monumento a Manuel Ibáñez Posada, conde de Ribadedeva*. Public commemorative monument at Colombres, Asturias. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **1902–1905:** *Monumento al coronel Francisco Bolognesi*. Monument in Lima whose original principal statue was replaced in 1954 and transferred to the Fortaleza del Real Felipe; the surviving displaced statue is a component of the original monument, not a new work. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **Circa 1903:** *Monumento a Daniel Carballo*. Public commemorative monument in A Coruña; the date remains approximate in the consulted corpus. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **1904–1906:** *Monumento a los Mártires de la Religión y de la Patria*. Zaragoza monument crowned by an angel carrying a fallen Aragonese figure, on a pedestal designed by Ricardo Magdalena; sources use both 1904 and the 1906 inauguration date. [Gutiérrez Viñuales](https://ifc.dpz.es/recursos/publicaciones/35/10/_ebook.pdf)
- **1904–1906:** *Ángel con el mártir*. Crowning group of the Zaragoza monument, separately documented in Querol’s posthumous holdings but conceived as an integral component. [Guinda Pérez](https://aacadigital.com/articulos/el-legado-postumo-de-agustin-querol-su-estudio-madrileno-su-museo-no-realizado-ni-en-madrid-ni-en-tortosa/)
- **1906:** *Monumento a Casilda Iturrizar*. Public monument in Bilbao combining portraiture, allegory and civic commemoration. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **1906:** *Monumento a Frederic Soler*. Barcelona public monument to the playwright known as Serafí Pitarra. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **1907–1918:** *Monumento a los Próceres del Nueve de Octubre de 1820*. Guayaquil independence monument designed by Querol but unfinished at his death and subsequently completed by other hands; it is best classified as his project with a posthumous, collaborative manifestation. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **1908:** *Monumento a los Sitios de Zaragoza*. Large stone-and-bronze commemorative ensemble made for the centenary exhibition, integrating allegorical Zaragoza, historical figures and the collective action of civilians. [Institución Fernando el Católico](https://ifc.dpz.es/recursos/publicaciones/35/10/_ebook.pdf)
- **1908:** *Zaragoza*. Personification crowning the Monument to the Sieges; documented separately but conceived as a component of the ensemble. [Guinda Pérez](https://aacadigital.com/articulos/el-legado-postumo-de-agustin-querol-su-estudio-madrileno-su-museo-no-realizado-ni-en-madrid-ni-en-tortosa/)
- **1908:** *Agustina de Aragón*. High-relief component of the Monument to the Sieges. [Guinda Pérez](https://aacadigital.com/articulos/el-legado-postumo-de-agustin-querol-su-estudio-madrileno-su-museo-no-realizado-ni-en-madrid-ni-en-tortosa/)
- **1908:** *Las mujeres de Zaragoza*. High-relief component of the Monument to the Sieges, representing collective civic action rather than an isolated portrait. [Guinda Pérez](https://aacadigital.com/articulos/el-legado-postumo-de-agustin-querol-su-estudio-madrileno-su-museo-no-realizado-ni-en-madrid-ni-en-tortosa/)
- **1909:** *Monumento a Segismundo Moret*. Public commemorative monument in Cádiz, completed at the end of Querol’s life. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **1909–1912:** *Monumento a Manuel Domingo y Sol*. Querol supplied the design, but José Casán Matamala and Víctor Cerveto Riba realized the monument after his death; the bronze statue was destroyed in 1936, while the pedestal survives. It is therefore a project and collaborative manifestation, not an entirely autographic object. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **1909–1927:** *Monumento a la Carta Magna y las Cuatro Regiones Argentinas* / *Monumento de los Españoles*. Monumental gift to Argentina, left unfinished when Querol died and completed through a prolonged posthumous process; several intended components were lost in the 1916 wreck of the *Príncipe de Asturias*, and at least one recovered sculpture survives in Brazil. [Gutiérrez Viñuales](https://ifc.dpz.es/recursos/publicaciones/35/10/_ebook.pdf); [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)

### Funerary monuments

- **1892:** *El Salvador para el panteón de Jaime Girona*. Religious sculpture designed for a private funerary monument in Madrid’s Sacramental de San Isidro. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **1903–1906:** *Mausoleo de Antonio Cánovas del Castillo*. Architectural and sculptural tomb in the Panteón de Hombres Ilustres, combining the recumbent statesman with allegorical figures and relief; its mixed media and spatial conception make it closer to a total funerary environment than a single statue. [Diccionari d’artistes catalans, valencians i balears](https://artistes.iec.cat/artista.asp?id=525)
- **1903–1906:** *El Dolor*. Allegorical component of the Cánovas mausoleum, separately recorded in posthumous photographs but not originally autonomous. [Guinda Pérez](https://aacadigital.com/articulos/el-legado-postumo-de-agustin-querol-su-estudio-madrileno-su-museo-no-realizado-ni-en-madrid-ni-en-tortosa/)
- **1905:** *Sepulcro de Pío Bermejillo*. Funerary monument in the church of San Severino at Balmaseda. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **1908–1911:** *Panteón Guirao*. Mausoleum in Madrid’s Sacramental de San Isidro, left incomplete at Querol’s death and finished posthumously; its completion date therefore does not imply that every executed part is autographic. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)

### Unrealized or superseded projects

- **1892:** *Proyecto de monumento a fray Bartolomé de las Casas*. Unexecuted proposal for Mexico; as an unrealized intention it remains a project, not a lost finished monument. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **1893:** *Proyecto de monumento a Juan Pablo Duarte*. Unexecuted proposal for Santo Domingo. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **1897:** *Proyecto de monumento al marqués de Larios*. Competition proposal for Málaga, superseded by Mariano Benlliure’s winning design. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **1900:** *Proyecto de monumento a la Guerra de la Independencia*. Unexecuted project intended for Tarragona. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **1901:** *Proyectos de monumento a Alfonso XII*. At least two monument schemes and a separate equestrian-statue design are documented; Querol received second prize and did not participate in the monument ultimately erected in Madrid’s Retiro. These are three project records, not components of the existing monument. [Guinda Pérez](https://aacadigital.com/articulos/el-legado-postumo-de-agustin-querol-su-estudio-madrileno-su-museo-no-realizado-ni-en-madrid-ni-en-tortosa/); [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **1902:** *Proyecto de monumento a Ramón de Cala y Barea*. Unexecuted proposal for Jerez de la Frontera. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **1904:** *Proyecto de monumento a Jaime I el Conquistador*. Unexecuted commemorative project; the consulted source does not identify a definitive intended site. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **1907:** *Proyectos de monumento al general Bartolomé Mitre*. Several competing designs rather than one fixed composition; their plurality is intrinsic to the project’s status. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **1909:** *Proyecto de monumento a Giuseppe Garibaldi*. Unexecuted proposal for Montevideo, left among Querol’s final monumental projects. [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)

### Paintings and drawings

- **1907–1908:** *Boceto para el Monumento a los Sitios de Zaragoza*. Oil design showing the selected monumental composition; both preparatory image and autonomous painting, now catalogued by the Ayuntamiento de Zaragoza. [CER.es](https://ceres.mcu.es/pages/ResultSearch?MuseumsRolSearch=7&amp;MuseumsSearch=&amp;hipertextSearch=1&amp;listaMuseos=null&amp;search=simpleSelection&amp;simpleSearch=0&amp;txtSimpleSearch=Monumento+a+Los+Sitios+de+Zaragoza)
- **Dates and individual titles not publicly recoverable:** *Paintings and drawings catalogued by Antonio Guinda Pérez*. Guinda’s catalogue raisonné contains sculpture, painting and drawing among 195 chronological records, including works known only from photographs or descriptions. Because the accessible thesis summary does not expose the individual titles, dates or catalogue entries, these cannot responsibly be fabricated into separate named bullets. [Guinda Pérez, thesis summary](https://dialnet.unirioja.es/servlet/tesis?codigo=175103&amp;info=resumen); [Artigrama thesis notice](https://papiro.unizar.es/ojs/index.php/artigrama/article/download/7766/6476/27673)

### Writings and acts

- **1894–1897:** *Writings signed “El Plutarco del Pueblo”*. Literary and journalistic texts published pseudonymously; the consulted scholarly sources establish the corpus’s existence but do not provide a verified title-by-title bibliography, so it resists conversion into invented individual “works.” [Escultura Urbana](https://esculturaurbana.com/podescultor/querol-subirats-agustin/)
- **1907–1909:** *Parliamentary proposals and speeches*. Political interventions made as deputy for Roquetes: texts that acted within parliamentary procedure and therefore resemble protocols or performative acts more than literary obras. Guinda catalogues their documentary corpus, but the accessible account does not supply a complete title-level list. [Artigrama thesis notice](https://papiro.unizar.es/ojs/index.php/artigrama/article/download/7766/6476/27673)
