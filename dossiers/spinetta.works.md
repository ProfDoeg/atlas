## Works by Luis Alberto Spinetta

### Musical projects and collective bodies of work

- **1967–1970; 1979–1981:** *Almendra*. Rock ensemble founded with Emilio del Guercio, Edelmiro Molinari and Rodolfo García; its recordings are collective works, although Spinetta wrote most of the first album and supplied the group’s defining poetic and visual language. [Discography](https://rock.com.ar/artistas/luis-alberto-spinetta/)
- **1971–1973:** *Pescado Rabioso*. Band and evolving compositional project; the name remained attached to *Artaud* after the ensemble had dissolved, making that record’s official authorship misleading. [Biography and discography](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **1973–1977:** *Invisible*. Trio, later quartet, formed with Héctor “Pomo” Lorenzo and Carlos “Machi” Rufino; authorship is partly collective and should not be reduced to Spinetta alone. [Discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **1977:** *Banda Spinetta*. Short-lived jazz-rock formation assembled for *A 18' del sol*; better understood as a recording and performance project than as a stable band. [Discography](https://www.spinettabootlegs.com.ar/discografia)
- **1980–1985:** *Spinetta Jade*. Changing electric ensemble through which Spinetta pursued jazz-rock, harmonic and electronic composition across four albums. [Discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **1986:** *Spinetta/Páez*. Temporary collaborative project with Fito Páez, manifested principally in the double album *La la la* rather than a permanent group. [Discography](https://rock.com.ar/artistas/luis-alberto-spinetta/)
- **1994–1999:** *Spinetta y los Socios del Desierto*. Power-trio project with Marcelo Torres and Daniel Wirtz; its delayed debut album was also an assertion of artistic independence against record-company conditions. [Discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **2009:** *Spinetta y las Bandas Eternas*. Five-hour concert project reassembling musicians from successive periods of Spinetta’s career; it functioned as a retrospective Gesamtkunstwerk of songs, bands, staging and historical memory. [Concert history](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spinetta_y_las_Bandas_Eternas)
- **2011:** *Spinetta Los Amigo*. Final trio project with Daniel Ferrón and Rodolfo García, recorded privately and left unpublished at Spinetta’s death; its status lies between finished album and posthumously completed project. [Discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)

### Studio albums with Almendra

- **1969:** *Almendra*. Debut studio album and collective recording; Spinetta composed most of its songs and devised its emblematic cover figure. [Discography](https://kiwix.tinku.pe/content/wikipedia_es_all_maxi_2024-05/A/Anexo%3ADiscograf%C3%ADa_de_Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **1970:** *Almendra II*. Double studio album by the original group; because its members contributed separately composed material, it is not solely a Spinetta work. [Discography](https://kiwix.tinku.pe/content/wikipedia_es_all_maxi_2024-05/A/Anexo%3ADiscograf%C3%ADa_de_Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **1980:** *El valle interior*. Studio album made by the reunited Almendra; a manifestation of the reunion rather than a continuation by an unchanged working band. [Discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)

### Studio albums with Pescado Rabioso

- **1972:** *Desatormentándonos*. Pescado Rabioso’s debut album; later reissues added “Me gusta ese tajo,” so the work’s track contents vary by manifestation. [Album history](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **1973:** *Pescado 2*. Double album accompanied by a 52-page handwritten booklet of lyrics, explanations, poems, drawings and photographs; the integrated sound-and-print object approaches a Gesamtkunstwerk rather than an ordinary record. [Album description](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pescado_2)
- **1973:** *Artaud*. In substance a Spinetta solo album, but contractually issued under Pescado Rabioso’s name after the band’s dissolution; its attribution is therefore formally and historically divided. Its irregular package, music, printed insert and polemical presentation form a deliberate cross-media object. [Study of the album and design](https://www.evelia.unrc.edu.ar/evelia/archivos/idAula110406438188/materiales/2_Teoricos/Bibliografia_/spinetta-artaud-verano-del-setenta-y-tres.pdf)

### Studio albums with Invisible

- **1974:** *Invisible*. Debut studio album by the trio; the composition and performance credits make it a collective obra with Spinetta as principal songwriter, not an unqualified solo work. [Discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **1975:** *Durazno sangrando*. Conceptually linked to Carl Jung’s and Richard Wilhelm’s writings, but still a rock album rather than an adaptation or book. [Discography](https://kiwix.tinku.pe/content/wikipedia_es_all_maxi_2024-05/A/Anexo%3ADiscograf%C3%ADa_de_Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **1976:** *El jardín de los presentes*. Studio album by Invisible’s expanded quartet; arrangements and performances are collective even where Spinetta supplied the songs. [Discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)

### Studio albums with Spinetta Jade

- **1980:** *Alma de diamante*. Debut studio album by Spinetta Jade, conceived around Spinetta’s encounter with Carlos Castaneda’s writings. [Discography](https://www.spinettabootlegs.com.ar/discografia)
- **1981:** *Los niños que escriben en el cielo*. Second Spinetta Jade studio album and a collective electric-band recording. [Discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **1983:** *Bajo Belgrano*. Studio album whose songs and visual presentation turn Spinetta’s neighbourhood into poetic and political geography. [Discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **1984:** *Madre en años luz*. Final Spinetta Jade album; its programmed drums and electronic production make it both a band record and a transition toward Spinetta’s later studio practice. [Discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)

### Studio albums with Los Socios del Desierto

- **1997:** *Spinetta y los Socios del Desierto*. Double studio album recorded by the trio in 1995–96 but withheld during Spinetta’s dispute with record companies; recording date and publication date therefore differ. [Album record](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spinetta_y_los_Socios_del_Desierto_%28%C3%A1bum%29)
- **1999:** *Los ojos*. Second and final studio album by Los Socios del Desierto, augmented by guest musicians. [Discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)

### Solo and collaborative studio albums

- **1971:** *Spinettalandia y sus amigos — La búsqueda de la estrella*. Loose, improvisatory studio collaboration with Pappo, Miguel Abuelo and Pomo; RCA first issued it misleadingly under the title *Almendra*, so both title and artist attribution have a contested publication history. [Discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **1977:** *A 18' del sol*. Jazz-rock studio album credited to Spinetta and made with the temporary Banda Spinetta ensemble. [Discography](https://www.spinettabootlegs.com.ar/discografia)
- **1980:** *Only Love Can Sustain*. English-language studio album recorded in the United States under substantial label and producer direction; Spinetta later rejected aspects of the result, so it resists being treated as an entirely self-determined obra. [Biographical account](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **1982:** *Kamikaze*. Solo studio album largely gathering compositions written years earlier but not previously recorded on an album; its publication date is not the date of every song’s making. [Discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **1983:** *Mondo di cromo*. Solo studio album combining songs with instrumental and electronic miniatures. [Discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **1986:** *Privé*. Solo studio album shaped by electronic production and by remnants of an unrealized collaborative project with Charly García. [Discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **1986:** *La la la*. Double studio album co-made with Fito Páez; authorship belongs to both musicians, with individual and jointly made songs, and cannot accurately be assigned wholesale to Spinetta. [Discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **1988:** *Téster de violencia*. Conceptual studio album organized around the body as an instrument registering social violence. [Album account](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C3%A9ster_de_violencia)
- **1989:** *Don Lucero*. Solo studio album made with a large ensemble and increasingly digital studio resources. [Discography](https://www.spinettabootlegs.com.ar/discografia)
- **1991:** *Pelusón of Milk*. Predominantly home-made solo studio album, with Spinetta performing or programming much of the sound himself. [Discography](https://rock.com.ar/artistas/luis-alberto-spinetta/)
- **1993:** *Fuego gris*. Seventeen-song studio album composed and performed from the screenplay for Pablo César’s film; it is simultaneously an autonomous concept album and a soundtrack, not merely incidental film music. [Production account](https://www.cesarproducciones.com/filmography/fuego-gris)
- **2001:** *Silver Sorgo*. Solo studio album and the source project for the subsequent *Spinetta Obras* concert recording. [Discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **2003:** *Para los árboles*. Solo studio album joining songs, samples and electronic sound design; its working title was reportedly *Canción de noche*. [Design interview](https://www.pagina12.com.ar/diario/suplementos/radar/subnotas/7690-1643-2012-02-12.html)
- **2004:** *Camalotus*. Four-track EP rather than a full album; later issued with an audiovisual component. [Discography](https://kiwix.tinku.pe/content/wikipedia_es_all_maxi_2024-05/A/Anexo%3ADiscograf%C3%ADa_de_Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **2006:** *Pan*. Solo studio album made with his late-period band. [Discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **2008:** *Un mañana*. Last studio album published during Spinetta’s lifetime. [Discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)

### Posthumously manifested studio projects

- **recorded 2011; published 2015:** *Los Amigo*. Final trio recordings by Spinetta, Rodolfo García and Daniel Ferrón, edited and issued after Spinetta’s death. The album is his creation at the level of performances and compositions, but its final public form is partly the work of his heirs and collaborators. [Discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **recorded 2008–2009; published 2020:** *Ya no mires atrás*. Album assembled posthumously from completed, previously unreleased recordings; classifying it as a studio album or an archival compilation remains inconsistent across discographies. [Discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)

### Live albums and concert manifestations

- **1980:** *Almendra en Obras I*. First album drawn from the reunited Almendra’s Obras Sanitarias concerts; a recorded manifestation of collective performances. [Discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **1980:** *Almendra en Obras II*. Companion live volume from the reunion concerts, sometimes treated with the first volume as one double work. [Discography](https://kiwix.tinku.pe/content/wikipedia_es_all_maxi_2024-05/A/Anexo%3ADiscograf%C3%ADa_de_Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **1990:** *Exactas*. Spinetta’s first released solo live album, recorded at the University of Buenos Aires’ Facultad de Ciencias Exactas. [Album record](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exactas)
- **1997:** *Estrelicia: MTV Unplugged*. Live acoustic television session issued as an album; broadcast, concert and record are distinct manifestations of the same performance project. [Discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **1998:** *San Cristóforo: un sauna de lava eléctrico*. Live album by Los Socios del Desierto, also manifested as a concert video. [Discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **2002:** *Argentina Sorgo Films presenta: Spinetta Obras*. Live album documenting the Obras Sanitarias presentation of *Silver Sorgo* and older material. [Biography](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **2010:** *Spinetta y las Bandas Eternas*. Multi-disc live album and video drawn from the 2009 retrospective concert; because it reunites several historically separate ensembles, it is more archive-in-performance than an ordinary solo live record. [Concert and album record](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spinetta_y_las_Bandas_Eternas)
- **performed 1973; published 2020:** *Presentación ARTAUD — 1973 — Teatro Astral*. Posthumous official release of an archival concert recording; Spinetta made the performance, but not its later mastering and publication object. [Release history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **performed 1973; published 2021:** *Presentación ARTAUD — 1973 — Teatro Astral, Vol. 2*. Second archival volume from the same concert, and therefore a posthumous editorial division rather than a separately conceived work by Spinetta. [Discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **performed 1975; published 2022:** *Invisible en vivo: Teatro Coliseo 1975*. Posthumous archival album of an Invisible concert; it documents a collective event, while the released album itself was not fashioned by Spinetta. [Discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)

### Curated recordings

- **1999:** *Elija y gane*. Career-spanning compilation whose selection makes it a curated retrospective rather than a new cycle of compositions. [Discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **2001:** *Obras cumbres*. Commercial anthology of Pescado Rabioso and Invisible recordings; it preserves Spinetta’s work but resists inclusion as a new work made by him, since the compilation’s editorial authorship is not clearly his. [Album record](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obras_cumbres_%28%C3%A1lbum_de_Invisible%29)

### Print, poetry and manifesto

- **1973:** *Pescado 2: booklet*. Fifty-two-page handwritten album insert containing drawings, photographs, poems, reflections, explanations and song texts. It is neither simply liner notes nor an independent book, but the printed component of the album’s larger cross-media obra. [Album description](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pescado_2)
- **1973:** *Rock: música dura, la suicidada por la sociedad*. Manifesto distributed to the audience at the Teatro Astral presentation of *Artaud*; it acts by opposing commercialized “rock” and proposing transformation, rather than merely describing the album. [Historical account](https://www.clarin.com/espectaculos/manifiesto-escrito-flaco-documento-definio_0_S1Xmqw2wme.html)
- **1978:** *Guitarra negra*. Spinetta’s sole published poetry collection during his lifetime, selected from poems, drawings, stories and notes accumulated in notebooks over several years. [Publisher](https://lamarcaeditora.com/catalogo/poesia-307/guitarra-negra-edicion-especial-tapa-dura-1181)
- **1986:** *El sonido primordial*. Lecture-text or aesthetic discourse delivered in the context of a music-therapy congress and circulated independently only later; it resists the status of a book authored and published by Spinetta, despite unauthorized or posthumous book-like editions. [Publication history](https://carasycaretas.org.ar/2024/04/19/spinetta-y-el-sonido-primordial/)

### Graphic and cross-media works

- **1969:** *The man on the cover of Almendra*. Cover drawing conceived by Spinetta as a visual key to the album’s songs; the label reportedly lost the first version and he drew it again, so the familiar image is itself a replacement. [Album and visual-work context](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **1973:** *Artaud album object*. Irregular green-and-yellow sleeve, inner bag and printed matter devised by Spinetta to resist the standardized square record package. Its inseparability of music, typography, drawing, polemic and physical form makes “Gesamtkunstwerk” more exact than “album cover.” [National Library essay](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/files/0a7aa945006f6e9505e36c8321bc5c4a.pdf)
- **throughout his career:** *Untitled drawings, notebooks, concert posters and album-cover designs*. Spinetta continuously drew and designed, but no consulted catalogue raisonné provides a complete item-level corpus; many objects remain unpublished, untitled or embedded in record packaging, so treating them as a finite list of autonomous “works” would manufacture false completeness. [Study of his intermedial practice](https://www.dspace.uce.edu.ec/server/api/core/bitstreams/d81a3940-7962-42f8-bbed-5ec5d0a662a5/content)

### Audiovisual and cinematic work

- **1973:** *Rock hasta que se ponga el sol: Pescado Rabioso sequences*. Filmed performances and a fictionalized sequence featuring Pescado Rabioso; Spinetta participated as performer, but the film as a whole was Aníbal Uset’s work. [Filmography](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Alberto_Spinetta)
- **1986:** *Spinetta, el video*. Music documentary/performance video directed by Pablo Perel; Spinetta is its subject and performer, not its sole cinematic author. [Filmography](https://www.dejavu.org/cgi-bin/get.cgi?url=https%3A%2F%2Fes.wikipedia.org%2Fwiki%2FLuis_Alberto_Spinetta&ver=95)
- **1987:** *Balada para un Kaiser Carabela*. Fernando Spiner short film containing Spinetta’s only credited acting appearance; this is participation in another maker’s obra, not a Spinetta film. [Filmography](https://www.dejavu.org/cgi-bin/get.cgi?url=https%3A%2F%2Fes.wikipedia.org%2Fwiki%2FLuis_Alberto_Spinetta&ver=95)
- **1993–1994:** *Fuego gris*. Feature film directed by Pablo César from a screenplay by César and Gustavo Viau, structured around Spinetta’s seventeen-song score. The film and album are mutually dependent manifestations, but authorship of the film is collaborative rather than Spinetta’s alone. [Production record](https://www.cesarproducciones.com/filmography/fuego-gris)
- **1993:** *El acto en cuestión: musical performances*. Spinetta performed music for Alejandro Agresti’s film; the contribution exists, but available filmographies do not establish it as a separately titled Spinetta score. [Filmography](https://www.dejavu.org/cgi-bin/get.cgi?url=https%3A%2F%2Fes.wikipedia.org%2Fwiki%2FLuis_Alberto_Spinetta&ver=95)
- **1998:** *San Cristóforo*. Concert video by Spinetta y los Socios del Desierto, an audiovisual manifestation of the live-album project. [Filmography](https://www.dejavu.org/cgi-bin/get.cgi?url=https%3A%2F%2Fes.wikipedia.org%2Fwiki%2FLuis_Alberto_Spinetta&ver=95)
- **2005:** *Camalotus DVD*. Audiovisual collection issued with the special edition of the EP; it supplements rather than duplicates the musical object. [Filmography](https://www.dejavu.org/cgi-bin/get.cgi?url=https%3A%2F%2Fes.wikipedia.org%2Fwiki%2FLuis_Alberto_Spinetta&ver=95)
- **2006:** *Fuga: a-cappella appearances*. Spinetta appears performing songs in Pablo Larraín’s feature; these are performances within another director’s work, not a film authored by Spinetta. [Filmography](https://www.dejavu.org/cgi-bin/get.cgi?url=https%3A%2F%2Fes.wikipedia.org%2Fwiki%2FLuis_Alberto_Spinetta&ver=95)
- **2008:** *Un mañana DVD*. Collection of videos included with the album’s special edition, making that edition a sound-and-image object. [Filmography](https://www.dejavu.org/cgi-bin/get.cgi?url=https%3A%2F%2Fes.wikipedia.org%2Fwiki%2FLuis_Alberto_Spinetta&ver=95)
- **2010:** *Spinetta y las Bandas Eternas*. Concert film and DVD derived from the 2009 performance; it is the audiovisual manifestation of the larger retrospective project. [Filmography](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spinetta_y_las_Bandas_Eternas)
