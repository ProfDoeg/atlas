## Works by Franz Pforr

### Paintings

- **1807:** *Self-Portrait*. An oil portrait now in the Germanisches Nationalmuseum, distinct from Pforr’s later Frankfurt self-portrait; the date and location are recorded in the scholarly biography. [Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd11884041X.html)
- **1808–1810:** *The Entry of King Rudolf of Habsburg into Basel in 1273*. A large oil-on-canvas history painting and one of Pforr’s principal completed works. [Städel Museum](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/werk/der-einzug-des-koenigs-rudolf-von-habsburg-in-basel-1273)
- **1809–1810:** *Rudolf of Habsburg and the Priest* (also called *The Count of Habsburg Gives His Horse to the Priest*). An oil painting conceived as the companion to the Basel entry; it remained unfinished, so it is simultaneously a painting and an unrealized historical project. [Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd11884041X.html), [Städel Museum collection](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/person/pforr-franz)
- **1810:** *Self-Portrait*. A small oil-on-canvas portrait mounted on cardboard, now in the Städel Museum. [Städel Museum](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/werk/selbstbildnis-5)
- **1811:** *Saint George and the Dragon*. An oil painting on panel, rediscovered only in 1923; doubts were subsequently raised about the attribution when the picture appeared on the market, although it continues to be catalogued under Pforr’s name. [Städel Museum collection](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/person/pforr-franz), [Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd11884041X.html)
- **1811:** *Sulamith and Maria*. A small oil-on-panel double image—often called a diptych, though both fictive women inhabit one elaborately structured object—made as a friendship picture for Friedrich Overbeck. It operates across painting, religious allegory, imagined biography and literary fiction and therefore approaches a miniature Gesamtkunstwerk rather than an ordinary narrative painting. [University of Pennsylvania, *Painting the Sacred in the Age of Romanticism*](https://arth.sas.upenn.edu/sites/www.sas.upenn.edu.arthistory/files/Painting-the-Sacred.pdf), [Schwabe](https://www.schwabeonline.ch/schwabe-xaveropp/elibrary/start.xav?start=%2F%2F%2A%5B%40attr_id%3D%27pdf.978-3-7965-3877-3.9783796538773_4._Reale_Momente_fuer_eine_erdichtete_Kunstwelt%27+and+%40outline_id%3D%27pdf.978-3-7965-3877-3.9783796538773_4._Reale_Momente_fuer_eine_erdichtete_Kunstwelt%27%5D)

### Drawing books and multipart projects

- **1794:** *Sketchbook of fifty-six sheets*. A childhood drawing book made when Pforr was six; it is one physical object containing numerous drawings rather than fifty-six independently titled works. [Badische Landesbibliothek, *Heidelberger Jahrbuch zur Geschichte der Stadt*](https://regionalia.blb-karlsruhe.de/files/24412/BLB_Heidelberg_Jahrbuch_2021_25.pdf)
- *Twelve Juvenile Drawings*. A surviving group catalogued by the Städel as one multipart work: *At a Church Service*; *Fair or Circus Scenes*; *Crouching Figure before a Hole from Which Cats Emerge*; *The Magic Flute*; *Horse-Drawn Cart and People in the Street*; *Procession*; *Students Duelling and an Assembly of Animals*; *Herd of Donkeys*; *Two Men and a Soldier with Speech Balloons*; *Three Armed Horsemen*; *A Messenger Brings a Seated Man a Letter*; and *Officer on Horseback before Three Soldiers*. [Städel Museum](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/werk/zwoelf-jugendzeichnungen)
- **c. 1807:** *Medieval Hunting Scene*. A pen-and-ink drawing formerly attributed to Moritz von Schwind; its present attribution to Pforr therefore has a documented history rather than being entirely uncontested by provenance. [Metropolitan Museum of Art](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/635402)
- **before April 1810:** *Illustrations to Goethe’s Götz von Berlichingen*, first version. A project of ten small compositional drawings shown to the Lukasbund in Vienna; their status is preparatory, but the intended cycle—not merely each surviving sheet—is the obra. [Goethezeitportal](https://www.goethezeitportal.de/wissen/illustrationen/johann-wolfgang-von-goethe/goetz-von-berlichingen/franz-pforr-zeichnungen-zu-goethes-goetz-von-berlichingen.html)
- **1810:** *Illustrations to Goethe’s Götz von Berlichingen*, contour version sent to Goethe. This version comprises a title page, twelve narrative scenes and a closing sheet: *Brother Martin and Götz*; *Adelheid and Weislingen*; *In the Spessart: Götz, Selbitz and Georg*; *Inn: Peasant Wedding*; *Two Nuremberg Merchants before Emperor Maximilian and Weislingen*; *Height with Watchtower: the Wounded Selbitz*; *Siege of Jaxthausen*; *Götz in Heilbronn Town Hall*; *Sickingen before Heilbronn*; *Götz and the Peasant Leaders*; *The Dying Weislingen and Maria*; and *Garden by the Tower: Death of Götz*. It is an illustrated cycle and gift, not a book authored by Pforr. [Goethezeitportal](https://www.goethezeitportal.de/wissen/illustrationen/johann-wolfgang-von-goethe/goetz-von-berlichingen/franz-pforr-zeichnungen-zu-goethes-goetz-von-berlichingen.html)
- **1811:** *Illustrations to Goethe’s Götz von Berlichingen*, Roman fair-copy version. Pforr began a third, more fully modelled version in an “old-German engraving manner” but died before completing it; calling it a project is more accurate than treating it as a finished publication. [Goethezeitportal](https://www.goethezeitportal.de/wissen/illustrationen/johann-wolfgang-von-goethe/goetz-von-berlichingen/franz-pforr-zeichnungen-zu-goethes-goetz-von-berlichingen.html), [Galerie Hans catalogue](https://epub.sub.uni-hamburg.de/epub/volltexte/2020/112040/pdf/Galerie_Hans_Katalog_Goethe.pdf)
- **c. 1810:** *Scene from Act IV of Goethe’s Götz von Berlichingen*. A pen-and-ink drawing with grey wash now in Washington; it belongs to Pforr’s repeated engagement with the illustration cycle, although the museum catalogues the sheet as an individual object. [National Gallery of Art](https://www.nga.gov/artists/5619-franz-pforr)
- *Illustrations to Don Quixote*. A three-sheet literary cycle comprising *Don Quixote’s Occupation at Home*, *Don Quixote’s Heroic Combat with the Biscayan Squire* and *Don Quixote’s Unfortunate Combat with the Enchanted Giants*. [Städel Museum collection](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/person/pforr-franz)
- *Studies and compositions for The Entry of Rudolf of Habsburg into Basel*. These include the separately catalogued drawing *Entry of Emperor Rudolf of Habsburg into Basel*; they are manifestations of the larger painting project rather than necessarily autonomous finished works. [Städel Museum collection](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/person/pforr-franz)
- *Studies and compositions for Saint George and the Dragon*. At least two sheets with the same subject are separately preserved and catalogued; their duplication is material evidence of a developing project, not a repeated title accidentally generated by a catalogue. [Städel Museum collection](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/person/pforr-franz)
- *Allegories of Friendship*. Two separately catalogued drawings bearing this title survive in the Städel collection; they belong to Pforr’s pictorial thinking about his bond with Overbeck, but should not be silently collapsed into one object. [Städel Museum collection](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/person/pforr-franz)

### Independent drawings and studies

- **1810:** *Raphael, Fra Angelico and Michelangelo on a Cloud above Rome*. A highly finished pencil drawing that makes artistic genealogy into a visionary manifestation of the hoped-for union of German and Italian art. [Städel Museum](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/werk/raffael-fra-angelico-und-michelangelo-auf-einer-wolke-ueber)
- **1810:** *Don Diego Ordóñez de Lara’s Combat with Fernando Gonzalo, after Herder’s Cid*. A signed and dated pencil drawing made in Rome as an album contribution; it is simultaneously an autonomous literary illustration and a leaf in another person’s friendship album. [*Treffpunkt Rom 1810*](https://books.ub.uni-heidelberg.de/arthistoricum/catalog/view/740/1213/94531)
- *Raphael and Dürer before the Throne of Art*. An allegorical drawing that joins Pforr’s and Overbeck’s respective artistic ideals; Overbeck made a related copy, so the surviving image participates in a dialogue between the two artists. [Princeton University, *Unwilling Moderns*](https://commons.princeton.edu/lgossman/wp-content/uploads/sites/64/2018/04/nazarene_essay.pdf)
- *The Three Witches Conjure a Bloody Child before Macbeth*. A literary illustration in drawing, not a freestanding written adaptation of Shakespeare. [Städel Museum collection](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/person/pforr-franz)
- *The Murder of Albert I by John of Swabia*. A historical composition preserved as a drawing. [Städel Museum collection](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/person/pforr-franz)
- *The Reconciliation of Emperor Otto the Great with His Brother Henry*. A historical drawing whose subject identification is explicitly catalogued with a question mark and must therefore remain uncertain. [Städel Museum collection](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/person/pforr-franz)
- *Saint Michael Casting Down the Devil*. A religious composition in drawing. [Städel Museum collection](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/person/pforr-franz)
- *Departure for Battle*. A drawn military-historical composition. [Städel Museum collection](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/person/pforr-franz)
- *Siege of a Castle*. A drawn medieval battle composition. [Städel Museum collection](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/person/pforr-franz)
- *The Wild Rider: Three Horsemen with Retinue and Five Hounds*. A pen-and-ink narrative or fantasy composition; the descriptive title is curatorial rather than necessarily Pforr’s own. [Städel Museum collection](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/person/pforr-franz)
- *A Knight Sets Out Hunting with Servants and Many Hounds*. A medievalizing hunting composition in drawing. [Städel Museum collection](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/person/pforr-franz)
- *Farmers at the Harvest*. A rural genre drawing. [Städel Museum collection](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/person/pforr-franz)
- *The Ossuary of Pfeffel*. A topographical or architectural drawing identified by the depicted site. [Städel Museum collection](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/person/pforr-franz)
- *Greeting of Two Men, with Another Man Carrying a Sword*. An untitled narrative drawing known through a modern descriptive title. [Städel Museum collection](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/person/pforr-franz)
- *A Bear and an Aggressive Wild Boar*. An animal study or imagined animal encounter in drawing. [Städel Museum collection](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/person/pforr-franz)
- *A Stag and a Rubbing Ibex*. An animal study in drawing. [Städel Museum collection](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/person/pforr-franz)
- *Elephant and Camel with Riders*. A figure-and-animal study. [Städel Museum collection](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/person/pforr-franz)
- *Three Poles*. A costume or figure study identified by a historical curatorial title. [Städel Museum collection](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/person/pforr-franz)
- *A Russian Priest*. A costume and figure study. [Städel Museum collection](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/person/pforr-franz)
- *Russian Boy with Hat*. A figure study preserved in Frankfurt. [Städel Museum](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/werk/russischer-knabe-mit-hut)
- *Head of Juno after a Plaster Cast*. An academic study after a cast rather than an independently invented composition; its resistance to “work” status is part of its function as training material. [Städel Museum](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/werk/kopf-der-juno-nach-einem-gipsabguss)
- *Mounted Soldier and Three Standing Soldiers in Different Uniforms*. A military costume study. [Städel Museum collection](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/person/pforr-franz)
- *Three Armed Horsemen*. A military study also preserved within the multipart group of juvenile drawings; whether it should be counted independently or as one component of that group depends on whether the physical leaf or the catalogued ensemble is treated as the obra. [Städel Museum](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/de/werk/zwoelf-jugendzeichnungen)

### Literary obra

- **c. 1810–1812:** *Das Buch Sulamith und Maria* (*The Book of Sulamith and Maria*). An unfinished Künstlerroman, preserved as a fragment rather than a completed book; its characters, interiors and symbolic relationships overlap with the painting *Sulamith and Maria*, making text and image manifestations of one larger imaginative project. [Schwabe](https://www.schwabeonline.ch/schwabe-xaveropp/elibrary/start.xav?start=%2F%2F%2A%5B%40attr_id%3D%27pdf.978-3-7965-3877-3.9783796538773_4._Reale_Momente_fuer_eine_erdichtete_Kunstwelt%27+and+%40outline_id%3D%27pdf.978-3-7965-3877-3.9783796538773_4._Reale_Momente_fuer_eine_erdichtete_Kunstwelt%27%5D), [University of Pittsburgh](https://d-scholarship.pitt.edu/9775/1/Horvath_ETD_2009.pdf)

### Collective protocols and manifestations

- **1809:** *Lukasbund Covenant Letter* (*Bundesbrief*). A collectively invented constitutive text of the Lukasbund, made with Overbeck and the other founding “brothers”; individual sentences cannot safely be assigned to Pforr, so it is a collective manifesto and social protocol rather than a solely authored work. [Freies Deutsches Hochstift](https://guide.freies-deutsches-hochstift.de/mediaguide/romantik-ausstellung/3-obergeschoss/lukasbrueder/)
- **1809 onward:** *Lukasbund Rules of Life and Work Plans*. Collective protocols governing artistic labour and communal conduct; Pforr participated in making and enacting them, but the surviving texts belong to the group and resist inclusion as individually authored works. [Freies Deutsches Hochstift](https://guide.freies-deutsches-hochstift.de/mediaguide/romantik-ausstellung/3-obergeschoss/lukasbrueder/)
