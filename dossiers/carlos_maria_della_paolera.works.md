## Works by Carlos María della Paolera

### Urban projects, plans, and technical studies

- **1912:** *Proyecto de provisión de agua para una ciudad de 100.000 habitantes*. Civil-engineering graduation thesis: an infrastructure project rather than a conventional book. The standard scholarly bibliography describes it more generally as a thesis on urban infrastructure. [Novick and Piccioni, pp. 3–4](https://studylib.es/doc/4782531/carlos-mar%C3%ADa-della-paolera--buenos-aires)
- **1927:** *Contribution à l’étude d’un plan d’aménagement, d’embellissement et d’extension de Buenos Aires: étude sur l’évolution de la ville*. Unpublished Institut d’Urbanisme de Paris thesis, supervised by Marcel Poëte; simultaneously an evolutionary urban study and the groundwork for a regulatory plan. Sources disagree whether the diploma was completed in 1927 or formally received in 1928. [Novick and Piccioni, bibliography](https://studylib.es/doc/4782531/carlos-mar%C3%ADa-della-paolera--buenos-aires); [Almandoz and Ibarra, eds., p. 168](https://estudiosurbanos.uc.cl/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/9789560106148-Almandoz-e-Ibarra-2018-Vi%CC%81speras-del-urbanismo.pdf)
- **1929–1935:** *Plan Regulador y de Extensión de Rosario*. Regulatory and extension plan made with Ángel Guido and Adolfo Farengo. Scholarship cautions that Guido ultimately wrote most of the completed plan, so this is a collaborative obra and not securely divisible into individual authorships. [HiSTOReLo study](https://revistas.unal.edu.co/index.php/historelo/article/download/86896/79147/524095?inline=1)
- **1932–1943:** *Plan de Urbanización y Extensión de Buenos Aires*. A municipal planning project directed by Della Paolera through the office he created; it generated surveys, maps, statistics, exhibitions, and discrete interventions but never became the comprehensive executed plan he intended. Its resistance to being called a finished “work” is central: scholarship characterizes the movement from global conception to fragmentary realization. [Novick and Piccioni, pp. 12–17](https://studylib.es/doc/4782531/carlos-mar%C3%ADa-della-paolera--buenos-aires); [Café de las Ciudades](https://cafedelasciudades.com.ar/articulos/que-es-el-amba-i/)
- **1933:** *Primera Exposición Municipal de Urbanismo*. A public manifestation of the Buenos Aires planning office’s historical maps, population studies, statistics, geological material, and land-use analyses; it was an exhibition-survey rather than a single authored text. [Novick and Piccioni, pp. 13–14](https://studylib.es/doc/4782531/carlos-mar%C3%ADa-della-paolera--buenos-aires)
- **1933:** *Avenida Parque Norte–Sur* (first proposal). Urban project replacing a full 100-metre-wide strip of blocks with an unbuilt park avenue and a rapid-traffic road below ground. It was not executed in this form and must not be identified without qualification with the subsequently built Avenida 9 de Julio. [Novick and Piccioni, pp. 14–15](https://studylib.es/doc/4782531/carlos-mar%C3%ADa-della-paolera--buenos-aires)
- **1934:** *Centro de Edificios de Gobierno en la Avenida Parque*. Variant of the north–south avenue project, adding a governmental centre at Avenida de Mayo and monumental terminations; an unrealized project. [Novick and Piccioni, pp. 14–15](https://studylib.es/doc/4782531/carlos-mar%C3%ADa-della-paolera--buenos-aires)
- **1937:** *Avenida Parque 9 de Julio* (revised proposal). Second version retaining traffic at grade while avoiding crossings with the existing subway. The realized avenue drew on a longer, multi-author history, so Della Paolera’s proposal is an antecedent, not sole authorship of the built work. [Novick and Piccioni, pp. 14–15](https://studylib.es/doc/4782531/carlos-mar%C3%ADa-della-paolera--buenos-aires)
- **1939:** *Plan de integración de la trama urbana de Junín / Avenida San Martín*. Urban intervention intended to connect sectors of Junín; the specialist study describes Della Paolera as its promoter, so “impulsó” is safer than claiming exclusive authorship of every component. [CONICET record](https://bicyt.conicet.gov.ar/fichas/produccion/en/8154251)
- **1939:** *Segunda Exposición Municipal de Urbanismo*. Exhibition of particular intervention projects produced by the Buenos Aires planning office; a collective institutional manifestation under Della Paolera’s direction, not an individually authored book. [Novick and Piccioni, pp. 13–14](https://studylib.es/doc/4782531/carlos-mar%C3%ADa-della-paolera--buenos-aires)
- **1942:** *Contribuciones al Código de Edificación de Buenos Aires*. Regulatory work rather than a single independently authored text; the scholarly account credits contributions, not sole authorship. [Novick and Piccioni, p. 15](https://studylib.es/doc/4782531/carlos-mar%C3%ADa-della-paolera--buenos-aires)
- **By 1944:** *Levantamiento del Catastro Municipal de Buenos Aires*. A cartographic-administrative undertaking credited among his contributions. Its collective institutional production resists classification as an individual obra. [Novick and Piccioni, p. 15](https://studylib.es/doc/4782531/carlos-mar%C3%ADa-della-paolera--buenos-aires)
- **Date not established:** *Museo Municipal de Arte Urbano de La Plata*. A posthumous memorial publication calls Della Paolera its author, but the accessible source does not clarify whether this means a building, collection, exhibition institution, or unrealized project; it therefore resists firmer classification. [*Jour mondial de l’urbanisme*, 1961](https://editionsanthonykrafft.com/media/filer_public/c0/49/c0492261-4a0b-401d-8134-501ed39a6c65/jour-mondial-de-lurbanisme-1961-arts-plastiques-amsterdam-amersfoort-alberto-sartoris.pdf)

### Institutional creations and civic manifestations

- **1929:** *Cátedra de Urbanismo de la Universidad Nacional del Litoral, sede Rosario*. Teaching institution established and initially led by Della Paolera, with Ángel Guido; an institutional creation rather than a text. [Almandoz and Ibarra, eds., pp. 167–168](https://estudiosurbanos.uc.cl/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/9789560106148-Almandoz-e-Ibarra-2018-Vi%CC%81speras-del-urbanismo.pdf)
- **1932:** *Oficina del Plan de Urbanización y Extensión de Buenos Aires*. Municipal planning body proposed, created, and directed by Della Paolera. Its drastic budgetary and administrative reduction in 1933 prevented it from becoming the autonomous planning apparatus originally projected. [Novick and Piccioni, pp. 12–13](https://studylib.es/doc/4782531/carlos-mar%C3%ADa-della-paolera--buenos-aires)
- **1933:** *Cátedra de Urbanismo de la Universidad de Buenos Aires*. A pedagogical and disciplinary institution promoted and led by Della Paolera; its made object was a curriculum and professional field, not merely the inaugural lecture. [Novick and Piccioni](https://studylib.es/doc/4782531/carlos-mar%C3%ADa-della-paolera--buenos-aires)
- **1934:** *Símbolo del Urbanismo*. Graphic symbol designed by Della Paolera. It later became the emblem of World Town Planning Day, but the symbol and the commemorative day are distinct creations. [*Jour mondial de l’urbanisme*, 1961](https://editionsanthonykrafft.com/media/filer_public/c0/49/c0492261-4a0b-401d-8134-501ed39a6c65/jour-mondial-de-lurbanisme-1961-arts-plastiques-amsterdam-amersfoort-alberto-sartoris.pdf)
- **1946–1948:** *Instituto Superior de Urbanismo de Buenos Aires*. Postgraduate teaching and research institution founded and directed by Della Paolera. Sources variously date its foundation to 1946 and the postgraduate program to 1948; these may denote successive institutional stages rather than a simple contradiction. [*Jour mondial de l’urbanisme*, 1961](https://editionsanthonykrafft.com/media/filer_public/c0/49/c0492261-4a0b-401d-8134-501ed39a6c65/jour-mondial-de-lurbanisme-1961-arts-plastiques-amsterdam-amersfoort-alberto-sartoris.pdf)
- **1949:** *Día Mundial del Urbanismo*. International civic manifestation initiated by Della Paolera and first observed on 8 November 1949. It is not a text or object but a recurring protocol of public attention and professional advocacy. [*Jour mondial de l’urbanisme*, 1961](https://editionsanthonykrafft.com/media/filer_public/c0/49/c0492261-4a0b-401d-8134-501ed39a6c65/jour-mondial-de-lurbanisme-1961-arts-plastiques-amsterdam-amersfoort-alberto-sartoris.pdf)

### Lectures, lecture-publications, and pedagogical texts

- **1928:** *Urbanismo: Rosario y sus problemas urbanos*. Two public lectures subsequently issued together as a pamphlet. The scholarly bibliography labels the item 1928 but prints “julio de 1920” for the lecture, while the surviving publication identifies 1928; the internal date is therefore a bibliographic error or unresolved inconsistency. [Novick and Piccioni, bibliography](https://studylib.es/doc/4782531/carlos-mar%C3%ADa-della-paolera--buenos-aires); [surviving publication record](https://www.scribd.com/document/691693027/DELLA-PAOLERA-C-Dos-Conferencias-Sobre-Urbanismo-Rosario-y-Sus-Problemas-Urbanos)

Lecture series delivered at the Facultad de Ciencias Exactas, Físicas y Naturales between May and September; a spoken pedagogical sequence, not securely documented as a standalone book. [Novick and Piccioni, bibliography](https://studylib.es/doc/4782531/carlos-mar%C3%ADa-della-paolera--buenos-aires)
- **1929:** *Urbanismo y problemas urbanos de Buenos Aires*. Lecture delivered at the Instituto Popular de Conferencias on 13 September and published in *La Ingeniería*, no. 660, in October. [Novick and Piccioni, bibliography](https://studylib.es/doc/4782531/carlos-mar%C3%ADa-della-paolera--buenos-aires)

### Posthumous collected manifestation

- **1977:** *Buenos Aires y sus problemas urbanos*. Posthumous anthology of Della Paolera’s articles, a 1933 lecture, and a 1936 pamphlet, selected and organized by Patricio Randle and OIKOS. It is a new editorial manifestation of earlier works—not a book made by Della Paolera as such—and therefore is not counted as an additional lifetime obra. [GCBA bibliographic record](https://www.estadisticaciudad.gob.ar/pergamo/documento.php?id=PERGAMO.1.1293&recno=1293&ui=1); [study of the anthology](https://www.ungs.edu.ar/wp-content/uploads/pdfs_ediciones/La_muralla_verde-completo.pdf)
