## Works by Johann Georg Faust

### Astrological works

- **1520:** *Nativity or judgment for Prince-Bishop Georg III Schenk von Limpurg*. A commissioned horoscope—probably a written natal chart with prognostic interpretation—for which the Bamberg episcopal accounts record paying “Doctor Faustus” ten gulden on 12 February 1520. The work itself is not known to survive, but the contemporary payment record makes this Faust’s only securely documented production. ([Henning, *Faust-Variationen*, pp. 12–13](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9783111561516_A24799614/preview-9783111561516_A24799614.pdf))

- **before 7 October 1534:** *Horoscope for Petrus Seuter* [no fixed title]. A combined astrological, physiognomic, and chiromantic judgment attributed in the Seuter–Ellenbog correspondence to “Master Georg Helmstetter”; Nicolaus Ellenbog returned the document because he could not interpret it and criticized its technical omissions. Frank Baron identifies its maker with the historical Faust, but that identification depends on accepting Georg Helmstetter and Faust as the same person; the horoscope’s present whereabouts are unknown. ([Baron, “Who Was the Historical Faustus?”, pp. 297–302](https://kuscholarworks.ku.edu/bitstreams/1d03288b-3474-49e6-9f09-f25089789b09/download); [secondary discussion](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/89v0434g))

### Performative creations reported but not recoverable as works

- **1506:** *Magical displays and horoscopes at Gelnhausen* [no individual titles]. A contemporary municipal account records payment connected with Faust’s “magic” and horoscope-making, but does not identify stable textual objects; these are better classified as performances or services than as recoverable works. Their contents, number, and physical form are unknown. ([Mahal’s documentary corpus summarized in the *Deutsche Biographie*](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd118683136.html))

- **1528:** *Astrological pronouncement at Rebdorf* [no fixed title]. Prior Kilian Leib’s weather diary records Faust orally asserting an astrological rule concerning the conjunction of the Sun and Jupiter and the birth of prophets. This is a witnessed utterance rather than a surviving authored text, so it resists classification as an obra. ([Henning, *Faust-Variationen*](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9783111561516_A24799614/preview-9783111561516_A24799614.pdf); [documentary summary](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd118683136.html))

### Works claimed by Faust but never made or not preserved

- **reported 1507:** *Restoration of the works of Plato and Aristotle* [project or boast, not a surviving work]. Johannes Trithemius reports Faust claiming that, were the philosophers’ writings lost, he could recreate them more elegantly; there is no evidence that he attempted or completed such a reconstruction. It belongs to Faust’s self-fashioned project rather than to his demonstrable oeuvre. ([Trithemius testimony summarized in the 1911 *Encyclopædia Britannica*](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/1911_Encyclop%C3%A6dia_Britannica/Faust))

### Pseudepigraphic magical writings

- **late sixteenth century onward; falsely dated 1405–1540:** *The Faustian grimoires* [collective attribution]. The *Höllenzwang*, *Praxis Magia Faustiana*, *Geister-Commando*, *Schwarzer Rabe*, *Miracul-Kunst- und Wunder-Buch*, and related books are protocols for conjuration published or copied under Faust’s name. Bibliographers catalogue many of them as “Faust writings,” but their anachronistic dates, predominantly late-sixteenth- to eighteenth-century witnesses, and use of “Johannes” rather than the historical figure’s contemporary name “Georg” prevent their treatment as things he made; they are posthumous pseudepigrapha and manifestations of the Faust legend. ([Engel, *Zusammenstellung der Faust-Schriften*, especially “Magische Schriften”](https://books.google.com/books?id=BJ0WAAAAMAAJ); [Ruickbie, *Faustus: The Life and Times of a Renaissance Magician*](https://books.google.com/books?id=9Uc7AwAAQBAJ); [bibliographical overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johann_Georg_Faust#Ascribed_works))

### No securely attributable books

- **c. 1480–1541:** *No extant authored book, treatise, letter, or manuscript*. The documentary corpus for the historical Faust consists principally of records and testimony written **about** him; apart from the two recorded horoscopes above, scholarship has not established a surviving text as his work. The 1587 *Historia von D. Johann Fausten* is an anonymous posthumous book about the legendary Faust, not a work made by Johann Georg Faust. ([Mahal, *Faust: Untersuchungen zu einem zeitlosen Thema*](https://books.google.com/books?id=ZvggAQAAIAAJ); [Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz15605.html))
