## Works by Captain Alfred Dreyfus

### Private correspondence

- **1889–1890:** *Lettres à Lucie*. Courtship correspondence addressed to Lucie Hadamard. This is an archival body of letters rather than a work conceived as a unified book; the editors of the standard collected edition treat it as the prologue to Dreyfus’s writings. ([Les Belles Lettres](https://www.lesbelleslettres.com/livre/9782251456317/oeuvres-completes-1894-1936))
- **1894–1899:** *Correspondance avec Lucie Dreyfus*. Letters written during Dreyfus’s imprisonment and deportation, serving simultaneously as intimate correspondence, testimony, and resistance to isolation. Their later arrangements—including *Lettres d’un innocent* and *Écris-moi souvent, écris-moi longuement*—are editorial manifestations of this larger jointly constituted exchange, not additional works by Alfred alone. ([Les Belles Lettres](https://www.lesbelleslettres.com/livre/9782251456317/oeuvres-completes-1894-1936); [Bibliographie de l’histoire de France](https://biblio-bhf.fr/person/ext-000002437))
- **1911–1919:** *Lettres à la marquise Arconati-Visconti*. A correspondence extending from debates surrounding Jean Jaurès’s *L’Armée nouvelle* through the First World War; the wartime letters form a chronological subdivision, not a separate project. ([Les Belles Lettres](https://www.lesbelleslettres.com/livre/9782251456317/oeuvres-completes-1894-1936))
- **1914–1918:** *Lettres de la guerre à Pierre Dreyfus*. Wartime correspondence to his son, recording military service and family relations. It resists classification as a deliberately composed work: its unity is archival and editorial. ([Les Belles Lettres](https://www.lesbelleslettres.com/livre/9782251456317/oeuvres-completes-1894-1936))

### Prison notebooks, journals, and memoirs

- **1894:** *Notes et correspondance pendant l’instruction et le procès de 1894*. A dossier of working notes, defensive arguments, and letters produced while Dreyfus was detained and tried. It is an archival corpus rather than a single titled obra; its collective title comes from the editors of the complete works. ([Les Belles Lettres](https://www.lesbelleslettres.com/livre/9782251456317/oeuvres-completes-1894-1936); [Fondation pour la Mémoire de la Shoah](https://www.fondationshoah.org/recherche/oeuvres-completes-1894-1936-alfred-dreyfus))
- **1898–1899:** *Cahiers de l’île du Diable*. Prison notebooks in which Dreyfus recorded events, examined the case against him, and challenged Alphonse Bertillon’s handwriting theories. These are working and testimonial documents, not a book originally prepared for publication. ([Les Belles Lettres](https://www.lesbelleslettres.com/livre/9782251456317/oeuvres-completes-1894-1936); [Fondation pour la Mémoire de la Shoah](https://www.fondationshoah.org/recherche/oeuvres-completes-1894-1936-alfred-dreyfus))
- **1894–1899; published 1901:** *Cinq années de ma vie*. A retrospective prison memoir assembled after Dreyfus’s return, drawing upon his journal and correspondence. It is both an authored memoir and a composite documentary obra, so its component letters and notebooks should not be counted again as independent books merely because later editions reproduce them. ([Les Belles Lettres](https://www.lesbelleslettres.com/livre/9782251456317/oeuvres-completes-1894-1936); [La Découverte](https://www.editionsladecouverte.fr/cinq_annees_de_ma_vie-9782707148063))
- **date uncertain; published posthumously in 1936:** *Souvenirs*. Autobiographical recollections published by Dreyfus’s son Pierre as part of *Souvenirs et correspondance*. The posthumous volume is an editorial construction, and its title and arrangement should not be mistaken for a book whose final published form Alfred Dreyfus authorized. ([Les Belles Lettres](https://www.lesbelleslettres.com/livre/9782251456317/oeuvres-completes-1894-1936); [Wikisource edition of the 1936 volume](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Souvenirs_et_Correspondance_%28Dreyfus%29/Texte_entier))
- **9 September 1899–1935:** *Après le procès de Rennes*. A long-running series of notebooks and notes begun after the Rennes verdict and continued, with interruptions, through Dreyfus’s later life. The portion through 4 June 1908 appeared critically as *Carnets 1899–1907*, but the complete edition reveals a larger, discontinuous project rather than one consistently finished work. ([Les Belles Lettres](https://www.lesbelleslettres.com/livre/9782251456317/oeuvres-completes-1894-1936))
- **1917–1918:** *La Guerre*. A wartime carnet recording Dreyfus’s military experience and observations. It is a manuscript notebook rather than a publication conceived as a standalone book. ([Les Belles Lettres](https://www.lesbelleslettres.com/livre/9782251456317/oeuvres-completes-1894-1936))
- **1930–1931:** *Notes sur le « réveil » de l’affaire Dreyfus*. Previously unpublished notes responding to the renewed public controversy over the Affair. This is an editorially named group of papers, not a securely titled or completed work. ([Les Belles Lettres](https://www.lesbelleslettres.com/livre/9782251456317/oeuvres-completes-1894-1936))

### Manifestos of innocence and judicial advocacy

- **1894–1899; first collected 1898:** *Lettres d’un innocent*. A public selection from Dreyfus’s prison letters, issued while he remained confined. As a manifesto of innocence it acts upon the public controversy, but bibliographically it is a manifestation assembled from correspondence rather than an entirely separate text newly composed as a book. ([Les Belles Lettres](https://www.lesbelleslettres.com/livre/9782251456317/oeuvres-completes-1894-1936))
- **30 January 1904:** *Mémoire devant la Cour de cassation (Chambre criminelle) de M. Alfred Dreyfus*. A formal judicial mémoire supporting the renewed revision of his conviction. It is an advocacy document—part legal argument, part evidentiary reconstruction—whose intended action was the reopening and correction of the case. ([Les Belles Lettres](https://www.lesbelleslettres.com/livre/9782251456317/oeuvres-completes-1894-1936))

### Public and military writings

- **1903–1914:** *Articles de La Revue historique*. A body of separately published historical and military articles. The collected edition groups them as a corpus; they resist treatment as one work, but available descriptions of the edition do not supply a dependable item-level title list, so individual titles should not be invented. ([Les Belles Lettres](https://www.lesbelleslettres.com/livre/9782251456317/oeuvres-completes-1894-1936); [Fondation pour la Mémoire de la Shoah](https://www.fondationshoah.org/recherche/oeuvres-completes-1894-1936-alfred-dreyfus))
- **1908–1912:** *Réponse à L’Action française*. A series of public replies to attacks by the nationalist newspaper and movement. These polemical interventions function as corrective manifestos rather than as a single conventionally composed book; the collective title is editorial. ([Les Belles Lettres](https://www.lesbelleslettres.com/livre/9782251456317/oeuvres-completes-1894-1936))
- **1894–1935:** *Miscellaneous public letters, press replies, and military writings*. Individually occasioned texts addressing accusations, political controversy, military questions, and public memory. The complete edition asserts that it gathers these writings—including replies to the far-right press and military articles—but they do not constitute a single authorial obra and should remain identified as a heterogeneous corpus unless each archival or periodical item is separately described. ([Les Belles Lettres](https://www.lesbelleslettres.com/livre/9782251456317/oeuvres-completes-1894-1936); [Fondation pour la Mémoire de la Shoah](https://www.fondationshoah.org/recherche/oeuvres-completes-1894-1936-alfred-dreyfus))

### Posthumous editorial manifestations—not additional works

- **1936:** *Souvenirs et correspondance*. A posthumous edition established by Pierre Dreyfus from his father’s recollections and letters. It is a manifestation of earlier materials, partly shaped by the son’s selection and arrangement, rather than a new work completed by Alfred Dreyfus in 1936. ([Wikisource edition of the 1936 volume](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Souvenirs_et_Correspondance_%28Dreyfus%29/Texte_entier); [Les Belles Lettres](https://www.lesbelleslettres.com/livre/9782251456317/oeuvres-completes-1894-1936))
- **1998:** *Après le procès de Rennes: Carnets 1899–1907*. Philippe Oriol’s critical edition of a portion of Dreyfus’s post-Rennes notebooks. This is an important scholarly manifestation, not a work newly attributable to Dreyfus in 1998; even its terminal year differs from the complete edition’s first section, which runs to 4 June 1908. ([Les Belles Lettres](https://www.lesbelleslettres.com/livre/9782251456317/oeuvres-completes-1894-1936))
- **2005:** *Écris-moi souvent, écris-moi longuement: correspondance de l’île du Diable, 1894–1899*. Vincent Duclert’s edition of the correspondence exchanged by Alfred and Lucie Dreyfus. It is jointly authored correspondence and a modern editorial manifestation, not an additional solo work by Alfred. ([Bibliographie de l’histoire de France](https://biblio-bhf.fr/person/ext-000002437))
- **2011:** *Alfred Dreyfus, officier en 14–18: souvenirs, lettres et carnets de guerre*. Georges Joumas’s documentary compilation of memoirs, correspondence, and wartime notebooks. Its contents are Dreyfus materials, but the volume as a whole is a later editor’s construction rather than a book made by Dreyfus. ([Les Belles Lettres table of contents and publication history](https://www.lesbelleslettres.com/livre/9782251456317/oeuvres-completes-1894-1936))
- **2017:** *Lettres à la marquise*. Philippe Oriol’s edition of the correspondence with Marie Arconati-Visconti. It manifests an archival exchange whose wartime portion is separately organized in the complete works; it is not a distinct book composed by Dreyfus. ([Les Belles Lettres](https://www.lesbelleslettres.com/livre/9782251456317/oeuvres-completes-1894-1936))
- **2024:** *Œuvres complètes (1894–1936)*. The standard collected-works edition, established by Vincent Duclert and Philippe Oriol, gathering private letters, notebooks, memoirs, judicial arguments, press replies, and military articles. It is the principal basis for this inventory, but its organization—and the paradoxical terminal date 1936, after Dreyfus’s death in 1935—includes Pierre Dreyfus’s posthumous framing materials and therefore is not itself an obra made by Alfred Dreyfus. ([Les Belles Lettres](https://www.lesbelleslettres.com/livre/9782251456317/oeuvres-completes-1894-1936); [Fondation pour la Mémoire de la Shoah](https://www.fondationshoah.org/recherche/oeuvres-completes-1894-1936-alfred-dreyfus))
