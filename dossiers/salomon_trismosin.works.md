## Works by Salomon Trismosin

### Pseudonymous corpus and compilation project

- **1598–1599:** *Aureum vellus, oder Guldin Schatz und Kunstkammer*. A three-part alchemical compilation presenting “Salomon Trismosin” as its arranger and German translator rather than as author of every constituent text; later continuations expanded it to five parts. Modern scholarship treats Trismosin as probably fictitious, so this is best classified as a pseudonymous editorial project, not securely as one historical person’s book. ([BnF authority record](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb16844240z.public); [1598–99 edition description](https://www.christies.com/en/lot/lot-1932450); [Wels, 2025, pp. 448–450](https://brill.com/view/journals/daph/53/2-3/article-p430_7.pdf))

### Alchemical journey narrative

- **1598 (narrative claims events beginning in 1473):** *Tractat vnnd Wanderschafft des hochberhümpten Herren Salomonis Trißmosini, sampt dreyen gewaltigen Tincturen* (“Wanderschafft Salomonis Trißmosini” in the contents). A first-person alchemical quest narrative followed by three tincture recipes: simultaneously a purported autobiography, an adept legend, and a protocol. Its autobiographical claims are unsupported, and scholarship describes the underlying person/text complex as an insoluble *ficta/facta* problem. ([Wels, 2025, pp. 448–450](https://brill.com/view/journals/daph/53/2-3/article-p430_7.pdf); [1598 contents](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/aureum-vellus-oder-guldin-schatz-und-kunstkammer-darinnen-trismosin/page/69bd9d7b6120d54bd0376fb4))

### Alchemical and medical protocols attributed in the *Aureum vellus*

- **1598:** *Copulatur*. An alchemical protocol placed immediately after the *Wanderschafft* and counted in the older bibliography among the first part’s Trismosinian writings; its heading itself names no author, so the attribution remains editorial and insecure. ([1598 contents](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/aureum-vellus-oder-guldin-schatz-und-kunstkammer-darinnen-trismosin/page/69bd9d7b6120d54bd0376fb4); [Lepsius, “Trismosin, Salomon”](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/ADB:Trismosin,_Salomon))

- **1598:** *Praefatio to Xopharis Tinctura*. A prefatory framing text attributed to Trismosin, attached to a tincture protocol itself assigned to Crinotus and the Egyptian king Xophar; the preface, not the recipe proper, is the Trismosinian object. ([1598 contents](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/aureum-vellus-oder-guldin-schatz-und-kunstkammer-darinnen-trismosin/page/69bd9d7b6120d54bd0376fb4))

- **1598:** *Liber Suforethon Salomonis Trißmosini, das lange Leben deß Menschen zu erhalten*. An alchemical-medical protocol promising preservation and rejuvenation of the human body. Its extravagant first-person claims help manufacture the undying-adept persona and therefore resist separation from the Trismosin fiction. ([1598 contents](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/aureum-vellus-oder-guldin-schatz-und-kunstkammer-darinnen-trismosin/page/69bd9d7b6120d54bd0376fb4); [Wels, 2025, pp. 449–450](https://brill.com/view/journals/daph/53/2-3/article-p430_7.pdf))

- **1598:** *Liber Cangeniueron Salomonis Trißmosini, mit neun Tincturen*. A collection of nine operative tincture protocols attributed to Trismosin. The invented-looking title belongs to the pseudonymous vocabulary that led historians to doubt Trismosin’s existence. ([1598 contents](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/aureum-vellus-oder-guldin-schatz-und-kunstkammer-darinnen-trismosin/page/69bd9d7b6120d54bd0376fb4); [Lepsius](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/ADB:Trismosin,_Salomon))

- **1598:** *Der rote Adler Salomonis Trißmosini*. A compound alchemical protocol comprising *Deß Felchs Schwaden* and *Electrum Soronella*. It is explicitly assigned to Trismosin by the printed contents but survives only within the pseudonymous compilation. ([1598 contents](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/avrevm-vellvs-oder-guldin-schatz-vnd-kunstkammer-darinnen-solis/page/69b51cf647b06ecd58182840))

- **1598:** *Philosophi Pitrumosinis Tinctur, durch H. Trißmosin beschrieben*. A tincture protocol purportedly belonging to “Pitrumosin” but written down or described by Trismosin; it is therefore a claimed act of transmission rather than straightforward authorship. ([1598 contents](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/aureum-vellus-splendor-solis-golden-fleece-trismosin/page/69b52bf7a2d71b015aa975b0))

- **1598:** *Tinctura Geroton Salomonis Trißmosini*. An operative tincture protocol explicitly attributed to Trismosin in the compilation’s contents; neither an independent date nor secure authorship is established. ([1598 contents](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/aureum-vellus-splendor-solis-golden-fleece-trismosin/page/69b52bf7a2d71b015aa975b0))

- **1598:** *Parasethon Trißmosini*. A short alchemical protocol bearing an invented or otherwise unidentified title and an editorial attribution to Trismosin. ([1598 contents](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/avrevm-vellvs-oder-guldin-schatz-vnd-kunstkammer-darinnen-solis/page/69b51cf647b06ecd58182841))

- **1598:** *Sarona Doap auri, Trißmosini*. An alchemical recipe or protocol concerning gold, attributed to Trismosin; the title has no securely established historical language or source outside this textual complex. ([1598 contents](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/avrevm-vellvs-oder-guldin-schatz-vnd-kunstkammer-darinnen-solis/page/69b51cf647b06ecd58182841))

- **1598:** *Augmentatio solis Trißmosini mit eim Amalgama*. A protocol for augmenting “Sol,” the alchemical name for gold, through an amalgam. It is an instructional procedure, not a narrative treatise. ([1598 contents](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/avrevm-vellvs-oder-guldin-schatz-vnd-kunstkammer-darinnen-solis/page/69b51cf647b06ecd58182841))

- **1598:** *Biatolon Trißmosini, darinn etliche particular Stück*. A collection of several “particular” alchemical operations attributed to Trismosin, rather than a single continuous argument. ([1598 contents](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/avrevm-vellvs-oder-guldin-schatz-vnd-kunstkammer-darinnen-solis/page/69b51cf647b06ecd58182841))

- **1598:** *Tractat Trißmosini von allerhand schwefelischen Kiß, Feuer- und Flintensteinen*. A ten-part practical treatise on sulphurous ores, firestones, and flints and their use in alchemical work. Its form is chiefly that of a mineralogical and laboratory protocol. ([1598 contents](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/avrevm-vellvs-oder-guldin-schatz-vnd-kunstkammer-darinnen-solis/page/69b51cf647b06ecd58182841))

- **1598:** *Sein Werck auß dem Hasenthurn von Fixation des Vsifur*. An alchemical protocol attributed to Trismosin concerning the fixation of “Usifur,” apparently cinnabar or a mercurial sulphide; the obscure heading should not be normalized into a more certain title than the source supplies. ([1598 contents](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/avrevm-vellvs-oder-guldin-schatz-vnd-kunstkammer-darinnen-solis/page/69b51cf647b06ecd58182841))

- **1598:** *Pars cum parte mit dem grossen Feuer Trißmosini*. A high-heat laboratory protocol, explicitly assigned to Trismosin in the printed contents. Its Latin-German heading functions more as a recipe label than as a stable literary title. ([1598 contents](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/avrevm-vellvs-oder-guldin-schatz-vnd-kunstkammer-darinnen-solis/page/69b51cf647b06ecd58182841))

### Illustrated alchemical Gesamtkunstwerk of disputed attribution

- **1531–1532 (earliest manuscript); 1598–1599 (first print):** *Splendor Solis oder Sonnen Glantz*. A seven-part text-image alchemical cycle whose twenty-two illuminations or woodcuts are integral to its operation, making it closer to a Gesamtkunstwerk or illustrated contemplative protocol than to a text alone. The earliest manuscript does not name Trismosin, and the German first printing does not claim that he authored it; the familiar attribution arose later and is rejected or treated as pseudonymous by modern scholarship. ([Katalog der deutschsprachigen illustrierten Handschriften des Mittelalters](https://kdih.badw.de/datenbank/untergruppe/2/3); [Solothurn manuscript description](https://www.e-codices.unifr.ch/de/description/zbs/SI-0185); [Skinner et al., *Splendor Solis*](https://rexresearch1.com/AlchemyArchives/SkinnerSplendorSolis.pdf))

### Epistolary manifestation

- **1515 (claimed); 1590s (earliest surviving copy):** *Letter from Salomon Trismosin to Paracelsus*. A letter presenting Trismosin as Paracelsus’s instructor and transmitting alchemical matter; the scholarly collected edition records it from a copy made only in the 1590s, so its date, authorship, and even status as a genuine letter are disputed. It is best treated as a pseudonymous epistolary manifestation of the Trismosin legend. ([Kühlmann and Telle, *Corpus Paracelsisticum*, vol. 3, no. 102, discussed by Wels](https://brill.com/view/journals/daph/53/2-3/article-p430_7.pdf); [*Corpus Paracelsisticum* bibliographic record](https://theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Indexes))
