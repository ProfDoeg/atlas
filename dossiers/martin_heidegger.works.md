## Works by Martin Heidegger

### Published writings and author-formed collections

- **1910–1916:** *Frühe Schriften* (GA 1). Early reviews, articles, dissertation, and habilitation writings; this is a retrospective editorial collection, not one work conceived as a book. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)
- **1927:** *Sein und Zeit* (GA 2). Philosophical treatise published as the first part of a projected work; the announced continuation never appeared, so its incompletion belongs to the obra’s form. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1929:** *Kant und das Problem der Metaphysik* (GA 3). Monograph developing Heidegger’s disputed, deliberately appropriative interpretation of Kant; later editions incorporated supplements and notes rather than constituting separate works. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1936–1968:** *Erläuterungen zu Hölderlins Dichtung* (GA 4). Author-formed and subsequently enlarged collection of elucidations, talks, and essays on Hölderlin rather than a single continuous treatise. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)
- **1935–1946:** *Holzwege* (GA 5). Author-formed essay collection, including *Der Ursprung des Kunstwerkes*, whose texts had distinct occasions and revision histories. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1936–1939:** *Nietzsche I* (GA 6.1). Retrospectively revised construction from lecture courses and shorter writings; it overlaps GA 43, 44, 47, and 48 and therefore is not simply another witness to the same texts. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1939–1946:** *Nietzsche II* (GA 6.2). Second half of the author-reworked Nietzsche corpus, combining revised lectures with independent essays. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1936–1954:** *Vorträge und Aufsätze* (GA 7). Author-formed collection of lectures and essays, including *Die Frage nach der Technik*, *Bauen Wohnen Denken*, *Das Ding*, *Logos*, *Moira*, and *Aletheia*; its components remain distinct works despite their collected form. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1951–1952:** *Was heißt Denken?* (GA 8). University lecture course subsequently made into a book by Heidegger; it is simultaneously performed teaching and authored publication. [Klostermann, GA structure](https://www.klostermann.de/epages/63574303.sf/de_DE/?ObjectPath=/Shops/63574303/Categories/Buecher/Philosophie/%22Martin+Heidegger%22/Gliederung_der_HGA)
- **1919–1961:** *Wegmarken* (GA 9). Author-formed collection of essays and lectures, including *Was ist Metaphysik?*, *Vom Wesen des Grundes*, *Vom Wesen der Wahrheit*, and the *Brief über den Humanismus*. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)
- **1955–1956:** *Der Satz vom Grund* (GA 10). Lecture course and related public address recast as an authored book. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)
- **1955–1957:** *Identität und Differenz* (GA 11). Small author-planned constellation of addresses rather than a continuous monograph. [Klostermann, GA structure](https://www.klostermann.de/epages/63574303.sf/de_DE/?ObjectPath=/Shops/63574303/Categories/Buecher/Philosophie/%22Martin+Heidegger%22/Gliederung_der_HGA)
- **1950–1959:** *Unterwegs zur Sprache* (GA 12). Author-formed collection of talks and dialogical texts about language; its forms range from lecture to staged conversation. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)
- **1910–1976:** *Aus der Erfahrung des Denkens* (GA 13). Author-planned miscellany of short prose, commemorations, travel texts, inscriptions, and thought-poems; it resists classification as either treatise or literary collection. [Klostermann, title register](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Register-HGA)
- **1962–1964:** *Zur Sache des Denkens* (GA 14). Collection centered on *Zeit und Sein*, its seminar protocol, and *Das Ende der Philosophie und die Aufgabe des Denkens*; the protocol records a collaborative event and is not straightforwardly Heidegger’s sole-authored text. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)
- **1951–1973:** *Seminare* (GA 15). Records of seminars including Le Thor and Zähringen; these are pedagogical events preserved through notes and protocols, so their status as authored works is mediated and sometimes collective. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)
- **1910–1976:** *Reden und andere Zeugnisse eines Lebensweges* (GA 16). Editorial corpus of speeches, official statements, interviews, dedications, and documentary utterances; some items act institutionally or politically and resemble manifestos or performative declarations more than philosophical works. [Klostermann, GA structure](https://www.klostermann.de/epages/63574303.sf/de_DE/?ObjectPath=/Shops/63574303/Categories/Buecher/Philosophie/%22Martin+Heidegger%22/Gliederung_der_HGA)

### Marburg lecture courses

- **1923–1924:** *Einführung in die phänomenologische Forschung* (GA 17). Winter-semester lecture course reconstructed from Heidegger’s teaching manuscript. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)
- **1924:** *Grundbegriffe der aristotelischen Philosophie* (GA 18). Summer-semester lecture course; a performed course whose posthumous book form is editorial. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)
- **1924–1925:** *Platon: Sophistes* (GA 19). Winter-semester lecture course, substantially concerned with Aristotle as preparation for Plato. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)
- **1925:** *Prolegomena zur Geschichte des Zeitbegriffs* (GA 20). Summer-semester course and an important workshop-stage antecedent to *Sein und Zeit*, not merely an early edition of that book. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)
- **1925–1926:** *Logik: Die Frage nach der Wahrheit* (GA 21). Winter-semester lecture course. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)
- **1926:** *Die Grundbegriffe der antiken Philosophie* (GA 22). Summer-semester survey course preserved as lecture manuscript. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)
- **1926–1927:** *Geschichte der Philosophie von Thomas von Aquin bis Kant* (GA 23). Winter-semester lecture course. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)
- **1927:** *Die Grundprobleme der Phänomenologie* (GA 24). Summer-semester course conceived in relation to the unrealized continuation of *Sein und Zeit* but not identical with that projected second part. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)
- **1927–1928:** *Phänomenologische Interpretation von Kants Kritik der reinen Vernunft* (GA 25). Winter-semester lecture course and workshop for Heidegger’s Kant book. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)
- **1928:** *Metaphysische Anfangsgründe der Logik im Ausgang von Leibniz* (GA 26). Summer-semester lecture course. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)

- **1935–1936:** *Die Frage nach dem Ding: Zu Kants Lehre von den transzendentalen Grundsätzen* (GA 41). Winter-semester lecture course, later published in revised form. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)

- **1936–1937:** *Nietzsche: Der Wille zur Macht als Kunst* (GA 43). Winter-semester lecture course, later revised into the author-published *Nietzsche* corpus. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)

- **1937–1938:** *Grundfragen der Philosophie: Ausgewählte “Probleme” der “Logik”* (GA 45). Winter-semester lecture course. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)
- **1938–1939:** *Zur Auslegung von Nietzsches II. Unzeitgemäßer Betrachtung* (GA 46). - **1939:** *Nietzsches Lehre vom Willen zur Macht als Erkenntnis* (GA 47). Summer-semester lecture course, later revised for *Nietzsche*. [Klostermann catalogue](https://download.klostermann.de/Kataloge/web_HGA_2021.pdf)

- **1941:** *Die Metaphysik des deutschen Idealismus* (GA 49). First-trimester lecture and summer seminar on Schelling combined editorially. [Klostermann catalogue](https://download.klostermann.de/Kataloge/web_HGA_2021.pdf)
- **1941–1942:** *Nietzsches Metaphysik* (GA 50). ### Unpublished treatises, dialogues, and sustained projects

- **1924:** *Der Begriff der Zeit* (GA 64). Treatise composed from the same inquiry as Heidegger’s 1924 lecture of that title; the volume preserves related but non-identical manifestations. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)
- **1936–1938:** *Beiträge zur Philosophie (Vom Ereignis)* (GA 65). Unpublished “treatise” organized as fugally related joinings rather than a conventional book; it was deliberately withheld during Heidegger’s lifetime. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1938–1939:** *Besinnung* (GA 66). Unpublished sustained manuscript belonging to the “history of beyng” project. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1938–1939:** *Die Überwindung der Metaphysik* (GA 67). Unpublished manuscript later editorially joined with *Das Wesen des Nihilismus*. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1946–1948:** *Das Wesen des Nihilismus* (GA 67). Separate manuscript project sharing the editorial volume *Metaphysik und Nihilismus*. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1938–1939:** *Die Negativität* (GA 68). Notes and sustained interpretation of Hegel, editorially paired with a later manuscript. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1942:** *Erläuterung der “Einleitung” zu Hegels “Phänomenologie des Geistes”* (GA 68). Unpublished interpretive manuscript, distinct from *Die Negativität*. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1938–1940:** *Die Geschichte des Seyns* (GA 69). Two related unpublished manuscripts, *Die Geschichte des Seyns* and *Koinon*, made into one editorial volume. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1941:** *Über den Anfang* (GA 70). Unpublished sustained manuscript in the history-of-beyng sequence. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1941–1942:** *Das Ereignis* (GA 71). Unpublished sustained manuscript; despite the title, it is distinct from both *Beiträge zur Philosophie (Vom Ereignis)* and the later miscellany *Zum Ereignis-Denken*. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1944:** *Die Stege des Anfangs* (GA 72). Unpublished manuscript announced in the complete-edition plan but not yet issued there; until publication, its completeness and final textual constitution remain unresolved. [Klostermann catalogue](https://download.klostermann.de/Kataloge/web_HGA_2021.pdf)
- **1932–1970s:** *Zum Ereignis-Denken* (GA 73.1–73.2). Vast posthumous editorial assemblage of notes and drafts, not a single author-completed work. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1935–1960:** *Zum Wesen der Sprache und Zur Frage nach der Kunst* (GA 74). Editorial collection of distinct manuscripts on language and art; its unity is archival rather than authorial. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1939–1970:** *Zu Hölderlin – Griechenlandreisen* (GA 75). Editorial corpus combining Hölderlin writings, wartime notes, dialogues, and Greek travel records; it is not one continuous work. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1962:** *Aufenthalte*. Journal of Heidegger’s journey to Greece, later incorporated into GA 75; a journey-record and reflective prose work rather than a treatise. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1935–1955:** *Leitgedanken zur Entstehung der Metaphysik, der neuzeitlichen Wissenschaft und der modernen Technik* (GA 76). Editorially constituted collection of twenty-four manuscripts, not an author-designed book. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1944–1945:** *Αγχιβασίη: Ein Gespräch selbdritt auf einem Feldweg zwischen einem Forscher, einem Gelehrten und einem Weisen* (GA 77). Philosophical dialogue whose staged voices make it neither ordinary essay nor transcript. [Klostermann catalogue](https://download.klostermann.de/Kataloge/web_HGA_2021.pdf)
- **1944–1945:** *Der Lehrer trifft den Türmer an der Tür zum Turmaufgang* (GA 77). Philosophical dialogue. [Klostermann catalogue](https://download.klostermann.de/Kataloge/web_HGA_2021.pdf)
- **1944–1945:** *Abendgespräch in einem Kriegsgefangenenlager in Rußland zwischen einem Jüngeren und einem Älteren* (GA 77). Philosophical dialogue set in a historical catastrophe but not a documentary conversation. [Klostermann catalogue](https://download.klostermann.de/Kataloge/web_HGA_2021.pdf)
- **Probably 1942:** *Der Spruch des Anaximander* (GA 78). Undated manuscript, probably written in summer or autumn 1942 for a lecture course that was never held; both date and intended manifestation remain qualified. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)

### Public lectures, addresses, and performative texts

- **1949:** *Einblick in das was ist* (GA 79). Bremen lecture cycle comprising *Das Ding*, *Das Ge-Stell*, *Die Gefahr*, and *Die Kehre*; it is one designed sequence whose components also acquired independent textual lives. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1957:** *Grundsätze des Denkens* (GA 79). Freiburg lecture series, editorially paired with the Bremen cycle. [Klostermann catalogue](https://download.klostermann.de/Kataloge/web_HGA_2021.pdf)
- **1915–1932:** *Vorträge, Teil 1* (GA 80.1). Editorial corpus of early addresses and lectures, including *Frage und Urteil*, *Über Psychologie*, the Dilthey lectures, *Phänomenologie und Theologie*, and several versions of *Vom Wesen der Wahrheit*; the volume is a container, not one work. [Klostermann catalogue](https://download.klostermann.de/Kataloge/web_HGA_2021.pdf)
- **1935–1967:** *Vorträge, Teil 2* (GA 80.2). Editorial corpus of later public talks, preserving first versions and variants that sometimes differ materially from Heidegger’s later publications. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)
- **Various dates:** *Gedachtes* (GA 81). Posthumous collection of thought-poems, aphoristic sequences, and reflective prose; it deliberately crosses philosophy and poetry without becoming a literal Gesamtkunstwerk. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)

### Seminar records, annotations, and workshop materials

- **Various dates:** *Zu eigenen Veröffentlichungen* (GA 82). Marginalia, annotations, and working notes on Heidegger’s own publications; these resist the status of autonomous works because they depend on other texts. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)
- **1920s–1930s:** *Seminare: Platon – Aristoteles – Augustinus* (GA 83). Editorial corpus of seminar preparations and records rather than one authored book. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)
- **Various dates:** *Seminare: Kant – Leibniz – Schiller*, Parts 1–2 (GA 84.1–84.2). Two-volume archival corpus of seminar materials; individual records differ in authorship and textual completeness. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)
- **1939:** *Seminar: Vom Wesen der Sprache* (GA 85). Seminar materials whose posthumous textual object records preparation and pedagogical activity rather than a finished treatise. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)
- **1927–1957:** *Seminare: Hegel – Schelling* (GA 86). Editorial collection of several seminars, including the 1934–1935 seminar on Hegel’s philosophy of right. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1937 and 1944:** *Nietzsche: Seminare* (GA 87). Records and preparations from two seminars rather than a continuous work. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1937–1938:** *Die metaphysischen Grundstellungen des abendländischen Denkens* (GA 88). Seminar exercise, editorially paired with a later course. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1941–1942:** *Einübung in das philosophische Denken* (GA 88). Seminar exercise and pedagogical protocol, not a conventional monograph. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1959–1969:** *Zollikoner Seminare* (GA 89). Heidegger’s notes and materials for seminars with physicians and psychiatrists; the earlier Boss edition includes overlapping but substantially different protocols, conversations, and letters, so no single edition exhausts the event. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1934–1954:** *Zu Ernst Jünger* (GA 90). Reading notes, treatises, and drafts responding to Jünger; an archival work-complex rather than one completed text. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **Various dates:** *Ergänzungen und Denksplitter* (GA 91). Planned posthumous container for supplements and thought-fragments; its heterogeneous materials resist classification as a single obra. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)

### Black notebooks

- **1931–1938:** *Überlegungen II–VI* (GA 94). Five private black notebooks made as serial thinking-writing rather than public treatises; *Überlegungen I* is missing from the archive, so the surviving sequence is incomplete. [Klostermann, GA structure](https://www.klostermann.de/epages/63574303.sf/de_DE/?ObjectPath=/Shops/63574303/Categories/Buecher/Philosophie/%22Martin+Heidegger%22/Gliederung_der_HGA)
- **1938–1939:** *Überlegungen VII–XI* (GA 95). Five black notebooks; their antisemitic and National Socialist passages are part of the textual record and cannot responsibly be detached from the corpus’s status. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1939–1941:** *Überlegungen XII–XV* (GA 96). Four black notebooks continuing the private serial project. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1942–1948:** *Anmerkungen I–V* (GA 97). Five black notebooks; neither diary in a simple biographical sense nor polished philosophical work. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1948–1951:** *Anmerkungen VI–IX* (GA 98). Four black notebooks continuing the serial note-project. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1947–1950:** *Vier Hefte I und II* (GA 99). Two surviving notebooks bearing a collective title that implies a larger configuration; the editorial title does not establish that four notebooks survive. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1952–1957:** *Vigiliae und Notturno* (GA 100). Black-notebook sequences combining philosophical, autobiographical, and aphoristic writing. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1957–1959:** *Winke I und II* (GA 101). Two black notebooks in the late sequence. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1963–1970:** *Vorläufiges I–IV* (GA 102). Four late black notebooks; the title marks them as provisional, so their resistance to being called finished works is explicit. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)

### Supplementary archival constellations

- **Various dates:** *Raum und Zeit – Stimmung und Grundstimmung – Über Marx* (GA 103). Supplementary volume grouping three otherwise distinct manuscript constellations; its unity is editorial. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)
- **Various dates:** *Gespräche – “Eigentum”: Der Weg zur Sprache – Vier Hefte* (GA 104). Supplementary assemblage of dialogues, language materials, and notebooks; it cannot securely be treated as one authored work. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)
- **Various dates:** *Vermächtnis der Seinsfrage* (GA 105). Supplementary late corpus presented as a legacy of the question of being; its exact internal units and dates must be taken from the edition rather than inferred from the collective title. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)

### Correspondence and collaborative textual objects

- **1910s–1976:** *Ausgewählte Briefe 1–2* (projected GA 92–93). Planned selected-letter volumes, not yet securely classifiable as completed GA works; letters are individually made communications, while the proposed selection is an editor’s future object. [Klostermann, GA plan](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Editionsplan)
- **Various dates:** *Martin Heidegger Briefausgabe*. A separately published, still projected multivolume correspondence edition covering scholars, family, friends, institutions, universities, and publishers; it documents many individual letters but is not itself a Heidegger-designed obra, and its planned extent should not be confused with completeness achieved. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1929:** *Davoser Disputation zwischen Ernst Cassirer und Martin Heidegger*. Recorded public disputation: a collaborative philosophical event preserved in reports and protocols, not a sole-authored Heidegger text. [Polt bibliography](https://heidegger-circle.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HeideggerbibliographyPoltNov2020.pdf)
- **1962:** *Protokoll zu einem Seminar über den Vortrag “Zeit und Sein”*. Seminar protocol produced from collective discussion and subsequently authorized for publication; its attribution to Heidegger is therefore mediated rather than simply authorial. [Klostermann, title register](https://www.klostermann.de/Buecher/Seite-/-Kategorie/Register-HGA)
