## Works by Emiliano Kargieman

### Ventures and institutional projects

- **Before 1996:** *Three unnamed technology ventures*. A corporate biography reports three technology endeavors preceding Core Security but supplies neither names nor dates; they therefore resist treatment as individually identifiable works. ([Officina Stellare board biography](https://www.officinastellare.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/Lista-1_Consiglio-di-Amministrazione.pdf))

- **1996–2015:** *Core Security Technologies*. A cybersecurity company co-founded by Kargieman and collaborators: an institutional obra rather than a solely authored object. Its principal technical creation was an automated penetration-testing platform, subsequently known as Core Impact. ([Satellogic biography](https://investors.satellogic.com/management/emiliano-kargieman); [Core Impact technical account](https://www.coresecurity.com/core-labs/publications/core-impact-penetration-test-automation))

- **2007–ca. 2010:** *Aconcagua Ventures*. A venture-capital project co-founded and directed by Kargieman to invest in Latin American high-technology startups and develop them as global businesses; its “work” was an investment-and-company-building practice, not a discrete text or product. ([IDB, *Impact of Early-Stage Equity Funds in Latin America*](https://publications.iadb.org/publications/english/document/Impact-of-Early-Stage-Equity-Funds-in-Latin-America.pdf); [Satellogic biography](https://investors.satellogic.com/management/emiliano-kargieman))

- **2009–:** *GarageLab*. A multidisciplinary problem-solving laboratory co-founded with Darío Wainer on 4 September 2009, joining science, technology, art, business, public data, and open innovation. It is best understood as an ongoing collective project or Gesamtkunstwerk-like working environment, not a single authored work. ([World Wide Web Foundation, *Opening Cities: Open Data in Buenos Aires*](https://webfoundation.org/docs/2017/09/Buenos-Aires-final-report.pdf); [Satellogic biography](https://investors.satellogic.com/management/emiliano-kargieman))

- **2010–:** *Satellogic*. A space-technology company and planetary-observation project co-founded by Kargieman and Gerardo Richarte on 13 July 2010. It combines spacecraft, sensors, software, machine learning, orbital infrastructure, and an Earth-imagery service, making it closer to a technological Gesamtkunstwerk than to a single invention attributable to Kargieman alone. ([Satellogic, “Satellogic Turns 12”](https://satellogic.com/2022/07/13/satellogic-turns-12-missions-milestones-and-memories/); [IDB, *Techno-Creative Entrepreneurships*](https://publications.iadb.org/publications/english/document/TechnoCreative_Entrepreneurships_Creativity_and_Technology_Allies_or_Enemies_en.pdf))

### Collective platforms and spacecraft programs

- **2011–2012:** *¿Qué pasa, Riachuelo?* An online, public-data-based environmental-monitoring and mapping platform created collectively by FARN and GarageLab with participating civil-society organizations. Kargieman’s connection is through his co-founded laboratory; the available sources do not justify assigning him sole or direct personal authorship. ([World Wide Web Foundation, *Opening Cities*](https://webfoundation.org/docs/2017/09/Buenos-Aires-final-report.pdf); [Buenos Aires annual environmental report](https://tsjcaba.opac.ar/pgmedia/Media/PDFs/1.11346_D-11885_Informe%20ambiental%20anual%202012.pdf))

- **2013:** *CubeBug-1 (“Capitán Beto”)*. Satellogic’s first experimental nanosatellite, launched in April 2013; it is a collective engineered object produced by the company Kargieman co-founded, not securely documented as his individual design. ([Satellogic, “Satellogic Turns 12”](https://satellogic.com/2022/07/13/satellogic-turns-12-missions-milestones-and-memories/))

- **2013:** *CubeBug-2 (“Manolito”)*. The second CubeBug nanosatellite and another collective manifestation of the Satellogic project. Sources associate it with Satellogic and Argentina’s sponsored CubeBug program, but do not provide a component-by-component personal attribution to Kargieman. ([Satellogic company history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satellogic); [Satellogic management biography](https://investors.satellogic.com/management/emiliano-kargieman))

- **2016–:** *Aleph-1 / NewSat constellation*. A continuing constellation of high-resolution Earth-observation satellites, together with the systems for manufacturing, tasking, and processing their imagery. It resists classification as one finished work: it is an expanding collective infrastructure whose individual satellites and technical revisions are manifestations of the larger project. ([Satellogic, Aleph-1 expansion announcement](https://satellogic.com/news/press-releases/satellogic-announces-successful-expansion-of-aleph-1-constellation-following-spacex-transporter-6-mission-launch/); [IDB, *Techno-Creative Entrepreneurships*](https://publications.iadb.org/publications/english/document/TechnoCreative_Entrepreneurships_Creativity_and_Technology_Allies_or_Enemies_en.pdf))

### Inventions documented by patent families

- **2003:** *Framework for Maintaining Information Security in Computer Networks*. A co-invented access-control system expressed as a technical protocol for distributing authorization data through tickets. The cited record is a US patent application, not evidence that Kargieman alone implemented every manifestation of the invention. ([US patent-family listing](https://patents.justia.com/inventor/emiliano-kargieman))

- **2003:** *Secure Auditing of Information Systems*. A co-invented system for parsing, mapping, filtering, and visually presenting audit-log events. It survives in the consulted corpus as a published patent application rather than a confirmed standalone product. ([US patent-family listing](https://patents.justia.com/inventor/emiliano-kargieman))

- **2015–2020:** *Imaging Device for Scenes in Apparent Motion*. A family of co-invented imaging systems that mechanically tracks apparent motion during exposure; multiple applications and grants are manifestations of one invention, not separate works. The record names Gerardo Richarte, Kargieman, and Juan Manuel Vuletich as inventors. ([US patent-family listing](https://patents.justia.com/inventor/emiliano-kargieman))

- **2017–2019:** *Hyper-Spectral Imaging When Observed Object Is Still*. A co-invented hyperspectral-imaging apparatus using a movable multispectral filter. Its application, US grant, and European grant are jurisdictional manifestations of the same technical obra; Kargieman is one of six named inventors. ([US patent-family listing](https://patents.justia.com/inventor/emiliano-kargieman); [European Patent Bulletin](https://www.epo.org/bulletin/downloads/2024/bulletin2404.pdf))

- **2017–2019:** *Dynamic Hyper-Spectral Imaging of Objects in Apparent Motion*. A co-invented system for dynamically configurable spectral imaging of moving scenes, documented by application and grant records. It is distinct from the still-object system but shares its six-person inventor group. ([US patent-family listing](https://patents.justia.com/inventor/emiliano-kargieman))

- **2017–2025:** *System for Planetary-Scale Analytics*. A co-invented “smart satellite” system that performs onboard analysis and prioritizes what data to capture or transmit. Continuations, grants, and foreign publications—including US patents 11,101,876 and 12,489,518—are manifestations of a single evolving patent family rather than separate inventions. ([US patent-family listing](https://patents.justia.com/inventor/emiliano-kargieman); [US Patent 12,489,518](https://pdfstore.patentorder.com/getminesoft/1492308872/US/20251202/B2/000012/48/95/18/US-12489518-B2-20251202.pdf))

- **2021–2023:** *Anonymous, Authenticated and Private Satellite Tasking System*. A cryptographic protocol and system enabling users to task satellites or retrieve data without exposing those activities to other users of the shared infrastructure. The consulted record is a published application naming Kargieman and Gerardo Richarte as co-inventors; grant status is not established here. ([US patent-family listing](https://patents.justia.com/inventor/emiliano-kargieman))

- **2022–2025:** *Dynamic Order and Resource Management Method and System for Geospatial Information Provision*. A co-invented optimization and orchestration system for matching geospatial-information orders to resources in a satellite constellation. The corpus records it as a published application, so it remains a claimed invention rather than a demonstrated completed product. ([US patent-family listing](https://patents.justia.com/inventor/emiliano-kargieman))

### Authored text

- **2014:** *Satellogic*. A short, signed expository article by Kargieman presenting the rationale for an accessible, distributed platform in low Earth orbit. It is a programmatic text approaching a manifesto for the Satellogic project, though the publication labels it as a company presentation rather than a manifesto. ([*Space Safety Magazine*, December 2014](https://ufdcimages.uflib.ufl.edu/AA/00/05/44/27/00131/12-2014.pdf))
