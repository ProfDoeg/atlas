## Works by Eduardo Sergio Elsztain

### Corporate real-estate obra

These are collective works executed by IRSA while Elsztain controlled and chaired it, not buildings personally designed by him. IRSA’s institutional chronology is the nearest thing available to a catalogue raisonné; ownership or chairmanship alone has not been treated as authorship.

- **1997–1998:** *Abasto Shopping*. Adaptive reuse of Buenos Aires’s former Mercado de Abasto as a shopping and entertainment complex, undertaken by IRSA with Parque Arauco. This is a corporate urban-development obra rather than Elsztain’s sole architectural work. ([IRSA](https://www.irsa.com.ar/compania/); [Abasto chronology](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abasto_de_Buenos_Aires))

- **1999–2000:** *Torres Abasto*. Residential-tower development adjoining Abasto Shopping. IRSA’s chronology identifies it as a development but does not assign Elsztain individual design authorship. ([IRSA](https://www.irsa.com.ar/compania/))

- **2003–2004:** *Alto Rosario Shopping*. Shopping centre created through the reuse and extension of former railway property in Rosario; its opening, rather than acquisition, is recorded in IRSA’s corporate chronology. It is a company-made urban complex under Elsztain’s leadership. ([IRSA](https://www.irsa.com.ar/compania/))

- **2005–2006:** *San Martín de Tours*. Residential real-estate development listed by IRSA as one of its developments. The public corporate chronology supplies only a date range and title, so a more precise completion date or individual authorship should not be inferred. ([IRSA](https://www.irsa.com.ar/compania/))

- **2007–2009:** *Torres Renoir*. Residential-tower development undertaken through IRSA’s joint venture with Cyrela. It resists attribution to Elsztain alone because the developer, joint-venture partner, architects, engineers and builders all participated in making it. ([IRSA](https://www.irsa.com.ar/compania/))

- **2007–2011:** *Horizons*. Residential complex initiated in 2007–2009 and recorded as under development again in 2010–2011. The corporate chronology does not establish a single completion date. ([IRSA](https://www.irsa.com.ar/compania/))

- **2007–2009:** *Dique IV*. Office-building construction in Puerto Madero. It is properly a collective corporate obra, not a personally authored building by Elsztain. ([IRSA](https://www.irsa.com.ar/compania/))

- **2009:** *Dot Baires Shopping*. Large shopping and mixed-use complex opened on land IRSA had acquired years earlier. Elsztain later described Dot as emblematic of the company’s practice of creating new urban spaces, but the physical work remains collectively authored. ([IRSA](https://www.irsa.com.ar/compania/); [Punto de Partida interview](https://puntodepartidatv.com/2024/11/24/eduardo-elsztain-vamos-a-ver-a-muchos-mas-empresarios-haciendo-cosas-en-el-pais/))

- **2010–2011:** *Oficina DOT*. Office development attached to the Dot complex. IRSA’s chronology names the development without identifying a separate architect or exact completion date. ([IRSA](https://www.irsa.com.ar/compania/))

- **2014:** *Distrito Arcos Premium Outlet*. Adaptive-reuse shopping complex in former railway structures at Palermo; its opening followed litigation that delayed public operation. It was developed by IRSA and cannot responsibly be called Elsztain’s individual architectural creation. ([IRSA](https://www.irsa.com.ar/compania/); [Eduardo Elsztain profile](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eduardo_Elsztain))

- **2015:** *Alto Comahue Shopping*. Shopping centre in Neuquén opened by IRSA. Elsztain has described its eventual role as a social centre for the city, but its authorship is institutional and collective. ([IRSA](https://www.irsa.com.ar/compania/); [Punto de Partida interview](https://puntodepartidatv.com/2024/11/24/eduardo-elsztain-vamos-a-ver-a-muchos-mas-empresarios-haciendo-cosas-en-el-pais/))

- **2017–2018:** *Alto Avellaneda expansion*. Enlargement of an existing shopping centre rather than an autonomous new building. IRSA’s chronology records it as work in progress and gives no single completion date. ([IRSA](https://www.irsa.com.ar/compania/))

- **2017–2018:** *Alto Rosario Zara expansion*. A new retail component within an existing complex, not a separately authored building. Its inclusion in the corporate chronology makes it part of IRSA’s made environment, although attribution to Elsztain personally would be excessive. ([IRSA](https://www.irsa.com.ar/compania/))

- **2017–2018:** *Mendoza Plaza Sodimac expansion*. Addition to an existing shopping centre, recorded as an expansion under development. No individual designer or exact completion date is supplied in the consulted corporate chronology. ([IRSA](https://www.irsa.com.ar/compania/))

- **2017–2018:** *Alto Comahue cinema expansion*. New cinemas added to the existing Neuquén centre. This is an alteration within a larger corporate obra rather than an independent Elsztain work. ([IRSA](https://www.irsa.com.ar/compania/))

- **2019:** *Edificio Zetta*. Office building constituting the first office park at Polo Dot, principally occupied by Mercado Libre and Falabella. It is a completed corporate development, not a building designed by Elsztain. ([IRSA](https://www.irsa.com.ar/compania/); [IRSA annual filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1128173/000156827621001786/ircp_20f.htm))

- **2020:** *200 Della Paolera*. Approximately 35,000-square-metre office tower in the Catalinas/Puerto Madero area, also called Torre Catalinas during development. The variation in names reflects its project and completed-building stages, not two separate works. ([IRSA](https://www.irsa.com.ar/compania/); [IRSA annual filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1128173/000156827621001786/ircp_20f.htm))

### Projects

- **2007–2009:** *Solares de Santa María*. Approved real-estate project recorded by IRSA without evidence in the consulted chronology that the proposed work was completed. Its status as an approved intention makes “project,” rather than building or obra, the accurate category. ([IRSA](https://www.irsa.com.ar/compania/))

- **2015–:** *Ampliación Alto Palermo Shopping*. Expansion project launched by IRSA. The corporate history records the launch but not a definitive completion in the same entry, so it should not be silently converted into a finished work. ([IRSA](https://www.irsa.com.ar/compania/))

- **2015–2019:** *Primer Office Park Polo Dot*. Office-park project launched beside Dot Baires and subsequently manifested in the Zetta building. The project and that first completed building are related but not synonymous. ([IRSA](https://www.irsa.com.ar/compania/))

- **2016–:** *Edificio del Plata redevelopment*. Initially conceived after IRSA bought the former municipal office building and later reformulated as a mixed residential and commercial development of approximately 720 dwellings. Because its program changed and construction remained ongoing, it is best classified as an evolving project rather than a completed Elsztain work. ([Edificio del Plata history](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edificio_del_Plata); [IRSA](https://www.irsa.com.ar/compania/))

- **2017–:** *La Plata mixed-use development*. Project on land purchased by IRSA for what Elsztain later described as La Plata’s first shopping centre. The consulted sources do not provide a stable final title or completed object, so the descriptive designation is retained. ([IRSA](https://www.irsa.com.ar/compania/); [Elsztain’s project statement](https://ar.linkedin.com/in/eduardo-elsztain))

- **2021–:** *Ramblas del Plata* (formerly *Costa Urbana*). Approved mixed-use waterfront district on the former Ciudad Deportiva de Boca site, planned over roughly fifteen to twenty years; IRSA envisages housing, commerce, offices and extensive public space. It is an especially clear case in which the intention and long process are the work: development parcels involve numerous other developers, so neither the future district nor its buildings can be attributed to Elsztain alone. ([IRSA](https://www.irsa.com.ar/compania/); [IRSA FY2025 factsheet](https://file.investorrelations.com.ar/archivos/files/IRSA/IRSAFactsheetFY25ESP.pdf))

### Institutional creations

- **1996:** *Fundación IRSA*. Corporate philanthropic institution created to support initiatives in education, social welfare and personal autonomy. Elsztain chairs it, but the foundation’s own history presents its creation institutionally rather than identifying him as sole founder. ([Fundación IRSA](https://www.fundacionirsa.org.ar/sobrenosotros))

- **1998:** *Endeavor Argentina*. Nonprofit entrepreneurial-support network established by a founding group that included Elsztain, Linda Rottenberg, Peter Kellner, María Eugenia Estenssoro and others. It is an organizational creation and continuing collective undertaking, not a sole-authored work. ([Endeavor Argentina](https://www.endeavor.org.ar/20-anos-endeavor-creyendo-la-transformacion/))

- **1999:** *Museo de los Niños Abasto*. Interactive learning environment and nonprofit foundation opened inside Abasto Shopping on 20 April 1999. Fundación IRSA’s history includes it among its early projects and Elsztain has presided over it, but the museum’s own account does not attribute its design or creation to him alone. ([Museo de los Niños Abasto](https://www.museoabasto.org.ar/institucional); [Fundación IRSA](https://www.fundacionirsa.org.ar/sobrenosotros))

### Manifesto of uncertain authorship

- *Manifiesto IRSA*. An undated corporate manifesto declaring IRSA’s intention to transform overlooked urban spaces into memorable places. It acts as a statement of collective purpose, but the published page supplies no author credit; consequently it cannot securely be entered as a text written by Elsztain, even though it articulates the development practice he leads. ([IRSA, “Manifiesto IRSA”](https://www.irsa.com.ar/compania/))
