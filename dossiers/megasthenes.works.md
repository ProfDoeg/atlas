## Works by Megasthenes of Ionia

### Fragmentary geographical and ethnographic obra

- **c. 310 BCE (conventional; dating disputed):** *Indika* (τὰ Ἰνδικά, *Indica*). A prose description of India combining geography, natural resources, political and social organization, religious-philosophical practices, history, and legend, informed partly by Megasthenes’ diplomatic residence at Chandragupta’s court. The obra itself is lost: what survives consists of quotations, paraphrases, and possible echoes in later authors—especially Diodorus, Strabo, Arrian, and Pliny—and editors disagree about which unattributed passages belong to it; its supposed four-book structure is likewise disputed, with recent editions reconstructing three books and treating the apparent ancient reference to a fourth as corrupt. The surviving fragments and later epitomes are manifestations of the lost work, not additional works made by Megasthenes.
