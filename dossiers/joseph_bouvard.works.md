## Works by Joseph-Antoine Bouvard

### Buildings and architectural interventions

- **1860s:** *Works at the church of Saint-Laurent, Paris*. Bouvard served as conductor of works rather than originating architect; this is therefore a supervised building campaign, not securely an independent design. ([INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911))

- **1860s:** *Works at the new mairie of Belleville, Paris*. Another early municipal building campaign conducted by Bouvard; the standard biographical notice does not identify a discrete design attributable solely to him. ([INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911))

- **1870–1871:** *Defensive works in the nineteenth and twentieth arrondissements of Paris*. Wartime public works undertaken through the municipal architectural service. They resist treatment as a single authored obra: the corpus supplies neither individual titles nor evidence that Bouvard was their sole designer. ([INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911))

- **1871:** *Adaptation of the Palais du Luxembourg*. An architectural conversion undertaken under Gabriel Davioud’s authority, making Bouvard a subordinate collaborator rather than the building’s independent author. ([INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911))

- **after 1871–1880:** *Restoration and enlargement of the Hôtel Carnavalet*. A restoration and museum-making intervention completed with Félix Roguet and associated by the Paris heritage study with the new rue Payenne wing; the work incorporated displaced historic façades, so it is simultaneously architecture, restoration and assemblage. ([INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911); [Ville de Paris heritage study](https://cdn.paris.fr/paris/2020/02/26/05b40ed2271196016739bacc574c5354.pdf))

- **1882–1884/1886:** *Gare de Saint-Étienne-Châteaucreux*. A railway station for the PLM, constructed around a metal frame with a hydraulic-jack system intended to counter mining subsidence. Sources disagree slightly on the building campaign: the municipal archives give 1884–1886 and an opening in October 1884, while other summaries use 1882–1884. ([Saint-Étienne Municipal Archives](https://archives.saint-etienne.fr/histoires-stephanoises-1/tranches-dhistoire/equipements-de-transports/gare-de-chateaucreux); [INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911))

- **1885–1887:** *École nationale professionnelle de Voiron*. A purpose-built vocational school, commonly dated 1885–1886 but recorded as a work of 1887 in the INHA notice. It belongs to Bouvard’s extensive public-school practice, much of which was executed by municipal teams and is not recoverable as a clean list of individually authored buildings. ([INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911); [D’Elboux, “Joseph Bouvard and La Construction Moderne”](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/287199790_Joseph_Bouvard_and_La_Construction_Moderne_some_notes_of_a_career_1885-1920))

- **by 1888:** *Caserne Louviers / Caserne de la Garde républicaine, rue de Schomberg*. A municipal barracks in Paris’s fourth arrondissement, exhibited under Bouvard’s name in 1889. The corpus uses both “Caserne Louviers” and “Caserne de la Garde républicaine”; they appear to designate the same complex rather than two safely separable works. ([INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911))

- **1888:** *Archives municipales de la Seine, boulevard Henri-IV*. A purpose-built municipal archive building in the fourth arrondissement, also exhibited in 1889 as the *Bâtiments des Archives de la Seine*. ([INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911))

- **by 1889:** *Bourse centrale du Travail, rue du Château-d’Eau*. A labour-exchange building and municipal institution, presented among Bouvard’s works at the 1889 Exposition. ([INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911))

- **by 1889:** *Refuge-ouvroir, rue Fessart*. A combined shelter and work institution exhibited under Bouvard’s name in 1889; the consulted corpus gives no firmer construction date. ([INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911))

- **by 1889:** *Établissement de désinfection, quai de Valmy*. A municipal disinfection plant rather than a conventional monumental building; it was exhibited in 1889 and is also described as an *usine de désinfection*. ([INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911))

- **1893–1896:** *Gare de Marseille-Saint-Charles*. A railway-station enlargement associated with Bouvard’s work as consulting architect to the PLM. France’s heritage inventory attributes the station to Bouvard and Gaston Castel but records several campaigns—1851, 1880, 1893, 1954 and 1983—so only the late-nineteenth-century intervention belongs to Bouvard. ([French Inventory of Cultural Heritage](https://pop.culture.gouv.fr/notice/merimee/IA13000799); [INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911))

- **1907–1911:** *Hospital and schools in Argentina*. The INHA career notice credits Bouvard with constructing a hospital and schools through his South American activity, but does not name, date or locate them precisely. They cannot responsibly be split into individual works from the consulted corpus. ([INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911))

### Monuments and unrealized monument projects

- **1881:** *Motifs décoratifs pour la place de la République*. A collaborative decorative project with sculptor Ulysse Gravigny, exhibited at the Salon des artistes français. Its title denotes a proposal or set of designs; the notice does not establish that it was executed. ([INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911))

- **1883:** *Monument à Alexandre Dumas*. A public monument at place Malesherbes, made collaboratively with Gustave Doré and Ulysse Gravigny; Bouvard’s contribution was architectural rather than sole authorship of the sculptural whole. ([INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911))

- **1886:** *Monument à Denis Diderot*. A public monument on boulevard Saint-Germain made with sculptor Jean Gautherin; it is a collaborative architectural-sculptural obra. ([INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911))

- *Projet de statue à Victor Hugo*. An apparently unrealized monument proposal attributed to Bouvard in the scholarly survey of *La Construction Moderne*. That survey supplies no secure date or evidence of manifestation, so it remains a project rather than a monument. ([D’Elboux, “Joseph Bouvard and La Construction Moderne”](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/287199790_Joseph_Bouvard_and_La_Construction_Moderne_some_notes_of_a_career_1885-1920))

- **1907:** *Monument à l’Indépendance argentine, Plaza del Congreso*. An unrealized Buenos Aires monument project identified as Bouvard’s own in the scholarly survey. It should not be confused with his broader proposal for the plaza or with the monument eventually erected there. ([D’Elboux, “Joseph Bouvard and La Construction Moderne”](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/287199790_Joseph_Bouvard_and_La_Construction_Moderne_some_notes_of_a_career_1885-1920))

### Exhibition architecture and Gesamtkunstwerke

- **1878:** *Pavillon de la Ville de Paris, Exposition universelle*. A temporary exhibition pavilion representing Paris, designed within the organizing structure of the 1878 Exposition. Its architecture, displays and civic representation make it closer to a temporary Gesamtkunstwerk than to an autonomous permanent building. ([INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911); [D’Elboux, “Joseph Bouvard and La Construction Moderne”](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/287199790_Joseph_Bouvard_and_La_Construction_Moderne_some_notes_of_a_career_1885-1920))

- **1886–1889:** *Palais des Industries diverses and Dôme central, Exposition universelle de 1889*. A vast temporary exhibition palace and its central dome, designed by Bouvard with engineer Victor Contamin after Bouvard’s appointment in July 1886. Contemporary and institutional sources distinguish it from Dutert and Contamin’s Galerie des Machines. ([Bureau International des Expositions](https://biennale.bie-paris.org/site/fr/1889-paris); [CNAM, official illustrated guide](https://cnum.cnam.fr/pgi/redir.php?ident=12XAE69&onglet=c); [INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911))

- **1889:** *Pavillon / exposition spéciale de la Ville de Paris*. The Paris municipal display at the 1889 Exposition, treated by the scholarly survey as Bouvard’s city pavilion. The INHA notice also places the *Palais des Expositions diverses* within this municipal presentation, leaving some ambiguity over whether “pavilion” names a separate structure or the city’s installation inside Bouvard’s palace. ([INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911); [D’Elboux, “Joseph Bouvard and La Construction Moderne”](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/287199790_Joseph_Bouvard_and_La_Construction_Moderne_some_notes_of_a_career_1885-1920))

- **1893:** *Pavillon de la Ville de Paris, World’s Columbian Exposition, Chicago*. A temporary civic exhibition pavilion credited to Bouvard by the INHA career notice. The consulted notice gives no design description and should not be taken to establish sole authorship of every installation within it. ([INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911))

- **1894:** *Pavillon de la Ville de Paris, Exposition universelle d’Anvers*. A temporary municipal exhibition building or installation credited to Bouvard. ([INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911))

- *Pavillons de la Ville de Paris at Brussels, Melbourne and Saint-Louis*. The INHA notice credits Bouvard with these temporary exhibition works but prints “1894” in connection with Melbourne and Saint-Louis, a chronologically impossible compression; it also does not specify which Brussels exhibition. These attributions are retained as disputed catalogue data, not silently redated works. ([INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911))

- **1897–1900:** *Plan général de l’Exposition universelle de 1900*. The master plan and administrative-architectural orchestration of the entire exposition, for which Bouvard was director of architecture and fêtes. This is a collective Gesamtkunstwerk and managerial obra: he authored the general plan, not every individual building erected by the many participating architects. ([INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911); [D’Elboux, “Joseph Bouvard and La Construction Moderne”](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/287199790_Joseph_Bouvard_and_La_Construction_Moderne_some_notes_of_a_career_1885-1920))

### Ephemeral civic manifestations

- **1896–1897:** *Decorations for the Franco-Russian fêtes*. A city-scale reception environment of flags, banners, temporary architecture and electric illumination made for the Russian sovereigns’ visit. It resists reduction to a building or text: Bouvard’s work was the orchestration of an ephemeral civic manifestation through the Société des fêtes de Paris. ([D’Elboux, “Joseph Bouvard and La Construction Moderne”](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/287199790_Joseph_Bouvard_and_La_Construction_Moderne_some_notes_of_a_career_1885-1920))

- **1903:** *Fêtes franco-italiennes*. An ephemeral urban Gesamtkunstwerk created for the Italian royal visit, including the decorated avenue de l’Opéra, flags, draperies and electric lighting. It was a performed and temporary city rather than a durable architectural object. ([D’Elboux, “Joseph Bouvard and La Construction Moderne”](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/287199790_Joseph_Bouvard_and_La_Construction_Moderne_some_notes_of_a_career_1885-1920))

### Urban projects

- **1902:** *Avant-projet d’aménagement de Constantinople*. An urban preliminary plan commissioned by Sultan Abdülhamid II and left without execution. A second Ottoman request reported in 1908 was not accomplished, so the surviving obra is the 1902 intention and preliminary design, not a transformed city. ([INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911); [D’Elboux, “Joseph Bouvard and La Construction Moderne”](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/287199790_Joseph_Bouvard_and_La_Construction_Moderne_some_notes_of_a_career_1885-1920))

- **1903:** *Studies for the approaches to the Palais de Justice and Porte de Namur, Brussels*. An urban-design consultation requested by Leopold II. It remained without sequel and is therefore a project whose principal made thing was advice or studies, not an executed urban intervention. ([INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911))

- **1907–1909:** *Plan for Buenos Aires*. A metropolitan improvement project initially prepared in six weeks and revised by 1909, involving thirty-two diagonals and other interventions. Its status is that of a planning corpus rather than one indivisible drawing or a fully implemented city. ([INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911); [*El modelo Beaux-Arts y la arquitectura en América Latina*](https://www.fau.unlp.edu.ar/web2018/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Libro-Beaux-Arts-digital.pdf))

- **1907:** *Urbanization of Quinta Hale*. A Buenos Aires development proposal forming part of Bouvard’s larger planning mission. The consulted scholarly synthesis records the proposal but does not establish its complete execution. ([*El modelo Beaux-Arts y la arquitectura en América Latina*](https://www.fau.unlp.edu.ar/web2018/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Libro-Beaux-Arts-digital.pdf))

- **1907:** *Project for Plaza del Congreso, Buenos Aires*. A design for the future civic square, distinct from Bouvard’s proposed independence monument for the same site. Its relationship to the plaza eventually realized requires caution and should not be treated as straightforward execution of his design. ([*El modelo Beaux-Arts y la arquitectura en América Latina*](https://www.fau.unlp.edu.ar/web2018/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Libro-Beaux-Arts-digital.pdf))

- **1907:** *Plan for a 2,000-bed hospital, Buenos Aires*. An architectural and institutional megaproject produced during Bouvard’s first Buenos Aires mission; the consulted source identifies the plan but not a completed building. ([*El modelo Beaux-Arts y la arquitectura en América Latina*](https://www.fau.unlp.edu.ar/web2018/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Libro-Beaux-Arts-digital.pdf))

- **1911:** *Plan de place for Rosario*. A design for a public square, recorded without a fixed title in the standard INHA notice. Because the notice does not establish realization, it remains a project rather than a securely manifested place. ([INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911))

- **1911:** *Plan d’embellissement de Montevideo*. A city-beautification plan whose precise constituent drawings and degree of implementation are not specified in the consulted corpus. ([INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911))

- **1911:** *Plano Bouvard for São Paulo*. A group of expansion, circulation and beautification studies developed in situ to mediate between competing municipal and state proposals. Contemporary reporting disagreed even over whether the state, municipality or both commissioned him; the plan was an advisory and conciliatory project, only selectively manifested in the city. ([D’Elboux, “Joseph Bouvard and La Construction Moderne”](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/287199790_Joseph_Bouvard_and_La_Construction_Moderne_some_notes_of_a_career_1885-1920); [INHA/AGORHA](https://agorha.inha.fr/ark:/54721/88efaadc-7ab2-4d02-892a-b881c5c3c911))

- **late 1911:** *Proposals for new districts in São Paulo*. A set of neighborhood-planning proposals influenced by picturesque and garden-city ideas. Later developments at Pacaembu, Butantã, Alto da Lapa and Jardim América have complicated corporate and professional authorship, so they should not simply be catalogued as buildings or districts “by Bouvard.” ([D’Elboux, “Joseph Bouvard and La Construction Moderne”](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/287199790_Joseph_Bouvard_and_La_Construction_Moderne_some_notes_of_a_career_1885-1920); [D’Elboux, “The Early Years of ‘Cia. City’ in São Paulo”](https://www.scielo.br/j/rbeur/a/8YvvZThsWyxLVjhjk3Rtg3D/?format=pdf&lang=en))

### Administrative text-object

- **1902:** *Recueil d’actes administratifs et de conventions relatifs aux voies publiques*. A directed reissue, prepared with Louis-Charles Taxil, of older Parisian prescriptions governing streets and façades. It is not an original authored treatise so much as a regulatory compilation: a protocol-like administrative text intended to act upon the city by recovering enforceable rules. ([D’Elboux, “Joseph Bouvard and La Construction Moderne”](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/287199790_Joseph_Bouvard_and_La_Construction_Moderne_some_notes_of_a_career_1885-1920); [*Livraisons de l’histoire de l’architecture*](https://journals.openedition.org/lha/pdf/111))
