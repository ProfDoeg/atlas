## Works by Mansur al-Hallaj

### Surviving or substantially recoverable works

- **No fixed date; before 922 in substance:** *Kitāb al-Ṭawāsīn* (“Book of the Ṭā-Sīns”). An obra of eleven mystical essays combining rhythmic prose, poetry, letter-symbolism, and diagrams; its surviving form was probably arranged by disciples, so it is both al-Hallaj’s work and a posthumously shaped Gesamtkunstwerk. [[Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/hallaj/); [Massignon’s critical edition](https://www.quranicthought.com/wp-content/uploads/post_attachments/607599d2d93ce.pdf)]
- **No fixed date; transmitted independently by the twelfth century:** *Bustān al-maʿrifa* (“Garden of Gnosis”). A short meditation on mystical knowledge, preserved both as an independent treatise and as the final section of some recensions of the *Ṭawāsīn*; whether al-Hallaj made it as a separate work therefore remains unresolved. [[Ruspoli edition record](https://hurqalya.ucmerced.edu/node/3881/); [Massignon’s critical edition](https://www.quranicthought.com/wp-content/uploads/post_attachments/607599d2d93ce.pdf)]
- **Before 922; reconstructed in 1931 and subsequently revised:** *Dīwān al-Ḥallāj*. A modern scholarly reconstruction from more than 150 scattered poetic fragments rather than a book arranged by al-Hallaj; individual poems and lines have unequal chains of transmission, so the collection resists treatment as a single authorial obra. [[Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/hallaj/); [UC Merced bibliography](https://hurqalya.ucmerced.edu/node/3881/)]
- **Before 922; surviving only through later quotation:** *Tafsīr* or Qurʾanic interpretations. These are exegetical sayings dispersed chiefly through al-Sulamī’s *Ḥaqāʾiq al-tafsīr*, not a recoverable continuous commentary; the modern “tafsir of al-Hallaj” is thus an editorial corpus, and its original completeness and organization are unknown. [[UC Merced bibliography](https://hurqalya.ucmerced.edu/node/3881/)]
- **Before 902, according to Massignon’s reconstruction:** *al-Riwāyāt*. Twenty-seven hadith-like visionary narrations or ecstatic discourses intended for circulation; their Arabic text is lost and they survive through Rūzbihān Baqlī’s later Persian translation and commentary, whose condition makes their authenticity uncertain. [[Massignon’s critical introduction](https://pdfcoffee.com/kitab-al-tawasin-by-prof-louis-massignon-pdf-free.html); [Massignon abridgement](https://dokumen.pub/hallaj-mystic-and-martyr-abridged-edition-9780691234540.html)]
- **Before 922; fragment only:** *Nafy al-tashbīh* (“Negation of Anthropomorphic Comparison”). A theological treatise known from an extracted creed preserved by al-Kalābādhī; the original work is lost, and even its surviving portion is mediated by quotation. [[Massignon’s critical introduction](https://pdfcoffee.com/kitab-al-tawasin-by-prof-louis-massignon-pdf-free.html)]
- **Before 922; fragment only:** *Faṣl fī al-maḥabba* (“Chapter on Love”). A mystical prose discussion surviving in Persian translation in Rūzbihān Baqlī’s *Sharḥ-i shaṭḥiyyāt*; its Arabic wording and original textual setting are lost. [[Massignon’s critical introduction](https://pdfcoffee.com/kitab-al-tawasin-by-prof-louis-massignon-pdf-free.html)]
- **Before 922; fragment only:** *ʿAyn al-jamʿ*. A mystical treatise identified by Jāmī, from which only a conclusion translated into archaic Persian by ʿAbdallāh Anṣārī is reported; the identification and original extent cannot be independently established. [[Massignon’s critical introduction](https://pdfcoffee.com/kitab-al-tawasin-by-prof-louis-massignon-pdf-free.html)]
- **Before 922; title unknown:** *Fragment on maʿrifa*. A passage about mystical knowledge preserved only in Baqlī’s Persian translation; because no secure original title survives, it should not be silently merged with *Bustān al-maʿrifa*. [[Massignon’s critical introduction](https://pdfcoffee.com/kitab-al-tawasin-by-prof-louis-massignon-pdf-free.html)]
- **Before 922; fragment only:** *Ruqʿa bi-khaṭṭ al-Ḥallāj* (“A note in al-Hallaj’s hand”). A short written communication copied into the tradition behind *Akhbār al-Ḥallāj*; the alleged autograph no longer survives, so this is a reported material object rather than a verifiable manuscript in his hand. [[Massignon’s critical introduction](https://pdfcoffee.com/kitab-al-tawasin-by-prof-louis-massignon-pdf-free.html)]

### Lost treatises recorded by Ibn al-Nadim

- **Before 922; lost:** *Ṭā-Sīn al-azal* (“Ṭā-Sīn of Pre-eternity”). Ibn al-Nadim’s catalogue title probably corresponds to the celebrated *Ṭā-Sīn al-azal wa-l-iltibās* now embedded in the *Ṭawāsīn*, but the exact relationship between the catalogued book and surviving chapter is disputed. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar); [Massignon](https://www.allamaiqbal.com/publications/journals/review/apr70/1.htm)]
- **Before 922; lost or absorbed into the preceding work:** *al-Jawhar al-akbar wa-l-shajara al-zaytūna al-nūriyya* (“The Greatest Jewel and the Luminous Olive Tree”). Manuscripts and editors disagree over whether this is a second title or the continuation of the title *Ṭā-Sīn al-azal*; Massignon’s corrected count of forty-seven works depends on separating them. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar); [Massignon](https://www.quranicthought.com/wp-content/uploads/post_attachments/607599d2d93ce.pdf)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *al-Aḥruf al-muḥdatha wa-l-azaliyya wa-l-asmāʾ al-kulliyya* (“Created and Pre-eternal Letters and Universal Names”). A treatise apparently concerning sacred letters and divine names; only its catalogue title remains. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *al-Ẓill al-mamdūd wa-l-māʾ al-maskūb wa-l-ḥayāt al-bāqiya* (“The Outspread Shade, the Poured Water, and the Abiding Life”). A catalogued religious or eschatological treatise whose contents cannot safely be reconstructed from its scriptural title. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *Ḥaml al-nūr wa-l-ḥayāt wa-l-arwāḥ* (“Bearing the Light, Life, and Spirits”). A catalogued prose work, now known only by title. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost except possibly for a preface:** *Kitāb al-Ṣayhūr* (also transmitted as *al-Ṣahyūn* or *al-Sayhūr*). The title is textually unstable; Massignon believed a preface survived, but no complete work does. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar); [Massignon abridgement](https://dokumen.pub/hallaj-mystic-and-martyr-abridged-edition-9780691234540.html)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *Tafsīr Qul huwa Allāhu aḥad*. An interpretation of Qurʾan 112; no continuous text securely corresponding to the catalogue entry survives. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *al-Abad wa-l-ābād* (“Eternity and the Eternities”). A catalogued metaphysical treatise known only by its unstable transmitted title. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *Qirāʾat al-Qurʾān wa-l-furqān* (“Reading the Qurʾan and the Criterion”). A catalogued Qurʾanic or hermeneutical work; some later lists give *Qurʾān al-Qurʾān wa-l-furqān*, illustrating uncertainty in the title itself. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *Khalq al-insān wa-l-bayān* (“Creation of the Human Being and Exposition”). A catalogued treatise whose title evokes Qurʾanic creation and language; nothing establishes its original form or length. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *Kayd al-shayṭān wa-amr al-sulṭān* (“Satan’s Guile and the Ruler’s Command”). A catalogued work apparently joining spiritual and political themes; only the title survives. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *al-Uṣūl wa-l-furūʿ* (“Roots and Branches”). A catalogued doctrinal or legal treatise, but its subject cannot be determined beyond the conventional title. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *Sirr al-ʿālam wa-l-baʿth* (“The Secret of the World and Resurrection”). A catalogued cosmological or eschatological treatise; variant lists transmit the last word differently. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *al-ʿAdl wa-l-tawḥīd* (“Justice and Divine Unity”). A theological treatise known only from Ibn al-Nadim’s title. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *al-Siyāsa wa-l-khulafāʾ wa-l-umarāʾ* (“Governance, Caliphs, and Commanders”). A political or ethical treatise known only by title. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *ʿIlm al-baqāʾ wa-l-fanāʾ* (“Knowledge of Subsistence and Annihilation”). A mystical treatise named for two central Sufi states; no continuous text survives. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *Shakhs al-ẓulumāt* (“The Figure of the Darknesses”). A catalogued work with no securely recoverable contents. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *Nūr al-nūr* (“Light of Light”). A mystical or cosmological treatise known only by title. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *al-Mutajalliyāt* (“The Divine Manifestations”). A catalogued work apparently concerning theophanies; the title itself names manifestations, but the text is absent. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *al-Haykal wa-l-ʿālam wa-l-ʿālim* (“The Temple, the World, and the Knower”). A catalogued metaphysical work; variant summaries omit or alter its final element. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *Madḥ al-nabī wa-l-mathal al-aʿlā* (“Praise of the Prophet and the Highest Exemplar”). A devotional or prophetic treatise known only from its catalogue title. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *al-Gharīb al-faṣīḥ* (“The Strange/Eloquent”). A catalogued work whose compressed title does not disclose a dependable genre. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *al-Yaqẓa wa-badʾ al-khalq* (“Awakening and the Beginning of Creation”). A cosmological or spiritual treatise known only by title; some later summaries substitute “the point” for “awakening.” [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *al-Qiyāma wa-l-qiyāmāt* (“Resurrection and the Resurrections”). An eschatological treatise recorded only by title. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *al-Kibr wa-l-ʿaẓama* (“Grandeur and Majesty”). A theological treatise known only by title. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *al-Ṣalāt wa-l-ṣalawāt* (“Prayer and Prayers”). A devotional or ritual work known only from Ibn al-Nadim’s bibliography. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *Khazāʾin al-khayrāt*, also called *al-Alif al-maqṭūʿ wa-l-alif al-maʾlūf* (“Treasuries of Good Things” / “The Disjoined and Familiar Alif”). A letter-mystical treatise bearing alternative titles; no continuous text survives. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *Mawāʾid al-ʿārifīn* (“Tables of the Gnostics”). A catalogued mystical treatise, now only a title. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost or surviving only through unidentified quotations:** *Khalq khalāʾiq al-Qurʾān wa-l-iʿtibār* (“Creation of the Qurʾan’s Creatures and Contemplation”). A Qurʾanic work whose relation to later collections printed under similar titles is uncertain; a modern edition cannot by itself establish an intact Hallajian original. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar); [UC Merced bibliography](https://hurqalya.ucmerced.edu/node/3881/)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *al-Ṣidq wa-l-ikhlāṣ* (“Truthfulness and Sincerity”). An ethical-mystical treatise known only from its catalogue title. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *al-Amthāl wa-l-abwāb* (“Similitudes and Chapters”). A catalogued prose work of uncertain form and subject. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *al-Yaqīn* (“Certainty”). A mystical or theological treatise known only by title. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *al-Tawḥīd* (“Divine Unity”). A theological or mystical treatise known only from the catalogue. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *Wa-l-najmi idhā hawā*. An interpretation of Qurʾan 53:1 and its context; only its verse-title survives. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *Wa-l-dhāriyāti dharwan*. An interpretation of Qurʾan 51:1 and perhaps the succeeding oath sequence; no continuous text survives. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *Fī “inna alladhī faraḍa ʿalayka al-Qurʾāna la-rādduka ilā maʿād”*. An interpretation of Qurʾan 28:85, recorded under the verse itself rather than a conventional title. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *al-Durra ilā Naṣr al-Qushūrī* (“The Pearl, to Naṣr al-Qushūrī”). An addressed epistle or treatise; its addressee is part of the catalogue title, but the object itself is lost. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *al-Siyāsa ilā al-Ḥusayn ibn Ḥamdān* (“Governance, to al-Husayn ibn Hamdan”). An addressed political or advisory epistle, known only through its bibliographical notice. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *Huwa huwa* (“He Is He”). A metaphysical treatise whose title enacts identity rather than merely describing it; no text securely survives. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *Kayfa kāna wa-kayfa yakūn* (“How It Was and How It Will Be”). A cosmological or eschatological treatise known only by title. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *al-Wujūd al-awwal* (“The First Existence”). The first of two catalogued metaphysical works on existence; no text survives. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *al-Kibrīt al-aḥmar* (“The Red Sulphur”). A work bearing an alchemical-metaphorical title; the title alone does not establish whether it was practical alchemy or mystical symbolism. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *al-Simmarī wa-jawābuhu* (“al-Simmarī and Its/His Answer”). Apparently a question-and-response or polemical text; the person or term named in its unstable title has not been securely identified. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *al-Wujūd al-thānī* (“The Second Existence”). A companion to *al-Wujūd al-awwal*, known only from the catalogue. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *Lā kayf* (“Without How”). A theological treatise on divine incomparability, possibly related to the surviving creed extracted from *Nafy al-tashbīh*; Massignon proposed the connection, but it is not demonstrable. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar); [Massignon’s critical introduction](https://pdfcoffee.com/kitab-al-tawasin-by-prof-louis-massignon-pdf-free.html)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *al-Kayfiyya wa-l-ḥaqīqa* (“Modality and Reality”). A metaphysical or theological treatise known only by title. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]
- **Before 922; lost:** *al-Kayfiyya bi-l-majāz* (“Modality Figuratively Understood”). A companion or counterstatement to *al-Kayfiyya wa-l-ḥaqīqa*; its exact relationship to that work is unknown. [[Ibn al-Nadim](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4735/252?lang=ar)]

### Utterances, prayers, letters, and performed teachings

- **Before 922; collected after his death:** *More than 350 isolated sayings*. These are brief teachings, paradoxes, prayers, and ecstatic pronouncements transmitted in Sufi commentaries and biographical collections; they resist being called a work because their original occasions, wording, and parent texts are usually unrecoverable. [[Massignon’s critical introduction](https://pdfcoffee.com/kitab-al-tawasin-by-prof-louis-massignon-pdf-free.html)]
- **Before 922; surviving incompletely:** *Letters and addressed notes*. Several pieces of correspondence are preserved or reported, but there is no authorially constituted letter collection, and hostile sources also circulated letters that al-Hallaj reportedly denied at his trial. [[Massignon abridgement](https://dokumen.pub/hallaj-mystic-and-martyr-abridged-edition-9780691234540.html)]
- **Before 922; recorded posthumously:** *Prayers, exhortations, and last-night discourses*. These are oral manifestations preserved within *Akhbār al-Ḥallāj*, not a book made by al-Hallaj: the collection was arranged around 971 by transmitters associated with Naṣrābādhī and subsequently revised. [[Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/hallaj/); [Massignon–Kraus edition record](https://louismassignon.fr/biographie/figures/intercesseurs/hallaj/)]
- **Date and historical occasion uncertain:** *Anā al-Ḥaqq* (“I am the Truth”). A theopathic utterance and performative manifesto inseparable from al-Hallaj’s later image, but it is absent from reliable manuscripts of his writings and only later reports place it in specific encounters; its resistance to classification as a stable authored text is essential evidence. [[Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/hallaj/)]
