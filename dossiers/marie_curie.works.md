## Works by Maria Salomea Skłodowska-Curie

### Scientific notes and research papers

- **1897:** *Propriétés magnétiques des aciers trempés*. Curie’s first published scientific note, reporting measurements made for the Société d’encouragement pour l’industrie nationale; it belongs to her pre-radioactivity research on magnetism. ([Académie des sciences](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Curie/Curie_pubd1.htm))
- **1898:** *Rayons émis par les composés de l’uranium et du thorium*. Foundational note establishing that radiation is an atomic property and extending it from uranium to thorium; published under “M. Curie,” an abbreviation that has sometimes obscured her authorship. ([Académie des sciences](https://comptes-rendus.academie-sciences.fr/page/marie-skodowska-curie_fr/))
- **1898:** *Sur une substance nouvelle radio-active, contenue dans la pechblende*. Joint note by Marie and Pierre Curie announcing evidence for the element they named polonium; it is simultaneously a research report and an act of scientific naming. ([Académie des sciences](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Curie/Curie_pubd1.htm))
- **1898:** *Sur une nouvelle substance fortement radio-active contenue dans la pechblende*. Joint note by Marie Curie, Pierre Curie, and Gustave Bémont announcing evidence for radium. Authorship is explicitly collective, although accounts often compress it into “the Curies’ discovery.” ([Académie des sciences](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Curie/Curie_pubd1.htm))
- **1899:** *Les Rayons de Becquerel et le polonium*. A substantial synthetic article presenting Curie’s early radioactivity research to a wider scientific readership; it is not merely another edition of the shorter Academy notes. ([Roberto de Andrade Martins, study and bibliography](https://www.ghtc.usp.br/server/pdf/RAM-Curie.PDF))
- **1899:** *Sur la radio-activité provoquée par les rayons de Becquerel*. Joint experimental note by Marie and Pierre Curie on induced radioactivity. ([Académie des sciences](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Curie/Curie_pubd1.htm))
- **1899:** *Effets chimiques produits par les rayons de Becquerel*. Joint note by Marie and Pierre Curie on chemical effects of radioactive radiation. ([Académie des sciences](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Curie/Curie_pubd1.htm))
- **1899:** *Sur le poids atomique du métal dans le chlorure de baryum radifère*. Research note recording an intermediate attempt to determine the atomic weight of the new radioactive constituent in radium-bearing barium chloride. ([Académie des sciences](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Curie/Curie_pubd1.htm))
- **1900:** *Sur la pénétration des rayons de Becquerel non déviables par le champ magnétique*. Experimental note distinguishing radiation through its penetration and magnetic behavior. ([Académie des sciences](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Curie/Curie_pubd1.htm))
- **1900:** *Sur la charge électrique des rayons déviables du radium*. Joint note by Marie and Pierre Curie identifying the electrical charge carried by deflectable radium rays. ([Académie des sciences](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Curie/Curie_pubd1.htm))
- **1900:** *Sur le poids atomique du baryum radifère*. Another stage in Curie’s chemical separation and atomic-weight work; its object is still radium-bearing barium rather than fully isolated radium. ([Académie des sciences](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Curie/Curie_pubd1.htm))
- **1900:** *Les nouvelles substances radioactives*. A congress report and extended synthesis of the emerging field, published in the proceedings of the International Congress of Physics; it is a distinct obra, not simply a reprint of the discovery notes. ([Wikisource bibliographic record](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Auteur:Marie_Curie), [CNAM digitization](https://cnum.cnam.fr/pgi/redir.php?ident=8XAE489.3&onglet=c))
- **1902:** *Sur les corps radioactifs*. Joint note by Marie and Pierre Curie surveying properties of radioactive bodies. ([Académie des sciences](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Curie/Curie_pubd1.htm))
- **1902:** *Sur le poids atomique du radium*. Note reporting Curie’s improved atomic-weight determination; it should not be conflated with the differently titled 1899, 1900, and 1907 stages of the same continuing project. ([Le Moyne College scientific-text archive](https://webserver.lemoyne.edu/~giunta/ea/CURIEann.HTML))
- **1906:** *Sur la diminution de la radioactivité du polonium avec le temps*. Note measuring polonium’s declining activity; Pierre Curie presented it shortly before his death, but Marie Curie is the named author. ([Académie des sciences](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Curie/Curie_pubd1.htm))
- **1907:** *Sur le poids atomique du radium*. A new and more precise determination, distinct from Curie’s 1902 note of the same title. ([Académie des sciences](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Curie/Curie_pubd1.htm))
- **1907:** *Action de la pesanteur sur le dépôt de la radioactivité induite*. Experimental note on whether gravity affects the deposition of induced radioactivity. ([Académie des sciences](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Curie/Curie_pubd1.htm))
- **1907:** *Sur la condensation de la vapeur d’eau en présence de l’émanation du radium*. Experimental note on condensation in ionized air containing radium emanation. ([Académie des sciences](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Curie/Curie_pubd1.htm))
- **1908:** *Action de l’émanation du radium sur les solutions de sels de cuivre*. Joint note by Marie Curie and Ellen Gleditsch; the collaborative authorship matters because Gleditsch’s role is sometimes lost in summaries of Curie’s work. ([Académie des sciences](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Curie/Curie_pubd1.htm))
- **1908:** *Sur la formation de brouillards en présence de l’émanation du radium*. Experimental note on fog formation in ionized gas. ([Académie des sciences](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Curie/Curie_pubd1.htm))
- **1910:** *Sur le radium métallique*. Joint note by Marie Curie and André Debierne reporting the preparation of metallic radium; the material specimen and the paper are related but different obras. ([Académie des sciences](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Curie/Curie_pubd1.htm))
- **1925:** *Dosage de radium dans les minéraux d’urane qui contiennent du tantale, du niobium et du titane*. Analytical protocol in the form of a research note, describing the measurement of radium in chemically difficult uranium minerals. ([Académie des sciences](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Curie/Curie_pubd1.htm))
- **1930:** *Sur une relation entre la constante de désintégration des radioéléments émettant des rayons et leur capacité de filiation*. Joint theoretical note by Marie Curie and Georges Fournier. ([Académie des sciences](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Curie/Curie_pubd1.htm))
- **1930:** *Sur la vie moyenne de l’ionium*. Joint measurement note by Marie Curie and Sonia Cotelle. ([Académie des sciences](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Curie/Curie_pubd1.htm))
- **1930:** *Sur la relation entre l’émission de rayons α de long parcours et de rayons γ*. Research note connecting two forms of nuclear radiation. Some electronic indexes lose the Greek-letter expressions from the title; the Academy record preserves the work but displays the title imperfectly. ([Académie des sciences](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Curie/Curie_pubd1.htm))
- **1931:** *Spectre magnétique des rayons du dépôt actif de l’actinon*. Joint note by Marie Curie and Salomon Rosenblum. ([Académie des sciences](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Curie/Curie_pubd1.htm))
- **1932:** *Sur la structure fine du spectre magnétique des rayons du radioactinium*. Joint note by Marie Curie and Salomon Rosenblum. ([Académie des sciences](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Curie/Curie_pubd1.htm))
- **1933:** *Sur la structure fine du spectre magnétique des rayons du radioactinium et de ses dérivés*. Joint note by Marie Curie and Salomon Rosenblum, extending their earlier spectrum study. ([Académie des sciences](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Curie/Curie_pubd1.htm))

### Theses, treatises, lectures, and books

- **1903:** *Recherches sur les substances radioactives*. Curie’s doctoral thesis and a major synthetic monograph incorporating earlier results. Later French editions, translations, and the English title *Radio-Active Substances* are manifestations of this obra, not additional works. ([Project Gutenberg transcription](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/43233/old/43233-h/43233-h.htm))
- **1903:** *The Radio-Active Elements*. A general article for *The Independent*, made for a nonspecialist anglophone readership rather than as a journal edition of the thesis. ([Bibliographic description in Marilyn Bailey Ogilvie’s scholarly biography](https://pdfbiograph.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Marie-Curie_-A-Biography-Greenwood-Biographies-PDFbiograph.pdf))
- **1908:** *Préface des Œuvres de Pierre Curie*. Editorial preface to the collected scientific works of Pierre Curie; it frames and acts upon another scientist’s posthumous corpus rather than presenting new experimental results. ([Wikisource](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Auteur:Marie_Curie))
- **1910:** *Traité de radioactivité*. Two-volume scientific treatise organizing radioactivity as a coherent field. The two volumes are parts of one designed obra, although catalogues often count them separately. ([Wikisource](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Auteur:Marie_Curie))
- **1911:** *Radium and the New Concepts in Chemistry*. Curie’s Nobel lecture, delivered on 11 December 1911; it is both a scientific synthesis and a public claim concerning the history and conceptual ownership of radioactivity. ([Nobel Prize](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/1911/marie-curie/lecture/))
- **1921:** *La Radiologie et la guerre*. A book combining technical explanation, institutional record, and advocacy for medical radiology; parts of it function as protocol by instructing readers in X-ray production and practice. ([Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/44098/44098-h/44098-h.htm))
- **1921/1924:** *L’Isotopie et les éléments isotopes*. A lecture-derived scientific synthesis, issued in book form; sources differ between the date of the lecture or initial issue and the 1924 publication commonly catalogued, so the range is retained rather than silently choosing one date. ([Wikisource](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Auteur:Marie_Curie), [scholarly primary-source bibliography](https://pdfbiograph.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Marie-Curie_-A-Biography-Greenwood-Biographies-PDFbiograph.pdf))
- **1923–1924:** *Pierre Curie*. A biography and scientific memorial, first published in English in 1923 with Curie’s autobiographical notes and then in French in 1924. The language editions are manifestations of the same composite obra, whose boundaries include both a life of Pierre and Marie’s account of herself. ([Wikisource](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Auteur:Marie_Curie), [scholarly primary-source bibliography](https://pdfbiograph.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Marie-Curie_-A-Biography-Greenwood-Biographies-PDFbiograph.pdf))
- **1933:** *Les Rayons alpha, bêta, gamma des corps radioactifs en relation avec la structure nucléaire*. Late monograph synthesizing radioactive radiation and nuclear structure. ([Wikisource](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Auteur:Marie_Curie))

### Working texts and pedagogical records

- **1897–1900:** *Carnets de laboratoire de la découverte du polonium et du radium*. Three small black-covered laboratory notebooks jointly made and used by Marie and Pierre Curie. They resist treatment as finished works: they are primary working objects, recording measurements and procedures but not all the information later published. ([Irène Joliot-Curie’s study](https://stm.cairn.info/pierre-curie--9782415002893-page-121?lang=fr), [Musée Curie example](https://curiotheque.musee.curie.fr/s/en/item/16857))
- **1906–1907:** *Journal following Pierre Curie’s death*. A private bereavement diary rather than a work prepared for publication; later editors made it public, so the published book is a posthumous manifestation of an intimate manuscript that resisted “work” status when written. ([BnF Curie holdings overview](https://www.bnf.fr/fr/les-cahiers-de-marie-curie-conserves-la-bnf-sont-ils-radioactifs))
- **1907:** *Leçons de Marie Curie: Physique élémentaire pour les enfants de nos amis*. A sequence of cooperative physics lessons whose surviving text was recorded by her pupil Isabelle Chavannes, not written out as a book by Curie. The teaching is Curie’s obra, but the textual manifestation and later title are Chavannes’s record and a posthumous editorial construction. ([Wikisource author record](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Auteur:Marie_Curie))
- **1897–1934:** *Research notebooks, course plans, drafts, lectures, and correspondence*. An archival corpus rather than a single titled work: the BnF preserves sixty-nine notebooks and bound volumes plus manuscripts, typescripts, proofs, and letters. Calling the whole archive an obra is useful but unstable because many objects were working documents, communications, or records never intended as autonomous works. ([Anne Liskenne, “Le fonds Curie à la Bibliothèque nationale”](https://www.persee.fr/doc/gazar_0016-5522_1989_num_145_1_4137))

### Material preparations and scientific manifestations

- **1898:** *Polonium*. A discovered and named chemical element, not an object Curie “made” in the ordinary sense. The enriched bismuth-like fractions were laboratory preparations made by Marie and Pierre Curie, while the element itself is better classified as a natural manifestation whose presence their work disclosed; their 1898 claim did not yet amount to isolation of pure polonium. ([BnF](https://www.bnf.fr/fr/les-cahiers-de-marie-curie-conserves-la-bnf-sont-ils-radioactifs), [Académie des sciences](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Curie/Curie_pubd1.htm))
- **1898:** *Radium*. Likewise a discovered and named element rather than a made work; the increasingly concentrated radium-bearing preparations were made things, but the element announced with Pierre Curie and Gustave Bémont was inferred through radioactivity and spectroscopy before isolation. ([Académie des sciences](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Curie/Curie_pubd1.htm))
- **1898–1902:** *Concentrated radium salts*. Material research products obtained by repeated chemical treatment of pitchblende residues. They are neither texts nor mere “discoveries”: they are manufactured experimental objects through which radium’s existence, atomic weight, and properties became measurable. ([Curie’s thesis](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/43233/old/43233-h/43233-h.htm))
- **1910:** *Metallic radium*. A minute material preparation produced jointly by Marie Curie and André Debierne. It is a made specimen as well as the subject of their note, though later shorthand that Curie “isolated radium” can blur the distinction between the 1898 discovery, the purified salts, and this metallic preparation. ([Académie des sciences](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Curie/Curie_pubd1.htm))

### Wartime radiological project

- **1914–1918:** *Fixed and mobile military radiology service*. A project and operational system—equipment, vehicles, personnel, training, procedures, and journeys to field hospitals—rather than one invention or book. Curie organized and developed fixed installations and mobile X-ray units later nicknamed *petites Curies*; the individual vehicles were collaborative technical assemblages, so attributing every component to her alone would overstate authorship. ([Musée Curie](https://musee.curie.fr/.uploads/2022-10/5257_curie-expo-10-ans_ok_ok-rvb-ce9c3f83.pdf))
- **1916–1918:** *Radiology training courses for nurses and operators*. A pedagogical and institutional creation intended to make wartime radiology reproducible through trained practitioners; surviving notes, certificates, and recollections are manifestations of the program rather than the program itself. ([Musée Curie](https://musee.curie.fr/.uploads/2022-10/5257_curie-expo-10-ans_ok_ok-rvb-ce9c3f83.pdf))
- **1916–1918:** *Service d’émanation and radon ampoules*. A laboratory production service established under Curie for manufacturing sealed radon sources for military medicine. The service is the project; each ampoule was a collaboratively made therapeutic object, and claims then made for wound treatment should not be mistaken for present-day medical endorsement. ([Musée Curie](https://musee.curie.fr/blog/la-creation-de-la-fondation-curie-chronologie))

### Laboratories and institutions

- **1909–1914:** *Institut du Radium, Curie laboratory*. A long-term scientific project shaped and directed by Curie within an institution jointly created by the University of Paris and Institut Pasteur. The buildings were designed by others, so the institute is not straightforwardly her architectural work; her obra was the research program, laboratory organization, collections, standards, and community built there. ([Musée Curie](https://musee.curie.fr/.uploads/2018-01/04_de-l-institut-du-radium-a-l-institut-curie-ccf1c12a.pdf))
- **1920–1921:** *Fondation Curie*. An institutional project for joining laboratory research to cancer treatment, created collectively around the Radium Institute rather than authored by Curie alone. Her role makes it part of her body of work, but its legal, medical, and organizational form was necessarily collaborative. ([Musée Curie chronology](https://musee.curie.fr/blog/la-creation-de-la-fondation-curie-chronologie))
