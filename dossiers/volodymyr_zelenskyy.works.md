## Works by Volodymyr Oleksandrovych Zelenskyy

### Foundational projects and collective Gesamtkunstwerke

- **1997–2003:** *Kvartal 95 KVN team*. A comedy troupe led by Zelenskyy, who performed, choreographed, and wrote sketches; because the material was collectively devised and repeatedly altered in performance, the surviving television appearances resist treatment as individually fixed texts. ([Encyclopedia of the History of Ukraine](https://resource.history.org.ua/cgi-bin/eiu/history.exe?C21COM=S&FT_PREFIX=&FT_REQUEST=&I21DBN=EIU&P21DBN=EIU&S21ALL=%28%3C.%3ETRN%3Dzelenskyj_volodymyr%3C.%3E%29&S21CNR=20&S21FMT=eiu_all&S21REF=10&S21SRW=nz&S21STN=1&Z21ID=); [Kvartal 95 history](https://kvartal95.com/en/the-history-of-the-studio/))
- **2003–2019:** *Studio Kvartal 95*. A production project and corporate Gesamtkunstwerk co-founded by Zelenskyy, Serhii Shefir, and Borys Shefir to make television, film, animation, and live comedy; its hundreds of collective productions cannot automatically be counted as Zelenskyy’s individual works. ([Kvartal 95 history](https://kvartal95.com/en/the-history-of-the-studio/))
- **2003–2004:** *Holiday concert cycle*. Kvartal 95’s first television concert project, conceived for the major holidays of 2004; the studio history does not supply a stable collective title or itemize Zelenskyy’s individual writing. ([Kvartal 95 history](https://kvartal95.com/en/the-history-of-the-studio/))
- **2005–2019:** *Evening Quarter* (*Vechirnii Kvartal*). A continuing satirical concert-and-television Gesamtkunstwerk for which Zelenskyy was an ideological leader, presenter, performer, director, and member of the writing team; its sketches are collaborative manifestations rather than a single closed text. ([Kvartal 95 history](https://kvartal95.com/en/the-history-of-the-studio/); [Teletriumph credit record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volodymyr_Zelenskyy#Teletriumph))
- **2007:** *FilmKvartal*. A film-production project founded within Kvartal 95, with director-screenwriter Andrii Yakovliev heading production; Zelenskyy’s status is that of co-founder and studio art director, not sole maker of every subsequent film. ([Kvartal 95 history](https://kvartal95.com/en/the-history-of-the-studio/))
- **2011:** *Crack Them Up* (*Rozsmishy komika*). An original television-game format developed by Kvartal 95 and performed and presented by Zelenskyy; later international adaptations are manifestations of the format rather than new Zelenskyy-authored works. ([Kvartal 95 history](https://kvartal95.com/en/the-history-of-the-studio/))
- **2012:** *Evening Kyiv*. A variety and comedy television project presented by Kvartal 95, with Zelenskyy among its principal hosts and creative leaders; precise individual authorship of its constituent segments is not catalogued. ([Kvartal 95 history](https://kvartal95.com/en/the-history-of-the-studio/))
- **2015:** *League of Laughter* (*Liha smikhu*). A televised comedy competition initiated by Kvartal 95 and hosted by Zelenskyy; it is best classified as a repeatable format and performance framework, not a single authored text. ([Kvartal 95 history](https://kvartal95.com/en/the-history-of-the-studio/))

### Screen obra: writing, direction, or production

- **2004:** *Three Musketeers* (*Tri mushketery*). A television musical for which Zelenskyy received a writing credit and performed d’Artagnan; the screenplay was collaborative. ([selected filmography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volodymyr_Zelenskyy#Selected_filmography))
- **2005:** *Dinner Is Served!* (*Yisty podano!, or Oberezhno, kokhannia!*). A television comedy credited to Zelenskyy as one of its screenwriters. ([filmography record](https://petition.president.gov.ua/petition/45046))
- **2007:** *A Very New Year’s Movie, or Night at the Museum*. A television musical written collectively and featuring Zelenskyy as both screenwriter and performer. ([filmography record](https://petition.president.gov.ua/petition/45046))
- **2008–2017:** *Matchmakers* (*Svaty*). A multi-season television comedy series co-written and produced by Zelenskyy; catalogues sometimes split its seasons and specials into separate works, so the series is treated here as one serial obra. ([Encyclopedia of the History of Ukraine](https://resource.history.org.ua/cgi-bin/eiu/history.exe?C21COM=S&FT_PREFIX=&FT_REQUEST=&I21DBN=EIU&P21DBN=EIU&S21ALL=%28%3C.%3ETRN%3Dzelenskyj_volodymyr%3C.%3E%29&S21CNR=20&S21FMT=eiu_all&S21REF=10&S21SRW=nz&S21STN=1&Z21ID=); [filmography record](https://petition.president.gov.ua/petition/52272))
- **2009:** *Miracle* (*Chudo*). A television melodrama series produced by Zelenskyy with other Kvartal 95 producers. ([filmography record](https://petition.president.gov.ua/petition/52272))
- **2009 or 2010:** *How the Cossacks…* (*Yak kozaky…*). A television musical produced by and featuring Zelenskyy; available filmographies disagree between 2009 and 2010, so the dating remains unsettled here. ([petition filmography](https://petition.president.gov.ua/petition/45046); [producer filmography](https://www.kinomania.ru/person/vladimir-zelenskiy/films?type=producer))
- **2010:** *New Year’s Matchmakers* (*Novorichni svaty*). A television-film offshoot of *Matchmakers*, co-written and produced by Zelenskyy; some catalogues subsume it under the parent series. ([producer and screenplay catalogue](https://merlin-series.org.ua/stati/2020-01-30/uk/vladimirovic-volodimir-zelenskij-biografia-informacia-osobiste-zitta.html))
- **2010:** *Love in the Big City 2*. A romantic-comedy feature co-written by Zelenskyy, who also returned as Igor; his authorship was shared with a larger writing team. ([biographical filmography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volodymyr_Zelenskyy))
- **2011:** *Office Romance: Our Time*. A feature-film remake co-written and produced by Zelenskyy, who also played Anatoly Novoseltsev; it deliberately reworks Eldar Ryazanov’s earlier film and therefore resists classification as a wholly original narrative. ([Ukrainian film catalogue](https://kino-teatr.ua/uk/person_films/zelenskiy-volodimir-1624.phtml))
- **2011:** *Girl Hunt* (*Divoche polyuvannia*). A television series produced by Zelenskyy as part of a collective production group. ([filmography record](https://petition.president.gov.ua/petition/52272))
- **2011:** *Champions from the Yard* (*Chempiony z pidvorittia*). A sports television drama produced by Zelenskyy; sources variously call it a series or television film. ([filmography record](https://petition.president.gov.ua/petition/52272))
- **2011:** *Heavenly Relatives* (*Nebesni rodychi*). A fantasy-comedy television series carrying a Zelenskyy production credit; authorship is collective. ([producer-credit catalogue](https://merlin-series.org.ua/stati/2020-01-30/uk/vladimirovic-volodimir-zelenskij-biografia-informacia-osobiste-zitta.html))
- **2011:** *Mitya’s Tales* (*Baiky Mityaya*). A *Matchmakers* spin-off television series produced by Zelenskyy and collaborators. ([producer-credit catalogue](https://merlin-series.org.ua/stati/2020-01-30/uk/vladimirovic-volodimir-zelenskij-biografia-informacia-osobiste-zitta.html))
- **2011:** *Legend: Lyudmila Gurchenko*. A television tribute project for which Zelenskyy is credited as writer and producer in filmographic compilations; the exact division of writing among collaborators is not stated. ([producer and screenplay catalogue](https://merlin-series.org.ua/stati/2020-01-30/uk/vladimirovic-volodimir-zelenskij-biografia-informacia-osobiste-zitta.html))
- **2011–2012:** *Dads* (*Papashy/Tatky*). A comedy-drama television series produced by Zelenskyy; catalogues disagree whether its relevant production date is 2011 or 2012. ([filmography record](https://petition.president.gov.ua/petition/52272); [producer-credit catalogue](https://merlin-series.org.ua/stati/2020-01-30/uk/vladimirovic-volodimir-zelenskij-biografia-informacia-osobiste-zitta.html))
- **2011–2012:** *Mothers* (*Mamy/Mamochky*). A television series produced by Zelenskyy; both the translated title and year vary among catalogues. ([filmography record](https://petition.president.gov.ua/petition/52272); [producer-credit catalogue](https://merlin-series.org.ua/stati/2020-01-30/uk/vladimirovic-volodimir-zelenskij-biografia-informacia-osobiste-zitta.html))
- **2011:** *Matchmakers: Life without Makeup—Alexander Feklistov*. A documentary adjunct to *Matchmakers* produced by Zelenskyy; some filmographies omit it or treat it as promotional material rather than an independent work. ([producer-credit catalogue](https://merlin-series.org.ua/stati/2020-01-30/uk/vladimirovic-volodimir-zelenskij-biografia-informacia-osobiste-zitta.html))
- **2012:** *I’ll Be Around* (*Ya budu poruch*). A feature melodrama produced by Zelenskyy and collaborators; he did not direct or star in it. ([Ukrainian film catalogue](https://kino-teatr.ua/uk/person_films/zelenskiy-volodimir-1624.phtml))
- **2012:** *Rzhevsky Versus Napoleon*. A historical farce produced by Zelenskyy, who also manifested its Napoleon as a comic performance. ([selected filmography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volodymyr_Zelenskyy#Selected_filmography); [producer filmography](https://www.kinomania.ru/person/vladimir-zelenskiy/films?type=producer))
- **2012:** *8 First Dates*. A romantic-comedy feature co-written and produced by Zelenskyy, who played Nikita Sokolov; contemporary credits name a sizeable collaborative writing and production team. ([contemporary production notice](https://versii.cv.ua/wp-content/uploads/pdf/versii_10_427.pdf))
- **2014:** *Love in the Big City 3* (also distributed as *Love in Vegas*). A romantic-comedy sequel produced by Zelenskyy, who again performed Igor; English-language catalogues use competing titles. ([Kvartal 95 project record](https://kvartal95.com/en/the-history-of-the-studio/); [Ukrainian film catalogue](https://kino-teatr.ua/uk/person_films/zelenskiy-volodimir-1624.phtml))
- **2014:** *Looking for a Wife with a Child*. A television romantic comedy produced by Zelenskyy; the credit belongs to a production group rather than to a sole auteur. ([producer-credit catalogue](https://merlin-series.org.ua/stati/2020-01-30/uk/vladimirovic-volodimir-zelenskij-biografia-informacia-osobiste-zitta.html))
- **2014–2015:** *8 New Dates*. A sequel produced by and starring Zelenskyy; sources variously date it by its 2014 production or its January 2015 release. ([Ukrainian film catalogue](https://kino-teatr.ua/uk/person_films/zelenskiy-volodimir-1624.phtml); [selected filmography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volodymyr_Zelenskyy#Selected_filmography))
- **2015–2019:** *Servant of the People*. A three-season political-comedy series produced through Kvartal 95 and starring Zelenskyy as Vasyl Holoborodko. It became more than fiction when its title, persona, and anti-corruption rhetoric migrated into Zelenskyy’s electoral campaign, making it at once obra, political project, and manifesto. ([Encyclopedia of the History of Ukraine](https://resource.history.org.ua/cgi-bin/eiu/history.exe?C21COM=S&FT_PREFIX=&FT_REQUEST=&I21DBN=EIU&P21DBN=EIU&S21ALL=%28%3C.%3ETRN%3Dzelenskyj_volodymyr%3C.%3E%29&S21CNR=20&S21FMT=eiu_all&S21REF=10&S21SRW=nz&S21STN=1&Z21ID=); [Kvartal 95 history](https://kvartal95.com/en/the-history-of-the-studio/))
- **2016:** *8 Best Dates*. A romantic-comedy sequel co-written by and starring Zelenskyy; its screenplay credit is collaborative. ([screenplay catalogue](https://merlin-series.org.ua/stati/2020-01-30/uk/vladimirovic-volodimir-zelenskij-biografia-informacia-osobiste-zitta.html))
- **2016:** *Servant of the People 2*. A theatrical feature produced by and starring Zelenskyy; portions were subsequently incorporated into the television series, so film and serial manifestations are not wholly independent. ([Ukrainian film catalogue](https://kino-teatr.ua/uk/person_films/zelenskiy-volodimir-1624.phtml))
- **2016:** *Relatives* (*Rodychi*). A television series produced by Zelenskyy and Kvartal 95 collaborators. ([producer-credit catalogue](https://merlin-series.org.ua/stati/2020-01-30/uk/vladimirovic-volodimir-zelenskij-biografia-informacia-osobiste-zitta.html))
- **2016:** *Patsyky*. A television comedy series produced by Zelenskyy; English catalogues have no stable translated title. ([filmography record](https://petition.president.gov.ua/petition/52272))
- **2016:** *The Deputy* (*Deputatyk*). A political-comedy television series for which Zelenskyy is catalogued as screenwriter and producer; the individual extent of his writing within the Kvartal 95 team is unspecified. ([screenplay and producer catalogue](https://merlin-series.org.ua/stati/2020-01-30/uk/vladimirovic-volodimir-zelenskij-biografia-informacia-osobiste-zitta.html))
- **2018:** *Me, You, He, She*. A feature romantic comedy co-directed by Zelenskyy and David Dodson, co-written and produced by Zelenskyy, who also played Maksym Tkachenko; it was his feature-directing debut and is emphatically collaborative rather than sole-authored. ([Ukrainian cinema filmography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_cinema_since_independence); [Ukrainian film catalogue](https://kino-teatr.ua/uk/person_films/zelenskiy-volodimir-1624.phtml))
- **2021:** *Gulliver Returns*. An animated feature bearing Zelenskyy screenplay and producer credits, although it premiered after he had left Kvartal 95 for the presidency; the delayed release makes its apparent date later than the creative work behind it. ([Ukrainian film catalogue](https://kino-teatr.ua/uk/person_films/zelenskiy-volodimir-1624.phtml); [Kvartal 95 history](https://kvartal95.com/en/the-history-of-the-studio/))

### Performances and vocal manifestations without a securely catalogued maker-credit

- **2004:** *Casanova Unwillingly*. A television miniseries containing a Zelenskyy acting performance; this is a manifestation he helped realize, but the catalogue does not credit him as its maker. ([filmography record](https://petition.president.gov.ua/petition/45046))
- **2004:** *Police Academy*. A television series in which Zelenskyy acted; it should not be mistaken for the unrelated American film franchise or described as his authored work. ([filmography record](https://petition.president.gov.ua/petition/52272))
- **2008:** *Horton Hears a Who!* A Ukrainian-language dubbing performance as Mayor Ned McDodd, not authorship of the animated film itself. ([selected filmography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volodymyr_Zelenskyy#Selected_filmography))
- **2009:** *Love in the Big City*. A feature-film performance as the dentist Igor; although made by companies associated with his collaborators, the consulted filmography securely identifies Zelenskyy here as actor rather than screenwriter or director. ([selected filmography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volodymyr_Zelenskyy#Selected_filmography))
- **2014:** *1+1 at Home*. A television musical featuring Zelenskyy as a performer; the performance is his manifestation, while the complete program is collective. ([filmography record](https://petition.president.gov.ua/petition/45046))
- **2014:** *1+1 at Home 2: March 8*. A second television musical containing a Zelenskyy performance, again without a securely documented individual maker-credit. ([filmography record](https://petition.president.gov.ua/petition/45046))
- **2014:** *Once Upon a Time near Poltava*. A television comedy series for which Zelenskyy supplied off-screen narration; the series itself is not thereby an individually authored Zelenskyy work. ([filmography record](https://petition.president.gov.ua/petition/52272))
- **2014:** *Paddington*. A Ukrainian-language vocal performance as Paddington Bear; it is a localized manifestation of another film, not a new screenplay or film by Zelenskyy. ([selected filmography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volodymyr_Zelenskyy#Selected_filmography))
- **2016:** *The Angry Birds Movie*. A Ukrainian-language vocal performance as Red, again a localized manifestation rather than authorship of the underlying animation. ([selected filmography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volodymyr_Zelenskyy#Selected_filmography))
- **2017:** *Paddington 2*. A second Ukrainian-language performance as Paddington Bear. ([Ukrainian film catalogue](https://kino-teatr.ua/uk/person_films/zelenskiy-volodimir-1624.phtml))
- **2023:** *Same Here*. A Brad Paisley song containing a spoken-word exchange with Zelenskyy; his recorded voice is part of the released phonogram, but the song is Paisley’s obra and should not be silently reassigned to Zelenskyy. ([media record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volodymyr_Zelenskyy#Other_media))

### Political manifestos, protocols, and serial addresses

- **31 December 2018:** *Presidential candidacy announcement*. A televised declaration inserted into 1+1’s New Year broadcast: both manifesto and performative speech, because saying the words publicly initiated the candidacy rather than merely describing it. ([biographical account](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volodymyr_Zelenskyy))
- **2019:** *2019 presidential campaign*. A distributed transmedia project made from social-media videos, comedy performances, the *Servant of the People* persona, and a deliberately sparse conventional platform. Its authorship was collective and strategic, and the campaign resists treatment as a single stable work. ([biographical account](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volodymyr_Zelenskyy))
- **20 May 2019:** *Inaugural address*. A constitutional speech-act and manifesto that accepted the presidency and articulated an initial governing position; as an official address, it is likely staff-assisted rather than demonstrably written by Zelenskyy alone.
- **2019–present:** *Presidential speeches, statements, greetings, and video addresses*. An open, serial corpus comprising hundreds of individually dated texts and audiovisual manifestations in the Presidential Office archive. It resists being called a finite “work”: many items are institutionally drafted, translated, revised, or jointly issued, and the official catalogue continues to grow. ([official speeches archive](https://www.president.gov.ua/en/news/speeches); [official video archive](https://www.president.gov.ua/en/videos/videos-archive))
- **24 February 2022–present:** *Wartime nightly addresses*. A continuing sequence of short audiovisual manifestos begun with Russia’s full-scale invasion, combining presidential report, morale-building performance, diplomacy, and appeals for action. The sequence has no final title or closed extent and therefore remains a living political project rather than a completed obra. ([official video archive](https://www.president.gov.ua/en/videos/videos-archive))
- **2022–present:** *Addresses to foreign parliaments and international assemblies*. A series of venue-specific diplomatic manifestos, often constructed through historical and cultural allusion to the audience addressed. They are individually archived speeches but also manifestations of the larger wartime communications project; institutional co-authorship is not disclosed. ([official speeches archive](https://www.president.gov.ua/en/news/speeches))
