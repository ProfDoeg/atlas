## Works by Saint Acacius of Byzantium

### Attributed oral manifestations

- **Traditionally 303:** Confessions before Firmus, Bibianus, and Flaccinus. The surviving *Passio* represents Acacius answering interrogations and refusing sacrifice, but these are embedded hagiographical speeches—not independently transmitted texts—and the martyr acts are judged historically inauthentic; their wording therefore cannot securely be treated as Acacius’s own obra. ([Acta Sanctorum, 8 May](https://www.heiligenlexikon.de/ActaSanctorum/8.Mai.html); [Enciclopedia dei Santi, “Acacio”](https://azioneromana.com/article/acacio-acathius-acacius-santo-martire))

- **Traditionally 303:** Catechesis delivered to fellow prisoners while being taken to Byzantium. The *Passio* says that Acacius instructed prisoners who asked him to explain Christianity, but no separately titled or independently witnessed discourse survives; it resists classification as a work because it may be the later hagiographer’s composition rather than a recoverable performance by Acacius. ([Acta Sanctorum, §§14–15](https://www.heiligenlexikon.de/ActaSanctorum/8.Mai.html))

- **Traditionally 303:** Prayers during imprisonment, transport, and immediately before execution. These are presented as oral devotional manifestations within the *Passio*, not as writings, protocols, or fixed compositions issued by Acacius; their form and authorship remain inseparable from the unauthenticated martyr narrative. ([Acta Sanctorum, §§13, 18, 21](https://www.heiligenlexikon.de/ActaSanctorum/8.Mai.html))

### No securely attributable textual or material works

- No writing, building, artwork, object, or other independently attested obra by Acacius is identified in the standard hagiographical repertories. The sole Greek dossier item, BHG 13, is an anonymous *Passio* **about** him, preserved in several manuscripts, not a work made by him; the corresponding Oriental records are likewise narratives of his martyrdom (BHO 20–21), not an authorial corpus. ([Biblissima/Pinakes, BHG 13](https://portail.biblissima.fr/en/ark%3A/43093/tdatafdff3220a5983167ff5ee4d48ad9830c357861d7); [Bibliotheca Hagiographica Arabica, Acacius the Centurion](https://bha.uob-dh.org/?q=node%2F66857))
