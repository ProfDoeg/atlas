## Works by Max Glücksmann

### Motion pictures: fiction

- **1914:** *Amalia*. Silent feature produced by Glücksmann’s company from José Mármol’s novel; Enrique García Velloso directed, Eugenio Py photographed, and Glücksmann is variously credited with production, assistant direction, or editing. The surviving credits therefore describe a collaborative production rather than a film authored by him alone. [[1]](https://cinenacional.com/pelicula/amalia/) [[2]](https://www.retinalatina.org/peliculas/amalia/)
- **1915:** *Mariano Moreno y la Revolución de Mayo*. Silent historical feature produced and edited by Glücksmann, directed by Enrique García Velloso. It was conceived as the opening installment of a much larger historical cycle that was never completed. [[1]](https://cinenacional.com/persona/max-glucksmann) [[3]](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/185601/Documento_completo.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)

### An unfinished historical-film project

- **1915–undated:** *Historical cycle from Mariano Moreno to Caseros*. Project, not a completed film: contemporary publicity described a second part of *Mariano Moreno*, *La Asamblea del año 1813*, episodes of the independence struggle, *Facundo*, and the civil wars through Caseros. These projected installments should not be silently promoted into completed works. [[3]](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/185601/Documento_completo.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)

### Newsreels and actuality series

- **1913–early 1930s:** *Actualidades Argentinas* / *Actualidades Glücksmann* / *Glücksmann Journal*. A continuing silent newsreel rather than one stable, singular film; one catalogue estimates roughly 600 editions, while surviving reels and fragments bear variant series titles and numbering. Its extent is consequently approximate, and the corporate authorship of Casa Lepage/Cinematografía Max Glücksmann resists reduction to personal direction by Glücksmann. [[4]](https://www.cinemargentino.com/films/914988655-actualidades-argentinas) [[5]](https://www.scielo.br/j/er/a/wtkKt4R9kKM59vJvVPzcWVj/?lang=es)
- **Undated:** *Noticiero 1*. Silent short catalogued under Glücksmann as director; the generic archival title may identify a surviving reel rather than an original release title, and no firm date is supplied. [[1]](https://cinenacional.com/persona/max-glucksmann)
- **Undated:** *Noticiero 2*. Silent short catalogued under Glücksmann as director; as with *Noticiero 1*, the title and date are not securely established. [[1]](https://cinenacional.com/persona/max-glucksmann)
- **1928:** *Actualidades*. Short actuality film catalogued separately under Glücksmann’s direction, although its relationship to the continuing newsreel series is not clarified by the catalogue. [[1]](https://cinenacional.com/persona/max-glucksmann)
- **1931:** *Actualidades Glücksmann*. Surviving composite newsreel containing horse racing, traffic control, and activity at Buenos Aires’s Nogaró Hotel; its precise issue number is not identified. [[6]](https://www.archivorta.com.ar/produccion/max-glucksmann/)
- **20 February 1932:** *Actualidades Glucksmann n.º 169*. Incomplete newsreel issue documenting Agustín P. Justo’s assumption of the Argentine presidency. [[7]](https://www.archivorta.com.ar/produccion/max-glucksmann/page/2/)

### Documentary and commissioned films

- **January 1911:** *Inauguración del Hotel de Inmigrantes*. Surviving fragment of an actuality documenting President Roque Sáenz Peña’s visit to the new complex; the bracketed archival title is descriptive rather than demonstrably original. [[7]](https://www.archivorta.com.ar/produccion/max-glucksmann/page/2/)
- **23 February 1913:** *Concurso de ejercicios físicos organizados por el Club Mar del Plata bajo la dirección del Profesor Sr. Juan Rosi*. A 6-minute-40-second silent documentary micro-film signed on its opening intertitle by Casa Lepage de Max Glücksmann; scholarship treats it as part of his experimental informational cinema. [[5]](https://www.scielo.br/j/er/a/wtkKt4R9kKM59vJvVPzcWVj/?lang=es)
- **1913:** *Reparto de ropa a niños pobres en el Tigre*. Charity actuality attributed to Glücksmann’s production; survival and original release context are not specified in the cited historical survey. [[8]](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cine_de_Argentina)
- **1915:** *Propaganda cinematográfica chilena en la Exposición de San Francisco*. Short documentary credited to Max Glücksmann as production company in the research-based CineChile catalogue. [[9]](https://cinechile.cl/persona/max-glucksmann/)
- **1915:** *Un viaje al río Bermejo*. Travelogue surviving substantially but apparently not completely; the archive describes it as a company production, probably commissioned by Argentina’s Ministry of Public Works, making both its institutional purpose and exact authorship qualified. [[7]](https://www.archivorta.com.ar/produccion/max-glucksmann/page/2/)
- **19 July 1915:** *El 45º aniversario del Colegio Militar*. A 2-minute-20-second silent documentary item identified by an opening intertitle carrying the name *Glücksmann Journal*; it records a military procession and gymnastic exercises. [[5]](https://www.scielo.br/j/er/a/wtkKt4R9kKM59vJvVPzcWVj/?lang=es)
- **1916:** *Asociación asistencia a domicilio a enfermos pobres*. Institutional or charitable actuality attributed to Glücksmann; the cited historical account supplies neither a fuller title nor preservation status. [[8]](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cine_de_Argentina)
- **1919:** *Patronato de la infancia. Visita a escuela agrícola de Claypole, Provincia de Buenos Aires*. Institutional documentary attributed to Glücksmann’s production and concerned with a charitable educational visit. [[8]](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cine_de_Argentina)
- **1920:** *Regatas en el Río Luján*. Silent documentary short catalogued under Glücksmann as director. [[1]](https://cinenacional.com/persona/max-glucksmann)
- **1924:** *Gran Premio Jockey Club*. Actuality recording the race held at Palermo on 7 September; the archive assigns its production to Glücksmann. [[6]](https://www.archivorta.com.ar/produccion/max-glucksmann/)
- **1925:** *La recepción triunfal del Presidente Alessandri*, alternatively *La llegada a América del Presidente Alessandri*. Short documentary credited to the Max Glücksmann production company; the catalogue preserves two titles rather than choosing between them. [[9]](https://cinechile.cl/persona/max-glucksmann/)
- **26 July 1925:** *Turf. El Clásico Pueyrredón*. Actuality documenting the long-distance race at Palermo’s Hipódromo Argentino; the displayed title may be archival. [[7]](https://www.archivorta.com.ar/produccion/max-glucksmann/page/2/)
- **1926:** *A los toros..!*. Documentary of a Jockey Club employees’ celebration for the institution’s new intendente and subintendente, catalogued by the archive as a Glücksmann production. [[6]](https://www.archivorta.com.ar/produccion/max-glucksmann/)
- **1927:** *Los cadetes chilenos en Buenos Aires*. Short documentary credited to the Max Glücksmann production company in CineChile’s national filmography. [[9]](https://cinechile.cl/persona/max-glucksmann/)
- **1928:** *Enlace Bosch Grondona–Pereyra Iraola*. Society-news actuality made by Cinematografía Argentina Max Glücksmann; the archival description treats the wording as a title, but its original release form is uncertain. [[7]](https://www.archivorta.com.ar/produccion/max-glucksmann/page/2/)
- **17 October 1930:** *La construcción e inauguración del Subterráneo Lacroze (hoy Línea B)*. Detailed silent industrial and civic documentary made by Glücksmann’s Cinematográfica Argentina; the bracketed archival title describes both construction and inauguration. [[6]](https://www.archivorta.com.ar/produccion/max-glucksmann/)
- **1931:** *El Partido Socialista Independiente y su organización*. Silent political-institutional short attributed by the archive to Max Glücksmann; its surviving intertitles present the party’s organization and program, so it functions partly as propaganda rather than detached reportage. [[7]](https://www.archivorta.com.ar/produccion/max-glucksmann/page/2/)
- **8 December 1935:** *Inauguración del hipódromo de San Isidro*. Fragment of a documentary recording the inauguration; the date is exact, but the bracketed archival title is descriptive and the survival is incomplete. [[7]](https://www.archivorta.com.ar/produccion/max-glucksmann/page/2/)

### The film archive as an obra

- **Circa 1904–1944:** *The Casa Glücksmann film corpus*. A dispersed corporate body of news, documentary, institutional, advertising, and commissioned footage rather than a single authored work: in 1944 the Argentine state declared 20,000 metres of film made over approximately forty years by Casa Glücksmann to be of public interest and expropriated it. Because an estimated 90 percent of Argentine silent production is lost and many surviving notes lack original titles or series contexts, no honest catalogue can turn this corpus into a complete title-by-title personal filmography. [[10]](https://bicyt.conicet.gov.ar/fichas/produccion/838052) [[5]](https://www.scielo.br/j/er/a/wtkKt4R9kKM59vJvVPzcWVj/?lang=es)

### Recorded-sound production and publishing

- **From circa 1904; especially 1917–1930s:** *Discos Glücksmann / Disco Nacional-Glücksmann / Disco Nacional / Disco Nacional-Odeón*. A changing record label and vast production programme, not a musical composition by Glücksmann: his enterprise recorded, manufactured, published, and marketed performances by such artists as Carlos Gardel and José Razzano, Ignacio Corsini, Roberto Firpo, and Francisco Canaro. The individual matrices belong in performer and label discographies; treating every performance as Glücksmann’s personal work would confuse entrepreneurial production with musical authorship. [[11]](https://www.victorian-cinema.net/glucksmann.php) [[12]](https://buenosaires.gob.ar/gcaba_historico/cultura/museos/museocasacarlosgardel/discografia)
- **1924–1930:** *Concurso del Disco Nacional*. A recurring cultural-production project—seven Buenos Aires competitions rather than a text or composition—created to select, record, and promote new tangos; the winning works remain works of their named composers and lyricists, not Glücksmann compositions. [[13]](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Glucksmann)

### Architecture and exhibition Gesamtkunstwerk

- **1917–1919:** *Grand Splendid*. Commissioned theatre and cinema complex in Buenos Aires, opened on 14 May 1919: architects Peró and Armengol produced the project, Pizoney and Falcope constructed it, and Nazareno Orlandi decorated the dome. It is therefore an entrepreneurial Gesamtkunstwerk made under Glücksmann’s patronage—not a building he personally designed—and it joined architecture, theatre, cinema, recorded sound, and later broadcasting within his larger exhibition system. [[13]](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Glucksmann)

### Published text

- **19 June 1917:** *Películas cinematográficas*. Newspaper contribution signed by Max Glücksmann in Chile’s *La Nación*. The surviving catalogue record establishes its existence and authorship but does not provide enough bibliographical or textual information to decide whether it is an article, trade statement, or promotional intervention. [[9]](https://cinechile.cl/persona/max-glucksmann/)
