## Works by Joaquín Trincado Mateo

### Doctrinal and philosophical works published during his lifetime

- **1909–1911; published 1931:** *Buscando a Dios y asiento del Dios Amor*. A doctrinal work described as Trincado’s first book; the surviving bibliography distinguishes its period of composition from its much later publication. Its dialogic or purportedly mediated voices make individual authorship less straightforward than the title page’s attribution suggests. ([UNAM](https://www.iifl.unam.mx/esoterismo/fichaAutor.php?idEs=146), [EMECU bibliography](https://emedelaculuzyverdad.blogspot.com/p/bibliotecade-la-escuelamagnetico.html))

- **1914:** *Discurso del obispo Strossmayer*. A pamphlet issued within Trincado’s corpus but built around a speech attributed to Bishop Josip Juraj Strossmayer; it therefore resists classification as a text authored by Trincado and is better understood as an editorial or propagandistic appropriation. The EMECU catalogue records the pamphlet but does not resolve responsibility for its underlying text. ([EMECU bibliography](https://emedelaculuzyverdad.blogspot.com/p/bibliotecade-la-escuelamagnetico.html), [Galicia Martínez](https://www.scielo.sa.cr/pdf/rehmlac/v16n1/1659-4223-rehmlac-16-01-43.pdf))

- **1916; expanded editions 1919 and 1928:** *El magnetismo en su origen*, also catalogued as *Manual de educación magnética* and *El método supremo*. A practical and doctrinal manual on magnetic healing and spiritual education. The variant titles and substantially enlarged editions make it uncertain whether these should be counted as one evolving work or several textual states. ([UNAM](https://www.iifl.unam.mx/esoterismo/fichaAutor.php?idEs=146), [EMECU bibliography](https://emedelaculuzyverdad.blogspot.com/p/bibliotecade-la-escuelamagnetico.html))

- **1917 or January 1918:** *El primer rayo de luz*. A doctrinal introductory book. The scholarly account dates it to 1917, while the movement’s bibliography gives January 1918 as its publication date. ([Galicia Martínez](https://www.scielo.sa.cr/pdf/rehmlac/v16n1/1659-4223-rehmlac-16-01-43.pdf), [EMECU bibliography](https://emedelaculuzyverdad.blogspot.com/p/bibliotecade-la-escuelamagnetico.html))

- **August 1919:** *El espiritismo en su asiento*. A systematic statement intended to establish Trincado’s “Espiritismo Luz y Verdad” as the legitimate form of spiritism; it is simultaneously philosophical exposition and institutional foundation-text. ([EMECU bibliography](https://emedelaculuzyverdad.blogspot.com/p/bibliotecade-la-escuelamagnetico.html), [Galicia Martínez](https://www.scielo.sa.cr/pdf/rehmlac/v16n1/1659-4223-rehmlac-16-01-43.pdf))

- **1920:** *Filosofía austera racional*. A synthetic philosophical treatise presenting the principal cosmological, historical, ethical, and political claims of Trincadismo. ([Galicia Martínez](https://www.scielo.sa.cr/pdf/rehmlac/v16n1/1659-4223-rehmlac-16-01-43.pdf), [UNAM](https://www.iifl.unam.mx/esoterismo/fichaAutor.php?idEs=146))

- **January 1922:** *Los cinco amores*. A doctrinal and ethical treatise organized around forms of love; scholarship treats it as one of the representative texts of Trincadismo. ([EMECU bibliography](https://emedelaculuzyverdad.blogspot.com/p/bibliotecade-la-escuelamagnetico.html), [Galicia Martínez](https://www.scielo.sa.cr/pdf/rehmlac/v16n1/1659-4223-rehmlac-16-01-43.pdf))

- **Written date reported as 1915; published June 1922:** *Profilaxis de la vida*. A prescriptive work joining bodily hygiene, morality, and spiritual discipline—a protocol as much as a philosophical text. Movement sources disagree or fail to distinguish consistently between its composition and publication dates. ([EMECU biography](https://www.emedelacu.mx/post/biografia-joaquin-trincado), [EMECU bibliography](https://emedelaculuzyverdad.blogspot.com/p/bibliotecade-la-escuelamagnetico.html))

- **1922:** *El espiritismo estudiado, o Política del Creador y gobierno del Espiritismo*. A political-theological treatise that describes a cosmic government while prescribing the organization and conduct of spiritism. ([UNAM](https://www.iifl.unam.mx/esoterismo/fichaAutor.php?idEs=146), [Galicia Martínez](https://www.scielo.sa.cr/pdf/rehmlac/v16n1/1659-4223-rehmlac-16-01-43.pdf))

- **Written July 1913–March 1914; published June 1924; revised 1931:** *Conócete a ti mismo: Fisiología, fixiognosía y etnología del universo*. A pedagogical and encyclopedic doctrinal work. Its corrected second edition is a materially distinct textual state, although the collected bibliography counts both editions as one obra. ([EMECU transcription](https://www.geocities.ws/eme_dela_cu/conoceteatimismo/catcap000index.htm), [UNAM](https://www.iifl.unam.mx/esoterismo/fichaAutor.php?idEs=146))

- **June 1924:** *Alfaquí vademécum (El maestro va conmigo)*. A compact instructional handbook: its “vademécum” form makes it a portable protocol for adherents rather than simply a speculative treatise. ([EMECU bibliography](https://emedelaculuzyverdad.blogspot.com/p/bibliotecade-la-escuelamagnetico.html), [Galicia Martínez](https://www.scielo.sa.cr/pdf/rehmlac/v16n1/1659-4223-rehmlac-16-01-43.pdf))

- **1926–1930:** *Filosofía enciclopédica universal*, volumes I–II. The published beginning of a twelve-volume encyclopedic project: volume I appeared in 1926 and volume II in April 1930. It is simultaneously a pair of completed books and the fragment of a larger, unfinished Gesamtkunstwerk of cosmology, science, history, and doctrine. ([EMECU bibliography](https://emedelaculuzyverdad.blogspot.com/p/bibliotecade-la-escuelamagnetico.html))

- **Written 1914–1916; published 15 November 1929 or 1930:** *Los extremos se tocan: Epílogo de la guerra y prólogo de la paz*. A political-philosophical response to war and the projected reorganization of society. The institutional bibliography dates publication to 1929, while other movement documentation gives 1930. ([EMECU bibliography](https://emedelaculuzyverdad.blogspot.com/p/bibliotecade-la-escuelamagnetico.html), [EMECU biography](https://www.emedelacu.mx/post/biografia-joaquin-trincado), [UNAM](https://www.iifl.unam.mx/esoterismo/fichaAutor.php?idEs=146))

- **September 1934:** *Código de Amor Universal para el régimen de la Comuna de Amor y Ley*, volume I. A constitution-like protocol for communal life: it acts by legislating conduct and institutions rather than merely describing Trincado’s utopia. Movement sources say the work was conceived or written much earlier, but the first volume’s fixed publication date is 1934. ([EMECU bibliography](https://emedelaculuzyverdad.blogspot.com/p/bibliotecade-la-escuelamagnetico.html), [UNAM](https://www.iifl.unam.mx/esoterismo/fichaAutor.php?idEs=146))

- **February 1935:** *Cuestionario espírita-racionalista*. A catechetical teaching protocol, reportedly prepared for children, structured as questions and answers rather than as a continuous treatise. ([EMECU bibliography](https://emedelaculuzyverdad.blogspot.com/p/bibliotecade-la-escuelamagnetico.html), [Trincado study](https://es.scribd.com/document/94320427/Joaquin-Trincado-Mateo))

### Historical and biographical counternarratives

- **1914:** *La revolución de México y el crimen de Norteamérica: Juicio biológico, etno-ético y fisiológico*. A political-historical interpretation of the Mexican Revolution and a vehicle for Trincado’s developing project of the Comuna Universal. The academic study identifies 1914 as its composition date but also stresses that the Trincadista corpus has not yet received a complete scholarly systematization. ([Galicia Martínez](https://www.scielo.sa.cr/scielo.php?pid=S1659-42232024000100043&script=sci_arttext&tlng=es))

- *Jesús, hombre y no Dios*. A revisionist spiritual biography or anti-dogmatic counternarrative. Movement bibliographies name it as a separate work but do not supply a securely verified composition or first-publication date. ([EMECU biography](https://www.emedelacu.mx/post/biografia-joaquin-trincado), [summary bibliography](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joaqu%C3%ADn_Trincado_Mateo))

- *Vida de María, madre de Jesús*. A revisionist spiritual biography published posthumously around 1978–1979. Sources also call related material *Historia verdadera de María de Nazareth*, *La verdadera vida de María de Jericó*, or simply *Libro de María*; the available catalogues do not establish whether all these names denote one work, successive versions, or separate texts. ([EMECU bibliography](https://emedelaculuzyverdad.blogspot.com/p/bibliotecade-la-escuelamagnetico.html), [EMECU biography](https://www.emedelacu.mx/post/biografia-joaquin-trincado))

### Posthumously issued and unfinished textual projects

- **Published March 1976:** *Código de Amor Universal*, volume II. The second half of the communal code appeared four decades after Trincado’s death. Its posthumous publication establishes the manuscript’s claimed existence but leaves the degree of later editorial intervention undocumented in the consulted bibliography. ([EMECU bibliography](https://emedelaculuzyverdad.blogspot.com/p/bibliotecade-la-escuelamagnetico.html))

- **Published 1994:** *Tercera etapa: Año de la esperanza*, volume I. A posthumously issued prophetic and social text. Because publication occurred nearly sixty years after Trincado’s death and no critical edition is available, its final authorial state and editorial history remain uncertain. ([EMECU bibliography](https://emedelaculuzyverdad.blogspot.com/p/bibliotecade-la-escuelamagnetico.html))

- *Tercera etapa: Año de la esperanza*, volume II. An unpublished or incompletely disseminated continuation. It is a project whose existence is asserted by the movement’s collected-work catalogue, but its completeness and present manuscript status are not independently documented. ([EMECU bibliography](https://emedelaculuzyverdad.blogspot.com/p/bibliotecade-la-escuelamagnetico.html))

- *Filosofía enciclopédica universal*, volumes III–XII. Ten projected or manuscript volumes completing the twelve-volume encyclopedia. They resist treatment as ten securely established works: the institutional catalogue calls them unpublished but provides neither individual titles nor dates nor evidence that every volume reached the same degree of completion. ([EMECU bibliography](https://emedelaculuzyverdad.blogspot.com/p/bibliotecade-la-escuelamagnetico.html), [UNAM](https://www.iifl.unam.mx/esoterismo/fichaAutor.php?idEs=146))

- *El libro rojo*. An unpublished manuscript named in the institutional catalogue. No date, physical description, or reliable account of completeness is supplied, so it is evidence of a project rather than a securely recoverable finished book. ([EMECU bibliography](https://emedelaculuzyverdad.blogspot.com/p/bibliotecade-la-escuelamagnetico.html))

- *Memorial del Electromagno*. An unpublished work or manuscript project concerning Trincado’s “electromagno.” References in his published writings corroborate the title, but the catalogue supplies no date and does not establish whether a complete manuscript survives. ([EMECU bibliography](https://emedelaculuzyverdad.blogspot.com/p/bibliotecade-la-escuelamagnetico.html), [*El espiritismo en su asiento*](https://maestrosdelsaber.com/wp-content/uploads/ftp-files/Espiritismo/Trincado%2C%20Joaquin/Espiri%20Trincado%2C%20Joaquin%20-%20Espiritismo%20en%20su%20asiento.PDF))

- *Prontuario o resumen del credo*. An unpublished doctrinal digest—apparently a concise protocol or reference text. Its title is attested both in the institutional catalogue and inside another Trincado work, but its date and completeness remain unstated. ([EMECU bibliography](https://emedelaculuzyverdad.blogspot.com/p/bibliotecade-la-escuelamagnetico.html), [*El espiritismo en su asiento*](https://maestrosdelsaber.com/wp-content/uploads/ftp-files/Espiritismo/Trincado%2C%20Joaquin/Espiri%20Trincado%2C%20Joaquin%20-%20Espiritismo%20en%20su%20asiento.PDF))

- *Anatomía política*. An unpublished political manuscript or project listed by the movement’s bibliography without date, description, or evidence of completeness. It should not be silently promoted to the status of a finished book. ([EMECU bibliography](https://emedelaculuzyverdad.blogspot.com/p/bibliotecade-la-escuelamagnetico.html))

- *La justicia en acción*. A title mentioned by movement documentation among Trincado’s unpublished writings. It is absent from the shorter institutional catalogue, so its independence, survival, and completion are disputed or at least unverified. ([Trincado study](https://es.scribd.com/document/94320427/Joaquin-Trincado-Mateo), [*El espiritismo en su asiento*](https://maestrosdelsaber.com/wp-content/uploads/ftp-files/Espiritismo/Trincado%2C%20Joaquin/Espiri%20Trincado%2C%20Joaquin%20-%20Espiritismo%20en%20su%20asiento.PDF))

### Institutional and social projects

- **20 September 1911:** *Escuela Magnético-Espiritual de la Comuna Universal (EMECU)*. An organization, pedagogical system, transnational network, and vehicle for “Espiritismo Luz y Verdad,” not merely a text. Because doctrine, ritual, publishing, governance, symbols, and communal experiments operated together, the school approaches a social Gesamtkunstwerk. ([UNAM](https://www.iifl.unam.mx/esoterismo/fichaAutor.php?idEs=146), [IAPSOP](https://iapsop.com/archive/materials/balanza/))

- **1911–1935:** *La Comuna Universal*. A gradually elaborated social and political project rather than one discrete object: Trincado developed it across books, codes, institutions, and settlement experiments. Scholarship explicitly treats it as the central utopian project of his corpus. ([Galicia Martínez](https://www.scielo.sa.cr/scielo.php?pid=S1659-42232024000100043&script=sci_arttext&tlng=es))

- **1920 or 12 October 1921:** *Unión Hispano-Américo-Oceánica (UHAO)*. A Pan-Hispanic political and fraternal organization intended to join Spanish-speaking peoples under a common law. Sources disagree between 1920 and 1921 for its foundation or constitutive referendum, so neither date should be silently preferred. ([IAPSOP](https://iapsop.com/archive/materials/balanza/), [Intellectual Foundations of the Nicaraguan Revolution](https://dokumen.pub/intellectual-foundations-of-the-nicaraguan-revolution-9780292777170.html))

- **By 1935:** *Network of EMECU cátedras*. A transnational organizational creation rather than a single authored work. Accounts report more than 180 branches, but the exact total varies, and the individual branches were made collaboratively rather than by Trincado alone. ([IAPSOP](https://iapsop.com/archive/materials/balanza/), [UNICACH thesis](https://repositorio.unicach.mx/bitstream/handle/20.500.12753/4356/Tesis%20lista.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1))

- *Colonias Los Libertadores and Los Franciscos*. Communal-settlement projects attributed to Trincado in a scholarly thesis. The consulted source gives neither secure foundation dates nor enough evidence to determine how completely either settlement embodied his plan; these are collective manifestations of his project, not sole-authored objects. ([UNICACH thesis](https://repositorio.unicach.mx/bitstream/handle/20.500.12753/4356/Tesis%20lista.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1))

- **25 July 1931:** *Colonia Jaime*. A 607-hectare communal settlement in Santiago del Estero designed to put the Comuna de Amor y Ley into practice. It is a lived, collaborative social manifestation: Trincado directed its founding, but its members continually made and maintained it. ([UNICACH thesis](https://repositorio.unicach.mx/bitstream/handle/20.500.12753/4356/Tesis%20lista.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1), [IAPSOP](https://iapsop.com/archive/materials/balanza/))

### Periodical, protocols, and symbols

- **1933–1935 under Trincado’s direction:** *La Balanza*. The semimonthly official periodical of the EMECU, founded or directed by Trincado and continued after his death into the 1950s. The periodical database reports an apparent total of 272 issues, but that entire run cannot be counted as his sole creation; his own unsigned or signed contributions have not been separated in a critical bibliography. ([IAPSOP](https://iapsop.com/archive/materials/balanza/))

- *Reglamento interno*. An administrative protocol for the EMECU. Movement documentation attributes it to Trincado’s regulatory corpus but supplies no verified date or edition history. ([EMECU biography](https://www.emedelacu.mx/post/biografia-joaquin-trincado))

- *Estatutos*. The school’s constitutive rules: a performative legal text that helped make the institution it governed. Surviving descriptions do not establish whether Trincado alone authored every version. ([EMECU biography](https://www.emedelacu.mx/post/biografia-joaquin-trincado))

- *Reglamento y régimen de la Asamblea Comunal*. A protocol for communal deliberation and governance. No secure date or critical text is identified in the consulted catalogue. ([EMECU biography](https://www.emedelacu.mx/post/biografia-joaquin-trincado))

- *Reglamento de celadores*. An institutional protocol defining supervisory duties within the school. Its existence is recorded, but its date, version history, and precise authorship remain unestablished. ([EMECU biography](https://www.emedelacu.mx/post/biografia-joaquin-trincado))

- *Programa perpetuo de estudios*. A pedagogical protocol intended to organize continuing study in the EMECU. It is better classified as a curricular instrument than as a book. ([EMECU biography](https://www.emedelacu.mx/post/biografia-joaquin-trincado))

- *Circulares*. A continuing body of administrative and doctrinal communications rather than one work. The sources do not provide a complete item-level register, so treating “the circulars” as individually enumerated finished works would manufacture a false bibliography. ([EMECU biography](https://www.emedelacu.mx/post/biografia-joaquin-trincado), [IAPSOP](https://iapsop.com/archive/materials/balanza/))

- *Proclamas, tópicos, máximas, axiomas, consejos y recomendaciones*. Several kinds of short performative or instructional text named collectively in movement documentation. They resist work-level classification because no standard bibliography identifies their individual titles, dates, boundaries, or authorship. ([EMECU biography](https://www.emedelacu.mx/post/biografia-joaquin-trincado))

- **1920 or 1921:** *Bandera universal de siete colores de la UHAO*. A visual-political emblem joining the rainbow colors with the Tree of Guernica. It is a manifestation of the union project rather than a text; sources associate it with Trincado but disagree about the union’s foundational year and do not document whether he personally executed the design. ([IAPSOP](https://iapsop.com/archive/materials/balanza/))

- *Emblema de la EMECU*. An institutional graphic symbol listed among Trincado’s regulatory creations. Its authorship and date are not securely established, so it should be treated as an attributed design rather than an unquestioned autograph work. ([EMECU biography](https://www.emedelacu.mx/post/biografia-joaquin-trincado), [UNAM](https://www.iifl.unam.mx/esoterismo/fichaAutor.php?idEs=146))

### Inventions and incompletely documented technical work

- *Electrical domestic-heating invention and patent*. A technical invention whose defense reportedly contributed to Trincado’s financial collapse. The consulted historical periodical database mentions the patent but supplies neither a patent number nor an exact title or date; consequently, the invention can be acknowledged but not responsibly reconstructed as a named work. ([IAPSOP](https://iapsop.com/archive/materials/balanza/))

- *Plans for other inventions*. Movement biography says that plans for some of Trincado’s inventions were stolen. No titles, dates, patent records, or surviving objects are identified, so these resist being called recoverable works at all: they are reports of lost technical projects. ([EMECU biography](https://www.emedelacu.mx/post/biografia-joaquin-trincado))
