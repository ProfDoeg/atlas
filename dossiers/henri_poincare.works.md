## Works by Jules Henri Poincaré

### Mathematical and scientific corpus

- **1878–1913:** *Published scientific and intellectual corpus*. The standard Henri Poincaré Papers bibliography presently records 731 publications—research articles, notes, books, chapters, prefaces, reviews, speeches, and commissioned reports—including posthumous appearances and translations; it is therefore a bibliographic manifestation of Poincaré’s output, not 731 wholly independent obras. [Henri Poincaré Papers](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/bibliohp/index.php)

- **1879:** *Sur les propriétés des fonctions définies par les équations aux différences partielles*. Doctoral thesis and Poincaré’s first extended mathematical obra; some bibliographies modernize “différences” to “différentielles,” so the title is not transmitted uniformly. [Académie française](https://www.academie-francaise.fr/les-immortels/henri-poincare)

- **1879–1880:** *Notes on quadratic and cubic forms*. A connected early body of short mathematical communications rather than a single fixed-title work; Poincaré’s own retrospective bibliography begins with these notes. [Poincaré, *Analyse des travaux scientifiques*](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/chp/hp-pdf/hp1921am.pdf)

- **1881–1884:** *Memoirs on Fuchsian and zeta-Fuchsian functions*. A research project distributed across several papers, including *Sur la théorie des fonctions fuchsiennes*, *Mémoire sur les fonctions fuchsiennes*, and *Mémoire sur les fonctions zétafuchsiennes*; it resists identification with any one publication. [Henri Poincaré Papers](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/bibliohp/?a=on&action=go&yr=1884)

- **1881–1886:** *Mémoire sur les courbes définies par une équation différentielle* (four parts). A serial mathematical obra establishing a qualitative theory of differential equations; its four installments are manifestations of one sustained project.

- **1883:** *Sur les fonctions à espaces lacunaires*. Mathematical memoir on lacunary functions. [Académie française](https://www.academie-francaise.fr/les-immortels/henri-poincare)

- **1884:** *Sur les groupes des équations linéaires*. Long research memoir on groups associated with linear differential equations. [Henri Poincaré Papers](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/bibliohp/?a=on&action=go&yr=1884)

- **1886:** *Sur les intégrales irrégulières des équations linéaires*. Mathematical memoir published in *Acta Mathematica*. [Henri Poincaré Papers](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/bibliohp/?a=on&action=go&yr=1886)

- **1886:** *Sur les fonctions abéliennes*. Mathematical memoir published in the *American Journal of Mathematics*; a later paper bearing the same title appeared in 1902 and should not be silently collapsed into it. [Henri Poincaré Papers, 1886](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/bibliohp/?a=on&action=go&yr=1886) and [1902](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/bibliohp/?a=on&action=go&yr=1902)

- **1889–1890:** *Sur le problème des trois corps et les équations de la dynamique*. Prize memoir for King Oscar II’s competition, substantially corrected before its 1890 appearance in *Acta Mathematica* after an error was discovered; the submitted manuscript, corrected publication, and later reworkings are not identical manifestations. [Société Mathématique de France](https://smf.emath.fr/smf-dossiers-et-ressources/bibliographie-yoccoz-bnf-2005)

- **1892–1899:** *Les méthodes nouvelles de la mécanique céleste* (three volumes). Monumental mathematical treatise: volume I appeared in 1892, volume II in 1893, and volume III in 1899. The three volumes form one extended obra rather than unrelated books. [Henri Poincaré Papers](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/bibliohp/?a=on&action=go&yr=1892)

- **1895:** *Analysis situs*. Foundational memoir in topology, followed by five separately published complements; the project is larger than the text conventionally identified by the original title. [CNRS, *Textes d’histoire des mathématiques—Analysis Situs*](https://analysis-situs.pages.math.cnrs.fr/site/Textes-d-histoire-des-mathematiques.html)

- **1899:** *Complément à l’Analysis Situs*. First supplement to the 1895 memoir and part of the continuing topology project.

- **1900:** *Second complément à l’Analysis Situs*. Supplement introducing further topological machinery; it is a distinct paper but not an independent project. [CNRS, *Analysis Situs* bibliography](https://analysis-situs.pages.math.cnrs.fr/site/Textes-d-histoire-des-mathematiques.html)

- **1902:** *Sur certaines surfaces algébriques; troisième complément à l’Analysis Situs*. Third supplement to the topology project.

- **1902:** *Sur les cycles des surfaces algébriques; quatrième complément à l’Analysis Situs*. Fourth supplement to the topology project.

- **1904:** *Cinquième complément à l’Analysis Situs*. Final supplement, containing the question later called the Poincaré conjecture; the conjecture is a mathematical problem made within the memoir, not a separate book or freestanding text.

- **1900:** *Sur les rapports de la physique expérimentale et de la physique mathématique*. Congress report and programmatic essay on mathematical physics. [Henri Poincaré Papers](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/bibliohp/index.php?a=on&action=go&yr=1900)

- **1900:** *La théorie de Lorentz et le principe de réaction*. Research article on electrodynamics and the Lorentz theory.

- **1901:** *Sur une forme nouvelle des équations de la mécanique*. Short mathematical note formulating what became known as the Euler–Poincaré equations; it is a note, not a treatise. [Scholarly study of the note](https://arxiv.org/abs/1302.4096)

- **1905:** *Sur la dynamique de l’électron*. Brief communication to the Académie des sciences, sometimes called the “June note”; it is not identical to the longer 1906 memoir of the same title.

- **1906:** *Sur la dynamique de l’électron*. Substantial memoir published in the *Rendiconti del Circolo Matematico di Palermo*. Its relation to special relativity remains historiographically disputed, especially over priority and conceptual equivalence with Einstein’s work; the two same-titled texts must not be conflated.

- **1907:** *Sur l’uniformisation des fonctions analytiques*. Research memoir on uniformization.

- **1907:** *Les limites de la loi de Newton*. Scientific essay concerning gravitation and possible modifications of Newtonian dynamics.

- **1912:** *Sur un théorème de géométrie*. Poincaré’s last geometric paper, source of the result now called the Poincaré–Birkhoff theorem; proof and subsequent completion are historically associated also with George David Birkhoff, so the later theorem-name should not be treated as Poincaré’s original title.

### Courses made into books

- **1885–1886:** *Cours de mécanique physique et expérimentale*. A course-object generated through teaching and student or editorial redaction; the surviving printed course is therefore collaborative in textual formation even when catalogued under Poincaré’s name. [Académie française](https://www.academie-francaise.fr/les-immortels/henri-poincare)

- **1886:** *La cinématique pure, mécanismes, potentiel, mécanique des fluides*. Printed course edited by Amédée Guillet; authorship of the mathematics is Poincaré’s, while the book-form is an editorial manifestation of lectures. [Henri Poincaré Papers](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/bibliohp/index.php?a=on&action=go&jo=Cours+de+physique+math%C3%A9matique)

- **1886:** *Potentiel—Mécanique des fluides*. Course redaction edited by Amédée Guillet; its overlap with the larger 1886 course means it should not automatically be counted as a wholly separate intellectual work. [Henri Poincaré Papers](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/bibliohp/?a=on&action=go&yr=1886)

- **1889:** *Leçons sur la théorie mathématique de la lumière*, volume I. Course book edited by Jules Blondin from lectures of 1887–1888. [Henri Poincaré Papers](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/bibliohp/index.php?a=on&action=go&jo=Cours+de+physique+math%C3%A9matique)

- **1890:** *Électricité et optique*, volume I. Course book edited by Jules Blondin and Bernard Brunhes; it is a mediated lecture text rather than an unassisted authorial manuscript. [Henri Poincaré Papers](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/bibliohp/index.php?a=on&action=go&jo=Cours+de+physique+math%C3%A9matique)

- **1891:** *Électricité et optique*, volume II. Continuation of the mathematical-physics course, subsequently translated and revised; those later versions are manifestations, not new obras.

- **1892:** *Leçons sur la théorie mathématique de la lumière*, volume II. Course on diffraction and Helmholtz’s theory of dispersion, completing but substantially extending the first volume. [Numdam](https://numdam.org/item/PHSC_1998-1999__3_2_61_0/)

- **1892:** *Thermodynamique*. Course redaction from lectures delivered in 1888–1889; the revised 1908 edition is a later manifestation, not securely a new work.

- **1892:** *Leçons sur la théorie de l’élasticité*. Printed lecture course in mathematical physics.

- **1893:** *Théorie des tourbillons*. Course book based on lectures from the second semester of 1891–1892. [Académie française](https://www.academie-francaise.fr/les-immortels/henri-poincare)

- **1894:** *Les oscillations électriques*. Course-derived book on electrical oscillations.

- **1895:** *Capillarité*. Lecture course based on teaching from 1888–1889. [Académie française](https://www.academie-francaise.fr/les-immortels/henri-poincare)

- **1895:** *Théorie analytique de la propagation de la chaleur*. Course book based on lectures of 1893–1894.

- **1896:** *Calcul des probabilités*. Sorbonne lecture course; revised editions alter the manifestation and should not be counted as independently conceived books. [Académie française](https://www.academie-francaise.fr/les-immortels/henri-poincare)

- **1899:** *Théorie du potentiel newtonien*. Mathematical-physics lecture course.

- **1899:** *La théorie de Maxwell et les oscillations hertziennes*. Course-derived exposition of electromagnetism; its English adaptation appeared as *Maxwell’s Theory and Wireless Telegraphy*.

- **1901:** *La lumière et les théories électrodynamiques*. Revised and enlarged course synthesis drawing on lectures delivered in 1888, 1890, and 1899; its relation to the earlier light and electricity courses is revisionary rather than wholly independent.

- **1902:** *Figures d’équilibre d’une masse fluide*. Printed course or monograph on rotating fluid masses. [Henri Poincaré Papers](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/bibliohp/?a=on&action=go&yr=1902)

- **1904:** *Cours d’électricité théorique: étude de la propagation du courant en période variable sur une ligne munie de récepteur*. Specialized instructional text associated with the École professionnelle supérieure des postes et des télégraphes. [Henri Poincaré Papers](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/bibliohp/index.php?a=on&action=go&yr=1904)

- **1905–1910:** *Leçons de mécanique céleste* (three volumes). Multi-volume lecture treatise, published successively rather than made as three unrelated books.

- **1911:** *Leçons sur les hypothèses cosmogoniques*. Lecture-based scientific synthesis on theories of cosmic formation.

### Philosophical and public-facing obras

- **1898:** *La mesure du temps*. Philosophical article examining temporal measurement and simultaneity; it later became material for a book chapter but first existed as an independent periodical essay. [Henri Poincaré Papers](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/bibliohp/?page=4&t=on)

- **1902:** *La Science et l’hypothèse*. Philosophical book assembled in part from earlier essays and lectures; it is nevertheless a deliberately organized book-obja rather than merely a reprint container. [Henri Poincaré Papers](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/bibliohp/?a=on&action=go&yr=1902)

- **1905:** *La Valeur de la science*. Philosophical book on objectivity, mathematics, space, and physics; several chapters had prior periodical lives, making the volume both an obra and a recomposed manifestation.

- **1908:** *Science et méthode*. Philosophical and methodological book incorporating the lecture *L’invention mathématique* and other previously circulating texts; the collection is a new arrangement but not wholly new prose.

- **1908:** *L’invention mathématique*. Lecture to the Institut général psychologique and subsequently a chapter of *Science et méthode*; it is simultaneously an oral event, a periodical text, and a book component.

- **1910:** *Savants et écrivains*. Collection of speeches, notices, and essays on intellectual and literary figures; its materials originated as occasional texts, so the volume is a composite obra.

- **1910:** *Ce que disent les choses*. Popular-science book made for younger or general readers; it is exposition rather than a research treatise.

- **1911:** *Les sciences et les humanités*. Public lecture or essay defending scientific education and its relation to humane culture; its force is argumentative, though it is not properly a manifesto.

- **1913:** *Dernières pensées*. Posthumous collection of philosophical essays. The constituent texts are Poincaré’s, but the final book-object appeared after his death and should not be treated without qualification as a collection whose definitive architecture he completed.

### Self-accounting, reports, prefaces, and institutional texts

- **1884:** *Notice sur les travaux scientifiques de M. Poincaré*. First printed self-account of his research; it is an autobiographical scientific inventory rather than a research monograph. [Henri Poincaré Papers](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/bibliohp/?a=on&action=go&yr=1884)

- **1886:** *Notice sur les travaux scientifiques de Henri Poincaré*. Expanded self-bibliographical account; it revises the 1884 notice and therefore is neither simply the same text nor an unrelated work. [Henri Poincaré Papers](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/bibliohp/?a=on&action=go&yr=1886)

- **Written before 1912; published 1921:** *Analyse des travaux scientifiques de Henri Poincaré faite par lui-même*. Extended intellectual self-portrait and bibliography, published posthumously; the text is Poincaré’s, while its 1921 public form is editorial. [Henri Poincaré Papers](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/bibliohp/?a=on&action=go&yr=1921)

- **1899:** *Préface* to Félix Tisserand’s *Leçons sur la détermination des orbites*. A commissioned framing text, not Poincaré’s book and not properly separable from the book-object for which it was made. [Henri Poincaré Papers](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/bibliohp/?page=4&t=on)

- **1900:** *Lettre à M. P. Painlevé* concerning the Dreyfus proceedings. A letter made to intervene in a legal-political controversy and subsequently printed in the trial record; as an act within a proceeding it approaches a manifesto or forensic intervention more than a descriptive essay. [Henri Poincaré Papers](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/bibliohp/index.php?a=on&action=go&yr=1900)

- **1903–1905:** *Rapports relatifs à la fondation Jean Debrousse*. Three commissioned institutional reports, dated 1 April 1903, 23 March 1904, and 15 March 1905, later printed together; they are administrative acts rather than scientific works. [Henri Poincaré Papers](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/bibliohp/?a=on&action=go&yr=1905)

- **1904:** *Notice sur Alfred Cornu*. Memorial-biographical notice; it also appeared inside the commemorative book *Alfred Cornu, 1841–1902*, so notice and host volume must not be confused. [Henri Poincaré Papers](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/bibliohp/index.php?a=on&action=go&yr=1904)

- **Undated composition; published 1921:** *Rapport sur les travaux de M. Cartan*. Institutional evaluation written for the Paris Faculty of Sciences and printed only after Poincaré’s death; it is an expert act, not a jointly authored mathematical work with Cartan. [Henri Poincaré Papers](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/bibliohp/?a=on&action=go&yr=1921)

- **Various dates:** *Thesis reports*. Poincaré participated in numerous Paris thesis defenses, and thirty-eight reports on theses or defenses survive in the scholarly archive; these texts resist the category “work” because they are procedural evaluations produced by an institution. [Henri Poincaré Papers](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/)

### Correspondence and unpublished working objects

- **1870s–1912:** *Correspondence*. More than 2,000 surviving private, administrative, and scientific letters form a heterogeneous archival body rather than a single authored book; individual letters are acts directed to recipients, and their survival is incomplete. [Henri Poincaré Papers](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/)

- **1880–1889; published 1921:** *Letters to Lazarus Fuchs and Gösta Mittag-Leffler concerning Fuchsian functions and the King Oscar II memoir*. Scientific correspondence later printed in *Acta Mathematica*; the publication is posthumous, and the letters’ dates—not 1921—are the dates of their making. [Henri Poincaré Papers](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/bibliohp/?a=on&action=go&yr=1921)

- **Various dates:** *Autograph manuscripts, drafts, course notes, and calculations*. These are working objects and unfinished projects held and selectively edited by the Henri Poincaré Papers; many lack fixed titles or publication dates, and it would falsify their status to promote every sheet into a completed “work.” [Henri Poincaré Papers](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/)

### Editorial manifestations not made as new works by Poincaré

- **1916–1956:** *Œuvres de Henri Poincaré* (eleven volumes). Posthumous collected-works edition published under the auspices of the Académie des sciences; indispensable as a scholarly corpus, but the selection, arrangement, and eleven-volume form are editorial creations rather than an eleven-volume obra designed by Poincaré.

- **1921:** *Des fondements de la géométrie*. Posthumous book-form compilation; the underlying texts are Poincaré’s, but the volume itself is an editorial manifestation appearing nine years after his death. [Henri Poincaré Papers](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/bibliohp/?a=on&action=go&yr=1921)

- **Translations and revised editions, various dates:** *Foreign-language and revised manifestations of Poincaré’s writings*. These belong to the transmission history of his obras, but translations, reprints, extracts, and differently paginated editions are not additional things he independently made; the standard bibliography includes some of them and must therefore be read critically rather than counted mechanically. [Henri Poincaré Papers bibliography](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/en/bibliohp/index.php)
