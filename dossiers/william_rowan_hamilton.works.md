## Works by Sir William Rowan Hamilton

### Books and sustained treatises

- **1824–1833:** *Theory of Systems of Rays*. A foundational optical project that began as the unpublished memoir *Caustics* (communicated in 1824), was substantially rewritten, and appeared as the 1828 essay plus three separately published supplements (1830, 1831, 1833). Its unprinted second and third parts resist treatment as completed works: Hamilton redirected much of their substance into the supplements and his dynamics papers. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html); [Wilkins, “Theory of Systems of Rays”](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/Rays/)
- **1853:** *Lectures on Quaternions*. A book and systematic exposition of the mathematical method Hamilton had announced in 1843 and taught in successive Trinity College lecture courses from 1848 onward. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html); [digitized first edition](https://books.google.com/books?id=TCwPAAAAIAAJ)
- **1858–1865; published 1866:** *Elements of Quaternions*. Hamilton’s final book-project, substantially but not completely prepared when he died; his son William Edwin Hamilton edited and completed it for posthumous publication, so the published book is not an unmediated authorial object. [Wilkins, “Hamilton’s Research on Quaternions”](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/Quaternions.html); [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)

### Optical memoirs, papers, and notices

- **1830:** *Review of Two Scientific Memoirs of James MacCullagh*. A critical review rather than an original mathematical memoir. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1832:** *On a View of Mathematical Optics*. A concise report or manifestation of the optical theory developed in *Theory of Systems of Rays*. [Wilkins, mathematical papers](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/Papers.html)
- **1833:** *On Some Results of the View of a Characteristic Function in Optics*. A research report connecting Hamilton’s characteristic function with optical phenomena. [Wilkins, mathematical papers](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/Papers.html)
- **1833:** *On a New Method of Investigating the Relations of Surfaces to the Directions of the Rays*. An optical paper developing the geometry of wave and ray surfaces. [Wilkins, mathematical papers](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/Papers.html)
- **1833:** *Note on Mr. Potter’s Reply*. A brief intervention in a published optical controversy, not a freestanding research programme. [*Mathematical Papers*, vol. I contents](https://books.google.com/books?id=-vI8AAAAIAAJ)
- **1833:** *Remarques de M. Hamilton, Directeur de l’Observatoire de Dublin, sur un Mémoire de M. Plana*. A French-language critical note on Plana’s optical memoir. [Wilkins, mathematical papers](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/Papers.html)
- **1833:** *On a General Method of Expressing the Paths of Light, and of the Planets, by the Coefficients of a Characteristic Function*. An expository bridge between optics and dynamics rather than merely an optical paper. [Wilkins, “Hamilton’s Contributions to Dynamics”](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/Dynamics.html)
- **1838:** *On the Propagation of Light in Vacuo*. A short theoretical paper. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1838:** *On the Propagation of Light in Crystals*. A short paper on crystalline optics. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1841:** *On a Mode of Deducing the Equation of Fresnel’s Wave*. An optical derivation published as a paper. [*Mathematical Papers*, vol. I contents](https://books.google.com/books?id=-vI8AAAAIAAJ)
- **1831–1833?:** *Optical Investigations*. A manuscript research object first printed by the editors of Hamilton’s collected papers; its dating is editorial and partly uncertain. [*Mathematical Papers*, vol. I contents](https://books.google.com/books?id=-vI8AAAAIAAJ)
- **1833?:** *The Aberrations of an Optical Instrument of Revolution*. A manuscript study whose date is explicitly uncertain in the collected-works edition. [*Mathematical Papers*, vol. I contents](https://books.google.com/books?id=-vI8AAAAIAAJ)
- **undated:** *Two Letters to Professor Phillips on the Construction of Object-Glasses*. Epistolary technical texts preserved and published as optical work; they resist classification as papers because they were made as correspondence. [*Mathematical Papers*, vol. I contents](https://books.google.com/books?id=-vI8AAAAIAAJ)
- **1859:** *On the Application of Quaternions to the Geometry of Fresnel’s Wave-Surface*. A hybrid optical–quaternion paper. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)

### Dynamics, mechanics, and the hodograph

- **1834:** *On a General Method in Dynamics; by Which the Study of the Motions of All Free Systems of Attracting or Repelling Points Is Reduced to the Search and Differentiation of One Central Relation, or Characteristic Function*. A major research memoir introducing Hamilton’s characteristic-function treatment of dynamics. [Wilkins, “On a General Method in Dynamics”](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/Dynamics/)
- **1834:** *On the Application to Dynamics of a General Mathematical Method Previously Applied to Optics*. A British Association report: an abstracted public manifestation of the larger dynamics project, not a separate full theory. [Wilkins, “On a General Method in Dynamics”](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/Dynamics/)
- **1835:** *Second Essay on a General Method in Dynamics*. A second major memoir, introducing the principal function and the canonical equations now bearing Hamilton’s name. [Wilkins, “On a General Method in Dynamics”](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/Dynamics/)
- **1836:** *On a General Method in Dynamics*. A further report or précis of the project rather than a third full memoir. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1836:** *On the Calculus of Principal Relations*. A paper extending the characteristic- and principal-function methods. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1846:** *On Two Theorems of Central Forces*. A communicated research notice. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1846:** *A New Method of Expressing in Symbolical Language the Newtonian Law of Attraction*. A paper introducing the circular hodograph formulation. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1847:** *On a Theorem of Hodographic Isochronism*. A research communication delivered in two stages. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1847:** *On the Application of the Calculus of Quaternions to the Theory of the Moon*. A quaternion treatment of lunar dynamics. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1863:** *On a General Centre of Applied Forces*. A brief mechanics communication, surviving in the Academy’s proceedings rather than as a developed memoir. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)

### Algebra, analysis, equations, and probability

- **1830:** *On the Error of a Received Principle of Analysis, Respecting Functions Which Vanish with Their Variables*. A short counterexample paper about a smooth non-analytic function. [Wilkins, “On the Error of a Received Principle of Analysis”](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/Received/)
- **1831:** *Note to a Paper on the Error of a Received Principle of Analysis*. A corrective supplement acknowledging Cauchy’s prior use of the example. [Wilkins, “On the Error of a Received Principle of Analysis”](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/Received/)
- **1834:** *On Conjugate Functions, or Algebraic Couples*. A British Association paper formulating complex numbers as ordered couples and relating them to imaginary logarithms. [digitized report](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/BACouples/BARep34B.pdf)
- **1835:** *Theory of Conjugate Functions, or Algebraic Couples; with a Preliminary and Elementary Essay on Algebra as the Science of Pure Time*. A philosophical-mathematical memoir whose preliminary essay makes it more than a technical paper: it is also a foundational project for algebra. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1835:** *On a New Theory of Logologues*. A short symbolic-algebra paper. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1838:** *On the Argument of Abel, Respecting the Impossibility of Expressing a Root of Any General Equation above the Fourth Degree, by Any Finite Combination of Radicals and Rational Functions*. A critical reconstruction of Abel’s impossibility argument. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1842:** *On Fluctuating Functions*. A memoir in real analysis whose “functions” include objects later mathematics would treat with greater caution; it is therefore also a conceptual project rather than only a solved theorem. [Wilkins, “Hamilton’s Papers on Real Analysis”](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/Analysis.html)
- **1842:** *Supplementary Remarks on Fluctuating Functions*. A communicated supplement to the preceding memoir. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1842:** *On a Mode of Expressing Fluctuating Functions by Formulæ*. A shorter published manifestation of the fluctuating-functions project. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1843:** *On a Theorem of Differences*. A short analysis paper. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1843:** *Investigations Connected with the Calculus of Probabilities*. A probability paper or report. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1843:** *On the Reduction of the General Equation of the Fifth Degree*. A paper in the theory of equations; its title describes a reduction, not a claim to solve the general quintic by radicals. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)

### Quaternions and symbolical geometry

- **1843:** *On a New Species of Imaginary Quantities Connected with a Theory of Quaternions*. The first formal communication of the quaternion discovery, presented to the Royal Irish Academy; its proceedings text is a notice rather than the whole subsequent theory. [Wilkins, “Hamilton’s Research on Quaternions”](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/Quaternions.html)
- **1844–1850:** *On Quaternions; or, on a New System of Imaginaries in Algebra*. A serial obra issued in numerous instalments in the *Philosophical Magazine*, including Hamilton’s letter to John T. Graves; the instalments belong to one evolving exposition rather than constituting separate books. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1844–1845:** *On Quaternions*. Two British Association reports, brief public manifestations of the emerging calculus. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1844–1846; published 1847:** *On Quaternions*. Three Royal Irish Academy communications, dated 11 November 1844, 23 June 1845, and 20 July 1846; related titles and subject do not erase their status as distinct communicated notices. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1845; privately printed, later recorded:** *Abstract of a Memoir on Quaternions*. A privately circulated abstract not received in time for the Academy appendix; Graves records copies among the Hamilton papers, making its incomplete public status part of the object. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1845; privately printed, later recorded:** *Abstracts of Memoirs on the New Imaginaries*. Abstracts of communications made on 14 and 21 July 1845 but omitted from the Academy appendix; they survive as privately printed liminal objects rather than ordinary publications. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1845; published 1847:** *Illustrations from Geometry of the Theory of Algebraic Quaternions*. A geometrical exposition preserved in the appendix to the Academy proceedings. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1845; published 1847:** *On the Application of the Method of Quaternions to Some Dynamical Questions*. A two-part communication joining the new calculus to dynamics. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1845; published 1847:** *Additional Applications of the Theory of Algebraic Quaternions*. A supplementary Academy communication. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1846–1849:** *On Symbolical Geometry*. A serial paper developing a coordinate-free geometrical language, issued in instalments in the *Cambridge and Dublin Mathematical Journal*. [Wilkins, “Hamilton’s Research on Quaternions”](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/Quaternions.html)
- **1847:** *On an Isoperimetrical Problem Treated by the Calculus of Quaternions*. A short British Association report. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1847:** *On Some Applications of the Calculus of Quaternions to the Theory of the Moon*. A British Association notice related to, but bibliographically distinct from, the Academy lunar-theory communication. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1847; published 1850:** *On Additional Applications of Quaternions to Surfaces of the Second Order*. A geometrical communication. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1848; published 1850:** *On Quaternions and the Rotation of a Solid Body*. A substantial proceedings paper applying quaternions to rigid-body rotation. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1848; published 1850:** *On Quaternions and the Determination of the Distances of Any Recently Discovered Comet or Planet*. A short astronomical application. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1848:** *Researches Respecting Quaternions, First Series*. A major Academy memoir; “First Series” announces a continuing project, but no comparably titled second series was completed and published by Hamilton. [Wilkins, “Hamilton’s Research on Quaternions”](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/Quaternions.html)
- **1848; published 1850:** *On Quaternions Applied to Problems Respecting the Construction of a Circle Touching Three Given Circles on a Sphere, and of a Sphere Touching Four Given Spheres*. A concise construction problem treated quaternionically. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1849:** *Exercises in Quaternions*. A pedagogical problem-set or protocol for mathematical practice, not merely a descriptive paper. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1849:** *On Some New Applications of Quaternions to Geometry*. A British Association report. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1852–1853:** *On Continued Fractions in Quaternions*. A serial investigation published in several instalments. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1851:** *On a Proof from Quaternions of the Celebrated Theorem of Joachimsthal*. A brief Academy communication. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1851; published 1853:** *On the Nature and Properties of the Aconic Function of Six Vectors*. A quaternion-analysis paper. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1851–1852; published 1853:** *On the Connexion of Quaternions with Continued Fractions, and Quadratic Equations*. A communication delivered in two stages. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1852:** *On Biquaternions*. A short British Association report introducing an extension of quaternion coefficients. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1853:** *Sur les Quaternions*. A French-language exposition, not a translation edition of *Lectures on Quaternions*. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1853:** *On the Geometrical Interpretation of Some Results Obtained by Calculation with Biquaternions*. A short geometrical paper. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1853:** *On the Geometrical Demonstration of Some Theorems by Means of the Quaternion Analysis*. An Academy paper. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1854:** *On Some Extensions of Quaternions*. An Academy notice and a longer, separately manifested serial paper in the *Philosophical Magazine*; the shared title covers related but not textually identical objects. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1854:** *On an Extension of Quaternions*. A British Association abstract of the extension project. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1855; published 1858:** *Symbolical Extensions of Quaternions; and Geometrical Applications of Quaternions*. Academy communications recorded across several proceedings notices rather than one continuous memoir. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)
- **1855:** *On the Conception of the Anharmonic Quaternion, and on Its Application to the Theory of Involution in Space*. A British Association research report. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)

### The Icosian Gesamtkunstwerk

- **1856:** *Memorandum Respecting a New System of Roots of Unity*. A one-page paper announcing the Icosian calculus, a noncommutative symbolic system modeled on paths through the icosahedron or dodecahedron. [Wilkins, “The Icosian Calculus”](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/Icosian/)
- **1856; published 1858:** *Account of the Icosian Calculus*. An Academy notice recording the new calculus. [Wilkins, “The Icosian Calculus”](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/Icosian/)
- **1857:** *On the Icosian Calculus*. A British Association presentation and printed report. [Wilkins, “The Icosian Calculus”](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/Icosian/)
- **1857:** *The Icosian Game*. A Gesamtkunstwerk joining mathematical calculus, planar projection, lithographed diagram, puzzle protocol, and a manufactured board with holes and ivory pegs; Hamilton sold its copyright, and commercial versions appeared as *The Icosian Game* and *A Voyage Round the World*. The surviving commercial object is therefore a collaborative manifestation involving its manufacturer, not simply a conventional authored text. [Wilkins, “The Icosian Calculus”](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/Icosian/); [Robert Stawell Ball on Hamilton](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GreatAst/GreatAst.html)

### Addresses, reviews, and institutional texts

- **1835:** *The Power of Social Sympathy as an Impulse for the Promotion of Science*. An address to the British Association that acts institutionally—welcoming and exhorting a scientific community—rather than merely describing a mathematical result; in that limited sense it approaches a manifesto. [Ingleby, “The Late Sir William Rowan Hamilton”](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/BritCon/BritCon.html)
- **1837–1845:** *Presidential Addresses to the Royal Irish Academy*. Institutional performances made during Hamilton’s presidency; not all have stable independent titles, and they should not be forced into the shape of separately authored books. [Memorial Address on Hamilton](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/Eloge/Eloge.html)
- **various dates:** *Reviews, replies, reports, and notices*. Graves’s bibliography includes small interventions whose identity is sometimes only the journal heading or the paper to which Hamilton responded; these resist elevation into autonomous “works,” although they remain authored textual acts. [Graves bibliography](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/GBiblio/GBiblio.html)

### Lecture courses, correspondence, poetry, and manuscript projects

- **1827–1865:** *Astronomy Lectures at Trinity College Dublin*. A recurring pedagogical project and oral performance, only fragments or published introductory versions of which survive as fixed texts; it is not one book and has no single settled title. [Trinity College Dublin, Hamilton materials](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/)
- **1848–1853 and later:** *Courses of Lectures on Quaternions*. Successive performed courses that fed into, but are not identical with, the 1853 book; their status is that of a continuing teaching project. [first-edition description](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL6938414M/Lectures_on_quaternions)
- **throughout his life:** *Poems*. Hamilton made a substantial body of verse, much of it occasional or circulated in correspondence and never established by him as a collected book; without a scholarly variorum or item-level catalogue, it resists honest enumeration as a set of fixed, titled works. [Pritchard on Hamilton](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/HamMNRAS/HamMNRAS.html)
- **throughout his adult life:** *Correspondence*. Thousands of letters constitute an epistolary body of work, but most are relational documents rather than works Hamilton presented as publications; later selected editions are editorial creations and should not be counted as additional Hamilton obras. [Trinity College Dublin, Hamilton materials](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/)
- **1820s–1865:** *Mathematical and scientific notebooks*. More than two hundred manuscript volumes preserve calculations, drafts, unfinished investigations, and daily intellectual work. They are material research objects and projects, not a single authored “text,” and the collected-works editors selected rather than exhaustively published their contents. [review of *Mathematical Papers*, vol. II](https://doi.org/10.1038/146180a0)
- **various dates:** *Unpublished mathematical manuscripts*. The collected-works edition prints a substantial but explicitly selective body of previously unpublished work in optics, dynamics, algebra, geometry, analysis, astronomy, and probability; editorial titles and conjectural dates sometimes belong to the modern edition rather than to Hamilton. Consequently these manuscripts cannot responsibly be converted into a supposedly complete list of authorially fixed works. [Wilkins, mathematical papers](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Hamilton/Papers.html); [Cambridge, *Mathematical Papers*, vol. IV](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/mathematical-papers-of-sir-william-rowan-hamilton/4A4706FB22240A756B66F091CC1BA9B0)
