## Works by Jair Messias Bolsonaro

### Articles and opinion texts

- **3 September 1986:** *O salário está baixo*. Signed opinion article published in *Veja* no. 939, protesting military pay; its authorship and date are confirmed by the contemporaneous record of the Brazilian Senate. ([Senado Federal](https://www25.senado.leg.br/web/atividade/pronunciamentos/-/p/pronunciamento/20142))

- **15 April 1995:** *A praga dos servidores*. Newspaper opinion article attributed to Bolsonaro by the Senate’s institutional bibliography. ([Biblioteca Digital do Senado Federal](https://www2.senado.leg.br/bdsf/browse?type=author&value=Bolsonaro%2C+Jair))

- **26 September 2011:** *Comissão de retaliação*. Opinion article published in *O Globo* and catalogued under Bolsonaro’s authorship by the Senate’s institutional bibliography. ([Biblioteca Digital do Senado Federal](https://www2.senado.leg.br/bdsf/browse?type=author&value=Bolsonaro%2C+Jair))

- **20 April 2015:** *Revisão do estatuto do desarmamento*. Newspaper opinion article catalogued under Bolsonaro’s authorship by the Senate’s institutional bibliography. ([Biblioteca Digital do Senado Federal](https://www2.senado.leg.br/bdsf/browse?type=author&value=Bolsonaro%2C+Jair))

### Disputed project

- **October 1987:** *Operação Beco Sem Saída*. An alleged project for exploding low-powered bombs at military installations and the Guandu water-supply conduit, documented in sketches published by *Veja*. Bolsonaro denied making the project or drawings; two forensic reports attributed the handwriting to him, while other examinations found that authorship could not be established conclusively, so this resists classification as a securely attributable work. ([Veja](https://veja.abril.com.br/revista-veja/fantasmas-do-passado/), [Folha de S.Paulo](https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/poder/2017/05/1884033-bolsonaro-admitiu-atos-de-indisciplina-e-deslealdade-no-exercito.shtml))

### Legislative projects

- **1991–2018:** *Parliamentary propositions of Jair Bolsonaro*. A body of legislative projects rather than a single authored literary work: a scholarly study identified 162 bills, while broader counts reach 172 projects when complementary bills and constitutional amendments are included; the Câmara’s complete authorship search also contains amendments, requests, indications and other procedural texts, many co-authored or drafted with legislative staff. The discrepancy is therefore classificatory, not safely resolvable as a single total. ([Câmara dos Deputados](https://www.camara.leg.br/buscaProposicoesWeb/resultadoPesquisa?autor=Jair+Bolsonaro&emtramitacao=Todas&page=false&tipoproposicao=%5B%5D), [Ricciele da Silva, *Da Diferença à Equivalência*](https://www.scielo.br/j/dados/a/PD35TLXnCHDWr5LZ53hv3Vq/?format=pdf))

### Campaign projects and protocols

- **2018:** *O caminho da prosperidade: proposta de plano de governo*. An 81-page campaign project registered with the Tribunal Superior Eleitoral for Bolsonaro’s presidential candidacy. It sets out intended governmental action and is therefore both project and political protocol; the document belongs to the candidacy and campaign team, so Bolsonaro’s sole textual authorship should not be presumed. ([TSE registration copy](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/candidaturas/oficial/2018/BR/BR/2022802018/280000614517/proposta_1534284632231.pdf))

- **2022:** *Pelo bem do Brasil: plano de governo 2023–2026*. A 48-page reelection-campaign project submitted in Bolsonaro’s name and organized as directions for a prospective second administration. Like the 2018 plan, it is an institutional campaign document whose individual passages cannot safely be assigned to Bolsonaro personally. ([document copy](https://static.poder360.com.br/2022/08/plano-de-governo-bolsonaro-definitivo.pdf))

### Presidential messages

- **February 2019:** *Mensagem ao Congresso Nacional 2019*. Constitutionally prescribed presidential message and governmental report, formally authored by “Brasil. Presidente (2019–2022: Jair Messias Bolsonaro)” and additionally indexed under Bolsonaro’s name; it is an institutional, collaboratively produced text rather than demonstrably his unaided composition. ([Biblioteca Digital do Senado Federal](https://www2.senado.leg.br/bdsf/handle/id/553350))

- **2020:** *Mensagem ao Congresso Nacional 2020*. Annual institutional message presented under Bolsonaro’s presidency, combining a report on government activity with a programme for the legislative year. Its status is presidential document, not securely personal authorship.

- **2021:** *Mensagem ao Congresso Nacional 2021*. Annual institutional message of the Presidency to Congress. It acts both as governmental account and agenda-setting protocol, but the Presidency—not Bolsonaro alone—is the responsible producing body.

- **2022:** *Mensagem ao Congresso Nacional 2022*. Final annual message of Bolsonaro’s term, formally issued by the Presidency as a report and programme of government; its institutional compilation and production make individual authorship indeterminate. ([Presidência da República](https://www.gov.br/planalto/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/acoes-e-programas/governanca/mensagem-ao-congresso-nacional/MensagemaoCongressoNacional2022.pdf))

### Speeches and official manifestations

- **1 January 2019–31 December 2022:** *Discursos do Presidente da República, Jair Bolsonaro*. The Presidential Library preserves this as an extensive body of individually dated speech transcripts, beginning with the two inauguration addresses and encompassing ceremonies, international meetings, policy launches and improvised remarks. These are manifestations performed by Bolsonaro, but the archive does not identify speechwriters, editors or the degree of improvisation, so “delivered by” is firmer than “written by.” ([Presidential Library: speeches](https://www.biblioteca.presidencia.gov.br/presidencia/ex-presidentes/bolsonaro/discursos), [inaugural congressional address](https://www.biblioteca.presidencia.gov.br/presidencia/ex-presidentes/bolsonaro/discursos/discurso-do-presidente-da-republica-jair-bolsonaro-durante-cerimonia-de-posse-no-congresso-nacional))

- **20 February 2019–31 December 2022:** *Pronunciamentos oficiais do Presidente Jair Bolsonaro*. A separately catalogued corpus of national radio-and-television addresses and other formal presidential statements. These are performative state texts—manifestations that announce, justify or enact political positions—but institutional preparation and occasional joint delivery prevent an assumption of sole literary authorship. ([Presidential Library: official addresses](https://www.biblioteca.presidencia.gov.br/presidencia/ex-presidentes/bolsonaro/pronunciamentos-oficiais))
