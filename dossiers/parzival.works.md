## Works by Parzival

### Performative utterance

- **13 May 848, according to Werner Greub; dating disputed:** *The redeeming question* (descriptive title). Parzival’s question to the suffering Anfortas is an utterance that acts: it heals Anfortas and effects Parzival’s accession as Grail king (Wolfram, *Parzival*, 794.27–796.27). It resists classification as an obra or manifesto because the critical text presents a single speech act, not an independently transmitted work; the date belongs solely to Greub’s contested attempt to read the romance as ninth-century history.

### No attributed material or textual works

- No securely attributable obra, project, building, protocol, literary text, or Gesamtkunstwerk is assigned to Parzival in Wolfram’s *Parzival*. The Grail temple mentioned at 816.15 already exists when Parzival becomes Grail king; Wolfram neither describes its construction nor names Parzival as its maker. The elaborate constructed Grail temple belongs instead to the later *Jüngerer Titurel*, traditionally associated with “Albrecht,” and scholarship cautions that it is not Wolfram’s work or evidence for a building by Parzival.
