## Works by Carl Gustav Jung the elder

### Medical and anatomical writings

- **1816:** *De evolutione corporis humani*. Jung’s Latin doctoral dissertation on the development of the human body; surviving biographical sources agree on the title and year, although it falls outside the later Basel anatomical bibliography. ([Kurz and Sasse](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7978334/))
- **1823:** *Können in Basel die nöthigsten Hülfsanstalten zur Förderung medizinischer Studien gegründet werden?* An anonymously published programmatic pamphlet advocating the infrastructure required for medical teaching in Basel; its attribution to Jung is supplied by Wilhelm His’s bibliography and retained by Heller. ([Heller, no. 189](https://karger.com/aan/article-pdf/8/Suppl.%2011/11/2129948/000142013.pdf))
- **30 August 1827:** *Animadversiones quaedam de ossibus geminatis et in specie de ossibus raphegeminantibus, quae vulgo ossa suturarum dicuntur*. A Latin medical rectoral programme on sutural or Wormian bones. The typography of the compound anatomical term varies in later transcriptions, so the title is retained in Heller’s bibliographical form. ([Heller, no. 190](https://karger.com/aan/article-pdf/8/Suppl.%2011/11/2129948/000142013.pdf))
- **26 September 1828:** *Über das Verhältnis der Anatomie zu der medizinischen Wissenschaft und über die Leistungen der Anatomen an der Baseler Hochschule*. A rectoral address on anatomy’s relation to medicine and the achievements of Basel anatomists: both ceremonial speech and institutional manifesto. ([Heller, no. 191](https://karger.com/aan/article-pdf/8/Suppl.%2011/11/2129948/000142013.pdf))
- **1834:** *Über die Bildung der Nägel*. A short anatomical communication on the formation of nails, printed in the proceedings of Basel’s natural-science society. ([Heller, no. 194](https://karger.com/aan/article-pdf/8/Suppl.%2011/11/2129948/000142013.pdf))
- **1835:** *Schleimbälge des äußeren Gehörganges und in den Nymphen*. A proceedings paper on mucous follicles of the external auditory canal and the labia minora. ([Heller, no. 198](https://karger.com/aan/article-pdf/8/Suppl.%2011/11/2129948/000142013.pdf))
- **1836:** *Beobachtungen über die Verwundbarkeit des Herzens bei Thieren*. An experimental report on wounds to animals’ hearts; its identity is that of a society communication rather than a freestanding book. ([Heller, no. 199](https://karger.com/aan/article-pdf/8/Suppl.%2011/11/2129948/000142013.pdf))
- **1837–1838:** *Muskulatur eines Leoparden*. A jointly authored anatomical communication by Jung and Martin Nusser on a leopard’s musculature; responsibility should therefore not be assigned to Jung alone. ([Heller, no. 203](https://karger.com/aan/article-pdf/8/Suppl.%2011/11/2129948/000142013.pdf))
- **1838:** *Über die Structur des Ammonshorns*. A journal article on the structure of the hippocampus, published in Johannes Müller’s anatomical archive. ([Heller, no. 207](https://karger.com/aan/article-pdf/8/Suppl.%2011/11/2129948/000142013.pdf))
- **1839:** *Über den Bau des Menschengehirns im Allgemeinen und die Wurzeln des Gewölbes im Besonderen*. A proceedings paper on the human brain generally and the roots of the fornix particularly. ([Heller, no. 209](https://karger.com/aan/article-pdf/8/Suppl.%2011/11/2129948/000142013.pdf))
- **1839:** *Über die Verschiedenheit menschlicher Schädelformen*. A proceedings communication on variation in human skull forms. ([Heller, no. 210](https://karger.com/aan/article-pdf/8/Suppl.%2011/11/2129948/000142013.pdf))
- **1840:** *Untersuchung eines Orang-Utang-Schädels*. A comparative-anatomical examination of an orangutan skull, preserved as a report in the Basel society proceedings. ([Heller, no. 211](https://karger.com/aan/article-pdf/8/Suppl.%2011/11/2129948/000142013.pdf))
- **1841:** *Über die Bildung des Schädels bei Idioten*. A proceedings communication on cranial formation in people then described by that historical medical term. The title is retained as bibliographical evidence, not endorsed as present-day terminology. ([Heller, no. 213](https://karger.com/aan/article-pdf/8/Suppl.%2011/11/2129948/000142013.pdf))
- **1843:** *Über die seitliche Erhabenheit in dem Lateral-Ventrikel des menschlichen Gehirns*. A separately printed anatomical study of an elevation in the brain’s lateral ventricle. ([Heller, no. 215](https://karger.com/aan/article-pdf/8/Suppl.%2011/11/2129948/000142013.pdf))
- **1845:** *Über das Gewölbe in dem menschlichen Gehirn*. A separately printed study of the cerebral fornix. ([Heller, no. 220](https://karger.com/aan/article-pdf/8/Suppl.%2011/11/2129948/000142013.pdf))
- **1845:** *Demonstration eines Mikroskopes*. A documented scientific demonstration rather than a conventional authored text; the proceedings notice is the manifestation through which the event survives bibliographically. ([Heller, no. 221](https://karger.com/aan/article-pdf/8/Suppl.%2011/11/2129948/000142013.pdf))
- **1848:** *Über das fünfte Nervenpaar im Allgemeinen und über die Heilung eines Falles von Gesichtsschmerz*. A clinical-anatomical communication on the trigeminal nerve and a treated case of facial pain. ([Heller, no. 229](https://karger.com/aan/article-pdf/8/Suppl.%2011/11/2129948/000142013.pdf))
- **1848:** *Von dem äußeren Ohr und seinen Muskeln beim Menschen*. A proceedings paper on the human external ear and its muscles. ([Heller, no. 230](https://karger.com/aan/article-pdf/8/Suppl.%2011/11/2129948/000142013.pdf))

### Drama

- **1831:** *Die Revolution: Originaldrama in drei Acten*. A three-act political drama issued under the name “Demius.” Later biographical bibliographies attribute the pseudonym to Jung, but the printed object itself does not disclose him, so the attribution is not self-authenticating. ([Dvorak-based works list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Gustav_Jung#Works); [contemporary-edition description](https://www.iberlibro.com/Revolution-Originaldrama-drei-Acten-Demius-T%C3%BCbingen/30177748400/bd))

### Manuscript records and journey writing

- **1844–1860:** *Reisetagebücher*. A surviving group of manuscript travel diaries, catalogued as a single archival unit; it is a serial, private record rather than one finished publication, and the catalogue does not warrant inventing separate titles for its constituent journeys. ([Universitätsbibliothek Basel, NL 34:5](https://www.e-manuscripta.ch/bau/nav/content/798220))
- **begun c. 1850; continued after 1864:** *Catalog der Sammlung für normale menschliche Anatomie*. A manuscript protocol/catalogue initiated by Jung for Basel’s normal-human-anatomy collection. Only its oldest portions are in his hand, while Wilhelm His and others continued it as late as about 1904, so the extant 105-leaf volume resists treatment as Jung’s completed work alone. ([Universitätsbibliothek Basel, NL 34:17](https://www.e-manuscripta.ch/bau/texte/content/titleinfo/797419))

### Institutional obra and projects

- **1824 onward:** *Anatomisches Museum Basel*. An anatomical teaching collection and museum founded by Jung from the university’s holdings: an institutional obra assembled through specimens, cataloguing, teaching and administration rather than a text. Later custodians necessarily transformed it, so neither its contents nor its surviving catalogues are exclusively his manufacture. ([Kurz and Sasse](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7978334/))
- **1820s–1830s:** *Reform of medical teaching at the University of Basel*. A sustained institutional project encompassing the revival of the medical faculty, curricular reform and improved teaching facilities. It has no single fixed title or terminal object, and therefore resists classification as one autonomous “work.” ([Historical Dictionary of Switzerland](https://hls-dhs-dss.ch/de/articles/014424/2006-08-24/))
- **1820s–1850s:** *Expansion of Basel’s Bürgerspital*. A hospital-development project promoted by Jung rather than a solely authored object; the historical record credits him with initiating its enlargement but does not support attributing the whole institution to him. ([Historical Dictionary of Switzerland](https://hls-dhs-dss.ch/de/articles/014424/2006-08-24/))
- **1857:** *Anstalt zur Hoffnung*. A charitable and educational institution for children with intellectual disabilities whose foundation Jung instigated. It is a collective social creation—not a text and not safely describable as Jung’s sole work—even though his initiative belongs in an account of what he made possible. ([Historical Dictionary of Switzerland](https://hls-dhs-dss.ch/de/articles/014424/2006-08-24/))
