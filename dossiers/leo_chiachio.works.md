## Works by Leo Chiachio

### Scope and authorship

- **1969–present:** *Leo Chiachio’s oeuvre*. The 2024 scholarly retrospective catalogue is the closest thing to a catalogue raisonné, but it is selective rather than complete. It documents Chiachio chiefly through **Chiachio & Giannone**, the affective and artistic unit he formed with Daniel Giannone in 2003; consequently, the jointly authored works below should not be reassigned to Chiachio alone, and no authoritative inventory of his earlier solo production was found. ([*Vivir sus vidas* catalogue](https://core.cambeiro.com.ar/0-229248-0.pdf), [artist biography and bibliography](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/cv-english/))

### Foundational works and early “paintings with thread”

- **2003:** *Hechizo*. Acrylic and embroidery on a reclaimed mattress—the couple’s first joint work and an obra that is also a bodily trace, likened in the retrospective literature to a domestic shroud. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2005-03/), [Ruth Benzacar](https://ruthbenzacar.com/en/artists/chiachio-giannone/))
- **2004:** *Próceres*. A hybrid drawing: graphite on paper joined to hand embroidery. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2005-03/))
- **2004:** *Sebastianos*. A large hand-embroidered denim obra; a different textile polyptych with the same title appeared in 2024, so the title does not designate one unique object across the oeuvre. ([artist work register, 2003–05](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2005-03/), [artist work register, 2024](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2024-2/))
- **2004:** *Fortuna y abundancia*. Variable-sized cotton-thread embroidery made with a punch/“feather” needle. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2005-03/))
- **2005:** *Chinitos Kids*. Hand embroidery on a pillowcase: domestic linen made into a queer family image. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2005-03/))
- **2005:** *Marineritos*. Embroidery, graphite, and applied stones on fabric. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2005-03/))
- **2005–06:** *La conquista*. A textile diptych; the artist register gives 2005 while the 2024 retrospective labels it 2005–06, so the dating is not uniform. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2005-03/), [retrospective account](https://www.arte-online.net/Notas/Chiachio-Giannone))
- **2005:** *Bagno*. Permanent-marker drawings on shower curtains, with variable dimensions—an installation-like domestic support rather than a conventional drawing. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2005-03/))
- **2007:** *Flautistas*. A mixed-media diptych, signed and dated jointly; it received the Premio Platt acquisition award. ([auction record citing the 2007 competition and 2024 retrospective catalogues](https://www.artnet.com/artists/chiachio-giannone/flautistas-Cr474PECi8NkQbf9aF1F2g2))
- **2009:** *Jinetes*. Hand embroidery in cotton, silk, and rayon on a Hermès linen towel. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2011-09/))
- **2009:** *Familia Guaraní*. Embroidered textile family portrait incorporating rayon and jewel-effect threads. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2011-09/))
- **2009:** *Rohayhu*. Large cotton-and-wool embroidery on fabric. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2011-09/), [retrospective account](https://www.arte-online.net/Notas/Chiachio-Giannone))
- **2009:** *Familia de la buena suerte*. Embroidery and appliqué on a handwoven blanket formerly belonging to architect José Lorenzo; the inherited support is part of the obra’s material history. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2011-09/))
- **2009:** *Panda & Conejo*. Embroidery on camouflage fabric. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2011-09/))
- **2009:** *Un día de nuestras vidas*. Embroidery on linen associated with the Guevara family and dated by the artists to about 1920; 2009 is the intervention date, not the support’s date. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2011-09/))
- **2009:** *Paisaje con calaveras*. Glitter and graphite on paper rather than a textile. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2011-09/))
- **2009:** *Una vida sin corporaciones*. Embroidery on Giannone’s blazer and trousers: clothing, self-portrait, and sculptural object at once. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2011-09/))
- **2010:** *Familia guaraní en la selva*. Cotton and jewel-effect-thread embroidery. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2011-09/))
- **2010:** *Familia guaraní en la selva guatemalteca*. Cotton-thread embroidery arising from the duo’s Guatemalan work. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2011-09/))
- **2010:** *Nacimiento*. Embroidery on camouflage fabric. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2011-09/))
- **2011:** *La familia en la Fontana di Trevi*. Cotton-thread embroidered family portrait. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2011-09/))
- **2011:** *Hina Matsuri*. Cotton, rayon, and jewel-effect-thread embroidery. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2011-09/))
- **2011:** *Postal de San Jacinto*. A three-part, 5.4-metre embroidered work on handwoven blankets, augmented with crocheted flowers and pompoms. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2011-09/))
- **2011–12:** *Familia coyita*. Cotton-thread embroidery. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2011-09/))
- **2011–12:** *Promesas*. Embroidery incorporating metal votive offerings; textile image and devotional object overlap. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2011-09/))
- **2011–12:** *Arco Iris de luna y sol*. Embroidery with pompoms. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2011-09/))
- **2012:** *Ciudad frondosa*. Monumental embroidery measuring 5.4 by 2.8 metres. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2014-12/))
- **2012:** *Enmarañados*. Cotton, rayon, wool, and jewel-effect embroidery. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2014-12/))
- **2012:** *Caza mayor*. Embroidery on gabardine and commercially printed Alexander Henry fabric. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2014-12/))
- **2012:** *Enlazados*. Embroidery on Alexander Henry fabric. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2014-12/))
- **2012:** *Bomberos*. Embroidery on commercially printed fabric. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2014-12/))
- **2012:** *Piolín Boogie Woogie*. Embroidery on a Hermès linen towel. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2014-12/))
- **2013:** *Tatuaje*. Large embroidered diptych. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2014-12/))
- **2013–15:** *Selva blanca*. Monumental embroidery accompanied by a hand-embroidered tasseled collar; its three-year date records an extended making process. ([artist project register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/proyectos/page/2/))
- **2013–19:** *La famille dans la joyeuse verdure / Familia en el alegre verdor*. A five-metre family embroidery developed over several years; an Aubusson tapestry version was produced after the design won an international tapestry prize, so design and woven manifestation are distinct objects. ([artist register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2019-2/), [*Vivir sus vidas* catalogue](https://core.cambeiro.com.ar/0-229248-0.pdf))
- **2014:** *Calaverita*. Textile obra now in the Denver Art Museum collection; it subsequently became the image for a commercially issued 1,000-piece puzzle, a derivative design object rather than the original work. ([artist project register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/proyectos/))
- **2016–17:** *Picos gemelos*. Embroidery with metallic threads and pompoms on a blanket. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2017-2/))
- **2016–17:** *Paisaje de Pompeya*. A 5.18-metre textile combining embroidery, dyeing, sublimation printing, and screen printing. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2017-2/))
- **2022:** *Coleccionistas de trapos*. Embroidery, reused textiles, patchwork, and quilting: an obra explicitly made from other fabrics’ prior lives. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2022-2/))
- **2022:** *Conversación sobre arte*. Embroidery and quilting on toile de Jouy. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2022-2/))
- **2022:** *Retrato con rosas*. Embroidered, patched, and quilted family portrait. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2022-2/))
- **2022:** *Retrato de familia*. Embroidery and patchwork. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2022-2/))
- **2023:** *Comechiffones #1–#4*. Four individually dimensioned monumental embroideries made from reused cloth, patchwork, and quilting; although exhibited as a series, the register treats them as four obras. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2023-2/))
- **2024:** *Comechiffones #5*. A 4.5-metre square Gesamtkunstwerk-like textile combining embroidery, cross-stitch, appliqué, patchwork, quilting, and screen printing. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2024-2/))
- **2024:** *Sebastianos*. Textile-mosaic polyptych measuring four metres when unfolded; it shares its title with the materially different 2004 embroidery. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2024-2/))
- **2025:** *Madurones #1–#6*. Six separately dimensioned embroidered, appliquéd, patched, and quilted textiles on dyed cotton. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2025-2/))

### Textile mosaics and soft architecture

- **2017:** *Mosaico pompeyano* group. At least four separately dimensioned textile mosaics appear under this repeated title; the online register does not number them, so their individual identities remain partly unstable. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2017-2/))
- **2017:** *Mosaico pompeyano. Los cuatro elementos*. Textile mosaic distinguished from the unnumbered *Mosaico pompeyano* works by its subtitle. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2017-2/))
- **2018:** *Criollos en la piscina*. Textile mosaic. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2018-2/))
- **2018:** *Criollo francés*. Textile mosaic. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2018-2/))
- **2018:** *Ranchita criolla*. Textile mosaic. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2018-2/))
- **2018:** *Splash criollo*. Monumental textile mosaic; it received the Argentine national acquisition prize for best work. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2018-2/), [artist CV](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/cv-english/))
- **2018:** *Familia a seis colores #1–#7 and #9–#11*. Ten individually sized textile mosaics are listed; no #8 appears in the artist’s register, and its omission should not be “repaired” by invention. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2018-2/))
- **2019:** *Desayuno en Long Beach*. Textile mosaic; the Spanish and English entries give contradictory dimensions, so neither measurement is privileged here. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2019-2/))
- **2019:** *Familia californiana a seis colores #1–#2*. Two textile mosaics recorded without dimensions. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2019-2/))
- **2019:** *Refugio #1–#4*. Four separate embroideries on silk; the gallery and artist register disagree on some dimensions, but agree on the titles, date, and medium. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2019-2/), [Ruth Benzacar](https://ruthbenzacar.com/artistas/chiachio-giannone/))
- **2022:** *Familia a seis colores francesa*. Variable-format textile mosaic made from fabrics recovered in Bourges flea markets. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2022-2/))
- **2021–23:** *Catedral #1–#11*. Eleven distinct embroideries and screen prints made over antique tapestries; the found supports prevent the series from being only painting, tapestry, or appropriation. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2023-2/))
- **2025:** *Refugio #6–#10*. Five gouaches on paper, materially distinct from the 2019 silk embroideries bearing the same series title; the consulted registers do not establish a *Refugio #5* on paper. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2025-2/))

### Drawings, books, clothing, and domestic objects

- **2009:** *Samurais*. A diptych of hand-decorated porcelain plates. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2011-09/))
- **2009:** *Jarrón*. Hand-painted porcelain vase on a wooden base. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2011-09/))
- **2010:** *Una selva en la ciudad*. Triptych drawn in permanent marker on nylon. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2011-09/))
- **2010:** *Ekeko*. Three separately registered decorated porcelain figures with wool hats; repeated title and identical listed measurements make their individual catalogue identities ambiguous. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2011-09/))
- **2010:** *Peluches*. Variable-sized decorated porcelain objects: the title calls them “soft toys,” but materially they are ceramic objects. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2011-09/))
- **2010–12:** *Ekekos*. A triptych of hand-decorated porcelain objects with crocheted hats; it may incorporate or restage the 2010 figures, but the online register does not settle that relationship. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2014-12/))
- **2010–12:** *Guirnalda Ekeko*. Approximately twenty-five-metre installation assembled from decorated rejected porcelain components and bronze hooks. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2014-12/))
- **2017:** *Cortinas homenaje*. A variable group of sublimation-printed curtains; the repeated register entries appear to be installation views rather than proof of two identically titled works. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2017-2/))
- **2017:** *Cortina marrón*. Hand-embroidered curtain. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2017-2/))
- **2017:** *Jarrones modernos / Modernos*. A series of hand-decorated ceramic vases produced with the Escuela Municipal de Cerámica Emilio Villafañe; the individual vessels are not separately titled in the consulted register. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2017-2/), [project record](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/proyectos/))
- **2017:** *Autorretrato abstracto*. Shelves clad in fabric from Chiachio and Giannone’s shirts: portrait, furniture, and archive of worn material. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2017-2/))
- **2017:** *La criollita roja – La criollita multicolor*. Paired embroidered textiles displayed on metal supports. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2017-2/))
- **2022:** *Florero con geometría*. Embroidery on an antique cushion cover. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2022-2/))
- **2022:** *El jardín de Sonia*. Embroidery applied to an older cross-stitch embroidery. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2022-2/))
- **2022:** *Fierté #1–#4*. Four found books, cut open and painted in gouache; they are altered-book objects, not authored literary texts. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2022-2/))
- **2022:** *Casa de señoras*. Screen printing and marker drawing on patchwork assembled from antique fabrics. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2022-2/))
- **2022:** *Barbas y Bigotes*. Series of collaged, dyed, and stiffened antique fabrics on men’s handkerchiefs; the online register illustrates multiple pieces without individual titles, so the series is the securely nameable obra. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2022-2/), [Ruth Benzacar](https://ruthbenzacar.com/artistas/chiachio-giannone/))
- **2022:** *Retratos Patch*. Drawing-and-collage series on paper; individual sheets remain untitled in the consulted register. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2022-2/))
- **2022:** *Las dos barbas*. Digital drawing. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2022-2/))
- **2022:** *El amigo Roy*. Screen-printed fabric applied to a denim shirt and presented with an aluminium ladder—clothing and installation rather than an autonomous print. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2022-2/))
- **2022:** *Los ojos del arte*. Collage series on vintage fabric; the illustrated pieces are not individually titled. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2022-2/))

### Installations, performances, films, and collaborative projects

- **2005–present:** *MUPI — Museo Piolín*. An evolving “museum” dedicated to the artists’ dachshund-child Piolín, first manifested at MACRO and subsequently remade in other venues. It resists being called a single work: it is simultaneously project, fictional institution, installation, collection, and affectionate family archive. ([artist project history](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/proyectos/page/2/), [artist CV](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/cv-english/))
- **2009:** *Sagrada Familia*. A collaborative project in which Guatemalan artisans interpreted photographs of Chiachio, Giannone, and Piolín through their own textile practices; authorship is deliberately distributed among the duo, named makers, and the OLAS S.A. collective. ([artist project record](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2009/01/23/sagrada-familia/))
- **2009:** *Brujas protectoras*. Installation made from embroidery on denim, handkerchiefs, and pompoms for the Fifth International Textile Art Biennial. ([artist project record](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2009/01/23/5ta-bienal-internacional-de-arte-textil/))
- **2015:** *Chaya o bendición del Friday Late*. Performance at the Victoria and Albert Museum; its obra is the enacted blessing, not a stable textile object. ([artist project register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/proyectos/page/2/))
- **2016–present:** *Banderas del Orgullo*. Participatory, continually expanding work begun with museum visitors and later activated in several countries. It is best understood as a project/protocol for collective making whose successive flags are manifestations, not necessarily a finished unitary object. ([artist project register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2018/11/25/banderas-del-orgullo-cck-2018/), [*Vivir sus vidas* catalogue](https://core.cambeiro.com.ar/0-229248-0.pdf))
- **2017:** *Estandarte Pompeyano*. Diptych of embroidered, sublimation-printed banners on iron supports. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2017-2/))
- **2017:** *Biombo Pompeyano*. Embroidered and printed fabric mounted on a wooden screen. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2017-2/))
- **2016–17:** *Pared bordada*. Variable installation made from embroidered cushion covers, napkins, and table runners; repeated photographs in the register should not be counted as separate works. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2017-2/))
- **2017:** *Avistaje*. Community-embroidery project made with students of master craftswoman Elba Rocco; it is a collective making situation as much as an object. ([artist project register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/proyectos/))
- **2018:** *Comrades in Shanghai*. Residency project comprising multiple works made in response to Shanghai; because it names a body of work rather than one securely bounded object, it resists singular-work classification. ([artist account](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/portfolio-item/chiachio-giannone-love-is-in-the-air-by-maria-carolina-baulo/), [Swatch Art Peace Hotel](https://www.swatch-art-peace-hotel.com/en/virtual-museum/Chiachio-%26-Giannone-333))
- **2018:** *Shanghai Landscape*. Approximately four-metre patchwork developed during the Shanghai residency. ([artist account](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/portfolio-item/chiachio-giannone-love-is-in-the-air-by-maria-carolina-baulo/))
- **2021:** *CUIR*. Virtual exhibition curated by Chiachio & Giannone with twenty queer artists from across the Americas. It is a curatorial creation and platform rather than an artwork authored solely by the duo. ([Isabel Croxatto Gallery](https://isabelcroxattogaleria.com/es/artists/28-chiachio-%26-giannone/overview/), [artist CV](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/cv-english/))
- **2022:** *Fierté*. Installation formed around the four altered books of the same name; the register distinguishes the installation view from *Fierté #1–#4*. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2022-2/))
- **2022:** *Fiesta*. Gesamtkunstwerk comprising prints, paintings, dyed and sublimation-printed fabrics, installation, dance, and Super-8 film; it refuses separation into a single medium. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2022-2/), [*Vivir sus vidas* catalogue](https://core.cambeiro.com.ar/0-229248-0.pdf))
- **2022:** *Fiesta // El afiche*. Printed poster, an autonomous component or manifestation of the larger *Fiesta* project. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2022-2/))
- **2022:** *Fiesta // El baile*. Installation of printed textiles, lights, and fans. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2022-2/))
- **2022:** *Fiesta // La película*. Thirteen-minute Super-8/HD film co-directed by Agustina Comedi and Chiachio & Giannone; its collaborative directing credit must be retained. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2022-2/))
- **2022:** *Nueva bandera // Nuevo orgullo*. Variable installation/object made from hand-dyed antique linen. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2022-2/))
- **2023:** *AKA Chiachio & Giannone (Also Known As)*. Ten-part embroidered polyptych on donated or second-hand napkins, table runners, and older embroideries. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2023-2/))
- **2023:** *Currículum Vitae*. Twenty-eight-part randa polyptych encoding the duo’s exhibition history; made collaboratively with twenty-four named lace-makers from El Cercado, Tucumán, so it is simultaneously artwork, CV, textile archive, and distributed-authorship project. ([artist work register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2023-2/), [project account](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/proyectos/))

### Design and reproducible objects

- **2016:** *Piolin’s Time*. Limited edition of 2,525 numbered Swatch watches designed for the Swatch Club’s twenty-fifth anniversary. It is an artist-designed manufactured object, not a unique artwork. ([artist project register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/proyectos/page/2/))
- **2024:** *Vivir sus vidas*. Three-hundred-page retrospective artist book/catalogue, issued in an edition of 1,200 with unique interventions inside some copies. Authorship is composite: Chiachio & Giannone supplied the oeuvre and interventions, while Leandro Martínez Depietri edited the volume and multiple scholars wrote its texts. ([publisher record](https://coleccionamalita.mitiendanube.com/productos/vivir-sus-vidas-chiachio-giannone/), [edition record](https://www.artsy.net/artwork/chiachio-and-giannone-vivir-sus-vidas-libro))
- **date not stated:** *Calaverita 1000-Piece Jigsaw Puzzle*. Commercial puzzle derived from the 2014 textile *Calaverita* and produced with Pomegranate; a licensed manifestation of an earlier image rather than the original obra. ([artist project register](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/proyectos/))
