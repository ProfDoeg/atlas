## Works by Gustav Landauer

### Books, pamphlets, and independently issued texts

- **1893:** *Der Todesprediger*. Landauer’s only novel: a sustained work of philosophical fiction, not a political treatise. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1893:** *An den Züricher Kongress: Bericht über die deutsche Arbeiterbewegung*. A twenty-page political report prepared for an international workers’ congress. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1895:** *Ein Weg zur Befreiung der Arbeiter-Klasse*. An anonymously published political pamphlet proposing workers’ self-emancipation; the attribution to Landauer is established bibliographically. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1895:** *Aus meinem Gefängniss-Tagebuch*. A five-part prison diary published serially in *Der sozialistische Akademiker*; it is one work distributed across five periodical installments. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1896:** *Von Zürich bis London: Bericht über die deutsche Arbeiterbewegung an den Londoner Kongress*. A congress report and movement document, issued by Landauer through his own publishing imprint. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1898:** *Der Fall Ziethen: Ein Appell an die öffentliche Meinung*. An anonymously issued intervention in a disputed murder conviction: less a descriptive book than a public act of advocacy. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1903:** *Skepsis und Mystik: Versuche im Anschluß an Mauthners Sprachkritik*. A linked sequence of philosophical essays that turns linguistic skepticism toward mysticism and community; the expanded 1923 version was assembled after Landauer’s death by Martin Buber and is therefore not simply a second work by Landauer. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1903:** *Macht und Mächte*. A book containing the novellas *Arnold Himmelheber* and *Lebendig tot*. The altered 1923 edition added *Der gelbe Stein* and should be treated as a posthumous reconfiguration, not a separate original composition. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1907:** *Die Revolution*. A social-psychological monograph and work of historical theory published as volume 13 of Martin Buber’s series *Die Gesellschaft*. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1911:** *Aufruf zum Sozialismus*. A lecture recast as a book-length appeal: both theoretical exposition and manifesto, because it calls its readers to begin socialist relations immediately. Landauer substantially revised it for the 1919 “Revolutionsausgabe,” making that edition an authorial remaking rather than a mere reprint. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- **1911:** *Die Abschaffung des Kriegs durch die Selbstbestimmung des Volks: Fragen an die deutschen Arbeiter*. An antiwar pamphlet framed as questions and directed toward action; a nominal 1912 issue has variant pagination but is not another work. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1916:** *Martin Buber – Werke über das Judentum*. A separately printed critical essay on Buber’s Jewish writings. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1916:** *Ein Weg deutschen Geistes*. A pamphlet-sized intellectual genealogy rather than a nationalist tract; Landauer constructs a path through German heterodox and mystical thought. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1918:** *Wie Hedwig Lachmann starb*. A privately printed memorial account intended for friends. Its bibliographic dating is internally problematic—the cited record prints “27. März bis 1. März 1918”—so no more precise composition range should be asserted. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1918:** *Die vereinigten Republiken Deutschlands und ihre Verfassung*. An eight-page constitutional project published as a leaflet: a proposed political form, not a constitution that entered into force. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1918–1919:** *Rechenschaft*. An author-assembled collection of antiwar writings from 1909–1916, completed in March 1918 for publication after the war and issued in 1919. It is both anthology and political accounting, rather than a newly written continuous treatise. [[Editorial history](https://gustav-landauer.org/materialien)]
- **1919:** *Briefe aus der französischen Revolution*. A two-volume documentary construction selected, translated, arranged, and annotated by Landauer. The source letters are not his, but the German montage and explanatory apparatus are unmistakably his made object. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]

### Manifestos, protocols, and organizational texts

- **1908:** *Die zwölf Artikel des Sozialistischen Bundes*. A founding protocol and manifesto for a voluntary socialist federation; a second formulation also survives, so the text has no single immutable version. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- **1908:** *Sozialistischer Bund: Organisation*. An organizational protocol defining the projected federation’s working form. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- **1908:** *Was will der Sozialistische Bund?* The first leaflet of the Sozialistischer Bund, simultaneously explanation, recruitment instrument, and manifesto. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- **1909:** *Was ist zunächst zu tun?* The second leaflet of the Sozialistischer Bund; a protocol of immediate practice rather than a general description of socialism. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- **1910:** *Die Siedlung*. The third leaflet of the Sozialistischer Bund, presenting cooperative settlement as a concrete project. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- **1909:** *Sätze vom Sozialistischen Bund*. A concise programmatic statement whose form lies between theses, manifesto, and organizational self-definition. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- **1909–1910:** *Die drei Flugblätter des Sozialistischen Bundes*. The collective obra formed by the three separately issued leaflets; the later title names their archival grouping rather than an additional contemporary book. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1910:** *Aufruf zur freien Schule*. A public appeal and practical initiative for libertarian education. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- **1911:** *Aufruf zur Mitarbeit an einer geplanten Jugendzeitschrift des Sozialistischen Bundes*. An unrealized publishing project preserved through its call for collaborators; its resistance to being called a completed work is part of its identity. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- **1912:** *Freier Arbeitertag*. A proposal for independent worker deliberation outside parliamentary and party structures; both text and projected event. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- **1912:** *An die Menschen, zu denen unsere Stimme dringt*. A collective-facing appeal associated with the Sozialistischer Bund; it acts by summoning participants rather than merely reporting ideas. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- **1912:** *Organisationsstatut der Siedlungs-Vereinigung „Gemeinschaft“*. A protocol for a settlement association: an instructional and constitutive document rather than an essay. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- **1912:** *Zur Vorbereitung der ersten Siedlung*. A working document for a projected community. The settlement remained a project, so the intention and preparatory text—not a completed colony—are the work. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- **1915:** *Vom Sozialismus und der Siedlung: Thesen zur Wirklichkeit und Verwirklichung*. A set of practical theses surviving as a fragment; its incompleteness should not be converted into a fictitious finished treatise. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- **1914:** *Thesen zur Gründungstagung des Forte-Kreises*. Founding theses for an antiwar intellectual association; the circle was a collaborative project, while these theses are Landauer’s constitutive contribution. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- **1918–1919:** *The cultural and educational program of the Bavarian revolutionary councils*. A body of speeches, proposals, proclamations, and administrative interventions made while Landauer served the Bavarian Revolution. It resists reduction to one authored “work”: its manifestations were collective, provisional, and embedded in political action; the documentary corpus is preserved in the later scholarly collection *Gustav Landauer und die Revolutionszeit 1918–1919*. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]

### Periodicals and editorial constructions

- **1893–1899:** *Der Sozialist*. A periodical obra Landauer inherited rather than originated: he became its leading editor in 1893 and reshaped it successively as an anarchist and revolutionary journal. Editorial responsibility changed during imprisonments, and unsigned or pseudonymous articles make the limits of his individual authorship uncertain. [[Editorial history](https://gustav-landauer.org/content/publizistisches-wirken-redaktionen-des-%E2%80%9Esozialist%E2%80%9C-mitte-friedrichshain-und-kreuzberg)]
- **1893–1899:** *Anarchistische Bibliothek*. A pamphlet series edited by Landauer alongside *Der Sozialist*. It is an editorial project and distribution system, not a single authored text. [[Periodical history](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Der_Sozialist)]
- **1895–1899:** *Literarische Beilage zum Sozialist*. A literary supplement used to place criticism, original writing, and translated literature inside the anarchist press; its identity is editorial and composite. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- **1909–1915:** *Der Sozialist: Organ des Sozialistischen Bundes*. A new periodical founded and largely written or edited by Landauer as the practical organ of the Sozialistischer Bund. Margarethe Faas-Hardegger shared early editorial responsibility, and the translated, unsigned, and collaborative contents prevent the whole journal from being assigned to Landauer alone. [[Editorial history](https://gustav-landauer.org/content/publizistisches-wirken-redaktionen-des-%E2%80%9Esozialist%E2%80%9C-mitte-friedrichshain-und-kreuzberg)]
- **1909–1915:** *Year-opening addresses for Der Sozialist*. A recurring editorial sequence—texts for the 1910 through 1915 volumes—whose serial continuity makes it a small body of work rather than one fixed essay. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]

### Fiction, drama, poetry, and satire

- *Er und Sie*. A short dramatic work preserved in the collected literary writings; the consulted scholarly contents do not supply a secure composition date, so none is imposed here. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- *Nach Jahren*. A dramatic work; no secure date appears in the consulted collected-works contents. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- *Ein Knabenleben*. A novella, distinct from Landauer’s political and philosophical prose; no fixed date is supplied by the consulted contents. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- *Arnold Himmelheber*. A novella later incorporated into *Macht und Mächte*; the story and the 1903 book containing it are related objects, not synonyms. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- *Lebendig tot*. A novella later incorporated into *Macht und Mächte*. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- **1910:** *Der gelbe Stein*. A fairy tale or short fantastic narrative, posthumously added to the altered 1923 *Macht und Mächte*. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- *Der Kinderdieb*. A fairy tale; the consulted collected-works contents do not establish a fixed date. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- *Spanische Ballade*. A poem; it is named here without reproduction and without an unsupported date. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- *Verkündigung*. A poem; the scholarly contents establish its inclusion but not a secure date. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- *Die wahrhaftige und tragische Geschichte einer Wanze*. A satirical narrative; its form deliberately resists assimilation to Landauer’s theoretical “works.” [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- *Verein der Millionäre*. A satire; no fixed date is supplied by the consulted contents. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- *Vom Schnee und vom König von England; von Eiern, Äpfeln, Weizen, Hafer und Unsinn; von der Liebe und von der Gemeinde*. A hybrid comic and reflective prose piece classified as “Satire/Miscellaneous” by the collected edition. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- **before 1889:** *Gedankensprünge*. An early manuscript mixing personal lyric writing with aesthetic criticism; its survival or completeness is not established by the consulted account, so it is better treated as a youthful project than as a securely constituted published work. [[Scholarly chronology](https://gustav-landauer.org/content/studienjahre-tilman-leder)]

### Translations and acts of textual remaking

- **1903:** *Meister Eckharts Mystische Schriften*. Landauer’s selection and intralingual translation of Meister Eckhart from Middle High German into contemporary German; it is simultaneously edition, translation, and philosophical appropriation. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1904:** Peter Kropotkin, *Gegenseitige Hilfe in der Entwicklung*. An authorized German edition prepared by Landauer; “prepared” leaves room for editorial as well as translational labor. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1904:** Peter Kropotkin, *Landwirtschaft, Industrie und Handwerk oder: Die Vereinigung von Industrie und Landwirtschaft, geistiger und körperlicher Arbeit*. Landauer’s authorized German translation; the nominal 1910 “second edition” merely received a new title page and is not a new translation. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1904:** Oscar Wilde, *Der Sozialismus und die Seele des Menschen – Aus dem Zuchthaus zu Reading – Aesthetisches Manifest*. A German collection credited jointly to Hedwig Lachmann and Gustav Landauer. Bibliographical scholarship notes that at least the first text, despite the printed joint credit, was translated by Landauer alone; attribution therefore varies within the volume. [[Bibliographic note](https://dadaweb.de/indexde7a.html?title=Landauer%2C_Gustav)]
- **1907:** George Bernard Shaw, *Sozialismus für Millionäre*. Landauer’s German translation of a satirical political essay. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1907:** Oscar Wilde, *Das Bildnis des Dorian Gray*. A German translation made jointly with Hedwig Lachmann; collaborative authorship must be retained. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1907:** Oscar Wilde, *Zwei Gespräche von der Kunst und vom Leben*. A joint Lachmann–Landauer German translation of Wilde’s dialogues. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1909:** Peter Kropotkin, *Die französische Revolution 1789–1793*. A two-volume German translation and editorial undertaking; the volumes divide the history at September 1792 but constitute one translated work. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1910–1911:** Étienne de La Boétie, *Von der freiwilligen Knechtschaft*. A German translation serialized in six installments of *Der Sozialist*; its first material form was serial rather than book-length. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1911:** Paul Berthelot, *Das Evangelium der Stunde*. A German translation attributed to Landauer by Siegbert Wolf, although the contemporary printing does not securely name him; the attribution must therefore remain qualified. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1913:** Thomas Malory, *Der Tod Arthurs*. A three-volume German translation printed under Hedwig Lachmann’s name. Siegbert Wolf attributes uncredited co-translation to Landauer, but because the books themselves do not name him, this remains a disputed or externally reconstructed collaboration. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1915:** Rabindranath Tagore, *Der König der dunklen Kammer*. A German dramatic translation made jointly by Hedwig Lachmann and Landauer. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1919:** Walt Whitman, *Der Wundarzt*. A composite translation: Iwan Goll translated the prose, while Landauer translated the poems. The whole volume consequently cannot be described as Landauer’s work alone. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **published 1921:** Rabindranath Tagore, *Das Postamt*. A jointly made Lachmann–Landauer stage translation published after both translators’ deaths; the publication date is not evidence of a 1921 act of composition. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **published 1921:** Walt Whitman, *Gesänge und Inschriften*. A posthumously issued collection of Landauer’s poetic translations. The book is an editorial manifestation of earlier translation work, not a new work made by him in 1921. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **various dates, chiefly 1909–1915:** *Translations in Der Sozialist*. A dispersed body of German translations from Élisée Reclus, Mikhail Bakunin, Peter Kropotkin, Voltairine de Cleyre, and especially Pierre-Joseph Proudhon. The standard bibliographical overview acknowledges this larger corpus without enumerating every contribution, while unsigned periodical publication sometimes obstructs secure attribution. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]

### Lectures, essays, reviews, speeches, and letters as bodies of work

- **1889–1919:** *Essays, reviews, editorials, polemics, obituaries, and public letters*. This is a very large dispersed textual obra rather than a single book: the scholarly *Ausgewählte Schriften* organizes it across internationalism, anarchism, antipolitics, war and revolution, philosophy, Judaism, and literature. Many first appeared anonymously or pseudonymously, particularly in *Der Sozialist*, so its exact boundary remains bibliographically revisable. [[Edition overview](https://www.edition-av.de/gl.htm)]
- **1890s–1919:** *Lectures and public speeches*. A performance-based body of work whose surviving texts range from scripts and reports to later reconstructions; a lecture’s occurrence and its printed text are related manifestations, not necessarily the same object. The online scholarly bibliography expressly includes known talks as well as texts. [[Online bibliography](https://gustav-landauer.org/bibliographie)]
- **13 March 1916:** *Friedrich Hölderlin in seinen Gedichten*. A Berlin lecture whose text was published independently only in 1922. The lecture is Landauer’s work; the posthumous booklet is its later editorial manifestation. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **before 1919; published 1920:** *Shakespeare: Dargestellt in Vorträgen*. A two-volume sequence of lectures prepared for posthumous publication under Landauer’s testamentary instructions and edited by Martin Buber. It is a Landauer project realized materially by another hand, so neither sole lifetime book nor ordinary posthumous anthology describes it adequately. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1884–1919:** *Correspondence*. Thousands of letters constitute a relational body of writing rather than a book Landauer designed. The modern critical editions comprise letters and diaries from 1884–1900 and seven volumes covering letters from 1899–1919; overlap between the editorial ranges means they must not be counted as two discrete authorial works. [[Bibliography and edition description](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1884–1900:** *Diaries*. Surviving diaries are transmitted alongside the early correspondence in a modern critical edition. They are manuscripts and records of ongoing life rather than a finished publication project. [[Bibliography and edition description](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **22 February 1918:** *Letter to Siegfried Landauer*. A private letter treated by the collected literary edition as Landauer’s “Testament.” “Testament” is an editorial classification of the letter’s function, not necessarily its original title or legal form. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]

### Social and theatrical projects

- **1892:** *Neue Freie Volksbühne*. A theatrical and educational association co-founded by Landauer and other dissidents from the existing Volksbühne. It is a collective institutional creation, not a text and not attributable to Landauer alone. [[Biographical history](https://gustav-landauer.org/gustav-landauer/unabh%C3%A4ngiger-sozialismus-und-volksb%C3%BChnenbewegung)]
- **1908–1915:** *Sozialistischer Bund*. A federation initiated by Landauer to make socialism through voluntary groups, cooperative production, education, and settlement. Its obra is the living association and its practices; its leaflets, statutes, meetings, and journal are manifestations of that broader project. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- **1908–1915:** *The socialist settlement project*. A repeatedly formulated but incompletely realized plan for communities linking land, cooperative labor, consumption, and culture. Its unfinished condition is constitutive: the project, preparatory organizations, and protocols exist even though the intended settlement did not become a completed Gesamtkunstwerk of social life. [[Collected-works contents](https://www.edition-av.de/info/inhaltsverzeichnis_glaw_%201-12.pdf)]
- **1919:** *Bavarian Council Republic cultural transformation*. Landauer’s brief revolutionary work in public instruction and culture was a collective, interrupted social project rather than an individually authored object. Its surviving decrees, speeches, and proposals are fragments and manifestations of that attempted institutional creation. [[Documentary bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]

### Posthumous books that resist attribution as Landauer-made works

- **1921:** *Der werdende Mensch: Aufsätze über Leben und Schrifttum*. An essay collection edited by Martin Buber under Landauer’s testamentary authorization. Its component texts are Landauer’s, but the selection, title, ordering, and book-object are partly Buber’s. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1924:** *Beginnen: Aufsätze über Sozialismus*. A posthumous collection edited by Buber. It should not be silently counted as a book composed by Landauer, although its constituent essays belong to his oeuvre. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1929:** *Gustav Landauer: Sein Lebensgang in Briefen*. A two-volume life-in-letters constructed by Martin Buber and Ina Britschgi-Schimmer. Landauer made the letters, but not this retrospective narrative arrangement. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **1968 onward:** *Later selected writings and thematic anthologies*. Volumes such as *Zwang und Befreiung*, *Erkenntnis und Befreiung*, *Staat und Geist*, *Stelle Dich, Sozialist!*, *Signatur: g.l.*, *Die Botschaft der Titanic*, and *Zeit und Geist* are editor-made selections from prior works, not additional works Landauer made. [[Bibliography](https://www.lebenshaus-alb.de/magazin/media/pdf/Landauer-2025.pdf)]
- **2008–2019:** *Ausgewählte Schriften*. Siegbert Wolf’s seventeen-part scholarly edition is the principal modern map of Landauer’s dispersed writings, but it is deliberately “selected,” thematically rearranged, and partly text-critical. It is a scholarly manifestation of Landauer’s oeuvre, not itself his creation. [[Edition overview](https://www.edition-av.de/gl.htm)]
