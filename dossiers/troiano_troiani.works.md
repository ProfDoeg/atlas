## Works by Troiano Troiani

### Early carvings, models, and sculptures

- **c. 1893–1894:** *Statuette of the beggar known as “Blanc di Venzòn”*. A clay figure, approximately ten centimetres high, reportedly modelled when Troiani was eight or nine; a plaster cast survived with his sister, but its present status is unreported. ([Menis, pp. 112–13](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- **Before 1908:** *Bondanze*. A small sculpture of Giovanni Durì, a travelling accordion player known by that nickname, preserved by the Civici Musei di Udine. ([Bergamini](https://www.dizionariobiograficodeifriulani.it/troiani-troiano/))
- **Before 1908:** *Bust of Bartolomeo Rizzotti*. An early portrait of the altar-maker in whose Artegna workshop Troiani trained. ([Bergamini](https://www.dizionariobiograficodeifriulani.it/troiani-troiano/))
- **Before 1908:** *Sculptures for the high altar of the parish church at Risano*. Architectural-religious sculpture; the scholarly biography does not enumerate or individually title its components. ([Bergamini](https://www.dizionariobiograficodeifriulani.it/troiani-troiano/))
- **Before 1908:** *Sculptures for the altar of San Rocco, Tonzolano*. An altar ensemble rather than a single autonomous statue; its components are not itemized in the consulted scholarly inventory. ([Bergamini](https://www.dizionariobiograficodeifriulani.it/troiani-troiano/))
- *Il figlio*. An early autonomous sculpture cited among the works that first established Troiani in Argentina; no secure date or present location is supplied. ([Menis, pp. 118, 130](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- *San Giorgio*. An early sculpture distinct from the later work associated with Bahía Blanca; its date and whereabouts remain unspecified. ([Menis, pp. 118, 130](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- **1921:** *Dante*. A large commemorative statue inaugurated in Buenos Aires, not a book or textual work; later summaries sometimes call it a bust, so the object’s form is inconsistently described in the secondary record. ([Menis, p. 118](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- **1925:** *Arquero* (*Arciere*; also *Arquero divino*). A monumental archer shown in Troiani’s first solo exhibition and subsequently awarded at the municipal exhibition of industrial arts; a cast stands in Plaza Moreno, La Plata, while Menis reported another version or cast stored at the Museo Nacional, making the object’s casting and installation history less simple than a single-work label suggests. ([Bergamini](https://www.dizionariobiograficodeifriulani.it/troiani-troiano/); [Argentina’s heritage record](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/escultura-el-arquero-divino); [Menis, p. 127](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- **1926:** *San Francesco d’Assisi*. A devotional statue inaugurated at the Circolo della Dante in Buenos Aires. ([Menis, p. 118](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- **1926:** *Karma* (*El karma*). A monumental allegorical sculpture incorporating an hourglass; Menis associates its prize with an international exhibition in Paris, while research from the Salón Nacional catalogues records it as the 1926 winner of Argentina’s prize for foreign artists, so the received exhibition-and-award narrative is not fully consistent. ([Menis, pp. 118–19](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1); [Serventi, pp. 4–5](https://papelcosido.fba.unlp.edu.ar/ojs/index.php/boa/article/download/894/1245/1606))
- **1935:** *Figura*. A small bronze figure, 17 × 5 × 5 cm; the published listing does not identify its subject more precisely. ([El Arte de los Argentinos](https://www.zurbaran.com.ar/troiani-troiano/))
- **1940:** *Niña con globo*. A small bronze figure, 33 × 18 × 10 cm. ([El Arte de los Argentinos](https://www.zurbaran.com.ar/troiani-troiano/))
- *La prescienza del destino*. A sculptural group of a mother and child organized around maternal foreknowledge and separation; neither date nor location is fixed by the consulted inventory. ([Menis, p. 123](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- *Humus*. A small allegorical sculpture personifying the sun’s fertilization of crops. ([Menis, p. 124](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- *La bandiera*. An autonomous sculpture known to Menis through at least a reproduced detail; its date, material, and location are not supplied. ([Menis, fig. 4 and p. 125](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- *La vittoria*. A sculpture named in the early scholarly inventory without a date, material, or location. ([Menis, p. 125](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- *Nudo femminile*. A female nude named in the inventory but otherwise unidentified; it should not automatically be equated with similarly titled sculptures in auction records. ([Menis, p. 125](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- *Testa di bambino*. A sculpted child’s head of unrecorded date and material. ([Menis, p. 125](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- *Le fonti mistiche di Sorella Acqua*. A sculpture or sculptural group named by Menis without secure date, material, or location. ([Menis, p. 125](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- *Resurrezione d’America*. An allegorical sculpture identified by contemporary criticism as belonging to Troiani’s mature phase; the source gives no fixed date or location. ([Menis, p. 130](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- *Promission* (*La promessa*). An expressive sculpture purchased or awarded by the Argentine state at the thirty-second Salón Nacional de Bellas Artes; the cited source does not supply the calendar year. ([Menis, pp. 124, 130](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- **1952:** *Il visionario*. A sculpture donated by Troiani to the municipality of Buia and preserved in its municipal building. ([Menis, p. 125](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))

### Architectural sculpture and civic Gesamtkunstwerke

- **1916:** *Decorative sculptures for the Bolsa de Comercio de Buenos Aires*. An architectural ensemble made with the sculptor Riganti; because authorship was collaborative and the components are not individually assigned, it resists division into a definitive list of single-author works. ([Bergamini](https://www.dizionariobiograficodeifriulani.it/troiani-troiano/))
- **1922:** *Sculptural groups and reliefs for the Argentine Pavilion at the Rio de Janeiro International Exposition*. An exhibition-architecture ensemble comprising two groups and additional reliefs; the standard sources do not preserve individual titles, and the pavilion context makes it closer to a temporary Gesamtkunstwerk than to one portable sculpture. ([Bergamini](https://www.dizionariobiograficodeifriulani.it/troiani-troiano/); [Menis, p. 118](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- **1927:** *Sculpture and relief program for the Banco Tornquist*. Monumental exterior statues and interior architectural reliefs integrated into Alejandro Bustillo’s bank building; the ensemble includes *Nettuno* and *L’Industria*, but the remaining reliefs are not individually titled in the consulted scholarly sources. ([Bergamini](https://www.dizionariobiograficodeifriulani.it/troiani-troiano/); [Argentina.gob.ar](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/edificio-tornquist); [Menis, p. 122](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- **1929–1930:** *Four allegorical reliefs for the Edison lamp standards at Plaza del Congreso*. A civic lighting-and-sculpture ensemble depicting worship of the sun, primitive fire-lighting, humanity’s capture of electricity, and the apotheosis of electric light; one source dates the lamp design to 1929, while the scholarly biography dates the reliefs to 1930. ([Menis, pp. 121–22](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1); [El Arte de los Argentinos](https://www.zurbaran.com.ar/troiani-troiano/); [Bergamini](https://www.dizionariobiograficodeifriulani.it/troiani-troiano/))
- **1931:** *Four allegorical sculptures for the Palacio del Concejo Deliberante*. Monumental architectural figures commissioned for the present Buenos Aires Legislature; they belong to the building’s sculptural program rather than functioning simply as four independent gallery works. ([Bergamini](https://www.dizionariobiograficodeifriulani.it/troiani-troiano/))
- **1932:** *Fontana del Tritone*. A fountain-monument at the Buenos Aires legislative palace, with Triton as its dominant sculptural figure; fountain, sculpture, plumbing, and urban site form one integrated obra. ([Bergamini](https://www.dizionariobiograficodeifriulani.it/troiani-troiano/); [Menis, pp. 121–22](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- **1936:** *Pedimental group for the Palacio de Justicia, Córdoba*. A monumental architectural group made for the courthouse fronton; its individual figures are not named in the consulted inventory. ([Bergamini](https://www.dizionariobiograficodeifriulani.it/troiani-troiano/))
- **1936:** *La verdad*. One of four monumental allegorical statues in the Córdoba Palacio de Justicia’s Salón de los Pasos Perdidos. ([Bergamini](https://www.dizionariobiograficodeifriulani.it/troiani-troiano/); [Menis, p. 122](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- **1936:** *La jurisprudencia*. A monumental courthouse allegory belonging to the same architectural ensemble. ([Menis, p. 122](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- **1936:** *La conciliación*. A monumental courthouse allegory belonging to the same architectural ensemble. ([Menis, p. 122](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- **1936:** *El ansia de libertad*. A monumental courthouse allegory belonging to the same architectural ensemble. ([Menis, p. 122](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- **c. 1936:** *Sculptures for the Ministerio de Obras Públicas, Buenos Aires*. Colossal architectural figures made for the ministry building; the standard biography neither enumerates nor titles them, and later popular interpretation of one palm-out figure as a covert “monument to bribery” is not established by the cited scholarly record. ([Bergamini](https://www.dizionariobiograficodeifriulani.it/troiani-troiano/))
- **1938:** *Historia patria*. The central monumental figure in the tripartite façade program of the Museo Histórico Provincial “Dr. Julio Marc,” Rosario. ([Bergamini](https://www.dizionariobiograficodeifriulani.it/troiani-troiano/); [Menis, pp. 122–23](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- **1938:** *América precolombina* (also recorded as *América india*). A monumental Indigenous female allegory on the Rosario museum façade; the two published titles may name the same figure rather than separate works. ([Menis, p. 123](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- **1938:** *América colonial*. A monumental female allegory holding a caravel on the Rosario museum façade. ([Menis, p. 123](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- **1940:** *El misionero*. One of four half-length allegorical figures on Alta Gracia’s quadrilateral tower commemorating the 350th anniversary of the city’s donation. ([Bergamini](https://www.dizionariobiograficodeifriulani.it/troiani-troiano/); [Menis, p. 123](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- **1940:** *El indio*. A half-length allegorical figure in the Alta Gracia commemorative ensemble. ([Bergamini](https://www.dizionariobiograficodeifriulani.it/troiani-troiano/))
- **1940:** *El conquistador*. A half-length allegorical figure in the Alta Gracia commemorative ensemble. ([Bergamini](https://www.dizionariobiograficodeifriulani.it/troiani-troiano/))
- **1940:** *El gaucho*. A half-length allegorical figure in the Alta Gracia commemorative ensemble. ([Bergamini](https://www.dizionariobiograficodeifriulani.it/troiani-troiano/))
- **1942:** *Reliefs for the Caja Nacional de Ahorro Postal*. Colossal architectural bas-reliefs; the scholarly biography treats them as an ensemble and does not give individual titles or an exact count. ([Bergamini](https://www.dizionariobiograficodeifriulani.it/troiani-troiano/); [Menis, pp. 124–25](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))

### Monuments and commemorative sculpture

- *Monument to Alberto del Solar*. A public monument in Mar del Plata honoring the promoter of the seaside esplanade; no secure date appears in the consulted inventory. ([Menis, p. 123](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- *Monument to David Peña*. A commemorative figure of the Argentine writer and scholar; Menis prints the surname as “Pegna,” apparently an Italianized or erroneous form, so the title’s spelling is not wholly stable in the source. ([Menis, p. 123](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- *Monument to Paul Groussac*. A commemorative monument in Buenos Aires’s Parque 3 de Febrero, selected through a national competition. ([Menis, p. 124](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- *Reliefs for the monument to General Julio A. Roca*. Narrative reliefs representing the subjugation of Indigenous people and the fertility of land taken from the desert; they are components of a larger monument, not the whole monument and not an ideologically neutral object. ([Menis, pp. 124–25](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- *Fernando III*. A crowning figure on the fronton of the colonial church at San Fernando de Maldonado; its architectural position makes it part of a façade program rather than a freely circulating statue. ([El Arte de los Argentinos](https://www.zurbaran.com.ar/troiani-troiano/))
- *Centauro*. A sculpture named in the published overview without a secure date, material, or location. ([El Arte de los Argentinos](https://www.zurbaran.com.ar/troiani-troiano/))

### Portraits, memorial reliefs, and funerary works

- *Bust of Intendente Josué Guerrico*. A civic portrait made for the Salón Blanco of the Buenos Aires municipal building; its date is not supplied. ([Menis, p. 122](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- *Bust of General José Félix Uriburu*. A life-size portrait bust; Menis’s text misspells the surname “Uruburu,” and gives neither date nor location. ([Menis, p. 124](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- *Portrait of Avelino Gutiérrez*. A sculptural portrait made for the Faculty of Medicine; its date and medium are not specified. ([Menis, p. 125](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- *Bas-relief of Basavilbaso*. A memorial relief centered on a standing male figure with three mourners; the source calls the subject “the learned Basavilbaso” but does not securely identify his full name. ([Menis, p. 123](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- *Sul confine*. A bas-relief of two women parting; its title is Italian in Menis, but its date, material, and location are unreported. ([Menis, p. 124](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- *Relief of six female figures*. A bas-relief documented in Rosario’s municipal museum without a recorded formal title; its descriptive label should not be mistaken for an artist-given title. ([Menis, p. 123](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- *Doors for an unidentified mausoleum in Recoleta Cemetery*. Sculpted funerary doors cited as an example of Troiani’s post-1950 manner; the patron, date, and original title are not given, so the object resists secure identification as an autonomous work. ([Menis, p. 130](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- *Reliefs and Christ for the D’Onofrio family vault*. Two granite reliefs flanking the entrance and a figure of Christ inside the Recoleta mausoleum; this is a funerary ensemble whose components are not individually titled. ([Buenos Aires Legislature, Recoleta heritage study](https://pergamo.legislatura.gob.ar/pgmedia/biblioteca/LIBROS%20DIGITALIZADOS/018085_D.pdf))
- **1952:** *Portrait of Pietro Menis*. A portrait made during Troiani’s visit to Buia and preserved there; it is reproduced by the sitter in his scholarly memoir. ([Menis, fig. 5 and p. 125](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))

### Sacred sculpture

- *Christus*. An autonomous representation of Christ of unknown date and present location. ([Menis, p. 123](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- *San Giorgio di Bahía Blanca*. A religious sculpture exhibited at the Salón Nacional de Bellas Artes; whether “di Bahía Blanca” records its intended destination, later location, or conventional title is not explained. ([Menis, p. 124](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- *San Giovannino*. A devotional statue of the young John the Baptist, undated and unlocated in the consulted inventory. ([Menis, p. 125](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- *Sant’Anna*. A large devotional statue made for an unidentified church in Buenos Aires. ([Menis, p. 125](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- *Santa Rosa da Lima*. A monumental devotional sculpture in the Buenos Aires national sanctuary dedicated to the saint. ([Menis, p. 125](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- *Deposizione*. A religious sculptural composition of unrecorded date, material, and location. ([Menis, p. 125](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- *Cristo che caccia i profanatori del tempio*. A religious sculpture or relief depicting Christ expelling the profaners of the temple; the inventory gives no date, material, or location. ([Menis, p. 125](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- **By 1952:** *San Giovanni Battista*. A bronze devotional statue preserved in the church at Avilla di Buia; it is distinct from the undated *San Giovannino*. ([Menis, p. 125](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))

### Unitemized bodies of work

- **c. 1910–1963:** *Portraits, plaques, medals, funerary reliefs, and private sculptures*. This is a body of work rather than one object: the standard scholarly biography confirms sustained production in these media but does not enumerate the commissions, while Menis explicitly calls his own inventory “very partial.” Treating this corpus as a complete list of individually recoverable works would therefore manufacture a completeness the sources deny. ([Bergamini](https://www.dizionariobiograficodeifriulani.it/troiani-troiano/); [Menis, pp. 121, 125](https://it.scribd.com/document/613858483/lo-scultore-troiano-troiani-memoria-16143-1))
- **c. 1920s–1940s:** *Small bronzes of children and other figures*. A recurring body of small-format sculpture rather than a single titled work; only isolated examples such as *Niña con globo* and *Figura* are individually identified in the accessible published listings. ([El Arte de los Argentinos](https://www.zurbaran.com.ar/troiani-troiano/); [Bergamini](https://www.dizionariobiograficodeifriulani.it/troiani-troiano/))
