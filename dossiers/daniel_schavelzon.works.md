## Works by Daniel Gastón Schávelzon

### Bibliographies, catalogues, atlases, and manuals

- **1980:** *Corpus bibliográfico de la cultura olmeca*. A scholarly bibliography co-authored with Nelly Gutiérrez Solana; it organizes prior writing rather than presenting a single archaeological argument.

- **1983:** *La pirámide de Cuicuilco: álbum fotográfico (1922–1980)*. A historical photographic atlas: the selected and organized image sequence is as important as the accompanying text.

- **2004:** *Plano del potencial arqueológico de Buenos Aires*. A digital archaeological map, co-created with Mario Silveira and issued on CD-ROM; it is a research and planning instrument rather than a conventional book.

- **2005:** *Treinta siglos de imágenes: maquetas y representaciones de arquitectura en México y América Central*. An illustrated corpus and interpretive catalogue of pre-Hispanic architectural representations. Some records date it 2004, while Schávelzon’s principal bibliography gives 2005.

- **2005:** *Guía del patrimonio cultural de Buenos Aires, vol. III: Arqueología urbana*. A municipal heritage guide and inventory, designed for consultation and public use.

- **2005:** *La imagen de América: los dibujos de Francisco Mujica Diez de Bonilla*. A study and visual catalogue co-authored with Jorge Tomassi; its underlying objects are Mujica’s drawings, not works claimed for Schávelzon.

- **2001; 2018:** *Catálogo de cerámicas históricas de Buenos Aires (siglos XVI–XX), con notas sobre la región del Río de la Plata*. A typological reference work first manifested as a CD-ROM in 2001 and substantially remade as a printed/digital book in 2018; these are two manifestations of one continuing catalogue project.

- **2019/2020:** *Manual de arqueología urbana: técnicas para excavar Buenos Aires*. A protocol-like technical manual: a text intended to direct archaeological practice. Bibliographies variously assign it to 2019 or 2020, apparently reflecting preparation and publication dates.

- **2020:** *Catálogo de baldosas cerámicas empleadas en la arquitectura de Buenos Aires (siglos XIX–XX)*. A reference catalogue co-authored with Francisco Girelli and Martín Nerguizian Raris.

### Archaeology, architecture, and urban-history monographs

- **1980:** *El complejo arqueológico Mixteca-Puebla*. An archaeological and art-historical monograph proposing a regional cultural complex.

- **1981:** *Arqueología y arquitectura del Ecuador prehispánico*. A synthetic study joining archaeological evidence to architectural history.

- **1990:** *Las ciudades mayas: historia de las teorías sobre su estructura urbana*. A historiography of interpretations of Maya urbanism.

- **1990:** *La conservación del patrimonio cultural en América Latina: restauración de edificios prehispánicos en Mesoamérica (1750–1980)*. A historical and critical study of restoration practice.

- **1991:** *Arqueología histórica de Buenos Aires: la cultura material porteña de los siglos XVIII y XIX*. A foundational synthesis of Buenos Aires historical archaeology.

- **1992:** *La arqueología urbana en la Argentina*. A programmatic scholarly monograph that treats the living city as the archaeological unit of analysis.

- **1992:** *Arqueología histórica de Buenos Aires: túneles y construcciones subterráneas*. A study of subterranean structures that also contests the mythology surrounding Buenos Aires’s tunnels.

- **1993:** *El expolio del arte en la Argentina: robos y tráfico ilegal de obras de arte*. An investigative history of theft and illicit circulation; it crosses art history, heritage policy, and public intervention.

- **1999:** *Arqueología de Buenos Aires*. A broad synthesis for a readership beyond excavation specialists.

- **2000:** *The Historical Archaeology of Buenos Aires: A City at the End of the World*. An English-language synthesis made for an international scholarly context, not merely catalogued as a translation of one earlier Spanish title.

- **2000:** *Historias del comer y del beber: arqueología de la vajilla de mesa en Buenos Aires*. A material-culture history constructed through tableware and practices of consumption.

- **2003:** *Buenos Aires negra: arqueología histórica de una ciudad silenciada*. An archaeological reconstruction of Afro-Argentine presence; “silenced city” names both its subject and its intervention in public memory.

- **2003:** *Ejercicio plástico: el mural de Siqueiros en la Argentina*. A history of Siqueiros’s mural, co-authored with Héctor Mendizábal; the mural itself is not Schávelzon’s work.

- **2004:** *Ciudad y territorio entre los mayas: historia de las teorías sobre el espacio urbano*. A revised, internationally published manifestation of Schávelzon’s continuing Maya-urbanism project, closely related to the 1990 and 2008 books rather than wholly independent in subject.

- **2005:** *Túneles de Buenos Aires: historias, mitos y verdades del subsuelo porteño*. A public-facing historical and archaeological reassessment of underground Buenos Aires.

- **2007:** *Historia de un terremoto: Mendoza 1861*. A historical reconstruction of the earthquake and the city it transformed.

- **2007:** *Viejos son los trapos: de arqueología, ciudades y cosas que hay debajo de los pisos*. A co-authored work of archaeological popularization with Ana Igareta.

- **2008:** *Las ciudades mayas: un urbanismo de América Latina*. A further recasting of the Maya-city research project for architectural and Latin American urban-history readers.

- **2008:** *Mejor olvidar: la conservación del patrimonio cultural argentino*. A polemical critique of Argentine preservation practice; its title and argument make it an intervention, though not formally a manifesto.

- **2008:** *El laberinto del patrimonio cultural: cómo gestionarlo en una gran ciudad*. A heritage-management study with protocol-like ambitions. An online preliminary text without illustrations and a definitive paper version are distinct manifestations.

- **2008:** *El Área Fundacional de Puerto Deseado: estudios*. An interdisciplinary historical and archaeological study of the town’s foundational area.

- **2009:** *Arte y falsificación en América Latina*. An art-historical and forensic study of forgery.

- **2009:** *El Caserón de Rosas: historia y arqueología del paisaje de Palermo*. A combined landscape history and archaeological monograph.

- **2010:** *Las muertes de un caudillo: la tumba de Facundo Quiroga*. A historical, forensic, and archaeological investigation co-authored with Patricia Frazzi.

- **2010:** *Haciendo un mundo moderno: la arquitectura de Edward Taylor (1801–1867)*. An architectural monograph on Taylor’s work and its role in nineteenth-century modernization.

- **2010:** *Palermo: el Parque 3 de Febrero de Buenos Aires*. A landscape and urban history co-authored with Sonia Berjman.

- **2011:** *Arqueología de un conflicto social: la Semana Trágica y los Talleres Vasena*. An archaeological reading of political violence and industrial space.

- **2011:** *El mural de Siqueiros en Argentina: la historia de Ejercicio plástico*. A renewed historical account associated with the mural’s recovery and conservation.

- **2013:** *La historia de un mural: Siqueiros en la Argentina*. Another manifestation of the long-running Siqueiros research project; it revises and retells material treated in the 2003 and 2011 books.

- **2013:** *La Tambería del Inca: Héctor Greslebin, una búsqueda americana*. A study of Greslebin, architectural archaeology, and an Americanist intellectual project.

- **2013:** *El Pórtico Bizantino del Jardín Zoológico de Buenos Aires: una reflexión sobre nosotros mismos*. A heritage and architectural-history study, produced with Marina Vasta and Patricia Corsani.

- **2017:** *El silencio es oro: tráfico de arte durante el nazismo en la Argentina*. An investigative history of art trafficking and Nazi-era networks.

- **2017:** *Teyú Cuaré: arqueología de un refugio nazi en la Argentina*. A public-facing interpretation of the Misiones site. The identification of the ruins as a Nazi refuge attracted controversy, so the title’s attribution should be understood as Schávelzon’s archaeological interpretation, not an uncontested fact.

- **2018:** *Historias de la historia de la arquitectura argentina*. A two-volume historiographical work about how Argentine architectural history was made and narrated.

- **2019:** *El árbol de cemento: arquitecturas de rocallas, Argentina y América Latina*. An architectural and landscape history of artificial rockwork, a hybrid object that conventionally falls between architecture, sculpture, garden design, and craft.

- **2022:** *Arqueología de la Segunda Guerra Mundial en Sudamérica: el asentamiento nazi de Teyú Cuaré*. A scholarly reassessment co-authored with Ana Igareta; like the 2017 book, its characterization of the site remains an argued attribution.

- **2023:** *La arqueología urbana de Buenos Aires: excavando una ciudad imaginada*. A synthetic monograph defining urban archaeology as the study of the living city and examining the deliberate remaking of Buenos Aires’s ground.

- **2023:** *La historia de Kurth, falsificador*. A hybrid of oral history, biography, investigation, and historical novel, built from interviews conducted with an art forger. “Kurth Walheimer” is identified as a fictive name, and the protagonist’s claims cannot all be independently verified; the book therefore resists classification as straightforward documentary history.

- **2023; revised through 2025:** *Las cabezas olmecas en el imaginario universal*. An archaeological and reception-history study of the colossal Olmec heads. Its digital colophon distinguishes an original 2023 text, a 2024 first edition, and a third edition dated July 2025.

- **2025:** *Arqueología de la población indígena de Buenos Aires*. A digital monograph synthesizing Indigenous finds, documentary evidence, the La Noria site, and the hypothesis of a settlement erased by the 1580 colonial foundation; that final settlement remains explicitly a hypothesis rather than a settled attribution.

### Excavation reports and site-specific research objects

- **1991:** *El Cabildo de Mendoza: arqueología e historia para su recuperación*. An excavation and conservation report co-authored with Roberto Bárcena; its purpose is also operative, supplying knowledge for recovery of the site.

- **1994:** *Arqueología e historia de la Imprenta Coni, Buenos Aires*. A site report combining excavation with documentary history.

- **1995:** *Arqueología e historia del Cabildo de Buenos Aires: informe de las excavaciones 1991–1992*. A formal excavation report rather than a general narrative of the building.

- **1996:** *Arqueología histórica de Buenos Aires: excavaciones en la Imprenta Coni*. A book-length manifestation of the Coni investigation, related to but bibliographically distinct from the 1994 report.

- **1998:** *Las ruinas de San Francisco: arqueología e historia*. A report and historical reconstruction arising from the Mendoza research program.

- **1998:** *Arqueología histórica de Buenos Aires: excavaciones en Michelángelo*. A site report co-authored with Mario Silveira.

- **2001:** *Excavaciones arqueológicas en San Isidro*. A municipal excavation report co-authored with Mario Silveira.

- **2002:** *El Área Fundacional de Mendoza*. A CD-ROM research object presenting results from the continuing Mendoza project.

- **2003:** *Buenos Aires arqueología: la casa donde Ernesto Sábato ambientó Sobre héroes y tumbas*. A collaborative site study by Schávelzon, Mario Silveira, Mariano Ramos, N. Pérez, and G. Páez; the literary association is part of the site’s interpretation, not evidence that Sábato’s fictional house was literally recovered.

- **2004:** *El Área Fundacional de Mendoza, vol. III*. A further CD-ROM manifestation of the Mendoza research program, not simply another edition of the 2002 disc.

- **2005:** *Los conventillos de Buenos Aires: la Casa Mínima, un estudio arqueológico*. A collaborative building-archaeology study with Mario Sabugo, Mario Silveira, Pablo López Coda, and Jorge Ramos.

- **2009:** *La casa de Perón: arqueología de su infancia en Roque Pérez*. A site-specific archaeological and biographical study co-authored with Ana Igareta.

- **2010:** *Café de Hansen: historia y hallazgos en Palermo*. A historical and archaeological report on the remembered entertainment site.

- **2012:** *La Casa del Naranjo: arqueología de la arquitectura en el contexto municipal de Buenos Aires*. A building-archaeology report in which the standing structure, its alterations, and the excavation form one research object.

- **2012:** *Lítica histórica: la piedra en Buenos Aires en los siglos XVI al XX, usos y tecnologías*. A specialist material-culture study grounded in excavated stone.

- **2013:** *El Caserón de Rosas: fotografías de la Escuela Naval Militar*. A photographic-historical study co-authored with César Gotta and Abel Alexander; it is an archival image corpus as well as a book.

- **2014:** *Ushuaia: arqueología, historia y patrimonio*. A collaborative site and regional study with Patricia Frazzi and Ricardo Orsini.

- **2014:** *Casa del Virrey Liniers: hallazgos arqueológicos*. An excavation report co-authored with Odlanyer Hernández de Lara.

- **2019:** *Arqueología de rescate en Casa Rosada: del Palacio de los Virreyes a la Casa de Gobierno Nacional (1594–1884)*. A rescue-archaeology report documenting remains exposed within a functioning seat of government.

- **2020:** *Los entonces lóbregos sótanos: arqueología de rescate del primer Museo Etnográfico (1905–1927)*. A rescue report and institutional archaeology of the museum’s former premises.

- **2021:** *Arqueología de la ex Aduana Taylor: excavaciones en Plaza Colón (2018)*. A report on the 2018 excavation of remains associated with Edward Taylor’s customs house.

- **2021:** *El Barrio del Tambor: arqueología histórica en espacios afro de Paraná; excavaciones en la capilla de San Miguel Arcángel*. An excavation report and study of an Afro-Argentine urban space, co-authored with Alejandro Richard.

- **2022:** *Arqueología de la Patagonia Trágica: la comisaría de Puerto San Julián, Santa Cruz*. A site report co-authored with Patricia Frazzi and Matías Hernández.

- **2023:** *Balcarce 1050, Buenos Aires: peritaje arqueológico*. An expert archaeological assessment prompted by structural cracking, subsidence, and broken pipes. It is an unpublished or locally circulated professional report later made available by the Centro de Arqueología Urbana, and therefore resists treatment as a conventional “work.”

### Edited and compiled works

- **1983:** *Representaciones de arquitectura en la arqueología de América, vol. I*. A coordinated collective volume; Schávelzon made its scholarly frame and editorial organization, not every constituent text.

- **1984:** *La polémica del arte nacional en México (1850–1910)*. A documentary compilation whose selection and arrangement construct the argument.

- **2002:** *Arqueología histórica argentina*. An edited collective volume; authorship belongs separately to its contributors, while Schávelzon’s work is editorial.

- **2013:** *Actas del V Congreso Nacional de Arqueología Histórica*, vols. I–II. Conference proceedings co-edited or coordinated with Eduardo Rodríguez Leirado; these are a collective record, not a sole-authored monograph.

### Institutional and archaeological projects

- **1984 onward:** *Programa de Arqueología Urbana de la Universidad de Buenos Aires*. A sustained research project initiated by Schávelzon. It is not one bounded text or excavation, and its results belong to many collaborators as well as to its founder.

- **1988 onward:** *Centro de Investigaciones Ruinas de San Francisco / Área Fundacional de Mendoza*. An interdisciplinary research and heritage project established and directed by Schávelzon for nearly two decades; the institution and accumulated fieldwork form a body of work rather than a single obra.

- **1991 onward:** *Centro de Arqueología Urbana, Universidad de Buenos Aires*. An institution founded and directed by Schávelzon that produced excavations, archives, training, publications, and methods. Calling the whole institution his “work” risks erasing its collective authorship, so it is better classified as an initiated and sustained project.

- **1996 onward:** *Área de Arqueología Urbana, Government of the City of Buenos Aires*. A municipal archaeological structure founded by Schávelzon. It is an institutional creation whose later work cannot be attributed to him alone.

- **2015 onward:** *Teyú Cuaré investigation*. A field-research project conducted with Ana Igareta that reinterpreted previously known ruins in Misiones as a Second World War refuge. The site itself was neither created nor first noticed by them, and the proposed Nazi association has been contested; their obra is the investigation and interpretation, not the ruins.
