## Works by René Guénon

### Books and booklets composed or authorized by Guénon

- **1921:** *Introduction générale à l’étude des doctrines hindoues*. A doctrinal study developed from Guénon’s proposed doctoral thesis; he subsequently authorized substantial revisions, published posthumously in 1952, so the 1921 and definitive texts are not identical. [Bibliography and revision history](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ren%C3%A9_Gu%C3%A9non#Ouvrages_de_Ren%C3%A9_Gu%C3%A9non)
- **1921:** *Le Théosophisme, histoire d’une pseudo-religion*. A historical and polemical monograph, expanded with Guénon’s additional notes in the authorized 1928 edition. [Collected-works bibliography](https://editionsethos.com/guenon/)
- **1923:** *L’Erreur spirite*. A polemical study of spiritualism, psychical research, and reincarnationism. [Collected-works bibliography](https://editionsethos.com/guenon/)
- **1924:** *Orient et Occident*. A civilizational diagnosis and program for renewed intellectual relations between East and West; Guénon substantially revised it for the 1948 edition. [Bibliography and revision history](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ren%C3%A9_Gu%C3%A9non#Ouvrages_de_Ren%C3%A9_Gu%C3%A9non)
- **1925:** *L’Ésotérisme de Dante*. A short interpretive monograph on initiatic structures and symbolism in Dante, revised by Guénon for the 1949 edition. [Bibliography and revision history](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ren%C3%A9_Gu%C3%A9non#Ouvrages_de_Ren%C3%A9_Gu%C3%A9non)
- **1925:** *L’Homme et son devenir selon le Vêdânta*. A metaphysical exposition rather than a history of Hinduism; Guénon removed two chapters for the definitive 1947 edition. [Bibliography and revision history](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ren%C3%A9_Gu%C3%A9non#Ouvrages_de_Ren%C3%A9_Gu%C3%A9non)
- **1927:** *Le Roi du monde*. A synthetic study of the idea of a supreme spiritual centre, assembled partly from earlier articles and revised for the 1950 edition. [Collected-works bibliography](https://editionsethos.com/guenon/)
- **1927:** *La Crise du monde moderne*. A polemical diagnosis of modernity, revised in places for the authorized 1946 edition. [Bibliography and revision history](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ren%C3%A9_Gu%C3%A9non#Ouvrages_de_Ren%C3%A9_Gu%C3%A9non)
- **1929:** *Saint Bernard*. A biographical and doctrinal booklet, first issued in the series *La Vie et les œuvres de quelques grands saints*. [Collected-works bibliography](https://editionsethos.com/guenon/)
- **1929:** *Autorité spirituelle et pouvoir temporel*. A doctrinal intervention prompted by contemporary disputes but framed as a general account of priestly and royal authority; Guénon revised it for the 1947 edition. [Bibliography and revision history](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ren%C3%A9_Gu%C3%A9non#Ouvrages_de_Ren%C3%A9_Gu%C3%A9non)
- **1931:** *Le Symbolisme de la Croix*. A metaphysical treatise expressed through symbolic form, extending arguments first developed in articles and in *L’Homme et son devenir selon le Vêdânta*. [Collected-works bibliography](https://editionsethos.com/guenon/)
- **1932:** *Les États multiples de l’être*. A metaphysical treatise on possibility, infinity, being, and manifestation. [Collected-works bibliography](https://editionsethos.com/guenon/)
- **1939:** *La Métaphysique orientale*. A published transcription of Guénon’s Sorbonne lecture of 17 December 1925; it is therefore both an oral event and its later textual manifestation, not simply a book conceived in 1939. [Bibliographical account](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ren%C3%A9_Gu%C3%A9non#Ouvrages_de_Ren%C3%A9_Gu%C3%A9non)
- **1945:** *Le Règne de la Quantité et les Signes des Temps*. A synthetic diagnosis of modernity and cyclical decline, composed as a book. [Collected-works bibliography](https://editionsethos.com/guenon/)
- **1946:** *Les Principes du calcul infinitésimal*. A philosophical and metaphysical study of mathematical concepts, reworking material connected with Guénon’s earlier academic work. [Collected-works bibliography](https://editionsethos.com/guenon/)
- **1946:** *Aperçus sur l’Initiation*. An author-arranged book constructed largely by revising and ordering articles published before the Second World War; it is an obra assembled from previously separate manifestations. [Publication history](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aper%C3%A7us_sur_l%27initiation)
- **1946:** *La Grande Triade*. A synthetic book on the Far-Eastern triad Heaven–Earth–Man and its analogues. [Collected-works bibliography](https://editionsethos.com/guenon/)

### Lecture and pedagogical manifestations

- **17 December 1925:** *La Métaphysique orientale*. A lecture delivered at the Sorbonne; its 1939 booklet is the textual manifestation listed above, while the performed lecture was the earlier made event. [Bibliographical account](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ren%C3%A9_Gu%C3%A9non#Ouvrages_de_Ren%C3%A9_Gu%C3%A9non)
- **1917–1918, attribution disputed:** *Cours de philosophie / Psychologie*. A pedagogical manuscript or course whose psychology section was edited by Alessandro Grossato and published in 2001 as *Psychologie*. The edition expressly presented it as an attribution to Guénon; later defenders argued for authenticity, but it should not be silently promoted to an undisputed authored book. [Contemporary bibliographical notice](https://www.sciencesacree.com/medias/files/10.-livres-revues-et-articles-recus-science-sacree-n-3-4-2002-.pdf)

### Periodical project and pseudonymous textual corpora

- **1909–1912:** *La Gnose*. A periodical project directed by Guénon, not a wholly authored book: it combined editorial action with signed, pseudonymous, unsigned, and collaborative matter. Its associated unpublished file includes programmatic notices and a projected glossary of traditional terms. [Analytical index](https://www.index-rene-guenon.org/index_books.php)
- **1909–1912:** *Writings signed “Palingénius”*. A corpus beginning with “Le Démiurge,” not one fixed work. Guénon later resisted treating “Palingénius” as a merely literary pseudonym and described such signatures as representing distinct “entities”; consequently, attribution to the unitary œuvre “René Guénon” remains conceptually disputed even though personal authorship is accepted. [David Ringenbach’s scholarly discussion](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9782296265011_A24215002/preview-9782296265011_A24215002.pdf)
- **1913–1914:** *Writings signed “Le Sphinx” in La France antimaçonnique*. A pseudonymous corpus of articles and polemical responses, not a single book. As with “Palingénius,” Guénon himself resisted reducing the signature to an ordinary authorial alias. [David Ringenbach’s scholarly discussion](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9782296265011_A24215002/preview-9782296265011_A24215002.pdf)
- **1925–1930:** *Réponses à des enquêtes*. Three short public responses—on appeals from the East, international culture, and a “new humanism”—preserved as a small corpus rather than an author-made book. [Analytical index](https://www.index-rene-guenon.org/index_books.php)
- **1920s–1950:** *Articles, notes, post-scripts, obituaries, and reviews signed René Guénon*. A dispersed textual body approaching 500 articles, besides a very large number of reviews; it resists treatment as one work because the pieces originated independently in numerous journals and were repeatedly revised, absorbed into books, or regrouped after Guénon’s death. André Désilets’s scholarly index-bibliography was itself criticized for omissions caused by relying too heavily on later collections. [Scholarly review of the index-bibliography](https://www.persee.fr/doc/assr_0335-5985_1980_num_50_2_2215_t1_0265_0000_4)
- **1925–1950:** *Articles et comptes rendus du Voile d’Isis et des Études traditionnelles*. A journal corpus comprising articles, book reviews, reviews of periodical articles, notices, and obituaries; it is a body of made texts rather than a book, although an editorial selection appeared as *Articles et comptes rendus, tome I*. [Analytical contents](https://www.index-rene-guenon.org/index_books.php)
- **1925–1927:** *Écrits pour Regnabit*. A corpus of articles on Christian and universal symbolism originally made for the journal *Regnabit*; the title belongs to the 1999 editorial collection, not to a book planned by Guénon. [Analytical contents](https://www.index-rene-guenon.org/index_books.php)
- **Various dates:** *Responses in The Speculative Mason*. A set of English-language answers to readers’ questions, sometimes issued under signatures other than “René Guénon”; later collections absorb them, but they originated as serial responses rather than a unified work. [Contents of the later collected corpus](https://www.rene-guenon.org/imaguen/CATA-26-RG.pdf)

### Posthumous editorial manifestations of existing texts

- **1952:** *Initiation et Réalisation spirituelle*. A posthumous sequel to *Aperçus sur l’Initiation*, edited by Jean Reyor from articles written between 1945 and the end of 1950. Its constituent texts are Guénon’s; the volume as volume is an editor’s posthumous construction. [Editor’s account](https://classiques.uqam.ca/classiques/guenon_rene/apercus_esoterisme_chretien/apercus_esoterisme_chretien_avant_pr.html)
- **1954:** *Aperçus sur l’ésotérisme chrétien*. A posthumous thematic collection of previously published articles, not a manuscript book completed by Guénon. [Contents and publication data](https://www.index-rene-guenon.org/index_books.php)
- **1962:** *Symboles fondamentaux de la Science sacrée*. A posthumous collection edited by Michel Vâlsan from Guénon’s symbolism articles; later editions also circulate under the shortened title *Symboles de la Science sacrée*. The thematic sequence is an editorial manifestation rather than a book Guénon finalized. [Posthumous bibliography](https://reneguenon.net/guenonbibliografia)
- **1964:** *Études sur la Franc-Maçonnerie et le Compagnonnage, tome I*. A posthumous arrangement of articles and reviews concerning Freemasonry and compagnonnage. [Analytical index](https://www.index-rene-guenon.org/index_books.php)
- **1965:** *Études sur la Franc-Maçonnerie et le Compagnonnage, tome II*. The continuation of that editorial collection; some later bibliographies date particular editions 1971, so 1965 identifies the first publication rather than every manifestation. [Posthumous bibliography](https://reneguenon.net/guenonbibliografia)
- **1968:** *Études sur l’Hindouisme*. A posthumous thematic collection of articles, reviews, and review notices rather than a continuous treatise composed under this title. Some bibliographies give 1967; the indexed edition and standard publication lists give 1968, so the date is not entirely stable across references. [Analytical index](https://www.index-rene-guenon.org/index_books.php)
- **1970:** *Formes traditionnelles et cycles cosmiques*. A posthumous collection arranged by Roger Maridort from articles and reviews on cycles, Atlantis, Kabbalah, Hermeticism, and related traditional forms. [Analytical contents](https://www.index-rene-guenon.org/index_books.php)
- **1973:** *Aperçus sur l’ésotérisme islamique et le taoïsme*. A posthumous thematic collection prepared by Roger Maridort; it gathers previously independent articles rather than realizing a demonstrable book project left by Guénon. [Posthumous bibliography](https://reneguenon.net/guenonbibliografia)
- **1973; later edition 1986:** *Comptes rendus*. An editorial gathering of Guénon’s reviews. Bibliographies sometimes cite only the 1986 edition, but the standard posthumous list records a 1973 first publication; the collection should not be mistaken for a book authored in that form. [Posthumous bibliography](https://reneguenon.net/guenonbibliografia)
- **1976:** *Mélanges*. A posthumous miscellany edited by Roger Maridort, joining independent pieces on metaphysics, cosmology, initiation, symbolism, and other subjects. Its heterogeneity is constitutive: it is an editorial container, not a unitary Guénonian project. [Analytical contents](https://www.index-rene-guenon.org/index_books.php)
- **1999:** *Écrits pour Regnabit*. A retrospective collection of Guénon’s contributions to *Regnabit*. The articles are works by Guénon; their regrouping and present title are posthumous editorial acts. [Analytical contents](https://www.index-rene-guenon.org/index_books.php)
- **2001, attribution disputed:** *Psychologie*. An editorially established portion of the alleged 1917–1918 *Cours de philosophie*, published explicitly as “René Guénon (attribution).” It therefore resists being called an undisputed Guénon work. [Contemporary bibliographical notice](https://www.sciencesacree.com/medias/files/10.-livres-revues-et-articles-recus-science-sacree-n-3-4-2002-.pdf)
- **2002:** *Articles et comptes rendus, tome I: 1925–1950*. A posthumous selection of articles, reviews, notices, and obituaries from *Le Voile d’Isis* and *Études traditionnelles*. The volume is an archival/editorial manifestation, not a new work by Guénon. [BnF bibliographical notice](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb38888076p)
- **2013:** *Recueil*. A posthumous attempt to gather writings omitted from earlier collections, including preliminary forms of later books, obscure journal contributions, reviews, and pseudonymous pieces. Its editor explicitly defined it as a recovery project, and it cannot establish that the gathered pieces formed one project for Guénon. [Publisher-description reproduced in catalogue](https://www.rene-guenon.org/imaguen/CATA-26-RG.pdf)
- **2013:** *Fragments doctrinaux*. An editorial extraction from approximately 600 letters to thirty correspondents, organized as doctrinal material. The letters are Guénon’s made texts, but the “fragments” and their book-form arrangement are posthumous creations. [Bibliographical listing](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ren%C3%A9_Gu%C3%A9non#Bibliographie)
- **2020–2021:** *L’Intégrale de René Guénon*. A modern collected-works manifestation in multiple volumes, dividing lifetime publications from posthumous collections. It is useful as the governing bibliography but is not itself an obra conceived by Guénon, and “integral” should not be read as resolving all questions surrounding dispersed, pseudonymous, or attributed texts. [Collected-edition description](https://editionsethos.com/guenon/)

### Correspondence

- **Lifelong; published in separate selections:** *Letters and correspondence*. Guénon made hundreds of letters that function variously as private counsel, doctrinal exposition, correction, and editorial direction; they resist classification as either books or a single work because surviving publications are selections organized by recipient or subject. The reported corpus behind *Fragments doctrinaux* alone comprises about 600 letters to thirty correspondents. [Bibliographical listing](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ren%C3%A9_Gu%C3%A9non#Bibliographie)
- **Correspondence published 2012:** *Paris–Le Caire: Correspondance entre Louis Cattiaux et René Guénon*. A bilateral correspondence, hence a jointly produced documentary corpus rather than a sole-authored Guénon book; its 2012 form is editorial and posthumous. [Bibliographical listing](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ren%C3%A9_Gu%C3%A9non#Bibliographie)
