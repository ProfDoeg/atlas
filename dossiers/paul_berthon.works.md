## Works by Paul Berthon

### Posters and other public graphic works

- **1895–1896:** *Leçons de violon, de violoncelle & de solfège*. Advertising poster in colour lithography; impressions without the lettering also circulated as decorative prints. The date is variously given as 1895 and 1896. ([BnF/Gallica record](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b9005041s); [Arwas catalogue cited by Christie’s](https://www.christies.com/en/lot/lot-732079))
- **1895–1896:** *Salon des Cent, 17e exposition*. Lithographic exhibition poster commissioned by *La Plume*; the BnF dates it 1895, while the Musée des Arts Décoratifs records its creation and use in 1896. ([BnF copy](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b9005040c); [MAD Paris record](https://collections.madparis.fr/fr/document/collections/627abaab3edd9548006c62d5))
- **1896:** *Almanach d’Alsace et de Lorraine*. Commercial lithographic poster advertising the almanac published by Strauss. ([BnF/Gallica image and catalogue data](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b90049279))
- **1896:** *Folies-Bergère: Liane de Pougy*. Tall colour-lithographic music-hall poster; later reduced as a collector’s print. ([Musée Carnavalet record](https://www.parismuseescollections.paris.fr/fr/musee-carnavalet/oeuvres/folies-bergere-liane-de-pougy); [BnF copy](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b53254636w))
- **c. 1896:** *Tisane Gauloise*. Commercial colour-lithographic poster. Catalogues disagree over whether it belongs precisely to 1896 or nearer 1900, so the date remains approximate. ([Christie’s catalogue record](https://www.christies.com/en/lot/lot-732079); [Arwas bibliography](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb34615911z))
- **1897:** *L’Ermitage*. Poster for the literary review, printed by G. Enard; Arwas catalogues it as no. 4. ([LACMA record](https://collections.lacma.org/object/59906); [auction record citing Arwas](https://ader-paris.fr/v/183186-affiches-anciennes-et-dapres-guerre?page=4))
- **1897:** *Chemins de fer de l’Ouest: Normandie–Bretagne*. Railway-tourism poster combining regional costume, landscape, monuments, and seaside imagery. Some surviving impressions shorten the heading to *Normandie*. ([MAD Paris record](https://collections.madparis.fr/fr/document/transport-ferroviaire/627abab43edd9548006cb22b); [BnF copy](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b9014458d))
- **1897–1898:** *Sainte-Marie-des-Fleurs*. Literary poster for René Boylesve’s novel; sources date it either 1897 or 1898, and Arwas catalogues it as no. 5. ([BnF/Gallica copy](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b9012663p); [record citing Arwas no. 5](https://www.interencheres.com/art-decoration/affiches-anciennes-et-dapres-guerre-690224/lot-89039560.html))
- **1898:** *Le Livre de Magda: Poésies par Armand Silvestre*. Colour-lithographic literary poster, not the book’s verbal text; it was printed by Chaix. ([Rijksmuseum record](https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/en/collection/RP-P-2015-26-2116); [Christie’s catalogue record](https://www.christies.com/en/lot/lot-732079))
- **c. 1898:** *Revue d’Art dramatique*. Poster for the Paris theatrical periodical and its published plays; no secure day or month is supplied in the consulted catalogue records. ([Cooper Hewitt collection search](https://collection.cooperhewitt.org/people/18041635/objects/); [Arwas bibliography](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb34615911z))
- **1899:** *Société des Amis des arts de la Manche*. Large colour-lithographic exhibition poster. ([BnF/Gallica copy](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b9004928q))
- **1899:** *Délicieuse eau de table: Source des Roches*. Commercial poster for a mineral water, printed by Chaix. ([MAD Paris record](https://collections.madparis.fr/document/eau-minerale-plate/627abaab3edd9548006c6324))
- **c. 1900:** *Jane Othello*. Entertainment poster showing the performer with a pearl necklace; the MAD holds an edition before the advertising text and explicitly relates it to the lettered BnF poster. ([MAD Paris record](https://collections.madparis.fr/fr/document/collections/627abaad3edd9548006c75ca); [BnF image](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b90131389))
- **c. 1900:** *Geneviève Williams*. Music-hall or theatrical advertising poster; the surviving BnF object establishes the title but not an exact date. ([BnF/Gallica copy](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b9004960x))
- **1900:** *Salon des Arts libéraux*. Exhibition poster representing two women at a printing press; versions without the lettering were also issued for collectors, making it both advertisement and autonomous print. ([Royal Albert Memorial Museum record](https://rammcollections.org.uk/object/20-1932-131/))
- **1901:** *Sa très gracieuse majesté la reine Wilhelmine*. Colour-lithographic portrait poster or commemorative print published by J. F. van de Ven at Baarn; the division between poster, portrait, and decorative panel is unstable. Arwas catalogues it as no. 61. ([Rijksmuseum record](https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/en/collection/RP-P-2015-26-2117); [Arwas-based description](https://www.artoftheprint.com/catalogue/catalogue_ba_bl2.htm))
- **1901:** *Sarah Bernhardt as Mélissinde in La Princesse lointaine*. Colour-lithographic theatrical portrait; it operates as both celebrity image and poster-derived decorative panel. Arwas catalogues it as no. 62, although auction descriptions sometimes give “Arwas 90,” apparently a page rather than a catalogue number. ([National Gallery of Art record](https://www.nga.gov/artworks/60945-sarah-bernhardt-melisande))
- **After 1901, date uncertain:** *Athalie, d’après la tragédie de Jean Racine*. Cinema poster for Michel Carré’s Pathé-Frères adaptation. Its appearance in later film advertising means it should not be assigned to Berthon’s pre-1902 production without qualification. ([BnF/Gallica copy](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b90121439))
- **1911:** *Quinquina Bonnet, apéritif de luxe*. Commercial poster carrying the date 1911. It is important evidence against the formerly repeated claim that Berthon died in 1909, but it falls outside the chronological limits assumed by Arwas’s graphic checklist and needs further documentary study. ([Bibliothèque Forney/Paris Musées image record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:BERTHON,_Paul._Quinquina_Bonnet,_ap%C3%A9ritif_de_luxe,_1911._(49799063546).jpg))

### Decorative panels and autonomous lithographs

- **1897:** *La Viole*. Numbered colour-lithographic decorative panel of a medievalizing woman musician; the title is supplied in brackets by the museum rather than securely inscribed on every state. ([MAD Paris record](https://collections.madparis.fr/fr/document/panneau-dcoratif/627abaaf3edd9548006c89e6))
- **1898:** *Mai*. Tall colour lithograph issued in numbered impressions; Arwas catalogues it as no. 23. ([Daguerre catalogue citing Arwas 23](https://www.gazette-drouot.com/telechargement/catalogue?venteId=155641))
- **1898:** *Mandore*. Decorative colour lithograph of a woman with a stringed instrument. Sources disagree between 1898 and 1901, probably because distinct states or later publication are being conflated. ([Pinacothèque exhibition dossier](https://www.marc-restellini.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/02/DP-Art-Nouveau-FR-BD.pdf); [later-state description](https://www.lot-art.com/auction-lots/Paul-Berthon-Mandore-1901/72-paul_berthon_1901-16.3.25-ncag))
- **1898:** *Femme de profil*. Original colour lithograph commissioned for *Les Maîtres de l’affiche*, rather than a reduced reproduction of an existing poster; Arwas catalogues it as no. 39 in some trade records, but that number is also assigned there to *Vision antique*, so the secondary numbering is inconsistent. ([Art of the Print catalogue](https://www.artoftheprint.com/catalogue/catalogue_ba_bl2.htm))
- **1899:** *La Viole de gambe*. Colour-lithographic decorative panel; Arwas catalogues it as no. 29. ([Arwas-based auction index](https://www.lotsearch.net/artist/paul-berthon/archive?order=DESC&orderBy=lot-title&perPage=50))
- **1899:** *Roses de Noël*. Colour-lithographic decorative panel, issued in several states; Arwas no. 30. ([National Gallery of Victoria record](https://www.ngv.vic.gov.au/explore/collection/work/24666/))
- **1899:** *Notre-Dame de Paris*. Architectural colour lithograph rather than a book illustration, despite its literary-sounding title; Arwas no. 34. ([catalogue entry citing Arwas 34](https://www.librairiedenis.fr/sites/librairiedenis.fr/files/2021-09/cat_lib_denis.pdf))
- **1899:** *La Pointe de Bretteville*. Landscape-and-figure decorative lithograph published by Edmond Sagot, forming part of a group of similarly proportioned panels. ([Arwas-based work description](https://www.1stdibs.com/de/creators/paul-berthon-1872-1909-french/))
- **1899:** *Effet de soir sur la côte*. Decorative colour lithograph in the same landscape-and-figure group as *La Pointe de Bretteville* and *Vision antique*. ([Arwas-based auction index](https://www.lotsearch.net/artist/paul-berthon/archive?order=DESC&orderBy=lot-title&perPage=80))
- **1899:** *Vision antique*. Horizontal colour-lithographic decorative panel published by Edmond Sagot; three states are recorded, and Arwas catalogues it as no. 39. ([Arwas-based catalogue entry](https://www.artoftheprint.com/artistpages/berthon_paul_emile_visionantique.htm))
- **1899:** *Le Président Krüger*. Lithographic portrait of Paul Kruger; it is a political portrait rather than straightforward advertising, and surviving descriptions do not establish whether it was commissioned or independently published. ([Rijksmuseum record](https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/en/collection/RP-P-1984-226); [Arwas-based auction index](https://www.lotsearch.net/artist/paul-berthon/archive?order=DESC&orderBy=lot-title&perPage=80))
- **c. 1899:** *Le Sureau I*. Tall colour-lithographic decorative panel; Arwas no. 49. The numeral records one composition in a related pair, not a printing state. ([Nelson-Atkins Museum record](https://art.nelson-atkins.org/objects/21032/the-elderberry-i-le-sureau-i))
- **c. 1899:** *Le Sureau II*. Companion decorative panel to *Le Sureau I*; its precise publication date is not fixed in the accessible catalogue records. ([Arwas bibliography](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb34615911z))
- **1899:** *La Princesse au crapaud*. Colour-lithographic decorative panel, sometimes shortened to *La Femme au crapaud*; Arwas no. 41. ([record citing Arwas 41](https://www.interencheres.com/en-US/art-decoration/affiches-anciennes-et-dapres-guerre-690224/lot-89039550.html))
- **1899:** *La Vague*. Colour-lithographic decorative panel printed by Chaix; Arwas no. 42. ([auction catalogue citing Arwas](https://ader-paris.fr/v/183186-affiches-anciennes-et-dapres-guerre?page=4); [BnF copy](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b53015697c))
- **1899:** *Six Petits Nus*. Suite of six narrow colour lithographs, catalogued by Arwas as nos. 43–48. Because the plates often circulate detached and most have no stable inscribed titles, the suite is the secure obra while such names as *Petit nu au lapin* are descriptive market titles. ([suite and numbering record](https://www.lotsearch.net/artist/paul-berthon/archive?perPage=80); [individual plate identified as Arwas 47](https://www.invaluable.com/artist/berthon-paul-dd607widon/sold-at-auction-prices/))
- **1899:** *Phryné*. Tall decorative lithograph representing the classical courtesan; it also appears within the publishing project *Les Courtisanes*. ([BnF-derived image record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Phryne_by_Paul_Berthon_(1899).jpg))
- **1899:** *Salomé*. Decorative lithograph announced as no. 1 of *Les Courtisanes, suite d’estampes décoratives*; it is therefore simultaneously an individual print and part of an incompletely documented serial project. ([Rijksmuseum object image and inscription](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Les_Courtisanes_Suite_d%27Estampes_Decoratives_Lithographies_Originals_de_Paul_Berthon_No_1_Salom%C3%A9,_asset_jo4ENaoVrkFdnJLMYhFEA4IU.tif))
- **1899:** *Les Courtisanes*. Projected suite of decorative lithographs, securely represented by *Salomé* and *Phryné*. Its intended extent and whether Berthon completed the whole project are not established by the consulted sources, so it resists treatment as a finished portfolio. ([BnF-derived *Salomé* record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Les_Courtisanes_Suite_d%27Estampes_Decoratives_Lithographies_Originals_de_Paul_Berthon_No_1_Salom%C3%A9,_asset_jo4ENaoVrkFdnJLMYhFEA4IU.tif); [Arwas bibliography](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb34615911z))
- **c. 1899:** *Les Fils de la Vierge*. Colour-lithographic decorative panel issued by the Atelier Paul Berthon; the title refers to gossamer threads rather than to a verbal text. ([BnF/Gallica copy](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b53015866f))
- **c. 1899:** *Les Pipeaux*. Horizontal decorative lithograph of a woman with pipes; the accessible catalogue evidence does not fix a precise year. ([BnF-derived image record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Les_Pipeaux_par_Paul_Berthon.png))
- **c. 1899:** *Bercerie*. Decorative print whose title is sometimes normalized as *Berceuse* or *Bercerie*; the latter is what appears in the digitized historical source. ([BnF-derived image record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bercerie_par_Paul_Berthon.png))
- **c. 1899:** *Harpiste*. Decorative lithograph identified descriptively by its harp-playing figure; it may also be catalogued as *Femme à la harpe*, so the title is not entirely stable. ([BnF-derived image record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Harpiste_par_Paul_Berthon.png))
- **c. 1899:** *La Femme aux iris*. Decorative figure study or panel; its surviving title is descriptive rather than demonstrably authorial. ([BnF-derived image record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:La_Femme_aux_iris_par_Paul_Berthon.png))
- **1900:** *Les Boules de neige*. Horizontal colour-lithographic decorative panel issued in lettered and unlettered states; Arwas no. 53. Some sale catalogues misdate it 1896. ([Cleveland Museum of Art record](https://www.clevelandart.org/art/2012.286))
- **1900:** *Les Églantines*. Colour-lithographic decorative panel issued in at least two states; Arwas no. 54. ([Arwas-based catalogue entry](https://williampcarlfineprints.com/artist/berthon/))
- **c. 1900:** *Les Violettes*. Colour-lithographic decorative panel; an auction catalogue’s date “1890” is incompatible with Berthon’s documented career and is almost certainly a transcription error, so no exact year should be forced. ([auction record citing Arwas no. 55](https://ader-paris.fr/v/183186-affiches-anciennes-et-dapres-guerre?page=4))
- **c. 1900:** *Jeunes filles lisant*. Colour lithograph of girls reading; its title is descriptive and the exact date is not securely documented in the accessible Arwas-derived records. ([Arwas-based auction index](https://www.lotsearch.net/artist/paul-berthon/archive?order=ASC&orderBy=dollarBasedPrice-startPrice&page=3&perPage=80))
- **1901:** *Concert mystique*. Colour-lithographic decorative panel with musical and devotional overtones; it is not a musical composition despite its title. ([Arwas-based auction index](https://www.lotsearch.net/artist/paul-berthon/archive?order=DESC&orderBy=lot-title&perPage=80))
- **1901:** *La Lyre*. Colour-lithographic decorative panel printed by Bourgerie; one Christie’s record cites “Arwas 112,” apparently a page reference rather than a checklist number. ([Christie’s record](https://www.christies.com/en/lot/lot-732079))

### Print portfolios and serial projects

- **1899:** *Six Petits Nus*. Portfolio or suite of six autonomous lithographic plates; individual sheets are works, but their frequently improvised modern titles make the suite the more reliable cataloguing unit. ([Arwas nos. 43–48 cited here](https://www.lotsearch.net/artist/paul-berthon/archive?perPage=80))
- **c. 1900:** *Visages: Réclames pour l’imprimeur Gerschel*. At least five lithographic faces used as demonstrations or advertisements for the lithographic supplier Gerschel. They resist a simple “fine-art print” classification because the image and commercial colour sample are materially the same thing. ([Ader catalogue citing Arwas, pp. 124–125](https://www.gazette-drouot.com/telechargement/catalogue?venteId=77367))
- **1902:** *Six Estampes originales de Paul Berthon (Les Six Vierges)*. Complete portfolio of six single-colour lithographic portraits, issued in 200 copies for Maison E. Gerschel; the wrapper is also Berthon’s design. - **1902:** *La Fille aux Houx*. First plate of *Les Six Vierges*; lithographic portrait. ([complete portfolio description](https://aba.org.uk/assets/catalogues/info%40simsreed.com/20250206_sims-reed-Modern-Illustrated-Books-February-2025-small.pdf))
- **1902:** *La Femme aux Aiglents*. Second plate of *Les Six Vierges*. The unusual printed title is sometimes silently “corrected” by dealers; it is retained here as listed on the portfolio. - **1902:** *La Vierge aux Lys*. Third plate of *Les Six Vierges*; single-colour lithographic portrait. ([complete portfolio description](https://aba.org.uk/assets/catalogues/info%40simsreed.com/20250206_sims-reed-Modern-Illustrated-Books-February-2025-small.pdf))
- **1902:** *La Florentine*. Fourth plate of *Les Six Vierges*. ([complete portfolio description](https://aba.org.uk/assets/catalogues/info%40simsreed.com/20250206_sims-reed-Modern-Illustrated-Books-February-2025-small.pdf))
- **1902:** *Sainte Philomène*. Fifth plate of *Les Six Vierges*. ([complete portfolio description](https://aba.org.uk/assets/catalogues/info%40simsreed.com/20250206_sims-reed-Modern-Illustrated-Books-February-2025-small.pdf))

### Book, portfolio, and periodical graphics

- **1895–1900:** *Les Maîtres de l’affiche* cover design. Recurrent cover or portfolio design for Jules Chéret’s publication; it is a publishing framework rather than one of the reproduced poster plates. ([digitized cover category](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Les_Ma%C3%AEtres_de_l%27Affiche_cover_by_Paul_Berthon))
- **1897:** *Les Maîtres de l’affiche*, first-volume binding design. Bookbinding design executed by Jean Engel after Berthon’s model; Berthon made the design, not necessarily the physical binding. ([publication history](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Ma%C3%AEtres_de_l%27affiche); [Arwas bibliography](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb34615911z))
- **1897:** *Les Chansons éternelles*. Cover or title-page drawing for Paul Redonnel’s poetry collection; Berthon was one contributor to a multi-artist Gesamtkunstwerk-like publication and was not the author of its poems. ([BnF-derived record](https://jenikirbyhistory.getarchive.net/amp/media/les-chansons-eternelles-par-paul-berthon-abc1c4); [edition description](https://www.abebooks.co.uk/signed/Chansons-%C3%A9ternelles-Nouvelle-%C3%A9dition-illustr%C3%A9e-REDONNEL/30644408014/bd))
- **July 1897:** *L’Image*, cover. Wood-engraved or woodcut periodical cover; it is a manifestation made for serial publication, not an autonomous lithograph, though detached copies now circulate as prints. ([digitized cover](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:L%27Image_cover_08_Berthon.png))
- **1898:** *Le Livre de Magda*. Poster and associated graphic design for Armand Silvestre’s poetry volume; Berthon’s contribution is visual and should not be mistaken for authorship of the poems. ([Rijksmuseum poster record](https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/en/collection/RP-P-2015-26-2116))
- **1899:** *La Plume*, portrait and ornamental contributions. Berthon supplied reproduced drawings—including *Phryné*, *Salomé*, *Bercerie*, musicians, and figure studies—to the journal’s survey of his work; these printed manifestations do not always establish separate editions of the underlying drawings. ([digitized 1899 volume](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:La_Plume,_ann%C3%A9e_1899.djvu))
- **c. 1900:** Invitation card with female profile and floral ornament. Ephemeral graphic design known through a reproduced surviving card; no secure original title or exact date is established, so it resists treatment as a conventionally titled work. ([digitized example](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Carte_d%27invitation_par_Paul_Berthon.png))
- **c. 1900:** *La Carrière d’un navigateur: Albert Ier, prince de Monaco*. Two-part commemorative print or illustrated biographical chart, combining portraiture, ships, and episodes from the prince’s career. It is closer to an infographic or visual biography than to a single pictorial panel. ([BnF/Gallica record](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b52506490n))

### Designs for furniture, ceramics, and bindings

- **1895:** Furniture designs exhibited at the Salon. Contemporary and later biographical sources attest designs for furniture, but the accessible catalogue does not furnish a secure item-by-item list or titles; these therefore remain documented projects rather than safely enumerable objects. ([Bénézit-derived biographical notice](https://www.oxfordartonline.com/benezit); [Arwas bibliography](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb34615911z))
- **c. 1895:** Untitled design for a cabinet or other piece of furniture. A lithographed *Projet pour un meuble* survives, but the generic wording is a description, not demonstrably Berthon’s title, and it is uncertain whether the furniture itself was made. ([auction record](https://ader-paris.fr/v/183186-affiches-anciennes-et-dapres-guerre?page=4))
- **Late 1890s:** Ceramic designs for Villeroy & Boch. These are an attributed body of industrial-design work rather than a securely reconstructed list of finished ceramic objects; the standard Berthon literature says that he designed ceramics for the firm, but completeness and production status remain unresolved. ([Musée d’Orsay authority record](https://www.musee-orsay.fr/fr/ressources/repertoire-artistes-personnalites/paul-berthon-3360); [Arwas bibliography](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb34615911z))
- **1897 and later:** Bookbindings and binding designs. Berthon designed bindings—including the *Maîtres de l’affiche* scheme—but surviving bindings may have been physically executed by professional binders such as Jean Engel; “made by Berthon” here means authorship of the decorative design, not necessarily fabrication. ([publication history](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Ma%C3%AEtres_de_l%27affiche); [Arwas bibliography](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb34615911z))

### Attribution and catalogue limits

- **Undated:** Works signed “Paul Émile Berton” or attributed to the landscape painter Paul Émile Berthon. These resist inclusion altogether: museum and authority records warn that Paul Louis Joseph Berthon, the poster designer born in Villefranche-sur-Saône, has repeatedly been confused with a different landscape painter born at Chartrettes. Such works are excluded unless the graphic catalogue or an institutional record securely assigns them to the designer. ([Wikimedia authority warning](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Paul_Berthon); [Musée d’Orsay record](https://www.musee-orsay.fr/fr/ressources/repertoire-artistes-personnalites/paul-berthon-3360))
- **1890s–1902:** Untitled studies, drawings, trial proofs, and alternate states. Arwas’s catalogue distinguishes many states and records ninety-four original lithographs, but a state is not automatically a separate obra; unlettered proofs, lettered posters, colour variants, and later reduced reproductions have therefore not been multiplied into independent works here. ([Arwas-based summary](https://www.artoftheprint.com/catalogue/catalogue_ba_bl2.htm))
- **After 1902:** Later output and the erroneous 1909 death date. Arwas and many museum databases treated Berthon as having died in 1909, whereas civil-status research and the Musée d’Orsay give 27 February 1934; consequently, the traditional catalogue cannot be assumed complete for 1902–1934. The dated 1911 *Quinquina Bonnet* poster demonstrates the problem rather than resolving the extent of the later oeuvre. ([Musée d’Orsay record](https://www.musee-orsay.fr/fr/ressources/repertoire-artistes-personnalites/paul-berthon-3360); [1911 poster record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:BERTHON,_Paul._Quinquina_Bonnet,_ap%C3%A9ritif_de_luxe,_1911._(49799063546).jpg))
