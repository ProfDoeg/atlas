## Works by Carl Gustav Jacob Jacobi

### Independent treatises, tables, and author-shaped collections

- **1825:** *Disquisitiones analyticae de fractionibus simplicibus*. Jacobi’s inaugural dissertation: an independently issued academic treatise on the decomposition of rational functions.
- **1829:** *Fundamenta nova theoriae functionum ellipticarum*. A monograph establishing a new systematic theory and notation for elliptic functions.
- **1832:** *Commentatio de transformatione integralis duplicis indefiniti in formam simpliciorem*. A separately issued Latin memoir on transforming an indefinite double integral.
- **1839:** *Canon arithmeticus, sive tabulae quibus exhibentur …*. A computational artefact as much as a book: Jacobi’s tables of indices for primes and prime powers below 1,000. It was omitted from the later *Gesammelte Werke*, so the collected edition alone is not complete.
- **1846–1857:** *Opuscula mathematica*. A three-volume selected collection: Jacobi prepared the first volume and substantially prepared the second, but Dirichlet issued volume II after his death and Borchardt compiled volume III; the whole therefore resists attribution as a single wholly author-made object.
- **1846:** *Widmung des ersten Bandes der Opuscula mathematica an Friedrich Wilhelm IV.* A dedicatory address, not a mathematical memoir; its action is ceremonial and political, so “manifesto” is closer than “treatise.”
- **1866; revised 1884:** *Vorlesungen über Dynamik*. A posthumous reconstruction of Jacobi’s 1842–43 Königsberg lectures from C. W. Borchardt’s notes, edited first by Alfred Clebsch and then by Eduard Lottner. It preserves Jacobi’s teaching project but is not a book whose final wording Jacobi authored or approved.
- **1881–1891:** *C. G. J. Jacobi’s Gesammelte Werke*. An eight-volume editorial manifestation of Jacobi’s corpus, commissioned by the Prussian Academy and edited principally by Borchardt, Clebsch, and Weierstrass. It is not itself a lifetime work by Jacobi and includes posthumous manuscripts, letters, reconstructed lectures, and texts by other hands.

### Elliptic and theta functions

- **1827:** *Extrait de deux lettres de M. Jacobi de l’Université de Kœnigsberg à M. Schumacher*. Two published mathematical letters announcing results on elliptic functions; epistolary communication made public as a research text.
- **1828:** *Demonstratio theorematis ad theoriam functionum ellipticarum spectantis*. A Latin research memoir proving a theorem in elliptic-function theory.
- *Addition au mémoire de M. Abel sur les fonctions elliptiques*. A published intervention in Abel’s memoir rather than a self-contained treatise.
- *Note sur la décomposition d’un nombre donné en quatre carrés*. A short note linking elliptic-function methods with arithmetic representation.
- *Notices sur les fonctions elliptiques*. A sequence of research notices rather than one continuously composed monograph.
- *Über die Anwendung der elliptischen Transcendenten auf ein bekanntes Problem der Elementar-Geometrie*. A mathematical memoir applying elliptic transcendents to an elementary-geometrical problem.
- *De functionibus ellipticis commentatio prima et altera*. Two connected Latin commentaries printed and subsequently collected as one bibliographical unit.
- *Note sur une nouvelle application de l’analyse des fonctions elliptiques à l’algèbre*. A short research note applying elliptic analysis to algebra.
- *Formulae novae in theoria transcendentium ellipticarum fundamentales*. A Latin memoir presenting fundamental new formulae.
- *Über die zur numerischen Berechnung der elliptischen Functionen zweckmässigsten Formeln*. A computational paper: it supplies procedures and formulae, and thus has a limited protocol-like character.
- *Über einige die elliptischen Functionen betreffenden Formeln*. A concise formula paper.
- *Anzeige von Legendre: Théorie des fonctions elliptiques, troisième supplément*. A critical notice of Legendre’s supplement, not an original treatise.
- *Über die unmittelbare Verification einer Fundamentalformel der Theorie der elliptischen Functionen*. A verification note.
- *Über die partielle Differentialgleichung, welcher die Zähler und Nenner der elliptischen Functionen Genüge leisten*. A research memoir identifying a partial differential equation for elliptic-function numerators and denominators.
- *Über die Entwickelung der elliptischen Functionen in Reihen*. A paper on series expansions.
- *Theorie der elliptischen Functionen aus den Eigenschaften der Thetareihen abgeleitet*. A substantial theoretical exposition deriving elliptic functions from theta-series properties.
- *Darstellung der elliptischen Functionen durch Potenzreihen*. A posthumously printed manuscript on power-series representations; it is a Nachlass text, and its published form is editorially mediated.
- *Über die Substitution und über die Reduction der Abelschen Integrale erster Ordnung in die Normalform*. A posthumously published manuscript on substitutions and reduction to normal form.

### Abelian functions and multiply periodic functions

- *De theoremate Abeliano observatio*. A short Latin observation on Abel’s theorem.
- *Considerationes generales de transcendentibus Abelianis*. A theoretical memoir on Abelian transcendents.
- *Über die Figur des Gleichgewichts*. A mathematical-physical paper connected with Abelian analysis and equilibrium figures.
- *De functionibus duarum variabilium quadrupliciter periodicis, quibus theoria transcendentium Abelianarum innititur*. A foundational memoir on quadruply periodic functions of two variables.
- *De usu theoriae integralium ellipticorum et integralium Abelianorum in analysi Diophantea*. A Latin memoir applying elliptic and Abelian integrals to Diophantine analysis.
- *Note von der geodätischen Linie auf einem Ellipsoid und den verschiedenen Anwendungen einer merkwürdigen analytischen Substitution*. A research note joining geodesics, substitutions, and Abelian analysis.
- *Demonstratio nova theorematis Abeliani*. A new proof of Abel’s theorem.
- *Über die Additionstheoreme der Abelschen Integrale zweiter und dritter Gattung*. A memoir on addition theorems.
- *Note sur les fonctions Abéliennes*. A short research communication.
- *Extrait d’une lettre de Jacobi adressée à Hermite*. An excerpted mathematical letter; it resists classification as a deliberately composed paper.
- *Über die Vertauschung von Parameter und Argument bei der dritten Gattung der Abelschen und höheren Transcendenten*. A research memoir on interchanging parameter and argument.
- *Über eine neue Methode zur Integration der hyperelliptischen Differentialgleichungen und über die rationale Form ihrer vollständigen algebraischen Integralgleichungen*. A methodological memoir proposing a new integration procedure.
- *Notiz über A. Göpel*. A scholarly notice about Göpel and his work, rather than a primary mathematical treatise.

### Algebra, determinants, transformations, and approximation

- *Über die Hauptaxen der Flächen der zweiten Ordnung*. A paper on principal axes of quadratic surfaces.
- *De singulari quadam duplicis integralis transformatione*. A Latin memoir on a special transformation of a double integral.
- *Exercitatio algebraica circa discerptionem singularem fractionum, quae plures variabiles involvunt*. An algebraic exercise or memoir on decomposing multivariable fractions.
- *De transformatione et determinatione integralium duplicium commentatio tertia*. The third commentary in a research sequence on double integrals.
- *De binis quibuslibet functionibus homogeneis secundi ordinis per substitutiones lineares in alias binas transformandis …*. A substantial Latin memoir on simultaneous linear transformation of quadratic forms and multiple integrals.
- *De formatione et proprietatibus determinantium*. A foundational systematic memoir on determinants.
- *De determinantibus functionalibus*. A memoir on functional determinants—the objects later called Jacobians.
- *De functionibus alternantibus earumque divisione per productum e differentiis elementorum conflatum*. A paper on alternating functions and Vandermonde-type products.
- *Zur combinatorischen Analysis*. A short contribution to combinatorial analysis.
- *Sulla condizione di uguaglianza di due radici dell’equazione cubica …*. An Italian research note on repeated roots in the cubic governing the principal axes of a quadric.
- **1845:** *Über eine neue Auflösungsart der bei der Methode der kleinsten Quadrate vorkommenden linearen Gleichungen*. An algorithmic memoir describing an iterative procedure for linear equations arising in least squares.
- *Über die Darstellung einer Reihe gegebener Werthe durch eine gebrochene rationale Function*. A paper on rational interpolation.
- *Extrait d’une lettre adressée à M. Liouville*. A published mathematical letter-extract, not an independently shaped memoir.
- *Über die Anzahl der Doppeltangenten ebener algebraischer Curven*. A short enumerative-geometrical paper.
- *Auszug dreier Schreiben von Herrn Prof. Hesse und eines Schreibens an Herrn Prof. Hesse*. A composite correspondence object involving Hesse; Jacobi authored only the letter addressed to Hesse, so the collected item is not wholly his work.
- *Bemerkungen zu einer Abhandlung Euler’s über die orthogonale Substitution*. A posthumously printed commentary on Euler.
- *Über ein leichtes Verfahren, die in der Theorie der Säcularstörungen vorkommenden Gleichungen numerisch aufzulösen*. A computational protocol for numerically solving the symmetric equations of secular-perturbation theory.

### Differential equations, variational calculus, and mechanics

- *Über die Integration der partiellen Differentialgleichungen erster Ordnung*. A memoir on first-order partial differential equations.
- *Über die Pfaff’sche Methode, eine gewöhnliche lineare Differentialgleichung zwischen 2n Variabeln durch ein System von n Gleichungen zu integriren*. A methodological analysis of Pfaff’s integration procedure.
- *Bemerkung zu der Abhandlung des Herrn Prof. Scherk: Über die Integration der Gleichung …*. A comment attached to another scholar’s paper rather than a freestanding treatise.
- *Sur le mouvement d’un point et sur un cas particulier du problème des trois corps*. A mechanics memoir on point motion and a special three-body problem.
- *Zur Theorie der Variationsrechnung und der Differentialgleichungen*. A connected study of the calculus of variations and differential equations.
- *Über die Reduction der Integration der partiellen Differentialgleichungen erster Ordnung … auf die Integration eines einzigen Systems gewöhnlicher Differentialgleichungen*. A long methodological memoir reducing a PDE problem to ordinary differential equations.
- *Note sur l’intégration des équations différentielles de la dynamique*. A short note on integrating dynamical equations.
- *Neues Theorem der analytischen Mechanik*. A concise theorem paper.
- *Sur un théorème de Poisson*. A note on a theorem of Poisson.
- *Dilucidationes de aequationum differentialium vulgarium systematis earumque connexione cum aequationibus differentialibus partialibus linearibus primi ordinis*. A major Latin memoir connecting systems of ordinary differential equations with linear first-order PDEs.
- *De integratione aequationis differentialis …*. A Latin paper on integrating a differential equation; its title is formula-dependent and is commonly abbreviated in catalogues.
- *De motu puncti singularis*. A Latin mechanics memoir on the motion of a singular point.
- *Sur un nouveau principe de la mécanique analytique*. A research announcement of a new principle in analytical mechanics.
- *Sur l’élimination des nœuds dans le problème des trois corps*. A celestial-mechanics note on eliminating the nodes.
- *Zusatz zu der vorhergehenden Abhandlung*. A supplement dependent on the preceding three-body paper; it resists treatment as a fully independent work.
- *Theoria novi multiplicatoris systemati aequationum differentialium vulgarium applicandi*. Jacobi’s principal Latin memoir on the last multiplier.
- *Sul principio dell’ultimo moltiplicatore e suo uso come nuovo principio generale di meccanica*. An Italian exposition of the last multiplier as a general mechanical principle.
- *Zwei Beispiele zur neuen Methode der Dynamik*. An examples paper demonstrating the new dynamical method.
- *Nova methodus aequationes differentiales partiales primi ordinis inter numerum variabilium quemcunque propositas integrandi*. A major posthumously published project on first-order PDEs; Weierstrass’s edition gave finished print form to manuscript material Jacobi had not published.
- *De investigando ordine systematis aequationum differentialium vulgarium cujuscunque*. A Nachlass treatise on determining the order of arbitrary systems of differential equations.
- *Über diejenigen Probleme der Mechanik, in welchen eine Kräftefunction existirt, und über die Theorie der Störungen*. A large posthumously edited mechanics manuscript.
- *Über die vollständigen Lösungen einer partiellen Differentialgleichung erster Ordnung*. A Nachlass memoir on complete solutions.
- *Über die Integration der partiellen Differentialgleichungen erster Ordnung zwischen vier Variabeln*. A posthumously printed research manuscript.
- *De aequationum differentialium isoperimetricarum transformationibus earumque reductione ad aequationem differentialem partialem primi ordinis non linearem*. A Nachlass memoir on isoperimetric differential equations.
- *De aequationum differentialium systemate non normali ad formam normalem revocando*. An unfinished or editorially recovered Nachlass study of reducing non-normal systems to normal form.

### Definite integrals, series, and number theory

- **1826:** *Über Gauss’ neue Methode, die Werthe der Integrale näherungsweise zu finden*. Jacobi’s first published paper, an exposition and development of Gaussian numerical quadrature.
- *Über den Ausdruck der verschiedenen Wurzeln einer Gleichung durch bestimmte Integrale*. A paper representing roots of an equation through definite integrals.
- *Mémoire sur l’évaluation approchée des intégrales définies*. A French memoir on numerical integration.
- *Über die Convergenz der Reihe …*. A convergence study; Jacobi produced several papers of this kind, and shortened citations can conceal that they are distinct texts.
- *De usu legitimo formulae summatoriae Maclaurinianae*. A Latin memoir on the legitimate use of the Euler–Maclaurin summation formula.
- *Über die Darstellung der Reihen durch bestimmte Integrale*. A paper on representing series by definite integrals.
- *Über die Entwickelung einer Function in eine trigonometrische Reihe*. A study of trigonometric expansion.
- *De transformatione et determinatione integralium duplicium*. A sequence of memoirs on transforming and evaluating double integrals; the collected edition separates its successive commentaries.
- *Demonstratio nova theorematis Wilsoniani*. A new proof of Wilson’s theorem.
- *De residuis cubicis commentatio numerosa*. A substantial Latin memoir on cubic residues.
- *Observatiunculae ad theoriam numerorum pertinentes*. Short observations in number theory.
- *De congruentia …*. A formula-titled Latin congruence paper whose mathematical expression forms an essential part of its title.
- *Über die Kreistheilung und ihre Anwendung auf die Zahlentheorie*. A paper joining cyclotomy to number theory.
- *Über die complexen Primzahlen, welche in der Theorie der Reste der fünften, achten und zwölften Potenzen zu betrachten sind*. A memoir on complex primes in higher-power reciprocity.
- *Über die Anzahl der Primzahlen unter einer gegebenen Grenze*. A note on prime-counting.
- *De compositione numerorum e quatuor quadratis*. A Latin memoir on representation by four squares.
- *Über die Darstellung der Zahlen durch sechs Quadrate*. A memoir on six-square representations.
- *Über die Darstellung der Zahlen durch acht Quadrate*. A memoir on eight-square representations.
- *Über eine neue Anwendung der Kettenbrüche*. A note applying continued fractions.
- *Allgemeine Theorie der kettenbruchähnlichen Algorithmen, in welchen jede Zahl aus drei vorhergehenden gebildet wird*. A generalized continued-fraction project, preserved through the Nachlass rather than completed for publication.
- *Über die Reduction der positiven quadratischen Formen mit drei unbestimmten ganzen Zahlen*. A posthumous number-theoretical manuscript.
- *Zur Theorie der quadratischen Formen*. A Nachlass contribution whose generic title reflects its manuscript status rather than a sharply delimited lifetime publication.

### Geometry and geodesy

- *Euleri formulae de transformatione coordinatarum*. A short Latin geometrical note on Euler’s coordinate-transformation formulae.
- *Théorème de géométrie*. A very short theorem communication.
- *Auszug eines Schreibens von C. G. J. Jacobi an J. Steiner*. A published letter-extract containing geometry; its epistolary form matters.
- *Zur Theorie der Curven*. A research paper on curves.
- *Über eine besondere Curve vierter Ordnung*. A memoir on a particular quartic curve.
- *Über die Linien kürzesten Weges auf krummen Flächen*. A study of geodesics on curved surfaces.
- *Demonstratio et amplificatio nova theorematis Gaussiani de curvatura integra trianguli in data superficie e lineis brevissimis formati*. A Latin proof and extension of Gauss’s theorem on geodesic triangles.
- *Über die geodätische Linie auf einem Ellipsoid*. A geodesic memoir.
- *Regel zur Bestimmung des Inhalts der Sternpolygone*. A compact computational rule—a geometrical protocol—for star-polygon areas.
- *Geometrische Theoreme*. A collection of theorems rather than a single continuous argument.
- *Geometrische Construction zweier geodätischen Formeln Bessel’s*. A construction note explicating two of Bessel’s geodetic formulae.
- *Über die Curve, welche alle von einem Punkte ausgehenden geodätischen Linien eines Rotationsellipsoides berührt*. A memoir on the envelope of geodesics from a point on an ellipsoid of revolution.
- *Über die Abbildung eines ungleichaxigen Ellipsoids auf einer Ebene, bei welcher die kleinsten Theile ähnlich bleiben*. A posthumously published manuscript on conformal mapping of a triaxial ellipsoid.
- *Solution nouvelle d’un problème fondamental de géodésie*. A posthumous geodetic solution.
- *Fragments sur la rotation d’un corps tirés des manuscrits de C. G. J. Jacobi et communiqués par E. Lottner*. Editorially assembled fragments, not a finished Jacobi text; even the title and boundaries are Lottner’s manifestation of the manuscripts.

### Astronomy and celestial mechanics

- *Über die Bestimmung der Rectascension und Declination eines Sterns aus den gemessenen Distanzen desselben von zwei bekannten Sternen*. An astronomical reduction method.
- *Über die Reduction der Beobachtungen eines Himmelskörpers ausserhalb des Meridians*. A practical astronomical protocol.
- *Über ein neues Coordinatensystem und eine neue Art, die Bewegung eines Himmelskörpers zu behandeln*. A celestial-mechanics memoir introducing what became the Jacobi coordinate system and integral.
- *Über die Convergenz der Reihe, durch welche das Kepler’sche Problem gelöst wird*. A convergence analysis of the series solution to Kepler’s problem.
- *Untersuchungen über die Convergenz der Reihe, durch welche das Kepler’sche Problem gelöst wird. Von Franz Carlini; bearbeitet von C. G. J. Jacobi*. An adaptation or reworking of Carlini, not an unambiguously sole-authored Jacobi creation.
- *Auszug zweier Schreiben des Professor C. G. J. Jacobi an Herrn Director P. A. Hansen*. Published astronomical correspondence.
- *Störungsrechnungen*. An unfinished computational project surviving in Jacobi’s astronomical Nachlass; Luther’s later report describes it, but that report is not Jacobi’s work.
- *Sur la rotation d’un corps*. A letter-extract sent to the Paris Academy on rigid-body rotation.

### Historical, institutional, and public texts

- *Über Descartes’ Leben und seine Methode, die Vernunft richtig zu leiten und die Wahrheit in den Wissenschaften zu suchen*. A historical and philosophical address rather than a mathematical memoir.
- *Vorwort zu A. L. Busch, Vorschule der darstellenden Geometrie*. A commissioned preface; it frames another author’s textbook and is not that book.
- *Mittheilung über einen Codex der Ptolemäischen Optik im Besitze der Königlichen Bibliothek zu Berlin*. A manuscript notice.
- *Opusculum de praxi numerorum quod Algorismum vocant*. Jacobi’s presentation or edition of a medieval algorism text; the underlying work is not his composition.
- *Über die Pariser polytechnische Schule*. A Nachlass essay on the École Polytechnique; it is an institutional reflection, unpublished in Jacobi’s lifetime.
- *Rede bei der Gedächtnissfeier für Friedrich Wilhelm Bessel*. A commemorative public address.
- *Berichte und akademische Anzeigen*. Short institutional texts produced in Jacobi’s role at the Berlin Academy; their dispersed, occasional character makes them a body of official writing rather than a single obra.

### Correspondence as mathematical work

- *Briefe Jacobi’s an Bessel*. A posthumously selected group of letters. The individual letters are made objects, but the grouping and title belong to the collected-works editors.
- *Neue Formeln Jacobi’s für einen Fall der Anwendung der Methode der kleinsten Quadrate, mitgetheilt von Bessel*. Jacobi’s formulae manifested through Bessel’s report; authorship of the mathematics and authorship of the printed text are not identical.
- *Briefe Jacobi’s an Gauss*. A posthumous editorial grouping of letters, not a collection Jacobi designed.
- *Briefwechsel zwischen C. G. J. Jacobi und M. H. Jacobi*. A correspondence later edited by Wilhelm Ahrens; it is jointly made through exchange and editorially constituted after both correspondents’ deaths.
- *Briefwechsel zwischen C. G. J. Jacobi und P. H. von Fuss über die Herausgabe der Werke Leonhard Eulers*. Correspondence documenting Jacobi’s project to publish Euler’s works; the letters are Jacobi’s, but the later book is an editorial creation.
- *Correspondance mathématique entre Legendre et Jacobi*. A jointly made mathematical exchange, published as a correspondence corpus rather than authored as a book.

### Teaching projects preserved without authorial books

- **1825–1851:** *Vorlesungen an den Universitäten Berlin und Königsberg*. A documented succession of courses in geometry, analysis, number theory, elliptic functions, differential equations, mechanics, astronomy, and related subjects. The collected edition prints Kronecker’s register of these courses, not the lectures themselves; most therefore survive as projects or performances of teaching, and it would be misleading to invent a separate Jacobi-authored “book” for every course.
- **1842–1843:** *Königsberger Vorlesungen über Dynamik*. The particular lecture performance underlying the posthumous *Vorlesungen über Dynamik*. Jacobi made the course, while Borchardt, Clebsch, and Lottner made the textual manifestation by which it is known.
- *Lectures represented only by students’ or editors’ notes*. These resist being called Jacobi texts at all: they preserve his teaching but may contain the note-taker’s selection, wording, mistakes, and later editorial reconstruction.
