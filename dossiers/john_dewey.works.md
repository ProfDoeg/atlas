## Works by John Dewey

### Philosophical, psychological, and ethical books

- **1887; revised 1889 and 1891:** *Psychology*. A systematic textbook and Dewey’s first book; the later editions are revised manifestations of the same obra, not separate titles. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1888:** *Leibniz’s New Essays Concerning the Human Understanding: A Critical Exposition*. A philosophical monograph developed from Dewey’s doctoral research. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1891:** *Outlines of a Critical Theory of Ethics*. A systematic ethical treatise. [Collected Works, EW 3](https://www.bibliovault.org/BV.titles.epl?exactAuth=Boydston%2C+Jo+Ann)
- **1894:** *The Study of Ethics: A Syllabus*. A pedagogical syllabus that is also a protocol for philosophical study rather than a conventional monograph. [Collected Works, EW 4](https://biblioteca.ucm.es/edu/recopilacion-de-su-obra)
- **1903:** *Studies in Logical Theory*. A collaboratively produced collection directed and introduced by Dewey; only part of the book is his writing, so the volume cannot be attributed to him alone. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1908; substantially revised 1932:** *Ethics*. A systematic textbook co-created with James H. Tufts. The 1932 version is a major reconstruction, not merely a reprint, and its division of authorship varies by chapter. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1910; rewritten 1933:** *How We Think*. A study of reflective thought and its education; the 1933 *Restatement of the Relation of Reflective Thinking to the Educative Process* is a substantially revised manifestation. [Collected Works, LW 8](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1916:** *Essays in Experimental Logic*. A deliberately assembled philosophical book made largely by revising and joining earlier essays. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1920; enlarged 1948:** *Reconstruction in Philosophy*. A book made from lectures delivered in Japan; Dewey supplied a new introduction for the enlarged manifestation. [Collected Works, MW 12](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1922:** *Human Nature and Conduct: An Introduction to Social Psychology*. A philosophical-social treatise developed from the Carus Lectures. [Collected Works, MW 14](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1925; revised 1929:** *Experience and Nature*. A metaphysical treatise based on the Paul Carus Lectures; the revision and Dewey’s two unpublished introductions complicate the notion of a single definitive manifestation. [Collected Works, LW 1](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1929:** *The Quest for Certainty: A Study of the Relation of Knowledge and Action*. A philosophical book based on the Gifford Lectures. [Collected Works, LW 4](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1934:** *Art as Experience*. A work of aesthetics developed from the William James Lectures. [Collected Works, LW 10](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1934:** *A Common Faith*. A philosophy of religious experience made from the Dwight H. Terry Lectures; it proposes a religious attitude without affirming a creed. [Collected Works, LW 9](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1938:** *Logic: The Theory of Inquiry*. Dewey’s mature systematic account of inquiry. [Collected Works, LW 12](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1939:** *Theory of Valuation*. A monograph in the *International Encyclopedia of Unified Science*, situated between a book and an extended commissioned essay. [Collected Works, LW 13](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1949:** *Knowing and the Known*. A jointly made philosophical book by Dewey and Arthur F. Bentley; its theses and terminology arose through their correspondence, making individual attribution especially resistant. [Collected Works, LW 16](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)

### Educational books, protocols, and pedagogical texts

- **1889:** *Applied Psychology: An Introduction to the Principles and Practice of Education*. A jointly authored educational textbook by Dewey and James Alexander McLellan. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1895:** *The Psychology of Number and Its Applications to Methods of Teaching Arithmetic*. A pedagogical and psychological manual co-authored with McLellan. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1896; revised 1899:** *Interest in Relation to Training of the Will*. An educational monograph later revised as *Interest as Related to Will* under Charles A. McMurry’s editorship. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1897:** *My Pedagogic Creed*. A manifesto: its form is a sequence of declarations intended to establish and promote an educational position, not merely describe one. [Collected Works, EW 5](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1899; revised and enlarged 1915:** *The School and Society: Being Three Lectures by John Dewey, Supplemented by a Statement of the University Elementary School*. A lecture-book inseparable from the collaborative Laboratory School project; the institutional statement and later chapters mean that its manifestations are not identical objects. [Collected Works, MW 1](https://www.bibliovault.org/BV.titles.epl?exactAuth=Boydston%2C+Jo+Ann)
- **1899:** *The Method of the Recitation*. A privately printed report made from Dewey’s lectures for use in teacher education; as an auditor-mediated report, it resists being treated as an entirely author-controlled text. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1901:** *Psychology and Social Practice*. A published address joining psychological argument to educational practice. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1902:** *The Child and the Curriculum*. A short educational monograph organized around the apparent opposition between children’s experience and organized subject matter. [Collected Works, MW 2](https://www.bibliovault.org/BV.titles.epl?exactAuth=Boydston%2C+Jo+Ann)
- **1902:** *The Educational Situation*. Three essays issued together as an intervention in contemporary schooling. [Collected Works, MW 1](https://www.bibliovault.org/BV.titles.epl?exactAuth=Boydston%2C+Jo+Ann)
- **1903:** *Ethical Principles Underlying Education*. An educational-ethical tract made from an earlier address. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1904:** *Education, Direct and Indirect*. A separately printed educational address or pamphlet rather than a full-length book. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1909:** *Moral Principles in Education*. A concise educational treatise. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1913:** *Interest and Effort in Education*. A pedagogical monograph developed from lectures. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1915:** *Schools of To-Morrow*. A jointly authored survey and advocacy book by John Dewey and Evelyn Dewey; its documentary reporting and examples are substantially Evelyn Dewey’s contribution, so sole attribution to John Dewey is misleading. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1916:** *Democracy and Education: An Introduction to the Philosophy of Education*. A systematic philosophy of education, not a protocol for a particular school despite its practical orientation. [Collected Works, MW 9](https://biblioteca.ucm.es/edu/recopilacion-de-su-obra)
- **1921:** *The Alexander-Dewey Arithmetic*. A three-volume school arithmetic created by Georgina Alexander and edited by Dewey; Dewey made an editorial and pedagogical contribution, not the whole textbook. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1922:** *Ideals, Aims, and Methods in Education*. A multi-author educational volume containing Dewey’s “Aims and Ideals of Education”; the book as a whole is not his obra. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1929:** *The Sources of a Science of Education*. A lecture-derived inquiry into what educational science can be, rather than a laboratory manual or settled protocol. [Collected Works, LW 5](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1929:** *Contrasts in Education*. A separately issued educational lecture or address. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1931:** *American Education Past and Future*. A lecture-pamphlet diagnosing American education and proposing its direction. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1931:** *The Way Out of Educational Confusion*. A short intervention made from the Inglis Lecture. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1934:** *Education and the Social Order*. A political-educational pamphlet, not the later collection published under a similar title. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1937:** *The Teacher and Society*. A multi-author book containing Dewey’s contribution alongside work by William H. Kilpatrick, George H. Hartmann, Ernest O. Melby, and others; the collected object is collaborative. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1938:** *Experience and Education*. A short book reconsidering both traditional and progressive education. [Collected Works, LW 13](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)

### Social and political books, manifestos, and reports

- **1888:** *The Ethics of Democracy*. A separately published philosophical-political paper arguing that democracy is an ethical form of associated life. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1915; revised 1942:** *German Philosophy and Politics*. A wartime political-philosophical book; the Second World War revision is a historically altered manifestation rather than a simple reissue. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1917:** *Enlistment for the Farm*. A Columbia War Papers pamphlet intended to mobilize agricultural labor; it acts on its readers and therefore approaches a manifesto or recruitment protocol. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1920:** *Letters from China and Japan*. A book of letters by John Dewey and Alice Chipman Dewey, edited by Evelyn Dewey; the published sequence is therefore a family-made editorial manifestation rather than John Dewey’s sole construction. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1921:** *China, Japan, and the U.S.A.: Present-Day Conditions in the Far East and Their Bearing on the Washington Conference*. A political pamphlet assembled for an immediate diplomatic context. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1923:** *Outlawry of War: What It Is and Is Not*. An activist pamphlet defining and advocating a political program; it functions partly as a manifesto. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1926:** *What Mr. John Dewey Thinks of the Educational Policies of México*. A Mexican government printing of Dewey’s evaluation; its title and physical manifestation were supplied institutionally, so it resists identification as a book deliberately authored under that title. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1927:** *The Public and Its Problems*. A work of political philosophy made in response to debates about modern publics and communication. [Collected Works, LW 2](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1929:** *Impressions of Soviet Russia and the Revolutionary World: Mexico—China—Turkey*. A travel-political collection assembled from previously published reports; it is a composite manifestation, not a newly written continuous book. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1930:** *Individualism, Old and New*. A social-philosophical book assembled from revised magazine essays. [Collected Works, LW 5](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1932:** *Are Sanctions Necessary to International Organizations?* A jointly made Foreign Policy Association pamphlet by Dewey and Raymond Leslie Buell, presenting opposed or differentiated positions rather than a single-author doctrine. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1935:** *Liberalism and Social Action*. A lecture-derived political argument and program for reconstructed liberalism. [Collected Works, LW 11](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1937:** *The Case of Leon Trotsky: Report of Hearings on the Charges Made Against Him in the Moscow Trials*. An institutional record made collectively by the Preliminary Commission of Inquiry, which Dewey chaired; it is a proceeding and evidentiary report, not Dewey’s individual obra. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1938:** *Not Guilty: Report of the Commission of Inquiry into the Charges Made Against Leon Trotsky in the Moscow Trials*. A collective commission report associated with Dewey, Suzanne La Follette, Benjamin Stolberg, and other participants; its authorship is corporate and juridical. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1939:** *What Is Democracy? Its Conflicts, Ends and Means*. A jointly produced political book by Dewey, Boyd H. Bode, and T. V. Smith. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1939:** *Freedom and Culture*. A political-philosophical book on democratic culture and human freedom. [Collected Works, LW 13](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)

### Addresses, syllabi, and other separately issued texts

- **1897:** *Hegel’s Philosophy of Spirit: Lectures*. A printed lecture text whose status lies between a teaching record and an authored philosophical work. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1897:** *The Significance of the Problem of Knowledge*. A separately issued philosophical address. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1899:** *Psychology and Philosophic Method*. The printed form of Dewey’s annual public address before the University of California Philosophical Union. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1903:** *Logical Conditions of a Scientific Treatment of Morality*. A separately printed philosophical paper. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1909:** *The Pragmatic Movement of Contemporary Thought: A Syllabus*. A course syllabus and therefore a protocol for study, not a finished systematic book. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1917:** *Creative Intelligence: Essays in the Pragmatic Attitude*. A collaborative collection containing Dewey’s “The Need for a Recovery of Philosophy”; the whole volume is not his work alone. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1930:** *Construction and Criticism*. A separately published address defending criticism as an active form of reconstruction. [Collected Works, LW 5](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1931:** *Context and Thought*. A philosophical essay issued as a numbered university publication, materially between an article and a small monograph. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)

### Collections assembled or authorized during Dewey’s lifetime

- **1910:** *The Influence of Darwin on Philosophy, and Other Essays in Contemporary Thought*. An authorial essay collection combining earlier and contemporary pieces into a new intellectual arrangement. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1928:** *The Philosophy of John Dewey*. Joseph Ratner’s selection from Dewey’s writings; it is an editorial manifestation of Dewey’s corpus, not a new continuous obra made by Dewey. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1929:** *Characters and Events: Popular Essays in Social and Political Philosophy*. A two-volume collection edited by Joseph Ratner from Dewey’s occasional writings; the essays are Dewey’s, but the collected object is Ratner’s construction. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1929; revised 1947 and 1954:** *Art and Education*. A Barnes Foundation collaborative volume by Dewey, Albert C. Barnes, Laurence Buermeyer, and others; later revisions, including the posthumous 1954 edition, are distinct editorial manifestations. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1931:** *Philosophy and Civilization*. A collection of Dewey’s philosophical essays; its unity is editorial and retrospective rather than that of a single sustained treatise. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1939:** *Intelligence in the Modern World: John Dewey’s Philosophy*. Joseph Ratner’s edited selection with an editorial introduction; it manifests Dewey’s thought but is not wholly a thing Dewey made. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1940:** *Education Today*. A collection selected and edited by Joseph Ratner with Dewey’s participation or authorization; the component texts are Dewey’s, while their arrangement is Ratner’s. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1946:** *Problems of Men*. A late authorial collection of essays and addresses, not a single newly composed argument. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1949:** *The Wit and Wisdom of John Dewey*. A. H. Johnson’s selection and arrangement of excerpts; it is an editorial manifestation that resists classification as Dewey’s own obra. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)

### Periodical, review, and occasional corpus

- **1882–1953:** Dewey’s periodical and occasional writings. This is not a single titled work: it is a corpus of hundreds of articles, reviews, forewords, letters to editors, encyclopedia entries, statements, interviews, and jointly signed declarations preserved item by item in the thirty-seven-volume critical edition. Treating it as one obra conceals its heterogeneous and often collaborative forms, while treating every later reprint as a new work confuses texts with editions. [Critical-edition index](https://books.google.com/books/about/The_Collected_Works_of_John_Dewey_Index.html?id=wtaNxAEACAAJ)
- **1882–1898:** *The Early Works of John Dewey*. Five editorial volumes containing the surviving early books, articles, reviews, addresses, and miscellany; this title names a posthumously constructed scholarly corpus, not a work conceived by Dewey. [Edition overview](https://biblioteca.ucm.es/edu/recopilacion-de-su-obra)
- **1899–1924:** *The Middle Works of John Dewey*. Fifteen editorial volumes preserving Dewey’s writings of the period; again, the series is a critical manifestation of many separate objects. [Edition overview](https://www.bibliovault.org/BV.titles.epl?exactAuth=Boydston%2C+Jo+Ann)
- **1925–1953:** *The Later Works of John Dewey*. Seventeen editorial volumes containing books and shorter writings, including items published after Dewey’s death; “1953” belongs to the edition’s documentary scope and does not imply that Dewey lived or wrote throughout that year. [Edition overview](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1884–1951; collected 2008:** *Supplementary Volume 1*. An electronic critical supplement containing writings omitted or discovered after the thirty-seven-volume edition; it demonstrates that even the “complete works” remained bibliographically open. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)

### Lectures preserved by other hands

- **1895–1896; published 1998:** *Principles of Instrumental Logic: John Dewey’s Lectures in Ethics and Political Ethics*. Student or auditor records edited by Donald F. Koch; these manifest Dewey’s teaching but are not verbatim texts whose every word can securely be called his. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1898; published 1976:** *Lectures on Psychological and Political Ethics*. Posthumously edited lecture records; their mediated transcription makes them evidence of a pedagogical event rather than straightforward authored copy. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1899; published 1966:** *Lectures in the Philosophy of Education*. Reginald D. Archambault’s edition of lecture records; the title and book-form unity are posthumous. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1900–1901; published 1991:** *Lectures on Ethics*. Donald F. Koch’s edition of notes from Dewey’s teaching; it is a documentary manifestation, not an author-published book. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1910–1911; published 1993:** *Philosophy and Education in Their Historic Relations*. Elsie Ripley Clapp transcribed the lectures and J. J. Chambliss later edited them; authorship is therefore layered among lecturer, recorder, and editor. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1915–1916; published 1990:** *Lectures by John Dewey: Moral and Political Philosophy*. Warren J. Samuels and Donald F. Koch’s posthumous edition of lecture materials; the published volume is not an object Dewey himself completed. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)
- **1919–1920; published 1973:** *Lectures in China*. Robert W. Clopton and Tsuin-Chen Ou reconstructed, translated, and edited lectures surviving largely through Chinese records; the English wording and book-form title therefore cannot be attributed unqualifiedly to Dewey. [Bibliography](https://www.johndeweysociety.org/dewey-studies/files/2017/01/WritingsbyJohnDewey.pdf)

### Poems and correspondence

- **principally 1910–1918; published 1977:** *The Poems of John Dewey*. A posthumous editorial manifestation of ninety-eight previously unpublished poems. Dewey did not make or authorize the collection as a book, and the manuscripts’ alterations preserve their unfinished or private status. [Southern Illinois University Press description](https://www.bibliovault.org/BV.book.epl?ISBN=9780809308002)
- **1871–1953:** Dewey’s correspondence. Thousands of private and professional letters form a documentary practice rather than a single titled obra; the comprehensive electronic edition also includes letters to and about him, so that edition is broader than what Dewey himself made. [Center for Dewey Studies](https://deweycenter.siu.edu/center-information/letters/index.php)
- **1871–2007; electronic edition 1999–2008:** *The Correspondence of John Dewey, Volumes I–IV*. Volumes I–III chiefly document Dewey’s lifetime, while Volume IV concerns his estate and institutional legacy after 1953; the fourth volume therefore cannot be classified as writing by Dewey. [InteLex edition description](https://www.nlx.com/collections/132)

### Educational and institutional project

- **1896–1904:** University of Chicago Laboratory School. An experimental school founded and directed by Dewey with Alice Chipman Dewey, teachers, parents, administrators, and pupils: a collaborative pedagogical project whose curriculum, social organization, teaching practices, reports, and daily life are the made thing. It resists reduction either to Dewey’s individual obra or to the books subsequently written about it. [University of Chicago Laboratory Schools history](https://www.ucls.uchicago.edu/about-lab/history)
