## Works by Suzanne Perrottet

### Movement scores, dance notations, and choreographic projects

- **1906–1907:** *Notations and records of Jaques-Dalcroze’s rhythmic gymnastics*. Ink-on-paper exercise records from Perrottet’s earliest pedagogical work; these are protocols for embodied practice rather than autonomous dances. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
- **c. 1915:** *Notes on vocal and movement exercises*. A pedagogical protocol joining voice and bodily movement; the surviving manuscript is held in the Kunsthaus Zürich estate as II.91:87. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
- **c. 1918–1926:** *Wege mit Rhythmen*. A pencil notation for moving through space with rhythms, preserved as II.91:19; it resists classification as either choreography or lesson plan because it could activate both performance and teaching. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
- **c. 1920:** *Sketches and drawings on Rudolf von Laban’s theoretical considerations*. A group of graphic thinking-sheets, not a finished treatise; their intellectual relation to Laban’s ideas makes sole conceptual authorship difficult to separate from Perrottet’s acts of recording and interpretation. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
- **1926:** *Pantomime*. A pencil movement notation belonging to a series of sheets marked “mit Laban”; that inscription makes it a collaborative or relational project rather than a securely autonomous Perrottet choreography. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
- **c. 1939:** *Verliebt*. A nine-sheet pencil dance notation. It is evidence of a choreographic work or project, but the surviving catalogue does not establish whether it was completed or performed. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
- **1945:** *Lac des Cygnes (Swan Lake), second act*. A combined musical score and dance notation in pencil and ink, estate number II.94:6; it is Perrottet’s movement realization or teaching transcription of pre-existing ballet material, not authorship of *Swan Lake* itself. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
- **Undated:** *Carmen*. A dance notation preserved by the SAPA Foundation. The source does not establish whether this was a complete choreography, an exercise, or Perrottet’s notation of somebody else’s movement, so it remains a documentary choreographic project. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
- **Undated:** *Danse Café*. A surviving dance notation; no performance history or secure date is supplied by the catalogue. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
- **Undated:** *Rausch-Tanz*. A surviving dance notation in the SAPA estate. Its status as a performed dance, projected choreography, or exercise is unresolved. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
- **Undated:** *Maxixe*. A dance notation whose title invokes the social dance; the document records Perrottet’s handling of that form but does not prove that she originated the dance material. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
- **Undated:** *Dreh Two-Stepp*. A movement notation based on the two-step. Like *Maxixe*, it is best treated as a protocol or arrangement rather than automatically as an original choreography. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
- **Undated:** *George*. At least two pages of a dance notation survive in the Perrottet family estate; its completeness, date, and performance status are not established. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
- **Undated:** *Familien-Besuch*. A mixed-media choreographic object comprising a musical score, at least six pages of dance notation, and a supplement. It is a small Gesamtkunstwerk in documentary form—music, movement, and implied dramatic situation—but the exhibited leaves do not establish whether the whole work survives. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
- **Undated:** *Notation on movements*. An untitled movement record in the SAPA estate. It resists being called an individual work because the catalogue does not distinguish exercise, observation, choreographic draft, or teaching aid. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
- **Undated:** *Collection of movements*. An assemblage of movement material preserved by SAPA; it is better understood as an open repertory or working archive than as a closed work. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))

### Performances and embodied manifestations

- **1917:** *Six piano pieces by Arnold Schönberg at the opening of Galerie Dada*. A musical performance by Perrottet, not a composition by her: the obra is interpretive and ephemeral, known from the event program. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
- **1919:** *Poppies at the Eighth Dada Soirée*. Perrottet’s piano performance of Cyril Scott’s composition at the Saal zur Kaufleuten; it belongs to her performance practice, while compositional authorship remains Scott’s. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
- **1921:** *Schüler-Abend im Kunsthaus*. A student evening directed by Perrottet through the Schule für Eurythmie. This was a collective pedagogical manifestation rather than a securely itemized set of individually authored dances. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
- **1928:** *Dance evening at the Schauspielhaus Zürich*. A live dance manifestation documented photographically; the surviving catalogue does not supply the individual numbers or establish which choreography was Perrottet’s. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
- **1928:** *Dances at Cabaret Der Krater*. A group of performed cabaret dances whose original musical scores survive as estate item II.92:23. The group cannot presently be resolved into a complete, securely titled work-list. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
- **3 November 1928:** *Die Bar*. A pantomime-and-dance cabaret number in which Perrottet embodied a prostitute without spoken narration; its status lies between choreographed dance, dramatic role, and ephemeral cabaret action. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf), [Zürcher Museen](https://zuercher-museen.ch/museen/cabaret-voltaire/archiv/thibault-lac-blue-moon))
- **1930:** *Dance evening in Zürich*. A performance documented in several photographs by Walter Schwabe and another unidentified photographer. No surviving programme in the consulted catalogue identifies a complete set of constituent works, so the evening itself—not conjectural individual dances—is the safely nameable manifestation. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))

### Music

- **1920:** *Komposition für Violine solo*. A composition for unaccompanied violin preserved in the Kunsthaus Zürich estate. The standard scholarly works list supplies no further title or publication/performance history, so “composition for solo violin” may be a descriptive designation rather than a fixed authorial title. ([Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Suzanne_Perrottet_(1889-1983)))
- **Undated:** *Familien-Besuch*. A musical score made as part of the dance project of the same name; because it survives alongside movement notation and a supplement, it should not be reduced to a freestanding composition. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))

### Drawings, diagrams, and graphic thought

- **1914:** *Labanschule Architektur*. A drawing or diagram of “Laban-school architecture,” 21.2 × 15 cm. It may record an actual spatial scheme, imagine a school, or diagram a pedagogical system; the catalogue does not silently resolve those possibilities. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
- **1914:** *Frau*. A graphite drawing on paper. Unlike the utilitarian movement sheets, this is catalogued as an individual graphic work, although no further subject identification is given. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
- **1940s:** *Sketches and drawings*. A group of pencil-and-ink sheets preserved as estate item II.94:26. It resists conversion into one “work”: the catalogue treats it as a heterogeneous archival unit and supplies neither individual titles nor exact dates. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
- **1950:** *Sketches and notes on the dances of Mary Wigman*. Graphic and textual observations of another dancer’s work, preserved as II.95:3. They are documentary analysis and embodied memory, not securely Perrottet-authored choreographies. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))

### Published texts and memoir

- **1917:** *Ueber moderne Kindererziehung*. A two-part published essay in supplements to issues 43 and 44 of *Schweizer Frauenheim: Familienblatt zur Unterhaltung und Belehrung*. It is a pedagogical argument rather than a literary book. ([Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Suzanne_Perrottet_(1889-1983)))
- **1995:** *Suzanne Perrottet: Ein bewegtes Leben*. Perrottet’s orally transmitted memoir, transcribed from recordings and edited posthumously by Giorgio J. Wolfensberger; authorship is therefore distributed between Perrottet’s spoken recollection and the editor’s documentary construction. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf), [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Suzanne_Perrottet_(1889-1983)))
- **2014:** *Die Befreiung des Körpers: Erinnerungen*. A posthumous edition of Perrottet’s orally transmitted memoir, edited by Giorgio J. Wolfensberger and Margarete Berg. It is not a manuscript book completed by Perrottet during her lifetime, but an editorial manifestation of recorded testimony. ([Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Suzanne_Perrottet_(1889-1983)), [Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))

### Unpublished reflections and working texts

- **Undated:** *Notes on memories of Emmy Hennings’s dance*. An unpublished act of recollection preserved in the SAPA estate; it is simultaneously witness testimony, dance description, and working note, and the source gives no secure date. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
- **Undated:** *My Memories of Dada*. Unpublished manuscript notes in the SAPA estate. The title belongs to the notes, but their completeness and relation to Perrottet’s later oral memoir are not established. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
- **Undated:** *Notes on Hugo Ball’s Flight Out of Time*. Reading or response notes rather than an autonomous publication; they document Perrottet making an interpretive text upon another author’s book. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
- **Undated:** *Notes on her understanding and concept of dance*. An unpublished theoretical text in the SAPA estate. It may be a draft, teaching memorandum, or personal statement, and consequently resists treatment as a finished manifesto. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
- **29 July 1975:** *Notes on a presentation by Rosalia Chladek in Locarno*. A dated critical or observational record of another practitioner’s presentation, not a Perrottet choreography. ([Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))

### Pedagogical and institutional projects

- **1918–1979:** *Movement school later named Bewegungsschule Suzanne Perrottet*. Perrottet took over the Zürich school associated with Laban in 1918, announced its change from *Schule für Eurythmie* to *Bewegungsschule Suzanne Perrottet* in 1924, and sustained it for decades. The school is an obra made through teaching, exercises, pupils, performances, and institutional continuity rather than a single text or building. ([Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Suzanne_Perrottet_(1889-1983)), [Kunsthaus Zürich archive](https://www.kunsthaus.net/museum/archive/), [Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
- **Lifelong, dates not fixed:** *Pedagogy of rhythmic movement and bodily liberation*. A body of practice realized through lessons, movement protocols, improvisation, and pupils rather than a single bounded work. It resists a catalogue of titles because its primary medium was the changing human body, while the archive preserves only partial textual and graphic traces. ([Kunsthaus Zürich archive](https://www.kunsthaus.net/museum/archive/), [Cabaret Voltaire exhibition catalogue](https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch/assets/contents/CV-Suzanne-Perrottet_EN_Reader_final.pdf))
