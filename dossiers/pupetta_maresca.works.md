## Works by Assunta "Pupetta" Maresca

### Song

- **1967:** *’O bbene mio*. A Neapolitan song written and performed by Maresca for *Delitto a Posillipo*; the surviving credits identify her as both writer and singer, although no independent discographic release has been located. ([IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0546498/), [Il Resto del Carlino](https://www.ilrestodelcarlino.it/cronaca/omaggio-alla-boss-arcuri-nella-bufera-addio-pupetta-forte-e-coraggiosa-0cab0dbe))

### Screen performance

- **1967:** *Delitto a Posillipo—Londra chiama Napoli*. Maresca’s sole documented film performance: she played the protagonist, variously credited as Pupetta Amoruso, Amoroso, or Amuruso, and sang *’O bbene mio*. This is a collaborative manifestation rather than a film authored by her; moreover, Rita Savagnone dubbed her spoken dialogue. ([ANICA](https://www.anica.it/online/copiaprivata/titoli/Elenco_Opere_Beneficiarie_1992-1998.pdf), [ComingSoon](https://www.comingsoon.it/film/delitto-a-posillipo-londra-chiama-napoli/7011/scheda/), [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0546498/))

### Manifesto-performance

- **13 February 1982:** Public declaration against Raffaele Cutolo and the Nuova Camorra Organizzata. Delivered at a press conference Maresca convened at Naples’s Circolo della Stampa, this untitled spoken intervention acted as a threat and declaration of alignment rather than merely describing her position; it therefore functions as a manifesto-performance, though it resists classification as an authored “work.” Contemporary and later accounts disagree about whether it demonstrated membership in the Nuova Famiglia or a more ambiguous opposition to Cutolo. ([Corriere della Sera](https://www.corriere.it/cronache/21_dicembre_30/pupetta-maresca-miss-che-si-fece-camorrista-c6d126c8-69b2-11ec-996c-3905d962e8a9.shtml), [Le Monde](https://www.lemonde.fr/archives/article/1982/08/23/la-prima-donna-de-la-camorra_2909767_1819218.html), [L’Unità archive](https://archivio.unita.news/assets/main/1982/02/23/page_016.pdf))

### Commercial projects

- **Late 1960s:** Two unnamed clothing shops in Naples. These were commercial projects operated by Maresca after her film appearance, not securely documented designed objects or artistic works; the available sources provide neither individual names nor opening dates, so they cannot responsibly be separated or titled. ([Assunta Maresca biographical record](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assunta_Maresca))
