## Works by Otto Hanish

### Mazdaznan as a Gesamtkunstwerk

- **Late 1890s–1936:** *Mazdaznan*. A syncretic religious, hygienic, dietary, breath-training, musical, and social system founded and continually elaborated by Hanish; it is better understood as a Gesamtkunstwerk or life-reform project than as any single text. Its historical sources were often Hanish’s own putative revelations and invented traditions, so “authorship” here includes institutional and ritual invention as well as writing. [Bigalke, “Religion and Medicine in Mazdaznan”](https://www.multiple-secularities.de/media/css_bigalke_mazdaznan_2.pdf)

- **By 1903–1936:** *Mazdaznan* / *Mazdaznan and the Messenger*. A periodical manifestation of the movement, edited and largely written by Hanish; surviving runs are incomplete, and the title and subtitle changed. Because unsigned organizational matter cannot always be separated from Hanish’s writing, the periodical resists division into a secure list of individually authored texts. [International Association for the Preservation of Spiritualist and Occult Periodicals](https://iapsop.com/archive/materials/mazdaznan/index.html)

- **1908–1916:** *Mazdaznan-Monatsschrift*, later *Masdasnan-Zeitschrift*. The German-language periodical established with David Ammann as the European vehicle for Hanish’s system. Hanish supplied teachings and articles, but translation, editing, and local production were collaborative; individual authorship therefore cannot be assigned uniformly. [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/)

### Courses, protocols, and systems of practice

- **1902:** *Health and Breath Culture According to Mazdaznan Philosophy (Sun-Worship)*. A practical and doctrinal breath-culture manual: part protocol, part health system, and part religious instruction. Later editions appeared as *Health and Breath Culture*, *Atmungs- und Heilkunde*, *Atmungs- und Gesundheitslehre*, and *Mazdaznan Atem- und Gesundheitspflege*, often revised or enlarged by translators and editors. [Bigalke, bibliography](https://www.multiple-secularities.de/media/css_bigalke_mazdaznan_2.pdf); [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/)

- **1902:** *Inner Studies: A Course of Twelve Lessons*. A graded esoteric and sexual-regeneration course rather than a conventional treatise. Its German manifestation, *Wiedergeburt* or *Mazdaznan-Wiedergeburts-Lehre*, was repeatedly revised, expanded, translated by David Ammann, and later edited by Otto Rauth, so no single later edition can be treated as an unchanged authorial text. [Bigalke, bibliography](https://www.multiple-secularities.de/media/css_bigalke_mazdaznan_2.pdf); [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/)

- **1904:** *Mazdaznan Encyclopedia of Dietetics and Home Cook Book of Cooked and Uncooked Foods: What to Eat and How to Eat It*. A combined dietary system, domestic protocol, and recipe collection. The scholarly bibliography identifies a 1904 second edition, leaving the date and form of the presumed first edition unresolved; German versions were translated, separated, recombined, and substantially edited by David and Frieda Ammann and Otto Rauth. [Bigalke, bibliography](https://www.multiple-secularities.de/media/css_bigalke_mazdaznan_2.pdf); [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/)

- **1904; published in later editions by 1921:** *Lebensrätsel*. An extract from Hanish’s October 1904 Los Angeles opening lecture, issued as a short pamphlet. It is a printed residue of an oral event rather than an independently composed book. [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/)

- **1912:** *How to Fast Scientifically: When, Where, Why?* A fasting protocol combining bodily discipline with Mazdaznan doctrine. A related German pamphlet, *Wie soll man fasten?*, was written or adapted by David Ammann “according to” Hanish, so it should not be silently treated as Hanish’s verbatim work. [Bigalke, “Religion and Medicine in Mazdaznan” bibliography](https://brill.com/view/journals/ges/69/2/article-p272_3.pdf); [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/)

- **By 1914:** *The Twelve Egyptian Exercises* / *Egyptian Postures*. A sequence of advanced bodily, breathing, vocal, and concentration instructions incorporated into editions of Hanish’s breath-culture manual. It is a protocol performed by bodies, not simply a separable literary work; its independent title and original date are not securely fixed. [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/); [Ian Whittlesea, *The Egyptian Postures*](https://www.ianwhittlesea.net/works_pages/wks_egyptian/wks_egyptian.html)

- **1916:** *Darmpflege und Heilung der Darmtätigkeit*. A short bowel-care protocol translated by David Ammann. The surviving bibliography documents the German publication but does not establish the date or title of an English antecedent. [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/)

- **1925:** *Mazdaznan-Gedächtnisübungen*. A sixteen-page set of memory exercises—a practical mental-training protocol rather than a discursive book. No earlier form is identified in the consulted bibliography. [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/)

- **1928:** *Monats-Ratschläge für Küche und Körperpflege*. A calendar-like cycle of dietary and bodily-care instructions, translated by Frieda Ammann and edited by Otto Rauth from material previously embedded in the cookery project. Its status is therefore both independent work and extracted/reorganized component. [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/)

- **1928:** *Drüsenlehre*. A system of glandular exercises and teachings published “after” Hanish, edited by Otto Rauth with Frieda Ammann’s authorization. The wording signals mediated or post-lecture construction, so Hanish’s authorship of the exact book-form text is not secure. [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/)

- **By 1929:** *Verstopfung*. A short health protocol on constipation documented in the scholarly bibliography as a Leipzig publication circa 1929; its precise first date and relationship to *Darmpflege* remain unclear. [Bigalke, “Religion and Medicine in Mazdaznan” bibliography](https://brill.com/view/journals/ges/69/2/article-p272_3.pdf)

### Revelatory narratives and sacred histories

- **1907–1909; book form circa 1913:** *Ainyahita in Pearls*. A revelatory sacred narrative first manifested serially and subsequently as a book, presenting the purported Tibetan source of Hanish’s teaching. The German *Ainyahita: Die köstlichste Perle von Tibet* appeared in 1911, before the documented Chicago book edition, complicating any simple “first-book” chronology. [Bigalke, bibliography](https://www.multiple-secularities.de/media/css_bigalke_mazdaznan_2.pdf); [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/)

- **1908; expanded English book 1917:** *Yehoshua Nazir: Jesus the Nazarite; Life of Christ*. An alternative sacred biography claiming otherwise unknown Oriental sources. A short German *Das Leben Jesu* appeared in 1908, while the fuller English manifestation dates from 1917 and later German editions were expanded; the textual genealogy is consequently not reducible to one stable edition. [Bigalke, bibliography](https://www.multiple-secularities.de/media/css_bigalke_mazdaznan_2.pdf); [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/)

- **1918:** *Die Ur-Religion*. A reconstructed primordial-religion narrative translated by David Ammann and adapted by Otto Rauth. Since the edition explicitly distributes responsibility among author, translator, and adapter, its precise wording is a collaborative manifestation of Hanish’s teaching. [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/)

- **1924:** *Irdisches und himmlisches Evangelium des Meisters Zaraduscht*. A privately printed “gospel” attributed to Hanish, edited by “St. Omar,” with Wilhelm Warschatka also named in the bibliographic record. The divided credits make sole authorship and exact textual responsibility uncertain. [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/)

- **1924–1925:** *Bambino Bezzari: Unser göttlicher Bambino* / *Unser Gotteskind*. A religious narrative issued under the persona “Sarmatian von Caspianya,” identified bibliographically with Hanish, and translated by Anna Magneta Sorge. Both the variant title and pseudonymous attribution should be retained rather than normalized away. [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/)

### Philosophy, lectures, and doctrinal compilations

- **1911:** *Die Philosophie der Wirklichkeit: Mazdaznan, der Zeitgeist*. A philosophical exposition published by the Mazdaznan circle in Berlin. The consulted specialist bibliography attributes it directly to Hanish but records no securely identified English original. [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/)

- **Circa 1918:** *Gedenke zu leben*. A didactic dialogue about Mazdaznan, printed “as manuscript” by Friedrich Kammerer. The bibliography records the object but does not clearly state whether Kammerer transcribed, edited, or authored its dialogic form, so direct authorship by Hanish remains uncertain. [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/)

- **1923–1925:** *Imanuel: Masdasnan-Denk-Lehre* / *Meistervorträge*. A two-volume body of Hanish’s master-lectures, edited by Frieda Ammann and issued in several differently paginated arrangements. It is an editorially constructed lecture corpus rather than a single manuscript authored for publication. [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/); [Theosophical Society Library record](https://www.theosofie.nl/2019/aanwinsten-bibliotheek-amsterdam-juli-2019/)

- **1923:** *Selbstdiagnostik: Selbsterkennungslehre*. A diagnostic and self-knowledge system edited by Otto Rauth, later substantially enlarged. Its instructional form places it between a protocol and a doctrinal treatise, while the editor’s role prevents assumption that every sentence is Hanish’s. [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/)

- **1925:** *Mazdaznan Lebenswissenschaft: Vorträge gehalten in Prag 1925, 5.–9. August*. A printed cycle of lectures delivered in Prague. It is the documentary manifestation of a time-bound oral event, not necessarily a text prepared by Hanish as a book. [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/)

- **1930:** *Ergänzungslehre oder Der Ruf an die Welt*. A large supplementary teaching or “call to the world,” combining doctrinal continuation with manifesto-like address. The bibliography establishes the 1930 German edition but not the date or form of any antecedent lectures or English text. [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/)

- **1931:** *Ever Creative Thought*. A continuing sequence of meditative or doctrinal pieces rather than an ordinary monograph; it also appeared through the movement’s periodical culture. The title is documented, but the consulted sources do not establish a definitive first collected edition. [IAPSOP, *Mazdaznan*, February 1935](https://iapsop.com/archive/materials/mazdaznan/mazdaznan_v34_n2_feb_1935.pdf)

- **1931–1932; published 1932:** *Offenbarungen: Meisterreden 1931/32*. A collection of “master addresses” edited by Otto Rauth. Like *Imanuel*, it is an editorial fixing of oral manifestations, so the published object and Hanish’s performances are related but not identical works. [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/)

- **1934:** *Omar Khaiyam in seinen Rubaiyat*. A biographical and interpretive framing of Omar Khayyam’s *Rubáiyát*, translated and edited by Otto Rauth. Hanish made the biographical study, not the underlying poems; the volume is consequently a composite object whose parts have different authors. [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/)

- **1934:** *Das Weltprogramm der Neuen Zivilisation*. A fourteen-page manifesto drafted by Otto Rauth “after” Hanish. It acts as a political-social program, but bibliographic wording makes it a derivative formulation of Hanish’s project rather than an securely authored Hanish text. [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/)

### Songs, prayers, and collective ritual texts

- **No fixed first date; editions documented by 1920:** *Manthra: Avesta in Lied und Wort* / *Manthra: Avesta im Lied und Gebet*. A ritual collection of songs, prayers, affirmations, and purported Avestan wisdom used in communal practice. Editions name David Ammann, Frieda Ammann, and Otto Rauth as editors and sometimes merely state Hanish’s authorization; it therefore resists classification as a solely authored work and is better treated as a collective liturgical manifestation of his project. [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/)

- **No fixed date; edition documented by 1925:** *Mazdaznan-Liederbuch*. A multilingual musical and ritual collection, with one edition consisting predominantly of musical notation. Its contents belong to the movement’s shared practice, and the consulted bibliography does not establish that Hanish wrote every text or melody. [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/)

### Pamphlets and manifesto-like interventions

- **No securely fixed date; circulated by the 1910s:** *Sexuelle Aufklärung*. A free instructional pamphlet extracted from *Inner Studies/Wiedergeburt*. As an extract it is a separate public intervention but not an independent underlying composition. [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/)

- **1924:** *Die kommende Frau: Wacht auf, ihr Frauen!*. A manifesto-like address included in the collective pamphlet *Frauen-Monatspflege*. Hanish’s contribution is identified within an otherwise composite publication rather than as a securely documented stand-alone work. [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/)

- **Circa 1929:** *War Jesus ein Jude?* A four-page polemical pamphlet described as being “in a new adaptation.” The phrase indicates an earlier form but neither its date nor the identity of the adapter is settled by the bibliography. [Mazdaznan Bibliografie](https://freies-verlagshaus.de/mazdaznan/)
