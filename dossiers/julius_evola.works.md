## Works by Julius Evola

### Poems, manifestos, and composite avant-garde works

- **1916–1922:** *Raâga Blanda*. A poetic cycle rather than a conventional book: its compositions circulated separately before Evola assembled them under this title in 1969. The dating belongs to the poems, not to their much later first collected edition. ([Fondazione Julius Evola](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/opere-di-julius-evola/); [Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/giulio-cesare-andrea-evola_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))
- **1920:** *Arte astratta: posizione teorica, 10 poemi, 4 composizioni*. A Gesamtkunstwerk-like portfolio combining an aesthetic manifesto, ten poems, and four visual compositions; reducing it to “a book” conceals its deliberate crossing of media. ([Fondazione Julius Evola](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/opere-di-julius-evola/))
- **1920:** *La parole obscure du paysage intérieur: poème à 4 voix*. An experimental French-language four-voice poem: a verbal score intended as polyphonic performance as much as printed text. ([Fondazione Julius Evola](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/opere-di-julius-evola/); [Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/giulio-cesare-andrea-evola_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))

### Paintings, prints, designs, and decorated objects

The 2025 catalogue raisonné is the first document-based analytical catalogue of Evola’s visual œuvre. It includes paintings, drawings, sketches, and decorated objects, but its complete checklist is not published in the freely accessible announcement; consequently, the named entries below are those individually identified in the Foundation’s public gallery and museum or heritage records. Evola also exhibited numerous works now lost, unidentified, or known only from historical exhibition lists, so those titles resist treatment as a securely surviving corpus. ([Fondazione Julius Evola, catalogue raisonné](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/notizie-comunicati/in-libreria-il-catalogo-ragionato-dellopera-artistica-di-julius-evola/))

- **1917–1918:** *Five o’clock tea*. A futurist-period oil painting, alternatively dated 1917 or 1917–18 in public records. ([Mart](https://www.mart.tn.it/en/mostre/julius-evola-lo-spirituale-nellarte-153256); [WikiArt checklist](https://www.wikiart.org/en/julius-evola/all-works/text-list))
- **1917–1918:** *Sequenza dinamica*. A futurist abstract composition; the date is generally given as a range rather than a single year. ([Exhibition checklist](https://www.futur-ism.it/esposizioni/Esp2022/ESP20220515_R.html))
- **circa 1917–1918:** *Fucina, studio di rumori*. A painting translating industrial sound into visual form; public checklists differ slightly over whether it belongs to 1917 or 1918. ([WikiArt checklist](https://www.wikiart.org/en/julius-evola/all-works/text-list))
- **1917–1918:** *Mazzo di fiori*. A painting from Evola’s early futurist phase; its apparently conventional subject does not make the object a conventional still life. ([WikiArt checklist](https://www.wikiart.org/en/julius-evola/all-works/text-list))
- **1918:** *Tendenza dell’idealismo estetico*. An abstract painting whose title makes a philosophical proposition; English-language listings render it as *The Tendency of Aesthetic Idealism*. ([WikiArt checklist](https://www.wikiart.org/en/julius-evola/all-works/text-list))
- **1918–1920:** *Paesaggio interiore, ore 10½*. An oil painting from the “inner landscape” sequence; sources date it across 1918–20 rather than fixing a single year. ([Mart](https://www.mart.tn.it/en/mostre/julius-evola-lo-spirituale-nellarte-153256))
- **1918–1920:** *Senza titolo (composizione)*. An untitled abstract composition; the parenthetical description is cataloguing language, not a secure authorial title. ([Exhibition checklist](https://www.futur-ism.it/esposizioni/Esp2022/ESP20220515_R.html))
- **1919:** *Composizione*. A woodcut on paper, not a painting; the generic title and medium are preserved by the Foundation’s public gallery. ([Fondazione Julius Evola, image gallery](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/immagini/immagini/))
- **1919:** *Mitragliatrice*. A futurist painting listed in the 1919 Grande Esposizione Nazionale Futurista; its present survival and identity are not secure in the accessible cataloguing record. ([Historical exhibition discussion](https://muromaestro.wordpress.com/2015/01/04/barone-giulio-cesare-evola-detto-giulio-pittore-a-roma-tra-arte-e-magia-1917-1921/))
- **1919:** *Cannone Déport*. A futurist painting exhibited in 1919; it is known through the exhibition record and should not be silently equated with any later untitled composition. ([Historical exhibition discussion](https://muromaestro.wordpress.com/2015/01/04/barone-giulio-cesare-evola-detto-giulio-pittore-a-roma-tra-arte-e-magia-1917-1921/))
- **1919:** *Dreadnought*. A futurist painting named after a battleship; like several works in the 1919 exhibition, it survives bibliographically more securely than materially. ([Historical exhibition discussion](https://muromaestro.wordpress.com/2015/01/04/barone-giulio-cesare-evola-detto-giulio-pittore-a-roma-tra-arte-e-magia-1917-1921/))
- **circa 1919–1921:** *Visione scenografica / Composizione Dada*. A painting circulating under two titles; the slash records unstable cataloguing rather than two independently established works. ([Artnet](https://www.artnet.com/artists/julius-evola/))
- **circa 1920:** *Progetto per L’universo degli eroi*. An oil study or project for another composition; its status as “project” is intrinsic to the title and should not be upgraded into a securely completed larger work. ([Fondazione Julius Evola, image gallery](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/immagini/immagini/))
- **1920:** *Tavolino, piano superiore*. A decorated tabletop: an applied-art object, neither painting nor text, and one of the objects explicitly embraced by the catalogue raisonné. ([WikiArt checklist](https://www.wikiart.org/en/julius-evola/all-works/text-list); [Fondazione Julius Evola, catalogue raisonné](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/notizie-comunicati/in-libreria-il-catalogo-ragionato-dellopera-artistica-di-julius-evola/))
- **date not securely published:** *Vaso in terracotta decorata e smaltata*. A decorated and glazed terracotta vessel included in the catalogue raisonné; the Foundation’s announcement supplies neither a fixed title nor date, so none is invented here. ([Fondazione Julius Evola, catalogue raisonné](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/notizie-comunicati/in-libreria-il-catalogo-ragionato-dellopera-artistica-di-julius-evola/))
- **1920–1921:** *DDDD – Paysage Dada*. An oil painting also catalogued descriptively as a Dada landscape. ([Fondazione Julius Evola, image gallery](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/immagini/immagini/))
- **1920–1921:** *Composizione (Paesaggio) Dada n. 3*. An oil painting whose numbering is not fully stable: some listings give “n. 3 (or n. 2),” and that uncertainty belongs in its identity. ([Fondazione Julius Evola, image gallery](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/immagini/immagini/); [WikiArt checklist](https://www.wikiart.org/en/julius-evola/all-works/text-list))
- **1921:** *Astrazione*. An abstract painting recorded under a generic title. ([WikiArt checklist](https://www.wikiart.org/en/julius-evola/all-works/text-list))
- **1921; replica in the 1960s:** *La fibra s’infiamma e le piramidi*. A Dada-period composition later remade by Evola; some secondary listings misprint *fibra* as *libra*. The replica is a distinct material manifestation, not merely a later edition. ([Fondazione Julius Evola, image gallery](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/immagini/immagini/); [WikiArt checklist](https://www.wikiart.org/en/julius-evola/all-works/text-list))
- **1921:** *Paesaggio interiore, apertura del diaframma*. An “inner landscape” painting linking abstraction to a staged alteration of perception. ([WikiArt checklist](https://www.wikiart.org/en/julius-evola/all-works/text-list))
- **1960s:** *Paesaggio interiore, illuminazione*. A late replica of an earlier Dada-period conception; it should be dated as a newly made object even though its composition looks backward. ([Fondazione Julius Evola, image gallery](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/immagini/immagini/))
- **1960s:** *Paesaggio interiore, intervallo*. Another late replica or reconstruction of an earlier conception, and therefore both retrospective project and independent material object. ([Fondazione Julius Evola, image gallery](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/immagini/immagini/))
- **1965–1970:** *Senza titolo [Cosmos]*. An oil painting whose bracketed name is a cataloguer’s identifying designation, not necessarily an authorial title. ([Fondazione Julius Evola, image gallery](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/immagini/immagini/))
- **dates and titles incompletely recoverable:** *Works shown in Evola’s 1920 Rome and 1921 Berlin exhibitions*. Historical accounts report fifty-four works in Rome and sixty in Berlin, but surviving titles, objects, and later replicas cannot all be matched one-to-one. This is a documented body of manifestations that resists being converted into a trustworthy list of extant individual works. ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/giulio-cesare-andrea-evola_(Dizionario-Biografico)/); [Fondazione Julius Evola, catalogue project](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/notizie-comunicati/catalogazione-delle-opere-artistiche-di-julius-evolain-libreria-studi-evoliani-2022/))

### Philosophical, esoteric, historical, and political obras

- **1925:** *Saggi sull’idealismo magico*. A collection of philosophical essays establishing Evola’s “magical idealism.” ([Fondazione Julius Evola](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/opere-di-julius-evola/))
- **1926:** *L’uomo come potenza: I Tantra nella loro metafisica e nei loro metodi di autorealizzazione magica*. A study of Tantra framed as a protocol of self-realization as well as a philosophical exposition; the first edition was undated but is bibliographically assigned to 1926. It was extensively remade as *Lo yoga della potenza*, not simply reprinted. ([Fondazione Julius Evola](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/opere-di-julius-evola/); [Edizioni Mediterranee](https://www.edizionimediterranee.net/autori/e/1927-julius-evola))
- **1926:** *L’individuo e il divenire del mondo*. Two lectures made into a short philosophical obra. ([Fondazione Julius Evola](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/opere-di-julius-evola/))
- **1927:** *Teoria dell’Individuo Assoluto*. The first half of Evola’s systematic philosophical project; he revised it later, with the revised form appearing only in 1973. ([Fondazione Julius Evola](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/opere-di-julius-evola/); [Edizioni Mediterranee](https://www.edizionimediterranee.net/autori/e/1927-julius-evola))
- **1928:** *Imperialismo pagano: Il fascismo dinanzi al pericolo euro-cristiano*. A polemical political manifesto, not a neutral description of fascism; Evola later repudiated or substantially qualified parts of its anti-Christian position. ([Fondazione Julius Evola](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/opere-di-julius-evola/); [Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/giulio-cesare-andrea-evola_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))
- **1930:** *Fenomenologia dell’Individuo Assoluto*. The second half of the “absolute individual” philosophical system. ([Fondazione Julius Evola](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/opere-di-julius-evola/))
- **1931:** *La tradizione ermetica nei suoi simboli, nella sua dottrina e nella sua “Arte Regia”*. A historical-esoteric interpretation of alchemy as a transformative “royal art,” thus poised between scholarly exposition and initiatory protocol. ([Fondazione Julius Evola](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/opere-di-julius-evola/); [Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/giulio-cesare-andrea-evola_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))
- **1932:** *Maschera e volto dello spiritualismo contemporaneo*. A critical survey of modern spiritualist, occult, and psychological movements. ([Fondazione Julius Evola](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/opere-di-julius-evola/))
- **1934:** *Rivolta contro il mondo moderno*. A synthetic philosophy of history and manifesto of “Tradition”; it acts polemically against modernity rather than merely recounting its history. ([Fondazione Julius Evola](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/opere-di-julius-evola/); [Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/giulio-cesare-andrea-evola_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))
- **1936:** *Tre aspetti del problema ebraico nel mondo spirituale, nel mondo culturale, nel mondo economico-sociale*. An antisemitic political tract. Its existence in the bibliography must not be confused with acceptance of its racial premises. ([Fondazione Julius Evola](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/opere-di-julius-evola/))
- **1937:** *Il Mistero del Graal e la tradizione ghibellina dell’Impero*. A historical-esoteric construction of the Grail tradition and imperial ideology. ([Fondazione Julius Evola](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/opere-di-julius-evola/))
- **1937:** *Il mito del sangue: Genesi del razzismo*. A genealogy and ideological treatment of racial theories, belonging to Evola’s explicitly racist corpus. ([Fondazione Julius Evola](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/opere-di-julius-evola/))
- **1941:** *Sintesi di dottrina della razza*. A systematic racial treatise accompanied in its first form by fifty-two photogravures; Evola’s “spiritual racism” supplemented rather than negated the work’s political racism. ([Fondazione Julius Evola](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/opere-di-julius-evola/); [Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/giulio-cesare-andrea-evola_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))
- **1941:** *Indirizzi per una educazione razziale*. A protocol-like political handbook prescribing racial education. ([Fondazione Julius Evola](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/opere-di-julius-evola/))
- **1943:** *La dottrina del risveglio: Saggio sull’ascesi buddhista*. A study of early Buddhist ascetic practice presented partly as a realizational path rather than solely as religious history. ([Fondazione Julius Evola](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/opere-di-julius-evola/))
- **1949:** *Lo yoga della potenza: Saggio sui Tantra*. A radical reworking of *L’uomo come potenza*, not an independent first conception and not a mere new edition. ([Fondazione Julius Evola](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/opere-di-julius-evola/); [Edizioni Mediterranee](https://www.edizionimediterranee.net/autori/e/1927-julius-evola))
- **1950:** *Orientamenti*. A concise political-existential manifesto intended to orient a postwar right-wing cadre; its function is directive and therefore partly protocol. ([Fondazione Julius Evola](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/opere-di-julius-evola/); [Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/giulio-cesare-andrea-evola_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))
- **1953:** *Gli uomini e le rovine*. A postwar political treatise expanding the program compressed in *Orientamenti*. ([Fondazione Julius Evola](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/opere-di-julius-evola/); [Edizioni Mediterranee](https://www.edizionimediterranee.net/autori/e/1927-julius-evola))
- **1958:** *Metafisica del sesso*. A comparative metaphysical study of erotic experience, neither sex manual nor simply a history of sexuality. ([Fondazione Julius Evola](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/opere-di-julius-evola/))
- **1960:** *L’“Operaio” nel pensiero di Ernst Jünger*. An extended interpretive essay on Jünger’s figure of the Worker. ([Fondazione Julius Evola](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/opere-di-julius-evola/))
- **1961:** *Cavalcare la tigre: Orientamenti esistenziali per un’epoca della dissoluzione*. An existential protocol for living through a civilization Evola regarded as irreversibly dissolving. ([Fondazione Julius Evola](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/opere-di-julius-evola/))
- **1963:** *Il cammino del cinabro*. An intellectual or “spiritual” autobiography organized around the development of Evola’s works and positions rather than a comprehensive life narrative. ([Fondazione Julius Evola](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/opere-di-julius-evola/); [Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/giulio-cesare-andrea-evola_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))
- **1964:** *Il fascismo: Saggio di un’analisi critica dal punto di vista della Destra*. A retrospective ideological analysis, initially issued in connection with *Note sul Terzo Reich*; its bibliographic boundaries therefore vary between descriptions of the original publication and later standalone editions. ([Fondazione Julius Evola](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/opere-di-julius-evola/); [Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/giulio-cesare-andrea-evola_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))
- **1968:** *L’arco e la clava*. A collection of essays made into a thematic obra rather than a continuously composed monograph. ([Fondazione Julius Evola](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/opere-di-julius-evola/))
- **1974:** *Ricognizioni: uomini e problemi*. A late collection of interpretive and polemical essays, published in the year of Evola’s death. ([Fondazione Julius Evola](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/opere-di-julius-evola/))
- **1974:** *Meditazioni delle vette*. A mountain-writing collection published in Evola’s final year; because it gathers previously written material, it is both an authored corpus and an editorially assembled object. ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/giulio-cesare-andrea-evola_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))

### Collective operative and editorial works

- **1927–1928:** *Ur: rivista di indirizzi per una scienza dell’Io*. A collaborative esoteric periodical and operative project directed by Evola with Arturo Reghini and other members of the Ur Group. Many contributions appeared under pseudonyms, so the whole cannot responsibly be attributed to Evola; later scholarship attributes the names “Arvo,” “Agarda,” and “Iagla” to him, but pseudonymic attribution remains part of the textual problem. ([IAPSOP archive](https://iapsop.com/archive/materials/ur_krur/); [Archè](https://www.arche-edizioni.com/index.php/it/categorie/l-m-n/magia/ur-krur-iv-tous-les-ecrits-de-ur-krur-1927-1928-1929-introduction-a-la-magie-1955.html))
- **1929:** *Krur*. The successor to *Ur*, directed by Evola after the rupture with Reghini and Parise; eight issues appeared, including two double issues. It remains a collective manifestation despite Evola’s stronger editorial control. ([IAPSOP archive](https://iapsop.com/archive/materials/ur_krur/); [publication history](https://evolaguenonebooks.wordpress.com/2012/06/29/gruppo-di-ur-krur-1929-3-vol-ed-tilopa/))
- **1955–1956; revised 1971:** *Introduzione alla magia quale scienza dell’Io*. A heavily revised three-volume reconstitution of material from *Ur* and *Krur*. It is an editorial Gesamtkunstwerk and practical protocol of the Ur Group, not a book wholly written by Evola, although he selected, revised, and contributed substantially to it. ([Edizioni Mediterranee](https://www.edizionimediterranee.net/autori/e/1927-julius-evola); [scholarly reference summary](https://ndl.ethernet.edu.et/bitstream/123456789/38998/1/33.pdf.pdf))
- **February–June 1930:** *La Torre*. A political-cultural fortnightly founded and directed by Evola; ten issues appeared before its suppression amid Fascist hostility. The periodical as a whole is an editorial project, while its individually signed articles remain separate texts. ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/giulio-cesare-andrea-evola_(Dizionario-Biografico)/); [Edizioni Mediterranee](https://www.edizionimediterranee.net/autori/e/1927-julius-evola))
- **1934–1943:** *Diorama filosofico: Problemi e prospettive nell’etica fascista*. A recurring page or intellectual forum curated by Evola in *Il Regime Fascista*, not an independently issued book during its original life. Because it incorporated other writers, its editorial architecture is Evola’s but its entire contents are not. ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/giulio-cesare-andrea-evola_(Dizionario-Biografico)/); [scholarly reference summary](https://ndl.ethernet.edu.et/bitstream/123456789/38998/1/33.pdf.pdf))
- **1941:** *Sangue e razza*. A projected Italian-German racial journal for which Evola drafted a program approved by Mussolini and began preliminary negotiations. It never materialized: the project—not a nonexistent run of issues—is the work. ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/giulio-cesare-andrea-evola_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))

### Translations and editorial adaptations

These are works Evola made as translator or editor, but not works he authored. The distinction matters because later bibliographies and catalogues sometimes file them indiscriminately under his name.

- **1939:** *La guerra occulta*, by Emmanuel Malynski and Léon de Poncins. An Italian edition edited by Evola; authorship remains Malynski’s and de Poncins’s. ([Bibliographic listing](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julius_Evola))
- **1949:** *Le madri e la virilità olimpica*, by Johann Jakob Bachofen. A Bachofen selection and Italian editorial construction prepared by Evola, involving curatorship rather than original authorship. ([Bibliographic listing](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julius_Evola))
- **1949:** *Studi sulla storia segreta dell’antico mondo mediterraneo*, by Johann Jakob Bachofen. An Italian Bachofen collection supplied with an introduction by Evola; the collected studies themselves are not his works. ([Bachofen bibliography](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johann_Jakob_Bachofen))
- **1957:** *Il tramonto dell’Occidente*, by Oswald Spengler. Evola’s Italian translation or editorial adaptation of Spengler; later bibliographic work distinguishes it from subsequent integral Italian translations. ([Spengler bibliography](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oswald_Spengler))

### Posthumous collections that resist classification as new works

- **1977 and later:** *La Torre*, *Simboli della tradizione occidentale*, *La tradizione di Roma*, *Ultimi scritti*, and numerous later thematic collections. These volumes contain texts Evola made, but the volumes themselves were assembled or issued after his death and therefore should not automatically be counted as new obras made by him. ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/giulio-cesare-andrea-evola_(Dizionario-Biografico)/); [Fondazione Julius Evola publications](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/pubblicazioni/))
- **posthumously published:** *Scritti sull’arte d’avanguardia*, *Scritti sugli Indoeuropei*, correspondence volumes, and comparable archival collections. Their component texts or letters are Evola’s manifestations, but their titles, selection, ordering, and book-form identities belong to later editors; without an article-level scholarly bibliography they cannot honestly be presented as independently designed works by Evola. ([Fondazione Julius Evola, Biblioteca evoliana](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/biblioteca-evoliana/); [Fondazione Julius Evola, Quaderni](https://www.fondazionejuliusevola.com/quaderni/))
