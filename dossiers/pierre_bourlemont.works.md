## Works by Pierre Bourlémont

### Charters and performative legal acts

- **Beginning of the thirteenth century:** *Confirmation of Étienne de Bourlémont’s gift of the Housselmont tithes*. A charter confirming to Mureau Abbey three quarters of the tithes donated by Pierre’s vassal; its date is not fixed more precisely. ([Poull, catalogue no. 22](https://fr.scribd.com/document/786552301/Georges-Poull-Le-Chateau-Et-Les-Seigneurs-de-Bourlemont-FULL))

- **Beginning of the thirteenth century:** *Undertaking not to molest the monks of Mureau*. A performative promise preserved through Mureau’s cartulary, apparently concluding an otherwise poorly documented dispute; it is an act rather than a descriptive text. ([Poull, catalogue no. 23](https://fr.scribd.com/document/786552301/Georges-Poull-Le-Chateau-Et-Les-Seigneurs-de-Bourlemont-FULL))

- **1201:** *Confirmation of Wiard de Prez’s donations to Mureau Abbey*. A joint confirmation issued by Pierre and Hugues, lord of La Fauche, validating another lord’s gifts rather than making a new donation of their own. ([Poull, catalogue no. 27](https://fr.scribd.com/document/786552301/Georges-Poull-Le-Chateau-Et-Les-Seigneurs-de-Bourlemont-FULL))

- **May 1202:** *Cession of the church of Burey-en-Vaux*. A charter recognizing Pierre’s transfer to the collegiate church of Liverdun of the church and its right of patronage. ([Poull, catalogue no. 28](https://fr.scribd.com/document/786552301/Georges-Poull-Le-Chateau-Et-Les-Seigneurs-de-Bourlemont-FULL))

- **1207:** *Gift of Fastolisle to Mureau Abbey*. A charter, made with the consent of his wife Félicité and son Joffroi, transferring an island in the Meuse within the territory of Domremy and reconfirming earlier family concessions. ([Poull, catalogue no. 29](https://fr.scribd.com/document/786552301/Georges-Poull-Le-Chateau-Et-Les-Seigneurs-de-Bourlemont-FULL))

- **1209:** *Gift of the “lescheria” of Greux*. A charter transferring the property or exploitation right called the *lescheria* to Mureau Abbey; the exact character of the medieval land-use term resists a confident modern classification. ([Poull, catalogue no. 30](https://fr.scribd.com/document/786552301/Georges-Poull-Le-Chateau-Et-Les-Seigneurs-de-Bourlemont-FULL))

- **1209:** *Gift of the meadow called “Preele” at Domremy*. A donation to Mureau Abbey made with the consent of Félicité and Pierre’s brother Simon. ([Poull, catalogue no. 31](https://fr.scribd.com/document/786552301/Georges-Poull-Le-Chateau-Et-Les-Seigneurs-de-Bourlemont-FULL))

- **About 1210:** *Declaration concerning Midrevaux*. A charter declaring that Pierre possessed nothing at Midrevaux and had no right to levy anything there—an operative renunciation, not a narrative work. ([Poull, catalogue no. 33](https://fr.scribd.com/document/786552301/Georges-Poull-Le-Chateau-Et-Les-Seigneurs-de-Bourlemont-FULL))

- **After 1210:** *Surety for one hundred livres*. A guarantee made by Pierre in an unidentified transaction concerning the county of Champagne. The surviving catalogue notice does not supply a fuller date or object, so treating it as a titled work would overstate the evidence. ([Poull, catalogue no. 38](https://fr.scribd.com/document/786552301/Georges-Poull-Le-Chateau-Et-Les-Seigneurs-de-Bourlemont-FULL))

- **1211:** *Gift of the “trait” of the churches of Frebécourt and Bourlémont*. A grant to Mureau Abbey, made with the assent of Pierre’s mother Agnès, stepfather Hanry of Fouvent, wife Félicité, and brother Simon; the precise right denoted by *trait* is not securely captured by a modern generic label. It is known through a charter issued by Agnès and Hanry rather than an independently surviving charter of Pierre. ([Poull, catalogue no. 39](https://fr.scribd.com/document/786552301/Georges-Poull-Le-Chateau-Et-Les-Seigneurs-de-Bourlemont-FULL))

- **1214:** *Gift of Martinvaux*. A grant to Rangéval Abbey of Pierre’s land called Martinvaux near Écrouves. ([Poull, catalogue no. 41](https://fr.scribd.com/document/786552301/Georges-Poull-Le-Chateau-Et-Les-Seigneurs-de-Bourlemont-FULL))

- **1219:** *Notification of Aubri de Pargny’s gift to Mureau*. Pierre’s charter records and validates Aubri’s perpetual-alms donation of land on the mountain beside the road called *Male Martir*; the underlying property was Aubri’s, so Pierre made the validating instrument rather than the gift itself. ([Poull, catalogue no. 48](https://fr.scribd.com/document/786552301/Georges-Poull-Le-Chateau-Et-Les-Seigneurs-de-Bourlemont-FULL))

- **February 1220, new style:** *Liege homage to the count of Bar*. A performative feudal declaration by which Pierre became the count’s liege man and promised the service of Bourlémont Castle against everyone except the count of Champagne. ([Poull, catalogue no. 50](https://fr.scribd.com/document/786552301/Georges-Poull-Le-Chateau-Et-Les-Seigneurs-de-Bourlemont-FULL))

- **1222:** *Renunciation of claims against the church of Liverdun*. An agreement made before the bishop of Toul, extinguishing Pierre’s claims at Bruley and Pagney-derrière-Barine. ([Poull, catalogue no. 53](https://fr.scribd.com/document/786552301/Georges-Poull-Le-Chateau-Et-Les-Seigneurs-de-Bourlemont-FULL))

- **February 1223, new style:** *Notification of Ancel Malvoisin’s sale to the chapter of Toul*. A charter declaring that Pierre’s brother-in-law Ancel, Pierre’s sister Agnès, and their son Étienne had sold their property at Trondes, except for a vineyard. The departmental archive describes the document as February 1222, apparently reflecting the old-style/new-style dating problem rather than a separate act. ([Poull, catalogue no. 54](https://fr.scribd.com/document/786552301/Georges-Poull-Le-Chateau-Et-Les-Seigneurs-de-Bourlemont-FULL); [Archives départementales de Meurthe-et-Moselle](https://archives.meurthe-et-moselle.fr/node/376))

### Joint authorizations and sealed manifestations

- **1181:** *Consent to the Bourlémont family’s gifts near Brixey*. Pierre appears with his parents Simon and Agnès and his sister Hersende in an episcopal confirmation. Because he was probably still a child and the surviving text is the bishop’s charter, this resists classification as an independent work by Pierre: what he “made” was legal assent, if even that assent was personally enacted. ([Poull, catalogue no. 14](https://fr.scribd.com/document/786552301/Georges-Poull-Le-Chateau-Et-Les-Seigneurs-de-Bourlemont-FULL))

- **1184:** *Consent to Étienne de Bourlémont’s donation*. Pierre joined Simon, Agnès, and Hersende in approving Étienne’s gift to Mureau Abbey. This is likewise a juridical participation preserved inside another person’s donation, not a securely independent Pierre charter. ([Poull, catalogue no. 16](https://fr.scribd.com/document/786552301/Georges-Poull-Le-Chateau-Et-Les-Seigneurs-de-Bourlemont-FULL))

- **Beginning of the thirteenth century:** *Consent to Pétronille de Domremy’s donation*. Pierre and the other heirs of his mother Agnès authorized Pétronille’s family donation to Mureau. The act creates consent but is not straightforwardly Pierre’s “work,” since the donation and surviving instrument belong principally to others. ([Poull, catalogue no. 19](https://fr.scribd.com/document/786552301/Georges-Poull-Le-Chateau-Et-Les-Seigneurs-de-Bourlemont-FULL))

- **Beginning of the thirteenth century:** *Sealing of Olry of Neufchâteau’s gift of Pargny*. Pierre, the lords Simon and Liébaut of Bauffremont, and the duke of Lorraine sealed Olry’s gift of his allod at Pargny. The made object attributable to Pierre is therefore a seal-authentication or co-manifestation, not the donation itself. ([Poull, catalogue no. 21](https://fr.scribd.com/document/786552301/Georges-Poull-Le-Chateau-Et-Les-Seigneurs-de-Bourlemont-FULL))

- **Ascension 1211:** *Consent to the gift of the allod of Perchies*. Pierre, Agnès, Hanry of Fouvent, and Simon assented to Thierry de Saint-Élophe’s family donation at Montbras to Mureau Abbey. It is a collective authorization embedded in another donor’s act and thus resists treatment as a separately authored text. ([Poull, catalogue no. 40](https://fr.scribd.com/document/786552301/Georges-Poull-Le-Chateau-Et-Les-Seigneurs-de-Bourlemont-FULL))

### Sources

- Georges Poull, *Le Château et les seigneurs de Bourlémont*, vol. I, *Pages d’histoire au pays de Jeanne d’Arc, 1149–1412* (Corbeil-Essonnes: Imprimerie Crété, 1962), especially the biographical chapter “Pierre, sire de Bourlémont” and the chronological catalogue of acts, nos. 14–54. Poull dates Pierre’s birth to about 1180—not about 1170—and his death only to 1225–1230; consequently the often-supplied “1226” is approximate, not a documented exact year.
