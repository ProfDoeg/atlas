## Works by Cristina Elisabet Fernández de Kirchner

### Book-length obra

- **2019:** *Sinceramente*. A first-person work of political memory and retrospective interpretation, published by Sudamericana; Fernández de Kirchner explicitly rejected the simpler label “autobiography.” The publisher and the author’s presentation identify it as her first book, and no later independently authored book appears in the publisher’s author catalogue.[^1][^2]

### Oratorical obra

- **2007–present:** *Discursos de Cristina Fernández de Kirchner*. An open-ended body of presidential, senatorial, vice-presidential, electoral, commemorative, and party-political speech rather than a single fixed text. Her official archive organizes the surviving transcripts and recordings by year from 2007 onward, but it is neither a critical collected-works edition nor demonstrably complete; some entries are editorial titles, translations, interviews, recordings, or texts by other people, so the archive itself resists treatment as a settled catalogue raisonné.[^3][^4][^5][^6]

### Political letters, statements, and interventions

- **2015–present:** *Cartas abiertas, declaraciones y documentos políticos*. A continuing, untitled-as-a-whole corpus of shorter interventions published through Fernández de Kirchner’s official site, including addressed letters, political analyses, legal statements, and audiovisual messages. Individual pieces sometimes act as manifestos—calling constituencies into political action—while others merely report or argue; the website does not supply a separate, complete authorial catalogue, and its mixed editorial authorship prevents every posted item from being attributed to her without qualification.[^7][^8][^9]

### Political project

- **2003–present:** *Proyecto nacional, popular y democrático* (also called the Kirchnerist political project). This is a project in the vocabulary of the atlas: an asserted program of government, political organization, and continuation extending beyond any single speech, law, administration, or person. Fernández de Kirchner repeatedly names and claims responsibility for advancing it, but it is a collective and contested formation—not her sole-authored obra—and therefore resists inclusion as an individually made work.[^8][^10]

### Institutional texts that resist classification as her works

- **1989–2023:** *Legislative initiatives, presidential messages, decrees, bills, and enacted policies*. Fernández de Kirchner signed, sponsored, introduced, or publicly defended many such texts as provincial legislator, national deputy, senator, president, and vice-president. They are official acts made through legislatures and the executive branch—often drafted by ministries, advisers, or parliamentary teams—and no authoritative catalogue treats them as her personal writings; accordingly, they are an institutional corpus associated with her agency, not securely attributable individual obras.[^11][^12]
