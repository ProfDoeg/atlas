## Works by Stanley Jeyaraja Tambiah

### Research projects and unpublished scholarship

- **1954:** *The Process of Secularization in Three Ceylonese Peasant Communities*. Cornell doctoral dissertation in sociology, social anthropology, and social psychology; its object was a research project embodied in an unpublished 391-page thesis with thirty-nine tables, rather than a commercially published book. [Sri Kantha’s retrospective bibliography](https://sangam.org/stanley-jeyaraja-tambiah-1929-2014/)

- **Date uncertain, before 1970:** *Monks, Priests and Peasants: A Study of Buddhism and Social Structure in Central Ceylon*. An apparently unpublished field-research manuscript or project concerning Buddhist temples in Sri Lanka. Retrospective accounts name it but do not establish a publication date or completed published form, so it resists classification as a finished work. [British Academy memoir](https://www.thebritishacademy.ac.uk/documents/1513/26_Tambiah1820.pdf)

- **1984:** *A Reformulation of Geertz’s Conception of the Theater State*. Conference paper presented at the Thirty-First International Congress of the Human Sciences in Asia and North Africa; it was subsequently expanded for *Culture, Thought, and Social Action*, making the conference paper an intermediate manifestation rather than a wholly separate finished study. [Fischer’s scholarly bibliography](https://anthrosource.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/aman.12152)

### Monographs and book-length obras

- **1958:** *The Disintegrating Village: Report of a Socio-Economic Survey*. Co-authored with N. K. Sarkar, this is a quantitative rural-sociology survey report issued by Ceylon University Press. Contemporary criticism was directed partly at its statistical method, but Tambiah’s joint authorship is not disputed. [Fischer’s scholarly bibliography](https://anthrosource.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/aman.12152)

- **1970:** *Buddhism and the Spirit Cults in North-East Thailand*. Ethnographic monograph and the first volume of the retrospective “Thai trilogy,” integrating doctrinal Buddhism and village spirit practices within one religious field. [British Academy memoir](https://www.thebritishacademy.ac.uk/documents/1513/26_Tambiah1820.pdf)

- **1973:** *Bridewealth and Dowry*. A jointly authored and internally divided comparative volume by Jack Goody and Tambiah, not a single-author monograph; Tambiah wrote its South Asian study and shared responsibility for the book’s larger comparative obra. [Fischer’s scholarly bibliography](https://anthrosource.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/aman.12152)

- **1976:** *World Conqueror and World Renouncer: A Study of Buddhism and Polity in Thailand against a Historical Background*. Historical-anthropological monograph and the second part of the Thai trilogy, moving from village religion to Buddhist kingship and polity. Some bibliographies misprint “Conqueror” as “Conquerer.” [British Academy memoir](https://www.thebritishacademy.ac.uk/documents/1513/26_Tambiah1820.pdf)

- **1979/1981:** *A Performative Approach to Ritual*. The 1979 Radcliffe-Brown Memorial Lecture, printed in volume 65 of the *Proceedings of the British Academy* and also issued separately in 1981; these are manifestations of one lecture-text, not two independent works. [British Academy bibliographic record](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/sacraments-of-the-law-and-the-law-of-the-sacraments/bibliography/C32266FF4F948BCC4172A6EA3C42325A)

- **1984:** *The Buddhist Saints of the Forest and the Cult of Amulets: A Study in Charisma, Hagiography, Sectarianism, and Millennial Buddhism*. Ethnographic and historical monograph completing the Thai trilogy, based chiefly on 1978–79 research into forest monks, charisma, amulets, and politics. [British Academy memoir](https://www.thebritishacademy.ac.uk/documents/1513/26_Tambiah1820.pdf)

- **1985:** *Culture, Thought, and Social Action: An Anthropological Perspective*. A deliberately composed collection of Tambiah’s theoretical essays, several revised from earlier articles or lectures; as a book it is a new composite obra, while its constituent texts retain earlier identities. [National Academy of Sciences memoir](https://www.nasonline.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/tambiah-stanley.pdf)

- **1986:** *Sri Lanka: Ethnic Fratricide and the Dismantling of Democracy*. An engaged political-anthropological tract as well as a scholarly monograph, written in response to Sri Lanka’s ethnic violence; its overt interventionist purpose makes it more than detached description. [British Academy memoir](https://www.thebritishacademy.ac.uk/documents/1513/26_Tambiah1820.pdf)

- **1990:** *Magic, Science, Religion, and the Scope of Rationality*. Book-length theoretical argument derived from the 1984 Lewis Henry Morgan Lectures; the lecture series and published book are successive manifestations of the same project. [National Academy of Sciences memoir](https://www.nasonline.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/tambiah-stanley.pdf)

- **1992:** *Buddhism Betrayed? Religion, Politics, and Violence in Sri Lanka*. Historical and political-anthropological monograph examining the conjunction of Buddhist institutions, nationalism, and violence; the question mark is part of its title. [University of Chicago Press](https://press.uchicago.edu/ucp/books/author/T/S/au5239110.html)

- **1996:** *Leveling Crowds: Ethnonationalist Conflicts and Collective Violence in South Asia*. Comparative monograph on riots and ethnonationalist mobilization in Sri Lanka, India, and Pakistan; it extends the Sri Lankan project into a regional analysis. [Fischer’s scholarly bibliography](https://anthrosource.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/aman.12152)

- **2002:** *Edmund Leach: An Anthropological Life*. Intellectual biography of Leach and, at the same time, a history and retrospective assessment of British social anthropology. [Cambridge University Press front matter](https://assets.cambridge.org/97805215/21024/frontmatter/9780521521024_frontmatter.pdf)

### Articles, chapters, and scholarly interventions

- **1955:** *Ethnic Representation in Ceylon’s Higher Administrative Services, 1870–1946*. Historical-sociological journal article on ethnicity and the colonial administrative service. [Retrospective research-paper bibliography](https://sangam.org/stanley-jeyaraja-tambiah-1929-2014/)

- **1956:** *Ritual and Drama in a Devil Dancing Ceremony*. Short ethnographic article published in the University of Ceylon periodical *Thunapaha*. [Retrospective research-paper bibliography](https://sangam.org/stanley-jeyaraja-tambiah-1929-2014/)

- **1957:** *Secularization of Family Values in Ceylon*. Co-authored with Bryce Ryan, this is a sociological journal article arising from the secularization research project. British and American bibliographies vary between “secularisation” and “secularization.” [Fischer’s scholarly bibliography](https://anthrosource.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/aman.12152)

- **1957:** *A Sociological Approach to the Problem of Crime: A Study of Criminal Behavior Resulting from Social Disruptions and Deviational Pressure under “Slum” Conditions*. Applied-sociology article in Colombo’s *Probation and Child Care Journal*. [Retrospective research-paper bibliography](https://sangam.org/stanley-jeyaraja-tambiah-1929-2014/)

- **1958:** *The Structure of Kinship and Its Relationship to Land Possession and Residence in Pata Dumbara, Central Ceylon*. Ethnographic kinship article; some later citations substitute “Central Province” for “Central Ceylon,” but the Royal Anthropological Institute title uses the latter. [Fischer’s scholarly bibliography](https://anthrosource.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/aman.12152)

- **1958:** *Some Sociological Problems of Colonization on a Peasant Framework*. Rural-sociology article in the *Ceylon Economist*, connected to Tambiah’s research on peasant settlement. [Retrospective research-paper bibliography](https://sangam.org/stanley-jeyaraja-tambiah-1929-2014/)

- **1963:** *Ceylon*. A substantial country study contributed to *The Role of Savings and Wealth in Southern Asia and in the West*; it is a commissioned comparative-report chapter rather than an autonomous monograph. [Retrospective research-paper bibliography](https://sangam.org/stanley-jeyaraja-tambiah-1929-2014/)

- **1965:** *Kinship Fact and Fiction in Relation to the Kandyan Sinhalese*. Extended analytical article revisiting contested descriptions of Kandyan kinship. [eHRAF scholarly record](https://ehrafworldcultures.yale.edu/cultures/ax04/documents)

- **1966:** *Polyandry in Ceylon, with Special Reference to the Laggala Region*. Long ethnographic chapter in *Caste and Kin in Nepal, India and Ceylon*; later catalogue records sometimes present it as a separate book, but the original work is a contributed chapter. [Fischer’s scholarly bibliography](https://anthrosource.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/aman.12152)

- **1967:** *The Politics of Language in India and Ceylon*. Comparative political-sociology article in *Modern Asian Studies*. [Retrospective research-paper bibliography](https://sangam.org/stanley-jeyaraja-tambiah-1929-2014/)

- **1967:** *The Caste System in Ceylon*. Brief contribution to the Ciba Foundation symposium volume *Caste and Race: Comparative Approaches*; its short printed span suggests a recorded symposium intervention rather than a conventional full chapter. [Retrospective research-paper bibliography](https://sangam.org/stanley-jeyaraja-tambiah-1929-2014/)

- **1968:** *The Ideology of Merit and the Social Correlates of Buddhism in a Thai Village*. Ethnographic chapter first published in *Dialectic in Practical Religion*, connecting Buddhist merit with village social relations. [Author-supplied NTU bibliography](https://dlbs.liberal.ntu.edu.tw/en/author/authorinfo.jsp?ID=20013)

- **1968:** *Literacy in a Buddhist Village in North-East Thailand*. Ethnographic chapter in Jack Goody’s *Literacy in Traditional Societies*, treating literacy as embedded in village religious and practical life. [British Academy memoir](https://www.thebritishacademy.ac.uk/documents/1513/26_Tambiah1820.pdf)

- **1968:** *The Magical Power of Words*. Malinowski Memorial Lecture published as an article in *Man*; it theorizes utterance as efficacious social action and therefore treats language itself as something made to act. [National Academy of Sciences bibliography](https://www.nasonline.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/tambiah-stanley.pdf)

- **1969:** *Animals Are Good to Think and Good to Prohibit*. Comparative article in *Ethnology* on animal classification and prohibition; capitalization varies among bibliographies. [National Academy of Sciences bibliography](https://www.nasonline.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/tambiah-stanley.pdf)

- **1973:** *Buddhism and This-Worldly Activity*. Article in *Modern Asian Studies* examining the relation between Buddhist values and practical worldly conduct. [Retrospective research-paper bibliography](https://sangam.org/stanley-jeyaraja-tambiah-1929-2014/)

- **1973:** *Dowry and Bridewealth, and the Property Rights of Women in South Asia*. Tambiah’s long component of the jointly authored *Bridewealth and Dowry*; it remains a separately attributable study within the composite book. [Retrospective research-paper bibliography](https://sangam.org/stanley-jeyaraja-tambiah-1929-2014/)

- **1973:** *Form and Meaning of Magical Acts: A Point of View*. Theoretical chapter in *Modes of Thought*, later revised and incorporated into *Culture, Thought, and Social Action*. [Fischer’s scholarly bibliography](https://anthrosource.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/aman.12152)

- **1973:** *The Persistence and Transformation of Tradition in Southeast Asia, with Special Reference to Thailand*. Synthetic article in *Daedalus* on historical transformation rather than static “tradition.” [Author-supplied NTU bibliography](https://dlbs.liberal.ntu.edu.tw/en/author/authorinfo.jsp?ID=20013)

- **1977:** *The Cosmological and Performative Significance of a Thai Cult of Healing through Meditation*. Ethnographic and theoretical article in *Culture, Medicine, and Psychiatry*. [Fischer’s scholarly bibliography](https://anthrosource.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/aman.12152)

- **1977:** *The Galactic Polity: The Structure of Traditional Kingdoms in Southeast Asia*. Article first presented in 1976 and published in the *Annals of the New York Academy of Sciences*; a revised version appeared in the 1985 essay collection, so the two printings are manifestations of one evolving argument. [Wiley publication record](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1749-6632.1977.tb41806.x)

- **1978:** *Sangha and Polity in Modern Thailand: An Overview*. Synthetic chapter in *Religion and Legitimation of Power in Thailand, Laos, and Burma*. [Author-supplied NTU bibliography](https://dlbs.liberal.ntu.edu.tw/en/author/authorinfo.jsp?ID=20013)

- **1978:** *The Buddhist Conception of Kingship and Its Historical Manifestations: A Reply to Spiro*. Polemical scholarly reply in the *Journal of Asian Studies*; some bibliographic records misidentify the journal as the “Journal of the Asiatic Society.” [Author-supplied NTU bibliography](https://dlbs.liberal.ntu.edu.tw/en/author/authorinfo.jsp?ID=20013)

- **1982:** *Famous Buddha Images and the Legitimation of Kings: The Case of the Sinhala Buddha (Phra Sihing) in Thailand*. Article in *RES: Anthropology and Aesthetics* about a religious image as a mobile embodiment and instrument of political legitimacy. [University of Chicago Journals](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.1086/RESv4n1ms20166675)

- **1983:** *On Flying Witches and Flying Canoes: The Coding of Male and Female Values*. Structural and symbolic analysis contributed to *The Kula: New Perspectives on Massim Exchange*. [National Academy of Sciences bibliography](https://www.nasonline.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/tambiah-stanley.pdf)

- **1984:** *The Buddhist Cosmos: Paradise Lost, Gained, and Transcended*. Article in *History of Religions* examining cosmology as a religious and social construction. [Author-supplied NTU bibliography](https://dlbs.liberal.ntu.edu.tw/en/author/authorinfo.jsp?ID=20013)

- **1986:** *The Reflexive and Institutional Achievements of Early Buddhism*. Comparative civilizational chapter in *The Origins and Diversity of Axial Age Civilizations*. [Author-supplied NTU bibliography](https://dlbs.liberal.ntu.edu.tw/en/author/authorinfo.jsp?ID=20013)

- **1987:** *At the Confluence of Anthropology, History, and Indology*. Programmatic article in *Contributions to Indian Sociology*, positioning its argument at a disciplinary junction rather than within a single method. [National Academy of Sciences bibliography](https://www.nasonline.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/tambiah-stanley.pdf)

- **1987:** *The Buddhist Arahant: Classical Paradigm and Modern Thai Manifestations*. Comparative chapter in *Saints and Virtues* concerning a religious ideal and its modern social embodiments. [Author-supplied NTU bibliography](https://dlbs.liberal.ntu.edu.tw/en/author/authorinfo.jsp?ID=20013)

- **1987:** *The Buddhist Conception of Universal King and Its Manifestations in South and Southeast Asia*. Comparative study of Buddhist kingship; bibliographic records treat it as a separately published text, though its exact publication container is inconsistently supplied. [Author-supplied NTU bibliography](https://dlbs.liberal.ntu.edu.tw/en/author/authorinfo.jsp?ID=20013)

- **1987:** *Reply to Symposium Participants*. A response associated with a review symposium on *World Conqueror and World Renouncer*; this is an intervention in a scholarly exchange rather than a freestanding research work, and bibliographic descriptions of its title are inconsistent. [Author-supplied NTU bibliography](https://dlbs.liberal.ntu.edu.tw/en/author/authorinfo.jsp?ID=20013)

- **1989:** *King Mahasammata: The First King in the Buddhist Story of Creation, and His Persisting Relevance*. Article in the *Journal of the Anthropological Society of Oxford*. [National Academy of Sciences bibliography](https://www.nasonline.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/tambiah-stanley.pdf)

- **1989:** *Bridewealth and Dowry Revisited: The Position of Women in Sub-Saharan Africa and North India*. Comparative reassessment in *Current Anthropology*, returning to the collaborative 1973 project with a new argument. [National Academy of Sciences bibliography](https://www.nasonline.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/tambiah-stanley.pdf)

- **1989:** *Ethnic Conflict in the World Today*. American Ethnological Association distinguished lecture published in *American Ethnologist*; it is simultaneously an address and a journal article. [National Academy of Sciences bibliography](https://www.nasonline.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/tambiah-stanley.pdf)

- **1990:** *Sri Lanka—Introduction*. Short editorial introduction to a Sri Lanka-focused section of the *Journal of Asian Studies*, not a full research article. [Retrospective research-paper bibliography](https://sangam.org/stanley-jeyaraja-tambiah-1929-2014/)

- **1990:** *Reflections on Communal Violence in South Asia*. Association for Asian Studies presidential address published in the *Journal of Asian Studies*; its form is both ceremonial address and analytical essay. [Retrospective research-paper bibliography](https://sangam.org/stanley-jeyaraja-tambiah-1929-2014/)

- **1992:** *The Nation-State in Crisis and the Rise of Ethnonationalism*. Public or institutional lecture-text circulated as a separate publication; available bibliographies do not consistently identify its original venue, so its precise material status remains uncertain. [National Academy of Sciences memoir](https://www.nasonline.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/tambiah-stanley.pdf)

- **1993:** *Buddhism, Politics, and Violence in Sri Lanka*. Chapter in *Fundamentalisms and the State: Remaking Polities, Economies, and Militance*, extending the argument of *Buddhism Betrayed?* in a comparative edited project. [Author-supplied NTU bibliography](https://dlbs.liberal.ntu.edu.tw/en/author/authorinfo.jsp?ID=20013)

- **1997:** *On the Subject of Buddhism Betrayed? A Rejoinder*. Short polemical response in *American Ethnologist* to Sasanka Perera’s review; it acts within a dispute rather than merely describing one. [Retrospective research-paper bibliography](https://sangam.org/stanley-jeyaraja-tambiah-1929-2014/)

- **1997:** *Friends, Neighbors, Enemies, Strangers: Aggressor and Victim in Civilian Ethnic Riots*. Analytical article in *Social Science & Medicine* on relational roles in collective violence. [Retrospective research-paper bibliography](https://sangam.org/stanley-jeyaraja-tambiah-1929-2014/)

- **1997:** *Participation in, and Objectification of, the Charisma of Saints*. Comparative chapter in *India and Beyond: Aspects of Literature, Meaning, Ritual and Thought*, written in honour of Frits Staal. [Author-supplied NTU bibliography](https://dlbs.liberal.ntu.edu.tw/en/author/authorinfo.jsp?ID=20013)

- **1998:** *What Did Bernier Actually Say? Profiling the Mughal Empire*. Historiographical article in *Contributions to Indian Sociology* reassessing the use of François Bernier in representations of Mughal rule. [Retrospective research-paper bibliography](https://sangam.org/stanley-jeyaraja-tambiah-1929-2014/)

- **2000:** *Transnational Movements, Diaspora, and Multiple Modernities*. Synthetic article in *Daedalus* treating modernity as plural and historically situated. [Retrospective research-paper bibliography](https://sangam.org/stanley-jeyaraja-tambiah-1929-2014/)

- **2005:** *Urban Riots and Cricket in South Asia: A Postscript to “Leveling Crowds”*. Article in *Modern Asian Studies* that explicitly reopens and updates the 1996 monograph rather than constituting an unrelated project. [Retrospective research-paper bibliography](https://sangam.org/stanley-jeyaraja-tambiah-1929-2014/)

- **2013:** *The Charisma of Saints and the Cult of Relics, Amulets, and Tomb Shrines*. Late comparative essay in *Radical Egalitarianism*, expanding Tambiah’s Thai work across Buddhist, Christian, Islamic, and Hindu settings. [Fischer’s scholarly bibliography](https://anthrosource.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/aman.12152)

### Interviews and dialogical texts

- **1983:** *Interview with Alan Macfarlane*. Recorded intellectual-life interview; it is a co-produced oral and audiovisual manifestation rather than a solely authored written work, and therefore resists being called a Tambiah “text” without qualification. [British Academy memoir](https://www.thebritishacademy.ac.uk/documents/1513/26_Tambiah1820.pdf)

- **1997:** *Continuity, Integration and Expanding Horizons*. Extended interview with Mariza Peirano, published in Portuguese translation in *Mana*; authorship is dialogical, and the English wording implied by the title is not necessarily a separately published original text. [University of São Paulo Encyclopedia of Anthropology](https://ea.fflch.usp.br/en/node/65)

### Editorial and collaborative projects

- **2013:** *Radical Egalitarianism: Local Realities, Global Relations*. Festschrift and edited collection credited to Felicity Aulino, Miriam Goheen, and Tambiah; Tambiah was both co-editor and contributor, although retrospective accounts differ in how prominently they credit his editorial role. [National Academy of Sciences memoir](https://www.nasonline.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/tambiah-stanley.pdf)
