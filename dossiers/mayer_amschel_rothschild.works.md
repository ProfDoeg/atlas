## Works by Mayer Amschel Rothschild

### Commercial obra

- **c. 1763–1810:** *M. A. Rothschild*. A trading and financial enterprise developed from commerce in coins, antiquities and assorted goods into exchange, credit and state-finance business; its beginning has no securely documented single foundation date, so it resists treatment as a conventionally dated “work.” ([Neue Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/11879132X.html); [Berghoeffer](https://books.google.com/books?id=OHzPAAAAMAAJ))

- **27 September 1810:** *Mayer Amschel Rothschild & Söhne*. A family banking partnership—an institutional obra rather than a building or book—created jointly with Amschel Mayer, Salomon Mayer and Carl Mayer Rothschild; Nathan was represented indirectly and the under-age Jacob received a copy but was not yet an active signatory. ([Rothschild Archive](https://www.rothschildarchive.org/business/origins_of_the_business/family_partnership); [archival text of the agreement](https://ubdocs.aau.at/open/voll/altbestand/AC06384322.pdf))

### Educational project and institution

- **1792; realized 1803–1804:** *Philanthropin*. A project for a modern Jewish school that Rothschild reportedly attempted in 1792 and later caused his employee Siegmund Geisenheimer to pursue; the school that opened in 1804 was formally organized by Geisenheimer and associates, making “founded by Rothschild” a useful but disputed simplification of collaborative authorship. ([Rothschild Archive](https://www.rothschildarchive.org/family/philanthropy/jewish_causes); [LAGIS Hessen](https://www.lagis-hessen.de/en/subjects/browse/current/33/section/0/pageSize/50/sn/edb); [I. E. Lichtigfeld-Schule](https://lichtigfeld-schule.de/was-sie-ueber-uns-wissen-sollten/die-geschichte-der-i-e-lichtigfeld-schule/))

### Political-financial project

- **1810–1811:** *Frankfurt Jewish emancipation negotiations*. A collaborative civic project in which Rothschild participated in negotiations with Karl Theodor von Dalberg and helped raise the 440,000-gulden redemption payment attached to the 1811 grant of civil equality. The resulting edict was Dalberg’s legal act, not a text authored by Rothschild, and the equality it created was revoked in 1816. ([Neue Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/11879132X.html))

### Protocols and manifestos of the family firm

- **27 September 1810:** *Gesellschaftsvertrag zwischen Meyer Amschel Rothschild, Amschel Mayer Rothschild, Salomon Mayer Rothschild und Carl Mayer Rothschild*. A jointly executed partnership protocol in five copies that constituted the new firm, allocated capital and authority, and regulated succession; it is therefore also a manifesto in the literal sense of a text that performed an institutional act. ([Rothschild Archive](https://www.rothschildarchive.org/business/origins_of_the_business/family_partnership); [Frankfurt archival transcription](https://ubdocs.aau.at/open/voll/altbestand/AC06384322.pdf))

- **1810:** *Circular announcing Meyer Amschel Rothschild und Söhne*. A printed business circular informing correspondents of the firm’s changed name and organization. It survives in the historical record as a corporate communication rather than an individually titled literary work, and its exact issue date is not established by the consulted sources. ([Berghoeffer](https://books.google.com/books?id=OHzPAAAAMAAJ))

- **1812:** *Testament des Meyer Amschel Rothschild*. A testamentary protocol governing property, the continued unity of the firm, succession and family participation; it acts upon the institution rather than merely describing it. The preserved text was registered at Frankfurt on 2 October 1812, after Rothschild’s death on 19 September. ([published documentary text](https://ubdocs.aau.at/open/voll/altbestand/AC06384322.pdf); [Neue Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/11879132X.html))

### Business records and correspondence

- **1771 and 1775:** *Zehend Buch of A. M. Rothschild in Frankfurt*. An account book or related commercial register recorded under this title in Salomon von Rothschild’s nineteenth-century family-archive register. The modern Rothschild Archive schedule marks it as not located, so its physical survival, contents and degree of autograph authorship remain uncertain. ([Rothschild Archive, “Salomon’s archive: a schedule”](https://www.rothschildarchive.org/materials/ar2002_salomons_archive.pdf))

- **1797:** *Balance of the accounts*. A business balance listed in the family-archive register but marked as removed because it remained in use; it is a product of Rothschild’s enterprise, although the schedule does not establish that Mayer Amschel personally wrote it. ([Rothschild Archive, “Salomon’s archive: a schedule”](https://www.rothschildarchive.org/materials/ar2002_salomons_archive.pdf))

- **1807:** *Balance of the accounts*. A second business balance similarly listed as removed for continuing use. Like the 1797 balance, it resists secure attribution to a single hand and may properly belong to the firm’s collective documentary production. ([Rothschild Archive, “Salomon’s archive: a schedule”](https://www.rothschildarchive.org/materials/ar2002_salomons_archive.pdf))

- **before 19 September 1812:** *Letters and business correspondence*. A dispersed and incomplete body of work comprising instructions, reports and negotiations rather than a fixed collection with an authorial title. Berghoeffer published selected archival documents, but extensive destruction and dispersal of early Frankfurt and private papers make a letter-by-letter complete list impossible from the surviving record. ([Berghoeffer](https://books.google.com/books?id=OHzPAAAAMAAJ); [Rothschild Archive guide to family papers](https://www.rothschildarchive.org/collections/research_guides/rothschild_papers); [Rothschild Archive guide to the Frankfurt house](https://www.rothschildarchive.org/business/m_a_rothschild_sohne_frankfurt/))
