## Works by Satyendra Nath Bose

### Scientific papers and research reports

- **1918:** *On the Influence of the Finite Volume of Molecules on the Equation of State*. A theoretical-physics paper made with Meghnad Saha; it applies statistical reasoning to a real gas and is Bose’s first item in the standard collected scientific papers. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf)

- **1919:** *The Stress-Equations of Equilibrium*. A mathematical paper on integrating stress equations for elastic bodies. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf)

- **1919:** *On the Herpolhode*. A short geometrical study in classical mechanics concerning the force-free motion of a rigid body. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf)

- **1920:** *On the Equation of State*. A further real-gas paper made with Meghnad Saha; some bibliographies abbreviate its journal citation inconsistently, but the 1920 attribution is stable. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf)

- **1920:** *On the Deduction of Rydberg’s Law from the Quantum Theory of Spectral Emission*. A theoretical paper applying early quantum conditions to atomic spectra. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf)

- **1924:** *Plancks Gesetz und Lichtquantenhypothese* (*Planck’s Law and the Light-Quantum Hypothesis*). The foundational quantum-statistical paper: Bose supplied the manuscript and Einstein translated it into German and arranged publication. The collected edition prints both the German publication and a later English translation, but these are manifestations of one obra, not two independently authored works. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf)

- **1924:** *Wärmegleichgewicht im Strahlungsfeld bei Anwesenheit von Materie* (*Thermal Equilibrium in the Radiation Field in the Presence of Matter*). A second quantum-statistical paper, likewise published in German through Einstein’s intervention; Einstein appended reservations about Bose’s proposed transition probabilities. The German and English versions in the collected edition are manifestations of the same paper. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf)

- **1927:** *Messungen der Zersetzungsspannung in nichtwässerigen Lösungsmitteln* (*Measurement of the Decomposition Voltage in Nonaqueous Solvents*). An experimental electrochemistry paper describing measurements of polarization and decomposition voltages; the collected English version is a translation, not a second work. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf)

- **1929:** *Beryllium Spectrum in the Region λ 3367–1964*. An experimental spectroscopy paper made with S. K. Mukherjee at Dhaka University. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf)

- **1936:** *On the Complete Moment-Coefficients of the D²-Statistic*. A mathematical-statistics paper on the sampling distribution associated with Mahalanobis’s \(D^2\) statistic. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf)

- **1937:** *On the Moment-Coefficients of the D²-Statistic and Certain Integral and Differential Equations Connected with the Multivariate Normal Population*. A continuation of Bose’s work in multivariate statistics. The INSA bibliography prints “central” in place of “certain” in one transcription; the collected-paper title reads “Certain.” [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf), [INSA biographical memoir and bibliography](https://www.insaindia.res.in/BM/BM7_8307.pdf)

- **1937:** *Anomalous Dielectric Constant of Artificial Ionosphere*. An experimental and theoretical ionospheric-physics note made with S. R. Khastgir. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf)

- **1938:** *On the Total Reflection of Electromagnetic Waves in the Ionosphere*. A mathematical-physics paper applying microscopic Maxwell–Lorentz equations and characteristic surfaces to radio-wave reflection. Later assessments found that some of its collisional results were not quantitatively accurate; that limitation belongs to the work’s history. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf)

- **1939:** *Studies in Lorentz Group*. A mathematical paper on decomposing the complex orthogonal/Lorentz group into commuting factors. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf)

- **1941:** *The Complete Solution of the Equation \(\nabla^2\phi-\partial^2\phi/(c^2\partial t^2)-k^2\phi=-4\pi\rho(x,y,z,t)\)*. A paper made with S. C. Kar on the inhomogeneous Klein–Gordon equation and related retarded potentials. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf)

- **1943:** *Reaction of Sulphonazides with Pyridine: Salts and Derivatives of Pyridine-Imine*. An experimental chemistry paper made with P. K. Dutta. Its disciplinary difference from Bose’s better-known physics is part of the obra rather than a reason to exclude it. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf)

- **1943:** *A Note on Dirac Equations and the Zeeman Effect*. A quantum-mechanics paper made with K. Basu, treating hydrogenic energy levels in an inhomogeneous magnetic field. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf)

- **1945:** *On an Integral Equation Associated with the Equation for Hydrogen Atom*. A mathematical-physics paper recasting the Coulomb problem as an integral equation in momentum space. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf)

- **1950:** *Germanium from Sphalerite*. A short analytical-chemistry report on germanium in Nepalese sphalerite. It belongs to a three-part cluster whose titles and authorship are not uniform: this preliminary note is followed by two explicitly numbered extraction papers. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf)

- **1950:** *Extraction of Germanium from Sphalerite Collected from Nepal—Part I*. An experimental extraction report made with R. K. Dutta. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf)

- **1950:** *Extraction of Germanium from Sphalerite Collected from Nepal—Part II*. The continuation of the extraction study, also made with R. K. Dutta. Some secondary lists omit Part I or give Part II’s first page as 217 rather than 271; the collected edition treats both parts as separate papers. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf), [INSA biographical memoir and bibliography](https://www.insaindia.res.in/BM/BM7_8307.pdf)

- **1953:** *Les Identités de divergence dans la nouvelle théorie unitaire*. A French-language note on divergence identities in Einstein’s nonsymmetric unified-field theory. The collected English translation, *The Identities of Divergence in the New Unified Theory*, is a later manifestation of this paper. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf)

- **1953:** *Une théorie du champ unitaire avec \(\Gamma_\mu\neq0\)*. A French-language unified-field paper deriving field equations from a variational principle. Its collected English translation, *A Unitary Field Theory with \(\Gamma_\mu\neq0\)*, is not a separate Bose work. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf)

- **1953:** *Certaines conséquences de l’existence du tenseur g dans le champ affine relativiste*. A French-language paper deriving integrability relations for a nonsymmetric tensor in an affine relativistic field. The English item printed beside it in the collected edition is a translation. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf)

- **1954:** *The Affine Connection in Einstein’s New Unitary Field Theory*. A mathematical paper giving explicit expressions for the affine connection in Einstein’s nonsymmetric theory. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf)

- **1955:** *A Report on the Study of Thermoluminescence*. A collaborative research report made with J. Sharma and B. C. Dutta; unlike a single-result paper, it records an experimental programme on impurities, self-absorption, energy transfer, colour centres, and luminescence. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf), [Bose biographical résumé](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Bose%27s%20e-Archive%20-%20Bose%27s%20Resume%20-%20final%20-%209.6.14%20%284%29.pdf)

- **1955:** *Solution d’une équation tensorielle intervenant dans la théorie du champ unitaire*. Bose’s last listed scientific paper, concerning an algebraic tensor equation in unified-field theory. Its English translation in the collected edition is a manifestation rather than another original paper. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf)

### Scientific addresses that became texts

- **1929:** *Tendencies in the Modern Theoretical Physics*. Bose’s presidential address to the Physics and Mathematics Section of the Indian Science Congress; it is an address subsequently fixed as a published text, not an ordinary research paper. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf)

- **1937:** *Recent Progress in Nuclear Physics*. A survey article or public scientific exposition rather than an original experimental report. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf)

- **1944:** *The Classical Determinism and the Quantum Theory*. Bose’s presidential address to the Indian Science Congress, subsequently published in its proceedings; its primary identity is performative and institutional—an address—although it survives as a text. [Collected Scientific Papers](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Collected%20Scientific%20Papers.pdf)

### Translation and editorial obra

- **1920:** *The Principle of Relativity: Original Papers by A. Einstein and H. Minkowski*. A collaborative English translation volume: Bose translated Einstein’s paper on general relativity, Meghnad Saha translated the special-relativity and Minkowski materials, and P. C. Mahalanobis supplied the historical introduction. Bose therefore made part of the English textual object, not the underlying papers and not the whole volume alone. [INSA biographical memoir and bibliography](https://www.insaindia.res.in/BM/BM7_8307.pdf), [Life, Lectures and Addresses, Miscellaneous Pieces](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Life%2C%20Lectures%20and%20Addresses%2C%20Miscellaneous%20Pieces.pdf)

### Institutional and material projects

- **1948 onward:** *Bangiya Bijnan Parishad*. A collective institution founded under Bose’s leadership to create and circulate scientific knowledge in Bengali. It is a project and continuing body of work rather than a text attributable to Bose alone. [S. N. Bose Archive](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/), [Life, Lectures and Addresses, Miscellaneous Pieces](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Life%2C%20Lectures%20and%20Addresses%2C%20Miscellaneous%20Pieces.pdf)

- **1948 onward:** *Jnan O Bijnan*. The Bengali science periodical associated with Bangiya Bijnan Parishad, to which Bose contributed and whose vernacular-science programme he helped make. It is a collective serial manifestation, not a book or a wholly single-authored work. [Life, Lectures and Addresses, Miscellaneous Pieces](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Life%2C%20Lectures%20and%20Addresses%2C%20Miscellaneous%20Pieces.pdf)

- **By 1954:** *Bose’s spectrophotometer*. A scientific instrument whose constructional details Bose presented at the International Crystallography Conference in Paris and which the biographical résumé says was subsequently adopted in several European laboratories. The surviving bibliographies do not provide a fixed formal title, patent, or sufficiently exact fabrication date, so this material obra should not be converted into a fictitious publication. [Bose biographical résumé](https://www.bose.res.in/Prof.S.N.Bose-Archive/objects/Bose%27s%20e-Archive%20-%20Bose%27s%20Resume%20-%20final%20-%209.6.14%20%284%29.pdf)

### Editorial limits and resistant material

- **Various dates:** *Uncollected Bengali writings and translations*. The 1994 editors explicitly state that their “Miscellaneous Pieces” are only a selection: they omitted several Bose translations from French or German and pieces whose subjects were treated more fully elsewhere. Consequently, no responsible title-by-title claim to “everything” survives in the standard edition; this remainder is a documented but incompletely catalogued body of manifestations, and inventing titles or dates would falsely turn an archival gap into works.
