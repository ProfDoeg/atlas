## Works by Javier Gerardo Milei

### Books and book-length obras

- **2014:** *Lecturas de economía en tiempos de kirchnerismo*. A collection of economic essays published under Milei’s sole name; the title is sometimes printed with inconsistent capitalization and, in one ISBN index, the typographical error “kirchserismo.” [S1][S2]
- **2014:** *Política económica contrarreloj: síntomas, diagnóstico y medidas para salir del cepo y volver a crecer*. A jointly authored economic-policy book by Milei, Diego Giacomini, and Federico Ferrelli Mazza, compiled partly from work produced for Fundación Acordar. [S1][S3]
- **2015:** *El retorno al sendero de la decadencia argentina*. A sole-authored economic and political essay. [S1]
- **2016–2017:** *Maquinita, infleta y devaluta: ensayos de economía monetaria para el caso argentino* / *Otra vez sopa: maquinita, infleta y devaluta*. A coauthored book with Diego Giacomini; *Otra vez sopa* is the retitled Ediciones B version of the earlier Unión Editorial obra, not a wholly separate work. [S1][S3]
- **2018:** *Desenmascarando la mentira keynesiana: Keynes, Friedman y el triunfo de la Escuela Austríaca*. A book-length polemic and history-of-economic-thought essay contrasting Keynesian, neoclassical, monetarist, and Austrian approaches. Unión Editorial dates the obra to 2018 although its Spanish product record gives a 2019 issue date. [S4]
- **2019:** *Libertad, libertad, libertad: para romper las cadenas que no nos dejan crecer*. A coauthored synthesis of libertarian philosophy, economics, and policy by Milei and Diego Giacomini. [S5]
- **2020:** *Pandenomics: la economía que viene en tiempos de megarrecesión, inflación y crisis global*. A topical economic and political essay responding to COVID-19 and government lockdowns. Investigative reporting has alleged that portions reproduce other authors without adequate attribution; Milei disputed the broader plagiarism accusations made against his writings. [S6][S7]
- **2022:** *El camino del libertario*. A hybrid obra combining autobiography, economic writings, and revised political speeches rather than a single continuous treatise. Planeta withdrew and replaced its 2024 Spanish edition because the publisher acknowledged errors in the biographical copy; that incident concerns the edition’s paratext, not the existence or original 2022 date of the obra. [S8][S9]
- **2023:** *El fin de la inflación: eliminar el Banco Central, terminar con la estafa del impuesto inflacionario y volver a ser un país en serio*. A programmatic economic essay whose prescriptions make it partly a political manifesto. [S8]
- **2024:** *Capitalismo, socialismo y la trampa neoclásica*. A book-length economic and ideological argument assembled around Milei’s critique of neoclassical market-failure theory and his defense of Austrian economics. [S8]
- **2025:** *La construcción del milagro*. An anthology of speeches, articles, and reflections presenting Milei’s governing program; reporting found that much of it reprinted material already published, including nine chapters from the 2024 book. It therefore resists treatment as an entirely new integral work. [S10]
- **2026:** *La construcción del milagro: las fuerzas del cielo*. The expanded Spanish edition issued by Unión Editorial, described by the publisher as a collection of speeches, essays, and reflections. Its relation to the 2025 Argentine book is principally that of an expanded or retitled edition, so it is not counted here as an independent underlying obra. [S11]
- **2026:** *La batalla por la macroeconomía*. A book-length work catalogued by Unión Editorial under Milei’s name. Because the available publisher record supplies bibliographical data but little account of how much material is new, its degree of overlap with the preceding books remains unresolved. [S12]

### Prefaces and other contributed texts

- **2020:** *Prólogo a 4000 años de controles de precios y salarios*. A commissioned prefatory essay rather than authorship of the book it introduces. Reporting has alleged substantial self-reuse between this text and *Pandenomics*, so its independence as a separate work is disputed. [S7][S11]
- **2024:** *Prólogo a La economía en una lección*, 9th Spanish edition. A prefatory contribution to Henry Hazlitt’s work, not a Milei-authored edition or translation. [S11]

### Peer-reviewed and scholarly articles

- **2004:** *Real Exchange Rate Targeting: ¿Trilema monetario o control de capitales? La política fiscal*. A sole-authored economics article on exchange-rate targeting and countercyclical fiscal policy; the journal volume is identified as 2004 although the surviving PDF also displays “Año 2005,” creating a minor dating ambiguity. [S13][S14]
- **2005:** *El origen político-fiscal de la inflación*. A coauthored journal article by Diego Giacomini and Milei. [S14]
- **2006:** *El pago con reservas al FMI: “La eterna historia del maltrato de la política a la economía”*. A coauthored journal article by Milei and Galo Hernán García Tamayo. [S14]
- **2007:** *Teoría de la inversión y mercados financieros: valuación de empresas y equilibrio macroeconómico*. A sole-authored technical article. [S14]
- **2008:** *El nuevo gobierno vs. los molinos de viento: los falsos dilemas al acecho del crecimiento y el bienestar*. A sole-authored economic-policy article. [S14]
- **2010:** *El elixir de la teoría monetaria argentina: apuesta por el error tipo II o una pasión desenfrenada por la inflación*. A sole-authored monetary-economics article. [S14]
- **2010:** *Desequilibrio global, rebalanceo y guerra de monedas*. A coauthored article by Milei and Nicolás Kerst. [S14]
- **2011:** *Teoría de la inversión y mercados financieros: la “q” de Tobin y su uso para la valuación de empresas*. A sole-authored technical article that develops a related but separately published argument from the 2007 paper. [S14]
- **2012:** *La crisis americana y una interpretación pigouviana en el marco del “3-Equation Model” del nuevo consenso macroeconómico*. A sole-authored macroeconomics article. [S14]
- **2014:** *De los picapiedras a los supersónicos: maravillas del progreso tecnológico con convergencia*. A sole-authored article about technological progress and economic convergence. [S14]
- **2017:** *Ensayos monetarios para economías abiertas: el caso argentino*. A coauthored journal article by Milei and Diego Giacomini. [S14]

### Journalism and public-economic commentary

- **Before 2018–present:** *Academic and journalistic article corpus*. Milei’s archived professional curriculum claims more than fifty academic articles and nearly one hundred journalistic pieces; later profiles claim still larger totals. No authoritative collected-works edition or complete enumerative bibliography has been published, while the scholarly index consulted records only eleven journal articles from 2004–2017. This corpus therefore resists item-by-item naming without substituting newspaper or database searches for the standard bibliography required here. [S14][S15]

### Music and performance

- **Circa 1989–early 1990s:** *Everest repertoire*. Milei sang in a rock band called Everest, which performed Rolling Stones covers and reportedly included songs he composed. No discography, stable titles, recordings, or reliable dates for those original compositions are documented in the consulted sources; the repertoire is therefore a reported body of creation, not a recoverable list of named works. [S16][S17]

### Political projects and manifestos

- **2021–present:** *La Libertad Avanza*. A political project and electoral coalition built around Milei’s candidacies, rather than an individually authored text. Its collective and evolving authorship prevents attribution of the entire organization to Milei as a sole obra. [S18]
- **2023:** *Electoral government program of La Libertad Avanza*. A collective political protocol setting out proposed state reforms. It acts programmatically rather than merely describing beliefs, but party authorship means it should not be silently converted into a sole-authored Milei work. [S19]
- **2024:** *Pacto de Mayo*. A ten-point political manifesto proposed by Milei and signed with provincial governors; it is both text and public act. Because it was negotiated, issued through the presidency, and signed collectively, it is a governmental manifestation associated with Milei rather than an uncontested personal work. [S20]
