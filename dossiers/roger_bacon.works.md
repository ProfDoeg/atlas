## Works by Roger Bacon

### University lectures and scholastic question-collections

- **c. 1240s:** *Quaestiones supra libros octo Physicorum Aristotelis*. A surviving set of university questions on all eight books of Aristotle’s *Physics*; modern scholarship continues to examine whether every part belongs to Bacon. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/); [Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://iep.utm.edu/roger-bacon/)
- **c. 1240s:** *Quaestiones supra libros Primae philosophiae Aristotelis* (Metaphysics I–II, V–X). A scholastic lecture-commentary in question form, transmitted among Bacon’s Parisian teaching materials. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/); [Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://iep.utm.edu/roger-bacon/)
- **c. 1240s:** *Quaestiones supra De plantis*. Questions on the pseudo-Aristotelian *De plantis*, representing a course or teaching text rather than an autonomous “book” in the modern sense. [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)
- **c. 1240s:** *Quaestiones supra librum De causis*. A question-commentary on the *Liber de causis*, surviving as part of Bacon’s university teaching corpus. [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)
- *Liber de sensu et sensato* (also catalogued as *Quaestiones supra De sensu et sensato*). A scholastic treatment of Aristotle’s work on sensation; its exact date and textual boundaries are not securely fixed. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/); [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)
- *Quaestiones supra librum De generatione et corruptione*. An unedited question-commentary preserved in Rome, Collegio di S. Isidoro MS I/10; Bacon’s authorship is possible but unascertained. [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)
- *Tractatus de anima*. A treatise concerning the soul listed in the modern Bacon works catalogue; its precise date, form, and attribution remain unsettled. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *Quaestiones supra librum De anima*. A question-commentary on Aristotle’s *De anima*, catalogued separately from the *Tractatus de anima* but not securely dated or attributed. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)

### Logic and grammar

- **c. 1240s:** *Summulae dialectices*. A systematic teaching manual of logic: both a pedagogical protocol and a philosophical work. [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)
- **c. 1240s:** *Summa de sophismatibus et distinctionibus*. A logical summa devoted to sophisms and distinctions, the kinds of linguistic puzzles used in university disputation. [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)
- **c. 1240s:** *Summa grammatica*. A grammatical summa associated with Bacon’s early arts teaching. [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)
- *Grammatica graeca*, version A (“Oxford Greek Grammar”). One of two substantially different manifestations of Bacon’s Greek grammar; the date is undetermined. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/); [Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://iep.utm.edu/roger-bacon/)
- *Grammatica graeca*, version B (“Cambridge Greek Grammar”). A second recension or reworking, sufficiently distinct to be catalogued separately; it resists treatment as merely another copy of version A. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *Grammatica hebraica*. A Hebrew grammar surviving only as a fragment; its exact date is undetermined. [Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://iep.utm.edu/roger-bacon/)

### The reform project and the three works for Clement IV

- **1266–1268:** *Opus maius*. The principal manifestation sent in response to Pope Clement IV’s demand for Bacon’s work: an encyclopedic argument for reforming Christian learning through languages, mathematics, optics, experimental inquiry, moral philosophy, and theology. It is better understood as a project-manifesto assembled from interacting sciences than as a single-topic book. [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/roger-bacon/); [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)
- **1266–1268:** *Epistola ad Clementem IV Papam*. A dedicatory letter or prologue transmitting and explaining the *Opus maius*; because it acts upon a patron and frames the reform proposal, it has something of the manifesto as well as the letter. [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)
- **1266–1268:** *De signis*. Bacon’s semiotic treatment of signs, preserved as material belonging to part III of the *Opus maius* but absent from early editions; it is a component of the larger project rather than an entirely independent obra. [Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://iep.utm.edu/roger-bacon/)
- **1266–1268:** *Perspectiva*. The optical part of the *Opus maius*, also transmitted and edited as a substantial treatise in its own right; it therefore occupies an unstable position between component and independent work. [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)
- **1266–1268:** *De scientia experimentali*. Part VI of the *Opus maius*, articulating the role of experiential knowledge; it is a programmatic component, not a freestanding modern treatise despite often being excerpted as one. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- **1266–1268:** *Philosophia moralis*. Part VII of the *Opus maius*, a large work of moral and political philosophy also circulating independently. [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)
- **1267:** *Opus minus*. A shorter companion, correction, and supplement to the *Opus maius*, made for the same papal project rather than simply an abridgment. [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/roger-bacon/); [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)
- **1267–1268:** *Opus tertium*. A third explanatory and supplementary work for Clement IV, partly recapitulating and partly extending the preceding works; portions survive separately, so its original completeness and organization have been debated. [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)
- **1266–1268:** *Letter to Pope Clement IV*. A separately preserved letter-fragment about Bacon’s commission and the transmission of his work; its relation to the prologue of the *Opus maius* makes its status as an independent work uncertain. [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)

### Parts of the projected *Scriptum principale*

- **after 1268:** *Communia naturalium*. A large, unfinished or incompletely preserved foundation for natural philosophy, apparently produced as part of Bacon’s projected *Scriptum principale*. The “work” may properly be the unrealized encyclopedic project, of which this text is one manifestation. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/); [Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://iep.utm.edu/roger-bacon/)
- **after 1268:** *De caelestibus*. The second book of the *Communia naturalium*, treating celestial things; catalogues distinguish it for convenience, although it belongs structurally to that larger work. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- **after 1268:** *De influentiis agentium*. A natural-philosophical treatment of causal influence, associated with the same larger project and closely related to the theory of species. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- **after 1268:** *De multiplicatione specierum*. A treatise explaining how agents propagate powers or “species” through media; it is both a substantial obra and a component of Bacon’s projected synthesis of nature. [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)
- **after 1268:** *De speculis comburentibus*. A treatise on burning mirrors and geometrical optics, transmitted with the natural-philosophical material; its alternative titles reflect unstable manuscript boundaries. [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)
- **after 1268:** *Communia mathematica*. An unfinished or incompletely transmitted foundation for mathematical knowledge within the projected synthesis. [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)
- *Geometria speculativa*. A theoretical geometry preserved as a continuation of the *Communia mathematica* but omitted from Steele’s edition; its independent title should not conceal its status as part of that larger text-project. [Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://iep.utm.edu/roger-bacon/)
- *Scriptum principale*. Bacon’s intended comprehensive exposition of knowledge. It resists being called a surviving work at all: it is fundamentally a project, known through plans and partially realized components rather than a completed textual object. [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/roger-bacon/)

### Compendia and polemical syntheses

- **1271–1272:** *Compendium studii philosophiae*. A compressed survey and critique of contemporary learning, linked to the unrealized *Scriptum principale* but possessing its own polemical form. [Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://iep.utm.edu/roger-bacon/); [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)
- *Metaphysica de vitiis contractis in studio theologiae*. A metaphysical and polemical text on errors acquired in theological study; manuscript titles vary substantially, and some witnesses assign it to other authors. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- **late 13th century:** *Compendium studii theologiae*. An unfinished theological compendium concerned especially with language, interpretation, and the structure of theology. [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)

### Commentary, translation, and textual intervention

- **late 1270s–1280s:** *Secretum secretorum cum glossis et notulis*. Bacon’s edition of the pseudo-Aristotelian *Secretum secretorum*, supplied with introduction, glosses, and notes. The underlying work is not his: Bacon’s obra is the editorial-commentarial apparatus and the transformed textual assemblage. [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/roger-bacon/); [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)
- *Tractatus ad declarandum quaedam obscure dicta in libro Secreti secretorum Aristotelis*. An explanatory sheet or short treatise clarifying obscure passages in the *Secretum secretorum*; it may overlap with the apparatus catalogued under that work, so its independence is uncertain. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)

### Medicine, regimen, and practical protocols

- *Antidotarius*. A pharmaceutical handbook or protocol of compounded remedies, edited among Bacon’s medical works; its Baconian authorship has not been examined as securely as that of the major philosophical works. [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)
- *De erroribus medicorum*. A critique of physicians’ errors, generally retained in the Bacon corpus. [Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://iep.utm.edu/roger-bacon/)
- *De graduatione medicinarum compositarum*. A technical protocol for determining the degrees or strengths of compound medicines; it is catalogued as Bacon’s, though its date is unknown. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *De diebus creticis*. A medical-astrological treatment of critical days in illness, catalogued among Bacon’s works but not securely dated. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *De crisi morborum*. A text on the crisis or turning point of diseases, likewise of uncertain date and incompletely established authorship. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *De universali regimine senum et seniorum*. A regimen for the elderly, edited among the Baconian medical texts; its attribution is less secure than that of Bacon’s principal works. [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)
- *De balneis senum et seniorum*. A practical text on baths for older people; it is transmitted within the medical corpus but remains attributionally insecure. [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)
- *De compositione quarundam medicinarum, in speciali quae iuvant sensum*. A protocol for preparing particular medicines affecting the senses, edited under Bacon’s name but not securely authenticated. [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)
- *De conservatione iuventutis*. A regimen concerned with preserving youth, transmitted under Bacon’s name; its place in the authentic corpus remains uncertain. [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)
- *Liber sex scientiarum*. A medical or practical work organized around “six sciences,” edited in the Bacon corpus but of uncertain authenticity. [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)

### Computation, astronomy, and calendar reform

- *Computus naturalium*. A work on calendrical or astronomical computation traditionally associated with Bacon. The text printed as *Compotus* in the collected edition was shown by Ferdinand Delorme to be spurious, so no uncomplicated Baconian obra can be asserted under that title. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/); [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)
- **1260s:** Bacon’s calendar-reform proposal within the *Opus maius*. This is a practical reform project embedded in a larger work, not a securely independent text; it sought correction of the ecclesiastical calendar through astronomical computation. [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/roger-bacon/)

### Letters and operative texts

- **13th century; transmitted incompletely:** *Epistola de secretis operibus artis et naturae et de nullitate magiae*. A letter-treatise distinguishing the powers of art and nature from magic and describing possible techniques and machines. The early manuscript survives only partially, while the familiar complete form incorporates later textual material, so its integrity as a single Baconian work is disputed. [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)

### Uncertain or disputed early works

- *Quaestiones supra undecimum Primae philosophiae Aristotelis* (Metaphysics XII). Once printed as Bacon’s, but now considered probably spurious; it should be recorded as an attribution, not silently counted as something Bacon made. [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)
- *De undecimo libro Primae philosophiae Aristotelis*. A separate treatment of Metaphysics XII listed in the modern catalogue; its relationship to the attributed *Quaestiones* and its authorship remain uncertain. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *Quaestiones supra libros quattuor Physicorum*. A four-book *Physics* question-commentary formerly attributed to Bacon but now judged probably spurious. [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)
- *Quaestiones alterae supra libros Primae philosophiae Aristotelis* (Metaphysics I–IV). An alternative metaphysics commentary printed as Bacon’s but now regarded as probably spurious. [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)
- *Si terminus ad quem generandus* / *Tractatus de tempore et motu*. A short treatise on time and motion attributed to Bacon by S. Harrison Thomson; the attribution has never been established. [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)

### Alchemical and magical attributions

- *De nigromantia*. A work on ritual or illicit magic catalogued under Bacon’s name, but not accepted securely as his. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *Speculum alchemiae* / *De transmutatione metallorum*. A famous alchemical text transmitted under Bacon’s name; it belongs to the Pseudo-Bacon corpus, and another version is attributed to Arnald of Villanova. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *Tractatus expositorius aenigmatum alchemiae*. An exposition of alchemical riddles attributed to Bacon, without secure evidence of authorship. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *De naturis metallorum*. An alchemical work circulating under numerous titles, including *Breve breviarium* and *Caelestis alchymia*; it is catalogued as apocryphal or doubtful. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *De Leone viridi*. An alchemical tract on the “green lion,” perhaps by the Franciscan Raymond Gaufredi rather than Bacon. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *Tractatus trium verborum* / *Epistolae tres ad Iohannem Parisiensem*. A group of three operative alchemical letters—*De separatione ignis ab oleo*, *De modo miscendi*, and *De ponderibus*—traditionally attributed to Bacon but treated as Pseudo-Baconian. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *Secretum secretorum naturae: de laude lapidis philosophorum*. An alchemical “secret of secrets” praising the philosophers’ stone; it is not the annotated pseudo-Aristotelian *Secretum secretorum* and is only pseudonymously Baconian. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *Radix mundi*. An alchemical treatise on the philosophers’ stone attributed to Bacon but placed in the doubtful or apocryphal corpus. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *De subiecto transmutationis*. An alchemical tract concerning the subject of transmutation, transmitted under Bacon’s name without secure authentication. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *Editio super Geberem de tribus ordinibus medicinae*. An alchemical-medical commentary attributed to Bacon; its authorship is unestablished. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *Compendium alchemiae*. An alchemical compendium assigned to Bacon only tentatively even in its manuscript attribution. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *Epistola de spiritu occulto in sulphure arsenico*. An alchemical letter on a hidden spirit in sulphur or arsenic, surviving as a Pseudo-Baconian attribution. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *Tractatus de quibusdam aquis alchemicis*. A protocol concerning alchemical waters, said to have been found in an old Baconian compilation but not thereby shown to be Bacon’s work. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *Thesaurus spirituum*. An occult or alchemical “treasury of spirits” attributed to Bacon in later tradition; it is catalogued as apocryphal or doubtful. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *De consideratione quintae essentiae*. A work on the fifth essence circulating under Bacon’s name, without secure Baconian authorship. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *Semita recta alchemiae*. An alchemical protocol or “straight path,” part of the later Pseudo-Bacon corpus. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *De oleo stibii*. A technical alchemical text on antimony oil, attributed rather than securely ascribed to Bacon. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)

### Other rejected or unresolved attributions

- *De retardandis senectutis accidentibus et de sensibus conservandis*. A regimen long printed as Bacon’s but now considered spurious; a separate exposition of it also circulated under his name. [Roger Bacon Research Society](https://rogerbaconresearchsociety.com/works/)
- *Quaestio disputata de intellectu*. A disputed question attributed instead to Rogerus Anglicus, a Franciscan who died around 1303. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *Compendium de logica*. An elementary logical treatise transmitted under Bacon’s name but catalogued among doubtful or spurious works. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *De intellectu et intelligentia*. A philosophical treatise on intellect and intelligence attributed to Bacon without secure authentication. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *Philosophia pauperum*. A philosophical compendium assigned to Bacon in some witnesses but retained only in the doubtful corpus. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *Rogeriana maior* and *Rogeriana minor*. Two later compilations bearing Bacon’s name; their titles indicate Baconian collections rather than securely authored works. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *Kalendarium*. A calendar attributed to Bacon, but not securely identifiable with his authenticated calendar-reform work. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *De magnete*. A work on the magnet attributed to Bacon; no secure authorship has been established. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *De musica*. A musical treatise transmitted under Bacon’s name but placed in the doubtful or apocryphal corpus. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *De sacrae Scripturae profundis mysteriis*. A work on scriptural mysteries attributed to Bacon in later tradition, without secure authentication. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *Tractatus super Psalterium*. A psalter commentary attributed to “Brother R. Bacon,” an identification too weak to establish Roger Bacon’s authorship. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *Quaestiones supra librum De caelo et mundo*. Questions on Aristotle’s *De caelo*, catalogued among doubtful Baconian works. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *Posteriora*. A work associated with Aristotle’s *Posterior Analytics*, attributed to Bacon but not securely authenticated. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *De fluxu et refluxu maris Anglici*. A work on the English sea’s tides transmitted under Bacon’s name but classified as doubtful or spurious. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *Extractiones*. A collection of extracts rather than necessarily an authored text; its attribution to Bacon does not establish that he selected or made the compilation. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *Voynich manuscript*. Formerly attributed to Bacon, but the modern catalogue places it among works only once attributed to him; it is not accepted as something he made. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
- *Tractatus de incarnatione sive de secretis philosophiae*. Formerly attributed to Bacon but assigned to Jean Quidort; it is not a Baconian work. [UAB catalogue](https://webs.uab.cat/rogerbacon/obres/)
