## Works by David Rockefeller

### Economic studies and lectures

- **1940–1941:** *Unused Resources and Economic Waste*. Rockefeller’s doctoral dissertation and first economic monograph. The Rockefeller Archive Center dates its publication to 1940, while the University of Chicago Press edition is dated 1941; the discrepancy should not be silently resolved. ([Rockefeller Archive Center](https://rockarch.org/resources/about-the-rockefellers/david-rockefeller/); [Google Books](https://books.google.com/books/about/Unused_Resources_and_Economic_Waste.html?id=_k0lAAAAMAAJ))

- **1964:** *Creative Management in Banking*. A short management treatise made from Rockefeller’s McKinsey Foundation lectures and issued by McGraw-Hill; it is therefore both an oral lecture project and a book. Contemporary Authors incorrectly calls the series “Kinsey,” while the bibliographic record gives “McKinsey.” ([Contemporary Authors](https://www.encyclopedia.com/arts/educational-magazines/rockefeller-david-1915); [EconBiz](https://www.econbiz.de/Record/creative-management-in-banking-rockefeller-david/10000677993))

- **1976:** *New Roles for Multinational Banks in the Middle East*. A policy essay or lecture-book published in Cairo by the General Egyptian Book Organization, proposing an expanded developmental role for multinational banks. Its small, occasional form resists a clean distinction between book and separately issued address. ([Contemporary Authors](https://www.encyclopedia.com/arts/educational-magazines/rockefeller-david-1915))

### Memoir

- **2002:** *Memoirs*. An autobiography and retrospective account of Rockefeller’s banking, family, philanthropic, diplomatic, and collecting activities, issued by Random House. It is credited to Rockefeller; the consulted standard bibliography does not identify a coauthor. ([Contemporary Authors](https://www.encyclopedia.com/arts/educational-magazines/rockefeller-david-1915); [Google Books](https://books.google.com/books?id=R84xS9eEgsAC))

### Recorded speech and interview manifestations

- **1973:** *Trade with the Soviet Union*. A commercially issued sound recording in the “Vital History Cassettes” series. Rockefeller’s authorship is performative rather than strictly literary: the object preserves his spoken views, and the bibliography does not establish whether he composed a fixed script. ([Contemporary Authors](https://www.encyclopedia.com/arts/educational-magazines/rockefeller-david-1915))

- *Oil, Money, and the Arab World*. A sound recording issued by the Encyclopedia Americana/CBS News Audio Resource Library. The standard bibliography supplies no date and describes its recording list only as inclusive, not demonstrably complete; Rockefeller is therefore best classified as its recorded speaker or participant rather than assumed sole textual author. ([Contemporary Authors](https://www.encyclopedia.com/arts/educational-magazines/rockefeller-david-1915))

### Archival body of work

- **1913–2016:** *David Rockefeller papers*. An archival manifestation comprising Rockefeller’s personal and professional papers, office files, associates’ files, photographs, correspondence, memoranda, and other documents. It resists being called a single obra: the collection contains texts made by Rockefeller alongside received papers, collaborative office products, and records made about him, and the archive states that additional material remains to be processed. ([Rockefeller Archive Center, DIMES](https://dimes.rockarch.org/collections/dpeGABqjx3PiDREar9o5VH))

### Sources

The standard biobibliographical work list used as the principal enumeration; it records three printed works and two sound recordings but omits the doctoral monograph.
- [“David Rockefeller, 1915–2017,” Rockefeller Archive Center](https://rockarch.org/resources/about-the-rockefellers/david-rockefeller/). Institutional biography confirming the dissertation, its reported 1940 publication, and Rockefeller’s professional projects.
- [*David Rockefeller papers*, Rockefeller Archive Center DIMES](https://dimes.rockarch.org/collections/dpeGABqjx3PiDREar9o5VH). Archival catalogue and collection-level account of the surviving documentary corpus.
