## Works by Karl Gräser

### Social and life-reform projects

- **1900–1901:** *Monte Verità*. A collectively founded Lebensreform settlement—made with Henri Oedenkoven, Ida and Jenny Hofmann, Gusto Gräser, Lotte Hattemer and others—conceived as a cooperative, property-light community rather than simply a sanatorium. Karl’s authorship is therefore participatory, not exclusive; after the project’s commercial turn he separated from the founding group. ([Fondazione Monte Verità](https://www.monteverita.org/de/geschichte); [Szeemann catalogue record](https://stroom.nl/stroom-algemeen/bibliotheek/online-catalogus/304))

- **From 1901:** Karl and Jenny Gräser’s independent household on Monte Verità. This was an enacted social project organized around subsistence gardening, hand production, barter and rejection of coercive authority and money; it resists classification as a discrete “work” because the made thing was a way of living. ([Erich Mühsam material collected by the Monte Verità Archiv](https://gusto-graeser.info/Monteverita/Personen/Muehsam.html); [Gräserhaus documentation](https://www.gusto-graeser.info/Monteverita/Graeserhaus/Graeserhaus_files/Graeserhaus.pdf))

- **From 1901:** The Gräser property and garden, later including the present Casa Francesco and what became Casa Bambu. The preservation documentation calls the whole ensemble a jointly created “Lebenslandschaft” made by Karl and Gusto Gräser, Jenny Hofmann and Lotte Hattemer; it is best understood as a Gesamtkunstwerk of dwelling, cultivation and social experiment, not as Karl’s sole architectural work. ([Gräserhaus documentation](https://www.gusto-graeser.info/Monteverita/Graeserhaus/Graeserhaus_files/Graeserhaus.pdf))

- **Undated, proposed during the Ascona years:** A rural home-school on Monte Verità. Karl and Gusto Gräser discussed this educational-community project with physician and Tolstoyan Albert Škarvan, but the available account does not establish that it was realized; the project, rather than a completed institution, is the work. ([Monte Verità Archiv, Gräserhaus](https://www.gusto-graeser.info/Monteverita/Graeserhaus/Graeserhaus_files/body_index.html))

### Buildings, landscape and access

- **1901–1902:** Karl Gräser’s first open-sided dwelling and workshop. He adapted an existing small structure, adding a masonry rear wall while leaving the upper sleeping level unusually exposed to the air; this was both shelter and a built protocol for living close to nature. ([Monte Verità Archiv biography and documentary dossier](https://gusto-graeser.info/Monteverita/Personen/Graeser%20Karl/GraeserKarl.html))

- **1902:** The access road across Gräser’s property toward Monte Verità. Municipal correspondence documents Karl seeking passage and quarrying rights; he levelled the way, cut trees and used the timber for building. It is functional infrastructure rather than an autonomous artwork, but it materially remade access to the settlement. ([Monte Verità Archiv biography and documentary dossier](https://gusto-graeser.info/Monteverita/Personen/Graeser%20Karl/GraeserKarl.html))

- **1904–1906:** *Casa Francesco*, also called the Gräserhaus or later the Demianhaus. Karl principally constructed the stone-and-timber house, with intermittent help from Gusto and Ernst Gräser, almost certainly from a design by Paul Evertz; attribution of the architecture itself therefore belongs primarily to Evertz, while Karl’s role was builder, collaborator and maker of its lived programme. The two Franciscan frescoes are by Alexander Wilhelm de Beauclair, not Karl. ([Gräserhaus documentation](https://www.gusto-graeser.info/Monteverita/Graeserhaus/Graeserhaus_files/Graeserhaus.pdf))

### Furniture and domestic constructions

- **Undated, probably 1901–1915:** Karl Gräser’s natural-wood furniture. Surviving photographs and exhibition documentation record a group of highly individual furnishings assembled from branches, poles and other minimally processed wood; no complete item-by-item catalogue or secure production dates were found, so the furniture must remain a body of work rather than a fabricated list of separately titled objects. ([Kunsthalle Düsseldorf exhibition documentation](https://www.kunsthalle-duesseldorf.de/media/handout_german_1.pdf); [Gräserhaus documentation](https://www.gusto-graeser.info/Monteverita/Graeserhaus/Graeserhaus_files/Graeserhaus.pdf))

- **Undated, probably 1901–1915:** Karl Gräser’s branch-built chair, commonly called the *Naturstuhl*. This is a surviving piece of natural furniture now associated with the Museo Casa Anatta; “Naturstuhl” functions as a descriptive name rather than a securely documented original title. ([Monte Verità Archiv exhibition record](https://gusto-graeser.info/Wirkung/Ausstellungen/ausstellungsliste_index_21Jahrhundert.html); [Kunsthalle Düsseldorf exhibition documentation](https://www.kunsthalle-duesseldorf.de/media/handout_german_1.pdf))

- **Undated, probably 1901–1915:** Karl Gräser’s bed suspended or built between two trees, with a small book shelf among the branches. Known from a photograph, it is simultaneously furniture, environmental construction and manifesto-like image of the Gräser programme; it has no established title. ([Gräserhaus documentation](https://www.gusto-graeser.info/Monteverita/Graeserhaus/Graeserhaus_files/Graeserhaus.pdf))

- **Undated, early twentieth century:** A decorated Christmas tree at the Gräserhaus, described in the preservation dossier as the first such tree in Ticino. This was an ephemeral domestic installation rather than a durable work, and the priority claim is made by that dossier rather than demonstrated by a catalogue raisonné. ([Gräserhaus documentation](https://www.gusto-graeser.info/Monteverita/Graeserhaus/Graeserhaus_files/Graeserhaus.pdf))

### Apiarian inventions and enterprise

- **By 1909:** *Reformstock* or *Bienenlade*. A redesigned beehive intended to simplify and broaden beekeeping; it received awards beginning in 1909 and was subsequently manufactured in Ascona. Sources variously describe it as patented and preserve a document catalogued as a “Patent for a New Type of Beehive,” but the accessible records do not supply a patent number or establish whether that sheet is an issued patent, application or design drawing. ([Monte Verità Archiv biography and documentary dossier](https://gusto-graeser.info/Monteverita/Personen/Graeser%20Karl/GraeserKarl.html); [Swiss Federal Office of Culture object list](https://www.bak.admin.ch/dam/bak/de/dokumente/rueckgabegarantie/gesetze/Anhang%20zum%20Antrag%20der%20Kunsthalle%20Bern%20%2820.03.2018%29.pdf.download.pdf/Objektliste%20RG18008.pdf))

- **By 1914:** Karl Gräser’s Ascona beehive factory. This was an entrepreneurial manifestation of the *Reformstock* project rather than a separate authored object; its output consisted of beehives and apicultural equipment, but no reliable model-by-model production list was located. ([Monte Verità Archiv biography and documentary dossier](https://gusto-graeser.info/Monteverita/Personen/Graeser%20Karl/GraeserKarl.html))

- **1914:** An illustrated prospectus for Karl Gräser’s beehive factory. This is a commercial and instructional printed object whose existence is recorded by the documentary archive, but its exact title, contents and survival status are not specified there. ([Monte Verità Archiv biography and documentary dossier](https://gusto-graeser.info/Monteverita/Personen/Graeser%20Karl/GraeserKarl.html))

### Technical invention

- **Applied for 1909; granted 1911:** *Einrichtung zur Sicherung des durch Gegengewicht aufklappbaren Sitzbrettes von Spülaborten*. A patented mechanism securing a counterweight-raised lavatory seat: a utilitarian invention and protocol embodied in hardware, not a literary text. The documentary account gives the application and grant years but not a patent number. ([Monte Verità Archiv biography and documentary dossier](https://gusto-graeser.info/Monteverita/Personen/Graeser%20Karl/GraeserKarl.html))

### Manuscripts and writings

- **Circa 1914:** *Die vereinfachte – grosszügige Bienenzucht in der Bienenlade: Für Anfänger, Leute aus Stadt und Land, die ohne grosse Vorkenntnisse nutzbringende Bienenzucht betreiben wollen*. An illustrated or illustration-planned instructional manuscript: simultaneously an apicultural handbook, protocol and philosophical account of the bee colony. It appears to have remained unpublished, and its completeness and present archival location are not established by the consulted sources. ([Monte Verità Archiv manuscript description](https://www.gusto-graeser.info/Monteverita/Personen/Broch/Bienen-Grossvater.html); [biographical dossier](https://gusto-graeser.info/Monteverita/Personen/Graeser%20Karl/GraeserKarl.html))

- **By 1905:** Untitled philosophical notes or “writings” that Erich Mühsam intended to edit. Mühsam referred to preparing Karl’s surviving records for publication, but no authoritative bibliography, stable titles or evidence of a completed edition was found; this is therefore an incompletely recoverable textual corpus, and inventing individual works from it would be misleading. ([Monte Verità Archiv, Mühsam dossier](https://gusto-graeser.info/Monteverita/Personen/Muehsam.html); [Otto Gross dossier](https://gusto-graeser.info/Monteverita/Personen/GrossOtto/GrossOttoNeueLiebeswelt.html))
