## Works by Juan Martín de Pueyrredón y O'Doggan

### Correspondence and letter books

- **1802–1806:** *Correspondencia*. A corpus of commercial, family, and personal letters preserved chiefly in three manuscript notebooks; the modern scholarly edition describes many texts as drafts rather than copies of letters demonstrably sent, so the corpus is both correspondence and writing-in-process. [Academia Nacional de la Historia edition review](https://repositorio.uca.edu.ar/bitstream/123456789/9778/1/juan-martin-pueyrredon-correspondencia.pdf)

- **1807–1808:** *Correspondencia de la misión ante la Corte de Madrid*. Letters, petitions, reports, and delegated instructions made while representing the Buenos Aires Cabildo in Spain, including communications to the Cabildo, Manuel de Velazco, Simón de Agreda, the Cádiz Consulado, and the Junta of Seville. The Museo Mitre edition treats this as a documentary series rather than a single authored “book.” [Museo Mitre, *Documentos del archivo de Pueyrredón*, contents](https://books.google.com/books?id=SkFCAQAAMAAJ); [Museo Mitre archival inventory](https://patrimonioargentino.cultura.gob.ar/index.php/Collections/childList/collection_id/667)

- **1811–1812:** *Correspondencia como gobernador-intendente y jefe del Ejército del Perú*. A body of official letters, reports, negotiations, and orders addressed principally to the provisional government and Manuel Belgrano. These are governmental and military acts as much as texts and resist treatment as individually titled literary works. [Museo Mitre archival inventory](https://patrimonioargentino.cultura.gob.ar/index.php/Collections/childList/collection_id/667)

- **1816–1819:** *Correspondencia del Director Supremo*. A large administrative corpus of letters and official communications to Congress, provincial authorities, commanders, diplomats, and figures including San Martín, Güemes, Artigas’s delegate Miguel Barreiro, and Manuel Antonio de Castro. Individual documents were often produced through government offices, so a signature does not invariably establish sole composition by Pueyrredón. [Museo Mitre archival inventory](https://patrimonioargentino.cultura.gob.ar/index.php/Collections/childList/collection_id/667); [Archivo Histórico de la Provincia de Buenos Aires, description of the Director’s official books](https://biblioteca.cfi.org.ar/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2022/05/informe-final-archivo-historico-para-cfi-3-ejemplar.pdf)

- **1820–1849:** *Later correspondence and papers*. Letters, replies, petitions, and defensive papers from his retirement and periods of exile, including exchanges with the Baron de la Laguna, Miguel Flangini, Fray Francisco Castañeda, Manuel García, Vicente Virgil, and the Buenos Aires government. This is an open archival remainder rather than a work Pueyrredón himself assembled. [Museo Mitre archival inventory](https://patrimonioargentino.cultura.gob.ar/index.php/Collections/childList/collection_id/667)

### Expositions, reports, and memorials

- **1806:** *Escrito sobre la conquista de los ingleses y defensa de Buenos Aires*. A retrospective account of the British occupation and the organization of resistance; it is an evidentiary political-military narrative rather than a formally published book. [Museo Mitre archival inventory](https://patrimonioargentino.cultura.gob.ar/index.php/Collections/childList/collection_id/667)

- **1807–1808:** *Manifiesto del Cabildo de la ciudad de Buenos Aires por su representante en la Corte de Madrid*. A report-manifesto on Pueyrredón’s mission, the French invasion of Spain, and metropolitan policy toward Buenos Aires. The surviving manuscript is catalogued as **1815**, evidently as a later copy or archival manifestation, while its narrated mission belongs to 1807–1808; that discrepancy should not be silently converted into a composition date. [Museo Mitre inventory description](https://museomitre.cultura.gob.ar/media/uploads/site-10/multimedia/documentos_del_archivo_de_pueyrredon_mm.pdf); [Museo Mitre, *Documentos del archivo de Pueyrredón*, contents](https://books.google.com/books?id=SkFCAQAAMAAJ)

- **1807–1808:** *Representación hecha por Juan Martín de Pueyrredón a la Corte de Madrid*. A diplomatic petition reporting news from the Río de la Plata, requesting an audience, and announcing his intended return. It is a performative administrative text—designed to obtain action—not a descriptive treatise. [Museo Mitre catalogue record](https://patrimonioargentino.cultura.gob.ar/index.php/Detail/collections/8122)

- **1809:** *Fiel exposición que hace don Juan Martín de Pueyrredón de su conducta pública desde el año 1806 hasta el presente de 1809*. A self-vindicating political narrative written at the Portuguese court in Brazil after his arrests and flight; later editions sometimes shorten its title to an “exposición de su conducta pública.” [*Anuario de Estudios Americanos*, with title, date, and documentary reference](https://ri.conicet.gov.ar/bitstream/handle/11336/121733/CONICET_Digital_Nro.3f672b81-fcbe-41c9-a38d-104865779627_A.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=2)

- **1811:** *Informe a la Junta de las Provincias Unidas sobre la retirada de los caudales de Potosí*. A military-financial report explaining the evacuation from Upper Peru and removal of the Potosí treasury after Huaqui. It is simultaneously testimony, justification, and an official act. [Pueyrredón Archivo Digital](https://pueyrredon.com/publicaciones/resolvi-intentarlo-todo/); [Wikisource author inventory](https://es.wikisource.org/wiki/Autor%3AJuan_Mart%C3%ADn_de_Pueyrred%C3%B3n)

- **1817:** *Exposición de los trabajos del Gobierno Supremo de las Provincias Unidas de Sud América en la presente administración*. A public governmental exposition reviewing the first fifteen months of his administration. Although issued under Pueyrredón’s name, it belongs to the hybrid genre of an official state account and may embody ministerial or secretarial labor as well as his authority. [Biblioteca Digital del Patrimonio Iberoamericano record](https://www.iberoamericadigital.net/BDPI/Search.do?matter=Argentina+Historia+Guerra+de+independencia%2C+1810-1817); [Museo Mitre catalogue](https://museomitre.cultura.gob.ar/media/uploads/site-10/multimedia/archivo_jose_de_san_martin.pdf)

- **9 August 1819:** *Memoria del general Pueyrredón después de haberse retirado del mando supremo de las Provincias Unidas en Sud América*. A retrospective defence and continuation of the 1817 exposition, written after his resignation. It is usually classified as autobiography or memoir, but its immediate purpose is political accounting and vindication. [Biblioteca de Mayo, vol. III](https://digitales.bcn.gob.ar/files/textos/Biblioteca-de-mayo---Tomo-3.pdf); [Museo Histórico Nacional](https://museohistoriconacional.cultura.gob.ar/media/uploads/site-6/multimedia/boletin_septiembre_2019.pdf)

- **1820:** *Manifiesto de Pueyrredón*. A political defence associated with the collapse of the central government and his removal from public power. The title supplied in the documentary edition is generic, and the piece survives as an archival intervention rather than a separately established literary work. [Museo Mitre, *Documentos del archivo de Pueyrredón*, IV, p. 184, cited here](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/366149447_Los_archivos_rioplatenses_y_su_organizacion_imagen_y_realidad_Oviedo_IFESXVIII_Ediciones_Trea_ACESXVIII_8_2021)

- **1826:** *Respuesta al impreso del “Compatriota adoptivo” publicado en la Gaceta Mercantil*. An unpublished rebuttal reconstructing his role in the revolutionary process from the British invasions onward. Contemporary scholarship notes that it did not circulate in print; the title is therefore editorial or archival rather than an authorially fixed book title. [Documentary note in *El redactor del Congreso Nacional*](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/19/El_redactor_del_Congreso_Nacional%2C_1816_%28microform%29_%28IA_3649770_1816%29.pdf); [Museo Mitre archival inventory](https://patrimonioargentino.cultura.gob.ar/index.php/Collections/childList/collection_id/667)

- **24 March 1829:** *Refutación a una atroz calumnia hecha con demasiada ligereza a un general de la República Argentina por Mr. Alejandro H. Everett*. A printed polemical vindication answering Everett’s allegation that Pueyrredón had been bought by Spanish agents. The pamphlet incorporates or appends earlier documentary material, so the printed object is a composite defence rather than a wholly new continuous narrative. [1829 transcription and title page](https://es.wikisource.org/wiki/Refutaci%C3%B3n_a_una_atroz_calumnia); [signed and dated principal statement](https://es.wikisource.org/wiki/Refutaci%C3%B3n_a_una_atroz_calumnia/Pueyrred%C3%B3n)

### Manifestos, proclamations, and governmental protocols

- **1808–1809:** *Representaciones and pedimentos to the Junta of Montevideo and the Buenos Aires Cabildo*. A cluster of legal-political instruments contesting his detention, seeking access to the proceedings, and defending his conduct. They are protocols of petition and self-defence rather than autonomous titled works. [Museo Mitre, *Documentos del archivo de Pueyrredón*, contents](https://books.google.com/books?id=SkFCAQAAMAAJ); [Museo Mitre archival inventory](https://patrimonioargentino.cultura.gob.ar/index.php/Collections/childList/collection_id/667)

- **1816:** *Plan de operaciones en la Cruz Alta, Córdoba*. A strategic project made while travelling to assume the supreme directorship. Its status is that of an operational plan: the intention and proposed sequence of action are the obra, whether or not every element was executed. [Museo Mitre archival inventory](https://patrimonioargentino.cultura.gob.ar/index.php/Collections/childList/collection_id/667)

- **1816:** *Proclama del director Pueyrredón*. A public manifesto issued in his capacity as Director Supremo. Because a proclamation acts—summoning, authorizing, or directing a political public—“manifesto” is more exact here than “essay”; official authorship may nevertheless include unrecorded secretarial drafting. [Museo Mitre, *Documentos del archivo de San Martín: Impresos (1810–1827)*](https://books.google.com/books?id=fJQ5AQAAMAAJ)

- **1816–1819:** *Bandos, decrees, regulations, and official instructions of the Supreme Government*. A corpus of performative state texts, including the **1818 bando establishing the Caja Nacional de Fondos de Sud América**. They resist attribution as personal works because they were institutional products promulgated under Pueyrredón’s authority, although archival catalogues index some under his name. [Museo Mitre inventory entry for the 12 November 1818 bando](https://museomitre.cultura.gob.ar/media/uploads/site-10/multimedia/documentos_del_archivo_de_pueyrredon_mm.pdf)

- **date not established:** *Proyecto sobre guardia cívica, padrón de barrios y escuelas*. An unsigned or unattributed four-folio project preserved among Pueyrredón’s papers. Its archival location does not prove that he composed it, so it belongs in the disputed penumbra of his oeuvre rather than among securely authored works. [Museo Mitre inventory entry](https://museomitre.cultura.gob.ar/media/uploads/site-10/multimedia/documentos_del_archivo_de_pueyrredon_mm.pdf)

### Fragments and uncertain attributions

- **date not established:** *Artículo incompleto*. An incomplete article catalogued under Pueyrredón’s name. Its lost beginning or ending, absence of a stable title, and fragmentary survival prevent a firmer classification. [Museo Mitre archival inventory](https://patrimonioargentino.cultura.gob.ar/index.php/Collections/childList/collection_id/667)

- **date not established:** *Papeles sobre las invasiones inglesas y la Reconquista de Buenos Aires*. A composite archival grouping rather than a work made as a unit; it combines Pueyrredón’s writings with evidentiary papers created by other people and institutions. Calling the whole dossier his “work” would confuse possession, compilation, and authorship. [Museo Mitre archival inventory](https://patrimonioargentino.cultura.gob.ar/index.php/Collections/childList/collection_id/667)

- **circa 1810, attribution unestablished:** *Poesía al 25 de Mayo de 1810*. The poem is preserved in the Pueyrredón archive, but the public inventory does not name Pueyrredón as its author. Archival proximity alone is insufficient attribution; it should therefore not be silently admitted to his poetic oeuvre. [Museo Mitre archival inventory](https://patrimonioargentino.cultura.gob.ar/index.php/Collections/childList/collection_id/667)
