## Works by Jordan Maxwell

### Books and compilations

- **1993? / 2003:** *That Old-Time Religion: The Story of Religious Foundations*. A collaborative book credited to Maxwell, Paul Tice, and Alan Snow, with material by Gerald Massey; the official archive dates it 1993, while a specialist bibliography records editions only from 2003 and 2011, so its first-publication date and precise division of authorship remain uncertain. [Official archive](https://jordanmaxwell.org/) [Bibliography of Occult and Fantastic Beliefs](https://d-nb.info/1190103281/34)

- **1990s:** *The Priesthood of the Illes*. A compilation-project assembled by Maxwell from three earlier books—*Thirty Thousand Gods Before Jehovah*, *The Axe Was God*, and *The Rod of Mercury*—by Henry Binkley Stein; it therefore resists classification as an authored Maxwell book and is more accurately his editorial Gesamtkunstwerk of appropriated historical texts. Maxwell’s store later sold it as “3 ebooks in 1,” without acknowledging Stein in the listing. [Maxwell store](https://www.jordanmaxwell.com/store.html) [Colin Rivas’s provenance statement](https://www.ebooksacces.com/?id=148655254)

- **2000:** *Matrix of Power: How the World Has Been Controlled by Powerful People Without Your Knowledge* (also catalogued as *Matrix of Power: Secrets of World Control*). An illustrated book written by Maxwell; the publisher record identifies Book Tree, 2000, while the shorter subtitle used by the official archive appears to describe the same obra rather than a separate work. [Google Books publisher record](https://books.google.com/books/about/Matrix_of_Power.html?id=GCIqdYN0GL0C) [Official archive](https://jordanmaxwell.org/)

- **2007:** *Symbols, Sex, and the Stars*. Not a Maxwell-authored book: it is Ernest Busenbark’s pre-existing work, to which Maxwell contributed a short preface; the official archive’s description of a 2007 “annotated edition” conflicts with bibliographic evidence identifying Busenbark as author and Maxwell as contributor. [Google Books](https://books.google.com/books/about/Symbols_Sex_and_the_Stars.html?id=XvUONxT9Oh8C) [Official archive](https://jordanmaxwell.org/)

- **2006:** *The Light Behind Masonry*. The official archive lists this as a Maxwell published work, but bibliographic evidence attributes a book of this title to Bill Schnoebelen, first published in 1998. Without an identifiable Maxwell text or edition, attribution to Maxwell is disputed and this entry may represent catalogue contamination rather than an obra he made. [Official archive](https://jordanmaxwell.org/) [Google Books bibliographic record](https://books.google.com/books/about/The_Light_Behind_Masonry.html?id=nFSuAAAACAAJ)

- **1993:** OLELO-TV programs. - **1992–2022:** *The World Is a Business*. A podcast series preserved by the authorized archive as a complete unit; the archive does not provide a reliable original date range or episode-level catalogue, so it is best treated as a serial project rather than falsely enumerated. [Official archive statement](https://jordanmaxwell.org/about.html)

### Research archive as obra

- **Accumulated over approximately six decades:** *Jordan Maxwell’s Files*. A raw research archive of more than 5,000 items arranged under 161 topics and occupying over 3.4 GB. Because it incorporates documents Maxwell collected rather than authored, it is an archival project and personal knowledge-environment—not a book, and not wholly a creation of his own. [Maxwell store catalogue](https://www.jordanmaxwell.com/store.html)

- **1959–2022:** Maxwell’s lectures, interviews, seminars, broadcasts, and written research as a body of work. The authorized archive explicitly treats this dispersed material as a unified research framework, but also admits that it was scattered across transient websites and recordings; the totality is therefore an open, partly lost obra whose individual manifestations cannot presently be exhaustively enumerated. [About the official archive](https://jordanmaxwell.org/about.html) [Official video archive](https://www.jordanmaxwellvideos.com/)
