## Works by Paul Casimir Marcinkus

### Academic text

- **By 1953:** An untitled canon-law doctoral dissertation. This is a scholarly text completed at the Pontifical Gregorian University; the consulted standard biographical reference records the doctorate but supplies neither the dissertation’s title nor a publication history, so a more specific title or date cannot responsibly be assigned. [Munzinger Biographie](https://www.munzinger.de/register/portrait/biographien/Paul%2BC%2BMarcinkus/00/16507)

### Translations

- **1959–1968:** English translations and interpretations for Popes John XXIII and Paul VI. This is a body of linguistic service rather than a securely catalogued authored oeuvre: the surviving biographical accounts do not identify individual texts or distinguish Marcinkus’s written translations from oral interpreting. [Munzinger Biographie](https://www.munzinger.de/register/portrait/biographien/Paul%2BC%2BMarcinkus/00/16507), [America](https://www.americamagazine.org/catholic-book-club/2026/02/10/popes-gorilla-archbishop-paul-marcinkus/)

### Travel projects

- **1964–1970:** The logistical design of Paul VI’s international journeys. This was a collaborative project—an administrative and ceremonial apparatus rather than a text—and Marcinkus is variously described as the pope’s travel marshal, “advance man,” or organizer; the evidence specifically credits him with preparing the 1964 Holy Land pilgrimage but does not permit every later itinerary to be treated as his sole creation. [Munzinger Biographie](https://www.munzinger.de/register/portrait/biographien/Paul%2BC%2BMarcinkus/00/16507), [Vatican itinerary of Paul VI’s travels](https://www.vatican.va/content/paul-vi/en/travels.index.2.html), [America](https://www.americamagazine.org/catholic-book-club/2026/02/10/popes-gorilla-archbishop-paul-marcinkus/)

- **Date not established:** *Cartintour*. Marcinkus is credited with founding this Vatican travel agency; it is an institutional creation, not a book or a securely bounded personal work, and the consulted biographical source gives no foundation date. [Munzinger Biographie](https://www.munzinger.de/register/portrait/biographien/Paul%2BC%2BMarcinkus/00/16507)

### Directed building project

- **Completed 1968:** *Cardinal Stritch Institute*. This Roman residence for American clergy was promoted by Cardinal John Cody and the American hierarchy, while Paul VI expressly credited Marcinkus with directing its execution; it is therefore a collaborative architectural project, not a building designed by Marcinkus. [Paul VI, address at the institute’s blessing](https://www.vatican.va/content/paul-vi/en/speeches/1968/june/documents/hf_p-vi_spe_19680629_cardinale-stritch.html)

### Financial instruments

- **September 1981:** IOR “letters of patronage” or “comfort letters” concerning Banco Ambrosiano’s offshore companies. These institutional instruments acted upon credit relations rather than merely describing them. Their authorship resists a simple attribution: contemporary reporting says the IOR supplied them and Marcinkus accepted responsibility, but also reports that he did not personally sign them; other accounts loosely say that he “issued” or “provided” them. [TIME, “The Great Vatican Bank Mystery”](https://content.time.com/time/subscriber/article/0%2C33009%2C951806-6%2C00.html), [INSEAD case study](https://flora.insead.edu/fichiersti_wp/Inseadwp1990/90-40.pdf), [UPI archive](https://www.upi.com/amp/Archives/1982/10/20/American-Archbishop-Paul-C-Marcinkus-president-of-the-Vatican/8579403934400/)

### Ceremonial address

- **20 December 1983:** An untitled address to the directors and staff of the Vatican Museums. The text itself was not located, but John Paul II’s reply explicitly acknowledges the words Marcinkus had just delivered on behalf of those assembled; its survival and completeness therefore remain uncertain. [John Paul II, reply to the Vatican Museums’ personnel](https://www.vatican.va/content/john-paul-ii/it/speeches/1983/december/documents/hf_jp-ii_spe_19831220_musei-vaticani.html)
