## Works by Robert Fludd

### Manifestos and Rosicrucian apologias

- **1616:** *Apologia compendiaria Fraternitatem de Rosea Cruce suspicionis et infamiae maculis aspersam veritatis quasi fluctibus abluens et abstergens*. Fludd’s first publication: a short polemical apology that acts publicly to clear the Rosicrucian fraternity of charges of heresy, magic, and sedition. [[Debus](https://www.encyclopedia.com/science/dictionaries-thesauruses-pictures-and-press-releases/fludd-robert)]

- **1617:** *Tractatus apologeticus integritatem Societatis de Rosea Cruce defendens*. A greatly expanded recasting of the 1616 *Apologia*—196 pages rather than 23—and therefore both a new manifestation of that defense and a manifesto-like intervention in the Rosicrucian controversy. [[Debus](https://www.encyclopedia.com/science/dictionaries-thesauruses-pictures-and-press-releases/fludd-robert)]

- **1617:** *Tractatus theologo-philosophicus, in libros tres distributus*. A theological-philosophical treatise presenting creation, life, death, resurrection, and Scripture through Fludd’s Christian-Hermetic natural philosophy; its appearance and date are recorded in the standard scholarly bibliography summarized by Debus. [[Debus](https://www.encyclopedia.com/science/dictionaries-thesauruses-pictures-and-press-releases/fludd-robert)]

- **1629:** *Summum bonum, quod est verum magiae, cabalae, alchymiae verae, Fratrum Roseae Crucis verorum subjectum*. A Rosicrucian and Hermetic manifesto published under the pseudonym “Joachim Frizius”; Fludd’s authorship is conventionally accepted, but the pseudonymous presentation is part of the work’s status and should not be suppressed. [[Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/la-rivoluzione-scientifica-i-domini-della-conoscenza-l-aristotelismo-e-le-sue-alternative_%28Storia-della-Scienza%29/); [ILAB description](https://ilab.org/assets/catalogues/catalogs_files_938_catalogue2012.pdf)]

### Encyclopedic Gesamtkunstwerk

- **composed substantially before 1615; published 1617–1626, unfinished:** *Utriusque cosmi maioris scilicet et minoris metaphysica, physica atque technica historia*. Fludd’s intended history of the macrocosm and microcosm is best treated as a Gesamtkunstwerk and unfinished encyclopedic project rather than a single ordinary book: theology, cosmology, music, images, diagrams, instruments, medicine, mnemonic architecture, astrology, geomancy, painting, warfare, and mechanical arts are made mutually explanatory. Its printed realization remained incomplete. [[Hauge](https://www.dym.dk/dym_pdf_files/volume_27/volume_27_009_030.pdf); [Debus](https://www.encyclopedia.com/science/dictionaries-thesauruses-pictures-and-press-releases/fludd-robert)]

- **1617:** *De metaphysico macrocosmi et creaturarum illius ortu*. The first tract of the macrocosmic volume of *Utriusque cosmi historia*, issued in seven books; it is a constituent manifestation of the larger project, not wholly independent of it. [[Hauge](https://www.dym.dk/dym_pdf_files/volume_27/volume_27_009_030.pdf)]

- **1618:** *De naturae simia seu technica macrocosmi historia*. The second macrocosmic tract, divided into eleven arts—including arithmetic, music, geometry, optics, painting, military art, motion, time, cosmography, astrology, and geomancy—through which human making becomes the “ape of nature.” [[Hauge](https://www.dym.dk/dym_pdf_files/volume_27/volume_27_009_030.pdf)]

- **1618:** *De templo musicae*. A seven-book treatise embedded in *De naturae simia* and organized around the image of a “Temple of Music”; it is simultaneously music theory, compositional protocol, organology, mnemonic architecture, and diagrammatic creation, so it resists reduction to a detachable book. [[Hauge](https://www.dym.dk/dym_pdf_files/volume_27/volume_27_009_030.pdf)]

- **1619:** *De supernaturali, naturali, praeternaturali et contranaturali microcosmi historia*. The opening manifestation of the microcosmic volume, treating the human being across supernatural, natural, preternatural, and contra-natural registers. It belongs to the unfinished *Utriusque cosmi* project. [[Hauge](https://www.dym.dk/dym_pdf_files/volume_27/volume_27_009_030.pdf); [Christie’s scholarly catalogue description](https://www.christies.com/en/lot/lot-5945289)]

- **1620:** *De technica microcosmi historia*. A seven-portion account of the human technical microcosm and another separately issued component of the larger encyclopedic project. Its bibliographical separateness should not be mistaken for conceptual independence. [[Hauge](https://www.dym.dk/dym_pdf_files/volume_27/volume_27_009_030.pdf); [PRDL](https://www.prdl.org/author_view.php?a_id=1072)]

- **1621:** *De praeternaturali utriusque mundi historia*. A separately printed continuation concerning preternatural phenomena in both worlds; it forms the second part of the microcosmic volume and is among the scarcer components of the unfinished whole. [[Christie’s scholarly catalogue description](https://www.christies.com/en/lot/lot-5945289)]

- **1623:** *Anatomiae amphitheatrum effigie triplici, more et conditione varia designatum*. A visual and textual “anatomical amphitheatre” presenting corporeal, spiritual, and cosmic anatomy; it is both an anatomical treatise and a diagrammatic theatre within Fludd’s larger microcosmic project. [[Debus](https://www.encyclopedia.com/science/dictionaries-thesauruses-pictures-and-press-releases/fludd-robert); [Christie’s scholarly catalogue description](https://www.christies.com/en/lot/lot-5945289)]

- **1626:** *Philosophia sacra et vere Christiana, seu Meteorologia cosmica*. A sacred cosmology or “cosmic meteorology,” issued as a further portion of the microcosmic history. Although bibliographically encountered as a book, it remains a continuation of the incompletely realized *Utriusque cosmi* project. [[Hauge](https://www.dym.dk/dym_pdf_files/volume_27/volume_27_009_030.pdf); [Wellcome Collection](https://wellcomecollection.org/search/works?contributors.agent.label=%22Robert+Fludd%22)]

### Controversial performances

- **1621:** *Veritatis proscenium*. A reply to Johannes Kepler’s criticism of Fludd’s cosmic harmonies; its theatrical title makes the treatise a staged “proscenium of truth,” a polemical performance intended to bring truth before the public. [[Debus](https://www.encyclopedia.com/science/dictionaries-thesauruses-pictures-and-press-releases/fludd-robert)]

- **1622:** *Monochordum mundi symphoniacum*. Fludd’s reply to Kepler’s answer, defending his cosmic monochord and analogical conception of harmony. It is both a polemic and a diagrammatic model of the world. [[Debus](https://www.encyclopedia.com/science/dictionaries-thesauruses-pictures-and-press-releases/fludd-robert)]

- **1629:** *Sophiae cum moria certamen*. A polemical “contest of Wisdom with Folly” answering Marin Mersenne’s attacks on Fludd’s philosophy; although commonly bound with *Medicina catholica*, it has its own title, pagination, and argumentative action. [[Debus](https://www.encyclopedia.com/science/dictionaries-thesauruses-pictures-and-press-releases/fludd-robert); [ILAB description](https://ilab.org/assets/catalogues/catalogs_files_938_catalogue2012.pdf)]

- **1631:** *Doctor Fludds Answer unto M. Foster; or, The Squeesing of Parson Fosters Sponge*. An English-language defense of the weapon-salve against William Foster: a deliberately performative reply that claims to “squeeze” the opponent’s refutation rather than merely describe the dispute. [[Debus](https://www.encyclopedia.com/science/dictionaries-thesauruses-pictures-and-press-releases/fludd-robert)]

- **1633:** *Clavis philosophiae et alchymiae Fluddanae*. A lengthy answer to Pierre Gassendi’s critique, presented as the “key” to Fluddian philosophy and alchemy; it functions simultaneously as polemic, clarification, and retrospective map of Fludd’s system. [[Debus](https://www.encyclopedia.com/science/dictionaries-thesauruses-pictures-and-press-releases/fludd-robert)]

### Medical project and diagnostic protocols

- **1629–1631, unfinished:** *Medicina catholica, seu mysticum artis medicandi sacrarium*. An intended two-volume “universal medicine” joining metaphysics, cosmology, diagnosis, and therapy; only the first volume was realized, so the project—not an imagined completed book—is the work. [[Google Books/Bavarian State Library digitization](https://books.google.com/books?id=XVBRAAAAcAAJ); [Debus](https://www.encyclopedia.com/science/dictionaries-thesauruses-pictures-and-press-releases/fludd-robert)]

- **1629:** *Sanitatis mysterium, seu tomi primi tractatus primus*. The first tract of *Medicina catholica*, on the cosmic and elemental mystery of preserving health. It is a medical-theological treatise within the larger unfinished project. [[Huffman bibliography excerpt](https://www.scribd.com/document/451646868/Western-esoteric-masters-series-Fludd-Robert-Fludd-Robert-Huffman-William-Harold-Robert-Fludd-2001-North-Atlantic-Books-pdf); [Google Books](https://books.google.com/books?id=XVBRAAAAcAAJ)]

- **1631:** *Integrum morborum mysterium, sive Medicinae catholicae tomi primi tractatus secundus*. The second tract of *Medicina catholica*, issued as a four-part folio complex on disease and its signs rather than as a single undifferentiated text. [[Wellcome Collection](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/m2ykf2sk)]

- **1631:** *Integrum morborum, seu meteororum insalubrium mysterium*. The first part of the 1631 medical complex, constructing diseases as unhealthy “meteors” invading the fortress of health. It is a constituent treatise, not merely an alternative title for the entire volume. [[Wellcome Collection](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/m2ykf2sk)]

- **1631:** *Catholicon medicorum catoptron*. A diagnostic “mirror” for physicians and patients, also identified as *Prognosticon supercoeleste*; its double naming is historical and should not be silently resolved into a single preferred title. [[Wellcome Collection](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/m2ykf2sk)]

- **completed by 1629; issued 1631:** *Pulsus seu nova et arcana pulsuum historia*. A protocol-like treatise for interpreting the pulse, embedded in the 1631 medical complex; it contains Fludd’s first printed defense of William Harvey’s circulation theory. [[Debus](https://www.encyclopedia.com/science/dictionaries-thesauruses-pictures-and-press-releases/fludd-robert); [Wellcome Collection](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/m2ykf2sk)]

- **1631:** *Ouromantia, hoc est, divinatio per urinam* and *Ouromantia physiologica*. Paired diagnostic protocols for reading urine, supplied with separate titles inside the 1631 complex. Their status lies between component books and sections, and surviving bibliographical descriptions do not treat them uniformly as independent works. [[Wellcome Collection](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/m2ykf2sk)]

### Manuscript works and unrealized publications

- **c. 1617:** *Declaratio brevis*. A manuscript defense addressed to James I, written to justify Fludd’s Rosicrucian position and publications. It never became a contemporary printed book, but its manuscript existence makes it a completed rhetorical act rather than merely an abandoned intention. [[Debus](https://www.encyclopedia.com/science/dictionaries-thesauruses-pictures-and-press-releases/fludd-robert); [Museum of the History of Science manuscript list](https://hsm.ox.ac.uk/sites/default/files/mhs/documents/media/summary_list_of_manuscripts_in_the_museum_of_the_history_of_science.pdf)]

- **c. 1618–1620:** *A Philosophicall Key, or Ocular Demonstration, opening and decyphering a great deale of the hidden mysteryes of Nature*. An illustrated English alchemical manuscript dedicated to James I, combining experiment, intellectual speculation, apology, copied alchemical materials, and a poem. Partly authorial and partly scribal, it was apparently prepared toward publication but survives in a text that may be incomplete; portions omitted from the manuscript reappeared later in Latin. [[Trinity College Cambridge manuscript catalogue](https://mss-cat.trin.cam.ac.uk/Manuscript/O.2.46); [Revue d’histoire des sciences](https://www.persee.fr/doc/rhs_0151-4105_1981_num_34_1_1745?pageId=T1_72)]

- **c. 1623:** *Truth’s Golden Harrow*. An English alchemical manuscript answering Patrick Scot’s *The Tillage of Light*. It resists the status of a contemporary “work” in print because it remained unpublished in Fludd’s lifetime, but it survives as a distinct polemical-alchemical composition in the Bodleian manuscript tradition. [[Debus](https://www.encyclopedia.com/science/dictionaries-thesauruses-pictures-and-press-releases/fludd-robert); [Wellcome Collection record of Josten’s scholarly edition](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/kyjytnu5)]

### Posthumous obra

- **written before 1637; published 1638:** *Philosophia Moysaica, in qua sapientia et scientia creationis explicantur*. Fludd’s final synthetic exposition of Mosaic natural philosophy, cosmology, magnetism, sympathetic medicine, and the weapon-salve. Publication occurred after his death, so 1638 dates the manifestation, not necessarily the act of composition. [[Debus](https://www.encyclopedia.com/science/dictionaries-thesauruses-pictures-and-press-releases/fludd-robert); [Christie’s scholarly catalogue description](https://www.christies.com/en/lot/lot-5945289)]
