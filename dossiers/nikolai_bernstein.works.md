## Works by Nikolai Aleksandrovich Bernstein

### Monographs, manuals, and collaborative books

- **1926:** *Общая биомеханика: Основы учения о движениях человека* (*General Biomechanics: Foundations of the Study of Human Movement*). A 416-page scientific monograph establishing biomechanics as a study of coordinated human action.
- **1926:** *Биомеханика для инструкторов* (*Biomechanics for Instructors*). A 184-page instructional manual translating biomechanical research into professional practice.
- **1926:** *Труд, утомление, отдых с точки зрения учения об энергии* (*Work, Fatigue, and Rest from the Standpoint of Energetics*). A short monograph on labor physiology.
- **1927:** *Практикум по экспериментальной психологии* (*Practical Workbook in Experimental Psychology*). A collaborative laboratory manual made with V. A. Artemov, L. S. Vygotsky, N. F. Dobrynin, and A. R. Luria under K. N. Kornilov’s editorship; Bernstein’s authorship is therefore partial, not sole.
- **1934:** *Техника изучения движений: Практическое руководство по циклограмметрии* (*The Technique of Studying Movement: A Practical Guide to Cyclogrammetry*). A 560-page methodological handbook coauthored with T. S. Popova and Z. V. Mogilyanskaya.
- **1935:** *Исследования по биодинамике локомоций. Кн. 1: Биодинамика ходьбы нормального взрослого мужчины* (*Studies in the Biodynamics of Locomotion, Book 1: The Biodynamics of Walking in a Normal Adult Man*). An edited collective research volume; Bernstein directed it and contributed substantial chapters, but the whole is not solely authored by him.
- **1940:** *Исследования по биодинамике ходьбы, бега, прыжка* (*Studies in the Biodynamics of Walking, Running, and Jumping*). An edited laboratory volume containing Bernstein’s studies and those of collaborators.
- **1946:** *Физиология человека* (*Human Physiology*). A 400-page textbook coauthored with A. N. Krestovnikov and M. E. Marshak; Bernstein’s precise division of responsibility is not identified in the standard bibliography.
- **1947:** *О построении движений* (*On the Construction of Movements*; Bernstein also rendered it *On the Structure of Movements*). His principal monograph on hierarchical motor control, begun in 1939, interrupted by the war, in press by 1945, and published in 1947.
- **1966:** *Очерки по физиологии движений и физиологии активности* (*Essays on the Physiology of Movements and the Physiology of Activity*). A late synthesis assembled as twelve essays and published in the year of his death.
- **Written in the late 1940s; suppressed before publication; first published 1990–1991:** *О ловкости и её развитии* (*On Dexterity and Its Development*). A book-length project halted during the anti-cosmopolitan campaign; its delayed publication makes the manuscript, rather than its later editions, the original obra.
- **1942; publication status uncertain:** *Пятизначные таблицы десятичных логарифмов для чисел от 1 до \(10^{10}\)* (*Five-place Tables of Decimal Logarithms for Numbers from 1 to \(10^{10}\)*). A computational reference work prepared during evacuation; the biographical source calls it his sole bibliographically indexed work of 1942, but the 1990 collected-works bibliography does not list it, so its circulation and survival require archival verification.

### Scientific and methodological articles

- **1922:** *Логарифмические свойства клавиатуры музыкальных инструментов*. A short mathematical-acoustical study of musical keyboards.

- **1924:** *Нормализация движений в ЦИТ*. Conference theses for the First International Scientific Management Congress in Prague.

- **1924:** *Нормализация движений. 1. Задачи и принципы нормализации трудовых движений*. The first article in a protocol-like series for rationalizing labor movements.

- **1924:** *Нормализация труда. 3. Нормализация рубки зубилом*. The third installment, turning biomechanical analysis into instructions for chisel work.
- **1924:** *Биомеханическая нормаль удара при одноручных ударно-режущих операциях*. A substantial technical study defining a biomechanical norm for one-handed striking and cutting.
- **1924:** *Нервная система и трудовые движения*. A popular-scientific article connecting nervous-system physiology with work movements.

- **1925:** *Происхождение видов в технике*. A popular essay treating the evolution of technical forms.

- **1927:** *Рецепторный момент реакции*. Bernstein’s chapter in the collaborative *Practical Workbook in Experimental Psychology*.
- **1927:** *Обработка результатов массовых психологических измерений*. A methodological chapter on processing large sets of psychological measurements.

- **1927:** *Новое в методике изучения рабочих движений*. A methodological article on recording occupational movements.
- **1927:** *Analyse aperiodischer trigonometrischer Reihen*. A mathematical article on aperiodic trigonometric series.
- **1928:** *Die kymozyclographische Methode der Bewegungsuntersuchung*. A handbook chapter presenting the kymocyclographic method.
- **1928:** *К методике измерения тонуса поперечнополосатой мускулатуры у человека*. A congress abstract coauthored with N. K. Vereshchagin.

### Instruments, recording systems, tables, and design projects

- **1920s:** *Kymocyclograph*. A movement-recording apparatus that combined light traces with a moving photographic surface; the apparatus and its 1927 description are related but not identical works.
- **1920s–1930s:** *Cyclogrammetric method*. A system of photographing luminous markers, reconstructing trajectories, and calculating velocity and acceleration; it is a made experimental protocol distributed across instruments, tables, nomograms, and publications rather than a single text.
- **By 1930:** *Mirror cyclography*. A method and apparatus for recording multiple spatial views of a moving body in one exposure.
- **1928; revised 1929:** *Проект переустройства рабочего места вагоновожатого московского трамвая* (*Project for Reorganizing the Moscow Tram Driver’s Workplace*). An ergonomic design project, not merely a descriptive article; a second version appeared in 1929, and the original version was republished posthumously in 1983.
- **1933:** *Zeitokular*. A slow-projection viewing apparatus described as a counterpart to slow-motion cinematography.
- **1934:** *Coordinate, velocity, acceleration, and center-of-gravity tables*. Computational instruments embedded in *The Technique of Studying Movement*.
- **1948:** *Предложения по конструкции рабочего протеза* (*Proposals for the Design of a Working Prosthesis*). A prosthetic design project developed with O. A. Salzgeber from analysis of spatial arm movements; the bibliography documents proposals, not necessarily a fabricated production model.

### Experimental image corpora and atlases

- **1920s–1930s:** *Cyclograms of hammering, filing, piano touch, walking, running, jumping, starts, and other movements*. These photographic traces were research objects as well as evidence: made configurations of light recording motion through time. No complete catalogue raisonné of the individual plates was found, so treating every surviving cyclogram as a separately titled work would invent boundaries the sources do not supply.
- **By 1929; unpublished:** *Atlas des Ganges und Laufes des Menschen* (*Atlas of Human Walking and Running*). An unpublished atlas associated with Bernstein’s locomotion research and reported as surviving in Dortmund; its precise authorship, completeness, arrangement, and date remain insufficiently established, so it resists classification as a finished book.

### Posthumous collections, translations, and editorial constructions

- **1967:** *The Co-ordination and Regulation of Movements*. A posthumous English selection and translation of Bernstein’s writings, not a new monograph composed by him in that form.
- **1969:** *Methods for Developing Physiology as Related to the Problems of Cybernetics*. An English translation or adaptation of a Bernstein text published posthumously in *A Handbook of Contemporary Soviet Psychology*.
- **1972:** *Probleme der Modellierung in der Biologie der Aktivität*. A German translation of the modeling essay, not a distinct original work.
- **1975; expanded edition 1988:** *Bewegungsphysiologie*. A German posthumous selection edited by L. Pickenhain and G. Schnabel; its boundaries belong partly to its editors.
- **1984:** *Human Motor Action: Bernstein Reassessed*. An edited scholarly volume about and around Bernstein, not an obra by Bernstein, despite its inclusion in the 1990 bibliography of his works.
- **1990:** *Физиология движений и активность* (*Physiology of Movements and Activity*). A posthumous collected-works volume prepared by I. M. Feigenberg, combining *On the Construction of Movements* and *Essays on the Physiology of Movements and the Physiology of Activity*; it is an editorial manifestation, not a third lifetime monograph.
- **1990–1991:** *О ловкости и её развитии*. The first Russian publication of the suppressed dexterity manuscript; sources differ between 1990, when the collected bibliography marked it “in press,” and 1991, commonly given for the completed publication.
- **1996:** *Dexterity and Its Development*. An English translation of *On Dexterity and Its Development* published with extensive modern commentaries; only the translated Bernstein text is his work, while the encompassing volume is collaborative and editorial.
