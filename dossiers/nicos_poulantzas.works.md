## Works by Nicos Poulantzas

### Monographic obras

- **1962:** *La renaissance du droit naturel en Allemagne après la Seconde Guerre mondiale*. A mimeographed doctoral dissertation in legal philosophy; the later book is a substantial reworking rather than simply another edition. [The principal posthumous bibliography records the dissertation separately](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

- **1965:** *Nature des choses et droit: essai sur la dialectique du fait et de la valeur*. A revised book-length obra developed from Poulantzas’s dissertation, joining philosophy and sociology of law. [The bibliographical record identifies it as his published law thesis](https://www.persee.fr/authority/229092).

- **1968:** *Pouvoir politique et classes sociales de l’État capitaliste*. A theoretical monograph on the capitalist state; translations and later two-volume printings are editions of this obra, not additional works. [The principal bibliography gives the original Maspero publication](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

- **1970:** *Fascisme et dictature: la IIIe Internationale face au fascisme*. A theoretical-historical monograph about fascism, dictatorship, and communist strategy; its 1974 Seuil issue and foreign translations are later manifestations of the same obra. [The original French publication is recorded in the principal bibliography](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

- **1974:** *Les classes sociales dans le capitalisme aujourd’hui*. A monograph on class formation, imperialism, and the “new petty bourgeoisie” in contemporary capitalism. [The French bibliographical record dates the Seuil edition to 1974](https://www.persee.fr/authority/229092).

- **1975:** *La Crise des dictatures: Portugal, Grèce, Espagne*. A conjunctural political monograph written amid the collapse of the southern European dictatorships; some translations bear 1976 dates, but the French obra appeared in 1975. [Persée records the original Maspero publication](https://www.persee.fr/authority/229092).

- **1978:** *L’État, le pouvoir, le socialisme*. Poulantzas’s final completed monograph, combining state theory with a project for democratic socialism. [Persée records the original PUF publication](https://www.persee.fr/authority/229092).

### Essays and theoretical interventions

- **1961:** *Histoire et éthique*. An early Greek-language essay, apparently left unpublished during Poulantzas’s lifetime and first manifested in *Anti* on 28 August 1981; its retrospective dating therefore remains bibliographically awkward. [The posthumous principal bibliography supplies both dates](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

- **1963:** *Notes sur la phénoménologie et l’existentialisme juridiques*. A journal article in the *Archives de philosophie du droit* on phenomenological and existential approaches to law. [Recorded in the principal bibliography](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

- **1964:** *L’examen marxiste de l’État et du droit actuels et la question de l’alternative*. A long essay in *Les Temps modernes*, moving from legal philosophy toward a Marxist analysis of state institutions and political alternatives. [The principal bibliography gives issue 219–220 and pages 274–302](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

- **1964:** *Dialectique et nature des choses*. A legal-philosophical article in the *Annales de la Faculté de droit de Toulouse*. [Recorded separately in the principal bibliography](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

- **1965:** *La critique de la raison dialectique de J.-P. Sartre et le droit*. An essay bringing Sartre’s dialectical philosophy into an argument about law. The bibliography’s printed volume number is inconsistently OCR-transcribed as “70,” so that detail should not be silently trusted. [See the principal bibliography](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

- **1965:** *Préliminaires à l’étude de l’hégémonie dans l’État*. A two-part *Les Temps modernes* essay developing the relation between hegemony and state power. [The contemporary NLR introduction identifies its November–December 1965 publication](https://newleftreview.org/issues/i43/articles/nlr-editors-introduction-to-poulantzas).

- **1966:** *La dialectique hégélienne-marxiste et la logique juridique moderne*. An article in the *Archives de philosophie du droit* on dialectic and legal logic. The standard bibliography’s volume number is corrupted in some digital transcriptions; independent citation places it in volume 11, pages 149–157. [Michael Kelly’s bibliographical essay supplies the latter details](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/004724418601600402).

- **1966:** *Sur la conception marxiste de l’État*. A Greek-published intervention arising from the Second Week of Contemporary Thought in Athens. The surviving bibliography gives publication in *Marxismos kai episteme*, but its pagination collides with that assigned to the following item, so completeness and exact location remain uncertain. [See the principal bibliography](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

- **1966:** *Les classes et l’État*. A Greek-language conference intervention, also associated with the Second Week of Contemporary Thought. Its bibliographical relationship to *Sur la conception marxiste de l’État* is unclear and may reflect an erroneous or duplicated record; it therefore resists secure classification as a wholly independent work. [Both entries appear in the principal bibliography](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

- **1966:** *La théorie politique marxiste en Grande-Bretagne*. A *Les Temps modernes* essay on British Marxist historiography and political theory. Its 1967 English translation, *Marxist Political Theory in Great Britain*, is a manifestation of this essay rather than another obra. [The French and English appearances are recorded together](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

- **1966:** *Vers une théorie marxiste*. A substantial *Les Temps modernes* essay marking Poulantzas’s movement toward a distinct Marxist theory of politics. [The contemporary journal record dates it to May 1966](https://www.abebooks.fr/magazines-periodiques/TEMPS-MODERNES-240-JEAN-PAUL-SARTRE-conscience/19260512748/bd).

- **1967:** *À propos de la théorie marxiste du droit*. An article in the *Archives de philosophie du droit* reconsidering the theoretical status of law within Marxism. [Recorded in the principal bibliography](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

- **1967:** *Les formes politiques du coup d’État militaire*. A Greek-language political analysis published in *Poreia* on 2 June 1967 and reprinted in *Politis* in 1979; the reprint is not a separate obra. [The publication history is given in the principal bibliography](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

- **1968:** *Brèves remarques sur l’objet du Capital*. An essay contributed to Victor Fay’s collective volume *En partant du “Capital”*. [The principal bibliography gives pages 235–247](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

- **1969:** *The Problem of the Capitalist State*. An English-language intervention in *New Left Review* that initiated the celebrated Poulantzas–Miliband exchange. A 1970 French version and a 1971 German version are translations, not independent works. [The principal bibliography records the original NLR appearance and translations](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

- **1970:** *Sur la question des alliances*. A Greek-language political intervention published in *Agonas* under the pseudonym “Skyrianos”; the pseudonymous authorship is explicitly documented rather than inferred. [See the principal bibliography](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

- **1972:** *Les classes sociales*. A journal essay in *L’Homme et la Société*, later translated as *On Social Classes* in *New Left Review* 78 and into Italian; those translations are manifestations of the same argument. [The French-to-English publication history is recorded in the principal bibliography](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

- **1973:** *Note à propos du totalitarisme*. A short theoretical note published in *Tel Quel*. [Recorded in the principal bibliography](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

- **1973:** *L’internationalisation des rapports capitalistes et l’État-nation*. A long *Les Temps modernes* essay on multinational capital and the nation-state, subsequently translated as *Internationalization of Capitalist Relations and the Nation-State*. [The principal bibliography records the original French appearance](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

- **1973:** *Klassenkampf und Repression: Einige Hauptzüge des kapitalistischen Staates*. A German-language essay in *Kursbuch* on class struggle, repression, and the capitalist state. The standard bibliography treats it as an independent contribution, though its relationship to Poulantzas’s French writings is not explained there. [See the principal bibliography](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

- **1974:** *Lettre ouverte à “Critique de l’économie politique”*. An open letter—an intervention that acts within a theoretical dispute rather than merely describing one. [The principal bibliography records its publication in issue 13](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

- **1976:** *The Capitalist State: A Reply to Miliband and Laclau*. A polemical reply in *New Left Review* 95, revising and defending Poulantzas’s state theory against two distinct critiques. [Bob Jessop’s bibliography records the issue and pages](https://bobjessop.wordpress.com/2014/05/01/on-the-originality-legacy-and-actuality-of-nicos-poulantzas/).

- **1976:** *Les transformations actuelles de l’État, la crise politique et la crise de l’État*. Poulantzas’s framing essay for *La Crise de l’État*, also translated as *The Political Crisis and the Crisis of the State*. It is simultaneously an autonomous theoretical essay and part of the architecture of an edited collective obra. [The later scholarly bibliography identifies the original 1976 setting](https://agora.edu.es/servlet/articulo?codigo=9359719).

- **1976:** *Problèmes actuels de la recherche marxiste sur l’État*. A theoretical discussion published in *Dialectiques* 13. Its conversational form blurs the boundary between article and interview. [The publication details are preserved with the later 1979 interview](https://viewpointmag.com/2017/12/18/state-social-movements-party-interview-nicos-poulantzas-1979/).

- **1977:** *The State and the Transition to Socialism*. An intervention first published in French in *Critique communiste* 16 and subsequently translated into English. [Jessop records the June 1977 original and 1978 translation](https://bobjessop.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/a-1982-jessop-capitalist-state.pdf).

- **1977:** *La gauche et l’État*. A political newspaper essay in *Le Monde*, applying state theory directly to the strategy of the French left. [The newspaper archive dates it to 11 March 1977](https://www.lemonde.fr/archives-du-monde/11-03-1977/).

- **1978:** *Towards a Democratic Socialism*. A *New Left Review* intervention derived from the final chapter of *L’État, le pouvoir, le socialisme*. Because it reworks book material for a journal public, it is best treated as a distinct political manifestation rather than an entirely independent theoretical obra. [The NLR publication is available in its original issue form](https://newleftreview.org/issues/i109/articles/nicos-poulantzas-towards-a-democratic-socialism.pdf).

- **1979:** *Is There a Crisis in Marxism?* A lecture-like essay published in the *Journal of the Hellenic Diaspora*, volume 6, number 3. [Jessop’s bibliography records the journal appearance](https://bobjessop.wordpress.com/2014/05/01/on-the-originality-legacy-and-actuality-of-nicos-poulantzas/).

- **1979:** *La crise des partis*. A late intervention in *Le Monde diplomatique* on authoritarian statism, political representation, and the declining role of parties. [The original newspaper page dates it to September 1979](https://www.monde-diplomatique.fr/1979/09/POULANTZAS/35274).

### Reviews, replies, and paratexts

- **1962:** *K. Larenz, Methodenlehre der Rechtswissenschaft*. A substantial review in the *Archives de philosophie du droit*; its object is another author’s book, but the review itself is a made critical text. [Recorded in the principal bibliography](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

- **1963:** *C. Tsatsos, Études de philosophie du droit*. A review essay in the *Archives de philosophie du droit*. [Recorded in the principal bibliography](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

- **1963:** *O. Ballweg, Zu einer Lehre von der Natur der Sache*. A review in the *Archives de philosophie du droit*, belonging to Poulantzas’s early legal-philosophical corpus. [Recorded in the principal bibliography](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

- **1970:** *Ralph Miliband, L’État dans la société capitaliste*. A short review in the *Archives de philosophie du droit*, distinct from Poulantzas’s longer NLR engagement with Miliband. [The principal bibliography records pages 460–462](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

- **1970:** *Sur l’État dans la société capitaliste*. A French reformulation of Poulantzas’s intervention in the Miliband debate, published in *Politique aujourd’hui*. Its degree of independence from *The Problem of the Capitalist State* is uncertain, so it resists treatment as an unquestionably new obra. [The principal bibliography records it separately](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

- **1970:** *Préface à Les Kapetanios*. A brief preface to Dominique Eudes’s historical book; a paratext made for another author’s obra. [The principal bibliography records pages vii–viii](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

- **1972:** *Préface à L’Internationale communiste et l’école de classe*. A preface to Daniel Lindenberg’s book, positioning that study within debates over education, class, and communist strategy. [The principal bibliography gives pages 7–16](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

- **1975:** *Duverger (Maurice), Sociologie de la politique: éléments de science politique*. A critical review in the *Revue française de science politique*, followed there by François Bourricaud’s letter and Poulantzas’s response. The exchange is a composite manifestation: Poulantzas made both the review and his reply, but not Bourricaud’s intervening letter. [Persée identifies both records and the divided authorship](https://www.persee.fr/authority/229092).

### Edited and collective obras

- **1973:** *Las clases sociales en América Latina: problemas de conceptualización*. A collective seminar volume coordinated by Raúl Benítez Zenteno, to which Poulantzas contributed alongside Florestan Fernandes, Alain Touraine, and others. It is not simply a Poulantzas book: his obra here is participation in a collective theoretical object, and bibliographies differ over whether to list the volume under his name. [The institutional bibliography records the 1973 volume](https://poulantzas.gr/en/nicos-poulantzas-biography-and-intellectual-legacy/).

- **1976:** *La Crise de l’État*. A collective book conceived and edited by Poulantzas, bringing multiple researchers into a deliberately plural inquiry. The editorial construction is itself his project, while the individual chapters remain their authors’ works. [The bibliographical record identifies Poulantzas as director of the volume](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicos_Poulantzas).

- **1976–1979:** *Politiques*. A book series founded and directed by Poulantzas at Presses universitaires de France. It is not one text and resists being called a single work: it is an editorial project whose made form is a sequence of books by different authors. [The series identifies itself as “collection fondée par Nicos Poulantzas”](https://fr.scribd.com/document/866073988/Poulantzas).

### Conversations and interview-manifestations

- **1977:** *L’État, le pouvoir, et nous*. An interview with David Kaisergruber in *Dialectiques* 17. Its dialogic authorship makes it neither solely a Poulantzas essay nor merely a record made by someone else. [The original issue and interviewer are identified in the later *Dialectiques* publication history](https://viewpointmag.com/2017/12/18/state-social-movements-party-interview-nicos-poulantzas-1979/).

- **1978:** *Les théoriciens doivent retourner sur terre*. A newspaper interview or conversation published in *Les Nouvelles littéraires* on 26 June; the bibliography does not establish a separate interviewer’s authorship. [Jessop records the title and date](https://bobjessop.wordpress.com/2014/05/01/on-the-originality-legacy-and-actuality-of-nicos-poulantzas/).

- **1979:** *Interview with Nicos Poulantzas*. A conversation with Stuart Hall and Alan Hunt, conducted at Coventry on 5 April and published in *Marxism Today* in July. The interview is a jointly produced oral-textual object rather than a conventionally authored essay. [The original publication records its circumstances and transcription](https://cesarmangolin.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2010/02/poulantzas-nicos-interview-marxism-today-july-1979.pdf).

- **1979:** *L’État, les mouvements sociaux, le parti*. A late interview in *Dialectiques* 28 on state power, parties, and relatively autonomous social movements. It was later reprinted under the title *La crise des partis*, but should not be conflated with the separately published *Le Monde diplomatique* article without qualification. [The original title, issue, and reprint history are documented here](https://viewpointmag.com/2017/12/18/state-social-movements-party-interview-nicos-poulantzas-1979/).

- **1979:** *Es geht darum, mit der stalinistischen Tradition zu brechen!*. An interview conducted by Rodrigo Vázquez-Prada about authoritarian statism and working-class strategy, published in *PROKLA* 37. It is another jointly made conversational text. [The journal supplies the interview attribution and publication date](https://www.prokla.de/index.php/PROKLA/en/article/view/1620).

### Manifestos and political projects

- **1977:** *Une déclaration de MM. Nicos Poulantzas et Régis Debray*. A jointly authored public declaration explaining the abandonment of an attempted appeal for unity among the French left parties. The declaration is a manifesto-like act; the intended appeal itself remained an unrealized project and therefore resists being counted as a completed text. [*Le Monde* attributes and dates the declaration](https://www.lemonde.fr/archives/article/1977/10/25/une-declaration-de-mm-nicos-poulantzas-et-regis-debray_2881720_1819218.html).

- **1977:** *Appel pour que les partis de la gauche surmontent leurs divergences et répondent ainsi aux espoirs de notre peuple*. A projected collective appeal initiated by Poulantzas and Régis Debray but withdrawn after prospective signatories defected. Its surviving status is uncertain: it is a political project and aborted manifesto, not securely a published work. [The initiators’ declaration describes the attempt and renunciation](https://www.lemonde.fr/archives/article/1977/10/25/une-declaration-de-mm-nicos-poulantzas-et-regis-debray_2881720_1819218.html).

### Posthumous manifestations of late work

- **1979; published 1980:** *Research Note on the State and Society*. A concise research memorandum published posthumously in the *International Social Science Journal*, volume 32, number 4. Its precise composition date is not securely supplied, so only its association with Poulantzas’s final research phase and its 1980 manifestation can be stated. [The journal citation gives pages 600–608](https://revistas.juridicas.unam.mx/index.php/derecho-comparado/article/viewFile/1969/2226).

- **Date uncertain; published 1980:** *Le déplacement des procédures de légitimation*. A posthumously published contribution to *Le nouvel ordre intérieur*. The available bibliography gives the 1980 manifestation but not a defensible composition date, so no date has been invented for the making of the text. [Jessop records the contribution and pages 138–143](https://bobjessop.wordpress.com/2014/05/01/on-the-originality-legacy-and-actuality-of-nicos-poulantzas/).

- **1979; published 1983:** *Une révolution copernicienne dans la politique*. A short late text published posthumously in *La Gauche, le pouvoir, le socialisme*. Its exact pre-publication status—finished essay, notes, or prepared intervention—is not established by the bibliographical record. [The later scholarly bibliography assigns it to 1979 and records the 1983 publication](https://classic.scielo.org.mx/pdf/rmcps/v69n250/0185-1918-rmcps-69-250-95.pdf).

- **1980:** *Repères, hier et aujourd’hui: textes sur l’État*. A posthumous collection of previously published and late writings. It is an editorial manifestation of Poulantzas’s corpus, not a new book made by him as a unit during his lifetime; its individual texts belong under their original dates where those can be established. [Persée records the Maspero collection](https://www.persee.fr/authority/229092).
