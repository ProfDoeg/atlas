## Works by Emma Kunz

### Pendulum drawings: a research project later received as art

- **1938–1963:** *Untitled pendulum-assisted drawings (581 entries in the current online work catalogue)*. This is the principal obra: drawings in pencil, coloured pencil, and chalk or oil crayon on graph paper, generated by posing questions and locating points with a pendulum; Kunz treated them as research, diagnostic instruments, and models of otherwise invisible forces rather than as autonomous artworks. Almost none was signed, titled, or dated by her, so the catalogue’s work numbers and formal classifications—circle, triangle, straight line, cross, human, image, and complex—are retrospective identifiers rather than original titles; the catalogue is explicitly updateable, and its present total should not be mistaken for a permanently closed corpus. [Emma Kunz Foundation work catalogue](https://shop.emma-kunz.com/E/e/work-catalogue.htm)

- **1939:** *Work No. 020* (untitled). A pencil-, crayon-, and oil-crayon drawing on graph paper, exceptionally datable because testimony connects it to Kunz’s alarm about a destructive weapon being developed in the United States; the interpretation is a reported prophecy, not an independently verifiable title or authorial inscription. [Serpentine Galleries exhibition guide](https://citiesofladies.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/emma_kunz_exhibition_guide_-1.pdf)

- **Undated:** *Work No. 012* (untitled; often called *Philosophy of Life*). A predominantly figurative cosmological diagram on graph paper; “Philosophy of Life” is a received descriptive name, not a secure title supplied by Kunz. [Serpentine Galleries exhibition guide](https://citiesofladies.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/emma_kunz_exhibition_guide_-1.pdf)

- **Undated:** *Work No. 019* (untitled; reproduced as *Grundformen* / *Basic Forms*). A drawing associated with the exposition of Kunz’s elementary formal vocabulary; *Grundformen* appears as a reproduction legend and should not automatically be treated as an original title. [Leuphana University study and image documentation](https://pubdata.leuphana.de/bitstreams/bce2a86c-e828-4fb0-bd57-159d763a39ef/download)

- **Undated:** *Work No. 095* (untitled). A pendulum drawing later reproduced on a Swiss postage stamp in 1993; that later use is a manifestation of the drawing, not another work by Kunz. [The Mathematical Intelligencer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00283-024-10402-w)

- **Undated:** *Work No. 117* (untitled). A pencil-and-crayon drawing on graph paper whose apparent fourfold symmetry contains internal differences; it remains a research diagram that resists reduction to either geometric abstraction or medical instrument alone. [Serpentine Galleries exhibition guide](https://citiesofladies.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/emma_kunz_exhibition_guide_-1.pdf)

- **Shortly before 16 January 1963:** *Work No. 190* (untitled). Kunz’s final drawing, according to the exhibition documentation; she understood it as completing her researches through the symbolic opening of a seventh chamber of the pyramid, making it simultaneously a terminal research result and an image rather than simply a late artwork. [Serpentine Galleries exhibition guide](https://citiesofladies.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/emma_kunz_exhibition_guide_-1.pdf)

- **Undated:** *Notebook of geometric constructions and scientific notes*. A working notebook rather than a finished publication: the surviving documentary record identifies it as a single notebook containing constructions and research notes, so it belongs to Kunz’s project even though it resists designation as a completed work. [*Emma Kunz Cosmos* exhibition documentation](https://agnieszkabrzezanska.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/emma-kunz-cosmos-tabakalera-2022.pdf)

### Poetry

- **1930:** *Leben: Gedichte*. A published collection of poems, issued in Zürich by Hans A. Gutzwiller AG; some later accounts call it self-published, while exhibition documentation names Gutzwiller as publisher, so its precise publishing arrangement is not uniform across the sources. [*Emma Kunz Cosmos* exhibition documentation](https://agnieszkabrzezanska.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/emma-kunz-cosmos-tabakalera-2022.pdf), [Kunstbreite bibliography](https://www.kunstbreite.ch/Kuenstlerwerdegaenge_aargau_kunz_emma.htm)

### Expositions of method: text as protocol and manifesto

- **1953:** *Neuartige Zeichnungsmethode: Gestaltung und Form als Mass, Rhythmus, Symbol und Wandlung von Zahl und Prinzip*. A self-published methodological booklet that explains the conceptual vocabulary and procedure behind the drawings; it functions partly as protocol and partly as manifesto, asserting a learnable mode of inquiry rather than merely describing pictures. Some documentation inserts *Offenbarung* into the title, while the Emma Kunz Foundation and modern bibliographic record use the title given here. [Emma Kunz Foundation work catalogue](https://shop.emma-kunz.com/E/e/work-catalogue.htm), [Swiss National Library bibliography](https://ead.nb.admin.ch/web/sb-pdf/2024/sb202402.pdf), [*Emma Kunz Cosmos* exhibition documentation](https://agnieszkabrzezanska.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/emma-kunz-cosmos-tabakalera-2022.pdf)

- **1953:** *Das Wunder schöpfender Offenbarung: Gestaltung und Form als Mass, Rhythmus, Symbol und Wandlung von Zahl und Prinzip*. A self-published companion booklet containing one of Kunz’s rare written explanations of her drawing research. Sources vary between *schöpfender* and *schöpferischer Offenbarung*; the former is retained here because it appears in the established bibliography, without silently eliminating the variant. [Kunstbreite bibliography](https://www.kunstbreite.ch/Kuenstlerwerdegaenge_aargau_kunz_emma.htm), [*Emma Kunz Cosmos* exhibition documentation](https://agnieszkabrzezanska.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/emma-kunz-cosmos-tabakalera-2022.pdf)

### Material remedies and healing preparations

- **1942 onward:** *AION A*. Kunz’s name and therapeutic project for a mineral substance she identified in the Roman quarry at Würenlos and subsequently used as a healing powder; the geological material itself was discovered rather than made by her, while its naming, processing, applications, and status as a remedy constitute the work. Claims for its healing efficacy belong to Kunz’s practice and later testimony, not to an established scientific attribution. [Serpentine Galleries exhibition guide](https://citiesofladies.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/emma_kunz_exhibition_guide_-1.pdf), [The Mathematical Intelligencer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00283-024-10402-w)

- **Date not established:** *House Elixir “Rochus”*. A medicinal preparation documented among the surviving objects of Kunz’s practice; the available exhibition inventory supplies neither a secure date nor enough information to determine whether the displayed substance was compounded by Kunz herself or preserved as an example of her formula. [*Emma Kunz Cosmos* exhibition documentation](https://agnieszkabrzezanska.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/emma-kunz-cosmos-tabakalera-2022.pdf)

- **Date not established:** *Fenomen 3*. A medicinal preparation or remedy documented in Kunz’s surviving practice materials; it resists treatment as a conventional artwork, and the consulted inventory does not establish its composition, date, or whether the surviving specimen was personally manufactured by Kunz. [*Emma Kunz Cosmos* exhibition documentation](https://agnieszkabrzezanska.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/emma-kunz-cosmos-tabakalera-2022.pdf)

- **Date not established:** *Fenomen 4*. A medicinal preparation or remedy documented alongside *Fenomen 3*; its authorship as a formula is associated with Kunz, but the date, composition, and manufacture of the surviving object remain unspecified in the consulted inventory. [*Emma Kunz Cosmos* exhibition documentation](https://agnieszkabrzezanska.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/emma-kunz-cosmos-tabakalera-2022.pdf)

### Instruments within the project, not securely independent works

- **Date not established:** *Pendulum with jade and silver ball*. Kunz’s working instrument for radiesthesia and for establishing points in drawings; it is an artefact of the method rather than securely an object fabricated by her, so it should not be promoted without qualification into an authored artwork. [*Emma Kunz Cosmos* exhibition documentation](https://agnieszkabrzezanska.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/emma-kunz-cosmos-tabakalera-2022.pdf)

- **Date not established:** *Wooden organising board*. A board used with the pendulum as an ordering or interpretive scheme; it belongs to the operative apparatus of Kunz’s research, but the consulted catalogue does not establish that she personally constructed it, and it therefore resists confident classification as a made work. [*Emma Kunz Cosmos* exhibition documentation](https://agnieszkabrzezanska.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/emma-kunz-cosmos-tabakalera-2022.pdf)
