## Works by Alberto Kenya Fujimori Inomoto

### Political books and compilations

- **2002:** *日本はテロと戦えるか* (*Nihon wa tero to tatakaeru ka?*; “¿Puede Japón combatir el terrorismo?”). Japanese-language political book in which Fujimori presented his counterterrorism doctrine and interpreted his Peruvian presidency through the post-11 September 2001 political climate; some Spanish accounts give it the descriptive rather than bibliographically exact title *Alberto Fujimori lucha contra el terrorismo*. It is absent from later Peruvian lists of his written works, making those lists incomplete.

- **2005:** *El peso de la verdad*. A compilation of Fujimori’s defensive and political articles rather than a continuously composed treatise; published by Zignos with ISBN 978-9972-9995-0-5.

- **2024:** *Chavín de Huántar: el rescate soñado*. A retrospective political narrative of the 1996–1997 Japanese ambassador’s residence hostage crisis and Operation Chavín de Huántar. It presents the rescue principally through Fujimori’s claimed conception and direction of it, an attribution contested by other participants and by judicial and historical investigations into both the operation’s planning and alleged extrajudicial executions.

- **2024, published posthumously:** *¡Viva la paz!: balas y misiles se convirtieron en progreso y desarrollo para dos países hermanos*. A retrospective account of the Peru–Ecuador conflict and subsequent peace process; Keiko Fujimori presented it in December 2024 as her father’s final book. Publication after his death does not make the text itself posthumously authored, but the available catalogue record does not disclose how complete the manuscript was when he died or what editorial intervention preceded publication.

### Collaborative memoirs

- **2021:** *La palabra del Chino I: el intruso*. The opening volume of a memoir project, written in collaboration with Ricardo Rivera Romero and Víctor Paredes Guerra. Its third-person narration and acknowledged collaborators make “autobiography” an incomplete description: it is a jointly constructed political life narrative issued under Fujimori’s authority.

- **2022:** *La palabra del Chino II: el inconforme*. The second published instalment of the memoir project, covering Fujimori’s government from the 1992 self-coup onward. Like the first volume, it is an apologetic reconstruction of his presidency rather than an independent historical account; the precise division of writing among Fujimori and his collaborators is not publicly documented.

### Unfinished memoir project

- **2021–2024:** *La palabra del Chino*, projected continuation. The two published volumes were announced as only the first part of a series that was to include four further volumes, including a special volume addressing Fujimori’s trials and convictions. No fixed titles for those projected volumes are securely documented, and reports also refer to two additional completed or partly completed books that remained unpublished; their relationship to the promised four-volume continuation is unclear. This is therefore a project rather than a demonstrably complete set of obras.

### Audiovisual memoirs

- **2024:** *Videomemorias #01: “No soy un asesino”*. A short, performed video memoir and legal-political manifesto released through Canal F on 27 March 2024. Fujimori speaks in his own person, but the channel and video were produced with assistance from his family and Fogata Ediciones, so the audiovisual object resists attribution to him alone.

- **2024:** *Videomemorias #02: “Tengo derecho a defenderme”*. A short video memoir released on 5 April 2024, in which Fujimori frames his flight to Japan, resignation and later journey to Chile as stages in an effort to defend himself judicially. It functions simultaneously as testimony, self-exculpatory manifesto and political media production.

- **2024:** *Canal F videomemorias*. The larger serial project of which the two numbered films above formed part. Contemporary reporting describes additional videos and social-media statements as a continuing series, but no authoritative episode catalogue or collected edition has been located; it therefore cannot responsibly be converted into a conjectural list of separately titled works.
