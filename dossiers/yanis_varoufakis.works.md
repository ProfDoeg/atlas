## Works by Yanis Varoufakis

### Dissertation and research manual

- **1987:** *Optimisation and Strikes*. Doctoral dissertation in economics at the University of Essex, comparing econometric and game-theoretical models of industrial strikes.
- **1988:** *Econometric Methods on MINITAB: A Manual*. Instructional manual—a protocol for performing econometric analysis with MINITAB—issued by the University of East Anglia.

### Authored and co-authored books

- **1991:** *Rational Conflict*. Scholarly monograph on conflict, rationality, and game theory.
- **1995; revised 2004:** *Game Theory: A Critical Introduction*; revised as *Game Theory: A Critical Text*. Co-authored with Shaun Hargreaves Heap; the 2004 title is a substantial revision, not a wholly independent obra.
- **1998:** *Foundations of Economics: A Beginner’s Companion*. Critical introductory textbook.
- **2004:** *Μικροοικονομικά Υποδείγματα Μερικής και Γενικής Ισορροπίας* (*Microeconomic Models of Partial and General Equilibrium*). Greek-language textbook co-authored with Nicholas Theocarakis; Varoufakis’s CV dates it 2004, while his website’s Greek bibliography says 2005.
- **2007:** *Θεωρία Παιγνίων: Η θεωρία που φιλοδοξεί να ενοποιήσει τις κοινωνικές επιστήμες* (*Game Theory: The Theory That Aspires to Unify the Social Sciences*). Greek-language scholarly book.
- **2007:** *Πολιτική Οικονομία: Η οικονομική θεωρία στο φως της κριτικής* (*Political Economy: Economic Theory in a Critical Light*). Greek-language critical textbook.
- **2007:** *The Globalising Wall*. Hybrid book and art-project component made with artist Danae Stratou: Varoufakis’s political economy and travel writing accompanies photographs generated through journeys to seven divided regions. It resists classification as a book alone because it arose within Stratou’s wider installation project *CUT: 7 Dividing Lines*.
- **2011:** *Κρίσης Λεξιλόγιο: Οι οικονομικοί όροι που μας καταδυναστεύουν* (*Crisis Lexicon: The Economic Terms That Oppress Us*). Greek-language critical lexicon.
- **2011:** *The Global Minotaur: America, the True Origins of the Financial Crisis and the Future of the World Economy*. Political-economic history and diagnosis; the expanded 2013 edition is a revision, not a separate work.
- **2011:** *Modern Political Economics: Making Sense of the Post-2008 World*. Scholarly synthesis co-authored with Joseph Halevi and Nicholas Theocarakis.
- **2012:** *Ο Παγκόσμιος Μινώταυρος: Τα πραγματικά αίτια της κρίσης* (*The Global Minotaur: The Real Causes of the Crisis*). Greek recasting of *The Global Minotaur*; sources treat it as a Greek book, but its exact independence from the English work is unclear.
- **2013/2014:** *Economic Indeterminacy: A Personal Encounter with the Economists’ Peculiar Nemesis*. Scholarly monograph; the dated discrepancy reflects its appearance as a forthcoming 2014 book in Varoufakis’s 2013–14 CV while his website lists 2013.
- **2014:** *Η Γένεση της Μνημονιακής Ελλάδας* (*The Genesis of Bailout Greece*). Greek-language political-economic book.
- **2014:** *Μιλώντας στην κόρη μου για την οικονομία* (*Talking to My Daughter About the Economy*). Explanatory work first made in Greek as an address to the author’s daughter; the substantially adapted English version appeared in 2017 as *Talking to My Daughter: A Brief History of Capitalism*.
- **2015:** *Europe after the Minotaur: Greece and the Future of the Global Economy*. Short political-economic intervention derived from and updating the “Global Minotaur” argument; its boundary from the earlier book is therefore porous.
- **2016:** *And the Weak Suffer What They Must? Europe’s Crisis and America’s Economic Future*. Political-economic history and polemic about the construction and crisis of Europe.
- **2016:** *The Euro Paradox*. Short-form political-economic book or extended essay; some bibliographies omit it, making its standing as an independent obra less secure than that of Varoufakis’s major trade books.
- **2017:** *Adults in the Room: My Battle with Europe’s Deep Establishment*. Political memoir and documentary reconstruction of the 2015 Greek debt negotiations.
- **2017:** *Talking to My Daughter About the Economy: A Brief History of Capitalism*. English-language reworking of the 2014 Greek book, not merely a mechanically translated edition.
- **2019:** *Ανίκητοι Ηττημένοι* (*Adults in the Room / Invincible Losers*). Greek rendering and reconfiguration of the 2017 memoir; it should not be counted uncritically as an unrelated work.
- **2020:** *Another Now: Dispatches from an Alternative Present*. Novel of political and economic ideas—Varoufakis’s first published fiction—using speculative narrative to model a post-capitalist society.
- **2023:** *Technofeudalism: What Killed Capitalism*. Political-economic argument presented through intellectual memoir, technological history, mythology, and cultural criticism.
- **2025:** *Raise Your Soul: A Personal History of Resistance*. Family history, memoir, and political work organized around five women and three generations; its publisher explicitly cautions that treating it only as autobiography or biography diminishes its mixed form.

### Edited obra

- **1990:** *Conflict in Economics*. Scholarly collection co-edited with David Young; Varoufakis also contributed two chapters.
- **2000:** *Game Theory: Critical Concepts*, volumes 1–5. A roughly 1,600-page edited anthology—*Foundations*, *Refinements*, *Economic Applications*, *Game Theory in the Social Sciences*, and *Philosophical Discontents*—with a new introductory essay by Varoufakis in each volume.

### Chapters, reference entries, and contributions to collections

- **1989:** *Game Theory, Oligopoly and Bargaining*. Chapter co-authored with Bruce Lyons; a 1997 appearance was a reprint rather than a new work.
- **1990:** *Conflict in Equilibrium*. Chapter in *Conflict in Economics*.
- **1990:** *Solidarity in Conflict*. Chapter in *Conflict in Economics*.
- **1991:** *Economic Theories of Information: A Critique*. Chapter co-authored with Shaun Hargreaves Heap.
- **1992:** *The Writing of an Economic Rationalist Manifesto*. Chapter about the making of a manifesto; despite its title, the chapter itself is principally critical analysis rather than the manifesto it discusses.
- **1993:** *Theories of Games and Contracts*. Italian-language collection chapter co-authored with Bruce Lyons.
- **1995:** *Experimenting with Neo-Classical Economics: A Critical Review of Experimental Economics*. Chapter co-authored with Shaun Hargreaves Heap.
- **1995:** *Comment*. Brief contribution to *The Pursuit of Full Employment in the 1990s*; it resists treatment as a freestanding work because its title and argument depend upon the surrounding collection.
- **1996:** *Economics as the Ethic of Our Times*. Essay in an edited collection.
- **2000:** *Great Expectations: Game Theory’s Promise to Unify the Social Sciences*. Original introduction to volume 1 of *Game Theory: Critical Concepts*.
- **2000:** *Second Wind: Game Theory Comes of Age*. Original introduction to volume 2 of *Game Theory: Critical Concepts*.
- **2000:** *Takeover: Game Theory’s Influence on Economics*. Original introduction to volume 3 of *Game Theory: Critical Concepts*.
- **2000:** *Beyond Economics: Game Theory’s Foray into the Social Sciences*. Original introduction to volume 4 of *Game Theory: Critical Concepts*.
- **2000:** *Game Theory’s Philosophical Foundations: Internal and External Critique*. Original introduction to volume 5 of *Game Theory: Critical Concepts*.
- **2007:** *What Is Neoclassical Economics? The Three Axioms Responsible for Its Theoretical Oeuvre, Practical Irrelevance and, Thus, Discursive Power*. Chapter co-authored with Christian Arnsperger.
- **2009:** *A Most Peculiar Success: Constructing UADPhilEcon, a Doctoral Program in Economics at the University of Athens*. Institutional case study about a pedagogical project Varoufakis founded and directed.
- **2010:** *Game Theory*. Reference entry in *The Encyclopedia of Political Theory*.
- **2013:** *We Are All Greeks Now: The Crisis in Greece in Its European and Global Context*. Collection chapter.

### Scholarly and critical articles

- **1987:** *An ARIMA Model of Tea Prices*. Econometric article co-authored with David Sapsford.
- **1989:** *Worker Solidarity and Strikes*. Labour-economics article.
- **1990:** *A Real Target Model of Wage Inflation with Variable Union Power: The Case of the UK, 1962–1985*. Econometric article co-authored with David Sapsford.
- **1991:** *The Neoclassical Theory of Choice: Introducing Undergraduates to Criticism*. Economics-education article.
- **1991:** *Discrete and Smooth Switching Regressions for Australian Labour Productivity Growth*. Econometric article co-authored with David Sapsford.
- **1991:** *Forecasting Coffee Prices: ARIMA versus Econometric Approaches*. Article co-authored with David Sapsford.
- **1992:** *Modelling Rational Conflict*. Game-theoretical article.
- **1992:** *Evaluating Probability Predictions: An Application of Copas’ Diagrammatic Method in Comparing Two Models of Strikes*. Methodological article.
- **1992–1993:** *Freedom within Reason: From Axioms to Praxis*. Philosophical-economics article; the volume spans two calendar years.
- **1993:** *Modern and Postmodern Challenges to Game Theory*. Methodological article.
- **1993–1994:** *Reason without Freedom: A Response to Mayer*. Scholarly reply; the journal volume crosses calendar years.
- **1995:** *Labour Productivity Growth Trends in OECD Countries: Selection of Sub-periods*. Empirical article co-authored with David Sapsford.
- **1996:** *Bargaining and Strikes: From an Equilibrium to an Evolutionary Framework*. Labour- and game-theory article.
- **1996:** *The Evolution of Inequality: Evolutionary Theories and the Social Status of Women*. Greek-language article co-authored with M. Anagnostopoulou.
- **1996:** *Coercion and Allocation*. Critical article; bibliographic descriptions sometimes identify it more fully as a comment on John Alcorn.
- **1997:** *Moral Rhetoric in the Face of Strategic Weakness: Experimental Clues for an Ancient Puzzle*. Experimental and philosophical article; an earlier working-paper form circulated with “Modern Clues” in the title.
- **1997:** *Towards a Critique of Social Asymmetry and Ethics: Hume and Marx Meet Evolutionary Game Theory*. Greek-language article in *Axiologika*.
- **1998–1999:** *Defending History*. Critical article in a journal volume spanning two years.
- **2000:** *Central Bank Independence and the Value of Ambiguity: A Three-Player Reputational Game*. Article co-authored with P. Gangopadhyay.
- **2002:** *Some Experimental Results on Discrimination, Co-operation and Perceptions of Fairness*. Experimental-economics article co-authored with Shaun Hargreaves Heap.
- **2002:** *Deconstructing Homo Economicus? Reflections on an Encounter between Postmodernity and Neoclassical Economics*. Methodological article; a version appeared the same year as *Why Critics of Economics Can Ill-Afford the “Postmodern Turn”*, so the two titles should not automatically be counted as independent works.
- **2002:** *Against Equality*. Political-philosophical article.
- **2002:** *In Search of a Dispassionate Tribunal*. Article co-authored with Nicholas Theocarakis.
- **2003:** *Toward a Theory of Solidarity*. Philosophical and game-theoretical article co-authored with Christian Arnsperger.
- **2003:** *The Global Minotaur*. Article co-authored with Joseph Halevi; it is the early manifestation of an argument later expanded into a book.
- **2003:** *Questions and Answers on the Global Minotaur*. Companion article co-authored with Joseph Halevi.
- **2005:** *A Hard Spectre to Silence: Meghnad Desai’s Marx’s Revenge*. Extended critical review that functions as an essay as well as a book review.
- **2006:** *The Bonds That Impede: A Model of the Joint Evolution of Corruption and Apathy*. Formal political-economy article.
- **2006:** *Rational Rules of Thumb in Finite Dynamic Games: N-Person Backward Induction with Inconsistently Aligned Beliefs and Full Rationality*. Game-theory article.
- **2008:** *Game Theory: Can It Unify the Social Sciences?*. Synthetic scholarly article.
- **2008:** *Capitalism according to Evolutionary Game Theory: On the Impossibility of a Sufficiently Evolutionary Model of Historical Change*. Critical theoretical article.
- **2009:** *Where the Customers Are Always Wrong: Some Thoughts on the Societal Impact of a Non-Pluralist Economic Education*. Economics-education article.
- **2009:** *Pristine Equations, Tainted Economics and the Postwar Order*. Conference paper included in the proceedings of Columbia University’s Cold War Politics and Social Science Workshop; its status lies between a delivered paper and a formally published article.
- **2010:** *A New Versailles Haunts Europe (or Furiosa Teutonicorum Insania)*. Political-economic essay.
- **2011:** *A Modest Proposal for Overcoming the Euro Crisis*. Journal article co-authored with Stuart Holland and related to the evolving policy project of the same name.
- **2013:** *From Contagion to Incoherence: Toward a Model of the Unfolding Eurozone Crisis*. Scholarly article.
- **2013:** *Europe Unhinged: What the Financial Crisis of 2008 Meant for the Integrity of the Eurozone*. Public-facing analytical article.

### Manifestos, policy protocols, and political projects

- **2010–2013:** *A Modest Proposal for Resolving the Eurozone Crisis*. Evolving policy protocol made chiefly with Stuart Holland and, in version 4.0, James K. Galbraith. Versions 1.0, 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, 2.0, 2.1, 2.2, 3.0, and 4.0 are manifestations of one revisable project rather than nine unrelated works.
- **2011–present:** *Thoughts for the Post-2008 World*. Varoufakis’s continuing website and public notebook, containing essays, speeches, interviews, and documentary materials. It resists enumeration as a single finished work: it is an open-ended archive and publishing project whose contents overlap with separately published texts.
- **2012–2013:** *Valve Economics*. Research project and blog produced while Varoufakis was Valve’s economist-in-residence, using virtual economies as economic laboratories. The public record supports his analysis of these economies, but not popular claims that he personally “made” Valve’s games or invented their monetization systems.
- **2016:** *DiEM25 Manifesto*. Collective performative text associated with the launch of the Democracy in Europe Movement 2025, which Varoufakis co-founded. Its authorship is deliberately plural: it acts by convening a transnational movement and should not be appropriated as his solo work.
- **2016–present:** *Democracy in Europe Movement 2025 (DiEM25)*. Continuing transnational political project co-founded by Varoufakis; it is an organization and field of collective action, not a text or a finished individual obra.
- **2017:** *European New Deal*. DiEM25 policy programme developed under Varoufakis’s political leadership. It combines manifesto and protocol, but remains collectively authored and institutionally revisable.
- **2018:** *MeRA25*. Greek political party founded by Varoufakis as DiEM25’s electoral expression. Calling it his “work” risks confusing political initiative with personal property: it is a collective, continuing organization.
- **2020–present:** *Progressive International*. Transnational organizing project launched from a call associated with DiEM25 and the Sanders Institute, with Varoufakis among its leading founders. Its institutional and collective authorship prevents classification as a solo obra.
- **2024–present:** *The Econoclasts*. Recurring economics podcast co-presented by Varoufakis; an episodic conversational work rather than a fixed book or single authored text.

### Works made from, around, or with Varoufakis—but not straightforwardly by him

- **2019:** *Adults in the Room*. Feature film adapted from Varoufakis’s memoir and directed by Costa-Gavras. Varoufakis made the source book and appears within the film’s subject matter, but the film itself should not be attributed to him as filmmaker.
- **2024:** *In the Eye of the Storm: The Political Odyssey of Yanis Varoufakis*. Six-part documentary series directed by Raoul Martinez. Varoufakis’s testimony and political-economic narration are constitutive materials, yet the series is principally Martinez’s authored documentary about and with him; it therefore resists inclusion among Varoufakis’s solo works.
