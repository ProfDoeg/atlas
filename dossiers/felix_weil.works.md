## Works by Felix José Weil

### Books and extended studies

- **1920–1921:** *Sozialisierung: Versuch einer begrifflichen Grundlegung nebst einer Kritik der Sozialisierungspläne*. Political-economy dissertation, accepted at Frankfurt in 1920 and published as a book in 1921; it develops, rather than simply reprints, Weil’s earlier intervention on socialization. The scholarly bibliography treats this as his first monograph. ([NDB](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd117251062.html?language=en); [Gandarilla Salgado](https://tramasyredes-ojs.clacso.org/ojs/index.php/tyr/article/view/409))

- **1923:** *Die Arbeiterbewegung in Argentinien: Ein Beitrag zu ihrer Geschichte*. A short Marxist history of Argentina’s labor movement, made from statistics, printed sources, interviews with veteran militants, and Weil’s encounters with Argentine communism; later reprinting and Spanish translations are manifestations of this work, not additional works. Scholars describe it as a pioneering—and possibly the first dedicated—history of the Argentine labor movement. ([Camarero](https://www.archivosrevista.com.ar/numeros/index.php/archivos/article/download/490/569?inline=1); [NDB](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd117251062.html?language=en))

- **1933:** *Concepto y alcance del impuesto a los réditos*. A fiscal-policy treatise issued by the Asociación de Estudios del Derecho Fiscal in Buenos Aires, connected with Weil’s work on progressive income taxation. Its relationship to the government’s legislation is collaborative rather than sole authorship of the law itself. ([Braunstein and Valerius](https://www.ifs.uni-frankfurt.de/publikationsdetails/ifs-dirk-braunstein-and-alexander-valerius-eds-felix-j-weil-s-the-social-function-of-the-race-philosophy-in-nazi-germany-an-introduced-facsimile-edition.html); [NDB](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd117251062.html?language=en))

- **1944:** *Argentine Riddle*. Weil’s principal published synthesis: a book-length work of political economy and contemporary history about Argentina immediately before Perón. *El enigma argentino* (2010) and separately published Spanish selections are translations or excerpted manifestations, not new obras. ([Cambridge History of Latin America](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/cambridge-history-of-latin-america/bibliographical-essays/E4ED5E6159044980327EE5E5B9CA8F46); [NDB](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd117251062.html?language=en))

- **1946:** *The Social Function of the Race Philosophy in Nazi Germany*. A corrected typescript study prepared for publication within the American Jewish Committee–Institute for Social Research “Treatise on Antisemitism” project, but never typeset or published in Weil’s lifetime. Long considered lost, the probably unique surviving copy was rediscovered in the Institute archive; it is therefore an unfinished publication project but a substantially finished manuscript obra. ([Institute for Social Research facsimile edition](https://www.ifs.uni-frankfurt.de/publikationsdetails/ifs-dirk-braunstein-and-alexander-valerius-eds-felix-j-weil-s-the-social-function-of-the-race-philosophy-in-nazi-germany-an-introduced-facsimile-edition.html))

- **1967:** *How to Conduct Your Case before a California Assessment Appeals Board*. A practical legal-fiscal protocol: a guide instructing California taxpayers how to present assessment appeals, rather than a descriptive scholarly book. ([NDB](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd117251062.html?language=en))

### Articles, interventions, and review essays

- **1919:** *Wesen und Wege der Sozialisierung*. A programmatic article in *Der Arbeiter-Rat*, the theoretical periodical of Germany’s workers’ councils; it is an intervention in an active revolutionary dispute and accordingly approaches a manifesto, although it remains analytical rather than declarative. ([Villarruel](https://www.archivosrevista.com.ar/numeros/index.php/archivos/article/download/492/571?inline=1); [Gandarilla Salgado](https://tramasyredes-ojs.clacso.org/ojs/index.php/tyr/article/view/409))

- **1924:** *Gildensozialistische Rechnungslegung: Kritische Bemerkungen zu Karl Polányi, “Sozialistische Rechnungslegung”*. A journal article on accounting and price calculation under guild socialism, written as a direct critique of Karl Polanyi. Bibliographies disagree over the volume citation—52 in the NDB, but 49/2 in some later scholarship—so the title and year are firmer than the frequently reproduced volume number. ([EconBiz record](https://www.econbiz.de/Record/gildensozialistische-rechnungslegung-kritische-bemerkungen-zu-karl-pol%C3%A1ny-sozialistische-rechnungslegung-in-diesem-archiv-49-2-weil-felix/10002974146); [NDB](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd117251062.html?language=en))

- **1928:** *Rosa Luxemburg über die russische Revolution: Einige unveröffentlichte Manuskripte*. A hybrid scholarly object: Weil’s introduction, report on the recovered manuscripts, and editorial presentation of writings made by Rosa Luxemburg. The Luxemburg material is not Weil’s work, while the framing, comparison, textual changes, and additions are. ([Friedrich-Ebert-Stiftung digital edition](https://library.fes.de/cgi-bin/gruenberg.pl?b=13&f=285&l=298); [Central European History](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/central-european-history/article/abs/rosa-luxemburg-belongs-to-us-german-communism-and-the-luxemburg-legacy/753E3125E923FABEAE27D0CE8E69524C))

- **1936:** *Neuere Literatur zum “New Deal”*. A critical literature survey published in the *Zeitschrift für Sozialforschung*, not a freestanding book. ([NDB](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd117251062.html?language=en); [Villarruel bibliography](https://www.archivosrevista.com.ar/numeros/index.php/archivos/article/view/492))

- **1938:** *Neuere Literatur zur deutschen Wehrwirtschaft*. A substantial review essay on recent literature concerning the German war economy, published in the *Zeitschrift für Sozialforschung*. ([NDB](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd117251062.html?language=en))

- **1939:** *Book-review section, Studies in Philosophy and Social Science*, pp. 306–316. A group of review notices rather than one securely titled autonomous work; the standard bibliography records the pages collectively, so imposing individual titles would create false precision. ([NDB](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd117251062.html?language=en))

- **1939:** *Book-review section, Studies in Philosophy and Social Science*, pp. 492 ff. Another bibliographically grouped set of reviews whose individual extent and titles are not supplied by the standard works list; it resists reliable item-by-item division. ([NDB](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd117251062.html?language=en))

- **1941:** *Book-review section, Studies in Philosophy and Social Science*, pp. 326–336. A further group of review writing documented under Weil’s name but not resolved into separately titled works by the standard bibliography. ([NDB](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd117251062.html?language=en))

- **1943:** *New Literature on Argentina*. A bibliographical and critical survey in *World Economics*, extending Weil’s larger project of interpreting contemporary Argentina. ([Villarruel bibliography](https://www.archivosrevista.com.ar/numeros/index.php/archivos/article/view/492))

- **1943:** *Review: Inter-American Statistical Yearbook, 1942*. A signed review in the *Hispanic American Historical Review*, not an original statistical work. ([Villarruel bibliography](https://www.archivosrevista.com.ar/numeros/index.php/archivos/article/view/492))

- **1944:** *Argentina and the U.S.A.* A periodical article in *Asia and the Americas* on Argentine–United States relations. ([Villarruel bibliography](https://www.archivosrevista.com.ar/numeros/index.php/archivos/article/view/492))

- **1944:** *Behind Argentine Neutrality*. A political article in *Common Sense* interpreting Argentina’s wartime position. ([Villarruel bibliography](https://www.archivosrevista.com.ar/numeros/index.php/archivos/article/view/492))

- **1944:** *“Good” Capital versus “Bad”*. A political-economic article in *World Affairs*. ([Villarruel bibliography](https://www.archivosrevista.com.ar/numeros/index.php/archivos/article/view/492))

- **1944:** *Argentina’s “Mad” Foreign Policy: The Role of “Hemisphere Solidarity” and British-American Trade Rivalry*. A topical newspaper or magazine intervention in *The New Leader*, rather than a sustained monograph. ([Villarruel bibliography](https://www.archivosrevista.com.ar/numeros/index.php/archivos/article/view/492))

- **1945:** *Problems of Argentine Fascism: Fascism sui generis*. A short political intervention in *The New Leader* on the specifically Argentine form of fascism. ([Villarruel bibliography](https://www.archivosrevista.com.ar/numeros/index.php/archivos/article/view/492))

- **1945:** *What Goes on in Argentina?* A substantial interpretive article in *The Antioch Review* on Argentine politics during the transition toward Peronism. ([NDB](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd117251062.html?language=en); [JSTOR record](https://www.jstor.org/stable/4609111))

- **1947:** *Literature on the Industrialization of Latin America*. A critical literature survey in *Inter-American Economic Affairs*. ([Villarruel bibliography](https://www.archivosrevista.com.ar/numeros/index.php/archivos/article/view/492))

- **1948:** *Reviewed Works*. A brief review contribution in *Political Science Quarterly*; the scholarly bibliography supplies this generic heading rather than a stable authored title, so it should not be silently retitled. ([Villarruel bibliography](https://www.archivosrevista.com.ar/numeros/index.php/archivos/article/view/492); [JSTOR record](https://www.jstor.org/stable/2144852))

- **1950:** *Can Perón Be Bought?* A political-economic article in *Inter-American Economic Affairs*. ([Villarruel bibliography](https://www.archivosrevista.com.ar/numeros/index.php/archivos/article/view/492))

- **1955:** *Book Notes*. A review contribution in *Political Science Quarterly*; it is a cluster of evaluative notes rather than a book or unified treatise. ([Villarruel bibliography](https://www.archivosrevista.com.ar/numeros/index.php/archivos/article/view/492); [JSTOR issue record](https://www.jstor.org/stable/i311335))

### Translation and adaptation

- **1959:** *Vorgeschichte des politischen Antisemitismus*. Weil’s German translation and adaptation of Paul W. Massing’s *Rehearsal for Destruction*. Massing remains the author of the study; Weil made its German-language manifestation and revised it for that readership, so it belongs here as translational work rather than as a Weil monograph. ([Institute for Social Research bibliography](https://www.ifs.uni-frankfurt.de/publikationsdetails/ifs-dirk-braunstein-and-alexander-valerius-eds-felix-j-weil-s-the-social-function-of-the-race-philosophy-in-nazi-germany-an-introduced-facsimile-edition.html); [CiNii bibliographic record](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/author/DA02843645))

### Institutional and editorial projects

- **1922:** *Denkschrift über die Begründung eines Instituts für Sozialforschung*. A five-page typescript memorandum made jointly with Kurt Albert Gerlach: at once an institutional design, funding proposal, and protocol for creating a research institute. Its joint authorship must be retained. ([Archival description](https://biblioteca.phorteeducacional.com.br/items?page=59&query=delta%28IX%29O))

- **1923:** *Erste Marxistische Arbeitswoche*. A convened intellectual project—financed and organized by Weil at Ilmenau—whose made thing was a temporary working community rather than a text. It helped test the intellectual direction from which the Institute for Social Research emerged. ([WOZ](https://www.woz.ch/2321/frankfurter-schule/das-werk-eines-roten-millionaerssohns/%2145YZJVEA99AT); [Frankfurter Personenlexikon](https://frankfurter-personenlexikon.de/node/1697))

- **1922–1924:** *Institut für Sozialforschung*. An institutional obra and continuing research project, negotiated and co-founded by Weil, his father Hermann Weil, and academic and state collaborators; Weil financed it, shaped its program and library, and intervened in Franz Roeckle’s building design. Calling the Institute solely “his work” would erase its collective authorship, but reducing his part to patronage would also be inaccurate. ([Frankfurter Personenlexikon](https://frankfurter-personenlexikon.de/node/1697); [Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz97925.html?language=en))

- **1924:** *Marx-Engels-Archivgesellschaft and the first Marx-Engels-Gesamtausgabe project*. A documentary and editorial infrastructure for collecting and publishing Marx and Engels, managed by Weil jointly with Friedrich Pollock and in cooperation with David Riazanov’s Moscow institute. The resulting edition was collective and remained incomplete; Weil’s obra here is organizational and archival rather than authorship of Marx and Engels’s texts. ([Frankfurter Personenlexikon](https://frankfurter-personenlexikon.de/node/1697); [International Review of Social History](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/2B7F08FA160D58BE8CA74AC9039ABBA9/S0020859002000639a.pdf/bibliography.pdf))

- **1920s:** *Library of the Institut für Sozialforschung*. A deliberately assembled specialist collection on sociology, socialism, and the labor movement. It resists classification as a single “work”: Weil built its collecting program, but the library is a cumulative institutional body made from innumerable works by others. ([Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz97925.html?language=en); [Frankfurter Personenlexikon](https://frankfurter-personenlexikon.de/node/1697))

- **1929:** *Soziologische Verlagsanstalt*. A publishing project founded by Weil in Berlin to issue Marxist theoretical literature, including work by Franz Mehring. It is an enabling structure for other people’s texts rather than a text authored by Weil. ([Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz97925.html?language=en); [Frankfurter Personenlexikon](https://frankfurter-personenlexikon.de/node/1697))

- **1932–1933:** *Argentine income-tax legislation*. Weil advised the Argentine government and participated in drafting the new fiscal regime. This is a collective legal protocol rather than an individually authored work, and the available scholarly sources do not justify assigning the statute to Weil alone. ([Frankfurter Personenlexikon](https://frankfurter-personenlexikon.de/node/1697); [Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz97925.html?language=en))

- **1934:** *Pestalozzi-Schule, Buenos Aires*. An educational institution co-founded by Weil as an alternative for families displaced or threatened by National Socialism. It is a collective institutional creation, not a written work, and the sources do not isolate a solely Weil-authored educational program. ([Frankfurter Personenlexikon](https://frankfurter-personenlexikon.de/node/1697))

### Unfinished memoir

- **c. 1970–1975:** *Aus der Art geschlagen: Erinnerungen eines roten Millionärssohnes*. A two-part autobiographical typescript left fragmentary at Weil’s death and preserved in the Institut für Stadtgeschichte Frankfurt; only selections later appeared in *Frankfurter Jüdische Erinnerungen*. Its incompletion is constitutive: it is an unfinished project and archival obra, not a completed book withheld from publication. ([Frankfurter Personenlexikon](https://frankfurter-personenlexikon.de/node/1697); [NDB](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd117251062.html?language=en))
