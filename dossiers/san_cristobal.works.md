## Works by San Cristobal

### No securely attributable obra

- No securely attributable work is known. The standard Greek and Latin hagiographic repertories catalogue anonymous *passiones*, lives, and later adaptations **about** San Cristóbal, but no text, object, project, or other made thing authored by him; those narratives therefore resist inclusion as his works. Even the underlying historical person and chronology remain uncertain: the traditional martyrdom under Decius conflicts with a scholarly reconstruction placing a possible historical martyr under Diocletian, while the earliest firmly dated evidence is a shrine inscription of 450–452 rather than a contemporary work by the saint.
