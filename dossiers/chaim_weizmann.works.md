## Works by Chaim Azriel Weizmann

### Scientific works and inventions

- **1898–1899:** *Elektrolytische Reduktion von 1-Nitroanthrachinon*. Doctoral research in organic chemistry, submitted at Fribourg and examined on 17 January 1899; accounts describe two short dissertations, so this title should not be mistaken for the entirety of his doctoral work. ([Israel Journal of Chemistry](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ijch.201510005); [Isaiah Berlin Virtual Library](https://berlin.wolf.ox.ac.uk/published_works/singles/bib84.pdf))

- **c. 1898–1952:** *Scientific papers*. An œuvre of 102 reported scientific publications, chiefly in organic and fermentation chemistry. No complete scholarly list is publicly available online: the standard biographical bibliography directs researchers to the Weizmann Archives for a nearly complete enumeration, so the papers cannot responsibly be expanded here into invented or catalogue-derived titles. ([Encyclopaedia Judaica bibliography](https://www.encyclopedia.com/people/history/israeli-history-biographies/chaim-weizmann); [Israel Journal of Chemistry](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ijch.201510005))

- **1902:** *Naphthoquinone process*. A chemical invention patented jointly with Deichler and assigned to Bayer; it belongs to Weizmann’s early synthetic-dye work rather than to the later fermentation process. ([Israel Chemical Society](https://www.chemistry.org.il/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/ICE_August-2016.pdf))

- **1912–1915:** *Production of acetone and alcohol by bacteriological processes*—the “Weizmann process.” An invention and industrial protocol using a bacterium later named *Clostridium acetobutylicum* to ferment starches into acetone, butanol and ethanol; the process, organism and patent family are related manifestations, not interchangeable objects. The priority date and wartime industrial implementation precede the 1919 US publication of patent US1315585. ([US patent 1,315,585](https://patents.google.com/patent/US1315585A/en); [Biotechnology and Bioengineering centenary collection](https://analyticalsciencejournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/toc/10.1002/%28ISSN%291097-0290.acetone-butanol-ethanol-fermentation))

- **1914–1952:** *Patented chemical inventions*. An œuvre of roughly one hundred patents—often filed under the anglicized name “Charles Weizmann”—covering fermentation, dyestuffs, titanium compounds and condensation processes. These are legal-technical protocols rather than scientific papers; different national grants may be manifestations of the same invention, and the archival bibliography is described as “nearly complete,” not definitive. ([Encyclopaedia Judaica bibliography](https://www.encyclopedia.com/people/history/israeli-history-biographies/chaim-weizmann); examples: [US1315585](https://patents.google.com/patent/US1315585A/en), [US1489183](https://patents.google.com/patent/US1489183A/en), [US2386374](https://patents.google.com/patent/US2386374A/en), [US2474175](https://patents.google.com/patent/US2474175A/en), [US2592365](https://patents.google.com/patent/US2592365A/en))

### Political projects and institutional obra

- **1902 onward:** *A Hebrew university in Jerusalem*. A long institutional project advocated by Weizmann with Martin Buber and Berthold Feiwel in a 1902 leaflet, developed in later speeches and organizational work, and manifested institutionally in the Hebrew University of Jerusalem. It resists attribution as an individual “work”: Weizmann was a persistent maker and advocate, not its sole creator. ([Hebrew University history](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebr%C3%A4ische_Universit%C3%A4t_Jerusalem); [Encyclopaedia Judaica](https://wit-resources.s3.amazonaws.com/Encyclopaedia/Vol.20_-_To-Wei.pdf))

- **1913:** *Die hebräische Universität in Jerusalem*. A programmatic speech and separately circulated text advocating the proposed university; it is simultaneously a political argument and a protocol for an institution still to be made. ([Encyclopaedia Judaica](https://wit-resources.s3.amazonaws.com/Encyclopaedia/Vol.20_-_To-Wei.pdf))

- **1918–1925:** *The Hebrew University of Jerusalem*. An institutional obra created collectively through fundraising, planning, diplomacy and construction; Weizmann participated in the 1918 foundation-stone ceremony and remained central to the project through its 1925 opening. Calling the university simply “his work” would erase its many other founders, scholars, donors and builders. ([Yad Chaim Weizmann](https://www.chaimweizmann.org.il/en/chaim-weizmann); [Friends of the Hebrew University](https://www.fhuj.org/geschichte/))

- **1919:** *Faisal–Weizmann Agreement*. A jointly signed diplomatic protocol between Emir Faisal and Weizmann, intended to coordinate Arab independence and Zionist development. Faisal attached a condition tying his assent to fulfilment of Arab independence promises; because that condition was not met, the document’s validity and practical force remain disputed. ([Faisal–Weizmann Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faisal%E2%80%93Weizmann_agreement))

- **1934:** *Daniel Sieff Research Institute*. A scientific institution founded by Weizmann at Rehovot with Israel and Rebecca Sieff’s support; it was subsequently enlarged and renamed the Weizmann Institute of Science. This is an institutional obra and collective project, not a text or a sole-authored creation. ([Weizmann Institute history](https://www.weizmann.ac.il/pages/the-institute/history))

- **1944–1949:** *Weizmann Institute of Science*. The transformation of the Sieff Institute into a broad research institute was an unfinished institutional project during Weizmann’s lifetime and was inaugurated under the new name in 1949. The name commemorates him, but naming is not authorship; the institution was made collectively by scientists, patrons and administrators. ([Weizmann Institute history](https://www.weizmann.ac.il/pages/the-institute/history); [Encyclopaedia Judaica](https://wit-resources.s3.amazonaws.com/Encyclopaedia/Vol.20_-_To-Wei.pdf))

### Speeches, testimony, memoranda and political interventions

- **1898–1931:** *Papers, Series B, Volume I*. The first chronological body of Weizmann’s public and quasi-public political works: speeches, interviews, testimony, meeting records, memoranda and articles. These pieces often resist individual authorship because later statements could express an organization’s negotiated position; the scholarly edition explicitly distinguishes them from his private letters. ([Google Books description](https://books.google.com/books/about/The_Letters_and_Papers_of_Chaim_Weizmann.html?hl=en&id=PsabNtx33VMC&output=html_text); [series description](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BA29705602.amp))

- **1931–1952:** *Papers, Series B, Volume II*. The continuation of that political œuvre, ending in April 1952. The collected volume is an editorial manifestation published after Weizmann’s death, not itself a book composed by him. ([Google Books description](https://books.google.com/books/about/The_Letters_and_Papers_of_Chaim_Weizmann.html?hl=en&id=PsabNtx33VMC&output=html_text); [series description](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BA29705602.amp))

- **25 November 1936:** *The Jewish People and Palestine*. Weizmann’s extended statement before the Palestine Royal Commission, subsequently issued as a pamphlet. It is testimony—a political act performed before an investigating body—rather than a descriptive monograph. ([ILAB catalogue description](https://ilab.org/assets/catalogues/Judaica_Aviv_2024.pdf); [Encyclopaedia Judaica](https://wit-resources.s3.amazonaws.com/Encyclopaedia/Vol.20_-_To-Wei.pdf))

- **1937:** *Devarim: Ne’umim, ma’amarim u-mikhtavim* (*Speeches, Essays and Letters*). A four-volume Hebrew selection of speeches and writings from 1901–1936. It is an authorized retrospective collection and editorial manifestation, not a single newly composed work. ([Project Ben-Yehuda](https://benyehuda.org/collections/5246); [Encyclopaedia Judaica](https://wit-resources.s3.amazonaws.com/Encyclopaedia/Vol.20_-_To-Wei.pdf))

- **1937:** *Reden und Aufsätze, 1901–1936*. A German-language selected collection overlapping the material gathered in *Devarim*. Its status is that of an editorial and translational manifestation of earlier speeches and essays, not an independent œuvre created in 1937. ([Google Books bibliographic record](https://books.google.com/books/about/Reden_und_Aufs%C3%A4tze_1901_1936.html?id=q04yAQAAIAAJ))

- **1938:** *Notes for “Rallying World Jewry to Partition”*. Working notes for a speech supporting partition: an unfinished or preparatory text whose projectual status is more important than literary finish. The scholarly source locates it in *Letters and Papers*, Series B, volume II. ([Center for Israel Education](https://israeled.org/resources/documents/chaim-weizmanns-notes-for-speech-rallying-world-jewry-to-partition/))

- **1941:** *We Do Not Want to Return to the Past*. An address delivered in New York on 26 March 1941 and later circulated under this title; it is an oratorical intervention whose pamphlet form is a later manifestation. Some bibliographies date the named publication to 1946, so speech date and edition date should not be collapsed. ([Encyclopaedia Judaica](https://wit-resources.s3.amazonaws.com/Encyclopaedia/Vol.20_-_To-Wei.pdf))

- **1942:** *Palestine’s Role in the Solution of the Jewish Problem*. A political essay published in *Foreign Affairs*, volume 20, pages 324–338. It is a public-policy argument rather than a freestanding book. ([Encyclopaedia Judaica](https://wit-resources.s3.amazonaws.com/Encyclopaedia/Vol.20_-_To-Wei.pdf))

- **1946:** *We Warned You, Gentlemen: Testimony of Dr. Chaim Weizmann before the Anglo-American Committee of Inquiry on Palestine*. Testimony that acts upon an official inquiry and was subsequently made into a pamphlet. Bibliographies sometimes assign the title to 1947, while the testimony and an identified edition belong to 1946; both dates describe manifestations, not necessarily contradictory composition dates. ([WorldCat work record](https://search.worldcat.org/title/We-warned-you-gentlemen-%3A-testimony-of-Dr.-Chaim-Weizmann-before-the-Anglo-American-Committee-of-Inquiry-on-Palestine/oclc/10246273); [Encyclopaedia Judaica](https://wit-resources.s3.amazonaws.com/Encyclopaedia/Vol.20_-_To-Wei.pdf))

### Correspondence

- **1885–1952:** *Letters*. A vast epistolary œuvre preserved in the Weizmann Archives and selectively published in twenty-three chronological volumes as Series A of *The Letters and Papers of Chaim Weizmann*. The published series is not complete—the archive holds more letters—and individual letters are situated acts between correspondents rather than “works” in the conventional literary sense. ([Center for Israel Education, collection description](https://israeled.org/topic/weizmann-letters/); [volume chronology](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BA29705602.amp))

### Memoir

- **1949:** *Trial and Error: The Autobiography of Chaim Weizmann*. An autobiography and retrospective political self-portrait. Its title, first-edition date and status are stable, although later translations, abridgments and reprints are manifestations of the same work rather than additional works. ([Encyclopaedia Judaica bibliography](https://www.encyclopedia.com/people/history/israeli-history-biographies/chaim-weizmann); [EBSCO Research Starters](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/history/chaim-weizmann))
