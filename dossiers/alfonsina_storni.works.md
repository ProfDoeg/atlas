## Works by Alfonsina Storni

### Poetry collections

- **1916:** *La inquietud del rosal*. First poetry collection, published in Buenos Aires with a prologue by Juan Julián Lastra. [CVC bibliography](https://cvc.cervantes.es/actcult/storni/bibliografia/bibliografia01.htm)
- **1918:** *El dulce daño*. Poetry collection; a second edition appeared in 1920, but that edition is not a separate obra. [CVC bibliography](https://cvc.cervantes.es/actcult/storni/bibliografia/bibliografia01.htm)
- **1919:** *Irremediablemente*. Poetry collection. [CVC bibliography](https://cvc.cervantes.es/actcult/storni/bibliografia/bibliografia01.htm)
- **1920:** *Languidez*. Poetry collection. [CVC bibliography](https://cvc.cervantes.es/actcult/storni/bibliografia/bibliografia01.htm)
- **1925:** *Ocre*. Poetry collection marking a turn toward a more ironic, compressed poetic practice. [CVC bibliography](https://cvc.cervantes.es/actcult/storni/bibliografia/bibliografia01.htm)
- **1926:** *Poemas de amor*. A sequence of prose poems rather than a conventional verse collection; its hybrid form makes “poetry book” an incomplete description. [CVC bibliography](https://cvc.cervantes.es/actcult/storni/bibliografia/bibliografia01.htm)
- **1934:** *Mundo de siete pozos*. Poetry collection; some later sources incorrectly pluralize the first word as *Mundos*. [CVC bibliography](https://cvc.cervantes.es/actcult/storni/bibliografia/bibliografia01.htm)
- **1938:** *Mascarilla y trébol: círculos imantados*. Final poetry collection, organized around poems Storni called “antisonnets”; it appeared in the year of her death. [CVC bibliography](https://cvc.cervantes.es/actcult/storni/bibliografia/bibliografia01.htm)

### Selections made by Storni

- **1920:** *Poesías (seleccionadas e inéditas)*. An authorial selection combining previously printed and then-unpublished poems; unlike a later editorial anthology, the act of selection belongs to Storni. [CVC bibliography](https://cvc.cervantes.es/actcult/storni/bibliografia/bibliografia01.htm)
- **1938:** *Antología poética*. A selection with a prologue by Storni herself and therefore an author-made retrospective configuration of her poetry, not merely a reprint assembled by somebody else. [CVC bibliography](https://cvc.cervantes.es/actcult/storni/bibliografia/bibliografia01.htm)

### Theatre for adults

- **1927:** *El amo del mundo*. Three-act comedy first published in *Bambalinas* on 16 April 1927; its original title was *Dos mujeres*. [CVC bibliography](https://cvc.cervantes.es/actcult/storni/bibliografia/bibliografia01.htm) [Losada collected theatre](https://editoriallosada.com/libro/teatro-2/)
- **c. 1927:** *La debilidad de Mister Dougall*. Completed play that remained unpublished during Storni’s life; sources date its composition only approximately, and it first appeared in print in 1984 according to the specialist collected-theatre account. [Losada collected theatre](https://editoriallosada.com/libro/teatro-2/)
- **1931:** *Cimbelina en el 1900 y pico*. Adult “farsa pirotécnica” published with *Polixena y la cocinerita* in *Dos farsas pirotécnicas*. Bibliographies disagree between 1931 and 1932 for the volume, apparently reflecting publication or cataloguing rather than two different obras. [CVC detailed bibliography](https://cvc.cervantes.es/actcult/storni/bibliografia/bibliografia01.htm) [Cervantes Virtual works bibliography](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonsina_storni/su_obra_bibliografia/) [Losada collected theatre](https://editoriallosada.com/libro/teatro-2/)
- **1931:** *Polixena y la cocinerita*. Adult “farsa pirotécnica,” companion to *Cimbelina en el 1900 y pico*; the same 1931/1932 bibliographical discrepancy applies. [CVC detailed bibliography](https://cvc.cervantes.es/actcult/storni/bibliografia/bibliografia01.htm) [Losada collected theatre](https://editoriallosada.com/libro/teatro-2/)
- **1931/1932:** *Dos farsas pirotécnicas*. The book-object joining *Cimbelina en el 1900 y pico* and *Polixena y la cocinerita*; it is both an obra in its arrangement and a container for two independently nameable plays. [CVC detailed bibliography](https://cvc.cervantes.es/actcult/storni/bibliografia/bibliografia01.htm) [Cervantes Virtual works bibliography](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonsina_storni/su_obra_bibliografia/)

### Theatre for children

- **Date not securely established; published 1950:** *Blanco… Negro… Blanco…*. Children’s play preserved among Storni’s six completed works for young audiences; its posthumous publication date must not be mistaken for a composition date. [CVC bibliography](https://cvc.cervantes.es/actcult/storni/bibliografia/bibliografia01.htm) [Losada collected theatre](https://editoriallosada.com/libro/teatro-2/)
- **Date not securely established; published 1950:** *Pedro y Pedrito*. Children’s play, first collected posthumously in *Teatro infantil*. [CVC bibliography](https://cvc.cervantes.es/actcult/storni/bibliografia/bibliografia01.htm) [Losada collected theatre](https://editoriallosada.com/libro/teatro-2/)
- **Date not securely established; published 1979:** *Jorge y su conciencia*. Dramatic dialogue for children; the surviving bibliographical record supplies a late publication date, not a reliable date of composition. [CVC bibliography](https://cvc.cervantes.es/actcult/storni/bibliografia/bibliografia01.htm) [Losada collected theatre](https://editoriallosada.com/libro/teatro-2/)
- **Date not securely established; published 1950:** *Un sueño en el camino*. Children’s play first collected posthumously in *Teatro infantil*. [CVC bibliography](https://cvc.cervantes.es/actcult/storni/bibliografia/bibliografia01.htm) [Losada collected theatre](https://editoriallosada.com/libro/teatro-2/)
- **1932:** *Los degolladores de estatuas*. Children’s play published in *La Nación* on 13 November 1932. [CVC bibliography](https://cvc.cervantes.es/actcult/storni/bibliografia/bibliografia01.htm) [Losada collected theatre](https://editoriallosada.com/libro/teatro-2/)
- **Date not securely established; published 1942:** *El Dios de los Pájaros*. Children’s play published posthumously in *Ser*; capitalization varies in bibliographic descriptions. [CVC bibliography](https://cvc.cervantes.es/actcult/storni/bibliografia/bibliografia01.htm) [Losada collected theatre](https://editoriallosada.com/libro/teatro-2/)
- **1950:** *Teatro infantil*. Posthumous volume containing *Blanco… Negro… Blanco…*, *Pedro y Pedrito*, and *Un sueño en el camino*. The constituent plays are Storni’s obras, but the posthumous book-object and its selection are editorial creations rather than a securely documented authorial project. [CVC bibliography](https://cvc.cervantes.es/actcult/storni/bibliografia/bibliografia01.htm)

### Unrecovered dramatic projects

- **Dates and titles not fully recoverable:** *Other dramatic works known only through reports*. The specialist collected-theatre edition states that Storni wrote or planned many plays but that only ten completed dramatic texts survive; the absent works resist treatment as a list of extant obras because neither their texts nor a complete, securely titled catalogue survives. [Losada collected theatre](https://editoriallosada.com/libro/teatro-2/)

### Narratives, essays, journalism, and fragmentary prose

- **During her working life, chiefly the 1910s–1930s:** *Periodical prose*. A dispersed body of signed and pseudonymous journalism, editorials, essays, chronicles, “acuarelas,” reflections, and short narrative pieces published in venues including *La Nota* and *La Nación*. This is a body of work rather than a single book, and the modern collected edition is a scholarly reconstruction: its variety and dispersal make any claim that it is a definitively closed corpus unsafe. [Losada collected prose](https://editoriallosada.com/libro/prosa-0/) [Biblioteca del Congreso de la Nación](https://bcn.gob.ar/alfonsina-storni)
- **During her working life, dates varying:** *Short narratives and diary-like prose*. Small stories, diary forms, verbal “snapshots,” and fragmentary meditations preserved by Delfina Muschietti’s research and compilation. These manifestations often resist stable genre boundaries, and they were not assembled by Storni as a single titled obra. [Losada collected prose](https://editoriallosada.com/libro/prosa-0/)
- **1910s–1930s:** *Bocetos femeninos*. A journalistic column or recurring textual project associated with the women’s pages of *La Nación*, rather than a book; individual installments circulated as periodical interventions on gender, labor, fashion, and civic life. Its limits depend upon attribution and archival recovery. [FLACSO study of Storni’s columns](https://repositorio.flacsoandes.edu.ec/server/api/core/bitstreams/94d0a052-5b14-4afc-b633-445277f87a3f/content)
- **1910s–1930s:** *Feminist and civic interventions in the press*. Essays and journalistic texts concerning women’s civil position and social rights; some function less as detached description than as public acts of argument, approaching the force of manifestos without forming one consistently titled manifesto. Their attribution and completeness remain dependent on periodical research. [Cervantes Virtual study of Storni’s journalistic prose](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/descargaPdf/la-prosa-periodistica-de-alfonsina-storni-por-los-derechos-civiles-de-las-mujeres-alfonsina-storni-y-el-campo-intelectual--1219127/) [Losada collected prose](https://editoriallosada.com/libro/prosa-0/)

### Lecture and performed discourse

- **Delivered before 1939; published 1939:** *Entre un par de maletas a medio abrir y la manecilla del reloj*. Conference text published posthumously by José D. Forgione. It is both a written text and the remainder of a performed event; the cited bibliography does not provide a secure delivery date. [CVC bibliography](https://cvc.cervantes.es/actcult/storni/bibliografia/bibliografia01.htm)

### Correspondence

- **Dates varying; published as a selection in 1959:** *Cinco cartas y una golondrina*. The letters are texts Storni made, but the titled posthumous volume is an editorial assemblage and should not be silently treated as an author-designed book. [Cervantes Virtual works bibliography](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonsina_storni/su_obra_bibliografia/)
- **Dates varying:** *Surviving correspondence*. Personal letters constitute documentary creations rather than a deliberately bounded literary obra; archival survival, selection, and later publication determine the visible corpus, so completeness cannot be claimed. [Cervantes Virtual archive categories](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/alfonsina_storni/su_obra_bibliografia/) [Losada collected prose](https://editoriallosada.com/libro/prosa-0/)
