## Works by El Gólem

### Recordings issued as El Golem

- **1998:** *Vol. 1*. Four-song demo cassette, recorded and mixed in Viña del Mar and self-released under the name El Golem; the discographic sources disagree slightly about whether the recording occurred in 1997 or May 1998, but agree on the 1998 release. ([Encyclopaedia Metallum](https://www.metal-archives.com/albums/Der_Golem/Vol._1/48996); [band interview and discographic account](https://metal2012.blogspot.com/2015/11/el-golem-vol-i-1998.html))

- **2003:** *En la mente del Golem*. CD compilation assembled from demos, rehearsals, and live recordings made between 1999 and 2002, including two cover versions; it is catalogued under Der Golem, although contemporary accounts call it an El Golem release and the group’s name change is dated to 2003. ([Encyclopaedia Metallum](https://www.metal-archives.com/albums/Der_Golem/En_la_mente_del_Golem/76963); [band discography](https://www.metal-archives.com/band/discography/id/21568/tab/all))

### Recordings issued as Der Golem

- **2005:** *La muerte (por fin)*. The group’s first full-length album. It belongs to the same continuing band-project as El Golem but appeared after the adoption of the name Der Golem. ([Encyclopaedia Metallum discography](https://www.metal-archives.com/band/discography/id/21568/tab/all))

- **2010:** *Blood of Innocence*. A split release rather than a self-contained Der Golem album; its shared authorship and format make it a collaborative manifestation, not wholly the band’s obra alone. ([Encyclopaedia Metallum discography](https://www.metal-archives.com/band/discography/id/21568/tab/all))

- **2013:** *Ciclos*. Independently released digital full-length album comprising twelve recordings. ([Encyclopaedia Metallum](https://www.metal-archives.com/albums/Der_Golem/Ciclos/362076))

- **2015:** *Cometa*. Independently released digital full-length album comprising eleven recordings. ([Encyclopaedia Metallum](https://www.metal-archives.com/albums/Der_Golem/Cometa/534717))

- **2020:** *Rey Angustia: Demos y Rarezas (1997–2004)*. Digital archival compilation of thirteen demos and rarities recorded across the El Golem/Der Golem transition; it remanifests earlier material rather than constituting thirteen newly made works. ([Encyclopaedia Metallum](https://www.metal-archives.com/albums/Der_Golem/Rey_Angustia:_Demos_y_Rarezas_\(1997-2004\)/1176939))

- **2020:** *En Vivo – Copiapó*. Digital live album released on 28 June 2020 from a performance recorded at Bar RockaRolla in Copiapó on 5 April 2017. Its date therefore divides between the event and its later manifestation as a release. ([Encyclopaedia Metallum](https://www.metal-archives.com/albums/Der_Golem/En_Vivo_-_Copiap%C3%B3/1176933))

- **2021:** *Jardín de cenizas*. Full-length album and the latest release in the consulted complete discography through 2026. ([Encyclopaedia Metallum discography](https://www.metal-archives.com/band/discography/id/21568/tab/all))

### Compilation participation

- **2015:** *Dark Art of Doom and Desolation*. Appearance on a Chilean doom-metal CD compilation reportedly limited to 100 copies. The catalogue does not identify the contributed recording on its band page, so this is best classified as a documented participation rather than a securely delimited standalone obra. ([Encyclopaedia Metallum band record](https://www.metal-archives.com/bands/Der_Golem/21568))
