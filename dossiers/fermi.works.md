## Works by Enrico Fermi

### Scientific papers and research reports

- **1921–1954:** *Scientific papers, reports, lectures, and addresses collected as Note e memorie (Collected Papers).* This two-volume obra comprises Fermi’s numbered scientific writings from his Italian and American periods, including jointly authored papers and formerly classified wartime reports; the editors described the collection as extensive rather than absolutely complete. Individual papers, whose existence and titles are indexed in the volumes, are treated here as parts of this scientific corpus rather than as later editions. ([Accademia Nazionale dei Lincei, vol. I](https://operedigitali.lincei.it/Fermi/Fermi_omnia1_Vol1_1_Frontespizio_Indice.pdf); [vols. I–II portal](https://operedigitali.lincei.it/Fermi/index.php); [Netlib analytical bibliography](https://www.netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.html))

- **1921:** *Sulla dinamica di un sistema rigido di cariche elettriche in moto traslatorio*. A research paper on relativistic electrodynamics and Fermi’s first numbered paper in the collected edition. ([Collected Papers bibliography](https://www.netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.html))

- **1921:** *Sull’elettrostatica di un campo gravitazionale uniforme e sul peso delle masse elettromagnetiche*. A research paper on gravitation, electrostatics, and electromagnetic mass, described in the scholarly bibliography as Fermi’s first scientific publication. ([Collected Papers bibliography](https://www.netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.html))

- **1922:** *Correzione di una grave discrepanza tra la teoria delle masse elettromagnetiche e la teoria della relatività: inerzia e peso dell’elettricità, I–II*. A two-part research paper on electromagnetic mass and relativity. ([Collected Papers bibliography](https://www.netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.html))

- **1922–1923:** *Un teorema di calcolo delle probabilità ed alcune sue applicazioni* and related ergodic investigations. These papers form an early mathematical-physics project concerning probability and dynamical systems. ([Collected Papers, vol. I](https://operedigitali.lincei.it/Fermi/index.php))

- **1922–1923:** *Raggi Röntgen* and *Formazione di immagini coi raggi Röntgen*. These experimental papers on X-ray diffraction and image formation substantially embody Fermi’s university thesis; their status lies between thesis, experiment, and journal article. ([Collected Papers bibliography](https://www.netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.html))

- **1923:** *Le masse nella teoria della relatività*. An appendix to August Kopff’s Italian exposition of relativity, hence a contribution inside another author’s book rather than an autonomous book by Fermi. ([Collected Papers bibliography](https://www.netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.html))

- **1923:** *Generalizzazione del teorema di Poincaré sopra la non esistenza di integrali uniformi di un sistema di equazioni canoniche normali*. A mathematical-physics article generalizing a theorem of Poincaré. ([Collected Papers bibliography](https://www.netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.html))

- **1923:** *Il principio delle adiabatiche ed i sistemi che non ammettono coordinate angolari*. A theoretical paper on the adiabatic principle. ([Collected Papers bibliography](https://www.netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.html))

- **1924:** *Sopra la riflessione e la diffusione della risonanza*. A paper on resonance radiation associated with Fermi’s Göttingen-period work. ([Collected Papers, vol. I](https://operedigitali.lincei.it/Fermi/index.php))

- **1924:** *Über die Wahrscheinlichkeit der Quantenzustände*. A statistical-mechanics paper that anticipates elements of Fermi’s later quantum-statistical project. ([Collected Papers, vol. I](https://operedigitali.lincei.it/Fermi/index.php))

- **1924–1925:** *Über die Theorie des Stoßes zwischen Atomen und elektrisch geladenen Teilchen* / *Sulla teoria dell’urto tra atomi e corpuscoli elettricamente carichi*. German and Italian manifestations of Fermi’s collision theory; the Italian version was omitted from the collected edition, so the two texts should not be mistaken for two unrelated discoveries. ([Bini and Geralico](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0205086))

- **1925:** *Sopra la teoria dei corpi solidi*. A conference-derived exposition on solid-state theory, occupying an ambiguous position between original paper and pedagogical lecture. ([Collected Papers bibliography](https://www.netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.html); [historical study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.12417))

- **1926:** *Sulla quantizzazione del gas perfetto monoatomico*. The foundational paper introducing what became Fermi–Dirac statistics; it is a theoretical research article, not a book-length “work.” ([Collected Papers, vol. I](https://operedigitali.lincei.it/Fermi/index.php))

- **1926–1928:** *Statistical-mechanics and atomic-structure papers based on Fermi statistics.* This research project includes Fermi’s applications of the exclusion principle and statistical distribution to ideal gases, atomic structure, and the Thomas–Fermi model. Its boundaries are porous because several articles restate or extend the same method. ([Collected Papers, vol. I](https://operedigitali.lincei.it/Fermi/index.php))

- **1927:** *Un metodo statistico per la determinazione di alcune proprietà dell’atomo*. A research paper establishing the statistical atomic model now called the Thomas–Fermi model; the model is jointly named because Llewellyn Thomas developed an independent formulation. ([Collected Papers, vol. I](https://operedigitali.lincei.it/Fermi/index.php))

- **1928–1932:** *Quantum electrodynamics and radiation papers.* An obra made of interdependent articles on quantization of the electromagnetic field, radiation, selection rules, and atomic spectra; treating each manifestation as wholly independent obscures the continuity of Fermi’s project. ([Collected Papers, vol. I](https://operedigitali.lincei.it/Fermi/index.php))

- **1929:** *Sul moto di un corpo di massa variabile*. A short theoretical paper on variable-mass motion. ([Collected Papers bibliography](https://www.netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.html))

- **1929–1930:** *Sopra l’elettrodinamica quantistica*. Two related papers on quantum electrodynamics, distinguishable bibliographically but belonging to one continuing investigation. ([Collected Papers bibliography](https://www.netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.html))

- **1930:** *Atomi e stelle*. A public scientific address or essay connecting atomic and stellar physics, not simply a research article. ([Collected Papers bibliography](https://www.netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.html))

- **1930:** *La fisica moderna*. A general scientific essay addressed beyond the specialist research community. ([Collected Papers bibliography](https://www.netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.html))

- **1930:** *I fondamenti sperimentali della nuova meccanica atomica*. A pedagogical survey of the experimental basis of quantum mechanics. ([Collected Papers bibliography](https://www.netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.html))

- **1930:** *L’interpretazione del principio di causalità nella meccanica quantistica* and *Sul principio di causalità nella meccanica ondulatoria*. Closely related philosophical-physical papers on causality in quantum theory. ([Collected Papers bibliography](https://www.netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.html))

- **1930:** *Sui momenti magnetici dei nuclei atomici*. A research memoir on nuclear magnetic moments. ([Istituto Superiore di Sanità authority record](https://arch.iss.it/detail/IT-ISS-EACCPF0001-000143/fermi-enrico))

- **1930:** *Sul calcolo degli spettri degli ioni*. A research memoir on calculating ionic spectra. ([Istituto Superiore di Sanità authority record](https://arch.iss.it/detail/IT-ISS-EACCPF0001-000143/fermi-enrico))

- **1932:** *L’effetto Raman nelle molecole e nei cristalli*. A research memoir on the Raman effect in molecules and crystals. ([Istituto Superiore di Sanità authority record](https://arch.iss.it/detail/IT-ISS-EACCPF0001-000143/fermi-enrico))

- **1933:** *Sulla teoria delle strutture iperfini*. A jointly authored research memoir by Fermi and Emilio Segrè on atomic hyperfine structure. ([Istituto Superiore di Sanità authority record](https://arch.iss.it/detail/IT-ISS-EACCPF0001-000143/fermi-enrico))

- **1933–1934:** *Tentativo di una teoria dei raggi β* / *Versuch einer Theorie der β-Strahlen*. The Italian and German manifestations of Fermi’s beta-decay theory; they introduce the interaction later called the Fermi interaction and should be treated as versions of one theoretical obra. ([Collected Papers translation index](https://wiki.ingeg.it/enrico-fermi-translations); [Collected Papers, vol. I](https://operedigitali.lincei.it/Fermi/index.php))

- **1934:** *Radioattività indotta da bombardamento di neutroni* and successive communications on neutron-induced radioactivity. A collaborative experimental project by Fermi and the Rome group, published through a rapid sequence of notes with changing coauthor lists; assigning every result to Fermi alone would misstate its authorship. ([Collected Papers, vol. I](https://operedigitali.lincei.it/Fermi/index.php); [bibliographic note on the translated communications](https://www.netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.html))

- **1934:** *Possible Production of Elements of Atomic Number Higher than 92*. A report interpreting neutron-bombardment results; its proposed transuranic identification was later shown to be incomplete because some products came from nuclear fission. ([Collected Papers, vol. I](https://operedigitali.lincei.it/Fermi/index.php))

- **1934:** *Conferencias sobre física atómica*. Five Buenos Aires lectures preserved through recordings and Spanish transcriptions, with related lectures at Córdoba and La Plata surviving only in summaries. They are manifestations mediated by recorders and editors, were listed but not reproduced in the original collected papers, and therefore resist uncomplicated attribution as fixed authored texts. ([De Angelis and Kenny](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.13368))

- **1934–1936:** *Slow-neutron research.* A collaborative experimental project showing that hydrogenous moderators greatly increase neutron-induced activity and developing the associated diffusion and absorption theory; the individual notes are manifestations of collective laboratory work rather than solitary works by Fermi. ([Collected Papers, vol. I](https://operedigitali.lincei.it/Fermi/index.php))

- **1938:** *Artificial Radioactivity Produced by Neutron Bombardment*. Fermi’s Nobel lecture, a retrospective scientific address describing the Rome group’s work rather than a new experimental paper. ([Nobel Prize lecture](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/1938/fermi/lecture/))

- **1939–1941:** *Neutron production, absorption, and diffusion papers at Columbia University.* A research corpus that joined experimental measurements to the design problem of obtaining a self-sustaining chain reaction. ([Collected Papers, vol. II](https://operedigitali.lincei.it/Fermi/index.php))

- **1941–1946:** *Metallurgical Laboratory and Manhattan Project reports.* Classified reports, memoranda, lecture notes, meeting notes, and calculations on neutron diffusion, exponential piles, reproduction factors, cooling, shielding, control, and reactor engineering. The collected edition printed only part of this dispersed documentary obra; later research recovered roughly 600 additional pages written by Fermi and collaborators, so completeness and individual authorship remain unsettled. ([Esposito and Pisanti](https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.1145); [University of Chicago archival guide](https://www.lib.uchicago.edu/e/scrc/findingaids/view.php?eadid=ICU.SPCL.FERMI))

- **1942:** *The Determination of the Ratio between the Absorption Cross-sections of Metal and Carbon for Thermal Neutrons*. A classified technical report, C-84. ([Collected Papers bibliography](https://www.netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.html))

- **1942:** *Neutron Reproduction in a Lattice of Uranium and Graphite: Theoretical Part*. A reactor-design report, C-12; the bibliography also records a substantially identical title as CP-12, creating a duplicate-manifestation problem rather than two securely distinct works. ([Collected Papers bibliography](https://www.netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.html))

- **1942:** *Slowing Down and Diffusion of Neutrons*. A classified theoretical report, C-29. ([Collected Papers bibliography](https://www.netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.html))

- **1942:** *Methods of Cooling Chain Reacting Piles*. A technical memorandum, CP-10, functioning as an engineering protocol and design proposal. ([Collected Papers bibliography](https://www.netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.html))

- **1942:** *Memorandum to S. K. Allison with Reference to Designs and Methods of Cooling of Chain Reacting Piles*. A six-page engineering memorandum; it overlaps conceptually with *Methods of Cooling Chain Reacting Piles*, and the surviving records do not justify silently collapsing them. ([Collected Papers bibliography](https://www.netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.html))

- **1942:** *The Projected Experiment at Argonne Forest and the Reproduction Factor in Metal Piles*. A jointly authored project report whose bibliographic record itself questions the author order. ([Collected Papers bibliography](https://www.netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.html))

- **1942:** *The Experimental Chain Reacting Pile and Reproduction Factor in Some Exponential Piles*. A collective monthly-report contribution; the bibliography records Fermi “and others” and flags the author order as uncertain. ([Collected Papers bibliography](https://www.netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.html))

- **1942:** *Feasibility of a Chain Reaction*. A classified technical report, CP-383; bibliographic evidence preserves uncertainty about whether Alvin Weinberg and Eugene Wigner should be named as coauthors. ([Collected Papers bibliography](https://www.netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.html))

- **1942:** *Chicago Pile-1*. The first artificial reactor and first controlled self-sustaining nuclear chain reaction: a built experimental apparatus, operational protocol, and collective project led by Fermi, not a text and not solely his fabrication. ([U.S. Department of Energy](https://www.energy.gov/lm/doe-history/manhattan-project-background-information-and-preservation-work/manhattan-project-signature-facilities/chicago-pile-1); [Atomic Heritage Foundation](https://ahf.nuclearmuseum.org/ahf/history/chicago-pile-1/))

- **1943:** *Tests on a Shield for the W Pile*. A jointly authored engineering report by Fermi and Walter H. Zinn. ([Collected Papers bibliography](https://www.netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.html))

- **1943:** *The Utilization of Heavy Hydrogen in Nuclear Chain Reactions*. A technical report jointly authored with Harold C. Urey. ([Collected Papers bibliography](https://www.netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.html))

- **1944:** *Chicago Pile-3*. A heavy-water-moderated reactor produced by a team under Fermi’s scientific direction; it is an engineered collective obra rather than an individually authored object. ([Argonne National Laboratory](https://www.anl.gov/article/remembering-chicago-pile3-the-worlds-first-heavy-water-reactor))

- **1944–1945:** *Los Alamos neutron and hydrodynamics work*. A body of calculations, experiments, meeting contributions, and reports produced within the Manhattan Project; classification, collective authorship, and uneven survival make it resist treatment as a closed list of personal “works.” ([Collected Papers, vol. II](https://operedigitali.lincei.it/Fermi/index.php); [University of Chicago archival guide](https://www.lib.uchicago.edu/e/scrc/findingaids/view.php?eadid=ICU.SPCL.FERMI))

- **1945:** *Trinity-test yield experiment*. Fermi’s paper-drop measurement was an experimental act and improvised measuring protocol, not a freestanding publication; it yielded an immediate estimate of the explosion’s energy. ([Los Alamos National Laboratory](https://discover.lanl.gov/publications/national-security-science/2020-summer/the-trinity-test/))

- **1946–1954:** *Cosmic-ray acceleration and galactic magnetic-field papers.* A theoretical research project proposing stochastic acceleration by moving magnetic structures and developing its astrophysical consequences. ([Collected Papers, vol. II](https://operedigitali.lincei.it/Fermi/index.php))

- **1948:** *On the Origin of the Cosmic Radiation*. A Physical Review paper presenting the mechanism now called second-order Fermi acceleration. ([APS Physical Review](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.75.1169))

- **1948:** *Note on Census-Taking in Monte-Carlo Calculations*. A Los Alamos report jointly authored with Robert D. Richtmyer, concerning population control in Monte Carlo computation. ([University of Pennsylvania Online Books Page](https://onlinebooks.library.upenn.edu/webbin/book/lookupname?key=Fermi%2C%20Enrico%2C%201901-1954))

- **1949:** *On the Origin of the Cosmic Radiation*. A fuller Physical Review article developing the cosmic-ray acceleration theory; it is related to, but not merely an edition of, the 1948 paper. ([APS Physical Review](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.75.1169))

- **1949:** *Are Mesons Elementary Particles?* A theoretical paper jointly authored with Chen Ning Yang, proposing a nucleon–antinucleon model of the pion. ([University of Chicago archival guide](https://www.lib.uchicago.edu/e/scrc/findingaids/view.php?eadid=ICU.SPCL.FERMI))

- **1950:** *High-Energy Nuclear Events*. A statistical theory of multiple particle production, initiating Fermi’s high-energy collision model. ([Collected Papers, vol. II](https://operedigitali.lincei.it/Fermi/index.php))

- **1951:** *Angular Distribution of the Pions Produced in High Energy Nuclear Collisions*. A research paper on pion production and angular distributions. ([University of Chicago archival guide](https://www.lib.uchicago.edu/e/scrc/findingaids/view.php?eadid=ICU.SPCL.FERMI))

- **1951–1954:** *Pion–nucleon scattering investigations.* A collaborative experimental and phenomenological project using the Chicago synchrocyclotron; individual papers have varying combinations of Fermi, Herbert Anderson, Darol Nagle, Gerson Yodh, and other collaborators. ([Collected Papers, vol. II](https://operedigitali.lincei.it/Fermi/index.php))

- **1953:** *A Course in Neutron Physics*. A lecture-based scientific text represented by student or editorial notes rather than a uniquely fixed authorial manuscript. ([University of Chicago archival guide](https://www.lib.uchicago.edu/e/scrc/findingaids/view.php?eadid=ICU.SPCL.FERMI))

- **1953:** *Studies of Nonlinear Problems*. A Los Alamos report by Fermi, John Pasta, and Stanislaw Ulam, with programming by Mary Tsingou; the numerical experiment and its computational implementation are a collective creation, conventionally but incompletely called the “Fermi–Pasta–Ulam” problem. ([Los Alamos National Laboratory](https://discover.lanl.gov/publications/national-security-science/2022-spring/its-not-a-problem/))

- **1954:** *Multiple Production of Pions in Pion–Nucleon Collisions*. A high-energy-physics paper extending Fermi’s statistical collision model. ([Netlib bibliography](https://netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.pdf))

- **1954:** *Multiple Production of Pions in Nucleon–Nucleon Collisions at Cosmotron Energies*. A Physical Review communication on multiple-pion production. ([Netlib bibliography](https://netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.pdf))

- **1954:** *Polarization in the Elastic Scattering of High Energy Protons by Nuclei*. A private communication dated 24 March and printed posthumously in the collected papers; it is not identical as an archival object to the published Italian paper that followed. ([Netlib bibliography](https://netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.pdf))

- **1954:** *Polarization of High Energy Protons Scattered by Nuclei*. A published research article in *Il Nuovo Cimento*. ([Netlib bibliography](https://netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.pdf))

- **1954:** *Galactic Magnetic Fields and the Origin of Cosmic Radiation*. A late astrophysical paper extending Fermi’s cosmic-ray project. ([Netlib bibliography](https://netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.pdf))

- **1954:** *Los Alamos Laboratory Has Deserved the Gratitude of This Nation*. A public letter in the *Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists*; it is an intervention in public affairs rather than a research work. ([Netlib bibliography](https://netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.pdf))

- **1954:** *Lezioni Varenna*. Lectures from the Varenna summer school; the bibliography supplies only this generic title, so a more specific title should not be invented. ([Netlib bibliography](https://netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.pdf))

- **May 1954:** *Untitled article for the U.S. Information Service*. The bibliography records the object only as “[unknown]”; its resistance to naming is part of its documentary status. ([Netlib bibliography](https://netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.pdf))

### Books, textbooks, and lecture-text manifestations

- **1928:** *Introduzione alla fisica atomica*. A book-length introduction to atomic physics. ([Istituto Superiore di Sanità authority record](https://arch.iss.it/detail/IT-ISS-EACCPF0001-000143/fermi-enrico))

- **1929:** *Fisica ad uso dei licei*. A two-volume school textbook; later plans to translate and revise it for American schools remained an abandoned project. ([Istituto Superiore di Sanità authority record](https://arch.iss.it/detail/IT-ISS-EACCPF0001-000143/fermi-enrico); [University of Chicago archival guide](https://www.lib.uchicago.edu/e/scrc/findingaids/view.php?eadid=ICU.SPCL.FERMI))

- **1934:** *Molecole e cristalli*. A monograph connecting molecular, crystalline, and quantum-statistical physics. ([Istituto Superiore di Sanità authority record](https://arch.iss.it/detail/IT-ISS-EACCPF0001-000143/fermi-enrico))

- **1936–1937:** *Thermodynamics*. A book made from Fermi’s Columbia University lectures; because the printed text was mediated through lecture notes, it is both a course manifestation and an attributed book. ([University of Chicago Press](https://press.uchicago.edu/ucp/books/book/chicago/T/bo3684681.html))

- **1938:** *Fisica per istituti tecnici*. A physics textbook for Italian technical schools. ([Enrico Fermi publication list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enrico_Fermi#Bibliography))

- **1938:** *Fisica per licei scientifici*. A school physics textbook jointly authored with Edoardo Amaldi. ([Enrico Fermi publication list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enrico_Fermi#Bibliography))

- **1945–1948:** *Neutron Physics: A Revision of I. Halpern’s Notes on E. Fermi’s Lectures in 1945*. A course manifestation edited by J. G. Beckerley and issued by the U.S. Atomic Energy Commission; the wording belongs to mediated lecture notes, so it is not straightforwardly a manuscript authored line by line by Fermi. ([University of Chicago archival guide](https://www.lib.uchicago.edu/e/scrc/findingaids/view.php?eadid=ICU.SPCL.FERMI))

- **1950:** *Conferenze di fisica atomica*. A collection of atomic-physics lectures assembled by professors and assistants from the Universities of Rome and Milan; its collective transcription and editing complicate simple authorship. ([Istituto Superiore di Sanità authority record](https://arch.iss.it/detail/IT-ISS-EACCPF0001-000143/fermi-enrico))

- **1950:** *Nuclear Physics: A Course Given by Enrico Fermi at the University of Chicago*. A printed course reconstructed from lectures, hence a pedagogical manifestation rather than a conventional manuscript monograph. ([University of Chicago Press](https://press.uchicago.edu/ucp/books/book/chicago/N/bo3630330.html))

- **1951:** *Elementary Particles*. A book based on Fermi’s Yale lectures and concerned with particle and nuclear physics. ([Yale University Press](https://yalebooks.yale.edu/book/9780300094749/elementary-particles/))

- **1954:** *Quantum Mechanics*. Lectures delivered to University of Chicago students and reportedly preserved in only a few institutional copies; the later *Notes on Quantum Mechanics* is a posthumous editorial manifestation. ([Netlib bibliography](https://netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.pdf); [University of Chicago archival guide](https://www.lib.uchicago.edu/e/scrc/findingaids/view.php?eadid=ICU.SPCL.FERMI))

- **Date not fixed:** *Lezioni di elettrodinamica*. A 95-page lithographed lecture text whose scholarly bibliography gives the year only as “19xx”; no more precise date should be supplied without further evidence. ([Collected Papers bibliography](https://www.netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.html))

### Patented inventions and engineering protocols

- **1940:** *Process for the Production of Radioactive Substances* (U.S. Patent 2,206,634). A patented neutron-irradiation process credited jointly to Fermi and collaborators; a patent is simultaneously a legal claim, technical description, and protocol. ([Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US2206634A/en))

- **1950:** *Neutron Velocity Selector* (U.S. Patent 2,524,379). A patented apparatus for selecting neutrons by velocity. ([Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US2524379A/en))

- **1953:** *Neutronic Reactor* (U.S. Patent 2,852,461). A jointly patented reactor design; the grant postdates much of the underlying wartime work. ([Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US2852461A/en))

- **1955, posthumous grant:** *Neutronic Reactor* (U.S. Patent 2,708,656). A reactor patent granted after Fermi’s death, illustrating why patent date and date of invention are not interchangeable. ([Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US2708656A/en))

- **1956, posthumous grant:** *Testing Material in a Neutronic Reactor* (U.S. Patent 2,768,134). A patented testing procedure and reactor arrangement. ([Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US2768134A/en))

- **1957, posthumous grant:** *Test Exponential Pile* (U.S. Patent 2,780,595). A patented experimental pile for measuring reactor-lattice properties. ([Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US2780595A/en))

- **1957, posthumous grant:** *Method of Operating a Neutronic Reactor* (U.S. Patent 2,798,847). An operational protocol expressed as patent claims. ([Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US2798847A/en))

- **1957, posthumous grant:** *Neutronic Reactor* (U.S. Patent 2,807,581). A reactor design whose legal manifestation appeared after Fermi’s death. ([Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US2807581A/en))

- **1957, posthumous grant:** *Neutronic Reactor Shield* (U.S. Patent 2,807,727). A patented radiation-shielding arrangement for a reactor. ([Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US2807727A/en))

- **1957, posthumous grant:** *Method of Sustaining a Neutronic Chain Reacting System* (U.S. Patent 2,813,070). A protocol for maintaining a chain reaction, cast as a legal and technical object. ([Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US2813070A/en))

- **1958, posthumous grant:** *Air Cooled Neutronic Reactor* (U.S. Patent 2,836,554). A patented air-cooled reactor design arising from earlier wartime engineering. ([Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US2836554A/en))

- **1958, posthumous grant:** *Chain Reacting System* (U.S. Patent 2,837,477). A patented nuclear chain-reacting system. ([Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US2837477A/en))

- **1960, posthumous grant:** *Neutronic Reactor* (U.S. Patent 2,931,762). A reactor patent whose delayed grant is a later legal manifestation of work done during Fermi’s lifetime. ([Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US2931762A/en))

- **1961, posthumous grant:** *Method of Testing Thermal-Neutron Fissionable Material for Purity* (U.S. Patent 2,969,307). A patented measurement protocol, granted more than six years after Fermi’s death. ([Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US2969307A/en))

### Unfinished, informal, or attribution-resistant creations

- **1940s:** *Unpublished pile-physics documents*. Approximately 600 pages of reports, patent drafts, meeting notes, calculations, and memoranda were recovered outside the original collected edition. Because many are collective, classified, untitled, or preserved in several versions, they resist reduction to a stable list of individually authored works. ([Esposito and Pisanti](https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.1145))

- **1941–1954:** *Unpublished articles, lectures, and addresses*. The University of Chicago archive preserves texts and notes that were never formally published; some are complete texts, others only preparatory traces, so their status ranges from obra to unfinished project. ([University of Chicago archival guide](https://www.lib.uchicago.edu/e/scrc/findingaids/view.php?eadid=ICU.SPCL.FERMI))

- **1940s–1954:** *Lecture transparencies, slides, punched tape, diagrams, and experimental visual aids*. These are working and pedagogical objects rather than conventional texts, including ordered slide sequences depicting the first pile and the Chicago cyclotron. ([University of Chicago archival guide](https://www.lib.uchicago.edu/e/scrc/findingaids/view.php?eadid=ICU.SPCL.FERMI))

- **1950:** *The “Fermi paradox” conversation*. The famous lunchtime question about extraterrestrial civilizations survives through later recollection rather than a text made and fixed by Fermi; it is better classified as an attributed conversational creation whose exact wording and argumentative form remain disputed. ([Los Alamos National Laboratory](https://www.lanl.gov/media/publications/national-security-science/2023-spring/fermis-paradox))

- **1954:** *Notes on Thermodynamics and Statistics*. A teaching manuscript prepared for a University of Chicago course and published only posthumously; its final textual form therefore includes editorial mediation beyond Fermi’s lifetime. ([Netlib bibliography](https://www.netlib.org/bibnet/authors/f/fermi-enrico.html))
