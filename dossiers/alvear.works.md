## Works by Carlos María de Alvear

### Military treatises and campaign accounts

- **1813:** *Elementos de táctica terrestre o evoluciones de un ejército en campaña*. An unpublished sixty-two-page manuscript manual of battlefield tactics; the Archivo General de la Nación identifies it as Alvear’s work, while Gregorio F. Rodríguez describes the surviving manuscript as a complete treatise rather than notes toward one. ([Archivo General de la Nación](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/archivos_privados.tomoi__0.pdf); [Rodríguez, *Historia de Alvear*](https://es.scribd.com/document/1036209379/historia-de-Alvear))

- **1827–1828:** *Exposición que hace el general Alvear para contestar al mensaje del gobierno del 14 de septiembre de 1827*. A book-length military and political defence of Alvear’s command in the war with Brazil, commonly shortened in bibliographies to *Exposición del General Alvear*. Its date is sometimes given as 1828, although its title answers a government message of 1827; the archival “memoria … sobre la Guerra del Brasil” may be this work or closely related manuscript material, so it should not automatically be counted as a separate work. ([Google Books](https://books.google.com/books/about/Exposici%C3%B3n_que_hace_el_general_Alvear_p.html?id=QZdxAAAAMAAJ); [Archivo General de la Nación](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/archivos_privados.tomoi__0.pdf))

### Public vindications and polemical pamphlets

- **1814:** *Exposición que hace el señor brigadier D. Carlos Alvear, general en jefe del ejército sitiador de Montevideo, de su conducta en la rendición de esta plaza, vulnerada por las falsas imputaciones de su gobernador D. Gaspar Vigodet*. A printed public vindication of Alvear’s conduct at Montevideo: part military report, part polemical intervention. The original is dated 1814; later facsimile reprints are editions, not additional works. ([Buenos Aires archival catalogue](https://expydoc.com/doc/6161931/descargar---ministerio-del-interior); [Parlamento del Uruguay](https://pmb.parlamento.gub.uy/pmb/opac_css/index.php?id=76262&lvl=categ_see))

- **10 January 1819:** *Refutación de la calumnia intentada contra don Carlos Alvear, inserta en la Extraordinaria de Buenos Aires del 28 de diciembre de 1818*. A ten-page political self-defence issued by the Imprenta Federal in Montevideo. The printers’ note says they received a copy of Alvear’s reflections from correspondents, leaving the precise editorial mediation visible even though the text speaks in Alvear’s name. ([Guillermo Feliú Cruz, *La Imprenta Federal de William P. Griswold y John Sharpe*](https://josemiguelcarrera.cl/home/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Imprenta-Federal.pdf))

- **18 March 1819:** *Otras calumnias refutadas*. A twelve-page continuation of Alvear’s public defence against the Buenos Aires government and press, issued by the same Montevideo press. Contemporary bibliography treats it as a separate pamphlet, not merely a second edition of the January *Refutación*. ([Guillermo Feliú Cruz, *La Imprenta Federal de William P. Griswold y John Sharpe*](https://josemiguelcarrera.cl/home/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Imprenta-Federal.pdf); [Library of Congress, *Handbook of Latin American Studies*](https://www.loc.gov/item/hlas-bi82102838/))

- **1819:** *Observaciones sobre la defensa de la provincia de Buenos Aires*. A printed military-political memorandum on the province’s defence, published by the Imprenta de Mayo. The standard list of Alvear’s writings supplies the year and title but not a more exact date. ([Bibliography of Alvear’s writings](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Mar%C3%ADa_de_Alvear#Bibliograf%C3%ADa))

### Diplomatic instruments

- **25 January 1815:** *Note to Lord Strangford*. A signed diplomatic instrument entrusted to Manuel José García and proposing British protection for the Río de la Plata. Its authorship resists the simple category of “work”: Alvear signed and authorized it as Supreme Director, but Nicolás Herrera is reported to have drafted it, and García did not formally deliver the original. ([Manuel José García biographical account](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manuel_Jos%C3%A9_Garc%C3%ADa); [José María Rosa, *La misión García ante Lord Strangford*](https://books.google.com/books/about/La_misi%C3%B3n_Garc%C3%ADa_ante_Lord_Strangford.html?id=h9E0AAAAIAAJ))

- **25 January 1815:** *Note to Lord Castlereagh*. The companion diplomatic instrument addressed to Britain’s foreign secretary, likewise signed and commissioned by Alvear but reportedly drafted by Herrera. Scholarship disputes how literally its language represented Alvear’s intended policy, while the documentary act—authorization of García to carry it—remains attributable to his government. ([Manuel José García biographical account](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manuel_Jos%C3%A9_Garc%C3%ADa); [José María Rosa, *La misión García ante Lord Strangford*](https://books.google.com/books/about/La_misi%C3%B3n_Garc%C3%ADa_ante_Lord_Strangford.html?id=h9E0AAAAIAAJ))

### Disputed attribution and pseudo-work

- **Published by 1819; purportedly earlier:** *Representación de D. Carlos María de Alvear dirigida al ministro español en Río de Janeiro*. A memorial printed and circulated under Alvear’s name but repudiated by him in *Otras calumnias refutadas* as a fabrication bearing a false signature. It therefore resists inclusion as an authored obra: its historical existence and attribution to Alvear are documented, but his authorship is expressly disputed. ([Library of Congress, *Handbook of Latin American Studies*](https://www.loc.gov/item/hlas-bi82102838/); [Guillermo Feliú Cruz, *La Imprenta Federal de William P. Griswold y John Sharpe*](https://josemiguelcarrera.cl/home/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Imprenta-Federal.pdf))

### Archival documentary corpus

- **1812–1852:** *Official communications, proclamations, speeches, diplomatic papers, and correspondence*. This is a documentary corpus rather than a single work: it includes such individually occasioned objects as Alvear’s 1814 proclamation to his troops, his plenipotentiary communications of 1823–1827, military orders, official reports, and private or confidential letters. Rodríguez’s documentary edition and the Fondo Carlos María de Alvear preserve and organize these materials, but neither converts the whole administrative residue of a military and diplomatic career into one authored book. ([*Archivo Artigas*, vol. XV](https://studylib.es/doc/8707307/comision-nacional-archivo-artigas-archivo-artigas); [Rodríguez, *Contribución histórica y documental*, vol. II](https://books.google.com/books?id=0qpoAAAAMAAJ); [Archivo General de la Nación](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/archivos_privados.tomoi__0.pdf))
