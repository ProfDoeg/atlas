## Works by Giuseppe Maria Garibaldi

### Autobiographical projects

- **1849–1872:** *Memorie*. An evolving autobiographical project begun at Tangier after the fall of the Roman Republic and repeatedly revised; it is not one stable text but a succession of manuscript redactions. Alexandre Dumas’s 1860 publication is an adaptation mediated by Dumas rather than an authorially definitive manifestation, while the National Edition prints both an earlier redaction and Garibaldi’s final 1872 text. [Brown University Library](https://library.brown.edu/cds/garibaldi/resources/scholarship/Pecout.php), [Rassegna degli Archivi di Stato](https://dgagaeta.cultura.gov.it/public/uploads/documents/Rassegna/67f4e3aadaede.pdf)

- **1872:** *Le memorie di Garibaldi nella redazione definitiva del 1872*. The final authorial manifestation of the memoir project, extended through the Vosges campaign and politically reworked from the earlier versions; although completed in 1872, it first appeared in an integral Italian edition posthumously in 1888. [National Edition record](https://opac.biblio.polimi.it/SebinaOpac/resource/edizione-nazionale-degli-scritti-di-giuseppe-garibaldi/PMI0014034), [ALAI](https://www.alai.it/dettaglio.php?ev_id=428&lang_id=2)

### Historical and political novels

- **1867–1870:** *Clelia, ovvero Il governo dei preti* (also published as *Clelia. Il governo del monaco*). A historical-political and explicitly anticlerical novel, the first of Garibaldi’s four novels; variation in its subtitle belongs to its publication history and should not be mistaken for a second work. [Giulia Iannuzzi, “Cantoni il volontario. Garibaldi romanziere e i generi popolari”](https://www.italianisti.it/pubblicazioni/atti-di-congresso/la-letteratura-degli-italiani-rotte-confini-passaggi/Iannuzzi%20Giulia_1.pdf)

- **1868–1869:** *Cantoni il volontario*. A completed historical-popular novel, published in 1870, that combines an invented love plot with Garibaldi’s memories of the campaigns of 1848–49 and 1867; its historical protagonist, Achille Cantoni, is deliberately transformed into a romantic hero. [Giulia Iannuzzi](https://www.italianisti.it/pubblicazioni/atti-di-congresso/la-letteratura-degli-italiani-rotte-confini-passaggi/Iannuzzi%20Giulia_1.pdf)

- **1874:** *I Mille*. A historical novel and memoir hybrid about the 1860 expedition: scholarship treats it as the third novel, but personal chronicle increasingly displaces fiction, so it resists clean separation into “novel” or “autobiography.” It forms volume III of the National Edition. [Giulia Iannuzzi](https://www.italianisti.it/pubblicazioni/atti-di-congresso/la-letteratura-degli-italiani-rotte-confini-passaggi/Iannuzzi%20Giulia_1.pdf), [Rassegna degli Archivi di Stato](https://dgagaeta.cultura.gov.it/public/uploads/documents/Rassegna/67f4e3aadaede.pdf)

- **1873–1881:** *Manlio*. Garibaldi’s fourth and last novel, a historical-political adventure projected into the future and apparently written over several late periods at Caprera; left unpublished at his death, its manuscript history and precise state of completion remain controversial, and the first edition appeared only in 1982. [Giulia Iannuzzi](https://www.italianisti.it/pubblicazioni/atti-di-congresso/la-letteratura-degli-italiani-rotte-confini-passaggi/Iannuzzi%20Giulia_1.pdf), [Paolo Sorba Editore](https://www.paolosorbaeditore.it/libro/manlio/)

### Drama

- **1879:** *Elisabetta d’Ungheria*. A historical drama in five acts rather than a novel or memoir; its presence in the wider Garibaldian bibliography, but absence from the six original volumes of the National Edition, makes the latter an incomplete inventory of his literary production. [Italian Wikisource bibliographic record](https://it.wikisource.org/wiki/Autore:Giuseppe_Garibaldi)

### Poetry

- **after 1862:** *Poema autobiografico*. A project of twenty-nine cantos in hendecasyllables, begun after Aspromonte and surviving in autograph; it converts autobiography and political vindication into verse and was published posthumously in 1911. [Italinemo, “Garibaldi poeta”](https://www.italinemo.it/fascicolo/il-lettore-di-provincia-2009-n-132-133/), [Direzione regionale Musei nazionali Sardegna](https://musei.sardegna.beniculturali.it/mostre/io-linfinito-qui-contemplo-tra-le-stanze-di-caprera-parole-e-sentimenti-in-poesia/)

- **date not securely established:** *Carme alla morte*. A separately identified poem preserved and published with the *Poema autobiografico* in 1911; the consulted scholarly and institutional sources do not establish a dependable composition date. [Liber Liber edition](https://www.liberliber.eu/mediateca/libri/g/garibaldi/poema_autobiografico/pdf/poema__p.pdf)

- **dates not securely established:** *Altri canti inediti*. A posthumously assembled corpus of shorter poems, including occasional, political, elegiac, and personal verse. Some pieces are known only by editorial titles or their opening words, so the collection is a scholarly manifestation of scattered writings rather than a single work planned by Garibaldi. [Liber Liber edition](https://www.liberliber.eu/mediateca/libri/g/garibaldi/poema_autobiografico/pdf/poema__p.pdf)

### Manifestos, speeches, commands, and political interventions

- **1838–1882:** *Scritti e discorsi politici e militari*. A posthumously constituted obra of proclamations, manifestos, speeches, orders of the day, military reports, appeals, memoranda, and political reflections—not one book conceived by Garibaldi. The National Edition organizes these individual acts chronologically in three volumes covering 1838–1861, 1862–1865, and 1866–1882; many are performative texts that recruited, commanded, constituted authority, or demanded action rather than merely describing events. [Rassegna degli Archivi di Stato](https://dgagaeta.cultura.gov.it/public/uploads/documents/Rassegna/67f4e3aadaede.pdf)

- **1881:** *Testamento politico*. A late manifesto addressed to posterity, not a private testament in the ordinary legal sense; it condenses Garibaldi’s political program and belongs to the wider corpus of political writings. [Italian Wikisource record](https://it.wikisource.org/wiki/Autore:Giuseppe_Garibaldi)

### Correspondence

- **1834–1882:** *Epistolario*. Thousands of letters, telegrams, notes, declarations, and instructions rather than a single authored “book”; taken together they form an epistolary obra, while each document remains an independent, occasion-bound text. The critical National Edition currently arranges the surviving material in nineteen chronological volumes, from 1834–1848 through 1 January 1879–29 May 1882; survival is necessarily incomplete, and earlier thematic selections such as the letters to Speranza von Schwartz are editorial subsets rather than separate works. [Istituto per la storia del Risorgimento italiano](https://www.risorgimento.it/edizioni-scritti-di-garibaldi/)
