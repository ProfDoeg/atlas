## Works by Joshua Milton Blahyi

### Testimonial books

- **2006:** *From Priesthood to Royal Priesthood: A Testimonial Account of a Liberian Brutal War General and Traditional Priest That Dramatically Met Christ and Is Now a Christian Ambassador*. A religious conversion testimony or spiritual autobiography, published in West Africa; scholarly bibliographies disagree over the imprint, identifying either BeeGee Productions in Monrovia or C.R.M. Press in Lagos. [[O’Mahony and Fair](https://doi.org/10.1177/1750635211434363)] [[Wratto](https://csq.fspac.ubbcluj.ro/wp-content/uploads/CSQ14-Charles-WRATTO.pdf)]

- **2013:** *The Redemption of an African Warlord: The Joshua Blahyi Story—A Modern Day Conversion from Saul to Paul* (also marketed with “a.k.a. General Butt Naked”). An evangelical autobiography and conversion testimony published by Destiny Image; scholarship lists it separately from the 2006 book, but the available sources do not establish how much text was reused or revised. Blahyi’s extraordinary autobiographical claims—including his claimed priesthood, supernatural experiences, and responsibility for twenty thousand deaths—are disputed and should not be treated as independently verified history. [[publisher record](https://play.google.com/store/books/details?id=Q26WiKE3yecC)] [[scholarly bibliography](https://csq.fspac.ubbcluj.ro/wp-content/uploads/CSQ14-Charles-WRATTO.pdf)] [[New Yorker investigation](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2016/03/14/general-butt-naked-the-repentant-warlord)]

### Public testimony

- **15 January 2008:** *Testimony before the Truth and Reconciliation Commission of Liberia*. An oral testimonial act—simultaneously confession, apology, self-narration, and bid for public reconciliation—preserved as a transcript and a nine-part audiovisual manifestation. It resists classification as a wholly autonomous “work” because commissioners’ questions shaped it, and its statements are testimony rather than judicially proven facts. [[official transcript](https://trcofliberia.org/transcripts/7.html)] [[official audiovisual record](https://www.trcofliberia.org/videosets/1/linitial/B/1.html)]

### Sermons and evangelical performance

- **After 1996; individual dates and titles not documented:** *Recorded sermons sold on cassette*. A body of oral preaching manifested as commercial audio cassettes; the surviving account confirms the practice but supplies neither a catalogue nor individual titles, so its contents and completeness cannot presently be enumerated. [[New Yorker investigation](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2016/03/14/general-butt-naked-the-repentant-warlord)]

- **Late 1990s–present; individual dates and titles not documented:** *Sermons and evangelistic addresses*. An ongoing body of performed religious texts delivered in refugee camps, churches, crusades, and public meetings. These resist treatment as discrete fixed works because the consulted sources preserve only reported occasions and fragments, not an authoritative sermon catalogue. [[New Yorker investigation](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2016/03/14/general-butt-naked-the-repentant-warlord)] [[publisher biography](https://play.google.com/store/books/details?id=Q26WiKE3yecC)]

### Digital self-presentation

- **19 January 2007:** *Untitled Twitter post*. A short-form digital text included as a separate Blahyi item in the standard scholarly bibliography used here; the obsolete cited URL no longer supplies enough evidence to name or safely describe its content. [[O’Mahony and Fair bibliography](https://doi.org/10.1177/1750635211434363)]

- **16 June 2008:** *Untitled Twitter post*. A second short-form digital text individually recorded by the scholarly bibliography; its content is not recoverable from the cited legacy search link and is therefore not reconstructed. [[O’Mahony and Fair bibliography](https://doi.org/10.1177/1750635211434363)]

- **By 24 July 2011:** *JoshuaBlahyi Twitter account*. An authorial stream and continuing digital self-presentation rather than a single fixed text; the scholarly source documents the account’s existence but does not provide a complete post-level corpus. [[O’Mahony and Fair bibliography](https://doi.org/10.1177/1750635211434363)]

- **By 29 January 2011:** *Joshua Milton Blahyi—Public Figure*. A Facebook public-figure page: part profile, part publication platform, and therefore an unstable composite digital obra rather than a conventional authored text. [[O’Mahony and Fair bibliography](https://doi.org/10.1177/1750635211434363)]

- **By 29–30 January 2011:** *Joshua Milton Blahyi personal Facebook profile*. A mutable personal profile treated as a source in the scholarly bibliography; its dispersed posts, images, and interactions resist itemization as one work, while no complete archival inventory is supplied. [[O’Mahony and Fair bibliography](https://doi.org/10.1177/1750635211434363)]

### Social and institutional projects

- **2007–:** *Journeys Against Violence (J.A.V.)*. A rehabilitation project and organization founded by Blahyi for former combatants and other vulnerable young people. Its status and efficacy have been contested: reporting describes both concrete residential work and skepticism about Blahyi’s self-presentation, funding, and claims, so the project should not be mistaken for an uncontested humanitarian achievement. [[New Yorker investigation](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2016/03/14/general-butt-naked-the-repentant-warlord)]

### Reported but unidentifiable writings

- **By 2016:** *Five books claimed, not individually identifiable*. The *New Yorker* reported that Blahyi had written five books, but named only *The Redemption of an African Warlord*; the consulted scholarly bibliographies identify only the 2006 and 2013 volumes above. The remaining claimed books resist entry as titled works because their titles, dates, publication status, and even whether they were completed manuscripts are not documented; inventing four entries would convert an aggregate claim into unsupported bibliography. [[New Yorker investigation](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2016/03/14/general-butt-naked-the-repentant-warlord)] [[O’Mahony and Fair bibliography](https://doi.org/10.1177/1750635211434363)] [[Wratto bibliography](https://csq.fspac.ubbcluj.ro/wp-content/uploads/CSQ14-Charles-WRATTO.pdf)]
