## Works by Ghislaine Noelle Marion Maxwell

### Compiled album

- **2003:** *The First Fifty Years*. A privately produced, three-volume, 238-page birthday album for Jeffrey Epstein, assembled and edited by Maxwell from solicited contributions; she also supplied its prologue and editorial framing. Individual contributions belong to their named or attributed makers, and at least one attribution has been publicly disputed, so the album is Maxwell’s compilation rather than her sole-authored text. ([released album](https://www.epsteinarchive.org/_pdfs/bday-book.pdf); [contemporary account of its provenance](https://time.com/7315441/trump-epstein-birthday-book-drawing/))

### Institutional project and digital platform

- **2012–2019:** *The TerraMar Project*. An ocean-conservation nonprofit, advocacy campaign, and web platform founded and led by Maxwell, conceived as a virtual polity for the high seas whose users could become “ocean citizens,” take an ocean pledge, and obtain an “Ocean Passport.” It is best classified as a project rather than a single obra: its manifestations included a website, educational material, newsletters, public events, and advocacy for including oceans in the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals; organizational authorship prevents every component from being assigned personally to Maxwell. ([founding and history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_TerraMar_Project); [Maxwell’s description of the project](https://medium.com/matter/when-the-oceans-failed-4a85c32a2241); [financial evidence filed in *United States v. Maxwell*](https://www.justice.gov/multimedia/Court%20Records/United%20States%20v.%20Maxwell%2C%20No.%20120-cr-00330%20%28S.D.N.Y.%202020%29/097-15.pdf))

### Essays and advocacy texts

- **2014-06-19:** *Tide Is Turning for Ocean Conservation*. A signed HuffPost advocacy essay presenting ocean conservation through the program of the TerraMar Project. The original contributor page is no longer readily accessible, so its title and date survive principally through archived and secondary documentation of Maxwell’s four-post HuffPost corpus. ([bibliographic record of the four posts](https://nationalfile.com/news/huffington-post-tries-to-distance-itself-from-its-former-contributor-ghislaine-maxwell))

- **2015-01-04:** *New Year, Old Challenges…For Climate Change*. A signed HuffPost commentary linking a New Year appeal to climate and ocean policy; its surviving bibliographic record gives the title and publication date but does not establish whether TerraMar staff contributed to its preparation. ([bibliographic record](https://nationalfile.com/news/huffington-post-tries-to-distance-itself-from-its-former-contributor-ghislaine-maxwell))

- **2015-04-01:** *The New “Bait and Switch” on Seafood*. A signed HuffPost advocacy article about seafood mislabelling and ocean governance. It belongs to Maxwell’s small public corpus of TerraMar-related writings rather than to a book-length oeuvre. ([bibliographic record](https://nationalfile.com/news/huffington-post-tries-to-distance-itself-from-its-former-contributor-ghislaine-maxwell))

- **2015-07-27; HuffPost manifestation dated 2015-07-30:** *When the Oceans Failed*. A short piece of speculative climate fiction presented as a first-person dispatch from 2031 and ending as an ocean-advocacy appeal; Medium records 27 July, while documentation of the HuffPost manifestation records 30 July. These appear to be manifestations of one text, not two separate works. ([Medium text and metadata](https://medium.com/matter/when-the-oceans-failed-4a85c32a2241); [HuffPost bibliographic record](https://nationalfile.com/news/huffington-post-tries-to-distance-itself-from-its-former-contributor-ghislaine-maxwell))

- **Date not fixed; *World Ocean Journal*, vol. 2:** *The Ocean Has a Recycling Problem: UNESCO Garbage Patch State*. An illustrated ocean-advocacy article credited to Maxwell in the journal’s contents under “Ocean Solutions.” The accessible issue metadata identifies the volume but does not securely establish a publication date, so none is supplied here. ([*World Ocean Journal*, vol. 2](https://www.worldoceanobservatory.org/files/journal-entry/WOJ-v2_mobile-FINAL.pdf))

### Talks and performed texts

- **2013-02-28:** *Sea Hope, Sea Change, Sea Future*. A roughly ten-minute TEDx talk and recorded audiovisual manifestation in which Maxwell introduced TerraMar and argued against overfishing, dumping, and other damage to the high seas. It is a performed advocacy text, not a book, and the surviving listing does not establish whether Maxwell alone wrote its script. ([programme record](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt23831088/))

- **2013-06-25:** *Oceans in the Sustainable Development Goals*. A United Nations press conference jointly delivered by Maxwell and Palau’s representative Stuart Beck; Maxwell’s contribution promoted TerraMar’s campaign to put oceans into the post-2015 development framework. Because this is a multi-speaker event, the recorded conference is a manifestation containing her speech rather than a solely authored work. ([UN Web TV record](https://webtv.un.org/en/asset/k13/k13gf0xtcu))

- **2014, exact title not securely preserved:** *[TerraMar presentation at TEDxCharlottesville]*. A performed promotional talk about ocean conservation and TerraMar; surviving records confirm the appearance but do not preserve a dependable title, and it should not be conflated with the separately catalogued 2013 talk. ([TEDxCharlottesville archive](https://tedxcharlottesville.com/archive/2014); [contemporary institutional summary](https://news.utdallas.edu/campus-community/learn-about-efforts-to-protect-high-seas-at-free-p/))

- **2014-11-19:** *Advancing a Platform for Transformation of the High Seas*. A public seminar at the University of Texas at Dallas in which Maxwell combined an account of maritime expeditions with a presentation of TerraMar, ocean threats, and global-ocean governance. The university’s announcement supplies the event title and programme but not a separately published script. ([University of Texas at Dallas announcement](https://news.utdallas.edu/campus-community/learn-about-efforts-to-protect-high-seas-at-free-p/))
