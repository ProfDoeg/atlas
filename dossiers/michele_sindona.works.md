## Works by Michele Sindona

### Financial constructions

- **Late 1940s–1960:** *Studio Sindona*. A Milanese legal and tax-consultancy practice through which Sindona devised tax-avoidance and capital-export arrangements; it was an enterprise and workshop for financial techniques rather than a conventional authored work. ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/michele-sindona_(Dizionario-Biografico)/), [D’Addea](https://amsdottorato.unibo.it/id/eprint/7294/))

- **By 1960–1974:** *Fasco network*. A shifting group of Italian, Liechtenstein, Swiss, Luxembourg, and American holding and shell companies used to acquire assets, move bank funds, and obscure ownership. No definitive catalogue exists: the wider “Sindona system” eventually comprised more than one hundred enterprises in eleven countries, and some companies attributed to it were acquired or merely controlled rather than created by Sindona. ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/michele-sindona_(Dizionario-Biografico)/), [U.S. Court of Appeals](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F2/712/15/415191/), [D’Addea](https://storicamente.org/oc/indice-2014/d-addea-giorgio-ambrosoli))

- **1960s–1974:** *The fiduciary-deposit mechanism*. A financial protocol by which Sindona-controlled Italian banks placed customers’ money with compliant foreign banks, which redirected it to companies in his group while the original deposits continued to appear as liquid assets. It is an identifiable method he developed and repeatedly deployed, but it resists designation as a “work” because courts and liquidators treated it as concealment and unlawful diversion. ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/michele-sindona_(Dizionario-Biografico)/), [Sindona v. Grant](https://www.casemine.com/judgement/us/591493baadd7b049345af755), [D’Addea](https://storicamente.org/oc/indice-2014/d-addea-giorgio-ambrosoli))

- **1971:** *Public offer for Bastogi*. An unsuccessful takeover project, notable for using a public tender offer—then unprecedented in Italy—rather than a privately negotiated transfer of control. The project was Sindona’s, but its financing and secret support involved Hambros, so it should not be represented as a solitary creation. ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/michele-sindona_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))

- **1973–1974:** *Finambro recapitalization project*. A proposed financial architecture in which Finambro would absorb Società Generale Immobiliare and raise approximately 120 billion lire without Sindona surrendering control. Finambro itself pre-existed Sindona’s involvement; what he made was the unfinished recapitalization-and-merger project, which failed when official authorization was withheld. ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/michele-sindona_(Dizionario-Biografico)/), [State Archive of Milan](https://archiviodistatomilano.cultura.gov.it/fileadmin/risorse/Patrimonio_archivistico/Fondi_e_Inventari/T/PU_56-DOCUMENTAZIONE_SEQUESTRATA_PRESSO_LA_BANCA_PRIVATA_ITALIANA.pdf), [D’Addea](https://doi.org/10.12977/stor509))

- **1973–1974:** *Banca Privata Italiana*. A corporate amalgamation made by incorporating Banca Privata Finanziaria into Banca Unione under a project directed by Sindona. It was constituted in December 1973, became operational as the merged bank in 1974, and entered compulsory liquidation that September; it was therefore a short-lived institutional construction assembled from existing banks, not a bank founded from nothing. ([Lombardia Beni Culturali](https://www.lombardiabeniculturali.it/archivi/soggetti-produttori/ente/MIDB001841/), [Banca d’Italia](https://www.bancaditalia.it/pubblicazioni/collana-storica/contributi/contributi-11/CSBI-contributi-11.pdf), [Sindona v. Grant](https://www.casemine.com/judgement/us/591493baadd7b049345af755))

- **1974–1979:** *Plans to rescue the Sindona banks*. A succession of political-financial projects intended to reverse liquidation, transfer losses to public institutions, and reduce Sindona’s criminal exposure. The plans changed over time and survive principally in administrative, judicial, and parliamentary records rather than as one fixed, authorially stable text. ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/michele-sindona_(Dizionario-Biografico)/), [Historical Archive of the Chamber of Deputies](https://archivio.camera.it/commissione/commissione-sul-caso-sindona-e-sulle-responsabilita-politiche-ed-amministrative-ad-esso-connesse-1980-1982))

### Texts and mediated testimony

- **By July 1979:** *Untitled autobiography in twenty chapters*. An unpublished autobiographical manuscript attributed contemporaneously to Sindona, deposited with a Milan magistrate and partially summarized by *Panorama*. Its complete title, precise date, and final status are not established by the accessible record, so it should not be conflated with Nick Tosches’s later book. ([L’Unità, 19 August 1979](https://archivio.unita.news/assets/derived/1979/08/19/issue_full.pdf))

- **1984–1986:** *Power on Earth: Michele Sindona’s Explosive Story* / *Il mistero Sindona: le memorie e le rivelazioni di Michele Sindona*. A posthumously published biography by Nick Tosches, constructed from extensive prison interviews with Sindona and a manuscript Sindona reportedly initialled page by page. It is properly Tosches’s book and only a mediated Sindona testimony or collaborative manifestation—not a securely attributable book “by Michele Sindona.” ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/michele-sindona_(Dizionario-Biografico)/), [Italian-edition description](https://www.ossidiane.it/dettagli-libro/?autore=Tosches+Nick&libro=61512&titolo=Il+Mistero+Sindona+-+Le+Memorie+e+le+Rivelazioni+di+Michele+Sindona))

### Fabricated political theatre and criminal productions

- **August–October 1979:** *The false kidnapping of Michele Sindona*. A clandestine political performance devised by Sindona with Mafia and P2 assistance: disappearance, concealment in Sicily, a gunshot wound, hostage photographs, correspondence, and a fictitious revolutionary captor organization were combined to pressure former allies and support his rescue projects. It is a Gesamtkunstwerk only in the literal sense of coordinated media and actions; calling it a “work” risks aestheticizing conduct established as fraud and obstruction. ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/michele-sindona_(Dizionario-Biografico)/), [Italian parliamentary inquiry record](https://www.archivioantimafia.org/p2/commissione_parlamentare/05.%20Volume%20III%20%2835-64%29/051.%20volume_03_tomo_12.pdf))

- **August–September 1979:** *Communiqués of the “Comitato proletario per una giustizia migliore”*. Typed manifestos purporting to emanate from Sindona’s kidnappers, accompanied by staged hostage material and threats. Their language acts rather than merely reports, but their individual authorship is uncertain: they belong to Sindona’s collective kidnapping fabrication and cannot safely be assigned to his hand alone. ([Le Monde, 17 September 1979](https://www.lemonde.fr/archives/article/1979/09/17/les-ravisseurs-du-banquier-sindona-envoient-sa-photo-a-son-avocat_2785125_1819218.html), [Historical Archive of the Chamber of Deputies](https://archivio.camera.it/commissione/commissione-sul-caso-sindona-e-sulle-responsabilita-politiche-ed-amministrative-ad-esso-connesse-1980-1982?leg=VIII+Legislatura))

- **August 1979:** *Letters written during the false kidnapping*. A group of handwritten letters sent to Sindona’s family, secretary, and American and Italian lawyers as props and communications within the staged abduction. They are texts by Sindona, but not an autonomous literary oeuvre; the judicially reconstructed performance supplies their operative meaning. ([Domani’s account based on the Milan Assize Court judgment](https://www.editorialedomani.it/la-misteriosa-scomparsa-di-sindona-e-il-sedicente-gruppo-proletario-blog-mafie-sentenza-corte-assise-yt1hkksv), [Historical Archive of the Chamber of Deputies](https://archivio.camera.it/commissione/commissione-sul-caso-sindona-e-sulle-responsabilita-politiche-ed-amministrative-ad-esso-connesse-1980-1982?leg=VIII+Legislatura))

- **1979:** *Murder of Giorgio Ambrosoli*. An assassination commissioned by Sindona and executed by others to remove the liquidator obstructing his bank-rescue projects. It produced an intended result but resists absolutely the honorific category of “work”: it belongs here only because a comprehensive account of what Sindona caused to be made cannot omit the criminal act for which he received a life sentence. ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/michele-sindona_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))

- **1986:** *Staging of his death as murder*. Sindona’s cyanide poisoning was judicially and biographically interpreted as suicide arranged to resemble assassination. The interpretation was once disputed publicly, but the standard scholarly biography accepts the staged-suicide finding; it is an authored deception and final self-directed act, not an obra in any ordinary sense. ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/michele-sindona_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))
