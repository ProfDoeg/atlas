## Works by Mark Stewart Cohen

### Treatise

- **2014:** *FINRA Arbitration and Enforcement: A Legal and Practical Guide*. A co-authored legal treatise and practical protocol, issued as Securities Practice Series Portfolio No. 289 by Bloomberg BNA; the credited authors are Cohen, Lawrence T. Gresser, C. Evan Stewart, Daniel H. Tabak, and Harvey B. Silikovitz. Bloomberg later listed the portfolio as ceased, so the work survives although that commercial manifestation is no longer available. [Cohen & Gresser portfolio sheet](https://www.cohengresser.com/pdfs/publications/FINRA_Treatise.pdf) [Bloomberg Law MARC notice](https://marc.bloombergbna.com/images/New-update-of-Bloomberg-Law-MARC-records-feb-26-2021.pdf)

### Articles, client alerts, and professional protocols

- **2005:** *The First Deposition: Themes and Variations*. A practice article organized around four substantive principles for preparing a deposition. Cohen’s professional bibliography also records a June manifestation under the expanded title *For the Young Lawyer—The First Deposition: Themes and Variations*; the surviving journal PDF identifies Cohen as sole author, but whether the two listings represent distinct works or two manifestations of one text is unclear. [Journal PDF](https://www.cohengresser.com/pdfs/publications/38.pdf) [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2010:** *Avoiding Antitrust Liability as the Stakes Get Higher*. A practical legal article or protocol advising counsel how to reduce the risk and effects of a Department of Justice antitrust prosecution. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2011:** *Failed Banks Cast Long Shadows: FDIC Targets Insiders, Other Professionals*. A risk-oriented legal article addressing litigation and criminal investigations involving failed banks, their officers, directors, and professional advisers. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2011:** *Insider Trading and Debt Securities: Practical Tips for Hedge Funds*. A compliance protocol for hedge funds facing heightened regulatory enforcement. Cohen’s bibliography separately lists *Insider Trading and Debt Securities: Practical Tips for Hedge Funds in Coping with Regulatory Enforcement* on the same date with the same description; these appear to be variant titles or manifestations, not securely distinguishable works. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2011:** *Lessons from the Galleon Prosecution*. A white-collar-defense article considering the implications of the Galleon case, particularly investigators’ use of wiretaps in insider-trading cases. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2011:** *FCPA Violations in the Whistleblower Era*. A legal and compliance article about incentives for Foreign Corrupt Practices Act whistleblowers and their effects on business conduct. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2012:** *DOJ and SEC Release Long-Awaited FCPA Guidance*. A client alert interpreting the agencies’ FCPA resource guide; because it converts governmental guidance into recommendations for multinational businesses, it partly functions as a protocol. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2013:** *2012 Saw an Increase in Enforcement Against Banks—Investigations Targeted Illicit Transactions of Funds*. A retrospective legal article on investigations and penalties involving ING, Standard Chartered Bank, and HSBC. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2013:** *The FTC Giveth and the FTC Taketh Away: New Proposed Premerger Notification Rules*. A client alert explaining a proposed rule governing withdrawal and refiling of merger notifications; the firm’s synopsis calls it an “SEC” rule, apparently an editorial error, while the title and regulatory citation concern the FTC. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2013:** *Clear Skies Seen Ahead for the American Airlines/U.S. Airways Merger*. An antitrust-analysis article questioning the prevailing assumption that government approval of the proposed merger was assured. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2013:** *Through a Glass Darkly: AU Optronics and the Extraterritorial Application of the Sherman Act*. A legal-analysis article about applying United States antitrust law to price-fixing conduct abroad; Cohen’s bibliography prints “A U Optronics,” leaving the title’s typography unstable. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2013:** *Apple Found Liable of Conspiracy to Fix Prices in E-books Case*. A brief antitrust article explaining per se liability in relation to the price-fixing case against Apple. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2013:** *Anti-Corruption Enforcement in Korea: Is an Old Law Coming of Age?*. An article assessing renewed enforcement of South Korea’s Foreign Bribery Prevention Act. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2014:** *SEC Renews Focus on Accounting and Disclosure Fraud*. A legal article interpreting SEC statements, enforcement actions, and the creation of its Financial Reporting and Audit Task Force as signs of renewed accounting-fraud enforcement. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2014:** *Halliburton II: The End of the Fraud-on-the-Market Presumption?*. A prospective legal-analysis article about the Supreme Court challenge to *Basic v. Levinson* and its possible effect on securities-class-action certification. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2015:** *Recent Developments in Insider Cases: Has the Pendulum Swung Back to the Defense?*. A practice article examining appellate and trial decisions that limited insider-trading prosecutions and SEC enforcement, especially in extended tipper–tippee chains. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2015:** *Trends, Developments in DOJ Investigation of Foreign Banks*. A legal article on the Department of Justice’s investigation of foreign banks, its Swiss Bank Program, and the possible extension of that enforcement campaign to other countries. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2015:** *Federal Sentencing: The Impact of ABA Report and Amendments to Guideline for Economic Crimes*. A professional legal article on federal economic-crime sentencing; the available bibliography supplies no synopsis or reliable co-author statement, so its precise form and attribution beyond Cohen’s inclusion remain incomplete. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2018:** *Managing the Risks of Cross-Border Investigations for Israeli Businesses*. An article for the *Israel Desks Guide* mapping the international regulatory environment faced by Israeli businesses; it is both analysis and risk-management protocol. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2022:** *Trial Setbacks Are Unlikely to Deter the Antitrust Division from Aggressively Pursuing Labor Cases*. A multi-author client alert interpreting two acquittals as setbacks unlikely to alter the Antitrust Division’s labor-market enforcement project; the bibliography associates the alert with Cohen but does not expose a complete byline in its listing. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2025:** *Ethical Challenges Facing Prosecutors and Defense Attorneys in White-Collar Cases*. A law-review article co-authored with Sri K. Kuehnlenz and adapted from Cohen’s 2024 Justice William Strong Lecture; it is therefore a textual manifestation of an earlier performed lecture rather than an entirely independent inception. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

### Lectures, courses, panels, and performed legal texts

- **2005:** *Hot Topics in SEC Enforcement: Keeping Up in a Time of Rapid Change*. A professional presentation or panel appearance. The bibliography preserves the title and date but not enough information to reconstruct Cohen’s individual contribution, so it resists treatment as a wholly authored work. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2008:** *Hot Topics in SEC Enforcement: A Changing Landscape*. A professional presentation or panel; Cohen’s individual spoken text, if any, is not separately documented in the bibliography. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2009:** *Hot Topics in Earnings Management Issues*. A professional presentation or panel recorded by title and date only; it is an event in which Cohen participated, not securely a sole-authored text. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2011:** *The Financial Crisis and the Federal Courts—Litigation Implications of the Dodd-Frank Act*. A collaborative CLE program on the litigation consequences of Dodd–Frank, moderated by Judge Paul G. Gardephe; Cohen’s contribution is a performed intervention within a collective event rather than a separately fixed work. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2013:** *Recent Trends in Developing and Implementing Anti-Corruption Compliance Programs*. An InsideCounsel panel on global compliance programs and the new FCPA Resource Guide; as a panel, its authorship is collective and event-based. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2013:** *When in Rome…? The FCPA and Doing Business Abroad with Third Parties*. A professional presentation whose title and date survive in Cohen’s bibliography, but whose format and individual contribution are not further specified. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2013:** *New Developments in U.S. Competition Law*. A presentation on criminal antitrust enforcement, the AU Optronics trial, enforcement structures, and recent civil trials; it is best understood as an oral professional text. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2013:** *Ethics for Commercial Litigators 2013*. A Practising Law Institute panel addressing confidentiality, privilege, work product, civil liability, and lawyer-whistleblowers; Cohen participated in a collective pedagogical event rather than making a separately attributable fixed text. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2014:** *Fundamentals of Taking and Defending Depositions*. A skills program functioning as an oral protocol for deposition practice; the bibliography does not delimit Cohen’s portion of the collaborative faculty presentation. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2014:** *Ethics for Commercial Litigators 2014*. A Practising Law Institute panel on privilege, confidentiality, conflicts, electronic discovery, no-contact rules, and whistleblower remedies; its made object is the collective instructional event, not demonstrably a sole-authored text. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2014:** *Representing the Board: Best Practices and Potential Hazards*. A panel on corporate-governance responsibilities and ethical risks in acquisitions and internal investigations. It functions as professional instruction, though Cohen’s individual contribution is not separately fixed. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2014:** *Insider Trading: Protecting the Company and Yourself*. A compliance panel covering enforcement trends, Rule 10b5-1 plans, hedging, pledging, and case-law developments; it is a collaborative protocol delivered as an event. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2014:** *First Monday in October*. Cohen appeared as one of nine mock Supreme Court justices in the Office of the Appellate Defender’s annual staged argument. The obra is a juridical performance and fundraiser, not a conventional authored text, and Cohen made only one part of the collective event. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2016:** *The Master 2 International Criminal Law and White Collar Crime (Droit pénal international et des affaires)*. A semester course at Université Paris I–Panthéon Sorbonne in which Cohen served as guest lecturer; its work-status lies in an unfolding pedagogical project rather than a single fixed publication. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2016:** *How to Navigate the New Normal: Legal Trends and the Job Market*. A presentation on changes in the legal profession after the financial crisis and strategies for succeeding within the altered market. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2017:** *U.S. White Collar Criminal Law*. A fall-semester Sorbonne course taught by Cohen and Muriel Goldberg-Darmon, with visiting lecturers Jonathan S. Abernethy and Christian R. Everdell and teaching assistant Reggie B. Schafer. It is a collaborative pedagogical project whose lectures and syllabus are manifestations of the course rather than separately catalogued works. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2017:** *Ethical Issues in Jury Trials*. A Practising Law Institute panel at which Cohen discussed witness preparation, changing testimony, jury consultants, juror investigation, and other ethical decisions. His contribution is documented as participation, not as a separately authored text. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2022:** *Choosing the Best Possible Jury*. A Practising Law Institute CLE panel on jury selection and trial practice; it is a collective instructional performance rather than a sole-authored work. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2024:** *Ethical Challenges Facing Prosecutors and Defense Attorneys*. Cohen’s annual Justice William Strong Lecture on Ethics and the Business Lawyer at Widener University Commonwealth Law School. It was an oral manifestation later adapted, with Sri K. Kuehnlenz, into the 2025 law-review article of the longer title. [Professional bibliography](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

### Institutional obra and continuing public project

- **2002–:** *Cohen & Gresser LLP*. The law firm co-founded by Cohen and Lawrence T. Gresser is an institutional obra rather than a text: an enduring organization through which litigation, investigation, teaching, and publication are collectively produced. Authorship is necessarily shared, and the institution should not be confused with every document made by its lawyers. [Cohen professional profile](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)

- **2011–:** *New York City Fire Department court monitorship*. A continuing court-appointed remedial project overseeing implementation of an order concerning racial discrimination in FDNY hiring. Cohen did not originate the judicial mandate, so it resists classification as his “work” in the proprietary sense; what he makes within it is an ongoing supervisory process involving recruitment, testing, screening, and equal-employment procedures. [Cohen professional profile](https://www.cohengresser.com/attorney/mark-s-cohen/)
