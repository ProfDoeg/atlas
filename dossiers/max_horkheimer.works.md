## Works by Max Horkheimer

### Early literary writings and notebooks

- **1914–1918:** *Aus der Pubertät: Novellen und Tagebuchblätter*. A corpus of youthful novellas, sketches, and diary-like writings rather than a single work; Horkheimer authorized their later arrangement and republication shortly before his death. ([*Gesammelte Schriften*, vol. 1](https://www.fischerverlage.de/buch/max-horkheimer-gesammelte-schriften-in-19-baenden-9783596273751))
- **1926–1931; published 1934:** *Dämmerung: Notizen in Deutschland*. An aphoristic constellation published in Switzerland under the pseudonym “Heinrich Regius”; the individual notes resist treatment as separate finished works. ([SEP bibliography](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/horkheimer/#Bib))
- **1950–1969; published 1974:** *Notizen 1950 bis 1969*. A posthumously published body of aphorisms and working notes, later partly translated as *Decline*; it is an evolving notebook-corpus, not a conventionally composed book. ([SEP bibliography](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/horkheimer/#Bib))

### Academic theses and lecture projects

- **1922:** *Zur Antinomie der teleologischen Urteilskraft*. Horkheimer’s doctoral dissertation, preserved as a bound typescript; some bibliographies shorten the title to *Antinomie der teleologischen Urteilskraft*. ([Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/dbo071185.html); [Frankfurt University Library chronology](https://www.ub.uni-frankfurt.de/archive/horkheimer_vita_en.html))
- **1925:** *Über Kants Kritik der Urteilskraft als Bindeglied zwischen theoretischer und praktischer Philosophie*. Habilitation thesis and Horkheimer’s first substantial academic monograph. ([Frankfurt University Library](https://www.ub.uni-frankfurt.de/archive/horkheimer_werk.html))
- **1925–1926:** *Geschichte der deutschen idealistischen Philosophie von Kant bis Hegel*. A fully written university lecture course, unpublished in Horkheimer’s lifetime and therefore poised between teaching protocol and philosophical work. ([S. Fischer, *Gesammelte Schriften*, series description](https://www.fischerverlage.de/buch/reihe/max-horkheimer-gesammelte-schriften-in-19-baenden-taschenbuchausgabe))
- **1926:** *Einführung in die Philosophie der Gegenwart*. A written lecture course, likewise transmitted through the posthumous papers rather than made into a lifetime publication. ([S. Fischer, *Gesammelte Schriften*, series description](https://www.fischerverlage.de/buch/reihe/max-horkheimer-gesammelte-schriften-in-19-baenden-taschenbuchausgabe))
- **1927:** *Vorlesung über die Geschichte der neueren Philosophie*. A sustained lecture-manuscript on modern philosophy, beginning with Renaissance politics and natural philosophy; its status is pedagogical project rather than authored book. ([S. Fischer, *Gesammelte Schriften*, series description](https://www.fischerverlage.de/buch/reihe/max-horkheimer-gesammelte-schriften-in-19-baenden-taschenbuchausgabe))

### Books and book-length philosophical works

- **1930:** *Anfänge der bürgerlichen Geschichtsphilosophie*. A philosophical-historical monograph developed from Horkheimer’s early academic work. ([Frankfurt University Library](https://www.ub.uni-frankfurt.de/archive/horkheimer_werk.html))
- **1944; revised book edition 1947:** *Dialektik der Aufklärung: Philosophische Fragmente*. A jointly made philosophical book by Horkheimer and Theodor W. Adorno, first circulated in mimeographed form as *Philosophische Fragmente* and then revised for publication; attribution of individual chapters is complicated by reciprocal drafting and revision, so the published work is properly collaborative. ([S. Fischer](https://www.fischerverlage.de/buch/reihe/max-horkheimer-gesammelte-schriften-in-19-baenden-taschenbuchausgabe); [SEP](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/horkheimer/#Bib))
- **1947:** *Eclipse of Reason*. An English-language philosophical book made by expanding Horkheimer’s 1944 Columbia University lectures; although it emerged from intellectual work with Adorno, Horkheimer is its sole author. ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, 2011 archive](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/fall2011/entries/horkheimer/))
- **1967:** *Zur Kritik der instrumentellen Vernunft*. A German reworking of *Eclipse of Reason*, not simply its original German version: its textual relation to the 1947 English book makes it a revision or second manifestation rather than an entirely independent composition. ([S. Fischer](https://www.fischerverlage.de/buch/max-horkheimer-gesammelte-schriften-in-19-baenden-9783596273850))

### Programmatic essays, manifestos, and interventions

- **1931:** *Die gegenwärtige Lage der Sozialphilosophie und die Aufgaben eines Instituts für Sozialforschung*. Horkheimer’s inaugural lecture as professor and Institute director; it functions as a research manifesto by assigning philosophy and empirical social science a collective task. ([SEP bibliography](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/horkheimer/#Bib); [SLUB Dresden](https://katalog.slub-dresden.de/id/0-1093728965))
- **1932:** *Bemerkungen über Wissenschaft und Krise*. A programmatic essay diagnosing science’s social function during crisis. ([SEP bibliography](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/horkheimer/#Bib))
- **1932:** *Geschichte und Psychologie*. An essay joining historical materialism to social psychology and thereby helping specify the Institute’s interdisciplinary project. ([*Gesammelte Schriften*, vol. 3 overview](https://www.fischerverlage.de/buch/max-horkheimer-gesammelte-schriften-in-19-baenden-9783596273775))
- **1933:** *Materialismus und Metaphysik*. A philosophical essay defining Horkheimer’s non-dogmatic materialism against metaphysics and positivism. ([SEP bibliography](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/horkheimer/#Bib); [SLUB Dresden](https://katalog.slub-dresden.de/id/0-1093728965))
- **1933:** *Materialismus und Moral*. A philosophical essay on materialism’s relation to moral action rather than a moral protocol. ([SEP bibliography](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/horkheimer/#Bib); [SLUB Dresden](https://katalog.slub-dresden.de/id/0-1093728965))
- **1934:** *Zum Rationalismusstreit in der gegenwärtigen Philosophie*. A long intervention in contemporary disputes over rationalism. ([SEP bibliography](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/horkheimer/#Bib))
- **1934:** *Zu Bergsons Metaphysik der Zeit*. A critical philosophical essay on Henri Bergson’s theory of time. ([SEP bibliography](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/horkheimer/#Bib))
- **1935:** *Bemerkungen zur philosophischen Anthropologie*. An essay resisting attempts to ground politics and society in a fixed essence of “man.” ([SEP bibliography](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/horkheimer/#Bib))
- **1935:** *Zum Problem der Wahrheit*. A substantial epistemological and social-philosophical essay. ([SEP bibliography](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/horkheimer/#Bib))
- **1935:** *Gedanke zur Religion*. A brief philosophical meditation; its singular title and miniature form make it closer to a published thought-fragment than a conventional essay. ([SEP bibliography](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/horkheimer/#Bib))
- **1936:** *Autorität und Familie*. Horkheimer’s theoretical contribution to the Institute’s collective *Studien über Autorität und Familie*, simultaneously an essay and the conceptual frame of a larger empirical project. ([SLUB Dresden](https://katalog.slub-dresden.de/id/0-1093728965))
- **1936:** *Egoismus und Freiheitsbewegung: Zur Anthropologie des bürgerlichen Zeitalters*. A large historical-philosophical essay on bourgeois political movements and their leaders. ([SEP bibliography](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/horkheimer/#Bib); [LEO-BW](https://www.leo-bw.de/detail/-/Detail/details/DOKUMENT/bsz_swb/1093729023/Gesammelte%20Schriften%20Max%20Horkheimer%20Hrsg%20von%20Alfred%20Schmidt%20))
- **1936:** *Zu Theodor Haeckers „Der Christ und die Geschichte“*. A critical review-essay; its object-specific form resists elevation into an independent philosophical “book.” ([chronological contents of the collected writings](https://www.uni-regensburg.de/assets/philosophie-kunst-geschichte-gesellschaft/geschichte-der-philosophie/dokumente/Schoenberger/horkheimergs.pdf))
- **1936:** *Bemerkungen zu Donald Greers „The Incidence of the Terror during the French Revolution“*. A short scholarly intervention whose bracketed title in the collected edition indicates an editorially supplied title rather than one securely given by Horkheimer. ([chronological contents of the collected writings](https://www.uni-regensburg.de/assets/philosophie-kunst-geschichte-gesellschaft/geschichte-der-philosophie/dokumente/Schoenberger/horkheimergs.pdf))
- **1937:** *Der neueste Angriff auf die Metaphysik*. A major critique of logical empiricism and positivism. ([SEP bibliography](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/horkheimer/#Bib))
- **1937:** *Traditionelle und kritische Theorie*. An essay that acts as the principal manifesto of Critical Theory by distinguishing emancipatory theory from theory that merely registers existing reality. ([SEP bibliography](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/horkheimer/#Bib); [LEO-BW](https://www.leo-bw.de/detail/-/Detail/details/DOKUMENT/bsz_swb/1093729023/Gesammelte%20Schriften%20Max%20Horkheimer%20Hrsg%20von%20Alfred%20Schmidt%20))
- **1937:** *Nachtrag*. A separately published postscript to *Traditionelle und kritische Theorie*, dependent on that essay but bibliographically treated as its own text. ([SEP bibliography](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/horkheimer/#Bib))
- **1937:** *Philosophie und kritische Theorie*. A programmatic statement published jointly with Herbert Marcuse; division of authorship cannot be reduced to Horkheimer alone. ([chronological contents of the collected writings](https://www.uni-regensburg.de/assets/philosophie-kunst-geschichte-gesellschaft/geschichte-der-philosophie/dokumente/Schoenberger/horkheimergs.pdf))
- **1937:** *Bemerkungen zu Jaspers’ „Nietzsche“*. A review-essay occasioned by Karl Jaspers’s book. ([chronological contents of the collected writings](https://www.uni-regensburg.de/assets/philosophie-kunst-geschichte-gesellschaft/geschichte-der-philosophie/dokumente/Schoenberger/horkheimergs.pdf))
- **1938:** *Montaigne und die Funktion der Skepsis*. A historical-philosophical essay treating skepticism as a socially situated intellectual practice. ([chronological contents of the collected writings](https://www.uni-regensburg.de/assets/philosophie-kunst-geschichte-gesellschaft/geschichte-der-philosophie/dokumente/Schoenberger/horkheimergs.pdf))
- **1938:** *Die Philosophie der absoluten Konzentration*. A critical essay on contemporary metaphysics and theology. ([chronological contents of the collected writings](https://www.uni-regensburg.de/assets/philosophie-kunst-geschichte-gesellschaft/geschichte-der-philosophie/dokumente/Schoenberger/horkheimergs.pdf))
- **1939:** *Die Juden und Europa*. A political diagnosis of fascism, monopoly capitalism, and Jewish persecution, published on the eve of war. ([*Gesammelte Schriften*, vol. 4](https://www.fischerverlage.de/buch/reihe/max-horkheimer-gesammelte-schriften-in-19-baenden-taschenbuchausgabe))
- **1940; revised 1942:** *Autoritärer Staat*. An essay first drafted in 1940 and revised for the 1942 Walter Benjamin memorial volume; the double dating records materially different states rather than an invented single publication date. ([chronological contents of the collected writings](https://www.uni-regensburg.de/assets/philosophie-kunst-geschichte-gesellschaft/geschichte-der-philosophie/dokumente/Schoenberger/horkheimergs.pdf))
- **1941:** *Art and Mass Culture*. An English-language essay on art under the culture industry. ([SEP bibliography](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/horkheimer/#Bib))
- **1941:** *The End of Reason*. An English-language essay diagnosing reason’s collapse into adaptation and self-preservation. ([*Gesammelte Schriften*, vols. 4–5 contents](https://www.uni-regensburg.de/assets/philosophie-kunst-geschichte-gesellschaft/geschichte-der-philosophie/dokumente/Schoenberger/horkheimergs.pdf))
- **1942:** *Vernunft und Selbsterhaltung*. A memorial-volume essay and direct precursor to the critique of instrumental reason. ([*Gesammelte Schriften*, vol. 5 contents](https://www.uni-regensburg.de/assets/philosophie-kunst-geschichte-gesellschaft/geschichte-der-philosophie/dokumente/Schoenberger/horkheimergs.pdf))
- **1943:** *Zur Soziologie der Klassenverhältnisse*. A wartime manuscript on class relations, published only posthumously; its delayed appearance makes it part of Horkheimer’s project without making it a lifetime publication. ([textual notes to *Dialectic of Enlightenment*](https://sisu.ut.ee/wp-content/uploads/sites/204/max_horkheimer_theodor_adorno_gunzelin_noeri_eb-ok.org_.pdf))
- **1948:** *Ernst Simmel und die Freudsche Philosophie*. A commemorative essay on psychoanalysis and the work of Ernst Simmel. ([chronological contents of the collected writings](https://www.uni-regensburg.de/assets/philosophie-kunst-geschichte-gesellschaft/geschichte-der-philosophie/dokumente/Schoenberger/horkheimergs.pdf))
- **1952:** *Zum Begriff der Vernunft*. A lecture-essay that continues the critique of instrumental reason after Horkheimer’s return to Frankfurt. ([S. Fischer, *Gesammelte Schriften*, vol. 7](https://www.fischerverlage.de/buch/max-horkheimer-gesammelte-schriften-in-19-baenden-9783596273812))
- **1961:** *Die deutschen Juden*. A historical and political essay on German-Jewish experience after the Holocaust. ([SEP bibliography](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/horkheimer/#Bib))
- **1963:** *Theismus und Atheismus*. A late philosophical-theological essay in which neither doctrinal theism nor confident atheism is allowed an uncomplicated status. ([SEP bibliography](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/horkheimer/#Bib))
- **1964:** *Feudalherr, Kunde und Fachmann*. A late essay on changing forms of authority and dependence. ([Max Horkheimer Internet Archive](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/horkheimer/index.htm))
- **1968:** *Vorwort zur Neuausgabe*. Horkheimer’s preface to the republication of his earlier essays as *Kritische Theorie*; it retrospectively frames—and partly distances him from—the original revolutionary project. ([SEP bibliography](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/horkheimer/#Bib))

### Collective research, editorial, and institutional works

- **1931–1973:** *The directorship of the Institut für Sozialforschung*. An institutional obra rather than a text: Horkheimer made a durable interdisciplinary research program, reorganized it in exile, re-established it at Columbia University, and later returned it to Frankfurt. Authorship is necessarily collective, and the Institute itself was founded before his directorship. ([Frankfurt University Library chronology](https://www.ub.uni-frankfurt.de/archive/horkheimer_vita_en.html); [Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/dbo071185.html))
- **1932–1941:** *Zeitschrift für Sozialforschung / Studies in Philosophy and Social Science*. An editorial and intellectual platform directed by Horkheimer, not a work solely written by him; its successive German, multilingual-exile, and English forms were manifestations of the Institute’s critical-theory project. ([S. Fischer, vols. 3–4](https://www.fischerverlage.de/buch/max-horkheimer-gesammelte-schriften-in-19-baenden-9783596273775))
- **1936:** *Studien über Autorität und Familie: Forschungsberichte aus dem Institut für Sozialforschung*. A collective research assemblage combining theory, surveys, documentary material, and specialist studies; Horkheimer planned and prefaced it and supplied “Autorität und Familie,” but the volume must not be assigned to him alone. ([Google Books record](https://books.google.com/books?id=5ZISAQAAMAAJ); [SLUB Dresden](https://katalog.slub-dresden.de/id/0-1093728965))
- **1939–1942:** *Analyses of economy, law, and the state under National Socialism*. A cluster of Institute studies later collected as *Wirtschaft, Recht und Staat im Nationalsozialismus*; the retrospective volume title should not be mistaken for a book Horkheimer published under that title during the war. ([Frankfurt University Library](https://www.ub.uni-frankfurt.de/archive/horkheimer_werk.html))
- **1943–1951:** *Research Project on Anti-Semitism / Studies in Prejudice*. An interdisciplinary research project led by Horkheimer for the American Jewish Committee and manifested in five major volumes issued in 1949–1951; he was organizer and co-editor, not sole author of the constituent studies. ([Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/dbo071185.html))
- **1950:** *The Authoritarian Personality*. A volume within *Studies in Prejudice*, chiefly authored by Theodor W. Adorno, Else Frenkel-Brunswik, Daniel Levinson, and Nevitt Sanford; it belongs to Horkheimer’s directed project but resists inclusion as a work “by” him in the ordinary authorial sense. ([Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/dbo071185.html))
- **1952:** *Survey of the Social Sciences in Western Germany: A Report on Recent Developments*. An institutional survey prepared under Horkheimer’s name for the Library of Congress; it is a research report and mapping instrument rather than a philosophical book. ([Frankfurt University Library](https://www.ub.uni-frankfurt.de/archive/horkheimer_werk.html))

### Dialogues, protocols, broadcasts, and correspondence

- **1956:** *Diskussion über Theorie und Praxis*. A multi-day recorded discussion with Theodor W. Adorno, later published from the protocol and translated as *Towards a New Manifesto*; it is collaborative speech made textual after the event, not a manifesto deliberately written for publication. ([SEP bibliography](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/horkheimer/#Bib); [S. Fischer, vol. 19](https://www.fischerverlage.de/buch/max-horkheimer-gesammelte-schriften-in-19-baenden-9783596273935))
- **1949–1973:** *Vorträge, Ansprachen, Gespräche und Rundfunk- und Fernsehaufnahmen*. A large performance-and-recording corpus rather than one fixed work: lectures, ceremonial speeches, interviews, radio talks, and television appearances survive in scripts, transcripts, or recordings, sometimes without authorial titles. The collected edition and its register inventory these manifestations; inventing separate titles or dates where the archive supplies none would falsely turn occasions into finished works. ([S. Fischer, vols. 7–8 and 19](https://www.fischerverlage.de/buch/reihe/max-horkheimer-gesammelte-schriften-in-19-baenden-taschenbuchausgabe))
- **1913–1973:** *Briefwechsel*. Thousands of letters form an epistolary corpus published in volumes 15–18 of the collected writings; each letter is a made text, but the correspondence is relational, often co-authored by exchange, and was not conceived as a finished work. ([S. Fischer, collected-edition description](https://www.fischerverlage.de/buch/reihe/max-horkheimer-gesammelte-schriften-in-19-baenden-taschenbuchausgabe))
- **1927–1969:** *Briefwechsel mit Theodor W. Adorno*. A four-volume correspondence whose intellectual arguments, editorial decisions, and collaborative planning sometimes become working parts of later works; it resists classification as either private residue or authored book. ([Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/dbo071185.html))
- **1914–1973:** *Nachgelassene Schriften, Entwürfe, Fragmente und Gesprächsnotizen*. The editors of volumes 9–14 and 19 preserve a very large residue of titled and untitled drafts, lecture notes, plans, fragments, and records made by Horkheimer or written down by others from his speech. They are evidence of projects and acts of thought, but many resist being called autonomous works, and bracketed titles in the edition are editorial descriptions rather than titles supplied by Horkheimer. ([S. Fischer, collected-edition description](https://www.fischerverlage.de/buch/reihe/max-horkheimer-gesammelte-schriften-in-19-baenden-taschenbuchausgabe); [vol. 19 description](https://www.fischerverlage.de/buch/max-horkheimer-gesammelte-schriften-in-19-baenden-9783596273935))
