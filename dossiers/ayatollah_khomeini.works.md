## Works by Ruhollah Mostafavi Musavi Khomeini

### Mystical and devotional treatises

- **1929:** *Šarḥ-e doʿā-ye saḥar* (*Sharḥ duʿāʾ al-saḥar*). An Arabic commentary on the Ramadan dawn supplication attributed to Imam Moḥammad al-Bāqer; Khomeini’s earliest securely dated surviving treatise, first published in Persian translation in 1980 and in Arabic in 1983. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **1930:** *Meṣbāḥ al-hedāya elā’l-ḵelāfa wa’l-welāya*. A compact Arabic work of speculative mysticism on prophecy, vicegerency, and *welāya*, deliberately written for a specialized readership; it remained unpublished until 1993. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **1936:** *Taʿliqa ʿalā Šarḥ Foṣuṣ al-ḥekam*. Arabic marginalia on Dāʾud Qayṣari’s commentary upon Ibn ʿArabi’s *Foṣuṣ al-ḥekam*; this is a layer of scholarly annotation rather than an autonomous book and was later printed with the following work. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **Date uncertain, probably the 1930s:** *Taʿliqa ʿalā Meṣbāḥ al-ons*. Arabic notes on Mollā Fanāri’s commentary upon Ṣadr-al-Din Qonavi; published together with the *Taʿliqa ʿalā Šarḥ Foṣuṣ al-ḥekam* as *al-Taʿliqāt ʿalā Šarḥ Foṣuṣ al-ḥekam wa Meṣbāḥ al-ons* in 1985. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **Undated:** *Leqāʾ Allāh*. A short Persian mystical essay on the possibility and modes of “meeting with God”; it first appeared as a supplement to another author’s treatise, so its initial printed object was composite. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **Undated; attribution uncertain:** *Ḥāšiya bar Asfār*. Reported Persian marginalia on Mollā Ṣadrā’s *Asfār al-arbaʿa*. The work has never been published, its extent is unknown, and Khomeini’s authorship is not secure; it therefore resists treatment as an established obra rather than a possibly once-existing body of notes. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)

### Prayer commentaries and spiritual protocols

- **1939:** *Serr al-ṣalāt: Meʿrāj al-sālekin wa ṣalāt al-ʿārefin*. A dense Persian exposition of the inward meanings of ritual prayer—a mystical commentary that also functions as a protocol for contemplative practice. It circulated in partial and retitled forms before the definitive 1996 edition reproduced Khomeini’s manuscript. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **1942:** *Ādāb al-ṣalāt: Ādāb-e namāz*. A more accessible Persian companion to *Serr al-ṣalāt*, explaining the spiritual disciplines and meanings of prayer; despite its instructional purpose, it is an extensive treatise rather than a simple ritual manual. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)

### Qurʾan and hadith commentaries

- **Probably 1929:** *Taʿliqa ʿalā Šarḥ ḥadiṯ raʾs al-jālut*, also called *al-Taʿliqa ʿalā al-Fawāʾed al-rażawiya*. Arabic annotations upon Qāżi Saʿid Qomi’s commentary on a disputation attributed to Imam Reżā; its identification with a work reportedly completed in 1929 is probable, not certain. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **1939:** *Šarḥ-e čehel ḥadiṯ* (*Arbaʿin ḥadiṯ*, “Forty Hadith”). Khomeini’s first and longest Persian book, combining forty transmitted reports with extensive ethical, mystical, and theological commentary. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **1944:** *Šarḥ-e ḥadiṯ-e jonud-e ʿaql wa jahl*. A Persian commentary on the hadith of the “battalions” of intelligence and ignorance, effectively an elaborate protocol of ethical self-formation. Khomeini explicitly left its treatment non-exhaustive and expressed an intention to return to it, making the surviving text a completed manuscript with an unfinished project behind it. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **Undated, after the 1979 revolution:** *Tafsiri az sura-ye ʿalaq*. A published lecture on the first four verses of Qurʾan 96 which turns from exegesis to defense of the new Islamic Republic; its nature is oral-political intervention preserved as a short text. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **17 December 1979–11 January 1980:** *Tafsir-e Sura-ye ḥamd*. Five televised exegetical lectures, halted after protests before Khomeini progressed beyond the sura’s first two verses. It is an unfinished broadcast project; the “complete” 1996 book supplements the lectures with passages extracted by editors from other works and is therefore not wholly a new Khomeini-authored text. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)

### Jurisprudential works and legal protocols

- **1941:** *Manāhej al-woṣul elā ʿelm al-oṣul*. A two-volume Arabic treatise on the language, principles, and methodology through which legal rulings are derived; written as scholarly jurisprudence and published posthumously in 1994. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **1948:** *Taʿliqa ʿalā Kefāyat al-oṣul*. Arabic glosses on Āḵund Moḥammad-Kāẓem Ḵorasāni’s foundational work of legal theory; a commentary attached to another scholar’s text, not a freestanding systematic treatise. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **1949–1954:** *Al-Rasāʾel*. Five Arabic legal-theoretical treatises on the no-harm principle, presumption of continuity, conflicting traditions, independent legal reasoning and emulation, and prudential concealment. Edited with Khomeini’s approval and issued as a composite volume in 1964; individual components also circulated separately. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **1949:** *Badāʿi al-dorar fi qāʿedat nafy al-żarar*. The first component of *Al-Rasāʾel*, concerning the legal principle against inflicting or reciprocating harm; its later separate publication does not make it a second composition. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **1951:** *Al-Ejtehād wa’l-taqlid*. The fourth component of *Al-Rasāʾel*, treating the qualifications and authority of the jurist and anticipating Khomeini’s doctrine of juristic guardianship; separately published in 1979 but not a distinct second work. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **1954–1957:** *Ketāb al-ṭahāra*. A four-volume Arabic legal work on ritual purity, generated from Khomeini’s Qom teaching and published complete in 1969. Ṣādeq Ḵalḵāli’s related *al-Demāʾ al-ṯalāṯa* derives from student notes that Khomeini approved, so it is a manifestation of his teaching but not straightforwardly his authored book. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **1955:** *Taʿliqa ʿalā al-ʿOrwa al-woṯqā*. Arabic glosses on Sayyed Moḥammad-Kāẓem Yazdi’s collection of rulings; early publication identified Khomeini only as “Ḵ” to reduce political scrutiny. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **Completion date unknown:** *Taʿliqa ʿalā Wasilat al-najāt*. Khomeini’s fatwas arranged as a Persian commentary on Abu’l-Ḥasan Eṣfahāni’s legal manual; the object combines another jurist’s base text with Khomeini’s interventions. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **By 1961:** *Resāla-ye najāt al-ʿebād*. A three-volume Persian collection of legal rulings; the standard bibliography specifically records publication of its second volume in 1961, leaving the publication history and completeness of the whole less clear. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **Undated:** *Ḥāšiya bar Resāla-ye erṯ*. Khomeini’s inheritance fatwas arranged as commentary upon Mollā Hāšem Ḵorāsāni’s treatise; like several legal works here, it is a layered text rather than wholly independent composition. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **1961:** *Al-Makāseb al-moḥarrama*. A two-volume Arabic legal treatise on forbidden forms of trade and gain, including rulings about images, performance, gambling, slander, and service to unjust rulers. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **1961–1976:** *Ketāb al-bayʿ*. A five-volume Arabic work on sale, property, contracts, and juristic authority, embodying courses taught first in Qom and then Najaf; the volumes were completed and published at different times, with the fifth appearing in 1977. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **From 1962:** *Tawżiḥ al-masāʾel-e Emām Ḵomeyni*, also issued as *Resāla-ye aḥkām*. A practical Persian protocol for lay followers, produced by merging Khomeini’s glosses with a refashioned version of Ayatollah Borujerdi’s manual. Its collaborative textual ancestry and varying numbers of rulings across editions prevent it from being treated as a single invariant text. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **1964–1965; completed 1969:** *Taḥrir al-wasila*. A two-volume Arabic legal manual made in Turkish exile by merging Khomeini’s commentary into Eṣfahāni’s *Wasilat al-najāt* and adding subjects absent from that source. It is simultaneously adaptation, expansion, and independent protocol, including rulings on modern financial, medical, and technological questions. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **Composition date uncertain; published 1988:** *Resāla fi taʿyin al-fajr fi’l-layāli al-moqmera*. A short Arabic legal essay determining the onset of dawn on moonlit nights; the publication date is known, but its date of composition is not. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **1983:** *Zobdat al-aḥkām*. A selection of rulings drawn from *Taḥrir al-wasila* and other writings. No compiler is identified, so this is best treated as an editorial manifestation of Khomeini’s rulings rather than securely as a book he designed and made. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **Principally 1979–1989; compiled 1996–2019:** *Esteftāʾāt*. A ten-volume posthumous compilation of 12,387 fatwas—Khomeini’s operative answers to legal questions, many first printed elsewhere. The individual answers are his acts of judgment; their organization as a ten-volume obra belongs to later editors. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)

### Ethical and pedagogical lecture-texts

- **October 1972:** *Mobāraza bā nafs yā jehād-e akbar*. A transcript of Najaf lectures exhorting religious students to struggle against the appetitive self and imperial domination. It is a sermon-cycle turned into a book and thus preserves an oral event rather than an originally written treatise. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)

### Political polemics and manifestos

- **1945:** *Kašf al-asrār*. An anonymously published Persian polemic answering ʿAli-Akbar Ḥakamizāda’s *Asrār-e hazārsāla* and attacking secular government and anti-clerical reform. It acts as a political-theological manifesto as well as an argumentative book; some sources date its first appearance to 1943, while Hamid Algar’s bibliographical study gives Qom, 1945. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **January–February 1970; published 1971:** *Welāyat-e faqih: Ḥokumat-e eslāmi*. A transcript, with some rearrangement and removal of repetition, of Najaf lectures advocating government by qualified Islamic jurists. It is at once lecture-cycle, editorially shaped book, political manifesto, and protocol for constructing an Islamic state. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)

### Poems and poetic corpus

- **From youth to 1989:** *Poems of Ruhollah Khomeini*. A dispersed Persian poetic corpus—principally mystical ghazals, with quatrains, qaṣidas, stanzaic poems, and occasional political verse. At least three notebooks were lost or destroyed, dates are unavailable for most surviving poems, and therefore the extant corpus cannot be called complete. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **1984–1988; published 1989:** *Bāda-ye ʿešq*. A small collection of twenty-four ghazals and forty-one quatrains composed at Fāṭema Ṭabāṭabāʾi’s request; unlike most of the poetic corpus, these poems have an approximate composition range. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **1986–1989; published 1989:** *Sabu-ye ʿešq*. Eight late ghazals, issued on the fortieth day after Khomeini’s death; the collection is a posthumous editorial vessel for poems he made, not necessarily a book-project he himself conceived. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **Various dates; published 1990:** *Rah-e ʿešq*. A posthumous small poetry collection prefaced by a facsimile letter to Fāṭema Ṭabāṭabāʾi; it is an editorial manifestation of the poetic corpus. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **Various dates; published 1990:** *Noqṭa-ye ʿaṭf*. A posthumous small poetry collection incorporating an exhortatory letter to Khomeini’s son Aḥmad; its mixed contents make it a composite literary object. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **1984–1986; published 1990:** *Maḥram-e rāz*. A posthumous collection of twenty-six ghazals and fourteen quatrains, preceded by a letter to Aḥmad dated 9 December 1987; poems and letter were made separately before editors joined them. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **Various dates; published 1993:** *Divān-e Emām: Majmuʿa-ye ašʿār-e Emām Ḵomeyni*. The posthumous collected-poems edition: 139 ghazals, 120 quatrains, three qaṣidas, two *mosammaṭs*, one *tarjiʿband*, and thirty-one *qeṭʿas*. It gathers the small collections and privately preserved poems, but the lost notebooks make “collected” mean surviving rather than complete works. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)

### Letters, speeches, decrees, interviews, and public acts

- **Throughout his public career, especially 1962–1989:** *Speeches, messages, interviews, letters, decrees, permissions, and fatwas*. These are thousands of distinct utterances and documentary acts rather than one authored book; many are manifestos or operative texts that commanded, authorized, prohibited, appointed, or dismissed. Their principal collected manifestation is the editorially assembled twenty-two-volume *Ṣaḥifa-ye Emām*, which must not be mistaken for a single work planned by Khomeini. [Encyclopædia Iranica: Works](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/), [Encyclopædia Iranica: Life](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-i-life/)
- **1984:** *Letters to Aḥmad Khomeini and Fāṭema Ṭabāṭabāʾi concerning Ādāb al-ṣalāt*. Two spiritual exhortations subsequently prefixed to editions of *Ādāb al-ṣalāt*; they are independent epistolary acts even though later publication makes them appear paratextual to the older treatise. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **19 March 1987:** *Fatwa concerning music and musical instruments*. A legal response modifying the practical treatment of musical performance and dual-use instruments; it is an operative juridical act, not a book, and its wording has been characterized as deliberately or inherently ambiguous. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
- **Undated:** *Fatwa concerning chess*. A legal response permitting chess when detached from gambling, preserved in *Esteftāʾāt*. It resists classification as a “work” in the literary sense but is unmistakably a made legal act with practical consequences. [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-ii-works/)
