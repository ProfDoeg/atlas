## Works by Joseph-Marie Jacquard

### Weaving machines and mechanisms

- **c. 1790:** *Early untitled loom mechanism*. In the specification of his later patent, Jacquard claimed that he had devised a machine in 1790 which was subsequently fitted to thousands of Lyon looms; the surviving description does not establish its construction clearly enough to identify it securely with the later Jacquard mechanism. It is therefore best treated as a reported early project, not as a reconstructable finished work. ([Boquillon, *Études techniques sur l’exposition des produits de l’industrie française en 1844*](https://cnum.cnam.fr/pgi/redir.php?ident=8XAE24&onglet=c))

- **1800–1801:** *Machine destinée à suppléer le tireur de lacs dans la fabrication des étoffes brochées et façonnées*. A patented mechanical attachment intended to replace the drawboy who selected warp threads on a figured-silk loom. Jacquard submitted the application on 2 July 1800 and received the ten-year patent in 1801; the Musée des Arts et Métiers gives the formal patent date as 23 December 1801. This imperfect machine shared its purpose, but not yet the complete construction, of the mechanism later called “Jacquard.” ([CTHS, “Jacquard Joseph Marie”](https://cths.fr/an/savant.php?id=111449); [Musée des Arts et Métiers, “Modèle : Métier original de Jacquard”](https://www.arts-et-metiers.net/musee/modele-metier-original-de-jacquard); [Boquillon](https://cnum.cnam.fr/pgi/redir.php?ident=8XAE24&onglet=c))

- **1803–1804:** *Reconstruction of Vaucanson’s figured-weaving loom*. While attached to the Conservatoire des Arts et Métiers, Jacquard reassembled or restored Jacques de Vaucanson’s dismantled loom. This resists classification as Jacquard’s own obra: it was conservation and reconstruction of another inventor’s machine, although studying it materially informed Jacquard’s subsequent mechanism. ([Fondation Napoléon, “Jacquard, Joseph-Marie”](https://www.napoleon.org/histoire-des-2-empires/biographies/jacquard-joseph-marie-1752-1834-mecanicien-tisserand/); [CNAM, *Jacques Vaucanson*, 1983 catalogue](https://cnum.cnam.fr/pgi/redir.php?ident=M5566&onglet=c))

- **1804–1805, subsequently modified:** *Mécanique Jacquard*. A programmable shedding mechanism mounted above a loom, using needles, hooks, springs, a perforated-card chain, and a rotating prism to select individual warp threads. It was an inventive synthesis of devices associated with Basile Bouchon, Jean-Baptiste Falcon, and Vaucanson rather than an invention without antecedents; its initially unreliable form was subsequently altered by other mechanics, especially Jean-Antoine Breton, so the mature commercial “Jacquard loom” is not solely Jacquard’s made object. ([CNAM, *Jacques Vaucanson*, entries 61–62](https://cnum.cnam.fr/pgi/redir.php?ident=M5566&onglet=c); [Treccani, “Jacquard, Joseph-Marie”](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/joseph-marie-jacquard_%28Enciclopedia-Italiana%29/); [Musée des Arts et Métiers, textile collections essay](https://www.arts-et-metiers.net/sites/arts-et-metiers/files/2021-10/field_media_document-348-pj_textile.pdf))

- **During his Conservatoire employment, probably 1803–1804:** *Mechanism for weaving ribbon with velvet on both faces*. A nineteenth-century technical biography credits Jacquard with this additional weaving apparatus but supplies neither a fixed title nor a sufficiently detailed description, and it is not comparably documented in the modern museum catalogue. Its attribution should therefore remain provisional. ([Timbs, *Stories of Inventors and Discoverers in Science and the Useful Arts*, pp. 332–33](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/99/Stories_of_inventors_and_discoverers_in_science_and_the_useful_arts_A_book_for_old_and_young_%28IA_inventdiscsciart00timbrich%29.pdf))

### Net-making machine

- **1802–1804:** *Métier mécanique à fabriquer les filets de pêche*. A machine intended to knot fishing nets mechanically, made in response to a prize competition announced in 1802 and demonstrated in Paris before Jacquard received a medal in February 1804. Later technical histories disagree over whether it should be called a completed invention: one records an 1804 patent, while another says Jacquard only began or sketched a solution and that his machine remained inferior to later designs. It is thus most securely classified as an experimental machine and partially realized project. ([*Bulletin de la Société d’encouragement pour l’industrie nationale*, historical study](https://cnum.cnam.fr/pgi/redir.php?ident=BSPI.148&onglet=c); [French jury report, 1904](https://cnum.cnam.fr/pgi/redir.php?ident=8XAE614.1&onglet=c); [Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/joseph-marie-jacquard_%28Enciclopedia-Italiana%29/))

### Patent text and drawings

- **1800–1801; published 1824:** *Description d’une machine destinée à suppléer le tireur de lacs dans la fabrication des étoffes brochées et façonnées*. The specification and accompanying technical representation of Jacquard’s patented drawboy-replacing machine constitute a protocol as well as the documentary manifestation of the invention: they explain how the apparatus is constructed and used. The patent text should not be confused with the later card-controlled mechanism, despite their shared objective; it first appeared in print after the patent expired, in the official *Description des machines et procédés*, volume VIII. ([Musée des Arts et Métiers](https://www.arts-et-metiers.net/musee/modele-metier-original-de-jacquard); [*Description des machines et procédés…*, bibliographical account](https://www.historyofinformation.com/detail.php?id=441); [Boquillon](https://cnum.cnam.fr/pgi/redir.php?ident=8XAE24&onglet=c))
