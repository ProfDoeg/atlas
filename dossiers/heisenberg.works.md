## Works by Werner Heisenberg

### Scientific papers and research reports

- **1922:** *Zur Quantentheorie der Linienstruktur und der anomalen Zeemaneffekte*. Scientific paper on atomic spectra and anomalous Zeeman effects; Heisenberg’s first substantial published research paper (Cassidy 1922a).
- **1922:** *Die absoluten Dimensionen der Kármánschen Wirbelbewegung*. Short hydrodynamics paper on von Kármán vortex streets (Cassidy 1922b).
- **1922:** *Eine Bemerkung über relativistische Röntgendubletts und Linienschärfe*. Scientific paper coauthored with Arnold Sommerfeld (Cassidy 1922c).
- **1922:** *Die Intensität der Mehrfachlinien und ihrer Zeeman-Komponenten*. Scientific paper coauthored with Sommerfeld (Cassidy 1922d).
- **1923:** *Über Phasenbeziehungen bei den Bohrschen Modellen von Atomen und Molekeln*. Scientific paper coauthored with Max Born (Cassidy 1923a).
- **1923:** *Die Elektronenbahnen im angeregten Heliumatom*. Scientific paper coauthored with Born (Cassidy 1923b).
- **1924:** *Zur Quantentheorie der Molekeln*. Scientific paper coauthored with Born (Cassidy 1924a).
- **1924:** *Über Stabilität und Turbulenz von Flüssigkeitsströmen*. Published version of Heisenberg’s doctoral dissertation: a hydrodynamics study rather than a quantum-physics text (Cassidy 1924b).
- **1924:** *Über den Einfluß der Deformierbarkeit der Ionen auf optische und chemische Konstanten. I*. Scientific paper coauthored with Born (Cassidy 1924c).
- **1924:** *Termstruktur der Multipletts höherer Stufe*. Scientific paper coauthored with Alfred Landé (Cassidy 1924e).
- **1924:** *Bemerkung zu einer Arbeit von F. v. Wiśniewski, “Zur Theorie des Heliums”*. Scientific note correcting or qualifying another physicist’s work (Cassidy 1924f).
- **1924:** *Über den Einfluß der Deformierbarkeit der Ionen auf optische und chemische Konstanten. II: Stabilität und Bildungswärme dreiatomiger Molekeln und Ionen*. Continuation of Heisenberg’s research on ion deformation (Cassidy 1924g).
- **1924:** *Über eine Abänderung der formalen Regeln der Quantentheorie beim Problem der anomalen Zeemaneffekte*. Scientific paper revising the formal rules of the older quantum theory (Cassidy 1924h).
- **1925:** *Über eine Anwendung des Korrespondenzprinzips auf die Frage nach der Polarisation des Fluoreszenzlichtes*. Scientific paper applying the correspondence principle (Cassidy 1925a).
- **1925:** *Über die Streuung von Strahlung durch Atome*. Scientific paper coauthored with Hendrik A. Kramers (Cassidy 1925b).
- **1925:** *Zur Quantentheorie der Multiplettstruktur und der anomalen Zeemaneffekte*. Scientific paper on multiplet structure (Cassidy 1925c).
- **1925:** *Über quantentheoretische Umdeutung kinematischer und mechanischer Beziehungen*. Foundational scientific paper establishing the matrix form of quantum mechanics; its title describes a “reinterpretation,” but the object is effectively a new mathematical mechanics (Cassidy 1925d).
- **1926:** *Mehrkörperprobleme und Resonanz in der Quantenmechanik*. Scientific paper on many-body problems and resonance.
- **1926:** *Quantenmechanik*. Extended review or systematic exposition of the new mechanics rather than another short discovery paper.
- **1926:** *Schwankungserscheinungen und Quantenmechanik*. Scientific paper on fluctuation phenomena.
- **1926:** *Über die Spektra von Atomsystemen mit zwei Elektronen*. Scientific paper on two-electron atomic spectra.
- **1926:** *Mehrkörperprobleme und Resonanz in der Quantenmechanik. II*. Continuation of the many-body and resonance investigation.
- **1926–1927:** *Über die Intensitäten der im elektrischen Feld erscheinenden Kombinationslinien*. Scientific papers on line intensities and molecular exchange, published in closely related forms; the bibliography notes uncertainty about which of the 1926 versions should receive priority in identifying the exchange-force work.
- **1927:** *Über den anschaulichen Inhalt der quantentheoretischen Kinematik und Mechanik*. Scientific paper formulating the uncertainty relation; “uncertainty principle” is the later identifying name, not its original title (Cassidy 1927c).
- **1928:** *Zur Theorie des Ferromagnetismus*. Scientific paper deriving an exchange-based quantum theory of ferromagnetism (Cassidy 1928a).
- **1929:** *Zur Quantentheorie der Wellenfelder*. First part of a quantum-field-theory project coauthored with Wolfgang Pauli.
- **1930:** *Zur Quantentheorie der Wellenfelder. II*. Second part of the Heisenberg–Pauli quantum-field-theory project.
- **1930:** *Die Selbstenergie des Elektrons*. Scientific paper on the electron’s self-energy.
- **1931:** *Über Energieschwankungen in einem Strahlungsfeld*. Scientific paper on energy fluctuations in a radiation field (Cassidy 1931a).
- **1931:** *Bemerkungen zur Strahlungstheorie*. Scientific paper on radiation theory (Cassidy 1931b).
- **1931:** *Zur Theorie der Magnetostriktion und der Magnetisierungskurve*. Scientific paper on magnetostriction and magnetization (Cassidy 1931c).
- **1931:** *Zum Paulischen Ausschließungsprinzip*. Scientific paper on Pauli’s exclusion principle (Cassidy 1931e).
- **1931:** *Über die inkohärente Streuung von Röntgenstrahlen*. Scientific paper on incoherent X-ray scattering (Cassidy 1931f).
- **1932:** *Über den Bau der Atomkerne. I*. First paper in Heisenberg’s neutron–proton model of the atomic nucleus.
- **1932:** *Über den Bau der Atomkerne. II*. Second paper in the nuclear-structure sequence.
- **1933:** *Über den Bau der Atomkerne. III*. Third paper in the sequence; together the three papers form a scientific project rather than three unrelated obras.
- **1934:** *Bemerkungen zur Diracschen Theorie des Positrons*. Scientific paper on Dirac’s positron theory.
- **1934:** *Berichtigung zu der Arbeit: “Bemerkungen zur Diracschen Theorie des Positrons”*. Correction notice; it resists classification as an independent work because its function is to repair the preceding paper (Cassidy 1934b).
- **1934:** *Über die mit der Entstehung von Materie aus Strahlung verknüpften Ladungsschwankungen*. Scientific paper on charge fluctuations accompanying pair creation (Cassidy 1934c).
- **1935:** *Bemerkungen zur Theorie des Atomkerns*. Scientific paper extending Heisenberg’s nuclear theory.
- **1936:** *Zur Theorie der “Schauer” in der Höhenstrahlung*. Scientific paper on cosmic-ray showers.
- **1936:** *Über die in der Theorie der Elementarteilchen auftretende universelle Länge*. Scientific paper proposing a universal length in particle theory.
- **1938:** *Theoretische Gesichtspunkte zur Deutung der kosmischen Strahlung*. Major review coauthored with Hans Euler; it synthesizes a research field rather than reporting a single result (Cassidy 1938f).
- **1938:** *Wahrscheinlichkeitsaussagen in der Quantentheorie der Wellenfelder*. Conference paper on probability statements in quantum field theory (Cassidy 1938l).
- **1939:** *Zur Theorie der explosionsartigen Schauer in der kosmischen Strahlung. II*. Continuation of the cosmic-ray-shower project (Cassidy 1939a).
- **1939–1945:** *Kernphysikalische Forschungsberichte*. Twenty-two surviving secret or restricted reports authored or coauthored by Heisenberg for the German wartime uranium project. They are a dispersed research dossier rather than a book; declassification and first collected publication occurred only after his death, and the surviving set may not represent every report originally produced.
- **1943:** *Die beobachtbaren Größen in der Theorie der Elementarteilchen. I*. First paper in Heisenberg’s S-matrix program.
- **1943:** *Die beobachtbaren Größen in der Theorie der Elementarteilchen. II*. Second part of the S-matrix program.
- **1944:** *Die beobachtbaren Größen in der Theorie der Elementarteilchen. III*. Third part of the S-matrix program.
- **1944; first published 1989:** *Die beobachtbaren Größen in der Theorie der Elementarteilchen* [further manuscript]. Unpublished continuation of the S-matrix project, recovered and printed in the *Collected Works*; its editorial title and posthumous status make it an unfinished project as much as a fixed text.
- **1948:** *Zur statistischen Theorie der Turbulenz*. Scientific paper returning to the turbulence problem of Heisenberg’s dissertation.
- **1949:** *Die Erzeugung von Mesonen in Vielfachprozessen*. Scientific paper in the sequence on multiple particle-production processes.
- **1953:** *Über die Isospingruppe in der Theorie der Elementarteilchen*. Scientific paper on isospin and elementary particles.
- **1953:** *Zur Quantentheorie nichtlinearer Wellengleichungen*. Opening work in Heisenberg’s nonlinear-field-theory project.
- **1955:** *Zur Theorie der Elementarteilchen*. Scientific paper continuing the nonlinear unified-field project.
- **1957:** *Quantum Theory of Fields and Elementary Particles*. Scientific exposition of the developing nonlinear spinor theory.
- **1957:** *Zur Quantentheorie der Elementarteilchen*. Scientific paper coauthored with Wolfgang Pauli; it belongs to an attempted unified theory that remained incomplete and was never generally accepted.
- **1958:** *On the Isospin Group in the Theory of the Elementary Particles*. Research paper associated with Heisenberg’s unified nonlinear spinor theory.
- **1958:** *Zur Theorie der Elementarteilchen*. Further statement of the nonlinear unified-field project, coauthored with Pauli.
- **1965–1967:** *Bemerkungen zur einheitlichen Feldtheorie der Elementarteilchen*. Late papers and notes revising the unified-field project. These resist treatment as a completed obra: Heisenberg continued altering the proposed theory, and it did not reach an agreed definitive form.

### Scientific books and lecture-obras

- **1930:** *Die physikalischen Prinzipien der Quantentheorie*. Book developed from Heisenberg’s 1929 University of Chicago lectures; *The Physical Principles of the Quantum Theory* is its English manifestation, not a second obra (Cassidy 1930c–d).
- **1943:** *Die Physik der Atomkerne*. Scientific book on nuclear physics, derived from lectures and later expanded; translations and revised editions are manifestations of the same teaching obra.
- **1946:** *Cosmic Radiation*. English-language book derived from Heisenberg’s lectures and research on cosmic rays; its relationship to earlier German lecture material makes it partly a new manifestation of an existing scientific exposition.
- **1955:** *Theoretische Physik*. Lecture-derived systematic presentation whose boundaries vary between editions; it is more accurately a teaching corpus than a single immutable text.
- **1966:** *Introduction to the Unified Field Theory of Elementary Particles*. Book based on Heisenberg’s 1965 Munich lectures. It presents an ongoing and ultimately unfinished theoretical project rather than a settled textbook.
- **1967:** *Einführung in die einheitliche Feldtheorie der Elementarteilchen*. German manifestation of the unified-field lectures; it should not be counted as an unrelated new work merely because its language and publication history differ.

### Philosophical books, essay collections, and memoir

- **1935:** *Wandlungen in den Grundlagen der Naturwissenschaft*. Essay collection assembled from previously delivered or published texts. Its repeatedly enlarged editions are changing manifestations, so no single contents list defines the obra for all dates.
- **1942:** *Die Einheit des naturwissenschaftlichen Weltbildes*. Published university lecture arguing for the unity of the scientific picture of the world (Cassidy 1942a).
- **1942–1944; first published 1984:** *Ordnung der Wirklichkeit*. Long philosophical manuscript left unpublished by Heisenberg and titled by the editors of the *Collected Works*. It resists classification as a finished book: it is an abandoned or suspended project whose familiar title is posthumous.
- **1955:** *Das Naturbild der heutigen Physik*. Philosophical book assembled around an earlier lecture; *The Physicist’s Conception of Nature* is an English manifestation.
- **1958:** *Physics and Philosophy: The Revolution in Modern Science*. Book made from the 1955–1956 Gifford Lectures at the University of St Andrews; its first book manifestation was in English.
- **1969:** *Der Teil und das Ganze: Gespräche im Umkreis der Atomphysik*. Memoir constructed as retrospective dialogues. It is not a transcript: historians dispute the reliability of particular reconstructed conversations, especially those concerning Heisenberg’s wartime actions and his 1941 meeting with Niels Bohr.
- **1971:** *Schritte über Grenzen: Gesammelte Reden und Aufsätze*. Authorial essay-and-address collection; *Across the Frontiers* is a translated and differently configured manifestation.
- **1977:** *Tradition in der Wissenschaft: Reden und Aufsätze*. Posthumous collection of speeches and essays. Because editorial selection followed Heisenberg’s death, it is partly an editorial creation and cannot be treated without qualification as a book he personally completed.

### Public lectures and philosophical essays

- **1927:** *Über die Grundprinzipien der “Quantenmechanik”*. Brief public scientific account of quantum mechanics (Cassidy 1927d).
- **1930–1931:** *Kausalgesetz und Quantenmechanik*. Philosophical lecture and published discussion on causality in quantum mechanics (Cassidy 1931g).
- **1931:** *Die Rolle der Unbestimmtheitsrelationen in der modernen Physik*. Lecture-text on the place of uncertainty relations in modern physics (Cassidy 1931i).
- **1931:** *Probleme der modernen Physik*. Newspaper essay for a general audience (Cassidy 1931j).
- **1933:** *Zur Geschichte der physikalischen Naturerklärung*. Historical-philosophical essay subsequently incorporated into collections.
- **1934:** *Die Entwicklung der Quantenmechanik*. Nobel lecture, delivered in 1933 and published in 1934; it is a ceremonial scientific account rather than an original research paper (Cassidy 1934i).
- **1934:** *Wissenschaft und technischer Fortschritt*. Public essay on science and technological progress (Cassidy 1934j).
- **1934:** *Wandlungen der Grundlagen der exakten Naturwissenschaft in jüngster Zeit*. Public lecture on recent changes in the foundations of exact science; later reprints and translations are manifestations of this text (Cassidy 1934e).
- **1936:** *Über die “Schauer” in der kosmischen Strahlung*. Popular scientific account of cosmic-ray showers (Cassidy 1936d).
- **1936:** *Zum Artikel “Deutsche und jüdische Physik”*. Newspaper intervention responding to the ideological attack on “Jewish physics.” It acts within a political controversy and therefore approaches a manifesto, although it is formally a rebuttal (Cassidy 1936e).
- **1936:** *Prinzipielle Fragen der modernen Physik*. Public lecture on foundational questions in modern physics (Cassidy 1936f).
- **1937:** *Gedanken der antiken Naturphilosophie in der modernen Physik*. Philosophical essay connecting ancient natural philosophy with modern physics.
- **1938:** *Die gegenwärtigen Aufgaben der theoretischen Physik*. Public lecture-text on the contemporary tasks of theoretical physics (Cassidy 1938g).
- **1938:** *Max Planck: Achtzig Jahre alt*. Newspaper tribute to Planck (Cassidy 1938k).
- **1942:** *Ein Grundproblem des Naturgeschehens: 100 Jahre Gesetz von der Erhaltung der Energie*. Broadcast lecture and newspaper essay marking the centenary of energy conservation; differently titled newspaper printings are manifestations, not separate obras (Cassidy 1942d).
- **1946:** *Wissenschaft als Mittel zur Verständigung unter den Völkern*. Address to Göttingen students proposing science as a means of international understanding.
- **1949:** *Naturwissenschaft und humanistische Bildung*. Address on the relation between scientific and humanistic education.
- **1950:** *Die Entwicklung der Deutung der Quantentheorie*. Historical lecture on the interpretation of quantum theory.
- **1952:** *Atomphysik und Kausalgesetz*. Public philosophical lecture on atomic physics and causality.
- **1953:** *Das Naturbild der heutigen Physik*. Lecture delivered to the Bavarian Academy of Fine Arts; it became the core of the 1955 book but remains a distinct oral and periodical manifestation.
- **1958:** *Die Plancksche Entdeckung und die philosophischen Grundfragen der Atomlehre*. Commemorative lecture connecting Planck’s discovery with philosophical problems in atomic theory.
- **1960:** *Die Abstraktion in der modernen Naturwissenschaft*. Ceremonial address on abstraction in modern science.
- **1964:** *Ο φυσικός κόσμος και η δομή της ύλης* (*Natural Law and the Structure of Matter*). Public lecture delivered on the Pnyx in Athens; the Greek first publication and later translations are manifestations of one lecture-obra.
- **1971:** *Die Bedeutung des Schönen in der exakten Naturwissenschaft*. Address, delivered in 1970 and published in 1971, on beauty in the exact sciences.

### Reviews, notices, and marginal texts

- **1924:** *Niels Bohr: Drei Aufsätze über Spektren und Atombau, 2. Auflage*. Book review; it is a made text but resists treatment as an autonomous work because its object and occasion are another author’s book (Cassidy 1924j).
- **1924:** *Der Kampf um die Überfahrt*. Unsigned youth-movement narrative attributed to Heisenberg in the *Collected Works*. The absence of a printed signature makes the attribution less secure than that of his signed scientific publications (Cassidy 1924k).
- **1925:** *Wilhelm Hanle: “Über magnetische Beeinflussung der Polarisation der Resonanzfluoreszenz”*. Review or abstracting notice; bibliographically distinct but not an independent research obra (Cassidy 1925e).
- **1927:** *Max Born: Probleme der Atomdynamik*. Book review (Cassidy 1927g).
- **1928:** *Discussione sulla communicazione Bohr*. Published conference discussion involving Born, Kramers, Heisenberg, Fermi, and Pauli. It records a collective oral event and cannot be cleanly assigned to Heisenberg alone (Cassidy 1928c).
- **1928:** *La mécanique des quanta*. Solvay report coauthored with Born, accompanied by multi-author discussion. It is simultaneously a review paper and the manifestation of a conference performance (Cassidy 1928d).
- **1931:** *Hermann Weyl: Gruppentheorie und Quantenmechanik, 2. umgearbeitete Auflage*. Book review (Cassidy 1931k).
- **1931:** *Max Planck: Positivismus und reale Aussenwelt*. Book review (Cassidy 1931l).
- **1936:** *Erwin Fues: Beugungsversuche mit Materiewellen; Einführung in die Quantenmechanik*. Book review (Cassidy 1936j).

### Protocols, memoranda, and manifestos

- **1949:** *Memorandum zur Förderung der naturwissenschaftlichen Forschung*. Policy memorandum directed to the West German Bundestag. It is a protocol-like administrative text intended to produce institutional action, not merely to describe science.
- **1955:** *Mainauer Kundgebung* (*Mainau Declaration*). Anti-nuclear declaration signed by Heisenberg with other Nobel laureates. It is a collective manifesto; signature establishes endorsement, not sole authorship.
- **1957:** *Erklärung der Göttinger Achtzehn* (*Göttingen Manifesto*). Public declaration opposing the nuclear armament of West Germany. Heisenberg was a signatory, while Carl Friedrich von Weizsäcker was its principal drafter; it therefore must not be attributed to Heisenberg as sole author.
- **1957–1958:** *Statements and memoranda on German nuclear-energy policy*. A body of interviews, press statements, committee texts, and institutional memoranda rather than one fixed work. Authorship is often collective or mediated by journalists, so individual items resist the status of fully author-controlled obras.

### Scientific and institutional projects

- **1925 onward:** *Matrix mechanics*. A mathematical-theoretical creation initiated by the 1925 “Umdeutung” paper and completed collaboratively with Born, Pascual Jordan, and others. It is an obra in the sense of a made formal system, not a single text solely owned by Heisenberg.
- **1927 onward:** *The uncertainty relation*. A theoretical relation first articulated in Heisenberg’s 1927 paper. “Uncertainty principle” names a scientific creation and its subsequent interpretive tradition, not a separate publication.
- **1932–1933:** *Neutron–proton model of the atomic nucleus*. Three-paper scientific project introducing an exchange-based nuclear model; its multipart form is integral to the work.
- **1939–1945:** *German uranium-reactor research*. Collective experimental and theoretical project in which Heisenberg led major portions of the reactor-physics work. It resists attribution as his work alone: the surviving apparatus, calculations, and reports were made by a network of institutes and researchers under the National Socialist state.
- **1943–1944:** *S-matrix theory of elementary particles*. Four-part theoretical project, one part remaining unpublished until 1989. Its unfinished condition is part of the work’s identity.
- **1953–1976:** *Nonlinear unified field theory of elementary particles*. Long-running research project pursued with Pauli and others through papers, lectures, and books. It remained unfinished at Heisenberg’s death and never became an accepted physical theory.
- **1922–1976; first publications continuing posthumously:** *Scientific and cultural writings*. The complete bibliographical corpus comprises 587 identified first publications within David C. Cassidy’s 1,165-entry bibliography; the larger number includes translations, reprints, excerpts, editions, and posthumous manifestations. Some wartime reports are missing or possibly lost, and some conversations and interviews survive only through editorial mediation, so “everything” cannot honestly mean every utterance or manuscript Heisenberg ever produced.
