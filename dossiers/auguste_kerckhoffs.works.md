## Works by Auguste Kerckhoffs

### Literary and linguistic studies

- **1852:** *An Essay on Milton’s Paradise Lost*. A periodical essay whose existence is known only from an 1859 newspaper notice; the standard scholarly bibliography does not identify a surviving copy, so its title, extent, and completeness remain bibliographically uncertain. [Caraco, Géraud-Stewart, and Naccache, pp. 32–33](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/556.pdf)
- **1852:** *Une étude étymologique*. A linguistic periodical article likewise attested retrospectively by the *Opregte Haarlemsche Courant* rather than by an examined copy; it resists treatment as a securely recovered work. [Caraco, Géraud-Stewart, and Naccache, pp. 32–33](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/556.pdf)
- **1859:** *Een word over de vlaemsche taal*. A sixteen-page pamphlet on the Flemish language; the bibliography prints *word*, while later references sometimes normalize the Dutch to *woord*, so the title form should not be silently regularized. [Caraco, Géraud-Stewart, and Naccache, p. 32](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/556.pdf)
- **1868:** *Petite grammaire anglaise, contenant un vocabulaire, des dialogues et des exercices de conversation sur des sujets littéraires*. A 135-page instructional grammar: not merely a description of English but a teaching protocol composed of vocabulary, dialogues, and exercises. [Caraco, Géraud-Stewart, and Naccache, p. 32](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/556.pdf)
- **1877:** *Daniel Casper von Lohenstein’s Trauerspiele mit besonderer Berücksichtigung der Cleopatra: Beitrag zur Geschichte des Dramas im XVII. Jahrhundert*. A 110-page work of literary history and criticism, apparently derived from Kerckhoffs’s doctoral research. [Caraco, Géraud-Stewart, and Naccache, p. 32](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/556.pdf); [digitized 1877 edition](https://books.google.com/books?id=M8syAQAAMAAJ)
- **circa 1880:** *Nouvelle méthode pour apprendre facilement les déclinaisons allemandes*. A ten-page pedagogical protocol for learning German declensions; the scholarly bibliography dates it only approximately, and no firmer year should be supplied. [Caraco, Géraud-Stewart, and Naccache, p. 32](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/556.pdf)
- **1892:** *Résumé de grammaire allemande, suivi d’un vocabulaire de 1600 principaux mots de la langue*. A compact, ninety-six-page German grammar and vocabulary manual made for instruction. [Caraco, Géraud-Stewart, and Naccache, p. 32](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/556.pdf)

### Cryptological study and procedure

- **January–February 1883:** *La cryptographie militaire, ou les chiffres usités en temps de guerre, avec un nouveau procédé de déchiffrement applicable aux systèmes à double clef*. A two-part journal study, also issued as a sixty-four-page offprint: simultaneously a survey, an institutional manifesto for reforming military cryptography, and a protocol describing a new decipherment procedure. The journal parts and separate printing are manifestations of one obra, not separate works. [Caraco, Géraud-Stewart, and Naccache, p. 32](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/556.pdf); [Philippe Guillot’s scholarly study](https://journals.openedition.org/bibnum/555); [contemporary bibliographical record](https://books.google.nl/books?id=VbQBAAAAYAAJ)

### Archaeology and anthropology

- **1873:** *L’Art monumental dans ses rapports avec les idées religieuses*. An archaeological essay relating monumental art to religious ideas, published in—and also extracted from—the bulletin of the Seine-et-Marne archaeological society; the extract is a manifestation, not another obra. [Caraco, Géraud-Stewart, and Naccache, p. 33](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/556.pdf)
- **1884:** *La mâchoire de Maestricht et les récentes découvertes*. A physical-anthropological article on the Maastricht jaw and related discoveries, published in the *Bulletins de la Société d’anthropologie de Paris*. [Caraco, Géraud-Stewart, and Naccache, p. 33](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/556.pdf)
- **1901:** *Discussions sur la circoncision, suite à une intervention de P. Garnault*. A published record of discussion involving Émile Atgier, Léon Azoulay, Kerckhoffs, and Charles Lejeune. It resists being called a singly authored work: the bibliographic object is a collectively produced transcript or report of spoken interventions. [Caraco, Géraud-Stewart, and Naccache, p. 33](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/556.pdf)

### Constructed-language studies, manuals, and reference works

- **1885:** *La nouvelle langue universelle*. An article introducing and evaluating Volapük in *L’Homme: Journal illustré des sciences anthropologiques*; it belongs as much to advocacy for a language project as to descriptive linguistics. [Caraco, Géraud-Stewart, and Naccache, p. 33](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/556.pdf)
- **1885:** *Examen critique de la langue universelle de Sudre*. A jointly authored critical study with Boleslas Gajewski of François Sudre’s musical universal language, or Solresol; some later references expand the title with *musicale*, but the scholarly bibliography records the shorter form. [Caraco, Géraud-Stewart, and Naccache, p. 33](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/556.pdf)
- **1886:** *Cours complet de Volapük contenant des thèmes et des versions avec corrigés et un vocabulaire de 2500 mots*. A full teaching protocol combining grammar, translation exercises, corrections, and vocabulary. It passed through numerous editions and adaptations; the bibliography groups the first through eighth editions as manifestations of one course, although other accounts place the eighth edition in 1887 rather than 1886. [Caraco, Géraud-Stewart, and Naccache, p. 33](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/556.pdf); [bibliographical account of the edition history](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volap%C3%BCk)
- **1886:** *Grammaire abrégée de volapük, précédée d’un exposé de la question*. An abridged grammar coupled to an argument for the international-language project: both manual and advocacy text. [Caraco, Géraud-Stewart, and Naccache, p. 33](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/556.pdf)
- **1886:** *Premiers éléments de Volapük: Langue commerciale internationale*. A sixteen-page elementary primer presenting Volapük specifically as a language of international commerce. [Caraco, Géraud-Stewart, and Naccache, pp. 22, 33](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/556.pdf)
- **1887:** *Dictionnaire volapük-français et français-volapük, précédé d’une grammaire complète de la langue*. A 319-page bilingual dictionary preceded by a complete grammar: a reference system as well as a lexical work. [Caraco, Géraud-Stewart, and Naccache, p. 33](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/556.pdf); [bibliographic description](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volap%C3%BCk)
- **1887:** *Examen critique de quelques simplifications qu’il y a lieu d’introduire dans le volapük*. A sixteen-page reform manifesto, first appearing in *Le Volapük* and also issued separately; it acts upon the language by proposing changes rather than merely describing it. [Caraco, Géraud-Stewart, and Naccache, pp. 22, 33](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/556.pdf)
- **1887:** *Yelabuk pedipedelas: Ninöl: 1ido Nemis e ladetis Volapükatidelas e Spodalas; 2ido Lisedi lefulnik bukas, penotas, gasedas e. l. pepüböl al pakön u födön Volapüki*. The first *Graduates’ Yearbook*: a directory of teachers and correspondents combined with a bibliography of publications promoting Volapük. It is an infrastructural document of a movement rather than an ordinary authored book. [Caraco, Géraud-Stewart, and Naccache, p. 33](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/556.pdf); [Volapük collection record](https://volapuk.temerov.org/Volap%C3%BCkanef/vol/lautan.php?nem=Kerckhoffs_A&rigapad=liseds%2Ftidals.php)
- **1889:** *Yelabuk pedipedelas, yelod telid*. The second annual volume of the graduates’ yearbook and bibliographical directory. Its serial character makes it a continuation of an institutional documentation project, while its distinct annual contents make the volume a separate made object. [Volapük collection record](https://volapuk.temerov.org/Volap%C3%BCkanef/vol/lautan.php?nem=Kerckhoffs_A&rigapad=liseds%2Ftidals.php)
- **1890:** *Glamat nomik u Noms stabik volapüka pelensumöl fa Kadem, dil balid*. A fifteen-page statement of the Volapük Academy’s newly adopted grammar or fundamental rules. Kerckhoffs is bibliographically named as author, but the title presents the rules as adopted by the Academy, so individual and institutional authorship overlap. [Caraco, Géraud-Stewart, and Naccache, p. 33](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/556.pdf)
- **1890:** *Timpenäd pepübül fa dilekef kadema*. A two-page text in *Calabled kadema* issued in connection with the Academy’s leadership. Its exact generic status is difficult to recover from the bibliography: it is best treated as a brief institutional communication rather than forced into “book” or “article.” [Caraco, Géraud-Stewart, and Naccache, p. 33](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/556.pdf)

### Language-reform and editorial projects

- **1886–1889:** *Le Volapük: Revue publiée sous le patronage de l’Association française pour la propagation du volapük*. A periodical and collective editorial project launched under Kerckhoffs’s direction; individual articles were written by multiple contributors, so the journal as a whole is not a singly authored text. The Hugo Schuchardt Archive calls Kerckhoffs its editor but mentions only three 1886 issues, whereas the surviving serial is conventionally dated 1886–1889. [Hugo Schuchardt Archive](https://gams.uni-graz.at/o:hsa.person.1898/sdef:TEI/get?locale=de); [serial description](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volap%C3%BCk)
- **1887–1890:** *Kerckhoffs’s simplified Volapük reform*. A language project—sometimes retrospectively called “Nuvo-Volapük”—embodied in the 1887 *Examen critique* and the Academy rules of 1890 rather than in one securely bounded work. It resists classification as a text because what Kerckhoffs attempted to make was a revised communicative system; the extent to which it constituted a distinct language, rather than proposals for reforming Volapük, is interpretive. [Hugo Schuchardt Archive](https://gams.uni-graz.at/o:hsa.person.1898/sdef:TEI/get?locale=de); [Caraco, Géraud-Stewart, and Naccache, pp. 22–24, 33](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/556.pdf)

### Dramatic work of disputed attribution

- **1898:** *Valkenbergs helden onder ’t Fransche schrikbewind in Limburg: drama in 2 bedrijven*. A two-act historical drama printed at Valkenburg. The standard scholarly bibliography explicitly warns that it may have been written by a namesake; it belongs here only as an unresolved attribution, not as a secure Kerckhoffs work. [Caraco, Géraud-Stewart, and Naccache, p. 32](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/556.pdf)

### Sources

- Jean-Claude Caraco, Rémi Géraud-Stewart, and David Naccache, [“Kerckhoffs’ Legacy”](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/556.pdf), especially its research bibliography, pp. 32–33, and its discussion of Kerckhoffs’s Volapük activity, pp. 21–24. This is the principal scholarly bibliography used here; its authors state that further sources remain unexploited, so the surviving record should not be represented as demonstrably complete.

- Philippe Guillot, [“Auguste Kerckhoffs et la cryptographie militaire”](https://journals.openedition.org/bibnum/555), *Bibnum*, used to establish the two-part publication and separate manifestation of *La cryptographie militaire*.
