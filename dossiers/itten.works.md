## Works by Johannes Itten

### Paintings, watercolours, drawings, and collages

- **1907–1938:** *Paintings, watercolours, drawings, and collages, catalogue numbers 1907–1938*. This is not one obra but the first chronological portion of Itten’s pictorial œuvre; every individual object is registered in volume I of Christoph Wagner’s catalogue raisonné, whose alphabetical title index and concordance preserve untitled, lost, and recto-verso works without turning them into invented titles. [[Catalogue raisonné, vol. I](https://press.uchicago.edu/ucp/books/book/distributed/J/bo37865322.html)]

- **1939–1967:** *Paintings, watercolours, drawings, and collages, catalogue numbers 1939–1967*. This is likewise a catalogue-defined body rather than a single work, comprising the second chronological portion of the pictorial œuvre. [[Catalogue raisonné, vol. II record](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BC14094077)]

- **1907–1967:** *Untitled, lost, destroyed, or documentary-only pictorial works*. These resist ordinary naming and, in some cases, even objecthood: the catalogue raisonné records works known only through photographs or written sources, as well as works without fixed titles. Earlier coverage was incomplete—Anneliese Itten’s 1972 catalogue listed about 1,200 works, while Wagner’s project added more than 1,000—so “complete” remains a scholarly claim subject to later discoveries. [[Regensburg research project](https://www.uni-regensburg.de/philosophie-kunst-geschichte-gesellschaft/institut-fuer-kunstgeschichte/forschung/forschungsprojekt-johannes-itten)] [[Catalogue methodology](https://dokumen.pub/handbuch-werkverzeichnis-uvrekatalog-catalogue-raisonne-9783110734492-9783110738872.html)]

### Prints, sculpture, textiles, and furniture

- **1888–1967:** *Graphic works*. A catalogue-defined corpus of prints and related graphic objects rather than a single obra; individual titles, dates, attributions, and present locations are registered in Wagner’s volume III. [[Catalogue raisonné, vol. III](https://press.uchicago.edu/ucp/books/book/distributed/J/bo214796104.html)]

- **1888–1967:** *Sculptures and three-dimensional studies*. This heterogeneous corpus includes autonomous sculpture and objects whose status approaches pedagogical demonstration rather than conventional finished artwork. [[Catalogue raisonné, vol. III](https://press.uchicago.edu/ucp/books/book/distributed/J/bo214796104.html)]

- **c. 1923–1967:** *Textiles, carpets, and tapestries*. These are textile obras and designs, not paintings translated into cloth; the corpus reflects Itten’s work at Herrliberg, Krefeld, and Zürich and is catalogued in volume III. [[Catalogue raisonné, vol. III](https://press.uchicago.edu/ucp/books/book/distributed/J/bo214796104.html)] [[Historical Lexicon of Switzerland](https://hls-dhs-dss.ch/de/articles/022036/2024-12-19/)]

- **1888–1967:** *Furniture and furnishing designs*. A small design corpus catalogued separately from Itten’s pictorial work; some entries are projects or documentary survivals rather than extant manufactured objects. [[Catalogue raisonné, vol. III](https://press.uchicago.edu/ucp/books/book/distributed/J/bo214796104.html)]

### Gesamtkunstwerk and pedagogical projects

- **1916–1919:** *Vienna private art school and preliminary-course experiments*. A pedagogical project combining bodily exercises, rhythm, materials, composition, colour, and spiritual discipline; its “work” survives diffusely in lesson structures, student work, and Itten’s diaries rather than as one bounded object. [[Regensburg research project](https://www.uni-regensburg.de/philosophie-kunst-geschichte-gesellschaft/institut-fuer-kunstgeschichte/forschung/forschungsprojekt-johannes-itten)]

- **1919–1923:** *Vorkurs at the Staatliches Bauhaus Weimar*. A protocol-generating educational project and practical Gesamtkunstwerk in which exercises, breathing and movement, material studies, formal analysis, and communal life were treated as inseparable. Authorship is necessarily distributed between Itten’s method and his students’ manifestations. [[Regensburg research project](https://www.uni-regensburg.de/philosophie-kunst-geschichte-gesellschaft/institut-fuer-kunstgeschichte/forschung/forschungsprojekt-johannes-itten)] [[Museo Thyssen-Bornemisza](https://www.museothyssen.org/en/collection/artists/itten-johannes)]

- **1926–1934:** *Itten-Schule, Berlin*. An institutional obra and pedagogical project for painting, graphic art, photography, architecture, and design; it was closed under National Socialism, so its identity lies in its curriculum, teaching community, and surviving documents rather than in a single building or text. [[Regensburg research project](https://www.uni-regensburg.de/philosophie-kunst-geschichte-gesellschaft/institut-fuer-kunstgeschichte/forschung/forschungsprojekt-johannes-itten)] [[Museo Thyssen-Bornemisza](https://www.museothyssen.org/en/collection/artists/itten-johannes)]

- **1932–1938:** *Höhere Fachschule für Textile Flächenkunst, Krefeld*. An educational and textile-design project directed by Itten, joining colour theory, industrial practice, photography, pattern, and weaving; the Nazi authorities closed it. [[Historical Lexicon of Switzerland](https://hls-dhs-dss.ch/de/articles/022036/2024-12-19/)] [[Regensburg research project](https://www.uni-regensburg.de/philosophie-kunst-geschichte-gesellschaft/institut-fuer-kunstgeschichte/forschung/forschungsprojekt-johannes-itten)]

- **1938–1953:** *Reorganisation and direction of the Kunstgewerbeschule Zürich*. An institutional project rather than an individually authored artwork: Itten shaped curricula, teaching methods, and the school’s conception of artistic education. [[Regensburg research project](https://www.uni-regensburg.de/philosophie-kunst-geschichte-gesellschaft/institut-fuer-kunstgeschichte/forschung/forschungsprojekt-johannes-itten)]

- **1943–1960:** *Zürcher Textilfachschule*. A long-term pedagogical and administrative project directed by Itten, extending his theories of colour and form into textile education and production. [[Regensburg research project](https://www.uni-regensburg.de/philosophie-kunst-geschichte-gesellschaft/institut-fuer-kunstgeschichte/forschung/forschungsprojekt-johannes-itten)]

- **1949–1956:** *Museum Rietberg, Zürich*. A curatorial and institutional project: Itten helped build the museum from 1949 and decisively shaped it as its founding director from 1952 to 1956. It resists attribution as an individual “work,” since collecting, municipal government, architecture, and other participants were constitutive. [[Regensburg research project](https://www.uni-regensburg.de/philosophie-kunst-geschichte-gesellschaft/institut-fuer-kunstgeschichte/forschung/forschungsprojekt-johannes-itten)]

### Books and autonomous theoretical obras

- **1961:** *Kunst der Farbe: Subjektives Erleben und objektives Erkennen als Wege zur Kunst*. A systematic colour-theory book combining subjective exercises, objective ordering systems, diagrams, and reproductions; later translations, abridgements, and reprints are editions or derivative manifestations, not new works. [[Zentralbibliothek Zürich](https://www.zb.uzh.ch/en/fokus/beitrag/johannes-itten)] [[Springer reference entry](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-27851-8_340-2)]

- **1963:** *Mein Vorkurs am Bauhaus: Gestaltungs- und Formenlehre*. A retrospective pedagogical book and collection of protocols for exercises in contrast, material, rhythm, form, and composition; much of its visual evidence consists of student work, so the book is Itten’s authored framework but not the sole authorship of every object reproduced in it. [[Zentralbibliothek Zürich](https://www.zb.uzh.ch/en/fokus/beitrag/johannes-itten)] [[Table of contents](https://toc.library.ethz.ch/objects/pdf03/z01_0-442-24044-9_01.pdf)]

### Essays, lectures, manifestos, and protocols

- **1916:** *Fragmentarisches*. Fragments rather than a completed treatise; their incompleteness is part of their status. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1917:** *Über Komposition*. A theoretical text on pictorial composition. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1921:** *Analysen alter Meister*. A published analytical and pedagogical text in *Utopia: Dokumente der Wirklichkeit*, accompanied by Itten’s twelve-part colour sphere; it acts as both theory and protocol for seeing and making. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)] [[Bibliographic account](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Itten)]

- **1922:** *Ausstellung von Arbeiten der Gesellen und Lehrlinge im Staatlichen Bauhaus Weimar*. An exhibition statement concerning work by Bauhaus students and apprentices; the exhibited objects are collaborative pedagogical manifestations, not all works by Itten. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1923:** *Kunst – Hand – Werk*. An intervention on art, handcraft, and work whose compressed, declarative character approaches a manifesto. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1925:** *Kunstgewerbe oder Arbeit*. An essay opposing or testing the categories of applied art and work. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **c. 1926:** *Mazdaznan*. An incompletely dated text on the spiritual doctrine that informed Itten’s teaching and daily practices. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1926:** *Lehrplan der Itten-Schule Berlin*. A curriculum and protocol for an institution, not merely a description of it. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1927:** *Wie lernt man Kunstwerke richtig sehen?* A pedagogical protocol for learning to perceive works of art. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1930:** *Pädagogische Fragmente einer Formenlehre*. Fragmentary components of a theory and protocol of form; the title itself refuses the status of a finished system. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1930:** *Einleitung zum „Tagebuch“*. A framing text for Itten’s diary material rather than an autonomous book. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1931:** *Kunstunterricht*. An essay or lecture on art instruction. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1932:** *Die Fachschule für Textile Flächenkunst in Krefeld*. An institutional statement describing and advocating the Krefeld school. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1932:** *Die Bedeutung der geschmacklichen Ausbildung für Drucker und Färber*. A vocational-pedagogical argument concerning aesthetic education for printers and dyers. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1937:** *Mode als Zeitausdruck*. An essay treating fashion as a manifestation of its historical moment. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1937:** *Muster mit dem Photoapparat*. A practical text on producing pattern through photography and therefore partly a technical protocol. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1938:** *Das Abstrakte in der Kunst und die Kunst der Abstrakten*. An art-theoretical essay or lecture on abstraction. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1938:** *Über die zukünftige Arbeit in Zürich*. A programmatic statement for Itten’s forthcoming Zürich work; it belongs to a project before its realization. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1938:** *Zwölf Thesen zu meinem Unterricht*. Twelve declarative propositions about teaching; their prescriptive and performative form makes them a pedagogical manifesto. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1939:** *Zur Ausstellung „Aus meinem Unterricht“*. An exhibition statement framing manifestations of Itten’s teaching rather than claiming the exhibited student works as his own. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1942:** *Ostasiatische Tuschmalerei*. An essay or lecture on East Asian ink painting. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1944:** *Zur Ausstellung „Die Farbe“*. A curatorial exhibition text on colour. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1945:** *Zur Ausstellung „Alte Glasmalerei der Schweiz“*. A curatorial text for an exhibition of historic Swiss stained glass. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1947:** *Zur Ausstellung „Schweizer Keramik“*. A curatorial text for an exhibition of Swiss ceramics. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1947:** *Wassily Kandinsky – Kunst und Erziehung*. A commemorative or critical essay connecting Kandinsky’s art with education. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1950:** *Grundlagen der Kunsterziehung*. A theoretical statement on the foundations of art education. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1950:** *Zur Ausstellung „Schweizerischer Werkbund“*. A curatorial or institutional exhibition text. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1950:** *Über die Möglichkeiten der modernen Kunst*. A lecture or essay on the possibilities of modern art. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1951:** *Erinnerungen an die „Sturm“-Zeit*. A memoir of Itten’s involvement with the *Sturm* milieu. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1951:** *Zur Ausstellung „Mosaiken aus Ravenna“*. A curatorial text accompanying an exhibition of Ravenna mosaics. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1949–1955:** *Das Museum Rietberg in Zürich*. A text developed across the years in which Itten was building and directing the museum; it is both institutional account and statement of curatorial intention. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1951:** *Lenin-Reliquien gegen altchinesische Plastik*. A polemical comparison involving political relics and ancient Chinese sculpture. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1952:** *Das Sammeln – Der Sammler – Die Sammlung (Eduard von der Heydt zum 70. Geburtstag)*. An essay on collecting, collectors, and collections written for Eduard von der Heydt’s seventieth birthday. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1953:** *Kinderzeichnung*. An essay or lecture on children’s drawing. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1954:** *Pädagogisches-Methodisches zum Kunstunterricht*. A methodological text whose subject and operation are pedagogical protocol. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1957:** *Die Ausbildung der Industrial Designer*. A programmatic text on training industrial designers. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1958:** *Abstrakte Malerei*. An essay or lecture on abstract painting. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1958:** *Schöpferischer Automatismus*. An essay or lecture on creative automatism. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1961:** *Über das Schöpferische in der Erziehung*. A theoretical text on creativity in education. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1961:** *Aus „Kunst der Farbe“*. An excerpt-manifestation of the book *Kunst der Farbe*, not a separate newly composed obra. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1962:** *Der Vorkurs*. A retrospective account of the preliminary course and its method. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1963:** *Adolf Hölzel und sein Kreis*. A memoir and historical essay about Itten’s teacher and artistic circle. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1964:** *Wie konnte die große Wirkung des Bauhauses entstehen?* A retrospective explanation of the Bauhaus’s influence. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1965:** *Dank für die Verleihung der Ehrendoktorwürde*. A ceremonial acceptance address rather than an autonomous theoretical treatise. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1965:** *Die Erziehung zum schöpferischen Menschen*. A pedagogical manifesto or lecture advocating education toward creative personhood. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1966:** *Die Lehrmethode an Kunstgewerbe- und Werkkunstschulen*. A methodological and prescriptive text about teaching at applied-arts schools. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

### Diaries, letters, and poems

- **1909–1967:** *Tagebücher*. A long-running body of notebooks combining autobiography, colour and form theory, teaching preparations, drawings, diagrams, spiritual reflection, and documentary fragments: a textual-visual Gesamtkunstwerk rather than a single finished publication. The later books *Elemente der Bildenden Kunst* and *Bildanalysen* are posthumous editorial selections, not additional works completed by Itten. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)] [[Regensburg research project](https://www.uni-regensburg.de/philosophie-kunst-geschichte-gesellschaft/institut-fuer-kunstgeschichte/forschung/forschungsprojekt-johannes-itten)]

- **1917–1918:** *Gedichte der Jahre 1917–1918*. A corpus of poems preserved and grouped by the collected-works editors; treating the group as one obra would conceal that its individual constituents have separate manuscript identities, but the accessible scholarly contents do not supply a complete title list. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1919–1923:** *Gedichte der Jahre 1919–1923*. A second editorially defined poetry corpus; some items may be untitled, and no title or date should be manufactured for them. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)]

- **1909–1967:** *Briefe*. A documentary corpus rather than a finite authored work: individual letters were situational communications, often co-produced by correspondence, and the surviving archive cannot be equated automatically with everything Itten wrote. [[Collected works contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)] [[Zentralbibliothek Zürich](https://www.zb.uzh.ch/en/fokus/beitrag/johannes-itten)]

- **undated and various dates:** *Pedagogical notes, preparatory studies, colour-theory drafts, and unpublished fragments*. These archival things resist the designation “works”: some are protocols, some stages of projects, and some documentary traces. The scholarly project reports that Itten’s written estate was digitised and is still being connected to the evolving digital catalogue raisonné. [[Regensburg research project](https://www.uni-regensburg.de/philosophie-kunst-geschichte-gesellschaft/institut-fuer-kunstgeschichte/forschung/forschungsprojekt-johannes-itten)]

### Posthumous manifestations, not new works

- **1972:** *Johannes Itten: Werke und Schriften*. A posthumous collected-works and catalogue-raisonné manifestation edited by Willy Rotzler, with the catalogue compiled by Anneliese Itten; its approximately 1,200 entries were selective and have been substantially superseded. [[Contents](https://d-nb.info/730060268/04)] [[Catalogue history](https://dokumen.pub/handbuch-werkverzeichnis-uvrekatalog-catalogue-raisonne-9783110734492-9783110738872.html)]

- **1988:** *Bildanalysen*. A posthumously edited assembly of Itten’s image analyses, not a book completed and issued by him during his lifetime. [[Scholarly bibliography summary](https://dewiki.de/Lexikon/Johannes_Itten)]

- **2002:** *Elemente der Bildenden Kunst: Studienausgabe des Tagebuchs*. A posthumous study edition selected from Itten’s diaries; it is an editorial manifestation of the diary project, not an independently authored 2002 obra. [[Scholarly bibliography summary](https://dewiki.de/Lexikon/Johannes_Itten)]
