## Works by Eugen Böhm Ritter von Bawerk

### Books, treatises, and multipart projects

- **1881:** *Rechte und Verhältnisse vom Standpunkte der volkswirtschaftlichen Güterlehre*. A critical monograph asking whether legal rights and relationships count as economic goods; it was later absorbed into the collected writings but is an independent obra. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1884–1914:** *Kapital und Kapitalzins*. Böhm-Bawerk’s principal, repeatedly revised theoretical project rather than one stable book: its constituent divisions appeared at different dates, and the familiar three-volume arrangement was made posthumously by editors. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1884; revised 1900 and 1914:** *Kapital und Kapitalzins. Erste Abteilung: Geschichte und Kritik der Capitalzins-Theorien*. The historical-critical first division of the project; the revisions added substantial new writing, including a survey of the literature since 1884, so the editions are successive states of the obra rather than mere reprints. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1889; revised 1902 and 1909–1912:** *Kapital und Kapitalzins. Zweite Abteilung: Positive Theorie des Capitales*. The constructive second division, presenting Böhm-Bawerk’s theory of capital, production time, and interest; its third edition appeared in two parts and carried new excursuses. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1909–1912:** *Exkurse zur positiven Theorie des Capitales*. A body of supplementary essays first attached to the two parts of the third edition of the *Positive Theorie*; their separate 1921 volume was an editorial, posthumous manifestation, not a new work made in 1921. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1886:** *Grundzüge der Theorie des wirthschaftlichen Güterwerthes*. A two-part journal treatise on subjective value, exchange value, price, competition, supply and demand; its later book-form reprint should not be counted as another obra. [Christian Gehrke’s scholarly survey](https://www.econ.mpg.de/79668/CCvW-Boehm-Bawerk.pdf)
- **1896:** *Zum Abschluss des Marxschen Systems*. A sustained critical essay written for the *Festgaben für Karl Knies*, examining the relation between the first and third volumes of Marx’s *Capital*; later translations often present it as a book. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1899–1900:** *Einige strittige Fragen der Capitalstheorie: Drei Abhandlungen*. Three connected capital-theory essays first serialized in 1899 and issued together as a pamphlet in 1900; the pamphlet is a collected manifestation of the serial work. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1906–1907:** *Zur neuesten Literatur über Kapital und Kapitalzins*. A critical survey of recent capital-and-interest literature, published in a journal and then as an offprint; portions also appeared in English, so its German and English forms should not be mistaken for wholly separate obras. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1913:** *Eine „dynamische“ Theorie des Kapitalzinses*. A lengthy intervention against Joseph Schumpeter’s theory of interest, published in two installments and also as an offprint; the second installment functions as concluding remarks, not an unrelated work. [Christian Gehrke’s scholarly survey](https://www.econ.mpg.de/79668/CCvW-Boehm-Bawerk.pdf)

### Essays and theoretical interventions

- **1890:** *Ein Zwischenwort zur Werththeorie*. A journal intervention in contemporary value theory. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1890:** *The Historical vs. the Deductive Method in Political Economy*. An English-language methodological essay, translated by Henrietta Leonard, defending a role for abstract theory against an exclusively historical economics; its status resists the category of a wholly new English work because it is a translated manifestation of Böhm-Bawerk’s German argument. [RePEc bibliographic record](https://ideas.repec.org/a/sae/anname/v1y1890i2p244-271.html)
- **1891:** *The Austrian Economists*. An expository and polemical essay introducing the Austrian school’s approach to an English-language readership, published through Henrietta Leonard’s translation. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1892:** *Wert, Kosten und Grenznutzen*. A major journal essay relating cost to marginal utility and defending the Austrian account of value. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1892:** *Unsere Aufgaben*. A programmatic inaugural editorial for the *Zeitschrift für Volkswirtschaft, Socialpolitik und Verwaltung*; because it declares the new journal’s intellectual task, it approaches a manifesto more closely than a descriptive article. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1894:** *Der letzte Maßstab des Güterwertes*. A substantial essay on the ultimate measure of value, followed in the same year by an additional note prompted by Rudolf Auspitz. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1894:** *Zusatzbemerkung zu „Der letzte Maßstab des Güterwertes“*. A short supplementary intervention concerning Auspitz; it resists treatment as a fully autonomous work because it exists to amend and extend the preceding essay. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1895–1896:** *The Positive Theory of Capital and Its Critics*. A three-part English-language polemic answering John Bates Clark, Francis Amasa Walker, Horace White, Hugo Bilgram, Silas MacVane, and Frederick Hawley; it is one serial argumentative project rather than three unrelated papers. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1895:** *The Origin of Interest: Reply to Professor Clark*. A separate reply in the Böhm-Bawerk–Clark controversy over the origin of interest. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1896:** *Historische und theoretische Nationalökonomie*. A methodological essay on the relation between historical and theoretical economics; it overlaps in argument with the earlier English essay but is catalogued as a distinct German publication. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1898:** *Zur theoretischen Nationalökonomie der letzten Jahre*. A synthetic critical essay on recent theoretical economics, containing the discussions conventionally identified as *Kostenwert und Nutzwert* and *Vom Gegenstand der Wertlehre*; those headings resist counting as wholly separate obras. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1901:** *The Function of Saving*. An English-language reply to L. G. Bostedo on the economic function of saving. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1906:** *Capital and Interest Once More, I: Capital versus Capital Goods*. An English intervention in the capital controversy; it is related to the contemporary German literature survey but has its own polemical occasion and publication. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1907:** *Capital and Interest Once More, II: A Relapse to the Productivity Theory*. The second English intervention in that controversy. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1907:** *The Nature of Capital: A Rejoinder*. A further reply on the definition and persistence of capital. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1907:** *Professor Clarks Lehren über Kapital und Kapitalzins*. A German reassessment of John Bates Clark’s doctrines of capital and interest. [Mises.at chronological catalogue](https://mises.at/en/denker/eugen-von-boehm-bawerk)
- **1912:** *Quelques remarques peu nouvelles sur une vieille question* / *Einige nicht neue Bemerkungen über eine alte Frage*. A French-published intervention on an established theoretical question; bibliographies vary between citing the French manifestation and supplying a German title, so no unattested separate German publication should be inferred. [Mises.at chronological catalogue](https://mises.at/en/denker/eugen-von-boehm-bawerk)
- **1914:** *Nachwort*. A short reply to Ludwig Mezey’s defense and supplementation of Böhm-Bawerk’s price theory; it is a dependent afterword rather than a self-standing treatise. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1914:** *Macht oder ökonomisches Gesetz?* A late, major essay on whether economic outcomes are governed by power or economic law. Although sometimes issued and translated as a small book, its original manifestation was a journal article. [Christian Gehrke’s scholarly survey](https://www.econ.mpg.de/79668/CCvW-Boehm-Bawerk.pdf)

### Reference articles

- **1892; later revised:** *Kapital*. An encyclopaedia article for the *Handwörterbuch der Staatswissenschaften*; later-edition dates, including 1910, represent revised manifestations of the entry rather than securely distinct works. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **by 1914; published posthumously:** *Zins*. An encyclopaedia article for the *Handwörterbuch der Staatswissenschaften*. Catalogues disagree between a prewar date around 1911 and publication in 1924; because Böhm-Bawerk died in 1914, the later date must denote a posthumous manifestation, and the extent of editorial intervention is not made explicit in the consulted bibliographies. [Mises.at chronological catalogue](https://mises.at/en/denker/eugen-von-boehm-bawerk), [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)

### Reviews and critical notices

- **1889:** *Die klassische Nationalökonomie*. A review essay on Lujo Brentano’s work, published in the *Göttingische Gelehrte Anzeigen*; it is criticism occasioned by another book, not a book by Böhm-Bawerk. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1890:** *Wasserab’s Preise und Krisen*. A critical review of Karl Wasserab’s study of prices and crises. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1890:** *MacVane’s Political Economy*. A substantial critical notice engaging Silas MacVane’s political economy. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1890:** *Professor Giddings on the Theory of Capital*. A brief jointly signed intervention by James Bonar and Böhm-Bawerk in the capital controversy; its coauthorship should not be silently converted into sole authorship. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1892:** *Patten’s Theory of Dynamic Economics*. A review of Simon Patten’s work. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1894:** *Bonar’s Philosophy and Political Economy*. A review of James Bonar’s book. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1894:** *Wicksell’s Über Wert, Kapital und Rente*. A review of Knut Wicksell’s study of value, capital, and rent. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1894:** *Smart’s Translation of Wieser’s Natural Value*. A review of William Smart’s English translation of Friedrich von Wieser. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1895:** *Sulzer’s Die wirtschaftlichen Grundgesetze*. A short critical notice of Hermann Sulzer’s work. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1895:** *Naumann’s Die Lehre vom Wert*. A short review of Friedrich Naumann’s work on value. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1896:** *Smart’s Studies in Economics*. A review notice of William Smart’s essays. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1896:** *Schüller’s Die classische Nationalökonomie*. A review notice of Richard Schüller’s work. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1896:** *Montemartini’s Il risparmio nella economia pura*. A review notice of Giovanni Montemartini’s work on saving. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1896:** *Pierson’s Leerboek der Staathuishoudkunde*. A review notice of Nikolaas Pierson’s economics textbook. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1898:** *Bergmann’s Wirtschaftskrisen*. A review notice of Eugen von Bergmann’s work on economic crises. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)

### Public-economic intervention

- **1914:** *Unsere passive Handelsbilanz* / *Unsere passiven Handelsbilanzen*. A newspaper intervention on Austria’s trade balance, subsequently collected under a slightly varying singular or plural title. Its serial newspaper manifestation makes it resist the idea of a single, fixed-title book. [Mises.at chronological catalogue](https://mises.at/en/denker/eugen-von-boehm-bawerk), [Google Books record for the collected writings](https://books.google.com/books/about/Gesammelte_schriften_von_Eugen_von_B%C3%B6hm.html?id=JyNBAAAAIAAJ)

### Editorial manifestations that are not new works by Böhm-Bawerk

- **1921:** *Kapital und Kapitalzins*, fourth edition. Friedrich von Wieser reorganized Böhm-Bawerk’s existing, separately revised material into three volumes after the author’s death; this is an important manifestation of the project but not a newly made Böhm-Bawerk text. [The History of Economic Thought Website](https://www.hetwebsite.net/het/profiles/bawerk.htm)
- **1924–1926:** *Gesammelte Schriften von Eugen von Böhm-Bawerk*. Franz Xaver Weiss’s posthumous collected-works edition is the principal scholarly container used here; its arrangement and editorial apparatus are Weiss’s, while the constituent writings are Böhm-Bawerk’s. [Google Books bibliographic record](https://books.google.com/books/about/Gesammelte_schriften_von_Eugen_von_B%C3%B6hm.html?id=JyNBAAAAIAAJ)
- **1926:** *Eugen von Böhm-Bawerks kleinere Abhandlungen über Kapital und Zins*. The second collected volume, edited by Weiss, gathers previously published shorter writings; it is not evidence that Böhm-Bawerk made a new book in 1926. [Google Books bibliographic record](https://books.google.com/books/about/Gesammelte_schriften_von_Eugen_von_B%C3%B6hm.html?id=JyNBAAAAIAAJ)
