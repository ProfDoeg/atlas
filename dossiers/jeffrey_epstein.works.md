## Works by Jeffrey Edward Epstein

### Financial and corporate instruments

- **1982 or 1988–2001:** *J. Epstein & Company, Inc.* A private investment-advisory firm and Epstein’s earliest documented independent business obra. Corporate extracts conflict over whether it was founded in 1982 or 1988, so the date cannot be resolved securely from the surviving record ([Dun & Bradstreet record](https://www.justice.gov/epstein/files/DataSet%2010/EFTA01405372.pdf); [corporate-record comparison](https://epstein-data.com/wiki/entities/j-epstein-and-co)).

- **1996–2012:** *Financial Trust Company, Inc.* A Virgin Islands financial company controlled by Epstein and granted substantial territorial tax benefits; it was a corporate instrument rather than a publicly documented financial product or body of writing. Later litigation treated it as part of the network of entities supporting the “Epstein Enterprise” ([Government of the U.S. Virgin Islands v. JPMorgan Chase](https://www.justice.gov/multimedia/Court%20Records/Government%20of%20the%20United%20States%20Virgin%20Islands%20v.%20JPMorgan%20Chase%20Bank%2C%20N.A.%2C%20No.%20122-cv-10904%20%28S.D.N.Y.%202022%29/070-01.pdf)).

- **2012–2019:** *Southern Trust Company, Inc.* A Virgin Islands company presented to territorial authorities as a data-analysis or “informatics” business. The Virgin Islands government alleged that it performed no such services and instead functioned as a conduit for Epstein’s money and enterprise, making its advertised purpose disputed rather than an accomplished technical work ([Government of the U.S. Virgin Islands v. JPMorgan Chase](https://www.justice.gov/multimedia/Court%20Records/Government%20of%20the%20United%20States%20Virgin%20Islands%20v.%20JPMorgan%20Chase%20Bank%2C%20N.A.%2C%20No.%20122-cv-10904%20%28S.D.N.Y.%202022%29/070-01.pdf)).

- **2013–2019:** *Southern Financial, LLC.* A financial shell managed by Epstein and owned by Southern Trust Company; its separateness as an obra is principally juridical, since the surviving evidence identifies an entity and accounts rather than an independent activity or product ([2015 certificate of incumbency](https://epstein-data.com/EFTA01282432?nav=search); [Government of the U.S. Virgin Islands v. JPMorgan Chase](https://www.justice.gov/multimedia/Court%20Records/Government%20of%20the%20United%20States%20Virgin%20Islands%20v.%20JPMorgan%20Chase%20Bank%2C%20N.A.%2C%20No.%20122-cv-10904%20%28S.D.N.Y.%202022%29/070-01.pdf)).

- **By 2019:** *The 1953 Trust*. A revocable trust created through Epstein’s will two days before his death to receive his property. It is a legal protocol made by Epstein with professional assistance, not a literary “last work”; commentators disputed whether its structure would impede victims’ recovery against the estate ([estate inventory and will](https://archive.shadowwarfare.info/Jeffery-Epstein-s-last-will-and-testament.pdf); [Time](https://time.com/5656776/jeffrey-epstein-will-estate/)).

### Foundations and patronage projects

- **By 1998:** *C.O.U.Q. Foundation, Inc.* A private charitable foundation controlled by Epstein and used to make grants. It resists treatment as a personal creative work: the entity was his instrument, but its funded research and recipients’ creations were not thereby authored by him ([Government of the U.S. Virgin Islands v. JPMorgan Chase](https://www.justice.gov/multimedia/Court%20Records/Government%20of%20the%20United%20States%20Virgin%20Islands%20v.%20JPMorgan%20Chase%20Bank%2C%20N.A.%2C%20No.%20122-cv-10904%20%28S.D.N.Y.%202022%29/070-01.pdf)).

- **2000–2019:** *J. Epstein Virgin Islands Foundation, Inc.*, later trading as *Enhanced Education*. A private-foundation apparatus established and controlled by Epstein for grants and payments relating chiefly to science and education. Records preserve overlapping names, so these should not be inflated into two securely distinct creations ([bank and corporate records](https://epstein-data.com/wiki/entities/enhanced-education); [Government of the U.S. Virgin Islands v. JPMorgan Chase](https://www.justice.gov/multimedia/Court%20Records/Government%20of%20the%20United%20States%20Virgin%20Islands%20v.%20JPMorgan%20Chase%20Bank%2C%20N.A.%2C%20No.%20122-cv-10904%20%28S.D.N.Y.%202022%29/070-01.pdf)).

- **2003:** *Program for Evolutionary Dynamics*. A Harvard research-center project proposed by Epstein and mathematician Benedict Gross and established by Harvard president Lawrence Summers after Epstein supplied $6.5 million. Epstein’s websites falsely claimed that his foundation established it through a $35 million gift; authorship therefore belongs to several institutional actors, not to Epstein alone ([Harvard’s investigative report](https://ogc.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum12481/files/ogc/files/report_concerning_jeffrey_e._epsteins_connections_to_harvard_university.pdf)).

- **2008–2009:** *Florida Science Foundation*. An entity represented as a science foundation but also used as Epstein’s nominal workplace during jail work release. No independently documented scholarly output makes it a body of work; its practical status was partly administrative and reputational ([Harvard’s investigative report](https://ogc.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum12481/files/ogc/files/report_concerning_jeffrey_e._epsteins_connections_to_harvard_university.pdf)).

- **2012–2019:** *Gratitude America, Ltd.* A private charitable foundation created after Epstein’s conviction and used to distribute grants. It is best classified as a philanthropic and financial instrument, while the works made by its grantees remain those grantees’ works ([Government of the U.S. Virgin Islands v. JPMorgan Chase, entity schedule](https://www.justice.gov/multimedia/Court%20Records/Government%20of%20the%20United%20States%20Virgin%20Islands%20v.%20JPMorgan%20Chase%20Bank%2C%20N.A.%2C%20No.%20122-cv-10904%20%28S.D.N.Y.%202022%29/145-01.pdf)).

### Architectural commissions and environments

- **1993–2019:** *Zorro Ranch compound*. A New Mexico estate expanded under Epstein with a hilltop mansion, private runway, and ancillary structures. This is a commissioned environment rather than architecture demonstrably designed by Epstein himself, and it also resists neutral treatment as a “work” because accusers identified it as a site of sexual abuse ([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/98ee07a273865c1cfc2b034ef182171a); [estate inventory](https://archive.shadowwarfare.info/Jeffery-Epstein-s-last-will-and-testament.pdf)).

- **1998–2019:** *Little Saint James compound*. A continually altered island environment comprising residences, cabanas, service buildings, landscaping, docks, and recreational infrastructure. Epstein commissioned and controlled the project, but resort architect Edward Tuttle and other designers—not Epstein—supplied architectural authorship; the compound also functioned as infrastructure for conduct alleged in civil and criminal proceedings ([Architect’s Newspaper](https://www.archpaper.com/2019/08/eavesdrop-architects-designers-jeffrey-epstein-black-book/); [estate inventory](https://archive.shadowwarfare.info/Jeffery-Epstein-s-last-will-and-testament.pdf)).

- **2003:** *Main house renovation, Little Saint James*. A tropical-resort architectural commission designed by Edward Tuttle’s practice for Epstein. It belongs in Epstein’s commissioned obra but cannot responsibly be attributed to him as designer ([Architect’s Newspaper](https://www.archpaper.com/2019/08/eavesdrop-architects-designers-jeffrey-epstein-black-book/)).

- **Date uncertain, before 2019:** *Blue-striped pavilion on Little Saint James*. A domed, blue-and-white building commonly—but without firm evidence—called a “temple.” Its intended function and designer remain disputed, so the descriptive title is preferable to treating the popular nickname as an established title.

### Media and self-fashioning

- **Circa 2010–2014:** *Jeffrey Epstein’s Profiles in Science*. A website and promotional assemblage presenting Epstein as a scientific patron through biographies, science summaries, and publicity material. Authorship of its individual texts is undocumented, some passages were reportedly copied from elsewhere, and it is therefore better understood as a commissioned reputational Gesamtkunstwerk than as Epstein’s personal writing ([Chronicle of Higher Education](https://www.chronicle.com/article/we-dug-up-jeffrey-epsteins-old-science-blog-its-as-weird-as-you-think/); [Harvard’s investigative report](https://ogc.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum12481/files/ogc/files/report_concerning_jeffrey_e._epsteins_connections_to_harvard_university.pdf)).

- **Circa 2010–2019:** *Jeffrey Epstein Foundation websites, blogs, press releases, and placed profiles*. A distributed publicity project designed to associate Epstein’s name with science and philanthropy and suppress the visibility of his conviction. The constituent texts were produced by publicists and outside writers, often without transparent attribution, so they should not be catalogued as his authored essays even where they spoke in his institutional voice ([PR Newswire archive](https://www.prnewswire.com/news/www.jeffreyepsteinfoundation.com/); [Chronicle of Higher Education](https://www.chronicle.com/article/we-dug-up-jeffrey-epsteins-old-science-blog-its-as-weird-as-you-think/)).

### Criminal system

- **At least 2001–2019:** *The Epstein Enterprise*. The name used by the Government of the U.S. Virgin Islands for the interconnected system of companies, trusts, properties, aircraft, employees, payments, and recruitment through which it alleged Epstein trafficked and sexually abused women and girls. Calling this a “work” risks aestheticizing harm: it is included only because it was a deliberately constructed operational system, while its scope, participants, and legal characterization remain matters of multiple proceedings ([Government of the U.S. Virgin Islands v. JPMorgan Chase](https://www.justice.gov/multimedia/Court%20Records/Government%20of%20the%20United%20States%20Virgin%20Islands%20v.%20JPMorgan%20Chase%20Bank%2C%20N.A.%2C%20No.%20122-cv-10904%20%28S.D.N.Y.%202022%29/070-01.pdf)).

### No established authored corpus

- *No verified bibliography of authored books, scholarly articles, musical works, or visual artworks.* No standard scholarly bibliography, catalogue raisonné, collected-works edition, or discography for Jeffrey Edward Epstein has been identified; similarly named scholars’ publications belong to other people. His surviving emails, address books, legal instruments, and institutional publicity are archival documents or collaborative productions, not evidence of a conventional authored oeuvre.
