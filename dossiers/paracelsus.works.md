## Works by Paracelsus

### Medical, pharmaceutical, and surgical obra

- **c. 1520:** *Eleven Treatises on Individual Diseases*. An early medical project on dropsy, consumption, jaundice, colic, stroke, deafness, worms, diarrhoea, gout, epilepsy, and intermittent fever; it survives as drafts, duplicate recensions, and fragments rather than a finished book. [Sudhoff I/1](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **c. 1520:** *Volumen medicinae Paramirum de medica industria*. A projected medicine of the “five entities”; only fragments of this early form survive. [Sudhoff I/1](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **c. 1520:** *Von der Gebärung der empfindlichen Dinge in der Vernunft*. An anthropological-medical treatise also transmitted under headings concerning human generation and human properties. [Sudhoff I/1](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **c. 1520:** *Philosophia de generatione hominis*. A short work on human generation. [Sudhoff I/1](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **c. 1520:** *Two Early Treatments of Podagra*. A pair of unfinished gout treatises with associated fragments, better understood as successive states of a project than as a single finished book. [Sudhoff I/1](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1525–1526:** *Herbarius*. A materia-medica protocol on hellebore, persicaria, salt, blessed thistle, coral, and the magnet. [Sudhoff I/2](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **c. 1525:** *Von den natürlichen Dingen*. The first book of a pharmaceutical-natural project treating turpentine, hellebore, salt, St John’s wort, magnet, sulphur, vitriol, arsenic, and tartar; its date is approximate. [Sudhoff I/2](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **c. 1525:** *On Turpentine*. An alternative working-out of the medicinal subject. [Sudhoff I/2](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **c. 1525:** *Über den Honig*. An unfinished pharmaceutical treatise on honey. [Sudhoff I/2](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **c. 1525:** *Virtutes herbarum*. Fragmentary notes on the powers of herbs and other natural substances; this resists treatment as a completed work. [Sudhoff I/2](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **c. 1525:** *Von den natürlichen Bädern*. A therapeutic treatise on natural baths. [Sudhoff I/2](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- *Fragments on Thermal Waters*. Notes or abandoned drafts rather than a securely bounded work. [Sudhoff I/2](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1525–1526:** *Von den natürlichen Wassern*, books 3–5. A medical-natural history of waters surviving without the complete projected sequence. [Sudhoff I/2](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- *Fragment on Wine*. A small remnant whose original textual boundaries are uncertain. [Sudhoff I/2](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- *De lithiasi*. A fragmentary booklet and early formulation of Paracelsus’s tartar theory. [Sudhoff I/2](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **c. 1525:** *The Sixth, Seventh, and Ninth Books in Medicine*. Three surviving units on tartaric disease, psychic disease, contractures, and paralysis from a larger, incomplete scheme. [Sudhoff I/2](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1526–1527:** *De viribus membrorum / De spiritu vitae*. A six-book medical project on the powers of the organs or “spirit of life,” surviving incompletely. [Sudhoff I/3](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1525–1526:** *Nine Books Archidoxis*. A pharmaceutical-alchemical protocol organized around arcana and the preparation of remedies. [Sudhoff I/3](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1526:** *De renovatione et restauratione*. A medical-alchemical treatise on bodily renewal. [Sudhoff I/3](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1526:** *Vom langen Leben*. A treatise on longevity distinct from the five-book Latin *De vita longa*. [Sudhoff I/3](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1526–1527:** *De vita longa*, five books. A Latin longevity treatise preserved through a recension associated with Johannes Oporinus, alongside German original fragments. [Theatrum Paracelsicum](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works/De_vita_longa)
- **1526–1527:** *Liber praeparationum / De praeparationibus*. A two-book protocol for preparing medicines; it survives only fragmentarily. [Sudhoff I/3](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1526–1527:** *Proceß und Art des Spiritus vitrioli*. A brief operative recipe or protocol concerning spirit of vitriol. [Sudhoff I/3](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1526–1527:** *Libellus de xylohebeno*. Drafts for preparing and administering guaiac wood, with an Oporinus Latin translation. [Sudhoff I/3](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1526–1527:** *De ruptura compendium*. A brief medical tract on rupture or hernia. [Sudhoff I/3](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1526–1527:** *De mumia libellus*. A short pharmaceutical work on medicinal “mumia.” [Sudhoff I/3](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1527:** *Scholia et observationes in poemata Macri*. Medical-botanical annotations to the poem attributed to Macer Floridus. [Sudhoff I/3](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1527:** *Theoricae figurae universalium morborum*. Tabular designs for classifying general disease types, surviving with theoretical fragments rather than as a polished work. [Sudhoff I/3](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1527:** *De gradibus et compositionibus receptorum et naturalium*, seven books. A pharmaceutical lecture-text and protocol for grading and compounding remedies. [Sudhoff I/4](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **Summer 1527:** *Lectures on Apostemes, Ulcers, Open Sores, Sirones, and Other Growths*. A pedagogical performance preserved in two listeners’ transcripts; the surviving “text” was therefore made collaboratively through oral delivery and note-taking. [Sudhoff I/4](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1527:** *Von Aderlaß, Purgieren und Schröpfen*. A therapeutic protocol on bloodletting, purging, and cupping. [Sudhoff I/4](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1527:** *De modo pharmacandi*. A projected three-book guide to administering medicines; the first book and fragments of the others survive. [Sudhoff I/4](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1527:** *German Commentary on the Aphorisms of Hippocrates*. A pedagogical commentary, partly transmitted through Basel lecture materials. [Sudhoff I/4](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **17 August 1527 onward:** *De urinarum ac pulsuum judiciis libellus*. A lecture-derived diagnostic manual on urine and pulse. [Sudhoff I/4](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1527:** *De physiognomia, quantum medico opus est*. A lecture-derived account of the physiognomic knowledge required by a physician. [Sudhoff I/4](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **Winter 1527–1528:** *Lectures on Tartaric Diseases*. An oral course surviving in student transcripts and Paracelsus’s draft sheets; it resists identification with one authorially fixed text. [Sudhoff I/5](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **Winter 1527–1528:** *De icteritiis liber unus*. A lecture on jaundice preserved in listeners’ notes. [Sudhoff I/5](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **Winter 1527–1528:** *Der Paragraphen vierzehn Bücher*. A fourteen-book course on special pathology and therapy, surviving through lecture notes and authorial drafts. [Sudhoff I/5](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **Winter 1527–1528:** *Lectures on Wounds and Wound Healing*. An oral work preserved in two interdependent student transcripts. [Sudhoff I/5](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1527–1528:** *Antimedicus*. A polemical surgical project on wound topography, professional duty, and consequences of organ injury; only drafts and fragments survive. [Sudhoff I/5](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1527–1528:** *De ulceribus*. Two drafts on ulceration, leprosy, and related conditions. [Sudhoff I/5](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1528:** *Bertheonea*, three books. A substantial surgical work accompanied by numerous authorial drafts and working notes. [Sudhoff I/6](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **8 August 1528:** *Von allen offenen Schäden, so aus der Natur geboren werden*, seven books. A surgical treatise dedicated to Conrad Wickram, with preparatory drafts and fragments. [Sudhoff I/6](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **11 June 1528:** *Von Blattern, Lähmi, Beulen, Löchern und Zitrachten der Franzosen und ihresgleichen*, ten books. A chemical-therapeutic work on syphilis, dedicated to Hieronymus Boner. [Sudhoff I/6](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1529:** *Vom Holz Guajaco gründlicher Heilung*. A polemical medical protocol rejecting or revising guaiac treatment for syphilis. [Sudhoff I/7](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **23 November 1529:** *Von der französischen Krankheit drei Bücher Para*. A three-book syphilis treatise also known as *Von den Imposturen*. [Sudhoff I/7](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1529:** *Von Ursprung und Herkommen der Franzosen samt der Recepten Heilung*, eight books. A syphilis treatise combining causal account and therapeutic recipes. [Sudhoff I/7](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1529:** *Spital-Buch*, part one. A medical handbook explicitly presented as the first part of a larger project. [Sudhoff I/7](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1528–1529:** *Drafts toward the Syphilis Writings*. A large workshop of preliminary pieces; their resistance to being separate “works” is part of their status. [Sudhoff I/7](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1529–1530:** *Paragranum*, early recension. A projected foundation of medicine surviving through a preface, two books, and drafts. [Sudhoff I/8](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1530:** *Paragranum / Liber quatuor columnarum artis medicae*. A medical manifesto grounding practice in philosophy, astronomy, alchemy, and the physician’s virtue. [Theatrum Paracelsicum](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works/Paragranum)
- **July 1530:** *Büchlein vom Mercurio*. Two fragments of a projected booklet on mercury. [Sudhoff I/8](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **Easter 1530:** *Von den hinfallenden Siechtagen / De caducis liber I*. A medical work on falling sickness or epilepsy, with surviving preliminary concepts. [Sudhoff I/8](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1530:** *Von hinfallenden Siechtagen der Mutter / De caduco matricis*. The second *De caducis* book, addressing uterine “falling sickness.” [Sudhoff I/8](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1529–1530:** *Zwei Bücher von der Pestilenz und ihren Zufällen*. A two-book plague treatise associated with Nördlingen; its precise year is uncertain. [Sudhoff I/8](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1531:** *Opus Paramirum*. A major medical-philosophical obra in three or four books—the count varies with its textual organization—surrounded by drafts and preliminary treatments. [Sudhoff I/9](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1531–1532:** *Von den unsichtbaren Krankheiten*, five books. A medical-theological account of diseases whose causes are not bodily visible, surviving with preliminary drafts. [Theatrum Paracelsicum](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works/Von_den_unsichtbaren_Krankheiten_und_ihren_Ursachen)
- **1533–1534:** *Von der Bergsucht und anderen Bergkrankheiten*. An occupational-medicine work on miners’ diseases, with small associated fragments. [Theatrum Paracelsicum](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works/Von_der_Bergsucht)
- **1534:** *Von der Pest an die Stadt Sterzingen*. A plague advisory addressed to Sterzing, accompanied in the archive by further, mostly fragmentary plague drafts. [Sudhoff I/9](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **31 August 1535:** *Von des Bades Pfäfers Tugenden, Kräften und Wirkung, Ursprung und Herkommen, Regiment und Ordnung*. A description and therapeutic protocol for the Pfäfers spa. [Sudhoff I/9](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **Summer 1535:** *Consilium for Abbot Johann Jakob Russinger*. A patient-specific medical consultation, not a general book. [Sudhoff I/9](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1535:** *Medical Advice for Adam Reißner*. An individualized consilium for the Mindelheim town clerk. [Sudhoff I/10](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1536:** *Die große Wundarznei*. Paracelsus’s major printed surgical obra; the Ulm and Augsburg textual forms differ, and Sudhoff questioned the authenticity of part of its transmitted continuation. [Sudhoff I/10](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **24 March 1536:** *Three Consilia for the Zerotin Family*. Patient-specific medical opinions, only allegedly connected with the Augsburg Zerotin family. [Sudhoff I/10](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1537–1538:** *Das Buch von den tartarischen Krankheiten*. A mature treatise on tartaric diseases dedicated to Johann von Brant. [Sudhoff I/11](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1537–1538:** *Sieben Defensiones*. Seven polemical self-defences: a manifesto-like intervention into disputes about Paracelsus’s medicine and professional identity. [Sudhoff I/11](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1537–1538:** *Labyrinthus medicorum errantium / Vom Irrgang der Ärzte*. A polemical guide out of the “labyrinth” of erroneous medicine. [Sudhoff I/11](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1537–1541:** *Late Consilia and Medical Notes*. A cluster of patient-specific consultations, recipes, and fragments, including a letter to Hans Ungnad; it is archival residue rather than one authored book. [Sudhoff I/11](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)

### Natural philosophy, alchemy, and cosmology

- **1526:** *Von den ersten dreien Principiis oder Essentiis*. A short natural-philosophical account of the three principles or essences. [Sudhoff I/3](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1526:** *De mineralibus*. A natural-philosophical book on minerals. [Sudhoff I/3](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1526:** *De transmutationibus metallorum*. An incomplete alchemical project: only books 9 and 10, *De cementis* and *De gradationibus*, plus a stray cementation recipe, survive. [Sudhoff I/3](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1537–1538:** *Astronomia magna / Philosophia sagax*. A vast cosmological-anthropological Gesamtkunstwerk joining medicine, theology, divination, and philosophy of the macrocosm and microcosm; drafts and excerpts survive alongside the main text. [Sudhoff I/12](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- *Philosophia de generationibus et fructibus quatuor elementorum*. A natural philosophy of the generation and products of the four elements; its date is not securely fixed. [Sudhoff I/13](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- *Liber meteororum / De meteoris*. A ten-chapter work on meteorological phenomena, accompanied by numerous drafts and alternative treatments. [Sudhoff I/13](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- *De fundamento scientiarum sapientiaeque*. Three epistemological-philosophical treatises, preserved with smaller fragments. [Sudhoff I/13](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- *Philosophiae tractatus quinque*. Five short philosophical treatises on time, elemental bodies, flesh and mumia, bodies and spirits, and sleeping and waking. [Sudhoff I/13](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- *Liber de imaginibus*. A work on operative images; its date remains unfixed. [Sudhoff I/13](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)

### Prognostications, celestial interpretations, and historical writing

- **1529–1534:** *Practica gemacht auf Europen*. A political-astrological prognostication extending from 1530 to 1534. [Sudhoff I/7–8](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **Early 1530:** *Pronosticatio … betreffend ein Confederation*. A topical political prognostication claiming to supply what other astrologers had overlooked. [Sudhoff I/7](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1529–1530:** *Auslegung über etliche Figuren Johann Liechtenbergers*. An interpretive work on selected prophetic images by Johannes Lichtenberger. [Sudhoff I/7](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **29 March 1530:** *De eclipsi solis*. An astrological interpretation of a solar eclipse, connected with further revisions of the European *Practica*. [Sudhoff I/8](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1531–1534:** *Writings on Comets, Earthquakes, Rainbows, and Heavenly Signs*. A family of separate topical interpretations—including *Auslegung der Erdbeben*, *Auslegung des Friedbogens*, and *Von den wunderbarlichen, übernatürlichen Zeichen*—whose transmission does not always preserve firm boundaries. [Sudhoff I/9](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1535:** *Practica teutsch aufs 1535. Jahr*. A German annual prognostication. [Sudhoff I/9](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **23/26 August 1536:** *Prognostikation auf vierundzwanzig zukünftige Jahre, 1537–1560*. A long-range political and astrological forecast; the Latin translation is by Marcus Tatius, not Paracelsus. [Sudhoff I/10](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1536?:** *Mantischer Entwurf, “Geben zu München”*. An unfinished divinatory draft; both its title and exact date remain unstable. [Sudhoff I/10](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1538:** *Chronik des Landes Kärnten*. A short regional chronicle transmitted with a dedication of three books to the Carinthian Estates. [Sudhoff I/11](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **1537–1541:** *Late Practica and Mantic Writings*. A cluster of annual prognostications, including the German *Practica* for 1538, and brief divinatory fragments; it resists reconstruction as one coherent work. [Sudhoff I/11](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- *Auslegung der Papstbilder*. An interpretation of the “Nuremberg figures” attributed to Joachim of Fiore; the work interprets an existing image-cycle rather than creating those images. [Sudhoff I/12](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)

### Manifestos, petitions, letters, and situated acts

- **Late 1526:** *Letter to Erasmus of Rotterdam*. A medical-consultative and self-presentational letter, not a treatise. [Sudhoff I/3](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **5 June 1527:** *Intimatio Theophrasti medicae artis studiosis*. The Basel lecture programme: a public manifesto that announces and performs a reform of medical teaching. [Sudhoff I/4](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **Summer 1527:** *Two Petitions to the Basel City Council*. Administrative interventions made to secure or defend Paracelsus’s teaching position, not literary “works” in the usual sense. [Sudhoff I/4](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **10 November 1527:** *Letter to Nikolaus Clauser*. A covering letter sent with the manuscript of *De gradibus*. [Sudhoff I/4](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- **11 November 1527:** *Letter to the Zurich Students on Froben’s Death*. A circumstantial letter occasioned by the death of Johann Froben. [Sudhoff I/4](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)

- *Es wird gefragt, ob auch Maria sei in Sünden empfangen und geboren*. A quaestio-like tract on Mary’s conception and sin. [Goldammer census](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Theological_Works_of_Paracelsus,_Goldammer%27s_Census)
- *Declaratio “Spiritus sanctus superveniet in te…”*. A Marian declaration preserved in several manuscripts, one only fragmentary. [Theatrum Paracelsicum](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works/Declaratio_spiritus_sanctus_superveniet_in_te_et_virtus_altissimi_obumbrabit_tibi)
- *De dignitate Mariae*. A Latin Marian work whose authenticity Goldammer marked as still requiring closer examination. [Goldammer census](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Theological_Works_of_Paracelsus,_Goldammer%27s_Census)
- *Super “Salve regina” explicatio*. A Latin exposition whose authenticity likewise remains unresolved. [Goldammer census](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Theological_Works_of_Paracelsus,_Goldammer%27s_Census)

### Works transmitted under his name but rejected or disputed

- **Allegedly 1537:** *De natura rerum*, nine books. Sudhoff printed it as spurious; its asserted Villach date is part of the doubtful transmission, not secure authorship evidence. [Sudhoff I/11](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- *Philosophia ad Athenienses*. A philosophical work long circulated as Paracelsus’s but classified as spurious by Sudhoff. [Sudhoff I/13](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- *Wanderbücher and Travel Records*. Supposed travel writings published in 1582 as if from an autograph; Sudhoff rejected them as spurious, so they are manifestations of Paracelsian authorship rather than things securely made by Paracelsus. [Sudhoff I/13](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- *Philosophia de divinis operibus et secretis naturae, volumen primum*. A collection of twenty-three writings accepted by Huser but placed among the spuria by Sudhoff; it must not silently be counted as an authentic Paracelsian obra. [Sudhoff I/14](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- *Liber de animalibus ex Sodomia natis*. A work transmitted under Paracelsus’s name and placed in Sudhoff’s volume of doubtful and spurious writings. [Sudhoff I/14](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- *Von der Scotomia*. A very short text absent from Huser and printed by Sudhoff beside the doubtful *De animalibus* material; its authorship is not secure. [Sudhoff I/14](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- *Archidoxis magica*. A magical-amuletic text frequently joined to the authentic *Archidoxis* but rejected in Sudhoff’s critical arrangement; it is a pseudo-Paracelsian manifestation, not securely Paracelsus’s protocol. [Sudhoff I/14](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- *De tinctura physicorum*. An alchemical text transmitted under Paracelsus’s name but included among Sudhoff’s spuria. [Sudhoff I/14](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- *Thesaurus thesaurorum alchimistarum*. A pseudo-Paracelsian alchemical tract, not securely an obra by Paracelsus. [Sudhoff I/14](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- *De tribus thesauris et leone septentrionali*. A brief prophetic-alchemical text explicitly classed as spurious by Sudhoff. [Sudhoff I/14](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
- *Manualia and Other Pseudo-Paracelsian Tracts*. A residual group of alchemical, magical, and medical writings formerly assigned to Paracelsus but segregated as spurious in Sudhoff’s critical edition; individual titles and boundaries vary across manuscripts and early prints. [Sudhoff I/14](https://www.theatrum-paracelsicum.com/Works_of_Paracelsus_-_Sudhoff%27s_Edition_by_Volumes)
