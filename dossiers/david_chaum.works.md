## Works by David Lee Chaum

### Theses, memoranda, and foundational systems

- **1978:** *Implementing Capability-Based Protection Using Encryption*. A coauthored Berkeley memorandum describing an encrypted capability system; it is both a technical text and the specification of a protection mechanism. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1979:** *Computer Systems Established, Maintained, and Trusted by Mutually Suspicious Groups*. A Berkeley memorandum proposing distributed trust, vaults, and linked records; it is an early system design rather than merely an essay. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1982:** *Computer Systems Established, Maintained and Trusted by Mutually Suspicious Groups*. Chaum’s doctoral dissertation, substantially developing the 1979 memorandum’s system; the two are related manifestations, not safely reducible to one bibliographic object. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1981:** *Untraceable Electronic Mail, Return Addresses, and Digital Pseudonyms*. A protocol paper creating the mix-network method for anonymous communication and pseudonymous replies. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/), [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/rec/journals/cacm/Chaum81)
- **1982:** *Blind Signatures for Untraceable Payments*. A protocol paper introducing blind signatures as machinery for privacy-preserving payments. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1982:** *Verification by Anonymous Monitors (“Silo Watching”)*. A short protocol contribution in the CRYPTO ’81 report, concerned with anonymous monitoring. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)

### Cryptographic protocols, constructions, and analyses

- **1983:** *Design Concepts for Tamper-Responding Systems*. A technical design paper about systems that respond to attempted physical compromise. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1984:** *How to Keep a Secret Alive: Extensible Partial Key, Key Safeguarding, and Threshold Systems*. A protocol paper specifying methods for distributing and preserving cryptographic secrets. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1985:** *Attacks on Some RSA Signatures*. A cryptanalytic paper identifying attacks on RSA signature constructions. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1985:** *Cryptanalysis of DES with a Reduced Number of Rounds: Sequences of Linear Factors in Block Ciphers*. A coauthored cryptanalytic study of reduced-round DES. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1985:** *Showing Credentials Without Identification: Signatures Transferred Between Unconditionally Unlinkable Pseudonyms*. A credential protocol whose operative object is an unlinkable transfer of attestations between pseudonyms. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1986:** *A Secure and Privacy-Protecting Protocol for Transmitting Personal Information Between Organizations*. A coauthored protocol for controlled disclosure of personal data. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1986:** *Demonstrating Possession of a Discrete Logarithm Without Revealing It*. A coauthored zero-knowledge identification protocol. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1986:** *Demonstrating That a Public Predicate Can Be Satisfied Without Revealing Any Information About How*. A protocol paper constructing a zero-knowledge proof of satisfaction. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1986:** *Some Variations on RSA Signatures and Their Security*. A technical analysis proposing and evaluating RSA-signature variants. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1987:** *An Improved Protocol for Demonstrating Possession of Discrete Logarithms and Some Generalizations*. A coauthored refinement and generalization of a zero-knowledge proof protocol. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1987:** *Blinding for Unanticipated Signatures*. A cryptographic construction permitting a message to be blinded before the eventual signer is known. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1987:** *Gradual and Verifiable Release of a Secret*. A coauthored protocol for releasing secret information incrementally and verifiably. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1987:** *Minimum Disclosure Proofs of Knowledge*. A coauthored protocol paper on proving knowledge while disclosing only a prescribed minimum. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1987:** *Multiparty Computations Ensuring Privacy of Each Party’s Input and Correctness of the Result*. A coauthored secure-computation protocol; the official bibliography also associates “Minimum Disclosure Proofs of Knowledge” with overlapping proceedings metadata, so the bibliographic boundary between these records should not be silently normalized. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1988:** *Minimum Disclosure Proofs of Knowledge*. A coauthored journal article developing minimum-disclosure proofs; this is a later, fuller manifestation related to—not simply another edition of—the 1987 conference work. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/), [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/rec/journals/jcss/BrassardCC88)
- **1988:** *Multiparty Unconditionally Secure Protocols*. A coauthored protocol paper establishing unconditional security for multiparty computation under stated adversarial limits. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1988:** *The Dining Cryptographers Problem: Unconditional Sender and Recipient Untraceability*. A protocol paper introducing the dining-cryptographers construction for anonymous communication. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/), [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/rec/journals/joc/Chaum88)
- **1989:** *The Spymasters Double-Agent Problem: Multiparty Computations Secure Unconditionally from Minorities and Cryptographically from Majorities*. A multiparty-computation protocol combining two distinct security guarantees. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1989:** *Undeniable Signatures*. A coauthored protocol introducing signatures whose verification requires the signer’s participation. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1990:** *Convertible Undeniable Signatures*. A coauthored protocol allowing an undeniable signature to be converted into a generally verifiable one. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1990:** *Unconditionally-Secure Digital Signatures*. A coauthored signature construction whose security claim is information-theoretic rather than computational. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1990:** *Zero-Knowledge Undeniable Signatures*. A protocol combining undeniable signatures with zero-knowledge verification. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1991:** *Cryptographically Strong Undeniable Signatures, Unconditionally Secure for the Signer*. A coauthored signature protocol distinguishing cryptographic security from unconditional protection of the signer. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1991:** *Direct Zero Knowledge Proofs of Computational Power in Five Rounds*. A coauthored five-round zero-knowledge proof construction. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1991:** *Group Signatures*. A coauthored protocol introducing signatures attributable to a group while concealing the individual signer. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1991:** *Some Weaknesses of “Weaknesses of Undeniable Signatures”*. A cryptographic rejoinder and analysis; it is an argumentative intervention within a technical dispute, not a new protocol alone. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **July 1991:** *Undeniable Signatures: Applications and Theory*. A coauthored technical report; the official bibliography supplies no publication venue. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1992:** *Provably Unforgeable Signatures*. A coauthored protocol paper presenting a signature construction with a proof of unforgeability. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **2021:** *W-OTS+ Up My Sleeve! A Hidden Secure Fallback for Cryptocurrency Wallets*. A coauthored wallet-security protocol, appearing both as IACR ePrint 2021/872 and as an ACNS paper; those are manifestations of one research work. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/), [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/acns/ChaumLYC21)
- **2022:** *Tweakable Sleeve: A Novel Sleeve Construction Based on Tweakable Hash Functions*. A coauthored cryptographic construction circulated as IACR ePrint 2022/888 and published through MARBLE 2022. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/), [DBLP bibliography](https://dblp.org/pid/c/DavidChaum.html)
- **2022:** *WOTSwana: A Generalized Sleeve Construction for Multiple Proofs of Ownership*. A coauthored proof-of-ownership protocol, manifested as IACR ePrint 2022/1623 and an ICISC paper. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/), [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/icisc/ChaumLY22)
- **2023:** *Phoenixx: Linear Consensus with Random Sampling*. A coauthored IACR ePrint specifying a distributed-consensus protocol. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/), [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/rec/journals/iacr/ChaumCCYA23)

### Electronic money and payment systems

- **1986:** *Highly Secure But Untraceable Transactions*. A conference paper describing privacy-preserving transaction machinery. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1988:** *Untraceable Electronic Cash*. A coauthored protocol for anonymous electronic money with mechanisms addressing double spending. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1989:** *Efficient Offline Electronic Checks*. A coauthored payment protocol intended to work without immediate online verification. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1989:** *Online Cash Checks*. A protocol for online electronic-cash validation. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1989:** *Privacy Protected Payments: Unconditional Payer and/or Payee Untraceability*. A payment-system design offering different combinations of payer and payee anonymity. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1990:** *SmartCash: A Practical Electronic Payment System*. A CWI technical report specifying an implementable electronic-payment system. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1991:** *Numbers Can Be a Better Form of Cash than Paper*. An argument and design account for digital cash, first listed as a contribution to *Smart Card 2000*. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1992:** *Transferred Cash Grows in Size*. A coauthored analysis of a limitation in transferable electronic-cash systems. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1992:** *Wallet Databases with Observers*. A coauthored protocol for electronic wallets incorporating observer hardware or processes. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1993:** *Numbers Can Be a Better Form of Cash than Paper*. A later conference-paper manifestation of the argument published in the ESAT ’91 proceedings; it should not be mistaken for a wholly separate project solely because its publication date differs. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1993:** *Prepaid Smart Card Techniques: A Brief Introduction and Comparison*. A DigiCash technical note comparing prepaid-card mechanisms. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **2021:** *How to Issue a Central Bank Digital Currency*. A coauthored Swiss National Bank working paper and protocol proposal, also circulated as arXiv:2103.00254. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/), [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.00254)
- **2022:** *eCash 2.0: Inalienably Private and Quantum-Resistant to Counterfeiting*. A coauthored, unpublished white paper proposing a quantum-resistant electronic-cash system; its status is a public project specification rather than a refereed publication. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **2022:** *Offline eCash 2.0: Robust In-Person Payments, Later Onlineable*. An unpublished white paper specifying offline operation for the eCash 2.0 project. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)

### Privacy, identity, communication, and social systems

- **1981:** *Trust Relationships and Information Security*. An invited conference paper about the organization of trust in information systems. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1984:** *A New Paradigm for Individuals in the Information Age*. A programmatic privacy paper; because it advocates a reordering of relations between persons and information systems, it approaches a manifesto as well as a technical essay. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/), [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/sp/Chaum84)
- **1985:** *Security Without Identification: Transaction Systems to Make Big Brother Obsolete*. A system-design essay proposing anonymous credentials and transactions; its declared social intervention gives it a manifesto-like force. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1992:** *Achieving Electronic Privacy*. An invited *Scientific American* essay explaining a program of privacy-preserving digital transactions to a general readership. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **2016:** *cMix: Anonymization by High-Performance Scalable Mixing*. A coauthored IACR ePrint specifying the cMix anonymous-communication system. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/), [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/rec/journals/iacr/DavidChaumJKKRS16)
- **2017:** *cMix: Mixing with Minimal Real-Time Asymmetric Cryptographic Operations*. A later, revised conference manifestation of the cMix protocol, with an expanded author list; it is not an unrelated second system. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/), [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/acns/ChaumDJKKRS17)
- **2022:** *UDM: Private User Discovery with Minimal Information Disclosure*. A coauthored protocol for discovering users while limiting disclosure of identifying information. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/), [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/rec/journals/cryptologia/ChaumYSR22)
- *7th Estate: Grassroots Democracy*. An unpublished civic-technology project or manuscript. The official bibliography supplies no date, venue, or stable publication status, so it resists classification as a completed work. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- *Own Your AI Before It Owns You: A Graph Theory of Civilization, and Its Only Two Futures*. A programmatic text about AI and social organization; the official bibliography gives neither a date nor a conventional publication description. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)

### Election protocols, systems, and field trials

- **1988:** *Elections with Unconditionally-Secret Ballots and Disruption Equivalent to Breaking RSA*. A cryptographic election protocol designed to preserve ballot secrecy while making disruption computationally difficult. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **2004:** *Secret-Ballot Receipts: True Voter-Verifiable Elections*. A voting-protocol paper introducing receipts that support verification without revealing vote choices. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/), [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/rec/journals/ieeesp/Chaum04)
- **2005:** *A Practical Voter-Verifiable Election Scheme*. A coauthored election protocol emphasizing practical voter verification. [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/esorics/ChaumRS05)
- **2007:** *Secret Ballot Elections with Unconditional Integrity*. A coauthored IACR ePrint proposing an election protocol with unconditional integrity properties. [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/rec/journals/iacr/ChaumGRV07)
- **2008:** *Scantegrity: End-to-End Voter-Verifiable Optical-Scan Voting*. A coauthored account of Scantegrity, which is both a protocol and a materially implemented voting system. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/), [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/rec/journals/ieeesp/ChaumECCPSV08)
- **2008:** *Scantegrity II: End-to-End Verifiability for Optical Scan Election Systems Using Invisible Ink Confirmation Codes*. A coauthored protocol paper describing the invisible-ink form of the Scantegrity II system. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **2009:** *Accessible Voter-Verifiability*. A coauthored study and design proposal extending verifiable voting to voters with accessibility requirements. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/), [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/rec/journals/cryptologia/ChaumHPV09)
- **2009:** *Scantegrity II: End-to-End Verifiability by Voters of Optical Scan Elections Through Confirmation Codes*. A journal manifestation and fuller specification of Scantegrity II. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **2010:** *Scantegrity Mock Election at Takoma Park*. A coauthored field report on a public mock election; the made thing under study is an event and deployed system as well as a text. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **2010:** *Scantegrity II Municipal Election at Takoma Park: The First E2E Binding Governmental Election with Ballot Privacy*. A coauthored field report documenting a binding governmental deployment; it records a collective civic creation that cannot be reduced to Chaum’s authorship alone. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **2010:** *Securing Optical-Scan Voting*. A technical chapter describing security measures for optical-scan elections. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **2011:** *Scantegrity III: Automatic Trustworthy Receipts, Highlighting Over/Under Votes, and Full Voter Verifiability*. A coauthored protocol and proposed third-stage Scantegrity system. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/), [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/uss/ShermanFCC11)
- **2013:** *Remotegrity: Design and Use of an End-to-End Verifiable Remote Voting System*. A coauthored paper and implemented remote-voting system; an IACR ePrint and the ACNS publication are manifestations of the same work. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/), [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/acns/ZagorskiCCCEV13)
- **2022:** *VoteXX: A Solution to Improper Influence in Voter-Verifiable Elections*. A many-authored IACR ePrint specifying the VoteXX anti-coercion election protocol. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/), [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/rec/journals/iacr/ChaumCCLNPSYYZZ22)
- **2024:** *VoteXX: Extreme Coercion Resistance*. A substantially revised many-authored VoteXX protocol paper; capitalization varies between “VoteXX” and “Votexx” in bibliographic sources. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/), [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/rec/journals/iacr/ChaumCYCNPSZZY24)
- **2025:** *Revisiting Silent Coercion*. A many-authored conference paper extending the VoteXX research program’s treatment of coercion. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- *Random-Sample Voting: More Democratic, Better Quality, and Far Lower Cost*. An unpublished white paper proposing random-sample governance. With no date supplied by the bibliography, it is best treated as an undated political-system project rather than assigned an invented year. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)

### Unpublished or incompletely manifested projects

- **1986:** *Pay-as-you-go Poker & Showing Satisfiability of an Arbitrary Predicate Without Revealing How, Both as Secure as Factoring*. An unpublished workshop record joining a cryptographic game protocol to a zero-knowledge construction; the conjunction in its title makes uncertain whether it is one work or two linked demonstrations. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- *An Efficient Credential Mechanism Allowing a Limited Number of Credential Types*. An undated, coauthored unpublished credential protocol; the bibliography records the project but not a public manifestation. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- *An Interactive Signature Scheme*. An undated, coauthored unpublished signature protocol. Its existence is bibliographically asserted, but no publication history is supplied. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- *A Provably Secure and Fast Message Authentication Scheme*. An undated, coauthored unpublished authentication protocol. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- *Combining Secrets in Most Concealing Ways: Dynamic Graphs for Restricted Choices in Hidden Key Transformations*. An undated unpublished research work; authorship is coauthored, while the title and author order vary within Chaum’s own duplicated webpage listing. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- *Provers Can Limit the Number of Verifiers*. An undated unpublished cryptographic project. The bibliography supplies no venue and, in one version of the listing, no explicit publication status beyond its placement among unpublished items. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- *Zero Information Circuits with DES*. An undated, coauthored unpublished cryptographic project involving DES-based zero-information circuitry. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)

### Edited collective works

- **1983:** *Advances in Cryptology: Proceedings of CRYPTO ’82*. A collectively produced conference proceedings volume edited by Chaum with Ronald L. Rivest and Alan T. Sherman; editorship makes it a shaped collection, not a sole-authored book. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/), [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/crypto/1982)
- **1984:** *Advances in Cryptology: Proceedings of CRYPTO ’83*. A conference proceedings volume edited by Chaum. Later Springer records dated 2013 are reissues, not new works. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/), [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/crypto/1983)
- **1985:** *Advances in Cryptology: Proceedings of CRYPTO ’84*. A conference proceedings volume coedited with G. R. Blakley; it appears in DBLP’s curated editorship bibliography but is omitted from the current “Books” block on Chaum’s site. [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/crypto/1984)
- **1988:** *Smart Card 2000: The Future of IC Cards*. A coedited collection emerging from the Smart Card 2000 program; it is a collective technical forecast rather than Chaum’s sole-authored treatise. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **1988:** *Advances in Cryptology – EUROCRYPT ’87*. A conference proceedings volume coedited with Wyn L. Price. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/), [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/eurocrypt/87)
- **1991:** *Smart Card 2000: Second International Smart Card 2000 Conference, Amsterdam 1989*. An edited proceedings volume whose event occurred in 1989 and whose book manifestation appeared in 1991. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **2010:** *Towards Trustworthy Elections: New Directions in Electronic Voting*. A seven-editor research collection; Chaum’s role is editorial and collaborative rather than sole authorship of the constituent works. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/), [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/wote/2010lncs)

### Bibliographic exclusions and contested boundaries

- **2017:** *Chaum’s Protocol for Detecting Man-in-the-Middle: Explanation, Demonstration, and Timing Studies for a Text-Messaging Scenario*. This paper studies and implements a protocol associated with Chaum, but Chaum’s own bibliography explicitly says he is not listed as an author; it therefore resists inclusion as something he made and is recorded here only to prevent false attribution. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/)
- **2017:** *Thwarting Vote Buying Through Decoy Ballots*. Chaum’s webpage includes the title but supplies no Chaum authorship, while the curated bibliographic record does not establish him as an author; it is therefore not attributed to him here. [Chaum bibliography](https://chaum.com/publications/), [DBLP bibliography](https://dblp.org/pid/c/DavidChaum.html)
- **1997:** *On Electronic Commerce: How Much Do You Trust Big Brother?* This is catalogued as an interview with Chaum, not straightforwardly as a work authored by him; it is a mediated manifestation of his speech and therefore sits outside the authored oeuvre. [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/rec/journals/internet/Chaum97)
- **2004:** *Evaluation of Voting Systems*. Chaum is one of numerous named contributors to this very short collective publication; it is included by DBLP but absent from his selected bibliography, making the extent and character of his authorship unclear. [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/rec/journals/cacm/VoraABCDJJLRSY04)
- **2009:** *Guest Editorial: Special Issue on Electronic Voting*. A jointly signed editorial rather than a research protocol; it belongs to Chaum’s editorial activity, though not to his selected bibliography. [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/rec/journals/tifs/RivestCPRSV09)
