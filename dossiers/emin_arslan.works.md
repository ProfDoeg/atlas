## Works by Emir Emin Arslan

### Books, treatises, novels, and collected prose

- **1892:** *Historia de Napoleón I*. A history written in Arabic; its existence and date are reported in Arslan’s later bibliographies, but the scholarly account consulted found no surviving copy, so its material form and completeness remain unverified. [Gasquet, pp. 111–112](https://www.redalyc.org/pdf/1817/181725488008.pdf)

- **1897:** *Asrār al-quṣūr* (*Los secretos de los palacios*; also rendered in French as *Les secrets des sérails*). An Arabic political, historical, sentimental, and moral novel; unauthorized second and third editions reportedly followed, while Arslan issued a fourth edition in Buenos Aires in 1911. [Tornielli, pp. 165–166](https://dirasathispanicas.org/index.php/dirasathispanicas/article/download/22/22/34)

- **1900–1901:** *Ḥuqūq al-milal wa-muʿāhadāt al-duwal, al-qism al-rābiʿ: fī al-ḥarb* (*Derechos de las naciones y acuerdos de los Estados. Parte cuarta: Sobre la guerra*). A treatise on international law published in Arabic by al-Hilāl; sources give 1900 or 1901. It is also an unfinished project: Arslan deliberately issued the fourth part first during the Anglo-Boer War, but the projected first three parts never appeared. [Tornielli, p. 166](https://dirasathispanicas.org/index.php/dirasathispanicas/article/download/22/22/34); [Gasquet, pp. 111–112](https://www.redalyc.org/pdf/1817/181725488008.pdf)

- **1916:** *La verdad sobre el Harén*. An explanatory essay on the harem and Muslim social customs, published at Arslan’s expense rather than a work of fiction despite its exoticizing title. [Gasquet, pp. 110–112](https://www.redalyc.org/pdf/1817/181725488008.pdf)

- **1916; second edition 1918:** *Recuerdos de Oriente*. A collection of autobiographical, historical, and political narratives previously dispersed in periodical form; bibliographies sometimes cite only the securely documented 1918 second edition, while the UNLP bibliography records a 1916 edition. [Tornielli, bibliography, p. 180](https://dirasathispanicas.org/index.php/dirasathispanicas/article/download/22/22/34); [Delgado, *Revista La Nota: antología 1915–1917*](https://libros.fahce.unlp.edu.ar/index.php/libros/catalog/view/264/338/1755-1)

- **1917:** *Final de un idilio*. A political novel framed as a romance, set amid the German occupation of Belgium; its composition had begun by 1914, and the sources disagree over whether the 1917 Rodríguez Giles volume was the first or second edition. [Tornielli, pp. 175–176](https://dirasathispanicas.org/index.php/dirasathispanicas/article/download/22/22/34); [Gasquet, pp. 110–112](https://www.redalyc.org/pdf/1817/181725488008.pdf)

- **1926:** *La revolución siria contra el mandato francés*. A 159-page political history and anticolonial intervention concerning the Great Syrian Revolt; it does not merely describe the revolt but argues the case for Syrian independence. [Tornielli, p. 178](https://dirasathispanicas.org/index.php/dirasathispanicas/article/download/22/22/34)

- **1932:** *Misterios de Oriente* (also cited as *Misterios del Oriente*). A collection of eight historical and political essays first published in *La Nota*; the 1932 Tor volume is identified as a second edition, making the date of the first edition uncertain. [Gasquet, pp. 110–112](https://www.redalyc.org/pdf/1817/181725488008.pdf)

- **1934 or 1935:** *Muḏakkarāt bi-qalam al-Amīr Amīn Arslān* (*Memorias políticas*). Arabic political memoirs printed in Buenos Aires: Tornielli records a 1934 Rustūm Hermanos edition, whereas Gasquet reports Arslan’s own reference to a 1935 edition but states that he could not locate it. [Tornielli, bibliography, p. 180](https://dirasathispanicas.org/index.php/dirasathispanicas/article/download/22/22/34); [Gasquet, pp. 111–112](https://www.redalyc.org/pdf/1817/181725488008.pdf)

- **1935:** *La verdadera historia de “Las desencantadas”*. A corrective historical-literary account engaging with the celebrated “désenchantées” narrative; it is neither simply a novel nor merely literary criticism, but a counter-narrative claiming documentary truth. [Gasquet, pp. 110–112](https://www.redalyc.org/pdf/1817/181725488008.pdf)

- **1939:** *Las mentiras*. A fourteen-page political pamphlet issued by the newspaper *Al-Istiklal*; its brevity and polemical purpose make “pamphlet” more exact than “book.” [Gasquet, note 10, p. 112](https://www.redalyc.org/pdf/1817/181725488008.pdf)

- **1941:** *Los árabes: reseña histórico-literaria y leyendas* (some bibliographies mistakenly print “y reseñas”). A synthetic work of cultural history, literary exposition, and legend designed to counter hostile stereotypes about Arabs and Islam. [Tornielli, pp. 178–180](https://dirasathispanicas.org/index.php/dirasathispanicas/article/download/22/22/34); [Gasquet, pp. 110–112](https://www.redalyc.org/pdf/1817/181725488008.pdf)

### Periodical obras and editorial projects

- **August 1894–July 1895:** *Kašf an-Niqāb* (*El descorrimiento del velo*). An Arabic opposition newspaper founded jointly by Arslan and Salīm Sarkīs in Paris; as a clandestinely circulated political instrument, it is both a periodical obra and an activist project rather than a solely authored text. [Tornielli, pp. 161–163](https://dirasathispanicas.org/index.php/dirasathispanicas/article/download/22/22/34)

- **1895–1897:** *Turkiyā al-Fatā / La Jeune Turquie*. A bilingual Young Ottoman propaganda fortnightly on which Arslan served as editorial secretary under Ḫalīl Ġānim. It resists attribution as his individual work: most articles were unsigned and the masthead assigned direction and authorship collectively. [Tornielli, pp. 161–163](https://dirasathispanicas.org/index.php/dirasathispanicas/article/download/22/22/34)

- **1915–1921:** *La Nota*. A Buenos Aires political and cultural weekly founded by Arslan, who directed it through no. 272; the complete run comprises 312 numbers according to one institutional collection, although an earlier bibliography counted 310. It is an editorial obra made collectively, not 312 individually authored works by Arslan. [Delgado, *Revista La Nota: antología 1915–1917*, pp. 7–9](https://libros.fahce.unlp.edu.ar/index.php/libros/catalog/view/264/338/1755-1)

- **May 1925–May 1926:** *El Lápiz Azul*. A humorous, political, and literary weekly founded and directed by Arslan, with Celso Tíndaro as editorial secretary; forty-seven numbers are reported. Like his other magazines, it is best classified as an editorial project and collective manifestation. [Biographical and bibliographical summary](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Em%C3%ADn_Arsl%C3%A1n)

- **1926–1943:** *Al-Istiqlāl* (*La Independencia*). An Arabic newspaper founded and directed by Arslan in Buenos Aires as a continuing instrument for Syrian independence; Tornielli says it continued until Arslan’s death, while later scholarship has also supplied the narrower range 1926–1940. [Tornielli, p. 178](https://dirasathispanicas.org/index.php/dirasathispanicas/article/download/22/22/34); [Zengin, pp. 22–24](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/download/article-file/749183)

### Named articles, interventions, and manifestos

- **1896:** *Les Affaires de Crète*. A French-language political article published in *La Revue blanche*. [Tornielli, p. 163](https://dirasathispanicas.org/index.php/dirasathispanicas/article/download/22/22/34)

- **1896:** *Les Affaires d’Orient*. A French-language political article published in *La Revue blanche*. [Tornielli, p. 163](https://dirasathispanicas.org/index.php/dirasathispanicas/article/download/22/22/34)

- **1896:** *Les Troubles de Syrie*. A French-language political article published in *La Revue blanche*. [Tornielli, p. 163](https://dirasathispanicas.org/index.php/dirasathispanicas/article/download/22/22/34)

- **1896:** *Les Arméniens à Constantinople*. A French-language denunciation of the Hamidian massacres published in *La Revue blanche*; because it sought to mobilize opinion as well as report events, it functions as an intervention. [Tornielli, p. 163](https://dirasathispanicas.org/index.php/dirasathispanicas/article/download/22/22/34)

- **5 June 1896:** *Le Parlement français insulté*. A signed French political article in *La Jeune Turquie*, exceptional within a periodical whose contributions were mostly anonymous. [Tornielli, pp. 162–163](https://dirasathispanicas.org/index.php/dirasathispanicas/article/download/22/22/34)

- **26 January 1897:** *Communiqué to the six powers signatory to the treaties of Paris and Berlin* [descriptive title]. A collective Young Turk manifesto signed by Murad Bey, Ḫalīl Ġānim, Ahmed Rıza, Arslan, H. Antony Salmoné, and others; authorship is therefore shared, and the text acts by petitioning foreign powers for constitutional reforms. [Tornielli, pp. 163–164](https://dirasathispanicas.org/index.php/dirasathispanicas/article/download/22/22/34)

- **1911:** *La joven Turquía y Europa*. A political-historical article published in the *Revista Argentina de Ciencias Políticas*, vol. II. [Bibliographical summary](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Em%C3%ADn_Arsl%C3%A1n)

- **1911:** *La Tripolitania*. A two-part geopolitical article published in the *Revista Argentina de Ciencias Políticas*, vol. III. [Bibliographical summary](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Em%C3%ADn_Arsl%C3%A1n)

- **1913:** *Historia diplomática de la Europa balcánica*. A historical study published in the *Revista Argentina de Ciencias Políticas*, vol. VI. [Bibliographical summary](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Em%C3%ADn_Arsl%C3%A1n)

- **9 June 1914:** *Los hombres políticos de Francia: recuerdos parlamentarios*. A memoir-portrait article published in *La Nación*. [Tornielli, pp. 175, 179](https://dirasathispanicas.org/index.php/dirasathispanicas/article/download/22/22/34)

- **10 June 1916:** *Reflexiones de un condenado a muerte*. An autobiographical and political article in *La Nota* responding to Arslan’s Ottoman death sentence in absentia. [Tornielli, pp. 175–176, 179](https://dirasathispanicas.org/index.php/dirasathispanicas/article/download/22/22/34)

- **1918:** *Note to President Woodrow Wilson requesting the complete independence of Syria* [descriptive title]. A diplomatic-political petition delivered through the United States ambassador in Buenos Aires; no fixed published title is established, and it is better understood as an operative manifesto than as a literary work. Its existence rests on Arslan’s later account. [Tornielli, pp. 177–178](https://dirasathispanicas.org/index.php/dirasathispanicas/article/download/22/22/34)

- **By 1921:** *Letter to the director of La Razón explaining Arslan’s application for Argentine citizenship* [descriptive title]. A political-autobiographical letter reproduced in Arabic by Salīm Sarkīs; Tornielli could not find it in *La Razón* and could not determine whether that newspaper ever published it. [Tornielli, pp. 176–177](https://dirasathispanicas.org/index.php/dirasathispanicas/article/download/22/22/34)

- **1924:** *Joaquín V. González, íntimo*. A memorial portrait published in *Nosotros*. [Tornielli, bibliography, p. 180](https://dirasathispanicas.org/index.php/dirasathispanicas/article/download/22/22/34)

- **3 July 1927:** *Lugones: la evolución de sus ideas políticas; sus estudios de etimología arábiga; su traducción de La Ilíada*. A critical and political defense of Leopoldo Lugones published in the “Letras, Artes” supplement of *La Nación*. [Tornielli, pp. 178–180](https://dirasathispanicas.org/index.php/dirasathispanicas/article/download/22/22/34)

- **23 October 1927:** *Oriente contra Occidente*. A geopolitical essay published in the “Letras, Artes” supplement of *La Nación*. [Tornielli, pp. 178–180](https://dirasathispanicas.org/index.php/dirasathispanicas/article/download/22/22/34)

- **Date not securely established:** *Los cuentos de Oriente*. A serial rubric or sequence in *La Nota*, rather than a demonstrably fixed book; later study identifies it as a series but does not establish a complete contents list. [Zengin, pp. 20–24](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/download/article-file/749183)

- **1890s–1943:** *Dispersed journalism, correspondence, diplomatic reports, and periodical prose*. This is a body of work rather than a single text and resists enumeration: Gasquet states that Arslan’s articles in *La Nota* alone would fill several volumes and calls his contributions elsewhere “innumerable,” while later research describes material scattered across three continents in Arabic, French, and Spanish. No collected-works edition or complete article bibliography yet fixes its boundaries, so claiming a complete item-by-item list would be false. [Gasquet, pp. 111–112](https://www.redalyc.org/pdf/1817/181725488008.pdf); [study of Arslan’s self-presentation in *La Nota*](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/398509330_espejo_del_Emir_El_semanario_La_Nota_como_plataforma_de_exhibicion_publica_del_Emir_Emin_Arslan)

### Dramatic projects known only by reference

- **Date unknown:** *La sultana*. A four-act play reported by Arslan in lists printed in his own books; scholars found no independent record or manuscript. It therefore survives as a project-title, not as a verifiable completed obra. [Gasquet, notes 9–10, p. 112](https://www.redalyc.org/pdf/1817/181725488008.pdf)

- **Date unknown:** *El Libertador*. A projected drama on the life of José de San Martín, described as a prologue and four acts; no manuscript or independent record has been located. [Gasquet, notes 9–10, p. 112](https://www.redalyc.org/pdf/1817/181725488008.pdf)

- **Date unknown:** *El amor en la diplomacia*. A three-act play known only through Arslan’s own bibliographical notices; its completion cannot be verified and the text is considered lost. [Gasquet, notes 9–10, p. 112](https://www.redalyc.org/pdf/1817/181725488008.pdf)

- **Date unknown:** *Estaba escrito*. A three-act play likewise known only from Arslan’s lists, without a located manuscript or independent record. [Gasquet, notes 9–10, p. 112](https://www.redalyc.org/pdf/1817/181725488008.pdf)

### Institutional creation

- **1926:** *Sociedad Drusa de Beneficencia*. A mutual-aid institution founded by Arslan in Buenos Aires, now the Asociación de Beneficencia Drusa. It is an institutional obra rather than a text: an organization intended to continue acting beyond its founder. [Biographical and bibliographical summary](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Em%C3%ADn_Arsl%C3%A1n)
