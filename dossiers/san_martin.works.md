## Works by José de San Martín

### Documentary obra: correspondence, orders, and state papers

- **1812–1850:** *Correspondence, proclamations, military orders, reports, decrees, and private papers*. This is San Martín’s largest textual obra, comprising thousands of individually dated documents rather than a single authored book. The foundational scholarly collection is the Museo Mitre’s twelve-volume *Documentos del Archivo de San Martín* (1910–1911); because it reproduces both papers by San Martín and documents addressed or related to him, inclusion there does not by itself establish his authorship. ([Museo Mitre edition record](https://onlinebooks.library.upenn.edu/webbin/book/lookupname?key=Museo+Mitre); [archive description](https://museomitre.cultura.gob.ar/media/uploads/site-10/multimedia/descripcion_museo_mitre_isdiah_revisada_2019.pdf))
- **1812–1822:** *Official correspondence and orders of the liberation campaigns*. This dispersed administrative and military corpus includes instructions, appointments, intelligence, logistical accounts, battle reports, and correspondence produced through secretaries and headquarters. These documents resist treatment as wholly autograph “works”: some were dictated, drafted by aides, countersigned, copied, or issued institutionally in San Martín’s name. ([Museo Mitre, *Documentos del Archivo de San Martín*](https://books.google.com.mx/books?id=5ahmAAAAMAAJ&lr=&num=20); [Colección Documental de la Independencia del Perú, vol. XIII](https://fuenteshistoricasdelperu.com/2021/03/27/cdip-tomo-xiii-obra-de-gobierno-y-epistolario-de-san-martin-2-vol/))
- **1822–1850:** *European and retirement correspondence*. Letters to political leaders, soldiers, friends, and relatives constitute an extended epistolary obra rather than a book; surviving collections are necessarily incomplete, and numerous letters remain scattered among different archives. ([Museo Mitre archive description](https://museomitre.cultura.gob.ar/media/uploads/site-10/multimedia/descripcion_museo_mitre_isdiah_revisada_2019.pdf); [Museo Mitre collection description](https://museomitre.cultura.gob.ar/noticia/conoce-nuestro-archivo-historico/))

### Projects and strategic designs

- **1814–1822:** *Plan Continental*. A strategic project to defend the Río de la Plata, build an army in Cuyo, cross the Andes, secure Chile, and reach Peru by sea. It was never a single formally promulgated text: historians reconstruct it from correspondence and its execution; moreover, the famous 1814 letter commonly treated as its concise statement has a disputed date and authenticity. ([Instituto Nacional Sanmartiniano overview](https://sanmartiniano.cultura.gob.ar/noticia/jose-de-san-martin-libertador-de-america/); [discussion of the disputed letter](https://sanmartiniano.cultura.gob.ar/noticia/abril-un-mes-decisivo-en-la-vida-del-general-jose-de-san-martin-y-en-el-recuerdo-de-su-legado/))
- **1814–1817:** *Army of the Andes*. A military and logistical creation assembled under San Martín’s direction in Cuyo from existing and newly organized units, workshops, supply systems, officers, soldiers, and civilian labor. It is an institutional obra with many makers, not an artifact or text attributable to San Martín alone. ([Instituto Nacional Sanmartiniano educational history](https://sanmartiniano.cultura.gob.ar/media/uploads/site-31/multimedia/Recursos/suite-san-martin.-historia.pdf))
- **1817:** *Crossing of the Andes*. A coordinated multi-column military operation designed and commanded within the Plan Continental. Calling it a “work” risks converting the actions and suffering of thousands of participants into one man’s creation; it belongs here as a realized collective project, not as San Martín’s sole manufacture. ([Instituto Nacional Sanmartiniano account](https://sanmartiniano.cultura.gob.ar/noticia/jose-de-san-martin-libertador-de-america/))
- **1819–1820:** *Expedition for the Liberation of Peru*. An amphibious military-political project organized jointly with the Chilean government and carried by multinational land and naval forces. San Martín supplied its strategic direction and commanded the expedition, but neither the fleet nor the undertaking was exclusively his creation. ([Instituto Nacional Sanmartiniano account](https://sanmartiniano.cultura.gob.ar/noticia/suite-san-martin-y-la-campana-libertadora/))

### Manifestos, proclamations, and performed declarations

- **1812–1822:** *Proclamations to soldiers and peoples*. A corpus of short public texts issued at successive stages of the campaigns, including proclamations to the inhabitants of the Río de la Plata, Chile, and Peru. They are manifestos in the strong sense: texts meant to recruit, authorize, reassure, command, and create political allegiance rather than merely describe events; individual wording may involve secretarial composition. ([Museo Mitre, *Documentos del Archivo de San Martín: Impresos, 1810–1827*](https://play.google.com/store/books/details/Museo_Mitre_Documentos_del_archivo_de_San_Mart%C3%ADn_I?id=EGpTUSzbxu8C); [CDIP, vol. XIII contents](https://fuenteshistoricasdelperu.com/2021/03/27/cdip-tomo-xiii-obra-de-gobierno-y-epistolario-de-san-martin-2-vol/))
- **22 July 1820:** *Proclamation to the inhabitants of the United Provinces*. A manifesto explaining San Martín’s refusal to divert the liberation army into the Río de la Plata’s civil conflict and announcing the expeditionary project. It acts as a public justification of command, not as a retrospective memoir. ([Instituto Nacional Sanmartiniano](https://sanmartiniano.cultura.gob.ar/noticia/el-retorno-de-san-martin-en-sus-propias-palabras/))
- **8 September 1820–1822:** *Proclamations, orders, and proclamatory bulletins of the Peruvian campaign*. A serial public-text obra addressed variously to Peruvians, royalist troops, Indigenous communities, enslaved people, and the expeditionary forces. The documentary edition groups these materials as a corpus; their collaborative governmental production makes sole literary authorship uncertain in individual cases. ([CDIP, vol. XIII contents](https://fuenteshistoricasdelperu.com/2021/03/27/cdip-tomo-xiii-obra-de-gobierno-y-epistolario-de-san-martin-2-vol/))
- **28 July 1821:** *Proclamation of the Independence of Peru*. A spoken political manifestation performed by San Martín in Lima: the public declaration itself is his historically consequential act, while the written Act of Independence was drafted by Manuel Pérez de Tudela and therefore must not be reassigned to San Martín. ([Instituto Nacional Sanmartiniano documentary presentation](https://sanmartiniano.cultura.gob.ar/noticia/la-independencia-del-peru/); [Congress of Peru historical account](https://www3.congreso.gob.pe/Docs/comisiones2021/CE-Tribunal-Constitucional/files/expedientes/exp-1-10/bicentenario-i_copia.pdf))
- **20 September 1822:** *Address on the installation of the Constituent Congress and renunciation of power*. A performative state text by which San Martín reported the transfer of authority and terminated the Protectorate. It belongs simultaneously to oratory, constitutional action, and political self-withdrawal rather than to literature in the narrow sense. ([CDIP, vol. XIII contents](https://fuenteshistoricasdelperu.com/2021/03/27/cdip-tomo-xiii-obra-de-gobierno-y-epistolario-de-san-martin-2-vol/))

### Protocols and constitutional instruments

- **12 February 1821:** *Reglamento Provisional*. A protocol for territorial division and civil administration in the Peruvian regions controlled by the liberation army. It was issued under San Martín’s authority but countersigned by Bernardo de Monteagudo and Juan García del Río, so it is a governmental instrument rather than securely his sole-authored prose. ([Congress of Peru text](https://www.congreso.gob.pe/Docs/sites/webs/constitucion/constituciones/Reglamento-Provisional-1821.pdf))
- **3 August 1821:** *Decree assuming the supreme political and military command of Peru*. A constitutive act that joined those powers in San Martín under the title Protector; its function was to make a governmental office and regime, not merely describe them. ([Peruvian Ministry of Foreign Affairs record](https://www.gob.pe/institucion/rree/normas-legales/6472510-1-1821-re); [Congress of Peru facsimile](https://www.congreso.gob.pe/Docs/participacion/museo/congreso/files/mensajes/1822-1840/a-mensaje-1821-3.pdf))
- **8 October 1821:** *Estatuto Provisional*. A constitutional protocol for the Protectorate, promulgated by San Martín and countersigned by his ministers. Its collective, official formation prevents uncomplicated classification as an individually authored “work.” ([Congress of Peru text](https://www3.congreso.gob.pe/Docs/sites/webs/constitucion/constituciones/Estatuto-Provisional-1821.pdf))
- **1821–1822:** *Decrees of the Protectorate of Peru*. A body of executive acts concerning government, justice, education, taxation, commerce, emancipation, honors, and public institutions. They form an administrative obra of government, but secretarial and ministerial drafting means that San Martín’s signature or promulgating authority cannot automatically be equated with verbal authorship. ([CDIP, *Obra de gobierno y epistolario de San Martín*](https://fuenteshistoricasdelperu.com/2021/03/27/cdip-tomo-xiii-obra-de-gobierno-y-epistolario-de-san-martin-2-vol/))

### Institutions and collective creations

- **16 March 1812 onward:** *Mounted Grenadiers Regiment*. A military institution created and initially organized under San Martín’s command. Its continuing corporate existence exceeds its founder and was made collectively by officers, soldiers, administrators, craftspeople, and successive governments. ([Instituto Nacional Sanmartiniano biography](https://sanmartiniano.cultura.gob.ar/noticia/jose-de-san-martin-libertador-de-america/))
- **1821:** *Protectorate of Peru*. A provisional governmental formation made through decrees, ministries, military occupation, and negotiated local authority. It can be regarded as a political-institutional obra, but not San Martín’s alone: ministers, councils, soldiers, officials, and Peruvian political actors materially constituted it. ([decree establishing San Martín’s authority](https://www.gob.pe/institucion/rree/normas-legales/6472510-1-1821-re); [CDIP documentary contents](https://fuenteshistoricasdelperu.com/2021/03/27/cdip-tomo-xiii-obra-de-gobierno-y-epistolario-de-san-martin-2-vol/))
- **1821:** *National Library of Peru*. A public institution founded during the Protectorate and initially supplied in part by San Martín’s own books. San Martín made the founding political act and donation, not the library’s entire collection or subsequent institutional life. ([catalogue of San Martín’s donated library](https://revistafenix.bnp.gob.pe/index.php/fenix/article/view/133); [CDIP documentary contents](https://fuenteshistoricasdelperu.com/2021/03/27/cdip-tomo-xiii-obra-de-gobierno-y-epistolario-de-san-martin-2-vol/))
- **1821:** *Order of the Sun of Peru*. A state order of merit instituted by the Protectorate as a device for producing and rewarding a new patriotic elite. Its legal creation belongs to San Martín’s government, while its insignia, manufacture, administration, and membership were collective works rather than objects personally fashioned by him. ([CDIP, *Obra de gobierno y epistolario de San Martín*](https://fuenteshistoricasdelperu.com/2021/03/27/cdip-tomo-xiii-obra-de-gobierno-y-epistolario-de-san-martin-2-vol/))

### Pedagogical and personal texts

- **1825:** *Máximas para Merceditas*. A concise pedagogical protocol written for the upbringing of San Martín’s daughter, Mercedes. It is a private list of principles rather than a treatise or book; the National Sanmartinian Institute dates its composition to San Martín’s Brussels residence in 1825. ([Instituto Nacional Sanmartiniano](https://sanmartiniano.cultura.gob.ar/noticia/las-maximas-de-san-martin-para-mercedes/))
- **23 January 1844:** *Testamento*. A notarized legal protocol governing San Martín’s estate, family obligations, burial, arms, and papers. Although it expresses personal decisions and bears his authority, a testament is conventionally mediated by legal form and not necessarily composed word for word by the testator. ([Instituto Nacional Sanmartiniano](https://sanmartiniano.cultura.gob.ar/noticia/testamento-del-general-san-martin/))

### Attributed, collaborative, or wrongly assigned things

- **1814:** *Letter to Nicolás Rodríguez Peña stating the “secret” route through Chile to Lima*. Often presented as the textual seed of the Plan Continental, but its authenticity and even its date—given as 22 March or 22 April in different institutional discussions—have been contested. It should remain an attributed document, not an unquestioned autograph cornerstone. ([Instituto Nacional Sanmartiniano, *Anales*, vol. VIII](https://sanmartiniano.cultura.gob.ar/media/uploads/site-31/multimedia/tomo_08_anales.pdf); [Institute discussion of the controversy](https://sanmartiniano.cultura.gob.ar/noticia/abril-un-mes-decisivo-en-la-vida-del-general-jose-de-san-martin-y-en-el-recuerdo-de-su-legado/))
- **15 July 1821:** *Act of Independence of Peru*. San Martín initiated and enabled the political process, but the act was drafted by Manuel Pérez de Tudela and subscribed collectively in Lima. It therefore resists inclusion as a work “by” San Martín and is recorded here specifically to prevent the public declaration he performed from becoming a false claim of textual authorship. ([Congress of Peru historical account](https://www3.congreso.gob.pe/Docs/comisiones2021/CE-Tribunal-Constitucional/files/expedientes/exp-1-10/bicentenario-i_copia.pdf))
- **1821–1822:** *Government publications bearing San Martín’s name*. Printed decrees, gazettes, regulations, and official notices sometimes bear his signature or title but were produced through ministers, secretaries, printers, and public offices. They are manifestations of his government; unless a manuscript or documentary apparatus establishes composition, they should not automatically be called personally authored texts. ([Museo Mitre, *Impresos, 1810–1827*](https://play.google.com/store/books/details/Museo_Mitre_Documentos_del_archivo_de_San_Mart%C3%ADn_I?id=EGpTUSzbxu8C); [CDIP, vol. XIII](https://fuenteshistoricasdelperu.com/2021/03/27/cdip-tomo-xiii-obra-de-gobierno-y-epistolario-de-san-martin-2-vol/))
