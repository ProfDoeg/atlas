## Works by Max Weber

### Legal and economic-historical studies

- **1887:** *Notes on Italian and Spanish statutes, commercial law, and common private law*. Preparatory research notes rather than a finished publication; their survival makes them an archival obra whose boundaries remain uncertain. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1889:** *Entwickelung des Solidarhaftprinzips und des Sondervermögens der offenen Handelsgesellschaft aus den Haushalts- und Gewerbegemeinschaften in den italienischen Städten*. Weber’s doctoral dissertation, publicly defended with appended theses; it is an academic qualification text and the first form of his medieval-commercial-law project. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1889:** *Zur Geschichte der Handelsgesellschaften im Mittelalter. Nach südeuropäischen Quellen*. A monograph expanding the dissertation project into a comparative history of medieval commercial partnerships. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1890:** *Review of Friedrich Conze, Kauf nach hanseatischen Quellen*. A scholarly review and intervention in commercial-law historiography, not a book-length obra. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1890:** *Review of A. von Kostanecki, Der öffentliche Kredit im Mittelalter*. A scholarly review of a study of medieval public credit. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1891:** *Die römische Agrargeschichte in ihrer Bedeutung für das Staats- und Privatrecht*. Weber’s habilitation study: a monograph joining agrarian history, Roman law, and institutional reconstruction. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1893:** *Review of Wilhelm Kaufmann, Das internationale Recht der ägyptischen Staatsschuld*. A short legal-economic review. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1893:** *Review of Georg Schaps, Zur Geschichte des Wechselindossaments*. A short contribution to the history of negotiable instruments and commercial law. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1894:** *Die römische Agrargeschichte in ihrer Bedeutung für das Staats- und Privatrecht: review and reply literature*. Weber’s replies occasioned by criticism belong to the continuing Roman-agrarian-history project rather than constituting a new monograph. [MWG I/2](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-2/)

### Land-labour inquiry and agrarian politics

- **1892:** *“Privatenquête” über die Lage der Landarbeiter*. A three-part report based on private inquiry into rural labour; it is empirical social research in serial newspaper form. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1892:** *Zur Rechtfertigung Göhres*. A polemical public defence of Paul Göhre and of empirical engagement with workers’ lives. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1892:** *Die Lage der Landarbeiter im ostelbischen Deutschland*. A massive commissioned survey of agricultural labour in the eastern German provinces; simultaneously a statistical report, regional social history, and political diagnosis. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1893:** *Die Erhebung des Vereins für Sozialpolitik über die Lage der Landarbeiter*. A six-part public account of the Verein’s rural-labour investigation. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1893:** *Wie werden einwandfreie Erhebungen über die Lage der Landarbeiter angestellt?* A protocol-like methodological text instructing investigators how to obtain defensible evidence about rural labour. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1893:** *Die ländliche Arbeitsverfassung*. A lecture-text and political-economic analysis of agrarian labour organization; reports and printed forms survive in more than one textual state. [MWG I/4](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-4/)
- **1893:** *Die Verhältnisse der Landarbeiter im ostelbischen Deutschland*. Weber’s address to the Verein für Sozialpolitik and its ensuing discussion: an oral intervention known through printed proceedings rather than a self-standing authored book. [MWG I/4](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-4/)
- **1894:** *Entwickelungstendenzen in der Lage der ostelbischen Landarbeiter*. A social-policy essay deriving political tendencies from the land-labour inquiry. [MWG I/4](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-4/)
- **1894:** *Agrarstatistische und sozialpolitische Betrachtungen zur Fideikommißfrage in Preußen*. An empirical-policy study of entailed estates and Prussian agrarian structure. [MWG I/4](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-4/)
- **1894:** *Die deutschen Landarbeiter*. A synthetic public account of the rural-labour question. [MWG I/4](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-4/)
- **1895:** *Der Nationalstaat und die Volkswirtschaftspolitik*. Weber’s Freiburg inaugural address: a programmatic political-economic speech whose performative nationalism makes it closer to a manifesto than to a neutral treatise. [MWG I/4](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-4/)
- **1895:** *Die Wanderungen der Landarbeiter*. A study of rural labour migration and its demographic, economic, and national-political implications. [MWG I/4](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-4/)
- **1895:** *Die ländliche Arbeitsverfassung*. A revised or differently transmitted treatment of agrarian labour relations; its identity overlaps with Weber’s earlier lectures and interventions rather than forming a wholly discrete “book.” [MWG I/4](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-4/)
- **1896:** *Agrarpolitik*. A five-lecture project—agrarian history, agrarian constitution, agrarian credit, land labour, and agrarian protection—preserved principally through newspaper reports; these reports are manifestations of Weber’s speech, not verbatim authored texts. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1896:** *Die Zukunft der deutschen Bodenverteilung*. A public lecture on land distribution known through press reports, and therefore resistant to treatment as a fixed Weber text. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1896:** *Die Gegensätze der deutschen Agrarverfassung in ihren Ursachen und ihrer nationalökonomischen Bedeutung*. A comparative agrarian lecture transmitted by reports rather than an authorially fixed publication. [MWG I/4](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-4/)
- **1897:** *Agrarverhältnisse im Altertum*. A contribution to the *Handwörterbuch der Staatswissenschaften*, repeatedly revised in later editions; it is better understood as a changing research project than as one stable text. [MWG I/6](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-6/)
- **1904:** *The Relations of the Rural Community to Other Branches of Social Science*. A lecture for the St Louis Congress, published in English translation in 1906; the lost or unavailable German original complicates claims about its final wording. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)

### Stock-exchange research and public-economic protocols

- **1894–1896:** *Die Ergebnisse der deutschen Börsenenquête*. A four-installment analytical project interpreting the German stock-exchange inquiry. [MWG I/5](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-5/)
- **1894:** *Die Börse. I. Zweck und äußere Organisation der Börsen*. A popular-educational pamphlet explaining the institution and organization of exchanges. [MWG I/5](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-5/)
- **1895:** *Börsenwesen*. An encyclopedia article and compact institutional account of exchange trading. [MWG I/5](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-5/)
- **1896:** *Die technische Funktion des Terminhandels*. A two-part explanatory article on futures trading. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1896:** *Die Börse. II. Der Börsenverkehr*. The second popular-educational pamphlet, explaining exchange transactions; a small excerpt later circulated separately as *Der Verkehr auf der Börse*. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1896:** *Proposals, report, and interventions concerning admission of securities to exchange trading*. Committee work comprising amendments, technical recommendations, and discussion contributions; it is jointly situated administrative production, not a single autonomous essay. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1896:** *Bericht des provisorischen Börsenausschusses, betreffend die Neuordnung der Verkehrsnormen an den deutschen Produktenbörsen*. An authorized committee report; Weber helped make and authorize it, but its collective institutional authorship resists attribution to him alone. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)

### Ancient economy and social structure

- **1896:** *Die sozialen Gründe des Untergangs der antiken Kultur*. A lecture and published essay explaining antiquity’s transformation through labour, military, and agrarian structures. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1897–1909:** *Agrarverhältnisse im Altertum*. An encyclopedia study successively enlarged and reconceived; its versions form a long-lived obra rather than simple reprints of one text. [MWG I/6](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-6/)
- **1909:** *Die Agrarverhältnisse im Altertum*. The final and greatly expanded manifestation of Weber’s ancient-agrarian project, approaching a comparative sociology of ancient economies. [MWG I/6](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-6/)

### Methodology and the logic of cultural science

- **1903–1906:** *Roscher und Knies und die logischen Probleme der historischen Nationalökonomie*. A three-installment methodological critique of historical economics and the problem of irrationality. [MWG I/7](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-7/)
- **1904:** *Die “Objektivität” sozialwissenschaftlicher und sozialpolitischer Erkenntnis*. A methodological manifesto for the *Archiv für Sozialwissenschaft und Sozialpolitik*, defining ideal types, value-relevance, and the limits of scientific judgment. [MWG I/7](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-7/)
- **1905:** *Knies und das Irrationalitätsproblem*. The second phase of *Roscher und Knies*, published serially and inseparable from that larger methodological project. [MWG I/7](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-7/)
- **1906:** *Kritische Studien auf dem Gebiet der kulturwissenschaftlichen Logik*. A paired methodological study of historical causation, interpretation, and objective possibility. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1907:** *R. Stammlers “Überwindung” der materialistischen Geschichtsauffassung*. A sustained methodological polemic against Rudolf Stammler’s attempt to refute materialist history. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1908:** *Die Grenznutzlehre und das “psychophysische Grundgesetz”*. A methodological-economic essay rejecting attempts to ground marginal utility directly in psychophysics. [MWG I/8](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-8/)
- **1909:** *Energetische Kulturtheorien*. A critical essay on theories that reduce cultural processes to energetic laws. [MWG I/8](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-8/)
- **1913:** *Über einige Kategorien der verstehenden Soziologie*. A foundational conceptual essay on interpretive sociology, social action, association, and order. [MWG I/12](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-12/)
- **1917:** *Der Sinn der “Wertfreiheit” der soziologischen und ökonomischen Wissenschaften*. A methodological intervention distinguishing empirical analysis from practical valuation; it grew from earlier committee debate and survives in successive formulations. [MWG I/12](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-12/)
- **1922:** *Nachtrag zu dem Aufsatz über R. Stammlers “Überwindung” der materialistischen Geschichtsauffassung*. A posthumously published supplement whose surviving manuscript belongs to the earlier Stammler project rather than to the editors’ collected volume as such. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)

### Capitalism, Protestantism, and sects

- **1904–1905:** *Die protestantische Ethik und der “Geist” des Kapitalismus*. A two-part journal study linking ascetic Protestant conduct to the formation of a capitalist ethos; Weber substantially revised it in 1919–1920, so no single version exhausts the obra. [MWG I/9](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-9/)
- **1906:** *“Kirchen” und “Sekten”*. A two-part newspaper essay comparing church and sect organization. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1906:** *“Kirchen” und “Sekten” in Nordamerika*. A revised church- and social-political sketch grounded partly in Weber’s American journey; it is a distinct manifestation of the sects project. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1907:** *Kritische Bemerkungen zu den vorstehenden “Kritischen Beiträgen”*. Weber’s first published reply to H. Karl Fischer’s criticism of the Protestant-ethic thesis. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1908:** *Bemerkungen zu der vorstehenden Replik*. A further reply in the controversy over the Protestant-ethic thesis. [MWG I/9](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-9/)
- **1908:** *Die protestantische Ethik und der Geist des Kapitalismus: Replik*. A polemical defence against Felix Rachfahl; its status is controversy-text rather than independent treatise. [MWG I/9](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-9/)
- **1910:** *Antikritisches zum “Geist” des Kapitalismus*. A continuation of Weber’s replies to Rachfahl. [MWG I/9](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-9/)
- **1910:** *Antikritisches Schlußwort zum “Geist des Kapitalismus”*. Weber’s extended closing statement in the Protestant-ethic controversy. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1919–1920:** *Die protestantische Ethik und der Geist des Kapitalismus, revised version*. The heavily revised book-form manifestation prepared for *Gesammelte Aufsätze zur Religionssoziologie*; it is neither merely a reprint nor an entirely new work. [MWG I/9](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-9/)
- **1919–1920:** *Die protestantischen Sekten und der Geist des Kapitalismus*. The final recasting of Weber’s church-and-sect writings for the collected sociology of religion. [MWG I/9](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-9/)

### Russian politics

- **1906:** *Zur Lage der bürgerlichen Demokratie in Rußland*. A long political-sociological analysis of Russian liberalism, constitutional development, and agrarian society. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1906:** *Rußlands Übergang zum Scheinkonstitutionalismus*. A second large study diagnosing the post-revolutionary Russian constitutional settlement as pseudo-constitutional. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1917:** *Rußlands Übergang zur Scheindemokratie*. A political analysis of the February Revolution and the fragile social foundations of Russian democracy. [MWG I/15](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-15/)

### Industrial labour and empirical sociology

- **1908:** *Methodologische Einleitung für die Erhebungen des Vereins für Sozialpolitik über Auslese und Anpassung der Arbeiterschaft der geschlossenen Großindustrie*. A research protocol defining an empirical programme for studying industrial workers. [MWG I/11](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-11/)
- **1908:** *Erhebungen über Auslese und Anpassung der Arbeiterschaft der geschlossenen Großindustrie*. A collective research project designed and methodologically directed by Weber; authorship is distributed across questionnaires, investigators, and reports. [MWG I/11](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-11/)
- **1908–1909:** *Zur Psychophysik der industriellen Arbeit*. A multi-part empirical and methodological study of fatigue, performance, work rhythm, and worker selection. [MWG I/11](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-11/)
- **1909:** *Zur Methodik sozialpsychologischer Enquêten und ihrer Bearbeitung*. A protocol-like contribution on the design and interpretation of social-psychological surveys. [MWG I/11](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-11/)
- **1910:** *Marie Bernays und die M.-Gladbacher Arbeiterschaft*. A public letter defending Bernays’s field research among factory workers. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)

### Sociological institutions and unrealized research projects

- **1909:** *Geschäftsbericht der Deutschen Gesellschaft für Soziologie*. An organizational report made within a collective institutional setting; Weber’s responsibility does not make every word exclusively his. [MWG I/13](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-13/)
- **1909–1910:** *Erhebung über die Soziologie des Zeitungswesens*. An ambitious empirical project on the press, represented by proposals, preliminary reports, questionnaires, and speeches but never completed as the planned investigation; the project itself is the work. [MWG I/13](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-13/)
- **1910:** *Die Deutsche Gesellschaft für Soziologie*. An anonymously published institutional manifesto explaining the new society’s purpose. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1910:** *Antrag auf Statutenänderung der Deutschen Gesellschaft für Soziologie*. A draft governance protocol, not a descriptive essay. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1910:** *Vorbericht über eine vorgeschlagene Erhebung über die Soziologie des Zeitungswesens*. An anonymously circulated prospectus surviving in multiple manuscript and printed states. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1910:** *Vorläufiger Entwurf eines Gründungsstatus der Abteilung für Statistik*. An anonymous draft statute: a protocol intended to bring an institutional structure into existence. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1910:** *Handbuch der politischen Ökonomie: Vorbemerkung zum Stoffverteilungsplan and Stoffverteilungsplan*. The architectural plan for a collaborative handbook; it became the project from which *Grundriß der Sozialökonomik* and Weber’s unfinished *Wirtschaft und Gesellschaft* emerged. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1914:** *Vorwort to Grundriß der Sozialökonomik*. A jointly authored programmatic preface; Weber’s share is real but not separable into an exclusively authored independent text. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)

### Comparative sociology of religion

- **1915–1920:** *Die Wirtschaftsethik der Weltreligionen*. An unfinished comparative programme published in eleven journal installments; its changing sequence and incomplete design make the project, not any later editorial synthesis, the most defensible obra. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1915:** *Einleitung to Die Wirtschaftsethik der Weltreligionen*. A programmatic introduction defining the comparative problem of religious ethics and social conduct. [MWG I/19](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-19/)
- **1915:** *Der Konfuzianismus*. The first major comparative study in the world-religions project; Weber revised it as *Konfuzianismus und Taoismus* in 1919–1920. [MWG I/19](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-19/)
- **1915:** *Zwischenbetrachtung: Stufen und Richtungen der religiösen Weltablehnung*. A theoretical interlude mapping conflicts between religious salvation and the differentiated orders of the world. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1916–1917:** *Hinduismus und Buddhismus*. A serial comparative study of Indian religion, status order, and salvation doctrines, subsequently revised for book publication. [MWG I/20](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-20/)
- **1917–1920:** *Das antike Judentum*. A serial study of ancient Judaism and its social bearers; the project was published in installments and remained connected to Weber’s uncompleted plans for further studies of Christianity and Islam. [MWG I/21](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-21/)
- **1919–1920:** *Vorbemerkung to Gesammelte Aufsätze zur Religionssoziologie*. A retrospective programmatic essay situating Weber’s inquiries into capitalism, rationalization, and the comparative uniqueness of Western development. [MWG I/18](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-18/)
- **1919–1920:** *Konfuzianismus und Taoismus*. The revised book-form manifestation of the China study, not simply identical with the 1915 installments. [MWG I/19](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-19/)
- **1919–1920:** *Hinduismus und Buddhismus, revised version*. The book-form revision prepared for the collected sociology of religion. [MWG I/20](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-20/)
- **1919–1920:** *Das antike Judentum, revised version*. Weber’s final revision of the ancient-Judaism study; publication was completed posthumously. [MWG I/21](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-21/)

### Music sociology

- **ca. 1910–1913; published 1921:** *Die rationalen und soziologischen Grundlagen der Musik*. A posthumously published manuscript on tuning, notation, instruments, harmony, and musical rationalization; Weber did not prepare the surviving text as a finished book, so its title and closure are editorial manifestations of an unfinished study. [MWG I/14](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-14/)

### War memoranda and political writings

- **1914:** *Report on disciplinary questions in the military hospitals*. An administrative report produced through Weber’s wartime hospital service. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1914:** *Submission concerning the preservation of discipline in military hospitals*. An administrative memorandum with appendices: an operative institutional text rather than political theory. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1915:** *Report on disciplinary affairs in the reserve hospitals of northern Baden*. An official wartime report. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1915:** *Abschließender Erfahrungsbericht über die Lazarettverwaltung*. A concluding administrative account, first printed posthumously; it records a practical organizational obra as well as describing it. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1915:** *Bismarcks Außenpolitik und die Gegenwart*. A two-part newspaper intervention comparing Bismarckian diplomacy with wartime policy. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **late 1915; published 1921:** *Zur Frage des Friedensschließens*. A wartime manuscript published only after Weber’s death. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1916:** *Zwischen zwei Gesetzen*. An open letter to Gertrud Bäumer on national duty and moral responsibility during war. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1916:** *Der verschärfte U-Boot-Krieg*. A confidential memorandum prepared with Felix Somary; its collaborative authorship must not be silently assigned to Weber alone. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1916:** *Protokoll der dritten Sitzung des Arbeitsausschusses für Mitteleuropa*. A committee protocol associated with Weber’s work for the Mitteleuropa organization; it is institutionally co-produced rather than an autonomous essay. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1916:** *Deutschland unter den europäischen Weltmächten*. A public political address and printed intervention on German war aims and international position. [MWG I/15](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-15/)
- **1916:** *Deutschlands äußere und Preußens innere Politik*. A wartime political essay connecting foreign policy with domestic constitutional reform. [MWG I/15](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-15/)
- **1917:** *Der Sinn der Neutralität*. A newspaper intervention on neutrality and German foreign policy. [MWG I/15](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-15/)
- **1917:** *Wahlrecht und Demokratie in Deutschland*. A political pamphlet advocating democratization and electoral reform. [MWG I/15](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-15/)
- **1917:** *Parlament und Regierung im neugeordneten Deutschland*, newspaper series. A multi-part wartime series on parliamentarization, leadership, bureaucracy, and party government, later reworked as a pamphlet. [MWG I/15](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-15/)
- **1917:** *Deutschlands künftige Staatsform*. A constitutional-political intervention anticipating the postwar order. [MWG I/15](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-15/)
- **1918:** *Innere Lage und Außenpolitik*. A three-part newspaper analysis linking domestic reform with Germany’s international position. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1918:** *Parlament und Regierung im neugeordneten Deutschland. Zur politischen Kritik des Beamtentums und Parteiwesens*. The expanded pamphlet manifestation of the parliamentary-government project. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1918:** *Der Sozialismus*. A published lecture analyzing socialist doctrine and organization; the print text derives from oral delivery rather than a conventional monograph. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1918:** *Die nächste innerpolitische Aufgabe*. A newspaper intervention urging immediate constitutional and political action. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1918:** *Waffenstillstand und Frieden*. An anonymously published newspaper intervention on armistice and peace. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1918:** *Die Staatsform Deutschlands / Die deutsche Staatsform*. A five-installment newspaper series whose title changed during publication; it is one constitutional project in two named manifestations. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)

### Constitution-making and democratic manifestos

- **1918:** *Discussion contributions on the constitution of the German Reich*. Weber’s interventions in official constitutional deliberations; the surviving protocol reports speech and cannot be treated as a verbatim authored essay. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1918:** *Zum Reichsverfassungsgesetz-Entwurf betreffend Rezeption von neu sich bildenden Staaten (“Teilung Preußens”)*. Draft constitutional language intended to act as law: a protocol in the broad sense, not descriptive prose. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1918:** *Zum Reichsverfassungsgesetz-Entwurf betreffend Garantien der Verfassungen der Freistaaten und Gemeinden*. A second piece of operative constitutional drafting. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1918–1919:** *Germany’s constitutional reconstruction project*. Weber’s draft clauses, memoranda, committee contributions, and public arguments helped shape—but did not singly author—the Weimar constitutional process; the political-institutional project is larger than any one surviving text. [MWG I/16](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-16/)
- **1919:** *Deutschlands Wiederaufrichtung*. A campaign speech known from several newspaper reports; it is a political manifestation without a fixed authorial text. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1919:** *Deutschlands Vergangenheit und Zukunft*. A campaign speech transmitted in press reports rather than a Weber manuscript. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1919:** *Die kommende Reichsverfassung*. A public lecture known from a newspaper report and therefore resistant to classification as a stable authored text. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1919:** *Probleme der Neuordnung*. A reported campaign speech; the title and wording belong partly to journalistic mediation. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1919:** *Der freie Volksstaat*. A democratic campaign speech surviving through multiple press reports and an announcement. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1919:** *Student und Politik*. A reported lecture to politically organized students, not an authorially established publication. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1919:** *Erklärung gegen antisemitische Propaganda*. A co-signed public declaration: a manifesto Weber endorsed but did not necessarily formulate. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1919:** *Aufruf an die demokratische Jugend*. A co-signed political appeal appearing in variant newspaper versions; authorization is clearer than sole verbal authorship. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1919:** *Für eine Politik des Rechts*. A co-signed declaration by the Heidelberg association for a politics of law; it acts publicly and thus fits “manifesto,” but Weber’s exact compositional share is undetermined. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1919:** *Aufruf zur Unterstützung der elsässisch-lothringischen Flüchtlinge*. A co-signed appeal whose collective authorization should not be confused with sole authorship. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)

### Vocational speeches

- **1917; published 1919:** *Wissenschaft als Beruf*. A lecture on the institutional and ethical conditions of scholarship; the familiar printed text appeared after the oral event and is an edited manifestation, not a stenographically exact transcript. [MWG I/17](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-17/)
- **1919:** *Politik als Beruf*. A lecture and subsequent publication on political leadership, vocation, responsibility, and conviction; a surviving keyword manuscript and the printed text document different manifestations. [MWG I/17](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-17/)

### The unfinished sociology

- **ca. 1909–1914:** *Prewar manuscript complex for Wirtschaft und Gesellschaft*. Drafts on communities, religion, law, domination, and the city, composed for the *Grundriß der Sozialökonomik* but never integrated by Weber into a finished book; calling this complex a single work is an editorial convenience. [MWG I/22](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-17/)
- **ca. 1910–1914:** *Gemeinschaften*. An unfinished manuscript complex on household, ethnic, market, political, and other forms of community, later incorporated by editors into *Wirtschaft und Gesellschaft*. [MWG I/22-1](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-22-1/)
- **ca. 1911–1913:** *Religiöse Gemeinschaften*. The unfinished sociology-of-religion manuscript traditionally called the religious-sociology chapter of *Wirtschaft und Gesellschaft*. [MWG I/22-2](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-22-2/)
- **ca. 1911–1913:** *Recht*. An unfinished sociology-of-law manuscript; posthumous arrangements and section titles do not always reproduce Weber’s own final architecture. [MWG I/22-3](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-22-3/)
- **ca. 1911–1914:** *Herrschaft*. Manuscripts on domination, bureaucracy, patrimonialism, feudalism, charisma, and political organization; they remained unreconciled with Weber’s later conceptual revision. [MWG I/22-4](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-22-4/)
- **ca. 1911–1914:** *Die Stadt*. An unfinished comparative urban sociology published posthumously; its common title is editorially stabilized from a manuscript not completed as a book. [MWG I/22-5](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-22-5/)
- **1919–1920:** *Wirtschaft und Gesellschaft. Soziologie*. Weber’s late, systematic recasting of his sociology for the *Grundriß*, left unfinished at his death; this is a distinct project from the earlier manuscript complex with which posthumous editions combined it. [MWG I/23](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-23/)
- **1919–1920:** *Soziologische Grundbegriffe*. The completed opening conceptual section of the late sociology, defining sociology and types of social action and order. [MWG I/23](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-23/)
- **1919–1920:** *Die Wirtschaft und die gesellschaftlichen Ordnungen und Mächte*. The intended encompassing project announced within the late sociology; death prevented its completion, so the intention and surviving fragments—not the posthumous synthetic book—are the obra. [MWG I/23](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-23/)
- **1921–1922:** *Wirtschaft und Gesellschaft*, first posthumous edition. This is principally Marianne Weber’s and the publisher’s editorial construction from heterogeneous manuscripts; Max Weber made its components and project, but not this completed book-object in the form in which it appeared. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1922:** *Die drei reinen Typen der legitimen Herrschaft*. A posthumously published editorial extraction from Weber’s unfinished sociology of domination, not a separately issued text prepared by him for publication. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)

### Courses, lecture scripts, and students’ manifestations

- **1894:** *Agrarrecht und Agrargeschichte*. A Berlin lecture course represented by Weber’s notes; the course as performed and the surviving written object are not identical. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1894–1897:** *Allgemeine und theoretische Nationalökonomie*. A repeatedly delivered lecture project in Freiburg and Heidelberg, preserved in Weber’s notes and outlines. [MWG III/1 overview](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/en/)
- **1894–1896:** *Finanzwissenschaft*. A recurring lecture course represented by preparatory notes rather than a finished treatise. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1895–1898:** *Praktische Nationalökonomie / Volkswirtschaftspolitik*. A changing course complex covering population, trade, industry, transport, and agrarian policy. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1895–1899:** *Agrarpolitik*. A recurrent lecture project, known through Weber’s materials and an Else von Richthofen student transcript; the latter is a manifestation of his teaching but not a text written by him. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1895:** *Die deutsche Arbeiterfrage in Stadt und Land*. A course surviving in an unidentified listener’s notes; it resists inclusion as a Weber-authored text even though it witnesses something he made orally. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1895–1896:** *Geld-, Bank- und Börsenwesen / Börsenwesen und Börsenrecht*. Lecture courses on money, banking, exchanges, and exchange law. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1896:** *Geschichte der Nationalökonomie*. A one-hour lecture course preserved in Weber’s materials. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1898:** *Grundriß zu den Vorlesungen über Allgemeine (“theoretische”) Nationalökonomie*. A privately printed course outline whose first book was *Die begrifflichen Grundlagen der Volkswirtschaftslehre*; it is a pedagogical protocol rather than a commercial book. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1898:** *Arbeiterfrage und Arbeiterbewegung*. A Heidelberg lecture course on labour and the workers’ movement. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1918:** *Positive Kritik der materialistischen Geschichtsauffassung*. A Vienna lecture course surviving through student notes; the notes are a mediated manifestation, not a Weber manuscript. [MWG digital](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/en/)
- **1919–1920:** *Abriß der universalen Sozial- und Wirtschaftsgeschichte*. Weber’s Munich course, later published posthumously as *Wirtschaftsgeschichte* or *General Economic History* from student transcripts; the familiar book is not an authorially completed text. [MWG digital](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/en/)
- **1919–1920:** *Staatssoziologie*. A Munich lecture course on the sociology of the state, surviving through notes and reconstructions rather than a final Weber manuscript. [MWG digital](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/en/)

### Editorial, journal, and collective textual labour

- **1904–1920:** *Editorial work for Archiv für Sozialwissenschaft und Sozialpolitik*. With Werner Sombart and Edgar Jaffé, Weber shaped a journal through programme, selection, correspondence, editorial notes, and interventions; this is a sustained institutional obra, not reducible to the articles bearing his name. [MWG digital](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/en/)
- **1904:** *Geleitwort for the Archiv für Sozialwissenschaft und Sozialpolitik*. A jointly authorized editorial programme announcing the journal’s direction; individual wording and collective responsibility cannot be perfectly separated. [MWG I/7](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/de/band-i-7/)
- **1914:** *Redaktionelles Nachwort to Arthur Salz, In eigener Sache*. An editorial intervention made in Weber’s capacity as journal editor. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1914:** *Zu dem redaktionellen Geleitwort im Märzheft 1914 gegen Herrn Prof. Dr. Sander in Prag*. An extended editorial-polemical reply. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)

### Public declarations, petitions, and collective manifestos

- **1905:** *Petition concerning reforms in sexual law*. A text for which Weber shared responsibility and which survives in differing versions; the MWG distinguishes authorization from demonstrable formulation. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1905:** *Aufruf des Bundes Heimatschutz gegen die Zerstörung der Laufenburger Stromschnellen*. A co-signed conservation appeal; Weber endorsed it but is not shown to have composed it. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1906:** *Erklärung gegen die preußische Schulvorlage*. A co-signed public protest whose collective authorization is certain but whose wording is not attributable to Weber. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1910:** *Aufruf zur Begründung eines Verbandes für internationale Verständigung*. A co-signed manifesto for an international-understanding association. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1910:** *Rundschreiben zur Organisation einer Festschrift für Otto von Gierke*. A co-signed organizational circular; Weber was responsible for the initiative without necessarily composing the text. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1915:** *Aufruf des Deutschen Vereins für Sanitätshunde*. A co-signed wartime appeal, not demonstrably written by Weber. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1915:** *Gegenadresse zur sogenannten Seeberg-Adresse*. A co-signed counter-manifesto opposing annexationist war aims; Weber’s endorsement is established, sole formulation is not. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)

### Correspondence and personal documentary production

- **1875–1920:** *Briefe*. Thousands of letters, postcards, telegrams, and drafts form a documentary corpus rather than a single authored “work”; the historically critical edition occupies eleven MWG volumes, and correspondents, lost letters, drafts, and editorial reconstruction make completeness intrinsically provisional. [MWG digital](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/en/)
- **1875–1886:** *Jugendbriefe*. Early family and school correspondence; these are made texts but were not conceived as public works. [MWG digital](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/en/)
- **1887–1894:** *Professional and family correspondence*. Letters documenting legal study, early scholarship, marriage, and the agrarian inquiries; their status is communication first and œuvre only retrospectively. [MWG digital](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/en/)
- **1894–1902:** *Freiburg, Heidelberg, and illness-period correspondence*. A discontinuous corpus whose gaps reflect illness, loss, and selective preservation rather than an intentional finished project. [MWG digital](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/en/)
- **1903–1909:** *Correspondence of scholarly return and editorial work*. Letters integral to the making of the methodological, Protestant-ethic, Russian, and industrial-labour projects. [MWG digital](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/en/)
- **1910–1914:** *Correspondence on sociology, religion, and the Grundriß*. Working communication that partly constitutes the unrealized newspaper inquiry and the architecture of *Wirtschaft und Gesellschaft*. [MWG digital](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/en/)
- **1914–1920:** *Wartime and revolutionary correspondence*. Political, administrative, scholarly, and intimate communication; it supplies evidence for projects but should not be silently converted into a single intentional work. [MWG digital](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/en/)

### Posthumous collections that are not straightforwardly works made by Weber

- **1921:** *Gesammelte Politische Schriften*. A posthumous collection assembled from previously published and unpublished pieces; Weber made the constituent texts, not this final collected-book architecture. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1922:** *Gesammelte Aufsätze zur Wissenschaftslehre*. A posthumous collection whose contents are Weber’s but whose final selection and arrangement are editorial. [MWG bibliography](https://mwg.badw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Files/MWG/08_1_MWG-Bibliographie2002.pdf)
- **1921–1922:** *Gesammelte Aufsätze zur Religionssoziologie*. Weber planned and revised this collection, and its first volume appeared during his lifetime, but later publication and completion involved Marianne Weber and the publisher; it is partly authorial project and partly posthumous manifestation. [MWG digital](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/en/)
- **1923:** *Wirtschaftsgeschichte. Abriß der universalen Sozial- und Wirtschaftsgeschichte*. A posthumous reconstruction from lecture notes, not a book manuscript completed by Weber. [MWG digital](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/en/)
- **1924:** *Gesammelte Aufsätze zur Sozial- und Wirtschaftsgeschichte*. An editorial collection of texts Weber wrote separately, not a unified work he completed under that title. [MWG digital](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/en/)
- **1924:** *Gesammelte Aufsätze zur Soziologie und Sozialpolitik*. A posthumous editorial collection rather than a discrete Weber-authored book. [MWG digital](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/en/)
- **1924:** *Gesammelte Aufsätze zur Religionssoziologie, volume III*. The posthumous book manifestation of *Das antike Judentum* and associated material; Weber made and revised the study but did not see this volume through publication. [MWG digital](https://mwg-digital.badw.de/en/)
