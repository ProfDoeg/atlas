## Works by Carl Gustav Jung

### Psychiatric and experimental studies

- **1902:** *On the Psychology and Pathology of So-Called Occult Phenomena*. Jung’s medical dissertation: a clinical study of mediumistic séances, later placed first in the *Collected Works*. [CW 1](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1902:** *A Case of Hysterical Stupor in a Prisoner in Detention*. A psychiatric case report. [CW 1](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1903:** *On Manic Mood Disorder*. A psychiatric paper. [CW 1](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1903:** *On Simulated Insanity*. A forensic-psychiatric study. [CW 1](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1903:** *On the Psychological Diagnosis of Facts*. A report on experimental methods for detecting emotionally charged knowledge. [CW 1](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1904:** *On Hysterical Misreading*. A short clinical study. [CW 1](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1904:** *A Medical Opinion on a Case of Simulated Insanity*. A forensic opinion rather than a theoretical treatise. [CW 1](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1904–1907, 1910:** *Studies in Word Association*. A collaborative experimental project whose component papers concern normal association, epilepsy, reaction times, memory, dreams, symptoms, reproduction disturbances and family constellations; Franz Riklin co-authored the foundational study of normal subjects. [CW 2](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1905:** *Cryptomnesia*. A study of apparently original ideas that reproduce forgotten material. [CW 1](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1906:** *A Third and Final Opinion on Two Contradictory Psychiatric Diagnoses*. A forensic-psychiatric opinion. [CW 1](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1906:** *Statistical Details of Enlistment*. A small empirical report retained as an appendix to the experimental researches. [CW 2](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1907–1908:** *Psychophysical Researches*. A collaborative experimental project using galvanometers and pneumographs; Frederick Peterson and Charles Ricksher share authorship of two component studies. [CW 2](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1907:** *The Psychology of Dementia Praecox*. A clinical monograph that applied association experiments and the complex theory to psychosis. [CW 3](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1908/1914:** *The Content of the Psychoses*. A lecture-derived essay substantially enlarged in its later version. [CW 3](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1908:** *New Aspects of Criminal Psychology*. A forensic-psychological paper. [CW 2](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1910:** *The Psychological Methods of Investigation Used in the Psychiatric Clinic of the University of Zurich*. A methodological report. [CW 2](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1911/1913:** *On the Doctrine of Complexes*. A lecture-derived formulation of the complex theory. [CW 2](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1911:** *A Criticism of Bleuler’s Theory of Schizophrenic Negativism*. A polemical clinical paper. [CW 3](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1914:** *On Psychological Understanding*. An essay distinguishing causal explanation from psychological understanding. [CW 3](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1914:** *On the Importance of the Unconscious in Psychopathology*. A clinical-theoretical essay. [CW 3](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1919:** *On the Problem of Psychogenesis in Mental Disease*. A psychiatric argument for psychological causation. [CW 3](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1928:** *Mental Disease and the Psyche*. A concise statement on psychological factors in mental illness. [CW 3](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1937:** *On the Psychological Diagnosis of Evidence*. A late reconsideration of forensic association testing. [CW 2](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1939:** *On the Psychogenesis of Schizophrenia*. A clinical-theoretical essay. [CW 3](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1957:** *Recent Thoughts on Schizophrenia*. A late reformulation of Jung’s position. [CW 3](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1958:** *Schizophrenia*. Jung’s final substantial statement on the disorder. [CW 3](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)

### Psychoanalysis, analytical psychology and psychological types

- **1906:** *Freud’s Theory of Hysteria: A Reply to Aschaffenburg*. A public defence of Freud’s work. [CW 4](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1908:** *The Freudian Theory of Hysteria*. An expository and critical essay. [CW 4](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1909:** *The Analysis of Dreams*. An early technical paper. [CW 4](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1909/1949:** *The Significance of the Father in the Destiny of the Individual*. A repeatedly revised psychoanalytic essay. [CW 4](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1910–1911:** *A Contribution to the Psychology of Rumour*. An empirical-interpretive study. [CW 4](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1910–1911:** *On the Significance of Number Dreams*. A short psychoanalytic study. [CW 4](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1910:** *On the Criticism of Psychoanalysis*. A defence qualified by Jung’s growing theoretical independence. [CW 4](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1911:** *The Mechanism and Interpretation of Dreams: A Critical Review*. A review of Morton Prince, not a freestanding book. [CW 4](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1911–1912/1952:** *Transformations and Symbols of the Libido* / *Symbols of Transformation*. A major monograph radically rewritten in 1952; the revision is not merely a new edition, and the two states should remain distinguishable. [CW 5 and Supplement B](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1912:** *Concerning Psychoanalysis*. A lecture-derived exposition. [CW 4](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1912:** *New Paths in Psychology*. An essay later reworked into Jung’s account of analytical psychology. [CW 7](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1912:** *The Theory of Psychoanalysis: New York Lectures*. A lecture cycle subsequently published and revised in 1913; it marks Jung’s divergence from Freud. [Philemon Foundation](https://philemonfoundation.org/works/)
- **1913:** *General Aspects of Psychoanalysis*. A lecture-derived essay. [CW 4](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1914:** *Some Crucial Points in Psychoanalysis*. A published correspondence with Loy rather than a singly authored treatise. [CW 4](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1916:** *The Structure of the Unconscious*. A transitional essay later absorbed into *The Relations between the Ego and the Unconscious*. [CW 7](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1916/1957:** *The Transcendent Function*. A theoretical manuscript written in 1916 but first published, with revisions, in 1957. [CW 8](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1917/1926/1943:** *On the Psychology of the Unconscious*. A repeatedly rewritten book-length essay; its successive versions materially alter Jung’s account of instincts, types and individuation. [CW 7](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1919:** *The Significance of Constitution and Heredity in Psychology*. A theoretical paper. [CW 8](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1919:** *Instinct and the Unconscious*. A theoretical essay linking instinct and archetype. [CW 8](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1921:** *Psychological Types*. A systematic monograph combining intellectual history, literary criticism, clinical typology and a glossary of concepts. [CW 6](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1927/1931:** *The Structure of the Psyche*. A revised theoretical essay. [CW 8](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1928:** *On Psychic Energy*. A theoretical essay on libido understood as general psychic energy. [CW 8](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1928:** *The Relations between the Ego and the Unconscious*. A book-length account of persona, anima/animus and individuation, developed from the 1916 essay. [CW 7](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1929:** *Freud and Jung: Contrasts*. A retrospective theoretical comparison. [CW 4](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1934:** *A Review of the Complex Theory*. A mature synthesis of Jung’s experimental and clinical concept of complexes. [CW 8](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1937:** *Psychological Factors Determining Human Behaviour*. A concise theoretical survey. [CW 8](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1947/1954:** *On the Nature of the Psyche*. A major theoretical essay, revised after its initial publication. [CW 8](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)

### Dreams, archetypes and individuation

- **1916/1948:** *General Aspects of Dream Psychology*. A substantially revised theory of dreams. [CW 8](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1934/1954:** *Archetypes of the Collective Unconscious*. A revised theoretical essay. [CW 9.i](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1934/1950:** *A Study in the Process of Individuation*. A visual and clinical case study built around a patient’s paintings. [CW 9.i](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1936:** *The Concept of the Collective Unconscious*. A lecture-derived definition and defence of the concept. [CW 9.i](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1936/1954:** *Concerning the Archetypes, with Special Reference to the Anima Concept*. A revised theoretical essay. [CW 9.i](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1938/1954:** *Psychological Aspects of the Mother Archetype*. A revised archetypal study. [CW 9.i](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1939:** *Conscious, Unconscious, and Individuation*. A theoretical essay. [CW 9.i](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1940/1950:** *Concerning Rebirth*. A revised lecture-derived typology of rebirth experiences. [CW 9.i](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1940:** *The Psychology of the Child Archetype*. A contribution to Jung and Karl Kerényi’s collaborative volume on mythology. [CW 9.i](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1941:** *The Psychological Aspects of the Kore*. Another contribution to the Jung–Kerényi collaboration. [CW 9.i](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1945/1948:** *On the Nature of Dreams*. A concise revised statement of dream theory. [CW 8](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1945/1948:** *The Phenomenology of the Spirit in Fairytales*. A revised archetypal study. [CW 9.i](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1950:** *Concerning Mandala Symbolism*. An interpretive essay accompanied by visual examples. [CW 9.i](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1951:** *Aion: Researches into the Phenomenology of the Self*. A composite book whose chapters treat ego, shadow, anima/animus, Christ, fish symbolism, Gnosticism and the self. [CW 9.ii](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1954:** *On the Psychology of the Trickster-Figure*. A contribution to Paul Radin’s *The Trickster*, not an independent monograph. [CW 9.i](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1955:** *Mandalas*. A pictorial appendix: a curated visual corpus rather than a continuous text. [CW 9.i](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)

### Religion, Gnosticism and Eastern thought

- **1916:** *Septem Sermones ad Mortuos* (*Seven Sermons to the Dead*). A privately printed pseudonymous manifesto or visionary scripture attributed within the work to “Basilides”; its status deliberately resists ordinary authorship. [Library of Congress](https://www.loc.gov/exhibits/red-book-of-carl-jung/the-red-book-and-beyond)
- **1928:** *Psychoanalysis and the Cure of Souls*. An essay addressing psychotherapy and pastoral practice. [CW 11](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1932:** *Psychotherapists or the Clergy*. A lecture-derived essay on the religious dimension of neurosis. [CW 11](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1933:** *Brother Klaus*. A psychological interpretation of Nicholas of Flüe. [CW 11](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1935/1953:** *Psychological Commentary on The Tibetan Book of the Dead*. A framing commentary to another culture’s translated text, not authorship of that text. [CW 11](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1936:** *Yoga and the West*. An essay on cultural translation and the risks of imitation. [CW 11](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1938/1940:** *Psychology and Religion*. The Terry Lectures recast as a book. [CW 11](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1939/1954:** *Psychological Commentary on The Tibetan Book of the Great Liberation*. A revised commentary, not authorship of the underlying Tibetan work. [CW 11](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1939:** *Foreword to Suzuki’s Introduction to Zen Buddhism*. A substantial interpretive foreword. [CW 11](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1942/1948:** *A Psychological Approach to the Dogma of the Trinity*. A revised religious-psychological essay. [CW 11](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1942/1954:** *Transformation Symbolism in the Mass*. A revised interpretive study of the liturgy. [CW 11](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1943:** *The Psychology of Eastern Meditation*. A lecture-derived study. [CW 11](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1944:** *The Holy Men of India*. An introduction to Heinrich Zimmer’s work, substantial enough to enter Jung’s collected writings but still a paratext. [CW 11](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1950:** *Foreword to the I Ching*. An influential interpretive foreword organized around synchronicity; Jung did not create or translate the underlying Chinese classic. [CW 11](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1952:** *Answer to Job*. A book-length theological-psychological confrontation written as an intensely personal argument rather than neutral exegesis. [CW 11](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)

### Alchemical studies

- **1929:** *Commentary on The Secret of the Golden Flower*. A psychological commentary created for Richard Wilhelm’s translation, not authorship of the Chinese work. [CW 13](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1936:** *Individual Dream Symbolism in Relation to Alchemy*. An extended dream-series study later incorporated into *Psychology and Alchemy*. [CW 12](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1937:** *Religious Ideas in Alchemy*. A major study later incorporated into *Psychology and Alchemy*. [CW 12](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1937:** *Unpublished Book on Alchemy and Individuation*. An unfinished book project surviving in manuscript; its incompletion is part of the obra, and the Philemon Foundation is preparing a scholarly edition. [Philemon Foundation](https://philemonfoundation.org/current-projects/)
- **1938/1954:** *The Visions of Zosimos*. A revised interpretation of an alchemical vision text. [CW 13](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1942:** *Paracelsus as a Spiritual Phenomenon*. A psychological and intellectual portrait. [CW 13](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1943/1948:** *The Spirit Mercurius*. A lecture transformed into a revised essay. [CW 13](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1944:** *Psychology and Alchemy*. A composite monograph joining revised studies of dreams, religion and alchemical symbolism. [CW 12](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1945/1954:** *The Philosophical Tree*. A revised study combining historical images with clinical material. [CW 13](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1955–1956:** *Mysterium Coniunctionis*. Jung’s final major monograph: an inquiry into alchemical images of separation and union. Its third part, Marie-Louise von Franz’s edition of the *Aurora Consurgens*, is a separate scholarly contribution and not Jung’s sole work. [CW 14](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)

- **1951:** *Fundamental Questions of Psychotherapy*. A late clinical synthesis. [CW 16](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)

### Culture, politics, art and literature

- **1918:** *The Role of the Unconscious*. An essay on collective psychology written in the aftermath of the First World War. [CW 10](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1922:** *On the Relation of Analytical Psychology to Poetry*. A theory of artistic creation that distinguishes the maker’s psychology from the autonomous work. [CW 15](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1927:** *Woman in Europe*. A historically situated cultural essay whose generalizations are contested. [CW 10](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1927/1931:** *Mind and Earth*. A revised essay connecting psychology, geography and collective inheritance. [CW 10](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1928/1931:** *The Spiritual Problem of Modern Man*. A revised diagnosis of modernity. [CW 10](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1928:** *The Love Problem of a Student*. An occasional address. [CW 10](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1928:** *The Swiss Line in the European Spectrum*. A national-character essay whose typological claims are historically situated. [CW 10](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1929:** *Paracelsus*. A commemorative address. [CW 15](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1930/1950:** *Psychology and Literature*. A revised theory of literary production and reception. [CW 15](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1930:** *Richard Wilhelm: In Memoriam*. A memorial address and intellectual portrait. [CW 15](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1930:** *The Complications of American Psychology*. A culturally specific and contested national-psychological essay. [CW 10](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1931:** *Archaic Man*. An anthropological-psychological essay whose terminology and generalizations belong to its period. [CW 10](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1932:** *Ulysses: A Monologue*. An experimental critical response to James Joyce, deliberately closer to a subjective monologue than conventional criticism. [CW 15](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1932:** *Picasso*. An exhibition essay interpreting Picasso’s work psychologically; it was controversial for appearing to diagnose images and artist together. [CW 15](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1932:** *Sigmund Freud in His Historical Setting*. A critical intellectual portrait. [CW 15](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1933/1934:** *The Meaning of Psychology for Modern Man*. A revised cultural essay. [CW 10](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1934:** *The State of Psychotherapy Today*. A professional and political position paper; its discussion of “Jewish” and “Aryan” psychologies is central to continuing disputes over Jung’s conduct in the 1930s. [CW 10](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1936:** *Wotan*. A mythopolitical interpretation of National Socialism, not a political manifesto and not an exculpation of Jung’s disputed institutional role. [CW 10](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1939:** *In Memory of Sigmund Freud*. A memorial essay written after Freud’s death. [CW 15](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1939:** *The Dreamlike World of India*. A travel-derived cultural essay. [CW 10](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1939:** *What India Can Teach Us*. A cultural and comparative-psychological essay. [CW 10](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1941:** *Paracelsus the Physician*. A commemorative address and historical portrait. [CW 15](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1945:** *After the Catastrophe*. A disputed postwar essay on German collective responsibility. [CW 10](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1946:** *The Fight with the Shadow*. A radio-derived political-psychological essay. [CW 10](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1946:** *Essays on Contemporary Events: Preface and Epilogue*. Framing texts that attempted to situate Jung’s politically contentious writings of the 1930s and 1940s. [CW 10](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1957:** *The Undiscovered Self (Present and Future)*. A short political-psychological book opposing mass society and ideological possession. [CW 10](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1958:** *Flying Saucers: A Modern Myth of Things Seen in the Skies*. A book treating UFO reports chiefly as modern myth and psychic manifestation while declining to settle their physical status. [CW 10](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1958:** *A Psychological View of Conscience*. A lecture-derived essay. [CW 10](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1959:** *Good and Evil in Analytical Psychology*. A late ethical-psychological address. [CW 10](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)

### Synchronicity and interdisciplinary collaborations

- **1952:** *Synchronicity: An Acausal Connecting Principle*. A monograph first issued with Wolfgang Pauli’s separate essay in *The Interpretation of Nature and the Psyche*; the volume is collaborative, but the synchronicity essay is Jung’s. [CW 8](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1955:** *Synchronicity: An Acausal Connecting Principle—Appendix*. Supplementary astrological statistical material whose methods and conclusions have remained disputed. [CW 8](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)

### Lectures and seminars: spoken works surviving through notes

- **1896:** *The Border Zones of Exact Science*. A Zofingia student-society lecture, published posthumously. [CW Supplement A](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1897:** *Some Thoughts on Psychology*. A Zofingia lecture, published posthumously. [CW Supplement A](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1897–1898:** *Inaugural Address upon Assuming the Chairmanship of the Zofingia Club*. An occasional manifesto-like address. [CW Supplement A](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1898:** *Thoughts on the Nature and Value of Speculative Inquiry*. A Zofingia lecture. [CW Supplement A](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1899:** *Thoughts on the Interpretation of Christianity, with Reference to the Theory of Albrecht Ritschl*. A Zofingia lecture. [CW Supplement A](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1912:** *New York Lectures on the Theory of Psychoanalysis*. A spoken cycle whose surviving textual states include reports and Jung’s subsequently published revision. [Philemon Foundation](https://philemonfoundation.org/works/)
- **1923:** *Polzeath Seminars on the Technique of Analysis and the Historical and Psychological Effects of Christianity*. Unpublished lecture projects known through participants’ notes; because Jung did not prepare a final text, their textual identity remains editorially mediated. [Philemon Foundation](https://philemonfoundation.org/current-projects/)
- **1925:** *Seminar on Analytical Psychology*. An autobiographical and theoretical seminar preserved in notes; it is not a book manuscript authored line by line by Jung. [CW 19](https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctt5hhqwh)
- **1925:** *Swanage Seminar*. A lecture series surviving in notes and still undergoing scholarly preparation. [Philemon Foundation](https://philemonfoundation.org/current-projects/)
- **1927:** *Zurich Seminar*. A lecture series surviving in notes and still undergoing scholarly preparation. [Philemon Foundation](https://philemonfoundation.org/current-projects/)
- **1928–1930:** *Dream Analysis Seminar*. A sustained spoken interpretation of dreams, reconstructed from seminar notes; auditors and editors helped make the surviving textual object. [CW 19](https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctt5hhqwh)
- **1930–1934:** *Visions Seminar*. A long, image-centred seminar surviving through audited notes rather than an authorial manuscript. [CW 19](https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctt5hhqwh)
- **1931:** *German Seminar on Active Imagination*. A predominantly clinical seminar surviving in notes; its scholarly edition remains a project. [Philemon Foundation](https://philemonfoundation.org/current-projects/)
- **1932:** *The Psychology of Kundalini Yoga*. A seminar on the chakra system, textually dependent on participants’ notes and later editors. [CW 19](https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctt5hhqwh)
- **1933:** *Berlin Seminar on Dreams and the East*. A joint intellectual event involving Heinrich Zimmer; the published text derives from notes, so single authorship would be misleading. [Philemon Foundation](https://philemonfoundation.org/works/)
- **1933–1941:** *ETH Lectures*. An eight-course pedagogical project encompassing modern psychology, consciousness and the unconscious, dreams, typology, yoga and meditation, Ignatius’s spiritual exercises and alchemy; several courses have appeared in critical editions and others remain editorial projects. [Philemon Foundation](https://philemonfoundation.org/works/)
- **1934–1939:** *Nietzsche’s Zarathustra Seminar*. A multi-year spoken commentary preserved by stenographic and participant records. [CW 19](https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctt5hhqwh)
- **1935:** *Tavistock Lectures*. Five public lectures presenting analytical psychology; the printed work is a transcript of speech and discussion. [CW 18](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1936–1940:** *Children’s Dreams Seminar*. A recurring seminar reconstructed from notes; its German and English editions are posthumous editorial manifestations. [Philemon Foundation](https://philemonfoundation.org/works/)
- **1936–1941:** *Dream Interpretation Ancient and Modern*. Seminar notes on dream interpretation, including the Pausanias and alchemical material; this is an edited record of teaching, not a finished authorial book. [Philemon Foundation](https://philemonfoundation.org/works/)
- **1941:** *Dream Symbols of the Individuation Process*. Lecture notes later related to Jung’s alchemical publications; their survival as a text depends on auditors and editors. [Philemon Foundation](https://philemonfoundation.org/works/)
- **1941:** *On Psychological and Visionary Art: Lecture on Gérard de Nerval’s Aurélia*. A lecture surviving in notes, hence a collaborative textual residue rather than a fully controlled publication. [Philemon Foundation](https://philemonfoundation.org/works/)

### Journals, illuminated books, fiction and autobiographical constructions

- **Childhood, date uncertain:** *Carved wooden figure, painted stone and pencil-box shrine*. A private assemblage made from a ruler-end, a clothed figurine, a painted Rhine stone and a pencil case. It resists the category “work”: Jung treated it as a secret ritual object, and knowledge of it comes from later autobiographical narration. [Architectural study](https://www.collectionscanada.gc.ca/obj/thesescanada/vol2/OWTU/TC-OWTU-3837.pdf)
- **1913–1932:** *The Black Books*. Seven private working notebooks recording fantasies, dreams, dialogues, drawings and experiments in active imagination; they are both documentary notebooks and the generative matrix of *Liber Novus*. [Philemon Foundation](https://philemonfoundation.org/works/black-books/)
- **1914–c.1930:** *Liber Novus (The Red Book)*. A calligraphic, painted manuscript combining visionary narrative, theology, psychology and image: a genuine Gesamtkunstwerk rather than merely a “book.” Jung left it unfinished and unpublished; its 2009 edition distinguishes drafted, calligraphed and omitted materials. [Library of Congress](https://www.loc.gov/exhibits/red-book-of-carl-jung/the-red-book-and-beyond)
- **Date uncertain:** *The A.E. Letters*. An unpublished epistolary novella or fictional letter-sequence; its status as fiction, private experiment and unfinished project is precisely what makes it resist classification. [Philemon Foundation](https://philemonfoundation.org/current-projects/)
- **1957–1961; published 1961–1963:** *Memories, Dreams, Reflections*. An autobiographical construction assembled by Aniela Jaffé from Jung’s interviews, manuscripts and her editorial narration. It should not be treated as an uncontested, wholly Jung-authored autobiography; different language editions embody materially different editorial decisions. [Philemon Foundation](https://philemonfoundation.org/works/)
- **1961; published 1964:** *Approaching the Unconscious*. Jung’s final essay, written for the collaborative illustrated volume *Man and His Symbols*; the remaining chapters are by Joseph Henderson, Marie-Louise von Franz, Aniela Jaffé and Jolande Jacobi, so the whole book is not solely his work. [CW bibliography](https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctt5hhqwh)

### Correspondence and dialogical works

- **1906–1913:** *The Freud–Jung Letters*. A jointly made correspondence corpus; individual letters are works of occasion, while the published sequence is an editorial construction. [CW 19](https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctt5hhqwh)
- **1915–1918:** *The Question of Psychological Types: Correspondence with Hans Schmid-Guisan*. A dialogical theoretical project that contributed to *Psychological Types*; neither correspondent alone made the exchange. [Philemon Foundation](https://philemonfoundation.org/works/)
- **1909–1961:** *Letters*. A vast corpus of occasional texts to patients, colleagues, friends and public correspondents. The standard published selections are incomplete editorial constructions, and the Philemon Foundation estimates the unpublished correspondence at as many as 35,000 letters; consequently no printed “Letters” edition is the whole obra. [Philemon Foundation](https://philemonfoundation.org/about-philemon/what-is-the-philemon-series/)
- **1933–1959:** *Analytical Psychology in Exile: Correspondence with Erich Neumann*. A jointly made intellectual correspondence, posthumously selected and edited. [Philemon Foundation](https://philemonfoundation.org/works/)
- **1934–1960:** *The Jung–White Letters*. A correspondence with Dominican theologian Victor White concerning evil, theology and psychology; it is dialogical and editorially reconstructed. [Philemon Foundation](https://philemonfoundation.org/works/)
- **Dates various:** *Correspondence with Adolf Keller*. A theological-psychological dialogue whose published form includes contextual editorial work and cannot be assigned to Jung alone. [Philemon Foundation](https://philemonfoundation.org/works/)
- **Dates various:** *Correspondence with Wilhelm Hauer, Heinrich Zimmer and Mircea Eliade*. Three cross-cultural exchanges presently treated as an editorial project rather than a finished Jungian book. [Philemon Foundation](https://philemonfoundation.org/current-projects/)

### Architecture, sculpture and visual obra

- **1906–1909:** *House and garden at Küsnacht*. A domestic architectural project commissioned from Ernst Robert Fiechter with garden architects Walter and Oskar Mertens; Jung played a decisive design role, but sole architectural authorship would be false. [C. G. Jung House Museum account](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C._G._Jung_House_Museum)
- **1923–1955:** *The Tower at Bollingen*. An evolving stone dwelling designed and partly built as a material representation of Jung’s psyche, enlarged in 1927, 1931, 1935 and 1955. Architecture, retreat, sculpture and lived ritual converge here, making it a Gesamtkunstwerk rather than a single static building. [Architectural record](https://openbibart.fr/vibad/index.php?action=getRecordDetail&idt=oba_0108206&lang=en)
- **1920s–1950s, exact dates partly uncertain:** *Reliefs, murals, inscriptions and carved stones at Bollingen*. A site-specific body of visual and sculptural work inseparable from the Tower; not all pieces have independent fixed titles or secure dates, so forcing them into a catalogue of autonomous artworks would misdescribe them. [Library of Congress](https://www.loc.gov/exhibits/red-book-of-carl-jung/the-red-book-and-beyond)
- **1950:** *Bollingen Stone*. A carved cubic monument made from a rejected building stone for Jung’s seventy-fifth birthday, bearing images and inscriptions on several faces. It is simultaneously sculpture, monument, symbolic text and architectural component. [Bollingen Tower record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bollingen_Tower)
- **c.1902–1961, dates often uncertain:** *Paintings, drawings, mandalas and carvings*. A dispersed visual corpus extending from psychiatric diagrams and private fantasies to calligraphic illuminations and stone reliefs. Much was not exhibited or titled as autonomous “art,” and no completed catalogue raisonné presently warrants inventing individual titles or dates. [Library of Congress](https://www.loc.gov/exhibits/red-book-of-carl-jung/the-red-book-and-beyond)

### Editorially assembled bodies of work

- **1953–1979; revised bibliography 1990:** *The Collected Works of C. G. Jung*. A twenty-volume scholarly edition edited by Herbert Read, Michael Fordham and Gerhard Adler, chiefly translated by R. F. C. Hull. It is the standard map of Jung’s published writings, not itself a single work made by Jung; its thematic rearrangement also obscures original chronology. [CW table of contents](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1966:** *The Symbolic Life: Miscellaneous Writings*. An editorially assembled volume of speeches, reports, forewords, reviews, letters, interviews and short papers. Its contents are genuine Jung materials, but the volume is not an author-conceived book and some component texts are collaboratively recorded speech. [CW 18](https://www.liquidfractal.org/projects/cdjt/jung_cw_toc/)
- **1973–2009 and continuing:** *Seminar editions*. Posthumous books made from shorthand records, mimeographed notes and participant transcripts. They manifest Jung’s teaching but resist simple attribution because auditors, translators and editors materially constitute every surviving text. [CW 19 seminar catalogue](https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctt5hhqwh)
- **2009 and continuing:** *Philemon Series / Complete Works project*. A scholarly publication project recovering manuscripts, seminars and correspondence omitted from the older *Collected Works*. It is not complete: the Foundation reports tens of thousands of unpublished manuscript and correspondence pages, so any claim to list literally every surviving Jung item remains provisional. [Philemon Foundation](https://philemonfoundation.org/about-the-foundation/)
