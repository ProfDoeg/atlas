## Works by Marta María Ezcurra Real de Azúa

### Doctrinal manuals and books

- **1937:** *Primeras nociones de doctrina social católica*. A didactic manual assembled from articles Ezcurra had previously published in *Anhelos*; contemporary scholarship identifies the 1937 volume as its first edition, while surviving records variously label later printings “fourth edition” in 1954 and 1965. It is therefore one evolving obra rather than three separate books. ([Omar Acha, “Activismo y sociabilidad en las jóvenes de la Acción Católica”](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/libreria_cm_archivos/pdf_1385.pdf); [bibliographic record for the 1965 edition](https://profesoradodonbosco.opac.com.ar/pergamo/opac.php?a=bsqMateria&n=DOCTRINA+SOCIAL+DE+LA+IGLESIA))

- **By 1950:** *Principios básicos de la doctrina social católica*. A systematic doctrinal handbook arranged around economic and social subjects. The documented 1950 printing calls itself the third edition, so neither that year nor the surviving title can safely be treated as the work’s original date or form; scholarship and contemporary book lists do not establish whether it is wholly independent of *Primeras nociones* or a substantially recast manifestation of it. ([CEDINPE–UNSAM record and contents](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/ezcurra-marta-principios-basicos-de-la-doctrina-social-catolica); [Universidade de Caxias do Sul catalogue of the Laudelino Teixeira de Medeiros collection](https://www.ucs.br/site/midia/arquivos/colecao-especial-laudelino-teixeira-de-medeiros.pdf))

- **1982:** *Manual de doctrina social de la Iglesia*. A 255-page instructional book issued by the Comisión Católica Argentina de la Campaña Mundial contra el Hambre. The consulted records do not say whether this is a new obra, a revision of either earlier doctrinal manual, or a newly titled manifestation, so its independence must remain unresolved. ([Biblioteca Central Salesiana record](https://profesoradodonbosco.opac.com.ar/pergamo/documento.php?id=PROFESORADODONBOSCO.1.16204&recno=16204&ui=1))

### Articles, conference papers, and reports

- **1941:** *Acción social femenina*. An article in the April 1941 issue of *Anhelos*, treating women’s social action within Ezcurra’s Catholic and gendered conception of social work. It is a periodical intervention, not a book. ([Omar Acha, “Catolicismo social y feminidad en la década de 1930,” in *Cuerpos, géneros e identidades*](https://es.scribd.com/document/970460763/Cuerpos-Generos-E-Identidades-Bodies-Genders-and-Paula-Halperin-y-Omar-Acha-Comp-Presentacion-de-Dora-Coleccion-Situaciones-3-978))

- **1942; published 1944:** *La asistencia del niño en la familia*. A conference paper presented to the Second National Conference on Abandoned and Delinquent Children and printed in its proceedings, pp. 91–98. It reports a study of 330 families and functions simultaneously as research report and professional-policy argument. ([Claudia Freidenraij, study citing the paper and proceedings](https://static.ides.org.ar/archivo/freidenraij-_claudia.pdf); [specialist history of social-work training](https://cdsa.aacademica.org/000-008/140.pdf))

- **1969:** Untitled report on the Fourth Latin American Seminar of Social Service in Concepción. This was an internal UCISS report or communiqué rather than a conventionally published work; Ezcurra characterized ECRO and allied Chilean and Uruguayan professionals as organized communist fronts, prompting a public professional controversy. Its original title, circulation, and completeness have not been established, so it resists treatment as a fixed bibliographical work. ([UNLP history of ECRO and the debate](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/192996/Documento_completo.pdf-PDFA.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1); [Norberto Alayón’s historical account](https://trabajosocial.usac.edu.gt/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Libro-version-final-publicada-en-la-pagina-de-Escuela-Dr.-Normberto-Alayon.pdf); [account preserving extracts and describing it as a report](https://www.coopmargen.ar/wp/?p=1565))

### Institutional obra and collective projects

- **1931–1932:** Association of the Young Women of Argentine Catholic Action and its first institutional report. Ezcurra organized the new female youth branch at episcopal request and presided over its provisional superior council; in 1932 she carried its first report to Rome in a white-leather binding. The association and report were collective and ecclesiastically commissioned creations, not Ezcurra’s sole-authored works, and the report’s fixed title is not documented. ([Argentine Catholic Action historical chapter](https://www.accioncatolica.org.ar/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Capitulo-2.pdf); [historical reconstruction of Ezcurra’s role and the report](https://www.coopmargen.ar/wp/?p=1565))

- **1936:** Christian seamstresses’ union. Ezcurra organized this trade association under Catholic Action and served as its technical adviser. It is an institutional and social project rather than a text, and the available scholarship does not credit her as its sole creator. ([specialist study of social-work formation and juvenile assistance](https://cdsa.aacademica.org/000-008/140.pdf))

- **1940:** Catholic School of Social Assistance at the Instituto de Cultura Religiosa Superior Femenina. A women’s professional-training institution created collectively within Catholic Action and directed technically by Ezcurra; scholarship differs between describing her as its founding director and simply placing the new school under her direction, so sole authorship should not be assigned. ([Mariana Elisabet Funkner, history of Catholic women’s social action](https://www.teseopress.com/debatesredhisel3/chapter/mariana-elisabet-funkner/); [Andrea Oliva, *Trabajo Social y lucha de clases*](https://tede2.pucsp.br/bitstream/handle/17834/1/ANDREA%20ANTONIA%20OLIVA.pdf))

- **1943:** Asociación para la Lucha contra la Parálisis Infantil (ALPI). A collaborative medical-social institution whose founding meetings established an organization for treating and assisting children affected by poliomyelitis; Ezcurra and surgeon Marcelo Fitte formed its early governing partnership, and she alternated as president and vice-president through 1950. This is a collective institutional obra, not something attributable to Ezcurra alone. ([Daniela Testa, “La lucha contra la poliomielitis”](https://scielosp.org/article/ssm/content/raw/?resource_ssm_path=%2Fmedia%2Fassets%2Fscol%2Fv8n3%2Fv8n3a07.pdf))

- **1961:** Proposal to observe 2 July as Argentina’s Day of the Social Worker. A calendrical-symbolic project aligning the profession with the Catholic feast of the Visitation; it became an operative professional convention rather than a freestanding literary text and was formally replaced in 2012. ([history and text of the Argentine federation’s 2012 resolution](https://www.coopmargen.ar/wp/?p=1565); [scholarly discussion in *Abordajes*](https://ri.conicet.gov.ar/bitstream/handle/11336/8667/7580-38663-1-PB.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=2))
