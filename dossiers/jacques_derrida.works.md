## Works by Jacques Derrida

### Obra: books, book-length essays, and constructed textual objects

- **1953–1954:** *Le problème de la genèse dans la philosophie de Husserl*. Diploma thesis, first published in 1990; its composition date and publication date therefore differ sharply. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1962:** *Introduction et traduction de “L’Origine de la géométrie” de Husserl*. Derrida’s translation of Husserl accompanied by an extensive introduction: simultaneously translation, commentary, and first book publication. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1967:** *De la grammatologie*. Philosophical monograph incorporating and enlarging work first published serially in *Critique*. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1967:** *L’écriture et la différence*. Constructed collection of essays and lectures originating between 1959 and 1967, rather than a single continuously composed treatise. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.idixa.net/Pixa/pagixa-0802190834.html)
- **1967:** *La voix et le phénomène: Introduction au problème du signe dans la phénoménologie de Husserl*. Philosophical monograph on voice, signification, and phenomenology. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1972:** *Marges—de la philosophie*. Essay-collection whose typographic margins and paratexts are part of the philosophical construction; its constituent texts were produced at different dates. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1972:** *Positions*. A book made from interviews; dialogical testimony rather than a conventional authored treatise. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1972:** *La Dissémination*. Composite book containing earlier essays, including “La pharmacie de Platon,” “La double séance,” and “La dissémination”; its prefaces and arrangement make the collection a new object without erasing those prior textual events. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.idixa.net/Pixa/pagixa-0802190834.html)
- **1973:** *L’archéologie du frivole: Lire Condillac*. Essay originally serving as an introduction to Condillac’s *Essai sur l’origine des connaissances humaines*, later detached and republished as Derrida’s own book. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1974:** *Glas*. Two-column typographic construction reading Hegel beside Genet; it resists both linear reading and the separation of philosophy, literary criticism, and visual page-design, and is accordingly a textual Gesamtkunstwerk. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1976:** *Le livre d’Élie*. Unpublished notebook or carnet, known partly through excerpts incorporated into *Circonfession*; because the complete object was not published, its status as a finished work is uncertain. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1978:** *Éperons: Les styles de Nietzsche*. Book developed from a conference and produced in multilingual and typographically conspicuous forms; not simply a transcript of the spoken event. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1978:** *La vérité en peinture*. Composite book on visual art and framing, assembling four related but formally distinct textual performances. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1980:** *La carte postale: De Socrate à Freud et au-delà*. Hybrid obra comprising pseudo-correspondence, philosophical essays, autobiography, and typographic interruptions; it deliberately resists classification as either fiction or treatise. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1983:** *D’un ton apocalyptique adopté naguère en philosophie*. Book-length lecture or performative essay on philosophical tone, first delivered orally. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1984:** *Otobiographies: L’enseignement de Nietzsche et la politique du nom propre*. Book fashioned from a lecture on Nietzsche, institutional teaching, signature, and the proper name. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1986:** *Schibboleth: Pour Paul Celan*. Book-length reading of Celan concerned with date, repetition, and poetic singularity. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1986; enlarged 2003:** *Parages*. Collection of writings around Maurice Blanchot; the 2003 form replaces rather than merely reprints the smaller 1986 configuration. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1987:** *Ulysse gramophone: Deux mots pour Joyce*. Two-part Joyce book made from lectures and concerned with the event of saying “yes.” [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1987:** *De l’esprit: Heidegger et la question*. Book made from a conference delivered on 14 March 1987. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1987; revised 1998 and 2003:** *Psyché: Inventions de l’autre*. An expanding archive of essays rather than a stable single work: the 1987 collection became a revised first volume in 1998 and acquired a second volume in 2003. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1987:** *Feu la cendre*. Polyphonic text developed from an earlier spoken recording and printed with facing textual voices; part book, part script, part memorial trace. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1988:** *Mémoires: Pour Paul de Man*. Memorial lectures reworked into a book; both philosophical argument and act of mourning. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1988:** *Signéponge*. Book on Francis Ponge and signature, first manifested as a bilingual American volume in 1984 before this French publication. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1990:** *Limited Inc*. Polemical and dialogical assemblage concerning speech-act theory; its English and French configurations do not contain precisely the same textual history, so it is not a simple one-to-one edition. [Jacques Derrida bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacques_Derrida_bibliography)
- **1990:** *Du droit à la philosophie*. Large collection of writings on philosophical education and institutions, not a single newly composed monograph. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1990:** *Mémoires d’aveugle: L’autoportrait et autres ruines*. Exhibition catalogue and philosophical essay created for Derrida’s Louvre exhibition; the book and exhibition are related manifestations, not interchangeable objects. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1991:** *Donner le temps, 1: La fausse monnaie*. Reworked publication of part of the 1978–1979 seminar; its projected numbering remained visibly incomplete during Derrida’s lifetime. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1991:** *L’autre cap*. Book joining an essay on European identity with a shorter intervention on public opinion. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1991:** *Circonfession*. Autobiographical text printed as fifty-nine circumfessions beneath Geoffrey Bennington’s systematic exposition in *Jacques Derrida*; simultaneously collaboration, countertext, page architecture, and resistance to biography. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1992:** *Points de suspension: Entretiens*. Curated collection of interviews: a retrospective dialogical corpus rather than one continuous work. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1987; book manifestation 1993:** *Khôra*. Essay first published in an earlier form and subsequently made into a separate book. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1993:** *Passions*. Independent essay-book, issued as one element of a related triad with *Khôra* and *Sauf le nom*. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1993:** *Sauf le nom*. Dialogical essay-book on negative theology and naming, paired editorially with *Passions* and *Khôra*. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1993:** *Spectres de Marx*. Book made from plenary lectures, combining philosophical argument, political intervention, and an explicit call for a “New International”; partly manifesto without becoming a programmatic party manifesto. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1994:** *Politiques de l’amitié, suivi de L’oreille de Heidegger*. Book developed from seminar work and accompanied by a separate essay; both philosophical obra and intervention in political language. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1994:** *Force de loi: Le “fondement mystique de l’autorité”*. Lecture-derived book whose two-part history joins deconstruction, law, justice, and Benjamin’s “Critique of Violence.” [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1995:** *Mal d’archive: Une impression freudienne*. Book developed from a lecture and concerned with archives as both institutions and psychic-political structures. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1995:** *Moscou aller-retour*. Travel-dialogue and political reflection arising from Derrida’s visit to Moscow; neither conventional travelogue nor autonomous monograph alone. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1996:** *Apories: Mourir—s’attendre aux “limites de la vérité”*. Book-length philosophical essay originating in a conference. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1996:** *Résistances de la psychanalyse*. Collection of three interventions on psychoanalysis and resistance. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1996:** *Le monolinguisme de l’autre, ou la prothèse d’origine*. Autobiographical-philosophical book on language and colonial identity, developed from an oral presentation. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1997:** *Cosmopolites de tous les pays, encore un effort!*. Public political address published as a short book; a manifesto-like appeal for cities of refuge rather than a neutral description. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1997:** *Adieu à Emmanuel Levinas*. Memorial obra combining Derrida’s funeral address with an extended philosophical lecture on hospitality. [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/derrida/)
- **1997:** *Le droit à la philosophie du point de vue cosmopolitique*. Institutional and political address published as a short book under UNESCO auspices. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1998:** *Demeure: Maurice Blanchot*. Book-length reading of testimony and fiction, developed in relation to Blanchot’s *L’instant de ma mort*. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1999:** *Donner la mort*. Book bringing together a revised earlier essay and “La littérature au secret”; the 1999 object is therefore a recomposition, not simply a first appearance of all its contents. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1999:** *Sur parole: Instantanés philosophiques*. Radio conversations edited into a book; oral exchange is the originating medium. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2000:** *Foi et savoir, suivi de Le siècle et le pardon*. Paired pocket-book manifestation joining a 1996 essay with an interview on forgiveness. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2000:** *États d’âme de la psychanalyse: L’impossible au-delà d’une souveraine cruauté*. Address to the Estates General of Psychoanalysis published as a book; an intervention that acts on an institution as much as it describes psychoanalysis. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2000:** *Le toucher, Jean-Luc Nancy*. Extended philosophical book on touch and the work of Nancy. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2001:** *L’université sans condition*. Lecture-manifesto arguing for an unconditional university; it is explicitly an institutional proposition and performative declaration. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2001:** *Papier machine*. Collection of essays and interviews on paper, media, technology, and publication; a retrospective assemblage rather than a monograph. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2002:** *Artaud le Moma: Interjections d’appel*. Art-critical and philosophical book produced in connection with the Museum of Modern Art’s Artaud exhibition. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2002:** *Fichus*. Acceptance address for the Adorno Prize made into a short book; autobiographical, political, and philosophical intervention. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2002:** *H.C. pour la vie, c’est-à-dire…*. Book on Hélène Cixous whose title and address make it also an act of friendship and dedication. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2002:** *Marx & Sons*. Polemical response concerning *Spectres de Marx*, first produced for an English-language critical collection and then published separately in French. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2003:** *Voyous: Deux essais sur la raison*. Two-essay political-philosophical book assembled from lectures on sovereignty, democracy, and reason. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2003:** *Chaque fois unique, la fin du monde*. Collection of funeral or memorial texts; a made archive of mourning whose individual addresses originated as singular occasions. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2003:** *Genèses, généalogies, genres et le génie: Les secrets de l’archive*. Lecture-derived book on Cixous, genesis, genre, and archival secrecy. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2004:** *Béliers: Le dialogue ininterrompu—entre deux infinis, le poème*. Memorial lecture for Hans-Georg Gadamer published as a short book; philosophical reading and mourning-address. [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/derrida/)

### Essays, prefaces, letters, and interventions that became distinct objects

- **1959:** *“Genèse et structure” et la phénoménologie*. Conference later collected in *L’écriture et la différence*; the lecture and collected essay are successive manifestations of one work. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.idixa.net/Pixa/pagixa-0802190834.html)
- **1961:** *Lettre à Pierre Nora à propos des Français d’Algérie*. Letter subsequently published in the revised edition of Nora’s *Les Français d’Algérie*; private correspondence made public posthumously. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1963:** *Force et signification*. Essay first published in *Critique* and later incorporated into *L’écriture et la différence*. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.idixa.net/Pixa/pagixa-0802190834.html)
- **1963–1964:** *Cogito et histoire de la folie*. Lecture and subsequently published essay responding to Michel Foucault; later collected in *L’écriture et la différence*. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.idixa.net/Pixa/pagixa-0802190834.html)
- **1963–1964:** *Violence et métaphysique: Essai sur la pensée d’Emmanuel Levinas*. Extended journal essay later incorporated into *L’écriture et la différence*. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.idixa.net/Pixa/pagixa-0802190834.html)
- **1964:** *Edmond Jabès et la question du livre*. Essay first published in *Critique* and later collected in *L’écriture et la différence*. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.idixa.net/Pixa/pagixa-0802190834.html)
- **1965:** *La parole soufflée*. Essay on Artaud, first published in *Tel Quel* and later collected in *L’écriture et la différence*. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.idixa.net/Pixa/pagixa-0802190834.html)
- **1965–1966:** *L’écriture avant la lettre*. Journal essay expanded into the first part of *De la grammatologie*; it resists treatment as entirely separate from that book. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.idixa.net/Pixa/pagixa-0802190834.html)
- **1966:** *Freud et la scène de l’écriture*. Psychoanalytic-institute lecture subsequently made into an essay in *L’écriture et la différence*. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.idixa.net/Pixa/pagixa-0802190834.html)
- **1966:** *Le théâtre de la cruauté et la clôture de la représentation*. Conference on Artaud later collected in *L’écriture et la différence*. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.idixa.net/Pixa/pagixa-0802190834.html)
- **1966:** *La structure, le signe et le jeu dans le discours des sciences humaines*. Johns Hopkins conference paper later collected in *L’écriture et la différence*. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.idixa.net/Pixa/pagixa-0802190834.html)
- **1966; published 2000:** *La phénoménologie et la clôture de la métaphysique*. Essay whose publication occurred decades after its composition. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.idixa.net/Pixa/pagixa-0802190834.html)
- **1967:** *De l’économie restreinte à l’économie générale: Un hégélianisme sans réserve*. Essay on Bataille later collected in *L’écriture et la différence*. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.idixa.net/Pixa/pagixa-0802190834.html)
- **1967:** *Ellipse*. Essay forming the closing text of *L’écriture et la différence*. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.idixa.net/Pixa/pagixa-0802190834.html)
- **1967:** *La forme et le vouloir-dire*. Phenomenological essay first published in a journal and later collected in *Marges—de la philosophie*. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.idixa.net/Pixa/pagixa-0802190834.html)
- **1968:** *La pharmacie de Platon*. Essay first published serially in *Tel Quel* and later reconstructed within *La Dissémination*. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.idixa.net/Pixa/pagixa-0802190834.html)
- **1968:** *La différance*. Conference whose anomalous spelling operates performatively in writing and cannot be conveyed identically by speech; later collected in *Marges—de la philosophie*. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.idixa.net/Pixa/pagixa-0802190834.html)
- **1968:** *Ousia et grammè*. Essay first published in a collective homage to Jean Beaufret and later collected in *Marges—de la philosophie*. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.idixa.net/Pixa/pagixa-0802190834.html)
- **1968:** *Le puits et la pyramide*. Seminar lecture reworked as an essay and later collected in *Marges—de la philosophie*. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.idixa.net/Pixa/pagixa-0802190834.html)
- **1968:** *Les fins de l’homme*. Conference composed amid the political events of 1968, delivered in New York, and later collected in *Marges—de la philosophie*. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.idixa.net/Pixa/pagixa-0802190834.html)
- **1968:** *Le Cercle linguistique de Genève*. Conference paper later collected in *Marges—de la philosophie*. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.idixa.net/Pixa/pagixa-0802190834.html)
- **1969–1970:** *La double séance*. Record and reworking of two sessions of the Groupe d’Études Théoriques, published serially before incorporation into *La Dissémination*. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.idixa.net/Pixa/pagixa-0802190834.html)
- **1969:** *La Dissémination*. Essay first published in *Critique* and later used as the title-bearing component of the 1972 book. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.idixa.net/Pixa/pagixa-0802190834.html)
- **1971:** *Le supplément de copule*. Journal essay on philosophy and linguistics, later collected in *Marges—de la philosophie*. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.idixa.net/Pixa/pagixa-0802190834.html)
- **1975:** *Économimésis*. Essay published within the collective volume *Mimésis des articulations*; an autonomous contribution, not a Derrida-authored book. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1976:** *Fors*. Substantial preface to Nicolas Abraham and Maria Torok’s *Le Verbier de l’Homme aux loups*; a parasitic or threshold-text whose identity remains bound to another book. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1977:** *Scribble (pouvoir/écrire)*. Substantial preface to Warburton’s *Essai sur les hiéroglyphes*; an independent philosophical intervention occupying another author’s threshold. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1980:** *Ocelle comme pas un*. Preface to Jean-Pierre Joliet’s *L’enfant au chien-assis*. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1981:** *Interpreting Signatures (Nietzsche/Heidegger): Two Questions*. Goethe-Institut conference surviving in English and German publication; Derridex reports that the original French text has never been published, so its textual identity is mediated by translation. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1985:** *Lecture du Droit de regards*. Essay accompanying Marie-Françoise Plissart’s photographic sequence; word-image collaboration rather than a freestanding prose book. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1985:** *Préjugés—Devant la loi*. Essay in the collective volume *La Faculté de juger*, developed around Kafka and judgment. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1985:** *Au-delà du principe de pouvoir*. Intervention at a New York memorial for Foucault, probably delivered on 27 March 1985; the date remains qualified in the bibliography, and the transcription was published only in 2014. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1986:** *Forcener le subjectile*. Substantial text accompanying Artaud’s drawings and portraits; a visual-textual collaboration with reproduced art rather than a conventional monograph. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1989:** *Some Statements and Truisms about Neologisms, Newisms, Postisms, Parasitisms, and Other Small Seisms*. English-language contribution to *The States of “Theory”*, only later translated into French. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1992:** *Nous autres Grecs*. Essay in the collective volume *Nos Grecs et leurs modernes*. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1992:** *Syllabe*. Exergue to Jean-Pierre Moussaron’s essay on Michel Deguy; a deliberately minor paratextual object rather than a book. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1993:** *Prégnances*. Text published with seven lithographs by Colette Deblé and reconfigured in 2004 with her washes and paintings; a collaborative livre d’artiste and thus a small Gesamtkunstwerk of word and image. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1994:** *Fourmis*. Essay contributed to *Lectures de la différence sexuelle*. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1994:** *Faxitexture*. Text dated May 1992 but first published in 1994 in *Noise*; composition and manifestation dates should not be collapsed. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1995:** *Avances*. Preface to Serge Margel’s *Le tombeau du dieu artisan*. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1995:** *Préface à En direct du couloir de la mort*. Preface to Mumia Abu-Jamal’s book; a political intervention attached to another person’s testimony. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1996:** *Lignées*. Text published with drawings by Micaëla Henich; a word-image object whose authorship is necessarily collaborative. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1996:** *Demeure, Athènes*. Text composed with Jean-François Bonhomme’s photographs, first issued bilingually in Athens and republished in 2009; photographic essay and memorial itinerary rather than prose alone. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1997:** *Marx, c’est quelqu’un* and *Manquements—du droit à la justice*. Two interventions published in the collaborative volume *Marx en jeu*. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1997:** *Joue—le prénom*. Text made for an improvisation by Ornette Coleman; a performative accompaniment to music, not a song, score, or ordinary essay. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1997–1999:** *Hostipitalité*. Workshop intervention delivered at Istanbul in 1997 and first published there in 1999; Derridex notes uncertainty about any French-language periodical manifestation. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2001:** *La forme et la façon*. Preface to Alain David’s *Racisme et antisémitisme*. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2001:** *De la couleur à la lettre*. Art-critical contribution to a volume on Jean-Michel Atlan. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2001:** *Une certaine possibilité impossible de dire l’événement*. Seminar intervention published in *Dire l’événement, est-ce possible?* [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2001:** *La veilleuse*. Preface to Jacques Trilling’s *James Joyce ou l’écriture matricide*. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2002:** *Peine de mort et souveraineté*. Sofia conference text based on the opening session of the 1999–2000 death-penalty seminar; derivative manifestation, not a wholly separate project. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2003:** *Abraham, l’autre*. Essay in *Judéités: Questions pour Jacques Derrida*, later republished in *Le dernier des Juifs*. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2004:** *Auto-immunités, suicides réels et symboliques*. Derrida’s dialogue with Giovanna Borradori in *Le “concept” du 11 septembre*; the containing book is jointly constituted with Borradori and Jürgen Habermas, not solely authored by Derrida. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2004:** *Le lieu-dit: Strasbourg*. Contribution to the collaborative volume *Penser à Strasbourg*. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2004:** *Son malin génie—Préparatifs pour l’infini*. Preface to Safaa Fathy’s plays *Ordalie* and *Terreur*. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2004:** *Et cetera… (and so on, und so weiter, and so forth, et ainsi de suite, und so überall, etc.)*. Multilingual essay published in the *Cahier de l’Herne* devoted to Derrida. [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/derrida/)
- **2004:** *Je suis en guerre contre moi-même*. Final major newspaper interview, published in *Le Monde* on 19 August 2004; a dialogical and autobiographical manifestation rather than a self-authored monograph. [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/derrida/)

### Dialogues and collaborative textual obras

- **1982:** *L’oreille de l’autre: Otobiographies, transferts, traductions*. Volume combining a Derrida text with debates involving Claude Lévesque, Christie McDonald, and others; distributed authorship makes it a record of an event as well as a book. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1986:** *Altérités*. Dialogical volume with Pierre-Jean Labarrière and studies by Francis Guibal and Stanislas Breton; not exclusively Derrida’s obra. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1996:** *Échographies de la télévision*. Filmed and written dialogues with Bernard Stiegler; audiovisual event and book manifestation coexist without being identical. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1997:** *Anne Dufourmantelle invite Jacques Derrida à répondre de l’hospitalité*. Dialogical book whose framing and questions belong to Dufourmantelle; not to be confused with the different 2001 collective manifesto also called *De l’hospitalité*. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1993–1995; Italian publication 1997; French publication 2018:** *Le goût du secret*. Interviews with Maurizio Ferraris; the Italian version preceded publication of the original French conversations. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1998:** *Voiles*. Diptych with Hélène Cixous, combining her “Savoir” and Derrida’s “Un ver à soie”; collaborative book whose two voices remain distinct. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1999:** *La contre-allée*. Travel-philosophy collaboration with Catherine Malabou, incorporating postcards, photographs, and alternating textual routes; a multimedia itinerary rather than a unitary monograph. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2000:** *Tourner les mots: Au bord d’un film*. Collaborative book with Safaa Fathy made around the filming of *D’ailleurs, Derrida*; commentary, diary, still image, and film remain interdependent. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2001:** *La connaissance des textes: Lecture d’un manuscrit illisible*. Collaborative book with Simon Hantaï and Jean-Luc Nancy organized around an unreadable manuscript and its visual materiality. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2001:** *De quoi demain… Dialogue*. Book-length dialogue with Élisabeth Roudinesco; shared conversational obra rather than two independent essays. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2001:** *Body of Prayer*. Collaborative publication with Michal Govrin and David Shapiro produced through an architecture school; a crossing of textual, bodily, religious, and architectural practices. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2002:** *Au-delà des apparences*. Published interview with Antoine Spire; dialogical record made into a short book. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2004:** *Lengua por venir / Langue à venir*. Bilingual Barcelona seminar publication; a pedagogical and dialogical event given a book manifestation. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)

### Visual, architectural, exhibition, musical, and cinematic works

- **1985:** *Épreuves d’écriture*. Textual material prepared at Jean-François Lyotard’s request for the exhibition *Les Immatériaux*; an exhibition component rather than a conventional independent work, with Derrida’s participation ultimately marked by withdrawal and displacement. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1985–1987:** *Chora L Works / La Villette project*. Collaborative architectural project with Peter Eisenman for Bernard Tschumi’s Parc de la Villette; never built, but the drawings, correspondence, models, and book make the unfinished project itself the work. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1990:** *Mémoires d’aveugle* exhibition. Derrida served as guest curator at the Louvre, selecting drawings and constructing an exhibition-essay on blindness and self-portraiture; the exhibition is a manifestation distinct from its catalogue. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1996:** *Erradid*. Gallery work associated with La Hune-Brenner; the bibliography records the title and venue but gives too little information to decide securely whether it is an exhibition, edition, or visual-textual collaboration, and that resistance to classification must remain visible. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1997:** *La langue de l’autre / Joue—le prénom*. Dialogue with Ornette Coleman followed by a text intended for Coleman’s improvisation; the musical event was co-made in performance, while Derrida did not compose the music itself. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1999:** *D’ailleurs, Derrida*. Film by Safaa Fathy containing Derrida’s speech, movements, locations, and staged encounters; authorship of the film belongs to Fathy, while Derrida’s filmed performance is a collaborative manifestation and should not be silently converted into a Derrida film. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2002:** *De tout cœur*. Video address to Israelis and Palestinians, followed by Derrida’s reading of “Nous?”; a political audiovisual intervention rather than a book. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2002:** *Derrida*. Documentary by Kirby Dick and Amy Ziering Kofman in which Derrida participates and performs resistance to documentary biography; it is not solely his work, although his filmed acts and reflections are constitutive materials. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)

### Manifestos, institutions, and collective projects

- **1974:** *Groupe de recherches sur l’enseignement philosophique (GREPH)*. Collective institutional project co-founded by Derrida to contest restrictions on philosophical education; an organized practice and archive of interventions, not a single text. Its writings were later gathered in *Du droit à la philosophie*. [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/derrida/)
- **1983:** *Collège international de philosophie*. Institution co-founded by Derrida with François Châtelet, Jean-Pierre Faye, and Dominique Lecourt; an ongoing collective project that cannot be credited to Derrida alone or reduced to its founding documents. [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/derrida/)
- **1985:** *Parlement international des écrivains*. Derrida helped create and lead this writers’ institution; the institution and its political activity constitute a collective project rather than an individually authored obra. [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/derrida/)
- **1994:** *Appel pour une Internationale nouvelle*. The performative political proposal within *Spectres de Marx* for a “New International”; intentionally lacks the conventional organization, membership, and party protocol of an institution. [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/derrida/)
- **1997:** *Villes-refuges*. Political-institutional project advocated in *Cosmopolites de tous les pays, encore un effort!*; both manifesto and proposed network of sanctuary cities, not merely a descriptive essay. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2001:** *De l’hospitalité*. Collective manifesto directed by Mohammed Seffahi, containing a Derrida contribution; it is distinct from Derrida and Dufourmantelle’s 1997 book bearing a similar title. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)

### Courses and seminars: pedagogical works, surviving archives, and unfinished projects

- **1960–1961:** *Penser, c’est dire non*. Sorbonne course, published posthumously in 2022; a teaching manuscript made for oral delivery rather than a book designed by Derrida for publication. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.idixa.net/Pixa/pagixa-0802190834.html)
- **1963:** *Du même à l’autre: Deux cours sur Husserl*. Two Sorbonne courses, published posthumously in 2024; editorially reconstructed pedagogical works. [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/derrida/)
- **1964–1965:** *Heidegger: La question de l’Être et de l’Histoire*. ENS course, published posthumously in 2013; the surviving lecture text is both pedagogical event and archival manuscript. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1969–1970:** *Psychanalyse et critique littéraire*. Seminar published posthumously in 2025; its late editorial manifestation should not be mistaken for a work newly made in 2025. [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/derrida/)
- **1975–1976:** *La vie la mort*. ENS seminar, published posthumously in 2019; the absence of a conjunction in its title is part of its conceptual construction. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1975–1977?:** *Théorie et pratique*. ENS course on Marx’s “Theses on Feuerbach” and Althusser, published in 2017; the scholarly bibliography reports uncertainty over whether it belongs to 1975–1976 or 1976–1977. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1976–1977:** *Walter Benjamin*. Seminar of which only three sessions are reported as preserved at IMEC; fragmentary archive, not a demonstrably complete work. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1978–1979:** *Donner le temps*. ENS seminar later divided between *Donner le temps, 1* and the posthumous *Donner le temps II*; the sixth session remains unpublished, so no published volume equals the complete seminar. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1984–1985:** *Geschlecht III: Sexe, race, nation, humanité*. Seminar on Heidegger, partly rewritten by Derrida under this title; only sessions 7–13 were published in 2018, making the book a partial manifestation rather than the entire seminar. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1995–1996:** *Hospitalité, volume I*. Seminar published posthumously in 2021; editorial book made from Derrida’s pedagogical manuscript. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1996–1997:** *Hospitalité, volume II*. Continuation of the seminar, published posthumously in 2022; distinct from both the 1997 Dufourmantelle dialogue and the 2001 manifesto. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.idixa.net/Pixa/pagixa-0802190834.html)
- **1997–1998:** *Le parjure et le pardon, volume I*. Seminar published posthumously in 2019. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1998–1999:** *Le parjure et le pardon, volume II*. Continuation published posthumously in 2020; some bibliographic listings incorrectly repeat 1997–1998 or give 2019, so the volume’s own sequence and the chronological bibliography should control. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.idixa.net/Pixa/pagixa-0802190834.html)
- **1999–2000:** *Séminaire: La peine de mort, volume I*. Seminar published posthumously in 2012; an edited manifestation of teaching notes and oral delivery. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2000–2001:** *Séminaire: La peine de mort, volume II*. Continuation published posthumously in 2015. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2001–2002:** *Séminaire: La bête et le souverain, volume I*. Seminar published posthumously in 2008. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2002–2003:** *Séminaire: La bête et le souverain, volume II*. Derrida’s final completed seminar cycle, published posthumously in 2010. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)

### Posthumous manifestations and editorially constituted works

- **2005:** *Apprendre à vivre enfin*. Derrida’s final interview, conducted shortly before his death and issued posthumously as a book; a dialogical testament, not a manuscript he designed as a monograph. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2005:** *Penser à ne pas voir*. Short posthumous publication on visual art; later also used as the title of a much larger 2013 editorial collection, so the two objects should not be conflated. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2006:** *L’animal que donc je suis*. Posthumously edited book derived from Derrida’s 1997 Cerisy address, portions of which had already appeared in 1999 and 2004; the complete book is an editorial manifestation of a pre-existing lecture project. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2006:** *Le sacrifice*. Postface to Daniel Mesguich’s *L’éternel éphémère*, based on an intervention delivered in 1991 and published posthumously. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2007:** *En composant “Circonfession”*. Posthumously published account of making *Circonfession*, included in *Des Confessions: Jacques Derrida—Saint Augustin*. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2010:** *Remarques sur la déconstruction et le pragmatisme*. Posthumous transcription of a recorded 29 May 1993 intervention; the editor’s transcription mediates the surviving work. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1991–1993; French publication 2011:** *Politique et amitié*. Conversations with Michael Sprinker on Marx and Althusser, finalized in 1991 and first published in English in 1993; the 2011 French book is a later manifestation. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1987; book publication 2012:** *Les yeux de la langue: L’abîme et le volcan*. Conference text with a complex publication history through English translation and the 2004 *Cahier de l’Herne*, issued separately only after Derrida’s death. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1997; book publication 2012:** *Histoire du mensonge: Prolégomènes*. Conference text circulated earlier in translation and in the 2004 *Cahier de l’Herne*, then constituted as a posthumous French book. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1997–1998; book publication 2012:** *Pardonner: L’impardonnable et l’imprescriptible*. Lecture delivered at several universities and previously printed in the 2004 *Cahier de l’Herne*; the 2012 volume is not its first manifestation. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2013:** *Penser à ne pas voir: Écrits sur les arts du visible, 1979–2004*. Editorial collection containing reprints alongside pieces and interviews previously unavailable in French; the collection as a whole is posthumously made by its editors, though its component texts are Derrida’s. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2014:** *Le dernier des Juifs*. Posthumous pairing of two texts on Judaism, one delivered in 1998 and one published in 2003; an editorially constituted book rather than a single work planned under that title by Derrida. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1988; publication 2014:** *La conférence de Heidelberg*. Published record of an encounter among Derrida, Hans-Georg Gadamer, and Philippe Lacoue-Labarthe; collective event and edited transcript, not solely Derrida’s work. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **2015:** *Les arts de l’espace*. Posthumous editorial collection of Derrida’s architectural writings and interventions from 1984–1997; the individual texts pre-existed the collected obra. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1997; book publication 2016:** *Surtout, pas de journalistes!*. Conference intervention on religion and media, previously printed without the complete question period; the 2016 book adds the exchanges and is therefore a changed documentary object. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1973; publication 2020:** *Le calcul des langues*. Unfinished two-column text on Condillac and Freud, published posthumously; its incompletion is constitutive and should not be repaired into an imaginary finished book. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
- **1978–1979; publication 2021:** *Donner le temps II*. Posthumously edited continuation of the seminar behind *Donner le temps, 1*; because the sixth session remains unpublished, the two volumes still do not constitute a complete printed seminar. [Derridex](https://derridex.fr/index.php/bibliographie-de-jacques-derrida/)
