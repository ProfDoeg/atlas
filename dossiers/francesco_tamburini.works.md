## Works by Francesco Tamburini

### Architectural studies and graphic works

- **Before 1883:** *Measured drawings of Pisa Cathedral*. Architectural survey drawings produced during Tamburini’s Italian teaching years; the surviving account does not supply a fixed collective title or date. [Biographical account](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francesco_Tamburini)
- **Before 1883:** *Measured drawings of San Francesco alle Scale, Ancona*. Detailed architectural surveys of Giorgio da Sebenico’s church rather than a new building by Tamburini; their status lies between scholarship, documentation, and graphic obra. [Biographical account](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francesco_Tamburini)
- **Before 1883:** *Measured drawings of Sant’Agostino, Ancona*. Architectural surveys subsequently published, but not securely identified by a fixed title or year in the consulted sources. [Biographical account](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francesco_Tamburini)

### Buildings and projects in Italy

- **1881:** *Villa Marulli* (also called *Palazzina Marulli*), Chiaravalle. An eclectic private villa commissioned by Gustavo Marulli, later used as the municipal library; the local account calls it one of only two buildings Tamburini realized in Italy. [Chiaravalle 360°](https://www.chiaravalle360gradi.it/fsp-palazzina-marulli/)
- **1882–1883:** *Palazzo Rheinold*, Ancona. A neo-Renaissance commercial or residential palace known from Tamburini’s façade design; sources cautiously describe it as his probable first building, so its precise status and date remain uncertain. [Biographical account](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francesco_Tamburini), [Habitual Tourist](https://www.habitualtourist.com/ascoli-piceno/ascoli-piceno-citta/francesco-tamburini)

### Government and civic architecture in Argentina

- **1884–1890:** *Casa de Gobierno de la Nación (Casa Rosada)*, Buenos Aires. A large obra of unification, enlargement, and refacing rather than an entirely new building: Tamburini joined the former Post Office and Government House with the monumental central arch and redesigned the complex. [Argentina.gob.ar](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/francisco-tamburini), [catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1886:** *Ampliación y reforma de la Casa de Gobierno de Córdoba*. An unrealized project for enlarging and reforming Córdoba’s government house or Cabildo. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920), [Carlos A. Page, appendix](https://www.carlospage.com.ar/wp-content/2022/08/Tamburini-en-Cordoba.pdf)
- **1883–1890:** *Palacio de Justicia*, Buenos Aires. An unbuilt national-government project; it should not be confused with the later Palace of Justice erected to another design. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1883–1890:** *Congreso de la Nación*, Buenos Aires. An unrealized parliamentary-palace project, not the subsequently constructed Congress building designed by Vittorio Meano. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1889:** *Biblioteca Nacional*, Buenos Aires. An unrealized project intended for Avenida de Mayo; its existence is the project and drawings, not a completed library. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920), [biographical summary](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francesco_Tamburini)
- **1883–1890:** *Museo de Productos Argentinos*, Buenos Aires. An unrealized museum project recorded in the standard works-and-projects list; no secure individual date was found. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1883–1890:** *Aduana de Baradero*. An unrealized customs-house project whose surviving catalogue title identifies the institution and locality rather than a formally named building. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1883–1890:** *Oficinas Nacionales*, San Luis. A national-office building classified as an executed obra in the catalogue, although the consulted source does not establish how much was completed under Tamburini. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1883–1890:** *Oficinas Nacionales*, Córdoba. An unrealized project for national administrative offices. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1883–1890:** *Casa de Correos y Telégrafos*, Santa Fe. An unrealized post-and-telegraph office project. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)

### Theatres

- **1888–1890:** *Teatro Colón*, Buenos Aires. Tamburini’s original project and the beginning of a building left incomplete at his death; Vittorio Meano continued and modified it, and Jules Dormal later completed the interiors before the 1908 opening, making the finished theatre a layered, multi-author obra. [Teatro Colón](https://teatrocolon.org.ar/el-teatro/), [Argentina.gob.ar](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/teatro-colon)
- **1887–1891:** *Teatro Rivera Indarte* (now *Teatro del Libertador General San Martín*), Córdoba. A theatre designed by Tamburini and inaugurated in 1891; later completion and alteration mean that the extant building is not simply an untouched authorial object. [Carlos A. Page, appendix](https://www.carlospage.com.ar/wp-content/2022/08/Tamburini-en-Cordoba.pdf), [catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)

### Schools and institutions of learning

- **1885–after 1890:** *Escuela Superior de Medicina* (Facultad de Medicina), Buenos Aires. A medical-school complex designed by Tamburini, subsequently altered and partly demolished; fragments survive within the present Faculty of Economic Sciences. [History of the UBA](https://www.scribd.com/document/782650755/Historia-Urbana-y-Arquitectonica-de-La-UBA-2%C2%BA-Galera), [catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1886–1889:** *Escuela Normal de Profesores “Mariano Acosta”*, Buenos Aires. A purpose-designed teacher-training school, completed and inaugurated during Tamburini’s lifetime. [Buenos Aires city guide](https://buenosaires.gob.ar/sites/default/files/media/document/2020/05/04/829ae51b08da4c303626c3a91e2cfcac5ac4f264.pdf), [catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1883–1890:** *Ampliación de la Escuela Normal de Profesoras de la Capital*, Buenos Aires. An enlargement rather than an autonomous new school; it is catalogued among executed works, but no secure individual date was found. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1883–1890:** *Escuela “Nicolás Avellaneda”*, Buenos Aires. A school at Talcahuano and Viamonte catalogued as an executed work; it must be distinguished from the Rosario normal school that later bore the same honorific. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1883–1890:** *Escuela Primaria de Sarmiento y Esmeralda*, Buenos Aires. An executed primary-school building identified in the catalogue by its street corner rather than a stable institutional title. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1883–1890:** *Escuela Normal de Maestras*, Rosario. A teacher-training school, later known as the Escuela Normal No. 2 “Juan María Gutiérrez” or “Nicolás Avellaneda” in differing secondary accounts; the changing names make the institutional identity less fixed than the architectural commission. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1883–1890:** *Escuela Normal de Maestros*, Santiago del Estero. An executed school commission in the catalogue, although the consulted evidence does not clarify whether Tamburini designed, supervised, or completed every part. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1883–1890:** *Colegio Nacional*, Santiago del Estero. A school building attributed to Tamburini in the catalogue and biographical record; no secure individual date was found. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1883–1890:** *Edificios escolares para la ciudad de Mendoza*. A plural body of school designs rather than one readily separable work; the catalogue does not identify the number, individual titles, or completion status of the buildings. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1889:** *Escuela Normal de Maestras*, Córdoba. An unbuilt project; Carlos A. Page’s appendix calls it simply *Escuela Normal*, while the general catalogue specifies a women’s normal school. [Carlos A. Page, appendix](https://www.carlospage.com.ar/wp-content/2022/08/Tamburini-en-Cordoba.pdf), [catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1883–1890:** *Escuela Normal de Maestros*, San Luis. An unrealized teacher-training-school project. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1883–1890:** *Escuela Normal de Maestras*, Concepción del Uruguay. An unrealized teacher-training-school project. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1883–1890:** *Colegio Nacional*, Mendoza. An unrealized secondary-school project, distinct from the catalogue’s plural group of Mendoza school buildings. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1889:** *Escuela Normal Mixta*, Río Cuarto. A mixed normal-school project; later national-office development of Tamburini’s educational prototypes complicates attribution of any subsequently constructed version. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920), [educational-architecture study](https://www.scribd.com/document/886075165/Arquitectura-Educacion-y-Patrimonio-Fabio-Grementieri-Claudia-Shmidt)
- **1883–1890:** *Escuela Normal Mixta*, Mercedes. An unrealized mixed normal-school project; the catalogue does not specify which province’s Mercedes in its short title. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1883–1890:** *Escuela Normal de Maestros*, Catamarca. An unrealized men’s teacher-training-school project. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1883–1890:** *Colegio Nacional*, Catamarca. An unrealized national-secondary-school project. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1883–1890:** *Escuela Normal Mixta*, San Nicolás. An unrealized mixed normal-school project. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1883–1890:** *Colegio Nacional*, La Rioja. An unrealized national-secondary-school project. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1883–1890:** *Escuela Normal de Maestros*, San Juan. An unrealized men’s normal-school project. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1883–1890:** *Escuela Normal de Maestras*, San Juan. An unrealized women’s normal-school project, separate from the men’s-school proposal for the same city. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1883–1890:** *Escuela Normal*, Salta. Catalogued as a project, although later accounts associate Tamburini with a women’s normal school in Salta; the relationship between his design and the ultimately realized building is not resolved by the consulted catalogue. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920), [biographical summary](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francesco_Tamburini)

### Hospitals, maternity buildings, and migration infrastructure

- **1883–1890:** *Hospital Militar Central*, Buenos Aires. A military-hospital complex formerly on Combate de los Pozos; it was later displaced by the present Hospital Garrahan, so the surviving institution is not Tamburini’s building. [Argentina.gob.ar](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/francisco-tamburini), [catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1883–1887:** *Asilo de Maternidad*, Buenos Aires. A three-pavilion maternity and teaching complex associated with the medical school; it is an executed institutional building rather than a textual “work.” [History of the UBA](https://www.scribd.com/document/782650755/Historia-Urbana-y-Arquitectonica-de-La-UBA-2%C2%BA-Galera), [catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1884–1913:** *Hospital de Clínicas*, Córdoba. Tamburini’s 1884 project was constructed only over a prolonged period and opened long after his death; the completed hospital therefore realizes his project without being wholly his supervised obra. [Carlos A. Page, appendix](https://www.carlospage.com.ar/wp-content/2022/08/Tamburini-en-Cordoba.pdf)
- **1883–1890:** *Hotel de Inmigrantes*, Buenos Aires. An unrealized immigrant-reception complex; its status is solely that of a project and should not be transferred to the later Hotel de Inmigrantes. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1886:** *Asilo de Huérfanas / Asilo de Huérfanos*, Córdoba. An unbuilt orphanage project: Carlos A. Page identifies the commission as an *Asilo de Huérfanas* for L. Vázquez, while the general catalogue uses the masculine or generic *Huérfanos*. [Carlos A. Page, appendix](https://www.carlospage.com.ar/wp-content/2022/08/Tamburini-en-Cordoba.pdf), [catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)

### Police, military, and penal architecture

- **1883–1889:** *Jefatura de Policía / Departamento Central de Policía*, Buenos Aires. A central police headquarters designed and constructed under Tamburini’s direction; the institutional names vary across sources. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920), [Dictionary of Architecture in Argentina](https://pdfcoffee.com/dicc-arq-tomo6-pdf-free.html)
- **1883–1890:** *Talleres del Arsenal de Guerra*, Buenos Aires. Arsenal workshops catalogued as an executed military-industrial work; no secure individual date or evidence of survival was found. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1883–1890:** *Edificios para comisarías*, Buenos Aires. A repeatable group or type of police-station buildings rather than one singular obra; the catalogue does not enumerate the individual stations. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1883–1890:** *Cárcel Correccional*, Buenos Aires. An unrealized correctional-prison project. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1886–1895:** *Penitenciaría*, Córdoba. A prison designed in 1886 and opened in 1895; although the general catalogue places it under “projects,” the Córdoba study records it as constructed, necessarily with posthumous execution. [Carlos A. Page, appendix](https://www.carlospage.com.ar/wp-content/2022/08/Tamburini-en-Cordoba.pdf), [catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)

### Commercial and infrastructural architecture

- **1886–1889:** *Mercado San Vicente*, Córdoba. A privately commissioned market designed for S. Palacios, opened in 1889 and subsequently demolished. [Carlos A. Page, appendix](https://www.carlospage.com.ar/wp-content/2022/08/Tamburini-en-Cordoba.pdf)
- **1887–1889:** *Banco Provincial de Córdoba*. A bank headquarters designed in 1887 and opened in 1889; it is one of Tamburini’s principal surviving Córdoba buildings. [Carlos A. Page, appendix](https://www.carlospage.com.ar/wp-content/2022/08/Tamburini-en-Cordoba.pdf), [catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1887:** *Mercado Pío Valdez*, Córdoba. An unbuilt private-market project; “Pío Valdez” identifies the client rather than a realized institution. [Carlos A. Page, appendix](https://www.carlospage.com.ar/wp-content/2022/08/Tamburini-en-Cordoba.pdf)
- **1889:** *Bolsa de Comercio*, Córdoba. An unbuilt stock-exchange project. [Carlos A. Page, appendix](https://www.carlospage.com.ar/wp-content/2022/08/Tamburini-en-Cordoba.pdf)
- **1883–1890:** *Edificio para el alumbrado eléctrico del Parque Tres de Febrero*, Buenos Aires. A utilitarian power or lighting-service building catalogued as an executed obra; the source does not provide a more stable title, precise date, or survival history. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)

### Houses and domestic projects

- **1883–1890:** *Casa de Miguel Juárez Celman*, Buenos Aires. A private residence occupying or addressing 25 de Mayo 551 and Avenida Leandro N. Alem 546; the catalogue supplies no secure construction date. [Catalogue-derived list](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1883–1890:** *Casco de la estancia “La Elisa”*, Capitán Sarmiento. A country-house project for Miguel Juárez Celman; it is reported in biographical accounts but is absent from the shortened catalogue list, so the extent of Tamburini’s executed contribution remains uncertain. [Catalogue-derived biographical account](https://www.revisionistas.com.ar/?p=19920)
- **1883–1890:** *Residence for Eduardo Wilde*. A private-house design attributed in later biographical summaries; the consulted standard list does not name or locate it, and its completion status is unresolved. [Biographical summary](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francesco_Tamburini)
- **1883–1890:** *Houses for Zorilla, Tricogen, Besail, Blauco, Gentili, and Tamburini himself*. A loosely documented group of private residences rather than six securely catalogued objects: spellings, locations, dates, and survival are not established by the consulted sources, so the group resists responsible subdivision into independently titled works. [Italian biographical account](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francesco_Tamburini)
