## Works by Augusto Pinochet Ugarte

### Military-geographical textbooks and studies

- **1953:** *Síntesis geográfica de Chile, Argentina, Bolivia y Perú*. A textbook assembled for military instruction and later issued in several revised forms; the standard biographical bibliography treats the four-country synthesis as one work rather than four separate books. Pinochet’s authorship is conventional rather than uncontested: Juan Cristóbal Peña’s investigation characterizes his scholarly production as heavily derivative. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional de Chile](https://www.bcn.cl/historiapolitica/resenas_parlamentarias/wiki/Augusto_Pinochet_Ugarte); [Revista Dossier](https://revistadossier.udp.cl/dossier/pinochet-detras-de-los-lentes/))

- **1957:** *Geografía militar*. A military-academy textbook, produced from the material Pinochet taught in Chile and Ecuador; it is a pedagogical manual rather than a general geography. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional de Chile](https://www.bcn.cl/historiapolitica/resenas_parlamentarias/wiki/Augusto_Pinochet_Ugarte); [Academia de Guerra del Ejército Ecuatoriano](https://journal.espe.edu.ec/ojs/index.php/Academia-de-guerra/en/article/download/3116/2418/12002))

- **1964:** *Significado militar de las relaciones espaciales*. A signed journal article in no. 317 of the *Memorial del Ejército de Chile*, applying geography to Chilean military security. It is absent from the usual book lists but documented by a scholarly bibliometric study of the journal; that study also warns that the surviving online run is incomplete, so no claim that this was Pinochet’s only periodical article is possible. ([Albuquerque, Ossandón and Quiroga](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/354898719_Presencia_de_la_Doctrina_de_Seguridad_Nacional_en_la_revista_Memorial_del_Ejercito_de_Chile_1960-1973))

- **1965:** *Ensayo de un estudio preliminar de una geopolítica de Chile*. A geopolitical monograph applying military-geographical concepts to Chile. Bibliographies vary slightly between *Ensayo de un estudio…* and *Ensayo sobre un estudio…*; the title is therefore not bibliographically stable across references. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional de Chile](https://www.bcn.cl/historiapolitica/resenas_parlamentarias/wiki/Augusto_Pinochet_Ugarte); [Security Praxis](https://www.securitypraxis.eu/chilean-geopolitics-under-the-military-dictatorship-of-augusto-pinochet/))

- **1968:** *Geopolítica: diferentes etapas para el estudio geopolítico de los Estados*. A military-academy textbook and Pinochet’s best-known theoretical publication. Its status as his intellectual obra is seriously disputed: Peña documents extensive unacknowledged borrowing from his teacher Gregorio Rodríguez Tascón, whose family recalled that Rodríguez regarded it as plagiarism and declined to write the requested prologue. ([Revista Dossier](https://revistadossier.udp.cl/dossier/pinochet-detras-de-los-lentes/); [Peña, “El aprendiz”](https://www.pagina12.com.ar/diario/suplementos/radar/subnotas/9011-2008-2013-07-28.html))

### Military history and commemorative production

- **1972:** *Guerra del Pacífico 1879: primeras operaciones terrestres (antecedentes, organización de las fuerzas, desarrollo de la campaña y ocupación del Departamento de Tarapacá)*. A 270-page operational history, commonly shortened in bibliographies to *La Guerra del Pacífico, campaña de Tarapacá*. It is a military-historical reconstruction, not a memoir of an event Pinochet witnessed. ([Google Books bibliographic record](https://books.google.com/books/about/Guerra_del_Pac%C3%ADfico_1879.html?hl=en&id=6VQOAQAAIAAJ&output=html_text); [Biblioteca Nacional Digital de Chile](https://www.bibliotecanacionaldigital.gob.cl/colecciones/BND/00/RC/RC0085956.pdf))

- **1979:** *Historia ilustrada de la Guerra del Pacífico, 1879–1884*. A large illustrated compilation directed by Hernán García Vidal and explicitly “sponsored” by Pinochet. It resists inclusion as an authored work: contemporary documentation assigns editorial direction to García Vidal and patronage—not textual authorship—to Pinochet. ([Memoria Chilena](https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/archivos2/pdfs/MC0051036.pdf); [Biblioteca Nacional Digital de Chile](https://www.bibliotecanacionaldigital.gob.cl/bnd/637/w3-article-225808.html))

### Regime narratives and political manifestos

- **1979:** *El día decisivo: 11 de septiembre de 1973*. A first-person justificatory narrative of the coup, functioning as political manifesto and official counter-history as much as historical account. Its successive editions are manifestations of one work, not separate works. ([Memoria Chilena](https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/602/w3-article-31395.html); [Memoria Chilena bibliography](https://www.memoriachilena.cl/archivos2/pdfs/MC0059400.pdf))

- **1983:** *Pinochet: patria y democracia*. A curated compilation of extracts from speeches and public interventions made between September 1973 and September 1983. It is a regime-produced manifestation of Pinochet’s public voice, not a continuously composed book, and its compiler is not clearly identified in the standard references. ([Varas and Carrasco, SciELO](https://www.scielo.cl/article_plus.php?lng=es&pid=S0718-50492020000100211&tlng=es); [Biblioteca Nacional Digital de Chile](https://www.bibliotecanacionaldigital.gob.cl/bnd/637/w3-article-234368.html))

- **1984:** *Política, politiquería, demagogia*. A book-length ideological essay contrasting military authority with party politics; in function it is a manifesto of authoritarian government. Contemporary criticism questioned both its literary character and intellectual substance, while later investigation casts wider doubt on how independently Pinochet composed books published under his name. ([Memoria Chilena](https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/archivos2/pdfs/MC0049129.pdf); [Ego sum Pinochet](https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/archivos2/pdfs/MC0038744.pdf))

- **1986:** *Repaso de la agresión comunista a Chile*. A 62-page illustrated anti-communist pamphlet: polemic and regime manifesto rather than disinterested history. Memoria Chilena’s author bibliography records it as a separate publication. ([Memoria Chilena](https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/602/w3-article-31395.html); [Memoria Chilena excerpt and attribution](https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/archivos2/pdfs/MC0014376.pdf))

- **1989:** *Transición y consolidación democrática, 1984–1989*. A compilation of speeches and reflections arranged to present Pinochet’s preferred interpretation of the transition to civilian rule. Because it repackages prior public texts, it is a documentary manifestation and political self-portrait rather than a wholly new continuous composition. ([Library of Congress, Handbook of Latin American Studies](https://www.loc.gov/item/hlas-bi94012404/))

### Memoirs

- **1990–1994:** *Camino recorrido*. A multipart autobiographical project published as three numbered tomes in four physical volumes: tome I, *Biografía de un soldado* (1990); tome II, *Memorias de un soldado* (1991); and tome III, *Memorias de un soldado*, volumes 1 and 2 (1993–1994). References sometimes call it four or five “volumes,” but the bibliographic structure is three tomes in four volumes; it is one memoir-project rather than four independent works. ([Memoria Chilena](https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/602/w3-article-31395.html); [CiNii bibliographic description](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BA27400036))

### Speeches and serial presidential texts

- **1974–1989:** *Mensaje presidencial*. The annual presidential messages form a serial body of state discourse, not a single authored book; a scholarly bibliography cites the sequence under this collective title for the years 1974–1989. Authorship is necessarily institutional and performative—Pinochet delivered or authorized the texts, but the surviving bibliographic sources do not establish that he personally drafted every sentence. ([Biblioteca Nacional de Chile, *Encuentros con el yanqui* bibliography](https://www.bibliotecanacional.gob.cl/sites/www.bibliotecanacional.gob.cl/files/2022-08/Encuentros%20con%20el%20yanqui.pdf))

- **11 March 1981:** *Discurso presidencial: 11 de marzo de 1981*. A separately published ceremonial address delivered at the opening of the presidential period established by the 1980 Constitution. It is a performative state text—an act of investiture and legitimation—as well as a speech. ([Memoria Chilena](https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/602/w3-propertyvalue-147051.html))

- **1995:** *Discursos principales, 1990–1994*. A retrospectively assembled volume of Pinochet’s addresses as commander-in-chief. The compilation is a manifestation of previously delivered speeches, and its editorial labor should not automatically be equated with sole literary authorship by the speaker. ([Memoria Chilena](https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/602/w3-article-31395.html); [Grafiati record](https://www.grafiati.com/en/literature-selections/augusto-pinochet/))

- **1995–1998:** *Discursos principales del Comandante en Jefe del Ejército*. A subsequent two-tome institutional compilation, sometimes cited simply as *Discursos principales 1995–1998*. It overlaps in title and function with the 1995 collection but covers a later period, so it is best treated as a continuing compilation project rather than silently collapsed into the earlier volume. ([Biblioteca Academia](https://catalogo.academia.cl/cgi-bin/koha/opac-detail.pl?biblionumber=12383&query_desc=); [Academia de Historia Militar](https://www.academiahistoriamilitar.cl/academia/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/TOMO-IV-Historia_digital.pdf))

### Interview-books and contested co-creations

- **1989; expanded 1996:** *Ego sum Pinochet*. A book of interviews conducted and shaped by journalists Raquel Correa and Elizabeth Subercaseaux; the later expanded edition gathers interviews from 1989–1993. Pinochet made the spoken answers, but the book as book is a collaborative editorial creation and should not be presented as his sole-authored prose. ([Memoria Chilena](https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/602/w3-article-31395.html); [Bibliographie Chili 1973–1988](https://referenciasbibliograficas.wordpress.com/2015/06/22/referencias-bibliograficas-chile-1973-a-1988-en-bibliotecas-francia/))

- **1999:** *Augusto Pinochet: diálogos con su historia: conversaciones inéditas*. María Eugenia Oyarzún’s selection of interviews with Pinochet conducted between 1995 and 1998. The testimony is his manifestation, but Oyarzún selected, organized and authored the interview-book; accordingly it resists classification as a work made by Pinochet alone. ([Museo de la Memoria y los Derechos Humanos](https://openbiblio.museodelamemoria.cl/shared/biblio_view.php?bibid=2807&tab=opac); [Biblioteca Nacional Digital de Chile](https://www.bibliotecanacionaldigital.gob.cl/bnd/628/w3-propertyvalue-967155.html))
