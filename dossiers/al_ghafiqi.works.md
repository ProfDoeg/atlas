## Works by Abd al-Rahman ibn Abd Allah al-Ghafiqi

### No securely attributed works

- **721–732:** *No surviving or documented obra*. The standard biographical scholarship identifies al-Ghafiqi as a military commander, governor, and traditionist, but records no authored text, manifesto, protocol, monument, or other independently attributable work. His governorships and campaigns were actions rather than titled works, so they are not converted into an artificial oeuvre here.

### Dubious architectural attribution

- **Before 732:** *Reconstruction of the bridge at Córdoba*. Some modern popular biographies credit al-Ghafiqi with building or strengthening Córdoba’s bridge, but the historical and architectural tradition assigns the early Islamic reconstruction to his predecessor al-Samh ibn Malik al-Khawlani, probably in 719–720; the structure itself was originally Roman. The attribution to al-Ghafiqi is therefore rejected as insecure, and the bridge resists classification as his obra.
