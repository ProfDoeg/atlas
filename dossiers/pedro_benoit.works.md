## Works by Pedro Simón del Corazón de Jesús Benoit

### Surveys, maps, and urban projects

- **1854:** *Nivelación y registro gráfico de terrenos de Buenos Aires*. A topographical survey rather than a building; a municipal historical resolution identifies it as Benoit’s first major undertaking. ([Municipality of Magdalena](https://sibom.slyt.gba.gob.ar/bulletins/5962.pdf))
- **1858:** *Estudios para la consolidación del Camino Blanco a Ensenada*. An engineering study and road-improvement project; the surviving accounts do not establish how much was executed. ([Temas de Patrimonio Cultural 8](https://www.buenosaires.gob.ar/areas/cultura/cpphc/archivos/libros/temas_8.pdf))
- **1859–1860:** *Trazas de Morón y Merlo*. Plans for organizing or extending two existing settlements, not wholly new cities; sources disagree by a year over the respective dates. ([Municipality of Magdalena](https://sibom.slyt.gba.gob.ar/bulletins/5962.pdf), [El Arcón de la Historia](https://elarcondelahistoria.com/benoit-pedro-1836/))
- **1860:** *Plano del pueblo y ejido de Magdalena*. A cadastral and urban plan fixing the town, common lands, and rural subdivisions; some later accounts date Benoit’s work there more generally to the 1860s. ([UNLP doctoral research](https://doczz.net/doc/2572489/documento-completo-descargar-archivo---sedici), [Municipality of Magdalena](https://sibom.slyt.gba.gob.ar/bulletins/5962.pdf))
- **1860s:** *Planos de pueblo y ejido para Azul, Bahía Blanca, San Fernando e Ituzaingó*. A group of surveying and settlement-ordering projects, not a single obra. The secondary record attributes these plans to Benoit but does not consistently preserve individual dates or distinguish new layouts from revisions. ([El Arcón de la Historia](https://elarcondelahistoria.com/benoit-pedro-1836/), [Municipality of Magdalena](https://sibom.slyt.gba.gob.ar/bulletins/5962.pdf))
- **1867:** *Plano de Buenos Aires*. A collaborative official survey made with other engineers; Benoit’s role was participation or direction, so it resists treatment as an individually authored work. ([Urbipedia](https://www.urbipedia.org/hoja/Pedro_Benoit))
- **1870:** *Estudio para un tranvía entre Tolosa y Ensenada*. An infrastructural project or feasibility study; the consulted sources do not show that the proposed line was built from Benoit’s design. ([Urbipedia](https://www.urbipedia.org/hoja/Pedro_Benoit))
- **1881–1882:** *Traza de la nueva capital de la provincia de Buenos Aires (La Plata)*. An urban Gesamtkunstwerk combining street geometry, parks, civic placement, circulation, sanitation, and territorial subdivision. It is traditionally called Benoit’s masterpiece, but the contemporary approval names the Departamento de Ingenieros collectively, not Benoit: Alberto de Paula and José María Rey therefore reject secure sole authorship, while a Benoit attribution first appears on a later version prepared for the 1889 Paris exposition. ([Alberto S. J. de Paula, summarized with documentary citations](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Benoit), [UNLP study of the attribution](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/54561/Documento_completo__.pdf?sequence=3))
- **1882–1885:** *Plan general de obras y servicios públicos de La Plata*. A coordinated civic-building and infrastructure program carried out by a departmental team under Benoit as director ejecutivo; it is a body of work and administrative project, not an individually drawable object. Attribution of particular buildings must consequently be separated from his supervision of the whole. ([UNLP, *Ingenieros y obras privadas*](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/73581/Documento_completo.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1), [Visita La Plata](https://www.visitalaplata.com.ar/2019/02/benoit-el-ingeniero-de-la-ciudad.html))
- **1884–1895:** *Traza del Cementerio de La Plata*. A “city of the dead” organized through streets, squares, diagonals, and burial plots; the cemetery opened in 1887, while a surviving Benoit plan titled *Cementerio de La Plata* is dated 1895. The differing dates describe design, opening, and surviving documentation rather than necessarily contradictory works. ([García, García, Flores and Viera](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/118258/Documento_completo.pdf-PDFA.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1))
- **Date not securely established:** *Traza del Cementerio de la Chacarita*. An urban-funerary planning attribution repeated in biographical surveys, but the consulted sources do not supply a securely dated or signed plan; it should remain an attribution rather than a settled work. ([Personalidades y Héroes Platenses](https://personalidadesplatenses.blogspot.com/))

### Hydraulic, transport, and military works

- **1855:** *Conversión del Fuerte Barragán en lazareto*. A partial reconstruction and adaptive reuse of the fort for quarantine purposes; Benoit’s precise share within the official work is not fully individualized. ([Temas de Patrimonio Cultural 8](https://www.buenosaires.gob.ar/areas/cultura/cpphc/archivos/libros/temas_8.pdf), [Municipality of Magdalena](https://sibom.slyt.gba.gob.ar/bulletins/5962.pdf))
- **Before 1863:** *Puentes de campaña*. A body of military and rural engineering works rather than one named object; available summaries say Benoit projected and built several but do not provide a complete itemized catalogue. ([Urbipedia](https://www.urbipedia.org/hoja/Pedro_Benoit), [El Arcón de la Historia](https://elarcondelahistoria.com/benoit-pedro-1836/))
- **Before 1863:** *Cuartel para cuatrocientos infantes y doscientos soldados de caballería*. A military-building project recorded in biographical scholarship without a stable title, site, or construction date; its realization status remains unclear. ([El Arcón de la Historia](https://elarcondelahistoria.com/benoit-pedro-1836/))
- **1863 onward:** *Rectificación y canalización del Riachuelo*. A hydraulic-engineering intervention rather than a discrete building. Sources credit Benoit with work on the project, but it formed part of a longer institutional process and should not be treated as his unaided creation. ([Visita La Plata](https://www.visitalaplata.com.ar/2019/02/benoit-el-ingeniero-de-la-ciudad.html), [Personalidades y Héroes Platenses](https://personalidadesplatenses.blogspot.com/))
- **Date not securely established:** *Defensas de Buenos Aires contra las inundaciones*. A body of hydraulic works performed through the Departamento de Ingenieros; no consulted oeuvre-level source resolves it into separately titled, individually authored objects. ([Urbipedia](https://www.urbipedia.org/hoja/Pedro_Benoit))

### Churches and cathedrals

- **c. 1860–1863:** *Iglesia de Nuestra Señora de la Merced, Merlo*. A parish church and one of Benoit’s earliest architectural designs; accounts variously associate the commission with the town plan around 1860 and date the building design to 1863. ([Urbipedia](https://www.urbipedia.org/hoja/Pedro_Benoit), [Pedro Benoit biography](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Benoit))
- **1876:** *Torres de la Iglesia de San Pedro Telmo, Buenos Aires*. Two additions to an older Jesuit church, not authorship of the church as a whole. The Buenos Aires heritage account explicitly attributes the towers to Benoit. ([Buenos Aires City](https://buenosaires.gob.ar/gcaba_historico/parroquia-san-pedro-telmo))
- **1876:** *Iglesia de San Vicente Ferrer, San Vicente*. A church project inaugurated in 1876 and attributed to Benoit; later enlargement and alteration mean that the present fabric is not simply identical with his design. ([Espacio & Confort](https://espacioyconfort.com.ar/amp/2404/un-templo-con-historia))
- **1883–1889:** *Iglesia de San Ponciano, La Plata*. A neo-Gothic church designed and directed by Benoit: the original single-nave chapel began in 1883, its tower followed in 1885, and later enlargement produced the present three-nave building. It is therefore a layered manifestation of his design rather than an untouched object. ([UNLP architectural inventory](https://host170.sedici.unlp.edu.ar/server/api/core/bitstreams/5aaab638-75a6-4b6f-9edc-028d087ba7fb/content), [CIC heritage study](https://digital.cic.gba.gob.ar/server/api/core/bitstreams/5a36011f-c9fc-405d-a659-b3d3e29f7f3b/content))
- **1884/1885–1897:** *Catedral de la Inmaculada Concepción de La Plata*. A monumental neo-Gothic cathedral project associated jointly with Benoit and Ernesto Meyer; Benoit subsequently directed the work. Construction continued long after both the initial design and Benoit’s death, so the finished cathedral cannot be equated without qualification with the nineteenth-century project. ([Visita La Plata](https://www.visitalaplata.com.ar/2019/02/benoit-el-ingeniero-de-la-ciudad.html), [Temas de Patrimonio Cultural 8](https://buenosaires.gob.ar/areas/cultura/cpphc/archivos/libros/temas_8.pdf))
- **1890:** *Iglesia de Nuestra Señora de Luján, Uribelarrea*. A neo-Gothic church attributed to Benoit and inaugurated on 14 July 1890. It is distinct from the great Basilica of Luján, with which summaries of Benoit’s work sometimes confuse it. ([Pedro Benoit biography](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Benoit))
- **1879–1891:** *Iglesia San Luis Gonzaga, Mercedes*. A church attributed to Benoit, built over an extended period and inaugurated in 1891; its long execution makes the inauguration date an endpoint, not necessarily the design date. ([Mercedes municipal history summarized here](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes_(Buenos_Aires)))
- **1893–1897:** *Iglesia de San Pedro y Santa Cecilia, Mar del Plata*. A neo-Gothic church project made with Emilio Coutaret and directed by Benoit; its roof was sufficiently complete for worship in February 1897, shortly before Benoit’s death, while later construction and elevation to basilica and cathedral transformed its institutional identity. ([Emilio Coutaret biography and attribution](https://www.urbipedia.org/hoja/Emilio_Coutaret), [0223 Mar del Plata](https://www.0223.com.ar/nota/2020-11-22-8-4-0-pedro-benoit-el-ingeniero-que-proyecto-la-obra-de-la-catedral-sin-cobrar-un-centavo))
- **Dates not securely established:** *Churches at Santa Catalina, San Justo, Moreno, Ensenada, Benito Juárez, and Azul*. These constitute an attributed body of ecclesiastical work, often counted among roughly ten churches assigned to Benoit. The consulted synthesis does not consistently give each project a formal title, date, documentary basis, or distinguish design from direction, so they resist conversion into falsely precise individual entries. ([El Arcón de la Historia](https://elarcondelahistoria.com/benoit-pedro-1836/), [Pedro Benoit biography](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Benoit))
- **Date not securely established:** *Catedral de Gualeguay*. An attributed cathedral project outside Buenos Aires Province; the accessible scholarly summary names it but does not establish a date or the limits of Benoit’s participation. ([UNLP, *Proyectarse* 51](https://ing.unlp.edu.ar/wp-content/uploads/acordeon/multimedia/revistas/020_Proyectarse/051_Proyectarse%20Nro%20051%20%28Nov%202000%29.pdf))
- **Attribution disputed or confused:** *Basílica de Nuestra Señora de Luján*. Some biographical records attribute a project to Benoit, but the claim conflicts with the better-documented history of the present basilica and may conflate it with the church at Uribelarrea. It should not be accepted as a secure realized Benoit work without the underlying plan. ([Buenos Aires heritage record](https://buenosaires.gob.ar/areas/cultura/cpphc/masoneria/fichas/BOV3_BENOIT.pdf), [Pedro Benoit biography](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Benoit))

### Civic, institutional, and domestic architecture outside La Plata

- **1855:** *Mercado Central de Buenos Aires*. A market-building project attributed to the young Benoit; the source gives neither a stable site identification nor enough evidence to equate it safely with later buildings bearing the same generic name. ([Municipality of Magdalena](https://sibom.slyt.gba.gob.ar/bulletins/5962.pdf))
- **1871:** *Asilo de Huérfanos, Buenos Aires*. An orphanage building attributed to Benoit and subsequently lost to fire; “Asilo de Huérfanos” functions as a descriptive institutional name rather than necessarily an original architectural title. ([ArchDaily, “Un incendio en la memoria”](https://www.archdaily.com/es/916276/un-incendio-en-la-memoria-reflexiones-sobre-la-pérdida-del-patrimonio-arquitectónico-en-argentina))
- **1874:** *Edificio de la Facultad de Derecho de la Universidad de Buenos Aires*. An academic building now occupied by the Museo Etnográfico Juan B. Ambrosetti; the Buenos Aires heritage guide attributes its construction to Benoit. ([Buenos Aires Heritage Guide](https://turismo.buenosaires.gob.ar/sites/turismo/files/751%20-%20Guia%20de%20Patrimonio_Parte%203.pdf))
- **1879–1881:** *Remodelación del Cabildo de Buenos Aires*. A transformation of an existing colonial building, including alterations subsequently undone or demolished; it is therefore an intervention rather than authorship of the Cabildo itself. ([Universidad Nacional de La Matanza bibliographic record for the specialist study](https://pergamo.unlam.edu.ar/pergamo/documento.php?id=PERGAMO.1.54301&recno=54301&ui=1), [Personalidades y Héroes Platenses](https://personalidadesplatenses.blogspot.com/))
- **Date not securely established:** *Municipalidad de San Isidro*. A civic building assigned to Benoit in architectural histories; the consulted synthesis does not provide a reliable design or construction date. ([Jorge Glusberg, *Breve historia de la arquitectura argentina*](https://architecture-history.org/books/Breve%20historia%20de%20la%20arquitectura%20Argentina.pdf))
- **Date not securely established:** *Municipalidad de Ensenada*. A municipal building attribution preserved in surveys of Benoit’s work, without a securely established date in the consulted sources. ([UNLP, *Proyectarse* 51](https://ing.unlp.edu.ar/wp-content/uploads/acordeon/multimedia/revistas/020_Proyectarse/051_Proyectarse%20Nro%20051%20%28Nov%202000%29.pdf))
- **After 1852; date not securely established:** *Ampliación de la Casa Benoit, Independencia y Bolívar, Buenos Aires*. An alteration by Pedro to the modest house originally built by his father Pierre; the remaining fabric was illegally demolished in 2008, and archaeological evidence rather than the building now carries much of the work’s identity. ([Instituto de Arte Americano](https://www.iaa.fadu.uba.ar/cau/?p=1779))

### Public architecture in La Plata

- **1883:** *Ministerio de Hacienda*. A provincial office building projected within the founding campaign and later demolished. Its disappearance, and Benoit’s simultaneous role as director of public works, make the boundary between personal design and departmental production especially important. ([Pedro Benoit biography](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Benoit), [El Arcón de la Historia](https://elarcondelahistoria.com/benoit-pedro-1836/))
- **1883:** *Cuarteles de Policía y Bomberos y Cárcel*. A combined security, emergency-service, and penal complex rather than a single-purpose building; attributed to Benoit within the founding works of La Plata. ([Pedro Benoit biography](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Benoit))
- **1883:** *Edificio del Departamento de Ingenieros*. An administrative building for the institution through which much of Benoit’s oeuvre was collectively produced. It is attributed to him, but that institutional setting makes purely individual authorship difficult to isolate. ([UNLP, *Ingenieros y obras privadas*](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/73581/Documento_completo.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1), [Pedro Benoit biography](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Benoit))
- **1883:** *Edificio administrativo del Observatorio Astronómico*. The administrative component of a larger scientific complex, not necessarily the whole observatory; biographical lists attribute this building to Benoit. ([Pedro Benoit biography](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Benoit))
- **1884:** *Arco de entrada del Paseo del Bosque*. A ceremonial urban threshold, subsequently demolished; sources sometimes say Benoit “directed” rather than designed it, so the nature of his authorship is not completely settled. ([Pedro Benoit biography](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Benoit), [El Arcón de la Historia](https://elarcondelahistoria.com/benoit-pedro-1836/))
- **1884–1890:** *Pórtico y frente del Cementerio de La Plata*. A Doric, symbolically charged architectural boundary to the cemetery; substantial portions were demolished during 1970s alterations. Research distinguishes Benoit as projector and builder of the entrance from the broader, evolving cemetery plan. ([García, García, Flores and Viera](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/118258/Documento_completo.pdf-PDFA.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1))
- **1886:** *Residencia de Dardo Rocha, La Plata*. A private house, now the Museo Dardo Rocha; Benoit prepared its plans, while master builder Fernando Cerdeña carried out the construction. ([Museo Dardo Rocha history](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museo_Dardo_Rocha))
- **Date not securely established:** *Ministerio de Gobierno*. A provincial office building attributed to Benoit and subsequently demolished; no secure project date appears in the consulted architectural summaries. ([Pedro Benoit biography](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Benoit))
- **Date not securely established:** *Mercado Central de La Plata*. A market building attributed within Benoit’s public works and now demolished. It should not be confused with the earlier Buenos Aires market carrying the same generic designation. ([El Arcón de la Historia](https://elarcondelahistoria.com/benoit-pedro-1836/))
- **Date not securely established:** *Asilo Marín*. A charitable institution realized from, or adapted out of, a project by Émile Vaudremer; Benoit’s contribution was therefore mediation, adaptation, or direction rather than unqualified original authorship. ([Pedro Benoit biography](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Benoit))
- **Date not securely established:** *Escuela de Artes y Oficios*. An educational building attributed to Benoit in the founding-era corpus; the accessible sources do not resolve its date, later alterations, or the division between design and supervision. ([Pedro Benoit biography](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Benoit))
- **Date not securely established:** *Matadero municipal*. A utilitarian slaughterhouse complex attributed to Benoit; it belongs to the civic-service system of the new city rather than to monumental architecture. ([Pedro Benoit biography](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Benoit))
- **Date not securely established:** *Hospital Melchor Romero*. A hospital project or intervention associated with Benoit in surveys of his La Plata work; the consulted sources do not establish that the whole surviving complex is his individual design. ([La obra de Pedro Benoit](https://danieleugeniopena.blogspot.com/2023/02/la-obra-de-pedro-benoit.html))

### Emblem and documentary corpus

- **c. 1890–1891:** *Escudo de la ciudad de La Plata*. A civic emblem rather than a building or literary text; Benoit drew or introduced the design while serving in the municipal government. Sources do not consistently separate the date of drawing from the date of municipal adoption. ([El Arcón de la Historia](https://elarcondelahistoria.com/benoit-pedro-1836/), [La obra de Pedro Benoit](https://danieleugeniopena.blogspot.com/2023/02/la-obra-de-pedro-benoit.html))
- **1850–1897:** *Approximately 1,800 architectural, engineering, surveying, and urban plans*. This is an archival body of work, reportedly preserved in part by Benoit’s descendants, not one obra and not a published catalogue raisonné. Because many sheets concern private houses, anonymous departmental work, unrealized projects, or lost buildings, the corpus actively resists conversion into a reliable list of 1,800 independent “works”; the figure itself is variously described as plans, projects, or buildings. ([Temas de Patrimonio Cultural 8](https://www.buenosaires.gob.ar/areas/cultura/cpphc/archivos/libros/temas_8.pdf), [Visita La Plata](https://www.visitalaplata.com.ar/2019/02/benoit-el-ingeniero-de-la-ciudad.html), [Personalidades y Héroes Platenses](https://personalidadesplatenses.blogspot.com/))
