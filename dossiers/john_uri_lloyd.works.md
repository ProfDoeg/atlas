## Works by John Uri Lloyd

### Fictional obras

- **1895:** *Etidorhpa; or, The End of Earth: The Strange History of a Mysterious Being and the Account of a Remarkable Journey*. A philosophical hollow-earth novel, scientific romance, and illustrated book-object made with artist J. Augustus Knapp; Lloyd’s archive preserves variant prefaces, excised chapters, maps, manuscripts, and publishing apparatus, so the obra exceeds any single edition. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- **1897:** *The Right Side of the Car*. A short railway narrative issued as an illustrated book; the archive preserves five drafts and excluded matter, making the published text one manifestation of a larger compositional project. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- **1900:** *Stringtown on the Pike: A Tale of Northernmost Kentucky*. A regional novel first developed under the working titles *Cupe and Dinah* and *The Dead Chemist*; its manuscript, dialect ledger, illustrations, and unrealized film negotiations make it a multimedia project as well as a book. Some catalogues give 1901 because that was the principal book-market manifestation. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- **1901:** *Warwick of the Knobs: A Story of Stringtown County, Kentucky*. A regional novel about northern Kentucky feud culture; its surviving drafts, research clippings, proofs, and specially crafted manuscript box form a documentary ensemble around the printed obra. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- **1903:** *Red-Head*. An illustrated regional novel, with decorations by Reginald B. Birch; Lloyd’s papers preserve an unpublished chapter, “The Meeting of Sammy Drew and Red-Head,” so the printed book does not exhaust the project. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- **1904:** *Scroggins*. A regional novel or novella; the archive identifies two manuscript parts, while some secondary lists incorrectly date it 1900. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- **ca. 1900–1934:** *Our Willie*. A long fictional project represented by early and late manuscripts, forty-four chapters, a prologue, alternative epilogues, and a preliminary version titled *The Haunted House*. Its publication status is unclear in the archival description, and it therefore resists classification as a finished book. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- **ca. 1900–1930:** *Felix Moses: The Beloved Jew of Stringtown on the Pike*. A historical-regional novel begun around 1900 and substantially remade before its 1930 publication; Lloyd combined fiction, remembrance, correspondence, memorial research, and illustrations by J. Augustus Knapp. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)

### The Sam Hill story-cycle

- *The Mother of Sam Hill’s Wife’s Sister*. A humorous Kentucky story surviving in manuscript and draft; no secure composition or publication date is supplied by the archival inventory. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- **1914 or earlier:** *The Wafted Wife of Sam Hill*. A humorous tale preserved in manuscript and in a 1914 revision; the date of its first form is not fixed. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- **1902 or earlier:** *The Wonderful Catostomus of Kentucky*. A Sam Hill story represented by a manuscript and a corrected 1902 draft. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- **1902 or earlier:** *The Pedigree of Sam Hill’s Wafted Wife*. A story-project surviving in multiple drafts, including two dated 1902. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- *The Quietest Election in the United States Was Held in Kaintuck, Sah*. An early draft of a humorous political story; its incomplete archival form resists treatment as a settled published work. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- **1902:** *Sam Hill, Sheriff of Knowlton, Kaintuck*. A humorous story preserved as a corrected draft. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- **1902–1934:** *Sam Hill in Literature* / *Sam Hill the Literary Man*. A repeatedly revised story-project documented by several 1902 manuscripts and a 1934 illustrated draft; the shifting title and versions prevent identification of one definitive text. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- **1903 or earlier:** *The Size of Daniel Boone*. A humorous historical story preserved in manuscript, corrected draft, and proof sheets for *The Criterion* of January 1903. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- *The Size of the Pilgrim Fathers*. A Sam Hill story surviving under this title and the variant *Size of the Pilgrims*; no secure date is given. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- *The Passing Over of Captain Sam Hill*. A story manuscript of uncertain date and publication status. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- *Why a Kentuckian Stands with His Back to the Stove*. A humorous story represented by multiple archival copies. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- *The Testing of Melinda*. A story surviving as a proof associated archivally with *Why a Kentuckian Stands with His Back to the Stove*; whether it is autonomous or part of that project is not settled by the finding aid. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- *The Wonderful Salve of Cap’n Sam Hill*. A humorous story manuscript of uncertain date. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- *The Two-Horned Rooster*. A tale attributed within the fiction to “Chinney Bill Smith”; that persona is part of Lloyd’s story-world rather than securely established separate authorship. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- *The Double-Spurred Hen*. Another “Chinney Bill Smith” tale within Lloyd’s manuscript corpus; its date and publication status are unrecorded in the finding aid. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- *The Many-Hammed Pig Can’t Fool Them Yankees*. A “Chinney Bill Smith” story preserved in the Sam Hill papers; title wording follows the archival inventory. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- *Did Aunt Patsy Die?* A manuscript catalogued with the uncertain or malformed heading “Grotesquely Abaresque”; the bibliographic identity of the text is unresolved. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- *Defeat of Chinny Bill Smith*. A story manuscript of uncertain date; the archive’s “Chinny” spelling differs from its “Chinney” elsewhere. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- *Why Henry Clay Voted for John Quincy Adams*. A humorous historical story preserved in manuscript. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- *Chimney Bill Smith’s Last Story*. A story surviving in two manuscript files; “Chimney” may be a variant or cataloguing error for “Chinney,” and should not be silently regularized. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)

### Pharmaceutical books, protocols, and reference works

- **1879:** *A Pronouncing Medical Dictionary*. A professional reference work rather than a narrative text; a contemporary institutional history includes it among Lloyd’s technical books. [Caswell A. Mayo, *The Lloyd Library and Its Makers*](https://cdm16998.contentdm.oclc.org/digital/api/collection/p16998coll15/id/511398/download)
- **1880:** *Supplement to the American Dispensatory*. A collaborative reference-work continuation made with John King; Lloyd’s role is editorial and supplemental rather than sole authorship. [Biographical bibliography](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Uri_Lloyd)
- **1881:** *The Chemistry of Medicines, Practical*. A chemistry textbook and reference book for pharmacists, physicians, and students; later editions are manifestations of this obra, not additional works. [Google Books bibliographic record](https://books.google.com/books/about/The_Chemistry_of_Medicines_Practical.html?id=6Vs3AAAAMAAJ)
- **1883:** *Pharmaceutical Preparations: Elixirs, Their History, Formulae, and Methods of Preparation*. A historical study and practical protocol collection for compounding elixirs; the enlarged 1892 *Elixirs and Flavoring Extracts* represents a developed manifestation rather than an unrelated work. [Google Books bibliographic record](https://books.google.com/books/about/Pharmaceutical_Preparations.html?id=LBJKAQAAMAAJ)
- **1884–1887:** *Drugs and Medicines of North America*. A serial pharmacognostic project produced with Curtis Gates Lloyd, combining botany, pharmacy, chemistry, therapeutics, and historical bibliography; it is collaborative and was left incomplete after two volumes. [Publication history](https://www.henriettes-herb.com/eclectic/dmna/bulletins.html)
- **1892:** *Elixirs and Flavoring Extracts: Their History, Formulae, and Methods of Preparation*. An expanded practical-historical compounding manual whose archival copyright survives; bibliographies disagree when they collapse it into earlier editions of *Pharmaceutical Preparations*. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- **1897:** *Veratrum Viride*. A pharmacognostic monograph on American hellebore, functioning simultaneously as scientific study and manufacturing literature. [Biographical bibliography](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Uri_Lloyd)
- **1898:** *A Systematic Treatise on Materia Medica and Therapeutics*. A collaborative medical reference work made with Finley Ellingwood; attribution to Lloyd alone would be misleading. [Biographical bibliography](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Uri_Lloyd)
- **1898–1900:** *A Study in Pharmacy*. A large research project on pharmaceutical physics rather than a conventionally completed book; archival manuscripts include “Physics in Pharmacy,” “Molecular Physics—Concerning Pharmacists,” and a separately issued capillarity chapter. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- **1902:** *References to Capillarity to the End of the Year 1900*. Chapter VII of *A Study in Pharmacy*, assembled with Sigmund Waldbott; it is both an autonomous bibliographic tool and a manifestation of the unfinished larger project. [Online Books Page](https://onlinebooks.library.upenn.edu/webbin/book/lookupname?key=Lloyd%2C+John+Uri%2C+1849-1936)
- **1898–1906:** *King’s American Dispensatory*. Lloyd served with Harvey Wickes Felter on the extensively revised eighteenth and nineteenth editions of John King’s dispensatory; this is a collaborative editorial reconstruction, not an originally authored book in the simple sense. [Biographical bibliography](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Uri_Lloyd)
- **1908:** *Hydrastis Canadensis*. A documentary pharmacognostic bulletin that reprinted and illustrated the earlier *Drugs and Medicines of North America* treatment; as “Reproduction Series No. 6,” it deliberately blurs new obra and archival manifestation. [Lloyd Library bulletin list](https://www.henriettes-herb.com/eclectic/dmna/bulletins.html)
- **1909:** *Life and Medical Discoveries of Samuel Thomson, and a History of the Thomsonian Materia Medica*. A medical biography, documentary history, and historiographical recovery project issued as Lloyd Library Reproduction Series No. 7. [Lloyd Library bulletin list](https://www.henriettes-herb.com/eclectic/dmna/bulletins.html)
- **1910:** *The Eclectic Alkaloids, Resins, Resinoids, Oleo-Resins, and Concentrated Principles*. A history and technical study of eclectic pharmaceutical preparations, issued as Lloyd Library Pharmacy Series No. 2. [Lloyd Library bulletin list](https://www.henriettes-herb.com/eclectic/dmna/bulletins.html)
- **1911:** *History of the Vegetable Drugs of the Pharmacopeia of the United States*. A historical-pharmacognostic reference work prepared in connection with the United States Pharmacopeia; Joseph P. Remington and Charles Rice are also associated with its bibliographic record, so sole authorship should not be presumed without qualification. [Biodiversity Heritage Library](https://field.biodiversitylibrary.org/creator/3882)
- **1911:** *A Treatise on Apis (the Bee), Tella Araneae (Cobweb), Spongia and Cantharis*. A materia-medica treatise on animal-derived substances, catalogued as Lloyd’s work by the Biodiversity Heritage Library. [Biodiversity Heritage Library](https://field.biodiversitylibrary.org/creator/3882)
- **1916:** *Pharmazeutische Studien*. A German-language manifestation of Lloyd’s pharmaceutical studies; whether it constitutes a separately remade obra or primarily a translated compilation is not resolved by the available bibliographic record. [Online Books Page](https://onlinebooks.library.upenn.edu/webbin/book/lookupname?key=Lloyd%2C+John+Uri%2C+1849-1936)
- **1921:** *Origin and History of All the Pharmacopeial Vegetable Drugs, Chemicals and Preparations, with Bibliography, Volume I: Vegetable Drugs*. A commissioned historical reference work issued under the auspices of the American Drug Manufacturers’ Association; only the vegetable-drug volume appeared, so the titled whole is an unfinished project. [Biodiversity Heritage Library](https://field.biodiversitylibrary.org/creator/3882)
- **1931:** *Quality versus Quantity*. A separately issued professional address or essay rather than a full-scale monograph. [Online Books Page](https://onlinebooks.library.upenn.edu/webbin/book/lookupname?key=Lloyd%2C+John+Uri%2C+1849-1936)

### Reproduction projects

- *Collection for an Essay Towards a Materia Medica of the United States*. Lloyd’s annotated or editorial reproduction of Benjamin Smith Barton’s 1798 and 1804 text, issued as Lloyd Library Reproduction Series No. 1; the historical text is Barton’s, while the recovery project is Lloyd’s. [Lloyd Library bulletin list](https://www.henriettes-herb.com/eclectic/dmna/bulletins.html)
- *The Indian Doctor’s Dispensatory*. Lloyd’s reproduction of Peter Smith’s 1813 work, issued as Reproduction Series No. 2; this is an editorial manifestation, not Lloyd authorship of the underlying text. [Lloyd Library bulletin list](https://www.henriettes-herb.com/eclectic/dmna/bulletins.html)
- *Materia Medica Americana Potissimum Regni Vegetabilis*. Lloyd’s reproduction of Johann David Schöpf’s 1787 work, issued as Reproduction Series No. 3. [Lloyd Library bulletin list](https://www.henriettes-herb.com/eclectic/dmna/bulletins.html)
- *An Account of Some of the Vegetable Productions Naturally Growing in This Part of America*. Lloyd’s reproduction of Manasseh Cutler’s historical botanical text, issued as Reproduction Series No. 4. [Lloyd Library bulletin list](https://www.henriettes-herb.com/eclectic/dmna/bulletins.html)
- *An Investigation of the Properties of the Sanguinaria Canadensis or Puccoon*. Lloyd’s reproduction project centered on William Downey’s study and associated early North American medical texts, issued as Reproduction Series No. 5; it is a constructed documentary assemblage rather than an original treatise by Lloyd. [Lloyd Library bulletin list](https://www.henriettes-herb.com/eclectic/dmna/bulletins.html)

- **1899:** *Vial or Medicine Bottle*, U.S. Patent 628,269. A patented pharmaceutical container and dispensing object. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)

- **1907:** *Process of Purifying Volatile Substances*, U.S. Patent 869,375. A patented chemical protocol rather than a freestanding machine. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- **1908:** *Concentrator for Solutions*, U.S. Patent 891,492. A further iteration in Lloyd’s continuing apparatus project. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- **1912:** *Alkaloidal Substance*, U.S. Patent 1,048,711. A patented chemical composition; the made thing is a substance defined through a legal-technical text. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- **1917:** *Alkaloidal Compound*, U.S. Patent 1,250,331. A patented pharmaceutical composition. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- **1919:** *Process of Extracting, Purifying, or Excluding Alkaloids and Alkaloidal Salts*, U.S. Patent 1,300,747. - **1920:** *Percolating and Concentrating Apparatus*, U.S. Patent 1,332,908. A patented pharmaceutical extraction machine. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)

- *Miscellaneous evaporators and condensers*. A group of additional patented or patent-related devices that the finding aid does not individually name; their resistance to itemization reflects the archive’s level of description, not evidence that they did not exist. ### Pharmaceutical preparations and manufacturing oeuvre

- **1870–1938:** *Lloyd Brothers Pharmacists’ Specific Medicines*. A collaborative manufacturing oeuvre developed by John Uri Lloyd with his brothers and their firm, eventually comprising 379 named preparations; these are material formulas and commodities, not “books,” and the archival overview does not assign every formula solely to John. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- *Lloyd’s Reagent for Precipitating Alkaloids*. A laboratory reagent and analytical method documented in Lloyd’s pharmaceutical manuscripts; it is a chemical tool whose identity lies in reproducible preparation and use. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- *Eldrin*. A pharmaceutical-formula project preserved in Lloyd’s research files; the archival description gives formulas but does not establish a single finished commercial object or date. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- *Glyconda / Neutralizing Mixture*. A pharmaceutical preparation whose discovery Lloyd documented; the finding aid does not fix its date or settle whether the commercial mixture and the manuscript account should be treated as one obra. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)

### Research projects, notebooks, and manuscript texts

- **1866–1917:** *Laboratory Notebooks*. A series of experimental records covering apprenticeship work, vaporization, capillarity, caffeine, alkaloidal assays, Fuller’s earth, and related investigations; this is a research corpus, not one authored “book.” [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- **1860–1906:** *Diaries, expense books, prescription notebooks, and address books*. A heterogeneous life-writing and working archive; its components resist being treated as a single obra, yet they are made textual objects rather than mere publications about Lloyd. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- **1867–1936:** *Autobiographical writings and reminiscences*. A dispersed manuscript project encompassing childhood, family, travel, pharmacy, politics, war taxation, Grover Cleveland, Sir Henry Irving, Cincinnati, and specific medication; no authorized collected form or stable collective title exists. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- **1879–1885:** *Physics in Pharmacy*. An experimental manuscript project surviving in corrected and incomplete versions; it may be part of *A Study in Pharmacy*, and treating it as a wholly independent book would overstate the evidence. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- *Molecular Physics—Concerning Pharmacists*. A multipart manuscript chapter associated with Lloyd’s pharmacy studies; its archival form suggests an unfinished component rather than a settled publication. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- *Experiments in Connection with the Formation of Dew*. An experimental paper surviving in multiple drafts derived from original notes; the finding aid does not supply a secure date or publication status. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- **ca. 1880:** *Pharmacy Lecture Notes*. A teaching protocol or pedagogical working text, not necessarily intended as a published obra. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- **1898–1902:** *Capillarity research*. A sustained experimental project embodied in notebooks, charts, articles, apparatus, and the bibliographic chapter prepared with Sigmund Waldbott; its distributed form resists separation into a single medium. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- **1906:** *Ottoman Empire journey archive*. A research-and-travel assemblage of correspondence, reports, notes, shipping records, an imported olive-oil bottle with Lloyd’s label, and writings on Turkish drugs and sites; it is closer to a documentary project than a finished travel book. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)

### Institutional and bibliographical Gesamtkunstwerke

- **1870s–1938:** *Lloyd Brothers Pharmacists*. A collaborative enterprise built by John Uri Lloyd and his brothers from laboratory research, machinery, formulas, packaging, publications, advertising, and commercial organization; because it joins texts, substances, technologies, and institutional practice, it is best understood as a pharmaceutical Gesamtkunstwerk rather than a single work by John alone. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)
- **late nineteenth century–1919:** *The Lloyd Library*. A research collection assembled by John Uri, Curtis Gates, and Nelson Ashley Lloyd and placed in trust in 1919; John made it through collecting, bibliography, reproduction publishing, and institutional design, but sole authorship would erase his brothers’ constitutive work. [Lloyd Library, Books and Serials](https://lloydlibrary.org/research/books-and-serials/)
- **1900s–1910s:** *Bulletin of the Lloyd Library of Botany, Pharmacy and Materia Medica*. A serial publishing project encompassing reproductions, pharmacy studies, and Curtis Gates Lloyd’s mycological works; John acted as author, editor, collector, and institutional maker, but not every bulletin is his text. [Lloyd Library bulletin list](https://www.henriettes-herb.com/eclectic/dmna/bulletins.html)
- **1890s–1936:** *Indexes to John Uri Lloyd’s published articles*. An authorial-bibliographical control project preserved in the archive; it points to hundreds or thousands of writings but is itself an index rather than a conventional literary work. [Lloyd Library finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf)

### Sources

- William Black and Rose Marie Weckenmann, [*John Uri Lloyd Papers, 1849–1936: Finding Aid, MSS #1*](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/John-Uri-Lloyd-Papers-Finding-Aid.pdf), Lloyd Library and Museum. This is the principal archival inventory used here; it covers manuscripts, notebooks, patents, fiction, research files, and published articles.
- Corinne Miller Simons, *John Uri Lloyd: His Life and His Works, 1849–1936, with a History of the Lloyd Library* (1972). This remains the standard biographical chronicle, but later scholarship notes that it is not a complete analytical bibliography; [bibliographical assessment](https://www.encyclopedia.com/science/dictionaries-thesauruses-pictures-and-press-releases/lloyd-john-uri).
- Michael A. Flannery, *John Uri Lloyd: The Great American Eclectic* (Southern Illinois University Press, 1998), the standard scholarly biography; its documented research basis is described in the [John Uri Lloyd Biography Project finding aid](https://lloydlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/john_uri_lloyd_biography_project.pdf).
- Caswell A. Mayo, [*The Lloyd Library and Its Makers: An Historical Sketch*](https://cdm16998.contentdm.oclc.org/digital/api/collection/p16998coll15/id/511398/download), used for the contemporary enumeration of Lloyd’s technical books and editorial work.
- Lloyd Library and Museum, [“Books and Serials”](https://lloydlibrary.org/research/books-and-serials/), used for the history and material scope of the institutional collection.
- [“Publications Issued by the Lloyd Library”](https://www.henriettes-herb.com/eclectic/dmna/bulletins.html), a transcription of the contemporary bulletin list, used to distinguish John Uri Lloyd’s pharmacy and reproduction series from Curtis Gates Lloyd’s mycological series.
- Biodiversity Heritage Library, [John Uri Lloyd creator record](https://field.biodiversitylibrary.org/creator/3882), used for the authorship and collaborative status of the pharmacopeial histories and the *Treatise on Apis*.
- University of Pennsylvania, [Online Books Page: John Uri Lloyd](https://onlinebooks.library.upenn.edu/webbin/book/lookupname?key=Lloyd%2C+John+Uri%2C+1849-1936), used selectively to verify separately issued technical texts, not as the basis of the list.
- No complete published bibliography of Lloyd’s writings is known; a scientific-biographical reference states that they number in the thousands. Consequently, the named books, projects, inventions, archival literary works, and materially distinct bodies above are traceable, but the list cannot honestly claim article-level completeness. [Dictionary of Scientific Biography assessment](https://www.encyclopedia.com/science/dictionaries-thesauruses-pictures-and-press-releases/lloyd-john-uri)
