## Works by Jean-Baptiste Joseph Fourier

### Treatises and book-scale projects

- **1807–1822:** *Théorie analytique de la chaleur*. A mathematical-physics project developed from the memoir submitted to the Institut in 1807 and recast as the 1822 treatise; its chapters form one obra, not a collection of independent books. [Darboux’s edition](https://books.google.com/books?id=Xs0EAAAAYAAJ) and the [University of Grenoble’s critical presentation](https://www.univ-irem.fr/fourier) document its long composition.
- **1830–1831:** *Analyse des équations déterminées, première partie*. An unfinished treatise-project on numerical equations, left in manuscript at Fourier’s death and edited posthumously by Claude-Louis-Marie-Henri Navier; its incompleteness is part of the object rather than a missing second publication. [Darboux’s bibliography](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/oeuvres-de-fourier/liste-des-ouvrages-scientifiques-de-fourier/C45C54373B389630970BB5FCB03A9FA5) calls it posthumous and unfinished.

### Memoirs and notes on heat

- **1807–1808:** *Mémoire sur la propagation de la chaleur dans les corps solides*. A research memoir presented to the Institut on 21 December 1807; only an extract appeared in 1808, while the full manuscript, with notes of 1808–1809, remained at the École des Ponts et Chaussées. It therefore resists identification with either its short printed manifestation or the later treatise. [Darboux, vol. II](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/oeuvres-de-fourier/9CA4D3AB013D089C7FAA059BB54A9E67).
- **1808–1809:** *Notes présentées en complément du Mémoire sur la propagation de la chaleur*. Manuscript supplements attached to the 1807 memoir; Darboux catalogued them as surviving archival material rather than autonomous publications. [Darboux’s bibliography](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/oeuvres-de-fourier/liste-des-ouvrages-scientifiques-de-fourier/C45C54373B389630970BB5FCB03A9FA5).
- **1816:** *Théorie de la chaleur*. A journal memoir in the *Annales de chimie et de physique*, distinct from—but part of the development toward—the 1822 treatise. [Darboux’s bibliography](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/oeuvres-de-fourier/liste-des-ouvrages-scientifiques-de-fourier/C45C54373B389630970BB5FCB03A9FA5).
- **1817:** *Note sur la chaleur rayonnante*. A short physical note on radiant heat, not a book or a restatement of the complete heat theory. [Darboux, vol. II contents](https://books.google.com/books?id=Wzu-Ic-kfEEC).
- **1817:** *Questions sur la théorie physique de la chaleur rayonnante*. An interrogative research text setting out problems and principles concerning radiation. [Darboux, vol. II contents](https://books.google.com/books?id=Wzu-Ic-kfEEC).
- **1817:** *Rapport à l’Institut sur un mémoire de M. Despretz relatif au refroidissement de plusieurs métaux*. An evaluative institutional report preserved in manuscript; its subject was Despretz’s work, so it is Fourier’s act of assessment rather than his experimental memoir. [Darboux’s bibliography](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/oeuvres-de-fourier/liste-des-ouvrages-scientifiques-de-fourier/C45C54373B389630970BB5FCB03A9FA5).
- **1818:** *Mémoire sur la température des habitations et sur le mouvement varié de la chaleur dans les prismes rectangulaires*. A combined mathematical and practical investigation known in print through an extract; the surviving printed object is explicitly not the whole memoir. [Darboux edition entry](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/oeuvres-de-fourier/memoire-sur-la-temperature-des-habitations-et-sur-le-mouvement-varie-de-la-chaleur-dans-les-prismes-rectangulaires-extrait/49D5A2D77D14296133DC5E0BCFE1CDAC).
- **1819–1820:** *Extrait d’un mémoire sur le refroidissement séculaire du globe terrestre*. A published extract concerning the Earth’s long-term cooling; the title itself marks it as a manifestation of a larger memoir, not necessarily the complete work. [Darboux’s bibliography and edition](https://books.google.com/books?id=Wzu-Ic-kfEEC).
- **1820:** *Sur le mouvement de la chaleur dans une sphère solide dont le rayon est très grand*. A journal note applying the heat theory to an idealized spherical body. [Darboux’s bibliography](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/oeuvres-de-fourier/liste-des-ouvrages-scientifiques-de-fourier/C45C54373B389630970BB5FCB03A9FA5).
- **1824:** *Théorie du mouvement de la chaleur dans les corps solides*. A large Academy memoir closely related to the 1807 submission and the 1822 treatise; Darboux reprinted only its continuation because its first portion substantially overlapped the book. It is therefore a revised manifestation of the heat project as well as a separately published memoir. [Darboux, vol. II](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/oeuvres-de-fourier/9CA4D3AB013D089C7FAA059BB54A9E67).
- **1824:** *Résumé théorique des propriétés de la chaleur rayonnante*. A synthetic article presenting the principles of radiant heat rather than another comprehensive heat treatise. [Darboux’s bibliography](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/oeuvres-de-fourier/liste-des-ouvrages-scientifiques-de-fourier/C45C54373B389630970BB5FCB03A9FA5).
- **1824/1827:** *Mémoire sur les températures du globe terrestre et des espaces planétaires*. A physical-cosmological memoir read and dated in the 1820s and printed in the Academy’s volume for 1824, issued in 1827; the differing dates describe composition, Academy year, and publication rather than different works. [Darboux edition entry](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/oeuvres-de-fourier/9CA4D3AB013D089C7FAA059BB54A9E67).
- **1826:** *Recherches expérimentales sur la faculté conductrice des corps minces soumis à l’action de la chaleur*. An experimental memoir, complementing Fourier’s mathematical constructions with measurements of thermal conduction. [Darboux, vol. II contents](https://books.google.com/books?id=Wzu-Ic-kfEEC).
- **Date not fixed; published 1890:** *Remarques sur la théorie mathématique de la chaleur rayonnante*. A manuscript or previously uncollected analytical discussion printed by Darboux; its late publication must not be mistaken for a date of composition. [Darboux, vol. II contents](https://books.google.com/books?id=Wzu-Ic-kfEEC).
- **1823; published 1833:** *Mémoire d’analyse sur le mouvement de la chaleur dans les fluides*. An analytical memoir read to the Academy on 10 and 17 November 1823 but printed posthumously; its subject is heat transport in fluids rather than the solid-body theory of the treatise. [Darboux, vol. II contents](https://books.google.com/books?id=Wzu-Ic-kfEEC).

### Algebra, analysis, mechanics, and waves

- **1796:** *Mémoire sur la statique contenant la démonstration du principe des vitesses virtuelles et la théorie des moments*. Fourier’s first published scientific memoir, a mechanics text combining a proof of virtual velocities with a theory of moments. [Darboux edition entry](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/oeuvres-de-fourier/9CA4D3AB013D089C7FAA059BB54A9E67).
- **1818:** *Question d’analyse algébrique*. A brief memoir on inequalities and algebraic analysis, later recognized as an early contribution to linear optimization. [Darboux, vol. II](https://books.google.com/books?id=Wzu-Ic-kfEEC) and the [historical bibliography at Numdam](https://www.numdam.org/article/CSHM_1991_2_1__1_0.pdf).
- **1818:** *Note relative aux vibrations des surfaces élastiques et au mouvement des ondes*. A mechanics and wave-analysis note rather than part of the thermal treatise. [Darboux, vol. II](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/oeuvres-de-fourier/9CA4D3AB013D089C7FAA059BB54A9E67).
- **1819:** *Mémoire sur la théorie analytique des assurances*. A mathematical treatment of insurance omitted from Darboux’s reprinted texts but recorded in his bibliography; its omission from the collected volumes does not make it nonexistent. [Darboux’s bibliography](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/oeuvres-de-fourier/liste-des-ouvrages-scientifiques-de-fourier/C45C54373B389630970BB5FCB03A9FA5).
- **1820:** *Sur l’usage du théorème de Descartes dans la recherche des limites des racines*. An algebraic memoir on bounding real roots, published in two portions in the Société philomathique’s bulletin. [Historical bibliography at Numdam](https://www.numdam.org/article/CSHM_1991_2_1__1_0.pdf).
- **1827:** *Mémoire sur la distinction des racines imaginaires et sur l’application des théorèmes d’analyse algébrique aux équations transcendantes qui dépendent de la théorie de la chaleur*. An Academy memoir joining Fourier’s theory of equations to the transcendental equations generated by heat problems. [Darboux edition entry](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/oeuvres-de-fourier/9CA4D3AB013D089C7FAA059BB54A9E67).
- **1827–1831:** *Remarques générales sur l’application des principes de l’analyse algébrique aux équations transcendantes*. A further Academy memoir on transcendental equations; bibliographies variously index it by the Academy year and the later publication year. [Historical bibliography at Numdam](https://www.numdam.org/article/CSHM_1991_2_1__1_0.pdf).
- **1823–1824:** *Mémoire d’analyse indéterminée sur le calcul des conditions d’inégalité*. A project read to the Academy but not published as a complete memoir; only official summaries and extracts survive in print. It resists being called a finished work because Fourier intended a much fuller theory. [Darboux’s reproduced Academy extracts](https://fr.scribd.com/document/1072187497/Fourier-Et-Al-1888-OEuvres-de-Fourier-compressed).
- **Date not fixed:** *Leçons d’analyse de l’École polytechnique*. A manuscript course of nineteen lectures preserved in one quarto notebook; it is pedagogical performance captured in notes, not an authorially published textbook. [Darboux’s bibliography](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/oeuvres-de-fourier/liste-des-ouvrages-scientifiques-de-fourier/C45C54373B389630970BB5FCB03A9FA5).
- **Date not fixed:** *Leçons d’analyse et de mécanique professées à l’École polytechnique*. Eight manuscript notebooks recording another, partly overlapping body of teaching. The unstable boundary between lecture, notes, and work should be retained rather than resolved. [Dictionary of Scientific Biography account](https://www.encyclopedia.com/people/science-and-technology/mathematics-biographies/baron-jean-baptiste-joseph-fourier).
- **Date not fixed:** *Manuscripts on equations, mechanics, dynamics, measurement, heat, and analysis*. Twenty-nine Bibliothèque nationale volumes, approximately 5,200 sheets, preserve numerous drafts and studies. This is an archive and body of work, not twenty-nine finished obras; many pieces have no settled title, date, or publication status. [Dictionary of Scientific Biography account](https://www.encyclopedia.com/people/science-and-technology/mathematics-biographies/baron-jean-baptiste-joseph-fourier).

### Probability, demography, and public statistics

- **1821:** *Notions générales sur la population*. The extensive introductory study to the first volume of *Recherches statistiques sur la ville de Paris et le département de la Seine*. It belongs to a collective administrative publication but is identified bibliographically as Fourier’s text. [Darboux’s scientific bibliography](https://fr.scribd.com/document/1072187497/Fourier-Et-Al-1888-OEuvres-de-Fourier-compressed).
- **1821–1829:** *Recherches statistiques sur la ville de Paris et le département de la Seine*. A four-volume collective statistical project issued under Fourier’s direction, not a four-volume sole-authored book; his obra here is editorial, methodological, and institutional as well as textual. [Historical study in *Mélanges de l’École française de Rome*](https://www.persee.fr/doc/mefr_1123-9891_1999_num_111_2_4667).
- **1823:** *Mémoire sur la population de la ville de Paris depuis la fin du XVIIe siècle*. A demographic memoir forming part of the second volume of the Paris statistical project. [Bibliographical discussion in *Histoire & mesure*](https://journals.openedition.org/histoiremesure/pdf/23236).
- **1826:** *Mémoire sur les résultats moyens déduits d’un grand nombre d’observations*. A statistical memoir on averages and observational results, published within the third Paris volume. [Darboux’s bibliography](https://fr.scribd.com/document/1072187497/Fourier-Et-Al-1888-OEuvres-de-Fourier-compressed).
- **1829:** *Second mémoire sur les résultats moyens et sur les erreurs des mesures*. A sequel concerning averages and measurement errors, printed in the fourth Paris volume. [Bibliographical citation](https://probabilityandfinance.com/articles/55.pdf).
- **1821/1826:** *Rapport sur les tontines*. A jointly authored Academy report by Fourier, Sylvestre-François Lacroix, and Siméon-Denis Poisson; attribution to Fourier alone would be incorrect. [Darboux, vol. II contents](https://books.google.com/books?id=Wzu-Ic-kfEEC).
- **Date not fixed:** *Rapport sur un mémoire intitulé “Tableau des consommations de l’industrie de Paris en 1817”*. A one-sheet manuscript institutional report; it is an act of evaluation rather than an independent statistical survey by Fourier. [Darboux’s bibliography](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/oeuvres-de-fourier/liste-des-ouvrages-scientifiques-de-fourier/C45C54373B389630970BB5FCB03A9FA5).

### Egypt: institutional project, historical writing, and reported designs

- **1798:** *Description d’une machine destinée à faciliter les irrigations et mue par la force du vent*. A machine-design project presented to the Institut d’Égypte; the surviving evidence is a contemporary report of the presentation, not a securely identified machine, drawing, or complete authored text. Its resistance to being called a surviving work is essential. [Arago’s biographical notice](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Joseph_Fourier_%28Arago%29/Texte_entier).
- **1798–1801:** *Communications to the Institut d’Égypte on algebra, mechanics, irrigation, and related subjects*. A reported body of presentations whose titles and textual survival are incomplete; Darboux’s bibliography omitted these Egyptian papers, while later scholarly accounts note the omission. They are manifestations of research activity, not a safely reconstructible list of finished texts. [Dictionary of Scientific Biography account](https://www.encyclopedia.com/people/science-and-technology/mathematics-biographies/baron-jean-baptiste-joseph-fourier).
- **1799–1829:** *Description de l’Égypte*. A monumental collective Gesamtkunstwerk of scholarship, surveying, drawing, engraving, typography, and administration. Fourier did not author the whole work: his contribution was to coordinate its materials and compose its general historical introduction, so the larger object belongs here only as a collaborative project he materially helped make. [National Library of Scotland](https://www.nls.uk/collections/stories/printing-and-publishing/description-de-legypte/) and [Arago](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Fourier/Fourier_pdf/Fourier_Arago.pdf).
- **1809; revised 1821:** *Préface historique de la Description de l’Égypte*. A long historical and political introduction to the collective publication; the 1821 version is a revised manifestation, while an earlier draft with variants was published only later by Champollion-Figeac. [1821 text](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Description_de_l%E2%80%99%C3%89gypte_%282nde_%C3%A9dition%29/Tome_1/Pr%C3%A9face_historique).

### Academic reports, biographies, and ceremonial texts

- **1822–1829:** *Analyses des travaux de l’Académie royale des sciences*. Annual institutional surveys written in Fourier’s office as permanent secretary. They constitute a serial critical and administrative practice rather than a single stable book, and individual annual reports circulated in the Academy’s *Histoire*. [Dictionary of Scientific Biography account](https://www.encyclopedia.com/people/science-and-technology/mathematics-biographies/baron-jean-baptiste-joseph-fourier).
- **1823:** *Éloge historique de Jean-Baptiste Joseph Delambre*. A ceremonial scientific biography acting as the Academy’s public commemoration of its deceased permanent secretary. [Fourier bibliography summary](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Fourier).
- **1824:** *Éloge historique de Sir William Herschel*. An Academy éloge pronounced publicly on 7 June 1824; it is simultaneously biography, institutional judgment, and commemorative act. [Bibliographical record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Fourier).
- **1825:** *Éloge historique d’Abraham-Louis Breguet*. A commemorative Academy discourse on the watchmaker and instrument-maker. [Dictionary of Scientific Biography account](https://www.encyclopedia.com/people/science-and-technology/mathematics-biographies/baron-jean-baptiste-joseph-fourier).
- **1828:** *Éloge historique de M. Charles*. A public Academy éloge of Jacques Alexandre César Charles, read on 16 June 1828. [Académie des sciences text](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/Fourier/Fourier_pdf/Fourier_Charles.pdf).
- **1827:** *Discours de réception à l’Académie française*. An inaugural institutional speech delivered when Fourier was received into the Académie française; as a performative text, it acts by taking possession of a seat as well as describing its predecessor. [Wikisource bibliographical record](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Auteur%3AJoseph_Fourier).
- **1829–1831:** *Éloge historique du marquis de Laplace*. Fourier’s commemorative biography of Laplace, published posthumously in the Academy’s volume; the publication date does not imply posthumous authorship. [Scholarly bibliography](https://ouci.dntb.gov.ua/en/works/7nPdDP6l/).
- **Date not fixed:** *Rallier des Ourmes*. A biographical dictionary article attributed to Fourier; it is a concise reference text rather than an Academy éloge. [Dictionary of Scientific Biography account](https://www.encyclopedia.com/people/science-and-technology/mathematics-biographies/baron-jean-baptiste-joseph-fourier).
- **Date not fixed:** *Viète*. A biographical dictionary article on François Viète attributed to Fourier. [Dictionary of Scientific Biography account](https://www.encyclopedia.com/people/science-and-technology/mathematics-biographies/baron-jean-baptiste-joseph-fourier).
- **Date not fixed:** *Jean Wallis*. A biographical dictionary article on John Wallis attributed to Fourier; the French title reflects its publication context. [Dictionary of Scientific Biography account](https://www.encyclopedia.com/people/science-and-technology/mathematics-biographies/baron-jean-baptiste-joseph-fourier).
