## Works by Salvador Guillermo Allende Gossens

### Medical and social-medicine studies

- **1933:** *Higiene mental y delincuencia*. Medical-degree thesis submitted to the University of Chile. Its discussions of heredity, eugenics, mental illness, and crime have produced sharply conflicting interpretations; later polemics sometimes treat contemporary ideas reported in the thesis as Allende’s own proposals, so the distinction between exposition and endorsement remains disputed. ([Digital facsimile](https://mail.archivochile.com/S_Allende_UP/doc_de_sallende/SAde0076.pdf); [Quiroga edition record](https://www.greenlibros.com/products/salvador-allende-gossens-obras-escogidas-1933-1948-vol-1-9789562330312))

- **1939:** *La realidad médico-social chilena: síntesis*. A book-length governmental study of demography, disease, nutrition, housing, labor, and social insurance, written while Allende was minister of health. It is simultaneously an authored study and an instrument of public administration, and therefore resists a clean separation between personal obra and ministerial document. ([Universidad de Chile edition](https://libros.uchile.cl/507); [Memoria Chilena record](https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/602/w3-article-7737.html))

- **1940:** *Proyecto de ley de esterilización de los alienados*. A draft public-health protocol prepared within Allende’s ministry rather than a law enacted by Congress. Its precise individual authorship is disputed: it circulated under Allende’s ministerial authority, but surviving scholarship identifies other physicians and officials as participants in its preparation, so it should not be treated uncritically as a sole-authored Allende work. ([Mario Amorós, *Allende: la biografía*](https://liberacion.cl/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/amoros-mario.-allende.-la-biografia.pdf))

- **1944:** *Panorama bio-demográfico, económico y cultural de Chile*. A statistical and public-health survey associated with Allende’s work in social medicine and social insurance; it is closer to a technical report than to a literary book. ([Memoria Chilena author bibliography](https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/602/w3-article-799.html))

- **1944:** *Proyecto de reforma de la Ley n.º 4.054 y de la Ley n.º 4.055*. A legislative project presented by Allende as minister of health, accompanied in print by a comparative study of existing law and the proposed reform. Its status is properly “project”: it records an intended transformation of social-insurance law, not an accomplished law authored by him alone. ([Memoria Chilena author bibliography](https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/602/w3-article-799.html))

### Political project and collectively authored manifestos

- **1933 onward:** *The Chilean road to socialism*. Not one fixed text but Allende’s long political project: achieving socialist transformation through elections, pluralism, and Chile’s constitutional institutions. Later books bearing titles such as *La vía chilena hacia el socialismo* are editorial compilations of speeches, not separately written books, so the project should not be confused with its post hoc manifestations. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional, *Discursos fundamentales*](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/allende/discursos-fundamentales.pdf); [Witker anthology discussion](https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/archivos2/pdfs/MC0005465.pdf))

- **1970:** *Programa básico de gobierno de la Unidad Popular*. A collective electoral manifesto negotiated by the Unidad Popular parties, endorsed and politically embodied by their presidential candidate Allende. It acts by committing a coalition to a governmental program, but sole authorship by Allende cannot responsibly be claimed. ([Programa PDF, Memoria Chilena](https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/archivos2/pdfs/MC0000544.pdf))

- **1970:** *Las primeras 40 medidas del Gobierno Popular*. A campaign manifesto and prospective protocol listing immediate government actions. It belongs to the collective Unidad Popular campaign rather than to an individually authored Allende bibliography. ([Memoria Chilena thematic collection](https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/602/w3-article-7738.html))

- **1970–1973:** *The Unidad Popular government*. A political and institutional obra directed by Allende but made collectively by ministers, parties, workers, administrators, and social movements. Calling an entire government a personal “work” is contestable; it is included because Allende repeatedly presented democratic transition to socialism as a project to be made in practice, not merely described in texts. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional, *Discursos fundamentales*](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/allende/discursos-fundamentales.pdf))

### Presidential messages: constitutional acts

- **21 May 1971:** *Primer mensaje al Congreso Pleno*. The first annual presidential message, a constitutionally situated account and program of government rather than an ordinary speech; editorial tradition often identifies it with the formulation of the Chilean road to socialism. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional, pp. 125–190](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/allende/discursos-fundamentales.pdf))

- **21 May 1972:** *Segundo mensaje del Presidente Allende ante el Congreso Pleno*. The second annual presidential message, combining governmental report, diagnosis, and prospective program. As a state document prepared with an administration, it resists simple sole-author attribution even though delivered and authorized by Allende. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional, pp. 269–324](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/allende/discursos-fundamentales.pdf))

- **21 May 1973:** *Tercer mensaje del Presidente Allende ante el Congreso Pleno: Por la democracia y la revolución, contra la guerra civil*. The third annual presidential message and a performative defense of the government’s constitutional project amid escalating institutional conflict. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional, pp. 441–496](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/allende/discursos-fundamentales.pdf))

### Speeches and broadcast manifestations

- **27 July 1960:** *Homenaje al primer aniversario de la Revolución Cubana*. A Senate speech that Alejandro Witker chose as the opening work in his scholarly anthology of Allende’s mature political discourse. The title is editorially descriptive rather than demonstrably an authorial title. ([Witker anthology discussion](https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/archivos2/pdfs/MC0005465.pdf))

- **4–5 September 1970:** *Discurso de la Victoria*. A public address delivered after Allende’s electoral plurality; sources differ by one day because the gathering followed the 4 September vote and continued after midnight, so both dates occur in catalogues and editions. It is an oratorical manifestation preserved through transcription and recording, not a book. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional, pp. 27–36](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/allende/discursos-fundamentales.pdf); [Fundación Salvador Allende transcript, dated 4 September](https://discursos.fundacionsalvadorallende.cl/))

- **5 November 1970:** *Discurso en el Estadio Nacional con motivo de la toma de posesión del Gobierno*. An inaugural mass address that announces the governmental project and seeks publicly to constitute its political subject. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional, pp. 37–56](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/allende/discursos-fundamentales.pdf))

- **21 December 1970:** *Secretaría de la Juventud, nacionalización del cobre y creación del Consejo Nacional Campesino*. A presidential address joining several policy announcements; its composite title is editorial and describes the occasion and subjects rather than naming a self-contained written work. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional, pp. 57–80](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/allende/discursos-fundamentales.pdf))

- **27 February 1971:** *Discurso sobre las relaciones diplomáticas de Chile con Estados Unidos*. A foreign-policy address preserved as a speech transcript. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional, pp. 81–88](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/allende/discursos-fundamentales.pdf))

- **7 April 1971:** *Discurso ante la Conferencia de la UNESCO sobre ciencia, tecnología y desarrollo*. A policy speech whose scholarly edition preserves only fragments; the surviving object is therefore incomplete and should not be mistaken for the whole performance. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional, pp. 99–102](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/allende/discursos-fundamentales.pdf))

- **1 May 1971:** *Discurso en conmemoración del Día del Trabajador*. A ceremonial and political mass address. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional, pp. 103–124](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/allende/discursos-fundamentales.pdf))

- **11 July 1971:** *Discurso con motivo de la nacionalización de la Gran Minería del Cobre*. A public speech marking the constitutional nationalization of copper; as an utterance bound to a legal and civic act, it functions partly as manifesto. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional, pp. 163–190](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/allende/discursos-fundamentales.pdf); [Fundación Salvador Allende transcript](https://discursos.fundacionsalvadorallende.cl/))

- **23 July 1971:** *Discurso en manifestación ofrecida por el presidente Alejandro Agustín Lanusse*. A diplomatic public address delivered in Argentina. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional, pp. 191–198](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/allende/discursos-fundamentales.pdf))

- **23 August 1971:** *Discurso ante la Conferencia Latinoamericana por la Reforma Agraria*. A regional policy address on agrarian transformation. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional, pp. 89–98](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/allende/discursos-fundamentales.pdf))

- **30 August 1971:** *Discurso ante el Congreso de Colombia*. A diplomatic and parliamentary address. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional, pp. 199–214](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/allende/discursos-fundamentales.pdf))

- **October 1971:** *Comunica en Chile el otorgamiento del Premio Nobel de Literatura a Pablo Neruda*. A presidential broadcast announcing and interpreting Neruda’s Nobel Prize; the consulted edition supplies only the month, so no exact day is imposed here. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional, pp. 215–218](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/allende/discursos-fundamentales.pdf))

- **4 December 1971:** *Discurso de despedida a Fidel Castro*. A ceremonial and diplomatic address closing Castro’s visit to Chile. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional, pp. 219–238](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/allende/discursos-fundamentales.pdf))

- **3 April 1972:** *Discurso en la inauguración del edificio de la UNCTAD III*. A ceremonial address associated with a building produced collectively in an exceptionally compressed construction campaign. The speech is Allende’s manifestation; the building itself is not his individual architectural work. ([Fundación Salvador Allende transcript](https://discursos.fundacionsalvadorallende.cl/))

- **13 April 1972:** *Discurso ante la Tercera Conferencia Mundial de Comercio y Desarrollo (UNCTAD III)*. An international economic-policy address; it is distinct from the 3 April inauguration speech despite their frequent conflation in later editions. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional, pp. 239–268](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/allende/discursos-fundamentales.pdf))

- **17 May 1972:** *Discurso en la inauguración del Museo de la Solidaridad*. A speech helping bring a transnational collection into public existence. The museum and collection were collective creations donated and organized by artists and cultural workers, not works individually made by Allende. ([Fundación Salvador Allende transcript](https://discursos.fundacionsalvadorallende.cl/))

- **4 September 1972:** *Discurso en celebración del segundo aniversario del triunfo popular*. A mass anniversary address reaffirming the Unidad Popular project. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional, pp. 325–356](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/allende/discursos-fundamentales.pdf))

- **3 November 1972:** *Informe al pueblo por radio y televisión en el segundo aniversario del Gobierno*. A broadcast governmental report: part accounting, part political intervention, and therefore closer to an official act than to an essay. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional, pp. 333–356](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/allende/discursos-fundamentales.pdf))

- **1 December 1972:** *Palabras pronunciadas en el Congreso Nacional de México*. A diplomatic parliamentary address made during Allende’s international tour. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional, pp. 357–374](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/allende/discursos-fundamentales.pdf))

- **2 December 1972:** *Discurso pronunciado en la Universidad de Guadalajara*. An address to students that became one of Allende’s most frequently anthologized speeches; its conventional title identifies the venue rather than an authorially titled composition. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional, pp. 375–398](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/allende/discursos-fundamentales.pdf); [Fundación Salvador Allende transcript](https://discursos.fundacionsalvadorallende.cl/))

- **4 December 1972:** *Exposición ante la Asamblea General de las Naciones Unidas*. An international address denouncing economic pressure on Chile and articulating the interests of smaller nations. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional, pp. 399–434](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/allende/discursos-fundamentales.pdf))

- **4 December 1972:** *En la ONU con la prensa internacional*. A press encounter surviving in the consulted edition only as a fragment concerning women; it is an interview-performance rather than a stable authored text. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional, pp. 435–440](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/allende/discursos-fundamentales.pdf))

- **4 September 1973:** *Discurso en el tercer aniversario del triunfo de la Unidad Popular*. A final anniversary address, preserved in the Fundación Salvador Allende’s documentary selection. ([Fundación Salvador Allende transcript](https://discursos.fundacionsalvadorallende.cl/))

- **11 September 1973:** *Últimos mensajes del Presidente Allende al pueblo de Chile*. A sequence of improvised radio messages during the coup, not one manuscript or a text bearing a securely authorial title; editions often collapse the broadcasts into a singular “last speech.” Their obra is inseparable from voice, transmission, siege, and the anticipated destruction of the radio signal. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional, pp. 511–518](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/allende/discursos-fundamentales.pdf); [Fundación Salvador Allende transcript](https://discursos.fundacionsalvadorallende.cl/))

- **1970–1973:** *Surviving official presidential speeches*. An incompletely preserved oral-textual corpus rather than a single work: the Fundación Salvador Allende holds approximately 217 distinct surviving speeches in about 247 documents, while many others were destroyed or lost. Only 50 had been digitized and transcribed in the associated Wikisource project, so no honest list can claim to enumerate “everything” Allende spoke. ([Wikimedia Chile–Fundación Salvador Allende project description](https://es.wikisource.org/wiki/Wikisource:Discursos_oficiales_de_Salvador_Allende); [50-discourse edition](https://discursos.fundacionsalvadorallende.cl/))

### Letters and political interventions

- **31 July 1972:** *Carta a los jefes de los partidos de la Unidad Popular*. A political letter intended to act inside the governing coalition, not merely to describe it; in that sense it functions as an internal manifesto. ([Fundación Salvador Allende transcript](https://discursos.fundacionsalvadorallende.cl/))

- **24 August 1973:** *Carta de respuesta al acuerdo de la Cámara de Diputados declarando la ilegitimidad del Gobierno*. An official presidential reply defending the government’s constitutional legitimacy; it acts within an institutional confrontation and is therefore both letter and state act. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional, pp. 497–504](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/allende/discursos-fundamentales.pdf))

- **25 August 1973:** *Carta al general Carlos Prats con motivo de su renuncia a la Comandancia en Jefe del Ejército*. An official-personal letter responding to Prats’s resignation. The consulted edition prints “Prat” in its contents, but the recipient’s surname is Carlos Prats. ([Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional, pp. 505–510](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/allende/discursos-fundamentales.pdf))

- **Dates not exhaustively established:** *Correspondence, press articles, interviews, parliamentary interventions, and party documents*. These constitute a dispersed corpus rather than a securely bounded work: scholarly selected editions cover 1933–1948 and 1970–1973, while later archives and anthologies reproduce only selections. Because no complete critical edition or catalogue raisonné exists, naming the corpus is more accurate than inventing completeness or treating every later compilation title as a book Allende made. ([Patricio Quiroga, ed., *Obras escogidas 1933–1948*](https://www.greenlibros.com/products/salvador-allende-gossens-obras-escogidas-1933-1948-vol-1-9789562330312); [record for *Obras escogidas 1970–1973*](https://biblioteca.cfe.edu.uy/pmbIPA/opac_css/index.php?id=6911&lvl=author_see); [bibliographical survey](https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/913642.pdf))

### Editorial creations that are not new Allende works

- **1971–present:** *La vía chilena hacia el socialismo*, *El pensamiento político de Salvador Allende*, *El pensamiento de Salvador Allende*, *Nuestro camino al socialismo*, *Discursos políticos*, *Obras escogidas*, *Salvador Allende: pensamiento y acción*, and similarly titled volumes. These are post-speech selections, rearrangements, translations, or republications made by editors; they manifest Allende’s surviving corpus but are not additional works independently composed under those book titles. ([Witker’s account of the principal anthologies](https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/archivos2/pdfs/MC0005465.pdf); [bibliographical survey](https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/913642.pdf); [Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional editorial statement](https://www.marxists.org/espanol/allende/discursos-fundamentales.pdf))
