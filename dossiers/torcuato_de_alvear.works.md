## Works by Torcuato Antonio de Alvear

### Municipal reports and documentary works

- **1880–1881:** *Memoria del Presidente de la Comisión Municipal al Concejo, correspondiente al ejercicio de 1880*. An institutional report signed and presented by Alvear, not a wholly personal literary work; it records the municipal administration’s proposals concerning public works, hygiene, finance, policing and theatrical regulation. ([UNLP study citing the report](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/38929/Documento_completo.pdf?sequence=1))

- **1881–1882:** *Memoria del Presidente de la Comisión Municipal al Concejo, correspondiente al ejercicio de 1881*. A two-volume administrative obra: Alvear’s report fills the first volume, while the second collects eleven annexes of regulations, contracts and technical reports produced by the municipality. Authorship is consequently both personal and institutional. ([*Anuario Bibliográfico de la República Argentina*](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/obra/anuario-bibliografico-de-la-republica-argentina-2/ec26c879-2106-4f64-92d1-7a04a7652ec6.pdf))

- **1882–1883:** *Memoria del Presidente de la Comisión Municipal al Concejo, correspondiente al ejercicio de 1882*. A two-volume illustrated administrative report containing plans of the church of the Recoleta asylum, the Barracas hospital, the Hospicio de las Mercedes and the projected Plaza Once de Septiembre; surviving copies differ over whether they contain one or two original photographs. ([contemporary bibliographical notice](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/descargaPdf/anuario-bibliografico-de-la-republica-argentina-3/); [physical description of a surviving copy](https://www.hilariosubastas.com/producto/buenos-aires-en-los-ochenta))

- **1883–1884:** *Memoria de la Intendencia Municipal de la Ciudad de Buenos Aires correspondiente a 1883*. Alvear’s first report as intendente, presented on 19 May 1884; it is simultaneously an account, statistical archive and program for municipal government, organized around hygiene, public works and finance. ([contemporary bibliographical description](https://ahira.com.ar/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/La-Literatura-Argentina_20.pdf))

- **1883:** *El Centenario de Simón Bolívar en la República Argentina*. A commemorative compilation created under Alvear’s municipal sponsorship, gathering documents and testimony associated with Argentina’s Bolívar centenary celebrations; it should not be treated as a conventional single-author book. Some modern catalogue records misprint the original year as **1833**, but contemporary evidence places the volume and celebration in **1883**. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes record](https://cervantesvirtual.com/obra/el-centenario-de-simon-bolivar-en-la-republica-argentina/); [Academia Nacional de la Historia discussion](https://biblat.unam.mx/hevila/BoletindelaAcademiaNacionaldelaHistoriaCaracas/1984/vol67/no267/10.pdf); [contemporary record of Alvear’s donation](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/56/Anales_de_la_Sociedad_Cient%C3%ADfica_Argentina_%28IA_analesdelasocied1884soci2%29.pdf))

- **1884–1885:** *Memoria de la Intendencia Municipal de la Ciudad de Buenos Aires correspondiente a 1884*. A two-volume municipal report presented by Alvear and Secretary Mariano Obarrio; its constituent departmental reports and annexes make it a collective administrative artefact under Alvear’s authorship and authority rather than an individually composed book. ([digitized first volume and Alvear’s signed address](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/05/BaANH37424_Memoria_de_la_Intendencia_Municipal_1884_%28Tomo_I%29.pdf); [Academia Nacional de la Historia record](https://repositorio.anh.org.ar/handle/anh/174?mode=full))

- **1885–1886:** *Memoria de la Intendencia Municipal de la Ciudad de Buenos Aires correspondiente a 1885*. An administrative report constituting the documentary manifestation of Alvear’s government rather than a freestanding literary work; scholarship uses it as his account of the enlarged municipal health, engineering and regulatory system. ([scholarly study citing the 1885 *Memoria*](https://fileserver-az.core.ac.uk/download/61701368.pdf); [Google Books record of the municipal *Memoria* published in 1886](https://books.google.com/books/about/Memoria.html?id=DYELAQAAIAAJ))

- **1886–1887:** *Memoria de la Intendencia Municipal de la Capital de la República correspondiente a 1886*. Alvear’s final report, issued in two parts—the report proper and extensive annexes—and covering revenue, paving, plazas, hospitals, lighting, tramways, public health, civil registration, statistics and the municipal archive. It is the terminal volume of his mayoral documentary corpus. ([contemporary entry in the *Anuario Bibliográfico*](https://es.scribd.com/document/999289837/anuario-bibliografico))

### Urban projects and transformations

- **1881–1883:** *Plaza Once de Septiembre: gruta, cascada y projected landscaping*. A municipal landscape work represented by plans and an original photograph in Alvear’s 1882 *Memoria*; the gruta and cascade were already described as newly constructed, while the larger plaza design remained partly project. Design and execution belonged to the municipal technical apparatus, so Alvear’s role is properly that of commissioning administrator rather than sole designer. ([description of the illustrated *Memoria*](https://www.hilariosubastas.com/producto/buenos-aires-en-los-ochenta))

- **1883–1884:** *Unification and remodelling of Plaza de Mayo*. An urban obra made partly through subtraction: Alvear obtained and ordered the demolition of the Recova Vieja, joining the former Plaza de la Victoria and Plaza 25 de Mayo into one civic space. Juan Antonio Buschiazzo directed the demolition and associated works, making sole authorship by Alvear untenable. ([Buenos Aires urban-history study](https://buenosaires.gob.ar/areas/cultura/paisaje/02_capitulo2.pdf); [historical account of the Recova](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/b9/Buenos_Aires_colonial_-_Jose_Antonio_Pillado.pdf))

- **1883–1887:** *Renewal of Buenos Aires’s central area*. A distributed urban project rather than one bounded object: paving, drainage, street widening, tree planting, lighting, street furniture and regulation were coordinated to alter the capital’s appearance and circulation. It resists attribution as Alvear’s personal “work” because the plans and physical execution belonged to municipal departments, engineers, contractors and property owners, especially the office directed by Buschiazzo. ([Buenos Aires urban-history study](https://buenosaires.gob.ar/areas/cultura/paisaje/02_capitulo2.pdf); [Alvear’s 1886 report as described in the contemporary bibliography](https://es.scribd.com/document/999289837/anuario-bibliografico))

- **1883–1887:** *Municipal paving programme*. A serial public-works corpus, not a single monument: Alvear’s reports distinguish wooden paving, stone setts, ordinary cobbling, maintenance and related street levels, and his final report records hundreds of thousands of square metres paved. Its completeness cannot be reduced responsibly to a list of named streets because the standard contemporary bibliography treats it statistically and departmentally. ([contemporary description of the 1881 report](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/obra/anuario-bibliografico-de-la-republica-argentina-2/ec26c879-2106-4f64-92d1-7a04a7652ec6.pdf); [contemporary description of the 1886 report](https://es.scribd.com/document/999289837/anuario-bibliografico))

- **1884–1894:** *Avenida de Mayo*. Alvear’s principal urban project: conceived and legally initiated during his intendancy as a boulevard between Plaza de Mayo and Plaza Lorea, but not completed in his lifetime or administration. Earlier boulevard proposals by José Marcelino Lagos and by Carlos Carranza and Daniel Solier prevent calling the underlying idea uniquely Alvear’s; Buschiazzo prepared and directed the municipal work, construction began after Alvear left office, and the avenue opened in 1894. ([Buenos Aires official history](https://turismo.buenosaires.gob.ar/es/otros-establecimientos/avenida-de-mayo); [architectural history identifying the antecedents and delayed execution](https://architecture-history.org/books/Breve%20historia%20de%20la%20arquitectura%20Argentina.pdf))

- **1883–1887:** *Proposed western boulevard of circumvallation and hygienic green belt*. An unrealized or only fragmentarily realized planning project joining circulation with parks and the relocation of “insalubrious” municipal services; its projected components included green reserves at Recoleta, an agronomic park to the west and a Convalecencia park in Barracas. Because no single definitive built object corresponds to it, the intention—not a completed boulevard—is the work. ([biographical synthesis preserving the project’s components](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torcuato_de_Alvear))

### Civic, medical and administrative infrastructure

- **1880–1887:** *Municipal public-health and welfare network*. An institutional creation comprising the Asistencia Pública and an expanding system of hospitals, isolation and chronic-care facilities, asylums, vaccination, disinfection and sanitary inspection. It resists classification as Alvear’s individual obra: scholarship attributes the governmental reorganization to his administration, but individual institutions and buildings had separate founders, physicians, architects and construction histories. ([scholarly study of the Alvear administration’s health network](https://fileserver-az.core.ac.uk/download/61701368.pdf); [contemporary description of the 1883 *Memoria*](https://ahira.com.ar/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/La-Literatura-Argentina_20.pdf))

- **1883:** *Asistencia Pública municipal*. An institution rather than a building or text, established as the organizing centre for municipal medical assistance, emergency care and sanitary oversight. Its creation belongs to Alvear’s government, but not to him alone, and later services continued to alter its form. ([scholarly account of its institutionalization](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/384174130_Territorial_planning_and_legal_system_the_construction_of_the_hospital_institution_in_Buenos_Aires_hygienic); [study of the wider municipal health apparatus](https://fileserver-az.core.ac.uk/download/61701368.pdf))

- **1883–1887:** *Hospital de Mujeres grounds at Palermo, later Hospital Rivadavia*. Alvear executed the landscaping, donated the pharmacy from his own funds and maintained it until his death; the hospital buildings themselves were designed by Enrique Aberg and administered by the Sociedad de Beneficencia, so they are not Alvear’s architectural work. ([official Hospital Rivadavia history](https://buenosaires.gob.ar/areas/salud/rivadavia/historia.php); [Buenos Aires heritage study](https://buenosaires.gob.ar/areas/cultura/cpphc/archivos/libros/temas_21.pdf))

- **1880–1887:** *Municipal engineering, statistics, civil-registration and archival apparatus*. An administrative obra consisting of offices, procedures and records—including the Oficina de Ingenieros Municipales, construction regulation, civil registration, municipal statistics and preservation or copying of the former Cabildo’s records. These are collective mechanisms of government rather than discrete authored works, and their inclusion marks precisely that resistance. ([study of the engineering and statistical apparatus](https://fileserver-az.core.ac.uk/download/61701368.pdf); [contemporary account of the 1886 report](https://es.scribd.com/document/999289837/anuario-bibliografico))

### Regulatory protocols

- **1880–1881:** *Project for a Código de Teatros*. A proposed regulatory protocol intended to govern theatrical businesses, public conduct and municipal censorship. The surviving evidence establishes Alvear’s advocacy of such a code, but does not establish that he personally drafted or completed a single promulgated text under that fixed title. ([UNLP study based on the 1880 and 1881 municipal reports](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/38929/Documento_completo.pdf?sequence=1))

- **1880–1887:** *Municipal building and street regulations*. A dispersed corpus of protocols governing building height, street lines, pavements, vacant lots, numbering, traffic and inspection rather than one stable authorial text. The rules were made through the Commission, Intendencia and deliberative council, so attribution to Alvear means executive sponsorship and enforcement, not exclusive composition. ([contemporary analytical bibliography of the 1881 report](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/obra/anuario-bibliografico-de-la-republica-argentina-2/ec26c879-2106-4f64-92d1-7a04a7652ec6.pdf); [scholarly account of the construction regulations](https://fileserver-az.core.ac.uk/download/61701368.pdf))
