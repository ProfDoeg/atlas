## Works by Claude-Henri de Rouvroy

### Scientific and encyclopedic projects

- **1802:** *À la société du Lycée*. A printed address announcing Saint-Simon’s intellectual program; less a treatise than a public declaration of a project. ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=314))
- **1802–1803:** *Lettres d’un habitant de Genève à ses contemporains*. An anonymously issued sequence of philosophical letters proposing a European scientific and moral reorganization; copies and bibliographies sometimes date it simply 1803. ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=314))
- **1802–1803:** *Lettres aux Européens*. An unpublished manuscript sequence: a project of address to Europe rather than a finished book. Its title and date are supplied through the manuscript scholarship of the critical edition. ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=314))
- **1804:** *Lettres de deux philanthropes*. An unpublished epistolary manuscript, possibly collaborative in conception; it resists treatment as a completed autonomous work. ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=314))
- **1805:** *Le passé, le présent et l’avenir*. An anonymous, undated manuscript attributed to Saint-Simon by the modern editors, who also allow for an unidentified collaborator, perhaps Rigomer Bazin or someone in the same circle. Its conventional title is therefore an editorial identification, not an authorially fixed publication title. ([Authorship discussion](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=209))
- **1807–1808:** *Introduction aux travaux scientifiques du XIXe siècle*. A scientific-philosophical undertaking issued in successive states rather than one stable, finished book; the critical edition also preserves associated drafts under the collective heading “texts on the sciences.” ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=314); [volume I contents](https://bm-grenoble.fr/detailstatic.aspx?RSC_BASE=SYRACUSE&RSC_DOCID=640721&TITLE=oeuvres-completes-vol-1-henri-saint-simon&_lg=fr-FR))
- **1807–1808:** *Manuscrits des textes sur les sciences*. A dossier of drafts, fragments, and variant formulations connected with the preceding scientific project. This is an editorially constituted body of work, not a single text Saint-Simon titled or completed. ([Volume I contents](https://bm-grenoble.fr/detailstatic.aspx?RSC_BASE=SYRACUSE&RSC_DOCID=640721&TITLE=oeuvres-completes-vol-1-henri-saint-simon&_lg=fr-FR))
- **1807–1808:** *La contestation avec M. de Redern, I*. The first dossier of memoranda and polemical manuscripts produced during Saint-Simon’s financial and legal dispute with the comte de Redern. It is an archival action as much as a literary obra, and its collective title is editorial. ([Volume I contents](https://bm-grenoble.fr/detailstatic.aspx?RSC_BASE=SYRACUSE&RSC_DOCID=640721&TITLE=oeuvres-completes-vol-1-henri-saint-simon&_lg=fr-FR))
- **1808:** *Lettres de C.-H. Saint-Simon* (“Lettres au Bureau des longitudes”). Letters intervening in contemporary scientific debate; the parenthetical title is a later descriptive label, not a separate authorial book title. ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=314))
- **1808–1809:** *Sur les Leçons d’histoire de Volney*. An unpublished manuscript response to Volney’s lectures. The title was assigned by the editors of the critical edition. ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=314))
- **1809:** *Fragments de l’histoire de sa vie*. Autobiographical fragments rather than a completed memoir; they were published posthumously by Olinde Rodrigues in 1832, so their familiar collective identity is partly an editorial creation. ([Bibliographical record of the 1832 publication](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k85583q))
- **1810:** *Esquisse d’une nouvelle encyclopédie*. A preliminary statement of an encyclopedic project, explicitly an outline rather than the projected encyclopedia itself. ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=314))
- **1810:** *Nouvelle encyclopédie*. A further manifestation of the same encyclopedic project; surviving prospectuses and manuscript states do not amount to the completed universal work envisaged by Saint-Simon. ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=314))
- **1810:** *Histoire de l’homme*. An unfinished anthropological and physiological manuscript project, not a completed history in the ordinary narrative sense. ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=314))
- **1810 or 1811:** *Des arts en général*. An unpublished manuscript essay; both its exact date and its status as a finished work remain uncertain. ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=315))
- **1811–1812:** *La contestation avec M. de Redern, II*. A second archival dossier of arguments and memoranda arising from the Redern dispute. Like the first dossier, it resists classification as a single authored work. ([Volume I contents](https://bm-grenoble.fr/detailstatic.aspx?RSC_BASE=SYRACUSE&RSC_DOCID=640721&TITLE=oeuvres-completes-vol-1-henri-saint-simon&_lg=fr-FR))
- **1812:** *Prospectus pour une nouvelle École normale*. An unpublished institutional project proposing a new school; its title was assigned by the critical editors. It is a protocol for an institution that was never realized, not merely a description of one. ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=315))
- **1813:** *Mémoire sur la science de l’homme*. A memoir seeking to establish a positive science of humanity; it remained entangled with the larger unfinished scientific system. ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=315); [volume II contents](https://www.eurolivre.fr/livre/isbn/9782130625971.html))
- **1813:** *Travail sur la gravitation universelle*. A scientific-philosophical work extending gravitation into a proposed general explanatory system; its form is that of work-in-progress rather than a settled treatise. ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=315); [volume II contents](https://www.eurolivre.fr/livre/isbn/9782130625971.html))

### Political manifestos, addresses, and institutional projects

- **1814:** *De la réorganisation de la société européenne, ou De la nécessité et des moyens de rassembler les peuples de l’Europe en un seul corps politique, en conservant à chacun son indépendance nationale*. A program for a federated Europe, published under the joint names of Saint-Simon and his pupil Augustin Thierry. It must therefore be treated as a collaborative political project, not silently assigned to Saint-Simon alone. ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=315); [authorship statement](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude-Henri_de_Rouvroy_de_Saint-Simon))
- **1814:** *Articles dans Le Censeur*. A group of journal interventions rather than a single work; the critical edition gathers them as a dossier. Individual responsibility is complicated by Saint-Simon’s practice of working with secretaries and collaborators. ([Volume II contents](https://www.eurolivre.fr/livre/isbn/9782130625971.html); [critical-edition scope](https://www.lemonde.fr/livres/article/2012/12/06/saint-simon-hors-doctrine_1800610_3260.html))
- **1814–1815:** *Le défenseur des propriétaires de domaines nationaux*. A prospectus for a projected collaborative publication on the devaluation of former national properties. The announced work did not appear, so the project—and its prospectus—is the obra that survives. ([Volume II contents](https://www.eurolivre.fr/livre/isbn/9782130625971.html); [description of the unrealized publication](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/quac001soci02_01/quac001soci02_01_0005.php))
- **1815:** *Profession de foi du comte de Saint-Simon au sujet de l’invasion du territoire français par Napoléon Bonaparte*. A topical political declaration whose form is deliberately performative: a profession of allegiance and position rather than a detached essay. ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=315))
- **1815:** *Opinion sur les mesures à prendre contre la coalition de 1815*. A political intervention responding to the renewed European war. It is a proposal for action, close to a policy memorandum or manifesto. ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=315))
- **1815:** *Note manuscrite adressée à Carnot, ministre de l’Intérieur*. An unpublished ministerial memorandum; its descriptive title was supplied by the critical editors. As an addressed intervention, it resists separation from the political transaction that produced it. ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=315))
- **1816:** *Quelques idées soumises par M. de Saint-Simon à l’assemblée générale de la Société d’instruction primaire*. A proposal delivered to an educational association: an institutional protocol more than an autonomous book. ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=315))
- **1816:** *À MM. les électeurs de 1816*. An electoral address designed to act upon its immediate audience, and therefore properly classed as a manifesto or public intervention. ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=315))
- **1818:** *Les Communes, ou Essai sur la politique pacifique*. An unpublished manuscript essay proposing a peaceful politics of communal organization. Its surviving state does not justify treating it as a fully realized publication. ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=315))
- **1819:** *Propositions relatives à la loi des finances*. A set of policy proposals extracted and titled by the critical editors rather than an independently titled authorial book. ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=315); [volume III contents](https://syracuse-test.bm-grenoble.fr/MUSEE-STENDHAL/doc/SYRACUSE/640723/oeuvres-completes-vol-3-henri-saint-simon))
- **1820:** *Lettres de Henri Saint-Simon à MM. les jurés qui doivent prononcer sur l’accusation intentée contre lui*. A printed forensic address to the jurors in Saint-Simon’s prosecution following the “Parabole.” It is simultaneously legal self-defense and a public political act. ([Volume III contents](https://syracuse-test.bm-grenoble.fr/MUSEE-STENDHAL/doc/SYRACUSE/640723/oeuvres-completes-vol-3-henri-saint-simon))
- **1820:** *Sur la loi des élections*. A political pamphlet or policy argument concerning electoral law. ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=315))
- **1822:** *Des Bourbons et des Stuarts*. A historical-political comparison published as an intervention in Restoration politics, followed by a separately issued continuation. ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=315))
- **1822:** *Suite à l’ouvrage ayant pour titre Des Bourbons et des Stuarts*. A continuation of the preceding pamphlet rather than a wholly independent conception; its publication as a separate object nevertheless makes it a distinct manifestation. ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=315))
- **1823:** *Trois écrits sur la révolution européenne*. An editorially titled group of three unpublished manuscripts about prospective European transformation. It is a dossier, not a single authorially constituted text, and its component titles were not fixed for publication. ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=315))

### Collaborative periodicals and serial obras

- **1816–1818:** *L’Industrie, ou Discussions politiques, morales et philosophiques dans l’intérêt de tous les hommes livrés à des travaux utiles et indépendants*. A four-volume subscription publication and collective intellectual enterprise, not a single-author book. Saint-Simon directed it and wrote within it, but several contributors participated, so authorship belongs at the level of particular sections as well as the serial as a whole. ([Publication history](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=92); [volume II contents](https://www.eurolivre.fr/livre/isbn/9782130625971.html))
- **1818–1819:** *Le Politique, par une société de gens de lettres, ou Essais sur la politique qui convient aux hommes du XIXe siècle*. A collaborative periodical directed by Saint-Simon. Contributions by Auguste Comte and others mean that neither the entire serial nor every unsigned article can be treated as Saint-Simon’s sole composition. ([Volume III contents](https://syracuse-test.bm-grenoble.fr/MUSEE-STENDHAL/doc/SYRACUSE/640723/oeuvres-completes-vol-3-henri-saint-simon); [Comte attribution evidence](https://augustecomte.org/maison-auguste-comte/biographie/))
- **1819–1820:** *L’Organisateur*. A periodical sequence and political-philosophical campaign. Its celebrated opening thought experiment later called the “Parabole de Saint-Simon” was not originally an independent work under that title; “Parabole” became established through Olinde Rodrigues’s 1832 edition. ([Volume III contents](https://syracuse-test.bm-grenoble.fr/MUSEE-STENDHAL/doc/SYRACUSE/640723/oeuvres-completes-vol-3-henri-saint-simon); [title history](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude-Henri_de_Rouvroy_de_Saint-Simon))
- **1820–1822:** *Du système industriel*. A serial political project issued in several parts, letters, and addresses, some written with secretarial or collaborative assistance. Its serial form and changing addressees make it a campaign for institutional change rather than one stable treatise. ([Volume III contents](https://syracuse-test.bm-grenoble.fr/MUSEE-STENDHAL/doc/SYRACUSE/640723/oeuvres-completes-vol-3-henri-saint-simon); [periodical classification](https://www.encyclopedia.com/social-sciences/applied-and-social-sciences-magazines/saint-simon))
- **1822:** *Du système industriel, troisième partie*. A further installment of the preceding serial project, bibliographically distinct but conceptually continuous with it. ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=315))
- **1823–1824:** *Catéchisme des industriels*. A four-cahier serial using catechism and exposition as protocols for organizing the “industrial” class. The first two cahiers and the scarce fourth are Saint-Simon’s; the third, *Système de politique positive*, is Auguste Comte’s work and should not be reassigned to Saint-Simon merely because it appeared inside the serial. ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=315); [cahier authorship and publication history](https://www.zvab.com/servlet/SearchResults?ds=30&dym=off&rollup=off&sgnd=on&sortby=0&vci=463849))
- **1824:** *Opinions littéraires, philosophiques et industrielles*. A collaborative miscellany intended as the first volume of a continuing enterprise. Within it, *Opinions littéraires* and *De l’organisation sociale* are identified as Saint-Simon’s, while other contributions belong to members of his circle; consequently the container and its individual texts require separate attribution. ([Critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=316); [component attribution](https://www.bibliorare.com/wp-content/uploads/catalogue/pdf/cat-vent_berge19-12-2012-utopistes.pdf))

### Religious manifestation

- **1825:** *Nouveau christianisme. Dialogues entre un conservateur et un novateur*. An anonymous dialogue-manifesto proposing a moral transformation of Christianity. It was conceived as material for a second volume of *Opinions littéraires, philosophiques et industrielles* but issued separately; only a “first dialogue” appeared, making it the final manifestation of an unfinished project rather than a completed religious system. ([Original-work description](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Nouveau_Christianisme); [critical-edition inventory](https://www.applis.univ-tours.fr/theses/2019/104060_SHIRASE_2019_archivage.pdf#page=316))

### Correspondence as a body of work

- **1782–1825:** *Correspondance*. A dispersed body of private, scientific, political, and professional letters, only assembled as a critical chronological corpus in 2025. It is evidence of continuing intellectual and practical work, but it resists being called one obra: Saint-Simon did not design the surviving correspondence as a unified publication, and survival is necessarily incomplete. ([Manucius critical edition](https://manucius.com/produit/saint-simon-correspondance/); [editorial record](https://www.persee.fr/authority/145421))
