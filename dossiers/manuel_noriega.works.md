## Works by Manuel Antonio Noriega Moreno

### Military doctrine and political argument

- **1980:** *Filosofía del don de mando militar*. A military-leadership treatise published by Editorial Acuario; it is Noriega’s earliest separately issued work identified in the specialist bibliography. ([Duke University](https://guides.library.duke.edu/c.php?g=289894&p=1934087))

- **1985:** *Una perspectiva militar: el proceso de paz centroamericano*. A political-military position paper issued by the Panamanian Defense Forces, treating the Central American peace process from the institution’s—and its commander’s—perspective. Its status lies between authored essay and official military doctrine. ([Duke University](https://guides.library.duke.edu/c.php?g=289894&p=1934087))

- **1987:** *Pensamiento, doctrina y praxis del comandante Noriega, 1983–1986*. A compilation or doctrinal portrait credited bibliographically to Noriega and M. F. A. Porras. The record does not distinguish Noriega’s own writing from speeches, statements, selection, or Porras’s editorial labor, so it resists treatment as a conventionally authored book. ([Duke University](https://guides.library.duke.edu/c.php?g=289894&p=1934087))

### Speeches made into pamphlets or recordings

- **1986:** *Remarks by General Manuel Antonio Noriega in Japan*. A speech preserved as an audio performance and later as Appendix V of *America’s Prisoner*; the collected text supplies only the year, while a later audio release dates the performance to 12 December 1986. It is an oral political act with textual and recorded manifestations, not originally a book. ([*America’s Prisoner*](https://robscholtemuseum.nl/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Manuel-Noriega-Peter-Eisner-The-Memoirs-of-Manuel-Noriega.pdf); [Amazon Music archival recording](https://music.amazon.com/podcasts/cc6300c2-9d6d-4ab1-b9d7-23686e6f884f/episodes/36495f49-eed2-4592-b07d-f235cc2a084f/general-manuel-antonio-noriega-speech-in-tokyo-japan-by-general-manuel-antonio-noriega-12-12-1986))

- **11 August 1988:** *El despertar bolivariano por la integración de América Latina*. The closing speech delivered at the gathering “Hacia el II Congreso Anfictiónico de Panamá,” subsequently made into a separately issued pamphlet and preserved as a recording. It functions as a political manifesto for Bolivarian Latin American integration. ([Duke University](https://guides.library.duke.edu/c.php?g=289894&p=1934087); [archival recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MEPmsFsnEdk))

- **1989:** *“Hacia las elecciones mayo-1989”*. A separately issued electoral address or campaign tract. The surviving bibliographic description gives neither a publisher nor a more exact date, and the quotation marks in the recorded title may signal a supplied or unstable title rather than a formal one. ([Duke University](https://guides.library.duke.edu/c.php?g=289894&p=1934087))

- **17 January 1989:** *Hacia el 2000 vencer*. A speech delivered at a Fundación Omar Torrijos dinner and issued as a pamphlet; an audio manifestation circulates as *Discurso a la Fundación Omar Torrijos*. It is political performance and manifesto rather than ordinary expository prose. ([Duke University](https://guides.library.duke.edu/c.php?g=289894&p=1934087); [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/discurso-a-la-fundaci%C3%B3n-omar-torrijos/1730979112))

### Collaborative memoir

- **1993–1997:** *America’s Prisoner: The Memoirs of Manuel Noriega*. A collaborative first-person political memoir made with journalist Peter Eisner from dozens of hours of prison interviews beginning on 11 September 1993. Eisner shaped the book and independently wrote its introduction, notes, and afterword, while Noriega reviewed and revised the bilingual transcripts; the book itself warns that the result is selective rather than a comprehensive memoir, so undivided authorship cannot be assigned to either man. ([Handbook of Latin American Studies](https://www.loc.gov/item/hlas-bi98011753/); [text and editorial account](https://robscholtemuseum.nl/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Manuel-Noriega-Peter-Eisner-The-Memoirs-of-Manuel-Noriega.pdf))

[DOI record](https://doi.org/10.1108/eb049128); [Cambridge bibliographic citation](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/modern-panama/bibliography/21A25378F77C920177FAA453CA69C12A)

- Duke University Libraries, “Dictators in Their Own Words,” a curated author bibliography used to identify Noriega’s separately issued texts and to distinguish them from works merely about him. Its entry for the 1927 *Recuerdos históricos de mis campañas* belongs to an older Panamanian namesake, not to Noriega Moreno; its court-case record is likewise not an authored work. [Duke University guide](https://guides.library.duke.edu/c.php?g=289894&p=1934087)

- Manuel Noriega and Peter Eisner, *America’s Prisoner: The Memoirs of Manuel Noriega* (Random House, 1997), consulted both as the collected memoir and for its account of composition and its preservation of the 1986 Japan speech.
