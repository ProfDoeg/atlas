## Works by Raúl Veroni

### Urania: bibliophile editions

The Biblioteca Nacional catalogue treats these limited editions—not their underlying literary texts—as Veroni’s obras: objects in which editing, typography, printing, paper, binding, and original graphic art converge. Its bibliography lists only six Urania titles, although the catalogue narrative says that Veroni issued eight; two therefore remain unidentified in the consulted standard source.

- **1943:** *Tres recuerdos del cielo*. Bibliophile edition of poems by Rafael Alberti, designed, typeset, printed, and illustrated by Veroni with five etchings; only twenty-four copies were made. It is a collaborative book-object rather than a literary work authored by Veroni. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, pp. 11, 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1945:** *Dos poemas*. Bibliophile edition of two poems by Gabriele D’Annunzio, with Veroni’s etchings and aquatints; twenty-five copies are recorded. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1948:** *Urania, la musa del cielo*. Bibliophile edition of Camille Flammarion’s text, issued in twenty-four copies; Veroni’s authorship concerns the edition as a made graphic object, not Flammarion’s writing. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1951:** *L’antico amor*. Bibliophile edition of Giacomo Leopardi, issued in eighty-two copies. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1952:** *Dos poemas*. Bibliophile edition of Edgar Allan Poe, issued in eighty-seven copies. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1954:** *La lluvia en el pinar*. Bibliophile edition of Gabriele D’Annunzio, issued in sixty copies. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)

### La Cabellera: bibliophile editions

- **1955:** *A una alondra*. Bilingual bibliophile edition of Percy Bysshe Shelley in Juan Rodolfo Wilcock’s translation, with an original Veroni print. The Biblioteca Nacional catalogue records seventy-five copies, while a surviving copy description from Ediciones Urania reports a total of sixty-three; the issue size is therefore disputed. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf); [Ediciones Urania](https://edicionesurania.blogspot.com/2016/)
- **1955:** *Intermedio*. Bibliophile edition of Tulio Carella, issued in fifty-seven copies; it is Veroni’s edition-object, not a claim to authorship of Carella’s poem. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1958:** *Odas*. Bibliophile edition of John Keats in Wilcock’s translation, issued in fifty-two copies; its individually composed colophon exemplifies Veroni’s treatment of the book’s normally documentary matter as graphic art. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, pp. 12, 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1958:** *A Fanny Brawne*. Bibliophile edition of John Keats, issued in seventy-five copies. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1958:** *Poema*. Bibliophile edition of Horacio J. Becco, issued in sixty-four copies. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1960:** *Corona de María*. Bibliophile edition of Basilio Uribe, issued in fifty-two copies. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1961:** *Poemas de Ariel*. Bibliophile edition of T. S. Eliot in Basilio Uribe’s translation, issued in thirty copies; Veroni made the typographic and graphic object, while Eliot remains author of the poems. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1962:** *Para un adiós*. Bibliophile edition of Horacio Esteban Ratti, issued in twenty-five copies. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1963:** *Preta*. Bibliophile edition of Tulio Carella, issued in twenty-seven copies. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1964:** *Due poemi*. Bibliophile edition of Giosuè Carducci, issued in seventeen copies. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1965:** *Poemas*. Bibliophile edition of Giosuè Carducci, issued in 125 copies. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1965:** *Cantos del anochecer*. Bibliophile edition of Enrique Banchs, issued in twenty copies. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1968:** *Sapfó*. Bibliophile edition of Sappho in Miguel Alfredo Olivera’s Spanish version, issued in twenty-two copies. The catalogue preserves this spelling of the title. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1968:** *Un zorzal llamaba*. Bibliophile edition of Ricardo Molinari, issued in twenty-six copies. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1968:** *Poemas de Safo*. Bibliophile edition of Sappho in versions by Héctor Miguel Angeli, issued in twenty-four copies. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1969:** *Poemas*. Bibliophile edition of Delmira Agustini, issued in twenty-three copies. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1971:** *Oda a Leandro*. Bibliophile edition of Miguel Alfredo Olivera, issued in thirty-five copies. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1971:** *A una alondra*. A second Veroni edition of Shelley’s poem, distinct from the 1955 object and issued in thirty-two copies. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1972:** *La oscuridad como una rosa*. Bibliophile edition of Oscar Hermes Villordo, issued in forty-one copies. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1973:** *Dino Campana*. Bibliophile edition of Campana’s poetry in Spanish versions by Horacio Armani, issued in twenty-one copies. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1973:** *Primera elegía de Duino*. Bibliophile edition of Rainer Maria Rilke, issued in forty-seven copies. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1975:** *Francesco Petrarca*. Bibliophile edition of Petrarch in versions with notes by Luis Arena, issued in thirty copies. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1976:** *Annabel Lee*. Bibliophile edition of Edgar Allan Poe in Miguel Alfredo Olivera’s Spanish version, issued in twenty-five copies. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1977:** *Poemas*. Bibliophile edition of Alfonsina Storni, issued in fifty copies. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1978:** *Giacomo Leopardi*. Bibliophile edition in versions with notes by Luis Arena, issued in fifteen copies. Its prints are credited to Víctor Rebuffo, so Veroni’s obra here is principally editorial and typographic rather than illustrative. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1978:** *Dos poemas*. Bibliophile edition of Héctor J. Puglia, issued in thirty-five copies. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1981:** *Dos poemas*. Bibliophile edition of Ricardo Molinari, issued in fifteen copies. The catalogue bibliography dates it to 1981, although the accompanying essay describes La Cabellera as ending in 1980; the range, not the individual entry, is inconsistent. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, pp. 11, 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)

### El Camino a Ocloyas: bibliophile editions of regional narratives

- **1956:** *Poesía*. Edition attributed in the Biblioteca Nacional bibliography to Roberto J. Payró, issued in seventy-five copies. The title is anomalous for a collection described as “relatos criollos” and should not be silently emended; other bibliographical records associate Veroni with Payró’s *El fantasma*, but the consulted standard catalogue does not resolve the discrepancy. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1956:** *Idilio pastoril*. Bibliophile edition of Juan Carlos Dávalos, issued in forty-four copies. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1967:** *La Juana Figueroa*. Bibliophile edition of Jorge Calvetti, issued in twenty-seven copies. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, p. 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)

### Independently catalogued prints

- **1938:** *Head* (*Cabeza*). Drypoint measuring approximately 26 × 27 cm, acquired through MoMA’s Inter-American Fund. This is an autonomous print, not an illustration extracted from one of Veroni’s books. [MoMA, *The Latin-American Collection*, cat. 45](https://www.moma.org/documents/moma_catalogue_2897_300099431.pdf)
- **1939:** *El niño y su mano*. Print measuring 50 × 25 cm, documented in the Comisión Nacional de Energía Atómica collection. The available institutional notice does not specify its printmaking process more narrowly. [*Medicina (Buenos Aires)*, vol. 60, supplement 3](https://www.medicinabuenosaires.com/demo/revistas/vol60-00/tapa_s3.html)
- **1942:** *Pequeño paisaje*. Etching documented as impression 20 from an edition of twenty-five. It is traceable through a later exhibition record rather than a catalogue raisonné, so its present location and complete edition history remain unestablished here. [Galería Mar Dulce](https://galeriamardulce.blogspot.com/2011/06/las-siguientes-obras-de-la-exposicion.html)

### Untitled watercolours and an incompletely enumerated body of work

- **1970s:** Untitled watercolours of leaves, fruit, and flowers made chiefly at Villa Gesell. Twenty-five were assembled posthumously as *Naturaleza* in 2015, but that is the exhibition’s collective title rather than secure evidence that Veroni titled the individual sheets; without an itemized scholarly checklist, they resist honest enumeration as twenty-five separately named obras. [Galería Mar Dulce, *Naturaleza*](https://galeriamardulce.blogspot.com/search/label/15.03%20naturaleza)
- **Undated:** Book illustrations, covers, title pages, initials, vignettes, colophons, printing proofs, drawings, woodblocks, clichés, and typographic compositions made for Veroni’s own and other publishers’ editions. These are genuine made things, and some colophons were conceived as unique graphic pieces, but the Biblioteca Nacional catalogue discusses them as a corpus rather than supplying an item-by-item inventory; forcing invented titles or dates onto them would misrepresent the evidence. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, pp. 11–16](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
- **1936–1980s:** Approximately eighty books comprising Veroni’s independent projects and collaborations. The standard exhibition catalogue explicitly gives this approximate total but enumerates only the forty editions in his four named collections; consequently, the preceding list is complete for that published bibliography, not for every collaborative book or autonomous artwork Veroni ever made. [Biblioteca Nacional catalogue, pp. 11, 17](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/issues/7c4277c14a59d9e4d1280e49e495f86f.pdf)
