## Works by Jean-Baptiste Pitois

### Historical and political obras

- **1838–1839:** *Résumé de l’histoire de Paris*. Historical survey contributed under the name P. Christian to Charles Nodier, Auguste Regnier and Champin’s multimedia illustrated publication *Paris historique: promenade dans les rues de Paris*; it is a component text, not the whole collaborative obra. [BnF authority record](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb12327453r)

- **1839:** *Études historiques sur les révolutions de Paris*. Historical study associated in contemporary bibliographies with Pitois’s work on revolutionary Paris; some later biographical accounts treat it as his first independent sequel to *Paris historique*, but its exact bibliographical independence from that enterprise is insufficiently settled. [Bibliographie de l’histoire de Paris pendant la Révolution française](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/1d/Bibliographie_de_l%27histoire_de_Paris_pendant_la_R%C3%A9volution_fran%C3%A7aise_%28IA_bibliographiedel01tour%29.pdf)

- **1840:** *Histoire du clergé de France, civilisateur, missionnaire et martyr, depuis la prédication de l’Évangile dans les Gaules jusqu’à nos jours*. Two-volume historical and apologetic compilation published as the work of P. Christian. [Digitized-volume record](https://play.google.com/store/books/details/P_CHRISTIAN_pseud_i_e_Christian_Pitois_Histoire_de?id=Sor3WTs8hcsC)

- **1845:** *Souvenirs du maréchal Bugeaud, de l’Algérie et du Maroc*. Two-volume, novelized historical narrative made while Pitois was associated with Bugeaud; the contemporary form mingles memoir, documentary history and fiction, and therefore resists classification as Bugeaud’s own memoir. [Bibliographical description](https://ilab.org/assets/catalogues/catalogs_files_CATALOGUE%20235.pdf) [Digitized edition](https://books.google.com/books/about/Souvenirs_du_Mar%C3%A9chal_Bugeaud_de_l_Alg.html?id=AnkoAAAAYAAJ)

- **1846:** *L’Afrique française, l’empire de Maroc et les déserts de Sahara: conquêtes, victoires et découvertes des Français, depuis la prise d’Alger jusqu’à nos jours*. Illustrated colonial history and geographical compilation, published as P. Christian; modern scholarship uses it as a source but has also shown how it incorporates earlier military testimony. [Digitized edition](https://books.google.com/books?id=a_5dvnuZsfcC) [Scholarly discussion](https://services.phaidra.univie.ac.at/api/object/o%3A1284182/download)

- **1846–1847:** *Histoire des pirates et corsaires de l’Océan et de la Méditerranée depuis leur origine jusqu’à nos jours*. Large illustrated maritime history issued in several volumes; surviving descriptions disagree between three and four volumes because later issues were rearranged and expanded. [Bibliographical description](https://edition-originale.com/en/authors/christian-pierre-1811-1872-6391) [Four-volume issue](https://www.abebooks.fr/rechercher-livre/auteur/p-christian-pseudonyme-de-pitois/)

- **1847:** *La Nouvelle France: souvenirs de l’Algérie et du Maroc*. Retitled manifestation of *Souvenirs du maréchal Bugeaud*, not a wholly new obra; a standard bibliography of Morocco explicitly identifies the reuse. [Playfair, *A Bibliography of Morocco*](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/ab/A_bibliography_of_Morocco%2C_from_the_earliest_times_to_the_end_of_1891_%28IA_bibliographyofmo00play%29.pdf)

- **No fixed date, circa 1850:** *L’Algérie de la jeunesse*. Illustrated juvenile history or adaptation of Pitois’s Algerian material; cataloguers date it only approximately, so no exact year should be imposed. [Bibliographical description](https://edition-originale.com/en/authors/christian-pierre-1811-1872-6391) [Contemporary-copy description](https://media.interencheres.com/244/2018/04/27/160112_b91a372147189d1930cf9d13ea363ab7.pdf)

- **No fixed date, circa 1850–1852:** *Histoire des marins, pirates et corsaires de l’Océanie et de la Méditerranée, comprenant la conquête de l’Algérie*. Four-volume illustrated recomposition of the pirates-and-corsairs history, enlarged with Algerian material. Descriptions variously assign it circa 1845, circa 1850 or 1852; its status is consequently that of a revised manifestation rather than an securely datable new work. [Gazette Drouot](https://www.gazette-drouot.com/lots/14316230-christian--histoire-des-marins----) [Bibliographical description](https://criminocorpus.org/media/filer_public/49/ab/49abad03-d887-41e4-8415-a4b86350c719/catalogue_vente_pz.pdf)

- **1853:** *Histoire de la Terreur, 1792–1794*. Two-volume illustrated narrative history of the French Revolution’s Terror, published under the name P. Christian. [Digitized volume](https://play.google.com/store/books/details/P_CHRISTIAN_pseud_i_e_Christian_Pitois_Histoire_de?id=Sor3WTs8hcsC)

- **By 1863:** *Histoires héroïques des Français racontées à Son Altesse monseigneur le prince impérial*. Patriotic, didactic collection of episodes from French history; the securely traceable 1863 text is a third, revised and continued edition, so the first manifestation remains undated here. [Record of the third edition](https://kwize.com/fr/textes/P-Christian-Histoires-h%C3%A9ro%C3%AFques-des-Fran%C3%A7ais-racont%C3%A9es-%C3%A0-Son-Altesse-monseigneur-le-prince-imp%C3%A9rial-3e-%C3%A9dition-revue-et-continu%C3%A9e-jusqu-%C3%A0-nos-jours/)

- **1872:** *Histoire de la guerre avec la Prusse et des deux sièges de Paris, 1870–1871*. Two-volume contemporary history encompassing the Franco-Prussian War, the sieges and the Commune. [Digitized second volume](https://books.google.com/books?id=S8lQAAAAcAAJ)

### Editorial and journalistic creations

- **1844:** *La Morale merveilleuse: contes de tous les temps et de tous les pays*. Illustrated anthology of ten tales “collected and arranged” by P. Christian: an editorial creation and adaptation rather than ten securely attributable original stories. [Carteret/Vicaire-based description](https://www.librairie-busser.fr/sites/librairie-busser.fr/files/2021-07/Bulletin%2021.pdf)

- **1848:** *Journal des Jacobins: sentinelle des droits de l’homme*. Republican newspaper appearing three times weekly, with Pitois-Christian identified as editor-in-chief. The periodical is a collective, serial creation rather than a single-authored book, and individual unsigned articles cannot automatically be assigned to him. [BnF periodical record](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/affinerCote.do?afficheRegroup=false&critereRecherche=&index=TOUS3&listeAffinages=FacNatDoc_a%3BFacSujGeoEtendu_11931476%3BFacEnLigne_gallicaintramurosrech&motRecherche=&nbResultParPage=10&numNotice=11975813&triResultParPage=5&trouveDansFiltre=NoticePUB&typeNotice=m)

- **1853–1857:** *Les Héros du christianisme: histoire universelle des temps anciens et modernes depuis l’avènement de Jésus-Christ*. Eight-volume editorial project attributed principally to “Dom Marie-Bernard”; P. Christian supplied an introduction and historical notes. It should therefore not be silently converted into a wholly authored Pitois work. [Digitized volume 4](https://play.google.com/store/books/details/Les_h%C3%A9ros_du_Christianisme_Histoire_universelle_Av?id=7Ym5TWxr9rUC) [Digitized volume 8](https://play.google.com/store/books/details/Les_h%C3%A9ros_du_Christianisme_Histoire_universelle_Av?id=iVSsHt1GZKAC)

### Translations and textual adaptations

- **1839:** *Le Petit Fauconnier*. French translation or adaptation, signed “Christian,” of a juvenile narrative by Christoph von Schmid. The underlying story is Schmid’s; Pitois made the French textual manifestation. [Schmid bibliography](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christoph_von_Schmid)

- **1843:** *Contes fantastiques de Hoffmann*. New French translation of E. T. A. Hoffmann tales, preceded by Pitois’s biographical *Souvenirs intimes sur la vie de l’auteur* and illustrated by Gavarni. The translation and introductory life are Pitois’s made texts; the tales themselves are not. [Contemporary publication notice](https://data.decalog.net/enap1/Liens/Gazette/ENAP_GAZETTE_TRIBUNAUX_18420420.pdf) [Bibliographical description](https://www.abebooks.co.uk/book-search/title/contes-fantastiques-traduits-m-p-christian/author/hoffmann/)

- **1860:** *L’Ange de Wehrda, ou le Sacrement de mariage*. Short religious narrative published under Pitois’s name; evidence located does not securely determine whether it is wholly original, translated or adapted, so it resists a firmer authorship category. [Digitized edition](https://play.google.com/store/books/details/Christian_Pitois_L_ange_de_Wehrda_ou_le_sacrement?id=i8I5AAAAcAAJ)

### Fiction

- **1865 or 1866:** *Fulla l’Égyptienne*. Historical-religious novel set in ancient Egypt, issued under the pseudonym Charles Moreau with “Christian” also indicated. Sources disagree between 1865 and 1866, so both dates are retained. [BnF authority record](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb12327453r) [Bibliographical discussion](https://egyptinthetarot.blogspot.com/2017/09/paul-christians-egyptian-initiation-and.html)

### Occult syntheses and operative texts

- **1863:** *L’Homme rouge des Tuileries*. Hybrid historical legend, occult treatise and operative astrological-divinatory manual, illustrated with twenty-two kabbalistic figures. Its instructional sections function as protocols, while its Napoleon narrative is fiction presented through historical and esoteric claims. [Original-edition description](https://www.abebooks.fr/rechercher-livre/auteur/p-christian-pitois-christian/) [CCFr record](https://ccfr.bnf.fr/portailccfr/ark:/16871/0012158198)

- **1863:** *Calendrier thébaïque*. Astrological system of 360 degree-images and prognostications embedded within *L’Homme rouge des Tuileries*; later publishers detached it as an independent text, but its original status is a component protocol rather than a separate book. [BnF component-work record](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb36627843t)

- **1870:** *Histoire de la magie, du monde surnaturel et de la fatalité à travers les temps et les peuples*. Illustrated encyclopedic synthesis of magic, astrology, divination, secret societies and related traditions. Although later English editions call it *The History and Practice of Magic*, that title names a translated manifestation, not another work made by Pitois. [Contemporary bibliographical record](https://www.marianotomatis.it/?code=CHR1870&lang=EN&page=biblioteca) [Caillet-based description](https://ilab.org/assets/catalogues/Farfadets.pdf)
