## Works by Lionel-Noël Royer

### Paintings exhibited at the Paris Salons

- **1874:** *Bataille du Mans, charge au plateau d’Auvours* (also catalogued as *Bataille d’Auvours, le 10 janvier 1871*). History painting and Royer’s Salon début; it is now in the Musée de Tessé, Le Mans. [Salon database; Musée d’Orsay artist file][1]
- **1875:** *La Fille de l’hôtesse*. Oil painting exhibited at the Salon and now in the Musée Baron-Martin, Gray. [Salon database][1]
- **1876:** *Mauresque*. Oil-on-canvas figure study, signed and dated 1876; now in the Musée des Beaux-Arts de Marseille. [POP/Joconde][2]
- **1879:** *Le Christ en croix*. Religious painting recorded in the contemporary Salon sequence; its present location is not established. [Champlin’s contemporary catalogue of painters][3]
- **1880:** *Daphné changée en laurier*. Mythological painting, now in the Musée de Tessé, Le Mans. [Champlin; Musée de Tessé catalogue][3]
- **1881:** *Vénus protégeant le corps d’Hector*. Mythological history painting; present location unestablished. [Champlin][3]
- **1883:** *Madame Roland*. Historical painting; present location unestablished. [Champlin][3]
- **1884:** *La Famille*. Figure painting for which Royer received a third-class Salon medal; present location unestablished. [Champlin; 1895 Salon catalogue][3]
- **1885:** *L’Amour et la Folie*. Allegorical painting; present location unestablished. [Champlin][3]
- **1886:** *Pour la patrie !* Military-history painting; present location unestablished. [Champlin][3]
- **1887:** *Le Christ au tombeau*. Religious oil painting exhibited at the Salon; Royer gave it to the Musée des Beaux-Arts de Chartres in 1924 (inv. 7153). [Musée de Chartres collection record][4]
- **1893:** *Le 14e de ligne à Eylau*. Military-history painting showing Captain Marbot saving the regiment’s standard and eagle; at least three versions or reformulations are reported, so the Salon canvas should not automatically be identified with every later market example. [1893 Salon entry and documented version][5]
- **1893:** *Notre-Dame de France*. Religious painting recorded in contemporary reporting on the 1893 Salon; its present location is not established. [Contemporary Salon report][6]
- **1896:** *Germanicus retrouvant les restes des légions de Varus*. Large history painting awarded Royer’s second-class Salon medal; a version or related canvas is held by the Musée de Tessé, but published dimensions and titles vary. [Contemporary Salon review; Musée d’Orsay artist file][7]
- **1899:** *Vercingétorix jette ses armes aux pieds de César* (also *Vercingétorix devant César*). Monumental history painting, oil on canvas, now in the Musée Crozatier, Le Puy-en-Velay; its familiar title exists in more than one form. [Musée Crozatier][8]
- **1909:** *Le 14e de ligne à Eylau*. A new, vertically composed treatment of the Marbot episode exhibited at the Salon d’Hiver; it is distinct from the 1893 composition. [Documented exhibition history][5]

### Competition paintings and academic projects

- **1880:** *La Reconnaissance d’Ulysse et de Télémaque dans la cabane d’Eumée*. Oil painting made for the Prix de Rome competition; purchased by the French state in 1880 and deposited at Castellane in 1883. Sources disagree about whether Royer’s relevant Prix de Rome distinction belongs to 1880 or 1882, but the painting itself and its 1880 purchase are documented. [Inventaire général du patrimoine culturel][9]
- **1882:** *Mattathias refusant de sacrifier aux idoles*. Prix de Rome project in history painting, associated with Royer’s second first grand prize; the surviving status of the competition canvas has not been securely established in the consulted records. [Musée d’Orsay artist file; École des beaux-arts prize chronology][10]

### Monumental and architectural decoration

- **1896:** *Le Chœur de la cathédrale du Mans*. Architectural-interior painting, not the cathedral decoration itself; it is held by the Musée de Tessé. [Musée de Tessé catalogue][11]
- **By 1906:** *Le Goût*. Allegorical mural project for the salle des fêtes of Paris’s 10th-arrondissement mairie; the Petit Palais preserves Royer’s painted esquisse. The sketch is an autonomous object but also a project for an architectural ensemble. [Paris Musées][12]
- **Date not securely established:** *Le Crucifix*. Religious painting installed at the altar of the Crucifix in Le Mans Cathedral; the consulted collection summaries do not supply a defensible date. [Musée d’Orsay/collections summary][13]
- **1910:** *La Communion des zouaves avant la bataille de Loigny*. Monumental oil painting, approximately six by three metres, made for the church of Saint-Lucain at Loigny-la-Bataille and classified as a historic monument. It is simultaneously a memorial image, an altarpiece-scale object and testimony by a veteran of the battle. [French heritage record; Loigny museum documentation][14]
- **1910:** *Le Général Gaston de Sonis sur le champ de bataille*. Monumental pendant to *La Communion des zouaves*, showing the wounded general during the night after the battle; installed in the opposite aisle of Saint-Lucain. [French heritage record; Loigny museum documentation][14]
- **1910–1913:** *Vie de Jeanne d’Arc*. Cycle of large canvases marouflaged onto the nave walls of the Basilica of Bois-Chenu at Domrémy. Sources variously count six, eight or ten “scenes,” apparently because some count only the six nave canvases while others include additional compositions or subdivisions; the cycle therefore resists treatment as a stable list of ten independent paintings. [Scholarly bibliography; basilica documentation; monument record][15]
- **1910–1913:** *Au Bois-Chenu*. First documented nave canvas in the *Vie de Jeanne d’Arc* cycle, representing Jeanne’s youth near the fountain and the “Beau May.” [Basilica documentation][16]
- **1910–1913:** *Jeanne à Vaucouleurs*. Episode in the Domrémy cycle concerning Jeanne’s appeal to Robert de Baudricourt; it is named in the scholarly description of the cycle, although its precise status as a separate canvas or subdivision is not consistent across inventories. [Scholarly bibliography][15]
- **1910–1913:** *Jeanne reconnaît le Dauphin à Chinon*. Second securely described nave canvas, showing Jeanne identifying Charles despite his disguise. [Basilica documentation][16]
- **1910–1913:** *Entrée de Jeanne d’Arc à Orléans*. Third securely described nave canvas, representing the triumphant reception of 8 May 1429. [Basilica documentation][16]
- **1910–1913:** *Sacre de Charles VII à Reims*. Scene in the Domrémy cycle representing the coronation; some descriptions count it as an independent canvas and others as part of the broader cycle. [Scholarly bibliography; basilica documentation][15]
- **1910–1913:** *Jeanne au combat*. Battlefield scene in the Domrémy cycle; no single stable historical title emerged from the consulted records. [Basilica documentation][15]
- **1910–1913:** *Capture de Jeanne à Compiègne*. Episode named in the scholarly account of the cycle; its counting within the six-, eight- or ten-scene totals remains inconsistent. [Scholarly bibliography][15]
- **1910–1913:** *Procès de Jeanne à Rouen*. Trial episode in the cycle; descriptions sometimes treat the Rouen material collectively rather than as separate trial and execution canvases. [Scholarly bibliography][15]
- **1910–1913:** *Le Bûcher de Rouen* (also *Le Supplice de Rouen*). Final martyrdom scene of the Domrémy cycle. A drawing called *Le Sentencier, état final* is documented as a preparatory study, not as another finished mural. [Basilica documentation; documented study][16]
- **1891–1934, exact contribution dates unresolved:** Cartoons for mosaics at the Basilica of Bois-Chenu. The monument record attributes mosaic designs collectively to Charles Lameire, Lionel Royer, Pierre Dié-Malet and Henri Pinta without assigning every surviving mosaic to an individual designer; Royer’s exact share must therefore remain disputed rather than being converted into fictitious individual works. [Official monument record][17]

### Portraits

- **1885:** *Colonel Athanase de Charette, commandant des Zouaves pontificaux*. Military portrait preserved in the Basilica-Cathedral of Mary Queen of the World, Montréal. [Collection summary][13]
- **Circa 1890:** *Le Comte d’Adhémar de Cransac*. Full-length oil portrait now in the Dayton Art Institute (1998.18). [Dayton Art Institute][18]
- **Circa 1890:** *Portrait de la marquise de Broc*. Society portrait known through the art-market record; the exact date and present ownership are not publicly fixed. [Christie’s provenance record cited in the scholarly bibliography][19]
- **Circa 1890:** *Portrait du marquis de Broc*. Pendant society portrait; exact date and present ownership remain uncertain. [Christie’s provenance record cited in the scholarly bibliography][19]
- **1902:** *Portrait du peintre Frédéric Le Brun dans son atelier*. Posthumous or memorial portrait—Le Brun died in 1901—signed and dated 1902; known from a documented sale rather than a public collection catalogue. [Documented dealer record][20]
- **Date unknown:** *Femme en deuil*. Oil-on-canvas portrait or figure painting in the Musée Muséum départemental des Hautes-Alpes, Gap; the museum does not assign a secure creation date. [Musée Muséum départemental][21]
- **Date unknown:** *Étude de femme ou Odalisque*. Oil study on panel in the Gap museum; “study” is part of its catalogued identity, and it should not be promoted into a presumed finished Salon painting. [Musée Muséum départemental][21]
- **Date unknown:** *Portrait de femme*. Signed female portrait known in reproductions and market records; sitter, date and original title are unestablished, so the phrase is descriptive rather than necessarily authorial. [Musée Muséum départemental comparative record][21]

### Mythological, allegorical and literary paintings outside the securely reconstructed Salon sequence

- **Date unknown:** *Diane surprise*. Mythological painting in the Musée de Tessé; no defensible date appears in the consulted museum summaries. [Musée de Tessé collection summary][13]
- **Date unknown:** *Le Triomphe de Vénus*. Mythological oil painting known from reference literature; location and date are unresolved. [Bénézit-derived work list][22]
- **1893 or earlier:** *Cupidon et Psyché*. Mythological painting exhibited at the World’s Columbian Exposition in Chicago in 1893; its present location is unestablished. [World’s Columbian Exposition record][13]
- **Date unknown:** *Le Jardin des Muses*. Allegorical or mythological painting known by title but presently unlocated; no date should be inferred from its style. [Collection summary][13]
- **Date unknown:** *Auguste Comte et ses trois muses* (also reported as *Auguste Comte et ses trois anges*). Allegorical portrait-history composition known through illustrated-press and secondary references; the competing titles should not be silently collapsed. [Musée départemental documentation][21]
- **Circa 1889–1902:** *Esméralda*. Literary-subject painting inspired by Victor Hugo, now in the Maison de Victor Hugo–Hauteville House; Paris Musées gives a broad rather than exact date range. [Paris Musées][12]
- **Date unknown:** *Allégorie de l’Été*. Signed allegorical canvas known from the market; its date and original exhibition history are unestablished. [Market record][23]

### Military and historical paintings outside the main Salon list

- **Date unknown:** *Bataille de Wagram*. Napoleonic history painting recorded in reference literature; present location and precise date are unresolved. [Bénézit-derived work list][22]
- **After 1893, date uncertain:** *Le capitaine Marbot sauve le drapeau et l’aigle du 14e régiment d’infanterie de ligne*. Smaller horizontal oil version of Royer’s Eylau subject. Because three treatments are reported, it is a related manifestation of the project rather than safely the same object as the 1893 Salon canvas. [Documented sale and exhibition labels][5]

### Press illustration

- **1895:** *Alfred Dreyfus dans sa cellule avant sa déportation à l’île du Diable*. Current-affairs illustration published on the cover of the illustrated supplement of *Le Petit Journal*, no. 218, 20 January 1895. It is a reproducible press image rather than a unique easel painting. [Bibliothèque nationale/press record][13]
- **Date unknown:** *Auguste Comte et ses trois muses*. Press illustration or reproduced drawing concerning Comte and the three principal women in his life; because the consulted descriptions do not establish whether a separate painting also existed, its material status remains unresolved. [Musée départemental documentation][21]

### Drawings and studies documented as independent objects

- **1910–1914:** *Le Sentencier, état final, étude pour Le Bûcher de Rouen*. Pencil study for the Domrémy mural cycle, bearing Royer’s studio stamp; it is a project-stage object, not another mural. [Documented sale catalogue][24]
- **Date unknown:** *Le Lieutenant Moncabier*. Pencil drawing bearing the studio stamp; identity of the sitter and intended function are not established. [Documented sale catalogue][24]
- **Date unknown:** *Le Songe du soldat*. Pencil drawing bearing the studio stamp; it may be a compositional study, but no finished corresponding painting was identified. [Documented sale catalogue][24]
- **Date unknown:** *Alice, étude*. Pencil-and-white-heightening study, titled on the sheet and bearing the studio stamp; “Alice” may identify either the sitter or the study rather than a formal exhibition title. [Crédit municipal sale record][25]
- **Date unknown:** *Jeune femme cueillant des fleurs*. Pencil drawing bearing the studio stamp; date and intended larger composition are unknown. [Crédit municipal sale record][25]
- **Date unknown:** *Paysage arboré*. Pencil, ink and wash drawing bearing the studio stamp; a study rather than a securely titled finished work. [Crédit municipal sale record][25]

### Paintings known only or chiefly through the market

- **Date unknown:** *Une jeune fille et sa poupée*. Genre painting known through a dealer record; date, original title and present location are uncertain. [Dealer record cited in the scholarly bibliography][19]
- **Date unknown:** *Fermière et ses poules*. Oil painting known from an auction catalogue; no secure date or exhibition history is given. [Ader auction record][26]
- **Date unknown:** *Nu allongé dans un intérieur*. Oil painting known from a Christie’s sale; the wording is descriptive and may not be Royer’s title. [Christie’s][27]
- **Date unknown:** *La Plaine*. Oil painting known from a municipal-auction record; title, authorship and medium are recorded, but no date or earlier provenance is supplied. [Crédit municipal record][25]
- **Date unknown:** *Élégante*. Portrait or genre painting known from an auction catalogue; the generic title may be modern rather than authorial. [Auction catalogue][28]
