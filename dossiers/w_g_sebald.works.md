## Works by Winfried Georg Sebald

### Early and archival writings

- **1960s:** *Wartend*. An unpublished, fragmentary short narrative preserved in Sebald’s literary estate; it belongs to his juvenilia and resists classification as a completed work. [Cambridge’s survey of the manuscripts](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/w-g-sebald-in-context/unpublished-juvenilia/3874AEDA5E94FDC2E22DBEE52E235462)

- **1960s:** Untitled story about Herr G. An unpublished youthful narrative in the Deutsches Literaturarchiv; lacking a settled title and publication history, it is better treated as an archival fragment than as a finished story. [Cambridge](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/w-g-sebald-in-context/unpublished-juvenilia/3874AEDA5E94FDC2E22DBEE52E235462)

- **1960s:** *Der Traum ein Leben oder die Geschichte des Fr. v. Sch.* A six-page dramatic fragment, unpublished in Sebald’s lifetime; its brevity and manuscript status make “play” a description of intention rather than proof of completion. [Cambridge](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/w-g-sebald-in-context/unpublished-juvenilia/3874AEDA5E94FDC2E22DBEE52E235462)

- **1960s:** Two versions of an untitled novel. These unpublished manuscripts constitute an abandoned novel-project, not two securely independent works; scholarship has not supplied a title that Sebald himself fixed. [Cambridge](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/w-g-sebald-in-context/unpublished-juvenilia/3874AEDA5E94FDC2E22DBEE52E235462)

### Scholarship, criticism, and editorial work

- **1966; revised 1969:** *Carl Sternheim: Kritiker und Opfer der Wilhelminischen Ära*. First a University of Fribourg dissertation and then a substantially revised scholarly monograph: the two manifestations belong to one critical project rather than being unrelated books. [Cambridge chronology](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781009059787_A47731445/preview-9781009059787_A47731445.pdf)

- **1973; revised 1980:** *The Revival of Myth: A Study of Alfred Döblin’s Novels* / *Der Mythus der Zerstörung im Werk Döblins*. Sebald’s doctoral thesis and its later, revised German book are successive manifestations of a study of myth and destruction in Döblin. [Cambridge chronology](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781009059787_A47731445/preview-9781009059787_A47731445.pdf)

- **1974:** *Die hölzernen Engel von East Anglia*. An illustrated travel article or itinerary through Norfolk and Suffolk, originally published in *Die Zeit*; it is an early hybrid of guide, journey and cultural observation rather than academic criticism narrowly understood. [Original publication](https://www.zeit.de/1974/31/die-hoelzernen-engel-von-east-angelia/seite-3)

- **1975:** *Zum Thema Messianismus im Werk Döblins*. A scholarly article on messianism in Döblin, produced within the larger Döblin project but independently published. [Bibliographic discussion](https://etd.ohiolink.edu/acprod/odb_etd/ws/send_file?accession=ucin1307321149&disposition=inline)

- **1985:** *Die Beschreibung des Unglücks: Zur österreichischen Literatur von Stifter bis Handke*. A collection of literary-critical essays whose subjects include Stifter, Schnitzler, Hofmannsthal, Broch, Canetti, Bernhard, Améry, Handke and Ernst Herbeck; some essays had earlier lives in journals, so the volume is both an obra and an editorial recomposition of prior texts. [Sebald works bibliography](https://www.wgsebald.de/literatur.html)

- **1988:** *A Radical Stage: Theatre in Germany in the 1970s and 1980s*. An English-language critical anthology edited by Sebald, with an editorial introduction; his making here is principally selection, arrangement and framing rather than sole authorship of the contents. [Routledge handbook description and bibliography](https://www.routledge.com/Saturns-Moons-A-WG-Sebald-Handbook/Catling/p/book/9780367603298)

- **1991:** *Unheimliche Heimat: Essays zur österreichischen Literatur*. A second essay collection on Austrian literature and compromised ideas of homeland; like its predecessor, it gathers and reshapes criticism written across a longer period. [S. Fischer’s account of the œuvre](https://www.fischerverlage.de/autor/w-g-sebald-1007845)

- **1992:** *Ich möchte zu ihnen hinabsteigen und finde den Weg nicht: Zu den Romanen Jurek Beckers*. An essay written for a 1992 collection but rejected and left unpublished until 2010; its delayed manifestation should not be mistaken for a work composed after Sebald’s death. [Publication record](https://www.wgsebald.de/literatur.html)

- **1993:** *Zwischen Geschichte und Naturgeschichte: Über die literarische Beschreibung totaler Zerstörung*. The Zürich lectures that initiated Sebald’s controversial inquiry into German writing about the Allied bombing; they became the principal component of *Luftkrieg und Literatur*. The lectures and book are related manifestations, not wholly separate intellectual projects. [Sebald handbook overview](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-476-05395-4)

- **1997:** *Der Schriftsteller Alfred Andersch*. A polemical critical essay examining Andersch’s conduct and literary self-fashioning under National Socialism; it was later incorporated, in revised form, into *Luftkrieg und Literatur*. [Publication and controversy summary](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/W._G._Sebald)

- **1998:** *Logis in einem Landhaus: Über Gottfried Keller, Johann Peter Hebel, Robert Walser und andere*. Six image-bearing literary portraits—of Hebel, Keller, Walser, Mörike, Rousseau and the artist Jan Peter Tripp—in which criticism, biography, memoir and travel writing become inseparable. It is consequently a literary-critical Gesamtkunstwerk more readily than a conventional essay collection. [Cambridge discussion of its hybrid form](https://assets.cambridge.org/97813165/11350/frontmatter/9781316511350_frontmatter.pdf)

- **1998:** *Ausgrabung der Vergangenheit*. A short introduction written for a Michael Hamburger reading and published in the *Tages-Anzeiger*; it is simultaneously an occasional address and a miniature literary portrait. [Scholarly publication history](https://publications.aston.ac.uk/id/eprint/24430/1/Interventionen.pdf)

- **1999:** *Luftkrieg und Literatur: Mit einem Essay zu Alfred Andersch*. A polemical essay-book formed from the revised Zürich lectures and the Andersch essay; it acts almost as a manifesto demanding that German literature confront the bombing war, though it remains historical criticism rather than a literal program of instructions. [S. Fischer](https://www.fischerverlage.de/autor/w-g-sebald-1007845)

- **2001:** *Europäische Peripherien*. An essay or lecture concerned with Europe’s geographical and cultural margins, separately indexed among Sebald’s critical writings; its exact status varies between spoken intervention and printed text. [Cambridge index](https://assets.cambridge.org/97813165/11350/index/9781316511350_index.pdf)

- **2001:** *Constructs of Mourning*. A late essay or lecture on remembrance and representation. It is an occasional critical text rather than a book-length obra and survives through publication and archival transmission. [Cambridge index](https://assets.cambridge.org/97813165/11350/index/9781316511350_index.pdf)

### Literary image-text books

- **1988:** *Nach der Natur: Ein Elementargedicht*. A book-length triptych in verse concerning Matthias Grünewald, Georg Wilhelm Steller and a partly autobiographical postwar speaker; “elementary poem,” historical narrative, biography and photographic book all apply, while none exhausts it. [Schütte’s scholarly overview](https://academic.oup.com/liverpool-scholarship-online/book/31202)

- **1990:** *Schwindel. Gefühle.* A four-part image-text prose composition joining Stendhal, an itinerant first-person narrator, Kafka and a return to the narrator’s Allgäu birthplace. Commonly marketed as fiction, it deliberately resists separation into novel, travelogue, biography, memoir and essay. [Schütte](https://academic.oup.com/liverpool-scholarship-online/book/31202)

- **1992:** *Die Ausgewanderten: Vier lange Erzählungen*. Four extended image-bearing narratives—“Dr. Henry Selwyn,” “Paul Bereyter,” “Ambros Adelwarth” and “Max Aurach”—made from testimony, photographs, documents, biography and fiction. “Max Aurach” became “Max Ferber” in English and in later German editions, a consequential textual alteration rather than a fifth narrative. [Work overview and name history](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_Ausgewanderten)

- **1995:** *Die Ringe des Saturn: Eine englische Wallfahrt*. A photographic prose journey through East Anglia whose digressions encompass natural history, colonial violence, biography, art and memory; “novel” and “travel book” each describe only part of the object. [Schütte](https://academic.oup.com/liverpool-scholarship-online/book/31202)

- **2001:** *Austerlitz*. A single, largely unchaptered image-text narrative organized around Jacques Austerlitz’s recovery of a childhood erased by displacement and the Holocaust. It is fiction, testimony, architectural history and photographic meditation without allowing those media to separate cleanly. [Schütte](https://academic.oup.com/liverpool-scholarship-online/book/31202)

### Poetry and collaborative visual works

- **1964–2001:** Sebald’s dispersed poetic corpus. Poems appeared intermittently in journals and newspapers—including five poems in *Zet* in 1974; “K.s Auswanderung,” “Mölkerbastei,” “Elisabethanisch” and “Unerschlossen” in 1975; early versions of the three sections of *Nach der Natur* in 1984–87; “Marienbader Elegie” in 1999; and six poems in *Akzente* in 2001. This is a body of individually made texts rather than a single book, later reconstructed editorially as *Über das Land und das Wasser*. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.wgsebald.de/literatur.html)

- **2001:** *For Years Now*. A collaborative artist’s book pairing Sebald’s brief English-language poems with Tess Jaray’s abstract colour images; neither illustration nor poetry is subordinate, making the book a small Gesamtkunstwerk. [*Saturn’s Moons* bibliographic framework](https://www.routledge.com/Saturns-Moons-A-WG-Sebald-Handbook/Catling/p/book/9780367603298)

- **2003, posthumous:** *Unerzählt: 33 Texte und 33 Radierungen*. Thirty-three very short texts by Sebald paired with thirty-three engravings of eyes by Jan Peter Tripp. Conceived collaboratively before Sebald’s death but published afterward, it is an image-text sequence rather than an illustrated poetry collection in the ordinary sense. [Hamburger’s translator’s note, cited in Oxford German Studies](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1179/0078719113Z.00000000043)

- **2008, posthumous:** *Über das Land und das Wasser: Ausgewählte Gedichte 1964–2001*. Sven Meyer’s estate-based selection of published and unpublished poems. It is an important manifestation of Sebald’s poetic corpus, but its selection, ordering and book-form are posthumous editorial work and therefore not wholly an object Sebald himself made. [Bibliographic record](https://www.wgsebald.de/literatur.html)

- **2011, posthumous:** *Gedichte aus dem Nachlass*. A further estate publication of poems omitted from earlier volumes; these are Sebald’s texts, but the collection as an obra is an editor’s posthumous construction. [Bibliographic record](https://www.wgsebald.de/literatur.html)

### Film and radio projects

- **c. 1980–1984:** *Jetzund kömpt die Nacht herbey: Ansichten aus dem Leben und Sterben des Immanuel Kant*. A projected television film preserved as an exposé and three script versions; broadcasters rejected it, so the film never existed. Its 2015 WDR radio production is a posthumous manifestation directed by Claudia Johanna Leist, not a production completed by Sebald. [Manuscript and production history](https://www.wgsebald.de/kant/kant.html)

- **1984–1989:** *Leben Ws: Skizze einer möglichen Szenenreihe für einen nichtrealisierten Film*. A proposed, deliberately achronological film about Ludwig Wittgenstein, surviving in an introduction and two drafts; Sebald sought funding in 1986 and published a fragment in 1989. The title itself identifies its resistance to “work” status: it is a project for a film that was never made. [Archival account](https://etd.ohiolink.edu/acprod/odb_etd/ws/send_file?accession=ucin1307321149&disposition=inline)

- **1994:** *Max Aurach*. A Bayerischer Rundfunk radio adaptation of the final narrative in *Die Ausgewanderten*, adapted and directed by Ulrich Gerhardt. Sebald participated in translating an existing prose obra into sound, so it is both adaptation and new manifestation rather than an independent originating story. [Audiovisual bibliography and broadcast record](https://www.wgsebald.de/literatur.html)

- **1995:** *Aurachs Mutter*. A second Gerhardt radio adaptation drawn from the maternal memoir embedded in “Max Aurach”; some sources call it *Ferbers Mutter*, reflecting the character’s later renamed form. The competing titles belong to the adaptation and transmission history and should not be silently standardized. [Adaptation record](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/W._G._Sebald)

- **c. 1999:** *To the Dark Shore*. A projected screen dramatization of *Die Ringe des Saturn* developed with APT Film and Television but never realized. It is an unfinished adaptation-project, not a lost completed film. [Project history](https://etd.ohiolink.edu/acprod/odb_etd/ws/send_file?accession=ucin1307321149&disposition=inline)

### Unfinished late prose projects

- **c. 1996–2001:** The Corsica Project, provisionally *Aufzeichnungen aus Korsika: Zur Natur- und Menschenkunde*. An extensive unfinished image-text prose project developed after *Die Ringe des Saturn*; the surviving archive is substantially larger than the four extracts later printed in *Campo Santo*. Its posthumous titles and arrangements must not be mistaken for a final book authorized by Sebald. [Cambridge assessment](https://assets.cambridge.org/97813165/11350/frontmatter/9781316511350_frontmatter.pdf)

- **2000–2001:** The “W. W.” or World War Project. An unfinished panoramic narrative intended to encompass 1900–1950, supported by a NESTA fellowship and researched through journeys, archives and interviews. It had no settled public title or final form at Sebald’s death, so “World War Project” names an intention and surviving research rather than a completed text. [Richard Hibbitt’s scholarly reconstruction](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/w-g-sebald-in-context/world-war-project/F2B20529EADC3B8AEAE094CC2DF6CD91)

### Posthumous assemblages and recovered occasional texts

- **2003, posthumous:** *Campo Santo: Prosa, Essays*. Sven Meyer’s estate-edited volume combines four surviving Corsican prose pieces with literary and cultural essays. The constituent texts are Sebald’s, but the heterogeneous book is a posthumous manifestation, not a collection whose final contents and order he approved. [Cambridge on the incomplete Corsica material](https://assets.cambridge.org/97813165/11350/frontmatter/9781316511350_frontmatter.pdf)

- **2008, posthumous:** *Aufzeichnungen aus Korsika: Zur Natur- & Menschenkunde*. A separate publication of material from the unfinished Corsica project. It should be counted as a documentary manifestation of that project, not as an additional completed Sebald book independent of *Campo Santo*. [Bibliographic record](https://www.wgsebald.de/literatur.html)

- **2008, posthumous:** *Zerstreute Reminiszenzen: Gedanken zur Eröffnung eines Stuttgarter Hauses*. An occasional address prepared for the opening of the Stuttgart Literaturhaus and issued later as a small book by Florian Höllerer. The speech is Sebald’s work; the standalone book is posthumous editorial presentation. [Bibliographic record](https://www.wgsebald.de/literatur.html)

- **Various dates; recovered 2011:** *Waterloo*. A short occasional prose piece recovered for *Saturn’s Moons*; the handbook establishes its existence but does not turn it into a book-length work. [Handbook contents](https://d-nb.info/1024052222/04)

- **Various dates; recovered 2011:** Two poems by Michael Hulse translated by Sebald. These are translations—made linguistic objects but not works originated by Sebald—and were recovered in *Saturn’s Moons*. [Handbook contents](https://d-nb.info/1024052222/04)

- **Various dates; recovered 2011:** *Feuer und Rauch*. A short recovered prose text; its occasional character and late recovery make its place in the finished œuvre uncertain. [Handbook contents](https://d-nb.info/1024052222/04)

- **1998; recovered 2011:** *Ausgrabung der Vergangenheit*. The text introducing Michael Hamburger’s reading was recovered and reprinted in *Saturn’s Moons*; this is a republication of the occasional work already listed, not another obra. [Oxford German Studies](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1179/0078719113Z.00000000043)

- **Date not securely established; recovered 2011:** *Michael Hamburger: Nomination for the Degree of Honorary DLitt*. A formal nomination text: institutional advocacy rather than literary work in the usual sense, though still a consequential text Sebald made to act upon the world. [Handbook contents](https://d-nb.info/1024052222/04)
