## Works by Benjamin Netanyahu

### Academic texts and unfinished projects

- **1973:** *Paper on the prospects for an Arab–Israeli pluralistic security community*. An apparently untitled paper written for a Harvard graduate course. Only its subject, not a fixed title or surviving publication, is documented; it therefore resists treatment as a formally published work. [[1]](https://news.mit.edu/1996/netanyahu-0605)
- **1975–1976:** *Unfinished architecture thesis*. A project toward an additional M.S. at MIT, abandoned after roughly one quarter had been completed; the available institutional account supplies no title. It is an unfinished project rather than a recoverable, complete text. [[1]](https://news.mit.edu/1996/netanyahu-0605)
- **1976:** *Computerization in the Newspaper Industry*. An M.S. management thesis co-authored under the name Benjamin Nitay with Zeev Zurr and advised by Lester C. Thurow. MIT’s repository preserves it as a completed academic text. [[2]](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/a16e9265-7160-4df1-91fe-55a285711a72)

### Edited memorial text

- **1978:** *Self-Portrait of a Hero: The Letters of Jonathan Netanyahu (1963–1976)*. A posthumous collection of Yonatan Netanyahu’s letters, co-edited with Iddo Netanyahu; Benjamin and Iddo supplied notes and an afterword. The letters themselves are Yonatan’s obra, so Benjamin’s made contribution is the editorial and memorial apparatus, not authorship of the principal text. [[3]](https://main.knesset.gov.il/en/apps/mk/mk-personal-details/90) [[4]](https://www.nli.org.il/en/articles/RAMBI990003244900705171/NLI)

### Conference proceedings and edited polemical collections

- **1979–1981:** *International Terrorism: Challenge and Response*. Edited proceedings of the 1979 Jerusalem Conference on International Terrorism, organized through the Jonathan Institute. Netanyahu’s official bibliography dates the work to 1979, while the documented book publication is 1981; the former is the conference date, the latter the manifestation as a volume. [[3]](https://main.knesset.gov.il/en/apps/mk/mk-personal-details/90) [[5]](https://www.routledge.com/International-Terrorism-Challenge-and-Response/Netanyahu/p/book/9781138526273) [[6]](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-D305-PURL-LPS57416/pdf)
- **1986:** *Terrorism: How the West Can Win*. An edited collection of essays advocating coordinated Western counterterrorism policy; Netanyahu also contributed framing material and a concluding argument. Some official Israeli listings give 1987, reflecting a later manifestation, but the Farrar, Straus and Giroux first edition appeared in 1986. [[3]](https://main.knesset.gov.il/en/apps/mk/mk-personal-details/90) [[7]](https://books.google.com/books/about/Terrorism.html?id=lISFAAAAIAAJ)

### Authored political books

- **1993:** *A Place Among the Nations: Israel and the World*. An authored work of political history and advocacy concerning Zionism, Israel’s security, and Israel’s relations with the Arab world and the West. The Knesset bibliography gives 1992, but the documented English book manifestation is dated 1993. [[3]](https://main.knesset.gov.il/en/apps/mk/mk-personal-details/90) [[8]](https://obnb.uk/p10711599-a-place-among-the-nations-israel-and-the-world)
- **1995:** *Fighting Terrorism: How Democracies Can Defeat Domestic and International Terrorists*. An authored counterterrorism argument and proposed program of action—part analysis, part protocol for democratic governments. Israeli official listings sometimes date it 1996, corresponding to the Hebrew manifestation, while the first English edition appeared in 1995. [[3]](https://main.knesset.gov.il/en/apps/mk/mk-personal-details/90) [[9]](https://us.macmillan.com/books/9780374524975/fightingterrorism/) [[10]](https://books.google.com/books/about/Fighting_Terrorism.html?id=2dVkaZF6uv4C)
- **2000:** *A Durable Peace: Israel and Its Place Among the Nations*. A revised and retitled manifestation of *A Place Among the Nations*, not an entirely independent obra; cataloguing explicitly identifies it as the revised edition of the 1993 book. Its substantial revision and new title make it worth naming while preserving that dependence. [[11]](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL16964420M/A_durable_peace) [[12]](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/23739770.2021.1892311)
- **2022:** *Bibi: My Story*. An autobiography covering Netanyahu’s family history, military service, political formation, and career. It is both memoir and political self-presentation rather than a neutral historical account. [[13]](https://www.simonandschuster.net/books/Bibi/Benjamin-Netanyahu/9781668008454)
