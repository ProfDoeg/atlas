## Works by Adolfo Bioy Casares

### Juvenilia and repudiated books

- **1929:** *Prólogo*. A privately printed collection of stories and miscellany, corrected in part by Bioy’s father; Bioy later repudiated it and excluded it from his recognized oeuvre, but that rejection is part of the object’s history. ([Instituto Cervantes](https://elcairo.cervantes.es/es/biblioteca_espanol/biografia_adolfo_bioy_casares_espanol.htm))
- **1933:** *Diecisiete disparos contra lo porvenir*. A story collection published as Martín Sacastrú and subsequently repudiated by Bioy; some bibliographies normalize the title as *17 disparos contra lo porvenir*. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/su_obra_bibliografia/))
- **1934:** *Caos*. A collection of stories later withdrawn from Bioy’s authorized narrative corpus. Its resistance to “work” status comes from the author’s own attempted erasure, not from any doubt that the book existed. ([Instituto Cervantes](https://elcairo.cervantes.es/es/biblioteca_espanol/biografia_adolfo_bioy_casares_espanol.htm))
- **1935:** *La nueva tormenta o La vida múltiple de Juan Ruteno*. An early novel issued by the author and later repudiated; sources disagree in typography, giving the relation between its two titles as either alternative or subtitle. ([Instituto Cervantes](https://www.cervantes.es/bibliotecas_documentacion_espanol/biografias/cairo_adolfo_bioy_casares_1.htm))
- **1936:** *La estatua casera*. An illustrated miscellany or story collection, with illustrations by Silvina Ocampo, later classed by Bioy among his “forgotten” books. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/autor_cronologia/))
- **1937:** *Luis Greve, muerto*. A story collection published under the imprint of *Destiempo* and later disowned with the other pre-1940 books. ([Instituto Cervantes](https://elcairo.cervantes.es/es/biblioteca_espanol/biografia_adolfo_bioy_casares_espanol.htm))

### Novels and novellas

- **1940:** *La invención de Morel*. A fantastic novella and the point from which Bioy elected to recognize his mature oeuvre. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/su_obra_bibliografia/))
- **1945:** *Plan de evasión*. A novel of perception, experimentation and imprisonment. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/su_obra_bibliografia/))
- **1954:** *El sueño de los héroes*. A novel joining Buenos Aires social life, fatal recurrence and the fantastic. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/su_obra_bibliografia/))
- **1969:** *Diario de la guerra del cerdo*. A novel presented through a diary-like chronology, about organized violence against the old. ([Biblioteca Nacional Mariano Moreno](https://www.bn.gob.ar/noticias/15-de-septiembre-de-1914-nace-en-buenos-aires-adolfo-bioy-casares))
- **1973:** *Dormir al sol*. A fantastic novel organized around psychiatric confinement, exchanged identities and the uncertain relation between body and personality. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/su_obra_bibliografia/))
- **1980–2001:** *Irse*. An unfinished novel-project begun in 1980; Daniel Martino reconstructed and published its first four chapters in 2001. It remains a project and fragment rather than a completed novel. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/autor_cronologia/))
- **1985:** *La aventura de un fotógrafo en La Plata*. A novel about a provincial photographer drawn into an increasingly coercive family arrangement; the title’s photographer is also a way of thinking about framing and capture. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/su_obra_bibliografia/))
- **1993:** *Un campeón desparejo*. A short novel centered on an unevenly gifted boxer and the social world around him. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/su_obra_bibliografia/))
- **1998:** *De un mundo a otro*. Bioy’s final published novel, an interplanetary fantasy and comic adventure. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/su_obra_bibliografia/))

### Story-books and hybrid literary objects

- **1945:** *El perjurio de la nieve*. A separately published long story; it was later incorporated into *La trama celeste*, so it is both an autonomous booklet and a constituent text of another book. ([Instituto Cervantes](https://www.cervantes.es/bibliotecas_documentacion_espanol/biografias/cairo_adolfo_bioy_casares_1.htm))
- **1948:** *La trama celeste*. A collection of fantastic stories, including revised or absorbed earlier material. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/su_obra_bibliografia/))
- **1949:** *Las vísperas de Fausto*. A small separately issued story-book whose texts also migrated through later collections; its bibliographical identity is therefore less stable than that of a conventional new collection. ([Instituto Cervantes](https://elcairo.cervantes.es/es/biblioteca_espanol/biografia_adolfo_bioy_casares_espanol.htm))
- **1954:** *Homenaje a Francisco Almeyra*. A separately issued narrative or commemorative fiction, later absorbed into Bioy’s collected narrative. ([Instituto Cervantes](https://www.cervantes.es/bibliotecas_documentacion_espanol/biografias/cairo_adolfo_bioy_casares_1.htm))
- **1956:** *Historia prodigiosa*. A story collection; later editions changed its contents, making the title a variable container rather than one invariant textual object. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/su_obra_bibliografia/))
- **1959:** *Guirnalda con amores*. A deliberately mixed construction of stories, brief narratives, aphorisms, observations and poems; it resists classification as merely a story-book. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/su_obra_bibliografia/))
- **1962:** *El lado de la sombra*. A collection combining fantastic and amorous stories. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/su_obra_bibliografia/))
- **1967:** *El gran Serafín*. A collection of stories, not a single novella despite the title’s singular form. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/autor_cronologia/))
- **1978:** *El héroe de las mujeres*. A story collection; some institutional chronologies incorrectly place it in 1979, while the scholarly bibliography and Bioy chronology give 1978. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/autor_cronologia/))
- **1983:** *Margarita o el poder de la farmacopea*. A separately issued story subsequently collected in *Historias desaforadas*; its autonomy is bibliographical as much as generic. ([Instituto Cervantes](https://www.cervantes.es/bibliotecas_documentacion_espanol/biografias/cairo_adolfo_bioy_casares_1.htm))
- **1986:** *Historias desaforadas*. A collection of stories extending Bioy’s fantastic fiction into grotesque and comic forms. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/su_obra_bibliografia/))
- **1987:** *Encuentro en Rauch*. A separately published short narrative rather than a full new collection. ([Instituto Cervantes](https://www.cervantes.es/bibliotecas_documentacion_espanol/biografias/cairo_adolfo_bioy_casares_1.htm))
- **1987:** *Catón*. A separately issued brief text; later aggregation makes it difficult to treat as a wholly independent “book” in the same sense as the major collections. ([Instituto Cervantes](https://www.cervantes.es/bibliotecas_documentacion_espanol/biografias/cairo_adolfo_bioy_casares_1.htm))
- **1987:** *A propósito de un olor*. A separately issued short narrative, subsequently part of Bioy’s broader collected corpus. ([Instituto Cervantes](https://www.cervantes.es/bibliotecas_documentacion_espanol/biografias/cairo_adolfo_bioy_casares_1.htm))
- **1991:** *Una muñeca rusa*. A collection of stories, several of which had first appeared in newspapers. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/autor_cronologia/))
- **1997:** *Una magia modesta*. A late story collection whose “modest magic” names Bioy’s increasingly domestic form of the fantastic. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/su_obra_bibliografia/))

### Essays, lexicography and documentary books

- **1968:** *La otra aventura*. A book of literary essays and criticism rather than fiction. ([Biblioteca Nacional Mariano Moreno](https://www.bn.gob.ar/noticias/15-de-septiembre-de-1914-nace-en-buenos-aires-adolfo-bioy-casares))
- **1970:** *Memoria sobre la pampa y los gauchos*. An essayistic and documentary book combining Bioy’s text with photographs; because word and image make the object together, it is closer to a small Gesamtkunstwerk than to a conventional essay collection. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/autor_cronologia/))
- **1971; revised 1978:** *Breve diccionario del argentino exquisito*. A satirical lexicographical work first signed “Javier Miranda”; the 1978 version appeared under Bioy’s name with a new prologue, corrections and additional entries, so the two manifestations are not wholly identical. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/autor_cronologia/))
- **1999:** *De las cosas maravillosas*. A late collection of essays and reflections, and the last book published during Bioy’s lifetime. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/autor_cronologia/))

### Diaries, memoirs, letters and commonplace books

- **1991:** *Unos días de viaje en el Brasil*. A travel diary issued as a special publication; it records a journey rather than constructing a conventional fiction. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/autor_cronologia/))
- **1994:** *Memorias: infancia, adolescencia y cómo se hace un escritor*. An autobiography shaped as a retrospective book rather than an unedited diary. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/autor_cronologia/))
- **1996:** *En viaje (1967)*. A sequence of letters written to Silvina Ocampo during a 1967 journey and assembled posthumously as an epistolary travel book; the composition date and book date therefore differ. ([Instituto Cervantes](https://www.cervantes.es/bibliotecas_documentacion_espanol/biografias/cairo_adolfo_bioy_casares_1.htm))
- **1997:** *De jardines ajenos: libro abierto*. A commonplace book of quotations, phrases, poems and miscellaneous texts, edited with Daniel Martino. Authorship is intentionally porous because collection, transcription and original composition coexist. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/su_obra_bibliografia/))
- **2001:** *Descanso de caminantes: diarios íntimos*. A posthumous selection from Bioy’s private diaries, edited by Daniel Martino; it is Bioy’s writing but not a final book-form fixed by Bioy alone. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/su_obra_bibliografia/))
- **2006; expanded 2026:** *Borges*. Bioy’s vast diary-portrait of Jorge Luis Borges, prepared as a project before Bioy’s death and edited by Daniel Martino from diaries, correspondence and papers; the substantially expanded 2026 edition shows that “the work” and any one published manifestation do not coincide. ([El País](https://elpais.com/argentina/2026-10-02/cotilleo-maledicencia-y-pura-literatura-publican-una-nueva-edicion-ampliada-del-borges-de-bioy-casares.html))
- **2021:** *Wilcock*. A posthumously edited diary-portrait of Juan Rodolfo Wilcock assembled by Daniel Martino from Bioy’s private papers. Martino notes that such a book may have been among Bioy’s plans, but its final form is editorially posthumous and therefore only partly Bioy’s completed obra. ([Eterna Cadencia](https://eternacadencia.com.ar/blog/wilcock-por-bioy-))

### Theatre and dramatic projects

- **1968:** *Siete soñadores*. A one-act “tragicomedy,” called a comedy by Bioy, published in *Sur*; it is a dramatic text rather than narrative prose. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/autor_cronologia/))
- **1971:** *La cueva de vidrio*. A comedy written at Mar del Plata. The standard chronology records its composition but not a contemporary publication or production, so it survives bibliographically as a dramatic project rather than a securely manifested public work. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/autor_cronologia/))

### Works made with Silvina Ocampo

- **1946:** *Los que aman, odian*. A detective novel written jointly with Silvina Ocampo; neither collaborator’s separate corpus adequately describes its composite authorship. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/autor_cronologia/))

### The “third writer”: works made with Jorge Luis Borges

- **1936–1937:** *Destiempo*. A literary magazine created with Borges and nominally directed by Ernesto Pissavini; only three numbers appeared. It is an editorial project and collective manifestation, not simply a text authored by either founder. ([Instituto Cervantes](https://elcairo.cervantes.es/es/biblioteca_espanol/biografia_adolfo_bioy_casares_espanol.htm))
- **1937:** *La leche cuajada*. A commercial explanatory pamphlet written with Borges for the dairy firm La Martona. It is applied prose—part promotion, part protocol for understanding and using a product—rather than a literary book. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/autor_cronologia/))
- **1942:** *Seis problemas para don Isidro Parodi*. Six detective stories written with Borges and signed by their fictional author H. Bustos Domecq; the pseudonym became a collaborative authorial manifestation often described as a “third writer.” ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/su_obra_bibliografia/))
- **1943:** *Los mejores cuentos policiales, primera serie*. An anthology selected with Borges, with a jointly written prologue. Its primary act is curatorial—selection and arrangement—rather than the sole creation of the constituent stories. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/su_obra_bibliografia/))
- **1946:** *Dos fantasías memorables*. Two collaborative fictions privately issued as the work of H. Bustos Domecq. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/su_obra_bibliografia/))
- **1946:** *Un modelo para la muerte*. A collaborative detective novella signed B. Suárez Lynch, another mask of the Borges–Bioy authorial compound. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/su_obra_bibliografia/))
- **1947; collected 1977:** *La fiesta del Monstruo*. A collaborative political satire by Borges and Bioy, initially published separately and later incorporated into *Nuevos cuentos de Bustos Domecq*; its attribution belongs to the Bustos Domecq collaboration even where editions foreground Borges and Bioy. ([Penguin, *Alias*](https://www.penguinlibros.com/es/novela-negra-misterio-y-thriller/310601-libro-alias-9788426413536))
- **1945–1955:** *El Séptimo Círculo*. A detective-fiction series directed by Borges and Bioy for Emecé. It is an extended editorial project—selection, commissioning and presentation of other writers’ works—rather than a jointly authored book. ([Instituto Cervantes](https://elcairo.cervantes.es/es/biblioteca_espanol/biografia_adolfo_bioy_casares_espanol.htm))
- **1951:** *Los mejores cuentos policiales, segunda serie*. A second collaboratively selected anthology, again a curatorial object rather than an original story collection. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/su_obra_bibliografia/))
- **1955:** *Cuentos breves y extraordinarios*. A collaborative anthology of miniature narratives, excerpts, translations, adaptations and some invented attributions; it intentionally troubles the boundary between selection and authorship. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/su_obra_bibliografia/))
- **1955:** *Poesía gauchesca*. A critical anthology prepared with Borges; its made object is the selection, ordering and scholarly apparatus, not the pre-existing poems themselves. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/su_obra_bibliografia/))
- **1960:** *Libro del cielo y del infierno*. A thematic anthology compiled with Borges from many traditions. Some bibliographical pages misdate it 1966, but the documented first edition is Buenos Aires, Sur, 1960. ([University of Pittsburgh Borges Center](https://www.borges.pitt.edu/sites/default/files/Corinne%20Ferrero.pdf))
- **1967:** *Crónicas de Bustos Domecq*. Collaborative satirical chronicles signed through the Bustos Domecq persona; some bibliographies give 1965, but the standard publication date is 1967. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes, chronology](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/autor_cronologia/))
- **1977:** *Nuevos cuentos de Bustos Domecq*. The final new book of Borges–Bioy collaborative stories, continuing the fictive authorship of Bustos Domecq. ([Penguin, *Alias*](https://www.penguinlibros.com/es/novela-negra-misterio-y-thriller/310601-libro-alias-9788426413536))
- **2002:** *Museo: textos inéditos*. A posthumous gathering of collaborative pieces originally dispersed through *Destiempo*, *Los Anales de Buenos Aires* and related venues. The volume is an editorial reconstruction, not a book-form completed by Borges and Bioy themselves. ([Google Books](https://books.google.com/books?id=EHtdAAAAMAAJ))

### Work made with Borges and Silvina Ocampo

- **1940; expanded 1965:** *Antología de la literatura fantástica*. A jointly selected and translated anthology by Borges, Bioy and Silvina Ocampo; the enlarged 1965 manifestation materially changes the original object. Its mixture of selection, translation, rewriting and unattributed intervention makes “authorship” deliberately unstable. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/su_obra_bibliografia/))
- **1941:** *Antología poética argentina*. A poetry anthology selected with Borges and Silvina Ocampo; it is a collective curatorial construction, not a collection of poems written by the three compilers. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/autor_cronologia/))

### Cinema: scripts and film-projects

- **1940; published 1955:** *El paraíso de los creyentes*. A screenplay or “cinematographic argument” written with Borges. It remained unrealized as a film and was published with *Los orilleros*, so its project-status is central to what it is. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/su_obra_bibliografia/))
- **1955:** *Los orilleros*. A screenplay written with Borges and published as a cinematographic argument; a television film was eventually made in 1975, but that adaptation is not itself Bioy’s work. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/autor_cronologia/))
- **1969:** *Invasión*. A film-script written with Borges for director Hugo Santiago, realized as Santiago’s film that year. The screenplay is collaborative; the completed film should not be attributed to Bioy alone. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/autor_cronologia/))
- **1971–1974:** *Les autres*. A film project and screenplay made with Borges and Hugo Santiago, filmed under Santiago’s direction and published in Paris in 1974. Because writing and realization cross three makers and two media, it approaches a Gesamtkunstwerk rather than a self-contained literary text. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/autor_cronologia/))

### Self-anthologies and editorial manifestations

- **1969:** *Adversos milagros*. An anthology of Bioy’s stories selected and introduced by Enrique Pezzoni; it is a new editorial manifestation of existing works, not a new primary story collection made wholly by Bioy. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/autor_cronologia/))
- **1972:** *Historias fantásticas*. Bioy’s thematic selection of previously published stories; the selection is the new made object. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/su_obra_bibliografia/))
- **1972:** *Historias de amor*. A complementary thematic selection of previously published stories; again, the obra is curatorial rather than a body of newly written narratives. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/su_obra_bibliografia/))
- **1981:** *La trama celeste y otros relatos*. A retrospective anthology selected and introduced by Carlos Dámaso Martínez; it belongs to Bioy’s publication history but its particular form is principally the editor’s work. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/su_obra_bibliografia/))
- **1985:** *Obras escogidas*. A selection of Bioy’s fiction with a prologue by Vlady Kociancich; it is an editorial repackaging rather than a newly composed obra. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/autor_cronologia/))
- **1985:** *Páginas de Adolfo Bioy Casares seleccionadas por el autor*. A self-anthology: its new creative act is Bioy’s own selection and arrangement of earlier writing. ([Instituto Cervantes](https://www.cervantes.es/bibliotecas_documentacion_espanol/biografias/cairo_adolfo_bioy_casares_1.htm))
- **1988:** *El viaje y la otra realidad*. A critical volume by Graciela Scheines combining an essay with five Bioy stories; it contains Bioy’s work but, as a total book-object, is not properly his sole obra. ([Instituto Cervantes](https://www.cervantes.es/bibliotecas_documentacion_espanol/biografias/cairo_adolfo_bioy_casares_1.htm))
- **1988:** *La invención y la trama*. A retrospective anthology selected and annotated by Marcelo Pichon Rivière; it is a critical-editorial manifestation of earlier works. ([Instituto Cervantes](https://www.cervantes.es/bibliotecas_documentacion_espanol/biografias/cairo_adolfo_bioy_casares_1.htm))

### Other projects and manifestations

- **1923:** *El batitú*. A childhood magazine written with Drago Mitre and J. and C. Menditeguy. It is an amateur collective project rather than part of Bioy’s professional literary oeuvre, but the standard chronology records it as something he made. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/autor_cronologia/))
- **1928:** *Vanidad o una aventura terrorífica*. Bioy’s first recorded fantastic-detective story. The chronology records its composition, but not a contemporary public edition; it therefore survives primarily as a biographical manuscript-work. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/adolfo_bioy_casares/autor_cronologia/))
- **1930s:** *Pasado mortal*. An abandoned novel-project. Because it was not completed or authorized as a book, intention and surviving draft—not publication—constitute the work. ([Universidad Nacional de La Plata](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/13020/Documento_completo.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1))
- **1930s:** *El problema de la torre china*. An abandoned novel-project known through Bioy’s early papers and chronology; it should not be silently promoted to the status of a finished novel. ([Universidad Nacional de La Plata](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/13020/Documento_completo.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1))
- **1930s:** *La navaja del muerto*. An abandoned narrative project surviving through draft or plot-summary evidence; the critical edition treats that incomplete documentary remainder separately from Bioy’s finished books. ([Universidad Nacional de La Plata](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/13020/Documento_completo.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1))
- **Across his adult life:** *Photographic archive*. Bioy made photographs extensively, but the photographs were not organized by him into a fixed catalogue raisonné or autonomous photographic oeuvre. The archive therefore resists enumeration as individually titled works: it is a visual practice and surviving body of images, while *Memoria sobre la pampa y los gauchos* is its clearest authorially published manifestation. ([Biblioteca Nacional Mariano Moreno, Bioy–Ocampo collection](https://www.bn.gob.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/files/c21d0c7d3826a9843731eb622093dbde.pdf))

The chronology is based in turn on Bioy’s *Memorias*, Suzanne Jill Levine’s publication of his *Autocronología*, Teresita Mauro’s chronology and Daniel Martino’s *ABC de Adolfo Bioy Casares*.
- Instituto Cervantes, [“Adolfo Bioy Casares: biografía y bibliografía”](https://elcairo.cervantes.es/es/biblioteca_espanol/biografia_adolfo_bioy_casares_espanol.htm).
- Instituto Cervantes, [“Cronología de obras”](https://www.cervantes.es/bibliotecas_documentacion_espanol/biografias/cairo_adolfo_bioy_casares_1.htm).
- Daniel Martino, ed., [contents and apparatus for *Obra completa*, vol. I](https://toc.library.ethz.ch/objects/pdf03/z01_978-950-04-3453-9_01.pdf), including notes on individual texts, alternative versions, plot summaries and works not collected in volume.
- Biblioteca Nacional Mariano Moreno, [“15 de septiembre de 1914: nace en Buenos Aires Adolfo Bioy Casares”](https://www.bn.gob.ar/noticias/15-de-septiembre-de-1914-nace-en-buenos-aires-adolfo-bioy-casares).
- Biblioteca Nacional Mariano Moreno, [*La biblioteca personal de Adolfo Bioy Casares y Silvina Ocampo*](https://www.bn.gob.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/files/c21d0c7d3826a9843731eb622093dbde.pdf).
- Penguin Random House, [*Alias: obra completa en colaboración*](https://www.penguinlibros.com/es/novela-negra-misterio-y-thriller/310601-libro-alias-9788426413536), the modern collected edition of the Borges–Bioy collaborative corpus.
- Ediciones Destino and Daniel Martino, as reported in [*El País*, “Publicada una nueva edición ampliada del ‘Borges’ de Bioy Casares”](https://elpais.com/argentina/2026-10-02/cotilleo-maledicencia-y-pura-literatura-publican-una-nueva-edicion-ampliada-del-borges-de-bioy-casares.html).
- Daniel Martino, [editorial account of *Wilcock*](https://eternacadencia.com.ar/blog/wilcock-por-bioy-).
