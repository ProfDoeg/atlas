## Works by Julen Kerman Madariaga Agirre

### Collective political projects

- **1952–1953:** *Ekin*. Clandestine study group and nationalist organization co-created by Madariaga and several fellow students; it began during the 1952–53 academic year, although sources often shorten the date to 1952. This is an institutional project rather than a solely authored obra, and its name originally belonged to the group’s internal bulletin. ([Iura Vasconiae](https://www.iuravasconiae.eus/eu/voz/madariaga-agirre-julen-905-eu/?nivel=347); [Ahotsak](https://gerra.ahotsak.eus/gaiak/0806/bil-003-008))

- **1958–1959:** *Euskadi Ta Askatasuna (ETA)*. Political organization collectively created from Ekin, with Madariaga among its founders; it became an armed organization, so calling it simply a “work” risks concealing the violence subsequently performed in its name. Its date is disputed: Madariaga described a process extending from the December 1958 decision and adoption of a new name to the letter of 31 July 1959 announcing the organization. ([Iura Vasconiae](https://www.iuravasconiae.eus/eu/voz/madariaga-agirre-julen-905-eu/?nivel=347); [El País interview](https://elpais.com/diario/2009/07/26/domingo/1248580355_850215.html); [Santiago de Pablo, “Julio de 1959: el nacimiento de ETA”](https://es.scribd.com/document/598449846/El-nacimiento-de-ETA))

- **2001:** *Aralar*. Collective political project co-founded by Madariaga and Patxi Zabaleta as a nonviolent current within the Basque nationalist left; it later became an independent political party. Sources differ in emphasis between Madariaga’s being a founder and his joining the current, so sole or primary authorship cannot be assigned to him. ([Iura Vasconiae](https://www.iuravasconiae.eus/eu/voz/madariaga-agirre-julen-905-eu/?nivel=347); [El Periódico](https://www.elperiodico.cat/opinio/20210406/julen-madariaga-militant-eta-va-defensar-11638850))

### Political interventions and collective declarations

- **October 1970:** *Análisis y crítica del trabajo titulado “Notas sobre el problema nacional” (Elaborado y difundido por los españolistas liquidacionistas recientemente expulsados de ETA)*. Signed polemical article published in *Zutik*, no. 57, pp. 1–2; it is an intervention intended to act within an organizational conflict and therefore approaches a manifesto more closely than a detached essay. ([Iura Vasconiae, “Obras”](https://www.iuravasconiae.eus/eu/voz/madariaga-agirre-julen-905-eu/?nivel=347))

- **22–29 November 1979:** *El nacimiento de ETA*. Collective historical-political declaration by Txillardegi, José Manuel Agirre, José Mari Benito del Valle and Madariaga, published in *Punto y Hora de Euskal Herria*, no. 152, p. 44. Its authorship is shared, and the bibliographical record does not establish how much of the wording was Madariaga’s. ([Jakin, *Txillardegiren gaikako aurkibidea*](https://jakin.eus/wp-content/themes/jakin/digitalizazioa/img/txillardegi_gaikako_aurkibidea.pdf?v=1.3.0))

- **8 December 1979; published February 1980:** *[Carta de los señores Aguirre, Benito del Valle, Madariaga y Alvarez Enparanza]*. Joint letter published in *Muga*, no. 3, pp. 13–14; the surviving bibliographical title is editorial and descriptive rather than a fixed title supplied by its authors. It resists treatment as an independent literary work: it is correspondence made public as a collective political intervention. ([Jakin, *Txillardegiren gaikako aurkibidea*](https://jakin.eus/wp-content/themes/jakin/digitalizazioa/img/txillardegi_gaikako_aurkibidea.pdf?v=1.3.0))

### Autobiographical testimony

- **2014:** *Egiari zor*. Basque-language autobiography and political testimony, published by Erein; its publisher presents it as Madariaga’s final first-person reckoning with his life and with the history and errors of the political movements in which he participated. ([Iura Vasconiae, “Obras”](https://www.iuravasconiae.eus/eu/voz/madariaga-agirre-julen-905-eu/?nivel=347); [Erein edition record via Katakrak](https://www.katakrak.net/eus/lib/egiari-zor))

- **2020:** *En honor a la verdad: autobiografía*. Spanish manifestation of *Egiari zor*, translated by Jela Martínez Urmeneta and “corrected and expanded by the author,” with a prologue by Antoni Batista. It is therefore not simply another original work, but neither is it a mechanically equivalent edition: Madariaga’s revisions and additions give this manifestation its own authorial status. ([Euskariana](https://www.euskariana.euskadi.eus/euskadibib/es/bib/1827587.do?lo=grid&position=5&queryId=26292))
