## Works by Johannes Reuchlin

### Lexica and language manuals

- **1475–1478:** *Vocabularius breviloquus*. A Latin dictionary assembled or redacted by Reuchlin for students. The work is conventionally dated 1475, while the critical *Sämtliche Werke* assigns it to 1478; its many early editions are manifestations of one made thing, not separate works. ([HAB manuscript description](https://diglib.hab.de/?catalog=Schnabel&db=mss&hi=Johannes&id=ki-ub-bord-112-2&list=ms); [*Sämtliche Werke* plan](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1706?lang=de))

- **c. 1478:** *Micropaedia sive grammatica Graeca*. An elementary Greek grammar surviving in manuscript and apparently never printed during Reuchlin’s lifetime; it is therefore an unfinished or unpublished pedagogical project rather than a publication. ([Meyers account](https://meyers.de-academic.com/116673/Reuchlin))

- **probably 1480s:** *De quatuor idiomatibus Graecae linguae*. A short account of four Greek dialects, transmitted with Reuchlin’s Greek teaching materials and first published much later. Its date is not fixed. ([Meyers account](https://meyers.de-academic.com/116673/Reuchlin); [Bibliotheca Augustana work-list](https://www.tha.de/~harsch/Chronologia/Lspost15/Reuchlin/reu_intr.html))

- **probably 1480s:** *Colloquia Graeca*. Greek instructional dialogues: classroom material rather than a conventional authored treatise, preserved without a secure date and published only centuries later. ([Meyers account](https://meyers.de-academic.com/116673/Reuchlin); [Bibliotheca Augustana work-list](https://www.tha.de/~harsch/Chronologia/Lspost15/Reuchlin/reu_intr.html))

- **1506:** *De rudimentis Hebraicis*. A large Hebrew–Latin lexicon and grammar designed as a practical protocol for learning biblical Hebrew; its dictionary and grammatical sections together form one pedagogical work. ([Catholic Encyclopedia](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12798a.htm); [Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz74609.html))

- **1512:** *In septem psalmos poenitentiales Hebraicos interpretatio de verbo ad verbum*. A Hebrew text of the seven Penitential Psalms with a deliberately literal Latin interlinear translation and brief instructional commentary—a reader and language-learning instrument as much as a biblical translation. ([Catholic Encyclopedia](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12798a.htm); [PRDL record](https://www.prdl.org/author_view.php?a_id=865))

- **1518:** *De accentibus et orthographia linguae Hebraicae libri tres*. A Hebrew orthography and accent manual, including discussion of rhetorical and musical accentuation; it functions as both linguistic treatise and performance protocol. ([Catholic Encyclopedia](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12798a.htm); [BLB Karlsruhe](https://digital.blb-karlsruhe.de/name/view/5504946))

- **before 1522; printed 1537:** *Lexicon Hebraicum*. A posthumously printed lexical text associated with Reuchlin. Its status as a separate made work is uncertain, since it may substantially reproduce or reorganize the lexicon contained in *De rudimentis Hebraicis* rather than represent an independent book. ([Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz74609.html); [survey of editions](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Reuchlin))

### Philosophical and kabbalistic dialogues

- **1494:** *De verbo mirifico*. A three-book philosophical-theological dialogue among an Epicurean, a Jewish scholar, and the Christian “Capnion”; it stages inquiry rather than simply expounding a doctrine and is therefore both dialogue and speculative project. ([*Sämtliche Werke*, I.1](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1706?lang=de); [Catholic Encyclopedia](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12798a.htm))

- **1517:** *De arte cabalistica libri tres*. A three-book dialogue presenting Christian Kabbalah through Jewish and classical materials; it is a syncretic philosophical construction, not a neutral description of Jewish Kabbalah. Only one edition appeared in Reuchlin’s lifetime. ([*Sämtliche Werke*, II.1](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1706?lang=de); [critical-edition report](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/sites/fh/files/public/downloads/reuchlin_werke_ii_1_inhaltsverzeichnis.pdf))

### Drama

- **1497–1498:** *Scaenica progymnasmata*, also called *Henno*. A Neo-Latin comedy derived in part from the French *Farce de maître Pathelin* but reshaped with contemporary social and legal satire; it is a theatrical script, not merely a literary “book.” ([*Sämtliche Werke* plan, III.1](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1706?lang=de); [ADB](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/ADB%3AReuchlin%2C_Johannes))

- **c. 1498; printed 1507:** *Sergius vel Capitis caput*. A Neo-Latin satirical comedy directed against a corrupt clerical figure; composition and first printing are separated by several years, so 1507 dates its manifestation rather than necessarily its making. ([*Sämtliche Werke* plan, III.1](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1706?lang=de); [Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz74609.html))

### Orations, poems, and occasional compositions

- **1498:** *Oratio ad Alexandrum VI pontificem maximum pro Philippo Bavariae duce*. A diplomatic speech made for Elector Philip at the papal court and then printed by Aldus Manutius; it is an act of representation as well as a text. ([LEO-BW](https://www.leo-bw.de/fr/detail/-/Detail/details/DOKUMENT/wlb_digitalisate/digibib-kxp17879926082/Oratio%20ad%20Alexandrum%20VI%20pro%20Philippo%20Bavariae%20Duce); [*Sämtliche Werke* plan, III.1](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1706?lang=de))

- **various dates:** *Gedichte*. Reuchlin made a dispersed body of Latin and Greek occasional verse rather than a single authorially constituted collection. The projected critical edition treats the poems collectively; individual titles and dates are not consistently fixed, so forcing them into invented separate “books” would misrepresent the corpus. ([*Sämtliche Werke* plan, III.1](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1706?lang=de))

- **various dates:** *Reden*. A dispersed corpus of ceremonial, academic, diplomatic, and forensic speeches. Some survive because they were printed or copied, but “the speeches” resist treatment as one work and the critical edition accordingly gathers them as a class of acts and texts. ([*Sämtliche Werke* plan, III.1](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1706?lang=de))

### Preaching protocol

- **c. 1502; printed 1504:** *Liber congestorum de arte praedicandi*. A compact manual for constructing sermons: explicitly a protocol for preachers, organized around rhetorical and dialectical procedures. Sources differ between the likely composition date and the first-print date. ([Cambridge University Press discussion](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/A2F304B63849649B00228649D38FBF6F/9781009564045int_1-48.pdf/introduction.pdf); [Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz74609.html))

### Translations, editions, and philological remakings

- **1473–1474; printed 1520:** *Xenophontis Apologia Socratis*. Reuchlin’s Latin translation of Xenophon’s *Apology of Socrates*, made during his early Greek studies but printed much later with Greek teaching texts; the making and publication dates must not be conflated. ([*Catalogus Translationum et Commentariorum* study](https://catalogustranslationum.org/PDFs/volume07/v07_xenophon.pdf); [Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz74609.html))

- **1473–1474; printed 1520:** *Xenophontis Agesilaus*. A Latin translation or bilingual pedagogical presentation of Xenophon’s *Agesilaus*, belonging to Reuchlin’s early Greek project and only later manifested in print. ([*Catalogus Translationum et Commentariorum* study](https://catalogustranslationum.org/PDFs/volume07/v07_xenophon.pdf); [Meyers account](https://meyers.de-academic.com/116673/Reuchlin))

- **1473–1474; printed 1520:** *Xenophontis Hiero*. A Latin translation or teaching edition of Xenophon’s dialogue *Hiero*, likewise an early philological making with a late printed manifestation. ([*Catalogus Translationum et Commentariorum* study](https://catalogustranslationum.org/PDFs/volume07/v07_xenophon.pdf); [Meyers account](https://meyers.de-academic.com/116673/Reuchlin))

- **1501:** *Marcus Tullius Cicero inn Tusculanischen Fragen*. A German translation of Book I of Cicero’s *Tusculan Disputations*, accompanied by Reuchlin’s notes and explanations and preserved in a presentation manuscript. It is incomplete as a translation of Cicero’s five-book work, but complete as the surviving commissioned object. ([Heidelberg manuscript catalogue](https://www.ub.uni-heidelberg.de/digi-pdf-katalogisate/sammlung2/werk/pdf/cpg482.pdf); [Handschriftencensus](https://handschriftencensus.de/20541))

- **1510:** *Batrachomyomachia Homeri*. A Greek–Latin teaching edition of the pseudo-Homeric *Battle of the Frogs and Mice* issued under Reuchlin’s Greek name, Capnion. The accompanying Latin version has been attributed to him, but scholarship also identifies it as Carlo Marsuppini’s translation; Reuchlin’s secure role is therefore editorial or pedagogical rather than certainly translational. ([PRDL](https://www.prdl.org/author_view.php?a_id=865); [study noting the attribution problem](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/367666634_Classroom_Commentaries_and_their_Communities_of_Interpretation_in_Central_Europe_in_the_1510s_and_1520s))

- **1512:** *Hippocrates, De praeparatione hominis ad Ptolemaeum regem*. A Latin translation or philological edition of a pseudo-Hippocratic text prepared for humanist study; the ancient authorship is itself pseudonymous. ([PRDL](https://www.prdl.org/author_view.php?a_id=865); [Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz74609.html))

- **1512:** *Rabi Joseph Hyssopaeus, Lanx argentea*. Reuchlin’s Latin translation of the Hebrew ethical poem *Kĕʿarat kesef* by Joseph ben Ḥanan ben Nathan Ezobi. It is a cross-linguistic remake of another author’s poem, not an original poem by Reuchlin. ([BLB Karlsruhe](https://digital.blb-karlsruhe.de/name/view/5504946); [Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz74609.html))

- **1512:** *Comedia nova quae Veterator inscribitur, alias Pathelinus*. A Latin version of the French *Farce de maître Pathelin* attributed to Reuchlin in bibliographical records. Because *Henno* independently reworks the same dramatic material and the surviving print gives uncertain attributional evidence, this translation should be retained with an explicit authorship caveat. ([USTC 200016](https://www.ustc.ac.uk/editions/200016); [BnF results](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/affiner.do?afficheRegroup=false&critereRecherche=&index=AUT3&listeAffinages=FacNatDocIMP_aMON%3BFacPays_fr&motRecherche=&nbResultParPage=50&numNotice=12359330&triResultParPage=3&trouveDansFiltre=NoticePUB&typeNotice=))

- **1513:** *Constantinus Magnus Romanorum imperator*. A humanist compilation, translation, or edition concerning Constantine the Great, assembled from Greek historical material; it resists the simple category “translation” because Reuchlin also shaped the source material into a new Latin presentation. ([BLB Karlsruhe](https://digital.blb-karlsruhe.de/name/view/5504946); [Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz74609.html))

- **1515:** *Athanasius, In librum Psalmorum*. A Latin rendering or edition of Athanasius’s commentary on the Psalms, made as a patristic and philological instrument rather than claimed as Reuchlin’s own theology. ([Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz74609.html); [survey of editions](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Reuchlin))

- **1519:** *Athanasius, De variis quaestionibus*. A Latin translation or edition of a question-and-answer text transmitted under Athanasius’s name; the ancient attribution is not secure, so “Athanasius” identifies the received textual tradition rather than certain authorship. ([Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz74609.html); [survey of editions](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Reuchlin))

- **1522:** *Graeciae excellentium oratorum Aeschinis et Demosthenis orationes adversariae*. An edition of the opposing speeches of Aeschines and Demosthenes on the crown, prepared for Greek instruction. The ancient speeches are not Reuchlin’s creation; the made thing is his paired pedagogical edition. ([PRDL](https://www.prdl.org/author_view.php?a_id=865); [Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz74609.html))

### Writings that intervene in the Jewish-books controversy

- **1505:** *Tütsch Missive, warumb die Juden so lang im ellend sind*. A German theological letter or memorandum on Jewish exile, originally not intended for publication. It is an intervention whose manuscript-letter status resists treating it as an ordinary book. ([*Sämtliche Werke*, IV.1](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1706/170600411?lang=en-gb); [ADB](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/ADB%3AReuchlin%2C_Johannes))

- **1510:** *Ratschlag, ob man den Juden alle ihre Bücher nehmen, abtun und verbrennen soll*. Reuchlin’s commissioned legal-scholarly opinion opposing indiscriminate confiscation and destruction of Jewish writings. It is an official act and expert report, later incorporated into the *Augenspiegel*, rather than originally an autonomous commercial publication. ([bpb](https://www.bpb.de/themen/zeit-kulturgeschichte/geteilte-geschichte/339639/augenspiegel-von-johannes-reuchlin/); [account of the *Augenspiegel*’s contents](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augenspiegel_%28Reuchlin%29))

- **1511:** *Augenspiegel*. A public defence of the 1510 opinion against Johannes Pfefferkorn’s *Handspiegel*, combining documentary republication, legal argument, philology, and personal vindication. It acts as a manifesto for discriminating among kinds of Jewish texts rather than condemning them collectively. ([*Sämtliche Werke*, IV.1](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1706/170600411?lang=en-gb); [bpb](https://www.bpb.de/themen/zeit-kulturgeschichte/geteilte-geschichte/339639/augenspiegel-von-johannes-reuchlin/))

- **1512:** *Ain clare Verstentnus in Tütsch von den Judenbüchern*. A concise German clarification of Reuchlin’s position on Jewish books, designed for intervention in the widening public controversy. It is best understood as a polemical clarification rather than a new systematic treatise. ([*Sämtliche Werke*, IV.1](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1706/170600411?lang=en-gb); [BLB Karlsruhe](https://digital.blb-karlsruhe.de/name/view/5504946))

- **1513:** *Defensio contra calumniatores suos Colonienses*. A large Latin forensic defence addressed to Emperor Maximilian against the Cologne theologians’ accusations. Its form combines legal brief, polemic, documentary record, and self-defence. ([*Sämtliche Werke*, IV.1](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1706/170600411?lang=en-gb); [BLB Karlsruhe](https://digital.blb-karlsruhe.de/name/view/5504946))

- **1518:** *Acta iudiciorum inter F. Jacobum Hochstraten et Johannem Reuchlin*. A printed dossier of the judicial proceedings between Hoogstraten and Reuchlin, drawn from an authenticated court register. It is not wholly an authored “work”: Reuchlin’s making lies in selecting, arranging, and publishing institutional documents produced by many hands. ([BLB Karlsruhe](https://digital.blb-karlsruhe.de/name/view/5504946); [Bibliotheca Augustana work-list](https://www.tha.de/~harsch/Chronologia/Lspost15/Reuchlin/reu_intr.html))

### Editorial and epistolary constructions

- **1514:** *Clarorum virorum epistolae Latinae, Graecae et Hebraicae ad Ioannem Reuchlin*. A curated publication of letters sent to Reuchlin. Most component texts were made by his correspondents, not by him; what belongs to Reuchlin’s œuvre is the editorial self-presentation created through their selection and arrangement. ([BLB Karlsruhe](https://digital.blb-karlsruhe.de/name/view/5504946); [Analytic Bibliography of Online Neo-Latin Texts](https://philological.cal.bham.ac.uk/bibliography/reuch.html))

- **1518:** *Epistolae trium illustrium virorum ad Hermannum comitem Nuenarium*. A small polemical packet involving Reuchlin, Hermann von dem Busche, and Ulrich von Hutten. It is a collaborative manifestation rather than a securely single-authored Reuchlin work. ([PRDL](https://www.prdl.org/author_view.php?a_id=865); [survey of editions](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Reuchlin))

- **1519:** *Illustrium virorum epistolae*. An expanded, two-book version of the 1514 collection of letters addressed to Reuchlin. Its new selection and ordering constitute a second editorial construction, although the incoming letters themselves remain the work of their senders. ([BLB Karlsruhe](https://digital.blb-karlsruhe.de/name/view/5504946); [PRDL](https://www.prdl.org/author_view.php?a_id=865))

- **1477–1522:** *Briefwechsel*. Reuchlin’s surviving correspondence, now critically edited in four chronological volumes. This is not one work he designed: it is a posthumously reconstructed corpus containing both his letters and letters received, so individual authorship belongs at the letter level. ([Heidelberg Academy project](https://www.hadw-bw.de/forschung/weitere-forschungsprojekte/edition-des-reuchlin-briefwechsels); [Frommann-Holzboog, volume IV](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1216/121600410?lang=de))

### Administrative and juridical writing

- **1480s–1522:** *Amtsschriften, Gutachten, Widmungen und sonstige Varia*. Reuchlin produced legal opinions, diplomatic and administrative writings, dedicatory texts, prefaces, and other occasional documents through his work as jurist and councillor. They resist classification as a single work, and some were institutional acts rather than literature; the critical edition therefore reserves a *Varia* volume for them instead of inventing a unified title or date. One attested legal opinion from 1488 is now lost. ([*Sämtliche Werke* plan, X](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1706?lang=de); [legal-history discussion](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9783161624018_A47125106/preview-9783161624018_A47125106.pdf))
