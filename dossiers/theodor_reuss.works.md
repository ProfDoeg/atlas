## Works by Theodor Reuss

### Political and public-information pamphlets

- **1885:** *Herr Theodor Reuss: London Season 1885*. A printed promotional leaflet documenting Reuss’s career as a singer and his connection with Angelo Neumann’s Wagner tour; the only known copy is reported at Amsterdam’s International Institute of Social History. It is self-presentation rather than an authored literary work. ([Howe and Möller, notes](https://freemasonry.bcy.ca/aqc/reuss/notes.html))
- **1887:** *The Matrimonial Question: From an Anarchistic Point of View*. Reuss’s first known pamphlet, published in London by the anarchist Henry Seymour; a political intervention on marriage and motherhood rather than an occult work. The surviving specialist literature treats the pamphlet as Reuss’s, although contemporary reception and its relationship to his later sexual-religious program remain imperfectly documented. ([König](https://theohistory.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/theohistory-vol_iv_no_6_7-1993_04_07.pdf); [Howe and Möller, notes](https://freemasonry.bcy.ca/aqc/reuss/notes.html))
- **1901:** *Was muss man von der Freimaurerei wissen? Eine allgemeinverständliche Darstellung des Ordens der Freimaurer, der Illuminaten und Rosenkreuzer*. Published as “Peregrinus,” this is an explanatory and promotional handbook about Freemasonry and related orders. Its function lies between popular reference work and organizational propaganda. ([O.T.O. bibliography](https://oto-usa.org/usgl/lion-eagle/theodor-reuss/))
- **1903:** *Was ist Okkultismus und wie erlangt man occulte Kräfte?* A popular occult manual: partly explanatory text, partly protocol promising practical cultivation of occult powers. ([O.T.O. bibliography](https://oto-usa.org/usgl/lion-eagle/theodor-reuss/))
- **1903:** *Was muss man von Richard Wagner und seinen Ton-Dramen wissen?* Published under the pseudonym “Hans von Schelling,” this is a popular guide to Wagner and his music dramas, not a musical composition by Reuss. ([Howe and Möller, notes](https://freemasonry.bcy.ca/aqc/reuss/notes.html))
- **1914:** *Parsifal und das enthüllte Grals-Geheimnis*. Issued under the name “Ur-Uter,” this is an esoteric reinterpretation of Wagner’s drama and the Grail as a sexual-religious mystery. Some references give the title as *Parsifal, das enthüllte Gralsgeheimnis*; a later 1920 issue is an edition, not a separate composition. ([Urban](https://www.in.yoga/ru/yoga-science/35-urban-yoga-of-sex/); [O.T.O. bibliography](https://oto-usa.org/usgl/lion-eagle/theodor-reuss/))

### Illuminist and Masonic histories

- **1894:** *Die Mysterien der Illuminaten*. A short expositional or initiatory text associated with Reuss’s attempt to revive Illuminism. Its precise publication history and division of authorship between Reuss and his associate Leopold Engel are insufficiently settled in the accessible specialist sources. ([O.T.O. bibliography](https://oto-usa.org/usgl/lion-eagle/theodor-reuss/))
- **1896:** *Geschichte des Illuminaten-Ordens*. An anonymous twenty-eight-page pamphlet, not the much larger book of the same title published by Leopold Engel in 1906. Reuss’s authorship is traditional but disputed: recent historical discussion says only that several clues point to him. ([Introvigne](https://bitterwinter.org/the-illuminati-6-the-second-illuminati/))
- **1914:** *An die freimaurerische Presse Deutschlands!* A polemical open address printed in the twelfth year of *Oriflamme*. It is best classified as an organizational manifesto: a text intended to intervene in Masonic public life rather than merely describe it. ([German bibliographical notice](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodor_Reu%C3%9F))

### Translations, adaptations, and appropriated texts

- **1906:** *Lingam-Yoni; oder die Mysterien des Geschlechts-Kultus*. A German adaptation of the anonymous English *Phallism* traditionally associated with Hargrave Jennings, furnished with Reuss’s own introduction. Because much of it is translated or appropriated rather than composed by Reuss, it resists treatment as an entirely original obra. ([Urban](https://www.in.yoga/ru/yoga-science/35-urban-yoga-of-sex/); [Oxford bibliography](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/363730797_Modernity_and_Neo-Tantra))
- **circa 1913:** *Die Arte Magica Ararita (Die Magie des Hochaltars)*. A German translation or adaptation of an Aleister Crowley sexual-magical paper, circulated as an *Oriflamme* pamphlet. It is Reuss’s linguistic and editorial making, but the underlying work is Crowley’s; the date is approximate. ([bibliographical discussion](https://libraryofagartha.com/Politics/National%20Socialism/Post%20WW2/pseudo-scholars%20on%20National%20Socialism/Levenda%2C%20Peter%20-%20Unholy%20Alliance_%20A%20History%20of%20Nazi%20Involvement%20with%20the%20Occult%20%282019%2C%20Ibis%20Press_Nicolas%20Hays%2C%20Inc%29%20-%20libgen.li.pdf))
- **circa 1913:** *Von den geheimen Hochzeiten der Götter mit den Menschen (De Nuptis Secretis Deorum cum Hominibus)*. Reuss’s German translation of a Crowley paper, issued as an *Oriflamme* pamphlet at Schmiedeberg. The standard Crowley bibliography dates it only approximately, so no exact year should be inferred. ([Yorke–Harper bibliography](https://www.100thmonkeypress.com/biblio/acrowley/foreign/nuptis/nuptis.htm))
- **circa 1913:** *De Homunculus (Von der Bereitung des Homunkulus)*. Another German translation or adaptation of a Crowley sexual-magical text. Like its companion pamphlets, it is simultaneously a Reuss-made translation and a manifestation of somebody else’s underlying work; its exact issue date is uncertain. ([bibliographical discussion](https://libraryofagartha.com/Politics/National%20Socialism/PostWW2/pseudo-scholars%20on%20National%20Socialism/Levenda%2C%20Peter%20-%20Unholy%20Alliance_%20A%20History%20of%20Nazi%20Involvement%20with%20the%20Occult%20%282019%2C%20Ibis%20Press_Nicolas%20Hays%2C%20Inc%29%20-%20libgen.li.pdf))
- **1914:** *Auszug aus dem englischen Manifesto der M∴M∴M∴ in deutscher Uebersetzung: Manifesto der M∴M∴M∴*. Reuss’s German excerpt and translation of the British O.T.O. manifesto associated with Crowley, published in *Oriflamme*. It is a translated manifesto—an operative declaration—not an independently authored treatise. ([Aries](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700593-tat00022))
- **1918; issued without a conventional date, commonly catalogued 1920:** *Ecclesiae Gnosticae Catholicae Canon Missae: Die Gnostische Messe*. Reuss’s German translation, under “Merlin Peregrinus,” of Aleister Crowley’s *Liber XV*. The title page’s esoteric dating and the distinction between translation in 1918 and later *Oriflamme* publication account for the competing 1918 and 1920 dates; it is a liturgical protocol and performed rite, but not Reuss’s original mass. ([Aries](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700593-tat00022); [O.T.O. bibliography](https://oto-usa.org/usgl/lion-eagle/theodor-reuss/))

### Constitutions, statutes, charters, and manifestos

- **1900–1901:** *First Foundation Document of the revived Order of Illuminati*. A purported instrument granting Reuss authority to found Masonic lodges, signed by Reuss and Leopold Engel. It survives through a 1914 *Oriflamme* transcript; König notes that it was allegedly written on 6 May 1901 although the document itself bears an esoteric 1900 date, so both authenticity and dating require reservation. ([König](https://theohistory.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/theohistory-vol_iv_no_6_7-1993_04_07.pdf))
- **1903:** *Konstitution, Statuten und Formulare*. A constitutional and administrative compilation for Reuss’s system of Scottish, Memphis, and Misraim Masonry. It is a collective institutional instrument rather than securely an individually authored book, and Reuss’s precise share cannot be separated from the documents and warrants it incorporates. ([König](https://theohistory.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/theohistory-vol_iv_no_6_7-1993_04_07.pdf))
- **1906:** *Allgemeine Satzungen des Ordens der Orientalischen Templer O.T.O.* A German-and-English body of general statutes for the reorganized O.T.O., accompanied by its lamen. This is a protocol that constitutes membership and governance; it acts upon an organization instead of merely describing one. ([König](https://theohistory.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/theohistory-vol_iv_no_6_7-1993_04_07.pdf))
- **10 September 1906, effective 24 June 1907:** *Edikt* separating the Scottish, Memphis, and Misraim rites. Reuss’s administrative proclamation declared the three rites independent and placed their governing bodies under his jurisdiction. It is an act-document whose “work” consists in the authority it claims to exercise. ([König](https://theohistory.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/theohistory-vol_iv_no_6_7-1993_04_07.pdf))
- **1912:** *Mysteria Mystica Maxima*. A concise doctrinal statement published in the jubilee issue of *Oriflamme*, presenting the O.T.O.’s inner sexual-magical mystery. It is simultaneously manifesto, guarded disclosure, and advertisement for initiation. ([Oxford bibliography](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/363730797_Modernity_and_Neo-Tantra))
- **22 January 1917:** *Verità Mystica: Manifesto of the Anational Grand Lodge and Mystic Temple*. A manifesto announcing Reuss’s “anational” O.T.O.–Hermetic Brotherhood of Light project at Monte Verità. The object is not simply informational: it attempts to bring a new transnational body into existence. ([O.T.O. historical account](https://oto-usa.org/usgl/lion-eagle/theodor-reuss/))
- **22 January 1917:** *Constitution of the Ancient Order of Oriental Templars, O.T.O., Ordo Templi Orientis, with an Introduction and a Synopsis of the Degrees of the O.T.O.* An English constitutional protocol for Reuss’s reorganized order, substantially dependent upon Crowley’s 1913 M∴M∴M∴ constitution and accompanied by degree synopses and an abridged Crowley text. Its mixed derivation prevents uncomplicated attribution to Reuss alone. ([O.T.O. historical account](https://oto-usa.org/usgl/lion-eagle/theodor-reuss/))
- **1920:** *Das Aufbau-Programm und die Leitsätze der Gnostischen Neo-Christen O.T.O.* A program and set of guiding principles for Reuss’s neo-Gnostic Christians. It is both manifesto and institutional protocol: a projected social and religious order expressed as rules, whether or not that order was ever fully realized. ([König](https://theohistory.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/theohistory-vol_iv_no_6_7-1993_04_07.pdf); [O.T.O. bibliography](https://oto-usa.org/usgl/lion-eagle/theodor-reuss/))
- **10 May 1921:** *X° charters for Charles Stansfeld Jones and Heinrich Tränker*. Separate appointment instruments naming national O.T.O. grand masters for the United States and Germany. These are made legal-symbolic objects rather than literary works; they belong to Reuss’s production only in the performative sense that issuing them purported to create authority. ([O.T.O. historical account](https://oto-usa.org/usgl/lion-eagle/theodor-reuss/))
- **30 July 1921:** *Gauge of Amity for H. Spencer Lewis*. A diplomatic instrument connecting Reuss’s O.T.O. jurisdiction with Lewis and A.M.O.R.C. Its authenticity as a physical document is accepted by A.M.O.R.C., but the organization disputes claims that it established more than an honorary relationship. ([Introvigne](https://www.cesnur.org/testi/oto.htm))
- **October 1921:** *T.A.W.U.C.–A.M.O.R.C. Weltkonzil manuscript*. A handwritten plan for an “A.M.O.R.C. World Union Council,” including a manifesto, an account of the order’s origin, an introduction, and proposed extracts from Reuss’s earlier writings. It is an unfinished editorial and organizational project rather than a completed publication. ([document description](https://www.themasonictrowel.com/ebooks/freemasonry/eb0048.pdf))

### Rituals and instructional papers of uncertain date

- **Date not securely established:** *Das wahre Geheimnis der Freimaurerei und das Mysterium der hl. Messe*. An esoteric essay identifying Masonic mystery with the ritual of the Mass. Its appearance in König’s collected Reuss corpus establishes the text, but the accessible scholarly citation does not supply a secure original date. ([Aries](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700593-tat00022))
- **Date not securely established:** *Die Gnostischen Templer, oder Neo-Christen*. A short programmatic account of Reuss’s Gnostic-Templar religious identity. It survives in the collected corpus and should be treated as a manifesto-like article, not silently dated from its 1997 reprinting. ([Urban bibliography](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/363730797_Modernity_and_Neo-Tantra))
- **Date not securely established:** *Von den Geheimnissen der okkulten Hochgrade unseres Ordens*. An instructional or revelatory paper on the order’s occult higher degrees. It is less an independent public book than an internal teaching text whose boundaries depended on its periodical or initiatory setting. ([Urban](https://www.in.yoga/ru/yoga-science/35-urban-yoga-of-sex/))
- **Date not securely established:** *Die Eucharistie, das Geheimnis des Abendmahls*. A sexual-mystical interpretation of the Eucharist listed among Reuss’s papers. The accessible catalogue does not establish whether it was completed as an autonomous publication, an article, or material for a projected compilation; that resistance to work-status must remain explicit. ([German works inventory](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodor_Reu%C3%9F))
- **Date not securely established:** *Das Erotische in Goethes Faust und die Tantriks*. A comparative sexual-esoteric essay listed in the specialist-derived works inventory. Its publication state and original date remain unclear, so it cannot safely be promoted to a dated book. ([German works inventory](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodor_Reu%C3%9F))
- **Date not securely established:** *Das Kreuz und die Sexual-Religion*. A doctrinal paper joining Christian symbolism to Reuss’s sexual religion. No secure original date or autonomous edition is established by the consulted corpus descriptions. ([German works inventory](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodor_Reu%C3%9F))
- **Date not securely established:** *Das Sexuelle in der Theosophie und Anthroposophie, mit den Gelöbnissen der verschiedenen Führer, im Original*. A polemical documentary compilation rather than a conventional authored treatise. Its title promises reproduced vows and commentary, making it partly an assemblage of other people’s texts; completion and publication status are uncertain. ([German works inventory](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodor_Reu%C3%9F))
- **Date not securely established:** *Übersicht über verschiedene freimaurerische Systeme und Einführung in die sämtlichen Grade*. A survey and curricular introduction to Masonic systems and degrees. It is best understood as a teaching protocol or syllabus; the available inventory does not establish a separate published edition. ([German works inventory](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodor_Reu%C3%9F))
- **Date not securely established:** *Die neuen Illuminaten und ihre Einrichtungen und Händel*. A historical-polemical paper about the revived Illuminati and its disputes. Its uncertain publication status and the entangled roles of Reuss and Engel prevent confident treatment as a finished, solely authored obra. ([German works inventory](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodor_Reu%C3%9F))

### Periodical and editorial Gesamtkunstwerk

- **1902–1914:** *Oriflamme*. Reuss’s periodical began as an organ of the Memphis–Misraim rites and became the official voice of the O.T.O.; it combined journalism, polemic, statutes, charters, translations, ritual disclosures, advertisements, and visual insignia. Authorship belongs to Reuss only at the level of editorship and institutional construction—individual unsigned items cannot all automatically be assigned to him—yet the serial as a whole approaches a Gesamtkunstwerk of print, ritual, organization, and self-mythology. ([König](https://theohistory.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/theohistory-vol_iv_no_6_7-1993_04_07.pdf); [O.T.O. bibliography](https://oto-usa.org/usgl/lion-eagle/theodor-reuss/))

### Organizations and enacted projects

- **1895–1902:** *Revived Order of Illuminati*. Reuss and Leopold Engel attempted to reactivate the Illuminati, producing organizations, offices, statutes, and competing histories rather than one bounded work. Engel later claimed responsibility for the curriculum and organization and minimized Reuss’s contribution, so this is a disputed collaborative project, not securely Reuss’s sole creation. ([Introvigne](https://bitterwinter.org/the-illuminati-6-the-second-illuminati/); [König](https://theohistory.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/TH-V-6-Apr1995a.pdf))
- **from 1902:** *German system of Scottish, Memphis, and Misraim Masonry*. Reuss assembled warrants, lodges, governing bodies, periodical publicity, and degrees into a working institutional structure. Because many warrants came from John Yarker and other authorities, this is better called an organizational assemblage than an original order created ex nihilo. ([König](https://theohistory.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/theohistory-vol_iv_no_6_7-1993_04_07.pdf))
- **1902–1923:** *Ordo Templi Orientis under Reuss*. Reuss transformed Carl Kellner’s projected “Masonic academy” and Hermetic inner circle into a public order with statutes, degrees, charters, publications, and a sexual-magical doctrine. “Founder” is therefore an unstable description: Kellner supplied the originating conception, while Reuss made much of the organization that actually appeared. ([König](https://theohistory.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/theohistory-vol_iv_no_6_7-1993_04_07.pdf))
- **15–25 August 1917:** *Anational Congress for Organising the Reconstruction of Society on Practical Cooperative Lines*. Convened by Reuss at Monte Verità, this was a temporary social artwork comprising congress, political project, poetry readings, ritual, and communal aspiration. It resists designation as a stable “work”: what Reuss made was an event and proposed social order, not a reproducible text. ([O.T.O. historical account](https://oto-usa.org/usgl/lion-eagle/theodor-reuss/))
- **1917–1920:** *Gnostic Neo-Christian church project*. Through the *Verità Mystica* manifesto, the translated Gnostic Mass, and the 1920 construction program, Reuss attempted to turn Crowley’s liturgy into the religious center of an international Masonic federation. This is a project in the strict sense—an intention embodied in texts and rites but never fully realized as the universal church Reuss proposed. ([O.T.O. historical account](https://oto-usa.org/usgl/lion-eagle/theodor-reuss/); [Aries](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700593-tat00022))

### Performance that resists inclusion as an authored work

- **1882:** *Participation in the Bayreuth première of Wagner’s Parsifal*. Reuss later claimed to have sung in the première, but the claim is not securely corroborated in the sources consulted. Even if true, this was a performance—a manifestation made partly through his body and voice—not a composition or text made by him, and therefore belongs at the edge of a works list rather than among his authored obra. ([biographical account](https://oto-usa.org/usgl/lion-eagle/theodor-reuss/); [Howe and Möller, notes](https://freemasonry.bcy.ca/aqc/reuss/notes.html))
