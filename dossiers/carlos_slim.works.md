## Works by Carlos Slim Helú

### Authored texts

- **1994; revised 2012 and 2024:** *Carta a los jóvenes… y a los no jóvenes*. An open letter of counsel and reflection—part manifesto, part ethical testament—first written for university students, later rewritten and condensed; the three states should be treated as versions, not separate works. ([Universidad Anáhuac](https://www.anahuac.mx/mexico/files/dhc/Carlos_Slim_2024.pdf))

- **2005:** *Acuerdo Nacional para la Unidad, el Estado de Derecho, el Desarrollo, la Inversión y el Empleo* (“Acuerdo de Chapultepec”). A collectively authored civic-economic manifesto promoted by Slim and ultimately signed by more than 1.2 million people; it is an act-producing text, not solely his individual composition. ([official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia_ing.html))

- **2009:** *Plan 2009 de Grupo Carso*. A corporate investment and employment plan issued by Grupo Carso under Slim’s leadership. It resists classification as a personal work: it is an institutional protocol and statement of intended action rather than an individually authored text. ([plan PDF](https://carlosslim.com/slimcomunicado.pdf))

- **2010:** *Plan 2010 de Grupo Carso*. A successor corporate protocol announcing investment, employment, research, and human-capital objectives; surviving documentation attributes its issuance to the group, not sole literary authorship to Slim. ([official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia.html))

- **2011:** *Retos y liderazgo*. A delivered conference rather than a fixed literary work, addressing historical change, education, family, and leadership; published transcripts preserve a manifestation of the event, not necessarily a definitive authorial text. ([official transcript](https://carlosslim.com/desde_slim_miraflores.html))

- **Undated and incompletely catalogued:** *Articles on baseball*. Slim’s official biography states that he wrote “various articles” about baseball, but supplies neither titles nor dates; no authoritative collected edition or complete bibliography was located, so the individual articles cannot responsibly be named. ([official biography](https://carlosslim.com/biografia.html))

### Companies and entrepreneurial systems

- **1965:** *Inversora Bursátil*. A securities-investment company incorporated by Slim, who became chairman; this is a legal and financial construction rather than a text. ([official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia_ing.html))

- **1965–1966:** *Inmobiliaria Carso*. A real-estate company created as part of the foundation of the later Grupo Carso; “Carso” combines Carlos and Soumaya. ([official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia_ing.html))

- **1967:** *Promotora del Hogar, S.A.* A residential-development company founded and directed by Slim. Its obra is organizational and architectural in aggregate, not a single building. ([official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia_ing.html))

- **1967:** *GM Maquinaria*. A construction-equipment sales and rental company founded and headed by Slim. ([official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia_ing.html))

- **1969:** *Bienes Raíces Mexicanos, S.A.* A real-estate company begun within Slim’s emerging business group; the chronology records its commencement but does not document sole authorship. ([official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia_ing.html))

- **1969:** *Nacional de Arrendamientos*. A leasing company begun within Slim’s group; it is a corporate instrument and continuing activity rather than a bounded personal work. ([official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia_ing.html))

- **1969:** *Invest Mentor Mexicana*. An investment company begun within Slim’s group. ([official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia_ing.html))

- **1972:** *Pedregales del Sur, S.A.* A real-estate enterprise whose operations began in 1972; the available chronology does not distinguish Slim’s individual contribution from that of the organization. ([official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia_ing.html))

- **1980–:** *Grupo Galas / Grupo Carso*. A conglomerate assembled across industry, construction, mining, retail, food, and tobacco; it is best understood as Slim’s evolving entrepreneurial Gesamtkunstwerk, though its constituent companies and products are collective works. ([official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia_ing.html))

- **1984:** *Grupo Financiero Inbursa*. A financial group created by integrating Seguros de México, Fianzas La Guardiana, and Casa de Bolsa Inbursa. Its making consisted principally in corporate combination rather than invention of the component firms. ([official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia_ing.html))

- **1990:** *Carso Global Telecom*. A holding company created following the consortium acquisition of Telmex; because Southwestern Bell and France Télécom participated in the acquisition, the resulting system cannot be treated as Slim’s unaided creation. ([official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia_ing.html))

- **1990:** *Telcel*. A telecommunications brand and operating identity created from Radiomóvil Dipsa. It is a commercial manifestation—subsequently a vast service system—not a newly founded underlying company. ([official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia_ing.html))

- **1996:** *Prepaid cellular service system at América Móvil*. Slim’s official biography describes him as a worldwide pioneer in introducing prepaid mobile service; the claim concerns a commercial model implemented by an organization, not a uniquely attributable technical invention. ([official biography](https://carlosslim.com/biografia.html))

- **1999:** *Carso Infraestructura y Construcción, S.A. (CICSA)*. A construction and engineering company created as Grupo Carso’s infrastructure division; authorship is institutional even though the official chronology places it within Slim’s entrepreneurial activity. ([official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia_ing.html))

- **2000:** *América Telecom*. A holding company formed for América Móvil’s expansion across Latin America. Its obra is an acquisition-and-integration structure rather than a freestanding manufactured object. ([official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia_ing.html))

- **2000:** *T1msn / ProdigyMSN*. A Spanish-language internet portal launched jointly by Telmex under Slim and Microsoft under Bill Gates; it is expressly a collaborative digital creation. ([official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia_ing.html))

- **2004:** *Telmex Latinoamérica*. A regional telecommunications company created following the purchase of AT&T Latin America; because it arose from purchased operations subsequently integrated with other firms, “creation” here means corporate reconfiguration. ([official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia_ing.html))

- **2005:** *Impulsora del Desarrollo y el Empleo en América Latina (IDEAL)*. An infrastructure and development company formed to invest in human and physical capital. Despite early descriptions emphasizing sustainable or non-profit-oriented investment, it is a public commercial corporation, making “social project” an intention rather than a complete description of the obra. ([official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia_ing.html))

### Foundations, institutions, and social projects

- **1986–:** *Fundación Carlos Slim Helú / Fundación Carlos Slim*. A philanthropic institution created to develop human capabilities through programs in education, employment, health, culture, justice, environmental protection, and related fields. It is an evolving body of work whose individual programs are collective institutional creations. ([Foundation history](https://fundacioncarlosslim.org/quienes-somos/))

- **1994–:** *Museo Soumaya*. A free-admission museum founded around the Slim collection in memory of Soumaya Domit; its obra comprises an institution, collection, research and conservation program, publications, exhibitions, and several venues—not merely one building. ([official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia_ing.html); [Foundation cultural program](https://fundacioncarlosslim.org/english/cultura/))

- **1995–:** *Fundación Telmex / Fundación Telmex-Telcel*. A philanthropic institution established for education, health, nutrition, justice, culture, sport, environmental conservation, and disaster relief. It is attributable to Slim’s institutional project but not to him as sole maker of every program it administers. ([official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia_ing.html))

- **2000–:** *Fundación del Centro Histórico de la Ciudad de México*. A foundation created to restore and revitalize Mexico City’s historic center. Slim is officially identified as its creator, but the continuing urban intervention is collaborative and resists treatment as a finished personal work. ([Grupo Carso](https://www.carso.com.mx/inversionistas/gobierno-corporativo/); [official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia_ing.html))

- **2007:** *Instituto Carlos Slim de la Salud*. A health institution created within the enlarged Fundación Carlos Slim program. Its research, public-health models, and digital systems form an open-ended collective project rather than a single work. ([official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia_ing.html))

- **2007:** *Instituto Telmex del Deporte*. A sports-development institution jointly created by Fundación Telmex and Fundación Carlos Slim to support athletes and equip public schools. ([official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia_ing.html))

- **2008–2009:** *Ciudad Jardín Bicentenario*. An ecological, commercial, educational, and sports redevelopment constructed on the former Bordo de Xochiaca dump and inaugurated in 2009. It is a multi-institutional urban project backed through Slim’s foundation, not a personally designed architectural work. ([Fundación Carlos Slim](https://fundacioncarlosslim.org/conoce-el-proyecto-ciudad-jardin-bicentenario/))

- **2009:** *Fundación Inbursa*. A social-development foundation announced by Slim with an initial endowment and collaboration from La Caixa; the institution extends rather than duplicates the earlier foundation’s work. ([official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia.html))

- **2009:** *Grameen-Carso*. A microfinance program created collaboratively by Fundación Carlos Slim and Grameen Trust. Its authorship is shared, and its “work” is a lending mechanism intended to generate continuing economic activity. ([official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia.html))

- **2010–:** *Iniciativa Slim en Medicina Genómica (SIGMA)*. A genomic-medicine research initiative launched collaboratively by Fundación Carlos Slim and the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard; its products include research capacity, data, tools, and scientific publications, none of which should be assigned to Slim personally. ([Foundation report](https://fundacioncarlosslim.org/wp-content/uploads/REPORTE2018-90.pdf))

- **2010–2015:** *Iniciativa Salud Mesoamérica 2015*. A regional public-health project created with the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation, Spain, and the Inter-American Development Bank to address health inequities. The date in its title is a program horizon, not the date on which the work ceased to matter. ([official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia.html))

- **2010–2011:** *Plaza Mariana*. A 67,255-square-metre religious, civic, museum, columbarium, and market complex financed and constructed through Fundación Carlos Slim and donated to the Archdiocese of Mexico City. It is a commissioned collective Gesamtkunstwerk rather than architecture authored by Slim himself. ([Basílica de Guadalupe](https://virgendeguadalupe.org.mx/plaza-mariana/); [Fundación announcement](https://carlosslim.com/responsabilidad_slim_mariana.html))

- **2011:** *Museo Soumaya, Plaza Carso*. A new museum building commissioned for the institution and collection founded by Slim. The building must not be attributed architecturally to him: Fernando Romero designed it with Ove Arup and Frank Gehry as advisers, while Slim and his foundation were patrons and clients. ([Museo Soumaya](https://www.museosoumaya.org/inicio/museo-soumaya/quienes_somos/))

- **2013:** *Plataforma Digital Naturalista*. A citizen-science platform presented jointly by CONABIO, Fundación Carlos Slim, and Telcel to document and protect nature. Its digital form is simultaneously archive, protocol for observation, and continuing public project. ([official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia.html))

- **By 2015:** *Capacítate para el Empleo*. A free, continuously expanded online technical-training platform developed by Fundación Carlos Slim. The surviving institutional account documents its 2015 operation and later expansion but does not provide a secure original creation date, so none is supplied here. ([Fundación Carlos Slim](https://fundacioncarlosslim.org/english/capacitate-empleo-en-aldea-digital/))

- **By 2016:** *Aprende.org*. A free digital platform integrating the foundation’s education, employment, culture, and health resources with material from partner institutions. It is a framework for other texts and protocols rather than a single authored text; the institutional pages do not state a precise launch date. ([Fundación Carlos Slim](https://fundacioncarlosslim.org/educacion-2/))

### Collections and patronage that resist personal authorship

- **Acquired principally from the 1980s onward:** *The Carlos Slim art collection / Museo Soumaya collection*. An assembled collection of tens of thousands of works rather than works made by Slim; his obra lies in selection, acquisition, preservation, institutionalization, and public presentation, while authorship of every constituent artwork remains with its artist. ([official chronology](https://carlosslim.com/biografia_ing.html); [Museo Soumaya](https://www.museosoumaya.org/inicio/museo-soumaya/quienes_somos/))

- **1992–:** *Centro de Estudios de Historia de México Carso*. Slim did not create the institution: it began at Condumex in 1965 and entered Grupo Carso through the 1992 acquisition of Condumex. His contribution is therefore stewardship, expansion, acquisition, and dissemination of its archive—not founding authorship. ([institutional history](https://fundacioncarlosslim.org/centro-de-estudios-de-historia-de-mexico-como-surgio-la-idea-de-crear-esta-institucion-cultural/))

- **2007–2018:** *Colección Biodiversidad Mexicana*. A twelve-volume environmental publishing project promoted by Fundación Carlos Slim. It belongs to Slim’s institutional patronage, but the foundation does not identify him as author of the volumes; calling the books his writings would erase their scientific and editorial collaborators. ([Fundación Carlos Slim](https://fundacioncarlosslim.org/conoce-la-iniciativa-coleccion-biodiversidad-mexicana/))

- **Ongoing:** *Revitalization of Mexico City’s Historic Center*. A program of building restoration, economic renewal, cultural activity, tourism management, and public-space intervention. It resists being called one finished “work”: it is a continuing, contested urban project involving government, residents, businesses, architects, conservators, and Slim’s foundation. ([Fundación Carlos Slim](https://fundacioncarlosslim.org/english/cultura/); [Grupo Carso](https://www.carso.com.mx/inversionistas/gobierno-corporativo/))
