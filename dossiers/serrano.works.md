## Works by Miguel Serrano

### Narrative, poetic and visionary obras

- **1938:** *Antología del verdadero cuento en Chile*. Anthology and generational manifesto edited by Serrano, with his own prologue and story “Hasta que llegue a la luz”; it is therefore simultaneously an editorial construction and an authored text. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1936-1940/)
- **1941:** *La época más oscura*. Collection of Serrano’s stories, some written as early as 1936; later editions do not constitute separate obras. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1941-1952/)
- **1960:** *Las visitas de la reina de Saba*. Symbolic prose narrative first issued in Spanish and English in the same year; the translated title is a manifestation of the same obra, not another work. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1953-1961/)
- **1960:** *Los misterios*. Brief visionary or initiatory narrative, also issued contemporaneously as *The Mysteries*. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1953-1961/)
- **1964/1969:** *La flor inexistente*. Visionary prose work dated by the bibliography to 16 February 1964 but first published as a book in 1969; *The Ultimate Flower* is its English manifestation. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1962-1970/)
- **1970/1972–1973:** *Elella, libro del amor mágico*. Hybrid myth, poem in prose and initiatory treatise completed at Bayonne on 24 August 1970; it first appeared in English as *El/ella, Book of Magic Love* in 1972 and in Spanish in 1973. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1971-1980-2/)
- **2003:** *El hijo del viudo*. Late initiatory narrative, also issued that year in the author’s English version as *The Son of the Widower*. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/2001-2/)
- **2004/2005:** *Maya, la realidad es una ilusión*. Visionary-philosophical text dated December 2004 and published in 2005; its 2006 English manifestation is *Maya, Reality Is an Illusion*. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/2001-2/)

### Journeys, encounters and mythic geography

- **1939:** *Un discurso de América del Sur*. Published address rather than a conventional book: an early manifesto of continental and generational identity. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1936-1940/)
- **1948:** *La Antártica y otros mitos*. Published conference arising from Serrano’s Antarctic expedition; it occupies the border between travel record, territorial claim and myth-making. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1941-1952/)
- **1950:** *Ni por mar ni por tierra… (historia de una generación)*. Generational memoir and journey narrative, later retitled *Ni por mar ni por tierra… Historia de la búsqueda en una generación*. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1941-1952/)
- **1957:** *Quién llama en los hielos*. Antarctic travel narrative transformed by legend and visionary experience; it resists classification as straightforward expedition history. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1953-1961/)
- **1961/1963:** *La serpiente del paraíso*. Account of an Indian pilgrimage completed on 20 July 1961 and published in 1963; memoir, travel narrative and spiritual quest coexist in it. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1962-1970/)
- **1974:** *Trilogía de la búsqueda en el mundo exterior*. Composite obra joining *Ni por mar ni por tierra*, *Quién llama en los hielos* and *La serpiente del paraíso* under a new introduction; it is an author-shaped cycle but not three newly made texts. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1971-1980-2/)

### Encounters, philosophical portraits and initiatory treatises

- **1963/1965:** *El círculo hermético. De Hermann Hesse a C. G. Jung*. Memoir of two friendships completed at Belgrade on 27 November 1963 and published in 1965; its many translated titles are manifestations of the same obra. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1962-1970/)
- **1973–1974:** *Nietzsche y el eterno retorno*. Philosophical essay first manifested as four newspaper installments in 1973, incorporated into the 1974 composite *El círculo hermético, el eterno retorno, elella*, and later issued independently. Its boundaries as a separate book are therefore historically unstable. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1971-1980-2/)
- **1974:** *El círculo hermético, el eterno retorno, elella*. Author-arranged composite volume with a new prefatory text, “El sentido”; it is a Gesamtkunstwerk-like conjunction of memoir, philosophy and magical-love narrative, although its three principal components pre-existed it. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1971-1980-2/)
- **1977/1980:** *Nietzsche y la danza de Siva*. Comparative philosophical-esoteric essay completed on 25 December 1977 and first published as a book in 1980. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1971-1980-2/)
- **1979/1980:** *Nos. Libro de la resurrección*. Initiatory and mythopoetic treatise completed at Montagnola on 21 February 1979 and published in 1980; the English *Book of the Resurrection* is a translated manifestation. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1971-1980-2/)
- **2004/2005:** *Hipocresía, la tortura en Chile*. Polemical essay dated 2004 and published in January 2005; its occasion is political, but its form is an independently issued pamphlet. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/2001-2/)

### Esoteric-Hitlerist cycle and ideological manifestos

- **1977/1978:** *El cordón dorado, hitlerismo esotérico*. Esoteric-Hitlerist treatise completed on 17 September 1977 and published in 1978; it inaugurates the sequence commonly treated as Serrano’s Hitler trilogy. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1971-1980-2/)
- **1981/1982:** *El ciclo racial chileno*. Racial-nationalist treatise incorporating the 1981 conference “Raza chilena” and published in 1982; it is propaganda presented as metaphysical and historical argument. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1981-1989/)
- **1984:** *Adolf Hitler, el último avatâra*. Esoteric-Hitlerist mythography dated 7 February 1984; it is the second major part of the Hitler cycle and functions as ideological scripture rather than critical biography. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1981-1989/)
- **1985/1986:** *Nacionalsocialismo, única solución para los pueblos de América del Sur*. Political manifesto completed on 3 December 1985 and published in 1986. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1981-1989/)
- **1986:** *La resurrección del héroe*. Esoteric-political collection or treatise, including Serrano’s translation of a text attributed in the bibliography to “Adolf Hitler”; that embedded attribution should not be treated as independently verified authorship. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1981-1989/)
- **1986:** *Los protocolos de los sabios de Sion y su aplicación en Chile*. Antisemitic conspiracist tract built upon the notorious forged *Protocols*; Serrano made the Chilean application and introductory apparatus, not the underlying anonymous forgery. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1981-1989/)
- **1987:** *Contra la usura*. Composite propaganda volume credited jointly to Gottfried Feder and Serrano; Serrano’s identifiable contribution is the prologue “Solución nacionalsocialista al problema de la deuda externa,” so the whole book resists attribution to him alone. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1981-1989/)
- **1987:** *El Plan Andinia. Estrategia sionista para apoderarse de la Patagonia argentina y chilena*. Conspiracist pamphlet with an introduction by Serrano; the bibliography lists the book under his production, but the extent of his authorship beyond that introduction is not made explicit. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1981-1989/)
- **1989:** *Informe Leuchter. Fin de una mentira. Cámaras de gas: holocausto judío*. Holocaust-denial publication built around Fred Leuchter’s report; Serrano supplied “Palabras iniciales,” so it is an editorial and propagandistic intervention rather than a report authored by him. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1981-1989/)
- **1990/1991:** *Manú, “por el hombre que vendrá”*. Esoteric-Hitlerist treatise completed on 26 November 1990 and published in 1991; it incorporates the 1989 conference “Weltanschauung” and completes the principal Hitler trilogy. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1990-2000/)
- **1993:** *Los ovnis de Hitler contra el nuevo orden mundial*. Conspiracist and esoteric-Hitlerist manifesto assembled from an address, an essay and polemical material. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1990-2000/)
- **1994:** *Nuestro honor se llama lealtad*. Joint publication by Serrano and Léon Degrelle; Serrano’s documented component is an address delivered on 20 April 1994, so sole authorship must not be inferred. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1990-2000/)
- **2005:** *C XVI años del nacimiento de Adolf Hitler*. Publication of Serrano’s commemorative address “Año nuevo CXVI”; it is a ritualized ideological manifesto rather than a descriptive historical study. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/2001-2/)

### Political and territorial interventions

- **1991:** *El nuevo orden transnacional y la Patagonia*. Published political address, simultaneously a pamphlet and manifesto concerning sovereignty and Patagonia. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1990-2000/)
- **1992:** *Defendamos nuestra Patagonia*. Published political speech; its book-form existence does not change its primary status as an intervention intended to mobilize an audience. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1990-2000/)
- **1992:** *No celebraremos la muerte de los dioses blancos en el quinto centenario de la llegada de Colón*. Published conference and counter-commemorative manifesto. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1990-2000/)
- **1994:** *Conspiración mundialista y traición a Chile*. Political-conspiracist pamphlet built partly from public addresses and previously circulated letters. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1990-2000/)
- **1994:** *Conspiración mundialista II, Laguna del Desierto y NAFTA*. Sequel-pamphlet combining territorial polemic, a speech and republished correspondence. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1990-2000/)
- **1995:** *Epistolario para impedir el fin de Chile*. Deliberately assembled collection of public letters with a new epilogue, “Metafísica de la traición”; the letters acted individually in newspapers before becoming a book-length political project. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1990-2000/)
- **1995:** *La destrucción de Chile*. Published speech rather than a conventional authored book; the standard bibliography records it independently but supplies no publisher or fuller physical description. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1990-2000/)
- **1996:** *Imitación de la verdad. La ciberpolítica. Internet, realidad virtual, telepresencia. Respuesta al ministro José Joaquín Brünner*. Published conference and technological-political manifesto, delivered at Concepción on 23 August 1996. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1990-2000/)
- **2001:** *Se acabó Chile. La estrella de Israel reemplaza a la estrella de Chile*. Antisemitic political pamphlet whose title announces its manifesto-like, interventionist character. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/2001-2/)
- **2003:** *La entrega de la Patagonia mágica*. Published conference dated 20 February 2003, joining territorial polemic to Serrano’s mythic geography. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/2001-2/)
- **2003:** *Rockefeller-Tompkins se compran Chile*. Political pamphlet assembled around public controversy; Serrano’s specifically documented contribution is its “Comentario final,” so attribution of the entire object remains uncertain. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/2001-2/)
- **2004:** *Lagos-Tompkins: el acuerdo que vende a Chile*. Political compilation incorporating Serrano’s previously published letters; the bibliography records the constituent texts but does not clearly establish him as sole maker of the complete volume. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/2001-2/)

### Autobiographical cycle

- **1995/1996:** *Memorias de él y yo. Volumen I: Aparición del “yo”, alejamiento de “Él”*. First volume of a four-part autobiographical and initiatory project, completed at Valparaíso on 28 August 1995 and published in 1996. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1990-2000/)
- **1996/1997:** *Memorias de él y yo. Volumen II: Adolf Hitler y la gran guerra*. Second volume, completed on 1 November 1996 and published in 1997; memoir and ideological self-mythology are inseparable in it. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1990-2000/)
- **1998:** *Memorias de él y yo. Volumen III: Misión en los Transhimalaya*. Third volume, completed at Valparaíso on 10 March 1998; diplomatic memoir, travel and initiatory narrative form one object. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1990-2000/)
- **1999:** *Memorias de él y yo. Volumen IV: El regreso*. Final volume, completed at Valparaíso on 4 April 1999. The four volumes constitute both separate books and a single autobiographical Gesamtkunstwerk that Serrano’s institutional profile describes as explaining the genesis of his larger oeuvre. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1990-2000/); [Memoria Chilena](https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/602/w3-article-96120.html)

### Periodical, editorial and collected textual projects

- **1939–1940:** *Página literaria de Trabajo*. A sequence of at least sixteen newspaper literary pages edited and critically shaped by Serrano, not a single book and not reducible to the individual texts printed within it. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1936-1940/)
- **1941–1943:** *La Nueva Edad*. Political periodical directed and edited by Serrano, documented through thirty-six numbered issues. It is a continuing editorial and propagandistic project rather than an individually authored text, and missing or incomplete issue data in the bibliography should not be silently repaired. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1941-1952/)
- **1936–2008:** *Articles, stories, speeches, conferences, letters, prologues, memorials and interviews*. Serrano’s standard chronological bibliography identifies these as hundreds of separately manifested short texts, including untitled or generically titled contributions; many were subsequently absorbed into the named obras above. Treating every later reprinting as a new work would confuse texts with editions, while treating the whole residue as one work would erase its resistance to that category. [Complete chronological bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/)
- **2016–2024, posthumous:** *Miguel Serrano, el escritor*, vols. 1–6. Collected-writings edition covering 1932–2008 and therefore an essential witness to the short-text corpus, but not an obra made by Serrano in this posthumous form; the selection, arrangement and six-volume architecture belong to later editors. [Posthumous bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/2001-2/)

### Editorial interventions that resist attribution as Serrano obras

- **1994:** *Mi lucha*, by Adolf Hitler. Serrano produced an introduction dated 21 September 1993 for this Spanish edition; the underlying book is not his obra, and the bibliography’s placement under his production records editorial intervention rather than authorship. [Bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/1990-2000/)
- **1952–2008:** *Prologues and paratexts for books by other writers*. These include introductions or prologues for Roberto Otaegui, Carmen Serrano Rosselot, Nicolás Palacios, Adolf Hitler and Fernando Saieh Alonso. Each is a made text, but none makes the host book Serrano’s obra; the chronological bibliography is the traceable item-level record. [Complete chronological bibliography](https://www.miguelserrano.cl/site/obra-bibliografia/)
