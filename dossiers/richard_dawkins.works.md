## Works by Richard Dawkins

### Doctoral project

- **1967:** *Selective Pecking in the Domestic Chick*. DPhil thesis in zoology: an experimental study of how chicks develop stimulus preferences. It is a research project as well as a text, preserved by the University of Oxford. [Oxford Research Archive](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid:df1dcbdc-951f-420c-8149-52d6cbe1b419)

### Scientific books and extended arguments

- **1976:** *The Selfish Gene*. A book-length theoretical synthesis arguing for a gene-centred understanding of evolution; it also introduced “meme” as a proposed cultural replicator, a conceptual creation rather than a separate text. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **1982:** *The Extended Phenotype*. A technical monograph extending the phenotypic effects of genes beyond an organism’s body. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **1986:** *The Blind Watchmaker*. A popular-science argument for cumulative natural selection, inseparable in conception from Dawkins’s computer-generated “biomorphs.” [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **1995:** *River Out of Eden: A Darwinian View of Life*. A short popular-science book in the Science Masters series, presenting evolutionary history as a river of genetic information. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **1996:** *Climbing Mount Improbable*. A popular-science book explaining how complex adaptations can arise through gradual cumulative selection. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **1998:** *Unweaving the Rainbow: Science, Delusion and the Appetite for Wonder*. A book-length defence of scientific explanation as a source of aesthetic experience. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **2004:** *The Ancestor’s Tale: A Pilgrimage to the Dawn of Life*. A reverse-chronological evolutionary history structured as a pilgrimage; the substantially revised 2016 edition was made with Yan Wong and is a revision, not an independent original obra. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **2006:** *The God Delusion*. A polemical book arguing against supernatural theism and religious privilege; because it seeks to change belief and public conduct, it sometimes functions as a secular manifesto as well as an argument. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **2009:** *The Greatest Show on Earth: The Evidence for Evolution*. A book assembling observational and experimental evidence for evolution. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **2011:** *The Magic of Reality: How We Know What’s Really True*. An illustrated explanatory book for younger and general readers, with artwork by Dave McKean; the visual-textual object is therefore collaborative. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **2019:** *Outgrowing God: A Beginner’s Guide*. A concise argument for abandoning religious belief, addressed especially to younger readers. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **2021:** *Flights of Fancy: Defying Gravity by Design and Evolution*. An illustrated study of flight in nature and human engineering; its first manifestation joins Dawkins’s prose to Jana Lenzová’s illustrations. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **2024:** *The Genetic Book of the Dead: A Darwinian Reverie*. A scientific meditation on organisms and genomes as records of ancestral environments, illustrated by Jana Lenzová. [Richard Dawkins Foundation](https://richarddawkins.net/)

### Memoirs and assembled prose

- **2003:** *A Devil’s Chaplain: Reflections on Hope, Lies, Science, and Love*. An edited gathering of essays, reviews, lectures, tributes and polemics, assembled by Latha Menon; many constituent texts had independent earlier manifestations. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **2013:** *An Appetite for Wonder: The Making of a Scientist*. The first volume of Dawkins’s memoir, ending around the making of *The Selfish Gene*. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **2015:** *Brief Candle in the Dark: My Life in Science*. The second memoir volume, combining autobiography with accounts of scientific and public projects. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **2017:** *Science in the Soul: Selected Writings of a Passionate Rationalist*. A collection of previously dispersed writings edited by Gillian Somerscales; as an obra it is a new arrangement of older manifestations. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **2021:** *Books Do Furnish a Life: Reading and Writing Science*. A collection of essays, reviews, forewords, afterwords and conversations; its constituent texts retain separate publication histories. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)

### Edited book

- **2008:** *The Oxford Book of Modern Science Writing*. An anthology selected and introduced by Dawkins. His obra here is curatorial—the choice, sequence and framing—not the collected writings themselves. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)

### Scientific papers and scholarly chapters

- **1968:** *The Ontogeny of a Pecking Preference in Domestic Chicks*. An experimental ethology paper derived from Dawkins’s doctoral research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1439-0310.1968.tb00011.x)
- **1969:** *A Threshold Model of Choice Behaviour*. A mathematical and experimental paper on animal decision-making. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/0003-3472(69)90120-1)
- **1969:** *The Attention Threshold Model*. A companion model of selective attention and choice. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/0003-3472(69)90121-3)
- **1969:** *Bees Are Easily Distracted*. A short scientific commentary in *Science*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.165.3895.751)
- **1969:** *The “Peck/No-Peck Decision-Maker” in the Black-Headed Gull Chick*. A co-authored experimental paper with Monica Impekoven. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/0003-3472(69)90009-8)
- **1971:** *The Hunting Behaviour of Individual Great Tits in Relation to Spatial Variations in Their Food Density*. A co-authored field-behaviour paper with James N. M. Smith. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0003-3472(71)80173-2)
- **1971:** *Selective Neurone Death as a Possible Memory Mechanism*. A brief theoretical proposal in *Nature*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/229118a0)
- **1971:** *A Cheap Method of Recording Behavioural Events, for Direct Computer-Access*. A methodological paper and, in part, a protocol for constructing and using an electronic event recorder. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1163/156853971X00375)
- **1972:** *“Blueprint for Survival”*. A many-author response in *Nature*; Dawkins’s individual contribution cannot be separated securely from the collective text. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/235405b0)
- **1973:** *Decisions and the Uncertainty of Behaviour*. A co-authored theoretical paper with Marian Dawkins. [JSTOR](https://www.jstor.org/stable/4533502)
- **1976:** *Growing Points in Ethology*. A scholarly book chapter proposing hierarchical organization as a productive principle for ethology. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **1976:** *Parental Investment, Mate Desertion and a Fallacy*. A co-authored theoretical paper with T. R. Carlisle. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/262131a0)
- **1976:** *The “Cost of Meiosis”: Is There Any?*. A co-authored theoretical paper with Michel Treisman. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-5193(76)90047-3)
- **1976:** *Universal Darwinism*. A chapter arguing that Darwinian selection belongs to a more general class of replicator processes. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **1978:** *Replicator Selection and the Extended Phenotype*. A paper developing the replicator-centred argument later expanded into *The Extended Phenotype*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1439-0310.1978.tb01823.x)
- **1978:** *Animal Signals: Information or Manipulation*. A co-authored chapter with John Krebs, treating signals as instruments in evolutionary conflicts of interest. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **1979:** *Twelve Misunderstandings of Kin Selection*. A corrective theoretical paper. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1439-0310.1979.tb00682.x)
- **1979:** *Arms Races Between and Within Species*. A co-authored paper with John Krebs developing the evolutionary-arms-race framework. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.1979.0081)
- **1979:** *Joint Nesting in a Digger Wasp as an Evolutionarily Stable Preadaptation to Social Life*. A co-authored empirical and theoretical paper with H. Jane Brockmann and Alan Grafen. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1163/156853979X00179)
- **1979:** *Evolutionarily Stable Nesting Strategy in a Digger Wasp*. A related co-authored mathematical study with H. Jane Brockmann and Alan Grafen. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-5193(79)90021-3)
- **1980:** *Good Strategy or Evolutionarily Stable Strategy*. A scholarly chapter distinguishing intentional language from evolutionary-strategy analysis. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **1980:** *Do Digger Wasps Commit the Concorde Fallacy?*. A co-authored behavioural paper with H. Jane Brockmann. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0003-3472(80)80149-7)
- **1981:** *Selfish Genes in Race or Politics*. A brief intervention resisting political misuse of gene-centred evolutionary language. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/289528a0)
- **1981:** *Genes and Culture: An Awkward Hybrid*. A critical review-essay in *Nature*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/290345a0)
- **1981:** *In Defence of Selfish Genes*. A philosophical defence of gene-centred selection. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0031819100050580)
- **1982:** *Replicators and Vehicles*. A scholarly chapter formalizing two roles in natural selection. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **1983:** *Adaptationism Was Always Predictive and Needed No Defense*. A short contribution to a target-article debate. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0140525X00016447)
- **1984:** *Animal Signals: Mind-Reading and Manipulation*. A revised co-authored chapter with John Krebs. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **1985:** *What Was All the Fuss About?*. A review-essay in *Nature* concerning sociobiology. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/316683a0)
- **1988:** *The Evolution of Evolvability*. A conference paper using Dawkins’s artificial-life programs to explore how developmental systems themselves may evolve; it was republished in a different form in 2003. [Artificial Life paper](https://biomorphbuilder.com/dawkins-paper/)
- **1990:** *Parasites, Desiderata Lists and the Paradox of the Organism*. A theoretical paper on adaptation and organismal integration. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0031182000073029)
- **1991:** *Evolution on the Mind*. A brief scientific commentary in *Nature*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/351686c0)
- **1992:** *Evolutionary Chemistry: Life in a Test Tube*. A co-authored review with Laurence Hurst. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/357198a0)
- **1993:** *Gaps in the Mind*. An essay for *The Great Ape Project* arguing against sharp species boundaries in moral thought. [PhilPapers](https://philpapers.org/rec/DAWGIT)
- **1994:** *The Eye in a Twinkling*. A short evolutionary-biology commentary. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/368690a0)
- **1994:** *Burying the Vehicle*. A short contribution to a scholarly debate about units and levels of selection. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0140525X00036207)
- **1994:** *The “St Jude” Mind Virus*. A co-authored letter with Oliver R. Goodenough proposing an analogy between circulating messages and self-replicating parasites. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/371023a0)
- **1995:** *Reply to Lucy Sullivan*. A formal reply in a Royal Society discussion of consciousness and natural selection. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.1995.0105)
- **1997:** *Religion and Science*. A co-authored exchange with Robin Holliday. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/bies.950190817)
- **1997:** *The Pope’s Message on Evolution: Obscurantism to the Rescue*. A critical review-essay. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1086/419951)
- **1997:** *Human Chauvinism*. An essay challenging morally privileged treatment of human species boundaries. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.2307/2411179)
- **1998:** *Postmodernism Disrobed*. A polemical review-essay in *Nature*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/28089)
- **1998:** *Arresting Evidence*. A scientific essay on forensic DNA evidence. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/j.2326-1951.1998.tb03673.x)
- **2000:** *W. D. Hamilton Memorial*. A scientific obituary and memorial text. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/35015793)
- **2002:** *Should Doctors Be Darwinian?*. A published medical-society lecture applying evolutionary reasoning to medicine. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17184029/)
- **2003:** *Is a Scientific Boycott Ever Justified?*. A jointly signed intervention by Colin Blakemore, Dawkins, Denis Noble and Michael Yudkin; it resists treatment as an individually authored work. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/421314b)
- **2003:** *The Evolution of Evolvability*. A revised book chapter derived from the 1988 artificial-life paper rather than an entirely independent creation. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **2004:** *Extended Phenotype—but Not Too Extended: A Reply to Laland, Turner and Jablonka*. A sustained clarification and defence of the extended-phenotype concept. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1023/B:BIPH.0000036180.14904.96)
- **2007:** *Genes Still Central*. A multi-participant published exchange with Denis Noble and Michael Yudkin; bibliographic records confusingly repeat Dawkins’s name, so its exact division of authorship should not be silently inferred. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0262-4079(07)63136-4)
- **2007:** *Should Science Speak to Faith?*. A paired debate with Lawrence M. Krauss, not a wholly Dawkins-authored essay. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17695847/)
- **2008:** *The Group Delusion / The Evolution of Altruism—What Matters Is Gene Selection*. Bibliographies give two titles for material attached to the same *New Scientist* page and DOI; whether these are distinct texts or variant labels is bibliographically unclear. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0262-4079(08)60086-X)

### Public essays and polemics separately catalogued

- **1982:** *The Myth of Genetic Determinism*. A popular explanatory essay distinguishing genetic causation from inevitability. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **1986:** *Creation and Natural Selection*. A popular-science article opposing creationist accounts of biological complexity. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **1992:** *Lions 10, Christians Nil*. A polemical magazine essay. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **1992:** *Is God a Computer Virus?*. An essay applying the replicator analogy to religious ideas. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **1993:** *Meet My Cousin, the Chimpanzee*. A popular-science essay on human evolutionary kinship. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **1993:** *The “Awe” Factor*. An essay defending scientific wonder. [Skeptical Inquirer](https://skepticalinquirer.org/)
- **1993:** *Viruses of the Mind*. A polemical essay treating some religious ideas as self-propagating informational parasites; it later reappeared in anthologies. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **1995:** *The Evolved Imagination*. A short essay in *Natural History*, also classified as an academic item by some bibliographies; that genre boundary is genuinely unstable. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **1995:** *Putting Away Childish Things*. A sceptical essay on religious belief. [Skeptical Inquirer](https://skepticalinquirer.org/)
- **1995:** *God’s Utility Function*. A popular essay interpreting suffering and apparent purpose through natural selection. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/scientificamerican1195-80)
- **1995:** *The Real Romance in the Stars*. A newspaper essay defending scientific accounts of the cosmos. [The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/)
- **1999:** *Snake Oil and Holy Water*. A magazine polemic against alternative medicine and religious reasoning. [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/)
- **2000:** *Hall of Mirrors*. A magazine essay on memes and cultural replication. [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/)
- **2001:** *What Is Science Good For?*. A public essay on the uses and cultural value of science. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11189460/)
- **2004:** *Gerin Oil*. A satirical anti-religious text whose title is an anagrammatic disguise; satire makes it an intervention as much as a descriptive essay. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **2005:** *The Giant Tortoise’s Tale*. A newspaper adaptation or manifestation of material from *The Ancestor’s Tale*, not an entirely separate conceptual project. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/books/2005/feb/19/featuresreviews.guardianreview10)
- **2005:** *The Turtle’s Tale*. Another newspaper manifestation of material associated with *The Ancestor’s Tale*. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/books/2005/feb/26/featuresreviews.guardianreview10)
- **2005:** *God’s Gift to Kansas*. A newspaper polemic prompted by the Kansas evolution controversy. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **2005:** *The Lava Lizard’s Tale*. A newspaper manifestation of evolutionary narrative associated with *The Ancestor’s Tale*; the standard bibliography does not supply a precise publication date. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/)

- **1999:** *Foreword to The Meme Machine*. A framing essay for Susan Blackmore’s study of memetics. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **2000:** *Foreword to Pyramids of Life*. A paratext for Harvey Croze and John Reader’s ecological book. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **2001:** *Foreword to Snake Oil and Other Preoccupations*. A memorial and framing text for John Diamond’s posthumous collection. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **2001:** *Foreword to The Lion Children*. A paratext for Angus, Maisie and Travers McNeice’s account of childhood in Botswana. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)
- **2002:** *Foreword to The Descent of Man*. A framing text for a new student edition of Charles Darwin’s work, not a new edition authored by Dawkins. [Bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins_bibliography)

### Software, algorithms and generated populations

- **1980s:** *Weasel*. A small computer simulation written by Dawkins to demonstrate cumulative selection. The meaningful obra is the algorithmic experiment rather than any one transient output or later reimplementation. [Program history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weasel_program)
- **c. 1984–1986:** *Blind Watchmaker / Monochrome Biomorphs*. An interactive Macintosh artificial-life program written by Dawkins, producing branching forms through mutation and user selection. It is a protocol, executable model and image-generating system at once; surviving modern versions are collaborative ports, not the original software manifestation. [Dawkins’s account](https://watchmakersuite.sourceforge.net/thisismyvisionoflife.html)
- **1990–1993:** *Colour Biomorphs*. An expanded artificial-life program adding colour genetics to the biomorph system. The surviving source belongs to a four-program suite written by Dawkins and collaborators, so sole authorship should not be assumed for every component. [WatchmakerSuite catalogue](https://sourceforge.net/p/watchmakersuite/wiki/Home/)
- **1990–1993:** *Arthromorphs*. An interactive evolutionary program generating segmented, arthropod-like forms. Like the other suite components, it is a collaborative executable teaching object whose outputs are manifestations of the program rather than individually titled works. [WatchmakerSuite](https://watchmakersuite.sourceforge.net/)
- **1990–1993:** *Conchomorphs*. A shell-generating evolutionary program in the Watchmaker suite. Its bibliographic identity is unstable because the software survives chiefly through source recovery and later ports. [WatchmakerSuite catalogue](https://sourceforge.net/p/watchmakersuite/wiki/Home/)

### Television works and audiovisual lecture-projects

- **1986:** *Nice Guys Finish First*. A BBC *Horizon* documentary written and presented by Dawkins, extending the argument about cooperation, conflict and the “selfish gene” into television. [Filmography](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/richard_dawkins)
- **1987:** *The Blind Watchmaker*. A BBC *Horizon* documentary presented and co-produced by Dawkins but written and produced by Jeremy Taylor; it is therefore a collaborative screen manifestation of Dawkins’s book and software project. [Programme record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Blind_Watchmaker_%28film%29)
- **1991:** *Growing Up in the Universe*. A five-part Royal Institution Christmas Lectures series written and presented by Dawkins. It is simultaneously a live lecture project, televised series and demonstration-based science Gesamtkunstwerk rather than simply a written text. [Screen credits](https://www.tvguide.com/celebrities/richard-dawkins/credits/3030758020/)
- **1996:** *Break the Science Barrier*. A Channel 4 television lecture-documentary written and presented by Dawkins. [Filmography](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/richard_dawkins)
- **2004:** *The Atheism Tapes*. A six-part compilation of extended interviews conducted by Jonathan Miller for *A Rough History of Disbelief*. Dawkins is one interview subject, not its maker; it resists inclusion as his obra and is retained only because standard Dawkins filmographies often misclassify participation as authorship. [Filmography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins)
- **2005:** *Why Are We Here?*. An episode of *The Big Question* presented by Dawkins. It is a commissioned television contribution rather than a wholly controlled Dawkins work. [Filmography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins)
- **2006:** *The Root of All Evil?*. A two-part Channel 4 polemical documentary written and presented by Dawkins. The broadcast title was imposed by Channel 4 and disliked by Dawkins, so title and authorial intention do not coincide perfectly. [Writer credits](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm1468026/)
- **2007:** *The Enemies of Reason*. A two-part documentary written and presented by Dawkins and directed by Russell Barnes; it is a collaborative television polemic against superstition and alternative medicine. [Programme record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Enemies_of_Reason)
- **2008:** *The Genius of Charles Darwin*. A three-part Channel 4 series written and presented by Dawkins. It combines biography, evolutionary explanation, interviews and public argument. [Programme record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Genius_of_Charles_Darwin)
- **2008:** *Discussions with Richard Dawkins: The Four Horsemen*. A filmed round-table conversation written and produced under Dawkins’s name with Daniel Dennett, Sam Harris and Christopher Hitchens; the event is irreducibly collaborative. [Screen credits](https://www.tvguide.com/celebrities/richard-dawkins/credits/3030758020/)
- **2010:** *Faith School Menace?*. A Channel 4 television documentary written and presented by Dawkins, arguing against state-supported religious schooling. [Writer credits](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm1468026/)
- **2012:** *Sex, Death and the Meaning of Life*. A three-part documentary series presented by Dawkins, exploring secular approaches to mortality, morality and meaning. Presentation does not establish sole authorship of the production. [Programme record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex%2C_Death_and_the_Meaning_of_Life)
- **2012:** *Beautiful Minds: Richard Dawkins*. A BBC biographical documentary about Dawkins. It is not his obra despite his central participation and is included only to mark that resistance explicitly. [Filmography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins)
- **2013:** *The Unbelievers*. A documentary following Dawkins and Lawrence Krauss on public-speaking tours. Both men are subjects and performers, not the film’s principal makers; it should not be silently absorbed into Dawkins’s works. [Filmography](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/richard_dawkins)

### Spoken-word and musical collaborations

- **2015:** *Endless Forms Most Beautiful* contributions. Dawkins supplied spoken narration for Nightwish’s “Shudder Before the Beautiful” and “The Greatest Show on Earth.” These are performed fragments inside compositions made principally by Tuomas Holopainen and the band, not Dawkins songs or independently titled literary works. [Filmography and performance record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins)
- **2015–2016:** *The Greatest Show on Earth* live narration. Dawkins performed his spoken contribution with Nightwish at Wembley Arena; the performance subsequently appeared in the concert release *Vehicle of Spirit*. It is a new performance manifestation, not a separately authored text. [Performance record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins)

### Institutional project

- **2006–present:** *Richard Dawkins Foundation for Reason & Science*. An advocacy and educational institution founded by Dawkins, later merged organizationally with the Center for Inquiry. It is a continuing project rather than a bounded authored work, and its campaigns and publications are collective productions. [Foundation](https://richarddawkins.net/)
