## Works by Hermann Hesse

### Novels and long prose fictions

- **1904:** *Peter Camenzind*. A novel of artistic formation and estrangement, and Hesse’s first major public success. It is treated as a novel in the complete works. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 2](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1906:** *Unterm Rad*. A novel, sometimes bibliographically called an “Erzählung,” about schooling and the destruction of a gifted pupil; the unstable genre label is part of its publication history. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 2](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1910:** *Gertrud*. A novel presented as the memoir of a musician, making musical composition part of its fictional form. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 2](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1914:** *Roßhalde*. A novel about a painter, his failed marriage, and the competing claims of art and domestic life. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 3](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1915:** *Knulp: Drei Geschichten aus dem Leben Knulps*. A cycle of three linked stories that functions as a short novel; its protagonist’s wandering life resists the conventional completed biography. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 3](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1917–1919:** *Demian: Die Geschichte von Emil Sinclairs Jugend*. A novel completed in 1917 and published in 1919 under the fictive authorship of “Emil Sinclair”; early attribution is therefore deliberately unstable rather than genuinely anonymous. [Museum Hermann Hesse Montagnola](https://www.hessemontagnola.ch/bibliographie); [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 3](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1919:** *Klein und Wagner*. A novella of flight, divided identity, and psychic crisis, later commonly bound with *Klingsors letzter Sommer*. [Museum Hermann Hesse Montagnola](https://www.hessemontagnola.ch/bibliographie); [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vols. 3 and 8](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1920:** *Klingsors letzter Sommer*. A novella about a painter’s final season; it is also the title of a book containing this and other prose, so the text and the collection must not be silently conflated. [Museum Hermann Hesse Montagnola](https://www.hessemontagnola.ch/bibliographie); [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 8](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1919–1922:** *Siddhartha: Eine indische Dichtung*. A two-part prose fiction conceived and written across a creative interruption, then published as an “Indian poem”; “novel” describes its modern reception but not its full generic claim. [Museum Hermann Hesse Montagnola](https://www.hessemontagnola.ch/bibliographie); [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 3](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1927:** *Der Steppenwolf*. A novel incorporating a preface, Harry Haller’s records, the separately typographed *Tractat vom Steppenwolf*, and the “Magisches Theater”; it is an intentionally composite textual object rather than a single uninterrupted narrative. [Museum Hermann Hesse Montagnola](https://www.hessemontagnola.ch/bibliographie); [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 4](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1930:** *Narziß und Goldmund*. A historical-philosophical novel contrasting monastic intellect with embodied artistic experience. [Museum Hermann Hesse Montagnola](https://www.hessemontagnola.ch/bibliographie); [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 4](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1932:** *Die Morgenlandfahrt*. A short novel, fictional confession, and allegory of a spiritual league; its journey is less a recoverable itinerary than a deliberately impossible collective project. [Museum Hermann Hesse Montagnola](https://www.hessemontagnola.ch/bibliographie); [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 4](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1931–1943:** *Das Glasperlenspiel: Versuch einer Lebensbeschreibung des Magister Ludi Josef Knecht samt Knechts hinterlassenen Schriften*. A novel, fictive biography, archive, educational utopia, poems, and three imagined “lives”; its form approaches a literary Gesamtkunstwerk while openly calling itself only a “Versuch.” [Museum Hermann Hesse Montagnola](https://www.hessemontagnola.ch/bibliographie); [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 5](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1930s, unfinished; published posthumously:** *Der vierte Lebenslauf Josef Knechts*. An abandoned continuation or alternative life associated with *Das Glasperlenspiel*, surviving in two versions; it is a project whose incompletion is constitutive, not a defective finished novel. [Hermann-Hesse-Portal/Suhrkamp bibliography](https://www.hermann-hesse.de/leben-und-werk/literatur/werke-von-und-ueber-hermann-hesse/)

### Story collections, tales, legends, and prose miniatures

- **1899:** *Eine Stunde hinter Mitternacht*. A privately printed sequence of nine lyrical prose pieces; it lies between story collection, prose poem, and deliberately fashioned first book. [Hermann-Hesse-Portal, work overview](https://www.hhesse.de/werk/); [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 1](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1900–1901; enlarged 1907:** *Hinterlassene Schriften und Gedichte von Hermann Lauscher* / *Hermann Lauscher*. A pseudo-posthumous archive by an invented author, mixing narrative, autobiography, diary, and verse; different issues contain different selections, so there is no single uncomplicated original extent. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 1](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41); [Hermann-Hesse-Portal/Suhrkamp bibliography](https://www.hermann-hesse.de/leben-und-werk/literatur/werke-von-und-ueber-hermann-hesse/)
- **1907:** *Diesseits*. A collection of stories rather than one continuous fiction; its contents include independently made and separately published texts. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vols. 6–7](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1908:** *Nachbarn*. A story collection whose book-level unity is editorial and thematic, while its constituent narratives remain independent works. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 7](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1915:** *Am Weg*. A collection of shorter prose; it is a made book, but not the first manifestation of every text assembled in it. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vols. 6–8](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1916:** *Schön ist die Jugend*. A two-novella volume whose title text became one of Hesse’s principal retrospective stories of youth. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 8](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1919:** *Märchen*. A collection of modern literary fairy tales; these are authored transformations of the genre, not transcriptions of anonymous folklore. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 9](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1920:** *Klingsors letzter Sommer*. At book level, a collection joining *Kinderseele*, *Klein und Wagner*, and the title novella; it therefore differs materially from the single novella of the same name. [Museum Hermann Hesse Montagnola](https://www.hessemontagnola.ch/bibliographie); [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 8](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1933:** *Die kleine Welt*. A selected story collection assembled as a book from previously made prose. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vols. 6–8](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1935:** *Fabulierbuch*. A retrospective collection of tales and imaginative prose; it is a new bibliographical manifestation, but many constituent works predate it. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vols. 6–9](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1951:** *Späte Prosa*. A collection of late stories, recollections, dreams, and prose miniatures, deliberately crossing fiction and autobiographical record. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vols. 8 and 11–14](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1900–1954:** *The complete corpus of individual stories*. More than the familiar book collections, this obra comprises all separately titled narratives, including juvenilia, newspaper appearances, variants, fairy tales, legends, idylls, and posthumously recovered texts; it resists reduction to “books,” and its authoritative enumeration is the title register to volumes 6–9 and 20 of the complete edition. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vols. 6–9 and 20](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)

### Poetry and verse works

- **1898:** *Romantische Lieder*. Hesse’s first printed poetry collection, privately issued; the 1899 date sometimes attached to it reflects circulation or cataloguing rather than the stated first publication year. [Hermann-Hesse-Portal, work overview](https://www.hhesse.de/werk/); [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vols. 1 and 10](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1902:** *Gedichte*. Hesse’s first commercially issued book devoted wholly to verse. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vols. 1 and 10](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1911:** *Unterwegs*. A poetry collection organized around travel, distance, and wandering. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 10](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1929:** *Trost der Nacht*. A collection of poems, not a single long poem; later selections often detach its contents from this original book-object. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 10](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1934:** *Vom Baum des Lebens*. A selected poetry book authorized during Hesse’s lifetime; as a selection it is a manifestation of the lyric corpus, not a new set of poems. [Hermann-Hesse-Portal/Suhrkamp bibliography](https://www.hermann-hesse.de/leben-und-werk/literatur/werke-von-und-ueber-hermann-hesse/)
- **1935–1936:** *Stunden im Garten: Eine Idylle*. A long verse idyll based on gardening and contemplation; its publication date varies by bibliographical convention between limited preliminary issue and trade edition. [Museum Hermann Hesse Montagnola](https://www.hessemontagnola.ch/bibliographie); [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vols. 9–10](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1942:** *Die Gedichte*. Hesse’s lifetime gathering of his verse; it is a self-curated corpus manifestation rather than the complete surviving poetic œuvre. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 10](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1961:** *Stufen*. A late authorial selection named after the poem “Stufen”; the book and the individual poem are distinct things sharing a title. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 10](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1892–1962:** *The complete corpus of poems*. This obra contains the titled and untitled verse, juvenilia, occasional poems, manuscript gifts, and poems embedded in other works; Volker Michels’s edition occupies 848 pages, making a short list of famous titles structurally misleading. [Hermann-Hesse-Portal, *Die Gedichte 1892–1962*](https://www.hermann-hesse.de/leben-und-werk/literatur/werke-von-und-ueber-hermann-hesse/); [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 10](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)

### Travel books, diaries, autobiographical constructions, and mixed-media books

- **1913:** *Aus Indien: Aufzeichnungen von einer indischen Reise*. A travel book assembled from diary, recollection, poem, and cultural observation after Hesse’s 1911 Asian journey; “India” in its title reflects contemporary geographical usage broader than the journey’s actual route. [Hermann-Hesse-Portal/Suhrkamp bibliography](https://www.hermann-hesse.de/leben-und-werk/literatur/werke-von-und-ueber-hermann-hesse/); [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vols. 11–13](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1918–1920:** *Wanderung: Aufzeichnungen*. A Gesamtkunstwerk-like book combining brief prose, poems, and Hesse’s own painted illustrations; separating “text” from “pictures” loses the form in which the project was conceived. [Hermann-Hesse-Portal, painting history](https://www.hermann-hesse.de/leben-und-werk/malerei/von-der-therapie-zur-leidenschaft/); [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vols. 11 and 13](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1923:** *Sinclairs Notizbuch*. A collection of reflections first associated with the “Emil Sinclair” persona; it occupies the unstable boundary between notebook, essay collection, and pseudonymous continuation. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vols. 13 and 15](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1925:** *Kurgast: Aufzeichnungen von einer Badener Kur*. An autobiographical spa journal reshaped into comic and self-analytical literary prose. [Museum Hermann Hesse Montagnola](https://www.hessemontagnola.ch/bibliographie); [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 11](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1926:** *Bilderbuch*. A collection of travel and landscape pieces; the title identifies a verbal “picture book,” not simply an illustrated volume. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vols. 11 and 13](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1927:** *Die Nürnberger Reise*. A travel narrative, diary, portrait gallery, and reflection on literary celebrity. [Museum Hermann Hesse Montagnola](https://www.hessemontagnola.ch/bibliographie); [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 11](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1937:** *Gedenkblätter*. Memorial portraits of deceased relatives, friends, writers, and contemporaries; it is both autobiography by indirection and an archive of other lives. [Hermann-Hesse-Portal/Suhrkamp bibliography](https://www.hermann-hesse.de/leben-und-werk/literatur/werke-von-und-ueber-hermann-hesse/); [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 12](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1920s–1962:** *Dream diaries and late diaries*. These manuscript and published records are autobiographical texts but not consistently prepared by Hesse as autonomous works; their resistance to the category “work” should be retained rather than erased by later thematic editions. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vols. 11–12](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **Childhood–1962:** *The complete corpus of autobiographical writings*. This includes childhood recollections, curriculum vitae, diaries, travel records, self-portraits, dreams, memorials, and late notes; the complete edition assigns it two volumes because no single book exhausts the obra. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vols. 11–12](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)

### Essays, criticism, political interventions, and active texts

- **1904:** *Boccaccio*. A short critical-biographical study made for a popular monograph series, not a fictional work. [Hermann-Hesse-Portal/Suhrkamp bibliography](https://www.hermann-hesse.de/leben-und-werk/literatur/werke-von-und-ueber-hermann-hesse/)
- **1904:** *Franz von Assisi*. A biographical and interpretive study of Francis rather than a devotional protocol, though its admiring rhetoric approaches spiritual exhortation. [Hermann-Hesse-Portal/Suhrkamp bibliography](https://www.hermann-hesse.de/leben-und-werk/literatur/werke-von-und-ueber-hermann-hesse/)
- **1914:** *O Freunde, nicht diese Töne!*. A wartime newspaper appeal against nationalist hatred; it operates as a manifesto-like public intervention rather than detached description. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 15](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1917:** *Soll Friede werden*. A political appeal written during the First World War; its purpose is performative and hortatory, so “manifesto” fits better than neutral essay. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 15](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1919:** *Zarathustras Wiederkehr: Ein Wort an die deutsche Jugend*. A pseudonymous postwar address to German youth, first presented under “Emil Sinclair”; it is a manifesto and fictional mask at once. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 15](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1928:** *Betrachtungen*. A book of reflective prose assembled from independently originating pieces; its unity is contemplative rather than narrative. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vols. 13–14](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1929:** *Eine Bibliothek der Weltliteratur*. A programmatic essay proposing a personal route through world literature; it resembles a reading protocol, but offers orientation rather than mandatory canon. [Hermann-Hesse-Portal, work overview](https://www.hhesse.de/werk/); [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 14](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1946:** *Krieg und Frieden: Betrachtungen zu Krieg und Politik seit dem Jahr 1914*. An authorially assembled documentation of antiwar and political writings; it is simultaneously collection, retrospective argument, and public manifesto. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 15](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1955:** *Beschwörungen*. A late collection of reflective and autobiographical prose whose title emphasizes language as invocation or active utterance. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vols. 12–14](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1899–1961:** *The complete corpus of Betrachtungen und Berichte*. Hundreds of observations, speeches, travel reports, commemorations, cultural essays, and occasional pieces form an obra dispersed across periodicals and books; the complete edition gathers them chronologically in two volumes. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vols. 13–14](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1914–1962:** *The complete corpus of political writings*. Appeals, open letters, polemics, speeches, and statements constitute a documentary body of action-oriented texts rather than one stable book; pseudonyms and revised republication complicate their individual attribution history. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 15](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1900–1962:** *Die Welt im Buch: Rezensionen und Aufsätze*. Hesse’s vast corpus of book reviews and literary essays, many originally ephemeral and unsigned or pseudonymous, fills five volumes in the scholarly edition; the editors describe it as the first complete chronological collection, so earlier enumerations are necessarily incomplete. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vols. 16–20](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)

### Dramatic fragments, translations, adaptations, and editorial creations

- **Youth–maturity; chiefly unpublished in Hesse’s lifetime:** *Dramatic works and fragments*. The surviving dramatic corpus includes juvenilia and unfinished experiments rather than a recognized theatrical œuvre; these projects resist being treated as completed plays. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vols. 1 and 9](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **Various dates:** *Translations and retellings*. Hesse translated or freely recast legends and older narratives, sometimes in collaboration or through intermediary texts; attribution therefore ranges from translation to adaptation and cannot be reduced to original authorship. [Suhrkamp, *Sämtliche Werke*, vol. 9](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1900s–1950s:** *Anthologies and editions selected by Hesse*. His selections of works by or associated with writers including Brentano, Eichendorff, Mörike, Jean Paul, Novalis, Christian Wagner, and traditions such as the *Gesta Romanorum* are editorial creations: Hesse made the selection, arrangement, introductions, or afterwords, but not the underlying texts. [Hermann-Hesse-Portal, “Hermann Hesse als Herausgeber”](https://www.hermann-hesse.de/leben-und-werk/literatur/werke-von-und-ueber-hermann-hesse/)
- **1918:** *Ein Schwabenbuch für die deutschen Kriegsgefangenen*. A collaboratively edited cultural book made for prisoners of war; Hesse’s work here is organizational and editorial, not sole authorship of the contents. [Reference Guide to World Literature](https://www.cur.ac.rw/mis/main/library/documents/book_file/digital-6476117c1434c2.34132592.pdf)
- **1907–1922:** *Von Poesie und Politik: Briefwechsel mit Conrad Haußmann*. A co-created correspondence rather than a unilateral authored book; its later edition manifests an exchange whose individual letters remain separately attributable. [Hermann-Hesse-Portal/Suhrkamp bibliography](https://www.hermann-hesse.de/leben-und-werk/literatur/werke-von-und-ueber-hermann-hesse/)

### Letters and communicative works

- **1880s–1962:** *The complete correspondence*. Tens of thousands of letters survive or are documented, but correspondence is relational and radically incomplete: Hesse made each letter, while every published “Briefwechsel” is a later editorial reconstruction rather than a work he designed as a book. [Hermann-Hesse-Portal/Suhrkamp bibliography](https://www.hermann-hesse.de/leben-und-werk/literatur/werke-von-und-ueber-hermann-hesse/); [Suhrkamp complete-edition description](https://www.suhrkamp.de/werkausgabe/hermann-hesse-saemtliche-werke-in-20-baenden-und-einem-registerband-leinen-w-41)
- **1895–1962:** *Gesammelte Briefe*. The four-volume chronological selection is the principal collected manifestation of Hesse’s letters, but “collected” does not mean complete and the editorial selection is post-authorial. [Hermann-Hesse-Portal/Suhrkamp bibliography](https://www.hermann-hesse.de/leben-und-werk/literatur/werke-von-und-ueber-hermann-hesse/)
- **1946–1962:** *Rundbriefe*. Circular letters made to answer many correspondents at once; these are simultaneously personal communications, reproducible public statements, and an improvised protocol for managing celebrity. [Hermann-Hesse-Portal/Suhrkamp bibliography](https://www.hermann-hesse.de/leben-und-werk/literatur/werke-von-und-ueber-hermann-hesse/)

### Paintings, drawings, illustrated manuscripts, and visual-textual works

- **1916–1962:** *The pictorial œuvre*. Approximately 3,000 watercolors, with additional drawings, sketches, self-portraits, and decorated manuscripts, form a major body of work rather than illustrations ancillary to the writing; many sheets are privately held, untitled, or imprecisely dated, so pretending to list them as securely titled individual works would invent information. [Hermann-Hesse-Portal, “Von der Therapie zur Leidenschaft”](https://www.hermann-hesse.de/leben-und-werk/malerei/von-der-therapie-zur-leidenschaft/)
- **1917:** *Self-portrait studies*. A group of early visual works arising from the period of psychoanalysis; the group is documented, but its individual sheets do not all possess stable public titles or dates. [Hermann-Hesse-Portal, “Von der Therapie zur Leidenschaft”](https://www.hermann-hesse.de/leben-und-werk/malerei/von-der-therapie-zur-leidenschaft/)
- **1918:** *Illustrated poem cycles for the German prisoners-of-war welfare project*. Handwritten poems and original images were made and sold for charitable support; each copy can be a materially distinct object, so the project resists identification with one reproducible book. [Hermann-Hesse-Portal, “Von der Therapie zur Leidenschaft”](https://www.hermann-hesse.de/leben-und-werk/malerei/von-der-therapie-zur-leidenschaft/)
- **1918–1920:** *The original image-and-text materials for Wanderung*. Watercolors, drawings, poems, and prose notes constitute the generative mixed-media project behind the printed book. [Hermann-Hesse-Portal, “Von der Therapie zur Leidenschaft”](https://www.hermann-hesse.de/leben-und-werk/malerei/von-der-therapie-zur-leidenschaft/)
- **1921:** *Elf Aquarelle aus dem Tessin*. A portfolio of reproductions after eleven Ticino watercolors, and therefore a designed visual publication distinct from both the original sheets and Hesse’s literary books. [Hermann-Hesse-Portal, “Von der Therapie zur Leidenschaft”](https://www.hermann-hesse.de/leben-und-werk/malerei/von-der-therapie-zur-leidenschaft/)
- **1920s–1962:** *Painted letters, poem manuscripts, and dedication sheets*. These unique hybrids of handwriting, watercolor, ornament, and personal address refuse the separation of correspondence, manuscript, and visual art; they are small-scale Gesamtkunstwerke, frequently untitled and not comprehensively catalogued in public. [Hermann-Hesse-Portal, painting history](https://www.hermann-hesse.de/leben-und-werk/malerei/von-der-therapie-zur-leidenschaft/); [Städtische Galerie Villingen-Schwenningen](https://galerie.villingen-schwenningen.de/kultur-vielfalt/staedtische-galerie/rueckblick/hermann-hesse-die-aquarelle)
- **Various dates:** *Ticino landscapes and village views*. This is a recurring pictorial body—houses, gardens, mountains, trees, and settlements—rather than one titled series fixed by Hesse; later exhibition and book titles are curatorial manifestations, not original titles for the whole. [Hermann-Hesse-Portal, “Von der Therapie zur Leidenschaft”](https://www.hermann-hesse.de/leben-und-werk/malerei/von-der-therapie-zur-leidenschaft/)

### Voice and recorded manifestations

- **1946:** *Nobel Prize award broadcast*. Hesse’s participation survives as a radio document, but the recording is an event-manifestation involving broadcasters and ceremony rather than a wholly authored sound work by Hesse. [Swiss National Sound Archives record, cited in the documented discography](https://www.fonoteca.ch/)
- **1949:** *Über das Glück, gelesen von Hermann Hesse*. A recording of Hesse reading his own prose; the vocal performance is a distinct manifestation made by the author, not merely another printed edition of the text. [Swiss National Sound Archives](https://www.fonoteca.ch/)
