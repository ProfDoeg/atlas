## Works by Hernando Arias de Saavedra

### Protocols and governmental texts

- **1598:** *Ordenanzas para el gobierno y tratamiento de los indígenas*. A protocol regulating encomienda labor, settlement, instruction, and treatment. Its status as an independent work is qualified: the surviving scholarship says it closely followed Juan Ramírez de Velasco’s 1597 ordinances and was subsequently incorporated and revised in Hernandarias’s 1603 code. ([Perusset](https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/5026770.pdf))

- **29 November 1603:** *Ordenanzas para el buen gobierno del Río de la Plata*. A thirty-one-article governmental protocol, promulgated in Spanish and Guaraní, governing encomiendas, Indigenous labor, subsistence, religious instruction, and punishment. Although framed as protective legislation, it remained an instrument of colonial resettlement and compulsory labor; its provisions also drew on earlier ordinances and the Asunción synod of 1603 rather than constituting a wholly individual composition. ([Perusset](https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/5026770.pdf), [Sušnik’s documentary references](https://portalguarani.com/965_branislava_susnik__/9664_el_indio_colonial_del_paraguay__tomo_i__obra_de_branislava_susnik__ano_1965.html))

- **1603:** *Order requiring Chaco cattle owners to build corrals*. An administrative protocol preserved in the Archivo Nacional de Asunción catalogue. It resists treatment as a literary “work”: it is an executive act recorded through scribal government machinery, and the catalogue does not establish that Hernandarias personally drafted the surviving wording. ([Archivo Nacional de Asunción catalogue](https://es.scribd.com/document/638677420/Archivo-Nacional-Asuncion-CATALOGO-CARPETAS))

- **1609:** *Order requiring horses to be brought across the river*. An executive order known from the Archivo Nacional de Asunción catalogue. As with other gubernatorial instruments, institutional issuance under Hernandarias’s name is secure, but personal penmanship or sole verbal authorship is not. ([Archivo Nacional de Asunción catalogue](https://es.scribd.com/document/638677420/Archivo-Nacional-Asuncion-CATALOGO-CARPETAS))

### Letters, reports, and memorials

- **30 January 1600:** *Carta al Rey*. An official report and petition concerning the condition and government of the Río de la Plata provinces, Hernandarias’s services, and his family’s poverty. The Biblioteca Nacional documentary edition numbers it 1 and preserves it as the first item in its 1600–1607 series. ([Biblioteca Nacional, *Cartas y memoriales*, first series](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/files/ac77ac0723f5383a2563085088fe5943.pdf))

- **20 April 1602:** *Carta al Rey*. An official letter recounting his military and governmental service and requesting royal recognition or support. It is item 2 in the Biblioteca Nacional documentary edition. ([Biblioteca Nacional, *Cartas y memoriales*, first series](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/files/ac77ac0723f5383a2563085088fe5943.pdf))

- **5 April 1604:** *Carta al Rey*. A governmental report written from Buenos Aires about the province, Hernandarias’s journey with the bishop toward Asunción, and the condition of the settlements visited. It belongs to the official-report genre rather than to travel literature, although it records a journey. ([Sušnik’s documentary references](https://portalguarani.com/965_branislava_susnik__/9664_el_indio_colonial_del_paraguay__tomo_i__obra_de_branislava_susnik__ano_1965.html), [Sposito](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/378694829_Portuguese_Invasions_in_Paraguay-Rio_de_la_Plata_During_the_Iberian_Union_Moving_Between_Local_Conflicts_and_Imperial_Projects1))

- **4 May 1607:** *Carta al Rey*. A report on the province, Indigenous communities, settlement, and government. Modern scholarship sometimes calls the accompanying piece a separate memorial, so the letter and enclosure should not automatically be collapsed into one text. ([Sušnik’s documentary references](https://portalguarani.com/965_branislava_susnik__/9664_el_indio_colonial_del_paraguay__tomo_i__obra_de_branislava_susnik__ano_1965.html), [Revista Paginas](https://ojs.rosario-conicet.gov.ar/index.php/revistaISHIR/article/download/722/768/2758))

- **4 May 1607:** *Memorial annexed to the letter to the King*. An official memorial describing provincial conditions and advocating governmental measures. Its attachment to the letter makes it bibliographically unstable: some references treat the packet as one communication, while others identify the memorial separately. ([Revista Paginas](https://ojs.rosario-conicet.gov.ar/index.php/revistaISHIR/article/download/722/768/2758))

- **5 May 1607:** *Carta al Rey*. A separate official communication concerning the political and economic condition of the Río de la Plata. Its existence and date are recorded in the Comisión Oficial’s documentary compilation cited by later scholarship. ([Amadori and Angeli](https://ojs2.fch.unicen.edu.ar/ojs-3.1.0/index.php/anuario-ies/article/download/1690/1556))

- **27 May 1607:** *Carta al Rey*. A report on Buenos Aires, including the enforcement of urban building obligations. The original is described as two folios in Archivo General de Indias, Audiencia de Charcas, legajo 27. ([Torre Revello](https://es.scribd.com/document/430802700/Archivum-02C1-Fray-Juan-Pascual-Rivadeneira-1944))

- **1608:** *Carta al Rey concerning the Guairá missions and related provincial affairs*. An official report supporting missionary expansion and addressing frontier government. References give the year, and some secondary citations specify July, but the exact day should not be supplied without the manuscript or critical edition. ([Mundo Agrario](https://www.acuedi.org/ddata/1922.pdf))

- **22 November 1608:** *Carta*. A surviving official letter cited by Efraím Cardozo in his documentary history of colonial Paraguay. The abbreviated citation available there does not safely establish its full original rubric or recipient, so no expanded title is invented here. ([Cardozo](https://portalguarani.com/586_efraim_cardozo__/14164_el_paraguay_colonial__las_raices_de_la_nacionalidad_1959__por_efraim_cardozo.html))

- **12 May 1609:** *Carta al Rey sobre los méritos del capitán Manuel de Frías y los antepasados de Leonor Martel de Guzmán*. A recommendation and service memorial embedded in official correspondence. It is simultaneously report, patronage instrument, and genealogical argument—not an autonomous literary work. ([Amadori and Angeli](https://ojs2.fch.unicen.edu.ar/ojs-3.1.0/index.php/anuario-ies/article/download/1690/1556))

- **1614:** *Certification concerning the Jesuit reductions*. An official testimonial supporting the status and work of the Jesuit missions. It survives in later documentary controversy; because a certification is evidentiary action and may have been engrossed by a secretary, it resists attribution as a solely authored composition. ([Cardiel documentary discussion](https://es.scribd.com/doc/208998940/DECLARACION-DE-LA-VERDAD-JOSE-CARDIEL-PORTALGUARANI))

- **25 May 1616:** *Carta al Rey on the visitation of settlements and reductions*. Item 67 in the Biblioteca Nacional edition, this report describes Hernandarias’s circuit through Asunción, Indigenous settlements, and Franciscan and Jesuit reductions. It is an administrative itinerary as much as a letter. ([Biblioteca Nacional, *Cartas y memoriales*, fourth series](https://www.scribd.com/document/904267673/80f16fb30931933321120451e498965f))

- **25 May 1616:** *Carta al Rey on the port, trade, and customs house*. Item 68 in the documentary edition, this distinct letter reports the regulation of shipping and commerce and the construction of a customs building. ([Biblioteca Nacional, *Cartas y memoriales*, fourth series](https://www.scribd.com/document/904267673/80f16fb30931933321120451e498965f))

- **25 May 1616:** *Carta al Rey on royal dispatches and proceedings against contraband networks*. Item 69 in the edition, this separate report concerns missing royal communications, Portuguese commerce, royal revenue, and proceedings involving Simón de Valdés and others. ([Biblioteca Nacional, *Cartas y memoriales*, fourth series](https://www.scribd.com/document/904267673/80f16fb30931933321120451e498965f))

- **28 July 1616:** *Carta al Rey sobre la villa de São Paulo, los ataques portugueses y la división del gobierno*. A strategic memorial-letter proposing the depopulation of São Paulo and administrative division as remedies for Portuguese slave-raiding in Guairá. It is a project expressed as an official report; the Crown later divided the government, but did not simply enact every proposal in the text. ([Sposito](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/378694829_Portuguese_Invasions_in_Paraguay-Rio_de_la_Plata_During_the_Iberian_Union_Moving_Between_Local_Conflicts_and_Imperial_Projects1))

- **8 July 1617:** *Carta al Rey sobre el cumplimiento de las ordenanzas de Francisco de Alfaro*. An official implementation report asserting that Alfaro’s ordinances had been enforced. It documents governmental action rather than constituting the ordinances themselves. ([Sušnik’s documentary references](https://es.scribd.com/document/37107588/BRANISLAVA-SUSNIK))

- **13 May 1618:** *Carta al Rey*. A three-folio report preserved in Archivo General de Indias, Audiencia de Charcas, legajo 27, describing church construction and repair, including Hernandarias’s personal expenditure and the labor of enslaved and encomienda workers. The letter’s claims of personal making therefore cannot be converted into sole architectural authorship. ([Torre Revello](https://es.scribd.com/document/430802700/Archivum-02C1-Fray-Juan-Pascual-Rivadeneira-1944))

- **1619:** *Letters and memorials to the King and Council of the Indies*. The Biblioteca Nacional’s fourth series extends through 1619 and preserves additional official reports. They form part of Hernandarias’s documentary corpus, but the accessible edition’s generic rubrics do not justify inventing individual descriptive titles here. ([Biblioteca Nacional, *Cartas y memoriales*, fourth series](https://www.scribd.com/document/904267673/80f16fb30931933321120451e498965f))

- **1600–1625:** *Correspondence and memorials to Philip III and the Council of the Indies*. This is the surviving body of official prose organized as a corpus by Walter Rela, rather than a single book conceived by Hernandarias. It includes letters, reports, recommendations, petitions, certifications, and memorials; secretarial drafting and scribal copies mean that signature, institutional authorship, composition, and handwriting must not be treated as synonymous. ([Rela edition record](https://pmb.parlamento.gub.uy/pmb/opac_css/index.php?id=36078&lvl=notice_display), [Biblioteca Nacional series](https://www.bn.gov.ar/micrositios/admin_assets/issues/files/ac77ac0723f5383a2563085088fe5943.pdf))

### Journeys, surveys, and territorial projects

- **1604:** *Visitation from Buenos Aires toward Asunción*. A governmental journey undertaken with the bishop to inspect cities, settlements, and Indigenous communities. It is better classified as an enacted administrative project than as a authored travel work; its principal surviving manifestation is Hernandarias’s report of 5 April 1604. ([Sposito](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/378694829_Portuguese_Invasions_in_Paraguay-Rio_de_la_Plata_During_the_Iberian_Union_Moving_Between_Local_Conflicts_and_Imperial_Projects1))

- **1607:** *Exploration of the Banda Oriental*. A reconnaissance journey through territory east of the Uruguay River, associated in the literature with the Santa Lucía and Queguay region and with later proposals for livestock settlement. Routes and dates vary in retellings, so it should be understood as an expedition reconstructed from reports, not as a fixed authored object. ([Azarola Gil](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/obra/hernandarias-de-saavedra-y-la-primera-exploracion-del-uruguay/), [Coni’s historical synthesis](https://es.scribd.com/document/341541917/Coni-Emilio-Historia-de-Las-Vaquerias-Del-Rio-de-La-Plata-1555-1750))

- **1615–1617:** *Project to divide the immense Río de la Plata–Paraguay government*. A repeatedly advocated administrative project intended to make government and frontier defense more workable. The royal decree of 16 December 1617 created separate governments, but the institutional outcome was a royal act shaped by several representations, not a work made by Hernandarias alone. ([Rivarola Paoli](https://www.portalguarani.com/1067_juan_bautista_rivarola_paoli__/12693_los_gobiernos_de_hernandarias__por_juan_b_rivarola_paoli.html), [Sposito](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/378694829_Portuguese_Invasions_in_Paraguay-Rio_de_la_Plata_During_the_Iberian_Union_Moving_Between_Local_Conflicts_and_Imperial_Projects1))

### Buildings and infrastructure

- **Before 1616:** *Customs house beneath the Buenos Aires fort*. A large customs building erected on the riverbank under Hernandarias’s direction to centralize unloading and protect royal revenue. The source presents it as something he “had made”; its builders and designer are unnamed, so it is a gubernatorial commission rather than demonstrable personal architectural authorship. ([Biblioteca Nacional, letter of 25 May 1616](https://www.scribd.com/document/904267673/80f16fb30931933321120451e498965f))

- **By 1618:** *Churches in the towns and Indigenous settlements of the province*. Hernandarias claimed responsibility for constructing or reconstructing numerous churches, including the cathedral, through his supervision, property, enslaved workers, encomienda labor, and municipal funds. This is a distributed and coercive building program, not a set of safely attributable individual architectural works; the letter does not provide a complete list or fixed titles. ([Torre Revello](https://es.scribd.com/document/430802700/Archivum-02C1-Fray-Juan-Pascual-Rivadeneira-1944))

- **By 1607:** *Urban building program for Buenos Aires*. Hernandarias compelled holders of vacant solares to build or risk losing them, producing a gradual increase in occupied houses. Because residents and laborers made the individual structures, this is an urban-regulatory project rather than a body of buildings authored by the governor. ([Torre Revello](https://es.scribd.com/document/430802700/Archivum-02C1-Fray-Juan-Pascual-Rivadeneira-1944))

- **After March 1607:** *Defensive tower at the Riachuelo*. A harbor-defense project traditionally attributed to an order by Hernandarias after English corsairs attacked shipping. The surviving summaries establish his commission more readily than the tower’s design, exact completion, or personal authorship, so it remains an attributed governmental work. ([Biographical summary and references](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hernando_Arias_de_Saavedra))

### Settlements, reductions, and colonial landscapes

- **Late sixteenth century–1618:** *Program of Indigenous reductions*. Hernandarias ordered, supported, visited, relocated, or assigned sites for numerous Franciscan and Jesuit reductions across Paraguay, Paraná, and Guairá. It resists being called his “work”: missionaries, Indigenous communities, local officers, and coerced labor created the actual settlements, while Hernandarias supplied governmental authorization and an encomienda-oriented colonial project. ([Biblioteca Nacional, letter of 25 May 1616](https://www.scribd.com/document/904267673/80f16fb30931933321120451e498965f), [Wilde](https://acervo.socioambiental.org/sites/default/files/documents/gml00001.pdf))

- **1609 onward:** *Jesuit mission project in Guairá and adjacent regions*. Hernandarias’s government enabled and promoted Jesuit expansion, but the individual reductions were founded by missionaries and Indigenous participants; Joseph Cataldini and other Jesuits, not Hernandarias, were their immediate founders. Accordingly this is a sponsored colonial project, not a catalogue of settlements authored by him. ([Joseph Cataldini study](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/327289880_Joseph_Cataldini_y_la_fundacion_de_pueblos_en_el_Guayra_por_aproximaciones_sucesivas_hay_que_saber_entenderse), [Wilde](https://acervo.socioambiental.org/sites/default/files/documents/gml00001.pdf))

- **1611:** *First documented release of cattle in the Banda Oriental*. A livestock-establishment project associated with Hernandarias’s islands near the confluence of the Negro and Uruguay rivers. The resulting herds were a biological and ecological manifestation rather than a bounded artifact, and historians dispute how much later Uruguayan cattle descended from this introduction rather than from Jesuit herds farther north. ([Coni](https://es.scribd.com/document/341541917/Coni-Emilio-Historia-de-Las-Vaquerias-Del-Rio-de-La-Plata-1555-1750), [summary of the historiographical dispute](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hernando_Arias_de_Saavedra))

- **1617:** *Second cattle release in the Banda Oriental and on San Gabriel*. A further livestock-establishment action, commonly described as approximately fifty heifers with bulls and another release on San Gabriel. Its long-term role in forming the great eastern cattle range is disputed and cannot be separated from natural reproduction, later introductions, and Jesuit pastoral activity. ([Coni](https://es.scribd.com/document/341541917/Coni-Emilio-Historia-de-Las-Vaquerias-Del-Rio-de-La-Plata-1555-1750), [summary of the historiographical dispute](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hernando_Arias_de_Saavedra))
