## Works by Golda Meir

### Early memoir and political writing

- **1932:** *Untitled contribution signed “G.M.” in The Plough Woman: Records of the Pioneer Women of Palestine*. A short autobiographical-political text published under initials rather than Meir’s full name; a later critical edition attributes it to her, but the original’s partly anonymous presentation makes the attribution less secure than that of her signed writings. ([Brandeis University](https://www.brandeis.edu/tauber/publications/books/raider-plough.html); [University of Haifa discussion of the attribution](https://law.haifa.ac.il/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/lawgov-20251027-golda-trigger.pdf))

- **1932:** *Impressions on Travel in America*. A newspaper article in *Davar* reflecting on women, socialism, and Zionist organization; it survives as an individual periodical text rather than an independent book. ([Golda Meir Institute, citing the original *Davar* publication](https://www.goldameir.org.il/files/homepage/source/Golda_Israel_Studies_Pioneer_Women.pdf))

### Collective manifesto

- **1948:** *Declaration of the Establishment of the State of Israel*. A manifesto in the strict sense—a declaration that performs the political act it announces—which Meir signed as “Golda Myerson” with the other members of the People’s Council. It resists classification as her individual work: she was a signatory and collective maker, not its sole author. ([Knesset](https://main.knesset.gov.il/EN/About/pages/declaration.aspx/about/pages/declaration.aspx))

### Selected writings and sayings

- **1962:** *This Is Our Strength: Selected Papers of Golda Meir*. An editor-shaped obra assembled by Henry M. Christman from Meir’s papers and speeches, with a foreword by Eleanor Roosevelt; it is a selected corpus, not a single newly composed argument. ([Knesset](https://main.knesset.gov.il/About/commemoration/Pages/golda.aspx); [Golda Meir Center for Political Leadership](https://www.msudenver.edu/bibliography/))

- **1970:** *As Good as Golda: The Warmth and Wisdom of Israel’s Prime Minister*. Israel and Mary Shenker’s anthology of brief statements by Meir. Its constituent sayings belong to her spoken and political practice, but the selection, arrangement, and book-object belong substantially to its editors. ([Encyclopaedia Judaica](https://www.rfservicesltd.co.uk/Jewish/Encyclopaedia%20Judaica/encyclopedia%20judaica/Encyclopaedia%20Judaica%20-%20vol%2013%20%28Lif-Mek%29.pdf); [Golda Meir Center for Political Leadership](https://www.msudenver.edu/bibliography/))

### Oral memoirs and autobiographies

- **1972:** *Beit Avi* (*My Father’s House*). An oral-remembrance obra created from Meir’s recorded recollections for an Israeli radio series and then given literary form for publication; the extent of editorial mediation means it is not straightforwardly a manuscript “written by” Meir. ([Knesset](https://main.knesset.gov.il/About/commemoration/Pages/golda.aspx); [Encyclopaedia Judaica](https://www.rfservicesltd.co.uk/Jewish/Encyclopaedia%20Judaica/encyclopedia%20judaica/Encyclopaedia%20Judaica%20-%20vol%2013%20%28Lif-Mek%29.pdf))

- **1973:** *A Land of Our Own: An Oral Autobiography of Golda Meir* / *Golda Meir Speaks Out*. One editor-constructed obra issued under two titles, not two independent works: Marie Syrkin assembled twenty-six speeches, interviews, testimony, informal statements, and articles into an autobiographical sequence. The “oral autobiography” designation is consequently unstable—it is at once documentary compilation, political self-portrait, and editorial creation. ([Golda Meir Center for Political Leadership](https://www.msudenver.edu/bibliography/); [contemporary JTA description](https://www.jta.org/archive/new-autobiography-of-golda-meir); [Google Books bibliographic record](https://books.google.com/books/about/A_Land_of_Our_Own.html?id=0YZtAAAAMAAJ))

- **1972–1975:** *My Life*. Meir’s full autobiography, dictated beginning in 1972 with journalistic assistance and published after her resignation. Sources variously date the first English publication to 1974 and the Hebrew edition to 1975, so a single unqualified year would falsely turn edition history into a composition date. ([Golda Meir Center for Political Leadership](https://www.msudenver.edu/bibliography/); [Knesset](https://main.knesset.gov.il/About/commemoration/Pages/golda.aspx); [Encyclopaedia Judaica](https://www.rfservicesltd.co.uk/Jewish/Encyclopaedia%20Judaica/encyclopedia%20judaica/Encyclopaedia%20Judaica%20-%20vol%2013%20%28Lif-Mek%29.pdf))

### Speeches, statements, and political protocols

- **Before 1948–1978:** *Speeches, interviews, testimony, articles, letters, and official statements*. This is a dispersed body of work rather than a fixed-title obra: no complete critical collected-works edition establishes every item or cleanly separates Meir’s own wording from secretarial drafts, institutional documents, transcripts, and editorial reconstruction. The Knesset identifies selected speeches from 1950, 1961, and 1969, while the published selections above preserve only parts of the larger corpus. ([Knesset](https://main.knesset.gov.il/About/commemoration/Pages/golda.aspx); [Golda Meir Center for Political Leadership](https://www.msudenver.edu/bibliography/))

- **1979:** *Golda Meir: Israel’s Woman of Valor*. A posthumous two-record audio manifestation containing excerpts from twenty-four speeches plus added commentary. Meir made the underlying performances, but not this compilation as a whole; its selection and documentary framing belong to Educational News Service. ([Golda Meir Center for Political Leadership](https://www.msudenver.edu/bibliography/))
