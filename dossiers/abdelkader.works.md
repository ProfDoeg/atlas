## Works by Emir Abdelkader ibn Muhieddine

### Spiritual, philosophical, and polemical works

- **1853–1855?:** *Dhikrā al-ʿāqil wa-tanbīh al-ghāfil* (*Rappel à l’intelligent, avis à l’indifférent*; later translated as *Lettre aux Français*). A philosophical and religious treatise composed during or shortly after Abdelkader’s residence at Bursa; its French titles designate translations and editions, not additional works. The precise date of composition is not securely established, although the authorized French translation appeared in 1858. [Gustave Dugat’s 1858 edition](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k9631437), [BnF data and editions](https://data.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb11887957h)

- **1848–1852?:** *Al-Miqrāḍ al-ḥādd li-qaṭʿ lisān muntaqiṣ dīn al-Islām bi-l-bāṭil wa-l-ilḥād* (*The Sharp Scissors for Cutting the Tongue of Whoever Disparages Islam through Falsehood and Unbelief*). A polemical apologetic treatise, generally assigned to his French captivity; catalogues and studies vary slightly in transcribing its title and do not supply a securely documented completion date. [Institut du monde arabe bibliography](https://www.fichier-pdf.fr/2012/11/22/biblioabdelkaderv-2/biblioabdelkaderv-2.pdf), [study of Abdelkader’s theological writings](https://asjp.cerist.dz/en/article/155281)

- **c. 1856–1883:** *Kitāb al-Mawāqif fī al-waʿẓ wa-l-irshād* (*The Book of Spiritual Halts*). A large, internally numbered body of mystical instruction, Qurʾanic and hadith commentary, visionary disclosure, poetry, and responses to disciples, written down by Abdelkader or transcribed from his oral teaching rather than conceived as a conventionally continuous book. Its textual history is composite and posthumous editions differ; although Michel Chodkiewicz and most modern scholarship accept it as Abdelkader’s principal spiritual obra, a minority polemic has disputed the attribution. [Michel Chodkiewicz edition review](https://www.persee.fr/doc/bcai_0259-7373_1984_num_1_1_873_t1_0344_0000_1), [Itzchak Weismann, study of the *Mawāqif*](https://ibnarabisociety.org/wp-content/uploads/PDFs/jmias-30-weismann.pdf), [record of the attribution dispute](https://www.khaldunalhasani.com/mawduat/emir)

- **Undated:** *Taʿlīqāt ʿalā ḥāshiyat jaddihi al-Sayyid ʿAbd al-Qādir ibn Khudda fī ʿilm al-kalām* (*Annotations on his grandfather ʿAbd al-Qādir ibn Khudda’s gloss on theology*). A set of theological annotations rather than an independent systematic treatise; it is named in biographical inventories, but its date, completeness, and surviving manuscript basis remain inadequately documented. [Study inventorying Abdelkader’s writings](https://asjp.cerist.dz/en/article/180812), [biographical bibliography](https://tarajm.com/people/22237)

- **Undated:** *Jawāb suʾāl ʿan al-rākinīn ilā al-kuffār wa-masāʾil al-jihād* (*Answer to a Question concerning Those Who Rely upon the Unbelievers and Questions of Jihad*). A responsum or fatwa-like text: it answers a particular juridical-political question and therefore resists classification as a freestanding “book.” Its date and textual extent are not firmly established in the accessible scholarly inventories. [Study inventorying Abdelkader’s writings](https://asjp.cerist.dz/en/article/180812)

### Autobiographical manifestation

- **1849:** *Al-Sīra al-dhātiyya* / *Mudhakkirāt al-Amīr ʿAbd al-Qādir* (*Autobiography* or *Memoirs of Emir Abdelkader*). Produced in captivity as answers to questions and in dialogue with his cousin and brother-in-law Muṣṭafā ibn al-Tuhāmī, this is a mediated autobiographical dossier rather than an entirely solitary, finished memoir; part of the manuscript is reportedly in Abdelkader’s hand, while modern French publications have often reproduced or translated only portions. [Account of the manuscript and its collaborative formation](https://fr.slideshare.net/slideshow/lemir-abdelkaderpdf/251751356), [Les Glycines catalogue record](https://www.catalogue-glycines.org/index.php?id=87&l_typdoc=&lvl=indexint_see&nb_per_page_custom=100&nbr_lignes=841&page=2)

### Military protocol and sovereign texts

- **1830s–1840s:** *Wishāḥ al-katāʾib wa-zīnat al-jaysh al-Muḥammadī al-ghālib* (*The Sash of the Battalions and Adornment of the Victorious Muhammadan Army*). A military protocol governing recruitment, ranks, pay, dress, insignia, pensions, discipline, and organization; the transmitted text is associated jointly with Abdelkader, his governing council, and the compiler or secretary Qaddūr ibn Ruwayla, so sole authorship should not be assumed. [1968 scholarly edition record](https://books.google.com/books?id=-4mx22hCK2wC), [catalogue attribution to Qaddūr ibn Ruwayla](https://pmb.univ-batna.dz/opac_flla/index.php?id=11825&lvl=author_see)

- **1832–1847:** *Political and military correspondence*. Hundreds of commands, appointments, diplomatic letters, petitions, replies, and instructions were produced through the chancery of Abdelkader’s emirate. This is an administrative corpus, often dictated, copied, translated, or countersigned by secretaries—not a single work—and no complete critical edition of the correspondence yet fixes its boundaries. [Study of the political letters](https://doi.org/10.31436/jlls.v3i1.40), [Institut du monde arabe bibliography](https://www.fichier-pdf.fr/2012/11/22/biblioabdelkaderv-2/biblioabdelkaderv-2.pdf)

- **1830s–1840s:** *Proclamations, ordinances, safe-conducts, appointments, and seals of the emirate*. These are performative state texts—manifestos and protocols that confer offices, demand obedience, regulate conduct, or guarantee protection—rather than descriptive literature. Their survival is dispersed among archival collections, and neither their total number nor Abdelkader’s degree of personal composition in every document is settled. [BnF/Gallica and Bibliothèque nationale d’Algérie archival survey](https://emir-abdelkader.vercel.app/archives), [study of the political correspondence](https://doi.org/10.31436/jlls.v3i1.40)

- **26 February 1834:** *Treaty of Desmichels*. A bilingual diplomatic protocol made jointly by Abdelkader’s government and General Louis-Alexis Desmichels; variant public and secret texts make it resist the status of a single stable document, and it cannot properly be described as Abdelkader’s sole-authored work. [Bibliographic record for the treaty correspondence and related documents](https://www.fichier-pdf.fr/2012/11/22/biblioabdelkaderv-2/biblioabdelkaderv-2.pdf)

- **30 May 1837:** *Treaty of Tafna*. A political protocol jointly negotiated and ratified by Abdelkader and General Thomas-Robert Bugeaud, defining authority and territorial relations between the emirate and France. It is an enacted agreement, not a literary work, and its “authorship” is necessarily collective and diplomatic. [French National Archives educational dossier](https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/education/colonisation-algerie/traite-tafna.html)

### Poetry

- **1830s–1883:** *Dīwān shiʿr al-Amīr ʿAbd al-Qādir al-Jazāʾirī* (*Poetical corpus of Emir Abdelkader*). A dispersed body of martial, occasional, elegiac, devotional, and mystical poems assembled retrospectively into differently constituted dīwāns; it is a body of work rather than one authorially fixed book, and individual poems do not all carry secure dates. An early printed selection appeared with the military regulations in 1848, while later editors added poems transmitted elsewhere. [BnF record for the 1848 poetry and regulations](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb30000535d), [BnF authority bibliography](https://data.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb11887957h)

- **c. 1856–1883:** *Metaphysical poems incorporated into or associated with the Mawāqif*. These poems are manifestations within Abdelkader’s spiritual teaching and are sometimes republished as though they formed an independent book titled *Poèmes métaphysiques*; that title belongs to a modern selection and translation, not to a distinct authorial collection. [BnF data and editions](https://data.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb11887957h), [Chodkiewicz edition review](https://www.persee.fr/doc/bcai_0259-7373_1984_num_1_1_873_t1_0344_0000_1)

### Collaborative hippological writings

- **1851–1870s:** *Comments and letters incorporated into Eugène Daumas’s Les Chevaux du Sahara et les mœurs du désert*. Abdelkader supplied Arabic letters, observations, corrections, traditions, and a short manuscript study of horses in response to Daumas; the resulting publication is a dialogic, cross-lingual obra whose successive editions progressively absorbed his material, not a book solely authored by either participant. [François Pouillon’s integral edition reviewed in the *Bulletin critique des Annales islamologiques*](https://www.persee.fr/doc/bcai_0259-7373_2010_num_25_1_1036_t4_0091_0000_1), [BnF record describing Abdelkader as collaborator and commentator](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb34966115g)

- **1854:** *Le Cheval barbe*. A French publication of one of Abdelkader’s Arabic responses to Daumas concerning North African horses; it is fundamentally a translated letter or commissioned expert memorandum, not an independently designed French essay. It also entered the expanding textual assemblage of *Les Chevaux du Sahara*. [BnF data and bibliography](https://data.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb11887957h)

- **1855:** *Le Cheval de guerre*. Another translated letter to Daumas, published as an article in the *Revue des Deux Mondes* and later absorbed into the hippological dialogue. Its periodical title gives a discrete manifestation to what remained part of an extended correspondence. [1855 periodical text and bibliographic data](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Le_Cheval_de_guerre), [Pouillon edition review](https://www.persee.fr/doc/bcai_0259-7373_2010_num_25_1_1036_t4_0091_0000_1)

- **1867:** *Le Cheval arabe pur sang: lettre de l’Émir Abd-el-Kader au général Daumas*. A translated epistolary treatise answering Daumas’s questions about bloodlines, breeding, and the judgment of horses. Its separate publication makes it look like a book, but formally it remains one response in their decades-long correspondence. [Library of Congress digitization](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/service/gdc/gdclccn/01/00/62/21/01006221/01006221.pdf), [Persée bibliography](https://www.persee.fr/authority/975714)

- **1850s:** *Answers concerning Arab women and domestic customs*. Responses supplied to Eugène Daumas and subsequently mediated through publications such as *La Femme arabe* and *Mœurs et coutumes de l’Algérie*. These are commissioned ethnographic answers surviving inside another author’s editorial construction; their wording, arrangement, and degree of translation make them resist identification as an autonomous Abdelkader work. [Daumas bibliography](https://www.persee.fr/authority/975714), [Institut du monde arabe bibliography](https://www.fichier-pdf.fr/2012/11/22/biblioabdelkaderv-2/biblioabdelkaderv-2.pdf)

### Letters and occasional texts outside the state corpus

- **1848–1883:** *Private, scholarly, diplomatic, and spiritual correspondence*. Letters to French officials, clerics, scholars, family members, disciples, Ottoman dignitaries, and figures including Eugène Daumas form a large, open corpus. Individual letters are made things, but no reliable collected-works edition yet permits an honest claim to enumerate “every” one; manuscript discoveries and scattered publications continue to alter the corpus. [Institut du monde arabe bibliography](https://www.fichier-pdf.fr/2012/11/22/biblioabdelkaderv-2/biblioabdelkaderv-2.pdf), [archival survey](https://emir-abdelkader.vercel.app/archives)

- **1860 and after:** *Letters and declarations concerning the protection of Damascus’s Christians*. Diplomatic and justificatory texts arising from Abdelkader’s intervention during the 1860 violence; they act as testimony, petition, protection, and political self-account rather than forming a single authorial “book.” Their documentary boundaries depend on the particular archival or edited correspondence consulted. [BnF/Gallica archival survey](https://emir-abdelkader.vercel.app/archives)

### Works sometimes misattributed or requiring exclusion

- **1876:** *ʿIqd al-ajyād fī al-ṣāfināt al-jiyād* and its abridgment *Nukhbat ʿiqd al-ajyād fī al-ṣāfināt al-jiyād*. These hippological works are sometimes placed in lists of Abdelkader’s writings, but catalogue evidence attributes them to his son Muḥammad ibn ʿAbd al-Qādir; they should not be counted as works made by the emir. [WorldCat attribution to Muḥammad Pasha al-Jazāʾirī](https://search.worldcat.org/fr/title/kitab-iqd-al-ajyad-fi-al-safinat-al-jiyad/oclc/82869890), [discussion identifying the son as author](https://ithraeyat.ithra.com/editions/art-of-sports/the-horse-what-poets-have-yet-to-say)

- **Posthumous:** *Écrits spirituels*, *Lettre aux Français*, *Poèmes métaphysiques*, and *Dialogues sur l’hippologie arabe*. These are important modern selections, translations, or editorial reconstructions, not additional works made under those titles by Abdelkader. They manifest portions of the *Mawāqif*, *Dhikrā al-ʿāqil*, the dispersed poetry, and the Daumas correspondence respectively. [BnF authority bibliography](https://data.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb11887957h), [review of *Écrits spirituels*](https://www.persee.fr/doc/bcai_0259-7373_1984_num_1_1_873_t1_0344_0000_1), [review of *Dialogues sur l’hippologie arabe*](https://www.persee.fr/doc/bcai_0259-7373_2010_num_25_1_1036_t4_0091_0000_1)
