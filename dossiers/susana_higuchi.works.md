## Works by Susana Shizuko Higuchi Miyagawa

### Engineering text and educational design

- **1974:** *Construcción de aulas prefabricadas*. Civil-engineering thesis or technical study on prefabricated classrooms, reportedly accompanied by plans later used for post-earthquake school construction. Later profiles call it a published book, but no standard bibliographic record or surviving edition was located, so its form, publication date, and publication status remain uncertain. ([El Comercio](https://elcomercio.pe/archivo-elcomercio/susana-higuchi-ex-primera-dama-de-la-nacion-alberto-fujimori-keiko-fujimori-fuerza-popular-nnsp-noticia/); [Juan Gasparini](https://www.voltairenet.org/Fujimori-por-Susana-Higuchi))

### Buildings and engineering oeuvre

- **From 1974:** *Houses designed, built, and sold through Construcciones Fuji/Fuyi*. This is an architectural and commercial body of work rather than a single text: accounts credit Higuchi with buying sites, designing houses, directing workers, building the dwellings, and selling them. No catalogue raisonné identifies the individual buildings, and even the company’s name varies between “Fuji” and “Fuyi” in the sources; consequently the houses cannot responsibly be separated into invented entries. ([El Comercio](https://elcomercio.pe/archivo-elcomercio/susana-higuchi-ex-primera-dama-de-la-nacion-alberto-fujimori-keiko-fujimori-fuerza-popular-nnsp-noticia/); [Juan Gasparini](https://www.voltairenet.org/Fujimori-por-Susana-Higuchi))

- **1974 or shortly afterward:** *Plans for prefabricated replacement classrooms*. Applied engineering designs prepared after an earthquake damaged school buildings; Alberto Fujimori reportedly supervised construction while Higuchi was entrusted with preparing the plans. The classrooms are collaborative built works, not simply manifestations of her thesis, and the available account supplies neither sites nor a complete inventory. ([Juan Gasparini](https://www.voltairenet.org/Fujimori-por-Susana-Higuchi))

### Institutions and collective projects

- **From 1974:** *Construcciones Fuji/Fuyi*. A family construction enterprise founded by Higuchi to design, build, and sell housing; as an organization it is an institutional creation rather than a discrete building. Sources disagree on the spelling of its name and do not provide a formal incorporation date beyond associating its creation with the period following her 1974 marriage. ([El Comercio](https://elcomercio.pe/archivo-elcomercio/susana-higuchi-ex-primera-dama-de-la-nacion-alberto-fujimori-keiko-fujimori-fuerza-popular-nnsp-noticia/); [Juan Gasparini](https://www.voltairenet.org/Fujimori-por-Susana-Higuchi))

- **Date not securely established:** *Academia preuniversitaria Wisconsin*. A jointly founded educational enterprise intended to prepare secondary-school graduates for university admission. It resists attribution as Higuchi’s sole obra: accounts describe it as a creation of the couple or a Fujimori–Higuchi family business. ([El Comercio](https://elcomercio.pe/archivo-elcomercio/susana-higuchi-ex-primera-dama-de-la-nacion-alberto-fujimori-keiko-fujimori-fuerza-popular-nnsp-noticia/); [Juan Gasparini](https://www.voltairenet.org/Fujimori-por-Susana-Higuchi))

- **1994:** *Movimiento Armonía Siglo XXI*. Political organization led by Higuchi and used for her attempted presidential and congressional candidacies. This was a collective political project, not a text, and its electoral lists were rejected as incomplete; a later municipal-registration effort also failed to authenticate enough adherents. ([El Comercio](https://elcomercio.pe/archivo-elcomercio/susana-higuchi-ex-primera-dama-de-la-nacion-alberto-fujimori-keiko-fujimori-fuerza-popular-nnsp-noticia/))

### Legislative protocols and projects

- **2000-07-28:** *Proyecto de Ley 00029/2000-CR: Deroga las resoluciones del Congreso 002, 003 y 004-97-CR y restituye a tres magistrados del Tribunal Constitucional*. A jointly authored legislative protocol that became Resolución Legislativa del Congreso 007-2000-CR; Higuchi was one of eight named authors, not its sole maker. ([Congressional record](https://www2.congreso.gob.pe/Sicr/TraDocEstProc/CLProLey2000.nsf/24ee362deedc4ddf05256dde007a4759/14d828f2248d72c105256ce1006d5595))

- **2000:** *Proyecto de Ley 1216/2000-CR: derogación de la Ley 26960*. A proposal attributed to Higuchi to repeal the law regulating the status of Peruvian National Police health-service personnel; the consulted committee record establishes its existence but not its complete subsequent history. ([Congressional committee record](https://www2.congreso.gob.pe/sicr/ApoyComisiones/comision2000.nsf/6415679e0018be8c05256a770079e3bb/b8c4d3d6acd1d36c052569dd0004a83f?Click=&OpenDocument=))

- **2001-09-11:** *Proyecto de Ley 00651/2001-CR: Ley que concede amnistía a miembros de las rondas campesinas y/o comunales procesados por tenencia ilegal de armas y secuestro*. A multipartite legislative project bearing Higuchi’s signature; it became Ley 27599, but collective sponsorship prevents treating the enacted law as hers alone. ([Congressional compilation](https://leyes.congreso.gob.pe/Documentos/2016_2021/Proyectos_de_Ley_y_de_Resoluciones_Legislativas/Proyectos-Terrorismo-2001-2006.pdf))

- **2002:** *Proyecto de Ley 4695/2002-CR: regulación de la producción, comercialización e industrialización de la hoja de coca y sus derivados*. A proposed statutory protocol attributed to Higuchi in the congressional coca-law dossier; it remained one component of a much larger, collectively processed legislative corpus. ([Project text](https://www2.congreso.gob.pe/sicr/tradocestproc/clproley2001.nsf/pley/956D5841AE09733C05256D25005CD4F1); [committee dossier](https://www2.congreso.gob.pe/Sicr/ApoyComisiones/Informesgestion.nsf/AplenoNS/ABE6DD85F409C1AC0525704B008145A5/%24FILE/MEMORIA_2005_TRABAJO_DESARROLLO_ALTERNATIVO.pdf))

- **2002-12-17:** *Proyecto de Ley 4916/2002-CR: declaración del mayor EP Luis Alberto García Rojas como héroe nacional*. A commemorative legislative project authored by Higuchi concerning an officer killed during the 1995 Peru–Ecuador conflict; the Culture Commission declined jurisdiction, so it did not become the proposed legal manifestation there. ([Commission catalogue](https://www.congreso.gob.pe/Docs/congresistas2001/ElviradelaPuente/files/memorias/memoria_presidentacomisionculturapatrim.pdf))

- **2002-12-17:** *Proyecto de Ley 05052/2002-CR: Ley de Promoción de la Estimulación Prenatal y Temprana*. A collectively authored protocol signed by Higuchi and eight other congresswomen; it became Ley 28124. The resulting law is therefore not attributable to Higuchi alone. ([Congressional record](https://www2.congreso.gob.pe/Sicr/TraDocEstProc/CLProLey2001.nsf/5bdfb910c0a2413205256d260054118b/1a4187727b41920005256d24007aa6c8))

- **2002:** *Proyecto de Ley 5966/2002-CR concerning coca*. A project attributed to Higuchi in the congressional dossier for the proposed framework law on coca. Its title is not stated in the consulted catalogue excerpt, so no fuller title is supplied here. ([Committee dossier](https://www2.congreso.gob.pe/Sicr/ApoyComisiones/Informesgestion.nsf/AplenoNS/ABE6DD85F409C1AC0525704B008145A5/%24FILE/MEMORIA_2005_TRABAJO_DESARROLLO_ALTERNATIVO.pdf))

- **2003-04-03:** *Incorporación de la caña de azúcar como cultivo alternativo a la coca para producción de etanol*. A legislative project coauthored with Tito Chocano Olivera, combining an agricultural-development proposal with instructions for compulsory fuel blending and regional processing centres. ([Congressional project text](https://www2.congreso.gob.pe/sicr/tradocestproc/clproley2001.nsf/0/6ec5a7d86a19a02105256d25005c4efb?Click=&OpenDocument=))

- **2003-10:** *Ley que adscribe la Empresa Nacional de la Coca Sociedad Anónima a la Presidencia del Consejo de Ministros*. Higuchi’s proposed one-article institutional protocol for moving ENACO S.A. under the Presidency of the Council of Ministers; the record consulted establishes the proposal but not its enactment. ([Congressional project text](https://www2.congreso.gob.pe/sicr/tradocestproc/clproley2001.nsf/pley/258EE9104EC48D5F05256DB7005B089C))

- **2003-10-21:** *Proyecto de Ley 08661/2003-CR: modificación de la Ley del Proceso Extrajudicial de Conciliación*. A sole-authored proposal to amend articles 6, 22, and 24 of Ley 26872; it was absorbed into a group of many initiatives that became Ley 28163, so that law cannot be equated with Higuchi’s original text. ([Congressional record](https://www2.congreso.gob.pe/sicr/tradocestproc/clproley2001.nsf/fa08063cac51e9d805256cdf006c92be/fdec950ea08f6d0805256dc60081fb19?Click=&OpenDocument=))

- **2003:** *Proyecto de Ley 9225/2003-CR concerning controlled chemical inputs and products*. A legislative proposal by Higuchi subsequently considered together with three other projects in a consolidated committee opinion; the final collective text is not solely her obra. ([Congressional committee opinion](https://www2.congreso.gob.pe/Sicr/ApoyComisiones/Dictamenes0106.nsf/DictamenesFuturo/238EDEB36CA2222E0525700D005A8DD1/%24FILE/8516PY.pdf))

- **2004-01:** *Ley de reforma constitucional que modifica el artículo 179 de la Constitución Política del Perú*. A constitutional project by Higuchi seeking to alter the composition of the Jurado Nacional de Elecciones. Its status in the consulted record is that of a proposal, not an enacted constitutional amendment. ([Congressional project text](https://www2.congreso.gob.pe/sicr/tradocestproc/clproley2001.nsf/pley/B56D452A13D9ADDA05256E2F00720EC1))

- **2004-03-03:** *Proyecto de Ley 09902/2003-CR: derecho de voto de los miembros en actividad de la Policía Nacional y las Fuerzas Armadas*. Higuchi’s constitutional-reform project was grouped with ten initiatives and contributed to Ley 28480; because the enacted amendment is composite, it should not be credited to her exclusively. ([Congressional record](https://www2.congreso.gob.pe/sicr/tradocestproc/clproley2001.nsf/0/51a66bf49c8b69eb05256e4c007cbf24?Click=&OpenDocument=))

- **2004:** *Ley de creación del impuesto a las ganancias inesperadas de las empresas que explotan el oro y el cobre nacional*. A fiscal legislative project intended to tax windfall gains in gold and copper extraction. The congressional text identifies Higuchi as proposer, but the consulted record does not show enactment. ([Congressional project text](https://www2.congreso.gob.pe/sicr/tradocestproc/clproley2001.nsf/0/b8d500833f56bb8505256e4d0052373b?Click=&OpenDocument=))

- **2005-05-10:** *Proyecto de Ley 12950/2004-CR: modificación de los artículos 16, 18, 34, 35, 36 y 76 del Reglamento del Congreso*. A proposed protocol concerning parliamentary immunity, ordinary commissions, and legislative initiatives; the congressional index attributes it to Higuchi and records it as remaining in committee. ([Congressional index](https://www2.congreso.gob.pe/Sicr/TraDocEstProc/InfSiste_2013.nsf/vf07web/F07F6274B135A9DC05257CF30054FAA6?opendocument=))

- **2005-11-02:** *Proyecto de Ley 13989/2005-CR: reforma de la Ley para la Educación Bilingüe Intercultural*. A sole-authored project recognizing cultural diversity and Indigenous languages and adding provisions to Ley 27818; the record ends with its referral to two commissions, not enactment. ([Congressional record](https://www2.congreso.gob.pe/Sicr/TraDocEstProc/CLProLey2001.nsf/Ambienteecologia/E6316AD5AF776914052570AD0082D4DB))

- **2005-11:** *Proyecto de Ley 14107/2005-CR: Ley de fiscalización de la exportación de la hoja de coca como insumo para bebidas de consumo masivo*. A proposed regulatory protocol for exports of coca leaves used in mass-market beverages. ([Congressional project](https://www2.congreso.gob.pe/Sicr/TraDocEstProc/CLProLey2001.nsf/PorLey/A3FD8EBC45C8A675052570C1006C18DE/%24FILE/14107.PDF))

- **2006-02:** *Proyecto de Ley 14419/2005-CR: Ley que incluye al Perú dentro de la red de computadoras portátiles de bajo costo para escolares de bajos recursos económicos*. A project seeking participation in the low-cost educational-laptop initiative associated with MIT; it is a programmatic protocol, not a computer or educational programme actually made by Higuchi. ([Congressional project](https://www2.congreso.gob.pe/Sicr/TraDocEstProc/CLProLey2001.nsf/PorLey/57E760A7CBD5A79C05257123007C08CE/%24FILE/14419.PDF))

- **2006-03:** *Proyecto de Ley 14461/2005-CR: Ley que establece la enseñanza y promoción de las MYPES y PYMES en la educación básica y superior*. A proposed educational protocol introducing instruction about micro- and small enterprises. ([Congressional project](https://www2.congreso.gob.pe/Sicr/TraDocEstProc/CLProLey2001.nsf/PorLey/0C5E81E7F8B3A86405257130004E27F5/%24FILE/14461.PDF))

- **2006-03-10:** *Proyecto de Ley 14462/2005-CR: Ley de promoción municipal al tratamiento y rehabilitación por el uso indebido de drogas*. A municipal-policy protocol authored by Higuchi and referred for committee study. ([Project text](https://www2.congreso.gob.pe/Sicr/TraDocEstProc/CLProLey2001.nsf/PorLey/679E347D5508795B05257130004E2FE3/%24FILE/14462.PDF); [committee minutes](https://www2.congreso.gob.pe/Sicr/ApoyComisiones/comision2005.nsf/1ActasComisiones/5BD63ACA0021763A0525716B007D4AEE/%24FILE/ACTADEFNAC22-03-2006.pdf))

- **2006:** *Proyecto de Ley 14614/2005-CR: Ley que declara de necesidad pública las expropiaciones de inmuebles afectados por proyectos viales y faculta a las municipalidades a realizarlas*. A proposed enabling protocol for urban-road expropriations; it remained a project rather than a built road or executed expropriation. ([Congressional project](https://www2.congreso.gob.pe/Sicr/TraDocEstProc/CLProLey2001.nsf/PorLey/12D8832A09AFCF9305256D007AA08E/%24FILE/14614.PDF))

### Testimony, denunciation, and unfinished writing

- **1992-03-24 onward:** *Public denunciations concerning the diversion of Japanese donations and corruption within the Fujimori government*. These statements acted politically and therefore have something of the manifesto, but they resist treatment as a stable authored work: they survive dispersed across press conferences, interviews, complaints, and later testimony rather than as one fixed text. ([El Comercio](https://elcomercio.pe/archivo-elcomercio/susana-higuchi-ex-primera-dama-de-la-nacion-alberto-fujimori-keiko-fujimori-fuerza-popular-nnsp-noticia/))

- **2002-02-26 and 2002-03-05:** *Testimony before the congressional investigating commission*. Recorded oral testimony concerning her alleged abduction, torture, and the Fujimori government, later transcribed into the commission’s documentation. It is a documentary manifestation created through examination and transcription rather than an independently composed publication. ([Congressional investigation report](https://www2.congreso.gob.pe/sicr/apoycomisiones/informes.nsf/0F1683791EB39A2005256AFF00789023/63C701EA8386E5B705256C09007365BB); [declarations index](https://www.congreso.gob.pe/Docs/sites/webs/CICCOR/infofinal/Declaraciones.pdf))

- **Announced by 2021; completion unverified:** *Autobiography covering her life in the Government Palace and her 1994 political rupture*. Higuchi said that she would prepare this book, but no completed manuscript or publication was located. It therefore belongs here as an unfinished or unrealized project, not as a book presumed to exist. ([El Comercio](https://elcomercio.pe/archivo-elcomercio/susana-higuchi-ex-primera-dama-de-la-nacion-alberto-fujimori-keiko-fujimori-fuerza-popular-nnsp-noticia/))
