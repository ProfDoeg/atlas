## Works by Osvaldo Pedro Pugliese

### Tangos composed or co-composed

- **1922:** *El frenopático*. Tango; the date appears in LetrasTango, while Todo Tango catalogues it without a date. ([LetrasTango](https://letrastango.com/artistas/osvaldo-pugliese/), [Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- **1922:** *Primera categoría*. Tango; as with *El frenopático*, the two specialist catalogues differ only in whether they supply the year. ([LetrasTango](https://letrastango.com/artistas/osvaldo-pugliese/), [Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- **1924:** *Recuerdo*. Tango composition, later supplied with words by Eduardo Moreno. Its early published and recorded editions credited Pugliese’s father, Adolfo: sources explain that Osvaldo, then legally a minor, was the actual composer, making the historical labels an attribution problem rather than evidence of paternal authorship. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/historias/cronica/73/Recuerdo-El-tango-Recuerdo-un-secreto-de-familia/), [LetrasTango](https://letrastango.com/artistas/osvaldo-pugliese/))
- **1931:** *Ausencia (Tango mío)*. Tango composition. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- **1941:** *Adiós Bardi*. Instrumental tango commemorating Agustín Bardi; Todo Tango documents Pugliese’s 17 October 1944 recording, while LetrasTango dates the composition to 1941. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese), [LetrasTango](https://letrastango.com/artistas/osvaldo-pugliese/))
- **1942:** *El encopao*. Tango, subsequently manifested both as a song and in instrumental orchestral performances. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- **1943:** *La beba*. Instrumental tango named for Pugliese’s daughter, the pianist Beba Pugliese. ([LetrasTango](https://letrastango.com/artistas/osvaldo-pugliese/), [Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- **1943:** *Recién*. Tango song: Pugliese composed the music and Homero Manzi supplied the words. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese), [LetrasTango](https://letrastango.com/artistas/osvaldo-pugliese/))
- **1946:** *Igual que una sombra*. Tango song, a collaborative made thing rather than a solely authored text: Pugliese composed its music. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- **1946:** *La yumba*. Instrumental tango and Pugliese’s defining rhythmic composition; the title names an onomatopoeic orchestral effect rather than a narrative text. Its first documented Pugliese recording was made on 21 August 1946. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese), [LetrasTango](https://letrastango.com/artistas/osvaldo-pugliese/))
- **1946:** *Una vez*. Tango song with music by Pugliese; its words are a separate collaborative contribution. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- **1959:** *Llegaste con tus alegrías*. Tango composition. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- **1961:** *Muchachita del amanecer*. Tango composition; the catalogue’s work date is 1961, although it also reports a 1960 recording, a chronology that should not be silently harmonized. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese), [LetrasTango](https://letrastango.com/artistas/osvaldo-pugliese/))
- **1963:** *Cardo y malvón*. Tango; LetrasTango dates the work to 1963 but reports a 1962 recording, another unresolved work-date/recording-date discrepancy. ([LetrasTango](https://letrastango.com/artistas/osvaldo-pugliese/))
- **1976:** *Yo payador*. Tango composition. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- **1980:** *Viejo barrio poligrillo*. Tango composition. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- **1981:** *Sentir que estamos juntos*. Tango composition. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- **1982:** *Hermosa japonesita*. Tango composition connected to Pugliese’s long Japanese reception. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- **1988:** *Se viene el dos mil*. Tango composition whose title projects toward the approaching millennium. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- **1989:** *Hoy por hoy*. Tango composition. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- *A los artistas plásticos*. Instrumental tango; Todo Tango documents an orchestral recording made in 1964 but does not give a secure composition date. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- *Andrés Selpa*. Tango composition bearing the name of the Argentine boxer. No fixed work date is supplied by the consulted specialist catalogue. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- *Barro*. Tango song with music by Pugliese; the catalogue documents recordings from 1951 and 1952 but does not securely date the composition itself. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese), [LetrasTango](https://letrastango.com/artistas/osvaldo-pugliese/))
- *Cardo y malvón*. Tango composition; see the dated entry above for the catalogue’s chronological inconsistency. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- *Corazoneando*. Instrumental tango; its title is sometimes encountered through the 1958 recording rather than as an independently dated score. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- *Don Atilio*. Instrumental tango, recorded by Pugliese’s orchestra in 1951; that recording year should not automatically be treated as the composition date. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- *El japanga*. Tango composition, documented in Pugliese’s recorded repertory in 1978 but not securely dated as a work. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- *Las marionetas*. Instrumental tango; Pugliese’s orchestra recorded it on 6 November 1945, but Todo Tango does not assert that this was its composition date. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- *Madrugados bien temprano*. Instrumental tango, documented in a 27 December 1979 orchestral recording; its date as a composition remains unstated. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- *Malandraca*. Instrumental tango and one of the principal pieces of Pugliese’s mature orchestral language; Todo Tango documents its 1949 recording without fixing a separate work date. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese), [LetrasTango](https://letrastango.com/artistas/osvaldo-pugliese/))
- *Negracha*. Instrumental tango, first documented in Pugliese’s discography in 1948; the catalogues do not consistently assign the composition itself a year. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese), [LetrasTango](https://letrastango.com/artistas/osvaldo-pugliese/))
- *No juegues a la guerra*. Tango song with music by Pugliese; no secure composition date is supplied. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- *Pa’ los médicos*. Tango in the long tradition of medically themed Argentine compositions; Todo Tango documents Pugliese’s 1982 recording but leaves the work undated. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- *Para Eduardo Arolas*. Instrumental commemorative tango addressed to composer and bandoneonist Eduardo Arolas. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- *Protocoleando*. Instrumental tango; documented in Pugliese’s 1985 recorded repertory, with no separate composition date established by the catalogue. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- *Retoños*. Tango composition without a securely catalogued date. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))

### Valses composed or co-composed

- *Marga (Un pensamiento)*. Vals composition; the alternative title belongs to the catalogued work rather than to a separate piece. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- *Navidad*. Vals; Todo Tango marks the title with a disambiguating “[b],” indicating that its catalogue distinguishes it from another work of the same name. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- *Noche de mayo*. Vals composition without a securely supplied date. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))

### Milongas and candombe-derived compositions

- **1961:** *Milonga para Fidel*. Political milonga addressed to Fidel Castro; it is a musical composition, not a prose manifesto, although its dedication gives it an overtly declarative public function. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- *El negro cambambá*. Milonga-candombe: a hybrid genre object rather than a tango narrowly defined. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- *La vaquita*. Milonga composition; Pugliese’s orchestra recorded it with Abel Córdoba on 22 December 1972, but the composition date is not independently established. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))

### Composite manifestation

- **1989:** *La yumba/Adiós Nonino*. Live orchestral conjunction of Pugliese’s *La yumba* and Astor Piazzolla’s *Adiós Nonino*, performed in Amsterdam on 26 June 1989. It resists classification as a new Pugliese composition: it is better understood as a collaborative concert manifestation or two-author composite arrangement. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))

### Collective project and recorded body of work

- **1929–1931:** *Sexteto Vardaro-Pugliese*. A performing project co-led with Elvino Vardaro, not a fixed musical text; its changing personnel and subsequent transformation into the Pugliese-Gobbi ensemble make the project itself the work-like object. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/historias/cronica/435/Orquesta-Tipica-Osvaldo-Pugliese/))
- **1931:** *Quinteto/‌Sexteto Pugliese-Gobbi*. A collaborative ensemble project with Alfredo Gobbi whose identity changed with its instrumentation and personnel; it therefore resists treatment as a single authored “work.” ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/historias/cronica/435/Orquesta-Tipica-Osvaldo-Pugliese/))
- **1936–1937:** *Early orchestras directed by Osvaldo Pugliese*. Two short-lived attempts at establishing his own orchestra. These are projects and collective manifestations rather than compositions, and Todo Tango documents changing line-ups in the two years. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/historias/cronica/435/Orquesta-Tipica-Osvaldo-Pugliese/))
- **1939–1995:** *Orquesta Típica Osvaldo Pugliese*. A durable collective project—arguably Pugliese’s principal Gesamtkunstwerk—combining composition, arrangement, interpretation, performance, recording and a cooperative organization of labour. Founded with its 11 August 1939 debut, it cannot accurately be reduced either to Pugliese’s individual authorship or to a list of scores. ([Todo Tango](https://www.todotango.com/historias/cronica/435/Orquesta-Tipica-Osvaldo-Pugliese/), [LetrasTango](https://letrastango.com/artistas/osvaldo-pugliese/))
- **1943–1989:** *Commercial recording corpus of Osvaldo Pugliese and his orchestra*. A body of recorded manifestations—performances of Pugliese’s compositions and hundreds of works by others—rather than 474 newly composed works. Tango en Vinilo catalogues 474 recording records across this period, while LetrasTango presently links Pugliese as performer to 328 works; the incompatible counts reflect different units and coverage, so neither should be silently presented as an uncontested total of distinct performances. ([Tango en Vinilo](https://tangoenvinilo.com/tangos.php?director=Osvaldo+Pugliese), [LetrasTango](https://letrastango.com/artistas/osvaldo-pugliese/))
- **1985:** *Osvaldo Pugliese y su Orquesta en el Teatro Colón*. Live concert manifestation of 26 December 1985, subsequently issued as recordings; it is a performed event and recorded document, not a new composition. ([Todo Tango discography](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
- **1989:** *Pugliese–Piazzolla concert at the Royal Carré Theatre, Amsterdam*. Live collaborative manifestation of 26 June 1989, including the composite *La yumba/Adiós Nonino*. Its authorship is necessarily distributed among composers, arrangers and performers. ([Todo Tango discography](https://www.todotango.com/creadores/ficha/28/Osvaldo-Pugliese))
