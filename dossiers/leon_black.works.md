## Works by Leon David Black

### Financial institutions

- **1990:** *Apollo Management, L.P.* An investment-management enterprise founded by Black and subsequently developed with Marc Rowan, Joshua Harris, and other former Drexel colleagues; later corporate forms use the name Apollo Global Management. Apollo and its SEC filings date the institution to 1990, although some secondary accounts give 1991, apparently reflecting the commencement of investment activity rather than legal formation. [Apollo history](https://www.apollo.com/institutional/aboutus/apollo-history); [Apollo 2020 proxy statement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1411494/000119312520225055/d23115ddef14a.htm)

- **1990:** *Lion Advisors, L.P.* An investment-management enterprise founded by Black alongside Apollo Management to manage institutional capital, initially emphasizing distressed securities, corporate restructurings, leveraged buyouts, and minority investments. It is an organization rather than a personally authored text or discrete artwork. [Apollo 2020 proxy statement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1411494/000119312520225055/d23115ddef14a.htm); [Apollo 2019 annual report](https://www.annualreports.com/HostedData/AnnualReportArchive/a/NYSE_APO_2019.pdf)

### Philanthropic institutions

- **1997–1998:** *Debra and Leon Black Family Foundation*. A private philanthropic institution made jointly with Debra Black, not an individually authored obra: accounts place its establishment in 1997, while the IRS record gives an August 1998 recognition date. Its early governance is also historically contested in the public record because Jeffrey Epstein was an initial director and later filings reportedly continued to name him after his asserted 2007 departure. [ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer record](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/133947890); [foundation profile and source notes](https://theinfoweb.org/organizations/black-family-foundation/)

- **2007–:** *Melanoma Research Alliance*. A continuing research-funding project and nonprofit institution co-founded by Leon and Debra Black under the auspices of the Milken Institute after Debra Black’s melanoma diagnosis. Its evolving grant program is the work; it resists classification as a single finished object. [Melanoma Research Alliance biography of Leon Black](https://www.curemelanoma.org/mra-overview/board-of-directors/leon-black); [Health Research Alliance institutional history](https://www.healthra.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/Marc_Hurlbert_Alexandria_2019_Presentation.pdf)

### Commissioning projects—not works authored by Black

- **2012:** *Dartmouth Panels*. A site-specific, five-panel sculpture that Leon and Debra Black commissioned from Ellsworth Kelly for Dartmouth College. Black identified the site and invited Kelly to devise the work, but the sculpture’s artistic authorship belongs to Kelly; it therefore resists inclusion as an obra “by” Black and is recorded here only as a patron-led project. [Hood Museum, *Quarterly*, Winter 2013](https://hoodmuseum.dartmouth.edu/sites/hoodmuseum.prod/files/hoodmuseum/publications/2013winterquarterlywebready.pdf); [Dartmouth dedication notice](https://campus-services.dartmouth.edu/news/2012/09/dartmouth-dedicate-new-black-family-visual-arts-center-september-14)

### Scope and attribution

- **No fixed date:** *No documented authored oeuvre*. Black is principally documented as a financier, institutional founder, philanthropist, collector, and patron; no standard scholarly bibliography, catalogue raisonné, collected-works edition, or discography for him was located. Consequently, acquisitions, investments, board service, donations, buildings merely bearing the family name, and works made by commissioned artists have not been silently converted into works “by” Black.
