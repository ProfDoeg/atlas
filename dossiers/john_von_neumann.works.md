## Works by John von Neumann

### Books and book-length projects

- **1932:** *Mathematische Grundlagen der Quantenmechanik*. A monograph establishing a Hilbert-space formulation of quantum mechanics; the 1955 English translation was reviewed and approved by von Neumann. [Taub I; MacTutor]
- **1935–1937:** *Continuous Geometry*. Multigraphed lecture notes later edited as a book; Part III remained unfinished, so this is both an obra and an incomplete project. [Taub IV; MacTutor]
- **1937:** *Continuous Geometries with a Transition Probability*. A manuscript surviving in a third draft and published only in 1981 with Israel Halperin’s editorial apparatus; its posthumous form resists treatment as a wholly finished book. [Beebe; MacTutor]
- **1940–1941:** *Invariant Measures*. Institute for Advanced Study lectures recorded principally by Paul Halmos, reviewed and partly written by von Neumann, but not formally published until 1999; authorship belongs jointly to lecture, transcription, and revision. [MacTutor]
- **1944:** *Theory of Games and Economic Behavior*. A book-length mathematical and economic obra coauthored with Oskar Morgenstern; revised editions appeared during von Neumann’s lifetime. [Taub VI; Beebe]
- **1950:** *Functional Operators, Volume I: Measures and Integrals*. A monograph developed from von Neumann’s functional-analysis work. [MacTutor]
- **1951:** *Functional Operators, Volume II: The Geometry of Orthogonal Spaces*. A companion monograph on Hilbert-space geometry. [MacTutor]
- **1952–1953:** *The Theory of Automata: Construction, Reproduction, Homogeneity*. An unfinished manuscript project describing a cellular universal constructor; Arthur W. Burks reconstructed and edited it for *Theory of Self-Reproducing Automata* (1966). [MacTutor]
- **1955–1956:** *The Computer and the Brain*. An unfinished Silliman Lectures manuscript written during von Neumann’s final illness and published posthumously in 1958; it is a project rather than a delivered lecture series. [MacTutor]
- **1949–1953:** *Theory and Organization of Complicated Automata*. Five 1949 Illinois lectures, preserved through notes and posthumous editing as Part I of *Theory of Self-Reproducing Automata*; the published text is not simply an author-finished manuscript. [MacTutor]

### Logic, set theory, and foundations

- **1922:** *Über die Lage der Nullstellen gewisser Minimumpolynome*. A mathematical paper coauthored with Michael Fekete. [Taub I; Beebe]
- **1923:** *Zur Einführung der transfiniten Zahlen*. A research paper on transfinite numbers. [Taub I]
- **1925:** *Eine Axiomatisierung der Mengenlehre*. A research paper proposing an axiomatization of set theory. [Taub I]
- **1925:** *Egyenletesen sűrű számsorozatok*. A Hungarian-language mathematical paper on uniformly dense number sequences. [Taub I; Beebe]
- **1926:** *Zur Prüferschen Theorie der idealen Zahlen*. A paper on Prüfer’s theory of ideal numbers. [Taub I]
- **1927:** *Zur Hilbertschen Beweistheorie*. A foundational paper on Hilbert’s proof theory. [Taub I]
- **1928:** *Die Zerlegung eines Intervalles in abzählbar viele kongruente Teilmengen*. A set-theoretic paper on partitioning an interval into countably many congruent subsets. [Taub I]
- **1928:** *Ein System algebraisch unabhängiger Zahlen*. A short mathematical paper constructing algebraically independent numbers. [Taub I]
- **1928:** *Über die Definition durch transfinite Induktion und verwandte Fragen der allgemeinen Mengenlehre*. A foundational paper on transfinite definition. [Taub I]
- **1928:** *Die Axiomatisierung der Mengenlehre*. A further systematic treatment of axiomatic set theory. [Taub I]
- **1929:** *Über eine Widerspruchfreiheitsfrage der axiomatischen Mengenlehre*. A paper addressing a consistency question in axiomatic set theory. [Taub I]
- *Bemerkungen zu den Ausführungen von Herrn S. Leśniewski über meine Arbeit „Zur Hilbertschen Beweistheorie“*. A published reply or corrective note rather than a freestanding theory. [Taub II]
- *Die formalistische Grundlegung der Mathematik*. A foundational essay on the formalist basis of mathematics. [Taub II]
- *On Some Analytic Sets Defined by Transfinite Induction*. A research paper connecting analysis and transfinite construction. [Taub IV]

### Quantum mechanics and mathematical physics

- **1927:** *Über die Grundlagen der Quantenmechanik*. A research paper on the foundations of quantum mechanics. [Taub I]
- **1927:** *Zur Theorie der Darstellungen kontinuierlicher Gruppen*. A paper on representations of continuous groups. [Taub I]
- **1927:** *Mathematische Begründung der Quantenmechanik*. A mathematical formulation of quantum mechanics coauthored with David Hilbert and Lothar Nordheim. [Taub I]
- **1927:** *Wahrscheinlichkeitstheoretischer Aufbau der Quantenmechanik*. A paper giving a probability-theoretic construction of quantum mechanics. [Taub I]
- **1927:** *Thermodynamik quantenmechanischer Gesamtheiten*. A paper on the thermodynamics of quantum ensembles. [Taub I]
- *Einige Bemerkungen zur Diracschen Theorie des Drehelektrons*. A paper commenting on Dirac’s theory of the spinning electron. [Taub I]
- *Zur Erklärung einiger Eigenschaften der Spektren aus der Quantenmechanik des Drehelektrons, I*. The first paper in a three-part investigation of spectral properties. [Taub I]
- *Zur Erklärung einiger Eigenschaften der Spektren aus der Quantenmechanik des Drehelektrons, II*. The second paper in that investigation. [Taub I]
- *Zur Erklärung einiger Eigenschaften der Spektren aus der Quantenmechanik des Drehelektrons, III*. The third paper in that investigation. [Taub I]
- *Über merkwürdige diskrete Eigenwerte*. A mathematical-physics paper on unusual discrete eigenvalues, associated with Eugene Wigner. [Taub I]
- *Über das Verhalten von Eigenwerten bei adiabatischen Prozessen*. A paper on eigenvalues under adiabatic processes. [Taub I]
- *Beweis des Ergodensatzes und des H-Theorems in der neuen Mechanik*. A paper applying ergodic reasoning to quantum mechanics. [Taub I]
- *A koordináta-mérés pontosságának határai az elektron Dirac-féle elméletében*. A Hungarian-language paper on limits to coordinate-measurement accuracy in Dirac’s electron theory. [Taub II]
- **1934:** *On an Algebraic Generalization of the Quantum Mechanical Formalism*. A joint paper with Pascual Jordan and Eugene Wigner. [Taub II]
- **1936:** *On an Algebraic Generalization of the Quantum Mechanical Formalism (Part I)*. A separate, expanded paper by von Neumann; its title overlaps the 1934 collaboration but it is a distinct object. [Taub III]
- **1936:** *The Logic of Quantum Mechanics*. A paper coauthored with Garrett Birkhoff, proposing a nonclassical logical structure for quantum propositions. [Taub IV]
- *Quantum Logics (Strict- and Probability-Logics)*. An unpublished or incompletely published manuscript represented in the collected works through editorial description; it resists classification as a finished paper. [Taub IV]
- *The Dirac Equation in Projective Relativity*. A mathematical-physics paper. [Taub II]
- **1940:** *Discussion of De Sitter’s Space and of Dirac’s Equation in It*. An unpublished manuscript later reviewed in the collected works. [Taub VI; Beebe]
- **1954:** *Significance of Loewner’s Theorem in the Quantum Theory of Collisions*. A joint paper with Eugene Wigner. [Taub IV]

### Operators, measure, ergodic theory, and groups

- **1929:** *Allgemeine Eigenwerttheorie Hermitescher Funktionaloperatoren*. A major paper on the spectral theory of Hermitian operators. [Taub II]
- *Zur Algebra der Funktionaloperatoren und Theorie der normalen Operatoren*. A research paper on operator algebra and normal operators. [Taub II]
- *Zur Theorie der unbeschränkten Matrizen*. A paper on unbounded matrices. [Taub II]
- *Über einen Hilfssatz der Variationsrechnung*. A technical mathematical note on a lemma in the calculus of variations. [Taub II]
- *Über Funktionen von Funktionaloperatoren*. A paper on functions of operators. [Taub II]
- *Algebraische Repräsentanten der Funktionen „bis auf eine Menge vom Masse Null“*. A paper on algebraic representatives of almost-everywhere equivalence classes. [Taub II]
- *Die Eindeutigkeit der Schrödingerschen Operatoren*. A paper on uniqueness questions for Schrödinger operators. [Taub II]
- *Zum Beweise des Minkowskischen Satzes über Linearformen*. A proof-oriented note on Minkowski’s theorem. [Taub II]
- *Über adjungierte Funktionaloperatoren*. A paper on adjoint operators. [Taub II]
- **1932:** *Proof of the Quasi-Ergodic Hypothesis*. A research article establishing the mean ergodic theorem. [Taub II]
- **1932:** *Physical Applications of the Ergodic Hypothesis*. A companion article on physical applications. [Taub II]
- *Dynamical Systems of Continuous Spectra*. A paper in ergodic and spectral theory. [Taub II]
- *Über einen Satz von Herrn M. H. Stone*. A paper concerning a theorem of Marshall Stone. [Taub II]
- *Einige Sätze über messbare Abbildungen*. A paper on measurable mappings. [Taub II]
- **1932:** *Zur Operatormethode in der klassischen Mechanik*. A paper applying operator methods to classical mechanics. [Taub II]
- *Zusätze zur Arbeit „Zur Operatormethode in der klassischen Mechanik“*. A supplement to the preceding paper, not merely a reprint. [Taub II]
- *Die Einführung analytischer Parameter in topologischen Gruppen*. A paper on analytic parameters in topological groups. [Taub II]
- *Zum Haarschen Mass in topologischen Gruppen*. A paper on Haar measure. [Taub II]
- *Almost Periodic Functions in a Group, I*. The first paper in a group-theoretic treatment of almost-periodic functions. [Taub II]
- *Almost Periodic Functions in Groups, II*. The second paper in that sequence. [Taub II]
- **1935:** *On Complete Topological Spaces*. A full research article, distinct from a short announcement carrying the same title. [Taub II; Beebe]
- *Comparison of Cells*. An unpublished manuscript known through the collected works; its incompleteness and editorial presentation make it resist the status of a finished work. [Taub II; Beebe]
- *Zur allgemeinen Theorie des Masses*. A paper on general measure theory. [Taub I]
- *Zusatz zur Arbeit „Zur allgemeinen Theorie des Masses“*. A substantive supplement to the preceding paper. [Taub I]
- *The Uniqueness of Haar’s Measure*. A paper on uniqueness of invariant measure. [Taub IV]
- **1942:** *Operator Methods in Classical Mechanics, II*. A joint continuation with Paul Halmos. [Taub IV]
- *A Theorem on Unitary Representations of Semisimple Lie Groups*. A representation-theory paper. [Taub IV]
- **1951:** *Eine Spektraltheorie für allgemeine Operatoren eines unitären Raumes*. A paper on spectral theory for general operators. [Taub IV]
- *On the Permutability of Self-Adjoint Operators*. A paper in operator theory. [Taub IV]
- *The Cross-Space of Linear Transformations, II*. A paper in the cross-space sequence; its numbering points to a larger collaborative or unpublished context. [Taub IV]
- *The Cross-Space of Linear Transformations, III*. A continuation of that sequence. [Taub IV]
- *Measure in Functional Spaces*. A paper on infinite-dimensional measure. [Taub IV]
- *Representation of Certain Linear Groups by Unitary Operators in Hilbert Space*. A representation-theory paper. [Taub IV]

### Operator rings, lattices, and continuous geometry

- *On a Certain Topology for Rings of Operators*. A research paper introducing a topology for operator rings. [Taub III]
- **1936:** *On Rings of Operators*. The first major paper in the series, coauthored with Francis J. Murray. [Taub III]
- **1937:** *On Rings of Operators, II*. The second Murray–von Neumann paper. [Taub III]
- **1940:** *On Rings of Operators, III*. The third paper in the series. [Taub III]
- **1943:** *On Rings of Operators, IV*. The fourth paper, again coauthored with Murray. [Taub III]
- **1938:** *On Infinite Direct Products*. A long paper connected with operator-algebraic constructions. [Taub III]
- **1949:** *On Rings of Operators: Reduction Theory*. A paper developing reduction theory. [Taub III]
- *On Some Algebraical Properties of Operator Rings*. A research paper on the algebraic structure of operator rings. [Taub III]
- *Characterization of Factors of Type II₁*. A manuscript or paper on operator-algebra factors. [Taub III]
- *On Compact Solutions of Operational-Differential Equations, I*. A paper whose numbering announces a continuation that was not necessarily completed or published. [Taub IV]
- *Charakterisierung des Spektrums eines Integraloperators*. A paper characterizing the spectrum of an integral operator. [Taub IV]
- *On Normal Operators*. A research paper in operator theory. [Taub IV]
- *On Inner Products in Linear, Metric Spaces*. A paper on inner-product characterization. [Taub IV]
- *The Determination of Representative Elements in the Residual Classes of a Boolean Algebra*. A paper in Boolean-algebra and measure theory. [Taub IV]
- **1936:** *Continuous Geometry*. A journal article announcing the theory, distinct from the book-length lecture notes. [Taub IV]
- **1936:** *Examples of Continuous Geometries*. A companion research article. [Taub IV]
- **1936:** *On Regular Rings*. A paper introducing and studying regular rings. [Taub IV]
- *Algebraic Theory of Continuous Geometries*. A research manuscript or paper in the continuous-geometry project. [Taub IV]
- **1937:** *Continuous Rings and Their Arithmetics*. A paper on the rings associated with continuous geometries. [Taub IV]
- *On the Transitivity of Perspective Mappings*. A continuous-geometry paper. [Taub IV]
- *Non-Isomorphism of Certain Continuous Rings*. An unfinished or unpublished text printed with an introduction by Irving Kaplansky; editorial mediation is part of the surviving object. [Taub IV]
- *Independence of F∞ of the Sequence ν*. A technical manuscript or paper whose collected form does not establish it as an independently finished publication. [Taub IV]
- **1940:** *Lattice Abelian Groups*. An unpublished manuscript subsequently reviewed in the collected works. [Taub IV; Beebe]
- *Some Matrix-Inequalities and Metrization of Matrix-Space*. A research paper on matrices and metric structure. [Taub IV]
- *Minimally Almost Periodic Groups*. A group-theory paper. [Taub IV]
- *Fourier Integrals and Metric Geometry*. A paper joining harmonic analysis and metric geometry. [Taub IV]
- *Approximative Properties of Matrices of High Finite Order*. A paper on large finite matrices. [Taub IV]

### Statistics and probability

- *The Mean Square Successive Difference*. A statistical paper on the successive-difference statistic. [Taub IV]
- *Distribution of the Ratio of the Mean Square Successive Difference to the Variance*. A statistical distribution paper. [Taub IV]
- *A Further Remark Concerning the Distribution of the Ratio of the Mean Square Successive Difference to the Variance*. A corrective or supplementary note. [Taub IV]
- *Tabulation of the Probabilities for the Ratio of the Mean Square Successive Difference to the Variance*. A numerical table as well as a text; its made object is partly computational data. [Taub IV]
- *Optimum Aiming at an Imperfectly Located Target*. An applied-probability and operations-research paper. [Taub IV]
- **1947:** *Statistical Methods in Neutron Diffusion*. A body of correspondence about what became the Monte Carlo method; it resists being called a single authored paper. [Taub V; MacTutor]
- **1950:** *Statistical Treatment of Values of the First 2,000 Decimal Digits of e and of π Calculated on the ENIAC*. A computational experiment and report, made jointly with Nicholas Metropolis and Gian-Carlo Rota. [Taub V]
- **1949–1951:** *Various Techniques Used in Connection with Random Digits*. A symposium contribution whose published summary was written by George Forsythe; the record is a mediated manifestation of von Neumann’s remarks. [Taub V]

### Game theory and economics

- **1928:** *Zur Theorie der Gesellschaftsspiele*. A paper proving the minimax theorem and inaugurating von Neumann’s formal game theory. [Taub VI]
- *Communication on the Borel Notes*. A brief intervention concerning Émile Borel’s game-theory notes, closer to scholarly communication than a freestanding treatise. [Taub VI]
- **1937/1945:** *A Model of General Economic Equilibrium*. A conference paper first presented in German and later published in English; the two dates describe presentation and manifestation, not two separate works. [Taub VI]
- *Solutions of Games by Differential Equations*. A game-theory paper. [Taub VI]
- *A Certain Zero-Sum Two-Person Game Equivalent to the Optimal Assignment Problem*. A paper connecting games and assignment optimization. [Taub VI]
- *Two Variants of Poker*. A mathematical analysis of stylized poker games, not a game or rulebook itself. [Taub VI]
- **1954:** *A Numerical Method to Determine Optimum Strategy*. A computational game-theory paper. [Taub VI; Beebe]
- *Discussion of a Maximum Problem*. A discussion contribution rather than an autonomous systematic paper. [Taub VI]
- *Numerical Method for Determination of Value and Best Strategies of a 0-Sum, 2-Person Game with Large Number of Strategies*. A computational memorandum or paper. [Taub VI]
- *Symmetric Solutions of Some General N-Person Games*. A game-theory paper. [Taub VI]
- **1955/1956:** *The Impact of Recent Developments in Science on the Economy and on Economics*. A talk delivered in 1955 and partially printed in 1956; the surviving publication is not the complete address. [Taub VI]

### Computing-machine design and programming protocols

- **1945:** *First Draft of a Report on the EDVAC*. A circulated technical report defining a stored-program computer organization. It bears von Neumann’s name, but its exclusive authorship is disputed because it drew on the work of J. Presper Eckert, John Mauchly, and their group. [Beebe; Scientific Publications]
- **1946:** *On the Principles of Large-Scale Computing Machines*. A report or lecture associated with Herman Goldstine; some bibliographic records differ over author order. [Taub V; Beebe]
- **1946:** *Preliminary Discussion of the Logical Design of an Electronic Computing Instrument, Part I, Volume I*. A technical design report coauthored with Arthur Burks and Herman Goldstine; it is a protocol for constructing a machine as much as a descriptive text. [Taub V]
- **1947:** *Planning and Coding of Problems for an Electronic Computing Instrument, Part II, Volume I*. A programming report with Herman Goldstine, functioning as a protocol. [Taub V]
- **1947:** *Planning and Coding of Problems for an Electronic Computing Instrument, Part II, Volume II*. A continuation of that programming protocol. [Taub V]
- **1948:** *Planning and Coding of Problems for an Electronic Computing Instrument, Part II, Volume III*. A further report with Goldstine; catalogues sometimes leave author order uncertain. [Taub V; Beebe]
- **1949/1951:** *The Future of High-Speed Computing*. A digest of an IBM seminar address, so the printed text is a manifestation prepared from a talk rather than necessarily von Neumann’s full manuscript. [Taub V; Scientific Publications]
- **1954:** *The NORC and Problems in High-Speed Computing*. A public address delivered at the unveiling of IBM’s Naval Ordnance Research Calculator. [Taub V]
- **1954:** *Entwicklung und Ausnutzung neuerer mathematischer Maschinen*. A German-language lecture or article on the development and use of newer computing machines. [Taub V; Beebe]
- **1954:** *Non-Linear Capacitance or Inductance Switching, Amplifying and Memory Devices*. An unpublished engineering memorandum or patent-related project; it resists classification as an ordinary scholarly paper. [Taub V; Beebe]
- *Notes on the Photon-Disequilibrium-Amplification Scheme*. Technical notes on a proposed physical computing or amplification device; their surviving status is that of notes, not a completed publication. [Taub V]
- **1946:** *One Proposed Design for the “Super”*. A joint Los Alamos patent application with Klaus Fuchs; it is a design and legal-technical instrument rather than a conventional text. [Beebe]

### Automata and reliable systems

- **1948/1951:** *The General and Logical Theory of Automata*. A Hixon Symposium lecture delivered in 1948 and published in 1951. [Taub V; MacTutor]
- **1952/1956:** *Probabilistic Logics and the Synthesis of Reliable Organisms from Unreliable Components*. Caltech lectures recorded by R. S. Pierce, revised by von Neumann, and published in 1956; the text is collaboratively mediated lecture notes. [Taub V]
- **1952–1953:** *A System of 29 States with a General Transition Rule*. Part of the cellular-automaton construction later edited by Burks; it is a formal machine specification and protocol, not merely an essay. [Beebe; MacTutor]
- **1952–1953:** The cellular universal constructor. A mathematical machine design embedded in unfinished manuscripts and completed editorially after von Neumann’s death; calling it a single “work” obscures its status as a project distributed across diagrams, transition rules, lectures, and fragments. [MacTutor]

- **1947:** *Numerical Inverting of Matrices of High Order*. A major paper coauthored with Herman Goldstine. [Taub V]

- *The Jacobi Method for Real Symmetric Matrices*. A numerical linear-algebra paper. [Taub V]

### Astrophysics and relativity

- **1935:** *The Statistics of the Gravitational Field Arising from a Random Distribution of Stars, I*. A joint astrophysical paper with Subrahmanyan Chandrasekhar. [Taub VI]
- *The Statistics of the Gravitational Field Arising from a Random Distribution of Stars, II*. The second Chandrasekhar–von Neumann paper in the series. [Taub VI]
- *Static Solution of Einstein Field Equation for Perfect Fluid with Tᵨᵨ = 0*. A relativity manuscript or paper. [Taub VI]
- *On the Relativistic Gas-Degeneracy and the Collapsed Configuration of Stars*. An astrophysical paper on collapsed stellar configurations. [Taub VI]
- *The Point Source Model*. A theoretical astrophysics text. [Taub VI]
- *The Point Source Solution, Assuming a Degeneracy of the Semi-Relativistic Type p = Kρ⁴ᐟ³ over the Entire Star*. A specialized continuation of the point-source project. [Taub VI]
- *The Point Source Solution*. A separate item under the same research project; the near-duplicate title should not be silently merged with the more specifically titled text. [Taub VI]

### Shock waves, hydrodynamics, meteorology, and applied physics

- *Theory of Shock Waves*. A wartime or technical research report. [Taub VI]
- **1942:** *Theory of Detonation Waves*. A classified or technical report subsequently included in the collected works. [Taub VI]
- *Oblique Reflection of Shocks*. A technical paper or report on shock-wave behavior. [Taub VI]
- *Refraction, Intersection and Reflection of Shock Waves*. A technical study of interacting shocks. [Taub VI]
- *The Mach Effect and the Height of Burst*. A weapons-effects report; its object is military calculation rather than a conventional scholarly article. [Taub VI]
- **1949/1951:** *Discussion on the Existence and Uniqueness or Multiplicity of Solutions of the Aerodynamical Equations*. A symposium discussion later printed as a chapter. [Taub VI]
- *Proposal and Analysis of a Numerical Method for the Treatment of Hydrodynamical Shock Problems*. A methodological report and computational protocol. [Taub VI]
- **1950:** *A Method for the Numerical Calculation of Hydrodynamic Shocks*. A joint paper with Robert D. Richtmyer introducing artificial viscosity. [Taub VI]
- *Blast Wave Calculation*. A computational report or memorandum. [Taub VI]
- **1950:** *Numerical Integration of the Barotropic Vorticity Equation*. A landmark numerical-weather-prediction paper coauthored with Jule Charney and Ragnar Fjørtoft. [Taub VI]
- *Taylor Instability at the Boundary of Two Incompressible Liquids*. A hydrodynamics paper or report. [Taub VI]
- *Taylor Instability Problem*. A related but separately catalogued text; its precise relation to the preceding item should not be collapsed without manuscript evidence. [Taub VI]
- *Recent Theories of Turbulence*. A survey or lecture rather than an original monographic theory. [Taub VI]
- *Description of the Conformal Mapping Method for the Integration of Partial Differential Equation Systems with 1+2 Independent Variables*. A methodological report. [Taub VI]
- **1948:** *On the Theory of Stationary Detonation Waves*. A technical file or report rather than a journal article. [Beebe]

- **1947:** *The Future Role of Rapid Computing: Meteorology*. A short article or address on computational meteorology. [Beebe]
- **1949:** *Review of Norbert Wiener’s Cybernetics*. A signed book review—an evaluative intervention, not a research paper. [Beebe]
- **1954:** *The Role of Mathematics in the Sciences and in Society*. An address to Princeton graduate alumni. [Taub VI]
- **1955:** *Method in the Physical Sciences*. A philosophical essay or invited contribution. [Taub VI]
- **1955:** *Can We Survive Technology?*. A public essay in *Fortune* on technology and planetary risk. [Taub VI]
