## Works by José Rafael Hernández

### Long poems and literary works

- **1872:** *El gaucho Martín Fierro*. A book-length gauchesque narrative poem, often called the “Ida”; Hernández continued revising it through the twelfth edition of 1883. Together with *La vuelta*, it forms the larger work *Martín Fierro*. [[1]](https://digital.eduvim.com.ar/library/publication/obras-completas-debates-parlamentarios-i)

- **1879:** *La vuelta de Martín Fierro*. The second book-length part of *Martín Fierro*, a gauchesque narrative poem rather than a separate novel; the first edition is the last version supervised by Hernández used in the critical collected works. [[1]](https://digital.eduvim.com.ar/library/publication/obras-completas-debates-parlamentarios-i)

### Shorter poems

- **1855:** *A mi amigo D. R. L. B.*. An early poem in a relatively classical register, identified by the critical collected-works project as Hernández’s first known poetic composition. [[2]](https://www.memoria.fahce.unlp.edu.ar/libros/pm.4632/pm.4632.pdf)

- **1859, attribution disputed:** *Un cielito ateruterado dirigido a Aniceto el Gallipavo*. A polemical gauchesque cielito published under the name “Juan Barriales.” Fermín Chávez and other scholars attributed that pseudonym to Hernández, but the identification remains conjectural rather than demonstrated. [[2]](https://www.memoria.fahce.unlp.edu.ar/libros/pm.4632/pm.4632.pdf) [[3]](https://revistaallaite.unla.edu.ar/150/un-cielito-ateruterado-de-juan-barriales-dirigido-a-aniceto-el-gallipavo)

- **1859, attribution disputed:** *El cielito de la luz dedicado al Ejército que va a invadir Buenos Aires*. Another political cielito signed “Juan Barriales”; it belongs in Hernández’s corpus only under the same disputed pseudonymous attribution. [[2]](https://www.memoria.fahce.unlp.edu.ar/libros/pm.4632/pm.4632.pdf) [[4]](https://www.cdeluruguay.gob.ar/images/bibliotecaVirtual/TOMO_II.pdf)

- **1855–1878, exact date not fixed here:** *Improvisación*. A gauchesque poem signed with Hernández’s pseudonym “José Pepe”; the scholarly edition places it among the dispersed poems of 1855–1878. [[2]](https://www.memoria.fahce.unlp.edu.ar/libros/pm.4632/pm.4632.pdf)

- **1855–1878, exact date not fixed here:** *Cantares* [I]. A gauchesque poem signed “José Pepe,” distinct from two later poems bearing the same generic title. [[2]](https://www.memoria.fahce.unlp.edu.ar/libros/pm.4632/pm.4632.pdf)

- **1855–1878, exact date not fixed here:** *A Carolina*. A short cultivated-register poem preserved among Hernández’s dispersed poetic productions. [[2]](https://www.memoria.fahce.unlp.edu.ar/libros/pm.4632/pm.4632.pdf)

- **1855–1878, exact date not fixed here:** *A Ana*. A short cultivated-register poem included in the critical collected works. [[2]](https://www.memoria.fahce.unlp.edu.ar/libros/pm.4632/pm.4632.pdf)

- **1855–1878, exact date not fixed here:** *Los dos besos*. A short allegorical poem in a cultivated rather than gauchesque register. [[2]](https://www.memoria.fahce.unlp.edu.ar/libros/pm.4632/pm.4632.pdf)

- **1855–1878, exact date not fixed here:** *Después del teatro*. A short cultivated-register poem recovered from Hernández’s dispersed production. [[2]](https://www.memoria.fahce.unlp.edu.ar/libros/pm.4632/pm.4632.pdf)

- **1855–1878, exact date not fixed here:** *El carpintero*. A short poem or song-like composition, not a book. [[2]](https://www.memoria.fahce.unlp.edu.ar/libros/pm.4632/pm.4632.pdf)

- **1855–1878, exact date not fixed here:** *Cantares* [II]. One of several distinct poems called *Cantares*; the bracketed number is an editorial means of distinguishing texts, not necessarily Hernández’s title. [[2]](https://www.memoria.fahce.unlp.edu.ar/libros/pm.4632/pm.4632.pdf)

- **1855–1878, exact date not fixed here:** *El viejo y la niña*. A comic-philosophical narrative poem in a cultivated register. [[2]](https://www.memoria.fahce.unlp.edu.ar/libros/pm.4632/pm.4632.pdf)

- **1855–1878, exact date not fixed here:** *Cantares* [III]. A further distinct composition sharing the title *Cantares*; its numbering is editorial and signals the instability of the title rather than a planned series. [[2]](https://www.memoria.fahce.unlp.edu.ar/libros/pm.4632/pm.4632.pdf)

- **1878:** *Carta que el gaucho Martín Fierro dirige a su amigo D. Juan Manuel Blanes con motivo de su cuadro Los Treinta y Tres*. A poem in the form of a public verse-letter, signed through the persona “Martín Fierro” and prompted by Blanes’s painting; it circulated as a loose sheet and in newspapers. It is sometimes shortened or misdescribed as *Los treinta y tres orientales*, but it is neither a prose tale nor a book by that title. [[5]](https://www.identidad-cultural.com.ar/leernota.php?cn=4140) [[6]](https://letras-uruguay.espaciolatino.com/salterain/cuadro_33.htm)

### Biographical and political prose

- **1863–1875:** *Rasgos biográficos del General D. Ángel V. Peñaloza*. A political biography and denunciatory essay usually known as *Vida del Chacho*. It began as articles in *El Argentino* in 1863, was gathered into a pamphlet that December, and was revised into a politically softened third version in 1875; the original newspaper sequence survives incompletely. [[1]](https://digital.eduvim.com.ar/library/publication/obras-completas-debates-parlamentarios-i) [[7]](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/122894/Documento_completo.pdf-PDFA.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)

- **1872:** *El camino trasandino*. A historical-geographical essay on routes across the Andes, first incorporated into the original edition of *El gaucho Martín Fierro*. Its material placement makes it a separate essay inside another made object, rather than another poem or autonomous book. [[1]](https://digital.eduvim.com.ar/library/publication/obras-completas-debates-parlamentarios-i)

- **1874:** *Cartas patagónicas*. A sequence of seven polemical newspaper letters concerning Bartolomé Mitre, published in *La Patria* of Montevideo. Their later presentation as part of *Vida de Mitre* is an editorial regrouping, not a book issued by Hernández under that title. [[8]](https://www.aal.edu.ar/BID/bid73_homenajejosehernandez.pdf)

- **1874:** *Vida de Mitre. Rasgos biográficos de su política*. This resists classification as a lifetime work: the collected-works editors constructed the unit from the seven *Cartas patagónicas* and twenty-two articles Hernández published in *La Patria*, supplemented by contextual documents. It is therefore a recoverable polemical corpus, but not an independently published Hernández book. [[8]](https://www.aal.edu.ar/BID/bid73_homenajejosehernandez.pdf)

- **1874, attributed:** *Manifiesto del Jefe de la Revolución Entrerriana*. A manifesto in the strict performative sense: it speaks politically in the name of Ricardo López Jordán, whose signature it bears. The critical collected works attributes its composition to Hernández but does not treat the signed authorship as certain. [[1]](https://digital.eduvim.com.ar/library/publication/obras-completas-debates-parlamentarios-i) [[7]](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/122894/Documento_completo.pdf-PDFA.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)

- **1881:** *Instrucción del estanciero: tratado completo para la planteación y manejo de un establecimiento de campo*. A commissioned manual of rural management: part technical treatise, part pedagogical protocol for running and supervising an estancia. It is neither fiction nor merely an essay about country life. [[9]](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/jose_hernandez/su_obra_catalogo/) [[10]](https://www.memoria.fahce.unlp.edu.ar/library?a=d&c=arti&d=Jpr20301)

### Newspaper projects and journalistic corpora

- **1860–1861:** *Articles in La Reforma Pacífica*. A corpus of twenty-two political newspaper pieces, some signed with the pseudonym “Vincha”; the collected works treat the corpus as Hernández’s earliest sustained journalism rather than as a single titled work. [[7]](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/122894/Documento_completo.pdf-PDFA.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)

- **1860:** *Articles in El Nacional Argentino*. A corpus of twenty newspaper articles. Hernández also directed the paper briefly, from 4 to 25 October 1860, but he did not create the periodical, which had existed since 1852. [[7]](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/122894/Documento_completo.pdf-PDFA.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)

- **1862:** *Articles in El Litoral*. Twenty newspaper pieces recovered for the critical collected works. Because earlier researchers knew only fragments and the surviving documentation is limited, the corpus is an archival reconstruction rather than a stable author-issued work. [[7]](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/122894/Documento_completo.pdf-PDFA.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)

- **1863:** *El Argentino*. Hernández’s first newspaper project, founded and edited by him in Paraná; the collected works recover ninety-eight editorials. The periodical is an obra made collectively and serially, not reducible to a book or to Hernández’s individually signed articles. [[7]](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/122894/Documento_completo.pdf-PDFA.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)

- **1868:** *Articles in El Eco*. Eight editorial articles are assigned to Hernández in the collected works, but his exact role at the paper is uncertain: scholars have supposed that he became its editor, while his name initially appears chiefly in official legislative transcriptions. [[7]](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/122894/Documento_completo.pdf-PDFA.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)

- **1868–1869:** *Articles in La Capital*. A corpus of fourteen newspaper pieces written for the Rosario paper. It is a dispersed journalistic manifestation, not a separately titled book. [[7]](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/122894/Documento_completo.pdf-PDFA.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)

- **1869–1870:** *El Río de la Plata*. Hernández’s second newspaper project and principal journalistic obra: a Buenos Aires daily founded and directed by him. The critical edition recovers 225 editorials from August–December 1869 and 113 from January–April 1870; the paper closed amid the political crisis following Urquiza’s assassination. [[7]](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/122894/Documento_completo.pdf-PDFA.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)

- **1874:** *Articles in La Patria*. Twenty-two Montevideo newspaper articles, including the anti-Mitre materials later editorially gathered as *Vida de Mitre*. The articles are the historical objects Hernández made; the later “book” is a collected-works construction. [[7]](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/122894/Documento_completo.pdf-PDFA.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1) [[8]](https://www.aal.edu.ar/BID/bid73_homenajejosehernandez.pdf)

- **1875:** *Three letters to Sarmiento in La Libertad*. A public polemical sequence addressed to Domingo F. Sarmiento and printed in Manuel Bilbao’s newspaper. These are open newspaper letters—political acts in epistolary form—not private correspondence. [[7]](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/122894/Documento_completo.pdf-PDFA.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)

- **1876, attributed:** *Contributions to El Bicho Colorado and Martín Fierro*. Brief pieces in two satirical weeklies, admitted to the complete corpus only as attributions; authorship should not be treated as settled. [[7]](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/122894/Documento_completo.pdf-PDFA.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)

### Parliamentary speech as political action

- **1879–1881:** *Speeches and interventions as provincial deputy*. A performative corpus made in debate rather than written as a book: interventions in twenty-eight sessions in 1879, twenty-four in 1880, and seven in early 1881. Their surviving form is mediated by legislative records and the later collected edition. [[7]](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/122894/Documento_completo.pdf-PDFA.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)

- **1880:** *Debate with Leandro N. Alem on the federalization of Buenos Aires*. A major sustained parliamentary intervention embedded within the preceding corpus. It resists treatment as an autonomous authored text because it was a live, adversarial political event preserved through official transcription. [[7]](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/122894/Documento_completo.pdf-PDFA.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)

- **1881:** *Speeches and interventions as provincial senator*. A corpus of participation in twenty-four Senate sessions during the remainder of 1881, concerning legislation, rural inequality, infrastructure and public institutions. [[7]](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/122894/Documento_completo.pdf-PDFA.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)

- **1882–1886:** *Later senatorial speeches and interventions*. A performative legislative corpus spanning twenty-five sessions in 1882, twelve in 1883, sixteen in 1884, fourteen in 1885 and six in 1886. These utterances became “texts” through institutional recording; authorship therefore coexists with parliamentary and editorial mediation. [[7]](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/122894/Documento_completo.pdf-PDFA.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)

### Letters and authorial framing

- **1872–1883:** *Peritexts to El gaucho Martín Fierro*. Hernández’s dedication-letter to José Zoilo Miguens, successive prologues and other authorial framing accumulated around the poem and were assembled in its twelfth edition. This is not one independent work but an evolving apparatus through which Hernández explained, promoted and repositioned the poem. [[1]](https://digital.eduvim.com.ar/library/publication/obras-completas-debates-parlamentarios-i)

- **Dates varying:** *Letters to Justo José de Urquiza*. Three surviving private or political letters included in the critical epistolary corpus. They are contingent communications rather than works conceived for publication. [[7]](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/122894/Documento_completo.pdf-PDFA.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)

- **Dates varying:** *Letters to Justo Urquiza*. Two surviving letters included in the critical epistolary corpus; their individuality survives, but the collective title is editorial. [[7]](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/122894/Documento_completo.pdf-PDFA.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)

- **Dates varying:** *Letters to Ricardo López Jordán*. Six surviving letters, politically significant but not conceived as a unified work; the grouping is archival and editorial. [[7]](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/122894/Documento_completo.pdf-PDFA.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)

- **Dates varying:** *Family letters*. Six letters addressed among Hernández’s wife, his daughter Isabel and his son-in-law complete the seventeen-item epistolary selection. They resist being called “works” at all: they are acts of intimate communication preserved by circumstance and subsequently admitted to the collected oeuvre. [[7]](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/122894/Documento_completo.pdf-PDFA.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)

### Diagrammatic and archival object

- **c. 1856–1857, attributed:** *Cuadro de las Facultades del Alma*. A synoptic philosophical diagram or table composed when Hernández was about twenty-two, discovered among his private papers only during preparation of the complete works. Its attribution is scholarly rather than secured by lifetime publication, and its hybrid visual-verbal form resists classification as poem, essay or conventional text. [[10]](https://www.memoria.fahce.unlp.edu.ar/library?a=d&c=arti&d=Jpr20301)
