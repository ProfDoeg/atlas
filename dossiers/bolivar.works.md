## Works by Simón Bolívar

### Documentary corpus: correspondence, orders and state papers

- **1795–1830:** *Escritos del Libertador*. The obra is not a single book but a vast, incompletely bounded documentary corpus—letters, memoranda, speeches, proclamations, manifestos, military orders, decrees, constitutional drafts and private papers. The critical edition issued by the Sociedad Bolivariana de Venezuela contains thousands of documents, while the standard *Doctrina del Libertador* explicitly presents only a representative selection; consequently, no short list of individually titled “works” can honestly claim to enumerate every surviving paper. [Sociedad Bolivariana de Venezuela](https://sociedadbolivarianavenezuela.org.ve/fondo-editorial/); [Biblioteca Ayacucho/CLACSO](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/items/9144a00e-f65c-406c-b25b-0f8e69d58910)

- **1799–1830:** *Cartas del Libertador*. This is an epistolary body of work, not a book conceived by Bolívar: thousands of personal letters, dispatches, instructions and drafts were assembled posthumously, and the corpus remains open because additional originals and copies have continued to surface. The standard direct bibliography distinguishes the eight-volume *Cartas del Libertador* from the critical *Escritos*. [Fundación Empresas Polar](https://bibliofep.fundacionempresaspolar.org/dhv/entradas/b/bolivar-simon/)

- **1813–1830:** *Decretos del Libertador*. This is another posthumously delimited corpus rather than an authored book: executive and military texts that acted upon people, property, institutions and territory, many prepared within governmental offices and authenticated by Bolívar’s signature. The standard direct bibliography records a three-volume scholarly collection. [Fundación Empresas Polar](https://bibliofep.fundacionempresaspolar.org/dhv/entradas/b/bolivar-simon/)

### Oaths, manifestos and public interventions

- **15 August 1805:** *Juramento de Roma* (also called *Juramento del Monte Sacro*). An oral vow—or subsequently manifested memory of one—by which Bolívar reportedly dedicated his life to Spanish American independence; it resists treatment as a securely preserved original text because Simón Rodríguez transmitted the full version only in 1850, and it was first printed in 1884. [*Doctrina del Libertador*, document 1 and editorial note](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download#page=42)

- **8 September 1810:** *Informe de los comisionados de Venezuela ante el gobierno británico*. A diplomatic report jointly made and signed by Bolívar and Luis López Méndez; it is therefore a collaborative state paper, not Bolívar’s solitary work. [*Doctrina del Libertador*, document 2](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download#page=44)

- **3–4 July 1811:** *Discurso ante la Sociedad Patriótica*. A spoken political intervention demanding an immediate declaration of Venezuelan independence; its surviving form is a textual record of an act of speech rather than an independently published book. [*Doctrina del Libertador*, document 3](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download#page=48)

- **15 December 1812:** *Memoria dirigida a los ciudadanos de la Nueva Granada por un caraqueño* (*Manifiesto de Cartagena*). A diagnostic political manifesto analyzing the First Venezuelan Republic’s collapse and advocating military action to restore it; some accounts associate Vicente Tejera with its preparation or signature, so solitary authorship should not be treated as entirely uncomplicated. [*Doctrina del Libertador*, document 4](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download#page=50)

- **24 December 1812:** *Discurso a los habitantes de Tenerife*. A victory address delivered after the town’s capture during the Magdalena campaign: an oral-political manifestation attached to a military event. [*Doctrina del Libertador*, document 5](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download#page=60)

- **15 June 1813:** *Decreto de Guerra a Muerte*. A manifesto and coercive wartime protocol issued at Trujillo, defining the treatment of Spaniards, Canary Islanders and Americans; it acts juridically and militarily rather than merely describing a policy. [*Doctrina del Libertador*, document 6](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download#page=64)

- **2 January 1814:** *Discurso ante la Asamblea Popular de Caracas*. A public defense of Bolívar’s conduct and authority, delivered in the Franciscan convent; its object is simultaneously an oration and a political performance of accountability. [Biblioteca Ayacucho, *Doctrina del Libertador*](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download)

- **7 September 1814:** *Manifiesto de Carúpano*. A manifesto explaining the failure of the Second Republic and defending the independence project while Bolívar departed Venezuela; it interprets defeat in order to preserve a future course of action. [Biblioteca Ayacucho, *Doctrina del Libertador*](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download)

- **6 September 1815:** *Contestación de un americano meridional a un caballero de esta isla* (*Carta de Jamaica*). Formally a dictated reply to Henry Cullen, but also a geopolitical essay and continental project; the original Spanish manuscript was long missing, making early printed versions textually consequential. [Biblioteca Ayacucho, *Doctrina del Libertador*](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download#page=104); [Fundación Empresas Polar](https://bibliofep.fundacionempresaspolar.org/dhv/entradas/b/bolivar-simon/)

- **28 September 1815:** *Artículo dirigido al editor de The Royal Gazette de Jamaica*. A public rebuttal written for the press in response to attacks on Spanish American independence; unlike the private form of a letter, its intended object was newspaper intervention. [Biblioteca Ayacucho, *Doctrina del Libertador*](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download)

- **2 June 1816:** *Decreto de libertad de los esclavos*. A wartime emancipation decree issued at Carúpano: simultaneously law, promise and recruitment measure, whose practical reach was constrained by the war and later legislative resistance. [Biblioteca Ayacucho, *Doctrina del Libertador*](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download); [Fundación Empresas Polar](https://bibliofep.fundacionempresaspolar.org/dhv/entradas/b/bolivar-simon/)

- **6 July 1816:** *Proclama sobre la libertad de los esclavos*. Issued at Ocumare, this manifesto renewed general emancipation and called formerly enslaved men into republican service; it is a performative political text whose promise exceeded what Bolívar alone could permanently enact. [Biblioteca Ayacucho, *Doctrina del Libertador*](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download)

- **5 August 1817:** *Manifiesto del Jefe Supremo a los pueblos de Venezuela*. A prosecutorial political manifesto against General Manuel Piar; because it helped prepare the public meaning of Piar’s trial and execution, it resists separation from the coercive act it justified. [*Doctrina del Libertador*, document note](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download#page=132)

- **12 June 1818:** *Proclama a los habitantes del Río de la Plata*. A transnational proclamation offering Venezuelan solidarity and presenting independence as an American rather than merely national undertaking. [Biblioteca Ayacucho, *Doctrina del Libertador*](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download)

- **20 November 1818:** *Declaración de la República de Venezuela*. A manifesto signed by Bolívar as supreme chief, asserting the republic’s determination to remain independent or perish; it is an official collective declaration voiced through its head of government. [*Doctrina del Libertador*, document 26](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download#page=155)

- **15 February 1819:** *Discurso de Angostura*. An inaugural congressional address, constitutional argument and program of social reform; it accompanied Bolívar’s resignation of dictatorial authority and proposed institutions rather than simply narrating political principles. [*Doctrina del Libertador*, document 27](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download#page=158); [Fundación Empresas Polar](https://bibliofep.fundacionempresaspolar.org/dhv/entradas/b/bolivar-simon/)

- **24 February 1820:** *Alocución a las matronas del Socorro*. A ceremonial-political address recognizing women’s contribution to independence; its category is public speech, not treatise. [*Doctrina del Libertador*, document 30](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download#page=198)

- **8 March 1820:** *Proclama a los colombianos*. A nation-making proclamation that names Venezuelans, New Granadans and Panamanians as “Colombians” and celebrates the newly constituted republic. [*Doctrina del Libertador*, document 31](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download#page=199)

- **1822, traditionally:** *Mi delirio sobre el Chimborazo*. A prose-poem or visionary political allegory, exceptional within Bolívar’s predominantly governmental corpus; its date, textual transmission and attribution have been disputed, so it should not be presented as an unquestionably authenticated 1822 autograph. [Biblioteca Ayacucho, *Doctrina del Libertador*](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download)

- **29 February 1828:** *Mensaje a la Convención de Ocaña*. A constitutional diagnosis prepared for delivery to a convention Bolívar did not attend; it is a governmental message whose purpose was to induce institutional change. [*Doctrina del Libertador*, document 84](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download#page=360)

- **April–June 1829:** *Una mirada sobre la América española*. An undated political article intended for a newspaper or pamphlet; Bolívar’s authorship is considered secure despite its third-person references to “the Liberator,” while its precise date remains an editorial inference. [*Doctrina del Libertador*, document 90](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download#page=378)

- **20 January 1830:** *Mensaje al Congreso Constituyente de Colombia*. Bolívar’s address to the “Admirable Congress,” combining resignation, political accounting and a last appeal to preserve Colombia; it is both a constitutional message and an attempted act of reconciliation. [Biblioteca Ayacucho, *Doctrina del Libertador*](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download)

- **10 December 1830:** *Última proclama*. A final public appeal for Colombian unity, dictated at Santa Marta shortly before Bolívar’s death; it is a political testament in the figurative, not legal, sense. [Fundación Empresas Polar](https://bibliofep.fundacionempresaspolar.org/dhv/entradas/b/bolivar-simon/)

### Constitutional designs and institutional projects

- **February 1819:** *Proyecto de Constitución para Venezuela*. A constitutional project submitted at Angostura; it was an intended governmental architecture, not identical with the constitution eventually deliberated and enacted by Congress. [Biblioteca Ayacucho, *Doctrina del Libertador*](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download)

- **February 1819:** *Proyecto de Poder Moral*. A proposed fourth power comprising chambers of Morals and Education. Congress judged it impracticable and printed it only as an appendix, so its unfinished institutional status is intrinsic to the work. [*Doctrina del Libertador*, document 28](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download#page=188)

- **14–17 December 1819:** *Proyecto de la República de Colombia*. Bolívar’s proposal to unite Venezuela, New Granada and Quito became the Republic of Colombia through congressional legislation; the resulting state was a collective political creation, not an individually authored object, although Bolívar was its principal advocate. [*Doctrina del Libertador*, document 29](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download#page=196)

- **20–27 November 1820:** *Armisticio y Tratado de Regularización de la Guerra*. A humanitarian-military protocol negotiated by republican and royalist commissioners and ratified by Bolívar and Pablo Morillo. It belongs to Bolívar’s political achievement but resists attribution as his text alone because its very form is bilateral. [Biblioteca Ayacucho, *Doctrina del Libertador*](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download)

- **1821–1826:** *Proyecto de confederación hispanoamericana*. A continuing diplomatic project expressed through missions, treaties, letters and invitations rather than one definitive text; its intended manifestation was a league of independent American republics. [Tecnos, *Obra política y constitucional*](https://www.tecnos.es/libro/clasicos-del-pensamiento/obra-politica-y-constitucional-simon-bolivar-9788430945177/)

- **7 December 1824:** *Convocatoria al Congreso de Panamá*. A circular invitation to the governments of Colombia, Mexico, Río de la Plata, Chile and Guatemala; it is a diplomatic protocol designed to bring a supranational institution into being. [Biblioteca Ayacucho, *Doctrina del Libertador*](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download)

- **1825–1826:** *Proyecto de Constitución para la República Boliviana*. Bolívar’s most sustained constitutional obra, drafted over approximately nine months and formally delivered on 25 May 1826; the Bolivian Congress altered and enacted it, while Antonio José de Sucre’s surviving manuscript additions further complicate any equation of Bolívar’s project with the promulgated constitution. [Fundación Empresas Polar](https://bibliofep.fundacionempresaspolar.org/dhv/entradas/b/bolivar-simon/); [CLACSO study](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstream/CLACSO/15388/1/Simon-Bolivar-ensayo.pdf)

- **25 May 1826:** *Mensaje al Congreso Constituyente de Bolivia*. A presentation speech explaining the constitutional project’s presidency-for-life, electoral power and four-part division of powers; it is the project’s argumentative companion, not the constitution itself. [Tribunal Constitucional Plurinacional de Bolivia](https://repositorio.uasb.edu.bo/bitstreams/fe3859e6-9e74-4ada-8ed1-f04b69274645/download)

- **1826:** *Un pensamiento sobre el Congreso de Panamá*. A short political meditation on the projected American assembly; it belongs to the confederative project rather than constituting a freestanding institutional design. [Biblioteca Ayacucho, *Doctrina del Libertador*](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download)

- **27 August 1828:** *Decreto Orgánico*. An emergency constitutional instrument by which Bolívar assumed supreme authority after the failure of the Ocaña Convention; signed within a ministerial government, it regulated a dictatorship and therefore acts as provisional constitutional machinery rather than commentary. [*Doctrina del Libertador*, document 88](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download#page=366)

### Social, educational, economic and environmental protocols

- **20 May 1820:** *Decreto sobre protección y educación de los indígenas*. A legal protocol issued at Rosario de Cúcuta to restore Indigenous rights and promote education and economic welfare; its effect depended on subordinate administrations, so decree and accomplished reform are not identical. [*Doctrina del Libertador*, document 33](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download#page=203)

- **21 May 1820:** *Decreto de creación de Juntas Provinciales de Agricultura y Comercio*. An institutional-economic protocol establishing provincial bodies to promote agriculture, settlement, industry and commerce. [*Doctrina del Libertador*, document 34](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download#page=207)

- **4 July 1825:** *Decreto sobre los derechos del indio*. A Cuzco decree prohibiting forms of coerced Indigenous labor and affirming civic equality; it is a normative act, not proof that the prohibited practices immediately disappeared. [*Doctrina del Libertador*, document 60](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download#page=276)

- **4 July 1825:** *Decreto de distribución de tierras a los indígenas*. A second Cuzco protocol ordering the division of state and communal lands; it manifests an agrarian project whose implementation and consequences extended beyond Bolívar’s authorship. [*Doctrina del Libertador*, document 61](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download#page=279)

- **11 December 1825:** *Decreto de organización del sistema educativo de Bolivia*. A nation-building educational protocol issued at Chuquisaca, assigning government responsibility for public instruction. [*Doctrina del Libertador*, document 64](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download#page=288)

- **11 December 1825:** *Decreto para la educación de niños huérfanos*. A related social-educational protocol ordering the collection, support and vocational education of orphaned children; its design was connected with Simón Rodríguez’s educational activity, so execution was collaborative. [*Doctrina del Libertador*, document 65](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download#page=291)

- **14 December 1825:** *Decreto de distribución de tierras del Estado en Santa Cruz*. An agrarian-colonization protocol for Bolivia, similar to but distinct from the Cuzco land decree. [*Doctrina del Libertador*, document 66](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download#page=293)

- **31 July 1829:** *Decreto sobre bosques*. A conservation and resource-management protocol regulating the use and replenishment of Colombia’s forests; it is an administrative act prepared within government, not a modern environmental treatise. [*Doctrina del Libertador*, document 92](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download#page=392)

- **24 October 1829:** *Decreto sobre minas*. A mining protocol declaring mines of every kind to belong to the republic and regulating their concession and exploitation; the critical anthology notes that the Council of Government prepared the text, so Bolívar’s role is executive adoption and signature rather than uncontested sole composition. [*Doctrina del Libertador*, document 96](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstreams/7960860c-b54a-4422-b082-f4f5c7d3a881/download#page=446)

### Historical and biographical writing

- **1825:** *Resumen sucinto de la vida del general Sucre*. A military-biographical narrative celebrating Antonio José de Sucre, printed at Lima by the state press. Surviving manuscripts and later editions also circulate under *La vida del general Sucre*; the textual history should not be flattened into a single modern title or edition. [Fundación Empresas Polar, direct bibliography](https://bibliofep.fundacionempresaspolar.org/dhv/entradas/b/bolivar-simon/)

### Personal legal act

- **10 December 1830:** *Testamento de Simón Bolívar*. A notarized dispositive protocol governing Bolívar’s property, debts, papers and burial wishes; it is something he made in law, but it resists the aesthetic or literary sense of “work.” [Fundación Empresas Polar](https://bibliofep.fundacionempresaspolar.org/dhv/entradas/b/bolivar-simon/)
