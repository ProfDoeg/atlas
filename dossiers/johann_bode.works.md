## Works by Johann Elert Bode

### Astronomical treatises, handbooks, and catalogues

- **1766:** *Berechnung und Entwurf der Sonnenfinsterniß vom 5ten August 1766*. Bode’s first printed astronomical calculation, a short predictive treatise accompanied by a diagram.
- **1768:** *Anleitung zur Kenntniß des gestirnten Himmels, auf jede einzele Monate des Jahres eingerichtet*. A practical, month-by-month guide to recognizing the night sky; repeated revisions made it a long-lived work rather than a single fixed text.
- **1768:** *Abhandlung von dem Durchgange der Venus durch die Sonne am 3ten Juni 1769*. A predictive observational treatise concerning the 1769 transit of Venus.
- **1769:** *Kurzgefaßte Abhandlung von dem im Herbst dieses 1769sten Jahres erschienenen Kometen nebst einem geometrischen Entwurf seiner wahren Laufbahn um die Sonne*. A report and geometrical reconstruction of the orbit of comet C/1769 P1.
- **1771:** *Kurzgefaßte Abhandlung nebst einem deutlichen Entwurf von der partialen Mondfinsterniß, welche in diesem 1771sten Jahre den 29sten April des Morgens frühe einfallen wird*. A predictive pamphlet and diagram for a lunar eclipse.
- **1771:** *Anleitung zur Kenntniß des Standes des Mondes und der Planeten*. A monthly observing guide; it belongs partly to the category of protocol because it instructs readers when and where to look.
- **1772:** *Deutliche Anleitung zur Kenntniß des gestirnten Himmels: Zum gemeinnützigen und beständigen Gebrauch*. A recast popular astronomy manual related to, but bibliographically distinct from, the 1768 *Anleitung*.
- **1776:** *Sammlung astronomischer Hülfstafeln*. A three-volume set of computational tables prepared with Johann Carl Gottlieb Schulze under the supervision of the Prussian Academy; this is a shared calculating instrument in print, not simply a prose book.
- **1778:** *Kurzgefaßte Erläuterung der Sternkunde und der dazu gehörigen Wissenschaften*. A two-part systematic astronomy textbook, subsequently expanded and issued as *Erläuterung der Sternkunde*; its editions are manifestations of an evolving pedagogical obra.
- **1779:** *Verzeichniß aller bisher entdeckten Nebelsterne und Sternhaufen*. A catalogue incorporated into Bode’s astronomical publishing programme, assembling earlier observations with Bode’s own additions; the underlying observations are therefore not all his creations.
- **1782:** *Ueber die Entdeckung eines neuen Planeten*. A scientific memoir on William Herschel’s discovery of Uranus and the determination of its planetary character; Bode was interpreter and advocate, not discoverer.
- **1784:** *Von dem neu entdeckten Planeten*. A more public-facing account of Uranus, its observations, orbit, and place in the solar system.
- **1786:** *Anleitung zur allgemeinen Kenntniß der Erdkugel*. An illustrated introductory geography and mathematical-cosmography handbook; revised editions appeared in 1803 and 1808.
- **1791:** *Allgemeine Untersuchungen und Bemerkungen über die Lage und Austheilung aller bisher bekannten Planeten- und Kometenbahnen*. A comparative study of planetary and cometary orbits issued with a large diagram; it is simultaneously treatise and visual model.
- **1794:** *Kurzer Entwurf der astronomischen Wissenschaften*. A concise illustrated textbook repeatedly revised through Bode’s lifetime; later editions are manifestations of the same pedagogical project.
- **1795:** *Claudius Ptolomäus’ Beobachtung und Beschreibung der Gestirne und der Bewegung der himmlischen Sphäre*. An edited and annotated German presentation of Ptolemy rather than an original Bode treatise; Bode’s obra here is editorial, explanatory, and visual.
- **1801:** *Allgemeine Beschreibung und Nachweisung der Gestirne nebst Verzeichniss der geraden Aufsteigung und Abweichung von 17,240 Sternen, Doppelsternen, Nebelflecken und Sternhaufen*. The catalogue and explanatory companion to the *Uranographia*; it compiles observations from many astronomers, so Bode made the system and publication but not all its constituent data.
- **1801:** *Allgemeine Betrachtungen über das Weltgebäude*. A popular cosmological survey; later impressions, including the often-cited 1808 edition, are manifestations rather than separate works.
- **1802:** *Von dem neuen, zwischen Mars und Jupiter entdeckten achten Hauptplaneten des Sonnensystems*. A topical treatise on Ceres, then classified as a principal planet; its title preserves a classification subsequently abandoned.
- **1805:** *Verzeichniß der geraden Aufsteigung und Abweichung von 5505 Sternen nach den Beobachtungen des Herrn Dr. Piazzi*. A processed star catalogue derived from Giuseppe Piazzi’s observations; Bode’s contribution is compilation and publication, not the observations themselves.
- **1811:** *J. E. Bode’s Erläuterungen über die Einrichtung und den Gebrauch seiner astronomischen Jahrbücher*. A protocol for reading and applying the tables in his yearbooks.
- **1817:** *Nachtrag zu seiner Anleitung zur Kenntniß des gestirnten Himmels, so wie zu dessen Betrachtung der Gestirne und des Weltgebäudes*. A supplement updating two established works for discoveries made since their earlier editions.

### Celestial atlases, charts, and geographical maps

- **1782:** *Vorstellung der Gestirne auf XXXIV Kupfertafeln, nach der Pariser Ausgabe des Flamsteadschen Himmelsatlas*. A thirty-four-plate celestial atlas adapted and enlarged from Jean-Baptiste Fortin’s French atlas based on Flamsteed; it is a composite visual obra whose source authorship should not be collapsed into Bode’s.
- **1783:** *Beschreibung und Gebrauch einer auf den Horizont von Berlin entworfenen neuen Weltcharte in zween Hemisphären, worauf die neuesten Entdeckungen angezeigt werden*. A descriptive protocol accompanying a double-hemisphere world map designed for the Berlin horizon.
- **1791:** *Charte der Planeten- und Kometenbahnen*. The large orbital chart issued with Bode’s *Allgemeine Untersuchungen*; although bound to a treatise in surviving manifestations, it functions independently as a graphic scientific model.
- **1797–1801:** *Uranographia sive astrorum descriptio viginti tabulis aeneis incisa ex recentissimis et absolutissimis astronomorum observationibus*. A monumental celestial atlas of twenty copperplate maps, planned and produced over several years and published in 1801. Its combination of positional data, constellation imagery, typography, and engraving makes it a scientific Gesamtkunstwerk; Bode directed and compiled it, while specialist engravers executed the plates.
- **1800:** *Beschreibung und Gebrauch einer allgemeinen Himmelscharte, mit einem durchscheinenden Horizont*. A protocol and movable or layered sky-chart apparatus, intended for practical orientation rather than continuous reading.
- **1805:** *Vorstellung der Gestirne auf XXXIV Kupfertafeln*, revised edition. A substantially renewed manifestation of the smaller celestial atlas, not an entirely unrelated obra.

### Editorial and serial projects

- **1773–1826:** *Astronomisches Jahrbuch oder Ephemeriden* (commonly the *Berliner Astronomisches Jahrbuch*), volumes for **1776–1829**. Bode founded, computed, edited, and issued fifty-one annual volumes, normally several years in advance; this is best understood as a sustained editorial and computational project containing both Bode’s work and contributions by many other astronomers, not as fifty-one solely authored books.
- **1776:** *Sammlung astronomischer Tafeln unter Aufsicht der Königlich-Preußischen Akademie der Wissenschaften*. Bode participated in the Academy’s collective production of astronomical tables; the precise division between his calculations and those of collaborators prevents treating the whole collection as exclusively authored by him.
- **1776; revised 1780:** *Dialogen über die Mehrheit der Welten*. Bode’s German translation and extensive astronomical annotation of Bernard le Bovier de Fontenelle’s *Entretiens sur la pluralité des mondes*, furnished with plates. Fontenelle made the dialogue; Bode made this translated, corrected, and visually supplemented manifestation.

### Scientific memoirs and articles

- **1775:** *Beschreibung eines bequemen Instruments, um scheinbare Entfernungen am Himmel zu messen*. An instrument description and operating protocol published in the *Beschäftigungen der Berlinischen Gesellschaft naturforschender Freunde*.
- **1775:** *Ueber den Cometen von 1773*. A periodical report on the comet of 1773.
- **1776:** *Gedanken über die Natur der Sonne und Entstehung ihrer Flekken*. A speculative physical essay on the Sun and sunspots.
- **1782:** *Ueber die Entdeckung eines neuen Planeten*. The academy-society memoir underlying the separately circulating Uranus publication; whether bibliography counts the offprint and journal appearance as one work or two manifestations depends on the level of description.
- **1787:** *Friedrichs Sternendenkmal*. A short commemorative article proposing a celestial memorial to Frederick II; it acts partly as a public proposal or project, not merely as astronomical description.
- **1790:** *Eine astronomische Nachricht von Herrn Pater Hell’s neuen Sternbildern*. A critical notice concerning Maximilian Hell’s proposed constellations.
- **1794:** *Herrn Doctor Herschels neueste Beobachtungen des Saturns, nebst Bemerkungen über den Ring, und über die Rotation und den Abstand des äussersten Trabanten*. A report and commentary transmitting Herschel’s Saturn observations to German readers; the observations themselves remain Herschel’s.
- **1795:** *Beschreibung und Gebrauch eines zur Bestimmung der Vergrößerungskraft eines Fernrohrs dienenden Werkzeuges*. A brief instrument description and protocol.
- **1795:** *Gedanken über die Austheilung der Nebelflecke und Sternhaufen im Weltraum, bey Gelegenheit der vom Herrn D. Herschel bekannt gemachten Beobachtungen dieser Himmelskörper*. A cosmological essay prompted by Herschel’s surveys.
- **1799:** *Gedanken über vermuthete Veränderungen der Erdpole und Axe*. A speculative geophysical memoir on possible changes in Earth’s poles and axis.
- **1803:** *Ueber die in den Jahren 1801 und 1802 gemachten neuen Entdeckungen im Planetensystem der Sonne*. A synthetic report on Ceres and Pallas and the changing architecture of the solar system.
- **1803:** *Ueber die neuen Entdeckungen im Planetensystem der Sonne*. A short, separately titled notice; its close relation to the preceding memoir makes it resist classification as an wholly independent work.

### Autobiographical and institutional texts

- **1806:** *Selbstbiographie*. Bode’s published autobiographical account for Johann Michael Siegfried Löwe’s *Bildnisse jetztlebender Berliner Gelehrten mit ihren Selbstbiographien*.
- **Probably 1810s–1820s; unpublished in Bode’s lifetime:** *Entwurf einer litterarischen Geschichte der hiesigen Königlichen Sternwarte*. An unfinished institutional history surviving in manuscript. It is a project and archival text rather than a completed publication; associated notes in the same file are not all securely attributable to Bode.
- **Undated and dispersed:** *Astronomical lecture manuscripts*. Schwemin’s work bibliography records unpublished lecture material alongside printed writings, but the surviving pieces do not form one securely titled, finished work; their resistance to work-status is part of their bibliographical identity.

### Instruments, models, and demonstrations

- **Dates not securely fixed:** *Celestial globes, planetary models, and planetaria used in Bode’s teaching*. Contemporary documentation and Schwemin’s study identify these among the media through which Bode popularized astronomy. Individual exemplars, makers, dates, and survival histories are not consistently recoverable, so it would be misleading to invent separate titles or to claim that Bode personally fabricated every physical component; they are collaborative pedagogical manifestations of his astronomical project.
