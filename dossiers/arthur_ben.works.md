## Works by Arthur Ben

### Scholarly article

- **1987:** *Indexing for the Online Catalog*. A scholarly article on information retrieval and catalogue design, published in *Information Technology and Libraries*, 6.4, pp. 297–304. It is the sole work by Chitty represented in CUNY’s institutional bibliography; bibliographic citations elsewhere confirm the journal, volume, issue, and pagination.[^1][^2]

### Labor history

- **2001–2018:** *From the Folks Who Brought You the Weekend: A Short, Illustrated History of Labor in the United States*; revised as *From the Folks Who Brought You the Weekend: An Illustrated History of Labor in the United States*. A collaboratively authored history by Priscilla Murolo and A. B. Chitty, with illustrations by Joe Sacco—not solely Chitty’s obra and not simply a conventional single-author book. First published in 2001, it received a substantially revised and updated manifestation in 2018, including new material and two new chapters; the publisher and Chitty himself identify him as coauthor.[^3][^4][^5]

### Institutional creation

- **2006 onward:** *Queens College Unions Joint Committee on Quality of Work Life*. A cross-union workplace-health-and-safety organization that Chitty helped create, convened, and chaired rather than a text in the ordinary bibliographical sense. It resists treatment as a discrete “work”: its obra was an enduring structure for collective action, and its continuing meetings were manifestations of that project.[^4][^6][^7]

### Spoken testimony

- **2019:** *Five Questions for Arthur “Ben” Chitty: An Activist Reflects on Decades of Resistance*. An edited interview rather than a wholly authored essay: Chitty’s spoken first-person account of his scholarship, labor activism, naval service, and antiwar work was shaped into a published text by the *Clarion* staff. Authorship is therefore shared or indeterminate at the level of the finished manifestation.[^4]
