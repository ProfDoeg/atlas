## Works by Keiko Sofia Fujimori Higuchi

### Government projects and electoral protocols

- **2011:** *Por un Perú con orden y seguridad, que crece más y comparte: el crecimiento en democracia*. Presidential government plan of Fuerza 2011. It is a collective party protocol rather than a securely attributable solo-authored text; the JNE records it under Fujimori’s candidacy, while CIDOB describes it as a concise catalogue of policy propositions.

- **2016:** *Plan Perú: Plan de Gobierno 2016–2021*. Presidential government plan of Fuerza Popular, organized around six policy “pillars.” It arose from party committees, consultations, and Fujimori’s campaign travels, so it resists classification as her individual work even though it embodied and performed her candidacy.

- **2021:** *Rescate 2021: Plan de Rescate y Reconstrucción Nacional*. Ninety-page presidential government plan submitted by Fuerza Popular to the JNE. The document expressly incorporates proposals contributed by citizens online: it is therefore a collaborative electoral protocol associated with Fujimori, not demonstrably her sole composition.

- **2026:** *Perú con Orden: Plan de Gobierno 2026–2031*. Fuerza Popular’s electoral program, structured around “orden ciudadano,” “orden económico,” and “orden social.” The JNE catalogues government plans as documents submitted by political organizations, so attribution to Fujimori is political and institutional rather than unambiguously literary.

### Political essays

- **2 April 2026:** *Perú con Orden: La oportunidad histórica del Pacífico Sur (2026–2031)*. Signed opinion essay presenting Fujimori’s regional-economic argument and recasting her electoral plan as a programmatic manifesto.

- **28 September 2026:** *Seguridad, confianza y futuro: un mensaje del Perú al mundo*. Signed presidential opinion essay published after her appearance at the United Nations General Assembly. It is simultaneously an account of a diplomatic journey and a manifesto of governmental intent.
