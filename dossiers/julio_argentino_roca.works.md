## Works by Julio Argentino Roca

### Military and territorial projects

- **1878–1879:** *Project for the occupation of the Río Negro and the southern territories*. A military-territorial project argued for by Roca as minister of war and implemented through several offensives culminating in the 1879 expedition commonly called the *Conquista del Desierto*. It resists attribution as Roca’s individual obra: Congress authorized and funded it, numerous officers and Indigenous allies participated, and present scholarship emphasizes both its collective institutional authorship and the killing, capture, displacement, and dispossession of Indigenous peoples. [Museo Roca](https://museoroca.cultura.gob.ar/media/uploads/site-15/cuadernillo_roca-baja.pdf)

- **1879:** *Expedición al Río Negro*. The campaign commanded personally by Roca was the operational manifestation of the preceding project rather than a textual work. The accompanying scientific report and Antonio Pozzo’s photographic album were made by the expedition’s commission and photographer, not by Roca, and therefore are not entered here as his works. [Educ.ar](https://www.educ.ar/recursos/adjuntos/descarga/22711/discurso-de-julio-a-roca-ante-el-), [Museo Roca](https://museoroca.cultura.gob.ar/media/uploads/site-15/cuadernillo_roca-baja.pdf)

### Ministerial reports and proposals

- **1878:** *Memoria del Ministerio de Guerra y Marina presentada al Congreso Nacional de 1878*. An official accounting and policy document issued by Roca as minister; its ministerial signature establishes political responsibility but not necessarily sole composition of every administrative section and annex. [Archivo y Biblioteca Histórica de la Armada de Chile](https://arhisar.infodi.cl/items/show/23366), [Academia Nacional de la Historia](https://repositorio.anh.org.ar/bitstream/anh/616/1/BaANH50713%20Memoria%20del%20Ministerio%20de%20Guerra%20y%20Marina%20-%20Marina%20%281878%29.pdf)

- **13 September 1878:** *Speech to Congress advocating the advance to the Río Negro*. This untitled parliamentary intervention functions as both manifesto and project argument for replacing the existing frontier policy with military occupation. Its title varies in later references, so no editorial title is imposed here. [Museo Roca](https://museoroca.cultura.gob.ar/media/uploads/site-15/cuadernillo_roca-baja.pdf)

- **1879:** *Memoria de Marina presentada al Honorable Congreso de 1879*. An institutional report presented under Roca’s ministerial authority, incorporating separately produced reports from the naval command, Naval School, Hydrographic Office, medical board, and port authority; consequently it resists treatment as a solely authored book. [CENPAT-CONICET repository](https://www.repositorio.cenpat-conicet.gob.ar/items/7ccc9bd2-c555-4a15-acb2-2b52b7c37853/full)

### Presidential inaugural addresses

- **12 October 1880:** *Discurso del brigadier general D. Julio A. Roca al recibirse de la Presidencia de la República ante el Congreso Argentino*. An inaugural address and programmatic manifesto for the first presidency, subsequently printed as a pamphlet. [Educ.ar](https://www.educ.ar/recursos/adjuntos/descarga/22711/discurso-de-julio-a-roca-ante-el-), [digitized printed edition](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Discurso_del_Brigadier_General_D._Julio_A._Roca.pdf)

- **12 October 1898:** *Discurso de asunción ante la Asamblea Legislativa*. The inaugural manifesto of Roca’s second presidency, presenting a renewed governmental program. The address belongs to presidential oratory rather than to an independently conceived book. [Ana Leonor Romero, “El presidente frente al Congreso”](https://fh.mdp.edu.ar/revistas/index.php/pasadoabierto/article/view/9748/10166)

### Annual messages to Congress: first presidency

- **1881:** *Mensaje del Presidente de la República al abrir las sesiones del Congreso Argentino*. A constitutionally required state-of-the-nation address: an authored political intervention but also an executive document assembled from ministerial information. [Cámara de Diputados series](https://www.hcdn.gob.ar/secparl/dgral_info_parlamentaria/mensajes_presidenciales/index.html)

- **1882:** *Mensaje del Presidente de la República al abrir las sesiones del Congreso Argentino*. The second annual message of the first presidency; later editions are witnesses to the same address, not additional works. [Cámara de Diputados series](https://www.hcdn.gob.ar/secparl/dgral_info_parlamentaria/mensajes_presidenciales/index.html)

- **4 May 1883:** *Mensaje del Presidente de la República al abrir las sesiones del Congreso Argentino*. An annual executive report and agenda delivered to the thirtieth legislative period. [Biblioteca del Congreso de la Nación](https://bcn.gob.ar/uploads/DOSSIER-legislativo-A3N82-Mensajes-presidenciales-Roca.pdf)

- **6 May 1884:** *Mensaje del Presidente de la República al abrir las sesiones del Congreso Argentino*. An annual executive report and programmatic address to the thirty-first legislative period. [Biblioteca del Congreso dossier series](https://bcn.gob.ar/la-biblioteca/publicaciones/dossiers)

- **7 May 1885:** *Mensaje del Presidente de la República al abrir las sesiones del Congreso Argentino*. An annual executive report and programmatic address to the thirty-second legislative period. [Biblioteca del Congreso dossier series](https://bcn.gob.ar/la-biblioteca/publicaciones/dossiers)

- **10 May 1886:** *Mensaje del Presidente de la República al abrir las sesiones del Congreso Argentino*. The final annual message of Roca’s first presidency, delivered to the thirty-third legislative period. [Biblioteca del Congreso dossier series](https://bcn.gob.ar/la-biblioteca/publicaciones/dossiers)

### Annual messages to Congress: second presidency

- **1899:** *Mensaje del Presidente de la República al abrir las sesiones del Congreso Argentino*. The first annual message of the second presidency; it is preserved both as an individual official printing and within the historical compilation of presidential messages. [Academia Nacional de la Historia](https://repositorio.anh.org.ar/handle/anh/416/browse?type=dateissued), [Mabragaña-based edition](https://julioarocahoy.wordpress.com/2014/08/21/multimedia-los-mensajes-presidenciales-de-roca-en-su-segunda-presidencia-1898-1904-2/)

- **May 1900:** *Mensaje del Presidente de la República al abrir las sesiones del Congreso Argentino*. An official annual report and legislative agenda attributed to Roca in the Academy’s presidential-message collection. [Academia Nacional de la Historia](https://repositorio.anh.org.ar/handle/anh/421)

- **1901:** *Mensaje del Presidente de la República al abrir las sesiones del Congreso Argentino*. The annual presidential message for 1901; like the others, it combines Roca’s political voice with information generated by the executive departments. [Cámara de Diputados series](https://www.hcdn.gob.ar/secparl/dgral_info_parlamentaria/mensajes_presidenciales/index.html)

- **May 1902:** *Mensaje del Presidente de la República al abrir las sesiones del Congreso Argentino*. An annual executive report and agenda preserved as an official printed text. [Academia Nacional de la Historia](https://repositorio.anh.org.ar/bitstream/anh/422/1/BaANH50574%20Mensaje%20del%20Presidente%20de%20la%20Republica%20-%20Julio%20A.%20Roca%20%281902%29.pdf)

- **May 1903:** *Mensaje del Presidente de la República al abrir las sesiones del Congreso Argentino*. The fifth annual message of the second presidency, catalogued under Roca’s authorship by the Academy’s documentary repository. [Academia Nacional de la Historia](https://repositorio.anh.org.ar/handle/anh/416/browse?type=dateissued)

- **1904:** *Mensaje del Presidente de la República al abrir las sesiones del Congreso Argentino*. The last of the six annual messages of Roca’s second presidency; surviving repositories differ in which years they have digitized, but the contemporary series and Mabragaña compilation establish the complete 1899–1904 sequence. [Cámara de Diputados series](https://www.hcdn.gob.ar/secparl/dgral_info_parlamentaria/mensajes_presidenciales/index.html), [Mabragaña-based edition](https://julioarocahoy.wordpress.com/2014/08/21/multimedia-los-mensajes-presidenciales-de-roca-en-su-segunda-presidencia-1898-1904-2/)

### Correspondence and documentary bodies

- **1861–1914:** *Correspondencia oficial y privada conservada en el Fondo Julio Argentino Roca del Archivo General de la Nación*. An approximately forty-thousand-document archive containing correspondence sent and received. It is not a finished work, nor is everything in it made by Roca; it is an archival body whose boundaries reflect preservation and acquisition rather than authorial design. [Archivo General de la Nación, guide to private archives](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/archivosprivadostomoii4.pdf)

- **1861–1914:** *Colección Julio Argentino Roca, AR-MR-JAR01*. The Museo Roca’s archival collection comprises 797 simple and eleven composite documentary units, principally family, administrative, and political correspondence assembled from several donations. It resists being called a single obra because it includes incoming material, later transcriptions, photographs, and groupings imposed after Roca’s death. [Patrimonio Argentino archival description](https://patrimonioargentino.cultura.gob.ar/index.php/Detail/collections/679)

- **First decades of the twentieth century:** *Letters to Marcos Sastre concerning Estancia La Larga*. A surviving body of forty-two letters by Roca to the estate’s administrator: working administrative texts through which the agricultural enterprise was continually made and managed, not a literary correspondence conceived for publication. [Museo Roca, Proyecto Estancia La Larga](https://museoroca.cultura.gob.ar/noticia/proyecto-la-larga/)

- **1905–1914:** *Letters to Gumersindo García*. A surviving body of approximately forty letters to Roca’s Buenos Aires steward, concerning La Larga, La Paz, travel, finances, organization, and personal matters. The corpus was later edited by the Museo Roca but was not designed by Roca as a book. [Museo Roca, Proyecto Estancia La Larga](https://museoroca.cultura.gob.ar/noticia/proyecto-la-larga/), [Museo Roca account of the corpus](https://museoroca.cultura.gob.ar/noticia/el-museo-roca-presente-en-la-larga-daireaux/)

- **Undated as a body; individual letters span several decades:** *Other family, political, military, diplomatic, and estate correspondence*. Hundreds of additional outgoing letters survive dispersed among the Museo Roca, the Archivo General de la Nación, the Academia Nacional de la Historia, and other collections. No complete critical edition or item-level catalogue of all letters written by Roca was identified, so assigning a fabricated title or claiming completeness at the individual-letter level would be misleading. [Academia Nacional de la Historia archival records](https://archivo.anhistoria.org.ar/index.php/informationobject/browse?creators=11974&languages=es&sf_culture=es&showAdvanced=1&sort=relevance&sortDir=asc&topLod=0), [Museo Roca collection description](https://patrimonioargentino.cultura.gob.ar/index.php/Detail/collections/679)
