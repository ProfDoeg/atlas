## Works by Arthur of Lotharingia

### No securely attributable works

No standard scholarly repertory consulted identifies a historical maker named “Arthur of Lotharingia” active in 841–848. Moreover, “Lotharingia” derives from the kingdom of Lothar II after 855, making the supplied designation anachronistic; assigning works to this name would therefore require inventing both a person and a corpus.
