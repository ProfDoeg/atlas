## Works by Avram Noam Chomsky

### Foundational manuscripts, theses, and linguistic monographs

- **1951:** *Morphophonemics of Modern Hebrew*. Master’s thesis and early linguistic study; it became a separately published monograph only in 1979, so the manuscript and book are two manifestations of substantially the same obra. [Bibliographic record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noam_Chomsky_bibliography_and_filmography)
- **1953:** *Systems of Syntactic Analysis*. Journal article proposing formal procedures for syntactic analysis. [Bibliographic record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noam_Chomsky_bibliography_and_filmography)
- **1955:** *The Logical Structure of Linguistic Theory*. Large unpublished typescript underlying Chomsky’s transformational-generative project; a revised and abridged book appeared in 1975, so the 1955 manuscript resists identification with its later manifestation. [Bibliographic record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noam_Chomsky_bibliography_and_filmography)
- **1955:** *Transformational Analysis*. Doctoral dissertation derived from part of *The Logical Structure of Linguistic Theory*, rather than an entirely independent project. [Bibliographic record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noam_Chomsky_bibliography_and_filmography)
- **1956:** *Three Models for the Description of Language*. Technical article defining and comparing finite-state, phrase-structure, and transformational models. [Koerner–Tajima bibliography](https://doi.org/10.1075/lisl.11)
- **1956:** *On Accent and Juncture in English*. Collaborative linguistic article with Morris Halle and Fred Lukoff. [Koerner–Tajima bibliography](https://doi.org/10.1075/lisl.11)
- **1957:** *Syntactic Structures*. Compact research monograph and the first public book-length manifestation of Chomsky’s transformational grammar. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1959:** *A Review of B. F. Skinner’s Verbal Behavior*. Extended review essay whose argument became an independent intervention in psychology and theories of language acquisition. [Koerner–Tajima bibliography](https://doi.org/10.1075/lisl.11)
- **1964:** *Current Issues in Linguistic Theory*. Monograph developed from the Ninth International Congress of Linguists plenary address. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1965:** *Aspects of the Theory of Syntax*. Research monograph establishing the “standard theory” of generative grammar. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1966:** *Cartesian Linguistics: A Chapter in the History of Rationalist Thought*. Historical-philosophical monograph; its construction of a continuous “Cartesian” tradition has remained disputed among historians of linguistics. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1966/1972:** *Topics in the Theory of Generative Grammar*. Lectures first issued in 1966 and listed as a 1972 book in Chomsky.info’s chronology; the date therefore depends on whether the lecture publication or later book manifestation is meant. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1968:** *Language and Mind*. Lecture-derived monograph joining linguistics, psychology, and philosophy; later editions enlarge the original text and are not wholly identical manifestations. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1968:** *The Sound Pattern of English*. Large collaborative monograph with Morris Halle presenting generative phonology. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1972:** *Studies on Semantics in Generative Grammar*. Author-organized collection of technical papers, hence both a book and a retrospective manifestation of previously separate texts. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1975:** *The Logical Structure of Linguistic Theory*. Revised, abridged publication of the 1955 typescript; it should not be mistaken for a wholly new work. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1975:** *Reflections on Language*. Philosophical-linguistic monograph written for a broader audience. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1977:** *Essays on Form and Interpretation*. Collection of technical linguistic essays. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1980:** *Rules and Representations*. Linguistic and philosophical monograph developed from lectures; the 2005 issue is a reissue, not a new obra. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1982:** *Lectures on Government and Binding: The Pisa Lectures*. Lecture-cycle turned technical monograph and a central manifestation of the principles-and-parameters project. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1982:** *Some Concepts and Consequences of the Theory of Government and Binding*. Expository technical monograph. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1982:** *Language and the Study of Mind*. Japanese lecture publication; its precise independence from Chomsky’s other lecture-derived books is bibliographically difficult to delimit. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1984:** *Modular Approaches to the Study of the Mind*. Published lecture series on linguistic and cognitive modularity. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1986:** *Barriers*. Technical monograph on constraints within government-and-binding theory. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1986:** *Knowledge of Language: Its Nature, Origin, and Use*. Programmatic monograph on language as an internal cognitive system. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1987:** *Language in a Psychological Setting*. Lecture publication; a small, locally published manifestation rather than a new large-scale theory. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1988:** *Language and Problems of Knowledge: The Managua Lectures*. Lecture-cycle converted into a linguistic monograph. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1993:** *Language and Thought*. Short lecture-derived book whose component material overlaps Chomsky’s longer linguistic project. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1995:** *The Minimalist Program*. Research monograph collecting and revising essays into the principal book manifestation of the minimalist project. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2000:** *New Horizons in the Study of Language and Mind*. Authorial collection of linguistic and philosophical essays. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2002:** *On Nature and Language*. Edited composite of essays, an interview, and discussion, prepared by Adriana Belletti and Luigi Rizzi; Chomsky supplied the substantive texts but did not alone determine the object’s final form. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2012:** *The Science of Language*. Book-length interviews with James McGilvray; a collaborative conversational text rather than a conventional authored treatise. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2015:** *Why Only Us: Language and Evolution*. Collaborative scientific monograph with Robert C. Berwick. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2015:** *What Kind of Creatures Are We?*. Published lecture cycle joining language, cognition, and political philosophy. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)

### Political and social criticism conceived as books, pamphlets, or manifestos

- **1967:** *The Responsibility of Intellectuals*. Political essay first published in *The New York Review of Books*; it operates partly as a manifesto, calling intellectuals to expose state deception, and later generated several book manifestations. [Chomsky.info account](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1969:** *American Power and the New Mandarins*. First major collection of Chomsky’s political essays; its polemical address gives parts of it the force of a manifesto. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1970:** *At War with Asia*. Political essay collection on American warfare in Southeast Asia. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1970:** *Two Essays on Cambodia*. Pamphlet collecting two political essays rather than a newly composed book. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1971:** *Problems of Knowledge and Freedom*. Russell Lectures joining political philosophy and the study of language; a lecture-event subsequently fixed as a book. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1973:** *Counter-Revolutionary Violence: Bloodbaths in Fact and Propaganda*. Collaborative pamphlet with Edward S. Herman; Warner Modular Publications withdrew it after its parent company objected, making suppression part of the object’s history. It became the germ of the later *Political Economy of Human Rights*. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1973:** *For Reasons of State*. Authorial collection of political and linguistic essays. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1974:** *Peace in the Middle East? Reflections on Justice and Nationhood*. Political essay collection, later incorporated into *Middle East Illusions*. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1978:** *“Human Rights” and American Foreign Policy*. Political pamphlet whose quotation marks make its title itself an argumentative act. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1979:** *The Washington Connection and Third World Fascism*. Volume I of *The Political Economy of Human Rights*, coauthored with Edward S. Herman; simultaneously a separate book and half of a larger two-volume obra. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1979:** *After the Cataclysm: Postwar Indochina and the Construction of Imperial Ideology*. Volume II of *The Political Economy of Human Rights*, coauthored with Edward S. Herman. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1982:** *Towards a New Cold War: Essays on the Current Crisis and How We Got There*. Political essay collection. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1983:** *The Fateful Triangle: The United States, Israel and the Palestinians*. Documentary political analysis; later editions contain added material but continue the same obra. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1985:** *Turning the Tide: U.S. Intervention in Central America and the Struggle for Peace*. Political monograph and activist intervention. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1986:** *Pirates and Emperors: International Terrorism in the Real World*. Political monograph later substantially updated as *Pirates and Emperors, Old and New*. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1987:** *On Power and Ideology: The Managua Lectures*. Political lecture-cycle preserved as a book. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1988:** *Manufacturing Consent: The Political Economy of the Mass Media*. Collaborative monograph with Edward S. Herman formulating the propaganda model; the later documentary bearing the opening words of its title was made by other filmmakers, not by Chomsky. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1988:** *The Culture of Terrorism*. Political monograph on U.S. intervention in Central America. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1989:** *Necessary Illusions: Thought Control in Democratic Societies*. Massey Lectures turned book; both spoken event and political-media critique. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1991:** *Deterring Democracy*. Political monograph on postwar American power. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1991:** *Terrorizing the Neighborhood: American Foreign Policy in the Post-Cold War Era*. Political pamphlet. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1992:** *What Uncle Sam Really Wants*. Short political primer assembled for broad circulation; later absorbed into *How the World Works*. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1993:** *Rethinking Camelot: JFK, the Vietnam War, and U.S. Political Culture*. Historical-political monograph. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1993:** *Discurs polític: Tres conferències a Catalunya*. Catalan publication of three political lectures; a local-language book manifestation rather than an English original. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1993:** *Year 501: The Conquest Continues*. Political history and critique organized around the Columbian quincentenary. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1994:** *Secrets, Lies and Democracy*. Short political primer later incorporated into *How the World Works*. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1994:** *World Orders, Old and New*. Political monograph derived partly from lectures. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1996:** *Cómo se reparte la tarta: políticas USA al final del milenio*. Spanish-language political book; its relationship to material published elsewhere makes it a regional compilation-manifestation rather than an easily isolated original work. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1996:** *Powers and Prospects: Reflections on Human Nature and the Social Order*. Collection joining political lectures with reflections on language and human nature. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1997:** *Democracy in a Neoliberal Order: Doctrines and Reality*. Published lecture and political pamphlet. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1997:** *Media Control: The Spectacular Achievements of Propaganda*. Pamphlet assembled from talks; the expanded 2002 publication is a revised manifestation. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1999:** *Acts of Aggression: Policing “Rogue States”*. Collaborative pamphlet containing contributions by Chomsky, Edward Said, and Ramsey Clark; it is a composite, not a solely authored Chomsky work. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1999:** *Profit over People: Neoliberalism and Global Order*. Political essay collection. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1999:** *The New Military Humanism: Lessons from Kosovo*. Political monograph responding to NATO’s Kosovo war. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1999:** *The Umbrella of U.S. Power*. Short political monograph on American policy and the Universal Declaration of Human Rights. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2000:** *A New Generation Draws the Line: Kosovo, East Timor and the Standards of the West*. Political monograph; the 2011 expanded edition continues rather than replaces the original obra. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2000:** *Rogue States: The Rule of Force in World Affairs*. Political essay collection. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2001:** *9-11*. Rapid-response political pamphlet assembled from interviews conducted after the attacks; the 2011 *Was There an Alternative?* edition adds a substantial essay but remains a revised manifestation. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2002:** *Peering into the Abyss of the Future*. Published lecture or pamphlet rather than a full-scale monograph. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2003:** *Objectivity and Liberal Scholarship*. Book publication of an older essay first issued within *American Power and the New Mandarins*; bibliographically separate, intellectually not a new work. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2003:** *Hegemony or Survival: America’s Quest for Global Dominance*. Political monograph. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2003:** *Middle East Illusions*. Composite political book incorporating *Peace in the Middle East?* and later essays; substantially an editorially enlarged manifestation of earlier work. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2004:** *Getting Haiti Right This Time*. Collaborative political book with Paul Farmer and Amy Goodman; Chomsky is a contributor, not its sole maker. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2005:** *El terror como política exterior de Estados Unidos*. Spanish-language political compilation whose status as an autonomous obra is weaker than that of its component texts. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2005:** *Government in the Future*. Book manifestation of Chomsky’s 1970 lecture; the text long predates the volume. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2006:** *Failed States: The Abuse of Power and the Assault on Democracy*. Political monograph. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2007:** *Interventions*. Collection of syndicated political columns, making the book an aggregate manifestation of previously circulating texts. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2010:** *Hopes and Prospects*. Political essay and lecture collection. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2010:** *Making the Future: The Unipolar Imperial Moment*. Collection of political columns; some bibliographies give later publication dates for expanded or differently distributed manifestations. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2010:** *Gaza in Crisis: Reflections on Israel’s War Against the Palestinians*. Collaborative political collection with Ilan Pappé. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2011:** *Power and Terror: Conflict, Hegemony, and the Rule of Force*. Political collection reconfiguring talks and interviews already published in other forms. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2012:** *Occupy*. Short intervention assembled from talks, discussions, and messages associated with the Occupy movement; closer to an activist manifesto than a conventional monograph. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2013:** *On Western Terrorism: From Hiroshima to Drone Warfare*. Book-length dialogue with André Vltchek. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2014:** *On Palestine*. Collaborative dialogue and essay collection with Ilan Pappé, edited by Frank Barat. It extends rather than duplicates *Gaza in Crisis*. [Haymarket Books](https://www.haymarketbooks.org/books/620-on-palestine)
- **2015:** *Because We Say So*. Collection of previously published political columns. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2016:** *Who Rules the World?*. Political essay collection rather than a single newly composed argument. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2017:** *Requiem for the American Dream: The Ten Principles of Concentration of Wealth and Power*. Book constructed from the interviews underlying the film of the same name; it is a textual manifestation of a multimedia project, while the film itself was directed by Peter D. Hutchison, Kelly Nyks, and Jared P. Scott. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2018:** *Yugoslavia: Peace, War, and Dissolution*. Davor Džalto’s edited collection of Chomsky’s earlier writings and interviews; an editorial archive-object more than a new author-composed book.
- **2019:** *Internationalism or Extinction*. Brief manifesto-like book on nuclear war, climate change, and international solidarity, developed around a lecture and interviews. [Chomsky.info account](https://chomsky.info/20200225/)
- **2020:** *Climate Crisis and the Global Green New Deal: The Political Economy of Saving the Planet*. Collaborative argument with Robert Pollin, presented through conversations moderated by C. J. Polychroniou. [Chomsky.info account](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2020/2021:** *Consequences of Capitalism: Manufacturing Discontent and Resistance*. Collaborative book with Marv Waterstone based principally on their University of Arizona course lectures; sources variously associate it with its 2020 composition/release cycle and 2021 trade publication. [Chomsky.info account](https://chomsky.info/20210514/)
- **2021:** *The Precipice: Neoliberalism, the Pandemic and the Urgent Need for Radical Change*. C. J. Polychroniou’s collection of interviews from the Trump years; Chomsky is the speaker, but the editor shaped the book-object. [Chomsky.info account](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2022:** *The Withdrawal: Iraq, Libya, Afghanistan, and the Fragility of U.S. Power*. Book-length conversation with Vijay Prashad; a jointly made dialogic work. [Publisher record](https://thenewpress.com/books/withdrawal)
- **2022:** *Notes on Resistance*. Collection of interviews conducted by David Barsamian; the spoken answers are Chomsky’s, while selection and book form are collaborative. [Haymarket Books](https://www.haymarketbooks.org/books/1769-notes-on-resistance)
- **2023:** *Illegitimate Authority: Facing the Challenges of Our Time*. C. J. Polychroniou’s edited collection of interviews; it resists classification as a newly authored monograph because its obra-form lies partly in editorial selection.
- **2024:** *On Cuba: Reflections on 70 Years of Revolution and Struggle*. Collaborative political dialogue with Vijay Prashad; some markets issued translated manifestations under different titles.
- **2024:** *The Myth of American Idealism: How U.S. Foreign Policy Endangers the World*. Collaborative political book with Nathan J. Robinson; Robinson’s role in organizing and shaping Chomsky’s longstanding analysis makes attribution explicitly joint. [Chomsky.info account](https://chomsky.info/books/)

### Conversations, debates, and lecture-events preserved as books

- **1979:** *Language and Responsibility*. Book based on conversations with Mitsou Ronat, translated from the French; a jointly produced interview-text rather than an ordinary monograph. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1980:** *Language and Learning: The Debate between Jean Piaget and Noam Chomsky*. Massimo Piattelli-Palmarini’s edited record of a multi-participant debate; Chomsky made contributions to the event but did not make the whole book. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1982:** *Noam Chomsky on the Generative Enterprise*. Extended discussion with Riny Huybregts and Henk van Riemsdijk; the revised 2004 *Generative Enterprise Revisited* adds new interlocutors and framing. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1992:** *Chronicles of Dissent*. Interviews with David Barsamian; conversational archive shaped by both speaker and interviewer. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1993:** *The Prosperous Few and the Restless Many*. Interviews with David Barsamian, later incorporated into *How the World Works*. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1994:** *Keeping the Rabble in Line*. Interviews with David Barsamian. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1995:** *Sprache als Organ, Sprache als Lebensform*. German-language dialogue with Günther Grewendorf on linguistics and politics; a collaboratively framed work with no exact original English book-equivalent. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1996:** *Class Warfare*. Interviews with David Barsamian. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1998:** *Noam Chomsky habla de América Latina*. Interviews with Heinz Dieterich, later republished under *Hablemos de terrorismo*; the changing title makes the book’s identity edition-dependent. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2001:** *Propaganda and the Public Mind*. Interviews with David Barsamian. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2002:** *The Common Good*. Interviews with David Barsamian; although Chomsky.info dates this book to 2002, an earlier Odonian edition is commonly dated 1998, so the first-publication date is bibliographically disputed. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2002:** *Understanding Power: The Indispensable Chomsky*. Peter R. Mitchell and John Schoeffel’s edited transcripts of public discussions; the editors, not Chomsky alone, made the book’s arrangement and extensive notes. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2003:** *Bush y los años del miedo*. Spanish-language conversations with Jorge Halperín. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2003:** *Power and Terror: Post-9/11 Talks and Interviews*. John Junkerman and Takei Masakazu’s edited textual companion to recorded appearances; a cross-media archive rather than a single authored argument. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2005:** *Imperial Ambitions: Conversations on the Post-9/11 World*. Interviews with David Barsamian. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2005:** *A Hated Political Enemy*. Published interview conducted by Allen Bell. [Bibliographic record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noam_Chomsky_bibliography_and_filmography)
- **2006:** *The Chomsky–Foucault Debate: On Human Nature*. Book manifestation of Chomsky’s 1971 televised debate with Michel Foucault; the debate is the event, while the 2006 volume is a later editorial object. [Bibliographic record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noam_Chomsky_bibliography_and_filmography)
- **2006:** *Perilous Power*. Dialogues with Gilbert Achcar, edited by Stephen R. Shalom. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2007:** *What We Say Goes*. Interviews with David Barsamian. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2009:** *Of Minds and Language: A Dialogue with Noam Chomsky in the Basque Country*. Multi-author conference volume edited by Massimo Piattelli-Palmarini, Juan Uriagereka, and Pello Salaburu; Chomsky is the central interlocutor but not maker of the entire book. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2013:** *Power Systems*. Interviews with David Barsamian. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2017:** *Global Discontents: Conversations on the Rising Threats to Democracy*. Twelve interviews with David Barsamian. [Chomsky.info account](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2017:** *Optimism over Despair: On Capitalism, Empire, and Social Change*. C. J. Polychroniou’s edited sequence of interviews. [Chomsky.info account](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2020:** *Chomsky for Activists*. Charles Derber and Suren Moodliar’s interview-based account of Chomsky’s activist experience; authorship of the book-object is shared and editorial. [Chomsky.info account](https://chomsky.info/books/)

### Retrospective collections and editorial constructions

- **1971:** *Chomsky: Selected Readings*. J. P. B. Allen and Paul Van Buren’s selection of Chomsky’s linguistic texts; Chomsky made the components, but the editors made the collection. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1981:** *Radical Priorities*. Carlos P. Otero’s edited selection of political writings; later editions alter its contents. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1987:** *The Chomsky Reader*. James Peck’s edited anthology; it is a manifestation of an oeuvre, not a new unitary work by Chomsky. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **1998:** *Language and Politics*. Carlos P. Otero’s edited collection of interviews and essays; later editions expand and rearrange it. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2000:** *The Architecture of Language*. Nirmalangshu Mukherji, Bibudhendra Narayan Patnaik, and Rama Kant Agnihotri’s edited lectures and discussion. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2003:** *Chomsky on Democracy and Education*. Carlos P. Otero’s edited collection; the book’s architecture is Otero’s, while the component writings and interviews are Chomsky’s. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2004:** *Chomsky on Miseducation*. Donaldo Macedo’s edited assemblage of Chomsky texts and framing material. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2004:** *Letters from Lexington: Reflections on Propaganda*. Collection of Chomsky’s letters and short texts; an archival object whose book form is editorial. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2005:** *Chomsky on Anarchism*. Barry Pateman’s edited anthology of writings and interviews from several decades. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2008:** *The Essential Chomsky*. Anthony Arnove’s edited anthology; the title names a constructed selection, not a work conceived by Chomsky as a whole. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2010:** *New World of Indigenous Resistance: Noam Chomsky and Voices from North, South, and Central America*. Lois Meyer and Benjamín Maldonado Alvarado’s polyphonic edited volume; Chomsky’s essay and responses participate in, but do not exhaust, the obra. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2010:** *Chomsky Notebook*. Jean Bricmont and Julie Franck’s edited collection of Chomsky texts and responses by other writers; attribution of the total book to Chomsky alone would be misleading. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2011:** *How the World Works*. Compilation of *What Uncle Sam Really Wants*, *The Prosperous Few and the Restless Many*, *Secrets, Lies and Democracy*, and *The Common Good*; a new container, not four new texts. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2014:** *Masters of Mankind: Essays and Lectures, 1969–2013*. Retrospective collection of previously dispersed writings and lectures. [Chomsky.info chronology](https://chomsky.info/books/)
- **2017:** *The Responsibility of Intellectuals*. Book pairing the 1967 essay with later reflections, including “The Responsibility of Intellectuals Redux”; a retrospective constellation rather than a wholly new text. [Chomsky.info account](https://chomsky.info/books/)

### Corpus, project, and manifestations that resist itemization

- **1951–1986:** *Writings in Linguistics and Related Fields*. Not one work but a documented body of articles, reviews, lectures, books, replies, and translations catalogued by E. F. K. Koerner and Matsuji Tajima; their bibliography explicitly presents itself as a personal Werkbibliographie and acknowledges that omissions may remain. [Koerner–Tajima bibliography](https://doi.org/10.1075/lisl.11)
- **1960s–2023:** *Political writings, talks, letters, interviews, and discussions*. A continuing public-intellectual project numbering in the hundreds or thousands of manifestations. No authoritative post-1986 catalogue raisonné fixes its boundaries, while Chomsky.info separately indexes books, articles, talks, debates, letters, interviews, audio, and video; calling this shifting corpus a finite list of “works” would create false completeness. [Chomsky.info](https://chomsky.info/) [Koerner–Tajima scope](https://benjamins.com/catalog/lisl.11)
- **1950s–present:** *Generative grammar*. A research project rather than a single text: successive transformational, government-and-binding, principles-and-parameters, and minimalist models revise one another across manuscripts, articles, lectures, and books. Its unfinished and self-revising status is part of the obra.
- **1960s–present:** *Critique of concentrated power and manufactured consent*. A political-intellectual project distributed across books, pamphlets, columns, correspondence, teaching, interviews, and activism; it has no single fixed title or terminal form.
- **Recorded appearances, various dates:** *Audio and video talks, debates, and interviews*. These are performances and documentary manifestations involving Chomsky, but authorship of each recording also belongs to interviewers, organizers, broadcasters, editors, and filmmakers; mere appearance in a film is not evidence that Chomsky made the film. [Chomsky.info media index](https://chomsky.info/)
- **1992–2017:** *Manufacturing Consent: Noam Chomsky and the Media*, *Power and Terror*, *Requiem for the American Dream*, and other Chomsky-centered documentaries. These resist inclusion as works “by” Chomsky: he supplied speech and screen presence, but the cited filmography attributes direction and production to others. [Filmography and credits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noam_Chomsky_bibliography_and_filmography)
