## Works by Juana Azurduy de Padilla

### Military-political projects

- **1811–1816:** *Los Leales*. Insurgent force organized by Azurduy and Manuel Asencio Padilla from Indigenous and mestizo combatants; some accounts distinguish within it an all-female cavalry guard called *Las Amazonas*. These were collective military formations—not works in the conventional sense—and the surviving evidence does not permit every organizational decision to be assigned separately to Azurduy. ([Wexler, “Juana Azurduy Llanos y las mujeres del ejército revolucionario”](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/descargaPdf/juana-azurduy-llanos-y-las-mujeres-del-ejercito-revolucionario-1337128/); [Torres, *Juana Asurdui de Padilla*](https://es.scribd.com/document/612812579/Juana-Asurdui-de-Padilla-1780-1862-Benjamin-Torres))

- **1813–1817:** *Guerrilla de La Laguna*, subsequently called the *Republiqueta de La Laguna*. A mobile insurgent territory and fighting organization jointly maintained by Azurduy, Padilla, local communities, and subordinate commanders. It resists classification as Azurduy’s individual obra: “republiqueta” is a later historiographical name, leadership was shared, and after Padilla’s death the force fragmented among several commanders. ([Demélas, *Nacimiento de la guerra de guerrilla: el diario de José Santos Vargas, 1810–1825*](https://books.google.com/books?id=R1F7AAAAMAAJ); [Torres](https://es.scribd.com/document/612812579/Juana-Asurdui-de-Padilla-1780-1862-Benjamin-Torres))

### Petitions and representations

- **29 April 1825:** *Solicitud a la Sala de Representantes de Salta*. A petition asking the provincial government for the means to return to Chuquisaca; simultaneously an administrative instrument and a retrospective account of her revolutionary service. Its text survives through documentary publication rather than as a separately titled literary work. ([Torres](https://es.scribd.com/document/612812579/Juana-Asurdui-de-Padilla-1780-1862-Benjamin-Torres); [Güemes, *Güemes documentado*, vol. 12](https://bibliotecadigital.camdipsalta.gov.ar/handle/123456789/538))

- **5 August 1825:** *Solicitud de calificación de méritos y servicios al Ayuntamiento de La Plata*. A formal representation asking the municipality to certify her and her late husband’s contributions to independence. It is a performative legal text intended to produce official recognition, not a memoir despite its autobiographical content. ([Torres](https://es.scribd.com/document/612812579/Juana-Asurdui-de-Padilla-1780-1862-Benjamin-Torres))

- **18 October 1825:** *Solicitud a Andrés de Santa Cruz para la devolución de documentos*. A signed administrative petition seeking the return of original papers submitted in support of her montepío claim; the archival record is AGN Argentina, Sala VII, colección Juan Ángel Farini, no. 214, fols. 144–162. ([Torres](https://es.scribd.com/document/612812579/Juana-Asurdui-de-Padilla-1780-1862-Benjamin-Torres))

### Receipts and declarations

- **19 May 1820:** *Recibo de ayuda económica del gobernador Aráoz*. A fiscal acknowledgment associated with twelve pesos granted for Azurduy’s illness, preserved in the Archivo Histórico de Tucumán. It is her documentary act, although the surviving form belongs equally to the government’s accounting system. ([Torres](https://es.scribd.com/document/612812579/Juana-Asurdui-de-Padilla-1780-1862-Benjamin-Torres))

- **2 August 1825:** *Recibo de cien pesos de auxilio*. A brief signed acknowledgment of emergency assistance ordered by Antonio José de Sucre. Torres notes that the available witness is a copy made in 1927, so the original’s survival and exact material form are uncertain. ([Torres](https://es.scribd.com/document/612812579/Juana-Asurdui-de-Padilla-1780-1862-Benjamin-Torres))

- **19 September 1827:** *Declaración como pobre de solemnidad*. A sworn juridical declaration made to obtain the procedural status of an indigent litigant. It is neither literary obra nor simple description: the declaration acts within a lawsuit by changing Azurduy’s legal standing. ([Torres](https://es.scribd.com/document/612812579/Juana-Asurdui-de-Padilla-1780-1862-Benjamin-Torres))

### Powers, contracts, and property instruments

- **3 December 1825:** *Poder otorgado a Gregorio Asurdui*. A legal power authorizing Gregorio Asurdui to act for her. Like the other notarial instruments, it was made through a scribe and witnesses; Azurduy is the juridical maker and grantor, not necessarily the verbal composer. ([Torres](https://es.scribd.com/document/612812579/Juana-Asurdui-de-Padilla-1780-1862-Benjamin-Torres))

- **11 May 1826:** *Venta de Pampaguasi, Loctoni, Chuspipampa, Lupeara, La Majada y Paclani*. A property transaction transferring six estates to Rosalía Asurdui Llanos and Manuel Párraga for 440 pesos. The documentary biography records the transaction but does not present it as a fixed-title authored work; it belongs here as a jointly made legal act. ([Torres](https://es.scribd.com/document/612812579/Juana-Asurdui-de-Padilla-1780-1862-Benjamin-Torres))

- **14 July 1832:** *Arrendamiento de la hacienda y molinos de Cullcu a Manuel Sandi*. A four-year notarial lease made jointly by Azurduy and Sandi for an annual rent of 400 pesos. Its operative clauses make it a protocol governing land, mills, tools, repairs, and payment rather than a descriptive text. ([Torres](https://es.scribd.com/document/612812579/Juana-Asurdui-de-Padilla-1780-1862-Benjamin-Torres))

- **25 October 1842:** *Compromiso con Pedro Zuleta para la entrega de Tipabuco*. A jointly signed settlement regulating the return of an estate, rent, crops, and obligations between Azurduy and her son-in-law. It is explicitly contractual and therefore resists attribution as her sole composition. ([Torres](https://es.scribd.com/document/612812579/Juana-Asurdui-de-Padilla-1780-1862-Benjamin-Torres))

- **4 May 1843:** *Obligación de pago a favor de Agustín Noya*. A signed debt instrument secured by a mortgage on Cullcu. Its language was produced in notarial form, so Azurduy’s authorship consists in undertaking and authenticating the obligation rather than necessarily drafting its wording. ([Torres](https://es.scribd.com/document/612812579/Juana-Asurdui-de-Padilla-1780-1862-Benjamin-Torres))

- **30 March 1860:** *Declaración de Pedro Vicente Carrasco sobre la compra de Robledo*. A notarial declaration confirming that Carrasco had purchased Robledo for Azurduy; she accepted the incorporated declaration, but a witness signed at her request because of her failing sight. It is a jointly constituted legal instrument, not a text straightforwardly authored by her. ([Torres](https://es.scribd.com/document/612812579/Juana-Asurdui-de-Padilla-1780-1862-Benjamin-Torres))

### Object of uncertain makerhood

- **Undated, before 1862:** *Wooden chest associated with Juana Azurduy*. A surviving chest donated in 1917 to the Sociedad Geográfica y de Historia de Sucre and held at the Casa de la Libertad. The documentary catalogue establishes ownership or association, not that Azurduy designed or made it; it therefore resists inclusion as her obra and is recorded only to prevent provenance from being mistaken for authorship. ([Torres](https://es.scribd.com/document/612812579/Juana-Asurdui-de-Padilla-1780-1862-Benjamin-Torres))

### Sources

- Norberto Benjamín Torres, *Juana Asurdui de Padilla (1780–1862): la historia detrás de la leyenda*. This source-critical documentary biography supplies archival references and reproduces the known surviving documents; its appendix is the closest available equivalent to a documentary catalogue, rather than a conventional collected-works edition. [Digitized text](https://es.scribd.com/document/612812579/Juana-Asurdui-de-Padilla-1780-1862-Benjamin-Torres).

- Mercedes H. Sánchez, *Juana Azurduy y la revolución altoperuana*. Its documentary appendix includes Azurduy’s 1825 Salta petition and contemporary records concerning her military service. [Centro Juana Azurduy PDF](https://www.centrojuanaazurduy.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Juana_Azurduy_y_la_revolucion_Altoperuana_2001.pdf).

- Berta Wexler, “Juana Azurduy Llanos y las mujeres del ejército revolucionario,” a scholarly study of Azurduy’s military organization and the women associated with it. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes PDF](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/descargaPdf/juana-azurduy-llanos-y-las-mujeres-del-ejercito-revolucionario-1337128/).

- Marie-Danielle Demélas, *Nacimiento de la guerra de guerrilla: el diario de José Santos Vargas (1810–1825)*, used to contextualize the collective and historiographically unstable category of the “republiqueta.” [Bibliographic preview](https://books.google.com/books?id=R1F7AAAAMAAJ).
