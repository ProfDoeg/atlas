## Works by Sigmund Freud

### Zoological, anatomical, neurological, and medical writings

- **1877:** *Observations on the Form and Fine Structure of Looped Organs of the Eel, Organs Considered as Testes*. Zoological research paper, Freud’s first publication; later summarized in his own bibliography of his scientific writings. [FB; SE 3]
- **1878:** *On the Origin of the Posterior Nerve Roots in the Spinal Cord of Ammocoetes*. Neuroanatomical paper with an illustrative plate. [FB; SE 3]
- **1879:** *Notice on a Method for Anatomical Preparations of the Nervous System*. Laboratory-method note rather than a psychoanalytic work. [FB]
- **1882:** *On the Structure of the Nerve Fibres and Nerve Cells in the River Crayfish*. Histological paper. [FB; SE 3]
- **1884:** *A New Histological Method for the Study of Nerve-Tracts in the Brain and Spinal Cord*. Technical research paper introducing a staining method. [FB; SE 3]
- **1884:** *On Coca*. Pharmacological monograph advocating medical uses of cocaine; its clinical claims and Freud’s responsibility in the early cocaine episode remain subjects of historical dispute. [FB; SE 3]
- **1885:** *Contribution to the Knowledge of the Effects of Coca*. Experimental pharmacological paper. [FB; SE 3]
- **1885:** *Remarks on Cocaine Addiction and the Fear of Cocaine*. Polemical medical intervention defending cocaine’s therapeutic use. [FB; SE 3]
- **1885:** *A Case of Cerebral Haemorrhage with Indirect Basal Focal Symptoms in a Patient Suffering from Scurvy*. Clinical neurological case report. [FB; SE 3]
- **1885:** *On the General Effect of Cocaine*. Short pharmacological paper. [FB; SE 3]
- **1886:** *Acute Multiple Neuritis of the Spinal and Cranial Nerves*. Clinical neurological paper. [FB; SE 3]
- **1886:** *Report on My Studies in Paris and Berlin*. Institutional report on Freud’s study under Charcot and others; written in 1886 but first published in 1956. [SE 1]
- **1886:** *Observation of a Severe Case of Hemi-Anaesthesia in a Hysterical Male*. Clinical demonstration and case report. [SE 1]
- **1886:** *On the Relationship of the Restiform Body to the Posterior Column and Its Nucleus, with Remarks on Two Fields of the Medulla Oblongata*. Neuroanatomical paper. [FB; SE 3]
- **1886:** *On the Origin of the Acoustic Nerve*. Neuroanatomical paper. [FB; SE 3]
- **1887:** *Review of Averbeck’s Die akute Neurasthenie*. Medical book review. [SE 1]
- **1887:** *Review of Weir Mitchell’s Die Behandlung gewisser Formen von Neurasthenie und Hysterie*. Medical book review. [SE 1]
- **1887:** *Review of Laufenauer on Hystero-Epilepsy in Boys*. Review whose place in the Freud bibliography rests on attribution research rather than a signed publication. [FB]
- **1888:** *Hysteria*. Encyclopaedia article, with an appendix on hystero-epilepsy. [SE 1]
- **1888:** *Brain*. Encyclopaedia article; one of several unsigned or editorially attributed medical reference entries. [FB]
- **1888:** *Aphasia*. Encyclopaedia article whose bibliographical status depends upon attribution. [FB]
- **1888:** *Paralysis*. Encyclopaedia article whose bibliographical status depends upon attribution. [FB]
- **1888–1893:** *Some Points for a Comparative Study of Organic and Hysterical Motor Paralyses*. Neurological paper written over several years and published in French in 1893. [SE 1]
- **1889:** *Review of August Forel’s Hypnotism*. Extended critical review. [SE 1]
- **1891:** *On Aphasia: A Critical Study*. Neurological monograph and theoretical intervention into contemporary localization theories. [FB]
- **1891:** *Hypnosis*. Encyclopaedia article that also functions as a clinical protocol for hypnotic treatment. [SE 1]
- **1892–1893:** *A Case of Successful Treatment by Hypnotism*. Clinical case report. [SE 1]
- **1893:** *Charcot*. Memorial and intellectual portrait. [SE 3]
- **1897:** *Abstracts of the Scientific Writings of Dr. Sigmund Freud, 1877–1897*. Freud’s annotated retrospective bibliography; it is simultaneously a text and a catalogue of earlier texts. [SE 3]

### Translations, editorial apparatus, and translated books

- **1886:** *German Translation of J.-M. Charcot’s Lectures on the Diseases of the Nervous System, Volume III*. A translated medical book with Freud’s preface and notes; Charcot, not Freud, authored the lectures. [SE 1; FB]
- **1888–1889:** *German Translation of Hippolyte Bernheim’s Suggestion and Its Applications to Therapy*. A translated medical book with Freud’s preface; a second-edition preface followed later. [SE 1]
- **1892–1894:** *German Translation of Charcot’s Tuesday Lectures*. A translated lecture collection supplied with Freud’s preface and extensive footnotes. [SE 1]
- **1892–1893:** *German Translation of Bernheim’s New Studies on Hypnotism, Suggestion and Psychotherapy*. Translation rather than an independently authored treatise. [FB]
- **1880–1881:** *Translations of Four Essays by John Stuart Mill*. German translations concerning women’s emancipation, Plato, labour, and socialism, prepared for Theodor Gomperz’s edition of Mill; these resist classification as Freud-authored works because the verbal object mediates Mill’s authorship. [FB]

### Foundational collaborations and case histories

- **1893:** *On the Psychical Mechanism of Hysterical Phenomena: Preliminary Communication*. Collaborative theoretical paper by Josef Breuer and Freud. [SE 2]
- **1893:** *On the Psychical Mechanism of Hysterical Phenomena: A Lecture*. Freud’s lecture-version of the emerging cathartic theory. [SE 3]
- **1893–1895:** *Studies on Hysteria*. Collaborative book by Breuer and Freud; its chapters and cases have separately assigned authorship, so it is not a uniformly co-written object. [SE 2]
- **1893–1895:** *Frau Emmy von N.*. Freud’s case history within *Studies on Hysteria*. [SE 2]
- **1893–1895:** *Miss Lucy R.*. Freud’s case history within *Studies on Hysteria*. [SE 2]
- **1893–1895:** *Katharina*. Freud’s case history within *Studies on Hysteria*. [SE 2]
- **1893–1895:** *Fräulein Elisabeth von R.*. Freud’s case history within *Studies on Hysteria*. [SE 2]
- **1895:** *The Psychotherapy of Hysteria*. Freud’s methodological chapter in *Studies on Hysteria*. [SE 2]
- **1901; published 1905:** *Fragment of an Analysis of a Case of Hysteria (“Dora”)*. Case history deliberately presented as a fragment; its incompleteness and retrospective construction are part of the object’s form. [SE 7]
- **1909:** *Analysis of a Phobia in a Five-Year-Old Boy (“Little Hans”)*. Case history mediated through reports by the child’s father; Freud’s authorship is therefore editorial and interpretive as well as clinical. [SE 10]
- **1909:** *Notes upon a Case of Obsessional Neurosis (“Rat Man”)*. Published case history. [SE 10]
- **1907–1908; published 1955:** *Original Record of the “Rat Man” Case*. Surviving clinical notes rather than a work Freud prepared for publication. [SE 10]
- **1911:** *Psycho-Analytic Notes on an Autobiographical Account of a Case of Paranoia (“Schreber”)*. Case study constructed from Daniel Paul Schreber’s published memoir rather than direct treatment. [SE 12]
- **1914; published 1918:** *From the History of an Infantile Neurosis (“Wolf Man”)*. Retrospective case history whose publication date and narrated treatment period differ. [SE 17]
- **1915:** *A Case of Paranoia Running Counter to the Psycho-Analytic Theory of the Disease*. Short case report and theoretical test case. [SE 14]
- **1920:** *The Psychogenesis of a Case of Homosexuality in a Woman*. Case history and theoretical essay; its clinical framing and terminology belong to its historical setting. [SE 18]
- **1923:** *A Seventeenth-Century Demonological Neurosis*. Historical case reconstruction from an archival manuscript. [SE 19]

### Psychoanalytic books and extended obra

- **1899 [dated 1900]:** *The Interpretation of Dreams*. Book-length theoretical and autobiographical obra; the title page bore 1900 although publication occurred in November 1899, and Freud repeatedly revised later editions. [SE 4–5; FB]
- **1901:** *On Dreams*. Condensed exposition of dream theory, not merely an edition of *The Interpretation of Dreams*. [SE 5]
- **1901:** *The Psychopathology of Everyday Life*. Book assembled and repeatedly expanded from studies of forgetting, slips, and symptomatic actions. [SE 6]
- **1905:** *Jokes and Their Relation to the Unconscious*. Book-length investigation of joke technique and psychical economy. [SE 8]
- **1905:** *Three Essays on the Theory of Sexuality*. Three-part theoretical book repeatedly and substantially revised; no single edition transparently represents an unchanged original work. [SE 7]
- **1907:** *Delusions and Dreams in Jensen’s Gradiva*. Book-length literary and psychoanalytic study, with a 1912 postscript. [SE 9]
- **1910:** *Leonardo da Vinci and a Memory of His Childhood*. Psychobiographical study; its factual premises, including the celebrated translation of Leonardo’s bird-memory, have been disputed. [SE 11]
- **1912–1913:** *Totem and Taboo*. Four linked essays issued as a book connecting psychoanalysis, anthropology, and conjectural prehistory. [SE 13]
- **1916–1917:** *Introductory Lectures on Psycho-Analysis*. Twenty-eight lectures forming a pedagogical work, although publication rather than live delivery fixes the received text. [SE 15–16]
- **1920:** *Beyond the Pleasure Principle*. Speculative metapsychological book. [SE 18]
- **1921:** *Group Psychology and the Analysis of the Ego*. Book-length social-psychological study. [SE 18]
- **1923:** *The Ego and the Id*. Metapsychological book introducing a revised structural model. [SE 19]
- **1925:** *An Autobiographical Study*. Intellectual autobiography, supplemented by a 1935 postscript. [SE 20]
- **1925–1926:** *Inhibitions, Symptoms and Anxiety*. Book-length revision of Freud’s theory of anxiety. [SE 20]
- **1926:** *The Question of Lay Analysis*. Dialogic polemic defending psychoanalysis by non-physicians, with a 1927 postscript. [SE 20]
- **1927:** *The Future of an Illusion*. Book-length critique of religion and account of civilization. [SE 21]
- **1930:** *Civilization and Its Discontents*. Book-length cultural and metapsychological essay. [SE 21]
- **1932; published 1933:** *New Introductory Lectures on Psycho-Analysis*. Seven written lectures continuing the earlier sequence; they were not delivered and therefore resist classification as records of spoken events. [SE 22]
- **1934–1938; published 1937–1939:** *Moses and Monotheism: Three Essays*. Composite, repeatedly reworked historical-speculative project published in stages and then as a book. [SE 23]
- **1938; published 1940:** *An Outline of Psycho-Analysis*. Unfinished synthetic book, published posthumously; the project’s incompletion is integral to its status. [SE 23]

### Clinical and metapsychological projects left unpublished

- **1892:** *Sketches for the Preliminary Communication*. Three preparatory texts—a letter to Breuer, the fragment conventionally titled “III,” and *On the Theory of Hysterical Attacks*—published only in 1940–1941. They are working documents, not a finished publication. [SE 1]
