## Works by Charlotte Bara

### Solo dances and choreographic miniatures

- **1917:** *Tanz der Mumie* (*Danse de la momie*; Dance of the Mummy). A solo choreography in Bara’s first Brussels programme; surviving programmes and costume records use both German and French titles, but no film of the dance survives. ([Monte Verità historical survey](https://www.elisarion.ch/en/print.html/monte_verita/monte_verita/du_oktober_1978/tanz_koerperferien.html); [Fondazione Monte Verità collection inventory](https://andreasschwab.ch/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/Quaderno-BSSI_12_total.pdf))
- **By 1920:** *Danse arabe*. A solo danced to music from Grieg’s second *Peer Gynt* suite and documented in Willem Pijper’s review of Bara’s Utrecht programme on 22 December 1920. ([DBNL critical edition](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/pijp004papi01_01/pijp004papi01_01_0252.php))
- **By 1920:** *Les misérables*. A solo probably performed to Couperin’s *Les fastes de la grande et ancienne Ménestrandise*; that musical identification is the modern editor’s inference, not a secure attribution by Bara. ([DBNL critical edition](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/pijp004papi01_01/pijp004papi01_01_0252.php))
- **By 1920:** *Papillon mourant* / *Sterbender Schmetterling*. A solo “dying butterfly,” documented in the 1920 Utrecht programme and performed in Locarno in 1922 to Chopin; the French and German titles appear to designate the same choreography. ([DBNL critical edition](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/pijp004papi01_01/pijp004papi01_01_0252.php); [Monte Verità historical survey](https://www.elisarion.ch/en/print.html/monte_verita/monte_verita/du_oktober_1978/tanz_koerperferien.html))
- **1920:** *Auferstehung*. A religious solo, performed without music in at least one Berlin appearance; an archival role portrait identifies Bara as the Madonna and elsewhere associates the work with Chopin, so its accompaniment evidently varied or the records conflate different performances. ([Deutsches Tanzarchiv Köln collection record](https://faust.sk-kultur.de/hzeig.FAU?desk=%23000001BD%23&dm=1&sid=A04AF870745&thes=5); [contemporary notice reproduced by the Staatliches Institut für Musikforschung](https://digital.sim.spk-berlin.de/viewer/image/775084921-02/199/))
- **1920:** *Totentanz* / *Makabrer Tanz* (*Danse macabre*). A solo choreography rather than the similarly titled lithographic portfolio made about Bara by Hans Windisch; scholarship dates the dance to 1920, while a 1928 review indicates that it remained in her repertory. ([University of California Press, *Empire of Ecstasy*](https://publishing.cdlib.org/ucpressebooks/public/book/empire-of-ecstasy-nudity-and-movement-in-german-body-culture-1910-1935.html); [Deutsches Tanzarchiv Köln](https://www.deutsches-tanzarchiv.de/archiv/nachlaesse-sammlungen/charlotte-bara/))
- **1920:** *Zu Ehren von Fra Angelico*. A choreographic homage derived from Bara’s study of the mystical gestures in Fra Angelico’s paintings: an embodied interpretation of images, not a written art-historical text. ([Deutsches Tanzarchiv Köln, *Tanz und Bild*](https://www.deutsches-tanzarchiv.de/fileadmin/user_upload/www.tanzarchiv-koeln.de/Archivalie_des_Monats/Nele_Lipp/Nele_Lipp_Tanz_Malerei-Ein_Lexikon.pdf))
- **By 1922:** *Ägyptischer Tanz* / *L’égyptienne*. An Egyptian-themed solo performed at the Kursaal in Locarno in 1922; archival descriptions also preserve its costume, but do not establish that it was identical to *Tanz der Mumie*. ([Monte Verità historical survey](https://www.elisarion.ch/en/print.html/monte_verita/monte_verita/du_oktober_1978/tanz_koerperferien.html); [Fondazione Monte Verità collection inventory](https://andreasschwab.ch/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/Quaderno-BSSI_12_total.pdf))
- **Date not fixed:** *Vanité des Vanités*. A named choreography in Bara’s touring repertory; the accessible museum documentation does not assign it a secure premiere date. ([Museo Castello San Materno](https://www.museoascona.ch/de/ausstellungen/charlotte-bara))
- **Date not fixed:** *Hinduistische Vision*. A solo or choreographic manifestation belonging to Bara’s “Oriental” repertory; its precise date and relation to her broader temple-dance programmes remain unstated in the museum account. ([Museo Castello San Materno](https://www.museoascona.ch/de/ausstellungen/charlotte-bara))
- **Date not fixed:** *L’adoration au temple*. A temple dance known through photographs/postcards and a surviving costume. Those remnants document a performed bodily work, not a recoverable complete choreography. ([Fondazione Monte Verità collection inventory](https://andreasschwab.ch/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/Quaderno-BSSI_12_total.pdf))
- **Date not fixed:** *Invocation au temple*. A temple dance documented by postcards and by a costume also associated with *Danse de la momie* and *Judith*; whether that shared costume implies a connected cycle is not established. ([Fondazione Monte Verità collection inventory](https://andreasschwab.ch/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/Quaderno-BSSI_12_total.pdf))
- **Date not fixed:** *Judith*. A biblical dance documented photographically and through a shared costume. The surviving collection description supplies neither a premiere date nor enough information to reconstruct the choreography. ([Fondazione Monte Verità collection inventory](https://andreasschwab.ch/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/Quaderno-BSSI_12_total.pdf))
- **Date not fixed:** *Danse primitive*. A named solo preserved in a postcard image; “primitive” is Bara’s period category, not a defensible modern description of a culture or dance form. ([Fondazione Monte Verità collection inventory](https://andreasschwab.ch/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/Quaderno-BSSI_12_total.pdf))
- **Date not fixed:** *La fée de la rose rouge (Promenade du soir en Espagne)*. A dance documented by a postcard; the parenthetical phrase may be a subtitle or programme description, and the archival notice does not resolve which. ([Fondazione Monte Verità collection inventory](https://andreasschwab.ch/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/Quaderno-BSSI_12_total.pdf))
- **Date not fixed:** *Das versunkene Kathedrale* / *Die versunkene Kathedrale*. A mystical solo known from the historical literature and photographic documentation; the title is reported with grammatical variation, so no normalized form should be treated as definitively authorial. ([Moissey Kogan Catalogue Raisonné research essay](https://moisseykogancr.org/themes-and-intimations-moissey-kogans-prints-for-the-die-schaffenden-portfolios-part-1/))
- **Date not fixed:** *Gothischer Tanz*. A Gothicizing choreography built from angular, devotional gestures inspired by medieval visual art. It belongs to Bara’s recurrent mode of dancing religious images and may resist separation from programmes advertised more generally as her “Gothic dances.” ([Moissey Kogan Catalogue Raisonné research essay](https://moisseykogancr.org/themes-and-intimations-moissey-kogans-prints-for-the-die-schaffenden-portfolios-part-1/))

### Dance dramas, cycles, and ensemble manifestations

- **1930:** *Aufstand der Engel* (Revolt of the Angels). A staged dance work for which Bara reportedly enlisted Benedictine monks; the surviving secondary account does not specify their exact creative or performing role. ([Gabriella Vincze dissertation](https://doktori.btk.elte.hu/art/vinczegabriella/diss.pdf))
- **1932:** *Die Visionen der Jeanne d’Arc*. A dance drama or choreographic cycle, created after Bara’s work expanded beyond solos and involved her Ascona ensemble. ([Monte Verità historical survey](https://www.elisarion.ch/en/print.html/monte_verita/monte_verita/du_oktober_1978/tanz_koerperferien.html); [Gabriella Vincze dissertation](https://doktori.btk.elte.hu/art/vinczegabriella/diss.pdf))
- **1934:** *Das verlorene Paradies*. An ensemble-oriented religious dance work presented at the Teatro San Materno; it is variously discussed as a dance, drama, or sequence of danced images rather than as a stable literary text. ([Monte Verità historical survey](https://www.elisarion.ch/en/print.html/monte_verita/monte_verita/du_oktober_1978/tanz_koerperferien.html); [Gabriella Vincze dissertation](https://doktori.btk.elte.hu/art/vinczegabriella/diss.pdf))
- **1934:** *Die Versuchung der Wüste*. A religious dance work presented alongside *Das verlorene Paradies*. Its precise scale, music, and division into scenes are not supplied by the accessible work chronology. ([Monte Verità historical survey](https://www.elisarion.ch/en/print.html/monte_verita/monte_verita/du_oktober_1978/tanz_koerperferien.html))
- **1935:** *Alte ägyptische Tänze*. A programme or cycle of “ancient Egyptian dances,” not securely divisible into individually titled works from the surviving chronology. It therefore resists treatment as a single fixed choreography. ([Monte Verità historical survey](https://www.elisarion.ch/en/print.html/monte_verita/monte_verita/du_oktober_1978/tanz_koerperferien.html))
- **1935:** *Bilder aus der Passion*. An Easter cycle of danced Passion images involving Bara’s ensemble; its identity lies between choreography, tableaux, religious manifestation, and Gesamtkunstwerk. ([Monte Verità historical survey](https://www.elisarion.ch/en/print.html/monte_verita/monte_verita/du_oktober_1978/tanz_koerperferien.html); [Gabriella Vincze dissertation](https://doktori.btk.elte.hu/art/vinczegabriella/diss.pdf))
- **1935:** *Mysterientänze aus dem Neuen Testament*. A summer programme or cycle of New Testament mystery dances. The source does not establish whether these were wholly new works or a regrouping of existing dances, so the programme itself—not each presumed component—is the safest obra. ([Monte Verità historical survey](https://www.elisarion.ch/en/print.html/monte_verita/monte_verita/du_oktober_1978/tanz_koerperferien.html))
- **1939:** *Mittelalterliche Legenden und Visionen aus dem Orient*. A composite programme joining medieval legends to “Oriental” visions. It resists classification as one choreography because the title describes a curatorial and performative assemblage. ([Monte Verità historical survey](https://www.elisarion.ch/en/print.html/monte_verita/monte_verita/du_oktober_1978/tanz_koerperferien.html))
- **1943:** *Totentanzspiel*. A dance-play staged and directed by Bara in Ascona with amateur performers; it should not be silently identified with her earlier solo *Totentanz*. ([Monte Verità historical survey](https://www.elisarion.ch/en/print.html/monte_verita/monte_verita/du_oktober_1978/tanz_koerperferien.html))
- **1955:** *Flucht nach Aegypten*. A late ensemble work on the Flight into Egypt, involving Bara’s Ascona dance group rather than functioning chiefly as one of her early solos. ([Gabriella Vincze dissertation](https://doktori.btk.elte.hu/art/vinczegabriella/diss.pdf))

### Ephemeral performance and embodied interpretation

- **1924:** *The blossoming of a camellia at the Locarno Camellia Festival*. An apparently untitled danced interpretation performed in a costume by Alexandre Cingria with Leo Kok at the piano. It resists being called a fixed “work”: the documented thing may have been a festival manifestation rather than a repeatable choreography. ([Museo Castello San Materno](https://www.museoascona.ch/de/ausstellungen/charlotte-bara))
- **1919–1935:** *Gothic, Egyptian, “Oriental,” and temple-dance repertories*. These are overlapping bodies of practice through which Bara toured Europe, not reliably enumerable works with fixed texts: programmes, titles, music, costumes, and gestures could be recombined. No moving image of Bara dancing is known to survive, making photographs, reviews, costumes, and programmes witnesses rather than the works themselves. ([Museo Castello San Materno](https://www.museoascona.ch/de/ausstellungen/charlotte-bara); [Moissey Kogan Catalogue Raisonné research essay](https://moisseykogancr.org/themes-and-intimations-moissey-kogans-prints-for-the-die-schaffenden-portfolios-part-1/))

### Texts and textual projects

- **1920–1958:** *Intime*. A continuing private project of poems and stories given to Bara’s mother Elvira for her birthday each year; the surviving corpus is a serial gift-work rather than a book conceived for publication, although it was posthumously edited in facsimile and transcription in 2016. ([Swiss National Bibliography](https://ead.nb.admin.ch/web/sb-pdf/2017/sb201712.pdf); [Museo Castello San Materno exhibition account](https://www.museoascona.ch/it/esposizioni/charlotte-bara))
- **Date not fixed:** *Autobiografia*. An unpublished autobiographical manuscript recorded in the scholarly music bibliography. Its extent, title status, and completeness are not specified, so it should be treated as an archival project rather than a securely finished book. ([Bibliografia musicale della Svizzera italiana](https://www.ricercamusica.ch/dizionario/56.html))

### Institutional and architectural project

- **1927–1928:** *Teatro San Materno and school of expressive dance*. A Gesamtkunstwerk-like institutional project joining theatre, school, performance space, guest accommodation, and Bara’s religious conception of dance. The building itself was commissioned by her father Paul Bachrach and designed by Carl Weidemeyer, so architectural authorship is not Bara’s; what she made was the artistic programme, school, and lived performance environment housed there. ([Museo Castello San Materno](https://www.museoascona.ch/de/ausstellungen/charlotte-bara); [Historical Dictionary of Switzerland](https://hls-dhs-dss.ch/de/articles/044709/2002-11-28/))
