## Works by Gustave Flaubert

### Juvenilia: school exercises, tales, dramas, essays, and intimate writing

- **1831:** *Louis XIII*. A schoolboy historical composition; the scholarly edition treats it as Flaubert’s earliest surviving work. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1832:** *[Trois pages d’un cahier d’écolier]*. Three surviving school-notebook pages, untitled by Flaubert; the brackets mark an editorial description rather than a work-title. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1835:** *Les Soirées d’étude*. A group of early literary exercises rather than a single conventional book. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1835:** *Narrations et discours*. A collected body of school compositions and rhetorical exercises; its unity is editorial and archival. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1835:** *Opuscules historiques*. A group of youthful historical compositions, not one continuous work. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1835:** *La Fiancée et la Tombe*. A youthful tale. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1835:** *La Grande Dame et le Joueur de vielle*. A youthful tale. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1836:** *Un parfum à sentir*. A youthful tale, also styled a philosophical or moral narrative in early editions. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1836:** *Chronique normande du Xe siècle*. A historical tale presented as a chronicle. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1836:** *La Femme du monde*. A youthful prose narrative. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1836:** *Un secret de Philippe le Prudent, roi d’Espagne*. A historical tale; shortened tables sometimes omit the subtitle. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1836:** *La Peste à Florence*. A historical tale. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1836:** *Bibliomanie*. A tale about obsessive book-collecting, first printed during Flaubert’s youth. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1836:** *Rage et impuissance*. A fantastic or macabre tale. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1837:** *La Dernière Heure*. A short prose fiction. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1837:** *Une leçon d’histoire naturelle. Genre « commis »*. A satirical “physiology” of the clerk; it appeared in a Rouen periodical and is closer to a mock taxonomy than to a tale. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1837:** *La Main de fer*. A brief youthful fiction. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1837:** *Rêve d’enfer*. A fantastic prose narrative. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1837:** *« Quidquid volueris »*. A youthful tale whose Latin title is conventionally retained in quotation marks. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1837:** *Passion et vertu*. A philosophical tale or psychological novella centered on adultery and abandonment. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1838:** *Loys XI*. A five-act prose historical drama, never established in the repertory as a performed play. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1838:** *Agonies*. A youthful prose work comprising meditations or scenes rather than a conventionally plotted narrative. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1838:** *La Danse des morts*. A dramatic-philosophical fantasy in dialogue and tableaux. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1838:** *Ivre et mort*. A youthful dramatic narrative. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1838:** *Les Mémoires d’un fou*. An autobiographical prose narrative cast as a memoir; it remained unpublished in Flaubert’s lifetime. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1839:** *Rome et les Césars*. A historical essay. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1839:** *Étude sur Rabelais*. A youthful critical essay. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1839:** *Smar*. A “vieux mystère”: a hybrid dramatic, philosophical, and fantastic composition, often understood as an ancestor of *La Tentation de saint Antoine*. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1839:** *Les Funérailles du docteur Mathurin*. A comic or satirical prose work. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1840:** *Mademoiselle Rachel*. A critical article on the actor Rachel, apparently conceived for publication. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1840–1841:** *[Cahier intime de 1840-1841]*. An untitled private notebook of memories, thoughts, and self-observation; it resists treatment as a finished work because neither its title nor its public form is authorial. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1842:** *Novembre*. A lyrical and autobiographical prose narrative subtitled “fragments de style quelconque”; it remained unpublished during Flaubert’s life. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1843–1845:** *L’Éducation sentimentale* [version of 1845]. A completed first novel, distinct in plot and form from the 1869 novel bearing the same title; it was not published in Flaubert’s lifetime. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)

### Travel writing, notebooks, and itinerant projects

- **1840:** *Pyrénées-Corse*. A travel notebook or travel narrative made from Flaubert’s journey through the Pyrenees and Corsica; its manuscript condition places it between private record and literary work. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://classiques-garnier.com/dictionnaire-gustave-flaubert-mode-d-emploi-du-present-dictionnaire-en.html?displaymode=full)
- **1845:** *[Voyage en Italie]*. An untitled travel record; its bracketed name is editorial, so it should not be mistaken for a book Flaubert titled and issued. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1847:** *Par les champs et par les grèves*. A literary account of the Brittany journey made with Maxime Du Camp; the travelers divided the chapters, making it a collaborative itinerary whose Flaubert sections nevertheless form a distinct authored component. [*Œuvres complètes*, II](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1847:** *[Carnets de Bretagne]*. Working notebooks for the Brittany journey, untitled as a work and therefore better regarded as documentary matter underlying *Par les champs et par les grèves*. [*Œuvres complètes*, II](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1847:** *Des pierres de Carnac et de l’archéologie celtique*. An archaeological note or short essay associated with the Brittany journey. [*Œuvres complètes*, II](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1849–1851:** *Voyage en Orient*. Flaubert’s travel notes from Egypt, Palestine, Lebanon, Asia Minor, Greece, and Italy, put into a more continuous manuscript form in 1851; later editors produced differently arranged and sometimes expurgated publications, so no lifetime book fixes its definitive shape. [*Œuvres complètes*, II](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1849–1851:** *Listes des bagages*. Practical packing lists preserved with the Oriental-travel papers; they resist being called a literary work, but are a made textual object included by the complete edition. [*Œuvres complètes*, II](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1849–1851:** *Scénario du début du « Voyage en Orient »*. A plan for shaping the travel record, not a completed autonomous text. [*Œuvres complètes*, II](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1849–1851:** *Notes concernant l’Égypte*. Fragmentary documentary notes rather than a finished work; the Pléiade prints extracts. [*Œuvres complètes*, II](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1849–1851:** *[Chant de la courtisane]*. An editorially titled poetic fragment preserved among the Oriental-travel papers. [*Œuvres complètes*, II](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1849–1851:** *Ébauche de fiction poétique*. An untitled poetic-fiction draft known by an editorial description; it remains an ébauche rather than a completed work. [*Œuvres complètes*, II](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1849–1851:** *Deux anecdotes*. Two brief narrative records grouped editorially; the label describes a pair of fragments rather than a firmly constituted work. [*Œuvres complètes*, II](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1858:** *[Voyage en Algérie et en Tunisie]*. An untitled travel notebook made during research for *Salammbô*; its function is simultaneously itinerary, documentation, and workshop material. [*Œuvres complètes*, III](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)

### Novels, tales, and large prose constructions

- **1851–1856; published 1856–1857:** *Madame Bovary*. A novel first serialized in the *Revue de Paris* and then issued as a book; manuscript incipits, an alternative epilogue, deleted episodes, and a plan of Yonville survive as parts of its workshop rather than separate finished works. [*Œuvres complètes*, III](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1857–1862:** *Salammbô*. A historical novel set in ancient Carthage. Its surviving scenarios, explanatory chapter, synopsis, travel research, and polemical dossier record a project in formation and reception rather than additional novels. [*Œuvres complètes*, III](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1864–1869:** *L’Éducation sentimentale* [version of 1869]. A novel subtitled “Histoire d’un jeune homme,” wholly distinct from the completed 1845 novel carrying the same main title. [*Œuvres complètes*, IV](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1875–1877:** *Un cœur simple*. A tale, subsequently made one component of *Trois contes*. [*Œuvres complètes*, V](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1875–1877:** *La Légende de saint Julien l’Hospitalier*. A legendary or hagiographic tale, subsequently made one component of *Trois contes*. [*Œuvres complètes*, V](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1876–1877:** *Hérodias*. A historical-biblical tale, subsequently made one component of *Trois contes*. [*Œuvres complètes*, V](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1877:** *Trois contes*. The authored collection formed by *Un cœur simple*, *La Légende de saint Julien l’Hospitalier*, and *Hérodias*; the collection is itself an obra, not merely a later publisher’s omnibus. [*Œuvres complètes*, V](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1872–1880; unfinished:** *Bouvard et Pécuchet*. An encyclopedic comic novel left incomplete at Flaubert’s death; the surviving chapters I–X do not realize the entire planned construction. [*Œuvres complètes*, V](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1870s–1880; unfinished:** *Pour le « Second volume » de Bouvard et Pécuchet*. A projected compilation or “copy” meant to continue the novel through rearranged quotations, clichés, and documentary fragments. Because Flaubert never completed its selection and ordering, it is a project and archive-in-construction rather than a stable book. [*Œuvres complètes*, V](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1870s–1880:** *Citations « Pour la copie »*. The mass of copied excerpts assembled for the projected second volume of *Bouvard et Pécuchet*; much of its wording originates with other writers, while Flaubert’s creative act lies in collection, selection, and intended montage. [*Œuvres complètes*, V](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1870s:** *Le Catalogue des idées chic*. A very short satirical catalogue intended for the documentary architecture surrounding *Bouvard et Pécuchet*. [*Œuvres complètes*, V](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1850s–1880; unfinished:** *Le Dictionnaire des idées reçues*. An alphabetic satirical lexicon of received opinions, repeatedly projected and revised but never completed or published by Flaubert; it is both an autonomous project and part of the intended “second volume” of *Bouvard et Pécuchet*. [*Œuvres complètes*, V](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)

### The recurring Saint Anthony project

- **1849:** *La Tentation de saint Antoine* [version of 1849]. The first large completed version of Flaubert’s dramatic-philosophical prose vision; although rejected after its private reading, it is not merely a draft of the published book. [*Œuvres complètes*, II](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1848–1849:** *Les Trois Grands Scénarios de « La Tentation de saint Antoine »*. Three extensive compositional plans for the 1849 version; they are project-documents, not manifestations intended for independent publication. [*Œuvres complètes*, II](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1856:** *La Tentation de saint Antoine* [version of 1856]. A radically shortened and reworked second version, partially published but textually distinct from both the 1849 and 1874 works. [*Œuvres complètes*, III](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1869–1874:** *La Tentation de saint Antoine* [version of 1874]. The final published form of the prose drama or visionary dialogue; its mixture of theatre, narrative, theology, visual tableau, and philosophical catalogue resists reduction to “novel” or “play.” [*Œuvres complètes*, V](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)

### Theatre, collaborative spectacles, and unrealized performance projects

- **1855:** *Pierrot au sérail*. A pantomime scenario written with Louis Bouilhet; collaborative authorship is integral, and it is a performance project rather than a conventional prose book. [*Œuvres complètes*, III](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1850s:** *Les Sept Fils du derviche*. An unfinished Oriental tale preserved in Flaubert’s workshop papers. [*Œuvres complètes*, II](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1850s:** *Une nuit de Don Juan*. An unrealized dramatic or narrative project surviving as workshop material. [*Œuvres complètes*, III](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1850s:** *La Spirale*. An unfinished scenario or fantastic project; it never acquired a definitive literary form. [*Œuvres complètes*, III](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1850s:** *Ballet*. A brief performance scenario without a completed staged manifestation. [*Œuvres complètes*, III](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1850s:** *Ébauches et scénarios de théâtre en collaboration*. A corpus of collaborative performance projects preserved in fragmentary form; individual attribution is sometimes inseparable from collective invention. [*Œuvres complètes*, II](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1850s:** *Ébauches et scénarios de théâtre non autographes*. A corpus preserved in hands other than Flaubert’s. Their inclusion records Flaubert’s theatrical workshop, but authorship of the wording is uncertain and they resist classification as works made by him alone. [*Œuvres complètes*, II](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1850s:** *Scénarios de récits autographes*. A small corpus of narrative projects in Flaubert’s hand, not completed stories. [*Œuvres complètes*, II](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1850s:** *Scénarios de récits non autographes*. Narrative plans preserved in other hands; their relationship to Flaubert’s intentions is real but their verbal authorship is not secure. [*Œuvres complètes*, II](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1863:** *Le Château des cœurs*. A féerie written with Louis Bouilhet and Charles d’Osmoy, combining dramatic text, spectacle, music, and stage effects; its collaborative, multimedia design approaches a Gesamtkunstwerk more closely than a “book.” [*Œuvres complètes*, IV](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1860s:** *Scénarios de féeries*. Plans for spectacular theatrical works, some connected with *Le Château des cœurs*; they remain performance projects rather than completed plays. [*Œuvres complètes*, IV](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1860s:** *La Queue de la poire de la boule de Monseigneur*. A comic dramatic scenario or collective jeu d’esprit preserved in Flaubert’s workshop; its title and textual survival do not make it a finished stage work. [*Œuvres complètes*, IV](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1873:** *Le Sexe faible*. A comedy developed from work by Louis Bouilhet and completed or extensively refashioned by Flaubert after Bouilhet’s death; the divided authorship must not be silently assigned to Flaubert alone. [*Œuvres complètes*, IV](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1873–1874:** *Le Candidat*. A four-act comedy written for performance and staged in 1874; unlike many of Flaubert’s theatrical projects, it reached public manifestation, though unsuccessfully. [*Œuvres complètes*, IV](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1870s:** *Bal donné au Czar*. A brief spectacle or dramatic scenario, left as workshop material. [*Œuvres complètes*, IV](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1870s:** *Vie et travaux du R. P. Cruchard par l’Abbé Pruneau*. A parodic biographical jeu d’esprit arising from Flaubert’s private comic mythology, not a conventional public biography. [*Œuvres complètes*, IV](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1870s:** *Sous Napoléon III*. An unfinished dramatic or satirical project surviving in workshop form. [*Œuvres complètes*, IV](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1870s:** *Les Bourgeois du XIXe siècle*. A very brief satirical project or scenario; it never became a completed autonomous work. [*Œuvres complètes*, IV](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)

### Essays, prefaces, public interventions, and documentary acts

- **1830s–1840s:** *[Deux rédactions d’histoire naturelle]*. Two school compositions grouped under an editorial title; they are exercises, not a single authorially constituted work. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1830s:** *Influence des Arabes d’Espagne*. A school or academic historical composition. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1830s:** *Lutte du sacerdoce et de l’empire*. An extended historical school composition. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1830s:** *Histoire*. A school exercise preserved under its generic title. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1830s:** *Histoire moderne*. A school exercise or set of notes rather than a finished historical treatise. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1830s:** *Les Arts et le Commerce*. A school discourse or essay. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1830s:** *De la littérature romantique en France*. A youthful critical essay. [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1862:** *Résumé de « Salammbô »*. An authorial synopsis produced in connection with the novel; it is an ancillary protocol of explanation, not another version of the novel. [*Œuvres complètes*, III](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1862–1863:** *La Querelle de « Salammbô »*. A dossier of Flaubert’s published responses and polemical interventions concerning the novel. Its unity is partly editorial, and not every item in the surrounding controversy is by Flaubert. [*Œuvres complètes*, III](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1870:** *Préface aux « Dernières chansons » de Louis Bouilhet*. A memorial and critical preface written to act publicly on behalf of Bouilhet’s posthumous reputation. [*Œuvres complètes*, IV](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1872:** *Lettre à la municipalité de Rouen*. A public letter defending plans for a monument to Louis Bouilhet; although epistolary in form, it acts as civic advocacy and therefore approaches a manifesto. [*Œuvres complètes*, IV](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)

### Working notebooks and compositional archives

- **1830s–1845:** *Scénarios de jeunesse*. A corpus of plans and narrative germs rather than finished works; the complete edition groups them as “L’Atelier de Flaubert.” [*Œuvres complètes*, I](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1850s–1870s:** *Carnets de travail*. A continuing body of notebooks containing observations, research, scenarios, addresses, and compositional notes. They are made textual objects essential to Flaubert’s practice, but resist being called a single work because their boundaries and purposes are heterogeneous. [Pléiade catalogue](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1857–1862:** *Scénarios de « Salammbô »*. A sequence of plans through which the novel’s architecture was made; collectively a project archive, not an alternate published novel. [*Œuvres complètes*, III](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1860s:** *Scénarios de « L’Éducation sentimentale »*. Plans and structural drafts for the 1869 novel; they document its construction without constituting independent narrative works. [*Œuvres complètes*, IV](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1870s:** *Scénarios et dossiers de « Trois contes »*. Plans, research notes, and drafts for the three tales; the Pléiade distributes them among appendices rather than treating them as autonomous works. [*Œuvres complètes*, V](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)
- **1872–1880:** *Scénarios et dossiers de « Bouvard et Pécuchet »*. Plans, reading notes, documentary files, and schemes for both volumes. This is an unfinished knowledge-machine and compositional archive whose limits cannot be reduced to the posthumously printed novel. [*Œuvres complètes*, V](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Flaubert.LF.htm)

### Correspondence

- **January 1830–May 1880:** *Correspondance*. Thousands of letters to family, friends, lovers, writers, publishers, and public officials, now established as a five-volume chronological corpus with supplements and letters of uncertain date. Individual letters were acts addressed to particular recipients, not components of a book designed by Flaubert; “Correspondance” is therefore a posthumously constituted body of work rather than one authorial obra.
