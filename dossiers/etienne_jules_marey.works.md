## Works by Étienne-Jules Marey

### Books, theses, and sustained textual obras

- **1857:** *Recherches hydrauliques sur la circulation du sang*. Marey’s first substantial published physiological study, an article-length investigation rather than a book; it inaugurated the graphic and hydraulic research program that occupied his early career. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1859:** *Recherches sur la circulation du sang à l’état physiologique et dans les maladies*. Doctoral thesis and Marey’s first separately printed scientific obra. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1860:** *Recherches sur le pouls au moyen d’un nouvel appareil enregistreur, le sphygmographe*. Separately printed memoir describing the practical sphygmograph; related articles appeared in 1859–60, but those manifestations should not be mistaken for additional inventions. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1863:** *Physiologie médicale de la circulation du sang, basée sur l’étude graphique des mouvements du cœur et du pouls artériel, avec application aux maladies de l’appareil circulatoire*. Book-length synthesis of Marey’s graphical cardiology, combining physiological argument, clinical application, instruments, and recorded traces. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1866–1868:** *Du mouvement dans les fonctions de la vie*. First manifested as seven published lectures in 1866 and then as a book in 1868; the serial and volume are manifestations of one developing textual obra, not unrelated works. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1869:** *Mémoire sur le vol des insectes et des oiseaux*. Extended memoir on animal flight, preceded and followed by lectures, extracts, and revised publications on the same project. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1872:** *Mémoire sur la torpille*. Experimental memoir on the electric discharge of the torpedo fish; its simultaneous appearances in two journals are duplicate manifestations, not two works. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1873:** *La Machine animale: locomotion terrestre et aérienne*. Book synthesizing Marey’s studies of walking, running, equine gaits, and flight; later French editions and translations are manifestations of this obra. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1875–1880:** *Physiologie expérimentale: travaux du laboratoire de M. Marey*. Four-volume laboratory series edited under Marey’s name and containing work by Marey and his collaborators; it is simultaneously an editorial project and a collective laboratory obra, so sole authorship of every constituent memoir cannot be assigned to Marey. [Biodiversity Heritage Library](https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/bibliography/43395)

- **1875:** *Du moyen d’économiser le travail moteur de l’homme et des animaux*. Experimental memoir on elastic devices and the economy of locomotor work, first presented in 1874 and later incorporated into *Physiologie expérimentale*. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1875–1876:** *Mémoire sur la pulsation du cœur*. Extended memoir assembled from research communicated in 1875 and published in the first volume of *Physiologie expérimentale*. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1876:** *Pression et vitesse du sang*. Two-part experimental study in the second volume of *Physiologie expérimentale*; bibliographies sometimes treat it as a separate title and sometimes as a constituent memoir. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1878:** *La Méthode graphique dans les sciences expérimentales et principalement en physiologie et en médecine*. Book-length account of recording instruments, experimental protocols, and the epistemology of traces; it is both a scientific treatise and a protocol-bearing manual. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1881:** *La Circulation du sang à l’état physiologique et dans les maladies*. Revised synthesis of Marey’s cardiovascular research; it develops rather than merely reprints the 1863 book. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1885:** *Études pratiques sur la marche de l’homme*. Applied study of human gait, published in installments and connected to Marey’s military and physiological investigations; its boundaries vary between bibliographies because portions also appeared as articles and reports. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1889–1890:** *Physiologie du mouvement: le vol des oiseaux*. Book-length synthesis of decades of research on avian flight; dated 1890 on the volume, although Marey presented the published book to the Académie des sciences in October 1889. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1892:** *Chronophotographie*. Seven-page text with two plates printed in Moscow; a concise account of the photographic method rather than a substantial monograph. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1893:** *Études de physiologie artistique faites au moyen de la chronophotographie*. Illustrated atlas and study jointly authored with Georges Demenÿ; its plates belong to a collaborative photographic program, and sole attribution to Marey would be misleading. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1894:** *Le Mouvement*. Marey’s principal late synthesis of graphical recording and chronophotography, incorporating instruments, experimental images, and methods; the book is a textual obra built from a much larger experimental archive. [Conservatoire numérique des arts et métiers](https://cnum.cnam.fr/pgi/redir.php?ident=8KE614&onglet=c)

### Articles, lectures, reports, and institutional texts

- **1858–1904:** *Scientific papers and communications*. Marey made a corpus of several hundred named articles, notes, lectures, letters, prize reports, institutional addresses, and laboratory reports concerning circulation, muscular contraction, locomotion, flight, chronophotography, fluid motion, aerodynamics, and work. This is a body of work rather than a single text; Serge Nicolas’s chronological scholarly bibliography records the individual titles and warns that, despite its intended completeness, omissions or errors may remain. [Complete chronological bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1861–1863:** *Détermination graphique des rapports du choc du cœur avec les mouvements des oreillettes et des ventricules, obtenue à l’aide d’un appareil enregistreur*. Collaborative papers with Auguste Chauveau reporting intracardiac measurements; authorship is explicitly joint. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1863:** *Appareils et expériences cardiographiques*. Substantial memoir made with Auguste Chauveau; it joins instrument description and experimental record and therefore resists classification as merely a text about an apparatus. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1866:** *Études graphiques sur la nature de la contraction musculaire*. Two-part journal study presenting muscular action through inscribed traces. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1867:** *Évolution historique des sciences; Rôle de l’analyse dans les sciences; La synthèse expérimentale dans les sciences naturelles*. Published sequence of lectures articulating Marey’s experimental program; collectively they behave almost as a methodological manifesto, although Marey did not label them one. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1868–1871:** *Le mécanisme du vol chez les animaux*. Serial article sequence derived from Marey’s lectures and flight experiments; installment numbering and republication make its exact textual boundary unstable. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1872:** *Des allures du cheval, étudiées par la méthode graphique*. Two-part Academy communication using pneumatic recording to analyze equine gait. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1875:** *Mouvement des ondes liquides dans les tubes élastiques*. Experimental paper on wave propagation, also printed in the proceedings of the Société française de physique; the two printings are manifestations of the same paper. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1875:** *Circulation*. Encyclopaedia article jointly signed by Marey and G. Carlet; it is collaborative and should not be silently absorbed into Marey’s sole-authored corpus. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1878:** *La décharge électrique de la torpille comparée à la contraction musculaire*. Congress paper comparing electrical discharge with muscular contraction. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1878:** *Correspondance sur les allures du cheval reproduites par la photographie instantanée*. Published letter responding to instantaneous photographs of horse locomotion; it documents Marey’s transition from graphic inscription to photography. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1882:** *Emploi de la photographie instantanée pour l’analyse des mouvements chez les animaux*. Programmatic Academy communication defining photography as an analytical instrument. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1883:** *La chronophotographie: nouvelle méthode pour analyser le mouvement dans les sciences physiques et naturelles*. Methodological communication naming and explaining the emerging practice; it is close to a manifesto but remains framed as a scientific report. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1889:** *La photochronographie et ses applications à l’analyse des phénomènes physiologiques*. Summary of the method’s physiological uses; Marey still used both “photochronographie” and “chronophotographie” during this period. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1889:** *Rapport présenté à M. le Ministre, au nom de la Commission de gymnastique*. Official report on physical training; it is an institutional, partly collective text issued under Marey’s responsibility rather than an autonomous scientific monograph. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1892:** *Le mouvement des êtres microscopiques analysé par la chronophotographie*. Academy communication applying chronophotography to microscopic organisms. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1892:** *Le mouvement du cœur, étudié par la chronophotographie*. Academy communication applying serial photography to cardiac motion. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1893:** *Des mouvements de natation de la raie*. Study of ray locomotion through serial images. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1893:** *Le mouvement des liquides étudié par la chronophotographie*. Study in which photographed fluid forms become experimental traces rather than conventional pictures. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1894–1895:** *La Station physiologique de Paris*. Two-part account of the Bois de Boulogne laboratory; simultaneously an institutional description, a report, and a statement of experimental method. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1901:** *La chronophotographie et les sports athlétiques*. Illustrated article connecting laboratory motion analysis to athletic practice. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1902:** *Le mouvement de l’air par la chronophotographie*. Late study of photographed air currents, part of Marey’s final experimental project. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1903:** *Fonctions et organes*. Published lecture on the relation between physiological function and anatomical form. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1904:** *L’économie de travail et l’élasticité*. Late synthesis of Marey’s longstanding project to measure and economize bodily work. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1904:** *Travail de l’homme dans les professions manuelles*. Study of human labor in manual occupations, published in the year of Marey’s death. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

### Recording instruments and experimental apparatus

- **1859–1860:** *Sphygmographe direct*. Portable mechanical recorder that transformed arterial pulsation into a drawn curve. Karl von Vierordt had devised an earlier sphygmograph, so Marey’s obra is the practical redesign, not the unqualified invention of the instrument’s principle. [Musée d’histoire de la médecine et des appareils](https://www.mohma.org/instruments/category/pulse/sphygmograph_marey/)

- **1860s:** *Artificial double-circulation model*. Hydraulic apparatus reproducing cardiac pulsation, valve disorders, and variations of arterial and venous pressure; it is both a model and a machine for making physiological hypotheses visible. [Académie nationale de médecine](https://www.academie-medecine.fr/etienne-jules-marey-linnovation-medicale/)

- **1861–1863:** *Intracardiac probes and cardiographic recording apparatus*. System developed with Auguste Chauveau to transmit pressures from a horse’s cardiac chambers to recording levers; construction and authorship are collaborative. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1863:** *Cardiograph*. Mechanical or pneumatic receiver for recording movements of the heart against the chest wall; Marey produced several forms rather than one immutable object. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1864:** *Thermograph*. Self-registering apparatus for temperature changes; later versions mean that “the thermograph” names an evolving instrument family. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1860s:** *Myographs*. Family of recording devices for muscular contraction, including direct and transmission forms. These are experimental apparatuses made and repeatedly modified within Marey’s laboratory, not a single finished work. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1860s–1870s:** *Marey tambour*. Air-filled membrane and lever unit for transmitting and inscribing bodily movement; it functioned as a modular component of many apparatuses rather than as a self-sufficient “work.” [Virtual Laboratory, Max Planck Institute for the History of Science](https://vlp.mpiwg-berlin.mpg.de/technology/search?img.exportcaption=pneumograph)

- **1868–1870:** *Artificial insect*. Mechanized wing model built to test the mechanics of insect flight; it is a synthetic experimental model, not a representation made chiefly for display. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1869–1872:** *Artificial bird and rotating-wing models*. Mechanical models used to test wing inclination, lift, and the sequence of flight; several variants existed, so no single object exhausts the project. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1872:** *Pneumatic apparatus for recording horse gaits*. Pressure bulbs fitted beneath the hooves and connected to recording tambours translated footfalls into synchronized traces. It is a protocol distributed across animal, tubes, recorder, and paper rather than a freestanding machine. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1873–1874:** *Chronograph*. Timing apparatus for placing regular temporal marks alongside physiological traces; Marey described more than one configuration. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1876–1878:** *Sphygmographe à transmission*. Pneumatic form separating the pulse sensor from the recording mechanism; it belongs to an instrument system incorporating tambours, tubes, and a moving recording surface. [Virtual Laboratory, Max Planck Institute for the History of Science](https://vlp.mpiwg-berlin.mpg.de/technology/search?img.exportcaption=pneumograph)

- **1870s:** *Pneumograph*. Chest-mounted apparatus transmitting respiratory movement to a recorder; multiple models and later improvements prevent treatment as one stable artifact. [Virtual Laboratory, Max Planck Institute for the History of Science](https://vlp.mpiwg-berlin.mpg.de/technology/search?img.exportcaption=pneumograph)

- **1870s:** *Exploratory capsules for carotid pulse and other bodily movements*. Interchangeable sensors used within Marey’s pneumatic recording system; these resist individual work-status because their significance lies in the assembled experimental network. [Virtual Laboratory, Max Planck Institute for the History of Science](https://vlp.mpiwg-berlin.mpg.de/technology/search?img.exportcaption=pneumograph)

- **1881:** *Polygraphe portatif*. Portable multi-channel recorder intended to register several physiological variables together. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1881:** *Nouveau pneumographe*. Redesigned respiratory recorder described alongside the portable polygraph. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1881:** *Microscopic movement recorder*. Apparatus for enlarging and inscribing movements too small for ordinary physiological recording. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1881:** *Nouveau thermographe*. Revised self-recording thermometer, distinct from the instrument announced in 1864. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1882:** *Fusil photographique*. Gun-shaped camera making twelve sequential exposures on a rotating circular plate; the surviving apparatus is a chronophotographic instrument, not itself a film camera in the later cinematographic sense. [Marta Braun, *Picturing Time*](https://www.bibliovault.org/BV.book.epl?ISBN=9780226071756)

- **1882–1883:** *Chronophotographe sur plaque fixe*. Camera-and-shutter system recording successive phases of motion on one stationary photographic plate. It produced analytic composite images rather than strips intended for projection. [CNAM, *Le Mouvement*](https://cnum.cnam.fr/pgi/redir.php?ident=8KE614&onglet=a)

- **1883–1886:** *Geometric chronophotography apparatus*. System combining black clothing, bright joint markers, timing, and photography to reduce a moving body to lines and points; the “apparatus” includes costume and protocol as well as camera. [Marta Braun, *Picturing Time*](https://www.bibliovault.org/BV.book.epl?ISBN=9780226071756)

- **1887–1888:** *Chronophotographe à plaque mobile*. Experimental camera moving a sensitive surface through the image field to separate successive phases. It was transitional and survives primarily through descriptions and experimental results. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1888:** *Chronophotographe à bande de papier sensible*. Camera carrying a moving paper roll; it made sequential images but did not yet produce a transparent projection film. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1889–1890:** *Chronophotographe à pellicule mobile*. Camera advancing an unperforated transparent film intermittently; developed in Marey’s laboratory with Georges Demenÿ and mechanics, so “by Marey” names leadership of a collective technical project rather than necessarily sole fabrication. [La Cinémathèque française](https://www.cinematheque.fr/catalogues/restaurations-tirages/film.php?id=113597)

- **1890:** *Appareil photochronographique applicable à l’analyse de toutes sortes de mouvements*. General-purpose roll-film apparatus described to the Académie des sciences and subsequently patented; it is a device and a declared research project. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1892–1894:** *Chronophotographic projector project*. Attempt to reanimate serial photographs, pursued with Demenÿ but suspended after their approaches diverged. It is an unfinished project and should not be turned into a successfully completed Marey projector. [La Cinémathèque française](https://www.cinematheque.fr/catalogues/restaurations-tirages/film.php?id=113597)

- **1890s:** *Hydrodynamic chronophotography apparatus*. Tanks, injected particles, illumination, and photographic recording used to visualize vortices and liquid motion; the made thing is an experimental installation and protocol, not just the resulting photograph. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1899–1904:** *Aerodynamic smoke-trace machine*. Wind and smoke apparatus that made otherwise invisible air currents photographically legible. Marey’s final obra is best understood as a Gesamtkunstwerk of machine, model, smoke, illumination, photograph, and scientific argument rather than as any single component. [Marta Braun, *Picturing Time*](https://www.bibliovault.org/BV.book.epl?ISBN=9780226071756)

### Experimental sites and institutional projects

- **1882–1904:** *Station physiologique du Parc des Princes*. Open-air laboratory established with support from the City of Paris for studying human and animal locomotion. Marey directed its scientific conception, but the station was an institutional and collaborative project rather than a building attributable to him as sole architect. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1882–1904:** *Chronophotographic track and black-screen field*. Experimental environment at the Station physiologique in which subjects moved before a calibrated dark background. It resists object-status: architecture, costume, timing system, camera, and bodily performance together constituted the recording instrument. [Marta Braun, *Picturing Time*](https://www.bibliovault.org/BV.book.epl?ISBN=9780226071756)

- **1898–1904:** *Institut Marey*. International laboratory and standardization project for graphical physiological recording, promoted and presided over by Marey. It is an institutional creation involving many participants, not Marey’s private work or a single authored text. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

### Photographic plates and experimental image series

- **1882:** *Birds in flight made with the photographic gun*. Series of circular-plate sequential negatives rather than individually titled photographs; catalogue descriptions identify subjects, but those descriptions should not automatically be promoted to authorial titles. [Marta Braun, *Picturing Time*](https://www.bibliovault.org/BV.book.epl?ISBN=9780226071756)

- **1882–1883:** *Chronophotographic studies of human locomotion on fixed plates*. Experimental negative and print series showing walking, running, jumping, and related actions. Individual plates often lack fixed contemporary titles and may involve laboratory assistants. [Marta Braun, *Picturing Time*](https://www.bibliovault.org/BV.book.epl?ISBN=9780226071756)

- **1882–1886:** *Chronophotographic studies of horses and other quadrupeds*. Fixed-plate series analyzing gait; this is an experimental corpus, not a single picture or a conventionally titled artwork. [Marta Braun, *Picturing Time*](https://www.bibliovault.org/BV.book.epl?ISBN=9780226071756)

- **1883–1886:** *Geometric chronophotographs of walking, running, and jumping figures*. Images reducing the body to luminous joint points and lines. Georges Demenÿ frequently served as subject and collaborator, complicating any assertion of solitary authorship. [Marta Braun, *Picturing Time*](https://www.bibliovault.org/BV.book.epl?ISBN=9780226071756)

- **1882–1890s:** *Chronophotographic studies of avian flight*. Plates of gulls, pigeons, pelicans, and other birds made through several camera systems; subject labels vary between archives and publications and are not always original titles. [Marta Braun, *Picturing Time*](https://www.bibliovault.org/BV.book.epl?ISBN=9780226071756)

- **1886–1891:** *Chronophotographic studies of insect flight*. High-speed image series of wing movements, often inseparable from the lighting apparatus and experimental mounting that produced them. [Marta Braun, *Picturing Time*](https://www.bibliovault.org/BV.book.epl?ISBN=9780226071756)

- **1886–1894:** *Chronophotographic studies of swimming fish and rays*. Aquarium-based series analyzing aquatic locomotion; later catalogue captions supply convenient names, but the corpus was not conceived as a set of titled films. [Marta Braun, *Picturing Time*](https://www.bibliovault.org/BV.book.epl?ISBN=9780226071756)

- **1888–1890:** *Paper-roll chronophotographic experiments*. Sequential negatives on moving sensitized paper, including human locomotion. These are transitional photographic records rather than projection works. [Marta Braun, *Picturing Time*](https://www.bibliovault.org/BV.book.epl?ISBN=9780226071756)

- **1889:** *Homme en marche*. Descriptive catalogue title for a surviving fragment from Marey’s first flexible-roll experiments; whether it was Marey’s own fixed title is uncertain. [La Cinémathèque française](https://www.cinematheque.fr/catalogues/restaurations-tirages/film.php?id=113597)

- **c. 1889:** *Homme sautant*. Descriptive title attached to an early roll-film experiment; the exact date and status as an authorial title remain uncertain. [La Cinémathèque française](https://www.cinematheque.fr/catalogues/restaurations-tirages/film.php?id=113597)

- **1890:** *Scènes d’escrime*. Chronophotographic strips made at Naples. They are experimental motion records, probably produced within Marey’s collaborative laboratory practice, not a narrative film with a single settled title. [La Cinémathèque française](https://www.cinematheque.fr/catalogues/restaurations-tirages/film.php?id=113597)

- **1890s:** *Cheval Bixio monté*. Archive title for a roll-film sequence of a mounted horse; surviving versions and frame counts differ, and the title may be catalogical rather than authorial. [La Cinémathèque française](https://www.cinematheque.fr/catalogues/restaurations-tirages/film.php?id=113597)

- **1890s:** *Cheval Bixio au pas*. Archive title for a 61-image chronophotographic horse sequence; it was made as analytical evidence, not as a projected entertainment film. [La Cinémathèque française](https://www.cinematheque.fr/catalogues/restaurations-tirages/film.php?id=113597)

- **1891:** *La Vague*. Roll-film study made in the Bay of Naples and commonly cited under this descriptive title. Its status lies between photographed experiment and film: it records temporal succession but was not made within a stable projection system. [Marta Braun, *Picturing Time*](https://www.bibliovault.org/BV.book.epl?ISBN=9780226071756)

- **1891:** *Georges Demenÿ prononce “Je vous aime”*. Chronophotographic portrait made in Marey’s laboratory for Demenÿ’s phonoscope experiments. Demenÿ is subject, technical collaborator, and principal advocate of its reanimation, so unqualified sole attribution to Marey is disputed. [La Cinémathèque française](https://www.cinematheque.fr/catalogues/restaurations-tirages/film.php?id=113597)

- **1892:** *Cheval attelé à une voiture, boulevard Delessert*. Descriptive archive title for a 36-image exterior strip taken from Marey’s residence; its apparent street-scene character should not obscure its status as a camera experiment. [Marta Braun, *Picturing Time*](https://www.bibliovault.org/BV.book.epl?ISBN=9780226071756)

- **1892–1894:** *Chronophotographs of microscopic organisms and the beating heart*. Experimental series produced to support Marey’s published communications; individual specimens generally lack stable authorial titles. [Nicolas bibliography](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work)

- **1893–1899:** *Liquid-motion chronophotographs*. Series recording drops, jets, waves, vortices, and fluid impacts. These images are experimental manifestations of a continuing project, and surviving prints, negatives, and published plates cannot always be matched one-to-one. [Marta Braun, *Picturing Time*](https://www.bibliovault.org/BV.book.epl?ISBN=9780226071756)

- **1894:** *Falling and righting cat*. Chronophotographic sequence used to analyze how a cat turns in the air. The familiar title is descriptive, and production belongs to Marey’s laboratory rather than securely to his unaided camera operation. [Marta Braun, *Picturing Time*](https://www.bibliovault.org/BV.book.epl?ISBN=9780226071756)

- **1899:** *Gare Saint-Lazare*. Fixed-view street film attributed jointly to Marey and Lucien Bull; its authorship is explicitly collaborative. [Cinémathèque française, digitized-film repertory](https://www.cinematheque.fr/media/pdf/repertoire-des-films-numerises-cinematheque-francaise.pdf)

- **1899–1904:** *Smoke-trace photographs of air movement*. Marey’s final photographic corpus, showing currents around planes and other forms. These photographs, the apparatus that generated them, and the accompanying theory are mutually dependent parts of one unfinished-at-death research project. [Marta Braun, *Picturing Time*](https://www.bibliovault.org/BV.book.epl?ISBN=9780226071756)

- **1882–1904:** *Surviving photographic negatives, prints, plates, and experimental films*. Marta Braun’s catalogues document the known photographic and chronophotographic holdings, while the Cinémathèque française notes that no definitive catalogue of all Marey films yet exists. The archive therefore resists a claim of absolute completeness: hundreds of rolls and fragments are anonymous, descriptively titled, duplicated, damaged, or jointly attributable to Marey, Demenÿ, Lucien Bull, and other laboratory workers. [Braun catalogue description](https://www.bibliovault.org/BV.book.epl?ISBN=9780226071756); [Cinémathèque française](https://www.cinematheque.fr/catalogues/restaurations-tirages/film.php?id=113597)

### Sources

- Serge Nicolas, [“Étienne-Jules Marey (1830–1904): Œuvre / Work—A Bibliography”](https://sites.google.com/site/etiennejulesmarey18301904/oeuvre-work). Chronological specialist bibliography of Marey’s writings, including collaborative papers, republications, reports, and notices; Nicolas explicitly cautions that omissions and errors may remain.

- Marta Braun, [*Picturing Time: The Work of Étienne-Jules Marey (1830–1904)*](https://www.bibliovault.org/BV.book.epl?ISBN=9780226071756), University of Chicago Press, 1992. Contains a bibliography of Marey’s writings and catalogues of photographic negatives and prints, original experiments, and films.

- Étienne-Jules Marey, [*Le Mouvement*](https://cnum.cnam.fr/pgi/redir.php?ident=8KE614&onglet=c), Paris: G. Masson, 1894, digitized by the Conservatoire numérique des arts et métiers. Primary synthesis used to identify apparatuses, methods, and the categories Marey applied to them.

- Étienne-Jules Marey, [*Notice sur les titres et travaux scientifiques du Dr Marey*](https://cnum.cnam.fr/pgi/redir.php?ident=4B65.105&onglet=c), 1870, digitized by the Conservatoire numérique des arts et métiers. Marey’s contemporary account of his early publications, instruments, and research.

- La Cinémathèque française, [“Bandes chronophotographiques: catalogue des restaurations et tirages”](https://www.cinematheque.fr/catalogues/restaurations-tirages/film.php?id=113597). Archival account emphasizing collaborative attribution and the continuing absence of a definitive catalogue of all Marey films.

- La Cinémathèque française, [*Répertoire des films numérisés*](https://www.cinematheque.fr/media/pdf/repertoire-des-films-numerises-cinematheque-francaise.pdf), March 2023. Used for the joint Marey–Lucien Bull attribution of *Gare Saint-Lazare* and for the status of modern compilations.

- Biodiversity Heritage Library, [*Physiologie expérimentale: travaux du laboratoire de M. Marey*, volumes I–IV](https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/bibliography/43395). Bibliographic and digitized record of the collective laboratory series.

- Académie nationale de médecine, [“Étienne-Jules Marey: l’innovation médicale”](https://www.academie-medecine.fr/etienne-jules-marey-linnovation-medicale/). Institutional study of Marey’s cardiovascular apparatuses and artificial circulation model.

- Max Planck Institute for the History of Science, Virtual Laboratory, [technology database entry for Marey recording systems](https://vlp.mpiwg-berlin.mpg.de/technology/search?img.exportcaption=pneumograph). Used to distinguish modular sensors, tambours, pneumographs, cardiographs, and complete recording assemblies.
