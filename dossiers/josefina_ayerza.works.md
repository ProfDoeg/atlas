## Works by Josefina Ayerza

### Serial editorial projects

- **1990–present:** *Lacanian Ink*. Ayerza founded and edits this New York journal of psychoanalysis, philosophy, and contemporary art; conceived as a “disquieting object” rather than merely a container for texts, it approaches a continuing Gesamtkunstwerk of writing, images, design, exhibitions, and public events. Issue 1 appeared in fall 1990, but the official archive and newsletter disagree about the journal’s enumeration: the author bibliography stops at issue 49, while later records identify issues 50–57/58, so no claim of final completeness is possible. ([Ayerza’s account](https://www.lacan.com/symptom/expose/); [journal archive](https://www.lacan.com/lacink/archive.html); [newsletter index](https://www.lacan.com/newsletter-index.html))

- **1990–present:** *To Begin With… / To Resume Again…*. A serial editorial obra written for *Lacanian Ink*: the official author bibliography lists an opening text for issue 1 and continuations associated with issues 2–49; issue 50 also contains *To Resume Again…*. These are separate installments but also one deliberately resumed, unfinished textual project. ([official author bibliography](https://www.lacan.com/JosArt.htm); [issue 1 contents](https://www.lacan.com/lacink/1.htm); [issue 50 installment](https://www.lacan.com/lacinkXLL1.html))

- **After 2017:** *Time of a Tear*. A memorial editorial in *Lacanian Ink* 51, made in response to Judith Miller’s death; it is closer to a public manifestation of mourning than to a conventional critical essay. ([official text record](https://www.lacan.com/lacinkXLII8.html))

- **Date not securely established:** *The Symptom*. An online journal associated with Ayerza’s Lacan.com publishing project. The available site index repeatedly credits her editorials but does not provide sufficiently clear evidence to call the whole journal her sole creation; it is therefore best treated as an editorial collaboration rather than an individually authored work. ([journal contents and credits](https://www.lacan.com/articles.htm))

### Digital publishing projects

- **1997–present:** *Lacan.com*. Ayerza’s web archive and publishing platform for Lacanian psychoanalysis, philosophy, contemporary art, bibliographies, video, and related journals. It is not one stable “text” but an evolving digital environment whose changing structure is part of the work. ([site map](https://www.lacan.com/sitemap.htm); [Ayerza’s retrospective account](https://www.lacan.com/symptom/expose/))

- **Date not securely established:** *Perfume*. A multimedia area of Lacan.com combining writing, artists’ images, moving pictures, interviews, and linked projects. Authorship is distributed, so the site resists classification as Ayerza’s individual work; her role is more accurately editorial and curatorial. ([Perfume author page](https://www.lacan.com/perfume/josefinaayerza.htm); [writers and project index](https://www.lacan.com/perfume/writerlinks.html))

- **Date not securely established:** *Written/Spoken/Drawn*. A participatory component of *Perfume* assembling drawings by numerous invited writers and artists. The surviving credits demonstrate a collective manifestation, not sole authorship by Ayerza, and the accessible project documentation does not fix a reliable first-publication date. ([contributors’ documentation](https://www.lacan.com/perfume/writerlinks.html); [artist documentation](https://www.lacan.com/bios2.htm))

### Psychoanalytic and theoretical texts

- **1990:** *Jacques-Alain Miller’s Perversion*. An essay published in *Lacanian Ink* 1 and given first position on its cover; Ayerza’s later account confirms both authorship and placement. ([issue 1 contents](https://www.lacan.com/lacink/1.htm); [Ayerza’s retrospective account](https://www.lacan.com/symptom/expose/))

- *So She Unfolds*. A psychoanalytic essay on feminine jouissance, topology, mysticism, and Bernini, published through *Perfume*. The official page supplies no secure publication date. ([official text](https://www.lacan.com/perfume/so....htm))

- *Follie à deux*. A psychoanalytic text listed in Ayerza’s official author bibliography; the surviving index does not establish a date or original venue. ([official author bibliography](https://www.lacan.com/JosArt.htm))

- *There Is No Sexual Relation*. A psychoanalytic essay represented both in Ayerza’s author bibliography and in *The Symptom* contents. The indexed sources do not securely identify its first appearance. ([official author bibliography](https://www.lacan.com/JosArt.htm); [The Symptom index](https://www.lacan.com/articles.htm))

- **Spring 2010:** *Going in Circles*. A revised and reformatted version of the earlier *Lacanian Ink* 10 text *Rena Grant*, prepared by Ayerza for the Paris–USA Lacan Seminar and published in *The Symptom* 11. The two titles therefore denote different states of one evolving text rather than unquestionably independent works. ([publication note and text](https://www.lacan.com/symptom11/going-in.html))

- *The Case of Auntie Lady*. A psychoanalytic case text published in *The Symptom*; its contents page credits Ayerza but does not supply a reliable date. ([The Symptom index](https://www.lacan.com/articles.htm))

- *Nervenanhang, Nerve-annexation*. A psychoanalytic essay published in *The Symptom*; the bilingual title is part of the indexed work. ([The Symptom index](https://www.lacan.com/articles.htm))

- *Secret Celebration*. A psychoanalytic or autobiographical essay listed under Ayerza’s name in *The Symptom*. ([The Symptom index](https://www.lacan.com/articles.htm))

- *What Is a Picture?*. A theoretical text on the image, distinct from Jan Avgikos’s similarly titled *“What Is a Picture?” Times Two*. ([The Symptom index](https://www.lacan.com/articles.htm); [author and contributor record](https://www.lacan.com/bios.htm))

- *By a Window Overlooking Baltimore*. An essay associated with Ayerza’s account of Lacan in Baltimore and indexed under her authorship in *The Symptom*. ([The Symptom index](https://www.lacan.com/articles.htm); [Spanish retrospective](https://www.lacan.com/perfume/mirador3.htm))

- *Americans, Still One More Effort…*. An essay indexed under Ayerza’s authorship in *The Symptom*; no secure first-publication date is supplied by the accessible record. ([The Symptom index](https://www.lacan.com/articles.htm))

- *Being and Appearance*. An introductory paper for an Alain Badiou event at the Drawing Center, subsequently manifested online as a two-part audiovisual/text presentation. It is simultaneously a lecture, an event protocol, and a digital document rather than simply an article. ([official presentation](https://www.lacan.com/symptom7_articles/josefina.html))

- *Woman Does Not Exist*. A lecture or psychoanalytic paper repeatedly announced in Lacan.com newsletters; the accessible index confirms the title but does not securely establish its original date or full publication history. ([newsletter index](https://www.lacan.com/newsletter-index.html))

- *The Psychoses*. A psychoanalytic presentation announced by Lacan.com; the available index supplies neither a secure date nor enough evidence to identify it as a formally published essay. ([newsletter index](https://www.lacan.com/newsletter-index.html))

### Interviews and dialogues

- **1995:** *More Hysteria, Please*. A staged psychoanalytic dialogue or performance with theatre-maker Richard Foreman at Columbia University’s Deutsches Haus, later transcribed in *Lacanian Ink* 12. Because Foreman and Ayerza jointly produced the encounter, it is a collaborative event rather than Ayerza’s sole-authored text. ([performance transcript and venue](https://www.lacan.com/issue14.htm); [contributor record](https://www.lacan.com/perfume/writerlinks.html))

- *It Doesn’t Have to Be a Jew*. Ayerza’s interview with Slavoj Žižek, published in *Lusitania* and subsequently indexed in *The Symptom*. It is a jointly generated conversation, with Ayerza responsible for the interview rather than for every utterance. ([The Symptom index](https://www.lacan.com/articles.htm); [Žižek contributor record](https://www.lacan.com/biosB.htm))

- *El Mirador de Josefina Ayerza I–III*. A three-part Spanish-language retrospective and interview-like meditation on Lacan, the United States, and the making of *Lacanian Ink*. Its parts form a sequence rather than three securely independent works. ([part I](https://www.lacan.com/perfume/mirador1.htm); [part III and sequence navigation](https://www.lacan.com/perfume/mirador3.htm))

### Exhibition catalogue

- **2002–2003:** *Rosemarie Trockel*. A multi-author exhibition catalogue for the Goetz Collection/Institut für Auslandsbeziehungen project, containing a text by Ayerza and edited or credited in different records to several participants. It is not properly a sole-authored Ayerza book: institutional documentation names multiple writers, while a later bibliographic description lists Ayerza and Nina Bingel among its editors, so responsibility is distributed and the date appears variously as 2002 and 2003. ([project and catalogue record](https://www.officeforart.org/projects/); [contemporary catalogue description](https://www.barbarawien.de/dl/1catalogue.pdf); [Fundación Proa documentation](https://www.proa.org/eng/exhibicion-proa-rosemarie-trocket-textos.php))

### Public-event series

- **1999 onward:** *Lacanian Ink events*. Ayerza initiated a recurring New York series in which journal issues were launched through lectures, introductions, readings, and discussions at the New Museum, Drawing Center, Deitch Projects, Tilton Gallery, and other venues. The series is a curatorial project and live manifestation of the journal, not a single authored text; the surviving event archive documents activity from the 1999 launch of issue 15 through at least 2010. ([event chronology](https://www.lacan.com/issue14.htm); [Ayerza’s event recollections](https://www.lacan.com/josefinaslide/))
