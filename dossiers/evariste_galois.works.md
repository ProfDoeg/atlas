## Works by Évariste Galois

### Published mathematical articles

- **1829:** *Démonstration d’un théorème sur les fractions continues périodiques*. Research article proving a theorem about periodic continued fractions; Galois’s first publication, printed in Gergonne’s *Annales de mathématiques pures et appliquées*. ([Picard’s collected edition](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/%C5%92uvres_math%C3%A9matiques); [Neumann’s chronology](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/20742))

- **1830:** *Analyse d’un mémoire sur la résolution algébrique des équations*. Published abstract announcing his research on algebraic solvability; it is a manifestation of a larger memoir rather than that memoir itself. ([Picard’s collected edition](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/%C5%92uvres_math%C3%A9matiques); [Neumann’s critical edition](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/20740))

- **1830:** *Note sur la résolution des équations numériques*. Short research note on locating real roots of equations, published during Galois’s lifetime. ([Picard’s collected edition](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/%C5%92uvres_math%C3%A9matiques); [Neumann’s critical edition](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/20740))

- **1830:** *Sur la théorie des nombres*. Research memoir introducing what were later called “Galois imaginaries,” an early construction of finite fields. ([Neumann’s introduction](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/20742); [Picard’s collected edition](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/%C5%92uvres_math%C3%A9matiques))

- **1830:** *Notes sur quelques points d’analyse*. A compound article containing two short mathematical essays, one concerning differentiability and one curvature; its heterogeneity makes it less a unified memoir than a paired publication. ([Picard’s collected edition](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/%C5%92uvres_math%C3%A9matiques); [Ji’s study](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00407-025-00355-7))

### Lost or repeatedly remade mathematical projects

- **1829:** *Recherches algébriques*. Lost memoir presented to the Académie des sciences through Cauchy on 25 May. Whether it and the next submission were independent works or two parts of one project remains disputed; evidence also suggests that Galois may have withdrawn them rather than Cauchy simply losing them. ([Neumann’s chronology](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Extras/Neumann_Peter_papers/); [Tangente’s scholarly guide](https://tangente-mag.com/en/articles/scientific-publications-and-writings))

- **1829:** *Recherches sur les équations algébriques de degré premier*. Lost memoir presented on 1 June, possibly a supplement to *Recherches algébriques* rather than a separate obra. Only the Académie’s record securely preserves its title and existence. ([Tangente’s scholarly guide](https://tangente-mag.com/en/articles/scientific-publications-and-writings); [Neumann’s chronology](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Extras/Neumann_Peter_papers/))

- **1830:** *Mémoire sur les conditions de résolubilité des équations par radicaux* (Grand Prix version). A now-lost version submitted for the Académie’s Grand Prix; it disappeared after Fourier’s death. It should not be silently identified with the surviving 1831 redaction, although both belong to the same evolving project. ([Tangente’s scholarly guide](https://tangente-mag.com/en/articles/scientific-publications-and-writings); [Neumann’s introduction](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/20742))

### Surviving major mathematical memoirs

- **1831:** *Mémoire sur les conditions de résolubilité des équations par radicaux* (“Premier Mémoire”). A rewritten research memoir submitted to the Académie on 17 January 1831, rejected as insufficiently clear, returned to Galois, and revised again in prison; Liouville published it posthumously in 1846. It is therefore an obra with several textual states, not a single immutable text. ([Neumann’s introduction](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/20742); [Taton’s review of Bourgne–Azra](https://www.persee.fr/doc/rhs_0048-7996_1964_num_17_1_2295))

- **c. 1830–1832:** *Des équations primitives qui sont solubles par radicaux* (“Second Mémoire”). A surviving but unfinished memoir on primitive solvable equations, published posthumously by Liouville in 1846. Its incompleteness is part of the object rather than an editorial defect to be concealed. ([Picard’s collected edition](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/%C5%92uvres_math%C3%A9matiques); [Neumann’s critical edition](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/20740))

- **1832:** *Théorie des intégrales dont les différentielles sont des fonctions algébriques* (“Troisième Mémoire,” project). Only a one-page drafted beginning and related calculations survive; the finished memoir invoked in the testamentary letter never appeared. The obra here is principally a project whose surviving manifestations resist treatment as a completed work. ([Taton’s review of Bourgne–Azra](https://www.persee.fr/doc/rhs_0048-7996_1964_num_17_1_2295))

### Drafts, fragments, and mathematical working papers

- **1830:** *Version ancienne de la proposition I*. A draft state of a proposition later incorporated into the First Memoir; catalogued as manuscript Dossier 6, it is textual evidence of revision rather than an autonomous finished paper. ([Neumann’s contents](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/20740))

- **1830:** *Esquisse de la proposition V*. Draft of a proposition related to the First Memoir, preserved as Dossier 7 and first fully brought into the critical corpus by Bourgne and Azra. ([Neumann’s contents](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/20740); [Taton’s review](https://www.persee.fr/doc/rhs_0048-7996_1964_num_17_1_2295))

- **Undated, before 1832:** *Fragment déchiré*. A physically torn mathematical leaf catalogued as Dossier 8. It resists being called a work: its boundaries and intended destination cannot be recovered securely. ([Neumann’s contents](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/20740))

- **Undated, before 1832:** *Fragments sur les permutations et les équations*. Several working fragments assembled as Dossier 15; the collective title is editorial, not evidence that Galois designed a single work under that name. ([Neumann’s contents](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/20740); [Tannery’s manuscript catalogue](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Manuscrits_de_%C3%89variste_Galois))

- **Undated, before 1832:** *Fragments relatifs à la proposition I*. Draft calculations and formulations grouped as Dossier 16. They are workshop remains belonging to the First Memoir project, not a completed memoir. ([Neumann’s contents](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/20740))

- **Undated, before 1832:** *Fragments sur la théorie des équations*. A dossier of separate algebraic notes, catalogued as Dossier 17; its apparent unity is archival and editorial. ([Neumann’s contents](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/20740))

- **Undated, before 1832:** *Note sur les équations non primitives*. A short mathematical note preserved as Dossier 18, incomplete in development but sufficiently coherent to be treated as an individual study. ([Neumann’s contents](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/20740))

- **Undated, before 1832:** *Addition au Mémoire sur la résolution des équations*. A proposed addition or supplement to the equation memoir, catalogued as Dossier 19; its status is dependent rather than autonomous. ([Neumann’s contents](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/20740); [Tannery’s catalogue](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Manuscrits_de_%C3%89variste_Galois))

- **Undated, before 1832:** *Sur la division des fonctions elliptiques*. An unfinished mathematical study preserved as Dossier 20. It belongs to Galois’s broader elliptic-functions project and was not prepared as a final publication. ([Neumann’s contents](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/20740); [Tannery’s catalogue](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Manuscrits_de_%C3%89variste_Galois))

- **Undated, before 1832:** *Sur l’intégration des équations linéaires*. A short research essay preserved as Dossier 21; unlike many surrounding scraps, it has enough continuous exposition to resemble an unfinished article. ([Neumann’s contents](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/20740); [Ji’s study](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00407-025-00355-7))

- **Undated, before 1832:** *Recherches sur les surfaces du second degré*. A mathematical essay preserved as Dossier 22. Its title is present in the manuscript, but the investigation remained unpublished and incomplete. ([Neumann’s contents](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/20740); [Ji’s study](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00407-025-00355-7))

- **Undated, before 1832:** *Sur les intégrales eulériennes*. A beginning of exposition accompanied by calculations, preserved as Dossier 23; it is an unfinished investigation rather than a completed memoir. ([Neumann’s contents](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/20740); [Tannery’s description](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Galois_-_Manuscrits%2C_%C3%A9dition_Tannery%2C_1908/%C3%89crits_math%C3%A9matiques_in%C3%A9dits_de_Galois))

- **Undated, before 1832:** *Un théorème d’Abel*. Approximately twenty lines concerning Abel’s theorem, catalogued as Dossier 24. It is a reading or research fragment and resists classification as an independent obra. ([Neumann’s contents](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/20740); [Tannery’s description](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Galois_-_Manuscrits%2C_%C3%A9dition_Tannery%2C_1908/%C3%89crits_math%C3%A9matiques_in%C3%A9dits_de_Galois))

- **Undated, chiefly 1830–1832:** *Brouillons et calculs*. Roughly fifty loose leaves of calculations concerning Eulerian integrals, integral calculus, elliptic functions, substitution groups, and miscellaneous problems. Bourgne and Azra organized these remains into editorial sequences, but they resist enumeration as discrete works because Galois supplied neither stable titles nor final boundaries. ([Taton’s review of Bourgne–Azra](https://www.persee.fr/doc/rhs_0048-7996_1964_num_17_1_2295); [Tannery’s description](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Galois_-_Manuscrits%2C_%C3%A9dition_Tannery%2C_1908/%C3%89crits_math%C3%A9matiques_in%C3%A9dits_de_Galois))

### Publication projects, prefaces, and manifestos of mathematical practice

- **Undated, probably 1831–1832:** *Discours préliminaire*. A polemical preliminary discourse intended to frame publication of Galois’s researches; it functions partly as a manifesto against existing mathematical institutions and conventions. ([Tannery’s catalogue](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Manuscrits_de_%C3%89variste_Galois); [Neumann’s contents](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/20740))

- **Undated, probably 1831–1832:** *Projet de publication et catalogue*. A plan and inventory for publishing Galois’s mathematical writings, joined in its manuscript dossier to a note on Abel. It is a project in the strict sense: a designed future body of work that was never realized in Galois’s lifetime. ([Neumann’s contents](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/20740); [Taton’s review](https://www.persee.fr/doc/rhs_0048-7996_1964_num_17_1_2295))

- **1831:** *Deux Mémoires d’Analyse pure suivis d’une dissertation sur la classification des problèmes*. A proposed collective title or prospectus for two memoirs and a dissertation; no completed publication matching the announced architecture appeared. ([Tannery’s catalogue](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Manuscrits_de_%C3%89variste_Galois))

- **1831:** *Préface à deux mémoires d’analyse pure*. A prison-written preface attacking the reception and organization of mathematical research. It is both paratext and manifesto, acting to reposition the memoirs and their author rather than merely describing them. ([Neumann’s contents](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/20740); [MacTutor transcription and documentation](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Extras/Galois_Sainte_Pelagie_preface/))

- **Undated, probably 1831–1832:** *Sciences mathématiques. Discussions sur les progrès de l’analyse pure*. An unfinished theoretical and polemical essay about mathematical progress, preserved as Dossier 12. ([Tannery’s catalogue](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Manuscrits_de_%C3%89variste_Galois); [Neumann’s contents](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/20740))

- **Undated, probably 1831–1832:** *Il serait en dehors de la gravité…*. Untitled fragment conventionally identified by its opening words. Its fragmentary condition prevents a secure decision about whether it was an independent essay or part of another preface. ([Tannery’s catalogue](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Manuscrits_de_%C3%89variste_Galois))

- **Undated, probably 1831–1832:** *Ici comme dans toutes les sciences…*. Untitled methodological fragment, named editorially from its incipit and preserved as Dossier 13. It is a manifestation of an argument, not demonstrably a finished work. ([Tannery’s catalogue](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Manuscrits_de_%C3%89variste_Galois); [Neumann’s contents](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/20740))

- **Undated, probably 1831–1832:** *Sciences ; Hiérarchie ; Écoles*. A compact polemical manuscript on scientific organization and education, preserved as Dossier 14. Its headings give it identity, though not the completeness of a finished essay. ([Tannery’s catalogue](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Manuscrits_de_%C3%89variste_Galois); [Neumann’s contents](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/20740))

### Public interventions and protocols for teaching

- **3 December 1830:** *Lettre insérée dans la “Réplique à M. Guigniault et au Lycée”*. An initially anonymous public letter attacking the director of the École normale and describing the school’s conduct during the July Revolution; its performative purpose makes it a political intervention rather than merely correspondence. Galois’s authorship was inferred immediately and led to his expulsion. ([Dupuy’s documentary account](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/La_vie_d%E2%80%99%C3%89variste_Galois/Texte_entier); [Gazette chronology](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89variste_Galois))

- **30 December 1830:** *Évariste Galois, élève de l’École normale, à ses camarades d’études*. An open letter answering fellow students who had repudiated the earlier attack on Guigniault. It acts publicly to contest their account and is therefore closer to a manifesto or declaration than to private correspondence. ([Dupuy’s documentary account](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/La_vie_d%E2%80%99%C3%89variste_Galois/Texte_entier); [Gazette bibliography](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89variste_Galois))

- **2 January 1831:** *Sur l’enseignement des sciences : Des professeurs — Des ouvrages — Des examinateurs*. Published critique of mathematical teaching, textbooks, and competitive examinations. It combines manifesto and protocol: it attacks prevailing instruction while prescribing a different relation between teachers, students, and mathematical discovery. ([Gazette bibliography](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89variste_Galois); [APMEP dossier](https://www.apmep.fr/Presence-d-Evariste-Galois))

### Correspondence and final declarations

- **31 August 1829:** *Lettre à Antoine Demante*. Private letter to Galois’s uncle concerning his uncertain choice of career; the autograph is lost and the text survives through a family copy published by Bourgne and Azra. ([Correspondence inventory](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89variste_Galois))

- **31 March 1831:** *Lettre au président de l’Académie des sciences*. Formal but confrontational inquiry about the memoir submitted on 17 January. The surviving institutional copy makes this simultaneously correspondence and an act within the Académie’s review process. ([Neumann’s contents](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/20740); [Correspondence inventory](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89variste_Galois))

- **May 1831:** *Lettre à Auguste Chevalier depuis Sainte-Pélagie*. Prison letter known only through an excerpt printed by Chevalier; the complete text and autograph are lost, so the surviving manifestation cannot establish its original extent. ([Correspondence inventory](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89variste_Galois))

- **January 1832:** *Lettre à Céleste-Marie Guinard*. Prison letter to Galois’s aunt, formerly known through a photographic reproduction; its transmission is documentary rather than autograph. ([Correspondence inventory](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89variste_Galois))

- **25 May 1832:** *Lettre à Auguste Chevalier*. Personal letter known from Chevalier’s obituary and Dupuy’s reproduction; no autograph survives. ([Correspondence inventory](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89variste_Galois))

- **29 May 1832:** *Lettre à tous les républicains*. Farewell declaration explaining that Galois was not dying for the republican cause. Addressed to a political collectivity rather than an individual, it functions as a manifesto controlling the public meaning of his impending death. ([Dupuy’s documentary account](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/La_vie_d%E2%80%99%C3%89variste_Galois/Texte_entier); [Correspondence inventory](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89variste_Galois))

- **29 May 1832:** *Lettre à N. L. et V. D.*. Farewell letter to Napoléon Lebon and Vincent Delaunay, whose identities were established by later scholarship. Its text survives through Chevalier’s publication, not through an autograph. ([Correspondence inventory](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89variste_Galois))

- **29 May 1832:** *Lettre à Auguste Chevalier* (“Lettre testamentaire”). A letter, mathematical synopsis, publication instruction, and intellectual testament in one object: it reports unfinished discoveries while charging Chevalier to secure judgment and publication. The autograph survives as Dossier 2, although its first printing was probably made from a now-lost copy by Chevalier. ([Neumann’s introduction](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/20742); [Picard’s collected edition](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/%C5%92uvres_math%C3%A9matiques))

- **29–30 May 1832:** *Fragments de lettre de rupture copiés au verso de la lettre testamentaire*. Copies, apparently made by Galois, of one or possibly two letters received during the affair preceding his duel. They resist inclusion as Galois-authored works: copying made the surviving objects, but the words originated with someone else, and recent scholarship disputes whether the fragments represent one letter or two. ([Manuscript and attribution note](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89variste_Galois))

### School exercises and institutional writing

- **1828–1829:** *Copies scolaires conservées par Louis-Paul-Émile Richard*. Mathematical school exercises later incorporated into the manuscript archive as Dossier 26. These are things Galois made, but they resist the status of authored “works”: they are responses to assigned problems, and the surviving dossier is not necessarily a complete record of his school production. ([Manuscript guide](https://www.gresham.ac.uk/sites/default/files/3november2011_handout.pdf); [manuscript inventory](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89variste_Galois))

- **1829:** *Copie de mathématiques du concours d’admission à l’École préparatoire*. Examination script produced under a prescribed prompt and rediscovered outside the supposedly complete 1962 edition. It is best classified as an institutional performance rather than a research work. ([Ehrhardt’s archival study](https://numdam.org/articles/10.24033/rhm.146/))

- **1829:** *Copies des autres épreuves du concours d’admission à l’École préparatoire*. Galois also sat prescribed exercises in French discourse, philosophy, Latin translation, and physics; their archival survival and identification are not equally complete, so they should not be assigned invented individual titles. ([Ehrhardt’s study of the competition](https://paisajes.matcuer.unam.mx/IMG/pdf/ehrhardt_copie-galois-ecole-preparatoire.pdf))

- **Undated, 1829:** *Demande d’admission à l’École préparatoire*. A brief administrative application transmitted by the rector on 12 August 1829; Galois’s own enclosed request is undated. It is a made text, but an administrative instrument rather than an oeuvre in the literary or mathematical sense. ([Archival presentation of the application](https://www.jacquescassabois.com/ecole-preparatoire.html))
