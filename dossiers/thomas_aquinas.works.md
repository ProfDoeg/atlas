## Works by Thomas Aquinas

### Theological syntheses and summae

- **1252–1256; revised 1265–1266?:** *Scriptum super libros Sententiarum*. A four-book theological commentary produced from Aquinas’s teaching on Peter Lombard; it functions as an original synthesis rather than mere annotation. The surviving *Alia lectura* of Book I may represent a later Roman revision, but its precise status and Aquinas’s supervision remain disputed. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1259–1265:** *Summa contra Gentiles* (probably originally *Liber de veritate catholicae fidei contra errores infidelium*). A four-book theological-philosophical synthesis repeatedly revised by Aquinas; much of his autograph survives. Its older description as a missionary handbook is disputed, and its authentic medieval title is not completely certain. [Leonine Commission](https://www.commissio-leonina.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/List_Leonine_Volumes_24_09_2014.pdf)
- **1265–1273:** *Summa theologiae*. A pedagogical theological synthesis conceived for beginners and left unfinished at III, q. 90 when Aquinas stopped writing in December 1273. The later *Supplementum*, assembled by pupils chiefly from the *Scriptum super Sententiis*, is not a work made by Aquinas in that form. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)

### Disputed-question records

- **1256–1259:** *Quaestiones disputatae de veritate*. A redacted record of university disputations: twenty-nine questions and 253 articles extending well beyond “truth” into knowledge, will, goodness, and grace. Aquinas’s dictated original survives for much of qq. 2–22. [Leonine Commission](https://www.commissio-leonina.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/List_Leonine_Volumes_24_09_2014.pdf)
- **1265–1266:** *Quaestiones disputatae de potentia Dei*. Ten disputed questions, the first six on divine power and creation and the remaining four principally on Trinitarian theology. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1265–1266:** *Quaestio disputata de anima*. A twenty-one-article academic disputation on the human soul, probably preparatory to the corresponding treatment in the *Summa theologiae*. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1267–1268:** *Quaestio disputata de spiritualibus creaturis*. An eleven-article disputation about the ontology of angels and human souls as spiritual creatures. [Leonine Commission](https://www.commissio-leonina.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/List_Leonine_Volumes_24_09_2014.pdf)
- **c. 1269–1272:** *Quaestiones disputatae de malo*. Sixteen disputed questions on evil, sin, freedom, the capital vices, and demons; qq. 1–15 were probably published around 1270, while q. 16 was added around 1272, so the collection is composite rather than a single occasion. [Leonine Commission](https://www.commissio-leonina.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/List_Leonine_Volumes_24_09_2014.pdf)
- **1271–1272:** *Quaestio disputata de virtutibus in communi*. A disputation on the nature and subject of virtue, forming the first part of the collection conventionally called *De virtutibus*. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1271–1272:** *Quaestio disputata de caritate*. A disputation on charity belonging to the *De virtutibus* group. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1271–1272:** *Quaestio disputata de correctione fraterna*. A short disputation on fraternal correction, transmitted as part of the *De virtutibus* complex. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1271–1272:** *Quaestio disputata de spe*. A disputation on hope within the *De virtutibus* group. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1271–1272:** *Quaestio disputata de virtutibus cardinalibus*. A disputation on the cardinal virtues, completing the conventional *De virtutibus* collection. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1272:** *Quaestio disputata de unione Verbi incarnati*. A five-article Christological disputation, probably held near the end of Aquinas’s second Paris regency. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)

### Quodlibetal events and their written determinations

- **1256–1259:** *Quodlibet VII*. A master’s formal determination of questions proposed freely by a university audience; its exact Advent-or-Lent placement remains uncertain. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1256–1259:** *Quodlibet VIII*. A separately held public disputation and its literary determination; exact dating within Aquinas’s first Paris regency is debated. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1256–1259:** *Quodlibet IX*. A public, audience-directed disputation surviving in Aquinas’s subsequently organized determination. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1256–1259:** *Quodlibet X*. A public disputation on freely submitted theoretical and practical questions; its exact semester is uncertain. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1256–1259:** *Quodlibet XI*. A written determination arising from Aquinas’s first Parisian series of quodlibetal disputations. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1268–1272:** *Quodlibet I*. A public disputation and determination from Aquinas’s second Paris regency; exact seasonal dating remains debated. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1268–1272:** *Quodlibet II*. A separately constituted quodlibetal disputation whose questions were chosen by participants. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1268–1272:** *Quodlibet III*. A formal determination of a public disputation from the second Paris regency. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1272, probably Lent:** *Quodlibet IV*. A public disputation whose subjects include the entry of young people into religious life and the relation of counsels to precepts; its relation in time to *Contra retrahentes* is disputed. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1268–1272:** *Quodlibet V*. A separately held quodlibetal event and its ordered written determination. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1268–1272:** *Quodlibet VI*. A public disputation and written determination from the later Paris period; its precise date is uncertain. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1268–1272:** *Quodlibet XII*. A reportatio of a later Paris disputation which Aquinas apparently did not revise; it therefore resists being treated simply as an authored book, although the teaching event is his. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)

### Biblical lectures, commentaries, and compilations

- **Before 1252, probably:** *Expositio super Isaiam ad litteram*. A cursory literal exposition of Isaiah, accompanied by marginal *collationes* for pastoral or spiritual development; its traditional Paris dating has yielded to a probable late-Cologne date. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **Before 1252, probably:** *Super Ieremiam*. A cursory literal commentary on Jeremiah with associated *collationes*, probably originating in Aquinas’s biblical teaching at Cologne. [Thomas-Institut catalogue](https://www.thomas-von-aquin.de/thomas-von-aquin/schriften/)
- **Before 1252, probably:** *Super Threnos*. A literal exposition of Lamentations, transmitted alongside the Jeremiah commentary but catalogued as a distinct work. [Thomas-Institut catalogue](https://www.thomas-von-aquin.de/thomas-von-aquin/schriften/)
- **1256:** *Rigans montes de superioribus*. Aquinas’s inaugural university lecture or *principium*, a performed academic act on the communication of sacred wisdom that survives as a text. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1256:** *Hic est liber mandatorum Dei*. A second inaugural address, probably the *resumptio*, combining a commendation of Scripture with a division of the biblical books. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1261–1265:** *Expositio super Iob ad litteram*. A literal exposition of Job, probably produced for Aquinas’s Dominican brethren at Orvieto and organized around providence and the suffering of the just. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1262/1263–1264:** *Glossa continua super Matthaeum*. The Matthew portion of the work later called the *Catena aurea*: a commissioned, verse-by-verse assemblage of patristic exposition made for Urban IV. Its obra is simultaneously compilation and critical arrangement rather than newly authored continuous commentary. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1265–1268:** *Glossa continua super Marcum*. The Mark portion of the *Catena aurea*, a structured compilation of patristic extracts dedicated with the remaining three Gospels to Cardinal Annibaldo degli Annibaldi. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1265–1268:** *Glossa continua super Lucam*. The Luke portion of the *Catena aurea*, made by selecting, translating, and arranging Greek and Latin exegetical authorities into continuous exposition. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1265–1268:** *Glossa continua super Ioannem*. The John portion of the *Catena aurea* and the fourth component of this commissioned patristic compilation. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1265–1273?:** *Expositio et lectura super Epistolas Pauli Apostoli*. A deliberately conceived corpus of lectures on Romans through Hebrews, probably reflecting more than one course in Rome, Paris, or Naples. Its material has unequal authorial status: Romans 1–8 was revised directly, much survives as reportatio, 1 Corinthians 7:10–10 is missing and was early replaced by material attributed to Peter of Tarentaise, and the dating of Hebrews is disputed. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1269–1270:** *Lectura super Matthaeum*. A reportatio of Aquinas’s oral lectures on Matthew. The traditional printed text is incomplete and interpolated with another author’s commentary, although a manuscript preserving a fuller Aquinas text has been identified. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1270–1272:** *Lectura super Ioannem*. Reginald of Piperno’s reportatio of Aquinas’s lectures on John; Aquinas’s own revision of the written record is considered unlikely, so the surviving text mediates an oral creation through another hand. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1273, probably:** *Postilla super Psalmos*. Reginald of Piperno’s reportatio of an unfinished lecture course covering Psalms 1–54, traditionally connected with Aquinas’s last weeks of teaching but not securely dated. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)

### Expositions of Aristotle

- **1267–1268:** *Sententia libri De anima*. A close doctrinal exposition of Aristotle’s three-book study of the soul, initiating Aquinas’s sustained Aristotelian commentary project. [Thomas-Institut catalogue](https://www.thomas-von-aquin.de/thomas-von-aquin/schriften/)
- **1268–1269:** *Sententia libri De sensu et sensato*. An exposition joining Aristotle’s *De sensu et sensato* and *De memoria et reminiscentia* as two parts of one commentary. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1268–1269, probably:** *Sententia super Physicam*. A complete exposition of the eight books of Aristotle’s *Physics*; an older later dating associated with Weisheipl has been rejected by subsequent scholarship. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **Before 1270 or 1273:** *Sententia super Meteora*. An unfinished exposition of Aristotle’s *Meteorology*. Its date is disputed; part of Aquinas’s Book II commentary is lost, and older editions added unauthentic continuations. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1270–1271:** *Expositio libri Peryermenias*. A close logical and semantic exposition of Aristotle’s *De interpretatione*, dedicated to William Berthout and unfinished in Book II. [Leonine Commission](https://www.commissio-leonina.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/List_Leonine_Volumes_24_09_2014.pdf)
- **1271–1272:** *Expositio libri Posteriorum*. An exposition of Aristotle’s *Posterior Analytics*, begun in Paris using one Latin translation and completed in Naples using William of Moerbeke’s version. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1271–1272:** *Sententia libri Ethicorum*. A complete doctrinal exposition of Aristotle’s *Nicomachean Ethics*, composed while Aquinas was also working on the moral portion of the *Summa theologiae*. [Leonine Commission](https://www.commissio-leonina.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/List_Leonine_Volumes_24_09_2014.pdf)
- **c. 1270:** *Tabula libri Ethicorum*. An alphabetical working index to Aristotle’s *Ethics* and Albert the Great’s commentary, initially assembled on slips by Aquinas’s secretaries. Aquinas did not revise the clean copy, and the tool remains unfinished, so it resists simple classification as an authored treatise. [Leonine Commission](https://www.commissio-leonina.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/List_Leonine_Volumes_24_09_2014.pdf)
- **1269–1272:** *Sententia libri Politicorum*. An unfinished exposition of Aristotle’s *Politics*, ending authentically in Book III, chapter 6. Peter of Auvergne supplied the continuation found in non-Leonine editions, and early printed books also corrupt Aquinas’s portion. [Leonine Commission](https://www.commissio-leonina.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/List_Leonine_Volumes_24_09_2014.pdf)
- **c. 1270–1272:** *Sententia super Metaphysicam*. An exposition of Aristotle’s *Metaphysics* whose sequence of composition, revisions, place, and precise dates remain difficult to reconstruct; it was probably begun in Paris and possibly completed in Naples. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1272–1273:** *Sententia super librum De caelo et mundo*. An unfinished cosmological exposition of Aristotle, breaking off shortly after the beginning of Book III. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1272–1273:** *Sententia super libros De generatione et corruptione*. Aquinas’s final unfinished Aristotelian exposition, ending in Book I, chapter 5; later continuations are not his. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)

### Expositions of Boethius and Neoplatonic texts

- **1257–1259:** *Super Boetium De Trinitate*. An unfinished commentary combining brief textual exposition with extended questions on theology and the division and epistemology of the sciences; part survives in Aquinas’s autograph. [Leonine Commission](https://www.commissio-leonina.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/List_Leonine_Volumes_24_09_2014.pdf)
- **After 1259; exact date unknown:** *Expositio libri Boetii De hebdomadibus*. A line-by-line metaphysical exposition centered on participation; scholarship has not securely established its date or circumstances. [Leonine Commission](https://www.commissio-leonina.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/List_Leonine_Volumes_24_09_2014.pdf)
- **1261–1268:** *Super librum Dionysii De divinis nominibus*. An exposition of Pseudo-Dionysius’s *Divine Names*; whether it belongs to the Orvieto or Roman period, and whether it originated as oral teaching, remain uncertain. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1272:** *Super librum De causis*. An exposition of the *Liber de causis* that compares it with Proclus’s *Elements of Theology* and identifies the former as an Arabic philosophical derivative rather than a work by Aristotle. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)

### Polemical interventions

- **1256:** *Contra impugnantes Dei cultum et religionem*. A polemical defense of mendicant religious life, teaching, preaching, confession, and poverty against William of Saint-Amour; it acts within an institutional conflict and therefore approaches a manifesto as well as a treatise. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1269–1270:** *De perfectione spiritualis vitae*. A polemical yet systematic response to Gerard of Abbeville on Christian perfection and mendicant life. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1271:** *Contra doctrinam retrahentium a religione*. A polemical intervention against arguments discouraging entry into religious life; whether it preceded or followed the related *Quodlibet IV* is disputed. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1270:** *De unitate intellectus contra Averroistas*. A sharply directed Parisian polemic rejecting the thesis that all human beings share one separate possible intellect. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1271, probably:** *De aeternitate mundi*. A controversial intervention arguing that reason cannot demonstrate either a temporal beginning or an eternal duration of the world, while creation’s beginning remains an article of faith. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)

### Independent treatises and unfinished projects

- **1252–1256:** *De ente et essentia*. A compact philosophical treatise on being, essence, logical intentions, and their relations, written for Aquinas’s Dominican companions before he became master. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **Before 1256:** *De principiis naturae ad fratrem Sylvestrum*. A concise instructional treatise on matter, form, privation, and the causes of natural change; its precise date, possibly even before Aquinas’s Paris baccalaureate, is uncertain. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1265–1267 and perhaps 1272–1273:** *Compendium theologiae ad fratrem Raynaldum*. A deliberately brief theological project organized by faith, hope, and charity. Aquinas completed the part on faith, later appears to have resumed the part on hope, and left it unfinished at Part II, chapter 10 before reaching charity. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **c. 1267:** *De regno ad regem Cypri*. An unfinished protocol-like work of moral and political instruction for a king, probably Hugh II of Cyprus; Aquinas’s authentic text ends in Book II, and the traditional continuation is by another author. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **After mid-1271:** *De substantiis separatis*. An unfinished treatise on angels, first surveying ancient philosophy and then presenting Christian teaching; it breaks off during the discussion of angelic sin. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)

### Commissions, responsa, letters, and expert opinions

- **1261–1265:** *Expositio super primam et secundam Decretalem ad Archidiaconum Tudertinum*. A commissioned doctrinal exposition of the Fourth Lateran Council’s decrees *Firmiter* and *Damnamus*, probably addressed to the archdeacon of Todi. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1261–1270:** *De articulis fidei et Ecclesiae sacramentis ad archiepiscopum Panormitanum*. A concise protocol of Catholic belief and the seven sacraments, organized partly around errors each doctrine excludes; its broad possible date follows the recipient’s episcopate. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1262:** *De emptione et venditione ad tempus*. A practical moral responsum to John of Viterbo concerning credit sales, pricing, and what contemporaries classified as usury. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1263–1264:** *Contra errores Graecorum*. An expert report commissioned by Urban IV on a florilegium of Greek patristic texts. Its traditional title is misleading: Aquinas often diagnoses ambiguous or defective translations rather than simply attacking Greek doctrine, but his report is constrained by an unreliable source collection. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **Shortly after 1265:** *De rationibus fidei ad Cantorem Antiochenum*. An apologetic protocol advising an unidentified cantor of Antioch how Christian doctrines may be defended in discussion with Muslims, Greeks, Armenians, and others without pretending rationally to prove faith. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1265–1267:** *Responsio de 108 articulis ad magistrum Ioannem de Vercellis*. An expert opinion requested by the Dominican master general, assessing 108 allegedly erroneous propositions extracted from Peter of Tarentaise’s Sentences commentary. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **22 February 1269, probably:** *De forma absolutionis*. A commissioned theological opinion defending the indicative formula of sacramental absolution against objections favoring a deprecatory formula. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1269:** *De secreto*. A committee report concerning a religious superior’s authority over confidential knowledge. It resists classification as Aquinas’s work in the ordinary sense: he participated in the commission and his position appears in two questions, but the document is collective. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1269–1272:** *De iudiciis astrorum*. A short responsum, perhaps addressed to Reginald of Piperno, distinguishing natural celestial effects from illicit astrological claims about free human acts; its date and recipient are not secure. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1268–1272:** *De operationibus occultis naturae ad quendam militem ultramontanum*. A short advisory treatise distinguishing hidden natural causation from alleged demonic or magical operations; the unnamed recipient cannot be identified. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1270 or 1271:** *Liber de sortibus ad dominum Iacobum de Tonengo*. A five-chapter moral and theological assessment of lots and divination addressed to the papal chaplain James of Tonengo. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **2 April 1271:** *Responsio de 43 articulis ad magistrum Ioannem de Vercellis*. A dated expert report on disputed propositions, especially cosmological ones, commissioned by the Dominican master general. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1271:** *Responsio de 30 articulis ad lectorem Venetum*. The first of two expert responses to Bassiano of Lodi on cosmology, celestial influence, angels, and related questions. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1271:** *Responsio de 36 articulis ad lectorem Venetum*. A revised and expanded second response prompted by further questions from the Venetian students and incorporating material related to the *Responsio de 43 articulis*. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **c. 1271:** *Responsio de 6 articulis ad lectorem Bisuntinum*. A reply to Gerard, lector at Besançon, dismissing several extravagant preaching claims and addressing one question about sacramental confession; its date is uncertain. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1271:** *Epistola ad ducissam Brabantiae*, also transmitted as *Ad comitissam Flandriae* and *De regimine Iudaeorum*. A political and financial advisory letter, probably to Margaret of Constantinople, countess of Flanders, rather than the duchess named in its familiar title; much of it concerns matters other than Jewish governance. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **c. 1270–1271:** *De mixtione elementorum ad magistrum Philippum de Castrocaeli*. A natural-philosophical reply concerning the four elements and their qualities, addressed to the physician Philip of Castrocaeli; its exact date is uncertain. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1273?:** *De motu cordis ad magistrum Philippum de Castrocaeli*. A short natural-philosophical argument that the heart’s motion is natural rather than violent; the proposed Neapolitan date is conjectural. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **Mid-February 1274:** *Epistola ad Bernardum abbatem Casinensem*. Aquinas’s last datable work, a letter explaining a passage of Gregory the Great on divine foreknowledge and human freedom, written during his final journey. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)

### Liturgical Gesamtkunstwerk

- **Before 11 August 1264:** *Officium de festo Corporis Christi*—the *Sacerdos in aeternum* office and *Cibavit eos* Mass. A commissioned liturgical ensemble joining scriptural compilation, prayers, readings, chants, poetry, and ritual performance for Corpus Christi; it is closer to a medieval Gesamtkunstwerk and protocol for communal worship than to a “book.” Its attribution was long disputed but was accepted by later scholarship following Pierre-Marie Gy; the precise attribution of every individual poetic unit still warrants caution. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)

### Preaching cycles and catechetical manifestations

- **1261–1268 or 1273:** *Collationes in decem praecepta*. A vernacular Italian preaching cycle on the two commandments of charity and the Ten Commandments, surviving through Peter of Andria’s later Latin reportatio; the performance, translation, and written object are therefore not identical creations. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1273, probably:** *Collationes in Symbolum Apostolorum*. A catechetical sermon cycle on the Apostles’ Creed, probably preached in the vernacular at Naples and preserved in a Latin reportorial form; its exact dating is less secure than that of the Lord’s Prayer cycle. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **Lent 1273, probably:** *Collationes in orationem dominicam*. A vernacular preaching cycle on the Lord’s Prayer, reported in Latin by Reginald of Piperno. The first petition in older printed editions contains an unauthentic substitution derived from Aldobrandino of Toscanella. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **1268–1272 or 1273:** *Collatio in salutationem angelicam*. A sermon and collatio on the Hail Mary, probably preached during Aquinas’s second Paris period, though some catalogues place it with the Neapolitan catechetical cycles. Its transmitted text is a report of a performed sermon rather than an authorial manuscript. [Thomas-Institut catalogue](https://www.thomas-von-aquin.de/thomas-von-aquin/schriften/)

### Individual university sermons and sermon remnants

- **Date uncertain:** *Veniet desideratus*. An academic sermon preserved under its biblical incipit; the Leonine editors include it among the sermons attributed to Aquinas, but certainty varies across the sermon corpus. [Leonine *Sermones*](https://www.persee.fr/doc/phlou_0035-3841_2016_num_114_2_8537_t1_0304_0000_3)
- **Date uncertain:** *Lauda et laetare*. A university sermon known by its incipit and preserved through manuscript reporting rather than an autograph. [Leonine *Sermones*](https://www.persee.fr/doc/phlou_0035-3841_2016_num_114_2_8537_t1_0304_0000_3)
- **Date uncertain:** *Abiciamus opera tenebrarum*. An academic sermon, in some witnesses accompanied by a collatio; its surviving textual form may represent only part of the preached event. [Thomas-Institut sermon list](https://www.thomas-von-aquin.de/thomas-von-aquin/schriften/)
- **Date uncertain:** *Hosanna filio David*. An academic sermon transmitted under its incipit; no fixed authorial title is known. [Thomas-Institut sermon list](https://www.thomas-von-aquin.de/thomas-von-aquin/schriften/)
- **Date uncertain:** *Ecce rex tuus*. A university sermon surviving in reportorial form; related sermons by Reginald of Piperno complicate the textual and attributional history. [Thomas-Institut sermon list](https://www.thomas-von-aquin.de/thomas-von-aquin/schriften/)
- **Date uncertain:** *Caelum et terra transibunt*. An academic sermon identified by its opening words, not by a title demonstrably assigned by Aquinas. [Thomas-Institut sermon list](https://www.thomas-von-aquin.de/thomas-von-aquin/schriften/)
- **Date uncertain:** *Ecce ego mitto*. A university sermon whose textual survival records a preaching manifestation rather than necessarily a text revised by Aquinas. [Thomas-Institut sermon list](https://www.thomas-von-aquin.de/thomas-von-aquin/schriften/)
- **Date uncertain:** *Puer Iesus*. An academic sermon transmitted from Aquinas’s preaching activity; its precise occasion is not securely established. [Thomas-Institut sermon list](https://www.thomas-von-aquin.de/thomas-von-aquin/schriften/)
- **Date uncertain:** *Exiit qui seminat*. A university sermon known from manuscript reportation and conventionally named from its incipit. [Thomas-Institut sermon list](https://www.thomas-von-aquin.de/thomas-von-aquin/schriften/)
- **Date uncertain:** *Petite et accipietis*. Long printed as an authentic Aquinas sermon but rejected as his by the final Leonine assessment; it is included here only as a disputed historical attribution, not as something confidently made by him. [Study of the Leonine edition](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/13660691.2019.1662564)
- **Date uncertain:** *Emitte spiritum tuum*. An academic sermon included in the modern sermon corpus, with attribution resting on manuscript and internal evidence rather than autograph testimony. [Thomas-Institut sermon list](https://www.thomas-von-aquin.de/thomas-von-aquin/schriften/)
- **Date uncertain:** *Seraphim stabant*. A university sermon surviving under its incipit; the degree of certainty assigned to individual sermons differs in the Leonine edition. [Leonine *Sermones*](https://www.persee.fr/doc/phlou_0035-3841_2016_num_114_2_8537_t1_0304_0000_3)
- **Date uncertain:** *Homo quidam fecit cenam magnam*. An academic sermon recovered outside older complete editions and accepted into the modern critical corpus. [University of St. Thomas guide](https://libguides.stthomas.edu/c.php?g=88920&p=572463)
- **Date uncertain:** *Attendite a falsis prophetis*. A university sermon preserved through reportorial notes, possibly including both sermon and collatio. [Thomas-Institut sermon list](https://www.thomas-von-aquin.de/thomas-von-aquin/schriften/)
- **Date uncertain:** *Homo quidam erat dives*. An academic sermon named by its incipit; the written witness is not necessarily a complete transcript of its oral manifestation. [Thomas-Institut sermon list](https://www.thomas-von-aquin.de/thomas-von-aquin/schriften/)
- **Date uncertain:** *Inveni David*. A university sermon attributed to Aquinas in the critical sermon tradition; its precise date and venue remain uncertain. [Leonine *Sermones*](https://www.persee.fr/doc/phlou_0035-3841_2016_num_114_2_8537_t1_0304_0000_3)
- **Date uncertain:** *Lux orta est iusto*. An academic sermon known by its scriptural incipit and transmitted in a mediated written form. [Thomas-Institut sermon list](https://www.thomas-von-aquin.de/thomas-von-aquin/schriften/)
- **Date uncertain:** *Germinet terra*. A university sermon whose surviving record may preserve only an outline of the preaching event. [Thomas-Institut sermon list](https://www.thomas-von-aquin.de/thomas-von-aquin/schriften/)
- **Date uncertain:** *Beati qui habitant*. An academic sermon accepted within the modern Aquinas sermon corpus, though not supported by an authorial manuscript. [Thomas-Institut sermon list](https://www.thomas-von-aquin.de/thomas-von-aquin/schriften/)
- **Date uncertain:** *Beata gens*. A university sermon transmitted under its incipit; its date is not securely recoverable. [Thomas-Institut sermon list](https://www.thomas-von-aquin.de/thomas-von-aquin/schriften/)
- **Date uncertain:** *Beatus vir*. An academic sermon established and published through modern manuscript research; it was absent from older standard collections. [University of St. Thomas guide](https://libguides.stthomas.edu/c.php?g=88920&p=572463)
- **Date uncertain:** *Sapientia confortabit sapientem*. Only a protheme—the preliminary component of a sermon—survives. It is a fragmentary manifestation of Aquinas’s preaching, not a complete work in the usual sense. [Study of the Leonine edition](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/13660691.2019.1662564)
- **Date uncertain:** *Surgere*. Only scraps or a fragment of a sermon survive, making both the original extent and the proper boundary of the obra unrecoverable. [Thomas-Institut sermon list](https://www.thomas-von-aquin.de/thomas-von-aquin/schriften/)

### Prayer of disputed attribution

- **Date unknown:** *Adoro te devote*. A Eucharistic prayer or hymn traditionally attributed to Aquinas. André Wilmart argued against the attribution, while later manuscript and biographical research supplied substantial arguments in its favor; it should therefore be retained as disputed rather than silently accepted or rejected. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)

### Things formerly presented as his but not securely his works

- **Late thirteenth century, probably:** *De fallaciis*. A beginner’s logical tract once imagined as a youthful work from Aquinas’s confinement at Roccasecca; modern scholarship regards it as inauthentic and probably the work of a southern French arts master. It is not an Aquinas obra, but its long attribution belongs to the history of his manufactured corpus. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **Late thirteenth century, probably:** *De propositionibus modalibus*. A short logical tract formerly paired with *De fallaciis* as youthful Aquinas; it is now regarded as inauthentic. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **Dates unknown:** *De modo studendi* and the prayer beginning *Concede mihi, misericors Deus*. Frequently attributed devotional or advisory texts whose authorship is not established securely enough for inclusion among Aquinas’s authenticated works. [Oxford chronological catalogue](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/28242/chapter/213362059)
- **Dates unknown:** *De natura accidentis; De natura generis; De quattuor oppositis; Summa totius logicae; De principio individuationis; De demonstratione; De dilectione Dei et proximi; De dimensionibus indeterminatis; De divinis moribus; De eruditione principis; De expositione missae; De fato; De humanitate Christi; De instantibus; De intellectu et intelligibili; De inventione medii; De natura loci; De natura materiae; De natura syllogismorum; De natura verbi intellectus; De officio sacerdotis; De potentiis animae; De puritate conscientiae et modo confitendi; De quo est et quod est; De tempore; De unitate vel pluralitate formarum; De usuris in communi; De venerabili sacramento altaris* in its several forms; *De virtutibus et vitiis; Primus tractatus de universalibus; Secundus tractatus de universalibus*. These titles entered expansive early-modern Aquinas collections but are inauthentic or insufficiently supported; they are one historical accretion rather than things Aquinas can be said to have made. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
- **Dates unknown:** *De differentia verbi divini et humani; De sensu respectu singularium et intellectu respectu universalium; De natura luminis*. These circulated as independent opuscules but are extracts cut from authentic larger works, not independently made texts. [Torrell–Emery catalogue](https://tomasdeaquino.org/breve-catalogo-de-las-obras-de-santo-tomas-j-p-torrell-iniciacion-santo-tomas-de-aquino/)
