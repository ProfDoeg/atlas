## Works by Georges Hébert

### Educational systems, protocols, and designed environments

- **1904–1912:** *Méthode naturelle*. An evolving system—and therefore an obra rather than a single text—combining ten families of useful movement with moral education; Hébert first tested it among naval personnel and publicly named it “méthode naturelle” around 1912. Sources differ by several years when assigning its moment of “creation,” because the practice preceded its definitive name and exposition. ([French Ministry of Armed Forces](https://www.colsbleus.defense.gouv.fr/fr/le-saviez-vous-hebertisme); [Villaret and Delaplace](https://shs.cairn.info/article/STA_063_0029/pdf?lang=fr))
- **1905–1914:** *Training program at the École des fusiliers-marins, Lorient*. A practical and institutional manifestation of Hébert’s developing method, not a separately titled work: he reorganized physical instruction and tested it on successive cohorts of sailors. Its status as an obra is shared with the naval institution and the bodies of its participants, rather than fixed in one authored object. ([Centre Hébertiste](https://www.hebertisme.org/l-histoire); [French Ministry of Armed Forces](https://www.colsbleus.defense.gouv.fr/fr/le-saviez-vous-hebertisme))
- **1913–1914:** *Technical program of the Collège d’athlètes de Reims*. Hébert directed the college’s physical program and made it a major demonstration ground for the Méthode naturelle, but he did not single-handedly make the institution: Melchior de Polignac founded and financed it, and Jean Bouin also participated. It therefore resists attribution as a wholly Hébertian work. ([Bibliothèque municipale de Reims](https://www.bm-reims.fr/patrimoine/georges-hebert.aspx?_lg=fr-FR); [French Ministry of Armed Forces](https://www.cheminsdememoire.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2024-09/Livret_100_sportifs_2024.pdf))
- **1916:** *Parcours du combattant*. A standardized obstacle-course protocol devised for rapidly training wartime troops; some secondary accounts date the invention to 1915, while the detailed Centre Hébertiste chronology places its normalization in 1916. It is a repeatable arrangement of bodies, terrain, obstacles, and instructions rather than a book. ([Centre Hébertiste](https://www.hebertisme.org/l-histoire); [French Ministry of Armed Forces](https://www.colsbleus.defense.gouv.fr/fr/le-saviez-vous-hebertisme); [Fédération Française Sports pour Tous](https://oldcd.sportspourtous.org/sites/cd.sportspourtous.org/files/Essonne/files/hebertisme_presentation.pdf))
- **1918–1919:** *La Palestra de Deauville*. A women’s and children’s educational community, training center, and landscaped physical-culture complex—a Gesamtkunstwerk joining exercise, communal life, manual work, dance, gardens, and nature. Accounts call it Hébert’s creation but also identify Yvonne Moreau and Hébert’s former Reims instructors as founders or organizers; 1918 is given for its creation and 1919 for its effective opening. ([Centre Hébertiste](https://www.hebertisme.org/l-histoire); [Pour l’Ère nouvelle](https://mrsh.unicaen.fr/pen/pdf/1927-30.pdf); [Archives du Calvados](https://archives.calvados.fr/document/brochure---aux-sports-citoyens-))
- **1920s–1940s:** *Champs d’ébats*. A project for open-air “centres of physical, virile and moral regeneration”: adaptable grounds whose obstacles and terrain materialized the Méthode naturelle. It resists treatment as a single designed site because Hébert proposed a reproducible type, later documenting the social project in a 1944 book. ([BnF author bibliography](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/rechercher.do?index=AUT3&numNotice=12050371); [Jacques Gleyse, “La labellisation de la méthode”](https://archives.uness.fr/revue-eps/media/articles/pdf/70266-9.pdf))

### Foundational expositions and polemics

- **1907:** *L’Éducation physique raisonnée*. Hébert’s first book-length exposition, a treatise on rational physical education with a preface by Georges Demenÿ; although the BnF copy itself is undated, the Hébert bibliography conventionally assigns it to 1907. ([BnF](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb30584724s); [Methode Naturelle bibliography](https://methodenaturelle.de/books-and-works/))
- **1909–1910:** *Guide pratique d’éducation physique*. A comprehensive illustrated manual and early general presentation of Hébert’s system. Its first-publication date is bibliographically unstable: a surviving record gives 1909, while later Hébert bibliographies commonly give 1910; these should not be mistaken for two separate works. ([ILEPS Infothèque](https://biblio.ipsl-edu.com/Default/doc/SYRACUSE/39930/guide-pratique-d-education-physique); [Methode Naturelle bibliography](https://methodenaturelle.de/books-and-works/))
- **1911:** *Le Code de la force: La Force physique, ses éléments constitutifs et sa mesure pratique*. A measurement protocol and theoretical treatise defining physical force through constituent capacities and practical tests. Later substantially revised editions remain manifestations of this work, not additional titles. ([BnF author bibliography](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/rechercher.do?index=AUT3&numNotice=12050371); [Methode Naturelle bibliography](https://methodenaturelle.de/books-and-works/))
- **1912:** *L’Éducation physique ou l’Entraînement complet par la méthode naturelle: Exposé et résultats*. A documentary exposition, manifesto for the named method, and record of its early applications and reception; its repeatedly enlarged editions incorporated reports and press material without becoming separate obras. ([Methode Naturelle bibliography](https://methodenaturelle.de/books-and-works/); [Gleyse and Soares](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/271723370_L'oeuvre_de_Georges_Hebert_au_Bresil_et_en_France_dans_les_ecrits_sur_l'Education_physique_Deux_facettes_de_la_nature_1909-1957))
- **1913:** *La Culture virile et les devoirs physiques de l’officier combattant*. A military-ethical treatise that makes physical cultivation act upon character and duty, approaching a manifesto rather than merely describing exercises. The substantially retitled 1943 fourth edition, *La Culture virile par l’action physique*, is a revised manifestation of the same work. ([BnF author bibliography](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/rechercher.do?index=AUT3&numNotice=12050371); [Methode Naturelle bibliography](https://methodenaturelle.de/books-and-works/))
- **1919:** *L’Éducation physique féminine: Muscle et Beauté plastique*. A treatise and training program adapting the Méthode naturelle to women’s physical education; later editions circulated under shortened variants such as *Muscle et beauté plastique féminine*. ([BnF author bibliography](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/rechercher.do?index=AUT3&numNotice=12050371); [Methode Naturelle bibliography](https://methodenaturelle.de/books-and-works/))
- **1925:** *Le Sport contre l’éducation physique*. A polemical manifesto opposing specialized, competitive, spectacular sport to comprehensive physical education. Later editions—including the often-cited fourth edition of 1946—are not separate works. ([Methode Naturelle bibliography](https://methodenaturelle.de/books-and-works/); [Georges Hébert bibliography at the BnF](https://data.bnf.fr/fr/ark:/12148/cb12050371t.pdf))
- **1944:** *Une œuvre féconde et de haute portée sociale: Les Champs d’ébats, centres de régénération physique, virile et morale*. A social-programmatic book presenting the champs d’ébats as reproducible educational environments; it is simultaneously documentation, advocacy, and a protocol for making places. ([BnF author bibliography](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/rechercher.do?index=AUT3&numNotice=12050371); [Gleyse and Soares](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/271723370_L'oeuvre_de_Georges_Hebert_au_Bresil_et_en_France_dans_les_ecrits_sur_l'Education_physique_Deux_facettes_de_la_nature_1909-1957))

### Lesson protocols and instructors’ manuals

- **1913–1915:** *Ma Leçon-type d’entraînement complet et utilitaire*. A protocol for conducting a complete, continuous, useful training lesson. Bibliographies often give 1915 because the BnF records a third edition then, while a scholarly study identifies a 1913 version; the original date consequently remains uncertain. ([BnF author bibliography](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/rechercher.do?index=AUT3&numNotice=12050371); [Jacques Gleyse, “La labellisation de la méthode”](https://archives.uness.fr/revue-eps/media/articles/pdf/70266-9.pdf))
- **1914:** *Ma Leçon-type de natation*. A specialized swimming-instruction protocol and the first of Hébert’s manuals devoted to one movement family. Subsequent “leçon-type” editions, including those of 1920, 1933, and 1943, are manifestations of this text. ([BnF author bibliography](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/rechercher.do?index=AUT3&numNotice=12050371); [BnF, fifth edition](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb32229610j))
- **1918:** *L’Entraînement physique complet par la méthode naturelle: Guide abrégé du moniteur chargé de l’entraînement dans les écoles, les sociétés de sport et de gymnastique, et en général dans les groupements de toutes sortes d’enfants ou d’adultes*. An instructors’ protocol explaining how to compose and conduct training rather than simply cataloguing exercises; later editions broadened the title to *Guide abrégé du moniteur et de la monitrice*. ([BnF author bibliography](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/rechercher.do?index=AUT3&numNotice=12050371); [Methode Naturelle bibliography](https://methodenaturelle.de/books-and-works/))

### The technological summa of the Méthode naturelle

- **1936:** *L’Éducation physique virile et morale par la méthode naturelle, tome I: Exposé doctrinal et principes directeurs de travail*. The doctrinal opening of Hébert’s multi-volume summa, conceived as the mature synthesis of decades of experiment. Later impressions dated 1941–1942 have led some lists to assign the volume those dates, but the standard work bibliography gives its first appearance as 1936. ([Methode Naturelle bibliography](https://methodenaturelle.de/books-and-works/); [Hébertisme bibliography](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%C3%A9bertisme))
- **1942:** *L’Éducation physique virile et morale par la méthode naturelle, tome II: Technique des exercices—Technologie, marche, course, saut*. A large technical manual covering equipment and three families of natural movement. It is one component of the summa but also a physically independent volume. ([Methode Naturelle bibliography](https://methodenaturelle.de/books-and-works/))
- **1943:** *L’Éducation physique virile et morale par la méthode naturelle, tome III, fascicule 1: Quadrupédie*. A technical protocol for quadrupedal movement, issued as an independent fascicle within the larger work. ([Methode Naturelle bibliography](https://methodenaturelle.de/books-and-works/))
- **1943:** *L’Éducation physique virile et morale par la méthode naturelle, tome III, fascicule 2: Grimper*. A technical protocol devoted to climbing, both part of the summa and a separately usable manual. ([Methode Naturelle bibliography](https://methodenaturelle.de/books-and-works/))
- **1946:** *L’Éducation physique virile et morale par la méthode naturelle, tome III, fascicule 3: Équilibrisme*. A technical protocol devoted to balance; shortened references that call it simply *Équilibrisme* do not identify a different work. ([Methode Naturelle bibliography](https://methodenaturelle.de/books-and-works/); [Hébertisme bibliography](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%C3%A9bertisme))
- **1947:** *L’Éducation physique virile et morale par la méthode naturelle, tome IV, fascicule 1: Lever*. A technical protocol for lifting and carrying within the mature system. ([Methode Naturelle bibliography](https://methodenaturelle.de/books-and-works/))
- **1950:** *L’Éducation physique virile et morale par la méthode naturelle, tome IV, fascicule 2: Lancer*. A technical protocol for throwing, issued as the second independent fascicle of tome IV. ([Methode Naturelle bibliography](https://methodenaturelle.de/books-and-works/))
- **1955:** *L’Éducation physique virile et morale par la méthode naturelle, tome IV, fascicule 3: Défense*. A technical protocol for personal defense and the final fascicle of tome IV. ([BnF author bibliography](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/rechercher.do?index=AUT3&numNotice=12050371); [Methode Naturelle bibliography](https://methodenaturelle.de/books-and-works/))
- **1957:** *L’Éducation physique virile et morale par la méthode naturelle, tome V: Technique des exercices—Natation*. The swimming volume completing the published technological sequence in the year of Hébert’s death. Some general bibliographies describe the whole series as 1936–1959, so the dating of its final publication history is not entirely uniform. ([Methode Naturelle bibliography](https://methodenaturelle.de/books-and-works/); [BnF author bibliography](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/rechercher.do?index=AUT3&numNotice=12050371))

### Periodical and collective textual work

- **1922–1940:** *L’Éducation physique*. Hébert did not create the periodical, which began in 1902, but he remade its postwar incarnation as president of its editorial committee and principal doctrinal voice; it is therefore an editorial project and collective manifestation, not a solely authored book. Individual unsigned items and the many articles distributed through its run cannot safely be converted into separate Hébert works without article-level attribution. ([BnF periodical record](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb34356955p); [Persée](https://education.persee.fr/doc/revpe_2021-4111_1924_num_85_2_9016_t1_0074_0000_2))
