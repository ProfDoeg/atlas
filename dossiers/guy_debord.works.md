## Works by Guy-Ernest Debord

### Films and unrealized film-projects

- **1952:** *Hurlements en faveur de Sade*. An imageless black-and-white film made from alternating spoken passages, silence, white leader and black screen; its destruction of ordinary cinema makes it simultaneously film, manifesto and anti-work. A substantially different illustrated scenario published in *Ion* before production survives as an unrealized first version. [Complete filmography](https://www.bopsecrets.org/SI/debord.films/filmography.htm)

- **1953:** *La Belle Jeunesse*. An announced film-project that was never produced; only the intention is securely documented, so it is a project rather than a completed film. [Complete filmography](https://www.bopsecrets.org/SI/debord.films/filmography.htm)

- **1959:** *Sur le passage de quelques personnes à travers une assez courte unité de temps*. A short black-and-white film combining documentary images, détourned footage, commentary and self-criticism; its first screening date is not securely known. [Debord filmography](https://www.bopsecrets.org/SI/debord.films/filmography.htm)

- **1961:** *Critique de la séparation*. A short black-and-white essay-film whose images, subtitles and commentary contest their own ability to represent lived experience; its first screening date is likewise uncertain. [Debord filmography](https://www.bopsecrets.org/SI/debord.films/filmography.htm)

- **By 1964:** *Portrait d’Ivan Chtcheglov*. An unrealized film listed among projects Debord would make only with complete artistic freedom; it may have supplied material to later films, but no autonomous film appeared. [Unrealized film projects](https://www.bopsecrets.org/SI/debord.films/filmography.htm)

- **By 1964:** *Les aspects ludiques manifestes et latents dans la Fronde*. An unrealized historical film-project known from the list printed on *Contre le cinéma*; its surviving status is intention, not manifestation. [Unrealized film projects](https://www.bopsecrets.org/SI/debord.films/filmography.htm)

- **By 1964:** *Éloge de ce que nous avons aimé dans les images d’une époque*. An unrealized compilation-film project; elements may have migrated into Debord’s later cinema, but that relationship remains conjectural. [Unrealized film projects](https://www.bopsecrets.org/SI/debord.films/filmography.htm)

- **1970:** *Traité de savoir-vivre à l’usage des jeunes générations*. A proposed film of Raoul Vaneigem’s book, mentioned in Debord’s internal correspondence and abandoned after their rupture; it is Debord’s project but not his source text and never became a film. [Unrealized film projects](https://www.bopsecrets.org/SI/debord.films/filmography.htm)

- **1973:** *La Société du spectacle*. A feature-length black-and-white film written and directed by Debord from his 1967 book, using newsreels, advertisements, fiction films, still photographs and commentary as a cinematic détournement rather than a conventional adaptation. Sources differ between the 1973 production date and its public release on 1 May 1974. [Filmography](https://www.bopsecrets.org/SI/debord.films/filmography.htm) [Chronology](https://liredebord.com/chronologie/)

- **1975:** *Réfutation de tous les jugements, tant élogieux qu’hostiles, qui ont été jusqu’ici portés sur le film « La Société du spectacle »*. A short film made from hostile and favorable responses to the preceding film; it acts as polemic, counter-review and cinematic sequel. [Filmography](https://www.bopsecrets.org/SI/debord.films/filmography.htm)

- **1978:** *In girum imus nocte et consumimur igni*. A feature-length autobiographical and historical essay-film, completed in 1978 but released theatrically in 1981; filmographies disagree on whether its running time is approximately 95 or 105 minutes. [Detailed filmography](https://www.bopsecrets.org/SI/debord.films/filmography.htm) [Chronology](https://liredebord.com/chronologie/)

- **1982–1984:** *De l’Espagne*. A contracted project for a two-to-four-hour film examining Spain from the fifteenth century onward, centered on Andalusia. Its seriousness is uncertain, and Debord abandoned it after Gérard Lebovici’s murder; the project survives principally through its contract. [Unrealized film projects](https://www.bopsecrets.org/SI/debord.films/filmography.htm)

- **1994–1995:** *Guy Debord, son art et son temps*. Debord wrote this television film’s montage-text and Brigitte Cornand directed it; completed around his death and first broadcast on 9 January 1995, it is consequently a collaborative, partly posthumous manifestation rather than a film directed by Debord. [Filmography and credits](https://www.bopsecrets.org/SI/debord.films/filmography.htm) [Chronology](https://liredebord.com/chronologie/)

### Books, montage-books and sustained texts

- **1957:** *Fin de Copenhague*. A détourned artist’s book made with Asger Jorn from found print, maps, advertisements, splashed color and Debord’s montage; authorship is collaborative and the object refuses separation into writing, graphic design and painting. [Debord bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1957:** *Rapport sur la construction des situations et sur les conditions de l’organisation et de l’action de la tendance situationniste internationale*. A programmatic report prepared for the founding of the Situationist International: part analysis, part organizational protocol and part manifesto. [Collected-work chronology](https://liredebord.com/chronologie/) [Bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1958–1959:** *Mémoires*. A sandpaper-covered montage-book by Debord with “supporting structures” by Asger Jorn, composed from fragments of print, maps and painted lines. The colophon dates it 1958, while some bibliographies and its effective circulation place it in 1959; it is a collaborative Gesamtkunstwerk rather than prose memoir in the ordinary sense. [Debord bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html) [Lire Debord corpus](https://liredebord.com/oeuvres/)

- **1960:** *Constant*. A German-language exhibition pamphlet concerning Constant Nieuwenhuys, issued by Galerie Van de Loo; it is criticism made for a particular exhibition rather than a freestanding theoretical book. [Debord bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1960:** *Préliminaires pour une définition de l’unité du programme révolutionnaire*. A jointly authored programmatic text with Pierre Canjuers, circulated before appearing in *Notes critiques*; its object is a common revolutionary program, so “manifesto” fits better than essay alone. [Debord bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1962:** *Sur la Commune*. Theses written with Attila Kotányi and Raoul Vaneigem as private notes for Henri Lefebvre, later incorporated into *Aux poubelles de l’histoire* and subsequently printed in *Internationale situationniste*; its publication history makes it both memorandum and collective manifesto. [Debord bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1963:** *Les Situationnistes et les nouvelles formes d’action dans la politique ou l’art*. A multilingual exhibition pamphlet and program for actions that exceed conventional art; it is both manifesto and protocol for détournement, scandal and communication. [Debord bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1964:** *Contre le cinéma*. An illustrated presentation of Debord’s first three film-scripts, edited and prefaced by Asger Jorn. It is a book-object made around the films, not a seventh film and not wholly authored by Debord. [Cinematic bibliography](https://www.bopsecrets.org/SI/debord.films/filmography.htm)

- **1965:** *Le Déclin et la chute de l’économie spectaculaire-marchande*. A political analysis of the Watts uprising, written in French but first issued in English translation and later printed in *Internationale situationniste*; its first manifestation therefore did not appear in its language of composition. [Debord bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1967:** *Le Point d’explosion de l’idéologie en Chine*. A polemical pamphlet on the Cultural Revolution, also reprinted in *Internationale situationniste* no. 11. [Debord bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1967:** *La Société du spectacle*. A book of 221 numbered theses arranged in nine chapters: philosophical critique, revolutionary theory and deliberately interventionist manifesto rather than neutral social description. [Collected-work corpus](https://liredebord.com/oeuvres/) [Chronology](https://liredebord.com/chronologie/)

- **1968:** *Enragés et situationnistes dans le mouvement des occupations*. A collective history and strategic interpretation credited to René Viénet with the collaboration of Debord, René Riesel, Raoul Vaneigem and Mustapha Khayati; it should not be silently converted into a Debord solo work. [Debord bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1972:** *La Véritable Scission dans l’Internationale*. A jointly authored book-manifesto by Debord and Gianfranco Sanguinetti announcing, explaining and performing the dissolution of the Situationist International. [Lire Debord corpus](https://liredebord.com/oeuvres/)

- **1978:** *Œuvres cinématographiques complètes, 1952–1978*. Debord’s illustrated book of the texts and documents of his six completed films; it is an authored reconfiguration of the cinema on paper, but not an additional film. [Cinematic bibliography](https://www.bopsecrets.org/SI/debord.films/filmography.htm)

- **1979:** *Préface à la quatrième édition italienne de « La Société du spectacle »*. A retrospective political preface that became an independent pamphlet and substantive return to the 1967 book. [Lire Debord corpus](https://liredebord.com/oeuvres/)

- **1982:** *Ordures et décombres déballés à la sortie du film « In girum imus nocte et consumimur igni » par différentes sources autorisées*. An uncommented montage of fourteen reviews selected and arranged by Debord; its made form is editorial détournement, and much of its verbal matter was written by others. [Cinematic bibliography](https://www.bopsecrets.org/SI/debord.films/filmography.htm)

- **1985:** *Considérations sur l’assassinat de Gérard Lebovici*. A polemical memorial and counter-investigation responding to Lebovici’s murder and its press treatment. [Lire Debord corpus](https://liredebord.com/oeuvres/)

- **1988:** *Commentaires sur la société du spectacle*. A thirty-three-chapter strategic revision of the 1967 analysis, addressing the “integrated spectacle”; it is sequel, diagnosis and deliberately guarded intervention. [Lire Debord corpus](https://liredebord.com/oeuvres/)

- **1989:** *Panégyrique, tome premier*. The first completed volume of Debord’s self-portrait, constructed through memoir, history, quotation and strategic self-presentation rather than conventional autobiography. [Lire Debord corpus](https://liredebord.com/oeuvres/)

- **1990:** *In girum imus nocte et consumimur igni: édition critique*. Debord’s annotated book-version of the film’s voice-over, with textual and documentary apparatus; it is a new paper manifestation of an existing film, not simply a transcript. [Cinematic bibliography](https://www.bopsecrets.org/SI/debord.films/filmography.htm)

- **1993:** *« Cette mauvaise réputation… »*. A book-length answer to press representations of Debord and his work, assembled as polemic and self-documentation. [Lire Debord corpus](https://liredebord.com/oeuvres/)

- **Completed by 1994; published 1997:** *Panégyrique, tome second*. A posthumously published sequence of photographs, documents, captions and quotations prepared by Debord as the second part of *Panégyrique*; it is a visual-textual montage and not a conventional second volume of memoir. [Lire Debord corpus](https://liredebord.com/oeuvres/) [BnF account of the archive](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb16680977r)

- **Published 1995:** *Des contrats*. Three film contracts with Gérard Lebovici, accompanied by Debord’s “Justifications”; the contracts are operative protocols as well as records, and the volume’s publication is posthumous. [Cinematic bibliography](https://www.bopsecrets.org/SI/debord.films/filmography.htm)

### Periodicals, collective manifestations and organizational works

- **1952–1954:** *Internationale lettriste*. Fourteen numbers of the Lettrist International’s collective periodical, in whose writing, direction and interventions Debord participated. Anonymous and collective authorship prevents a defensible allocation of every item to him, so the periodical is listed as a collaborative body of work rather than converted into a series of fictitious solo texts. [Debord bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1954–1957; 1959:** *Potlatch*. Twenty-nine original information bulletins followed by a new-series number, collectively produced but substantially edited and written by Debord. It functioned as a deliberately noncommercial gift, correspondence network, manifesto series and organizational instrument, resisting the category of ordinary magazine. [Debord bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1957–1972:** *Internationale situationniste*. The organization co-founded and repeatedly shaped by Debord was itself a sustained political-aesthetic project, not merely an authorial affiliation; its exclusions, conferences and eventual self-dissolution formed part of what it made. Responsibility was collective and contested, so the organization cannot simply be attributed to Debord alone. [Chronology](https://liredebord.com/chronologie/)

- **1958–1969:** *Internationale situationniste*. Twelve numbers of the SI’s collective review, directed and extensively written by Debord but normally preserving collective or anonymous attribution. Its 143 texts constitute a jointly made theoretical and organizational corpus; assigning every unsigned article to Debord would exceed the evidence. [Lire Debord corpus](https://liredebord.com/oeuvres/) [Bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1968:** *Conseil pour le maintien des occupations*. A revolutionary organization and publishing intervention created during the May occupations, in which Debord participated with other situationists and occupiers. It is a collective political manifestation—not a Debord “work” in the proprietary sense—and its posters, songs, communiqués and actions retain separate or anonymous authorship. [Chronology](https://liredebord.com/chronologie/)

- **1969–1971; published 1974:** *Débat d’orientation de l’ex-Internationale situationniste*. A body of correspondence and internal debate concerning the SI’s direction, only partly written by Debord and later published as a dossier; it is a collective process preserved as text rather than a single-authored book. [Debord bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

### Named shorter texts, manifestos and protocols

- **1952:** *Prolégomènes à tout cinéma futur*. A theoretical declaration published in *Ion* alongside the first scenario of *Hurlements en faveur de Sade*; it acts as a manifesto for a cinema Debord was simultaneously dismantling. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1952:** *Mort d’un commis voyageur*. A signed article in the first number of *Internationale lettriste*. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1953:** *Notice pour la Fédération française des ciné-clubs*. A short statement concerning Debord’s cinema, published in *Internationale lettriste*. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1953:** *Pour en finir avec le confort nihiliste*. A polemical Lettrist International article whose proper form is intervention rather than detached criticism. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1953:** *Totem et Tabou*. A short Lettrist International text; its title is sometimes catalogued with the English spelling “Taboo.” [Chronological bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1953:** *Ne travaillez jamais*. A chalk inscription made on a Paris wall, known chiefly through a photograph. It is slogan, urban intervention and manifesto, and resists treatment as either literary text or durable visual artwork. [Visual-work bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1954:** *Quel sens donnez-vous au mot poésie ?* A collective response signed by Debord, Mohamed Dahou, Henry de Béarn, Ivan Chtcheglov, Gaëtan Langlais and Gil J Wolman; the precise authorship of individual phrases is not separable. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1954:** *Est-ce que la pensée nous illumine, nous et nos actions, avec la même indifférence que le soleil… ?* A collective response signed by Debord and six other Lettrist International participants; it should be retained as shared authorship. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1954:** *Ordre de boycott*. A directive signed with Gil J Wolman: literally a protocol for collective action as well as a polemical tract. [Tract bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1954:** *Résumé 1954*. A review of the year written with Jacques Fillon for *Potlatch*. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1955:** *Introduction à une critique de la géographie urbaine*. An essay-manifesto defining psychogeography and the transformation of urban life, first published in *Les Lèvres nues*. [Chronology](https://liredebord.com/chronologie/) [Bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1956:** *Mode d’emploi du détournement*. A jointly written text with Gil J Wolman; “protocol” is literal here, since it supplies principles and practical instructions for reusing existing cultural material. [Chronology](https://liredebord.com/chronologie/) [Bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1956:** *Théorie de la dérive*. A theoretical and practical protocol for experimental movement through urban space, first published in *Les Lèvres nues* and later reprinted in *Internationale situationniste*. [Chronology](https://liredebord.com/chronologie/) [Bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1956:** *Deux comptes rendus de dérive*. Two reports of actual urban dérives, joining field record to experimental narrative. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1956:** *Le Labyrinthe éducatif*. An internal Lettrist International statement; its limited circulation makes it an organizational document rather than a public essay. [Debord bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1957:** *Remarques sur le concept d’art expérimental*. An internal Situationist response to Walter Olmo, not circulated publicly at the time; it survives as a working intervention rather than a finished publication. [Debord bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1958:** *Thèses sur la révolution culturelle*. A set of theses for the SI, published in its first review and subsequently prepared for an international conference; manifesto and organizational proposition coincide. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1958:** *La Déclaration d’Amsterdam*. A programmatic declaration jointly written with Constant, defining minimum principles for situationist construction. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1959:** *Positions situationnistes sur la circulation*. Theses on traffic and urbanism published in *Internationale situationniste* no. 3. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1961:** *Perspectives de modifications conscientes dans la vie quotidienne*. A conference contribution delivered by tape recorder after the organizers declined Debord’s preferred form of address; it is lecture, recorded intervention and manifesto. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1962:** *Das Unbehagen in der Kultur*. A declaration concerning the conviction and imprisonment of Uwe Lausen, written with Raoul Vaneigem; it is an act of solidarity rather than descriptive criticism. [Tract bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1965:** *Correspondance avec un cybernéticien*. Debord’s hostile exchange with Abraham Moles, issued by the SI; the work is the enacted correspondence, not a retrospective essay about it. [Debord bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1969:** *La Question de l’organisation pour l’I.S.*. A strategic internal text subsequently printed in the final number of *Internationale situationniste*. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1974:** *De l’architecture sauvage*. A preface to *Le Jardin d’Albisola*, treating spontaneous construction as lived architecture; it remains materially part of another author’s book. [Debord bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1985:** *Abat-faim*. A signed contribution to the collective *Encyclopédie des nuisances*. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1986:** *Ab irato*. A signed contribution to the *Encyclopédie des nuisances*. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1987:** *Abolir*. A signed contribution to the *Encyclopédie des nuisances*. [Chronological bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

### Psychogeographical maps, photographs, collages and paintings

- **1951:** *Portrait de Guy-Ernest Debord par lui-même*. A photographic self-portrait printed on deliberately damaged film and published the following year in *Ion*; photograph, damaged material and authorial persona form one object. [Visual-work bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1952:** *L’Anticoncept*. A found map détourned by Debord in relation to Gil J Wolman’s film of that title; the title belongs originally to Wolman’s film, making Debord’s associated map an attributed intervention rather than an autonomous, securely titled work. [Visual-work bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **Undated, early 1950s:** *Portrait de Gil J Wolman*. A collage made on a photograph; the available bibliography supplies no secure date, which should not be invented. [Visual-work bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1954:** *Le temps passe, en effet, et nous passons avec lui*. A color collage known through later reproduction. [Visual-work bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **Undated:** *Critique de la séparation*. A collage sharing its title with Debord’s later film; no secure date or necessary identification with the film has been established. [Visual-work bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1957:** *Paris sous la neige: relevé des principaux courants psychogéographiques du centre de Paris*. A psychogeographical diagram exhibited in Brussels; it maps affective movement rather than geographic continuity. [Visual-work bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1957:** *Axe d’exploration et échec dans la recherche d’un Grand Passage situationniste*. A psychogeographical diagram whose record of failure is part of the work, not evidence that no work exists. [Visual-work bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1957:** *The Most Dangerous Game: pistes psychogéographiques vraies ou fausses*. An exhibited psychogeographical diagram; its map-like form is both speculative protocol and visual object. [Visual-work bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1957:** *The Naked City: illustration de l’hypothèse des plaques tournantes en psychogéographie*. A cut-up map of Paris joined by directional arrows, making discontinuous zones and possible movements visible; it is map, diagram and urban hypothesis. [Visual-work bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1957:** *Guide psychogéographique de Paris: discours sur les passions de l’amour*. A fragmented map of Paris designed for affective orientation rather than practical navigation; it functions as visual manifesto and protocol for dérive. [Visual-work bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1961:** *Œuvre collective de la conférence de Göteborg*. An oil painting executed collectively by Debord, Jørgen Nash, Asger Jorn, Hardy Strid, J. V. Martin, Jacqueline de Jong, Hans-Peter Zimmer, Heimrad Prem and Helmut Sturm; individual contributions cannot properly be disentangled. [Visual-work bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **Date uncertain:** *Abolition du travail aliéné*. A painting attributed jointly to Debord and Giuseppe Pinot-Gallizio; the standard bibliography supplies no secure date, and its attribution depends on later reproductions. [Visual-work bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

### Game and strategic model

- **Conceived by 1965; manufactured 1977:** *Le Jeu de la guerre* (also called *Kriegspiel*). A two-player strategic board game designed by Debord, materialized in a very small edition with a gridded board and metal pieces after he founded the Société des Jeux stratégiques et historiques. It is simulation, theoretical model and playable protocol, not an illustration of a book. [Game history](https://www.r-s-g.org/kriegspiel/about.php)

- **1987:** *Le Jeu de la guerre: relevé des positions successives de toutes les forces au cours d’une partie*. A book made with Alice Becker-Ho that records and comments on one complete match. It is documentation of an enacted game and a strategic treatise, not the game-object itself. [Debord bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

### Translations as made works

- **1976:** *Véridique rapport sur les dernières chances de sauver le capitalisme en Italie*. Debord’s French translation of Gianfranco Sanguinetti’s Italian book; authorship of the source remains Sanguinetti’s, while Debord made the French verbal manifestation. [Translation bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1979:** *Protestation devant les libertaires du présent et du futur sur les capitulations de 1937*. A bilingual edition translated from Spanish with Alice Becker-Ho from an anonymous “Uncontrolled” member of the Iron Column, with a translator’s note by Debord; it is collaborative translation, editorial recovery and political intervention. [Translation bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1980:** *Stances sur la mort de son père*. Debord’s French translation of Jorge Manrique’s Castilian poem, issued bilingually with his translator’s note; the poem is Manrique’s work, while this French linguistic object is Debord’s translation. [Translation bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

### Correspondence and posthumously constituted corpora

- **1949–1954; published 2004:** *Le Marquis de Sade a des yeux de fille*. Debord’s early letters, poems and documents, assembled and titled posthumously; the constituent items are his, but the published book as a whole is an editorial creation he did not make in that form. [Correspondence bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html)

- **1951–1994; published 1999–2010:** *Correspondance*. Debord’s surviving letters, issued posthumously in seven chronological volumes including volume “0.” The correspondence is a genuine lifetime practice and often an instrument of projects, exclusions and publishing decisions, but the collected edition is an editorially constituted obra rather than a book planned by Debord. [Bibliography](https://www.notbored.org/bibliography.html) [Chronology](https://liredebord.com/chronologie/)
