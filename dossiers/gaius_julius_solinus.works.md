## Works by Gaius Julius Solinus

### Chorographical compendium

- **Late 3rd or 4th century CE:** *Collectanea rerum memorabilium* (also transmitted as *Collectanea rerum mirabilium*). An encyclopedic obra combining chorography, paradoxography, natural history, ethnography, antiquarian material, and accounts of precious stones; it survives in two recensions, and its date depends partly on whether Solinus’s unusual Latin is judged innovative or late. The first recension includes a dedicatory letter to an otherwise uncertain Adventus and presents itself as a concise survey of the world. ([Oxford Classical Dictionary](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/61673/chapter-abstract/549653162); [Mommsen’s critical edition](https://archive.org/details/collectanearerum00soliuoft))

### Disputed revised manifestation

- **After the first recension; date unknown:** *Polyhistor*. An expanded and verbally revised manifestation of the same compendium, not a securely independent work: its prefatory letter speaks in Solinus’s voice, disowns prematurely circulated copies, and replaces the earlier title with *Polyhistor*. Mommsen regarded this recension and preface as a medieval interpolation, whereas Hermann Walter and much subsequent scholarship defend it as Solinus’s own second edition; *De mirabilibus mundi*, *Mirabilia*, and related forms are later transmission titles rather than additional creations. ([Oxford Classical Dictionary](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/61673/chapter-abstract/549653162); [Walter, bibliographical record](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BA07280248); [Bianchetti on the two editions](https://www.persee.fr/doc/ista_0000-0000_2004_act_939_1_2096?pageId=T1_60); [Bryn Mawr Classical Review](https://bmcr.brynmawr.edu/2015/2015.10.04/))
