## Works by Max Rafailovich Levchin

### Companies, laboratories, and entrepreneurial projects

- **1995:** *SponsorNet New Media*. A classified-advertising start-up co-founded with Luke Nosek and Scott Banister while Levchin was a student; it was one of several short-lived university ventures and survives chiefly as a biographical project rather than a continuing obra. [The Founders](https://cdn.bookey.app/files/pdf/book/en/the-founders.pdf)

- **1996–1998:** *NetMeridian Software*. A company founded and led by Levchin to develop security applications for palmtop computers. Contemporary corporate biographies establish the company but do not supply a catalogue of its individual programs. [Yelp SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/vprr/1300/13001855.pdf)

- **1990s:** *NetMomentum*. An unsuccessful university-era project intended to provide classified-advertising technology to newspapers. The surviving account treats the intention and attempt—not a durable product—as the work. [The Founders](https://cdn.bookey.app/files/pdf/book/en/the-founders.pdf)

- **1998–2001:** *Fieldlink / Confinity / PayPal*. A security and digital-wallet company co-founded by Levchin and Peter Thiel that successively changed name and purpose, ultimately producing the PayPal payment service; because several founders and merged organizations made it, Levchin’s obra here is collaborative rather than sole-authored. [Affirm corporate biography](https://investors.affirm.com/management/max-levchin), [Google Patents payment-system record](https://patents.google.com/patent/US7089208B1/en)

- **2004:** *MRL Ventures*. An “exploration company” founded to create and fund projects using the emerging social web. It is better classified as a generative framework for works—including Slide and Yelp—than as a single product. [Affirm SEC registration statement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1820953/000110465921004276/tm2026663-21_s1a.htm)

- **2004–2010:** *Slide*. A personal-media and social-application company founded by Levchin within MRL Ventures and acquired by Google in 2010. Its obra comprised slideshow widgets, social-network applications, and games made by a larger development team, so the individual applications should not automatically be treated as Levchin’s sole creations. [Affirm SEC registration statement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1820953/000110465921004276/tm2026663-21_s1a.htm), [VentureBeat interview](https://venturebeat.com/business/q-and-a-with-max-levchin-slide-more-than-a-widget-maker)

- **2004–:** *Yelp*. A consumer-review service incubated at MRL Ventures, seeded by Levchin, and principally founded and designed by Jeremy Stoppelman and Russel Simmons. Official sources consequently say that Levchin “helped create” it rather than naming him its sole or principal author; this is an enabling collaboration whose status as his own work remains deliberately qualified. [Affirm SEC registration statement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1820953/000110465921004276/tm2026663-21_s1a.htm), [Yelp’s account of Levchin’s role](https://www.yelp-ir.com/news/press-releases/news-release-details/2015/Yelp-announces-resignation-of-Max-Levchin-from-Board-of-Directors/default.aspx)

- **2011–:** *HVF (Hard, Valuable, Fun)*. An exploration company and innovation laboratory founded to create and fund companies that exploit large data sets. Like MRL Ventures, it resists classification as a single work: it is a continuing project designed to generate other projects. [Affirm SEC registration statement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1820953/000110465921004276/tm2026663-21_s1a.htm)

- **2012–:** *Affirm*. A financial-technology company and payment network founded by Levchin and spun out of HVF. Corporate accounts also associate Nathan Gettings and Jeffrey Kaditz with its creation, so it is a collective commercial and technical obra rather than an individually authored object. [Affirm SEC registration statement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1820953/000110465921004276/tm2026663-21_s1a.htm), [Affirm corporate biography](https://investors.affirm.com/management/max-levchin)

- **2013–:** *Glow*. A data-driven fertility and women’s-health company founded at HVF, with Levchin described variously as founder or co-founder and chairman. The differing language reflects institutional collaboration rather than a securely separable Levchin-only product. [Affirm SEC registration statement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1820953/000110465921004276/tm2026663-21_s1a.htm), [Affirm corporate biography](https://investors.affirm.com/management/max-levchin)

### Software, protocols, and interfaces

- **1999–2000:** *System and Method for Electronically Exchanging Value Among Distributed Users*. A jointly invented payment protocol underlying a family of PayPal patent applications: users exchange value through existing identifiers, including email addresses and telephone numbers. The patent family contains later continuations and narrower digital-wallet variants; these are legal manifestations of a shared invention, not necessarily separate obras. [Google Patents family record](https://patents.google.com/patent/US7089208B1/en)

- **circa 2000:** *Gausebeck–Levchin test*. A CAPTCHA-like anti-automation protocol made by David Gausebeck and Levchin to impede fraudulent PayPal account creation. It is an operational text in the broadest sense—a machine-readable challenge that acts on users—rather than a literary work. [Sequoia Capital’s PayPal history](https://sequoiacap.com/podcast/crucible-moments-paypal)

- **2001:** *System and Method for Depicting On-line Transactions*. A jointly invented graphical and aural interface for rendering payment entities as nodes and transactions as edges, with visual attributes indicating magnitude, time, and status; filed in 2001 and later issued as US 7,249,094. [Patent record](https://patents.justia.com/inventor/max-levchin?page=2)

- **2003:** *Method and Architecture for Facilitating Payment to E-commerce Merchants via a Payment Service*. A co-invented checkout protocol that moves a customer between a merchant and payment service while preserving the merchant’s checkout flow. It was published as an application rather than identified in the consulted inventor record as a Levchin-authored product release. [Patent record](https://patents.justia.com/inventor/max-levchin?page=2)

- **2004–2009:** *Automatic Dispute Resolution / Automatic Restitution of Transaction Fees, Punishment of Non-paying Bidders, and Management of Appeals*. A large, jointly invented protocol for resolving online-marketplace disputes and administering restitution and appeals. The differing titles and later filings are related patent manifestations, not evidence of separately made consumer products. [Levchin inventor record](https://patents.justia.com/inventor/max-levchin)

- **2007:** *Method and Apparatus for Presenting Media Content*. A Slide patent application, co-invented with Jared Kopf, Jeremiah Robison, Johnnie Manzari, and Michael Powers, for a scrolling media ticker whose items can be enlarged, opened, saved, or transmitted. Its documented form is an application and interface design, not a freestanding authored text. [Levchin inventor record](https://patents.justia.com/inventor/max-levchin)

- **2014:** *Reminders and Action Items for Personal Interactions*. A patent application co-invented with Phil Libin for generating reminders when a person, place, or other contextual condition becomes proximate. The inventor catalogue records an application, not proof that the proposed system became a completed product. [Levchin inventor record](https://patents.justia.com/inventor/max-levchin)

- **2013–2014:** *Customized-food generation systems*. A collaborative cluster of patent projects concerning automated machines that generate food according to ingredient-integrity preferences, direct users to suitable machines, suggest substitute machines, and customize food or packaging. Levchin is one of roughly sixteen named inventors; the records therefore support co-inventorship but not individual authorship, and several entries are continuations or parallel filings of the same concepts. [Max R. Levchin inventor record](https://patents.justia.com/inventor/max-r-levchin)

- **2015–2016:** *Machine/Article/Composition/Process State(s) for Tracking Philanthropic and/or Other Efforts*. A jointly conceived patent project for accepting and presenting data about philanthropic or related activity. Its several applications are legal manifestations of one broadly framed system, and the unusually large inventor group makes individual creative responsibility indeterminate. [Patent application record](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20170046670), [Max R. Levchin inventor record](https://patents.justia.com/inventor/max-r-levchin)

- **2015–2024:** *Refinancing Tools for Purchasing Transactions*. A co-invented Affirm patent family for selecting completed purchases and offering terms to refinance them. Successive applications and grants—including US 11,030,685, US 11,455,683, US 11,720,960, US 12,026,773, and US 12,086,878—are continuations or manifestations of the same system rather than five independently made works. [Levchin inventor record](https://patents.justia.com/inventor/max-levchin)

### Film

- **2005–2006:** *Thank You for Smoking*. A feature film directed and written for the screen by Jason Reitman, with Levchin credited as one of several executive producers. His making-role was financial and executive rather than authorship of the screenplay or direction; the film is therefore a collaborative manifestation to which he contributed, not simply “a film by Max Levchin.” [Pressman Film credits](https://www.pressman.com/thank-you-for-smoking)
