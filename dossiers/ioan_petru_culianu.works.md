## Works by Ioan Petru Culianu

### Theses and early research projects

- **1970:** *Motivul „coincidentia oppositorum” la Giordano Bruno*. University paper on Giordano Bruno, presented in Bucharest; it is an early manifestation of the Renaissance project that Culianu developed throughout his career. ([study of Culianu’s journalism and intellectual formation](https://licentiada.org/ioan-petru-culianupublicist-si-erudit-intertextualitate-si-discursivitate-55251/))
- **1972:** *Marsilio Ficino e il platonismo nel Rinascimento* (also catalogued in Romanian as *Marsilio Ficino [1433–1499] și problemele platonismului în Renaștere*). Undergraduate thesis supervised by Nina Façon; published only posthumously, so the 2015 book is an edition of a 1972 scholarly work, not a new work. ([BCU Iași account](https://www.ziaruldeiasi.ro/stiri/expozitie-in-memoria-lui-ioan-petru-culianu-la-bcu-iasi-incepand-de-astazi--98526.html); [Bondor bibliography’s classification](https://bibgtkneamt.ebibliophil.ro/mon/ioan-petru-culianu-bibliografie-vol-1-opera-fqsjyyp5))
- **1975:** *Gnosticismo e pensiero contemporaneo: Hans Jonas*. Italian laurea thesis, defended on 5 November 1975 under Ugo Bianchi; its revised book manifestation, *Gnosticismo e pensiero moderno: Hans Jonas*, appeared in 1985. Calling the 1975 degree a PhD is misleading in the Italian academic context. ([documentary study of the thesis](https://www.ihr-acad.ro/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/6.Ciurtin.Proceedings.European.Congress.Bucharest.Archaeus.vol_.14.2010.pdf); [Hans Jonas interview dossier](https://www.orizzonticulturali.it/it_incontri_Hans-Jonas-intervista.html))
- **1986–1987:** *Recherches sur les dualismes d’Occident: Analyse de leurs principaux mythes*. Doctoral thesis at Paris IV under Michel Meslin, reproduced by Lille-Thèses in 1986 and defended in January 1987. Its later Italian, French, and English recensions differ materially, making this an evolving research project rather than a title with merely interchangeable translations. ([French thesis record](https://www.diffusiontheses.fr/lang-en/4652-these-de-culianu-ioan-p.html); [scholarly account of the differing recensions](https://www.encyclopedia.com/environment/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/culianu-ioan-petru))

### Scholarly books and extended essays

- **1978:** *Mircea Eliade*. Critical monograph on Eliade, first published in Italian; later Romanian editions enlarge or reframe the object through editorial matter but do not constitute separate works by Culianu. ([Elena Bondor, *Bibliografie, vol. I: Opera*](https://polirom.ro/biblioteca-ioan-petru-culianu/7836-ioan-petru-culianu-bibliografie-1-opera.html); [bibliographical list](https://www.encyclopedia.com/environment/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/culianu-ioan-petru))
- **1981:** *Iter in silvis: Saggi scelti sulla gnosi e altri studi*. Authorial collection of essays on Gnosticism and related subjects, published as number 2 of *Gnosis*; later Romanian *Iter in silvis* volumes reorganize a wider corpus and are therefore editorial manifestations rather than straightforward reprints. ([Bondor bibliography record and contents](https://bibgtkneamt.ebibliophil.ro/mon/ioan-petru-culianu-bibliografie-vol-1-opera-fqsjyyp5); [scholarly bibliography](https://www.encyclopedia.com/environment/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/culianu-ioan-petru))
- **1981:** *Religione e accrescimento del potere*. Long essay on religion and the growth of power, contributed to the collaboratively edited *Religione e potere* by Gianpaolo Romanato, Mario G. Lombardo, and Culianu. It is a contribution within a collective book, not a sole-authored monograph. ([scholarly bibliography](https://www.encyclopedia.com/environment/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/culianu-ioan-petru); [Bondor bibliography’s separate categories for edited volumes and contributions](https://bibgtkneamt.ebibliophil.ro/mon/ioan-petru-culianu-bibliografie-vol-1-opera-fqsjyyp5))
- **1983:** *Psychanodia: A Survey of the Evidence Concerning the Ascension of the Soul and Its Relevance*. Scholarly monograph on traditions of the soul’s ascent, issued by Brill; the later Romanian *Psihanodia* is a translated manifestation of this work. ([University of Chicago finding aid](https://www.lib.uchicago.edu/e/scrc/findingaids/view.php?eadid=ICU.SPCL.CULIANU); [scholarly bibliography](https://www.encyclopedia.com/environment/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/culianu-ioan-petru))
- **1984:** *Éros et magie à la Renaissance, 1484*. Interdisciplinary monograph treating Renaissance magic as a system of imagination, desire, communication, and control; its many translated titles are manifestations of the same obra. ([Bondor bibliography](https://polirom.ro/biblioteca-ioan-petru-culianu/7836-ioan-petru-culianu-bibliografie-1-opera.html); [University of Chicago finding aid](https://www.lib.uchicago.edu/e/scrc/findingaids/view.php?eadid=ICU.SPCL.CULIANU))
- **1984:** *Expériences de l’extase: Extase, ascension et récit visionnaire de l’hellénisme au Moyen Âge*. Historical study of ecstasy, ascent, and visionary narrative; subsequent Italian, Spanish, and Romanian versions are translations and revised editions, not additional works. ([Bondor bibliography](https://polirom.ro/biblioteca-ioan-petru-culianu/7836-ioan-petru-culianu-bibliografie-1-opera.html); [University of Chicago finding aid](https://www.lib.uchicago.edu/e/scrc/findingaids/view.php?eadid=ICU.SPCL.CULIANU))
- **1985:** *Gnosticismo e pensiero moderno: Hans Jonas*. Revised book made from the 1975 thesis, with interviews Culianu conducted with Jonas in 1975 and 1980; it is simultaneously monograph, intellectual encounter, and documentary dossier. ([Hans Jonas interview history](https://www.orizzonticulturali.it/it_incontri_Hans-Jonas-intervista-2.html); [scholarly bibliography](https://www.encyclopedia.com/environment/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/culianu-ioan-petru))
- **1989:** *I miti dei dualismi occidentali: Dai sistemi gnostici al mondo moderno*. Italian book recension of the Western-dualisms project; because its organization and content differ from the later French and English versions, it should not be reduced to a translation of the thesis. ([account of the two differing editions](https://www.encyclopedia.com/environment/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/culianu-ioan-petru))
- **1990:** *Les Gnoses dualistes d’Occident: Histoire et mythes*. French reconstruction of the dualisms project, extending its history and mythology beyond the doctoral form. It overlaps substantially with, but is not textually identical to, *I miti dei dualismi occidentali* or *The Tree of Gnosis*. ([scholarly bibliography](https://www.encyclopedia.com/environment/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/culianu-ioan-petru); [Bondor bibliography](https://polirom.ro/biblioteca-ioan-petru-culianu/7836-ioan-petru-culianu-bibliografie-1-opera.html))
- **1991:** *Out of This World: Otherworldly Journeys from Gilgamesh to Albert Einstein*. Comparative study of journeys beyond the ordinary world, written for a broader readership but continuing Culianu’s research into ascent and altered worlds. ([University of Chicago finding aid](https://www.lib.uchicago.edu/e/scrc/findingaids/view.php?eadid=ICU.SPCL.CULIANU); [Bondor bibliography](https://polirom.ro/biblioteca-ioan-petru-culianu/7836-ioan-petru-culianu-bibliografie-1-opera.html))
- **1991:** *I viaggi dell’anima: Sogni, visioni, estasi*. Italian synthesis concerning dreams, visions, ecstasy, and journeys of the soul. Its close thematic kinship with *Out of This World* makes its boundary as an independent work permeable, but standard bibliographies list it separately. ([Bondor bibliography’s analytical treatment of the volumes](https://bibgtkneamt.ebibliophil.ro/mon/ioan-petru-culianu-bibliografie-vol-1-opera-fqsjyyp5))
- **completed by 1991; published 1992:** *The Tree of Gnosis: Gnostic Mythology from Early Christianity to Modern Nihilism*. Culianu’s English reworking and culmination of the Western-dualisms project, published posthumously; it is not simply the English dress of the French book because the recensions differ. ([University of Chicago finding aid](https://www.lib.uchicago.edu/e/scrc/findingaids/view.php?eadid=ICU.SPCL.CULIANU); [account of the differing versions](https://www.encyclopedia.com/environment/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/culianu-ioan-petru))

### Collaborative reference works and editorial obra

- **1987:** *The Encyclopedia of Religion* contributions. Culianu made a distributed body of signed reference articles and served within Mircea Eliade’s encyclopedic project; this corpus resists treatment as one authored book, while each article remains a separate text. Bondor inventories such contributions individually, and they were later gathered editorially as *Cult, magie, erezii*. ([Bondor bibliography’s categories](https://bibgtkneamt.ebibliophil.ro/mon/ioan-petru-culianu-bibliografie-vol-1-opera-fqsjyyp5); [account of the collected encyclopedia articles](https://www.encyclopedia.com/environment/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/culianu-ioan-petru))
- **1990:** *Dictionnaire des religions*. Reference work made with Mircea Eliade and with the collaboration of Hillary S. Wiesner; later *Guide*, *Handbuch*, and *Dicționar* titles are linguistic and editorial manifestations of this collaborative obra. Attribution solely to Culianu or solely to Eliade erases its explicitly composite authorship. ([Bondor bibliography](https://polirom.ro/biblioteca-ioan-petru-culianu/7836-ioan-petru-culianu-bibliografie-1-opera.html); [Polirom author record](https://polirom.ro/autori/777-ioan-petru-culianu))
- **through 1991:** *Mircea Eliade’s unpublished papers and editorial estate*. As Eliade’s literary executor, Culianu undertook a project to organize and make publishable Eliade’s surviving notes. This was custodial and editorial labor rather than a Culianu-authored text, and it remained incomplete at Culianu’s death. ([document assigning the papers to Culianu](https://filosofieromaneasca.institutuldefilosofie.ro/sifr/volumul-18-2022/addenda-la-culianu-eliade-si-felix-culpa/))

### Fiction

- **1967–1972:** *Arta fugii*. Early cycle of stories, submitted under this title in 1971 and withdrawn after rejection by Romanian censorship; the surviving cycle appeared as a book only in 2002. It is both a completed youthful obra and a frustrated publication project, and the posthumous volume may not perfectly reproduce the censored typescript. ([study of the manuscript and publication history](https://www.diacronia.ro/indexing/details/A22175/pdf); [Bondor bibliography](https://bibgtkneamt.ebibliophil.ro/mon/ioan-petru-culianu-bibliografie-vol-1-opera-fqsjyyp5))
- **completed by August 1980; published 1991–1992:** *Hesperus*. Romanian-language speculative novel—at once science fiction, philosophical journey, gnostic allegory, and parallel-world creation. Culianu reported the manuscript finished in 1980; it first appeared in Italian in 1991 and in Romanian in 1992, so publication dates should not be mistaken for dates of making. ([manuscript chronology](https://www.diacronia.ro/indexing/details/A22175/pdf); [Polirom description](https://polirom.ro/biblioteca-ioan-petru-culianu/7081-hesperus-.html?chapter=ebook))
- **1981–1984:** *Tozgrec*. Unfinished novel-project surviving in four incomplete versions rather than in one authorially settled text. The 2010 book is Tereza Culianu-Petrescu’s posthumous edition of that plural archive; its incompleteness is part of the work’s identity. ([Polirom edition description](https://polirom.ro/biblioteca-ioan-petru-culianu/3971-tozgrec.html); [bibliographical chronology](https://teze.doctorat.ubbcluj.ro/doctorat/teza/fisier/1289))
- **1986; published 1989:** *La collezione di smeraldi*. Authorial collection of fantastic stories, Culianu’s first published literary book. It overlaps with stories later regrouped in Romanian collections, so those later volumes should not automatically be counted as wholly new fiction. ([documentary study identifying it as his literary debut](https://filosofieromaneasca.institutuldefilosofie.ro/sifr/volumul-18-2022/ioan-petru-culianu-mircea-eliade-si-felix-culpa-supplementa/); [1989 edition record](https://www.unilibro.it/libro/culianu-ioan-petru/la-collezione-di-smeraldi/9788816500464))
- **summer–autumn 1987:** *The Emerald Game* (*Jocul de smarald*). English-language historical detective novel written with the collaboration of Hillary S. Wiesner; it survived as an unpublished 441-page printout in the family archive and first appeared in Romanian translation in 2005. The title-page attribution to Culianu alone and the publisher’s statement of Wiesner’s collaboration leave the precise division of authorship unresolved. ([Polirom edition note](https://polirom.ro/biblioteca-ioan-petru-culianu/3017-jocul-de-smarald.html))
- **by 1991; published posthumously:** *Pergamentul diafan / Le Rouleau diaphane*. Fantastic-story project associated by Culianu with 1986 and later issued in changing Romanian configurations. Some stories were written with Hillary S. Wiesner, and the uncertain overlap among manuscript project, *La collezione di smeraldi*, *Pergamentul diafan*, and *Ultimele povestiri* resists any claim that their book titles define four cleanly separate obras. ([discussion of the 1986 project and uncertain contents](https://www.bibliotecadeva.ro/periodice/luceafarul/2007/09/luceafarul_2007_09_33.pdf); [Polirom’s collaboration statement](https://polirom.ro/biblioteca-ioan-petru-culianu/8405-pergamentul-diafan-ultimele-povestiri-.html))
- **date uncertain; known posthumously:** *Rîul Selenei*. A novel named in accounts of Culianu’s mature fictional projects, but not securely represented by a standard published authorial text. It therefore resists being called a completed work: the evidence establishes a title or project, not its recoverable completeness. ([literary-historical discussion](https://www.bibliotecadeva.ro/periodice/luceafarul/2007/09/luceafarul_2007_09_34.pdf))

### Essays, interventions, interviews, and correspondence

- **1970–1991:** *Studies, articles, short reviews, and literary journalism*. A multilingual, distributed corpus published in Romania, Western scholarly periodicals, and Romanian exile periodicals; it is not honestly reducible to a single “book.” Bondor’s standard bibliography inventories the items separately across studies, articles, reviews, and fiction, including publications before and after Culianu’s emigration and posthumous appearances. ([Bondor bibliography’s detailed contents](https://bibgtkneamt.ebibliophil.ro/mon/ioan-petru-culianu-bibliografie-vol-1-opera-fqsjyyp5))
- **principally 1989–1991:** *Political writings against totalitarian and post-communist power*. Interventions in the Romanian exile press—some polemical, some satirical, some published under rubric or pseudonymous conditions—later gathered as *Păcatul împotriva spiritului: Scrieri politice*. The posthumous collection is an editorial manifestation of dispersed acts of journalism, not a book Culianu assembled in that form. ([Bondor bibliography’s exile-press divisions](https://bibgtkneamt.ebibliophil.ro/mon/ioan-petru-culianu-bibliografie-vol-1-opera-fqsjyyp5))
- **1975 and 1980:** *Interviews with Hans Jonas*. Two interviews conducted by Culianu at New Rochelle and Mondorf-les-Bains and incorporated into *Gnosticismo e pensiero moderno*. They are jointly produced documentary texts—Culianu’s questions and Jonas’s answers—rather than works attributable without qualification to either participant alone. ([interview history](https://www.orizzonticulturali.it/it_incontri_Hans-Jonas-intervista-2.html))
- **dates various, through 1991:** *Interviews conducted and interviews given*. A two-directional oral-textual corpus: some items Culianu made as interviewer, while others are manifestations of speech elicited and shaped by another interviewer. Bondor accordingly separates “interviews realized” from “interviews granted,” resisting the fiction that they share one kind of authorship. ([Bondor bibliography’s contents](https://bibgtkneamt.ebibliophil.ro/mon/ioan-petru-culianu-bibliografie-vol-1-opera-fqsjyyp5))
- **1970s–1991:** *Correspondence*. Hundreds of sent letters survive alongside received letters in the Culianu and Eliade archives; only Culianu’s outgoing letters are unambiguously texts he made, while an edited correspondence volume is a later relational and editorial object. *Dialoguri întrerupte* names the posthumously constituted Eliade–Culianu exchange, not a book the correspondents jointly planned. ([Bondor bibliography’s correspondence inventory](https://bibgtkneamt.ebibliophil.ro/mon/ioan-petru-culianu-bibliografie-vol-1-opera-fqsjyyp5); [University of Chicago archival guide](https://www.lib.uchicago.edu/e/scrc/findingaids/view.php?eadid=ICU.SPCL.CULIANU))

### Archival and non-book manifestations

- **dates various, through 1991:** *Unpublished manuscripts, notes, teaching and conference materials*. The Culianu Papers preserve drafts and working materials that are manifestations of intellectual labor but are not necessarily finished works or texts intended for publication. Bondor records these separately from books and periodical publications rather than silently promoting every archival item to an obra. ([University of Chicago finding aid](https://www.lib.uchicago.edu/e/scrc/findingaids/view.php?eadid=ICU.SPCL.CULIANU); [Bondor bibliography’s archival-materials section](https://bibgtkneamt.ebibliophil.ro/mon/ioan-petru-culianu-bibliografie-vol-1-opera-fqsjyyp5))
- **dates various:** *Drawings, inscriptions, and other graphic traces*. Bondor’s bibliography identifies drawings, handwritten dedications, textual facsimiles, and images as a distinct iconographic corpus. These are made things, but many resist classification as autonomous works: some are records, paratexts, or gestures attached to other objects. ([Bondor bibliography’s iconography divisions](https://bibgtkneamt.ebibliophil.ro/mon/ioan-petru-culianu-bibliografie-vol-1-opera-fqsjyyp5))
