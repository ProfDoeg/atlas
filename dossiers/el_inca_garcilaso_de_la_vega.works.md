## Works by El Inca Garcilaso de la Vega

### Translation

- **1590:** *La traduzión del Indio de los tres Diálogos de Amor de León Hebreo*. Spanish translation of Judah Abravanel’s Italian philosophical dialogues, furnished with Garcilaso’s own dedicatory material; it is a made linguistic and interpretive object rather than an original treatise. The 1590 printing is the first published work bearing his authorship. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/inca_garcilaso_de_la_vega/su_obra_catalogo/))

### Genealogical project

- **1596:** *Relación de la descendencia del famoso Garci Pérez de Vargas con algunos pasos de historia dignos de memoria*. A manuscript genealogy and family panegyric, apparently conceived as prefatory material for *La Florida del Inca* but separated from that book and left unpublished; its first, incomplete transcription appeared only in 1929. It is therefore both an obra and the surviving form of an abandoned component of a larger project. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://cervantesvirtual.com/obra/relacion-de-la-descendencia-del-famoso-garci-perez-de-vargas-manuscrito--0/), [Ministerio de Cultura del Perú](https://repositorio.cultura.gob.pe/bitstream/handle/CULTURA/1277/Inca%20Garcilaso%20de%20la%20Vega%20-%20Vida%20e%20historia.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1), [José Antonio Mazzotti](https://repositorio.minedu.gob.pe/bitstream/handle/20.500.12799/8252/Historia%20de%20las%20literaturas%20en%20el%20Per%C3%BA.%20Volumen%202.%20Literatura%20y%20cultura%20en%20el%20Virreinato%20del%20Per%C3%BA%20apropiaci%C3%B3n%20y%20diferencia.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1))

### Historical narratives

- **1605:** *La Florida del Inca*. A prose history of Hernando de Soto’s expedition into Florida, constructed from testimony, other written materials, and Garcilaso’s historiographical shaping rather than from his own eyewitness experience. Although published as a book in Lisbon, it had passed through earlier drafts, so the 1605 object is the completed manifestation of a much longer historical project. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/inca_garcilaso_de_la_vega/su_obra_catalogo/), [AECID](https://peru.aecid.es/fr/web/guest/vitrina-2-inca-garcilaso))

- **1609:** *Primera parte de los Comentarios Reales*. The first published half of Garcilaso’s large historical project: a prose account of Andean peoples, Inca origins, institutions, religion, and imperial history, explicitly mediated through memory, family testimony, written chronicles, and philological commentary. Calling it simply a “book” obscures its status as one part of a planned two-part obra. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/inca_garcilaso_de_la_vega/su_obra_catalogo/), [Centro Cultural Inca Garcilaso](https://www.ccincagarcilaso.gob.pe/actividades/inca-garcilaso-de-la-vega/))

- **completed by 1612; published posthumously 1617:** *Segunda parte de los Comentarios Reales*, published as *Historia general del Perú*. The continuation treats the Spanish conquest and subsequent civil wars and was complete enough to enter the licensing process during Garcilaso’s lifetime. Surviving contracts, approvals, and page headings call it *Segunda parte de los Comentarios Reales*; *Historia general del Perú* appears on the posthumous edition’s title page, so whether that familiar title represents Garcilaso’s own naming is disputed. ([Biblioteca Nacional del Perú](https://repositoriodigital.bnp.gob.pe/bnp/recursos/2/html/boletin-de-la-biblioteca-nacional-n-37-38/10/), [CLACSO edition and bibliographical study](https://biblioteca.clacso.edu.ar/clacso/se/20190904031821/Comentarios_reales_1_Inca_Garcilaso_de_la_Vega.pdf), [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/inca_garcilaso_de_la_vega/su_obra_catalogo/))

### Annotated book

- **after 1555; annotation date unknown:** *La historia general de las Indias y Nuevo Mundo* by Francisco López de Gómara, annotated by the Inca Garcilaso. This is not a book authored by Garcilaso but a pre-existing 1555 printed book made into a new, composite object by manuscript marginalia associated with him and with the conquistador Gonzalo Silvestre. The catalogue attributes annotations to Garcilaso, while scholarship identifies some notes as reporting or marking Silvestre’s interventions; the object therefore resists treatment as either a wholly independent Garcilasan work or a securely single-authored text. ([Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/inca_garcilaso_de_la_vega/su_obra_historia_indias/), [Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú](https://repositorio.pucp.edu.pe/bitstreams/70f3004d-872f-48c5-9568-ee9063097458/download))

### Surviving correspondence

- **31 December 1592:** *Letter to Juan Fernández Franco*. An autograph private letter responding to the Andalusian antiquarian; it is documentary correspondence, not an autonomous obra, but it is a surviving text made and signed by Garcilaso. Eugenio Asensio recovered it together with the following letter and published both in 1953. ([Eugenio Asensio, “Dos cartas desconocidas del Inca Garcilaso”](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/319888431_Dos_cartas_desconocidas_del_Inca_Garcilaso), [Biblioteca Nacional Digital de Chile](https://www.bibliotecanacionaldigital.gob.cl/pywb/dibam/20190911020237mp_/http%3A/www.bibliotecanacionaldigital.gob.cl/bnd/628/w3-article-257248.html))

- **20 May 1593:** *Letter to Juan Fernández Franco*. A second autograph reply concerning personal circumstances, books, and Franco’s contemplated journey; like its companion, it resists classification as a “work” and survives instead as an act of correspondence. ([Eugenio Asensio, “Dos cartas desconocidas del Inca Garcilaso”](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/319888431_Dos_cartas_desconocidas_del_Inca_Garcilaso), [Biblioteca Nacional Digital de Chile](https://www.bibliotecanacionaldigital.gob.cl/pywb/dibam/20190911020237mp_/http%3A/www.bibliotecanacionaldigital.gob.cl/bnd/628/w3-article-257248.html))
