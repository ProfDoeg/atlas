### Botanical experiments and protocols

- **late 1890s–early 1900s:** *Yerba-mate germination method*. A reproducible horticultural protocol developed through experiments at the Buenos Aires Botanical Garden and subsequently published; it is a made procedure rather than a book or garden.[^2]
- **1891–1913:** *Program of native-tree acclimatization and street planting in Buenos Aires*. A long-duration civic and botanical obra that introduced and propagated species such as tipa, lapacho, jacarandá and palo borracho; it resists division into discrete titled works.[^2]
- **1891 onward:** *Buenos Aires street-tree system*. A citywide planting program carried out through the Dirección de Paseos; its thousands of individual plantings form infrastructure and living collection rather than one conventionally bounded artwork.[^4]

### Writings, reports and photographic surveys

- **1883:** *Plantes utiles cultivées au Paraguay*. A horticultural periodical article, partly translated from the *Gardeners’ Chronicle*; attribution therefore includes both Thays’s editorial or translational work and borrowed source material.[^7]
- **1891:** *Informe de acción futura*. A programmatic competition report for the directorship of Buenos Aires parks: part administrative proposal, part manifesto for hygienic and socially accessible urban gardens.[^4]
- **1910:** *El Jardín Botánico de Buenos Aires*. A book-length institutional and botanical catalogue describing the garden’s history and living collections—not merely a guidebook, but the textual manifestation of the garden as scientific obra.[^8]
- **March 1912:** *Report on the Cataratas del Iguazú region*. An expedition report, conservation proposal and photographic survey containing 103 photographs; it is simultaneously document, project and visual atlas.[^4]
- **1913:** *Les Forêts naturelles de la République Argentine: projets de parcs nationaux*. A book and congress paper on Argentine forests and proposed national parks, especially Iguazú and Nahuel Huapi.[^8]
- **dates not fully enumerated:** *Articles for the Revue Horticole*. A decade-long body of horticultural journalism rather than one work; the consulted collected-writings edition establishes the corpus, but the accessible catalogue does not expose a complete article-by-article contents list.[^9]
- **dates not fully enumerated:** *Municipal reports and annual memorias*. Administrative and technical texts produced through the Dirección de Paseos. They resist treatment as autonomous “works,” and the standard collected-writings source describes them as numerous without supplying a complete open enumeration.[^4][^9]
