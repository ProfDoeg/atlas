## Works by Maria Tecla Artemisia Montessori

### Medical, psychiatric, and anthropological studies

- **1896:** *Sul significato dei cristalli del Leyden nell’asma bronchiale*. A medical research article on leucine crystals in bronchial asthma, and Montessori’s earliest publication recorded in the standard Opera Nazionale Montessori bibliography. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)
- **1897:** *Ricerche batteriologiche sul liquido cefalo-rachidiano dei dementi paralitici*. A laboratory study of cerebrospinal fluid, published as an offprint from a journal of psychology, psychiatry, and neuropathology. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)
- **1897:** *Sulle cosiddette allucinazioni antagonistiche*. A jointly authored psychiatric article with Sante De Sanctis; its shared authorship should not be collapsed into sole authorship by Montessori. [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/montessori-maria/), [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)
- **1901–1902:** *Norme per una classificazione dei deficienti in rapporto ai metodi speciali di educazione*. A congress paper proposing a classification connected to special-education methods; its historical terminology is retained only because it belongs to the title. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)
- **1903:** *L’Antropologia pedagogica*. A shorter early study in pedagogical anthropology, distinct from the substantially larger 1910 book bearing nearly the same title. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)
- **1903:** *La teoria lombrosiana e l’educazione morale*. An article examining Lombrosian theory in relation to moral education. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)
- **1904:** *Sui caratteri antropometrici in relazione alle gerarchie intellettuali dei fanciulli nelle scuole*. An empirical anthropometric study correlating schoolchildren’s physical measurements with purported intellectual hierarchies; its categories belong to now-discredited racialized and positivist anthropology. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)
- **1904:** *Influenze delle condizioni di famiglia sul livello intellettuale degli scolari*. A social and anthropological study of family circumstances and pupils’ measured intellectual level. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)
- **1905:** *Caratteri fisici delle giovani donne del Lazio*. An extended anthropometric study of young women in Lazio, issued both in proceedings and as an offprint. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)
- **1907:** *L’importanza dell’etnologia regionale nell’antropologia pedagogica*. A scientific article arguing for regional ethnology within pedagogical anthropology. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)
- **1910:** *Antropologia pedagogica*. A book-length synthesis of Montessori’s university teaching and research in anthropology, hygiene, and education; it is related to, but not merely an edition of, the shorter 1903 study. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)

### Feminist, social, and educational interventions

- **1898–1899:** *Intervento al Congresso di Torino*. A spoken intervention at the First Italian National Pedagogical Congress, surviving as a short text in the published proceedings; it resists treatment as a self-standing book because the documentary object is a record of an act of speech. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)
- **1898:** *Miserie sociali e nuovi ritrovati della scienza*. A two-part periodical essay joining social reform to contemporary science. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)
- **1899:** *La questione femminile e il Congresso di Londra*. A two-part feminist report and intervention concerning the London women’s congress. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)
- **1900:** *Riassunto delle lezioni di didattica*. Lithographed teaching notes: a course-summary or protocol rather than a conventional authored monograph; it was later absorbed into an appendix to *L’autoeducazione nelle scuole elementari*. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)
- **1907:** *La Casa dei Bambini dell’Istituto Romano dei Beni Stabili*. The published form of Montessori’s 7 April 1907 conference on the Children’s House; both lecture-event and printed text belong to the obra. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)
- **1908–1912:** *La morale sessuale nell’educazione*. A congress address on sexual morality in education, delivered in 1908 but printed in the congress proceedings in 1912; a contemporary report also survives, so the spoken manifestation and printed text are not identical evidence. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)
- **1908:** *Come si insegna a leggere e a scrivere nelle “Case dei Bambini” di Roma*. A periodical article explaining literacy practice in the Roman Children’s Houses. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)
- **1909:** *Corso di Pedagogia Scientifica*. A printed course or course-outline from Città di Castello; it is pedagogical instruction and therefore partly a protocol, not simply a descriptive text. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)

### Pedagogical books and protocols

- **1909–1950:** *Il Metodo della Pedagogia Scientifica applicato all’educazione infantile nelle Case dei Bambini*; later *La scoperta del bambino*. An evolving pedagogical obra and practical protocol, enlarged in 1913, 1926, and 1935 and substantially recast under a new Italian title in 1950; those states should not be counted as wholly independent books, although their differences are bibliographically significant. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/), [ONM critical-edition catalogue](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/product-category/libri-onm/)
- **1914:** *Dr. Montessori’s Own Handbook*; Italian title *Manuale di pedagogia scientifica*. A compact instructional handbook for using the environment and materials; the English original preceded the 1921 Italian edition. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)
- **1916:** *L’autoeducazione nelle scuole elementari*. A large continuation of *Il Metodo* for older children, combining theory with protocols for grammar, geometry, arithmetic, music, and other elementary work; English publication divided it into *Spontaneous Activity in Education* and *The Montessori Elementary Material*, not two independently conceived Italian works. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/), [Nature review of the two-volume English manifestation](https://doi.org/10.1038/101221a0)
- **1923–1936:** *Das Kind in der Familie*; Italian title *Il bambino in famiglia*. A work assembled from lectures about the child’s position in domestic life; the German manifestation of 1923 predates the Italian book of 1936. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)
- **1934:** *Psico Geométria*. A Spanish-first geometry treatise and teaching protocol built around manipulation, movement, and visual demonstration; later English and Italian manifestations regularize the title as *Psychogeometry*. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/), [Montessori-Pierson series](https://montessori-pierson.com/products/montessori-series-total-serie)
- **1934:** *Psico Aritmética*. A Spanish-first arithmetic treatise and material-based protocol; its Italian manifestation, *Psicoaritmetica*, did not appear until 1971 and is not a new work by Montessori. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)
- **1936–1938:** *L’Enfant*; Italian title *Il segreto dell’infanzia*. A theoretical account of early development and the child’s often-concealed psychic life; the French first edition appeared in 1936 and the Italian manifestation in 1938. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)
- **1946:** *Education for a New World*. A book developed from Montessori’s wartime Indian teaching about human development and educational reconstruction; it is a lecture-derived text, so the exact boundary between Montessori’s spoken composition and editorial transcription is not completely transparent. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/), [Montessori Foundation bibliography](https://www.montessori.org/a-montessori-bibliography/)
- **1947:** *To Educate the Human Potential*. A lecture-derived exposition of education for children approximately six to twelve and of what became known as cosmic education. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)
- **1948:** *De l’enfant à l’adolescent*; Italian title *Dall’infanzia all’adolescenza*. A developmental and institutional project extending Montessori education through elementary school and adolescence; it includes the unrealized Erdkinder or farm-school plan, which is a project even where no corresponding school was made by Montessori herself. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/), [AMI account of the developmental continuum](https://montessori-ami.org/sites/default/files/downloads/AMIJournal2020.pdf)
- **1949:** *Formazione dell’uomo*. A synthetic work on language, human formation, and the social implications of education. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)
- **1949–1952:** *The Absorbent Mind*; Italian title *La mente del bambino. Mente assorbente*. A late synthesis of Montessori’s developmental theory. The 1949 English text was based on students’ notes from lectures, while Montessori personally prepared the substantially revised Italian text published in 1952; they are therefore disputed manifestations rather than safely interchangeable editions. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/), [Montessori Foundation bibliography](https://www.montessori.org/a-montessori-bibliography/)

### Liturgical protocols

- **1922:** *I bambini viventi nella Chiesa*. Montessori’s first book presenting Catholic liturgy from the child’s point of view; it is both theological reflection and a protocol for children’s participation. [AMI timeline](https://montessori-ami.org/node/1821), [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)
- **1931:** *La vita in Cristo*. A liturgical-educational work continuing Montessori’s program of children’s religious participation. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)
- **1932:** *The Mass Explained to Children*; Italian title *La Santa Messa spiegata ai bambini*. A practical explanation of the Mass designed for children; the English manifestation preceded the 1949 Italian edition. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)

### Peace texts and manifestos

- **1932–1939:** *Education and Peace* / *Educazione e pace*. A body of congress addresses and lectures rather than a single text written at one sitting; as an appeal to reorganize education against war, it operates partly as manifesto. The 1949 book is an editorial collection of earlier interventions, not evidence that every component originated that year. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/), [Montessori Foundation bibliography](https://www.montessori.org/a-montessori-bibliography/)
- **1949:** *Formazione dell’uomo*. Besides being pedagogical theory, this book functions as a social manifesto: education is presented as the formation of humanity rather than the transmission of a school syllabus. It is listed here by function, not as a second work. [ONM bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)

### Lecture-courses and posthumously constructed books

- **1913:** *The 1913 Rome Lectures*. A course-event preserved through lecture records and published much later as a collected book; the modern volume is an editorial manifestation, not a book Montessori herself issued in 1913. [Montessori-Pierson collected series](https://montessori-pierson.com/products/montessori-series-total-serie)
- **1915:** *The California Lectures of Maria Montessori, 1915*. Lectures, speeches, and writings associated with Montessori’s California visit, collected posthumously; their unity is archival and editorial rather than that of an original monograph. [Montessori Foundation bibliography](https://www.montessori.org/a-montessori-bibliography/)
- **1939:** *Creative Development in the Child*. Notes from Montessori’s Madras course, translated by Mario Montessori as a study aid and only edited into two volumes in 1994 and 1998; the lectures are Montessori’s, but the surviving book-object is jointly mediated by note-takers, translator, compiler, and editor. [Montessori Foundation bibliography](https://www.montessori.org/a-montessori-bibliography/)
- **1939–1941:** *The Montessori Approach to Music*. A course-derived treatment of music education later placed in the authorized Montessori series; it resists classification as a book wholly made by Montessori because its present form depends on subsequent compilation. [Montessori-Pierson collected series](https://montessori-pierson.com/products/montessori-series-total-serie)
- **1941–1942:** *Cosmic Education*. A body of Indian lecture work presenting the universe and human interdependence as the organizing frame of elementary education; later publication turns a course and pedagogical project into a book. [Montessori Foundation bibliography](https://www.montessori.org/a-montessori-bibliography/), [scholarly account of the Indian project](https://rivistedigitali.erickson.it/pedagogia-piu-didattica/archivio/vol-10-n-1/leducazione-cosmica-di-maria-montessori-alle-origini-di-un-progetto-ancora-oggi-attuale/)
- **1946:** *The 1946 London Lectures*. Thirty-three lectures recorded by an assistant and published decades later; the course is Montessori’s made event, while the wording and boundaries of the book depend upon transcription and editing. [Association Montessori de France](https://www.montessori-france.asso.fr/page/155448-bibliographie-de-maria-montessori), [Montessori-Pierson collected series](https://montessori-pierson.com/products/montessori-series-total-serie)
- **1948:** *Child Education*. A sequence of talks delivered through All India Radio and subsequently printed; it is simultaneously a broadcast manifestation and a lecture-derived text. [Montessori Foundation bibliography](https://www.montessori.org/a-montessori-bibliography/)
- **undated lectures, published posthumously:** *Maria Montessori Speaks to Parents*. An editorially assembled group of talks to parents; it should not be assigned a single composition date merely from the publication date of a later volume. [Montessori-Pierson collected series](https://montessori-pierson.com/products/montessori-series-total-serie)
- **various dates, published 1989:** *The Child, Society and the World: Unpublished Speeches and Writings*. A posthumous editorial collection, not a unitary manuscript left by Montessori; individual speeches and writings retain their separate origins even though the authorized series binds them as one object. [Montessori Foundation bibliography](https://www.montessori.org/a-montessori-bibliography/)
- **various dates, posthumous:** *Citizen of the World*. A thematic editorial gathering of Montessori’s lectures and writings about social development and humanity; the title names a later constructed collection rather than a lifetime book-project securely documented under that name. [Montessori-Pierson collected series](https://montessori-pierson.com/products/montessori-series-total-serie)
- **various dates, posthumous:** *Education for Human Development*. An editorial selection from Montessori’s teaching, included in the authorized series; it resists treatment as an independently composed lifetime book. [Montessori-Pierson collected series](https://montessori-pierson.com/products/montessori-series-total-serie)
- **various dates, posthumous:** *Educateurs sans Frontières*. A later thematic compilation associated with Montessori’s international and humanitarian educational vision; the collected object is editorial, although its component discourse derives from Montessori. [Montessori-Pierson collected series](https://montessori-pierson.com/products/montessori-series-total-serie)
- **various dates, posthumous:** *Interpreting Montessori*. A later authorized compilation or interpretive arrangement within the Montessori series; without item-level archival identification it should not be mistaken for a manuscript or fixed project bearing that title during Montessori’s life. [Montessori-Pierson collected series](https://montessori-pierson.com/products/montessori-series-total-serie)

### Environments, materials, and Gesamtkunstwerk

- **1907 onward:** *Casa dei Bambini*. A prepared social and educational environment first realized in Rome’s San Lorenzo district: school, laboratory, furniture system, daily practice, and community institution at once. Montessori directed its educational form but did not solely make the building or originate every institutional circumstance, so it is best understood as a collaborative Gesamtkunstwerk rather than her architectural work. [ONM history and bibliography](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/), [historical study](https://rivisteopen.unimc.it/index.php/hecl/article/download/3856/7451/27850)
- **circa 1907–1952:** *The prepared environment*. An evolving environmental protocol joining child-scaled furnishings, ordered activity, freedom of movement, observation, and teacher restraint. It has no single object, title, or completion date and therefore resists being called one work, yet it is among Montessori’s most consequential made systems. [ONM account of the method](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/), [Nature review](https://doi.org/10.1038/101221a0)
- **circa 1907 onward:** *The Montessori didactic materials*. A system of manipulable objects for practical life, sensory discrimination, language, mathematics, geometry, and culture. Montessori selected, adapted, tested, and extended materials indebted in part to Jean-Marc-Gaspard Itard and Édouard Séguin; attribution to her as the sole inventor of every object would therefore be false, and the system is better classified as an evolving material ecology. [Nature review](https://doi.org/10.1038/101221a0), [ONM account of the method](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)
- **1910s onward:** *Elementary materials*. A coordinated family of grammar symbols, movable language components, bead-based arithmetic devices, geometric constructions, charts, and classified cultural materials. Their protocols and objects form a Gesamtkunstwerk with *L’autoeducazione*, while individual pieces often emerged through classroom collaboration and later standardization rather than a single documented act of invention. [Nature review](https://doi.org/10.1038/101221a0)
- **1930s–1940s:** *Erdkinder*. Montessori’s farm-school project for adolescents, combining study, productive work, economic exchange, residence, and social organization. It remained substantially unrealized by Montessori and is therefore precisely a project—not a completed school or fixed textual object. [AMI developmental overview](https://montessori-ami.org/sites/default/files/downloads/AMIJournal2020.pdf), [study of Montessori programme succession](https://cejsh.icm.edu.pl/cejsh/element/ojs-doi-10_26881_pwe_2024_58_12)
- **1939–1946:** *Cosmic education*. A curricular project developed and tested especially during Montessori’s years in India, integrating natural history, human history, geography, science, ethics, and peace into a unified account of interdependence. It is neither one book nor merely a set of lessons, but a cross-media framework of stories, charts, experiments, materials, and activity. [Pedagogia più Didattica](https://rivistedigitali.erickson.it/pedagogia-piu-didattica/archivio/vol-10-n-1/leducazione-cosmica-di-maria-montessori-alle-origini-di-un-progetto-ancora-oggi-attuale/), [University of Bologna research record](https://hdl.handle.net/11585/666458)

### Organizations as institutional works

- **1924:** *Opera Nazionale Montessori*. An institution founded to develop and protect Montessori education in Italy. It is a collaborative legal and political body rather than a solely authored object; Montessori and Mario resigned in 1934 when Fascist control became incompatible with their educational principles, and the organization was reconstituted after the war. [Opera Nazionale Montessori](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/chi-siamo/), [ONM biographical account](https://operanazionalemontessori.it/maria-montessori-2/)
- **1929:** *Association Montessori Internationale*. An international organization founded by Maria Montessori with Mario Montessori to maintain the identity of the educational work and coordinate its worldwide movement. Like any institution, it exceeds individual authorship and continued changing after its founders’ acts. [AMI founding history](https://montessori-ami.org/news/1929-where-it-all-began), [AMI timeline](https://montessori-ami.org/node/1821)
