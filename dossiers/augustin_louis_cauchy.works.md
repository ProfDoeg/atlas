## Works by Augustin-Louis Cauchy

### Course-books and pedagogical protocols

- **1821:** *Cours d’analyse de l’École royale polytechnique, première partie: Analyse algébrique*. A foundational course-book and protocol for rigorous algebraic analysis; the announced continuation never appeared in this form, so the projected *Cours* remained incomplete. [[OC II.3](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k29058v)]
- **1823:** *Résumé des leçons données à l’École royale polytechnique sur le calcul infinitésimal*. A compressed teaching protocol covering differential and integral calculus; Cauchy later replaced this exhausted volume with two planned books, only the differential-calculus volume being completed. [[OC II.4](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k90195t)]
- **1826:** *Leçons sur les applications du calcul infinitésimal à la géométrie*. A course-book applying calculus to curves, surfaces, and geometry. It is a pedagogical work rather than a research memoir, although it contains original formulations. [[OC II.5](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k29060f)]
- **1828:** *Leçons sur les applications du calcul infinitésimal à la géométrie, deuxième partie*. The continuation devoted principally to solid geometry and related applications; bibliographies sometimes collapse both parts into the single date-range 1826–1828. [[OC II.5](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k29060f)]
- **1829:** *Leçons sur le calcul différentiel*. A substantially revised replacement for the differential portion of the 1823 *Résumé*. Its intended companion on integral calculus was a project, not a completed book. [[Cambridge, “Avertissement”](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/oeuvres-completes/avertissement/3D87601F47453CCA2D84477D24176DB6)]

### Author-issued research series: works made by accumulation

- **1826:** *Exercices de mathématiques, première année*. A periodically issued research miscellany whose installments combine memoirs, notes, problems, and continuations; it resists classification as either one book or a mere journal because Cauchy authored and organized the whole sequence. [[OC II.6](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k90196f)]
- **1827:** *Exercices de mathématiques, deuxième année*. The second annual sequence of Cauchy’s independently issued mathematical investigations. [[OC II.7](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k901977)]
- **1828:** *Exercices de mathématiques, troisième année*. The third annual sequence, again a container made from individually titled memoirs rather than a continuous treatise. [[OC II.8](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k90198k)]
- **1829:** *Exercices de mathématiques, quatrième année*. The fourth annual sequence of mathematical memoirs and notes. [[OC II.9](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k90199x)]
- **1830:** *Exercices de mathématiques, cinquième année*. The final annual sequence of the original *Exercices*; the five years together comprise fifty-one fascicles, so treating the title as one book conceals its serial form. [[National Library of Ireland contents](https://catalogue.nli.ie/Record/vtls000401741/TOC)]
- **1833:** *Résumés analytiques*. A Turin series of five research fascicles, not a conventional résumé or textbook despite its title; it gathers investigations produced during Cauchy’s exile. [[OC II.10 contents](https://www.e-rara.ch/zut/content/structure/5700345)]
- **1835–1836:** *Nouveaux exercices de mathématiques*. Eight Prague fascicles continuing the author-issued *Exercices* model. Its original parts are rare, and later collected editions can misleadingly make it look like a single retrospectively composed book. [[OC II.10 contents](https://www.e-rara.ch/zut/content/structure/5700345)]
- **1840–1847:** *Exercices d’analyse et de physique mathématique*. A four-volume authorial research series containing separately titled investigations in analysis, mechanics, optics, and mathematical physics; it is best treated as an œuvre-program made through serial publication. [[OC II.11–13 and II.15](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/%C5%92uvres_compl%C3%A8tes_d%27Augustin_Cauchy)]

### Separately constituted scientific memoirs

- **1811:** *Recherches sur les polyèdres. Premier mémoire*. Cauchy’s first published scientific memoir, on convex polyhedra. It is an article-sized research object, not a book. [[OC II.1](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/oeuvres-completes/frontmatter/D406B9567510B9D50D266DCA3B698485)]
- **1811:** *Sur les polygones et les polyèdres. Second mémoire*. A companion memoir extending the first investigation. [[OC II.1](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/oeuvres-completes/frontmatter/D406B9567510B9D50D266DCA3B698485)]
- **1813:** *Recherches sur les nombres*. A research memoir in number theory. [[OC II.1](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/oeuvres-completes/frontmatter/D406B9567510B9D50D266DCA3B698485)]
- **1815:** *Mémoire sur le nombre des valeurs qu’une fonction peut acquérir lorsqu’on y permute de toutes les manières possibles les quantités qu’elle renferme*. A memoir on permutations and the values of functions. [[OC II.1](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/oeuvres-completes/frontmatter/D406B9567510B9D50D266DCA3B698485)]
- **1815:** *Mémoire sur les fonctions qui ne peuvent obtenir que deux valeurs égales et de signes contraires par suite des transpositions opérées entre les variables qu’elles renferment*. A memoir on alternating functions, originally communicated earlier and printed in 1815; presentation and publication dates therefore differ. [[OC II.1](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/oeuvres-completes/frontmatter/D406B9567510B9D50D266DCA3B698485)]
- **1815:** *Mémoire sur la détermination du nombre des racines réelles dans les équations algébriques*. A research memoir on counting real roots of algebraic equations. [[OC II.1](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/oeuvres-completes/frontmatter/D406B9567510B9D50D266DCA3B698485)]
- **1815:** *Sur les racines imaginaires des équations*. A short mathematical memoir on non-real roots. [[OC II.1](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/oeuvres-completes/frontmatter/D406B9567510B9D50D266DCA3B698485)]
- **1815:** *Mémoire sur une espèce particulière de mouvement des fluides*. A mathematical-physics memoir on fluid motion. [[OC II.1](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/oeuvres-completes/frontmatter/D406B9567510B9D50D266DCA3B698485)]
- **1815:** *Mémoire sur l’intégration des équations linéaires aux différentielles partielles et à coefficients constants*. A research memoir on linear partial differential equations. [[OC II.1](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/oeuvres-completes/frontmatter/D406B9567510B9D50D266DCA3B698485)]
- **1815; published 1827:** *Théorie de la propagation des ondes à la surface d’un fluide pesant d’une profondeur indéfinie*. A prize memoir on water waves. The work’s date is commonly given as 1815, when it was submitted and awarded, although its full publication came later. [[OC I.1](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k90181b)]
- **1814; published 1827:** *Mémoire sur les intégrales définies*. A long foundational memoir on definite integrals, presented in 1814 but printed in the Academy’s memoirs only in 1827; dating it simply “1827” erases its manuscript and presentation history. [[OC I.1](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k90181b)]
- **1825:** *Mémoire sur les intégrales définies prises entre des limites imaginaires*. A separately printed memoir that helped constitute Cauchy’s theory of complex integration. [[Gallica](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k90193x)]
- **1827:** *Mémoire sur les développements des fonctions en séries périodiques*. A printed Academy memoir on periodic-series expansions, associated with work presented in 1823. [[Académie des sciences](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k3221x)]
- **1829:** *Mémoire sur la résolution des équations numériques et sur la théorie de l’élimination*. A separately issued algebraic memoir on numerical equations and elimination. [[OC II.14–15 catalogue structure](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb302073189)]
- **1830:** *Mémoire sur la théorie de la lumière*. A separately issued physical-mathematical memoir; it belongs to Cauchy’s larger and repeatedly revised optical project rather than exhausting that project. [[OC II.14–15 catalogue structure](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb302073189)]
- **1843:** *Memoria sui metodi analitici*. An Italian-language memoir issued in Rome. It is a distinct manifestation of Cauchy’s analytical work, but its relationship to overlapping French papers makes it unsafe to treat every repeated theorem as a separate creation. [[OC bibliographic structure](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb302073189)]

### Academy communications, journal articles, reports, and speeches: a distributed œuvre

- **1811–1857:** *Mémoires, notes et articles extraits des recueils de l’Académie des sciences de l’Institut de France*. This is not one work made by Cauchy but a posthumously delimited corpus of hundreds of separately titled communications, memoirs, notes, reports, and interventions, now occupying twelve volumes of the first series of the *Œuvres complètes*. The corpus resists a book-by-book ontology: many items began as oral presentations, manuscript submissions, committee reports, or brief acts in the *Comptes rendus* before becoming printed texts. [[BnF catalogue](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb302073189)] [[Wikisource structure](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/%C5%92uvres_compl%C3%A8tes_d%27Augustin_Cauchy)]
- **1811–1857:** *Mémoires publiés dans divers recueils autres que ceux de l’Académie*. Another editorially constituted corpus rather than a work titled by Cauchy: it comprises his separately existing contributions to the *Journal de l’École polytechnique*, Crelle’s journal, Turin and Prague publications, and other venues, collected in the first two volumes of the second series. [[OC II.1 structure](https://www.e-rara.ch/zut/content/structure/5700345)] [[BnF catalogue](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb302073189)]
- **1835–1857:** *Notes aux Comptes rendus hebdomadaires des séances de l’Académie des sciences*. A vast sequence of short scientific acts—often announcing, correcting, extending, or disputing results—rather than a single authored book. The collected edition numbers more than five hundred Academy items; continuations and near-repetitions make “one thing” unstable at the level of individual notes. [[OC I.12 contents](https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511702433.077)] [[BnF catalogue](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb302073189)]
- **1811–1857:** *Scientific œuvre of Augustin-Louis Cauchy*. At its broadest defensible level this is an obra—roughly eight hundred papers spanning analysis, algebra, geometry, number theory, mechanics, probability, astronomy, elasticity, and optics—not a single text and not completely reducible to his separately issued books. The twenty-seven-volume collected edition is a posthumous manifestation assembled by the Académie des sciences, not itself something Cauchy made; its final volume appeared only in 1974, and bibliographic counts vary according to whether continuations, reports, and repeated manifestations are counted separately. [[BnF catalogue](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb302073189)] [[CTHS](https://cths.fr/an/savant.php?id=1390)]

### Projects, lectures, and objects that resist work-status

- **1820s:** *Cours d’analyse de l’École polytechnique*. Beyond the published 1821 volume, this was a larger pedagogical project encompassing lectures, curricular obligations, and projected continuations. Only part became the book bearing that title, so the project must not be equated with its surviving first volume. [[OC II.3](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k29058v)]
- **1829 onward:** *Treatise on integral calculus*. Announced as the companion to *Leçons sur le calcul différentiel* but never completed as the promised separate book. Related material survives across memoirs and exercises, yet those manifestations do not establish a finished treatise. [[Cambridge, “Avertissement”](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/oeuvres-completes/avertissement/3D87601F47453CCA2D84477D24176DB6)]
- **1830s:** *Turin courses and lectures*. A teaching project in mathematical physics and analysis known partly through the *Résumés analytiques* and related memoirs. The lectures as events are manifestations rather than stable texts, and the surviving publications do not justify inventing a complete course-title or date. [[OC II.10 structure](https://www.e-rara.ch/zut/content/structure/5700345)]
- **1830s:** *Prague courses and lectures*. A pedagogical project associated with Cauchy’s service to the exiled Bourbon household and with the *Nouveaux exercices de mathématiques*. The instruction itself is not securely recoverable as a single authored work. [[OC II.10 structure](https://www.e-rara.ch/zut/content/structure/5700345)]
- **1848–1857:** *Sorbonne lectures on mathematical astronomy*. A lecture project rather than a completed publication; portions of its mathematical material appeared elsewhere, but no integral author-issued course survives under a fixed title. [[Œuvres complètes, collected-edition record](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb302073189)]
- **No single date:** *Reports made for the Académie des sciences*. These are committee acts, often collaboratively authorized even when drafted or delivered by Cauchy. They resist uncomplicated attribution as sole-authored œuvres; the collected edition includes them because they form part of his scientific activity, not because each is necessarily an autonomous Cauchy work. [[BnF, example of a report naming Cauchy as rapporteur](https://data.bnf.fr/fr/ark:/12148/cb11895590x.pdf)]
- **No single date:** *Funeral and institutional addresses*. Speeches delivered through the Académie—including Cauchy’s address at Jacques-Philippe-Marie Binet’s funeral—are performative manifestations: spoken institutional acts subsequently printed, rather than mathematical books. Some printed records combine Cauchy’s words with those of other speakers, so sole authorship applies only to his contribution. [[BnF authority bibliography](https://data.bnf.fr/fr/ark:/12148/cb11895590x.pdf)]

### Posthumous manifestation, not an authored work

- **1882–1974:** *Œuvres complètes d’Augustin Cauchy*. A twenty-seven-tome scholarly collected edition in two series (physically catalogued by the BnF as twenty-eight volumes because of its separate table). It is indispensable for identifying what Cauchy made, but it was edited and arranged after his death by the Académie des sciences; it is therefore a manifestation of his œuvre, not a work authored by him under this title. [[BnF catalogue](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb302073189)] [[Wikisource contents](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/%C5%92uvres_compl%C3%A8tes_d%27Augustin_Cauchy)]
