## Works by Ibn Arabi

### Encyclopedic and synthetic obras

- **1202–1238:** *al-Futūḥāt al-Makkiyya* (RG 135, *The Meccan Openings*). An encyclopedic obra of 560 chapters combining mystical testimony, Qurʾanic interpretation, jurisprudence, cosmology, poetry, and spiritual instruction; Ibn ʿArabī completed a first recension in 1231 and a substantially rewritten second recension in 1238.
- **1229–1230:** *Fuṣūṣ al-ḥikam* (RG 150, *The Ringstones of Wisdom*). A compact prophetic and metaphysical summa arranged as twenty-seven “wisdoms,” each associated with a prophet; its authorial manuscript evidence places it among the securely authenticated works.
- **Before 1234:** *Muḥāḍarat al-abrār wa-musāmarat al-akhyār* (RG 493). A large, miscellany-like gathering of reports, poems, anecdotes, teachings, and edifying material; textual evidence establishes the attribution, although no authorially authenticated witness survives.
- **Before 1229:** *Kitāb al-ʿAbādila* (RG 2). A systematic obra of 117 sections in which figures named ʿAbd Allāh embody relationships among divine names, prophets, and human qualities.
- **Before 1209:** *Kitāb al-Tajalliyāt* (RG 738, *Book of Theophanies*). A sequence of visionary manifestations and dialogues with earlier saints which also functions as instruction about events encountered on the spiritual path.
- **Date unknown:** *ʿUqlat al-mustawfiz* (RG 802, *The Snare for the One Preparing to Leap*). A securely authenticated cosmological synthesis concerning the world’s production and the human being’s place within it.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Muqniʿ fī īḍāḥ al-sahl al-mumtaniʿ* (RG 511). A securely authenticated book whose title presents it as an elucidation of what is simultaneously easy and inaccessible.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb Marātib ʿulūm al-wahb* (RG 423). A securely authenticated classification of the ranks of divinely bestowed knowledge.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Masāʾil* (RG 433). A securely authenticated book of questions or problems; its title names a textual form rather than a single continuous argument.

### Visionary journeys and manifestations

- **1194:** *Mashāhid al-asrār al-qudsiyya* (RG 432, *Contemplations of the Holy Mysteries*). Fourteen dialogical visionary contemplations written in al-Andalus; it is as much a record of manifestations as a discursive treatise.
- **After 1198:** *Kitāb al-Isrāʾ* (RG 313, *Book of the Night Journey*). A rhymed-prose account of mystical ascent through the prophetic heavens, making visionary itinerary and literary construction inseparable.
- **1199:** *ʿAnqāʾ mughrib* (RG 30, *The Fabulous Gryphon of the West*). An intricately rhymed meditation on the Mahdī, the Seal of the Saints, and the Muhammadian reality, conceived as a companion to *al-Tadbīrāt al-ilāhiyya*.
- **1205:** *Risālat al-Anwār*, also *Kitāb Asrār al-khalwa* (RG 33, *Treatise of Lights/Secrets of Retreat*). An epistle mapping ascent through levels of existence and the return to creatures; the two established titles show how the object circulated as both letter and manual.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Isfār ʿan natāʾij al-asfār* (RG 307, *Unveiling the Effects of Journeys*). A meditation on unending spiritual journeying and the journeys of the prophets.
- **Date unknown:** *Risālat al-Ittiḥād al-kawnī* (RG 317). A securely authenticated epistle on cosmic conjunction or union.
- **Date unknown:** *Risālat al-Mubashshirāt* (RG 485). A securely authenticated collection or epistle concerning auspicious dreams and annunciations.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb Shawāhid al-ḥaqq fī al-qalb* (RG 689). A securely authenticated book on manifestations or attestations of Reality in the heart.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Fanāʾ fī al-mushāhada* (RG 125). A contemplative treatise on annihilation in witnessing, probably written at Baghdad around 1212.

### Cosmological, metaphysical, and letter-science obras

- **1201:** *Kitāb Inshāʾ al-dawāʾir* (RG 289, *Construction of the Circles*). A diagrammatic cosmology employing circles and tables to relate existence, nonexistence, manifestation, and the human microcosm; its visual reasoning makes it more than continuous prose.
- **Before 1207:** *Kitāb Ayyām al-shaʾn* (RG 67). A metaphysical investigation of time, the days of the week, and divine activity.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Alif wa-huwa kitāb al-aḥadiyya* (RG 26). The “Book of Alif,” or of Oneness, belonging to Ibn ʿArabī’s alphabetically organized series of short works.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Bāʾ* (RG 71). A securely authenticated member of the letter-books, treating the letter *bāʾ* as a metaphysical object rather than merely a written sign.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Mīm* (RG 462). A securely authenticated letter-book centered on *mīm*.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Hū* (RG 205). A securely authenticated metaphysical book centered on the divine pronoun “He.”
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Ḥaqq* (RG 219). A securely authenticated book on “the Real” or “the Truth.”
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Azal* (RG 68). A securely authenticated book on beginningless eternity.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-ʿAẓama* (RG 70). A securely authenticated book on divine magnificence.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Jalāla* (RG 169). A securely authenticated treatment of divine majesty.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Ḥujub* (RG 247). A securely authenticated book on veils.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb Mafātiḥ al-ghuyūb* (RG 386). A securely authenticated “keys to the unseen,” apparently a classificatory or initiatic treatment rather than a narrative.
- **Date unknown:** *Maqṣid al-asmāʾ* (RG 418). A securely authenticated work on the aim or referent of the divine names.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Qasam al-ilāhī bi-l-ism al-rabbānī* (RG 565). A securely authenticated book concerned with the divine oath and lordly name.
- **Date unknown:** *Nuskhat al-ḥaqq* (RG 551). A securely authenticated “transcript” or “copy of the Real”; its title deliberately troubles the distinction between text, archetype, and manifestation.
- **Date unknown:** *Madkhal fī ʿilm al-ḥurūf* (RG 384). A probable introduction to the science of letters; the attribution rests on strong but not conclusive circumstantial evidence.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Mabādiʾ wa-l-ghāyāt* (RG 380). A securely authenticated work on beginnings and ends; another text with the same title is classified as not by Ibn ʿArabī and must not be conflated with it.

### Qurʾanic, prophetic, and transmitted-knowledge works

- **1202–1203:** *Mishkāt al-anwār fī mā ruwiya ʿan Allāh* (RG 480, *The Niche of Lights*). A deliberately structured collection of 101 divine sayings, combining hadith transmission with contemplative arrangement.
- **1206:** *Ishārāt al-Qurʾān fī ʿālam al-insān* (RG 303). A sustained meditation on the Qurʾanic suras in relation to the human world, conceived as a companion to *al-Tanazzulāt al-Mawṣiliyya*.
- **Date unknown:** *Ījāz al-bayān fī tarjama ʿan al-Qurʾān* (RG 268). A securely authenticated Qurʾanic interpretive work; the surviving witness is in Ibn ʿArabī’s hand.
- **Date unknown:** *Faṣl mufīd fī tafsīr fātiḥat al-Kitāb* (RG 132). A short, securely authenticated commentary on the opening sura.
- **Date unknown:** *al-Juzʾ al-awwal min tafsīr kalām Allāh taʿālā* (RG 188.1). The first part of a Qurʾanic commentary, surviving under a probable rather than certain attribution; its completeness and relation to any larger project remain unclear.
- **Date unknown:** *Ikhtiṣār al-sīra al-nabawiyya al-Muḥammadiyya* (RG 276). A securely attributed abridgment of the Prophet Muḥammad’s biography, authenticated through internal textual evidence.
- **Date unknown:** *al-Kawkab al-durrī fī manāqib Dhī l-Nūn al-Miṣrī* (RG 347.1). A securely attributed hagiographic work on Dhū l-Nūn al-Miṣrī, though its evidence is textual rather than authorial-manuscript evidence.
- **Date unknown:** *Sharḥ Khalʿ al-naʿlayn* (RG 681). Ibn ʿArabī’s securely authenticated commentary on Ibn Qasī’s *Khalʿ al-naʿlayn*; it is an interpretive intervention in another author’s work, not an independently conceived summa.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Muʿashsharāt* (RG 484). A securely authenticated collection organized around groups of ten.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb Natāʾij al-adhkār* (RG 536). A securely authenticated book on the results or fruits of devotional recollections.

### Protocols and manuals of spiritual practice

- **1203:** *Ḥilyat al-abdāl* (RG 237, *Adornment of the Substitutes*). A short protocol-like treatise on seclusion, silence, hunger, and wakefulness, written for Badr al-Ḥabashī.
- **1205:** *al-Tanazzulāt al-Mawṣiliyya* (RG 762). A ritual and symbolic exposition of ablution and prayer; it instructs by disclosing meanings within embodied practice.
- **1205:** *Kitāb Kunh mā lā budda lil-murīd minhu* (RG 352). A concise protocol setting out what a spiritual aspirant cannot dispense with.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Khalwa* (RG 255). A securely authenticated manual or treatise on spiritual retreat.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Ifāda li-man arāda al-istifāda* (RG 266). A securely authenticated instructional book “for one who desires benefit.”
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Naṣāʾiḥ* (RG 532). A securely authenticated collection of counsels.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Yaqīn* (RG 834). A securely authenticated treatise on certainty.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Maḥajja al-bayḍāʾ* (RG 392). A securely authenticated guide whose title evokes the “white” or manifest path.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Amr al-muḥkam* (RG 28). A securely authenticated book concerning the decisive or firmly established command.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Iʿlām bi-ishārāt ahl al-ilhām* (RG 281). A securely authenticated exposition of the allusions used by people of inspiration.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb Manzil al-manāzil* (RG 412). A securely authenticated mapping of a “station of stations.”
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb Maqām al-qurba* (RG 414). A securely authenticated treatment of the station of proximity.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Nuqabāʾ* (RG 548). A securely authenticated work on the spiritual rank known as the *nuqabāʾ*.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Quṭb wa-l-imāmayn* (RG 585). A securely authenticated account of the Pole and the two Imams in the hierarchy of saints.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Aqṭāb* (RG 35). A securely attributed work on the spiritual Poles, though established through textual rather than autograph evidence.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Lawāqiḥ al-anwār* (RG 368.1). A securely attributed spiritual treatise whose authorship rests on internal evidence.
- **Date unknown:** *Awrād al-usbūʿ* (RG 64). Fourteen prayers arranged for the days and nights of the week: a performative devotional protocol rather than a merely descriptive text. Its attribution is probable, not certain.
- **Date unknown:** *Ḥizb al-aḥadiyya* (RG 238). A performative litany of divine unity; attribution remains probable because the surviving manuscripts are late.
- **Date unknown:** *Ḥizb al-wiqāya* (RG 244), also known as *al-Dawr al-aʿlā*. A protective devotional protocol meant to be recited and enacted; its attribution is probable rather than verified.
- **Date unknown:** *al-Ṣalāt al-fayḍiyya* (RG 702). A prayer of blessing, probably by Ibn ʿArabī but supported only by late witnesses.
- **Date unknown:** *Waṣiyya fī kayfiyyat ḥuṣūl al-maʿrifa al-quṣwā* (RG 821). A probable testament or protocol concerning attainment of the highest knowledge.

### Poetry and composite literary creations

- **Compiled 1215:** *Tarjumān al-ashwāq* (RG 767, *Interpreter of Ardent Desires*). A cycle of sixty-one love poems inspired by Niẓām and subsequently furnished by Ibn ʿArabī with a prose commentary; poem-cycle and interpretive defense together form a composite creation.
- **1215:** *Kitāb al-Dhakhāʾir wa-l-aʿlāq* (RG 116). Ibn ʿArabī’s commentary on *Tarjumān al-ashwāq*, created to explicate its spiritual meanings; it should be treated as a distinct interpretive obra, not merely as part of later editions of the poems.
- **1203:** *Tāj al-rasāʾil* (RG 736, *Crown of Epistles*). Eight literary love letters addressed to the Kaʿba and associated with divine-name manifestations during circumambulation.
- **By 1237:** *al-Dīwān al-kabīr* (RG 102, *The Great Dīwān*). Ibn ʿArabī’s large poetic corpus-project, apparently intended to gather all his poetry; divergent manuscripts and a later editorially arranged print corpus make its boundaries unstable.
- **Date unknown:** *al-Muwashshaḥāt al-ilāhiyya wa-l-azjāl* (RG 517). A securely authenticated collection of strophic poems and *azjāl*; it is a poetic form-object, not a prose book.
- **Date unknown:** *Tāj al-tarājim* (RG 737). A securely authenticated literary work whose “translations” or interpretive portraits form a crown-like collection.
- **Date unknown:** *Takhmīs qaṣīdat Abī Madyan* (RG 748). A probable poetic expansion of a poem by Abū Madyan, adding lines to another poet’s composition; it is collaborative across generations and resists simple treatment as wholly original.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Jalāl wa-l-jamāl* (RG 168). A securely authenticated, rhetorically patterned meditation on majesty, beauty, and the perfection reconciling them.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb Kashf al-maʿnā* (RG 338). A securely attributed literary-theoretical or contemplative “unveiling of meaning,” known through textual evidence.

### Biographical, affiliative, and self-mapping obras

- **1203:** *Rūḥ al-quds fī munāṣaḥat al-nafs* (RG 639). An epistolary obra combining spiritual complaint, autobiographical testimony, and sketches of roughly fifty-five western Sufis.
- **Probably 1236:** *Risālat al-Khirqa*, also *Nasab al-khirqa* (RG 530). A record of Ibn ʿArabī’s initiatic affiliations and of mantles he conferred on others; its date is uncertain.
- **1229–1230:** *Fihris muʾallafāt al-shaykh al-akbar* (RG 142). Ibn ʿArabī’s self-bibliography listing 248 works then completed: simultaneously a catalogue, an assertion of authorship, and a map of a still-unfinished life-project.
- **1231–1232:** *Ijāzat al-shaykh al-akbar* (RG 271). An authorially authenticated certificate of transmission; it is an operative document that creates permission and lineage rather than merely describing them.
- **1234:** *Ijāza li-l-Malik al-Muẓaffar* (RG 269). A certificate for the Ayyubid ruler al-Malik al-Muẓaffar listing about 290 works and seventy teachers; textual evidence establishes authorship, but the surviving witnesses are later.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Mabādiʾ wa-l-ghāyāt* (RG 380). Besides its doctrinal content, this securely authenticated work is important as an authorially signed witness; it should not be confused with the homonymous text rejected by the archive project.

### Answers, counsels, and letters that act

- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Ajwiba* (RG 9). A securely attributed book of answers; its authorship is established by internal evidence, while a second work catalogued under the same title (RG 10) remains unverified.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Jawāb al-mustaqīm ʿammā saʾala* (RG 177). A securely authenticated direct response to questions posed to Ibn ʿArabī.
- **Date unknown:** *Jawāb suʾāl Ibn Sawdakīn* (RG 182). A securely authenticated answer to a question from Ibn Sawdakīn; its dialogical occasion is constitutive of the work.
- **Date unknown:** *Risālat al-Kutub* (RG 354). A securely attributed epistle concerning books or writings.
- **Date unknown:** *Risālat al-Intiṣār* (RG 294). A securely attributed epistle of defense or vindication.
- **Date unknown:** *Risālat al-Maʿlūm min ʿaqāʾid ʿulamāʾ al-rusūm* (RG 402). A securely authenticated doctrinal epistle concerning the known beliefs of formal scholars.
- **Date unknown:** *Risālat al-Shaykh al-ʿArabī ilā Abī ʿAlī al-Ghazzāl* (RG 611). A securely attributed letter to Abū ʿAlī al-Ghazzāl.
- **Date unknown:** *Risālat al-Shaykh ilā al-Imām al-Rāzī* (RG 612). A securely attributed letter challenging Fakhr al-Dīn al-Rāzī to seek realized rather than exclusively rational knowledge.
- **Date unknown:** *Jawāban wa-naṣīḥan li-l-Sulṭān ʿIzz al-Dīn Kaykāʾūs* (RG 847). A securely attributed answer and admonition to the Seljuk sultan: a counsel text intended to intervene in rule, and therefore close to a manifesto.
- **Date unknown:** *Risāla fī al-waʿẓ li-baʿḍ aḥbābihi* (RG 826). A securely authenticated admonitory epistle addressed to one or more companions.

### Works whose attribution remains unresolved

- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Adhkār* (RG 6). A devotional book attributed to Ibn ʿArabī, but the archive project classifies it as unverified because the manuscript evidence is insufficient.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Ajwiba* (RG 10). A second “Book of Answers,” distinct from verified RG 9; its authorship remains unverified.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Asrār al-thalātha* (RG 51). A “Book of the Three Mysteries” whose attribution remains unverified.
- **Date unknown:** *Daʿwat al-asmāʾ al-ḥusnā* (RG 98). An invocation of the divine names preserved only in evidence inadequate to establish authorship.
- **Date unknown:** *Duʿāʾ al-ism al-aʿẓam* (RG 109). A prayer of the Greatest Name attributed to Ibn ʿArabī, but known from late, insufficient evidence.
- **Date unknown:** *Duʿāʾ ʿaẓīm mubārak* (RG 112). A performative prayer whose attribution is unverified.
- **Date unknown:** *Duʿāʾ sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ* (RG 113). A prayer associated with the Sura of Sincerity; attribution unverified.
- **Date unknown:** *al-Futūḥāt al-Madaniyya* (RG 152). A work transmitted under this title but not securely attributable; it must not be silently treated as a second authenticated counterpart to the *Meccan Openings*.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Ḥikam* (RG 233). A book of wisdoms attributed to Ibn ʿArabī, but its authorship remains unverified.
- **Date unknown:** *Māhiyyat al-qalb*, also *Kitāb al-Ḥulal* (RG 249). A treatise on the heart whose authorship cannot presently be decided.
- **Date unknown:** *Risālat al-Khawf wa-l-rajāʾ* (RG 259). An epistle on fear and hope with inadequate evidence for attribution.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Iʿlām fī mā buniya ʿalayhi al-Islām* (RG 282). A book on Islam’s foundations whose attribution remains unverified.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Insān al-kullī* (RG 293). A work on the universal human being, preserved under Ibn ʿArabī’s name but not authenticated.
- **Date unknown:** *Risālat al-Kalām ʿalā ḥurūf al-muʿjam wa-maʿānīhā* (RG 323). A letter-science treatise of unresolved authorship.
- **Date unknown:** *Kalimāt fī faḍāʾil al-futuwwa* (RG 325). Sayings on the virtues of spiritual chivalry whose attribution remains unverified.
- **Date unknown:** *Kashf al-ḍamīr min aḥruf al-taksīr* (RG 333). A letter-science work whose late manuscript evidence does not settle authorship.
- **Date unknown:** *Risāla fī al-maḥabba* (RG 391). An epistle on love of unresolved attribution.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Malḥama* (RG 400). A work transmitted as an apocalyptic or conflict text; authorship is unverified.
- **Date unknown:** *Risāla fī maʿrifat al-nafs wa-l-rūḥ* (RG 427). An epistle on knowing the soul and spirit whose attribution remains unresolved.
- **Date unknown:** *Kitāb al-Muwāzanāt* (RG 518). A book of balances or comparisons known only from insufficient evidence.
- **Date unknown:** *Qaṣāʾid* (RG 564.1). Poems attributed collectively to Ibn ʿArabī without adequate evidence to define or authenticate the corpus.
- **Date unknown:** *al-Qaṣīda al-ʿishqiyya* (RG 565.2). A love poem under his name, but preserved too late to authenticate confidently.
- **Date unknown:** *al-Tāʾiyya* (RG 757). A poem rhyming in *tāʾ* whose attribution remains unverified.

### Things wrongly made into “works by Ibn ʿArabī”

- **Not applicable:** *Risālat al-Aḥadiyya* (RG 13). Frequently printed under Ibn ʿArabī’s name, but the archive project rejects the attribution; it therefore resists inclusion as his obra.
- **Not applicable:** *Shajarat al-kawn* (RG 666). A widely reprinted cosmological treatise actually attributable to Ghānim al-Maqdisī, not Ibn ʿArabī.
- **Not applicable:** *Khalʿ al-naʿlayn fī al-uṣūl ilā ḥaḍrat al-jamʿayn* (RG 253.1). The underlying work is by Ibn Qasī; Ibn ʿArabī made its commentary, not this source text.
- **Not applicable:** *Hayākil al-nūr* (RG 204). A work wrongly placed under Ibn ʿArabī’s name.
- **Not applicable:** *Kitāb al-Fatq wa-l-ratq* (RG 815). A work rejected by the manuscript archive as not by Ibn ʿArabī.
- **Not applicable:** *al-Shajara al-nuʿmāniyya* (RG 665). An attributed prognostic work classified as not by Ibn ʿArabī.
- **Not applicable:** *Sharḥ al-Jaljalūtiyya* (RG 676). A commentary transmitted under his name but rejected as his work.
- **Not applicable:** *Kitāb al-Sulūk fī ṭarīq al-qawm* (RG 661). An instructional text classified as not by Ibn ʿArabī.
- **Not applicable:** *Kitāb al-Waʿẓ bi-l-aḥādīth al-nabawiyya* (RG 825). A work of prophetic-hadith exhortation rejected as Ibn ʿArabī’s.
- **Not applicable:** Numerous separately titled “works” in Osman Yahia’s bibliography are extracts from *al-Futūḥāt al-Makkiyya* or duplicates of other texts. They resist counting as independent obras because later transmission manufactured separateness from portions or alternate titles.
