## Works by Eva Duarte de Perón

### Books and disputed book-objects

- **1951:** *La razón de mi vida*. A political autobiography and manifesto issued under Eva Perón’s name; Manuel Penella de Silva prepared the text from conversations with her, and Raúl Mendé revised it, so its authorship is collaborative and contested rather than straightforwardly literary. ([Museo Evita](https://museoevita.org.ar/a-75-anos-de-la-publicacion-de-la-razon-de-mi-vida/); [Grinberg Pla](https://istmo.denison.edu/n10/articulos/relaciones.html))
- **1952; first published 1987:** *Mi mensaje*. An unfinished or last-stage political testament reportedly dictated during her final illness; her sisters challenged it as apocryphal, although litigation ending in 2006 recognized the surviving text as authentic. Its status remains unusually unstable: testament, manuscript, posthumous book, and manifesto. ([Mi mensaje](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mi_mensaje))

### Lectures: *Historia del peronismo*

- **15 March 1951:** *Primera clase: Concepción peronista de la historia*. The first of nine oral lessons delivered at the Escuela Superior Peronista; the printed text is a transcript of a pedagogical and political performance, not a book composed as such. ([CEDINPE](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-eva-clases-y-escritos-completos-1946-1952))
- **29 March 1951:** *Segunda clase: La historia y los grandes hombres*. A lecture-manifesto treating political leadership as the motor of history. ([CEDINPE](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-eva-clases-y-escritos-completos-1946-1952))
- **5 April 1951:** *Tercera clase: Los pueblos en la historia*. An orally made lesson preserved through transcription. ([CEDINPE](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-eva/clases-y-escritos-completos-1946-1952))
- **12 April 1951:** *Cuarta clase: Los pueblos en la historia y el espíritu de la oligarquía*. A doctrinal lecture whose action is formative as much as descriptive. ([CEDINPE](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-eva-clases-y-escritos-completos-1946-1952))
- **19 April 1951:** *Quinta clase: El capitalismo en la historia*. A spoken lesson in the Escuela’s ideological curriculum. ([CEDINPE](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-eva-clases-y-escritos-completos-1946-1952))
- **10 May 1951:** *Sexta clase: Las causas del justicialismo*. A doctrinal lecture; the collected edition gives an exact date but contains a typographical error in the month line. ([CEDINPE](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-eva-clases-y-escritos-completos-1946-1952))
- **1951, exact date not supplied:** *Séptima clase: La historia del 17 de octubre*. A lecture converting a political event into a foundational narrative. ([CEDINPE](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-eva-clases-y-escritos-completos-1946-1952))
- **1951, exact date not supplied:** *Octava clase: Los actores del peronismo. Recapitulación*. A pedagogical synthesis and protocol for understanding the movement’s constituent actors. ([CEDINPE](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-eva-clases-y-escritos-completos-1946-1952))
- **1951, exact date not supplied:** *Novena clase: El peronismo ante el mundo*. The final recorded lesson in the cycle, simultaneously exposition and international political manifesto. ([CEDINPE](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-eva-clases-y-escritos-completos-1946-1952))

### Newspaper writings

- **1946–1952, individual date not established:** *Por qué soy peronista*. A political newspaper article collected among Eva Perón’s writings for *Democracia*. ([CEDINPE](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-eva-clases-y-escritos-completos-1946-1952))
- **1946–1952, individual date not established:** *Ayuda social, sí, limosna, no*. A manifesto-like article distinguishing social justice from charity. ([CEDINPE](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-eva-clases-y-escritos-completos-1946-1952))
- **1946–1952, individual date not established:** *Significación social del “Descamisado”*. A political article constructing the “descamisado” as a social subject. ([CEDINPE](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-eva-clases-y-escritos-completos-1946-1952))
- **1946–1952, individual date not established:** *Olvidar a los niños es renunciar al porvenir*. A programmatic article on children and social responsibility. ([CEDINPE](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-eva-clases-y-escritos-completos-1946-1952))
- **1946–1952, individual date not established:** *El deber actual de la mujer argentina*. A political exhortation addressed to Argentine women and therefore closer to manifesto than neutral essay. ([CEDINPE](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-eva-clases-y-escritos-completos-1946-1952))
- **1946–1952, individual date not established:** *La justicia social se consolida con una mayor producción*. A programmatic newspaper intervention joining social justice to economic production. ([CEDINPE](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-eva-clases-y-escritos-completos-1946-1952))
- **1946–1952, individual date not established:** *“Después que uno está perdido, no lo salvan ni los santos”*. A titled newspaper piece included in the collected writings; its precise date is not supplied by the bibliography consulted. ([CEDINPE](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-eva-clases-y-escritos-completos-1946-1952))
- **1946–1952, individual date not established:** *Hacia la total emancipación de los “Descamisados” del campo*. A manifesto-like intervention on rural workers. ([CEDINPE](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-eva-clases-y-escritos-completos-1946-1952))
- **1946–1952, individual date not established:** *Mis conversaciones con el General Perón*. A newspaper text fashioned as reported political conversation; its title should not be mistaken for a separately published memoir. ([CEDINPE](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-eva-clases-y-escritos-completos-1946-1952))
- **1946–1952, individual date not established:** *Mensaje de gratitud a los “Descamisados”*. A public message whose function is performative acknowledgment. ([CEDINPE](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-eva-clases-y-escritos-completos-1946-1952))
- **1946–1952, individual date not established:** *Ante la proximidad del Día de la Raza*. A topical newspaper article. ([CEDINPE](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-eva-clases-y-escritos-completos-1946-1952))
- **1946–1952, individual date not established:** *Significado nacional del 17 de octubre*. A commemorative political interpretation of Peronism’s foundational date. ([CEDINPE](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-eva-clases-y-escritos-completos-1946-1952))
- **1946–1952, individual date not established:** *Los rezagados del despertar nacional*. A polemical newspaper intervention. ([CEDINPE](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-eva-clases-y-escritos-completos-1946-1952))
- **1946–1952, individual date not established:** *La mujer argentina apoya la reforma*. A political declaration acting to mobilize women’s support. ([CEDINPE](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-eva-clases-y-escritos-completos-1946-1952))
- **1951:** *Ante la tercera batalla electoral*. An electoral intervention; its exact publication day is not established in the consulted collected-work index. ([CEDINPE](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-eva-clases-y-escritos-completos-1946-1952))
- **1951:** *El pueblo quiere soluciones argentinas para los problemas argentinos*. A campaign-period political manifesto rather than a descriptive essay. ([CEDINPE](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-eva-clases-y-escritos-completos-1946-1952))
- **1951:** *Decide el 11 de noviembre*. An electoral call to action whose title refers to the 1951 general election. ([CEDINPE](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-eva-clases-y-escritos-completos-1946-1952))

### Unpublished writings

- **Date not established:** *El mundo no puede ser insensible a la suerte de los ancianos*. A previously unpublished social-policy text preserved in the collected writings; without fuller manuscript evidence, its original occasion and degree of authorial revision remain uncertain. ([CEDINPE](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-eva-clases-y-escritos-completos-1946-1952))
- **Date not established:** *Emoción cristiana y justicia social*. An unpublished political-religious text named in the collected edition; its manuscript status and exact date are not specified there. ([CEDINPE](https://cedinpe.unsam.edu.ar/content/per%C3%B3n-eva-clases-y-escritos-completos-1946-1952))

### Speeches and radio addresses

- **1946–1952:** *Discursos*. A large, dispersed body of spoken political acts rather than one fixed text: broadcasts, ceremonial addresses, union speeches, campaign interventions, and messages survive as recordings, press versions, scripts, and later transcriptions that need not be textually identical. Megafón’s *Discursos completos* covers 1946–48, while the Biblioteca del Congreso/Instituto Eva Perón edition explicitly presents only a selection, so no consulted edition warrants a claim of absolute completeness. ([Google Books](https://books.google.com/books/about/Discursos_completos_1946_1948.html?id=0krjAAAAMAAJ); [Biblioteca del Congreso](https://bcn.gob.ar/uploads/Obra-completaEva-Peron.pdf))

### Stage performances

- **28 March 1935:** *La señora de los Pérez*. Eva Duarte’s professional stage debut, in a small role; the obra is the production/performance she helped manifest, not a play she authored. ([Museo Evita](https://museoevita.org.ar/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/Propuesta-didactica-Museo-Evita.pdf))
- **19 June or July 1935:** *Cada casa es un mundo*. A stage performance in a play by Goycoechea and Cordone; secondary chronologies disagree on whether the opening fell in June or July, so the month remains disputed. ([Historia del Peronismo](https://historiadelperonismo.com/?p=3292); [La Voz de los Barrios](https://lavozdelosbarrios.com/la-vida-artistica-de-eva-maria-duarte/))
- **26 November 1935:** *Madame Sans-Gêne*. A stage manifestation in which Duarte reportedly played two small parts; the underlying play belongs to Victorien Sardou and Émile Moreau. ([La Voz de los Barrios](https://lavozdelosbarrios.com/la-vida-artistica-de-eva-maria-duarte/))
- **2 January 1936:** *La dama, el caballero y el ladrón*. A stage performance, reportedly as a typist, rather than an authored work. ([La Voz de los Barrios](https://lavozdelosbarrios.com/la-vida-artistica-de-eva-maria-duarte/))
- **1936:** *Doña María del Buen Aire*. A touring stage production in which Duarte appeared; surviving notices establish participation but not a stable, creator-level authorship. ([Biographical chronology](https://espaciodehorbebii.blogspot.com/2016/05/maria-eva-duarte-de-peron.html))
- **1938:** *El cura de Santa Clara*. A theatrical performance with Pascual Pellicciotta; the date and title survive in career chronologies, but the precise role is not securely identified. ([Biographical chronology](https://www.buscabiografias.com/biografia/verDetalle/6026/Maria%2520Eva%2520Duarte%2520de%2520Peron))
- **1939:** *Mercado de amor en Argelia*. A stage performance with Camila Quiroga’s company, reportedly as an odalisque; sources also shorten the title to *Mercado de Argelia*. ([Biographical chronology](https://www.buscabiografias.com/biografia/verDetalle/6026/Maria%2520Eva%2520Duarte%2520de%2520Peron))
- **1940:** *Llegaron parientes de España*. A stage performance with the Simari company. ([Historia del Peronismo](https://historiadelperonismo.com/?p=3292))
- **1940:** *Corazón de manteca*. A stage performance at the Teatro Smart. ([Historia del Peronismo](https://historiadelperonismo.com/?p=3292))
- **16 August 1940:** *La plata hay que repartirla*. A stage performance in Antonio Botta’s comedy and one of Duarte’s last theatrical appearances. ([Historia del Peronismo](https://historiadelperonismo.com/?p=3292))

### Radio manifestations and companies

- **1937:** *Oro blanco*. Duarte’s first major radiodrama, concerning cotton workers in Chaco; she performed rather than wrote it. ([Eva Perón biography](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eva_Per%C3%B3n))
- **1939:** *Los jazmines del ochenta*. A serial radiodrama by Héctor Pedro Blomberg, performed by Duarte’s Teatro del Aire ensemble; it is a collective broadcast manifestation, not her authored text. ([UC dissertation](https://escholarship.org/content/qt50m9x4f4/qt50m9x4f4_noSplash_ff7583b71088edb6a4f071ef4c3cf038.pdf?t=nrzlmt))
- **1939:** *Las rosas de Caseros*. A Blomberg radiodrama performed by Duarte on Radio Prieto; the performance is hers, the script is not. ([Historia del Peronismo](https://historiadelperonismo.com/?p=3292))
- **1939:** *La estrella del pirata*. A further Blomberg radiodrama performed by Duarte; the surviving career chronology does not establish an exact opening date. ([Historia del Peronismo](https://historiadelperonismo.com/?p=3292))
- **1941:** *Los amores de Schubert*. A successful radio production featuring Duarte; it resists attribution as “her work” except in the specific sense of an acting performance. ([Eva Perón biography](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eva_Per%C3%B3n))
- **1941–1942:** *La hora de las sorpresas*. A sponsored radio cycle featuring Duarte; it is a broadcast framework rather than a single authored text. ([Career study](https://rebelion.org/docs/172986.pdf))
- **1942:** *Compañía Juvenil de Radioteatro*. A performing company organized by Duarte and later joined with Compañía Candilejas; this is an institutional and repertorial project, not one discrete text. ([Biographical chronology](https://www.buscabiografias.com/biografia/verDetalle/6026/Maria%2520Eva%2520Duarte%2520de%2520Peron))
- **1943–1945:** *Biografías de mujeres ilustres* / *Grandes mujeres de todos los tiempos*. A Radio Belgrano dramatic-biographical series starring Duarte as historical women; titles vary in the sources, and the scripts were written by others, making the cycle a performed corpus rather than her literary work. ([Noemí Castiñeiras/Museo Evita](https://museoevita.org.ar/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/TO-BE-EVITA-2003_2007.pdf))

### Film performances

- **1937:** *¡Segundos afuera!*. Duarte’s first film appearance, a small acting contribution; some credits rendered her surname as “Durante.” ([Filmography](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eva_Per%C3%B3n))
- **1940:** *La carga de los valientes*. A supporting film performance directed by Adelqui Millar; production documentation survives in the Museo Evita collections. ([Museo Evita archive](https://colecciones.museoevita.org.ar/index.php/radioteatro))
- **1941:** *El más infeliz del pueblo*. A supporting film performance directed by Luis Bayón Herrera. ([La Nueva](https://www.lanueva.com/nota/2005-6-5-9-0-0--la-prodiga-el-unico-protagonico-de-eva-duarte))
- **1942:** *Una novia en apuros*. A supporting film performance directed by John Reinhardt. ([La Nueva](https://www.lanueva.com/nota/2005-6-5-9-0-0--la-prodiga-el-unico-protagonico-de-eva-duarte))
- **1945:** *La cabalgata del circo*. A film performance as Chila Ruca, directed by Mario Soffici and Eduardo Boneo; it is a collaborative cinematic manifestation rather than a work authored by Duarte. ([Cine Nacional](https://cinenacional.com/pelicula/la-cabalgata-del-circo))
- **1945; released 1984:** *La pródiga*. Duarte’s sole leading film role, as Julia; made in 1945 but suppressed from contemporary release and first shown nearly four decades later, it has two materially different dates that should not be collapsed. ([La Nueva](https://www.lanueva.com/nota/2005-6-5-9-0-0--la-prodiga-el-unico-protagonico-de-eva-duarte); [Museo Evita archive](https://colecciones.museoevita.org.ar/index.php/radioteatro))
- **Planned 1945; unrealized:** *Amanece en las ruinas*. A proposed starring film with Mario Soffici that was never made; as an abandoned project it belongs to her career history but not to her completed filmography. ([La Nueva](https://www.lanueva.com/nota/2005-6-5-9-0-0--la-prodiga-el-unico-protagonico-de-eva-duarte))

### Political and social projects

- **1943:** *Asociación Radial Argentina*. A performers’ union co-founded by Duarte, who became its president in 1944; it is a collective institutional creation, not hers alone. ([Museo Evita](https://museoevita.org.ar/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/Propuesta-didactica-Museo-Evita.pdf))
- **1948–1952:** *Fundación Eva Perón*. A vast social and material project—administration, hospitals, schools, homes, nursing education, food provision, and direct aid—created and directed by Eva Perón but physically realized by a large institution. It approaches a social *Gesamtkunstwerk*: buildings, objects, rituals, publicity, policy, and care cannot usefully be separated into one “text.” ([Argentina.gob.ar](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/node/244619))
- **1949–1952:** *Partido Peronista Femenino*. A women’s political organization founded and structured under Eva Perón’s leadership; this is an organizational obra and mobilizing protocol rather than a written work. Scholarly accounts describe it as autonomous in organization while also one of Peronism’s three constituent branches. ([Carolina Barry](https://www.cepc.gob.es/publicaciones/revistas/historia-y-politica/numero-26-juliodiciembre-2011/eva-peron-y-las-primeras-dirigentes-del-peronismo-1949-1955-1); [Buenos Aires Ministry of Women](https://ministeriodelasmujeres.gba.gob.ar/gestor/uploads/CUADERNILLO%20DE%20PARTICIPACION%20POLITICA.pdf))
