## Works by Arnold Rikli

### Therapeutic system and environments

- **1846–1857/58:** *Türkischrot-Garnfärberei in Seebach*. An industrial enterprise founded with his brother Johann Rudolf, rather than an individually authored work; it nevertheless belongs to Rikli’s made environment and became the site of his first hydropathic experiments. The enterprise was never profitable and was liquidated in 1857/58. ([NDB-online](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd1016911963.html#dbocontent))

- **By 1854:** *Bettdampf-Apparat*. A bed-steam apparatus that Rikli designed and built himself while still working at Seebach; it is an apparatus rather than a text, and the surviving scholarship supplies no more precise date or technical specification. ([Levental, pp. 395, 398](https://brill.com/view/journals/ges/34/3-4/article-p394_10.pdf))

- **1855–1906:** *Naturheilanstalt Mallnerbrunn, Veldes*. A therapeutic institution and evolving built environment founded and directed by Rikli at present-day Bled. Its first purpose-built wooden bathhouse dates from 1859; it later comprised two bathhouses, pools, showers, steam baths, more than fifty open sleeping huts, and outdoor exercise or “air” parks, so it resists classification as a single building. ([NDB-online](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd1016911963.html#dbocontent); [Levental, pp. 395, 398–399](https://brill.com/view/journals/ges/34/3-4/article-p394_10.pdf))

- **From the 1850s, progressively developed through the 1860s:** *Atmosphärische Cur*. Rikli’s principal project and protocol: an integrated regimen of hydrotherapy, alternating thermal and atmospheric stimuli, light and sun baths, barefoot movement and exercise, outdoor sleeping, work or play, and moderate vegetarian diet. It is not one text but a bodily, architectural, dietary, and instructional Gesamtkunstwerk, repeatedly revised in practice and publication. ([NDB-online](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd1016911963.html#dbocontent); [Levental, pp. 398–399](https://brill.com/view/journals/ges/34/3-4/article-p394_10.pdf); [Zupanič-Slavec and Toplak](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9684537/))

### Books, manuals, protocols, and manifestos

- **1857:** *Aufruf an die kranke Menschheit an Körper und Geist, nach den Gesetzen der Natur-Heillehre zu genesen, oder leichtfaßliche Darstellung der Natur, resp. Wasserheilkunde, nach ihrem heutigen Standpunkt*. A popular exposition of natural and water healing whose address to “sick humanity” gives it the character of both instructional treatise and manifesto. One later bibliography shortens its opening word to *Anruf*, but NDB-online records *Aufruf*. ([NDB-online](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd1016911963.html#dbocontent); [Zupanič-Slavec and Toplak, bibliography](https://brill.com/downloadpdf/journals/ges/55/1-2/article-p58_5.pdf))

- **1862:** *Wegweiser zu den Umgebungen des Kurortes Veldes in Oberkrain*. A guidebook to the surroundings of Rikli’s resort: simultaneously topographical orientation, resort literature, and an extension of his therapeutic landscape. ([NDB-online](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd1016911963.html#dbocontent); [Zupanič-Slavec and Toplak, bibliography](https://brill.com/downloadpdf/journals/ges/55/1-2/article-p58_5.pdf))

- **1869:** *Die Thermodiätetik. Oder das tägliche thermoelectrische Licht- und Luftbad …*. A programmatic medical-hygienic treatise in which Rikli distinguished his own system from conventional hydrotherapy; the scholarly work-list truncates the long subtitle, so it should not be silently reconstructed. ([NDB-online](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd1016911963.html#dbocontent))

- **By 1871:** *Allgemeine Cur-Regeln, speziell angepasst den Curordinationsbüchern der Wasserheil-Anstalten Mallnerbrunn am Veldeser See in Oberkrain und in Via Guardiella in Triest*. A clinical protocol keyed to the order books of Rikli’s establishments. A fifth edition is documented in 1871, proving earlier manifestations but not securely dating the first; the work subsequently expanded and changed title into *Die Grundlehren der Naturheilkunde*. ([contemporary bibliographic record](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9783111618319_A40081430/preview-9783111618319_A40081430.pdf); [Trieste medical bibliography](https://www.openstarts.units.it/server/api/core/bitstreams/57d05dfc-9e6a-4c95-bec2-553876be9538/content))

- **By 1872:** *Rikli’s Bett- und Theildampfbäder*. An illustrated technical and therapeutic manual for the steam apparatus and procedures Rikli had developed; the third revised edition appeared in 1872, the fourth in 1889, and the fifth in 1900, while the first edition’s date is not established by the consulted scholarly bibliography. Later records also modernize *Theil-* as *Partial-*, an edition-level variation rather than a separate work. ([dLib.si digitized-edition record](https://www.dlib.si/results/?faccess=public&fpublisher=T.+Grieben&pageSize=25&query=%27rel%3Dknjige%40OR%40rel%3Dvisoko%C5%A1olska+dela%27&sort=date&sortDir=ASC); [Levental, p. 401](https://brill.com/view/journals/ges/34/3-4/article-p394_10.pdf))

- **1872:** *Süddeutsches Vegetarianer-Kochbuch mit Speisekarte für das ganze Jahr*. A vegetarian cookbook and year-long menu protocol: a practical dietary component of Rikli’s larger therapeutic project, not merely a medical treatise. ([NDB-online](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd1016911963.html#dbocontent))

- **1877:** *Das Lichtluftbad, auf zwölfjährige diätetische und therapeutische Erfahrung gegründet*. A focused protocol and evidentiary claim for the light-and-air bath, presented as the result of twelve years of practice. Its precise textual relationship to *Die Thermodiätetik* and the later *Atmosphärische Cur* is not resolved by the consulted bibliographies, so it is retained as a separately titled manifestation. ([Trieste medical bibliography](https://www.openstarts.units.it/server/api/core/bitstreams/57d05dfc-9e6a-4c95-bec2-553876be9538/content))

- **1886:** *Cur- und Hausordnung der Naturheilanstalt Mallnerbrunn am Veldeser See in Oberkrain*. An institutional rulebook and treatment protocol: a text meant to organize conduct, bodies, schedules, and space inside the sanatorium. A digitized record gives the variant title *Kur- und Hausordnung der Naturheilanstalt des Arnold Rikli in Veldes (Krain), Österreich*. ([NDB-online](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd1016911963.html#dbocontent); [dLib.si](https://www.dlib.si/results/?pageSize=25&query=%27contributor%3DRikli%2C+Arnold%27))

- **1890:** *Die Grundlehren der Naturheilkunde (früher „Allgemeine Curregeln“ betitelt), mit besonderer Berücksichtigung der atmosphärischen Cur*. A substantially retitled and revised continuation of the *Allgemeine Cur-Regeln*, combining doctrine with treatment instructions. Sources disagree over whether to date the underlying obra to 1875, the retitled manifestation to 1890, or a later edition to 1894/95; the ninth edition appeared in 1911, after Rikli’s death, so edition dates must not be mistaken for new works. ([NDB-online](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd1016911963.html#dbocontent); [Historisches Lexikon der Schweiz](https://hls-dhs-dss.ch/de/articles/046147/2024-11-11/); [dLib.si](https://www.dlib.si/details/URN%3ANBN%3ASI%3ADOC-3BDJFNJ4))

- **1894:** *„Es werde Licht“ und Es wird Licht! oder Die atmosphärische Cur: ein Beitrag zur natürlichen Gesundheitslehre*. A polemical exposition and manifesto for the atmospheric cure. The same fourth edition is also catalogued under its extended alternative title *Die atmosphärische Cur oder das Lichtluftbad und das Sonnenbad und die Sonne der schärfste Diagnostiker und Prognostiker*; these are manifestations of one evolving text, not two securely distinct works. ([NDB-online](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd1016911963.html#dbocontent); [Schroeder, p. 40 n. 42](https://knowledge.uchicago.edu/record/2693/files/Schroeder_uchicago_0330D_15515.pdf); [Zupanič-Slavec and Toplak, bibliography](https://brill.com/downloadpdf/journals/ges/55/1-2/article-p58_5.pdf))

- **Undated:** *Die diätetische Katarrhalisirung*. A therapeutic tract described by the scholarly biography as published at Leipzig without a date. Its title denotes a treatment concept or procedure; no invented publication year is supplied here. ([Levental, p. 401](https://brill.com/view/journals/ges/34/3-4/article-p394_10.pdf))

- **By 1900:** *Die Fieberkrankheiten: Volksthümliches Lehrbuch mit besonderer Berücksichtigung der Blattern*. A popular instructional and polemical work on fever and smallpox; only its second edition, Leipzig 1900, is securely dated in the consulted sources. Austrian authorities prohibited the book in 1900, and its rejection of contagion theory and vaccination made it an intervention in public conduct as much as a descriptive text. ([Levental, pp. 394, 401](https://brill.com/view/journals/ges/34/3-4/article-p394_10.pdf); [contemporary publication notice](https://dlibra.bibliotekaelblaska.pl/Content/67896/PDF%20czarno-bia%C5%82y/1900-34czb.pdf))

- **1903:** *Abschiedsworte an meine verehrten Kollegen und Gesinnungsgenossen*. A self-published valedictory address, simultaneously retrospective credo and manifesto directed to fellow natural healers. ([Levental, p. 401](https://brill.com/view/journals/ges/34/3-4/article-p394_10.pdf))

- **By 1903:** *Prospekt der A. Rikli’schen Anstalt, Veldes*. An illustrated or promotional prospectus for the institution, turning its buildings, landscape, regimen, and founder into a public-facing composite object. The consulted sources attest a 1903 prospectus and also cite an undated *Prospekt der Naturheilanstalt Mallnerbrunn bei Veldes in Oberkrain*; whether these are separate works or successive versions is unresolved. ([Levental, p. 401](https://brill.com/view/journals/ges/34/3-4/article-p394_10.pdf); [Zupanič-Slavec and Toplak, bibliography](https://brill.com/downloadpdf/journals/ges/55/1-2/article-p58_5.pdf))

### Manuscripts and dispersed writings

- **1856–1863:** *Tagebuch-Notizen über seine frühverstorbenen Kinder*. Manuscript diary notes about Rikli’s children who died young, preserved in the Rikli family archive. This private record resists easy classification as a finished “work”: the scholarly source identifies notes, not a prepared publication. ([Levental, p. 401](https://brill.com/view/journals/ges/34/3-4/article-p394_10.pdf))

- **December 1867:** *Selbstbiographie*. An unpublished autobiographical manuscript written for the family chronicle and ending with the year 1852. It survives through the family archive and a later typescript with accompanying extracts and commentary by Wilhelm Rikli; therefore the surviving form is partly a transmitted archival object rather than an authorially fixed edition. ([Levental, p. 401](https://brill.com/view/journals/ges/34/3-4/article-p394_10.pdf))

- **Dates not comprehensively established:** *Articles in Naturarzt*. A dispersed body of periodical writing rather than a single titled obra. Levental reports that “a larger number” of Rikli’s articles appeared in the German journal *Naturarzt* but does not enumerate them; without the complete bibliography he cites elsewhere, forcing individual titles or claiming completeness would turn periodical manifestations into invented works. ([Levental, p. 401](https://brill.com/view/journals/ges/34/3-4/article-p394_10.pdf))

### Translated and posthumous manifestations

- **1905:** *Médecine naturelle et bains de soleil*. A French-language manifestation of Rikli’s natural-healing doctrine, associated by the *Historisches Lexikon der Schweiz* with the German work first issued under the *Allgemeine Curregeln/Grundlehren* title sequence. It is listed here as a translation or adaptation, not counted as an independently conceived obra. ([Historisches Lexikon der Schweiz](https://hls-dhs-dss.ch/fr/articles/046147/2024-11-11/); [Levental, p. 401](https://brill.com/view/journals/ges/34/3-4/article-p394_10.pdf))

- **1909, posthumous:** *Opis sončnega zdravilišča Arnolda Riklija na Bledu*. A Slovenian description of Arnold Rikli’s sun sanatorium published after his death. The consulted bibliography attributes it to “A. Rikli,” but does not establish whether this is Rikli’s translated text, an institutional publication issued under his name, or another family member’s work; its authorship therefore remains disputed. ([Zupanič-Slavec and Toplak, bibliography](https://brill.com/downloadpdf/journals/ges/55/1-2/article-p58_5.pdf))
