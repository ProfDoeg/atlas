## Works by Maria Orsic

### Documentary status

No catalogue raisonné, collected-works edition, or scholarly bibliography exists for Maria Orsic (also Oršić or Orsitsch). The standard scholarly literature treats her as a figure in a postwar mythology first documented in Norbert Jürgen-Ratthofer and Ralf Ettl’s 1992 *Das Vril-Projekt*, not as a historically verified person active from 1895 to 1945. Consequently, no work can presently be authenticated as hers; the items below are alleged manifestations within that mythology, not surviving historical works.

### Alleged mediumistic manifestations

- **December 1919 (alleged):** [Untitled transcripts of telepathic communications from Aldebaran]. These are said to have been written in an “old Templar” script and subsequently identified as Sumerian. They resist classification as authored texts because the story presents Orsic as their medium rather than their author; no manuscript, edition, provenance, or contemporary reference has been authenticated, and Nicholas Goodrick-Clarke explicitly locates the account within Jürgen-Ratthofer and Ettl’s fictional mythology. ([Goodrick-Clarke, pp. 164–66](https://books.google.com/books?id=xaiaM77s6N4C); [Jürgen-Ratthofer and Ettl, *Das Vril-Projekt*](https://es.scribd.com/document/808578559/Das-Vril-Projekt-Teil-1-Aufl-1992-N-J-Ratthofer-Ralf-Ettl))

- **After December 1919 (alleged):** [Untitled instructions for constructing a time-travel or “beyond-flight” machine]. This is described as a received technical protocol rather than an invention independently authored by Orsic; the legend assigns construction of a 1922 prototype to a group led by W. O. Schumann. No original instructions or independently documented machine survive, and the attribution derives from the same 1992 myth-cycle. ([Goodrick-Clarke, pp. 165–66](https://books.google.com/books?id=xaiaM77s6N4C); [Strube, pp. 247–52](https://publikationen.uni-tuebingen.de/xmlui/bitstream/handle/10900/141893/Strube_023.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1))

### Alleged organizational project

- **1919 (alleged):** [The Vril Society’s mediumistic program]. *Das Vril-Projekt* places Orsic within a collective project combining séances, communications with Aldebaran, and the pursuit of spacecraft technology; it does not establish an individually authored obra. Scholarship traces this narrative to the early 1990s and regards its supposed Third Reich history as a postwar construction without contemporary documentary foundations. ([Goodrick-Clarke, pp. 162–67](https://books.google.com/books?id=xaiaM77s6N4C); [Strube, pp. 223–68](https://publikationen.uni-tuebingen.de/xmlui/bitstream/handle/10900/141893/Strube_023.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1))

### Works sometimes misattributed to her

- **1922 and later (alleged):** [Prototype time machine and subsequent Vril flying discs]. These machines are consequences attributed to the alleged communications, not objects the legend says Orsic personally built: the prototype is assigned to Schumann’s group, while later craft are assigned to collective or military development programs. Their existence is itself unsupported, so they cannot responsibly be catalogued as Orsic’s obra. ([Goodrick-Clarke, pp. 165–67](https://books.google.com/books?id=xaiaM77s6N4C))
