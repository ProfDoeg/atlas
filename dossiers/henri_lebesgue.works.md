## Works by Henri Léon Lebesgue

### Research monographs and extended memoirs

- **1898:** *Sur l’approximation des fonctions*. Lebesgue’s first published mathematical paper: a new proof of Weierstrass’s polynomial-approximation theorem. ([University of Auckland](https://www.math.auckland.ac.nz/hat/people/lebesgue.html); [Œuvres scientifiques, vol. III](https://www.unige.ch/math/EnsMath/monographies))
- **1902:** *Intégrale, longueur, aire*. Doctoral thesis and extended journal memoir establishing Lebesgue measure and integration and applying them to length and surface area; the thesis and journal printing are manifestations of the same obra, not separate works. ([BnF/SMF bibliography](https://smf.emath.fr/smf-dossiers-et-ressources/bibliographie-yves-meyer-bnf-2006); [MacTutor](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Lebesgue/))
- **1904; substantially revised 1928:** *Leçons sur l’intégration et la recherche des fonctions primitives*. Research monograph derived from Collège de France lectures; the enlarged second edition is a revision of the same obra rather than an independent work. ([Dictionary of Scientific Biography](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Lebesgue.pdf); [MacTutor](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Lebesgue/))
- **1906:** *Leçons sur les séries trigonométriques professées au Collège de France*. Research monograph developed from lectures, joining measure theory to Fourier and trigonometric-series questions. ([Wikisource scan](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Livre:Lebesgue_-_Le%C3%A7ons_sur_les_s%C3%A9ries_trigonom%C3%A9triques,_1906.djvu); [MacTutor](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Lebesgue/))
- **1909:** *Sur les intégrales singulières*. Extended research memoir introducing and exploiting a modulus of continuity in the study of singular integrals. ([University of Auckland](https://www.math.auckland.ac.nz/hat/people/lebesgue.html))
- **1910:** *Sur l’intégration des fonctions discontinues*. Extended research memoir consolidating and extending Lebesgue integration beyond the compact exposition of his earlier notes and thesis. ([Dictionary of Scientific Biography](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Lebesgue.pdf))
- **1915:** *Sur la mesure des grandeurs*. Published lecture or short monograph on measurement; its form lies between public exposition and mathematical treatise. ([Wikisource author bibliography](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Auteur:Henri-L%C3%A9on_Lebesgue); [Persée authority record](https://www.persee.fr/authority/665873))
- **1922:** *Notice sur les travaux scientifiques de M. Henri Lebesgue, professeur au Collège de France*. Intellectual autobiography and analytical bibliography prepared by Lebesgue himself: simultaneously a text, a catalogue of his pre-1922 obra, and an argument about the unity and importance of that work. It lists nearly ninety books and papers but is not neutral evidence about their significance. ([MacTutor](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Lebesgue/); [Dictionary of Scientific Biography](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Lebesgue.pdf))

### Research articles and notes

- **1899:** *Sur les fonctions de deux variables*. Research note extending a theorem of René Baire to functions of two variables; early bibliographies sometimes identify it by subject rather than consistently reproducing its title. ([MacTutor](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Lebesgue/))
- **1899–1901:** *Notes on developable surfaces, skew polygons, and least-area surface integrals*. A sequence of five Comptes rendus communications rather than one fixed-title obra; the sources describe the sequence by subject, and it should not be silently converted into a single invented title. ([MacTutor](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Lebesgue/); [Encyclopaedia summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henri_Lebesgue))
- **1901:** *Sur une généralisation de l’intégrale définie*. Short research announcement giving the new integral its first published formulation. ([JEHPS bibliography](https://www.jehps.net/decembre2012/BruBruEid.pdf); [Wikisource](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Auteur:Henri-L%C3%A9on_Lebesgue))
- **1903:** *Sur le problème des aires*. Research article on defining the area of non-planar surfaces; the later continuation makes this the first of two distinct papers with the same title. ([Wikisource](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Auteur:Henri-L%C3%A9on_Lebesgue))
- **1903:** *Sur les séries trigonométriques*. Research article on representation by trigonometric series, subsequently absorbed into the larger trajectory culminating in the 1906 monograph. ([Wikisource](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Auteur:Henri-L%C3%A9on_Lebesgue))
- **1904:** *Une propriété caractéristique des fonctions de classe 1*. Research article concerning Baire class-one functions. ([Wikisource](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Auteur:Henri-L%C3%A9on_Lebesgue))
- **1905:** *Sur le problème des aires*. Second research article bearing the same title as the 1903 paper; it is a continuation, not a reprint. ([Henri-Léon Lebesgue bibliography](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henri-L%C3%A9on_Lebesgue))
- **1905:** *À propos de quelques travaux mathématiques récents*. Expository and critical essay on contemporary mathematics, later gathered with Lebesgue’s scientific writings. ([Secondary bibliographical discussion](https://ru.djvu.online/file/AKA29O0UKbMzc))
- **1907:** *Contribution à l’étude des correspondances de M. Zermelo*. Research note engaging Zermelo’s set-theoretic correspondence and the choice problem. ([Henri-Léon Lebesgue bibliography](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henri-L%C3%A9on_Lebesgue))
- **1908:** *Sur la représentation approchée des fonctions*. Research article on approximation, distinct from the similarly titled 1898 paper. ([University of Auckland](https://www.math.auckland.ac.nz/hat/people/lebesgue.html))
- **1908:** *Sur la méthode de M. Goursat pour la résolution de l’équation de Fredholm*. Research note evaluating Goursat’s method for solving the Fredholm equation. ([Henri-Léon Lebesgue bibliography](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henri-L%C3%A9on_Lebesgue))
- **1909:** *Remarques sur un énoncé dû à Stieltjes et concernant les intégrales singulières*. Corrective research note: a text that acts partly as a scholarly intervention by qualifying a proposition attributed to Stieltjes. ([Wikisource](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Auteur:Henri-L%C3%A9on_Lebesgue))
- **1910:** *Sur la représentation trigonométrique approchée des fonctions satisfaisant à une condition de Lipschitz*. Research paper on quantitative trigonometric approximation under a Lipschitz condition. ([University of Auckland](https://www.math.auckland.ac.nz/hat/people/lebesgue.html))
- **1912:** *Sur un théorème de M. Volterra*. Research note addressing a theorem of Vito Volterra. ([Persée authority record](https://education.persee.fr/authority/665873))
- **1917:** *Sur certaines démonstrations d’existence*. Methodological research article about existence proofs rather than a protocol prescribing one universal method. ([Henri-Léon Lebesgue bibliography](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henri-L%C3%A9on_Lebesgue))
- **1918:** *Remarques sur les théories de la mesure et de l’intégration*. Retrospective and corrective research article comparing formulations of measure and integration. ([Wikisource](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Auteur:Henri-L%C3%A9on_Lebesgue))
- **1920:** *Sur une définition due à M. Borel (lettre à M. le Directeur des Annales scientifiques de l’École normale supérieure)*. An open letter functioning as mathematical criticism; its epistolary form is integral to what the text is. ([Henri-Léon Lebesgue bibliography](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henri-L%C3%A9on_Lebesgue))
- **1921:** *Exposé géométrique d’un mémoire de Cayley sur les polygones de Poncelet*. Historical-mathematical reconstruction and geometric exposition of Cayley’s work, neither a new theorem paper nor merely a review. ([Henri-Léon Lebesgue bibliography](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henri-L%C3%A9on_Lebesgue))
- **1921:** *Sur les diamètres rectilignes des courbes algébriques planes*. Research article in algebraic geometry. ([Persée authority record](https://www.persee.fr/authority/665873))
- **1922:** *Sur la théorie de la résiduation de Sylvester*. Historical and mathematical reconstruction of Sylvester’s theory of residuation. ([Henri-Léon Lebesgue bibliography](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henri-L%C3%A9on_Lebesgue))
- **1924:** *Remarques sur les deux premières démonstrations du théorème d’Euler relatif aux polyèdres*. Historical-critical article reconstructing early proofs of Euler’s polyhedron theorem. ([Henri-Léon Lebesgue bibliography](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henri-L%C3%A9on_Lebesgue))
- **1926; republished 1927:** *Sur le développement de la notion d’intégrale*. Historical and conceptual essay first published in Danish mathematical proceedings and then in French and Spanish manifestations; those appearances are not three separate works. ([ORESME Reading Group bibliography](https://www.exhibit.xavier.edu/oresme_2017Feb/))
- **1935:** *Démonstration du théorème fondamental de la théorie projective des coniques faite à l’aide des droites focales de M. P. Robert*. Research and pedagogical note giving a synthetic proof in projective geometry. ([Henri-Léon Lebesgue bibliography](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henri-L%C3%A9on_Lebesgue))

### Pedagogical and historical works

- **1923:** *Notice sur la vie et les travaux de Camille Jordan (1838–1922)*. Institutional memorial biography and critical account of Jordan’s mathematics, written for the Académie des sciences. ([BnF authority bibliography](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/affiner.do?afficheRegroup=false&affinageSupprimer=true&codeFacetteAffine=FacDis&critereRecherche=&index=AUT3&listeAffinages=FacPays_fr%3BFacDis_322&motRecherche=&nbResultParPage=10&numNotice=11911865&triResultParPage=0&trouveDansFiltre=NoticePUB&typeNotice=&valeurFacetteAffine=322))
- **1933:** *Les coniques dans l’enseignement secondaire*. Pedagogical essay or course-text arguing for a particular place and manner of teaching conics; later appearances under nearby titles should not automatically be counted as new works. ([Persée](https://www.persee.fr/authority/665873); [BnF](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/affiner.do?afficheRegroup=false&affinageSupprimer=true&codeFacetteAffine=FacDis&critereRecherche=&index=AUT3&listeAffinages=FacPays_fr%3BFacDis_322&motRecherche=&nbResultParPage=10&numNotice=11911865&triResultParPage=0&trouveDansFiltre=NoticePUB&typeNotice=&valeurFacetteAffine=322))
- **Before 1941; published 1942:** *Les Coniques*. Posthumously issued geometrical course or treatise. The printed book is an editorial manifestation completed after Lebesgue’s death, so the precise boundary between his manuscript and its editors’ realization must not be treated as self-evident. ([BnF](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/affiner.do?afficheRegroup=false&affinageSupprimer=true&codeFacetteAffine=FacDis&critereRecherche=&index=AUT3&listeAffinages=FacPays_fr%3BFacDis_322&motRecherche=&nbResultParPage=10&numNotice=11911865&triResultParPage=0&trouveDansFiltre=NoticePUB&typeNotice=&valeurFacetteAffine=322))
- **1940–1941; published 1950:** *Leçons sur les constructions géométriques*. Final Collège de France lecture-course, reconstructed posthumously rather than published by Lebesgue as a finished book; it therefore resists classification as an independently completed authored monograph. ([BnF](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/affiner.do?afficheRegroup=false&affinageSupprimer=true&codeFacetteAffine=FacDis&critereRecherche=&index=AUT3&listeAffinages=FacPays_fr%3BFacDis_322&motRecherche=&nbResultParPage=10&numNotice=11911865&triResultParPage=0&trouveDansFiltre=NoticePUB&typeNotice=&valeurFacetteAffine=322); [Persée](https://www.persee.fr/authority/665873))
- **Various dates; collected posthumously:** *Notices d’histoire des mathématiques*. A posthumous thematic gathering of Lebesgue’s historical notices, not a single work planned under this title during his lifetime. ([L’Enseignement Mathématique](https://www.e-periodica.ch/cntmng?pid=ens-001%3A1979%3A25%3A%3A27); [Persée](https://www.persee.fr/authority/665873))

### Contributions to other authors’ works

- **1905; revised 1928:** *Notes to Émile Borel’s Leçons sur les fonctions de variables réelles et les développements en séries de polynômes*. Authorial annotations contributed by Lebesgue to a book whose lectures are Borel’s and whose principal redaction is Maurice Fréchet’s; the whole book must not be misattributed to Lebesgue. ([BnF](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/affiner.do?afficheRegroup=false&affinageSupprimer=true&codeFacetteAffine=FacDis&critereRecherche=&index=AUT3&listeAffinages=FacPays_fr%3BFacDis_322&motRecherche=&nbResultParPage=10&numNotice=11911865&triResultParPage=0&trouveDansFiltre=NoticePUB&typeNotice=&valeurFacetteAffine=322); [Persée](https://education.persee.fr/authority/665873))

### Correspondence as an archival obra

- **c. 1901–1918; published 1991 and 2004:** *Letters to Émile Borel*. A sustained mathematical correspondence documenting the making and aftermath of the Lebesgue integral. It was not conceived as a book by Lebesgue: *Lettres d’Henri Lebesgue à Émile Borel*, *La Genèse de l’intégrale*, and *Les Lendemains de l’intégrale* are modern editorial selections or arrangements of an archival body, not original Lebesgue titles. ([NUMDAM](https://archive.numdam.org/item/CSHM_1991__12__1_0/); [Persée](https://www.persee.fr/authority/665873))
- **Dates vary; published 1981:** *Four letters to Maurice Fréchet*. Mathematical correspondence later edited as a historical source; it resists treatment as a work because neither the four-letter grouping nor its published title was Lebesgue’s project. ([Persée bibliographical record](https://education.persee.fr/authority/665873))

### Posthumous collected manifestation

- **1972–1973:** *Œuvres scientifiques de Henri Lebesgue*. Five-volume scholarly collected-works edition comprising integration and differentiation; representation of functions; surface structure and area; harmonic functions, topology, and geometry; algebraic and elementary geometry; pedagogy; reviews; and memorial notices. It is the standard corpus used here, but as a posthumous editorial manifestation it is not itself an obra made by Lebesgue, and it does not turn every lecture, review, or editorial fragment into an autonomous work. ([L’Enseignement Mathématique, official description](https://www.unige.ch/math/EnsMath/monographies); [SMF/BnF bibliography](https://smf.emath.fr/smf-dossiers-et-ressources/bibliographie-yves-meyer-bnf-2006))
