## Works by Erich Seligmann Fromm

### Scope of the obra

- **1922–1992:** *The writings of Erich Fromm*. This inventory treats a “thing” as an independently titled book, dissertation, research report, manifesto, edited symposium, sustained dialogue, or posthumously constituted manuscript-work—not every review, preface, interview, lecture transcript, or variant edition. The 12-volume *Gesamtausgabe* remains the exhaustive textual record: volumes 1–9 contain everything Fromm published himself, volumes 11–12 the posthumously published writings, and volume 10 the full bibliography through 1999. [International Erich Fromm Society](https://fromm-online.org/werk/gesamtausgabe/)

### Dissertation and early sociopsychoanalytic studies

- **1922:** *Das jüdische Gesetz: Ein Beitrag zur Soziologie des Diaspora-Judentums*. Fromm’s Heidelberg doctoral dissertation, a sociological study of Jewish law and diaspora communities; although completed in 1922, it first appeared as a book posthumously in 1989. [Funk bibliography, pp. 325–337](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1926/27:** *Dauernde Nachwirkung eines Erziehungsfehlers*. A psychoanalytic case-study article and Fromm’s earliest item in Funk’s chronological bibliography of his published writings. [Funk bibliography, p. 325](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1927:** *Der Sabbath*. A psychoanalytic and socioreligious study of the Sabbath, published in *Imago*; it is an article-sized obra rather than a book. [Funk bibliography, p. 325](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1929:** *Psychoanalyse und Soziologie*. A programmatic article arguing for an encounter between psychoanalysis and sociology. [Funk bibliography, p. 325](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1930:** *Die Entwicklung des Christusdogmas: Eine psychoanalytische Studie zur sozialpsychologischen Funktion der Religion*. A long sociopsychoanalytic study of changing Christological doctrine; later English collections sometimes make it look like a book, but its original manifestation was an article in *Imago*. [Funk bibliography, p. 325](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1930:** *Der Staat als Erzieher: Zur Psychologie der Strafjustiz*. An article on punishment, criminal justice, and the state’s psychological function. [Funk bibliography, p. 325](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1930:** *Ödipus in Innsbruck: Zum Halsmann-Prozess*. A psychoanalytic intervention in a contemporary murder trial; it resists classification as detached scholarship because it acts within a public controversy. [Funk bibliography, p. 325](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1931:** *Zur Psychologie des Verbrechers und der strafenden Gesellschaft*. A study of the criminal and the punitive society, joining psychoanalytic interpretation to social criticism. [Funk bibliography, p. 325](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1931:** *Politik und Psychoanalyse*. A programmatic essay on the relation between political action and psychoanalytic knowledge. [Funk bibliography, p. 325](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1932:** *Über Methode und Aufgabe einer analytischen Sozialpsychologie: Bemerkungen über Psychoanalyse und historischen Materialismus*. A methodological manifesto for analytic social psychology, intended to connect Freud’s account of psychic formation with historical materialism. [Funk bibliography, p. 325](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1932:** *Die psychoanalytische Charakterologie und ihre Bedeutung für die Sozialpsychologie*. A theoretical article making characterology the bridge between individual psychic structure and social organization. [Funk bibliography, p. 325](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1933:** *Robert Briffaults Werk über das Mutterrecht*. A critical study of Briffault’s work on matriarchy, anticipating Fromm’s continuing interest in maternal and patriarchal social forms. [Funk bibliography, p. 326](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1934:** *Die sozialpsychologische Bedeutung der Mutterrechtstheorie*. A sociopsychoanalytic essay on matriarchal theory and its political implications. [Funk bibliography, p. 326](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1935:** *Die gesellschaftliche Bedingtheit der psychoanalytischen Therapie*. An article arguing that psychoanalytic therapy is socially conditioned rather than a purely private technique. [Funk bibliography, p. 326](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1936:** *Sozialpsychologischer Teil*. Fromm’s social-psychological contribution to the Institute for Social Research’s collaborative *Studien über Autorität und Familie*; it is a component of a collective research project, not a freestanding book. [Funk bibliography, p. 327](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1936:** *Geschichte und Methoden der Erhebung*. A second contribution to *Studien über Autorität und Familie*, documenting the history and methods of its empirical inquiry; responsibility belongs within the Institute’s collective project even though Funk bibliographs it under Fromm. [Funk bibliography, p. 327](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1929–1931; first published 1980:** *Arbeiter und Angestellte am Vorabend des Dritten Reiches: Eine sozialpsychologische Untersuchung*. An unfinished empirical survey project on German workers and employees, reconstructed and edited by Wolfgang Bonß from Fromm’s English manuscript and research materials. Its posthumous book form should not obscure its status as an incomplete collective investigation; the English edition appeared in 1984 as *The Working Class in Weimar Germany*. [Funk bibliography, p. 336](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

### Books: psychoanalysis, ethics, religion, and social character

- **1941:** *Escape from Freedom*. A book-length work of social psychology and political diagnosis explaining authoritarian submission through the psychic burdens of modern freedom; British editions use the title *The Fear of Freedom*. [Funk bibliography](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1947:** *Man for Himself: An Inquiry into the Psychology of Ethics*. A systematic presentation of humanistic ethics and Fromm’s character orientations, made as a companion to *Escape from Freedom*. [Fromm book-title register](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/pdf/Book-Titles%20-%20Copyright%20Holder%20-%20Translations%20-%20Copy%20Estimation%5B%2Cenglish%5D.pdf)

- **1950:** *Psychoanalysis and Religion*. A short book developed from the Dwight H. Terry Lectures, distinguishing authoritarian religion from humanistic religious experience. [Funk, *The Courage to Be Human*](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1951:** *The Forgotten Language: An Introduction to the Understanding of Dreams, Fairy Tales and Myths*. A book interpreting dreams, myths, and folktales as symbolic manifestations rather than treating them as separate literary genres. [Fromm-Online textual record](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/fromm-titles/1927a-eng.pdf)

- **1955:** *The Sane Society*. A book of sociopsychoanalytic diagnosis and utopian reconstruction, asking whether a society—not merely an individual—can be ill. [Funk bibliography](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1956:** *The Art of Loving: An Inquiry into the Nature of Love*. A theoretical and practical book about love as a disciplined human capacity; despite its recurrent marketing as advice literature, it is neither a simple protocol nor a romance manual. [Fromm book-title register](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/pdf/Book-Titles%20by%20Erich%20Fromm%20and%20Their%20Translations%5B%2Cenglish%5D.pdf)

- **1959:** *Sigmund Freud’s Mission: An Analysis of His Personality and Influence*. A psychobiographical interpretation of Freud and the historical mission embodied in psychoanalysis. [Funk bibliography](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1960:** *Zen Buddhism and Psychoanalysis*. A composite conference-book made with D. T. Suzuki and Richard De Martino after a 1957 seminar in Cuernavaca; Fromm made the psychoanalytic essay, not the whole three-author object alone. [Fromm book-title register](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/pdf/Book-Titles%20by%20Erich%20Fromm%20and%20Their%20Translations%5B%2Cenglish%5D.pdf)

- **1961:** *Marx’s Concept of Man*. An interpretive book framing and introducing Marx’s early humanist writings; because it incorporates translated Marx texts, the published volume is partly Fromm’s obra and partly an editorial vehicle for another author. [Funk, *The Courage to Be Human*](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1962:** *Beyond the Chains of Illusion: My Encounter with Marx and Freud*. An intellectual autobiography organized as a comparative interpretation of Freud and Marx rather than a comprehensive memoir. [Funk bibliography](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1963:** *The Dogma of Christ and Other Essays on Religion, Psychology and Culture*. A retrospective collection whose title essay originated in 1930; the collection is a new bibliographic object but not a wholly new text. [International Foundation Erich Fromm bibliography](https://www.ifefromm.it/opere-di-fromm/)

- **1964:** *The Heart of Man: Its Genius for Good and Evil*. A book-length study of biophilia, necrophilia, narcissism, incestuous fixation, freedom, and human destructiveness. [Funk bibliography](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1966:** *You Shall Be as Gods: A Radical Interpretation of the Old Testament and Its Tradition*. A humanistic interpretation of Hebrew scripture and Jewish tradition, presented as a developing history of human freedom rather than orthodox theology. [Funk, *The Courage to Be Human*](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1970:** *The Crisis of Psychoanalysis: Essays on Freud, Marx, and Social Psychology*. A collection gathering essays written across several decades; it is an editorial constellation of prior works rather than a single newly composed argument. [International Foundation Erich Fromm bibliography](https://www.ifefromm.it/opere-di-fromm/)

- **1960s; published 1970:** *Social Character in a Mexican Village: A Sociopsychoanalytic Study*. A collaborative empirical study by Fromm and Michael Maccoby, based on field research in a Mexican peasant community; its questionnaires, interviews, and interpretive model make it a research project as well as a book. [International Foundation Erich Fromm bibliography](https://www.ifefromm.it/opere-di-fromm/)

- **1973:** *The Anatomy of Human Destructiveness*. A large interdisciplinary study distinguishing defensive aggression from specifically human destructiveness and developing Fromm’s account of necrophilia. [International Foundation Erich Fromm bibliography](https://www.ifefromm.it/opere-di-fromm/)

- **1976:** *To Have or to Be?*. A book of social diagnosis and ethical orientation contrasting the having and being modes of existence; it also functions as a manifesto for psychological and socioeconomic transformation. [Funk bibliography, p. 335](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1979; English publication 1980:** *Greatness and Limitations of Freud’s Thought*. Fromm’s final completed book, a critical assessment of Freud that acknowledges psychoanalysis’s discoveries while contesting its patriarchal, instinctual, and bourgeois assumptions. The bibliography dates the work 1979 but records the New York edition as 1980. [Funk bibliography, p. 336](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

### Political manifestos and interventions

- **1960:** *Let Man Prevail: A Socialist Manifesto and Program*. A manifesto in the strong sense: a short text intended to organize democratic-socialist thought and action, not simply to describe socialism. [Fromm book-title register](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/pdf/Book-Titles%20by%20Erich%20Fromm%20and%20Their%20Translations%5B%2Cenglish%5D.pdf)

- **1961:** *May Man Prevail? An Inquiry into the Facts and Fictions of Foreign Policy*. A political book intervening in Cold War foreign policy and nuclear confrontation. [Funk bibliography](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1961:** *War Within Man: A Psychological Enquiry into the Roots of Destructiveness*. A public symposium or composite discussion involving Fromm, Thomas Merton, D. T. Suzuki, and others; attribution to Fromm alone would be misleading because the publication is a collective manifestation. [Fromm book-title register](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/pdf/Book-Titles%20by%20Erich%20Fromm%20and%20Their%20Translations%5B%2Cenglish%5D.pdf)

- **1963:** *The Revolutionary Character*. An essay defining revolutionary character as independence from irrational authority; it operates partly as political manifesto and partly as psychoanalytic characterology. [Funk bibliography](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1965:** *Socialist Humanism: An International Symposium*. An international anthology conceived and edited by Fromm, with his introduction and contribution. The symposium as a whole is an editorial and political project rather than a sole-authored book. [International Foundation Erich Fromm bibliography](https://www.ifefromm.it/opere-di-fromm/)

- **1968:** *The Revolution of Hope: Toward a Humanized Technology*. A political-philosophical book and manifesto for a humanized technological society, written in the milieu of Eugene McCarthy’s presidential campaign. [Funk bibliography](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1968:** *The Nature of Man*. A compact dialogue or debate between Fromm and Ramón Xirau; the conversational encounter, rather than either participant’s contribution alone, is the published object. [Fromm book-title register](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/pdf/Book-Titles%20by%20Erich%20Fromm%20and%20Their%20Translations%5B%2Cenglish%5D.pdf)

- **1975:** *Remarks on the Policy of Détente*. Written testimony submitted to the United States Senate Committee on Foreign Relations; it is a political intervention preserved inside a governmental protocol rather than an independent book. [Funk bibliography, p. 335](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

### Dialogues and recorded manifestations

- **1966:** *Dialogue with Erich Fromm*. A structured interview conducted by Richard I. Evans and published as a book; Fromm’s spoken responses are his contribution, but the dialogic object is co-made by interviewer, speaker, transcription, and editing. [Funk bibliography](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1974:** *Im Namen des Lebens: Ein Gespräch mit Hans Jürgen Schultz*. A sustained published conversation with Hans Jürgen Schultz; it resists classification as a sole-authored treatise because its form and sequence arise from exchange. [Funk bibliography, p. 334](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1975:** *Aggression und Charakter: Ein Gespräch mit Adalbert Reif*. A book-length conversation concerning aggression and character, jointly generated by Reif’s questions and Fromm’s replies. [Funk bibliography, p. 335](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1975:** *Anmerkungen zur psychoanalytischen Theorie und Therapie*. A recorded lecture delivered at the symposium for Fromm’s seventy-fifth birthday and issued on cassette; this is an audio manifestation, not originally a printed work. [Funk bibliography, p. 335](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1980:** *Erich Fromm, il coraggio di essere: Intervista di Guido Ferrari*. A book-form interview by Guido Ferrari, published in the year of Fromm’s death; its authorship is necessarily dialogic. [Funk bibliography, p. 336](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

### Posthumous collections and reconstructed manuscript-works

- **1981:** *On Disobedience and Other Essays*. A posthumous collection of previously written political and humanistic essays. It is an editorial assemblage, not a new book completed by Fromm in 1981. [Funk bibliography, p. 337](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/secondary-titles/Funk_R_1982b.pdf)

- **1983:** *Über die Liebe zum Leben: Rundfunksendungen*. Hans Jürgen Schultz’s posthumous collection of radio broadcasts, later issued in English as *For the Love of Life*; the underlying manifestations were broadcasts, while the book is an editor-made sequence. [Funk editorial bibliography](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/FUNK/Fromm-Ed_by_Funk.pdf)

- **1985:** *Erich-Fromm-Lesebuch*. Rainer Funk’s anthology of chapters and selections from several Fromm books; it is a reader made from Fromm’s obra, not an additional work authored as such by Fromm. [Fromm book-title register](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/pdf/Book-Titles%20-%20Copyright%20Holder%20-%20Translations%20-%20Copy%20Estimation%5B%2Cenglish%5D.pdf)

- **1974–1975; first published 1989:** *Vom Haben zum Sein: Wege und Irrwege der Selbsterfahrung* / *The Art of Being*. Rainer Funk constructed this posthumous book from material originating during Fromm’s work on *To Have or to Be?*; it is a genuine unfinished project but its final title, selection, and book-form are editorial. [Funk editorial bibliography](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/FUNK/Fromm-Ed_by_Funk.pdf)

- **1922; first published 1989:** *Das jüdische Gesetz*. The posthumous book manifestation of Fromm’s doctoral dissertation; it is the same underlying work as the 1922 thesis, not a second composition. [Fromm book-title register](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/pdf/Book-Titles%20-%20Copyright%20Holder%20-%20Translations%20-%20Copy%20Estimation%5B%2Cenglish%5D.pdf)

- **1989:** *Schriften über Sigmund Freud*. An editor-made German collection of Fromm’s writings on Freud; the constituent texts are Fromm’s, but the collection as collection is posthumous. [Fromm book-title register](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/pdf/Book-Titles%20-%20Copyright%20Holder%20-%20Translations%20-%20Copy%20Estimation%5B%2Cenglish%5D.pdf)

- **first published 1990:** *The Revision of Psychoanalysis*. Volume 3 of Rainer Funk’s posthumous writings, assembled from Fromm’s unpublished or previously uncollected papers on psychoanalytic theory. Its status is therefore divided between Fromm’s manuscripts and Funk’s editorial construction. [Fromm Estate title register](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/lists/2.05%20Book-Titles%20%E2%80%93%20Copyright%20Holder%20%E2%80%93%20Translations%20%E2%80%93%20Copy%20Estimation.pdf)

- **first published 1990:** *Ethics and Politics*. Volume 4 of the posthumous writings, collecting manuscript material on humanistic ethics, politics, and social transformation rather than reproducing a book Fromm had finalized. [Fromm Estate title register](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/lists/2.05%20Book-Titles%20%E2%80%93%20Copyright%20Holder%20%E2%80%93%20Translations%20%E2%80%93%20Copy%20Estimation.pdf)

- **1990:** *Wege zur Befreiung*. A posthumous German anthology; because it selects already existing texts, it belongs to the reception and editorial organization of Fromm’s obra rather than to his list of newly composed works. [Fromm book-title register](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/pdf/Book-Titles%20by%20Erich%20Fromm%20and%20Their%20Translations%5B%2Cenglish%5D.pdf)

- **first published 1991:** *The Art of Listening*. A posthumous reconstruction from lectures and seminar material on psychoanalytic technique. It resists the category “book by Fromm”: the discourse is his, but its continuous printed architecture is Funk’s editorial work. [Fromm Estate title register](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/lists/2.05%20Book-Titles%20%E2%80%93%20Copyright%20Holder%20%E2%80%93%20Translations%20%E2%80%93%20Copy%20Estimation.pdf)

- **first published 1991:** *The Pathology of Normalcy*. Volume 6 of the posthumous writings, assembled from lectures and manuscripts concerning social pathology and supposedly normal life. [Fromm Estate title register](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/lists/2.05%20Book-Titles%20%E2%80%93%20Copyright%20Holder%20%E2%80%93%20Translations%20%E2%80%93%20Copy%20Estimation.pdf)

- **first published 1992:** *Society and Psyche* / *Beyond Freud*. Volume 7 of the posthumous writings, bringing together unpublished or uncollected work on social psychoanalysis; the varying English title is an editorial and translational fact, not evidence of two distinct underlying works. [Fromm Estate title register](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/lists/2.05%20Book-Titles%20%E2%80%93%20Copyright%20Holder%20%E2%80%93%20Translations%20%E2%80%93%20Copy%20Estimation.pdf)

- **first published 1992:** *On Being Human*. Volume 8 of the posthumous writings, composed from Fromm’s surviving papers rather than a manuscript he had prepared as a finished book. [Fromm Estate title register](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/lists/2.05%20Book-Titles%20%E2%80%93%20Copyright%20Holder%20%E2%80%93%20Translations%20%E2%80%93%20Copy%20Estimation.pdf)

- **1992:** *Worte wie Wege*. A posthumous anthology of short selections and sayings; it is an editor-shaped manifestation of excerpts and should not be mistaken for an aphorism-book composed by Fromm. [Fromm book-title register](https://fromm-online.org/wp-content/uploads/pdf/Book-Titles%20by%20Erich%20Fromm%20and%20Their%20Translations%5B%2Cenglish%5D.pdf)

### Sources
