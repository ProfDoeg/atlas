## Works by Johann Gottlieb Fichte

### Political manifestos and public interventions

- **1793:** *Zurückforderung der Denkfreiheit von den Fürsten Europens, die sie bisher unterdrückten. Eine Rede*. An anonymous revolutionary pamphlet and manifesto: it does not merely discuss intellectual freedom but publicly demands it from Europe’s rulers. [GA I/1](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210110?lang=de)

- **1793:** *Beitrag zur Berichtigung der Urtheile des Publikums über die französische Revolution*. An anonymous political treatise defending the principles of the French Revolution; only its “first part” appeared, so the announced larger project remained incomplete. [GA I/1](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210110?lang=de)

- **1793:** *Beweis der Unrechtmäßigkeit des Büchernachdrucks*. A juridical-political argument against unauthorized reprinting, made as an intervention in contemporary publishing practice rather than as a freestanding philosophical system. [GA I/1](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210110?lang=de)

- **1794:** *Gegenerklärung über des Hn. Prof. Schmid Erklärung*. A short public counter-declaration in an academic controversy; its obra is the polemical act itself. [GA I/2](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210210?lang=de)

- **1796:** *Erklärung gegen den Aufsatz: Verunglückter Versuch, im christlichen Deutschland eine Art von öffentlicher Vernunft-Religionsübung einzuführen*. A polemical declaration defending Fichte’s position against an attack in *Eudämonia*. [GA I/3](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210310?lang=de)

- **1797:** *Annalen des philosophischen Tons*. A satire and polemical essay directed at the prevailing tone of philosophical controversy. [GA I/4](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210410?lang=de)

- **1797–1798:** *Anzeige; An die Käufer des Philosophischen Journals; Unter der Firma …; Des Prof. Fichte Verantwortung*. Four notices and defensive declarations produced through Fichte’s editorship of the *Philosophisches Journal*; they resist treatment as autonomous “works” because they are acts within an editorial dispute. [GA I/4](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210410?lang=de)

- **1799:** *Ankündigung der Appellation an das Publikum*. A publication announcement that functions as part of Fichte’s campaign during the atheism controversy rather than as an independent treatise. [GA I/5](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210510?lang=de)

- **1799:** *J. G. Fichte’s Appellation an das Publikum über die durch ein kurfürstlich-sächsisches Confiscationsrescript ihm beigemessenen atheistischen Äußerungen*. A public appeal and manifesto of intellectual self-defence in the atheism controversy. [GA I/5](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210510?lang=de)

- **1799:** *Der Herausgeber des Philosophischen Journals gerichtliche Verantwortungsschriften gegen die Anklage des Atheismus*. A dossier of juridical defences: not one seamless book but a set of texts made to answer an official accusation. [GA I/6](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210610?lang=de)

- **1799–1800:** *Flugschriften zum Atheismusstreit*. A cluster of pamphlets and documentary interventions surrounding the atheism controversy; the critical edition preserves them collectively, and their boundaries as individual “works” are not always stable. [GA I/6](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210610?lang=de)

- **1800:** *An das philosophische Publikum*. A public response concerning a review of Johann Heinrich Gottlieb Heusinger, made as an act in periodical controversy. [GA I/6](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210610?lang=de)

- **1800:** *Der geschloßne Handelsstaat*. A systematic political-economic project for a legally closed commercial state; it is simultaneously treatise, institutional design, and protocol for ordering economic life. [GA I/7](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210710?lang=de)

- **1800:** *Seit sechs Jahren …*. An announcement dated 4 November 1800; it is bibliographically preserved but resists elevation into an independent work. [GA I/7](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210710?lang=de)

- **1801:** *Erklärung gegen Biester*. A short public declaration against Johann Erich Biester, made within a continuing literary dispute. [GA I/7](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210710?lang=de)

- **1801:** *Erklärung gegen den Buchhändler Gabler*. A public declaration in a bookselling dispute; its status is closer to an operative notice than to a philosophical obra. [GA I/8](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210810?lang=de)

- **1804–1807:** *Ankündigungen zu den Vorlesungen in Berliner Zeitungen*. Newspaper announcements advertising Fichte’s lecture series. They are textual acts organizing audiences and occasions, not descriptions of the lectures themselves. [GA I/8](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210810?lang=de), [GA I/9](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210910?lang=de)

- **1807:** *Ueber Machiavell, als Schriftsteller, und Stellen aus seinen Schriften*. A political-historical essay incorporating selected passages from Machiavelli; consequently it is partly authored argument and partly editorial assemblage. [GA I/9](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210910?lang=de)

- **1808:** *Reden an die deutsche Nation*. Fourteen public addresses delivered in occupied Berlin: a lecture-cycle that became a nationalist political manifesto, rather than simply a book written at a desk. [GA I/10](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120211010?lang=de)

- **1811:** *Ueber die einzig mögliche Störung der akademischen Freiheit*. Fichte’s inaugural address as rector of the University of Berlin; an institutional speech whose performance and occasion are integral to the obra. [GA I/10](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120211010?lang=de)

### Published philosophical systems and expositions

- **1792; revised 1793:** *Versuch einer Critik aller Offenbarung*. A systematic critique of the concept of revelation; the anonymous first edition was initially mistaken for a work by Kant, while the second edition added chapters and a new preface. [GA I/1](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210110?lang=de)

- **1794; revised 1798:** *Ueber den Begriff der Wissenschaftslehre oder der sogenannten Philosophie*. A programmatic invitation essay defining the task and form of the *Wissenschaftslehre*; the second edition contains revisions and supplements. [GA I/2](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210210?lang=de)

- **1794–1795; later editions 1801–1802:** *Grundlage der gesammten Wissenschaftslehre als Handschrift für seine Zuhörer*. The first large systematic presentation of the *Wissenschaftslehre*, issued in instalments as a “manuscript for his hearers”; its hybrid existence as lecture aid, publication, and repeatedly revised system resists the category of an ordinary book. [GA I/2](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210210?lang=de)

- **1795:** *Grundriß des Eigenthümlichen der Wissenschaftslehre in Rücksicht auf das theoretische Vermögen*. A systematic outline supplementing the *Grundlage*, especially its account of theoretical cognition, space, and time. [GA I/3](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210310?lang=de)

- **1795:** *Von der Sprachfähigkeit und dem Ursprung der Sprache*. A philosophical essay on linguistic capacity and the origin of language. [GA I/3](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210310?lang=de)

- **1796:** *Vergleichung des vom Herrn Professor Schmid aufgestellten Systems mit der Wissenschaftslehre*. A technical comparative polemic distinguishing Fichte’s system from Carl Christian Erhard Schmid’s. [GA I/3](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210310?lang=de)

- **1796–1797:** *Grundlage des Naturrechts nach Principien der Wissenschaftslehre*. A two-part systematic philosophy of right, recognition, embodiment, property, contract, and the state. [GA I/3](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210310?lang=de), [GA I/4](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210410?lang=de)

- **1797–1798:** *Versuch einer neuen Darstellung der Wissenschaftslehre*. A projected new presentation comprising two introductions and the beginning of a first chapter; the publication stopped, so incompletion is constitutive of the surviving obra. [GA I/4](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210410?lang=de)

- **1798:** *Das System der Sittenlehre nach den Principien der Wissenschaftslehre*. A systematic ethics and theory of embodied moral agency. [GA I/5](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210510?lang=de)

- **1798:** *Ueber den Grund unseres Glaubens an eine göttliche Weltregierung*. A philosophy-of-religion essay whose publication precipitated the atheism controversy; its historical manifestation includes the controversy it performed, not only the printed argument. [GA I/5](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210510?lang=de)

- **1800:** *Aus einem Privatschreiben*. A published extract recasting Fichte’s position on God and the moral world-order; because an excerpt from a private letter was made public as argument, its genre remains deliberately unstable. [GA I/6](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210610?lang=de)

- **1800:** *Die Bestimmung des Menschen*. A popular philosophical composition in three books—doubt, knowledge, and faith—designed as a dramatic passage through positions rather than a conventional systematic treatise. [GA I/6](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210610?lang=de)

- **1800:** *Ueber Geist und Buchstab in der Philosophie*. An aesthetic and metaphilosophical essay originally connected with an unsuccessful publication exchange with Schiller. [GA I/6](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210610?lang=de)

- **1801:** *Sonnenklarer Bericht an das größere Publikum über das eigentliche Wesen der neuesten Philosophie: Ein Versuch, die Leser zum Verstehen zu zwingen*. A popular exposition whose performative subtitle makes it almost a pedagogical protocol: an attempt not merely to state the system but to compel the reader’s act of understanding. [GA I/7](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210710?lang=de)

- **1801:** *Antwortschreiben an Reinhold*. A technical open reply to Karl Leonhard Reinhold concerning the *Wissenschaftslehre*. [GA I/7](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210710?lang=de)

- **1801:** *Friedrich Nicolai’s Leben und sonderbare Meinungen: Ein Beitrag zur Literargeschichte des vergangenen und zur Pädagogik des angehenden Jahrhunderts*. A satirical intellectual biography and polemical literary-history project rather than a neutral life. [GA I/7](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210710?lang=de)

- **1804–1805; published 1806:** *Die Grundzüge des gegenwärtigen Zeitalters*. A public lecture-cycle transformed into a philosophy of history and diagnosis of the present age. [GA I/8](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210810?lang=de)

- **1806:** *Ueber das Wesen des Gelehrten, und seine Erscheinungen im Gebiete der Freiheit*. A sequence of lectures serving as a general and pedagogical introduction to Fichte’s teaching at Erlangen. [GA I/8](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210810?lang=de)

- **1806:** *Die Anweisung zum seeligen Leben, oder auch die Religionslehre*. A public lecture-cycle and philosophy of religion organized as a practical “instruction” toward blessed life; its title explicitly casts the text as protocol as well as doctrine. [GA I/9](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210910?lang=de)

- **1810:** *Die Wissenschaftslehre, in ihrem allgemeinen Umrisse dargestellt*. A compressed, polished outline of the later *Wissenschaftslehre*, developed from a concluding lecture rather than as a complete replacement for the larger oral systems. [GA I/10](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120211010?lang=de)

### Lectures and repeatedly remade philosophical projects

- **1794:** *Züricher Vorlesungen über den Begriff der Wissenschaftslehre*. An early oral presentation of the new philosophical project, surviving through lecture materials rather than as a book authorized for publication by Fichte. [Stanford Encyclopedia bibliography](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/spr2022/entries/johann-fichte/)

- **1794:** *Einige Vorlesungen über die Bestimmung des Gelehrten*. A five-lecture public course on the scholar’s vocation; the printed text is one manifestation of an event of teaching. [GA I/3](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210310?lang=de)

- **1794:** *Ueber die Würde des Menschen*. A concluding lecture on human dignity, preserved as a discrete ceremonial address. [GA I/2](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210210?lang=de)

- **1794:** *Die am Ende des Winters 1794 gehaltene Schlußvorlesung*. A concluding lecture whose identity is tied to its occasion; it resists becoming a freestanding book-work. [GA I/4](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210410?lang=de)

- **1794–1795:** *Eigne Meditationen über Elementarphilosophie / Practische Philosophie*. Private working manuscripts in which Fichte constructed the first *Wissenschaftslehre* and its practical component. These are project-documents, not completed works intended for an audience; the critical edition places such material in its series of posthumous writings. [GA, Series II](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202)

- **1796–1799:** *Wissenschaftslehre nova methodo*. A repeatedly delivered, substantially new presentation of the *Wissenschaftslehre*, known through Fichte’s manuscripts and auditors’ transcripts; no single witness is simply “the book,” and the exact text must be critically reconstructed. [Stanford Encyclopedia bibliography](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/spr2022/entries/johann-fichte/)

- **1796–1812:** *Vorlesungen über Platners philosophische Aphorismen*. A long-running body of lecture annotations and commentaries on Ernst Platner. It is a teaching project accumulated and revised over years, not one finished treatise, and occupies a dedicated volume of the critical edition. [GA II/4 listing](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202)

- **1799–1800; published 1802:** *Philosophie der Maurerei: Briefe an Konstant*. Masonic lectures recast as fictional letters; the work crosses oral instruction, epistolary form, and institutional reflection. [GA I/8](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210810?lang=de)

- **1800:** *Neue Darstellung der Wissenschaftslehre*. A new systematic manuscript or project surviving only partially; it should not be confused with the similarly titled 1797–1798 publication. [Stanford Encyclopedia bibliography](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/spr2022/entries/johann-fichte/)

- **1801–1802:** *Darstellung der Wissenschaftslehre*. A new course and manuscript presentation of the system, posthumously transmitted rather than published by Fichte as a settled book. [GA, Series II](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202)

- **1804:** *Wissenschaftslehre 1804, erste Vortragsreihe*. The first of three distinct 1804 presentations; it survives as a lecture project rather than a lifetime publication. [GA, Series II](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202)

- **1804:** *Wissenschaftslehre 1804, zweite Vortragsreihe*. The most widely studied of the three 1804 courses, but still an orally delivered remaking of the system preserved through manuscript evidence. [Stanford Encyclopedia bibliography](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/spr2022/entries/johann-fichte/)

- **1804:** *Wissenschaftslehre 1804, dritte Vortragsreihe*. A third, separate presentation from the same year; treating all three courses as editions of one fixed work would conceal Fichte’s continuing reconstruction of the project. [GA, Series II](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202)

- **1805:** *Wissenschaftslehre 1805*. An Erlangen lecture-course and surviving posthumous manuscript presentation of the system. [GA II/9 listing](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202)

- **1807:** *Wissenschaftslehre 1807*. A later lecture presentation made during Fichte’s Königsberg period; it belongs to the evolving *Wissenschaftslehre* project, not to a single stable master-text. [GA II/10 listing](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202)

- **1810:** *Wissenschaftslehre 1810*. A Berlin lecture-course distinct from the short published *Umriß* of the same year. [GA II/11–12 listing](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202)

- **1811:** *Wissenschaftslehre 1811*. A new lecture presentation preserved among Fichte’s posthumous papers and auditors’ notes. [GA II/12 listing](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202)

- **1812:** *Wissenschaftslehre 1812*. Another recasting of the system, transmitted through both authorial materials and lecture transcripts; the textual object is therefore distributed between witnesses. [GA II/13 and IV/5 listings](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202)

- **1812:** *System der Rechtslehre*. A late lecture-course on right, surviving posthumously rather than as a publication authorized by Fichte. [GA II/13–14 listing](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202)

- **1812:** *System der Sittenlehre*. A late lecture-course on ethics, not a revision or edition of the 1798 printed *System der Sittenlehre* but a distinct manifestation of the later system. [GA II/13–14 listing](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202)

- **1812:** *Vorlesungen über die Bestimmung des Gelehrten*. Two late lectures returning to the scholar’s vocation; they are a new teaching event, not merely a reprint of the 1794 course. [GA I/10](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120211010?lang=de)

- **1813:** *Wissenschaftslehre 1813*. A late course and manuscript presentation, part of the unfinished serial remaking of Fichte’s central project. [GA II/14–16 listing](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202)

- **1813:** *Die Staatslehre, oder über das Verhältnis des Urstaates zum Vernunftreiche*. A late political-philosophical lecture project, published only after Fichte’s death; its received text is therefore editorially constituted. [GA II/16 listing](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202)

- **1814:** *Wissenschaftslehre 1814*. Fichte’s final presentation, interrupted by his last illness and death; it is an unfinished project rather than a completed terminal version of the system. [GA II/17 and IV/6 listings](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202)

- **1794–1814:** *Other lecture-courses in logic, metaphysics, philosophy of nature, right, ethics, religion, history, and the scholar’s vocation*. These survive unevenly in authorial manuscripts and notes made by immediate auditors. The critical edition deliberately separates Fichte’s posthumous papers from student transcripts, so neither series alone can be treated as a catalogue of self-contained authored “books.” [GA series structure](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202)

### Reviews, editorial matter, and periodical acts

- **1788:** *Fourteen reviews*. Fourteen early reviews, printed anonymously and recovered by the critical editors as Fichte’s, form a corpus rather than a single work; the volume’s contents do not present them as one titled composition. Their attribution is editorial and should not be confused with lifetime publication under Fichte’s name. [GA I/10](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120211010?lang=de)

- **1793:** *Review of Leonhard Creuzer, Skeptische Betrachtungen über die Freyheit des Willens*. A critical review that helped prepare Fichte’s transition toward the *Wissenschaftslehre*. [GA I/2](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210210?lang=de)

- **1793:** *Review of Friedrich Heinrich Gebhard, Ueber die sittliche Güte aus uninteressirtem Wohlwollen*. A philosophical review of a work on disinterested benevolence. The reviewed book bears 1792 on its title page, while Fichte’s review appeared in 1793, a distinction sometimes blurred in short bibliographies. [GA I/2](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210210?lang=de)

- **1794:** *Review of Aenesidemus*. A review of G. E. Schulze’s sceptical attack on Reinhold; although formally a review, it became a programmatic philosophical intervention crucial to Fichte’s emerging project. [GA I/2](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210210?lang=de)

- **1796:** *Review of Kant’s Zum ewigen Frieden*. A review that is also a compact political-philosophical intervention on perpetual peace. [GA I/3](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210310?lang=de)

- **1797–1798:** *Review of Journal für Wahrheit*. A periodical review preserved among Fichte’s published polemical writings. [GA I/4](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210410?lang=de)

- **1797–1800:** *Editorial notices, prefatory and concluding remarks, and annotations in the Philosophisches Journal*. These include *Anmerkung zu dem folgenden Aufsatze*, *Nacherinnerung und Vorerinnerung*, annotations to articles, and later declarations. They were made through editorial practice and often have no defensible autonomy as “works,” but excluding them would erase something Fichte demonstrably made. [GA I/4](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210410?lang=de), [GA I/6](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210610?lang=de)

- **1800:** *Review of C. G. Bardili, Grundriß der ersten Logik*. A critical review in which engagement with another philosopher becomes an extension of Fichte’s own systematic controversy. [GA I/6](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210610?lang=de)

- **1800:** *Jahrbücher der Kunst und Wissenschaft*. Editorial and programmatic material for a projected periodical enterprise; this is better classified as a publishing project than as one completed authored text. [GA I/6](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210610?lang=de)

### Poems, adaptations, and translation

- **1801–1802:** *Idylle*. A poem published in the *Musenalmanach des Jahres 1802*; it is a literary composition, not a philosophical book. [GA I/8](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210810?lang=de)

- **1805:** *Sonette und Hymnen aus dem Lateinischen*. A group of sonnets and hymnic adaptations from Latin published in the 1805 *Musenalmanach*; the collective heading identifies a cluster, not necessarily one unified work. [GA I/8](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210810?lang=de)

- **1806–1807:** *Dantes irdisches Paradies*. Fichte’s German translation or adaptation of the earthly-paradise portion of Dante; it is a translational making whose source authorship remains Dante’s. [GA I/9](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120210910?lang=de)

- **1808–1812:** *Petrarka’s 63stes Sonett nach Laura’s Tode*. A German poetic re-creation of Petrarch’s sixty-third sonnet after Laura’s death; the critical edition classifies it as a *Nachdichtung*, not an independently conceived poem. [GA I/10](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120211010?lang=de)

- **1808–1812:** *Aus Camões’ Lusiade, Gesang 3, Stanze 118*. A German poetic re-creation of a stanza from Camões’s *Lusiads*. Its mixed authorship should not be silently converted into an original Fichte poem. [GA I/10](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202/12021/120211010?lang=de)

### Correspondence and documentary writing

- **1775–1814:** *Briefe*. Fichte’s surviving correspondence is a large body of individually made letters rather than a book or single oeuvre; the critical edition devotes eight volumes to it. Some letters became philosophical acts or raw material for publication, but the corpus also includes practical, familial, institutional, and fragmentary communications. [GA, Series III](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202)

- **1780–1814:** *Nachgelassene Schriften*. Drafts, notebooks, fragments, lecture preparations, unfinished systems, poems, exercises, and other manuscript remains occupy seventeen substantive volumes plus a supplement in the critical edition. “Work” is often precisely what these materials resist: some are projects, some stages of composition, and some merely documentary traces. [GA, Series II](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202)

- **1794–1814:** *Kollegnachschriften*. Six volumes preserve notes made by Fichte’s immediate auditors. They are manifestations of his lectures but not straightforwardly texts made by Fichte: their wording belongs partly to the note-takers, and the critical edition therefore gives them a separate series rather than silently attributing them as authored books. [GA, Series IV](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1202)
