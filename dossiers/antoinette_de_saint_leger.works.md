## Works by Antoinette de Saint Léger

### Garden and island ensemble

- **1885–1927:** *Garden of the Isole di Brissago*. A living, continuously revised garden made by Saint Léger with gardeners and, initially, her husband Richard Flemyng: she designed paths, improved the soil, imported plants, created a rock garden and cultivated subtropical species on the Isola Grande. It resists classification as a single authored work—part horticultural experiment, part landscape architecture, part habitat and part decades-long project—and Max Emden substantially altered it after purchasing the islands. ([Frassi](https://archividonneticino.ch/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Saint_Leger_Antonietta_web.pdf), pp. 1–2; [Desmond](https://www.british-italian.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/RIVISTA-2018_19-web-6.pdf), pp. 14–15; [Forestaviva](https://www.federlegno.ch/files/federlegno/images/Giornale_federlegno/FORESTAVIVA_numero_86.pdf), p. 8)

- **From 1885:** *Villa Borrani–Saint Léger and island estate*. Rather than designing an entirely new building, Saint Léger restored and transformed the existing Villa Borrani—called “the convent” in her own account—and organized the surrounding island as a residence with paths, conservatory, kitchen garden and service structures. This was a collaborative Gesamtkunstwerk of dwelling, garden, collection and hospitality, not securely attributable to her alone; Emden demolished the villa and other old buildings in 1928. ([Forestaviva](https://www.federlegno.ch/files/federlegno/images/Giornale_federlegno/FORESTAVIVA_numero_86.pdf), p. 8; [Desmond](https://www.british-italian.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/RIVISTA-2018_19-web-6.pdf), pp. 14–15; [Ascona Magazine](https://fliphtml5.com/onwfs/svby/Ascona_Magazine_2025/), p. 75)

### Texts

- **1913:** *The Vegetation of the Isole St. Leger on the Lago Maggiore*. A horticultural account of how Saint Léger created and cultivated the island garden, written under the name A. Tzikos de Saint Léger for the *Journal of the Royal Horticultural Society*. Sources sometimes date the associated lecture or submission to 1912; the journal text is cited as 1913 and was republished in Italian in 2006 as *La vegetazione delle Isole di St. Léger sul Lago Maggiore*. ([Forestaviva](https://www.federlegno.ch/files/federlegno/images/Giornale_federlegno/FORESTAVIVA_numero_86.pdf), p. 8; [Frassi](https://archividonneticino.ch/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Saint_Leger_Antonietta_web.pdf), p. 3; [Frey et al.](https://www.jjh.cz/upload/38966.pdf), bibliography)

- **Dates and present whereabouts unknown:** *Diaries*. A reported run of more than sixty manuscript volumes, apparently a long-duration autobiographical practice rather than a prepared publication. Their survival and location are unknown, so their contents, exact dates and completeness cannot presently be verified; they resist treatment as an accessible completed work. ([Eva Frassi’s study as bibliographically identified by the Società dei Verbanisti](https://www.societaverbanisti.it/pubblicazioni/pubblicazioni.html); [RSI archival biography](https://www.rsi.ch/archivi/Antonietta-di-Saint-L%C3%A9ger-e-le-isole-di-Brissago--1602446.html))

### Social and institutional creations

- **After 1885:** *Isole St. Léger post office*. Saint Léger established an island postal office with its own stamps and cancellations, including an “Isole St Leger” postmark. This was an institution and identity-making apparatus rather than merely a text; the surviving stamps and postal marks are manifestations of it. ([Frassi](https://archividonneticino.ch/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Saint_Leger_Antonietta_web.pdf), p. 2; [Desmond](https://www.british-italian.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/RIVISTA-2018_19-web-6.pdf), p. 15)

- **Circa 1886–1914, with later continuations:** *Cultural gathering-place on the Isole di Brissago*. Through invitations, hospitality, music-making and patronage, Saint Léger made her estate into a meeting place for artists, musicians and writers. This is better understood as a social creation or continuing cultural project than as a bounded obra; individual visitors made their own works, which should not be reassigned to her. ([Frassi](https://archividonneticino.ch/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Saint_Leger_Antonietta_web.pdf), p. 2; [Swiss Historical Dictionary](https://hls-dhs-dss.ch/fr/articles/002092/2024-01-22/); [RSI](https://www.rsi.ch/archivi/Antonietta-di-Saint-L%C3%A9ger-e-le-isole-di-Brissago--1602446.html))

- **Date unknown, before 1928:** *Art library and collection*. Saint Léger assembled a substantial library and a collection of artworks and art objects in the island villa. A collection is made through selection and arrangement but is not authorship of its constituent objects; it was later dispersed, and the consulted scholarly inventory supplies no complete catalogue. ([Frassi](https://archividonneticino.ch/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Saint_Leger_Antonietta_web.pdf), p. 2; [Desmond](https://www.british-italian.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/RIVISTA-2018_19-web-6.pdf), p. 15)

### Industrial projects and manufactured things

- **Date unknown, before 1928:** *Doll workshop and dolls*. Saint Léger had a workshop built on the islands for producing dolls, an activity connected with her personal enthusiasm for them. The dolls are manufactured objects and the workshop an entrepreneurial project; the consulted scholarly account gives neither individual titles nor evidence for a surviving complete production list. ([Frassi](https://archividonneticino.ch/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Saint_Leger_Antonietta_web.pdf), pp. 2–3)

- **Date unknown:** *Project for extracting alcohol from peat*. An industrial project pursued by Saint Léger in an attempt to restore her finances. The evidence describes the intention and unsuccessful enterprise, not a securely documented finished product, so it should remain classified as a project rather than a completed obra. ([Frassi](https://archividonneticino.ch/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Saint_Leger_Antonietta_web.pdf), p. 2)

- **Date unknown:** *Liqueur factory*. Another unsuccessful commercial undertaking attributed to Saint Léger. No product name, formula or demonstrably surviving bottle is supplied by the standard study; what can safely be listed is the factory project, not an invented brand or finished work. ([Frassi](https://archividonneticino.ch/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Saint_Leger_Antonietta_web.pdf), p. 2)
