## Works by Taj al-Din Ali ibn Muhammad Ibn al-Durayhim al-Mawsili

### Qurʾanic exegesis, letters, and recitation

- *Al-Nasamāt al-fāʾiḥa fī āyāt al-Fātiḥa* (النسمات الفائحة في آيات الفاتحة). A treatise on the verses of the opening sura; it is known from al-Ṣafadī’s author-derived bibliography, while its survival and completeness are uncertain. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Ishrāq al-nafs fī al-ḥamdalāt al-khams* (إشراق النفس في الحمدلات الخمس). An exegetical study of five Qurʾanic expressions of praise; some later witnesses corrupt the title as *Ishrāf al-nafs*. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Al-Dirāya fī “mā nansakh min āya”* (الدراية في «ما ننسخ من آية»). A focused exegetical treatise on Qurʾan 2:106 and abrogation, known presently as a bibliographical obra rather than a securely identified surviving text. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Al-Sabīl al-awṭā fī al-ṣalāt al-wusṭā* (السبيل الأوطى في الصلاة الوسطى). A topical exegesis concerning the “middle prayer”; the transmitted title has minor orthographic uncertainty. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Laṭāʾif al-aḥkām ʿalā “huwa alladhī yuṣawwirukum fī al-arḥām”* (لطائف الأحكام على «هو الذي يصوركم في الأرحام»). An exegetical-theological treatment of Qurʾan 3:6, surviving status unknown. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Muddat al-buḥūr ʿalā “inna ʿiddata al-shuhūr”* (مدة البحور على «إن عدة الشهور»). A topical commentary on Qurʾan 9:36, apparently relating months to calendrical or astronomical learning; the exact sense of its title is uncertain. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Iḍāʾat Nūḥ ʿalā “wa-yasʾalūnaka ʿan al-rūḥ”* (إضاءة نوح على «ويسألونك عن الروح»). A treatise on Qurʾan 17:85; the reading *Nūḥ* may be textual corruption, so the title resists confident translation. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Mathānī al-kitāb al-mubīn ʿalā anna qalba al-Qurʾān “Yā Sīn”* (مثاني الكتاب المبين على أن قلب القرآن «يس»). An exegetical work on Sūrat Yā Sīn and its traditional designation as the Qurʾan’s “heart.” [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Al-Āthār al-rāʾiʿa fī asrār al-Wāqiʿa* (الآثار الرائعة في أسرار الواقعة). An esoteric or exegetical treatment of Sūrat al-Wāqiʿa, known by title but not securely documented as extant. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Manām al-naṣūḥ fī al-kalām ʿalā Sūrat Nūḥ* (منام النصوح في الكلام على سورة نوح). A commentary on Sūrat Nūḥ; the word *manām* in the transmitted title may itself require emendation. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Asrār al-badr fī Laylat al-Qadr* (أسرار البدر في ليلة القدر). A devotional-exegetical treatise on the Night of Power, possibly also engaging calendrical correspondences. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Al-Iftiḥāṣ ʿalā Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ* (الافتحاص على سورة الإخلاص). A commentary on Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ; both the unusual first word and the work’s survival are uncertain. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Al-ʿIbārāt fī ikhtiyār al-qirāʾāt* (العبارات في اختيار القراءات). A study selecting or comparing Qurʾanic readings, rather than a general commentary. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Munāsabat al-ḥisāb fī asmāʾ al-anbiyāʾ al-madhkūrīn fī al-kitāb* (مناسبة الحساب في أسماء الأنبياء المذكورين في الكتاب). A numerical study of prophets’ names in the Qurʾan, combining exegesis with letter-number calculation. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Naẓāʾir al-āyāt wa-l-kalimāt al-mukarrarāt* (نظائر الآيات والكلمات المكررات). A concordance-like study of parallel verses and repeated Qurʾanic words. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Kanz al-durar fī ḥurūf awāʾil al-suwar* (كنز الدرر في حروف أوائل السور). A treatise on the disconnected letters opening certain suras, situated between exegesis and the occult science of letters. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Sibr al-ṣarf fī sirr al-ḥarf* (سبر الصرف في سر الحرف). A linguistic and esoteric inquiry into morphology and the properties of letters; some later references give variant readings of the first word. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Al-Zayn fī maʿānī al-ʿayn* (الزين في معاني العين). A lexical or letter-science work on the meanings of *ʿayn*, whose many senses make it resistant to a narrower classification. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Al-Munāsabāt al-ʿadadiyya fī al-asmāʾ al-Muḥammadiyya* (المناسبات العددية في الأسماء المحمدية). A numerical and letter-symbolic study of names associated with Muḥammad. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Ghāyat al-maghnam fī al-ism al-aʿẓam* (غاية المغنم في الاسم الأعظم). A devotional and esoteric treatise on the “Greatest Name” of God; manuscript copies survive, although their dates do not securely date the composition. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html), [manuscript notice](https://makrim.org/manuscrits.cfm?PN=296&aut=&bib=0&nbr_man=0&order=6&suj=0&tr=)

### Theology, interreligious polemic, and confessional argument

- *Al-Burhān ʿalā ʿadam al-naskh li-l-millatayn min al-Qurʾān* (البرهان على عدم النسخ للملتين من القرآن). A polemical theological treatise concerning whether earlier religious communities or laws were abrogated by the Qurʾan; the precise force of the title is debatable. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Madāḥiḍ al-ikrāh fī tanāquḍ al-Tawrāh* (مداحض الإكراه في تناقض التوراة). An anti-Jewish scriptural polemic alleging contradictions in the Torah; it is a polemical act as much as a descriptive text. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Taʿrīf al-tabdīl fī taḥrīf al-Injīl* (تعريف التبديل في تحريف الإنجيل). An anti-Christian polemic on the alleged alteration of the Gospel, known from the authorial title list. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Naẓm Nafāʾis ʿuqūd al-durar al-zāhira fī hadm kanāʾis al-Yahūd bi-Miṣr wa-l-Qāhira* (نظم نفائس عقود الدرر الزاهرة في هدم كنائس اليهود بمصر والقاهرة). A versification advocating or commemorating the destruction of Jewish places of worship in Egypt and Cairo; its title makes it a polemical manifesto rather than neutral description. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Al-Yaqīn li-man bihi nadīn fī al-dalīl ʿalā khilāfat al-khulafāʾ al-rāshidīn* (اليقين لمن به ندين في الدليل على خلافة الخلفاء الراشدين). A Sunni confessional argument defending the legitimacy of the rightly guided caliphs. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Ikhlāṣ al-khawāṣṣ* (إخلاص الخواص). A theological, devotional, or esoteric work listed without further description; the title alone does not permit firmer classification. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Manhaj al-ṣawāb fī qubḥ istiktāb ahl al-kitāb* (منهج الصواب في قبح استكتاب أهل الكتاب). A juridical-political polemic opposing the employment of Jews and Christians as state secretaries. It is absent under this title from al-Ṣafadī’s list but survives in manuscripts attributed to Ibn al-Durayhim; it should not be confused with two unrelated treatises appended to a modern edition. [Princeton manuscript](https://digital-collections.princeton.edu/i/manhaj-als%CC%A3awa%CC%84b-fi%CC%84-qubh%CC%A3-istikta%CC%84/item/beaf44fa-ab9e-46ef-83eb-30650c79e098/metadata), [edition contents and authorships](https://www.muslim-library.com/arabic/%D9%85%D9%86%D9%87%D8%AC-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B5%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D9%82%D8%A8%D8%AD-%D8%A5%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D8%A7%D9%87%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D8%8C/)

### Law, ritual, Hadith, and inheritance

- *Zajr al-sufahāʾ bi-ādāb al-fuqahāʾ* (زجر السفهاء بآداب الفقهاء). A disciplinary or ethical protocol presenting the conduct expected of jurists. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Ikhtiyār al-rāghib fī ikhtiyār al-madhāhib* (اختيار الراغب في اختيار المذاهب). A comparative legal work about choosing among schools or doctrines. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Asrār al-ʿibādāt bi-tafṣīl al-iʿādāt* (أسرار العبادات بتفصيل الإعادات). A work on the inward meanings and repeated performance of ritual acts; its exact scope cannot be recovered from the title alone. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Sharḥ Naẓm al-Ḥāwī* (شرح نظم الحاوي). An unfinished commentary on a versified form of the Shāfiʿī legal manual *al-Ḥāwī*; al-Ṣafadī explicitly records it as incomplete, so the project—not a finished book—is the obra. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Fāʾidat al-abḥāth bi-aḥwāl al-wālida fī al-mīrāth* (فائدة الأبحاث بأحوال الوالدة في الميراث). A focused inheritance-law study of the mother’s shares or legal situations. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Mansak al-ḥajj* (منسك الحج). A pilgrimage manual: a protocol prescribing or organizing the rites of the hajj. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Al-Radd ʿalā Taqī al-Dīn al-Ikhnāʾī al-Mālikī fī masāʾil mutafarriqa* (الرد على تقي الدين الإخنائي المالكي في مسائل متفرقة). A disputational response to the Mālikī jurist al-Ikhnāʾī on assorted questions. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Al-Muʿlim bi-muʿāḍadāt Muslim* (المعلم بمعاضدات مسلم). A Hadith study apparently assembling corroborating material for Muslim’s collection; the exact reading and relationship to similarly titled Hadith works remain uncertain. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Al-Khīra fī ḥusn masāʾil al-faʾl wa-karāhiyat al-ṭīra* (الخيرة في حسن مسائل الفأل وكراهية الطيرة). A religious treatment distinguishing acceptable good omens from condemned augury. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Nafʿ al-jadwā fī al-jamʿ bayna aḥādīth al-ʿadwā* (نفع الجدوى في الجمع بين أحاديث العدوى). A Hadith-reconciliation treatise addressing apparently conflicting reports about contagion. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Shawāriq al-anwār fī sharḥ Lawāmiʿ al-anwār* (شوارق الأنوار في شرح لوامع الأنوار). An unfinished commentary on a work titled *Lawāmiʿ al-anwār*; al-Ṣafadī records an incomplete project rather than a completed text. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Taʿrīf al-ifrād li-l-muqtadī li-tatimmat al-Dhahabī wa-l-Ṣafadī* (تعريف الإفراد للمقتدي لتتمة الذهبي والصفدي). An uncertainly titled scholarly supplement connected with al-Dhahabī and al-Ṣafadī; neither its genre nor the correct reading of *al-ifrād* is secure. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Al-Manāsakhāt al-Miṣriyya* (المناسخات المصرية). A collection of Egyptian inheritance problems involving successive deaths and redistribution of estates. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Al-Ajwiba al-Qāhiriyya* (الأجوبة القاهرية). A collection of answers produced in Cairo, probably legal or mathematical responses; it is a dossier rather than necessarily a unified authored book. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Al-Manāsakhāt al-Ḥalabiyya* (المناسخات الحلبية). A collection of Aleppine successive-inheritance problems. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Al-Ajwiba al-Ḥalabiyya* (الأجوبة الحلبية). A collection of answers associated with Aleppo; its internal unity and exact field are not stated. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Al-Ajwiba al-Ḥamawiyya* (الأجوبة الحموية). A collection of responses associated with Hama, surviving status unknown. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Al-Ajwiba al-Mawṣiliyya* (الأجوبة الموصلية). A collection of responses associated with Mosul; it may represent accumulated answers rather than a deliberately composed book. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Al-Ajwiba al-Shāmiyya* (الأجوبة الشامية). An unfinished collection of Syrian or Damascene answers; because al-Ṣafadī marks it incomplete, it is best classified as a project. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)

### Grammar, rhetoric, verse, and literary criticism

- *Al-Nukhab fī al-taṣādīq wa-l-khuṭab* (النخب في التصاديق والخطب). An anthology or handbook of formal attestations and speeches; the first technical term is textually uncertain. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Khulāṣat al-Khulāṣa*. A roughly five-hundred-line versification or epitome, probably relating to Ibn Mālik’s grammatical *al-Khulāṣa*; the catalogue supplies the approximate length but not secure evidence of survival. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Tashīl al-Tashīl* (تسهيل التسهيل). A grammatical abridgment or clarification of Ibn Mālik’s *Tashīl al-fawāʾid*. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Al-ʿArūḍ al-mujmala min al-masmūʿa wa-l-muhmala* (العروض المجملة من المسموعة والمهملة). A compact prosodic study distinguishing attested from unused metrical possibilities. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Al-Zinād al-qādiḥ fī ikhtiyār al-ṣādiḥ* (الزناد القادح في اختيار الصادح). A work of poetic or vocal selection whose precise object is unclear from its rhymed title. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Dhāt al-qawāfī* (ذات القوافي). A thirty-line panegyric poem in praise of Muḥammad, constructed so that each line deploys a distinct rhyme; it is a formal poetic object, not a “book.” [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html), [specialist study](https://arabacademy-sy.org/uploads/magazine/mag77/mag77-2-1.pdf)
- *Al-Qaṣīda al-alfiyya* (القصيدة الألفية). A thousand-line poem or poem entitled “The Alfiyya”; its subject, date, and survival are not stated in the authorial list. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Tanwīl al-rāthī fī taʾwīl al-marāthī* (تنويل الراثي في تأويل المراثي). A literary or rhetorical treatment of elegies and their interpretation. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Iftiqād al-kharāʾid al-mawāhir fī intiqād Farāʾid al-jawāhir* (افتقاد الخرائد المواهر في انتقاد فرائد الجواهر). A critical response to a work titled *Farāʾid al-jawāhir*; several words in the alliterative title have variant or doubtful readings. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Al-Tarassulāt al-Shāmiyya* (الترسلات الشامية). A body or collection of Syrian official and literary correspondence. Its resistance to being called one work is material: it may be an assembled epistolary practice rather than a single composition. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Risālat al-nuṣḥ al-ʿAlawī ʿalā lisān al-Jāmiʿ al-Umawī* (رسالة النصح العلوي على لسان الجامع الأموي). A personification in which the Umayyad Mosque delivers counsel, combining epistle, sermon, and literary prosopopoeia. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Poetic corpus*. Al-Ṣafadī describes Ibn al-Durayhim as producing much verse, often strained or artificial, but does not constitute it as a titled *dīwān*; this body of occasional poetry therefore resists enumeration as a single fixed work. [Biographical notice](https://elibrary.mediu.edu.my/books/BSH0048.pdf)

### Cryptology, riddles, and combinatorial texts

- *Īḍāḥ al-mubham fī ḥall al-mutarjam* (إيضاح المبهم في حل المترجم). A cryptanalytic protocol for deciphering encoded writing. Al-Ṣafadī and Ibn Ḥajar shorten the title to *Al-Mubham fī ḥall al-mutarjam*, while the fuller form occurs in Ibn al-Durayhim’s own cross-reference. [Title analysis](https://ar.wikisource.org/wiki/%D8%B5%D9%81%D8%AD%D8%A9:%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%85_%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9_%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AC_%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%89_%D8%B9%D9%86%D8%AF_%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%B1%D8%A8_%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B2%D8%A1_%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%88%D9%84.pdf/109)
- *Mukhtaṣar al-Mubham fī ḥall al-mutarjam* (مختصر المبهم في حل المترجم). An abridgment of the preceding cryptanalytic work and therefore a distinct redaction, not simply another edition. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Miftāḥ al-kunūz fī īḍāḥ al-marmūz* (مفتاح الكنوز في إيضاح المرموز). A systematic cryptological treatise describing methods of encipherment and decipherment. Al-Ṣafadī’s transmitted text has *Miftāḥ al-nuz/kunūz fī ḥall al-rumūz*, but the manuscript title supports the form used here. [Specialist study](https://cp.alukah.net/culture/2/2661/%D8%A7%D8%A8%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%87%D9%85-%D9%88%D8%AC%D9%87%D9%88%D8%AF%D9%87-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B4%D9%81%D9%8A%D8%B1/), [historical analysis](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/01611194.2014.885801)
- *Ghāyat al-iʿjāz fī al-aḥājī wa-l-alghāz* (غاية الإعجاز في الأحاجي والألغاز). A collection or manual of riddles and verbal puzzles, belonging simultaneously to recreation, pedagogy, and combinatorial language practice. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Mushājarat man intaṣara fī al-mufākhara bayna al-samʿ wa-l-baṣar* (مشاجرة من انتصر في المفاخرة بين السمع والبصر). A literary debate or riddle staging a contest between hearing and sight. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Ikhtibāʾ al-ikhtibār* (اختباء الاختبار). An enigmatically titled work probably involving testing, concealment, or puzzle craft; its form cannot safely be inferred beyond that. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)

### Mathematics, magic squares, optics, and divinatory sciences

- *Iqnāʿ al-ḥudhāq fī anwāʿ al-awfāq* (إقناع الحذاق في أنواع الأوفاق). A manual on varieties of magic squares and numerical configurations. Sources variously print *Iqnāʿ/Itisāq/Iqtināʿ* and *al-ḥudhāq/al-ḥuddāq*; these are best treated as corrupt variants of one work, not several. [Variant analysis](https://ar.wikisource.org/wiki/%D8%B5%D9%81%D8%AD%D8%A9:%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%85_%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9_%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AC_%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%89_%D8%B9%D9%86%D8%AF_%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%B1%D8%A8_%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B2%D8%A1_%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%88%D9%84.pdf/109), [bibliographical notice](https://scribetools.com/en/library/hadith/%D9%83%D8%B4%D9%81-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B8%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86/kz1-e42c6efd/text/0006)
- *Basṭ al-fawāʾid fī sharḥ Ḥisāb al-qawāʿid* (بسط الفوائد في شرح حساب القواعد). A mathematical commentary or expansion on a work called *Ḥisāb al-qawāʿid*; shortened sources omit *sharḥ*. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Sharḥ al-Isʿardiyya fī al-ḥisāb* (شرح الإسعردية في الحساب). A commentary on an arithmetic poem or manual associated with Siʿird/Isʿard. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Al-Masʾala al-ḥajmiyya* (المسألة الحجموية). A mathematical problem concerning volume; whether it was a short problem-text or an independent treatise is unknown. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Tanāʾī al-manāẓir fī al-marāyā wa-l-manāẓir* (تنائي المناظر في المرايا والمناظر). A work on mirrors and optics; the first word appears in substantially corrupted forms in later sources, including *Tanbīh* and *Thunāʾī*. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html), [Ibn Ḥajar’s derivative list](https://elibrary.mediu.edu.my/books/BSH0048.pdf)
- *Sullam al-ḥirāsa fī ʿilm al-firāsa* (سلم الحراسة في علم الفراسة). A manual of physiognomy; some witnesses read *Sullam al-ḥadāsa*, so the exact rhyming title is disputed. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Taṣārīf al-dahr fī taʿārīf al-zajr* (تصاريف الدهر في تعاريف الزجر). A divinatory work on interpreting omens or deterrent signs; it functions as a protocol for reading events rather than merely describing them. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Taqrīr al-abḥāth fī khawāṣṣ al-muwalladāt al-thalāth* (تقرير الأبحاث في خواص المولدات الثلاث). A natural-philosophical or occult-properties treatise on the three classes of generated things—commonly minerals, plants, and animals. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)

### Medicine, natural history, and the body

- *Al-Isāʾa bi-adwāʾ al-nisāʾ wa-l-bāh* (الإساءة بأدواء النساء والباه). A medical or sexual-health treatise concerning women’s diseases and sexual capacity; the transmitted first word may be corrupt. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- **755 AH / 1354:** *Kitāb Manāfiʿ al-ḥayawān* (كتاب منافع الحيوان). An illustrated bestiary and materia-medica compilation, not simply an authored prose “book”: Ibn al-Durayhim acted as compiler, bringing earlier zoological and therapeutic traditions into a manuscript containing animal images. The Escorial manuscript names him and dates the compilation, while attribution of the underlying materials belongs to the older Ibn Bakhtīshūʿ tradition. [Anna Contadini’s manuscript study](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Anna-Contadini/publication/281652677_Contadini_Anna_1989_%27The_Kitab_Manafi_al-Hayawan_in_the_Escorial_Library%27_Islamic_Art_3_pp_33-57/links/585325dd08ae7d33e01ab5d2/Contadini-Anna-1989-The-Kitab-Manafi-al-Hayawan-in-the-Escorial-Library-Islamic-Art-3-pp-33-57.pdf), [Harvard description of the facsimile](https://archive.blogs.harvard.edu/finearts/2012/05/29/a-medieval-islamic-bestiary/)

### Ethics, governance, hospitality, and social protocols

- *Al-Inṣāf bi-l-dalīl fī awṣāf māʾ al-Nīl* (الإنصاف بالدليل في أوصاف ماء النيل). A descriptive or scientific-literary treatment of Nile water; some later bibliographies omit *māʾ*, changing the apparent subject from water to the Nile generally. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Mukāfaʾat al-aqdār li-l-akhyār wa-l-ashrār* (مكافأة الأقدار للأخيار والأشرار). A moralizing work on the recompense allotted by fate or providence to good and evil people. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Bawādir al-ḥulūm fī nawādir al-ʿulūm* (بوادر الحلوم في نوادر العلوم). A miscellany of unusual learning or intellectual curiosities, resisting assignment to a single discipline. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Risālat al-tarāḍī bayna al-amīr wa-l-qāḍī* (رسالة التراضي بين الأمير والقاضي). An epistle dramatizing or regulating relations between political and judicial authority; later sources sometimes corrupt *al-tarāḍī* and *al-amīr*. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Siyādat al-khiyam* (سيادة الخيم). An independently listed but opaque title; neither its genre nor even the stability of its reading is established, so it resists being called a recoverable work beyond its catalogue manifestation. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Al-Baṣāra fī adab al-akl wa-l-ziyāra* (البصارة في أدب الأكل والزيارة). A conduct manual giving protocols for eating and visiting. Its punctuation in the source leaves a slight possibility that it was joined to the preceding title. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Naẓm al-sulūk fī ādāb al-mulūk* (نظم السلوك في آداب الملوك). A mirror-for-princes or courtly-conduct text, possibly in verse, prescribing royal behavior. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
- *Īqāẓ al-muṣīb fī al-shaṭranj wa-l-manāṣib* (إيقاظ المصيب في الشطرنج والمناصيب). A chess treatise concerning play, positions, or problems; the variant *mā fī al-shaṭranj min al-manāṣib* occurs in Ibn Ḥajar’s derivative list. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html), [Ibn Ḥajar](https://elibrary.mediu.edu.my/books/BSH0048.pdf)
- *Al-Ināfa ʿalā al-ḍiyāfa* (الإنافة على الضيافة). A hospitality manual or literary treatment of hosting, operating as social protocol. [Catalogue](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/aaian-alasr-009.html)
