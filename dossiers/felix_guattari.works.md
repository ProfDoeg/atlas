## Works by Pierre-Félix Guattari

### Books and author-made collections

- **1972:** *Psychanalyse et transversalité: Essais d’analyse institutionnelle*. A collection of Guattari’s earlier essays and interventions, with a preface by Gilles Deleuze; it is an assembled theoretical obra rather than a newly composed monograph. [1]
- **1977:** *La Révolution moléculaire*. An authorial assemblage of political, psychiatric, and semiotic writings; the substantially rearranged 1980 edition is a different configuration of the same textual project, not a wholly separate work. [1][2]
- **1979:** *L’Inconscient machinique: Essais de schizo-analyse*. A theoretical book proposing machinic rather than representational models of the unconscious. [1]
- **1979:** *Lignes de fuite: Pour un autre monde de possibles*. A book-length manuscript contemporaneous with *L’Inconscient machinique* but left unpublished during Guattari’s lifetime; its 2011 edition, prefaced by Liane Mozère, is therefore a posthumously manifested obra whose final authorial status is uncertain. [1][2]
- **1980–1985; published 1985/1986:** *Les Années d’hiver: 1980–1985*. A politically situated collection of articles rather than a continuous treatise; sources differ between 1985 and 1986 for the first edition. [1][2]
- **1989:** *Cartographies schizoanalytiques*. A book of theoretical cartographies incorporating work on subjectivity, art, literature, photography, and architecture. [1][2]
- **1989:** *Les Trois Écologies*. A compact ecosophical manifesto joining environmental, social, and mental ecology; it acts programmatically as much as it describes. [1][2]
- **1992:** *Chaosmose*. Guattari’s final lifetime book, an ethico-aesthetic theoretical obra developed partly from previously published essays. [1][2]

### Books made with others

- **1972:** *L’Anti-Œdipe: Capitalisme et schizophrénie 1*. Made with Gilles Deleuze, this is a jointly produced theoretical and political book whose collective enunciation resists division into separately attributable contributions. The expanded 1973 issue is an edition, not another obra. [1][3]
- **1975:** *Kafka: Pour une littérature mineure*. A critical-theoretical book made with Deleuze, treating Kafka’s writing as a collective, political machine rather than conventional literary biography. [1][3]
- **1976:** *Rhizome: Introduction*. A freestanding book made with Deleuze and later incorporated, in revised form, into *Mille plateaux*; it is both an independent manifestation and a component of a larger obra. [1][3]
- **1977:** *Politique et psychanalyse*. A pamphlet-like collaborative assemblage involving Guattari, Deleuze, Claire Parnet, and André Scala; its components include the collectively credited “L’interprétation des énoncés.” [1][3]
- **1980:** *Mille plateaux: Capitalisme et schizophrénie 2*. Made with Deleuze, this deliberately non-linear assemblage of “plateaus” approaches a textual Gesamtkunstwerk: philosophy, political analysis, linguistics, ethnology, music, and diagrams are made inseparable. [1][3]
- **1985:** *Les Nouveaux Espaces de liberté*. A political manifesto-dialogue made with Toni Negri, supplemented by separate texts by each author; attribution therefore belongs partly to the pair and partly to the named individual contributions. [1][2]
- **1985:** *Pratique de l’institutionnel et politique*. A book containing three separate interviews with Jean Oury, François Tosquelles, and Guattari; Guattari made one conversational component, not the entire volume as a single-author work. [2]
- **1986:** *Micropolitiques*. A Portuguese-language assemblage produced through Guattari’s discussions in Brazil and Suely Rolnik’s texts and editorial work; its dialogic and editorial authorship resists classification as a Guattari monograph. [2]
- **1991:** *Qu’est-ce que la philosophie?* Made with Deleuze, this jointly composed philosophical book defines philosophy through the creation of concepts. [1][3]

### Screenplays and unrealized cinema projects

- **circa 1977:** *Projet de film au sujet des radios libres*. An archival screenplay or film outline concerning free radio; it was not realized, and its title describes a project rather than a completed film. [6]
- **1979:** *Untitled film project concerning two Italian fugitives escaping to France*. An archival film outline made with Robert Kramer; no secure title is reported, so one should not be invented. [6]
- **1980–1987:** *Un amour d’UIQ*. An unrealized science-fiction film project surviving in multiple scripts: the first two versions were made with Robert Kramer, while the last was substantially Guattari’s. Its unfinishedness is the work’s defining condition—the obra is the changing screenplay-project, not a missing completed movie. [7][8]

### Poetry and unpublished literary projects

- **date not established:** *Crac en plan, pas un pli*. An unpublished poetry collection preserved in the IMEC archive; without a settled publication or established date, it remains an archival project rather than a finalized public book. [4]
- **composition date not established; published 1999:** *Ritournelles*. Besides its autobiographical-prose status, scholarship describes it as poetic and surrealist; its refusal of a stable genre is part of the obra. [1][4]

### Collective institutions, periodicals, and social machines

- **1965–1987:** *Centre d’études, de recherches et de formation institutionnelles (CERFI)*. A research collective founded by Guattari and sustained by many collaborators; it is a social and institutional project, not his individually authored work. Its collective production included the journal *Recherches*. [9]
- **1965:** *Société de psychothérapie institutionnelle*. An organization created by Guattari ten years after he began working at La Borde; its obra was an ongoing institutional practice rather than a bounded object. [9]
- **1966–1983:** *Recherches*. A journal and collective research apparatus closely associated with CERFI and Guattari; authorship belongs to shifting collectives, so the journal cannot be reduced to a Guattari work even though he helped make its conditions and contributed texts. [9]
- **1987–1992:** *Chimères*. A journal founded by Guattari and Deleuze and made with an editorial collective; it is best understood as an enduring platform or collective assemblage rather than a book or individually authored oeuvre. [9]
- **1955–1992:** *Guattari’s institutional practice at Clinique de La Borde*. Guattari did not found or singly “make” La Borde, but his clubs, organizational experiments, seminars, analyses, and daily work helped continually remake it; calling the clinic itself his work would erase Jean Oury, patients, staff, and the collective institutional process. [9]
