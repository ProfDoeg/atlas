## Works by Paolo Soleri

### Architectural works and realized environments

- **1949–1950:** *Dome House*. Experimental desert residence at Cave Creek, Arizona, commissioned by Leonora Woods and built from inexpensive and reused materials; the Museum of Modern Art subsequently requested a model for a traveling exhibition. ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/paolo-soleri_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))
- **1951–1954:** *Ceramica Artistica Solimene*. Working ceramics factory at Vietri sul Mare and Soleri’s only realized building in Italy; its projected bridge across the road was not built, so the surviving factory is an incomplete manifestation of the original project. ([Italian Ministry of Culture](https://censimentoarchitetturecontemporanee.cultura.gov.it/scheda-opera?id=3470), [Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/paolo-soleri_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))
- **1956–1974:** *Cosanti*. Residence, studio, foundry, gallery, experimental landscape and construction laboratory in Paradise Valley, Arizona. Built incrementally with students and artisans, it is best understood as a collaborative Gesamtkunstwerk rather than a single autonomous building. ([SAH Archipedia](https://sah-archipedia.org/buildings/AZ-01-013-0028), [Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/paolo-soleri_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))
- **1956:** *Earth House*. Partly subterranean residence and the first of Cosanti’s documented earth-cast structures. ([Arcology and Arcosanti](https://stax.strath.ac.uk/downloads/8p58pd63g?locale=en))
- **1958:** *Ceramics Apse*. Earth-cast ceramic workshop at Cosanti, simultaneously a production space and an experiment in apsidal form. ([Arcology and Arcosanti](https://stax.strath.ac.uk/downloads/8p58pd63g?locale=en))
- **1961:** *North Studio*. Earth-cast studio at Cosanti, realized through the workshop labor that makes individual authorship less tidy than the conventional “building by Soleri” formulation. ([Arcology and Arcosanti](https://stax.strath.ac.uk/downloads/8p58pd63g?locale=en))
- **1964:** *Metal Studio*. Earth-cast metalworking structure at Cosanti. ([Arcology and Arcosanti](https://stax.strath.ac.uk/downloads/8p58pd63g?locale=en))
- **1964:** *North Apse*. Earth-cast apsidal workspace at Cosanti. ([Arcology and Arcosanti](https://stax.strath.ac.uk/downloads/8p58pd63g?locale=en))
- **1965:** *Cat House*. Earth-cast residential structure at Cosanti. ([Arcology and Arcosanti](https://stax.strath.ac.uk/downloads/8p58pd63g?locale=en))
- **1966:** *Pool Canopy*. Earth-cast shelter at Cosanti; it is architectural infrastructure rather than an independent building. ([Arcology and Arcosanti](https://stax.strath.ac.uk/downloads/8p58pd63g?locale=en))
- **1967:** *Pumpkin Apse*. Earth-cast apsidal structure at Cosanti. ([Arcology and Arcosanti](https://stax.strath.ac.uk/downloads/8p58pd63g?locale=en))
- **1968:** *Student Apse*. Earth-cast communal structure made in conjunction with Cosanti’s student workshops. ([Arcology and Arcosanti](https://stax.strath.ac.uk/downloads/8p58pd63g?locale=en))
- **1966–1970:** *Paolo Soleri Amphitheater*. Outdoor theater at the Institute of American Indian Arts in Santa Fe, conceived with the institution and its Indigenous theater program; the commission therefore resists attribution as Soleri’s unaided work. ([A History of the Paolo Soleri Amphitheater](https://paolosoleriamphitheaterhistory.com/construction/))
- **1970–2013:** *Arcosanti*. Experimental “urban laboratory” near Mayer, Arizona: part building site, part community, part educational protocol and part demonstration of arcology. Construction involved generations of workshop participants, and only a fraction of the projected city was realized, so the project—not a finished city—is the obra. ([Arcosanti](https://arcosanti.org/vision/the-arcosanti-story/), [Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/paolo-soleri_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))
- **1974:** *Antioch Building*. The last of the ten Cosanti structures identified in the earth-casting literature, made through the site’s workshop practice. ([Arcology and Arcosanti](https://stax.strath.ac.uk/downloads/8p58pd63g?locale=en))
- **1982:** *DeConcini House*. Private residence in Phoenix, Arizona; published dates sometimes identify the commission by this single year without distinguishing design from completion. ([Paolo Soleri—major projects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paolo_Soleri#Major_projects))
- **1986:** *Arizona Cancer Center Chapel*. Chapel at the University of Arizona medical campus in Tucson. ([Paolo Soleri—major projects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paolo_Soleri#Major_projects))
- **1995:** *Glendale Community College Amphitheater*. Outdoor performance structure in Glendale, Arizona. ([Paolo Soleri—major projects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paolo_Soleri#Major_projects))
- **2000:** *Arcosanti/Venice Precast Wall*. Architectural installation made for the Venice Architecture Biennale; it is a wall, exhibition object and demonstration, not a building. The Biennale catalogue misspells “Arcosanti” as “Arcostanti.” ([La Biennale di Venezia archive](https://asac.labiennale.org/persone/422114))
- **2010:** *Soleri Bridge and Plaza*. Pedestrian bridge, solar-calendar marker and public plaza in Scottsdale, Arizona; realized late in Soleri’s life from a much longer investigation of bridge forms. ([Paolo Soleri—major projects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paolo_Soleri#Major_projects))

### Unbuilt architectural projects and urban propositions

- **1948–1949:** *The Beast*. Prestressed-concrete bridge project devised while Soleri was associated with Frank Lloyd Wright; Elizabeth Mock published it in *The Architecture of Bridges* in 1949. It survives as a project and representation, not a bridge. ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/paolo-soleri_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))
- **early 1950s:** *Convivium*. Unbuilt residence projected for the Amalfi coast; flooding and unfavorable circumstances contributed to its abandonment. ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/paolo-soleri_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))
- **early 1950s:** *Sciuscella*. Unbuilt Amalfi-coast residence developed during Soleri’s Italian period. Its precise date is not fixed in the consulted scholarly biography. ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/paolo-soleri_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))
- **1957:** *Omega Residential Bridge*. Inhabited-bridge proposal represented by architectural drawings; no bridge was built. ([Museum of Modern Art](https://www.moma.org/artists/8101-paolo-soleri))
- **1958–1967:** *Mesa City*. Theoretical regional city for approximately two million inhabitants and the matrix from which Soleri’s arcology concept developed; it exists through drawings, models and publications rather than construction. ([Arcology research chronology](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/312160731_Ecopolis_Arcology_and_Arcosanti_Planning_and_Design_Principles_Presentation_at_Universita_degli_Studi_di_Firenze_Florence_Italy), [Arcosanti archive publication](https://arcosanti.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Sketchbook3_Final.pdf))
- **published 1969:** *Novanoah I*. Marine arcology project included among the thirty designs in *Arcology*; a speculative city, not a realized building. ([*Arcology*, contents](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Novanoah II*. Second marine arcology proposition in the canonical thirty-project sequence. ([*Arcology*, contents](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Noahbabel*. Unbuilt arcology represented through drawings, diagrams and quantified design data. ([*Arcology*, contents](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Babelnoah*. Unbuilt arcology proposition combining the “Babel” and marine-city design families. ([*Arcology*, contents](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Arcoforte*. Fortified or strongly bounded arcology study; its obra is the project and its representations. ([*Arcology*, contents](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Babel IIA*. First of the published Babel II variants, conceived as a complete city-scale organism. ([*Arcology*, contents](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Arcvillage I*. Relatively small arcology proposition that tests Soleri’s urban principles at village scale. ([*Arcology*, contents](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Logology*. Unbuilt arcology project; the title identifies an intellectual and urban construct rather than a commissioned building. ([*Arcology*, contents](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Babel IIB*. Second published Babel II variation, an unbuilt megastructural city. ([*Arcology*, contents](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Babel IIC*. Third published Babel II variation, documented through plans, sections and comparative data. ([*Arcology*, contents](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Arcanyon*. Canyon-spanning arcology project; a deliberate fusion of infrastructure, landscape and city. ([*Arcology*, contents](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Babel IID*. Fourth published Babel II variation and an unrealized urban organism. ([*Arcology*, contents](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Babel Canyon*. Canyon arcology project whose object is inseparably architectural, infrastructural and territorial. ([*Arcology*, contents](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Arcvillage II*. Second village-scale arcology proposition. ([*Arcology*, contents](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Arckibuz*. Unbuilt arcology in the canonical thirty-project sequence. ([*Arcology*, contents](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Arcollective*. Arcology project framing the city as a collective organism rather than a collection of separate buildings. ([*Arcology*, contents](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Veladiga*. Dam-associated arcology project; city and civil engineering are treated as one work. ([*Arcology*, contents](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Arcodiga*. Arcology-and-dam proposition, unbuilt. ([*Arcology*, contents](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Theodiga*. Cliff-and-dam arcology project represented as part of a larger multi-arcology site plan. ([*Arcology*, project data](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Babeldiga*. Dam-city arcology project, architectural and infrastructural at once. ([*Arcology*, contents](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Stonebow*. Monumental spanning arcology project; it exists as a drawn and modeled proposition. ([*Arcology*, contents](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Arcbeam*. Beam-like linear arcology and infrastructure project. ([*Arcology*, contents](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Infrababel*. Cliff-topography arcology included in the composite site plan with Theodiga, Theology and the two Arcoindian projects. ([*Arcology*, project data](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Arcoindian I*. Arcology proposition whose historical title is Soleri’s; the title should not be mistaken for evidence of Indigenous authorship or consultation. ([*Arcology*, contents](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Arcoindian II*. Second project bearing the historical “Arcoindian” title, likewise an unbuilt Soleri proposition. ([*Arcology*, contents](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Theology*. Cliff-topography arcology project; despite its title, it is a city design rather than a theological text. ([*Arcology*, project data](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Arcube*. Cubic arcology proposal, unbuilt. ([*Arcology*, contents](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Hexahedron*. Geometrically defined arcology project; a speculative city represented in graphic form. ([*Arcology*, contents](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Asteromo*. Space-habitat arcology; it extends the project of the city beyond Earth and therefore resists classification as ordinary architecture. ([*Arcology*, contents](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **published 1969:** *Arcosanti*. The initial arcology proposition published among the thirty designs; it is not identical with every later master plan or with the fragmentary settlement begun in 1970. ([*Arcology*, contents](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology))
- **1975 onward:** *Two Suns Arcology*. Solar-oriented “second-generation” arcology developed through multiple designs rather than one fixed scheme; Soleri later described eight variations. ([Arcology research chronology](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/312160731_Ecopolis_Arcology_and_Arcosanti_Planning_and_Design_Principles_Presentation_at_Universita_degli_Studi_di_Firenze_Florence_Italy), [*What If?*, contents](https://www.rapeutation.com/arch.paolosoleri.toc.htm))
- **1980s:** *Space for Peace*. Space-habitat project and accompanying proposition for redirecting military technology toward extraterrestrial settlement; it is simultaneously an architectural project and an argumentative manifesto. Sources date the monograph to 1984, 1985 and 1989, apparently reflecting presentation and edition history rather than a single secure date. ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/paolo-soleri_(Dizionario-Biografico)/), [scholarly bibliography](https://stax.strath.ac.uk/downloads/8p58pd63g?locale=en))
- **1980s–1990s:** *Ecominutiae*. Family of self-contained space habitats rather than one stable design; consequently it resists treatment as a single finished work. ([*What If?*, contents](https://www.rapeutation.com/arch.paolosoleri.toc.htm))
- **1980s–1990s:** *Hiro-Naga and the Ecominutiae*. Space-habitat project documented in Soleri’s collected writings. ([*What If?*, contents](https://www.rapeutation.com/arch.paolosoleri.toc.htm))
- **1987 and 1993:** *New York Pulse Bridge*. Bridge project represented in at least two dated versions; the Venice Biennale archive lists both, so they should not be silently collapsed into a single fixed design. ([La Biennale di Venezia archive](https://asac.labiennale.org/persone/422114))
- **1991:** *Double Exedra: The Indian School Proposal*. Unpublished Cosanti Foundation paper and architectural proposal; its title appears in the scholarly bibliography, but the project’s precise authorship context and intended site are not fully described there. ([scholarly bibliography](https://stax.strath.ac.uk/downloads/8p58pd63g?locale=en))
- **1992:** *Scottsdale Pedestrian Bridge*. Unbuilt or preliminary bridge project exhibited at the 2000 Venice Architecture Biennale; it belongs to the design lineage eventually manifested in the 2010 bridge. ([La Biennale di Venezia archive](https://asac.labiennale.org/persone/422114))
- **1993:** *Tubular*. Urban or infrastructural design exhibited at the Venice Architecture Biennale; the catalogue record supplies the title and date but not enough information to force a narrower object category. ([La Biennale di Venezia archive](https://asac.labiennale.org/persone/422114))
- **1996:** *Hyper Building*. Commissioned proposal for a kilometer-high arcology intended for the third millennium; it remained a project represented through drawings, models and later digital publication. ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/paolo-soleri_(Dizionario-Biografico)/), [*What If?*, contents](https://www.rapeutation.com/arch.paolosoleri.toc.htm))
- **1997:** *The Paradox Project*. Draft proposal preserved as an unpublished Cosanti Foundation paper; its provisional status belongs to the work rather than constituting a bibliographic defect. ([scholarly bibliography](https://stax.strath.ac.uk/downloads/8p58pd63g?locale=en))
- **late 1990s:** *Tsumari Project: A Preliminary Proposal*. Community-development proposal for Japan’s Tsumari region; “preliminary” is part of its status, and it was not a completed architectural work. ([*What If?*, contents](https://www.rapeutation.com/arch.paolosoleri.toc.htm))
- **2000:** *Canal Multi-Use Bridge*. Bridge and infrastructure project exhibited at the Venice Architecture Biennale. ([La Biennale di Venezia archive](https://asac.labiennale.org/persone/422114))
- **2002:** *The Secular Cathedral*. Proposal contributed to a collection of designs for a new World Trade Center; it is a published project, not a realized building. ([scholarly study and bibliography](https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=0fcfc7ca2f5167bc9dd93a6334f377d916923c87&repid=rep1&type=pdf))
- **2005–2012:** *Lean Linear City / Arterial Arcology*. Late urban project arranging dense settlements along transportation and infrastructure arteries. Its successive publications and digital models make it a continuing research program rather than one finished plan. ([Cosanti Foundation publications](https://www.arcosanti.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/Cosanti-Foundation_Publications_Order_Form.pdf), [Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/paolo-soleri_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))

### Designed objects, sculpture and production systems

- **1950s–2013:** *Ceramic and bronze windbells*. A continuing family of cast objects whose sale supported Cosanti and Arcosanti. Thousands were produced by Soleri, workshop participants and later artisans, so “the Soleri bell” is a design lineage and collective production system, not a finite list of individually authored works. ([Arcosanti Archives](https://arcosanti.org/vision/arcosanti-archives/), [Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/paolo-soleri_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))
- **1950s–2013:** *Earth-cast and silt-cast sculptural panels*. Reliefs, decorative surfaces, molds and architectural elements made by casting against carved earth. They occupy the boundary between sculpture, construction process and protocol; no complete object-by-object catalogue is publicly available. ([SAH Archipedia](https://sah-archipedia.org/buildings/AZ-01-013-0028), [Arcosanti Archives](https://arcosanti.org/vision/arcosanti-archives/))
- **undated continuing body:** *Furniture designs*. Furniture appears among Soleri’s design production, including the publication known as *Life Time Furniture Cloister Styles*, but the consulted standard sources do not establish a complete titled chronology; individual undocumented objects should not be invented from auction or sales records. ([major-publications summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paolo_Soleri#Major_publications))
- **career-long:** *Models*. Soleri and his workshops made architectural and urban models for Mesa City, the arcologies, Arcosanti and later projects. These are manifestations of projects and sometimes exhibition objects in their own right, but the archive describes thousands of drawings and models without yet publishing a complete catalogue, so they resist enumeration as discrete authored works. ([Arcosanti Archives](https://arcosanti.org/vision/arcosanti-archives/))

### Sketchbooks, drawings and graphic bodies of work

- **career-long:** *The Sketchbooks of Paolo Soleri*. Twelve large-format sketchbook bodies, combining drawing, notation, philosophy, urban theory and project development; they are neither merely preparatory drawings nor conventional books. Only selections were published, while the archival whole comprises thousands of pages. ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/paolo-soleri_(Dizionario-Biografico)/), [Arcosanti Archives](https://arcosanti.org/vision/arcosanti-archives/))
- **career-long:** *Architectural scrolls and presentation drawings*. Large drawings for bridges, arcologies and urban studies, often functioning simultaneously as project documents and autonomous graphic works. The Arcosanti archive explicitly describes its online examples as representative, not complete. ([Arcosanti Archives](https://arcosanti.org/vision/arcosanti-archives/))
- **career-long:** *Poster art and graphic design*. Posters, event materials and visual identities connected with Cosanti and Arcosanti; the archive warns that this collection also contains works by other designers, making item-level attribution necessary and presently incomplete. ([Arcosanti Archives](https://arcosanti.org/vision/arcosanti-archives/))

### Books, manifestos, protocols and collected texts

- **1951:** *Su Dome House*. Article written with Mark Mills about the Cave Creek house; a documentary text by its designers rather than the architectural work itself. ([Politecnico di Torino bibliography](https://webthesis.biblio.polito.it/4519/))
- **1956:** *Archetipi Cosanti—Paolo Soleri Architetto*. Early booklet presenting Soleri’s architecture and developing vocabulary. ([Politecnico di Torino bibliography](https://webthesis.biblio.polito.it/4519/))
- **1965:** *In ricordo di Le Corbusier*. Memorial essay published in *L’Architecture d’Aujourd’hui*. ([Politecnico di Torino bibliography](https://webthesis.biblio.polito.it/4519/))
- **1965:** *Projet pour la Fondation Cosanti, Arizona, États-Unis*. Illustrated architectural article on the Cosanti Foundation project. ([Politecnico di Torino bibliography](https://webthesis.biblio.polito.it/4519/))
- **1969–1970:** *Arcology: The City in the Image of Man*. Manifesto-book combining urban theory, diagrams and thirty designed arcologies. Sources variously give 1969 and 1970, reflecting publication and exhibition chronology; the discrepancy should remain visible. ([original work and contents](https://www.organism.earth/library/document/arcology), [Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/paolo-soleri_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))
- **1970:** *Documenta: Paolo Soleri*. Boxed catalogue for the Corcoran Gallery retrospective, containing a printed catalogue and sixteen rolled drawings. It is an exhibition-as-object and near-Gesamtkunstwerk rather than an ordinary bound book. ([Columbia University Libraries](https://exhibitions.library.columbia.edu/exhibits/show/novelties/documenta/documenta))
- **1971:** *The Sketchbooks of Paolo Soleri*. Edited publication of selections from the much larger sketchbook corpus; it should not be mistaken for the complete twelve-sketchbook archival body. ([Politecnico di Torino bibliography](https://webthesis.biblio.polito.it/4519/))
- **1973:** *The Bridge Between Matter & Spirit Is Matter Becoming Spirit: The Arcology of Paolo Soleri*. Philosophical and architectural manifesto relating arcology to cosmological development. ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/paolo-soleri_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))
- **1980:** *Envisioned Cities of the 21st Century*. Publication presenting Soleri’s projected cities; the standard summary records it separately, although later editions and overlapping project imagery complicate its bibliographic identity. ([major-publications summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paolo_Soleri#Major_publications))
- **1981:** *Fragments: A Selection from the Sketchbooks of Paolo Soleri; The Tiger Paradigm-Paradox*. Curated assemblage of drawings and theoretical fragments, not a complete sketchbook edition. ([scholarly bibliography](https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=0fcfc7ca2f5167bc9dd93a6334f377d916923c87&repid=rep1&type=pdf))
- **1981:** *The Omega Seed: An Eschatological Hypothesis*. Cosmological and theological hypothesis rather than an architectural manual; some bibliographies associate an earlier 1973 date with the text, while the documented Anchor/Doubleday edition is 1981. ([Politecnico di Torino bibliography](https://webthesis.biblio.polito.it/4519/), [Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/paolo-soleri_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))
- **1983:** *Arcosanti: An Urban Laboratory?* Project report and argumentative account of Arcosanti; later editions are not separate works. Sources sometimes date it 1984, 1987 or 1993 because of successive editions. ([scholarly bibliography](https://stax.strath.ac.uk/downloads/8p58pd63g?locale=en), [Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/paolo-soleri_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))
- **1984:** *Paolo Soleri’s Earth Casting: For Sculpture, Models and Construction*. Co-authored with Scott M. Davis; an illustrated protocol for earth casting as well as a record of earlier constructions. ([scholarly bibliography](https://stax.strath.ac.uk/downloads/8p58pd63g?locale=en))
- **1984–1989:** *Space for Peace: A Matter of Mind*. Manifesto and project monograph proposing peaceful uses for space technology. Bibliographies report 1984, a 1985 presentation or periodical appearance, and a 1989 Cosanti Press version. ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/paolo-soleri_(Dizionario-Biografico)/), [Politecnico di Torino bibliography](https://webthesis.biblio.polito.it/4519/))
- **1985–1986:** *Technology and Cosmogenesis*. Philosophical study of technology within cosmic and human evolution; the year varies between 1985 and 1986 in the standard bibliographies. ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/paolo-soleri_(Dizionario-Biografico)/), [Politecnico di Torino bibliography](https://webthesis.biblio.polito.it/4519/))
- **1986–2002:** *What If? Quaderni*. Continuing sequence of short theoretical books clustered around Soleri’s “Lean Hypothesis.” Numbering extends at least to Quaderno 14, while the foundation later marketed a nine-volume selection; the series is therefore not safely describable as only nine works. ([Cosanti Foundation catalogue](https://www.arcosanti.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/Cosanti-Foundation_Publications_Catalogue.pdf), [Quaderno 14](https://cosanti.com/a/s/products/what-if-quaderno-14-lean-linear-city))
- **1986–2002:** *What If? Quaderno 01: Introduction*. Introductory theoretical notebook in the Quaderni series. ([Cosanti Originals](https://cosanti.com/products/what-if-quaderno-01-introduction))
- **1986–2002:** *What If? Quaderno 02: Space as Reality*. Notebook developing Soleri’s spatial hypothesis. ([Politecnico di Torino bibliography](https://webthesis.biblio.polito.it/4519/))
- **1986–2002:** *What If? Quaderno 03: Earthcasting*. Protocol and theoretical discussion of earth-cast construction. ([Cosanti Foundation order form](https://www.arcosanti.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/Cosanti-Foundation_Publications_Order_Form.pdf))
- **1986–2002:** *What If? Quaderno 04: Howness*. Philosophical notebook concerned with process and becoming. ([Cosanti Foundation order form](https://www.arcosanti.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/Cosanti-Foundation_Publications_Order_Form.pdf))
- **1986–2002:** *What If? Quaderno 05: Ecominutiae*. Text and project-book on compact self-contained habitats. ([Cosanti Foundation order form](https://www.arcosanti.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/Cosanti-Foundation_Publications_Order_Form.pdf))
- **1986–2002:** *What If? Quaderno 06: Lean Hypothesis/Lean Alternative*. Statement of Soleri’s ethic of material and spatial frugality. ([Cosanti Foundation order form](https://www.arcosanti.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/Cosanti-Foundation_Publications_Order_Form.pdf))
- **1986–2002:** *What If? Quaderno 08: Bubbles*. Theoretical and graphic notebook using the bubble as a spatial and cosmological model. ([Cosanti Foundation order form](https://www.arcosanti.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/Cosanti-Foundation_Publications_Order_Form.pdf))
- **1986–2002:** *What If? Quaderno 11: Arcosanti Genesis*. Retrospective project-text on Arcosanti’s development. ([Cosanti Foundation order form](https://www.arcosanti.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/Cosanti-Foundation_Publications_Order_Form.pdf))
- **1986–2002:** *What If? Quaderno 12: Animism vs. Animation*. Philosophical notebook contrasting two accounts of vitality. ([Cosanti Originals](https://cosanti.com/products/what-if-quaderno-12-animism-vs-animation))
- **1987:** *Arcosanti Workshop Program Brochure*. Instructional and recruiting document for the workshop process; it functions as a protocol because participation was also a method of constructing the city. ([scholarly bibliography](https://stax.strath.ac.uk/downloads/8p58pd63g?locale=en))
- **1988:** *Minds for History: An Arcosanti Institute*. Institutional proposal published by the Cosanti Foundation. ([scholarly bibliography](https://stax.strath.ac.uk/downloads/8p58pd63g?locale=en))
- **1990:** *Five Afflictions of the American Dream*. Essay published in *Pan Ecology*. ([scholarly bibliography](https://stax.strath.ac.uk/downloads/8p58pd63g?locale=en))
- **1993:** *Selected Paolo Soleri Papers: 1981–93, Volume 1*. Authorial archive-selection rather than a newly composed unitary text; the cited bibliography identifies a first volume but does not establish a completed multi-volume edition. ([scholarly bibliography](https://stax.strath.ac.uk/downloads/8p58pd63g?locale=en))
- **1996:** *Six Paradoxes of the Silicon Cyberspace Revolution*. Cosanti Foundation essay on digital technology and urban life. ([scholarly bibliography](https://stax.strath.ac.uk/downloads/8p58pd63g?locale=en))
- **1997:** *Su Wright e la città*. Dated manuscript on Frank Lloyd Wright and the city; the consulted bibliography records 17 October 1997 but no publication history. ([Politecnico di Torino bibliography](https://webthesis.biblio.polito.it/4519/))
- **1997:** *Transforming the Urban Condition*. Essay contributed to *Design Outlaws on the Ecological Frontier*. ([scholarly bibliography](https://stax.strath.ac.uk/downloads/8p58pd63g?locale=en))
- **2001:** *The Urban Ideal: Conversations with Paolo Soleri*. Edited conversations with John Strohmeier; Soleri is the speaking subject and co-creator, but the work’s final textual form is editorial and collaborative. ([Cosanti Foundation catalogue](https://www.arcosanti.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/Cosanti-Foundation_Publications_Catalogue.pdf))
- **2002:** *What If? Collected Writings 1986–2000*. Kathleen Ryan’s edited selection of thirty-nine essays, hypotheses and reflections; it is a collected-work manifestation, not thirty-nine newly written works from 2002. ([table of contents](https://www.rapeutation.com/arch.paolosoleri.toc.htm), [Cosanti Foundation catalogue](https://www.arcosanti.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/Cosanti-Foundation_Publications_Catalogue.pdf))
- **2003:** *Itinerario di architettura: Antologia dagli scritti*. Italian anthology edited by Kathleen Ryan; as an anthology it remakes earlier writings rather than adding a wholly separate conceptual oeuvre. ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/paolo-soleri_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))
- **2007:** *The Mind Garden: Conversations with Paolo Soleri*. Published conversations and reflections; a collaborative textual object mediated by interview and editorial form. ([Cosanti Foundation catalogue](https://www.arcosanti.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/Cosanti-Foundation_Publications_Catalogue.pdf))
- **2012:** *What If? Quaderno 14: Lean Linear City*. Late project-book joining urban drawings and theory; it is both a publication and the principal textual manifestation of the Lean Linear City project. ([Cosanti Originals](https://cosanti.com/a/s/products/what-if-quaderno-14-lean-linear-city), [Cosanti Foundation order form](https://www.arcosanti.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/Cosanti-Foundation_Publications_Order_Form.pdf))

### Institutions, practices and works that resist finite enumeration

- **1965 onward:** *The Cosanti Foundation*. Educational and research institution established to sustain Soleri’s architectural experiments. It is a made social instrument, but not solely an artwork or building, and its history exceeds Soleri’s personal authorship. ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/paolo-soleri_(Dizionario-Biografico)/))
- **1960s onward:** *Siltpile and Arcosanti workshops*. Recurring educational-construction protocol through which students learned by physically producing Cosanti and Arcosanti. No single workshop is “the work”; the continuing process redistributed authorship among Soleri, staff and participants. ([SAH Archipedia](https://sah-archipedia.org/buildings/AZ-01-013-0028), [Arcology and Arcosanti](https://stax.strath.ac.uk/downloads/8p58pd63g?locale=en))
- **career-long:** *The Paolo Soleri archival corpus*. Thousands of personal papers, journals, drawings, models, photographs, films, graphic designs and digital reconstructions survive at Arcosanti and Arizona State University. The archive states that cataloguing and digitization remain in progress, so no honest public list can presently enumerate “everything” object by object; the corpus itself is a body of work whose boundaries and attributions remain open. ([Arcosanti Archives](https://arcosanti.org/vision/arcosanti-archives/))
