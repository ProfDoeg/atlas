## Works by Edward Witten

### Scientific oeuvre

- **1976–:** *Scientific papers*. An ongoing obra of several hundred research articles, preprints, lecture texts, proceedings contributions, reviews, corrections, and memorial essays, many coauthored. INSPIRE currently associates 455 records with Witten, but that record count is not a catalogue raisonné: it can include multiple manifestations, nonresearch material, and database records added or revised after publication, so the corpus resists being equated mechanically with 455 distinct works.

### Dissertation

- **1976:** *Some Problems in the Short Distance Analysis of Gauge Theories*. Witten’s Princeton doctoral dissertation, supervised by David Gross; it is an unpublished academic thesis rather than a commercial book.

### Books and multi-volume works

- **1984:** *Current Algebra and Anomalies*. A collaboratively authored set of lecture notes and papers by Sam B. Treiman, Roman Jackiw, Bruno Zumino, and Witten; it is simultaneously a teaching text and a collected research document.
- **1987:** *Superstring Theory, Volume 1: Introduction*. A research monograph and advanced textbook coauthored with Michael B. Green and John H. Schwarz.
- **1987:** *Superstring Theory, Volume 2: Loop Amplitudes, Anomalies and Phenomenology*. The second, separately constituted volume of the Green–Schwarz–Witten monograph.
- **2012:** *Superstring Theory: 25th Anniversary Edition*. A later manifestation of the two-volume work, with a new preface but not a wholly new scientific obra; it should therefore not be counted as two additional original books.

### Early gauge theory, field theory, and supersymmetry papers

- **1977:** *Some Exact Multi-Instanton Solutions of Classical Yang–Mills Theory*. A research article presenting a family of classical gauge-field solutions.
- **1977:** *Anomalous Cross Section for Photon–Photon Scattering in Gauge Theories*. A research article in perturbative gauge theory.
- **1978:** *The S-Matrix of the Kinks of the \((\bar\psi\psi)^2\) Model*. A coauthored research article with R. Shankar.
- **1978:** *S Matrix of the Supersymmetric Nonlinear Sigma Model*. A coauthored research article with R. Shankar.
- **1978:** *Chiral Symmetry, the \(1/N\) Expansion and the \(SU(N)\) Thirring Model*. A research article on two-dimensional field theory.
- **1978:** *An Interpretation of Classical Yang–Mills Theory*. A research article connecting gauge fields with twistor-geometric constructions.
- **1978:** *Supersymmetry Algebras That Include Topological Charges*. A coauthored research article with David Olive; it formulates the central-charge structure associated with solitons.
- **1979:** *Some Comments on the Recent Twistor Space Constructions*. A proceedings contribution, not an independent monograph.
- **1979:** *Current Algebra Theorems for the \(U(1)\) Goldstone Boson*. A research article on the axial \(U(1)\) problem.
- **1979:** *Baryons in the \(1/N\) Expansion*. A research article interpreting baryons as solitonic objects in the large-\(N\) limit.
- **1979:** *Instantons, the Quark Model, and the \(1/N\) Expansion*. A research article in nonperturbative gauge theory.
- **1980:** *Some Comments on Massive Neutrinos*. A workshop proceedings text rather than a freestanding book.
- **1981:** *Mass Hierarchies in Supersymmetric Theories*. A research article.
- **1981:** *Dynamical Breaking of Supersymmetry*. A research article that develops criteria and mechanisms for spontaneous supersymmetry breaking.
- **1982:** *Constraints on Supersymmetry Breaking*. A research article introducing what became known as the Witten index.
- **1982:** *Supersymmetry and Morse Theory*. A research article that also functions as a bridge between quantum mechanics and differential topology.
- **1982:** *An \(SU(2)\) Anomaly*. A research article identifying the global four-dimensional gauge anomaly now bearing Witten’s name.
- **1982:** *Non-Abelian Bosonization in Two Dimensions*. A research article giving a field-theoretic construction with a Wess–Zumino term.
- **1983:** *Introduction to Supersymmetry*. A pedagogical lecture text published in an edited proceedings volume.
- **1983:** *Global Aspects of Current Algebra*. A research article developing the geometric and topological meaning of the Wess–Zumino action.
- **1984:** *Non-Abelian Bosonization in Two Dimensions*. A further manifestation of the bosonization work in the collected volume *Current Algebra and Anomalies*, not an independently created second paper.
- **1984:** *Holomorphic Morse Inequalities*. A proceedings article applying supersymmetric ideas to complex geometry.
- **1984:** *Cosmic Separation of Phases*. A research article on false-vacuum decay and cosmological phase separation.
- **1984:** *Fermion Quantum Numbers in Kaluza–Klein Theory*. A research article on compactification and fermionic quantum numbers.
- **1984:** *Some Properties of \(O(32)\) Superstrings*. A research article from the first superstring revolution.
- **1985:** *Global Gravitational Anomalies*. A research article using spectral and topological invariants to analyze gravitational anomalies.
- **1985:** *Global Anomalies in String Theory*. A conference contribution; it is related to, but not identical with, Witten’s journal articles on anomalies.
- **1985:** *Symmetry Breaking Patterns in Superstring Models*. A research article.
- **1985:** *Some Properties of \(O(32)\) Superstrings*. A journal publication whose preprint and published manifestations should not be counted as separate works.
- **1986:** *Unification in Ten Dimensions*. A proceedings contribution on ten-dimensional superstring unification.
- **1986:** *Topological Tools in Ten-Dimensional Physics*. A lecture/proceedings text also published in a journal; those appearances are manifestations of one work.
- **1986:** *Noncommutative Geometry and String Field Theory*. A research article proposing the star-product formulation of open-string field theory.
- **1987:** *Covariant Description of Canonical Formalism in Geometrical Theories*. A coauthored chapter with Čedomir Crnković; it constructs a covariant phase-space formalism.
- **1987:** *Elliptic Genera and Quantum Field Theory*. A research article relating supersymmetric field theory to elliptic genera.

### Topology, geometry, and quantum field theory

- **1988:** *Topological Quantum Field Theory*. A research article that constructs Donaldson theory as a twisted supersymmetric field theory; it is also the foundational text of a broader research project.
- **1988:** *Topological Sigma Models*. A research article introducing topologically twisted sigma models.
- **1988:** *Quantum Field Theory and the Jones Polynomial*. A research article interpreting knot invariants through Chern–Simons quantum field theory.
- **1988:** *\(2+1\)-Dimensional Gravity as an Exactly Soluble System*. A research article recasting three-dimensional gravity as a Chern–Simons theory.
- **1989:** *Quantum Field Theory and the Jones Polynomial*. The journal manifestation of work circulated earlier; the preprint and publication are not separate obras.
- **1989:** *Gauge Theories and Integrable Lattice Models*. A research article on the relation between gauge theory and integrable systems.
- **1990:** *A Note on the Antibracket Formalism*. A research note concerning the Batalin–Vilkovisky framework.
- **1991:** *Two-Dimensional Gravity and Intersection Theory on Moduli Space*. A research article formulating the conjectural relation between two-dimensional gravity and intersection numbers.
- **1991:** *On Quantum Gauge Theories in Two Dimensions*. A research article on exactly soluble two-dimensional gauge theories.
- **1992:** *On Holomorphic Factorization of WZW and Coset Models*. A research article on conformal field theory.
- **1992:** *On Background Independent Open-String Field Theory*. A research article defining a project for open-string field theory on the space of boundary interactions.
- **1992:** *Some Computations in Background Independent Open-String Field Theory*. A sequel testing that project through explicit calculations.
- **1993:** *Algebraic Geometry Associated with Matrix Models of Two-Dimensional Gravity*. A proceedings article rather than a book.
- **1993:** *Phases of \(N=2\) Theories in Two Dimensions*. A research article whose gauged linear sigma models connect distinct geometric and conformal phases.
- **1993:** *Quantum Background Independence in String Theory*. A research article on background-independent formulations.
- **1994:** *Monopoles and Four-Manifolds*. A research article introducing the Seiberg–Witten equations into four-manifold topology.
- **1994:** *Supersymmetric Yang–Mills Theory on a Four-Manifold*. A research article extending the topological gauge-theory project.
- **1994:** *Non-Perturbative Superpotentials in String Theory*. A research article.
- **1995:** *String Theory Dynamics in Various Dimensions*. A research article proposing a web of dualities and the eleven-dimensional project subsequently called M-theory; “M-theory” is a project or conjectural framework, not a single completed text made by Witten alone.
- **1995:** *Bound States of Strings and \(p\)-Branes*. A research article on duality and brane bound states.
- **1995:** *Some Comments on String Dynamics*. A research article elaborating nonperturbative string dualities.
- **1995:** *On \(S\)-Duality in Abelian Gauge Theory*. A research article connecting electromagnetic duality with four-manifold invariants.
- **1996:** *Five-Brane Effective Action in M-Theory*. A research article.
- **1996:** *Small Instantons in String Theory*. A research article on nonperturbative gauge symmetry arising from shrinking instantons.
- **1996:** *Non-Perturbative Superpotentials in String Theory*. A journal manifestation of the earlier preprint; it is not an additional work.
- **1996:** *On Flux Quantization in M-Theory and the Effective Action*. A research article showing that the M-theory flux quantization law has a gravitational shift.
- **1996:** *Strong Coupling Expansion of Calabi–Yau Compactification*. A coauthored research article with Petr Hořava.
- **1996:** *Reflections on the Fate of Spacetime*. A public-facing conceptual essay, not a technical research paper.
- **1997:** *Solutions of Four-Dimensional Field Theories via M-Theory*. A research article interpreting gauge-theory dynamics with brane geometry.
- **1997:** *Branes and the Dynamics of QCD*. A research article.
- **1997:** *On the Conformal Field Theory of the Higgs Branch*. A research article.
- **1998:** *Anti-de Sitter Space and Holography*. A research article giving a precise generating-functional formulation of the AdS/CFT correspondence.
- **1998:** *Theta Dependence in the Large \(N\) Limit of Four-Dimensional Gauge Theories*. A research article.
- **1998:** *D-Branes and K-Theory*. A research article classifying D-brane charge through K-theory.
- **1998:** *AdS/CFT Correspondence and Topological Field Theory*. A research article connecting holography with topological sectors.
- **1998:** *Toroidal Compactification Without Vector Structure*. A research article.
- **1999:** *Baryons and Branes in Anti-de Sitter Space*. A research article.
- **1999:** *World-Sheet Corrections via D-Instantons*. A research article on nonperturbative corrections.
- **2000:** *The Verlinde Algebra and the Cohomology of the Grassmannian*. A mathematical-physics research article.
- **2000:** *Noncommutative Tachyons and String Field Theory*. A research article.
- **2001:** *Overview of K-Theory Applied to Strings*. A survey and lecture text rather than a new monograph.
- **2001:** *Quantum Gravity in de Sitter Space*. A research article on the conceptual status of observables in de Sitter quantum gravity.
- **2001:** *The M-Theory Three-Form and \(E_8\) Gauge Theory*. A coauthored research article with Emanuel Diaconescu and Gregory Moore.
- **2002:** *Deconstruction, \(G_2\) Holonomy, and Doublet–Triplet Splitting*. A coauthored research article.
- **2003:** *SL(2,\(\mathbb Z\)) Action on Three-Dimensional Conformal Field Theories with Abelian Symmetry*. A research article.
- **2003:** *Perturbative Gauge Theory as a String Theory in Twistor Space*. A research article proposing a twistor-string construction.
- **2004:** *Parity Invariance for Strings in Twistor Space*. A research article.
- **2004:** *An Interpretation of Classical Yang–Mills Theory*. The title also belongs to Witten’s distinct 1978 paper; bibliographic systems must not collapse works merely because later twistor-string research revisited closely related language.
- **2005:** *Two-Dimensional Models with \((0,2)\) Supersymmetry: Perturbative Aspects*. A research article.
- **2005:** *A New Look at the Path Integral of Quantum Mechanics*. A research article/lecture text on complexified integration cycles.

### Named projects and conceptual constructions that resist treatment as discrete texts

- **1988–:** *Topological quantum field theory*. A scientific project inaugurated through several articles rather than a single object; Witten made decisive formulations of it but did not solely create every theory now gathered under the name.
- **1988–:** *Chern–Simons theory as a theory of knots*. A research construction spread across articles, lectures, and later analytic-continuation work; it is an obra in the sense of a sustained body of work, not one fixed text.
- **1994–:** *Seiberg–Witten theory*. A jointly created gauge-theoretic project associated with Nathan Seiberg and Witten. The equations and invariants have lives beyond their originating papers, so the named theory is a creation and research program rather than a sole-authored textual work.
- **1995–:** *M-theory*. A conjectural unification project named and forcefully formulated by Witten from a wider network of duality discoveries. It remains inappropriate to call it a finished work or to attribute its entire creation to one person.
- **1998–:** *The holographic dictionary*. Witten’s generating-functional prescription is a foundational construction within AdS/CFT, but the correspondence itself was proposed by Juan Maldacena and elaborated collectively; authorship of the total project is therefore distributed.
- **2003–:** *Twistor string theory*. A proposed research framework whose earliest form is attached to Witten’s 2003 article. It generated later variants and corrections, so the project is larger than—and not identical with—the originating text.
- **2006–:** *Gauge-theoretic geometric Langlands*. A sustained joint and collective project, especially associated with Witten and Anton Kapustin, that manifests in multiple papers and lectures rather than one finished object.
- **2011–:** *Gauge-theoretic construction of Khovanov homology*. A research project distributed across several papers; parts are conjectural or depend on difficult analytic foundations, and that unfinished status belongs to the work rather than disqualifying it.
