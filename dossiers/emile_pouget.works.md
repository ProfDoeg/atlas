## Works by Émile Pouget

### Newspapers and serial works

- **1888:** *Le Ça ira*. Short-lived anarchist newspaper made collectively with Constant Martin, Charles Malato and others; Pouget was a contributor rather than its sole maker. The surviving record does not securely assign every unsigned article to an individual author. ([Miguel Chueca’s critical edition](https://fr.anarchistlibraries.net/library/emile-pouget-l-action-directe-et-autres-ecrits-syndicalistes-1903-1910-1), [digitized first issue](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Le_%C3%87a_ira_-_N%C2%B001.pdf))

- **1889–1894; 1894–1895; 1896–1900:** *Le Père Peinard*. An anarchist weekly—and during Pouget’s London exile a succession of pamphlet-like issues—founded, edited and largely written by him; its slang voice, typography, illustrations and recurring persona make the serial a composite obra rather than merely a container for detachable texts. Most articles are unsigned, so the common attribution of nearly all of them to Pouget is probable rather than demonstrable item by item; the original Paris run comprised 253 issues, while bibliographic records also describe later issues not made by Pouget, including a 1902 continuation that should not be absorbed into his work. ([René Bianco/FICEDL repertory](https://cgecaf.ficedl.info/mot190.html), [Miguel Chueca’s critical edition](https://fr.anarchistlibraries.net/library/emile-pouget-l-action-directe-et-autres-ecrits-syndicalistes-1903-1910-1), [BnF serial record](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb344472805), [Denis Delaplace’s critical edition](https://classiques-garnier.com/le-pere-peinard-tome-i-fevrier-juillet-1889-n-1-23.html))

- **1895–1896:** *La Sociale*. Weekly newspaper edited by Pouget, issued in 76 numbers from 12/19 May 1895 to 18/25 October 1896; it continued the exiled *Père Peinard* project under another name before becoming *Le Père Peinard* again. It is a collective serial manifestation, not a wholly Pouget-authored book. ([BnF serial record](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb32868862z), [René Bianco/FICEDL repertory](https://cgecaf.ficedl.info/mot190.html))

- **1900–1908:** *La Voix du Peuple*. CGT weekly whose first number appeared in December 1900 and which Pouget shaped as editor and, from 1901, confederal assistant secretary; it joined reporting, agitation, organizational documentation and collective contributions. It resists attribution as a single authored work: Pouget wrote extensively and directed its form, but the newspaper belonged institutionally to the CGT and had many contributors. ([Miguel Chueca’s critical edition](https://fr.anarchistlibraries.net/library/emile-pouget-l-action-directe-et-autres-ecrits-syndicalistes-1903-1910-1))

- **4 February–28 March 1909:** *La Révolution*. Pouget’s long-planned independent syndicalist daily, subtitled a daily of social struggle; it ceased after 56 numbers. Its broad announced roster of anarchist, literary and trade-union contributors makes it an editorial project and collective manifestation rather than a text authored solely by Pouget. ([Miguel Chueca’s critical edition](https://fr.anarchistlibraries.net/library/emile-pouget-l-action-directe-et-autres-ecrits-syndicalistes-1903-1910-1))

### Almanacs

- **1893:** *Almanach du Père Peinard, farci de galbeuses histoires et de prédictions épatarouflantes pour 1894–an 102*. A 56-page illustrated almanac edited and substantially written by Pouget, combining calendar, polemic, fiction and images; that mixed form makes it closer to a popular-print Gesamtkunstwerk than to a conventional authored book. It includes the signed pieces *Pourquoi et comment le Père Peinard s’est bombardé journaleux* and *Jabotage entre bibi et un fiston*. ([René Bianco/FICEDL bibliography](https://cgecaf.ficedl.info/mot1106.html), [Wikisource contents record](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Le_P%C3%A8re_Peinard))

- **1895:** *Almanach du Père Peinard pour 1896–an 104, farci de chouettes histoires, de galbeuses illustrations*. Illustrated almanac edited by Pouget and issued in 1895 for the following year; its constituent texts include *Faramineuse consultation sur l’avenir* and *Le Muselage universel*. Authorship of the entire collective object should not be confused with sole authorship of every contribution or image. ([René Bianco/FICEDL bibliography](https://cgecaf.ficedl.info/mot1106.html), [Wikisource contents record](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Le_P%C3%A8re_Peinard))

- **November 1896:** *Almanach du Père Peinard pour 1897–an 105, farci de chouettes histoires, de galbeuses illustrations*. A 64-page illustrated almanac edited by Pouget; his named contributions include the four seasonal texts *Le Printemps*, *L’Été*, *L’Automne* and *L’Hiver*. It is a collective periodical object, not four independent books. ([René Bianco/FICEDL bibliography](https://cgecaf.ficedl.info/mot1106.html), [Wikisource contents record](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Le_P%C3%A8re_Peinard))

- **October 1897:** *Almanach du Père Peinard pour 1898–an 106, farci de chouettes histoires, de galbeuses illustrations*. A 64-page illustrated almanac edited by Pouget and published for 1898; it contains his programmatic article *Le Sabotage*, an early formulation distinct from the much-expanded 1911 book bearing the same title. ([René Bianco/FICEDL bibliography](https://cgecaf.ficedl.info/mot1106.html), [René Bianco/FICEDL Père Peinard record](https://cgecaf.ficedl.info/mot190.html))

- **1898:** *Almanach du Père Peinard pour 1899–an 107, farci de chouettes histoires et de galbeuses illustrations indispensables pour se tenir la rate en bonne humeur et se décrasser les boyaux de la tête*. The fifth and last known *Père Peinard* almanac edited by Pouget. As with the preceding volumes, “by Pouget” principally identifies the editorial obra and his textual presence, not sole responsibility for every illustration and contribution. ([René Bianco/FICEDL bibliography](https://cgecaf.ficedl.info/mot1106.html), [René Bianco/FICEDL Père Peinard record](https://cgecaf.ficedl.info/mot190.html))

- **1905:** *Almanach illustré de la révolution pour 1906*. Collective illustrated almanac containing a contribution by Pouget among texts by René Chaughi, Lucien Descaves, Paul Delesalle, Jean Grave, Pierre Kropotkin and others. Pouget made a constituent text, not the almanac as a whole; the available bibliographic description does not name that contribution. ([René Bianco/FICEDL bibliography](https://cgecaf.ficedl.info/mot1106.html))

- **1907:** *Almanach illustré de la révolution pour 1908*. Collective illustrated almanac containing an unnamed contribution by Pouget. Its bibliographic record establishes participation but does not warrant inventing a title for his piece. ([René Bianco/FICEDL bibliography](https://cgecaf.ficedl.info/mot1106.html))

- **1908:** *Almanach illustré de la révolution pour 1909*. Collective illustrated almanac containing an unnamed Pouget text alongside work by Victor Méric, Georges Sorel, Kropotkin and others. It is evidence of a contribution, not of Pouget’s authorship of the complete volume. ([René Bianco/FICEDL bibliography](https://cgecaf.ficedl.info/mot1106.html))

### Pamphlets, compilations and books

- **ca. 1894:** *L’ABCD de la révolution*. Illustrated 28-page London pamphlet issued by *Le Père Peinard*; its date is approximate and should not be made more exact. Its alphabetic, popular-propaganda form makes it simultaneously a primer and manifesto. ([René Bianco/FICEDL bibliography](https://cgecaf.ficedl.info/mot1106.html))

- **1896?:** *Variations guesdistes*. A 36-page compilation assembled and annotated by Pouget for the Bibliothèque de *La Sociale*. His act of making here is editorial and polemical—selection plus annotation—rather than authorship of every gathered passage; the publication year remains conjectural. ([René Bianco/FICEDL bibliography](https://cgecaf.ficedl.info/mot1106.html))

- **1899:** *Les Lois scélérates de 1893–1894*. Collective polemical dossier by Francis de Pressensé, Léon Blum writing as “un juriste,” and Pouget; Pouget’s component is *L’Application des lois d’exception de 1893–1894*. The whole volume should therefore not be silently assigned to him alone. ([René Bianco/FICEDL bibliography](https://cgecaf.ficedl.info/mot1106.html))

- **1903; early editions not securely dated, fifth edition dated 1904:** *Les Bases du syndicalisme*. Doctrinal pamphlet defining the union, its history and its prospective social role. The collected-works editor dates its composition/publication to 1903, whereas surviving bibliographic records chiefly describe undated third and fourth editions and a 1904 fifth edition; a 1921 edition adds an introduction absent from the earlier text. ([Miguel Chueca’s critical edition](https://fr.anarchistlibraries.net/library/emile-pouget-l-action-directe-et-autres-ecrits-syndicalistes-1903-1910-1), [René Bianco/FICEDL bibliography](https://cgecaf.ficedl.info/mot1106.html))

- **ca. 1904:** *Le Syndicat*. Twenty-four-page explanatory and practical pamphlet—the second number of Pouget’s *Bibliothèque syndicaliste*—that treats the union as both present fighting organization and embryo of a transformed society. The first edition is only approximately dated; later editions appeared around 1906 and in 1921. ([René Bianco/FICEDL bibliography](https://cgecaf.ficedl.info/mot1106.html), [Miguel Chueca’s critical edition](https://fr.anarchistlibraries.net/library/emile-pouget-l-action-directe-et-autres-ecrits-syndicalistes-1903-1910-1))

- **1904:** *L’Action directe*. Doctrinal pamphlet and practical manifesto of worker action, with a cover illustrated by Jules Grandjouan; it was number 4 of the *Bibliothèque syndicaliste*. Some later title pages and catalogues date reprints to 1910 or 1921, but the repertory identifies the first edition as 1904. ([René Bianco/FICEDL bibliography](https://cgecaf.ficedl.info/mot1106.html), [Miguel Chueca’s critical edition](https://fr.anarchistlibraries.net/library/emile-pouget-l-action-directe-et-autres-ecrits-syndicalistes-1903-1910-1))

- **1905:** *Le Parti du travail*. Twenty-eight-page pamphlet, with a Grandjouan cover, describing the federation of unions and labor exchanges as an operative counter-society. It is political theory but also a project: a proposed social organization intended to be made through syndical practice. ([René Bianco/FICEDL bibliography](https://cgecaf.ficedl.info/mot1106.html), [Miguel Chueca’s critical edition](https://fr.anarchistlibraries.net/library/emile-pouget-l-action-directe-et-autres-ecrits-syndicalistes-1903-1910-1))

- **1908:** *La Confédération générale du travail*. Sixty-four-page institutional and doctrinal study of the CGT, first issued by Marcel Rivière and revised for a second edition in 1910. It describes an existing organization while arguing performatively for its federalist, direct-action form. ([René Bianco/FICEDL bibliography](https://cgecaf.ficedl.info/mot1106.html))

- **1909:** *Comment nous ferons la révolution*. Speculative novel co-written with Émile Pataud and first published by Jules Tallandier, with a preface by Peter Kropotkin; a 1911 edition followed. It is both fiction and an operational projection of a syndicalist revolution, so neither “novel” nor “protocol” alone exhausts what it is. ([René Bianco/FICEDL bibliography](https://cgecaf.ficedl.info/mot1106.html), [Miguel Chueca’s critical edition](https://fr.anarchistlibraries.net/library/emile-pouget-l-action-directe-et-autres-ecrits-syndicalistes-1903-1910-1))

- **1911:** *Le Sabotage*. Sixty-eight-page theoretical and tactical treatise developed from Pouget’s earlier congress report, journalism and 1898 almanac article. It is at once history, argument, manifesto and protocol of workplace resistance; its first book-form edition should not be confused with those earlier manifestations. ([René Bianco/FICEDL bibliography](https://cgecaf.ficedl.info/mot1106.html), [Miguel Chueca’s critical edition](https://fr.anarchistlibraries.net/library/emile-pouget-l-action-directe-et-autres-ecrits-syndicalistes-1903-1910-1))

- **1914:** *L’Organisation du surmenage : le système Taylor*. Seventy-page study attacking Taylorist labor organization, issued as number 15 of the *Bibliothèque du mouvement prolétarien*. It is industrial criticism directed against a newly made system for organizing bodies and time at work. ([René Bianco/FICEDL bibliography](https://cgecaf.ficedl.info/mot1106.html))

### Reports, motions and other collective action-texts

- **1897:** *Rapport sur le boycottage et le sabotage*. Congress report prepared for the CGT’s Toulouse congress and published afterward by its commission. The official document names Pouget, Paul Delesalle and “Cumora,” but scholarship generally regards it as principally Pouget’s work; that attribution remains a judgment, not an uncontested signature. As a report designed to secure adoption of tactics, it functions as both manifesto and protocol. ([Miguel Chueca’s critical edition](https://fr.anarchistlibraries.net/library/emile-pouget-l-action-directe-et-autres-ecrits-syndicalistes-1903-1910-1))

- **1901:** *Grève générale et révolution* and *La Grève générale révolutionnaire. Réponse à Jaurès*. Two polemical action-texts published under the authority of the CGT’s committee for the general strike. Maxime Leroy attributed the response to Pouget, and textual parallels support that attribution, but the collective signature prevents absolute certainty; conversely, the separately issued 1900 pamphlet *La Grève générale*, signed by the same committee, cannot presently be assigned to Pouget and therefore resists inclusion as his work. ([Miguel Chueca’s critical edition](https://fr.anarchistlibraries.net/library/emile-pouget-l-action-directe-et-autres-ecrits-syndicalistes-1903-1910-1))

- **1904:** *La Grève générale*. Long historical essay contributed to *Le Mouvement socialiste*’s international inquiry into the general strike and later reprinted in *La Grève générale et le socialisme*. It is an essay within a collective inquiry, distinct from the uncertain 1900 committee pamphlet of the same title. ([Miguel Chueca’s critical edition](https://fr.anarchistlibraries.net/library/emile-pouget-l-action-directe-et-autres-ecrits-syndicalistes-1903-1910-1))

- **1904:** *Le Congrès confédéral de Bourges*. Pouget’s extended report and interpretation of the CGT congress, including its organization, voting arrangements and campaign for the eight-hour day. It is reportage that also helps organize future action, and thus stands between record, argument and protocol. ([Miguel Chueca’s critical edition](https://fr.anarchistlibraries.net/library/emile-pouget-l-action-directe-et-autres-ecrits-syndicalistes-1903-1910-1))

- **1906:** *Motion sur les rapports entre les organisations syndicales et les partis politiques*, subsequently called the *Charte d’Amiens*. Collective manifesto adopted by the CGT congress at Amiens and co-drafted by Pouget and Victor Griffuelhes; because it was debated, amended, adopted and owned by the congress, it resists treatment as Pouget’s private authored work. Its action consists in declaring the union movement’s independence and defining its immediate and revolutionary tasks. ([Social History Portal](https://socialhistoryportal.org/news/articles/110350), [Miguel Chueca’s critical edition](https://fr.anarchistlibraries.net/library/emile-pouget-l-action-directe-et-autres-ecrits-syndicalistes-1903-1910-1), [Dissidences review](https://preo.ube.fr/dissidences/index.php?id=773&lang=en))

- **1906:** *Le Congrès syndicaliste d’Amiens*. Pouget’s polemical account of the congress and of the motion later canonized as the *Charte d’Amiens*. Originally a long intervention in contemporary debate, it is now often encountered as part of a posthumous edited book; the modern book is an edition, not a new work made by Pouget. ([Dissidences review](https://preo.ube.fr/dissidences/index.php?id=773&lang=en), [René Bianco/FICEDL bibliography](https://cgecaf.ficedl.info/mot1106.html))

- **date not securely established:** *Syndicat et syndicalisme : opinions*. Twelve-page collective brochure presenting statements by Victor Griffuelhes, Georges Yvetot, Pouget, Paul Delesalle and others. Pouget made a contribution to this polyphonic object, but the repertory supplies neither a secure date nor a separately titled Pouget text. ([René Bianco/FICEDL bibliography](https://cgecaf.ficedl.info/mot1106.html))
