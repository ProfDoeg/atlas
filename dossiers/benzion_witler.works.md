## Works by Ben-Zion Witler

### Compositions and songs

- *Rivkele*. A Yiddish song credited to Witler in contemporary record metadata; it is a composition as well as the basis of several recorded manifestations. No secure composition date is given. [National Library of Israel](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL990043796460205171/NLI)
- *Leb un lakh* (“Live and Laugh”). A song credited jointly to Witler and Carl Sigman; some sources abbreviate or vary Sigman’s name, so the precise division of words and music remains unclear. [National Library of Israel](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL990043796460205171/NLI)
- *Abi men zet zikh*. A song credited to Witler; no secure date is supplied by the discographic source. [National Library of Israel](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL990043796460205171/NLI)
- *Mayn alte heym*. A song credited to Witler. It should not be confused with the later albums and compilations in which his recording reappears. [National Library of Israel](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL990043796460205171/NLI)
- *Vi nemt men a bisl mazl?* A song credited to Witler and one of his best-known numbers; transliterations vary widely, including *Wi nemt men a bissale mazel*. Its composition date is not securely documented. [National Library of Israel](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL990030319300205171/NLI)
- *A geshikhte fun libe*. A song credited to Witler; the 1997 discographic record documents a posthumously compiled manifestation rather than the date of composition. [National Library of Israel](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL990030319300205171/NLI)
- *Hit op dos bisele koyekh*. A Yiddish song credited to Witler on *Zun un regn*. [National Library of Israel](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL990043818670205171/NLI)
- *Der lomp*. A song credited to Witler; later listings also render the title *Der Lomo*, apparently a cataloguing or transliteration variant. [National Library of Israel](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL990043818670205171/NLI)
- *Nem dir nisht tsum hartsn*. A song credited to Witler on *Zun un regn*; another compilation labels it traditional, so authorship is disputed in the surviving discography. [National Library of Israel: 1959 attribution](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL990043818670205171/NLI), [National Library of Israel: later “traditional” attribution](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL990030319300205171/NLI)
- *Shik mir a brivele*. A song credited to Witler on *Zun un regn*. [National Library of Israel](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL990043818670205171/NLI)
- *Alts ineynem*. A song credited to Witler on *Zun un regn*; a later compilation calls it traditional, leaving the attribution unsettled. [National Library of Israel: 1959 attribution](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL990043818670205171/NLI), [National Library of Israel: later “traditional” attribution](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL990030319300205171/NLI)
- *Es hoybt zikh nisht on*. A song credited to Witler on *Zun un regn*. [National Library of Israel](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL990043818670205171/NLI)
- *Ikh hob tsayt, ikh vel vartn*. A song credited jointly to Witler and Mierzejewski; later compilation metadata calls it traditional, so neither authorship nor the allocation of words and music is fully stable. [National Library of Israel: 1959 attribution](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL990043818670205171/NLI), [National Library of Israel: later attribution](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL990030319300205171/NLI)
- *Zun un regn*. A song credited jointly to Witler and Louis Silvers. It also supplied the title of Witler’s 1959 album, but the composition and the album are distinct objects. [National Library of Israel](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL990043818670205171/NLI)
- *Zol es geyn*. A song credited to Witler in the discographic notes to *More Gems of Jewish Folksongs*. [National Library of Israel](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL990030319450205171/NLI)
- *Khasidimlekh zingen*. A song credited to I. Kolodny and Witler; the source does not specify which collaborator supplied words or music. [National Library of Israel](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL990030319450205171/NLI)
- *Zing ikh mir a lidele*. A song credited to Witler in compilation metadata; its date is not given. [National Library of Israel](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL990030319450205171/NLI)
- *Varshe*. A song credited to Witler and recorded by him, concerning Warsaw. It is sometimes rendered *Varshe mayn* and must not be confused with Shmerke Kaczerginski and Leon Wajner’s different song *Varshe*. [Jewish Folk Songs](https://www.jewishfolksongs.com/en/Jewish-World), [Lexicon of the Yiddish Theatre](https://www.museumoffamilyhistory.com/yt/lex/W/witler-benzion.htm)
- *Lomir ale freylekh zayn*. A song credited to Witler in the discography; it later became an album title, making the composition and recording-object easy to conflate. [National Library of Israel](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL990030319450205171/NLI)
- *Ales farloyrn*. A song credited to Witler in the notes to *More Gems of Jewish Folksongs*. [National Library of Israel](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL990030319450205171/NLI)
- *A trope-le, a kapele*. A song credited to Witler; the compilation metadata runs several titles together, so the normalized title and boundaries should be treated cautiously. [National Library of Israel](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL990030319450205171/NLI)

### Theatrical obra

- **Before 1938:** *Bandit Gentleman*. A Yiddish stage work credited jointly to Zagranitshni, I. Shtshogol, and Witler. The standard theatrical lexicon supplies neither a firm composition date nor a division of authorship, so it cannot safely be called Witler’s play alone. [Lexicon of the Yiddish Theatre](https://www.museumoffamilyhistory.com/yt/lex/W/witler-benzion.htm)

### Recorded manifestations

- **c. 1947:** *Akhtsik er un zibetsik zi / Bialistok*. A commercial 78-rpm disc by Witler with the Mendelsohn Orchestra. This is a performed and recorded manifestation, not evidence that Witler composed both songs. [National Library of Israel](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL990034273620205171/NLI)
- **1954?:** *Benzion Witler* (container title: *Chansons populaires yiddish*). A ten-song, 12-inch LP issued by O.S.I. Disques in Paris; the bracketed year is supplied by the catalogue and is therefore approximate. It preserves Witler’s performances but is not a ten-work authorial collection. [National Library of Israel](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL997012399474505171/NLI)
- **1950s:** *Benzion Witler with Shifra Lerer*. An eight-song Hed Arzi 78-rpm disc shared with Lerer. The catalogue dates it only “[195-?]”; its contents mix Witler compositions with works by Itzik Manger, Max Kletter, Mordechai Gebirtig, and traditional sources. [National Library of Israel](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL997009761189605171/NLI)
- **1959:** *Zun un regn: 8 Yiddish Songs*. A 10-inch Hed Arzi album performed by Witler with the Hed Arzi Orchestra, arranged and conducted by Marcel Kreisler. It is both a designed album-object and a manifestation of eight separately credited songs. [National Library of Israel](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL990043818670205171/NLI)
- **Before 1961:** *Benzion Wittler*. An eleven-song Argentine Londisc LP, with Shifra Lerer and an orchestra conducted by Simon Tenovsky. The discographic record can establish only that it predates Witler’s death, not a particular year. [National Library of Israel](https://www.nli.org.il/en/systemlibrary/umbracoitempage?docId=NNL_MUSIC_AL990039264900205171&lang=he&type=items&vid=NLI)
- **1960:** *Lomir ale freylekh zayn*. An album attributed to Witler in the Israeli Qube discography. Its track listing and original-label particulars are not supplied there, so its completeness and relationship to similarly titled releases remain uncertain. [Qube discography](https://qube.co.il/%D7%91%D7%9F-%D7%A6%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9F-%D7%95%D7%99%D7%98%D7%9C%D7%A8)
- **1960s?:** *Eight Yiddish Songs*. A 10-inch Felsted album containing eight Witler performances. The catalogue’s “[196-?]” date may place the release before or after his death; it is therefore a recording he helped make but not necessarily a release he supervised. [National Library of Israel](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL990043796460205171/NLI)
- **1960s?:** *Eight Yiddish Songs, Volume 2* (also catalogued as *Yiddish Songs*). A second eight-song Felsted album. As with the first volume, its uncertain decade-level release date prevents a claim that Witler himself assembled or authorized the album-object. [National Library of Israel](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL990034620580205171/NLI)
- *Chansons populaires yiddish*. A four-disc Tel Aviv series recorded by Witler and Shifra Lerer, according to a biographical discographic survey. No dependable original release date or complete division into four volumes is provided, and later one-disc issues under the same title appear to recombine its recordings. [Benzion Witler discographic biography](https://sc4e3d76965f4f9d3.jimcontent.com/download/version/1293543017/module/2996069511/name/Benzion%20Witler.pdf)
- *Exitos de Benzion Witler y Shifrele Lerer*. A shared recording with Shifra Lerer documented by title in the discographic biography. Its date, label, format, and contents remain unverified, so it resists treatment as a securely bounded album-work. [Benzion Witler discographic biography](https://sc4e3d76965f4f9d3.jimcontent.com/download/version/1293543017/module/2996069511/name/Benzion%20Witler.pdf)

- *Geselekh*. A recorded performance of a song credited in album metadata to Max Kletter, not Witler. [National Library of Israel](https://www.nli.org.il/en/items/NNL_MUSIC_AL990043796460205171/NLI)

- *Fun mayn shtetl*. A recorded performance listed without secure author or date; similarly titled songs in later compilations make identification uncertain. [Lexicon of the Yiddish Theatre](https://www.museumoffamilyhistory.com/yt/lex/W/witler-benzion.htm)
- *Zing, brider, zing*. A recorded performance listed by the lexicon; later metadata calls the song traditional, so it should not automatically be assigned to Witler as composer. - *Eretz Yisroel*. A recording with Shifra Lerer; the lexicon’s form *Eretz Israel lem Israel* may contain a transcription error, and the discographic evidence does not establish authorship. [Lexicon of the Yiddish Theatre](https://www.museumoffamilyhistory.com/yt/lex/W/witler-benzion.htm)

### Journalism and correspondence

- **14 November 1933:** *A briv in redaktsye*. A letter to the editor published in the Vilna newspaper *Tsayt*. It is an authored text, but the lexicon bibliography supplies no fuller description of its subject. [Lexicon of the Yiddish Theatre](https://www.museumoffamilyhistory.com/yt/lex/W/witler-benzion.htm)
- **5 February 1934:** *A briv in redaktsye*. A second letter to the editor, published in *Di tsayt*. Its identical generic title does not establish that it was a reprint of the 1933 letter; they should be treated as separate texts unless the originals prove otherwise. [Lexicon of the Yiddish Theatre](https://www.museumoffamilyhistory.com/yt/lex/W/witler-benzion.htm)
- **1936:** *Mayn bazukh in beys yesomim*. A newspaper article credited to “Artist Ben Zion Witler” in *Unzer pinsker lebn*, issue 108. The translated lexicon citation is typographically uncertain, but it documents an authored journalistic text rather than a song or stage work. [Lexicon of the Yiddish Theatre](https://www.museumoffamilyhistory.com/yt/lex/W/witler-benzion.htm)

### Unbounded performance work

- **1919–1961:** *Stage, concert, and touring repertoire*. Witler made a career-long body of performances encompassing operetta, comedy, drama, folk song, original songs, and duets; the standard lexicon documents numerous roles and productions but generally attributes the underlying plays to other authors. This body of work resists itemization as a set of authored obras: performance itself is what Witler made, while surviving sources preserve only a selective itinerary and an estimated repertoire of roughly 200 songs. [Lexicon of the Yiddish Theatre](https://www.museumoffamilyhistory.com/yt/lex/W/witler-benzion.htm), [discographic biography](https://sc4e3d76965f4f9d3.jimcontent.com/download/version/1293543017/module/2996069511/name/Benzion%20Witler.pdf)
