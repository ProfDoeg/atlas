## Works by Jaufre Rudel

### Cansos (lyric songs)

- **Date unknown:** *Belhs m’es l’estius e·l temps floritz*. An Old Occitan *canso*, identified by its incipit rather than an author-given title; it survives as a verbal text without a securely transmitted melody. It belongs to the six-song canon accepted by the critical editions and repertory ([RIALTO, BdT 262.1](https://www.rialto.unina.it/bdt/jaufre-rudel/)).

- **Date unknown:** *Lanquan li jorn son lonc en mai*. An Old Occitan *canso*—both lyric text and song—with melodies preserved in three manuscripts; its many witnesses differ in stanza order and wording, so the transmitted versions resist reduction to one unquestionable authorial text ([RIALTO, BdT 262.2](https://www.rialto.unina.it/trovatori/jaufre-rudel/262-2chiarini/)).

- **Date unknown:** *Non sap chantar qui so non di*. An Old Occitan *canso* with a surviving melody; manuscripts transmit several substantially different configurations, while two stanzas found only in one witness are generally considered apocryphal ([RIALTO, BdT 262.3](https://www.rialto.unina.it/trovatori/jaufre-rudel/262-3chiarini/)).

- **Date unknown:** *Pro ai del chan essenhadors*. An Old Occitan *canso* preserved as words without a securely transmitted melody. Its seven-stanza form is reconstructed from only three manuscript witnesses, and editors disagree over readings and the status of material unique to individual witnesses ([RIALTO, BdT 262.4](https://www.rialto.unina.it/trovatori/jaufre-rudel/262-4chiarini/)).

- **Date unknown:** *Quan lo rius de la fontana*. An Old Occitan *canso* with a surviving melody; its manuscript transmission includes divergent stanza arrangements and additional stanzas whose authenticity is disputed ([RIALTO, BdT 262.5, repertory entry](https://www.rialto.unina.it/bdt/jaufre-rudel/)).

- **Date unknown; one transmitted version possibly 1146–1148:** *Quan lo rossinhols el folhos*. An Old Occitan *canso* with a surviving melody and markedly different manuscript versions. A crusading stanza may place one version around the Second Crusade, but that stanza may itself be apocryphal, so it cannot securely date the obra as a whole ([RIALTO, BdT 262.6](https://www.rialto.unina.it/trovatori/jaufre-rudel/262-6chiarini/)).

### Disputed attribution

- **Date unknown:** *Qui no sap esser chantaire*. A *canso* attributed to Jaufre only by a single manuscript and traditionally printed as BdT 262.7. Modern editors commonly exclude it from his canon on stylistic and metrical grounds; Maria Luisa Meneghetti instead proposed Bernart Marti as a possible author, making this an attributed manifestation rather than a secure obra by Jaufre ([Trobadorica, BdT 262.7](https://trobadorica.org/en/testi/262.7-qui-no-sap-esser-chantaire/); [Corpus de Trobadors, editorial introduction](https://trobadors.iec.cat/veure_estudi.asp?id_estudi=132); [RIALTO attribution note](https://www.rialto.unina.it/trovatori/jaufre-rudel/262-3chiarini/)).
