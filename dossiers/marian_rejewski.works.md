## Works by Marian Adam Rejewski

### Mathematical and cryptanalytic constructions

- **1929:** *Opracować teorię funkcji podwójnie periodycznych drugiego i trzeciego rodzaju oraz jej zastosowanie*. Rejewski’s master’s thesis in mathematics at Poznań University; the surviving bibliographical record gives the title but does not identify a separate publication. ([Histmag](https://histmag.org/Marian-Rejewski-i-sekret-Enigmy-18330))

- **late 1932–early 1933:** *Mathematical reconstruction of the military Enigma*. A cryptanalytic obra comprising permutation equations, the recovery of the machine’s secret rotor wiring, and a method for deriving keys—not a conventional text or a machine physically made by Rejewski alone. The resulting Enigma replicas were manufactured by the AVA company from the reconstruction, so authorship of the intellectual design and fabrication must be distinguished. ([Polish Biographical Dictionary](https://www.ipsb.nina.gov.pl/a/biografia/marian-adam-rejewski-1905-1980-matematyk-kryptolog-etc), [IPN](https://ipn.gov.pl/pl/historia-z-ipn/157145%2Cgiganci-nauki-infografiki-historyczne-marian-rejewski.html))

- **1933–1934:** *Method of characteristics*. A mathematical protocol for recovering Enigma’s daily settings from the cycle structure of doubled message indicators. It has no firmly established independent title and survives principally through Rejewski’s later descriptions; it therefore resists treatment as a separately published “work.” ([Rejewski’s retrospective paper](https://historiainformatyki.pl/dokument.php?handle=892&nonav=&nrar=7&nrzesp=1&sygn=VII%2F1%2F13), [Kozaczuk bibliography and discussion](https://books.google.com/books?id=5hJnAAAAMAAJ))

- **probably 1934–1935:** *Cyclometer*. A special-purpose electromechanical cryptanalytic instrument designed by Rejewski to determine the cycle structures generated by Enigma. The date is not completely fixed—specialist accounts say “probably 1934 or 1935,” while an IPN chronology gives 1934—and the physical apparatus was constructed within the Cipher Bureau/AVA technical system rather than solely by Rejewski’s hands. ([Cyclometer study and references](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclometer), [IPN](https://ipn.gov.pl/pl/historia-z-ipn/157145%2Cgiganci-nauki-infografiki-historyczne-marian-rejewski.html))

- **c. 1935–1938:** *Catalog of characteristics*. A card-based data object containing 105,456 rotor-position entries, produced with the cyclometer and used as a lookup protocol for daily Enigma keys. Its construction took the Cipher Bureau team more than a year; a German reflector change in November 1937 forced a second catalog, and the 1938 indicator change made the system obsolete, so this is both a collective work and a lost operational artifact. ([Cyclometer and catalog account](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclometer), [Rejewski’s Catalog](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/01611190701299487))

- **c. October–November 1938:** *Bomba kryptologiczna*. An electromechanical key-finding design consisting operationally of six coupled Enigma replicas; Rejewski devised its principle after Germany changed its indicator procedure, while six physical bombas were built for the Cipher Bureau by mid-November. It should not be conflated with the later British bombe, whose method differed despite the related name and purpose. ([NSA](https://www.nsa.gov/History/Cryptologic-History/Historical-Figures/Historical-Figures-View/Article/1621548/marian-rejewski/), [IPN](https://ipn.gov.pl/pl/historia-z-ipn/157145%2Cgiganci-nauki-infografiki-historyczne-marian-rejewski.html), [Bomba history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bomba_%28cryptography%29))

- **1943–1944:** *Solutions of German hand ciphers at Station N, Boxmoor*. Rejewski and Henryk Zygalski jointly developed decrypting work that yielded 3,124 messages according to the Polish Biographical Dictionary. This is a body of operational results rather than a stable titled work, and individual authorship within it cannot be separated. ([Polish Biographical Dictionary](https://www.ipsb.nina.gov.pl/a/biografia/marian-adam-rejewski-1905-1980-matematyk-kryptolog-etc))

- **1976:** *Decipherment of Józef Piłsudski’s 1904 clandestine correspondence*. A commissioned cryptanalytic result made for the Józef Piłsudski Institute of America, concerning correspondence between Piłsudski and fellow Polish Socialist conspirators. The sources do not supply a fixed title or establish that Rejewski prepared a publishable edition, so it resists classification as a text in the ordinary bibliographical sense. ([Computer History Museum biography](https://history.computer.org/rejewski.html), [biographical account](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marian_Rejewski))

### Reports, memoirs, and historical testimony

- **1942:** *Sprawozdanie z prac kryptologicznych nad niemieckim szyfrem maszynowym Enigma*. A classified manuscript report written at Uzès on the Polish cryptanalytic work against Enigma. The archival record places a 1942 Enigma report by Rejewski in the Polish Institute and Sikorski Museum; it was not originally a public publication. ([Polish Biographical Dictionary](https://www.ipsb.nina.gov.pl/a/biografia/marian-adam-rejewski-1905-1980-matematyk-kryptolog-etc), [specialist bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptanalysis_of_the_Enigma))

- **April 1967:** *Wspomnienia z mej pracy w Biurze Szyfrów Oddziału II Sztabu Głównego w latach 1930–1945*. A typescript memoir and historical report acquired by Poland’s Military Historical Institute, later issued in scholarly editions. Sources and editions vary between *mej* and *mojej* and occasionally give the covered period as 1932–1945; the 2011 critical/facsimile edition uses 1930–1945, so those variations should not be turned into separate works. ([Rejewski chronology](https://marian-rejewski.pl/kalendarium-2/), [AMU edition review](https://press.amu.edu.pl/pub/media/productattach/r/e/rejewski_szyfry_wiad_mat.pdf), [Kórnik Digital Library record](https://platforma.bk.pan.pl/pl/search_results/1041092))

- **July–August 1974:** *Uzupełnienie do „Wspomnień z mej pracy w Biurze Szyfrów Oddziału II Sztabu Głównego w latach 1930–1945”*. A supplementary typescript deposited at the Military Historical Institute, correcting and enlarging the memoir in response to Gustave Bertrand’s book and inaccurate press accounts. It was later printed with the memoir and should not be mistaken for a revised edition of the whole. ([Rejewski chronology](https://marian-rejewski.pl/kalendarium-2/), [bibliographical description](https://biblioteka.bydgoszcz.pl/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Marian-Rejewski-bibliografia.pdf))

- **1970s, unfinished:** *Polish translation of Gustave Bertrand’s Enigma ou la plus grande énigme de la guerre 1939–1945*. A translation project begun at Bertrand’s encouragement; available accounts say Rejewski began translating the book but do not document a completed or published translation. It is therefore a project rather than a securely extant finished obra. ([biographical account](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marian_Rejewski))

- **1978; first published 1982:** *A Conversation with Marian Rejewski*. An oral-history interview made jointly with Richard A. Woytak and subsequently edited, translated, and published in differing manifestations, including a fuller transcript in *Werble historii*. Because interviewer, speaker, transcriber, translator, and editor all shaped the surviving text, it is testimony rather than an uncomplicated sole-authored article. ([MacTutor bibliography](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Rejewski/), [bibliographical account](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marian_Rejewski))

### Mathematical and historical articles

- **1979:** *Matematyczne podstawy rozwiązania niemieckiego szyfru maszynowego „Enigma”*. A technical-historical study published as the documentary appendix to Władysław Kozaczuk’s *W kręgu Enigmy*. Its later English manifestation, *The Mathematical Solution of the Enigma Cipher*, appeared in *Cryptologia* in 1982 and as Appendix E of Kozaczuk’s expanded English edition; these are translations/republications, not additional works. ([Polish Biographical Dictionary](https://www.ipsb.nina.gov.pl/a/biografia/marian-adam-rejewski-1905-1980-matematyk-kryptolog-etc), [Cryptologia bibliography](https://ftp.math.utah.edu/pub/tex/bib/cryptologia.pdf))

- **1979–1980:** *Zastosowanie teorii permutacji do złamania szyfru Enigma* / *An Application of the Theory of Permutations in Breaking the Enigma Cipher*. A mathematical paper in *Zastosowania Matematyki–Applicationes Mathematicae*, volume 16, no. 4, pp. 543–559. Bibliographical records variously attach 1977–1980 to the volume and 1980 to the issue; it overlaps substantially with, but is not bibliographically identical to, *Matematyczne podstawy rozwiązania…*. ([RCIN scholarly record](https://rcin.org.pl/dlibra/publication/61932/edition/45489?action=ChangeMetaLangAction&lang=en&language=pl), [journal text](https://bibliotekanauki.pl/articles/740785.pdf))

- **written 1979; published 1980:** *Jak matematycy polscy rozszyfrowali Enigmę*. A historical and technical article in *Wiadomości Matematyczne*, volume 23, no. 1, pp. 1–28, published posthumously. *How Polish Mathematicians Deciphered the Enigma* in the 1981 *Annals of the History of Computing* and *How the Polish Mathematicians Broke Enigma* in Kozaczuk’s English edition are translated manifestations of this text, accompanied in the journal by other authors’ afterwords. ([Polish History of Computing archive](https://historiainformatyki.pl/dokument.php?handle=892&nonav=&nrar=7&nrzesp=1&sygn=VII%2F1%2F13), [AMU bibliographical review](https://press.amu.edu.pl/media/productattach/1/3/1343-WIADOMOSCI_MATEMATYCZNE_t_48_2012_3.pdf))

- **1979; published 1982:** *Remarks on Appendix 1 to British Intelligence in the Second World War by F. H. Hinsley*. A corrective critical note responding to the treatment of the Polish Enigma contribution in the official British intelligence history. The surviving English publication was translated by Christopher Kasparek and is sometimes jointly indexed under Rejewski and Kasparek, although Kasparek’s documented role was translator rather than co-originator of the Polish argument. ([MacTutor bibliography](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Rejewski/), [contemporary bibliographical discussion](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/269568870_Enigma_the_French_the_Poles_and_the_British_1931_-1940))

- **date of original compilation not securely established; published 1984:** *Summary of Our Methods for Reconstructing Enigma and Reconstructing Daily Keys, and of German Efforts to Frustrate Those Methods*. A compressed technical-historical memorandum preserved as Appendix C of the expanded English *Enigma*. Its 1984 publication is posthumous and does not establish when Rejewski composed the underlying text, so no invented composition date should be assigned. ([Kozaczuk edition record](https://books.google.com/books?id=5hJnAAAAMAAJ), [specialist citation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclometer))

### Collaborative manifestations and contributions

- **1979:** *Sekret Enigmy* / *Tajemnica Enigmy*. A feature film and related television serial for which Rejewski served as a historical consultant, not as screenwriter or director. His contribution is real but resists treatment as an independently authored work: the audiovisual manifestations belong chiefly to director Roman Wionczek and the credited production team. ([Rejewski chronology](https://marian-rejewski.pl/kalendarium-2/), [Polish Biographical Dictionary](https://www.ipsb.nina.gov.pl/a/biografia/marian-adam-rejewski-1905-1980-matematyk-kryptolog-etc))
