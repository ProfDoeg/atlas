## Works by Élisabeth Sonrel

### Paintings, watercolours, and drawings documented by scholarship or exhibition catalogues

- **February 1890:** *L’Hiver*. Watercolour depicting a procession of veiled women in a winter forest; the date and medium are recorded by Charlotte Foucher, who places it in a private collection.

- **1892:** *Pax et Labor*. Painted diploma work, subsequently preserved by the Musée des Beaux-Arts de Tours; it is distinct from the similarly dated work sometimes circulated online as *Le Jardin des vierges*. [Foucher](https://fr.scribd.com/document/489598945/Elisabeth-Sonrel-1874-1953-une-artiste-pdf)

- **1894:** *Âmes errantes*. Large symbolist watercolour exhibited at the Salon; its present location is not securely documented. [Musée d’Orsay Salon database](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/317552)

- **1894:** *Vierge à l’Enfant*. Religious composition reported in the scholarly literature; its medium, present location, and exact exhibition history remain uncertain.

- **1894–1895:** *Le Sommeil de la Vierge*. Large watercolour exhibited at the 1895 Salon and again at the 1900 Exposition universelle. A surviving sheet dated 1894 may be the exhibited work or a modello; that identity remains disputed. [Le Cloître de l’art](https://lecloitredelart.com/produit/elisabeth-sonrel-le-sommeil-de-la-vierge/)

- **1895:** *Jeunes filles chantant*. Watercolour exhibited at the Salon alongside *Le Sommeil de la Vierge*; its present location is unrecorded. [Le Cloître de l’art](https://lecloitredelart.com/produit/elisabeth-sonrel-le-sommeil-de-la-vierge/)

- **1896:** *Le Christ pleuré par les saintes femmes*. Salon watercolour, now listed as unlocated. [Musée d’Orsay Salon database](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/344720)

- **1896:** *Automne*. Salon watercolour, now listed as unlocated. [Musée d’Orsay Salon database](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/objects/346432)

- **1896:** *Un après-midi au jardin*. Painting dated 1896; documented through the art market rather than the Salon record, so its early title and provenance require further confirmation. [Art.Salon](https://www.art.salon/artwork/elisabeth-sonrel_un-apres-midi-au-jardin_AID1039764)

- **1897:** *Le Cortège de Flore*. Large Salon painting, now in the Musée des Beaux-Arts de Mulhouse. [Foucher](https://fr.scribd.com/document/489598945/Elisabeth-Sonrel-1874-1953-une-artiste-pdf)

- **1897:** *Les Rameaux*. Salon oil painting, presently in a private collection; Sonrel subsequently made a closely related watercolour now in the Ashmolean Museum, so the two manifestations should not be collapsed into one object. [Ashmolean Museum](https://ashmolean.web.ox.ac.uk/article/elisabeth-sonrel-les-rameaux)

- **1898:** *La Vierge consolée* (also reported as *La Sainte Vierge consolée*). Watercolour exhibited with the Union des femmes peintres et sculpteurs; the variant title comes from contemporary reviews rather than proof of a second work. [Contemporary-review survey](https://elles-d-artistes.blogspot.com/2023/01/elisabeth-sonrel-1874-1953.html)

- **1898:** *Un pensionnat de jeunes filles* (contemporary shorthand: *Pensionnat en 1830*). Watercolour exhibited with the Union des femmes peintres et sculpteurs; the two wordings appear to name the same composition. [Contemporary-review survey](https://elles-d-artistes.blogspot.com/2023/01/elisabeth-sonrel-1874-1953.html)

- **1898:** *Dame rousse buvant du thé*. Watercolour known only through a contemporary review; this is a descriptive designation, not necessarily Sonrel’s fixed title. [Contemporary-review survey](https://elles-d-artistes.blogspot.com/2023/01/elisabeth-sonrel-1874-1953.html)

- **1898:** *L’Offrande à la Vierge*. Salon painting or large watercolour representing a religious procession; its current location is unknown. [Contemporary-review survey](https://elles-d-artistes.blogspot.com/2023/01/elisabeth-sonrel-1874-1953.html)

- **1899:** *Les Esprits de l’abîme*. Large Salon watercolour later recorded at auction; Foucher cautiously gives its subsequent location as a private collection, with a question mark. [Foucher](https://fr.scribd.com/document/489598945/Elisabeth-Sonrel-1874-1953-une-artiste-pdf)

- **1901?:** *La Vierge entourée d’anges*. Religious painting probably exhibited at the 1901 Salon and later installed in a chapel at Doué-la-Fontaine; the Salon date is explicitly uncertain in the scholarly source. [Foucher](https://fr.scribd.com/document/489598945/Elisabeth-Sonrel-1874-1953-une-artiste-pdf)

- **1902:** *Nymphe des bois*. Watercolour exhibited by the Société des arts de Mulhouse; its present location is not given. [Musée d’Orsay Salon database](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/objects/358877)

- **1903:** *La Princesse lointaine*. Salon portrait of Mélissinde, the heroine of Edmond Rostand’s play; the original is unlocated, while a reproductive lithograph survives in the Bibliothèque nationale de France. [Foucher](https://fr.scribd.com/document/489598945/Elisabeth-Sonrel-1874-1953-une-artiste-pdf)

- **1904:** *Ligéia*. Oil painting exhibited at the Salon, based on Edgar Allan Poe’s heroine; once described as unlocated, it has since reappeared in a private collection. [Galerie Alexis Bordes](https://www.alexis-bordes.com/fr/lagalerie/nouvelles-acquisitions/peinture/article/ligeia-1407)

- **1904:** *Le Bouquet de roses*. Salon watercolour, listed as unlocated. [Musée d’Orsay Salon database](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/332509)

- **circa 1910:** *Mère et enfants sur la place des Halles du Faouët*. Breton genre painting owned by the Département du Morbihan and deposited at the Musée du Faouët. [Foucher](https://fr.scribd.com/document/489598945/Elisabeth-Sonrel-1874-1953-une-artiste-pdf)

- **1912:** *La Fée de la forêt*. Salon work inspired by Celtic and Breton imagery; the original is unlocated, but a postcard reproduction documents its composition. [Foucher](https://fr.scribd.com/document/489598945/Elisabeth-Sonrel-1874-1953-une-artiste-pdf)

- **1912:** *Jeune dentellière de Pont-l’Abbé*. Salon watercolour, listed as unlocated. [Musée d’Orsay Salon database](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/417136)

- **1912:** *Fille de pêcheur (Concarneau)*. Salon watercolour, listed as unlocated. [Musée d’Orsay Salon database](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/objects/467137)

- **1913:** *Concert mystique*. Salon watercolour, now unlocated. [Musée d’Orsay Salon database](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/objects/480834)

- **1913:** *Les Deux Sœurs (Plougastel-Daoulas)*. Salon watercolour, now unlocated. [Musée d’Orsay Salon database](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/424022)

- **1913:** *Bretonnes à l’église*. Painting exhibited at the Première exposition des artistes tourangeaux; present location unknown. [Foucher](https://fr.scribd.com/document/489598945/Elisabeth-Sonrel-1874-1953-une-artiste-pdf)

- **1913:** *Vieille fileuse de Comfort*. Breton subject exhibited at the Première exposition des artistes tourangeaux; the catalogue’s spelling “Comfort” has been retained and the work is unlocated. [Foucher](https://fr.scribd.com/document/489598945/Elisabeth-Sonrel-1874-1953-une-artiste-pdf)

- **1913:** *Femme et enfant de Plougastel*. Breton figure painting exhibited at the Première exposition des artistes tourangeaux; present location unknown. [Foucher](https://fr.scribd.com/document/489598945/Elisabeth-Sonrel-1874-1953-une-artiste-pdf)

- **1913:** *La Sortie des barques (Concarneau)*. Breton marine or harbour scene exhibited at the Première exposition des artistes tourangeaux; present location unknown. [Foucher](https://fr.scribd.com/document/489598945/Elisabeth-Sonrel-1874-1953-une-artiste-pdf)

- **1913:** *Jeunes filles de Pont-L’Abbé*. Breton figure painting exhibited at the Première exposition des artistes tourangeaux; present location unknown. [Foucher](https://fr.scribd.com/document/489598945/Elisabeth-Sonrel-1874-1953-une-artiste-pdf)

- **1913:** *Jeune bigoudène à l’église (Loctudy)*. Breton figure painting exhibited at the Première exposition des artistes tourangeaux; Foucher notes that the original catalogue misspells both “Bigoudène” and “Loctudy.” [Foucher](https://fr.scribd.com/document/489598945/Elisabeth-Sonrel-1874-1953-une-artiste-pdf)

- **1914:** *Mireille*. Oil painting exhibited at the Salon; its quotation-marked catalogue title probably identifies Frédéric Mistral’s heroine, but the original is unlocated. [Musée d’Orsay Salon database](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/427261)

- **1914:** *La Liseuse au jardin*. Salon watercolour, listed as unlocated. [Musée d’Orsay Salon database](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/427261)

- **1914:** *Poésie du soir*. Salon watercolour, listed as unlocated. [Musée d’Orsay Salon database](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/objects/486634)

- **1916:** *Vierge à l’Enfant, entre sainte Geneviève et Jeanne d’Arc*. Religious painting whose title and date are preserved in later institutional documentation; its present status requires confirmation.

- **1924:** *Rêverie*. Salon portrait of a woman in medievalizing dress, modelled on Sonrel’s friend Éliane Chenal; recorded in a private collection in the United Kingdom. [Foucher](https://fr.scribd.com/document/489598945/Elisabeth-Sonrel-1874-1953-une-artiste-pdf)

- **circa 1926:** *Madone entourée d’enfants*. Religious painting discussed by Foucher without a firm title; the wording is therefore descriptive and should not be treated as a securely established catalogue title. [Foucher](https://fr.scribd.com/document/489598945/Elisabeth-Sonrel-1874-1953-une-artiste-pdf)

- **1931:** *Mélisande*. Drawing and watercolour exhibited at Remiremont; it is listed as unlocated. [Musée d’Orsay Salon database](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/objects/360856)

- **1931:** *Jeune Bretonne (Plougastel-Daoulas)*. Drawing and watercolour exhibited at Remiremont; it is listed as unlocated. [Musée d’Orsay Salon database](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/353911)

- **1940:** *Portrait de Jacqueline*. Late portrait drawing or watercolour; the sitter and date are identified by the dealer’s catalogue, but no continuous provenance is supplied. [Galerie Alexis Bordes](https://www.alexis-bordes.com/fr/lagalerie/nouvelles-acquisitions/dessin/article/portrait-de-jacqueline-1940)

- **1941:** *Vase d’anémones*. Watercolour still life, one of Sonrel’s last documented exhibited works.

### Undated or insecurely dated easel works

- **Undated:** *La Beauté cernée par l’envie*. Symbolist allegory known through Foucher’s study; neither a secure date nor present location is supplied.

- **Undated:** *L’Ange blond*. Painting or watercolour of an androgynous angel, recorded in a Monaco private collection. [Foucher](https://fr.scribd.com/document/489598945/Elisabeth-Sonrel-1874-1953-une-artiste-pdf)

- **Undated:** *Walkyries*. Watercolour mentioned in scholarship but not reproduced or fully catalogued there; its title, date, dimensions, and location remain uncertain. [Foucher](https://fr.scribd.com/document/489598945/Elisabeth-Sonrel-1874-1953-une-artiste-pdf)

- **Undated:** *Jeune femme à la tapisserie*. Symbolist female portrait repeatedly attributed to Sonrel, but without a securely published date or exhibition record.

- **Undated:** *La Forêt de Brocéliande*. Breton landscape or symbolist forest scene; surviving references do not establish a date or distinguish possible variants.

- **Undated:** *Le Goareva*. Breton marine or coastal landscape; date, medium, and present location remain unsettled.

- **Undated:** *Jeanne d’Arc*. Oil painting exhibited at the Salon in an incompletely dated catalogue record; now unlocated. [Musée d’Orsay Salon database](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/408581)

- **Undated:** *Jeune fille de Concarneau*. Salon watercolour, listed as unlocated. [Musée d’Orsay Salon database](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/408581)

- **Undated:** *À l’église (Bretagne)*. Salon watercolour, listed as unlocated. [Musée d’Orsay Salon database](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/408581)

- **Undated:** *La Reine du tournoi*. Watercolour documented in an exhibition catalogue and now unlocated. [Musée d’Orsay Salon database](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/419900)

- **Undated:** *Fleurs sauvages*. Watercolour documented in the same exhibition catalogue and now unlocated; it should not automatically be identified with the lithographic panel *Fleurs des champs*. [Musée d’Orsay Salon database](https://salons.musee-orsay.fr/Detail/entities/419900)

- **Undated:** *Sainte Élisabeth*. Small watercolour and black-chalk devotional image, usually dated circa 1900; the dating is stylistic rather than documentary. [Le Cloître de l’art](https://lecloitredelart.com/produit/elisabeth-sonrel-sainte-elisabeth/)

- **Undated:** *Jeune fille du Cap Sizun*. Watercolour portrait; no secure date has been published.

- **Undated:** *Portrait de femme aux yeux verts*. Oil portrait known through the art market; its descriptive title may not be original. [Mathieu Néouze catalogue](https://www.mathieuneouze.fr/content/9-catalogues/2-tableaux-dessins-sculptures-2013/cataloguemars2013.pdf)

- **Undated:** *Five portraits of the children of Éliane Chenal*. A group visible in a period photograph but not individually titled or dated. They resist entry as five named works because the surviving scholarly record does not identify the children or establish whether the group remained intact. [Foucher](https://fr.scribd.com/document/489598945/Elisabeth-Sonrel-1874-1953-une-artiste-pdf)

### Graphic cycles and reproducible images

- **circa 1900:** *Fleurs des champs*. Colour lithographic decorative panel and one member of the four-part *Fleurs* cycle. [Le Feu Follet](https://www.abebooks.fr/art-affiches/Fleurs-champs-SONREL-Elisabeth/22471349941/bd)

- **circa 1900:** *Fleurs de montagne*. Colour lithographic decorative panel belonging to the *Fleurs* cycle.

- **circa 1900:** *Fleurs des eaux*. Colour lithographic decorative panel belonging to the *Fleurs* cycle.

- **circa 1900:** *Fleurs de serre*. Colour lithographic decorative panel belonging to the *Fleurs* cycle.

- **circa 1900:** *Printemps*. Decorative lithographic or poster panel belonging to Sonrel’s cycle of the four seasons.

- **circa 1900:** *Été*. Decorative lithographic or poster panel belonging to Sonrel’s cycle of the four seasons.

- **circa 1900:** *Automne*. Decorative lithographic or poster panel belonging to Sonrel’s cycle of the four seasons; this manifestation should not be assumed to be the same object as the 1896 Salon watercolour.

- **circa 1900:** *Hiver*. Decorative lithographic or poster panel belonging to Sonrel’s cycle of the four seasons; it is distinct from the watercolour dated 1890.

- **circa 1900:** *Les Quatre Oiseaux*. A four-image decorative cycle commonly separated into individual panels; surviving references do not establish whether Sonrel conceived it as one obra or four autonomous works.

- **circa 1900:** *L. Brancher*. Commercial poster designed for the printing firm L. Brancher; this is an advertisement—an image intended to act in public—rather than an easel painting.

- **1913 onward:** *Cover design for La Touraine littéraire et artistique*. Periodical cover used from October 1913; it is a repeatable graphic manifestation rather than a unique object. [Foucher](https://fr.scribd.com/document/489598945/Elisabeth-Sonrel-1874-1953-une-artiste-pdf)

- **First World War:** *Le Héros*. Patriotic print or illustrated sheet preserved by La Contemporaine; its precise date and production circumstances remain to be established.

- **First World War:** *L’Enfant serbe, l’enfant belge, l’enfant français*. Patriotic image—possibly conceived as a composite sheet or related series—preserved by La Contemporaine; the surviving bibliographic references do not settle whether the three children constitute one obra or three manifestations.

- **1916:** *Vierge à l’Enfant, entre sainte Geneviève et Jeanne d’Arc*. The composition also circulated as a reproductive image; the print is a manifestation derived from the painted design, not necessarily a second independent project.

### Illustrated missals and book projects

- **1899:** *Missel avec illustrations de Mademoiselle Sonrel*. Illustrated Catholic missal published by Mame, with polychrome Art Nouveau decoration throughout. The generic title and later reissues make it difficult to determine whether every surviving “Missel Sonrel” represents this project or a new commission. [Drouot](https://drouot.com/fr/l/34576867-sonrel-missale-con-illustrazioni-della-signorina-sonrel)

- **1900:** *Missel des saintes femmes de France*. Luxury missal published by Mame with ten colour plates and ornamental borders by Sonrel; her individual images include representations of Bernadette of Lourdes, Sainte Odile, and Sainte Germaine. [Christie’s](https://www.christies.com/en/lot/lot-5039071?from=siteindex&intobjectid=5039071&lid=1)

- **1902:** *Missel des Saints anges*. Illustrated missal published by A. Mame et fils; this is a coordinated book-design project rather than an authored liturgical text. [Foucher](https://fr.scribd.com/document/489598945/Elisabeth-Sonrel-1874-1953-une-artiste-pdf)

- **1909:** *Missel*. Illustrated missal published by Maison Mame and catalogued by the Metropolitan Museum of Art under this generic title; whether its illustrations constitute a new Sonrel cycle or reuse earlier designs is not stated. [Metropolitan Museum of Art](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/354088)

- **Undated, late nineteenth or early twentieth century:** *Missel des Litanies de la Sainte Vierge*. Missal with a chromolithographic frontispiece, a second full-page illustration, and page borders attributed to Sonrel; no secure publication year is given. [Librairie Pierre Brunet](https://www.librairie-pierre-brunet.fr/librairie-en-ligne/fm30642-missel-misseldeslitaniesdelasaintevierge/category_pathway-487.html)

- **1932:** *Missel de Lourdes, dédié aux fidèles serviteurs de Marie*. Illustrated devotional book published at Turnhout; Sonrel supplied images rather than the liturgical text.

- **1943:** *Missel de Jésus-Enfant*. Late illustrated missal published by Droguet, R. Ardant et Fils, with colour figures attributed to Sonrel. [Bibliothèque nationale de France](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb368148092)

### Designs realized by other makers

- **1899:** *Flora*. Sonrel’s design translated by Neyret Frères into a woven-silk picture. The silk is a manufactured manifestation after her image, not an object woven by Sonrel herself. [Foucher](https://fr.scribd.com/document/489598945/Elisabeth-Sonrel-1874-1953-une-artiste-pdf)

- **Undated, early twentieth century:** *Le Fil de la Vierge*. Sonrel design realized as a woven-silk picture by Neyret Frères; the division of authorship between image-maker and textile manufacturer should be retained. [Dell’Arte](https://dellarte.fr/ja/sakuhin/le-fil-de-la-vierge--10230007898)

- **Undated, early twentieth century:** *Les Rameaux*. Woven-silk image manufactured by Neyret Frères after Sonrel’s composition; it is a materially distinct manifestation of the 1897 design.

- **Undated, early twentieth century:** *Fourth woven-silk design preserved at Saint-Étienne*. Foucher records three Sonrel titles among a group of four silks in the Musée d’Art et d’Industrie de Saint-Étienne, but the fourth title is not supplied in the accessible scholarly account; inventing one would falsely complete the set.

- **Undated:** *Jewellery designs*. Sonrel reportedly had pieces of jewellery fabricated from some of her drawings. No secure object list, makers, or dates have been published, so this survives as a documented design practice rather than a catalogue of nameable works. [Galerie Alexis Bordes](https://www.alexis-bordes.com/IMG/pdf/abordescatalogue_catalogue_expomars2019_10.pdf)

### Architectural and domestic decoration

- **circa 1910–1953:** *Painted overmantel representing the legend of the Rhine*. Mural or fixed domestic decoration formerly in Sonrel’s house at 53 rue des Chêneaux, Sceaux; known from later occupants’ recollections and apparently lost. It resists the status of a securely catalogued work because neither its title nor its completion date is documented. [Foucher](https://fr.scribd.com/document/489598945/Elisabeth-Sonrel-1874-1953-une-artiste-pdf)

- **circa 1910–1953:** *Decorated cupboard doors at 53 rue des Chêneaux*. Fixed domestic paintings attributed to Sonrel and reportedly removed or discarded after her death. They are evidence of an obra embedded in a house, but their number, subjects, and dates are unknown. [Foucher](https://fr.scribd.com/document/489598945/Elisabeth-Sonrel-1874-1953-une-artiste-pdf)

- **circa 1910–1953:** *Unidentified prize-winning painting formerly at 53 rue des Chêneaux*. A work remembered as having won a prize in the United States; no title, date, image, or prize record is supplied, so it cannot responsibly be equated with another named painting. [Foucher](https://fr.scribd.com/document/489598945/Elisabeth-Sonrel-1874-1953-une-artiste-pdf)

- No published catalogue raisonné or complete collected-works inventory for Sonrel was located. Consequently, this is the traceable corpus recoverable from the standard scholarly study, primary Salon catalogues, and institutional object records—not a claim that every privately held, untitled, lost, or unrecorded sheet has been recovered. Auction titles unsupported by those sources have generally been excluded, because the same composition may recur in oil, watercolour, lithograph, postcard, and woven silk, and art-market naming often obscures those distinctions.
