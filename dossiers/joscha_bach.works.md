## Works by Joscha Bach

### Book, dissertation, and edited proceedings

- **2006–2007:** *Principles of Synthetic Intelligence: Building Blocks for an Architecture of Motivated Cognition*. Doctoral dissertation developing the MicroPsi cognitive architecture; sources differ between the 2006 degree date and the manuscript’s dated submission of 30 March 2007, so the dating is not silently resolved. ([Bach CV](https://bach.ai/pub/CV%20Joscha%20Bach.pdf), [dissertation manuscript](https://cognitive-ai.com/publications/assets/Draft-MicroPsi-JBach-07-03-30.pdf))
- **2009:** *Principles of Synthetic Intelligence: PSI: An Architecture of Motivated Cognition*. Scholarly monograph and substantially reworked manifestation of the dissertation, adapting Dietrich Dörner’s Psi theory into a theoretical and implementable cognitive architecture. ([Oxford University Press](https://academic.oup.com/book/1947))
- **2011:** *KI 2011: Advances in Artificial Intelligence*. Edited conference proceedings, co-edited with Stefan Edelkamp; this is editorial work rather than a solely authored text. ([DBLP](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/ki/2011))
- **2012:** *Artificial General Intelligence: 5th International Conference, AGI 2012*. Edited conference proceedings, co-edited with Ben Goertzel and Matthew Iklé. ([DBLP](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/agi/2012))

### Cognitive architectures, software, and computational projects

- **2003–2009:** *MicroPsi*. A project, cognitive architecture, and Java software framework for motivated agents—not merely a text. Bach originated it, while papers and implementations were also made with collaborators; consequently sole authorship of the entire evolving system should not be inferred. ([MicroPsi project](https://cognitive-ai.com/page10/page10.html), [project description](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psi-theory))
- **2012–:** *MicroPsi 2*. A second-generation Python cognitive-architecture project comprising a node-net runtime, editors, monitoring tools, and a simulated environment; its open-ended development makes it a continuing project rather than a finished obra. ([project page](https://cognitive-ai.com/page10/page10.html), [GitHub repository](https://github.com/joschabach/micropsi2))
- **Undated:** *motivation_machine*. An open-source demonstration implementation of MicroPsi’s motivation model. It resists treatment as an independent theoretical work because the repository presents it as a software demonstration. ([GitHub profile](https://github.com/joschabach))
- **Undated:** *reputation_game*. An open-source simulation of indirect reciprocity. The public record identifies the software object but supplies no defensible fixed creation date. ([GitHub profile](https://github.com/joschabach))
- **Undated:** *biblioskop*. A public Objective-C repository attributed to Bach; its sparse catalogue description does not establish whether it is completed software, an experiment, or an abandoned project. ([GitHub profile](https://github.com/joschabach))
- **2016:** *Hypergraph Visualization for Understanding Entanglement*. A Wolfram Summer School research project using rewriting hypergraphs to investigate digital physics; it is documented as a project rather than a formally published paper. ([Wolfram Summer School](https://education.wolfram.com/summer-research-institute/alumni/2016/bach/))

### Journal articles, chapters, and refereed papers

- **2000:** *AT Humboldt 2000 (Team Description)*. Collaborative RoboCup team-description paper; Bach is one of seven authors. ([DBLP](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/robocup/BurkhardBSWGMS00))
- **2001:** *Supporting Textual Input by Using Multiple Entropy Models*. Journal article on text compression. ([DBLP](https://dblp.org/rec/journals/fuin/Bach01))
- **2001:** *Mental Models for Robot Control*. Collaborative conference paper with Hans-Dieter Burkhard, Ralf Berger, Birger Brunswieck, and Michael Gollin. ([DBLP](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/dagstuhl/BurkhardBBBG01))
- **2001:** *Self-Localisation Revisited*. RoboCup conference paper co-authored with Michael Gollin. ([DBLP](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/robocup/BachG01))
- **2002:** *Enhancing Perception and Planning of Software Agents with Emotion and Acquired Hierarchical Categories*. Workshop paper on emotion, perception, and planning in software agents. ([Bach’s publications bibliography](https://bach.ai/publications/))

- **2003:** *Emotionale Virtuelle Agenten auf der Basis der Dörnerschen Psi-Theorie*. German-language workshop paper on emotional virtual agents grounded in Dörner’s Psi theory. ([Bach’s publications bibliography](https://bach.ai/publications/))
- **2003:** *Connecting MicroPsi Agents to Virtual and Physical Environments / Applications of the AEP Toolkit*. The official bibliography runs these titles together, leaving it unclear whether they denote one contribution, two linked workshop texts, or a cataloguing error; they should not be silently split into two certain works. ([Bach’s publications bibliography](https://bach.ai/publications/))
- **2003:** *The AEP Toolkit for Agent Design and Simulation*. Conference paper co-authored with Ronnie Vuine, describing a software toolkit as well as a method. ([DBLP](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/mates/BachV03))
- **2003:** *Designing Agents with MicroPsi Node Nets*. Conference paper co-authored with Ronnie Vuine. ([DBLP](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/ki/BachV03))
- **2003:** *Designing Agent Behavior with the Extensible Agent Behavior Specification Language XABSL*. RoboCup paper co-authored with Martin Lötzsch, Hans-Dieter Burkhard, and Matthias Jüngel. ([DBLP](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/robocup/LotzschBBJ03))
- **2003:** *Recognizing and Predicting Agent Behavior with Case Based Reasoning*. RoboCup paper co-authored with Jan Wendler. ([DBLP](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/robocup/WendlerB03))

- **2004:** *A Neural Implementation of Plan-Based Control*. Workshop paper co-authored with Ronnie Vuine. ([Bach’s publications bibliography](https://bach.ai/publications/))
- **2005:** *Representations for a Complex World: Combining Distributed and Localist Representations for Learning and Planning*. Book chapter/conference contribution on combining representational schemes. ([DBLP](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/mirrorbot/Bach05))

- **2006:** *MicroPsi: Contributions to a Broad Architecture of Cognition*. Conference paper co-authored with Colin Bauer and Ronnie Vuine. ([DBLP](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/ki/BachBV06))

- **2011:** *No Room for the Mind: Enactivism and Artificial Intelligence*. Philosophy-of-computing conference paper; it appears in Bach’s later references but not in the older web bibliography, an incompleteness in the latter rather than grounds to suppress the work. Conference paper co-authored with Mario Verdicchio; Bach’s bibliography labels it “to appear,” while later bibliographies record the proceedings publication, so its status changed after the original list was compiled. - **2015:** *Modeling Motivation in the MicroPsi Architecture* / *Modeling Motivation in MicroPsi 2*. Refereed AGI paper; Bach’s CV and the published record use slightly different titles, so they are treated as variant manifestations rather than two works. ([Bach CV](https://bach.ai/pub/CV%20Joscha%20Bach.pdf), [DBLP bibliography](https://dblp.org/pid/09/5780.html))

- **2017:** *The Cortical Conductor Theory: Towards Addressing Consciousness in AI Models*. Conference/workshop paper proposing a functional architecture of consciousness. ([Bach CV](https://bach.ai/pub/CV%20Joscha%20Bach.pdf), [author manuscript](https://bach.ai/pub/Bach%20-%20Cortical%20Conductor%20Theory%20%28BICA%202018%29.pdf))

- **2017:** *The Animat Path to Artificial General Intelligence*. Collaborative workshop paper with Claes Strannegård, Nils Svangård, David Lindström, and Bas Steunebrink. ([Bach CV](https://bach.ai/pub/CV%20Joscha%20Bach.pdf))
- **2017:** *Generic Animats*. Refereed conference paper co-authored with Claes Strannegård, Nils Svangård, and Bas Steunebrink. ([DBLP bibliography](https://dblp.org/pid/09/5780.html))

- **2022:** *Functionality and Phenomenology of Consciousness*. Models of Consciousness conference contribution; its status appears closer to a conference text or abstract than a fully documented article. ([Bach CV](https://bach.ai/pub/CV%20Joscha%20Bach.pdf))

- **2023:** *Generative Image Model Benchmark for Reasoning and Representation (GIMBRR)*. Workshop paper co-authored with Jascha Achterberg, Itamar Arel, Achraf Chaibi, and Nikos Tzagarakis; it describes a benchmark, but the cited record does not establish a separately released dataset. ([Bach CV](https://bach.ai/pub/CV%20Joscha%20Bach.pdf))
- **2023:** *A Path to Generative Artificial Selves*. Refereed conference paper co-authored with Liane Gabora. ([DBLP](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/epia/GaboraB23))
- **2024:** *Building Artificial Intelligence with Creative Agency and Self-hood*. Co-authored preprint with Liane Gabora, explicitly presented as a lay summary of related research rather than a new full technical study. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.10978))

### Tutorials, symposia, and instructional protocols

- **2005:** *Psi and MicroPsi: Building Blocks for a Cognitive Architecture*. Collaborative conference symposium with Dietrich Dörner, Jörn Gerdes, and Andreas Zundel; it is an organized scholarly event and presentation, not securely documented as a standalone publication. ([Bach’s publications bibliography](https://bach.ai/publications/))
- **2006:** *Psi and MicroPsi: A Novel Approach to Modeling Emotion and Cognition in a Cognitive Architecture*. Tutorial co-created with Dietrich Dörner and Ronnie Vuine; as a tutorial it is an instructional protocol performed at a conference rather than simply a descriptive paper. ([Bach’s publications bibliography](https://bach.ai/publications/))
- **2011:** *Modeling Emotion as an Interaction Between Motivation and Modulated Cognition*. Workshop contribution/tutorial whose bibliographic record does not establish a full published text. ([Bach’s publications bibliography](https://bach.ai/publications/))
- **2013:** *Autonomy and the Computational Mind*. Presentation at the Icelandic Institute for Intelligent Machines; the surviving documentation identifies a performed talk, not a formal publication. ([AAAI tutorial document](https://bach.ai/pub/Tutorial%20Implementing%20Motivation%20and%20Emotion%20in%20AI%20models%20AAAI%2018.pdf))
- **2014:** *The MicroPsi Cognitive Architecture*. Conference tutorial co-created with Ronnie Vuine. Its primary form was instruction and demonstration, not an autonomous book or article. ([AAAI tutorial document](https://bach.ai/pub/Tutorial%20Implementing%20Motivation%20and%20Emotion%20in%20AI%20models%20AAAI%2018.pdf))
- **2014:** *The Machine of Desire*. Tutorial presentation at Google; the available record documents the event but not a stable published text. ([AAAI tutorial document](https://bach.ai/pub/Tutorial%20Implementing%20Motivation%20and%20Emotion%20in%20AI%20models%20AAAI%2018.pdf))
- **2018:** *Implementing Motivation and Emotion in AI Architectures*. AAAI tutorial and accompanying slide document: part protocol, part lecture, and part technical synthesis. ([tutorial document](https://bach.ai/pub/Tutorial%20Implementing%20Motivation%20and%20Emotion%20in%20AI%20models%20AAAI%2018.pdf))
