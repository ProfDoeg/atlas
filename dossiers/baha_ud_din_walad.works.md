## Works by Baha ud-Din Walad

### Recorded discourses and their textual manifestation

- **Before 1231:** *Maʿārif* (“Knowledges”). His sole surviving literary obra: a Persian, four-book assemblage of sermons, mystical reflections, Qurʾanic and prophetic interpretation, and answers to theological and legal questions. It resists simple authorship—the discourses are Bahāʾ al-Dīn’s, but disciples recorded and collected them; moreover, Book IV may partly preserve drafts for material in Books I–III, and Rūmī may have added glosses to it ([Algar](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/baha-al-din-mohammad-walad-b/); [Meier](https://brill.com/display/book/9789004671485/front-2.xml)).

### Juridical acts rather than a surviving work

- **Date unknown (before 1231):** Fatwās issued as a mufti. These legal rulings were protocols in the operative sense: decisions meant to govern conduct, not merely describe doctrine. They resist treatment as a discrete obra because no titled or independently transmitted collection is known; their existence is attested within *Maʿārif*, where Bahāʾ al-Dīn says that he signed fatwās with the title *Sulṭān al-ʿulamāʾ* ([Algar](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/baha-al-din-mohammad-walad-b/)).
