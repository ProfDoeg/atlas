## Works by Arthur Bispo do Rosário

### Gesamtkunstwerk, project, and inventory

- **c. 1938–1989:** *Inventário do Mundo*. Lifelong project to collect, name, classify, embroider, build, and preserve the things he believed must be presented at the Last Judgment; the surviving corpus—more than one thousand catalogued items—can be understood as one Gesamtkunstwerk rather than merely a collection of autonomous artworks. Its boundaries remain unstable because objects were combined, dismantled, lost, or only later identified; the museum describes its catalogue as complete but continually revisable. [Catalogue methodology](https://museubispodorosario.com/catalogoraisonne/sobre/)
- **c. 1938–1989:** *Museu-casa*. The ordering of Bispo’s rooms, furnishings, numbered possessions, vitrines, supports, and containers constituted an inhabited archive made before there was an institutional museum. It resists classification as a separate work: its original arrangement largely survives only through constituent objects and documentation. [Vinícius José, “O ‘museu’ antes do museu”](https://museubispodorosario.com/catalogoraisonne/sobre/)

### Estandartes: embroidered textile records

- *África*. Estandarte combining paper, wood, cloth, metal, drawing, painting, and writing; catalogued as MBRAC.AB.0506 and treated as part of the inventory project rather than as a conventional picture.
- *Avenida Rosangela Maria*. Embroidered and written textile estandarte, MBRAC.ABR.0005, joining institutional life, personal attachment, and inventory.
- *Colônia Juliano Moreira / Reconheceram o filho de Deus*. Embroidered estandarte, MBRAC.ABR.0006; the compound catalogue title preserves two inscriptions or identities rather than resolving them into a single stable title.
- *Dicionário de nomes letra A I*. Large embroidered estandarte, MBRAC.ABR.0007, functioning as a dictionary or register of names as much as a textile object.
- *Eu preciso destas palavras escrita / Desenhos Geométricos*. Double-sided or compoundly identified estandarte, MBRAC.ABR.0001, in which writing, drawing, embroidery, and inventory are inseparable.
- *Navios de guerra*. Embroidered estandarte, MBRAC.ABR.0004, carrying Bispo’s maritime memory into a textile register.  
  [Catalogue records for the six estandartes](https://museubispodorosario.com/catalogoraisonne/obras/)

### Indumentária and ceremonial manifestation

- *Manto da Apresentação*. Embroidered mantle, MBRAC.ABR.0013, intended for Bispo’s presentation before God rather than simply for exhibition; it is simultaneously garment, cosmological register, ritual apparatus, and manifesto of his mission. The museum’s page heading also calls it *Manto de Apresentação*, so the title is not entirely uniform. [Catalogue record](https://museubispodorosario.com/catalogoraisonne/obras/)
- *Dulce Mãe*. Embroidered “faixa de miss,” MBRAC.ABR.0621 and personal inventory no. 3.001; it borrows the form of a beauty-pageant sash for Bispo’s system of naming people and places. [Catalogue record](https://museubispodorosario.com/catalogoraisonne/obras/)
- *Capas protetoras*. A variably sized set, MBRAC.ABR.0791, of sewn plastic, cloth, paper, and rubberized covers made to protect parts of the collection. The plural title and varied dimensions make this a functional ensemble rather than an securely bounded single work. [Catalogue record](https://museubispodorosario.com/catalogoraisonne/obras/)

### Embarcações and maritime constructions

- *Grande veleiro*. Large constructed vessel, MBRAC.ABR.0427 and personal inventory no. 956, assembled from wood, textile, plastic, metal, paper, and found materials; it is a model, mnemonic object, and vehicle for an imagined passage.
- *Navio Indústria de adubos Jaguaré S.A.* Framed drawing, MBRAC.ABR.0802, made in graphite and colored pencil; unlike Bispo’s constructed boats, this is a two-dimensional maritime representation.
- *Moinho de cana*. Small wrapped and embroidered object, MBRAC.ABR.0139 and personal inventory no. 415, classified as an ORFA: an “object wrapped in blue thread,” Bispo’s transformation of a referent into a bound, textile-covered surrogate.  
  [Catalogue records for the maritime works and ORFA](https://museubispodorosario.com/catalogoraisonne/obras/)

### Vitrines, fichários, and cartelas

- *Botões para paletó*. Cartela, MBRAC.ABR.0610 and personal inventory no. 8.034, on which buttons of many materials are sewn and classified; it is simultaneously specimen board, material archive, and text-bearing object.
- *Coleção de fichários*. Wooden vitrine, MBRAC.ABR.0440, containing filing structures through which people or things could be ordered; its title names both the container and the collection it contains.
- *Talheres*. Vitrine, MBRAC.ABR.0395, arranging manufactured cutlery and other materials as an inventory display; Bispo’s making lies principally in selection, construction, and ordering rather than fabrication of the utensils.
- *Vitrine-fichário VI Alice Santo Oliveira Jesus*. Wooden vitrine-fichário, MBRAC.ABR.0433 and personal inventory no. 60, combining written records with an architectural display structure to register a named person.
- *Fichas de relatos – Mataram mulher tiro foi encontrada…*. Set of written, pierced, sewn, and painted record cards, MBRAC.ABR.0420.2. The catalogue title is an identifying incipit ending in an ellipsis, not necessarily an authorially fixed title; the work is both textual record and material filing apparatus.  
  [Catalogue records for the vitrines, cartela, and fichas](https://museubispodorosario.com/catalogoraisonne/obras/)

### Assemblages, compositions, furniture, and supports

- *Caixa depósito*. Box-like composition, MBRAC.AB.0776, assembled from wood, polystyrene, paper, metal, and plastic; it is simultaneously container, labelled inventory component, and constructed object.
- *Cama Romeu e Julieta*. Full-sized wooden and textile bed, catalogued as MBAC.ABR.667 on the museum page, joining furniture, embroidered inscription, institutional life, and Bispo’s relationship with Rosangela Maria. The abbreviated accession prefix may be a catalogue transcription variant rather than a different collection.
- *Chaleira e Urinol*. Wheeled “carrinho de espera,” MBRAC.ABR.0653 and personal inventory no. 238, holding manufactured utensils and diverse materials. It resists treatment as sculpture alone because the museum identifies such carts as functional storage furniture within Bispo’s rooms.
- *Muro no fundo da minha casa*. Small composition, MBRAC.AB.0299 and personal inventory no. 434, made from cement, wood, glass, and metal; it is an architectural fragment or model rather than an actual wall.
- *Roda da Fortuna*. Constructed composition, MBRAC.ABR.0298.0, combining wood, metal, textile, plastic, writing, and paint; its hybrid construction prevents a clean division between model, apparatus, game, and sculpture.
- *Vote presidente*. Small wooden miniature, MBRAC.ABR.0471, with writing and paint; it is a model-like political sign or scene rather than a manifesto in the strict textual sense.  
  [Catalogue records for the compositions, furniture, cart, and miniature](https://museubispodorosario.com/catalogoraisonne/obras/)

### Manuscripts and textual objects

- *Conjunto de escritos*. Group of manuscripts on paper, MBRAC.ABR.0973, made with graphite, ink, and fountain-pen ink. The catalogue treats the surviving papers as one assembled set; “conjunto” signals that neither its original completeness nor the autonomy of each leaf should be assumed. [Catalogue record](https://museubispodorosario.com/catalogoraisonne/obras/)

### Collected industrial objects: attribution resisted

- *Jornais*. Manufactured newspapers stored in a wooden-and-plastic arrangement, MBRAC.ABR.0626. The catalogue designates them “objeto industrializado,” meaning the papers themselves were not made or directly altered by Bispo; their inclusion records his collecting and ordering, so authorship belongs to the assemblage or inventory act, not to the printed objects. [Catalogue record](https://museubispodorosario.com/catalogoraisonne/obras/)
