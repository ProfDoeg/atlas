## Works by Georges Sorel

### Historical, philosophical, and scientific studies

- **1889:** *Contribution à l’étude profane de la Bible*. A book-length work of secular biblical criticism, signed “E. G. [Eugène-Georges] Sorel” and completed by eight appendices on the prophets. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1889:** *Les Girondins du Roussillon*. A regional historical study first published in the local learned society’s bulletin and also issued as an offprint; the offprint is a manifestation of the article, not a different work. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1889:** *Le procès de Socrate. Examen critique des thèses socratiques*. A book-length historical and philosophical reinterpretation of Socrates. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1888–1889:** *Les représentants du peuple à l’armée des Pyrénées-Orientales*. A historical study serialized in the *Revue historique de la Révolution* and issued without a printed date as an offprint conventionally assigned to 1889. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1892:** *Françoise Ducruix. Contribution à la psychologie des Maratistes*. A historical-psychological study published in a learned-society bulletin and separately as an offprint. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1892:** *Essai sur la philosophie de Proudhon*. A two-part journal essay rather than an independently conceived book; its later appearance in collections does not constitute another work. [Bibliography](https://liberale.erasmo.it/testi/0478.htm)
- **1894:** *L’ancienne et la nouvelle métaphysique*. A serial philosophical study in *L’Ère nouvelle*, later posthumously remade as the book *D’Aristote à Marx*. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1894:** *La fin du paganisme*. A serialized historical interpretation subsequently expanded and transformed into *La ruine du monde antique*; the two forms are related but not textually identical. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1895:** *Les théories de M. Durkheim*. A sociological critique published in *Le Devenir social*, later issued as an offprint and incorporated into posthumous collections. [CCFr record](https://ccfr.bnf.fr/portailccfr/ark%3A/16871/0014742833)
- **1896:** *Étude sur Vico*. A three-part journal study in *Le Devenir social*, not originally a book; it later became the principal Vico text in posthumous collections. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1896:** *La science dans l’éducation*. A journal study whose projected French book edition never appeared; the 1984 Italian volume is a posthumous translation and editorial manifestation, not a new work by Sorel. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1901:** *La valeur sociale de l’art*. The text of a lecture at the École des hautes études sociales, published first as an article and then as a short book: simultaneously lecture, essay, and printed manifestation. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1902:** *La ruine du monde antique. Conception matérialiste de l’histoire*. A book developed from the 1894 *La fin du paganisme* articles and incorporating “Église, Évangile et socialisme”; Sorel prepared additions for the posthumous 1925 edition. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1903:** *La crise de la pensée catholique*. A short book manifestation of an essay first published in 1902 in the *Revue de métaphysique et de morale*. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1903:** *Sur divers aspects de la mécanique*. A scientific-philosophical journal essay also issued as a 31-page offprint; it resists classification as a “book” despite that separate physical manifestation. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1905–1906:** *Le système historique de Renan*. A sustained study issued as four fascicles—introduction, Judaism, Christianity, and the apostolic period—forming one volume and one composite obra. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1905/1907:** *Les préoccupations métaphysiques des physiciens modernes*. A philosophical essay first published in 1905 and made into a *Cahiers de la quinzaine* volume in April 1907, with a previously published review appended. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1910–1911:** *Vues sur les problèmes de la philosophie*. A two-part journal essay closely connected with Sorel’s pragmatist project rather than a separately published book. [Mil neuf cent](https://shs.cairn.info/revue-mil-neuf-cent-2014-1-page-93?lang=fr)
- **1920/1928:** *Ultime meditazioni*. Sorel’s last extended meditation, written in January 1920 but published posthumously in Italian in 1928; its delayed, translated manifestation makes the original textual status unusually mediated. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1921:** *De l’utilité du pragmatisme*. A book-length philosophical study of truth, experiment, Bergson, and William James, assembled from Sorel’s mature work on pragmatism. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1894/1935:** *D’Aristote à Marx. L’ancienne et la nouvelle métaphysique*. A posthumous book manifestation, edited and introduced by Édouard Berth from Sorel’s 1894 serial study; the underlying work is Sorel’s, while the title and book-object are posthumous editorial constructions. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)

### Marxist criticism, economics, and syndicalist theory

- **1898:** *L’avenir socialiste des syndicats*. A programmatic essay first serialized and then issued as a pamphlet; Sorel repeatedly revised and enlarged it, and it later became part of *Matériaux d’une théorie du prolétariat*. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1898:** *La necessità e il fatalismo nel marxismo*. An Italian pamphlet/offprint derived from an article; later absorbed into *Saggi di critica del marxismo*. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1899:** *Dove va il marxismo?* A short Italian pamphlet/offprint asking where Marxism was headed; materially a separate publication, intellectually a contribution to Sorel’s continuing revisionist controversy. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1899:** *Le idee giuridiche nel marxismo*. An Italian legal-theoretical essay issued separately and later incorporated into *Saggi di critica del marxismo*. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1899:** *L’evoluzione del socialismo in Francia*. An Italian offprint of an essay on French socialism; the bibliography prints 1898 on the publication but records its journal appearance in June 1899, leaving a real dating discrepancy. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1899:** *Lo spirito pubblico in Francia*. A nine-page Italian offprint on French public opinion, originating in the *Rivista popolare di politica*. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1899:** *Il Vangelo, la Chiesa e il socialismo*. An Italian pamphlet assembled from two journal installments; its French version, “Église, Évangile et socialisme,” was later incorporated into *La ruine du monde antique*. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1899:** *L’éthique du socialisme*. A lecture delivered at the Collège libre des sciences sociales and published both in a journal and in the collective volume *La morale sociale*; it is a lecture-text, not a Sorel-authored book. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1900:** *Costruzione del sistema della storia secondo Marx*. An Italian offprint of an essay on Marx’s construction of history, subsequently incorporated into *Saggi di critica del marxismo*. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1900:** *Les polémiques pour l’interprétation du marxisme. Bernstein et Kautsky*. A two-part journal intervention also issued as a pamphlet and later collected; it belongs to a controversy and therefore acts as an argumentative intervention as much as a descriptive essay. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1900:** *Science et morale* and *Les facteurs moraux de l’évolution*. Two lecture-texts contributed to the collective volume *Questions de morale*; the host volume is not itself a work authored by Sorel. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1901:** *Essai sur l’Église et l’État* / *De l’Église et de l’État. Fragments*. A serialized essay issued in two near-contemporary book manifestations under different titles; “Fragments” describes its deliberately non-systematic form rather than a separate work. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1903:** *Introduction à l’économie moderne*. A major treatise repeatedly revised by Sorel; the 1922 edition added earlier and newly framed material, so no single printing completely exhausts the obra. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1903:** *Saggi di critica del marxismo*. An Italian-language collection of previously published essays, translated and introduced by Vittorio Racca; Sorel made the constituent texts, while translator and editor made this particular composite book-object. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1903–1905/1907:** *Insegnamenti sociali della economia contemporanea. Degenerazione capitalista e degenerazione socialista*. A substantial economic work written in French but surviving as a 1907 Italian translation after the French manuscript was lost; it therefore resists identification with an extant authorial-language text. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1905–1906:** *Lo sciopero generale e la violenza*. An Italian collection of articles on class struggle, general strike, morality, and violence; after extensive revision it became *Réflexions sur la violence*, so it is both an earlier work-state and a distinct composite manifestation. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1906/1908:** *Les illusions du progrès*. A serialized argument remade as a book and enlarged in 1911 and 1922 with new appendices; the work is better understood as a changing textual project than as one immutable edition. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1906–1908:** *Réflexions sur la violence*. Sorel’s best-known composite work, made from Italian articles, a French serial, a letter to Daniel Halévy, and later appendices; its successive editions altered the object substantially, including the later “Plaidoyer pour Lénine.” [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1908:** *La décomposition du marxisme*. A pamphlet-length intervention in Marxist theory, developed from an earlier version; its title may have been borrowed from Charles Andler, so title-making is not securely Sorel’s alone. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1909:** *La religion d’aujourd’hui*. A two-part French journal essay whose first book manifestation was an Italian translation with a new, revised and enlarged preface by Sorel. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1909:** *La révolution dreyfusienne*. A political pamphlet reconsidering the Dreyfus movement; Sorel revised it with a new preface for the 1911 edition. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1910:** *Le confessioni. Come divenni sindacalista*. A text conceived as a preface, published in Italian under a translator- and publisher-supplied title after the French manuscript was lost; Sorel reconstructed it back into French as “Mes raisons du syndicalisme,” making authorship secure but the authoritative wording unstable. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1914/1919:** *Matériaux d’une théorie du prolétariat*. A deliberately composite obra whose manuscript was delivered before the First World War but published in 1919, bringing together revised essays, prefaces, *L’avenir socialiste des syndicats*, and the reconstructed “Mes raisons du syndicalisme.” [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1919:** *Pour Lénine* / *Plaidoyer pour Lénine*. A political apology first appearing in Italian as “Chiarimenti su Lenin” and incorporated in revised form into the 1920 edition of *Réflexions sur la violence*; it acts as an intervention and approaches the force of a manifesto. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1921:** *Exégèses proudhoniennes: classes, justice supplétive, patrie*. A group of Proudhonian exegeses appended to the second edition of *Matériaux d’une théorie du prolétariat*, physically dependent on that volume but conceptually a distinct late work. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)

### Political interventions and journalism

- **1919:** *La Dalmazia*. A newspaper intervention translated and republished with Enrico Leone as *La Dalmazia è terra d’Italia. Socialisti francesi e italiani per l’italianità della Dalmazia*; the ten-page pamphlet is jointly framed, although Sorel’s contribution originated as a separately authored article. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1919–1921/1932:** *L’Europa sotto la tormenta*. A posthumously assembled book made from Sorel’s articles in *Il Resto del Carlino*; Sorel made the articles, while Mario Missiroli and the publisher made the 1932 collection and title-bearing book-object. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1919–1921/1949:** *Da Proudhon a Lenin*. A posthumous collection of Sorel’s articles on the Russian Revolution; it is an editorial creation from authentic constituent texts, not a book planned in this form by Sorel. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1886–1922:** *Periodical articles, reviews, notes, and polemical letters*. This is a dispersed body of work rather than one text: Sorel’s standard scholarly bibliography contains hundreds of entries across French, Italian, German, and other journals, beginning with “Sur les applications de la psychophysique.” Treating every offprint, translation, or later reprint as a separate obra would falsely turn manifestations into new works; Sand’s bibliography records 705 references, while scholarship stresses that the greater part of Sorel’s œuvre consists of journal articles. [Bibliographical history](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/) [Study of Sorel’s review practice](https://www.persee.fr/doc/mcm_0755-8287_1987_num_5_1_947)

### Prefaces and framing texts

- **1897:** *Préface aux Essais sur la conception matérialiste de l’histoire d’Antonio Labriola*. An independently authored framing essay attached to another writer’s book and later republished separately in collections. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1897–1898:** *Pour et contre le socialisme*. A journal essay repurposed as the preface to Francesco Saverio Merlino’s *Formes et essence du socialisme*; it is Sorel’s text but not Sorel’s book. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1900:** *Préface au Socialisme de Napoleone Colajanni*. A framing essay later absorbed into *Saggi di critica del marxismo* and *Matériaux d’une théorie du prolétariat*. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1901:** *Lettre-préface à la Bibliographie générale des éditions de la Librairie Jacques*. A paratext in epistolary form: made by Sorel, but subordinate to a publisher’s bibliography rather than an autonomous book. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1901–1902:** *Socialismes nationaux*. A preface to Gerolamo Gatti’s *Le socialisme et l’agriculture*, also issued in the *Cahiers de la quinzaine* and later incorporated into *Matériaux d’une théorie du prolétariat*. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1902:** *Préface à l’Histoire des Bourses du travail de Fernand Pelloutier*. A framing essay for Pelloutier’s posthumous book, later translated under the interpretive title “On Revolution without Politics.” [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1902:** *Préface à L’interprétation économique de l’histoire d’Edwin R. A. Seligman*. A philosophical-historiographical framing essay attached to a translated book by another author. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1905:** *L’humanité contre la douleur*. A preface to Georges Castex’s *La douleur physique*, later incorporated into *Introduction à l’économie moderne*; its movement between paratext and chapter makes it resist a single bibliographical identity. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1909:** *Préface aux Objectifs de nos luttes de classes de Victor Griffuelhes et Louis Niel*. A political framing text for another authors’ pamphlet. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1911:** *Lettre-préface à Le ferrovie ai ferrovieri de Vito Mercadante*. An epistolary endorsement and framing text, not a coauthored book. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1914:** *Préface aux Méfaits des intellectuels d’Édouard Berth*. A substantial prefatory intervention attached to a book dedicated to Sorel. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1915:** *Préface à Il papa in guerra de Mario Missiroli*. A prefatory political text written for Missiroli’s wartime book. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1915/1931:** *Germanesimo e storicismo di Ernesto Renan*. A preface written for an Italian edition of Renan’s *La réforme intellectuelle et morale* that never appeared; the project failed, but the text survived and was published posthumously in *La Critica*. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1915/1923:** *Préface à Karl Marx. L’économiste, le socialiste d’Arturo Labriola*. Written in 1915 but published posthumously in the 1923 French edition; it is Sorel’s work embedded in another author’s book. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)

### Review-making and unrealized projects

- **1895–1898:** *Le Devenir social*. A collaborative journal project founded with Paul Lafargue, Gabriel Deville, and Alfred Bonnet. Sorel contributed extensively, but the periodical as a whole is neither his sole-authored work nor merely a container: it is a collective intellectual manifestation. [Cambridge chronology](https://assets.cambridge.org/97805215/51175/frontmatter/9780521551175_frontmatter.pdf) [Journal record](https://books.google.com/books?id=-ZgWAAAAYAAJ)
- **1910:** *La Cité française*. An unrealized review project announced by a signed prospectus involving Sorel, Édouard Berth, Jean Variot, Pierre Gilbert, and Georges Valois. It resists being called a completed work: the intention and organizing declaration existed, but the journal itself never appeared. [Bibliographical note](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/) [Historical study](https://www.persee.fr/doc/mcm_0755-8287_1987_num_5_1_947)
- **1910:** *Projected collection of articles for La Cité française*. A planned volume associated with the failed journal, known through the surviving “Préface pour une œuvre nouvelle”; it remains a project rather than a finished book. [Mil neuf cent](https://www.persee.fr/doc/mcm_1146-1225_1994_num_12_1_1114?pageId=T1_152)
- **1911–1913:** *L’Indépendance*. A review created by Sorel with Jean Variot after the failure of *La Cité française*, producing forty-eight issues before ending. Its authorship is collective and contested in practice: Variot supplied financing and exercised influence, while Sorel organized content and supplied much of the journal’s intellectual identity. [Scholarly study](https://www.persee.fr/doc/mcm_0755-8287_1987_num_5_1_947)

### Letters, conversations, and other unstable works

- **1897:** *Correspondence with Antonio Labriola*. Labriola’s letters were published as *Socialisme et philosophie. Lettres à Georges Sorel*; the book is Labriola’s, not Sorel’s, although Sorel’s role as addressee and interlocutor helped generate the exchange. It should therefore not be misclassified as a Sorel-authored work. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1898–1910:** *Letters to Hubert Lagardelle*. A correspondence corpus rather than a single composed work, published only later and incompletely in different editorial settings. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1901–1921:** *Letters to Agostino Lanzillo*. A corpus of at least 137 surviving letters, eventually published as *“Cher camarade…” Georges Sorel ad Agostino Lanzillo*; the letters are Sorel’s, but the titled volume is a posthumous editorial construction. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1903–1921:** *Letters to Paul Delesalle*. A corpus of 65 published letters, despite the later volume’s misleading title *Lettres à Paul Delesalle, 1914–1921*: the correspondence represented actually begins in July 1903. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1907–1920:** *Letters to Daniel Halévy*. A correspondence corpus, parts of which entered editions of *Réflexions sur la violence* while other letters were published by *Mil neuf cent*; it has no single authorially fixed title or complete original manifestation. [Mil neuf cent](https://www.persee.fr/doc/mcm_1146-1225_1994_num_12_1_1114?pageId=T1_152)
- **1908–1913/1935:** *Propos de Georges Sorel*. Conversations recorded by Jean Variot and published first in newspapers and then as a book after Sorel’s death. They resist straightforward attribution as authored text: the speech is attributed to Sorel, but selection, wording, arrangement, and title depend upon Variot’s mediation. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **1908–1921:** *Letters to Mario Missiroli*. A corpus published posthumously as *Lettere a un amico d’Italia*, initially comprising 240 and later 243 letters; the correspondence is Sorel’s obra, while both title and selection are editorial. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **Dates dispersed:** *Letters to Benedetto Croce*. A correspondence corpus published posthumously as *Lettere a Benedetto Croce*; it is not one continuous treatise and its book-form unity belongs to the editor. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **Dates dispersed:** *Correspondence with Vilfredo Pareto*. Letters by both men survive and have been published in correspondence editions; this is a relational corpus, not a single-authored work, and published selections do not establish completeness. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)
- **Dates dispersed:** *Letters to Robert Michels*. A small correspondence corpus published as an appendix to a book chiefly concerning Michels; the letters are Sorel’s texts, but the host book is not his obra. [Bibliography](https://isspe.it/rassegna-siciliana/rassegna-siciliana-di-storia-e-cultura-n-17-dicembre-2002/georges-sorel-une-bibliographie-di-alain-de-benoist-n-17-dicembre-2002/)

### Civil engineering

- **1869–1892:** *Public-works and hydraulic engineering undertaken in state service*. Sorel worked in Corsica, Albi, Gap, Draguignan, Mostaganem, and Perpignan, ultimately as chief engineer of the hydraulic service. No consulted catalogue raisonné identifies a secure list of individually attributable structures, canals, roads, or reports, so assigning named constructions to him would confuse administrative responsibility with demonstrated authorship; this body of practice must remain an incompletely catalogued obra. [Cambridge chronology](https://assets.cambridge.org/97805215/51175/frontmatter/9780521551175_frontmatter.pdf)
