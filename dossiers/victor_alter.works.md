## Works by Victor Alter

### Political studies, programs, and pamphlets

- **1918:** *Marks un di visnshaft fun geshikhte* (*Marx and the Science of History*). A Yiddish essay first printed in the Bundist anthology *Zum ondenk fun Karl Marks* and subsequently issued as a brochure; it is therefore one obra with at least two publishing manifestations. ([Biographical Dictionary of Modern Yiddish Literature](https://yleksikon.blogspot.com/2014/06/viktor-victor-wiktor-alter-february-7.html))

- **1921:** *Grunt-printsipn fun der proletarisher kooperatsye* (*Basic Principles of Proletarian Cooperation*). A fifty-page Yiddish programmatic pamphlet on working-class cooperative organization. ([Biographical Dictionary of Modern Yiddish Literature](https://congressforjewishculture.org/people/6923/))

- **1923:** *Di profesionele bavegung in Poyln in 1922* (*The Trade-Union Movement in Poland in 1922*). A Yiddish report or political-economic survey of the preceding year’s Polish labor movement. ([Biographical Dictionary of Modern Yiddish Literature](https://yleksikon.blogspot.com/2014/06/viktor-victor-wiktor-alter-february-7.html))

- **1925:** *Der emes vegn Palestine* (*The Truth about Palestine*). A sixty-six-page Yiddish political pamphlet, framed by its title as an intervention in the contemporary Palestine debate rather than as a neutral description. ([Biographical Dictionary of Modern Yiddish Literature](https://congressforjewishculture.org/people/6923/))

- **1926–1927:** *Socjalizm walczący* / *Der sotsyalizm in kamf* (*Militant Socialism* or *Socialism in Struggle*). A socialist political book with Polish and Yiddish manifestations: the standard bibliography dates the Polish publication to 1926 and the 126-page Yiddish one to 1927, while a modern discussion treats both language forms as the same 1926 work. The linguistic manifestations should therefore not automatically be counted as two independent obras. ([Biographical Dictionary of Modern Yiddish Literature](https://yleksikon.blogspot.com/2014/06/viktor-victor-wiktor-alter-february-7.html); [Der Spekter](https://www.derspekter.org/the-bund-and-colonialism-the-perspective-of-wiktor-alter/))

- **1931:** *Jak zwalczyć bezrobocie?* (*How to Fight Unemployment?*). A Polish policy pamphlet whose interrogative title introduces a practical socialist intervention on unemployment. ([Biographical Dictionary of Modern Yiddish Literature](https://congressforjewishculture.org/people/6923/))

- **1932:** *Comment réaliser le socialisme?* (*How Can Socialism Be Realized?*). A French programmatic text concerned with implementing socialism—closer to a political protocol or proposal than to descriptive history. The standard bibliography supplies no evidence that it is merely a translation of one of Alter’s Polish or Yiddish titles, so it remains a separate entry. ([Biographical Dictionary of Modern Yiddish Literature](https://yleksikon.blogspot.com/2014/06/viktor-victor-wiktor-alter-february-7.html))

- **1934:** *Gdy socjaliści dojdą do władzy…!* (*When the Socialists Come to Power…!*). A Polish prospective political program: a text about what socialist government should do, and thus partly a protocol for an anticipated future. ([Biographical Dictionary of Modern Yiddish Literature](https://congressforjewishculture.org/people/6923/))

- **1934:** *Esquisse d’un programme économique socialiste* (*Outline of a Socialist Economic Program*). A French economic program or policy blueprint. Its bibliographic record does not establish whether it derives from another of Alter’s works, so it cannot safely be collapsed into one of them. ([Biographical Dictionary of Modern Yiddish Literature](https://yleksikon.blogspot.com/2014/06/viktor-victor-wiktor-alter-february-7.html))

- **1935:** *„Jedność” i „Plan”* (*“Unity” and “Plan”*). A Polish political pamphlet or essay; the quoted terms in the title name concepts under argument, not separate works. ([Biographical Dictionary of Modern Yiddish Literature](https://congressforjewishculture.org/people/6923/))

- **1937:** *Tsu der yidn-frage in Poyln* (*On the Jewish Question in Poland*). A 164-page Yiddish political study and one of Alter’s principal works. The standard Yiddish bibliography dates it to 1937; an *Encyclopaedia Judaica* entry gives 1927, but that conflicts both with the specialist bibliography and with another encyclopedia’s identification of the 1937 book, so the earlier date should be treated as an error rather than silently adopted. ([Biographical Dictionary of Modern Yiddish Literature](https://yleksikon.blogspot.com/2014/06/viktor-victor-wiktor-alter-february-7.html); [Encyclopaedia Judaica](https://www.encyclopedia.com/religion/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/alter-victor); [Visuotinė lietuvių enciklopedija](https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/wiktor-alter/))

- **1937:** *Antysemityzm gospodarczy w świetle cyfr* (*Economic Antisemitism in the Light of Statistics*). A Polish statistical-political study using quantitative evidence to intervene against economic antisemitism. ([Biographical Dictionary of Modern Yiddish Literature](https://congressforjewishculture.org/people/6923/); [Encyclopaedia Judaica](https://www.encyclopedia.com/religion/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/alter-victor))

- **1938:** *Człowiek w społeczeństwie* / *Der mentsh iz der gezelshaft* (*Man in Society*). Polish and Yiddish manifestations of a social-theoretical work, both listed for 1938. The standard bibliography lists them separately by language but does not explain their textual relationship, so describing them as two editions of one obra is probable rather than definitively established. ([Biographical Dictionary of Modern Yiddish Literature](https://yleksikon.blogspot.com/2014/06/viktor-victor-wiktor-alter-february-7.html))

### Collective and co-authored interventions

- **1936:** *O Żydach i antysemityzmie* (*On Jews and Antisemitism*). A sixty-one-page Polish collective anthology containing an essay by Alter alongside contributions attributed in the standard bibliography to Zygmunt Zaremba, M. Azher, Adam Próchnik, Z. Artur, and L. Honigvil. The anthology as a whole resists attribution to Alter as a sole “work”: his made thing is his contribution, while the volume is a collective manifestation; a contemporary account likewise calls it a collective work and specifically identifies an Alter essay within it. ([Biographical Dictionary of Modern Yiddish Literature](https://congressforjewishculture.org/people/6923/); [contemporary press account](https://elibrary.mab.lt/server/api/core/bitstreams/5d117a17-7a52-4282-baf9-0300f551341e/content))

- **1937:** *Hiszpania w ogniu* (*Spain in Flames*). A Polish co-authored intervention on the Spanish Civil War, made with Austrian socialist Julius Deutsch; responsibility for the whole text is therefore shared and should not be assigned to Alter alone. ([Biographical Dictionary of Modern Yiddish Literature](https://yleksikon.blogspot.com/2014/06/viktor-victor-wiktor-alter-february-7.html))

### Prison work and posthumous manifestation

- **1940–1941; published 1951:** *Di naye fizik* (*The New Physics*). A scientific-philosophical essay written while Alter was imprisoned in Moscow and first published posthumously in *Henrik Erlich un Viktor Alter*, pp. 434–458. The obra belongs to the prison years, while 1951 dates only its surviving public manifestation. ([Biographical Dictionary of Modern Yiddish Literature](https://congressforjewishculture.org/people/6923/))

### Journalism, translations, and editorial acts

- **Before 1912:** *Articles signed “Vik Fort” in the Belgian French-language socialist press*. A dispersed body of early journalism rather than a single fixed work; the specialist biographical bibliography names the pseudonym but not the individual articles, titles, or dates, so inventing item-level entries would misrepresent the evidence. ([Biographical Dictionary of Modern Yiddish Literature](https://yleksikon.blogspot.com/2014/06/viktor-victor-wiktor-alter-february-7.html))

- **1914–1917:** *Articles in Arbeter-vort*. A body of Yiddish socialist and antimilitarist journalism written in Britain. It resists treatment as one work, while the consulted standard bibliography does not enumerate its constituent texts. ([Biographical Dictionary of Modern Yiddish Literature](https://yleksikon.blogspot.com/2014/06/viktor-victor-wiktor-alter-february-7.html))

- **1917–1918:** *Articles in Der Veker*. Contributions to the Bund’s Yiddish daily central organ in Minsk; this is a journalistic corpus, not a securely bounded obra, and its individual titles are not supplied by the standard bibliography. ([Biographical Dictionary of Modern Yiddish Literature](https://yleksikon.blogspot.com/2014/06/viktor-victor-wiktor-alter-february-7.html))

- **1918:** *Translations from English and French into Russian*. Alter produced translations while living in Moscow, but the specialist source gives neither source authors nor titles. These are made texts whose identity and completeness cannot presently be recovered from the consulted bibliography, so they resist entry as named works. ([Biographical Dictionary of Modern Yiddish Literature](https://yleksikon.blogspot.com/2014/06/viktor-victor-wiktor-alter-february-7.html))

- **1919–1939:** *Contributions to Folks-tsaytung and other Bundist periodicals*. A sustained multilingual body of political journalism in Yiddish and Polish. The standard biographical bibliography describes Alter as a permanent contributor but does not provide an article bibliography; consequently this corpus cannot honestly be decomposed here into titled works or claimed as complete. ([Biographical Dictionary of Modern Yiddish Literature](https://yleksikon.blogspot.com/2014/06/viktor-victor-wiktor-alter-february-7.html))

- **14 September 1939:** *Lubliner shtime* (*Voice of Lublin*). A one-time newspaper issued by Alter in Lublin during the German invasion. It is best understood as an emergency editorial and political manifestation rather than a conventionally authored book; the source does not identify the individual contents or distinguish what Alter wrote from what he organized and issued. ([Biographical Dictionary of Modern Yiddish Literature](https://yleksikon.blogspot.com/2014/06/viktor-victor-wiktor-alter-february-7.html))
