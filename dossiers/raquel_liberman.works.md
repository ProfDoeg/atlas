## Works by Raquel Liberman

### Correspondence

- **1921–1922:** *Letters from Warsaw to Yaacov Ferber in Argentina*. A surviving, untitled sequence of private letters, principally in Yiddish, preserved with Ferber’s replies and published in facsimile and English translation by Nora Glickman; because the scholarly edition presents an exchange rather than a catalogue of separately titled works, the sequence is treated here as one epistolary corpus. Its exact completeness is unknown, and its editorial numbering and English wording are not Liberman’s originals. ([Glickman](https://deanmurray.info/dc/slavery/t84j23w90s56t17.pdf), pp. 91–156; [Martínez](https://escholarship.org/content/qt0nj0d2pm/qt0nj0d2pm_noSplash_c11eb86012d98a9ec5fb3314acbcb89e.pdf), pp. 108–109)

### Denunciations and testimony

- **2 June 1928:** *Denunciation of José Salomón Korn*. An untitled police complaint and narrated statement accusing Liberman’s second husband of deception, theft, and coercion; it is not securely a text written in her own hand but a bureaucratically recorded speech-act—a manifesto in the limited sense that it sought official action. Later retellings frequently replace this date and object with the broader complaint of 31 December 1929. ([Yarfitz](https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctvscxsh2.5); [Butierrez and Yarfitz](https://latfem.org/una-mirada-critica-sobre-la-estacion-callao-raquel-liberman/))

- **31 December 1929:** *Formal denunciation associated with the Zwi Migdal proceedings*. A renewed or formalized complaint to police commissioner Julio Alsogaray that became the initiating document of the celebrated criminal case. Sources disagree over whether this was Liberman’s first denunciation or the continuation of her 1928 complaint; its surviving form was produced through police procedure, so Liberman is the declarant rather than necessarily the scribe. ([Glickman](https://deanmurray.info/dc/slavery/t84j23w90s56t17.pdf), pp. 45–46; [Buenos Aires Ídish](https://www.buenosaires.gob.ar/areas/cultura/cpphc/archivos/libros/temas_19.pdf); [Yarfitz](https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctvscxsh2.5))

- **1930:** *Court testimony in the proceedings against members of the former Varsovia Society/Zwi Migdal*. An oral juridical manifestation made before Judge Manuel Rodríguez Ocampo and preserved only through the court record and its publication in *La Gaceta del Foro*. It resists classification as an authored “work”: the testimony was hers, but its documentary wording, selection, and arrangement belong partly to judicial intermediaries. ([Yarfitz](https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctvscxsh2.2); [Butierrez and Yarfitz](https://latfem.org/una-mirada-critica-sobre-la-estacion-callao-raquel-liberman/))

### Administrative project

- **1934:** *Application for a visa to visit Poland*. An untitled administrative application embodying an unrealized project of return; the voyage never occurred. Glickman records the application but does not establish that its surviving wording was composed or written personally by Liberman, and the better-documented related letter to the Polish consulate was made by the Jewish Scientific Institute, not by her. ([Glickman](https://deanmurray.info/dc/slavery/t84j23w90s56t17.pdf), p. 46)
