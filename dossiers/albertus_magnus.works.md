## Works by Albertus Magnus

### Logical commentaries and instruments

- *Super Porphyrium De V universalibus*. A paraphrastic commentary on Porphyry’s *Isagoge* and its five predicables; the critical edition treats it as authentic. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *De praedicamentis*. A paraphrastic commentary on Aristotle’s *Categories*, organized as part of Albert’s larger project of making the Aristotelian sciences available in Latin. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/albert-great/)
- *De sex principiis*. A paraphrastic commentary on the medieval *Liber sex principiorum*, the supplement to Aristotle’s treatment of the categories traditionally associated with Gilbert of Poitiers. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/albert-great/)
- *Liber divisionum*. A logical protocol concerning division as a method for reaching knowledge of complex objects; it survives as a distinct treatise rather than as commentary on a single Aristotelian book. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Alberti Magni e-corpus](https://www.arts.uwaterloo.ca/~albertus/Downloading.html)
- *Peri hermeneias*. A paraphrastic commentary on Aristotle’s *De interpretatione*, treating propositions and their logical relations. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *Analytica priora*. A paraphrastic commentary on Aristotle’s *Prior Analytics* and thus a sustained account of syllogistic reasoning. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *Analytica posteriora*. A paraphrastic commentary on Aristotle’s *Posterior Analytics*, concerned with demonstration and scientific knowledge. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/albert-great/)
- *Topica*. A paraphrastic commentary on Aristotle’s *Topics* and an instrument for dialectical argument. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *De sophisticis elenchis*. A paraphrastic commentary on Aristotle’s *Sophistical Refutations*, concerned with fallacious argument. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)

### Natural-philosophical project

- *Physica*. An eight-book paraphrastic commentary on Aristotle’s *Physics*, transmitted and critically edited in two parts; it is one large component of Albert’s project to reconstruct the whole Aristotelian scientific curriculum. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/albert-great/)
- *De caelo et mundo*. A paraphrastic commentary on Aristotle’s *On the Heavens*, concerning the cosmos, celestial bodies, and terrestrial elements. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *De natura loci*. An independent natural-philosophical treatise on place, geography, and the physical conditions that differentiate regions. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *De causis proprietatum elementorum*. A treatise explaining the distinctive powers and properties of the elements, including the effects of celestial and geographical conditions. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *De generatione et corruptione*. A paraphrastic commentary on Aristotle’s *On Generation and Corruption*, treating substantial change and elemental mixture. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *Meteora*. A commentary and natural-scientific synthesis based on Aristotle’s *Meteorology*, concerned with atmospheric, hydrological, and terrestrial phenomena. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *Mineralia*. A work on stones, metals, mineral formation, and their properties; it is more than a commentary because Albert incorporates other sources and reported or personal observations. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/albert-great/)
- *De anima*. A paraphrastic commentary on Aristotle’s *On the Soul*, but also a major synthesis of Greek and Arabic psychology. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/albert-great/)
- *De nutrimento et nutrito*. A short natural-philosophical treatise on nourishment and what is nourished, incorporated into Albert’s sequence of works on living beings. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *De sensu et sensato*. A treatise on sense and sensible objects; what has often circulated separately as *De memoria et reminiscentia* is, in the critical edition, its second book rather than an independent obra. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *De intellectu et intelligibili*. An original short treatise on intellect and intelligible objects, not simply a commentary on an extant Aristotelian book. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/albert-great/)
- *De somno et vigilia*. A natural-philosophical treatise on sleep and waking in the sequence conventionally called the *Parva naturalia*. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *De spiritu et respiratione*. A treatise on breath, respiration, and the physiological “spirit,” expanding the Aristotelian natural-science corpus. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *De motu animalium*. A treatise on animal movement; older editions also print its title as *De motibus animalium*. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Alberti Magni e-corpus](https://www.arts.uwaterloo.ca/~albertus/Downloading.html)
- *De iuventute et senectute*. A short treatise on youth, aging, and their biological causes. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *De morte et vita*. A short natural-philosophical treatise on life and death. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *De vegetabilibus*. A seven-book botanical synthesis derived partly from the Latin work known as Aristotle’s *De plantis* but enlarged through other authorities and Albert’s own investigations; it therefore resists classification as mere commentary. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Alberti Magni e-corpus](https://www.arts.uwaterloo.ca/~albertus/Downloading.html); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/albert-great/)
- *De animalibus*. A twenty-six-book zoological Gesamtkunstwerk of commentary, compilation, anatomical description, received lore, and reported or personal observation; its three projected Cologne volumes are manifestations of one very large work. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/albert-great/)
- *De natura et origine animae*. An original short treatise on the nature and origin of the soul. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/albert-great/)
- *De principiis motus processivi*. A treatise on the principles of animals’ progressive or locomotor movement. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Alberti Magni e-corpus](https://www.arts.uwaterloo.ca/~albertus/Downloading.html)
- *Quaestiones super De animalibus*. A set of scholastic questions on Aristotle’s zoological books, arising from teaching and disputation rather than from Albert’s paraphrastic-commentary project. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/albert-great/)

### Moral and political philosophy

- *Ethica*. Albert’s later paraphrastic commentary on Aristotle’s *Nicomachean Ethics*, distinct from the earlier question-commentary *Super Ethica*. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/albert-great/)
- *Super Ethica*. A question-commentary generated in a scholastic teaching setting and transmitted in ten books; it is a different obra from the later *Ethica*, not an alternative title for it. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/albert-great/)
- *Politica*. A literal commentary on Aristotle’s *Politics* and the first complete commentary on that work produced in the Latin West. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/albert-great/)

### Metaphysics, causation, and public determinations

- *Metaphysica*. A thirteen-book paraphrastic commentary and philosophical synthesis based on Aristotle’s *Metaphysics*. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/albert-great/)
- *De unitate intellectus*. A polemical opusculum opposing the theory that all human beings share a single intellect; it acts against a position as well as describing it and thus approaches a manifesto. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/albert-great/)
- **1270s:** *De XV problematibus*. A formal response to fifteen propositions submitted to Albert for judgment, making it a determinate academic intervention rather than a free-standing systematic book. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *Problemata determinata*. A collection or record of formally determined problems; its identity is tied to acts of scholastic determination and consequently resists the model of a conventionally authored book. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *De fato*. A short disputed-question treatise on fate, providence, celestial causation, and human agency. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Alberti Magni e-corpus](https://www.arts.uwaterloo.ca/~albertus/Downloading.html)
- *De causis et processu universitatis a prima causa*. A metaphysical synthesis whose first book draws on al-Ghazālī and whose second functions as a paraphrastic commentary on the *Liber de causis*. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/albert-great/)

### Biblical exegesis

- *Super Iob*. A commentary on the Book of Job, preserved outside the published Cologne edition in Melchior Weiß’s critical edition. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Alberti Magni e-corpus](https://www.arts.uwaterloo.ca/~albertus/Downloading.html)
- *De muliere forti*. An extended exposition of the “valiant woman” passage at the end of Proverbs; although exegetical, its bounded biblical subject allowed it to circulate as a distinct treatise. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *Postilla super Isaiam*. A postilla or continuous exegetical commentary on Isaiah. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *Postillae super Ieremiam fragmentum*. The surviving fragment of Albert’s commentary on Jeremiah; it is materially incomplete, so the extant manifestation cannot be equated confidently with the original whole. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Alberti Magni e-corpus](https://www.arts.uwaterloo.ca/~albertus/Downloading.html)
- *Postillae super Ezechielem fragmentum*. The surviving fragment of a commentary on Ezekiel; as with the Jeremiah fragment, what remains is evidence of an exegetical project rather than a complete work. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *Super Threnos*. A commentary on Lamentations. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *Super Baruch*. A commentary on Baruch. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *Super Danielem*. A commentary on Daniel. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *Super prophetas minores*. A collected exegetical work on the twelve Minor Prophets; the collective title names a sequence of commentaries rather than a single biblical object. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *Super Matthaeum*. A commentary on Matthew; its two critical-edition parts, divided after chapter 14, are editorial manifestations of one work. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *Super Marcum*. A commentary on Mark. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *Super Lucam*. A commentary on Luke. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *Super Iohannem*. A commentary on John. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)

### Early theological works and scholastic question collections

- **First half of the 1230s:** *De natura boni*. Albert’s earliest securely placed work, a theological treatise on the nature of the good. [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/albert-great/); [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *Quaestiones*. A collection of theological questions placed beside *De natura boni* in the critical edition; the plural title reflects a dossier produced through scholastic inquiry rather than a unitary narrative book. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *De sacramentis*. A systematic theological treatise on the sacraments. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *De incarnatione*. A theological treatise on the Incarnation. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *De resurrectione*. A theological treatise on resurrection. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *De IV coaequaevis*. A study of the “four coevals” of creation—prime matter, time, the highest heaven, and angelic nature—developed from exegesis of Genesis. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/albert-great/)
- *De homine*. A large theological-anthropological synthesis on the human being, incorporating physiology, psychology, ethics, and doctrine; its mixed construction makes it more than a narrowly theological tract. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/albert-great/)
- *De bono*. A systematic treatment of the good, virtue, conscience, and related moral-theological questions. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)

### Commentaries on Peter Lombard and theological summa

- *Super I librum Sententiarum*. A commentary on Book I of Peter Lombard’s *Sentences*; distinctions 1–3 and 4–48 occupy separate projected critical-edition parts but constitute one commentary. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *Super II librum Sententiarum*. A commentary on Book II of the *Sentences*. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *Super III librum Sententiarum*. A commentary on Book III of the *Sentences*. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *Super IV librum Sententiarum*. A commentary on Book IV of the *Sentences*; its two projected volumes are editorial divisions of a single commentary. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- **Late life; unfinished at Albert’s death in 1280:** *Summa de mirabili scientia Dei*, also called *Summa theologiae*. An unfinished theological summa: Book I is divided editorially at question 50, while Book II may contain work by anonymous collaborators or continuators and cannot safely be treated as wholly Albert’s sole production. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/albert-great/)

[AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/albert-great/)
- *Super Dionysium De ecclesiastica hierarchia*. A question-commentary on Pseudo-Dionysius’s *Ecclesiastical Hierarchy*. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/albert-great/)
- *Super Dionysium De divinis nominibus*. A question-commentary on Pseudo-Dionysius’s *Divine Names*. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/albert-great/)
- *Super Dionysii Mysticam theologiam*. A question-commentary on Pseudo-Dionysius’s *Mystical Theology*. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/albert-great/)
- *Super Dionysii epistulas*. A commentary on the letters attributed to Dionysius; it is catalogued separately from the four commentaries on the principal Dionysian treatises. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)

### Liturgical and Eucharistic works

- *De mysterio missae*. A liturgical-theological exposition of the Mass; parts of it function as interpretation of ritual action rather than merely as abstract doctrine. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Alberti Magni e-corpus](https://www.arts.uwaterloo.ca/~albertus/Downloading.html)
- *De corpore Domini*. A theological treatise on the Eucharist and the body of Christ. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Alberti Magni e-corpus](https://www.arts.uwaterloo.ca/~albertus/Downloading.html)

### Mathematics

- *Super Euclidem*. The surviving commentary on Books I–IV of Euclid’s *Elements*. Because only this portion survives, it is uncertain whether Albert completed more of the project; the extant object is explicitly incomplete. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/albert-great/)

### Sermons, determinations, letters, and documentary acts

- *Sermones*. A corpus of sermons rather than one authored “book”; its individual items, variant reports, authenticity, and boundaries remain editorial problems assigned collectively to volume XL of the Cologne plan. It therefore resists being called a single work at all. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *Determinatio magistri Alberti de novo spiritu*. A magisterial determination concerning the “new spirit,” preserved as the record of an authoritative academic or ecclesiastical intervention; the act of deciding is integral to the text’s identity. [Alberti Magni e-corpus](https://www.arts.uwaterloo.ca/~albertus/Downloading.html)
- *Epistula de ungelt*. A letter concerning the levy or tax called *Ungelt*; it is a practical documentary act, not a literary book. [Alberti Magni e-corpus](https://www.arts.uwaterloo.ca/~albertus/Downloading.html)
- *Testamentum Domini Alberti*. Albert’s testament: a juridical-documentary act whose operative force matters more than literary authorship. [Alberti Magni e-corpus](https://www.arts.uwaterloo.ca/~albertus/Downloading.html)
- *Epistulae*. The residual corpus of Albert’s letters is listed collectively in the Cologne plan. It is not securely one obra, and the plan leaves the identification and authentication of its individual documentary manifestations to the projected edition. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)

### Disputed works and unstable attributions

- **Between 1241 and 1277; attribution disputed:** *Speculum astronomiae*, also called *Contra libros nigromanticorum*. A bibliographical and disciplinary protocol for distinguishing licit astronomical or astrological books from illicit magical ones. It was widely attributed to Albert, but modern scholars have proposed Albert, Roger Bacon, Richard de Fournival, Philip the Chancellor, or an unidentified author; the Cologne conspectus does not include it among the securely authentic works. [IRHT-CNRS FAMA notice](https://fama.irht.cnrs.fr/oeuvre/271027); [scholarly survey of the dispute](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-0787/10/4/117)
- *Quaestio de quiditate et esse*. A question on quiddity and existence published separately under Albert’s name, but absent from the authentic-work sequence of the current Cologne conspectus; it should therefore be retained as a disputed attribution, not silently promoted to the secure corpus. [Alberti Magni e-corpus](https://www.arts.uwaterloo.ca/~albertus/Downloading.html); [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *Opera dubia et spuria*. Not a work Albert made, but the critical edition’s projected holding category for texts historically attributed to him whose authorship is doubtful or false. Its inclusion in the atlas records the unstable boundary of “Albert’s works”; it must not be confused with an Albertine corpus of authentic creations. [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *Compendium theologicae veritatis*. Not Albert’s obra: the work was long transmitted and printed under his name but is now attributed to Hugh Ripelin of Strasbourg. [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/albert-great/)
- *Philosophia pauperum*. Not included among Albert’s authentic works in the Cologne plan, although printed under his name in the Jammy corpus; its Albertine attribution is rejected or doubtful. [Alberti Magni e-corpus](https://www.arts.uwaterloo.ca/~albertus/Downloading.html); [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *De apprehensione*. A work printed under Albert’s name in the early collected editions but excluded from the authentic Cologne corpus; it should not be counted straightforwardly as something he made. [Alberti Magni e-corpus](https://www.arts.uwaterloo.ca/~albertus/Downloading.html); [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *Paradisus animae*. A devotional text formerly printed under Albert’s name but not admitted to the authentic Cologne corpus. [Alberti Magni e-corpus](https://www.arts.uwaterloo.ca/~albertus/Downloading.html); [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *De adhaerendo Deo*. A devotional treatise traditionally attributed to Albert but excluded from the authentic Cologne corpus; it is commonly associated instead with Johannes of Kastl. [Alberti Magni e-corpus](https://www.arts.uwaterloo.ca/~albertus/Downloading.html); [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *De alchimia*. An alchemical text printed under Albert’s name in the Jammy edition but rejected from the authentic corpus. Its pseudonymous circulation helped create the later image of “Albert the alchemist,” which is a reception-history creation rather than secure evidence of an obra he made. [Alberti Magni e-corpus](https://www.arts.uwaterloo.ca/~albertus/Downloading.html); [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *Mariale*. A Marian work printed under Albert’s name in early collected editions but excluded from the authentic Cologne conspectus. [Alberti Magni e-corpus](https://www.arts.uwaterloo.ca/~albertus/Downloading.html); [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *De laudibus Beatae Mariae Virginis*. A Marian compilation formerly attributed to Albert but not accepted into the authentic critical corpus. [Alberti Magni e-corpus](https://www.arts.uwaterloo.ca/~albertus/Downloading.html); [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
- *Biblia Mariana*. A Marian biblical compilation transmitted under Albert’s name but excluded from the authentic Cologne corpus. [Alberti Magni e-corpus](https://www.arts.uwaterloo.ca/~albertus/Downloading.html); [AMI conspectus](https://institutionen.erzbistum-koeln.de/albertus-magnus-institut/editio_coloniensis/werkausgabe/)
