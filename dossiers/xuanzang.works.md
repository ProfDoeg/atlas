## Works by Xuanzang

### Sanskrit treatises composed in India

- **c. 635–640:** *Huizong lun* (會宗論, *Treatise on the Harmony of the Schools*). A reported three-thousand-verse synthesis of Yogācāra and Madhyamaka; known only through traditional biographies and now lost.
- **c. 641–642:** *Zhi e-jian lun* (制惡見論, *Treatise for Refuting Evil Views*; also *Po e-jian lun*). A reported sixteen-hundred-verse Sanskrit polemic defending Mahāyāna, subsequently used as Xuanzang’s public debate thesis; the work is lost, although Kuiji preserves references to some arguments.
- **c. 642:** *Sanshen lun* (三身論, *Treatise on the Three Bodies*). A reported three-hundred-verse exposition composed for Kumāra, king of Kāmarūpa; it is known from the biographical tradition but no text survives.

### Chinese translations and editorial syntheses

These are the seventy-five works traditionally counted as Xuanzang’s 1,335-fascicle translation corpus. “Xuanzang’s translation” names an imperial, collaborative translation workshop—Xuanzang interpreted and directed while assistants recorded, checked, polished, and copied—rather than solitary authorship in the modern sense.

- **645:** *Da pusa zang jing* (大菩薩藏經, *Bodhisattvapiṭaka*). A twenty-fascicle Mahāyāna scripture preserved as the twelfth assembly of the composite *Mahāratnakūṭa* (T310), so it resists treatment as an independent book in the received canon.
- **645:** *Xianyang shengjiao lun song* (顯揚聖教論頌, *Verses of the Prakaraṇāryavāca-śāstra*). Xuanzang’s one-fascicle translation of verses attributed to Asaṅga (T1603).
- **645:** *Liu men tuoluoni jing* (六門陀羅尼經, *Sūtra of the Six-Gate Dhāraṇī*). A one-fascicle dhāraṇī scripture (T1360).
- **645:** *Fo di jing* (佛地經, *Buddhabhūmi-sūtra*). A one-fascicle scripture on the Buddha-stage (T680).
- **645–646:** *Xianyang shengjiao lun* (顯揚聖教論, *Prakaraṇāryavāca-śāstra*). A twenty-fascicle Yogācāra treatise attributed to Asaṅga (T1602).
- **646:** *Dasheng apidamo za ji lun* (大乘阿毘達磨雜集論, *Mahāyānābhidharmasamuccaya-vyākhyā*). A sixteen-fascicle scholastic compilation associated with Asaṅga and Sthiramati (T1606).
- **646–648:** *Yuqie shi di lun* (瑜伽師地論, *Yogācārabhūmi-śāstra*). A hundred-fascicle Yogācāra summa traditionally attributed to Maitreya (T1579), and one of Xuanzang’s principal reasons for travelling to India.
- **647:** *Dasheng wu yun lun* (大乘五蘊論, *Mahāyāna-pañcaskandhaka*). A one-fascicle translation of Vasubandhu’s analysis of the five aggregates (T1612).
- **647–649:** *She dasheng lun shi* (攝大乘論釋, *Mahāyānasaṃgraha-upanibandhana*). A ten-fascicle commentary attributed to Asvabhāva (T1598), distinct from the similarly titled Vasubandhu commentary.
- **647:** *Yinming ru zhengli lun* (因明入正理論, *Nyāyapraveśa*). A one-fascicle handbook of Buddhist logic attributed to Śaṅkarasvāmin (T1630).
- **647:** *Jie shenmi jing* (解深密經, *Saṃdhinirmocana-sūtra*). A five-fascicle Yogācāra scripture (T676).
- **648:** *Tian qingwen jing* (天請問經, *Devaparipṛcchā-sūtra*). A one-fascicle scripture framed as a deity’s questions (T592).
- **648:** *Shengzong shi ju yi lun* (勝宗十句義論, *Treatise on the Ten Categories of Vaiśeṣika*). A one-fascicle translation of a non-Buddhist Indian philosophical text attributed to Candramati (T2138).
- **648:** *Weishi sanshi lun song* (唯識三十論頌, *Triṃśikā-vijñaptimātratā*). Xuanzang’s one-fascicle translation of Vasubandhu’s thirty verses (T1586).
- **648; revised or retranslated 660–663:** *Nengduan jingang bore boluomiduo jing* (能斷金剛般若波羅蜜多經, *Vajracchedikā-prajñāpāramitā*). The early independent translation is counted in the traditional seventy-five but its textual identity and survival are problematic; a version survives in the Korean canon, while another manifestation forms assembly nine of T220.
- **648:** *Dasheng baifa mingmen lun* (大乘百法明門論, *Mahāyāna-śatadharma-prakāśamukha-śāstra*). A one-fascicle text attributed to Vasubandhu (T1614).
- **648:** *She dasheng lun shi* (攝大乘論釋, *Mahāyānasaṃgraha-bhāṣya*). A ten-fascicle commentary attributed to Vasubandhu (T1597), not the Asvabhāva commentary bearing the same Chinese title.
- **649:** *She dasheng lun ben* (攝大乘論本, *Mahāyānasaṃgraha*). A three-fascicle translation of Asaṅga’s Yogācāra treatise (T1594).
- **649:** *Yuanqi shengdao jing* (緣起聖道經, scripture on dependent origination and the noble path). A one-fascicle sūtra (T714).
- **649:** *Apidamo shishen zu lun* (阿毘達磨識身足論, *Abhidharma-vijñānakāya-pāda*). A sixteen-fascicle Sarvāstivāda Abhidharma treatise attributed to Devaśarman (T1539).
- **649:** *Rulai shijiao Shengjun wang jing* (如來示教勝軍王經, scripture of the Tathāgata’s instruction to King Prasenajit). A one-fascicle royal-instruction sūtra (T515).
- **649:** *Shen xiyou jing* (甚希有經, *Adbhuta-sūtra*). A one-fascicle scripture (T689).
- **traditionally 649:** *Bore boluomiduo xin jing* (般若波羅蜜多心經, *Heart Sūtra*). A one-fascicle text canonically credited to Xuanzang (T251); Jan Nattier’s influential study instead argues that the short text was composed in Chinese from earlier translations and only subsequently associated with him, so both translation and attribution remain disputed.
- **649:** *Pusa jie jiemo wen* (菩薩戒羯磨文, *Bodhisattva Precepts Karman*). A one-fascicle ritual protocol extracted from the Yogācāra tradition (T1499).
- **649:** *Wangfa zhengli lun* (王法正理論, treatise on the correct principles of kingship). A one-fascicle political-ethical treatise traditionally attributed to Maitreya (T1615).
- **649:** *Zui wubi jing* (最無比經, *Anupama-sūtra*). A one-fascicle scripture (T691).
- **649:** *Pusa jie ben* (菩薩戒本, *Bodhisattva Precepts*). A one-fascicle disciplinary protocol attributed to Maitreya (T1501).
- **649:** *Dasheng zhangzhen lun* (大乘掌珍論, *Mahāyāna-hastaratna-śāstra*). A two-fascicle Madhyamaka treatise by Bhāviveka (T1578).
- **649:** *Fo di jing lun* (佛地經論, *Buddhabhūmi-sūtra-śāstra*). A seven-fascicle commentary attributed to Bandhuprabha and others (T1530).
- **649:** *Yinming zhengli men lun ben* (因明正理門論本, *Nyāyamukha*). A one-fascicle work on logic attributed to Dignāga (T1628).
- **650:** *Chengzan jingtu Fo sheshou jing* (稱讚淨土佛攝受經, scripture praising the Buddha’s Pure Land). A one-fascicle Pure Land sūtra (T367).
- **650:** *Yuqie shi di lun shi* (瑜伽師地論釋, commentary on the *Yogācārabhūmi*). A one-fascicle commentary attributed to Jinaputra and others (T1580).
- **650:** *Fenbie yuanqi chu sheng famen jing* (分別緣起初勝法門經, scripture distinguishing the principal teaching of dependent origination). A two-fascicle sūtra (T717).
- **650:** *Shuo Wugoucheng jing* (說無垢稱經, *Vimalakīrtinirdeśa-sūtra*). A six-fascicle translation of the Vimalakīrti scripture (T476).
- **650:** *Yaoshi liuliguang Rulai benyuan gongde jing* (藥師琉璃光如來本願功德經, *Bhaiṣajyaguru-vaiḍūryaprabharāja-sūtra*). A one-fascicle Medicine Buddha scripture (T450).
- **650:** *Guang bai lun ben* (廣百論本, *Catuḥśataka* verses). A one-fascicle translation of Āryadeva’s verses (T1570).
- **650:** *Dasheng guang bai lun shi lun* (大乘廣百論釋論, *Catuḥśataka-ṭīkā*). A ten-fascicle commentary by Dharmapāla on Āryadeva’s verses (T1571).
- **650:** *Ben shi jing* (本事經, *Itivṛttaka*). A seven-fascicle collection of narratives and teachings (T765).
- **650:** *Zhu Fo xin tuoluoni jing* (諸佛心陀羅尼經, dhāraṇī scripture of the Buddhas’ heart). A one-fascicle ritual text (T918).
- **651:** *Shouchi qi Fo minghao suo sheng gongde jing* (受持七佛名號所生功德經, scripture on the merit of retaining the seven Buddhas’ names). A one-fascicle devotional sūtra (T436).
- **651:** *Dasheng daji Dizang shilun jing* (大乘大集地藏十輪經, *Daśacakra-kṣitigarbha-sūtra*). A ten-fascicle Kṣitigarbha scripture (T411).
- **651:** *Apidamo zang xianzong lun* (阿毘達磨藏顯宗論, *Abhidharma-samayapradīpikā*). A forty-fascicle Sarvāstivāda treatise by Saṃghabhadra (T1563).
- **651–654:** *Apidamo jushe lun* (阿毘達磨俱舍論, *Abhidharmakośabhāṣya*). A thirty-fascicle translation of Vasubandhu’s Abhidharma treatise and auto-commentary (T1558).
- **651:** *Apidamo jushe lun ben song* (阿毘達磨俱舍論本頌, *Abhidharmakośa-kārikā*). A one-fascicle independent translation of Vasubandhu’s root verses (T1560).
- **651:** *Dasheng chengye lun* (大乘成業論, *Karmasiddhiprakaraṇa*). A one-fascicle treatise on karma attributed to Vasubandhu (T1609).
- **652:** *Dasheng apidamo ji lun* (大乘阿毘達磨集論, *Abhidharmasamuccaya*). A seven-fascicle work attributed to Asaṅga (T1605).
- **652:** *Fo lin niepan ji fazhu jing* (佛臨涅槃記法住經, scripture recording the Dharma’s duration at the Buddha’s nirvāṇa). A one-fascicle prophetic sūtra (T390).
- **653–654:** *Apidamo shun zhengli lun* (阿毘達磨順正理論, *Nyāyānusāra*). An eighty-fascicle Sarvāstivāda critique by Saṃghabhadra (T1562).
- **654:** *Da aluohan Nandimituoluo suo shuo fazhu ji* (大阿羅漢難提蜜多羅所說法住記, record spoken by the arhat Nandimitra). A one-fascicle account of the sixteen arhats and the endurance of the Dharma (T2030).
- **654:** *Chengzan dasheng gongde jing* (稱讚大乘功德經, scripture praising the merits of Mahāyāna). A one-fascicle sūtra (T840).
- **654:** *Baji kunan tuoluoni jing* (拔濟苦難陀羅尼經, dhāraṇī for deliverance from suffering). A one-fascicle ritual-protective text (T1395).
- **654:** *Ba ming pumi tuoluoni jing* (八名普密陀羅尼經, dhāraṇī of eight universally secret names). A one-fascicle ritual text (T1365).
- **654:** *Xian wubian Fotu gongde jing* (顯無邊佛土功德經, scripture revealing the merits of the boundless Buddha-land). A one-fascicle sūtra (T289).
- **654:** *Shengchuang bi-yin tuoluoni jing* (勝幢臂印陀羅尼經, dhāraṇī of the victorious-banner arm-seal). A one-fascicle ritual text (T1363).
- **654:** *Chishi tuoluoni jing* (持世陀羅尼經, *Vasudhārā-dhāraṇī*). A one-fascicle prosperity and protection ritual (T1162).
- **656:** *Shiyimian shenzhou xin jing* (十一面神呪心經, heart scripture of the eleven-faced spell). A one-fascicle Avalokiteśvara dhāraṇī text (T1071).
- **656–659:** *Apidamo da piposha lun* (阿毘達磨大毘婆沙論, *Abhidharma-mahāvibhāṣā-śāstra*). A two-hundred-fascicle Sarvāstivāda encyclopedic commentary attributed to five hundred arhats (T1545).
- **657:** *Apidamo fazhi lun* (阿毘達磨發智論, *Jñānaprasthāna*). A twenty-fascicle Sarvāstivāda treatise attributed to Kātyāyanīputra (T1544).
- **657:** *Guan suoyuanyuan lun* (觀所緣緣論, *Ālambanaparīkṣā*). A one-fascicle epistemological treatise by Dignāga (T1624).
- **658:** *Ru apidamo lun* (入阿毘達磨論, *Abhidharmāvatāra*). A two-fascicle introduction to Abhidharma attributed to Skandhila (T1554).
- **659:** *Bukong juansuo shenzhou xin jing* (不空羂索神呪心經, heart scripture of the Amoghapāśa spell). A one-fascicle ritual scripture (T1094).
- **659:** *Apidamo fayun zu lun* (阿毘達磨法蘊足論, *Abhidharma-dharmaskandha-pāda*). A twelve-fascicle Abhidharma treatise traditionally attributed to Mahāmaudgalyāyana (T1537).
- **659:** *Cheng weishi lun* (成唯識論, *Demonstration of Consciousness-Only*). A ten-fascicle editorial synthesis of ten Indian commentaries on Vasubandhu’s *Triṃśikā* (T1585), chiefly privileging Dharmapāla; it is neither a simple translation nor solely Xuanzang’s original treatise, but a collaborative translated compilation shaped by Xuanzang and Kuiji.
- **660–663:** *Da bore boluomiduo jing* (大般若波羅蜜多經, *Mahāprajñāpāramitā-sūtra*). A six-hundred-fascicle assemblage in sixteen assemblies (T220), Xuanzang’s largest translation project and itself closer to a library or textual Gesamtkunstwerk than a single book.
- **660:** *Apidamo pinlei zu lun* (阿毘達磨品類足論, *Abhidharma-prakaraṇapāda*). An eighteen-fascicle Abhidharma treatise attributed to Vasumitra (T1542).
- **660:** *Apidamo jiyimen zu lun* (阿毘達磨集異門足論, *Abhidharma-saṅgītiparyāya-pāda*). A twenty-fascicle Abhidharma treatise traditionally associated with Śāriputra (T1536).
- **661:** *Bian zhongbian lun song* (辯中邊論頌, *Madhyāntavibhāga-kārikā*). A one-fascicle independent translation of verses attributed to Maitreya (T1601).
- **661:** *Bian zhongbian lun* (辯中邊論, *Madhyāntavibhāga-bhāṣya*). A three-fascicle translation of Vasubandhu’s commentary on those verses (T1600).
- **661:** *Weishi ershi lun* (唯識二十論, *Viṃśatikā-vijñaptimātratā*). A one-fascicle translation of Vasubandhu’s twenty verses and commentary (T1590).
- **661:** *Yuanqi jing* (緣起經, scripture on dependent origination). A one-fascicle sūtra (T124).
- **662:** *Yibu zonglun lun* (異部宗輪論, *Samayabhedoparacanacakra*). A one-fascicle doxography of Buddhist schools attributed to Vasumitra (T2031).
- **663:** *Apidamo jieshen zu lun* (阿毘達磨界身足論, *Abhidharma-dhātukāya-pāda*). A three-fascicle Abhidharma treatise attributed in Xuanzang’s transmission to Vasumitra, though another tradition assigns it to Pūrṇa (T1540).
- **663:** *Wushi piposha lun* (五事毘婆沙論, commentary on the five categories). A two-fascicle Abhidharma commentary attributed to Dharmatrāta (T1555).
- **663:** *Jizhao shenbian sanmodi jing* (寂照神變三摩地經, scripture of the samādhi of tranquil illumination and miraculous transformation). A one-fascicle meditation scripture (T648).
- **664:** *Zhou wu shou* (呪五首, *Five Spells*). A one-fascicle collection of dhāraṇīs (T1034), traditionally the last item in Xuanzang’s translation chronology; sources that convert its Linde-era date to 665 conflict with Xuanzang’s securely recorded death in 664.

### Dictated, compiled, and documentary works

- **646:** *Da Tang Xiyu ji* (大唐西域記, *Great Tang Records on the Western Regions*). A twelve-fascicle geographical, ethnographic, political, and Buddhist survey dictated from Xuanzang’s travel knowledge and written or compiled by his disciple Bianji (T2087); its divided production makes sole authorship misleading. The major English-language translations are Samuel Beal's two-volume *Buddhist Records of the Western World* (1884), Thomas Watters' annotated *On Yuan Chwang's Travels in India* (1904–05), and Li Rongxi's rendering for the Numata translation series (1996). (Wikipedia, "Great Tang Records on the Western Regions")
- **645–664; collected later:** *Da Tang Da Ci’en si sanzang fashi shang biaoqi* (大唐大慈恩寺三藏法師上表啟; canonically *Da Tang Daci’ensi sanzang fashi shang biaoqi*). A one-fascicle collection of Xuanzang’s memorials, petitions, acknowledgements, letters, prayers, and related court documents (T2119); the individual documents are his occasional acts, but the received “book” is a later compilation rather than a volume he designed.
- **date unknown; attribution disputed:** *Bashi guiju song* (八識規矩頌, *Verses Delineating the Eight Consciousnesses*). A compact doctrinal verse-primer traditionally attributed to Xuanzang, but absent from the early canonical catalogue of his works and first securely visible much later; modern scholarship therefore treats his authorship as doubtful rather than established.
- **date unknown:** *San zhi biliang yi* (三支比量義, teaching on the three-member inference). A logical formulation attributed to Xuanzang survives only through later explanations and excerpts, including the much later *San zhi biliang yi chao* (X861); the received commentary is not Xuanzang’s work, and the extent of his underlying text cannot be recovered.

### Chinese-to-Sanskrit translation project

- **647:** *Daode jing* (道德經, *Classic of the Way and Virtue*), Sanskrit translation. Traditional biographies report that Xuanzang translated—or began translating—Laozi’s text for transmission to India after discussions with the Daoist scholar Cheng Xuanying; the Sanskrit version is lost, and accounts differ over whether the project was completed, so it is better classified as a reported translation project than a securely extant work.

### Architectural and institutional obra

- **645–664:** Xuanzang’s imperial translation workshop. This was an organized, moving production system operating successively at several monasteries and palatial sites, with interpreters, textual recorders, Sanskrit specialists, literary revisers, proofreaders, scribes, and court sponsors; it is the collective obra that produced the translations above, not itself a text.
- **652:** *Great Wild Goose Pagoda* (大雁塔), original five-storey structure at Da Ci’en Monastery. Xuanzang requested and supervised its construction to house the manuscripts, images, and relics brought from India; the present seven-storey monument incorporates major later rebuilding, so the building now visible is a manifestation descended from, but not identical with, his original architectural project.
