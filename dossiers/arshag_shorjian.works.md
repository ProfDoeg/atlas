## Works by Arshag Shorjian

### Orthopedic footwear

- **Before 1926–c. 1998:** Orthopedic shoes, individually untitled and unenumerated. This was a career-long body of made-to-measure utilitarian objects: Shorjian learned shoemaking in a Lebanese orphanage, used the trade after reaching Argentina in 1926, and reported at age 102 that he had made orthopedic shoes throughout his working life and had worked until age 100. No individual shoe, design, workshop catalogue, or catalogue raisonné is identified in the available source; the body of work therefore resists division into separately nameable obras. ([AGBU Magazine](https://agbu.org/agbu-magazine/ola-buenos-aires))
