## Works by Juan Manuel José Domingo Ortiz de Rozas y López de Osornio

### Protocols of rural administration

- **1819:** *Instrucciones para los mayordomos o encargados de estancias*. A working protocol for the personnel administering Rosas’s rural properties, not originally a literary book. Its first publication is uncertain; Adolfo Saldías dated the manuscript to 1819, while later editors sometimes conflated it with the longer 1825 recension. ([1942 critical preface](https://es.wikisource.org/wiki/P%C3%A1gina:Instrucciones_a_Los_Mayordomos_de_estancias_-_Juan_Manuel_de_Rosas.pdf/31); [Fradkin and Gelman](https://es.scribd.com/document/428604619/Fradkin-y-Gelman-Juan-Manuel-de-Rosas-La-Construccion-de-Un-Liderazgo-Politico-COMPLETO))

- **1819:** *Instrucciones para los encargados de las chacras*. A separate agricultural protocol concerning crop farms rather than cattle stations. Its manuscript reportedly survived inside a bound newspaper volume; it must not be silently merged with the better-known instructions for estate managers. ([“El labriego Juan Manuel,” *La Nación*](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/economia/campo/el-labriego-juan-manuel-nid503494/))

- **1825:** *Administración de estancias y demás establecimientos pastoriles en la campaña de Buenos Aires*. A substantially enlarged administrative protocol, first printed by the Imprenta Bonaerense in 1856 and subsequently issued under variants of the title *Instrucciones para la administración de estancias*. An editor described the 1819 and 1825 texts as fundamentally the same work but recorded long sections present only in this recension; it is therefore best treated as a revised manifestation whose independence remains bibliographically ambiguous. ([1942 critical preface](https://es.wikisource.org/wiki/P%C3%A1gina:Instrucciones_a_Los_Mayordomos_de_estancias_-_Juan_Manuel_de_Rosas.pdf/31); [digitized edition](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/bb/Instrucciones_a_Los_Mayordomos_de_estancias_-_Juan_Manuel_de_Rosas.pdf))

### Survey and expedition records

- **1825–1826:** *Diario de la comisión nombrada para establecer la nueva línea de frontera al sud de Buenos-Aires, bajo la dirección del señor coronel D. Juan Manuel de Rosas, con las observaciones astronómicas practicadas por el señor Senillosa*. A collective commission diary and surveying record rather than an unambiguously single-authored book: Rosas directed the undertaking, while Felipe Senillosa supplied the astronomical observations. Its institutional and composite production resists assignment to Rosas alone. ([Academia Mexicana de la Lengua digital record](https://www.letrasmexicanas.mx/portales/academia_mexicana_de_la_lengua/obras/autor/rosas-juan-manuel-de-1793-1877-5151))

- **1833–1834:** *Diario de la expedición al desierto*. The official record of Rosas’s military and diplomatic expedition, subsequently edited as a work under his name. Because such campaign diaries incorporated reports, orders, correspondence, and secretarial labor, it is more securely described as an expeditionary documentary corpus made under Rosas’s direction than as continuous personal authorship. ([bibliographic record](https://catalogo.uns.edu.ar/vufind/Record/CEM.cems007420); [1965 edition record](https://books.google.com.ec/books/about/Diario_de_la_expedici%C3%B3n_al_desierto_183.html?id=rnPjAAAAMAAJ))

### Linguistic compilations

- **c. 1819–1876; unfinished or continuously augmented:** *Gramática y diccionario de la lengua pampa (pampa–ranquel–araucano)*. A manuscript linguistic project comprising grammatical material and Pampa–Spanish and Spanish–Pampa vocabularies, preserved among Rosas’s papers and bequeathed to his daughter in 1876; it appeared in book form only in 1947. The surviving compilation reflects knowledge obtained through Indigenous speakers and intermediaries, so the traditional attribution to Rosas identifies the compiler and owner of the manuscript, not necessarily the sole creator of all its linguistic contents. ([Instituto Nacional de Investigaciones Históricas Juan Manuel de Rosas](https://institutorosas.cultura.gob.ar/noticia/gramatica-y-diccionario-de-la-lengua-pampa-pampa-ranquel-araucano-de-rosas-juan-manuel-2/); [study and manuscript history](https://institutorosas.cultura.gob.ar/media/uploads/site-35/multimedia/gramatica_y_diccionario_de_la_lengua_pampa_pampa_ranquel_araucano.pdf))

### Political interventions

- **20 December 1834:** *Carta de la Hacienda de Figueroa*. A dictated letter to Facundo Quiroga that functions as a political program: it argues that provincial order must precede national constitutional organization. It is a letter, but its intended intervention in Quiroga’s mission and its later canonical status make it closer to a manifesto than to private correspondence; its physical production involved Rosas’s aide as amanuensis. ([UNLP study](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/115096/Documento_completo.pdf-PDFA.pdf%3Fsequence%3D1%26isAllowed%3Dy); [Revista del Instituto account](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Posta_de_Figueroa))

- **1829–1852:** *Messages, proclamations, decrees, orders, diplomatic instructions, and official correspondence of the governments of Juan Manuel de Rosas*. This is a large administrative and performative corpus rather than one titled obra: the texts announced policy, commanded institutions, negotiated with provinces and foreign governments, and helped construct political authority. Authorship ranges from autograph or dictated documents to secretarial and departmental drafting issued over Rosas’s name, so signatures and gubernatorial responsibility cannot automatically be equated with sole composition. The corpus is preserved, but not rendered complete, in Saldías’s *Papeles de Rozas* and later documentary collections. ([*Papeles de Rozas*, 1904–1907](https://dehesa.unex.es/server/api/core/bitstreams/49f4c3fc-246a-4663-89ec-b66921a7d5ae/content); [*Papeles de Rosas*, vol. I](https://institutorosas.cultura.gob.ar/media/uploads/site-35/multimedia/juan_manuel_de_rosas_1.pdf); [Instituto Rosas, “Cartas y documentos”](https://institutorosas.cultura.gob.ar/categoria/58/cartas-y-documentos/))

### Correspondence

- **c. 1820–1876:** *Correspondence of Juan Manuel de Rosas*. Thousands of public, political, business, family, and exile letters constitute a distributed body of work rather than a book conceived by Rosas. The surviving record is incomplete—his correspondence reportedly filled nineteen or twenty cases at his death—and modern volumes are selections assembled by editors, not authorial works in their own right. ([CONICET description of the documentary corpus](https://bicyt.conicet.gov.ar/fichas/produccion/713900); [Museo Udaondo collection](https://archivomuseoudaondopba.com/index.php/coleccion-de-cartas-de-juan-manuel-de-rosas?sf_culture=es); [study of the private letters](https://institutorosas.cultura.gob.ar/media/uploads/site-35/multimedia/juan_manuel_de_rosas.la_revelacion_de_su_pensamiento_a_traves_de_sus_cartas_privadas.pdf))

- **1853–1875:** *Letters from exile*. This is a chronological portion of the correspondence, not a separately conceived obra; *Cartas del exilio, 1853–1875* is José Raed’s posthumous selection and title. The letters nevertheless form a distinct documentary manifestation of Rosas’s life at Southampton and his retrospective political argument. ([bibliographic description of Raed’s selection](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/author/DA09255113))

### Testamentary acts

- **28 August 1862–22 April 1876:** *Testamento y disposiciones adicionales*. A legal act repeatedly amended during Rosas’s English exile, rather than a literary work. The final instrument executed at Burgess Farm on 22 April 1876 governs property, papers, burial, and the transmission of the grammar and dictionary; sources variously call the later clauses a new testament, additions, or a codicil, so the documentary sequence should not be reduced to a single uncontested date. ([transcription of the 1876 instrument](https://documentosdenuestrahistoria.blogspot.com/2015/01/testamento-de-juan-manuel-de-rosas.html); [Instituto Rosas discussion](https://institutorosas.cultura.gob.ar/media/uploads/site-35/multimedia/gvmg_-_brig.gral_juan_manuel_de_rosas_aspectos_poco_conocidos_de_su_vida.pdf))
