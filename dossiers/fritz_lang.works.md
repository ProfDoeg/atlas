## Works by Fritz Lang

### Directed films — the principal obra

- **1919:** *Halbblut*. Lang’s directorial debut, a silent melodrama for Decla; he also wrote the screenplay. The film is lost. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1919:** *Der Herr der Liebe*. Silent feature directed by Lang, who also performed in it; no complete surviving copy is known. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1919:** *Die Spinnen, 1. Teil: Der goldene See*. Silent adventure film written and directed by Lang; the first manifestation of an originally projected four-film serial. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1919:** *Harakiri*. Silent feature directed by Lang, adapted from the same source material as Puccini’s *Madama Butterfly*. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1920:** *Die Spinnen, 2. Teil: Das Brillantenschiff*. Written and directed by Lang; the second and final completed installment of the projected four-part serial. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1920:** *Das wandernde Bild*. Silent melodrama directed and co-written by Lang with Thea von Harbou; it survives only in an incomplete reconstruction. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1921:** *Kämpfende Herzen* (also *Vier um die Frau*). Silent crime melodrama directed, co-written, and edited by Lang; its variant titles name the same obra, not separate films. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1921:** *Der müde Tod*. Silent episodic fantasy directed, co-written, and edited by Lang; English-language manifestations have circulated as *Destiny* and *Between Two Worlds*. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1922:** *Dr. Mabuse, der Spieler, 1. Teil: Der große Spieler. Ein Bild der Zeit*. The first feature-length part of Lang and von Harbou’s silent crime epic. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1922:** *Dr. Mabuse, der Spieler, 2. Teil: Inferno. Ein Spiel von Menschen unserer Zeit*. The second feature-length part of the same two-part obra, directed and co-written by Lang. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1924:** *Die Nibelungen, 1. Teil: Siegfried*. The first film of Lang’s monumental silent diptych, scripted with Thea von Harbou. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1924:** *Die Nibelungen, 2. Teil: Kriemhilds Rache*. The second film of the diptych; it is both an individual manifestation and half of the larger obra *Die Nibelungen*. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1927:** *Metropolis*. Feature-length silent science-fiction film directed, co-written, and edited by Lang. Its radically shortened release versions and later reconstructions make “the film” a historically unstable object rather than one fixed manifestation. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1928:** *Spione*. Espionage thriller directed, co-written, and produced through Fritz Lang-Film GmbH. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1929:** *Frau im Mond*. Silent science-fiction feature directed and produced by Lang; it was co-written with Thea von Harbou. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1931:** *M*. Lang’s first sound feature, directed and co-written with von Harbou; Lang also supplied the murderer’s whistled musical motif, an uncredited sonic performance. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1933:** *Das Testament des Dr. Mabuse*. Sound crime feature directed and co-written by Lang. A separately shot French-language manifestation, *Le Testament du docteur Mabuse*, was supervised by Lang, with René Sti directing some French-version material; treating the versions as one or two works therefore remains contestable. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1934:** *Liliom*. French feature directed by Lang from Ferenc Molnár’s play; it was his only completed French production. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1936:** *Fury*. Lang’s first completed American feature; he directed and contributed to the screenplay, although Bartlett Cormack received the principal screenplay credit. [AFI Catalog](https://catalog.afi.com/Catalog/PersonDetails/56225)
- **1937:** *You Only Live Once*. American crime melodrama directed by Lang. [AFI Catalog](https://catalog.afi.com/Catalog/PersonDetails/56225)
- **1938:** *You and Me*. Feature directed and produced by Lang, incorporating songs by Kurt Weill; its mixed dramatic, musical, and didactic construction resists a single genre. [AFI Catalog](https://catalog.afi.com/Catalog/PersonDetails/56225)
- **1940:** *The Return of Frank James*. Technicolor western directed by Lang. [AFI Catalog](https://catalog.afi.com/Catalog/PersonDetails/56225)
- **1941:** *Western Union*. Technicolor western directed by Lang; filmographies variously date it by production in 1940 or release in 1941. [AFI Catalog](https://catalog.afi.com/Catalog/PersonDetails/56225)
- **1941:** *Man Hunt*. Anti-Nazi thriller directed by Lang from Geoffrey Household’s novel *Rogue Male*. [AFI Catalog](https://catalog.afi.com/Catalog/PersonDetails/56225)
- **1943:** *Hangmen Also Die!*. Anti-Nazi feature directed and produced by Lang, from a story devised with Bertolt Brecht and adapted by Lang and John Wexley; the precise division of screenplay authorship became contentious. [AFI Catalog](https://catalog.afi.com/Catalog/PersonDetails/56225)
- **1944:** *Ministry of Fear*. Espionage thriller directed by Lang from Graham Greene’s novel. [AFI Catalog](https://catalog.afi.com/Catalog/PersonDetails/56225)
- **1944:** *The Woman in the Window*. Psychological crime film directed by Lang. [AFI Catalog](https://catalog.afi.com/Catalog/PersonDetails/56225)
- **1945:** *Scarlet Street*. Crime melodrama directed and produced by Lang through Diana Productions. [AFI Catalog](https://catalog.afi.com/Catalog/PersonDetails/56225)
- **1946:** *Cloak and Dagger*. Espionage feature directed by Lang; the released film omits an ending Lang shot, so the surviving studio manifestation does not fully represent his intended project. [AFI Catalog](https://catalog.afi.com/Catalog/PersonDetails/56225)
- **1947:** *Secret Beyond the Door…*. Psychological thriller directed and produced by Lang through Diana Productions; some catalogues date it 1948, its general-release year. [AFI Catalog](https://catalog.afi.com/Catalog/PersonDetails/56225)
- **1950:** *House by the River*. Period crime feature directed by Lang; production began in 1949, accounting for the 1949–50 catalogue date. [AFI Catalog](https://catalog.afi.com/Catalog/PersonDetails/56225)
- **1950:** *American Guerrilla in the Philippines*. War feature directed by Lang. [AFI Catalog](https://catalog.afi.com/Catalog/PersonDetails/56225)
- **1952:** *Rancho Notorious*. Western directed by Lang; it was produced in 1951 and released in 1952. [AFI Catalog](https://catalog.afi.com/Catalog/PersonDetails/56225)
- **1952:** *Clash by Night*. Film drama directed by Lang from Clifford Odets’s play. [AFI Catalog](https://catalog.afi.com/Catalog/PersonDetails/56225)
- **1953:** *The Blue Gardenia*. Crime film directed by Lang. [AFI Catalog](https://catalog.afi.com/Catalog/PersonDetails/56225)
- **1953:** *The Big Heat*. Crime film directed by Lang from William P. McGivern’s novel. [AFI Catalog](https://catalog.afi.com/Catalog/PersonDetails/56225)
- **1954:** *Human Desire*. Railroad crime melodrama directed by Lang, adapted indirectly from Émile Zola’s *La Bête humaine*. [AFI Catalog](https://catalog.afi.com/Catalog/PersonDetails/56225)
- **1955:** *Moonfleet*. Color period adventure directed by Lang; he later repudiated aspects of MGM’s production and release, making authorship of the finished manifestation qualified rather than absolute. [AFI Catalog](https://catalog.afi.com/Catalog/PersonDetails/56225)
- **1956:** *While the City Sleeps*. Newspaper crime drama directed by Lang; it was produced in 1955 and released in 1956. [AFI Catalog](https://catalog.afi.com/Catalog/PersonDetails/56225)
- **1956:** *Beyond a Reasonable Doubt*. Crime feature directed by Lang. [AFI Catalog](https://catalog.afi.com/Catalog/PersonDetails/56225)
- **1959:** *Der Tiger von Eschnapur*. West German–French–Italian adventure feature directed and co-written by Lang; it is the first half of his two-film “Indian epic.” [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1959:** *Das indische Grabmal*. Second half of the “Indian epic,” directed and co-written by Lang. The condensed English-language *Journey to the Lost City* is a later manifestation assembled from both films, not a separate original project. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1960:** *Die 1000 Augen des Dr. Mabuse*. Crime feature directed and co-written by Lang; failing eyesight made it his final completed directorial work. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)

### Partly directed films — incomplete authorship

- **1941:** *Confirm or Deny*. Lang began directing this wartime newspaper drama but was removed or withdrew after several days; Archie Mayo completed the film and received sole screen credit. It therefore resists inclusion among Lang’s completed works, although some catalogues preserve his directorial contribution. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1942:** *Moontide*. Lang directed the opening weeks before leaving the production; Archie Mayo completed and was credited for the released film. What Lang made survives only as material inside another director’s finished obra. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)

### Screenplays and scenarios realized by other directors

- **1916:** *Die Peitsche*. Detective-film scenario usually identified as Lang’s first produced screenplay; the attribution is not wholly secure, and some filmographies explicitly mark it uncertain. The film is lost. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1917:** *Die Hochzeit im Excentric-Club*. Detective-film screenplay by Lang, directed by Joe May; Lang was not credited on the released film. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1917:** *Hilde Warren und der Tod*. Screenplay by Lang for Joe May’s silent fantasy melodrama; Lang also appeared in a minor role, but did not direct it despite occasional erroneous filmography listings. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1918:** *Die Frauen des Josias Grafenreuth*. Silent-film screenplay attributed to Lang and directed by Otto Rippert; the film is lost. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1918–1919:** *Bettler-G.m.b.H.*. Silent crime-film screenplay by Lang, directed by Alwin Neuß. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1918–1919:** *Die Rache ist mein*. Silent-film screenplay by Lang, directed by Alwin Neuß. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1919:** *Die Frau mit den Orchideen*. Silent-film screenplay by Lang, directed by Otto Rippert. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1919:** *Die Pest in Florenz*. Silent historical-fantasy screenplay by Lang, directed by Otto Rippert. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1919:** *Lilith und Ly*. Silent-film screenplay by Lang, directed by Erich Kober. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1919:** *Totentanz*. Silent-film screenplay by Lang, directed by Otto Rippert; the film is lost. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1919:** *Wolkenbau und Flimmerstern*. Produced screenplay credited to Lang; the director is not securely identified, and surviving information is fragmentary. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1919–1920:** *Die Herrin der Welt, 8. Teil: Die Rache der Maud Fergusson*. Lang contributed the screenplay for the concluding installment of Joe May’s eight-part serial; it is a component text, not authorship of the whole serial. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1921:** *Das indische Grabmal, 1. Teil: Die Sendung des Yoghi*. Lang and Thea von Harbou wrote the screenplay, intending Lang to direct; producer Joe May instead directed it. The screenplay thus survives as a realized but authorially displaced project. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1921:** *Das indische Grabmal, 2. Teil: Der Tiger von Eschnapur*. Second half of the Lang–von Harbou screenplay project directed by Joe May; Lang later returned to the material in his own 1959 diptych. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1951:** *M*. Joseph Losey’s American remake derives from the Lang–von Harbou scenario rather than from a new screenplay made by Lang; it is therefore a later manifestation of his earlier text, not a fresh Lang obra. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)

### Printed narrative and critical texts

- **1919–1920:** *Die Spinnen*. Lang’s adventure narrative appeared both as a book and serially in *Film-Kurier* alongside the films. Novel, newspaper serial, screenplay, and two completed films are distinct manifestations of a cross-media project rather than interchangeable editions of one “book.” [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/en/person/fritz-lang_efc121b064de6c3fe03053d50b3736f2)
- **1924:** *Worauf es beim Nibelungen-Film ankam*. An essay by Lang explaining the artistic project of *Die Nibelungen*; it is a programmatic critical text, though not quite a manifesto. [Thomas Elsaesser, *The Films of Fritz Lang*](https://dokumen.pub/the-films-of-fritz-lang-allegories-of-vision-and-modernity-0851707424-9780851707426.html)
- **1924:** *Stilwille im Film*. A short theoretical statement on cinematic style by Lang; it acts as an aesthetic declaration and approaches manifesto form. [Thomas Elsaesser, *The Films of Fritz Lang*](https://dokumen.pub/the-films-of-fritz-lang-allegories-of-vision-and-modernity-0851707424-9780851707426.html)
- **1924:** *Kitsch—Sensation—Kultur und Film*. Lang’s intervention in the German “cinema debate,” first published in *Das Kulturfilmbuch*; it is an argumentative essay about popular cinema rather than a screenplay or film. [Bibliographic record](https://bibliotekanauki.pl/articles/2083942.pdf)

### Performances as authored presences

- **1917:** *Hilde Warren und der Tod*. Lang’s minor screen performance is a manifestation distinct from his screenplay contribution; the film was directed by Joe May. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1919:** *Der Herr der Liebe*. Lang performed in the silent feature he directed; the acting contribution cannot be separated cleanly from the directorial obra because the film is lost. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1931:** *M*. Lang himself whistled the recurring theme associated with the murderer; this is an uncredited sound performance embedded in the film rather than a freestanding musical composition. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)
- **1963:** *Le Mépris*. Lang plays a fictionalized “Fritz Lang” directing an adaptation of the *Odyssey* in Jean-Luc Godard’s film. The performance deliberately occupies the unstable boundary between person, persona, and authored work. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/person/fritz-lang_f3c303c2f8924d8c876acc4e2004b6d0)

### Unrealized projects

- **1919–1920:** *Die Spinnen*, projected third and fourth parts. Lang conceived the serial as four films but completed only *Der goldene See* and *Das Brillantenschiff*. The absent installments are projects—intentions belonging to the larger obra—not lost completed films, and their final titles are not securely fixed. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/en/person/fritz-lang_efc121b064de6c3fe03053d50b3736f2)
- **1930s–1950s:** *The Man Behind You*. An unproduced American Mabuse-like story and screenplay project that Lang repeatedly reworked; because no definitive film or single settled text resulted, it resists being called a completed work. [Tom Gunning, *The Films of Fritz Lang*](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781838718855_A40384916/preview-9781838718855_A40384916.pdf)
- **1950s:** *20 July Plot project*. A proposed film concerning the attempted assassination of Hitler on 20 July 1944; it never reached production, and the consulted catalogue does not establish a single fixed title. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/en/person/fritz-lang_efc121b064de6c3fe03053d50b3736f2)
- **1950s:** *Störtebeker project*. An unrealized film project about the pirate Klaus Störtebeker. It remains a project rather than a film, and the catalogue supplies no authoritative finished title or date. [DFF/filmportal](https://www.filmportal.de/en/person/fritz-lang_efc121b064de6c3fe03053d50b3736f2)
- **1930s–1970s:** *Unproduced scripts, treatments, and research projects preserved in the Fritz Lang papers*. This is an archival corpus rather than one work, and the USC collection is unprocessed; consequently its contents cannot honestly be converted into a complete title-by-title oeuvre without inventing completeness. [USC Cinematic Arts Library finding aid](https://oac.cdlib.org/findaid/ark%3A%2F13030%2Fc8gq7552)
