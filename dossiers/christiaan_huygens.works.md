## Works by Christiaan Huygens

### Mathematical treatises and interventions

- **1649:** *Contributions to Frans van Schooten’s edition of Descartes’s Geometria*. Mathematical notes and solutions incorporated into another author’s edition rather than issued as an independent book; revised contributions followed in 1659. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, pp. 375–376](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1651:** *Theoremata de quadratura hyperboles, ellipsis et circuli… quibus subjuncta est Exetasis Cyclometriae*. A printed mathematical obra combining theorems on quadrature and centres of gravity with a critical examination of Grégoire de Saint-Vincent’s circle-squaring. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XI](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv11_01/huyg003oeuv11_01_0046.php)
- **1654:** *De circuli magnitudine inventa. Accedunt ejusdem problematum quorundam illustrium constructiones*. A printed mathematical treatise on approximating the circle, accompanied by constructions of celebrated geometrical problems. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 375](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1656:** *Ad C.V. Fran. Xaver. Ainscom S.I. epistola…*. A separately printed polemical letter defending Huygens’s earlier *Exetasis Cyclometriae*; it is both correspondence and public mathematical intervention. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 375](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1657:** *Tractatus de ratiociniis in aleae ludo*. A foundational probability treatise published in Latin translation by Frans van Schooten; the translation is Schooten’s, while the mathematical obra is Huygens’s. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 375](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1659:** *Revised contributions to Frans van Schooten’s edition of Descartes’s Geometria*. Mathematical supplements embedded in another scholar’s edition, and therefore resistant to classification as a freestanding book. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 375](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1660:** *Tractaet handelende van Reeckening in Speelen van Geluck* / *Van Rekeningh in Spelen van Geluk*. The Dutch manifestation of Huygens’s probability treatise, issued both within Van Schooten’s *Mathematische Oeffeningen* and separately; these are manifestations of the same obra, not two works. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, pp. 375–376](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1668:** *Examen de la Vera Circuli & Hyperboles Quadratura… à Jacobo Gregorio Scoto*. A journal review and mathematical critique of James Gregory’s claimed quadratures. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 376](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1668:** *Maniere pour trouver par le moyen des logarithmes la dimension de l’espace hyperbolique…*. A journal memoir giving a logarithmic procedure for measuring a hyperbolic area. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 376](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1668:** *Second article on Gregory’s Vera Circuli & Hyperboles Quadratura*. A further journal intervention in the quadrature controversy. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 376](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1673:** *Contributions to Excerpta ex Epistolis… Slusio & Hugenio*. Published correspondence concerning solutions of Alhazen’s problem; authorship is distributed among Huygens, René-François de Sluse and editor Henry Oldenburg. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, pp. 377–378](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1682:** *Response to the abbé de Catelan concerning proposition IV of the treatise on centres of oscillation*. A published mathematical reply, appearing as an extract from a letter rather than an autonomous treatise. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 379](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1684:** *Response to Catelan’s reply concerning centres of oscillation*. A second published epistolary intervention in the same dispute. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 379](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1687:** *Solution du Probleme proposé par M. Leibnitz*. A short published solution to Leibniz’s mathematical challenge. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 379](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1690:** *Remarque… sur la lettre du Marquis de l’Hospital… et sur le récit de Mr. Bernoulli*. A journal note intervening in discussion of the centre of oscillation of a compound pendulum. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 379](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1691:** *Solutio problematis funicularii*. A published solution of the catenary problem. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 379](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1693:** *Letter concerning the rectification of the logarithmic curve, the catenary, and related questions*. A published letter whose unity is conversational rather than treatise-like. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 379](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1693:** *Constructio loci ad hyperbolam per asymptotos*. A concise geometrical construction published in the French Academy’s collective volume. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 380](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1693:** *Demonstratio regulae de maximis et minimis*. A mathematical demonstration concerning maxima and minima, published as an Academy memoir. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 380](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1693:** *Regula ad inveniendas tangentes curvarum*. A protocol-like mathematical rule for finding tangents to curves. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 380](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1693:** *Constructio d’un problème d’optique qui est la IX proposition du livre V d’Alhazen*. A geometrical construction solving an optical problem attributed to Alhazen. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 380](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1693:** *De problemate Bernouliano*. A published response to a Bernoulli problem. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 380](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1694:** *Constructio universalis Problematis a… Jo. Bernoulio… propositi*. A general construction answering Johann Bernoulli’s challenge. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 380](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1694:** *Excerpta ex epistola C.H.Z. ad G.G.L.*. Extracts from Huygens’s letter to Leibniz, made public as an epistolary mathematical text rather than composed as a separate treatise. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 380](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)

### Astronomy and cosmology

- **1656:** *De Saturni lunâ observatio nova*. A printed astronomical announcement of the satellite now called Titan; it acts as a priority claim as well as an observational report. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 375](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1659:** *Systema Saturnium, sive de causis mirandorum Saturni phaenomenôn, et comite ejus planeta novo*. An illustrated astronomical treatise explaining Saturn’s changing appearance by means of a ring and treating its newly observed satellite. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 375](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1660:** *Brevis Assertio Systematis Saturnii sui*. A brief printed defence of Huygens’s Saturn system, addressed to Prince Leopold de’ Medici and issued in The Hague and Florence. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 376](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1669:** *Observation de Saturne faite à la Bibliothèque du Roy*. A journal report of a specific observation; its English translation is a later manifestation, not another obra. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 376](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1671:** *Contribution to Suite des Observations des taches du soleil… avec… Observations concernant Saturne*. Observational material incorporated into an Academy publication; it does not possess the independence of a book. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 377](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1671:** *Extract of a letter to Henry Oldenburg on Saturn and related matters*. A letter transformed by publication into an astronomical article. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 377](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1672:** *Extract of a letter concerning the figure of Saturn*. A short journal manifestation of Huygens’s continuing Saturn observations. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 377](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1684:** *Astroscopia compendiaria, tubi optici molimine liberata*. A printed proposal for observing celestial objects without the cumbersome tube of an extremely long telescope: part treatise, part instrumental project. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 379](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1695; published 1698:** *Kosmotheoros, sive de Terris Coelestibus, earumque ornatu, Conjecturae*. A posthumously published cosmological speculation on planetary worlds and their possible inhabitants; its explicitly conjectural status is essential to what kind of text it is. The 1699 printing and contemporary translations are manifestations, not new works. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 380](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)

### Timekeepers, navigation and mechanical physics

- **1656; published 1703:** *De motu corporum ex percussione*. A treatise on collision completed long before its posthumous printing; the work existed as manuscript obra before it became a book. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 381](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1657 onward:** *Pendulum-clock project*. A continuing technical project joining mathematical theory, clock designs, prototypes and trials rather than a single object. Huygens devised the regulating system, while surviving clocks were materially constructed with clockmakers, so “made by Huygens” cannot mean sole fabrication. [Huygens ING, “Instruments and inventions”](https://dwc.knaw.nl/en/biografie/christiaan-huygensweb/instrumenten-en-uitvindingen/), [Rijksmuseum Boerhaave collection](https://www.rijksmuseumboerhaave.nl/en/collection)
- **1658:** *Horologium*. A short printed announcement and description of the pendulum clock; it is simultaneously technical disclosure, priority instrument and precursor to the larger 1673 treatise. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 375](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1659; published 1703:** *De vi centrifuga*. A manuscript treatise deriving results on centrifugal force, printed only after Huygens’s death. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 381](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1665:** *Kort onderwijs aengaende het gebruyck der Horologien tot het vinden der Lenghten van Oost en West*. A protocol instructing navigators how to use pendulum clocks to determine longitude; the partial English publication of 1669 is a manifestation of the same work. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 376](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1665:** *Letter extract on the “sympathy” of clocks*. A report of coupled-clock synchronization, made public in the *Journal des Sçavans*; because it was extracted from a private letter, its published boundaries were editorially produced. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 376](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1669:** *Regles du mouvement dans la rencontre des corps*. A concise public statement of collision rules, distinct from but related to the fuller posthumous *De motu corporum ex percussione*. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 377](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1673:** *Horologium oscillatorium, sive de motu pendulorum ad horologia aptato demonstrationes geometricae*. A mathematical-mechanical Gesamtkunstwerk joining clock design, pendulum theory, cycloidal geometry, centres of oscillation and centrifugal force in one printed object. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 377](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1673:** *Unpublished piece on the hydrostatic paradox*. A manuscript sent for publication but apparently left unprinted because the *Journal des Sçavans* issued no number that year; it is best classified as an unrealized publication project, not a lost book. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 377](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1675:** *Letter on “une invention… par laquelle les horloges sont renduës tres justes ensemble & portatives”*. A public announcement of a portable, highly regular balance-spring watch; the text also acts as a priority claim. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 378](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1675 onward:** *Balance-spring watch project*. A family of portable timekeeper designs and implementations, not one securely isolable artifact; questions of priority and the work of collaborating craftsmen prevent simple attribution of every physical watch to Huygens alone. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XVIII, contents](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv00_01/), [Huygens ING, “Instruments and inventions”](https://dwc.knaw.nl/en/biografie/christiaan-huygensweb/instrumenten-en-uitvindingen/)
- **1693:** *Démonstration de l’équilibre de la balance*. A mechanics memoir demonstrating the equilibrium of the balance. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 379](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1693:** *De potentiis fila funesque trahentibus*. A mechanics memoir on forces exerted through threads and ropes. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 380](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)

### Optics, light and atmospheric phenomena

- **1653–1692; published 1703:** *Dioptrica*. A long-developing body of geometrical optics assembled posthumously from manuscripts; the editors acknowledged difficulties in arranging it, so it resists identification with one finished authorial text. [*Oeuvres complètes*, I, preface](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv01_01/huyg003oeuv01_01_0001.php), [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 380](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1662 or 1663; published 1703:** *De coronis et parheliis*. A Dutch manuscript treatise on halos, coronas and parhelia, first printed after Huygens’s death in Latin translation as *Dissertatio de coronis et parheliis*. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XVII and XXII](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv00_01/)
- **1667:** *Relation d’une observation… d’un halo ou couronne à l’entour du soleil, avec un discours de la cause de ces meteores & de celle des parelies*. An observational report joined to a causal essay on atmospheric optics; only part was reproduced in the collected works. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 376](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1672:** *Extract of a letter concerning Newton’s catoptric telescope*. A journal response to an instrument and its optical principle. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 377](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1672:** *Reflexions sur la description d’une lunette publiée sous le nom de M. Cassegrain*. A critical optical note on the reflecting-telescope design associated with Cassegrain. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 377](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1673:** *Considerations upon Mr. Newton’s doctrine of Colors… and different Refractions… in Telescopical Glasses*. An English editorial translation of a Huygens letter; the underlying obra is epistolary, while its English article-form is Oldenburg’s manifestation. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, pp. 377–378](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1673:** *Answer concerning Newton’s doctrine of colours*. A second letter translated and published by Henry Oldenburg, continuing Huygens’s intervention in the colours controversy. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 378](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1674:** *Letter concerning Hooke’s observations offered as proof of the Earth’s motion*. A published extract from correspondence evaluating an observational claim. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 378](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1678:** *Extract of a letter concerning a new kind of microscope*. A journal announcement of an optical instrument brought from Holland; the accompanying Hartsoeker piece was reportedly redacted by Huygens, making its authorship collaborative rather than wholly his. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 378](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1690:** *Traité de la lumière*. A theoretical treatise explaining reflection, refraction and Iceland spar through a wave construction; it was printed together with, but remains conceptually distinct from, the *Discours de la cause de la pesanteur*. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 379](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **Before 1703; published 1703:** *Memorien aengaende het slijpen van glasen tot verrekijckers*. A Dutch technical manuscript on forming and polishing telescope lenses, published posthumously in Latin as *Commentarii de formandis poliendisque vitris ad telescopia*; it is a protocol as much as a treatise. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, pp. 380–381](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)

### Pneumatics, fluids, surveying and engines

- **1672:** *Extract of a letter concerning water purged of air*. An experimental report addressing the anomalous suspension of mercury in a Torricellian apparatus. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 377](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1672:** *Extract of a letter concerning a new kind of barometer*. A public description of an instrumental design, simultaneously text and prototype project. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 377](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1673:** *Nouvelle force mouvante par le moyen de la poudre à canon et de l’air*. A gunpowder-and-vacuum engine project recorded experimentally in 1673 and published as an Academy memoir in 1693. The surviving work is both a machine conception and a text; individual apparatuses should not automatically be treated as sole-authored artifacts. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, pp. 240–251, 380](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0191.php)
- **1674:** *Contributions to Denis Papin’s Nouvelles experiences du vuide*. Experimental contributions embedded in Papin’s publication; their boundaries and authorship are collaborative. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 378](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1680:** *Nouvelle invention d’un niveau à lunette qui porte sa preuve avec soy*. A published description of a self-checking telescopic spirit level: both instrumental design and textual announcement. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 378](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1680:** *Demonstration de la justesse du Niveau*. A companion demonstration validating the telescopic level. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 379](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1693:** *Remarque… sur le livre de la Manoeuvre des Vaisseaux*. A critical journal note on Renau d’Éliçagaray’s naval mechanics. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 380](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1694:** *Replique… à la Reponse de Mr. Renau*. A published reply continuing the naval-mechanics controversy; the collected edition notes uncertainty about its exact issue date. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 380](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **1694:** *Raisons pour ne plus continuer la dispute avec Mr. Renau*. The final, originally untitled piece in the dispute; the familiar title is supplied by the collected edition and should not be mistaken for Huygens’s fixed title. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 380](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **Undated; published 1950:** *Calculs et considérations sur les résistances éprouvées par différentes surfaces… et sur les vitesses que le vent peut donner à des voiliers*. A manuscript study of fluid resistance and sailing performance, reconstructed from papers rather than issued by Huygens as a finished book. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, pp. 325–354](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0191.php)

### Music and harmonic systems

- **1691:** *Lettre de Mr. Huygens touchant le Cycle Harmonique*. A published letter proposing a harmonic cycle; its epistolary form matters because it announces and argues rather than presenting a finished comprehensive treatise. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 379](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **Before 1695; published 1724:** *Novus cyclus harmonicus*. A posthumously issued music-theoretical work on tuning and division of the octave. Its 1724 appearance is a publication manifestation of an earlier Huygens manuscript, not a work made after his death. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XX, contents](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv00_01/)

### Designed instruments and technical projects

- **1650s–1690s:** *Telescope designs and telescope assemblies*. A continuing instrumental obra encompassing optical calculations, long-focus telescopes, eyepieces and tubeless mounting schemes; Huygens designed and used them with his brother Constantijn and specialist makers, so no undifferentiated class of “Huygens telescopes” should be attributed to his hands alone. [Huygens ING, “Instruments and inventions”](https://dwc.knaw.nl/en/biografie/christiaan-huygensweb/instrumenten-en-uitvindingen/)
- **1650s–1690s:** *Lens-grinding machines*. Mechanical projects for producing telescope lenses, preserved through drawings, notes and posthumously published instructions; these are protocols and machine designs, not necessarily surviving Huygens-built objects. [Huygens ING, “Instruments and inventions”](https://dwc.knaw.nl/en/biografie/christiaan-huygensweb/instrumenten-en-uitvindingen/)
- **1658:** *Three-pole apparatus for raising an astronomical telescope*. An engineering drawing or project rather than a securely documented surviving machine. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 181](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0191.php)
- **1658:** *Four drawings*. A small visual corpus identified by the collected edition as drawings by Huygens; their status is documentary and graphic rather than textual. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 177](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0191.php)
- **1659:** *Spring sandal*. A sketched mechanical curiosity; it resists being called a completed work because the record establishes a design, not a realized object. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, pp. 196–197](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0191.php)
- **1659:** *Projection apparatus for macabre figures (“magic lantern”)*. A note or design for projecting images with convex lenses and a lamp. Attribution of the general invention of the magic lantern is historically contested, so this entry records Huygens’s project rather than awarding exclusive invention. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 197](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0191.php)
- **1664 or 1665:** *Marine clock with remontoire*. A design and description within the longitude-clock project, not a securely separable surviving artifact. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, pp. 202–203](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0191.php)
- **1665 or 1666; 1668; 1694?:** *Carriage designs*. Several drawings or notes for carriage construction; the collected edition itself places a question mark against the 1694 “new carriages,” so realization and even precise status remain uncertain. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, pp. 203, 278, 322](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0191.php)
- **1666–1681:** *Pneumatic pumps and compressed-air apparatus*. A dispersed family of machine designs and experiments, including an apparatus intended to compress air hundreds of times; it is a research project rather than one stable object. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, pp. 213–292](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0191.php)
- **1668:** *Chair for taking altitude aboard ship*. An observational-seat design intended to stabilize astronomical measurement at sea; the source documents the project, not necessarily a completed chair. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, pp. 227–228](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0191.php)
- **1669:** *Nova chalcographiae ratio*. A proposed new method of printing or engraving; it survives as a technical conception rather than an established production process. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, pp. 233–235](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0191.php)
- **1672:** *Improved fountain of Heron and other water-raising apparatus*. A group of hydraulic designs, best treated as related projects rather than one machine. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, pp. 238–240](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0191.php)
- **1678:** *Ship-saving spring systems*. Several designs intended to prevent vessels breaking when grounding; repeated reformulation shows an unfinished engineering project rather than a settled invention. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, pp. 256, 260–266](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0191.php)
- **1680:** *Silent clock without wheels*. A recorded clock project whose surviving textual description does not prove completion of a physical instrument. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, pp. 281–283](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0191.php)
- **1682:** *Automatic heliocentric planetarium*. A clockwork model designed to Huygens’s specifications and constructed by a craftsman; it is therefore a collaborative material obra, not an object fabricated by Huygens alone. [*Descriptio Automati Planetarii*, below](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php), [Rijksmuseum Boerhaave collection](https://www.rijksmuseumboerhaave.nl/en/collection)
- **Before 1695; published 1703:** *Descriptio Automati Planetarii*. A posthumously printed description and design of the automatic planetarium: the textual manifestation of a collaborative instrument project. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, p. 381](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0147.php)
- **Various dates:** *Improvements to microscopes, pneumatic pumps, carriages, wind-force meters, barometers, spirit levels and depth gauges*. Huygens ING attributes improvements in these instrument families to him, but this is not a catalogue of individually surviving artifacts; the appropriate unit is a body of technical work whose physical execution often involved makers. [Huygens ING, “Instruments and inventions”](https://dwc.knaw.nl/en/biografie/christiaan-huygensweb/instrumenten-en-uitvindingen/)

### Correspondence, notebooks and resistant corpora

- **1638–1695:** *Correspondence*. Thousands of letters, drafts and extracts form an epistolary obra distributed across senders, recipients, languages and later editorial selections. It resists treatment as either one authored book or thousands of wholly independent works; the standard edition devotes its first ten volumes plus supplements to the corpus. [*Oeuvres complètes*, I–X and XXII](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv00_01/)
- **1645–1695:** *Mathematical, astronomical, optical, mechanical and musical working papers*. Calculations, observations, diagrams, unfinished treatises and drafts preserved in the *Codices Hugeniani* constitute Huygens’s workshop on paper. Many have no authorial title or fixed boundary, and the collected edition organizes them retrospectively by subject; that resistance to discrete “work” status is part of their identity. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XI–XXII](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv00_01/)
- **Various dates:** *Astronomical observation records and drawings*. A continuing observational corpus—including drawings of planetary appearances—rather than a single text; some records became publications, while others remained dated notebook events. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XV](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv00_01/)
- **Various dates:** *Notes on books, extracts from scientific journals and newspapers, addresses, recipes and accounts*. A heterogeneous archival accumulation preserved by the collected edition. These materials were written or copied by Huygens but often resist being called works because they function as reading traces, practical memory and household record rather than composed obra. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, pp. 355–371](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0191.php)
- **Various dates:** *Marginalia in the Acta Eruditorum*. Reading annotations rather than an autonomous text; their later editorial publication does not retroactively make them a book conceived by Huygens. [*Oeuvres complètes*, XXII, pp. 785–811](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv22_01/huyg003oeuv22_01_0191.php)
