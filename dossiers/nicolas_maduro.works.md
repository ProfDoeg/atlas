## Works by Nicolás Maduro Moros

### Political projects and protocols

- **2013:** *Líneas estratégicas del Gobierno Revolucionario presentadas por el presidente de la República Nicolás Maduro Moros*. A twelve-point governmental protocol delivered as a presidential address and subsequently issued by Ediciones de la Presidencia; the publication presents the proposals as Maduro’s, although the book-object itself was produced institutionally rather than authored by him in the conventional literary sense. ([digitized edition](https://id.scribd.com/document/948408058/2-12-Garis-Strategis-Presiden-Maduro-1))

- **2018:** *7 líneas estratégicas del presidente Nicolás Maduro*. A governmental project or action protocol proposed after his disputed 2018 reelection and issued by the Ministry of Communication and Information; editorial staff, rather than Maduro, assembled the published object from his statements. ([edition and credits](https://es.readkong.com/page/7-l-neas-estrat-gicas-del-presidente-nicol-s-maduro-5188566))

### Speeches issued as discrete works

- **2019:** *Rebelión popular contra el neoliberalismo en América Latina*. The closing address Maduro delivered at the Anti-Imperialist Encounter of Solidarity for Democracy and against Neoliberalism, treated as a discrete work when the Ministry of Culture published it as a 78-page volume. It remains an oration rather than a book conceived as such. ([bibliographic record](https://books.google.com/books/about/Rebeli%C3%B3n_popular_contra_el_neoliberalis.html?id=FvOGzgEACAAJ))

- **10 January 2019:** *Discurso de juramentación como presidente constitucional de la República Bolivariana de Venezuela para el período 2019–2025*. A performative political text—a manifesto-like act of assuming office, not merely a description of one. Its status is inseparable from the dispute over the legitimacy of the presidential term; scholarship identifies the official transcript as a primary text without thereby resolving that dispute. ([scholarly corpus citation](https://bibliotekanauki.pl/articles/1179268.pdf))

- **23 January 2019:** *Discurso después de la autoproclamación de Juan Guaidó como presidente interino*. An extemporaneous political address responding to Guaidó’s claim to the presidency; unlike the inauguration speech, the scholarly corpus relies on a researcher’s transcription, so wording and title have no independently established authorial form. ([scholarly corpus citation](https://bibliotekanauki.pl/articles/1179268.pdf))

### Paratext

- **2013:** *Pórtico a El Libro Azul*. Maduro’s signed prologue to an edition of Hugo Chávez’s *El Libro Azul*. It is Maduro’s text but not Maduro’s book: the distinction matters because bibliographic records sometimes display both names while identifying Chávez as the work’s author and Maduro specifically as prologue-writer. ([edition contents](https://es.readkong.com/page/el-libro-azul-hugo-ch-vez-fr-as-nicol-s-maduro-moros-2436749); [independent bibliographic description](https://www.agapea.com/Hugo-Chavez-Frias/El-libro-azul-9788415674665-i.htm))

### Institutional compilations containing Maduro’s texts

- **2014:** *Un año de unidad, lucha, batalla y victoria*. An illustrated governmental retrospective of Maduro’s first presidential year. Its opening presentation is signed by Maduro, but the publication credits institutional editors and news and presidential photographic services; the whole volume therefore resists classification as his authored work and is best understood as a state-produced documentary compilation containing his paratext. ([edition and credits](https://www.yumpu.com/es/document/view/28752073/xqw5k))

- **2015:** *La verdad sobre el Esequibo*. A state dossier combining chronology, maps, historical documents, declarations, and Maduro’s 6 July 2015 National Assembly speech. Maduro is named in the front matter as president, not as the volume’s author; only his declarations and speech are properly attributable to him. ([complete institutional edition and contents](https://fundayacucho.gob.ve/documentos/libro2.pdf))

- **2018; digital edition 2020:** *Nicolás Maduro: la juventud y los estudiantes*. An institutional compilation of presidential speeches, bibliographically credited to the Ministry of University Education rather than unequivocally to Maduro as book-author. The constituent orations are his performances; the compilation and its title are editorial creations. ([edition analysis and publication data](https://mundoescritores.com/en/nicolas-maduro-la-juventud-y-los-estudiantes-la-compilacion-de-discursos-como-libro-institucional/))

### Broadcast manifestations

- **2014–2017:** *En contacto con Maduro* (also reported as *Contacto con Maduro*). A recurring radio-and-television program presented by Maduro and transmitted through Venezuelan state media. It is a serial broadcast manifestation—part speech, interview, governmental announcement, and spectacle—rather than a single fixed text, and no complete critical episode catalogue was located. ([contemporary launch report](https://www.infobae.com/2014/03/11/1549469-en-contacto-maduro-el-nuevo-programa-radio-y-tv-que-lanzo-el-presidente-venezuela/); [IACHR documentation](https://www.oea.org/en/iachr/docs/annual/2014/docs-en/Annual2014-chap4Venezuela.pdf))

### Disputed musical attribution

- **c. 1982–1983:** performances with a Caracas rock group called *Enigma*. Later reports identify Maduro as a bassist, guitarist, or singer in the group, and one account associates it with the recorded song *La carrera del viajero*; these claims are disputed by people presented as members of Enigma, who say Maduro never belonged to the band and that a frequently circulated image shows Carlos Carrillo instead. No reliable discography credits Maduro as composer or recording musician, so neither the song nor the band’s output can safely be entered as his obra. ([early report of the claim](https://www.elespectador.com/mundo/mas-paises/asi-era-nicolas-maduro-durante-su-epoca-de-rockero-article-409710/); [later account noting the disputed identification](https://www.razon.com.mx/virales/2025/08/26/nicolas-maduro-estuvo-en-una-banda-de-heavy-metal-en-los-80-esto-se-sabe/))
