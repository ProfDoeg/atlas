## Works by Vinton Gray Cerf

### Network protocols and architectures

- **1973–present:** *Transmission Control Protocol / Internet Protocol (TCP/IP)*. A jointly designed protocol suite and network architecture, made with Robert E. Kahn and subsequently revised by a large standards community; it resists treatment as Cerf’s solitary “work” because implementation, testing, and governance are constitutive parts of it. [ACM](https://amturing.acm.org/award_winners/cerf_1083211.cfm)
- **1976–1982:** *DARPA Internet Program*. A research-and-development project directed by Cerf, encompassing packet-radio, packet-satellite, internetworking, and security work; its products were collective systems and protocols rather than a single authored object. [Internet Hall of Fame](https://www.internethalloffame.org/vint-cerf/)
- **1983–1986:** *MCI Mail*. A commercial electronic-mail system whose engineering Cerf led; ACM’s SIGCOMM biography says he “created” it, while institutional biographies more cautiously describe him as leading its development. [ACM SIGCOMM](https://sigcomm.sigcomm.hosting2.acm.org/awards/sigcomm-awards/vint-cerf-award-details), [Computer History Museum](https://computerhistory.org/profile/vint-cerf-honorary-council/)
- **1989:** *MCI Mail–Internet interconnection*. An experiment initiated by Cerf that connected a commercial mail service to the federally supported Internet, helping demonstrate commercial interoperation; it is an intervention in network policy and infrastructure rather than a bounded text. [IEEE Spectrum](https://spectrum.ieee.org/vint-cerf)
- **1998–present:** *InterPlanetary Internet*. A continuing collective project begun by Cerf with researchers at NASA’s Jet Propulsion Laboratory to develop interoperable deep-space networking; it remains an evolving intention embodied in several protocols and institutions, not a finished singular work. [JPL](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/universe/archive/un9808.pdf), [IPNSIG](https://www.ipnsig.org/)
- **1998–present:** *Delay/Disruption-Tolerant Networking (DTN)*. A store-and-forward network architecture developed collaboratively for intermittently connected environments and space communication; Cerf helped initiate the program, but neither sole authorship nor a single date can properly be assigned to the resulting system. [NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/communicating-with-missions/delay-disruption-tolerant-networking/)
- **2007:** *Delay-Tolerant Networking Architecture*. A jointly authored architectural protocol text, published as RFC 4838; it formalizes part of the larger DTN project rather than constituting the whole project. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc4838/)

### RFC protocol texts, reports, policies, memorials, and jeux d’esprit

- **August 1969:** *Zero Text Length EOF Message*. RFC 13, a short protocol proposal authored by Cerf. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc13/)
- **September 1969:** *IMP-IMP and HOST-HOST Control Links*. RFC 18, a protocol-design memorandum authored by Cerf. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc18/)
- **October 1969:** *ASCII Format for Network Interchange*. RFC 20, a protocol specifying character encoding for network communication. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc20/)
- **October 1969:** *Network Meeting*. RFC 21, a meeting record and coordination memorandum rather than a protocol. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc21/)
- **October 1969:** *Host-Host Control Message Formats*. RFC 22, a protocol specification. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc22/)
- **July 1970:** *Belated Network Meeting Report*. RFC 63, a report rather than an operative specification. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc63/)
- **April 1971:** *Status Report on Proposed Data Reconfiguration Service*. RFC 138, a project-status text. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc138/)
- **May 1971:** *Invitation to the Second Symposium on Problems in the Optimization of Data Communications Systems*. RFC 157, an invitation: an event-producing text rather than a research work in the usual sense. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc157/)
- **May 1971:** *Data Transfer Protocols*. RFC 163, a protocol-design text. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc163/)
- **May 1971:** *Data Reconfiguration Service: An Implementation Specification*. RFC 166, an instructive implementation protocol. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc166/)
- **June 1971:** *UCLA—Computer Science Graphics Overview*. RFC 174, an institutional technical overview. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc174/)
- **July 1971:** *The Data Reconfiguration Service—Compiler/Interpreter Implementation Notes*. RFC 194, implementation documentation rather than a freestanding theoretical paper. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc194/)
- **March 1972:** *Well Known Socket Numbers*. RFC 322, a protocol registry and operational reference. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc322/)
- **March 1972:** *Formation of Network Measurement Group (NMG)*. RFC 323, a constitutive institutional text that helps create the group it describes. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc323/)
- **August 1972:** *NCP Statistics*. RFC 388, a measurement report. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc388/)
- **January 1973:** *PARRY Encounters the DOCTOR*. RFC 439, a transcript of an interaction between two conversational programs; Cerf transmitted and framed the artifact, but the dialogue itself resists ordinary human authorship. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc439/)
- **January 1973:** *Current Flow-Control Scheme for IMPSYS*. RFC 442, an operational protocol memorandum. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc442/)
- **April 1974:** *Assessment of ARPANET Protocols*. RFC 635, a technical assessment. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc635/)
- **December 1974:** *Specification of Internet Transmission Control Program*. RFC 675, written with Yogen Dalal and Carl Sunshine; a protocol that instructs implementations of the early internetworking design. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc675/)
- **July 1975:** *Comments on the IMP/Host and Host/IMP Protocol Changes*. RFC 696, a protocol-review memorandum. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc696/)
- **September 1980:** *Mail Transition Plan*. RFC 771, written with Jon Postel; an operational plan for migration from NCP to TCP mail service. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc771/)
- **October 1980:** *Comments on NCP/TCP Mail Service Transition Strategy*. RFC 773, a policy and implementation response. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc773/)
- **September 1981:** *Pre-emption*. RFC 794, a protocol-policy memorandum concerning precedence and pre-emption. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc794/)
- **November 1982:** *Packet Satellite Technology Reference Sources*. RFC 829, a bibliography: a finding aid rather than an original protocol. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc829/)
- **December 1985:** *’Twas the Night Before Start-up*. RFC 968, a technical-community poem and commemorative jeu d’esprit, not a protocol despite its place in the RFC series. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc968/)
- **April 1988:** *IAB Recommendations for the Development of Internet Network Management Standards*. RFC 1052, a collectively authored standards-policy recommendation. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc1052/)
- **August 1989:** *Report of the Second Ad Hoc Network Management Review Group*. RFC 1109, a committee report for which Cerf is one of several authors. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc1109/)
- **September 1989:** *Internet Activities Board*. RFC 1120, an institutional description and report. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc1120/)
- **September 1989:** *Act One—The Poems*. RFC 1121, a comic poetic text embedded in the technical-document series rather than an engineering protocol. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc1121/)
- **May 1990:** *Internet Activities Board*. RFC 1160, a revised institutional description. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc1160/)
- **July 1990:** *Thoughts on the National Research and Education Network*. RFC 1167, a policy essay. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc1167/)
- **August 1990:** *Explaining the Role of GOSIP*. RFC 1169, written with Kevin Mills; an explanatory policy text. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc1169/)
- **August 1990:** *IAB Recommended Policy on Distributing Internet Identifier Assignment and IAB Recommended Policy Change to Internet “Connected” Status*. RFC 1174, an operative policy text that does institutional work rather than merely describing it. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc1174/)
- **March 1991:** *Network and Infrastructure User Requirements for Transatlantic Research Collaboration*. RFC 1210, a jointly produced requirements report based on workshops in Brussels and Washington. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc1210/)
- **April 1991:** *Memo from the Consortium for Slow Commotion Research (CSCR)*. RFC 1217, an April Fools’ parody that resists classification as a technical work. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc1217/)
- **October 1991:** *Guidelines for Internet Measurement Activities*. RFC 1262, a protocol-like ethical and operational guide. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc1262/)
- **December 1991:** *Towards the Future Internet Architecture*. RFC 1287, a multi-author architectural report rather than an implementation specification. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc1287/)
- **February 1993:** *A Strategic Plan for Deploying an Internet X.500 Directory Service*. RFC 1430, a multi-author strategic project plan. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc1430/)
- **April 1994:** *A View from the 21st Century*. RFC 1607, an April Fools’ speculative fiction masquerading as a technical retrospective. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc1607/)
- **April 1995:** *An Agreement between the Internet Society and Sun Microsystems, Inc. in the Matter of ONC RPC and XDR Protocols*. RFC 1790, an institutional agreement: an operative legal-administrative text rather than a descriptive paper. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc1790/)
- **October 1998:** *I Remember IANA*. RFC 2468, Cerf’s memorial text for Jon Postel. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc2468/)
- **April 2002:** *The Internet Is for Everyone*. RFC 3271, a manifesto arguing for an open and widely accessible Internet rather than specifying a protocol. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc3271/)
- **April 2007:** *Delay-Tolerant Networking Architecture*. RFC 4838, written with Scott Burleigh and others; an informational architectural specification. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc4838/)
- **July 2016:** *Host Address Availability Recommendations*. RFC 7934, a multi-author best-current-practice protocol text recommending that general-purpose hosts receive multiple globally scoped addresses. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc7934/)

### Unfinished protocol projects

- **Undated; expired:** *draft-cerf-dtn-4838bis*. An unfinished Internet-Draft intended to revise RFC 4838; its expiration does not erase it as a project, but it never became an RFC. [IETF Datatracker author record](https://datatracker.ietf.org/person/vint@google.com)
- **Undated; expired:** *draft-irtf-ipnrg-arch*. An unfinished Internet-Draft on interplanetary-network architecture; it survives as an expired project rather than an adopted standard. [IETF Datatracker author record](https://datatracker.ietf.org/person/vint@google.com)

### Scholarly texts and theses

- **1971:** *Measurement of Recursive Programs*. Written with Gerald Estrin, this is a conference paper on program measurement. [Bibliotheca Alexandrina selected bibliography](https://www.bibalex.org/Attachments/Publications/Files/Cerf.pdf)
- **1972:** *An Experimental Service for Adaptable Data Reconfiguration*. Written with Eric Harslem, John Heafner, Robert Metcalfe, and James White, this is a journal article reporting an experimental network service. [DBLP](https://dblp.org/rec/journals/tcom/CerfHHMW72)
- **1972:** *Multiprocessors, Semaphores, and a Graph Model of Computation*. Cerf’s UCLA doctoral dissertation, a thesis rather than a book written for the trade. [UCLA Library record](https://search.library.ucla.edu/discovery/search?query=any,contains,Multiprocessors%20Semaphores%20and%20a%20Graph%20Model%20of%20Computation)
- **1973:** *Host and Process Level Protocols for Internetwork Communication*. Written with Robert Kahn as INWG Note 39, this is an early internetworking protocol proposal whose circulation preceded the definitive journal article. [ICANN historical report](https://www.icann.org/en/system/files/files/creation-administration-unique-identifiers-1967-2017-18nov20-en.pdf)
- **1974:** *A Protocol for Packet Network Intercommunication*. Written with Robert E. Kahn, this is the foundational scholarly article setting out the internetworking architecture; its joint authorship and subsequent collective realization are essential to its status. [DBLP](https://dblp.org/rec/journals/tcom/CerfK74), [IEEE](https://doi.org/10.1109/TCOM.1974.1092259)
- **1978:** *Issues in Packet-Network Interconnection*. Written with Peter Kirstein, this is a survey and architectural paper on connecting heterogeneous packet networks. [ICANN historical report](https://www.icann.org/en/system/files/files/creation-administration-unique-identifiers-1967-2017-18nov20-en.pdf)
- **1983:** *The DoD Internet Architecture Model*. Written with Ed Cain, this is an explanatory architectural article. [Bibliotheca Alexandrina selected bibliography](https://www.bibalex.org/Attachments/Publications/Files/Cerf.pdf)
- **1983:** *Military Requirements for Packet-Switched Networks and Their Implications for Protocol Standardization*. Written with Robert E. Lyons, this is a requirements and standards-analysis article. [Bibliotheca Alexandrina selected bibliography](https://www.bibalex.org/Attachments/Publications/Files/Cerf.pdf)
- **1989:** *The Internet Activities Board*. An institutional-history and governance article distinct from the similarly titled RFCs. [Bibliotheca Alexandrina selected bibliography](https://www.bibalex.org/Attachments/Publications/Files/Cerf.pdf)
- **1991:** *Computer Networks: Past and Future*. Written with Alfred C. Weaver, this is a short historical and prospective article. [Bibliotheca Alexandrina selected bibliography](https://www.bibalex.org/Attachments/Publications/Files/Cerf.pdf)
- **1991:** *Networks*. A synthetic essay for *Scientific American*, not a protocol specification. [DBLP author bibliography](https://dblp.org/pid/c/VintonGCerf.html)
- **1997:** *A Brief History of the Internet*. Written with Barry Leiner, David Clark, Robert Kahn, Leonard Kleinrock, Daniel Lynch, Jon Postel, Larry Roberts, and Stephen Wolff, this is a collectively authored historical account. [Internet Society](https://www.internetsociety.org/internet/history-internet/brief-history-internet/)
- **2001:** *Beyond the Post-PC Internet*. A prospective essay on networked computing. [Bibliotheca Alexandrina selected bibliography](https://www.bibalex.org/Attachments/Publications/Files/Cerf.pdf)
- **2001:** *The Interplanetary Internet: A Communications Infrastructure for Mars Exploration*. A multi-author architectural paper describing proposed deep-space communication infrastructure. [NASA Technical Reports Server](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20210001823)
- **2003:** *Requirements for the Internet*. A short requirements-oriented article. [Bibliotheca Alexandrina selected bibliography](https://www.bibalex.org/Attachments/Publications/Files/Cerf.pdf)
- **2004:** *On the Evolution of Internet Technologies*. A retrospective scholarly article. [DBLP](https://dblp.org/rec/journals/pieee/Cerf04)
- **2004:** *Taking Internet’s Temperature: Prescriptions for the 21st Century*. A conference keynote text or proceedings paper; its “prescriptions” make it partly manifesto-like rather than merely descriptive. [Bibliotheca Alexandrina selected bibliography](https://www.bibalex.org/Attachments/Publications/Files/Cerf.pdf)
- **2005:** *A Protocol for Packet Network Intercommunication*. A republication of the 1974 paper in *ACM SIGCOMM Computer Communication Review*, not a separately authored work; it is included only to expose the edition/work distinction. [DBLP](https://dblp.org/rec/journals/ccr/CerfK05)
- **2009:** *The Day the Internet Age Began*. A historical commentary in *Nature*. [Nature](https://doi.org/10.1038/4611202a)
- **2015:** *Print Them Out*. An essay on preserving digital materials, later collected in conference proceedings; the collected appearance is a manifestation, not a second work. [Google Books contents record](https://books.google.com/books?id=Lgy3DAAAQBAJ)

### Patented inventions

- **2000–2002:** *Internet Radio Communication System*. A jointly invented wireless packet-network system by Cerf and Scott Huddle, filed in 2000 and issued as US 6,418,138 in 2002; the patent document and the claimed invention are related but not identical objects. [Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US6418138)
- **2003:** *System for Distributed Task Execution*. A patented distributed-computing invention attributed jointly to Cerf and other inventors; the patent is a legal-technical protocol that both describes and claims an invention. [Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US6574628)

### Institutions as made things

- **1992:** *Internet Society*. A professional institution co-founded by Cerf; it is an organizational creation rather than an authored text, and its subsequent work cannot be treated as his individual oeuvre. [Computer History Museum](https://computerhistory.org/profile/vint-cerf-honorary-council/)
- **1998:** *InterPlanetary Networking Special Interest Group*. A research community founded by Cerf and collaborators in academia and at NASA/JPL, later made an Internet Society chapter. [IPNSIG](https://www.ipnsig.org/)
