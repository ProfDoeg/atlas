## Works by Gilles Louis Rene Deleuze

### Books and book-length philosophical obras

- **1953:** *Empirisme et subjectivité: Essai sur la nature humaine selon Hume*. Deleuze’s first acknowledged monograph, a philosophical study of Hume. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1962:** *Nietzsche et la philosophie*. A monograph that reconstructs Nietzsche’s philosophy around force, value, sense, and critique. ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/deleuze/))
- **1963:** *La philosophie critique de Kant*. A compact philosophical monograph on the relations among Kant’s faculties. ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/deleuze/))
- **1964–1976:** *Proust et les signes* (originally *Marcel Proust et les signes*). A book repeatedly remade rather than merely reprinted: the 1970 edition added “La machine littéraire,” and the 1976 edition added a conclusion, producing the work’s fullest authorial form. ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/deleuze/), [Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1966:** *Le Bergsonisme*. A monograph presenting Bergson through intuition, duration, memory, and multiplicity. ([Protevi bibliography](https://www.protevi.com/DG/PDF/Bibliography.pdf))
- **1967:** *Présentation de Sacher-Masoch: Le froid et le cruel*. A hybrid book-object pairing Deleuze’s study “Le froid et le cruel” with Sacher-Masoch’s *La Vénus à la fourrure*; the whole volume is therefore not solely Deleuze’s obra. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1968:** *Différence et répétition*. Deleuze’s principal doctoral thesis and a systematic philosophical obra in its own right. ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/deleuze/))
- **1968:** *Spinoza et le problème de l’expression*. His complementary doctoral thesis, a monograph on expression in Spinoza. Some bibliographies date it 1968 and some publisher listings 1969, reflecting publication rather than composition rather than a second work. ([Protevi bibliography](https://www.protevi.com/DG/PDF/Bibliography.pdf), [Éditions de Minuit](https://leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Gilles_Deleuze-1381-1-1-0-1.html))
- **1969:** *Logique du sens*. A philosophical-literary construction of thirty-four “series” with appendices; it incorporates revised forms of previously published texts, not independent new works at every reappearance. ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/deleuze/))
- **1970–1981:** *Spinoza* / *Spinoza: Philosophie pratique*. The 1970 primer and anthology was substantially expanded and reconceived in 1981 with three additional chapters; the later title is a revised obra, not simply another edition. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1977:** *Dialogues*, with Claire Parnet. A co-created book of philosophical conversations whose alternating or exchanged voices make individual sentence-level authorship unstable; the 1996 edition added “L’Actuel et le virtuel.” ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/deleuze/), [Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1978:** *Superpositions*, with Carmelo Bene. A theatre-philosophy diptych containing Bene’s *Richard III* and Deleuze’s “Un manifeste de moins”; the volume as a whole is collaborative, while Deleuze’s contribution is a manifesto-like intervention in theatrical practice. ([Éditions de Minuit](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Deleuze_Gilles-1381-1-1-0-1.html))
- **1981:** *Francis Bacon: Logique de la sensation*. A two-volume visual-philosophical assemblage: Deleuze’s text formed one volume and reproductions of Bacon’s paintings the other. The augmented 1984 manifestation added images but did not alter Deleuze’s text. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1983:** *Cinéma 1: L’Image-mouvement*. The first half of a two-book philosophical taxonomy of cinematic images and signs. ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/deleuze/))
- **1985:** *Cinéma 2: L’Image-temps*. The second cinema book; it is a distinct obra rather than a numbered reprint of the first. ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/deleuze/))
- **1986:** *Foucault*. A philosophical monograph on knowledge, power, subjectivation, and the fold in Michel Foucault’s work. ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/deleuze/))
- **1988:** *Périclès et Verdi: La philosophie de François Châtelet*. A short philosophical portrait and memorial book; its scale and occasional origin do not make it merely a preface. ([Éditions de Minuit](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Deleuze_Gilles-1381-1-1-0-1.html))
- **1988:** *Le Pli: Leibniz et le Baroque*. A monograph joining Leibnizian philosophy, mathematics, architecture, and Baroque art through the concept of the fold. ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/deleuze/))
- **1990:** *Pourparlers, 1972–1990*. An author-arranged book of interviews and short interventions; the interviews pre-existed individually, but their selection and organization constitute another obra. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1993:** *Critique et clinique*. An author-composed collection of literary and philosophical essays, some revised from earlier publications. It is both a book and a new arrangement of already manifested texts. ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/deleuze/))
- **1997:** *L’Oiseau philosophie*, illustrated by Jacqueline Duhême. A posthumously published illustrated children’s book assembled from Deleuze’s words and Duhême’s images; it is a collaborative manifestation, and Deleuze did not authorize the finished publication after seeing it. ([Éditions de Minuit bibliography](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Deleuze_Gilles-1381-1-1-0-1.html))

### Collaborative books and conceptual machines with Félix Guattari

- **1972:** *L’Anti-Œdipe: Capitalisme et schizophrénie 1*, with Félix Guattari. A jointly authored philosophical-political machine rather than a separable pairing of essays; assigning particular passages to either collaborator is generally impossible. ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/deleuze/))
- **1975:** *Kafka: Pour une littérature mineure*, with Félix Guattari. A jointly made study that treats Kafka’s writing as a collective assemblage rather than an individual author’s private expression. ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/deleuze/))
- **1976:** *Rhizome*, with Félix Guattari. A separately issued manifesto-like booklet whose form performs its concept; revised, it became the introduction to *Mille plateaux*, so its two manifestations should not be mistaken for two wholly independent works. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1980:** *Mille plateaux: Capitalisme et schizophrénie 2*, with Félix Guattari. A deliberately non-linear Gesamtkunstwerk of philosophy, politics, science, linguistics, music, geography, and diagrams, organized as independently enterable “plateaus.” ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/deleuze/))
- **1991:** *Qu’est-ce que la philosophie?*, with Félix Guattari. Their final joint book, defining philosophy, science, and art as different modes of creation. ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/deleuze/))

### Anthologies and editorial obras

- **1953:** *Instincts et institutions*. An anthology selected and edited by Deleuze, with his introduction; the collected documents are not his writings, but their selection and arrangement are his editorial obra. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1957:** *Mémoire et vie: Textes choisis par Gilles Deleuze*. A constructed selection from Bergson, made and organized by Deleuze but composed of Bergson’s words. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1967:** *Cahiers de Royaumont, Philosophie VI: Nietzsche*. A conference volume directed or edited by Deleuze, also containing his “Conclusions: Sur la volonté de puissance et l’éternel retour”; editorial responsibility is distinct from authorship of the other contributions. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1965–1975:** *Revue de métaphysique et de morale*. Deleuze served on its directing committee. This is institutional editorial labor rather than a singular authored text and resists classification as his personal work. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))

### Manifestos, protocols, essays, prefaces, dialogues, and interventions

- **1946:** *Du Christ à la bourgeoisie*. An early journal article, published before the 1953 boundary Deleuze later imposed on collections of his work; its exclusion from his acknowledged corpus makes its status contested rather than nonexistent. ([Lapoujade’s editorial account](https://altanweeri.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/LIle-deserte.-Textes-et-entretiens-1953-1974-Gilles-Deleuze-Z-Library-1.pdf))
- **1946:** *Description de la femme: Pour une philosophie d’Autrui sexuée*. An early essay likewise excluded by Deleuze’s later prohibition on republishing pre-1953 texts; it resists inclusion in the authorized oeuvre. ([Lapoujade’s editorial account](https://altanweeri.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/LIle-deserte.-Textes-et-entretiens-1953-1974-Gilles-Deleuze-Z-Library-1.pdf))
- **1947:** *Dires et profils*. An early essay omitted from the authorized collected corpus under the same pre-1953 rule. ([Lapoujade’s editorial account](https://altanweeri.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/LIle-deserte.-Textes-et-entretiens-1953-1974-Gilles-Deleuze-Z-Library-1.pdf))
- **1953:** *Introduction à Instincts et institutions*. An editorial essay that explains the protocol governing Deleuze’s anthology. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1953:** *Régis Jolivet—Le problème de la mort chez M. Heidegger et J.-P. Sartre*. A signed specialist book review; slight in scale but bibliographically a distinct made text. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1953:** *K. E. Løgstrup—Kierkegaard und Heideggers Existenzanalyse und ihr Verhältnis zur Verkündigung*. A signed book review. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1953:** *Helmut Kuhn—Encounter with Nothingness / Begegnung mit dem Nichts*. A signed book review. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1953:** *Bertrand Russell—Macht und Persönlichkeit*. A signed book review. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1953:** *Carl Jørgensen—Two Commandments*. A signed book review. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1954:** *Jean Hyppolite, Logique et existence*. A substantial critical review that is also an early statement of Deleuze’s own philosophical project. ([L’Île déserte editorial contents](https://altanweeri.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/LIle-deserte.-Textes-et-entretiens-1953-1974-Gilles-Deleuze-Z-Library-1.pdf))
- **1956:** *La conception de la différence chez Bergson*. A scholarly article and an important preliminary manifestation of the project later developed in *Le Bergsonisme*. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1956:** *Bergson, 1859–1941*. A concise philosophical portrait first published in a collective history of philosophy. ([L’Île déserte editorial contents](https://altanweeri.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/LIle-deserte.-Textes-et-entretiens-1953-1974-Gilles-Deleuze-Z-Library-1.pdf))
- **1959:** *Sens et valeurs*. A contribution to a colloquium on Nietzsche, later associated with the project of *Nietzsche et la philosophie*. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1964:** *Il a été mon maître*. A memorial text on Jean-Paul Sartre’s intellectual importance. ([L’Île déserte editorial contents](https://altanweeri.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/LIle-deserte.-Textes-et-entretiens-1953-1974-Gilles-Deleuze-Z-Library-1.pdf))
- **1965:** *Nietzsche*. An introductory article or reference text distinct from the 1962 monograph. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1966:** *Philosophie de la Série noire*. An essay on hard-boiled detective fiction as a modern production of signs and social forces. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1966:** *Gilbert Simondon—L’Individu et sa genèse physico-biologique*. A substantial review that helped introduce Simondon’s theory of individuation into Deleuze’s work. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1966:** *L’homme, une existence douteuse*. A review-essay on Foucault’s *Les Mots et les choses*. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1966:** *Renverser le platonisme*. An article later revised as an appendix to *Logique du sens*; the initial article and incorporated version are manifestations of one developing text. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1967:** *Conclusions: Sur la volonté de puissance et l’éternel retour*. A conference conclusion published in the Royaumont Nietzsche volume. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1968:** *Différence et répétition: Préface*. A synopsis submitted for Deleuze’s doctoral defense; it is a presentation of the thesis rather than a second thesis. ([L’Île déserte editorial contents](https://altanweeri.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/LIle-deserte.-Textes-et-entretiens-1953-1974-Gilles-Deleuze-Z-Library-1.pdf))
- **1968:** *La méthode de dramatisation*. A conference paper and discussion developing a methodological protocol for philosophical concepts. ([L’Île déserte editorial contents](https://altanweeri.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/LIle-deserte.-Textes-et-entretiens-1953-1974-Gilles-Deleuze-Z-Library-1.pdf))
- **1968:** *Schizologie*. A preface to Louis Wolfson’s *Le Schizo et les langues*, later revised for *Critique et clinique*. ([L’Île déserte editorial contents](https://altanweeri.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/LIle-deserte.-Textes-et-entretiens-1953-1974-Gilles-Deleuze-Z-Library-1.pdf))
- **1969:** *Gilles Deleuze parle de la philosophie*. An interview-manifestation in which spoken answers became a published text; interviewer participation complicates sole authorship. ([L’Île déserte editorial contents](https://altanweeri.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/LIle-deserte.-Textes-et-entretiens-1953-1974-Gilles-Deleuze-Z-Library-1.pdf))
- **1970:** *Faille et feux locaux*. A short philosophical-literary text written for Kostas Axelos’s *Arguments d’une recherche*. ([L’Île déserte editorial contents](https://altanweeri.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/LIle-deserte.-Textes-et-entretiens-1953-1974-Gilles-Deleuze-Z-Library-1.pdf))
- **1970:** *Hume*. A concise reference essay distinct from *Empirisme et subjectivité*. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1972:** *À quoi reconnaît-on le structuralisme?* A book-length reference chapter on structuralism, subsequently issued separately in translation but not thereby a new obra. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1972:** *Trois problèmes de groupe*. A preface to Guattari’s *Psychanalyse et transversalité* that functions as a programmatic intervention. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1972:** *Les Intellectuels et le pouvoir*, with Michel Foucault. A published conversation whose dialogic form makes it a genuinely joint manifestation, not a Deleuze essay disguised as one. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1972:** *Appréciation*. A brief intervention concerning Jean-François Lyotard’s *Discours, figure*. ([L’Île déserte editorial contents](https://altanweeri.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/LIle-deserte.-Textes-et-entretiens-1953-1974-Gilles-Deleuze-Z-Library-1.pdf))
- **1973:** *Pensée nomade*. A Nietzsche conference paper that acts as a manifesto for nomadic thought. ([L’Île déserte editorial contents](https://altanweeri.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/LIle-deserte.-Textes-et-entretiens-1953-1974-Gilles-Deleuze-Z-Library-1.pdf))
- **1973:** *Capitalisme et schizophrénie*, with Félix Guattari. A published discussion of *L’Anti-Œdipe*; it is a conversational manifestation related to, but not identical with, the book. ([L’Île déserte editorial contents](https://altanweeri.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/LIle-deserte.-Textes-et-entretiens-1953-1974-Gilles-Deleuze-Z-Library-1.pdf))
- **1973:** *Sur le capitalisme et le désir*, with Félix Guattari. An interview whose question-and-answer construction distributes authorship among speakers and editors. ([L’Île déserte editorial contents](https://altanweeri.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/LIle-deserte.-Textes-et-entretiens-1953-1974-Gilles-Deleuze-Z-Library-1.pdf))
- **1974:** *Cinq propositions sur la psychanalyse*. A conference intervention functioning as a compact anti-psychoanalytic manifesto. ([L’Île déserte editorial contents](https://altanweeri.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/LIle-deserte.-Textes-et-entretiens-1953-1974-Gilles-Deleuze-Z-Library-1.pdf))
- **1974:** *L’Anti-Œdipe: une introduction à la vie non fasciste*. A preface written for the American edition of *Anti-Oedipus* and often associated with Foucault; the famous English preface is Foucault’s, whereas Deleuze’s own related prefatory and interview texts must not be conflated with it. The attribution and titling of circulating versions require edition-level checking. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1976:** *Avenir de linguistique*. A preface to Henri Gobard’s *L’Aliénation linguistique*, simultaneously manifested under a longer newspaper title. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1976:** *Trois questions sur Six fois deux*. An essay-interview on Godard and Miéville’s television work, later incorporated into *Pourparlers*. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1976:** *Gilles Deleuze fasciné par “Le Misogyne”*. A review of Alain Roger’s novel. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1977:** *Nous croyons au caractère politique de la folie*, with Félix Guattari. A political interview in which analysis and intervention are inseparable. ([Deux régimes de fous](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Deleuze_Gilles-1381-1-1-0-1.html))
- **1977:** *Le pire moyen de faire l’Europe*. A political intervention rather than a descriptive philosophical essay. ([Deux régimes de fous](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Deleuze_Gilles-1381-1-1-0-1.html))
- **1978:** *Les gêneurs*. A political intervention connected with prisons and judicial power. ([Deux régimes de fous](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Deleuze_Gilles-1381-1-1-0-1.html))
- **1978:** *Spinoza et nous*. An essay first separately published and then made a chapter of the expanded *Spinoza: Philosophie pratique*. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1978:** *Deux questions sur la drogue*. A discussion/interview about drugs, desire, and causal explanation. ([Deux régimes de fous](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Deleuze_Gilles-1381-1-1-0-1.html))
- **1979:** *Un manifeste de moins*. An essay on Carmelo Bene whose title and performative critical program justify calling it a manifesto. ([Éditions de Minuit](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Deleuze_Gilles-1381-1-1-0-1.html))
- **1979:** *Lettre ouverte aux juges de Negri*. A public political intervention intended to act upon Antonio Negri’s judicial situation. ([Deux régimes de fous](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Deleuze_Gilles-1381-1-1-0-1.html))
- **1980:** *Mille plateaux ne font pas une montagne, ils ouvrent mille chemins philosophiques*. An interview with Deleuze and Guattari concerning their new book, later collected in *Pourparlers*. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1981:** *Peindre le cri*. An advance extract from *Francis Bacon: Logique de la sensation*, hence a manifestation of that larger obra rather than an independently composed essay. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1982:** *La peinture enflamme l’écriture*. An interview concerning painting and *Francis Bacon*. ([Deux régimes de fous](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Deleuze_Gilles-1381-1-1-0-1.html))
- **1983:** *Cinéma 1, première*. An interview around *L’Image-mouvement*, later incorporated into *Pourparlers*. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1984:** *Grandeur de Yasser Arafat*. A political tribute and intervention whose rhetoric is closer to a manifesto than neutral commentary. ([Deux régimes de fous](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Deleuze_Gilles-1381-1-1-0-1.html))
- **1984:** *Il était une étoile de groupe*. A text on François Châtelet, later related to Deleuze’s memorial book on him. ([Deux régimes de fous](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Deleuze_Gilles-1381-1-1-0-1.html))
- **1985:** *Le philosophe et le cinéma* / *Sur L’Image-temps*. An interview whose revised collected manifestation bears a different title in *Pourparlers*. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1986:** *Boulez, Proust et les temps: “Occuper sans compter”*. A short music-philosophy essay. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1986:** *Optimisme, pessimisme et voyage: Lettre à Serge Daney*. A letter-preface later incorporated into *Pourparlers*. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1986:** *Le plus grand film irlandais*. A short text on Beckett’s *Film*, later revised for *Critique et clinique*. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1986:** *Le cerveau, c’est l’écran*. A multi-interviewer conversation on cinema; it is an oral-dialogic object stabilized as print. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1986:** *The Intellectual and Politics: Foucault and the Prison*. An interview concerning Foucault, prisons, and political intellectual work. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1986:** *Sur le régime cristallin* / *Doutes sur l’imaginaire*. An essay first published under the former title and revised for *Pourparlers* under the latter. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1986:** *Fendre les choses, fendre les mots*. An interview on Foucault, continued in a separately printed installment and later unified in *Pourparlers*. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1986:** *Michel Foucault dans la troisième dimension*. The second newspaper installment of *Fendre les choses, fendre les mots*, not wholly independent in composition. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1986:** *La vie comme une œuvre d’art*. An interview on Foucault’s late work, expanded for *Pourparlers*. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1987:** *Qu’est-ce que l’acte de création?* A filmed lecture on philosophy, cinema, information, and resistance; its later transcripts are incomplete textual manifestations of an audiovisual event. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1988:** *Les intercesseurs*. An interview on creation and the need for mediating people, things, and disciplines, later collected in *Pourparlers*. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1988:** *Le devenir révolutionnaire et les créations politiques*, with Toni Negri. A political-philosophical interview later collected in *Pourparlers*. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1989:** *Lettre à Réda Bensmaïa*. A published letter that became part of Deleuze’s acknowledged corpus. ([Deux régimes de fous](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Deleuze_Gilles-1381-1-1-0-1.html))
- **1989:** *Préface pour l’édition américaine de Dialogues*. A new prefatory essay rather than a translation artifact. ([Deux régimes de fous](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Deleuze_Gilles-1381-1-1-0-1.html))
- **1989:** *Lettre à Uno: Comment nous avons travaillé à deux*. A letter explaining Deleuze and Guattari’s collaborative practice; it resists the fiction of individually attributable coauthored passages. ([Deux régimes de fous](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Deleuze_Gilles-1381-1-1-0-1.html))
- **1990:** *Contrôle et devenir*. An interview with Toni Negri, later collected in *Pourparlers*. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1990:** *Post-scriptum sur les sociétés de contrôle*. A compact diagnostic text whose programmatic force has made it function as a manifesto. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1990:** *Les conditions de la question: Qu’est-ce que la philosophie?* A preliminary manifestation, subsequently revised into the book written with Guattari. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1990:** *Lettre-préface à Sahara*. A brief preface-letter to Mireille Buydens’s study of Deleuze’s aesthetics. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1990:** *Adresse au gouvernement français*, with Pierre Bourdieu, Jérôme Lindon, and Pierre Vidal-Naquet. A collective political address concerning the Gulf crisis; as a public act it is closer to manifesto than descriptive essay. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1990:** *Avoir une idée en cinéma: À propos du cinéma des Straub-Huillet*. A partial transcription from *Qu’est-ce que l’acte de création?*, not the complete audiovisual lecture. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1991:** *A Return to Bergson*. A new afterword made for the English-language *Bergsonism*. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1991:** *Preface to the English-language Edition of Empiricism and Subjectivity*. A new prefatory text, although first manifested in translation. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1991:** *Préface à Les Temps capitaux*. A foreword to Éric Alliez’s book. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1991:** *Una nuova stilistica*. A preface to Giorgio Passerone’s *La linea astratta*, first manifested in Italian translation. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1991:** *La guerre immonde*, with René Schérer. A public intervention against the Gulf War. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1992:** *L’épuisé*. An extended philosophical essay accompanying French television editions of Beckett’s works; it crosses literary criticism, theatre, television, and performance theory. ([Deux régimes de fous](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Deleuze_Gilles-1381-1-1-0-1.html))
- **1992:** *Ce que les enfants disent*. An essay later included in *Critique et clinique*. ([Deux régimes de fous](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Deleuze_Gilles-1381-1-1-0-1.html))
- **1993:** *Pour Félix*. A memorial text for Félix Guattari. ([Deux régimes de fous](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Deleuze_Gilles-1381-1-1-0-1.html))
- **1993:** *Préface à l’édition américaine de Différence et répétition*. A new retrospective preface, first manifested in English translation. ([Deux régimes de fous](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Deleuze_Gilles-1381-1-1-0-1.html))
- **1993:** *Préface à l’édition américaine de Logique du sens*. A new prefatory text, distinct from the 1969 book. ([Deux régimes de fous](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Deleuze_Gilles-1381-1-1-0-1.html))
- **1994:** *Désir et plaisir*. A letter-text addressed to Michel Foucault in 1977 and published much later; its date may therefore mean composition or manifestation, and both must be retained. ([Deux régimes de fous](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Deleuze_Gilles-1381-1-1-0-1.html))
- **1995:** *L’immanence: une vie…* Deleuze’s final published essay, a short philosophical text whose punctuation is part of its title and whose status approaches a last manifesto of immanence. ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/deleuze/))
- **1995:** *L’Actuel et le virtuel*. A late text subsequently added to *Dialogues*; its first publication and later incorporation are two manifestations of the same essay. ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/deleuze/))

### Lectures and seminar-series: oral obras

- **1955–1956:** *Qu’est-ce que fonder?* A lycée course surviving through Pierre Lefebvre’s extensive student notes rather than an authorial manuscript or recording; calling the extant text “by Deleuze” therefore requires qualification. ([Deleuze Seminars](https://deleuze.cla.purdue.edu/data-rescue/))
- **1959–1960:** *Rousseau et Bergson*. A Sorbonne course surviving through documentation and summaries; Deleuze’s oral event and later textual reconstruction are not identical objects. ([Deleuze Seminars](https://deleuze.cla.purdue.edu/seminars/))
- **1969:** *Multiplicités*. A short seminar-series surviving in archival transcription. ([Deleuze Seminars](https://deleuze.cla.purdue.edu/seminars/))
- **1971–1972:** *Anti-Œdipe I: Logique des flux*. A nine-session seminar project feeding into *L’Anti-Œdipe* but not reducible to the finished book. ([Deleuze Seminars full index](https://deleuze.cla.purdue.edu/full-index/))
- **1972–1973:** *Anti-Œdipe II*. A five-session continuation whose preservation is incomplete. ([Deleuze Seminars full index](https://deleuze.cla.purdue.edu/full-index/))
- **1973–1974:** *Anti-Œdipe III*. A three-session continuation surviving in recordings or transcripts of uneven completeness. ([Deleuze Seminars full index](https://deleuze.cla.purdue.edu/full-index/))
- **1975–1976:** *Mille plateaux I*. A seminar-series connected with the book project and partly preserved on video. ([Deleuze Seminars](https://deleuze.cla.purdue.edu/seminars/))
- **1976–1977:** *Mille plateaux II: Deux formes de segmentarité*. An oral research series rather than a draft edition of the later book. ([Deleuze Seminars](https://deleuze.cla.purdue.edu/seminars/))
- **1977–1978:** *Mille plateaux III: Guerre et État*. A seminar-series contributing to the conceptual project later manifested in *Mille plateaux*. ([Deleuze Seminars](https://deleuze.cla.purdue.edu/seminars/))
- **March–April 1978:** *Kant: Synthèse et temps*. A short lecture-series on Kant. ([Deleuze Seminars](https://deleuze.cla.purdue.edu/seminars/))
- **1978–1979:** *Mille plateaux IV: Appareil d’État et machines de guerre I*. A seminar-series whose surviving record is fragmentary. ([Deleuze Seminars](https://deleuze.cla.purdue.edu/seminars/))
- **1979–1980:** *Mille plateaux V: Appareil d’État et machines de guerre II*. An oral course preserved within the BnF recordings and posthumously edited in part as *Sur l’appareil d’État et la machine de guerre*. ([Deleuze Seminars](https://deleuze.cla.purdue.edu/seminars/), [BnF fonds description](https://ccfr.bnf.fr/portailccfr/ark%3A/16871/006FRFONDS-751131014-739))
- **April–May 1980:** *Leibniz: Philosophie et création de concepts*. A seminar-series distinct from the later 1986–1987 Leibniz course. ([Deleuze Seminars](https://deleuze.cla.purdue.edu/seminars/))
- **May–June 1980:** *Anti-Œdipe et autres réflexions*. A short seminar sequence posthumously manifested in part as *Sur les lignes de vie*. ([Deleuze Seminars](https://deleuze.cla.purdue.edu/seminars/), [Éditions de Minuit](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Deleuze_Gilles-1381-1-1-0-1.html))
- **November 1980–March 1981:** *Spinoza: Les vitesses de la pensée*. A fifteen-lecture seminar; several early web transcripts were missing or hybrid reconstructions, while the recordings and critical edition supply a different, more complete manifestation. ([Deleuze Seminars](https://deleuze.cla.purdue.edu/seminar/spinoza-velocities-thought/))
- **March–June 1981:** *La peinture et la question des concepts*. An oral course engaging painting as a generator of philosophical concepts; it was posthumously edited as *Sur la peinture*. ([Deleuze Seminars](https://deleuze.cla.purdue.edu/seminar/painting-and-question-concepts/))
- **1981–1982:** *Cinéma: L’image-mouvement*. A year-long seminar-series and research process preceding the first cinema book. ([Deleuze Seminars](https://deleuze.cla.purdue.edu/seminars/))
- **1982–1983:** *Cinéma: Classification des signes et du temps*. A second year-long cinema seminar. ([Deleuze Seminars](https://deleuze.cla.purdue.edu/seminars/))
- **1983–1984:** *Cinéma, vérité et temps: Le faussaire*. A third cinema seminar organized around truth, falsification, and time. ([Deleuze Seminars](https://deleuze.cla.purdue.edu/seminars/))
- **1984–1985:** *Cinéma et pensée*. The fourth cinema seminar-series, related to but not identical with *L’Image-temps*. ([Deleuze Seminars](https://deleuze.cla.purdue.edu/seminars/))
- **1985–1986:** *Foucault*. A three-part oral project on knowledge, power, and subjectivation, preserved in recordings and thirty-four cassettes cited by Murphy. ([Deleuze Seminars](https://deleuze.cla.purdue.edu/seminars/), [Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1986–1987:** *Leibniz et le Baroque*. A seminar-series preceding *Le Pli*. ([Deleuze Seminars](https://deleuze.cla.purdue.edu/seminars/))
- **1979–1987:** *Paris-VIII recorded-course corpus*. Approximately 400–413 hours of student-made recordings preserved as 177 archival units with Deleuze’s consent. This is not one authored text: it is an archival manifestation of multiple oral works, and its recording, cataloguing, and transcription involve other makers. ([BnF fonds description](https://ccfr.bnf.fr/portailccfr/ark%3A/16871/006FRFONDS-751131014-739), [BnF centenary program](https://www.bnf.fr/fr/deleuze-sur-un-plateau-manifestations))

### Audiovisual and phonographic manifestations

- **1973–1974:** *Ouais Marchais, mieux qu’en 68 (ex: Le voyageur)*. Deleuze reads Nietzsche’s “Le voyageur” on a single by Schizo/Richard Pinhas, later reissued on Heldon’s *Electronique Guerilla*. His contribution is a vocal performance of Nietzsche’s text, not authorship of the words or music. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1977–1978:** *Avez-vous lu Baruch? ou le portrait présumé de Spinoza*. A twelve-minute-fifty-second France Culture radio presentation, recorded in December 1977 and first broadcast on 4 March 1978. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1978:** *Freud et la psychanalyse*. A five-minute France Culture radio presentation recorded on 7 April and broadcast the following day. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1981:** *L’Éthique 1* and *L’Éthique 3*. Deleuze’s electronically distorted voice appears on Richard Pinhas’s album *L’Éthique*; Murphy describes the material only as possibly a reading of or commentary on Spinoza, so its verbal content and authorship must remain uncertain. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1982/1994:** *Livre 5: L’Éthique* and *1992: Iceland: The Fall*. Distorted Deleuze vocals appear on Richard Pinhas’s later-released *Rhizosphere/Live at Bobino*; the recordings’ exact dates and textual sources are not securely identified. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **17 March 1987:** *Qu’est-ce que l’acte de création?* A fifty-minute color video lecture produced for FEMIS and television. It is simultaneously a lecture, broadcast object, and philosophical performance, while later printed excerpts are partial manifestations. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1988–1989; broadcast 1995:** *L’Abécédaire de Gilles Deleuze*, with Claire Parnet, directed by Pierre-André Boutang. An approximately seven-and-a-half-hour filmed dialogue organized from A to Z; Deleuze made it under the condition that it be shown only after his death, but it was broadcast beginning in January 1995 with his permission while he was still alive. Its questions, filming, editing, and answers make it a collaborative audiovisual obra. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187), [Deleuze Seminars](https://deleuze.cla.purdue.edu/seminars/))
- **Date uncertain; released 1996:** *Statement on “l’herbe”*. A thirty-two-second recording issued posthumously on *In Memoriam Gilles Deleuze*; Murphy reports that it may have been recorded or released earlier, so no exact creation date should be supplied. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1996:** *rhizome: no beginning no end*. Hazan and Shea sampled Deleuze’s voice from *L’Abécédaire* after his death; this track is their creation and only a posthumous reuse of his recorded performance, not a new Deleuze work. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))

### Collective political acts that resist individual authorship

- **1970s–1990s:** *Petitions, questionnaires, communiqués, and collective declarations*. Deleuze signed or helped formulate numerous political texts concerning prisons, Palestine, Italy, terrorism laws, immigration, and war. David Lapoujade expressly excluded most such collective texts from the collected editions, and the available standard bibliography does not establish sentence-level authorship; they therefore resist honest enumeration as individually authored Deleuze works. ([Lapoujade’s editorial account](https://altanweeri.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/LIle-deserte.-Textes-et-entretiens-1953-1974-Gilles-Deleuze-Z-Library-1.pdf))
- **1977:** *Appel contre la répression en Italie*. A collective political address associated with Deleuze, Guattari, Foucault, and others; it acts publicly rather than merely describing events, but individual verbal authorship is not secure. ([Deux régimes de fous](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Deleuze_Gilles-1381-1-1-0-1.html))
- **1984:** *Les Palestiniens*. A political intervention associated with the Palestinian cause; bibliographic manifestations vary between individual article and collective political context. ([Deux régimes de fous](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Deleuze_Gilles-1381-1-1-0-1.html))
- **1990:** *Adresse au gouvernement français*. A jointly signed Gulf-crisis intervention by Deleuze, Bourdieu, Lindon, and Vidal-Naquet; it should not be silently converted into a sole-authored Deleuze essay. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))
- **1991:** *La guerre immonde*. A jointly authored anti-war intervention with René Schérer. ([Murphy bibliography](https://www.webdeleuze.com/textes/187))

### Posthumous constructions from Deleuze’s materials

- **2002:** *L’Île déserte et autres textes: Textes et entretiens 1953–1974*, edited by David Lapoujade. A posthumous scholarly construction gathering nearly all eligible short texts from the period; Deleuze neither made nor projected this book, and Lapoujade explicitly avoided implying otherwise. ([Lapoujade’s editorial account](https://altanweeri.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/LIle-deserte.-Textes-et-entretiens-1953-1974-Gilles-Deleuze-Z-Library-1.pdf))
- **2003:** *Deux régimes de fous et autres textes: Textes et entretiens 1975–1995*, edited by David Lapoujade. A posthumous collected-text volume and indispensable documentary manifestation, but not an author-arranged Deleuze obra. ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/deleuze/))
- **2015:** *Lettres et autres textes*, edited by David Lapoujade. A posthumous archival edition of letters and other materials; the individual documents are Deleuze’s, but the book’s selection, title, and architecture belong to its editor. ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/deleuze/))
- **2023:** *Sur la peinture: Cours mars–juin 1981*, edited by David Lapoujade. A posthumous textual manifestation of the oral course, reconstructed from recordings rather than a book authored and finalized by Deleuze. ([Éditions de Minuit](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Deleuze_Gilles-1381-1-1-0-1.html))
- **2024:** *Sur Spinoza: Cours novembre 1980–mars 1981*, edited by David Lapoujade. A critical posthumous conversion of a fifteen-session oral seminar into a book; its completeness exceeds earlier web transcripts, but it remains an editorial reconstruction. ([Éditions de Minuit](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/livre-Sur_Spinoza-3448-1-1-0-1.html), [Deleuze Seminars](https://deleuze.cla.purdue.edu/seminar/spinoza-velocities-thought/))
- **2025:** *Sur l’appareil d’État et la machine de guerre: Cours novembre 1979–mars 1980*, edited by David Lapoujade. A posthumously constituted book from recorded teaching, not a manuscript Deleuze prepared for publication. ([Éditions de Minuit](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Deleuze_Gilles-1381-1-1-0-1.html))
- **2025:** *Sur les lignes de vie: Cours mai–juin 1980*, edited by David Lapoujade. A posthumous course edition; its book title and finished textual form belong to the editorial manifestation rather than Deleuze’s lifetime bibliography. ([Éditions de Minuit](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Deleuze_Gilles-1381-1-1-0-1.html))

### Unfinished, rejected, or unstable projects

- **Before 1953:** *Early writings*. Deleuze expressly required that pre-1953 publications not be included in the posthumous short-text collections. They exist, but their resistance to the authorized oeuvre is itself part of their status. ([Lapoujade’s editorial account](https://altanweeri.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/LIle-deserte.-Textes-et-entretiens-1953-1974-Gilles-Deleuze-Z-Library-1.pdf))
- **Around 1989:** *Esquisse de bibliographie*. Deleuze prepared or authorized a bibliographical outline used by Lapoujade to delimit the collected short texts. It is a working protocol for an oeuvre, not itself a projected collected book. ([Lapoujade’s editorial account](https://altanweeri.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/LIle-deserte.-Textes-et-entretiens-1953-1974-Gilles-Deleuze-Z-Library-1.pdf))
- **Dates various:** *Letters, lecture notes, recordings, and unpublished documents*. These materials survive unevenly, but existence in an archive does not automatically make each an autonomous work. Lost recordings and student transcriptions especially resist claims of completeness or sole authorship. ([Deleuze Seminars data-rescue project](https://deleuze.cla.purdue.edu/data-rescue/), [BnF fonds description](https://ccfr.bnf.fr/portailccfr/ark%3A/16871/006FRFONDS-751131014-739))
