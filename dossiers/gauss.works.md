## Works by Carl Friedrich Gauss

### Mathematical treatises and memoirs

- **1796–1814; published 1903:** *Mathematisches Tagebuch*. A private scientific diary of 146 terse records of discoveries and projects, many never separately published; it resists classification as a finished work because it is simultaneously notebook, priority record, and compressed research archive. [*Werke*, VIII; publication record](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/6F3175A0EE68A9EE48F5E1F3BC3592E5)

- **1799:** *Demonstratio nova theorematis omnem functionem algebraicam rationalem integram unius variabilis in factores reales primi vel secundi gradus resolvi posse*. Gauss’s doctoral dissertation and first proof of the fundamental theorem of algebra. [*Werke*, III](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/0075304640500FEF9DA26221FCF83EC4)

- **1801:** *Disquisitiones arithmeticae*. A book-length systematic reconstitution of number theory, not merely a collection of results; Gauss completed much of it earlier, but publication was delayed. [*Werke*, I](https://books.google.com/books?id=uOUGAAAAYAAJ)

- **1802:** *Berechnung des jüdischen Osterfestes*. A short computational protocol for determining the date of Passover. [*Werke*, VI, “Verschiedene Aufsätze”](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/werke/verschiedene-aufsatze-uber-astronomie/CB464E19A8768101DB7AB7BBCC373DCC)

- **1808:** *Theorematis arithmetici demonstratio nova*. A new proof of the theorem now called quadratic reciprocity. [*Werke*, II](https://www.cambridge.org/core/product/identifier/CBO9781139058230A004/type/BOOK_PART)

- **1811:** *Summatio quarundam serierum singularium*. A number-theoretical memoir evaluating quadratic exponential sums. [*Werke*, II](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/6AF9F11F7BAA9C4061B2AF5B53827C99)

- **1812:** *Disquisitiones generales circa seriem infinitam*. A foundational memoir on the hypergeometric series, including convergence, transformations, and continued fractions. [*Werke*, III](https://books.google.com/books?id=WFxYAAAAYAAJ)

- **1813:** *Theoria attractionis corporum sphaeroidicorum ellipticorum homogeneorum methodo nova tractata*. A mathematical-physical memoir on the attraction of homogeneous ellipsoids. [*Werke*, V](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-642-49319-5)

- **1815:** *Demonstratio nova altera theorematis omnem functionem algebraicam rationalem integram unius variabilis in factores reales primi vel secundi gradus resolvi posse*. Gauss’s second proof of the fundamental theorem of algebra. [Collected with the four proofs in *Werke*, III](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/0075304640500FEF9DA26221FCF83EC4)

- **1816:** *Theorematis de resolubilitate functionum algebraicarum integrarum in factores reales demonstratio tertia*. His third proof of the fundamental theorem of algebra. [*Werke*, III](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/0075304640500FEF9DA26221FCF83EC4)

- **1817:** *Theorematis fundamentalis in doctrina de residuis quadraticis demonstrationes et ampliationes novae*. Further proofs and extensions of quadratic reciprocity. [*Werke*, II](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/6AF9F11F7BAA9C4061B2AF5B53827C99)

- **1828:** *Beweis eines algebraischen Lehrsatzes*. A short algebraic memoir concerning the reality and interlacing of roots. [*Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik*, 3](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Carl_Friedrich_Gau%C3%9F#Journal_f%C3%BCr_die_reine_und_angewandte_Mathematik)

- **1831:** *Theoria residuorum biquadraticorum, commentatio prima*. The first published memoir of Gauss’s theory of biquadratic residues. [*Werke*, II](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/6AF9F11F7BAA9C4061B2AF5B53827C99)

- **1832:** *Theoria residuorum biquadraticorum, commentatio secunda*. The second memoir on biquadratic reciprocity; it gave “complex integers” an explicit geometrical and arithmetic role. [*Werke*, II](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/6AF9F11F7BAA9C4061B2AF5B53827C99)

- **1849:** *Beiträge zur Theorie der algebraischen Gleichungen*. A memoir containing Gauss’s fourth proof of the fundamental theorem of algebra and further results on polynomial roots. [Göttingen publication record](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Carl_Friedrich_Gau%C3%9F)

- **Undated manuscripts, chiefly 1790s–1830s; published 1863–1917:** *Disquisitiones generales de congruentiis; Analysis residuorum, caput octavum; Disquisitionum circa aequationes puras ulterior evolutio; Démonstration de quelques théorèmes concernant les périodes des classes des formes binaires du second degré; De nexu inter multitudinem classium … earumque determinantem*. These are distinct but unfinished or unpublished number-theoretical projects recovered from the papers; their posthumous editorial titles and boundaries are not always Gauss’s own. [*Werke*, II contents](https://www.cambridge.org/core/product/identifier/CBO9781139058230A004/type/BOOK_PART)

- **Undated manuscripts, chiefly 1790s–1810s; published 1866 and 1900:** *De origine proprietatibusque generalibus numerorum mediorum arithmetico-geometricorum* and the manuscripts on elliptic functions, theta functions, power series, continued fractions, and the “new transcendents.” This is an unfinished research complex rather than a single completed obra; editors divided notebooks and drafts into textual units after Gauss’s death. [*Werke*, III](https://books.google.com/books?id=WFxYAAAAYAAJ) and [VIII](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/6F3175A0EE68A9EE48F5E1F3BC3592E5)

### Probability, computation, and actuarial work

- **1818:** *Theoria combinationis observationum erroribus minimis obnoxiae*. A memoir establishing the theory of combining observations by least squares and connecting it with a probabilistic error law. [*Werke*, IV](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/17D5902D1C148F28AB35E8BFDD615EB5)

- **1823:** *Supplementum theoriae combinationis observationum erroribus minimis obnoxiae*. A continuation and practical elaboration of the theory of least squares. [*Werke*, IV contents](https://www.lehmanns.de/shop/mathematik-informatik/20076216-9781108032261-werke)

- **Date uncertain; published posthumously in 1873:** *Bestimmung der Genauigkeit der Beobachtungen*. An unfinished manuscript on estimating observational precision, preserved as part of the least-squares project. [*Werke*, IV contents](https://www.lehmanns.de/shop/mathematik-informatik/20076216-9781108032261-werke)

- **Date uncertain; published posthumously in 1873:** *Anwendung der Wahrscheinlichkeitsrechnung auf die Bestimmung der Bilanz für Witwenkassen*. An actuarial application of probability to widows’ funds; it is administrative mathematics as much as a conventional treatise. [*Werke*, IV contents](https://www.lehmanns.de/shop/mathematik-informatik/20076216-9781108032261-werke)

- **Date uncertain; published posthumously in 1873:** *Tafeln zur Bestimmung von Leibrenten* and *Einrichtung und Gebrauch dieser Tafeln*. Numerical life-annuity tables accompanied by an operating protocol; the tables, not simply the explanatory prose, are the made object. [*Werke*, IV contents](https://www.lehmanns.de/shop/mathematik-informatik/20076216-9781108032261-werke)

- **Various dates; published posthumously in 1900:** *Numerisches Rechnen*. A collected body of algorithms, tables, worked calculations, and computational notes; editorial assembly makes it impossible to treat the heading as one authorially finished text. [*Werke*, VIII](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/6F3175A0EE68A9EE48F5E1F3BC3592E5)

### Geometry, cartography, and geodesy

- **1822:** *Allgemeine Auflösung der Aufgabe, die Theile einer gegebenen Fläche auf einer andern gegebenen Fläche so abzubilden, dass die Abbildung dem Abgebildeten in den kleinsten Theilen ähnlich wird*. A prize memoir on conformal mapping between surfaces. [*Werke*, IV contents](https://www.lehmanns.de/shop/mathematik-informatik/20076216-9781108032261-werke)

- **1821–1825:** *Hannoversche Landesvermessung*. A state geodetic project directed and substantially designed by Gauss, comprising triangulation, field observations, reductions, network adjustment, coordinates, and maps. It is not one text and not solely his handiwork: surveyors and assistants performed much of the field labour. [*Werke*, IX](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/39ABEEE1DB861241E06847CDF7710B42)

- **1821:** *Heliotrop*. An optical surveying instrument devised to reflect sunlight toward distant triangulation stations; surviving descriptions and working embodiments make this an instrument-project rather than a book. [*Werke*, IX contents](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/werke/contents/C24AD57ED8767B488C7396B6FB8B856B)

- **1827:** *Disquisitiones generales circa superficies curvas*. A memoir on the intrinsic geometry of curved surfaces, including what became known as the *theorema egregium*. [*Werke*, IV](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/17D5902D1C148F28AB35E8BFDD615EB5)

- **1828:** *Bestimmung des Breitenunterschiedes zwischen den Sternwarten von Göttingen und Altona durch Beobachtungen am Ramsdenschen Zenithsector*. A book-length report of an observational geodetic determination. [*Werke*, IX contents](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/werke/contents/C24AD57ED8767B488C7396B6FB8B856B)

- **1820s–1840s; published posthumously in 1903:** *Erdellipsoid und geodätische Linie*. A manuscript complex on the reference ellipsoid and geodesics; it remained a working project rather than a finished publication. [*Werke*, IX](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/39ABEEE1DB861241E06847CDF7710B42)

- **1820s–1840s; published posthumously in 1903:** *Conforme Doppelprojection des Sphäroids auf die Kugel und die Ebene*. A cartographic-projection manuscript associated with the Hanoverian survey. [*Werke*, IX contents](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/werke/contents/C24AD57ED8767B488C7396B6FB8B856B)

- **1820s–1840s; published posthumously in 1903:** *Conforme Übertragung des Sphäroids auf den Kegelmantel*. A project for conformally transferring the terrestrial spheroid to a cone. [*Werke*, IX contents](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/werke/contents/C24AD57ED8767B488C7396B6FB8B856B)

- **1820s–1840s; published posthumously in 1903:** *Conforme Abbildung des Sphäroids in der Ebene (Projectonsmethode der Hannoverschen Landesvermessung)*. The projection method used for the Hanoverian survey, surviving through notes and computations rather than an authorially issued treatise. [*Werke*, IX contents](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/werke/contents/C24AD57ED8767B488C7396B6FB8B856B)

- **1820s–1840s; published posthumously in 1903:** *Trigonometrische Punktbestimmung; Ausgleichung einfacher Figuren; Stationsausgleichungen; Zur Netzausgleichung; Dreieckskranz um Oldenburg; Zur Hannoverschen Triangulation; Höhenmessungen; Messungsfehler*. A family of operational protocols, calculations, and working papers generated by the survey. Their separation into titled “works” is largely the collected edition’s editorial act. [*Werke*, IX contents](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/werke/contents/C24AD57ED8767B488C7396B6FB8B856B)

- **1841:** *Elementare Ableitung eines zuerst von Legendre aufgestellten Lehrsatzes der sphärischen Trigonometrie*. A very short geometrical demonstration rather than a book-length work. [*Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik*, 22](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Carl_Friedrich_Gau%C3%9F#Journal_f%C3%BCr_die_reine_und_angewandte_Mathematik)

- **1843 and 1846:** *Untersuchungen über Gegenstände der höheren Geodäsie*, first and second memoirs. Two linked memoirs on the geometry and computation of large-scale geodetic networks. [*Werke*, IV](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/17D5902D1C148F28AB35E8BFDD615EB5)

- **Various dates; published posthumously in 1900:** *Aufgaben und Lehrsätze der elementaren Geometrie angehörig; Verwendung complexer Grössen für die Geometrie; Theorie der krummen Flächen*. Bodies of geometrical notes and unfinished investigations, including material relevant to non-Euclidean geometry; they resist treatment as completed, authorially titled works. [*Werke*, VIII](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/6F3175A0EE68A9EE48F5E1F3BC3592E5)

### Astronomy: books, calculations, and observing projects

- **1801:** *Berechnung der Bahn der Ceres*. A computational achievement communicated through reports and tables rather than one stable, separately titled book; Gauss’s predicted orbit enabled the recovery of Ceres. [*Werke*, VI](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/2B20B7EDF156C8EC385A8060D3FE4A25)

- **1802–1807:** *Bahnbestimmungen und Ephemeriden der kleinen Planeten*. A sequence of calculations, notices, and numerical tables for Ceres, Pallas, Juno, and Vesta; the obra is a continuing computational practice rather than a single text. [*Werke*, VI, “Beobachtungen und Rechnungen”](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/2B20B7EDF156C8EC385A8060D3FE4A25)

- **1809:** *Theoria motus corporum coelestium in sectionibus conicis solem ambientium*. A book on determining and computing the orbits of celestial bodies from observations. [*Werke*, VII](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/F25283553F59BF112D6687D30A23E40D)

- **1810:** *Disquisitio de elementis ellipticis Palladis*. A memoir determining the elliptic elements of Pallas’s orbit. [*Werke*, VI](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/2B20B7EDF156C8EC385A8060D3FE4A25)

- **1810s–1850s; published posthumously in 1906:** *Zusätze zur Theoria motus corporum coelestium und andere Nachträge zur elliptischen Bewegung*. Corrections, additions, and working continuations of the 1809 book; this is an evolving project rather than a new unified treatise. [*Werke*, VII](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/F25283553F59BF112D6687D30A23E40D)

- **1810s–1840s; published posthumously in 1906:** *Zur parabolischen Bewegung*. Manuscripts and computations concerning parabolic orbits. [*Werke*, VII](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/F25283553F59BF112D6687D30A23E40D)

- **1810s–1840s; published posthumously in 1906:** *Störungen der Ceres*. A body of calculations on the perturbations of Ceres, never fashioned by Gauss into a finished book. [*Werke*, VII](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/F25283553F59BF112D6687D30A23E40D)

- **1802–1817 and later revisions; published posthumously in 1906:** *Störungen der Pallas*. An exceptionally extensive mass of numerical and theoretical work on Pallas; its thousands of calculations make it closer to a long-running research project than to a conventional text. [*Werke*, VII](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/F25283553F59BF112D6687D30A23E40D)

- **Date uncertain; published posthumously in 1906:** *Theorie der Bewegung des Mondes*. An unfinished lunar-theory project reconstructed from Gauss’s papers. [*Werke*, VII](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/F25283553F59BF112D6687D30A23E40D)

- **1807–1855:** *Göttingen Observatory observations and reductions*. A continuing corpus of positional observations, comet and planet observations, eclipse records, time determinations, and numerical reductions. It is institutional and collaborative material, not wholly reducible to discrete authored texts. [*Werke*, VI, “Beobachtungen und Rechnungen”](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/2B20B7EDF156C8EC385A8060D3FE4A25)

- **Various dates:** *Astronomical notices, reports, reviews, and announcements*. The collected edition preserves numerous short pieces, including notices of Gauss’s own calculations and critical reviews of other astronomers; titles and textual limits vary with their original journal settings. [*Werke*, VI contents](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/2B20B7EDF156C8EC385A8060D3FE4A25)

### Mechanics, potential theory, and optics

- **1829:** *Principia generalia theoriae figurae fluidorum in statu aequilibrii*. A memoir on the equilibrium figures and capillary behaviour of fluids. [*Werke*, V](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-642-49319-5)

- **1829:** *Über ein neues allgemeines Grundgesetz der Mechanik*. A compact manifesto-like statement of the principle of least constraint: the text proposes a general law rather than merely describing a particular mechanism. [*Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik*, 4](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Carl_Friedrich_Gau%C3%9F#Journal_f%C3%BCr_die_reine_und_angewandte_Mathematik)

- **1840:** *Allgemeine Lehrsätze in Beziehung auf die im verkehrten Verhältnisse des Quadrats der Entfernung wirkenden Anziehungs- und Abstossungs-Kräfte*. A potential-theory memoir establishing general propositions for inverse-square attraction and repulsion. [Digitized original and *Werke* reference](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Carl_Friedrich_Gau%C3%9F)

- **1841:** *Dioptrische Untersuchungen*. A treatise on first-order optical systems, lenses, focal points, and image formation. [*Werke*, V contents](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-642-49319-5)

### Magnetism, instruments, and communication systems

- **1832:** *Intensitas vis magneticae terrestris ad mensuram absolutam revocata*. A memoir and measurement protocol that expressed terrestrial magnetic intensity in absolute mechanical units. [*Werke*, V; bibliographic record](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Carl_Friedrich_Gau%C3%9F)

- **1832–1833:** *Unifilar magnetometer*. A precision instrument-system for measuring magnetic direction and absolute intensity. Gauss designed its principles and observational method; physical construction involved Göttingen instrument makers, so authorship of the object is collaborative. [*Werke*, V](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-642-49319-5)

- **1833:** *Göttingen electromagnetic telegraph*. An experimental wire communication system made with Wilhelm Weber between the observatory and the physics institute. It is a collaborative apparatus and operating practice, not a Gauss-authored text, and priority claims for “the first telegraph” depend on how telegraph is defined. [Göttingen Academy account of Gauss’s physical work in *Werke*, XI.2](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/werke/contents/A4F9D14E5E0BEA46E09D9805DED802E1)

- **1834–1841:** *Magnetischer Verein*. A coordinated international observing project established with Wilhelm Weber, using standardized instruments, times, and reduction procedures. The network, its protocol, and its accumulated observations constitute the obra; individual measurements were made by many participants. [*Werke*, V](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-642-49319-5)

- **1836–1841:** *Resultate aus den Beobachtungen des magnetischen Vereins*. A six-year serial publication edited and substantially authored with Wilhelm Weber, combining protocols, observations, theory, and reports; attribution of individual unsigned components is not always separable. [*Werke*, V](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-642-49319-5)

- **1837:** *Erdmagnetismus und Magnetometer*. An exposition of geomagnetic measurement and its instrumentation. [*Werke*, V contents](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-642-49319-5)

- **1837:** *Ein neues Hülfsmittel für die magnetischen Beobachtungen*. A short instrument-method paper proposing an aid to magnetic observation. [*Werke*, V contents](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-642-49319-5)

- **1837:** *Über ein neues, zunächst zur unmittelbaren Beobachtung der Veränderungen in der Intensität des horizontalen Theils des Erdmagnetismus bestimmtes Instrument*. A description and protocol for a new magnetic instrument. [*Werke*, V contents](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-642-49319-5)

- **1837:** *Anleitung zur Bestimmung der Schwingungsdauer einer Magnetnadel*. An observational protocol for determining a magnetic needle’s period of oscillation. [*Werke*, V contents](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-642-49319-5)

- **1839:** *Allgemeine Theorie des Erdmagnetismus*. A global mathematical theory of Earth’s magnetic field, published within the collaborative magnetic-observation programme. [*Werke*, V](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-642-49319-5)

- **1840:** *Atlas des Erdmagnetismus nach den Elementen der Theorie entworfen*. A cartographic manifestation of Gauss’s magnetic theory, produced with Wilhelm Weber: a set of global magnetic charts rather than a conventional prose book. [Gauss bibliography and historical discussion](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Gauss.pdf)

### Reviews, notices, correspondence, and institutional texts

- **1799–1854:** *Anzeigen eigener Schriften*. Gauss’s notices and abstracts of his own work form a distinct corpus of self-commentary, often supplying interpretations or results not stated identically in the longer works. [*Werke*, II](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/6AF9F11F7BAA9EE48F5E1F3BC3592E5) and [VI](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/werke/anzeigen-eigner-schriften/C3CDB5BD767EB67FC094B863700FAD18)

- **1799–1854:** *Beurteilungen und Anzeigen nicht eigener Schriften*. A large corpus of signed and attributed reviews of mathematical, astronomical, geodetic, and physical publications. These are acts of scholarly judgment rather than merely descriptions; exact attribution of anonymous review copy sometimes rests on the collected edition’s editors. [*Werke*, VI](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/werke/2B20B7EDF156C8EC385A8060D3FE4A25)

- **1800–1855:** *Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen contributions*. Reviews, notices, and reports written for the Göttingen learned journal, including announcements of Gauss’s own papers. The corpus does not have one authorial title and overlaps the “Anzeigen” sections of the collected works. [Wikisource author bibliography](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Carl_Friedrich_Gau%C3%9F)

- **1790s–1855:** *Scientific correspondence*. Thousands of letters to and from mathematicians, astronomers, geodesists, physicists, administrators, and family members constitute an epistolary research infrastructure. The correspondence resists treatment as a finite authored “work”: surviving sets are incomplete, jointly produced, and were published selectively by later editors. [Survey of published Gauss correspondences](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Carl_Friedrich_Gau%C3%9F#Briefwechsel)

- **1802–1819:** *Briefwechsel zwischen Olbers und Gauss*. A jointly made astronomical correspondence, especially important for minor planets and comets; the surviving and later printed corpus is incomplete and editorially arranged. [Digital edition records](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Carl_Friedrich_Gau%C3%9F#Briefwechsel)

- **1804–1850:** *Briefwechsel zwischen Gauss und Bessel*. A jointly authored scientific correspondence on astronomy, geodesy, instruments, and mathematics; it is an exchange rather than a single-author text. [Digital edition record](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Carl_Friedrich_Gau%C3%9F#Briefwechsel)

- **1807–1850:** *Briefwechsel zwischen C. F. Gauss und H. C. Schumacher*. A very large six-volume published correspondence whose made form is dialogic and whose printed arrangement is posthumous. [Digital edition records](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Carl_Friedrich_Gau%C3%9F#Briefwechsel)

- **1797–1853:** *Briefwechsel zwischen Carl Friedrich Gauss und Wolfgang Bolyai*. A jointly authored correspondence concerning geometry, education, and personal affairs; its preservation and later selection are incomplete. [Digital edition record](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Carl_Friedrich_Gau%C3%9F#Briefwechsel)

- **1820–1854:** *Briefwechsel zwischen Carl Friedrich Gauss und Christian Ludwig Gerling*. A collaborative epistolary record of geodesy, astronomy, teaching, and instruments. [Digital edition record](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Carl_Friedrich_Gau%C3%9F#Briefwechsel)

- **1828–1854:** *Briefe zwischen Alexander von Humboldt und Gauss*. A jointly produced correspondence on terrestrial physics, geomagnetism, scientific organization, and patronage. [Digital edition record](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Carl_Friedrich_Gau%C3%9F#Briefwechsel)

### Posthumously constituted oeuvre

- **1863–1933:** *Carl Friedrich Gauss Werke*, twelve volumes. This is not a work Gauss himself made or authorized as a whole, but the principal scholarly construction of his oeuvre: it combines publications, drafts, notebooks, tables, observations, reviews, and selected correspondence, while later volumes also contain editors’ and historians’ studies rather than Gauss’s own work. [Volume-by-volume record](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Carl_Friedrich_Gau%C3%9F#Werke)

- **Unfinished:** *Projected volume XIII of the Werke*. The editors planned a further volume of biography, bibliography, and indexes, reportedly bringing it close to completion, but it was never issued; it is therefore an editorial project about Gauss’s works, not part of Gauss’s own oeuvre. [Dictionary of Scientific Biography study](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Gauss.pdf)
