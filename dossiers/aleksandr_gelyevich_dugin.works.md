## Works by Aleksandr Gelyevich Dugin

### Monographs, treatises, essay collections, and teaching texts

- **1990:** *Пути абсолюта* (*Paths of the Absolute*). An esoteric-traditionalist treatise; later reprinted inside larger collections, but those editions are not separate works. [INION bibliographical profile](https://pa.inion.ru/documents/1848/)
- **1992:** *Конспирология* (*Conspirology*). A treatise presenting conspiracy theory as a method of political and metaphysical interpretation; the 2005 publication is a revised edition, not another obra. [INION bibliographical profile](https://pa.inion.ru/documents/1848/)
- **1993:** *Гиперборейская теория: опыт ариософского исследования* (*The Hyperborean Theory: An Experiment in Ariosophical Research*). An esoteric historical treatise. The retitled 2008 *Знаки великого норда* is explicitly a republication of this work, not a new one. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **1994:** *Консервативная революция* (*The Conservative Revolution*). A study and anthology-like exposition of German and Russian revolutionary-conservative thought; its mixture of translation, commentary, and original essays makes sole-authored “monograph” an imperfect category. [INION bibliographical profile](https://pa.inion.ru/documents/1848/)
- **1996:** *Тамплиеры пролетариата* (*Templars of the Proletariat*). A collection of essays joining Traditionalism, National Bolshevism, and cultural criticism. [INION bibliographical profile](https://pa.inion.ru/documents/1848/)
- **1996:** *Мистерии Евразии* (*Mysteries of Eurasia*). An esoteric-geopolitical treatise assembled from earlier writing; at least one archive dates an earlier form to 1991, so 1996 is the secure book publication rather than an uncontested date of composition. [Eurasianist Internet Archive](https://eurasianist-archive.com/2019/04/26/alexander-dugin-mysteries-of-eurasia/), [INION bibliographical profile](https://pa.inion.ru/documents/1848/)
- **1996:** *Метафизика благой вести* (*Metaphysics of the Good News*). A theological and Traditionalist interpretation of Christianity. The work reportedly existed in an earlier form before its revised publication in 1996. [Biographical chronology](https://adebiportal.kz/ru/authors/view/424)
- **1997:** *Основы геополитики: геополитическое будущее России* (*Foundations of Geopolitics: The Geopolitical Future of Russia*). A geopolitical textbook and strategic manifesto, prepared with reported assistance from General Nikolai Klokotov; the exact extent of that assistance is not consistently specified, so uncomplicated sole authorship is questionable. [Stanford FSI study](https://fsi.stanford.edu/docs/aleksandr-dugins-foundations-geopolitics), [biographical chronology](https://adebiportal.kz/ru/authors/view/424)
- **1999:** *Абсолютная Родина* (*Absolute Homeland*). A large retrospective collection of Dugin’s Traditionalist, geopolitical, and National-Bolshevik writings; it is simultaneously an obra and an assemblage of previously circulating texts. [INION bibliographical profile](https://pa.inion.ru/documents/1848/)
- **1999:** *Наш путь* (*Our Path*). A programmatic Eurasianist essay collection, republished in 2002 as *Евразийский путь* (*The Eurasian Path*); the latter is treated here as a retitled edition. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2000:** *Мыслить пространством* (*Thinking Spatially*). A geopolitical text named in contemporary academic biographical literature, though omitted from some later author bibliographies; its precise relationship to Dugin’s other geopolitical collections is therefore not fully settled. [Academic biographical survey](https://renessans-edu.uz/files/books/2024-12-10-11-43-58_9ea83ad238b57e947d1e653a51d4b8e4.pdf)
- **2001–2006:** *Русская вещь* (*The Russian Thing*), volumes 1–2. A two-volume philosophical and cultural assemblage rather than a conventional continuous monograph; Dugin’s own site calls it a radical attempt to constitute an autonomous Russian philosophy. [Author’s work page](https://dugin.ru/ru/book/russkaya-veshch-t-1), [bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2002:** *Эволюция парадигмальных оснований науки* (*The Evolution of the Paradigmatic Foundations of Science*). A scholarly monograph derived from Dugin’s academic research; bibliographies also identify it with his dissertation work. [INION bibliographical profile](https://pa.inion.ru/documents/1848/)
- **2002:** *Философия традиционализма* (*The Philosophy of Traditionalism*). A book made from lectures delivered through Dugin’s “New University”; it retains the oral and pedagogical structure of a lecture cycle. [INION bibliographical profile](https://pa.inion.ru/documents/1848/)
- **2002:** *Основы евразийства* (*Foundations of Eurasianism*). A textbook-like collective exposition edited and substantially authored by Dugin; because it incorporates several contributors, describing the whole volume as solely written by him would overstate authorship. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2004:** *Проект «Евразия»* (*Project Eurasia*). A programmatic book whose category is literally a project: it describes an intended geopolitical and political construction rather than merely an existing object. [INION bibliographical profile](https://pa.inion.ru/documents/1848/)
- **2004:** *Евразийская миссия Нурсултана Назарбаева* (*The Eurasian Mission of Nursultan Nazarbayev*). A political interpretation of Nazarbayev’s Eurasian initiatives and a manifesto for their expansion. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2004:** *Философия политики* (*The Philosophy of Politics*). A systematic philosophical teaching text. [INION bibliographical profile](https://pa.inion.ru/documents/1848/)
- **2004:** *Философия войны* (*The Philosophy of War*). An essay collection and political-philosophical treatise rather than a military protocol. [INION bibliographical profile](https://pa.inion.ru/documents/1848/)
- **2005:** *Поп-культура и знаки времени* (*Pop Culture and the Signs of the Times*). A collection of cultural criticism interpreting popular media as political and metaphysical manifestations. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2007:** *Обществоведение для граждан Новой России* (*Social Studies for Citizens of the New Russia*). A commissioned civic textbook; a contemporary biography says it was written at the request of the Russian presidential administration, a provenance requiring caution because the claim comes from a sympathetic profile. [Biographical chronology](https://adebiportal.kz/ru/authors/view/424)
- **2007:** *Геополитика постмодерна* (*The Geopolitics of Postmodernity*). A geopolitical essay collection examining post-Soviet and postmodern power. [INION bibliographical profile](https://pa.inion.ru/documents/1848/)
- **2009:** *Постфилософия* (*Postphilosophy*). A philosophical treatise on three paradigms of thought; expanded or retitled editions appeared in 2020 and 2023 as *Постфилософия: три парадигмы в истории мысли*, not as independent works. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2009:** *Радикальный субъект и его дубль* (*The Radical Subject and Its Double*). A metaphysical and political-philosophical treatise. [INION bibliographical profile](https://pa.inion.ru/documents/1848/)
- **2009:** *Четвёртая политическая теория* (*The Fourth Political Theory*). Both a treatise and a manifesto: it proposes an ideological project intended to supersede liberalism, communism, and fascism rather than merely describing political theory. [INION bibliographical profile](https://pa.inion.ru/documents/1848/)
- **2010:** *Структурная социология* (*Structural Sociology*). A university-level textbook applying structuralist methods to social analysis. [INION bibliographical profile](https://pa.inion.ru/documents/1848/)
- **2010:** *Логос и мифос: глубинное регионоведение* (*Logos and Mythos: Deep Regional Studies*). A textbook proposing “deep regional studies” as a discipline. [INION bibliographical profile](https://pa.inion.ru/documents/1848/)
- **2010:** *Кризис: конец экономической теории* (*Crisis: The End of Economic Theory*). A polemical economic and political treatise. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2010:** *Мартин Хайдеггер: философия другого Начала* (*Martin Heidegger: The Philosophy of Another Beginning*). The first of Dugin’s book-length Heidegger studies. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2010:** *Социология русского общества: Россия между Хаосом и Логосом* (*Sociology of Russian Society: Russia Between Chaos and Logos*). A sociology textbook and civilizational interpretation of Russia. [INION bibliographical profile](https://pa.inion.ru/documents/1848/)
- **2010:** *Социология воображения* (*Sociology of the Imagination*). A teaching text treating imagination as a collective social structure. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2011:** *Мартин Хайдеггер: возможность русской философии* (*Martin Heidegger: The Possibility of Russian Philosophy*). A philosophical monograph connecting Heidegger’s thought to the project of a distinct Russian philosophy. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2011:** *Археомодерн* (*Archaeo-Modernity*). A theoretical work naming the unstable coexistence of archaic and modern structures; versions circulated first as essays, making it a concept-project as well as a book. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2011:** *Геополитика* (*Geopolitics*). A general textbook; later “teaching-aid” editions should not be counted as new works without evidence of substantial reconstruction. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2011:** *Этносоциология* (*Ethnosociology*). A textbook constructing ethnosociology as a distinct sociological field. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2011:** *Социология геополитических процессов* (*Sociology of Geopolitical Processes*). A sociological teaching text on geopolitics. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2012:** *Геополитика России* (*The Geopolitics of Russia*). A textbook and geopolitical argument centred on Russia as a continental power. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2012:** *Теория многополярного мира* (*The Theory of a Multipolar World*). A systematic international-relations treatise and manifesto for multipolarity; the 2015 *Плюриверсум* teaching edition is not counted separately. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2012:** *В поисках тёмного Логоса* (*In Search of the Dark Logos*). A collection of philosophical-theological essays; the 2021 publication is an expanded or republished form, not securely a distinct obra. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2013/2014:** *Международные отношения: парадигмы, теория, социология* (*International Relations: Paradigms, Theory, Sociology*). A textbook. Bibliographies disagree between 2013 and the dated 2014 edition, so both years are retained rather than silently choosing one. [INION bibliographical profile](https://pa.inion.ru/documents/1848/), [bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2014:** *Четвёртый путь* (*The Fourth Way*). A large introduction and extension to the Fourth Political Theory; the 2024 edition bears an expanded subtitle but is not counted as a new work. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2014:** *Мартин Хайдеггер: Последний бог* (*Martin Heidegger: The Last God*). The third book-length component of Dugin’s Heidegger project. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2014:** *Евразийский реванш России* (*Russia’s Eurasian Revenge*). A topical geopolitical essay collection; the 2024 issue is a republication rather than another obra. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2015:** *Украина: моя война. Геополитический дневник* (*Ukraine: My War—A Geopolitical Diary*). A dated political diary and intervention, not a neutral history. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2015:** *Воображение: философия, социология, структуры* (*Imagination: Philosophy, Sociology, Structures*). A broader theoretical treatment of material first organized pedagogically in *Sociology of the Imagination*. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2015:** *Русская война* (*The Russian War*). A political essay collection and wartime manifesto. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2015:** *Русский Логос — русский Хаос* (*Russian Logos—Russian Chaos*). A revised or retitled development of *Sociology of Russian Society*; its independence as an entirely new work is doubtful. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2016:** *Мартин Хайдеггер: метаполитика, эсхатология бытия* (*Martin Heidegger: Metapolitics, Eschatology of Being*). A further monograph in Dugin’s multi-book Heidegger project. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2020:** *Politica Aeterna: политический платонизм и «Чёрное Просвещение»* (*Politica Aeterna: Political Platonism and the “Dark Enlightenment”*). A political-philosophical essay collection joining Platonism to criticism of modernity. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2021:** *The Great Awakening vs. the Great Reset*. An English-language political essay collection and counter-manifesto; it is not merely a translation of one earlier Russian monograph, although portions originated as topical essays. [Arktos bibliographical record](https://arktos.com/product/the-great-awakening-vs-the-great-reset/)
- **2022:** *Интернальные онтологии: сакральная физика и опрокинутый мир* (*Internal Ontologies: Sacred Physics and the Inverted World*). A metaphysical treatise; the 2026 publication is treated as a new edition rather than a second work. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2022:** *Антикейменос: эпистемологические войны; Боги чумы; Великое пробуждение* (*Antikeimenos: Epistemological Wars; Gods of the Plague; The Great Awakening*). A composite volume containing three linked polemical-philosophical texts; calling it one monograph conceals its deliberately assembled form. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2022:** *Четвёртая Русь: контргегемония; Русский концепт* (*The Fourth Rus’: Counter-Hegemony; The Russian Concept*). A composite political-philosophical volume and manifesto. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2022:** *В пространстве Великих снов (путешествия на край утра): Русская вещь-3* (*In the Space of Great Dreams: Journeys to the Edge of Morning—The Russian Thing 3*). A third, belated volume of the *Russian Thing* assemblage, mixing philosophical travel, dream, and cultural writing. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2023:** *Бытие и Империя: онтология и эсхатология Вселенского Царства* (*Being and Empire: Ontology and Eschatology of the Universal Kingdom*). A large metaphysical-political treatise. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2025:** *Революция Дональда Трампа: порядок Великих Держав* (*The Donald Trump Revolution: The Order of Great Powers*). A topical geopolitical interpretation and political manifesto. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2025:** *Десятый Интеллект и физика Воскресения* (*The Tenth Intellect and the Physics of Resurrection*). A metaphysical and religious-philosophical treatise. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2025:** *Феноменология Гегеля: опыт трансверсальных интерпретаций* (*Hegel’s Phenomenology: An Experiment in Transversal Interpretations*). A philosophical monograph made as a sequence of interpretations rather than a conventional commentary. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2026:** *Государство-цивилизация* (*The Civilization-State*). A book-length political and civilizational theory. Its appearance is documented in the current bibliography, but its very recent date means its relationship to earlier essays under the same concept remains insufficiently catalogued. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2026:** *Время I* (*Time I*). The first volume of a projected philosophical investigation of time; as “I” indicates, this is both a published book and an unfinished larger project. [Publisher PDF](https://www.dugin.ru/sites/default/files/2025-12/%D0%94%D1%83%D0%B3%D0%B8%D0%BD%20%D0%90.%D0%93.%20%D0%92%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%BC%D1%8F-I_221225.pdf)

### *Noomakhia*: a many-volume project

- **2014–2020:** *Ноомахия: войны ума* (*Noomakhia: Wars of the Mind*). A civilizational “map of meanings” designed as a single encyclopedic project and Gesamtkunstwerk of philosophy, religion, anthropology, geopolitics, and myth. Dugin’s present site calls it a 24-volume cycle written in 2014–2020, while the published bibliography enumerates 25 volumes; that discrepancy is preserved here. [Author’s project page](https://www.dugin.ru/ru/topic/bolshaya-noomakhiya), [volume bibliography](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Серия_«Ноомахия»)
- **2014:** *Три Логоса: Аполлон, Дионис, Кибела* (*Three Logoi: Apollo, Dionysus, Cybele*). The project’s theoretical foundation and statement of its threefold noological method. [Volume bibliography](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Серия_«Ноомахия»)
- **2014:** *Цивилизации границ: Россия, американская цивилизация, семиты и их цивилизация, арабский Логос, туранский Логос* (*Civilizations of the Borderlands*). A synoptic comparative volume; its compressed coverage overlaps subjects later given independent books. [Volume bibliography](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Серия_«Ноомахия»)
- **2014:** *Логос Европы: средиземноморская цивилизация во времени и пространстве* (*The Logos of Europe*). A civilizational monograph on Mediterranean Europe. [Volume bibliography](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Серия_«Ноомахия»)
- **2014:** *По ту сторону Запада: индоевропейские цивилизации — Иран, Индия* (*Beyond the West: Indo-European Civilizations—Iran and India*). A paired civilizational study. [Volume bibliography](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Серия_«Ноомахия»)
- **2014:** *По ту сторону Запада: Китай, Япония, Африка, Океания* (*Beyond the West: China, Japan, Africa, Oceania*). A synoptic volume whose subjects were subsequently expanded into separate studies. [Volume bibliography](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Серия_«Ноомахия»)
- **2015:** *Германский Логос: человек апофатический* (*The Germanic Logos: Apophatic Man*). A civilizational and philosophical monograph. [Volume bibliography](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Серия_«Ноомахия»)
- **2015:** *Французский Логос: Орфей и Мелюзина* (*The French Logos: Orpheus and Melusine*). A civilizational study organized through mythic figures. [Volume bibliography](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Серия_«Ноомахия»)
- **2015:** *Англия или Британия? Морская миссия и позитивный субъект* (*England or Britain? The Maritime Mission and the Positive Subject*). A geopolitical and noological study. [Volume bibliography](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Серия_«Ноомахия»)
- **2016:** *Латинский Логос: солнце и крест* (*The Latin Logos: Sun and Cross*). A study of Latin and Roman civilizational structures. [Volume bibliography](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Серия_«Ноомахия»)
- **2016:** *Эллинский Логос: долина истины* (*The Hellenic Logos: Valley of Truth*). A noological history of Greek civilization and philosophy. [Volume bibliography](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Серия_«Ноомахия»)
- **2016:** *Византийский Логос: эллинизм и империя* (*The Byzantine Logos: Hellenism and Empire*). A study of Byzantine philosophical and imperial forms. [Volume bibliography](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Серия_«Ноомахия»)
- **2016:** *Иранский Логос: световая война и культура ожидания* (*The Iranian Logos: The War of Light and the Culture of Expectation*). A civilizational study of Iranian metaphysics and eschatology. [Volume bibliography](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Серия_«Ноомахия»)
- **2017:** *Семиты, монотеизм Луны и гештальт Ва’ала* (*Semites, Lunar Monotheism, and the Gestalt of Baal*). A comparative noological study; its broad civilizational generalizations make “scholarly monograph” a contested description rather than a neutral classification. [Volume bibliography](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Серия_«Ноомахия»)
- **2017:** *Геософия: горизонты и цивилизации* (*Geosophy: Horizons and Civilizations*). A second methodological foundation for the cycle, defining the spatial projection of its three Logoi. [Paideuma work page](https://pavel.paideuma.tv/book/noomahiya-geosofiya-gorizonty-i-civilizacii)
- **2017:** *Цивилизация Нового Света (Америка)* (*The Civilization of the New World—America*). A book-length expansion of material compressed into the earlier border-civilizations volume. [Volume bibliography](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Серия_«Ноомахия»)
- **2017:** *Логос Турана: индоевропейская идеология вертикали* (*The Logos of Turan: The Indo-European Ideology of the Vertical*). A civilizational study of the Eurasian steppe. [Volume bibliography](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Серия_«Ноомахия»)
- **2017:** *Жёлтый Дракон: цивилизации Дальнего Востока* (*The Yellow Dragon: Civilizations of the Far East*). A comparative study of East Asian civilizations. [Volume bibliography](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Серия_«Ноомахия»)
- **2018:** *Логос Африки: люди чёрного солнца* (*The Logos of Africa: People of the Black Sun*). A noological survey of sub-Saharan African civilizations. [Paideuma work page](https://pavel.paideuma.tv/book/noomahiya-logos-afriki-lyudi-chernogo-solnca)
- **2018:** *Океания: вызов Воды* (*Oceania: The Challenge of Water*). A civilizational monograph treating the maritime environment as a structuring philosophical force. [Volume bibliography](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Серия_«Ноомахия»)
- **2018:** *Неславянские горизонты Восточной Европы: песнь упыря и голос глубин* (*Non-Slavic Horizons of Eastern Europe: Song of the Vampire and Voice of the Depths*). A comparative regional study. [Volume bibliography](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Серия_«Ноомахия»)
- **2018:** *Хамиты: цивилизация африканского Норда* (*Hamites: Civilization of the African North*). A North-African civilizational study; its inherited racial-ethnological terminology makes the work resist neutral classification as contemporary anthropology. [Paideuma work page](https://pavel.paideuma.tv/book/noomahiya-hamity-civilizacii-afrikanskogo-norda)
- **2018:** *Восточная Европа: Славянский Логос, Балканская Навь и сарматский стиль* (*Eastern Europe: The Slavic Logos, Balkan Nav, and Sarmatian Style*). A civilizational study of Slavic and Balkan traditions. [Volume bibliography](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Серия_«Ноомахия»)
- **2019:** *Русский Логос I: Царство Земли — структура русской идентичности* (*The Russian Logos I: The Kingdom of Earth—The Structure of Russian Identity*). The first large Russian culmination of the cycle. [Volume bibliography](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Серия_«Ноомахия»)
- **2019:** *Русский Логос II: Русский историал — народ и государство в поисках субъекта* (*The Russian Logos II: Russian Historial—People and State in Search of a Subject*). The second Russian volume, joining philosophy of history to political ontology. [Volume bibliography](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Серия_«Ноомахия»)
- **2020:** *Образы русской мысли: солнечный царь, блик Софии и Русь Подземная* (*Images of Russian Thought: The Solar Tsar, the Gleam of Sophia, and Subterranean Rus’*). The published bibliography calls this the twenty-fifth volume, while Dugin’s overview calls the completed cycle twenty-four volumes; whether it is an appendix, culmination, or full numbered volume is therefore bibliographically unstable. [Author’s project page](https://www.dugin.ru/ru/topic/bolshaya-noomakhiya), [volume bibliography](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Серия_«Ноомахия»)

### Dialogues, interviews, and collaborative books

- **2013:** *США и новый мировой порядок* / *Os EUA e a Nova Ordem Mundial* (*The United States and the New World Order*), with Olavo de Carvalho. A published debate between opposed geopolitical positions; authorship belongs to the exchange, not to Dugin alone. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Основные_труды)
- **2013:** *L’Appel de l’Eurasie* (*The Call of Eurasia*), with Alain de Benoist. A book-length dialogue; it is a jointly made conversational object rather than a sole-authored treatise. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#На_иностранных_языках)
- **2023:** *Talking to the Wolf: The Alexander Dugin Interviews*, with Nicholas Rooney. A constructed interview book: Dugin supplies the answers, Rooney the questions and dialogic form, so neither “monograph” nor uncomplicated co-authorship quite fits. [Bibliographical list](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#На_иностранных_языках)

### Editorial and publishing Gesamtkunstwerke

- **1988–1991:** *ЭОН* (*EON/Aeon*). An historico-philosophical centre and publishing operation edited by Dugin; it is an institution that produced texts rather than a single text, and thus resists being called one work. [Shekhovtsov’s scholarly study](https://compass.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1749-8171.2009.00158.x)
- **1990–1991 onward:** *Арктогея* (*Arktogeya/Arctogaia*). A historical-religious association, publisher, and later website founded by Dugin. It functioned as the material workshop for many of his books and translations, making it closer to an ongoing publishing project than an individual obra. [Umland’s scholarly study](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1016/j.euras.2010.04.008)
- **1990–2000:** *Милый Ангел* (*Dear Angel*). An almanac on religious history edited by Dugin; issue-level contents are collective, so the periodical is his editorial project, not wholly his authored text. [Biographical chronology](https://adebiportal.kz/ru/authors/view/424), [scholarly overview](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0962629801000439)
- **1992–1998:** *Элементы: евразийское обозрение* (*Elements: Eurasian Review*). A journal founded and edited by Dugin; scholarly research counts nine issues, although some biographical timelines give broader terminal dates. It operated as a multimedia ideological and design environment, approaching a serial Gesamtkunstwerk. [Umland’s scholarly study](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1016/j.euras.2010.04.008)
- **1990s:** *Вторжение* (*Invasion*). A periodical published by Dugin. Surviving scholarly accounts establish the project but do not provide a secure complete issue chronology, so it cannot responsibly be decomposed here into invented issue-level works. [Scholarly overview](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0962629801000439)
- **1996 onward:** *Арктогея* website. A networked continuation of the publishing association and an early archive for Dugin’s texts; because pages, editions, and domains changed, it resists treatment as a fixed work. [Biographical chronology](https://adebiportal.kz/ru/authors/view/424)

### Performances, broadcasts, and audiovisual constructions

- **1996–1998:** *FINIS MUNDI*. A cycle of hour-long theatrical, musical, and philosophical radio productions on Radio 101, with episodes devoted to thinkers, poets, mystics, and political figures. It is a broadcast Gesamtkunstwerk rather than a book, and the surviving bibliography lists only seventeen episode subjects, not necessarily a complete transmission log. [Performance catalogue](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Перформансы)
- **1997–1999:** *Геополитическое обозрение* (*Geopolitical Review*). A recurring radio programme authored and presented by Dugin; it is a serial broadcast project, and no complete episode catalogue was found in the consulted scholarly or author bibliographies. [Biographical chronology](https://adebiportal.kz/ru/authors/view/424)
- **1999:** *Богема против НАТО* (*Bohemia Against NATO*). A collaborative video-art performance involving Dugin, the noise act CISFINITUM, Alexei Borisov, Pavel Zhagun, and the art group Sever; participation is documented, but sole creation by Dugin is not. [Performance catalogue](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Дугин,_Александр_Гельевич#Перформансы)
- **2007 onward:** *Русская вещь* (*The Russian Thing*). A weekly philosophical and political radio programme presented by Dugin. It shares a title with his book sequence but is a separate serial broadcast manifestation; the consulted record does not establish a complete run. [Biographical chronology](https://adebiportal.kz/ru/authors/view/424)

### Political and institutional projects

- **1990–1991 onward:** *Центр специальных метастратегических исследований* (*Centre for Special Metastrategic Studies*), later *Centre for Geopolitical Expertise*. A think tank founded by Dugin: an institutional project producing analysis and ideological networks, not a text. [Umland’s scholarly study](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1016/j.euras.2010.04.008)
- **1993–1998:** *Национал-большевистская партия* (*National Bolshevik Party*). A political organization co-founded by Eduard Limonov, Dugin, and others. Dugin helped make its early ideological form and programme, but the party was collective and continued after his departure, so the whole organization cannot be attributed to him as a sole obra. [Umland’s scholarly study](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1016/j.euras.2010.04.008), [party history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Bolshevik_Party)
- **1990s:** *Новый университет* (*New University*). An attempted independent lecture and educational institution created around Dugin’s intellectual programme; it generated lecture cycles subsequently turned into books, but remained a project rather than a stable university. [Scholarly overview](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0962629801000439)
- **2001 onward:** *Евразия* (*Eurasia*). A socio-political movement founded by Dugin and subsequently reorganized as a party and then as the International Eurasian Movement. Because its legal and organizational forms changed, it is best classified as a continuing political project rather than one organization with an unbroken identity. [Umland’s scholarly study](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1016/j.euras.2010.04.008), [scholarly overview](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0962629801000439)
- **2005 onward:** *Евразийский союз молодёжи* (*Eurasian Youth Union*). A youth movement produced within Dugin’s wider Eurasianist network. Its actions and publications were collective; the project is associated with Dugin’s ideological authorship but is not his individual work. [Umland’s scholarly study](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1016/j.euras.2010.04.008)
