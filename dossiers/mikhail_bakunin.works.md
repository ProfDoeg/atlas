## Works by Mikhail Aleksandrovich Bakunin

### Philosophical and critical writings

- **Summer–autumn 1837:** *Article on Shakespeare’s Hamlet*. An early literary-critical essay, preserved among Bakunin’s writings in the IISH complete-works edition. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **Spring 1838:** *Hegel’s Gymnasium Lectures: Translator’s Preface*. A preface attached to Bakunin’s Russian translation of Hegel; it is both an essay and part of another author’s translated work. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **1839–1840:** *On Philosophy*. A two-article philosophical series, not a freestanding book. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **July–September 1842:** *The Reaction in Germany: A Fragment by a Frenchman*. A philosophical-political newspaper article published pseudonymously; its self-description as a “fragment” is integral to the obra. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **May–June 1843:** *Communism*. A critical essay on contemporary communism rather than a program for Bakunin’s later anarchism. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **1867:** *Essence of Religion*. A philosophical manuscript, apparently unfinished and unpublished during Bakunin’s lifetime. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **1867–1868:** *Federalism, Socialism and Anti-Theologism*. An unfinished theoretical exposition prepared from Bakunin’s intervention at the League of Peace and Freedom; the surviving obra includes several drafts and variants rather than one final text. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **Summer 1868:** *Science and the People*—also catalogued as *How to Pose Revolutionary Questions*. A theoretical article or projected article concerning knowledge and popular revolution. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **July–August 1869:** *Integral Education*. A sequence of articles proposing education without the division between intellectual and manual labor. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **July–August 1869:** *On Cooperation*. A political-economic article, not a practical cooperative manual. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **March 1871:** *The Principle of the State*. An unfinished theoretical essay connected with the larger *Knouto-Germanic Empire* project. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **1871:** *God and the State*. Not an independently completed book by Bakunin: it is a posthumously titled and editorially altered portion of the unfinished *Knouto-Germanic Empire and the Social Revolution*, first issued by Carlo Cafiero and Élisée Reclus in 1882. [Bakunin-Portal collected-works description](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/franzoesisch.html)
- **1871:** *Philosophical Considerations on the Divine Phantom, the Real World and Man*. A long philosophical appendix to the *Knouto-Germanic Empire* project; its later separate publication can obscure its manuscript relationship to that larger, unfinished obra. [Bakunin-Portal collected-works description](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/franzoesisch.html)
- **1874:** *Anarchy According to Proudhon*. A late fragment or set of notes, not a completed systematic treatise. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)

### Books and large unfinished projects

- **End of 1848–April 1849:** *The Situation in Russia*. A projected analytical work surviving as sections on the people, nobility, church, clergy, officials and finances, together with sketches; its multiple pieces resist treatment as a finished book. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **1868:** *Russia: The Revolutionary Question in Russia and Poland*. An unfinished project surviving as a preface, fragment and variant. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **August–September 1870:** *Letters to a Frenchman on the Present Crisis*. A political series composed as “letters” but functioning as an extended revolutionary analysis; it survives in extracts, continuations and six numbered installments rather than a single settled book-text. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **September 1870–1872:** *The Knouto-Germanic Empire and the Social Revolution*. Bakunin’s largest theoretical project: partly printed, repeatedly rewritten and ultimately unfinished, with preambles, warnings, an appendix and at least eighteen catalogued fragments or variants. *God and the State* is an editorially detached part of this obra, not a separate manuscript conceived under that title. [Archives Bakounine edition record](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/franzoesisch.html)
- **1870:** *The Political Situation in France*—the “Marseilles Manuscript” or *Letter to Palix*. A 114-page manuscript produced amid the Franco-Prussian War and Lyon uprising; formally a letter, materially an unfinished political book. [Bakunin-Portal collected-works contents](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/franzoesisch.html)
- **1871:** *History of Socialism*. An incomplete historical-theoretical project known through manuscript material rather than a finished publication. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **August–November 1871:** *The Political Theology of Mazzini and the International*. An extended anti-Mazzinian project growing out of *Reply of an Internationalist to Mazzini*; it survives in parts, summaries, plans, fragments and variants, so no single final text exists. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **1873:** *The Historical Development of the International*. An unfinished project comprising a possibly non-Bakunin preface, a survey of the International in Belgium, material on Belgian socialism and *The International Alliance of Social Revolutionaries*. James Guillaume may have written the preface. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **January–August 1873:** *Statism and Anarchy*. A political-theoretical book with Appendices A and B; it was Bakunin’s only book-length work published during his lifetime, although its projected continuation was never completed. [Bakunin-Portal critical edition](https://www.bakunin.de/schriften/band_4.html)
- **1875:** *Revolutionary Socialism in Russia*. A late unfinished work surviving with two variants rather than an authorized final form. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **January–February 1876:** *On Democracy in France*. A late political manuscript, unfinished or fragmentary. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **1876:** *On Europe*. A late fragment whose status resists the category of completed “work.” [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)

### Manifestos, proclamations and public appeals

- **June 1848:** *Fundamental Principles of the New Slavic Policy*. A programmatic declaration intended to act upon the revolutionary Slavic movement rather than merely describe it. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **October–November 1848:** *Appeal to the Slavs by a Russian Patriot*. A manifesto calling for a revolutionary federation of Slavic peoples; it belongs to the performative politics of 1848. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **2 February 1862:** *To Russians, Poles, and All Friends of the Slavic Cause*. A public manifesto written after Bakunin’s escape from Siberia. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **April 1862:** *A Few Words to the Southern Slavs*. A public revolutionary appeal rather than an analytical essay. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **June–July 1862:** *The People’s Cause: Romanov, Pugachev, or Pestel?*. A manifesto-like intervention presenting rival political futures for Russia. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **Summer 1862:** *To the Slav Brothers from the Pan-Slavic Society*. A proclamation issued in the voice of a political body, making collective and individual authorship difficult to separate. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **Late March 1863:** *Draft Proclamation to the Poles*. A project for a manifesto; its unfinished state is the work’s historical form. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **2 February 1869:** *A Few Words to the Young Brothers in Russia*. A clandestine appeal directed to revolutionary youth. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **Spring–summer 1869:** *Principles of Revolution*. A clandestine revolutionary declaration associated with Bakunin and Sergei Nechaev; the exact division of authorship is disputed and it should not be silently treated as Bakunin’s sole composition. [IISH edition corrections and discussion](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **Autumn 1869–1870:** *To the Russian Nobility* / *The Noble Russian Nobility*. Two proclamations associated jointly with Bakunin and Nechaev; they are manifestations of a collaboration whose precise authorship is not securely divisible. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **January 1870:** *To the Officers of the Russian Army*. A revolutionary appeal addressed directly to serving officers. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **March 1870:** *To the Russian Youth*. A political proclamation connected with Bakunin’s collaboration with Nechaev. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **26 September 1870:** *Revolutionary Federation of Communes*. A proclamation of the Lyon revolutionary movement; as a collectively issued text and attempted political act, it resists classification as Bakunin’s private literary work. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **1870:** *The Awakening of the Peoples*. A revolutionary proclamation or projected proclamation rather than a completed treatise. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **March 1872:** *To the Russian Revolutionaries*. A manifesto addressed to the Russian revolutionary movement. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **16 August 1872:** *Appeal of the Russian Émigrés to the Swiss Authorities*. A collective public declaration; Bakunin’s contribution belongs to a jointly voiced political act. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **September 1873:** *To the Russian Revolutionaries*. A second late appeal, distinct from the March 1872 text. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)

### Protocols, programs and organizational projects

- **September–October 1864:** *Secret International Society of the Revolution: Provisional Program Adopted by the Founding Brothers*. A protocol for a projected clandestine organization; the intention and organizational mechanism, not successful institutional realization, constitute the obra. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **September–October 1864:** *Program of an International Secret Society for the Emancipation of Humanity*. A second organizational project, closely related to but not synonymous with the preceding program. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **October 1864:** *Project for the Organization of the Family of Scandinavian Brothers*. A projected protocol for a regional branch of Bakunin’s secret international network. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **1865–1866:** *Revolutionary Catechism*. A programmatic protocol for Bakunin’s International Brotherhood, distinct from the 1869 *Catechism of a Revolutionary* associated with Nechaev. [Bakunin-Portal overview](https://www.bakunin.de/schriften/index.html)
- **March 1866:** *Principles and Organization of the International Revolutionary Society*. An organizational constitution surviving with a variant. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **March 1866:** *Organization of the International Revolutionary Society*. A related but separately catalogued protocol specifying structure and procedure. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **1866:** *Program of the Italian Social-Democratic Revolution*. A national revolutionary program within Bakunin’s international organizational project. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **Probably 1866:** *Project for a Revolutionary Association*. An undated organizational draft conventionally assigned to 1866; both title and date describe an editorially identified project, not a finished publication. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **Probably 1866:** *Project for an International and Revolutionary Society without Phrases*. An organizational project surviving in a principal text and two variants; the dating remains approximate. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **Autumn 1868:** *Program and Regulations of the International Alliance of Socialist Democracy*. A public constitution and protocol made for an actual political association; parts were necessarily collective institutional texts. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **Autumn 1868:** *Secret Statutes of the Alliance of Socialist Democracy*. A clandestine organizational protocol distinct from the Alliance’s public program. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **Late 1868:** *International Brotherhood: Program and Purpose*. A program for Bakunin’s clandestine network, preserved as an organizational text rather than evidence that the projected body existed exactly as described. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **Late 1868:** *Draft Statutes for the Federation of the Romance Sections of Switzerland*. A proposed institutional protocol issued from the Geneva sections; Bakunin’s authorship is contributory rather than necessarily exclusive. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **April 1869:** *Program and Regulations of the Geneva Section of the Alliance of Socialist Democracy*. A local organizational protocol. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **September 1869:** *Articles IV–VII of the Basel Administrative Resolutions*. Collective congress rules in which Bakunin later said he had made at least three-quarters of the text; this is collaborative protocol, not secure sole authorship. [Bakunin-Portal additions and corrections](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **12 May 1869:** *Mandate Issued to Nechaev*. A credential or authorization rather than a literary work; it survives as an administrative-political act in textual form. [Bakunin-Portal additions and corrections](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **8 June 1872:** *Program of the Polish Social-Revolutionary Society in Zurich*. A political program designed to constitute and direct an organization. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **5 August 1872:** *Program of the Serbian Socialist Party*. A protocol written for another movement and therefore neither a private essay nor straightforward evidence of an organization made by Bakunin alone. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **14–15 August 1872:** *Program of the Slavic Section in Zurich*. The first version of an organizational program; a second version became Appendix B of *Statism and Anarchy*. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **30 August–13 September 1872:** *Program of the International Brotherhood*. A renewed clandestine organizational project, not simply a reprint of the programs of 1864–1866. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)

### Speeches and lectures

- **29 November 1847:** *Speech on the Seventeenth Anniversary of the Polish Revolution*. An oration whose political effect and subsequent print circulation make it both event and text. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **30 March 1863:** *Malmö Speech*. A public address connected with Bakunin’s attempt to join the Polish insurrection. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **28 May 1863:** *Speech at the Stockholm Banquet*. A political oration preserved textually after its performative occasion. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **10 September 1867:** *Speech at the Congress of the League of Peace and Freedom*. An intervention at the congress’s second sitting, surviving in more than one version. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **23–25 September 1868:** *Five Speeches at the Second Congress of the League of Peace and Freedom*. A sequence of oral interventions, not one authored book; they manifest Bakunin’s public break with the League. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **23 November 1868:** *Speech of Citizen Bakunin to the Public Meeting of Foreign Socialists*. A public political address subsequently preserved as a text. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **May 1871:** *Three Lectures to the Workers of the Val de Saint-Imier*. A lecture series rather than a book, combining political education with oral agitation. [Bakunin-Portal collected-works contents](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/franzoesisch.html)

### Journalism, polemics and public letters

- **January 1845:** *Letter to La Réforme*. An open newspaper letter and political intervention. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **6 February 1846:** *Letter to Le Constitutionnel*. A public rebuttal in epistolary form. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **February–March 1848:** *Letters to Count Duchâtel and La Réforme*. Two public interventions from the February Revolution, related by occasion but separately addressed. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **July–September 1848:** *Public Letters to the Allgemeine Oder-Zeitung, Die Reform and Neue Rheinische Zeitung*. A cluster of interventions, drafts and variants generated by accusations against Bakunin; many were projects rather than published letters. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **Late July 1848:** *The Wallachian Uprising and Russian Intervention*. A projected article surviving as a draft, variant and several sets of notes; it resists being counted as one finalized text. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **August 1848:** *Fragments of an Article Concerning the Campaign against Bakunin*. Three defensive fragments rather than a completed article. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **12 October 1848:** *Protests to the Prussian National Assembly and the Saxon Second Chamber*. Two formally distinct petitions or declarations, political acts addressed to legislatures. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **January–March 1850:** *My Defence*. A prison-era legal and political self-defence prepared under interrogation; neither free-standing treatise nor ordinary autobiography. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **1860–1861:** *Letters and Articles for Kolokol and Amur*. Public journalism on a duel, the social life of Irkutsk and eastern Siberian affairs; these are discrete occasional texts grouped here as a journalistic corpus. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **September 1862:** *An Indiscretion from St Petersburg*. A topical newspaper article. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **Autumn 1862:** *Bakunin on the Slavs*. An editorially titled article or fragment; the modern title describes its subject rather than necessarily reproducing an authorial title. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **November–December 1862:** *The Warsaw Central Committee and the Russian Military Committee: Reply to General Mieroslawski*. A public polemic over the Polish and Russian revolutionary movements. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **May 1863:** *Letter on Russia*. An open political letter rather than private correspondence. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **October 1863:** *Official Russia and the Russian People*. A political article contrasting the imperial state with the population. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **October 1866:** *The Situation in Italy*. A topical political analysis. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **August 1867:** *The Slavic Question: Bakunin’s Reply to Herzen*. A polemical public response in letter form. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **May–June 1868:** *A Last Word on Mr Mieroslawski*. A renewed polemical article. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **1868:** *A Necessary Explanation*. A public declaration concerning the journal *Narodnoe Delo*. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **Summer 1868:** *Our Program*. A programmatic newspaper article, distinct from Bakunin’s later text of the same title. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **23 February–10 July 1869:** *To the Companions of the International Working Men’s Association of Le Locle and La Chaux-de-Fonds*—the “Letters on Patriotism.” A seven-article serial whose installments include the sections on natural or physiological patriotism. [Bakunin-Portal collected-works contents](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/franzoesisch.html)
- **March 1869:** *Madame André Léo and L’Égalité*. A newspaper polemic. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **April 1869:** *The Double Strike at Geneva*. A labor-journalism article. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **April 1869:** *In Russia*. A topical article on Russian affairs. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **April–May 1869:** *How the Revolutionary Question Presents Itself*. A programmatic article related to, but not identical with, the similarly titled 1868 text. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **21 May 1869:** *The International Workers’ Movement*. A newspaper article on the International. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **June–July 1869:** *The Sleepers*. A polemical article. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **Summer 1869:** *Our Program*. A separate programmatic text from the article bearing the same title in 1868. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **Summer 1869:** *View of How to Understand Action in the Past and Present*. A strategic memorandum or essay. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **August 1869:** *The Politics of the International*. A newspaper article defining the International’s political stance. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **August 1869:** *Report of the Commission on the Question of Inheritance*. A congress report produced in an institutional setting; Bakunin was its authorial agent, but the document speaks for a commission. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **October 1869:** *Study on the German Jews*. A polemical project associated with Bakunin’s letter to the editors of *Le Réveil*; its antisemitic argument and unfinished manuscript form are part of its historical status. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **6 February 1870:** *Obituary of Alexander Herzen*. A commemorative newspaper text. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **7–15 February 1870:** *Capital Punishment in Russia*. A political-juridical article or article series. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **19 February 1870:** *The Swiss Police*. A topical polemical article. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **March 1870:** *The Bears of Berne and the Bear of St Petersburg*. A satirical political pamphlet. [Bakunin-Portal collected-works contents](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/franzoesisch.html)
- **April 1870:** *Pan-Slavism*. A political article distinguishing Bakunin’s revolutionary federalism from state-centered Pan-Slavism. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **July–August 1870:** *The Intrigues of Monsieur Utin*. A polemic concerning conflict within the International. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **July–August 1871:** *Protest of the Alliance*. A two-part institutional defence; it is both polemic and collective organizational declaration. [Bakunin-Portal collected-works contents](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/franzoesisch.html)
- **25–28 July 1871:** *Reply of an Internationalist to Mazzini*. A newspaper polemic that expanded into the much larger, unfinished *Political Theology of Mazzini and the International*. [Bakunin-Portal collected-works contents](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/franzoesisch.html)
- **28 July–8 August 1871:** *Report on the Alliance*. A multipart institutional report and defence, not a private theoretical work. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **September–October 1871:** *Reply to L’Unità Italiana*. A newspaper response surviving both separately and as an appendix to the Mazzini project; the lost French manuscript was later reconstructed through retranslation from Italian. [Bakunin-Portal collected-works description](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/franzoesisch.html)
- **19–28 October 1871:** *Socialism and Mazzini: Letter to My Friends in Italy on the Workers’ Congress Convened in Rome*. A circular meant to intervene directly in Italian socialist organization. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **16–28 November 1871:** *Mazzinism and Capitalism, the Cooperatives and the International*. A public letter to the editors of *Il Proletario Italiano*. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **December 1871:** *Letter to the Internationalists of Bologna: Explanatory and Supporting Document No. 1*. An evidentiary memorandum within Bakunin’s conflict in the International. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **December 1871:** *My Personal Relations with Marx: Supporting Document No. 2*. A polemical autobiographical memorandum surviving with a variant. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **15 December 1871:** *The Idealist Mazzini and the Social Question*. A public letter to the editors of *Gazzettino Rosa*. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **22–28 December 1871:** *Italy and the General Council of the International Working Men’s Association*. A polemical article in the developing Bakunin–Marx conflict. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **1–2 January 1872:** *The International and Mazzini’s Idealism*. A public letter to *Gazzettino Rosa*. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **7 January 1872:** *French Article*—on conflict in the International, bourgeois consciousness, Mazzini and Italy. The title is editorial and descriptive because no stable authorial title is known. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **February–March 1872:** *To the Companions of the Federation of International Sections of the Jura*. A circular surviving in four textual states rather than one definitive version. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **March 1872:** *Germany and State Communism*. A polemical essay directed against centralized socialism. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **May–June 1872:** *Article for the Polish Review Gmina*. A political article also described as a program for Polish social revolutionaries. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **August 1872:** *Is Nechaev a Political Criminal or Not?*. A public defence addressing the legal and political character of Nechaev’s case, not an endorsement of all his conduct. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **1–8 October 1872:** *Letter to La Liberté of Brussels*. A public letter within the conflict over the International. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **November–December 1872:** *Writing against Marx*. An unfinished polemical continuation of the historical argument in the second installment of *The Knouto-Germanic Empire*; the title is editorial rather than authorial. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **Ca. June 1872:** *En presencia de la cuestión…* An untitled anonymous Spanish newspaper article attributed to Bakunin by Max Nettlau and Paul Lafargue but considered unlikely to be his by Arthur Lehning; it belongs among disputed attributions, not secure works. [Bakunin-Portal additions and corrections](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **1873:** *Where to Go and What to Do?*. A strategic article surviving with a variant. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **25 September 1873:** *Letter Concerning the Pamphlet on the Alliance*. A public letter to the *Journal de Genève*. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **12 October 1873:** *Letter to the Comrades of the Jura Federation Announcing Withdrawal from Political Activity*. An open letter whose declaration was itself a political act, although Bakunin did not cease all revolutionary engagement. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **28–29 July 1874:** *Explanatory Memorandum on the Baronata and Relations with Cafiero*. A private-political apologia concerning the failed Baronata communal project; it is testimony and self-defence rather than a theoretical work. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)

### Autobiographical and carceral texts

- **July–August 1851:** *Confession to Tsar Nicholas I*. A coerced prison confession: textually Bakunin’s composition, but produced under autocratic captivity and addressed to the sovereign. Its resistance to being called a freely made “work” is essential to its classification. [Archives Bakounine publication history](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/franzoesisch.html)
- **1871:** *History of My Life*. An autobiographical fragment or project, not a completed memoir. [Chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)

### Correspondence as a body of work

- **1820s–1876:** *Correspondence*. The IISH complete-works project inventories hundreds of surviving personal, familial, philosophical and political letters individually; as a whole they form an epistolary body rather than one author-planned obra, and many open letters have therefore been listed separately above according to their public function. The corpus is incomplete because survival is accidental, some leaves are missing, recipients or dates remain uncertain, and the CD-ROM deliberately omitted letters addressed to Bakunin. [IISH complete-works description and letter inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **1845–1876:** *Letters known only through fragments, copies or later quotations*. These include the approximately 1845 letter to Ivan Golovin, an incomplete 1872 letter probably addressed to Charles Alerini, and other documents whose physical or textual integrity is disputed; they resist treatment as complete works. [Bakunin-Portal additions and corrections](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)

### Translations, notes and disputed attributions

- **1838:** *Hegel’s Gymnasium Lectures*. A Russian translation by Bakunin, accompanied by his own preface; the translated discourse remains Hegel’s obra while Bakunin made the Russian verbal object. [IISH edition inventory](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **1840s:** *Philosophical and Historical Notes*. The complete-works edition preserves notebooks and isolated notes, but these are working traces rather than securely bounded, author-titled works. [IISH complete-works contents](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **1869:** *Russian Translation of the Communist Manifesto*. Traditionally attributed to Bakunin, chiefly on Nikolai Utin’s testimony, but modern scholarship considers his participation improbable and finds no supporting evidence from Bakunin or his circle; it should be catalogued as dubia, not as a secure work. [Bakunin-Portal attribution correction](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
- **1869:** *Catechism of a Revolutionary*. Commonly confused with Bakunin’s earlier *Revolutionary Catechism*, but generally attributed to Sergei Nechaev; any Bakunin contribution remains disputed. It is therefore not securely a Bakunin obra. [IISH-based chronological inventory](https://ru.anarchistlibraries.net/library/mikhail-bakunin-spisok-sochinenii-bakunina-v-biblioteke-anarkhizma)
- **1849–1850:** *Statements before the Saxon and Austrian Investigating Commissions*. These are recorded manifestations of Bakunin’s speech under legal compulsion, not straightforward authored works; the IISH CD-ROM omitted them, one of the acknowledged limits on its claim to completeness. [IISH complete-works assessment](https://www.bakunin.de/primaerliteratur/oeuvres_completes_cdrom.html)
