## Works by William Joseph Casey

### Tax, investment, and business protocols

- **1951; revised and expanded 1955:** *Tax Sheltered Investments*. A practical investment-and-tax protocol; the revised issue is a later manifestation of the same work, not a separate obra. ([CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))
- **1953:** *Tax Planning for Foundations and Charitable Giving*. A professional guide to structuring charitable gifts and foundations for tax purposes. ([CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))
- **1953–1956:** *Tax Shelter for the Family*. A household tax-planning protocol first issued in 1953 and subsequently revised; the CIA bibliography’s compressed dating of the revised edition (“1956 1955”) is ambiguous, so no single revision year is imposed here. ([CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))
- **1953:** *Tax Shelter in Business*. A business tax-planning manual. ([CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))
- **1957–:** *Tax Control*. A continuously updated professional reference project rather than a text with one stable, final state. ([CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))
- **1957:** *Tax Shelter in Accounting*. A technical protocol applying tax-shelter methods to accounting practice. ([CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))
- **1959:** *Tax Saver*. A practical tax-reduction guide. ([CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))
- **1959; 1961:** *What You Can Do Now to Cut Taxes and Save Cash This Year: Recommendations, Check-Lists, Worksheets*. A checklist-and-worksheet protocol issued in dated manifestations; the CIA transcription appears once to read “not” rather than “now,” an evident but unresolved OCR discrepancy. ([CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))
- **1965–:** *Corporate Planning*. An updating business-reference project, not a single closed composition. ([CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))
- **1968–:** *Estate Planning Ideas*. A serial or updating advisory work whose open-ended date resists treatment as one fixed edition. ([CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))
- **1969–:** *Encyclopedia of Mutual Fund Investment Planning for Security and Profit*. An updating reference compilation; its collective, editorial mode of production makes Casey’s precise share of every component uncertain. ([CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))
- **1970:** *Hidden Gold and Pitfalls in the New Tax Law: New Opportunities and Techniques for Increasing Personal and Company Wealth in the 1970s*. An Institute for Business Planning special report credited jointly to Casey and the institute’s board of editors and research staff, so it is a corporate production rather than an uncomplicated sole-authored book. ([CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))
- **1965–1979:** *Estate Planning*. A repeatedly updated professional-reference project; the bibliography records an open series beginning in 1965 and a 1979 manifestation rather than a single definitive text. ([CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))
- **1977:** *Estate Planning Desk Book*. A desk-reference protocol for estate-planning practice. ([CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))
- **1974:** *Closely-Held Corporations*. A professional guide to the legal and financial organization of closely held companies. ([CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))
- **1977:** *Successful Techniques That Multiply Profits and Personal Payoff in the Closely Held Corporation*. An operational business manual, more protocol than descriptive treatise. ([CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))
- **1970s, exact year unresolved:** *Regulation of Securities Ideas*. An updating advisory project recorded by the CIA bibliography only as “197-”; a precise year cannot responsibly be supplied. ([CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))
- *Tax Planning on Excess Profits*. A tax-planning manual named in Casey’s official confirmation-era publication list, but the consulted sources do not establish a dependable first-publication date. ([Senate confirmation material preserved by CIA](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100280001-9.pdf))
- *How to Raise Money to Make Money*. A financing manual for businesspeople; its existence is securely attested, but the consulted bibliography does not supply a reliable date. ([Senate confirmation material preserved by CIA](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100280001-9.pdf))
- *How to Build and Preserve Executive Wealth*. A personal-finance and executive-planning manual; contemporary reporting places it among the thirty-plus Institute for Business Planning publications but does not establish a secure date here. ([CIA-preserved contemporary profile](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP90-00845R000100190001-6.pdf))
- *How Federal Tax Angles Multiply Real Estate Profits*. A tax-and-property investment protocol named in Casey’s official publication list; no dependable date was found in the consulted bibliography. ([Senate confirmation material preserved by CIA](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100280001-9.pdf))

### Real-estate protocols and calculation tools

- **1957; second edition 1959:** *Tax Shelter in Real Estate*. A real-estate tax manual whose second edition is a revised manifestation of the same work. ([CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))
- **1958:** *Real Estate Investments and How to Make Them*. A practical investment protocol. ([CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))
- **1959–:** *Real Estate Investment Deals, Ideas, Forms*. An open-ended reference and model-form project, not a single stable reading text. ([CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))
- **1959:** *Tax Tested Real Estate Forms*. A collection of operative legal forms: texts intended to be used and executed rather than merely read. ([CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))
- **1960:** *Where and How to Find Real Estate Deals*. A 1960–1961-edition field guide to locating property investments. ([CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))
- **1961–1977:** *Real Estate Investment Tables*. A computational reference tool, reaching at least a sixth edition in 1977; it is better understood as a repeatedly recalculated instrument than as six separate works. ([CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))
- **1978:** *Real Estate Desk Book*. A consolidated professional reference work. ([CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))

### Legal forms and desk books

- **1956:** *Tax Tested Forms of Agreements, Resolutions and Plans: Annotated*. A repertoire of model legal instruments; its contents are protocols designed to generate further operative documents. ([CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))
- **1961:** *Trust Manual and Guide*. A professional protocol for creating and administering trusts. ([CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))
- **1962–:** *Forms of Business Agreements and Resolutions: Annotated, Tax Tested*. An updating bank of executable business forms. The open-ended bibliographical date and collaborative publishing context make it a continuing editorial project rather than a closed sole-authored work. ([CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))
- **by 1965; fourth edition 1975; 1978 manifestation:** *Lawyer’s Desk Book: The Lawyer’s Everyday Instant Answer Book*. A repeatedly revised legal-reference system; biographical sources date an edition to 1965, while the CIA bibliography records the fourth edition in 1975 and another manifestation in 1978. ([CIA biographical record](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250005-8.pdf), [CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))
- **by 1966; second edition 1970; third edition 1972; fourth edition 1974; completely revised fifth edition 1977:** *Accounting Desk Book: The Accountant’s Everyday Instant Answer Book*. A continuously revised professional reference rather than five independent obras; the fifth edition was adapted from Casey’s first four editions by the Institute for Business Planning’s research and editorial staff, so its authorship is explicitly collaborative and derivative. ([CIA biographical record](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250005-8.pdf), [CIA bibliography](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP91-00901R000100250001-2.pdf))

### Historical works

- **1976:** *Where and How the War Was Fought: An Armchair Tour of the American Revolution*. An illustrated or geographically organized historical guide made for the American Bicentennial—part narrative history, part armchair itinerary. Some secondary references misstate the title as *Where and How the War Was Won*; the Hoover finding aid establishes *Fought*. ([Hoover Institution finding aid](https://oac.cdlib.org/findaid/ark:/13030/kt1s20357r))
- **completed before 1983; published posthumously 1988:** *The Secret War Against Hitler*. A memoir-history of OSS intelligence operations based partly on Casey’s wartime experience. Its status is unusually layered: Hoover preserves extensive, long-developed drafts and records extensive revision, while publication occurred after Casey’s death, so the 1988 volume is a posthumous manifestation whose final editorial boundary cannot simply be equated with the author’s last manuscript. ([Hoover Institution finding aid](https://oac.cdlib.org/findaid/ark:/13030/kt1s20357r), [CIA-preserved contemporary profile](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp90-00845r000200830010-4))

### Speeches gathered posthumously

- **1989:** *Scouting the Future: The Public Speeches of William J. Casey*. A posthumous collection made from speeches Casey delivered rather than a book he composed as such. The individual speeches are Casey’s works, but the collected volume is an editorial manifestation assembled after his death; the consulted finding aid nevertheless labels him its author and does not name an editor. ([Hoover Institution finding aid](https://oac.cdlib.org/findaid/ark:/13030/kt1s20357r))

### Institutional and collective creations that resist sole authorship

- **1950 onward:** *Institute for Business Planning reference-publication program*. Casey founded the institute and authored or edited numerous business-law manuals through it; contemporary accounts describe more than thirty publications produced with editorial and research staffs. This is a body of work—an obra in the aggregate—but it resists conversion into a list of uncomplicated Casey-authored books because individual volumes and later editions frequently involved corporate editorial labor. ([Hoover Institution finding aid](https://oac.cdlib.org/findaid/ark:/13030/kt1s20357r), [CIA-preserved contemporary profile](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP90-00845R000100190001-6.pdf))
- **1967–1977:** *Casey Foundation*. A philanthropic institution established by Casey. It is a made organizational project rather than a text, and the archival record treats its decade of activity as a distinct creation while not reducing its collective actions to Casey’s sole authorship. ([Hoover Institution finding aid](https://oac.cdlib.org/findaid/ark:/13030/kt1s20357r))
