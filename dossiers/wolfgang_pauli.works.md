## Works by Wolfgang Ernst Pauli

### Scientific articles and research papers

- **1919:** *Merkurperihelbewegung und Strahlenablenkung in Weyls Gravitationstheorie*. Pauli’s first listed scientific paper, a journal article on Weyl’s gravitational theory (Peierls bibliography no. 1).

- **1919:** *Über die Energiekomponenten des Gravitationsfeldes*. A short research article on gravitational-field energy components (Peierls no. 2).

- **1919:** *Zur Theorie der Gravitation und der Elektrizität von Hermann Weyl*. A critical theoretical article on Weyl’s attempted unification of gravitation and electromagnetism (Peierls no. 3).

- **1920:** *Theoretische Bemerkungen über den Diamagnetismus einatomiger Gase*. A scientific article on the diamagnetism of monatomic gases (Peierls no. 4).

- **1920:** *Die Ausbreitung des Lichtes in bewegten Medien*. A mathematical-physics article on light propagation in moving media (Peierls no. 5).

- **1920:** *Quantentheorie und Magneton*. A brief contribution to early quantum theory and the magneton (Peierls no. 6).

- **1921:** *Zur Theorie der Dielektrizitätskonstante zweiatomiger Dipolgase*. A research article on the dielectric constant of diatomic dipole gases (Peierls no. 7).

- **1922:** *Über die Quantelung gestörter mechanischer Systeme*. A jointly authored article with Max Born on the quantization of perturbed mechanical systems (Peierls no. 8).

- **1922:** *Über das Modell des Wasserstoffmolekülions*. The journal manifestation of Pauli’s doctoral research; it is substantially the same obra as his 1921 dissertation rather than an independent project (Peierls no. 9).

- **1923:** *Über die Gesetzmäßigkeiten des anomalen Zeemaneffektes*. A research paper on the anomalous Zeeman effect (Peierls no. 10).

- **1923:** *Zur Theorie der Bandenspektren*. A jointly authored paper with Hendrik A. Kramers on band spectra (Peierls no. 11).

- **1923:** *Über das thermische Gleichgewicht zwischen Strahlung und freien Elektronen*. A quantum-theoretical paper on equilibrium between radiation and free electrons (Peierls no. 12).

- **1923:** *Zur Frage der Zuordnung der Komplexstrukturterme in starken und in schwachen äußeren Feldern*. A spectroscopy paper on assigning complex-structure terms in external fields (Peierls no. 13).

- **1924:** *Bemerkungen zu den Arbeiten „Die Dimension der Einsteinschen Lichtquanten“ und „Zur Dynamik des Stoßes zwischen einem Lichtquant und einem Elektron“ von L. S. Ornstein und H. C. Burger*. A published critical note responding to two papers (Peierls no. 14).

- **1924:** *Zur Frage der theoretischen Deutung der Satelliten einiger Spektrallinien und ihrer Beeinflussung durch magnetische Felder*. A spectroscopy paper whose historical interpretation became entangled with later, sometimes incorrect, attributions concerning nuclear spin (Peierls no. 15).

- **1925:** *Über den Einfluß der Geschwindigkeitsabhängigkeit der Elektronenmasse auf den Zeemaneffekt*. A theoretical article on relativistic mass dependence and the Zeeman effect (Peierls no. 16).

- **1925:** *Über den Zusammenhang des Abschlusses der Elektronengruppen im Atom mit der Komplexstruktur der Spektren*. The article in which Pauli formulated the exclusion principle; it is both a paper and the principal published manifestation of that discovery (Peierls no. 17).

- **1925:** *Über die Intensitäten der im elektrischen Felde erscheinenden Kombinationslinien*. A research paper on combination-line intensities in electric fields (Peierls no. 18).

- **1925:** *Über die Absorption der Reststrahlen in Kristallen*. A brief published communication on residual-ray absorption in crystals (Peierls no. 19).

- **1926:** *Über das Wasserstoffspektrum vom Standpunkt der neuen Quantenmechanik*. A major matrix-mechanics treatment of hydrogen, independently deriving its spectrum at approximately the same time as Schrödinger’s wave-mechanical derivation (Peierls no. 20).

- **1926:** *Über die Dielektrizitätskonstante von Dipolgasen nach der Quantenmechanik*. A jointly authored paper with Lucy Mensing; the reference bibliography itself flags uncertainty about the published author order (Peierls no. 21).

- **1927:** *Über Gasentartung und Paramagnetismus*. A research paper establishing what became known as Pauli paramagnetism (Peierls no. 22).

- **1927:** *Zur Quantenmechanik des magnetischen Elektrons*. A research article introducing the matrices now called the Pauli matrices (Peierls no. 23).

- **1927:** *Über den auf die Teilchen in den Kometenschweifen ausgeübten Strahlungsdruck*. A jointly authored astrophysical paper with Walter Baade; the reference bibliography flags uncertainty about author order (Peierls no. 24).

- **1928:** *Zur Quantenelektrodynamik ladungsfreier Felder*. A joint paper with Pascual Jordan on quantizing the free electromagnetic field (Peierls no. 25).

- **1929:** *Zur Quantendynamik der Wellenfelder*. The first of two foundational quantum-field-theory papers written with Werner Heisenberg (Peierls no. 26).

- **1930:** *Zur Quantentheorie der Wellenfelder. II*. The second Heisenberg–Pauli paper on quantum field theory (Peierls no. 27).

- **1931:** *Zur Hyperfeinstruktur von Li⁺. Teil II*. A joint article with Paul Güttinger on lithium-ion hyperfine structure (Peierls no. 29).

- **1932:** *La théorie unitaire d’Einstein et Mayer et les équations de Dirac*. A joint paper with Jacques Solomon published in two journal installments; the installments are manifestations of one study, not two separately conceived works (Peierls no. 31).

- **1932:** *Diracs Wellengleichung des Elektrons und geometrische Optik*. A theoretical article relating Dirac’s electron equation to geometrical optics (Peierls no. 32).

- **1933:** *Einige die Quantenmechanik betreffenden Erkundigungsfragen*. A research article framed as exploratory questions about quantum mechanics (Peierls no. 33).

- **1933:** *Über die Intensität der Streustrahlung bewegter freier Elektronen*. A paper on radiation scattered by moving free electrons (Peierls no. 34).

- **1933:** *Über die Formulierung der Naturgesetze mit fünf homogenen Koordinaten*. A two-part article—Part I on classical theory and Part II on Dirac equations for matter waves—constituting one extended five-dimensional project (Peierls no. 35).

- **1934:** *Über die Quantisierung der skalaren relativistischen Wellengleichung*. A joint paper with Victor Weisskopf on quantizing the scalar relativistic wave equation; Pauli reportedly called it his “anti-Dirac paper” (Peierls no. 37).

- **1935–1936:** *Beiträge zur mathematischen Theorie der Dirac’schen Matrizen / Contributions mathématiques à la théorie des matrices de Dirac*. A conference contribution followed by an enlarged French journal manifestation; their status as versions of one obra is clearer than treating the translation and expansion as wholly separate works (Peierls nos. 38 and 41).

- **1936:** *Remarks on the Polarization Effects in the Positron Theory*. A joint article with M. E. Rose (Peierls no. 40).

- **1936:** *Théorie quantique relativiste des particules obéissant à la statistique d’Einstein–Bose*. A paper on relativistic quantum theory for Bose–Einstein particles (Peierls no. 42).

- **1937:** *Über das H-Theorem in der Quantenmechanik*. A joint paper with Markus Fierz on the quantum-mechanical H-theorem (Peierls no. 43).

- **1938:** *Zur Theorie der Emission langwelliger Lichtquanten*. A joint study with Markus Fierz, appearing both as a journal article and a short separately issued volume; those are two manifestations of the same work (Peierls no. 44).

- **1938:** *On Asymptotic Series for Functions in the Theory of Diffraction of Light*. A mathematical-physics article on diffraction theory (Peierls no. 45).

- **1938:** *Some Considerations of Principle on the Theory of β Decay*. A short paper published in a Russian scientific bulletin (Peierls no. 46).

- **1939:** *Über ein Kriterium für Ein- oder Zweiwertigkeit der Eigenfunktionen in der Wellenmechanik*. A paper on single- and double-valued wave functions (Peierls no. 47).

- **1939:** *Über relativistische Feldgleichungen von Teilchen mit beliebigem Spin im elektromagnetischen Feld*. A short joint paper with Markus Fierz (Peierls no. 48).

- **1939:** *On Relativistic Wave Equations for Particles of Arbitrary Spin in an Electromagnetic Field*. A longer joint work with Markus Fierz, related to but not merely an edition of the preceding German note (Peierls no. 49).

- **1940:** *On the Statistical Behavior of Known and Unknown Elementary Particles*. A joint article with Frederik Belinfante (Peierls no. 50).

- **1940:** *The Connection Between Spin and Statistics*. Pauli’s major proof of the spin–statistics connection; a later 1950 article with nearly the same title revisited the subject and is separately listed below (Peierls no. 51).

- **1940:** *Über die Invarianz der Dirac’schen Wellengleichungen gegenüber Ähnlichkeitstransformationen des Linienelementes im Fall verschwindender Ruhmasse*. A research note on invariance of the massless Dirac equation (Peierls no. 52).

- **1941:** *Relativistic Field Theories of Elementary Particles*. A substantial review article that also functioned as an original synthesis of contemporary field theory (Peierls no. 53).

- **1942:** *The Pseudoscalar Meson Field with Strong Coupling*. A joint research article with Sidney Dancoff (Peierls no. 54).

- **1942:** *Strong Coupling Mesotron Theory of Nuclear Interactions*. A conference abstract with Dancoff. Its survival only as an abstract makes it a very slight manifestation rather than a full paper.

- **1943:** *On the Theory of a Mixed Pseudoscalar and a Vector Meson Field*. A joint paper with S. Kusaka; duplicate bibliographic records describe the same publication, not two works (Peierls no. 55).

- **1943:** *On the Non-Existence of Regular Stationary Solutions of Relativistic Field Equations*. A joint article with Albert Einstein (Peierls no. 56).

- **1943:** *On Dirac’s New Method of Field Quantization*. A substantial theoretical article (Peierls no. 57).

- **1943:** *On Applications of the λ-Limiting Process to the Theory of the Meson Field*. A research paper on meson-field theory (Peierls no. 58).

- **1943:** *On Strong Coupling and Weak Coupling Theories of the Meson Field*. A meeting abstract rather than a full research article.

- **1944:** *On the Application of Dirac’s Method of Field-Quantization to the Problem of Emission of Low Frequency Photons*. A short joint conference publication with Josef-Maria Jauch (Peierls no. 59).

- **1945:** *On the Strong Coupling Case for Spin-Dependent Interactions in Scalar- and Vector-Pair Theories*. A joint article with Ning Hu (Peierls no. 61).

- **1946:** *Diracs Feldquantisierung und Emission von Photonen kleiner Frequenzen*. A brief article continuing Pauli’s work on field quantization and soft-photon emission (Peierls no. 63).

- **1947:** *Eine Methode zur Bestimmung von Oszillatorenstärken (f-Werten) aus dem Starkeffekt*. A research article on obtaining oscillator strengths from the Stark effect; it appears in the expanded bibliography but lacks a Peierls number.

- **1949:** *On the Invariant Regularization in Relativistic Quantum Theory*. The joint Pauli–Villars paper introducing the regularization method bearing their names (Peierls no. 68).

- **1950:** *On the Connection Between Spin and Statistics*. A later paper revisiting and extending the subject of Pauli’s 1940 proof (Peierls no. 69).

- **1953:** *On the Hamiltonian Structure of Non-Local Field Theories*. A journal article on nonlocal field theory (Peierls no. 76).

- **1955:** *On the Mathematical Structure of T. D. Lee’s Model of a Renormalizable Field Theory*. A joint monographic paper with Gunnar Källén, dedicated to Niels Bohr (Peierls no. 82).

- **1956:** *Remarks on Problems Connected with the Renormalization of Quantized Fields*. A conference paper on quantum-field renormalization (Peierls no. 83).

- **1957:** *On the Conservation of the Lepton Charge*. A research paper on lepton-number conservation (Peierls no. 88).

- **1958:** *Zur Thermodynamik dissoziierter Gleichgewichtsgemische in äußeren Kraftfeldern*. A thermodynamics article; two bibliographic records describe the same journal publication (Peierls no. 92).

- **1958:** *The Indefinite Metric with Complex Roots*. A two-page conference-proceedings contribution, identified in the Peierls bibliography as Pauli’s last published scientific article (Peierls no. 93).

- **written before December 1958; published 1959:** *Report and Comment on F. Gürsey’s “Group Structure of Elementary Particles”*. A joint report with Bruno Touschek, published posthumously. Its publication date is not its date of making.

### Dissertations, treatises, handbooks, and lecture books

- **1921:** *Über das Modell des Wasserstoff-Molekülions*. Pauli’s Munich doctoral dissertation under Arnold Sommerfeld; its 1922 journal publication is a second manifestation of the same research.

- **1921; revised 1956–1958:** *Relativitätstheorie*. A long encyclopedia treatise written while Pauli was still a student. The 1958 German revision and English and Italian translations are later manifestations with supplementary notes, not newly authored books (Peierls no. 94).

- **1924:** *Störungstheorie*. A concise reference-encyclopedia article on perturbation theory (Peierls no. 95).

- **1926:** *Quantentheorie*. A 278-page handbook article that functions as a book-length systematic treatise (Peierls no. 96).

- **1928:** *Über das H-Theorem vom Anwachsen der Entropie vom Standpunkt der neuen Quantenmechanik*. An essay contributed to the volume honoring Sommerfeld’s sixtieth birthday (Peierls no. 97).

- **1929:** *Theorie der schwarzen Strahlung*. A substantial textbook contribution on black-body radiation (Peierls no. 98).

- **1929:** *Allgemeine Grundlagen der Quantentheorie des Atombaues*. A book-length textbook contribution on the foundations of atomic quantum theory (Peierls no. 99).

- **1933; revised 1958:** *Die allgemeinen Prinzipien der Wellenmechanik*. A handbook treatise on wave mechanics; the 1958 printing is a revised manifestation, while later English books are translations (Peierls no. 100).

- **1946; revised 1948:** *Meson Theory of Nuclear Forces*. A short scientific monograph developed from lectures; the second edition is a revision of the same obra (Peierls no. 101).

- **1947:** *Statistische Mechanik*. ETH lecture notes prepared by M. R. Schafroth. Their authorship is collaborative and mediated: the intellectual content derives from Pauli’s lectures, while the written object was prepared by another person.

- **1955:** *Niels Bohr and the Development of Physics*. A Festschrift edited by Pauli with Léon Rosenfeld and Victor Weisskopf. It is an editorial construction and collaborative book-object, not a solely authored text.

- **1956:** *Continuous Groups in Quantum Mechanics*. A CERN lecture report. It is a textual manifestation of teaching rather than a conventional authored monograph.

- **1957:** *Vorlesungen über Optik und Elektronentheorie*. ETH lecture notes associated with Pauli and Paul Erdős; the surviving book resists simple single authorship because lecture, transcription, preparation, and publication are distributed acts.

- **lecture courses delivered principally at ETH; posthumously published 1973:** *Pauli Lectures on Physics*. Six courses—*Electrodynamics*, *Optics and the Theory of Electrons*, *Thermodynamics and the Kinetic Theory of Gases*, *Statistical Mechanics*, *Wave Mechanics*, and *Selected Topics in Field Quantization*. These are Pauli’s teaching projects, but their familiar English book forms are posthumous translations edited from lecture notes and should not be mistaken for six books personally prepared for publication by him.

### Conference interventions, lectures, and scientific addresses

- **1930:** *Les théories quantiques du magnétisme dans “L’électron magnétique”*. Pauli’s report to the sixth Solvay Physics Conference, published with an extensive recorded discussion (Peierls no. 28).

- **1931:** *Antwort bei Verleihung der Lorentzmedaille*. A ceremonial response delivered on receiving the Lorentz Medal (Peierls no. 30).

- **June 1931:** *The Neutrino Postulate*. An unpublished Pasadena conference talk. Pauli declined to publish it because he remained uncertain about the particle’s existence; accordingly, it is a project and event, not a stable authored text.

- **1934:** *Discussion of M. Heisenberg’s “La Structure du Noyau”*. A published intervention in the seventh Solvay Conference proceedings, presenting Pauli’s reasoning for the light neutral particle later called the neutrino.

- **1945–1946:** *Exclusion Principle and Quantum Mechanics*. Pauli’s Nobel lecture, delivered for the 1945 prize and subsequently printed in several languages and venues. Those printings are manifestations of one lecture, not separate works (Peierls no. 64).

- **1947:** *Difficulties of Field Theories and of Field Quantization*. A conference report or published lecture (Peierls no. 65).

- **1952:** *Der Begriff der Wahrscheinlichkeit und seine Rolle in den Naturwissenschaften*. A lecture and recorded discussion involving Pauli, B. L. van der Waerden, and S. Rosin. The jointly printed event resists attribution as a wholly Pauli-authored article (Peierls no. 77).

- **1953:** *État actuel de la théorie quantique des champs: la renormalisation*. The delayed proceedings manifestation of a 1950 Paris lecture (Peierls no. 71).

- **1953:** *Discussion Remark to A. Pais’s “Isotopic Spin and Mass Quantization”*. A one-page printed intervention, closer to the trace of an event than to a freestanding paper.

- **1954; published 1957:** *Phänomen und physikalische Realität*. A lecture delivered at the International Congress of Philosophy in Zürich and later published as an essay (Peierls no. 89).

- **1955; published 1956:** *Ansprache durch den Präsidenten der Konferenz; Zusammenfassung und Schlußwort; Relativitätstheorie und Wissenschaft*. Opening, closing, and summary remarks from the Bern relativity conference. The bibliography groups three spatially separated proceedings texts as one entry (Peierls no. 85).

- **1956:** *Announcement*. A one-page CERN Symposium item whose subject is not identified by the standard bibliography. It therefore resists classification even as a definite text (Peierls no. 86).

- **1957:** *Zur älteren und neueren Geschichte des Neutrinos*. An abstract of a Zürich lecture; the substantially longer text published posthumously in 1961 is another manifestation whose exact relationship to the delivered lecture requires editorial study (Peierls no. 87).

### Physics, history, philosophy, and psychology essays

- **1933:** *Paul Ehrenfest*. An obituary and intellectual memorial (Peierls no. 36).

- **1936:** *Raum, Zeit und Kausalität in der modernen Physik*. An essay joining modern physics to questions of space, time, and causality (Peierls no. 39).

- **1945:** *Niels Bohr on His 60th Birthday*. A commemorative scientific-biographical essay (Peierls no. 60).

- **1946:** *Remarks on the History of the Exclusion Principle*. A historical account by Pauli of the making of his exclusion principle (Peierls no. 62).

- **1948:** *Sommerfelds Beiträge zur Quantentheorie*. A historical assessment of Sommerfeld’s role in quantum theory (Peierls no. 66).

- **1948:** *Die Idee der Komplementarität*. An editorial-philosophical essay on complementarity (Peierls no. 67).

- **1949:** *Einstein’s Contributions to Quantum Theory*. A historical and critical essay; its 1955 German publication is a translated manifestation, not another work (Peierls no. 102).

- **1950:** *Die philosophische Bedeutung der Idee der Komplementarität*. An epistemological essay on complementarity (Peierls no. 70).

- **1951:** *Arnold Sommerfeld*. An obituary appearing in more than one venue; the bibliography assigns separate numbers to its manifestations, but they do not establish distinct acts of authorship (Peierls nos. 72–73).

- **1952:** *Der Einfluss archetypischer Vorstellungen auf die Bildung naturwissenschaftlicher Theorien bei Kepler*. A long historical and psychological essay published with C. G. Jung’s *Synchronizität als ein Prinzip akausaler Zusammenhänge* in *Naturerklärung und Psyche*. The paired book is a cross-disciplinary composite rather than a seamless coauthored Gesamtkunstwerk.

- **1952:** *Die Geschichte des periodischen Systems der Elemente*. A brief historical article on the periodic system (Peierls no. 74).

- **1952:** *Theorie und Experiment*. A short epistemological editorial (Peierls no. 75).

- **1953:** *Remarques sur le problème des paramètres cachés dans la mécanique quantique et sur la théorie de l’onde pilote*. A philosophical-technical essay opposing hidden-variable and pilot-wave accounts (Peierls no. 104).

- **1954:** *Matter*. An essay for the symposium volume *Man’s Right to Knowledge*.

- **1954:** *Naturwissenschaftliche und erkenntnistheoretische Aspekte der Ideen vom Unbewussten*. An essay explicitly crossing physics, epistemology, and depth psychology (Peierls no. 81).

- **1954:** *Wahrscheinlichkeit und Physik*. An essay with a printed discussion on probability and physics (Peierls no. 78).

- **1955:** *Exclusion Principle, Lorentz Group and Reflection of Space-Time and Charge*. Pauli’s contribution to the Bohr Festschrift that he also edited (Peierls no. 80).

- **1955:** *Rydberg and the Periodic System of the Elements*. A historical conference paper (Peierls no. 79).

- **1956:** *Die Wissenschaft und das abendländische Denken*. A congress essay on science and Western thought; the 1959 journal printing is a posthumous reprint, not a new work (Peierls no. 84).

- **1957:** *Phänomen und physikalische Realität*. In its published form, an epistemological essay derived from Pauli’s 1954 lecture (Peierls no. 89).

- **1958:** *Albert Einstein in der Entwicklung der Physik*. A newspaper essay assessing Einstein’s historical place in physics (Peierls no. 90).

- **1958:** *Die Verletzung von Spiegelungs-Symmetrien in den Gesetzen der Atomphysik*. A scientific and interpretive essay on parity violation (Peierls no. 91).

### Manifestos, protocols, and conceptual creations

- **1925:** *The exclusion principle*. A principle rather than a text: Pauli made it as a theoretical rule, while the 1925 spectroscopy paper is its principal textual manifestation. Calling the principle itself a “work” is useful only if conceptual creations belong in the atlas.

- **1927:** *The Pauli-matrix formalism*. A mathematical construction embodied in *Zur Quantenmechanik des magnetischen Elektrons*, not an independently titled text.

- **4 December 1930:** *Open letter to the “radioactive ladies and gentlemen” at the Tübingen meeting*. A letter proposing a light neutral particle to preserve conservation laws in beta decay. It acts upon a scientific community and therefore has something of a manifesto, but it remains a private-circulation scientific letter rather than a conventional publication.

- **1930–1934:** *The neutrino hypothesis*. A scientific project first circulated by letter, discussed in an unpublished lecture, and cautiously defended in conference discussion before receiving an ordinary printed-paper form. It resists identification with any single text.

- **1949:** *Pauli–Villars regularization*. A calculational protocol created with Felix Villars and embodied in their 1949 paper. It is appropriately called a protocol because it instructs physicists how to regulate divergent expressions.

- **1953:** *Non-Abelian Kaluza–Klein gauge construction*. An unpublished theoretical project communicated in letters and manuscripts. Later historians describe it as an early non-Abelian gauge theory, but Pauli did not publish it and questioned its physical usefulness; priority claims therefore concern an unfinished or withheld project, not an established publication.

### Correspondence, manuscripts, and archival bodies of work

- **1919–1958:** *Scientific correspondence with Bohr, Einstein, Heisenberg, Jung, and others*. More than a collection of incidental messages: Pauli repeatedly developed scientific and philosophical ideas in letters before or instead of publication. The critical edition fills eight physical parts across four chronological volumes, and CERN records more than 2,100 letter items; this is a body of work, not one text, and the surviving corpus cannot be presumed complete.

- **1932–1958:** *Pauli–Jung correspondence*. A sustained collaborative inquiry into psychology, symbolism, matter, and mind. Its later edited publications are editorial selections from an exchange, so neither the correspondence nor its book manifestations should be treated as a singly authored work.

- **dates various:** *Lecture manuscripts and student-prepared notes*. CERN preserves a Pauli Lecture Collection of 66 catalogue records and a Manuscript Collection of hundreds of records. These objects range from Pauli autographs to transcriptions and teaching documents, so “authorship” and even textual boundaries must be decided item by item rather than assigned wholesale.

- **dates various:** *Dream records, diagrams, and psychological materials*. Some survive through Pauli’s exchanges with Jung and Jungian publications, often under protective anonymity or later editorial framing. They resist being called public works: they are personal records that became evidence in another author’s psychological project.

### Posthumous editorial manifestations, not new works by Pauli

- **1961:** *Aufsätze und Vorträge über Physik und Erkenntnistheorie*. A posthumous collection of previously written essays and lectures; it is an editorial manifestation, not a book newly made by Pauli in 1961.

- **1964:** *Collected Scientific Papers*. Two posthumous volumes edited by Ralph Kronig and Victor Weisskopf. This is the principal collected-works manifestation of Pauli’s scientific corpus, but the collection itself was made by its editors.

- **1979–2005; supplement projected:** *Wissenschaftlicher Briefwechsel mit Bohr, Einstein, Heisenberg u.a.* A critical edition of Pauli’s correspondence edited by Armin Hermann, Karl von Meyenn, and Victor Weisskopf. CERN states that a supplementary volume remained projected; the edition is therefore an unfinished editorial project even though Pauli’s letters themselves are finished historical objects.

- **1973:** *Pauli Lectures on Physics*. A six-volume English edition translated and edited from lecture notes after Pauli’s death. It manifests Pauli’s teaching but should not be dated as six works made in 1973.

- **1994:** *Writings on Physics and Philosophy*. An English selection edited by Charles P. Enz and Karl von Meyenn and translated by Robert Schlapp. It is a posthumous anthology and translation, not an additional Pauli obra.
