## Works by Antoni Gaudí i Cornet

### Student projects and works before qualification

- **1870:** *Restoration of Poblet Monastery*. An architectural project made with Eduard Toda and Josep Ribera; it remained a proposal rather than a realized restoration. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1872–1878:** *Works in the Parc de la Ciutadella*. Contributions under master builder Josep Fontserè to the Cascade, water reservoir, Aribau-monument fence, entrance gates, and Passeig de Sant Joan railings; these resist treatment as autonomous Gaudí works because his precise share is collaborative and subordinate. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1874:** *Competition design for a funerary monument to Anselm Clavé*. An unrealized and now-lost competition project. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1875:** *Cemetery Gate*. A student architectural project known from a surviving photograph rather than the original object. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1876:** *Pavilion for the Centennial Exhibition, Philadelphia*. A student project whose original documentation is lost. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1876:** *Courtyard for a Provincial Council Building*. A student architectural exercise rather than a commission intended for construction. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1876:** *Royal Landing Stage*. Two versions of a student project for a ceremonial waterside structure. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1876:** *Wardrobe for Miss Malera*. A lost furniture project made before Gaudí qualified as an architect. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1876:** *Alterations to the Grabulosa House*. A lost architectural project, apparently unrealized. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1876:** *Designs for Application to Industry*. A lost submission to a competition organized by the Ateneu Barcelonès; the collective title describes an industrial-design exercise rather than a single securely identifiable object. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1876:** *Counter for the Vilardell Pharmacy*. A lost design project for commercial furniture. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1876:** *Pavilion for Montserrat Monastery*. A project made while collaborating with Francisco de Paula del Villar; Gaudí’s individual contribution is not an autonomous work with settled boundaries. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1876:** *Slaughterhouse Project*. A collaborative architectural project undertaken with Francisco de Paula del Villar. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1876:** *Armchair for the Ateneu Barcelonès*. A furniture design made in collaboration with Francisco de Paula del Villar. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1876:** *Altar at El Masnou*. An early liturgical furnishing produced in collaboration with Leandre Serrallach; Gaudí’s exact share is not separately defined. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1876–1877:** *Pavilion for Villa Arcadia*. A collaborative architectural project for a recreational residence, made with Leandre Serrallach. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1876–1877:** *Tramway from Montjuïc to Can Tunis*. An infrastructure project undertaken with the Padrós y Borràs concern; it is an intention and technical scheme rather than a building or art object. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1877:** *General Hospital*. A lost student project. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1877:** *Monumental Fountain for Plaça de Catalunya*. A student project for an urban monument, not a realized fountain. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1877–1878:** *University Assembly Hall*. Gaudí’s final-degree project, preserved as a set of five drawings; its identity is the project rather than a constructed hall. The archive dates its presentation to 1877, while catalogues also associate it with his 1878 qualification. [Càtedra Gaudí archival record](https://mdc.csuc.cat/digital/collection/gaudi/id/308/), [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)

### Buildings, landscapes, and architectural Gesamtkunstwerke

- **1878–1879:** *Streetlights of Plaça Reial and Pla de Palau*. Urban lighting structures; two Plaça Reial lamps survive, while only two of the original four Pla de Palau lamps remain. Catalogues disagree over whether to give the latter a separate date of 1890. [Gaudí Museum catalogue](https://gaudi.museum/en/discover-gaudi/antoni-gaudi/catalogue-of-works/), [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1878–1883:** *Cooperativa Obrera Mataronense*. A factory-and-housing complex in Mataró, only partly built; part of the factory nave and a sanitation building survive, so the obra is simultaneously a realized fragment and a larger unfulfilled project. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/), [Consell Gaudí](https://consellantonigaudi.cat/es/obra/)
- **1883:** *House for Salvador Pagès*. A house in Mataró that was constructed and later demolished. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1883–1885/1888:** *Casa Vicens*. A house conceived across architecture, fixed decoration, garden, and furnishings—a genuine Gesamtkunstwerk—subsequently enlarged by J. B. Serra de Martínez in 1925; much of its garden disappeared and sources differ over whether Gaudí’s campaign ended in 1885 or 1888. [Gaudí Museum catalogue](https://gaudi.museum/en/discover-gaudi/antoni-gaudi/catalogue-of-works/), [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1883–1885:** *Casa de Máximo Díaz de Quijano (“El Capricho”)*. A constructed villa in Comillas integrating architecture and applied ornament. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/), [Consell Gaudí](https://consellantonigaudi.cat/es/obra/)
- **1883–1926:** *Basílica i Temple Expiatori de la Sagrada Família*. An evolving sacred Gesamtkunstwerk comprising architecture, sculpture, liturgical design, models, and successive projects; Gaudí inherited an existing project, completed only part of his design, and the basilica remains under construction by later architects. [Gaudí Museum catalogue](https://gaudi.museum/en/discover-gaudi/antoni-gaudi/catalogue-of-works/), [Consell Gaudí](https://consellantonigaudi.cat/es/obra/)
- **1884–1887:** *Works at the Güell Estate*. A compound of three gates, two pavilions, a belvedere, enclosure wall, and roof stair at Les Corts; the pavilions and two gates survive, while another gate has been reconstructed. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/), [Consell Gaudí](https://consellantonigaudi.cat/es/obra/)
- **1886–1889/1890:** *Palau Güell*. An urban palace integrating structure, interiors, furniture, ironwork, and rooftop sculpture; sources variously close its construction in 1889 or 1890. [Gaudí Museum catalogue](https://gaudi.museum/en/discover-gaudi/antoni-gaudi/catalogue-of-works/), [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1887–1893:** *Episcopal Palace of Astorga*. A bishop’s palace abandoned by Gaudí before completion and finished in 1909—or, in another chronology, between 1907 and 1915—by Ricardo García Guereta with changes to Gaudí’s project. The resulting building is therefore not wholly Gaudí’s realized obra. [Gaudí Museum catalogue](https://gaudi.museum/en/discover-gaudi/antoni-gaudi/catalogue-of-works/), [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1887–1912:** *Priest’s House and Gaudí’s Workshop at the Sagrada Família*. Functional buildings on the temple site, destroyed in 1936; the loss of the workshop also destroyed much project documentation and many models. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1888:** *Compañía Transatlántica Pavilion at the Barcelona Universal Exposition*. Ephemeral exhibition architecture, later demolished. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1888–1890:** *Col·legi de les Teresianes*. A convent-school building, completed and still operating as a school; some catalogues date the principal campaign 1888–1889. [Gaudí Museum catalogue](https://gaudi.museum/en/discover-gaudi/antoni-gaudi/catalogue-of-works/), [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1891–1894:** *Casa Fernández Andrés (“Casa Botines”)*. A combined commercial and residential building in León; alternate catalogues date it 1891–1893. [Gaudí Museum catalogue](https://gaudi.museum/en/discover-gaudi/antoni-gaudi/catalogue-of-works/), [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **c. 1895–1897:** *Bodegas Güell*. A winery complex at Garraf; attribution is disputed, with Francesc Berenguer proposed as designer, so Gaudí’s signature or association cannot settle authorship by itself. [Gaudí Museum catalogue](https://gaudi.museum/en/discover-gaudi/antoni-gaudi/catalogue-of-works/), [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1895:** *Rooftop Porter’s Room at Eusebi Güell’s House, Carrer del Comte de l’Assalt 9*. A small architectural addition rather than an independent building. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1898–1904:** *Casa Calvet*. A residential and commercial Gesamtkunstwerk encompassing architecture and purpose-designed interiors and furniture; another catalogue restricts the principal building campaign to 1898–1900. [Gaudí Museum catalogue](https://gaudi.museum/en/discover-gaudi/antoni-gaudi/catalogue-of-works/), [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1899–1900:** *Casa Clapés*. A house listed in the Fundación’s catalogue but not among the buildings currently recognized by the Consell Gaudí, making its status within a strict canon uncertain. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/), [Consell Gaudí](https://consellantonigaudi.cat/es/obra/)
- **1900:** *Alterations to Dr. Santaló’s House*. An architectural intervention rather than a freestanding building. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1900–1907/1916:** *First Mystery of Glory, Monumental Rosary of Montserrat*. A devotional outdoor ensemble combining architecture and sculpture; Jeroni Martorell completed it with modifications to Gaudí’s project, and catalogues differ over whether its full realization extends to 1916. [Gaudí Museum catalogue](https://gaudi.museum/en/discover-gaudi/antoni-gaudi/catalogue-of-works/), [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1900–1909:** *Casa Figueras (“Bellesguard”)*. A house and grounds conceived as a historical-symbolic architectural ensemble. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/), [Consell Gaudí](https://consellantonigaudi.cat/es/obra/)
- **1900–1914:** *Park Güell*. An unfinished garden-city project realized principally as landscape, infrastructure, architecture, sculpture, and urban furniture; its refusal of medium boundaries makes “Gesamtkunstwerk” more accurate than “park” alone. [Gaudí Museum catalogue](https://gaudi.museum/en/discover-gaudi/antoni-gaudi/catalogue-of-works/), [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1901–1902:** *Portal and Enclosure Wall of the Miralles Estate*. A surviving entrance and wall, later altered; the crowning iron cross is a replica. [Gaudí Museum catalogue](https://gaudi.museum/en/discover-gaudi/antoni-gaudi/catalogue-of-works/), [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **c. 1902–1905:** *Xalet del Catllaràs*. A mountain chalet formerly treated as an attribution; a 2026 study commissioned by the Generalitat concluded that Gaudí designed the project but did not supervise construction, leaving a meaningful distinction between project authorship and the built manifestation. [Gaudí Museum catalogue](https://gaudi.museum/en/discover-gaudi/antoni-gaudi/catalogue-of-works/), [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1903–1905:** *House for Josep Pardo, now the Gaudí House Museum*. A Park Güell residence whose plans bear Gaudí’s signature but whose design is attributed to Francesc Berenguer; it should not be silently counted as an uncomplicated Gaudí work. [Gaudí Museum catalogue](https://gaudi.museum/en/discover-gaudi/antoni-gaudi/catalogue-of-works/), [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1903–1914:** *Liturgical Restoration of Mallorca Cathedral*. An unfinished transformation of an existing cathedral through spatial changes, lighting, furniture, and liturgical objects; it is an intervention and Gesamtkunstwerk, not a building authored from the ground up. [Gaudí Museum catalogue](https://gaudi.museum/en/discover-gaudi/antoni-gaudi/catalogue-of-works/), [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1903–1914:** *Alterations to the South Gallery of the Episcopal Palace, Palma*. An architectural intervention associated with the Mallorca Cathedral campaign. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1904–1906:** *Casa Batlló*. A radical reconstruction of an existing house integrating architecture, interior surfaces, joinery, stained glass, and furniture—a Gesamtkunstwerk rather than merely a façade. [Gaudí Museum catalogue](https://gaudi.museum/en/discover-gaudi/antoni-gaudi/catalogue-of-works/), [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **c. 1904–1905:** *Jardins de Can Artigas*. A landscape-and-architecture ensemble at La Pobla de Lillet; it is traditionally attributed to Gaudí, but the attribution lacks supporting documentation. [Gaudí Museum catalogue](https://gaudi.museum/en/discover-gaudi/antoni-gaudi/catalogue-of-works/), [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1904:** *Warehouse for the Badia Foundry*. A constructed industrial structure that has disappeared. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1904–1905:** *Sala Mercè*. A lost interior and entertainment-space decoration; as an environmental design, it lay between architecture, spectacle, and applied art. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1906–1910/1912:** *Casa Milà (“La Pedrera”)*. A residential Gesamtkunstwerk combining structural experiment, apartments, courtyards, ironwork, roofscape, and designed fittings; catalogues variously end Gaudí’s campaign in 1910 or 1912. [Gaudí Museum catalogue](https://gaudi.museum/en/discover-gaudi/antoni-gaudi/catalogue-of-works/), [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1906:** *Torre Mateu (“La Miranda”)*. A house attributed to Gaudí and demolished in 1962; the attribution remains qualified rather than secure. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1908–1910:** *Alteration and Enlargement of Casa Larrard*. A transformation of Eusebi Güell’s residence in Park Güell, subsequently altered again and converted into a school. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1908–1916:** *Church of the Colònia Güell*. An experimental sacred project of which only the crypt was built; the surviving structure is a fragment of a deliberately larger obra, and catalogues sometimes date Gaudí’s active campaign only through 1915. [Gaudí Museum catalogue](https://gaudi.museum/en/discover-gaudi/antoni-gaudi/catalogue-of-works/), [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1908–1909:** *Sagrada Família Schools*. A small school building using ruled brick surfaces; repeatedly damaged and reconstructed, it was moved to a new site in 2002, so the present manifestation is not wholly the untouched 1909 object. [Gaudí Museum catalogue](https://gaudi.museum/en/discover-gaudi/antoni-gaudi/catalogue-of-works/), [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1922:** *Stained-glass Window for the House of Lluís Plandiura Pou*. An architectural glasswork rather than a building. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)

### Unbuilt architectural, urban, and scenic projects

- **1878:** *Illuminated Street Signs for Barcelona*. An unbuilt urban-lighting project commissioned by J. M. Carré. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1878:** *Flower Kiosks and Public Lavatories for Barcelona*. An unbuilt street-furniture and public-amenity system commissioned by Enric Girossi. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1878:** *Irrigation System in the Caldes Valley, Palau-solità i Plegamans*. A technical landscape and water-management project, not a building. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1880:** *Electric Lighting for Barcelona’s Sea Wall*. An unrealized infrastructure and illumination project. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1880:** *House for Eulàlia Dalmau Galtès*. An unbuilt residential project. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1880:** *Competition Project for a Theatre in Reus*. An unrealized project whose documentation is lost. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1882:** *Competition Project for a Casino in San Sebastián*. A lost and unrealized architectural proposal. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1882:** *Hunting Pavilion for Eusebi Güell at Garraf*. An unbuilt architectural project. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1882:** *Benedictine Monastery of the Holy Spirit, Villarricos*. An unbuilt project made in collaboration with Joan Martorell; its authorship is shared rather than exclusively Gaudí’s. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1882:** *Façade for Barcelona Cathedral*. An unbuilt project made while collaborating with Joan Martorell. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1883:** *Chapel of the Blessed Sacrament, Alella*. An unbuilt chapel project for the parish church of Sant Feliu. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1887–1888:** *Reform of Barcelona City Hall’s Saló de Cent and Access Stair*. An unrealized architectural-interior project; only the related chair for the Queen Regent’s office was made. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1890:** *General Plan for the Colònia Güell*. An urban-planning project rather than a single building; later architecture in the settlement was produced by several designers. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1892–1893:** *Franciscan Missions Complex, Tangier*. An unbuilt religious and institutional complex known through its project documentation. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1894:** *Shed for Florencio Bayona Massagué, Passeig de Colom*. A set of plans for a utilitarian structure; the catalogue does not establish a surviving realization. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1895:** *Shed for Water Tanks, Carrer Lancaster 7*. A project prepared for Eusebi Güell; its catalogue identity is the design rather than a documented surviving structure. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1895:** *Rooftop Washhouses, Rambla dels Caputxins 30*. An architectural-service project for Eusebi Güell’s property. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1895:** *Güell Family Pantheon, Montserrat*. An unbuilt funerary project. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1897:** *Rooftop Washhouses, Carrer Còdols 16*. Plans for service structures on one of Eusebi Güell’s properties; no realized work is asserted by the catalogue. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1900 and 1914:** *Reform of the Sanctuary of Misericòrdia, Reus*. Two versions of an unbuilt restoration project. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1900:** *Modification of Street Levels on the Lower Muntaner de Dalt Estate*. An unrealized grading and urbanization project. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1900:** *First-Mass Keepsake for Mn. Norbert Font i Sagué*. An unrealized graphic-design collaboration with Ricard Opisso, not an architectural work. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1904–1906:** *Bridge over the Torrent de Pomeret*. An unbuilt bridge project for Sarrià. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1904:** *Xalet Graner*. An unbuilt house project in Sarrià. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1907:** *Urbanization of Plaça del Rei and Monument to Jaume I*. An unrealized urban and commemorative ensemble. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1908:** *Chapel for the Teresian College*. An unbuilt addition to the school. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1908:** *Urbanization of the Bellesguard Estate*. An unrealized planning project. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1908:** *Hotel in New York*. An attributed, unbuilt skyscraper-hotel project; because both authorship and intended circumstances have been contested, it resists entry as a securely documented Gaudí work. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1909:** *Scenography for Oedipus Rex at Park Güell*. An unrealized stage-environment project; its medium would have been theatrical manifestation rather than permanent architecture. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1916:** *Monument to Bishop Torras i Bages at the Sagrada Família*. An unbuilt commemorative project. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1918:** *Monument to Enric Prat de la Riba*. An unbuilt project for Castellterçol, known through a drawing by Lluís Bonet Garí rather than a surviving Gaudí original. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **c. 1920:** *Barcelona Railway Station*. An unbuilt station project; the date is approximate and should not be treated as fixed. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1922:** *Church of the Assumption, Rancagua*. A chapel project sent to Chile and posthumously used as the basis for a modern construction; the current building process is a later manifestation, not a structure supervised by Gaudí. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1923:** *Calvet Chapel, Torelló*. An unbuilt sacred-architecture project. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1924:** *Pulpit for an Unidentified Church in Valencia*. An unbuilt liturgical-furniture project whose intended site is unknown. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)

### Furniture, liturgical objects, graphic works, and ephemeral manifestations

- **1878:** *Worktable for Gaudí’s Office*. A piece of furniture destroyed in 1936. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1878:** *Comella Glove Shop Display Case*. A commercial display cabinet shown at the Paris Universal Exposition and subsequently lost. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1878:** *Door for P. Miró’s Theatre*. A lost architectural fitting made for a theatre in Sant Gervasi de Cassoles. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1878:** *Stained-glass Window and Rose Window from Can Pujades*. Architectural glass made for a farmhouse chapel at Vallgorguina, now held by the Museu Nacional d’Art de Catalunya. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1878:** *Reliquary Project*. A design for a sacred container; the catalogue identifies the project but does not assert a realized reliquary. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1878:** *Furniture for the Chapel of the Sobrellano Palace*. A suite of liturgical furnishings at Comillas, not a building and not reducible to a single object. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1878:** *Professional Card*. Gaudí’s own printed professional identity object; two examples survive. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1879:** *Decoration of the Gibert Pharmacy*. A lost commercial interior. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1879:** *Allegorical Cavalcade Honouring Mossèn Vicent Garcia*. An ephemeral public manifestation at Vallfogona de Riucorb, now represented by five surviving drawings rather than the event itself. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1879–1881:** *Altar, Monstrance, Lighting Devices, and Mosaic for the College of Jesús-María*. A liturgical ensemble at Sant Andreu del Palomar; the mosaic survives, and some furnishings and lights were transferred to the order’s Sant Gervasi chapel. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1880–1884:** *Altar for the College of Jesús-María, Tarragona*. A liturgical structure destroyed in 1936 and later reconstructed, so the current altar is a later manifestation rather than the untouched original. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1881:** *Garden Templet for the Sobrellano Palace*. A small ornamental structure transferred to the Güell Estate and subsequently lost. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1881:** *Emblem of the Associació Catalanista d’Excursions Científiques*. A collaborative graphic or heraldic design made with Antoni Torrents Torras and Rossinyol; it resists attribution to Gaudí alone. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1883:** *Topographical Plans of Can Rosell de la Llena*. Two surviving survey plans; these are technical records and working instruments rather than autonomous architectural obras. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1884:** *Banner for La Obrera Mataronense*. A lost cooperative banner; only the bronze bee that crowned it survives. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1885:** *Bocabella Family Oratory*. A portable or domestic devotional ensemble now in a private collection. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1885:** *Dining Table for Francesc Ullar Roca*. A piece of domestic furniture made for Sant Feliu de Codines. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1885:** *Altar of Saint Joseph, Sagrada Família Crypt*. A liturgical work destroyed in 1936 and later reconstructed; the extant object is not wholly the original material work. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1887:** *Compañía Transatlántica Pavilion at the Cádiz Maritime Exposition*. An ephemeral exhibition pavilion of uncertain attribution; it should not be presented as securely by Gaudí. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1887–1888:** *Chair for the Queen Regent’s Office*. Furniture realized from the otherwise unbuilt project to reform Barcelona City Hall’s Saló de Cent and stairway. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1894:** *Cloister Windows for the Astorga Seminary*. Architectural fittings rather than an independent building. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1900:** *Banner of the Orfeó de Sant Feliu de Codines*. A ceremonial textile object preserved at the Museu Municipal Can Xifreda. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1900:** *Banner of the Reus Pilgrims Resident in Barcelona*. A ceremonial banner made for the Sanctuary of Misericòrdia, destroyed and known today through a replica displayed at the Sagrada Família. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1901–1904:** *Decoration of the Marquès de Castelldosrius House*. A lost domestic interior ensemble. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1902:** *Decoration of Bar Torino*. A lost commercial interior to which several Modernista designers contributed; Gaudí’s contribution belongs to a collective decorative environment. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1903–1914:** *Pavement of Nostra Senyora dels Àngels, Pollença*. A church floor attributed to Gaudí, but the attribution is uncertain. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1903–1914:** *Advice on Festive Tapestries for the Convent of Sant Jeroni, Palma*. Advisory participation rather than a clearly authored object; it resists being called an independent work by Gaudí. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1904–1910:** *Monument to Dr. Robert*. A commemorative monument of uncertain attribution to Gaudí. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1906:** *Banner of the Guild of Locksmiths and Blacksmiths*. A ceremonial object destroyed in 1936. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1906:** *Chasuble for the First Mass of Mn. Joan Roquet-Jalmar*. A liturgical vestment destroyed in 1939; Gaudí’s collaboration with Rafael Masó is itself only attributed. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1910:** *Streetlights for the Centenary of Jaume Balmes*. Ephemeral urban fixtures erected at Vic and subsequently removed; only some components survive. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1910:** *Camp-Nonell Pantheon*. A funerary monument at Lloret de Mar of uncertain attribution. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1912:** *Pulpits for the Parish Church of Blanes*. Liturgical furnishings subsequently destroyed. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1916:** *Portrait Bust of Bishop Josep Torras i Bages*. A collaborative sculpture executed by Llorenç Matamala under Gaudí’s direction and polychromed by Joan Llimona, then destroyed in 1936; authorship therefore belongs to a directed collaboration, not Gaudí’s hand alone. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1922:** *Dedication to the Orfeó Català*. A graphic-textual presentation object with polychromy by Josep Maria Jujol, preserved in the Orfeó Català archive; it is a collaborative inscription rather than a literary text or manifesto. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)

### Collaborative buildings and advisory acts

- **1879:** *Advisory Commission for the Restoration of Sant Esteve, Granollers*. Participation in a consultative body rather than a separately authored design; it resists classification as a Gaudí work at all, but belongs in the record of what he helped make possible. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1882–1885:** *Church and Convent of the Saleses, Barcelona*. Architectural work under Joan Martorell to which Gaudí contributed; the catalogue does not delimit his contribution sufficiently to call the entire building his obra. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1883–1889:** *Jesuit Church, Barcelona*. A collaboration with Joan Martorell rather than an independently authored Gaudí building. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
- **1915:** *Advisory Commission for the Restoration and Façade of Manresa Cathedral*. Professional advice and committee participation rather than a discrete made object; its resistance to “work” status is part of its proper classification. [Fundación Antonio Gaudí](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/lista-de-obras-de-gaudi/)
