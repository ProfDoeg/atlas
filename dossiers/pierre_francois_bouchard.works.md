## Works by Pierre-François-Xavier Bouchard

### Manuscript narratives

- **1805:** *Journal historique de la défense d’El-Arich*. A retrospective military journal describing the siege and fall of El-Arich in December 1799; the manuscript is preserved as SHD 1 M 537 and was later published by Gaston Wiet under the editorial title *La chute d’El-Arich*. ([Service historique de la Défense](https://www.servicehistorique.sga.defense.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/ir_pdf/ir-924547.pdf); [Laurens](https://www.lajauneetlarouge.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/jr-464-vif.pdf))

- **1801 or later:** *Journal du siège du fort Jullien à la bouche occidentale du Nil*. A first-person military relation of the 1801 siege, preserved in manuscript as SHD 1 M 552 and apparently never issued by Bouchard as a book. The archival catalogue dates the events—not necessarily the act of composition—to 1801, so a more precise writing date cannot safely be supplied. ([Service historique de la Défense](https://www.servicehistorique.sga.defense.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/ir_pdf/ir-924547.pdf); [Leclant](https://www.asphor.org/bibliotheque/personnages/bouchard/bulletin_de_la_societe_francaise_d_egyptologie_n_146.pdf))

### Military-engineering works and projects

- **1798–1799:** *Repairs and defensive improvements to Fort Julien (Borg Rachid)*. An engineering project rather than a wholly new building: Bouchard appears to have assisted Captain Charles-Marie-Benjamin d’Hautpoul in repairing an existing fort, so sole authorship cannot be assigned securely. The surviving biographical reconstruction explicitly treats this appointment as probable rather than certain. ([ASPHOR](https://www.asphor.org/le-patrimoine/les-hommes/pierre-bouchard-1-14.htm); [Leclant](https://www.asphor.org/bibliotheque/personnages/bouchard/bulletin_de_la_societe_francaise_d_egyptologie_n_146.pdf))

- **1800:** *Two unnamed defensive works between Lake Menzaleh and the Mediterranean*. These were military fortifications constructed during Bouchard’s posting in the Damietta district. The standard scholarly account supplies neither individual names nor enough detail to determine whether they survive, and therefore they cannot responsibly be separated into two titled obras. ([Leclant](https://www.asphor.org/bibliotheque/personnages/bouchard/bulletin_de_la_societe_francaise_d_egyptologie_n_146.pdf))

- **1801:** *Fortification of the western mouth of the Nile*. This was an official engineering project assigned to Bouchard on 4 February 1801, encompassing work associated with Fort Julien, the river bar or *Bogaz*, and Rosetta’s artillery positions. It resists classification as his individual work because the surviving order places him inside a larger command, financing, and construction apparatus. ([Leclant](https://www.asphor.org/bibliotheque/personnages/bouchard/bulletin_de_la_societe_francaise_d_egyptologie_n_146.pdf); [Sanson’s contemporary order](https://www.sothebys.com/en/buy/auction/2021/collection-of-a-connoisseur-history-in-manuscript-part-2/n-a-sanson-letter-signed-as-engineer-in-chief-to))

### Discovery rather than made work

- **Between 14 and 25 July 1799:** *The recovery and recognition of the Rosetta Stone*. This is a manifestation or archaeological discovery, not an obra made by Bouchard: the inscribed object was ancient, and even the physical act of finding it may have belonged to an unnamed soldier working under the engineering officers. Bouchard’s better-supported contribution was recognizing its importance and arranging its transmission; both the customary date of 19 July and the attribution of the initial discovery exclusively to him remain disputed. ([Leclant](https://www.asphor.org/bibliotheque/personnages/bouchard/bulletin_de_la_societe_francaise_d_egyptologie_n_146.pdf); [ASPHOR](https://www.asphor.org/le-patrimoine/les-hommes/pierre-bouchard-1-14.htm))
