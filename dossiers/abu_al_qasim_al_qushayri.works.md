## Works by Abu al-Qasim Abd al-Karim ibn Hawazin al-Qushayri

### Qurʾānic commentaries

- **Before 410 AH / 1019–20:** *al-Taysīr fī ʿilm al-tafsīr*, also called *al-Tafsīr al-kabīr*. A large, predominantly exoteric Qurʾānic commentary surviving in manuscripts; it is distinct from the later mystical commentary *Laṭāʾif al-ishārāt*. ([Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297); [bibliographical notice](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/9535/27))
- **Completed 434 AH / 1042–43:** *Laṭāʾif al-ishārāt*. A complete allusive or mystical Qurʾānic commentary: an exegetical obra organized around the Qurʾān rather than a free-standing Sufi manual. ([Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297); [Usul edition and description](https://usul.ai/t/tafsir-al-qushayri))

### Sufi teaching works and protocols

- **437 AH / 1045–46:** *al-Risāla al-Qushayriyya*, originally an epistle “to the community of Sufis in the lands of Islam.” Part collective biography, part lexicon of technical terms, part doctrinal defence and part protocol for conduct, it became a handbook but began as an intervention addressed to a living community. ([dated authorial notice](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/9533/15); [Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297))
- **Undated:** *al-Taḥbīr fī l-tadhkīr*, also transmitted as *al-Taḥbīr fī sharḥ asmāʾ Allāh al-ḥusnā*. An extant commentary on the divine names that turns their explanation into ethical and contemplative instruction. ([Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297); [manuscript record](https://app.alreq.com/ar/authors/author/68c1f342-66cf-4804-dffb-08d7902f2e12))
- **Undated:** *Naḥw al-qulūb al-ṣaghīr* (“The Lesser Grammar of Hearts”). An extant allegorical grammar in which grammatical categories become a map of inward discipline; it resists being called merely a grammar or merely a Sufi treatise. ([Chiabotti](https://ojs.unito.it/index.php/kervan/article/view/984); [Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297))
- **Undated:** *Naḥw al-qulūb al-kabīr* (“The Greater Grammar of Hearts”). A larger spiritual grammar, once reported lost but subsequently recovered in several manuscripts; some older notices confused the two *Naḥw al-qulūb* texts or knew only one. ([Iványi](https://arabist.hu/wp-content/uploads/articles/volume-17/Tamas-Ivanyi-1996.pdf); [manuscript-discovery notice](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/9535/27))
- **Undated:** *Kitāb al-Miʿrāj*. An extant devotional-mystical account of the Prophet’s night journey and ascension, arranged as a narrative of ascent with spiritual interpretation. It should not be confused with the later Arabic source behind the European *Book of the Ladder*, despite recurrent popular claims connecting the two. ([Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297); [Iványi’s primary-source bibliography](https://arabist.hu/wp-content/uploads/articles/volume-17/Tamas-Ivanyi-1996.pdf))
- **Undated:** *Manthūr al-khiṭāb fī mashhūr al-abwāb*. A surviving compact sequence of approximately forty-four discourses on Sufi stations, resembling an abbreviated manual without simply being an abridgement of the *Risāla*. ([Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297))
- **Undated:** *Istifādāt al-murādāt*. A short Sufi treatise transmitted under al-Qushayrī’s name; its title and manuscript tradition are secure enough for inclusion, although its precise date and original setting remain unknown. ([Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297))
- **Undated:** *al-Waṣiyya li-l-murīdīn*. A testament or protocol addressed to aspirants, giving practical directions for attachment to a master and conduct on the path. It is a prescriptive text rather than a descriptive survey of Sufism. ([Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297); [Malamud on its instructions](https://www.ghazali.org/articles/Malamud-94.pdf))
- **Undated; attribution disputed:** *Tartīb al-sulūk fī ṭarīq Allāh*. A protocol for initiation, remembrance and progressive spiritual practice, long attributed to al-Qushayrī. Eliyahu Stern’s textual comparison argues that its concepts and organization differ substantially from al-Qushayrī’s authenticated works, so it must remain an attributed rather than secure obra. ([Stern](https://brill.com/view/journals/si/107/1/article-p65_3.pdf); [Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297))
- **Undated:** *al-Futuwwa*. A treatise on spiritual chivalry reported in the bibliographical tradition; its independent textual survival and extent are uncertain. ([Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297))
- **Undated:** *Aḥkām al-samāʿ*. A reported treatment of audition—the rules and spiritual dangers of listening to music or recitation. Its present textual status is uncertain, so it resists classification as a recoverable work rather than a remembered title. ([Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297); [early works list](https://www.sunnaonline.org/text.php?action=show&id=593))
- **Undated:** *al-Maqāmāt al-thalātha*. A short work on three spiritual stations, known from the bibliographical tradition but incompletely documented. ([Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297); [Basyūnī-derived works list](https://usul.ai/t/tafsir-al-qushayri))
- **Undated:** *al-Qaṣīda al-ṣūfiyya*. A didactic Sufi poem rather than a prose book; surviving notices do not establish a firm date or complete textual history. ([Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297); [works list](https://usul.ai/t/tafsir-al-qushayri))

### Theology and public interventions

- **Undated:** *Lumaʿ fī l-iʿtiqād*. A concise Ashʿarī creed, preserved as a short dogmatic treatise. ([Richard M. Frank bibliographical notice](https://journals.rudn.ru/philosophy/article/view/11746); [Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297))
- **Undated:** *al-Fuṣūl fī l-uṣūl*. A second concise theological exposition, treating foundational doctrines in numbered sections; it is independent of, though doctrinally close to, the creedal opening of the *Risāla*. ([Frank bibliographical notice](https://journals.rudn.ru/philosophy/article/view/11746); [Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297))
- **Circa 445 AH / 1053–54:** *Shikāyat ahl al-sunna bi-ḥikāyat mā nālahum min al-miḥna*. A polemical open letter documenting and opposing the Seljuq persecution of Ashʿarīs in Khurasan. It is both historical testimony and manifesto: its purpose is to mobilize Sunni scholarly opinion, not merely to narrate events. ([Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297); [historical discussion of the 445 AH crisis](https://ahlulkalam.wordpress.com/2020/01/31/teil-7-sultan-tughrul-beg-gest-455-n-h-ordnet-im-jahre-445-n-h-an-abu-al-hasan-al-ashari-gest-324-n-h-und-die-ashariyyah-zu-verfluchen/))
- **Undated:** *al-Tawḥīd al-nabawī*. A reported theological work grounding divine unity in prophetic teaching; no firm date is known and its survival as an independent text is uncertain. ([Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297); [works list](https://usul.ai/t/tafsir-al-qushayri))
- **Undated:** *Fatwā*. A juridical-theological responsum reported as a separate item. “Fatwā” may describe the document’s form rather than preserve its original title, so its identity as a discrete work is resistant and uncertain. ([Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297))

### Ḥadīth collections and performed teaching

- **Undated:** *al-Arbaʿūn fī taṣḥīḥ al-muʿāmala*. A forty-ḥadīth ethical collection organized to reform conduct; it functions as a protocol of character formation as well as a compilation of transmitted reports. ([Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297))
- **Undated:** *al-Arbaʿūn min masānīd al-mashāyikh al-ʿishrīn*. A second, distinct forty-ḥadīth collection, assembled from the transmissions of twenty teachers; it is a deliberately structured record of scholarly lineage. ([text record](https://usul.ai/t/arbacuna-min-masanid/46); [Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297))
- **Undated:** *Nāsikh al-ḥadīth wa-mansūkhuhu*. A reported technical work on abrogating and abrogated prophetic reports; its text is not securely known to survive. ([Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297))
- **Eleventh century; individual sessions undated:** *al-Amālī*. A surviving series of ḥadīth dictation sessions recorded through the practice of oral teaching. This is not straightforwardly a “book” made at one sitting: the obra is the textual residue of performed scholarly sessions and their subsequent transmission. ([Orfali and Chiabotti](https://www.bilalorfali.com/storage/pdf/1597436009mdL-Amali-Qushayri.pdf); [edition description](https://www.darelmachreq.com/storage/catalog/culture_15523105551057476942.pdf))

### Lost or textually unlocated works reported under his name

- **Undated; lost:** *Ādāb al-ṣūfiyya*. A reported work on Sufi comportment; no independent text is presently identified, and citations or overlapping material elsewhere cannot safely be treated as the lost whole. ([Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297); [early bibliography](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/9535/27))
- **Undated; lost:** *Kitāb al-Jawāhir*. A title preserved by bibliographers, without enough surviving evidence to determine whether its “jewels” were aphorisms, traditions or another literary form. ([Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297); [early bibliography](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/9535/27))
- **Undated; lost:** *ʿUyūn al-ajwiba fī uṣūl al-asʾila*. A reported work on fundamental questions and answers; its discipline and precise architecture cannot be recovered securely from the title alone. ([Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297); [early bibliography](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/9535/27))
- **Undated; lost:** *Kitāb al-Munājāt*. A reported collection or composition of intimate prayers. Because the text is unlocated, it is unclear whether it was an authored sequence, a compilation of orally delivered prayers, or a later extraction. ([Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297); [early bibliography](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/9535/27))
- **Undated; lost:** *al-Muntahā fī nukat ulī l-nuhā*. A reported collection of subtle observations for “people of understanding”; only the title is securely recoverable. ([Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297); [early bibliography](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/9535/27))
- **Undated; lost or unidentified:** *Ḥayāt al-arwāḥ wa-l-dalīl ilā ṭarīq al-ṣalāḥ*. A reported guide to spiritual life and rectitude whose independent survival has not been securely established. ([Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297))
- **Undated; uncertain identity:** *al-Bulgha fī l-taṣawwuf* or a similarly transmitted title. The bibliographical record preserves variants but does not establish beyond doubt whether this was an independent work, an alternate title, or a mistaken attribution; it therefore resists treatment as a securely individuated obra. ([Nguyen and Chiabotti](https://doi.org/10.1163/15700585-12341297))
