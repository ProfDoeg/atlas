## Works by Alexis-Charles-Henri Clérel de Tocqueville

### Books and book-length obras

- **1833:** *Du système pénitentiaire aux États-Unis et de son application en France*. Investigative report and reform treatise made jointly with Gustave de Beaumont after their official American mission; its appendices and later revisions belong to the collaborative obra rather than constituting independent books. ([Gallimard critical-edition bibliography](https://classiques.uqam.ca/classiques/De_tocqueville_alexis/tocqueville_biblio/toc_biblio.html); [Online Library of Liberty](https://oll.libertyfund.org/people/alexis-de-tocqueville/titles))
- **1835 and 1840:** *De la démocratie en Amérique*. One large political and social inquiry issued in two substantially distinct installments—what scholarship commonly calls the first and second *Démocratie*—rather than two editions of a single 1835 text. ([Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm); [1848 edition and revision history](https://www.davidmhart.com/liberty/FrenchClassicalLiberals/Tocqueville/1848-DA/index.html))
- **1856:** *L’Ancien Régime et la Révolution*. Published historical obra conceived as the first portion of a larger inquiry into the Revolution; Tocqueville died before completing the projected continuation. ([Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm); [Online Library of Liberty](https://oll.libertyfund.org/people/alexis-de-tocqueville/titles))

### Historical projects, drafts, and private recollection

- **1836:** *État social et politique de la France avant et depuis 1789*. Historical-political essay commissioned for John Stuart Mill’s *London and Westminster Review*; it is an autonomous study, although later editors place it beside Tocqueville’s larger work on the Revolution. ([Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm); [scholarly chronology](https://classiques.uqam.ca/classiques/De_tocqueville_alexis/tocqueville_reperes/reperes_chronologiques.html))
- **1850–1851:** *Souvenirs*. A private memoir of the Revolution of 1848 and the Second Republic, expressly withheld from contemporary publication and first published posthumously in 1893; it resists being called a public work because Tocqueville wrote it for himself and a restricted future readership. ([Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm); [Fundacja Tocqueville](https://tocqueville.eu/tocqueville/dziela/))
- **1850–1858:** *[Considérations sur la Révolution]*. An unfinished historical project reconstructed from manuscripts; the bracketed title is editorial, not a fixed title supplied by Tocqueville. ([Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm))
- **1853–1856:** *Esquisses de « L’Ancien Régime et la Révolution »*. Drafts, abandoned formulations, documentary extracts, and working notes for the published book and its unrealized continuation; this is the workshop of an obra, not another completed book. ([Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm))
- **1856–1859:** Unfinished continuation of *L’Ancien Régime et la Révolution*. Research notes and fragments toward later volumes survive, but no final title, boundary, or completed text does; the project itself is the made thing. ([Gallimard/Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm))

### Travel notebooks and investigative writing

- **1826–1827:** *Voyage en Sicile*. Travel narrative or notebook arising from Tocqueville’s Italian and Sicilian journey; its identity as a separate work is chiefly posthumous and editorial. ([Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm); [scholarly chronology](https://classiques.uqam.ca/classiques/De_tocqueville_alexis/textes_economiques/textes_economiques_dates.html))
- **1831–1832:** *Voyage en Amérique*. A heterogeneous body of notebooks, interview records, route notes, and narrative fragments made during the journey with Beaumont; later editions turn the papers into a “journey,” but Tocqueville did not publish them as a finished travel book. ([Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm))
- **1831:** Writings on Lower Canada, later gathered as *Regards sur le Bas-Canada* or *Tocqueville au Bas-Canada*. Notes, conversations, and related later writings rather than a book Tocqueville himself constituted under either modern title. ([UQAM contents](https://classiques.uqam.ca/classiques/De_tocqueville_alexis/au_bas_canada/au_bas_canada_tdm.html); [Pléiade catalogue note](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm))
- **1833:** *Voyage en Angleterre de 1833*. Travel and social-observation notebook; a documentary investigation rather than a lifetime publication. ([Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm))
- **1835:** *Voyage en Angleterre et en Irlande de 1835*. Notes, conversations, and observations from the second British journey, posthumously organized as a travel work. ([Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm))
- **1836:** *Voyage en Suisse*. Travel notebook concerned especially with political institutions; it remained manuscript material during Tocqueville’s lifetime. ([Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm))
- **1841:** *Notes du voyage en Algérie de 1841*. Field notes and observations from Tocqueville’s first Algerian journey, furnishing material for his colonial analyses without becoming a finished book. ([Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm))
- **1846:** *Voyage en Algérie*. Notes from the second Algerian journey; the title identifies an editorially bounded notebook corpus, not a work published by Tocqueville. ([Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm))
- **1840s:** *Ébauches d’un ouvrage sur l’Inde*. Project for an unwritten or unfinished book, surviving as drafts, reading notes, and miscellaneous notes; it is specifically a project whose incompletion is part of its identity. ([Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm))

### Social inquiry and reform protocols

- **1835:** *Mémoire sur le paupérisme*. Academic memorandum diagnosing modern poverty and proposing associative remedies; it is simultaneously analysis and a reform protocol. ([Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm); [scholarly chronology](https://classiques.uqam.ca/classiques/De_tocqueville_alexis/tocqueville_reperes/reperes_chronologiques.html))
- **1837:** *Deuxième article sur le paupérisme*. Unfinished or unreleased sequel to the 1835 memorandum; Tocqueville neither presented nor published it, so its modern title and separation are manuscript-editorial facts. ([Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm); [scholarly chronology](https://classiques.uqam.ca/classiques/De_tocqueville_alexis/tocqueville_reperes/reperes_chronologiques.html))
- **1843–1845:** Reports on foundlings for the Conseil général de la Manche. Three successive local-government reports, not a single book, made to alter departmental welfare policy; the collected edition treats such local writings as a corpus. ([scholarly chronology](https://classiques.uqam.ca/classiques/De_tocqueville_alexis/tocqueville_reperes/reperes_chronologiques.html); [Gallimard bibliography](https://classiques.uqam.ca/classiques/De_tocqueville_alexis/textes_economiques/textes_economiques_biblio.html))
- **1843–1844:** Parliamentary reports on prison reform. Legislative protocols translating Tocqueville’s penitentiary research into a proposed French prison system; their intended object was institutional action, not literary description. ([scholarly chronology](https://classiques.uqam.ca/classiques/De_tocqueville_alexis/tocqueville_reperes/reperes_chronologiques.html); [Gallimard bibliography](https://classiques.uqam.ca/classiques/De_tocqueville_alexis/tocqueville_biblio/toc_biblio.html))
- **1844–1845:** Reports on the proposed Paris–Cherbourg railway. Local administrative and economic reports made for the Conseil général de la Manche; they resist classification as authored “works” because they belong equally to deliberative government procedure. ([scholarly chronology](https://classiques.uqam.ca/classiques/De_tocqueville_alexis/tocqueville_reperes/reperes_chronologiques.html))

### Colonial and antislavery writings

- **1837:** *Première lettre sur l’Algérie*. Newspaper letter intervening in French colonial policy and electoral debate; its epistolary form is public and programmatic rather than private correspondence. ([UQAM text inventory](https://classiques.uqam.ca/classiques/De_tocqueville_alexis/de_tocqueville.html); [scholarly chronology](https://classiques.uqam.ca/classiques/De_tocqueville_alexis/tocqueville_reperes/reperes_chronologiques.html))
- **1837:** *Deuxième lettre sur l’Algérie*. Second public colonial-policy letter, printed separately from the first and therefore a distinct intervention. ([UQAM text inventory](https://classiques.uqam.ca/classiques/De_tocqueville_alexis/de_tocqueville.html); [Johns Hopkins contents](https://books.google.com/books/about/Writings_on_Empire_and_Slavery.html?id=LL-X6oxqm2EC))
- **1839:** *Rapport fait au nom de la commission chargée d’examiner la proposition de M. de Tracy, relative aux esclaves des colonies*. Parliamentary report advocating a legislated path to emancipation; it is a collective institutional instrument voiced and drafted by Tocqueville, not simply an essay. ([Tocqueville Foundation concordance](https://tocquevillefoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Tocqueville-Esclavage.pdf))
- **1841:** *Travail sur l’Algérie*. Substantial policy study composed after the first Algerian journey; unfinished as a book yet complete enough to operate as a colonial program. ([Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm); [UQAM text inventory](https://classiques.uqam.ca/classiques/De_tocqueville_alexis/de_tocqueville.html))
- **1843:** *De l’émancipation des esclaves*. Six newspaper articles published in *Le Siècle* and functioning together as a public campaign for colonial emancipation; the series is one intervention made through serial manifestations. ([Tocqueville Foundation concordance](https://tocquevillefoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Tocqueville-Esclavage.pdf); [Johns Hopkins description](https://books.google.com/books/about/Writings_on_Empire_and_Slavery.html?id=LL-X6oxqm2EC))
- **1845:** Intervention on the law governing enslaved people in the colonies. Parliamentary speech delivered on 30 May; an enacted political intervention preserved as text, not a separately conceived publication. ([Tocqueville Foundation concordance](https://tocquevillefoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Tocqueville-Esclavage.pdf))
- **1847:** *Premier rapport sur l’Algérie*. Parliamentary commission report joining investigation, colonial diagnosis, and recommendations; authorship is institutionally shared even though Tocqueville was its principal drafter and reporter. ([Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm); [Johns Hopkins contents](https://books.google.com/books/about/Writings_on_Empire_and_Slavery.html?id=LL-X6oxqm2EC))
- **1847:** *Second rapport sur l’Algérie*. Companion parliamentary report with a distinct argument and legislative occasion, likewise both Tocquevillean text and commission document. ([Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm); [Johns Hopkins contents](https://books.google.com/books/about/Writings_on_Empire_and_Slavery.html?id=LL-X6oxqm2EC))

### Political articles, programs, and manifestos

- **1843:** *Lettres sur la situation intérieure de la France*. Serial newspaper letters diagnosing the July Monarchy; together they form a public political intervention rather than a book. ([Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm); [scholarly chronology](https://classiques.uqam.ca/classiques/De_tocqueville_alexis/tocqueville_reperes/reperes_chronologiques.html))
- **1840s:** *La centralisation administrative et le système représentatif*. Short political study on the relation between centralized administration and representative government. ([Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm))
- **1840s:** *De la classe moyenne et du peuple*. Short political analysis preserved among Tocqueville’s political writings; the standard edition does not present it as a book. ([Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm))
- **1844–1845:** Contributions to *Le Commerce*. Editorial direction and unsigned or collectively mediated journalism make this a political publishing project whose individual textual authorship is not always securely separable; it should not be converted into a fictitious list of signed works. ([scholarly chronology](https://classiques.uqam.ca/classiques/De_tocqueville_alexis/tocqueville_reperes/reperes_chronologiques.html))
- **1847:** Draft program for *La Jeune Gauche*. An unfinished governmental manifesto prepared for Tocqueville’s parliamentary group; it remained an ébauche, so the project—not a nonexistent finished platform—is the work. ([scholarly chronology](https://classiques.uqam.ca/classiques/De_tocqueville_alexis/tocqueville_reperes/reperes_chronologiques.html))
- **1848:** *Rapport sur « La démocratie en Suisse » de Cherbuliez*. Critical report on Antoine-Élisée Cherbuliez’s study, made for the Académie des sciences morales et politiques; it is review, institutional judgment, and political analysis at once. ([Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm))

### Speeches as political acts

- **1839–1851:** Parliamentary speeches and interventions. The complete-edition corpus encompasses speeches on Algeria, slavery, prisons, constitutional design, education, foreign affairs, socialism, and other legislation; each was an oral political act whose surviving “text” may be Tocqueville’s draft, an official transcript, or both, so the corpus resists treatment as a shelf of authored books. ([Gallimard edition structure](https://classiques.uqam.ca/classiques/De_tocqueville_alexis/tocqueville_biblio/toc_biblio.html))
- **27 January 1848:** *Discours prononcé le 27 janvier 1848*. Chamber speech warning that France was approaching revolution; it functioned as an immediate political alarm and therefore approaches a manifesto more closely than a descriptive essay. ([Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm); [scholarly chronology](https://classiques.uqam.ca/classiques/De_tocqueville_alexis/tocqueville_reperes/reperes_chronologiques.html))
- **12 September 1848:** *Discours prononcé le 12 septembre 1848*. Constituent Assembly speech opposing a constitutional “right to work”; a deliberative act directed at the wording and meaning of the new constitution. ([Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm); [Cambridge bibliography reference](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/tocqueville-and-the-frontiers-of-democracy/bibliography-of-works-cited/109CFCAF218E9BAF8178F435BD24415B))
- **1849:** Speech to the Legislative Assembly of 20 June. Foreign-policy speech made while Tocqueville was minister of foreign affairs; office, occasion, and oral delivery make it partly a governmental act rather than solely a personal literary work. ([Lévy collected-edition contents](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/%C5%92uvres_compl%C3%A8tes_d%E2%80%99Alexis_de_Tocqueville%2C_L%C3%A9vy/%C3%89tudes_%C3%A9conomiques%2C_politiques_et_litt%C3%A9raires))

### Academic addresses

- **1841:** *Discours à l’Académie française*. Tocqueville’s reception address, a ceremonial and institutional speech rather than a treatise. ([Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm))
- **1838–1852:** Addresses and interventions at the Académie des sciences morales et politiques. An institutional corpus—including interventions on slavery and the public annual address of 1852—whose individual manifestations were oral performances before becoming printed texts. ([Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm); [Académie des sciences morales et politiques](https://academiesciencesmoralesetpolitiques.fr/les-academiciens-de-1832-a-nos-jours/les-academiciens-de-1832-a-nos-jours-section-ii/alexis-de-tocqueville/))

### Diplomatic papers

- **June–October 1849:** Ministerial dispatches, instructions, memoranda, and diplomatic correspondence. These texts were made by Tocqueville as foreign minister but speak through the state and may involve secretarial or collective drafting; they are governmental acts whose personal authorship can be textual, institutional, or both. ([Gallimard edition bibliography](https://classiques.uqam.ca/classiques/De_tocqueville_alexis/tocqueville_biblio/toc_biblio.html); [Cambridge contents](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/tocqueville-on-america-after-1840/speeches-articles-and-diplomatic-papers/651C9389ADC55986437E8689F2E01F02))

### Correspondence and working papers

- **1820s–1859:** Correspondence. Thousands of letters to family, friends, intellectual collaborators, constituents, politicians, and foreign correspondents form an epistolary body of work rather than one text; Gallimard’s critical edition distributes it among English, foreign, family, local, and named-correspondent series, and completeness remains contingent on surviving and accessible letters. ([Gallimard edition structure](https://classiques.uqam.ca/classiques/De_tocqueville_alexis/textes_economiques/textes_economiques_biblio.html); [selected-letters description](https://books.google.com/books?id=dwDWCAhP5EMC))
- **1820s–1859:** Reading notes, documentary extracts, marginalia, outlines, discarded drafts, and miscellaneous pensées. These are workshop manifestations preserved among Tocqueville’s papers; many have no authorial title or stable boundary and resist being promoted retrospectively into independent “works.” ([Library of Congress contents of the Beaumont edition](https://www.loc.gov/item/15010567/); [Gallimard/Pléiade contents](https://www.catalogue-pleiade.fr/Tocqueville.dG.htm))
