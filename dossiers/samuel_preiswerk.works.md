## Works by Samuel Preiswerk

### Hebrew-language scholarship

- **1838:** *Grammaire hébraïque, précédée d’un précis historique sur la langue hébraïque*. A French-language Hebrew grammar and historical survey, conceived as a teaching manual rather than a literary book. Later revised editions—including the second edition of 1864—are manifestations of the same obra, not separate works ([Personenlexikon Basel-Landschaft](https://personenlexikon.bl.ch/Samuel_Preiswerk); [1864 edition record](https://www.livre-rare-book.com/book/5472703/15699)).

- **Date uncertain; extracted from the grammar:** *Paradigmes du verbe parfait, des verbes imparfaits et des noms de la langue hébraïque, extraits de la grammaire hébraïque*. A separately issued grammatical table or instructional aid derived from the larger grammar; it therefore resists classification as a wholly independent obra ([Deutsche Nationalbibliothek authority-linked work listing](https://d-nb.info/gnd/116282150); [bibliographic notice](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Preiswerk)).

### Periodical project and contributions

- **1838–1843:** *Das Morgenland: Altes und Neues für Freunde der heiligen Schrift*. A six-year periodical project, beginning in June 1838, which Preiswerk edited and to which he contributed; it is a continuing collaborative publication rather than a single-authored book, and individual unsigned items cannot automatically be assigned to him ([Allgemeine Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz38549.html#adbcontent); [digitized first volume](https://books.google.com/books/about/Das_Morgenland.html?id=WiVIAJTKJQYC); [Personenlexikon Basel-Landschaft](https://personenlexikon.bl.ch/Samuel_Preiswerk)).

### Hymnological and editorial works

- **1844:** *Evangelischer Liederkranz aus älterer und neuerer Zeit*. A 270/271-item hymn anthology containing sixteen hymns by Preiswerk. The first edition was anonymously compiled by his friends Emanuel Eglinger and Carl Friedrich Zimmermann, not by Preiswerk himself; it is therefore principally a manifestation of his hymn corpus rather than his authored book, although a later edition was reportedly supervised by him ([Allgemeine Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz38549.html#adbcontent); [Hymnary, citing John Julian’s *Dictionary of Hymnology*](https://hymnary.org/person/Preiswerk_Samuel); [Personenlexikon Basel-Landschaft](https://personenlexikon.bl.ch/Samuel_Preiswerk); [1844 bibliographical description](https://www.zvab.com/%C3%A4lterer-neuerer-Zeit-Herausgegeben-Emauel-Eglinger/18011536693/bd)).

- **1854:** *Evangelisches Gesangbuch für Kirche, Schule und Haus in Basel-Stadt und Basel-Land*. An official communal hymnal made by an editorial body; Preiswerk was one of its editors, so this is collaborative liturgical infrastructure rather than an individually authored obra ([*Handbook to the Mennonite Hymnary*](https://www.cyberlibrary.org/en/books/handbook-to-the-mennonite-hymnary-54966/chapters/8/); [1854 edition record](https://lancasterlibrary.moravian.edu/bib/100008)).

### Hymn and devotional-poem texts

The standard hymnological accounts state that sixteen Preiswerk hymns appeared in the 1844 *Evangelischer Liederkranz*, but the consulted sources do not supply a secure complete concordance of all sixteen. The following are the individually identifiable texts; titles in quotation marks are conventional titles derived from their opening lines.

- **1829:** *Die Sach’ ist dein, Herr Jesu Christ*. A missionary hymn whose first two stanzas are Preiswerk’s; the familiar third stanza is attributed to Felician von Zaremba, so the expanded hymn is a composite work rather than Preiswerk’s alone ([Hymnary](https://hymnary.org/text/die_sach_ist_dein_herr_jesu_christ); [*Handbook to the Mennonite Hymnary*](https://www.cyberlibrary.org/en/books/handbook-to-the-mennonite-hymnary-54966/chapters/8/); [Liederdatenbank](https://liederdatenbank.strehle.de/artist/9250)).

- **By 1844:** *Das ist der Gemeine Stärke*. A five-stanza mission hymn, numbered 98 in the 1844 *Evangelischer Liederkranz*; later sources sometimes alter its opening to *Dies ist der Gemeinde Stärke*, which John Julian identifies as a misquotation ([Hymnary, citing Julian](https://hymnary.org/person/Preiswerk_Samuel)).

- **By 1844:** *Einer nur ist’s ewig wert*. A Christological hymn or devotional poem included in Preiswerk’s hymn tradition; a posthumous memorial prints the variant opening *Einer ewig nur ist werth* ([Liederdatenbank](https://liederdatenbank.strehle.de/artist/9250); [1871 memorial, pp. 39–40](https://zop.zb.uzh.ch/bitstreams/9f44a49c-c5b1-41a5-adde-244ffc72c62d/download)).

- **By 1844:** *Wir treten in das neue Jahr*. A New Year hymn. Hymnological databases attribute the German text to Preiswerk, but the consulted standard accounts do not provide a more exact composition date ([Hymnary](https://hymnary.org/person/Preiswerk_Samuel)).

- **By 1844:** *Wohlan, wir schlagen Hand in Hand*. A pilgrimage-and-discipleship hymn; it was used as the concluding poetic text in Preiswerk’s 1871 memorial, confirming attribution but not its original date ([Hymnary](https://hymnary.org/person/Preiswerk_Samuel); [1871 memorial, pp. 29–30](https://zop.zb.uzh.ch/bitstreams/9f44a49c-c5b1-41a5-adde-244ffc72c62d/download)).

- **Date unknown:** *Volk des Herrn, du hast hienieden*. A hymn on the church’s earthly struggle, preserved among the selected songs appended to Preiswerk’s memorial. The appendix gives no composition date, so none can safely be supplied ([1871 memorial, pp. 35–36](https://zop.zb.uzh.ch/bitstreams/9f44a49c-c5b1-41a5-adde-244ffc72c62d/download)).

- **Date unknown:** *Was Menschen herrlich nennen*. A devotional poem contrasting worldly appearance with grace and Scripture; it survives in the memorial appendix without a fixed title beyond its opening line ([1871 memorial, pp. 36–37](https://zop.zb.uzh.ch/bitstreams/9f44a49c-c5b1-41a5-adde-244ffc72c62d/download)).

- **Date unknown:** *Treuer Jesu! alle Tage*. A personal devotional hymn framed as an address to Jesus. Its surviving memorial manifestation supplies neither an original title nor a date ([1871 memorial, pp. 37–38](https://zop.zb.uzh.ch/bitstreams/9f44a49c-c5b1-41a5-adde-244ffc72c62d/download)).

- **Date unknown:** *Wenn ich auch im Schattenthale*. A death-and-consolation hymn preserved in the posthumous selection; the memorial also cites its final stanza in the funeral address ([1871 memorial, pp. 5, 38–39](https://zop.zb.uzh.ch/bitstreams/9f44a49c-c5b1-41a5-adde-244ffc72c62d/download)).

- **Date unknown:** *Schlangenkopf-Zertreter*. A devotional hymn addressed to God in explicitly biblical imagery. Its heading may be an incipit rather than an authorially fixed title ([1871 memorial, pp. 40–41](https://zop.zb.uzh.ch/bitstreams/9f44a49c-c5b1-41a5-adde-244ffc72c62d/download)).

- **Date unknown:** *In Sorgen*. A short devotional poem or hymn whose heading describes its occasion; it is closer to a situational prayer-text than to a formally titled independent book-work ([1871 memorial, pp. 41–42](https://zop.zb.uzh.ch/bitstreams/9f44a49c-c5b1-41a5-adde-244ffc72c62d/download)).

- **1868:** *Friede heißt: der Streit ist aus*. A short devotional poem on death and peace, explicitly said in the memorial biography to have been written in 1868. No evidence establishes that the opening line was Preiswerk’s formal title ([1871 memorial, pp. 21–22](https://zop.zb.uzh.ch/bitstreams/9f44a49c-c5b1-41a5-adde-244ffc72c62d/download)).

- **Date unknown:** *Dunkel sind des Ew’gen Wege*. A hymn or devotional poem on providence, printed without a separate heading in the posthumous appendix; treating its first line as a title is therefore editorial convenience, not proof of an authorial title ([1871 memorial, p. 42](https://zop.zb.uzh.ch/bitstreams/9f44a49c-c5b1-41a5-adde-244ffc72c62d/download)).

- **Date unknown:** *Vor der Predigt*. A prayer in verse intended for use before preaching: functionally a liturgical protocol as well as a poem, because it prepares and directs an act rather than merely describing one ([1871 memorial, p. 42](https://zop.zb.uzh.ch/bitstreams/9f44a49c-c5b1-41a5-adde-244ffc72c62d/download)).

### Theological essay

- **Before 1862; edition cited 1862:** *Der Heilige Geist und die christliche Kirche*. A theological essay published in *Zur Verantwortung des christlichen Glaubens*, pp. 214–233 of the second edition. The consulted bibliography dates that edition, not necessarily the essay’s first composition or first publication ([Personenlexikon Basel-Landschaft](https://personenlexikon.bl.ch/Samuel_Preiswerk)).

### Works reported but not individually recoverable

- **Various dates, chiefly before 1844:** *Sixteen hymns included in the Evangelischer Liederkranz*. This is a documented body of work, but not all sixteen texts can be securely named from the consulted hymnological reference and surviving posthumous selection; translations, altered incipits, and later composite versions further complicate the count. The corpus therefore resists conversion into sixteen confidently titled independent entries ([Hymnary, citing John Julian](https://hymnary.org/person/Preiswerk_Samuel); [Allgemeine Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz38549.html#adbcontent)).

- **1821–1871:** *Sermons and pastoral addresses*. Preiswerk’s memorial describes fifty years of preaching and teaching, but no collected-works edition or scholarly catalogue identifying each sermon was found in the consulted bibliography. This is an extensive performed and manuscript textual practice, not a safely enumerable set of published works ([1871 memorial](https://zop.zb.uzh.ch/bitstreams/9f44a49c-c5b1-41a5-adde-244ffc72c62d/download)).
