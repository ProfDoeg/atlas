## Works by Friedrich Ludwig Jahn

### Political, linguistic, and geographical writings

- **1800:** *Über die Beförderung des Patriotismus im Preußischen Reiche*. A political program or manifesto, published pseudonymously as “D. C. C. Höpffner”; the attribution to Jahn is accepted by the standard collected edition and subsequent bibliographies. [Euler, vol. 1](https://books.google.com/books?id=p9WoorO3rNwC), [Meyers Konversations-Lexikon](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/MKL1888:Jahn)
- **1806:** *Bereicherung des Hochdeutschen Sprachschatzes, versucht im Gebiete der Sinnverwandtschaft*. A lexicographical and language-reform work: not merely a dictionary, but a project for enlarging and differentiating German vocabulary. [Euler, vol. 1](https://books.google.com/books?id=p9WoorO3rNwC), [Mainz sport-history bibliography](https://download.uni-mainz.de/fb02-sport-mueller/downloads/LennartzB2_Personen.pdf)
- **Date uncertain; printed 1884:** *Über Briefschreiben*. A short reflection on letter-writing preserved and first made generally available in Euler’s collected edition; its independent-work status is uncertain because it survives there as a small posthumous text rather than as an authorized publication. [Euler, vol. 1, contents](https://books.google.com/books?id=p9WoorO3rNwC)
- **1809–1810:** *Subskriptions-Anzeige des Deutschen Volkstums*. A prospectus and solicitation for subscribers: an operative publishing text rather than the book it advertises. [Euler, vol. 1, contents](https://books.google.com/books?id=p9WoorO3rNwC)
- **1810:** *Deutsches Volksthum*. A large political, educational, ethnographic, and linguistic program for a unified German nation; its mixture of description, prescriptions, and mobilizing rhetoric makes it part treatise, part manifesto. [Euler, vol. 1](https://books.google.com/books?id=p9WoorO3rNwC), [Deutsches Textarchiv](https://www.deutschestextarchiv.de/book/show/jahn_volksthum_1810), [Neue Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/pnd118556622.html)
- **1814:** *Runenblätter*. A sequence of political aphorisms and polemical “leaves,” formed from the crisis of the Napoleonic wars; it is closer to a manifesto-cycle than to a continuous treatise. [Euler, vol. 1](https://books.google.com/books?id=p9WoorO3rNwC), [Wikisource bibliography](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Friedrich_Ludwig_Jahn)
- **17 August 1814:** *Über die Notwendigkeit eines besonderen Unterrichts für die aus dem Felde zur Wissenschaft zurückgekehrten Freiwilligen*. An educational memorandum submitted to the ministry—a proposal for institutional action, not originally a commercial book. It was later printed with Jahn’s legal self-defence. [Mainz sport-history bibliography, item 23971](https://download.uni-mainz.de/fb02-sport-mueller/downloads/LennartzB2_Personen.pdf)
- **Spring 1817:** *Vorträge über deutsches Volkstum*. A public lecture series and therefore an oral manifestation rather than a fixed text; surviving reports do not establish a single authorially finalized transcript. [Neue Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/pnd118556622.html)
- **1827:** *Wegweiser in das preußische Sachsenland und Rahmen zu den Lebensbildern aus dem preußischen Sachsenlande des Dr. W. Harnisch*. A polemical geographical and historical corrective to two works by Wilhelm Harnisch. It is simultaneously a review, a counter-map of regional memory, and an argument about how Prussian Saxony should understand itself. [Mainz sport-history bibliography, item 23974](https://download.uni-mainz.de/fb02-sport-mueller/downloads/LennartzB2_Personen.pdf), [Jahrbuch für Antisemitismusforschung](https://www.static.tu.berlin/fileadmin/www/10002032/Jahrbuecher/Jahrbuch_2016.pdf)
- **1828:** *Neue Runen-Blätter, 1. Rolle*. A second collection of political, geographical, and military-national reflections. “1. Rolle” announces a serial project, but no completed continuation followed, so the larger project remained unfinished. [Euler, vol. 2](https://play.google.com/store/books/details?id=xgSauqTYMy8C), [Wikisource bibliography](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Friedrich_Ludwig_Jahn), [Mainz sport-history bibliography, item 23975](https://download.uni-mainz.de/fb02-sport-mueller/downloads/LennartzB2_Personen.pdf)
- **1833:** *Merke zum Deutschen Volksthum*. A collection of admonitions and extensions to *Deutsches Volksthum*, conceived as an intervention against contemporary liberalism, cosmopolitanism, emigration, and “Young Germany”; its imperative character makes it a manifesto as much as an essay collection. Sources vary between 1832, the period of composition, and 1833, the publication date. [Euler, vol. 2, pp. 469–721](https://play.google.com/store/books/details?id=xgSauqTYMy8C), [Wikisource bibliography](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Friedrich_Ludwig_Jahn), [Bartmuß and Ulfkotte](https://dokumen.pub/nach-dem-turnverbot-quotturnvaterquot-jahn-zwischen-1819-und-1852-9783412214050-9783412207342.html)
- **1 November 1833 onward:** *Briefe an die Auswanderer*. Nine newspaper installments in the *Weißensee’r allgemeines Unterhaltungsblatt*, polemicizing against German emigration. They imitate letters but operate publicly as a serialized political intervention; Euler’s research found the newspaper copies only as discarded sheets, so the transmission is precarious. [Jahrbücher der deutschen Turnkunst](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/7b/Jahrb%C3%BCcher_der_deutschen_Turnkunst_-_Bl%C3%A4tter_f%C3%BCr_die_Angelegenheiten_des_deutschen_Turnwesens._Volume_32_%28Volume_5_of_new_series%29%2C_1886._%28IA_s1127id1376029%29.pdf), [Bartmuß and Ulfkotte](https://dokumen.pub/nach-dem-turnverbot-quotturnvaterquot-jahn-zwischen-1819-und-1852-9783412214050-9783412207342.html)
- **1837** *(some early accounts say 1836)*: *Leuwagen für Dr. Heinrich Leo*. A named polemical pamphlet directed against the historian Heinrich Leo; the variation reflects composition or announcement versus publication dating. [Mainz sport-history bibliography, item 23978](https://download.uni-mainz.de/fb02-sport-mueller/downloads/LennartzB2_Personen.pdf), [Allgemeine Deutsche Biographie](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/ADB:Jahn,_Friedrich_Ludwig)
- **1845:** *Mittelgart*. An antiquarian-historical essay printed in the eighth annual report of the Altmärkischer Verein; it was also part of a much larger research project that Jahn attempted to reconstruct after losing papers in the fire of 1838. [Mainz sport-history bibliography, item 23979](https://download.uni-mainz.de/fb02-sport-mueller/downloads/LennartzB2_Personen.pdf), [Konrad Dürre’s account](https://wintersonnenwende.com/scriptorium/deutsch/archiv/grossedeutsche/gdtJahn.html)
- **1848:** *Schwanenrede*. A printed political declaration associated with the Frankfurt Parliament and the September crisis. Despite its title, it was never delivered: it is a speech-project whose only historical manifestation was print. [Allgemeine Deutsche Biographie](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/ADB:Jahn,_Friedrich_Ludwig), [Mainz sport-history bibliography, item 23980](https://download.uni-mainz.de/fb02-sport-mueller/downloads/LennartzB2_Personen.pdf)

### Gymnastics as protocol, environment, and social system

- **1810–1811:** *The outdoor exercise excursions and the Hasenheide Turnplatz*. Jahn first organized excursions involving games and physical exercise and in 1811 established Berlin’s public Hasenheide exercise ground. This obra was a practiced environment—people, apparatus, rules, vocabulary, clothing, excursions, and collective discipline—not a single text or building, and it became the first durable manifestation of his *Turnen* project. [Neue Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/pnd118556622.html), [Meyers Konversations-Lexikon](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/MKL1888:Jahn)
- **From 1811:** *Turnen*. A coined or revived name and a pedagogical-national system of bodily practices, developed collectively at the Hasenheide under Jahn’s direction. Jahn’s traditional title as sole “inventor” overstates the case: assistants, pupils, earlier gymnastic educators, and especially Ernst Eiselen participated in making the system. [Meyers Konversations-Lexikon](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/MKL1888:Jahn), [Neue Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/pnd118556622.html)
- **1811–1819:** *Hasenheide apparatus and exercise repertory*. Horizontal bars, parallel bars, climbing and balancing equipment, games, and named exercises formed a material-and-verbal system developed on the Turnplatz. Later tradition assigns particular apparatus to Jahn, but the historical obra was collaborative, so attribution of every device or exercise to him individually is unsafe. [Meyers Konversations-Lexikon](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/MKL1888:Jahn), [Deutsche Turnkunst](https://www.deutschestextarchiv.de/book/show/jahn_turnkunst_1816)
- **1816:** *Die Deutsche Turnkunst zur Einrichtung der Turnplätze*. Co-created with Ernst Eiselen, this is a protocol for establishing and operating exercise grounds: it names exercises and apparatus, records the movement’s development, and prescribes organization and conduct. Jahn must not be treated as its sole author. [Deutsches Textarchiv](https://www.deutschestextarchiv.de/book/show/jahn_turnkunst_1816), [Wikisource bibliography](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Friedrich_Ludwig_Jahn), [Neue Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/personen/friedrich-ludwig-jahn-118556622/)
- **1847:** *Friedrich Ludwig Jahn’s Deutsche Turnkunst. Zum zweiten Male und sehr vermehrt herausgegeben, 1. Hälfte*. An expanded remaking of the 1816 protocol, issued under Jahn’s name but anonymously edited, probably by Hans Ferdinand Maßmann with assistance from other gymnasts. Only the exercise-descriptive first half appeared; the promised didactic-methodological second half remained an unfinished project, and the extent of Jahn’s direct authorship is disputed. [Wikisource bibliography](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Friedrich_Ludwig_Jahn), [Bartmuß and Ulfkotte](https://dokumen.pub/nach-dem-turnverbot-quotturnvaterquot-jahn-zwischen-1819-und-1852-9783412214050-9783412207342.html)

### Memoir, journey narrative, and self-fashioning

- **1813; first printed 1870:** *Denknisse aus dem Jahre 1813*. Recollections or memorial sketches of the liberation-war year, transmitted posthumously; their delayed printing makes the distinction between the date of experience, composition, and publication essential. [Euler, vol. 1, contents](https://books.google.com/books?id=p9WoorO3rNwC), [Mainz sport-history bibliography, item 23982](https://download.uni-mainz.de/fb02-sport-mueller/downloads/LennartzB2_Personen.pdf)
- **Date uncertain; printed in the collected works:** *Der Geleiter*. A journey or remembrance narrative included by Euler among Jahn’s writings. Its status resists easy classification because the surviving collected-edition form does not by itself establish a separate authorized publication or a secure composition date. [Euler, vol. 1, contents](https://books.google.com/books?id=p9WoorO3rNwC)
- **Date uncertain; printed in the collected works:** *Der Abend in Mattiach*. A short travel-memory narrative preserved by the collected edition; no secure independent publication date should be inferred from its posthumous placement. [Euler, vol. 1, contents](https://books.google.com/books?id=p9WoorO3rNwC)
- **Date uncertain; printed in the collected works:** *Die Fahrt durch das Gerau zum Jettenbühel*. A journey narrative whose title names movement through a landscape. Like the neighboring memoir pieces in Euler, it may be a component or textual manifestation of a broader autobiographical project rather than an autonomous book. [Euler, vol. 1, contents](https://books.google.com/books?id=p9WoorO3rNwC)
- **1835:** *Denknisse eines Deutschen, oder Fahrten des Alten im Bart*. A collection of autobiographical journey narratives issued by Karl Schöppach. Contemporary bibliography says that Schöppach wrote it down from Jahn’s oral narration, so it is a co-produced oral-to-print manifestation and cannot safely be described as an ordinary book written wholly in Jahn’s hand. [Allgemeine Deutsche Biographie](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/ADB:Jahn,_Friedrich_Ludwig), [CiNii bibliographic record](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/author/DA03714750)

### Songs, poems, and collective publications

- **1813:** *Deutsche Wehrlieder für das Königlich-Preußische Frei-Corps*. A wartime song project associated with the Lützow corps. Bibliographies place it among Jahn’s primary works, but individual textual responsibility within the collective repertory requires caution. [Berlin-Brandenburg Academy, primary-literature list](https://berlinerklassik.bbaw.de/personen/549)
- **1817:** *Dank- und Denk-Lieder zur Jahresfeier der Leipziger Schlacht*. A collective commemorative song publication to which Jahn contributed; because it was made “with others,” the volume is not wholly his obra and individual items should not be reassigned to him without separate evidence. [Wikisource bibliography](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Friedrich_Ludwig_Jahn)
- **Dates uncertain:** *Rundenlieder*. A body of communal songs associated with Jahn in the scholarly primary-literature bibliography. It resists treatment as one fixed work because the title denotes a song group or social repertory rather than a securely bounded authorial book. [Berlin-Brandenburg Academy, primary-literature list](https://berlinerklassik.bbaw.de/personen/549)
- **Dates uncertain; published posthumously in 1884:** *Gedichte von Friedrich Ludwig Jahn*. A small posthumous group of poems communicated by Rudolf Juet in the *Deutsche Turn-Zeitung*. The source establishes a corpus but not that Jahn himself designed it as a collection. [Mainz sport-history bibliography, item 23983](https://download.uni-mainz.de/fb02-sport-mueller/downloads/LennartzB2_Personen.pdf)

### Legal and epistolary texts

- **9 October 1824; published 1863:** *Selbstvertheidigung*. Jahn’s manuscript defence in the political prosecution against him, later edited and published by Eduard Burckhardt. It is an operative forensic text—made to secure acquittal—whose book form is a posthumous manifestation rather than Jahn’s original publication. [Mainz sport-history bibliography, items 23973 and 23981](https://download.uni-mainz.de/fb02-sport-mueller/downloads/LennartzB2_Personen.pdf), [Wikisource bibliography](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Friedrich_Ludwig_Jahn)
- **1806–1852:** *Correspondence*. Jahn made a large, dispersed body of personal, political, pedagogical, and organizational letters. No historical edition is complete: Wolfgang Meyer’s supposedly complete 1913 edition was followed by editions of previously unpublished material, and the Friedrich-Ludwig-Jahn-Gesellschaft is seeking letters for a new comprehensive edition; “the correspondence” is therefore an open archival corpus, not a finished authorial work. [Friedrich-Ludwig-Jahn-Museum](https://jahn-museum.de/en/gesucht-briefe-von-friedrich-ludwig-jahn-1778-1852-fuer-eine-neue-gesamtausgabe-der-jahnbriefe/), [*Turnvater* Jahn und sein patriotisches Umfeld: Briefe und Dokumente 1806–1812](https://books.google.com/books/about/Turnvater_Jahn_und_sein_patriotisches_Um.html?id=EWoiAQAAIAAJ)

### Organizations and collective political projects

- **1814:** *Berlinische Gesellschaft für deutsche Sprache*. Jahn participated in founding this language society. It was a collective institutional obra intended to intervene in language use, not something attributable to Jahn alone. [Meyers Konversations-Lexikon](https://www.retrobibliothek.de/retrobib/seite.html?id=108712)
- **1810s:** *The Turnbewegung*. Jahn initiated and organized a network of exercise grounds, instructors, pupils, vocabulary, customs, and political-pedagogical aims. It is best understood as a collective social project or Gesamtkunstwerk of bodies, spaces, texts, apparatus, dress, journeys, festivals, and nationalist organization—not as a discrete work owned by one maker. [Neue Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/pnd118556622.html), [Meyers Konversations-Lexikon](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/MKL1888:Jahn)

### Lost or unfinished projects

- **Before 1810:** *Earlier manuscript of Deutsches Volksthum*. Jahn reportedly lost an initial manuscript after the battle of Jena and reconstructed the work largely from memory. The lost manuscript is a vanished state of the project, not another securely recoverable text. [Konrad Dürre’s biographical account](https://wintersonnenwende.com/scriptorium/deutsch/archiv/grossedeutsche/gdtJahn.html)
- **Before 1838:** *History of the Thirty Years’ War*. Preparatory materials for an intended history were destroyed in the fire that consumed Jahn’s Freyburg residence and library. It survives only as evidence of a project; there is no complete work to title or reconstruct. [Meyers Konversations-Lexikon](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/MKL1888:Jahn)
- **Before 1838:** *History of the Lützow corps*. Preparatory materials for an intended history of the volunteer corps were likewise destroyed in the 1838 fire. The intention is documented, but completeness, final title, and textual extent cannot be supplied. [Meyers Konversations-Lexikon](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/MKL1888:Jahn)
- **Before and after 1838:** *Mittelgart research project*. A larger antiquarian undertaking, partly destroyed in the fire and subsequently resumed; only the 1845 essay provides a secure published manifestation. Treating the projected whole as a completed book would erase precisely its fragmentary condition. [Konrad Dürre’s biographical account](https://wintersonnenwende.com/scriptorium/deutsch/archiv/grossedeutsche/gdtJahn.html), [Mainz sport-history bibliography, item 23979](https://download.uni-mainz.de/fb02-sport-mueller/downloads/LennartzB2_Personen.pdf)
