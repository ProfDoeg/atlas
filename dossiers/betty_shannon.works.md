## Works by Mary Elizabeth Moore Shannon

### Research protocol and musical system

- **15 November 1949:** *Composing Music by a Stochastic Process*. Co-authored with John R. Pierce as Bell Telephone Laboratories Technical Memorandum MM-49-150-29, this is simultaneously a research report, a compositional protocol, and a probabilistic system for generating four-part tonal harmony; the resulting music is better understood as the system’s manifestation than as music composed solely by either author. Contemporary scholarship confirms the memorandum’s authorship and date while emphasizing how unusual Shannon’s named credit was for a woman employed as a Bell Labs “computer.” ([Yu and Varshney](https://www.ideals.illinois.edu/items/111923/bitstreams/366650/data.pdf))

### Collaborative apparatus

- **1950 (publicly demonstrated by 1952):** *Theseus*. A maze-learning electromechanical apparatus conventionally attributed to Claude Shannon, but Mary Elizabeth Moore Shannon wired both the mouse and the relays beneath its maze; it is therefore a collaborative obra whose design authorship and fabrication authorship are not identical. Sources differ between 1950 for its initial construction and 1952 for the documented demonstration or museum object. ([MIT News](https://news.mit.edu/2007/shannon-0530), [Morven Museum & Garden](https://sale.morven.org/online/ma-bell/gallery4/claude-shannon), [IEEE Information Theory Society obituary](https://www.itsoc.org/sites/default/files/2021-01/67nits02-JunerWeb-lowres.pdf))

### Weaving manual

- **1980:** *Weavers’ Wisdom: 250 Aids to Happier Weaving*. A 23-page practical weaving monograph edited by Rosita Corey and Mary Elizabeth Shannon and issued as Weavers’ Guild of Boston Monograph no. 2; it is a collective handbook or protocol rather than a single-author book. Some bibliographies credit only the Guild, while the Guild’s own instructional bibliography names Corey and Shannon as editors. ([Weavers’ Guild of Boston bibliography](https://www.weaversguildofboston.org/_files/ugd/c50db8_b78ed82b8ca74e45ac7203fa3b087bd3.pdf), [contemporary bibliographic review](https://www2.cs.arizona.edu/patterns/weaving/periodicals/wj_39.pdf))

### Textile and weaving draft

- **Made by September–October 2000; exact date and formal title unrecorded:** *“Betty Shannon’s twill draft” and accompanying woven sample*. This point-twill draft translates the pitches and durations of Claude Debussy’s *The Girl with the Flaxen Hair* into threading and sett; the resulting linen textile is a material manifestation of musical notation, not merely an illustration or written text. The quoted designation is the magazine caption and may not be a title assigned by Shannon herself. ([*Handwoven*, September/October 2000](https://www.datocms-assets.com/75077/1687812818-hw_sep_oct_2000_emagazine.pdf))

### Uncatalogued weaving oeuvre

- **Approximately the 1950s–1990s:** *Forty years of handwoven textiles and experiments in computerized handweaving*. This body of work resists item-by-item treatment as named works: the available biographical and Guild sources document sustained production and early experiments with computerized weaving, but no catalogue raisonné, object list, dates, or stable titles have been published, so individual textiles cannot responsibly be invented from the record. ([IEEE Information Theory Society obituary](https://www.itsoc.org/sites/default/files/2021-01/67nits02-JunerWeb-lowres.pdf), [Boston Globe obituary](https://www.legacy.com/us/obituaries/bostonglobe/name/mary-shannon-obituary?id=15651343))

### Uncredited editorial and mathematical collaboration

- **From 1948; extent and terminal date uncertain:** *Editorial and computational work on Claude E. Shannon’s papers*. This is labor that made other texts possible but resists classification as a separately attributable work: accounts state that Betty Shannon took dictation, edited manuscripts, checked calculations, and served as a mathematical interlocutor, yet neither the affected papers nor her individual contributions were formally itemized or credited. It should therefore be recorded as an incompletely documented collaborative project, not silently converted into co-authorship of Claude Shannon’s bibliography. ([IEEE Information Theory Society Newsletter](https://www.itsoc.org/sites/default/files/2021-01/NITS-Dec2019_web.pdf))
