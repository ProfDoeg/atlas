## Works by Vittorio Meano

### Architectural obras and projects

- **1890–1904:** *Teatro Colón, Buenos Aires*. A building-project and an unfinished collaborative obra: Meano inherited Francesco Tamburini’s design, substantially reworked its volume and programme, secured approval for definitive plans in 1892, and directed construction until his death; Jules Dormal subsequently altered and completed the interiors before the 1908 opening. It therefore resists attribution to Meano alone, although the surviving plan archive bears his studio’s stamp. ([Molinos and Sabugo](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9789873411212_A42612673/preview-9789873411212_A42612673.pdf); [Buenos Aires City Government](https://buenosaires.gob.ar/gcaba_historico/noticias/se-presento-el-libro-carpeta-de-planos-teatro-colon-de-buenos-aires-1892); [Vissi d’Arte catalogue](https://www.italiateatrilatinoamerica.it/upload/VissiDArte-Catalogo.pdf))

- **1895–1904:** *Palacio del Congreso de la Nación Argentina*. A prize-winning competition project developed into a monumental public building under Meano’s direction from 1896; construction began in 1898, but Dormal continued it after Meano’s death, the building opened in 1906, and later work continued until 1946. “Meano’s Congress” consequently names both his architectural project and a materially posthumous, multi-author building. ([Cámara de Diputados](https://www3.hcdn.gob.ar/dependencias/secparl/dgral_info_parlamentaria/dip/estudios-investigaciones/estudios-investigaciones-historicas/edificio-del-congreso-de-la-nacion.pdf); [Molinos, “El edificio para el Congreso Nacional”](https://publicacionescientificas.fadu.uba.ar/index.php/anales/article/download/27/32/))

- **1904:** *Proyecto de Palacio para erigirse en la ciudad de Montevideo* (competition motto *Agraciada*). An architectural project for Uruguay’s Palacio Legislativo, selected from the 1904 international competition only after Meano’s death. The realized palace is not simply his obra: José Vittone and Antonio Banchini adapted the plans, and Gaetano Moretti substantially revised the elevations and introduced the central lantern. ([Molinos, “El edificio para el Congreso Nacional”](https://publicacionescientificas.fadu.uba.ar/index.php/anales/article/download/27/32/); [Sociedad de Arquitectos del Uruguay](https://www.sau.org.uy/wp-content/uploads/OBRAS-FINALISTAS-PREMIOS-Y-MENCIONES_con-autores.pdf))

- **No securely established date; before 1904:** *Meano’s house on Rodríguez Peña near Rivadavia, Buenos Aires*. A modest domestic building designed for himself and the place where he died. It is mentioned in accounts drawing on Instituto de Arte Americano research, but the consulted standard monograph’s accessible synopsis does not supply a fixed title or date; it should therefore remain a described building rather than acquire an invented name. ([La Nación](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/seguridad/cronicas-del-crimen-el-asesinato-de-vittorio-meano-el-arquitecto-del-congreso-y-del-colon-nid2307725/))

### Graphic architectural bodies

- **1892–1902:** *Plans for the Teatro Colón*. A body of large-format architectural drawings rather than a book: plans, elevations, sections, interior studies, and construction details produced through Meano’s office. The extant sheets include authentic studio stamps, but “studio” production makes individual draughtsmanship difficult to assign sheet by sheet. ([Buenos Aires City Government](https://buenosaires.gob.ar/gcaba_historico/noticias/los-planos-originales-del-teatro-colon-que-se-convirtieron-en-libro); [CPAU](https://cpau.opac.com.ar/pergamo/opac.php?a=bsqMateria&n=MEANO%2C+VITTORIO+%281860-1904%29))

### Publications and architectural texts

- **1892:** *El Nuevo Teatro Colón: planos definitivos aprobados por el Superior Gobierno con decreto fecha 10 de septiembre de 1892*. An illustrated project publication or architectural dossier credited to Víctor Meano and concessionaire Ángel Ferrari; it documents the approved design rather than constituting a separate building. ([Molinos and Puppo, “80 teatros de Vittorio Meano”](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/10915/154514/Documento_completo.pdf?sequence=1))

- **1900:** *El Nuevo Teatro Colón y los principales teatros del mundo*. An illustrated comparative study of approximately eighty theatres, assembled to explain and defend the Colón project. It is simultaneously a book, a typological research corpus, and a textual manifestation of Meano’s architectural project—not merely a description of the eventual building. ([University of the Republic repository](https://repositorio.fadu.edu.uy/items/dcd25df3-174e-4199-984d-2cfda2603175); [Molinos and Puppo](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/handle/10915/150033))

- **1904:** *Génesis del Congreso (1895)*. An architectural essay published in *Revista Técnica—Arquitectura*, I, no. 4, retrospectively setting out the genesis of Meano’s Congress design. Its parenthetical date belongs to the project discussed, not to the article’s publication. ([Molinos, “El edificio para el Congreso Nacional”](https://publicacionescientificas.fadu.uba.ar/index.php/anales/article/download/27/32/))

- **1904:** *Proyecto de Palacio para erigirse en la ciudad de Montevideo*. A printed competition portfolio issued under the pseudonym *Agraciada*. This is also the documentary manifestation of the Palacio Legislativo project listed above, not evidence for an additional architectural work. ([Molinos, “El edificio para el Congreso Nacional”](https://publicacionescientificas.fadu.uba.ar/index.php/anales/article/download/27/32/))

### Collaborative work not securely separable as Meano’s obra

- **1884–1890:** *Work in Francesco Tamburini’s architectural office*. Meano contributed to an office responsible for major public commissions, including the unification and enlargement of the Casa Rosada, but the standard Meano study treats him as Tamburini’s collaborator and does not establish a separable Meano project for each commission. This professional labour existed, but resists conversion into a list of individually authored obras. ([Molinos and Sabugo](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9789873411212_A42612673/preview-9789873411212_A42612673.pdf); [Italia–Argentina Cultural Atlas](https://italiaargentina.com/es/resources/cultural-atlas/architettura-quartiere))
