## Works by Johann Jost Textor

### Juridical obra

- **[1761]:** *Dissertatio inauguralis iuridica de caussis et sententiis quae secundum statuta et privilegia illustris reipublicae Moeno-Francofurtensis appellationem non admittunt: ad illustrationem T. XLIII P. 1 Def. Francof.* A 96-page Latin doctoral dissertation on Frankfurt judgments from which no appeal was permitted—the only publication assigned to Textor in the VD18 record located for him. The imprint is bibliographically dated “[1761],” although the biographical literature dates his doctorate to 1760; this discrepancy should remain visible rather than be resolved by conjecture. ([Deutsche Digitale Bibliothek/VD18](https://www.deutsche-digitale-bibliothek.de/item/VYDAT56D6KNKDGFNDIX4WAO4W6GPPA2O); [Frankfurter Personenlexikon](https://frankfurter-personenlexikon.de/node/1466))

### Disputed attribution: occasional print

- **17 February 1766:** *Gedicht zur Vermählung von Textor mit Maria Magdalena Möller* [catalogue title]. Kalliope attributes this four-page German wedding poem to Johann Jost Textor and records a copy annotated by Johann Friedrich Heinrich Schlosser, but the attribution is not secure: the catalogue title makes Textor the bridegroom as well as the alleged author, while a separate Yale description records a printed ribbon celebrating the same marriage without naming an author. It therefore resists inclusion as a confirmed obra by Textor and is retained only as a disputed catalogue attribution. ([Kalliope](https://kalliope.staatsbibliothek-berlin.de/ead?ead.id=DE-611-HS-4337277); [Yale, *Goethe and Goetheana Collection* finding aid, p. 7](https://ead-pdfs.library.yale.edu/1883.pdf))
