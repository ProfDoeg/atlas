## Works by Guido Keller

### Published texts

- **13 June 1920:** *La nuova scuola. Amore*. A programmatic article published in *La Testa di Ferro*, presenting education as a union of practical and contemplative life; its numbered propositions make it closer to a pedagogical manifesto than to a descriptive essay. [S1]
- **8 August 1920:** *A chi l’onore?*. An article published in *La Testa di Ferro*, documented under Keller’s name in the scholarly literature and reprinted in the collected writings. [S2]
- **1920:** *Per una vita dell’arte*. A polemical article arguing for an art liberated from bourgeois convention; the precise original issue and date are not supplied by the accessible scholarly discussion. [S3]
- **4 December 1920:** *Arte aumana*. An anti-Marinettian statement published in the fourth and last issue of *Yoga*. The text is attributed to Keller in recent scholarship, but the original periodical printed its texts anonymously, so the attribution is not typographically explicit in the first edition. [S3; S4]
- **1920 and undated:** *Articles in La Testa di Ferro, Yoga, and other Fiuman or futurist publications*. Alessandro Gnocchi’s collected-works edition claims to assemble all Keller’s writings, including previously unpublished material, but its publicly accessible publisher description does not expose a complete contents list; this corpus therefore cannot responsibly be decomposed here into additional invented or conjectural titles. [S5]

### Manifestos and collective declarations

- **13 November 1920:** *Il Fior di Loto – Il Movimento della Yoga*. The programmatic manifesto opening the first issue of *Yoga*, reprinted with the title *Movimento Yoga* on 4 December. The documentary catalogue assigns it collectively or alternatively to the anonymous editors Giovanni Comisso and Guido Keller and warns that it has sometimes been confused with the separate leaflet *Fondazione a Fiume della Yoga*; individual authorship is consequently unresolved. [S4]
- **November 1920:** *Fondazione a Fiume della Yoga*. A separate foundation manifesto issued as a leaflet, not the same text as *Il Movimento della Yoga*. Keller’s participation follows from his co-foundership and direction of Yoga, but the accessible catalogue evidence does not establish sole authorship. [S4]
- **1920:** *Lo Stato e il diritto*. A philosophical-juridical commentary developed from the definition of the state in the Charter of Carnaro. It was prepared for printing in 1920 but remained unpublished during Keller’s lifetime; its project-status and unfinished publication history are therefore part of the work. [S6]
- **1920:** *Movement texts published anonymously in Yoga*. These include *Del discutere*, *Dei giovani*, *Del Governo*, *Eleviamo l’orgoglio italiano!*, *Dell’uomo d’armi. Il Legionario*, and *Imposizione del principio europeo*. Because *Yoga* left these texts unsigned and was jointly directed by Keller and Comisso, they belong to the collective manifestation of the movement but cannot safely be entered as Keller’s individual works. [S4]

### Periodicals and editorial Gesamtkunstwerke

- **13 November–4 December 1920:** *Yoga = Unione di Spiriti Liberi Tendenti alla Perfezione*. A four-number weekly periodical jointly founded and directed by Keller and Giovanni Comisso. It joined manifestos, polemics, poetry, politics, typography, symbols, gatherings, and schemes for collective living, and is thus better understood as a short-lived Gesamtkunstwerk or movement-organ than as a book by either editor; most contributions were unsigned, making the division of authorship uncertain. [S4; S7]
- **15 June 1920:** *Il Ballo di S. Vito: Primo Quaderno “Yoga”*. A one-off printed object for the Yoga festivity, directed as a series by Mino Somenzi. It contains a dedication and an aphorism on love attributed to Keller, but the whole quaderno is collaborative and cannot be assigned to him alone. [S8]

### Organizations and forms of collective life

- **1920:** *Yoga, Unione di spiriti liberi tendenti alla perfezione*. A movement founded by Keller with Giovanni Comisso before becoming the four-issue periodical of the same name. Neither a conventional political party nor merely a text, it was an experimental association combining naturism, spiritual and artistic revolt, public discussion, festivity, and political provocation. [S7; S9]

### Festivals, spectacles, and unrealized projects

- **29 February–15 June 1920:** *Assalto al Castello d’Amore*. A festival-spectacle devised by Keller and Comisso, announced in *La Testa di Ferro* and associated with the Ballo di San Vito. Behind its scenario of women defending a seaside “castle” against pirate-costumed legionaries lay a reported plan to abduct Luisa Baccara; the project was discovered and never carried out, so the intention—not a completed performance—is the obra. [S4]
- **15 June 1920:** *Ballo di San Vito*. A Yoga festivity joining dance, printed matter, costumes, and political-theatrical play. Keller’s participation is documented through the associated quaderno and the jointly devised *Castello d’Amore*, but surviving descriptions do not justify treating every element of the event as his sole creation. [S4; S8]
- **c. 1928–1929:** *Poema or theatre of aerial flight*. Later testimony associates Keller with a projected futurist “teatro aereo,” conceived as spectacle, popular school of courage, and aerial poem. The accessible source speaks of realizing “the poem of Guido Keller” but does not provide a fixed Keller title, finished score, or evidence that it was staged; it should therefore remain an incompletely documented project rather than be promoted to a completed work. [S10]

### Actions as manifestos

- **14 November 1920:** *Flight over Rome and allegorical gift to Parliament*. Keller flew an Ansaldo SVA over Rome, casting flowers toward the Vatican and Quirinal and a chamber pot containing vegetables toward Montecitorio, accompanied by a dedicatory message. This was an authored symbolic action—a manifesto performed through aircraft, objects, route, and text—rather than a literary work, though details of the flowers and destinations vary in later accounts. [S1; S11]
- **1920:** *Uscocco raids and requisition actions*. Keller organized or participated in raids intended to provision Fiume, including the seizure of horses and the celebrated transport of a pig by aeroplane. These actions helped create his public persona, but the evidence treats them as expeditions, thefts, or political theatre rather than stable works; their resistance to the category *obra* should be preserved. [S9]

### Lecture and autobiographical manifestation

- **Undated, before 1929:** *Nel pensiero e nelle gesta*. An autobiographical lecture attributed to Keller. The available reference supplies neither a secure delivery date nor evidence of an authorially published book, so it is properly a spoken or projected text whose surviving form and completeness remain unclear. [S12]

### Posthumous textual body

- **1933:** *Appendix of writings by Guido Keller in Sandro Pozzi, Guido Keller: nel pensiero, nelle gesta*. The volume is not a Keller-authored book, but its appendix is the earliest identified posthumous gathering of his texts. Its editorial selection should not be confused with an authorially constituted oeuvre. [S13]
- **2019:** *Ala = pensiero e azione: scritti di un rivoluzionario fiumano*. A posthumous collected-works edition edited by Alessandro Gnocchi, described by its publisher as the first collection of all Keller’s writings and as including numerous previously unpublished texts. The collection is an editorial manifestation of Keller’s textual remains, not a book designed or completed by Keller himself. [S5]
