## Works by Abu al-Qasim al-Junayd ibn Muhammad al-Baghdadi

### Surviving correspondence

- **Before 298 AH/910:** *Risāla ilā baʿḍ ikhwanihi* (“Epistle to Some of His Brethren,” first letter). A private spiritual letter preserved in Istanbul, Şehid Ali MS 1374; its lack of a named recipient and distinctive title makes it an act of correspondence rather than a conventional “book.” ([Abdel-Kader, pp. 58–60](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **Before 258 AH/871:** *Risāla ilā Yaḥyā ibn Muʿādh al-Rāzī*. A letter cast partly as a dialogue about mystical knowledge, addressed to the preacher and ascetic Yaḥyā ibn Muʿādh; Abdel-Kader distinguishes it from another excerpt addressed to Yaḥyā that al-Sarrāj transmits. ([Abdel-Kader, pp. 59–60](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **Before 298 AH/910:** *Risāla ilā baʿḍ ikhwanihi* (“Epistle to Some of His Brethren,” second letter). An untitled letter to unnamed fellow practitioners, also cited by al-Sarrāj; it survives as correspondence without an independently fixed work-title. ([Abdel-Kader, pp. 59–60](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **Before 298 AH/910:** *Risāla ilā Abī Bakr al-Kisāʾī al-Dīnawarī*. A spiritual letter whose manuscript witness preserves only the ending; al-Sarrāj supplies a fuller version, so its present textual object is composite and incomplete. ([Abdel-Kader, pp. 59–60](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **Before 298 AH/910:** *Untitled epistle beginning on fol. 33a of Şehid Ali MS 1374*. A short letter or treatise for which the manuscript provides neither a title nor an identified recipient; naming it more precisely would manufacture information the source does not contain. ([Abdel-Kader, pp. 59–60](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **Before 298 AH/910:** *Risāla ilā ʿAmr ibn ʿUthmān al-Makkī*. An incomplete letter to the Meccan Sufi ʿAmr ibn ʿUthmān; material following it in the manuscript addresses an Abū ʿAbd Allāh and therefore does not belong securely to this letter. ([Abdel-Kader, pp. 59–60](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **Before 298 AH/910:** *Risāla ilā Yūsuf ibn al-Ḥusayn al-Rāzī*. An incomplete letter to Yūsuf ibn al-Ḥusayn. It must not be confused with the adjacent reply by Yūsuf, which Brockelmann and others mistakenly attributed to Junayd. ([Abdel-Kader, pp. 59–61](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **Before 298 AH/910:** *Risāla ilā baʿḍ ikhwanihi* (“Epistle to Some of His Brethren,” transmitted by Abū Nuʿaym, pp. 260–61). A letter preserved by quotation in Abū Nuʿaym’s *Ḥilyat al-awliyāʾ*, not as an autonomous early manuscript; its boundaries are consequently those imposed by the later compiler. ([Abdel-Kader, p. 60](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **Before 298 AH/910:** *Kitāb al-Junayd ilā Abī al-ʿAbbās al-Dīnawarī*. A letter to Abū al-ʿAbbās al-Dīnawarī preserved by Abū Nuʿaym; *kitāb* here means a written communication, not necessarily a book. ([Abdel-Kader, p. 60](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **Before 298 AH/910:** *Kitāb al-Junayd ilā Abī Isḥāq al-Māristānī*. A letter to Abū Isḥāq al-Māristānī known through quotation in the *Ḥilyat al-awliyāʾ*. ([Abdel-Kader, p. 60](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **Before 298 AH/910:** *Risāla ilā baʿḍ ikhwanihi* (“Epistle to Some of His Brethren,” transmitted by Abū Nuʿaym, p. 279). One of several separately transmitted letters with the same generic rubric; the surviving evidence does not license a more distinctive title. ([Abdel-Kader, p. 60](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **Before 298 AH/910:** *Another Risāla ilā baʿḍ ikhwanihi*. Abdel-Kader counts a further letter to unnamed brethren among the pieces preserved by Abū Nuʿaym. Its identity is resistant to modern work-cataloguing because neither recipient nor original title is recoverable. ([Abdel-Kader, p. 60](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **Before 258 AH/871:** *Fragment of a letter to Yaḥyā ibn Muʿādh*. Al-Sarrāj preserves part of this communication; Abdel-Kader explicitly treats it as distinct from the complete letter to Yaḥyā in Şehid Ali MS 1374. ([Abdel-Kader, p. 60](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))

### Surviving mystical treatises and protocols

- **Before 298 AH/910:** *Dawāʾ al-arwāḥ* (“The Cure of Souls”). A short spiritual treatise surviving in both Istanbul and Cairo witnesses and extensively in Abū Nuʿaym. One transmission assigns material associated with it to al-Ḥārith al-Muḥāsibī, but Abdel-Kader judges its style Junayd’s and allows that Junayd may have recast something learned from his teacher. ([Arberry’s manuscript account](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-the-royal-asiatic-society/article/abs/book-of-the-cure-of-souls/9D5D009F4FE65B0F5DB3D2460632FE84); [Abdel-Kader, pp. 59–60](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **Before 298 AH/910:** *Kitāb al-Fanāʾ* (“Book of Annihilation”). A doctrinal discourse on mystical annihilation and continued existence in God, structured partly as a dialogue rather than as a systematic book in the later sense. ([Abdel-Kader’s edition, p. 152 onward](https://merton.bellarmine.edu/files/original/dde4cbafed0452d8e375fa0a80097ec1a93ba8ee.pdf); [Qudsi](https://www.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2%3A1614605/FULLTEXT01.pdf))
- **Before 298 AH/910:** *Kitāb al-Mīthāq* (“Book of the Covenant”). A doctrinal treatise interpreting the primordial covenant of Qurʾān 7:172 as the pre-eternal ground of mystical return. ([Qudsi](https://www.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2%3A1614605/FULLTEXT01.pdf); [Abdel-Kader, p. 60](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **Before 298 AH/910:** *Kitāb fī al-Ulūhiyya* (“On Divinity”). A compact theological-mystical treatise preserved within the *Rasāʾil* manuscript corpus. Its rubric describes a subject rather than necessarily recording an authorial title. ([Abdel-Kader, p. 60](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **Before 298 AH/910:** *Kitāb fī al-farq bayna al-ikhlāṣ wa-l-ṣidq* (“On the Difference Between Sincerity and Truthfulness”). A discriminative spiritual treatise defining two closely related ethical states. ([Abdel-Kader, p. 60](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **Before 298 AH/910:** *Bāb ākhar fī al-tawḥīd* (“Another Chapter on Divine Unity”). A short doctrinal chapter on realized divine unity; its heading marks it as part of a textual sequence rather than an unquestionably autonomous book. ([Abdel-Kader, p. 60](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **Before 298 AH/910:** *Eight questions on tawḥīd*. Eight brief *masāʾil*—juridically shaped questions and answers on divine unity—follow the preceding chapter in the manuscript; one is independently quoted by al-Qushayrī. They resist treatment as eight securely titled works, since seven carry only variants of “another question” and the last “final question”; they are best understood as a textual suite. ([Abdel-Kader, pp. 57, 60](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **Before 298 AH/910:** *Adab al-muftaqir ilā Allāh* (“The Conduct of One Who Is in Need of God”). A protocol of spiritual discipline: it instructs the practitioner how to distinguish thoughts arising from God, the lower self, and Satan and how to act upon them. ([Abdel-Kader’s edition and translation](https://merton.bellarmine.edu/files/original/dde4cbafed0452d8e375fa0a80097ec1a93ba8ee.pdf); [later scholarly discussion](https://www.deepdyve.com/browse/journals/0955-2340/2021/v32/i1?rows=10&start=0))
- **Before 298 AH/910:** *Kitāb Dawāʾ al-tafrīṭ* (“Book of the Remedy for Spiritual Negligence”). A separately transmitted spiritual treatise preserved in Mingana Arabic (Islamic) MS 905 at Birmingham and, for much of its text, in Abū Nuʿaym. It therefore survives through more than one material manifestation rather than through the principal Istanbul collection alone. ([Abdel-Kader, p. 60](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))

### Commentary, verse, sayings, and oral manifestations

- **Before 298 AH/910:** *Sharḥ shaṭaḥāt Abī Yazīd al-Bisṭāmī* (“Explanation of Abū Yazīd al-Bisṭāmī’s Ecstatic Utterances”). An interpretive commentary quoted at length by al-Sarrāj; the autonomous written object is no longer preserved, but Abdel-Kader accepts the quoted material as Junayd’s on stylistic and doctrinal grounds. ([Abdel-Kader, pp. 57, 60](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **Before 298 AH/910:** *Qaṣīda ṣūfiyya* (“Sufi Ode”). A poem attributed to Junayd in Berlin MS 7542. Abdel-Kader had not examined the manuscript and therefore left its authenticity unresolved; it belongs in the atlas as an attributed poetic object, not as a securely authenticated work. ([Abdel-Kader, p. 60](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **Before 298 AH/910; collected later:** *Sayings, definitions, prayers, and occasional verse transmitted under Junayd’s name*. This is a dispersed body of oral teaching preserved by al-Sarrāj, Abū Nuʿaym, al-Qushayrī, and other later compilers, not a book Junayd assembled. It resists being called a single work: the individual utterances may be his manifestations in speech, but their selection, wording, and textual arrangement belong partly to transmitters. ([Abdel-Kader, pp. 54–58](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html); [Salamah-Qudsi](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/34621F44EB600B6C7DDDE1ACBB016CB2/S0041977X20002657a.pdf/exchange_of_letters_in_early_sufism_a_preliminary_study.pdf))

### Works reported but not preserved

- **Before 298 AH/910; now lost:** *Kitāb Amthāl al-Qurʾān* (“Book of the Similitudes of the Qurʾān”). Ibn al-Nadīm attributes this work to Junayd, and Abdel-Kader lists it among the lost works; no surviving text permits its scale, exact form, or authorship to be tested. ([Abdel-Kader, pp. 57, 60](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **Before 298 AH/910; now lost:** *Taṣḥīḥ al-irāda* (“Correction of the Will”). A work reported by al-Hujwīrī but no longer extant; it was presumably concerned with disciplining or rectifying spiritual intention, though its exact kind and contents cannot now be established. ([Abdel-Kader, pp. 57, 60](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **Before 298 AH/910; now lost:** *Kitāb al-Munājāt* (“Book of Intimate Prayers”). A devotional composition cited by al-Sarrāj. Because only its report or excerpt survives, it cannot be determined whether it was a unified authored book, a prayer, or a later collection of Junayd’s invocations. ([Abdel-Kader, pp. 57, 60](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **Before 298 AH/910; now lost:** *Muntakhab al-asrār fī ṣifāt al-ṣiddīqīn wa-l-abrār* (“Selected Mysteries Concerning the Qualities of the Truthful and the Righteous”). A work cited by Ibn ʿArabī and accepted by Abdel-Kader among the lost titles; its title may describe an excerpt or selection rather than an original authorial book. ([Abdel-Kader, p. 60](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))

### Disputed and rejected attributions

- **Date unknown; attribution rejected:** *Reply to Junayd’s letter to Yūsuf ibn al-Ḥusayn*. Brockelmann and A. J. Arberry initially treated this as a letter by Junayd, but the manuscript marks it as Yūsuf’s reply, parallel passages are assigned to Yūsuf by Abū Nuʿaym and al-Qushayrī, and its named teachers and style also point to Yūsuf. It is therefore not an obra made by Junayd, though its former placement in his corpus is part of its history. ([Abdel-Kader, pp. 60–61](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **After Junayd; attribution rejected:** *Risāla fī al-sukr* (“Epistle on Intoxication”). Massignon and Brockelmann attributed it to Junayd, but the text refers to Junayd in the third person among other Sufis and is in a different hand; Arberry and Abdel-Kader consequently identify it as a later composition. ([Abdel-Kader, p. 61](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **After Junayd; attribution rejected:** *Risāla fī al-ifāqa* (“Epistle on Recovery/Sobriety”). The companion of the preceding epistle was likewise once assigned to Junayd but refers to him as an earlier authority and is regarded as the product of a later writer. ([Abdel-Kader, p. 61](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **After 395 AH/1004–05; attribution rejected:** *Kitāb al-qaṣd ilā Allāh* (“Book of the Journey/Intention toward God”). Ḥājjī Khalīfa attributed this work to Junayd, but Nicholson found an internal date later than 395 AH, almost a century after Junayd’s death; it cannot be his. ([Abdel-Kader, p. 61](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **Date unknown; attribution rejected:** *Maʿālī al-himam* (“The Exalted Aspirations”). Transmitted in the same manuscript context as *Kitāb al-qaṣd*, it cites people who lived after Junayd and even mentions Junayd himself; Arberry assigned it instead to an Abū al-Qāsim al-ʿĀrif. ([Abdel-Kader, p. 61](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **Date unknown; attribution rejected:** *Al-Sirr fī anfās al-ṣūfiyya* (“The Secret Concerning the Breaths of the Sufis”). Although attributed to Junayd in later bibliography, Arberry’s examination concluded that it was certainly not his work. ([Abdel-Kader, p. 61](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **Date unknown; attribution rejected:** *Ḥikāyāt* (“Stories”). Brockelmann listed this under Junayd, but Abdel-Kader identifies it as a work associated with Junayd’s disciple al-Khuldī rather than something Junayd made. ([Abdel-Kader, p. 60](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
- **Compiled after Junayd; not an authored work:** *Al-Mutafarriqāt al-maʾthūra ʿan al-Junayd wa-l-Shiblī wa-Abī Yazīd al-Bisṭāmī* (“Dispersed Traditions from Junayd, al-Shiblī, and Abū Yazīd”). This title denotes a later assemblage of sayings attributed to three masters, not a text composed by Junayd; its resistance to “work” status is categorical, not merely bibliographical. ([Abdel-Kader, p. 60](https://pdfcoffee.com/abul-qasim-al-junayd-frpdf-pdf-free.html))
