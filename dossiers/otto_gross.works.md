## Works by Otto Gross

### Medical handbooks and monographs

- **1901:** *Compendium der Pharmako-Therapie für Polikliniker und junge Ärzte*. A practical pharmacotherapy handbook for outpatient clinicians and young physicians—the most conventionally book-like of Gross’s early works. ([Werkverzeichnis](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2); [contemporary publisher’s notice](https://digital.slub-dresden.de/data/kitodo/Brsfded_39946221X-19020111/Brsfded_39946221X-19020111_tif/jpegs/Brsfded_39946221X-19020111.pdf))
- **1902:** *Die cerebrale Sekundärfunction*. A neurological-psychiatric monograph proposing a theory of “cerebral secondary function”; it is a sustained theoretical obra rather than a clinical manual. ([Werkverzeichnis](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2))
- **1909:** *Über psychopathische Minderwertigkeiten*. A book-length psychiatric and psychopathological study, published by Braumüller in Vienna and Leipzig. ([Werkverzeichnis entry](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2/19-ueber-psychopathische-minderwertigkeiten))

### Medical, neurological, and psychiatric papers

- **1901:** *Zu den cardiorenalen Theorien*. A short medical journal article on cardio-renal theories. ([Complete Bibliography](https://ottogross.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/Otto_Gross-Bibliography.170423.pdf))
- **1901:** *Zur Frage der socialen Hemmungsvorstellungen*. A criminological-psychiatric journal article on socially inhibiting mental representations. ([Complete Bibliography](https://ottogross.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/Otto_Gross-Bibliography.170423.pdf))
- **1902:** *Über Vorstellungszerfall*. A psychopathological journal article on the disintegration of mental representations. ([Werkverzeichnis entry](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2/5-ueber-vorstellungszerfall))
- **1902:** *Die Affektlage der Ablehnung*. A psychiatric paper on the affective condition associated with rejection or refusal. ([Complete Bibliography](https://ottogross.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/Otto_Gross-Bibliography.170423.pdf))
- **1902:** *Zur Phyllogenese der Ethik*. A short interdisciplinary paper that treats ethics through an evolutionary and psychopathological framework. ([Complete Bibliography](https://ottogross.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/Otto_Gross-Bibliography.170423.pdf))
- **1903:** *Beitrag zur Pathologie des Negativismus*. A clinical-psychiatric contribution on negativism. ([Werkverzeichnis](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2))
- **1903:** *Über die Pathogenese des spezifischen Wahns bei Paralytikern*. A short clinical paper on the origin of a specific delusional syndrome in patients with general paralysis. ([Werkverzeichnis](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2))
- **1904:** *Ein Todesfall infolge von latentem Aneurysma arteriae vertebralis*. A medical case report concerning a fatal, previously latent vertebral-artery aneurysm. ([Werkverzeichnis](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2))
- **1904:** *Über Bewußtseinszerfall*. A psychiatric paper on the disintegration of consciousness. ([Werkverzeichnis entry](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2/11-ueber-bewusstseinszerfall))
- **1904:** *Zur Biologie des Sprachapparates*. A substantial neuropsychiatric article on the biological organization of language. ([Werkverzeichnis entry](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2/12-zur-biologie-des-sprachapparates))
- **1904:** *Zur Differentialdiagnostik negativistischer Phänomene*. A two-part clinical paper on the differential diagnosis of negativistic phenomena. ([Werkverzeichnis entry](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2/13-zur-differentialdiagnostik-negativistischer-phaenomene))
- **1904:** *Zur Nomenclatur „Dementia sejunctiva“*. A terminological intervention proposing or defending a psychiatric diagnostic name. ([Werkverzeichnis entry](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2/14-zur-nomenclatur-dementia-sejunctiva))
- **1907:** *Das Freud’sche Ideogenitätsmoment und seine Bedeutung im manisch-depressiven Irresein Kraepelins*. A psychiatric paper applying a Freudian causal concept to Kraepelin’s category of manic-depressive illness. ([Werkverzeichnis](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2))
- **1908:** *Die cerebrale Sekundärfunktion. Autoreferat*. Gross’s own abstract or summary of his earlier monograph, prepared for the published proceedings of the 1907 Amsterdam international congress; it is a derivative self-report but still a distinct text. ([Werkverzeichnis](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2))
- **1908:** *[Diskussionsbeiträge]*. Recorded interventions in discussions at the 1907 Amsterdam congress, published without a single authorial title; they resist classification as a finished work because the surviving “text” is a congress record of speech. ([Werkverzeichnis](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2); [bibliographical record](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_Gross))
- **1914:** *Über Destruktionssymbolik*. A psychoanalytic journal article on destructive symbolism. ([Werkverzeichnis entry](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2/28-ueber-destruktionssymbolik))
- **1915:** *Beitrag zur Kasuistik posttyphöser Systemaffektionen*. A clinical case contribution on systemic disorders following typhus. ([Werkverzeichnis](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2?start=20))

### Psychoanalytic, ethical, and social essays

- **1908:** *Elterngewalt*. A polemical essay on parental power, published in *Die Zukunft*; its interventionist purpose places it between social criticism and manifesto. ([Werkverzeichnis](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2))
- **1913:** *Zur Überwindung der kulturellen Krise*. A programmatic essay in *Die Aktion* on overcoming cultural crisis; it acts as a manifesto for joining psychological emancipation to social transformation. ([Werkverzeichnis](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2))
- **1913:** *Ludwig Rubiners „Psychoanalyse“*. A critical response to Rubiner’s treatment of psychoanalysis, published in *Die Aktion*. ([Werkverzeichnis](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2?start=20))
- **1913:** *Die Psychoanalyse oder wir Kliniker*. A polemical essay contrasting psychoanalysis with conventional clinical practice. ([Werkverzeichnis](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2?start=20))
- **1913:** *Die Einwirkung der Allgemeinheit auf das Individuum*. A social-psychological essay on collective pressure upon the individual. ([Werkverzeichnis](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2?start=20))
- **1913:** *Anmerkungen zu einer neuen Ethik*. A compressed statement toward a new ethics; its declarative and programmatic character makes it closer to a manifesto than to a neutral exposition. ([Werkverzeichnis](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2?start=20))
- **1913:** *Notiz über Beziehungen*. A brief theoretical note on human relations; its small scale is integral to the object and should not be inflated into a book or treatise. ([Werkverzeichnis](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2?start=20))
- **1916:** *Vom Konflikt des Eigenen und Fremden*. A psychoanalytic and social essay on conflict between what is experienced as one’s own and what is imposed as alien, published in *Die freie Straße*. ([Werkverzeichnis entry](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2/31-vom-konflikt-des-eigenen-und-fremden))
- **1920:** *Drei Aufsätze über den inneren Konflikt*. A book-length assemblage comprising *Über Konflikt und Beziehung*, *Über Einsamkeit*, and *Beitrag zum Problem des Wahnes*; it is one published obra made from three individually titled essays, the last incorporating case studies. ([De Gruyter bibliographical record](https://www.degruyterbrill.com/de/document/doi/10.1515/9783111414171/html); [contemporary-text record](https://de.anarchistlibraries.net/library/otto-gross-drei-aufsatze-uber-den-inneren-konflikt1))

### Revolutionary manifestos and political interventions

- **1919:** *Die kommunistische Grundidee in der Paradiessymbolik*. A psychoanalytic-revolutionary essay in *Sowjet* that reads paradise symbolism as carrying a communist idea; it deliberately refuses a clean division between clinical interpretation and political manifesto. ([Werkverzeichnis entry](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2/32-die-kommunistische-grundidee-in-der-paradiessymbolik))
- **1919:** *Zum Problem: Parlamentarismus*. A political intervention on parliamentarism, published in *Die Erde*. ([Werkverzeichnis](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2?start=20))
- **1919:** *Protest und Moral im Unbewußten*. A revolutionary-psychological essay linking unconscious protest with morality. ([Werkverzeichnis](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2?start=20))
- **1919:** *Zur funktionellen Geistesbildung des Revolutionärs*. A programmatic text on the psychological or intellectual formation of the revolutionary, issued as a supplement to the *Räte-Zeitung*; it approaches a protocol for revolutionary self-formation without becoming a literal set of instructions. ([Werkverzeichnis](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2?start=20))
- **1919:** *Orientierung der Geistigen*. A political-cultural manifesto addressed to intellectuals, published in *Sowjet*. ([Werkverzeichnis](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2?start=20))
- **1920:** *Zur neuerlichen Vorarbeit: Vom Unterricht*. An educational and revolutionary essay in *Das Forum*; “Vorarbeit” marks it as preparatory work toward a larger transformation rather than a closed system. ([Werkverzeichnis](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2?start=20))

### Public letter

- **1914:** *Der Fall Otto Groß. Brief an Maximilian Harden*. A public letter about Gross’s own confinement and legal-medical case, printed in *Die Zukunft*; it is simultaneously correspondence, testimony, self-defense, and political act. ([Werkverzeichnis](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2?start=20))

### Collaborative texts and publishing projects

- **1913:** *Sigyn*. A jointly signed text with Franz Jung announcing or articulating the projected journal *Sigyn*. Because the journal project was intended to unite individual psychology with radical social change, this item is best understood as both manifesto and trace of a larger publishing project, not simply as an article. ([Werkverzeichnis](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2?start=20))
- **1913:** *Sigyn* journal project. Gross and Franz Jung planned a periodical devoted to individual psychology and radical cultural and economic transformation. The project itself—not merely its announcement—is part of the obra, although its incomplete or unrealized publication history makes it resist treatment as a finished periodical. ([project history](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_Gross); [Werkverzeichnis](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2?start=20))
- **1916:** *Bemerkung*. A jointly signed editorial statement by Gross and Franz Jung introducing the fourth installment of their “Vorarbeit”; it acts upon its readers and contributors and is therefore an editorial manifesto rather than a descriptive essay. ([Werkverzeichnis](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2?start=20); [Franz Jung edition](https://www.nautilus-stiftung.org/Res/Doc/FranzJung/1_1.pdf))
- **1916:** *Um Weisheit und Leben. Vierte Folge der Vorarbeit*. A periodical or pamphlet assemblage edited by Gross and associated with *Die freie Straße*. It is a collective editorial object—arguably a small Gesamtkunstwerk of manifesto, arrangement, and contributions—so Gross’s making here is curatorial as well as authorial. ([Complete Bibliography](https://ottogross.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/Otto_Gross-Bibliography.170423.pdf))

### Posthumously recovered or incompletely documented texts

- **Undated; intended for publication circa 1920, first published posthumously:** *Zum Solidaritätsproblem im Klassenkampf*. A revolutionary-psychological essay apparently intended for *Die Erde*; scholarship disagrees about how it relates to other projected work mentioned by Franz Jung, and the surviving title may represent an intended rather than completed publication. It therefore resists being called an achieved lifetime work. ([Werkverzeichnis](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2?start=40); [discussion of the attribution](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/otto-gross/150-gefaehrten-otto-gross-franz-jung))
- **Undated; first published posthumously:** *Themen der revolutionären Psychologie*. A manuscript on revolutionary psychology published together with *Zum Solidaritätsproblem im Klassenkampf* in the twenty-first century. Its original date, intended venue, and degree of completion are not securely established, so the posthumous publication date must not be mistaken for its date of composition. ([Werkverzeichnis](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-werkverzeichnis-2?start=40); [publication documentation](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/database/otto-gross-bibliographie/1030?layout=print&print=1&tmpl=component))
- **By 1919; unfinished or unpublished:** *Book on the contemporary Astarte cult* [no established title]. A book project reported as being in progress, intended to interpret a modern form of the Astarte cult. No secure finished text or authorized title is established, so the project—not a conjectural book—is the work that can responsibly be listed. ([biographical-bibliographical account](https://www.dehmlow.de/index.php/de/otto-gross/65-otto-gross-biographisches-und-bibliographisches))
