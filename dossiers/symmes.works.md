## Works by John Cleves Symmes Jr.

### Foundational letter, manifesto, and memoirs

- **August 17, 1817:** *Untitled letter to Anthony Lockwood*. A private letter containing the earliest presently identified statement of Symmes’s hollow-planet idea; treating it as a “work” is debatable because it survives as correspondence rather than a prepared publication. [Griffin](https://doi.org/10.2747/0272-3646.24.5.382)
- **April 10, 1818:** *No. I—Circular*. A one-page printed manifesto announcing the concentric-sphere theory, soliciting adherents, and offering to lead a polar expedition; Symmes subsequently revised it. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **June 17, 1818; revised September 23, 1819:** *Geometry—Memoir No. II*. An illustrated scientific memoir or demonstration intended to establish the geometrical necessity of hollow planetary spheres. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **June 24, 1818:** *Geology—Memoir No. III*. A geological memoir extending the concentric-sphere hypothesis into an account of planetary formation. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **July 4, 1818:** *Allegany Mountains*. A speculative geological memorandum interpreting the mountain chain as debris from a shattered planet. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **January 18, 1819; revised October 13, 1819:** *Whole Number 6—Miscellany*. A miscellaneous memoir and self-commentary on Symmes’s developing theory and practice of continual revision. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **February 18, 1819:** *Arctic Memoir* and *Note*. A memoir assembling purported polar evidence for the theory and arguing for an advocate or expedition; the note belongs to the same textual unit in the working bibliography. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **October 23, 1819?:** *Experiment*. Diagrams and notes appended to the third memoir rather than a clearly independent essay; its date is conjectural in the bibliography. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)

### Observational and polemical newspaper texts, 1819–1820

- **July 8, 1819:** *The Weather*. A brief meteorological observation, apparently the first of a sequence connecting local weather with Symmes’s natural philosophy. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **July 17, 1819:** *Ohio Staple Commodities—Provisions*. An agricultural and meteorological newspaper article, also identified as “Remarks on the Weather—No. 2.” [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **August 19, 1819:** *On Light Between the Spheres*. An illustrated explanatory memorandum about illumination within the proposed concentric shells. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **July 8, 1819 [as catalogued]:** *Remarks on the Weather—No. 3*. A meteorological article; the bibliography’s date duplicates that of *The Weather* and may require correction from the original newspaper. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **September 15, 1819:** *Reply to D. P.’s Strictures*. A polemical newspaper reply defending the new theory against a named critic. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **September 20, 1819:** *Untitled statement on the benefits of Symmes’s discoveries*. A newspaper intervention known by description rather than a fixed title. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **September 23, 1819:** *Untitled notice concerning expeditions undertaken at the expense of Romanzow*. A short editorial letter presenting Russian exploration as support for Symmes’s proposed polar enterprise. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **September 27, 1819:** *On the Fever Prevailing about Springfield*. A medical and environmental observation that sits uneasily within an oeuvre usually defined by the hollow-earth theory. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **October 2, 1819:** *Remarks on “Galileo’s” Strictures on Mr. Tuffts’ Wooden Globe Experiment*. A polemical response defending an experimental model used in debate over the theory. The wooden globe itself is not securely attributable to Symmes. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **October 11, 1819:** *Remarks on the Blowing Up of Mines*. A short natural-philosophical newspaper text applying physical observations to Symmes’s arguments. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **October 13, 1819:** *Remarks on the Aurora Borealis*. An observational argument treating the aurora as evidence relevant to polar openings. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **October 23 or November 6, 1819:** *Supplement to the Western Spy*. A composite printed manifestation gathering revised memoirs, diagrams, and related material rather than a wholly new single text. The working bibliography dates it October 23, while the surviving photostat described by the State Historical Society of Missouri is dated November 6. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/); [SHSMO finding aid](https://files.shsmo.org/manuscripts/columbia/C3200.pdf)
- **November 1, 1819:** *Criticism on St. Pierre, &c.* A critical newspaper essay engaging Bernardin de Saint-Pierre’s natural philosophy. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **November 8, 1819:** *Answers to Cleon’s Strictures*. A serial polemical answer to objections against the theory. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **November 25, 1819:** *A Card for D. P.* A public challenge or corrective notice, subsequently printed in the *National Intelligencer* as “Cimmerian Theory.” [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **December 23, 1819:** *To the Editors*. An editorial letter forwarding or introducing an address received by Symmes; it appeared in the *National Intelligencer* on January 19, 1820. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **January 6, 1820:** *Symmes’ Theory*. An editorial communication built around “Galileo’s” reply to “Rittenhouse,” published in the *National Intelligencer* on January 26. Its dialogic or excerpting form complicates the boundary between Symmes’s own text and material he transmitted. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **July 7, 1820:** *Cursory Remarks on Symmes’s Theory*. A newspaper defense using reports of extreme cold at Council Bluffs as evidence in the geographic argument. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **September 21, 1820:** *The Pamphlet*. A notice announcing that a promised pamphlet had been delayed. It is evidence of an unfinished or unrealized publication project, not the pamphlet itself. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **December 27, 1820:** *On the Degrees of Latitude*. A geographical memorandum belonging to Symmes’s continuing public demonstration of the theory. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)

### Appeals, queries, maps, and lecture-derived texts, 1821–1824

- **March 14, 1821:** *Queries*. A set of questions rather than a descriptive treatise: a polemical protocol intended to compel opponents to answer the theory point by point. No more specific title is established. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **April 14, 1821:** *Polar Concavities*. A geopolitical and geographical newspaper argument linking prospective American territory to access into the supposed northern concavity. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **April 17, 1821:** *To the Maritime and Other Civilized Powers of the World*. A manifesto and international appeal offering Symmes’s services for a polar expedition. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **Published April 28, 1821:** *To the President of the Historical Society of New York*. An institutional appeal seeking learned attention to the theory; the bibliography supplies publication rather than composition date. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **July 31, 1821:** *To the Liberal and Enlightened*. A public subscription appeal arguing that an impoverished discoverer deserved material support. It acts as fundraising rhetoric as much as scientific exposition. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **August 1, 1821:** *To Geologists*. An open challenge asking geologists and learned bodies publicly to accept or reject the theory. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **August 14, 1821:** *Extract from a Lecture of March 25, 1820*. A newspaper manifestation of an earlier oral performance, published in the *National Intelligencer* on August 29, 1821; it is neither the whole lecture nor necessarily an exact transcript. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **August 15, 1821:** *Further Extract from the Lecture of March 25, 1820*. A second textualization of the same performance, published October 23, 1821. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **November 7, 1821:** *Extract from Lectures of February and March 1820*. A lecture-derived argument using Samuel Hearne’s observations of Arctic animals; it appeared in the *National Intelligencer* on December 10. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **November 22, 1821:** *Extract from Lectures of February and March 1820*. A separate lecture-derived argument concerning Alexander Mackenzie’s Arctic geography, published December 7. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **April 4, 1822:** *A General Request to Learned Institutions*. An open institutional challenge asking philosophical and literary societies formally to record their assent, dissent, or refusal to judge. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **July 29, 1822:** *From a London Paper*. A mediated newspaper item presented by Symmes; because it republishes or frames another paper’s material, it resists attribution as an entirely authored work. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **July 29, 1822:** *To the Editors of the Public Journals Throughout the United States*. A general press appeal designed for recirculation across newspapers. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **August 20, 1822:** *Map of the Northern Verge*. A cartographic argument depicting the supposed curvature into the northern opening. It is a map, not merely a written text, and survives within the documentary history of the theory. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **October 30, 1822:** *The Polar Verge*. A geographical essay reinterpreting John Franklin’s reported route and hardships through the geometry of Symmes’s proposed opening. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **November 6, 1822:** *Untitled remarks on the Greenland whale fishery*. A newspaper observation known by subject rather than a stable title. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **January 9, 1823:** *Remarks on Passing Events Applied as Corroborating Proofs of the New Theory of the Earth*. An evidentiary miscellany interpreting contemporary weather and ice reports as confirmation of the theory. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **January 9, 1823:** *The First Chapter of a Series of Questions*. The opening installment of an interrogative polemic addressed to doubters. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **January 24, 1823:** *Remarks on Franklin’s Arctic Expedition*. A critical geographical commentary treating Franklin’s published experiences as evidence relevant to the polar-verge claim. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **January 31, 1823:** *Untitled remarks on Dr. Newman’s work*. A composite or responsive newspaper piece that included an article from the *National Intelligencer*; its boundary as an independently authored text is uncertain. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **April 17, 1823:** *To the Editors*. A brief critical letter disputing a report that Parry’s ships had been seen off Icy Cape. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **April 17, 1823:** *Letter from Newark, Ohio*. A personal-public appeal for financial assistance while Symmes was ill and unable to continue travelling. It resists classification as a work because it is simultaneously correspondence, publicity, and emergency fundraising. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **May 29, 1823:** *To the Editors*. A geological letter construing earth-bearing icebergs as fragments broken from the planet’s concave crust. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **July 23, 1823:** *To the Editors*. A geographical letter interpreting a Russian report of open polar sea as corroboration of Symmes’s prediction. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **August 16, 1823:** *Second Series of Queries*. The second interrogative challenge to opponents, although the letter refers to an August 1 date not fully reconciled with the bibliography’s heading. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **August 26, 1823:** *Third Series of Queries*. A third installment of questions directed at critics of the earth-and-planets theory. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **Published October 7, 1823:** *Remarks on Passing Events—A Rare Exotic*. A botanical observation interpreting an unfamiliar plant near Newport, Kentucky, as possible evidence for warm regions associated with the theory. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **October 16, 1823:** *Fourth Series of Queries*. The fourth installment in the unanswered public interrogation concerning the form of Earth and the other planets. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **December 19, 1823:** *Summary of Positions*. A concise systematic statement of Symmes’s astronomical and geographical claims. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **Published May 29, 1824:** *Correspondence*. A letter to “Mr. Foote” promising an answer to Matthews’s lecture on concentric spheres; it records an intended rebuttal whose completion is not established. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **November 22, 1824:** *To the Editor of the National Gazette*. An editorial letter listed in the chronological bibliography; no more distinctive title is established there. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)

### Dialogues, appeals, and late scientific notes, 1825–1828

- **May 2, 1825:** *The Inside of the World*. A newspaper exposition of the inhabitable interior, published in the *Daily National Intelligencer*. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **June 24, 1825:** *A Dialogue Between Two Friends*. A didactic dialogue dramatizing objections and answers rather than presenting the argument as a conventional essay. - **August 4, 1825:** *A Dialogue, at Newport, in Kentucky, between a Pupil of Symmes’ School, and a Proselyte to His Theory*. A staged instructional dialogue whose speakers embody degrees of conversion to the theory. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **February 28, 1826:** *To the Editors*. A public demand that Washington audiences and the wider learned world judge the theory after Symmes’s lectures there. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **March 7, 1826:** *Reply to a Card*. A newspaper response in the *Daily National Intelligencer*, apparently arising from controversy surrounding the Washington lectures. - **June 15, 1827:** *To the Well-Wishers of the Author of the New Theory of the Earth*. A public fundraising appeal reporting that ill health had interrupted Symmes’s lecture tour through Maine and Lower Canada. - **June 27, 1827:** *Remarks on the Use of Rain-Water*. A practical or medical-natural-philosophical newspaper note published in Charleston’s *City Gazette*. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **August 26, 1827:** *Untitled note on the source of animal heat*. A physiological hypothesis locating the production of animal heat in food and the heart rather than the lungs; it is known by description, not a fixed title. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **September 14, 1827:** *Of the Magellanic Clouds*. An astronomical article linking observations of the Magellanic Clouds to Symmes’s cosmological reasoning. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **Published October 16, 1827:** *To Such of the Public as Have Read, or May Shortly Read, the Public Queries I Have Advanced Relative to the Magellan Clouds*. A defensive public address combining astronomical controversy with an appeal for institutional support of discoverers. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **October 20, 1827:** *A New Game*. A contribution to the *Pennsylvania Gazette* whose precise form is not specified in the working bibliography; the title suggests a devised game or satirical rule-system, not necessarily an essay. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **May 6, 1828:** *Appeal*. A public solicitation published in the *National Intelligencer* on May 20; it belongs to the continuing effort to finance Symmes and his proposed exploration. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **May 15, 1828:** *An Imagined Dialogue*. A fictionalized argumentative exchange, sometimes published together with the *Appeal*. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **1828?:** *Second Imagined Dialogue*. ### Oral obra and expedition project

- **February–March 1820:** *Early public lectures on the new theory of the earth*. A series of oral performances rather than one fixed text; only extracts were subsequently printed, so neither their full wording nor their exact number is recoverable from the bibliography. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **1822–1827:** *Lecture tour through the United States and British North America*. A changing performed obra in which demonstrations, maps, models, and spoken argument were assembled for different audiences. It resists division into stable individual “works,” and the surviving newspaper notices do not constitute a complete performance catalogue. [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)
- **1818–1829:** *Polar expedition to enter the earth’s interior*. An unrealized exploratory project initiated by the first circular and pursued through appeals, lectures, and lobbying. Because no Symmes expedition sailed, the intention and organizational campaign—not a completed journey—are the obra. [Griffin](https://doi.org/10.2747/0272-3646.24.5.382); [Wilbur bibliography](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/)

### Books made partly from, or attributed to, Symmes

- **1820:** *Symzonia: A Voyage of Discovery*. An anonymously published fictional voyage signed “Captain Adam Seaborn,” long attributed by some commentators to Symmes but not securely his work. Hans-Joachim Lang and Benjamin Lease instead argued for Nathaniel Ames; because the evidence is circumstantial and the title page names neither man, it belongs here only as a disputed attribution. [Lang and Lease](https://doi.org/10.2307/364490); [Online Books Page](https://onlinebooks.library.upenn.edu/webbin/book/lookupname?key=Symmes%2C%20John%20Cleves%2C%201780-1829)
- **1826:** *Symmes’s Theory of Concentric Spheres: Demonstrating That the Earth Is Hollow, Habitable Within, and Widely Open about the Poles*. A book-length exposition made by James McBride from Symmes’s theory, writings, lectures, and reported demonstrations. It is not simply a book authored by Symmes: the contemporary volume was anonymous, scholarship attributes its composition to McBride, and Symmes is best classified as its intellectual source or contributor. [Digitized 1826 text](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/54329/54329-h/54329-h.htm); [Online Books Page](https://onlinebooks.library.upenn.edu/webbin/book/lookupname?key=Symmes%2C%20John%20Cleves%2C%201780-1829)
- **1878:** *The Symmes Theory of Concentric Spheres: Demonstrating That the Earth Is Hollow, Habitable Within, and Widely Open about the Poles*. A posthumous collected-work manifestation compiled by Americus Symmes from his father’s writings. The constituent writings are Symmes’s, but the selection, organization, title, and 1878 book-object were made by his son; it should not be silently treated as a book completed by Symmes during his lifetime. [Online Books Page](https://onlinebooks.library.upenn.edu/webbin/book/lookupname?key=Symmes%2C%20John%20Cleves%2C%201780-1829)

### Sources

- Shawn P. Wilbur, [“The Hollow Earth Theories of John Cleves Symmes”](https://www.libertarian-labyrinth.org/the-mundane-system/the-hollow-earth-theories-of-john-cleves-symmes-2/). Chronological working bibliography of Symmes’s writings, arranged by date of composition. Wilbur explicitly describes the archive as unfinished, so the list above is complete only relative to the presently identified bibliography, not a claim that every lost letter or performance has been recovered.
- Duane A. Griffin, [“Hollow and Habitable Within: Symmes’s Theory of Earth’s Internal Structure and Polar Geography,” *Physical Geography* 24, no. 5 (2003), 382–397](https://doi.org/10.2747/0272-3646.24.5.382). Scholarly reconstruction based in part on the Symmes Papers in the Draper Manuscript Collection.
- Conway Zirkle, [“The Theory of Concentric Spheres: Edmund Halley, Cotton Mather, and John Cleves Symmes,” *Isis* 37, nos. 3–4 (1947), 155–159](https://doi.org/10.1086/348022). Historical study of the theory’s intellectual genealogy.
- James McBride, [*Symmes’s Theory of Concentric Spheres* (Cincinnati, 1826), digitized by Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/54329/54329-h/54329-h.htm). Contemporary book-length presentation and biographical source.
- Hans-Joachim Lang and Benjamin Lease, [“The Authorship of *Symzonia*: The Case for Nathaniel Ames,” *The New England Quarterly* 48, no. 2 (1975), 241–252](https://doi.org/10.2307/364490). Study contesting the traditional attribution of *Symzonia* to Symmes.
- University of Pennsylvania, [Online Books Page: John Cleves Symmes, 1780–1829](https://onlinebooks.library.upenn.edu/webbin/book/lookupname?key=Symmes%2C%20John%20Cleves%2C%201780-1829). Used to check the authorship status of the 1826, 1878, and disputed 1820 books, not as the basis of the chronological works list.
- State Historical Society of Missouri, [*John Cleves Symmes Papers, 1819–1887 (C3200): Finding Aid*](https://files.shsmo.org/manuscripts/columbia/C3200.pdf). Archival description of the surviving *Western Spy* supplement photostat and later Symmes materials.
