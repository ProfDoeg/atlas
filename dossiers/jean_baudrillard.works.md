## Works by Jean Baudrillard

### Scholarly projects and theses

- **1948:** *Mémoire sur Nietzsche et Luther*. A diplôme d’études supérieures thesis; no published title or surviving public text is identified in the consulted scholarly bibliography, so it remains an incompletely documented academic project. [Critical Secret](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **1966:** *Le Système des objets*. Baudrillard’s doctoral thesis under Henri Lefebvre, examined by Roland Barthes, Pierre Bourdieu, and Lefebvre; it became the substantially public obra of the 1968 book. [Critical Secret](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **1986–1987:** *L’Autre par lui-même: Habilitation*. An academic habilitation submitted in 1986 and published as a short theoretical book in 1987. [Critical Secret](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)

### Books, essays, poetry, and chronicles

- **1968:** *Le Système des objets: La consommation des signes*. A sociological-theoretical monograph derived from Baudrillard’s doctoral research, treating domestic objects as a system of signs. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1970:** *La Société de consommation: Ses mythes, ses structures*. A theoretical study of consumption as a social and symbolic system. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1972:** *Pour une critique de l’économie politique du signe*. A collection of linked theoretical essays extending political economy into sign-value. [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **1973:** *Le Miroir de la production, ou l’illusion critique du matérialisme historique*. A polemical monograph directed against productivism in Marxist theory. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1976:** *L’Échange symbolique et la mort*. A major theoretical monograph combining political economy, anthropology, psychoanalysis, and semiotics. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1977:** *L’Effet Beaubourg: Implosion et dissuasion*. A short polemical essay on the newly opened Centre Pompidou as cultural machine and implosive mass spectacle. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1977:** *Oublier Foucault*. A polemical essay whose injunction-like title gives it something of a manifesto’s force, although it remains an argument rather than a programmatic protocol. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1978:** *À l’ombre des majorités silencieuses, ou la fin du social*. An essay on “the masses” and the implosion of the social; bibliographies variously date the original booklet to 1978 and the expanded Denoël-Gonthier publication to 1982, so those manifestations should not be silently conflated. [Critical Secret](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html), [University of Graz](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1978:** *L’Ange de stuc*. A small, unpaginated collection of poems—an important exception to classifying Baudrillard’s production simply as theory. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1978:** *Le PC ou les paradis artificiels du politique*. A political essay issued through *Cahiers d’Utopie*, closer to an intervention or pamphlet than a conventional monograph. [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **1979:** *De la séduction*. A theoretical essay that turns “seduction” into a challenge to production, desire, and interpretive mastery. [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **1981:** *Simulacres et simulation*. A collection of interconnected essays on simulation, models, signs, and hyperreality rather than a single continuous treatise. [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **1983:** *Les Stratégies fatales*. A theoretical-literary essay whose deliberately excessive propositions resist the conventions of systematic social science. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1984–1985:** *La Gauche divine: Chronique des années 1977–1984*. A political chronicle assembled from interventions on the French left; specialist sources disagree between 1984 and 1985 for its first appearance. [Critical Secret](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html), [University of Graz](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1986:** *Amérique*. A travel-philosophy book generated by journeys through the United States; it is neither conventional travel reportage nor a systematic sociological survey. [Critical Secret](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **1987:** *Cool Memories I, 1980–1985*. The first volume of Baudrillard’s dated notebooks: aphorisms, scenes, observations, and theoretical fragments rather than a continuous argument. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1989:** *Paradoxe Kommunikation*. A lecture delivered at the Kunstmuseum Bern and published in German translation; its first separate manifestation is therefore not a French-language book. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1990:** *Cool Memories II, 1987–1990*. The second notebook-volume, continuing the fragment as its constitutive form. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1990:** *La Transparence du mal: Essai sur les phénomènes extrêmes*. A collection of theoretical essays organized around phenomena that persist after their supposed liberation or completion. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1991:** *La Guerre du Golfe n’a pas eu lieu*. Three topical newspaper essays gathered as a book; its provocative title functions almost like a manifesto, but the obra is a media-theoretical intervention, not a denial that violence and deaths occurred. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1992:** *L’Illusion de la fin, ou La Grève des événements*. A theoretical essay on history, retrospection, and the apparent disappearance of meaningful endings. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1994:** *La Pensée radicale*. A very short autonomous essay or pamphlet; one bibliography dates it to 1994, while another lists a 1998 manifestation, apparently a later issue. [University of Graz](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm), [Critical Secret](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **1994–1995:** *Le Crime parfait*. A theoretical essay on the “murder” or disappearance of reality; sources disagree between 1994 and the documented Galilée edition of 1995. [Critical Secret](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html), [University of Graz](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1995:** *Fragments: Cool Memories III, 1990–1995*. The third notebook-volume, making discontinuity and the aphoristic fragment its form. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1995–1996:** *Chroniques de Libération*. A body of twice-monthly newspaper columns rather than a single titled book; later collections reuse some of this material, but the serial journalistic manifestation is itself distinct. [Critical Secret biography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **1996:** *Le Complot de l’art*. A short, intentionally inflammatory art-critical essay first issued as a pamphlet; its polemical agency makes it manifesto-like, though it advances no collective program. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1997:** *Écran total*. A collection of contemporary-media chronicles, not a newly composed continuous treatise. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1997:** *Illusion, désillusion esthétiques*. A short essay on contemporary art and aesthetic disappearance. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1998:** *À l’ombre du millénaire, ou Le Suspens de l’an 2000*. A brief essay on millennial anticipation and deferred endings. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1998:** *De l’exorcisme en politique, ou La Conjuration des imbéciles*. A pamphlet gathering two political texts first published in 1997; the book is thus a new material manifestation, not two newly written works. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1999:** *L’Échange impossible*. A theoretical monograph on the absence of any final equivalent, value, or external standpoint from which the world could be exchanged. [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **1999:** *Architecture: Vérité ou radicalité?* A separately published architectural essay whose initial book manifestation was in German translation. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **2000:** *Cool Memories IV, 1995–2000*. The fourth volume of aphoristic notebooks. [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **2000:** *Mots de passe*. A compact lexicon-like traversal of Baudrillard’s recurrent concepts; it is neither a dictionary nor a systematic introduction, but a sequence of conceptual “passwords.” [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **2001:** *Télémorphose, précédé de L’Élevage de poussière*. Two linked essays published as a short book on reality television, screens, and forced visibility. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **2002:** *Au royaume des aveugles*. A short political-cultural essay or pamphlet. The consulted bibliographies also call it *Le Royaume des aveugles*, so the title is not reported consistently. [Monoskop bibliography](https://monoskop.org/Jean_Baudrillard), [Critical Secret](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **2002:** *L’Esprit du terrorisme*. A book-length manifestation of Baudrillard’s essay on the September 11 attacks, first published in *Le Monde* in November 2001. [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **2002:** *Pataphysique*. A small book recovering Baudrillard’s early pataphysical writing; its publication date does not necessarily date the composition of every text it contains. [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **2002:** *Power Inferno*. A group of essays on terrorism, global power, and the “spirit” of political violence; some editions combine or overlap it with *L’Esprit du terrorisme*. [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **2004:** *Le Pacte de lucidité, ou L’Intelligence du Mal*. A late theoretical monograph proposing lucidity and the irreducibility of evil against projects of total transparency. [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **2005:** *Cool Memories V, 2000–2004*. The fifth and last notebook-volume published during Baudrillard’s lifetime. [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **2007:** *Pourquoi tout n’a-t-il pas déjà disparu?* A final short essay published at the end of Baudrillard’s life; sources describe its manifestation as 2007–2008, so the boundary between lifetime and posthumous publication is bibliographically unstable. [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **2008:** *Carnaval et Cannibale*. A posthumously published final essay. It is Baudrillard’s text, but the book-object is a posthumous editorial manifestation and therefore resists being treated as wholly made by him. [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)

### Dialogues, interviews, and collaborative texts

- **1983:** *Suite vénitienne / Please Follow Me*. A collaborative image-text book: Sophie Calle supplied the photographic pursuit, while Baudrillard’s *Please Follow Me* responds to it. Authorship belongs to the conjunction, not to Baudrillard alone. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1993–1994:** *Figures de l’altérité*. A dialogue or jointly authored book with Marc Guillaume; specialist bibliographies disagree between 1993 and 1994 for its first publication. [University of Graz](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm), [Critical Secret](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **1993:** *Baudrillard Live: Selected Interviews*. An edited collection assembled by Mike Gane from Baudrillard’s interviews. Baudrillard made the interview performances, but the selection and book architecture are Gane’s, so the volume resists attribution as a sole-authored work. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1997:** *Entrevues à propos du “Complot de l’art”*. A multi-participant discussion with Geneviève Breerette, Catherine Francblin, Françoise Gaillard, and others; it is a conversational manifestation rather than a sole-authored essay. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1997:** *Le Paroxyste indifférent: Entretiens avec Philippe Petit*. A book of conversations; its form and authorship arise from the exchange between interviewer and respondent. [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **2000:** *Les Objets singuliers: Architecture et philosophie*. A dialogue with architect Jean Nouvel, preserving an encounter between architectural practice and Baudrillard’s theoretical position rather than merging them into one authorial voice. [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **2001:** *D’un fragment l’autre: Entretiens avec François L’Yvonnet*. A sequence of conversations and autobiographical-theoretical fragments produced with François L’Yvonnet. [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **2003:** *La Violence du monde*. A paired intervention with Edgar Morin, issued in connection with the Institut du monde arabe; it is a dialogical response to contemporary violence, not a unified co-written treatise. [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **2004:** *Au jour le jour*. A collaborative image-text or diary-like book with Louise Merzeau, joining Baudrillard’s fragments to Merzeau’s visual practice. [The Baudrillard Dictionary bibliography](https://www.truthcloud.net/truthcloud/life-and-death/the-baudrillard-dictionary.pdf)
- **2005:** *Oublier Artaud*. Conversations with Sylvère Lotringer; the object is an interview-dialogue rather than a unilateral act of criticism. [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **2005:** *Les Exilés du dialogue*. A book of conversations with Enrique Valiente Noailles whose reversible title recalls Baudrillard’s earlier translation of Brecht’s *Dialogues d’exilés*. [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)

### Editorial constructions and returns to earlier writing

- **1963:** *Les Allemands*. An edited and introduced photobook: Baudrillard selected and presented texts around René Burri’s photographs. The photographs are Burri’s, so the Gesamtkunstwerk-like book-object is collaborative rather than Baudrillard’s photographic work. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1980:** *Enrico Baj*. An artist monograph presented by Baudrillard in French and English; his contribution is the framing text, not Baj’s artwork or the entire visual object. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1990:** *Enrico Baj: Transparence du kitsch*. An art-critical monograph on Baj, combining Baudrillard’s text with another artist’s oeuvre. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **2001:** *Le Ludique et le policier, et autres textes parus dans Utopie, 1967–1978*. A retrospective gathering of Baudrillard’s earlier *Utopie* essays; the writings are his, but their 2001 conjunction is editorial rather than a newly composed work. [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **2004:** *Cahier de l’Herne: Jean Baudrillard*. A collective critical volume containing contributions by and about Baudrillard. It resists classification as his work because the volume’s construction and much of its contents belong to its editors and other contributors. [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **2005:** *À propos d’Utopie*. A retrospective publication concerning the journal and its collective project; it is a return to, rather than a replacement for, the original periodical manifestation. [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **2005:** *Le Complot de l’art & Cie*. An expanded constellation around the earlier essay, including responses and associated texts; it is not simply a new work with a similar title. [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **2006:** *Utopia Deferred: Writings for Utopie, 1967–1978*. An English-language editorial selection of Baudrillard’s *Utopie* writings. The constituent texts are his, but this post hoc English collection was made by its editors and translators. [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.sydney.edu.au/entries/baudrillard/)
- **2010:** *The Agony of Power*. A posthumous English collection of late writings and a talk, edited by Sylvère Lotringer. It is a manifestation of Baudrillard’s texts but not a book-project he completed in this form. [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.sydney.edu.au/entries/baudrillard/)
- **2015:** *From Hyperreality to Disappearance: Uncollected Interviews, 1986–2007*. A posthumous selection edited by Richard G. Smith and David B. Clarke; the interviews are Baudrillard’s spoken acts, while the corpus and arrangement are editorial creations. [Monoskop bibliography](https://monoskop.org/Jean_Baudrillard)
- **2017:** *The Disappearance of Culture: Uncollected Interviews*. A second posthumous construction by Smith and Clarke, including interviews originally made across several decades; it should not be mistaken for a manuscript left by Baudrillard. [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.sydney.edu.au/entries/baudrillard/)

### Translation-works

- **Date undocumented; unpublished:** *Translations of Hölderlin*. A translation project made during or after Baudrillard’s German studies but reportedly never published; because the source supplies neither the poems nor a stable title, it must remain an unfinished or inaccessible project rather than a recoverable book. [Critical Secret biography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **1956 or 1965:** *Dialogues d’exilés*, by Bertolt Brecht. A French translation made with Gilbert Badia; sources conflict sharply over whether to date the translation’s first publication to 1956 or 1965, while later editions must not be counted as new translations. [Bibliography](https://baudrillard.en-academic.com/167/Bibliography), [BnF attribution](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb39461155b/PUBLIC)
- **1961:** *Mécanique des sols appliquée aux travaux publics et au bâtiment*, by Karl Terzaghi and Ralph B. Peck. A technical translation made with Claude Meunier, demonstrating that Baudrillard’s translation-work extended beyond philosophy and literature. [Translation bibliography](https://baudrillard.en-academic.com/167/Bibliography)
- **1964:** *Point de fuite*, by Peter Weiss. Baudrillard’s French translation of Weiss’s autobiographical novel *Abschied von den Eltern*. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1965:** *La Persécution et l’assassinat de Jean-Paul Marat représentés par le groupe théâtral de l’hospice de Charenton sous la direction de Monsieur de Sade*, by Peter Weiss. A French theatrical translation, commonly shortened to *Marat/Sade*. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1966:** *L’Instruction: Oratorio en onze chants*, by Peter Weiss. A French translation of Weiss’s documentary drama concerning the Frankfurt Auschwitz trials. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1968:** *Chant du fantoche lusitanien*, by Peter Weiss. Baudrillard’s French theatrical translation. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1968:** *Discours sur la genèse et le déroulement de la très longue guerre de libération du Vietnam…*, by Peter Weiss. A French translation of a long-titled political theatre-work; its protocol-like title belongs to Weiss’s play, not to an independent Baudrillard manifesto. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1968:** *Messianismes révolutionnaires du tiers-monde*, by Wilhelm E. Mühlmann. A translated and adapted French version, making Baudrillard’s role more interventionist than literal translation alone. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1968:** *L’Idéologie allemande*, by Karl Marx and Friedrich Engels. A collective French translation made with Henri Auger, Gilbert Badia, and Renée Cartelle; Baudrillard cannot be assigned sole responsibility for the resulting text. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1969:** *Le Rôle de la violence dans l’histoire*, by Friedrich Engels. A collective French translation credited to Émile Bottigelli, Paul A. Stéphane, and Baudrillard. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)

### Periodicals, associations, and collective projects

- **1962:** *Association populaire franco-chinoise*. An organization created with Félix Guattari during the period of the Chinese Cultural Revolution. This is a political-organizational project, not a text, and collective intention matters more than sole authorship. [Critical Secret biography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **1967–1977/1978:** *Utopie*. An interdisciplinary journal and collective project founded with Hubert Tonka and others, joining urbanism, architecture, politics, and theory. Its issues are collective manifestations; Baudrillard’s individual contributions later became a retrospectively assembled corpus. [Critical Secret biography and bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **From 1977:** *Traverses*. A journal of the Centre de Création Industrielle that Baudrillard co-founded. It is an institutional and editorial project rather than an individually authored obra. [Critical Secret biography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **1967–1990:** *Articles for Encyclopædia Universalis*. A dispersed body of reference articles, including work associated with the entry “La modernité”; the consulted bibliography does not enumerate every contribution, so completeness at the individual-article level cannot honestly be claimed. [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)

### Photography and image-text works

- **From 1981:** *Photographic oeuvre*. A continuing body of color photographs begun after Baudrillard received his first camera in Japan. It has no single fixed title or closed catalogue raisonné in the consulted sources and therefore resists division into a definitive list of discrete “works.” [Critical Secret biography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **1986:** *Jean Baudrillard photographs, Galerie Michel Delorme*. His first documented solo photographic exhibition, a manifestation of the developing photographic oeuvre rather than a separately titled photographic series. [Exhibition chronology](https://www.tfam.museum/File/BookStore/78/20151225104326952369.pdf)
- **1991:** *Richard Avedon: Under den Linden*. An image-text portfolio in *Égoïste*, combining Avedon’s photographs with Baudrillard’s text; it is not Baudrillard’s photography. [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **1992:** *Jean Baudrillard photographs, Galerie Gérard Piltzer*. A Paris exhibition of his photographs; the source gives no distinct exhibition title. [Exhibition chronology](https://www.tfam.museum/File/BookStore/78/20151225104326952369.pdf)
- **1993:** *Slittamenti*. A photographic manifestation at the Venice Biennale on Giudecca; the documentation does not establish whether *Slittamenti* named Baudrillard’s individual installation or the larger exhibition context. [Exhibition chronology](https://www.tfam.museum/File/BookStore/78/20151225104326952369.pdf)
- **1994–1997:** *Photographic exhibitions in Australia, Paris, Toronto, New Delhi, Rio de Janeiro, and Tokyo*. A sequence of manifestations of the same open photographic oeuvre, not securely documented as six newly titled works. [Exhibition chronology](https://www.tfam.museum/File/BookStore/78/20151225104326952369.pdf)
- **1998:** *Car l’illusion ne s’oppose pas à la réalité*. A book combining Baudrillard’s photographs and texts; neither medium merely illustrates the other, making it the closest item here to a photographic-theoretical Gesamtkunstwerk. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1999:** *Jean Baudrillard: Photographies, 1985–1998*. A major exhibition and trilingual catalogue edited by Peter Weibel. The photographs are Baudrillard’s, but the retrospective selection, catalogue, and exhibition architecture are collaborative curatorial constructions. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1999:** *Jean Baudrillard, Galerie municipale du Château d’eau*. A Toulouse photographic exhibition with its own short catalogue; the generic title marks it as a manifestation of the oeuvre rather than a securely bounded series. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1999:** *L’Autre*. A photobook by Luc Delahaye with a text by Baudrillard. Baudrillard made the textual intervention, not the photographs or the book’s entire authorship. [University of Graz bibliography](https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/lexikon/klassiker/baudrillard/04bib.htm)
- **1999:** *Nous traversons la violence du monde*. A Tendance Floue photographic publication introduced by Baudrillard; his contribution is a framing text within a collective photographic object. [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **1999–2005:** *FNAC itinerant photographic exhibition*. A touring manifestation shown first in Paris and subsequently in roughly twenty French cities; repeated venues are manifestations of a traveling project, not twenty separate Baudrillard obras. [Exhibition chronology](https://www.tfam.museum/File/BookStore/78/20151225104326952369.pdf)
- **1999–2006:** *International photographic exhibitions*. Exhibitions in Rome, Amsterdam, Istanbul, Paris, Oslo, Lyme Regis, Arles, Stockholm, Auckland, Sydney, Milan, Galway, Vancouver, Calgary, Moscow, Siena, Kassel, Vienna, Seoul, and Vendôme manifested Baudrillard’s photographic corpus in changing curatorial settings; the source does not supply stable individual titles for most of them. [Exhibition chronology](https://www.tfam.museum/File/BookStore/78/20151225104326952369.pdf)
- **2006:** *Sommes-nous?* A retrospective book of fifteen years of photography by Tendance Floue with a text by Baudrillard. His text is a contribution to, not authorship of, the collective photographic obra. [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
- **2009–2015:** *Posthumous photographic exhibitions*. Presentations in Reggio Emilia, Reims, Lianzhou, Guangzhou, Beijing, Macao, Taipei, and Stockholm are posthumous curatorial manifestations: Baudrillard made the photographs, but not these later exhibition projects as such. [Exhibition chronology](https://www.tfam.museum/File/BookStore/78/20151225104326952369.pdf)

### Recorded and audiovisual manifestations

- **1984/1987:** *The Evil Demon of Images*. A lecture delivered at the University of Sydney in 1984 and issued separately in English in 1987. Its primary event was an oral performance; the booklet is a textual manifestation of that event. [Monoskop bibliography](https://monoskop.org/Jean_Baudrillard)
- **1999–2000; released 2004:** *Mot de passe*. A 90-minute, two-volume PAL VHS interview conceived and directed by Leslie F. Grunberg and Pierre Bourgeois, in which Baudrillard answers through the key terms of his work. His speech is the performed content, but the audiovisual object is jointly made and directed by others. [Critical Secret bibliography](https://www.criticalsecret.com/n15/index.php@art=433.html)
