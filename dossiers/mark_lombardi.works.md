## Works by Mark Lombardi

### Paintings and pre-network works

- **Late 1970s–early 1990s:** *Untitled abstract paintings and drawings*. This incompletely catalogued obra comprises geometric, letter-form, and map-like abstractions made before Lombardi developed his mature “narrative structures”; neither the 2003 scholarly catalogue nor the surviving gallery inventory establishes a complete title-by-title list.
- **Early 1990s:** *Index*. A series of black-and-white paintings assembled from fragmented or deconstructed letter forms. Only a few reproductions have been located, and no complete checklist is published.
- **Early 1990s:** *Panoramas*. Large horizontal paintings or drawings that preceded the network diagrams and helped Lombardi develop their extended, quasi-cartographic format; the surviving literature treats them as a body of experiments rather than a securely enumerated series.

### Narrative structures and their successive versions

- **1994:** *World Finance Corp.–Guillermo Hernandez Cartaya (Miami, FL) (1st Version)*. An early graphite network drawing organized partly as a timeline; it begins Lombardi’s repeatedly revised World Finance Corporation obra.
- **1994:** *World Finance Corporation, Miami, ca. 1970–79 (2nd Version)*. A revised narrative structure; its status as a “version” matters because Lombardi treated new information and new composition as grounds for making another physical work.
- **1994–1999:** *World Finance Corporation, Miami, ca. 1970–79/84 (3rd–7th Versions)*. Five separately made graphite drawings, not editions or copies: the sequence progressively displaces Guillermo Hernández-Cartaya from its visual center and changes from a horizontal chronology into a more circular network. The seventh version is sometimes dated circa 1999 and survives with associated preparatory studies.
- **1997:** *World Finance Corporation, Miami, and Nugan Hand Ltd. of Sydney, Australia, ca. 1972–80 (8th Version)*. A large composite narrative structure joining two financial networks; the numbering continues the WFC project, although its expanded title makes it also function as a distinct obra.
- **1995:** *Nugan Hand Bank, Sydney, Australia, ca. 1974–80*. A graphite network drawing concerning the Australian bank; catalogues also identify it by the expanded title *Frank Nugan, Michael Hand, and Nugan Hand Ltd. of Sydney, Australia, ca. 1972–80*.
- **1995:** *BNL, Reagan, Bush, & Thatcher and the Arming of Iraq, ca. 1983–91 (1st Version)*. A graphite narrative structure mapping the Banca Nazionale del Lavoro affair and Western arms transfers to Iraq.
- **1996:** *Banca Nazionale del Lavoro, Reagan, Bush, Thatcher, and the Arming of Iraq, ca. 1979–90 (3rd Version)*. A separately executed revision of the BNL network; sources disagree in shortened titles over whether the chronology begins in 1979 or 1983.
- **1998:** *Banca Nazionale del Lavoro, Reagan, Bush, Thatcher, and the Arming of Iraq, ca. 1979–90 (4th Version)*. A further graphite revision of the BNL project, distinct from the earlier versions rather than a reproduction of them.
- **1995–1998:** *Inner Sanctum: The Pope and His Bankers Michele Sindona and Roberto Calvi, ca. 1959–82*. A multi-version narrative-structure project on Vatican finance. The fifth version is securely dated 1998; a complete public accounting of every preliminary version has not been established.
- **1995:** *Global International Airways and Indian Springs State Bank, Kansas City, ca. 1977–83 (3rd Version)*. A graphite diagram joining an airline, a failed bank, organized crime, and intelligence-linked figures.
- **1998:** *Global International Airways and Indian Springs State Bank, Kansas City, ca. 1977–83 (4th Version)*. A separately drawn enlargement and revision of the same network.
- **1995:** *International Systems and Control, Houston, ca. 1972–77*. A narrative structure about the arms manufacturer also known as International Signal and Control; variant titles in inventories reflect that corporate-name ambiguity.
- **1995–1996:** *Charles Keating, ACC, and Lincoln Savings, ca. 1978–90*. A multi-version project mapping the Lincoln Savings and American Continental Corporation scandal; individual sheets also circulate under shortened working titles such as *Lincoln, Silverado*.
- **1996:** *Neil Bush and Silverado #2*. A graphite narrative structure concerning Silverado Banking, Savings and Loan Association; “#2” identifies a separately made revision, while the disposition of a first version is not consistently documented.
- **1995–1996:** *George Bush and Palmer National Bank of Washington, D.C., ca. 1983–86*. A multi-version network drawing joining political, banking, and intelligence relationships.
- **1996–1999:** *George W. Bush, Harken Energy, and Jackson Stephens, ca. 1979–90*. A sequence of narrative structures; the fifth version, dated 1999, is in graphite and colored pencil. Earlier versions are autonomous working drawings, but published inventories do not supply a complete, concordant chronology for all five.
- **1996–2000:** *BCCI–ICIC & FAB, 1972–91*. A multi-version project on the Bank of Credit and Commerce International and related institutions. The monumental fourth version was worked on from 1996 to 2000 and is often treated as Lombardi’s culminating drawing; studies and earlier versions remain part of the project rather than disposable drafts.
- **1996–1999:** *Meyer Lansky’s Financial Network, ca. 1960–78*. A revised narrative-structure project mapping Lansky’s banking and business associations; working sheets also bear titles such as *Lansky Banks*.
- **1996–1999:** *Chicago Outfit and Satellite Regimes, ca. 1931–83*. A multi-version historical network of organized crime and its associated political and commercial structures.
- **1996–1999:** *Oliver North, Lake Resources of Panama, and the Iran-Contra Operation, ca. 1984–86*. A multi-version graphite network concerning Iran-Contra finance.
- **1996–1999:** *Hans Kopp, Trans K-B, and Shakarchi Trading AG of Zurich, ca. 1981–89*. A revised narrative structure about Swiss political and financial connections; at least a third version is documented.
- **1996–1999:** *Gerry Bull, Space Research Corporation, and Armscor of Pretoria, South Africa, ca. 1972–90*. A multi-version project about Gerald Bull, long-range artillery, and arms trading. The fifth version is dated 1999; some titles shorten the historical span to “ca. 1972–80.”
- **1996–1999:** *First Penn Bank, SRC, and Armscor of South Africa, ca. 1972–80*. A related but separately titled network drawing; its overlap with the Gerald Bull project makes it resist classification as either an independent subject or merely a developmental branch.
- **1996–1999:** *Industrias Carlos Cardoen of Santiago, Chile, ca. 1982–90 (2nd Version)*. A graphite narrative structure about the Chilean arms manufacturer; another related sheet is titled *Carlos Cardoen “Military Pyrotechnics,” Santiago, Chile, ca. 1982–92*.
- **1996–1999:** *Arochem, Bayoil and Bay Industries: Stamford, London, Baghdad, ca. 1988–91*. A network drawing of oil, arms, and Iraqi commercial relationships.
- **1996–1999:** *Ron Rewald & Bishop, Baldwin, Rewald, Dillingham & Wong of Honolulu, ca. 1978–83*. A narrative structure concerning the investment firm and its alleged intelligence connections.
- **1996–1999:** *George Francorero, Bank of Bloomfield and State Bank of Chatham, New Jersey, ca. 1973–76*. A financial-network drawing; surviving references vary in their spelling of Francorero’s surname and in abbreviating the bank names.
- **1996–1999:** *Bruce Campbell, Esq., Cayman Islands, ca. 1966–78 (3rd Version)*. A narrative structure centered on offshore financial relationships in the Cayman Islands.
- **1996–1999:** *Paul Helliwell, Castle Bank & Trust, Mercantile Bank (2nd Version)*. A network drawing concerning intelligence-linked offshore banking; abbreviated working titles vary substantially.
- **1996–1999:** *Cabazon Arms of Indio, California, ca. 1978–88*. A narrative structure about weapons development, tribal land, and intelligence-associated figures.
- **1996–1999:** *EATSCO (Egyptian American Transport & Services Co.), Falls Church, Virginia, ca. 1979–82*. A network drawing concerning the Iran-Contra-era transport company.
- **1996–1999:** *First National Bank of Maryland and Associated Traders, ca. 1982–86*. A graphite financial-network drawing.
- **1996–1999:** *Peregrine International Associates of Dallas, ca. 1979–90*. A narrative structure about a corporate and arms-trading network.
- **1996–1999:** *Trafalgar House/Cementation–Armscor and the Malaysia Dam Project, ca. 1980–95: London–Johannesburg–Kuala Lumpur*. A transnational network drawing whose extended geographic title is integral to its cartographic function.
- **1996–1999:** *Casalee Group S.A.: Rhodesia–Luxembourg–Belgium, ca. 1976–90*. A narrative structure concerning an international arms and corporate network.
- **1996–1999:** *US Corporate Interlock (Banks, Oil, and Aircraft), ca. 1971–81*. A diagrammatic drawing of interlocking corporate control; it is closer to a systemic map than to a story centered on one scandal.
- **1996–1999:** *Untitled (all parties—BCCI, Nugan Hand, et al.)*. An unusually synthetic working network combining several of Lombardi’s recurring subjects. Its lack of a stable authorial title and its developmental appearance make it resist secure classification as either finished work or study.
- **1996–1999:** *Untitled (UPI Saga: Pat Robertson/Beurt Servaas/Middle East Broadcasting)*. A working narrative structure that developed into Lombardi’s Pat Robertson project; the descriptive parenthesis is an archival identifier, not necessarily a fixed title chosen for exhibition.
- **1998–1999:** *Bill Clinton, the Lippo Group, and Jackson Stephens of Little Rock, Arkansas*. A multi-version narrative structure concerning campaign finance and banking connections; the fifth version is dated 1999.
- **1999:** *Bill Clinton, the Lippo Group, and China Ocean Shipping Co., a.k.a. COSCO, Little Rock–Jakarta–Hong Kong, ca. 1990s (5th Version)*. A large graphite network drawing and a related but distinct expansion of the Clinton–Lippo project.
- **1999–2000:** *Pat Robertson, Beurt Servaas, and the UPI Takeover Battle, ca. 1985–91*. A late narrative-structure project, represented in sources by working drawings and a finished or near-finished version dated 2000.
- **1999:** *World Finance Corporation and Associates, ca. 1970–84*. A late reformulation of Lombardi’s longest-running network subject; catalogues differ over whether to fold it into the numbered WFC sequence or list it separately.

### Preparatory drawings, legends, and display experiments

- **1994–2000:** *Preparatory Drawings*. Hundreds of layouts, trial diagrams, transfer drawings, Xerox collages, and compositional studies for the narrative structures. They are made objects and parts of Lombardi’s recursive project, but a complete item-level catalogue has never been published; some were deliberately kept together because separating them would destroy their evidentiary relation.
- **Circa 1995–2000:** *Keys and legends for the narrative structures*. Hand-drawn protocols explaining the meanings of solid, dotted, and colored lines and other graphic conventions. These are both instructional texts and components of the drawings’ representational system.
- **Circa 1999:** *Untitled (Study for World Finance Corporation, Version 7)*. A graphite-and-Xerox-collage preparatory drawing; it demonstrates that mechanical reproduction within Lombardi’s process produced a new working object, not an edition of the finished network.
- **Circa 1999–2000:** *Untitled network drawing transferred to Plexiglas and mounted in a light box*. A prototype for a new display format. It is an experimental manifestation rather than a securely titled finished work, and Lombardi died before determining whether the format would become a continuing project.
- **Undated:** *Approximately thirty early network sketches*. A group of loose, intuitive studies preserved by Pierogi as a coherent corpus. Their dealer explicitly resisted treating them as individually saleable finished works; that resistance is part of their status.

### Research archive as project

- **Early 1990s–2000:** *Index-card archive*. Approximately 14,000 handwritten and typed cards recording names, events, transactions, sources, and cross-references. Lombardi’s dealer did not regard the file as an artwork, yet it is an authored research device inseparable from the drawings and therefore an obra that resists being called a work of art.
- **Early 1990s–2000:** *Research library and clipping files*. An annotated working collection of books, syndicated news reports, charts, advertisements, and photocopies used to construct the narrative structures. The collection is better classified as an evolving apparatus than as either a text or an artwork, and its contents include things Lombardi selected rather than made.
- **Circa 1994–2000:** *Written narratives accompanying the network drawings*. Short explanatory essays or indexes supplied for exhibitions and collectors. They are paratexts to particular drawings rather than a single fixed publication, and no complete collected edition has been issued.
- **Circa 1995:** *Proposal for “Over the Line”*. An unpublished exhibition proposal and artist’s statement describing the political and formal aims of the narrative structures; it is both a project document and a manifesto-like text.
- **Circa 1995:** *Unpublished notes on corporate diagrams, information, and representation*. Studio writings articulating Lombardi’s visual method. They survive in the archive as working texts rather than as a finished book.

### Longer writings and unfinished book projects

- **1980s–1990s:** *Unpublished manuscript on the drug wars*. A book-length research project that preceded and generated the diagrams. It remained unfinished or unpublished, and surviving accounts do not provide a secure authorial title.
- **Late 1980s–1990s:** *Unpublished manuscript on political, banking, and savings-and-loan corruption*. A second evolving prose project, redirected after Lombardi studied reporting on the Houston savings-and-loan scandal. Its boundaries relative to the drug-war manuscript are unclear, so the archive does not support silently turning it into a finished “book.”
- **Written before 2000; published 2001:** *The “Offshore” Phenomenon: Dirty Banking in a Brave New World*. An essay on offshore finance, published posthumously in *Cabinet* no. 2. It is a finished expository text, not one of the diagrammatic artworks, although it draws upon the same research apparatus.
