## Works by Katja Wulff

### Schools, ensembles, and unrealized projects

- **1918:** *Schule für Eurythmie*. A jointly founded pedagogical project with Suzanne Perrottet, conceived as a successor to Rudolf von Laban’s Zürich school; the collaboration was brief, so this is better understood as an institutional project than as a discrete authored work. ([Simon Baur](https://thedancersact.ch/katja-wulff/); [Historical Dictionary of Switzerland](https://hls-dhs-dss.ch/fr/articles/044712/2013-08-19/))

- **1923–1980s:** *Schule für Ausdruckstanz* (also called the Wulffschule or Labanschule Käthe Wulff). Wulff’s Basel school was a long-duration pedagogical obra—curriculum, movement practice, and succession of pupils—rather than a single text or performance; surviving prospectuses and notices document activity from 1924 into the 1960s, while biographical sources report that she taught into the 1980s. ([Deutsches Tanzarchiv Köln, fonds inventory](https://faust.sk-kultur.de/hzeig.FAU?desk=%2300000190%23&dm=1&sid=6DF198F012424&thes=4); [Historical Dictionary of Switzerland](https://hls-dhs-dss.ch/fr/articles/044712/2013-08-19/))

- **1926–mid-1940s:** *Tanzstudio Wulff*. A dance ensemble and continuing collaborative obra founded by Wulff with Mariette von Meyenburg. Wulff directed it, but von Meyenburg choreographed most of its individual pieces; consequently the ensemble and its production framework belong here, while those choreographies cannot silently be reassigned to Wulff. ([Deutsches Tanzarchiv Köln, Mariette von Meyenburg](https://www.deutsches-tanzarchiv.de/archiv/nachlaesse-sammlungen/mariette-von-meyenburg/); [Historical Dictionary of Switzerland](https://hls-dhs-dss.ch/fr/articles/044712/2013-08-19/))

- **1929:** *Tanzhaus / Laban-Schule mit Wohnung an der Hirzbrunnenallee*. An unrealized architectural and pedagogical project initiated according to Wulff’s requirements and promoted through the Verein Freunde der Labanschule Käthe Wulff Basel; Artaria & Schmidt produced preliminary designs, but insufficient funds prevented construction. The project—not a completed building—is the work attributable to Wulff. ([Simon Baur](https://thedancersact.ch/katja-wulff/))

### Choreographic and performance constructions

- **1927:** *Entwurf für einen Bewegungschor*. A design or protocol for a movement choir preserved in Wulff’s papers; the archive dates the document but supplies neither a more specific title nor evidence that the project was performed or completed. ([Deutsches Tanzarchiv Köln, fonds inventory](https://faust.sk-kultur.de/hzeig.FAU?desk=%2300000190%23&dm=1&sid=6DF198F012424&thes=4))

- **1927:** *Tanzsuite und Barabau*. A jointly made stage evening at the Stadttheater Basel comprising group and solo dances to Alfredo Casella and Vittorio Rieti’s ballet *Barabau*. The *Theaterlexikon der Schweiz* describes the production as made jointly by Wulff and Mariette von Meyenburg; because von Meyenburg created most Tanzstudio Wulff choreography, Wulff’s precise choreographic share remains unresolved. ([Deutsches Tanzarchiv Köln, Mariette von Meyenburg](https://www.deutsches-tanzarchiv.de/archiv/nachlaesse-sammlungen/mariette-von-meyenburg/))

- **1927–1928:** *Fünf Studienabende über die Elemente des modernen Bühnentanzes*. A serial laboratory-performance by Tanzstudio Wulff exploring dances with noise instruments, silent or gestural dance, music, costume and masks, and spatial transformation. It is a collaborative Gesamtkunstwerk-like format under Wulff’s direction, not securely a cycle of five solo-authored Wulff choreographies. ([Simon Baur](https://thedancersact.ch/katja-wulff/); [Deutsches Tanzarchiv Köln, fonds inventory](https://faust.sk-kultur.de/hzeig.FAU?desk=%2300000190%23&dm=1&sid=6DF198F012424&thes=4))

- **Undated:** *Choreographische Notizen/Entwürfe für Tanzstücke*. A surviving corpus of choreographic notes and drafts, catalogued collectively rather than as individually titled works. Their number, dates, completion, and relationship to performed dances are not specified, so the corpus resists conversion into a falsely precise work-list. ([Deutsches Tanzarchiv Köln, fonds inventory](https://faust.sk-kultur.de/hzeig.FAU?desk=%2300000190%23&dm=1&sid=6DF198F012424&thes=4))

### Writings, lectures, and notation

- **1916, 1932–1934, 1938, 1958:** *Zeitungsartikel*. A corpus of newspaper articles by Wulff catalogued under these years; the finding aid does not disclose individual titles or establish whether every surviving article is complete. ([Deutsches Tanzarchiv Köln, fonds inventory](https://faust.sk-kultur.de/hzeig.FAU?desk=%2300000190%23&dm=1&sid=6DF198F012424&thes=4))

- **1926 or later:** *Ausführungen und Skizzen zu Rudolf von Labans „Choreographie: erstes Heft“*. Movement realizations and sketches made by Wulff and some of her pupils in response to Laban’s 1926 book. This is collaborative working notation rather than a separately published book, and the archive does not divide Wulff’s contributions from those of the pupils. ([Deutsches Tanzarchiv Köln, fonds inventory](https://faust.sk-kultur.de/hzeig.FAU?desk=%2300000190%23&dm=1&sid=6DF198F012424&thes=4))

- **1928:** *Vortrag zur Tanzpädagogik beim Tänzerkongress in Essen*. A lecture manuscript on dance pedagogy: simultaneously a text and the protocol for an oral intervention at the congress. No supplied catalogue title beyond this descriptive designation is known. ([Deutsches Tanzarchiv Köln, fonds inventory](https://faust.sk-kultur.de/hzeig.FAU?desk=%2300000190%23&dm=1&sid=6DF198F012424&thes=4))

- **Undated:** *Schriften zu Tanz, Bewegung, Körperbildung, Pädagogik und Rudolf von Laban*. An archival body of writings and notes rather than one work; the finding aid groups the material thematically and supplies neither titles nor an item count. ([Deutsches Tanzarchiv Köln, fonds inventory](https://faust.sk-kultur.de/hzeig.FAU?desk=%2300000190%23&dm=1&sid=6DF198F012424&thes=4))

- **Undated:** *Aufzeichnungen über die Ausbildung bei Rudolf von Laban und Mary Wigman, die Idee der Labanschule und die Zusammenarbeit mit Laban*. A grouped body of recollective and theoretical texts. Its archival description does not establish whether Wulff intended a unified memoir, separate essays, or private notes; that resistance to work-status must remain open. ([Deutsches Tanzarchiv Köln, fonds inventory](https://faust.sk-kultur.de/hzeig.FAU?desk=%2300000190%23&dm=1&sid=6DF198F012424&thes=4))

- **Undated:** *Vortragsmanuskripte zu Körperbildung, Bewegungsschulung, Gymnastikschulen und Lehrarten*. A corpus of lecture manuscripts functioning partly as pedagogical protocols. The archive does not provide individual titles, dates, or evidence that every lecture was delivered. ([Deutsches Tanzarchiv Köln, fonds inventory](https://faust.sk-kultur.de/hzeig.FAU?desk=%2300000190%23&dm=1&sid=6DF198F012424&thes=4))

- **Undated:** *Gedichte*. A collectively catalogued body of poems by Wulff. No titles, dates, item count, or publication status are supplied, so the poems can be named only as an archival corpus. ([Deutsches Tanzarchiv Köln, fonds inventory](https://faust.sk-kultur.de/hzeig.FAU?desk=%2300000190%23&dm=1&sid=6DF198F012424&thes=4))

- **Undated:** *Reisetagebuch (Niederlande) und Reisenotizen (England)*. A travel diary and associated notes preserved among Wulff’s personal papers. They are writings she made, but the inventory gives no fixed title or date and does not establish that she regarded them as publishable works. ([Deutsches Tanzarchiv Köln, fonds inventory](https://faust.sk-kultur.de/hzeig.FAU?desk=%2300000190%23&dm=1&sid=6DF198F012424&thes=4))

### Drawings and designs

- **Undated:** *Zeichnungen*. A corpus including dance sketches and portraits of Mary Wigman and Rudolf von Laban. The archival inventory neither enumerates nor individually titles the sheets, so they remain a body of graphic works rather than safely separable catalogue entries. ([Deutsches Tanzarchiv Köln, fonds inventory](https://faust.sk-kultur.de/hzeig.FAU?desk=%2300000190%23&dm=1&sid=6DF198F012424&thes=4))

- **Undated:** *Weitere Zeichnungen, wahrscheinlich von Katja Wulff*. A separate group of drawings whose attribution to Wulff is explicitly uncertain. They should not be merged without qualification into the securely attributed drawing corpus. ([Deutsches Tanzarchiv Köln, fonds inventory](https://faust.sk-kultur.de/hzeig.FAU?desk=%2300000190%23&dm=1&sid=6DF198F012424&thes=4))

### Sources

- Deutsches Tanzarchiv Köln. [*Wulff, Katja (1890–1992): fonds inventory*](https://faust.sk-kultur.de/hzeig.FAU?desk=%2300000190%23&dm=1&sid=6DF198F012424&thes=4). The principal work-level source: a 28-box archival inventory organized under “Beruf und Werk,” including choreography, writings, drawings, poems, programs, and documentation of the school and ensemble.

- Pellaton, Ursula. [“Mariette von Meyenburg,” in *Theaterlexikon der Schweiz*, vol. 2 (2005), p. 1238](https://www.deutsches-tanzarchiv.de/archiv/nachlaesse-sammlungen/mariette-von-meyenburg/). Used to distinguish Wulff’s direction and joint work from von Meyenburg’s choreographic authorship.

- Pellaton, Ursula. [“Wulff, Katja,” *Historical Dictionary of Switzerland*](https://hls-dhs-dss.ch/fr/articles/044712/2013-08-19/), revised 19 August 2013. Used for the institutional chronology and the respective roles of Wulff and von Meyenburg.

- Baur, Simon. [“Wer hat Katja Wulff tanzen gesehen?”](https://thedancersact.ch/katja-wulff/). Used for the chronology and character of the schools, Tanzstudio Wulff’s study evenings, and the unrealized Tanzhaus project.
