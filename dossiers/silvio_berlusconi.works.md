## Works by Silvio Berlusconi

### Urban and architectural projects

- **1963–1966:** *Brugherio residential development*. Berlusconi’s first large development through Edilnord; it is an entrepreneurial project rather than a personally designed building, and the architectural authorship belongs to the professionals he employed. ([Ordine degli Architetti di Milano](https://ordinearchitetti.mi.it/en/cultura/itinerari-di-architettura/55-milano-est/opere/2219-milano-2/scheda))

- **1968–c. 1979:** *Milano 2*. A planned residential settlement at Segrate promoted and substantially shaped by Berlusconi through Edilnord, but designed by Giancarlo Ragazzi, Giuseppe Marvelli, Enrico Hoffer and collaborators. It is best understood as a collective urban project—or an early Berlusconian Gesamtkunstwerk joining housing, landscape, commerce, publicity and television—not as architecture solely authored by Berlusconi. ([Ordine degli Architetti di Milano](https://ordinearchitetti.mi.it/en/cultura/itinerari-di-architettura/55-milano-est/opere/2219-milano-2/scheda); [Jaque, *Transmedia Urbanism*](https://officeforpoliticalinnovation.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/2017.3-Transmedia-Urbanism.-Berlusconi-and-the-Birth-of-Targeted-Difference.pdf))

- **c. 1980–1991:** *Milano 3*. A second Edilnord new town, at Basiglio, again promoted by Berlusconi but designed and executed collectively by architects, planners and companies. Its attribution to him is therefore one of patronage, development and programmatic direction, not individual architectural authorship. ([Ordine degli Architetti di Milano](https://ordinearchitetti.mi.it/it/cultura/itinerari-di-architettura/57-sud-ovest/opere/2238-milano-3))

### Media and corporate systems

- **1974–1980:** *Telemilano 58 / Canale 5*. What began as Milano 2’s cable service became Berlusconi’s national commercial television project; it is an institutional and technological obra made by a corporate network, not a single television text. The precise origin is sometimes dated 1974 and sometimes 1976 because the local cable operation preceded Berlusconi’s acquisition and expansion of it. ([Fininvest, “Il Fondatore”](https://fininvest.it/il-gruppo/il-fondatore/); [Ordine degli Architetti di Milano](https://ordinearchitetti.mi.it/en/cultura/itinerari-di-architettura/55-milano-est/opere/2219-milano-2/scheda))

- **1978 onward:** *Fininvest*. A holding-company system founded by Berlusconi to consolidate his property, television, advertising and publishing interests. It resists treatment as a conventional work: it is an institutional obra made through incorporation, acquisition and management by many people. ([Fininvest, “History”](https://fininvest.it/en/the-group/history/))

- **1980s–1990s:** *The Fininvest commercial-television system*. The linked system of Canale 5, Italia 1, Rete 4 and advertising sales was assembled under Berlusconi rather than wholly created ex novo: Italia 1 and Rete 4 were acquisitions. Its relevant “made thing” is consequently the integrated national network and programming-commercial model, not each channel considered as his personal authorship. ([Fininvest, “History”](https://fininvest.it/en/the-group/history/); [Fininvest, “Il Fondatore”](https://fininvest.it/il-gruppo/il-fondatore/))

### Political institutions and projects

- **1993–1994:** *Forza Italia*. A political party founded around Berlusconi, his companies and a wider organizing group. Scholarship calls it a personal party and a business-firm party, so attribution to Berlusconi is unusually strong but never exclusive institutional authorship. ([McDonnell, “Silvio Berlusconi’s Personal Parties”](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1467-9248.2012.01007.x); [Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/forza-italia_%28Dizionario-di-Storia%29/))

- **2006–2009:** *Il Popolo della Libertà*. First articulated by Berlusconi as a project for a unified centre-right party and formally constituted with Gianfranco Fini and numerous component organizations. It is a collective political institution, not solely Berlusconi’s creation, and the project predates the completed party. ([McDonnell, “Silvio Berlusconi’s Personal Parties”](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1467-9248.2012.01007.x); [Treccani, “Forza Italia”](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/forza-italia_%28Dizionario-di-Storia%29/))

- **2013:** *Forza Italia (refounded)*. Berlusconi reactivated the name and organization after the dissolution of the Popolo della Libertà. This is simultaneously a new institutional manifestation and a claimed continuation of the 1994 party. ([McDonnell, “Silvio Berlusconi’s Personal Parties”](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1467-9248.2012.01007.x))

### Manifestos, protocols and campaign objects

- **26 January 1994:** *L’Italia è il paese che amo* (“discesa in campo”). A prerecorded nine-minute televised manifesto announcing Berlusconi’s entry into electoral politics. Its carefully constructed domestic set and national dissemination make it both an audiovisual work and a performative political act. ([Treccani, “Berlusconi: io, la gente e me”](https://www.treccani.it/magazine/lingua_italiana/speciali/politici/Dell_Anna.html))

- **2001:** *Una storia italiana*. An illustrated autobiographical campaign book or “book-newspaper,” issued under Berlusconi’s name and mailed in millions of copies to Italian households. Although catalogued as his book, it is better classified as a collectively produced self-mythologizing campaign object; the uncredited extent of staff authorship prevents secure attribution of every word to Berlusconi. ([Google Books bibliographic record](https://books.google.com/books/about/Una_storia_italiana.html?id=idVDAAAACAAJ); [Italian Political Science](https://italianpoliticalscience.com/index.php/ips/article/download/175/132/1167); [Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/magazine/lingua_italiana/speciali/politici/Dell_Anna.html))

- **8 May 2001:** *Contratto con gli italiani*. A five-point electoral protocol presented and signed by Berlusconi on television, promising specified government actions and making his future candidacy conditional upon their fulfillment. It acts rather than merely describes, although it was a political pledge rather than a legally enforceable private contract. ([“Contratto con gli italiani”](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contratto_con_gli_italiani))

- **18 November 2007:** *Discorso del predellino*. An improvised public manifesto announcing the projected Partito/Popolo della Libertà from the running board of a car. It is simultaneously an oral text, a media event and the performative launch of an unfinished-at-that-moment institutional project. ([“Discorso del predellino”](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discorso_del_predellino))

### Books and edited bodies of political speech

- **2000:** *L’Italia che ho in mente: i discorsi “a braccio” di Silvio Berlusconi*. A shaped collection of extemporaneous political speeches rather than a newly composed continuous treatise; Paolo Guzzanti is associated with its editorial presentation. ([Forza Italia bibliography](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forza_Italia_%281994%29); [Paolo Guzzanti bibliography](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paolo_Guzzanti))

- **2001:** *Discorsi per la democrazia*. A collection of Berlusconi’s parliamentary and political speeches. The obra is the edited compilation; the component speeches originated as separate oral and institutional acts. ([German Historical Institute in Rome bibliography](https://dhi-roma.it/fileadmin/user_upload/pdf-dateien/Online-Publikationen/Bibl_Inf/bi_125.pdf); [Radio Radicale](https://www.radioradicale.it/scheda/129783/assemblea-regionale-seniores-di-forza-italia-centro-congressi-via-corridoni-6))

- **2004:** *La forza di un sogno: i discorsi per cambiare l’Italia*. A further authorized collection of political speeches, marketed as the third principal Berlusconi volume after *L’Italia che ho in mente* and *Discorsi per la democrazia*. It is an editorially assembled body of utterances rather than a single original argument. ([Feltrinelli](https://www.lafeltrinelli.it/forza-di-sogno-libro-silvio-berlusconi/e/9788804497189); [Forza Italia bibliography](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forza_Italia_%281994%29))

- **2006:** *Verso il Partito della Libertà: l’identità, i valori, il progetto*. A political project-book, with an introduction by Sandro Bondi, developing Berlusconi’s intention to unite the Italian centre-right. Its status lies between manifesto, speech collection and blueprint for an institution not yet completed at publication. ([Feltrinelli](https://www.lafeltrinelli.it/verso-partito-della-liberta-libro-silvio-berlusconi/e/9788804558392); [CiNii bibliographic record](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BA80069431))

- **2010:** *L’amore vince sempre sull’invidia e sull’odio*. A hybrid commemorative and political volume built largely from supporters’ messages, supplemented by Berlusconi speeches and a summary of government actions. Because much of its matter was written or compiled by others, it resists classification as a wholly authored Berlusconi book. ([Unilibro bibliographic description](https://www.unilibro.it/libro/silvio-berlusconi/amore-vince-sempre-sull-invidia-odio/13559404); [Corriere della Sera, *Style*](https://style.corriere.it/spettacoli/libri/silvio-berlusconi-libri/2/))

- **2013:** *Discorsi per la libertà*. A retrospective anthology of speeches delivered in settings including the Italian parliament, the United States Congress and the Knesset. The individual speeches have their own occasions and dates; 2013 dates the collected obra. ([Feltrinelli](https://www.lafeltrinelli.it/discorsi-per-liberta-libro-silvio-berlusconi/e/9788804630555))

### Song texts and recorded collaborations

- **1993–1994:** *Forza Italia*. A political anthem with words credited to Berlusconi, music by Renato Serio and arrangement by Augusto Martelli. As a manifesto-song it was made to organize collective feeling and political identification, not simply to describe a program. ([“Forza Italia” work record](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forza_Italia_%28brano_musicale%29))

- **1999:** *Azzurra libertà*. A second political anthem, again credited to Berlusconi as lyricist and Renato Serio as composer, made for Forza Italia Giovani. Contemporary reporting confirms Berlusconi’s authorship of the text. ([*Il manifesto*](https://ilmanifesto.it/archivio/1999003428); [“Forza Italia” work record](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forza_Italia_%28brano_musicale%29))

- **2003:** *Meglio ’na canzone*. An album performed and composed musically by Mariano Apicella, with Neapolitan song texts attributed to Berlusconi. The album is therefore a collaboration and recorded manifestation of his lyrics, not a solo Berlusconi recording. ([Estatica discography](https://www.estatica.it/en/musica/silvio-berlusconi-e-mariano-apicella/discografia); [Mariano Apicella discography](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariano_Apicella))

- **2006:** *L’ultimo amore*. A second Apicella album containing lyrics credited to Berlusconi, sometimes with additional lyricists. Sources agree on the album and general division of labor but do not support treating every musical or performing element as Berlusconi’s. ([*L’ultimo amore* album record](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%27ultimo_amore); [Sky TG24](https://tg24.sky.it/politica/2011/03/09/silvio_berlusconi_cantante_siae_diritti_autore_mariano_apicella))

- **2006:** *Il fantasma di un amore*. A song text on *L’ultimo amore*, credited within the Berlusconi–Apicella collaboration; Apicella supplied the musical and performing realization. ([*L’ultimo amore* album record](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%27ultimo_amore))

- **by 2011:** *Male di te*. A registered song text credited to Berlusconi, with possible collaborating lyricists depending on the registration. It belongs to the dispersed song corpus rather than securely to one independently authored book-like work. ([Sky TG24](https://tg24.sky.it/politica/2011/03/09/silvio_berlusconi_cantante_siae_diritti_autore_mariano_apicella))

- **by 2011:** *Ma se ti perdo*. A registered popular-song lyric attributed to Berlusconi; musical authorship is separate. ([Sky TG24](https://tg24.sky.it/politica/2011/03/09/silvio_berlusconi_cantante_siae_diritti_autore_mariano_apicella))

- **by 2011:** *Le stagioni del cuore*. A registered song text attributed to Berlusconi, not a prose or poetic volume. ([Sky TG24](https://tg24.sky.it/politica/2011/03/09/silvio_berlusconi_cantante_siae_diritti_autore_mariano_apicella))

- **by 2011:** *La tua arroganza*. A registered song lyric attributed to Berlusconi, made for musical performance. ([Sky TG24](https://tg24.sky.it/politica/2011/03/09/silvio_berlusconi_cantante_siae_diritti_autore_mariano_apicella))

- **by 2011:** *Amore mio goodbye*. A registered popular-song text attributed to Berlusconi; the hybrid Italian-English title is fixed by the repertory reporting. ([Sky TG24](https://tg24.sky.it/politica/2011/03/09/silvio_berlusconi_cantante_siae_diritti_autore_mariano_apicella))

- **by 2011:** *Donna della mia vita*. A registered song text attributed to Berlusconi, sometimes within a shared-credit corpus. ([Sky TG24](https://tg24.sky.it/politica/2011/03/09/silvio_berlusconi_cantante_siae_diritti_autore_mariano_apicella))

- **by 2011:** *Col cuore in gola*. A registered song text attributed to Berlusconi. It should not be confused with the musical composition or recording, which required other makers. ([Sky TG24](https://tg24.sky.it/politica/2011/03/09/silvio_berlusconi_cantante_siae_diritti_autore_mariano_apicella))

- **date not securely established:** *Sto nummero ’e telefono*. A song jointly credited to Berlusconi and Tonino Apicella for text, with Apicella also associated with the music. The available repertory report does not establish a reliable composition date, so none is supplied here. ([Sky TG24](https://tg24.sky.it/politica/2011/03/09/silvio_berlusconi_cantante_siae_diritti_autore_mariano_apicella))

- **2011:** *Il vero amore*. An eleven-song album of love lyrics written by Berlusconi over approximately the preceding two years, set to music and performed by Mariano Apicella. Contemporary accounts disagree slightly over its anticipated release timetable, but the released album belongs to 2011. ([*la Repubblica*](https://www.repubblica.it/spettacoli-e-cultura/2011/11/16/news/disco_apicella-25115968/); [*Corriere della Sera*](https://www.corriere.it/cronache/11_novembre_17/apicella-berlusconi-brano-esclusiva-cruccu_83f4735e-106d-11e1-a756-4c2fd73eac66.shtml))

- **2011:** *Cascasse il mondo*. A song text by Berlusconi from *Il vero amore*, with music and performance by Apicella. ([*Corriere della Sera*](https://www.corriere.it/cronache/11_novembre_17/apicella-berlusconi-brano-esclusiva-cruccu_83f4735e-106d-11e1-a756-4c2fd73eac66.shtml))

- **2011:** *Stay with me*. Despite its English title, a Neapolitan-language love song in the Berlusconi–Apicella corpus; Berlusconi supplied the words and Apicella the music and voice. ([Linkiesta](https://www.linkiesta.it/2011/11/apicella-che-noia-anche-ieri-una-serata-bunga-bunga-con-silvio/))

- **2011:** *Musica*. A song from *Il vero amore*, credited within the established division of labor between Berlusconi as lyricist and Apicella as composer-performer. ([Linkiesta](https://www.linkiesta.it/2011/11/apicella-che-noia-anche-ieri-una-serata-bunga-bunga-con-silvio/))

- **2011:** *Se tu non fossi tu*. A love-song text from *Il vero amore*, written by Berlusconi and musically realized by Apicella. ([Linkiesta](https://www.linkiesta.it/2011/11/apicella-che-noia-anche-ieri-una-serata-bunga-bunga-con-silvio/))
