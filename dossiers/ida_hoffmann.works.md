## Works by Ida Hofmann

### Manifestos and programmatic essays

- **1902:** *Wie gelangen wir Frauen zu harmonischen und gesunden Daseinsbedingungen? Offener Brief an die Verfasserin von „Eine Mutter für Viele“*. A self-published feminist manifesto in the form of an open letter, advocating transformed conditions of education, work, health, and intimate life for women; it acts as a program for reform rather than merely describing one. It appears in the standard Monte Verità bibliography compiled for Harald Szeemann’s 1978 exhibition and in Andreas Schwab’s scholarly source survey. [[Szeemann bibliography](https://sitterwerk-prod-public.s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/books/bib_GM00690369_20180618_134213.pdf), pp. 179–80; [Schwab](https://andreasschwab.ch/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/Monte-Verit%C3%A0_Scan_2.pdf), p. 21]

- **1905:** *Vegetabilismus! Vegetarismus! Blätter zur Verbreitung vegetarischer Lebensweise*. A self-published set of advocacy sheets: simultaneously a dietary manifesto and practical Lebensreform propaganda, promoting a wholly plant-based mode of living rather than simply discussing vegetarianism. [[Szeemann bibliography](https://sitterwerk-prod-public.s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/books/bib_GM00690369_20180618_134213.pdf), pp. 179–81; [Schwab](https://andreasschwab.ch/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/Monte-Verit%C3%A0_Scan_2.pdf), p. 21]

- **undated; commonly assigned 1915 or circa 1920:** *Beiträge zur Frauenfrage*. A collection of programmatic contributions on the “woman question.” Its date is genuinely unsettled: the Szeemann bibliography records no date and conjectures 1915, Schwab uses 1915, while later scholarship gives approximately 1920; the uncertainty should not be concealed. [[Szeemann bibliography](https://sitterwerk-prod-public.s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/books/bib_GM00690369_20180618_134213.pdf), pp. 184–85; [Schwab](https://andreasschwab.ch/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/Monte-Verit%C3%A0_Scan_2.pdf), p. 21; [Carstensen and Schmid bibliography](https://www.ssoar.info/ssoar/bitstream/document/95117/1/ssoar-2024-dumschat-rehfeldt_et_al-Literatur_am_Ende_Putting_Schopfung.pdf)]

### Chronicle and self-account

- **1906:** *Monte Verità. Wahrheit ohne Dichtung. Aus dem Leben erzählt*. A first-person history and ideological self-account of the settlement’s beginnings, issued as a booklet rather than a neutral institutional chronicle. Hofmann made the narrative, but the collective life it narrates cannot be treated as her solitary creation. [[Szeemann bibliography](https://sitterwerk-prod-public.s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/books/bib_GM00690369_20180618_134213.pdf), pp. 182–83; [Schwab](https://andreasschwab.ch/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/Monte-Verit%C3%A0_Scan_2.pdf), p. 21]

### Protocol for an unrealized institution

- **1905:** *Provisorische Statuten der vegetabilischen Gesellschaft des Monte Verità*. A forty-five-article protocol and promotional prospectus made jointly by Hofmann and Henri Oedenkoven for a proposed cooperative society. The organization was never put into practice, so the operative work is the printed project—the rules and declared intention—not a successfully constituted institution. [[Schwab, “Die Statuten”](https://andreasschwab.ch/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/Monte-Verit%C3%A0_Scan_2.pdf)]

### Collaborative life project

- **1900–1920:** *Monte Verità*. A collaboratively made settlement, nature-cure institution, social experiment, and way-of-life project founded by Hofmann with Henri Oedenkoven, Gusto and Karl Gräser, Lotte Hattemer, and others. It approaches a social *Gesamtkunstwerk* because diet, dress, dwelling, bodily practice, gender relations, labor, health, and eventually dance were treated as one composition; nevertheless, it resists attribution as Hofmann’s individual obra, and the founding collective soon fragmented. [[Andreas Schwab, *Historical Dictionary of Switzerland*](https://hls-dhs-dss.ch/it/articles/027825/2009-11-17/); [Limmat Verlag, *Sinnsuche und Sonnenbad*](https://www.limmatverlag.ch/programm/titel/388-sinnsuche-und-sonnenbad.html)]

### Reported work of uncertain bibliographical status

- **No date established:** *L’Importanza della Teosofia vera*. A reported Italian-language theosophical text, with neither place nor year securely identified in the accessible record. It is absent from the standard Szeemann and Schwab bibliographies used above, so its publication status, attribution, and exact form remain insufficiently verified; it should be registered as a reported text, not silently promoted into Hofmann’s secure canon. [[Biographical dossier and works list](https://www.gusto-graeser.info/Monteverita/Personen/HofmannIda/HoffmanIda.html)]
