## Works by Saint Eustace

### No attributable obra

- **No secure date:** *No surviving or attributable work*. The standard Greek dossier (BHG 641–643) and Latin repertory (BHL 2760–2771) catalogue anonymous or later texts **about** Eustace, not texts or objects made by him; BHG 643, for example, is explicitly assigned to Nicetas David Paphlagon. Because the earliest tradition is a hagiographic narrative of disputed historicity rather than contemporary documentation, no book, building, image, protocol, manifesto, project, or other *obra* can responsibly be attributed to Eustace—an absence that resists being catalogued as a work rather than constituting a lost-work entry. ([Pinakes/IRHT](https://pinakes.irht.cnrs.fr/notices/saint/322/); [ARLIMA](https://arlima.net/eh/eustache_saint.html); [Delehaye bibliography](https://arlima.net/eh/eustache_saint.html); [Encyclopaedia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/eustathius-acts-of/))
