## Works by Tony Hilton Royle Skyrme

### Wartime reports, computational methods, and dissertation

- **1943 or 1944:** *A Reduction in Neutron Density Caused by an Absorbing Disc* (MS-91). A classified British technical report on neutron-density corrections; contemporary bibliographies disagree between 1943 and 1944, so its date is not silently normalized. [S2][S3]
- **1944–1945:** *IBM Calculations of Implosion Hydrodynamics* and related calculations. Classified Los Alamos work rather than a single stable publication: surviving records include *IBM Calculations of Implosion Hydrodynamics—II (Problem 7)*, released as LA-219 on 21 February 1945. [S4]
- **written 1944–1945; issued 1947/1948:** *Treatment of Discontinuities in Lagrangian Integration of Symmetrical Hydrodynamic Problems* (LAMS-562). A technical report embodying Skyrme’s shock-fitting algorithm; the surviving publication date is later than its composition, and the modern historical study expressly treats it as a foundational computational method rather than a journal paper. [S4]
- **1946:** *Hydrodynamic Theory of the Reaction Zone in High Explosives* (MDDC-506). A 53-page declassified report and the work submitted for Skyrme’s Cambridge doctorate, treating stationary and finite-age detonation waves and two-dimensional flow. [S3]
- **1944–1946:** untitled or unstable-titled Los Alamos hydrodynamic calculations and memoranda. The historical reconstruction finds Skyrme’s staggered-grid and shock-fitting work distributed among laboratory reports, letters, and calculations; these resist treatment as a closed set of individually authored “works,” particularly because some remain classified or are known only from administrative records. [S4]

### Experimental and instrumental papers

- **1952:** *A Proportional Counter for Neutron Flux Measurements in the Energy Range 0.1 to 1 MeV*. A jointly authored instrumentation paper by Skyrme, P. R. Tunnicliffe, and A. G. Ward describing a neutron-flux counter. [S1]
- **1955:** *Helium-3 Filled Proportional Counter for Neutron Spectroscopy*. A jointly authored experimental-instrument paper by R. A. Batchelor, R. Aves, and Skyrme. [S1]

### Nuclear structure and effective-interaction papers

- **1952:** *The Theory of the Double Compton Effect*. A jointly authored theoretical paper by Franz Mandl and Skyrme; the Royal Society archive also preserves Peierls’s referee report on its submission. [S1][S5]
- **1954:** *A New Model for Nuclear Matter*. A theoretical journal paper proposing a model of nuclear matter, subsequently reprinted in the collected-works volume. [S1][S6]
- **1955:** *Meson Theory and Nuclear Matter*. A theoretical journal paper extending the nuclear-matter project through meson fields. [S6]
- **1955:** *Centre-of-Mass Effects in the Nuclear Shell-Model*. A jointly authored paper by J. P. Elliott and Skyrme on removing or evaluating centre-of-mass effects in shell-model calculations. [S1]
- **1956:** *The Alpha-Particle and Shell Models of the Nucleus*. A jointly authored paper by J. K. Perring and Skyrme relating antisymmetrized α-particle wavefunctions to shell-model wavefunctions; the collected edition treats it as a distinct strand of Skyrme’s work. [S7]
- **1956:** *The Nuclear Surface*. A theoretical paper developing the short-range effective interaction that became part of the “Skyrme interaction”; its original heading numbers it “CVII,” an issue-level sequence rather than part of the title’s conceptual substance. [S1][S8]
- **1956:** *The Nuclear Spin-Orbit Coupling*. A jointly authored paper by J. S. Bell and Skyrme, printed immediately after *The Nuclear Surface* and headed “CVIII.” [S1][S8]
- **1958–1959:** *The Effective Nuclear Potential*. A long theoretical paper in *Nuclear Physics* defining the effective interaction used in later Skyrme–Hartree–Fock work; databases variously label it 1958 or 1959 because the volume straddled those years. [S1][S8]
- **1958–1959:** *The Spin-Orbit Interaction in Nuclei*. A shorter companion paper in the same volume of *Nuclear Physics*; its dating likewise varies between 1958 and 1959. [S1][S8]

### Mesic-fluid and nonlinear-field-theory papers

- **1956:** *The Concept of a Mesic Fluid in Relation to Pseudoscalar Meson Theory*. A conference-proceedings paper in the supplement to *Il Nuovo Cimento*, presenting the mesic-fluid conception that fed into Skyrme’s nonlinear-field project. [S9]
- **1956:** *A Variational Method in Relativistic Quantum Field Theory*. A short methodological paper immediately following the mesic-fluid paper in *Il Nuovo Cimento*. [S9]
- **1958:** *A Non-Linear Theory of Strong Interactions*. A theoretical paper initiating the published sequence that sought to make nucleons as extended configurations of meson fields. [S1][S6]
- **1959:** *A Unified Model of K- and π-Mesons*. A theoretical paper extending the nonlinear program to kaons and pions. [S10]
- **1961:** *A Non-Linear Field Theory*. The paper formulating the stabilized nonlinear field theory now associated with the Skyrme model; it is a made mathematical model as well as a text. [S1][S10]
- **1961:** *Particle States of a Quantized Meson Field*. A theoretical paper addressing the particle states obtained by quantizing the field configurations. [S1][S10]
- **1962:** *A Model Unified Field Equation*. A jointly authored paper by J. K. Perring and Skyrme, using numerical modelling to study the field equation. [S11]
- **1962:** *A Unified Field Theory of Mesons and Baryons*. A theoretical synthesis identifying the topological number of the meson field with baryon number; it is the clearest published manifestation of the project later called the skyrmion. [S12]
- **1971:** *Kinks and the Dirac Equation*. A mathematical-physics paper connecting kink solutions with the Dirac equation and extending Skyrme’s soliton reasoning beyond the original three-dimensional model. [S1]
- **1984; published posthumously 1988:** *The Origins of Skyrmions*. A historical lecture delivered at the Cosener’s House Workshop on Skyrmions in November 1984 and published after Skyrme’s death; it is testimony about the project as much as a research paper. [S2][S10]

### Probability paper

- **1985:** *The Maximum of a Random Walk Whose Mean Path Has a Maximum*. A jointly authored probability paper by H. E. Daniels and Skyrme on the joint distribution of a random walk’s maximum and the time at which it occurs. [S13]

### Unpublished research projects and drafts

- **c. 1943:** *Phyllotaxy*. Manuscript calculations and problem material from Skyrme’s undergraduate period; the archive describes a project fragment, not a finished publication. [S2]
- **1946–1948:** *Introduction*; *Conformal Transformation*; *Final Honours Course: Dynamics Revision*; *Superfluidity*; *Superconductivity*; and *Relativity Theory (Supplementary to Eddington)*. Separate manuscript lecture texts preserved together; they resist classification as publications because their surviving form is pedagogical and sometimes incomplete. [S2]
- **c. 1952:** *Characteristics of a Theory T*. A one-page theoretical note later reproduced by R. H. Dalitz; its brevity and cryptic archival title make “work” a less secure category than “research note.” [S2]
- **probably 1952:** *Wall Corrections for a Monoenergetic Isotropic Reaction*. An unpublished typescript whose date was conjectured by Dalitz rather than supplied by Skyrme. [S2]
- **probably 1952:** *Wall Corrections in Proportional Counters*. An unpublished eight-page typescript; both title and approximate date derive from the surviving archival object. [S2]
- **probably 1955:** *Wall Corrections for He-3 Counter*. An unpublished typescript with calculations, tables, and graphs; the archive preserves it as project material rather than a completed paper. [S2]
- **probably 1952:** *Three Body Problem at Low Energies (Part 1)*. A nineteen-page typescript and seminar sheet; “Part 1” survives without a securely identified continuation, so the project is evidently incomplete. [S2]
- **probably 1953:** *Deuteron Cross-Sections for Heavy Nuclei at High Energies*. An unpublished typescript whose date is Dalitz’s suggestion. [S2]
- **probably 1953–1954:** *The Stripping of Fast Deuterons*. An unpublished ten-page typescript; its date is conjectural. [S2]
- **1955–1957:** *An Alternative Description of the Many-Body Problem*. A six-page manuscript draft within a larger folder of research notes. [S2]
- **1955–1957:** *Coulomb Excitation*; *Influence of Angular Momentum on Neutron Evaporation*; and *Variational Formula for Harmonic Vibrations*. Three short unpublished projects surviving as typescript or manuscript drafts rather than resolved articles. [S2]
- **1957:** *Distortion in the P-Shell*. Notes explicitly marked “not to be quoted in this form”; the warning makes the text’s resistance to being called a finished work part of its status. [S2]
- **1957–1958:** *Electrodynamics and a Fifth Dimension of Space*; *Generalisation of Quantum Field Theory*; and the draft labelled *Iso Spin &c*. Interrelated speculative field-theory projects preserved in four archival folders, none known to have reached publication. [S2]
- **1958:** *Time Reversal etc.*; *Spinor Analysis*; *Structure of the Nucleon*; *A Review*; *Meson Fields in Nuclear Matter*; and *The Lagrangean in Quantum Field Theory*. A sequence of unpublished drafts around the nonlinear-field program; some may be stages toward published papers rather than autonomous completed works. [S2]
- **1958–1959:** *Dispersion Relations in Elementary Particle Physics*. A course outline and seventy-nine pages of manuscript lectures made during Skyrme’s visiting professorship at the University of Pennsylvania. [S2]
- **1958–1959:** *Collective Motion*. Manuscript lectures from the Pennsylvania appointment, including notes on vibrational states. [S2]
- **1959:** *Nuclear Level Densities and the Shell Model*. A six-page joint draft by D. W. Lang and Skyrme prepared during Skyrme’s visit to the Australian National University; no completed publication is identified in the archival catalogue. [S2]
- **c. 1959:** *The Level Structure of A³⁷*. A joint typescript draft by M. B. Hooper and Skyrme; its date is inferred from its latest bibliographical reference. [S2]
- **1960:** *The Language and Methods of Field Theory*. A typescript lecture or exposition dated 17 October 1960, surviving in several differently corrected versions. [S2]
- **1964:** *Symmetry*. A seven-page paper delivered to the Malaysian Mathematical Society on 22 January 1964; it remained unpublished and is included by Dalitz among Skyrme’s unpublished research material. [S2]
- **undated:** *Quantum Mechanics (Supplementary to Dirac’s Principles of Quantum Mechanics)*. A twenty-page manuscript supplement; its lack of date and publication history prevents firmer classification. [S2]
- **undated:** *Development of the Non-Linear Field Theory in 3D*; an untitled companion draft; and *3D Transformations*. Three substantial typescripts in a folder labelled “3D Old”; they appear to be stages of one evolving project rather than three securely separable works. [S2]
- **undated:** *Analogies, 1 & 3 Dimensions*. A forty-page typescript missing page 21, accompanied by shorter Green-function drafts; incompleteness is material to its status. [S2]
- **undated:** *Meson-Nucleon Interactions* and *Nucleon Magnetic Moments*. Two substantial unpublished typescripts preserved with miscellaneous graphs, appendices, and notes. [S2]
- **undated:** *Collective Motion: Classical Dynamics*; *Alternative Treatment*; *Classical Comparisons*; an untitled draft; *Collective Nuclear Motion*; *Rotational States*; and *Collective Rotational Motion in Nuclei*. A stapled constellation of related drafts: an evolving project whose archival divisions should not be mistaken automatically for seven finished works. [S2]
- **undated:** *An Effective [Nuclear Potential]*. An eleven-page typescript with a damaged or incomplete title, preserved among the collective-motion drafts; the bracketed wording is an archival reconstruction, not a supplied title. [S2]
- **undated:** *The Conjectured Value of ε*; *Are Fermions Fundamental?*; *Mesons and Particles*; and an untitled draft headed *On the Particle Number*. Lecture and research drafts belonging to the nonlinear-field project; their order and dates are not fixed. [S2]
- **undated:** *The Effective Interaction Between Nucleons in Nuclei*. A thirteen-page manuscript draft, possibly preparatory to Skyrme’s published effective-potential work rather than an independent completed text. [S2]
- **undated:** *Multi-Valuedness*; *Construction of Fermion Operators*; *A. Conventional Theory*; and *Anticommutation*. Interrelated drafts on constructing fermionic behavior from bosonic fields; they survive as research material, not as a completed manifesto or treatise. [S2]
- **undated:** *Radiation Diffusion Equation*; *Diffraction from a Vibrating Source*; *Polarisation and Plural Scattering*; *Tensor Interaction*; *Brueckner Model: Relation to Meson Model*; and *On the Unitary Transformation*. Short technical drafts whose survival establishes activity but not finished-work status. [S2]

### Teaching corpora and applied mathematical consultations

- **1962–1964:** University of Malaya lecture courses, tutorial sheets, examinations, and solutions. This is a pedagogical corpus—including mechanics, calculus, probability, statistics, differential equations, Fourier theory, statics, electricity and magnetism—rather than a stable sequence of publishable “works”; the archive sometimes cannot distinguish Skyrme’s authorship from inherited departmental material. [S2]
- **1964–1987:** University of Birmingham undergraduate-course materials. Lecture texts, problems, solutions, and examinations survive for *Mathematics for Physicists*, *Mathematical Applications and Methods*, *Differential Equations*, Newtonian mechanics, probability and statistics, *Engineering Systems*, *Information Theory*, *Concepts of Quantum Mechanics*, complex-variable theory, and related courses; this is an evolving teaching practice rather than a closed oeuvre. [S2]
- **undated:** *Field Theory* and *Gauge Field Theories*. Two sets of postgraduate lecture notes, extensively paginated but not dated; their status is course material, not published monographs. [S2]
- **20 February 1985:** untitled physics-colloquium text on the question “what is matter?” A manuscript and typescript talk concerning Skyrme’s long-term field-theory interests; because the archive supplies no title, none is invented here. [S2]
- **1986 or 1987:** untitled transparencies for a research seminar on skyrmions. A visual teaching or presentation object, not a paper; even its year is uncertain in the catalogue. [S2]
- **1964–1987:** applied-mathematics consultancy files. Six folders contain calculations, notes, and computer printouts made while advising other university departments and outside clients, including the “tool heat” problem and “tilting a hogshead” for Wolverhampton and Dudley Breweries; the files resist individuation into authored works because they document collaborative problem-solving. [S2]
- **1940s–1980s:** eighteen research and seminar notebooks. These contain Skyrme’s calculations, experimental records, conference notes, travel records, and records of other people’s lectures; they are made objects and evidence of projects, but not everything written in them is Skyrme’s intellectual creation. [S2]
- **1940s–1980s:** photographic travel collections made by Tony and Dorothy Skyrme. The surviving attribution is joint and the photographs have not been catalogued as a Tony Skyrme catalogue raisonné, so they cannot responsibly be divided here into individual works. [S14]
