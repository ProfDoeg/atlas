## Works by Georg Wilhelm Friedrich Hegel

### Books and philosophical obra published by Hegel

- **1801:** *Dissertatio philosophica de orbitis planetarum*. A Latin habilitation dissertation on planetary orbits, submitted and printed as an academic qualification. [GW 5; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1801:** *Differenz des Fichte’schen und Schelling’schen Systems der Philosophie*. Hegel’s first published philosophical book, intervening in the dispute over post-Kantian idealism. [Oxford chronology](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/28104/chapter/212212873)

- **1807:** *System der Wissenschaft. Erster Theil: Die Phänomenologie des Geistes*. A book conceived as the first part of a system, though that projected continuation never appeared in the announced form; later editions usually call it simply *Phänomenologie des Geistes*. [GW 9; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1812–1813:** *Wissenschaft der Logik. Erster Band: Die objective Logik*. A two-book first volume comprising *Die Lehre vom Seyn* (1812) and *Die Lehre vom Wesen* (1813). [GW 11; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1816:** *Wissenschaft der Logik. Zweiter Band: Die subjective Logik oder die Lehre vom Begriff*. The concluding published volume of the *Logic*. [GW 12; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1817:** *Encyklopädie der philosophischen Wissenschaften im Grundrisse*. The first edition of a compact teaching handbook and synoptic presentation of Hegel’s system, made for use alongside his lectures rather than as a fully expanded treatise. [GW 13; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1820–1821:** *Naturrecht und Staatswissenschaft im Grundrisse: Grundlinien der Philosophie des Rechts*. A published lecture compendium and independent obra on abstract right, morality, ethical life, civil society, and the state; the title page bears 1821, while publication occurred in winter 1820–21. [GW 14; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1827:** *Encyklopädie der philosophischen Wissenschaften im Grundrisse*, second edition. A substantially changed and enlarged remaking of the 1817 handbook, not merely a reprint. [GW 19; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1830:** *Encyklopädie der philosophischen Wissenschaften im Grundrisse*, third edition. Hegel’s final published version of his systematic handbook, again revised and enlarged. [GW 20; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1831–1832:** *Wissenschaft der Logik. Erster Band: Die Lehre vom Seyn*, second edition. A radical rewriting of the doctrine of being completed shortly before Hegel’s death and published posthumously in 1832; it is Hegel’s work, but its publication was not his completed act. [GW 21; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

### Published interventions, translations, articles, reviews, and prefaces

- **1798:** *Vertrauliche Briefe über das vormalige staatsrechtliche Verhältnis des Waatlandes zur Stadt Bern*. An anonymously issued, extensively annotated German translation of Jean-Jacques Cart’s political letters: Hegel made the translation and apparatus, not the underlying French letters. [GW 2; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1801:** Reviews for the *Erlanger Literatur-Zeitung*. Short anonymous or unsigned acts of criticism whose individual attribution has required editorial reconstruction rather than resting on named publication. [Jaeschke-based works list](https://hegel.net/en/hegel-writings.htm)

- **1802:** *Über das Wesen der philosophischen Kritik überhaupt und ihr Verhältnis zum gegenwärtigen Zustand der Philosophie insbesondere*. The programmatic introduction to the *Kritisches Journal der Philosophie*, co-edited with Schelling; it functions partly as a manifesto for the journal’s mode of criticism. [GW 4; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1802:** *Wie der gemeine Menschenverstand die Philosophie nehme, dargestellt an den Werken des Herrn Krug*. A satirical-critical journal essay directed against Wilhelm Traugott Krug’s demand that idealism “deduce” ordinary particulars. [Jaeschke-based works list](https://hegel.net/en/hegel-writings.htm)

- **1802:** *Verhältniß des Skepticismus zur Philosophie*. A critical-journal essay and review of Gottlob Ernst Schulze that develops Hegel’s distinction between philosophical and modern skepticism. [Jaeschke-based works list](https://hegel.net/en/hegel-writings.htm)

- **1802:** *Glauben und Wissen*. A long critical-journal essay on Kant, Jacobi, and Fichte; although often republished as a book, its made form was an article. [GW 4; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1802–1803:** *Über die wissenschaftlichen Behandlungsarten des Naturrechts, seine Stelle in der praktischen Philosophie und sein Verhältnis zu den positiven Rechtswissenschaften*. A journal treatise on natural law, published in installments in the *Kritisches Journal*. [Jaeschke-based works list](https://hegel.net/en/hegel-writings.htm)

- **1807:** *Wer denkt abstract?* A short polished essay, apparently intended for public circulation but not published by Hegel; it therefore resists classification as either private manuscript or completed publication. [GW 5; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1807–1808:** Contributions to the *Bamberger Zeitung*. Editorial notices and newspaper pieces produced during Hegel’s editorship; anonymity and the collaborative manufacture of a newspaper make the boundaries of his authorship uneven. [GW 5; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1817:** *Beurteilung der Verhandlungen in der Versammlung der Landstände des Königreichs Württemberg im Jahr 1815 und 1816*. A political review-essay on the Württemberg estates, published in the *Heidelbergische Jahrbücher*. [Oxford chronology](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/28104/chapter/212212873)

- **1817:** Review of volume III of Friedrich Heinrich Jacobi’s works. A substantial review-essay rather than a freestanding book. [Oxford chronology](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/28104/chapter/212212873)

- **1821–1822:** *Erklärung* concerning Gustav Hugo’s review of the *Grundlinien*. A published reply defending the book; the critical edition treats it as an appendix to the *Philosophy of Right*. [GW 14.2; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1822:** Preface to Hermann Friedrich Wilhelm Hinrichs’s *Die Religion im inneren Verhältnisse zur Wissenschaft*. A commissioned prefatory essay—often known as *Vorrede zu Hinrichs*—whose object is another author’s book. [Jaeschke-based works list](https://hegel.net/en/hegel-writings.htm)

- **1827:** Review of Wilhelm von Humboldt’s essay on the *Bhagavad-Gītā*. A learned review in the *Jahrbücher für wissenschaftliche Kritik*, not a book about Indian philosophy despite later separate editions. [Oxford chronology](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/28104/chapter/212212873)

- **1827:** *Über das Sehen und die Farben*. A review and philosophical intervention concerning Goethe’s theory of color. [Jaeschke-based works list](https://hegel.net/en/hegel-writings.htm)

- **1828:** *Ueber die Bekehrten*. A brief newspaper polemic published in the *Berliner Schnellpost*. [GW 16; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1828:** Review of J. G. Hamann’s writings. A large review-essay in the *Jahrbücher für wissenschaftliche Kritik*, assembled after preparatory drafts. [Oxford chronology](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/28104/chapter/212212873)

- **1828:** Review of K. W. F. Solger’s posthumous writings and correspondence. A critical commemoration whose object and documentary materials were edited by others. [Oxford chronology](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/28104/chapter/212212873)

- **1829:** Review of Carl Friedrich Göschel’s *Aphorismen über Nichtwissen und absolutes Wissen*. A journal review that also publicly interprets and defends Hegel’s own philosophical position. [Oxford chronology](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/28104/chapter/212212873)

- **1829:** Reply to Karl Ernst Schubarth and Karl August Carganico. A journal polemic responding to *Ueber die Unvereinbarkeit der Hegel’schen Staatslehre mit dem obersten Lebens- und Entwickelungsprincip des Preußischen Staats*; preparatory fragments also survive. [GW 16; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1830:** *Oratio in sacris saecularibus tertiis Confessionis Augustanae*. A Latin ceremonial address delivered for the tercentenary of the Augsburg Confession: simultaneously a written text and a performed academic occasion. [GW 16; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1831:** Review of A. L. J. Ohlert’s *Der Idealrealismus*. One of Hegel’s last published reviews. [Oxford chronology](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/28104/chapter/212212873)

- **1831:** *Über die englische Reformbill*. A political article written for the *Allgemeine Preußische Staats-Zeitung*; publication was curtailed, so the complete essay survives as a work whose intended public manifestation was interrupted. [GW 16; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

### Early manuscripts, fragments, poems, and disputed attributions

- **1785–1800:** *Frühe Exzerpte*. A corpus of reading extracts and study notes rather than one authored “work”; the critical edition preserves them because they are material acts of intellectual formation. [GW 3; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1793–1794:** *Volksreligion und Christentum*. An unfinished, multiply reworked manuscript project on popular religion and Christianity; the familiar title is editorial, not a title fixed for publication by Hegel. [Jaeschke-based works list](https://hegel.net/en/hegel-writings.htm)

- **1794–1795:** Manuscripts on spirit, law, duties, religion, and philosophical encyclopedia. A group of pedagogical and philosophical drafts whose editorial titles—*Geisteslehre*, *Rechts-, Pflichten- und Religionslehre*, *Religionslehre*, and *Philosophische Enzyklopädie*—describe their subjects rather than certify separate finished works. [Jaeschke-based works list](https://hegel.net/en/hegel-writings.htm)

- **1795:** *Das Leben Jesu*. A completed manuscript retelling of Jesus’s life in rational-moral terms, never published by Hegel and not presented by him as scripture, biography, or novel. [Jaeschke-based works list](https://hegel.net/en/hegel-writings.htm)

- **1795–1796:** Manuscript on psychology and transcendental philosophy. An incomplete philosophical draft known through its subject and material form, without a securely authorial title. [Jaeschke-based works list](https://hegel.net/en/hegel-writings.htm)

- **1795–1796:** Studies toward *Die Positivität der christlichen Religion*. A layered manuscript project rather than a single completed treatise; Hegel subsequently revised it in Frankfurt. [Jaeschke-based works list](https://hegel.net/en/hegel-writings.htm)

- **1795–1796:** Fragments on constitutional and ecclesiastical law. Untitled working manuscripts conventionally grouped as *Staatsrecht und Kirchenrecht*. [Jaeschke-based works list](https://hegel.net/en/hegel-writings.htm)

- **August 1796:** *Eleusis. An Hölderlin*. A manuscript poem addressed to Friedrich Hölderlin; it is a poetic obra, not a philosophical book. [Jaeschke-based works list](https://hegel.net/en/hegel-writings.htm)

- **1797–1800:** Frankfurt manuscripts on Israelite, Jewish, Greek, and Christian religion. A shifting body of fragments and revisions, including material later editorially isolated as *Der Geist des Christentums und sein Schicksal*; the critical edition rejects the older fiction that these necessarily formed one finished “theological” work. [GW 2; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1797–1800:** Two fragments on religion. Late Frankfurt drafts without a stable authorial title or completed publication-form. [GW 2; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1797–1800:** Geometrical studies, notes on dialectic, and encyclopedic sketches. Working objects rather than readily bounded works; the archive preserves several independent calculations, diagrams, and conceptual fragments. [GW 2; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1798:** *Daß die Magistrate von den Bürgern gewählt werden müssen*. Four surviving fragments for a political pamphlet on Württemberg; the project remained unfinished and unpublished. [GW 2; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1798–1799:** Assessment of Schiller’s *Wallenstein*. A small critical manuscript, not issued by Hegel and not securely shaped as an independent publication. [GW 2; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1796–1797?:** *Das älteste Systemprogramm des deutschen Idealismus* (the manuscript beginning “eine Ethik”). The surviving copy is in Hegel’s hand, but the author remains unidentified and has variously been argued to be Hegel, Schelling, or Hölderlin; handwriting is therefore evidence of manufacture as copyist, not decisive evidence of intellectual authorship. [GW 2; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1799–1803:** *Fragmente einer Kritik der Verfassung Deutschlands*. Several reconceptions of an unfinished political project, traditionally published under the editorial title *Die Verfassung Deutschlands*; there is no single finished text silently recoverable from the fragments. [GW 5; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1799–1808:** *Entschluß*. A poem preserved with otherwise unconnected calculations and manuscript remnants; its date is not more securely fixed within this range by the critical-edition summary. [GW 5; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

### Jena system-projects and teaching manuscripts

- **1801–1802:** Fragments from lecture manuscripts on logic and metaphysics. Protocol-like teaching preparations for Hegel’s first Jena courses; incomplete survivals, not books. [GW 5; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1802–1803:** *System der Sittlichkeit*. A fair-copy system project surviving only fragmentarily and never published by Hegel. [GW 5; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1803:** Fragments from lecture manuscripts on logic, metaphysics, and philosophy of nature. Teaching materials whose survival is partial and whose later editorial grouping should not be mistaken for an authorially issued volume. [GW 5; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1803–1804:** *Jenaer Systementwurf I*. An editorially reconstructed system-draft complex, including logic, metaphysics, and philosophy of nature; unfinished and untitled as a single work by Hegel. [GW 6; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1804–1805:** *Jenaer Systementwurf II*. A second surviving system project, editorially named and materially incomplete. [GW 7; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1805–1806:** *Jenaer Systementwurf III*. A third system project encompassing nature and spirit; it survives as lecture manuscript material rather than a finished book. [GW 8; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1803–1806:** *Jenaer Notizenbuch*. A notebook of aphorisms, reminders, excerpts, and conceptual experiments; its resistance to being one “work” is part of its documentary identity. [Jaeschke-based works list](https://hegel.net/en/hegel-writings.htm)

### School protocols, speeches, and teaching obra

- **1808–1816:** *Nürnberger Gymnasialkurse*. Manuscripts and dictated materials for courses in philosophical propaedeutics, logic, psychology, law, duty, and religion; they form a pedagogical corpus rather than one book. Some content also survives only in pupils’ notebooks. [GW 10; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1809–1815:** *Nürnberger Gymnasialreden*. Ceremonial and administrative speeches delivered as rector, including end-of-year addresses and an address honoring his predecessor; these are performed institutional acts as well as texts. [GW 10; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1816:** Heidelberg inaugural address. A performed academic self-positioning preserved through Hegel’s manuscript, not a separately published book. [GW 18; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1818:** Berlin inaugural address. A university performance and manuscript marking Hegel’s assumption of his Berlin chair. [GW 18; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

### Lecture manifestations preserved by other hands

- **1801–1802; 1817; 1823–1826; 1828–1829; 1831:** *Vorlesungen über die Wissenschaft der Logik*. A changing sequence of oral courses, not one stable Hegel-authored text; ten student transcripts preserve particular manifestations, while later “additions” are editorial compilations. [GW 23; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1819–1820; 1821–1822; 1823–1824; 1825–1826; 1828:** *Vorlesungen über die Philosophie der Natur*. Six Berlin lecture courses preserved through multiple student transcripts and fragments of Hegel’s notes. The familiar posthumous *Philosophy of Nature* with “Zusätze” is Michelet’s compilation, not a book Hegel made in that form. [GW 24; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1822; 1825; 1827–1828:** *Vorlesungen über die Philosophie des subjektiven Geistes*. Three lecture manifestations based on successive editions of the *Encyclopaedia* and preserved chiefly in students’ notes; the critical edition refuses to fuse them into a timeless single text. [GW 25; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1817–1818; 1818–1819; 1819–1820; 1821–1822; 1822–1823; 1824–1825; 1831:** *Vorlesungen über die Philosophie des Rechts*. Seven courses, the last broken off by Hegel’s death; all are known through student transcripts, supplemented by Hegel’s marginal notes and dictations. [GW 26; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1822–1823; 1824–1825; 1826–1827; 1830–1831:** *Vorlesungen über die Philosophie der Weltgeschichte*. Four oral courses; the first course’s prepared manuscript is lost, and all complete course forms survive through auditors’ notes. The posthumous synthetic *Philosophy of History* is consequently an editorial creation from Hegelian materials, not a text Hegel completed. [GW 27; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1820–1821; 1823; 1826; 1828–1829:** *Vorlesungen über die Philosophie der Kunst*. Four Berlin courses and Hegel’s fullest manifestation of his aesthetics, preserved substantially only in student transcripts. Hotho’s posthumous *Vorlesungen über die Ästhetik* is a composite editorial work and cannot simply be treated as Hegel’s authored book. [GW 28; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1821; 1824; 1827; 1831:** *Vorlesungen über die Philosophie der Religion*. Four distinct lecture courses, each differently conceived; the 1821 course also has a substantial authorial manuscript, while the received posthumous “work” combines materials that Hegel did not combine. [GW 29; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1829:** *Vorlesungen über die Beweise vom Dasein Gottes*. A lecture sequence supported by Hegel’s manuscript and secondary transmission; its exact textual form is not equivalent to any later editor’s continuous book. [GW 18 and 29; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1805–1806; 1816–1817; 1817–1818; 1819; 1820–1821; 1823–1824; 1825–1826; 1827–1828; 1829–1830; 1831:** *Vorlesungen über die Geschichte der Philosophie*. A long-running oral project repeatedly remade; no transcripts are presently known for the Jena or Heidelberg courses, whereas every Berlin series is represented, and the final 1831 course ended during its introduction. Michelet’s posthumous volumes are compilations, not a single Hegel-authored text. [GW 30; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

### Surviving lecture manuscripts and marginal projects

- **1816–1831:** Manuscripts and notes for lectures on logic, metaphysics, nature, anthropology, psychology, subjective spirit, right, religion, art, world history, and the history of philosophy. This is a corpus of protocols and supports for performance, mostly fragmentary; only the religion manuscript approaches continuous book-length exposition. [GW 13, 14, 17–18; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1820 and 1823:** Manuscripts for the history of philosophy. Prepared lecture texts surviving alongside, but not identical with, what auditors heard and recorded. [GW 18; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1820s:** Two fragments on the philosophy of art. Authorial remnants of Hegel’s aesthetics, which otherwise exists most fully as the manifestation recorded by students. [GW 18; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1828 and 1830:** Introductions to the philosophy of world history. Two separately prepared manuscripts reflecting successive versions of the project rather than chapters of a finished book. [GW 18; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **Before 1831:** Fragment on the proofs of God’s existence and fragment on the cosmological proof. Incomplete authorial manuscript material, some of it surviving only through secondary transmission. [GW 18; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1817–1831:** Annotated personal copies of the *Encyclopaedia* and *Philosophy of Right*. Printed books transformed into working objects by inserted leaves, corrections, and extensive manuscript notes; these are neither wholly new books nor merely passive copies. [GW 13–14; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

### Notes, excerpts, correspondence, and institutional writing

- **1809–1831:** *Exzerpte und Notizen*. At least 200 surviving manuscript units—113 from the Rosenkranz portion of the estate, 73 deposited with the Royal Library, and additional pieces elsewhere—containing extracts from books, journals, and newspapers. They resist treatment as 200 titled “works”: their made form is a dispersed working archive. [GW 22; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1785–1831:** Letters by Hegel. A large corpus of individually situated communications rather than a single authored book; later volumes titled *Briefe von und an Hegel* also include letters written to him and therefore cannot in their entirety be counted as things he made. [GW edition description](https://meiner.de/fmv_de/programm/editionen-werkausgaben/g-w-f-hegel-gesammelte-werke)

- **1808–1831:** Official and administrative correspondence. School, university, editorial, and governmental documents produced in Hegel’s institutional offices; authorship may be shared, formulaic, or secretarial, so each document does not automatically qualify as an autonomous philosophical work. [GW edition description](https://meiner.de/fmv_de/programm/editionen-werkausgaben/g-w-f-hegel-gesammelte-werke)

- **1826–1831:** Editorial work for the *Jahrbücher für wissenschaftliche Kritik*. Hegel helped establish and direct a journal, solicited and processed contributions, and wrote reviews and replies; the journal as a whole is a collective project, not solely his obra. [Oxford chronology](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/28104/chapter/212212873)

### Things often called Hegel’s works that he did not make in their received form

- **1832–1845:** *Werke. Vollständige Ausgabe durch einen Verein von Freunden des Verewigten*. A posthumous collected edition made by Hegel’s associates. It transmits Hegel’s writings but also manufactures composite lecture-books, so the edition itself is not Hegel’s obra. [GW editorial principles](https://meiner.de/fmv_de/programm/editionen-werkausgaben/g-w-f-hegel-gesammelte-werke)

- **1832:** *Vorlesungen über die Philosophie der Religion*, edited by Philipp Konrad Marheineke. A posthumous synthesis from Hegel’s manuscript and lecture transcripts; attributable to Hegel as a course-project, but not as a book whose final wording and architecture he authorized. [GW 29; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1833–1836:** *Vorlesungen über die Geschichte der Philosophie*, edited by Karl Ludwig Michelet. A posthumous composite built from different course-years; it is a major manifestation of Hegel’s teaching but also an editorial obra by Michelet. [GW 30; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **1835–1838:** *Vorlesungen über die Ästhetik*, edited by Heinrich Gustav Hotho. A highly influential synthetic text assembled from Hegel’s materials and student notes; modern scholarship disputes how faithfully Hotho’s continuous book represents any lecture Hegel actually delivered. [GW 28; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **Posthumous:** *Vorlesungen über die Philosophie der Geschichte*. The familiar book is an editorial amalgam of introductions, manuscripts, and reports from several courses; the attributable obra is Hegel’s recurrent lecture project, not this stabilized single text. [GW 27; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)

- **Posthumous:** The *Zusätze* to the *Encyclopaedia*. Additions compiled by Hegel’s students and editors from manuscripts, excerpts, and lecture notes. They preserve Hegelian material but their selection, combination, and often their wording resist attribution to Hegel as author. [GW 24–25; Hegel-Archiv](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/i/hegel_edition/works/works.html.de)
