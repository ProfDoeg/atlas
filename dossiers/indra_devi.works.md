## Works by Indra Devi

### Books and written protocols

- **1948:** *Yoga: The Technique of Health and Happiness*. Her first book, published in India, is a practical hatha-yoga protocol combining postures, breathing, diet, relaxation, and spiritual interpretation; Devi herself identified it as her first book, while later reference bibliographies sometimes omit it or misdate reprints. ([International Vegetarian Union](https://ivu.org/hv-congress-1957-congress-wvc57-souvenir-index.html?catid=184&id=3902%3Acongress-wvc57-souvenir-devi&view=article); [Encyclopedia of Occultism and Parapsychology](https://www.encyclopedia.com/science/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/devi-indra-1899))

- **1953:** *Forever Young, Forever Healthy: Simplified Yoga for Modern Living*. A popular instructional book that recast hatha yoga as an accessible regimen for health, beauty, relaxation, and everyday life; some bibliographies give 1954, apparently reflecting a later edition rather than the work’s first publication. ([Elliott Goldberg’s scholarly account](https://www.simonandschuster.net/books/The-Path-of-Modern-Yoga/Elliott-Goldberg/9781620555675); [Indra Devi bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indra_Devi#Works))

- **1959:** *Yoga for Americans: A Complete Six-Week Course for Home Practice*. A six-week domestic practice protocol rather than a general treatise: illustrated lessons organize postures, breathing, relaxation, diet, and meditation for self-instruction. *Yoga para todos* is its Spanish translation/adaptation, not a separate original obra; its editions retain the 1959 copyright. ([Google Books](https://books.google.com/books?id=RtmREQAAQBAJ); [Spanish-edition description](https://www.todocoleccion.net/libros-segunda-mano/yoga-para-todos-indra-devi~x520694907))

- **1963:** *Renew Your Life Through Yoga: The Indra Devi Method for Relaxation Through Rhythmic Breathing*. An instructional book centered on rhythmic breathing and relaxation; references occasionally assign later dates to reissues, but the standard bibliography dates the original work to 1963. ([Encyclopedia of Occultism and Parapsychology](https://www.encyclopedia.com/science/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/devi-indra-1899); [Indra Devi bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indra_Devi#Works))

- **1991:** *Sai Yoga*. A short illustrated protocol joining asana to contemplation, self-analysis, and devotion to Sathya Sai Baba. Devi presented “Sai Yoga” as a newly named practice; the later *Yoga y espiritualidad: Sai Yoga* is an expanded or retitled manifestation of this work rather than securely a new text. ([1991 edition evidence](https://www.scribd.com/document/640021468/Sai-Yoga-Indra-Devi); [later-edition description](https://www.quade.com.ar/espiritualidad/39485-yoga-y-espiritualidad-9789501561715.html))

- **1995:** *Respirar bien para vivir mejor*. A Spanish-language breathing manual and practical protocol, assembled with the editorial collaboration of Iana and David Lifar; their acknowledged role means sole authorship should not be assumed simply from the cover attribution to Devi. ([Indra Devi International](https://www.indradevi-international.net.ar/); [bibliographic record](https://cofubace.bcu.gub.uy/opac_css/index.php?dcote=&id=27&lcote=0&location=7&lvl=section_see&main=&nb_per_page_custom=25&nbr_lignes=12979&nc=0&page=414&plettreaut=&ssub=0))

- **1996:** *Palabras desde el corazón*. A collection of spiritual reflections attributed to Devi but compiled by Marilén Stengel. It therefore resists classification as a wholly authored book: it is also an editorially constituted post-teaching collection. ([Vicente López municipal-library record](https://bibliotecamunicipal.vicentelopez.gov.ar/meran/opac-detail.pl?id1=10990); [publisher record](https://www.casadellibro.com/libro-palabras-desde-el-corazon/9789501516050/519215))

- **1999:** *Yoga y espiritualidad: Sai Yoga*. A revised, expanded, or retitled manifestation of the 1991 *Sai Yoga*, presenting postural practice as simultaneously physical, mental, contemplative, and devotional. Sources call it both a new book and a “new edition” of the earlier text, so its independence as an obra is uncertain. ([Casa Asia bibliography](https://static.casaasia.es/media/asset_publics/resources/000/017/923/original/filosofia_india-BCN.pdf); [edition description](https://www.quade.com.ar/espiritualidad/39485-yoga-y-espiritualidad-9789501561715.html))

- **2000:** *Una mujer de tres siglos*. A Spanish-language autobiography or memoir, and thus a constructed life narrative rather than a yoga protocol. It was published near the end of Devi’s life; available bibliographies attribute it to her without identifying a collaborator or ghostwriter. ([bibliographic record](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL7534916W/Una_Mujer_de_Tres_Siglos); [Indra Devi International](https://www.indradevi-international.net.ar/))

- **By 2000–2002, unfinished or uncertain:** *Book about Sai Yoga* [fixed title unknown]. A contemporary biographical reference reported that Devi was working on such a book in 2000; it may have referred to a revision of *Sai Yoga* or *Yoga y espiritualidad*, rather than an independent manuscript. Its survival, completion, and title are unverified, so it is a project, not a securely extant obra. ([Encyclopedia of Occultism and Parapsychology](https://www.encyclopedia.com/science/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/devi-indra-1899))

### Spoken-word and instructional recordings

- **1965:** *Yoga for Americans*. A spoken-word instructional LP translating the six-week book’s practice into timed, embodied direction; it is not merely an edition of the printed text because breathing, relaxation, and postural sequences are performed in sound. The date is reported in biographical sources, while surviving commercial records sometimes assign it only approximately to the 1960s or 1970s. ([record description and catalogue number](https://allnightflightrecords.com/products/indra-devi-yoga-for-americans); [biographical discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indra_Devi))

- **1965:** *Indra Devi Presents Concentration & Meditation* (also catalogued as *Relaxation and Concentration*). A spoken meditation-and-concentration LP on Mace Records; the inconsistent titles attached to the same catalogue family make its canonical title uncertain. ([discographic record](https://www.e-profession.com/records/Madam_Indra_Devi_Mobile_record.html); [biographical discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indra_Devi))

- **Date not established:** *Renew Your Life with Yoga*. A recorded instructional talk or series of talks later circulated on audio media. It should not be silently equated with the 1963 book: the surviving description establishes a recording but not whether its verbal text is identical, newly performed, or subsequently edited. ([recording notice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indra_Devi))

- **Dates and original recording circumstances not established:** *Charlando con Mataji 1: Sobre la vida*, *Charlando con Mataji 2: Sobre el yoga*, and *Charlando con Mataji 3*. Three compact-disc compilations of Devi speaking, retained by her institutional successors. These resist treatment as three works made solely by Devi because the surviving objects may be posthumous editorial selections from talks rather than recordings she conceived as albums. ([Indra Devi International catalogue](https://www.indradevi-international.net.ar/newsletter_eshop.htm))

### Embodied teaching systems

- **From 1939:** *Indra Devi’s gentle hatha-yoga method* [no single fixed title]. An embodied teaching system developed through classes in Shanghai and subsequently in Hollywood: a deliberately small repertory of slowly practiced postures, rhythmic breathing, and prolonged relaxation. It is an obra distributed through bodies, lessons, books, and recordings rather than a single text, and its debt to Krishnamacharya makes it a transformation of inherited practice rather than an invention ex nihilo. ([Michelle Goldberg’s biographical account](https://time.com/3909621/the-woman-who-hooked-hollywood-stars-on-yoga/); [Elliott Goldberg, *The Path of Modern Yoga*](https://www.simonandschuster.net/books/The-Path-of-Modern-Yoga/Elliott-Goldberg/9781620555675))

- **From the late 1960s; named by 1991:** *Sai Yoga*. A devotional reformulation of Devi’s postural method in which bodily positions become occasions for meditation, self-examination, and devotion to Sathya Sai Baba. It is simultaneously a protocol, a lineage claim, and a living practice, so neither the 1991 book nor any one class exhausts the obra. ([1991 book evidence](https://www.scribd.com/document/640021468/Sai-Yoga-Indra-Devi); [description of the method](https://www.quade.com.ar/espiritualidad/39485-yoga-y-espiritualidad-9789501561715.html))

### Schools and institutional projects

- **1939–1946:** *Shanghai yoga school* [formal name not established]. Devi opened and maintained a school in Shanghai, initially teaching at Soong Mei-ling’s house and continuing through the Japanese occupation. This was an institutional and pedagogical project, not a text; sources describe it as the first modern yoga school or classes in China, but phrase that priority claim cautiously rather than conclusively. ([Encyclopedia of Occultism and Parapsychology](https://www.encyclopedia.com/science/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/devi-indra-1899); [biographical account](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indra_Devi))

- **1948 onward:** *Hollywood yoga studio, 8806 Sunset Boulevard*. A teaching space and social institution through which Devi fashioned yoga as an accessible practice for American householders and performers. It is an obra of pedagogy and cultural translation rather than architecture attributable to her. ([Michelle Goldberg’s biographical account](https://time.com/3909621/the-woman-who-hooked-hollywood-stars-on-yoga/); [Elliott Goldberg, *The Path of Modern Yoga*](https://www.simonandschuster.net/books/The-Path-of-Modern-Yoga/Elliott-Goldberg/9781620555675))

- **1961–1977:** *Indra Devi Foundation / International Training Center for Yoga Teachers, Rancho Cuchumá, Tecate*. A residential school and teacher-training project established on a ranch purchased by Sigfrid Knauer; because property, financing, and direction were shared, it should not be treated as Devi’s unaided creation. The center closed when she moved to Bangalore. ([institutional history](https://www.indradevi-international.net.ar/); [biographical account](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indra_Devi))

- **1988–2020:** *Fundación Indra Devi, Buenos Aires*. An institution created by Iana and David Lifar to continue Devi’s teaching, with Devi as its living source and supervising master rather than clearly its legal or sole founder. Its name and pedagogy belong to her legacy, but the institution resists attribution as something she alone made. ([Indra Devi International institutional history](https://www.indradevi-international.net.ar/))

### Performance and self-fashioning

- **1920s:** *Touring performances with the Blue Bird theatre company* [individual roles and dates not securely identified]. Devi worked as an actor and dancer in this European touring ensemble. These were collaborative, ephemeral performances, not independently authored works; the surviving scholarship does not supply a reliable role-by-role catalogue. ([biographical account](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indra_Devi); [Michelle Goldberg’s biography overview](https://www.hachette.com.au/michelle-goldberg/the-goddess-pose-the-audacious-life-of-indra-devi-the-woman-who-helped-bring-yoga-to-the-west))

- **Late 1920s:** *Indian “temple dance” performances* [no fixed titles]. Devi devised or performed Indian-styled dances in Theosophical and social settings before receiving formal authority as a yoga teacher. They resist being called discrete works because titles, choreography, and dates do not survive in the consulted scholarly record. ([biographical account](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indra_Devi))

- **1930:** *Sher-e-Arab* (*Arabian Knight*). An Indian feature film in which Devi appeared under her newly adopted stage name. The film itself was directed by Bhagwati Mishra and is not her authored obra; what belongs in her corpus is the credited performance, whose precise role is not identified in the consulted sources. ([biographical account](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indra_Devi); [Michelle Goldberg’s biography overview](https://www.hachette.com.au/michelle-goldberg/the-goddess-pose-the-audacious-life-of-indra-devi-the-woman-who-helped-bring-yoga-to-the-west))

- **By 1930; consolidated after 1947:** *Indra Devi*. A stage name, public persona, and eventually legal identity fashioned from Eugenia Peterson’s successive lives as dancer, diplomat’s wife, yogini, author, and “Mataji.” It is not conventionally a work, yet its resistance to that word is material: the persona was the medium binding her books, clothing, performances, institutions, and teaching into something approaching a Gesamtkunstwerk. ([Michelle Goldberg’s biographical account](https://time.com/3909621/the-woman-who-hooked-hollywood-stars-on-yoga/); [biographical chronology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indra_Devi))
