# Bill Gates: Research Dossier

**Subject:** William Henry Gates III  
**Coverage:** Birth (28 October 1955) through 24 August 2026  
**Status:** Living  
**Method:** Chronological documentary reconstruction. “Documented,” “reported,” “alleged,” “denied,” “found,” and “unverified” are used deliberately. Corporate conduct is not automatically attributed to Gates personally; institutional impact is not credited to one donor where it resulted from a coalition.

## Basic Identifying Information

- **Full legal name:** William Henry Gates III.
- **Common name:** Bill Gates.
- **Family nickname:** “Trey,” referring to “the third.”
- **No documented pseudonym.** He published his mathematics paper as **William H. Gates**.
- **Born:** 28 October 1955, Seattle, Washington, United States.
- **Citizenship:** United States.
- **Principal descriptions:** programmer; software entrepreneur; co-founder, chief executive, chairman, and chief software architect of Microsoft; investor; author; philanthropist; chair of the Gates Foundation; founder of Breakthrough Energy; founder and chair of TerraPower.
- **Present status (24 August 2026):** alive, aged 70. The Gates Foundation identifies him as chair; Microsoft’s 2020 announcement said he would remain a technology adviser after leaving its board.
- **Principal residences and bases:** Seattle during childhood; Cambridge, Massachusetts, while at Harvard; Albuquerque, New Mexico, during Microsoft’s first years; Bellevue and then the Seattle region after Microsoft returned to Washington; a long-built Lake Washington estate in Medina, Washington, popularly called **Xanadu 2.0**. He has also owned or controlled other residential, ranch, equestrian, and agricultural properties through personal entities; ownership and post-divorce allocation are not fully public.

## Family and Ancestry

### Parents, siblings, and social background

Gates was the middle child and only son of **William Henry Gates II** (commonly styled Bill Gates Sr., 1925–2020) and **Mary Ann Maxwell Gates** (1929–1994). His elder sister is **Kristianne “Kristi” Gates Blake**; his younger sister is **Elizabeth “Libby” Gates Armintrout**. Published genealogical accounts describe principally English, German, and Scots-Irish/Irish ancestry, but no single archival genealogy was located for every branch.

Bill Gates Sr. was a prominent Seattle lawyer and a co-founder of the firm that became Preston Gates & Ellis. Mary Gates, a University of Washington graduate and former teacher, became a powerful civic volunteer and corporate director: she led United Way organizations, served on the University of Washington Board of Regents, and sat on corporate boards. Her father, **James Willard Maxwell**, was a banker; the maternal family therefore supplied both civic and financial connections. The children grew up in comfortable upper-middle-class circumstances, not poverty. Gates has repeatedly acknowledged the decisive advantages of a stable family, private schooling, and early computer access.

Mary Gates served with IBM chairman **John Opel** in the United Way network. Contemporary obituaries report that she spoke with Opel in 1980 about IBM’s dealings with Microsoft. This connection may have improved Microsoft’s visibility, but the popular story that she simply “gave” Microsoft the IBM operating-system contract is too strong: IBM’s PC team separately approached Microsoft because of its existing language business, and the operating-system transaction involved multiple executives and negotiations.

Mary Gates died of breast cancer on 10 June 1994. Bill Gates Sr. later became a central steward of his son’s philanthropy and co-chair of the foundation; he died in 2020 after Alzheimer’s disease.

### Marriage, partner, children, and grandchildren

Gates met **Melinda Ann French**, a Microsoft product manager, after she joined the company in 1987. They married on **1 January 1994** on Lānaʻi, Hawaii. Their children are:

- **Jennifer Katharine Gates Nassar** (born 1996), physician and equestrian; married equestrian Nayel Nassar. Their daughters Leila (born 2023) and Mia (born 2024) made Gates a grandfather.
- **Rory John Gates** (born 1999), the least publicly visible of the three.
- **Phoebe Adele Gates** (born 2002), Stanford graduate and entrepreneur/media figure.

The couple announced their separation in May 2021; a King County court finalized the divorce in August 2021. The **petition itself did not cite Jeffrey Epstein**. It described the marriage as “irretrievably broken” and asked the court to enforce a private separation contract. [*The Wall Street Journal* reported](https://www.wsj.com/business/melinda-gates-was-meeting-with-divorce-lawyers-since-2019-to-end-marriage-with-bill-gates-11620579924) that Melinda began consulting divorce lawyers in 2019, around the public disclosure of Gates’s meetings with Epstein. In 2022 she said the Epstein meetings were one among “many things” contributing to the breakdown. The evidentiary distinction matters: Epstein was not a stated ground in the filed petition, but was later identified by Melinda as a factor in her decision.

Gates had a relationship in the 1980s with venture capitalist **Ann Winblad**. A 1997 *Time* profile reported that, with Melinda’s knowledge, Gates and Winblad continued an annual weekend at her North Carolina beach cottage after his marriage. Gates acknowledged marital infidelity in later statements and in 2026 congressional testimony; allegations and admissions are detailed below.

Since 2023 Gates has appeared publicly with **Paula Hurd**, widow of Oracle co-CEO Mark Hurd. In February 2025 he publicly called her his “serious girlfriend.”

## Childhood and Early Life, 1955–1973

Gates grew up in Seattle’s Laurelhurst/Sand Point milieu. Family accounts describe a competitive household and a child who read reference works, argued intensely, resisted parental direction, and had difficulty with ordinary social expectations. His parents took him to a therapist during adolescence. In his 2025 memoir he wrote that, if raised in the present era, he probably would have been assessed as being on the autism spectrum. **This is retrospective self-description, not a documented clinical diagnosis.** He also disclosed youthful experimentation with LSD; no evidence of addiction was found.

At **Lakeside School**, a private boys’ school that later became coeducational, a Mothers’ Club rummage sale financed access to a Teletype terminal connected to a remote computer. Gates, **Paul Allen**, **Kent Evans**, and **Ric Weiland** became unusually experienced programmers while still teenagers. Gates wrote a tic-tac-toe program, learned BASIC, and spent large amounts of time testing systems. The group found bugs in Computer Center Corporation’s system and exchanged debugging labor for access after being temporarily banned for exploiting it.

The school’s and region’s institutional resources were decisive. Teacher **Bill Dougall** encouraged computing; **Fred Wright** and other Lakeside adults helped turn the students’ obsession into work. Gates and Evans built a class-scheduling program. Evans died in a climbing accident in 1972, a formative loss Gates has repeatedly described as devastating; Allen helped complete the scheduling work.

Gates and Allen also worked with Information Sciences Inc. and explored an automated traffic-counting business. Their **Traf-O-Data** system used traffic tapes and an Intel 8008-based device to prepare municipal traffic reports. It earned some revenue but did not become a durable company. Gates served briefly as a U.S. congressional page in 1972. He graduated from Lakeside in 1973 as a National Merit Scholar and reportedly scored 1590/1600 on the SAT.

## Education and Formation

Gates entered **Harvard College** in autumn 1973. He did not complete a degree and never declared a concentration. He took demanding mathematics and computer-science courses, lived in Currier House, and met **Steve Ballmer**, who later became Microsoft’s president and CEO.

In a combinatorics course associated with **Harry Lewis**, Gates worked on the pancake-sorting problem. With computer scientist **Christos Papadimitriou**, he published “Bounds for Sorting by Prefix Reversal” in *Discrete Mathematics* (1979). The paper gave upper and lower bounds that remained important for decades. It is evidence that Gates was not merely a salesman: he was an able programmer and mathematical problem-solver, although he did not build an academic research career.

During summer 1974 he worked near Allen at Honeywell in Massachusetts. When the January 1975 issue of *Popular Electronics* featured MITS’s Altair 8800, Allen and Gates contacted **Ed Roberts** at MITS and claimed they had a BASIC interpreter. They had not yet completed it. Gates, Allen, and Harvard student **Monte Davidoff** built the program on a PDP-10 simulator; Allen flew to Albuquerque for the demonstration. It worked. Gates took a leave from Harvard and moved to New Mexico. Harvard later awarded him an honorary degree in 2007, but this did not convert his incomplete undergraduate study into an earned bachelor’s degree.

### Formative intellectual influences

- Early computing culture: BASIC, time-sharing, DEC PDP systems, Intel microprocessors, and the conviction that software would become a separately valuable product.
- Family culture: competition, organized civic service, professional networks, and his mother’s insistence that wealth carried public obligations.
- Business and strategic collaborators: Paul Allen, Ed Roberts, Steve Ballmer, Kazuhiko Nishi, and later Warren Buffett.
- Scientific and philanthropic reading: public-health statistics, energy systems, agriculture, and technological “leverage.” Gates’s public philosophy became a data-oriented, technocratic optimism: markets and states often fail the poor, but science, measurement, advance purchasing, and institutional partnerships can redirect incentives.

## Early Career and Microsoft Formation, 1975–1980

### “Micro-Soft,” MITS, and commercial software

Gates and Allen formed **Micro-Soft** in Albuquerque in 1975, initially to sell Altair BASIC. Allen was the technical partner on-site at MITS; Gates managed licensing, coding, contracts, and expansion to other microprocessors. The hyphen soon disappeared. Early collaborators included Monte Davidoff, Ric Weiland, **Marc McDonald**, and **Gordon Letwin**.

Gates’s **“Open Letter to Hobbyists”** (dated 3 February 1976 and circulated through computer-club publications) condemned unauthorized copying of Altair BASIC and argued that unpaid duplication discouraged professional software. It became a foundational text in the transition from hobbyist sharing toward proprietary mass-market software. Admirers treat it as a defense of software labor; critics treat it as an early declaration of enclosure against communal computing. Both readings arise from the same document.

The MITS relationship deteriorated over distribution and licensing. Arbitration in 1977 allowed Microsoft to retain and license BASIC independently. Gates had traffic-law encounters in New Mexico, producing the widely reproduced 1977 Albuquerque mugshot; these were traffic matters, not evidence of serious criminality.

Microsoft moved from Albuquerque to **Bellevue, Washington, on 1 January 1979**. Japanese entrepreneur **Kazuhiko Nishi** helped expand the firm in Japan through ASCII Microsoft. By 1980 Microsoft supplied programming languages to many microcomputer platforms, including Apple machines.

## Chronological Life History

### 1980–1985: IBM, DOS, incorporation, and rupture with Allen

IBM approached Microsoft during the secret development of its personal computer. Microsoft initially directed IBM toward Digital Research for CP/M. When that negotiation failed, Microsoft agreed to supply an operating system. It licensed **86-DOS/QDOS**, written by **Tim Paterson** for Seattle Computer Products (SCP), adapted it for IBM, and bought the rights in 1981. Microsoft’s decisive commercial achievement was not inventing DOS from nothing; it was acquiring, improving, and licensing it while retaining the right to supply compatible manufacturers. The IBM PC launched on 12 August 1981 with PC DOS among its software options. As clones proliferated, Microsoft’s MS-DOS became an industry standard.

Microsoft reincorporated in Washington on 25 June 1981. Gates became president and chairman; Allen was vice-president and vice-chairman. Their equity split had already shifted in Gates’s favor because Gates argued that he was working more intensively. Allen was diagnosed with Hodgkin lymphoma in 1982 and left active work in 1983. In his 2011 memoir, Allen alleged that he overheard Gates and Ballmer discussing dilution of his stake during his illness. Gates and Ballmer did not carry out the contemplated dilution; Gates later called the episode a mistake in judgment. Allen retained shares that made him a billionaire. The friendship recovered partially, though Allen’s account records lasting grievance.

Gates drove a high-pressure internal culture: long hours, exacting code reviews, combative meetings, and aggressive recruitment. Former colleagues describe both exceptional technical command and humiliating criticism. There is abundant evidence of abrasive management; there is no court finding that Gates committed criminal abuse of employees.

Microsoft announced **Windows** in 1983 and released Windows 1.0 in 1985. Its graphical-interface development drew on ideas circulating through Xerox PARC and Apple. Apple licensed specified Macintosh visual elements to Microsoft, then sued in 1988 over later Windows versions. The Ninth Circuit ultimately held that much of the disputed expression was licensed or unprotectable. The slogan “Gates stole Windows from Steve Jobs” therefore compresses a real history of imitation, licensing, common GUI concepts, and litigation into a legally inaccurate theft claim.

### 1986–1994: IPO, Windows dominance, marriage, and first foundation

Microsoft moved to its Redmond campus in February 1986 and went public on **13 March 1986** at $21 per share. Gates sold about $1.6 million in shares and retained roughly 45 percent, then valued near $350 million. In 1987, aged 31, he appeared on the Forbes billionaire list. Microsoft’s fortunes accelerated with MS-DOS, Windows 3.0 (1990), Word, Excel, and the integrated Office suite.

The company’s scale produced conflict. The Federal Trade Commission investigated Microsoft; after a deadlock, the Justice Department pursued licensing practices. A 1994 consent decree restricted certain per-processor licensing arrangements while permitting product integration. Gates and Microsoft maintained that standardization and integration benefited users; competitors argued that contract leverage foreclosed alternatives.

In 1989 Gates created **Interactive Home Systems**, later Continuum and then **Corbis**, to acquire and digitize art and photographic rights for an anticipated electronic-image market. Corbis built a major archive, including the Bettmann collection, but never became the consumer platform Gates imagined. Its image-licensing assets were sold to Visual China Group in 2016; the remaining rights/entertainment business evolved into BENlabs.

Gates and Melinda French married in January 1994. That year, after Mary Gates’s death, the family created the **William H. Gates Foundation**, initially led by Bill Gates Sr. The foundation concentrated on global health and local needs.

### 1995–2000: Windows 95, peak power, wealth, and antitrust defeat

**Windows 95** launched on 24 August 1995 with an unprecedented marketing campaign. The graphical consumer OS, Office, developer tools, and OEM relationships made Microsoft the central platform company of the PC era. Gates’s internal 1995 “Internet Tidal Wave” memorandum redirected the firm toward the internet after initially underestimating its speed.

Gates published *The Road Ahead* (1995; revised 1996) and became a mass-media futurist. Some predictions—networked information, electronic commerce, digital devices—were broadly prescient; others were vague, mistimed, or omitted the later dominance of mobile and social platforms.

Microsoft created a family office under investment manager **Michael Larson** in 1994; the investment organization now uses the name **Cascade Asset Management Company**. Gates progressively sold or donated Microsoft stock and diversified into public equities, hotels, rail, waste management, agriculture, energy, real estate, and private ventures. In 1995 he became Forbes’s richest person; in 1999, amid the dot-com boom, estimates briefly placed him above $100 billion, making him the first publicly identified “centibillionaire.” These figures are estimates, not audited personal balance sheets.

The Justice Department and 20 state attorneys general filed the landmark antitrust case in 1998. Gates gave a videotaped deposition remembered for narrow, evasive answers; the transcript and video became public evidence affecting his reputation. Judge Thomas Penfield Jackson’s 1999 findings said Microsoft held monopoly power in Intel-compatible PC operating systems and had used anticompetitive conduct against Netscape, Java, OEMs, Intel, and others. In 2000 Jackson found Sherman Act violations and ordered Microsoft split in two.

The appellate disposition was narrower but still adverse. In 2001 the D.C. Circuit [**affirmed monopoly-maintenance liability**](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F3/253/34/576095/), reversed attempted-monopolization liability, remanded the browser-tying theory for different analysis, vacated the breakup remedy, and disqualified Jackson from further proceedings because of improper press contacts. The United States and Microsoft then settled; the final judgment imposed conduct and disclosure obligations but no breakup. Thus “Microsoft was convicted of every antitrust accusation” is false; “Microsoft was cleared” is also false. A final appellate judgment sustained liability for unlawful monopoly maintenance.

Gates relinquished the CEO role to **Steve Ballmer** in January 2000 and became chief software architect while remaining chairman. The transition coincided with the antitrust crisis but had been planned as an operating reorganization.

### 2000–2008: foundation building and gradual Microsoft exit

In 2000 the William H. Gates Foundation merged with the **Gates Learning Foundation** (rooted in a 1997 library-access initiative) to form the **Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation**. Bill and Melinda were public co-chairs; Bill Gates Sr. was a third co-chair. Early signature programs included childhood vaccination, reproductive and maternal health, scholarships, internet access in libraries, and U.S. school reform.

The foundation’s **$750 million founding pledge to Gavi** helped launch the vaccine alliance in 1999–2000. Later Gates funding supported the Global Fund, malaria research, polio eradication, HIV/TB programs, sanitation, agricultural development, and U.S. education. Attribution must remain collective: for example, Gavi reports immunizing more than one billion children and preventing millions of future deaths, but those results derive from recipient governments, health workers, WHO, UNICEF, states, other donors, manufacturers, Gavi, and the Gates Foundation—not Gates alone.

Microsoft released Windows XP and entered consoles with Xbox in 2001. Gates’s 2002 “Trustworthy Computing” memo responded to severe security problems. The European Commission opened cases over interoperability and bundling and in 2004 fined Microsoft €497 million; later compliance and browser decisions generated further fines, some after Gates ceased day-to-day leadership.

In 2005 Queen Elizabeth II appointed Gates an honorary KBE. In June 2006 Gates announced that he would leave daily Microsoft work in 2008. The same month **Warren Buffett** pledged annual gifts of Berkshire Hathaway shares to the Gates foundation under spending and governance conditions. Buffett’s giving eventually exceeded $47 billion.

Gates founded **TerraPower** in 2006 to develop advanced nuclear technologies, eventually concentrating on traveling-wave concepts and the sodium-cooled **Natrium** reactor with energy storage. He formally ended full-time Microsoft employment on 27 June 2008, though he remained chairman.

### 2008–2014: full-time philanthropy, investments, and Epstein meetings

After leaving Microsoft operations, Gates organized a small private office first known as **bgC3** and later **Gates Ventures**, distinct from the foundation and Cascade. It supports research, writing, policy, health, climate, and investment activity.

Foundation initiatives expanded: the Reinvent the Toilet Challenge; polio eradication; vaccine advance-market commitments; agricultural programs, including support for the Alliance for a Green Revolution in Africa (AGRA); and U.S. initiatives involving small high schools, Common Core standards, charter schools, teacher effectiveness, and postsecondary completion.

Gates met convicted sex offender **Jeffrey Epstein** beginning in 2011—three years after Epstein’s Florida conviction and jail term. Earlier reporting documented dinners and visits at Epstein’s Manhattan townhouse, an overnight stay reported by *The New York Times*, a 2013 meeting with Norwegian Nobel Committee chair Thorbjørn Jagland at Jagland’s Strasbourg residence, a flight on Epstein’s aircraft from New Jersey to Florida, and interactions involving foundation staff. Gates has denied going to Epstein’s island, ranch, or Florida home and denied witnessing or participating in sexual abuse.

The fullest official account as of this dossier is Gates’s **[10 June 2026 transcribed interview with the House Oversight Committee](https://oversight.house.gov/release/oversight-committee-releases-gates-and-groff-transcripts/)** and the **[July 2026 WilmerHale review commissioned by the foundation](https://www.gatesfoundation.org/ideas/media-center/press-releases/2026/07/external-review)**. Gates told Congress that he had three preliminary meetings in 2011, two in 2012, and more extensive discussions in 2013–14, believing Epstein might recruit ultra-wealthy donors for global health. The review found that foundation/Gates engagement concerned a proposed donor-advised fund and an International Peace Institute polio project; it found no evidence that the foundation paid or employed Epstein, knew of or participated in his ongoing criminal activity, or created the proposed fund. It also found that staff had raised reputational concerns and that vetting and escalation systems were inadequate. Because the review was commissioned by the foundation, it is not equivalent to an independent government adjudication; its published method included more than 50 interviews and document review.

### 2014–2019: technology adviser, climate network, global-health influence

In February 2014 Gates stepped down as Microsoft chairman when **Satya Nadella** became CEO. He became technology adviser; John Thompson became chairman. Gates continued periodic product reviews but no longer managed the company.

Gates launched **Breakthrough Energy** in 2015, gathering wealthy investors and creating venture, policy, fellowship, and demonstration programs for low-carbon technologies. His climate framework emphasizes reducing the “green premium,” scaling electricity and industrial innovation, nuclear power, carbon removal, and public-private research. Critics argue that this approach overweights technological supply and underweights consumption, redistribution, regulation, and fossil-fuel politics. The difference is a policy dispute, not evidence that his climate program is fraudulent.

At a 2015 TED talk and in a *New England Journal of Medicine* essay, Gates warned that the world was unprepared for a highly contagious pandemic and advocated surveillance, reserve personnel, vaccine platforms, and exercises. The prediction later became central both to his public-health reputation and to conspiracy narratives.

Gates reviewed **Yuval Noah Harari’s** *Sapiens* in 2016, praising its sweeping synthesis, and later reviewed *Homo Deus* and *21 Lessons for the 21st Century*. The documented relationship is principally intellectual and promotional: Gates amplified Harari’s books and engaged his questions about technology and humanity. Evidence was not found that Harari directs Gates policy or that they share an institutional project.

Gates’s relationship with **Carlos Slim Helú** is mainly philanthropic collaboration. In 2013 the Carlos Slim Foundation committed $100 million to the Global Polio Eradication Initiative; its own account says the partnership helped procure hundreds of millions of vaccine doses. Slim and Gates were parallel billionaire donors and sometimes rivals in wealth rankings, not known business partners in Microsoft.

On 18 October 2019 the Gates Foundation, Johns Hopkins Center for Health Security, and World Economic Forum held [**Event 201**](https://centerforhealthsecurity.org/our-work/tabletop-exercises/event-201-pandemic-tabletop-exercise) in New York, a publicly announced pandemic tabletop exercise using a fictional coronavirus scenario. Its timing shortly before COVID-19 later fueled claims of foreknowledge. Pandemic exercises and coronavirus emergence risks predated 2019; no evidence shows Event 201 caused COVID-19 or possessed secret knowledge of SARS-CoV-2.

### 2020–2024: pandemic prominence, board exit, divorce, and foundation separation

Gates resigned from the Microsoft and Berkshire Hathaway boards on **13 March 2020**, publicly to spend more time on health, development, education, and climate. Microsoft said he remained a technology adviser.

During COVID-19, the foundation committed more than $2 billion to response activities, co-founded or supported vaccine-development and access mechanisms, and helped shape ACT-Accelerator/COVAX policy. Gates became a prominent advocate of vaccination, testing, preparedness, and government spending. He also initially opposed broad intellectual-property waivers, arguing that manufacturing know-how, quality, and capacity were the binding constraints. Critics argued that this position privileged pharmaceutical control and slowed technology transfer. In May 2021 the foundation supported a temporary TRIPS waiver, while continuing to favor negotiated licensing and market-shaping. The record shows a real policy conflict, not proof that Gates personally owned COVID vaccines or created the virus.

Microsoft’s board had opened an investigation in 2019 after an employee reported a years-long sexual relationship with Gates beginning around 2000. A Gates spokesperson later acknowledged an affair and called it consensual, while denying that the investigation caused his board resignation. *The Wall Street Journal* reported that some directors concluded he should leave while the investigation was pending; he resigned before it concluded. No criminal charge or court finding resulted from the reported affair.

Bill and Melinda announced divorce in May 2021 and finalized it that August. Transfers of public-company shares worth billions were disclosed, but their private separation agreement and full property division remain confidential. They continued as foundation co-chairs under a two-year contingency arrangement. Warren Buffett resigned as a foundation trustee in June 2021, without publicly attributing the decision to one cause.

In February 2022 Gates visited Pakistan and received the Hilal-e-Pakistan for polio work. He published *How to Prevent the Next Pandemic* in 2022. In 2024 Melinda announced her departure from the foundation, effective 7 June, with **$12.5 billion supplied personally by Bill Gates** for her future work on women and families; the money did not come from the foundation endowment. The organization became the **Gates Foundation**, with Bill as sole chair.

### 2025–24 August 2026: memoir, spend-down, AI, and renewed Epstein scrutiny

Gates published *Source Code: My Beginnings* on 4 February 2025, the first of a planned three-volume memoir. It covers childhood through Microsoft’s beginnings and is the most detailed first-person source for those years, but remains a self-authored retrospective requiring comparison with Allen, employees, court records, and earlier reporting.

On **8 May 2025** Gates [announced](https://www.gatesnotes.com/work/save-lives/reader/20-years-to-give-away-virtually-all-my-wealth) that the foundation would spend more than **$200 billion** through 2045 and close on **31 December 2045**, and that he intended to give it about 99 percent of his remaining fortune. The figure is prospective and depends on markets and inflation. The foundation said it had spent more than $100 billion in its first 25 years. Its [2024 annual report](https://www.gatesfoundation.org/about/financials/annual-reports/annual-report-2024) recorded about $8.015 billion in charitable support. Gates’s net worth varies daily and differs by estimator; figures near $107–130 billion circulated in 2025–26. Private holdings make exact verification impossible.

In 2026 the foundation announced AI-health partnerships, including Horizon1000 with OpenAI for African primary-care systems. These initiatives extend Gates’s long-standing belief in technical leverage while raising familiar questions about data governance, language representation, private vendors, and democratic oversight.

The U.S. Justice Department’s large 2026 Epstein-file releases renewed scrutiny. Some released emails were Epstein’s drafts or messages containing salacious claims about Gates, including claims about sexual conduct and medication. **The existence of a document proves that Epstein wrote or possessed the claim; it does not prove its substance.** Gates called certain allegations false and “absurd.” In his June House interview he admitted extramarital affairs, said they were unrelated to Epstein’s victims, and said Epstein had made veiled attempts to use information about his infidelity to pressure him back into contact. He denied visiting Epstein’s island, victimizing anyone, or observing criminal conduct. As of 24 August 2026, no indictment, charge, or court finding accuses Gates of participating in Epstein’s trafficking or sexual abuse.

After the July external review, the foundation adopted centralized vetting and risk-escalation procedures. In July 2026 Buffett omitted the Gates Foundation from his annual stock gifts and directed the donation to four family foundations. Reporting connected the pause/cessation to Epstein-related concerns; Buffett had already said in 2024 that his posthumous fortune would go through a trust overseen by his children rather than the Gates Foundation. Therefore, “Epstein alone caused Buffett to redirect his entire estate” overstates the evidence.

## Companies, Institutions, Technologies, Projects, and Financial Interests

### Microsoft

Gates’s principal contributions were strategic and technical rather than a single invention: commercial microcomputer BASIC; platform licensing; the IBM DOS transaction; developer ecosystems; Windows and Office strategy; recruitment and review; and the business model of licensing software broadly to hardware manufacturers. He did **not** invent the computer, graphical interface, internet, BASIC language, or original 86-DOS. He did co-write Altair BASIC and helped build the dominant personal-computer software company.

### Cascade and personal wealth

**Cascade Asset Management Company** manages assets for Gates and the Gates Foundation Trust. The Trust is legally and operationally separate from the grantmaking foundation; the foundation says it does not control Trust investments. Public filings have shown major interests at various times in Berkshire Hathaway, Canadian National Railway, Waste Management/Republic Services, Ecolab, Deere, AutoNation, Four Seasons Hotels, and other firms. Positions change, and online “Bill Gates portfolio” lists often combine his personal holdings, Cascade holdings, and the foundation Trust incorrectly.

Gates-linked entities became the largest private U.S. farmland owner by acreage in widely cited Land Report rankings—about **242,000 acres of farmland** plus other land in the 2021 accounting. The land is dispersed through holding companies and farm operators. This makes Gates a large owner, not the largest U.S. landowner overall and not the owner of a significant fraction of all American farmland.

His best-known residence is the roughly 66,000-square-foot Medina estate. He also acquired Leonardo da Vinci’s **Codex Leicester** at auction in 1994 for $30.8 million and has collected cars and art. Property values and ownership after divorce should be treated as estimates unless supported by title records.

### Other entities and projects

- **Traf-O-Data** (early 1970s): traffic-counting/data venture with Allen and Paul Gilbert; technically instructive, commercially limited.
- **Corbis** (founded 1989): digital-image archive and licensing business; major collection, unsuccessful original consumer-display thesis.
- **Gates Ventures** (2008; formerly bgC3): personal research and project office.
- **TerraPower** (founded 2006): advanced nuclear-energy and isotope company; Natrium demonstration plant in Kemmerer, Wyoming, developed with GE Hitachi and public funding.
- **Breakthrough Energy** (founded 2015): investor and policy network for climate technologies.
- **Strategic investments:** plant-based/fermentation food, batteries, carbon removal, water and sanitation, diagnostics, and Alzheimer’s-disease research. Gates’s role ranges from investor to advocate; it is inaccurate to treat every Gates-backed company as personally controlled by him.
- **Patents:** Patent databases list Gates as co-inventor on numerous patent families, especially collaborative Microsoft/Gates Ventures-era concepts. Examples include [US 7,689,524](https://patents.google.com/patent/US7689524B2/en) (“Dynamic environment evaluation and service adjustment”), [US 7,930,197](https://patents.google.com/patent/US7930197B2/en) (“Personal data mining”), and [US 8,725,567](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8725567B2/en) (“Targeted advertising in brick-and-mortar establishments”). Patent inventorship is a legal attribution to claimed contributions, not proof of a commercial product or sole invention. The Microsoft patent [WO 2020/060606](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2020060606A1/en), often attached to Gates conspiracy narratives, names other inventors and Microsoft Technology Licensing—not Bill Gates—as applicant; it concerns cryptocurrency rewards using sensed body-activity data and does not claim an injectable vaccine chip.

## Political, Religious, and Intellectual Development

### Politics and public policy

Gates long cultivated bipartisan access and avoided party identification, meeting U.S. presidents and leaders across political systems. His policy agenda includes foreign aid, vaccine funding, charter and standards-based education reform, immigration for skilled workers, progressive taxation, nuclear energy, carbon pricing/innovation, pandemic preparedness, digital public infrastructure, and global development.

The foundation is barred from campaign spending and says it does not fund direct lobbying, but it funds policy research, advocacy, communications, and institutions that influence governments. Its 2024 accounts classified $354 million under Global Policy & Advocacy. Critics call this democratically unaccountable agenda-setting; the foundation describes it as transparent grantmaking and partnership. Both descriptions identify real private influence; whether it is legitimate is a governance judgment.

Gates personally has donated to candidates and causes. Reporting in 2024 said he privately gave **$50 million** to Future Forward USA Action, supporting Kamala Harris, while his statement emphasized health, poverty, and climate rather than a formal endorsement. Dark-money organizational structures limit independent public verification of the donor at the time; the claim rests on strong named-outlet reporting, not a conventional FEC candidate filing.

He has clashed publicly with Donald Trump over foreign aid and pandemic policy and with Elon Musk over climate technology, COVID commentary, and 2025 aid cuts. He has also worked with Republican and Democratic administrations and with governments whose human-rights records are criticized. No evidence establishes that Gates holds public office or commands national or international agencies.

### Religion and ethics

Gates was raised in a Congregationalist Christian environment. As an adult he has spoken of the moral value of religious systems and attended Catholic church with Melinda and their children, while expressing uncertainty about conventional theology. The most accurate label is religiously non-dogmatic/agnostic rather than a documented conversion to atheism or a specific creed.

His explicit ethic centers on the proposition that “all lives have equal value,” measurable reduction of suffering, and the use of wealth where marginal impact is high. This resembles utilitarian and effective-altruist reasoning but predates that movement’s prominence and retains a strong faith in institutions, patents, markets, and technological scale.

## Important Relationships and Networks

- **Paul Allen:** childhood friend, co-programmer, co-founder, strategic complement, equity adversary, later partially reconciled friend.
- **Steve Ballmer:** Harvard friend, Microsoft business leader, president, successor CEO; collaborator in Microsoft’s scaling and antagonist in Allen’s account.

### Foundation governance, investments, and conflicts

Private foundations provide tax advantages and allow donors substantial control over public-purpose spending. Critics argue that Gates converted wealth accumulated under a court-confirmed monopoly into unelected influence over education and health. Defenders point to public Form 990-PF filings, audited statements, a searchable grant database, and measurable health programs. Both the tax subsidy and the disclosed grantmaking are factual.

The foundation’s grantmaking entity and investment Trust are separate, but reputational conflicts have arisen when Trust holdings included companies connected to health, fossil fuels, pollution, or foundation programs. Legal separation does not erase perceived conflict; neither does overlap by itself establish self-dealing. No court finding reviewed for this dossier establishes that Gates used the foundation to launder money or illegally enrich himself.

### Education failures and course corrections

The foundation spent heavily on small schools, Common Core implementation, data systems, and teacher evaluation. Some small-school programs improved graduation indicators, but the foundation acknowledged that many did not produce expected achievement gains. A six-year RAND/AIR evaluation of the **Intensive Partnerships for Effective Teaching** found no student-outcome gains overall and unintended staffing effects after hundreds of millions of dollars. The episode is a documented policy failure, not proof that every Gates education grant failed.

### Global health and research ethics

The India HPV demonstration run by PATH became a major controversy after seven participants died and investigators identified consent and oversight defects. India’s parliamentary committee sharply criticized the project. Subsequent reviews did not establish that the vaccines caused the deaths. The correct formulation separates **serious ethical/regulatory failures** from an **unproven causal allegation of lethal vaccination**.

Polio eradication has reduced wild-polio cases by more than 99 percent since 1988, but eradication deadlines have repeatedly slipped, and oral-vaccine-derived outbreaks occur where population immunity is low. Vaccine-derived poliovirus is real and monitored; the claim that Gates programs secretly “introduced polio” omits the distinction between attenuated vaccine strains, low coverage, and wild-virus eradication.

### Agriculture and AGRA

Gates-backed AGRA promoted improved seeds, fertilizer, markets, and state capacity. Independent critics and a donor-commissioned evaluation concluded that headline income and food-security goals were not met at the intended scale. AGRA disputed some methodologies and emphasized policy and system gains. The available record supports **mixed outputs and failure to meet major promised outcomes**, not a simple claim that Gates personally seized African farms.

### Workplace culture around wealth management

Investigative reporting in 2021 described a culture of fear and alleged harassment by Cascade investment chief Michael Larson. Larson denied some allegations, and Cascade said it had investigated complaints. These claims concern a Gates-controlled wealth-management environment and his oversight, but they are not findings that Gates personally committed the alleged acts.

## Myths, Legends, Rumors, Propaganda, and Disputed Narratives

### “Self-made garage genius”

**Story:** Gates rose alone from a garage and personally invented the PC revolution.  
**Appearance/circulation:** business hagiography, motivational media, and simplified Microsoft lore from the 1980s onward.  
**Evidence:** He had extraordinary ability and work intensity, but also affluent parents, Lakeside computer access, collaborators, Harvard resources, family networks, purchased/licensed technology, IBM distribution, and an ecosystem of engineers. Microsoft began in commercial offices, not the canonical Silicon Valley garage.  
**Effect:** Built an entrepreneurial archetype while obscuring institutions and collaborators.

### “Mary Gates handed IBM to her son”

**Story:** Mary’s United Way relationship with John Opel directly caused IBM to award DOS to Microsoft.  
**Evidence:** She did discuss Microsoft with Opel, and the connection was advantageous. IBM’s PC team already had technical and commercial reasons to approach Microsoft, and the OS deal emerged after Digital Research negotiations failed.  
**Effect:** Used alternately to puncture self-made mythology and to allege nepotistic conspiracy. The cautious conclusion is **helpful access, not sole cause**.

### “Gates stole DOS for $50,000”

**Story:** Gates fraudulently stole Gary Kildall’s or Tim Paterson’s operating system and resold it to IBM.  
**Evidence:** Microsoft licensed and then purchased 86-DOS from SCP, adapted it, and retained broad licensing rights. SCP later sued, alleging Microsoft concealed IBM’s identity, and settled. Similarity to CP/M has been debated, but no adjudication established that Gates stole CP/M source code.  
**Effect:** A durable counter-myth to Microsoft’s innovation story.

### “Gates predicted/planned COVID-19”

**Story:** The 2015 pandemic warning and October 2019 Event 201 prove advance knowledge or orchestration of COVID-19.  
**Evidence:** Both are real and well documented. Experts had long warned about pandemic risk; Event 201 used a fictional coronavirus but did not model SARS-CoV-2’s exact origin or prove foreknowledge. No causal evidence connects Gates to creation or release of the virus.  
**Effect:** Turned preparedness advocacy into evidence of guilt within anti-globalist and anti-vaccine communities.

### “Vaccines are Gates’s population-reduction program”

**Story:** Gates’s 2010 TED statement that vaccines and reproductive health could reduce projected population growth means vaccines are intended to kill or sterilize people.  
**Evidence:** Gates was describing demographic transition: when child survival improves and voluntary family planning is available, parents tend to choose smaller families, reducing **future population growth**, not killing recipients. Real concerns about reproductive-health consent and donor power remain separate and deserve scrutiny.  
**Effect:** Became one of the most circulated anti-vaccine narratives in several languages.

### “Injectable microchips, ID2020, patent 060606, and the Mark of the Beast”

**Story:** Gates planned vaccine-injected tracking chips tied to digital currency, often linked to Microsoft patent WO 2020/060606 and Revelation 13.  
**Origin/circulation:** accelerated in March–April 2020 through social video, religious-apocalyptic interpretation, anti-vaccine networks, and misleading aggregation of separate projects.  
**Underlying facts:** Gates-backed research did explore invisible quantum-dot skin marks for vaccine records; the foundation supports digital public infrastructure; Microsoft participated in digital-identity work; and Microsoft filed WO 2020/060606 on cryptocurrency using body-activity sensor data.  
**Against the composite claim:** the quantum-dot research was not a location-tracking chip; ID2020 advocated digital identity rather than vaccine microchips; patent 060606 neither names Gates as inventor nor claims an injected vaccine device.  
**Effect:** Made Gates perhaps the single most recognizable villain of pandemic conspiracy culture, generating threats and damaging vaccine trust.

### “Gates owns WHO”

**Story:** Gates personally dictates WHO policy.  
**Evidence:** The Gates Foundation and Gavi are exceptionally important voluntary funders, and earmarked funding can shape priorities. WHO’s constitutional authority remains with its member states, and multiple governmental and private donors compete. “Influence” is demonstrable; personal ownership or command is not.  
**Effect:** Condenses a serious governance debate about donor dependence into a personalized sovereignty myth.

### Epstein narratives

**Story spectrum:** from the documented claim that Gates knowingly met a convicted sex offender to claims that he participated in trafficking, visited the island dozens of times, or belongs to a protected intelligence-sex network.  
**Evidence:** repeated post-conviction meetings are established. Gates admitted bad judgment and infidelity. He denies criminal involvement and island visits. The 2026 external review found no foundation payments or criminal participation; no charge or victim accusation located for this dossier identifies Gates as an abuser. Epstein’s own claims are not self-authenticating and may include attempted leverage.  
**Effect:** Contributed to divorce reporting, board/foundation scrutiny, congressional testimony, an external review, and Buffett’s 2026 donation decision. It is the most consequential hostile narrative because it begins with substantial documented contact but often expands beyond the evidence.

## Successes and Failures

### Major successes

- Co-created a commercially viable microcomputer BASIC and recognized software as an independent mass market.
- Built Microsoft into the dominant PC software platform and one of the world’s most valuable companies.
- Used broad OEM licensing to make a common software platform available across competing hardware.
- Helped institutionalize very large-scale philanthropy, Gavi market-shaping, polio and malaria R&D, sanitation technology, and the Giving Pledge.
- Redirected substantial private capital toward advanced nuclear energy and other difficult climate technologies.
- Correctly elevated pandemic preparedness before COVID-19.

### Major failures or reversals

- Microsoft’s monopoly-maintenance practices violated U.S. antitrust law; his deposition damaged his credibility.
- Microsoft missed or arrived late to several shifts under his leadership, including web search, smartphones, and some consumer-internet models.
- Corbis failed to realize its original mass-market electronic-art vision.
- Foundation education programs, especially intensive teacher-effectiveness work, failed to deliver promised overall gains.
- Polio eradication deadlines and several high-profile development goals slipped despite enormous spending.
- Post-conviction Epstein meetings reflected a severe failure of judgment and institutional vetting; personal infidelity harmed his family and philanthropy’s reputation.
- His initial COVID IP position became a focal point for access criticism.

## Present Status and Legacy

Gates remains chair of one of the world’s largest private foundations and a central funder/advocate in global health, climate technology, nuclear energy, education, and AI for development. He intends the foundation to close in 2045 after spending down its assets. Melinda French Gates no longer serves in it; Warren Buffett omitted it from his 2026 annual gifts after contributing more than $47 billion over two decades.

His historical reputation has passed through distinct phases:

1. **1970s–1980s:** brilliant young programmer-entrepreneur and proprietary-software advocate.
2. **1990s:** embodiment of PC-era wealth and Microsoft market power; celebrated futurist and feared monopolist.
3. **1998–2004:** antitrust defendant whose deposition became a symbol of evasive corporate power.
4. **2000s–2010s:** reconstructed public identity as global philanthropist and quantitative problem-solver.
5. **2020s:** pandemic authority and conspiracy target; subject of renewed scrutiny over private philanthropy, sexual conduct, Epstein contacts, and unelected policy influence.

The technological legacy is not that Gates single-handedly invented personal computing. It is that he understood early that a licensable software platform could sit at the center of a hardware ecosystem, then executed that strategy with exceptional intensity. The philanthropic legacy is similarly collective and unresolved: enormous funding helped institutions deliver vaccines and research, while concentrating agenda-setting power in a private foundation. The 2026 disclosures do not erase documented program results, and program results do not erase documented failures of judgment and governance.
