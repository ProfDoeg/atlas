## Works by Johannes Kepler

### Cosmological and astronomical treatises

- **1596:** *Prodromus dissertationum cosmographicarum, continens Mysterium Cosmographicum*. A cosmological treatise and geometrical model of the planetary system, proposing that the planetary intervals correspond to the five regular solids; the later revision is a distinct manifestation of the same obra. ([KGW I and VIII](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1604:** *Astronomiae Pars Optica, Ad Vitellionem Paralipomena*. A treatise on light, vision, astronomical refraction, eclipses, and optical projection; although usually called a book of astronomy, it is also a foundational work of geometrical optics. ([KGW II](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1606:** *De Stella Nova in Pede Serpentarii*. A composite astronomical, astrological, chronological, and theological investigation of the nova observed in 1604. ([KGW I](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1609:** *Astronomia Nova Αἰτιολογητός, seu Physica Coelestis*. A causal or “physical” astronomy of Mars, incorporating what are now called Kepler’s first two planetary laws; it is simultaneously a report of inquiry, a mathematical argument, and a reconstructed research journey. ([KGW III](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1618–1621:** *Epitome Astronomiae Copernicanae*. A seven-book instructional synthesis of heliocentric astronomy, issued in parts; its catechetical question-and-answer form makes it partly a pedagogical protocol rather than merely a descriptive treatise. ([KGW VII](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1619:** *Harmonices Mundi Libri V*. A five-book Gesamtkunstwerk joining geometry, music theory, astrology, metaphysics, and planetary astronomy; its fifth book announces the relation now called Kepler’s third law. ([KGW VI](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1619–1620:** *De Cometis Libelli Tres*. Three linked treatises about the comets of 1607 and 1618, combining observations, physical hypotheses, calculation, and astrological interpretation. ([KGW VIII](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1621:** *Mysterium Cosmographicum, editio altera cum notis*. A heavily annotated second manifestation of the 1596 work: not simply a reprint, but Kepler’s retrospective correction, defence, and historical commentary upon his younger self. ([KGW VIII](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1622:** *Apologia adversus Demonstrationem Analyticam Roberti de Fluctibus*. A polemical mathematical defence of Kepler’s harmonic theory against Robert Fludd. ([KGW VI](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1625:** *Tychonis Brahei Dani Hyperaspistes*. A polemical defence of Tycho Brahe and Kepler against Scipione Chiaramonti; its title casts the text as a “shield-bearer,” so it functions as an intervention as much as a treatise. ([KGW VIII](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))

### Observational reports and astronomical interventions

- **1602:** *Appendix ad Progymnasmatum Tomum Primum*. A short astronomical supplement to Tycho Brahe’s *Astronomiae Instauratae Progymnasmata*, authored by Kepler within another astronomer’s publication. ([KGW XI.2](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1604:** *Gründtlicher Bericht von einem Newen Stern*. A German public report on the nova of 1604, distinct from the larger Latin *De Stella Nova*. ([KGW I](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1605:** *De Solis Deliquio*. An observational and computational account of a solar eclipse. ([KGW IV](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1608:** *Außführlicher Bericht von dem diß 1607. Jahrs erschienenen Cometen*. A German public report on the comet of 1607, mixing observation with physical and astrological interpretation. ([KGW IV](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1609:** *Phaenomenon singulare seu Mercurius in Sole*. A report interpreting an apparent dark mark on the Sun as Mercury; the observation was subsequently recognized as a sunspot, so the text preserves a productive misidentification. ([KGW IV](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1609:** *Antwort auff Röslini Discurs*. A polemical response to Helisaeus Röslin about cosmology, comets, and the nova. ([KGW IV](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1610:** *Dissertatio cum Nuncio Sidereo*. A published conversation with Galileo’s *Sidereus Nuncius*, endorsing the telescopic discoveries before Kepler had independently confirmed them. It acts publicly to establish credibility and alliance and therefore has something of the manifesto. ([KGW IV](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1610:** *Tertius Interveniens*. A German intervention between wholesale belief in and wholesale rejection of astrology; its “third-party” form is deliberately mediating and polemical. ([KGW IV](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1611:** *Narratio de observatis a se quatuor Iovis satellitibus*. An observational report confirming the four Jovian satellites announced by Galileo. ([KGW IV](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1621:** *Astronomischer Bericht von zweyen im 1620. Jahr gesehenen Mondsfinsternussen*. A German report comparing two lunar eclipses observed in 1620. ([KGW XI.2](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1629:** *Ad Epistolam Bartschii Responsio*. Kepler’s published response to Jakob Bartsch’s 1628 open letter; it is a brief public epistolary intervention rather than a freestanding treatise. ([KGW XI.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1629:** *De raris mirisque Anni 1631 Phaenomenis*. A predictive notice of unusual celestial phenomena expected in 1631, including planetary transits. ([KGW XI.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1630:** *Terrentii Epistolium cum Commentatiuncula Joannis Kepleri*. Kepler’s commentary attached to a short letter by “Terrentius”; because it incorporates another person’s text, it is a collaborative printed object rather than an exclusively Keplerian work. ([KGW XI.2](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))

### Geometry, optics, measurement, and calculation

- **1601:** *De Fundamentis Astrologiae Certioribus*. A set of theses seeking firmer physical and geometrical foundations for astrology; it is both a theoretical text and a reform project. ([KGW IV](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1611:** *Strena seu de Nive Sexangula*. A New Year’s gift-text on the six-cornered snowflake, treating symmetry, packing, and natural form; its gift format resists reduction to a conventional scientific book. ([KGW IV](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1611:** *Dioptrice*. A geometrical-optical treatise explaining lenses, telescopes, and projected images, including the design now called the Keplerian telescope. ([KGW IV](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1615:** *Nova Stereometria Doliorum Vinariorum*. A mathematical treatise on the volumes of wine barrels and other solids, prompted by a practical dispute over gauging. ([KGW IX](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1616:** *Ausszug auß der Uralten Messekunst Archimedis* (“*Messekunst Archimedis*”). A German adaptation and practical manifestation of the stereometrical work, intended for measurers and merchants rather than only Latin-reading mathematicians. ([KGW IX](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1624:** *Chilias Logarithmorum*. A thousand logarithms accompanied by instructions for their construction and use; it is simultaneously a numerical object and a computational protocol. ([KGW IX](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1625:** *Supplementum Chiliadis Logarithmorum*. A supplement extending and explaining the logarithmic tables. ([KGW IX](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated manuscripts:** *De quantitatibus libelli*. A group of manuscript studies on mathematical magnitudes; the collected edition preserves it as a project rather than a finished publication. ([KGW XXI.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated manuscripts:** *De genesi magnitudinum*. A manuscript investigation of the generation of geometrical magnitudes, surviving as an unfinished research object. ([KGW XXI.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated manuscripts:** *Manuscripta Arithmetica*. The editors’ collective title for Kepler’s dispersed arithmetic papers. This is an archival corpus, not one work Kepler himself titled or completed. ([KGW XXI.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated manuscripts:** *Manuscripta Geometrica*. The editors’ collective title for geometrical notes and drafts; it resists singular-work status because its unity is editorial. ([KGW XXI.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))

### Tables, ephemerides, and predictive protocols

- **1617–1619:** *Ephemerides Novae Motuum Coelestium ab anno 1617 ad annum 1620*. Calculated daily planetary positions for 1617–1620; these are operational tables meant to be used, not merely read. ([KGW XI.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1630:** *Ephemeridum Pars Secunda ab anno 1621 ad 1628*. The second part of Kepler’s ephemerides, printed after the years it tabulates. ([KGW XI.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1630:** *Ephemeridum Pars Tertia a 1629 in 1636*. The third part, projecting planetary positions through 1636 and thus continuing to act after Kepler’s death. ([KGW XI.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1627:** *Tabulae Rudolphinae*. Astronomical tables calculated by Kepler from Tycho Brahe’s observational archive, with rules for calculating planetary and stellar positions. Authorship is necessarily layered: Tycho initiated the project and supplied much of its observational basis, while Kepler transformed, completed, edited, and personally supervised the printing of the tables. ([KGW X](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html); [Austrian National Library](https://www.onb.ac.at/sammlungen/kartensammlung/50-zimelien-die-wertvollsten-objekte-der-kartensammlung/philipp-eckebrecht-johannes-kepler-weltkarte-1630/nach-1658))

### Calendars and prognostications

- **1596:** *Practica auff das Jahr 1597*. An annual astrological forecast, a time-bound predictive text made for civic and practical use. ([KGW XI.2](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1597:** *SchreibCalender auff das Jahr 1598*. A writing calendar combining calendrical data with space for use by its owner; it is therefore both publication and working instrument. ([KGW XI.2](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1598:** *Practica auff das Jahr 1599*. An annual prognostication. ([KGW XI.2](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **circa 1599:** *Collectanea ad Prognosticum Anno 1600*. Preparatory material for a forecast for 1600; it survives as a fragment or project, not as a securely complete publication. ([KGW XI.2](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1602:** *Calendarium und Bericht vom feurigen Triangel 1603*. A calendar and astrological report on the “fiery trigon,” joining numerical calendar, forecast, and public interpretation. ([KGW XI.2](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1603:** *Prognosticum auf 1604*. An annual forecast. ([KGW XI.2](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1604:** *Prognosticum auf 1605*. An annual forecast. ([KGW XI.2](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1605:** *Prognosticum auf 1606*. An annual forecast. ([KGW XI.2](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1617:** *SchreibCalender auff 1618*. A writing calendar and practical astronomical object. ([KGW XI.2](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1617:** *Prognosticum auf 1618*. An annual forecast distinct from the writing calendar for the same year. ([KGW XI.2](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1618:** *Prognosticon auf das 1618. und 1619. Jahr*. A two-year prognostication. ([KGW XI.2](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1619:** *Prognosticum auf 1620*. An annual forecast. ([KGW XI.2](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1623:** *Discurs von der grossen Conjunction 1623, sambt Prognostico*. A discourse and forecast concerning the great conjunction; it combines explanation with public prediction. ([KGW XI.2](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1623:** *Prognosticum auf 1624*. Kepler’s surviving annual forecast for 1624. ([KGW XI.2](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))

### Chronology and calendar reform

- **1606:** *De Iesu Christi vero anno natalitio*. A short Latin chronological study of the true year of Christ’s birth, printed with *De Stella Nova*. ([KGW I](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1613:** *Widerholter Außführlicher Teutscher Bericht vom Geburtsjahr Christi*. A substantially developed German account of the chronology of Christ’s birth. ([KGW V](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1614:** *De vero anno natali Christi*. A major Latin chronological treatise; it is related to, but not merely identical with, the earlier German and Latin treatments. ([KGW V](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1614:** *Ad Epistolam Sethi Calvisii Chronologi Responsio*. A response to chronologist Sethus Calvisius concerning historical dating. ([KGW V](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1615:** *Eclogae Chronicae*. Chronological studies using astronomy to adjudicate dates in ancient and biblical history. ([KGW V](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1620:** *Kanones Pueriles*. Elementary chronological rules designed for instruction; they function as a compact teaching protocol. ([KGW V](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated manuscripts:** *De Calendario Gregoriano*. A body of Latin and German writings on the Gregorian calendar. Its boundaries are editorial, and it survives as a multi-text reform project rather than one finished work. ([KGW XXI.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated manuscripts:** *In libellum Sleidani de IV monarchiis*. Notes or commentary on Johann Sleidan’s account of the four monarchies, preserved as a chronological manuscript project. ([KGW XXI.2.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated manuscripts:** *De septuaginta hebdomadibus in Daniele discursus*. A chronological-theological discourse on the seventy weeks in Daniel. ([KGW XXI.2.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated manuscripts:** *Chronologia a mundo condito*. An extensive chronology from the creation of the world; it remained a manuscript project rather than a completed publication. ([KGW XXI.2.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated manuscripts:** *Astronomia Chronologiae Salutem plurimam*. A manuscript joining astronomical method to chronological inquiry. ([KGW XXI.2.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated manuscripts:** *Dispositio historicorum in chronologia*. A short arrangement of historians within a chronological scheme. ([KGW XXI.2.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))

### Theology, confession, advocacy, and translation

- **Undated:** *De Omnipraesentia Christi*. A brief theological text on Christ’s omnipresence; the collected edition does not assign it a publication date. ([KGW XII](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1617:** *Unterricht vom H. Sacrament des Leibs und Bluts Jesu Christi unsers Erlösers*. A theological instruction on the Eucharist, composed amid Kepler’s disputes with Lutheran orthodoxy. It is a confessional intervention and protocol for belief, not a scientific treatise. ([KGW XII](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1621:** *Conclusion-Schrifft*. Kepler’s legal-argumentative closing submission in the witchcraft proceedings against his mother Katharina. It is advocacy that acts within a judicial process and therefore resists treatment as a detached “work.” ([KGW XII](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1623:** *Glaubensbekandtnus*. Kepler’s confession of faith, made to state and defend his doctrinal position. It operates as a personal theological manifesto. ([KGW XII](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1625:** *Notae ad Epistolam Matthiae Hafenrefferi*. Notes responding to a letter by the theologian Matthias Hafenreffer; the work belongs to a contested epistolary and confessional exchange rather than to a self-contained book. ([KGW XII](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1625:** *Taciti Historische Beschreibung: Das Erste Buch*. Kepler’s German translation of Book I of Tacitus’s *Histories*. It is a translation—an authored verbal transformation, but not an original narrative by Kepler. ([KGW XII](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))

### Manuscript astronomical projects

- **circa 1600:** *Apologia Tychonis contra Ursum*. A defence of Tycho Brahe against Nicolaus Reimers Ursus, left unpublished by Kepler; questions surrounding Tycho’s role and Kepler’s access to the materials make it a commissioned, contested manuscript project rather than an uncomplicated personal work. ([KGW XX.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated:** *Judicium de hypothesibus Tychonianis*. A manuscript assessment of Tychonic astronomical hypotheses. ([KGW XX.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated:** *Refutatio libelli, cui titulus Capnuraniae Restinctio*. A short manuscript refutation of a now-obscure polemical work. ([KGW XX.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated:** *Catalogus librorum a Tychone Brahe scriptorum*. A catalogue of Tycho Brahe’s writings compiled by Kepler; it is bibliographical labour rather than an autonomous scientific obra. ([KGW XX.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated:** *Problemata astronomica*. A manuscript collection of astronomical problems rather than a finished continuous treatise. ([KGW XX.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated:** *De motu terrae*. A manuscript complex on the Earth’s motion, incorporating Kepler’s German rendering of chapters 13–14 of Aristotle’s *De caelo* and his response to Francesco Ingoli; its composite structure resists singular authorship and genre. ([KGW XX.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated:** *Responsio ad Ingoli Disputationem*. Kepler’s response to Francesco Ingoli’s objections to terrestrial motion, preserved inside the *De motu terrae* complex. ([KGW XX.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated, unfinished:** *Hipparchus*. A large projected reconstruction of lunar and planetary theory. It remained unfinished, but the project—the intention embodied in extensive manuscript work—is itself the obra. ([KGW XX.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated:** *Lunaria*. Manuscript investigations of lunar theory and tables. The editorial title gathers working materials that do not constitute a finished book. ([KGW XX.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated:** *Restitutionum lunarium adversaria*. Working papers toward a reconstruction of lunar theory; explicitly adversaria, they are research notes rather than a completed exposition. ([KGW XX.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated:** *Consideratio observationum Regiomontani et Waltheri*. A manuscript analysis of observations made by Regiomontanus and Bernhard Walther. ([KGW XX.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1600–1605, approximately:** *Commentaria in Theoriam Martis*. The extensive calculations, drafts, and working papers behind *Astronomia Nova*. The collected edition describes them as preliminary work, so their identity is that of a research process rather than a finished text. ([KGW XX.2](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated manuscripts:** *Eclipses Lunae et Solis*. A large corpus of eclipse observations and calculations; its unity is archival and editorial. ([KGW XXI.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated manuscripts:** *Vorarbeiten zum Mysterium Cosmographicum*. Drafts and calculations for the cosmographical work; these are manifestations of its making, not independent finished works. ([KGW XXI.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated manuscripts:** *Vorarbeiten zur Astronomiae Pars Optica*. Preparatory optical papers, grouped under an editorial title. ([KGW XXI.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated manuscripts:** *Vorarbeiten zu De Stella Nova*. Preparatory observations and drafts for the nova treatise. ([KGW XXI.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated manuscripts:** *Ergänzungen zu Commentaria in theoriam Martis*. Additional Mars calculations and drafts excluded from the principal *Commentaria* complex. ([KGW XXI.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated manuscripts:** *Vorarbeiten zur Narratio de observatis a se quatuor Iovis satellitibus*. Preparatory notes for Kepler’s report on Jupiter’s satellites. ([KGW XXI.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated manuscripts:** *Vorarbeiten zur Epitome Astronomiae Copernicanae*. Drafts for the *Epitome*, surviving as traces of a larger pedagogical project. ([KGW XXI.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated manuscripts:** *Vorarbeiten zu den Tabulae Rudolphinae*. Working calculations and drafts for the Rudolphine Tables; these belong to a long collaborative project founded on Tycho Brahe’s observations. ([KGW XXI.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))

### Harmonic manuscripts

- **Undated:** *De stellis*. A short manuscript included among Kepler’s harmonic papers. ([KGW XXI.2.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated:** *Brevis et dilucida explicatio fundamentorum harmonicorum*. A concise explanation of harmonic foundations. ([KGW XXI.2.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated:** *Ex dialogis Vincentii Galilaei De musica*. Notes or extracts from Vincenzo Galilei’s musical dialogue; because the source language and argument belong partly to Galilei, this is an act of appropriation and study rather than wholly original composition. ([KGW XXI.2.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **Undated:** *Appendix ad Harmonices Mundi librum V*. Kepler’s manuscript commentary and translation work on Book III of Ptolemy’s *Harmonics*, conceived as an appendix to *Harmonices Mundi* but not incorporated into its printed manifestation. ([KGW XXI.2.1](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))

### Fiction, poetry, and occasional writing

- **1590:** *Wedding poem for Johannes Huldenreich*. A Latin occasional poem written for a wedding; its separately recovered manuscript witnesses make it a small social artefact, not a “book.” ([Kepler-Kommission edition notice](https://kepler.badw.de/publikationen.html))
- **1590–1630:** *Gedichte*. Kepler’s surviving poems, epigrams, elegies, dedicatory verses, and occasional pieces form a heterogeneous corpus rather than one work; individual dating and attribution vary, and the critical edition prints them collectively. ([KGW XII](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))
- **1601:** *In obitum Tychonis Brahe*. A Latin elegy on Tycho Brahe’s death, an occasional poem and memorial act. ([Kepler-Kommission edition notice](https://kepler.badw.de/publikationen.html))
- **circa 1593–1630; published 1634:** *Somnium seu Opus posthumum de Astronomia Lunari*. A continuously reworked project combining dream fiction, lunar astronomy, autobiography, folklore, and extensive scientific notes—a genuine Gesamtkunstwerk. Kepler did not complete its publication; his son Ludwig issued it posthumously, so the 1634 book is partly an editorial manifestation after Kepler’s death. ([KGW XI.2](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))

### Horoscopes and astrological casework

- **circa 1590s–1630:** *Horoskopsammlung*. Hundreds of natal charts, calculations, family charts, consultations, and case records preserved in manuscript. This is a working archive rather than a single authored text, and many constituent items concern patrons, relatives, or correspondents rather than a public readership. ([KGW XXI.2.2](https://kepler.badw.de/publikationen.html?cHash=1d2426451ccad8321b20fdf3f627c540&tx_badwdb_publications%5Baction%5D=show&tx_badwdb_publications%5Bcontroller%5D=Publications&tx_badwdb_publications%5Bpub_id%5D=936))
- **Undated manuscripts:** *Manuscripta pneumatica*. Miscellaneous manuscript material classified by the editors alongside the astrological papers. The designation is an archival category, not a title or a single work Kepler himself declared complete. ([KGW XXI.2.2](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))

### Correspondence as a body of work

- **1590–1630:** *Briefe*. Kepler’s surviving correspondence, edited across six chronological volumes, is a distributed body of scientific, theological, personal, legal, and patronage writing. Individual letters are acts within relationships and often collaborative exchanges, so the corpus resists being treated as one work or as hundreds of autonomous publications. ([KGW XIII–XVIII](https://kepler.badw.de/die-edition.html))

### Designed objects, machines, and cartography

- **1596, proposed:** *Cosmographic cup*. Kepler proposed embodying the nested-soli​​ds cosmos of the *Mysterium Cosmographicum* in an elaborate drinking vessel. No completed object is securely documented, so this belongs as an unrealized design project, not as an extant artefact.
- **1598, designed:** *Planetary machine*. Letters to Michael Mästlin describe a single-drive mechanical model intended to display Copernican planetary motions, including retrogradation as seen from Earth. The surviving made thing is the design in correspondence; whether Kepler built a functioning machine is not established. ([study and reconstruction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.04815))
- **1630 / surviving impressions after 1658:** *Nova Orbis Terrarum Delineatio Singulari Ratione Accommodata Meridiano Tabb. Rudolphi Astronomicarum*. A double-eagle world map conceived as an adjunct to the *Tabulae Rudolphinae*. Philipp Eckebrecht made it at Kepler’s request and J. P. Walch engraved it, so it is a collaborative/commissioned creation rather than Kepler’s sole work; although dated 1630, all known impressions carry a dedication proving manufacture after 1658. ([Austrian National Library](https://www.onb.ac.at/sammlungen/kartensammlung/50-zimelien-die-wertvollsten-objekte-der-kartensammlung/philipp-eckebrecht-johannes-kepler-weltkarte-1630/nach-1658))

- Daniele L. R. Marini, [“The Planetary Machine by Johannes Kepler”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.04815), on the 1598 design preserved in Kepler’s letters.
