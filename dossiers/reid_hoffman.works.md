## Works by Reid Garrett Hoffman

### Games and playable protocols

- **1982:** *Borderlands: A RuneQuest Campaign in Seven Scenarios*. A boxed tabletop role-playing campaign collectively credited to Hoffman and fourteen other creators; Hoffman, then fourteen, described receiving both credit and payment, but the surviving credits do not isolate which passages or scenarios were his. ([Chaosium](https://www.chaosium.com/borderlands-pdf/); [Hoffman’s account](https://greylock.com/greymatter/reid-hoffman-game-on/))
- **2016:** *Trumped Up Cards*. A satirical party game and political intervention modeled on *Cards Against Humanity*; Hoffman called it a game he created, so it functions both as a playable protocol and as campaign speech rather than merely describing a political position. ([Hoffman](https://www.reidhoffman.org/memes-cartoons-battle-rap-a-digital-campaign-to-satirize-trump-mobilize-voters/); [Economic Club of New York](https://www.econclubny.org/documents/10184/109144/2018HoffmanTranscript.pdf))

### Books and book-length collaborations

- **2012; revised and updated 2022:** *The Startup of You: Adapt, Take Risks, Grow Your Network, and Transform Your Career*. A career-strategy book co-written with Ben Casnocha; the 2022 text is a substantial revision of the same obra, not simply another copy of the first edition. ([Hoffman’s official books bibliography](https://www.reidhoffman.org/books/))
- **2014:** *The Alliance: Managing Talent in the Networked Age*. A management book co-written with Ben Casnocha and Chris Yeh; it proposes the “alliance” as a protocol for relations between employers and employees. ([Hoffman’s official books bibliography](https://www.reidhoffman.org/books/))
- **2018:** *Blitzscaling: The Lightning-Fast Path to Building Massively Valuable Companies*. A business-strategy book co-written with Chris Yeh, presenting speed-before-efficiency as a conditional protocol for rapid organizational growth. ([Hoffman’s official books bibliography](https://www.reidhoffman.org/books/))
- **2021:** *Masters of Scale: Surprising Truths from the World’s Most Successful Entrepreneurs*. A book made with June Cohen and Deron Triff from more than one hundred podcast interviews, supplemented by previously unaired material; it is therefore a transformation of a collective audio archive rather than a freestanding single-author text. ([Penguin Random House](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/669987/masters-of-scale-by-reid-hoffman-with-june-cohen-and-deron-triff/))
- **2023:** *Impromptu: Amplifying Our Humanity Through AI*. An experimental book written by Hoffman “with GPT-4” and published by Dallepedia; its dialogues and generated passages make its authorship deliberately hybrid, although Hoffman remains the named human author and curator. ([Hoffman’s announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/impromptu-my-new-book-possible-podcast-reid-hoffman); [published text and credits](https://www.impromptubook.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/impromptu-rh.pdf))
- **2025:** *Superagency: What Could Possibly Go Right with Our AI Future*. A book co-written with Greg Beato that operates partly as an optimistic manifesto for widely distributed human agency through AI. ([Simon & Schuster / Authors Equity](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/Superagency/Reid-Hoffman/9798893310108))

### Founded network and AI projects

- **1997:** *SocialNet.com*. Hoffman’s first startup, an online matching and social-network service. It is a collective software-and-company project, not a work attributable to Hoffman alone; available accounts also vary between calling him its founder and co-founder. ([Founded](https://www.founded.org/founders/reid-hoffman/))
- **2002–2003:** *LinkedIn*. A professional-network platform and company co-founded in December 2002 and publicly launched in 2003; its continuing, collectively engineered service resists treatment as a finished individual obra. ([Oxford, Wolfson College](https://www.wolfson.ox.ac.uk/person/reid-hoffman/); [Wharton](https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2014-04-08-KW-WhartonSF-Special-Report-R2.pdf))
- **2022:** *Inflection AI*. An AI-company project co-founded by Hoffman, Mustafa Suleyman, and Karén Simonyan to develop consumer-facing AI systems. The company—not Hoffman individually—subsequently made its models and the assistant Pi, so those products are not silently reassigned to him here. ([founding announcement](https://greylock.com/blog/a-new-paradigm-in-human-machine-interaction/); [Inflection AI](https://inflection.ai/about))
- **2024–2025:** *Manas AI*. An AI-assisted drug-discovery company co-founded by Hoffman, Siddhartha Mukherjee, and, in the company’s present account, Ujjwal Singh; research began in 2024, the venture launched publicly in January 2025, and Singh’s designation as co-founder was announced later in 2025. It is an ongoing scientific and corporate project rather than a completed work. ([Manas AI](https://manasai.co/company); [launch announcement](https://www.pressrelease.com/news/manas-ai-launches-to-transform-discovery-of-novel-cancer-medicines-22507055))

### Audio and transmedia projects

- **2017–:** *Masters of Scale*. A podcast created around Hoffman’s interviews with entrepreneurs and organizational leaders, later expanded into a book, newsletters, events, an app, and the *Rapid Response* audio series. In that expanded form it approaches a Gesamtkunstwerk or media platform: Hoffman is its founding host, but its production and derivative forms are collective. ([Hoffman’s podcast catalogue](https://www.reidhoffman.org/podcasts/))
- **2019:** *Hamilton vs. Satoshi*. A staged Bitcoin rap-battle video initiated and promoted by Hoffman as a public discussion about cryptocurrency. It combines song, performance, costume, political economy, and online video, but Hoffman’s role was project instigator rather than sole writer, composer, or performer. ([Hoffman’s writing index](https://www.reidhoffman.org/writing/); [contemporary account](https://decrypt.co/8981/founding-fathers-hamilton-nakamoto-duke-it-out-in-epic-bitcoin-rap-battle))
- **2023–:** *Possible*. A weekly interview and speculative-futures podcast launched by Hoffman with co-host Aria Finger; its original format also used GPT-4-generated companion stories, making each episode a human–machine collaborative manifestation rather than solely Hoffman’s authored text. ([Hoffman’s launch announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/impromptu-my-new-book-possible-podcast-reid-hoffman); [official podcast site](https://www.possible.fm/); [KQED](https://www.kqed.org/radio/programs/possible))

### AI manifestations

- **2024–:** *REID AI*. Hoffman’s experimental “digital twin”: a custom chatbot trained on his writings and recordings, voiced through ElevenLabs and embodied through an Hour One avatar. It is at once portrait, software agent, performance device, and ongoing project; because its outputs are generated anew, it resists being called a fixed work. ([Hoffman](https://www.reidhoffman.org/perugia-speech2/); [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2024/07/09/digital-twins-reid-hoffman-health))

### Speeches, essays, posts, and campaign media

- **2012–:** *Online writings*. An open body of essays, reviews, announcements, speeches, cartoons, videos, and political or entrepreneurial interventions rather than one stable book. Hoffman’s official writing index describes itself as a searchable list of all his online writing since 2012; individual entries often originated on other platforms, and translated versions are manifestations of the same underlying texts rather than new works. ([official writing index](https://www.reidhoffman.org/writing/))
- **2020:** *Memes, cartoons & battle rap: a digital campaign to satirize Trump & mobilize voters*. An umbrella political-media project encompassing memes, cartoons, videos, and musical satire. Its dispersed and collaborative artifacts resist both singular authorship and treatment as one bounded text; Hoffman presented the campaign as something “we” made. ([Hoffman](https://www.reidhoffman.org/memes-cartoons-battle-rap-a-digital-campaign-to-satirize-trump-mobilize-voters/))
- **2023:** *Amplifying Humanity*. A commencement speech delivered at Bologna Business School and subsequently issued online and in numerous machine-translated manifestations; the translations are versions of the speech, not separately authored Hoffman works. ([official writing index](https://www.reidhoffman.org/writing/); [Hoffman’s account](https://www.reidhoffman.org/ai-voice-synthesis-tech-impromptu/))
- **2024:** *Humanity’s Hegelian Golden Braid*. A speech delivered upon Hoffman’s receipt of an honorary doctorate from the University of Perugia. Its later multilingual deliveries by REID AI turn the speech into a transmedia human–machine manifestation, while leaving Hoffman’s original English text as the underlying obra. ([Hoffman](https://www.reidhoffman.org/perugia-speech2/))
