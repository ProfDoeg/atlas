## Works by Mohammad Mosaddegh

### Academic theses and legal studies

- **1913:** *Should the State Be Held Responsible for Unlawful Acts Committed by Public Officials in the Exercise of Their Duties?* A French-language examination paper in civil law, assigned at the University of Neuchâtel on 15 April 1913; some later bibliographies incorrectly associate it with the École libre des sciences politiques in Paris. The university archive also records contemporary questions about outside help with its German-language references and French rédaction, though the faculty accepted Mosaddegh’s explanation and treated the substance as his own work. [University of Neuchâtel](https://www.unine.ch/luniversite/portrait/histoire/mohammad-mossadegh/)

- **1913:** *What Are Your Views on the Principle of Non-Extradition for Political Offences?* A French-language criminal-law examination paper, companion to the civil-law paper above rather than, as some Persian bibliographies imply, part of a single thesis. [University of Neuchâtel](https://www.unine.ch/luniversite/portrait/histoire/mohammad-mossadegh/)

- **1914:** *Le testament en droit musulman (secte chyite), précédé d’une introduction sur les sources du droit musulman*. His doctoral thesis on wills in Shiʿi Islamic law, defended at Neuchâtel on 1 May 1914 and accepted for the doctorate after deposit of the required copies that July. Partial Persian translations appeared in 1923/24, but those later editions are manifestations of the French thesis, not new works by Mosaddegh. [University repository](https://libra.unine.ch/entities/publication/a4c1a9e7-c3e3-4987-899a-88f5e07300ec)

### Legal, parliamentary, and financial treatises

- **1914 (date disputed):** *Kāpitolāsion va Irān* (*Capitulation and Iran*). A legal-political tract—and, in its effort to bring consular jurisdiction to an end, something close to a manifesto—explaining the consequences of capitulations and urging their abolition in Iran. The standard works list dates publication to Ābān 1293 SH (October–November 1914), while a later reprint’s introduction says 1290 SH; the former accords with Mosaddegh’s own account that he wrote after the Ottoman abolition announcement of October 1914. [Works bibliography](https://iranboom.ir/drmosadegh/3107-talifat-dr-mohammad-mosadegh.html), [digital-edition history](https://iranboom.ir/ketab-khaneh/anbar/9983-ketab-capitolasion-iran.html)

- **1914:** *Sherkat-e sahāmi dar Orupā* (*The Joint-Stock Company in Europe*). A 103-page comparative company-law primer intended to introduce Iranian merchants to a commercial form not yet adequately explained in Persian; Mosaddegh described preparing it from European legislation. [Works bibliography](https://iranboom.ir/drmosadegh/3107-talifat-dr-mohammad-mosadegh.html), [Mosaddegh’s retrospective account](https://mail.iranboom.ir/drmosadegh/7679-dr-mosadegh-be-ghalam-mosadegh-pas-az-khateme-tahsilat.html)

- **1915:** *Dastur dar mahākem-e hoquqi* (*Procedure in Civil Courts*). A procedural-law textbook distilled from a manuscript said to have exceeded one thousand pages, created for Mosaddegh’s teaching at Tehran’s School of Political Science; the printed book, issued in Mordād 1294 SH, ran to 497 pages. It is therefore both a treatise and a teaching protocol. [Works bibliography](https://iranboom.ir/drmosadegh/3107-talifat-dr-mohammad-mosadegh.html)

- **1923/24:** *Mokhtasari az hoquq-e pārlomāni dar Irān va Orupā barā-ye āqāyān-e mohtaram-e namāyandegān-e dowra-ye panjom-e taqninieh* (*A Brief Account of Parliamentary Law in Iran and Europe for the Honorable Representatives of the Fifth Legislative Session*). A 72-page parliamentary handbook addressed to legislators: less a general book than an operating guide to constitutional practice. [Works bibliography](https://iranboom.ir/drmosadegh/3107-talifat-dr-mohammad-mosadegh.html)

- **1925/26:** *Osul, qavāʿed va qavānin-e māliyeh dar mamālek-e khārejeh va Irān, qabl az mashrutiyat va dar dowra-ye mashrutiyat, jeld-e avval: budjeh* (*Principles, Rules, and Financial Laws in Foreign Countries and Iran, Before and During the Constitutional Period, vol. I: The Budget*). A comparative public-finance treatise presenting Mosaddegh’s administrative experience to a general readership. Its designation as “volume I” announces a larger project, but no subsequent volume appears in the standard works list; the intended series therefore seems unfinished. [Works bibliography](https://iranboom.ir/drmosadegh/3107-talifat-dr-mohammad-mosadegh.html), [digitized-copy index](https://mossadegh.org/external/)

### Articles and reform proposals

- **1914:** *Esqāt-e daʿāvi yā qāʿedeh-ye morur-e zamān* (*Extinction of Claims, or the Rule of Limitation*). A legal-reform article in the first issue of *Majalleh-ye ʿElmi*. Mosaddegh substituted this practicable proposal for an unrealized project on introducing the Swiss land-registration system; clerical objections that limitation contradicted Islamic law made the article an intervention rather than merely a description. [Works bibliography](https://iranboom.ir/drmosadegh/3107-talifat-dr-mohammad-mosadegh.html), [Mosaddegh’s account](https://mail.iranboom.ir/drmosadegh/7679-dr-mosadegh-be-ghalam-mosadegh-pas-az-khateme-tahsilat.html)

- **1925/26:** *Tasvib-e budjeh dar pārlomānhā-ye mokhtalef* (*Approval of the Budget in Different Parliaments*). An anonymously published comparative article in the first series of *Āyandeh*, signed only “name withheld,” on budget procedure in the United States, Britain, France, Germany, and constitutional Iran. The concealed contemporary byline makes its authorship less visible, though the standard Mosaddegh bibliography attributes it to him. [Works bibliography](https://iranboom.ir/drmosadegh/3107-talifat-dr-mohammad-mosadegh.html)

- **1926/27:** *Entekhabāt dar Orupā va Irān* (*Elections in Europe and Iran*). A comparative study of electoral laws in France, Britain, Germany, Italy, Canada, and Iran, published in the second series of *Āyandeh*. [Works bibliography](https://iranboom.ir/drmosadegh/3107-talifat-dr-mohammad-mosadegh.html)

- **1926/27:** *Tābeʿiyat dar Irān* (*Nationality in Iran*). A legal-historical article on Iranian nationality rules governing foreigners, including provisions originating under Nāṣer al-Din Shah and continuing under Reżā Shah. [Works bibliography](https://iranboom.ir/drmosadegh/3107-talifat-dr-mohammad-mosadegh.html)

- **1926/27:** *Osul-e mohemme-ye hoquq-e madani va hoquq-e tejāri-ye Irān* (*Important Principles of Iranian Civil and Commercial Law*). An article surveying civil law, contracts, family law, inheritance, property, and commerce from the coming of Islam through the constitutional era. [Works bibliography](https://iranboom.ir/drmosadegh/3107-talifat-dr-mohammad-mosadegh.html)

- **23 May 1944:** *Tarh-e pishnehādi barā-ye eslāh-e qānun-e entekhabāt* (*Proposed Plan for Reforming the Election Law*). A short reform proposal published in *Ettelāʿāt* while Mosaddegh represented Tehran in the Fourteenth Majles; it is a protocol-like legislative intervention, not merely commentary. [Works bibliography](https://iranboom.ir/drmosadegh/3107-talifat-dr-mohammad-mosadegh.html)

- **October 1944:** *Masʾaleh-ye entekhabāt* (*The Question of Elections*). A substantial study, organized as an introduction and six chapters, commissioned by the third series of *Āyandeh* and directed at Iran’s election laws and their reform. [Works bibliography](https://iranboom.ir/drmosadegh/3107-talifat-dr-mohammad-mosadegh.html)

### Memoir and historical testament

- **1953–1956:** *A Brief Account of My Life and Memories*. The first of two prison manuscripts later joined editorially as *Khāterāt va taʾallomāt-e Mosaddegh* (*Mosaddegh’s Memoirs and Sorrows*), covering 1882–1924. Mosaddegh did not give the combined work its familiar title: editor Iraj Afshar devised it after Mosaddegh’s death, so the published volume is partly an editorial creation rather than a single authorially titled obra. [Mossadegh Library](https://mohammadmossadegh.com/library/), [Afshar’s explanation](https://iraniferi1.blogspot.com/2012/07/blog-post_4380.html)

- **1961:** *A Brief History of the Nationalization of the Oil Industry in Iran*. The second memoir manuscript, composed under house arrest at Ahmadabad as both historical narrative and answer to Mohammad Reza Shah’s *Mission for My Country*. It was subsequently bound with the earlier autobiographical manuscript under Afshar’s title *Khāterāt va taʾallomāt*. [Mossadegh Library](https://mohammadmossadegh.com/library/)

### Speeches, messages, and parliamentary interventions

- **1924–1928:** *Speeches and Writings from the Fifth and Sixth Majles*. A body of parliamentary performances subsequently assembled by Hoseyn Makki and, in a broader editorial series, as part of *Notq-hā va maktubāt-e Doktor Mosaddegh*. The speeches are Mosaddegh’s acts, but their familiar book titles, selection, sequence, and publication are the collectors’ work and should not be mistaken for books he composed. [Works bibliography](https://iranboom.ir/drmosadegh/3107-talifat-dr-mohammad-mosadegh.html)

- **1944–1946:** *Speeches in the Fourteenth Majles*. A corpus later published by Hoseyn Key-Estovān as *Siyāsat-e movāzeneh-ye manfi dar Majles-e chahārdahom* (*The Policy of Negative Equilibrium in the Fourteenth Majles*). “Negative equilibrium” names the political project enacted through the speeches, while the volumes themselves are post-performance editorial manifestations. [Works bibliography](https://iranboom.ir/drmosadegh/3107-talifat-dr-mohammad-mosadegh.html)

- **1950–1951:** *Speeches in the Sixteenth Majles*. Parliamentary oratory associated with the struggle for oil nationalization, later issued in collected-speech volumes. These resist classification as authored books: they were spoken political acts whose printed form depends on parliamentary records and later editors. [Mossadegh archive index](https://mossadegh.org/external/)

- **1951–1953:** *Prime-ministerial speeches, messages, reports, and correspondence*. A dispersed body of governmental texts—including reports to the Majles and Senate on oil negotiations—later gathered in the multi-volume *Notq-hā va maktubāt*. Authorship is institutional as well as personal: Mosaddegh delivered or signed them, but ministries, secretariats, translators, and editors helped produce their surviving forms. [Mossadegh Foundation catalogue](https://www.mossadegh.com/bibliotheque/publique/listautlivre.php?Autor=1), [archive index](https://mossadegh.org/external/)

### Legal pleadings and forensic performances

- **1951–1952:** *Iran’s pleadings in the Anglo-Iranian Oil Company dispute*. Written and oral arguments before the United Nations Security Council and the International Court of Justice, later collected under titles such as *Modāfeʿāt-e Mosaddegh va Rolin dar Divān-e beynolmelali-ye Lāheh*. This is not securely a sole-authored work: Mosaddegh’s advocacy was made jointly with Belgian counsel Henri Rolin and Iran’s legal and diplomatic team. [Works bibliography](https://iranboom.ir/drmosadegh/3107-talifat-dr-mohammad-mosadegh.html)

- **1953–1954:** *Defences before the Military Tribunal*. Mosaddegh’s spoken pleadings at his post-coup trial, preserved in transcripts and later edited by Jalil Bozorgmehr as *Doktor Mohammad Mosaddegh dar mahkameh-ye nezāmi*. The defence functions as a manifesto of lawful premiership, but the two-volume publication is an editorial record, not a manuscript book made by Mosaddegh. [Works bibliography](https://iranboom.ir/drmosadegh/3107-talifat-dr-mohammad-mosadegh.html)

- **1954:** *Defences before the Military Court of Appeal*. A further body of courtroom speech, later edited by Jalil Bozorgmehr. Its obra is the forensic performance; the book published in 1986 is a posthumous documentary manifestation. [Works bibliography](https://iranboom.ir/drmosadegh/3107-talifat-dr-mohammad-mosadegh.html)

- **1954–1956:** *Petitions and arguments in cassation proceedings before the Court of Cassation*. Legal submissions later published as *Doktor Mohammad Mosaddegh va residegi-ye farjāmi dar Divān-e Keshvar*. Their exact textual boundaries and division of labor between Mosaddegh and his legal assistants are not established by the consulted bibliography. [Works bibliography](https://iranboom.ir/drmosadegh/3107-talifat-dr-mohammad-mosadegh.html)

- **1953–1956:** *Prison dictations*. Recollections and observations dictated to Colonel Jalil Bozorgmehr and published after both men’s editorial mediation as *Taqrirāt-e Mosaddegh dar zendān*, later retitled *Ranj-hā-ye siyāsi-ye Doktor Mosaddegh*. Because another person recorded the words and later editors shaped the book, it resists treatment as a wholly authorial written work. [Works bibliography](https://iranboom.ir/drmosadegh/3107-talifat-dr-mohammad-mosadegh.html)

### Correspondence

- **Dates uncertain–1967:** *General correspondence and handwritten notes*. Mohammad Torkamān’s two-volume *Nāmeh-hā-ye Doktor Mosaddegh* reproduces 553 items in its first volume and 693 in its second, addressed to individuals and institutions in Iran and abroad. This is a body of work rather than one titled work; its limits reflect what the editor could locate, not a complete authorial corpus. [Works bibliography](https://iranboom.ir/drmosadegh/3107-talifat-dr-mohammad-mosadegh.html), [bibliographic record](https://books.google.com/books/about/%D9%86%D8%A7%D9%85%D9%87%D9%87%D8%A7%D9%89_%D8%AF%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%B1_%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%AF%D9%82.html?id=wPdtAAAAMAAJ)

- **1956–1966:** *Letters from Ahmadabad to Nosratollah Amini*. A surviving sequence of letters written during house arrest to Mosaddegh’s lawyer, friend, and confidant, later selected and introduced by Fariba Amini as *Letters from Ahmadabad*. The selection is not the correspondence itself and makes no claim to completeness. [Mossadegh Library](https://mohammadmossadegh.com/library/)

### Collective political projects

- **1949 onward:** *The National Front of Iran*. A political project co-founded and led by Mosaddegh, not a sole-authored object: its organization, program, and public manifestations were made collectively by politicians, parties, associations, and supporters. It belongs in an account of what he helped make, but resists inclusion in a conventional bibliography. [Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Mohammad-Mosaddegh)

- **1951:** *Nationalization of the Iranian oil industry*. A legislative and governmental project with Mosaddegh as its defining advocate and executor, but neither his private creation nor a single text: the Oil Committee, Majles, Senate, shah, National Front, oil workers, and wider national movement all participated in bringing it into being. The later memoir treats the project historically, while the project itself remains a collective political obra. [Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Mohammad-Mosaddegh), [Mossadegh Library](https://mohammadmossadegh.com/library/)

- **1952–1953:** *Governmental reform legislation under the emergency-powers laws*. A large legislative program—described in the Dehkhoda biographical entry as roughly eighty enactments concerning courts, finance, taxation, budgets, housing, health, the military, social insurance, and freedom of the press. These measures are protocols and institutional creations rather than individually authored literary works; cabinet officials and legal drafters shared their making, and the consulted works bibliography does not enumerate every bill. [Dehkhoda entry](https://iranboom.ir/drmosadegh/1065-drmosadegh-dehkhoda.html)

### Supported publication rather than authored work

- **1920s:** *La Cité antique* (*Ancient Society/Ancient City*) by Numa Denis Fustel de Coulanges, Persian translation by Nasrollah Falsafi. Mosaddegh paid for and distributed this translation from his parliamentary salary, but he neither wrote nor translated it. It therefore resists inclusion as one of his works: what he made possible was the publication and its free circulation. [Dehkhoda entry](https://iranboom.ir/drmosadegh/1065-drmosadegh-dehkhoda.html)
