## Works by Jürgen Habermas

### Academic theses and research works

- **1954:** *Das Absolute und die Geschichte. Von der Zwiespältigkeit in Schellings Denken*. Doctoral dissertation on Schelling, submitted at Bonn and issued by Bouvier; it is Habermas’s first book-length scholarly obra.
- **1961:** *Student und Politik. Eine soziologische Untersuchung zum politischen Bewußtsein Frankfurter Studenten*. Collaborative empirical study made with Frank Benseler, Ludwig von Friedeburg, Christoph Oehler, and Friedrich Weltz; Habermas wrote its theoretical introduction, so the whole cannot be treated as solely authored by him.
- **1961–1962:** *Strukturwandel der Öffentlichkeit. Untersuchungen zu einer Kategorie der bürgerlichen Gesellschaft*. Habilitation thesis completed in 1961 and published as a monograph in 1962; the substantially new 1990 preface makes the later edition a revision as well as a reissue.
- **1967; expanded 1970 and 1982:** *Zur Logik der Sozialwissenschaften*. A methodological study first appearing as a journal supplement and subsequently reconstructed as a book; its changing extent resists treatment as one invariant text.
- **1968:** *Erkenntnis und Interesse*. A systematic philosophical obra developed from Habermas’s Frankfurt inaugural lecture and other studies; the 1973 edition added a substantial postscript.
- **1973:** *Legitimationsprobleme im Spätkapitalismus*. A monographic diagnosis of crisis tendencies in advanced capitalism.
- **1981:** *Theorie des kommunikativen Handelns*. A two-volume theoretical obra—*Handlungsrationalität und gesellschaftliche Rationalisierung* and *Zur Kritik der funktionalistischen Vernunft*—rather than an essay collection.
- **1985:** *Der philosophische Diskurs der Moderne. Zwölf Vorlesungen*. A lecture-cycle made into a systematic book; its chapters retain the character of spoken interventions.
- **1992:** *Faktizität und Geltung. Beiträge zur Diskurstheorie des Rechts und des demokratischen Rechtsstaats*. A systematic work of legal and democratic theory, notwithstanding its subtitle’s description of its chapters as “contributions.”
- **2001:** *Kommunikatives Handeln und detranszendentalisierte Vernunft*. A compact philosophical monograph restating the foundations of communicative rationality.
- **2001; expanded 2002:** *Die Zukunft der menschlichen Natur. Auf dem Weg zu einer liberalen Eugenik?* A bioethical intervention assembled around lectures and essays; the expanded edition added the postscript *Postskriptum Ende 2001*.
- **2019; revised 2022:** *Auch eine Geschichte der Philosophie*. A two-volume genealogical project comprising *Die okzidentale Konstellation von Glauben und Wissen* and *Vernünftige Freiheit*; the 2022 edition added a new afterword. Its English translation divides the same obra into three volumes, which are manifestations rather than three new works.

### Essay constellations and reconstructed books

- **1963; substantially revised 1971:** *Theorie und Praxis. Sozialphilosophische Studien*. A deliberately composed constellation of studies, not a single continuous treatise; the revised edition removed two essays and added a new introduction and extensive notes.
- **1968:** *Technik und Wissenschaft als „Ideologie“*. A three-essay book whose title essay originated as a radio lecture; the volume is a made constellation rather than a new unitary manuscript.
- **1969:** *Protestbewegung und Hochschulreform*. A documentary and argumentative assemblage of Habermas’s writings on universities and the student movement.
- **1971; enlarged 1981:** *Philosophisch-politische Profile*. Portrait-essays on philosophers and intellectuals; the enlarged edition materially altered the boundaries of the collection.
- **1973:** *Kultur und Kritik. Verstreute Aufsätze*. A gathering of previously dispersed essays, explicitly presented as such.
- **1976:** *Zur Rekonstruktion des Historischen Materialismus*. A programmatic collection of twelve studies; several component texts had appeared independently.
- **1978:** *Politik, Kunst, Religion. Essays über zeitgenössische Philosophen*. A selective profile collection that substantially overlaps *Philosophisch-politische Profile* and therefore resists being counted as wholly new textual matter.
- **1983:** *Moralbewußtsein und kommunikatives Handeln*. A constructed sequence of essays and lectures developing discourse ethics.
- **1984:** *Vorstudien und Ergänzungen zur Theorie des kommunikativen Handelns*. A companion constellation of preliminary studies, replies, and supplements rather than a sequel conceived as one continuous book.
- **1988:** *Nachmetaphysisches Denken. Philosophische Aufsätze*. A philosophical essay collection organized as a common project of postmetaphysical thought.
- **1990:** *Die Moderne – ein unvollendetes Projekt. Philosophisch-politische Aufsätze*. A Leipzig selection of existing essays; it is a new editorial manifestation, but only partly a new Habermasian obra.
- **1991:** *Erläuterungen zur Diskursethik*. A collection of essays and replies that clarifies and defends the discourse-ethical project.
- **1991:** *Texte und Kontexte*. A collection of philosophical profiles and contextual essays.
- **1996:** *Die Einbeziehung des Anderen. Studien zur politischen Theorie*. A composed collection joining essays on discourse ethics, political liberalism, rights, nationhood, and cosmopolitanism.
- **1997:** *Vom sinnlichen Eindruck zum symbolischen Ausdruck. Philosophische Essays*. A small philosophical profile collection organized around the transition from experience to symbolic form.
- **1998:** *Die postnationale Konstellation. Politische Essays*. A constellation of political diagnoses rather than a unitary treatise.
- **1999:** *Wahrheit und Rechtfertigung. Philosophische Aufsätze*. A systematic essay collection on pragmatism, truth, realism, and justification.
- **2003:** *Zeitdiagnosen. Zwölf Essays 1980–2001*. A retrospective selection; its texts pre-existed the volume, so the book is principally a new arrangement and manifestation.
- **2005:** *Zwischen Naturalismus und Religion. Philosophische Aufsätze*. A collection joining philosophy of language, moral theory, naturalism, and postsecular religion.
- **2009:** *Philosophische Texte*. A five-volume Studienausgabe organized as *Sprachtheoretische Grundlegung der Soziologie*, *Rationalitäts- und Sprachtheorie*, *Diskursethik*, *Politische Theorie*, and *Kritik der Vernunft*. It is Habermas’s large self-curated corpus and rearrangement of earlier writings, not five newly written obras.
- **2012:** *Nachmetaphysisches Denken II. Aufsätze und Repliken*. A second constellation of essays and replies, extending rather than replacing the 1988 project.
- **2022:** *Ein neuer Strukturwandel der Öffentlichkeit und die deliberative Politik*. A compact reconstructed book combining a previously published study of digital public spheres with a new preface and an essay answering objections to deliberative democracy; some passages reuse earlier material.

### Political writings: a serial obra

- **1981:** *Kleine politische Schriften I–IV*. A retrospective four-part corpus gathering Habermas’s interventions from the university-reform debates through the politics of the 1970s.
- **1985:** *Die neue Unübersichtlichkeit. Kleine politische Schriften V*. Political essays diagnosing the exhaustion of utopian energies and transformations of the welfare state.
- **1987:** *Eine Art Schadensabwicklung. Kleine politische Schriften VI*. A documentary-interventionist book centered on the *Historikerstreit*; several component texts acted publicly in that controversy and are closer to manifestos than detached descriptions.
- **1990:** *Die nachholende Revolution. Kleine politische Schriften VII*. Contemporary interventions on the revolutions of 1989 and German unification.
- **1995:** *Die Normalität einer Berliner Republik. Kleine politische Schriften VIII*. Interventions on German historical memory, reunification, asylum, citizenship, and the emerging Berlin Republic.
- **2001:** *Zeit der Übergänge. Kleine politische Schriften IX*. Essays and interviews addressing globalization, national identity, Europe, and democratic politics.
- **2004:** *Der gespaltene Westen. Kleine politische Schriften X*. Political interventions shaped by the Iraq War, international law, and the division between Europe and the United States.
- **2008:** *Ach, Europa. Kleine politische Schriften XI*. Essays, memorial portraits, and European-policy interventions; it is a mixed public-intellectual constellation.
- **2013:** *Im Sog der Technokratie. Kleine politische Schriften XII*. Essays and speeches on the European crisis, democratic legitimacy, and technocratic rule.
- **1981–2013:** *Kleine politische Schriften I–XII*. Taken together, the twelve parts form a serial obra of public intervention. Individual pieces often began as newspaper articles, speeches, or manifestos, so the series cannot be reduced either to twelve ordinary books or to one continuous text.

### Lectures, speeches, and compact interventions issued as works

- **1980:** *Die Moderne – ein unvollendetes Projekt*. Adorno Prize lecture and programmatic manifesto for continuing the emancipatory project of modernity; later absorbed into several collections.
- **1984:** *Vorlesungen zu einer sprachtheoretischen Grundlegung der Soziologie*. Lectures circulated in the *Vorstudien* corpus rather than an independently completed monograph; their status is preparatory and pedagogical.
- **1986:** *Eine Art Schadensabwicklung*. Newspaper intervention against revisionist treatments of National Socialism; it acted within the *Historikerstreit* before becoming the title-piece of a collection.
- **1990:** *Die nachholende Revolution*. Political diagnosis of the East German transformation; originally an intervention, then the organizing title of *Kleine politische Schriften VII*.
- **2001:** *Glauben und Wissen*. Peace Prize address examining secular reason and religious inheritance; the separately issued booklet is a manifestation of the speech, not a newly composed second work.
- **2003:** *Was bedeutet der Denkmalsturz?*. A public intervention on the Iraq War and international order, made with Jacques Derrida and published in newspapers under differing titles; its precise bibliographic identity varies by manifestation.
- **2005:** *Religion in der Öffentlichkeit. Kognitive Voraussetzungen für den „öffentlichen Vernunftgebrauch“ religiöser und säkularer Bürger*. Holberg Prize lecture; a spoken scholarly intervention subsequently published in several forms.
- **2011:** *Zur Verfassung Europas. Ein Essay*. A political essay accompanied by related texts on human rights and European constitutionalization.
- **2021–2022:** *Überlegungen und Hypothesen zu einem erneuten Strukturwandel der politischen Öffentlichkeit*. Essay first published in an edited research volume and then made the principal component of *Ein neuer Strukturwandel der Öffentlichkeit*; these are two manifestations of substantially the same text.
- **2021:** *Corona und der Schutz des Lebens*. Public intervention on fundamental rights and compulsory solidarity during the pandemic; the longer scholarly version appeared as *Grundrechtsschutz in der pandemischen Ausnahmesituation*.
- **2022:** *Krieg und Empörung*. Newspaper intervention on Germany, arms deliveries, and the war in Ukraine; it acts in an immediate political dispute and thus approaches a manifesto without presenting itself as one.
- **2023:** *Ein Plädoyer für Verhandlungen*. Newspaper intervention urging diplomatic initiatives over Ukraine; its genre is an argumentative public appeal.
- **2023:** *Grundsätze der Solidarität. Eine Stellungnahme*. Collective public statement made with Nicole Deitelhoff, Rainer Forst, and Klaus Günther concerning Israel and Gaza; authorship is shared, and the text acts rather than merely describes.
- **2025:** *Für Europa*. Newspaper appeal for deeper European integration and collective defence.
- **2025:** *Von hier an müssen wir alleine weitergehen*. Habermas’s final substantial newspaper intervention, on Europe’s autonomy amid the United States’ authoritarian turn; translations appeared under materially different English titles.

### Dialogues and collectively authored discursive objects

- **1971:** *Theorie der Gesellschaft oder Sozialtechnologie: Was leistet die Systemforschung?* A staged theoretical controversy with Niklas Luhmann, supplemented by contributions from other scholars; it is a dialogical object, not a co-written monograph expressing one position.
- **1985:** *Autonomy and Solidarity: Interviews with Jürgen Habermas*. Interviews edited by Peter Dews; the answers are Habermas’s speech-texts, while the selection and English book-object belong partly to the editor.
- **1991:** *Vergangenheit als Zukunft? Das alte Deutschland im neuen Europa?* A conversation with Michael Haller; its form and authorship are dialogical.
- **1994:** *The Past as Future*. English reconstruction of the Haller conversation plus other interview material; it is not simply a translation of one fixed German original.
- **1995:** *Die Normalität einer Berliner Republik: Kleine politische Schriften VIII*. Although catalogued as an authored collection, several components are interviews and debates, making the volume a mixed-authorship discursive object.
- **1998:** *The Inclusion of the Other: Studies in Political Theory*. English collection whose contents and arrangement do not exactly duplicate the German *Die Einbeziehung des Anderen*; it is an authorized translated reconstruction.
- **2005:** *Dialektik der Säkularisierung. Über Vernunft und Religion*. Published dialogue between Habermas and Joseph Ratzinger, arising from their 2004 Munich encounter; neither participant authored the whole argument.
- **2007:** *Ein Bewußtsein von dem, was fehlt*. Discussion with philosophers from the Munich School of Philosophy, edited by Michael Reder and Josef Schmidt; Habermas made the initiating statement and replies, not the entire book.
- **2008:** *European Prospects / Europäische Perspektiven*. Bilingual political dialogue made with Frank-Walter Steinmeier and framed editorially by Julian Nida-Rümelin and Wolfgang Thierse.
- **2010:** *An Awareness of What Is Missing: Faith and Reason in a Post-secular Age*. English manifestation of the Munich discussion; a translated collective object rather than a new Habermas work.
- **2024:** *„Es musste etwas besser werden …“. Gespräche mit Stefan Müller-Doohm und Roman Yos*. A book of autobiographical and intellectual conversations; Habermas is the answering voice, while Müller-Doohm and Yos co-produced its questions, structure, and final dialogical form.

### Editorial projects

- **1979:** *Stichworte zur „Geistigen Situation der Zeit“, Band 1: Nation und Republik*. First volume of a collective diagnosis edited by Habermas; the contributions are not all his works.
- **1979:** *Stichworte zur „Geistigen Situation der Zeit“, Band 2: Politik und Kultur*. Second volume of the same editorial project; its obra is Habermas’s commissioning and arrangement of a plural intellectual intervention.
- **2009:** *Philosophische Texte: Studienausgabe in fünf Bänden*. Besides being a collected manifestation of his writings, this is a major act of self-editing: Habermas selected, grouped, revised, and supplied texts for a thematic presentation of his philosophical corpus.

### Unauthorized or unstable manifestations

- **1970:** *Arbeit, Erkenntnis, Fortschritt. Aufsätze 1954–1970*. An unauthorized Amsterdam “pirate” collection of early essays. It exists as a book-object but resists inclusion as an obra made or approved by Habermas.
- **1973:** *Arbeit–Freizeit–Konsum: Frühe Aufsätze*. Another unauthorized compilation of early writings; the component texts are Habermas’s, but this particular arrangement is not securely attributable to him.
- **Various dates:** Translated readers including *Toward a Rational Society*, *Communication and the Evolution of Society*, *The New Conservatism*, *The Postnational Constellation*, *Religion and Rationality*, and *Europe: The Faltering Project*. These are important authorized manifestations, but their selections often cut across German books and combine independently made essays; they should not be silently counted as wholly new original obras.

### The dispersed corpus

- **1951–2025:** *Articles, reviews, lectures, replies, letters, interviews, memorial pieces, prefaces, and newspaper interventions*. Habermas made several hundred separately appearing texts, beginning with the school essay *Ist unsere Generation modern?* (1951) and ending with writings published in 2025. They resist honest compression into one bullet per “work”: many were retitled, revised, abridged, translated, absorbed into books, or produced through interviews, and the scholarly bibliography records those relationships rather than pretending each manifestation is a distinct creation.
