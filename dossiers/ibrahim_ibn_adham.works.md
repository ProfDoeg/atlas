## Works by Ibrahim ibn Adham

### Transmitted teachings

- **Before 161–162 AH / 778–779 CE:** *Oral counsels and ascetic sayings*. An untitled, dispersed body of admonitions delivered in teaching circles and preserved only through later transmitters, principally in al-Sulamī’s *Ṭabaqāt al-ṣūfiyya* and Abū Nuʿaym’s *Ḥilyat al-awliyāʾ*; it is an oral obra rather than a book, and the distance between utterance and surviving record makes the wording and individual attributions uncertain. The teachings concern renunciation, lawful livelihood, generosity, worship, and a threefold division of *zuhd* (obligatory, supererogatory, and precautionary). [TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi](https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/ibrahim-b-edhem#1)

- **Before 161–162 AH / 778–779 CE:** *Responses to questions*. Short written or spoken answers sent to people who sought his advice, without an authorial title or recoverable independent collection. They resist classification as separate works: the evidence establishes a practice of answering correspondents, but the answers survive embedded in later biographical compilations rather than in an autograph or author-arranged text. [TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi](https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/ibrahim-b-edhem#1)

### Correspondence

- **Before 161–162 AH / 778–779 CE:** *Letters exchanged with Sufyān al-Thawrī and al-Awzāʿī*. A lost or fragmentarily quoted correspondence rather than a titled book; the standard biographical account says that Ibrāhīm corresponded with both men but does not establish a complete corpus, fixed titles, or dates for individual letters. [TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi](https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/ibrahim-b-edhem#1)

### Devotional texts

- **Before 161–162 AH / 778–779 CE:** *Friday morning and evening wird*. A devotional protocol beginning *Marḥaban bi-yawm al-mazīd wa-l-ṣubḥ al-jadīd*, reportedly recited ten times every Friday morning and evening and recorded by Abū Nuʿaym. Its existence is known through later transmission; whether its precise surviving wording is Ibrāhīm’s own composition cannot be demonstrated. [TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi](https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/ibrahim-b-edhem#1)

- **Before 161–162 AH / 778–779 CE:** *Munājāt at the Kaʿba*. A six-couplet Arabic supplication-poem beginning *Hajartu al-khalqa ṭurran fī hawāka*, traditionally said to have been sung or recited by Ibrāhīm while visiting the Kaʿba. It is a lyric devotional manifestation preserved in later miscellanies, not a securely documented authorial manuscript; attribution and exact textual form therefore remain traditional rather than critically established. [TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi](https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/ibrahim-b-edhem#1)

- **Before 161–162 AH / 778–779 CE:** *Occasional verses attributed to Ibrāhīm ibn Adham*. Early and medieval sources say that he sometimes composed or recited poetry and preserve several attributed lines, but no authorial collection, stable title, or complete corpus is known. This is best treated as a disputed residue of oral poetry, not as a lost *dīwān* whose existence can safely be assumed. [TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi](https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/ibrahim-b-edhem#1)

### Disputed and misclassified works

- **Undated; compiled after Ibrāhīm’s lifetime:** *Jawāb Ibrāhīm ibn Adham ʿan ʿadam istijābat al-duʿāʾ* (“Ibrāhīm ibn Adham’s Answer concerning the Non-acceptance of Prayer”). A manuscript compilation attributed to him because its opening contains an explanation, itself merely reported as his, of why prayers go unanswered; scholarship identifies the larger work as an anonymous compilation, so it should not be accepted as his obra. [TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi](https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/ibrahim-b-edhem#1)

- **Late fourth/tenth century compilation:** *Musnad Ibrāhīm ibn Adham al-Zāhid* (also *Musnad aḥādīth Ibrāhīm ibn Adham*). This collection of fifty-one transmitted reports is not a book made by Ibrāhīm: Ibn Manda assembled it from reports that Ibrāhīm had narrated, some of them *mursal*. It belongs to the history of his transmission and reception, not to his authored works. [TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi](https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/ibrahim-b-edhem#1), [digital edition](https://dl.lib.iq/reader/2569/1)
