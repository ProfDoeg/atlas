## Works by Diego Gutiérrez Zaldívar

### Digital platforms and technological ventures

- **1996:** *Clarín Digital*. The original web edition of the Argentine newspaper *Clarín*, launched on 10 March 1996. Gutiérrez Zaldívar’s professional CV places him on its founding team rather than presenting it as an individually authored work; this is therefore a collective digital publication and continuously changing platform. ([CV](https://archivo.consejo.org.ar/Cvs/zaldivar_diego.html); [Clarín’s anniversary history](https://www.clarin.com/sociedad/clarincom-cumple-anos_0_H1kW6UHaPQe.html))

- **1997:** *Patagon.com*. An online financial-information, community and trading platform. His CV describes him as part of the founding team, but contemporary accounts name Wenceslao Casares and Constancio Larguía—and in another account Federico Agárdy—as the founders; his precise founding status is therefore inconsistent across the sources. ([CV](https://archivo.consejo.org.ar/Cvs/zaldivar_diego.html); [contemporary history](https://www.clarin.com/economia/historia-futuro-venta-patagoncom_0_H1g-tOjg0tg.html))

- *Internet Argentina*. An Internet-service venture identified in his CV as the first Argentine ISP to provide ADSL and as another project whose founding team included him. The consulted professional record supplies no foundation date, while independent telecommunications histories complicate the claim of ADSL priority; the date and exact extent of his authorship should consequently remain open. ([CV](https://archivo.consejo.org.ar/Cvs/zaldivar_diego.html))

- *Edunexo*. A company and SaaS platform for administering public and private educational institutions in Latin America and Spain. His CV credits him as a member of its founding team but supplies neither a date nor an individual technical authorship claim. ([CV](https://archivo.consejo.org.ar/Cvs/zaldivar_diego.html))

- *Xinergia*. A web-development company in which a 2014 profile identifies him as a partner; a separate biographical directory calls him its founder. Because the stronger contemporary source does not establish that he created it, this resists inclusion as an undisputed obra and is retained as a disputed entrepreneurial attribution. ([Aduana News interview](https://aduananews.com/en/entrevista-a-diego-gutierrez-zaldivar-pionero-del-bitcoin-en-argentina/); [biographical directory](https://www.weusecoins.com/diego-gutierrez/))

- *Cero a Cien*. A venture attributed to him as founder by a biographical directory, but absent from the more detailed professional CV consulted here. Its date, nature and attribution could not be corroborated, so it remains a named but incompletely documented project rather than a securely established work. ([biographical directory](https://www.weusecoins.com/diego-gutierrez/); [CV](https://archivo.consejo.org.ar/Cvs/zaldivar_diego.html))

- **2013:** *Koibanx*. A jointly founded blockchain-services company initially oriented toward asset tokenization and later toward financial infrastructure. It is an organization and evolving technical project, not a single authored software object. ([TED speaker biography](https://www.ted.com/talks/diego_gutierrez_zaldivar_tu_identidad_reputacion_y_confianza_en_blockchain); [Koibanx](https://koibanx.com/about))

- *Restocoins*. A Bitcoin-payment system for restaurants that was operating by March 2014. One directory attributes its foundation to Gutiérrez Zaldívar, while his contemporary interview merely presents it as an existing example; authorship and its exact creation date therefore remain uncertain. ([Aduana News interview](https://aduananews.com/en/entrevista-a-diego-gutierrez-zaldivar-pionero-del-bitcoin-en-argentina/); [biographical directory](https://www.weusecoins.com/diego-gutierrez/))

- **2015–present:** *Rootstock* (originally *RSK*). A collective project for a Bitcoin-secured, Ethereum-compatible smart-contract sidechain: the idea and white paper emerged in 2015, a test network followed, and the public mainnet launched in January 2018. Gutiérrez Zaldívar co-founded the organization behind it, but Sergio Demian Lerner claims design and white-paper authorship; Rootstock should therefore not be attributed to Gutiérrez Zaldívar alone. ([Rootstock Labs history](https://www.rootstocklabs.com/about-us/); [Sergio Lerner’s account](https://bitslog.com/about/); [2019 TED biography](https://www.ted.com/talks/diego_gutierrez_zaldivar_tu_identidad_reputacion_y_confianza_en_blockchain))

- **2015–present:** *RSK Labs / IOV Labs / RootstockLabs*. The company formed to develop the Rootstock ecosystem, repeatedly renamed as its scope and public identity changed. It is a continuing collective infrastructure organization rather than a separable authored work; the sources disagree on whether the corporate foundation belongs in 2015, 2016 or 2017, so no single incorporation year is silently preferred here. ([Rootstock Labs history](https://www.rootstocklabs.com/about-us/); [RoadShow interview](https://www.roadshow.com.ar/diego-gutierrez-zaldivar-en-el-mundo-cripto-estan-fallando-los-intermediarios-no-las-plataformas/); [TED biography](https://www.ted.com/talks/diego_gutierrez_zaldivar_tu_identidad_reputacion_y_confianza_en_blockchain))

### Communities and institutions

- **2013–present:** *Comunidad Bitcoin Argentina / ONG Bitcoin Argentina*. A community and subsequently organized nonprofit co-founded with Rodolfo Andragnes and Franco Amati after the early Buenos Aires Bitcoin meetups. Contemporary reporting called the proposed “Fundación Bitcoin Argentina” still in formation, whereas the present organization describes itself as active since 2013; its changing legal and institutional identity resists treatment as one fixed work. ([ONG history](https://bitcoin.ar/nosotros/); [2014 interview](https://aduananews.com/en/entrevista-a-diego-gutierrez-zaldivar-pionero-del-bitcoin-en-argentina/); [MIT thesis](https://cmsw.mit.edu/wp/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/guarna-tguarna-sm-cms-2022.pdf))

- **2013–present:** *Fundación Bitcoin Iberoamérica* (also described in earlier sources as *Bitcoin Latam NGO*). A transnational institution intended to connect and support Bitcoin communities across Latin America and Ibero-America, co-founded rather than singly made by Gutiérrez Zaldívar. Its present website treats LABITCONF and several educational and cultural initiatives as projects under its umbrella. ([Foundation website](https://bitcoiniberoamerica.org/); [biographical account](https://www.bitgivefoundation.org/speakers/diego-gutierrez-zaldivar/))

### Conferences and recurring manifestations

- **2013–present:** *LABITCONF / Latin American Bitcoin & Blockchain Conference*. A recurring conference initiated by Gutiérrez Zaldívar, Rodolfo Andragnes and Franco Amati; its first manifestation took place in Buenos Aires on 7–8 December 2013 and later editions moved among Latin American countries. It is simultaneously a continuing project and a sequence of distinct annual manifestations, not one stable text or event-object. ([MIT thesis](https://cmsw.mit.edu/wp/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/guarna-tguarna-sm-cms-2022.pdf); [Gutiérrez Zaldívar’s project description](https://ar.linkedin.com/in/diegogutierrezzaldivar); [Foundation website](https://bitcoiniberoamerica.org/))

### Spoken and audiovisual texts

- **2019:** *Tu identidad, reputación y confianza en blockchain*. A short TEDxRíodelaPlata talk—an oral and audiovisual text—about using blockchain-based identity and reputation to support relationships of trust. The talk is authored and performed by Gutiérrez Zaldívar, while the recording is a TEDx production. ([TED](https://www.ted.com/talks/diego_gutierrez_zaldivar_tu_identidad_reputacion_y_confianza_en_blockchain))

### Journey-project and art vehicle

- **By 2019–present:** *El Bondi 60*. A jointly created journey-project centered on a Mercedes-Benz 1114 bus and an intention to travel from Ushuaia to Alaska, later extended into a continuing series of journeys. It resists being called a discrete work: it is at once vehicle, social group, itinerary and unfinished travel project, and the available account names nine creators without identifying all of them. ([Zarpado](https://www.zarpado.com/el-festival-burningman-comienza-hoy-y-cuenta-con-una-instalcion-artistica-de-un-colectivo-de-la-linea-60-fileteado-por-la-artista-argentina-silvia-dotta/))

- **2025:** *El Bondi 60: Burning Man Art Car*. A manifestation of the journey-project as a mobile installation at Burning Man, developed collectively after Gutiérrez Zaldívar proposed converting the bus into an Art Car. The bus’s fileteado was executed by Silvia Dotta and Diana Neumeyer, with the Caminito structure and figures made by other named collaborators; Gutiérrez Zaldívar is thus a project co-creator, not the sole visual artist. ([Zarpado](https://www.zarpado.com/el-festival-burningman-comienza-hoy-y-cuenta-con-una-instalcion-artistica-de-un-colectivo-de-la-linea-60-fileteado-por-la-artista-argentina-silvia-dotta/))
