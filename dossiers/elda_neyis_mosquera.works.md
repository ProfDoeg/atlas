## Works by Elda Neyis Mosquera García

### Co-authored testimonial obra

- **2025:** *Volver a ser Elda: Una biografía íntima de alias Karina*. Book-length memoir and testimony co-authored with political scientist Gustavo Duncan; Mosquera supplied the first-person narrative, while Duncan shaped it in writing, so it is neither an unmediated autobiography nor Duncan’s biography of her alone. Debate’s author catalogue lists this as Mosquera García’s sole published title. ([Debate/Penguin](https://www.penguinlibros.com/pe/tematicas/430594-ebook-volver-a-ser-elda-9786287669918), [El País](https://elpais.com/america-colombia/2025-09-07/elda-mosquera-alias-karina-mi-verdad-no-es-la-misma-que-la-del-secretariado-de-las-farc.html), [author catalogue](https://www.penguinlibros.com/us/1002405-elda-neyis-mosquera-garcia))

### Testimonial action and documentary manifestation

- **2021:** *¿Por qué nos arrebató a nuestros hijos? La pregunta de las mujeres de Argelia a Elda Mosquera, alias “Karina”*. A five-month truth-and-responsibility process involving Mosquera and mothers whose children were recruited or disappeared, subsequently manifested as a documentary. Mosquera is its central respondent and participant—not its sole filmmaker or conventional “author”—and the underlying private encounters resist classification as a discrete authored work. ([Comisión de la Verdad](https://web.comisiondelaverdad.co/actualidad/noticias/por-que-nos-arrebato-a-nuestros-hijos-el-documental-en-el-que-alias-karina-reconoce-el-dano-causado-a-las-madres-de-argelia), [ICTJ](https://www.ictj.org/sites/default/files/2024-02/ictj_report_systematization_Colombia_2024_sp_0.pdf))

### Peace-work project

- **2009–at least 2015:** *Gestora de paz*. An official, open-ended project rather than a titled artwork: Mosquera proposed assisting with hostage releases and conducted outreach intended to discourage imprisoned guerrillas from returning to armed activity. The documentation establishes activities and institutional status, but not a complete catalogue of individually authored texts or interventions. ([Caracol Radio](https://caracol.com.co/radio/2009/09/01/nacional/1251823080_871064.html), [Fiscalía General de la Nación](https://www.fiscalia.gov.co/colombia/wp-content/uploads/2026-05-06-ELDA-NEYIS-MOSQUERA-Y-OTROS.pdf), [El Colombiano](https://www.elcolombiano.com/colombia/politica/las-farc-estan-abonando-el-terreno-para-su-entrada-a-la-politica--alias-karina-BE2978726))

### Uncatalogued material production

- **Date not established; documented in 2025:** *Garments made for third parties*. Mosquera described her civilian occupation as making clothing under contract. This is a body of material work, but the garments are anonymous, untitled, and uncatalogued; available sources do not permit them to be separated into individual obras without inventing titles, dates, or quantities. ([Semana](https://www.semana.com/amp/politica/articulo/karina-la-desmovilizada-de-las-farc-que-mas-persiguieron-las-autoridades-escribio-su-biografia-revelo-como-un-guerrillero-trozo-el-brazo-de-su-jefe-y-lo-entrego-al-ejercito/202550/))
