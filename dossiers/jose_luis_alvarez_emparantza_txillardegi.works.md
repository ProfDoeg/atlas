## Works by José Luis Álvarez Enparantza

### Novels

- **1957:** *Leturiaren egunkari ezkutua*. Existentialist diary-novel, generally treated as the decisive break with Basque costumbrista fiction and the first modern Basque novel. The title originally appeared as *Leturia-ren egunkari ezkutua*; later normalized spellings are not separate works. [Euskaltzaindia bibliography](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1960:** *Peru Leartzako*. Existentialist novel about time, alienation and mental collapse; its first edition spelled the title *Peru Leartza’ko*. [EHU, Euskal Literaturaren Hiztegia](https://www.ehu.eus/ehg/literatura/idazleak/?p=55)
- **1969:** *Elsa Scheelen*. Novel set in Brussels, combining an individual existential crisis with contemporary political and religious debates. Some accounts date it 1968, but the Euskaltzaindia bibliography and first-edition record give 1969. [Euskaltzaindia bibliography](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1979:** *Haizeaz bestaldetik*. Self-published lyrical, symbolist and formally experimental novel; its circular construction makes it less a conventional narrative than a prose meditation. [EHU, Euskal Literaturaren Hiztegia](https://www.ehu.eus/ehg/literatura/idazleak/?p=55)
- **1988:** *Exkixu*. Political novel about a young ETA member, commonly situated near socialist realism and criticized for subordinating characterization to ideology. [Auñamendi Eusko Entziklopedia](https://aunamendi.eusko-ikaskuntza.eus/es/alvarez-emparanza-jose-luis/ar-9273/)
- **1999:** *Putzu*. Historical novel set around the Second Carlist War and the emergence of Basque nationalism, supported by extensive documentary research. [EHU, Euskal Literaturaren Hiztegia](https://www.ehu.eus/ehg/literatura/idazleak/?p=55)
- **2005:** *Labartzari agur*. Political and existential novel about a prisoner returning after seventeen years to a home that can no longer be recovered. Its classification remains unsettled: critics have called it a political novel, while its structure returns to Txillardegi’s existential concerns. [EHU, Euskal Literaturaren Hiztegia](https://www.ehu.eus/ehg/literatura/idazleak/?p=55)

### Short fiction

- **1957:** *Orangutanen ugartea*. Short story first published in *Egan* and later incorporated into *Kosmodromo*; the later collection is a new arrangement, not a second creation of the story. [Selected bibliography, entry 6](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1957:** *Taupadak*. Short literary text published in *Euzko-Gogoa*. [Selected bibliography, entry 7](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1958:** *Piarres gaxoaren pasadizuak*. Short story first published in *Egan* and subsequently collected in *Kosmodromo*. [Selected bibliography, entry 9](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1984:** *Kosmodromo*. Collection of short fiction, including previously published stories; it is simultaneously an authored book and a retrospective reconfiguration of separate texts. [Auñamendi Eusko Entziklopedia](https://aunamendi.eusko-ikaskuntza.eus/es/alvarez-emparanza-jose-luis/ar-9273/)

### Essay collections, testimony and historical writing

- **1965:** *Huntaz eta hartaz: hamar saiakera labur*. Collection of ten short essays on existential, religious, ethical and political questions; it helped establish a flexible modern essay prose in Basque. [Selected bibliography, entry 21](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1971:** *Erlijioa hauzipean*. Essay volume placing religion under critical examination. Its bibliographical status is less firmly documented than the author’s principal collections, but it is included in the contemporary bibliography of his writings. [Txillardegi bibliography](https://txillardegi.wordpress.com/bibliografia/)
- **1978:** *Euskal Herritik erdal herrietara*. Self-published assemblage of eighty-five earlier articles. It is an obra as a selected and ordered collection, although most component texts had already appeared independently. [Selected bibliography, entry 85](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1984:** *Euskal kulturaren zapalketa, 1956–1981*. Historical-political essay collection documenting the suppression of Basque culture over the period named in its title. [Auñamendi Eusko Entziklopedia](https://aunamendi.eusko-ikaskuntza.eus/es/alvarez-emparanza-jose-luis/ar-9273/)
- **1985:** *Gertakarien lekuko*. Autobiographical testimony assembled partly from earlier writings; it resists separation into “memoir” and “essay” because it makes a life-history through previously public interventions. [Selected bibliography, abbreviations](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1992:** *Antigua 1900*. Local-history study of Txillardegi’s native Donostia neighbourhood. The Euskaltzaindia bibliography dates the Basque original to 1992 and his Spanish translation to 1993; secondary lists sometimes give 1991 or label the 1993 version as the work’s date. [Selected bibliography, entries 53 and 57](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1994:** *Euskal Herria helburu*. A hybrid of political memoir, intellectual autobiography and essay; “memoir” alone does not describe its programmatic argument about the Basque national project. [Auñamendi Eusko Entziklopedia](https://aunamendi.eusko-ikaskuntza.eus/es/alvarez-emparanza-jose-luis/ar-9273/)
- **1996:** *Lingua navarrorum*. Historical and political essays about Basque language and identity. [Selected bibliography, entry 64](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1997:** *Euskal Herria en el horizonte*. Spanish-language form of *Euskal Herria helburu*; it should not be counted as an wholly independent work, although translation and revision give it a distinct textual manifestation. [Selected bibliography, abbreviations](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **2004:** *Santa Klara, gure uharte ezezaguna*. Illustrated local-history study of Santa Clara Island in Donostia. [Selected bibliography, entry 71](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **2004:** *Euskararen aldeko borrokan: euskalgintza eta euskalaritzako idazlan hautatuak, 1956–1983*. Retrospective selection of writings on Basque-language activism and scholarship; the editorial constellation is new, while the constituent texts are earlier works. [Selected bibliography, entry 72](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **2007:** *Horretaz: zazpi saiakera labur*. Collection of seven short essays and a late companion in form to *Huntaz eta hartaz*. [Txillardegi bibliography](https://txillardegi.wordpress.com/bibliografia/)

### Linguistic and sociolinguistic works

- **1959:** *Batasunaren bidea*. Conference paper proposing a route toward a unified written Basque; later collected under the normalized title *Batasunerako bidea*. It is both scholarly argument and protocol-like intervention because it proposes how language standardization should proceed. [Selected bibliography, entry 4](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1966–1972:** *Hizkuntza eta pentsakera*. Linguistic-philosophical essay first published in shorter form in *Branka* in 1966 and expanded into a book in 1972; these are stages of one developing work rather than unrelated titles. [Selected bibliography, entries 5 and 14](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1970:** *Sustrai bila: zenbait euskal korapilo*. Collection of investigations into disputed problems of Basque language and culture. [Selected bibliography, entry 12](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1974:** *Euskara batua zertan den*. Expository and prescriptive account of Standard Basque. A 1975 printing was merely a reprint; the substantially recognized second edition appeared in 1977, although its title page called itself the third. [Selected bibliography, entries 15 and 20](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1977:** *Oinarri bila: morfosintasian eta fonologian*. Technical study and practical guide to Basque morphosyntax and phonology. [Selected bibliography, entry 22](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1978:** *Euskal gramatika*. Basque grammar intended both as linguistic description and as an instrument for teaching and normalization. [Selected bibliography, entry 25](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1979:** *Fonologiaren matematikuntza*. Technical monograph applying mathematical formalization to phonology. [Selected bibliography, entry 29](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1979:** *Euskal aditz batua*. Standardized Basque verb tables prepared by Txillardegi for Euskaltzaindia. Institutional authorship belongs to Euskaltzaindia, so this resists treatment as his sole-authored work; his specifically documented making was the preparation of the tables. [Selected bibliography, entry 93](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1980:** *Euskal fonologia*. Systematic monograph on Basque phonology. [Selected bibliography, entry 30](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1983:** *Euskal dialektologiaren hastapenak*. Foundational teaching work on Basque dialectology directed by Txillardegi; the revised 1987 edition was jointly directed with Gotzon Aurrekoetxea. It is a collaborative pedagogical construction, not securely a sole-authored monograph. [Selected bibliography, entries 44 and 106](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1983:** *El acento vasco*. Doctoral thesis on Basque accentuation; a summary appeared in the documentation for the 1987 conference on Basque accent. The thesis and the later *Euskal azentuaz* are related manifestations, not safely interchangeable titles. [EHU exhibition catalogue](https://www.ehu.eus/documents/1738121/2349786/TxillardegiKatalogoa2019.pdf/ccdfe1a9-2bce-26ae-60b1-765a843be52a?t=1573737039000)
- **1984:** *Elebidun gizarteen azterketa matematikoa*. Mathematical model for analysing language use in bilingual societies; bibliographies also print the title in the singular as *Elebidun gizartearen azterketa matematikoa*. [Selected bibliography, entry 38](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1984:** *Euskal azentuaz*. Monograph on the Basque accent, derived from Txillardegi’s research and doctoral work. [Selected bibliography, entry 39](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1987:** *Connaissance et utilisation des deux langues en milieu partiellement bilingue*. French-language sociolinguistic research monograph on linguistic knowledge and use in a partly bilingual community. [Selected bibliography, entry 46](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1994:** *Soziolinguistika matematikoa*. Mathematical-sociolinguistic treatise made with Xabier Isasi; it extends and systematizes Txillardegi’s earlier modelling and must be credited as collaborative. [Selected bibliography, entry 60](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **2001:** *Hacia una socio-lingüística matemática*. Spanish-language development of the mathematical-sociolinguistic project. Some sources date it 2002, while the Euskaltzaindia selected bibliography records 2001. [Selected bibliography, entry 70](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)

### Critical editions, direction and revision

- **1971:** *Atsotitzak eta neurtitzak*. Critical edition of Arnauld Oihenart’s proverbs and verse. The underlying writings are Oihenart’s; Txillardegi’s obra is the scholarly edition, not the primary text. [Selected bibliography, entry 13](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1977:** *Diccionario general y técnico = Hiztegi orokor-teknikoa*. Luis Mari Mujika’s two-volume dictionary, published under Txillardegi’s supervision. It resists attribution as a Txillardegi work: his documented role is reviser or supervisor, not author. [Selected bibliography, entry 78](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1982:** *Hizkuntzalaritza hiztegia*. Two-volume linguistic dictionary produced by UZEI and jointly directed by Ana María Toledo and Txillardegi. This is an institutional and collaborative obra rather than an individually authored book. [Selected bibliography, entry 35](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)

### Translations and adaptations

- **1956:** *Aita Gaucherren mixtela*. Basque translation of Alphonse Daudet’s short story *L’Élixir du révérend père Gaucher*, published in *Egan*. The story is Daudet’s; Txillardegi made this Basque textual manifestation. [Selected bibliography, entry 1](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1971:** *Mundu berdearen gauza harrigarriak*. Basque translation of a work by Th. van Leeuwen. [Selected bibliography, entry 45](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1972:** *Zientzien erakaskuntza*. Basque version, made by Txillardegi, of the collective pedagogical text directed by Célestin Freinet. Its authorship is necessarily layered between the original collective and the translator. [Selected bibliography, entry 51](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1972:** *Zenbait aholku gazteentzako*. Basque translation of a work by Célestin Freinet. [Selected bibliography, entry 52](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1972:** *Freinet-teknikak lilitegian*. Basque translation of Madeleine Porquet’s study of Freinet techniques in nursery education. [Selected bibliography, entry 53](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1972:** *Pedagogi-ikastaroa*. Pedagogical translation whose source author and exact original title are not specified in the selected bibliography; no attribution should be invented. [Selected bibliography, entry 54](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1972:** *Berezko irakurpidea*. Basque version of the 1961 pedagogical work *Méthode naturelle de lecture*. [Selected bibliography, entry 55](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1972?:** *Matematika*. Three-volume educational work of uncertain publication date and unspecified original authorship; the bibliography records it among Txillardegi’s translations but marks the date conjecturally. [Selected bibliography, entry 57](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1974:** *Kolonizatuaren ezagugarria*. Basque translation of Albert Memmi’s *Portrait du colonisé*. The argument is Memmi’s; Txillardegi’s made object is its Basque linguistic form. [Selected bibliography, entry 65](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1978:** *Itxaskaria: los euskaros y el mar*. Bilingual publication for which Txillardegi translated the original text into Basque; it is not securely attributable to him as an original authored work. [Selected bibliography, entry 84](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1984:** *Alfredo Bikondoa: artista pintore = artista pintor*. Bilingual art publication whose original text Txillardegi rendered into Basque; his obra here is translation rather than the book’s underlying authorship. [Selected bibliography, entry 109](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)

### Manifestos, protocols and language-making interventions

- **1956:** *Karta idigia: Arantzazun biltzen zeraten guzioi*. Open letter read at Euskaltzaindia’s first post-war congress. It acts as an intervention in the standardization debate and therefore approaches a manifesto rather than a merely descriptive article. [Selected bibliography, entry 3](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1964:** *Baiona’ko Biltzarraren erabakiak*. Resolutions of the Baiona Congress organized by Euskal Idazkaritza, with Txillardegi among the organizers. This is a collective protocol for written Basque, not an individually authored essay, and its institutional authorship must be retained. [Selected bibliography, entry 19](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1966:** *Hizkuntza eta herri-kidetasuna*. Programmatic essay joining language to collective or national belonging; its argumentative function makes it both analysis and political-linguistic manifesto. [Selected bibliography, entry 23](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1967:** *El marxismo-leninismo y los problemas nacionales*. Political thesis against reducing national questions to orthodox Marxism-Leninism. It is an intervention meant to redirect political action, hence manifesto-like rather than neutral description. [Selected bibliography, entry 27](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1968:** *Euskera osotuaren beharra aipatuko dut lehenik…*. Report delivered at Euskaltzaindia’s Arantzazu congress advocating a unified language. It contributed to the project that became Euskara Batua, but the standardized language itself is a collective historical creation and cannot be attributed to Txillardegi alone. [Selected bibliography, entry 31](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1968:** *Del frente español al frente nacional vasco*. Political manifesto-like article published in *Branka*, arguing for a Basque national front. [Selected bibliography, entry 32](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)
- **1969:** *Sí al frente nacional vasco*. Programmatic continuation of the national-front argument; its object is political alignment and action rather than detached exposition. [Selected bibliography, entry 37](https://jakin.eus/show/472bb9c839b85be43e4f06d7f5fd8765fe7f9bcd)

### Periodicals and bodies of writing

- **1966–1971:** *Branka*. Socialist Basque political journal created and directed by Txillardegi in exile, and a vehicle for many of his linguistic and political interventions. It is best treated as a sustained editorial project and collective platform, not as a single-authored text. [Jakin biography](https://jakin.eus/digitalizazioa/txillardegi/biografia)
- **1956–2011:** *Periodical articles, chapters, prologues, reports, interviews and other short writings*. This is a body of work rather than one fixed-title obra: Jakin’s collected digital corpus identifies 1,782 writings by Txillardegi—40 books, 173 book sections, 1,500 articles, 46 translations, eight editorial/directorial/revision works, ten re-editions and five “special” items. Individual enumeration remains unstable because Euskaltzaindia’s 954-entry bibliography ends in 1999 and explicitly says it was not exhaustive, while Jakin’s later corpus runs through 2010–11 and separates original works from re-editions and other people’s writings about him. [Jakin, “Digitalizazioaz eta obraz”](https://jakin.eus/digitalizazioa/txillardegi/digitalizazioaz)
- **1990–2001:** *Bat: Soziolinguistika Aldizkaria*. Sociolinguistics journal directed by Txillardegi for approximately ten years. The journal as a whole is collective and institutional; his made contribution was its intellectual and editorial direction, not sole authorship of its contents. [Auñamendi Eusko Entziklopedia](https://aunamendi.eusko-ikaskuntza.eus/es/alvarez-emparanza-jose-luis/ar-9273/)

### Political and cultural projects

- **Early 1950s:** *Ekin*. Basque nationalist study and activist group co-founded by Txillardegi and other students. It is a collective political project rather than a text or individually authored work. [EHU, Euskal Literaturaren Hiztegia](https://www.ehu.eus/ehg/literatura/idazleak/?p=55)
- **1959:** *Euskadi Ta Askatasuna (ETA)*. Organization co-founded by Txillardegi and others out of Ekin; sources also credit him with proposing its name. It strongly resists classification as his “work”: it was a collective political organization whose later armed history cannot be collapsed into the intentions or responsibility of one founder, who left in 1967. [Auñamendi Eusko Entziklopedia](https://aunamendi.eusko-ikaskuntza.eus/es/alvarez-emparanza-jose-luis/ar-9273/)
- **1963–1964:** *Euskal Idazkaritza Elkartea and the Baiona standardization project*. Cultural association co-founded in Baiona, with Txillardegi responsible for language matters; its congress formulated practical orthographic and morphological agreements that anticipated Euskara Batua. The project was collective and its resolutions—not the association alone—are the surviving protocol. [Jakin biography](https://jakin.eus/digitalizazioa/txillardegi/biografia)
- **1960s–1968:** *Euskara Batua proposals*. A sustained project of arguments, draft norms and congress interventions advocating unified written Basque. Euskara Batua itself is not properly “a work by Txillardegi”: it is a collective standard adopted and developed by Euskaltzaindia, although scholarship describes him as one of its principal initiators. [Jean-Louis Davant, “Txillardegi, batuaren aita Baionan”](https://www.euskaltzaindia.eus/dok/plazaberri/2013/urtarrila/txillardegi_davant.pdf)
- **1976:** *Euskal Sozialista Biltzarrea (ESB)*. Political party co-founded with Iñaki Aldekoa after exile. It is a collective political project rather than a text, and Txillardegi’s subsequent expulsion prevents treating its full later existence as his continuing creation. [Auñamendi Eusko Entziklopedia](https://aunamendi.eusko-ikaskuntza.eus/es/alvarez-emparanza-jose-luis/ar-9273/)
- **1979:** *Euskal Herrian Euskaraz*. Basque-language activist movement of which Txillardegi was one of the founders. Its practices and later history are collective manifestations, not his individually authored obra. [Auñamendi Eusko Entziklopedia](https://aunamendi.eusko-ikaskuntza.eus/es/alvarez-emparanza-jose-luis/ar-9273/)
