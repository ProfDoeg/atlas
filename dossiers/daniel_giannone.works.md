## Works by Daniel Giannone

### Collaborative obra: Chiachio & Giannone

- **2003–present:** *Chiachio & Giannone*. Daniel Giannone and Leo Chiachio describe themselves as a single collaborative artist, so the works below are jointly authored and cannot responsibly be divided into separate individual contributions. Their official chronology begins in 2003; the available catalogues do not establish a complete pre-2003 catalogue for Giannone alone. [Artists’ official site](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/); [Fundación Konex](https://www.fundacionkonex.org/b4441-daniel-giannone)

- **2010–2012:** *Ekekos*. A porcelain triptych with hand-crocheted hats, apparently developed from the 2010 *Ekeko* project; whether the earlier catalogue entries are preparatory states or the same three objects is unresolved. [Official catalogue](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2014-12/)

- **2010–2012:** *Guirnalda Ekeko*. An approximately 25-metre installation assembled from decorated, factory-rejected porcelain components, Ekeko heads, and bronze hooks. [Official catalogue](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2014-12/)

- **2010–2016:** *Osito*. A porcelain figure with leather-and-steel harness. The unusually long date range suggests an object revised or completed over time. [Official catalogue](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2016-2/)

- *Cortinas homenaje*. An undated series of sublimation-printed curtains made for *Arqueología suave*. The exhibition reportedly contained roughly sixty pieces overall, but the catalogue does not enumerate these curtains individually. [Official catalogue](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2017-2/); [contemporary review](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/portfolio-item/merceria-de-ficcion-radar/)

- *Cortina Marrón*. An undated hand-embroidered curtain, catalogued within the 2017 *Arqueología suave* ensemble. [Official catalogue](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2017-2/)

- *Jarrones modernos*. An undated series of hand-decorated ceramic vases. Three catalogue images do not prove that the complete series contains exactly three objects. [Official catalogue](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2017-2/)

- *Autorretrato abstracto*. An undated installation or object made from wooden shelves covered with fabric from the artists’ own shirts. Repeated views do not establish whether there is one configuration or multiple versions. [Official catalogue](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2017-2/)

- **2016:** *Piolin’s Time*. A Swatch watch designed by Chiachio & Giannone in a numbered edition of 2,525. It is an editioned design object rather than a unique art object. [Official project archive](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/proyectos/page/2/)

- **2022:** *El amigo Roy*. A screen-printed textile applied to a denim shirt and shown with an aluminium ladder; garment, sculpture, and installation at once. [Official catalogue](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2022-2/)

### Installations, manifestations, performances, film, and Gesamtkunstwerke

- **2005–2007:** *MUPI — Museo Piolín*. A participatory, affective museum-project devoted to the artists’ dog Piolín, first manifested at MACRO in 2005 and presented in a second edition at Centro Cultural Recoleta in 2007. It resists being called a single work: it is simultaneously project, collection, exhibition architecture, and framework for contributions by other artists. [Official project archive](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/proyectos/page/2/)

- **2008:** *¿Quién puede vivir en esta casa?* The duo’s contribution to Lucrecia Urbano’s nine-artist house project; the accessible project record names the manifestation but does not isolate or title their intervention, so it cannot safely be catalogued as a discrete object. [Official project archive](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/proyectos/page/2/)

- **2010:** *Ekeko*. A factory-residency project at Verbano–Faiart in which the porcelain works were developed. Here the project—the intention, research, and industrial encounter—is larger than any one surviving ceramic manifestation. [Official project archive](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/proyectos/page/2/)

- **2013–2019:** *La Famille dans la joyeuse verdure / Familia en el alegre verdor*. A project moving through distinct manifestations: a 2013 design, an Aubusson tapestry commission, and the monumental embroidered textile catalogued in 2019. Sources vary between French and Spanish titles and between 2013–2015 and 2019 dating, reflecting stages rather than necessarily competing dates for one unchanged object. [Official 2013 design](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2014-12/); [project account](https://www.mrxstitch.com/chiachio-giannone/); [2019 catalogue](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2019-2/)

- **2015:** *Chaya o bendición del Friday Late*. A performance at the Victoria and Albert Museum, made for *Journeys from the South*. It is an event rather than a durable object. [Official project archive](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/proyectos/page/2/)

- **2015:** *Selva refugio*. A collective drawing performance at the Victoria and Albert Museum; the participation protocol and event are more securely documented than any finished autonomous drawing. [Official project archive](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/proyectos/page/2/)

- **2017:** *Arqueología suave*. An exhibition-scale assemblage of printed curtains, embroidery, ceramics, textile mosaics, screens, and altered domestic forms. It is best treated as a Gesamtkunstwerk or curatorial installation rather than an additional object duplicating every component displayed within it. [Official catalogue](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2017-2/); [contemporary review](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/portfolio-item/merceria-de-ficcion-radar/)

- **2018–2019:** *Celebrar la diversidad*. A work-in-progress and public protocol in which participants contributed to textile mosaics and pride flags, first presented at the CCK and subsequently at MOLAA. Authorship is shared with participating communities, and its changing manifestations resist a closed object list. [MOLAA project page](https://molaa.org/cds-chiachio-giannone); [artists’ video archive](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/videos/)

- **2021:** *CUIR*. A group-exhibition project conceived and curated by Chiachio & Giannone around queer identity, family, eroticism, and generational experience. It is a curatorial proposition involving twenty artists, not an artwork made solely by Giannone. [Artists’ project statement](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2021/07/26/cuir/)

- **2022:** *Fierté*. An installation assembling the four altered books *Fierté #1–#4*; the catalogue documents an installation view but gives no independent materials or dimensions for the whole. [Official catalogue](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2022-2/)

- **2022:** *Fiesta*. A Gesamtkunstwerk comprising prints and paintings on paper, dyed and sublimation-printed fabrics, lights, fans, installation design, and Super-8 film. Its variable installation cannot be reduced to any one component. [Official catalogue](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2022-2/)

- **2022:** *Fiesta // El baile*. An installation of sublimation-printed fabrics, lights, and fans, functioning as a spatial and kinetic component of *Fiesta*. [Official catalogue](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2022-2/)

- **2022:** *Fiesta // La película*. A thirteen-minute Super-8/HD film jointly directed by Agustina Comedi and Chiachio & Giannone. Its authorship therefore belongs to three directing parties, not to Giannone alone. [Official catalogue](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2022-2/)

- **2022:** *Hope Will Never Be Silent*. An exhibition made with “Chiachio & Giannone & Family,” incorporating the duo’s objects, installations, recovered textiles, and family/community logic. It is a manifestation or exhibition-project rather than one securely bounded artwork. [Exhibition catalogue](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Hope-will-never-be-silent-catalogo-expo-Transpalette-CHG-2022-.pdf); [artists’ video archive](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/videos/)

- **2023:** *Comechiffones*. An exhibition and continuing series drawing together family portraiture, reused domestic fabrics, and Comechingón ancestry. The exhibition is a manifestation of separately catalogued works, not itself necessarily an additional saleable object. [Official catalogue](https://www.chiachiogiannone.com/Web/2023-2/)
