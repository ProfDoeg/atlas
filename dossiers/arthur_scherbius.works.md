## Works by Arthur Scherbius

### Technical writings

- **1904:** *Vorschläge zum Bau eines indirekt wirkenden Wasser-Turbinen-Reglers*. Doctoral dissertation and engineering proposal for an indirectly acting water-turbine governor, submitted at the Technische Hochschule Hannover. ([Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz111694.html))
- **1910:** *Neues System regelbarer Drehstrommotoren und ein Vergleich desselben mit bisher angewandten Systemen zum Regeln der Umlaufzahl elektrischer Motoren*. Technical article in *Elektrische Bahnen* 8, pp. 101–131, describing and comparing his system of variable-speed three-phase motors. ([Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz111694.html))
- **1919:** *Die magnetische Induktion in geschlossenen Spulen: Eine grundsätzliche Betrachtung über die physikalischen und technischen Möglichkeiten und Grenzen der Periodenumformung in Transformatoren und kollektorlosen Maschinen der Nieder- und Hochfrequenztechnik*. A technical monograph on magnetic induction and frequency conversion rather than a general book of electrical engineering. ([Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz111694.html))
- **1923:** *Das Scherip-Heizkissen, seine Konstruktion, Fabrikation und Prüfung*. Technical article in *Der elektrische Betrieb* 21, pp. 263–265; simultaneously a description of a manufactured object and a production-and-testing protocol. ([Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz111694.html))
- **1923:** *Gesichtspunkt für den Vergleich von Energieübertragung mit Gleichstrom und Wechselstrom*. Technical article in *Elektrotechnische Zeitschrift* 44, p. 657, comparing direct- and alternating-current transmission. ([Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz111694.html))
- **1923:** *Radiotelegraphie und Geheimschrift*. Technical and promotional article in *Zeitschrift für Fernmeldetechnik, Werk- und Gerätebau* 4, pp. 70–74, presenting machine encryption as an answer to the insecurity of radio telegrams. ([Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz111694.html); [Taaks](https://ecp.ep.liu.se/index.php/histocrypt/article/download/706/612/723))
- **1923:** *„Enigma“ Chiffriermaschine*. Illustrated technical article in *Elektrotechnische Zeitschrift* 44, pp. 1035–1036. It is a description of the commercial cipher machine, not the machine itself, and also functions as a public manifestation of the name “Enigma.” ([Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz111694.html))

### Electrical-machine inventions and projects

- **1905–1912:** *Scherbius-Schaltung* / *Brown-Boveri–Scherbius system*. An obra of electrical engineering: a cascade of an induction motor and an AC commutator machine that permitted comparatively efficient variable-speed operation. The name denotes a principle embodied in many industrial drives, not one uniquely bounded object. ([Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz111694.html); [US 1,187,180](https://patents.google.com/patent/US1187180A/en))
- **From 1912:** Work on mercury-vapour rectifiers. The biographical record identifies a sustained engineering activity but no securely bounded, titled apparatus; it therefore resists treatment as a single completed work. ([Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz111694.html))
- **1914:** Processes for ceramic electric radiators. Developed with an unnamed established ceramics manufacturer as a wartime substitute for metal radiators; this is a jointly developed manufacturing process rather than one surviving, individually titled object. ([Taaks](https://ecp.ep.liu.se/index.php/histocrypt/article/download/706/612/723))
- **During the First World War:** *Birka-Regler*, Scherbius’s developed form. A small inexpensive thermostat developed from Swedish patents he had purchased; because the underlying principle was acquired, this is an adaptation and development, not an uncontested wholly original invention. ([Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz111694.html))
- **By 1920:** *Scherip-Heizkissen*. A mass-produced, temperature-regulated electric heating pad built around the developed thermostat and presented as an unusually safe domestic appliance; it was a product of the Scherbius–Ritter enterprise rather than a solitary handcrafted object. ([Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz111694.html); [Taaks](https://ecp.ep.liu.se/index.php/histocrypt/article/download/706/612/723))
- **1921:** *Anordnung zur Sicherung von elektrischen Heiz- und Kochapparaten gegen Beschädigung durch zu hohe Temperatur* (DE396400). A safety-switch arrangement for heated appliances, jointly assigned to Scherbius & Ritter and Arthur Lewerenz; the patent record does not justify assigning sole authorship to Scherbius. ([DE396400](https://patents.google.com/patent/DE396400C/en))
- **1918–1929:** High-voltage direct-current transmission machine. An unfinished project for transmitting energy by high-voltage DC; Scherbius died before completing its development, so the project—not a finished machine—is the work. ([Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz111694.html))

### Cipher-machine prototypes, machines, and unrealized projects

- **1917–1918:** First rotor-cipher project. Commissioned by the German War Ministry and realized as a demonstration machine with two wired rotors, a 5×5 keyboard, and a 5×5 lamp field; contemporary documents attribute the project to Scherbius, although the surviving research suggests the prototype was probably constructed in E. Richard Ritter & Co.’s workshop. ([Taaks](https://ecp.ep.liu.se/index.php/histocrypt/article/download/706/612/723))
- **1918:** Two seven-rotor test machines. Experimental electromechanical machines produced after the navy requested interchangeable rotors; their many electrical contacts made them too unreliable for service. They are failed prototypes, and that failure is part of their status as works. ([Taaks](https://ecp.ep.liu.se/index.php/histocrypt/article/download/706/612/723))
- **1918–1920:** Pneumatic or hydraulic cipher machine. A project and patent manifestation proposing tubes carrying air, water, or oil in place of electrical wiring; no such machine is known to have been built in Germany. ([Taaks](https://ecp.ep.liu.se/index.php/histocrypt/article/download/706/612/723); [Crypto Museum patent census](https://www.cryptomuseum.com/crypto/enigma/patents/index.htm))
- **1919:** First *Handelsmaschine*. A four-rotor “writing” cipher-machine design supplemented by Scherbius’s line-by-line transposition or *Umwürfelung*. Its status lies between prototype, process, and commercial project; the military rejected the reduced rotor count as insufficiently secure. ([Taaks](https://ecp.ep.liu.se/index.php/histocrypt/article/download/706/612/723))
- **1920:** *Postmaschine* proposal. A projected printing machine for the Reich Telegraph Administration, intended to deliver its output through a Siemens Wheatstone perforator; the offer and design are documented, but the proposed form should not be confused with later machines carrying the same functional description. ([Taaks](https://ecp.ep.liu.se/index.php/histocrypt/article/download/706/612/723))
- **1920:** “Big machine” prototype. Demonstrated by Scherbius to the Reichswehr and Reichspost on 2 December 1920; a version was reportedly tested by the postal administration, but the documentation does not supply a stable independent model name. ([Taaks](https://ecp.ep.liu.se/index.php/histocrypt/article/download/706/612/723))
- **By 1923:** Commercial *Handelsmaschine*, subsequently advertised as *Enigma*. A manufactured rotor cipher machine made in very small series and tested by German government offices. “Enigma” names an evolving commercial system rather than one immutable object, and Scherbius’s personal role in its later redesigns is not fully resolved. ([Taaks](https://ecp.ep.liu.se/index.php/histocrypt/article/download/706/612/723); [Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz111694.html))
- **By July 1923:** *Diplomatenmaschine*. A printing-machine branch represented by construction drawings and unfinished parts transferred to Chiffriermaschinen AG. It resists attribution as a wholly personal Scherbius work because it belonged to a collaborative corporate development program. ([Taaks](https://ecp.ep.liu.se/index.php/histocrypt/article/download/706/612/723))
- **By July 1923:** *Artilleriemaschine*. A lamp-output cipher-machine variant represented among the machines, parts, and drawings contributed to Chiffriermaschinen AG. Its surviving documentary name describes a military application, while individual design responsibility remains uncertain. ([Taaks](https://ecp.ep.liu.se/index.php/histocrypt/article/download/706/612/723))
- **1923–1929:** Continuing *Enigma* development. Scherbius participated through Scherbius & Ritter and Chiffriermaschinen AG, but the later machine family incorporated work by Paul Bernstein, Willi Korn, Alfred Wallenstein, and other engineers; scholarship explicitly states that Scherbius’s role in these later developments remains incompletely known. Accordingly, the post-1923 Enigmas cannot responsibly be catalogued as his sole works. ([Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz111694.html); [Crypto Museum family tree](https://www.cryptomuseum.com/crypto/enigma/tree.htm); [Taaks](https://ecp.ep.liu.se/index.php/histocrypt/article/download/706/612/723))

### Cipher patent specifications and protocols

- **1918:** *Chiffrierapparat* (DE416219). The foundational patent specification for Scherbius’s electrically wired rotor machine, with keyboard input and lamp output; filed on 23 February 1918 and published only in 1925. ([Crypto Museum patent census](https://www.cryptomuseum.com/crypto/enigma/patents/index.htm))
- **1918:** *DE416833*. Supplementary patent specification replacing electrical connections with fluid- or air-carrying tubes; it is a protocol for a possible machine, not evidence that the machine was built. ([Crypto Museum patent census](https://www.cryptomuseum.com/crypto/enigma/patents/index.htm))
- **1920:** *DE387893*. Patent specification by Scherbius and Ritter for a multiple-alphabet cipher machine using a shift character to alternate among letters, capitals, and numbers. ([Crypto Museum patent census](https://www.cryptomuseum.com/crypto/enigma/patents/index.htm))
- **1920:** *DE425147*. Patent specification for irregular movement of several cipher discs and for pneumatic line-by-line transposition; it preserves an unrealized mechanical protocol as much as a machine design. ([Crypto Museum patent census](https://www.cryptomuseum.com/crypto/enigma/patents/index.htm); [Taaks](https://ecp.ep.liu.se/index.php/histocrypt/article/download/706/612/723))
- **1921:** *DE378238*. Patent specification by Scherbius for converting numbers into letters and letters into numbers, with lamp-panel output. The near-identical Dutch filing claimed priority one day earlier, making the international filing history unusually tangled. ([Crypto Museum patent census](https://www.cryptomuseum.com/crypto/enigma/patents/index.htm))
- **1921:** *DE409301*. Patent specification for reducing the number of contacts through shift keys and coupling electrical coding wheels to a drum printer. It was filed institutionally by Securitas Berlin; Scherbius had transferred ten granted or pending patents to that organization, but the catalogue warns that ownership and inventor attribution within these related filings can be opaque. ([Crypto Museum patent census](https://www.cryptomuseum.com/crypto/enigma/patents/index.htm); [Taaks](https://ecp.ep.liu.se/index.php/histocrypt/article/download/706/612/723))
- **1922:** *DE383594*. Patent specification for a cipher machine with electrical coding wheels, filed by the Amsterdam Securitas company in several national versions. Its place in the Scherbius patent portfolio is documented, but the corporate filing does not by itself establish exclusive personal authorship. ([Crypto Museum patent census](https://www.cryptomuseum.com/crypto/enigma/patents/index.htm))
- **1922:** *DE385682*. Patent specification applying multiswitches to the selection of substitution alphabets in a typewriter-style machine; Taaks identifies it as the electromechanical publication of the previously guarded transposition process. ([Crypto Museum patent census](https://www.cryptomuseum.com/crypto/enigma/patents/index.htm); [Taaks](https://ecp.ep.liu.se/index.php/histocrypt/article/download/706/612/723))
- **1923:** *DE400795*. Patent specification for two separate printers, one recording plaintext and the other ciphertext, filed by Securitas Amsterdam; personal authorship within the corporate development group is not securely itemized by the patent census. ([Crypto Museum patent census](https://www.cryptomuseum.com/crypto/enigma/patents/index.htm))
- **1923:** *DE411126*. Patent specification for index or letter rings on cipher wheels and turnover components, allowing a message key to be set. It belongs to the Securitas portfolio, but sole Scherbius authorship is not established. ([Crypto Museum patent census](https://www.cryptomuseum.com/crypto/enigma/patents/index.htm))
- **1923:** *DE408949*. Patent specification for a brake mechanism for rotating printing wheels, filed by Scherbius & Ritter; it is a jointly institutional work rather than an securely separable personal invention. ([Crypto Museum patent census](https://www.cryptomuseum.com/crypto/enigma/patents/index.htm))
- **1929:** *DE541702*. Patent specification by Arthur Scherbius and Willi Korn for using electromagnets to actuate printing in a typewriter or cipher machine; it is explicitly a co-invention. ([Crypto Museum patent census](https://www.cryptomuseum.com/crypto/enigma/patents/index.htm))

### Organizational creations

- **1920:** *Scherbius & Ritter*. Development and manufacturing firm founded with E. Richard Ritter to develop cipher machines, thermostatic controllers, and heating pads. A company is not a conventional “work,” but here it was an apparatus for making and therefore part of Scherbius’s obra. ([Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz111694.html); [Taaks](https://ecp.ep.liu.se/index.php/histocrypt/article/download/706/612/723))
- **1923:** *Chiffriermaschinen Aktiengesellschaft*. Manufacturing and sales company formed around the Securitas patent and prototype portfolio. Scherbius belonged to its “inventors’ group,” but the corporation was assembled with financiers and other engineers and cannot be treated as his sole creation. ([Taaks](https://ecp.ep.liu.se/index.php/histocrypt/article/download/706/612/723); [Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz111694.html))

- United States Patent Office, [US1187180A, *Means for Improving the Power Factor of Induction-Machines*](https://patents.google.com/patent/US1187180A/en). Primary patent-family record for Scherbius’s induction-machine work.
