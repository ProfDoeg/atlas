## Works by Egeria

### Epistolary travel account — obra

- **c. 381–384 (journey; date of composition uncertain):** *Itinerarium Egeriae* (also transmitted and edited as *Peregrinatio Aetheriae* and *Peregrinatio ad loca sancta*). A Latin account addressed to the writer’s “sisters,” combining pilgrimage narrative with detailed observation of Jerusalem’s liturgical year; these are sections of one epistolary obra, not securely separate works. Its original title is unknown, while the conventional title and the attribution to Egeria are scholarly reconstructions: the sole substantially surviving manuscript does not name its author, and identification depends chiefly on Valerius of Bierzo’s later letter. The work is materially incomplete—its beginning and ending and two internal leaves are lost—and passages preserved indirectly by Peter the Deacon and in fragmentary witnesses do not constitute additional works. The journey’s dating to 381–384 is widely accepted but remains an inference rather than an explicit date in the text; later dates have also been argued. ([BIBLINDEX](https://dapytains.biblindex.org/en/work-edition/497); [Sources Chrétiennes](https://www.editionsducerf.fr/librairie/sc-296-journal-de-voyage/); [Usuarium edition record and contents](https://usuarium.elte.hu/book/7835/view); [Bryn Mawr Classical Review](https://bmcr.brynmawr.edu/2021/2021.11.48/); [Oxford research on the manuscript transmission](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid%3A82e0de0f-5f43-4502-a2e2-70726d6a6d53/files/dg158bj21b))
