## Works by Pierre-Simon Laplace

### Synthetic treatises and independent books

- **1784:** *Théorie du mouvement et de la figure elliptique des planètes*. Laplace’s first separately published treatise, synthesizing earlier investigations of planetary motion, attraction, and cometary orbits; the later *Œuvres complètes* omitted it. [Gillispie and Hahn, bibliography, pp. 96–97](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=97)
- **1796:** *Exposition du système du monde*. A two-volume, largely nonmathematical synthesis of astronomy and cosmology, subsequently revised in 1799, 1808, 1813, and 1824. Laplace was correcting a sixth edition when he died; the posthumous 1835 text restored material from the fourth edition, so it is not simply a final authorially completed state. [Gillispie and Hahn, bibliography, pp. 85–86](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=86)
- **1799–1825:** *Traité de mécanique céleste*. A five-volume composite obra—simultaneously treatise, textbook, research archive, reference work, and applied astronomical manual—issued as volumes I–II in 1799, III in 1802, IV in 1805, and V in 1823–1825. A supplement discovered after Laplace’s death was presented in 1827, making the boundary of the completed work slightly unstable. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 57, 85–86, 95](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=58)
- **1806:** *Théorie de l’action capillaire*. A separately issued physical treatise later absorbed into volume IV of *Mécanique céleste* as its first supplement to Book X; it is therefore both an independent obra and a component of a larger one. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 93, 99](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=94)
- **1807:** *Supplément à la théorie de l’action capillaire*. A separately printed continuation, sometimes bound with the 1806 treatise and later incorporated as the second supplement to Book X of *Mécanique céleste*. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 93, 99](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=94)
- **1808:** *Supplément au Traité de mécanique céleste*. An appendix on planetary perturbations, first issued separately and then incorporated into later printings of volume III; its identity lies between independent text and replacement component. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 93–94](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=94)
- **1812–1825:** *Théorie analytique des probabilités*. A technical probability treatise issued in 1812, revised in 1814 and 1820, and extended through four supplements dated 1816, 1818, 1820, and 1825; the first edition itself appeared in at least two installments. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 86, 94–95](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=87)
- **1814–1825:** *Essai philosophique sur les probabilités*. A conceptual and philosophical exposition first printed independently and then used as the introduction to the second edition of the *Théorie analytique*; five authorial editions appeared through 1825, so it is both an autonomous obra and the threshold of another. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 74–75, 86](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=75)
- **1821:** *Précis de l’histoire de l’astronomie*. A separately issued manifestation of Book V of the fourth edition of *Exposition du système du monde*, not a newly composed book despite its independent title page. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 102](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=103)

### Mathematical and probabilistic memoirs

- **1771:** *Disquisitiones de calculo integrale*. A Latin mathematical memoir derived from a paper presented in January 1771; it was omitted from the *Œuvres complètes*. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 95–96](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=96)
- **probably 1771:** *Recherches sur le calcul intégral aux différences infiniment petites, et aux différences finies*. A substantial memoir whose periodical bears no publication date; the scholarly bibliography judges 1771 probable, and the *Œuvres complètes* omitted it. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 95–96](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=96)
- **1774:** *Disquisitiones de maximis et minimis fluentium indefinitarum*. A Latin memoir on maxima and minima, developed from Laplace’s earliest recorded Academy submission of 1770 and omitted from the collected works. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 96](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=97)
- **1774:** *Mémoire sur les suites récurro-récurrentes et sur leurs usages dans la théorie des hasards*. A mathematical-probabilistic memoir on recurrent sequences and games of chance. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 96](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=97)
- **1774:** *Mémoire sur la probabilité des causes par les événements*. A foundational inverse-probability memoir; its exact composition is undocumented but is placed in 1773–1774 by the scholarly bibliography. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 96](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=97)
- **1775:** *Mémoire sur les solutions particulières des équations différentielles et sur les inégalités séculaires des planètes*. A combined mathematical and celestial-mechanical memoir assembled from sections presented at different times between 1772 and 1774. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 96](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=97)
- **1775:** *Addition au mémoire sur les solutions particulières des équations différentielles et sur les inégalités séculaires des planètes*. A short continuation printed with the preceding investigation. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 96](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=97)
- **1776:** *Recherches, 1°, sur l’intégration des équations différentielles aux différences finies, et sur leur usage dans la théorie des hasards; 2°, sur le principe de la gravitation universelle, et sur les inégalités séculaires des planètes qui en dépendent*. One printed memoir containing two differently directed investigations; the collected edition split it into two texts, so its work-boundary is editorially disputed. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 96](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=97)
- **1776:** *Mémoire sur l’inclinaison moyenne des orbites des comètes, sur la figure de la Terre, et sur les fonctions*. A mixed astronomical and mathematical memoir whose presentation date is unknown. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 96](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=97)
- **1776:** *Recherches sur le calcul intégral et sur le système du monde*. A long memoir joining analysis to celestial mechanics. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 96](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=97)
- **1777:** *Additions aux recherches sur le calcul intégral et sur le système du monde*. A continuation reportedly read in 1773 but not submitted for publication until December 1776. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 96](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=97)
- **1780:** *Mémoire sur l’intégration des équations différentielles par approximation*. An analytical memoir tentatively identified by the bibliographers with a paper read in February 1778. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 96](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=97)
- **1780:** *Mémoire sur l’usage du calcul aux différences partielles dans la théorie des suites*. A memoir on partial-difference methods in sequence theory. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 96](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=97)
- **1781:** *Mémoire sur les probabilités*. A large memoir developed from a 1780 paper on applying sequence calculus to probability. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 96–97](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=97)
- **1782:** *Mémoire sur les suites*. A mathematical memoir with no corresponding presentation recorded in the Academy minutes. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 96–97](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=97)
- **1785:** *Mémoire sur les approximations des formules qui sont fonctions de très grands nombres*. A mathematical-probabilistic memoir on large-number approximations. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 96–97](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=97)
- **1786:** *Mémoire sur les approximations des formules qui sont fonctions de très grands nombres (Suite)*. A continuation based on drafts read in June 1785. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 97](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=98)
- **1798:** *Mémoire sur la détermination d’un plan qui reste toujours parallèle à lui-même, dans le mouvement d’un système de corps agissant d’une manière quelconque les uns sur les autres et libres de toute action étrangère*. A short mechanics memoir defining an invariant plane for an interacting system. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 98](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=99)
- **1799:** *Sur la mécanique*. A very short mathematical-mechanical note rather than a treatise. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 98](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=99)
- **1800:** *Leçons de mathématiques professées à l’École normale en 1795*. The printed manifestation of Laplace’s 1795 lectures: a teaching sequence and protocol of mathematical instruction, later reprinted in the *Œuvres complètes*. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 98](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=99)
- **1809:** *Mémoire sur divers points d’analyse*. A mathematical memoir collecting several analytical investigations. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 99](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=100)
- **1810:** *Mémoire sur les approximations des formules qui sont fonctions de très grands nombres, et sur leurs applications aux probabilités*. A further large-number and probability memoir. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 99–100](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=100)
- **1810:** *Supplément au mémoire sur les approximations des formules qui sont fonctions de très grands nombres*. A short extension of the preceding memoir. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 99–100](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=100)
- **1810:** *Notice sur les probabilités*. A public-facing synopsis of probability; it anticipates material later developed in the *Essai philosophique* but was not included in the collected works. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 100](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=101)
- **1811:** *Mémoire sur les intégrales définies, et leur application aux probabilités, et spécialement à la recherche du milieu qu’il faut choisir entre les résultats des observations*. A major memoir joining definite integrals, probability, and statistical estimation. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 100](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=101)
- **1811:** *Du milieu qu’il faut choisir entre les résultats d’un grand nombre d’observations*. A shorter manifestation of Laplace’s work on estimation from repeated observations. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 100](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=101)
- **1815:** *Sur l’application du calcul des probabilités à la philosophie naturelle*. A memoir applying probability to natural philosophy and bounds of observational error. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 100](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=101)
- **1818:** *Application du calcul des probabilités aux opérations géodésiques*. A geodetic probability memoir later incorporated, with additions, as the second supplement to the third edition of the *Théorie analytique*. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 100–101](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=101)
- **1819:** *Mémoire sur l’application du calcul des probabilités aux observations et spécialement aux opérations du nivellement*. A journal abstract of work that became the third supplement to the *Théorie analytique*; it is a distinct publication but not an independent research project. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 101](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=102)
- **1819:** *Application du calcul des probabilités aux opérations géodésiques de la méridienne de France*. Another concise manifestation of the same geodetic project, later incorporated into the third supplement. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 101](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=102)

### Astronomy, geodesy, and celestial-mechanical memoirs

- **1778:** *Recherches sur plusieurs points du système du monde*. The first installment of a sustained investigation of tides, fluid oscillations, and terrestrial or planetary motion, formed by combining and revising earlier submissions. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 96](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=97)
- **1779:** *Recherches sur plusieurs points du système du monde (Suite)*. The first published continuation, with article numbering continuous from the 1778 memoir. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 96](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=97)
- **1779:** *Recherches sur plusieurs points du système du monde (Suite)*. A second continuation, including an investigation of wave motion; identical titling makes it resist bibliographic individuation without its publication context. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 96](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=97)
- **1780:** *Mémoire sur la précession des équinoxes*. An astronomical memoir read after an “addition” to it had already been presented and subsequently defended against objections. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 96](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=97)
- **1784:** *Mémoire sur la détermination des orbites des comètes*. A protocol as well as a memoir: its final article gives instructions for applying Laplace’s orbital method and derives from material communicated earlier to Pingré. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 90, 97](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=91)
- **1785:** *Théorie des attractions des sphéroïdes et de la figure des planètes*. A substantial memoir on attraction and planetary form. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 97](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=98)
- **1786:** *Mémoire sur la figure de la Terre*. A geodetic memoir whose presentation is not recorded in the Academy minutes. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 97](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=98)
- **1787:** *Mémoire sur les inégalités séculaires des planètes et des satellites*. A celestial-mechanical memoir on the long-term variations of planetary and satellite systems. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 97](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=98)
- **1788:** *Théorie de Jupiter et de Saturne*. The principal memoir explaining the planets’ great inequality. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 97](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=98)
- **1788:** *Théorie de Jupiter et de Saturne (Suite)*. A continuation that also constitutes Part III of the preceding memoir, making its status both component and separately published manifestation. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 97](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=98)
- **1788:** *Sur l’équation séculaire de la Lune*. A memoir on the Moon’s apparent secular acceleration, revised after its initial presentation. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 97](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=98)
- **1789:** *Mémoire sur la théorie de l’anneau de Saturne*. A celestial-mechanical memoir on Saturn’s ring. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 97](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=98)
- **1789:** *Mémoire sur les variations séculaires des orbites des planètes*. A short general proof concerning the periodic character of orbital eccentricities and inclinations. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 44, 97](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=45)
- **1790:** *Sur la théorie des satellites de Jupiter*. A preliminary periodical text omitted from the collected works. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 97](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=98)
- **1791:** *Théorie des satellites de Jupiter*. A full memoir on the Jovian satellite system. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 97](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=98)
- **1793:** *Sur quelques points du système du monde*. A multipart memoir, probably incorporating a 1789 paper on the inclination of the ecliptic. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 97](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=98)
- **1793:** *Théorie des satellites de Jupiter (Suite)*. A continuation not recorded in the Academy minutes. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 97](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=98)
- **1797:** *Mémoire sur le flux et le reflux de la mer*. A large tidal memoir begun before the Academy in December 1790. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 98](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=99)
- **1797:** *Sur le mouvement de l’apogée de la Lune et sur celui de ses nœuds*. A short preliminary notice, absent from the collected works. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 98](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=99)
- **1797:** *Sur les équations séculaires du mouvement de la Lune*. A short notice of Laplace’s developing lunar theory, also absent from the collected works. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 98](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=99)
- **1798:** *Mémoire sur les mouvements des corps célestes autour de leurs centres de gravité*. A memoir on celestial bodies’ rotational dynamics. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 98](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=99)
- **1798:** *Sur les équations séculaires des mouvements de l’apogée et des nœuds de l’orbite lunaire*. A preliminary published statement whose calculations were deferred to the 1799 memoir. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 98](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=99)
- **1798:** *Sur les plus grandes marées de l’an IX*. A practical prediction of exceptional tides, joining theoretical work to an almanac function. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 98](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=99)
- **1799:** *Mémoire sur les équations séculaires des mouvements de la Lune, de son apogée et de ses nœuds*. The full calculation promised by the 1798 notice. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 98](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=99)
- **1799:** *Proof concerning an invisible massive luminous body* [no fixed French title recorded]. A German-language journal manifestation of a proof supplied to F. X. von Zach, elaborating the “dark body” passage of the first *Exposition*; Laplace removed that passage from later editions. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 98](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=99)
- **1799:** *Sur quelques équations des tables lunaires*. A concise note on lunar-table equations. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 98](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=99)
- **1800:** *Sur l’orbite du dernier satellite de Saturne*. A one-page astronomical notice omitted from the collected works. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 98](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=99)
- **1800:** *Sur les mouvements des orbites des satellites de Saturne et d’Uranus*. A preliminary abstract of the full memoir published in 1801. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 98–99](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=99)
- **1800:** *Sur la théorie de la Lune*. An abstract announcing a periodic inequality in the nutation of the lunar orbit. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 98–99](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=99)
- **1801:** *Mémoire sur les mouvements des orbites des satellites de Saturne et d’Uranus*. The full memoir corresponding to the 1800 abstract. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 98–99](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=99)
- **1801:** *Mémoire sur la théorie de la Lune*. A full lunar-theory memoir. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 98–99](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=99)
- **1801:** *Sur la théorie de la Lune*. A separate almanac abstract concerning an inequality in lunar parallax; it was not included in the collected works. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 99](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=100)
- **1803:** *Sur les marées*. A brief notice based on observations presented to the Institut, omitted from the collected works. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 93, 99](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=94)
- **1804:** *Sur les tables de Jupiter et sur la masse de Saturne*. An astronomical note linking revised tables to an estimate of Saturn’s mass. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 99](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=100)
- **1804:** *Sur la théorie de Jupiter et de Saturne*. A later note revisiting the theory of the two planets. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 99](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=100)
- **1809:** *Sur l’anneau de Saturne*. A short almanac memoir. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 99](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=100)
- **1809:** *Mémoire sur la diminution de l’obliquité de l’écliptique qui résulte des observations anciennes*. An astronomical memoir extracting secular change from ancient observations. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 99](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=100)
- **1811:** *Sur l’inégalité à longue période du mouvement lunaire*. The first published portion of a short two-part lunar investigation. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 100](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=101)
- **1812:** *Sur l’inégalité à longue période du mouvement lunaire*. The continuation of the 1811 paper, published under the same title. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 100](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=101)
- **1813:** *Sur les comètes*. An almanac memoir on cometary astronomy. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 100](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=101)
- **1815:** *Sur le flux et reflux de la mer*. A preliminary abstract of the expanded 1820 tidal memoir; an older catalogue wrongly identified it with another text. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 100](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=101)
- **1817:** *Sur la longueur du pendule à secondes*. A full geodetic-physical text, distinct from the shorter 1816 excerpt with which an older bibliography confused it. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 100](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=101)
- **1817:** *Addition au mémoire précédent sur la longueur du pendule à secondes*. A short correction or extension to the pendulum memoir. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 100](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=101)
- **1818:** *Sur la figure de la Terre, et la loi de la pesanteur à sa surface*. An abstract of the fuller memoir published in 1819. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 100–101](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=101)
- **1819:** *Sur la rotation de la Terre*. An almanac memoir on terrestrial rotation. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 100–101](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=101)
- **1819:** *Mémoire sur la figure de la Terre*. The full geodetic memoir behind the 1818 abstract. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 101](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=102)
- **1819:** *Sur l’influence de la grande inégalité de Jupiter et de Saturne dans le mouvement des corps du système solaire*. A short celestial-mechanical memoir. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 101](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=102)
- **1819:** *Sur la loi de la pesanteur, en supposant le sphéroïde terrestre homogène et de même densité que la mer*. A theoretical geodesy memoir. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 101](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=102)
- **1819:** *Addition au mémoire précédent*. A brief addition to the preceding gravitation memoir whose title depends on its serial placement. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 101](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=102)
- **1820:** *Sur les inégalités lunaires dues à l’aplatissement de la Terre*. A lunar-theory memoir connecting orbital inequalities to Earth’s shape. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 101](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=102)
- **1820:** *Sur le perfectionnement de la théorie et des tables lunaires*. A methodological and practical proposal for improving lunar theory and tables. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 101](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=102)
- **1820:** *Sur l’inégalité lunaire à longue période dépendante de la différence des deux hémisphères terrestres*. A memoir relating a lunar inequality to terrestrial hemispheric asymmetry. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 101](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=102)
- **1820:** *Mémoire sur le flux et le reflux de la mer*. An expanded tidal memoir developed from the shorter 1815 manifestation. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 101](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=102)
- **1820:** *Addition au mémoire sur la figure de la Terre, inséré dans le volume précédent*. A continuation of the 1819 geodetic memoir, also printed separately under the simpler title *Sur la figure de la Terre*. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 101](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=102)
- **1820:** *Mémoire sur la diminution de la durée du jour par le refroidissement de la Terre*. A memoir later incorporated into Book XI of *Mécanique céleste*, hence both article and component of the treatise. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 101](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=102)
- **1820:** *Addition au mémoire précédent sur la diminution de la durée du jour*. A continuation likewise absorbed into Book XI of *Mécanique céleste*. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 101](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=102)
- **1820:** *Sur la densité moyenne de la Terre*. A short geophysical memoir. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 101](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=102)
- **1820:** *Éclaircissements sur les mémoires précédents, relatifs aux inégalités lunaires dépendantes de la figure de la Terre, et au perfectionnement de la théorie et des tables de la Lune*. A clarification whose identity is explicitly relational to the preceding memoirs. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 101](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=102)
- **1821:** *Sur les variations des éléments du mouvement elliptique, et sur les inégalités lunaires à longues périodes*. A celestial-mechanical memoir. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 101–102](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=102)
- **1821:** *Sur la détermination des orbites des comètes*. A renewed statement of Laplace’s cometary-orbit method, including Bouvard’s worked application to the comet of 1805. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 101–102](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=102)
- **1823:** *De l’action de la Lune sur l’atmosphère*. An atmospheric-tide memoir incorporated into Book XIII of *Mécanique céleste*. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 102](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=103)
- **1824:** *Sur les variations de l’obliquité de l’écliptique et de la précession des équinoxes*. A late celestial-mechanical note. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 102](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=103)
- **1825:** *Sur le développement en série du radical qui exprime la distance mutuelle de deux planètes et sur le développement du rayon vecteur elliptique*. A technical celestial-mechanical memoir on series expansions. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 102](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=103)
- **1826:** *Mémoire sur deux grandes inégalités de Jupiter et de Saturne*. A late memoir revisiting long-period perturbations of the two planets. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 102](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=103)
- **1826:** *Mémoire sur divers points de mécanique céleste*. A three-part memoir on Saturn’s outermost satellite, a long-period inequality of Mercury, and stellar action on the planetary system. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 102](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=103)
- **1827:** *Mémoire sur le développement de l’anomalie vraie et du rayon vecteur elliptique en séries ordonnées suivant les puissances de l’excentricité*. A late mathematical-astronomical memoir published in the year of Laplace’s death. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 102](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=103)
- **1827:** *Mémoire sur le flux et reflux lunaire atmosphérique*. A memoir on atmospheric lunar tides, also published in the posthumously presented supplement to volume V of *Mécanique céleste*. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 102](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=103)

### Experimental physics, matter, heat, light, sound, and capillarity

- **1783:** *Mémoire sur la chaleur*. A jointly made experimental and theoretical memoir by Laplace and Antoine Lavoisier; its separate printing preceded its Academy publication. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 31, 96–97](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=97)
- **1781–1784 experiments; report conventionally dated 1793:** *De l’action du calorique sur les corps solides, principalement sur le verre et sur les métaux*. A joint Lavoisier–Laplace experimental report whose original printing was undated and probably about 1803; “1793” reflects the bibliographers’ reconstruction, not a secure publication date. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 97–98](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=98)
- **1783–1784 experiments; report conventionally dated 1793:** *Mémoire contenant les expériences faites sur la chaleur pendant l’hiver de 1783 à 1784*. A second joint Lavoisier–Laplace report, likewise absent from the collected works and bibliographically difficult to date. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 97–98](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=98)
- **1801:** *Sur un problème de physique relatif à l’électricité*. An editorially transmitted application of Laplace’s formulas for Earth’s figure to electricity; the journal’s note attributes the application to him. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 99](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=100)
- **1803:** *Mémoire sur le mouvement d’un corps qui tombe d’une grande hauteur*. A physical-mechanical memoir on terrestrial falling motion. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 99](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=100)
- **1803:** *Two notes on the relation of pressure to temperature among the molecules of an enclosed gas* [no collective title]. Two contributions embedded as notes in Berthollet’s *Essai de statique chimique*; they resist treatment as a freestanding work. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 99](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=100)
- **1806:** *Sur la théorie des tubes capillaires*. A journal abstract of the larger capillary theory presented in 1805–1806. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 99](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=100)
- **1806:** *Sur l’attraction et la répulsion apparente des petits corps qui nagent à la surface des fluides*. A physical memoir on surface interactions. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 99](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=100)
- **1806:** *Sur l’action capillaire*. A journal abstract of the separately issued *Théorie de l’action capillaire*, also appearing in another periodical. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 99](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=100)
- **1806:** *De l’adhésion des corps à la surface des fluides*. A partial publication of a memoir read to the Institut in November 1806. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 99](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=100)
- **1807:** *Supplément à la théorie de l’action capillaire*. A journal abstract of the continuation separately printed that year; it is a manifestation of that larger text rather than a wholly new project. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 99](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=100)
- **1809:** *Mémoire sur la double réfraction de la lumière dans les cristaux diaphanes*. A preliminary abstract of Laplace’s longer optical memoir. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 99](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=100)
- **1809:** *Sur la loi de la réfraction extraordinaire de la lumière dans les cristaux diaphanes*. A second abstract drawn from the same optical project, emphasizing the principle of least action and short-range forces. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 99](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=100)
- **1810:** *Mémoire sur le mouvement de la lumière dans les milieux diaphanes*. The full optical memoir behind the 1809 abstracts; the collected edition incorrectly dates it 1808. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 99](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=100)
- **1810:** *Sur la dépression du mercure dans un tube de baromètre, due à sa capillarité*. A practical capillarity memoir; Bouvard later published corrections to its numerical table. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 99–100](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=100)
- **1816:** *Sur la longueur du pendule à secondes*. A short excerpt from the fuller 1817 memoir and therefore a distinct manifestation rather than a separate research project. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 100](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=101)
- **1816:** *Sur l’action réciproque des pendules et sur la vitesse du son dans les diverses substances*. A physical memoir connecting coupled pendulums and acoustic propagation. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 100](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=101)
- **1816:** *Sur la transmission du son à travers les corps solides*. A brief abstract related to the reciprocal-pendulum memoir. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 100](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=101)
- **1816:** *Sur la vitesse du son dans l’air et dans l’eau*. A short acoustic memoir comparing propagation through different media. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 100](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=101)
- **1819:** *Considérations sur la théorie des phénomènes capillaires*. A late reconsideration of Laplace’s capillarity theory. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 101](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=102)
- **1821:** *Éclaircissements de la théorie des fluides élastiques*. A clarification of Laplace’s caloric theory of gases. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 76–77, 101](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=77)
- **1821:** *Sur l’attraction des sphères et sur la répulsion des fluides élastiques*. A memoir applying mathematical attraction theory to the repulsive forces postulated in gases. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 76–77, 101–102](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=77)
- **1822:** *Développement de la théorie des fluides élastiques et application de cette théorie à la vitesse du son*. A developed statement of Laplace’s gas theory and its acoustic consequences, later reworked in volume V of *Mécanique céleste*. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 76–77, 101–102](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=77)
- **1822:** *Addition au mémoire précédent sur le développement de la théorie des fluides élastiques*. A continuation whose title and identity depend on its placement after the preceding memoir. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 102](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=103)
- **1822:** *Sur la vitesse du son*. A concise acoustic note. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 102](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=103)
- **1822:** *Addition au mémoire sur la théorie des fluides élastiques*. A further brief addition to the elastic-fluid project. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 102](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=103)
- **1825:** *Sur la réduction de la longueur du pendule au niveau de la mer*. A physical-geodetic memoir giving a reduction protocol for pendulum measurements. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 102](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=103)
- **1826:** *Mémoire sur un moyen de détruire les effets de la capillarité dans les baromètres*. A practical protocol for suppressing capillary error in barometric measurements. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 102](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=103)

### Demography and social calculation

- **1786:** *Sur les naissances, les mariages et les morts à Paris, depuis 1771 jusqu’en 1784, et dans toute l’étendue de la France, pendant les années 1781 et 1782*. A demographic-statistical memoir applying probability and population data to births, marriages, and deaths. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 97](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=98)

### Administrative, political, and performative texts

- **1796:** *Discours prononcé aux deux conseils au nom de l’Institut national des sciences et des arts*. A ceremonial and institutional report on the Institut’s first year; as a speech delivered for the institution, it acts publicly rather than merely describing research. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 97–98](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=98)
- **1798:** *Report on the questions to be presented to the Institut d’Égypte* [no independent title recorded]. A jointly authored institutional project by Laplace, Lacépède, and Fourcroy, later combined with reports from other classes; its collective authorship prevents secure isolation of Laplace’s portion. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 92](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=93)
- **1799:** *Two ministerial letters and an accompanying report of 8 and 17 December 1799* [no fixed collective title]. Administrative acts made while Laplace was minister of the interior and printed in the *Moniteur universel*. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 85–86](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=86)
- **1800:** *Rapport sur la situation de l’École polytechnique*. An administrative report submitted on behalf of the Conseil de perfectionnement; collective institutional responsibility complicates individual authorship. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 98](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=99)
- **1805:** *Rapport sur le projet portant rétablissement du calendrier grégorien*. A senatorial report supporting restoration of the Gregorian calendar: a policy text intended to produce governmental action. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 99](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=100)
- **1814:** *Opinion on the proposed authorization of grain exports* [no separate title recorded]. A parliamentary intervention supporting free trade; it survives as proceedings rather than as an autonomous work. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 100](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=101)
- **1816:** *Sur une disposition du Code d’instruction criminelle*. A pamphlet using probability to argue that Article 351 treated defendants inequitably; it is a manifesto-like intervention aimed at changing criminal procedure. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 100](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=101)
- **1817:** *Recommendation on the cadastre* [no separate title recorded]. A parliamentary intervention urging completion of a geodetically grounded cadastre as the basis of fair land taxation. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 100](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=101)
- **1819:** *Recommendation on the lottery* [no separate title recorded]. A parliamentary intervention urging abolition of the state lottery because public revenue should not depend on citizens’ probabilistic illusions. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 101](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=102)
- **1821:** *Opinion on Article 351 of the Code d’instruction criminelle* [no separate title recorded]. A renewed parliamentary intervention on mathematically equitable jury majorities, continuing the project of the 1816 pamphlet. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 102](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=103)
- **1824:** *Calculation concerning conversion of the public debt* [no separate title recorded]. A parliamentary calculation comparing amortization schemes; it survives inside a debate rather than as an autonomous text. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 102](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=103)
- **1825:** *Opinion on conversion of the government bond issue* [no separate title recorded]. A parliamentary intervention favoring a reduced interest rate combined with increased capital. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 102](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=103)

### Projects, unpublished communications, lost works, and resistant objects

- **1770:** *Recherches sur les maxima et minima des lignes courbes*. Laplace’s earliest recorded Academy submission and the project-state behind the Latin memoir published in 1774; the report survives, but the submitted manuscript does not. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 88, 95–96](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=89)
- **1770:** *Sur quelques usages du calcul intégral appliqué aux différences finies*. An early draft of the 1771 Turin memoir; it resists being counted as a separate completed work because only archival reporting and a fragmentary description survive. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 88](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=89)
- **1770:** *Sur une méthode pour déterminer la variation de l’écliptique, du mouvement des nœuds et de l’inclinaison de l’orbite des planètes*. An unpublished Academy paper known from its title and referees’ record. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 88–89](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=89)
- **probably 1770:** *Sur la détermination de la variation de l’inclinaison et les mouvements des nœuds de toutes les planètes et principalement la variation de l’obliquité de l’écliptique*. An unpublished sequel whose date and relationship to a similarly titled “Suite” are uncertain because the Academy record appears erroneous. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 88–89](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=89)
- **1771:** *Sur le calcul intégral, les suites récurrentes, et la détermination de l’orbite lunaire*. A three-part submitted project known through the referees’ description; no manuscript is recorded as surviving. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 89](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=90)
- **1771:** *Sur les perturbations du mouvement des planètes causées par l’action de leurs satellites*. An unpublished Academy submission known from the institutional record. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 89](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=90)
- **1771:** *Une théorie générale du mouvement des planètes*. An unpublished general treatise-project, probably expanded into the 1784 *Théorie du mouvement et de la figure elliptique des planètes*; its manuscript has not survived. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 32, 89](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=33)
- **1777:** *Sur la nature du fluide qui reste dans le récipient de la machine pneumatique*. A communication of which the scholarly bibliography says no trace remains; its resistance to classification is literal, since only the Academy entry establishes that it once existed. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 90](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=91)
- **1777:** *Mémoire sur les oscillations des fluides qui recouvrent les planètes*. An unfinished publication-state: Laplace read an addition, withdrew it, and then revised and combined the project into the 1778 *Recherches sur plusieurs points du système du monde*. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 90](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=91)
- **1781:** *Applications of Laplace’s cometary-orbit method to Herschel’s object* [several untitled calculations]. These include a manuscript calculation and orbital elements recorded in the Academy minutes; they are working products rather than stable autonomous works. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 90–91](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=91)
- **1801–1802:** *Memoirs on lunar theory and a newly discovered long-period lunar inequality* [titles recorded only in minutes]. These communications were not separately published and may have been incorporated into Book VII of *Mécanique céleste*; their independent completeness cannot now be established. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 93](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=94)
- **1805:** *Mémoire sur la diminution de l’obliquité de l’écliptique*. An Institut communication for which the bibliography finds no publication; it may represent a project-state related to the 1809 memoir but should not be silently equated with it. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 93](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=94)
- **1813:** *Les éléments des variations des orbites planétaires*. An Academy memoir for which no publication is recorded. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 94](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=95)
- **before 1827:** *Projected treatise on molecular actions distinct from universal attraction* [no title]. Laplace announced an intention to gather his results on molecular forces into a separate work but did not complete it; as a project, the intention itself is the work-state that survives. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 86–87](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=87)
- **1827:** *Supplement to volume V of Mécanique céleste* [posthumously presented]. The manuscript was found among Laplace’s papers after his death; although printed within the treatise, its final authorization and completeness cannot be tested against an authorially published state. [Gillispie and Hahn, p. 95](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=96)
- **throughout his working life:** *Scientific correspondence*. An extensive epistolary body of work—including exchanges with Lagrange, Lavoisier, Condorcet, d’Alembert, Pingré, Lacroix, Bouvard, Delambre, Fourier, Fabroni, Blagden, Young, and Herschel—but not a recoverable closed corpus: Laplace’s retained copies and many family papers burned in 1925, while further material was reportedly destroyed at Caen in 1944. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 85–86](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=86)
- **1784–1789:** *Reports on artillery-cadet examinations and recommendations for reform* [no fixed collective title]. Archival administrative writings rather than a deliberately constituted obra; the bibliography records reports for 1784–1786 and reform recommendations from July–August 1789. [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 85–86](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=86)
- **various dates:** *Academy and Institut commission reports*. Laplace helped evaluate many other scientists’ submissions; the standard bibliography includes only reports for which he was chiefly responsible or spokesman, and even there collective authorship is sometimes uncertain. This activity resists conversion into a definitive list of individual “works.” [Gillispie and Hahn, pp. 87–95](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Laplace.pdf#page=88)
