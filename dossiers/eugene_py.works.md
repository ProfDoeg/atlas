## Works by Eugène Py

### Actuality views and filmed records

- **1897:** *La bandera argentina*. A seventeen-metre actuality view of the Argentine flag in Plaza de Mayo, photographed with a Gaumont camera. Long described as Argentina’s first film, that priority is disputed because Federico Figner may have filmed three Buenos Aires views in 1896; Py’s film is nevertheless the first surviving title in the established Argentine filmography. [Cine Nacional](https://cinenacional.com/pelicula/la-bandera-argentina/); [GRIMH](https://www.grimh.org/index.php?Itemid=1028&id=5178&lang=es&layout=edit&option=com_content&view=article)

- **1898:** *Exposición Rural*. An actuality reportedly shown at the Teatro Casino on 29 April 1898. It is mentioned in contemporary exhibition evidence but absent from the known Casa Lepage title lists, so both its attribution and its status as a separately constituted film remain less secure than those of later catalogue entries. [GRIMH](https://www.grimh.org/index.php?Itemid=1028&id=5178&lang=es&layout=edit&option=com_content&view=article)

- **1899:** *Premio Internacional en el Hipódromo Argentino*. An actuality reportedly exhibited at the Cine Pabellón Argentino on 4 November 1899 but missing from the surviving Lepage lists; it consequently resists treatment as a securely catalogued work rather than a documented screening title. [GRIMH](https://www.grimh.org/index.php?Itemid=1028&id=5178&lang=es&layout=edit&option=com_content&view=article)

- **1899, although 1897 and 1900 are also reported:** *Operación de quiste hidatídico de pulmón*. A scientific and pedagogical surgical film commissioned by Dr Alejandro Posadas, with Py responsible for filming and production; the uncertain date must not be silently resolved. It survives and is sometimes grouped with the hernia film under the collective title *Operaciones del Doctor Posadas*. [La pantalla letrada](https://www.colibri.udelar.edu.uy/jspui/bitstream/20.500.12008/8122/1/la%20pantalla%20letrada.pdf); [Interface](https://interface.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/v22n67.pdf); [Cine Nacional](https://cinenacional.com/pelicula/operacion-de-quiste-hidatidico-de-pulmon/)

- **1899, date uncertain:** *Operación de hernia inguinal*. The second of the two pedagogical records commissioned by Posadas. Some scholarship dates the hydatid-cyst view to 1897 and this view to 1899, while other accounts place both operations around 1899; the surviving material is therefore clearer than its chronology. [La pantalla letrada](https://www.colibri.udelar.edu.uy/jspui/bitstream/20.500.12008/8122/1/la%20pantalla%20letrada.pdf); [Interface](https://interface.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/v22n67.pdf)

- **1900:** *Revista de tropas argentinas el 25 de mayo*. An actuality of the May observances, reportedly exhibited at the Teatro Casino on 9 July. Like the two preceding pre-1900 reports, it is not found in the known Lepage lists, making the historical event, screening title and film-object difficult to separate conclusively. [GRIMH](https://www.grimh.org/index.php?Itemid=1028&id=5178&lang=es&layout=edit&option=com_content&view=article)

- **1900:** *Viaje del Doctor Campos Salles a Buenos Aires* (also catalogued as *Llegada del Presidente de la República de Brasil Dr. Campos Salles en Buenos Aires*). A three-part actuality record made on 25 October of the Brazilian president’s visit; “one film” is partly a catalogue convenience for three numbered views. [GRIMH](https://www.grimh.org/index.php?Itemid=1028&id=5178&lang=es&layout=edit&option=com_content&view=article); [Cine Nacional](https://cinenacional.com/pelicula/viaje-del-doctor-campos-salles-a-buenos-aires/)

- **1901:** *La revista de la Escuadra Argentina en mayo de 1901* (also described as *Maniobras navales en Bahía Blanca*). A roughly 140-metre naval actuality recording fleet manoeuvres at Puerto Belgrano; variant descriptive titles make it uncertain whether every reference denotes precisely the same assembled film. [Cine Nacional](https://cinenacional.com/pelicula/la-revista-de-la-escuadra-argentina-en-mayo-de-1901/); [Historia de los primeros años del cine en la Argentina](https://books.google.com/books?id=wKcuAAAAYAAJ)

- **1901:** *Salvamento de los náufragos Newman por el transporte Guardia Nacional*. An approximately eighty-metre reconstructed actuality concerning two Falkland Islanders rescued after drifting at sea. Its staging makes it neither an unmediated news record nor yet conventional fiction. [Eugenio Py: luz, cámara, acción](https://historiasconarte.com/2025/10/08/eugenio-py/)

- **1901:** *Visita del teniente general Bartolomé Mitre al Museo Histórico Nacional*. A roughly sixty-metre ceremonial actuality filmed during Mitre’s visit with the Jubilee Commission and shown on 19 November. [Eugène Py](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eug%C3%A8ne_Py); [Eugenio Py: luz, cámara, acción](https://historiasconarte.com/2025/10/08/eugenio-py/)

- **1901:** *El general Mitre visitando la Casa Lepage*. A twenty-metre actuality made when Mitre subsequently visited Py’s workplace. The visit appears to have occasioned the film rather than an autonomous authorial project. [Eugenio Py: luz, cámara, acción](https://historiasconarte.com/2025/10/08/eugenio-py/)

- **1901:** *En la Plaza de Mayo*. A twenty-metre street view including the arrival of an electric tram. It is a catalogue “view”—a photographed occurrence whose boundaries as an obra derive principally from sale and exhibition. [Eugenio Py](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugenio_Py); [Eugenio Py: luz, cámara, acción](https://historiasconarte.com/2025/10/08/eugenio-py/)

- **1901:** *Fiesta en el Talar de Pacheco*. A roughly hundred-metre record of a rural celebration, rodeo and horse-breaking. [Eugenio Py](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugenio_Py); [Eugenio Py: luz, cámara, acción](https://historiasconarte.com/2025/10/08/eugenio-py/)

- **1901:** *Pericón nacional*. A roughly ninety-metre filmed performance by the Podestá company. It is simultaneously a dance record, a theatrical fragment and an early screen manifestation, rather than an independently authored dramatic film in the later sense. [Eugenio Py: luz, cámara, acción](https://historiasconarte.com/2025/10/08/eugenio-py/)

- **1901:** *Manifestación radical del 25 de julio de 1901*. A roughly 140-metre actuality of the Radical demonstration passing through Plaza San Martín. [Eugenio Py: luz, cámara, acción](https://historiasconarte.com/2025/10/08/eugenio-py/)

- **1901:** *Club de Gimnasia y Esgrima*. A twenty-metre actuality view; the surviving descriptive title identifies its subject but does not establish a more elaborate independent project. [Eugenio Py: luz, cámara, acción](https://historiasconarte.com/2025/10/08/eugenio-py/)

- **1901 or shortly afterward:** *Alberto Santos-Dumont in Buenos Aires* [descriptive title]. A short actuality without a securely established title, remembered for an accidental comic interruption involving a man blocking Py’s camera. The incident exists in the historiography, but the film’s title, date and surviving status remain uncertain. [Eugène Py](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eug%C3%A8ne_Py); [Eugenio Py](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugenio_Py)

- **1902:** *El soldado Sosa en capilla*. An approximately hundred-metre actuality or reconstructed news scene concerning a condemned soldier whose sentence was commuted; it was shown at the Salón París on 31 January. Its mixture of event, reenactment and publicity resists a clean documentary/fiction division. [Eugenio Py: luz, cámara, acción](https://historiasconarte.com/2025/10/08/eugenio-py/)

- **1902:** *Llegada de Sir Thomas Holdich a Buenos Aires* [descriptive title]. An actuality of the British boundary arbitrator, premiered at the Salón París on 4 March; the sources describe the subject but do not provide a stable original title. [Eugenio Py: luz, cámara, acción](https://historiasconarte.com/2025/10/08/eugenio-py/)

### Trans-Andean journey and Chilean views

- **1902:** *Ferrocarril Transandino*. A roughly forty-metre travelling view made during Py and journalist Enrique Casellas’s government-backed journey toward Chile, reportedly with the camera secured to the locomotive. It is both a record of travel and an image made by the journey’s machinery. [Aisthesis 68](https://www.scielo.cl/pdf/aisthesis/n68/0718-7181-aisthesis-68-0111.pdf); [Eugenio Py: luz, cámara, acción](https://historiasconarte.com/2025/10/08/eugenio-py/)

- **1902:** *En los Andes*. A twenty-metre expedition view of the caravan climbing toward the summit. [Aisthesis 68](https://www.scielo.cl/pdf/aisthesis/n68/0718-7181-aisthesis-68-0111.pdf); [Eugenio Py: luz, cámara, acción](https://historiasconarte.com/2025/10/08/eugenio-py/)

- **1902:** *De Mendoza a Cacheuta*. A roughly ninety-metre journey film associated with the same trans-Andean expedition. The surviving secondary transcription of the Lepage material is typographically damaged, so the exact title division should be treated cautiously. [Eugenio Py: luz, cámara, acción](https://historiasconarte.com/2025/10/08/eugenio-py/)

- **1902:** *De Cacheuta a Uspallata*. A roughly 250-metre travelling view from the expedition, unusually long in relation to Py’s ordinary catalogue views. [Eugenio Py: luz, cámara, acción](https://historiasconarte.com/2025/10/08/eugenio-py/)

- **1902:** *De Puente del Inca a la Cumbre* [title reconstructed from a damaged catalogue transcription]. A roughly 190-metre expedition view. The wording is not completely secure and should not be mistaken for a normalized modern title. [Eugenio Py: luz, cámara, acción](https://historiasconarte.com/2025/10/08/eugenio-py/)

- **1902:** *Firma en Santiago de Chile de las actas de la paz*. A view photographed on 20 May at the signing connected with the Pactos de Mayo. It belongs to a state-sponsored actuality project rather than a self-contained personal statement. [GRIMH](https://www.grimh.org/index.php?Itemid=1028&id=5178&lang=es&layout=edit&option=com_content&view=article)

- **1902:** *La calle Ahumada con el paseo y sus aristocráticas damas*. A twenty-metre urban actuality emphasizing Santiago’s fashionable public life. [El caso de los pactos de mayo](https://revistas.usp.br/prolam/es/article/download/224139/219196/798027)

- **1902:** *Tranvías eléctricos en Santiago de Chile con señoritas que se desempeñan como mayorales*. A twenty-metre urban-modernity view; shorter references call it simply *Tramways eléctricos en Santiago de Chile*. [El caso de los pactos de mayo](https://revistas.usp.br/prolam/es/article/download/224139/219196/798027); [Cinechile](https://cinechile.cl/filmografia-del-documental-chileno-temprano-1897-1932-aportes-a-la-historia-de-la-cinematografia-nacional/)

- **1902:** *Fiesta en el Club Hípico de Santiago de Chile*. A twenty-metre social and sporting actuality listed in the Lepage catalogue. [El caso de los pactos de mayo](https://revistas.usp.br/prolam/es/article/download/224139/219196/798027)

- **1902:** *Procesión de Corpus Christi en Santiago de Chile*. A twenty-metre ceremonial and religious actuality. [El caso de los pactos de mayo](https://revistas.usp.br/prolam/es/article/download/224139/219196/798027)

- **1902:** *Cuartel de San Ignacio*. A twenty-metre military view showing the Chilean presidential escort regiment; the descriptive subtitle varies in modern accounts. [El caso de los pactos de mayo](https://revistas.usp.br/prolam/es/article/download/224139/219196/798027)

- **1902:** *Desfile de lanceros y cazadores del ejército chileno*. A twenty-metre military actuality. [El caso de los pactos de mayo](https://revistas.usp.br/prolam/es/article/download/224139/219196/798027)

- **1902:** *Escuela Militar de Santiago de Chile y artillería*. A twenty-metre military-institutional view. [El caso de los pactos de mayo](https://revistas.usp.br/prolam/es/article/download/224139/219196/798027)

- **1902:** *La Alameda de Santiago de Chile*. A twenty-metre city view presenting Santiago’s principal avenue. [El caso de los pactos de mayo](https://revistas.usp.br/prolam/es/article/download/224139/219196/798027)

- **1902:** *Views of General Emilio Körner and the German military mission* [collective descriptive title]. Several twenty-metre military views were made, but the consulted scholarship does not recover every individual catalogue title. Treating them as one “work” would be misleading; this entry marks an incompletely reconstructed set. [El caso de los pactos de mayo](https://revistas.usp.br/prolam/es/article/download/224139/219196/798027)

- **1902–1903:** *Thirty-two Chile-related views in Casa Lepage Catalogue no. 130* [catalogued corpus]. The 1904 catalogue records thirty-two views associated with Chile, including approximately twenty photographed there and later films of Chilean delegations in Buenos Aires. Because the modern scholarly sources publish only selected titles, the unnamed remainder cannot responsibly be turned into invented works or silently omitted from the record. [Cinechile](https://cinechile.cl/filmografia-del-documental-chileno-temprano-1897-1932-aportes-a-la-historia-de-la-cinematografia-nacional/); [El caso de los pactos de mayo](https://revistas.usp.br/prolam/es/article/download/224139/219196/798027)

### Staged, musical and synchronized films

- **1900:** *Tango argentino*. A short filmed tango performance. Its status is closer to a screen manifestation of dance and music than to a narrative motion picture. [Cine Nacional](https://cinenacional.com/pelicula/tango-argentino/)

- **1901:** *Bohemia criolla*. A short manifestation of Enrique de María’s theatrical work, with music attributed to Antonio Reynoso; later accounts place it among Py’s approximately sixty-metre musical or tango films. [Cine Nacional](https://cinenacional.com/pelicula/bohemia-criolla/); [El cine y el tango: etapa muda](https://es.scribd.com/document/299909040/El-Cine-y-El-Tango-Etapa-Muda)

- **1901:** *El calotero* (also transmitted as *El carlotero*). A short staged musical or theatrical fragment. The title itself is unstable in filmographies, and the surviving record does not justify choosing one spelling as unquestionably original. [Cine Nacional](https://cinenacional.com/persona/eugenio-py); [Breve historia del tango](https://maestrosdelsaber.com/wp-content/uploads/ftp-files/Culturas%20Varias/Cult%20Argentina/Cult%20Carretero%2C%20Andres%20-%20Breve%20historia%20del%20Tango.pdf)

- **1901:** *En casa del fotógrafo*. A short screen comedy attributed to Py in early-film listings but omitted from Cine Nacional’s twenty-five-title filmography. That disagreement makes its authorship and place in the oeuvre less secure. [List of Argentine films before 1930](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Argentine_films_before_1930)

- **1903:** *Pica, pica compadrito*. A roughly sixty-metre staged musical fragment derived from popular theatre; Cine Nacional credits Py with direction and photography. [Cine Nacional](https://cinenacional.com/pelicula/pica-pica-compadrito/); [El cine y el tango: etapa muda](https://es.scribd.com/document/299909040/El-Cine-y-El-Tango-Etapa-Muda)

- **1904:** *Abajo la careta*. A roughly sixty-metre filmed theatrical-musical number. It is a manifestation of a pre-existing stage work rather than a freestanding screenplay conceived solely for film. [Cine Nacional](https://cinenacional.com/pelicula/abajo-la-careta/); [El cine y el tango: etapa muda](https://es.scribd.com/document/299909040/El-Cine-y-El-Tango-Etapa-Muda)

- **1904:** *Los políticos*. A roughly sixty-metre staged musical short derived from the work of Nemesio Trejo and Antonio Reynoso; Py is credited with direction and photography. [Cine Nacional](https://cinenacional.com/pelicula/los-politicos/); [El cine y el tango: etapa muda](https://es.scribd.com/document/299909040/El-Cine-y-El-Tango-Etapa-Muda)

- **1905:** *Ensalada criolla*. A roughly sixty-metre film of a popular theatrical-musical work by Enrique de María, with music attributed to Enrique García Lalanne. [Cine Nacional](https://cinenacional.com/pelicula/ensalada-criolla/); [El cine y el tango: etapa muda](https://es.scribd.com/document/299909040/El-Cine-y-El-Tango-Etapa-Muda)

- **1906:** *Gabino, el Mayoral* (also catalogued as *Gabino, el Mayora*). A roughly sixty-metre theatrical-musical film associated with Enrique García Velloso and Enrique García Lalanne. The truncated *Mayora* form in some filmographies is probably a transmission error, but the variant must be retained. [Cine Nacional](https://cinenacional.com/pelicula/gabino-el-mayora/); [El cine y el tango: etapa muda](https://es.scribd.com/document/299909040/El-Cine-y-El-Tango-Etapa-Muda)

- **1906:** *El pechador*. A roughly sixty-metre staged short, preserved in the historical filmography as a title but poorly documented as an independent narrative object. [Cine Nacional](https://cinenacional.com/pelicula/el-pechador/); [El cine y el tango: etapa muda](https://es.scribd.com/document/299909040/El-Cine-y-El-Tango-Etapa-Muda)

- **1906:** *El soldado de la Independencia*. A roughly sixty-metre staged patriotic-musical film, distinct from the 1902 actuality *El soldado Sosa en capilla*. [Cine Nacional](https://cinenacional.com/pelicula/el-soldado-de-la-independencia/); [El cine y el tango: etapa muda](https://es.scribd.com/document/299909040/El-Cine-y-El-Tango-Etapa-Muda)

- **1907:** *Los tocayos*. An approximately eighty-metre theatrical or musical short. [Cine Nacional](https://cinenacional.com/pelicula/los-tocayos/); [El cine y el tango: etapa muda](https://es.scribd.com/document/299909040/El-Cine-y-El-Tango-Etapa-Muda)

- **1907:** *Mister Wiskey* (also *Mr. Whisky*). An approximately eighty-metre staged short and one of the titles explicitly associated with Lepage’s synchronized-film experiments; both title spellings circulate. [Cine Nacional](https://cinenacional.com/pelicula/mister-wiskey/); [Eugenio Py: luz, cámara, acción](https://historiasconarte.com/2025/10/08/eugenio-py/)

- **1907:** *Dejá é jugar, ché, ché* (also *Dejá de jugar, che, che*). An approximately eighty-metre musical short associated with Ángel Villoldo. Its image was designed to be synchronized with a separately reproduced phonograph recording, so the obra is properly the film-and-disc performance apparatus, not merely the strip of film. [Cine Nacional](https://cinenacional.com/pelicula/deja-e-jugar-che-che/); [El cine y el tango: etapa muda](https://es.scribd.com/document/299909040/El-Cine-y-El-Tango-Etapa-Muda)

- **1908:** *Los carreros*. A roughly sixty-metre musical or theatrical short in Py’s synchronized-film cycle. [Cine Nacional](https://cinenacional.com/pelicula/los-carreros/); [El cine y el tango: etapa muda](https://es.scribd.com/document/299909040/El-Cine-y-El-Tango-Etapa-Muda)

- **1909:** *El cochero de tranvía*. A roughly sixty-metre musical short, possibly associated with Ángel Villoldo; that authorship is reported as probable rather than certain. [Cine Nacional](https://cinenacional.com/pelicula/cochero-de-tranvia/); [El cine y el tango: etapa muda](https://es.scribd.com/document/299909040/El-Cine-y-El-Tango-Etapa-Muda)

- **1909:** *La beata*. An approximately eighty-metre filmed theatrical-musical number associated with Ezequiel Soria and Antonio Podestá. [Cine Nacional](https://cinenacional.com/pelicula/la-beata/); [El cine y el tango: etapa muda](https://es.scribd.com/document/299909040/El-Cine-y-El-Tango-Etapa-Muda)

- **Date uncertain, within the 1907–1911 cycle:** *A Palermo*. A roughly sixty-metre musical short attributed in tango-film scholarship to Agustín Fontanella and Antonio Podestá. It does not appear in Cine Nacional’s Py filmography, so its inclusion in Py’s oeuvre remains provisional. [El cine y el tango: etapa muda](https://es.scribd.com/document/299909040/El-Cine-y-El-Tango-Etapa-Muda)

- **1910:** *Justicia criolla*. A roughly sixty-metre film of Ezequiel Soria and Antonio Reynoso’s popular theatrical-musical material. It belongs to the synchronized manifestation cycle rather than straightforward silent narrative cinema. [Cine Nacional](https://cinenacional.com/pelicula/justicia-criolla/); [El cine y el tango: etapa muda](https://es.scribd.com/document/299909040/El-Cine-y-El-Tango-Etapa-Muda)

- **1910:** *La trilla*. A roughly sixty-metre filmed number associated with Nemesio Trejo and Enrique García Lalanne. [Cine Nacional](https://cinenacional.com/pelicula/la-trilla/); [El cine y el tango: etapa muda](https://es.scribd.com/document/299909040/El-Cine-y-El-Tango-Etapa-Muda)

- **1911:** *Los escruchantes*. A filmed popular-theatre work associated in contemporary filmography with Py’s direction. Cine Nacional calls it a silent feature, whereas tango-film scholarship treats it within the short synchronized cycle; its length, format and sound status are therefore disputed. [Cine Nacional](https://cinenacional.com/pelicula/los-escruchantes/); [El cine y el tango: etapa muda](https://es.scribd.com/document/299909040/El-Cine-y-El-Tango-Etapa-Muda)

- **1907–1911:** *Thirty-two synchronized films advertised by Casa Lepage* [catalogued project/corpus]. Lepage advertised thirty-two films made for phonographic synchronization, averaging about sixty metres; surviving scholarship does not provide a dependable one-to-one list of all thirty-two. This corpus resists being called either thirty-two autonomous “works” or a single work: it was an experimental production-and-exhibition protocol whose image and separately played sound together constituted each manifestation. [24 Cuadros](https://revista24cuadros.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/24-cuadros-11.pdf); [Breve historia del tango](https://maestrosdelsaber.com/wp-content/uploads/ftp-files/Culturas%20Varias/Cult%20Argentina/Cult%20Carretero%2C%20Andres%20-%20Breve%20historia%20del%20Tango.pdf)

### Commissioned cinematography and collaborative productions

- **1910:** *Centenary celebrations* [untitled or multiply titled commissioned corpus]. Py photographed several official films during Argentina’s Centenary, including a survivingly garbled catalogue reference to the Infanta Isabel’s departure. The consulted records do not permit the separate films or exact titles to be reconstructed without invention, so this remains an incompletely identified state-ceremonial project. [Eugenio Py: luz, cámara, acción](https://historiasconarte.com/2025/10/08/eugenio-py/)

- **1914:** *Amalia*. A silent feature directed and written by Enrique García Velloso, adapted from José Mármol’s novel and produced for the Sociedad del Divino Rostro; Py supplied camera work and cinematography, probably alongside Enrique Luchetti. Some accounts give 1912, but the established release date and Cine Nacional record are 1914, and Py should not be described as its director. [Cine Nacional](https://cinenacional.com/pelicula/amalia/); [Amalia](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amalia_%28pel%C3%ADcula_de_1914%29)

- **1915:** *Mariano Moreno y la revolución de Mayo*. A historical silent production scripted by Enrique García Velloso and premiered on 20 April 1915; Py’s credited contribution is cinematography, not sole authorship of the film. It is therefore a collaborative obra in which what Py “made” was its photographed moving image. [Cine Nacional](https://cinenacional.com/pelicula/mariano-moreno-y-la-revolucion-de-mayo/); [Eugenio Py: luz, cámara, acción](https://historiasconarte.com/2025/10/08/eugenio-py/)
