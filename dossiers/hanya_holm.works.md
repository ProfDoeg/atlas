## Works by Hanya Holm

### Concert choreography and dance-theatre works

- **1928:** *Bacchae*. Concert choreography; the standard work list attributes it to Holm, but the surviving summary does not specify whether it was an independent work or made within Mary Wigman’s institutional orbit. [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29)
- **1929:** *Farewell to His Friends*. Dance-theatre work also documented in Holm’s archive as *The Farewell*, with a script dated 1927–1928 and related material through 1930; the variant title and dates should not be silently harmonized. [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29); [NYPL finding aid, pp. 17–18](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf)
- **1930:** *Das Totenmal*. Monumental dance-theatre Gesamtkunstwerk by Albert Talhoff and Mary Wigman, for which Holm was assistant director and co-dancer; it resists classification as a Holm-authored work because her documented contribution was collaborative rather than sole authorship. [NYPL finding aid, pp. 1–2, 23–24](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf); [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29)
- **1935?:** *Dance in Two Parts*. Archival choreography comprising “A Cry Rises in the Land” and “New Destinies”; its date is explicitly conjectural in the finding aid. [NYPL finding aid, p. 15](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf)
- **1936:** *First Program at the Broadway Theatre, Denver*. A concert program rather than a single dance: Holm’s first American concert and therefore a composite manifestation of her emerging repertory. The standard list treats the event as a choreography entry, but the constituent works are not fully enumerated there. [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29); [NYPL finding aid, p. 2](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf)
- **1936:** *Dance of Joy*. Concert choreography documented through an annotated Wallingford Riegger score; it is absent from the shorter standard work list, so its status as a separately performed work is less securely documented. [NYPL finding aid, p. 26](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf)
- **1937:** *Trend*. Large-scale social-protest dance and Gesamtkunstwerk, made with composer Wallingford Riegger and scenic designer Arch Lauterer for seven soloists, thirty ensemble dancers, platforms, and ramps. [NYPL finding aid, pp. 2, 15–16](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf); [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29)
- **1938:** *Etudes*. Choreographed pedagogical demonstration: simultaneously concert dance and protocol for showing movement principles. It survives in demonstration notes rather than as an autonomous archival file. [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29); [NYPL finding aid, p. 15](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf)
- **1938:** *Metropolitan Daily*. Modern-dance composition later manifested on television; archive and standard chronology date it 1938, although some secondary lists give 1939. [NYPL finding aid, pp. 2, 15, 27](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf); [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29)
- **1939:** *Dance Sonata*. Concert choreography preserved through Holm’s and her dancers’ notation and a score by Harrison Kerr. [NYPL finding aid, pp. 15, 22, 26](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf); [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29)
- **1939:** *Dance of Work and Play* / *Dances of Work and Play*. Concert choreography whose title varies between singular and plural in the sources; notes, photographs, and Norman Lloyd’s score survive. [NYPL finding aid, pp. 2, 15, 23, 26](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf); [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29)
- **1939:** *Tragic Exodus*. Concert choreography documented by Holm’s and her dancers’ notes; it received a *Dance Magazine* award. [NYPL finding aid, pp. 2, 16](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf); [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29)
- **1939 and 1941–1942:** *Dance of Introduction*. Concert choreography with conflicting manifestations or dates: the biographical chronology gives 1939 and 1941, while archival notes are dated 1941–1942 and an orchestral score 1941. [NYPL finding aid, pp. 2, 15, 26](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf)
- **1940:** *They Too Are Exiles*. Concert choreography preserved in dancers’ part-notes; an associated score is also labeled “Opening Dance,” making its musical and sectional identity less than fixed. [NYPL finding aid, pp. 2, 16, 27](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf); [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29)
- **1941:** *The Golden Fleece*. Concert-dance Gesamtkunstwerk with choreography, notation, an Alex North score, and visual material concerning designer Kurt Seligmann. [NYPL finding aid, pp. 2, 15, 26](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf); [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29)
- **1941:** *From This Earth*. Concert choreography documented in the standard chronology and by an annotated Roy Harris score whose archival date remains tentative. [NYPL finding aid, p. 26](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf); [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29)
- **1942:** *What So Proudly We Hail*. Concert choreography listed in the standard biographical work chronology; its surviving form and collaborators are not specified there. [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29)
- **1942:** *Namesake*. Concert choreography preserved through correspondence, notes, and a script; the mixed documentation makes it more than a purely kinetic object. [NYPL finding aid, p. 15](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf); [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29)
- **1943:** *Parable*. Concert choreography whose archival notes identify a section as “Wise and Foolish Virgins”; an associated Couperin score is labeled “Virgins.” [NYPL finding aid, pp. 15, 27](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf); [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29)
- **1943:** *Suite of Four Dances*. Concert-dance suite listed in the standard chronology; it may correspond to archival material titled *Dance Suite*, but the sources do not establish that identity. [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29); [NYPL finding aid, p. 15](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf)
- **1943:** *Orestes and the Furies*. Concert choreography documented in Holm’s notebooks and performance photographs as *Orestes* and under its fuller title. [NYPL finding aid, pp. 23, 35](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf); [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29)
- **1944:** *What Dreams May Come*. Concert choreography documented in notes and performance photographs. [NYPL finding aid, pp. 16, 35](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf); [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29)
- **1945:** *Walt Whitman Suite*. Concert choreography; archival notes are dated 1946 and undated, while the standard chronology dates the work 1945. [NYPL finding aid, p. 16](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf); [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29)
- **1945:** *The Gardens of Eden*. Concert choreography listed in the standard chronology; the consulted archive inventory does not supply a dedicated file or clarify whether the plural title is original. [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29)
- **1946:** *Dance for Four*. Concert choreography listed in the standard chronology. [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29)
- **1946:** *Windows*. Concert choreography surviving in notes and photographs. [NYPL finding aid, pp. 16, 33](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf); [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29)
- **1947:** *And So Ad Infinitum*. Concert choreography listed in the standard chronology. [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29)
- **1948:** *Xochipilli*. Concert choreography; the archive’s photograph captions spell the title *Xochipili*, so orthography is not consistent across sources. [NYPL finding aid, p. 35](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf); [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29)
- **1949:** *Ionization*. Concert choreography to Edgard Varèse’s music; archival notes also connect *Ionisation* with “The Gates Are Desolate” from *Trend*, leaving some ambiguity about whether the 1949 item was a new work, a restaging, or an extracted section. [NYPL finding aid, pp. 15, 26](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf); [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29)
- **1950:** *Five Old French Dances*. Concert-dance suite listed in the standard chronology. [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29)

[NYPL finding aid, p. 27](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf); [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29)

- **1948:** *The Eccentricities of Davey Crockett*. Musical-theatre ballet created for the cooperative production *Ballet Ballads*; it was Holm’s first commercial-stage choreography. [NYPL finding aid, p. 2](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf); [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29)
- **1948:** *Ballet Ballads*. Composite theatrical production to which Holm contributed *The Eccentricities of Davey Crockett*; it is a collaborative container rather than wholly her obra. [NYPL finding aid, p. 17](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf)
- **1948:** *The Insect Comedy*. Dramatic production choreographed or movement-directed in collaboration with José Ferrer; the exact division of creative authorship is not specified by the finding aid. [NYPL finding aid, pp. 2, 21](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf); [Sächsische Biografie](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Hanya_Holm_%281893-1992%29)

### Pedagogical and institutional projects

- **1931–1936:** *New York Wigman School of the Dance*. Institutional and pedagogical project founded and directed by Holm as Wigman’s American branch; authorship is organizational and curricular rather than that of a bounded artwork. [NYPL finding aid, pp. 1, 12](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf)
- **1936–1968:** *Hanya Holm Studio / Hanya Holm School of the Dance*. Holm’s long-running school and pedagogical obra, renamed in 1949 and closed in 1968; its manifestations include curricula, classes, demonstrations, brochures, schedules, roll books, and tuition records. [NYPL finding aid, pp. 1, 12–14](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf)
- **1930s–1950s:** *Demonstration Program*. A choreographed manual of dance and movement theory combining Holm’s lecture, performed movement, Harvey Pollins’s music, and dancers’ percussion: simultaneously protocol, performance, and pedagogical Gesamtkunstwerk. It changed across programs rather than existing as one fixed text. [NYPL finding aid, pp. 1, 14–15](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf)
- **1941–1983:** *Colorado College Summer School of the Dance*. Institutional project founded and annually directed, taught, and choreographed by Holm; because students also made dances there, the school’s total output cannot be treated as exclusively Holm-authored. [NYPL finding aid, pp. 1, 20–21](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf)
- **Undated, developed across her teaching career:** *Holm’s movement pedagogy*. A body of practices concerning space, gravity, momentum, rhythm, and improvisational exploration rather than a codified “technique”; it resists being called a single work because Holm deliberately taught adaptable principles rather than a closed movement vocabulary. [Routledge Encyclopedia of Modernism](https://www.rem.routledge.com/articles/holm-hanya-1893-1992); [NYPL finding aid, pp. 1, 12–14](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf)

[NYPL finding aid, p. 22](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/dan19791.pdf)
- **1932–1955:** *Lecture Demonstrations*. Scripts and notes for pedagogical performances; these are protocols activated in speech and movement, not merely descriptive essays. - **Undated:** *Speeches*. An archival body of spoken texts without an item-level title list; individual authorship and completion cannot be assessed from the finding aid.
