## Works by Rudolf Ritsema

### Studies of the *I Ching*

- **1970–1971:** *Notes for Differentiating Some Terms in the I Ching*. A two-part lexical study published in *Spring*; its division across two issues does not make it two works. It is recorded in both Chung-ying Cheng’s scholarly bibliography and the later annotated bibliography of the *I Ching*. [Cheng](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1540-6253.1987.tb00331.x), [Hacker, Moore, and Patsco](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781000940732_A46912683/preview-9781000940732_A46912683.pdf)

- **1972:** *The Corrupted: A Study of the 18th Hexagram in the I Ching*. An interpretive study published in *Spring*, pp. 90–109. [Cheng](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1540-6253.1987.tb00331.x)

- **1973:** *The Pit and the Brilliance: A Study of the 29th and 30th Hexagrams of the I Ching*. A paired-hexagram study published in *Spring*, pp. 142–170. [Cheng](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1540-6253.1987.tb00331.x)

- **1976–1977:** *The Quake and the Bound: A Study of the 51st and 52nd Hexagrams of the I Ching*. One study issued in two parts in *Spring*—part one in 1976, pp. 191–211, and part two in 1977, pp. 89–104. [Hacker, Moore, and Patsco](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781000940732_A46912683/preview-9781000940732_A46912683.pdf)

- **1978:** *The Great’s Vigour: A Study of the 34th Hexagram in the I Ching (with a Note on Consulting the I Ching)*. A hexagram study that also functions partly as a protocol for consulting the oracle, published in *Spring*, pp. 193–206; one later bibliography gives the opening page as 183, so the pagination is disputed. [Yixue Bibliography](https://www.hermetica.info/YixueBib.htm), [Hacker, Moore, and Patsco](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781000940732_A46912683/preview-9781000940732_A46912683.pdf)

- **1979:** *The Hidden: A Study of the 4th Hexagram in the I Ching*. An interpretive study published in *Spring*, pp. 163–188. [Yixue Bibliography](https://www.hermetica.info/YixueBib.htm)

- **1982:** *Adorning: A Study of the 22nd Hexagram in the I Ching*. An interpretive study published in *Spring*, pp. 39–69. [Hacker, Moore, and Patsco](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781000940732_A46912683/preview-9781000940732_A46912683.pdf)

- **1984:** *Analyzing: A Study of the 40th Hexagram in the I Ching*. An interpretive study published in *Spring*, pp. 77–104. [Hacker, Moore, and Patsco](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781000940732_A46912683/preview-9781000940732_A46912683.pdf)

### The Eranos *I Ching* project and its translation-obras

- **1988–2001:** *Eranos I Ching Project*. A collective research, translation, divination, and conference project directed principally by Ritsema, first with Stephen Karcher and later with Shantena Augusto Sabbadini. Its thirty-eight round tables and changing provisional texts make it a project and something close to a Gesamtkunstwerk rather than a single authored book; the Eranos Foundation dates the round-table activity from 1990 to 2001, while its historical account places the project’s inception in 1988–1989. [Eranos Foundation project record](https://eranosfoundation.org/PRE/publications_b.htm), [Eranos Foundation history](https://eranosfoundation.org/PRE/history_b.htm)

- **1989–1991:** *Chou Yi: The Oracle of Encompassing Versatility*. A three-volume provisional translation and concordance made with Stephen L. Karcher: vol. I, hexagrams 1–30; vol. II, hexagrams 31–64; and vol. III, supplementary materials and concordance. Issued as *Eranos-Jahrbuch* 58–60 specifically for participants in the project’s round tables, it resists classification as a finished book: it is the working manifestation of an evolving translation project. [Eranos Foundation bibliography](https://eranosfoundation.org/PRE/publications_b.htm)

- **1994:** *I Ching: The Classic Chinese Oracle of Change—The Divinatory Texts with Concordance*. The substantially realized English translation and interpretive concordance made with Stephen L. Karcher, published simultaneously as parts I.4–6 of the Eranos project and as *Eranos-Jahrbuch* 62–64. It is a revised manifestation of the 1989–1991 provisional *Chou Yi*, not three new works merely because it occupies three yearbook numbers. [Eranos Foundation bibliography](https://eranosfoundation.org/PRE/publications_b.htm), [Daimon Eranos catalogue](https://www.daimon.ch/Eranos.pdf)

- **1996–2005:** *Eranos I Ching / I Ching: Il libro della versatilità / The Original I Ching Oracle*. A second translation-recension, made with Shantena Augusto Sabbadini directly from the Chinese and first published in Italian; sources variously date that first manifestation to 1996 and 1997. The 2005 English *Original I Ching Oracle: The Pure and Complete Texts with Concordance* is a later linguistic manifestation and revision of this same collaborative obra, not simply another unrelated book. [Brill scholarly history](https://brill.com/display/book/9789004513945/BP000001.pdf), [Google Books record](https://books.google.com/books?id=a8NlooeZGC8C)

- **2000:** *Yi-jing: Das Buch der Wandlungen*. A German-language manifestation of Ritsema’s translation work, associated with Hansjakob Schneider. The available bibliographies describe Schneider and Ritsema inconsistently as translators or editors; it should therefore not be counted confidently as an independent translation-obرا separate from the English Eranos recension. [CiNii author bibliography](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/author/DA01425559)

### Essays, memorials, and statements on Eranos

- **1982:** *Adolf Portmann 1897–1982*. A six-page memorial text in *Eranos-Jahrbuch* 51. [Eranos Foundation author index](https://eranosfoundation.org/page.php?page=11&pagename=lecturers)

- **1982:** *Gershom Scholem 1897–1982*. A two-page memorial text in *Eranos-Jahrbuch* 51. [Eranos Foundation author index](https://eranosfoundation.org/page.php?page=11&pagename=lecturers)

- **1987:** *The Origins and Opus of Eranos / Eranos: Ursprünge und Werk / L’œuvre d’Eranos et ses origines*. A trilingual historical and programmatic essay written for the fifty-fifth Eranos conference and printed in *Eranos-Jahrbuch* 56, pp. vii–xlvii. It describes Eranos, but also acts as a retrospective manifesto for its collective obra. [Eranos Foundation author index](https://eranosfoundation.org/page.php?page=11&pagename=lecturers)

- **1988:** *Encompassing Versatility: Keystone of the Eranos Project / Allumfassende Wendigkeit: Schlußstein des Eranos-Projekts / Versatilité englobante: Clef de voûte du projet Eranos*. A trilingual programmatic essay in *Eranos-Jahrbuch* 57, pp. vii–lvii; it is best classified as the manifesto of Ritsema’s reconstitution of Eranos around divination and participatory experiment. [Eranos Foundation author index](https://eranosfoundation.org/page.php?page=11&pagename=lecturers)

- **1992:** *The Ethic of the Image*. A short position paper delivered within the Eranos/Uehiro round-table project and published in *The Yi Ching and the Ethic of the Image*, pp. 5–7. It is a compact manifesto of an image-based ethical practice rather than a general treatise. [Daimon Eranos catalogue](https://www.daimon.ch/Eranos.pdf), [Eranos Foundation author index](https://eranosfoundation.org/page.php?page=11&pagename=lecturers)

- **1997:** *Images of the Unknown: The Eranos I Ching Project 1989–1997*. A collaborative retrospective with Shantena Augusto Sabbadini, published in *Gateways to Identity*, pp. 7–43. It documents a project whose events, consultations, and working procedures resist reduction to a self-contained text. [Daimon Eranos catalogue](https://www.daimon.ch/Eranos.pdf), [Eranos Foundation author index](https://eranosfoundation.org/page.php?page=11&pagename=lecturers)

### Prefaces and afterwords

- **1936:** *Vorwort*. The Eranos Foundation’s author index attributes the preface to Supplementary Volume 4, pp. 7–8, to Ritsema. Because he was seventeen and had no documented Eranos role then—and because the entry conflicts with the established chronology—this attribution is doubtful and is retained here only as a disputed catalogue claim. [Eranos Foundation author index](https://eranosfoundation.org/page.php?page=11&pagename=lecturers)

- **1969:** *Vorwort*. A jointly written preface with Adolf Portmann for *Eranos-Jahrbuch* 38, pp. 5–6. [Eranos Foundation author index](https://eranosfoundation.org/page.php?page=11&pagename=lecturers)

- **1970–1976:** *Preface / Vorwort*. Eight annual editorial prefaces jointly made with Adolf Portmann for *Eranos-Jahrbuch* 39–45; they are distinct occasional texts, although the index supplies the same generic title for each. [Eranos Foundation author index](https://eranosfoundation.org/page.php?page=11&pagename=lecturers)

- **1977–1981:** *Nachwort*. Five annual afterwords jointly made with Adolf Portmann for *Eranos-Jahrbuch* 46–50. Their shared generic title should not conceal that they belong to five different conference-volumes. [Eranos Foundation author index](https://eranosfoundation.org/page.php?page=11&pagename=lecturers)

- **1982–1988:** *Nachwort*. Seven annual afterwords written by Ritsema alone for *Eranos-Jahrbuch* 51–57. [Eranos Foundation author index](https://eranosfoundation.org/page.php?page=11&pagename=lecturers)

### Institutional and performative obra

- **1962–1988:** *The annual Eranos conferences under Ritsema’s direction*. A sustained curatorial and intellectual project comprising invitations, themes, encounters, lectures, discussions, and their yearbook manifestations. It resists being called a work by Ritsema alone—the participants and Catherine Ritsema were constitutive makers—but the Foundation’s history identifies his organizing activity as central, so excluding it would mistake the printed residue for the whole obra. [Eranos Foundation history](https://www.eranosfoundation.org/page.php?page=4&pagename=history)

- **1988 onward:** *The participatory reconstitution of Eranos*. A controversial institutional project that replaced the traditional lecture conference with experimental round tables centered on the *I Ching* and personal engagement. It is neither merely a text nor solely an administrative reform: it is a protocol enacted as a continuing collective event, subsequently manifested in conferences and yearbooks. [Eranos Foundation history](https://eranosfoundation.org/PRE/history_b.htm), [Eranos Foundation project record](https://eranosfoundation.org/PRE/publications_b.htm)
