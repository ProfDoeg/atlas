## Works by René Just Haüy

### Foundational treatises and books

- **1784:** *Essai d’une théorie sur la structure des crystaux, appliquée à plusieurs genres de substances crystallisées*. A book-length theoretical work with eight folding plates: Haüy’s first printed book and the first systematic statement of his model of crystals as ordered accumulations of polyhedral constituent units. ([Schuh’s annotated bibliography](https://mineralogicalrecord.com/new_biobibliography/hauy-rene-just/))

- **1787:** *Exposition raisonnée de la théorie de l’électricité et du magnétisme, d’après les principes de M. Æpinus*. A synthetic treatise intended to organize and disseminate the mathematical theory of electricity and magnetism developed by Æpinus and Coulomb; it is exposition rather than a claim to having originated the entire theory. ([Christine Blondel](https://www.persee.fr/doc/rhs_0151-4105_1997_num_50_3_1291))

- **1792–1793:** *Exposition abrégée de la théorie sur la structure des crystaux*. A short, separately issued condensation of the 1784 theory, reworked from periodical publication; surviving copies bear both 1792 and 1793 imprints, so the date is not completely uniform. ([Schuh’s annotated bibliography](https://mineralogicalrecord.com/new_biobibliography/hauy-rene-just/))

- **1797 [an V]:** *Extrait d’un traité élémentaire de minéralogie*. A prospectus-like extract announcing and sampling Haüy’s projected mineralogical treatise; it had already appeared in parts in the *Journal des mines*. It is both an independent publication and a provisional manifestation of the larger project completed in 1801. ([Schuh’s annotated bibliography](https://mineralogicalrecord.com/new_biobibliography/hauy-rene-just/))

- **1801:** *Traité de minéralogie*. A comprehensive obra in four volumes and a fifth atlas volume containing 86 engraved plates: simultaneously a theory of crystal structure, a mineral-classification system, a reference work, and a visual atlas. The first edition appeared in two reset formats, quarto and octavo; these are manifestations of the same work rather than two separate works. ([Schuh’s annotated bibliography](https://mineralogicalrecord.com/new_biobibliography/hauy-rene-just/))

- **1803:** *Traité élémentaire de physique*. A two-volume instructional treatise commissioned for the new French lycée system, drawing upon Haüy’s teaching and advice from Laplace, Biot, Lagrange, and Berthollet. It is a pedagogical synthesis rather than solely a record of Haüy’s original discoveries. ([Christine Blondel](https://www.persee.fr/doc/rhs_0151-4105_1997_num_50_3_1291))

- **1806:** *Traité élémentaire de physique*, second edition. A substantially enlarged and revised realization of the 1803 textbook project; its extensive alteration justifies treating it as more than a simple reprint. ([Persée authority record](https://www.persee.fr/authority/582794))

- **1809:** *Tableau comparatif des résultats de la cristallographie et de l’analyse chimique, relativement à la classification des minéraux*. A tabular and argumentative classification work comparing crystallographic determinations with chemical analyses and updating the system of the 1801 *Traité*. ([Schuh’s annotated bibliography](https://mineralogicalrecord.com/new_biobibliography/hauy-rene-just/))

- **1817:** *Traité des caractères physiques des pierres précieuses, pour servir à leur détermination lorsqu’elles ont été taillées*. A practical determinative treatise with tables and three plates, designed to identify cut gemstones through physical properties; it functions partly as a protocol for testing objects. ([Schuh’s annotated bibliography](https://mineralogicalrecord.com/new_biobibliography/hauy-rene-just/))

- **1822:** *Traité de cristallographie, suivi d’une application des principes de cette science à la détermination des espèces minérales, et d’une nouvelle méthode pour mettre les formes cristallines en projection*. Haüy’s final crystallographic synthesis, comprising two text volumes and an atlas of 84 plates. It culminates rather than replaces the theoretical project begun in 1784. ([Schuh’s annotated bibliography](https://mineralogicalrecord.com/new_biobibliography/hauy-rene-just/))

- **1822–1823:** *Traité de minéralogie*, second edition. A corrected and considerably enlarged four-volume revision with a 120-plate atlas; the text volumes began appearing in 1822, while the atlas is dated 1823, after Haüy’s death. The title pages say that the revision was made by the author, but its posthumous completion makes the final publication partly an editorial manifestation of Haüy’s project. ([Schuh’s annotated bibliography](https://mineralogicalrecord.com/new_biobibliography/hauy-rene-just/))

### Scientific memoirs, reports, and notices

- **1781–1782:** *Extrait d’un mémoire sur la structure des crystaux de grenat*. Haüy’s first crystallographic memoir, approved by the Académie in 1781 and printed in 1782; the mismatch between presentation, approval, and publication dates explains its occasional dating to 1781, 1782, or 1784. ([Alfred Lacroix](https://doi.org/10.3406/bulmi.1944.4560))

- **1784:** *Observations sur les schorls*. A short mineralogical memoir read to the Académie royale des sciences and subsequently printed in its *Mémoires*. ([Alfred Lacroix](https://doi.org/10.3406/bulmi.1944.4560))

- **1784:** *Mémoire sur la structure des cristaux de feldspath*. A crystallographic research memoir applying cleavage and structural analysis to feldspar. ([Alfred Lacroix](https://doi.org/10.3406/bulmi.1944.4560))

- **1787:** *Mémoire sur la structure des cristaux de schorl*. A research memoir extending Haüy’s structural method to schorl crystals. ([Alfred Lacroix](https://doi.org/10.3406/bulmi.1944.4560))

- **1787:** *Des principes généraux de la théorie de l’électricité*. A scientific article or memoir presenting the general principles underlying Haüy’s larger electrical treatise; it belongs to that project but is a distinct periodical text. ([Christine Blondel](https://www.persee.fr/doc/rhs_0151-4105_1997_num_50_3_1291))

- **1788:** *Mémoire où l’on expose une méthode analytique pour résoudre les problèmes relatifs à la structure des cristaux*. A mathematical protocol for deriving and testing crystalline forms. ([Alfred Lacroix](https://doi.org/10.3406/bulmi.1944.4560))

- **1788:** *Mémoire sur la double réfraction du spath d’Islande*. An optical and crystallographic investigation of Iceland spar. ([Alfred Lacroix](https://doi.org/10.3406/bulmi.1944.4560))

- **1789:** *Exposition abrégée de la théorie de la structure des cristaux*. A periodical statement in the *Annales de chimie* that preceded or underlay the separately issued abridgment; bibliographies do not always distinguish the article cleanly from the 1792–1793 book manifestation. ([Schuh’s annotated bibliography](https://mineralogicalrecord.com/new_biobibliography/hauy-rene-just/))

- **1789:** *Mémoire sur la manière de ramener à la théorie du parallélépipède celle de toutes les autres formes primitives des crystaux*. A theoretical memoir attempting to reduce the several primitive crystalline forms to a common geometrical account. ([Alfred Lacroix](https://doi.org/10.3406/bulmi.1944.4560))

- **1790:** *Mémoire sur les crystaux appelés communément pierres de croix*. A mineralogical study of cruciform crystals, read in 1790 but printed later; its date therefore varies between catalogues. ([Alfred Lacroix](https://doi.org/10.3406/bulmi.1944.4560))

- **1791:** *Description de la gemme orientale*. A short descriptive mineralogical notice in the *Bulletin de la Société philomatique*. ([Alfred Lacroix](https://doi.org/10.3406/bulmi.1944.4560))

- **1793:** *Observations sur différentes variétés du sulfate baritique (spath pesant)*. A manuscript memoir known initially through Alexandre Brongniart’s printed extract; the surviving publication is therefore a mediated manifestation, not simply a text printed by Haüy himself. ([Alfred Lacroix](https://doi.org/10.3406/bulmi.1944.4560))

- **1793:** *De la structure considérée comme caractère distinctif des minéraux*. A programmatic scientific text arguing that structure can define and distinguish mineral species. ([Alfred Lacroix](https://doi.org/10.3406/bulmi.1944.4560))

- **1793:** *Rapport sur les moyens employés pour mesurer le poids d’un pied cube d’eau*. A jointly authored experimental report by Haüy and Antoine Lavoisier; it records a measurement procedure connected with defining the new unit of mass and is therefore both report and protocol. ([Alfred Lacroix](https://doi.org/10.3406/bulmi.1944.4560))

- **1794 [an II]:** *Instruction sur les mesures déduites de la grandeur de la Terre, uniformes pour toute la République, et sur les calculs relatifs à leur division décimale*. A collective protocol issued by the Temporary Commission of Republican Weights and Measures, to which Haüy belonged. It resists treatment as an individually authored Haüy work: his responsibility is institutional and collaborative. ([Persée authority record](https://www.persee.fr/authority/582794))

- **1795–1796 [an III–IV]:** *Leçons de physique des Écoles normales*. A spoken teaching course captured by stenographers and reviewed by the professors before publication in the collective *Séances des Écoles normales*. It is neither an ordinary authored book nor an unmediated transcript; the surviving text is a collaborative documentary manifestation of Haüy’s lectures. ([Christine Blondel](https://www.persee.fr/doc/genesis_1167-5101_2003_num_20_1_1384))

- **1796 [an IV]:** *Observations sur la structure des crystaux appelés zéolithes, et sur les propriétés électriques de quelques-uns*. A memoir combining crystallographic description with experiments on mineral electricity. ([Persée authority record](https://www.persee.fr/authority/582794))

- **1810:** *Sur l’électricité des minéraux*. A substantial memoir on pyroelectric and pressure-induced electrical behavior in minerals. Its German separate edition of 1811, *Ueber die Elektrizität der Mineralkörper*, is a translated manifestation with additions by Carl Cäsar von Leonhard, not a new work made solely by Haüy. ([Schuh’s annotated bibliography](https://mineralogicalrecord.com/new_biobibliography/hauy-rene-just/))

- **1809:** *Mémoire sur la théorie d’une nouvelle espèce de décroissement intermédiaire, relative à la structure des cristaux qui dérivent du rhomboïde, et sur quelques propriétés générales de cette forme*. A technical crystallographic memoir defining a new type of decrement and applying it to calcite. ([Persée authority record](https://www.persee.fr/authority/582794))

- **1812:** *Sur des cristaux de pyroxène des environs de New-York*. A mineralogical notice applying Haüy’s descriptive system to American pyroxene specimens. ([Persée authority record](https://www.persee.fr/authority/582794))

- **1815–1816:** *Mémoire sur une loi de cristallisation appelée loi de symétrie*. A multipart memoir published across successive installments and journals; the work is one extended argument despite its several manifestations. Haüy had taught the principle earlier, but 1815 marks its first full publication. ([Schuh’s annotated bibliography](https://mineralogicalrecord.com/new_biobibliography/hauy-rene-just/))

- **1815:** *Description d’une nouvelle variété d’amphibole*. A short taxonomic and crystallographic mineral description. ([Persée authority record](https://www.persee.fr/authority/582794))

- **1781–1822:** *Scientific memoirs, reports, reviews, and mineral descriptions*. Lacroix’s standard bibliography enumerates about 140 scientific publications, including short notices, extracts, academy reports, revised reprints, and serial installments; the Royal Society catalogue counts roughly 100 journal papers because it groups and excludes items differently. This publication stream resists honest conversion into 140 wholly independent obras: many entries are fragments, recastings, or manifestations of the continuing crystallographic, mineralogical, electrical, and classificatory projects named above. ([Alfred Lacroix, bibliography at pp. 95–105](https://doi.org/10.3406/bulmi.1944.4560); [Christine Blondel’s discussion of the count](https://www.persee.fr/doc/rhs_0151-4105_1997_num_50_3_1291))

### Models, collections, and taxonomic creations

- **Before 1781–1822:** *Haüy’s mineral collection*. An evolving research collection ultimately numbering about 12,000 specimens, many supplied with handwritten labels and selected as physical “types” for Haüy’s classifications. It is an obra assembled and ordered by Haüy, not an object wholly fabricated by him; more than 6,000 associated specimens have been identified in later museum holdings. ([Schuh’s annotated biography](https://mineralogicalrecord.com/new_biobibliography/hauy-rene-just/))

- **Circa 1801–1822:** *Sets of wooden crystallographic models*. Hundreds of precisely proportioned pearwood solids embodying the forms and decrements represented in Haüy’s treatises, made for teaching, demonstration, exchange, and the identification of specimens. Haüy designed, commissioned, selected, and ordered these sets, but specialist woodworkers—including Pleuvin, Beloeuf, and Lambotin—physically fabricated many models, so sole manual authorship should not be assigned to him. ([Teylers Museum model history](https://www.teylersmuseum.nl/en/collection/instruments/crystal-models); [Schuh’s annotated biography](https://mineralogicalrecord.com/new_biobibliography/hauy-rene-just/))

- **Late eighteenth–early nineteenth century:** *Crystalline-form nomenclature and projection system*. A body of names, diagrams, measurements, and conventions for describing and projecting crystal forms. It is not a single text or object, but a working representational system manifested across memoirs, atlases, lectures, labels, and wooden models.

- **1797–1822:** *Mineral and rock species, varieties, and names*. A dispersed taxonomic creation including such enduring names as actinolite, anatase, amphibole, apophyllite, diorite, dolerite, eclogite, pegmatite, psammite, phthanite, and trachyte, alongside many names later reduced to synonyms. This is a body of classificatory acts rather than one book, and priority or modern validity is not identical for every name. ([Muséum-related summary](https://www.persee.fr/doc/bulmi_0366-3248_1944_num_67_1_4562))

### Manuscripts, courses, and unfinished projects

- **1794–1795:** *Manuscript course for the École normale de l’an III*. A prepared teaching manuscript whose relation to the stenographic *Séances* and to the later *Traité élémentaire de physique* shows repeated rewriting rather than simple reproduction. It is an intermediate project with its own documentary identity. ([Christine Blondel](https://www.persee.fr/doc/genesis_1167-5101_2003_num_20_1_1384))

- **Late eighteenth–early nineteenth century:** *Manuscript courses in mineralogy, crystallography, and physics*. Lecture manuscripts survive from Haüy’s teaching at the École normale, École des mines, Muséum, and Sorbonne. They are working pedagogical projects—some autograph, some copied or mediated—and their archival survival is incomplete. ([Bénédicte Bilodeau-Guinamard](https://www.persee.fr/doc/rhs_0151-4105_1997_num_50_3_1293))

- **Eighteenth–nineteenth century:** *Catalogue de minéraux*. A manuscript catalogue of approximately seventy leaves attributed to Haüy and preserved by the Muséum. It is a working collection instrument rather than a finished publication and consequently resists being called a self-contained literary work. ([Persée authority record](https://www.persee.fr/authority/582794))

- **Before 1822:** *Second edition of the Traité de minéralogie*. As a project, this was Haüy’s extensive rewriting and expansion of the 1801 classification; publication crossed his death, with the atlas appearing in 1823. The completed edition is listed above, but the authorial project and its posthumous manifestation are not perfectly identical. ([Schuh’s annotated bibliography](https://mineralogicalrecord.com/new_biobibliography/hauy-rene-just/))

### Literary and epistolary works

- **1763:** *Templum pacis*. A Latin school poem conventionally attributed to the young Haüy. It lies outside his scientific oeuvre, and the attribution rests on later bibliographical and archival cataloguing rather than on the mature author’s scientific publication record. ([Persée authority record](https://www.persee.fr/authority/582794))

- **Throughout his adult life:** *Correspondence*. A dispersed body of scientific, institutional, pedagogical, and personal letters, including substantial exchanges with Martinus van Marum, Pierre Prévost, mineral collectors, and fellow academicians. It is a documentary creation rather than a work Haüy prepared for publication, and no complete collected edition exists. ([R. Hooykaas, “La correspondance de Haüy et de Van Marum”](https://www.persee.fr/doc/bulmi_0037-9328_1949_num_72_10_5166))
