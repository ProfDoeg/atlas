## Works by George Soros

### Unfinished philosophical project

- **1962–1963:** *The Burden of Consciousness: A Study of the Relationship between Thinking and Society*. An unpublished philosophical manuscript—or, more accurately, an abandoned project—on the relation between modes of thought and forms of society. Soros later called it his first major essay; accounts differ between treating it as a completed unpublished essay and an unfinished book assembled in successive drafts, while a revised portion entered *The Age of Fallibility*. ([Soros, “The General Theory of Reflexivity”](https://www.georgesoros.com/2009/10/26/the-general-theory-of-reflexivity/); [Soros, “Fallibility, Reflexivity, and the Human Uncertainty Principle”](https://www.georgesoros.com/2014/01/26/fallibility-reflexivity-and-the-human-uncertainty-principle/))

### Books and book-length arguments

- **1987:** *The Alchemy of Finance: Reading the Mind of the Market*. A book combining an investment diary with a theoretical exposition of reflexivity; its hybrid of recorded market experiment, financial memoir, and philosophy resists classification as a conventional investment manual. ([George Soros official bibliography](https://www.georgesoros.com/books/); [Wiley](https://www.wiley.com/en-gb/shop/general-finance-investments/the-alchemy-of-finance-reading-the-mind-of-the-market-p-9780471043133))

- **1990:** *Opening the Soviet System*. A short political-economic book proposing ways to transform the collapsing Soviet order into an open society; its prescriptions give parts of it the force of a policy manifesto. ([George Soros official bibliography](https://www.georgesoros.com/books/))

- **1991:** *Underwriting Democracy: Encouraging Free Enterprise and Democratic Reform Among the Soviets and in Eastern Europe*. A book of political-philanthropic testimony and programmatic argument drawn from Soros’s interventions in Eastern Europe. It documents a project while also attempting to direct that project’s future. ([George Soros official bibliography](https://www.georgesoros.com/books/))

- **1995:** *Soros on Soros: Staying Ahead of the Curve*. An autobiographical and theoretical book made in interview form with Byron Wien and Krisztina Koenen; bibliographies variously foreground Soros alone or describe Wien and Koenen as collaborators or coauthors, so it should not be treated as an unmediated single-author memoir. ([Wiley](https://uat.store.wiley.com/en-us/soros-on-soros-staying-ahead-of-the-curve-p-9780471119777); [George Soros official bibliography](https://www.georgesoros.com/books/))

- **1998:** *The Crisis of Global Capitalism: Open Society Endangered*. A book-length diagnosis of the global financial system and a defense of open society, joining economic theory to political warning. ([George Soros official bibliography](https://www.georgesoros.com/books/))

- **2000:** *Open Society: Reforming Global Capitalism*. A revised and extended political-economic argument arising from *The Crisis of Global Capitalism*, but presented by Soros’s bibliography as a separate book rather than merely a new edition; it proposes an “open society alliance” and therefore partly functions as a manifesto. ([George Soros official bibliography](https://www.georgesoros.com/books/))

- **2002:** *George Soros on Globalization*. A programmatic book criticizing existing international financial institutions and proposing institutional reforms. Soros’s website dates a later issue to 2005, but bibliographic records place the first publication in 2002; the latter date identifies the obra rather than a reissue. ([Open British National Bibliography](https://obnb.uk/a00066402-george-soros); [George Soros official bibliography](https://www.georgesoros.com/books/))

- **2003:** *The Bubble of American Supremacy: Correcting the Misuse of American Power*. A political polemic against the Bush administration’s post-September 11 foreign policy, written to intervene in contemporary policy and thus close to a manifesto. Soros’s site gives a 2004 edition date, while bibliographies identify the original American publication as 2003. ([George Soros official bibliography](https://www.georgesoros.com/books/); [PBS, “Books by George Soros”](https://panhandlepbs.org/now/politics/sorosbooks.html))

- **2006:** *The Age of Fallibility: Consequences of the War on Terror*. A philosophical-political book applying fallibility and reflexivity to American power and the war on terror; it incorporates a revised version of material from *The Burden of Consciousness*. The official site displays a 2007 issue date, but the first edition appeared in 2006. ([George Soros official bibliography](https://www.georgesoros.com/books/); [Open British National Bibliography](https://obnb.uk/a00066402-george-soros); [Soros, “Fallibility, Reflexivity, and the Human Uncertainty Principle”](https://www.georgesoros.com/2014/01/26/fallibility-reflexivity-and-the-human-uncertainty-principle/))

- **2008; revised 2009:** *The New Paradigm for Financial Markets: The Credit Crisis of 2008 and What It Means*; revised as *The Crash of 2008 and What It Means: The New Paradigm for Financial Markets*. A book interpreting the financial crisis through reflexivity and the idea of a long “super-bubble.” Soros’s official list counts the 2008 and 2009 titles separately among fifteen books, but the latter is a revised and updated manifestation of the former, not an independent obra. ([George Soros official bibliography](https://www.georgesoros.com/books/); [Soros’s 2014 scholarly bibliography](https://chian.io/projects/george-soros/writings/fallibility-reflexivity-and-the-human-uncertainty-principle))

### Lectures transformed into a book

- **2009–2010:** *The Soros Lectures at the Central European University*. Five lectures delivered in Budapest in October 2009 and published together as a book in 2010; the spoken series and printed volume are two manifestations of the same intellectual work. Soros’s website dates its featured edition to 2011, but bibliographic records date the original book to 2010. ([George Soros official bibliography](https://www.georgesoros.com/books/); [Open British National Bibliography](https://obnb.uk/a00066402-george-soros))

### Essay collections

- **2012:** *Financial Turmoil in Europe and the United States: Essays*. A curated book of previously dispersed interventions written in response to the 2008 crash, banking reform, and the euro crisis; it is a later collection rather than fifteen newly made essays under one continuous title. ([George Soros official bibliography](https://www.georgesoros.com/books/))

- **2019:** *In Defense of Open Society*. A retrospective collection of speeches and essays, including previously unpublished material, on political philanthropy, Central European University, reflexivity, technology, and the European Union. Its status is compilatory: the volume is a new editorial object, while many constituent texts predate it. ([George Soros official bibliography](https://www.georgesoros.com/books/))

### Collaborative dialogue

- **2014:** *The Tragedy of the European Union: Disintegration or Revival?* A book-length dialogue between Soros and journalist Gregor Peter Schmitz, assembled from interviews and correspondence; cataloguing it solely under Soros obscures its collaborative form. ([George Soros official bibliography](https://www.georgesoros.com/books/); [PublicAffairs](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/george-soros/the-tragedy-of-the-european-union/9781610394222/))
