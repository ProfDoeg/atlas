## Works by Juan Eduardo Cirlot Laporta

### Musical compositions

- **1942:** *Vals*. Musical composition premiered in Zaragoza; its score is not known to survive. [Instituto Cervantes](https://cvc.cervantes.es/el_rinconete/anteriores/junio_17/05062017_01.htm)
- **1943:** *Himno para piano*. Composition for solo piano; it belongs to Cirlot’s largely lost early musical production. [Universidad de Sevilla](https://idus.us.es/bitstreams/c1833f52-0d67-47bb-8f37-988496d75e65/download)
- **1947:** *Suite atonal*. Five-part atonal composition, including a concluding “Fuga”; it is the only score by Cirlot known to survive, although sources also mention a second version. [Museo Reina Sofía](https://radio.museoreinasofia.es/sites/default/files/audio/material/experimentacion-sonora_1940-1952_miguel-molina.pdf)
- **1947–1948:** *Preludio para cinco instrumentos de cuerda*. Chamber composition; the surviving accounts do not establish whether this is identical with the differently described *Preludio para quinteto y doble violonchelo*. [Instituto Cervantes](https://cvc.cervantes.es/el_rinconete/anteriores/junio_17/05062017_01.htm)
- **1948:** *Preludio para quinteto y doble violonchelo*. Chamber prelude performed in Cirlot’s Círculo Manuel de Falla period; its relationship to the *Preludio para cinco instrumentos de cuerda* is unclear. [Instituto Cervantes](https://cvc.cervantes.es/el_rinconete/anteriores/junio_17/05062017_01.htm)
- **1948:** *Poema de Rilke*. Musical setting or Rilke-inspired composition; the score is apparently lost. [Instituto Cervantes](https://cvc.cervantes.es/el_rinconete/anteriores/junio_17/05062017_01.htm)
- **1948:** *Concertino para instrumentos de cuerda*. Chamber-orchestral composition and one of Cirlot’s last documented musical works. [Instituto Cervantes](https://cvc.cervantes.es/el_rinconete/anteriores/junio_17/05062017_01.htm)

### Poetry, poetic sequences, and verbal-visual works

- **1942:** *Pájaros tristes y otros poemas a Pilar Bayona*. Early poetic group preserved unpublished until 2001; its posthumous book form is editorial rather than an edition issued by Cirlot. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1943:** *La muerte de Gerión. Ballet*. A poem conceived through the form and movement of a ballet: a literary-musical construction rather than a staged ballet score. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1943:** *Seis sonetos y un poema del amor celeste*. Poetry collection. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1944:** *Oda a Ígor Strawinsky y otros versos*. Poetry pamphlet whose homage to a composer also announces Cirlot’s transfer of musical procedures into verse. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1945:** *Árbol agónico*. Poem or short poetic work first appearing in the periodical *Fantasía*. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1945:** *En la llama*. Poetry collection. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1946:** *Canto de la vida muerta*. Book-length poetic canto and the first of a sequence Cirlot extended across several decades. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1946:** *Cordero del abismo*. Poetry collection. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1946:** *Donde las lilas crecen*. Poetry collection. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1947:** *Susan Lenox*. Poetic work generated from the screen image and fictional persona associated with Greta Garbo, complicating any division between poem, film response, and icon. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1948:** *El poeta conmemorativo. Doce sonetos-homenaje. El libro inconsútil*. Composite poetic book joining a sequence of homage sonnets to another named textual unit. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1949:** *Elegía sumeria*. Poetic elegy drawing on the symbolic world of ancient Mesopotamia. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1949:** *Diariamente*. Poetry collection. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1949:** *Eros*. Poetry collection issued by Cobalto. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1949:** *Yo también canto la Navidad*. Nativity-themed poetic work. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1949:** *Lilith*. Poetic-symbolic book centred on the mythic figure Lilith. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1949:** *Montserrat*. Poetic work; the standard bibliography supplies no publisher or fuller material description. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1950:** *Ontología*. Poetry book whose philosophical title does not make it a systematic treatise. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1951:** *80 sueños*. Collection of dream texts, resisting a firm boundary between prose notation, oneiric document, and poetry; an expanded *88 sueños* appeared posthumously in 1988. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1951:** *Amor*. Poetry collection. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1951:** *7 homenajes*. Sequence of seven poetic homages. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1951:** *13 poemas de amor*. Sequence of thirteen love poems. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1952:** *Libro de oraciones*. Poetic book adopting the operative form of prayers rather than merely describing prayer. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1953:** *Segundo canto de la vida muerta*. Second poetic canto in the *vida muerta* sequence. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1954:** *Tercer canto de la vida muerta*. Third poetic canto in the sequence. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1955:** *El palacio de plata*. Poetry book; it was later coupled with *Cristo, cristal*, but that combined edition is not a separate new work in its entirety. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1956:** *Cuarto canto de la vida muerta y otros fragmentos*. Fourth canto plus poetic fragments, written or fixed in 1956 but not published as a book until 1961. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1957:** *La dama de Vallcarca*. Poetic work first published in *Correo de las Artes*. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1961:** *Blanco*. Poetry book whose title and pared material form participate in its meaning. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1962:** *Los espejos*. Poetry collection. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1966:** *Regina tenebrarum*. Poetic invocation or sequence; its Latin title means “queen of darkness.” [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1966:** *Las oraciones oscuras*. Poems acting in the form of dark prayers, between lyric sequence and verbal rite. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1967:** *Las hojas del fuego*. Poetry collection. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1967:** *Once poemas romanos*. Sequence of eleven Roman poems. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1967:** *Oraciones a Mithra y a Marte*. Invocatory poems addressed to Mithras and Mars; prayer here is an enacted poetic form. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1967:** *Marco Antonio*. Poetic work centred on Mark Antony. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1967:** *La doncella de las cicatrices*. Poetic work; the standard bibliography records no publisher. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1967:** *Dos poemas*. Two-poem pamphlet. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1967:** *Bronwyn*. First book or unit of the poetic project prompted by Rosemary Forsyth’s character in *The War Lord*; “Bronwyn” becomes an image, name, symbolic presence, and generative verbal matrix. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1967:** *Bronwyn, 2*. Further manifestation of the Bronwyn project, published in *Árbol de Fuego*; its numbering overlaps awkwardly with the later Roman-numeral series and should not be silently regularized. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1968:** *Anahit*. Mythic-symbolic poetic work named for the Armenian goddess. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1968:** *Cristo, cristal*. Permutational and phonetic poem published together with *El palacio de plata*; it operates on the material resemblance of words and therefore resists reduction to a conventionally discursive text. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1968:** *Bronwyn, II*. Numbered continuation of the Bronwyn poetic cycle. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1968:** *Bronwyn, III*. Numbered continuation of the Bronwyn poetic cycle. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1968:** *Bronwyn, IV*. Numbered continuation of the Bronwyn poetic cycle. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1968:** *Bronwyn, V*. Numbered continuation of the Bronwyn poetic cycle. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1969:** *El incendio ha empezado*. Poetic work first published in *Árbol de Fuego*. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1969:** *Cosmogonía*. Poetic construction of a cosmos rather than a scientific cosmology. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1969:** *El pensamiento de Edgar Poe*. Poetic or hybrid meditation published among Cirlot’s poetry, despite its essay-like title. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1969:** *La sola virgen*. Poetry pamphlet. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1969:** *Bronwyn, VI*. Numbered continuation of the Bronwyn cycle. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1969:** *Bronwyn, VII*. Numbered continuation of the Bronwyn cycle. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)

- **1969:** *Hamlet*. Poetic work responding to the Shakespearean figure rather than a dramatic adaptation. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1969:** *Del no mundo*. Aphoristic-poetic work: neither ordinary verse collection nor systematic philosophy, but a series of terse formulations of Cirlot’s “no-world.” [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1970:** *Un poema del siglo VIII*. Poem published in *Árbol de Fuego*; the “eighth century” belongs to its title and imagined temporal situation, not its actual date. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1970:** *Denuncio la tortura*. Declarative poem whose title performs a denunciation and thus approaches the force of a manifesto. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)

- **1970:** *Orfeo*. Poetic work organized around the Orphic figure and the relation of poetry to music and descent. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1970:** *Inger Stevens. In memoriam*. Memorial poem or sequence made in response to the actor’s death; like Bronwyn, Inger Stevens becomes both person and generative image. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1971:** *Donde nada lo nunca ni, I y II*. Two-part experimental poetic work whose syntax deliberately resists ordinary semantic completion. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)

- **1971:** *Homenaje a Bécquer I y II*. Two-part poetic homage to Gustavo Adolfo Bécquer. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1971:** *Ocho sonetos por un sueño*. Eight-sonnet sequence generated around a dream. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)

- **1971:** *La quête de Bronwyn*. Poetic “quest” within the Bronwyn cycle, invoking the medieval romance tradition without becoming a narrative romance. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1971:** *44 sonetos de amor*. Sonnet cycle written in 1971 but first published as a book in 1993 under Victoria Cirlot’s editorship. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1971:** *Ínger, permutaciones*. Permutational poetic score based on Inger Stevens’s name, displacing conventional semantic progression with serial recombination. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1972:** *Inger (Ocho variaciones fonovisuales sobre el nombre Inger Stevens)*. Eight visual-phonetic variations: a Gesamtkunstwerk-like page object in which typography, sound, name, and image cannot be cleanly separated; first issued posthumously in 1996. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1973:** *Perséfone*. Late poem published in *Cuadernos Hispanoamericanos* in the year of Cirlot’s death. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **Date uncertain; posthumously assembled 1998:** *El libro de Cartago*. Poetic Cartago project edited by Victoria Cirlot from posthumous materials; its book-form completeness is editorial and should not be assumed to represent a final authorially fixed work. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **Date uncertain; posthumously published 1996:** *Variaciones fonovisuales*. Verbal-visual compositions edited by Enrique Granell; they resist classification as either poems to be read, scores to be sounded, or images to be viewed. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)

### Fiction and dream narrative

- **Summer 1950:** *Nebiros*. Cirlot’s only known novel, withheld after censorship difficulties and first published in 2016; its long non-publication makes it both a completed manuscript and a frustrated publication project. [Ediciones Siruela](https://www.siruela.com/catalogo.php?id_libro=3035)

### Dictionaries and symbolic instruments

- **1949:** *Diccionario de los ismos*. Lexicographic-critical dictionary of modern artistic and intellectual “isms”; later editions are manifestations of the same reference work, not new works. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1958–1969:** *Diccionario de símbolos tradicionales / Diccionario de símbolos*. Symbolic dictionary first issued under the longer title in 1958 and enlarged and retitled by 1969; its successive states make it an evolving research instrument rather than a wholly stable text. The 1962 English *A Dictionary of Symbols* is a translated manifestation, not a separate authored work. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)

### Art, architecture, music, and cultural criticism

- **1949:** *Ígor Strawinsky*. Critical monograph on the composer. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1949:** *Joan Miró*. Art-critical monograph on Miró. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1950:** *El arte de Gaudí*. Study of Antoni Gaudí’s architecture. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1950:** *Ferias y atracciones*. Cultural and visual study of fairs and amusement attractions; it treats an ephemeral environment rather than a conventional fine-art corpus. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1951:** *La pintura abstracta*. Critical-historical study of abstraction in painting. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1951:** *Manuel Capdevila*. Artist monograph. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1952:** *El estilo del siglo XX*. Synthetic critical study of twentieth-century style. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1953:** *Introducción al surrealismo*. Historical and theoretical introduction to Surrealism. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1953:** *El mundo del objeto bajo la luz del surrealismo*. Essay on the surrealist object, left unpublished until 1986; as a suppressed or stranded publication project, it cannot be identified simply with its much later edition. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1954:** *La imagen surrealista*. Study of the surrealist image, recovered and published in 1996 after remaining in an editorial archive. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1954:** *El ojo en la mitología. Su simbolismo*. Symbolic-mythological study written in 1954 and first published in 2019; its posthumous edition does not establish that Cirlot regarded the surviving state as final. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1955:** *La pintura surrealista*. Art-historical and critical study of Surrealist painting. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1955:** *Del expresionismo a la abstracción*. Critical history tracing a movement from Expressionism toward abstraction. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1955:** *Morfología y arte contemporáneo*. Theoretical study applying morphological analysis to contemporary art. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1955:** *Arte románico en el Museo de Arte de Cataluña*. Museum-based study of Romanesque art; the English *Romanesque Art* issued the same year is a linguistic manifestation rather than securely a distinct work. ### Graphic work

- **Undated:** *[Gravat sobre partitura]*. An engraving made over a musical score; the museum-supplied descriptive title is not necessarily Cirlot’s. It materially fuses graphic intervention and musical notation and therefore resists separation into “visual art” and “music.” [MACBA](https://www.macba.cat/es/actor/juan-eduardo-cirlot/)

### Posthumously constituted textual bodies

- **Dates various; published 1996:** *Confidencias literarias*. Posthumously edited body of literary reflections rather than a securely documented book completed under that title by Cirlot. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **Dates various; published 1997:** *De la crítica a la filosofía del arte*. Lourdes Cirlot’s posthumous selection or reconstruction of Juan Eduardo Cirlot’s art-critical thought; it is an editorially constituted corpus, not an original book simply “published late.” [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **Dates various; published 2008:** *Cirlot en Vallcarca*. Posthumous textual assemblage associated with Cirlot’s Vallcarca world; its status is that of an editorially mediated archive or constellation rather than a lifetime publication. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **1955–1969; assembled 2020:** *Se parece el dolor a un gran espacio. Escritos sobre informalismo 1955–1969*. Posthumous collected body of writings edited by Lourdes Cirlot and Enrique Granell; the collection is not itself a work fixed by Cirlot, though its component texts are his. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
- **Dates various; assembled 2019:** *Aforismos del no mundo*. Antonio Rivero Taravillo’s posthumous edition of Cirlot’s aphoristic material; it is a scholarly manifestation of a dispersed body of texts, not necessarily an authorially completed book bearing this exact title. [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/portales/juan_eduardo_cirlot/bibliografia/)
