## Works by Erich Mühsam

### Poetry books, songbooks, and commemorative objects

- **1904:** *Die Wüste. Gedichte 1898–1903*. Mühsam’s first poetry collection, gathering poems composed across the stated date range. [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)
- **1909:** *Der Krater*. A poetry collection; its reappearance in the 1914 collected poems makes the later volume a new manifestation rather than a separate poetic work. [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)
- **1914:** *Wüste – Krater – Wolken. Die Gedichte*. A consolidated poetic obra joining the earlier *Wüste* and *Krater* with the new cycle *Wolken*. [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)
- **1919:** *1919. Dem Andenken Gustav Landauers*. A memorial publication for the murdered Gustav Landauer: simultaneously poem-cycle, mourning object, and political commemoration. [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)
- **1920:** *Brennende Erde. Verse eines Kämpfers*. A collection of wartime and revolutionary poems whose subtitle presents lyric speech as an instrument of struggle. [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)
- **1924:** *Bilder und Verse für Zenzl: Meiner Zenzl zum 9. Hochzeitstag*. A unique illustrated manuscript made in Plötzensee prison for Mühsam’s wife: part picture-book, part poetry manuscript, part anniversary gift, and therefore close to a small Gesamtkunstwerk rather than an ordinary book. The later editions are facsimile manifestations of this object. [Erich-Mühsam-Gesellschaft chronology](https://erich-muehsam.de/erich-muehsam/lebensdaten/), [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)
- **1925:** *Revolution. Kampf-, Marsch- und Spottlieder*. A song-text collection intended for agitation, marching, performance, and ridicule; the poems are therefore also performative political instruments. [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)
- **1925:** *Seenot*. A separately issued poetic text or small verse publication; the later editorial title *Dampfer „Deutschland“ in Seenot* is a posthumous manifestation and should not be mistaken for another original work. [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)
- **1928:** *Sammlung 1898–1928*. Mühsam’s own retrospective assemblage of poetry and prose. It is an authored self-collection—an obra made by selection and arrangement—not a posthumous “complete works” edition. [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)

### Plays, revues, and performance works

- **1906:** *Die Hochstapler. Lustspiel in vier Aufzügen*. A four-act comedy written for theatrical performance. Some secondary lists date it 1904, but the standard bibliography records the first book publication in 1906; the dates of composition and publication should not be conflated. [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php), [Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd118584758.html)
- **1914:** *Im Nachthemd durchs Leben. Ein Süddeutsches Weihebühnen-Festspiel in sieben Bildern, nebst Prolog und Epilog sowie Gesang und bengalischer Beleuchtung*. A collaboratively written parody of the Wagnerian festival drama, made with Reinhard Koester and Carl Georg von Maassen and issued only as a manuscript print. Its fusion of drama, song, spectacle, and lighting makes it a comic Gesamtkunstwerk; individual authorship of particular passages is not securely separable. [Erich-Mühsam-Gesellschaft](https://erich-muehsam.de/erich-muehsam-gesellschaft/publikationen/), [bibliographical discussion](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9783110855401_A33482795/preview-9783110855401_A33482795.pdf)
- **1914:** *Die Freivermählten. Polemisches Schauspiel in drei Aufzügen*. A three-act polemical play: theatre used as an argument against the legal and moral regulation of intimate relationships. [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)
- **1921:** *Judas. Arbeiterdrama in fünf Akten*. A five-act workers’ drama about a revolutionary mass strike, betrayal, and the danger of substituting intrigue for collective agency. [Erich-Mühsam-Gesellschaft](https://erich-muehsam.de/erich-muehsam/literarisches-werk/), [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)
- **1928:** *Staatsräson. Ein Denkmal für Sacco und Vanzetti*. A documentary-political stage work and textual monument to Sacco and Vanzetti. Its subtitle matters: this is not merely a play representing an event but a commemorative object intended to intervene in public memory. [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)
- **1930:** *Alle Wetter. Volksstück mit Gesang und Tanz*. An antifascist popular play combining speech, song, and dance. The scholarly record calls it a fragment, whereas some later accounts describe it as completed; its textual completeness is therefore disputed. [Scholarly review](https://www.gdw-berlin.de/IWK/1970er/1978_01.pdf), [German Historical Museum](https://www.dhm.de/lemo/biografie/erich-muehsam)
- **date uncertain:** *Der Loreleyerkasten. Eine satirische Revue*. A satirical revue preserved in the literary estate and first issued as a book in 1978. The publication date is not a creation date, and the available bibliographical record does not establish a secure date of composition. [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)

### Narrative, satire, and memoir

- **1900:** *Tante Klodt*. A novella surviving in the estate and first published in 1994; its delayed manifestation should not be mistaken for its date of making. [Erich-Mühsam-Gesellschaft](https://erich-muehsam.de/erich-muehsam-gesellschaft/publikationen/)
- **1904:** *Billy’s Erdengang. Eine Elephantengeschichte für artige Kinder*. A collaborative illustrated children’s satire written with Hanns Heinz Ewers and illustrated by Paul Haase. Text, co-authorship, and illustration together constitute the made book-object; Mühsam’s individual share cannot simply be isolated as a single-author work. [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)
- **1905:** *Die Psychologie der Erbtante. Eine Tanthologie aus 25 Einzeldarstellungen als Beitrag zur Lösung der Unsterblichkeits-Frage*. A mock-scientific satirical sequence of twenty-five character studies, organized as a spoof taxonomy rather than as a conventional narrative. [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)
- **1921–1923:** *Ein Mann des Volkes*. An unfinished novel project satirizing a careerist socialist and the corruption of revolutionary organizations. It resists being called a completed work: the surviving thing is a fragment, first published from the estate in 1984. [Erich-Mühsam-Gesellschaft](https://erich-muehsam.de/erich-muehsam/literarisches-werk/)
- **by 1931:** *Unpolitische Erinnerungen* (also published as *Namen und Menschen*). An autobiographical memoir consisting of recollected people and milieus rather than a continuous life-history. *Namen und Menschen* is an alternative editorial title, not another memoir. [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)

### Essays, polemics, investigations, and political protocols

- **1903:** *Die Homosexualität. Ein Beitrag zur Sittengeschichte unserer Zeit*. A sociological and political polemic against the persecution of homosexuality; later editions call it a *Streitschrift*, but that retrospective subtitle is not its original one. [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)
- **1905:** *Ascona. Eine Broschüre*. A critical report on the alternative-life and reform communities around Monte Verità. It is observation, polemic, and self-positioning rather than a neutral travel book. [Erich-Mühsam-Gesellschaft](https://erich-muehsam.de/erich-muehsam/literarisches-werk/), [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)
- **1908:** *Die Jagd auf Harden*. A polemical intervention in the public and legal persecution surrounding Maximilian Harden. [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)
- **1920–1922:** *Die Einigung des revolutionären Proletariats im Bolschewismus*. A political treatise first manifested serially in *Die Aktion*. Sources give the span as either 1920–1922 or 1921–1922, so no more exact single date should be imposed. [Erich-Mühsam-Gesellschaft chronology](https://erich-muehsam.de/erich-muehsam/lebensdaten/), [Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd118584758.html)
- **1923:** *Das Standrecht in Bayern*. A documentary-polemical investigation of Bavarian summary justice. It acts as an indictment of state violence rather than merely describing judicial procedure. [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)
- **1925:** *Alarm. Manifeste aus zwanzig Jahren*. An authorial collection of manifestos and public interventions. Its components are texts that seek to provoke action, while the collected book is a retrospective political obra. [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)
- **1926:** *Gerechtigkeit für Max Hoelz!*. A campaign pamphlet produced in support of the imprisoned revolutionary Max Hoelz. It is advocacy and political action in printed form, not a detached account. [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)
- **1929:** *Von Eisner bis Leviné. Die Entstehung der bayerischen Räterepublik*. A participant-history of the Bavarian council republic. Later editions expand the title with “und Niederlage” or alter “Die Entstehung” to “Zur Entstehung”; those are editorial manifestations, not separate works. [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)
- **1930:** *Alle Macht den Räten!*. A programmatic essay proposing council organization and a stateless social order. Its imperative title and practical institutional argument make it part manifesto, part political protocol. [Contemporary *Fanal* text](https://xn--mnster-ist-bunt-zvb.de/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/erich-muehsam_alle-macht-den-raeten_fanal-1930-1.pdf)
- **1932–1933:** *Die Befreiung der Gesellschaft vom Staat. Was ist kommunistischer Anarchismus?*. A programmatic exposition of communist anarchism, first appearing as a *Fanal* special issue and bearing a 1933 book imprint; sources consequently date it either 1932 or 1933. It is simultaneously treatise, manifesto, and outline for social reorganization. [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php), [biographical account](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erich_M%C3%BChsam)
- **date uncertain:** *Die Freiheit als gesellschaftliches Prinzip*. A political-philosophical essay known through later publication; the 1977 book date is an editorial manifestation, not evidence for the date of composition. [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)
- **date uncertain:** *Abrechnung*. A polemical prose work preserved in the literary estate. The standard posthumous collected edition confirms its existence but the readily available apparatus does not establish a secure composition date, so it should not be supplied with one. [Collected-edition contents record](https://www.tte-buecherei.de/katalog/alphaKatalog.pdf)
- **during the First World War, unfinished:** *Untitled book project on the war-guilt question*. An unfinished nonfiction project mentioned in the critical diary registers. It resists treatment as a fixed work because neither a stable title nor a completed text is established. [Diary register](https://www.muehsam-tagebuch.de/pdf/Register-Band-6.pdf)

### Periodicals and editorial projects

- **1911–1914, 1918–1919:** *Kain. Zeitschrift für Menschlichkeit*. A periodical project edited and, according to the Erich-Mühsam-Gesellschaft, solely authored by Mühsam. The journal as serial political-literary practice is the obra; its individual issues and articles are manifestations within it. [Erich-Mühsam-Gesellschaft](https://erich-muehsam.de/erich-muehsam/literarisches-werk/)
- **1911:** *Kain-Kalender für das Jahr 1912*. An edited literary-political almanac made as an extension of the *Kain* project, not merely a book authored in the ordinary singular sense. [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)
- **1912:** *Kain-Kalender für das Jahr 1913*. The second *Kain* almanac, likewise an editorial assemblage and annual manifestation of the journal’s project. [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)
- **1926–1931:** *Fanal. Anarchistische Monatsschrift*. Mühsam’s anarchist monthly and a sustained editorial-political project joining essays, polemics, poems, correspondence, and organizational intervention. Bibliographical summaries sometimes extend its nominal history to 1933, but regular publication ended in 1931; the later special issue carrying *Die Befreiung der Gesellschaft vom Staat* complicates the endpoint. [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl.php), [Erich-Mühsam-Gesellschaft biography](https://erich-muehsam.de/erich-muehsam/literarisches-werk/)

### Diaries, notebooks, correspondence, and works that resist work-status

- **1891:** *Sylter Tagebuch*. An adolescent diary later published from the surviving document. It is a private record that became a literary manifestation posthumously and therefore resists uncomplicated classification as a deliberately published “work.” [Erich-Mühsam-Gesellschaft](https://erich-muehsam.de/erich-muehsam-gesellschaft/publikationen/)
- **1910–1924:** *Tagebücher*. A forty-two-notebook diary project, of which only thirty-five notebooks are known to survive. Because seven are missing, every “complete” edition is complete only with respect to the surviving record, not to what Mühsam made. [Critical edition report](https://www.xn--mhsam-tagebuch-gsb.de/tb/edition.php)
- **1926–1933:** *Notizbücher*. Working notebooks recording journeys, lectures, contacts, appointments, political activity, and drafts; they replace neither the earlier diaries nor a finished memoir. Some passages remain undeciphered, and the surviving material is known principally through copies received by the Akademie der Künste, so completeness cannot be assumed. [Gustav Landauer Initiative](https://gustav-landauer.org/erich-muehsam-notizbuecher)
- **1900–1934:** *Briefe*. A dispersed body of correspondence rather than a single work designed by Mühsam. Later volumes such as *Briefe an Zeitgenossen* and *In meiner Posaune muß ein Sandkorn sein!* are editorial constructions from that body, not titles he gave to an epistolary obra. [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)
- **dates various:** *Handzeichnungen*. A surviving body of drawings, caricatural sketches, and illustrated manuscripts. It resists reduction to a textual bibliography: *Handzeichnungen und Gedichte* is a posthumous editorial assemblage, while the drawings themselves are independent visual objects of varying date and status. The bibliography warns that its record for the 1936 publication was adopted from earlier bibliographies without verification. [Bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)
- **dates various:** *Cabaret songs, poems, speeches, newspaper articles, aphorisms, and manifestos*. This is a large dispersed body rather than one bounded work; many pieces first existed as performance, periodical intervention, or oral political action before later collection. The specialist bibliography expressly states that some individual periodical contributions remain unrecorded, so no honest list can claim item-level completeness. [Bibliographical scope note](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl.php)

### Uncertain or rejected attributions

- **1903:** *Die Eigenen. Ein Tendenzroman für freie Geister*. Published under Emil F. Ruedebusch’s name and sometimes placed in lists of Mühsam’s originals, but the standard Mühsam bibliography does not list it among his books. Its attribution to Mühsam is therefore unsafe and it should not silently be counted as his obra. [Standard bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)
- **1905:** *Lebt die Liebe! Aphorismen von Emil F. Ruedebusch und Helmar Lerski*. Sometimes included in derivative Mühsam work lists, but it names Ruedebusch and Lerski and is absent from the standard Mühsam bibliography. Without stronger documentary evidence, Mühsam’s authorship remains unestablished. [Standard bibliography](https://www.muehsam.de/appl/bibl_select.php)
- **1904:** *Die Killeberger*. The Neue Deutsche Biographie explicitly assigns this novella to Mühsam’s brother Siegfried, not to Erich Mühsam; it is included here only to prevent a recurrent false attribution. [Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd118584758.html)
