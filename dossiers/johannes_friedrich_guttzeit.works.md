## Works by Johannes Friedrich Guttzeit

### Poems and poetry collections

- **1889:** *Jugendblüten: Ein Strauß lyrischer Gedichte*. A collection of lyric poems included in Franz Brümmer’s contemporary biobibliography; it is poetry, not a reform treatise. [Brümmer, p. 19](https://www.deutschestextarchiv.de/book/show/bruemmer_lexikon03_1913)
- **1893:** *Das Buch der Liebe: Ein Stück Herzensgeschichte in Gedichten*. An autobiographically framed sequence or collection of love poems; shorter references sometimes reduce the title to *Das Buch der Liebe*, but these appear to denote the same obra rather than two securely distinct works. [Monte Verità Archive](https://gusto-graeser.info/Diefenbach/guttzeit.html), [Guttzeit’s 1922 back-list](https://muvs.org/media/pdf/ein-dunkler-punkt.pdf)
- **1897:** *Leitsterne: Sinngedichte*. A collection of aphoristic or reflective poems. [Monte Verità Archive](https://gusto-graeser.info/Diefenbach/guttzeit.html), [Guttzeit’s 1922 back-list](https://muvs.org/media/pdf/ein-dunkler-punkt.pdf)
- **By 1900:** *Auf dem Schlachtfelde*. A separately circulated long anti-war poem, also associated with *Der Bruder*; its date is not securely established by the consulted bibliography, so it should not be silently assigned the journal’s founding year. [Monte Verità Archive](https://gusto-graeser.info/Diefenbach/guttzeit.html), [Guttzeit’s 1922 back-list](https://muvs.org/media/pdf/ein-dunkler-punkt.pdf)

### Religious and philosophical reform writings

- **1881 or 1882:** *Von der Kirche zur Natur: Ausdruck der Hauptgeistesströmung unseres Zeitalters*. A programmatic religious-philosophical tract announcing Guttzeit’s movement from institutional Christianity toward “nature”; sources disagree between 1881 and 1882. Later editions were substantially retitled or expanded as *Von der Kirche zur Natur oder Vom Buchstaben zum Geist in der Bibel: I. Schöpfung und Sündenfall*, with appendices, making the later manifestation more than a simple reprint. [Brümmer, p. 19](https://www.deutschestextarchiv.de/book/show/bruemmer_lexikon03_1913), [Literaturport](https://www.literaturport.de/literaturlandschaft/autoren-berlinbrandenburg/autor/johannes-guttzeit/), [Guttzeit’s 1922 back-list](https://muvs.org/media/pdf/ein-dunkler-punkt.pdf)
- **1891:** *Der volle Mensch oder Freier Fortschritt zum Ideale*. A systematic statement of his ideal of complete or “full” humanity: part philosophical treatise, part manifesto for personal and social transformation. [Brümmer, p. 19](https://www.deutschestextarchiv.de/book/show/bruemmer_lexikon03_1913), [Monte Verità Archive](https://gusto-graeser.info/Diefenbach/guttzeit.html)
- **1893:** *Die Macht des Glaubens und des Willens (Über Selbst-Hypnose)*. A short psychological and philosophical tract on belief, volition, and autosuggestion; some secondary lists date it 1917, but an identified second edition appeared in 1897 and the Monte Verità bibliography gives 1893. [Open Library work record](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL44468942W/Die_Macht_des_Glaubens_und_des_Willens_%28Ueber_Selbst-Hypnose%29), [Monte Verità Archive](https://gusto-graeser.info/Diefenbach/guttzeit.html)
- **1893:** *Unsterblichkeit auf Erden: Ein Beitrag zur Versöhnung des Gefühls mit der Einsicht*. A philosophical-religious pamphlet attempting to reconcile an affective desire for immortality with rational inquiry. [Google Books](https://play.google.com/store/books/details/Johannes_Guttzeit_Unsterblichkeit_auf_Erden?id=SZJPAAAAYAAJ), [Guttzeit’s 1922 back-list](https://muvs.org/media/pdf/ein-dunkler-punkt.pdf)
- **1895:** *Himmel und Erde, Hübbe und Egidy oder Mein Reich ist von dieser Welt*. A polemical religious-philosophical tract whose juxtaposed names place it within contemporary reform debates; it is documented in the specialist biographical works list but absent from many shorter selections. [Monte Verità Archive](https://gusto-graeser.info/Diefenbach/guttzeit.html)
- **1896:** *Wer lästert Gott? Nachweis der Seelenvergiftung an Kindern und Volk durch Missbrauch des Alten Testaments*. A religious-critical polemic and manifesto against inculcating the Old Testament in children and the wider public. [Literaturport](https://www.literaturport.de/literaturlandschaft/autoren-berlinbrandenburg/autor/johannes-guttzeit/), [Monte Verità Archive](https://gusto-graeser.info/Diefenbach/guttzeit.html)
- **1898:** *Was will der Naturprediger? 40 Lehrsätze des reinen Menschentums*. A concise manifesto in forty propositions, defining Guttzeit’s “pure humanity”; it was later incorporated as an appendix to *Von der Kirche zur Natur*. [Monte Verità Archive](https://gusto-graeser.info/Diefenbach/guttzeit.html), [Guttzeit’s 1922 back-list](https://muvs.org/media/pdf/ein-dunkler-punkt.pdf)
- **Circa 1890 or by 1900:** *Unsinn und Unmoral im Alten Testament oder Die Blut- und Eisenreligion*. A religious-critical polemic published or serialized in connection with *Der Bruder*. The consulted sources do not settle whether the named object was originally an independent pamphlet, a periodical contribution, or both; that resistance to a single-work description is part of its bibliographical status. [Frecot–Geist–Kerbs reference](https://www.payer.de/religionskritik/panizza09.htm), [Monte Verità Archive](https://gusto-graeser.info/Diefenbach/guttzeit.html)
- **By 1893:** *Zukunfts-Menschlichkeit und Gegenwarts-Philosophistik*. A philosophical and ethical reform tract addressing vegetarianism and animal protection; the specialist literature dates it to 1893, while Guttzeit’s 1922 list confirms its continued independent circulation. [Frecot–Geist–Kerbs reference](https://www.payer.de/religionskritik/panizza09.htm), [Guttzeit’s 1922 back-list](https://muvs.org/media/pdf/ein-dunkler-punkt.pdf)

### Social, educational, and sexual-reform writings

- **1892:** *Das Prügeln in der Schule: Eine Gefahr für Bildung und Sittlichkeit*. An educational-reform pamphlet and practical intervention against corporal punishment in schools. [Brümmer, p. 19](https://www.deutschestextarchiv.de/book/show/bruemmer_lexikon03_1913)
- **1892:** *Über Soldatenmisshandlungen und Beschwerderecht*. An antimilitarist legal and social-reform pamphlet opposing abuse within the army and arguing for soldiers’ right of complaint. [Brümmer, p. 19](https://www.deutschestextarchiv.de/book/show/bruemmer_lexikon03_1913)
- **1893:** *Auch ein heiliger Rock: Vernunft, Geschmack und Gewissen im Kampfe gegen die Mode*. A clothing-reform polemic and manifesto tied to Guttzeit’s own experimental dress; it criticizes fashion rather than merely describing a proposed costume. [Brümmer, p. 19](https://www.deutschestextarchiv.de/book/show/bruemmer_lexikon03_1913), [Monte Verità Archive](https://gusto-graeser.info/Diefenbach/guttzeit.html)
- **1893:** *Spiel und Ernst mit Reformen: Drei Gespräche mit Guttzeit*. A dialogic reform text, staged as three conversations; because “with Guttzeit” can describe a represented speaker as well as an author, its exact authorial construction deserves caution even though it circulates in his back-list. [Monte Verità Archive](https://gusto-graeser.info/Diefenbach/guttzeit.html), [Guttzeit’s 1922 back-list](https://muvs.org/media/pdf/ein-dunkler-punkt.pdf)
- **1893:** *Verbildungsspiegel: Über die moralischen Krankheiten der Zivilisation; eine Vorschule der Wiedergeburt*. A two-volume diagnosis of modern civilization comprising *Band I: Scheinsucht* and *Band II: Verlehrtentum*. It is a composite obra rather than a single undifferentiated pamphlet; it remained in circulation and was reviewed again in 1928. [Frecot–Geist–Kerbs reference](https://www.payer.de/religionskritik/panizza09.htm), [1928 review notice](https://jbc.bj.uj.edu.pl/Content/697228/0041_NDIGCZAS041140_111151708.pdf)
- **1895:** *Reinmenschliche Kindererziehung: Grundzüge einer Gesundheitspflege der Kindesseele*. An educational protocol in the broad sense: it sets out principles for protecting and cultivating the child’s mental and moral life. [Brümmer, p. 19](https://www.deutschestextarchiv.de/book/show/bruemmer_lexikon03_1913), [Monte Verità Archive](https://gusto-graeser.info/Diefenbach/guttzeit.html)
- **1896:** *Meine Ehe: Aufhebung des Gegensatzes zwischen Ehe und freier Liebe*. A personal case history made to act as a sexual- and marital-reform argument; its autobiographical form and manifesto function cannot be cleanly separated. [Monte Verità Archive](https://gusto-graeser.info/Diefenbach/guttzeit.html)
- **1897:** *Edle Sinnlichkeit: Brief an einen jungen Ehemann*. An epistolary sexual-ethics tract, framed as practical counsel to a newly married man. [Monte Verità Archive](https://gusto-graeser.info/Diefenbach/guttzeit.html), [Guttzeit’s 1922 back-list](https://muvs.org/media/pdf/ein-dunkler-punkt.pdf)
- **1897 or 1900:** *Naturrecht oder Verbrechen? Über Liebe zum gleichen Geschlecht (urnische Liebe)*. A homosexual-emancipation pamphlet arguing that same-sex love is natural rather than criminal. Evidence exists for an 1897 printing, whereas prominent works lists assign 1900; the discrepancy may reflect different editions and should not be collapsed into a single certain first-publication date. [Queer.de bibliographical discussion](https://www.queer.de/detail.php?article_id=56338), [Monte Verità Archive](https://gusto-graeser.info/Diefenbach/guttzeit.html)
- **1899:** *Belzebub: Über Willkür und Rache beim Strafen*. A penal-reform polemic against arbitrary and retaliatory punishment. [Monte Verità Archive](https://gusto-graeser.info/Diefenbach/guttzeit.html)
- **1914:** *Ein dunkler Punkt: Die Abtreibung der menschlichen Leibesfrucht; medizinisch, geschichtlich, rechtlich und gesellschaftlich; für Forscher und Volk*. A large interdisciplinary treatise on abortion rather than a brief moral tract; a fifth edition is documented in 1914, so references dating the work simply to 1922 describe a later manifestation, not its secure origin. [Google Books](https://books.google.com/books/about/Ein_dunkler_Punkt.html?id=3UUxAQAAMAAJ), [digitized later edition](https://muvs.org/media/pdf/ein-dunkler-punkt.pdf)
- **1920; later editions by 1922:** *Schamgefühl, Anstand und Sittlichkeit, herkömmlich und natürlich, mit besonderer Berücksichtigung der Nacktheit*. A sexual-ethics and body-reform treatise, also advertised in altered form as *Die falsche Scham oder Schamgefühl, Anstand und Sittlichkeit*. Sources giving 1922 appear to refer to a later illustrated edition; the specialist works list gives 1920. [Monte Verità Archive](https://gusto-graeser.info/Diefenbach/guttzeit.html), [Guttzeit’s 1922 back-list](https://muvs.org/media/pdf/ein-dunkler-punkt.pdf)
- **1921 or earlier:** *Die Verantwortlichkeit des Arztes bei Mißerfolgen*. A medico-legal treatise on professional responsibility for unsuccessful treatment; a June 1921 trade notice establishes that it existed by then. [*Wochenblatt für den deutschen Buchhandel*, 29 June 1921](https://digital.slub-dresden.de/data/kitodo/Brsfded_39946221X-19210629/Brsfded_39946221X-19210629_tif/jpegs/Brsfded_39946221X-19210629.pdf)
- **By 1922:** *Die Tyrannei der Mode: Geschichte eines erfolgreichen persönlichen Kampfes dagegen*. A retrospective clothing-reform narrative and polemic; references assigning 1914 may describe an earlier manifestation, while Guttzeit’s 1922 list advertises a second edition. [Guttzeit’s 1922 back-list](https://muvs.org/media/pdf/ein-dunkler-punkt.pdf), [Brümmer-derived works list](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Friedrich_Guttzeit)
- **By 1922:** *Die falsche Scham*. This title appears in Guttzeit’s own back-list as an alternative or expanded title for *Schamgefühl, Anstand und Sittlichkeit*, not securely as an autonomous obra; it therefore resists treatment as a separate work. [Guttzeit’s 1922 back-list](https://muvs.org/media/pdf/ein-dunkler-punkt.pdf)

### Travel narrative and national-political history

- **1912:** *Auf der Flucht zu Fuß durch Marokko oder Die Hölle von Ain-Sefra*. A travel and escape narrative reportedly constructed from the communications of a former Foreign Legion soldier and later Moroccan officer. That mediation makes Guttzeit’s role closer to compiler or narrative maker than unambiguously first-person traveller. [Contemporary-edition description](https://www.booklooker.de/B%C3%BCcher/Johannes-Guttzeit%2BAuf-der-Flucht-zu-Fu%C3%9F-durch-Marokko-oder-Die-H%C3%B6lle-von-Ain-Sefra/id/A01ExltY01ZZr), [Guttzeit’s 1922 back-list](https://muvs.org/media/pdf/ein-dunkler-punkt.pdf)
- **By 1928:** *Geschichte der deutschen Polenentrechtung: Fingerzeige zur besseren Vertretung des Deutschtums*. A national-political historical argument, documented in a 1928 review notice; the consulted standard pre-1913 bibliography necessarily cannot record it. [1928 review notice](https://jbc.bj.uj.edu.pl/Content/697228/0041_NDIGCZAS041140_111151708.pdf)

### Periodicals and editorial projects

- **1885 onward:** *Der Bruder: Zeitschrift des Bundes für volle Menschlichkeit*. The periodical organ of Guttzeit’s reform association, edited by him and containing his contributions. It is a serial, collective manifestation and movement instrument—not wholly a single-authored “work,” although Guttzeit made its editorial program. Some accounts instead name the associated organ *Zentralblatt für humane Bestrebungen*, and the precise relationship between the two titles remains unresolved in the consulted sources. [Monte Verità Archive](https://gusto-graeser.info/Diefenbach/guttzeit.html), [Frecot–Geist–Kerbs reference](https://www.payer.de/religionskritik/panizza09.htm)
- **1900 or 1901–1903:** *Der Neue Mensch*. A reform periodical created and edited by Guttzeit, with contributions by several other writers; it is therefore an editorial project and collective platform, not a text authored throughout by him. Sources disagree over whether publication began in 1900 or 1901, but agree that it ended in 1903. [Monte Verità Archive](https://gusto-graeser.info/Diefenbach/guttzeit.html), [Frecot–Geist–Kerbs reference](https://www.payer.de/religionskritik/panizza09.htm)

### Associations, embodied designs, and life-reform projects

- **1884 onward:** *Pythagoräer-Bund*, subsequently *Bruder-Bund* and associated with the *Bund für volle Menschlichkeit*. An intentional community or reform association founded by Guttzeit: a social project enacted through membership, preaching, itinerancy, and print rather than a bounded text. The shifting names and organizational succession are not consistently described, so it resists classification as one stable institution. [Monte Verità Archive](https://gusto-graeser.info/Diefenbach/guttzeit.html), [biographical account](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Friedrich_Guttzeit)
- **By 1890:** Guttzeit’s untitled reform costume, later called the *Naturmenschen-Tracht*. A designed and publicly performed mode of dress—an embodied manifesto against fashion—which led to legal proceedings for “gross mischief.” It has no securely fixed authorial title and should not be converted into a fictitious titled artwork. [biographical account](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Friedrich_Guttzeit), [Monte Verità Archive](https://gusto-graeser.info/Diefenbach/guttzeit.html)
- **Late 1880s–1908:** Guttzeit’s itinerant “Naturprediger” practice. A sustained life-work comprising public preaching, lectures, reform dress, travel, and the organization of disciples rather than a single reproducible text. It resists being called an individual work at all, but the unity between doctrine, bodily appearance, and social action makes it an important Gesamtkunstwerk-like project within his production. [Monte Verità Archive](https://gusto-graeser.info/Diefenbach/guttzeit.html), [biographical account](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Friedrich_Guttzeit)
