## Works by Elsa Lindenberg

### Choreographed dances

- **1935:** *På skyggesiden*. An expressionist solo choreographed and performed by Lindenberg at Oslo’s Samfundsteater, portraying an unhappy mother in capitalist crisis society. The title and attribution are documented in Frida Forsgren’s scholarly reconstruction of Lindenberg’s Norwegian work, drawing on Valdemar Hansteen’s standard history of Norwegian ballet ([Forsgren](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/379696288_Den_levende_kroppen_Else_Hagen_og_modernistisk_dans); [Hansteen cited in Roar](https://taju.uniarts.fi/bitstream/handle/10024/6073/Acta_Acenica_44.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)).

- **1935:** *På solsiden*. A companion solo choreographed and performed by Lindenberg, presenting a happy mother in an imagined socialist society; it formed an explicitly political diptych with *På skyggesiden* ([Forsgren](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/379696288_Den_levende_kroppen_Else_Hagen_og_modernistisk_dans)).

- **c. 1935–1939:** *Untitled Labour-movement dance-choir choreography*. Lindenberg made choreographic work with an amateur workers’ group in Oslo, apparently resembling Laban’s movement choirs. The surviving account supplies neither a fixed title nor a secure date and says union leaders rejected its continuation; it may overlap with her Samfundsteater work and therefore resists treatment as a securely separate work ([Roar, citing Hansteen, pp. 141–142](https://taju.uniarts.fi/bitstream/handle/10024/6073/Acta_Acenica_44.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)).

### Choreography for theatre

- **1937:** *H. C. Andersens eventyr*. Choreography for Vera Smirnova’s two-part children’s stage adaptation of H. C. Andersen, directed by Gerda Ring and premiered at Nationaltheatret on 26 December 1937 ([Nationaltheatret production archive](https://forest.nationaltheatret.no/produksjon/c1f58fd4-09e2-47be-915f-3ac0db680198)).

- **1938:** *Faust*. Dance staging—catalogued variously as *dansinnstudering* and choreography—for Nationaltheatret’s production, premiered 8 August 1938. The variation in the institutional records matters: Lindenberg prepared the production’s movement, but the catalogue does not identify a freestanding dance work ([Nationaltheatret person catalogue](https://forest.nationaltheatret.no/person/elsa-lindenberg); [Sceneweb](https://sceneweb.no/nb/artist/89685/Elsa_Lindenberg)).

- **1939:** *Forfengelighetens komedie*. Choreography for Nationaltheatret’s production of Molière, premiered 18 April 1939; Lindenberg also danced in it as part of Tri-balletten. It is consequently both her choreographic contribution to a larger theatrical obra and a performance manifestation, not an independently documented ballet ([Nationaltheatret person catalogue](https://forest.nationaltheatret.no/person/elsa-lindenberg); [Sceneweb](https://sceneweb.no/nb/artist/89685/Elsa_Lindenberg)).

- **1946:** *Stor ståhei – Bryllup – Jubileum – Frieri*. Choreography for a Studioteatret production premiered 6 January 1946. Sceneweb records the credit but gives no evidence that Lindenberg authored the dramatic texts or that the choreography ever had an autonomous title ([Sceneweb](https://sceneweb.no/nb/artist/89685/Elsa_Lindenberg)).

### Collective project

- **1938–1939:** *Tri-balletten*. A free-dance ensemble co-founded by Lindenberg, Gerd Kjølaas, and Signe Hofgaard for the *Vi kan* exhibition and described as Norway’s first independent professional free-dance group. This is best classified as a collaborative project rather than a single choreography: all three women contributed as choreographers, while lack of funds prevented further performances; the sources do not enumerate Lindenberg’s individual numbers ([Sceneweb on Hofgaard](https://sceneweb.no/nb/artist/20093/Signe_Hofgaard); [Danseinformasjonen historical timeline](https://sceneweb.no/nGe78dc1c3ab173605896b7e063qu7hvjlgvUr3q23KggZqLr2Uw2DrAdL2AqrgjkgmAXHVFUXx2gj2325ghjgsBBsG0bPaBw3BjTpuPasbPw2Aw3DzH7w2DSw27w27XPoRahbshsopaw25D1TRMw25D1rsaoPaow25D1Z4ow25D10w25GWw25lSoBw25D1D1knO6iTgjkgXzKw2DmqHZgvnhjA10B0ghvkgmzK23Ig)).

### Embodied practice and pedagogical obra

- **From 1964:** *Spenningsregulering* (“tension regulation”). An embodied somatic and therapeutic practice established under this name by Lindenberg from Elsa Gindler’s *Arbeit am Menschen*, combined in Lindenberg’s teaching with her own Labanian and body-therapeutic experience. It is neither a fixed text nor wholly an individually invented “work”: it is a transmitted, modified protocol of attention and movement whose manifestations were classes, treatments, teacher training, and its continuation through pupils ([Oslo National Academy of the Arts](https://khio.no/en/research/research-in-the-arts/current-projects/to-be-a-body-for-you-on-sensing-and-making-sense-of-another-dancers-life); [Roar](https://taju.uniarts.fi/bitstreams/e7313880-a349-4ec4-83db-8d107983fbcf/download); [Norwegian Gestalt Therapy Association](https://ngfo.no/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Arbok-2023-NGF_web.pdf)).

- **c. 1940–early 1980s:** *Movement, free-dance, and body-therapy teaching*. A long-duration pedagogical obra conducted through private classes, therapy, and training rather than a stable authored object. Accounts differ over whether its earlier phase should be called dance, “vegetative dance,” movement therapy, or preparation for the later *Spenningsregulering* practice; treating every class as an individual work would manufacture a catalogue that the evidence does not support ([Forsgren](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/379696288_Den_levende_kroppen_Else_Hagen_og_modernistisk_dans); [Store norske leksikon](https://snl.no/Elsa_Lindenberg); [Young](https://www.courtenay-young.co.uk/courtenay/articles/Elsa_Lindenberg_and_Reich.pdf)).

### Corpus status

- **Undated:** *Uncatalogued archival choreographies, teaching materials, and traces*. No catalogue raisonné or collected-works edition exists: current specialist research calls Lindenberg’s body of work “largely undocumented,” and her extensive private archive is only now being researched and prepared for transfer to Norway’s National Archives. This is not itself a work, but its resistance to itemization is essential evidence: the entries above are the presently traceable corpus, not proof that Lindenberg made nothing else ([Oslo National Academy of the Arts](https://khio.no/en/research/research-in-the-arts/current-projects/to-be-a-body-for-you-on-sensing-and-making-sense-of-another-dancers-life)).
