## Works by Étienne Bazeries

### Cryptographic apparatus and systems

- **1891:** *Cryptographe à vingt rondelles-alphabets*. A cylindrical cipher apparatus with twenty rotatable alphabet discs, accompanied by a cryptographic procedure; Bazeries presented it to the Association française pour l’avancement des sciences and described it in the association’s proceedings. It was an independent reinvention rather than the first such device: Thomas Jefferson had devised the same principle roughly a century earlier, although his design was then unknown to Bazeries. ([Galland, pp. 18–19](https://files.4in1.ws/Joseph%20S.%20Galland%20-%20An%20Historical%20And%20Analytical%20Bibliography%20Of%20The%20Literature%20Of%20Cryptology%20%281945%29.pdf); [Kruh, pp. 193–208](https://www.cs.umd.edu/~gasarch/TOPICS/miscrypto/jefferson.pdf))

- **1893:** *Chiffre Bazeries*. A cipher system embodied in two enciphering-and-deciphering tables and an instructional fascicle, rather than simply a book; it joined a one-table code vocabulary to a keyed transposition. Bazeries’s own 1901 advertisement distinguishes the introduction, Table no. 1, and Table no. 2 as physical components of the publication. ([Galland, p. 18](https://files.4in1.ws/Joseph%20S.%20Galland%20-%20An%20Historical%20And%20Analytical%20Bibliography%20Of%20The%20Literature%20Of%20Cryptology%20%281945%29.pdf); [Bazeries, 1901, publisher’s list](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/0b/Les_chiffres_secrets_d%C3%A9voil%C3%A9s%2C_%C3%89tienne_Bazeries%2C_1901.pdf))

- **1898:** *Military cipher of Commandant Bazeries*. A hand cipher combining keyed substitution with irregular reversal of groups whose lengths are determined by a numerical key. It survives principally as a system and a body of challenge messages, not as a securely identified standalone publication by Bazeries; Rosario Candela’s later monograph established the conventional name and demonstrated that the supposedly resistant cipher could be solved. ([Candela, bibliographic record](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BA72920879); [American Cryptogram Association, “BAZERIES”](https://www.cryptogram.org/resource-area/cipher-types/))

### Published cryptographic and historical texts

- **1891:** *Cryptographe à 20 rondelles-alphabets (25 lettres par alphabet)*. A six-page conference paper describing the cylinder, published in volume XX of the proceedings of the Association française pour l’avancement des sciences; it is the textual manifestation of the apparatus rather than a second invention. Some later references date the printed proceedings 1892, but Galland and the volume’s session designation assign the communication to Marseille in 1891. ([Galland, p. 18](https://files.4in1.ws/Joseph%20S.%20Galland%20-%20An%20Historical%20And%20Analytical%20Bibliography%20Of%20The%20Literature%20Of%20Cryptology%20%281945%29.pdf); [Kruh, note 24](https://www.cs.umd.edu/~gasarch/TOPICS/miscrypto/jefferson.pdf))

- **1893:** *Tables chiffrantes et déchiffrantes, ou Chiffre Bazeries*. A protocol and working cryptographic kit comprising instructions and two tables. Copies may be catalogued as a pamphlet, but its advertised portfolios and tables resist treatment as an ordinary book; the appended *Application du chiffre secret de M. A. Hermann…* is Hermann’s contribution, not silently attributable to Bazeries. ([Galland, p. 18](https://files.4in1.ws/Joseph%20S.%20Galland%20-%20An%20Historical%20And%20Analytical%20Bibliography%20Of%20The%20Literature%20Of%20Cryptology%20%281945%29.pdf); [Bazeries, 1901, publisher’s list](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/0b/Les_chiffres_secrets_d%C3%A9voil%C3%A9s%2C_%C3%89tienne_Bazeries%2C_1901.pdf))

- **1893:** *Le Masque de fer: Révélation de la correspondance chiffrée de Louis XIV; étude appuyée de documents inédits des Archives du Dépôt de la guerre*. A co-authored historical argument by Émile Burgaud and Bazeries, built around Bazeries’s reconstruction and decipherment of Louis XIV’s Great Cipher. Its identification of Vivien Labbé de Bulonde with the Man in the Iron Mask was disputed immediately: the crucial group 330 occurred only once, so its reading was acknowledged as hypothetical. ([Batiffol’s 1894 scholarly review](https://www.persee.fr/doc/bec_0373-6237_1894_num_55_1_447793_t1_0359_0000_2); [Friedman Collection guide, item 241](https://files.4in1.ws/Rose%20Mary%20Sheldon%20-%20The%20Friedman%20Collection%20Guide.pdf))

- **1896:** *Les Chiffres de Napoléon Ier pendant la campagne de 1813: Épisodes du siège de Hambourg*. A 57-page historical and cryptanalytic study, first appearing through *L’Abeille de Fontainebleau* and also issued as an offprint; it presents decipherments and archival evidence rather than a new cipher. ([Galland, p. 18](https://files.4in1.ws/Joseph%20S.%20Galland%20-%20An%20Historical%20And%20Analytical%20Bibliography%20Of%20The%20Literature%20Of%20Cryptology%20%281945%29.pdf); [Bazeries, 1901, publisher’s list](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/0b/Les_chiffres_secrets_d%C3%A9voil%C3%A9s%2C_%C3%89tienne_Bazeries%2C_1901.pdf))

- **1901:** *Les Chiffres secrets dévoilés: Étude historique sur les chiffres, appuyée de documents inédits tirés des différents dépôts d’archives*. A synthetic historical and practical treatise containing archival decipherments, criticism of contemporary military cryptography, and descriptions of Bazeries’s own systems. It is one obra but also a container for earlier projects and apparatuses, so those constituent creations are listed separately above rather than mistaken for later editions of the book. ([Galland, p. 18](https://files.4in1.ws/Joseph%20S.%20Galland%20-%20An%20Historical%20And%20Analytical%20Bibliography%20Of%20The%20Literature%20Of%20Cryptology%20%281945%29.pdf); [digitized first edition](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/0b/Les_chiffres_secrets_d%C3%A9voil%C3%A9s%2C_%C3%89tienne_Bazeries%2C_1901.pdf))

### Cryptanalytic reconstructions and interventions

- **circa 1890–1893:** *Reconstruction and decipherment of the Great Cipher of Louis XIV*. An untitled, multi-year cryptanalytic project that recovered the syllabic basis of the Rossignols’ nomenclator and produced readable versions of archival dispatches. The reconstruction is a made scholarly instrument, but not wholly Bazeries’s “work” in the ordinary authorial sense: the ciphertexts and original cipher belonged to the seventeenth-century correspondents, while some of Bazeries’s individual readings—most notoriously group 330—remain contested. ([Batiffol’s review](https://www.persee.fr/doc/bec_0373-6237_1894_num_55_1_447793_t1_0359_0000_2); [Galland, p. 18](https://files.4in1.ws/Joseph%20S.%20Galland%20-%20An%20Historical%20And%20Analytical%20Bibliography%20Of%20The%20Literature%20Of%20Cryptology%20%281945%29.pdf))
