## Works by Gustav Arthur "Gusto" Gräser

### Paintings, reliefs, and sculpture

- **1898:** *Mit Gott vereint* (also described as *Ein umgekehrter Paradiesmythos*). A figurative relief, probably executed in repoussé silver; the object is lost and survives only in a photograph Gräser enclosed with a 1900 letter, so its material and present title remain uncertain. [Deutsches Monte Verità Archiv](https://gusto-graeser.info/Leben/Lebenslauf/UmgekehrterParadiesmythos.html)
- **1898/99; substantially altered, probably 1915/16:** *Der Liebe Macht*. A large allegorical oil painting, and apparently the sole surviving painting from a body of visual art Gräser later said he destroyed; contemporary descriptions differ markedly from its present appearance, supporting—but not conclusively proving—the attribution of later revisions to Gräser. [Work documentation](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/body_liebemacht.html) [1899 review and comparison](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/DerLiebeMacht/18990215Siebenbuerger-Tageblatt.htm)
- **1899:** *Aufbruch*. A visual artwork recorded in the estate-based works survey; the survey supplies neither medium nor secure survival history, so it should not be silently treated as another extant painting. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)
- **Date unknown, probably 1890s:** *Portraitplastik*. A small metal portrait bust, probably depicting Gräser’s teacher Karl Wilhelm Diefenbach, preserved at Casa Anatta; the identification is explicitly tentative. [Relief and sculpture documentation](https://gusto-graeser.info/Leben/Lebenslauf/UmgekehrterParadiesmythos.html)
- **1918:** *Mörder* / *Dies ist der Mörder*. A visual-textual composition joining a poem or saying to an image; the archive’s presentation also shows a later Ulrich Holbein collage, which must not be confused with Gräser’s original object. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)

### Manifestos, broadsides, and public interventions

- **1911/12:** *Ein Freund ist da! Mach auf!*. A jointly issued leaflet associated with Willo Rall: less a book than a direct address or manifesto intended to act upon its reader. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)
- **1911/12:** *Heimat*. A leaflet belonging to Gräser’s Berlin series of activist texts; the chronological catalogue groups this material under *Heimatkämpfer*. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html) [Chronological catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Chronologie-Dichtwerke.html)
- **1912:** *Wegweiser ins Menschenland*. The first numbered sequence in the *Heimatkämpfer* series, combining polemical instruction, verse, and appeal rather than functioning simply as a poetry book. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)
- **1912:** *Heimatkämpfer (Wehr und Waffen für Menschen trutz Laffen!!!)*. The second sequence and the title-bearing core of the series; it is a poetic-political manifesto assembled as campaigning print. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)
- **1912:** *Gegengift*. The third sequence of *Heimatkämpfer*, conceived as a verbal countermeasure and therefore closer to manifesto or intervention than descriptive literature. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)
- **1914:** *Der Jahrleiter*. A tear-off calendar devised as a daily sequence of sayings—part literary object, part protocol for living; contemporary testimony confirms that Gräser called the projected calendar a *Jahrleiter*. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html) [Contemporary account](https://gusto-graeser.info/Wirkung/Pressestimmen/1917-DerBund-Gusto.html)

### Published or distributable poem-and-image objects

- **1902:** *Epheublätter* / *Die Wildniss ruft* (also catalogued as *Efeublätter*). An early collection of poems whose title and spelling vary among the archival catalogue and later bibliographies. [Chronological catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Chronologie-Dichtwerke.html)
- **1918:** *Winke zur Genesung unsres Lebens*. A composite of sayings and poems conceived as a “humanity-book” for social and personal renewal—not merely a verse collection, but a manifesto-like healing address. [Chronological catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Chronologie-Dichtwerke.html) [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)
- **1918:** *TAO – das heilende Geheimnis*. A poetic-philosophical sequence made in Stuttgart, Ascona, and Zürich; its familiar book editions are posthumous manifestations of an earlier work and should not be mistaken for its date of creation. [Chronological catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Chronologie-Dichtwerke.html)
- **1925:** *Zeichen des Kommenden*. Seven lithographic images accompanied by text leaves: a portfolio or image-text Gesamtkunstwerk rather than an illustrated book in the ordinary sense. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)
- **1925–26:** *Dem Volke stillgewaltig* / *Notwendwerk*. A portfolio of drawings and poems, issued in lithographic form; the archival catalogue dates the poetic complex to 1925, while bibliographies date the portfolio manifestation to 1926. [Chronological catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Chronologie-Dichtwerke.html) [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)
- **1930:** *Bucheckern*. A graphic writing project and printed specimen built from Gräser’s invented letterforms; it is typography, image, and language-theory at once, and thus resists classification as an ordinary textual publication. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html) [*AllBeDeut* documentation](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Allbedeut/Allbedeut_Auswahl_sw_illu_2.html)

### Manuscript books and poetic composites

- **1896?:** *Der Jahre Buch*. An early manuscript poem-work from Mediasch; the catalogue marks the date with a question mark and preserves it only through a copy, so neither dating nor textual completeness is secure. [Chronological catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Chronologie-Dichtwerke.html)
- **1912 and/or 1922?:** *Notwendworte*. A changing manuscript complex known through fragments and later extracts; the works survey labels fragments “1912” and extracts “1920,” while the chronological catalogue assigns the poetic work a tentative 1922 date. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html) [Chronological catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Chronologie-Dichtwerke.html)
- **1927–30:** *Wortfeuerzeug*. An unpublished assemblage of 16 groups on 175 poster-backed pages, with roughly 10–15 separately pasted sayings or poems per page; the chronological catalogue gives 1931, while the manuscript description dates its formation to 1927–30. [Unpublished-works catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Gusto-Buchtitel-unveroeffentlicht.html) [Chronological catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Chronologie-Dichtwerke.html)
- **1932:** *Wort – Werkzeug zu unsrer Urerbauung*. A poetic work treating language as a tool for remaking human life; the chronological catalogue records it, but the accessible survey gives no physical description or publication status. [Chronological catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Chronologie-Dichtwerke.html)
- **1930s?:** *Geduld*. An untitled manuscript collection subsequently identified by this archival title, comprising 38 pages and 58 poems and sayings; both title and date are therefore cataloguing descriptions rather than securely authorial publication data. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html) [Chronological catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Chronologie-Dichtwerke.html)
- **Around 1933:** *Mut*. A poetic composite listed by the chronological catalogue; its material form and exact date remain undocumented there. [Chronological catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Chronologie-Dichtwerke.html)
- **1930s:** *Winke eines Wirklichkeitfrommen zur Dornenrose als zur Aufheiterung unsres Lebens* (catalogued more briefly as *Winke zur Dornenrose*). An unpublished typescript of about 150 pages in nine chapters, with ink title-pages and individual drawings: a designed poem-book manuscript rather than an edition. [Unpublished-works catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Gusto-Buchtitel-unveroeffentlicht.html) [Chronological catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Chronologie-Dichtwerke.html)
- **Probably 1930s, with additions around 1950:** *Ein Vorschlag, weils genug der Quaal!*. An unpublished, partly typed and partly handwritten work of 96 large-format pages, 24 chapters, and approximately 150 poems; its layered construction makes a single date misleading. [Unpublished-works catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Gusto-Buchtitel-unveroeffentlicht.html)
- **1930s or 1940s:** *Dreibuch Leben* (also catalogued as *Dreiblatt Leben*). An unpublished three-part poetic construction comprising 19 groups, 89 sequences, and about 140 pages; the variant title and broad dating reflect its manuscript status. [Unpublished-works catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Gusto-Buchtitel-unveroeffentlicht.html) [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)
- **Probably 1940s, with later insertions:** *Winke von Grund durch seinen Heitermund*. Twenty groups of typed and handwritten short-form poems, fastened into packets of three or four; it is a modular manuscript object rather than a fixed book. [Unpublished-works catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Gusto-Buchtitel-unveroeffentlicht.html)
- **1945–55 or 1946–56:** *Siebenmahl oder Notwendmahl*. An unfinished handwritten work in seven groups extending to roughly 200 pages; the two archival descriptions disagree slightly about its date range, and its incompletion is integral to what it is. [Unpublished-works catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Gusto-Buchtitel-unveroeffentlicht.html) [Chronological catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Chronologie-Dichtwerke.html)
- **After 1945:** *AllBeDeut: Unsere Sprachlaute – heimliche Schlüssel zum Aufschluss unsrer Welt*. An unpublished inquiry into sound symbolism, universal language, and Gräser’s *Bucheckern* lettering; it combines language philosophy, graphic design, and poetic play and is therefore best treated as a Gesamtkunstwerk of script. [*AllBeDeut* documentation](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Allbedeut/Allbedeut_Auswahl_sw_illu_2.html)
- **Around 1950:** *Brieflein Wohlgemut*. A late poetic “little letter” preserved in the Freudenstein estate; whether it was conceived as correspondence, book, or distributable address is not clarified by the catalogue. [Chronological catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Chronologie-Dichtwerke.html)
- **1955/56:** *Brieflein Wunderbar*. A late poetic composite made in Munich and preserved at Freudenstein; its diminutive “letter” form makes it an address to a reader rather than an ordinary book-length collection. [Chronological catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Chronologie-Dichtwerke.html)
- **After 1955:** *Denkzettel*. A fragmentary handwritten work of about 40 large-format pages, arranged into 40 groups and furnished with several monochrome and coloured title-pages; the chronological catalogue’s description of “about 100 poems” and the manuscript catalogue’s group count are not equivalent measures of completeness. [Unpublished-works catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Gusto-Buchtitel-unveroeffentlicht.html) [Chronological catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Chronologie-Dichtwerke.html)
- **Around 1957:** *Fünklein, flieg!*. A late manuscript poem-work; one catalogue gives “1956?” and another “around 1957,” so its exact dating is unresolved. [Unpublished-works catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Gusto-Buchtitel-unveroeffentlicht.html) [Chronological catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Chronologie-Dichtwerke.html)
- **Around 1958:** *TRäumers Frucht oder Notwendmahl oder WErde!* (also called *Notwendwerk* in archival commentary). An unfinished work on approximately 90 square sheets, handwritten diagonally; its multiple competing titles and incomplete state make the project, rather than any stabilized text, the obra. [Unpublished-works catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Gusto-Buchtitel-unveroeffentlicht.html) [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)
- **Date unknown:** *Das blühende Büchlein*. A lost poetic work known only from the catalogue; no fixed date, contents, or surviving object is documented. [Chronological catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Chronologie-Dichtwerke.html)
- **Date unknown:** *Mappen (“Notwendworte”)*. A set of manuscript folders in the Monacensia rather than a single securely bounded text; it resists being called one work because the catalogue itself describes containers or groupings. [Chronological catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Chronologie-Dichtwerke.html)
- **Date unknown:** *Sonndonnerdrein*. A poetic manuscript preserved at Freudenstein; the catalogue supplies no date or adequate material description. [Chronological catalogue](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Chronologie-Dichtwerke.html)

### Archival constellations and unfinished projects

- **Date unknown:** *In Herz Behaupten*. A book manuscript identified in the estate survey; it should be distinguished from the incomplete manuscript letter *Herz behaupten* addressed to Hans Fischer around 1943. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)
- **Around 1943:** *Herz behaupten*. An incomplete manuscript letter to Hans Fischer; it is correspondence moving toward poetic work, and its resistance to classification as an autonomous “work” should be retained. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)
- **Date unknown:** *Eigenfrucht*. Drafts and fragments responding to numerous authors; a working archive or intellectual project rather than a finished authored text. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)
- **Date unknown:** *Bildung*. Drafts toward a collection, not a completed collection; the title names an archival project whose intended final shape is unknown. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)
- **Date unknown:** *Land – Tier*. Poem drafts and other fragments gathered under a thematic heading; its unity may be archival rather than authorial. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)
- **Date unknown:** *Kampf*. A manuscript poetry collection, distinct from individual poems and sections bearing the same recurrent word. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)
- **Date unknown:** *Wirklichkeit*. Drafts and fragments rather than a completed work; the title is also used elsewhere for individual leaves and sections. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)
- **Date unknown:** *Freiheit*. A body of fragments whose status as one authored work is uncertain. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)
- **Date unknown:** *Not*. Fragments, poems, and quotations gathered under a recurring thematic word; this is an archival constellation rather than demonstrably a finished work. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)
- **Date unknown:** *Waltung*. Drafts and fragments for an unrealized or unfinished poetic project. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)
- **Date unknown:** *Wortwalter*. Fragments and commentary, apparently constituting a language project rather than a stabilized text. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)
- **Date unknown:** *Sprichwortleben*. Poems and drafts organized around the remaking of proverbial speech; the surviving materials do not establish a completed book. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)
- **Date unknown:** *Zeichen des Kommenden: 7 Gedichte*. A manuscript sequence bearing the same title as the 1925 lithographic portfolio; the source does not establish whether it is that object’s textual component, a preparatory state, or a separate manifestation. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)
- **Date unknown:** *Fegfieber brenn!*. Large-format text sheets known in the Freudenstein archive through copies of Monacensia originals; their relationship to a finished work is unspecified. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)
- **Date unknown:** *Hochzeit*. A manuscript poetry collection; because Gräser also used this title for individual poems, the collection cannot automatically be identified with every surviving *Hochzeit* text. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)
- **Date unknown:** *Kind*. A manuscript poetry collection recorded without a secure date or description of completeness. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)
- **1950s:** *Leben ohne Mark*. Drafts and quotations from Gräser’s Munich-Freimann years; it is surviving workshop material rather than a demonstrably finished work. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)
- **Date unknown:** *Schatten der Lebendigen*. An anthology-like assemblage of texts by other writers that Gräser selected, clipped, or copied; the making lies in compilation and arrangement, not in authorship of all its words. [Works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)

### Built environments, communal projects, and performed life

- **1900 onward:** *Monte Verità*. Gräser was one of the small group that founded the settlement on Monte Monescia, but the place is a collective social and ecological project, not his individual work; his obra within it was the attempted inseparability of dwelling, diet, clothing, poetry, dance, and social reform. [Monte Verità documentation](https://gusto-graeser.info/Monteverita/monteverita_index.html)
- **Early 1900s:** *Gräserhaus and grotto on Monte Verità*. A dwelling and landscape of retreat associated with Gusto and Karl Gräser; construction was collaborative, so individual authorship of the building should not be assigned exclusively to Gusto. [Architectural study](https://www.gusto-graeser.info/Monteverita/Graeserhaus/Graeserhaus_files/Graeserhaus.pdf)
- **1907–08 onward:** *Public poetry, lecture, and dance appearances*. Gräser presented poems, talks, and dances as embodied events rather than separable literary products; the individual performances are incompletely documented, making the practice—a performed Gesamtkunstwerk of body, speech, costume, and movement—more securely identifiable than a definitive event list. [Literaturportal Bayern biography](https://www.literaturportal-bayern.de/autorenlexikon?autorenlexikon%3Ftask=letters.default&letter=A&oid=ODB_A00000900&pnd=118541285&task=lpbauthor.default) [Performance documentation](https://gusto-graeser.info/Leben/Darstellungen/darstellungen_index.html)
- **Date and site uncertain; envisioned through 1956:** *Ringhort*. A projected communal “temple” or gathering place, variously associated with the Engadin and Stuttgart; it remained an intention repeatedly reformulated rather than a securely documented completed building, so the project itself is the work. [*Ringhort* documentation](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/Ringhort/Ringhort.html)
- **Adult life, especially the wandering years:** *The wandering “nature prophet” persona*. Gräser’s dress, vegetarian practice, itinerancy, speech, poetry, and bodily presence were deliberately fused, but calling his whole life an authored artwork risks converting biography into an object; it is better recorded as a lived manifestation that resists the category of “work.” [Biographical and works documentation](https://gusto-graeser.info/Leben/Lebenslauf/lebenslauf_index.html) [Deutsches Monte Verità Archiv works survey](https://gusto-graeser.info/Werk/werk_index.html)
