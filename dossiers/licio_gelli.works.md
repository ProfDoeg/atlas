## Works by Licio Gelli

### Memoirs, chronicles, and autobiographical narratives

- **1940:** *Fuoco! Cronache legionarie dell’insurrezione antibolscevica di Spagna*. A memoir-chronicle of Gelli’s service with the Italian Fascist expeditionary force in Spain. Some bibliographies date its first appearance to 1939; the surviving Pistoia imprint and later bibliographies give 1940, while 1991 and 2003 issues are reprints rather than new works. [Archivio di Stato di Pistoia](https://archiviodistatopistoia.cultura.gov.it/fileadmin/risorse/inventari_pt/PCI-_sezione_Biagini_Cotti_inv_125.pdf); [bibliographical résumé](https://www.poetinellasocieta.it/public/0309.pdf)
- **1944:** *E la morte a paro a paro*. A wartime narrative or memoir; an authorial bibliography lists it as a separate volume but supplies no subtitle, and some later summaries incorrectly place it in 1940. [Bibliographical résumé](https://www.poetinellasocieta.it/public/0309.pdf); [author’s publication list](https://www.club.it/autori/licio.gelli/indice-i.html)
- **1950:** *Anno del gran perdono e ritorno*. A prose narrative whose exact genre is not consistently identified; it also appears as *Giorno del gran perdono e ritorno* in an early authorial list, so the title is bibliographically unstable. [Bibliographical résumé](https://www.poetinellasocieta.it/public/0309.pdf); [author’s publication list](https://www.club.it/autori/licio.gelli/indice-i.html)
- **1987 or 1989:** *La verità*. An autobiographical self-defence: bibliographies disagree between 1987 and the 1989 Demetra edition, and the discrepancy should not be silently converted into a single date. [Bibliographical résumé](https://www.poetinellasocieta.it/public/0309.pdf); [archival bibliography](https://liciogelli.blogspot.com/2007/12/libri-e-poesie-di-licio-gelli.html)
- **1991:** *Banco Ambrosiano: una favola triste all’italiana*. A polemical narrative about the Banco Ambrosiano affair, also catalogued in shortened form as *Una storia triste all’italiana*. It sits uneasily between memoir, apologia, and political intervention. [Archival bibliography](https://liciogelli.blogspot.com/2007/12/libri-e-poesie-di-licio-gelli.html); [author’s publication list](https://www.club.it/autori/licio.gelli/indice-i.html)
- **1995:** *Dossier Spagna: gli italiani nella guerra civile, 1936–1939*. A historical dossier and retrospective memoir made with Antonio Lenoci, not solely by Gelli; it revisits the material of *Fuoco!* rather than merely reprinting it. [Bibliographical résumé](https://www.poetinellasocieta.it/public/0309.pdf)
- **1997:** *Lettera a Wanda*. A book-length personal letter or epistolary memoir addressed to his wife; its classification as “narrative” in the bibliographical résumé does not erase its status as an enacted address. [Bibliographical résumé](https://www.poetinellasocieta.it/public/0309.pdf); [archival bibliography](https://liciogelli.blogspot.com/2007/12/libri-e-poesie-di-licio-gelli.html)
- **2016, posthumous:** *La mia verità*. A posthumously issued autobiographical text. Its publication after Gelli’s death makes the degree of editorial intervention material; the accessible bibliographies do not establish whether it was a finished manuscript, an edited interview, or a publisher-shaped compilation. [Bibliographic listing](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licio_Gelli#Opere)

### Instructional and commercial protocols

- *Materie energetiche*. A technical or commercial instructional volume named in Gelli’s early publication list; no secure date is supplied there, and its precise contents and authorship history remain insufficiently documented. [Author’s publication list](https://www.club.it/autori/licio.gelli/indice-i.html)
- **1952:** *Nozioni commerciali: settore vendita*. A sales-training manual: a protocol in the practical sense, intended to instruct commercial conduct rather than narrate or commemorate it. [Archival bibliography](https://liciogelli.blogspot.com/2007/12/libri-e-poesie-di-licio-gelli.html)
- **1962:** *L’arte del vendere*. A sales manual and behavioral protocol; one early list instead calls it *L’arte del vendere*, while later summaries normalize the wording to *L’arte di vendere*. [Bibliographical résumé](https://www.poetinellasocieta.it/public/0309.pdf); [author’s publication list](https://www.club.it/autori/licio.gelli/indice-i.html)
- **1990 or 1991:** *Come arrivare al successo*. A prescriptive self-improvement and business manual. The bibliographies disagree between 1990 and 1991, possibly reflecting separate issues rather than distinct works. [Archival bibliography](https://liciogelli.blogspot.com/2007/12/libri-e-poesie-di-licio-gelli.html); [bibliographical résumé](https://www.poetinellasocieta.it/public/0309.pdf)

### Political projects, manifestos, and operational documents

- **c. 1975–1976:** *Piano di rinascita democratica*. An operational political project—part manifesto, part protocol, part feasibility plan—for institutional, judicial, media, and economic change. The parliamentary inquiry treated it as expressing the P2 program but concluded that Gelli probably did not personally draft its legally specialized language; intellectual sponsorship, custody, and use are therefore clearer than sole textual authorship. [Commissione parlamentare d’inchiesta sulla Loggia P2](https://inchieste.camera.it/p2/documenti.html?leg=09&legLabel=IX+legislatura); [Relazione Anselmi](https://www.genovaweb.org/doc/relazione_anselmi_loggia_P2.pdf)
- **c. 1975–1976:** *Memorandum sulla situazione politica in Italia*. A political memorandum transmitted with the *Piano di rinascita democratica*. It belongs to the same collective P2 project and likewise resists treatment as an securely autographed “work by Gelli”: it was seized from his daughter’s possession, but possession and programmatic authority do not prove solitary composition. [Parliamentary-document provenance](https://www.stragi.it/pianorinascita); [Relazione Anselmi](https://www.genovaweb.org/doc/relazione_anselmi_loggia_P2.pdf)
- *Ombre sull’istruttoria dell’Ambrosiano*. A polemical dossier concerning the Banco Ambrosiano investigation. The author’s early list establishes the title but gives no dependable date; the text acts as forensic self-defence and public intervention rather than detached description. [Author’s publication list](https://www.club.it/autori/licio.gelli/indice-i.html)
- *Interviste*. A collected-interview volume named in Gelli’s early bibliography. It resists simple authorship because an interview is made jointly by questioner, respondent, editor, and publisher; neither date nor contributors are established by the accessible list. [Author’s publication list](https://www.club.it/autori/licio.gelli/indice-i.html)
- **2001:** *La verità dei giudici sulla loggia P2*. A documentary-apologetic compilation about judicial findings on P2; its title claims evidentiary authority, making it as much a forensic intervention as a book. [Archival bibliography](https://liciogelli.blogspot.com/2007/12/libri-e-poesie-di-licio-gelli.html)

### Fiction, stories, and aphoristic prose

- **1991:** *Racconti e storie*. A collection of short prose narratives. [Archival bibliography](https://liciogelli.blogspot.com/2007/12/libri-e-poesie-di-licio-gelli.html)
- **1992:** *Il ritorno di Gesù*. A novel or extended religious fiction. [Bibliographic listing](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licio_Gelli#Opere)
- **1994:** *Lo strizzacervelli*. A collection classified variously as aphorisms or nonfiction rather than narrative; that instability is part of the object’s status. [Bibliographic listing](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licio_Gelli#Opere); [bibliographical résumé](https://www.poetinellasocieta.it/public/0309.pdf)
- **1995:** *C’era una volta messer Leonardo*. A short story or illustrated literary narrative concerning Leonardo da Vinci, not securely documented as a novel-length work. [Archival bibliography](https://liciogelli.blogspot.com/2007/12/libri-e-poesie-di-licio-gelli.html); [bibliographical résumé](https://www.poetinellasocieta.it/public/0309.pdf)
- **1995:** *Gli ultimi cavalieri*. A novel. [Bibliographic listing](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licio_Gelli#Opere)
- **1999:** *Il potere dei Vis*. A prose work classed as narrative in the contemporary bibliography; “Vis” is the attested title word and should not be silently emended to *vizi*. [Italian national publications index](https://biblioteche.cultura.gov.it/export/sites/dgbid/it/documenti/rop_2000_new.pdf); [bibliographical résumé](https://www.poetinellasocieta.it/public/0309.pdf)
- **1999:** *Il falco*. A prose work registered under Gelli’s name, but the consulted bibliographies do not consistently specify whether it is a story, novel, or essay. [Italian national publications index](https://biblioteche.cultura.gov.it/export/sites/dgbid/it/documenti/rop_2000_new.pdf); [archival bibliography](https://liciogelli.blogspot.com/2007/12/libri-e-poesie-di-licio-gelli.html)

### Poetry collections and poetic sequences

- **1959; revised or republished 1971:** *Luce di stelle alpine*. Gelli’s first poetry collection, initially privately issued and later republished; “1959–71” describes the work’s publication history, not necessarily a continuous period of composition. [Archival bibliography](https://liciogelli.blogspot.com/2007/12/libri-e-poesie-di-licio-gelli.html); [bibliographic listing](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licio_Gelli#Opere)
- **1990:** *Poesie del silenzio*. A poetry collection; some listings give the variant article *Le poesie del silenzio*. [Archival bibliography](https://liciogelli.blogspot.com/2007/12/libri-e-poesie-di-licio-gelli.html); [author’s publication list](https://www.club.it/autori/licio.gelli/indice-i.html)
- **1991 or 1992:** *L’albero delle poesie*. A poetry collection; the bibliographies disagree by one year. [Bibliographic listing](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licio_Gelli#Opere); [archival bibliography](https://liciogelli.blogspot.com/2007/12/libri-e-poesie-di-licio-gelli.html)

- **1993:** *Il cassetto delle poesie*. A poetry collection; one later résumé dates it to 1992, but the archival bibliography gives 1993. [Archival bibliography](https://liciogelli.blogspot.com/2007/12/libri-e-poesie-di-licio-gelli.html); [bibliographical résumé](https://www.poetinellasocieta.it/public/0309.pdf)

- **1994:** *Canzone per Wanda*. A book-length poetic sequence or collection, despite “canzone” in the title; it is not documented as a musical composition. [Archival bibliography](https://liciogelli.blogspot.com/2007/12/libri-e-poesie-di-licio-gelli.html)

- **1994:** *Uomini dal cielo*. A poetic work, although one contemporary résumé places it among narrative titles; its genre is therefore not bibliographically settled. ### Collected and archival constructions

- **2004:** *Opera omnia*. A collected-works construction credited jointly to Licio Gelli and Paolo Borruto. It is a new editorial object but not a single newly composed text, and its claim to completeness is limited by works published after 2004. [Bibliographic listing](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licio_Gelli#Opere)
- **2005; revised issue 2006:** *Catalogo della “donazione Licio Gelli” all’Archivio di Stato di Pistoia*. An author-arranged catalogue of the archive he donated: simultaneously finding aid, self-portrait, and monument to his own corpus. Its order is not neutral, since the archive arrived with an inventory prepared by Gelli himself. [SIAS finding-aid record](https://sias-archivi.cultura.gov.it/cgi-bin/pagina.pl?Chiave=33665&RicProgetto=as-pistoia&TipoPag=strumcorr); [SIAS fonds description](https://sias-archivi.cultura.gov.it/cgi-bin/pagina.pl?Chiave=430554&TipoPag=comparc)
- **2008:** *Dizionario poetico: antologia composta da 2535 poesie, 1950–2008*. A massive alphabetically or lexically conceived anthology: less a conventional collection than a self-curated poetic corpus. It claims 2,535 poems, but that figure is the anthology’s own completeness claim and has not been independently reconciled with all earlier volumes. [Bibliographic listing](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licio_Gelli#Opere)

### Dispersed writings and resistant bodies of work

- **Dates not exhaustively established:** articles for *Il Ferruccio*, *Il Piave*, *Mondo Libero*, *La Gazzetta di San Severo*, *I Giorni*, *Cultura e Ambiente*, *Flash*, *Alfa Omega*, *Corriere di Roma*, and *Oggifuturo*. This is a dispersed journalistic body of work rather than one obra; the archival descriptions confirm its existence but do not supply an item-level scholarly bibliography, so pretending to enumerate every article would manufacture completeness. [Archival bibliography](https://liciogelli.blogspot.com/2007/12/libri-e-poesie-di-licio-gelli.html); [Archivio di Stato fonds description](https://sias-archivi.cultura.gov.it/cgi-bin/pagina.pl?Chiave=430554&TipoPag=comparc)
- **1950–2008:** approximately 2,535 individually titled poems gathered in *Dizionario poetico*, with additional claims elsewhere that Gelli wrote “more than 2,500” poems. They constitute a body of works as well as components of collections, but the consulted institutional descriptions do not expose a reliable item-level table of contents; consequently, the poems can be named collectively here but not responsibly fabricated into a title-by-title list. [Bibliographic listing](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licio_Gelli#Opere); [Archivio di Stato fonds description](https://sias-archivi.cultura.gov.it/cgi-bin/pagina.pl?Chiave=430554&TipoPag=comparc)
