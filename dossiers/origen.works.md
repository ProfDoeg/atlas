## Works by Origen of Alexandria

### Text-critical project

- **Begun c. 220; continued for decades:** *Hexapla*. An enormous synoptic edition of the Hebrew Bible and its Greek versions, normally arranged in six columns and expanded for some books; it is better classified as an unfinished or continuously revised scholarly project than as an ordinary book. The master copy is lost, but fragments and derivative witnesses survive; whether the four-column *Tetrapla* was a distinct earlier or abbreviated work remains disputed. ([Encyclopedia.com](https://www.encyclopedia.com/people/philosophy-and-religion/philosophy-biographies/origen); [Hexapla Institute](https://hexapla.org/origens-hexapla-the-hexapla-institute-and-deep-work/); [Catholic Encyclopedia](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07316a.htm))

### Continuous scriptural commentaries

- **Before 231:** *Commentary on Genesis*. A continuous commentary of twelve or thirteen books, according to conflicting ancient catalogues, extending only through the early chapters of Genesis; only fragments survive. ([Eusebius, *Ecclesiastical History* 6.24](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Nicene_and_Post-Nicene_Fathers:_Series_II/Volume_I/Church_History_of_Eusebius/Book_VI/Chapter_24); [Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Before 231:** *Commentary on Isaiah*. A thirty-six-book commentary, now fragmentary; its existence and extent are recorded in Jerome’s catalogue. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Before 231:** *Commentary on the Psalms*. A sequence of commentaries begun with Psalms 1–25 in Alexandria and subsequently extended: Jerome lists single- or multi-book treatments of Psalms 1–16, 20, 24, 29, 38, 40, 43–46, 50–53, 57–59, 62–65, 68, 70–72, and 103. Only fragments survive, and the ancient numerical evidence does not establish that Origen completed a continuous commentary on the entire Psalter. ([Eusebius, *Ecclesiastical History* 6.24](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Nicene_and_Post-Nicene_Fathers:_Series_II/Volume_I/Church_History_of_Eusebius/Book_VI/Chapter_24); [Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Before 231:** *Commentary on Lamentations*. A five-book continuous exposition, known principally through fragments and ancient notices. ([Eusebius, *Ecclesiastical History* 6.24](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Nicene_and_Post-Nicene_Fathers:_Series_II/Volume_I/Church_History_of_Eusebius/Book_VI/Chapter_24))

- **Alexandrian period, begun after c. 218; continued at Caesarea:** *Commentary on John*. Origen’s first major commentary for his patron Ambrose: the catalogue gives thirty-two books, although Eusebius knew twenty-two and the extant thirty-second book reaches only John 13. Nine books, substantial portions of another, and fragments survive; the disagreement concerns transmission and extent, not the work’s authenticity. ([Eusebius, *Ecclesiastical History* 6.24](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Nicene_and_Post-Nicene_Fathers:_Series_II/Volume_I/Church_History_of_Eusebius/Book_VI/Chapter_24); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/origen/))

- **Alexandrian period:** *Commentary on Hosea concerning Ephraim*. A one-book exposition separately identified by Jerome; it is lost apart from possible fragments. Its relation to Origen’s more general commentary on Hosea is uncertain. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Commentary on Hosea*. A continuous exposition distinct in Jerome’s list from the book “concerning Ephraim”; only fragments survive. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/); [Schaff-Herzog Encyclopedia](https://www.ccel.org/s/schaff/encyc08/htm/ii.viii.htm))

- **Date unknown:** *Commentary on Joel*. A two-book commentary, now lost except for fragments or later excerpts. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Commentary on Amos*. A six-book commentary, now lost except for fragments or later excerpts. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Commentary on Jonah*. A one-book commentary, now lost except for fragments or later excerpts. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Commentary on Micah*. A three-book commentary, now lost except for fragments or later excerpts. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Commentary on Nahum*. A two-book commentary, now lost except for fragments or later excerpts. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Commentary on Habakkuk*. A three-book commentary, now lost except for fragments or later excerpts. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Commentary on Zephaniah*. A two-book commentary, now lost except for fragments or later excerpts. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Commentary on Haggai*. A one-book commentary, now lost except for fragments or later excerpts. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Commentary on the Beginning of Zechariah*. A two-book but explicitly incomplete exposition of Zechariah; the project’s restricted title records its unfinished biblical coverage. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Commentary on Malachi*. A two-book commentary, now lost except for fragments or later excerpts. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Commentary on Ezekiel*. A twenty-nine-book commentary according to Jerome, although another ancient witness describes twenty-five books; only excerpts, including material from Book 20, survive. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/); [Schaff-Herzog Encyclopedia](https://www.ccel.org/s/schaff/encyc08/htm/ii.viii.htm))

- **Date unknown:** *Commentary on Proverbs*. A three-book commentary, now lost except for fragments. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Youthful work; before the mature commentary:** *Early Commentary on the Song of Songs*. A two-volume exposition which Jerome distinguishes from the later ten-book commentary; little survives, and its exact title and relation to the mature work are uncertain. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/); [Schaff-Herzog Encyclopedia](https://www.ccel.org/s/schaff/encyc08/htm/ii.viii.htm))

- **c. 240s:** *Commentary on the Song of Songs*. A ten-book commentary and one of Origen’s major exegetical works; substantial portions survive chiefly through Rufinus’s Latin translation, with Greek fragments. ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/origen/); [Catholic Encyclopedia](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11306b.htm))

- **Date unknown:** *Commentary on Certain Problems in Proverbs*. A separate problem-oriented work, known only from Jerome’s catalogue and now lost. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Commentary on Luke*. A fifteen-book continuous commentary, distinct from the surviving homilies; it is lost apart from fragments. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **c. 244–249:** *Commentary on Matthew*. A twenty-five-book commentary written late in Origen’s life. Eight books survive in Greek and a further section in an anonymous Latin version; the transmission is incomplete and sometimes abbreviated. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/); [Catholic Encyclopedia](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11306b.htm))

- **c. 240s:** *Commentary on Romans*. A fifteen-book commentary surviving substantially in Rufinus’s shortened and editorially altered Latin translation, supplemented by Greek fragments and papyrus remains. The surviving object is therefore not identical with Origen’s original composition. ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/origen/); [Schaff-Herzog Encyclopedia](https://www.ccel.org/s/schaff/encyc08/htm/ii.viii.htm))

- **Date unknown:** *Commentary on Galatians*. A five-book commentary according to manuscript evidence and another notice by Jerome; the figure of fifteen in Jerome’s catalogue is generally treated as a textual error. Only fragments survive. ([Jerome’s catalogue and annotations](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/); [Schaff-Herzog Encyclopedia](https://www.ccel.org/s/schaff/encyc08/htm/ii.viii.htm))

- **Date unknown:** *Commentary on Ephesians*. A three-book commentary, now fragmentary. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Commentary on Philippians*. A one-book commentary extending at least to Philippians 4:1; only fragments survive. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/); [Schaff-Herzog Encyclopedia](https://www.ccel.org/s/schaff/encyc08/htm/ii.viii.htm))

- **Date unknown:** *Commentary on Colossians*. Jerome’s catalogue assigns it two books, while manuscript evidence indicates three; only fragments survive. ([Jerome’s catalogue and annotations](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Commentary on First Thessalonians*. A three-book commentary, now fragmentary; Jerome preserves a substantial Latin excerpt from its third book. ([Jerome’s catalogue and annotations](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Commentary on Second Thessalonians*. A one-book commentary, now lost or fragmentary. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Commentary on Titus*. A one-book commentary, now lost or fragmentary. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Commentary on Philemon*. A one-book commentary, now lost or fragmentary. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

### Scholia and short exegetical notes

- **Date unknown:** *Scholia on Genesis*. Brief exegetical notes rather than a continuous commentary; their existence is known from fragmentary evidence, but they cannot always be separated securely from excerpts of other works. ([Oxford Handbook discussion](https://gnosis.study/library/%D0%93%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B7%D0%B8%D1%81/%2B%20%D0%9D%D0%B5%20%D1%82%D0%BE%D0%BB%D1%8C%D0%BA%D0%BE%20%D0%BE%20%D0%B3%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B7%D0%B8%D1%81%D0%B5/ENG/The%20Oxford%20Handbook%20of%20Early%20Christian%20Biblical%20Interpretation.pdf))

- **Date unknown:** *Scholia on Exodus*. A collection of concise philological and exegetical notes, now lost except for possible fragments. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Scholia on Leviticus*. A collection of concise exegetical notes, now lost except for possible fragments. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Scholia on Numbers*. Brief notes attested by fragmentary evidence, although absent from the surviving portion of Jerome’s principal list. ([Oxford Handbook discussion](https://gnosis.study/library/%D0%93%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B7%D0%B8%D1%81/%2B%20%D0%9D%D0%B5%20%D1%82%D0%BE%D0%BB%D1%8C%D0%BA%D0%BE%20%D0%BE%20%D0%B3%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B7%D0%B8%D1%81%D0%B5/ENG/The%20Oxford%20Handbook%20of%20Early%20Christian%20Biblical%20Interpretation.pdf))

- **Date unknown:** *Scholia on Deuteronomy*. Brief notes attested by fragmentary evidence; their original boundaries are uncertain. ([Oxford Handbook discussion](https://gnosis.study/library/%D0%93%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B7%D0%B8%D1%81/%2B%20%D0%9D%D0%B5%20%D1%82%D0%BE%D0%BB%D1%8C%D0%BA%D0%BE%20%D0%BE%20%D0%B3%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B7%D0%B8%D1%81%D0%B5/ENG/The%20Oxford%20Handbook%20of%20Early%20Christian%20Biblical%20Interpretation.pdf))

- **Date unknown:** *Scholia on Isaiah*. A collection of short notes, distinct from the thirty-six-book commentary and now almost wholly lost. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Scholia on Psalms 1–15*. A short-note exposition specifically catalogued for the first fifteen Psalms; one ancient variant may have read “twenty-five.” ([Jerome’s catalogue and annotations](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Scholia on the Whole Psalter*. A broader collection listed separately from the notes on Psalms 1–15 and from the continuous commentaries; its original shape and surviving fragments cannot be securely reconstructed. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/); [Encyclopedia.com](https://www.encyclopedia.com/people/philosophy-and-religion/philosophy-biographies/origen))

- **Date unknown:** *Scholia on Ecclesiastes*. A collection of short exegetical notes, almost entirely lost. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Scholia on John*. A one-book collection of notes on selected passages, distinct from the large commentary and now fragmentary. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Scholia*. Jerome’s catalogue ends by mentioning further scholia without identifying their biblical subjects. This resists treatment as a securely bounded work: it may denote unidentified collections, loose notes, or a damaged catalogue heading. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

### Homiletic manifestations

- **Mostly c. 238–244:** *Homilies on Genesis*. A preached series conventionally numbered at seventeen in Jerome’s catalogue; sixteen are securely transmitted in Rufinus’s Latin, while the authenticity or textual independence of the seventeenth is disputed. As stenographically recorded preaching, the corpus lies between oral event and authored text. ([Jerome’s catalogue and annotations](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/origen/))

- **Mostly c. 238–244:** *Homilies on Exodus*. Jerome lists eight, but thirteen survive in Rufinus’s Latin translation, showing that the ancient catalogue is incomplete. ([Jerome’s catalogue and annotations](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/); [Schaff-Herzog Encyclopedia](https://www.ccel.org/s/schaff/encyc08/htm/ii.viii.htm))

- **Mostly c. 238–244:** *Homilies on Leviticus*. Jerome’s transmitted numeral appears as eleven, while sixteen Latin homilies survive and other older inventories count eighteen; the size of Origen’s original series is therefore unsettled. ([Jerome’s catalogue and annotations](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/); [Schaff-Herzog Encyclopedia](https://www.ccel.org/s/schaff/encyc08/htm/ii.viii.htm))

- **Mostly c. 238–244:** *Homilies on Numbers*. A twenty-eight-homily series surviving principally through Rufinus’s Latin translation. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/); [Schaff-Herzog Encyclopedia](https://www.ccel.org/s/schaff/encyc08/htm/ii.viii.htm))

- **Mostly c. 238–244:** *Homilies on Deuteronomy*. A thirteen-homily series known from the ancient catalogue but otherwise lost or fragmentary. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Mostly c. 238–244:** *Homilies on Joshua*. Jerome lists twenty-six; sixteen survive substantially in Rufinus’s Latin translation. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/); [Schaff-Herzog Encyclopedia](https://www.ccel.org/s/schaff/encyc08/htm/ii.viii.htm))

- **Mostly c. 238–244:** *Homilies on Judges*. A nine-homily series surviving in Latin translation. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/); [Schaff-Herzog Encyclopedia](https://www.ccel.org/s/schaff/encyc08/htm/ii.viii.htm))

- **Date unknown:** *Homilies on Passover*. An eight-homily series listed by Jerome and now lost; it is not necessarily identical with the later-discovered prose *Treatise on the Passover*. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Homilies on First Kings (First Samuel)*. Jerome lists four. A Greek homily on 1 Samuel 28, concerning the medium of Endor, survives and is conventionally identified as the fifth homily in another arrangement, exposing uncertainty in the series’ numbering and boundaries. ([Jerome’s catalogue and annotations](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/origen/))

- **Date unknown:** *Homily on Melchizedek*. A sermon mentioned by Jerome but no longer extant; it may have belonged to the Genesis series rather than constituting an independent work. ([Jerome’s catalogue and annotations](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Homilies on Job*. A twenty-two-homily series known from Jerome’s catalogue and now lost or fragmentary. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Homilies on Proverbs*. A seven-homily series known from Jerome’s catalogue and now lost or fragmentary. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Homilies on Ecclesiastes*. An eight-homily series known from Jerome’s catalogue and now lost or fragmentary. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Before the mature commentary on the Song of Songs:** *Homilies on the Song of Songs*. Two pastoral sermons surviving in Jerome’s Latin translation; they are distinct from both Origen’s youthful and mature continuous commentaries. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/origen/))

- **Date unknown:** *Homilies on Isaiah*. Jerome lists thirty-two; nine survive substantially in Latin translation. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/); [Schaff-Herzog Encyclopedia](https://www.ccel.org/s/schaff/encyc08/htm/ii.viii.htm))

- **Mostly c. 238–244:** *Homilies on Jeremiah*. The catalogue’s fourteen represents the set translated by Jerome, but twenty Greek homilies, Latin witnesses, fragments, and references to still higher original numbering survive; the transmitted corpus does not preserve the original series intact. ([Jerome’s catalogue and annotations](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/); [Via Patrum](https://viapatrum.org/works/origen-homilies-jeremiah/))

- **Mostly c. 238–244:** *Homilies on Ezekiel*. Jerome lists twelve, while fourteen survive in Latin translation; the mismatch again shows that his list is not exhaustive. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/); [Schaff-Herzog Encyclopedia](https://www.ccel.org/s/schaff/encyc08/htm/ii.viii.htm))

- **Mostly c. 238–244, with some possibly as late as c. 251:** *Homilies on the Psalms*. Jerome catalogues more than one hundred sermons on individually specified Psalms; nine were long known in Rufinus’s Latin, and twenty-nine Greek homilies in Codex Monacensis Graecus 314 were identified in 2012. The newly recovered collection is accepted by its critical editor as authentic and possibly Origen’s last work, though the complete ancient corpus remains unrecoverable. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/); [Catholic University of America Press](https://www.bibliovault.org/BV.book.epl?ISBN=9780813233192); [De Gruyter edition description](https://books.google.com/books/about/Codex_Monacensis_Graecus_314.html?id=h-r-7hLr9O8C))

- **Date unknown:** *Homilies on Matthew*. A twenty-five-homily series now lost or fragmentary, distinct from the continuous commentary. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Homilies on Luke*. A thirty-nine-homily series preserved chiefly in Jerome’s Latin translation, with Greek fragments. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/); [Schaff-Herzog Encyclopedia](https://www.ccel.org/s/schaff/encyc08/htm/ii.viii.htm))

- **Date unknown:** *Homilies on Acts*. A seventeen-homily series known from Jerome’s catalogue and now lost or fragmentary. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Homilies on Corinthians*. Jerome’s catalogue reads “eleven on Second Corinthians,” but surviving fragments and his testimony elsewhere point instead to extensive preaching on First Corinthians; the addressee and exact series are disputed. ([Jerome’s catalogue and annotations](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Homilies on Thessalonians*. Two sermons are catalogued without specifying whether they treated First or Second Thessalonians. ([Jerome’s catalogue and annotations](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Homilies on Galatians*. A seven-homily series, now lost or fragmentary. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Homily on Titus*. A single sermon known from Jerome’s catalogue and now lost or fragmentary. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Homilies on Hebrews*. An eighteen-homily series known from Jerome’s catalogue; fragments preserved by Eusebius attest Origen’s discussion of the epistle’s disputed human authorship. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Homily on Peace*. A topical sermon known only from the ancient catalogue and now lost. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Exhortation to Pionia*. A homiletic exhortation known only from Jerome’s catalogue; both its occasion and the identity represented by the transmitted name are uncertain. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *On Fasting*. A topical homily, now lost. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Homilies on Monogamy and Trigamy*. Two sermons concerning successive first and third marriages, not simultaneous polygamy; both are lost. ([Jerome’s catalogue and annotations](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *Homilies at Tarsus*. Two sermons identified by their place of delivery and now lost; no other evidence securely documents an Origenian visit to Tarsus. ([Jerome’s catalogue and annotations](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

### Theological, philosophical, and apologetic works

- **Before 231, probably c. 220s:** *On First Principles* (*Peri Archōn*). A four-book systematic theological project treating God, creation, rational beings, freedom, salvation, and scriptural interpretation. It survives completely only in Rufinus’s freely revised Latin translation, alongside Greek fragments and hostile excerpts, so the transmitted work cannot always be equated securely with Origen’s wording. ([Eusebius, *Ecclesiastical History* 6.24](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Nicene_and_Post-Nicene_Fathers:_Series_II/Volume_I/Church_History_of_Eusebius/Book_VI/Chapter_24); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/origen/))

- **Reign of Alexander Severus, 222–235:** *Stromateis* (*Miscellanies*). A ten-book comparison of Christian teaching with Greek philosophy, also containing scriptural exegesis; only a few fragments survive. Its miscellany form resists classification as either a single doctrinal treatise or a commentary. ([Eusebius, *Ecclesiastical History* 6.24](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Nicene_and_Post-Nicene_Fathers:_Series_II/Volume_I/Church_History_of_Eusebius/Book_VI/Chapter_24))

- **Before 231:** *On the Resurrection*. A two-book theological treatise dedicated to Ambrose, now lost except for fragments and later criticism. ([Eusebius, *Ecclesiastical History* 6.24](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Nicene_and_Post-Nicene_Fathers:_Series_II/Volume_I/Church_History_of_Eusebius/Book_VI/Chapter_24); [Schaff-Herzog Encyclopedia](https://www.ccel.org/s/schaff/encyc08/htm/ii.viii.htm))

- **Before 231:** *Dialogues on the Resurrection*. Two dialogue-form books listed separately from *On the Resurrection*; whether they constituted an independent work or another form of the same project is unknown. ([Eusebius, *Ecclesiastical History* 6.24](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Nicene_and_Post-Nicene_Fathers:_Series_II/Volume_I/Church_History_of_Eusebius/Book_VI/Chapter_24))

- **Date unknown:** *Dialogue against Candidus the Valentinian*. A lost anti-Valentinian dialogue known from Jerome’s catalogue; “Candidus” names Origen’s interlocutor, but nothing securely establishes how literally dramatic the written form was. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **Date unknown:** *On Free Will*. A lost treatise reported by later bibliographical tradition; it may overlap in subject with discussions preserved in *On First Principles*, but the catalogue treats it as a separate object. ([Catholic Encyclopedia](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11306b.htm))

- **c. 233–234:** *On Prayer*. A practical-theological treatise written for Ambrose and Tatiana, combining reflection on prayer with an exposition of the Lord’s Prayer and instructions concerning its practice. ([Schaff-Herzog Encyclopedia](https://www.ccel.org/s/schaff/encyc08/htm/ii.viii.htm); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/origen/))

- **235:** *Exhortation to Martyrdom*. An exhortative text addressed to Ambrose and Protoctetus during Maximinus Thrax’s persecution; it functions as a manifesto of steadfast conduct as much as a descriptive treatise. ([Schaff-Herzog Encyclopedia](https://www.ccel.org/s/schaff/encyc08/htm/ii.viii.htm); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/origen/))

- **c. 245:** *Treatise on the Passover*. A prose theological-exegetical treatise surviving in the Tura papyri; it should not be conflated automatically with the eight lost Passover homilies. ([Paulist Press description](https://books.google.co.uk/books?id=PceLcOGH_w0C))

- **c. 248:** *Against Celsus*. An eight-book point-by-point apologetic response to Celsus’s *True Doctrine*, composed at Ambrose’s request and preserved in Greek. Because it follows and answers an adversary’s text serially, it is simultaneously a treatise and a sustained responsive performance. ([Schaff-Herzog Encyclopedia](https://www.ccel.org/s/schaff/encyc08/htm/ii.viii.htm); [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/origen/))

- **Title and date unknown:** *Monobibla*. Jerome records this unexplained title or category, literally “single books.” Nothing surviving permits a confident decision whether it names one work, a collection of independent treatises, or merely a catalogue rubric; its resistance to being called a determinate work is itself the principal recoverable fact. ([Jerome’s catalogue and annotation](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

### Recorded disputation

- **c. 244–249:** *Dialogue with Heraclides and His Fellow Bishops on the Father, the Son, and the Soul*. A stenographic record of a synod-like doctrinal examination in which Origen was the principal speaker. It is more accurately a manifestation of his spoken intervention than a literary dialogue demonstrably composed by him; some scholarship also considers whether the surviving document combines more than one conversation. ([Paulist Press description](https://books.google.co.uk/books?id=PceLcOGH_w0C); [Oxford guide to late-antique dialogues](https://dokumen.pub/christians-in-conversation-a-guide-to-late-antique-dialogues-in-greek-and-syriac-0190915471-9780190915476.html))

### Letters and documentary acts

- **Date unknown:** *Letter to Gregory Thaumaturgus*. A short pedagogical letter urging Gregory to employ Greek learning in scriptural study; it survives substantially and is one of only two nearly complete Origenian letters. ([Catholic Encyclopedia](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11306b.htm))

- **c. 240s:** *Letter to Julius Africanus*. A scholarly epistle defending the Greek additions to Daniel, especially the story of Susanna; it survives and operates as textual argument conducted through correspondence. ([Catholic Encyclopedia](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11306b.htm))

- **Date unknown:** *Letter to Fabian of Rome*. A lost defensive letter written amid attacks on Origen’s orthodoxy; only its reported existence remains. ([Schaff-Herzog Encyclopedia](https://www.ccel.org/s/schaff/encyc08/htm/ii.viii.htm))

- **Date unknown:** *Letter in Defence of His Writings*. A self-defensive epistle formerly included in the second ancient book of Origen’s correspondence; whether it is identical with the letter to Fabian cannot be established from the catalogue. ([Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

- **During and after the Decian persecution, c. 250–251:** *Prison Letters*. Letters of encouragement written while Origen was imprisoned and tortured; they are reported by Eusebius but have not survived as individually recoverable texts. ([Catholic Encyclopedia](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11306b.htm))

- **Collected by the early fourth century:** *Letters to Various Persons*. Eusebius knew a collection exceeding one hundred letters, while Jerome’s catalogue describes nine books of Origen’s own correspondence. The collection is a post-compositional archival manifestation rather than necessarily a single project designed by Origen, and almost all of it is lost. ([Schaff-Herzog Encyclopedia](https://www.ccel.org/s/schaff/encyc08/htm/ii.viii.htm); [Jerome’s catalogue](https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/2014/02/08/a-list-of-the-works-of-origen-jerome-letter-33/))

### Attributed works not securely his

- **Third century; attribution rejected or doubtful:** *Dialogue on the Orthodox Faith* (*Dialogus de recta in Deum fide*). Transmitted under Origen’s name in some traditions but not accepted as his work; it belongs in an attribution history, not in the secure oeuvre. ([Schaff-Herzog Encyclopedia](https://www.ccel.org/s/schaff/encyc08/htm/ii.viii.htm))

- **Third century; attribution rejected:** *Philosophumena* (*Refutation of All Heresies*). Formerly attributed to Origen but conventionally associated with Hippolytus or an otherwise unidentified Roman author; it is not an Origenian creation. ([Schaff-Herzog Encyclopedia](https://www.ccel.org/s/schaff/encyc08/htm/ii.viii.htm))

- **Sixth century; attribution rejected:** *Commentary on Job*. A work by Julian of Halicarnassus formerly ascribed to Origen; it must be excluded from Origen’s oeuvre despite its transmission history. ([Schaff-Herzog Encyclopedia](https://www.ccel.org/s/schaff/encyc08/htm/ii.viii.htm))

- **Late antique; attribution rejected:** *Tractatus Origenis*. Twenty Latin homiletic treatises once presented as Origenian but now recognized as another author’s compositions using Origen’s material. ([Catholic Encyclopedia](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11306b.htm))

- **c. 360s; not Origen’s work:** *Philocalia of Origen*. An anthology of extracts from Origen traditionally associated with Basil of Caesarea and Gregory of Nazianzus. It preserves authentic Origenian material but is a later editorial creation, not a book made by Origen. ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/origen/))
