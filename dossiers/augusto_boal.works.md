## Works by Augusto Boal

### Dramaturgy and performance texts

- **1953:** *O logro*. A play written and directed by Boal for the Teatro Experimental do Negro. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1953–1955:** *The House Across the Street*. An early play written during Boal’s New York years; the catalogue supplies a range rather than a fixed completion or production date. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1953–1955:** *Suave Song*. An early English-titled play whose production status is not specified by the catalogue. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1954:** *O cavalo e o santo*. A play mounted by the Teatro Experimental do Negro. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1955:** *Martim pescador*. A play by Boal, first directed by Leo Jusi for the Teatro Experimental do Negro and published in 1958. [Instituto Augusto Boal: works](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/); [publications](https://augustoboal.com.br/publicacoes/)
- **1955:** *Maria Conga*. A play mounted by the Teatro Brasileiro da Juventude. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1956:** *Filha moça*. A play mounted by the Teatro Experimental do Negro under Geraldo Campos de Oliveira; its text was censored. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1957:** *Marido magro, mulher chata*. A play co-directed by Boal and Carlos Murtinho. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1958:** *Laio se matou*. A play mounted by the Teatro Experimental do Negro under Raul Martins. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1960:** *Revolução na América do Sul*. A satirical play, first directed by José Renato and published in the same year; *Ay, ay! No hay Cristo que aguante, no hay* and *Revolution auf südamerikanisch* are later-language manifestations, not separate obras. [Instituto Augusto Boal: works](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/); [publications](https://augustoboal.com.br/publicacoes/)
- **1960:** *Os macacos ou O preço do homem*. The first version of a text made for television; its status as a stage play is therefore unstable. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1960:** *A gran cruz del mal necessario: novela picaresca em seis porções e algumas canções*. A hybrid theatrical “picaresque novel” incorporating songs rather than an ordinary prose novel. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1961:** *José, do parto à sepultura*. A play first directed by Antônio Abujamra. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1964:** *Show Opinião*. A collectively authored musical-theatrical show by Boal, Armando Costa, Paulo Pontes and Oduvaldo Vianna Filho, directed by Boal; its authorship cannot properly be reduced to Boal alone. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1965:** *Arena canta Bahia*. A theatrical-musical show credited to Boal and Gianfrancesco Guarnieri, with Boal responsible for script and direction. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1965:** *Tempo de guerra*. A theatrical-musical work co-written with Gianfrancesco Guarnieri and directed by Boal. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1965:** *Arena conta Zumbi*. A play-with-music co-written with Gianfrancesco Guarnieri and first directed by Boal; later productions called simply *Zumbi* are manifestations of the same obra. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1966:** *A lua girô, girô*. A jointly written and jointly directed work by Boal and Gianfrancesco Guarnieri. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1967:** *A criação do mundo segundo Ary Toledo*. A theatrical show co-written with Gianfrancesco Guarnieri, with musical direction by Carlos Castilho. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1967:** *Arena conta Tiradentes*. A play-with-music co-written with Gianfrancesco Guarnieri and first directed by Boal; it is also an exemplary manifestation of his “Joker” system. [Instituto Augusto Boal: works](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/); [publications](https://augustoboal.com.br/publicacoes/)
- **1968:** *A lua pequena e a caminhada perigosa*. A short play written and directed for the collectively constituted *Primeira Feira Paulista de Opinião*. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1968:** *Sérgio Ricardo na praça do povo*. A performance text written and directed by Boal. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1969:** *Chiclete e Banana*. A play or theatrical show written and directed by Boal. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1969–1970:** *Arena conta Bolívar*. A play written and directed by Boal, censored in Brazil and first performed in Mexico; its earliest public existence was therefore in exile and on tour. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1971:** *Torquemada*. A prison play written after Boal’s arrest and torture, first mounted in exile; it is both drama and political testimony without ceasing to be a play. [Instituto Augusto Boal: works](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/); [chronology](https://augustoboal.com.br/vida-e-obra/)
- **1971:** *El gran acuerdo internacional del Tío Patilludo* / *O grande acordo internacional do Tio Patinhas*. A political play written and directed by Boal; later Spanish productions use the variant *Tío Rico McPato*. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1975:** *Histórias americanas*. A dramatic work listed by the Instituto without production or publication details; its precise material status remains unclear. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1976:** *A tempestade*. Boal’s theatrical rewriting of Shakespeare’s play, with music by Manduca; it is an adaptation and anticolonial counter-work, not a claim to authorship of Shakespeare’s source. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1976:** *Mulheres de Atenas*. A theatrical adaptation of Aristophanes’ *Lysistrata*, with music by Chico Buarque. [Instituto Augusto Boal: works](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/); [chronology](https://augustoboal.com.br/vida-e-obra/)
- **1977:** *A Barraca conta Tiradentes*. A new Tiradentes production/text made for the Portuguese company A Barraca; its title and company-specific form distinguish it from, while deriving from, *Arena conta Tiradentes*. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1977:** *Ao qu’isto chegou*. A collective Feira de Opinião manifestation authored and directed by Boal with A Barraca, so sole authorship would be misleading. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1977–1978:** *Murro em ponta de faca*. A play about political exile, completed in Lisbon and first staged in Brazil in 1978 under Paulo José, with music by Chico Buarque; foreign-language titles are manifestations of this obra. [Instituto Augusto Boal: works](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/); [chronology](https://augustoboal.com.br/vida-e-obra/)
- **1978–1980:** *Le nouveau badache*. A Forum Theatre play written and directed in France; the Instituto’s catalogue gives a date range rather than a single creation date. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1978–1980:** *Le dragon jaune et la famille sourde* / *O dragão esverdeado e a família surda*. A Forum Theatre text written and directed in France; the Portuguese and French titles vary in the institutional accounts. [Instituto Augusto Boal: works](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/); [chronology](https://augustoboal.com.br/vida-e-obra/)
- **c. 1980:** *La surprise*. A Forum Theatre play written within CEDITADE; the chronology names it but does not provide a firmer date or production record. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/vida-e-obra/)
- **c. 1980:** *La cohérence*. A Forum Theatre play written within CEDITADE; its precise completion and production dates are not supplied. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/vida-e-obra/)
- **c. 1980:** *Comme d’habitude*. A Forum Theatre play written within CEDITADE; it resists treatment as a fixed literary work because the surviving institutional notice identifies a workshop-performance context rather than a publication. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/vida-e-obra/)
- **c. 1980:** *L’anniversaire de la mère*. A Forum Theatre play written within CEDITADE; no fixed publication or premiere date is given. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/vida-e-obra/)
- **1981:** *On a tous les jours cent ans*. A Forum Theatre play written and directed by Boal. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1981:** *L’ogre méchant et les gentils marchands de couteaux*. A Forum Theatre play written and directed by Boal. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1985:** *O corsário do rei*. A musical-theatrical work written and directed by Boal, with music by Edu Lobo and lyrics by Chico Buarque. [Instituto Augusto Boal: works](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/); [chronology](https://augustoboal.com.br/vida-e-obra/)
- **1987:** *As aventuras do Tio Patinhas*. A play by Boal directed by José Luiz Ribeiro; the catalogue does not explain its textual relation to the 1971 *Grande acordo internacional*. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1998:** *Amigo oculto*. A “bulevar macabro” comedy written in 1998 and first staged by Marília Pêra in 2000. [Instituto Augusto Boal: works](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/); [chronology](https://augustoboal.com.br/vida-e-obra/)
- **1998:** *A herança maldita ou A jangada dos canibais: bulevar macabro em um ato falho*. A macabre boulevard comedy; the Instituto dates its writing to 1998 but catalogues productions under 2003–2007, so production dates should not be mistaken for composition. [Instituto Augusto Boal: works](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/); [chronology](https://augustoboal.com.br/vida-e-obra/)
- **1999:** *Chuveiro iluminado*. A staged work for which Boal supplied script and direction; the catalogue does not classify its genre more precisely. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1999:** *Carmém: sambópera*. A Gesamtkunstwerk-like “sambópera” adapting Bizet and Prosper Mérimée, made with Celso Branco and Marcos Leite; it intentionally crosses spoken theatre, opera and samba. [Instituto Augusto Boal: works](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/); [chronology](https://augustoboal.com.br/vida-e-obra/)
- **2001:** *La traviata: sambópera*. A second hybrid opera-samba-theatre adaptation, made with Celso Branco from Verdi and directed by Boal, with musical direction by Jayme Vignolli. [Instituto Augusto Boal: works](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/); [chronology](https://augustoboal.com.br/vida-e-obra/)
- **2005:** *O homem que era uma fábrica*. A play by Boal first directed by Tuna Serzedello. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)

### Adaptations, translations and dramaturgical transformations

- **1960:** *A engrenagem*. A stage adaptation of Jean-Paul Sartre made jointly by Boal and José Celso Martinez Corrêa and directed by Boal; authorship remains Sartre’s. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1963:** *As famosas asturianas*. Boal’s stage adaptation of Lope de Vega, directed by Yan Michalski. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1963:** *O melhor juiz, o rei*. An adaptation of Lope de Vega jointly made by Boal, Gianfrancesco Guarnieri and Paulo José Gomes de Souza, directed by Boal. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1966:** *O inspetor geral*. A Portuguese translation of Gogol made by Boal and Gianfrancesco Guarnieri for Boal’s production. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1968:** *MacBird!*. Boal’s translation and Brazilian staging of Barbara Garson’s play; the underlying drama is Garson’s, while the translation and production are Boal’s made objects. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1981:** *Nada a Pehuajó* / *Nada más a Calingasta*. Boal’s stage adaptation and direction of a work by Julio Cortázar; the institutional sources give variant titles. [Instituto Augusto Boal: works](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/); [chronology](https://augustoboal.com.br/vida-e-obra/)
- **1981:** *Mulher judia*. A Forum Theatre transformation of Bertolt Brecht’s scene, adapted and directed by Boal rather than authored by him. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1983:** *L’incroyable et triste histoire de la candide Eréndira et de sa grand-mère diabolique*. A theatrical adaptation of Gabriel García Márquez made by Miguel Torres and Boal and directed by Boal. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1984:** *La Malasangre*. Boal’s French translation and production of Griselda Gambaro’s play; the original play is not his obra. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1984:** *Das Publikum* / *El público*. Boal’s adaptation and German production of Federico García Lorca’s play. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1985–1986:** *Tête de Turc*. An unproduced adaptation made by Boal and Klaus Schuffels from Günter Wallraff; as an unrealized project-text it resists identification with a performance. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1986:** *Fedra*. Boal’s adaptation and production of Racine’s tragedy, staged with Fernanda Montenegro. [Instituto Augusto Boal: works](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/); [chronology](https://augustoboal.com.br/vida-e-obra/)
- **1987:** *A mulher do padeiro*. Boal’s translation of Marcel Pagnol; the Instituto explicitly says it was never staged, making it a completed text but unrealized production project. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1989:** *O encontro marcado*. Boal’s stage adaptation and direction of Fernando Sabino’s novel. [Instituto Augusto Boal: works](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/); [chronology](https://augustoboal.com.br/vida-e-obra/)
- **1995:** *Iphigenie*. A free translation and production of Euripides directed by Boal; the classical drama itself is not attributed to him. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)

### Collective shows and curatorial-dramaturgical constructions

- **1968:** *Primeira Feira Paulista de Opinião*. A multi-author theatrical event conceived and directed by Boal under censorship; it is better understood as a collective political manifestation and curatorial Gesamtkunstwerk than as his sole-authored play. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1970:** *Teatro Jornal — Primeira edição*. A collectively authored production directed by Boal and an early manifestation of Newspaper Theatre; its changing relation to current news resists the status of a fixed work. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1972:** *Latin American Fair of Opinion*. A multi-author political theatre event directed by Boal in exile; it is a collective manifestation rather than a sole-authored text. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1980:** *Stop! C’est magique*. A Forum Theatre performance made with CEDITADE/Centre du Théâtre de l’Opprimé and presented with the Théâtre du Soleil under Boal’s direction; it also became a book, but the participatory event is not reducible to that publication. [Instituto Augusto Boal: chronology](https://augustoboal.com.br/vida-e-obra/); [publications](https://augustoboal.com.br/publicacoes/)

### Protocols, systems and participatory obras

- **1958 onward:** *Seminário de Dramaturgia do Teatro de Arena*. A continuing dramaturgical project initiated under Boal at the Arena: a protocol for collective aesthetic and political analysis, not a single authored text. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/vida-e-obra/)
- **1965–1967:** *Sistema Coringa*. A dramaturgical and production system developed through the Arena “conta” works, especially *Arena conta Zumbi* and *Arena conta Tiradentes*; it is a reusable protocol rather than one performance. [Frances Babbage, *Augusto Boal*](https://www.routledge.com/Augusto-Boal/Babbage/p/book/9781138592018)
- **c. 1970:** *Teatro Jornal*. A protocol for converting news and journalistic forms into performance; individual “editions” are manifestations, while the technique itself resists being called a bounded work. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/)
- **1970s:** *Teatro Imagem*. A participatory theatre protocol using bodies and spatial images rather than relying on spoken dramatic text; it is made anew by each group and therefore has no single definitive manifestation. [Frances Babbage, *Augusto Boal*](https://www.routledge.com/Augusto-Boal/Babbage/p/book/9781138592018)
- **1970s:** *Teatro Invisível*. A political-performance protocol in which enacted situations enter public space without being announced as theatre; its intended resistance to recognition as a “work” is constitutive, not an archival defect. [Frances Babbage, *Augusto Boal*](https://www.routledge.com/Augusto-Boal/Babbage/p/book/9781138592018)
- **1970s:** *Teatro Fórum*. A protocol in which spectators become “spect-actors” and intervene in a performed conflict; every forum is a new manifestation, so no final authoritative performance exists. [Frances Babbage, *Augusto Boal*](https://www.routledge.com/Augusto-Boal/Babbage/p/book/9781138592018)
- **1970s onward:** *Teatro do Oprimido*. The umbrella project joining theatrical methods, political pedagogy, books, workshops and institutions; it is a body of work and transnational practice rather than a single obra, although one of Boal’s books bears the same name. [Frances Babbage, *Augusto Boal*](https://www.routledge.com/Augusto-Boal/Babbage/p/book/9781138592018); [Clara de Andrade and Christopher Balme](https://doi.org/10.5282/gthj/5128)
- **1977–1986:** *CEDITADE / Centre du Théâtre de l’Opprimé Augusto Boal*. A Paris-based institutional project for studying and disseminating active expressive techniques; its productions, workshops and protocols collectively form the made thing. The institutional name changed over time. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/vida-e-obra/)
- **1986:** *Fábrica de Teatro Popular*. A theatre-in-education project directed by Boal in Rio de Janeiro at Darcy Ribeiro’s invitation; it was a working structure and process, not a bounded text. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/vida-e-obra/)
- **1986 onward:** *Centro de Teatro do Oprimido do Rio de Janeiro*. An institution co-created around Boal’s returning practice and directed by him; it is a durable collective project whose work exceeds individual authorship. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/vida-e-obra/); [Diccionario Biográfico de las Izquierdas Latinoamericanas](https://diccionario.cedinci.org/boal-augusto/)
- **1980s–1990:** *Arco-íris do desejo*. A family of introspective theatre-and-therapy techniques developed through practice and systematized in a 1990 book; it is simultaneously protocol, theoretical model and recurring workshop event. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/publicacoes/)
- **1992–1996:** *Teatro Legislativo*. A political-theatrical project developed during Boal’s term as a Rio city councillor, using Forum Theatre to formulate legislation; because fourteen of thirty-three submitted projects became municipal laws, its manifestations include performances, proposals and enacted legal texts rather than only theatre. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/vida-e-obra/)
- **late 1990s–2009:** *Estética do Oprimido*. Boal’s final theoretical-practical project, joining theatre to image, sound and word-making; its refusal of separated media makes it his closest approach to a Gesamtkunstwerk, while the 2009 book is only one manifestation. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/vida-e-obra/)

### Books: manifestos, protocols, testimony and theory

- **1971:** *Categorias de teatro popular*. A short work of political-theatrical classification, first issued by the Teatro de Arena; it functions partly as manifesto because its categories prescribe political action as much as they describe theatre. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/publicacoes/)
- **1973:** *Crônicas de Nuestra América*. A collection of political-literary chronicles; the Instituto also records a 1977 edition and translations, which are manifestations rather than new works. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/publicacoes/)
- **1974–1975:** *Teatro do oprimido e outras poéticas políticas*. A theoretical manifesto and historical argument first published in Spanish in 1974 and in Portuguese in 1975; the Instituto’s chronology instead calls 1974 the first Brazilian edition, conflicting with its publication list, so the Brazilian date is disputed in the source itself. [Instituto Augusto Boal: publications](https://augustoboal.com.br/publicacoes/); [chronology](https://augustoboal.com.br/vida-e-obra/)
- **1975:** *Técnicas latino-americanas de teatro popular: uma revolução copernicana ao contrário*. A theoretical and practical study of popular theatre, simultaneously argument and protocol. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/publicacoes/)
- **1975:** *200 exercícios e jogos para o ator e o não-ator com vontade de dizer algo através do teatro*. A protocol-book of exercises and games, first listed in Spanish as *200 ejercicios y juegos* and subsequently expanded and retitled; undated author and Mexican issues in the bibliography prevent a perfectly secure edition sequence. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/publicacoes/)
- **1976:** *Milagre no Brasil*. A prison-and-exile testimony rather than a theatre manual; the Instituto’s bibliography accidentally lists the same Lisbon edition twice. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/publicacoes/)
- **1977:** *Jane Spitfire*. A novel, later republished under the expanded title *A deliciosa e sangrenta aventura latina de Jane Spitfire, espiã e mulher sensual*; the later title is a manifestation of the same fiction, not a second novel. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/publicacoes/)
- **1978:** *Jeux pour acteurs et non-acteurs*. A substantially developed protocol-book descended from *200 exercícios e jogos*; later Portuguese and English versions, including *Jogos para atores e não-atores* and *Games for Actors and Non-Actors*, contain revisions and expansion, so the boundary between translation, edition and new recension is unstable. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/publicacoes/)
- **1980:** *Stop: c’est magique*. A book arising from Forum Theatre practice and its Paris production; it records and instructs but cannot contain the participatory performance itself. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/publicacoes/)
- **1980:** *Augusto Boal: förtrollad, förvandlad, förstenad—teater för alla*. A Swedish-language publication listed by the Instituto; its relationship to *Stop: c’est magique* and other exercise books is not clarified there, so it cannot safely be declared an independent original work. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/publicacoes/)
- **1986:** *Teatro de Augusto Boal, vol. 1*. A collected-dramaturgy volume: a new editorial object containing earlier plays rather than a newly composed play. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/publicacoes/)
- **1986:** *Teatro de Augusto Boal, vol. 2*. The second collected-dramaturgy volume, likewise an editorial obra whose components predate it. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/publicacoes/)
- **1990:** *O arco-íris do desejo: método Boal de teatro e terapia*. A theory-and-protocol book systematizing therapeutic and introspective theatre practices; the French edition appeared in the same year, leaving priority between its first-language manifestations unclear in the institutional bibliography. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/publicacoes/)
- **1992:** *O suicida com medo da morte*. A novel completed in 1990 and published in 1992. [Instituto Augusto Boal: chronology](https://augustoboal.com.br/vida-e-obra/); [publications](https://augustoboal.com.br/publicacoes/)
- **1996:** *Teatro legislativo*. A manifesto, report and protocol for converting theatrical intervention into legislative action. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/publicacoes/)
- **1996:** *Aqui ninguém é burro!*. A compilation of Boal’s speeches and interventions as a Rio city councillor; it is an editorially assembled political-performance record rather than a continuous authored treatise. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/publicacoes/)
- **2000:** *Hamlet e o filho do padeiro: memórias imaginadas*. An autobiography that announces its own imaginative construction and therefore resists classification as unmediated documentary testimony. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/publicacoes/)
- **2003:** *O teatro como arte marcial*. A late collection of theoretical and political reflections presenting theatre as intervention rather than representation alone. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/publicacoes/)
- **2006/2009:** *A estética do oprimido*. Boal’s final theoretical work: first listed in English as *The Aesthetics of the Oppressed* in 2006 and in Portuguese in 2009. The publication order makes any claim to a uniquely “original” language manifestation unsafe without manuscript evidence. [Instituto Augusto Boal](https://augustoboal.com.br/publicacoes/)

### Sources

- [Instituto Augusto Boal, “Peças”](https://augustoboal.com.br/pecas-e-publicacoes/). The foundation’s work catalogue, explicitly intended to gather the full extent of Boal’s authored, translated, adapted and directed theatre work; used as the principal catalogue raisonné-like source.
- [Instituto Augusto Boal, “Publicações”](https://augustoboal.com.br/publicacoes/). The foundation’s bibliography of approximately twenty books and their translated, revised and reprinted manifestations; editions were consolidated here rather than counted as separate works.
- [Instituto Augusto Boal, “Vida e obra”](https://augustoboal.com.br/vida-e-obra/). Institutional chronology used to distinguish composition, publication, premiere, exile, workshop and project dates.
- [Acervo Augusto Boal, “Apresentação”](https://acervoaugustoboal.com.br/apresentacao). Description of the approximately 10,000-document archive and its chronological series of theatre texts, intellectual production, programmes, photographs, audio and video.
- [José Eduardo Paraíso Razuk, *Muito além do Teatro do Oprimido: um panorama da obra dramatúrgica de Augusto Boal* (2019), bibliographic record](https://diccionario.cedinci.org/boal-augusto/). Scholarly survey of Boal’s dramaturgy cited by the CeDInCI biographical dictionary; used as a check on the foundation catalogue’s status as a dramaturgical corpus.
- [Frances Babbage, *Augusto Boal* (Routledge, 2018)](https://www.routledge.com/Augusto-Boal/Babbage/p/book/9781138592018). Scholarly study used for the status of Theatre of the Oppressed, the Joker system, Forum Theatre and workshop practice.
- [Clara de Andrade and Christopher Balme, “Transnational Networks of the Theatre of the Oppressed: The Institutionalization of a Circulating Method” (2020)](https://doi.org/10.5282/gthj/5128). Scholarly account used to treat Theatre of the Oppressed as a circulating, institutionally reproduced method rather than a single fixed work.
- [Diccionario Biográfico de las Izquierdas Latinoamericanas, “Boal, Augusto”](https://diccionario.cedinci.org/boal-augusto/). Scholarly biographical synthesis and bibliography used to cross-check exile institutions, the Centro de Teatro do Oprimido and the principal research literature.
