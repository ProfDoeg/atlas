## Works by Alexander von Humboldt

### Early scientific monographs and experimental works

- **1790:** *Mineralogische Beobachtungen über einige Basalte am Rhein*. Humboldt’s first independently published monograph, a mineralogical study written before his mature volcanic theories; the standard Fiedler–Leitner sequence numbers it 1.1. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))
- **1793:** *Florae Fribergensis specimen plantas cryptogamicas praesertim subterraneas exhibens*. A Latin cryptogamic flora based on Humboldt’s Freiberg mining work, accompanied by his *Aphorismi ex doctrina physiologiae chemicae plantarum*; the latter also circulated separately in German and therefore resists a simple one-book identity. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))
- **1797:** *Versuche über die gereizte Muskel- und Nervenfaser nebst Vermuthungen über den chemischen Process des Lebens in der Thier- und Pflanzenwelt*. A two-volume experimental physiology and galvanism study; its later French and Spanish forms are translations and revisions, not additional obras. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))
- **1799:** *Ueber die unterirdischen Gasarten und die Mittel ihren Nachtheil zu vermindern*. A mining-safety protocol as well as a scientific treatise: it analyzes subterranean gases and proposes practices and apparatus for protecting miners. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))
- **1799:** *Versuche über die chemische Zerlegung des Luftkreises und über einige andere Gegenstände der Naturlehre*. A collection of chemical and pneumatic experiments coauthored with Jean-François de La Métherie; its composite authorship should not be silently reduced to Humboldt alone. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))

### Travel prospectus and literary-natural history

- **1805:** *Notice d’un voyage aux tropiques*. A preliminary announcement or prospectus of the American journey rather than its definitive account; the independently issued German version is what the Fiedler–Leitner-derived digital bibliography records. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))
- **1808; substantially revised 1826 and 1849:** *Ansichten der Natur, mit wissenschaftlichen Erläuterungen*. A hybrid obra joining literary landscape portrait, natural history, and scientific annotation. Its three authorially revised editions materially differ, so “the work” is a continuing project rather than one fixed text. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))

### The American travel work: a collaborative Gesamtkunstwerk

- **1805–1834, with some plates delivered as late as 1838:** *Voyage aux régions équinoxiales du Nouveau Continent, fait en 1799–1804*. The overarching American travel project, produced with Aimé Bonpland and many specialist collaborators, appeared in numerous fascicles, formats, and approximately twenty-nine volumes rather than as one stable book. It is best treated as a Gesamtkunstwerk of narrative, measurements, taxonomies, maps, tables, diagrams, and images. ([Edition Humboldt Digital](https://edition-humboldt.de/?l=en); [Kraft and Einert](https://doi.org/10.18443/133))
- **1805/1807:** *Essai sur la géographie des plantes; accompagné d’un tableau physique des régions équinoxiales*. A coauthored Humboldt–Bonpland essay that formulates plant geography through prose, measurements, tables, and a large visual synthesis; copies dated 1805 and 1807 complicate its publication date. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))
- **1805/1807:** *Tableau physique des régions équinoxiales*. A large engraved diagram integrating altitude, vegetation, climate, agriculture, and physical measurements around an idealized Andean profile. Although issued with the *Essai*, it is also an autonomous visual argument—a manifestation that cannot adequately be classified as merely a book illustration. ([PNAS study](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1904585116); [Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))
- **1805–1817:** *Plantes équinoxiales recueillies au Mexique, dans l’île de Cuba, dans les provinces de Caracas, de Cumana et de Barcelone, aux Andes de la Nouvelle-Grenade, de Quito et du Pérou, et sur les bords du Rio-Negro, de l’Orénoque et de la rivière des Amazones*. A two-volume folio botanical plate work by Humboldt and Bonpland; description, specimen, image, and expedition collection jointly constitute the object. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))
- **1806–1823:** *Monographie des Melastomacées*. A two-volume illustrated botanical monograph associated with Humboldt and Bonpland’s collections; Bonpland and later taxonomic collaborators carry substantial authorship, making “by Humboldt” an incomplete description. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))
- **1807–1810:** *Recueil d’observations astronomiques, d’opérations trigonométriques et de mesures barométriques faites pendant le cours d’un voyage aux régions équinoxiales du Nouveau Continent*. A two-volume measurement archive and computational work prepared by Humboldt with Jabbo Oltmanns; it turns field observations into coordinates, elevations, and astronomical results. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))
- **1808–1811:** *Essai politique sur le royaume de la Nouvelle-Espagne*. A statistical, geographical, economic, and political model of New Spain, first issued in large-format parts and then in other formats. Its publication history contains variant dates and arrangements, so translations and later editions should not be counted as new obras. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))
- **1808–1811:** *Atlas géographique et physique du royaume de la Nouvelle-Espagne*. The cartographic counterpart to the *Essai politique*: an atlas of maps, sections, profiles, and statistical graphics rather than merely an appendix in prose. It belongs to the New Spain obra while retaining a distinct material and epistemic identity. ([Fiedler–Leitner-derived bibliography](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469); [Kraft and Einert](https://doi.org/10.18443/133))
- **1810–1813:** *Vues des Cordillères et monumens des peuples indigènes de l’Amérique*. A large-format “picturesque atlas” of landscapes, architectural remains, manuscripts, artifacts, and interpretive essays. Drafts and observations were Humboldt’s, but professional artists and engravers produced its plates, so it is collaborative image-making rather than a portfolio drawn solely by him. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))
- **1811–1833:** *Recueil d’observations de zoologie et d’anatomie comparée, faites dans l’océan Atlantique, dans l’intérieur du Nouveau Continent et dans la mer du Sud*. A two-volume zoological and comparative-anatomical work with Bonpland and specialist contributors; bibliographies bracket the second volume’s long, fascicle-based publication as 1813–1833. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))
- **1814–1825/1831:** *Relation historique du Voyage aux régions équinoxiales du Nouveau Continent*. Humboldt’s first-person American travel narrative, issued in three quarto volumes and a parallel octavo sequence. It remained unfinished—ending before the narrative reached much of the journey—so its incompleteness is part of the obra rather than a missing bibliographical datum. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))
- **1814–1834, final maps delivered by 1838:** *Atlas géographique et physique des régions équinoxiales du Nouveau Continent*. A physical and geographical atlas tied to the *Relation historique*. Its staggered deliveries and maps made or revised across decades make it a continuing cartographic project, not a single moment of publication. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))
- **1815–1826:** *Nova genera et species plantarum quas in peregrinatione ad plagam aequinoctialem orbis novi collegerunt, descripserunt, partim adumbraverunt Amat. Bonpland et Alex. de Humboldt*. A seven-volume systematic flora principally worked into taxonomic form by Carl Sigismund Kunth from Humboldt and Bonpland’s collections. The title names the travelers, but modern attribution must preserve Kunth’s decisive authorship. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))
- **1817:** *De distributione geographica plantarum secundum coeli temperiem et altitudinem montium, prolegomena*. A Latin theoretical synthesis of plant distribution, extracted and reconfigured from the botanical project; its semi-independent status makes it both a component and a separate manifestation. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))
- **1819–1824:** *Mimoses et autres plantes légumineuses du Nouveau Continent*. An illustrated botanical folio based on Humboldt and Bonpland’s expedition collections and prepared taxonomically by Kunth. Attribution belongs to this collaborative chain, not unqualifiedly to Humboldt. ([Fiedler–Leitner-derived bibliography](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))
- **1822–1826:** *Synopsis plantarum quas in itinere ad plagam aequinoctialem orbis novi collegerunt Al. de Humboldt et Am. Bonpland*. A compact four-volume synopsis derived from the larger botanical publications, prepared with Kunth; it is a reorganized scientific instrument rather than simply another edition of the folio flora. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))
- **1826:** *Essai politique sur l’île de Cuba*. A two-volume political-statistical essay separated and substantially reworked from the Cuban portions of the *Relation historique*. Its independent form is textually distinct, not merely an offprint; the work combines colonial statistics with an explicit attack on slavery. ([Humboldt Digital editorial note](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))
- **1829–1834:** *Révision des Graminées publiées dans les Nova genera et species plantarum*. A three-part illustrated taxonomic revision associated with Kunth’s botanical labor on the American collections. Its title pages carry dates that do not always match actual delivery dates, which the bibliography records in brackets. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))
- **1831:** *Tableau statistique de l’île de Cuba*. A statistical supplement to the Cuban essay, separately issued but conceptually dependent on it; it is better understood as an updating apparatus than as an entirely new argument. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))
- **1836–1839:** *Examen critique de l’histoire de la géographie du Nouveau Continent et des progrès de l’astronomie nautique aux quinzième et seizième siècles*. A five-volume critical history of exploration, cartography, and nautical astronomy that grew out of the American atlas. Though catalogued within the travel work, it became an independent historiographical obra. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))

### Geology, geomagnetism, and Central Asia

- **1823; revised French edition 1826:** *Essai géognostique sur le gisement des roches dans les deux hémisphères*. A comparative geognostic synthesis proposing a global ordering of rock formations. The 1823 and 1826 forms represent revision and translation within one obra, not separate creations. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))
- **1831:** *Fragmens de géologie et de climatologie asiatiques*. A two-volume provisional synthesis drawn from the Russian-Siberian expedition and other Asian evidence; “fragments” accurately signals a stage in an expanding project. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))
- **1843:** *Asie centrale: recherches sur les chaînes de montagnes et la climatologie comparée*. A three-volume revision and enlargement of the *Fragmens*, combining expedition results with comparative physical geography. It is both the mature Asian obra and a deliberately unfinished contribution to a global science. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))

### Maps, diagrams, and scientific visualizations

- **1800:** *Map of the Casiquiare–Orinoco connection*. A field-generated hydrographic map establishing the navigable connection between the Orinoco and Amazon basins; it survives through manuscript and printed transformations, so no single printed sheet exhausts the made thing. ([Edition Humboldt Digital](https://edition-humboldt.de/?l=en))
- **1804–1814:** *Carte itinéraire du cours de l’Orénoque, de l’Atabapo, du Casiquiare et du Rio Negro*. A developed printed itinerary map derived from Humboldt’s route surveys and astronomical measurements, incorporated into the American atlases. It is a map-object within a larger publication system rather than an autonomous book. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))
- **1817:** *Des lignes isothermes et de la distribution de la chaleur sur le globe*. An essay and map introducing isothermal lines as a comparative visualization of terrestrial temperature. The object is simultaneously article, analytical method, and reusable graphical protocol. ([Sämtliche Schriften Digital](https://humboldt.unibe.ch/text))
- **1845–1848:** *Physikalischer Atlas*. Heinrich Berghaus made this atlas in close association with Humboldt’s *Kosmos* and Humboldt’s program of physical geography. It should not be silently attributed to Humboldt: for this atlas he was intellectual catalyst and source, while Berghaus was the named cartographer-author. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))

### Lectures as performed works

- **1827–1828:** *Vorlesungen über physische Weltbeschreibung* (“Kosmos lectures”). Two related lecture cycles—sixty-two university lectures and sixteen public Sing-Akademie lectures—were performed works preceding *Kosmos*. Humboldt left no authorized complete lecture text; surviving student notes are witnesses to the event, not straightforwardly books he wrote. ([Edition Humboldt Digital](https://edition-humboldt.de/?l=en))

### The world-description project

- **1845–1862:** *Kosmos. Entwurf einer physischen Weltbeschreibung*. Humboldt’s culminating five-volume project to present nature as an interconnected whole—scientific synthesis, literary composition, historical account of knowledge, and visual program. Volumes I–IV appeared during his life; volume V, assembled from his manuscript materials and completed editorially by Eduard Buschmann after Humboldt’s death, appeared in 1862, so the obra is both monumental and posthumously unfinished. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))
- **1853:** *Kleinere Schriften*. An authorial collection of scientific essays and addresses, with a related French volume published in 1854. It is a curated retrospective manifestation of texts that also retain separate identities in the periodical corpus. ([Humboldt Digital](https://www.avhumboldt.de/?page_id=469))

### Travel journals and working manuscripts

- **1790–1805:** *European travel journals*. A discontinuous manuscript corpus covering England (1790); Dresden, Vienna, and Salzburg (1797–1798); Paris to Toulon (1798); Spain (1799); and Italy (1805). These are working records rather than a single titled work, and the scholarly edition explicitly treats them as an unconnected series. ([Edition Humboldt Digital](https://edition-humboldt.de/reisetagebuecher/index.xql?id=H0018392&l=en))
- **1799–1804:** *American travel journals*. Approximately 3,500 manuscript pages now arranged as volumes I–IX plus the Cuba/Antilles and Mexico–Veracruz journals. They combine itinerary, data, experiment protocols, essays, sketches, and drawings; their later bindings and numbering do not always reproduce Humboldt’s original organization, so the corpus resists treatment as one finished text. ([Edition Humboldt Digital](https://edition-humboldt.de/reisetagebuecher/index.xql?id=H0018396&l=en))
- **1799–1804:** *Field notebooks, measurement tables, drawings, profiles, and loose travel papers from the American expedition*. These are working objects and evidentiary instruments rather than uniformly titled “works”; many were incorporated into the travel journals and published travel obra, while others survive only as papers. ([Edition Humboldt Digital](https://edition-humboldt.de/?l=en))
- **1829:** *Fragments of the Siberian travel journal*. A surviving, incomplete narrative-and-data manuscript from the Russian-Siberian expedition; its fragmentary form is the object’s actual status, not a defect to be repaired bibliographically. ([Edition Humboldt Digital](https://edition-humboldt.de/?l=en))
- **1829:** *Observations astronomiques*. A Russian-journey measurement journal containing astronomical and geodetic records. It functions as a field protocol and data object rather than a literary travel book. ([Edition Humboldt Digital](https://edition-humboldt.de/?l=en))
- **1829:** *Observations magnétiques*. A Russian-journey geomagnetic observation journal, again a protocol and measurement archive rather than a conventional authored volume. ([Edition Humboldt Digital](https://edition-humboldt.de/?l=en))

### Essays, articles, notices, prefaces, and public interventions

- **1789–1859:** *The corpus of independently published articles, essays, extracts, reviews, notices, speeches, prefaces, and contributions to other authors’ books*. The Berne *Sämtliche Schriften* identifies roughly 750 distinct writings that appeared during Humboldt’s lifetime in about 3,600 printings; the corpus—not a falsely compressed list of “books”—is the defensible obra-level unit here. Individual texts often changed title, language, length, and genre between printings, and the edition preserves those unstable textual identities. ([Sämtliche Schriften Digital](https://humboldt.unibe.ch/text); [edition description](https://humboldt.unibe.ch/))
- **1792:** *Florae subterraneae Fribergensis prodromus* and associated mining-botanical reports. These shorter publications anticipate the 1793 flora and demonstrate the difficulty of deciding whether a journal contribution, preliminary report, and later monograph constitute one text or several manifestations. ([Sämtliche Schriften Digital](https://humboldt.unibe.ch/text))
- **1804–1859:** *Scientific memoirs on terrestrial magnetism and the “magnetic crusade.”* A dispersed body of articles, reports, appeals, observation instructions, and institutional correspondence through which Humboldt helped organize coordinated global magnetic measurement. It is a project enacted through protocols and networks, not one fixed-title work. ([Sämtliche Schriften Digital](https://humboldt.unibe.ch/text))
- **1829–1859:** *Public and private interventions against slavery and the slave trade*. These occur across the Cuban essay, prefaces, letters, and shorter publications rather than forming one authorized manifesto. Calling the whole a manifesto would overstate its textual unity, but many individual interventions act politically as well as describe. ([Sämtliche Schriften Digital](https://humboldt.unibe.ch/text))

### Correspondence and institutional action

- **c. 1780s–1859:** *Correspondence*. Humboldt made thousands of letters—scientific reports, recommendations, patronage requests, political interventions, data exchanges, and personal communications—but did not make them as one titled work. The still-growing digital edition therefore presents correspondence as a documentary corpus; its current online selection is not evidence of the corpus’s completeness. ([Edition Humboldt Digital letters](https://edition-humboldt.de/briefe/index.xql))
- **1835–1859:** *Recommendations and petitions on behalf of scientists, artists, travelers, and politically vulnerable correspondents*. These brief texts are performative documents: they seek appointments, money, access, protection, or publication and thus act more like institutional protocols or interventions than descriptive literature. They resist the category “work” while remaining things Humboldt deliberately made. ([Edition Humboldt Digital](https://edition-humboldt.de/briefe/index.xql))

### Projects that remained open

- **c. 1796–1862:** *Physique du monde / physical description of the universe*. Humboldt conceived a comprehensive “physics of the world” decades before *Kosmos*. The intention became lectures and four lifetime volumes but was never completed according to its full plan; as a project, it exceeds the published book called *Kosmos*. ([Edition Humboldt Digital](https://edition-humboldt.de/?l=en))
- **1799–1859:** *Global network of comparable environmental measurements*. Through instruments, observation instructions, tables, correspondence, observatories, and coordinated travelers, Humboldt built a distributed scientific project for comparing climate, altitude, magnetism, and living distribution. It has no single title or bounded text, and that resistance to work-status is essential to describing what he made. ([Edition Humboldt Digital](https://edition-humboldt.de/?l=en); [Sämtliche Schriften Digital](https://humboldt.unibe.ch/text))
