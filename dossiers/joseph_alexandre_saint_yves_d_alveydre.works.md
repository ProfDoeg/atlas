## Works by Joseph Alexandre Saint-Yves

### Poetic and dramatic works

- **1874:** *Le Retour du Christ: appel aux femmes*. An anonymously published religious and social appeal, attributed to Saint-Yves by his principal bibliographer, Jean Saunier; contemporary readers disputed or misidentified its authorship because no author appeared on the book ([scholarly discussion](https://archipel.uqam.ca/19333/1/D4967.pdf)).
- **1877:** *Testament lyrique*. A large poetic cycle organized into books associated with stages of Saint-Yves’s life; prose portions were also issued separately as *Les Clefs de l’Orient* ([L’Initiation Traditionnelle](https://www.linitiation.eu/images/revues/L-Initiation-Traditionnelle-2025-numero-1.pdf)).
- **1877:** *Les Clefs de l’Orient*. Three prose “keys”—on birth, sex and love, and death—extracted and reconfigured from *Testament lyrique*. It is therefore simultaneously an independent book and part of another obra ([L’Initiation Traditionnelle](https://www.linitiation.eu/images/revues/L-Initiation-Traditionnelle-2025-numero-1.pdf)).
- **1878:** *Le Mystère du progrès*. A “tragédie héroïque” or historical drama in five acts with choruses, rather than a political treatise despite its title ([contemporary bibliography](https://www.cercle-papus.com/ouvrages/papus-donato-almanach-de-la-chance-1910.pdf)).
- **1889:** *Le Poème de la reine*. A short ceremonial poem dedicated, with permission, to Queen Victoria; an English verse translation by the Earl of Lytton circulated separately and should not be counted as another Saint-Yves composition ([BnF notice](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb312857866)).
- **1889:** *Maternité royale et mariages royaux: Danemark, Suède, Angleterre, Grèce, Russie, Hanovre, France*. A twenty-one-page dynastic and esoteric poem, later advertised as an adaptation of the mysteries of Odin ([BnF notice](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb31285778k); [contemporary bibliography](https://www.cercle-papus.com/ouvrages/papus-donato-almanach-de-la-chance-1910.pdf)).
- **1889:** *L’Empereur Alexandre III: épopée russe*. A political-dynastic epic and an archéométrique experiment in poetic form, described by its early promoters as an adaptation of an alphabet of twenty-eight letters ([contemporary bibliography](https://www.cercle-papus.com/ouvrages/papus-donato-almanach-de-la-chance-1910.pdf)).
- **1890:** *Jeanne d’Arc victorieuse: épopée nationale*. A national epic presenting Joan’s celestial vocation and terrestrial mission; it is poetry, sacred history, and political symbolism at once ([Albert Verwey’s contemporary study](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/verw008proz06_01/verw008proz06_01_0002.php)).
- **1900:** *Souvenir du jeudi 20 septembre 1900*. A privately printed commemorative suite containing *Amrita*, *Credo*, *Bénédiction*, and *L’Étoile des Mages*; the advertised limitation to one hundred copies makes the suite an occasional object as much as a text ([contemporary bibliography](https://www.cercle-papus.com/ouvrages/papus-donato-almanach-de-la-chance-1910.pdf)).

### “Missions”: social-theoretical obras and manifestos

- **1882:** *Mission actuelle des souverains* (also issued as *Mission des souverains, par l’un d’eux*). A programmatic address to rulers proposing “synarchy”; variants dated 1882 and 1884 appear to be retitled issues of one printing rather than distinct works ([Saunier’s bibliography](https://www.linitiation.eu/telechargement/L-Initiation-1979-1.pdf)).
- **1882:** *Mission des ouvriers*. A social and political manifesto addressed to labor, forming one part of the projected series of “Missions” ([Saunier’s bibliography](https://www.linitiation.eu/telechargement/L-Initiation-1979-1.pdf)).
- **1882:** *La Synarchie: discours prononcé au Congrès international d’arbitrage et de fédération de la paix*. A six-page congress address: a manifesto in the strong sense, intended to institute an international political order rather than merely describe one. Saunier knew only one copy when compiling his bibliography ([Saunier’s bibliography](https://www.linitiation.eu/telechargement/L-Initiation-1979-1.pdf)).
- **1884:** *Mission des Juifs*. A 947-page religious-historical and political synthesis that treats Jewish and Christian esotericism as the organic foundation of European society; later tables and two-volume issues are apparatus and editions, not new obras ([Saunier’s bibliography](https://www.linitiation.eu/telechargement/L-Initiation-1979-1.pdf)).
- **1886 [first public edition 1910]:** *Mission de l’Inde en Europe, mission de l’Europe en Asie: la question du Mahatma et sa solution*. A purported report on Agarttha framed as a geopolitical and initiatory mission. The text was printed in 1886 but suppressed—according to Papus, nearly the entire printing was destroyed after threats from India—and survives through a single reported copy used for the posthumous facsimile; the reason for suppression remains unresolved ([Saunier’s bibliography](https://www.linitiation.eu/telechargement/L-Initiation-1979-2.pdf)).
- **1887:** *La France vraie, ou la Mission des Français*. A national history, political program, and partially autobiographical “Mission”; its hybridity makes it more than either memoir or treatise ([Saunier’s bibliography](https://www.linitiation.eu/telechargement/L-Initiation-1979-1.pdf)).
- **1887:** *Vœux du Syndicat de la presse économique et professionnelle de France*. A six-page collective petition associated with a syndicate over which Saint-Yves exercised strong influence. Because it speaks institutionally, it resists treatment as an exclusively individual work even though Saunier includes it in the Saint-Yves bibliography ([Saunier’s bibliography](https://www.linitiation.eu/telechargement/L-Initiation-1979-1.pdf)).
- **1888:** *Les États-généraux du suffrage universel*. A constitutional-political proposal issued through the Syndicat de la presse économique: a protocol for reorganizing representation by social function rather than simply an essay ([Saunier’s bibliography](https://www.linitiation.eu/telechargement/L-Initiation-1979-1.pdf)).
- **1889:** *Le Centenaire de 1789 — sa conclusion*. A centennial intervention that attempts to turn commemoration into a political conclusion and program; it belongs to the Syndicat’s collective publication activity ([Saunier’s bibliography](https://www.linitiation.eu/telechargement/L-Initiation-1979-1.pdf)).
- **1889:** *L’Ordre économique dans l’électorat et dans l’État*. A report addressed to the bureau and patronage committee of the Syndicat de la presse économique and professionnelle; its form is institutional protocol rather than autonomous book ([Sorbonne fonds inventory](https://www.linitiation.eu/telechargement/L-Initiation-1981-2.pdf)).

### Memorial and polemical interventions

- **1885:** *Les Funérailles de Victor Hugo*. An eight-page occasional memorial dated 1 June 1885 and signed “par l’auteur des Missions”; it is a public intervention tied to an event, not a full-length book ([Sorbonne fonds inventory](https://www.linitiation.eu/telechargement/L-Initiation-1981-2.pdf)).
- **16 July 1885:** Untitled article in *Le Rappel*. A polemical newspaper response denying Victor Émile Michelet’s allegations that Saint-Yves had plagiarized Fabre d’Olivet; Saunier identifies the article but supplies no independent title ([Saunier’s bibliography](https://www.linitiation.eu/telechargement/L-Initiation-1979-2.pdf)).
- No fixed date or title: article or articles in the *Journal des débats* affirming Saint-Yves’s Catholic—meaning “universal”—position. Their existence is reported by Édouard Schuré, but Saunier could not locate or date them; they resist secure treatment as individually established works ([Saunier’s bibliography](https://www.linitiation.eu/telechargement/L-Initiation-1979-2.pdf)).

### Scientific and industrial projects

- **1879–1880:** *De l’utilité des algues marines* / *Les Algues marines: leur application à l’hygiène, à l’alimentation, à l’industrie*. A practical research project on medical, nutritional, and industrial uses of seaweed, embodied both in a publication and in a memorandum dated 21 February 1880 to the president of the Académie de médecine; the title varies between witnesses ([Sorbonne fonds inventory](https://www.linitiation.eu/telechargement/L-Initiation-1981-2.pdf)).
- **1890s–1909:** *L’Archéomètre*. A Gesamtkunstwerk and unfinished project: a colored circular instrument and a proposed universal system joining alphabets, number, color, music, architecture, religion, and cosmology. Saint-Yves made diagrams, tables, notes, scales, and instrumental models, but did not complete the book later published under this title ([Lyon manuscript catalogue](https://ccfr.bnf.fr/portailccfr/jsp/index_view_direct_anonymous.jsp?record=eadcgm%3AEADC%3ALYOF010023); [publication history](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%27Arch%C3%A9om%C3%A8tre)).
- **By 1903:** *Étalon cosmologique*. A physical or graphic standard preserved as a tracing mounted on a wooden roller. It is an archéométrique instrument rather than a conventional text, and the archival inventory does not give it a firmer date ([Sorbonne fonds inventory](https://www.linitiation.eu/telechargement/L-Initiation-1981-2.pdf)).
- **1903:** *Étalon archéométrique* (French patent no. 333,393). A patented measuring and design instrument, filed 26 June and granted 19 September 1903; the patent specification is a protocol for operating part of the larger Archéomètre project ([Sorbonne fonds inventory](https://www.linitiation.eu/telechargement/L-Initiation-1981-2.pdf)).
- **1904–1906:** *Means of Designing Scales, for Applying the Musical Scale to Architecture, the Fine-Arts, Graphic and Plastic Trades and Industries*. The British manifestation of the archéométrique patent project. The archival inventory records an application of 23 June 1904 accepted 1 December 1904, while a later study calls it a 1906 patent; that dating discrepancy should remain visible ([Sorbonne fonds inventory](https://www.linitiation.eu/telechargement/L-Initiation-1981-2.pdf); [later study](https://www.scribd.com/document/506377283/Initiation-2008-4-pages-241-320)).
- No individual dates or titles: thirty-one printed archéométrique plates and tables. These are constituent visual objects of the Archéomètre rather than thirty-one securely separable works; the archival catalogue supplies no titles with which to force such a separation ([Sorbonne fonds inventory](https://www.linitiation.eu/telechargement/L-Initiation-1981-2.pdf)).

### Posthumously manifested and editorially composite works

- **1909:** *La Théogonie des patriarches: Jésus, Moïse—adaptations de l’Archéomètre à une nouvelle traduction de l’Évangile de saint Jean et du Sepher de Moïse*. A posthumous archéométrique translation-project accompanied by extracts from *Mission des Juifs*, an introduction by the Amis de Saint-Yves, and drawings by Gabriel Goulinat. The published object is consequently composite, not wholly identical with a manuscript authored and finalized by Saint-Yves ([Saunier’s bibliography](https://www.linitiation.eu/telechargement/L-Initiation-1979-2.pdf)).
- **1913:** *L’Archéomètre: clef de toutes les religions et de toutes les sciences de l’Antiquité; réforme synthétique de tous les arts contemporains*. A posthumous reconstruction assembled by the Amis de Saint-Yves under Papus’s direction from unfinished papers. The book itself resists attribution as a completed Saint-Yves work: 1911 is the date of its prefatory warning, while the first edition was announced and issued in 1913; earlier bibliographies and reprints often misdate it 1910 or 1911 ([publication history](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%27Arch%C3%A9om%C3%A8tre); [Saunier’s bibliography](https://www.linitiation.eu/telechargement/L-Initiation-1979-2.pdf)).

### Unpublished and incompletely individuated manuscript works

- No fixed date: *Le Rituel du sanctuaire psychurgique — Le G∴ Hiér∴*. An unpublished ritual manuscript listed in the Sorbonne papers; the inventory does not establish whether Saint-Yves regarded it as complete or intended it for publication ([Sorbonne fonds inventory](https://www.linitiation.eu/telechargement/L-Initiation-1981-2.pdf)).
- No fixed date: manuscript literary corpus including *À Madame la comtesse*, *À Strauss*, *Adieu*, *L’Amour*, *L’Amour et les sens*, *Appel au Christ*, *Au bivouac*, *La Création*, *Déesse et femme*, *Les Deux Voix*, *L’Enfance*, *La Lyre*, *La Mort*, and *Paris brûlé*. Some pieces were intended for *Testament lyrique*, but the archive does not permit all of them to be classified confidently as finished, autonomous works; the corpus therefore resists a clean work/not-work boundary ([Sorbonne fonds inventory](https://www.linitiation.eu/telechargement/L-Initiation-1981-2.pdf)).
- No fixed date: Sanskrit texts and translations. The Sorbonne inventory records a bundle but provides neither individual titles nor enough evidence to distinguish original compositions, exercises, transcriptions, and translations; it should not be inflated into fictitious named works ([Sorbonne fonds inventory](https://www.linitiation.eu/telechargement/L-Initiation-1981-2.pdf)).
- No fixed date: chemistry notebook and miscellaneous research notebooks. These are things Saint-Yves made and used, but the surviving descriptions mix research notes, accounts, and household records; they resist being called works in the bibliographical sense ([Sorbonne fonds inventory](https://www.linitiation.eu/telechargement/L-Initiation-1981-2.pdf)).
