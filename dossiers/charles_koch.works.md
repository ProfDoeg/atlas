## Works by Charles de Ganahl Koch

### Management framework and continuing project

- **c. 1960s–present:** *Market-Based Management® / Principle Based Management™*. An evolving organizational framework created by Koch from economic, psychological, and managerial models and tested within Koch companies; it is a project and protocol rather than a fixed text. The later name *Principle Based Management* marks its continuing reformulation, not an independently completed work. ([Koch, Inc.](https://www.kochinc.com/our-principled-approach), [Charles Koch official site](https://www.charleskoch.com/principled-leader))

### Books and working manuals

- **2007:** *The Science of Success: How Market-Based Management Built the World’s Largest Private Company*. A management treatise and protocol explaining the five dimensions of Market-Based Management; Koch reportedly conceived it partly as a training text for employees entering the rapidly enlarged company. Some secondary lists call a supposed 2006 work *Market Based Management* a separate book, but Koch’s current official works catalogue does not: absent reliable evidence of an independent publication, it is not counted separately here. ([Google Books/Wiley bibliographic record](https://books.google.com/books?id=ZG1pPhxlFAIC), [Koch official works catalogue](https://www.charleskoch.com/principled-leader))

- **2015:** *Good Profit: How Creating Value for Others Built One of the World’s Most Successful Companies*. A business book combining corporate history, argument, and an instructional restatement of Market-Based Management; its publisher dates the first publication to 13 October 2015. ([Penguin Random House](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/253407/good-profit-by-charles-g-koch/))

- **2020:** *Continually Transforming Koch Industries Through Virtuous Cycles of Mutual Benefit*. A short employee-oriented working manual subsequently made publicly downloadable; it presents organizational and personal transformation as an unfinished, recurrent process rather than a completed program. Koch Industries published it on 22 June 2020. ([Google Books bibliographic record](https://books.google.com/books/about/Continually_Transforming_Koch_Industries.html?id=-uN3zQEACAAJ), [official text](https://principlebasedmanagement.com/content/dam/koch-pbm-assets/documents/books/Continually%20Transforming%20Koch%20VCMBs.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf))

- **2020; revised 2026:** *Believe in People: Bottom-Up Solutions for a Top-Down World*. A jointly authored social and philanthropic manifesto-protocol by Koch and Brian Hooks, first published by St. Martin’s Press on 17 November 2020. The 24 March 2026 “America’s 250th Anniversary Edition” is a revised manifestation of the same obra—with a new preface and foreword—not a separate work. ([Macmillan’s 2020 catalogue](https://webservices.macmillan.com/macmillan-us/maccatalog/assets/previous/SMP_Fall-2020_11_2019.pdf), [Simon & Schuster 2026 edition](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/Believe-in-People/Charles-Koch/9781637634905))

- **2026:** *Becoming a Principle-Driven Leader: 41 Principles to Build an Enduring Business*. A jointly authored book by Charles Koch and Chase Koch, published 5 May 2026. It is both corporate narrative and protocol: forty-one principles are offered as transferable instructions for leadership and organizational practice. ([Simon & Schuster](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/Becoming-a-Principle-Driven-Leader/Charles-Koch/9781637635247), [Charles Koch official site](https://www.charleskoch.com/becoming-a-principle-driven-leader))
