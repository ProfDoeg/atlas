## Works by Friedrich Fröbel

### Educational institutions and social projects

- **1816–1831:** *Allgemeine deutsche Erziehungsanstalt*. An experimental school founded at Griesheim and transferred to Keilhau in 1817; it was simultaneously an institution, a communal way of life, and the practical testing ground for Fröbel’s educational theory. Its making was collective—especially with Wilhelm Middendorff, Heinrich Langethal, and Fröbel’s wife Wilhelmine—so it cannot be treated as Fröbel’s unaided creation. [Friedrich Fröbel Museum](https://froebel-museum.de/pages/de/friedrich-froebel/leben-und-werk.php)

- **1829–1831:** *Volkserziehungsanstalt in Helba*. A projected national educational establishment near Meiningen, announced publicly by Fröbel but never fully realized; as an unfinished institutional project, its intention is the principal surviving work. [Froebelweb](https://www.froebelweb.de/index.php/froebels-schaffen/keilhauer-schriften)

- **1831–1833:** *Erziehungsanstalt auf Schloss Wartensee*. A Swiss educational institution established by Fröbel in the canton of Lucerne; political and confessional opposition prevented it from becoming permanent. [Friedrich Fröbel Museum](https://froebel-museum.de/pages/de/friedrich-froebel/leben-und-werk.php)

- **1833–1838:** *Erziehungsanstalt in Willisau*. The continuation in another place of Fröbel’s Swiss school project; Fröbel left Switzerland in 1836, while Wilhelm Middendorff continued the institution until 1838. Its shifting direction makes authorship institutional rather than exclusively personal. [International Froebel Society—Deutschland](https://ifs-d.de/2020/02/10/froebel-biographie/)

- **1835–1836:** *Elementarschule und Lehrerbildung am Waisenhaus Burgdorf*. A school and teacher-training experiment conducted while Fröbel directed the Burgdorf orphanage; it was a pedagogical manifestation inside an existing institution, not a separately authored “work.” [Friedrich Fröbel Museum](https://froebel-museum.de/pages/de/friedrich-froebel/leben-und-werk.php)

- **1837–1844:** *Anstalt zur Pflege des Beschäftigungstriebes der Kindheit und Jugend*. A Blankenburg play-and-activity institute in which Fröbel developed and manufactured early play materials; its successive names and functions resist treatment as one stable school or object. [BBF](https://bbf.dipf.de/de/sammeln-entdecken/besondere-bestaende-sammlungen/zur-geschichte-des-kindergartens-und-der-paedagogik-friedrich-froebels)

- **1839:** *Spiel- und Beschäftigungsanstalt mit Anstalt für Kinderführer*. A demonstration institution for children coupled to the training of their adult guides. It was a transitional manifestation between the 1837 activity institute and the kindergarten project. [International Froebel Society—Deutschland](https://ifs-d.de/2020/02/10/froebel-biographie/)

- **1840:** *Allgemeiner deutscher Kindergarten*. A model institution and movement inaugurated at Blankenburg on 28 June 1840: not merely a school, but a project joining children’s play, mothers’ education, trained adults, gardens, songs, and specially designed materials. Its realization depended on collaborators and subscribers, while “kindergarten” subsequently became the generic name for institutions substantially different from Fröbel’s original conception. [Friedrich Fröbel Museum](https://froebel-museum.de/pages/de/friedrich-froebel/leben-und-werk.php)

- **1842:** *Kindergärtnerinnenkurse in Blankenburg*. Courses devised to train women in Fröbel’s kindergarten practice. They were a pedagogical protocol enacted through teaching rather than a fixed authored text. [Friedrich Fröbel Museum](https://froebel-museum.de/pages/de/friedrich-froebel/leben-und-werk.php)

- **1849–1852:** *Anstalt für allseitige Lebenseinigung durch entwickelnd-erziehende Menschenbildung*. A kindergarten and training institution opened at Bad Liebenstein and transferred to Marienthal in 1850; commonly described as Fröbel’s school for kindergarten teachers, it was also his final attempt to embody his system in a community. [Bundeszentrale für politische Bildung](https://www.bpb.de/themen/bildung/dossier-bildung/276523/fruehkindliche-bildung-erziehung-und-betreuung-eine-zeitleiste/)

### Manifestos, prospectuses, and institutional declarations

- **1820:** *An unser deutsches Volk. Zweyte Anzeige von einem für den Zweck einer allgemeinen Erziehungs-Anstalt in Keilhau bey Rudolstadt im Thüringischen sich gebildeten Vereine*. The first of the six “Keilhau writings”: both a prospectus for the school and a manifesto seeking public participation in a national educational project. Although issued in the name of an association, standard Fröbel bibliographies attribute the text to Fröbel. [Froebelweb](https://www.froebelweb.de/index.php/froebels-schaffen/keilhauer-schriften)

- **1821:** *Durchgreifende, dem deutschen Charakter erschöpfend genügende Erziehung ist das Grund- und Quell-Bedürfniß des deutschen Volkes*. A sequence of propositions presenting education as a national necessity; it acts as an appeal and manifesto rather than merely describing Keilhau. [Thüringer Literaturrat](https://www.thueringer-literaturrat.de/autorenlexikon/froebel-friedrich-wilhelm-august/)

- **1821:** *Grundsätze, Zweck und inneres Leben der allgemeinen deutschen Erziehungsanstalt in Keilhau bey Rudolstadt*. A programmatic account of Keilhau’s principles, curriculum, and daily life—part institutional prospectus, part educational protocol, and part philosophical statement. [Froebelweb](https://www.froebelweb.de/index.php/froebels-schaffen/keilhauer-schriften)

- **1822:** *Die allgemeine deutsche Erziehungsanstalt in Keilhau bey Rudolstadt betreffend*. A public declaration concerning the institution and its intended national system of education. Its address to prospective supporters makes it a manifesto-like intervention rather than a neutral report. [Thüringer Literaturrat](https://www.thueringer-literaturrat.de/autorenlexikon/froebel-friedrich-wilhelm-august/)

- **1822:** *Ueber deutsche Erziehung überhaupt, und über das allgemeine Deutsche der Erziehungsanstalt in Keilhau insbesondere*. A philosophical and polemical pamphlet connecting Keilhau with a proposed German education; its national terminology belongs to its historical argument and should not be silently generalized into Fröbel’s later idea of “human education.” [Froebelweb](https://www.froebelweb.de/index.php/froebels-schaffen/keilhauer-schriften)

- **1823:** *Fortgesetzte Nachricht von der allgemeinen deutschen Erziehungsanstalt in Keilhau*. The sixth Keilhau pamphlet, continuing the institution’s public self-description and appeal for recognition and support. [Froebelweb](https://www.froebelweb.de/index.php/froebels-schaffen/keilhauer-schriften)

- **1829:** *Anzeige von der Volkserziehungsanstalt in Helba bei Meiningen*. A prospectus issued by Fröbel as the proposed institution’s director; because the establishment was not fully realized, this announcement is also the clearest surviving manifestation of the project. [Froebelweb](https://www.froebelweb.de/index.php/froebels-schaffen/keilhauer-schriften)

- **1836:** *Erneuerung des Lebens erfordert das neue Jahr 1836*. A programmatic text announcing renewal in education and life after Fröbel’s Swiss period. It belongs to his manifesto-like small writings, not to the later editorial constructions called his “books.” [Friedrich Fröbel overview](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_Fr%C3%B6bel)

- **1839–1840:** *Dokumente zur Gründung des Allgemeinen deutschen Kindergartens*. A group of appeals, plans, subscription papers, and founding declarations rather than one securely bounded work; modern listings collect them under a retrospective title. Their plurality and occasional collaborative or administrative authorship should be preserved rather than collapsed into a single book. [Friedrich Fröbel overview](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_Fr%C3%B6bel)

- **1840:** *Plan zur Gründung eines Kindergartens*. A founding plan that gives institutional form to the kindergarten idea. It is a project-text whose intended effects—subscriptions, organization, and establishment—matter as much as its descriptive content. [Fröbelseminar catalogue](https://www.froebelseminar.de/buecher)

- **1840:** *Katechismus der Kindheitspflege*. A question-and-answer protocol for early-childhood care associated with the kindergarten project. The surviving text was only partly printed in the twentieth century, so its textual completeness and original status remain uncertain. [Beiträge zur Fröbelforschung](https://www.froebelseminar.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Buchkatalog/Beitraege_zur_Froebelforschung__Helmut_Heiland___Hrsg._.pdf)

- **1847:** *Plan der Bildungsanstalt für Kinderpflegerinnen und Erzieherinnen*. A plan for a women’s training institution, surviving through later publication from manuscript sources. It is best classified as an institutional project rather than evidence that the proposed establishment already existed in the form described. [Beiträge zur Fröbelforschung](https://www.froebelseminar.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Buchkatalog/Beitraege_zur_Froebelforschung__Helmut_Heiland___Hrsg._.pdf)

### Books and unfinished treatises

- **1826:** *Die Menschenerziehung, die Erziehungs-, Unterrichts- und Lehrkunst, angestrebt in der allgemeinen deutschen Erziehungsanstalt zu Keilhau. Erster Band: Bis zum begonnenen Knabenalter*. Fröbel’s principal systematic treatise, joining philosophy, developmental account, curriculum, and educational protocol. “Erster Band” announces a larger project, but no corresponding second volume appeared; the published book is complete as an object and incomplete as the projected system. [Friedrich Fröbel Museum](https://froebel-museum.de/pages/de/friedrich-froebel/leben-und-werk.php)

- **1827:** *Autobiographischer Brief an den Herzog von Meiningen*. A long autobiographical account written as a letter, not conceived as a freestanding book; later collected editions detached and published it as Fröbel’s “Autobiographie.” Its resistance to the book category is bibliographically important. [Friedrich Fröbel’s collected writings bibliography](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/5a/Froebel%27s_chief_writings_on_education_%28IA_froebelschiefwri00frrich%29.pdf)

- **1844:** *Kommt, laßt uns unsern Kindern leben! Mutter-, Spiel- und Koselieder*. A pedagogical Gesamtkunstwerk combining Fröbel’s songs, play directions, philosophical commentary, Friedrich Unger’s picture plates, and Robert Kohl’s musical settings. Fröbel conceived and edited the whole, but the images and music are collaborative creations; later editions variously retitled, rearranged, and separated its components. [International Froebel Society—Deutschland](https://ifs-d.de/2020/02/10/froebel-biographie/)

### Periodicals and serial works

- **1826:** *Die erziehenden Familien. Wochenblatt für Selbstbildung und die Bildung Anderer*. A weekly periodical founded and edited by Fröbel as an extension of *Die Menschenerziehung* into continuing instruction and exchange. Its individual numbers contain several textual forms, so the serial is both one editorial obra and a collection of smaller works. [BBF](https://bbf.dipf.de/de/sammeln-entdecken/besondere-bestaende-sammlungen/zur-geschichte-des-kindergartens-und-der-paedagogik-friedrich-froebels)

- **1833–1836:** *Grundzüge der Menschenerziehung*. A Swiss-period journal through which Fröbel presented his educational ideas and institutional work. It is an editorial platform containing contributions rather than a single-authored book, and surviving descriptions differ over its precise run. [Friedrich Fröbel overview](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_Fr%C3%B6bel)

- **1838–1840:** *Kommt, laßt uns unsern Kindern leben! Ein Sonntagsblatt für Gleichgesinnte*. A serial edited by Fröbel to organize a community around early-childhood education and explain the first play gifts. Publication was intermittent—1838 and 1840 are represented—so it should not be imagined as an uninterrupted weekly run. [Friedrich Fröbel Museum](https://froebel-museum.de/pages/de/sammlungen-und-forschung/spezialbibliothek.php?lang=DE)

- **1850:** *Friedrich Fröbels Wochenschrift: Ein Einigungsblatt für alle Freunde der Menschenbildung*. A weekly editorial project joining reports, games, instructions, and polemical advocacy. Its collective contributions and practical purpose make it a movement-organizing instrument as well as a text. [BBF](https://bbf.dipf.de/de/sammeln-entdecken/besondere-bestaende-sammlungen/zur-geschichte-des-kindergartens-und-der-paedagogik-friedrich-froebels)

- **1851–1852:** *Zeitschrift für Friedrich Fröbels Bestrebungen zur Durchführung entwickelnd-erziehender Menschenbildung in allseitiger Lebenseinigung*. Fröbel’s final periodical and public vehicle for his educational movement. It continued into the year of his death and contains materials by Fröbel and his circle, so editorship, contribution, and authorship must not be treated as identical. [UNESCO/Helmut Heiland study](https://medull.webs.ull.es/pedagogos/FROEBEL/heiland.pdf)

### Play materials, occupations, and embodied protocols

- **1837–1850:** *Spielgaben und Beschäftigungsmittel*. An ordered pedagogical system joining reusable geometric “gifts” to transformative activities such as folding, weaving, sewing, pricking, laying, drawing, and modelling. Fröbel made the sequence, relations, and protocols rather than personally manufacturing every surviving object; later Froebelians expanded and renumbered the system, sometimes to ten gifts or roughly twenty gifts and occupations, although only six numbered gifts are securely associated with Fröbel’s lifetime system. [Froebel Trust](https://files.froebel.org.uk/production/documents/pwbookmedium.pdf)

- **from 1837:** *Erste Gabe: Ball*. A soft ball, eventually presented in six colours and used with movement, speech, and song. It is not simply a toy but an object-plus-performance protocol, and the familiar standardized boxed set reflects development and later manufacture rather than one immutable prototype. [Froebel gifts](https://www.das-froebelhaus.de/en/friedrich-froebel/froebel-gifts)

- **from 1837:** *Zweite Gabe: Kugel, Würfel und Walze*. A set of solid bodies designed to disclose contrast, mediation, rest, and movement through handling and comparison. Different historical sets and accounts vary in their fittings and presentation, so the “thing” is fundamentally a relational exercise rather than one definitive manufactured specimen. [Froebel gifts](https://www.das-froebelhaus.de/en/friedrich-froebel/froebel-gifts)

- **late 1830s:** *Dritte Gabe: der einmal allseitig geteilte Würfel*. A cube divided into eight equal cubes, conceived for transformation into “forms of life,” “forms of beauty,” and “forms of knowledge.” The material set and its prescribed succession of play together constitute the work. [Froebel Trust](https://files.froebel.org.uk/production/documents/Froebels-gifts-and-block-play-today.pdf)

- **late 1830s:** *Vierte Gabe: der zweimal längs geteilte Würfel*. A cube divided into rectangular prisms or “bricks,” extending the building possibilities of the third gift. Numbering and names were stabilized through Fröbel’s publications and later manuals, while individual manufactured sets are instances rather than the obra itself. [Froebel gifts](https://www.das-froebelhaus.de/en/friedrich-froebel/froebel-gifts)

- **by 1840:** *Fünfte Gabe: der mehrfach geteilte Würfel*. A construction set combining small cubes with diagonally divided pieces. It enlarges the vocabulary of possible forms and belongs to a cumulative system, not to a collection of independent toys. [Froebel gifts](https://www.das-froebelhaus.de/en/friedrich-froebel/froebel-gifts)

- **by 1840:** *Sechste Gabe: der mehrfach in Säulen und Prismen geteilte Würfel*. The last of the six gifts securely codified during Fröbel’s lifetime, dividing the cube chiefly into elongated and flattened rectangular forms. Later lists that assign additional numbered gifts to Fröbel conflate his own system with subsequent Froebelian systematization. [Froebel gifts](https://www.das-froebelhaus.de/en/friedrich-froebel/froebel-gifts)

- **late 1830s–1852:** *Beschäftigungen*. A deliberately open body of productive practices—including paper folding, cutting, weaving, sewing, drawing, stick and tablet laying, beadwork, and clay modelling—in which material is changed rather than merely rearranged. These resist being called discrete works: Fröbel made a grammar of activity, while particular patterns and manuals were often elaborated by collaborators and later followers. [Froebel Trust](https://files.froebel.org.uk/production/documents/pwbookmedium.pdf)

- **late 1830s–1852:** *Bewegungs-, Kreis- und Ballspiele*. A repertoire of communal games joining gesture, rhythm, song, imitation, and social participation. Their transmission through demonstrations, periodicals, letters, and later editorial collections makes the boundary between Fröbel’s own invention and the Froebel circle’s collective practice uncertain. [BBF](https://bbf.dipf.de/de/sammeln-entdecken/besondere-bestaende-sammlungen/zur-geschichte-des-kindergartens-und-der-paedagogik-friedrich-froebels)

- **1840s:** *Kindergarten-Gartenarbeit*. The arrangement of common and individual garden plots as an educational medium connecting personal responsibility with communal life. It is a spatial and seasonal protocol rather than a designed garden surviving as a single fixed object. [Froebelweb chronology](https://www.froebelweb.org/webline.html)

### Instructions for the play system

- **1838–1840:** *Anleitungen zur ersten Gabe*. Instructions, demonstrations, songs, and short explanations for play with the ball, published principally through the *Sonntagsblatt* and associated brochures. Bibliographic reconstruction treats these as a textual group because their original boundaries and issue contexts do not yield one stable book. [German National Library contents record](https://d-nb.info/956821944/04)

- **1838–1840:** *Anleitungen zur zweiten Gabe*. A corresponding body of protocols for sphere, cube, and cylinder. Like the first-gift material, it was dispersed across serial and pamphlet forms and was later reorganized by collected-work editors. [German National Library contents record](https://d-nb.info/956821944/04)

- **1838–1840:** *Erste Übersicht des Spielganzen*. A synoptic presentation of the early play system, intended to show relations among gifts and activities rather than to describe an isolated toy. Its status is diagrammatic and programmatic: a map of a developing system. [German National Library contents record](https://d-nb.info/956821944/04)

- **1838–1840:** *Zweite Übersicht des Spielganzen: Vierte und fünfte Gabe*. A later overview extending the system through the fourth and fifth gifts. It records a stage in an evolving project and should not be read as the final canonical numbering adopted by later manuals. [German National Library contents record](https://d-nb.info/956821944/04)

- **1850:** *Des Kindes Zeichenlust*. An instructional text on children’s drawing published within Fröbel’s weekly periodical. It is a protocol for developing activity, not a freestanding art-theory book. [German National Library contents record](https://d-nb.info/956821944/04)

- **1850:** *Anleitung zum Papierfalten*. A protocol for folding as one of the kindergarten occupations, published in the context of Fröbel’s periodical work. Later manuals greatly enlarged “Froebelian” paper folding, so their complete pattern repertories should not automatically be attributed to him. [German National Library contents record](https://d-nb.info/956821944/04)

- **1850–1851:** *Die Vermittelungsschule* (also called *Vorschule*). A projected mediating stage between kindergarten and school, articulated in Fröbel’s late writings. It remained more project than fully established institution, and its variant terminology signals that the conception was still developing. [German National Library contents record](https://d-nb.info/956821944/04)

- **1851:** *Anleitung zum rechten Gebrauch der dritten Gabe des entwickelnd-erziehenden Spiel- und Beschäftigungsganzen: des einmal allseitig geteilten Würfels „die Freude der Kinder“*. A detailed play protocol for the third gift and one of Fröbel’s last separately published works. Its title insists that the divided cube belongs to a larger “play-and-occupation whole,” resisting classification as either merely a manual or merely a toy description. [Thüringer Literaturrat](https://www.thueringer-literaturrat.de/autorenlexikon/froebel-friedrich-wilhelm-august/)

### Correspondence and manuscript body of work

- **1808–1852:** *Briefe*. A large surviving corpus of personal, institutional, theoretical, and autobiographical letters, now presented in a scholarly complete digital edition. It resists classification as one obra: individual letters acted as plans, reports, appeals, instructions, and relationships, while survival is necessarily incomplete and some texts became “works” only through posthumous editorial extraction. [BBF complete letter edition](https://editionen.bbf.dipf.de/exist/apps/briefedition-friedrich-froebel/about.xml)

- **dates various:** *Unpublished plans, notes, drafts, and pedagogical manuscripts*. Fröbel’s collected and selected writings contain texts first published from manuscripts after his death, but their editorial titles, boundaries, and states of completion are not always Fröbel’s own. They are a manuscript remainder rather than a securely enumerable shelf of finished works; inventing uniform titles or dates for them would falsify that condition. [Collected-edition record](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_Fr%C3%B6bel)
