# Ioan Petru Culianu (1950-1991), research report

*2026-08-31, for `ioan_petru_culianu.journey.json`. Historian of religions; born Iasi, murdered in a lavatory stall in Swift Hall, University of Chicago, 21 May 1991; no suspect ever charged.*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named · **[R]** reconstruction or tradition. A claim marked *corrected* differs from what the research pool delivered; the correction is stated, never folded in silently.

**Structural warning.** A large part of the reachable record descends from one document: the chronology "O biografie" by his sister **Tereza Culianu-Petrescu**, in Sorin Antohi's memorial volume *Omul si opera*. It is dated to the day, is the only source for perhaps a third of the life, and is the account of a sister who was also his editor and posthumous publisher. Where it stands alone the claim is **[R]**, however precise the date looks.

## 1. Iasi, 1950-1967

- Born **5 January 1950**, Iasi. **[A]** English Wikipedia, and independently the **FBI investigative file** (archive.org OCR, "Ioan Culianu Part 01 of 01", fetched directly).
- Birth house **13 Strada Sfantul Atanasie**, Sararie slope. **[A]**, upgraded in verification: **7iasi.ro**'s property investigation confirms address, neighbourhood, **1801** build date and Bogdan/Culianu ownership. The 1926 acquisition remains **[R]**.
- Father **Sergiu-Andrei Culianu**, trained in mathematics and physics at Paris and law at Iasi, dies 1964 when Ioan is fourteen. **[R]**. *Contradiction flagged:* FBI file gives his birth as **1903**, family chronology **1904**. No third source. Mother **Elena, nee Bogdan**, b. **1907**, chair of inorganic chemistry at Alexandru Ioan Cuza University, **[A]** FBI file.
- Sister **Tereza**, b. **10 October 1945**. **[R]**, memorial volume only. *Corrected:* the pool's "almost five years older" is wrong in its own terms; Oct 1945 to Jan 1950 is four years and under three months.
- **Varatec monastery** summers; the former nun **Manea** teaches the children the prayer of the heart in a skeptical household. **[R]**, memorial volume only. Kept, *as tradition*, because it is the only reported source of his first contact with a technique of ecstasy, the subject of three of his books.

## 2. Bucharest, 1967-1972

- Enters in Romanian literature, transfers after a year to the **Italian section**, Faculty of Romance, Classical, and Oriental Languages. **[A]** Wikipedia. **December 1968**, candlelit carol procession to the **Grozavesti** dormitories, stopped and questioned: **[R]** and weak, **no arrest file** and no independent record located.
- **January 1970**, takes a Communist Party card. **[A]**, and unusually well attested for an embarrassing fact: it is in his own sworn **INS affidavit of December 1989**, reproduced in the FBI file, with his reason. Three verbatim sentences from that affidavit are quoted in the journey.
- **1971**, sends Eliade papers on *coincidentia oppositorum* in Bruno and on sun-and-moon symbolism. **[R]**. The **mentorship itself** is **[A]** via the Chicago finding aid.
- Graduates in Italian; thesis on **Ficino and Renaissance Platonism** under **Nina Facon**. **[A]** for degree and year; title and the reported 9.87/10 average **[R]**, from his CV/affidavit as cited in the dossier. *Contradiction flagged:* the chronology lens dates graduation **May 1972**, the geography lens **June 1972**.

## 3. The crossing, 1972-1973

- **4 July 1972**, leaves on a one-month Italian scholarship to **Perugia** and does not return. **[A]**, the date is his own ("I defected from Romania on July 4, 1972, never again to return there"); Wikipedia independently gives Perugia and July 1972 asylum.
- Roughly five months in Italian refugee facilities; recognised as a Convention refugee **13 December 1972**. **[R]** for span and date; **[A]** for Italian asylum in 1972.
- **Which camp is unresolved.** The pool names both **Trieste** and **Latina**, and its own researcher states that identifying the Trieste facility as **Padriciano** is *their inference*, not a sourced fact. Both stops are kept, both **[R]**.
- **Suicide attempt** by wrist-cutting during the camp months; survives. **[R]**, family testimony and Ted Anton's interviews as characterised in secondary literature, not verified against Anton directly. Stated flat in the journey, one sentence, no interpretation attached.
- Weeks in a religious community near the **Via Appia Antica**; no house or order named anywhere reached, pin corridor-approximate. **March 1973**, Perugia's *Corsi di Alta Cultura* and the **Lupatelli Prize**; **1973**, expelled in absentia from the party. All **[R]**.

## 4. Milan and the first doctorate, 1973-1975

- **October 1973**, **Domus Nostra**, residence of the Universita Cattolica del Sacro Cuore, under **Ugo Bianchi**. Institution **[A]**; residence name, hardship and mentorship **[R]**. **September 1974**, first meeting with **Eliade**, Paris, **[R]**; no venue documented, so the journey uses the **canonical atlas Paris pin 48.8566 / 2.3522**.
- **1975**, a semester at the Divinity School with Eliade and **Carsten Colpe**; New York and New England; meets **Hans Jonas**. **[R]**. *Tag corrected:* the pool tagged it **[A]** on the Chicago finding aid, which was fetched in full and documents only his **later** Chicago career. The New School pin for the Jonas meeting is likewise the researcher's inference; the journey folds Jonas into the Chicago stop rather than staging a scene at a guessed address.
- **November 1975**, *dottore in lettere*, Milan, thesis on Jonas and Gnosticism. **[A]**. *Contradiction flagged:* Wikipedia titles it *pensiero **contemporaneo***; the 1985 book from it (L'Erma di Bretschneider, per OpenLibrary) is *pensiero **moderno***.

## 5. Groningen, 1976-1987

- **University of Groningen**, assistant in Romanian language and culture. **[A]** for institution and the **1976-1985** span. *Corrected:* the **November 1976** start and **Willem Noomen**'s sponsorship are **not** in the cited source; downgraded to **[R]**. **1978**, *Mircea Eliade*, applying Popperian falsifiability to Eliade's phenomenology, **[A]** on the publication record.
- **19 July 1979** meets **Carmen-Zoe Georgescu**; **28 December 1979** civil marriage; **1980** celebration aboard the *Normandie*. All **[R]**. Wikipedia confirms only that he was divorced by his death. The two dates may be two ceremonies; unresolved.
- **17 June 1980**, *doctorat de troisieme cycle*, Sorbonne, under **Michel Meslin**. **[R]**. *Tag conflict resolved downward:* the pool tagged it **[A]** in one lens and **[R]** in another, and nothing verifying it was fetched, **theses.fr** and **data.idref.fr** having **refused connections**.
- **1984**, *Eros et magie a la Renaissance. 1484*. **[A]** via the Chicago Press page for the 1987 translation. Pinned at **Groningen**, where he was writing, not Paris, where it was published. **1986-1987**, fellow at **NIAS**, Wassenaar, **[R]**, uncorroborated.
- **Paris IV**, *doctorat d'Etat*, *Recherches sur les dualismes d'Occident*. **[A]** for the degree; ***year contested, day dropped.*** Wikipedia gives "January 1987"; the theses.fr identifier the pool itself cites, **1986PA040274**, encodes **1986**, and OpenLibrary lists 1986. theses.fr unreachable. The pool's "10 January 1987" is unsupported and removed.
- **30 January 1987**, divorce final. **[R]**; surviving material is partisan on both sides and none of it is a judicial finding.

## 6. Chicago, 1986-1991

- Return to the Divinity School. ***Date contested, tag corrected.*** The pool gave "March 1986, visiting professor and Hiram Thomas Guest Lecturer" as **[A]**. The finding aid says only "visiting lecturer, then professor", **with no dates**; Wikipedia gives **"University of Chicago (1988-1991)"**; the "Hiram Thomas" title is in nothing fetched. Downgraded to **[R]**, March dropped, both readings named.
- **22 April 1986**, Eliade dies after eight days in hospital. Death **[A]**; **Culianu's presence at the bedside [R]**, and not stated in the Eliade file, which has a stop for the same hospitalisation. The **May 1986** memorial at **Rockefeller Chapel** likewise has a stop there naming **only Saul Bellow**; Culianu's co-reading is **[R]**.
- **Hillary Susan Wiesner**: engagement at his death **[A]**. ***Two errors corrected.*** (1) The pool called her a Chicago Divinity School doctoral student; Wikipedia describes her at his death as "a 27-year-old graduate student at **Harvard University**". (2) The pool said the Chicago finding aid "independently confirms" the engagement; the finding aid, read in full, **never mentions her**. A false source attribution, withdrawn.
- Travel with Wiesner to Italy, France, Switzerland, **Egypt** and **Israel**, 1987-1990. **[R]**, undated, and **no source names one site on either itinerary**; the pool's Giza and Jerusalem pins are admitted by its own researcher to be plausible waypoints, not stations. The journey keeps **one** stop, Jerusalem, whose confidence field says so; the Giza pin is dropped rather than carry two invented pins for one undocumented trip.
- **November 1989**, apartment burglary after appeals for his brother-in-law **Dan Petrescu**. ***Tag split.*** The FBI file confirms **only the address** (1700 East 56th Street, Apt 906) **[A]**; its text was searched specifically for a break-in and contains none, so **burglary, items and date are [R]**. Culianu read it as political; his student Greg Spinner and the police read it as an ordinary neighbourhood burglary, and no political connection was established.
- **1990**, the column *Scoptophilia* in **Lumea libera romaneasca** (New York) against the National Salvation Front and Iliescu; **spring 1990**, visiting chair at Siena/Arezzo. Both **[R]**.
- **5 April 1991**, interview in the Bucharest weekly **22**. **[A]** only in the sense that the FBI file records investigators' attention to it; the text was not refetched. **10-14 April**, the **Drake Hotel** and former King Michael I, **[R]**; the reported death threat there appears in **none of the police records reviewed** and surfaced only after the murder. **16-17 May**, hosts **"Other Realms: Death, Ecstasy and Otherworldly Journeys in Recent Scholarship"**, tells a contact threats have increased and cancels a Romania trip, both **[R]**.

## 7. 21 May 1991

- Shot once through the back of the head with a **.25 automatic** in a stall of the **third-floor men's room, Swift Hall**; door locked from inside; **wallet and cash undisturbed**; no weapon recovered. **[A]**, FBI file read directly.
- ***Stall number corrected.*** The pool said stall 4. The file says verbatim: *"One fired bullet and a Winchester .25 automatic spent cartridge were found on the floor to the right of the toilet in stall number five, closest to the window."* It says "fourth stall" elsewhere. The verbatim reading is used; **the contradiction is in the source** and is left standing. **Time:** the file gives both **1:05** and **1:15 p.m.**; Wikipedia says "at noon". The journey says "about one o'clock" and lists all three.
- ***Hospital corrected.*** The pool said **Northwestern Memorial**, eight miles north, pronounced dead **2:15 p.m.**, citing the finding aid. The FBI file says he was taken to *"the University of Chicago Hospital located at 58th Street and Ellis Street"*, a few hundred metres away, and the finding aid gives **no hospital and no time**; the 2:15 p.m. pronouncement **appears in no source fetched** and is dropped. Age 41 and the gunshot-homicide finding stand **[A]**.
- **No suspect has ever been indicted, tried or convicted.** **[A]** finding aid, verbatim: "Neither the motive nor the killer's identity is known."

- **April 1992**: Antohi records that no plaque and no talk of one existed at Chicago and that "all traces of his presence there had been carefully erased by the administration". **[A]** as testimony, a documented **negative** iconography finding, and a stop. ## Interlock decisions

**Named in campas**, each a direct or one-directional relation of his own. **`mircea_eliade`**: correspondent from 1971, mentor, subject of his 1978 monograph, at whose deathbed he stood; named in seven stops, and the Eliade file already carries a **named Culianu stop** (the Eliade Papers, 1990), so the relation is bidirectional and already canon. **`giordano_bruno`**: subject of the 1971 paper sent to Eliade and a central figure of *Eros et magie*; named twice. **`frances_yates`**: *Giordano Bruno and the Hermetic Tradition* (1964) is the ground *Eros et magie* revises twenty years on; named once.

**Refused.** **`giovanni_pico_della_mirandola`**, proposed on the strength of the 1972 Ficino thesis: Ficino is what the sources name, and **Pico appears in nothing fetched about Culianu's own writing**, so naming him would be shared subject area, not a relation. **`julius_evola`** and **`stefan_odobleja`**, see above. **Buenos Aires** and **Kaaba / Temple Mount**, both negative findings, deliberate: no South American travel, correspondence or reception is documented, and his engagement with Islamic and Jewish mysticism is scholarly, never pilgrimage. **Neither canonical pin is inherited**, and nothing was invented to supply one. He did travel to Israel, but no source pins it to the Temple Mount.

**Canonical pins inherited byte-identical:** Paris (unlocated) `48.8566 / 2.3522`, shared by the Eliade, Bruno and Pico files; and from `mircea_eliade.journey.json`, the Chicago hospital `41.7887 / -87.6034`, Rockefeller Chapel `41.78858 / -87.59704`, and the Special Collections reading room `41.7924 / -87.5997`.

**One pin deliberately not inherited.** The Eliade file pins the Divinity School at **41.7897 / -87.5997**, which the interlock pass identified as Swift Hall while flagging that as its own inference, tagged **[R]**. All six Swift Hall stops here use the Nominatim geocode, **41.7889 / -87.6001**, about **90 metres** away and invisible at globe scale. If the operator wants the files pinned identically, this is the line to change.

**Eight pins in the pool were wrong** and were replaced with Nominatim geocodes; each stop's `date_confidence` states its own old value, new value and error. Worst first: **Varatec** 18 km, **Padriciano** 4.3 km, **Eternitatea** 3.6 km (wrong side of Iasi), **NIAS** 1.7 km, **Sfantul Atanasie** 775 m, **Grozavesti** 675 m, **1700 E 56th St** 500 m, **Swift Hall** 340 m. Eternitatea also resolves a conflict between the geography and afterlife lenses, which pinned the same cemetery differently.

## Honest gaps

**No plot number and no burial register** for the grave. **No arrest file** for the 1968 procession, which may not have happened as remembered. **Which refugee camp**, Trieste or Latina, and therefore where the suicide attempt happened. **No venue** for the 1974 Paris meeting, **no site** for the Jonas meeting, **no station** for Egypt or Israel, **no mooring** for the *Normandie*: four places where the atlas wants an address and the record has none.

**Both French doctorates rest on secondary characterisation only**, because `theses.fr` and `data.idref.fr` refused connections on repeated attempts. The largest verification failure of the pass.

**The murder is documented; the investigation is not.** The FBI file is a redacted OCR text; the police case file, the medical examiner's report and the witness statements were not reached at all. Every theory about who killed him lies outside what was verified here; **the journey advances none of them.** Two threats are recorded as reported, both with their uncorroborated status stated. The Iron Guard theory is neither adjudicated nor insinuated: the journey says what Culianu wrote and when, says nobody has been charged, and does not connect the two.
