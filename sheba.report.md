Quotes cross-checked against Sahih International / Noble Quran.
- **Kebra Nagast** (Ge'ez, c. 1314-1322; Budge translation) — the Ethiopian canon: Tamrin's report, Makeda's conversion (ch. 28), the oath and the water/honey trick (ch. 30), the ring (ch. 31), the birth of Menelik (ch. 32), Menelik's return via Gaza (ch. 33-38), the carrying-off of Zion (ch. 48), the enthronement at Aksum and the Solomonic dynasty.

## The tradition's own time-folds and geographic splits
- **The double homeland.** The canon locates the queen's realm in two places at once, and this journey honors BOTH: **Marib in Yemen** (Sabaean capital, the Great Dam, Mahram Bilqis — the Arab/Quranic Bilqis) and **Aksum in Ethiopia** (Makeda's highland throne — the Kebra Nagast). Segment 1 opens in Yemen and deliberately crosses to Ethiopia; the caravan is routed up the historical Arabian incense road, while the return and the dynasty resolve to Aksum. This split is the myth, not an error to be reconciled.
- **The name-fold.** She is unnamed in scripture; "Bilqis" is later Arabic tradition, "Makeda" the Ethiopian. Both are carried in the title.
- **The conversion, twice.** Quran 27:44 (submission at the glass floor) and Kebra Nagast ch. 28 (renouncing the sun) are treated as one theophanic turn narrated by two scriptures — placed together in the Jerusalem segment.
- **Deep-time anachronism.** The 10th-c. BC dates are traditional/inferred throughout (marked honestly); the medieval True Cross Sibyl episode is a much later Christian overlay folded into the itinerary at the point the legend places it (on the road to Solomon).

## Judgment calls
- **Dates** are inferred anchors around a traditional c. 966 BC (Solomon's reign), spacing the caravan legs plausibly and giving Menelik ~22 years before his return (Kebra Nagast) — all flagged `traditional`/`inferred`; none `attested`.
- **Coordinates** point to the actual traditional sites (Marib dam and Awwam temple to 4 decimals; Aksum's stelae field, Dungur palace, and the Mary of Zion church distinguished; incense-road oases at Najran, Al-'Ula, Tayma).
- **Quotes** are used only where a canon records words. The frankincense grove, the Sibyl/bridge, Menelik's birth and Gaza journey carry `null` quotes rather than invented ones.

## The five richest episodes
1. **The hoopoe's report and the letter in the beak** (Quran 27:22-31) — a bird crosses between two thrones bearing "In the name of Allah… come to me in submission."
2. **The glass floor** (Quran 27:44 + Targum Sheni) — the crystal pavement mistaken for water; the bared legs; deception becomes conversion.
3. **"The half was not told me"** (1 Kings 10:7) — the queen's breath fails; reality outruns every rumor.
4. **The oath and the stolen water** (Kebra Nagast ch. 30) — the salted feast, the bowl by the bed, "Why hast thou broken thine oath?", and the conception of Menelik.
5. **The carrying-off of Zion** (Kebra Nagast ch. 48) — the Ark lifted by night, wood set in its place, the wagons riding above the earth to Aksum — where the corpus's hidden relic-thread and the Lion of Judah begin.
