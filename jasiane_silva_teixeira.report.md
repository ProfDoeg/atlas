## 0. The state of the evidence, stated first

This is a subject with no biography. Every reachable trace of her is a Brazilian news item published between February 2017 and May 2025, and of roughly forty such items exactly **one** was retrieved as full body text (Se Ligue Bahia, 27 March 2025). Everything else here is a headline plus a date. **[A: retrieval log]** The following are **entirely absent** from every source reached, and are recorded as gaps rather than filled by inference:

- birth date, birthplace, parents, siblings, schooling, occupation before the trade, year of entry into it **[gap]**
- her husband's name, and whether he is dead or merely former **[contradiction, §6]**
- her daughter's name or age; the founding, structure, territory or membership of the faction she is said to lead **[gap]**
- any docket number, minister or written opinion from the two 2019 STF decisions **[gap]**
- anything between 31 Aug 2020 and 24 Jan 2025 **[gap of four years five months, §5]** or after 25 May 2025 **[gap of fifteen months, §8]**

The prior research pool carried a harder failure: **no web query on the subject had executed successfully at all**, its search budget exhausted at 200/200. Its afterlife lens partially corrected itself, recovering the announced-book datum and the Ficco arrest. **[A: verified pool]** The recovery here was done through Google News RSS, which is not rate-limited the way WebSearch is; four queries produced the chronology below. **[R: method note]**

Two headline-only items settle it to a high but not certain standard (Mais Região 13 Feb 2017, UOL 7 Mar 2017); neither names her, and the identification rests on the coincidence of date, sex, faction, PCC linkage and location. **[A as to the headlines; R as to the identification]**

## 1. Origins: nothing

No source reached gives a birth date, a birthplace, a family, or a year of entry into the trade. **[gap]** The journey file therefore opens at Vitória da Conquista in 2017 with a file-opening marker rather than a birth stop. That city, third of Bahia, on the plateau where the BR-116 comes north out of Minas Gerais, is attested as the base of the faction whose financial operations she managed. **[A: Se Ligue Bahia, 27 Mar 2025, full text]** Nothing places her *birth* there, and this report does not.

The faction is named in two January 2025 items as the **Bonde do Neguinho**. **[A: Diário do Centro do Mundo and Metrópoles, 26 Jan 2025, headline-only]** No reachable source describes it further; it has been printed in headlines about her since 2017 without once being characterised. **[gap]**

**7 March 2017.** UOL: a PCC-linked faction leader called "Dama de Copas" is spreading terror in the south of Bahia. **[A, headline-only; the identification is R, see §0]**

**Not established for 2017:** when she entered the deck (the headline reports the fact, not the act); which card was assigned; whether the February "líder de facção" and the March "Dama de Copas" are one person. **[gaps]**

## 3. Late September 2019: the first arrest

The densest week in the record. All ten items below are **[A, headline-only]**.

- **25 Sep**, G1: arrested in **Biritiba Mirim**, SP. G1, same day: convicted over the **death of a prison officer**. Extra: the police consider her the largest trafficker in Bahia.
- **26 Sep**, G1: her lawyer says she is innocent, "**Não pode pagar pelos atos do ex-marido**". jornaldosudoeste carries the same arrest in Bahian regional framing.
- **27 Sep**, G1: transferred to Bahia "**com mãos e pés algemados**". Mais Região: she "**desembarca em Salvador**".
- **28 Sep**, Jornal Correio: members of a **rival faction** arrested in Vitória da Conquista.
- **30 Sep**, G1: denies the accusations at a press conference and **says she will write a book**. Jornal Correio publishes the video, "**Não sou um monstro, não sou um bicho**".

**Arrest date.** The reports of the 25th describe the arrest as accomplished, so the operative date is **24 September 2019** **[R: inference from reporting lag, matching the January 2025 pattern of a Friday arrest reported on Saturday]**. No source states the day. **Biritiba Mirim** is a municipality in the Alto Tietê, the reservoir belt east of São Paulo city **[A: named by G1]**; no address, operation name or arresting force is given for 2019 **[gap]**, in contrast to January 2025, where the Ficco is named.

**The conviction over a prison officer's death** is the only conviction of any kind attested anywhere in the record. **[A: G1 headline]** No sentence, court, date or victim's name was reached. **[gap]** **The disembarkation in Salvador** is reported with the word *desembarca*; the journey file pins it at the airport as a flagged inference, since no source names the airport or the mode of transport. **[R]** **The rival-faction arrests four days later** are connected to her removal by nobody; the causal reading in the journey campa is **[R]**.

**The press conference and the book.** The only sustained first-person material in the record: she denies the accusations and announces a memoir **[A]**, and the video of the same day is the only verbatim utterance of hers anybody has published **[A]**. The prior pool's afterlife lens is right about how to state it: an *announced but unrealised* memoir, not an absent premise. **[A: verified pool, corrected afterlife entry]**

## 6. January 2025: the second arrest, and the widow contradiction

**Friday 24 January 2025.** Found in São Paulo by the **Força Integrada de Combate ao Crime Organizado (Ficco)** with **R$ 66.000 in cash**, **ten mobile telephones** and trafficking documents. **[A: G1, 25 Jan, cash and fugitive status; Se Ligue Bahia, 27 Mar, phones and documents; Instagram item, 25 Jan, the Ficco and the Friday]** No São Paulo municipality is named by any source. **[gap]**

**25 to 27 January.** Metrópoles, SBT News, Jornal Correio, A Tarde, UOL, Diário do Centro do Mundo and Bnews all lead with the PCC alliance rather than with her. **[A, all headline-only]** Minha Bahia, 27 January: the Bahian courts decree her arrest, three days *after* São Paulo has already made it. **[A, headline-only; URL returned 404]**

### The contradiction, flagged and not resolved

G1, quoting her lawyer, 26 Sep 2019: she should not pay for the acts of her **ex-marido**. Estadão, 30 Jan 2025: she is a **viúva** and a **sócia do PCC** in her own right. A Tarde, 27 Mar 2025: she is the **primeira dama do PCC**.

Three incompatible accounts of one relationship, across six years. *Ex-marido* makes her a separated woman inheriting a man's liabilities; *viúva* makes her a widow; *sócia* makes her a partner with standing of her own, and *primeira dama* takes that standing back out and re-derives it from a marriage. **No reachable source names the man, dates a death, dates a separation, or notices the conflict.** **[contradiction, not adjudicated]**

This is the most load-bearing unresolved question in her biography, whether she inherited an operation or built one, and it rests on the choice between two Portuguese nouns in two headlines. Recorded as open at the "São Paulo: the widow" stop; it stays unresolved because Estadão and G1 both refuse direct retrieval. **[A: retrieval log]**

## 7. March to May 2025: the only substantive account of what she does

**26 March 2025.** Jornal Correio: the courts determine her transfer to Bahia. **[A, headline-only]**

**27 March 2025, the one full text.** Se Ligue Bahia, retrieved complete, the evidentiary spine of the journey file's second half: **[all A]**

A Bahian court orders **preventive detention** at the request of the **Ministério Público da Bahia**, on a charge of **money laundering**. The **Gaeco** investigation holds that she moved illicit proceeds **through third-party accounts** to obscure their origin, and that she **led a criminal faction operating in Vitória da Conquista, managing financial operations**. **Four pending criminal cases** for homicide and trafficking stand against her; she is listed on the **Baralho do Crime**; the January seizure was **R$ 66.000**, **ten telephones** and trafficking documents. Detention is ordered to prevent flight and obstruction, with transfer to Bahia for prosecution.

One wrinkle: the article dates the decision to "quarta-feira" (26 March) but is published on the 27th. **[minor discrepancy, left standing]**

This is the only place in the record where anyone says **what she actually does inside the organisation**. She is the treasury, not the gunman; every other characterisation, *maior traficante*, *homicida*, *líder*, is a label, and this is a job description. **[A]**

**Same day, three other retrospectives, all headline-only. [A]** **Estadão**: the third-party account is her **under-age daughter's**; the daughter is the only family member appearing anywhere in the record, has no name or age, and is never mentioned again **[gap]**. **O Globo**: "Baralho do crime, **quatro mortes e fuzis**", the fullest retrospective written, body unreachable, its four deaths consistent with Se Ligue Bahia's four pending cases **[R as to the equation]**. **A Tarde**: "Traficante, homicida e **primeira dama do PCC**", see §6.

Also 27 March: **G1**, **Terra** and **Correio Braziliense** run profiles **[A, headline-only]**; the synchrony indicates a shared wire or a coordinated MP-BA release **[R]**.

**3 April 2025.** Bnews: a judicial decision determining transfer to a Bahian prison unit, a week after Jornal Correio reported the same thing. **Two decisions or one reported twice: undeterminable from headlines** **[open]**; Bnews returned HTTP 403. **No source confirms the transfer was ever executed.** **[gap]**

**25 May 2025.** G1 and A Tarde: a man described as her **parceiro**, himself a Bahian faction leader, is arrested in São Paulo. **[A, headline-only]** He is not named, and whether *parceiro* means a business partner or a domestic one is **not specified by either outlet**, a fourth variant of the husband problem. **[gap]**
