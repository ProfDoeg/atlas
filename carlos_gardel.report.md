# Carlos Gardel: research report

*Journey file `carlos_gardel.journey.json`: 9 segments, 45 stops, register "national mythology: the
canon is true", calendar gregorian. The years 1890-1935 are themselves a position in the dispute of
section 1.*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or a claim resting on one
retrospective testimony. Gardel's facts fall in two classes: what happened on a stage is dated to
the day by press and discography and is solid, what he was is dated by his own word and is not.
This report concentrates on the second class, on corrections to the pool, and on what was
unreachable.

## 1. The two birth certificates

**French account [A].** Toulouse civil register: Charles Romuald Gardès, born Hôpital de La Grave,
11 December 1890, to Berthe Gardès, unmarried, 25, laundress; father "unknown"; midwife Jenny Bazin
witnessing. Both Wikipedia editions carry this as the consensus and name Collier, Barsky, Turón,
Ruffié and Galopa on that side. On 22 December 1890 Berthe signs a statement naming Paul Laserre,
a married man, as father; the recognition is never made official [A, en.wikipedia]. **The register
itself was not fetched**: it reaches this file second-hand, and `date_confidence` says so.

**Uruguayan account [R].** Tacuarembó tradition and Gardel's own 1920 declaration: born Valle Edén,
11 December 1887, to Carlos and María Gardel; es.wikipedia carries it as the *hipótesis
uruguayista*. No 1887 document corroborates any part of it. Day and month match the Toulouse
register exactly and the year does not, which is worth stating rather than explaining.

**What is attested about the 1920 act is far thinner than the pool implied.** es.wikipedia says
only: *"En 1920 tramitó ante el consulado uruguayo en Buenos Aires una certificación de
nacionalidad y una cédula de identidad."* No month, no folio, no witnesses. The witness names
**Juan Lagisquet** and **José Razzano** came from the dossier alone and are **dropped**. The pool
also conflated two documents: the Uruguayan certificate and the Argentine *cédula* of 4 October
1920 are papers from different states, and es.wikipedia dates 4 October to the cédula. Two stops.

**He contradicts himself in writing, inside eleven years [A on both sides].** 1920 consulate:
Tacuarembó, 1887. 1931 witnessed document: *"I am French, born in Toulouse, 11 December 1890, son
of Berthe Gardes"* (en.wikipedia citing Alvarez 1995). 1933, El Telégrafo of Paysandú: *"I'm
Uruguayan, born in Tacuarembó."* June 1935, Caretas: *"My heart is Argentine, but my soul is
Uruguayan."* Posthumously, a handwritten will produced by Armando Defino stating Toulouse. The file
carries all five and adjudicates none.

**Quote-attribution corrections made to the pool.**

* Caretas: the pool credited Alvarez (1995) via en.wikipedia note 29, but raw wikitext shows the
  sentence carries **no footnote**; the Alvarez ref sits on the next sentence. quote_source now
  reads "given inline and uncited in Wikipedia (English)". The pool also gave place `n/a`, lat 0,
  lng 0; Caretas is of Antioquia and June 1935 is the month Gardel died at Medellín, so the stop is
  pinned there, not at null island.
* Teatro Ópera recollection: the pool called the underlying publication unnamed. It is named in the
  raw wikitext, **revista Antena (Buenos Aires), 18 March 1933**, cited in Barsky & Barsky (2004),
  p. 89. Note the 1933 utterance against the c.1902 events recalled.
* **"Gardel sings better every day" is not in the journey file.** It is a posthumous saying *about*
  him, not his speech, and the pool's NPR citation for it is misassigned. A quote field would have
  made it look like words he spoke.

## 2. Childhood and San Nicolás, 1893-1906

The crossing, the arrival under the name Charles Gardès, and the residence at Calle Uruguay 162
with Berthe working on Calle Montevideo are attested [A, en.wikipedia]. **Contradiction,
unresolved:** en.wikipedia gives arrival **11 March 1893**, fr.wikipedia says Argentine documents
attest **9 March 1893**. The file uses 11 March and flags it; neither day is settled.

**Schooling [A as to year, gap as to place].** es.wikipedia puts him at the Colegio Salesiano Pío
IX, Buenos Aires, 1901. **The pool cited es.wikipedia's "Colegio Pío IX", which is the MONTEVIDEO
school** (Av. Lezica 6375) and cannot support the pin. The Buenos Aires school is in Almagro, by
the Basílica de María Auxiliadora y San Carlos; the file pins it approximately at -34.611, -58.420
and says the coordinate awaits a Buenos Aires source. The pool's -58.3919 points at Constitución.

**Theatre apprenticeship [R].** Claque work under "Patasanta" around 1900, stagehand at the Teatro
Ópera in 1902; the pool's "the following year" is arithmetically wrong, es.wikipedia having 1900
and 1902. Both are tradition, so the Ópera stop is tagged [R] against the pool's [A]. **Café
O'Rondeman [R]**: location confirmed ("en Agüero y Humahuaca"), the year an ordering device.

**Dropped.** The confitería La Frazenda in Bajo Belgrano, c.1903 [R], has no address in any source
consulted, so rather than invent a pin it is recorded here as a gap. Corrientes 1553 (1904-1914) is
attested [A] but carried no scene the Calle Uruguay stop lacks.

## 3. The duo, 1911-1925

**The 1911 Razzano meeting is [R], not [A] as the pool had it.** es.wikipedia is explicit that the
year is *"ubicado por Razzano en este año"*: his own memoirs, one retrospective testimony, no
document. Those memoirs put the encounter in a friend's house on Guardia Vieja near the market. The
**Pasaje Carlos Gardel is that block of Guardia Vieja, renamed later**, not "the site" of the
meeting. **Recording debut [A]**: seven Columbia discs through Casa Tagini, contracted 1912, issued
1913, the pin approximate for want of an address.

**Armenonville: date disputed inside one source family, not resolved.** es.wikipedia's Gardel
article gives **14 June 1914**; its own *Armenonville* article says the pair performed there at a
New Year party on **1 January 1914**. The file dates the stop 1914-01-01 and states both. Two
further pool errors: the cabaret was on Avenida Alvear in **Recoleta**, near where the Automóvil
Club Argentino now stands, not Palermo; and the "70 pesos nightly" figure is **uncited in either
article** and is not asserted. **Montevideo, Teatro Royal, 18 June 1915 [A]**, hemisphere corrected.

**Brazil tour: dropped, with reason.** The pool contradicts itself: its chronology assigns the 1915
tour to the "Compañía Dramática Rioplatense", its geography to **Elías Alippi's** company. The
venue (Theatro Municipal de São Paulo) is the researcher's inference, and the geography lens itself
advised dropping it rather than staging a scene at a theatre no source places him in. The pool's
"1915-11" month also collides with the 12 November 1915 *Juan Moreira* staging in Buenos Aires.
Recorded here as unreconciled rather than silently resolved.

**Shot in a street altercation, 10 December 1915 [A as to fact and day]**: no street, assailant,
cause or account of recovery in any source, so the gap is stated and the pin is the canonical
Buenos Aires coordinate. **Empire Theatre, Corrientes and Maipú**: the six-year engagement is
attested [A, es.wikipedia] but **the source gives no start year**, so the pool's "1917-1923
(approx.)" is the researcher's bracket; cut for stop count, flagged so no one restoring it inherits
invented dates.

## 4. Mi noche triste, 1917

**[A]** Year, composers (Castriota music, Contursi lyric) and roughly 10,000 copies, per
en.wikipedia; the 10,000 figure is **independently corroborated inside this corpus** by
`max_glucksmann.journey.json`, on its own sourcing rather than from this dossier.

**Correction.** The pool said Gardel "recorded it with Razzano". es.wikipedia's *Mi noche triste*
specifies **José Ricardo on guitar** and the **"Disco Gardel-Razzano" imprint** (later Odeón):
Gardel-Razzano is the label and billing, the sung voice is Gardel's alone. **The Glücksmann label
tie stays inference on both sides**, `max_glucksmann.journey.json` saying its source does not name
the label; the Gardel campa names Glücksmann's house on the contract, not on the disc.

**Flor de durazno, premiered 28 September 1917 [A as to date].** Two pool errors: the director is
**Francisco Defilippis Novoa** (es.wikipedia), not the pool's "Julio/José-credited" phantom, and
**Razzano's involvement is unsupported** and dropped. No premiere cinema is named, so the pin is
the canonical Buenos Aires coordinate.

## 5. Europe, the microphone, Paris

Gràcia) and Jean Jaurés 735, where the pool's own two lenses disagreed. Further hedges:

* Electrical recording in Barcelona, 1926: [A] as to fact, [R] as to studio, the Casa Odeón
  identification and coordinate being inferred. The 70,000 records in three months is carried by
  en.wikipedia as a report, not an audited number, and is worded as such.
* **The pool's claim of Gardel concerts at the Opéra Garnier across 1928-29 came from the dossier

  **Monte Carlo, 1929 [R], cut**: the source names cities and no venue, so the casino is inference.
* **The letter to Razzano**: text verbatim from es.wikipedia note 44 (ref "CartaGardelRazzano"),
  provenance [R], no archival repository given. Pinned at the canonical Paris coordinate.

## 6. The films

**Two studio periods the pool folded into one, now separated.**

* **Joinville-le-Pont, 1931-1932 [A]:** *Las luces de Buenos Aires*, *Espérame*, *La casa es
  seria*, *Melodía de arrabal*. The pool gave the range as **1931-1934, which is wrong**. Pin
  corrected to 48.8194, 2.4711 (fr.wikipedia, *Studios de Joinville*), ~1.3 km off in the pool.
* **Kaufman Astoria, Queens, 1934-1935 [A]:** *Cuesta abajo*, *El tango en Broadway*, *El día que
  me quieras*, *Tango Bar*. en.wikipedia: "All the films starring tango icon Carlos Gardel made in
  the United States were shot at Astoria Studios." Pin corrected to 40.7567, -73.9247, ~1.9 km off.

The pool put the whole song cluster (*Mi Buenos Aires querido*, *Volver*, *Cuesta abajo*, *Por una
cabeza*) at "New York and Paris" in 1934. **Mi Buenos Aires querido comes from Melodía de arrabal,
Joinville 1932**; *Por una cabeza* and *El día que me quieras* belong to Astoria. Split in two.
**January 1931 is a departure, not a start of filming [A]**: es.wikipedia has *"Enero de 1931:
nueva gira a Francia"* but *"Finales de abril de 1931: llega a París para filmar con Paramount en
Joinville"*, the months between spent on the Riviera.

**Nice is [R], not [A], and its pin is unverified.** es.wikipedia mentions Nice appearances but
does **not** name the Casino de la Jetée-Promenade; the pool tagged it [A] regardless, and no
fr.wikipedia page for the pier could be reached, so 43.6947, 7.2725 stands unchecked and reads too
far east (the pier stood off the Jardin Albert 1er, nearer 7.265). The same article frames the
dismissal of guitarist José Aguilar as provoked by a homophobic remark against Gardel, which the
pool's "during the run" flattened; the campa restores it. **The Cine Astral premiere of the Morera
shorts, 3 May 1931 [A]**, is folded into the shorts campa: Gardel was in France that night.

## 7. Medellín and after

**The crash, 24 June 1935 [A].** Additions from es.wikipedia, *Accidente aéreo de Medellín*:
**seventeen dead**; the second aircraft SCADTA's Ford 5-AT-D C-31 *Manizales*, seven aboard, pilot
Hans Ulrich Thoms; Gardel's pilot **Ernesto Samper Mendoza**; guitarist **José María Aguilar
Porras** among the F-31 dead, and named in the campa. en.wikipedia, *Olaya Herrera Airport*,
independently gives "Gardel and 16 others".

**The repatriation is [R].** en.wikipedia gives transport through New York and Rio de Janeiro and
two days lying in state at Montevideo, with the year only. Montevideo and the Chacarita burial are
dated with ordering months inside 1935 and say so; **no source consulted gives an arrival date in
any of the three cities**, and the widely repeated 1936 Buenos Aires arrival is not in the pool and
is not asserted. Rio and New York are named in the Montevideo campa rather than given stops.

**The tomb [A].** Life-size bronze, smiling, suit, left hand in the trouser pocket, right hand bent
at abdomen height; visitors place lit cigarettes between the fingers (Buenos Aires city tourism
board, Cementerio de la Chacarita page, fetched directly). **Sculptor and date of erection are
given by no source consulted.** Open gap. **Museo Casa Carlos Gardel [A]**: 1997 monument decree,
Eurnekian donation 2000, opened 4 March 2003, exhibiting authenticated copies of the Toulouse birth
certificate and the will. **UNESCO Memory of the World, 2003, is [R]**: es.wikipedia carries the
registration of Gardel's voice, it **could not be verified against UNESCO**, and so the pool's
closing observation pairing it with the museum opening as a 2003 "institutional consolidation" is
**not made** in the journey file.

**Folded into `suggested_refs` rather than staged as scenes**, being other people's acts at places
Gardel never went alive: the 1983 Toulouse monument by Martha Baez and Norberto Perlmutter in the
Jardins Compans-Caffarelli (fr.wikipedia says it faces *sa maison de naissance*, not the hospice
1.5 km away, so the pool's "facing the hospice" cannot be true); the Museo Casa Gardeliana, Carrera
45 no. 76-50, Manrique, Medellín, founded 1972 by Leonardo Nieto Jardón (es.wikipedia closes the
pool's gaps on address, founder and year); and the Museo Carlos Gardel at Valle Edén, inaugurated
on Gardel's own birthday, 11 December 1999.

## 9. Interlocks used, and interlocks refused

**Named in a campa (two).** Both direct, one-directional, contemporaneous.
**`angel_villoldo`**: Gardel and Razzano record Villoldo's *Cantar eterno* in 1917, two years
before Villoldo dies; Villoldo's file states that no meeting, letter or photograph connects the men
and that the transmission is musical only, and the Gardel campa says the same.
**`max_glucksmann`**: the 1917 exclusive contract, the Odeón studio inside the Grand Splendid from
1920, the radio debut of 1 October 1929. A documented business relation.

**Refused, shared geography only.** The map already shows co-location; naming these people in a
campa would assert a relation no source supports. `antoine_de_saint_exupery`, `le_corbusier`,
`victoria_ocampo`, `pablo_escobar`, and **`ceferino_namuncura`**, who entered the academy at
Colegio Pío IX, Buenos Aires, on 2 April 1901, the same year one strand of Gardel biography puts
Gardel at that school. Two boys possibly in one building is co-location, and the Gardel half rests
on the contested birth dispute; it is the strongest of the refused threads, and a source placing
them in one class or choir would make it real.

2. **`osvaldo_pugliese`, FORWARD.** Pugliese's 1980 Colombia tour, and his file's line that

3. **`joaquin_trincado_mateo`, FORWARD.** News of the crash reaches Trincado in Buenos Aires on 24

If the author wants any of these to reach into Gardel's file, that is a grant recorded in
`EXCEPTIONS.md`, not a call this pass made. No backward-direction relation was found: Gardel is not
