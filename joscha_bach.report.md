# Joscha Bach: research report
*Compiled 2026-09-03 for `joscha_bach.journey.json`. Subject living; coverage window closes with the reporting of late November 2025.*

Legend: **[A]** = attested, source named · **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition, or dossier assertion not independently confirmed by a fetched source. Contradictions between sources are flagged and left standing, not adjudicated.

**A standing correction that governs this whole file.** The Psychology Today profile "The Wizard of Consciousness" (4 September 2018), the richest single source for Bach's childhood, family, education, and personality, is by **George Musser**, not Matt Huston. The misattribution repeats across roughly a dozen items in the research pool and was corrected by direct fetch of `psychologytoday.com/us/articles/201809/the-wizard-consciousness`. Huston is a real Psychology Today editor, which is why the error reads as plausible. Every citation below and in the journey file names Musser.

## 1. Weimar and the mill, 1973–1989

**Birth.** Born in Weimar, then in the German Democratic Republic. Year and city corroborated across sources **[A, en.wikipedia, "Joscha Bach"]**. The date 21 December 1973 is widely repeated but no civil record has been produced behind it; day and month carried as **[R]** in effect, though universally reported.

**The mill.** Both parents were architecture students. They bought an old mill in the countryside southeast of Weimar and converted it into what the profile calls "an arcadia of sculpture gardens and concert nooks," rejecting the standardized design of Eastern Bloc housing **[A, Musser, Psychology Today, 4 Sept 2018]**. The exact site is unpublished. The journey file uses 50.95, 11.42, an approximate point over farmland southeast of Weimar, and says so in `date_confidence`.

**The parents.** Mother from a family of Communist politicians, able (per the profile) to hold party ideology and its domestic failure in mind at once; father an impulsive architect hostile to the East German political climate while acknowledging that socialism gave him economic security **[A, Musser]**. Self-reported in interview; no independent genealogical documentation located. Recorded as a gap.

**The father and the wall.** "My father would sometimes get up at the breakfast table staring at a wall, deciding that it would be a door instead." Verified verbatim **[A, Musser]**. The profile separately records him beginning to hammer the doorway while still in pyjamas.

**The Commodore 64.** Bach designed variants of board and arcade games, including Parcheesi and Missile Command, because he had no peers to play with **[A, Musser]**. No surviving childhood code located.

**End of school.** The profile states he finished high school as the Berlin Wall fell **[A, Musser]**. Neither school nor examination date is published. **Contradiction flagged:** the pool cites en.wikipedia here, but the en.wikipedia article carries no schooling detail at all, so Musser is the only source. **Second problem, unresolved:** a December 1973 birth makes him fifteen in November 1989, awkward against an Abitur normally taken at eighteen. No source addresses the arithmetic; the journey file states the coincidence and asserts no age.

## 2. The bicycle, the year abroad, Berlin, 1990–2001

**The recumbent bicycle.** He built one and rode more than 3,000 miles through the United States and Canada, after school and before university **[A, Musser]**. Route, entry points, and dates unpublished. The journey file's coordinate (41, -95) is a nominal mid-continent placeholder and says so.

**The year abroad and text compression.** The profile confirms a year abroad during which a course on text compression transformed his understanding of cognition as data compression **[A, Musser]**. It does **not** name New Zealand or the University of Waikato, and neither does en.wikipedia, which the pool cited for it. Waikato is plausible (Ian Witten's text-compression group was based there) but is **[R]**, on the dossier alone. The coordinate -37.7878, 175.3119 verifies at 0.48 km from the university, so if the claim is right the pin is right.

**Humboldt University of Berlin.** Computer science and philosophy; degree around 2000, rendered variously as an M.A. and as a German Diplom **[A, en.wikipedia; the M.A./Diplom variance is flagged, not resolved]**.

**Marriage.** He met the artist who became his wife at a birthday party in Berlin **[R]**. Venue and year unpublished; the source chain is thin. Retained because the "Elves" quotation depends on the relationship, and hedged in `date_confidence`.

**Publication.** "Supporting Textual Input by Using Multiple Entropy Models" (2001) **[R, dossier bibliography; not independently fetched]**.

## 3. Osnabrück and MicroPsi, 2001–2009

**The Institute of Cognitive Science.** Advisers Dietrich Dörner, Kai-Uwe Kühnberger, and Hans-Dieter Burkhard, all three confirmed in the en.wikipedia infobox **[A]**. Dörner's Psi theory of motivated cognition is the direct ancestor of MicroPsi; the descent is grounded by the advisory relation itself.

**Coordinate correction.** The pool gave 52.2705, 8.0553 for Wachsbleiche 27, Osnabrück. That point is 2.07 km southeast of the address, which geocodes to approximately **52.2852, 8.0366** (OSM/Nominatim). The journey file uses the corrected pin.

**MicroPsi node nets.** Bach and Ronnie Vuine, "Designing Agents with MicroPsi Node Nets," KI 2003 proceedings, pp. 164–178 **[A, DBLP]**. The conference city is not established by anything fetched, so the stop sits at Bach's own institute rather than at an assumed venue.

**The doctorate. Contradiction flagged and not resolved.** Both en.wikipedia and de.wikipedia give the PhD as **2006**. The dossier gives the dissertation as publicly dated **March 2007**. The Osnabrück institutional repository record sits behind an Anubis challenge page and could not be read. The journey file dates the stop 2006 and states the discrepancy in `date_confidence`.

**The monograph.** *Principles of Synthetic Intelligence: PSI: An Architecture of Motivated Cognition*, Oxford University Press, 2009 **[A as to publication]**. The bibliographic detail (xxv + 374 pp., ISBN 978-0-19-537067-6) rests on the dossier's listing; a direct fetch of the OUP product page returned no content **[R for the physical description]**. It is his only authored book. No translation, no collected edition, no second monograph located.

## 4. Berlin companies and the Chaos Communication Congress, 2008–2016

**txtr. Date correction, nine years.** The dossier dated the founding to **1999**. de.wikipedia's txtr article states the company was founded in **2008 as Wizpac Ltd**, headquartered in Berlin, with Christophe Maire and Andreas Steinhauser as managing directors, and an April 2010 report calling Bach "txtr-Mitbegründer" at the reader's launch **[A, de.wikipedia, "Txtr"]**. Insolvency filed January 2015 **[A, same]**. The 1999 date is discarded; it would precede his own degree.

**Hotheaven.** An AI task-planning app founded *after* txtr, per Musser's ordering **[A as to sequence]**. The dossier's "c. early 2000s" is therefore impossible and is discarded; the journey file places it after the 2015 insolvency and says the year is not attested.

**KI 2011.** Proceedings of the 34th Annual German Conference on AI, Berlin, 4–7 October 2011, edited by Joscha Bach and Stefan Edelkamp, LNCS 7006, Springer **[A, DBLP]**. This was **[R]** in the pool and is upgraded. Only the building within Berlin remains unknown.

**AGI 2012.** *Artificial General Intelligence, 5th International Conference*, Oxford, 8–11 December 2012, edited by Bach, Ben Goertzel, and Matthew Iklé, LNCS 7716 **[A, DBLP]**. Also upgraded from **[R]**.

**Berlin School of Mind and Brain.** Research fellow, 2012 **[R, en.wikipedia only]**.

**Micropsi Industries GmbH.** Berlin, 2014, with Ronnie Vuine, Ulrik Deichsel, Dominik Welland, and Priska Herger **[R, en.wikipedia only]**.

## 5. Cambridge, Massachusetts: the Epstein subsidy, 2013–2019

**The gifts.** Epstein made donations in **November 2013, July 2014, and September 2014**, totalling **$300,000**, to support Bach's research **[A, Kate Selig, "Former MIT scientist funded by Epstein made racist and sexist claims in private emails," Boston Globe, 21 November 2025, citing MIT's report]**.

**Citation correction.** The pool cited a Globe article headlined "Epstein emails with MIT researcher reveal racist, sexist claims." **No such article exists.** The piece is Selig's, headlined as above, at `bostonglobe.com/2025/11/21/metro/epstein-emails-mit-joscha-bach/`, retrieved via Wayback capture 20251121115151. Wrong headline and wrong byline propagate through at least four pool items.

**Who introduced whom. Contradiction flagged and not resolved.** Three incompatible accounts: the pool says **Ben Goertzel introduced Bach to Epstein** in 2013; the Globe, citing MIT's report, says **Epstein introduced Bach to Joi Ito** and separately **to Martin Nowak**; Bach's own post says **Goertzel, Stephen Kosslyn, and Roger Schank** recommended him to Epstein. Three different relations, not three phrasings of one. The journey file notes the first is unsupported by any fetched source and carries the other two side by side.

**Employment.** MIT Media Lab, **April 2014 to October 2016** **[A, Selig]**. Bach's own post gives the funding as **2013 to 2017**, a year past the payroll and a year past the last gift. Unreconciled. His post does not state employment dates and must not be cited for them.

**MIT's report.** The Goodwin Procter report is dated **21 January 2020**, not 10 January as the pool gave, per the link in the Selig article (`facultygovernance.mit.edu/.../20200121GoodwinProcterReport.pdf`). It finds the lab hired Bach "in large part because Epstein subsidized the cost" and identifies "collective and significant errors in judgment." **Bach declined to speak with the investigators [A, Selig].** The PDF itself was not fetched; the quotations reach this file through the Globe.

**Harvard.** Intermittent access to the Program for Evolutionary Dynamics under Martin Nowak, listed as research scientist, through 2019; Harvard's 2020 review found no visitor participation agreement and no Harvard payment to him **[A, en.wikipedia; introduction corroborated by Selig]**. The Harvard report itself was not fetched.

**The two emails.** July 2016, to Epstein: claims about slower cognitive development among Black American children; and about two weeks later, that women "tend to find abstract systems, conflicts and mechanisms intrinsically boring," offered as an explanation for gender disparities in STEM **[A, Selig, direct quotation]**. Place of writing unrecorded; the journey file pins both at the Media Lab and says so.

**The island. Not attested. Flagged and excluded from the journey file.** The dossier asserts a one-evening 2015 visit by Bach to Little Saint James, with a description of a library and a grand piano inside the temple building. Four problems: en.wikipedia's Bach article does not mention the island, referencing only a Caribbean visit **invitation** discussed in 2014; the Boston Globe article mentions no island visit; Bach's own Substack post does not either; and the interior description (bookcases, "small black grand piano") is sourced by en.wikipedia to a **piano technician's 2012 visit**, so the dossier puts another visitor's words in Bach's mouth.

A visit by a living person to a documented trafficking site is not a claim to place on a map on an unsourced assertion. The stop is omitted, and recorded here so the operator can restore it if a primary source is produced. `jeffrey_epstein.journey.json` already carries Little Saint James at 18.3013, -64.8236 as Epstein's own stop, which is where it belongs on present evidence.

**Coordinate correction, three stops.** The pool repeatedly placed Bach and CIMC in the "South Bay" at 37.386, -122.0838, which is Mountain View, some 60 km from San Francisco. Corrected to San Francisco throughout.

**Vienna.** TED AI Conference, September 2025, quoted saying "it would be surprising if superintelligence were not feasible" **[R, Der Standard interview; not confirmed against a recording]**.

## 7. November 2025

**The Globe.** 21 November 2025, Kate Selig **[A]**. Identifies Bach as executive director of CIMC; sources the Liquid AI advisory role to his LinkedIn; records that "Liquid AI did not respond to a request for comment."

**Correction to a widely repeated claim.** The pool asserted that the Globe reported Bach "had recently ended his advisory role with Liquid AI after the company learned of the emails." **The Globe does not report this**, and Bach's Substack post does not mention Liquid AI at all. The pool's separate assertion that "Liquid AI confirms its collaboration with him ended" is likewise in neither fetched source. Carried as **[R]**, stated as unconfirmed in the journey file.

**Bach's answer.** "On the Jeffrey Epstein Affair," joscha.substack.com, 28 November 2025 **[A, fetched directly]**. Confirmed verbatim: "I receive death threats on social media"; "Presentations, a seminar and public talks have been cancelled"; "I feel pain and despair, but I don't feel any anger"; "A liberal society requires freedom of thought, and not just freedom of speech"; "I am extremely uncomfortable discussing topics that touch on heritability of intelligence in public" (a fragment; the sentence continues about nuance lost when instrumentalized by racists). Funding given as 2013 to 2017. Three recommenders named, not two: **Goertzel, Kosslyn, and Schank**.

**Quotation discipline applied.** Three pool quotations were misrendered. "I don't feel any anger" is a subordinate clause; the full sentence, used here, is "I feel pain and despair, but I don't feel any anger." The happiness quotation carries **an unmarked elision** (the printed text runs "...not important,' he says. 'You realize it's not part of your value system. Happiness is for children.'") and is not used. "People like me and my wife are basically Elves. We are dreamers..." splices across narration; only the first fragment is used.

## 8. Afterlife, monuments, and gaps

Bach is **alive**. No tomb, no grave, no cause of death, no posthumous edition; the "afterlife" here is institutional. The nearest things to monuments are the media.ccc.de archive of the congress talks, free and unedited; the Oxford monograph; the DBLP-indexed papers; the Substack; the patent. No feature documentary, dramatized film, book-length biography, or collected edition was located. **No birthplace commemoration in Weimar was located** either, stated as a gap and not confirmed by a site visit.

**The "Wizard of Consciousness" epithet** originates in the 2018 profile and is promotional journalism, not a title, prize, or office **[A, Musser]**.

**The MicroPsi origin myth**, that Bach invented Psi from nothing, is false in its strong form: Dörner is his listed doctoral adviser **[A]** and Vuine his confirmed KI 2003 co-author **[A, DBLP]**. The AEP toolkit attribution remains **[R]**.

**The professorship myth**, that he held a chair at MIT and Harvard, is contradicted by both institutional reviews: research-fellow work at the Media Lab, irregular research-scientist status at Harvard PED **[R, via the Globe; neither primary PDF fetched here]**.
