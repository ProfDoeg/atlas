# Rabia al-Adawiyya (Rābi'a bint Ismā'īl al-'Adawiyya al-Qaysiyya): transits

Sky at 0h UT of each day-precise stop (0 of 38 stops); no time of day is known for events, so the Moon is good to ~13° and the angles are not computed.

*birth not day-precise (year): sky only, no transits to a natal chart.*
