# Ahmad al-Majriti (the name over the Rutbat al-Hakim and the Ghayat al-Hakim; in the sources a patronymic, ibn Ahmad, son of Ahmad): transits

Sky at 0h UT of each day-precise stop (0 of 42 stops); no time of day is known for events, so the Moon is good to ~13° and the angles are not computed.

*birth not day-precise (year): sky only, no transits to a natal chart.*
