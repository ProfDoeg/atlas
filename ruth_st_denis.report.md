# Ruth St. Denis (1879-1968): research report

*2026-08-23, for `ruth_st_denis.journey.json`. Slug: `ruth_st_denis`.*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named · **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, inference, or a claim resting only on her own retrospective narration · **gap** something a source should carry and does not.

The governing structural fact: she is her own principal source. *An Unfinished Life* (1939) was written by a woman who had spent thirty-five years perfecting a persona, and the standard chronology descends from it through Shawn's 1920 hagiography before Shelton's 1981 biography began working against the grain. A claim tracing back only to her is tagged **[R]** even when uncontested; uncontested and independently attested are not the same condition.

## 1. Birth and Pin Oaks Farm, 1879-1893

**Birth, 20 January, Newark, New Jersey [A, Wikipedia 'Ruth St. Denis']**, to Ruth Emma Hull Dennis, a physician, and Thomas Laban Dennis, a machinist and inventor. Wikipedia adds that the parents were not married.

**Birth year contested, left contested.** 1879 is the modern consensus (Wikipedia; the dossier reports NYPL and UCLA concurrence). Older reference works give **1877** and **1880**; Encyclopedia.com gives **"probably 1878"**. The stop's `date_confidence` carries all four.

**Correction to the pool.** Its chronology asserted she was born "at Pin Oaks farm" while giving Newark coordinates. Wikipedia says **born in Newark**, **raised** at Pin Oaks Farm; the file carries that.

**Pin Oaks childhood [R]**: the reform-minded household, the mother's physical-culture and dress-reform interests, the private melodramas, the Delsarte exercises. All of it reaches the file through the dossier's synthesis of Shelton and the memoir, neither read directly. The parcel was not pinpointed, so 40.5654 / -74.6099 is the Somerville locale.

**Gap:** no schooling record, no date for the family's move, nothing on the father beyond his trade.

## 3. The cigarette poster and Radha, 1904-1906

**c. 1904, Buffalo, the Egyptian Deities advertisement [R].** Touring context and advertising image are documented; the moment exists only in her own repeated retelling, with no contemporaneous record of the drugstore, the date, or the poster, and the afterlife lens calls "one poster created American modern dance" self-mythologising. The file stages the scene, the register being national mythology, and says in `date_confidence` that nothing contemporaneous verifies it.

**1905, takes the stage name, turns soloist [R]**: standard year, no document fixes it.

**1906, Radha, with The Incense and The Cobras.**

> **Contradiction flagged, not resolved.** The dossier, citing **Schlundt**, gives **22 March 1906 at the Hudson Theatre**. The Wikipedia article cited alongside it says only that Radha was "performed in 1906" and places her Hudson appearance in **1909** (the Salome dance). Radha also had earlier vaudeville showings that year, so "premiere" overstates the March matinee. Schlundt was not obtainable. The file keeps his date and venue and prints the disagreement.

> **Coordinate corrected.** The pool's 40.7484 / -73.9857 for the Hudson is Herald Square, a kilometre off. Wikipedia gives 139-141 West 44th Street at 40.75694 / -73.98472; the file uses **40.7569 / -73.9847**.

## 5. Denishawn, 1914-1926

- **1914, Shawn arrives in New York [A, Wikipedia 'Ted Shawn']**, month a **gap**; **13 April 1914, first joint concert [R]**, Shelton and Schlundt via the dossier, venue and city unconfirmed; **13 August 1914, marriage [A, Wikipedia]**, ceremony site a **gap**.
- **6 February 1915, Portland: the name Denishawn originates [A, Encyclopedia.com]**. At this date it is a company and ballroom-act label, not yet a school name.
- **1916, Martha Graham enters the school [A, Encyclopedia.com]**, "over six years" in the company.

**Summer 1915, 600 St. Paul Avenue, Los Angeles.**

> **Institutional name corrected.** The pool said they established "the first Denishawn School of Dancing and Related Arts." Wikipedia 'Denishawn school' is verbatim to the contrary: the school founded here was the **Ruth St. Denis School of Dancing and Its Related Arts**. `martha_graham.journey.json` already had this right.

> **Coordinate discrepancy flagged, deliberately unresolved.** The interlock lens and `martha_graham.journey.json` both carry the address at **34.0535 / -118.2665**; OSM Nominatim returns **34.0534 / -118.2608**, 500 m east. Canonical pins must be inherited byte-identical, so the file keeps the inherited value and prints the discrepancy. If the operator moves one file, both must move. Also softened: the pool put the house "in the Los Angeles hills," but the address sits in the flats west of downtown.

**1916, Egyptian Ballet, with Tillers of the Soil and Shawn's all-male Pyrrhic Dance; Denishawn at New York's Palace Theater [R on the venue]**. The pool's geography lens left the premiere city as "Los Angeles or New York." The file uses the Palace and says the city is unconfirmed.

**Second Los Angeles campus at West Lake Park [A, Wikipedia]**, with garden, tennis court and outdoor platform, dated only to "the 1910s." **Coordinate corrected** to **34.0587 / -118.2789**; the pool's 34.07 / -118.275 sat 1.3 km north.

**Dropped stops:** the **Parkinson Estate film, c. 1915-1917 [R]**, three minutes of school footage whose estate coordinates were not located; and **Louise Brooks with the touring company, 1922-24 [A]**, folded into the West Lake Park campa, the pool giving no location for it.

## 6. The Asian tour, 1925-1926: the weakest evidence in the file, by a wide margin

**[A, Encyclopedia.com]:** Denishawn toured **India, Ceylon, Burma, Malaya, Java, China, Japan and the Philippines**, and was the first American company to present Western concert dance in several of them (that last part unchecked).

**Everything below the country level is [R].** The city-by-city itinerary, the dates and the order of legs live in **Schlundt's chronology** and **Jane Sherman's *Soaring*** (Wesleyan, 1976). **Neither was obtainable.** Consequently:

- Every Asian city in the file (Bombay, Colombo, Rangoon, Singapore, Batavia, Shanghai, Tokyo, Manila) is **inferred as the principal port or city** of an attested tour country. None is confirmed.
- The dates 1925-10 through 1926-05 **order the segment only**. They are not attested months and not a reconstructed itinerary, and the sequence follows Encyclopedia.com's order of listing the countries. Each `date_confidence` says so.

> **Geographical error corrected.** The pool labelled a stop "Singapore, Malaya." In 1925-26 Singapore belonged to the **Straits Settlements**, a separate Crown colony, not to the Malay States. The coordinates 1.2903 / 103.8519 are right; the label is not, and the file says so in the stop name and the confidence field.

The Javanese court-dance encounter, the Kuan Yin and Kwannon photographs feeding the late personae, and the reading that the tour confirmed rather than created her Asia are the dossier's, not documented findings. The campas state where nothing was written down.

## 8. The long third act, 1938-1968

**1938, founds the dance program at Adelphi College [A, Wikipedia 'Adelphi University', verbatim]**. **Coordinate corrected** to **40.720 / -73.652**; the pool's 40.7268 / -73.6343 fell 1.7 km east in Westbury. Its geography lens had this right and its chronology lens did not.

**1939, *An Unfinished Life*, Harper and Brothers [A]**; Dance Horizons reprint 1969.

> **Four page citations corrected.** The pool put two quotations at **p. ~427**; both are at **ch. XIII, p. 277**. It put two more at **p. ~571**; both are at **ch. XIV, "Denishawn House," p. 330**. The volume runs roughly 390 printed pages, so neither figure could have been right. The "Dare to dance your own dance" lines also sit **inside a quoted address to young dancers**, not in flat narration, and the file's confidence field says so.

**1940, School of Natya with La Meri.**

> **City corrected: New York, not Los Angeles.** The pool's geography lens placed the school in Los Angeles with downtown coordinates. Wikipedia 'La Meri' puts it in **New York City in 1940**, La Meri having settled there because of the war, and records her absorbing it into the Ethnologic Dance Center in **1945**. The pool's chronology lens said New York; the two contradicted each other. **Gap:** no street address.

**1941 and 1949, Jacob's Pillow films [R]**, The Delirium of the Senses from Radha and Liebestraum. Both rest on the dossier's citation of the Pillow's archive; the reachable Wikipedia article corroborates neither. **Coordinate corrected** to **42.2644 / -73.1181**; the pool's 42.2934 / -73.0837 sat 4.3 km northeast in open country.

**23 April 1951, "Search for Spiritual Expression," University of Illinois [R on the source]**: the dossier cites a surviving campus recording, not played this pass.

**1950s, studio at 3433 Cahuenga Boulevard West [A, Wikipedia, verbatim]**. **Coordinate corrected** to **34.1321 / -118.3532**; the pool's 34.117 / -118.335 sat 2.4 km southeast, contradicting the source's own "near Universal City."

**Siddhas of the Upper Air, the fiftieth-anniversary reunion with Shawn.**

> **Date corrected by roughly twenty-three years, the largest single error in the pool.** The pool dated it **1941**. Wikipedia 'Ted Shawn' calls *Siddhas of the Upper Air* Shawn's final stage appearance in his own theatre at Jacob's Pillow, reuniting with St. Denis for their fiftieth anniversary. Partnership and marriage both date to **1914**, putting the fiftieth at **1964**; and Shawn, who died in 1972, cannot have made a final stage appearance in 1941. The pool's own note flagged this incoherence without acting on it. The file dates the stop **1964-01-01**. **Gap:** the month.

**21 July 1968, death by stroke, Hollywood, aged 89 [A, Wikipedia; corroborated in the dossier from the NYPL cause-of-death record]**.

**Burial at Forest Lawn, Hollywood Hills [R, and thin].** What exists is a **Wikipedia category tag** plus secondary cemetery-listing pages; the cemetery's own article does not list her among its notable interments. No plot, no marker, no primary record reached. **The interment date is a gap**; the file repeats the death date to order the segment and says so.

**Not given stops, posthumous, nobody travelled:** the **1987** National Museum of Dance Hall of Fame induction at Saratoga Springs, inaugural year [A]; and the **29 September 2006** Baltimore Museum of Art "Art of the Solo," at which her 1906 "The Peacock" was reconstructed from film, accounts and photographs under Mino Nicolas, with "The Incense" danced live by Cynthia Word [A]. That second is the reconstructors' stop, not hers.
