# Hilde Holger (1905-2001): research report

*Vienna, Bombay, London. Dancer, choreographer, teacher; founder of three schools on three
continents; one of the first in Europe to put a performer with Down syndrome on a professional stage
as a dancer.*

**Legend.** **[A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, estate-derived, or
carried by one derivative source only.

**The dominant-source problem.** Roughly two thirds of what is known about Holger passes through the
Hilde Holger Archive (hildeholger.com), run by her daughter Primavera Boman-Behram, publishing her
unpublished autobiographical "History Pages" and three performance registers: a primary source held
and edited by an interested party. Where the estate is the only witness the claim is [R] even when
almost certainly true.

---

## 1. Vienna, 1905-1919

- Born **Hilde Sofer, 18 October 1905, Vienna** **[A: en.wikipedia; Wien Geschichte Wiki]**. No
  birth address located; city pin used. Father **Alfred Sofer, poet, dead by 1908**, when she is
  about three **[A: de.wikipedia, "Alfred Sofer (Dichter, gestorben 1908)"; en.wikipedia; her own
  line on the estate site]**. *Upgraded from the pool's [R]: more than family tradition.* Mother
  **Elise Sofer**; a **stepfather, Heinrich Wohl**, appears later in the Holocaust record **[A:
  en.wikipedia]**. **Dance training from about age six [A]**.
- **The Poetzleinsdorf episode [R: hildeholger.com/vienna/]**: she describes dancing naked outdoors
  by moonlight for the sculptor **Joseph Heu** and a friend. *Correction: the pool had this as a 1926
  sitting as nude model for a sculpture by "Josef Heu"; the source has no sculpture, no sitting and
  no 1926, and spells the name Joseph.*

## 2. Vienna, 1919-1926

- **1919: enters Gertrud Bodenwieser's class**, k.k. Akademie fuer Musik und darstellende Kunst,
  aged 13 or 14 **[A: en.wikipedia; METROMOD; estate]**. That Bodenwieser advised Elise Sofer to
  withdraw her is **[R]**, in no academy record. **c.1921: tours with the Bodenwieser group** through
  Germany, Poland, Italy, Czechoslovakia **[R: the Guardian obituary, via the dossier]**.
- **1923: solo debut at the Vienna Secession**, her own choreography to Schubert's *Die Forelle* and
  Debussy's *Le Martyre de Saint Sebastien* **[A: en.wikipedia; estate Vienna register]**.
- **1927: a Paris notice.** Robert Mangin is said to have written in the *Journal des Theatres* that
  with Holger "dance is really a prayer" **[R: dossier only; the issue was not located]**, so the
  journey file carries it as reported speech, not as a quote.
- **Documented influences**: **Rudolf von Laban**, **Mary Wigman**, the Bauhaus and **Oskar
  Schlemmer**, and among painters **Paul Klee**. The lineage is the standard account of
  Ausdruckstanz **[A]**; personal acquaintance with Wigman rests on an estate network list **[R]**.
  No document puts Holger in a room with any of them, and the journey file names them only as
  sources of her method.

## 3. Vienna, 1926-1938: the Neue Schule fuer Bewegungskunst

- **1926: founds the school** in the Singerstrasse palace the estate calls **Palais Ratibor** and
  scholarship identifies as **Palais Neupauer-Breuner** **[A: en.wikipedia; estate; Kampe 2013]**.
  Her name for it is "New School of the Art of Movement"; the curriculum ran to dance and music
  history, art appreciation, costume and stage design, anatomy **[A: estate Vienna page]**.
- **Vienna venues, estate register [A]**: Urania 1928, 1929, 1935; Redoutensaal der Hofburg 1929;
  Johann Strauss-Denkmal, Stadtpark, 1930; **Hagenbund exhibition "Der Tanz", 1933**, where she
  showed *Colour and Line and Music* including *Orchid*, to Ravel. *Correction: the pool gave the
  Stadtpark performances a range of 1926-1938; the register lists it once, for 1930.*
- **Therapeutic massage training, Rothschild-Spital**, undated within the 1930s **[R]**; the trade
  later supported her in Bombay. **1934: listed in *Kunst in Oesterreich*** **[R: dossier]**.
- **A documented error in the estate's own biography**: it names "the great mathematician Max Plank"
  among her pupils. Planck was a physicist (1858-1947) with no connection to her school. A myth in
  the source record, **not carried into the journey file**.

## 4. 1938-1939: Anschluss and escape

- **March 1938**: as a Jew she may not work or perform; the school is closed **[A: en.wikipedia,
  dating the flight from 12 March 1938]**. Underground teaching afterwards is **[R]**, and no Reich
  file naming her an *entartete Kuenstlerin* was located.
- **July 1938: Elias Canetti inscribes *Die Blendung*** to her, "in herzlicher Freundschaft" **[R:
  an estate-held book nobody outside the family is shown to have examined]**. *Pool contradiction
  resolved downward: its chronology lens tagged this [R], its geography lens [A].*
- **June 1939: leaves Austria via Paris, reaching Bombay that summer [A: en.wikipedia; METROMOD]**,
  carrying costumes, photographs, scores and designs **[R: an unnamed German museum chronology]**.
  The friend who arranged it is **Charles Petrach**, already in India **[A: en.wikipedia,
  de.wikipedia and the estate, all with that spelling]**. *Correction: the pool gave "Karl Petrasch
  (later Charles Petras)"; neither form exists in any source.* Her own reason: "Having the choice of
  going to the United States or to India I chose the latter being a country of attraction to most
  Western artists" **[A: estate]**, which does not mention Britain refusing her entry, as the dossier
  asserts elsewhere. Unreconciled.
- **The family who stayed.** Her **mother**, **stepfather Heinrich Wohl** and other relatives were
  murdered in the Holocaust **[A: en.wikipedia]**. *Three corrections.* (1) The pool dated the
  murders to **1938**, the Anschluss year; Vienna's deportations ran **1941-42**, the window the
  journey file uses. (2) The pool named a **sister**; no fetched source does. (3) The **figure of
  fourteen**, from the Guardian obituary, has no victim-by-victim record behind it.

## 5. Bombay, 1939-1948

- **1939: first Indian engagement, Taj Mahal Palace Hotel**, with the Taj Cabaret and Teddy
  Weatherford's orchestra **[A: METROMOD; estate, "After giving her first performance at the Taj
  Mahal Hotel she received an invitation from the Nawab of Bhophal"]**. *Correction: the pool's
  chronology lens dated this 1941, contradicting its own geography lens.* The **refusal anecdote**
  (declining the dinner-floor parquet, holding out for a stage) rests on **Krishna Lupus, BLITZ, 27
  March 1948** **[R]**.
- **The princely invitation: the Nawab of Bhopal [A: estate names him outright]**. *Correction: the
  pool hedged between Bhopal and Akbar Hydari of Hyderabad, who appears in no fetchable source. Only
  the seventy-mile piano stays [R].* **1940: marries Dr Ardeshir Kavasji ("Adi") Boman-Behram**, a
  Parsi physician **[A: en.wikipedia]**.
- **October 1941: opens the School of Art for Modern Movement**, second floor, **Queen's Mansion,
  Prescott Road, Fort**, advertised as the foremost professional European school for creative dance
  and contemporary ballet in India, admission restricted to "Ladies and Children" so the premises
  would not be read as a brothel **[A: METROMOD throughout]**. Her *Dance* article: "The word
  'professional' had, till the last years, the same meaning as 'prostitution'."
- **Bombay venues, estate register [A]**: Parsi Girls' Orphanage 1939; Capitol Cinema 1939-1942;
  **Excelsior, 28 February and 4 March 1943**, *Three Enchanting Ballets*, sets and costumes by
  **Magda Nachman** and **Shiavax Chavda**; Alexandra Girls' English Institution, 20 April 1945; St
  Xavier's College Hall, 7-8 and 18 February 1948. *Correction: the pool hedged the 1943 venue as
  "Capitol/Excelsior"; METROMOD's Capitol is the outlier against the dated register.*
- **Gandhi**: she attended his daily prayer meetings and describes meeting him **[A: her own Bombay
  pages]**; no date given. **1946: daughter Primavera born in Bombay [A]**. **30 January 1948**: she
  is directing a dress rehearsal, in a theatre no source names, when the news of the assassination
  arrives **[A]**.
- **1948: departure for England** amid Partition violence and the assassination **[A: en.wikipedia;
  METROMOD]**. **Ballard Pier is an inference**; no manifest or ship found **[R]**.

## 6. London, 1948-1968

- **1948: first English home in Hampstead**, and the **Hilde Holger School of Contemporary Dance**
  founded there, moving to Camden around 1951 **[A: Islington Tribune; en.wikipedia and estate for
  the name]**. *Two corrections: the pool cited hildeholger.com/biography/ for the Hampstead
  residence, and that page never mentions Hampstead; and it called the school "School of Modern
  Dance", where both sources give **Contemporary**.* **Parliament Hill Fields** and the
  **suspicious-neighbours** anecdote are **[R]**.
- **1949: son Darius born in London** with Down syndrome and heart and lung problems **[A:
  en.wikipedia]**. A doctor: "You can put him in an institution, you must not keep him at home." Her
  answer: "I am his mother and brought him in this world and he will stay with me as long as I
  live." **[A: estate London page]**
- **1951: *Under the Sea*, to Saint-Saens, at St Martin-in-the-Fields [A: en.wikipedia; estate]**;
  an estate choreography list dates the same work to Sadler's Wells in **1955**. **c.1951-2001: Oval
  Road, Camden Town**, the basement studio, roughly fifty years **[A: estate; Islington Tribune]**.
- **London venues, estate register [A]**: Rudolf Steiner Theatre, 1 May 1954 and 2-3 February 1963;
  Salle Erard, 2 April 1955; Waterlow Park, 9-11 June 1958 and 20-22 June 1960, under London County
  Council auspices; St Philip's, Battersea, 13-14 July 1963, 27-28 February 1965, 13-14 July 1968,
  where she staged *The Creation of Adam and Eve* to Messiaen. *Correction: the pool made 1952 a
  second Salle Erard performance; that item is a brochure feature.*
- **1963: the marriage is dissolved [R: The Independent; other derivative sources give 1975]**.

## 7. 1968-1984: integrated dance

- **16 March 1968: *Towards the Light*, to Grieg, with Darius performing [A: estate London page]**.
  Her gloss: "The idea that Towards the Light was to express is that handicapped people are to be
  accepted by those more fortunate in the community, sharing their lives and not to live a lonely
  life." **Two contradictions, carried as such.** (1) **Venue**: the estate's
  London page says **Sadler's Wells**; the same estate's register puts the date at **Brent Town
  Hall** (Handshake Gateway Club) and lists Sadler's Wells only once, for a 1991 workshop. Both
  buildings get a stop and each says the other exists. (2) **Year**: 1968 (en.wikipedia, estate)
  against 1969 (The Independent).
- **13-14 July 1972: *Man Against Flood*, Commonwealth Institute**, from material associated with
  Rewi Alley, music credited to Yin Chengzong, with *Bamboo* and a Bauhaus-themed dance **[A:
  en.wikipedia; estate register]**. The **Daily Telegraph** found the dances limited to statements of
  mood **[R]**.
- **Hampstead Theatre: 11-12 April 1975; 23 April 1983; 6-7 April 1984 (*Die Stadt*); 25-26 November
  1988 [A: estate register, verbatim]**. *Correction: the bill Mobiles / Paul Klee: Spring Awakening
  / Rockpaintings / Toulouse Lautrec is attached to the 1975 date by no source fetched. Dates [A],
  bill [R], split so in the journey file.*
- **1980: Wolfgang Stange, her pupil, founds Amici Dance Theatre Company [A: de.wikipedia;
  amicidance.org has him as founder without a year]**. *Upgraded from [R].* Stange's act, not hers,
  so it appears only inside the campa for the 1996 production she attended.
- **Summer 1983: first return to Bombay since 1948**, invited by **Sachin Shankar**; she stages
  *Apsaras* **[A: en.wikipedia; METROMOD]**. No venue located.

## 8. 1990-2001

- **1990: the only book-length biography**, Hirschbach and Takvorian, *Die Kraft des Tanzes: Hilde
  Holger, Wien, Bombay, London*, Bremen: Zeichen + Spuren **[A: the estate bibliography lists it
  verbatim]**. *Upgraded from [R].* **No English translation exists**, thirty-five years on.
- **1992-93: four Vienna solos reconstructed** for **Liz Aggiss** with **Billy Cowie**: *Die
  Forelle*, *Le Martyre de Saint Sebastien*, *Mechanisches Ballett*, *Golem*, at the **Manchester
  Festival of Expressionism (1992)** and the **ICA (June 1993)** **[A: en.wikipedia; R for the
  Manchester venue]**. Sophie Constanti in the Guardian: her choreography "reaches the British stage
  at last and triumphs" **[A: quoted in en.wikipedia]**.
- **20-23 March 1996: *Hilde***, Amici's ninetieth-birthday production, **Riverside Studios**,
  Holger present in a wheelchair **[A: en.wikipedia, which calls it "Riverside Theatre"; the estate's
  "Riverside Studios, London W6" settles it]**. *Upgraded from [R].*
- **1998: *Hilde* at the Odeon, Vienna [A: en.wikipedia]**. *Upgraded from [R].* Commemorative, not
  a resumption; no source says whether she travelled to it. The claim that it prompted the Vienna
  State Opera Ballet's ballet master to mount a production with performers with learning disabilities
  is **[R]** and is not carried.
- **2000: *Dances of the Unconscious*, Lilian Baylis Theatre [R: dossier only]**. **June 2001: still
  teaching masterclasses**, planning more after entering an unidentified care home **[R]**.
- **Death, London (Camden), aged 95. The date is contested and is not resolved here.** en.wikipedia
  gives **22 September 2001**; **Wien Geschichte Wiki** (City of Vienna, official) and
  **de.wikipedia** give **24 September 2001**. Two German-language authorities against one English
  one: the journey file uses the 24th and records the 22nd. Place and age are agreed by all three.
  **No source gives a cause of death.**
- **Cremated at Golders Green Crematorium [A, thinly: the en.wikipedia infobox, misspelled
  "Goldner's Green Crematorium"; no register located]**. *Pool contradiction resolved upward: its
  geography lens tagged this [R], its afterlife lens [A].* **There is no grave, headstone or
  monument**: searched for, not found, and stated as an absence in the journey file.

## Apparatus relations for the operator

Hilde Holger is **not** listed in `EXCEPTIONS.md`, so nothing is staged as an apparatus stop.
Relations not rooted in her own life are recorded here, not deleted, with the direction named.

**Forward, therefore not licensable without the author's say-so.** Five posthumous events sit in the
pool. None is a stop: Holger was not there and could not have been, and all are other people's
engagements with her archive.

1. **2010, "MoveABOUT: Transformation through Movement"**, Hampstead Town Hall Centre, organised by
   six former students (Primavera Boman, Carl Campbell, Thomas Kampe, Royston Maldoom, Wolfgang
   Stange, Jacqueline Waltz) **[A: en.wikipedia]**.
2. **2016-17, "Body Luggage: Migration of Gestures"**, Kunsthaus Graz, 24 September to 8 January,
   curated by Zasha Colah **[A: estate past-events page, spelling it "Zasha Colash"]**.
3. **2019-20, "Alles tanzt: Kosmos Wiener Tanzmoderne"**, Theatermuseum Vienna; Primavera
   Boman-Behram travelled to the opening with the Jewish Welcome Service **[A: jewish-welcome.at]**.
4. **20 May 2025, "The Basement Dances of Hilde Holger"**, Salzburg; concept Claudia Kappenberg and
   Thomas Kampe, moderated by Miriam Althammer, from 1990s video and students' recollections **[A:
   plus.ac.at]**.
5. **The trunk.** Primavera Boman-Behram has said she learned the scale of her mother's fame only
   after finding photographs and documents in a trunk after the death **[A: Islington Tribune]**.

Corrected coordinates: Hampstead Town Hall approx. 51.5487, -0.1620; Kunsthaus Graz 47.0713,
15.4341; Unipark Nonntal 47.7947, 13.0542.

Material for a future queue subject, not for stops on this file.

**Backward, equally not staged.** The dossier records Holger's own claim that an **uncle knew
Sigmund Freud** through a Viennese hospital, and flags it as chronologically impossible. Freud has a
journey file, but the relation is a family anecdote the dossier itself disbelieves, so he is named in
no campa; the shared 1938 Vienna-to-Hampstead exile route is shared geography only. Recorded so the
operator can decide otherwise.

**Dropped for want of any fetchable source.** The **1979** screening of Marylin Halford's film
*Inspirations* at the Oesterreichisches Theatermuseum within "Tanz 20. Jahrhundert in Wien", called
in the pool her first institutional return to Vienna. Neither exhibition, film nor date appears
anywhere fetchable, the estate's past-events page begins at 2008, and it is unclear whether Holger
attended at all.

## Honest gaps

- No birth address (city pin only); no cause of death; no care-home location; no grave and no
  crematorium register; no ship or manifest for the 1948 departure.
- No venue named for the interrupted dress rehearsal of January 1948, for *Apsaras* in 1983, or for
  the Manchester festival in 1992; no date for the Rothschild-Spital training or the prayer meeting;
  no 1927 *Journal des Theatres* issue behind "dance is really a prayer".
- No Reich document naming her a banned or degenerate artist; the ban is inferred from her Jewishness
  and the closure of the school, as every source infers it.
- No independent witness to the Canetti dedication, the Bhopal piano, the neighbours' suspicion of
  her accent, or the Krishnamurti performance.
