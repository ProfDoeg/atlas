# Ruhollah Khomeini (1900/1902-1989): research report

*Atlas of Journeys working file. Slug `ayatollah_khomeini`. Compiled 2026-08-11 from the verified research pool, carrying the adversarial corrections through rather than smoothing them away.*

**Legend.** **[A]** = attested. **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition, or inference, not carried by a dated record. Where lenses disagree, both readings are printed and neither is adjudicated. Corrected coordinates are given corrected, with the superseded value shown.

---

## 1. Khomein, 1900/1902 to 1920

**Birth** in Khomein, Markazi province, in the house of his father Sayyid Mostafa Musavi, the town's cleric. **[A: Wikipedia; Moin, *Khomeini: Life of the Ayatollah*]**

**Contradiction 1, unresolved: the birth date.** Birth certificate **17 May 1900**; his brother and Iranian official biography **24 September 1902**. The chronology lens leads with the certificate and flags the conflict; the geography lens dates the birth-house entry 1902. The file uses 1900-05-17 with the conflict in `date_confidence`. Downstream: the two dates yield **89** and **86** for the age at death, both in the pool (§9).

**Contradiction 2, unresolved: when the father was killed.** Sayyid Mostafa was murdered near Khomein at a local landowner's order. Chronology lens (Wikipedia): the boy was **about two**, so 1903. Geography lens (Moin): **five months after the birth**. Both stand; the stop is dated 1903-01-01 and prints both. **[A** for the murder, **R** for date and site, not pinpointed beyond the vicinity of the town **]**

**The household.** Raised by his mother (**Agha Khanum** in one lens, **Hajar** in the other) and his aunt (**Sahebeth** / **Sahebeh**). The name discrepancy is unreconciled. **[A]**

**Early schooling.** Quran and elementary Persian from about six, religious schooling from about seven, then family tutors through the 1910s. **[A** for the schooling, **R** for the 1908-1920 decade **]** No stop; folded into the birth and father stops.

**Arak.** Seminary study from about 1920 under **Sheikh Abdolkarim Haeri Yazdi**, in the school Haeri had newly founded there. Roughly two years. **[A: Wikipedia; Iranica]**

## 2. Qom, 1921/1922 to 1963

**Contradiction 3, unresolved: the year of the move to Qom.** Chronology lens **1921**, geography lens **1922**, one event (Haeri accepting the Qom clergy's invitation). File uses 1922, flags 1921.

**Dar al-Shafa.** A cell beside the shrine of Fatima Masumeh; Qom is his base four decades. **[A: Moin]**

**Downgrade applied.** The geography lens tagged a Fatima Masumeh Shrine entry **[A]** for praying and teaching "across his Qom decades, 1920s-1960s". Verification corrected it to **[R]**: coordinates right, but a generalization over a forty-year range is not a dated event. No stop of its own; the shrine appears inside the Dar al-Shafa campa, where a generalization can be stated as one.

**1928.** *Sharh Du'a al-Sahar*, on the Ramadan dawn supplication, in the vocabulary of the mystics. **[A]**

**1929, marriage** to **Khadijeh Saqafi**, daughter of a Tehran cleric; seven children, among them Mostafa, Zahra, Farideh, Ahmad. **Gap:** no available biography names the house or venue, Tehran being recorded solely as the bride's family city. **[A** for the marriage, **R** for Tehran as a map point **]**

**The hajj.** Tagged **[R]**, "circa 1930s-40s", with the statement that no source fixes a year: it rests on the expectation attaching to a scholar of his standing. Carried as an [R] stop at the canonical Kaaba pin, gap stated in `date_confidence`.

**Contradiction 4, unresolved: the year of *Kashf al-Asrar*.** Chronology lens **1942**, geography lens **1943**, same book and description (a polemic against secularizing critics of the clergy). File uses 1943, flags 1942.

**The rank of marja.** The chronology lens ties the rise to marja to the death of Grand Ayatollah **Borujerdi** and places both in **1963**. No other lens carries that year and the pool offers no check on it. Recorded as the pool has it, **[A]** on the succession, **flagged unverified on the year**, folded into the 22 January declaration stop.

## 3. The rising of Khordad, 1963-1964

**22 January 1963.** Declaration against the White Revolution: land redistribution, women's suffrage. **[A]**

**Correction applied: the Feyziyeh raid.** The geography lens dated the storming to **January 1963**. Verification: **22 March 1963**, the death anniversary of Ja'far al-Sadiq, with Nowruz running; the January event conflated with it was the 26 January referendum. Coordinates corrected to **34.6428, 50.8793** from 34.6408, 50.8791, used for all Feyziyeh stops. **[A, corrected]**

**3 June 1963.** The Ashura sermon at the Feyziyeh, setting the Shah beside **Yazid**. **[A]**

**5 June 1963.** Arrest at dawn in Qom, transfer to Tehran; three days of riots in Qom, Tehran, Mashhad, Shiraz, hundreds dead. **[A]**

**Correction applied: the length of the detention.** The chronology lens said he was released "that August". Verification: **in August 1963 he was moved from prison to house arrest in Tehran and was not freed until 7 April 1964**, after roughly ten months ("15 Khordad uprising"). The stop is dated 1963-08-01 with the release inside the campa. **[A, corrected]**

**Coordinate correction: Qasr Prison.** Submitted 35.7345, 51.4488; actual **35.7236, 51.4483**, about 1.2 km south. The geography lens's separate [R] stop for military-base house arrest (approximate, 35.70, 51.43) is folded into the Qasr campa rather than pinning an approximation.

**26 October 1964.** The sermon against the capitulation law. **[A]**

## 4. Exile: Turkey and Iraq, 1964-1978

**Correction applied: the deportation itinerary.** The chronology lens had him deported 4 November 1964 straight to Bursa. Verification: **flown first to Ankara, moved to Bursa about a week later, c. 12 November 1964**, to the home of Colonel **Ali Cetiner**. Stop dated 1964-11-12, Ankara leg in the campa. **[A, corrected]**

**Pin note.** The Bursa coordinates (**40.1826, 29.0665**) are byte-identical to the Bursa pin in `abdelkader.journey.json`. Both men were settled there as deported or defeated exiles, 111 years apart. Geographic coincidence, not documentary, and named as such in the campa.

**Najaf, October 1965 to 1978.** Thirteen years near the shrine of Imam Ali. **[A]** Coordinates unified on the geography lens **32.0011, 44.316**; the afterlife value 32.0286, 44.3489 was corrected as about 3.7 km northeast of the shrine district.

**Karbala.** Tagged **[R]**: no available biography dates a specific visit, and it rests on the ordinary Najaf-Karbala circuit. Carried as an [R] stop with that gap stated. The 1920s Najaf study visits are also [R] and get no stop: the file does not need two pins for one shrine district.

**The velayat-e faqih lectures.** Chronology and geography lenses **1970**; afterlife lens **"around 1969-1970"**, published as *Hokumat-e Eslami*, translated by **Algar** (Mizan Press, 1981). Printed, not resolved; file uses 1970. **[A]**

**1978.** Saddam Hussein, then vice president and seeking rapprochement with the Shah, has him expelled; he passes through Baghdad, is refused by Kuwait at the frontier, turns toward France. **[A: Moin]** The Kuwait border coordinates (30.0994, 47.6836) are the pool's, uncorrected.

## 5. France, October 1978 to January 1979

**Contradiction 5, unresolved: the date of arrival.** Chronology lens **6 October 1978** for the flight; the verifier's Neauphle note says the house was **occupied from 8 October 1978**. Both are in the file (Orly 1978-10-06, Neauphle 1978-10-08). Nothing in the pool reconciles the gap.

Both men are in the Paris region in the same weeks. **No meeting is attested in either direction**; the campa says so.

## 6. The return and the seizure of the state, 1979

**16 January.** The Shah leaves Iran. **[A]**

**1 February.** Return to Mehrabad after fifteen years; crowd estimated at several million, no count possible. **[A]** The "Hichi" reply to Peter Jennings is dated to this day **[A: Wikipedia, n. 90]**.

**Contradiction 7, coordinates: Behesht-e Zahra.** Geography lens **35.5686, 51.4381** (arrival speech) and **35.5722, 51.4383** (funeral); afterlife lens **35.5581, 51.3672**; verifier **35.536, 51.370**, finding the geography **longitude to be the error**, 7 to 8 km off. The file uses **35.536, 51.370** for the cemetery and **35.5492, 51.3665** for the mausoleum (submitted 35.585, 51.455, about 9 km off). The 1.5 km between the two pins sits inside a cemetery of that size and is not a contradiction.

**Correction applied, residual conflict standing: the Bazargan appointment.** The chronology lens dated it **11 February 1979**; verification corrected to **4 February 1979** ("Mehdi Bazargan"), 11 February being the day the monarchy collapsed. **The quotes lens still dates "since I have appointed him, he must be obeyed" to 11 February** (n. 98). The stop is dated 1979-02-04 and flags the quote's attribution. Not resolved.

**11 February.** The army declares neutrality; the Pahlavi state ends. **[A]**

**Interlock, Ahmadabad.** `mohammad_mosaddegh.journey.json` states that in 1979 the villagers renamed the high school built at Mosaddegh's grave-house **Emam Khomeini High School**, and that Khomeini denounced Mosaddegh even as the revolution he led ended the monarchy the 1953 coup preserved. Canonical pin **35.99361, 50.474722** inherited byte-identical. That file dates it 1979-01-01; the journey file uses 1979-04-01 and flags the January.

**31 March.** Referendum, announced at **98 percent**. **[A]**

**19 August.** Fatwa declaring jihad against Kurdish rebel groups. **[A]** **Gap:** place given only as "Kurdistan, Iran", no coordinates. The file uses 35.31, 46.99, a representative point in the Iranian Kurdish provinces, and says so. Weakest coordinate in the file.

**22 October and 4 November.** The United States admits the Shah to New York; students seize the embassy on the fifteenth anniversary of the 1964 deportation and hold 52 hostages 444 days, with Khomeini's approval. **[A]** New York is folded into the embassy campa: no pin, since Khomeini was not there.

**3 December.** Installed as Supreme Leader; the December referendum enshrines him as leader for life. **[A]** Two chronology entries for one act, given one stop.

**Quds Day and the Temple Mount.** The interlock lens offers the canonical Temple Mount pin (**31.778, 35.2354**) against the 1979 institution of Quds Day and states: **not attested in any atlas file, offered as a reconstructable pin inheritance.** Carried as **[R]**, pin byte-identical, non-attestation in `date_confidence`.

**Jamaran**, seat of government 1979-1989. **Coordinate correction:** geography lens 35.8103, 51.4708 (2 km southeast) and afterlife lens 35.8171, 51.4405; the verifier unified on **35.817, 51.450**, used for every Jamaran stop. **[A: Iranica]**

**February 1980.** Banisadr elected first president **[A]**. Folded into the Supreme Leader campa.

**September 1980.** Iraq invades; Khomeini refuses early truce offers **[A]**. **Gap:** place given as "Iran-Iraq border", no coordinates. The file uses 30.44, 48.17, a representative point on the southern front.

**July and August 1988.** Acceptance of the UN ceasefire, described as drinking the cup of poison; the war ends in August **[A]**. Month precision only; days default to 01 and are marked.

**1987, Mecca.** The interlock lens offers the canonical Kaaba pin against the 1987 hajj disaster, in which Saudi security clashed with Iranian pilgrims demonstrating under the Republic's banners and over 400 died, and states: **not attested in any atlas file.** Carried as **[R]**, year only.

**14 February 1989.** The fatwa on *The Satanic Verses*, Radio Tehran. **[A: n. 35]** Full text verbatim in the stop's `quote` field.

## 8. Quotes: what survived verification, and what did not

Thirteen quote fields are used. Four items required action, one was refused.

- **Refused: "Neither East, nor West, Islamic Republic."** Not a Khomeini quotation. It is the mass slogan of the 1979 revolution and the Republic's foreign-policy doctrine, with **no dated primary utterance by Khomeini**. It appears in the file only in the 11 February 1979 campa, unquoted, attributed to the crowds and the doctrine. Never in a `quote` field.
- **Corrected by truncation: the awliya line.** The submission dropped three final words with no ellipsis. Restored: **"and His Generosity"**. Source corrected to *Pithy Aphorisms*, ed. Limba, p. 3.
- **Corrected wording: the Letter to the Clergy.** Submitted "our conduct in the war"; the source reads **"our performance during the war"**. The gloss "[with Iraq]" is not in the original and is dropped. Moin, p. 285.
- **Corrected wording, tag raised: the export-of-revolution line.** Submitted "There is no god but God"; attested wording **"There is no god but Allah"**, dated **11 February 1979** (per Hiro), not 1980 as the submitter guessed.
- **Source corrections, wording intact:** the two Theology and Mysticism aphorisms (light/darkness, p. 3; God's Threshold, p. 4) were submitted as having no citation beyond a section heading. Inaccurate: both carry the *Pithy Aphorisms* citation, now given with pages.
- **Left at [R]:** "Anyone who will say that religion is separate from politics is a fool." Not traced past Wikiquote; the hedge is recorded in the stop's `sources`.
- **Single secondary reproduction, so noted:** "I shall kick their teeth in. I appoint the government." and "since I have appointed him, he must be obeyed." Both reach the pool only through Wikipedia footnotes.

## 9. Death and afterlife

**3 June 1989.** Death in a Tehran hospital after surgery for internal bleeding, following heart disease and cancer. **[A]** Pinned at the geography lens hospital coordinates 35.805, 51.465.

**Contradiction 8, unresolved: the age at death.** Chronology lens **approximately 89**; afterlife lens **86**, corrected to **89** on the ground that 86 follows only from the disputed 1902 birth date, which Britannica does not adopt. Both numbers are printed in the campa: both are what the record yields from the two birth dates, and §1 leaves the birth date open.

**6 June 1989, the funeral.** Crowd estimates diverge sharply and are printed as diverging: roughly **two million** in some sources, **ten million** in others, a disagreement the pool notes. Coffin overturned, shroud torn, burial aborted, body removed by helicopter, interment completed in a second controlled ceremony **[A: wire reporting; Moin]**.

**19 July 1989.** Mausoleum groundbreaking; four minarets of 91 m each, both confirmed. The master plan (seminary, university, convention centre, hotel, commerce) is tagged **[R]**, with cost figures inconsistently reported and unconfirmed. The campa says the figures do not agree.

**Correction applied: the anniversary date.** The afterlife lens glossed 14 Khordad as "mid-June". Verification: **14 Khordad is 4 June**, the day after the death, counted so because he died after sunset. **[A, corrected]**

**Succession.** Ali Khamenei named Leader **[A]**; stop dated 1989-06-04. That he was not a marja is carried in the campa. **The 1989 constitutional amendment resolving this is not in the pool** and is asserted nowhere in the file.

**Sahifeh-ye Imam** (commonly cited at 22 volumes) and the posthumous **Divan-e Imam** (1989, mystical verse withheld in his lifetime by his own choice, tagged **[R]**). **2016:** Hassan Khomeini disqualified by the Guardian Council **[A: BBC/Reuters]**.

**Reformist reappropriation, 2009-2020s.** Tagged **[R]** by the afterlife lens, which states the underlying quotes are attested in *Sahifeh-ye Imam* but their political use is a later contested reading. Not carried into the journey file: no place to pin it, and no register in which to state it without asserting the reading.
