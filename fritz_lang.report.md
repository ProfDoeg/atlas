# Fritz Lang: research report

*2026-08-04. Friedrich Christian Anton Lang, 1890-1976. Vienna, Berlin, Paris, Hollywood, Berlin again.*

**Method.** The largest single source on Lang's life is Lang, unreliable in one consistent direction: what happened to him is retold as something he chose, and what is undated is retold as a single dramatic night. McGilligan's *Fritz Lang: The Nature of the Beast* (1997) took the self-mythology apart; German Wikipedia carries the documentary corrections (baptismal register, enrolment protocol, muster record, Academy files) the English article lacks. Where the two disagree, the one citing a document is followed and the disagreement is printed.

**Legend.** **[A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, or Lang's own single-witness testimony. **CORRECTED** marks a fix to the earlier draft of this dossier and states what the earlier pin or date was.

---

## 1. Vienna and the invented youth (1890-1913)

- **Born 5 December 1890, Vienna [A]** (both Wikipedias; McGilligan; Eisner). Second son of the architect Anton Lang and of Pauline Schlesinger, born Jewish, received into the Catholic church before his birth [A]. **She ran the building firm A. Lang und Cie from 1895 [A, German Wikipedia];** Lang described her as a housewife.
- **Family address Schenkenstrasse 6, Innere Stadt [A]**, from the baptismal register via German Wikipedia; approx. 48.2114, 16.3634. Corroborated by the **baptism at the Schottenkirche on the Freyung, 28 December 1890 [A]**, a few hundred metres away. Lang claimed an address near the Stephansdom; that is his account, not the register's. **CORRECTED:** the geography lens placed the family in Mariahilf, 48.1975, 16.3542, about 2 km southwest, with no source support.
- **Technische Hochschule Wien: matriculated winter semester 1909/10, withdrew after one semester [A, enrolment protocol via German Wikipedia].** **CORRECTED:** chronology lens gave 1908, geography lens a 1908-1910 course in civil engineering and architecture; neither is supported. The Karlsplatz coordinates were right and are kept at 48.1985, 16.3699.
- **Rejected by the Academy of Fine Arts 1910/11, admitted 1918 [A, German Wikipedia].** The rejection appears in no account he gave of himself.
- **The wandering years, c. 1910-1913 [R].** Lang's interviews describe travel through Europe, Africa, Asia and the Pacific, with a Schwabing period living off caricatures and postcards. **CORRECTED:** the chronology lens tagged the 1910 departure [A]; it is [R]. McGilligan finds the itinerary largely unsupported, and the geography lens flagged the same material as contested.
- **Paris, painting, 1913-1914 [R as to texture; the sojourn itself broadly accepted, Eisner 1976].** Montmartre pin 48.8867, 2.3431 is a district anchor.

## 2. The war (1914-1918)

- **Arrested in Paris as an enemy alien at the outbreak of war, escaped, returned to Vienna [A for the arrest, English Wikipedia; R for the escape, which rests on Lang].** Day unestablished; defaulted to 1914-08-01.
- **Landsturm muster November 1914, conscription January 1915 as an Einjaehrig-Freiwilliger [A, muster record via German Wikipedia].** **CORRECTED:** the chronology lens said he volunteered, English Wikipedia says drafted, the geography lens says conscripted. The service category contains the word volunteer and is not an act of volunteering; the document is followed.
- **Russian front in Galicia and the Romanian front; wounded several times; lost the sight of his right eye [A, McGilligan; Gunning citing the Austrian military record].** The documented Russian-front wound is dated **17 June 1916 [A]**. **CORRECTED:** the geography range of 1914-1915 for Galicia begins before the muster and becomes 1915-1916. **Discharged 1918, rank of lieutenant [A].**
- **Convalescence in Vienna, scenarios written and sold to Decla, brief Viennese theatre work [A for the sales; R for the place].** No source names the hospital; 48.2200, 16.3467 marks the Allgemeines Krankenhaus district as an approximation.

## 3. Decla and Berlin (1918-1924)

- **Hired by Erich Pommer at Decla, as writer and story editor, then director [A, McGilligan].** Office address unconfirmed; 52.5096, 13.3759 marks the Potsdamer Platz film district.
- **1919: Halbblut, Der Herr der Liebe, Die Spinnen part 1, Harakiri [A]. Married Elisabeth (Lisa) Rosenthal at Charlottenburg, 13 February 1919 [A].** The Halensee working base, 52.4964, 13.2953, is [R], from Gunning.
- **1920: Die Spinnen part 2 and Das wandernde Bild; meets Thea von Harbou through Joe May [A]. On 25 September Elisabeth Rosenthal dies of a gunshot wound; Lang and von Harbou are charged with failure to render aid, charge dropped [A].** Nothing reachable establishes what happened in the room, and this dossier does not adjudicate it. The flat's address is unestablished; the pin is the marriage-register district.
- **1921: Vier um die Frau and Der muede Tod at Neubabelsberg [A, Eisner, Gunning].** The influence on Fairbanks's *The Thief of Bagdad* is carried "by report", so [R].
- **Married Thea von Harbou 26 August 1922 [A date per McGilligan; the chronology lens gives only the year].** Ceremony location unspecified; corpus Berlin pin 52.5200, 13.4050. **1922: Dr. Mabuse der Spieler, four hours in two parts [A]. 1922-1924: Die Nibelungen, five hours in two parts, built entirely from constructed sets [A, Eisner].**

## 4. Metropolis and the coming of sound (1924-1932)

- **1924, New York [R].** Lang's statements about the skyline giving him Metropolis are attested as statements (Minden and Bachmann 2002, p. 4; Grant 2003, p. 69, both via the Wikipedia Metropolis article). The crossing itself is not independently documented in anything reached. **CORRECTED (quotation integrity):** the pool rendered one quote with commas and disclosed the change in a note. The printed text uses em dashes: "I looked into the streets—the glaring lights and the tall buildings—and there I conceived Metropolis". Commas turn an appositive interruption into a list, and house style does not reach inside quotation marks; the dashes are restored in the journey file.
- **Metropolis shot at Babelsberg over roughly a year, 1925-1926: thousands of extras, the Schuefftan process, enormous cost to Ufa [A, Eisner, Gunning]. Premiere at the Ufa-Palast am Zoo, 10 January 1927; mixed reception, a flop whose costs feed Ufa's crisis [A].**
- **1928: Spione, and an affair with Gerda Maurus [A]. 1928-1929: Frau im Mond, Hermann Oberth consulting, the launch countdown popularised as drama [A, Gunning].**
- **1930-1931: M, shot partly in the converted Zeppelin hangar at Staaken, Peter Lorre's starring debut [A].** The story that Lang threw Lorre down a staircase for the bruises is **[R]**: repeated without primary citation, flagged "allegedly" even by the source that carries it. **1932: filming begins on Das Testament des Dr. Mabuse [A].**

## 5. 1933: the ban, the ministry, the train

The most heavily mythologised passage of the life, and the one where the sources openly fight.

- **30 March 1933: the regime bans Das Testament des Dr. Mabuse; Goebbels cancels the screening [A].**
- **The stated rationale**, that the film showed "an extremely dedicated group of people are perfectly capable of overthrowing any state with violence," comes through Kalat, *The Strange Case of Dr. Mabuse* (2005), via Wikipedia. **CORRECTED:** the pool clipped the sentence (the original runs "showed that an extremely dedicated group...") and presented it as a Goebbels quotation. It is the reported ban rationale, not a verified utterance from a primary document.
- **Lang's statement of intent [A as a statement]:** "The film was made as an allegory to show Hitler's processes of terrorism. Slogans and doctrines of the Third Reich have been put into the mouths of criminals in the film." Foreword written for the American release, 1933.
- **The Goebbels meeting at Wilhelmstrasse 49, c. March 1933 [R].** **CORRECTED:** the geography lens tagged this [A] while tagging the flight that follows from it [R], though its own note concedes that no contemporary record fixes the meeting and that Lang embellished it across retellings. Goebbels's diary does not mention it; the praise for Metropolis and M and the offer of the industry come entirely from Lang, from the 1940s onward. Coordinates 52.5137, 13.3838 are correct.
- **The flight [contradiction, unresolved].**
  - Lang: he sold his wife's jewelry and took the evening train to Paris the same night, leaving bank accounts and possessions [R].
  - The record: "Goebbels' diary makes no mention of such a meeting and Lang's passport also shows that he did not leave until June and made repeated trips between France and Germany throughout 1933" [A as a sentence in the Wikipedia article on *The Testament of Dr. Mabuse*]. German Wikipedia adds three months of shuttling between Berlin, London and Paris, and a currency exchange at his bank.
  - **CORRECTED (citation):** Wikipedia attaches that sentence to Kracauer, *From Caligari to Hitler* (1947), pp. 248-250, which cannot support it: the passport research postdates Kracauer by decades and derives from Goesta Werner (1990) and McGilligan (1997). The finding stands; the citation must not be reproduced.
  - The chronology lens gives **31 July 1933 as the permanent departure [A]**, and that is the journey's date; the one-night story sits beside it in `date_confidence`.
- **Divorce from Thea von Harbou, 1933 [A];** she stays in Germany and joins the Nazi party [A].

## 6. Paris and the crossing (1933-1934)

- **About a year in Paris in the French film milieu [A, McGilligan].** Address unestablished; corpus Paris pin 48.8566, 2.3522.
- **Liliom (1934), his only French film, from Molnar's play, with Charles Boyer and Madeleine Ozeray, produced by Erich Pommer [A].** **CORRECTED:** the geography lens placed the shoot at Studios Francoeur/Epinay, 48.9550, 2.3106. It was shot at the **Joinville studios, Joinville-le-Pont**, approx. 48.8206, 2.4703, where Pommer's Fox-Europa was based after April 1933; an error of about 17 km.
- **Arrival in New York June 1934, contracted to MGM through David O. Selznick [A for move and contract; R for the pier].** **CORRECTED:** the chronology lens dated the move to 1936 by conflating it with the release of *Fury* (5 June 1936).

## 7. Hollywood (1934-1956)

- **Fury (1936), MGM, with Spencer Tracy; You Only Live Once (1937); You and Me (1938) [A].** **CORRECTED:** the MGM lot at 10202 West Washington Boulevard, Culver City, is approx. 34.0180, -118.4017; the earlier pin, 34.0247, -118.3892, sits 1.3 km northeast.
- **Naturalised a United States citizen, 1939 [A, both Wikipedias].** **CORRECTED:** the geography lens gave 1935, contradicting its own chronology. The downtown Los Angeles courthouse pin, 34.0537, -118.2427, remains an unsourced approximation.
- **The Return of Frank James (1940) and Western Union (1941), his first colour productions [A].** **CORRECTED:** the earlier stop anchored both at Sedona, Arizona, 34.8697, -111.7610. *Western Union* was shot at **Fredonia, Arizona** (approx. 36.95, -112.53) and in Utah at Johnson Canyon, Paria, The Gap and Alton, plus the Fox lot, some 230 km from Sedona; *The Return of Frank James* is documented on the **Sierra Railroad in Tuolumne County, California**. The Sedona pin is dropped and the journey anchors one stop at the Sierra Railroad's Jamestown depot, 37.9527, -120.4224, a regional anchor and not a documented setup.
- **Man Hunt (1941); Hangmen Also Die! (1943), co-written with Bertolt Brecht, on the killing of Reinhard Heydrich; Ministry of Fear and The Woman in the Window (1944); Scarlet Street (1945) with Edward G. Robinson and Joan Bennett; The Blue Gardenia and The Big Heat (1953); While the City Sleeps and Beyond a Reasonable Doubt (1956), his last American films [A].** The Beverly Hills pin, 34.0736, -118.4004, marks a district; no address is established. No contract file or studio correspondence was reached; this section rests on McGilligan and per-film Wikipedia articles.

## 8. The return to Germany (1958-1960)

- **Artur Brauner brings Lang back after two decades to CCC-Filmkunst for Der Tiger von Eschnapur, Das indische Grabmal (1959) and Die 1000 Augen des Dr. Mabuse (1960), his last film, made as his eyesight failed [A, McGilligan].** **CORRECTED:** CCC-Filmkunst is at Verlaengerte Daumstrasse 16, Berlin-Haselhorst, approx. 52.5407, 13.2196; the earlier pin, 52.5390, 13.1930, falls 1.8 km west.
- **Rajasthan location work at Udaipur, City Palace and Lake Pichola [A].** Some sources add Jaipur-area forts; not confirmed here.
- **"I am not an artist. I am a craftsman." [A].** **CORRECTED:** the pool tagged this [R] from quotation-aggregator sites. It is traceable to the **Directors Guild of America Quarterly, Spring 2012** feature, which reports him saying "I am not an artist" to an actor on the set of *The 1,000 Eyes of Dr. Mabuse* in 1960, then elaborating "I am a craftsman." Tag upgraded; the two-part delivery is kept in the quote source line rather than smoothed into an epigram.

## 9. Last years and death (1960-1976)

- **Star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame, 1600 Vine Street, 8 February 1960 [A]. President of the Cannes jury, 1964 [A]. Married Lily Latte, 1971, in the United States [A]**, with no place or month in reachable sources.
- **Appears as himself in Godard's Le Mepris (1963) [A].** The pool records the location only as "Italy/France (production)"; no coordinate is claimed, and the stop is folded into Cannes rather than pinned on a guess.

## 10. Afterlife

- **1979: memorial plaque at Zeltgasse 1**, the corner house whose frontages give it three street names (Zeltgasse, Piaristengasse, Neudeggergasse), 8th district, Josefstadt [A, German Wikipedia citing Schnauber]. A **later family residence, not the birth address.** In **1986 Cornelius Schnauber records that the plaque carries wrong dates [A]:** "Die Fritz-Lang-Gedenktafel am Wohnhaus in der Piaristengasse in Wien enthaelt leider falsche Daten." (Umlauts transliterated.)
- **1 July 2008: a 16mm safety reduction negative holding about 25 minutes of the original 1927 cut is announced from the Museo del Cine Pablo Ducros Hicken, Buenos Aires**, traced by Fernando Martin Pena and identified with the museum's director Paula Felix-Didier [A]. **CORRECTED:** the earlier pin, -34.6118, -58.4120, is 5.5 km from the museum, at -34.6297, -58.3572 in La Boca. **Unresolved gap:** the museum moved into that building only on 1 August 2011, so in 2008 the reels sat at earlier premises, unidentified in reachable sources.
- **12 February 2010: the restored Metropolis premieres at the Friedrichstadtpalast, in the open air at the Brandenburg Gate, and at the Alte Oper in Frankfurt; North American premiere 25 April 2010 at Mann's Chinese Theatre [A].**

## 12. Contradictions recorded and not resolved

1. **The Goebbels meeting and the flight.** Lang's one-night story against the passport and the absent diary entry; the permanent departure is 31 July 1933. All carried side by side.
2. **Volunteer or conscript.** Chronology lens against English Wikipedia against the muster record.
3. **1935 or 1939 for the naturalisation.** The two lenses against each other; both Wikipedias say 1939.
4. **1934 or 1936 for the move to America.** The crossing conflated with the release of *Fury*.
5. **Birth address.** Baptismal register against Lang's claim against the Mariahilf pin.
6. **Babelsberg coordinates.** 52.3925 (geography lens) against 52.4009 (corpus), under a kilometre apart.
7. **The Kracauer citation.** Wikipedia's footnote for the passport finding is anachronistic.
8. **The Lorre staircase.** Repeated everywhere, sourced nowhere.

## 13. Honest gaps

- No street address for the Munich or Paris lodgings, the Berlin flat where Elisabeth Rosenthal died, or the Beverly Hills house. No residence registration, sale record or exhibition catalogue was reached for the painting years.
- The circumstances of that death in September 1920 are not established. The charge and its dismissal are attested; nothing else is.
- Unit designations and the Romanian deployment date are unreached; the Austrian military record is cited by Gunning and German Wikipedia but not consulted directly.
- The 2008 premises of the Museo del Cine are unidentified.
- The pier of the June 1934 arrival, the courthouse of the 1939 naturalisation, the venue of the 1971 marriage and the day of the 1976 interment are unestablished; each is defaulted and flagged in the relevant `date_confidence`.
- No German-language biography beyond the German Wikipedia synthesis was consulted.
