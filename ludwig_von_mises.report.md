## 1. Galicia, the university, the Chamber, 1881-1913

- Born **29 September 1881, Lemberg** (now Lviv), to the railway engineer **Arthur von Mises** and
  **Adele née Landau**. **[A**, University of Vienna Memorial Book, gedenkbuch.univie.ac.at;
  English Wikipedia**]**
- **1881**, great-grandfather **Mayer Rachmiel Mises** raised to hereditary Austrian nobility by
  Franz Joseph: the source of the "Edler von". **[A**, mises.org, "Ludwig von Mises and the Vienna
  of His Time"**]**
- **Relocation to Vienna** when Arthur joins the Ministry of Railways. Addresses: Elisabethgasse 3,
  Friedrichstrasse 4, Wollzeile 24. The date is given by the source itself as **"1889/1892"** and is
  **not resolved**. **[R**, same article**]** The file uses 1889 as an ordering device and says so.
- **Akademisches Gymnasium** from **1892** **[A**, mises.org, "Who Was Ludwig von Mises?"**]**;
  classmate **Hans Kelsen** **[A**, English Wikipedia; dossier**]**, who is his colleague again in
  Geneva after 1934.
- **1900**, enrols in law and government science, University of Vienna **[A**, mises.org**]**;
  student study of Galician landlord-peasant relations **[A**, English Wikipedia; dossier**]**.
- **1903**, father dies after surgery at **Halberstadt** **[A**, mises.org**]**. Month, day,
  hospital and procedure all unrecorded.
- **c. 1903**, reads **Carl Menger's** *Grundsätze* and dates his conversion from historicism to it.
  **[R**, the sole authority is Mises's own retrospective *Notes and Recollections*; no contemporary
  document exists**]**
- **From 1904**, **Böhm-Bawerk's seminar** with **Schumpeter, Otto Bauer, Hilferding** **[A**,
  dossier citing *Notes and Recollections*; standard in the literature**]**.
- **February 1906**, Dr. jur. conferred **[A**, mises.org**]**. The dossier's day, **20 February**,
  is carried without independent confirmation.
- **1906**, enters the state financial administration **[R**, stated generally by mises.org; no
  appointment record consulted**]**.
- **1909**, joins the **Lower Austrian Chamber of Commerce and Industry**; c. **25 years**, ending
  as head of its finance department **[A**, mises.org; Memorial Book**]**.
- **1912**, *Theorie des Geldes und der Umlaufsmittel* **[A**, econlib.org**]**.
- **1913** Privatdozent; **1918** the unpaid honorary **extraordinarius** title **[A**, Memorial
  Book; mises.org**]**. The file folds 1918 into the 1913 stop.

**Gaps, stated as gaps.** No Lemberg house address is recoverable; the birth pin is the city point.
Arthur von Mises's birth year is nowhere given, so his age at death cannot be stated. And no source
consulted says *why* Mises never held a paid Austrian chair: antisemitism and the unpopularity of
his conclusions are the standing explanations in the secondary literature and are documented in
nothing reached here, so the campa says only that no reason is on record.

## 2. War and the collapse of the empire, 1914-1922

- **1 August 1914**, mobilized; field artillery incl. the **30th Field Cannon Regiment**; combat in
  Galicia and the Carpathians, later Russian Ukraine and Crimea; rises to captain. **[R as to
  detail**, the dossier cites Hülsmann's biography and personnel records, neither fetched here**]**
- **1917-1918**, memoranda on inflation, war finance, currency and trade for the **War Ministry**
  **[R**, dossier, citing records recovered from Moscow**]**.
- **1918-1920**, directs an Austrian **restitution-and-settlements office**. The claim that a talk
  with **Otto Bauer** dissuaded a Bolshevik seizure of power rests on **Mises's own recollection and
  nothing else**; the dossier flags it and so does the campa. **[R]**
- **1919** *Nation, Staat und Wirtschaft*; **1920** *Die Wirtschaftsrechnung im sozialistischen
  Gemeinwesen*. **[A / C]** An earlier dossier pass called both "unresolved and not independently
  attested". **That non-attestation is itself wrong**: English Wikipedia's "Economic calculation
  problem" refers throughout to "his 1920 article", and the 1919 book is standard bibliography.
- **1922**, advises on the reorganization of the **Austrian National Bank** and the League of
  Nations stabilization **[A**, Memorial Book**]**. The campa says plainly that the
  one-economist-stopped-the-hyperinflation story compresses a collective international operation.

## 3. The private seminar and the last Vienna decade, 1920s-1934

- The **Privatseminar**, alternate Fridays in his Chamber office, continuing at an Italian
  restaurant and the **Café Künstler**; regulars **Hayek, Machlup, Morgenstern, Schütz, Voegelin**
  **[A**, dossier; the Memorial Book independently attests Hayek**]**. No start date is attested;
  1923 in the file orders the segment.
- **1926**, American lecture tour on the **Laura Spelman Rockefeller Memorial** **[A**, dossier**]**.
  No itinerary or venue survives; the pin is the generic New York point.
- **Institute for Business Cycle Research: contradiction, flagged, not silently resolved.** The
  incoming dossier gave **1926** in its chronology and **1927** in its geography index for the same
  institution. English and German Wikipedia give **1927**, with Hayek as first director; econlib.org
  gives 1926. The file uses **1927** and records the disagreement. **[C]**
- **1933**, economic adviser to Chancellor **Engelbert Dollfuss** under the authoritarian
  corporatist regime **[R**, austriainusa.org, repeated in the dossier; no appointment document or
  record of what he advised was reached**]**. Stated flat in the campa.

## 4. Geneva, dispossession, escape, 1934-1940

- Leaves Vienna **3 October 1934**; first lecture at the **Graduate Institute of International
  Studies 25 October**, teaching in French beside **Rappard, Mantoux, Kelsen** **[A**, dossier;
  appointment confirmed by econlib.org**]**. First paid academic post of his life, at 53.
- **March 1938**, the Gestapo empties the **Vienna apartment** held since 1911: twenty-one boxes in
  late March, then library, correspondence, paintings, silver, and a coin collection of some 150
  gold pieces and 3,000+ other coins and medals **[A**, FEE, Ebeling, "Mises's Lost Papers"; Graz
  Mises Private Archive project**]**. **Contradiction, flagged:** English Wikipedia puts the raid
  **on the day German forces entered Vienna, 12 March**, the dossier **days after**. Both are
  carried in `date_confidence`; neither is adopted.
- **April 1938**, expelled from the University of Vienna as a Jew. **Contradiction, flagged:**
  institutional pages give **8 April** and **22 April** and do not reconcile. **[A as to the fact]**
- **6 July 1938**, marries **Margit Herzfeld Serény** in Geneva **[A as to year**, mises.org;
  **R as to day and venue**, dossier via Margit's memoir**]**.
- **August 1938**, **Colloque Walter Lippmann**, Paris, with Hayek, Röpke, Rüstow, Rougier,
  Lippmann **[A**, dossier**]**. **Pin note:** the venue could not be established, so the generic
  Paris centroid (48.8566, 2.3522) is an explicit fallback. Freud's precise rue Adolphe-Yvon pin was
  **not** reused: it belongs to an unrelated event and would be false precision.
- **1940**, *Nationalökonomie*, Editions Union, Geneva; wartime distribution nearly nil **[A**,
  dossier; English Wikipedia**]**.
- The exit papers are the best-documented stretch of his life **[A**, Procesos de Mercado, "The
  Secret Passage" (2025)**]**: lawyer **Pierre-Em. Atteslander** requests Swiss identity
  certificates **April 1940**; the Swiss Federal Police delivers them **31 May**; the Portuguese
  consulate wires Lisbon **5 June**; Lisbon authorizes the urgent transit visa **6 June**.
- **4 July 1940** they leave Geneva; southern France, the Spanish frontier (repeatedly refused),
  Lisbon, and by ship to a landing **2 August 1940**. **[R as to route]**: **Margit's memoir is the
  only detailed witness**, and later accounts disagree with her and with each other about transport
  and road. Rockefeller funding for the passage is **[A**, mises.org; austriainusa.org**]**.
- **Alfred Schütz meeting the ship**: **[R]**, dossier only. A grep found no Schütz journey file and
  no corroboration. Kept and flagged, per the dossier's own recommendation that it be checked
  against Margit's memoir rather than carried on dossier authority.

## 5. America, 1940-1969

- **December 1940**, Harvard lecture "Money as a Dynamic Factor"; the American network forms around
  **Hazlitt, Chamberlain, Eastman, Read, Fertig** **[A**, dossier**]**.
- **15 December 1940**, guest researcher at the **NBER**, Rockefeller-supported grant of **$2,500**
  for a year **[R**, dossier; no grant record reached**]**. **October 1941**, apartment **3E, 777
  West End Avenue**, kept for life **[A**, dossier**]**.
- **1942**, visiting professor at **UNAM, Mexico City** **[A]**. **[C] Serious dossier
  misattribution corrected:** the dossier attached *Economic Policy: Thoughts for Today and
  Tomorrow* to this trip. The Mexico lectures appeared as *Problemas económicos de México*;
  *Economic Policy* is the **1959 Buenos Aires** material (mises.org: University of Buenos Aires,
  1959, written up in prose, Regnery/Gateway 1979, Free Market Books 1995).
- **February 1945 to May 1969**, visiting professor at **NYU**, seminar on **Trinity Place**, paid
  by the **William Volker Fund** and later a **Fertig** consortium, not by NYU **[A**, mises.org**]**.
- **1946**, US citizenship **[A**, mises.org; econlib.org**]**, no courthouse detail found; adviser
  to **FEE**, Irvington-on-Hudson, from its first year to his death **[A**, dossier**]**.
- **10 April 1947**, founding meeting of the **Mont Pèlerin Society**, Hotel du Parc, 39
  participants **[A**, English Wikipedia**]**. The **walkout anecdote** ("you're all a bunch of
  socialists") is **[R]**: Friedman's recollection via the Hoover Institution, of this or a later
  meeting, consistent with the transcripts but not a verified exchange. Kept as [R] inside the
  founding stop, not staged as its own scene.
- **1949**, Yale University Press publishes *Human Action*. **[A / C]** The dossier tagged this
  **[R]**, wrongly: the 1949 Yale first edition is in the Open Library record and English
  Wikipedia's "Human Action". Only the **day, 14 September**, is unattested in anything reachable
  here; the file uses it and says so in `date_confidence`.
- **1957** Grove City honorary LL.D. **[A**, English Wikipedia**]**; **1963** NYU and **1964**
  Freiburg honorary doctorates **[A**, dossier**]**. Folded into one stop; every one comes from an
  institution that never carried him on a faculty payroll.
- **1959**, **six lectures at the University of Buenos Aires** **[A**, mises.org**]**. **The
  incoming dossier missed this journey entirely**; recovered by the correction above and written in.
  The venue is unidentified, so the pin is the city point.
- **1963** defective Yale revision ("scandalous botchery"); **1966** corrected Regnery third edition
  from Chicago **[A**, English Wikipedia; dossier**]**.

## 6. Death, papers, afterlife

- **10 October 1973**, dies in New York City at 92 **[A**, Memorial Book; mises.org; English
  Wikipedia**]**. **No public source consulted gives a cause of death.** That he died in the West
  End Avenue apartment is **[R]**, inferred from the lifelong tenancy.
- Buried at **Ferncliff Cemetery, Hartsdale**; library bequeathed by will to **Hillsdale College**,
  Michigan **[A**, English Wikipedia**]**. Interment date unattested; 13 October orders the segment.
- The seized papers: stored by German authorities, removed by the Red Army to **Moscow, May 1945**,
  sealed on Stalin's order until 1991 **[A**, FEE, Ebeling**]**. **October 1996**, **Richard and
  Anna Ebeling** photocopy nearly **10,000 pages** there. **[A / C]** The dossier tagged this
  **[R]** with an "8,000-10,000 pages" hedge; Ebeling's own account on fee.org gives October 1996
  and "nearly 10,000 pages", so the hedge is dropped and the tag corrected. Originals restituted to
  Austria in the **early 2000s**, now in the **Austrian State Archive** **[A**, Graz project**]**.
- **Absence recorded as an absence:** no plaque, statue, or named street for Mises in Vienna could
  be confirmed. Searches returned nothing and the dossier claims none. Reported as "nothing found",
  not as "none exists".
- **Never received the Nobel Memorial Prize.** Hayek's shared 1974 prize, the year after Mises's
  death, is called "Mises's Nobel" by advocates; that is movement symbolism, not an award. **[A]**
  The dossier gave it a Mont Pèlerin coordinate; since the entry concedes it is "symbolic, not a
  stop", it was given **no stop and no pin** and appears only here.

## Interlocks with the existing corpus

Named in a campa, each a real relation on Mises's own side of the ledger and inside his lifetime:

- **`carl_menger`**: Mises reads the *Grundsätze* c. 1903 and credits it with making an economist of
  him. One-directional (Menger's file never mentions Mises; a grep confirms it), but a genuine
  relation of influence and citation, with Menger alive across the city at the time.
- **`murray_rothbard`**: attends the Trinity Place seminar from 1949, calls Mises his teacher for
  life, breaks with him over anarchism the same winter, and receives Mises's 1962 commendation of
  *Man, Economy, and State*. Attested inside Rothbard's own file. Direct and personal.

Not named: **`freud`** (same city, same year, same persecution mechanism, but no documented relation
between the two men; shared geography is not a reason to name-drop and the map already shows
co-location) and **`einstein`** (a parallel exodus from another city seven years earlier, no
relation). **`javier_milei`** and **`charles_koch`** are handled below.

Checked and irrelevant, stated per instruction: the **Kaaba** (21.4225, 39.8262) and **Temple
Mount** (31.7780, 35.2354) pins have no findable connection to Mises in the dossier or any grepped
journey file. **Buenos Aires** was flagged as having no Mises connection; that proved wrong for an

## Apparatus relations for the operator

**Mises is not listed in `EXCEPTIONS.md`**, so no apparatus stops were written. Per the adversary's
duty in that file, relations found but not rooted in Mises's own life are listed here rather than
deleted, with the direction stated.

1. **`javier_milei`: FORWARD.** Milei's file records that around **2013** he read Rothbard and
   adopted a lineage "through Mises and Hayek to Adam Smith". Milei was born in 1970; a 2013
   artifact cannot be embedded in a file whose clock stopped in 1973, so it appears in **no campa
   and no `suggested_ref`** here. It is Milei's stop, on Milei's file, already there. Flagged
   because the Argentine reception of Mises is the live nerve in this subject.
2. **`charles_koch`: FORWARD in substance, though inside Mises's lifetime.** Koch's file records
   Mises among his 1950s Freedom School reading: Koch-to-text, one-directional, with nothing on
   Mises's side recording Koch at all. Not written in; it belongs as a `suggested_ref` on Koch's own
   file, where the interlock pass also placed it.
3. **The Ludwig von Mises Institute (Auburn, 1982): FORWARD.** Founded nine years after his death by
   Rockwell, Blumert and **Rothbard**, with Ron Paul funding. It carries his name and nothing else
   of his. Not written in; it is Rothbard's stop.
4. **A Moscow stop was considered and declined.** The Ebeling recovery of October 1996 is
   *Ebeling's* travel, not Mises's. Under "a stop belongs to whoever was actually there" the papers
   stay with the subject: the seizure is a stop at Mises's own Vienna apartment, and the Moscow
   removal, the 1996 recovery and the restitution are carried in the campas and sources of the
   apartment and grave stops. Putting Moscow on the map is a ruling for the operator.

No **backward** apparatus relation was found. Mises's framework is nowhere applied to material he
did not himself treat.
