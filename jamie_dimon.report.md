**[A]** The paternal grandfather was a Greek immigrant banker who changed the family surname from Papademetriou to Dimon (Wikipedia, 'Jamie Dimon'), which also names the parents: Theodore Dimon, stockbroker, and Themis (née Kalos).

**[R]** Family accounts place the grandfather, Panos Papademetriou, as a banker in Smyrna and Athens before emigration. The Smyrna stop (38.4192, 27.1287, dated 1921-01-01) says in `date_confidence` that the date orders the segment only and that Dimon never lived there.

**[C]** The afterlife lens bundled the ancestry claim with a fringe conspiracy cluster (Dimon secretly Jewish, a Rothschild proxy, an intelligence operative) and flagged the whole item unverified. Split: ancestry and surname are **[A]**; the conspiracy cluster traced to no source and is not in the journey file. Noted so the operator knows it was dropped deliberately.

**[A]** Born New York City, 13 March 1956. **[R]** Childhood in Jackson Heights, Queens; Public School 69 to roughly fifth grade before the family moved to Manhattan. Wikipedia's PS 69 page returned 404, so that pin is neighbourhood-level. **[A]** Graduated the Browning School, 52 East 62nd Street, 1974; coordinates corrected from 40.7654 / -73.9678 to **40.764917 / -73.969222**.

**Gap.** Nothing about the schoolboy is on the record anywhere reachable; the Queens and Browning years exist only as institutional facts.

## 6. 2008 and the crisis settlements (2008-2013)

### Bear Stearns

**[C] Three pool errors corrected and reconciled.** (1) The geography lens's range began **1998**-03-16, a typo for 2008. (2) The afterlife lens called 16 March 2008 a *closing* date; nothing closed in March. Per Wikipedia, 'Bear Stearns': agreement **14 March 2008**, price revised from $2 to **$10/share on 24 March**, shareholder approval **29 May**. (3) The two lenses gave *different* coordinates for the same building, both south of 383 Madison Avenue, which sits between East 46th and East 47th Streets. Reconciled to **40.7562 / -73.9765**.

**[A] Maiden Lane.** The Federal Reserve Bank of New York lent **$29 bn** against a **$30 bn** asset pool; JPMorgan contributed **$1 bn** subordinated, taking first losses. This is the arithmetic that complicates the "saved Wall Street unaided" legend, stated flatly and not as commentary.

### Washington Mutual

**[C] Wrong building in the pool.** WaMu Center is at **1301 Second Avenue**, not 1201 Third Avenue: Wikipedia's article says it stands "on the opposite corner from 1201 Third Avenue that was previously named Washington Mutual Tower." Its coordinates are corrected to **47.607311 / -122.337109**, and the pool's "now Russell Investments Center" is stale: the building carries the JPMorganChase name. **[A]** OTS closed the bank 25 September 2008, FDIC appointed receiver, JPMorgan bought the banking operations for **$1.9 bn**.

### TARP

**[C]** The pool's Treasury pin landed near the Ellipse, about 465 m off; corrected to **38.897556 / -77.034278**. **[A]** $25 bn of preferred stock and warrants under the Capital Purchase Program, October 2008; repaid June 2009. **[R]** The 13 October meeting of the nine chief executives is widely reported, but Dimon's presence in the building that day is unconfirmed, and the stop says so.

### The London Whale and the 2013 settlements

**[A]** Disclosure May 2012, losses eventually about $6.2 bn. **[A]** The "tempest in a teapot" phrase is attested *indirectly but reliably*: Dimon's own 2012 letter quotes it back and dates it to the 13 April 2012 earnings call. No transcript of that call was located in either pass. **[R]** The roughly **$920 m** in combined US/UK penalties (September 2013, the SEC order including an admission of securities-law violations) and the **$13 bn** RMBS settlement (November 2013) rest on the pool's synthesis of the regulators' and DOJ announcements; no primary page was refetched.

**Gap, stated as a gap.** Whether the securities in the $13 bn settlement were JPMorgan's own or came in with Bear Stearns and Washington Mutual is the most-repeated claim about it and is *not* in the pool. The file declines to assert it, and the campa says the settlement draws no such distinction.
