# Wolfgang Ernst Pauli (1900-1958): research report
*Compiled 2026-08-14 for `wolfgang_pauli.journey.json`. QUEUE.md entry: "physics <-> Jung <-> number mysticism; 7 atlas files already name him, an orphan hub, the dreams analyzed at Kusnacht, the Pauli effect, 137 and the room he died in."*

Legend: **[A]** = attested, source named · **[R]** = reconstruction, tradition, anecdote, inference. Contradictions are flagged and **not adjudicated**. Unreachable sources are named with the reason. Where a stop's date or pin is defaulted, inferred or contested, the journey's `date_confidence` field carries the hedge.

Method note: the pool arrived adversarially verified and many first-pass claims came back **corrected**. Those corrections are kept in full, because several contradict English Wikipedia and would otherwise be re-inherited.

---

## 1. Vienna and school (1900-1918)

- Born **25 April 1900 in Vienna**, son of the chemist Wolfgang Joseph Pauli and Bertha Camilla Schütz; christened 31 May 1900. **[A: Wikipedia "Wolfgang Pauli"; MacTutor, which adds the christening date and the silver cup]**
- Godfather: **Ernst Mach**, whose name he carries as his middle name. **[A: Wikipedia; MacTutor]**
- **CORRECTION.** The first pass called him the *second* child. He was the **first**; his only sibling, **Hertha Pauli**, was born 4 September 1906. **[A: Wikipedia "Hertha Pauli"]**
- Graduates with distinction from the **Döblinger-Gymnasium**, 1918, and publishes his first paper, on general relativity, within two months. **[A: Wikipedia]** Month and day not established; defaults to 1918-01-01.
- **CORRECTION (coordinates).** The first-pass pin 48.2404, 16.3421 is ~700 m off. The Gymnasium (G19) stands on Lannerstraße, 1190 Wien, at **48.2355, 16.3480**. **[A: OpenStreetMap/Nominatim]**
- **GAP.** No birth address survives in anything consulted; the Vienna stops use a city-centre placeholder pin (48.2082, 16.3738).

## 2. Munich (1918-1921)

- Studies under **Arnold Sommerfeld**; doctorate 1921 on the quantum theory of ionized diatomic hydrogen. **[A: Wikipedia; MacTutor]** Month not established; defaults to 1921-01-01.
- The **237-page relativity article** for the Encyclopedia of Mathematical Sciences, issued separately as a monograph; **Einstein** calls it masterly. **[A: Wikipedia]**
- **Heisenberg** enters the same seminar in autumn 1920, a year behind him; lifelong rival-friendship. **[A: `heisenberg.journey.json`]** Pin **48.1351, 11.582** inherited byte-identical from that file, distinct from the LMU pin used for the doctorate stop.

## 3. Göttingen, Copenhagen, Hamburg (1921-1928)

- Assistant to **Max Born**, Göttingen. **CONTRADICTION, unresolved:** MacTutor gives **October 1921** through 1922; the pool's chronology lens said 1922, its geography lens 1921-1922. Journey follows MacTutor. **[A/R]**
- A year at **Niels Bohr's institute**, Blegdamsvej. **CONTRADICTION, unresolved:** MacTutor gives **1922-23**, one lens gave 1923; the journey follows MacTutor. **[A]** Pin **55.6989, 12.5654** inherited byte-identical from `niels_bohr.journey.json`.
- **Hamburg lectureship, 1923**, held five years. **[A: Wikipedia]**
- **Exclusion principle. CORRECTION (date).** The pool's lenses disagreed, 1924 against 1925. The standard dating splits them: the **two-valued degree of freedom** is proposed **late 1924**; the exclusion paper (*Zeitschrift für Physik* 31, 765-783) is **received January 1925**. **[A: Wikipedia "Pauli exclusion principle"]**
- **CORRECTION (conflation).** The first pass folded spin and the Pauli matrices into 1925. Uhlenbeck and Goudsmit identify the two-valuedness as **spin in 1925**; Pauli's nonrelativistic theory of spin, the **Pauli equation and matrices, is 1927**. **[A: Wikipedia "Pauli equation"]**
- **The Pauli effect at Hamburg:** Otto Stern bars him from the experimental hall. **[R: Wikipedia "Pauli effect", undated within the source]** Journey places it at 1926-01-01, inside the Hamburg years.

## 4. Solvay, the mother's death, the Zurich chair (1927-1930)

- **Fifth Solvay Congress, Brussels, 24-29 October 1927**, with Einstein, Bohr, Heisenberg, Schrödinger, Dirac, Born. **[A: `dirac.journey.json`; Wikipedia "Solvay Conference"]** Pin **50.8175, 4.3528** (Parc Léopold) inherited byte-identical from `dirac.journey.json`; two lenses gave 50.8398/4.3766 and 50.8503/4.3517 for the same event, and the canonical pin wins.
- The Dirac remark. **VARIANT WORDINGS, not adjudicated.** The pool gives "There is no God and Dirac is his Prophet," citing Heisenberg, *Der Teil und das Ganze* (1969), p. 119; `dirac.journey.json` carries "God does not exist and Dirac is His prophet." The journey prints the pool's wording and cites Heisenberg.
- **Death of Bertha Camilla Pauli. MAJOR CORRECTION.** English Wikipedia places the suicide "at the end of 1930, immediately after his divorce", and the pool's chronology and afterlife lenses inherited that. **She died in 1927.** **[A: MacTutor; German Wikipedia; Wiener Persönlichkeiten, "Bertha Pauli (1878-1927), Journalistin"]** Three years separate the death from the divorce. Journey dates it 1927-11-01 and states the contradiction.
- **ETH Zurich professorship, 1928**, held to his death apart from the war years. **[A: Wikipedia]** Pin **47.3763, 8.5476** inherited byte-identical from `robert_oppenheimer.journey.json`.
- **Robert Oppenheimer** sent by Paul Ehrenfest to Zurich in 1929 to be ground down by Pauli. **[A: `robert_oppenheimer.journey.json`, stop "Zurich: under the lash of Pauli"]**
- **Marriage to Käthe Deppner. CORRECTION (place).** One lens claimed "Zurich hosts the wedding." It was **Berlin, 23 December 1929**. **[A: MacTutor]** Journey pins Berlin, 52.52 / 13.405.
- **Divorce. CORRECTION (place).** Granted in **Vienna, 29 November 1930**, not Zurich. **[A: MacTutor]**
- **The neutrino letter, 4 December 1930**, "Dear Radioactive Ladies and Gentlemen," written in Zurich for the Tübingen meeting he did not attend. **[A: Wikipedia; Wikipedia "Neutrino"]** A Tübingen stop was considered and **dropped**: he was not there, and the reading-aloud is narrated inside the Zurich stop.
- **CORRECTION (the "terrible thing" quote).** The wording is genuine (Open Library full-text search returns 24 books carrying it), but citation and geolocation were both wrong. Correct citation per Wikiquote: **Frederick Reines, foreword to Christine Sutton, *Spaceship Neutrino* (1992), p. xi, as a "Statement of 1930."** The 1995 Nobel lecture attribution **could not be verified** and was dropped. Reines reports it as a remark to a friend, unconnected to Tübingen, which the first pass had pinned with coordinates.

## 5. Crisis, Küsnacht, second marriage (1931-1934)

- **Ann Arbor, Michigan summer symposium, 1931.** **[A: Wikipedia]** Month inferred.
- **Letter to Rudolf Peierls, 29 September 1931**, on semiconductors. **[A: *Wolfgang Pauli, Wissenschaftlicher Briefwechsel*, Band II (1985), p. 94]**
- **DROPPED STOP: Rome, via Panisperna, October 1931.** **[R: standard neutrino historiography]** Attendance is a reconstruction, and the Fermi link is already carried by the neutrino-letter campa.
- **January 1932: consults Carl Gustav Jung at Küsnacht**, on his father's advice. **[A: Wikipedia]** Pin **47.3117, 8.5789** (Seestraße 228) inherited byte-identical from `jung.journey.json`. **COORDINATE CONTRADICTION noted:** the lenses gave 47.3122/8.5825, 47.3244/8.5836 and 47.3242/8.5875 for the same house, and Nominatim proposed 47.3106, 8.5828; the canonical pin was kept.
- **MAJOR CORRECTION (who conducted the analysis).** The first pass had Jung assigning Pauli to **Erna Rosenbaum** "at first," then taking him into direct analysis and personally recording 400+ dreams. That inverts the record: German Wikipedia has Pauli in treatment **with Rosenbaum from 1932 to 1934**, the whole period assigned to Jung, and **Jung states in *Psychology and Alchemy* that he had no personal contact with the dreamer during the large initial series**. **[A]**
- **Marriage to Franziska "Franca" Bertram, 1934.** **[A: Wikipedia]** The day 4 April appears in one lens only and is unconfirmed.
- **Franca's assessment. CORRECTION (transcription).** The pool read "his unworldliness stemmed precisely his belief," which is ungrammatical. Open Library returns the passage in three books (Miller's *137*, its US twin *Deciphering the Cosmic Number*, and **Pais, *The Genius of Science***) reading "**stemmed precisely from his belief**."
- **Honeymoon breakdown, 1934.** **[R: Wikipedia "Pauli effect"]** **CORRECTIONS:** the chronology lens tagged it [A] and called the vehicle a taxi; the source describes a **car**, and the geography lens tagged the same incident [R]. Route and town **not recorded**; Zurich is pinned as departure point only.
- **DROPPED STOP: seventh Solvay Congress, October 1933.** The congress is real ("Structure et propriétés des noyaux atomiques") **[A]**, but **Pauli's attendance was not independently confirmed**, and the first pass's claim that **Fermi presented his beta-decay theory there is false**: Fermi wrote it *after* the meeting, publishing a tentative paper in December 1933 and the full version in 1934. **[A: Wikipedia "Enrico Fermi"]**

## 6. Princeton and the war (1935-1946)

- **Visiting professorship, Institute for Advanced Study, 1935 [A]**; permanent professorship and emigration **1940 [A]**, after a refused Swiss application.
- **1938:** the Anschluss makes him automatically a German citizen. **[A: Wikipedia]**
- **1940:** re-derives the **spin-statistics theorem**. **[A: Wikipedia]**
- **Nobel Prize in Physics 1945** for the exclusion principle, nominated by **Einstein**, received at Princeton rather than Stockholm. **[A: Wikipedia; Nobel Prize organization]**
- **CORRECTION (what happened at the reception).** The first pass had **Einstein presenting the medal in person** and Pauli calling it the proudest moment of his career. Neither is supported. MacTutor confirms a "special Princeton celebration on **10 December 1945**" in place of Stockholm, at which **Einstein spoke**, praising Pauli as his successor. No source has the medal changing hands.
- **1946:** naturalized as a **US citizen [A: German Wikipedia]** and returns to the ETH chair. **CORRECTION:** the claim that the American passport "lapsed into a wartime formality" after 1949 is **unsupported**; he is normally described as holding both. Dropped.
- **Nobel lecture, Stockholm, 13 December 1946.** **[R: Nobel records via the pool, not re-fetched]**

## 7. Zurich again: physics and the psyche (1946-1952)

- **The vase at the founding of the C.G. Jung Institute, 1948. [R]** Founding year confirmed (**Wikipedia, "C. G. Jung Institute in Zürich"**: founded 1948, moved to Küsnacht 1979); the vase is in "Pauli effect." **CORRECTIONS:** the **April dating appears in no source consulted**; the **tag is [R], not [A]**; the first-pass pin 47.3717, 8.5424 **does not match the institute's original premises**, which were not located, so the journey pins the city centre.
- **CORRECTION ("Background Physics").** Not a published article analysing the Pauli effect, but **"Moderne Beispiele zur Hintergrundsphysik" (1948), a private manuscript sent to Jung**, on **physical concepts surfacing in dream imagery**.
- **Eranos, Ascona, Kepler lecture. [R]** Placed at 1948-08 by one lens; year and month **not confirmed**.
- **Letter to Markus Fierz, 12 August 1948**, on constructing a new idea of reality. **[A: cited in Suzanne Gieser, *The Innermost Kernel* (2005)]**
- **Swiss citizenship, 1949. [A: Wikipedia]**
- **The Göttingen station anecdote. [R: Wikipedia "Pauli effect"]** **CONTRADICTION, unresolved:** one lens dated it 1950, the other "undated (1930s)", and **Wikipedia gives no date at all**. Journey places it at 1950-01-01 for ordering only. Pin 51.5354, 9.935 is ~230 m from Göttingen Hbf.
- **Princeton cyclotron fire, February 1950. [R: Wikipedia "Pauli effect"]** **CORRECTIONS:** one lens tagged it [A]; it is an anecdote. The pin 40.3346, -74.667 is the **Institute for Advanced Study**, whereas the cyclotron was **Princeton University's**, in the Palmer Physical Laboratory, ~**40.3465, -74.6545**.
- **Naturerklärung und Psyche (Rascher, Zurich, 1952)**: Jung's "Synchronicity" essay bound with Pauli's Kepler study. **[A: Wikipedia "Synchronicity"]** The 1932-1958 correspondence appeared posthumously as **Atom and Archetype**, ed. C.A. Meier (English ed. 2001). **[A]**

## 8. The last years, and 137 (1957-1958)

- **Parity violation, 1957**, and the "weak left-hander" line. **CORRECTION (citation).** The quote is real; the book was wrong. Open Library full-text search for the exact string returns **one** hit: **Lederman with Hill, *Beyond the God Particle* (2013)**. A broader search returns 141 books with the phrase, but **Lederman's *The God Particle* (1993), which Wikiquote cites, is not among them**. Dating: Wu's results were announced mid-January 1957, while Wikiquote says 1956, meaning the December run. The Weisskopf bet **was not re-verified**.
- **The Heisenberg world formula, Munich, 1958**, enthusiasm turning to scorn. **[A: `heisenberg.journey.json`]** Pin **48.176, 11.593** inherited byte-identical.
- **The blank canvas to George Gamow, 1958.** **[A: cited in Michio Kaku, *Hyperspace* (1994), p. 137]** Month not established; 1958-02 is inferred from its place in the world-formula quarrel.
- **Berkeley, May 1958**, the "misunderstand at a deeper level" remark to Jagdish Mehra. **[A: Mehra and Rechenberg, *The Historical Development of Quantum Theory* (2000)]**
- **Death: 15 December 1958, pancreatic cancer, Rotkreuzspital, Zurich, room 137.** **[A: Wikipedia; German Wikipedia, "ein Zimmer mit der Nummer 137," treated as an ill omen]** The remark to **Charles Enz** is **[A as reported, R as to wording]**: secondhand, and phrased differently between retellings.
- **CORRECTION (hospital coordinates).** Two lenses gave 47.3737/8.5486 and 47.3769/8.5417 (the city centre). The Rotkreuzspital stood on **Gloriastrasse in Fluntern, now the Careum Campus, 47.3751, 8.5539**. **[Nominatim]**
- **Burial, Friedhof Zollikon. CORRECTIONS:** the tag is **[A]**, not [R]: German Wikipedia carries a photograph captioned "Paulis Grabstein auf dem Friedhof von Zollikon." Coordinates **47.3362, 8.5822** (Zollikon Dorf, Nominatim), not the lenses' 47.3325/8.5745 or 47.3419/8.5772; two other cemeteries share the municipality, so the one meant is named. **GAP:** the funeral date is not established, and the stop carries the date of death instead.

## 9. Afterlife

- **Wolfgang-Pauli-Gasse, Vienna, designated 1969, Penzing. [A: German Wikipedia]** **CORRECTION:** the first-pass pin 48.1897, 16.3 is off by ~13 km, landing in inner Vienna; the street is in **Weidlingau, 48.2073, 16.2161** [Nominatim].
- **Wolfgang-Pauli-Strasse and the Bänninger bust at ETH Hönggerberg, and the annual Pauli Lectures. [A: German Wikipedia]** **GAP:** no naming year in anything consulted, so the stop's date is a **placeholder** and says so. A Göttingen street and the largest Hamburg physics lecture hall also carry the name; **neither was checked**.
- **Asteroid (13093) Wolfgangpauli** (1992 SQ24) **[A: Wikipedia, "Meanings of minor-planet names 13001-14000"]**, but that source gives **no naming date**, so the first pass's "2000" was dropped.
- **Lunar crater Pauli**, ~100 km, farside, **45.24 S, 137.52 E**, designated by the **IAU in 1970 [A: German Wikipedia, "Pauli (Mondkrater)"]**, not 2000. The longitude is noted for what it is.
- **"Pauli-Jung conjecture"** is a **retrospective coinage by later scholars, notably Harald Atmanspacher**, and the revival of interest dates from the 2010s. **[R: Wikipedia]**

## Honest gaps

1. No birth address in Vienna, no venue for the 1934 Zurich wedding, no located premises for the 1948 Jung Institute founding. Three stops use city-centre placeholders and say so.
2. The funeral date is unknown; the burial stop carries the date of death.
3. The Göttingen station anecdote has **no date in any source**; two lenses invented two different ones.
4. Pauli's attendance at the 1933 Solvay Congress is unconfirmed; the stop was cut rather than asserted.
5. The Eranos Kepler lecture rests on one lens's dating alone.
6. The Weisskopf parity bet, the Göttingen street, and the Hamburg lecture hall were **not verified this session**.
7. The room-137 remark is secondhand and its wording varies; printed as the pool gives it, flagged.
