# Nicolás Maduro Moros (b. 1962): research report
*2026-08-18, for `nicolas_maduro.journey.json`. Subject living, in pretrial custody in the United States, untried and unconvicted. Trial set 1 June 2027.*

Legend: **[A]** attested, source named. **[R]** reconstruction, tradition, self-report, or contested detail. Contradictions are flagged, not resolved. Corrected coordinates carry the authority for the correction.

## 1. El Valle: birth and formation (1962-1986)

- Born **23 November 1962** in Caracas, parish of **El Valle**, to **Nicolás Maduro García**, a union-linked activist of the left, and **Teresa de Jesús Moros**. **[A: Britannica; CIDOB]** No hospital, street or house is documented anywhere reviewed; the pin (10.463 / -66.9269) is the parish. **Gap.** Opposition figures later alleged a Colombian birth (Cúcuta); the Supreme Tribunal affirmed Caracas.
- **Liceo José Ávalos**, El Valle, with student politics. Record-based reporting says he did **not** complete the requirements for the secondary diploma **[R]**; several biographies call him a graduate. **Contradiction flagged.** All sources agree he never attended university. **[A: Britannica, EBSCO, both noting no degree]**
- **Liga Socialista.** The pool's chronology lens said "1980s"; too late. es.wikipedia gives **c. 1977**, with later seats on the Comité Nacional and the Comité Regional de Caracas. The file uses 1977 and flags the disagreement. Affiliation **[A]**, offices and chronology **[R]**.
- **Cuba, c. 1986, about a year of cadre training** on a Liga Socialista scholarship. The fact is attested **[A]**; the school is disputed. en.wikipedia: Escuela Nacional de Cuadros **Julio Antonio Mella** (Young Communists). es.wikipedia and Runrun.es: the Communist Party's **Ñico López** school. Both cannot be right; the file names both. The coordinates 23.1187 / -82.3934 fit **only** the Ñico López reading. Claims of intelligence recruitment are unproven allegation and are not used.

## 2. The wheel and the union (late 1980s to 1994)

- **Bus driver** in the Caracas transport system, then organizer and leader in **SITRAMECA**, the metro workers' union **[A: Britannica; pre-presidency profiles cited by InSight Crime]**. No depot or union office is located in any source; the pin is the Metro system generally. **Gap.** **4 February 1992**: Chávez's coup fails; Maduro active in the release campaigns **[A: Britannica]**.
- **16 December 1993, Yare**: Maduro says he first met Chávez that day. **[R: his own later account, not independently documented.]** **Pool contradiction:** the chronology lens tagged this [R], the geography lens tagged the same fact [A]; the file resolves to **[R] with documentary support**. **Coordinates corrected**: from 10.22092 / -66.72709, about 4.8 km north of the site, to **10.18029 / -66.74231** (Wikidata Q1498168).
- **Chávez released, March 1994.** Day contested: **26 March** (Asamblea Nacional, RNV) vs **27 March** (es.wikipedia).

## 3. The movement becomes a state, then the ministry (1997-2012)

- **MBR-200**, then the **Movimiento Quinta República**, 1997, year only **[A: Britannica; EBSCO]**. **8 November 1998**: elected to the old **Chamber of Deputies**, Chávez taking the presidency in December **[A: CIDOB]**. **1999**: member of the **National Constituent Assembly** **[A]**, no month in the sources, so 1999-08-01 orders the segment. **2000**: elected to the unicameral **National Assembly**; **January 2005**: elected its **president** **[A]**, merged into one stop.
- **August 2006**: foreign minister for **over six years**, managing the Cuban relationship and the bloc-building meant to reduce dependence on Washington; reputation as a negotiator rather than an ideologue. **[A: Britannica; CIDOB; diplomatic profiles]**
- **Repeated official visits to Havana** across the tenure **[A for the pattern]**, but **no individual visit is itemized** anywhere reviewed, so the Havana stop is dated 2008-01-01 purely to order the segment. **Gap.** **Coordinates corrected**: from 23.0731 / -82.3859 for the Palacio de la Revolución, about 5.5 km too far south, to **23.1226 / -82.3865** (en.wikipedia). The longitude was roughly right, the latitude was not.

## 4. The designation and the death (2012-2013)

- **October 2012**: made **executive vice president** over Diosdado Cabello after Chávez beats Capriles. **[A: Britannica; CIDOB]** Month attested, day not given.
- **8 December 2012**: Chávez, on national television, tells Venezuelans to elect Maduro if a new election becomes necessary. **[A: cadena nacional, widely reported (BBC, Reuters); transcript in Javier Biardeau R., 'El mensaje de Chávez el 8-12-2012', rebelion.org, pp. 3-4]** The file's quote comes from that transcript.
- **December 2012 to March 2013, CIMEQ, Havana**: Maduro present for surgery and treatment. **[A for the fact; the day orders the segment, the pool giving only the range.]** **Coordinates corrected**: from 23.1099 / -82.4276 to about **23.0767 / -82.4735** (Calle 216, Reparto Siboney, Playa), some 5.5 km.
- **5 March 2013**: Chávez dies; Maduro assumes executive authority; the opposition disputes the succession, arguing the interim belonged to the president of the National Assembly **[A: Britannica]**. **8 March**: eulogy at the **Academia Militar de Caracas** **[A: El Espectador]**.
- **15 March 2013, Cuartel de la Montaña: two pool errors corrected.** **Date**: the mausoleum was inaugurated and the body moved on **15 March 2013**, not "from July 2013"; the July date appears invented **[A: es.wikipedia]**. **Coordinates**: from 10.5115 / -66.9235 to **10.507222 / -66.926667**, about 550 m.

## 5. The bird and the ballot (2013)

- **2 April 2013, the pajarito**: Chávez's spirit as a small bird in a chapel, circling three times, blessing the candidacy **[A for the words: EFE wire]**; durable opposition mockery followed, **Leopoldo López suggesting a psychiatric evaluation** **[A: Infobae, Excélsior, La Nación]**.
  - **Wording corrected**: the wire reads **"acá arriba"**, not "aquí arriba", confirmed in three independent reproductions of 2 Apr 2013 (La Nación, Ecuavisa, Confirmado.com.ve). The file cites La Nación/EFE, not the HuffPost text the pool used.
  - **Place contradiction:** the quote lens says **Sabaneta, Barinas**, 2 April; the geography lens describes an elaboration at **Catia La Mar, Vargas**, 9 April; the afterlife lens says a chapel in Barinas and notes a repetition in July 2014. One stop at Sabaneta, rival place stated in `date_confidence`. He told the story more than once.
- **14 April 2013 election, figures corrected.** The chronology lens carried the **election-night preliminary bulletin** (7,586,251 / 7,361,512) beside percentages that do not match it, while the geography lens carried the correct finals: **the two lenses contradicted each other.** Used here: **7,587,579 (50.61%)** to **7,363,980 (49.12%)**, margin **223,599 / 1.49 points**, valid votes 14,990,543, turnout 79.65%. **[A: en/es.wikipedia]** Sworn in **19 April** **[A: CNN, ABC News, Washington Post]**; the two merged into one stop.
- **July 2013**: marries **Cilia Flores**, month per profiles that the pool flags for corroboration and that disagree on the day **[A/R]**. **2013**: the **ojos de Chávez** graphic mandated onto billboards, state buildings and Gran Misión Vivienda blocks, the intent described by officials as a gaze made perennial and eternal **[A: Univision/N+; Wikipedia]**, year only.

## 6. Decree, street and constituent assembly (2013-2017)

- **8 October 2013**: enabling-law speech, printed as **¡Voy a fondo, vamos con todo!** (Fondo Editorial de la Asamblea Nacional Willian Lara) **[R: survives chiefly through the government's own edition; WorldCat]**. **2014**: nationwide protests, **Leopoldo López** imprisoned **[A]**; the pool gives the year only, and February, the start of the wave, orders the segment. **September 2014**: the **69th UN General Assembly**, printed as a ministry pamphlet **[A: MINCI]**.
- **6 June 2015, the massacre remark: wording and citation both corrected.** The pool had "...un tiempo de masacre y muerte si fracasa la revolución", credited to El Mundo Internacional, 1 July 2015, Caracas, via es.wikiquote. Traced instead to **ABC (abc.es) Internacional, 6 June 2015**, reading **"prepárense para un tiempo de masacre y de muerte si fracasa la revolución bolivariana"**, after an invocation of the massacre of 27 February. Note **"y de muerte"** and **"la revolución bolivariana"**. Said on **Venezolana de Televisión** from **Ciudad Belén, estado Miranda**, not Caracas. **[A]** No source gives coordinates for Ciudad Belén; pinned approximately to the Guarenas area (10.4736 / -66.6136) and declared in `date_confidence`, the weakest pin in the file. The remark is stated flat in the campa, not aestheticized.
- **6 December 2015**: the opposition wins **112 of 167 seats**; Maduro acknowledges it **[A: PBS NewsHour]**. **2016**: recall referendum halted by procedural and judicial action before any vote **[A: Reuters/AP]**, folded into the 2017 stop. **30 July / August 2017**: Constituent Assembly elected under boycott and installed; **Smartmatic states turnout was inflated by at least a million votes** **[A: Reuters]**. **31 July 2017**: **OFAC designates Maduro personally** under EO 13692 **[A: Treasury]**, placed at Miraflores rather than Washington, since a stop belongs to whoever was there.

## 7. Two presidents (2018-2020)

- **20 May 2018**: early election, boycott, exclusions; CNE gives **6,248,864 (67.8%)** against Henri Falcón's **1,927,958 (20.9%)**, turnout near 46%; the **OAS declares the process illegitimate, 5 June 2018** **[A]**. **10 January 2019**: sworn in before the Supreme Tribunal **[A]**. **23 January 2019**: **Juan Guaidó** declares himself interim president, recognized by dozens of governments, while Maduro keeps forces, bureaucracy and territory; balcony reply quoted from Radio Universidad de Chile **[A]**.
- **3 November 2019, Havana**: closing speech at the Antiimperialist Encounter, printed as a pamphlet. **[R: venue within Havana unspecified, pin approximate to the city centre]** The economic background in that campa (collapse, hyperinflation, emigration) is **general context, not from the verified pool**, and is flagged in `date_confidence`. **19 November 2019**: the dollarization remark **[A: MercoPress]**; "escape valve" is the outlet's framing, not his.
- **26 March 2020**: DOJ unseals narco-terrorism and cocaine-importation charges, alleging FARC ties and the **Cartel de los Soles** **[A]**. **Quote dropped**: the pool's Barr sentence, beginning "The Venezolano regime, once led by Nicolás Maduro Moros...", **cannot be sourced and is internally corrupt** ("Venezolano regime" is not English). **justice.gov returned 403/401 to every automated fetch** and archive.org was offline. Attested is only the fragment, via Wikipedia citing the DOJ release: Barr describing Venezuela under Maduro's rule as **"plagued by criminality and corruption"**. Only that is used, in prose, of the country. **Do not publish the longer sentence until someone opens the DOJ page by hand.**

## 8. The vote that was not published (2021-2025)

- **6 March 2021**: Sputnik V, publicly, with Cilia Flores **[R: VTV, RT]**. Left out of the journey file for space, not for doubt.
- **3-4 November 2021, ICC: place corrected.** The pool put Karim Khan's announcement at **The Hague**. He announced it **in Caracas**, at a joint press conference with Maduro, where the two signed a memorandum of understanding. **[A: ICC OTP; en.wikipedia]** **Date contested**: en.wikipedia 4 November, contemporary wires 3 November; the file uses 3 November and states both.
- **December 2021**: state television launches **Súper Bigote** **[A: The Conversation; CNN; NBC]**; effigies of the character burned at the **Quema de Judas**, 2023 **[R: Banca y Negocios]**, plain Maduro effigies having been burned in 2014. **October 2023**: the **Barbados agreement** **[A: CRS; Carter Center]**, signed at Bridgetown between delegations. Maduro did not travel, so that stop sits at his own seat and names Bridgetown in the prose.
- **28 July 2024**: CNE president **Elvis Amoroso** announces, at about 80% of transmissions, **5,150,092 (51.2%)** against **Edmundo González Urrutia's 4,445,978 (44.2%)**; **station-level results are never published**; opposition-collected actas from about 80% of polling places show González near **67%**; the **Carter Center** cannot verify the result **[A]**. The **place of the declaration** is not established anywhere; Miraflores is used as his own seat and the reconstruction declared. **Gap.** **August 2024**: the Supreme Tribunal certifies, the **OAS General Secretariat rejects the certification** for want of disaggregated results **[A]**. **10 January 2025**: sworn in, third term **[A]**.
- **Bounty**: $15m (2020), **$25m (Jan 2025)**, **$50m (7 Aug 2025)**, with a State Department poster naming him head of the Cartel de los Soles, designated a terrorist entity by Treasury on 25 July 2025. **[A: state.gov]**
- **Canada, correction.** The pool said Canada listed Maduro among 19 officials on 6 November 2017. Wrong tranche and date: he was in the **first tranche, 40 individuals on 22 September 2017**; the 19-person tranche of 3 November did not add him. Later tranches reach roughly 139, so the "139 by October 2025" figure is credible. **[A: en.wikipedia; Global Affairs Canada]** **Maduro himself has never been on the EU list**; the 69-person figure matches, but the renewal "to 10 January 2027" could not be verified.

## 9. Capture, arraignment, custody (2026)

- **3 January 2026**: U.S. forces (Delta Force landed by 160th SOAR helicopters, taking fire) strike **Fuerte Tiuna**; **Maduro and Cilia Flores are seized from his residence inside the base**; **Delcy Rodríguez** becomes acting president. **[A: USNI News; CBS News; NPR; CNN]** The **legality** is disputed, including by the UN Secretary-General; that is separate from the physical fact of the capture, which is not. **5 January**: arraigned in Manhattan before **Judge Alvin K. Hellerstein**, pleads **not guilty**, and leaving calls himself a prisoner of war. **[A: ABC News; CNBC; NBC News]**
- **Detention: MDC Brooklyn** **[A: CNN, 6 Jan and 25 Mar 2026; Fox News; en.wikipedia]**. **Coordinates corrected** from 40.6748 / -73.9998 (Gowanus, about 2.1 km north) to **40.6562 / -74.0027**, 80 29th Street, Sunset Park.
- **22 July 2026**: trial set for **1 June 2027** **[A: Al Jazeera; amNewYork; JURIST]**. **District corrected**: the pool said Eastern District of New York; it is the **Southern District** (indictment returned in SDNY, arraignment before an SDNY judge). EDNY sits in Brooklyn, which is only where the jail is.
- **No legacy narrative can close**: he is alive, untried, 63, so the afterlife material is iconography and prosecution, not tomb and cult.
