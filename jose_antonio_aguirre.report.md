# José Antonio de Aguirre y Lecube (1904-1960): research report

*For `jose_antonio_aguirre.journey.json`: 45 stops, 9 segments, gregorian, register "national mythology: the canon is true".*

**[A]** = attested, source named in line. **[R]** = reconstruction or dossier narrative not independently confirmed. Contradictions are flagged and left standing. Where the pool corrected the background dossier, the correction is stated with its source and the dossier's original value recorded, so that nothing is quietly disappeared.

---

## 1. Bilbao and the chocolate house, 1904-1925

**Birth.** 6 March 1904, Bilbao, to Teodoro de Aguirre and Bernardina Lecube **[A, euskadi.eus, the Basque Government's own lehendakari biography]**. The address, 6 Calle de la Cruz, and the same-day baptism are **[R]**, dossier only. **Contradiction, unresolved:** a Wikipedia-summary fetch gave 1 March 1904 against euskadi.eus's 6 March; euskadi.eus is preferred, but the discrepancy is recorded, not adjudicated away.

**Schooling, split verdict.** The dossier gave this one tag; it does not deserve one. Orduña is **[A]**: Basque Wikipedia, *"Bigarren Hezkuntzako ikasketak Urduñako jesuiten ikastetxean hasi zituen"*, with cuadernosdefutbol.com (CIHEFE) naming the school, **Colegio de Santa María de la Antigua**. The Plaza Nueva ikastola is **[R]**: English Wikipedia says only "the Basque Country's first Ikastola" and names no plaza.

**Athletic Club.** Debut **[A]**: 27 November 1921 against S.D. Deusto, Campeonato Regional del Norte (cuadernosdefutbol). Everything numerical after that is contested and left contested. Basque Wikipedia gives 46 matches; cuadernosdefutbol gives "una cincuentena" including friendlies plus four goals in three official Regional matches in 1924-25; English and Spanish Wikipedia bracket the career 1921-1925 while cuadernosdefutbol runs it 1921-22 to 1925-26. The dossier's "43 appearances (eleven official) and five official goals" matches **no** retrievable source and is not used; the campa states the disagreement instead of picking a winner. One datable fixture closes the stop: 2 March 1924, friendly at Las Llanas de Sestao against Sestao Sport, 0-0.

**The law degree, contradiction resolved by a specialist source.** Dossier said 1925; English Wikipedia and sabinoarana.eus said 1926. cuadernosdefutbol gives the day: *"obtuvo el título de Licenciado el 23 de noviembre de 1925"*. **Used: 1925-11-23 [A]**, the disagreement recorded in `date_confidence`.

**The law office, unresolved.** Dossier: Calle Iturribide 2. cuadernosdefutbol: an office "across from the Calzadas de Mallona." The two are adjacent in the Casco Viejo and may be one building described twice, but the street number is unsupported. The office got no stop; it is named inside the Deusto campa in the sourced form only.

Also **[A]** (English Wikipedia): Teodoro dies 1920, the family moves to Algorta in Getxo, and the sixteen-year-old takes over household and chocolate business. City-level coordinate; no house located.

## 2. The Republic and the statute, 1931-1936

- Councillor then mayor of Getxo at twenty-seven, April 1931, and deputy for Navarre in the Constituent Cortes on 12 July 1931, resigning the mayoralty **[A, Spanish Wikipedia]**. The town-hall coordinate is **[R]**.
- **Estella is a gap.** The 1931 four-province statute project is widely reported, but no source fetched names Estella in connection with Aguirre personally. The stop is kept, tagged **[R]**, and named as a gap.
- **Marriage: dossier off by two months and by a building.** Dossier: 8 July 1933, "Basque Country." Basque Wikipedia: *"1933ko irailaren 8an, Bilboko Begoñako basilikan Mari Carmen Zabalarekin ezkondu zen."* **Used: 8 September 1933, Basilica of Begoña [A]**, with the daughter Aintzane from the same source. Begoña thereby doubles (marriage 1933, pre-oath Mass 1936), and both campas use it.
- Referendum of 5 November 1933, roughly 459,000 to about 14,000, the Alava counting method a lasting dispute **[A, Spanish Wikipedia]**; deputy for Bizkaia 8 December 1933 **[A]**.
- *Entre la libertad y la revolución, 1930-1935* (1935): **[A]** for existence, on the Basque Wikipedia bibliography independently of the dossier. Place of publication **not established, a gap**; folded into the referendum stop rather than given a pin it does not have.

## 3. The oath, October 1936

- Statute approved by the Cortes, 1 October 1936 **[A, Spanish Wikipedia]**.
- **The Cortes quotation, corrected twice.** The dossier's version was truncated and undated. Spanish Wikipedia gives it in full (*"...estamos enfrente de este movimiento subversivo porque a ello nos impelen nuestros principios, nuestros principios honrados y profundamente cristianos"*), places it at the final session of the democratic Republican Cortes on 1 October 1936, and cites **Vidarte, *Todos fuimos culpables* (1973), 514-515**, a participant memoir. The journey quotes it in full and dates it to the day.
- Mass at Begoña, then Gernika, 7 October 1936 **[A, English Wikipedia: "Basilica of Begoña, then Gernika"]**. Elected lehendakari and sworn under the tree **[A]**; the hour, 6.20 p.m., is **[R]**.
- **The oath text.** The journey uses the Basque original confirmed across two separate fetches of the Spanish Wikipedia article, with that article's own Spanish rendering in `quote_source`. The dossier's alternative Spanish wording ("Ante Dios humillado, en pie sobre la tierra vasca...") did not appear on the page fetched and is **not used**.
- **The cabinet is [R].** Four PNV, three PSOE, two Republicans, one ANV, one Communist, CNT excluded, Aguirre holding Defense: dossier plus general historiography, no primary list retrieved.

## 4. Nine months of a country, 1936-1937

**The prison massacre, upgraded from [R] to [A], minus one attribution.** Spanish Wikipedia ("Telesforo Monzón", "Batalla de Bilbao"): 4 January 1937, *"tras un bombardeo franquista"*, *"la matanza de más de 230 prisioneros"*, in *"las cárceles de Bilbao —la de Larrinaga, casa Galera, el convento de los Ángeles Custodios y el Carmelo—"*, by UGT battalions. Date, scale and four named prisons are now **[A]**. **Dropped:** the attribution to "historian Carlos Larrínaga Montenegro", which no source supports; the real Carlos Larrinaga is an economic historian and "Montenegro" is not his surname, so it looks like a conflation with the prison's own name. The stop cites the four prisons instead, its Larrinaga coordinate staying an **[R]** reconstruction standing for all four.

**Gernika, 26 April 1937 [A, English Wikipedia].** Both casualty figures reported, neither certified: the Basque government's contemporary **1,654 dead and 889 wounded**, against later name-by-name research producing roughly **126**, then about **153**, with other estimates in the low hundreds. (The dossier's geography lens transposed 1,654 as 1,645; its chronology lens had it right.)

**The broadcast, moved forward one day.** The dossier's geography lens had it going out the same evening. The NYRB is explicit: *"The morning after the bombing, Radio Bilbao broadcast a statement by the Basque president José Antonio Aguirre."* **Used: 27 April 1937 [A]**, with the Spanish Wikipedia text as the quote.

**The Iron Ring, dossier off by four months.** Dossier: Goicoechea defected "shortly before it collapsed." Spanish Wikipedia ("Cinturón de Hierro"): *"consiguió pasar las líneas del frente en febrero de 1937, llevándose consigo todos los planos."* The Ring broke 11-12 June, Bilbao fell 19 June. **Used: February 1937 [A]**, inside a stop dated to the fall. The same source backs the caution against the one-traitor framing: linear trenches, weak anti-aircraft cover, thin bunkers, *"demasiados puntos débiles como para ser un instrumento plenamente efectivo."*

**Santoña, 24 August 1937**, Ajuriaguerra's signature date from Basque Wikipedia. Aguirre's knowledge and authorization remain **disputed and are left disputed**; what is **[A]** is that he personally refused to surrender. Catalonia and the crossing into France are **[R]**, and **Le Perthus is an explicit reconstruction**: no source names a crossing point.

## 5. The escape, 1939-1941

- Paris as the pre-1940 exile hub organizing refugee camps **[A, English Wikipedia]**. **11 Avenue Marceau is [R]**, not reconfirmed, flagged as a gap in the stop.
- **Belgium, split verdict.** That he was there at the occupation is **[A]** (English Wikipedia), and La Estrella de Panamá names La Panne and Brussels. **The sister's death during the invasion is uncorroborated**; the campa reports it explicitly as what his memoir says.
- Panamanian identity, consul **Germán Gil Guardia Jaén**, the coaching, Madame Tirlemont's house, the passport collected in Berlin: **[A] per La Estrella de Panamá**, with Aguirre's memoir underneath it, a memoir the dossier flags for date errors. Coordinates city-level.
- **Sailing and arrival are two dates, not one.** Basque Wikipedia: *"1941eko uztailaren 31n, Vasholm merkataritza-ontzia hartu eta Amerikarantz irten zen"*, sailed **31 July 1941**; English Wikipedia has him reaching Rio **27 August 1941**. The dossier collapsed them; both are used. **The ship is spelled two ways**, Vasholm against Vasaholm, and the campa carries that. **Gothenburg stays [R]**: no source names the port.
- **Two sign errors in the dossier's coordinates, both fixed.** Rio was given lat **+22.8952**, plotting in the Red Sea off Sudan (**used -22.8952, -43.1804**); Montevideo lat **+34.9011**, plotting off Syria (**used -34.9011, -56.1645**). The dossier's afterlife lens had Montevideo negative and correct while its geography lens did not: the two lenses disagreed with each other.
- Montevideo, 8 October 1941, public resumption of his real name **[A, English Wikipedia]**.

## 6. New York, the tour, the exile government, 1941-1959

- **Columbia lectureship [A]** (English Wikipedia). **The money is [R]**: the Ynchausti donation, the US$4,500 salary and the residence regularized through it are corroborated by nothing retrievable.
- **The 1942 Latin American tour is a clear gap.** It rests entirely on the dossier; the English Wikipedia summary fetched does not mention it. Three stops depend on it, Mexico City, Buenos Aires and Panama City, and all three say so; Havana was folded into the Mexico City campa.
- **FBI/OSS cooperation is [R]**. The dossier's own correction is kept: "CIA agent" characterizations are anachronistic, the agency being founded in 1947.
- *Escape via Berlin*: **[A]** per the archive.org record, Macmillan 1944, reissued Nevada 1991. **Contradiction flagged:** the Spanish original is dated 1942, 1943 and 1944 across sources.
- **The 1947 strike, corrected twice.** Spanish Wikipedia: *"la huelga general del primero de mayo de 1947 en Vizcaya y Guipúzcoa, alentada por el Gobierno Vasco desde el exilio"*, repression confirmed via the Genaro Riestra article. It covered **Gipuzkoa as well as Bizkaia**, and it was **called by his own government**, which is what makes it his at all.
- **The Hague 1948, tag split.** Congress, venue, dates 7-11 May, Prieto's attendance, Madariaga's College of Europe proposal: **[A]** (English Wikipedia). **Aguirre's own attendance [R]**: neither he nor Landaburu is named there. **A contradiction inside the pool itself:** its chronology lens names Landaburu as his companion, its geography lens Madariaga. The campa reports both.
- **[R]**, dossier only: the mother's death at Saint-Jean-de-Luz in 1950, Galíndez in March 1956, the convening of the First World Basque Congress in autumn 1956, ETA's founding in 1959. **[A]**: the 1978 *Veinte años de gestión del Gobierno Vasco (1936-1956)*.

## 7. Death and afterlife

- Death in Paris, 22 March 1960, sudden cardiac event reported as angina pectoris, aged 56, about six in the evening **[A, Spanish and English Wikipedia; euskadi.eus for the hour]**; exact address unconfirmed. Body to Saint-Jean-de-Luz, one night at the Monzón house, buried 28 March 1960 after a funeral Mass at the parish church against a Francoist prohibition **[A, Basque and English Wikipedia]**.
- **Gap, stated as a gap:** no source found describes any later reburial or transfer of the body. That is absence of evidence, not evidence of absence, and the campa says "no source consulted records any later transfer."
- **Statue contradiction, resolved by the source.** Dossier flagged Calle Ercilla against Plaza Moyua; Basque Wikipedia states plainly *"estatua bat Moyua plazan"*, and the dossier's own coordinate 43.263, -2.935 is Plaza Moyua, so only the hedging needed changing. **Sculptor and unveiling date remain unfound.**
- Also **[A]**: square and statue at Gernika-Lumo, bust at Saint-Jean-de-Luz, streets in more than fifty municipalities including Montevideo, the posthumous Gran Cruz del Árbol de Gernika (2010), lehendakariagirre.eu, Onaindía's 1960 eulogy, the 2004 centenary, *Obras completas* (Sendoa, 1981). **[R]**: the Souletin folk piece *Aguirre presidenta*.
- **Not carried as fact:** the dossier's own R-tagged legend paragraph (the Francoist counter-myth that the Basques burned Gernika; the sharpening of "he hid from Hitler in Berlin"). The Berlin material appears in its sober form.

## 8. Stops that belong to somebody else

Applying the rule that a stop belongs to whoever was actually there:

1. **The dossier's Paris / Café de Flore stop is dropped as a scene.** "Ucelay tells Larrea; Larrea finds Picasso at the Café de Flore" is a documented consequence of Aguirre's broadcast, but the traveller is Larrea, the painter is Picasso, and Aguirre was in Bilbao. The relation is kept **on his own broadcast stop**, in the campa and in `suggested_refs`.
2. Three events that are his without his presence sit where he actually was: the **Galíndez disappearance** in Manhattan (his delegate, so the relation is real, written as news arriving in Paris), the **1 May 1947 strike** in Bizkaia and Gipuzkoa (his part is the convocation, not the picket line), and **ETA's founding** in 1959.

## 9. Apparatus relations for the operator

**No backward apparatus relation was found, and none is needed.** Aguirre is not a theorist and has no framework to apply to material he never treated. He is not in `EXCEPTIONS.md`, and nothing here requires that he be added. One **forward-direction** item was caught in the pool and is reported rather than silently deleted:

- **Balenciaga cutting a wedding dress for Franco's granddaughter, Madrid, 1972.** The pool offers this as an Aguirre interlock because Franco is the dictator his government fought. It postdates Aguirre's death by twelve years: a new hash in an old block. **Not written.** It stays where it already is, in `cristobal_balenciaga.journey.json`.

**Shared-geography-only** relations, declined for campa mention because the map already shows co-location, listed here so nothing vanishes silently: Balenciaga (Getaria), Durruti (Bilbao c. 1922-23, itself **[R]** in his own file; and the Bidasoa corridor, 1924), García Lorca (same rising, never met), Debussy (Saint-Jean-de-Luz 1917), Mises (1940, a different border and the opposite route), Perón (Ciudad Trujillo 1958-60; the Trujillo thread runs to Galíndez, not between Aguirre and Perón).

**Carried into campa, both one-directional, both rooted in Aguirre's own acts.** `pablo_picasso`: his 27 April 1937 broadcast is the news that reaches Paris and turns Picasso from the pavilion mural he had begun to *Guernica*, **[A]** for the broadcast and for the painting's origin in the bombing's international reporting, though Picasso's file never names Aguirre. `allen_dulles`: Dulles ran the OSS New York station at 630 Fifth Avenue from October 1941, the same city and months as the Columbia appointment, and a claim that he smoothed Aguirre's residence papers circulates, **[R]**, written into the campa as exactly that; Dulles's file never names Aguirre.

The Dulles item is the borderline case, kept because it is a **claimed direct relation** rather than a coincidence of place, and hedged in the prose as well as in the confidence field.

**Canonical pins inherited byte-identical:** Paris **48.8566, 2.3522** (`pablo_picasso`, `roland_barthes`); Buenos Aires **-34.6037, -58.3816** (`juan_peron`); Saint-Jean-de-Luz **43.3889, -1.6633** (`claude_debussy`), where the pool's geography lens gave 43.3888 and its afterlife lens 43.3889. The Basque Government's Paris headquarters keeps its own **48.8677, 2.3009** pin, distinct from the canonical Paris pin used for the death and the 1956 congress.
