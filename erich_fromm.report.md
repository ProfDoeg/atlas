# Erich Fromm, 1900-1980: research report

*Compiled 2026-08-24 for `erich_fromm.journey.json`. Frankfurt to Muralto: an Orthodox
childhood in the Westend, a Heidelberg doctorate on the sociology of Jewish law, the
psychoanalytic half of the Institut für Sozialforschung, exile through Geneva to Columbia,
twenty-four years in Mexico, six last years on Lago Maggiore.*

**Legend.** **[A]** attested, source named in line. **[R]** reconstruction, tradition,
house source, or unconfirmed dossier lead. Contradictions are printed and left standing.
The principal source, the International Erich Fromm Society chronology
(`fromm-online.org/en/life/important-dates/`) maintained by Rainer Funk, Fromm's literary
executor, is the most detailed timeline in existence and also the estate's account of its
own subject; where it stands alone it is marked **[A, house source]**.

---

## 1. Frankfurt, 1900-1919

**[A]** Born Erich Seligmann Fromm, Frankfurt am Main, 23 March 1900, only child of
Naphtali Fromm, wine merchant, and Rosa Fromm née Krause; Orthodox household, rabbis on
both sides (Fromm Society; NYPL; English Wikipedia). **[R]** Liebigstrasse 27 is
unconfirmed by anything reached, so the file pins the birth at city centre and says so.
**[A]** Final examination at the Wöhler-Realgymnasium, 1918; enrolment at the University
of Frankfurt in jurisprudence, abandoned after two semesters.

### The Lehrhaus problem, unresolved

**[R]** The Fromm Society chronology reads "1919 - Co-founded the Freie Jüdische
Lehrhaus". Three defects, none silently repaired. **Year wrong:** German Wikipedia gives
**1920**, Rosenzweig first director. **Fromm absent from the institution's own record:**
that article names Buber, Löwenthal, Benno Jacob, Koch, Strauss, Pappenheim, Kracauer,
Agnon and Scholem, and does not mention Fromm in any role. **Address wrong:** it "hatte
entgegen seinem Namen keine feste Adresse", and Börsenstrasse 7 appears only as a 1923
venue. Against all this, `martin_buber.journey.json` states that Fromm taught there
alongside Buber. **Both readings sit in the stop's campa and `date_confidence`; neither is
adopted.** The pin is inherited byte-identical from the Buber file.

## 2. Heidelberg, 1919-1926

**[A]** Heidelberg from summer 1919; switch to sociology under **Alfred Weber**, in the
lecture orbit of Jaspers and Rickert. **[A, house source]** Talmud with **Salman Baruch
Rabinkow**, a Chabad-line Lithuanian, to 1925. **[A]** PhD 1922, *Das jüdische Gesetz*,
under Weber. **[A, house source]** 1924, the **Therapeutikum** on the Mönchhofstrasse in
Heidelberg-Neuenheim with **Frieda Reichmann**, psychoanalytic and Torah-observant at
once; Fromm's own analysis begins there with her. **[A]** Marriage to Reichmann, 16 June
1926; she is about ten years his senior and had been his analyst. **[R]** Contact with
**Georg Groddeck** at Baden-Baden, 1926, house source only; folded into the marriage stop
rather than pinned.

## 3. Berlin and the Institute, 1927-1933

**[A, house source]** 1928, didactic analysis with **Hanns Sachs** at the Berlin
Psychoanalytic Institute; qualification as a lay analyst with no medical degree. The pin
is an approximate Tiergarten-district location, not an address, and says so. **[A]** 1929,
co-founding of the South German (Frankfurt) Institute for Psychoanalysis, folded into the
Institut für Sozialforschung stop for want of an address. **[A]** 1930, joins the
**Institut für Sozialforschung** under **Horkheimer**, and opens his own Berlin practice.

**[A]** Residence at **Bayerischer Platz 1**, Berlin-Schöneberg: German Wikipedia, "Am
Bayerischen Platz 1 wohnte Erich Fromm", same page confirming the 1993 *Orte des
Erinnerns* signage. **[R]** The plaque-series name "Mit Freud in Berlin", from an
automated summary, is unconfirmed and unused. **[A]** 1932, the two programmatic essays in
the *Zeitschrift für Sozialforschung*. **[A, house source]** 1931, tuberculosis,
separation from Frieda Reichmann, the **Schatzalp** above Davos through 1934 and again for
six months in 1938; 1933, guest lectures in Chicago and the death of Naphtali Fromm.
**Gap:** the Chicago host and topics are unknown; pin at city precision.

## 4. Exile, 1933-1940

**[A]** Geneva. The pool first recorded this as an unconfirmed lead; English Wikipedia
states he moved there after the Nazi takeover and then to Columbia in 1934. A search
failure corrected, not an absence. The Fromm Society does not mention Geneva at all.
**[A, house source]** Forced emigration 25 May 1934, arrival New York 31 May 1934. **[A]**
1936, the social-psychological section of *Studien über Autorität und Familie* and its
survey material.

**[R]** The break with Horkheimer's Institute, conventionally 1938-39, from McLaughlin,
"Origin Myths in the Social Sciences". **Sources differ on whether there was one decisive
break or a gradual estrangement**, and no act fixes the date. Tagged [R] and hedged.

**Contradiction, not resolved: naturalization.** Fromm Society, 25 May 1940. NYPL finding
aid, 1941. Both printed, in the campa and in `date_confidence`.

## 5. The American books, 1941-1949

**Contradiction, stated.** The pool gave Bennington as 1941-49, contradicting its own cited
source: the Fromm Society has him beginning at the New School in 1941 and accepting the
part-time Bennington professorship in 1942. English Wikipedia's 1941-1949 is the outlier.
**The file gives the New School 1941, Bennington 1942, and prints the disagreement.**

**Contradiction, resolved against the pool: the William Alanson White Institute.** The
pool asserted 1946 from the dossier against the Fromm Society's 1943. The current English
Wikipedia article gives "founded in 1943" and names "Erich Fromm and Clara Thompson,
joined by Harry Stack Sullivan, Frieda Fromm-Reichmann, David Rioch and Janet Rioch". Two
sources now agree on **1943**; the dossier's claim that Wikipedia says 1946 is no longer
true of the article. Nothing fetched documents 1946. The pool's duplicate 1943 and 1946
pins are merged into one stop.

**[A]** 24 July 1944, marriage to **Henny Gurland**, née Meyer, photographer, who crossed
the Pyrenees in September 1940 with the party travelling with **Walter Benjamin** (no
file, so prose, not an interlock). **[A]** *Man for Himself*, 1947.

**Contradiction, not resolved: the Terry Lectures.** Fromm Society, 1948; NYPL and Yale's
own roll, 1949-50; book 1950. The file dates the stop 1949 and prints both.

## 6. Mexico, 1950-1965

**[A, house source]** Mexico City, 6 June 1950, the move made for Henny Gurland's health.
**[A]** 1951, professor in the Faculty of Medicine at UNAM, founding a psychoanalytic
training programme; *The Forgotten Language*. **[A]** 4 June 1952, death of Henny Gurland
Fromm; **gap:** nothing of the funeral survives. **[A]** 18 December 1953, marriage to
**Annis Freeman**, née Glover. **[A]** 1955, *The Sane Society*, answered the same year by
**Marcuse**'s *Eros and Civilization*. **[A, house source]** 1956, Cuernavaca; *The Art of
Loving*, put by Funk at over 1.5 million English copies by 1970. **[R]** 1957, the Zen
seminars with **Suzuki** and de Martino at Cuernavaca, published 1960: house source, venue
unconfirmed; Frieda Fromm-Reichmann dies the same year. **[A]** 1957-61 Michigan State;
1961 *Marx's Concept of Man*; 1962 an NYU adjunct chair; 1964 *The Heart of Man*; 1965
retirement from UNAM and *Socialist Humanism*.

**Gap, and a stop not staged.** The 1958 Mike Wallace interview is attested but no source
names a studio or city, so it is cited on the Michigan State stop rather than placed at an
invented location. The NYU chair lost its stop to the stop cap and survives as a
`suggested_ref`. **[R]** 1960-1968, fieldwork with **Michael Maccoby** in a Morelos
village, published 1970 as *Social Character in a Mexican Village*. **The village is
unnamed everywhere, Maccoby's own entry included;** rural Morelos is pinned approximately
and the gap is stated in the campa itself.

**SANE: a claim demoted.** The pool carried "Helps found the National Committee for a Sane
Nuclear Policy" as [A], dated 1957 and misplaced between the 1961 and 1962 entries.
**Overturned.** English Wikipedia gives SANE as founded November 1957 by Lenore Marshall
and Norman Cousins, without Fromm, and states that "The name 'SANE' came from the concepts
put forth by Erich Fromm in his book The Sane Society." He was a patron, not a founder.
**No founding claim is in the file;** the naming relation appears in the 1955 campa.

## 7. The late books, 1966-1974

**[A, house source]** 1966, *You Shall Be as Gods*; Humanist of the Year; first heart
attack, 6 December. **[A]** 1968, *The Revolution of Hope*; work for **Eugene McCarthy**'s
antiwar campaign. **Gap:** no source says whether that work was done in the United States
or from Cuernavaca by post; the campa states that gap outright rather than inferring a
location from his residence, and the `date_confidence` records the same. **[A, house source]** 1969, a Locarno apartment for
the summers; 1973, *The Anatomy of Human Destructiveness*; 1974, permanent relocation to
Muralto and the written submission "Remarks on the Policy of Détente" to the Senate
Foreign Relations Committee. **[R]** The Washington item is an unconfirmed dossier lead,
probably written rather than delivered. **No Washington stop is staged;** it is folded
into the Muralto stop, whose campa says whether he travelled is not recorded.

## 8. Muralto and the end, 1975-1980

**Correction, two defects in one pool entry.** The pool read "1976: Publishes To Have or
To Be?; suffers a third heart attack", out of sequence after the 1977 item. The Fromm
Society gives a second attack in 1977 and a third in 1978, nothing in 1976, and records
the 1975 gallbladder surgery in New York, which the pool dropped. **The file dates the
book 1976, keeps the surgery, and gives the attacks as 1977 and 1978.** A second
correction: the works list dates *The Greatness and Limitations of Freud's Thought* to
1980; 1979 is the German original, *Sigmund Freuds Psychoanalyse: Grösse und Grenzen*. The
file gives both.

**[A]** 1979, the Nelly Sachs Prize from the city of Dortmund (English Wikipedia).
**Gap:** nothing says whether Fromm travelled to receive it; the stop is kept, at Dortmund,
stating the uncertainty in its own campa rather than staging an attendance.

**[A]** Died at home in Muralto, 18 March 1980, five days short of eighty, of what the
Fromm Society specifies as a **fourth** heart attack, a detail both pool lenses dropped.
**[A]** Cremation at Bellinzona: German Wikipedia, "Erich Fromm wurde in Bellinzona
(Schweiz) beigesetzt", verified verbatim, with the Fromm Society saying outright "cremated
in Bellinzona", closing the rite question the German verb leaves open. **Gap:** the
cemetery is unnamed everywhere.

## 9. Afterlife, cited but not staged

He was not there; other people did these things after he died. All carried as `sources`
and `suggested_refs`, never as stops. **[R]** Erich Fromm papers, NYPL, MssCol 1090 ([R]
only because the page was not reloaded). **[A]** The **Erich Fromm Institut Tübingen**,
Hintere Grabenstrasse 24-30, holding the estate and unpublished texts. **[A]** The
**Internationale Erich-Fromm-Gesellschaft**, founded 1985, about 650 members in late 2023,
awarding the Erich-Fromm-Preis. **[A]** The Erich Fromm Study Center, IPU Berlin: roughly
**60 million copies** sold worldwide. **[R]** Friedman, *The Lives of Erich Fromm* (2013),
not refetched. The Bayerischer Platz plaque **is** used on a stop: he lived in the
building.

**Two afterlife contradictions, not resolved.** (1) *Gesamtausgabe*: German Wikipedia's
death sentence says a **ten-volume** edition appeared in 1980; the same article's editions
section and the Fromm Institute describe **twelve** completed by 1999. Both printed.
(2) **The Amnesty International legend.** A circulating claim makes Fromm a founder;
Amnesty traces to Benenson's 1961 "Appeal for Amnesty" and no record of a Fromm role was
located. An unresolved legend, **absent from the journey file**.

## Apparatus relations for the operator

`EXCEPTIONS.md` read before writing. **Fromm is not on the list** and holds no forward
grant. **Nothing in the pool required the exception:** no stop applies his framework to
material he did not himself treat. Two things came close enough to name:

1. **Backward, and it needed no licence.** *You Shall Be as Gods* (1966) and *The Forgotten
   Language* (1951) read the Hebrew Bible, myth and folk tale through his own apparatus:
   his own books, at his own pin, in the year he wrote them, so not apparatus stops in the
   licensed sense. Applying biophilia and necrophilia, or the social character, backward
   onto material Fromm never discussed would require adding him to the list. Not done.
2. **Forward, caught and not written.** The obvious forward reach is *Escape from
   Freedom*'s automaton conformity, and the technotronic boredom of *The Anatomy of Human
   Destructiveness*, applied to algorithmic recommendation and machine-generated speech.
   Fromm died in 1980 and is not on record on any of it. **The direction caught is
   forward, which the exceptions file does not licence and which no agent may grant
   itself. Nothing of the kind is in the file.** Recorded so the operator can rule on it.

## Quotations: five corrected, one refused

All verified through Wikiquote's sourced compilation and, for five, against Open Library
search-inside. **No complete original scan was reachable** (Internet Archive
lending-restricted, Monoskop HTTP 403).

1. "Care and responsibility are constituent elements of love..." is **Ch. 3** of *Man for
   Himself*, not Ch. 4; it recurs in *The Art of Loving*, Ch. 2.
2. "There is only one meaning of life: the act of living itself" is **not a sentence**; it
   closes "He is aware of himself as an active and creative individual and recognizes
   that..." (*Escape from Freedom*, Ch. 7). Quoted as the fragment it is.
3. "If it is true that love is the only sane and satisfactory answer..." drops ", as I have
   tried to show," and cuts at "perish". Restored to "...perish of its own contradiction
   with the basic necessities of human nature." Wikiquote gives no chapter and it is not
   in Ch. 2, so the file cites the book alone.
4. "Just as modern mass production requires the standardization of commodities..." was cut
   before its point: "...and this standardization is called equality" (Ch. 2, p. 16).
5. "By necrophilia is meant love for all that is violence and destruction..." stopped at
   "the worship of force" with no ellipsis; restored in full, cited as "Credo" (1965).

**Refused:** the technotronic-boredom sentence (*Anatomy*, p. 273), whose verbatim text
carries em dashes around a middle clause. Left out rather than altered.
